## XVII The Accusing Finger / 十七 指控的手指
===Original===
Of my wanderings after I left the Museum on that black and dismal *dies irae*, I have but a dim recollection. But I must have traveled a quite considerable distance, since it wanted an hour or two to the time for returning to the surgery, and I spent the interval walking swiftly through streets and squares, unmindful of the happenings around, intent only on my present misfortune, and driven by a natural impulse to seek relief in bodily exertion. For mental distress sets up, as it were, a sort of induced current of physical unrest; a beneficent arrangement, by which a dangerous excess of emotional excitement may be transformed into motor energy, and so safely got rid of. The motor apparatus acts as a safety-valve to the psychical; and if the engine races for a while, with the onset of a bodily fatigue the emotional pressure-gauge returns to a normal reading.

===Chinese===
在那个阴沉晦暗的*dies irae*（震怒之日）离开博物馆之后，我一路如何游荡，如今只余模糊的记忆。但想必走了颇远的一段路，因为离回诊所应诊的时辰还差一两个钟头，我便把这段光阴消磨在疾行于街衢广场之间，对周遭发生的一切浑不在意，一心只念着眼下的不幸，又受着天性冲动的驱使，想借身体的操劳寻求排遣。原来精神上的痛苦，好比诱发出一股肉体不安的感应电流；这是一种仁慈的安排，凭着它，情绪亢奋那危险的超载得以转化为运动的能量，从而安然消解。运动器官之于心灵，恰似一只安全阀；引擎纵然轰鸣狂转一阵，待到肉体疲劳袭来，情绪的压力表也就回落到正常的读数上了。

===Original===
And so it was with me. At first I was conscious of nothing but a sense of utter bereavement, of the shipwreck of all my hopes. But, by degrees, as I threaded my way among the moving crowds, I came to a better and more worthy frame of mind. After all, I had lost nothing that I had ever had. Ruth was still all that she had ever been to me﻿—perhaps even more; and if that had been a rich endowment yesterday, why not today also? And how unfair it would be to her if I should mope and grieve over a disappointment that was no fault of hers and for which there was no remedy? Thus I reasoned with myself, and to such purpose that, by the time I reached Fetter Lane, my dejection had come to quite manageable proportions and I had formed the resolution to get back to the *status quo ante bellum* as soon as possible.

===Chinese===
我便是如此。起初我只觉万般失落，所有希望尽数倾覆。然而，在熙攘人流中穿行渐久，我的心境也渐渐好转，变得较为达观。毕竟，我并未失去任何曾经拥有的东西。露丝于我仍是她一向的那样——甚或更有过之；倘若这份丰厚的赐予昨天值得珍视，今天何尝不然？况且，这场失意既非她的过错，又无可挽回，我若为此郁郁寡欢、自伤自怜，对她岂非不公？我如此这般与自己讲理，竟颇见成效：待走到菲特巷时，我的沮丧已缩成相当可控的分量，并且拿定主意，要尽早恢复*status quo ante bellum*（战前原状）。

===Original===
About eight o’clock, as I was sitting alone in the consulting-room, gloomily persuading myself that I was now quite resigned to the inevitable, Adolphus brought me a registered packet, at the handwriting on which my heart gave such a bound that I had much ado to sign the receipt. As soon as Adolphus had retired (with undissembled contempt of the shaky signature) I tore open the packet, and as I drew out a letter a tiny box dropped on the table.

===Chinese===
八点钟左右，我正独自坐在诊室里，阴郁地劝说自己如今已甘心认命，阿道弗斯送进来一个挂号邮包，一瞥那上面的笔迹，我的心便猛地一跳，连回执都几乎签不成字。等阿道弗斯退下去（他对那发颤的签名毫不掩饰地表示鄙夷）之后，我扯开邮包，往外抽信时，一只小小的盒子落在了桌上。

===Original===
The letter was all too short, and I devoured it over and over again with the eagerness of a condemned man reading a reprieve:

===Chinese===
信写得未免太短，我读了一遍又一遍，那份急切，恰似死囚诵读缓刑的文书：

===Original===
> My Dear Paul,
> Forgive me for leaving you so abruptly this afternoon, and leaving you so unhappy, too. So I commend you to the good Osiris; may his eye be upon you, ever watchful over your welfare in the absence of
> Your affectionate friend, **Ruth**.

===Original===
It was a sweet letter, I thought, even if it carried little comfort; quiet and reticent like its writer, but with an undertone of affection. I laid it down at length, and, taking the ring from its box, examined it fondly. Though but a copy, it had all the quaintness and feeling of the antique original, and, above all, it was fragrant with the spirit of the giver. Dainty and delicate, wrought of silver and gold, with an inlay of copper, I would not have exchanged it for the Koh-i-noor; and when I had slipped it on my finger its tiny eye of blue enamel looked up at me so friendly and companionable that I felt the glamour of the old-world superstition stealing over me too.

===Chinese===
这是一封亲切可爱的信，我想，尽管其中安慰的成分寥寥；它安静而含蓄，一如写信的人，字里行间却透着深情底韵。我终于把信放下，从盒中取出戒指，深情地端详。它虽只是件仿制品，却有古物原型的全部雅趣与情韵，而尤其可贵的是，它散发着赠予人的气质。精致纤巧，以银与金制成，又嵌着铜饰，拿光山钻石（Koh-i-noor）来换我都不肯；等我把它套上手指，那小小的蓝珐琅眼睛友善而亲昵地仰望着我，我不觉也感到那古老迷信的魅力悄悄袭上身来。

===Original===
Not a single patient came in this evening, which was well for me (and also for the patient), as I was able forthwith to write in reply a long letter; but this I shall spare the long-suffering reader excepting its concluding paragraph:

===Chinese===

===Original===

> 你忠实而深情的朋友，**保罗**。

===Original===
This letter I addressed and stamped, and then, with a wry grimace which I palmed off on myself (but not on Adolphus) as a cheerful smile, I went out and dropped it into the postbox; after which I further deluded myself by murmuring *Nunc dimittis* and assuring myself that the incident was now absolutely closed.

===Chinese===
我在信封上写好地址、贴上邮票，随即扮出一个扭曲的怪相——我骗自己说（却骗不过阿道弗斯）那是一副愉快的笑容——出门把信投进了邮筒；之后我又低声念着*Nunc dimittis*（西面颂），哄骗自己说这段公案如今已彻底了结。

===Original===
But despite this comfortable assurance I was, in the days that followed, an exceedingly miserable young man. It is all very well to write down troubles of this kind as trivial and sentimental. They are nothing of the kind. When a man of essentially serious nature has found the one woman of all the world who fulfils his highest ideals of womanhood, who is, in fact, a woman in ten thousand, to whom he has given all that he has to give of love and worship, the sudden wreck of all his hopes is no small calamity. And so I found it. Resign myself as I would to the bitter reality, the ghost of the might-have-been haunted me night and day, so that I spent my leisure wandering abstractedly about the streets, always trying to banish thought and never for an instant succeeding. A great unrest was upon me; and when I received a letter from Dick Barnard announcing his arrival at Madeira, homeward bound, I breathed a sigh of relief. I had no plans for the future, but I longed to be rid of the now irksome, routine of the practise﻿—to be free to come and go when and how I pleased.

===Chinese===
尽管有这句宽心话垫底，接下来的那些日子里，我仍是个愁苦万状的年轻人。把这类烦恼一笔勾销为琐屑多感，倒也轻巧；它们其实绝非如此。一个本质上严肃的人，既已在普天之下寻到了那唯一一个符合他女性理想之极致的女子——事实上是万里挑一的女子——并已把他在爱与崇敬上所有能给的一切都给了她，那么全部希望的骤然倾覆便绝非小小的灾难。我也确实领教了。任凭我怎样向这苦涩的现实低头认命，那“本可如何”的幽灵仍日夜缠着我不放，害得我把闲暇都耗在心不在焉的街头漫游里，一味想驱散思绪，却一刻也不曾成功。一股大大的不安压在我身上；等到收到迪克·巴纳德的信，说他已抵达马德拉（Madeira）、踏上归程，我才长出了一口气。我对前途并无筹划，只渴望摆脱如今已变得烦人的诊所例行公事——渴望来去自由，何时走、怎么走，全凭自家高兴。

===Chinese===
