## I The Vanishing Man / 一 失踪的人

===Original===
The school of St. Margaret’s Hospital was fortunate in its lecturer on Medical Jurisprudence, or Forensic Medicine, as it is sometimes described. At some schools the lecturer on this subject is appointed apparently for the reason that he lacks the qualifications to lecture on any other. But with us it was very different: John Thorndyke was not only an enthusiast, a man of profound learning and great reputation, but he was an exceptional teacher, lively and fascinating in style and of endless resources. Every remarkable case that had ever been reported he appeared to have at his fingers’ ends; every fact﻿—chemical, physical, biological, or even historical﻿—that could in any way be twisted into a medico-legal significance, was pressed into his service; and his own varied and curious experiences seemed as inexhaustible as the widow’s curse. One of his favorite devices for giving life and interest to a rather dry subject was that of analyzing and commenting upon contemporary cases as reported in the papers (always, of course, with a due regard to the legal and social proprieties); and it was in this way that I first became introduced to the astonishing series of events that was destined to exercise so great an influence on my own life.

===Chinese===
圣玛格丽特医院（St. Margaret's Hospital）的医学校有幸拥有一位出色的法医学（Medical Jurisprudence，有时亦称 Forensic Medicine）讲师。在某些学校，这门课的讲师之所以得到任命，理由看来仅仅是：他已不具备讲授其他任何课程的资格。但在我们这里，情况却大不相同：约翰·桑代克（John Thorndyke）不仅对此道满怀热忱、学识渊博、声名卓著，更是一位出类拔萃的教师，讲风生动迷人，材料层出不穷。凡曾有报道的著名案例，他似乎无一不烂熟于心；凡是能以任何方式引申出法医学意义的种种事实——化学的、物理的、生物的、乃至历史的——都被他征调来为己所用；而他自己那一段段光怪陆离的亲身经历，更如寡妇的诅咒一般没完没了，仿佛永不枯竭。他惯用的招数之一，是拿报纸上报道的时事案例来剖析评点，给这门不免枯燥的学科注入生气与趣味（当然，始终对法律与社交上的种种规矩给予应有的尊重）；而我最初得以知晓那一连串惊人的事件——它们注定要对我自己的人生产生极为深远的影响——恰恰正是通过这个途径。

===Original===
The lecture which had just been concluded had dealt with the rather unsatisfactory subject of survivorship. Most of the students had left the theater, and the remainder had gathered round the lecturer’s table to listen to the informal comments that Dr. Thorndyke was wont to deliver on these occasions in an easy, conversational manner, leaning against the edge of the table and apparently addressing his remarks to a stick of blackboard chalk that he held in his fingers.

===Chinese===
刚刚结束的那堂课，讲的是“生存者问题”（survivorship）这个颇不能令人满意的题目。大多数学生已经离开阶梯教室，余下的围拢到讲台四周，听桑代克博士发表他惯常于此类场合所作的即兴评论。他斜倚着桌沿，神态闲适，语气如谈天一般，那一番话仿佛是说给指间捏着的一截黑板粉笔听的。

===Original===
“The problem of survivorship,” he was saying, in reply to a question put by one of the students, “ordinarily occurs in cases where the bodies of the parties are producible, or where, at any rate, the occurrence of death and its approximate time are actually known. But an analogous difficulty may arise in a case where the body of one of the parties is not forthcoming, and the fact of death may have to be assumed on collateral evidence.

===Chinese===
“生存者问题，”他正在回答一个学生提出的问题，“通常出现在各方当事人的遗体均可出示的案件中，或者至少，死亡的发生及其大致时间确属已知的案件中。但相仿的难题也可能出现在另一种情形里：一方当事人的遗体下落不明，死亡事实只能依据间接证据加以推定。

===Original===
“Here, of course, the vital question to be settled is, what is the latest instant at which it is certain that this person was alive? And the settlement of that question may turn on some circumstance of the most trivial and insignificant kind. There is a case in this morning’s paper which illustrates this. A gentleman has disappeared rather mysteriously. He was last seen by the servant of a relative at whose house he had called. Now, if this gentleman should never reappear, dead or alive, the question as to what was the latest moment at which he was certainly alive will turn upon the further question: ‘Was he or was he not wearing a particular article of jewelry when he called at the relative’s house?’ ”

===Chinese===
“在这类案件里，需要解决的关键问题当然是：能够确定此人尚在人世的最后一刻究竟在何时？而这一问题的解决，可能系于某个最为琐碎、最不起眼的情节。今天早晨的报纸上正好有一个案子可以说明这一点。一位绅士相当神秘地失踪了。最后见到他的，是他登门拜访的一位亲戚家的仆人。现在，倘若这位绅士从此不再露面——无论死活——那么，他确知尚在人世的最后时刻究竟为何时，就将取决于另一个问题：‘他到那位亲戚家拜访时，究竟有没有佩戴那件特殊的珠宝饰物？’”

===Original===
He paused with a reflective eye bent upon the stump of chalk he still held; then, noting the expectant interest with which we were regarding him, he resumed:

===Chinese===
他停了下来，若有所思的目光落在手中仍捏着的那截粉笔头上；随即，察觉到我们正满怀期待地注视着他，便又接了下去：

===Original===
“The circumstances in this case are very curious; in fact, they are highly mysterious; and if any legal issues should arise in respect of them, they are likely to yield some very remarkable complications. The gentleman who has disappeared, Mr. John Bellingham, is a man well known in archeological circles. He recently returned from Egypt, bringing with him a very fine collection of antiquities﻿—some of which, by the way, he has presented to the British Museum, where they are now on view﻿—and having made this presentation, he appears to have gone to Paris on business. I may mention that the gift consisted of a very fine mummy and a complete set of tomb-furniture. The latter, however, had not arrived from Egypt at the time when the missing man left for Paris, but the mummy was inspected on the fourteenth of October at Mr. Bellingham’s house by Dr. Norbury of the British Museum, in the presence of the donor and his solicitor, and the latter was authorized to hand over the complete collection to the British Museum authorities when the tomb-furniture arrived; which he has since done.

===Chinese===
“本案的情节十分离奇；说实在的，堪称高度神秘；倘若日后围绕它产生任何法律争端，多半会引出一些非常罕见的复杂情况。失踪的这位绅士，约翰·贝林厄姆先生（Mr. John Bellingham），是考古学界的一位知名人士。他不久前刚从埃及（Egypt）归来，随身带回一大批精美的古物——顺便一提，其中一部分他已捐赠给大英博物馆（British Museum），目前正在那里展出——办完这桩捐赠之后，他似乎因事前往巴黎（Paris）去了。可以说明的是，那件赠品包括一具十分精美的木乃伊和一整套墓葬随葬品。不过，在失踪者动身赴巴黎时，后者尚未从埃及运抵；木乃伊则已于十月十四日在贝林厄姆先生府上由大英博物馆的诺伯里博士（Dr. Norbury）查验，捐赠人及其律师均在场，并授权后者待随葬品运到后，将全部藏品移交大英博物馆方面；此事他后来已经办妥。

===Original===
“From Paris he seems to have returned on the twenty-third of November, and to have gone direct to Charing Cross to the house of a relative, a Mr. Hurst, who is a bachelor and lives at Eltham. He appeared at the house at twenty minutes past five, and as Mr. Hurst had not yet come down from town and was not expected until a quarter to six, he explained who he was and said he would wait in the study and write some letters. The housemaid accordingly showed him into the study, furnished him with writing materials, and left him.

===Chinese===

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Original===

He had disappeared without leaving a trace, and what made the incident more odd was that the housemaid was certain that he had not gone out by the front door. At any rate﻿—and this is the important fact﻿—he was not in the house, and no one had seen him leave it.

===Chinese===

===Original===
“After a hasty meal Mr. Hurst returned to town and called at the office of Mr. Bellingham’s solicitor and confidential agent, a Mr. Jellicoe, and mentioned the matter to him. ===Chinese===
“匆匆吃过一顿便饭，赫斯特先生便赶回城里，到贝林厄姆先生的律师兼机密代理人——杰利科先生（Mr. Jellicoe）——的事务所登门拜访，向他提起了这件事。杰利科先生对自己当事人从巴黎返回一事一无所知，两人当即乘火车前往伍德福德（Woodford），失踪者的弟弟戈弗雷·贝林厄姆先生（Mr. Godfrey Bellingham）就住在那里。开门迎客的仆人说戈弗雷先生不在家，但他的女儿在藏书室里。那是一座独立的小屋，坐落在宅子后面花园再往外的灌木丛中。两位来客在那里不仅找到了贝林厄姆小姐（Miss Bellingham），还找到了她的父亲——他是从后门进来的。

===Original===
“Mr. Godfrey and his daughter listened to Mr. Hurst’s story with the greatest surprise, and assured him that they had neither seen nor heard anything of John Bellingham.

===Chinese===

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Original===

桑代克博士的这番建议得到了在场众人的一致响应，因为法医学在圣玛格丽特是一门生气勃勃的学科，我们人人都对它怀有浓厚的兴趣。于是我们全体出动，结伴前往最近的报摊，每人买了一份《每日电讯报》（*Daily Telegraph*），然后一同转移到公共休息室，狼吞虎咽地读完那篇报道，随即就本案的种种关窍展开讨论，全然不受那类细谨顾忌的束缚——而正是那些顾忌，折磨着我们那位过于神经过敏、过于一丝不苟的老师。
