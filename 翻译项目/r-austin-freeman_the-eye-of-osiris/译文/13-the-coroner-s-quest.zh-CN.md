## XIII The Coroner’s Quest / 十三 验尸官的追查
===Original===
The proceedings of that fine old institution, the coroner’s court, are apt to have their dignity impaired by the somewhat unjudicial surroundings amidst which they are conducted. The present inquiry was to be held in a long room attached to the inn, ordinarily devoted, as its various appurtenances testified, to gatherings of a more convivial character.

===Chinese===
验尸法庭这一古老而体面的机构，其开庭程序每每因场所环境的几分不类司法，而使庄严为之减损。眼下的这场审讯，便设在客栈附设的一间长屋里；屋里种种陈设物件俱可作证：这地方平素本是供性质更为欢畅的聚会之用的。

===Original===
Hither I betook myself after a protracted lunch and a meditative pipe, and being the first to arrive﻿—the jury having already been sworn and conducted to the mortuary to view the remains﻿—whiled away the time by considering the habits of the customary occupants of the room by the light of the objects contained in it. A wooden target with one or two darts sticking in it hung on the end wall and invited the Robin Hoods of the village to try their skill; a system of incised marks on the oaken table made sinister suggestions of shove-halfpenny; and a large open box filled with white wigs, gaudily colored robes and wooden spears, swords and regalia, crudely coated with gilded paper, obviously appertained to the puerile ceremonials of the Order of Druids.

===Chinese===
一顿从容的长饭、一斗沉思的板烟之后，我便移步到此；我是到得最早的一个——陪审团已经宣誓完毕，被领去停尸房验看遗骸了——于是我便借着屋里器物所提供的线索，揣摩起这屋子惯常那些光顾者的习性来，以此消磨光阴。尽头的墙上挂着一个木靶，上头插着一两支飞镖，招引村里的罗宾汉们前来一试身手；橡木桌上一套刻划的道道，隐隐透出“推半便士”赌局的可疑痕迹；还有一口敞开的大箱，盛着白色假发、彩袍和木制的矛、剑及仪仗，都粗糙地包着金纸，一望而知是德鲁伊教团（Order of Druids）那些幼稚仪式的行头。

===Original===
I had exhausted the interest of these relics and had transferred my attentions to the picture gallery when the other spectators and the witnesses began to arrive. Hastily I seated myself in the only comfortable chair beside the one placed at the head of the table, presumably for the coroner; and I had hardly done so when the latter entered accompanied by the jury. Immediately after them came the sergeant, Inspector Badger, one or two plainclothes men, and finally the divisional surgeon.

===Chinese===
这批古旧的遗物既已看乏，我又把注意力转移到这儿的“画廊”上去，这时其余的旁听者和证人们陆续到来。我急忙在那把唯一的舒适椅子上坐了——它挨着桌子顶端那把想必为验尸官而设的椅子——我刚坐定，验尸官便由陪审团陪同进来了。随后进来的是警佐、巴杰探长、一两名便衣，最后是警区医生。

===Original===
The coroner took his seat at the head of the table and opened his book, and the jury seated themselves on a couple of benches on one side of the long table.

===Chinese===
验尸官在桌子顶端的座位上落座，翻开他的簿册；陪审员们则在长桌一侧的两条长凳上坐定。

===Original===
I looked with some interest at the twelve “good men and true.” They were a representative group of British tradesmen, quiet, attentive, and rather solemn; but my attention was particularly attracted by a small man with a very large head and a shock of upstanding hair whom I had diagnosed, after a glance at his intelligent but truculent countenance and the shiny knees of his trousers, as the village cobbler. He sat between the broad-shouldered foreman, who looked like a blacksmith, and a dogged, red-faced man whose general aspect of prosperous greasiness suggested the calling of a butcher.

===Chinese===
我饶有兴味地端详这十二位“善良而正直的人”。他们是英国市井商贩中的一班代表人物，安静、专注，而且颇为肃穆；但格外引我注意的是一个小个子男人，生着一颗极大的脑袋，一头硬发根根直立——朝他那副聪明而好斗的面容和磨得发亮的裤膝望了一眼，我便“诊断”出他是村里的鞋匠。他坐在两个人中间：一边是膀阔肩圆的陪审长，看模样像个铁匠；另一边是个执拗的红脸汉子，通身那股兴旺发亮的油腻气象，暗示出他操的是屠夫这一行。

===Original===
“The inquiry, gentlemen,” the coroner commenced, “upon which we are now entering concerns itself with two questions. The first is that of identity: who was this person whose body we have just viewed? The second is: How, when, and by what means did he come by his death? We will take the identity first and begin with the circumstances under which the body was discovered.”

===Chinese===
“诸位，”验尸官开口道，“我们此刻开始的这场审讯，涉及两个问题。第一是身份问题：我们刚才验看的这个人究竟是谁？第二是：他是怎样、何时、因何而死的？我们先从身份入手，从遗骸被发现时的情形说起。”

===Original===
Here the cobbler stood up and raised an excessively dirty hand.

===Chinese===
这时那鞋匠站起身来，举起一只脏得可以的手。

===Original===
“I rise, Mr. Chairman,” said he, “to a point of order.” The other jurymen looked at him curiously and some of them, I regret to say, grinned. “You have referred, sir,” he continued, “to the body which we have just viewed. I wish to point out that we have not viewed a body; we have viewed a collection of bones.”

===Chinese===
“主席先生，”他说，“我起身是要提出一个程序问题。”其余陪审员好奇地打量他，其中有几位——说来遗憾——咧开了嘴。“先生，”他接着说，“您方才提到我们刚才验看的那个遗体。我要指出，我们验看的并不是一具遗体；我们验看的是一批骨头。”

===Original===
“We will refer to them as the remains, if you prefer it,” said the coroner.

===Chinese===
“倘若你愿意，我们可以称它们为遗骸。”验尸官说。

===Original===
“I do prefer it,” was the reply, and the objector sat down.

===Chinese===
“我正是这个意思。”对方答道，说完便坐下了。

===Original===
“Very well,” rejoined the coroner, and he proceeded to call the witnesses, of whom the first was a laborer who had discovered the bones in the watercress-bed.

===Chinese===
“很好。”验尸官应道，随即开始传唤证人；第一个是那个在水田芥田里发现骨头的劳工。

===Original===
“Do you happen to know how long it was since the watercress-beds had been cleaned out previously?” the coroner asked, when the witness had told the story of the discovery.

===Chinese===
证人把发现的经过讲完之后，验尸官问道：“你可知道那片水田芥田上回清理干净，距今有多久了？”

===Original===
“They was cleaned out by Mr. Tapper’s orders just before he gave them up. That will be a little better than two years ago. In May it were. I helped to clean ’em. I worked on this very same place and there wasn’t no bones there then.”

===Chinese===
“那是塔珀（Tapper）先生把地退掉之前，吩咐人清理的。算来是两年前还多一点。五月里的事。我帮着清理的。我干活的正是眼下这块地，那会儿这儿可没得什么骨头。”

===Original===
The coroner glanced at the jury. “Any questions, gentlemen,” he asked.

===Chinese===
验尸官朝陪审团看了一眼。“诸位有什么问题要问吗？”他问。

===Original===
The cobbler directed an intimidating scowl at the witness and demanded:

===Chinese===
鞋匠朝证人摆出一副吓人的黑脸，质问道：

===Original===
“Were you searching for bones when you came on these remains?”

===Chinese===
“你撞见这些遗骸的时候，是不是正在找骨头？”

===Original===
“Me!” exclaimed the witness. “What should I be searching for bones for?”

===Chinese===
“我！”证人叫起来，“我找骨头干什么呀？”

===Original===
“Don’t prevaricate,” said the cobbler sternly; “answer the question: Yes or no.”

===Chinese===
“别支吾其词，”鞋匠厉声说，“回答问题：是，还是不是。”

===Original===
“No, of course I wasn’t.”

===Chinese===
“不是，我当然不是。”

===Original===
The juryman shook his enormous head dubiously as though implying that he would let it pass this time but it mustn’t happen again; and the examination of the witnesses continued, without eliciting anything that was new to me or giving rise to any incident, until the sergeant had described the finding of the right arm in the Cuckoo Pits.

===Chinese===
那位陪审员似信似疑地摇了摇他的大脑袋，那意思仿佛是说：这一回姑且容你过去，下不为例。证人的讯问便如此继续下去，既没有问出什么于我为新的东西，也没有生出任何枝节，直到警佐陈述了在杜鹃坑发现右臂的经过。

===Original===
“Was this an accidental discovery?” the coroner asked.

===Chinese===
“这一次可是偶然发现的？”验尸官问。

===Original===
“No. We had instructions from Scotland Yard to search any likely ponds in this neighborhood.”

===Chinese===
“不是。我们奉苏格兰场的指示，要把这一带所有可能藏东西的池塘都搜一遍。”

===Original===
The coroner discreetly forbore to press this matter any further, but my friend the cobbler was evidently on the qui vive, and I anticipated a brisk cross-examination for Mr. Badger when his turn came. The inspector was apparently of the same opinion, for I saw him cast a glance of the deepest malevolence at the too inquiring disciple of St. Crispin. In fact, his turn came next, and the cobbler’s hair stood up with unholy joy.

===Chinese===
验尸官识趣地住口，不再深究此事；可是我那位鞋匠朋友分明已竖起了耳朵，我预料等轮到巴杰先生作证时，一场干脆利落的盘诘在所难免。探长显然所见相同，因为我看见他朝圣克里平（St. Crispin）这位过分好问的门徒投去了怨毒至深的一瞥。果然，下一个便轮到了他，鞋匠的头发也随即带着一股邪乎的狂喜根根倒竖起来。

===Original===
The finding of the lower half of the trunk in Staple’s Pond at Loughton was the inspector’s own achievement, but he was not boastful about it. The discovery, he remarked, followed naturally on the previous one in the Cuckoo Pits.

===Chinese===
在洛顿的斯特普尔池里找到躯干的下半截，是探长本人的功绩，但他并不以此自夸。他说，这一发现是杜鹃坑前一次发现之后顺理成章的结果。

===Original===
“Had you any private information that led you to search this particular neighborhood?” the cobbler asked.

===Chinese===
“你可是得了什么私下的消息，才到这个特定地方来搜查的？”鞋匠问。

===Original===
“We had no private information whatever,” replied Badger.

===Chinese===
“我们什么私下消息也没有。”巴杰答道。

===Original===
“Now I put it to you,” pursued the juryman, shaking a forensic, and very dirty, forefinger at the inspector; “here are certain remains found at Sidcup; here are certain other remains found at St. Mary Cray, and certain others at Lee. All those places are in Kent. Now isn’t it very remarkable that you should come straight down to Epping Forest, which is in Essex, and search for those bones and find ’em?”

===Chinese===
“那我把话挑明，”陪审员追问，一面朝探长摇晃着一根深具法庭风度、也脏得可以的食指；“这儿是一些遗骸，是在锡德卡普发现的；那儿是另一些，是在圣玛丽克雷发现的，还有一些在李。这些地方全在肯特郡。而你们却径直下到埃平森林——那可是在埃塞克斯——来找这些骨头，还把它们找到了，这难道不非常蹊跷么？”

===Original===
“We were making a systematic search of all likely places,” replied Badger.

===Chinese===
“我们当时正对所有可能的地方进行系统的搜查。”巴杰答道。

===Original===
“Exactly,” said the cobbler, with a ferocious grin, “that’s just my point. I say, isn’t it very funny that, after finding the remains in Kent some twenty miles from here, with the River Thames between, you should come here to look for the bones and go straight to Staple’s Pond, where they happen to be﻿—and find ’em?”

===Chinese===
“着啊，”鞋匠咧嘴狞笑，“这正是我要说的。我问你：在肯特郡——离这儿约莫二十英里，中间还隔着一条泰晤士河——发现了那些遗骸之后，你们却赶到这儿来找骨头，而且径直奔到斯特普尔池，骨头偏偏就在那儿——还把它们找到了，你说这可笑不可笑？”

===Original===
“It would have been more funny,” Badger replied sourly, “if we’d gone straight to a place where they happened *not* to be﻿—and found them.”

===Chinese===
“要是我们径直奔到一个骨头偏偏*不*在的地方——还把它们找到了，那才更可笑呢。”巴杰酸溜溜地答道。

===Original===
A gratified snigger arose from the other eleven good men and true, and the cobbler grinned savagely; but before he could think of a suitable rejoinder the coroner interposed.

===Chinese===
其余十一位善良而正直的人发出一阵称快的窃笑，鞋匠也咧嘴狞笑；但他还没想出一句得体的回敬，验尸官便插话了。

===Original===
“The question is not very material,” he said, “and we mustn’t embarrass the police by unnecessary inquiries.”

===Chinese===
“这个问题不甚切要，”他说，“我们不该用不必要的盘问使警方为难。”

===Original===
“It’s my belief,” said the cobbler, “that he knew they were there all the time.”

===Chinese===
“依我看，”鞋匠说，“他早就知道骨头一直在那儿。”

===Original===
“The witness has stated that he had no private information,” said the coroner; and he proceeded to take the rest of the inspector’s evidence, watched closely by the critical juror.

===Chinese===
“证人已经声明，他没有任何私下的消息。”验尸官说；随后他继续听取探长证词的其余部分，那位爱挑剔的陪审员则在一旁紧盯不放。

===Original===
The account of the finding of the remains having been given in full, the police surgeon was called and sworn; the jurymen straightened their backs with an air of expectancy, and I turned over a page of my notebook.

===Chinese===
发现遗骸的经过已尽数陈述完毕，警方医生被传上来宣誓作证；陪审员们一个个挺直了腰背，露出一副有所期待的神气，我也把笔记本翻到了新的一页。

===Original===
“You have examined the bones at present lying in the mortuary and forming the subject of this inquiry?” the coroner asked.

===Chinese===
“你已经查验过那些现停放在停尸房、构成本案审讯对象的骨头了吧？”验尸官问。

===Original===
“I have.”

===Chinese===
“查验过了。”

===Original===
“Will you kindly tell us what you have observed?”

===Chinese===
“请把你观察到的情况告诉我们，好吗？”

===Original===
“I find that the bones are human bones, and are, in my opinion, all parts of the same person. They form a skeleton which is complete with the exception of the skull, the third finger of the left hand, the kneecaps, and the leg-bones﻿—I mean the bones between the knees and the ankles.”

===Chinese===
“我查明这些骨头是人的骨头，而且依我之见，都属于同一个人。它们构成一副骨架，只除了头骨、左手的第三根手指、膝盖骨和小腿骨——我是说膝盖和脚踝之间的那几根骨头。”

===Original===
“Is there anything to account for the absence of the missing finger?”

===Chinese===
“有什么迹象可以解释那根手指的缺失吗？”

===Original===
“No. There is no deformity and no sign of its having been amputated during life. In my opinion it was removed after death.”

===Chinese===
“没有。没有畸形，也没有生前截除的迹象。依我看，它是死后才被取下来的。”

===Original===
“Can you give us any description of the deceased?”

===Chinese===
“你能向我们描述一下死者的形貌吗？”

===Original===
“I should say that these are the bones of an elderly man, probably over sixty years of age, about five feet eight and a half inches in height, of rather stout build, fairly muscular, and well preserved. There are no signs of disease excepting some old-standing rheumatic gout of the right hip-joint.”

===Chinese===
“依我看，这些是一个上了年纪的男人的骨头，年纪大概在六十开外，身高约五英尺八英寸半，体格颇为壮实，肌肉相当发达，保存良好。除了右髋关节有些陈年的风湿性痛风之外，没有任何疾病的迹象。”

===Original===
“Can you form any opinion as to the cause of death?”

===Chinese===
“关于死因，你能形成什么看法吗？”

===Original===
“No. There are no marks of violence or signs of injury. But it will be impossible to form any opinion as to the cause of death until we have seen the skull.”

===Chinese===
“不能。骨头上没有暴力的痕迹，也没有损伤的迹象。但是，在没有见到头骨之前，要对死因形成任何看法都是不可能的。”

===Original===
“Did you note anything else of importance?”

===Chinese===
“你还注意到别的什么要紧之处吗？”

===Original===
“Yes. I was struck by the appearance of anatomical knowledge and skill on the part of the person who dismembered the body. The knowledge of anatomy is proved by the fact that the corpse has been divided into definite anatomical regions. For instance, the bones of the neck are complete and include the top joint of the backbone known as the atlas; whereas a person without anatomical knowledge would probably take off the head by cutting through the neck. Then the arms have been separated with the scapula (or shoulder-blade) and clavicle (or collarbone) attached, just as an arm would be removed for dissection.

===Chinese===
“注意到了。肢解尸体的人所表现的解剖学知识与技巧，令我印象深刻。解剖学知识由这样一桩事实证明：尸身是按明确的解剖部位分割开来的。譬如，颈部的骨头齐全完整，还包括脊柱最顶上的那一节，即所谓寰椎（atlas）；换了没有解剖学知识的人，多半是齐颈一刀把头砍下了事。再者，两臂是被连着肩胛骨（scapula）和锁骨（clavicle）一并分离下来的，正如解剖时摘取上肢的那种做法。

===Original===
“The skill is shown by the neat way in which the dismemberment has been carried out. The parts have not been rudely hacked asunder, but have been separated at the joints so skilfully that I have not discovered a single scratch or mark of the knife on any of the bones.”

===Chinese===
“至于技巧，则见于肢解手法的干净利落。尸身的各部分并非被粗暴地乱剁乱劈，而是在关节处一一分离，其手法之精熟，竟使我在任何一块骨头上都找不出一道刀痕或刻痕。”

===Original===
“Can you suggest any class of person who would be likely to possess the knowledge and skill to which you refer?”

===Chinese===
“你能设想出哪一类人，可能具备你所说的这种知识和技巧吗？”

===Original===
“It would, of course, be possessed by a surgeon or medical student, and possibly by a butcher.”

===Chinese===
“这种本领，外科医生或医科学生自然具备，屠夫也可能具备。”

===Original===
“You think that the person who dismembered this body may have been a surgeon or a medical student?”

===Chinese===
“那么你认为，肢解这具尸体的人，可能是个外科医生或医科学生？”

===Original===
“Yes; or a butcher. Someone accustomed to the dismemberment of bodies and skilful with the knife.”

===Chinese===
“是的；或者是个屠夫。一个惯于肢解躯体、并且使刀娴熟的人。”

===Original===
Here the cobbler suddenly rose to his feet.

===Chinese===
这时鞋匠霍地站了起来。

===Original===
“I rise, Mr. Chairman,” said he, “to protest against the statement that has just been made.”

===Chinese===
“主席先生，”他说，“我站起来，是要抗议方才所做的那项陈述。”

===Original===
“What statement?” demanded the coroner.

===Chinese===
“什么陈述？”验尸官问。

===Original===
“Against the aspersion,” continued the cobbler, with an oratorical flourish, “that has been cast upon a honorable calling.”

===Chinese===
“抗议那种诽谤，”鞋匠拿出一副演说家的腔调继续说道，“对我们一个体面行当的诽谤。”

===Original===
“I don’t understand you,” said the coroner.

===Chinese===
“我不明白你的意思。”验尸官说。

===Original===
“Doctor Summers has insinuated that this murder was committed by a butcher. Now a member of that honorable calling is sitting on this jury﻿—”

===Chinese===
“萨默斯（Summers）医生影射说，这桩凶案是一个屠夫干的。而此刻，那个体面行当的一位成员，正坐在这陪审团里——”

===Original===
“You let me alone,” growled the butcher.

===Chinese===
“你少来惹我。”屠夫咕噜道。

===Original===
“I will not let you alone,” persisted the cobbler. “I desire﻿—”

===Chinese===
“我偏要管，”鞋匠不依不饶，“我要求——”

===Original===
“Oh, shut up, Pope!” This was from the foreman, who, at the same moment, reached out an enormous hairy hand with which he grabbed the cobbler’s coattails and brought him into a sitting posture with a thump that shook the room.

===Chinese===
“咳，住嘴吧，波普（Pope）！”说话的是陪审长；与此同时他伸出一只毛茸茸的大手，一把攥住鞋匠的衣摆，咚地一声把他按坐下去，震得满屋子直颤。

===Original===
But Mr. Pope, though seated, was not silenced. “I desire,” he said, “to have my protest put on record.”

===Chinese===
可是波普先生人虽坐下，嘴却不曾闭上。“我要求，”他说，“把我的抗议记录在案。”

===Original===
“I can’t do that,” said the coroner, “and I can’t allow you to interrupt the witnesses.”

===Chinese===
“这个我办不到，”验尸官说，“我也不能容许你打断证人。”

===Original===
“I am acting,” said Mr. Pope, “in the interests of my friend here and the members of a honorable﻿—”

===Chinese===
“我这是，”波普先生说，“在维护我这位朋友的利益，还有这个体面——”

===Original===
