## VI Sidelights / 六 侧闻
===Original===
The association of coal with potatoes is one upon which I have frequently speculated, without arriving at any more satisfactory explanation than that both products are of the earth, earthy. Of the connection itself Barnard’s practise furnished several instances besides Mrs. Jablett’s establishment in Fleur-de-Lys Court, one of which was a dark and mysterious cavern a foot below the level of the street, that burrowed under an ancient house on the west side of Fetter Lane﻿—a crinkly, timber house of the three-decker type that leaned back drunkenly from the road as if about to sit down in its own back yard.

===Chinese===
煤与马铃薯的搭配，是我屡屡加以推究的一件事，却始终不曾得出比这更满意的解释：两种产品都是土里生土里长的土产。至于这种搭配本身，除了贾布利特太太在百合花院的那家铺面，巴纳德的诊业还提供了好几处实例；其中之一，是一个阴暗而神秘的地窖，比街面低下一英尺，钻在菲特巷西边一座老宅底下——那是一座皱巴巴的三层旧木楼，带着醉意从街边朝后仰去，仿佛就要一屁股坐进自家的后院里。

===Original===
Passing this repository of the associated products about ten o’clock in the morning, I perceived in the shadows of the cavern no less a person than Miss Oman. She saw me at the same moment, and beckoned peremptorily with a hand that held a large Spanish onion. I approached with a deferential smile.

===Chinese===
一天早晨十点钟光景，我打这两样联合产品的储藏所门前经过，竟在地窖的暗影里望见一位人物——不是别人，正是奥曼小姐。她也同时瞧见了我，一只握着大个儿西班牙洋葱的手，专断地朝我招了招。我堆起恭恭敬敬的笑脸迎上前去。

===Original===
“What a magnificent onion, Miss Oman! and how generous of you to offer it to me﻿—”

===Chinese===
“好一只漂亮的大洋葱，奥曼小姐！您肯把它送给我，真是太大方了——”

===Original===
“I wasn’t offering it to you. But there! Isn’t it just like a man﻿—”

===Chinese===
“谁要送你来着。可你瞧！男人不就是这个样儿么——”

===Original===
“Isn’t what just like a man?” I interrupted. “If you mean the onion﻿—”

===Chinese===
“什么就是这个样儿？”我插嘴道，“要是您说的是这只洋葱——”

===Original===
“I don’t!” she snapped; “and I wish you wouldn’t talk such a parcel of nonsense. A grown man and a member of a serious profession, too! You ought to know better.”

===Chinese===
“我没说洋葱！”她厉声道，“我也请你别再讲这一大包胡话。堂堂一个大人，还是个正经行当里的人呢！你也该懂点好歹。”

===Original===
“I suppose I ought,” I said reflectively. And she continued:

===Chinese===
“大约是该懂点好歹。”我若有所思地说。她接着道：

===Original===
“I called in at the surgery just now.”

===Chinese===
“我方才到诊所去过。”

===Original===
“To see me?”

===Chinese===
“去看我？”

===Original===
“What else should I come for? Do you suppose that I called to consult the bottle-boy?”

===Chinese===
“不看你还能去看谁？难道你以为，我是去请教那个摆药瓶的小厮的么？”

===Original===
“Certainly not, Miss Oman. So you find the lady doctor no use, after all?”

===Chinese===
“当然不是，奥曼小姐。这么说，到底还是觉得那位女大夫不中用？”

===Original===
Miss Oman gnashed her teeth at me (and very fine teeth they were too).

===Chinese===
奥曼小姐对我咬牙切齿（而且那口牙也着实漂亮）。

===Original===
“I called,” she said majestically, “on behalf of Miss Bellingham.”

===Chinese===
“我去诊所，”她端然庄严地说，“是替贝林厄姆小姐去的。”

===Original===
My facetiousness evaporated instantly. “I hope Miss Bellingham is not ill,” I said with a sudden anxiety that elicited a sardonic smile from Miss Oman.

===Chinese===
我的一脸打趣顿时烟消云散。“但愿贝林厄姆小姐没有生病，”我说；那阵突发的焦灼，惹得奥曼小姐讥诮地一笑。

===Original===
“No,” was the reply, “she is not ill, but she has cut her hand rather badly. It’s her right hand too, and she can’t afford to lose the use of it, not being a great, bulky, lazy, lolloping man. So you had better go and put some stuff on it.”

===Chinese===
“没有，”回答是，“她没生病，可她的手割破了，伤得不轻。偏偏又是右手；她又不是那种高大粗笨、懒懒散散、晃晃悠悠的大男人，这只手可闲废不起。所以你最好赶紧去给上点药。”

===Original===
With this advice, Miss Oman whisked to the right-about and vanished into the depths of the cavern like the witch of Wokey, while I hurried on to the surgery to provide myself with the necessary instruments and materials, and thence proceeded to Nevill’s Court.

===Chinese===
撂下这句忠告，奥曼小姐霍地一个向后转，像乌基（Wokey）洞里的巫婆似的，一头没入地窖深处；我则赶忙奔往诊所，给自己备齐必要的器械材料，随即取道前往内维尔巷。

===Original===
Miss Oman’s juvenile maidservant, who opened the door to me, stated the existing conditions with epigrammatic conciseness.

===Chinese===
开门的是奥曼小姐手下那个半大不小的女仆，她以格言式的简洁说明了眼下的情形。

===Original===
“Mr. Bellingham is hout, sir; but Miss Bellingham is hin.”

===Chinese===
“贝林厄姆先生出去咧，先生；不过贝林厄姆小姐在家咧。”

===Original===
Having thus delivered herself she retreated toward the kitchen and I ascended the stairs, at the head of which I found Miss Bellingham awaiting me with her right hand encased in what looked like a white boxing-glove.

===Chinese===
如此交代完毕，她便退往厨房去了；我登上楼梯，见贝林厄姆小姐正在楼梯口等我，右手裹在一个白色的什么东西里，活像一只拳击手套。

===Original===
“I’m glad you have come,” she said. “Phyllis﻿—Miss Oman, you know﻿—has kindly bound up my hand, but I should like you to see that it is all right.”

===Chinese===
“您来了我很高兴，”她说，“菲莉斯——就是奥曼小姐——好心替我把手包扎上了，不过我想请您瞧瞧，包得妥当不妥当。”

===Original===
We went into the sitting-room, where I laid out my paraphernalia on the table while I inquired into the particulars of the accident.

===Chinese===
我们走进起居室，我把随身的家什摊在桌上，一面细问出事的缘由。

===Original===
“It is most unfortunate that it should have happened just now,” she said, as I wrestled with one of those remarkable feminine knots that, while they seem to defy the utmost efforts of human ingenuity to untie, yet have a singular habit of untying themselves at inopportune moments.

===Chinese===
“偏偏赶上这个当口出事，真是再倒霉不过了，”她说；这时我正跟一个女人家打下的稀奇结子较劲——那种结子，仿佛人间的聪明才智再怎么使尽也解它不开，偏偏又会不合时宜地自己松开。

===Original===
“Why just now in particular?” I asked.

===Chinese===
“为什么偏偏是这个当口？”我问。

===Original===
“Because I have some specially important work to do. A very learned lady who is writing an historical book has commissioned me to collect all the literature relating to the Tell-el-Amarna letters﻿—the cuneiform tablets, you know, of Amenhotep the Fourth.”

===Chinese===
“因为我有一件特别要紧的工作要做。一位正在著一部史书的非常有学问的女士，委托我把有关泰尔-埃尔-阿马尔纳（Tell-el-Amarna）书信的全部文献搜集起来——您知道，就是阿蒙霍特普四世的那批楔形文字泥板。”

===Original===
“Well,” I said soothingly, “I expect your hand will soon be well.”

===Chinese===
“唔，”我宽慰道，“我看您的手很快就会好的。”

===Original===
“Yes, but that won’t do. The work has to be done immediately. I have to send in completed notes not later than this day week, and it will be quite impossible. I am dreadfully disappointed.”

===Chinese===
“是会好的，可那不顶事。这工作得立刻就做。我最迟下星期的今天就得交出完整的笔记，那可就完全办不到了。我失望得不得了。”

===Original===
By this time I had unwound the voluminous wrappings and exposed the injury﻿—a deep gash in the palm that must have narrowly missed a good-sized artery. Obviously the hand would be useless for fully a week.

===Chinese===
这时我已解开那层层叠叠的裹伤布，露出了伤处——掌心里一道深口子，差一丝儿就要伤着一条不小的动脉。显而易见，这只手足足一个星期使唤不得。

===Original===
“I suppose,” she said, “you couldn’t patch it up so that I could write with it?”

===Chinese===
“您看，”她说，“能不能把它拾掇拾掇，好歹让我还能拿它写字？”

===Original===
I shook my head.

===Chinese===
我摇了摇头。

===Original===
“No, Miss Bellingham. I shall have to put it on a splint. We can’t run any risks with a deep wound like this.”

===Chinese===
“不能，贝林厄姆小姐。我得给它上夹板。这样深的伤口，我们可冒不起半点风险。”

===Original===
“Then I shall have to give up the commission, and I don’t know how my client will get the work done in time. You see, I am pretty well up in the literature of Ancient Egypt; in fact, I was to receive special payment on that account. And it would have been such an interesting task, too. However, it can’t be helped.”

===Chinese===
“那我就只好辞掉这桩委托了，也不知道我的主顾怎么来得及把这活儿赶出来。您知道，古埃及的文献我相当熟；老实说，人家也正是为这个才特别加钱的。再说，这本会是一桩多么有趣的工作啊。可是，没法子。”

===Original===
I proceeded methodically with the application of the dressings, and meanwhile reflected. It was evident that she was deeply disappointed. Loss of work meant loss of money, and it needed but a glance at her rusty black dress to see that there was little margin for that. Possibly, too, there was some special need to be met. Her manner seemed almost to imply that there was. And at this point I had a brilliant idea.

===Chinese===
我有条不紊地继续敷药包扎，一面暗自思量。她显然失望得很。误了工作便是误了钱，只消朝她那身褪色的黑衣裙望上一眼，便看得出这里面没有多少余地。说不定还有一笔什么特别的用项等着应付呢。她的神气几乎就暗示着有。恰恰在这当口，我灵机一动。

===Original===
“I’m not sure that it can’t be helped,” said I.

===Chinese===
“也未必就没法子。”我说。

===Original===
She looked at me inquiringly, and I continued: “I am going to make a proposition, and I shall ask you to consider it with an open mind.”

===Chinese===
她带着询问的神色望着我，我便接着说：“我要提一个建议，并且要请您敞开来考虑。”

===Original===
“That sounds rather portentous,” said she; “but I promise. What is it?”

===Chinese===
“这话听着怪郑重其事的，”她说，“不过我答应。什么建议？”

===Original===
“It is this: When I was a student I acquired the useful art of writing shorthand. I am not a lightning reporter, you understand, but I can take matter down from dictation at quite respectable speed.”

===Chinese===
“是这样：我做学生的时候，学会了一门有用的本事——速记。您明白，我不是什么闪电式的访员，不过口授给我、由我记下来，速度还相当过得去。”

===Original===
“Yes.”

===Chinese===
“嗯。”

===Original===
“Well, I have several hours free every day﻿—usually the whole afternoon up to six or half-past﻿—and it occurs to me that if you were to go to the Museum in the mornings you could get out your books, look up passages (you could do that without using your right hand), and put in bookmarks. Then I could come along in the afternoon and you could read out the selected passages to me, and I could take them down in shorthand. We should get through as much in a couple of hours as you could in a day using longhand.”

===Chinese===
“是这样：我每天有几个钟头的空——通常整个下午到六点或六点半都闲着——我想，您若是上午去博物馆，把书提出来，把段落查好（这不用右手也办得了），再夹上书签；然后我下午过来，您把选定的段落念给我听，我用速记录下来。两个钟头里我们能赶完的，抵得上您用寻常写法写一整天。”

===Original===
“Oh, but how kind of you, Dr. Berkeley!” she exclaimed. “How very kind! Of course, I couldn’t think of taking up all your leisure in that way; but I do appreciate your kindness very much.”

===Chinese===
“哦，您心肠真好，伯克利大夫！”她高声道，“真是太好心了！当然，我怎么也不能让您把闲暇全这么搭进来；不过您的好意，我是万分感激的。”

===Original===
I was rather chapfallen at this very definite refusal, but persisted feebly:

===Chinese===
这般斩钉截铁的谢绝叫我相当垂头丧气，但我还是软软地坚持了一句：

===Original===
“I wish you would. It may seem rather a cheek for a comparative stranger like me to make such a proposal to a lady; but if you’d been a man﻿—in those special circumstances﻿—I should have made it all the same, and you would have accepted as a matter of course.”

===Chinese===
“我但愿您肯。像我这样一个沾亲不带故的人，向一位小姐提出这样的建议，也许显得冒失；可您要是个男人——处在这等特别的境地——我照样会提，而您也会理所当然地接受。”

===Original===
“I doubt that. At any rate, I am not a man. I sometimes wish I were.”

===Chinese===
“那我可怀疑。无论如何，我不是个男人。不过我有时倒希望自己是个男人。”

===Original===
“Oh, I am sure you are much better as you are!” I exclaimed, with such earnestness that we both laughed. And at this moment Mr. Bellingham entered the room carrying several large brand-new books in a strap.

===Chinese===
“哦，我敢说您照现在的样子好得多！”我喊道，那份认真劲儿惹得我们俩都笑了。正在这时，贝林厄姆先生走进屋来，用皮带捆着几部崭新的大书。

===Original===
“Well, I’m sure!” he exclaimed genially; “here are pretty goings on. Doctor and patient giggling like a pair of schoolgirls! What’s the joke?”

===Chinese===
“嚯，真是的！”他和和气气地嚷道，“这儿可有意思了。医生和病人，像一对女学生似的咯咯直笑！有什么乐子呀？”

===Original===
He thumped his parcel of books down on the table and listened smilingly while my unconscious witticism was expounded.

===Chinese===
他把那捆书咚地一声撂在桌上，笑吟吟地听着人讲解我那句无心的妙语。

===Original===
“The doctor’s quite right,” he said. “You’ll do as you are, chick; but the Lord knows what sort of man you would make. You take his advice and let well alone.”

===Chinese===
“医生说得全然不错，”他说，“你照现在的样子就很好，小丫头；可你要做了男人会是个什么款儿，只有天晓得。听他的劝，安于现状、莫再折腾吧。”

===Original===
Finding him in this genial frame of mind, I ventured to explain my proposition to him and to enlist his support. He considered it with attentive approval, and when I had finished turned to his daughter.

===Chinese===
见他有这般好兴致，我便大着胆子把我的建议对他讲了，想争取他的支持。他专心一意、连连称许地听完了，便转向他的女儿。

===Original===
“What is your objection, chick?” he asked.

===Chinese===
“你有什么不乐意的，小丫头？”他问。

===Original===
“It would give Doctor Berkeley such a fearful lot of work,” she answered.

===Chinese===
“这要给伯克利大夫添好大好大一堆活儿。”她答道。

===Original===
“It would give him a fearful lot of pleasure,” I said. “It would really.”

===Chinese===
“这也要给他添好大好大一堆乐子，”我说，“真的。”

===Original===
“Then why not?” said Mr. Bellingham. “We don’t mind being under an obligation to the Doctor, do we?”

===Chinese===
“那还有什么说的？”贝林厄姆先生说，“受医生一个情，咱们不介意吧？”

===Original===
“Oh, it isn’t that!” she exclaimed hastily.

===Chinese===
“哦，不是那个意思！”她急忙叫道。

===Original===
“Then take him at his word. He means it. It is a kind action and he’ll like doing it, I’m sure. That’s all right, Doctor; she accepts, don’t you, chick?”

===Chinese===
“那你就信他的话。他是当真的。这是一桩好心的举动，他准乐意做，我敢保。这就妥了，医生；她答应了——你答应不答应，小丫头？”

===Original===
“Yes, if you say so, I do; and most thankfully.”

===Chinese===
“是，既然你这么说，我就答应；而且是万分感激地答应。”

===Original===
She accompanied the acceptance with a gracious smile that was in itself a large repayment on account, and when we had made the necessary arrangements, I hurried away in a state of the most perfect satisfaction to finish my morning’s work and order an early lunch.

===Chinese===
她允诺时伴以一个温雅的微笑，那微笑本身就算得一笔不小的预付酬金。必要的安排逐一讲定之后，我便怀着万分圆满的心境匆匆离去，赶完上午的事务，又吩咐提早开午饭。

===Original===
When I called for her a couple of hours later I found her waiting in the garden with the shabby handbag, of which I relieved her, and we set forth together, watched jealously by Miss Oman, who had accompanied her to the gate.

===Chinese===
两个钟头后我来接她，见她已在花园里等候，手里拎着那只寒碜的手提包——我把它接了过来；我们便一同出发，奥曼小姐把她送到大门口，一路妒意森森地盯着我们。

===Original===
As I walked up the court with this wonderful maid by my side I could hardly believe in my good fortune. By her presence and my own resulting happiness the mean surroundings became glorified and the commonest objects transfigured into things of beauty. What a delightful thoroughfare, for instance, was Fetter Lane, with its quaint charm and medieval grace! I snuffed the cabbage-laden atmosphere and seemed to breathe the scent of the asphodel. Holborn was even as the Elysian Fields; the omnibus that bore us westward was a chariot of glory; and the people who swarmed verminously on the pavements bore the semblance of the children of light.

===Chinese===
同这位奇妙的小姐并肩走上巷子时，我几乎不敢相信自己的好运。有她在身旁，加上我自己随之而来的幸福，鄙陋的环境全都变得辉煌，最寻常的物件也化成了美的东西。譬如菲特巷，多么可喜的一条通衢，带着它古雅的情致和中世纪的风韵！我吸着满载白菜味的空气，却仿佛吸到了日光兰（asphodel）的芬芳。霍尔本（Holborn）直如极乐野（Elysian Fields）一般；载着我们西去的公共马车是一辆荣耀的战车；人行道上虫豸般糜集的人们，也都俨然光明之子的模样。

===Original===
Love is a foolish thing judged by workaday standards, and the thoughts and actions of lovers foolish beyond measure. But the workaday standard is the wrong one, after all; for the utilitarian mind does but busy itself with the trivial and transitory interests of life, behind which looms the great and everlasting reality of the love of man and woman. There is more significance in a nightingale’s song in the hush of a summer night than in all the wisdom of Solomon (who, by the way, was not without his little experiences of the tender passion).

===Chinese===
拿日常营生的标准来衡量，爱情是一件蠢东西，恋人的心思和举动更蠢得无以复加。可是日常的标准终究是要不得的；因为功利之心所忙碌的，不过是生活中琐屑而短暂的利害，而在这些利害背后，隐然耸立着男女之爱这一伟大而永恒的真实。夏夜岑寂中的一声夜莺啼啭，其意味胜过所罗门的全部智慧（附带说一句，所罗门在柔情蜜意这件事上，也并非没有他自己的一点小阅历）。

===Original===
The janitor in the little glass box by the entrance to the library inspected us and passed us on, with a silent benediction, to the lobby, whence (when I had handed my stick to a bald-headed demigod and received a talismanic disc in exchange) we entered the enormous rotunda of the reading-room.

===Chinese===
图书馆入口处小玻璃匣子里的门房把我们打量了一番，默默祝福着放我们进入前厅；再往前（等我把手杖交给一位秃头的半神、换回一枚符咒似的圆牌之后），我们便走进了阅览室那巨大的圆厅。

===Original===
I have often thought that, if some lethal vapor of highly preservative properties﻿—such as formaldehyde, for instance﻿—could be shed into the atmosphere of this apartment, the entire and complete collection of books and bookworms would be well worth preserving, for the enlightenment of posterity, as a sort of anthropological appendix to the main collection of the Museum. For, surely, nowhere else in the world are so many strange and abnormal human beings gathered together in one place. And a curious question that must have occurred to many observers is: Whence do these singular creatures come, and whither do they go when the very distinct-faced clock (adjusted to literary eyesight) proclaims closing time? The tragic-faced gentleman, for instance, with the corkscrew ringlets that bob up and down like spiral springs as he walks? Or the short, elderly gentleman in the black cassock and bowler hat, who shatters your nerves by turning suddenly and revealing himself as a middle-aged woman? Whither do they go? One never sees them elsewhere. Do they steal away at closing time into the depths of the Museum and hide themselves until morning in sarcophagi or mummy cases? Or do they creep through spaces in the bookshelves and spend the night behind the volumes in a congenial atmosphere of leather and antique paper? Who can say?
