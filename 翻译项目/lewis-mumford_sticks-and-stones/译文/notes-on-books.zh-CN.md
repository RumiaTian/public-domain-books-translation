## Notes on Books / 书目注

## I Historical Background / 一 历史背景

===Original===
The best introductions to the historic setting of our architecture and civilization are the local guidebooks and histories. See, for example, Stokes’s excellent and exhaustive *Iconography of Manhattan*, and the *Memorial History of Boston*, edited by Justin Winsor. Both are profusely illustrated. In the wave of civic enthusiasm that swept over the country in the ’nineties, many local descriptions and histories were written. For the most part, they are loose, rambling, credulous, and devoid of sociological insight: but occasionally there is a nugget in the matrix. Powell’s *Historic Towns* series covers broad ground. As regional histories, Weeden’s *Economic and Social History of New England*, and Mr. Samuel Eliot Morison’s *Maritime History of Massachusetts*, stand in a class by themselves: in them we have the beginnings of what W. H. Riehl called a “natural history” of the human community.
===Chinese===
要了解我们建筑与文明的历史背景，最好的入门读物是地方指南与地方史。例如可参看斯托克斯（Stokes）那部出色而详备的《曼哈顿图像志》（Iconography of Manhattan），以及贾斯廷·温莎（Justin Winsor）主编的《波士顿纪念史》（Memorial History of Boston）。两书都有丰富的插图。在十九世纪九十年代席卷全国的市民热忱浪潮中，人们写下了许多地方记述与地方史。它们大多写得松散、枝蔓、轻信，缺乏社会学的洞见：但母岩之中偶尔也藏有一块金子。鲍威尔（Powell）的《历史名镇》（Historic Towns）丛书涵盖的地域很广。作为地区史，威登（Weeden）的《新英格兰经济与社会史》（Economic and Social History of New England）与塞缪尔·艾略特·莫里森（Samuel Eliot Morison）先生的《马萨诸塞海事史》（Maritime History of Massachusetts）独树一帜：在它们里面，我们看到了 W. H. 里尔（W. H. Riehl）所谓的人类共同体「自然史」的发端。

## II Architectural History / 二 建筑史

===Original===
Ever since colonial architecture was reappreciated after the Civil War, a large amount of material has appeared on the early architecture of the colonies. Before 1900 the greater part of this was uncritical. Isham and Brown’s work on the early architecture of Connecticut and Rhode Island made a new departure, which Messrs. Cousins and Riley’s studies of the architecture of Salem and Philadelphia have carried on. Mr. Fiske Kimball’s compendious study of the *Domestic Architecture of the Colonies and the Early Republic* brings together a large amount of authenticated data. Articles and illustrations dealing with particular aspects of our pre-industrial architecture, or with particular regions﻿—like the Lebanon Valley in Pennsylvania﻿—are scattered through the architectural periodicals. Beyond the early republican period, our architectural histories come to an end. Works like John Bullock’s *The American Cottage Builder*, New York: 1854, occur in almost every old library and are full of interesting data. To fill the gap in later years we must have recourse to a comprehensive German treatise, *Das Amerikanische Haus*, by F. R. Vogel, Berlin: 1910. This may be supplemented by *Homes in City and Country*, by Russell Sturgis, J. W. Root and others, New York: 1893.
===Chinese===
自南北战争后殖民时期建筑重新得到赏识以来，关于各殖民地早期建筑的材料已出了不少。1900 年以前，这类材料的大部分都缺乏批判的眼光。艾沙姆（Isham）与布朗（Brown）关于康涅狄格与罗得岛早期建筑的著作开了新的风气，卡曾斯（Cousins）与赖利（Riley）两位先生对塞勒姆与费城建筑的研究又将它延续下去。菲斯克·金博尔（Fiske Kimball）先生那部详备的《殖民地与早期共和国的住宅建筑》（Domestic Architecture of the Colonies and the Early Republic）汇集了大量经过考订的资料。凡论述我们前工业时代建筑的某些特定方面、或特定地区——比如宾夕法尼亚的黎巴嫩谷（Lebanon Valley）——的文章与图版，则散见于各种建筑期刊。越过早期共和国时期，我们的建筑史便告终结。像约翰·布洛克（John Bullock）的《美国村舍营造者》（The American Cottage Builder），纽约：1854 一类的著作，几乎每座老图书馆里都找得到，而且满是饶有趣味的资料。要填补更晚年代的空缺，我们必须求助于 F. R. 福格尔（F. R. Vogel）的一部综合性德文论著《美国住宅》（Das Amerikanische Haus），柏林：1910。此书可由拉塞尔·斯特吉斯（Russell Sturgis）、J. W. 鲁特（J. W. Root）等人合著的《城市与乡村的住宅》（Homes in City and Country），纽约：1893 加以补充。

## III Biographical Studies / 三 传记研究

*Samuel McIntire: His Life and Work*. F. Cousins and P. M. Riley, Boston: 1916.
*The Life and Letters of Charles Bulfinch*. Ellen Susan Bulfinch, New York: 1896.
*The Journal of Latrobe*. Benjamin Henry Latrobe, New York: 1905.

## IV Contemporary Work / 四 当代作品

===Original===
Portfolios of work by contemporary architects are so numerous that to single out any would be invidious. The files of the *Architectural Record*, the *American Architect*, *House and Garden*, and *Arts and Decorations*﻿—to mention only the more available periodicals﻿—should be consulted particularly for illustrations.
===Chinese===
当代建筑师的作品图集多不胜数，单单挑出任何一种都难免厚此薄彼。《建筑实录》（Architectural Record）、《美国建筑师》（American Architect）、《住宅与花园》（House and Garden）与《艺术与装饰》（Arts and Decorations）——这里只举几种较易到手的期刊——的历年存档，尤应查阅其中的图版。

## V Esthetics / 五 美学

===Original===
As an introduction to architecture in general the formal textbooks are occasionally useful. Let me commend particularly, however, Viollet-le-Duc’s *The Habitations of Man in All Ages*. The archaeology and ethnology of this work are, it goes without saying, outmoded: but for all that it has a permanent interest, and it is high time that someone took up Viollet-le-Duc’s theme and redeveloped it in the light of contemporary research. While I am restoring a classic, let me add another: Ruskin’s *The Seven Lamps of Architecture*. Ruskin is disregarded nowadays, as he was in his own generation, by people who have not yet caught up with him. His insight and unflinching intelligence are both needed, however, and it is no longer necessary to warn the student against his quirks and solecisms. Ruskin wrote the apology for modernism in art when he said: “There would be hope if we could change palsy for puerility,” and he anticipated modern decoration when he said: “I believe the only manner of rich ornament that is open to us is geometrical color mosaic, and that much might result from strenuously taking up that mode of design.” For that matter, Ruskin even predicted the architectural use of steel frames. *The Seven Lamps of Architecture* closes on a prophetic word which means far more to us today than to Ruskin’s contemporaries. “I could smile,” he said, “when I hear the hopeful exultation of many, at the new reach of worldly science and vigor of worldly effort; as if we were again at the beginning of new days. There is thunder on the horizon, as well as dawn.” We who have seen the lightning strike may well reread these words.﻿ ﻿…
As for modern books on architecture and esthetics, let me recommend a handful. Among them note W. R. Lethaby’s *Form in Civilization*. In sharp contrast to Professor Lethaby is Geoffrey Scott’s *The Architecture of Humanism*, Boston: 1914. I do not accept Mr. Scott’s main position; but there is something to be said for it, and he says it well. Both points of view are embraced in the distinction Mr. Claude Bragdon makes between the Organic and the Arranged, in one of *Six Lectures on Architecture*. From a limited field, Rhys Carpenter’s *Esthetic Basis of Greek Art* reaches conclusions which illuminate almost every province of esthetics. There is an able exposition of the absolutist, mechanical point of view in Vers *Une Architecture*, by the architect whose pen-name is “Le Corbusier-Saugnier.” In *Speculations*, Mr. T. E. Hulme presents an interesting philosophic apology for mechanism.
===Chinese===
作为对建筑总体情形的入门，正规教科书偶尔有用。不过我要特别推荐维奥莱-勒-杜克（Viollet-le-Duc）的《历代人类居所》（The Habitations of Man in All Ages）。这部书的考古学与民族学内容，不言而喻，已经过时：但尽管如此，它仍有经久不衰的价值，而且早该有人接过维奥莱-勒-杜克的题目，依据当代研究把它重新做一番了。既然我在为经典恢复名誉，那就再添一部：罗斯金（Ruskin）的《建筑的七盏灯》（The Seven Lamps of Architecture）。罗斯金如今受到冷落，正如在他自己的时代一样，而冷落他的，是那些尚未赶上他的人。然而，他的洞见和他那决不含糊的智性都是我们需要的，而且如今已无须再提醒学生提防他的怪癖与文病。罗斯金说「如果我们能把麻痹换成稚气，便还有希望」，就此写下了艺术中现代主义的辩词；他说「我相信，向我们敞开的唯一一种富丽装饰之道是几何彩色镶嵌，而认真致力于这种设计方式，当大有可为」，就此预示了现代装饰。就此而论，罗斯金甚至预言过钢框架在建筑上的用途。《建筑的七盏灯》以一句预言作结，这句话在今天对我们意味得远比对罗斯金的同代人深长。他说：「听到许多人因世俗科学的新疆域、世俗努力的劲头而发出满怀希望的欢呼，仿佛我们又站在新岁月的开端，我不禁莞尔。地平线上不只是黎明，还有雷声。」我们这些亲眼见过闪电劈落的人，实在应当把这些话重读一遍。……
至于建筑与美学方面的现代书籍，容我推荐寥寥数种。其中可注意 W. R. 莱塔比（W. R. Lethaby）的《文明中的形式》（Form in Civilization）。与莱塔比教授恰成鲜明对照的，是杰弗里·斯科特（Geoffrey Scott）的《人文主义的建筑》（The Architecture of Humanism），波士顿：1914。我不接受斯科特先生的基本立场；但它也并非全无道理，何况他说得漂亮。克劳德·布雷格登（Claude Bragdon）先生在《建筑六讲》（Six Lectures on Architecture）的一讲里对「有机的」与「安排的」所作的区分，则把这两种观点一并囊括。里斯·卡彭特（Rhys Carpenter）的《希腊艺术的审美基础》（Esthetic Basis of Greek Art）取材虽出自一个有限的领域，得出的结论却照亮了美学的几乎每一个园地。《走向新建筑》（Vers Une Architecture）对那种绝对主义的、机械论的观点作了一番出色的阐述，其作者是一位笔名为「勒·柯布西耶-索涅（Le Corbusier-Saugnier）」的建筑师。在《思辨集》（Speculations）中，T. E. 休姆（T. E. Hulme）先生为机械论提供了一份有趣的哲学辩护。

## VI Sociology / 六 社会学

===Original===
For the civic and sociological background of this study, consult Professor Patrick Geddes’s *Cities in Evolution*, London: 1915, likewise his *Principles of Sociology in Relation to Economics*. The latter can be obtained through Le Play House, 65 Belgrave Road, London, S. W. 1. The chapter on Westminster, by Mr. Victor Branford, in *Our Social Inheritance*, London: 1919, is a unique introduction to the direct study of social institutions and their architectural forms. The other volumes in *The Making of the Future* series, edited by Messrs. Geddes and Branford, should also have an important place on the student’s shelf.
Light on our more immediate problems will be found in the files of the *Journal of the American Institute of Architects*. Note particularly Mr. F. L. Ackermann’s article on “Craftsmen﻿—Machines﻿—Speed﻿—Credit,” June, 1923, and Mr. Benton Mackaye’s article on the proposed Appalachian Trail. See, also, the Power number of the *Survey Graphic*. The report of the Committee on Community Planning of the American Institute of Architects (1924) should be read in connection with the last chapter: it treats in detail the difficulties that the architect confronts under our present economic and social order. See, likewise, Mr. Ebenezer Howard’s classic *Garden Cities of Tomorrow*.
===Chinese===
关于本研究的市政与社会学背景，可参看帕特里克·格迪斯（Patrick Geddes）教授的《进化中的城市》（Cities in Evolution），伦敦：1915，以及他的《与经济学相关联的社会学原理》（Principles of Sociology in Relation to Economics）。后一书可向勒普莱学社（Le Play House）购得，地址为伦敦贝尔格雷夫路 65 号（65 Belgrave Road, London, S. W. 1）。维克托·布兰福德（Victor Branford）先生在《我们的社会遗产》（Our Social Inheritance，伦敦：1919）中所写的论威斯敏斯特的一章，是对社会制度及其建筑形态作直接研究的独一无二的入门向导。由格迪斯与布兰福德两位先生主编的《未来的缔造》（The Making of the Future）丛书的其他各卷，也应在学生的书架上占有一个重要位置。
至于我们更切近的问题，可在《美国建筑师学会会刊》（Journal of the American Institute of Architects）的存档中觅得光亮。要特别留意 F. L. 阿克曼（F. L. Ackermann）先生 1923 年 6 月论「工匠——机器——速度——信贷」的文章，以及本顿·麦凯（Benton Mackaye）先生论拟议中的阿巴拉契亚山道（Appalachian Trail）的文章。另可参看《调查画报》（Survey Graphic）的「动力」专号。美国建筑师学会社区规划委员会的报告（1924）应与最后一章对照阅读：它详细讨论了建筑师在我们当前经济与社会秩序之下所面临的种种困难。同样，还请参看埃比尼泽·霍华德（Ebenezer Howard）先生的经典《明日的田园城市》（Garden Cities of Tomorrow）。
