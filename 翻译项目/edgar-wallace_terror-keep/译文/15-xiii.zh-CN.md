## XIII / 十三

===Original===
At the last moment the bank authorities had changed their minds, and overnight had sent £53,000 worth of gold for conveyance to the ship. They had borrowed for the purpose an army lorry from Woolwich, a service which is sometimes claimed by the national banking institution.
The lorry had been accompanied by eight detectives, the military driver also being armed. Tilbury was reached at half-past eleven o’clock at night, and the lorry, a high-powered Lassavar, had returned to London at two o’clock in the morning. It had been again loaded in the bank courtyard under the eyes of the officer, sergeant, and two men of the guard that is on duty on the bank premises from sunset to sunrise. A new detachment of picked men from Scotland Yard, each carrying an automatic pistol, loaded the lorry for its second journey, the amount of gold this time being £73,000 worth. After the boxes had been put into the van, they had climbed up, and the lorry had driven away from the bank. Each of the eight men guarding this treasure was passed under review by a high officer of Scotland Yard who knew everyone personally. The lorry was seen in Commercial Road by a detective inspector of the division, and its progress was noted also by a police cyclist patrol who was on duty at the junction of Ripple and Barking roads.
The main Tilbury road runs within a few hundred yards of the village of Rainham, and it was at this point, only a few miles distant from Tilbury, that the lorry disappeared. Two motorcyclist policemen, who had gone out to meet the gold convoy and who had received a telephone message from the Ripple road to say that it had passed, grew uneasy and telephoned to Tilbury.

到了最后关头，银行当局改变了主意，连夜调运了价值 53,000 英镑的黄金，送往船上。为此，他们从伍利奇（Woolwich）借来一辆军用卡车——向军队借车这项服务，那家国家银行机构有时也是要享用的。
随行的有八名侦探，开车的军用司机也带了枪。夜里十一点半抵达蒂尔伯里（Tilbury），凌晨两点，这辆大功率的拉萨瓦尔（Lassavar）卡车回到伦敦。卡车在银行院子里再度装货，自日落至日出在行内值守的卫队——一名军官、一名中士加两名士兵——全程监视。苏格兰场（Scotland Yard）新派出一队精选人员，人人携带自动手枪，为卡车的第二趟行程装货，这一次装载的黄金价值 73,000 英镑。钱箱装进车斗之后，众人爬上车，卡车驶离银行。守卫这批财宝的八个人，个个都由苏格兰场一位对他们人人识得的高级官员当面查验过。本区的一名探长在商业路（Commercial Road）上看见了这辆卡车，在里普尔路（Ripple Road）与巴金路（Barking Road）交叉口执勤的一名自行车巡警也记下了它的行踪。
蒂尔伯里干道从雷纳姆村（Rainham）几百码外经过，正是在这里——离蒂尔伯里不过几英里——卡车失踪了。两名骑摩托车的警察原是出迎黄金车队的，又接到里普尔路打来的电话，说车队已经过去，久候不至，心下不安，便打电话到蒂尔伯里询问。

===Original===
It was an airless morning, with occasional banks of mist lying in the hollows, and part of the road, especially near the river, was covered with patches of white fog, which dispersed about eight o’clock in the morning under a southeasterly wind. The mist had almost disappeared when the search party from Tilbury pursued their investigations and came upon evidence of the tragedy which the morning was to reveal. This was an old Ford motor car that had evidently run from the read, miraculously missed a telegraph pole, and ditched itself. The machine had not overturned; there were no visible marks of injury; yet the man who sat at the wheel was stone dead when he was found. An immediate medical examination failed to discover an injury of any kind to the man, who was a small farmer of Rainham, and on the face of it it looked as though he had died of a heart attack whilst on his way to town.
Just beyond the place where he was found, the road dips steeply between high banks. It is known as Coles Hollow, and at its deepest part the cutting is crossed by a single-track bridge, which connects two portions of the farm through which the road runs. The dead farmer and his car had been removed when Reeder and Inspector Simpson of Scotland Yard, who had been put in charge of the case, arrived on the spot. No news of any kind had been received of the lorry; but the local police, who had been following its tracks, had made two discoveries. Apparently, in going through the cutting, the lorry had run almost head-on into the wall of the bank on the right, for there was a deep scoop in the clayey soil which the impact had hollowed out.
“It almost appears,” said Simpson, “that the lorry swerved here to avoid the farmer’s car. There are his wheel tracks, and you notice they were wobbling from side to side. Probably the man was already dying.”

===Chinese===
那是个无风的早晨，洼地里时不时横着几团雾，部分路段——尤以近河处为甚——覆着一层层的白雾，到早晨八点上下，才被东南风吹散。蒂尔伯里来的搜索队循迹勘察时，雾已将近散尽，他们撞见了这个清晨即将揭开的惨剧的迹象。那是一辆老旧的福特汽车，显然是冲出了路面，奇迹般地躲过一根电线杆，栽进了沟里。车身没有翻覆，也看不见任何撞伤的痕迹，然而坐在方向盘后的男人被发现时已经死透了。随即进行的医学检查，在这名雷纳姆的小农身上找不出半点伤处，表面看来，他像是进城途中心脏病发作猝死的。
就在发现他的地点再往前一点，道路在高峻的路坡之间陡然下陷。此地名叫科尔斯洼（Coles Hollow），在最深处，一道路堑被一座单轨桥横跨，桥把道路所穿过的农场两部分连在一起。死者和他的车已被移走，这时里德先生和奉命主办此案的苏格兰场辛普森督察（Inspector Simpson）赶到了现场。卡车方面没有任何消息；但一路循着车辙跟踪的地方警察有了两项发现。看样子，卡车在穿过路堑时几乎迎头撞上了右边的坡壁，因为黏土里有一个被撞击掏空的深坑。
“看情形很像是，”辛普森说，“卡车在这里打了个急转，避开农夫的车。那儿有他的车辙，您也注意到了，辙印左歪右晃。多半这个人当时已经不行了。”

===Original===
“Have you traced the lorry tracks from here?” asked Reeder.
Simpson nodded and called a sergeant of the Essex Constabulary, who had charted the tracks.
“They seem to have turned up north toward Becontree,” he said. “As a matter of fact, a policeman at Becontree said he saw a large lorry come out of the mist and pass him, but that had a tilt on it and was going toward London. It was an army lorry, too, and was driven by a soldier.”
Mr. Reeder had lit a cigarette and was holding the flaming match in his hand, staring at it solemnly.

===Chinese===
“从这儿往后的卡车车辙，你们循迹查过了吗？”里德问。
辛普森点点头，唤来一名埃塞克斯郡警察队（Essex Constabulary）的中士，车辙就是他测绘的。
“车辙像是朝北折向贝孔特里（Becontree）去了。”他说，“实际上，贝孔特里的一名警察说，他看见一辆大卡车从雾里钻出来，从他身边驶过，不过那车带着篷，是往伦敦去的。也是辆军用卡车，开车的是个士兵。”
里德先生点了一支烟，火柴还燃在手里，他神情肃然地盯着它。

===Original===
“Dear me!” he said, and dropped the match and noticed that its flame was soon extinguished.
And then he began what seemed to be a foolish search of the ground, striking match after match.
“Isn’t there light enough for you, Mr. Reeder?” asked Simpson irritably.
The detective straightened his back and smiled. Only for a second was he amused, and then his long face went longer than ever.

===Chinese===
“哎呀！”他说着，丢下火柴，注意到火苗很快就灭了。
接着，他开始了一番看似愚蠢的地面搜索，一根接一根地划火柴。
“您是嫌光线还不够亮吗，里德先生？”辛普森不耐烦地问。
这位侦探直起腰来，笑了笑。那点兴味只维持了一秒钟，随即他的长脸拉得比先前更长了。

===Original===
“Poor fellow!” he said softly. “Poor fellow!”
“Whom are you talking about?” demanded Simpson, but Mr. Reeder did not reply. Instead, he pointed up to the bridge in the centre of which was an old and rusted water wagon, the type which certain English municipalities still use. He climbed up to the bank and examined the iron tank, opened the hatches and groped inside, lighting matches to aid his examination.
“Is it empty?” asked Simpson.
“I am afraid it is,” said Mr. Reeder, and inspected the worn hose leading from its iron spindles. He descended the cutting more melancholy than ever.

===Chinese===
“可怜的人！”他轻声说，“可怜的人！”

“是空的吗？”辛普森问。
“恐怕是空的。”里德先生说，又查看了从铁轴上引出的那条磨旧的水龙软管。他步下路堑，神色比先前愈发郁郁。

===Original===
Mr. Reeder nodded.
“Nevertheless, this is where the convoy was attacked and overcome,” he said. “I think you ought to look for the lorry with the tilt, and get on to your Becontree man and get a closer description of the machine he saw.”
In a quarter of an hour the police car brought them to the little Essex village, and the policeman who had seen the wagon was interviewed. It happened a few minutes before he went off duty, he said. There was a thick mist at the time, and he heard the rumble of the lorry wheels before it came into sight. He described it as a typical army wagon. So far as he could tell it was gray, and had a black tilt with “W.D.” and a broad arrow painted on the side, “W.D.” standing for War Department, the broad arrow being the sign of the Government. He saw one soldier driving and another sitting by his side. The back of the tilt was laced up and he could not see into the interior. The soldier, as he passed, had waved his hand in greeting, and the policeman had thought no more about the matter, until the robbery of the gold convoy was reported.
“Yes, sir,” he said, in answer to Reeder’s inquiry, “I think it was loaded. It went very heavily on the road. We often get these lorries coming up from Shoeburyness.”

Either that or one similar was seen entering the Blackwall Tunnel, but the Greenwich police, on the south side of the river, had failed to identify it, and from there on all trace of the lorry was lost.

“Then where are the men?” asked Simpson.

It was Scotland Yard, in the presence of an incredulous and horrified commissioner, that Mr. J. G. Reeder reconstructed the crime.
“Flack is a chemist; I think I impressed it upon you. Did you notice, Simpson, on the bridge across the cutting an old water cart? I think you have since learned that it does not belong to the farmer who owns the land, and that he has never seen it before. It may be possible to discover where that was purchased. Suppose this, or, as it may prove, a more deadly gas, has been so stored, do you realize how simple a matter it would be on a still, breathless morning to throw a big hose over the bridge and fill the hollow with the gas?
