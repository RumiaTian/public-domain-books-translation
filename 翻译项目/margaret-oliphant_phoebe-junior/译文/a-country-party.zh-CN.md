It was not for pleasure that this country party had come to London. Sir Robert’s second son, who was in India, had sent his eldest children home to the care of his father and sisters. They were expected at Portsmouth daily, and the aunts, somewhat excited by the prospect of their charge, had insisted upon coming to town to receive them. As for Ursula May, who was a poor relation on the late Lady Dorset’s side, as Mrs. Copperhead had been a poor relation on Sir Robert’s, London at any season was a wonder and excitement to her, and she could not sufficiently thank the kind relations who had given her this holiday in her humdrum life. She was the daughter of a poor clergyman in the little town of Carlingford, a widower with a large family. Ursula was the eldest daughter, with the duties of a mother on her much burdened hands; and she had no special inclination towards these duties, so that a week’s escape from them was a relief to her at any time. And a ball! But the ball had not been so beatific as Ursula hoped.

厄休拉同菲比·比彻姆真是有天壤之别。她全无自命才学之心，从不附庸风雅。她宁可同最小的几个弟弟妹妹做伴，也不愿听爸爸谈话，尽管他是教区里有口皆碑的俊杰之一。就连她非常爱戴的哥哥雷金纳德从大学回家时，厄休拉对长姐的这份特权也无动于衷——凭这份特权，她可以熬夜旁听他同父亲之间的谈话。雷金纳德也非常聪明；他靠奖学金在大学里求上进——这也是梅先生的儿子上得了大学的唯一途径——听他同父亲谈论希腊诗歌与哲学，实在是件美事；这机会若是落到菲比·比彻姆头上，她会多么珍视啊；可厄休拉却不稀罕这份特权。她宁可看一场哑剧，看戏院里最蹩脚的演出，哪怕是杜莎夫人的蜡像展也行。她甚至更愿意跟着多塞特小姐的女仆逛繁华的街道，看看橱窗，猜猜下一季会流行什么。可怜的小姑娘！趣味这样天真轻浮，可以想见，长女兼管家婆这个位置对她并不合适。

===Original===

Her father was no more than Incumbent of St. Roque, an old perpetual curacy merged in a district church, which was a poor appointment for an elderly man with a family; he was very clever and superior, but not a man who got on, or who did much to help his children to get on; and had Ursula been of the kind of those who suffer and deny themselves by nature, she would have had her hands full, and abundant opportunity afforded her to exercise those faculties. But she was not of this frame of mind. She did what she was obliged to do as well as time and opportunity permitted; but she did not throw herself with any enthusiasm into her duties. To keep seven children in good condition and discipline in a small house, on a small income, is more, it must be allowed, than most girls of twenty are equal to; only enthusiasm and self-devotion could make such a task possible, and these qualifications poor little Ursula did not possess. Oh! how glad she was to get away from it all, from having to think of Janey and Johnny, and Amy and little Robin. She was not anxious about how things might be going on in her absence, as kind Miss Dorset thought she must be. The happiness of escaping was first and foremost in her thoughts.

她父亲不过是圣罗克堂的主持人，一个并入区教堂的老常任牧师职——对一个上了年纪还拖家带口的人来说，这是份清苦的差事；他人很聪明、很杰出，却不是个会往上走的人，也帮不上子女什么忙；厄休拉若是那种天生吃苦克己的性子，她本会忙得不可开交，也本有大把机会施展那些本领。可她不是这种心性。她把非做不可的事，在时间和条件允许的范围内做得还算好；可她对这份职责没有半点热忱。必须承认，靠微薄的收入，在小小的宅子里让七个孩子吃饱穿暖、循规蹈矩，超出了大多数二十岁姑娘的能力；唯有热忱和献身精神才能使这样一副担子挑得起来，而这两样本钱，可怜的小厄休拉偏偏都没有。
哦！她是多么高兴能从中脱身，不必再惦记珍妮和约翰尼、艾米和小罗宾。她并不担心自己不在家事情会怎样——好心的多塞特小姐却以为她一定放心不下。逃跑的幸福在她心里占了第一位。
