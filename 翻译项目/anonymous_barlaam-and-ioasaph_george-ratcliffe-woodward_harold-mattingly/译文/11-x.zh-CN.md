## X / 十

===Original===
But Ioasaph was filled hereby with deep compunction, and was melted into tears; and he said to the elder, “Thou hast told me everything plainly, and hast completed unerringly thy terrible and marvellous tale. With such truths set before us, what must we do to escape the punishments in store for sinners, and to gain the joy of the righteous?”

Barlaam answered: “It is written of Peter, who was also called chief of the Apostles, that once when he was preaching the people were pricked in their heart, like thyself today: and when they asked, ‘What shall we do?’ Peter said unto them, ‘Repent, and be baptized every one of you for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost. For to you is the promise, and to your children, and to all that are afar off even as many as the Lord our God shall call.’ Behold therefore upon thee also hath he poured forth the riches of his mercy, and hath called thee that wert afar off from him in heart, and didst serve others, not Gods, but pernicious devils and dumb and senseless wooden images. Wherefore before all things approach thou him who hath called thee, and from him shalt thou receive the true knowledge of things visible and invisible. But if, after thy calling, thou be loth or slack, thou shalt be disherited by the just judgement of God, and by thy rejection of him thou shalt be rejected. For thus too spake the same Apostle Peter to a certain disciple.6 But I believe that thou hast heard the call, and that, when thou hast heard it more plainly, thou wilt take up thy Cross, and follow that God and Master that calleth thee, calleth thee to himself from death unto life, and from darkness unto light. For, soothly, ignorance of God is darkness and death of the soul; and to serve idols, to the destruction of all nature, is to my thinking the extreme of all senselessness.

“But idolaters﻿—to whom shall I compare them, and to what likeness shall I liken their silliness? Well, I will set before thee an example which I heard from the lips of one most wise.

约阿萨弗听了这些话，心里充满深切的痛悔，潸然泪下；他对长老说：「你已将一切明明地告诉我，把你那可畏而奇妙的故事毫无差错地讲完了。这些真理既已摆在我们眼前，我们该怎样行，才能逃避那为罪人存留的刑罚，得着义人的喜乐呢？」

巴拉阿姆回答说：「论到彼得——就是那称为使徒之首的——经上记着：有一次他讲道，众人都觉得扎心，正如你今日一样。他们问他说：『我们当怎样行？』彼得就对他们说：『你们各人要悔改，受洗使罪得赦，就必领受所赐的圣灵。因为这应许是给你们和你们的儿女，并给一切远方的人，就是主我们的神所召来的。』看哪，他也照样将丰盛的怜悯倾在你身上，召了你——你这心里远离他、素来事奉别神的；那并不是神，乃是有害的鬼魔，并哑口无觉的木像。所以，你第一要紧的，是亲近那召你的主；惟独从他那里，你才能领受关于可见与不可见之事的真知识。但你蒙召之后，若心里迟延怠惰，就必按神公义的审判被夺去基业；你既弃绝他，也必被他弃绝。因为同一位使徒彼得，也曾这样对某一个门徒说过这话6。但我相信你已听见了这呼召，并且当你听得更清楚的时候，你必背起你的十字架，跟从那召你的神和主——他召你归他自己，从死亡归入生命，从黑暗归入光明。因为实在说来，不认识神，就是灵魂的黑暗与死亡；而事奉偶像以致全然败坏本性，依我看来，正是愚昧的极处。

「但是，拜偶像的人——我拿什么来比拟他们呢？又用什么样式比拟他们的愚妄呢？好，我要把一个譬喻摆在你面前，是我从一位极有智慧之人口中听来的。

“ ‘Idol worshippers,’ said he, ‘are like a fowler who caught a tiny bird, called nightingale. He took a knife, for to kill and eat her; but the nightingale, being given the power of articulate speech, said to the fowler, “Man, what advantageth it thee to slay me? for thou shalt not be able by my means to fill thy belly. Now free me of my fetters, and I will give thee three precepts, by the keeping of which thou shalt be greatly benefited all thy life long.” He, astonied at her speech, promised that, if he heard anything new from her, he would quickly free her from her captivity. The nightingale turned towards our friend and said, “Never try to attain to the unattainable: never regret the thing past and gone: and never believe the word that passeth belief. Keep these three precepts, and may it be well with thee.” The man, admiring the lucidity and sense of her words, freed the bird from her captivity, and sent her forth aloft. She, therefore, desirous to know whether the man had understood the force of her words, and whether he had gleaned any profit therefrom, said, as she flew aloft, “Shame, sir, on thy fecklessness! What a treasure that hast lost today! For I have inside me a pearl larger than an ostrich-egg.” When the fowler heard thereof, he was distraught with grief, regretting that the bird had escaped out of his hands. And he would fain have taken her again. “Come hither,” said he, “into my house: I will make thee right welcome, and send thee forth with honour.” But the nightingale said unto him, “Now I know thee to be a mighty fool. Though thou didst receive my words readily and gladly, thou hast gained no profit thereby. I bade thee never regret the thing past and gone; and behold thou art distraught with grief because I have escaped out of thy hands﻿—there thou regrettest a thing past and gone. I charged thee not to try to attain to the unattainable, and thou triest to catch me, though thou canst not attain to my path. Besides which, I bade thee never believe a word past belief, and behold thou hast believed that I had inside me a pearl exceeding the measure of my size, and hadst not the sense to see that my whole body doth not attain to the bulk of ostrich eggs. How then could I contain such a pearl?” ’

「『拜偶像的人，』他说，『就像一个捕鸟的，捉住了一只小鸟，名叫夜莺。他拿刀要宰了它吃；那夜莺却得了说人话的能力，对捕鸟人说：「人哪，你杀了我，与你有什么益处呢？因为你借着我，并不能填满你的肚腹。如今你解开我的捆绑，我就给你三条训言；你若遵守，必一生大得益处。」捕鸟人听了它的话，甚是惊奇，就应许说，若能从它那里听见什么新事，就快快释放它脱离捆锁。夜莺转向我们这位朋友，说：「不可强求那得不着的事；不可追悔那已过去的事；不可轻信那难以置信的话。你遵守这三条训言，愿你平安。」那人佩服它言语的明白晓畅、句句有理，就释放了那鸟，任它高飞。那鸟却想知道那人是否领悟了它话中的力量、是否从中得了益处，便在高飞的时候说：「先生，你的无用真是可耻！你今日丢失了何等的珍宝啊！因为在我这身子里面，藏着一颗比鸵鸟蛋还大的珍珠。」捕鸟人听了这话，忧伤迷乱，懊恨那鸟从手中逃脱，又想再捉住它，就说：「到这里来，进我的屋里；我要好好地款待你，体体面面地送你走。」夜莺却对他说：「如今我知道你是个大大的愚人了。你虽然欣然领受了我的话，却没有从中得着益处。我吩咐你不可追悔那已过去的事；看哪，你却因我从你手中逃脱而忧伤迷乱——你正是在追悔一件已过去的事。我嘱咐你不可强求那得不着的事；你却想要再捉住我，尽管你根本赶不上我所行的路径。再者，我吩咐你不可轻信那难以置信的话；看哪，你竟相信我身内藏着一颗超过我身量的珍珠，却没有想到，我这一身根本及不上一枚鸵鸟蛋的大小。这样，我又怎能容得下那样的珍珠呢？」』

“Thus senseless, then, are also they that trust in idols: for these be their handiwork, and they worship that which their fingers made, saying, ‘These be our creators.’ How then deem they their creators those which have been formed and fashioned by themselves? Nay more, they safeguard their gods, lest they be stolen by thieves, and yet they call them guardians of their safety. And yet what folly not to know that they, which be unable to guard and aid themselves, can in no wise guard and save others! ‘For’ saith he, ‘why, on behalf of the living, should they seek unto the dead?’ They expend wealth, for to raise statues and images to devils, and vainly boast that these give them good gifts, and crave to receive of their hands things which those idols never possessed, nor ever shall possess. Wherefore it is written, ‘May they that make them be like unto them, and so be all such as put their trust in them, who,’ he saith, ‘hire a goldsmith, and make them gods, and they fall down, yea, they worship them. They bear them upon the shoulders, and go forward. And if they set them in their place, they stand therein: they shall not remove. Yea, one shall cry unto them, yet call they not answer him, nor save him out of his trouble.’ ‘Wherefore be ye ashamed with everlasting shame, ye that trust in graven images, that say to the molten images, Ye are our gods.’ ‘For they sacrificed,’ he saith, ‘unto devils, and not to God; to gods whom their fathers knew not. There came new and fresh gods; because it is a froward generation, and there is no faith in them.’

“Wherefore out of this wicked and faithless generation the Lord calleth thee to him, saying, ‘Come out from among them, and be thou separate, and touch no unclean thing,’ but ‘save thyself from this untoward generation.’ ‘Arise thou, and depart, for this is not thy rest;’ for that divided lordship, which your gods hold, is a thing of confusion and strife and hath no real being whatsoever. But with us it is not so, neither have we many gods and lords, but one God, the Father, of whom are all things, and we unto him: and one Lord Jesus Christ, by whom are all things and we by him, ‘who is the image of the invisible God, the first born of every creature’ and of all ages, ‘for in him were all things created that are in the heavens and that are upon the earth, visible and invisible, whether they be thrones, or dominions, or principalities, or powers.’ ‘All things were made by him, and without him was not anything made that was made:’ and one Holy Ghost, in whom are all things, ‘the Lord and Giver of life,’ God and making God, the good Spirit, the right Spirit, ‘the Spirit the Comforter,’ ‘the Spirit of adoption.’ Of these each person, severally, is God. As the Father is, so also is the Son, and as the Son, so also the Holy Ghost. And there is one God in three, one nature, one kingdom, one power, one glory, one substance, distinct in persons, and so only distinct. One is the Father, whose property it is not to have been begotten; one is the only-begotten Son, and his property it is to have been begotten; and one is the Holy Ghost, and his property it is that he proceedeth. Thus illuminated *by* that light, which is the Father, *with* that light, which is the Son, *in* that light, which is the Holy Ghost, we glorify one Godhead in three persons. And he is one very and only God, known in the Trinity: for of him and through him, and unto him are all things.

「这样，那些倚靠偶像的人也正是这般无知：偶像原是他们的手所做的工，他们却敬拜自己指头所造之物，说：『这些是造我们的。』这些由他们自己造出来、塑造成形的东西，他们怎会当作自己的创造者呢？更有甚者，他们看守自己的神明，惟恐被贼偷去，却又称它们为自己安全的守护者。它们连自己都不能保护、不能救助，又怎能保护拯救别人——不明白这一点，是何等的愚昧！因为他说：『活人为何要求问死人呢？』他们耗费钱财，为鬼魔立像造形，又虚妄地自夸，说这些会将美物赐给他们，又渴想从它们手中领受那些偶像从来未曾拥有、也永不会拥有的东西。所以经上记着说：『愿造它们的和它们一样，凡倚靠它们的也是如此』——论到这样的人，他又说：『他们雇了银匠，为自己造神像，便俯伏叩拜。他们将神像抬在肩头上，行走。他们把像安置在原处，它就站在那里，总不挪移。人呼求它，它不能答应，也不能救他脱离患难。』『所以，你们这些倚靠雕刻的偶像、对铸造的偶像说「你们是我们的神」的，必永远蒙羞，抱愧不已。』他说：『他们献祭给鬼魔，不是献给神，乃是献给他们的列祖所不认识的神；那些是近来新兴的神；因为这世代乖僻背逆，其中没有忠信。』

「所以，主正从这邪恶无信的世代中召你归他，说：『你务要从他们中间出来，与他们分别，不要沾不洁净的物』；又说：『你当救自己脱离这弯曲的世代。』『你起来去吧，因为这不是你的安息之所』；因为你们的诸神所分掌的那分裂的权柄，乃是混乱纷争之物，绝无丝毫真实的存在。但我们不是这样，我们也没有许多的神、许多的主，只有一位神，就是父，万物都本于他，我们也归于他；并有一位主，就是耶稣基督，万物都是借着他有的，我们也是借着他有的。他是『那不能看见之神的像，在一切受造的以先是首生的』，也是万世以先首生的，『因为天上的、地上的、能看见的、不能看见的，或是有位的、主治的、执政的、掌权的，万有都是在他里面受造的』。『万物是借着他造的；凡被造的，没有一样不是借着他造的。』还有一位圣灵，万有都在他里面，他是『赐生命的主』，是神，又是使人成神的，是良善的灵、正直的灵，是『作保惠师的灵』，是『儿子名分的灵』。这三者，每一位各自是神。父怎样，子也怎样；子怎样，圣灵也怎样。三位之中乃是一位神：一本性、一国度、一权能、一荣耀、一本体，在位格上各有分别，也惟独在位格上有分别。一位是父，他的特性是不受生；一位是独生的子，他的特性是受生；一位是圣灵，他的特性是由父而出。这样，我们既被那光——就是父——光照，与那光——就是子——相偕，在那光——就是圣灵——里面，就荣耀那三位格中的独一神性。他是真实独一的神，在圣三一中受认识；因为万有都是本于他、倚靠他、归于他。

“By his grace also, I came to know thy case, and was sent to teach thee the lessons that I have learned and observed from my youth even to these grey hairs. If then thou shalt believe and be baptized, thou shalt be saved; but if thou believe not, thou shalt be damned. All the things that thou seest today, wherein thou gloriest﻿—pomp, luxury, riches, and all the deceitfulness of life﻿—quickly pass away; and they shall cast thee hence whether thou wilt or no. And thy body will be imprisoned in a tiny grave, left in utter loneliness, and bereft of all company of kith and kin. And all the pleasant things of the world shall perish; and instead of the beauty and fragrance of today, thou shalt be encompassed with horror and the stink of corruption. But thy soul shall they hurl into the nether-regions of the earth, into the condemnation of Hades, until the final resurrection, when reunited to her body, she shall be cast forth from the presence of the Lord and be delivered to hell fire, which burneth everlastingly. These, and far worse haps than these, shall be thy destiny, if thou continue in unbelief.

“But and if thou readily obey him that calleth thee to salvation, and if thou run unto him with desire and joy, and be signed with his light, and follow him without turn, renouncing everything, and cleaving only unto him, hear what manner of security and happiness shall be thine. ‘When thou sittest down, thou shall not be afraid of sudden fear. When thou liest down, sweet shall be thy sleep.’ And thou shalt not be afraid of terror coming or the assaults of evil spirits, but shalt go thy way bold as any lion, and shalt live in bliss and everlasting joyance. For ‘joy and praise shall crown thy head, and gladness shall befall thee there, where pain and sorrow and wailing shall flee away.’ ‘Then shall thy light break forth as the morning, and thine health shall rise speedily: and thy righteousness shall go before thee, and the glory of the Lord shall be thy reward.’ Then shalt thou call, and the Lord shall answer; while thou art yet speaking, he shall say, ‘Here am I.’ ‘I, even I, am he that blotteth out thy transgressions, and will not remember them. Put me in remembrance: let us plead together: declare thou thy sins that thou mayst be justified.’ ‘Though thy sins be as scarlet, I will make them white as snow: though they be red as crimson I will make them white as wool, for the mouth of the Lord hath spoken it.’ ”

「也是靠着他的恩典，我才得知你的事，奉差来将我从年幼直到如今这满头白发所学习、所遵守的教训教导你。你若信而受洗，必然得救；你若不信，必被定罪。你今日所看见、并在其中夸耀的一切——浮华、宴乐、财富，并今生一切的迷惑——都要速速过去；它们要将你从这里抛出去，由不得你。你的身体将被禁锢在小小的坟墓里，孤孤单单地撇在那里，失去一切骨肉亲人的相伴。世上一切令人喜悦的美物都要消逝；今日的秀美芬芳不复再有，环绕你的将是可怖的景象与腐朽的恶臭。你的灵魂却要被摔进地的深处，落在阴间的定罪里，直等到末次的复活；那时它既重新与身体联合，就必从主面前被逐出，交与那永燃不熄的地狱之火。你若仍旧存着不信，这些事，以及比这些更坏得多的遭遇，就是你的定命。

「但你若欣然顺从那召你来得救的主，怀着渴慕与喜乐奔向他，蒙他光明的印记，毫不转顾地跟从他，弃绝万事，单单依傍他，那么且听你将得着何等的安稳与幸福：『你坐下，必不惧怕忽然的惊恐；你躺卧，睡得香甜。』你必不害怕将临的恐怖，也不怕邪灵的侵攻，倒要胆壮如狮子，行你的路，住在福乐与永远的欢欣里。因为『喜乐和赞美必冠你的头，欢欣必临到你，就在那里，疼痛、忧愁、哀号都要逃避』。『那时你的光必如晨光突发，你的医治必速速兴起；你的公义必行在你前面，耶和华的荣耀必作你的赏赐。』那时你求告，耶和华必应允；你还在说话，他必说：『我在这里。』『我，惟有我，是涂抹你过犯的那位，我必不再记念你的罪。你要提醒我，你我可以一同辩明；你要陈说你的罪，好显为义。』『你的罪虽像朱红，我必使它白如雪；虽红如丹颜，我必使它白如羊毛，因为这是耶和华亲口说的。』」
