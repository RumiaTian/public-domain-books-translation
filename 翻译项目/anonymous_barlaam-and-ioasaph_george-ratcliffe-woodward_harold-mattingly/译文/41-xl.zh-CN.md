## XL / 四十

===Original===
Then did Ioasaph embrace the good father, with all the devotion and sorrow that can be told, and washed his corpse with his tears. Then he wrapped it in the hair-shirt, which Barlaam had given him in his palace; and over him he recited the proper psalms, chanting all the day long, and throughout the night, and watering the venerable body of the Saint with his tears. On the morrow, he made a grave hard by the cave, and thither reverently bore the sacred body, and there, like a good and honourable son, laid his spiritual father in his sepulchre. And then, the fire of grief kindling all the hotter within his soul, he set himself to pray the more earnestly, saying:

“O Lord my God, hearken unto my voice, when I cry unto thee. Have mercy upon me, and hear me, for I seek thee with all my heart. My soul hath sought for thee: O hide not thy face from me, and turn not away in anger from thy servant. Be thou my helper; cast me not utterly away, and forsake me not, O God my Saviour, because my father and mother forsake me; but do thou, O Lord, take me up. Teach me thy way, O Lord, and lead me in the right way because of mine enemies. Deliver me not over unto the souls of them that afflict me; for I have been cast upon thee ever since I was born; thou art my God even from my mother’s womb. O go not from me, because, except thee, there is none to help me. For lo, I set the hope of my soul upon the ocean of thy mercies. Be thou the pilot of my soul, thou that steerest all creation with the unspeakable forethought of thy wisdom; and show thou me the way that I should walk in; and, as thou art a good God and a lover of men, save me by the prayers and intercessions of Barlaam thy servant, for thou art my God, and thee I glorify, the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost, world without end. Amen.”

Thus prayed he, and sat him down nigh the sepulchre, a-weeping. And as he sat, he fell asleep, and saw those dread men, whom he had seen before, coming to him, and carrying him away to the great and marvellous plain, and bringing him to that glorious and exceeding bright city. When he had passed within the gate, there met him others, gloriously apparelled with much light, having in their hands crowns radiant with unspeakable beauty, such as mortal eye hath never seen. And, when Ioasaph enquired, “Whose are these exceeding bright crowns of glory, which I see?” “Thine,” said they, “is the one, prepared for thee, because of the many souls which thou hast saved, and now made still more beautiful because of the religious life that thou leadest, if thou continue therein bravely until the end. And this other crown is thine also; but it must thou give unto thy father, who, by thy means, turned from his evil way unto the Lord, and was truly penitent.” But Ioasaph was as one sore vexed, and said, “How is it possible that, for his repentance alone, my father should receive reward equal to mine, that have laboured so much?” Thus spake he, and straightway thought that he saw Barlaam, as it were, chiding him and saying, “These are my words, Ioasaph, which I once spake unto thee, saying, ‘When thou waxest passing rich, thou wilt not be glad to distribute,’ and thou understoodest not my saying. But now, why art thou displeased at thy father’s equality with thee in honour, and art not rather glad at heart that thine orisons in his behalf have been heard?” Then Ioasaph said unto him, as he was ever wont to say, “Pardon! father, pardon! But show me where thou dwellest?” Barlaam answered, “In this mighty and exceeding fair city. It is my lot to dwell in the midmost street of the city, a street that flasheth with light supernal.” Again Ioasaph thought he asked Barlaam to bring him to his own habitation, and, in friendly wise, to show him the sights thereof. But Barlaam said that his time was not yet come to win those habitations, while he was under the burden of the flesh. “But,” said he, “if thou persevere bravely, even as I charged thee, in a little while thou shalt come hither, and gain the same habitations, and obtain the same joy and glory, and be my companion forever.” Hereupon Ioasaph awoke out of sleep, but his soul was still full of that light and ineffable glory; and greatly wondering, he raised to his Lord a song of thanksgiving.

于是约阿萨弗以言语所能述说的一切敬爱与哀恸，抱住那良善的父，用眼泪洗濯他的遗体；随后用那件粗毛衣将遗体裹好——就是巴拉阿姆从前在王宫里赠给他的那件；又在他身旁诵念合宜的诗篇，终日唱诵，彻夜方休，用泪水不住浇灌圣徒那可敬的身体。次日，他在洞窟近旁掘了一座坟墓，恭恭敬敬地把圣体抬到那里，如同良善而孝敬的儿子，将他的属灵之父安放在坟墓里。此后，忧伤的火在他魂中燃得愈发炽烈，他就愈发恳切地专心祷告说：

「主我的神啊，我呼吁你的时候，求你垂听我的声音。求你怜恤我，应允我，因为我一心寻求你。我的灵魂寻求了你：求你不要向我掩面，也不要在怒中转脸远离你的仆人。求你作我的帮助；不要把我全然丢弃，不要离弃我，我救恩的神啊，因为我父母离弃我，惟愿你收留我。耶和华啊，求你将你的道指教我，因我仇敌的缘故，引导我走正直的路。不要把我交给那些苦害我的人；因为我自出母胎就被交在你手里，从我母亲生我，你就是我的神。求你不要远离我，因为除你以外，无人帮助我。看哪，我已将我灵魂的盼望，安放在你怜悯的洋海之上。求你作我灵魂的舵手——你正以你智慧那不可言喻的先见，为一切受造之物掌舵；求你将我当行的路指示我；你是良善的神、爱世人的主，求你借你仆人巴拉阿姆的祷告与代求拯救我；因为你是我的神，我荣耀你——圣父、圣子、圣灵，直到世世代代，永永远远。阿们。」

他这样祷告完了，就在坟墓旁边坐下哭泣。他坐着的时候睡着了，看见那些可畏的人——就是他从前见过的——来到他那里，把他带到那广大奇妙的平原，又领他进那座荣耀而极其明亮的城。他进了城门以后，另有几位迎着他来，身披大光，极其荣耀，手中拿着冠冕，闪耀着说不出的华美，是必死之人的眼目从未见过的。约阿萨弗问说：「我所看见的这些极其明亮的荣耀冠冕，是谁的呢？」他们说：「其中一顶是你的，是为你预备的，因你拯救了许多灵魂；如今又因你所行的修道生活而愈加美丽——只要你奋勇坚持到底。这另一顶也是你的；但你必须把它交给你的父亲，因他借着你从恶道上归向主，真心悔改了。」约阿萨弗却像被大大触恼了一般，说：「怎么可能呢——单凭悔改，我父亲所得的赏赐，竟与我这劳苦繁多的人相等？」他这样说着，立时仿佛看见巴拉阿姆在责备他，说：「约阿萨弗啊，这正是我从前对你说过的话——我说，『当你富足无比的时候，你就不再甘心施舍了』——那时你不明白我的话。如今你为何因父亲与你同得尊荣而不悦，反不为你为他献上的祷告已蒙垂听而心中欢喜呢？」约阿萨弗便照他素来惯常的口吻对他说：「恕罪，父啊，恕罪！但请你告诉我，你住在哪里？」巴拉阿姆回答说：「就在这座宏大而极其华美的城里。我所得的分，是住在城中最当中的那条街——那街闪耀着属天之光。」约阿萨弗又仿佛求巴拉阿姆带他到自己的居所，好亲亲热热地把那里的种种景象指给他看。但巴拉阿姆说，他还在肉体的重担之下，得着那些居所的时候尚未来到。他说：「但你若照我所嘱咐你的奋勇坚持，过不多时你必来到这里，得着同样的居所，获致同样的喜乐与荣耀，永远作我的同伴。」于是约阿萨弗从睡眠中醒来，他的魂仍然满载着那光亮而不可言喻的荣耀；他大为惊奇，向他的主高唱了一首感恩的颂歌。

And he continued to the end, verily leading on earth the life of an angel, and after the death of his aged friend using himself to severer austerity. Twenty and five years old was he when he left his earthly kingdom, and adopted the monastic life; and thirty and five years in this vast desert did he, like one dis-fleshed, endure rigours above the endurance of man, but not before he had delivered the souls of many men from the soul-devouring dragon, and presented them to God, saved for aye; winning herewith the Apostolic grace. In will he had proved a martyr, and had with boldness confessed Christ before kings and tyrants, and had proved himself the mighty-voiced preacher of his greatness, and had overthrown many spirits of wickedness in the desert, and had overcome all in the strength of Christ. Partaking richly of the gift of grace from above, he kept his mind’s eye purified from every earthborn cloud, and looked forward to the things that are to come, as though they were already come. Christ was his recompense for all: Christ was his desire: Christ he ever saw as present with him: Christ and his fair beauty everywhere met his sight, according to the saying of the prophet, “I have set God always before me; for he is on my right hand, therefore I shall not fall.” And again, “My soul cleaveth to thee; thy right hand hath upholden me.” For verily Ioasaph’s soul clave to Christ, being knit to him in indissoluble union. From this marvellous work he never swerved, never altered the rule of his ascetic life, from beginning to end, but maintained his zeal from his youth even until old age; or rather, he daily advanced higher in virtue, and daily gained purer power of vision.

Thus did Ioasaph spend his days, and render unto him that called him labour worthy of his calling, having crucified the world to himself, and himself unto the world, and, at the last, departed in peace unto the God of peace, and passed to that Master whom he had alway longed for. There he appeared in the immediate presence of the Lord, and was crowned with the crown of glory already prepared for him: there it is granted to him to behold Christ, to be with Christ, to rejoice forever in the fair beauty of Christ, into whose hands he commended his spirit, when he departed to walk in the land of the living, where is the song of them that feast, the dwelling-place of them that rejoice.

As for his venerable body, it befell thus; about the very hour of Ioasaph’s death, there came by divine revelation, from one of the neighbouring cells, a certain holy man. It was the same that once pointed out to Ioasaph his way to Barlaam. This man honoured the corpse with sacred hymns, and shed tears, the token of affection, over him, and performed all the last Christian rites, and laid him in the sepulchre of his father Barlaam; for it was only meet that their bodies should rest side by side, since their souls were to dwell through eternity together.

此后他持守到底，在地上实在度着天使般的生活；自他年迈的朋友去世以后，又使自己惯于更严厉的苦行。他离开地上王国、投身修道生活的时候，年二十五岁；此后三十五年，他在那广大的旷野里，如同脱去肉身之人一般，忍受了超乎人力所能忍受的严酷；只是他未走完这路程以先，已先将许多人的灵魂从那吞吃灵魂的龙口中救出，献与神，使他们永远得救，并借此赢得了使徒的恩典。他在心志上已作了殉道者，曾在君王和暴君面前放胆认信基督，证明自己是传扬他威严的大声传道者，在旷野中倾覆了许多邪恶之灵，又靠着基督的大能胜过了一切。他丰盛地有分于从上头来的恩赐，保守心思之眼洁净，脱尽一切属地的云翳，前瞻那将要来的事，如同它们已经来到。基督是他一切的报偿：基督是他的切慕：他常看基督如与自己同在：基督并他的荣美处处映入他的眼目，正如先知所说：「我将神常摆在我面前，因他在我右边，我便不至摇动。」又说：「我的灵魂紧贴于你；你的右手扶持了我。」约阿萨弗的魂实在紧贴着基督，与他结成了不能拆散的联合。这奇妙的事他从未偏离，从始至终从未更改他苦修生活的规矩，乃是从青年直到老年持守他的热心；更可以说，他天天在德行上攀升更高，天天得着更纯净的属灵视力。

约阿萨弗就这样度尽了他的岁月，向那选召他的主，献上了与蒙召相称的劳苦；他既已把世界钉在十字架上，又把自己钉在世界的十字架上，末了便在平安中离世，往那赐平安的神那里去，归到他素来长久切慕的主那里。在那里，他得以显现在主面前，戴上那早已为他预备的荣耀冠冕；在那里，他蒙恩得见基督，与基督同在，又永远欢欣于基督的荣美——他离世往活人之地去的时候，就是把自己的灵魂交在这位基督手里；那地有赴筵者的歌声，是欢乐者的居所。

至于他那可敬的遗体，事情经过是这样：约在约阿萨弗去世的那时辰，有一位圣洁之人，因着神的启示，从邻近的一间修士小室来到他那里。他正是从前向约阿萨弗指明往巴拉阿姆去的那条路的人。这人用神圣的颂歌尊荣遗体，又在他身上流下热泪，作为情爱的印证，行完了基督徒殡葬的一切礼节，把他安放在他父亲巴拉阿姆的坟墓里；因为他们二人的身体理当并排安息，他们的灵魂也必同住直到永世。

In obedience to the strict command of a dread Angel that appeared to him in a dream, this hermit, who had performed the last rites, journeyed to the kingdom of India, and, entering in to King Barachias, made known unto him all that had befallen Barlaam, and this blessed Ioasaph. Barachias, making no delay, set forth with a mighty host, and arrived at the cave, and beheld their sepulchre, and wept bitterly over it, and raised the gravestone. There he descried Barlaam and Ioasaph lying, as they had been in life. Their bodies had not lost their former hue, but were whole and uncorrupt, together with their garments. These, the consecrated tabernacles of two holy souls, that sent forth full sweet savour, and showed naught distressful, were placed by King Barachias in costly tombs and conveyed by him into his own country.

Now when the people heard tell of that which had come to pass, there assembled a countless multitude out of all the cities and regions round about, to venerate and view the bodies of these Saints. Thereupon, sooth to say, they chanted the sacred hymns over them, and vied one with another to light lamps lavishly, and rightly and fitly, might one say, in honour of these children and inheritors of light. And with splendour and much solemnity they laid their bodies in the Church which Ioasaph had built from the very foundation. And many miracles and cures, during the translation and deposition of their relics, as also in later times, did the Lord work by his holy servants. And King Barachias and all the people beheld the mighty virtues that were shown by them; and many of the nations round about, that were sick of unbelief and ignorance of God, believed through the miracles that were wrought at their sepulchre. And all they that saw and heard of the Angelic life of Ioasaph, and of his love of God from his childhood upward, marvelled, and in all things glorified God that alway worketh together with them that love him, and granteth them exceeding great reward.

Here endeth this history, which I have written, to the best of my ability, even as I heard it from the truthful lips of worthy men who delivered it unto me. And may God grant that all we that read or hear this edifying story may obtain the heritage of such as have pleased the Lord, by the prayers and intercessions of blessed Barlaam and Ioasaph, of whom this story telleth, in Christ Jesu our Lord; to whom belongeth worship, might, majesty and glory, with the Father and the Holy Ghost, now and forevermore, world without end. Amen.

这位行完殡葬之礼的隐修士，因着一位在梦中向他显现的可畏天使的严命，动身前往印度王国，进见巴拉加王，把临到巴拉阿姆和这位可称颂的约阿萨弗身上的一切事，都一一奏知他。巴拉加毫不迟延，率领一支大军启程，到了那洞窟，看见他们的坟墓，伏在上面痛哭，随后揭起墓石。他在那里看见巴拉阿姆和约阿萨弗躺着，如同生前一样。他们的身体没有失去本来的颜色，完好不朽，连衣裳也一并如此。这两具成圣的帐幕——两个圣洁的灵魂曾居于其中——发出浓郁的馨香，毫无朽坏凄惨之相；巴拉加王将他们安放在贵重的墓冢里，运回自己的本国。

百姓听见所成就的这事，就有数不尽的群众从四围各城各方聚集而来，要敬礼、瞻仰这二圣的遗体。于是，说实在的，他们向二圣唱诵神圣的颂歌，又彼此争竞，不惜大量地点燃灯火——为着尊荣这些光明之子、光明之后嗣，若容我说，这实在是合宜又恰当的。他们又以盛大隆重的威仪，将二圣的身体安放在那座教堂里，就是约阿萨弗从根基上亲手建造的。在奉迁与安放他们圣髑的时候，以及后来的年日里，主借他这两位圣仆行了许多神迹与医治。巴拉加王和众百姓都看见了借他们显出的大能作为；四围许多民族中，那些患着不信与不认识神之病的人，都因在他们坟墓那里所行的神迹归信了。凡看见或听见约阿萨弗这天使般的生活、并他自幼年以来对神之爱的人，都无不惊奇，就在凡事上荣耀神——这位神常与爱他的人同工，又赐给他们极其浩大的赏赐。

本史传至此终结。我已竭尽所能，照着从那些将此书传给我的可敬之人诚实的口中所听见的，把它写述出来。愿神赐恩，叫我们一切阅读或听闻这造就人之故事的人，都靠着我们主基督耶稣，借本故事所讲述的可称颂的巴拉阿姆与约阿萨弗的祷告与代求，得以承受那蒙主喜悦之人的基业；愿敬拜、权能、尊大与荣耀都归于他，与圣父、圣灵同归，从今以后，直到永永远远，世世无尽。阿们。
