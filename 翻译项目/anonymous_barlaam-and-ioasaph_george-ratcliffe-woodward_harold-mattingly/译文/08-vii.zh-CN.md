## VII / 七

===Original===
Again therefore Barlaam took up his parable and said, “If thou wilt learn who is my Master, it is Jesus Christ the Lord, the only-begotten Son of God, ‘the blessed and only potentate, the King of kings, and Lords of lords; who only hath immortality, dwelling in the light which no man can approach unto’; who with the Father and the Holy Ghost is glorified. I am not one of those who proclaim from the housetop their wild rout of gods, and worship lifeless and dumb idols, but one God do I acknowledge and confess, in three persons glorified, the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost, but in one nature and substance, in one glory and kingdom undivided. He then is in three persons one God, without beginning, and without end, eternal and everlasting, uncreate, immutable and incorporeal, invisible, infinite, incomprehensible, alone good and righteous, who created all things out of nothing, whether visible or invisible. First, he made the heavenly and invisible powers, countless multitudes, immaterial and bodiless, ministering spirits of the majesty of God. Afterward he created this visible world, heaven and earth and sea, which also he made glorious with light and richly adorned it; the heavens with the sun, moon and stars, and the earth with all manner of herbs and divers living beasts, and the sea in turn with all kinds of fishes. ‘He spake the word and these all were made; he commanded and they were created.’ Then with his own hands he created man, taking dust of the ground for the fashioning of his body, but by his own in-breathing giving him a reasonable and intelligent soul, which, as it is written, was made after the image and likeness of God: after his image, because of reason and free will; after his likeness, because of the likeness of virtue, in its degree, to God. Him he endowed with free will and immortality and appointed sovran over everything upon earth; and from man he made woman, to be an helpmeet of like nature for him.

“And he planted a garden eastward in Eden, full of delight and all heart’s ease, and set thereto the man whom he had formed, and commanded him freely to eat of all the heavenly trees therein, but forbade him wholly the taste of a certain one which was called the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, thus saying, ‘In the day that ye eat thereof ye shall surely die.’ But one of the aforesaid angel powers, the marshall of one host, though he bore in himself no trace of natural evil from his Maker’s hand but had been created for good, yet by his own free and deliberate choice turned aside from good to evil, and was stirred up by madness to the desire to take up arms against his Lord God. Wherefore he was cast out of his rank and dignity, and in the stead of his former blissful glory and angelic name received the name of the ‘Devil’ and ‘Satan’ for his title. God banished him as unworthy of the glory above. And together with him there was drawn away and hurled forth a great multitude of the company of angels under him, who were evil of choice, and chose in place of good, to follow in the rebellion of their leader. These were called Devils, as being deluders and deceivers.

“Thus then did the devil utterly renounce the good, and assume an evil nature; and he conceived spite against man, seeing himself hurled from such glory, and man raised to such honour; and he schemed to oust him from that blissful state. So he took the serpent for the workshop of his own guile. Through him he conversed with the woman, and persuaded her to eat of that forbidden tree in the hope of being as God, and through her he deceived Adam also, for that was the first man’s name. So Adam ate of the tree of disobedience, and was banished by his maker from that paradise of delight, and, in lieu of those happy days and that immortal life, fell alas! into this life of misery and woe, and at the last received sentence of death. Thenceforth the devil waxed strong and boastful through his victory; and, as the race of man multiplied, he prompted them in all manner of wickedness. So, wishing to cut short the growth of sin, God brought a deluge on the earth, and destroyed every living soul. But one single righteous man did God find in that generation; and him, with wife and children, he saved alive in an Ark, and established him on a desolate earth. But, when the human race again began to multiply, they forgat God, and ran into worse excess of wickedness, being in subjection to divers sins and strange delusions, and wandering apart into many branches of error.

巴拉阿姆于是又提起诗歌说：「你若愿知道我的主是谁，他便是主耶稣基督，神的独生子，就是那『可称颂、独有权能的万王之王、万主之主，独一不死、住在人不能靠近之光里的』；他与父与圣灵同受荣耀。我并非那等在房顶上宣扬诸神乌合之群、敬拜无生命无声之偶像的人；我所承认、所宣信的，乃是一位神，在三个位格中同受荣耀，就是父、子、圣灵，却同在一本性、一实体，同在一荣耀、一无分的国度里。这样，他便是三位格中的一位神，无始无终，亘古常存，永世无尽，非受造、不变、无形体，不能看见、无限、不可测度，独一为善、为义；他从无有中创造了万有，或可见的，或不可见的。他先造了天上那些看不见的权能——无数的大群，无形无质的，是事奉神威荣的服役之灵。随后他创造了这可见的世界，就是天地与海，又使它以光华得荣耀，妆饰得丰美：诸天以日月星辰，大地以各样菜蔬和种种活物，海洋也以各类的鱼。『他一发言，这一切便都造成；他一出令，它们便都受造。』随后他亲手造人，取地上的尘土塑造他的身体，又借着自己吹入的生气，赋他一个有理性、有聪明的灵魂；正如经上所记，这人乃是照着神的形象、按着神的样式造的：照他的形象，是因理性和自由意志；按他的样式，是因德性按其程度与神相似。神赋他自由意志和不死，立他作地上万物的主宰；又从那人身上造出女人，作他同性情的配偶帮助他。

「神又在东方的伊甸栽了一个园子，满了赏心乐事，又有一切使人心安逸的，把所造的人安置在那里，吩咐他随意吃其中各样属天的美树，只是全然禁止他尝其中一棵，那树称为分别善恶树，并且说：『你吃的日子必定死。』但上述天使权能中的一位、一支天军的统领，虽然从造他的主手中受造时，里面没有丝毫天然之恶的痕迹，原是为善而造，却凭自己自由而故意的抉择，从善偏离到恶，又在癫狂中被激动，起了向他的主神兴兵的恶欲。因此他被逐出自己的等次和尊位，先前福祉的荣耀与天使之名都废去了，所得的称号，换成了『魔鬼』和『撒但』。神把他驱逐出去，因为他不配得上头的荣耀。与他一同被引诱、被摔出去的，还有他麾下天使会众的一大群；他们都是抉择了恶的，拣选了跟随首领的叛逆，以代替善。这些便称为鬼魔，因为它们是迷惑欺骗人的。

「魔鬼就这样全然弃绝了善，披上了恶的性情；他既见自己从那样的荣耀被摔下来，人却被抬举到那样的尊荣，就对人怀怨生妒，筹算要把他逐出那福祉的境况。于是他拿蛇作自己施行诡诈的工场，借着蛇与那女人交谈，劝她吃那棵禁树，指望自己能如神一样；又借着女人骗了亚当——那就是第一个人的名字。于是亚当吃了那悖逆之树的果子，被造他的主逐出那喜乐的乐园；那些福乐的日子、那不朽的生命既已失去——哀哉！——他就坠入这悲惨愁苦的生涯，末了还受了死亡的判语。从此魔鬼因着得胜而气焰强盛、自矜自夸；人类既繁衍增多，他就挑唆他们行各样的恶。神要将罪的滋长截断，就使洪水泛滥在地上，毁灭了凡有血气的活物。惟独在那世代中，神寻见一个义人，便叫他和妻子儿女一同在方舟里保全性命，在这荒凉的大地上将他坚立。但人类再度开始繁衍的时候，竟忘了神，陷入更甚的邪恶放肆之中，服在各样的罪和古怪的迷惑之下，流离分散，走入错谬的许多歧路。

“Some deemed that everything moved by mere chance, and taught that there was no Providence, since there was no master to govern. Others brought in fate, and committed everything to the stars at birth. Others worshipped many evil deities subject to many passions, to the end that they might have them to advocate their own passions and shameful deeds, whose forms they moulded, and whose dumb figures and senseless idols they set up, and enclosed them in temples, and did homage to them, ‘serving the creature more than the Creator.’ Some worshipped the sun, moon and stars which God fixed, for to give light to our earthly sphere; things without soul or sense, enlightened and sustained by the providence of God, but unable to accomplish anything of themselves. Others again worshipped fire and water, and the other elements, things without soul or sense; and men, possest of soul and reason, were not ashamed to worship the like of these. Others assigned worship to beasts, creeping and four-footed things, proving themselves more beastly than the things that they worshipped. Others made them images of vile and worthless men, and named them gods, some of whom they called males, and some females, and they themselves set them forth as adulterers, murderers, victims of anger, jealousy, wrath, slayers of fathers, slayers of brothers, thieves and robbers, lame and maim, sorcerers and madmen. Others they showed dead, struck by thunderbolts, or beating their breasts, or being mourned over, or in enslavement to mankind, or exiled, or, for foul and shameful unions, taking the forms of animals. Whence men, taking occasion by the gods themselves, took heart to pollute themselves in all manner of uncleanness. So an horrible darkness overspread our race in those times, and ‘there was none that did understand and seek after God.’

“Now in that generation one Abraham alone was found strong in his spiritual senses; and by contemplation of Creation he recognized the Creator. When he considered heaven, earth and sea, the sun, moon and the like, he marvelled at their harmonious ordering. Seeing the world, and all that therein is, he could not believe that it had been created, and was upheld, by its own power, nor did he ascribe such a fair ordering to earthly elements or lifeless idols. But therein he recognized the true God, and understood him to be the maker and sustainer of the whole. And God, approving his fair wisdom and right judgement, manifested himself unto him, not as he essentially is (for it is impossible for a created being to see God), but by certain manifestations in material forms, as he alone can, and he planted in Abraham more perfect knowledge; he magnified him and made him his own servant. Which Abraham in turn handed down to his children his own righteousness, and taught them to know the true God. Wherefore also the Lord was pleased to multiply his seed beyond measure, and called them ‘a peculiar people,’ and brought them forth out of bondage to the Egyptian nation, and to one Pharaoh a tyrant, by strange and terrible signs and wonders wrought by the hand of Moses and Aaron, holy men, honoured with the gift of prophecy; by whom also he punished the Egyptians in fashion worthy of their wickedness, and led the Israelites (for thus the people descended from Abraham were called) through the Red Sea upon dry land, the waters dividing and making a wall on the right hand and a wall on the left. But when Pharaoh and the Egyptians pursued and went in after them, the waters returned and utterly destroyed them. Then with exceeding mighty miracles and divine manifestations by the space of forty years he led the people in the wilderness, and fed them with bread from heaven, and gave the Law divinely written on tables of stone, which he delivered unto Moses on the mount, ‘a type and shadow of things to come’ leading men away from idols and all manner of wickedness, and teaching them to worship only the one true God, and to cleave to good works. By such wondrous deeds, he brought them into a certain goodly land, the which he had promised aforetime to Abraham the patriarch, that he would give it unto his seed. And the task were long, to tell of all the mighty and marvellous works full of glory and wonder, without number, which he showed unto them, by which it was his purpose to pluck the human race from all unlawful worship and practice, and to bring men back to their first estate. But even so our nature was in bondage by its freedom to err, and death had dominion over mankind, delivering all to the tyranny of the devil, and to the damnation of hell.

“So when we had sunk to this depth of misfortune and misery, we were not forgotten by him that formed and brought us out of nothing into being, nor did he suffer his own handiwork utterly to perish. By the good pleasure of our God and Father, and the cooperation of the Holy Ghost, the only-begotten Son, even the Word of God, which is in the bosom of the Father, being of one substance with the Father and with the Holy Ghost, he that was before all worlds, without beginning, who was in the beginning, and was with God even the Father, and was God, he, I say, condescended toward his servants with an unspeakable and incomprehensible condescension; and, being perfect God, was made perfect man, of the Holy Ghost, and of Mary the Holy Virgin and Mother of God, not of the seed of man, nor of the will of man, nor by carnal union, being conceived in the Virgin’s undefiled womb, of the Holy Ghost; as also, before his conception, one of the Archangels was sent to announce to the Virgin that miraculous conception and ineffable birth. For without seed was the Son of God conceived of the Holy Ghost, and in the Virgin’s womb he formed for himself a fleshy body, animate with a reasonable and intelligent soul, and thence came forth in one substance, but in two natures, perfect God and perfect man, and preserved undefiled, even after birth, the virginity of her that bore him. He, being made of like passions with ourselves in all things, yet without sin, took our infirmities and bare our sicknesses. For, since by sin death entered into the world, need was that he, that should redeem the world, should be without sin, and not by sin subject unto death.

「有人以为万有仅凭机缘运转，便教训人说没有神的护理，因为没有主宰治理。另有人引入命运，把万事都托付给人出生时的星宿。又有人敬拜许多受各样情欲支配的邪恶神祇，为的是要这些神祇替他们自己的情欲和可耻的行径辩护；他们为这些神祇捏塑造形，立起它们哑口的形像、无识的偶像，把它们安奉在庙宇里，向它们行敬礼，『事奉受造之物，过于造物的主』。有人敬拜日月星辰——那是神所安置，为要光照我们这属地的境界；这些东西没有灵魂，没有知觉，靠神的护理蒙光照、蒙维系，自己却不能成就任何事。又有人敬拜火与水，并其余的元素，这些同样是没灵没觉之物；而享有灵魂理性的人，竟不以为耻，去敬拜这类东西。又有人将敬拜归给走兽、爬物和四足之物，证明自己比所敬拜的更近兽性。又有人为卑贱无用的男人造像，称他们为神，其中一些唤作男的，一些唤作女的；又亲手把他们描述成奸淫的、杀人的、受忿怒嫉妒暴怒辖制的、弑父的、杀兄弟的、盗贼和强盗、瘸腿的和伤残的、行邪术的和癫狂的。另一些神明又被显为死了的、遭雷殛的、捶胸哀恸的、被人哀哭的、服在人为奴之轭下的、被放逐的，或为污秽可耻的结合而变作兽形的。人既从诸神自身得了把柄，就放胆用各样的不洁玷污自己。于是那时期，可怕的黑暗遮盖了我们的族类，『没有明白的，没有寻求神的』。

「在那世代中，惟独寻得亚伯拉罕一人，在属灵的官觉上刚强；他借着对受造万物的默观，认出了造物的主。他思想天、地、海，并日月等等，就希奇它们和谐的次序。他观看这世界和其中的万物，不能相信它是凭自己的力量受造、得维持的，也不能将这般美好的安排归给属地的元素或无生命的偶像。倒是在其中他认出了真神，明白他是万有的创造者和扶持者。神既悦纳他美好的智慧和正直的判断，就向他显现——不是照他本体的真相（因为受造之物不可能见神），乃是照他独能的方式，借着物质形像的种种显现；又将更完全的认识栽种在他里面，尊崇他，使他作自己的仆人。这亚伯拉罕又把自己所行的义传给儿女，教训他们认识真神。因此主喜悦使他的后裔多到无可衡量，称他们为『特作的子民』，领他们脱离埃及民族、脱离法老那一个暴君的辖制——是借着圣洁之人摩西和亚伦的手行的，他们蒙了预言的恩赐——行了奇异可畏的神迹奇事；又借这些惩罚埃及人，按他们邪恶所当受的；又领以色列人（因为从亚伯拉罕而出的百姓是这样称呼的）过红海，行在干地上，海水分开，左右作了墙垣。法老和埃及人追赶他们，跟着下到海里，水便回流，将他们全然淹没。此后四十年之久，他以极其大能的神迹和属天的显现，领百姓行走旷野，用天上的粮喂养他们，又将律法赐下——以神的指头写在石版上，在山上交付摩西——那律法是『将来美事的预表和影儿』，引领人离弃偶像和各样的邪恶，教训他们惟独敬拜独一的真神，专心行善。借着这样奇妙的作为，他领他们进入那美地，就是他先前应许赐给先祖亚伯拉罕、要赐给他后裔的。他在他们中间所行的，满有荣耀奇事、不可胜数的奇妙作为，若要述说，这任务便漫长了；他行这些，本是要将人类从一切不合法的敬拜和行为中拔出来，把人领回起初的境况。但即使这样，我们的本性仍被那可错的自由捆为奴仆，死亡在世人身上作了王，把众人交付魔鬼的暴虐，交付地狱的刑罚。

「我们既沉沦到这不幸悲惨的深渊，那造化我们、领我们从无有进入存在的，并没有忘记我们，也不容他自己手中的工作全然灭亡。因着我们神和父的美意，并圣灵的协力，那独生子，就是神的道，在父怀里的那位，与父与圣灵同一性体的，那在万世以先、无始的那位，那在太初就有的、与神——就是父——同在、自己就是神的，我说，这一位以无可言喻、无可测度的俯就，屈尊垂顾他的众仆人；他是完全的神，成了完全的人，由圣灵，由圣童女马利亚——神之母——而生；不是从人的种子，不是从人的意思，也不是借着肉体的结合，乃是因圣灵，在那童贞无瑕的胎中成孕；正如在他成孕以先，有一位天使长奉差遣，向那童女宣告这奇妙的成孕、莫可名状的诞生。因为神的儿子是因圣灵无种而成孕的；他在童女的胎里为自己形成了一个血肉的身体，配着一个有理性、有聪明的灵魂；他从那里出来，乃同属一个实体，却具两种本性，是完全的神，也是完全的人；并且在诞生之后，仍保全了那生他的童女的童贞，毫无玷污。他在凡事上与我们同具诸般情感，只是没有罪；他『代替我们的软弱，担当我们的疾病』。因为死既是借着罪进入世界，那要救赎世界的，就必须自己无罪，不因罪而服在死权之下。

“When he had lived thirty years among men, he was baptized in the river Jordan by John, an holy man, and great above all the prophets. And when he was baptized there came a voice from heaven, from God, even the Father, saying, ‘This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased,’ and the Holy Ghost descended upon him in likeness of a dove. From that time forth he began to do great signs and wonders, raising the dead, giving sight to the blind, casting out devils, healing the lame and maim, cleansing lepers, and everywhere renewing our outworn nature, instructing men both by word and deed, and teaching the way of virtue, turning men from destruction and guiding their feet toward life eternal. Wherefore also he chose twelve disciples, whom he called Apostles, and commanded them to preach the kingdom of heaven which he came upon earth to declare, and to make heavenly us who are low and earthly, by virtue of his Incarnation.

“But, through envy of his marvellous and divine conversation and endless miracles, the chief priests and rulers of the Jews (amongst whom also he dwelt, on whom he had wrought his aforesaid signs and miracles), in their madness forgetting all, condemned him to death, having seized one of the Twelve to betray him. And, when they had taken him, they delivered him to the Gentiles, him that was the life of the world, he of his free will consenting thereto; for he came for our sakes to suffer all things, that he might free us from sufferings. But when they had done him much despite, at the last they condemned him to the Cross. All this he endured in the nature of that flesh which he took from us, his divine nature remaining free of suffering: for, being of two natures, both the divine and that which he took from us, his human nature suffered, while his Godhead continued free from suffering and death. So our Lord Jesus Christ, being without sin, was crucified in the flesh, for he did no sin, neither was guile found in his mouth; and he was not subject unto death, for by sin, as I have said before, came death into the world; but for our sakes he suffered death in the flesh, that he might redeem us from the tyranny of death. He descended into hell, and having harrowed it, he delivered thence souls that had been imprisoned therein for ages long. He was buried, and on the third day he rose again, vanquishing death and granting us the victory over death: and he, the giver of immortality, having made flesh immortal, was seen of his disciples, and bestowed upon them peace, and, through them, peace on the whole human race.

“After forty days he ascended into heaven, and sitteth at the right hand of the Father. And he shall come again to judge the quick and the dead, and to reward every man according to his works. After his glorious Ascension into heaven he sent forth upon his disciples the Holy Ghost in likeness of fire, and they began to speak with other tongues as the Spirit gave them utterance. From thence by his grace they were scattered abroad among all nations, and preached the true Catholic Faith, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, and teaching them to observe all the commandments of the Saviour. So they gave light to the people that wandered in darkness, and abolished the superstitious error of idolatry. Though the enemy chafeth under his defeat, and even now stirreth up war against us, the faithful, persuading the fools and unwise to cling to the worship of idols, yet is his power grown feeble, and his swords have at last failed him by the power of Christ. Lo, in few words I have made known unto thee my Master, my God, and my Saviour; but thou shalt know him more perfectly, if thou wilt receive his grace into thy soul, and gain the blessing to become his servant.”

「他在人中间住了三十年，就在约旦河里受了约翰的洗；约翰是圣洁的人，比众先知都大。他受洗的时候，有声音从天上来，从神、就是父那里发出，说：『这是我的爱子，我所喜悦的。』圣灵仿佛鸽子，降在他身上。从那时起，他开始行大事、行神迹奇事：叫死人复活，使瞎子看见，赶出鬼魔，医治瘸腿的和伤残的，洁净患大麻风的，在各处更新我们这衰残的本性；他用言语和行为教训人，教导德行之道，叫人转离灭亡，把他们的脚引到永生的路上。因此他又拣选了十二个门徒，称他们为使徒，吩咐他们传扬他所到地上来宣告的天国，并靠着他人道成肉身的功效，使我们这些卑微属土的人成为属天的人。

「但是，犹太人的祭司长和官长——他也住在他们中间，又在他们身上行了上述的神迹奇事——因嫉妒他那奇妙属天的言行和无尽的奇迹，就在癫狂中忘了一切，把他定了死罪，又收拿了十二门徒中的一个来出卖他。他们拿住他之后，就把他——那本是世界之生命的——交给外邦人；这是他自愿同意的，因为他为我们的缘故来受这一切的苦难，好释放我们脱离苦难。他们多多地凌辱他，末了将他定罪，钉十字架。这一切，他是在那从我们所取的肉身之本性中忍受的，他的神性仍不受苦难：因为他既具两性——神性，并那从我们所取的本性——他的人性受苦，他的神格却始终无苦无死。这样，我们的主耶稣基督本是无罪的，却在肉身中被钉十字架，因为『他并没有犯罪，口里也没有诡诈』；他原不在死权之下，因为——如我先前所说——死是借着罪进入世界的；但他为我们的缘故在肉身中受了死，为要救赎我们脱离死亡的暴虐。他降在阴间，掳掠了它，把历代被囚其中的灵魂从那里释放出来。他被埋葬了，第三天复活，胜过了死亡，又将胜过死亡的胜利赐给我们；他，那赐不朽者，既使肉身成为不朽，就显给他的门徒看，将平安赐给他们，又借着他们，将平安赐给全人类。

「过了四十天，他升上高天，坐在父的右边。他还要再来，审判活人死人，照各人的行为报应各人。他荣耀升天之后，将圣灵以火的样式浇灌在门徒身上，他们就照圣灵所赐的口才，说起别国的话来。从那里起，他们靠他的恩典分散在万民之中，传扬那真实的大公信仰，奉父、子、圣灵的名给他们施洗，教训他们遵守救主的一切诫命。这样，他们光照了那在黑暗中流离的百姓，废除了敬拜偶像的迷信错谬。那仇敌虽然因败北而愤懑，甚至如今还向我们——信的人——挑动争战，劝诱愚妄无知的人持守对偶像的敬拜，但他的权势已经衰弱，他的刀剑也终因基督的大能，从他手中废掉了。看哪，我用寥寥数语，已将我的主、我的神、我的救主指示了你；但你若肯把他的恩典领受在你灵魂里，又得着那作他仆人的福分，就必更完全地认识他。」
