## XXXII / 三十二

===Original===
Theudas said unto him, "Behold, it is evident that our religion was instituted by many mighty wise men, and interpreters, marvellous in virtue and learning; and all the kings and rulers of the earth have received it as good and sure in every point. But that of the Galileans was preached by some country peasants, poor and common men, a mere handful, not exceeding twelve in number. How then should one prefer the preaching of these few obscure countrymen to the ordinance of the many that are mighty and brilliantly wise? What is the proof that your teachers be right and the others wrong?"

===Chinese===
特乌达斯对他说：「看哪，我们的宗教显然是由许多有权能的智士和诠释者所创立的，他们在德行和学问上都奇妙非凡；地上的君王和掌权者也无不领受了它，认它每一方面都良善而稳固。而加利利人的宗教，却是由几个乡野农夫传讲的——贫穷的平民，不过寥寥一小群，数目不超过十二人。这样，人怎能宁取这几个无闻乡人的传讲，而舍弃那许多有权能、有辉煌智慧之人的典章呢？你们的师傅对、别人错，凭何证明呢？」

===Original===
Again the king's son made answer, "Belike, Theudas, thou art the ass of the proverb, that heard but heeded not the harp; or rather the adder that stoppeth her ears, that she may not hear the voice of the charmers. Well, therefore, spake the prophet concerning thee, If the Ethiopian can change his skin, or the leopard his spots, then mayest thou also do good, that hast been taught to do evil. Thou fool and blind, why doth not the force of truth bring thee to thy senses? The very fact that your foul idols are commended by many men of marvellous wisdom, and established by kings, while the Gospel is preached by a few men of no mark, showeth the might of our religion and the weakness and deadliness of your wicked doctrines. Because your side, despite its having wise advocates and mighty champions, is dying down, and waxing weak, whilst our religion, though possessed of no human help, shineth from afar brighter than the sun, and hath won the fullness of the world. If it had been set up by orators and philosophers, and had had kings for its succour, thou that art evil wouldst have found occasion to declare that it was wholly of human power. But now, seeing, as thou dost, that the holy Gospel, though composed but by common fishermen, and persecuted by every tyrant, hath after this won the whole world—for its sound hath gone out into all lands, and its words into the ends of the world—what canst thou say but that it is a divine and unconquerable power establishing its own cause for the salvation of mankind? But what proof seekest thou, O fool, that thy prophets are liars and ours true, better than the truths I have told thee? Except thy cause had been vain talk and falsehood, it could not, possessing such human support as it did, have suffered loss and decline. For he saith, 'I have seen the ungodly in great power, and exalted like the cedars of Libanus: and I went by and lo, he was gone: and I sought him but his place could nowhere be found.'

===Chinese===
王子又回答说：「特乌达斯啊，恐怕你就是俗语所说的那头驴，听见了琴声，却不领会；或更像是那塞住耳朵的虺蛇，免得听见行法术者的声音。所以先知论到你的话，说得实在好：『埃塞俄比亚人岂能改变皮肤呢？豹岂能改变斑点呢？若能，你这受教行恶的也能行善了。』你这无知瞎眼的人哪，真理的力量为何不能叫你醒悟过来呢？你们污秽的偶像既被许多智慧非凡的人所称许，又被君王所坚立，而福音却由几个无名之辈传讲——这事实本身，正显明我们宗教的大能，并你们那邪恶教理的软弱与致命。因为你们这一方虽拥有智士为辩护人、勇士为斗士，却正渐渐消沉、日益衰微；我们的宗教虽无任何人的帮助，却自远方照耀，光明胜过太阳，并且赢得了普天之下。这福音若是由演说家和哲学家所建立，又有君王作它的援助，你这行恶的人原会寻得借口，宣称它全然是出于人的权能。但如今，你既亲眼看见：圣福音虽不过由寻常渔夫写成，又被各样暴君所逼迫，至终却赢得了全世界——『它的声音传遍天下，它的言语传到地极』——除了承认那是一种属神而不可征服的大能、为世人的救恩坚立它自己的事业以外，你还能说什么呢？愚妄人哪，你要寻求什么凭据，证明你们的先知是说谎的、我们的是真实的，胜过我所告诉你的这些真理呢？你们的事业若非虚谈与谎言，既有这样的人助，就不至于遭受亏损与衰微了。因为他说：『我见过恶人大有势力，高耸如利巴嫩的香柏树；我从那里经过，看哪，他已经不在了；我寻找他，却寻不见他的所在。』

===Original===
"Concerning you, the defenders of idolatry, were these words spoken by the prophet. For a very, very little while and your place shall not be found: but, like as the smoke vanisheth, and like as wax melteth in face of the fire, so shall ye fail. But, as touching the divine wisdom of the Gospel, thus saith the Lord, 'Heaven and earth shall pass away, but my words shall not pass away.' And again the Psalmist saith, 'Thou, Lord, in the beginning hast laid the foundation of the earth; and the heavens are the work of thy hands. They shall perish, but thou endurest; and they all shall wax old as doth a garment, and as a vesture shalt thou fold them up, and they shall be changed, but thou art the same, and thy years shall not fail!' And those divine preachers of the coming of Christ, those wise fishers of the world, whose nets drew all men from the depths of deceit, whom thou, in thy vileness and bondage to sin, dost vilify, did by signs and wonders and manifold powers shine as the sun in the world, giving sight to the blind, hearing to the deaf, motion to the lame, and life to the dead. Their shadows alone healed all the ailments of men. The devils, whom ye dread as gods, they not only cast forth from men's bodies, but even drave out of the world itself by the sign of the cross, whereby they destroyed all sorcery, and rendered witchcraft powerless. And these men, by curing every disease of man by the power of Christ, and renewing all creation, are rightly admired as preachers of truth by all men of sound mind. But what hast thou thyself to say of thy wise men and orators, whose wisdom God hath made foolish, the advocates of the devil? What worthy memorial have they bequeathed to the world? Tell me. And what canst thou tell of them but unreason and shamefulness, and vain craft that with glosing words concealeth the mire of their unsavoury worship?

===Chinese===
「先知这些话，正是论到你们这些偶像崇拜的护卫者说的。再过片时片时，你们的所在就必寻不见；但如烟之消散、如蜡之在火面前熔化，你们也必这样灭没。至于福音那属神的智慧，主如此说：『天地要废去，我的话却不能废去。』诗篇作者又说：『主啊，你起初立了地的根基，天也是你手所造的。天地都要灭没，你却要长存；天地都要像衣服渐渐旧了，你要将天地卷起来，像一件外衣，天地就都改变了；惟有你永不改变，你的年数没有穷尽！』还有那些传讲基督降临的神圣传道者、那些智慧的渔人——他们的网曾将众人从诡诈的深处拉上来——你这卑贱而受罪捆绑的人竟加以诋毁；他们曾借着神迹、奇事并各样的异能，如太阳一般在世间照耀，叫瞎子看见、聋子听见、瘸子行走、死人复活。单凭他们的影子，就医好了人的一切疾病。你们当作神敬畏的鬼魔，他们不仅从人的身体里赶出去，更借十字架的记号把它们从这世界本身驱逐出去，借此除灭了一切的邪术，使巫术归于无能为力。这些人既凭基督的大能医好了人的各样疾病，又更新了一切受造之物，就理当受一切神志清明的人尊崇，奉为真理的传道者。至于你那些智士和演说家——他们的智慧已蒙神使之变为愚拙，是魔鬼的辩护人——你自己还有什么可说的呢？他们给世界留下了什么值得记念的东西？你说说看。除了悖谬与可耻，除了那用谄媚巧言掩盖其污秽敬拜之泥沼的虚妄诡计，你还能论他们什么呢？

===Original===
"Moreover such of your poets as have been able to soar a little above this great madness have said, with more truth, that they, which are called gods, were men; and because certain of them had been rulers of regions and cities, and others had done something of no great account in their lifetime, men were so deceived as to call them gods. It standeth on record that the man Seruch12 was the first to bring in the use of images. For it is said that in the old times he honoured those who had achieved some memorable deed of courage, friendship, or any other such virtue with statues and pillars. But after generations forgat the intention of their ancestors: and, whereas it was only for remembrance sake that they had set up statues and pillars to the doers of noble deeds, now they were, little by little, led astray through the working of the prince of evil, the devil, and treated as immortal gods men of like passions and corruptible as themselves and further devised sacrifices and drink-offerings for them—the devils, thou mayest know, taking up their abode in these images and diverting to themselves these honours and sacrifices. Accordingly these devils persuade men, who refuse to have God in their knowledge, to consider them as gods for two reasons: first, that they may be glorified by this title (for they are puffed up with arrogance, and delight to be honoured as gods) next, that they may drag their poor dupes into the unquenchable fire prepared for themselves. Hence they teach men all iniquity and filthiness, seeing that they have once subjected themselves to their deceit. So when men had arrived at this pinnacle of evil, they, being darkened, set up every man an idol of his own vice and his own lust, and call it a god. They were abominable in their error, more abominable in the absurdity of the objects that they chose to worship, until the Lord came, and of his tender mercy redeemed us that trust in him from this wicked and deadly error, and taught men the true knowledge of God. For there is no salvation except in him, and there is none other God, neither in heaven, nor in earth, except him only, the Maker of all, who moveth all things by the word of his power: for he saith, 'By the word of the Lord were the heavens made steadfast, and all the power of them by the breath of his mouth,' and, 'All things were made by him, and without him was not anything made that was made.'"

===Chinese===
「再者，你们诗人中凡能稍为飞腾于这大癫狂之上的，都曾以更真的话说过：那些称为神的，原是人；因其中有人曾作地区与城邑的掌权者，又有人在世时行过一些无足轻重的事，世人竟受骗到称他们为神的地步。史有明载：西鹿（Seruch）12 其人首开立像之用。因为据说古时，他以雕像和柱碑尊荣那些成就了某种英勇、友谊或别样德行之可记念事迹的人。但后来的世代忘记了他们列祖的本意：先人给高贵作为者立像立碑，原只是为记念的缘故；后人却因那恶君——魔鬼——的运行，渐渐被引入迷途，把和他们自己一样有血气情欲、一样必朽的人奉为不朽的神明，更为他们制定了献祭与奠祭——那些鬼魔，你可以知道，就住进这些像里，把这些尊荣和祭物转归自己。于是这些鬼魔劝诱那些故意不认识神的人看它们为神，理由有二：第一，好叫它们可以因这名号得荣耀（它们因狂傲而自高，喜悦被人当作神尊荣）；其次，好叫它们能把那些可怜的受骗者拖进为它们自己所预备的不灭之火里。因此，人既一经服在它们的欺骗之下，它们就教训人行种种的不义与污秽。及至人达到了这邪恶的绝顶，他们既心里昏暗，便各人为自己的恶德和私欲立一个偶像，称它为神。他们的错谬是可憎的，他们所拣选敬拜之物的荒谬更是可憎——直到主来临，以他温柔的怜悯，把我们这些信靠他的人从这邪恶致命的错谬里救赎出来，并将神的真知识教训世人。因为除他以外，别无拯救；除了那造化万有、以他权能的命令托住万有的独一者以外，无论在天上、在地上，都再没有别的神：因为经上说：『诸天借耶和华的命而造，万象借他口中的气而成』；又说：『万物是借着他造的；凡被造的，没有一样不是借着他造的。』」

===Original===
When Theudas had heard these sayings, and seen that the word was full of divine wisdom, like one thunderstruck, he was smitten dumb. Now late in time, and with difficulty, came he to understand his own misery, for the word of salvation had touched the darkened vision of his heart, and there fell upon him deep remorse for his past sins. He renounced the error of his idols, and ran towards the light of godliness, and from henceforth departed from his miserable life, and made himself as bitter an enemy of vile affections and sorceries as he before had pledged himself their devoted friend, For at this season he stood up in the midst of the assembly,13 and cried with a loud voice, saying, "Verily, O king, the Spirit of God dwelleth in thy son. Verily, we are defeated, and have no further apology, and have no strength to face the words that he hath uttered. Mighty therefore, in sooth, is the God of the Christians: mighty is their faith: mighty are their mysteries."

Then he turned him round toward the king's son and said, "Tell me now, thou man, whose soul is enlightened, will Christ accept me, if I forsake my evil deeds and turn to him?" "Yea," said that preacher of truth; "Yea, he receiveth thee and all that turn to him. And he not only receiveth thee, but he goeth out to meet thee returning out of the way of iniquity, as though it were a son returning from a far country. And he falleth on his neck and kisseth him, and he strippeth him of the shameful robe of sin, and putteth on him a cloak of brightest glory, making mystic gladness for the powers on high, keeping feast for the return of the lost sheep. The Lord himself saith, 'There is exceeding great joy in heaven over one sinner that repenteth': and again, 'I am not come to call the righteous but sinners to repentance.' And he saith also by the Prophet, 'As I live, saith the Lord, I have no pleasure in the death of the sinner, and the ungodly, but that he should turn from his way and live. Turn ye, turn ye from your evil way. And why will ye die, O house of Israel?' For the wickedness of the wicked shall not hurt him in the day that he turneth from his wickedness, if he do righteousness and walk in the statutes of life, he shall surely live; he shall not die. None of his sins which he hath committed shall be remembered against him. Because he hath done the decree of righteousness, he shall live thereby. And again he crieth by the mouth of another prophet, 'Wash you, make you clean; put away the evil of your doings from before mine eyes; cease to do evil: learn to do well. Come now, and let us reason together: though your sins be as scarlet, I will make them white as snow; though they be red like crimson, I will make them white as wool.' Such therefore being the promises made by God to them that turn to him, tarry not, O thou man, nor make delay: but draw nigh to Christ, our loving God, and be enlightened, and thy face shall not be ashamed. For as soon as thou goest down into the laver of Holy Baptism, all the defilement of the old man, and all the burden of thy many sins, is buried in the water, and passeth into nothingness, and thou comest up from thence a new man, pure from all pollution, with no spot or wrinkle of sin upon thee; and thenceforward it is in thy power ever to keep for thyself the purity that thou gainest hereby through the tender mercy of our God."

When Theudas had been thus instructed, he went out immediately and gat him to his evil den, and took his magical books, and, because they were the beginnings of all evil, and the storehouses of devilish mysteries, burnt them with fire. And he betook himself to the cave of that same holy man, to whom Nachor also had resorted, and told him that which had befallen him, casting dust upon his head, and groaning deeply, and watering himself with his tears, and telling the aged man the full tale of his loathly deeds. He, well skilled in the saving of a soul and the snatching it from the jaw of the wily serpent, charmed away his sorrow with words of salvation, and pledged him forgiveness and promised him a merciful Judge. Then, after he had instructed and charged him to fast many days, he cleansed him in Holy Baptism. And all the days of his life Theudas heartily repented him of his misdeeds, with tears and sighs seeking the favour of God.

===Chinese===
特乌达斯听了这些话，见这话满有属神的智慧，便如遭雷击，哑口无言。只是为时已晚，又历尽艰难，他才明白了自己的悲惨，因为救恩的道触及了他心中昏昧的眼目，他便为已往的罪深自痛悔。他弃绝了他诸偶像的错谬，奔向敬虔之光，从此离开他那悲惨的生活，使自己成为邪恶情欲与邪术的死敌，正如他先前曾立誓作它们的挚友一般。因为就在这时节，他当着会众站立起来，13 放声呼喊说：「王啊，实在地，神的灵住在你儿子里面。实在地，我们败了，再无辩词，也没有力量面对他口中所出的话。所以诚然，基督徒的神是大有能力的：他们的信仰是大有能力的：他们的奥迹也是大有能力的。」

随后他转身向着王子，说：「现在请告诉我，你这灵魂已经蒙光照的人，我若离弃我的恶行、归向他，基督肯收纳我么？」那真理的传道者说：「肯，他收纳你，也收纳一切归向他的人。他不但收纳你，还要出来迎接你这从不义之途上转回的人，如同儿子从远方归来一样；他伏在你的颈项上，连连与你亲嘴，给你脱去那羞辱的罪袍，给你披上至荣耀的衣裳，为在高处的众权能者成就奥秘的喜乐，为那失丧之羊的归回守节设筵。主亲自说：『一个罪人悔改，在天上要为他有极大的喜乐』；又说：『我来本不是召义人，乃是召罪人悔改。』他又借先知说：『主说：我指着我的永生起誓，我断不喜悦罪人死，也不喜悦不虔的人死，惟喜悦他转离自己的道而活。你们转回吧，转回离开你们的恶道。以色列家啊，你们为何要死呢？』恶人的恶，在他转离恶行的那日，必不能害他；他若行公义、遵行生命的律例，就必存活，必不至死。他所犯的一切罪必不被记念在他身上；他既行了公义的定例，就必因此存活。他又借另一位先知的口呼喊说：『你们要洗濯、自洁，从我眼前除掉你们的恶行；要止住作恶，学习行善。你们来，我们彼此辩论：你们的罪虽像朱红，必变成雪白；虽红如丹颜，必白如羊毛。』神既向归向他的人应许了这些，你这人哪，就不要踌躇，不要耽延；只要亲近基督——我们那满有爱心的神——就必蒙光照，你的脸必不蒙羞。因为你一走下圣洗礼的洗濯盆，旧人一切的玷污、你众多罪孽的一切重担，就都埋葬在水里，归于无有；你从那里上来，成了新人，洁净而无染，身上没有罪的玷污、斑点或皱纹；从此以后，你借着我们神温柔的怜悯所取得的这洁净，就常在你的能力之内，可以为自己永远持守。」

特乌达斯既受了这样的教导，立刻出去，回到他罪恶的巢穴，取出他的巫术书——这些书既是一切邪恶的根由，又是鬼魔奥秘的库藏——就用火焚烧了。他又去到那位圣者所住的洞窟，就是纳霍尔先前也曾投奔的那一位，将所遭遇的事告诉他：把尘土撒在自己头上，深深地呻吟，以泪洗面，把那老人所当听的他一切可憎的作为，原原本本向他述说。那位圣者深谙拯救灵魂、从狡诈之蛇的颚中将它夺回之道，就用救恩的言语驱散他的忧伤，向他担保必蒙赦免，又应许他有一位施行怜悯的审判者。然后他教导他、嘱咐他禁食多日，就在圣洗礼中洁净了他。特乌达斯一生的年日，都为自己的恶行诚心悔改，以眼泪叹息寻求神的恩惠。
