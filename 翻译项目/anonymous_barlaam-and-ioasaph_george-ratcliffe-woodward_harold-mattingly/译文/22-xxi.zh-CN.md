## XXI / 二十一

===Original===
With such moral words did the reverend elder exhort the king’s son, and then withdrew to his own hospice. But the young prince’s servants and tutors marvelled to see the frequency of Barlaam’s visits to the palace; and one of the chiefest among them, whom, for his fidelity and prudence, the king had set over his son’s palace, named Zardan, said to the prince, “Thou knowest well, sir, how much I dread thy father, and how great is my faith toward him: wherefore he ordered me, for my faithfulness, to wait upon thee. Now, when I see this stranger constantly conversing with thee, I fear he may be of the Christian religion, toward which thy father hath a deadly hate; and I shall be found subject to the penalty of death. Either then make known to thy father this man’s business, or in future cease to converse with him. Else cast me forth from thy presence, that I be not blameable, and ask thy father to appoint another in my room.”

The king’s son said unto him, “This do, Zardan, first of all. Sit thou down behind the curtain, and hear his communication with me: and then thus will I tell thee what thou oughtest to do.”

===Chinese===
可敬的长老就用这样的道德训言劝勉了王的儿子，随后退回自己的寓所。年轻的王子有众仆役与师傅在侧，见巴拉阿姆这样频频进宫，都甚诧异。其中有位居于首位的，王因他的忠信与谨慎，立他掌管王子的宫邸，这人名叫扎尔丹（Zardan），他对王子说：「我主，你深知我何等惧怕你的父亲，也深知我向他存着何等的忠心：正因这忠心，他吩咐我来侍奉你。如今我见这外方人不住地与你交谈，就惧怕他或许是属基督教的人——你父亲对那教是怀着死仇的；那样，我就要被查出难逃死罪的刑罚。所以，你或是把这人的事向你父亲陈明，或是从今以后不再与他交谈；不然，就把我从你面前撵出去，使我没有可责之处，再求你父亲另立一人代替我的职分。」

王的儿子对他说：「扎尔丹，你先要这样行：你且坐在帷幔后面，听他与我的谈论；然后我必告诉你，你当怎样行。」

===Original===
So when Barlaam was about to enter into his presence, Ioasaph hid Zardan within the curtain, and said to the elder, “Sum me up the matter of thy divine teaching, that it may the more firmly be implanted in my heart.” Barlaam took up his parable and uttered many sayings touching God, and righteousness toward him, and how we must love him alone with all our heart, and with all our soul, and with all our mind, and keep his commandments with fear and love: and how he is the Maker of all things visible and invisible. Thereon he called to remembrance the creation of the first man, the command given unto him, and his transgression thereof, and the sentence pronounced by the Creator for this transgression. Then he reckoned up in order the good things wherefrom we excluded ourselves by the disannulling of his commandment. Again he made mention of the many grievous misfortunes that unhappily overtook man, after the loss of the blessings. Besides this he brought forward God’s love toward mankind; how our Maker, heedful of our salvation, sent forth teachers and prophets proclaiming the Incarnation of the Only-begotten. Then he spake of the Son, his dwelling among men, his deeds of kindness, his miracles, his sufferings for us thankless creatures, his Cross, his spear, his voluntary death; finally, of our recovery and recall, our return to our first good estate; after this, of the kingdom of heaven awaiting such as are worthy thereof; of the torment in store for the wicked; the fire that is not quenched, the never ending darkness, the undying worm, and all the other tortures which the slaves of sin have laid up in store for themselves. When he had fully related these matters, he ended his speech with moral instruction, and dwelt much upon purity of life, and utterly condemned the vanity of things present, and proved the utter misery of such as cleave thereto, and finally made an end with prayer. And therewith he prayed for the prince, that he might hold fast the profession of the Catholic Faith without turning and without wavering, and keep his life blameless and his conversation pure, and so ending with prayer again withdrew to his hospice.

===Chinese===
巴拉阿姆将要进来见他的时候，约阿萨弗便把扎尔丹藏在帷幔之内，对长老说：「请把你神圣教训的要义为我总括述说，使它更坚固地栽植在我心里。」巴拉阿姆就提起诗歌说，发出许多讲论：论到神，论到人向他所当尽的义，论到我们当如何尽心、尽性、尽意爱他、惟独爱他，又当怀着敬畏与爱谨守他的诫命；并论到他是那可见与不可见万物的创造主。随后他追想起初那人受造的事、所领受的诫命、他对诫命的干犯，以及创造主因这过犯所宣判的判语。接着他依次数算：我们因废掉他的诫命，便将自己排除在外的那些美福。他又提到人既失丧了那些福分，此后不幸临到人的许多沉重灾殃。此外，他又陈明神对世人的慈爱：我们的创造主如何顾念我们的救恩，差遣众教师与众先知出去，传扬独生子道成肉身的事。然后他讲到子：他怎样住在人间，他的恩慈作为，他的神迹，他为我们这些忘恩受造之物所受的苦难，他的十字架，他的枪，他甘愿受的死；末了，又讲我们的复原与挽回，我们归回起初美好的境况；再后，又讲那等候配得之人的天国，并那为恶人积存的刑罚：不灭的火、不止息的黑暗、不死的虫，并罪的奴仆们为自己积存的一切别的酷刑。他把这些事一一讲论完备了，就以道德的训诲结束他的讲论，多重于生活的纯洁，痛斥今世诸事的虚空，又证明确实紧贴这些事的人是何等全然悲惨，末了以祷告作结。他随即为王子祷告，愿他毫不偏转、毫不摇动地持守对大公信仰的认信，保守一生无可指摘、行为纯洁。这样，他又以祷告结束，退回自己的寓所。

===Original===
But the king’s son called Zardan forth, and, to try his disposition, said unto him, “Thou hast heard what sort of discourses this babbler maketh me, endeavouring to be-jape me with his specious follies, and rob me of this pleasing happiness and enjoyment, to worship a strange God.” Zardan answered, “Why hath it pleased thee, O prince, to prove me that am thy servant? I wot that the words of that man have sunk deep into thine heart; for, otherwise, thou hadst not listened gladly and unceasingly to his words. Yea, and we also are not ignorant of this preaching. But from the time when thy father stirred up truceless warfare against the Christians, the men have been banished hence, and their teaching is silenced. But if now their doctrine commend itself unto thee, and if thou have the strength to accept its austerity, may thy wishes be guided straight toward the good! But for myself, what shall I do, that am unable to bear the very sight of such austerity, and through fear of the King am divided in soul with pain and anguish? What excuse shall I make, for neglecting his orders, and giving this fellow access unto thee?”

The King’s son said unto him, “I knew full well that in none other wise could I requite thee worthily for thy much kindness, and therefore have I tasked myself to make known unto thee this more than human good, which doth even exceed the worth of thy good service, that thou mightest know to what end thou wast born, and acknowledge thy Creator, and, leaving darkness, run to the light. And I hoped that when thou heardest thereof thou wouldst follow it with irresistible desire. But, as I perceive, I am disappointed of my hope, seeing that thou art listless to that which hath been spoken. But if thou reveal these secrets to the king my father, thou shalt but distress his mind with sorrows and griefs. If thou be well disposed to him, on no account reveal this matter to him until a convenient season.” Speaking thus, he seemed to be only casting seed upon the water; for wisdom shall not enter into a soul void of understanding.

===Chinese===
王的儿子随后叫扎尔丹出来，为要试验他的心性，就对他说：「你听见这饶舌的人向我讲论的是些什么话了：他企图用似是而非的愚妄戏弄我，要夺去我这可喜悦的幸福与享受，去敬拜一位生疏的神。」扎尔丹回答说：「王子啊，你何苦喜悦试探我这作你仆人的呢？我知道那人的话已经深深沉入你心里；不然，你断不会这样欢喜、这样不间断地听他的话。诚然，这所传的道我们也并非不知道。但自从你父亲向基督徒挑起不休的战事以来，那些人都已被逐离此地，他们的教训也都被禁声了。如今他们的道理若在你眼中蒙悦纳，你若有力接受它的严苛，愿你的心愿蒙引导，直趋向那善！至于我自己，我当怎样行呢？我这样的人，连见一见那严苛都担受不起，又因惧怕王，魂里痛苦忧伤，如同撕裂；我疏忽了王的命令，又放这人来见你，还能有什么托辞呢？」

王的儿子对他说：「我深知，你向我施了这么多的恩慈，我无论怎样都难报答得尽；所以我勉力定意，要将这超乎人间之上的善事指示你——它甚至超过你忠心服事的价值——好叫你知道自己为何而生，认识你的创造主，离弃黑暗，奔向光明。我又指望你听见了，便以不能抗拒的恋慕跟从它。但如今我看出来，我的指望落了空，因为你对所听的话无动于衷。只是你若将这些隐秘事告诉王我父亲，不过叫他的心被忧愁悲哀困迫罢了。你若真对他存好意，无论如何不可将这事告诉他，直等到合宜的时候。」他这样说话，就像只把种子撒在水面上；因为智慧总不进入无悟性的灵魂。

===Original===
Upon the morrow came Barlaam and spake of his departure: but Ioasaph, unable to bear the separation, was distressed at heart, and his eyes filled with tears. The elder made a long discourse, and adjured him to continue unshaken in good works, and with words of exhortation established his heart, and begged him to send him cheerfully on his way; and at the same time he foretold that they should shortly be at one, never to be parted more. But Ioasaph, unable to impose fresh labours on the elder, and to restrain his desire to be on his way, and suspecting moreover that the man Zardan might make known his case to the King and subject him to punishment, said unto Barlaam, “Since it seemeth thee good, my spiritual father, best of teachers and minister of all good to me, to leave me to live in the vanity of the world, while thou journeyest to thy place of spiritual rest, I dare no longer let and hinder thee. Depart therefore, with the peace of God for thy guardian, and ever in thy worthy prayers, for the Lord’s sake, think upon my misery, that I may be enabled to overtake thee, and behold thine honoured face forever. But fulfil this my one request; since thou couldest not receive aught for thy fellow monks, yet for thyself accept a little money for sustenance, and a cloak to cover thee.” But Barlaam answered and said unto him, “Seeing that I would not receive aught for my brethren (for they need not grasp at the world’s chattels which they have chosen to forsake), how shall I acquire for myself that which I have denied them? If the possession of money were a good thing, I should have let them share it before me. But, as I understand that the possession thereof is deadly, I will hazard neither them nor myself in such snares.”

===Chinese===
次日，巴拉阿姆来了，说起他离别的事；约阿萨弗却受不住这分离，心里伤痛，双眼满泪。长老作了长长的讲论，切切嘱咐他在善工上坚定不移，又用劝勉的话坚固他的心，求他欢欢喜喜地送他上路；同时又预言，他们不久必再合一，永不再分离。但约阿萨弗既不忍再将新的劳苦加在长老身上，又拦不住他那要上路的热望，更疑心扎尔丹这人或许会把他的事禀告王、使他受刑罚，就对巴拉阿姆说：「我的属灵之父、最好的教师、向我施行诸般善益的人啊，你既看为好，要留下我活在世界的虚空中，自己往你属灵安息之所去，我就不敢再留难阻拦你了。所以你去吧，愿神的平安作你的护卫；又愿你在配得的祷告中，为主的缘故，记念我这苦情，使我能得着能力赶上你，永远瞻仰你可敬的面容。只是求你应允我这一个请求：你既不肯为你的同道修士们收受什么，就为你自己收下少许养身的银钱，并一件遮身的外袍吧。」巴拉阿姆回答他说：「我既不肯为我的众弟兄收受什么（因为他们既已拣选撇弃世上的财物，就无须再攫取它们），我怎能为我自己获取我曾拒绝给他们的东西呢？钱财的拥有若是件好事，我本该叫他们先我同得。但我知道那拥有是致命的，就不愿叫他们、也不愿叫我自己冒这等网罗的危险。」

===Original===
But when Ioasaph had failed once again to persuade Barlaam, ’twas but a sign for a second petition, and he made yet another request, that Barlaam should not altogether overlook his prayer, nor plunge him in utter despair, but should leave him that stiff shirt and rough mantle, both to remind him of his teacher’s austerities and to safeguard him from all the workings of Satan, and should take from him another cloak instead, in order that “When thou seest my gift,” said he, “thou mayest bear my lowliness in remembrance.”

But the elder said, “It is not lawful for me to give thee my old and worn out vestment, and take one that is new, lest I be condemned to receive here the recompense of my slight labour. But, not to thwart thy willing mind, let the garments given me by thee be old ones, nothing different from mine own.” So the king’s son sought for old shirts of hair, which he gave the aged man, rejoicing to receive his in exchange, deeming them beyond compare more precious than any regal purple.

Now saintly Barlaam, all but ready for to start, spake concerning his journey, and delivered Ioasaph his last lesson, saying, “Brother beloved, and dearest son, whom I have begotten through the Gospel, thou knowest of what King thou art the soldier, and with whom thou hast made thy covenant. This thou must keep steadfastly, and readily perform the duties of thy service, even as thou didst promise the Lord of all in the script of thy covenant, with the whole heavenly host present to attest it, and record the terms; which if thou keep, thou shalt be blessed. Esteem therefore nought in the present world above God and his blessings. For what terror of this life can be so terrible as the Gehenna of eternal fire, that burneth and yet hath no light, that punisheth and never ceaseth? And which of the goodly things of this world can give such gladness as that which the great God giveth to those that love him? Whose beauty is unspeakable, and power invincible, and glory everlasting; whose good things, prepared for his friends, exceed beyond comparison all that is seen; which eye hath not seen, nor ear heard, neither have entered into the heart of man: whereof mayest thou be shown an inheritor, preserved by the mighty hand of God!”

===Chinese===
约阿萨弗又一次未能说服巴拉阿姆，这不过成了他再次恳求的记号，他又提出另一个请求：求巴拉阿姆不要全然不顾他的祷告，不要叫他陷于全然绝望，只求把那件硬扎的粗毛衣和粗糙的外氅留给他——既可叫他记念教师的严苛操练，又可护卫他脱离撒但的一切运行——另换一件外袍带去，好叫「你看见我的礼物，」他说，「就记念我的卑微。」

长老却说：「我不可把我这旧而敝坏的衣服给你，另取一件新的，免得我在这里就被定罪，领受我微小劳苦的报偿。但为不拂逆你的美意，你给我的衣服须是旧的，与我自己的毫无分别。」于是王的儿子寻来旧的粗毛衣衫，给了这位老人，欢然换得他的衣服，看那衣服比任何帝王的紫袍都宝贵，无可比拟。

圣洁的巴拉阿姆行装已齐、将要启程，就论到他的行程，向约阿萨弗讲了他末了的一课，说：「亲爱的弟兄、至爱的儿子、我在福音里所生的啊，你知道你是哪一位王的军兵，你曾与谁立了约。这约你必须坚定持守，甘心履行你当差的职分，正如你在立约的书卷上向万有之主所应许的——当时全体天军在场作证，记录了那些条款；你若遵守，就必蒙福。所以，在今世万物之中，你不要看重任何过于神和他的福分。因为今生的什么威吓，能可怕得过那永火的地狱呢？它焚烧，却没有光；它刑罚，却永不止息。今世的什么美物，能赐下像大神赐给爱他之人的那样的喜乐呢？他的美是无法言说的，他的能力无可敌挡，他的荣耀存到永远；他为朋友所预备的那些美物，远超一切眼见之物，无可比拟——是眼睛未曾看见、耳朵未曾听见、人心也未曾想到的。愿你也得显为那美物的承受者，蒙神大能的手保守！」

===Original===
Here the king’s son burst into tears of pain and vexation, unable to bear the parting from a loving father and excellent teacher. “And who,” quoth he, “shall fill thy place, O my father? And whom like unto thee shall I find to be shepherd and guide of my soul’s salvation? What consolation may I find in my loss of thee? Behold thou hast brought me, the wicked and rebellious servant, back to God, and set me in the place of son and heir! Thou hast sought me that was lost and astray on the mountain, a prey for every evil beast, and folded me amongst the sheep that had never wandered. Thou hast shown me the direct road to truth, bringing me out of darkness and the shadow of death, and, changing the course of my feet from the slippery, deadly, crooked and winding pathway, hast ministered to me great and marvellous blessings, whereof speech would fail to recount the exceeding excellence. Great be the gifts that thou receivest at God’s hand, on account of me who am small! And may the Lord, who in the rewards of his gifts alone overpasseth them that love him, supply that which is lacking to my gratitude!”

Here Barlaam cut short his lamentation, and rose and stood up to pray, lifting up his either hand, and saying, “O God and Father of our Lord Jesu Christ, which didst illuminate the things that once were darkened, and bring this visible and invisible creation out of nothing, and didst turn again this thine handiwork, and sufferedst us not to walk after our foolishness, we give thanks to thee and to thy Wisdom and Might, our Lord Jesu Christ, by whom thou didst make the worlds, didst raise us from our fall, didst forgive us our trespasses, didst restore us from wandering, didst ransom us from captivity, didst quicken us from death by the precious blood of thy Son our Lord. Upon thee I call, and upon thine only begotten Son, and upon the Holy Ghost. Look upon this thy spiritual sheep that hath come to be a sacrifice unto thee through me thine unworthy servant, and do thou sanctify his soul with thy might and grace. Visit this vine, which was planted by thy Holy Spirit, and grant it to bear fruit, the fruit of righteousness. Strengthen him, and confirm in him thy covenant, and rescue him from the deceit of the devil. With the wisdom of thy good Spirit teach him to do thy will, and take not thy succour from him, but grant unto him, with me thine unprofitable servant, to become an inheritor of thine everlasting bliss, because thou art blessed and glorified forever, Amen.”

===Chinese===
说到这里，王的儿子痛楚悲愤，眼泪夺眶而出，受不住与这位慈父、这位至善的教师分离。「我的父啊，」他说，「谁能填补你的位置呢？我到哪里再寻得一个像你的人，作我灵魂救恩的牧人和引导者呢？我失去了你，还能得着什么安慰呢？看哪，我这恶而悖逆的仆人，你把我领回神面前，安放在儿子和后嗣的地位上！你寻找我这迷失流荡在山上、作了各样恶兽之掳食的羊，把我收拢在从未走迷的羊群中间。你将通往真理的直路指示我，领我出离黑暗和死荫之地，又把我脚步的行径从那滑跌、致命、弯曲而崎岖的路上改换过来，将伟大而奇妙的福分施行给我——那福分何等超绝，言语述说不尽。因着我这微小的人，愿你在神手中领受的恩赏何等浩大！又愿主补足我感谢中的缺欠——因为在恩赐的赏报上，惟独他所赐给爱他之人的，总过于他们所配得的！」

说到这里，巴拉阿姆截住了他的哀声，站起来祷告，举起双手，说：「神啊，我们主耶稣基督的父，你曾使那一度昏暗了的得着光照，又将这可见与不可见的造化从无有中领出，又使你这手所造的归回，不容我们随自己的愚妄而行：我们感谢你，又感谢你的智慧与大能、我们的主耶稣基督——你曾借着他创造诸世界，叫我们从跌倒处起来，赦免我们的过犯，从流迷中恢复我们，从被掳中赎出我们，借你儿子我们主的宝血叫我们从死里活过来。我呼求你，并呼求你的独生子，呼求圣灵。求你垂看你这属灵的羊——他借着我这不配的仆人，成了献与你的祭物——求你以你的大能和恩典使他的魂成圣。求你眷顾这棵葡萄树，就是你的圣灵所栽种的，赐它结果子，结仁义的果子。求你坚固他，在他身上坚定你的约，搭救他脱离魔鬼的诡诈。求你以你善灵的智慧指教他遵行你的旨意，不要将你的救助从他撤去；又赐他与我这无益的仆人一同作你永远福乐的承受者；因为你是可称颂、得荣耀，直到永远的。阿们。」

When that he had ended his prayer, he turned him round and embraced Ioasaph, now a son of his heavenly father, wishing him eternal peace and salvation, and he departed out of the palace, and went his way, rejoicing and giving thanks to God, who had well ordered his steps for good.

===Chinese===
他祷告完了，就转过身来，拥抱约阿萨弗——他如今已是那位天父的儿子了——愿他得着永远的平安与救恩。随后巴拉阿姆出了宫殿，上路而去，一路欢欢喜喜地称谢神，因为神使他的脚步稳妥，引他行在美善之中。
