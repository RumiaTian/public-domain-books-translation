## XXXIV / 三十四

===Original===
When king Abenner saw this, though late and loth, he came to his senses, and renounced his false gods with all their impotence and vain deceit. Again he called an assembly of his chief counsellors, and brought to light the thoughts of his heart. As they confirmed his words (for the dayspring from on high had visited them, the Saviour who had heard the prayer of his servant Ioasaph), it pleased the king to signify the same to his son. Therefore on the morrow he wrote a letter to Ioasaph, running thus:

> “King Abenner to his well-beloved son Ioasaph, greeting. Dearest son, many thoughts have been stealing into my soul, and rule it with a rod of iron. I see our state vanishing, like as smoke vanisheth, but thy religion shining brighter than the sun; and I have come to my senses, and know that the words which thou hast ever spoken unto me are true, and that a thick cloud of sin and wickedness did then cover us, so that we were unable to discern the truth, and recognize the Creator of all. Nay, but we shut our eyes, and would not behold the light which thou didst enkindle more brightly for us. Much evil did we do unto thee, and many of the Christians, alas! did we destroy; who, strengthened by the power that aided them, finally triumphed over our cruelty. But now we have removed that dense mist from our eyes, and see some small ray of truth, and there cometh on us repentance of our misdeeds. But a new cloud of despair would overshadow it; despair at the multitude of mine offences, because I am now abominable and unacceptable to Christ, being a rebel and a foeman unto him. What, then, sayest thou, dearest son, hereto? Make known to me thine answer, and teach me that am thy father what I should do, and lead me to the knowledge of my true weal.”

===Chinese===
阿本内尔王看见这事，虽已迟晚，虽出于不甘，终究醒悟过来，弃绝了他那些假神，并它们一切的无能与虚妄的欺哄。他又召集众首席谋臣开会议事，将心中的意念陈明出来。他们既都附和他的话（因为清晨的日光从高天临到了他们，就是那位已垂听他仆人约阿萨弗祷告的救主），王便乐意将这心意晓谕他的儿子。于是次日他写了一封信给约阿萨弗，信上说：

> 「阿本内尔王致至爱的儿子约阿萨弗，问安。极亲爱的儿子啊，许多意念悄悄潜入我的灵魂，用铁杖辖管它。我看见我们的景况如烟消散，你的信仰却照耀得比太阳更亮；我已醒悟过来，知道你历来对我所说的话都是真实的；又知道那时有一层罪恶的厚云遮盖我们，使我们不能分辨真理，不能认识万有的创造主。非但如此，我们还闭上了眼睛，不肯看你为我们点得更亮的那光。我们向你行了许多恶，又毁灭了许多基督徒——哀哉！他们却蒙那帮助他们的能力所坚固，终久胜过了我们的残暴。但如今我们已把那浓厚的迷雾从眼前除去，得见真理的一线微光，痛悔己行的恶事之心也临到我们。但有一朵绝望的新云又要把这悔悟遮蔽；是为着我过犯的众多而绝望，因为我如今在基督面前可憎可弃，我作了他的叛徒，是他的仇敌。极亲爱的儿子啊，对此你有何话说？求你把你的答复指示我，教训我这作你父亲的当怎样行，领我认识我真正的福祉。」

===Original===
When Ioasaph had received this letter, and read the words therein, his soul was filled with mingled joy and amazement. Forthwith he entered his closet, and falling on his face before the image of his Master, watered the ground with his tears, giving thanks to his Lord and confessing him, and tuning lips of exultation to sing an hymn of praise, saying:

“I will magnify thee, O God, my King, and I will praise thy name forever and ever. Great art thou O Lord, and marvellous-worthy to be praised, and of thy greatness there is no end. Who can express thy noble acts, or show forth all thy praise, who hast turned the hard rock into a standing water and the flint-stone into a springing well? For behold this my father’s flinty and more than granite heart is at thy will melted as wax; because thou art able of these stones to raise up children unto Abraham. I thank thee, Lord, thou lover of men, and God of pity, that thou hast been, and art, long-suffering towards our offences, and hast suffered us until now to go unpunished. Long have we deserved to be cast away from thy face, and made a byword on earth, as were the sinful inhabiters of the five cities, consumed with fire and brimstone; but thy marvellous long-suffering hath dealt graciously with us. I give thanks unto thee, vile and unworthy though I be, and insufficient of myself to glorify thy greatness. And, by thine infinite compassions, I pray thee, Lord Jesu Christ, Son and Word of the invisible Father, who madest all things by thy word, and sustainest them by thy will; who hast delivered us thine unworthy servants from the bondage of the archfiend our foe: thou that wast stretched upon the Rood, and didst bind the strong man, and award everlasting freedom to them that lay bound in his fetters: do thou now also stretch forth thine invisible and almighty hand, and, at the last, free thy servant my father from that cruel bondage of the devil. Show him full clearly that thou art the ever living true God, and only King, eternal and immortal. Behold, O Lord, with favourable and kindly eye, the contrition of my heart; and, according to thine unerring promise, be with me that acknowledge and confess thee the Maker and protector of all creation. Let there be a well of water within me springing up, and let utterance be given unto me that I may open my mouth, and a mind well fixed in thee, the chief cornerstone, that I, thine unprofitable servant, may be enabled to preach to my father, as is right, the mystery of thine Incarnation, and by thy power deliver him from the vain deceit of wicked devils, and bring him unto thee his God and Lord, who willest not the death of us sinners, but waitest for us to return and repent, because thou art glorified forever and ever. Amen.”

===Chinese===
约阿萨弗接了这信，读了其中的言语，他的魂就充满喜乐与惊奇交织之情。他随即进了内室，在他主的圣像面前俯伏于地，用眼泪浇湿了地面，向他主献上感谢、认他称颂他，又调正欢呼的嘴唇，唱出赞美的诗歌，说：

「神我的王啊，我要尊崇你，我要永永远远称颂你的名。主啊，你本为大，配受奇妙的赞美，你的伟大没有穷尽。你既使坚硬的磐石变为水池，使火石变为泉源，谁能传说你尊贵的作为？谁能述尽你的赞美呢？看哪，我父亲那火石般、比花岗岩更坚的心，已照你的旨意如蜡熔化，因为你能从这些石头中给亚伯拉罕兴起子孙来。主啊，爱世人的主、怜悯人的神，我感谢你，因你曾经、如今仍是长久忍耐我们的过犯，又容让我们直到今日未受刑罚。我们久已配得从你面前被弃绝，在地上成为笑谈，如那五城犯罪的居民被火与硫磺所灭一般；但你奇妙的恒久忍耐，却恩待了我们。我虽卑贱不配，靠自己也不足以荣耀你的伟大，我仍要感谢你。主耶稣基督啊，无形之父的儿子与道，你曾凭你的话创造万有，凭你的旨意托住万有；你曾救我们这些不配的仆人，脱离我们仇敌那魔王的奴役；你曾受钉伸展于十字架上，捆绑了那壮士，又将永远的自由判给那些躺卧在他锁链中的人：求你如今也伸出你无形而全能的手，至终救你的仆人我父亲，脱离魔鬼那残忍的奴役。向他显明得全然清楚：你是那永远活着的真神，是独一的王，永在不朽。主啊，求你以恩惠慈爱的眼目，垂顾我心中的痛悔；照你无误的应许，与我这承认你、认你作万有的创造者与保护者的人同在。愿我里面有泉源涌出水来，赐我口才，使我得以开口；赐我心志坚定于你——那房角的头块石头——使我这无用的仆人，得以照所当行的向我父亲传讲你道成肉身的奥秘，靠你的能力救他脱离邪恶魔鬼的虚妄欺哄，领他到你——他的神、他的主——面前；你不愿我们罪人死亡，只等候我们回转悔改，因为你要世世无穷、永远受荣耀。阿们。」

===Original===
When he had thus prayed, and received fullness of assurance that he should not miscarry in his desire, he took courage by the tender mercy of Christ, and arose thence, with his royal bodyguard, and arrived at his father’s palace. When it was told unto his father, “Thy son is come,” he went forth straightway for to meet him, and embraced and kissed him lovingly, and made exceeding great joy, and held a general feast in honour of the coming of his son. And afterward, they two were closeted together.

But how tell of all that the son spake with his father, and of all the wisdom of his speech? And what was that speech but the words put into his mouth by the Holy Ghost, by whom the fishermen enclosed the whole world in their nets for Christ and the unlearned are found wiser than the wise. This Holy Spirit’s grace and wisdom taught Ioasaph to speak with the king his father, enlightening him with the light of knowledge. Before now he had bestowed much labour to drag his father from superstitious error, leaving nothing unsaid and nothing undone to win him over, but he seemed to be twanging on a broken string, and speaking to deaf ears. But when the Lord looked upon the lowliness of his servant Ioasaph, and, in answer to his prayer, opened the closed gates of his father’s heart (for it is said, he will fulfil the desire of them that fear him, and will hear their cry), then the king easily understood the things that were spoken; so that, when a convenient season came, through the grace of Christ, this son triumphed over those evil spirits that had lorded it over the soul of his father, and clean freed him from their error, and made the word of salvation clearly known unto him, and joined him to the living God on high.

===Chinese===
他这样祷告了，又得了满有确据的把握，知道他的心愿必不至落空，就靠基督的温柔怜悯壮起胆来，从那里起身，带着王的卫队，来到他父亲的宫殿。有人禀报他父亲说：「你儿子来了。」他便立刻出去迎接，与他亲嘴拥抱，满怀深情，欢喜极大，又设摆盛大的筵席，庆贺儿子的来临。此后，他们二人便密室独处，共相谈论。

然而，儿子与父亲所谈的一切，他言谈中的一切智慧，怎能述说得尽呢？他的话语，岂不正是圣灵放在他口中的言语么——渔夫们正是靠着这位圣灵，为基督把全世界圈在他们的网里，叫没有学问的显为比有智慧的更有智慧。正是这圣灵的恩典和智慧，教导约阿萨弗与王他父亲说话，用知识的光光照他。在此以前，他曾下过许多劳苦，要把父亲从迷信的错谬中拉出来；为要得着他，凡当说的没有一句不说，凡当行的没有一样不行；然而他宛如拨动一根断弦，宛如对聋耳说话。及至主垂顾他仆人约阿萨弗的卑微，照他所求的应允他，打开了他父亲心中关闭的门（因为经上说：他必成就敬畏他之人的心愿，也必听他们的呼求），王就容易明白所讲论的事；及至合宜的时机来到，这儿子就借着基督的恩典，胜过了那些曾在他父亲灵魂上称王管辖的恶灵，把他从他们的错谬中全然释放，使救恩之道清楚显明于他，并将他连于在高天永活的神。

===Original===
Ioasaph took up his tale from the beginning, and expounded to his father great and marvellous things which he knew not, which he had never heard with the ears of his heart; and he told him many weighty sayings concerning God, and showed him righteousness: to wit that there is no other God in heaven above, nor in the earth beneath, except the one God, revealed in the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost. And he made known unto him many mysteries of divine knowledge; and amongst them he told him the history of creation, visible and invisible, how the Creator brought everything out of nothing, and how he formed man after his own image and likeness and endowed him with power of free will, and gave him Paradise to his enjoyment, charging him only to abstain from one thing, the tree of knowledge; and how, when man had broken his commandment, he banished him out of Paradise; and how man, fallen from union with God, stumbled into these manifold errors, becoming the slave of sins, and subject unto death through the tyranny of the devil, who, having once taken men captive, hath made them utterly forget their Lord and God, and hath persuaded them to serve him instead, by the abominable worshipping of idols. So our Maker, moved with compassion, through the goodwill of the Father, and the cooperation of the Holy Ghost, was pleased, for our sakes, to be born of an holy Virgin, Mary, the mother of God, and he, that cannot suffer, was acquainted with sufferings. On the third day he rose again from the dead, and redeemed us from our first penalty, and restored to us our first glory. When he ascended into the heavens, from whence he had descended, he raised us up together with him; and thence, we believe that he shall come again, to raise up his own handiwork; and he will recompense every man according to his works. Moreover Ioasaph instructed his father concerning the kingdom of heaven that awaiteth them that are worthy thereof, and the joy unspeakable. Thereto he added the torment in store for the wicked, the unquenchable fire, the outer darkness, the undying worm and whatsoever other punishment the servants of sin have laid up in store for themselves.

All these things set he forth in many words, which bore witness that the grace of the Spirit was dwelling richly within him. Then he described the uncharted sea of the love of God towards mankind, and how he is ready to accept the repentance of them that turn to him; and how there is no sin too great for his tender mercy, if we will but repent. And when he had confirmed these truths by many an example, and testimony of Scripture, he made an end of speaking.

===Chinese===
约阿萨弗便从头讲起，向父亲讲解了许多又大又奇妙的事，是他所不知道、心中的耳朵从未听过的；又将许多郑重关乎神的话告诉他，把公义指示他，就是说：在天上，在地上，除了在父、子、圣灵里显明的那独一的神以外，再无别神。他又使他知道神圣知识的许多奥秘；其中，他讲述了看得见与看不见之万有的受造历史：创造主怎样从无有中造出一切；怎样照自己的形像、按自己的样式造人，赋他自由意志的能力，将乐园赐给他享受，只吩咐他一件事须禁戒，就是分别善恶树；人违了诫命之后，他怎样将他逐出乐园；人既从与神的联合中坠落，又怎样失脚陷入这诸般的错谬，作了罪的奴仆，在魔鬼的暴虐辖制下服于死亡——那曾一次掳掠了人的，就叫他们全然忘记他们的主、他们的神，又劝服他们转去事奉他，就是借着那可憎的敬拜偶像之礼。于是我们的造物主动了怜悯，借着父的美意与圣灵的同工，乐意为我们从圣童女、神之母马利亚而生；那不能受苦的，也亲历了苦难。第三天，他从死里复活，救赎我们脱离那起初的刑罚，恢复了我们起初的荣耀。他升上诸天——他原是从那里降下的——又叫我们与他一同升上高天；我们信他必再从那里来，叫他自己手中的工作复活过来；他要照各人所行的报应各人。此外，约阿萨弗又教训他的父亲，论到那为配得之人存留的天国，和那说不出来的喜乐；又加上那为恶人存留的刑罚：不灭的火，外边的黑暗，不死的虫，并罪的仆人们为自己积存的一切别的刑罚。

这一切事，他用许多话一一陈明；这些话见证圣灵的恩典丰盛地住在他里面。随后他描绘神对世人的爱——那未经测绘过的大海；描绘他怎样随时乐于接纳归向他之人的悔改；只要我们肯悔改，在他温柔的怜悯面前，就没有甚么罪大到不能赦免。他用许多例证和圣经的见证坚立了这些真理之后，就结束了讲论。
