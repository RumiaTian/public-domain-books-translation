===Original===
(From the *Smith City Mirror*, June 26, 1927)

Police are searching today for Dr. J. E. Lawlor, well-known physician and scientist, following a report from his secretary, James Brock, that he had disappeared from his home at 2100 Van Ness Avenue.

According to Brock, Dr. Lawlor locked himself into his private laboratory twelve days ago, ordering his servants not to disturb him, and to send food down by means of a dumbwaiter. As he had followed this plan several times before while working on experiments, Brock complied with his request. The time set was ten days and when there had been no response from the laboratory during the two days following the elapse of this period, Brock feared some accident and, with the help of Georges Dmitri, Dr. Lawlor’s cook, and Emil Bonner, his chauffeur, he forced the door this morning and found that the doctor was gone.

===Chinese===
（载《史密斯城镜报》（*Smith City Mirror*），1927 年 6 月 26 日）

警方今日正在搜寻知名医生、科学家 J. E. 劳洛尔（Dr. J. E. Lawlor）的下落——据其秘书詹姆斯·布罗克（James Brock）报告，劳洛尔医生已从他位于范尼斯大道（Van Ness Avenue）2100 号的住所中失踪。

据布罗克所述，劳洛尔医生十二天前把自己锁进了私人实验室，吩咐仆人们不得打扰，饭食一律用食物升降机送下去。由于他从前埋头做实验时也曾几度照此办理，布罗克便依言照办。约定的期限是十天；期满之后接连两天，实验室里毫无动静，布罗克生怕出了意外，今晨在劳洛尔医生的厨师乔治·德米特里（Georges Dmitri）和司机埃米尔·邦纳（Emil Bonner）的协助下撬开了房门，发现医生已不见踪影。

===Original===
A weird angle is added to the incident by the dead body of a large ape which Brock found in a corner of the disordered laboratory. Although Dr. Lawlor was known to be interested in natural history and to have conducted several experiments with animals recently, Brock stated that he was sure the ape was not in the laboratory when it was closed twelve days ago. The table was covered with papers, which have been turned over to the police. Brock, Dmitri and Bonner are held for questioning by Chief of Police John Walton.

Dr. Lawlor has no immediate family. A brother, Stanley Lawlor, of Topeka, Kansas, has been notified.

===Chinese===
布罗克在凌乱不堪的实验室一角发现了一头大猿的尸体，给这桩事件平添了一层诡异的色彩。尽管众所周知，劳洛尔医生素来爱好博物学，近来也做过几回动物实验，但布罗克声称，十二天前实验室闭门之时，他确信里面并没有这头猿。桌上摊满了纸张文件，已悉数移交警方。布罗克、德米特里和邦纳三人现被警察局长约翰·沃尔顿（John Walton）扣留问话。

劳洛尔医生并无直系亲属在世。他有一位兄弟斯坦利·劳洛尔（Stanley Lawlor），住在堪萨斯州托皮卡（Topeka, Kansas），已获通知。
