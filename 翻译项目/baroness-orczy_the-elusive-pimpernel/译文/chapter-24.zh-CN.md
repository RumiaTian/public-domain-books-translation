## XXIV. Colleagues / XXIV. 同僚

===Original===
As soon as the door had closed behind Marguerite, there came from somewhere in the room the sound of a yawn, a grunt and a volley of oaths.

The flickering light of the tallow candles had failed to penetrate into all the corners, and now from out one of these dark depths, a certain something began to detach itself, and to move forward towards the table at which Chauvelin had once more resumed his seat.

===Chinese===
玛格丽特走出房门，门扇刚在她身后合拢，屋里什么地方便响起了个哈欠、一声咕哝，外加一串咒骂。

牛油蜡烛摇曳的光照不进所有角落，此刻，就从这样一团黑黢黢的暗处，有个什么东西渐渐分离出来，朝着桌边挪近——肖夫兰又已在桌旁重新坐定。

===Original===
“Has the damned aristocrat gone at last?” queried a hoarse voice, as a burly body clad in loose-fitting coat and mud-stained boots and breeches appeared within the narrow circle of light.

“Yes,” replied Chauvelin curtly.

===Chinese===
「那个该死的贵族总算走了？」一个嘶哑的声音问道；一个五大三粗的身形走进了窄窄的光圈，身上是宽大的外衣、溅满泥浆的靴子和马裤。

「走了。」肖夫兰简短地答道。

===Original===
“And a cursed long time you have been with the baggage,” grunted the other surlily. “Another five minutes and I’d have taken the matter in my own hands.

“An assumption of authority,” commented Chauvelin quietly, “to which your position here does not entitle you, Citizen Collot.”

===Chinese===
「跟那个臭婆娘一磨就磨这么半天，」对方乖戾地咕哝道。「再过五分钟，我就要自己动手了。」

「这倒是一副揽权的口气，」肖夫兰不动声色地评论道，「可惜您在此地的职位，还容不得您这样，科洛公民。」

===Original===
Collot d’Herbois lounged lazily forward, and presently he threw his ill-knit figure into the chair lately vacated by Marguerite. His heavy, square face bore distinct traces of the fatigue endured in the past twenty-four hours on horseback or in jolting market wagons. His temper too appeared to have suffered on the way, and, at Chauvelin’s curt and dictatorial replies, he looked as surly as a chained dog.

===Chinese===
科洛·德埃尔布瓦懒洋洋地踱上前来，不多时，便把他那副松散的骨架往玛格丽特方才空出的椅子上一掼。他那厚重的四方脸膛上，清清楚楚带着这二十四小时里骑在马背上、或颠簸在集市货车里熬出来的困顿。他的火气似乎也在路上磨坏了，肖夫兰答话那样简慢专断，他瞧上去活像一条拴着链子的恶狗。

===Original===
“You were wasting your breath over that woman,” he muttered, bringing a large and grimy fist heavily down on the table, “and your measures are not quite so sound as you fondly imagine, Citizen Chauvelin.”

“They were mostly of your imagining, Citizen Collot,” rejoined the other quietly, “and of your suggestion.”

“I added a touch of strength and determination to your mild milk-and-water notions, Citizen,” snarled Collot spitefully. “I’d have knocked that intriguing woman’s brains out at the very first possible opportunity, had I been consulted earlier than this.”

===Chinese===
「你跟那女人磨嘴皮子，纯粹白费唾沫，」他咕哝着，一只污黑的大拳头沉沉捶在桌上，「你那些法子，也远不像你自己美滋滋想的那样牢靠，肖夫兰公民。」

「那些法子多半本来就是您构想出来的，科洛公民，」对方安安静静地回敬道，「也是您提议的。」

「我不过给您那些温吞水一样的念头，添了点狠劲和决断罢了，公民，」科洛咬牙切齿地咆哮道，「若是早点儿问我，头一个机会我就把那个耍阴谋的女人的脑袋敲开了。」

===Original===
“Quite regardless of the fact that such violent measures would completely damn all our chances of success as far as the capture of the Scarlet Pimpernel is concerned,” remarked Chauvelin drily, with a contemptuous shrug of the shoulders. “Once his wife is dead, the Englishman will never run his head into the noose which I have so carefully prepared for him.”

“So you say, Chauvelin; and therefore I suggested to you certain measures to prevent the woman escaping which you will find adequate, I hope.”

“You need have no fear, Citizen Collot,” said Chauvelin curtly, “this woman will make no attempt at escape now.”

===Chinese===
「全然不顾这样一动手，就会把咱们擒获猩红石榴红的胜算断送个干净，」肖夫兰冷冷地说，轻蔑地耸了耸肩。「他妻子一死，那英国人就再也不会把脑袋伸进我为他苦心备下的那个圈套里去了。」

「你说是这么说，肖夫兰；所以我才向你建议了几条防那女人逃走的法子——但愿你会觉得够用。」

「您尽可放心，科洛公民，」肖夫兰简短地说，「这女人如今不会动逃跑的念头了。」

===Original===
“If she does﻿ ﻿…” and Collot d’Herbois swore an obscene oath.

“I think she understands that we mean to put our threat in execution.”

“Threat?﻿ ﻿… It was no empty threat, Citizen.﻿ ﻿… *Sacré tonnerre!* if that woman escapes now, by all the devils in hell I swear that I’ll wield the guillotine myself and cut off the head of every able-bodied man or woman in Boulogne, with my own hands.”

===Chinese===
「她要是敢……」科洛·德埃尔布瓦骂出一句不堪入耳的粗话。

「我想她明白，我们是要把那威胁当真兑现的。」

「威胁？……那可不是一句空吓唬，公民。……该死的天雷！（Sacré tonnerre!）那女人这回要是跑了，我指着地狱里所有的魔鬼起誓：我要亲手抡起断头台，亲手砍掉布洛涅每一个壮年男女的脑袋。」

===Original===
As he said this his face assumed such an expression of inhuman cruelty, such a desire to kill, such a savage lust for blood, that instinctively Chauvelin shuddered and shrank away from his colleague. All through his career there is no doubt that this man, who was of gentle birth, of gentle breeding, and who had once been called M. le Marquis de Chauvelin, must have suffered in his susceptibilities and in his pride when in contact with the revolutionaries with whom he had chosen to cast his lot. He could not have thrown off all his old ideas of refinement quite so easily, as to feel happy in the presence of such men as Collot d’Herbois, or Marat in his day﻿—men who had become brute beasts, more ferocious far than any wild animal, more scientifically cruel than any feline prowler in jungle or desert.

===Chinese===
说话间，他脸上浮出那样一种非人的凶残、那样一种杀机、那样一种野蛮的嗜血欲，肖夫兰不由自主地打了个寒噤，朝他的同僚缩避开去。毫无疑问，此人一生仕途之中，与这些他自己择定同流共命的革命者周旋时，他那敏感的心思和自尊必定饱受过折磨——他本是世家出身、教养温文之人，从前人称肖夫兰侯爵先生（M. le Marquis de Chauvelin）。他到底没能把旧日的斯文讲究一下子甩干净，甩到能在科洛·德埃尔布瓦这路人、或当年的马拉那路人面前安然自得的地步——这些人已经沦为了兽类，比任何野兽都远为凶残，比丛林荒漠里任何潜行的猫科猛兽都更精于残忍之道。

===Original===
One look in Collot’s distorted face was sufficient at this moment to convince Chauvelin that it were useless for him to view the proclamation against the citizens of Boulogne merely as an idle threat, even if he had wished to do so. That Marguerite would not, under the circumstances, attempt to escape, that Sir Percy Blakeney himself would be forced to give up all thoughts of rescuing her, was a foregone conclusion in Chauvelin’s mind, but if this highborn English gentleman had not happened to be the selfless hero that he was, if Marguerite Blakeney were cast in a different, a rougher mould﻿—if, in short, the Scarlet Pimpernel in the face of the proclamation did succeed in dragging his wife out of the clutches of the Terrorists, then it was equally certain that Collot d’Herbois would carry out his rabid and cruel reprisals to the full. And if in the course of the wholesale butchery of the able-bodied and wage-earning inhabitants of Boulogne, the headsman should sink worn out, then would this ferocious sucker of blood put his own hand to the guillotine, with the same joy and lust which he had felt when he ordered one hundred and thirty-eight women of Nantes to be stripped naked by the soldiery before they were flung helter-skelter into the river.

===Chinese===
此刻，只消朝科洛那张扭曲的脸看上一眼，便足以叫肖夫兰确信：即便他有意把那份针对布洛涅公民的公告只当作一句空吓唬，也是枉然。玛格丽特处此境地必不会企图脱逃，珀西·布莱克尼爵士本人也势必被迫打消营救她的一切念头——这在肖夫兰心里早成定局；然而，倘若这位出身高贵的英国绅士偏偏不是他那样一个无私的英雄，倘若玛格丽特·布莱克尼生就的是另一种更粗粝的坯子——总而言之，倘若猩红石榴红顶着那份公告当真把他妻子从恐怖党人的魔掌里硬拽了出去，那么同样可以断定：科洛·德埃尔布瓦必会把他那疯狂残忍的报复推行到十足。倘若在把布洛涅全城壮年的、挣钱养家的居民屠戮殆尽的过程中，刽子手累得瘫倒了，那么这个凶残的吸血鬼还会亲自上手断头台，那份欢畅和欲念，同他当年下令叫士兵们把南特（Nantes）一百三十八名妇女先剥得精光、再七零八落地抛进河里时一模一样。

===Original===
A touch of strength and determination! Aye! Citizen Collot d’Herbois had plenty of that. Was it he, or Carriere, who at Arras commanded mothers to stand by while their children were being guillotined? And surely it was Maignet, Collot’s friend and colleague, who at Bedouin, because the Red Flag of the Republic had been mysteriously torn down over night, burnt the entire little village down to the last hovel and guillotined every one of the three hundred and fifty inhabitants.

And Chauvelin knew all that. Nay, more! he was himself a member of that so-called government which had countenanced these butcheries, by giving unlimited powers to men like Collot, like Maignet and Carriere. He was at one with them in their republican ideas and he believed in the regeneration and the purification of France, through the medium of the guillotine, but he propounded his theories and carried out his most bloodthirsty schemes with physically clean hands and in an immaculately cut coat.

===Chinese===

这些，肖夫兰全知道。岂止知道！他本人便是那个所谓政府的一员，而正是那个政府，把不受限度的权力授予科洛、梅涅、卡里埃之流，坐视了这一场场屠戮。在共和的理念上，他与他们本是一气；他也笃信法兰西的更生与涤罪，须借断头台这件利器来成就；只是，他阐述理论、推行他那些最嗜血的计划时，双手干干净净，身上是一件裁剪得无懈可击的外套。

===Original===
Even now when Collot d’Herbois lounged before him, with mud-bespattered legs stretched out before him, with dubious linen at neck and wrists, and an odour of rank tobacco and stale, cheap wine pervading his whole personality, the more fastidious man of the world, who had consorted with the dandies of London and Brighton, winced at the enforced proximity.

But it was the joint characteristic of all these men who had turned France into a vast butchery and charnel-house, that they all feared and hated one another, even more wholeheartedly than they hated the aristocrats and so-called traitors whom they sent to the guillotine. Citizen Lebon is said to have dipped his sword into the blood which flowed from the guillotine, whilst exclaiming: “*Comme je l’aime ce sang coulé de traître!*” but he and Collot and Danton and Robespierre, all of them in fact, would have regarded with more delight still the blood of any one of their colleagues.

At this very moment Collot d’Herbois and Chauvelin would with utmost satisfaction have denounced, one the other, to the tender mercies of the Public Prosecutor. Collot made no secret of his hatred for Chauvelin, and the latter disguised it but thinly under the veneer of contemptuous indifference.

===Chinese===

===Chinese===
