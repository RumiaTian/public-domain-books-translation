## XXI. Suspense / XXI. 悬念

===Original===
In his memorable interview with Robespierre, the day before he left for England, Chauvelin had asked that absolute power be given him, in order that he might carry out the plans for the capture of the Scarlet Pimpernel, which he had in his mind. Now that he was back in France he had no cause to complain that the revolutionary government had grudged him this power for which he had asked.

Implicit obedience had followed whenever he had commanded.

===Chinese===
动身赴英格兰的前一天，肖夫兰在与罗伯斯庇尔那场令人难忘的会谈中，曾要求授予他绝对权力，以便施行他心中拟就的擒拿猩红石榴红的计划。如今既已回到法兰西，他全然没有理由抱怨革命政府吝惜了他所讨的这份权力。

他每有号令，众人无不唯命是从。

===Original===
As soon as he heard that a woman had been arrested in the act of uttering a passport in the name of Céline Dumont, he guessed at once that Marguerite Blakeney had, with characteristic impulse, fallen into the trap which, with the aid of the woman Candeille, he had succeeded in laying for her.

He was not the least surprised at that. He knew human nature, feminine nature, far too well, ever to have been in doubt for a moment that Marguerite would follow her husband without calculating either costs or risks.

===Chinese===
一听说有个女人在使用塞琳·迪蒙名义的护照时被当场拿住，他立刻便猜到：玛格丽特·布莱克尼又凭她素来的冲动，一头栽进了他借康代伊那女人之手、终于替她设下的圈套。

对此他丝毫不觉意外。他太洞悉人性，尤其洞悉女人的天性，从未有过片刻迟疑——玛格丽特要追随丈夫而去，是断不会计较什么代价、什么风险的。

===Original===
Ye gods! the irony of it all! Had she not been called the cleverest woman in Europe at one time? Chauvelin himself had thus acclaimed her, in those olden days, before she and he became such mortal enemies, and when he was one of the many satellites that revolved round brilliant Marguerite St. Just. And tonight, when a sergeant of the town guards brought him news of her capture, he smiled grimly to himself; the cleverest woman in Europe had failed to perceive the trap laid temptingly open for her.

===Chinese===
诸天神明！这一切何其讽刺！她不是一度被称作全欧洲最聪明的女人么？肖夫兰自己便曾这样礼赞过她——在那久远的岁月里，那时他俩尚未结成这般死敌，他还只是环绕着光彩夺目的玛格丽特·圣茹斯特旋转的众多星辰之一。而今夜，当城防军的一名中士带来她被俘的消息时，他不由得暗自狞笑：全欧洲最聪明的女人，竟看不出那个洞开着诱她入彀的陷阱。

===Original===
Once more she had betrayed her husband into the hands of those who would not let him escape a second time. And now she had done it with her eyes open, with loving, passionate heart which ached for self-sacrifice, and only succeeded in imperilling the loved one more hopelessly than before.

===Chinese===
她又一次把丈夫出卖到了那些决计不让他二度脱身的人手里。而这一回，她是睁着眼睛做的——怀着一颗热恋的、炽烈的、渴望自我牺牲的心，结果却只是叫所爱的人陷进了比先前更无望的险境。

===Original===
The sergeant was waiting for orders. Citizen Chauvelin had come to Boulogne, armed with more full and more autocratic powers than any servant of the new republic had ever been endowed with before. The governor of the town, the captain of the guard, the fort and municipality were all as abject slaves before him.

As soon as he had taken possession of the quarters organized for him in the Town Hall, he had asked for a list of prisoners who for one cause or another were being detained pending further investigations.

===Chinese===
中士正候着示下。公民肖夫兰来到布洛涅，手中握有的权力之充分、之专擅，为新共和国的任何仆从前所未有。城防司令、卫队长、堡垒与市政当局，在他面前一概如同卑贱的奴隶。

他一进占市政厅里替他收拾停当的寓所，便索要一份囚犯名册——那些因这样那样的缘故被拘押、以候进一步查究的人。

===Original===
The list was long and contained many names which were of not the slightest interest to Chauvelin: he passed them over impatiently.

“To be released at once,” he said curtly.

He did not want the guard to be burdened with unnecessary duties, nor the prisons of the little seaport town to be inconveniently encumbered. He wanted room, space, air, the force and intelligence of the entire town at his command for the one capture which meant life and revenge to him.

===Chinese===
名册很长，许多名字于肖夫兰毫无分毫干系：他不耐烦地一掠而过。

「一概立即开释。」他简截地说。

他不愿卫队被无谓的差事拖累，也不愿这滨海小城的监狱塞得转圜不开。他要的是回旋的余地、开阔的空间、流通的空气，要全城的人力与才智尽听他调遣——只为那一桩在他意味着性命与复仇的擒获。

===Original===
“A woman﻿—name unknown﻿—found in possession of a forged passport in the name of Céline Dumont, maid to the Citizeness Désirée Candeille﻿—attempted to land﻿—was interrogated and failed to give satisfactory explanation of herself﻿—detained in room No. 6 of the Gayole prison.”

===Chinese===
「一女子——姓名不详——查获时持有伪造护照，为塞琳·迪蒙之名，系女公民德西蕾·康代伊的女仆——企图登岸——经讯问，未能自圆其说——现押于加约勒（Gayole）监狱六号牢房。」

===Original===
This was one of the last names on the list, the only one of any importance to Citizen Chauvelin. When he read it he nearly drove his nails into the palms of his hands, so desperate an effort did he make not to betray before the sergeant by look or sigh the exultation which he felt.

For a moment he shaded his eyes against the glare of the lamp, but it was not long before he had formulated a plan and was ready to give his orders.

===Chinese===
这是名册末尾几个名字之一，也是唯一一个于公民肖夫兰要紧的名字。读到它时，他险些把指甲掐进掌心——他便是这般拼命克制，才没有在中士面前以一丝神色或一声叹息，泄露心头那阵狂喜。

他抬手遮了一会儿刺目的灯光，但不多时便已定下一条计策，预备发号施令了。

===Original===
He asked for a list of prisoners already detained in the various forts. The name of l’Abbé Foucquet with those of his niece and nephew attracted his immediate attention. He asked for further information respecting these people, heard that the boy was a widow’s only son, the sole supporter of his mother’s declining years: the girl was ailing, suffering from incipient phthisis, and was blind.

*Pardi!* the very thing! L’Abbé himself, the friend of Juliette Marny, the pathetic personality around which this final adventure of the Scarlet Pimpernel was intended to revolve! and these two young people! his sister’s children! one of them blind and ill, the other full of vigour and manhood.

===Chinese===
他又索要一份已拘押在各处堡垒中的囚犯名册。富凯神父的名字，连同他外甥和外甥女的名字，立刻攫住了他的注意。他追问这几个人的详情，得知那少年是寡母的独子，独自撑持着母亲的暮年；那姑娘则孱弱多病，染了初起的痨症，眼睛又是瞎的。

见鬼！正中下怀！富凯神父本人，朱丽叶特·马尔尼的朋友，猩红石榴红这最后一场历险，本就是要围着这个楚楚可怜的人物旋转的！还有这两个年轻人！他妹妹的孩子！一个又瞎又病，一个血气方刚、正当青春。

===Original===
Citizen Chauvelin had soon made up his mind.

A few quick orders to the sergeant of the guard, and l’Abbé Foucquet, weak, helpless and gentle, became the relentless jailer who would guard Marguerite more securely than a whole regiment of loyal soldiers could have done.

===Chinese===
公民肖夫兰的主意很快便定了下来。

向卫队的中士匆匆下了几道命令，富凯神父——衰弱、无依而温厚——便成了那铁面无情的狱卒，看守起玛格丽特来，比整整一团的忠诚士兵还要牢靠。

===Original===
Then, having despatched a messenger to the Committee of Public Safety, Chauvelin laid himself down to rest. Fate had not deceived him. He had thought and schemed and planned, and events had shaped themselves exactly as foreseen, and the fact that Marguerite Blakeney was at the present moment a prisoner in his hands was merely the result of his own calculations.

As for the Scarlet Pimpernel, Chauvelin could not very well conceive what he would do under these present circumstances. The duel on the southern ramparts had of course become a farce, not likely to be enacted now that Marguerite’s life was at stake. The daring adventurer was caught in a network at last, from which all his ingenuity, all his wit, his impudence and his amazing luck could never extricate him.

===Chinese===
随后，他差一名信使前往公共安全委员会，自己便躺下歇息了。命运没有欺他。他思虑过、运谋过、布置过，事态也分毫不爽地照他预见的模样铺展开来；玛格丽特·布莱克尼此刻成了他掌中的囚徒，也无非是他自己筹算的结果罢了。

至于猩红石榴红，肖夫兰委实想不出处此境地，他还能有什么作为。南面城墙上的那场决斗，如今自然成了一出闹剧——玛格丽特性命攸关，断不会再演了。这个胆大包天的冒险家到底落进了罗网，凭他再多的机变、再敏的才思、再狂的厚颜、再惊人的运气，也休想脱身了。

===Original===
And in Chauvelin’s mind there was still something more. Revenge was the sweetest emotion his bruised and humbled pride could know: he had not yet tasted its complete intoxicating joy: but every hour now his cup of delight became more and more full: in a few days it would overflow.

In the meanwhile he was content to wait. The hours sped by and there was no news yet of that elusive Pimpernel. Of Marguerite he knew nothing save that she was well guarded; the sentry who passed up and down outside room No. 6 had heard her voice and that of the Abbé Foucquet, in the course of the afternoon.

===Chinese===
而在肖夫兰心里，还有更深一层的滋味。复仇，是他那受创而屈辱的自尊所能尝到的最甘美的情感：他还不曾啜到它醺然完满的甘甜：可如今每一个时辰，他杯中的欢愉都愈斟愈满：再过几天，便要满溢出来了。

其间他安心等着。时辰飞逝，那难觅踪影的石榴红却仍无半点消息。至于玛格丽特，他只知道她被看守得很好：六号牢房外来回踱步的哨兵，午后听见过里头她的声音，也有富凯神父的声音。

===Original===
Chauvelin had asked the Committee of Public Safety for aid in his difficult task, but forty-eight hours at least must elapse before such aid could reach him. Forty-eight hours, during which the hand of an assassin might be lurking for him, and might even reach him ere his vengeance was fully accomplished.

That was the only thought which really troubled him. He did not want to die before he had seen the Scarlet Pimpernel a withered abject creature, crushed in fame and honour, too debased to find glorification even in death.

===Chinese===
肖夫兰已向公共安全委员会乞援，以济这桩难办的差使，但那援手至少须得四十八小时方能到临。四十八小时——在这当口，刺客的手或许正潜藏着候他，甚至可能赶在他的复仇全然成就之前，便先够着他。

这是唯一真正搅扰他的念头。他不甘心就这么死了——不甘心在亲见猩红石榴红沦为枯槁卑贱的可怜虫、声名与荣光尽遭碾碎、堕落得纵然一死也镀不上半分荣光之前，便闭上眼睛。

===Original===
At this moment he only cared for his life because it was needed for the complete success of his schemes. No one else he knew would have that note of personal hatred towards the enemy of France which was necessary now in order to carry out successfully the plans which he had formed.

===Chinese===
眼下他所以顾惜性命，只因图谋要大功告成，还少不了这条命。他所识得的人里，再没有一个怀有那种对法兰西之敌的个人仇恨——而此刻要办成他拟下的计划，这份仇恨正是必不可少的。

===Original===
Robespierre and all the others only desired the destruction of a man who had intrigued against the reign of terror which they had established; his death on the guillotine, even if it were surrounded with the halo of martyrdom, would have satisfied them completely. Chauvelin looked further than that. He hated the man! He had suffered humiliation through him individually. He wished to see him as an object of contempt rather than of pity. And because of the anticipation of this joy, he was careful of his life, and throughout those two days which elapsed between the capture of Marguerite and the arrival of Collot d’Herbois at Boulogne, Chauvelin never left his quarters at the Hotel de Ville, and requisitioned a special escort consisting of proved soldiers of the town guard to attend his every footstep.

===Chinese===
罗伯斯庇尔和其余诸人，所求的不过是除掉一个跟他们一手建立的恐怖统治阴谋作对的人；他上断头台一死，哪怕罩着殉道者的光环，也尽够叫他们心满意足了。肖夫兰的图谋却不止于此。他恨这个人！他个人在他手里蒙受过羞辱。他要看他沦为人所不齿的货色，而非惹人怜悯的对象。也正是为了贪图这份指望中的快意，他爱惜起自己的性命来：从玛格丽特被俘到科洛·德埃尔布瓦抵达布洛涅的那两天里，肖夫兰寸步不离市政厅（Hôtel de Ville）的寓所，还征调了一支特派卫队——尽从城防军里久经考验的士兵中挑出——随侍左右，步步护持。

===Original===
On the evening of the 22nd, after the arrival of Citizen Collot in Boulogne, he gave orders that the woman from No. 6 cell be brought before him in the ground floor room of the Fort Gayole.

===Chinese===
二十二日傍晚，公民科洛到达布洛涅之后，他下令把六号牢房的那名女子，提到加约勒堡垒的底层房间里来见他。
