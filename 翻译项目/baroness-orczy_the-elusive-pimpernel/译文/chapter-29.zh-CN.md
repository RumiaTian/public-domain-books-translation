## XXIX. The National Fête / XXIX. 国家庆典

===Original===
“Citizens of Boulogne, awake!”

They had not slept, only some of them had fallen into drowsy somnolence, heavy and nerve-racking, worse indeed than any wakefulness.

Within the houses, the women too had kept the tedious vigil, listening for every sound, dreading every bit of news, which the wind might waft in through the small, open windows.

If one prisoner escaped, every family in Boulogne would be deprived of the breadwinner. Therefore the women wept, and tried to remember those Paters and Aves which the tyranny of liberty, fraternity and equality had ordered them to forget.

Broken rosaries were fetched out from neglected corners, and knees stiff with endless, thankless toil were bent once more in prayer.

===Chinese===
「布洛涅的公民们，醒来吧！」

他们并没有睡，只是有些人坠入了昏昏沉沉的瞌睡，又滞重、又煎熬神经，实在比干脆醒着不睡还要难受。

屋子里，女人们也同样守着这难熬的长夜，谛听着每一丝声响，惧怕着风也许会从敞开的小窗里吹进来的每一点消息。

只要有一名囚徒脱逃，布洛涅每一户人家就会失去养家的那口子。于是女人们哭泣着，努力回忆那些《天主经》和《圣母经》（Paters and Aves）——自由、博爱、平等的暴政早已勒令她们忘掉的那些经文。

断了线的念珠从无人过问的角落里翻了出来，那因无尽劳苦、无人称谢而僵硬的膝盖，又一次弯下去祈祷。

===Original===
“Oh God! Good God! Do not allow that woman to flee!”

“Holy Virgin! Mother of God! Make that she should not escape!”

Some of the women went out in the early dawn to take hot soup and coffee to their men who were watching outside the prison.

===Chinese===
「哦上帝！仁慈的上帝！别让那女人逃走！」

「圣处女！圣母！保佑她逃不出去吧！」

天蒙蒙亮，有几个女人出了门，给守在监狱外头的男人们送去热汤和咖啡。

===Original===
“Has anything been seen?”

“Have ye seen the woman?”

“Which room is she in?”

“Why won’t they let us see her?”

“Are you sure she hath not already escaped?”

Questions and surmises went round in muffled whispers as the steaming cans were passed round. No one had a definite answer to give, although Désiré Melun declared that he had, once during the night, caught sight of a woman’s face at one of the windows above: but as he could not describe the woman’s face, nor locate with any degree of precision the particular window at which she was supposed to have appeared, it was unanimously decided that Désiré must have been dreaming.

===Chinese===
「看见什么动静没有？」

「你们见过那女人吗？」

「她关在哪一间？」

「他们为什么不让咱们见她？」

「你敢保她还没逃走么？」

热气腾腾的罐子传着递着，压低的窃窃私语里，问话与猜测转来转去。谁也说不出个准信儿，虽说德西雷·梅伦（Désiré Melun）一口咬定，夜里有一回，他在上头哪扇窗户里瞥见过一张女人的脸：可他既描不出那女人的眉眼，也说不清她究竟出现在哪一扇窗，于是众人一致断定，德西雷准是做梦来着。

===Original===
“Citizens of Boulogne, awake!”

The cry came first from the Town Hall, and therefore from behind the crowd of men and women, whose faces had been so resolutely set for all these past hours towards the Gayole prison.

They were all awake! but too tired and cramped to move as yet, and to turn in the direction whence arose that cry.

“Citizens of Boulogne, awake!”

===Chinese===
「布洛涅的公民们，醒来吧！」

喊声先从市政厅那边传来，也就是从男女人群的背后传来——这许多个钟头里，他们的脸一直执拗地朝着加约勒监狱的方向。

他们都醒着呢！只是一身疲乏、手脚僵麻，一时还动弹不得，转不过身去望那喊声起处的方向。

「布洛涅的公民们，醒来吧！」

===Original===
It was just the voice of Auguste Moleux, the town-crier of Boulogne, who, bell in hand, was trudging his way along the Rue Daumont, closely followed by two fellows of the municipal guard.

Auguste was in the very midst of the sullen crowd, before the men even troubled about his presence here, but now with many a vigorous “*Allons donc!*” and “*Voyez-moi ça, fais donc place, voyons!*” he elbowed his way through the throng.

He was neither tired nor cramped; he served the Republic in comfort and ease, and had slept soundly on his paillasse in the little garret allotted to him in the Town Hall.

The crowd parted in silence to allow him to pass. Auguste was lean and powerful, the scanty and meagre food doled out to him by a paternal government had increased his muscular strength whilst reducing his fat. He had very hard elbows, and soon he managed, by dint of pushing and cursing to reach the gateway of Gayole.

===Chinese===
那不过是布洛涅街头公告人奥古斯特·莫勒（Auguste Moleux）的嗓门：他手里摇着铃，正沿着多蒙街（Rue Daumont）一路走来，身后紧跟着两个城防军的兵。

男人们还没顾上理会他怎么跑到这里来，奥古斯特已经钻进了阴沉人群的正中；这会儿他一声高过一声地喊着「让开让开！（*Allons donc!*）」「都给我瞧着，快腾个地方来，喂！（*Voyez-moi ça, fais donc place, voyons!*）」，一面用胳膊肘在人群里开出一条路来。

他既不疲乏，也不僵麻；他为共和国当差，过得安闲自在，就在市政厅分派给他的小阁楼里，在那张草垫子上酣睡了一夜。

人群默默分开，给他让路。奥古斯特精瘦而有力，慈父般的政府配给他的那点菲薄口粮，减了他的膘，倒添了他的筋骨。他那两只胳膊肘硬得很，连推带骂之间，不多时就挤到了加约勒的大门口。

===Original===
“*Voyons! enlevez-moi ça*,” he commanded in stentorian tones, pointing to the proclamation.

The fellows of the municipal guard fell to and tore the parchment away from the door whilst the crowd looked on with stupid amazement.

What did it all mean?

Then Auguste Moleux turned and faced the men.

===Chinese===
「喂！把这个给我撤下来！（*Voyons! enlevez-moi ça*）」他指着那份公告，扯开洪亮的嗓门发令。

城防军的兵们动手把那张羊皮纸从门上撕了下来，人群只管呆呆望着，满脸骇异。

这是要干什么？

接着，奥古斯特·莫勒转过身来，面对着众人。

===Original===
“*Mes enfants*,” he said, “my little cabbages! wake up! the government of the Republic has decreed that today is to be a day of gaiety and public rejoicings!”

“Gaiety? … Public rejoicings forsooth, when the breadwinner of every family …”

“Hush! Hush! Be silent, all of you,” quoth Auguste impatiently, “you do not understand! … All that is at an end … There is no fear that the woman shall escape. … You are all to dance and rejoice. … The Scarlet Pimpernel has been captured in Boulogne, last night …”

“*Qui ça* the Scarlet Pimpernel?”

“*Mais!* ’tis that mysterious English adventurer who rescued people from the guillotine!”

===Chinese===
「我的孩子们（*Mes enfants*），」他说，「我的小卷心菜们！醒醒吧！共和国政府已经颁令：今天要成为欢庆和公众同乐的日子！」

「欢庆？……还公众同乐呢，如今家家户户养家的那口子……」

「嘘！嘘！你们都别作声，」奥古斯特不耐烦地说，「你们不明白！……那一切都完了……那女人逃不出去，不用怕。……你们大家只管跳舞、只管欢庆。……猩红石榴红昨儿夜里在布洛涅被抓住啦……」

「『猩红石榴红』是谁呀（*Qui ça*）？」

「可不是嘛（*Mais*）！就是那位神秘莫测的英国冒险家，专从断头台底下救人的那位！」

===Original===
“A hero? *quoi*?”

“No! no! only an English spy, a friend of aristocrats … he would have cared nothing for the breadwinners of Boulogne …”

“He would not have raised a finger to save them.”

“Who knows?” sighed a feminine voice, “perhaps he came to Boulogne to help them.”

“And he has been caught anyway,” concluded Auguste Moleux sententiously, “and, my little cabbages, remember this, that so great is the pleasure of the all-powerful Committee of Public Safety at this capture, that because he has been caught in Boulogne, therefore Boulogne is to be specially rewarded!”

===Chinese===
「是位英雄么？什么（*quoi*）？」

「不！不！不过是个英国奸细，贵族们的朋友……布洛涅人养家的那口子，他才不放在心上……」

「要救这些人，他连一根指头也不会抬。」

「谁知道呢？」一个女人的声音叹道，「说不定他到布洛涅来，正是为了帮他们。」

「反正他已经落网了，」奥古斯特·莫勒拿腔拿调地下了断语，「还有，我的小卷心菜们，记住这一点：权势无边的公共安全委员会为这次擒获如此欢心，正因为他是在布洛涅被捉住的，所以布洛涅要受到特别的犒赏！」

===Original===
“Holy Virgin, who’d have thought it?”

“Sh … Jeanette, dost not know that there’s no Holy Virgin now?”

“And dost know, Auguste, how we are to be rewarded?”

It is a difficult matter for the human mind to turn very quickly from despair to hope, and the fishermen of Boulogne had not yet grasped the fact that they were to make merry and that thoughts of anxiety must be abandoned for those of gaiety.

Auguste Moleux took out a parchment from the capacious pocket of his coat; he put on his most solemn air of officialdom, and pointing with extended forefinger to the parchment, he said:

===Chinese===
「圣母啊，谁想得到呢？」

「嘘……让娜特（Jeanette），你不知道如今没有圣母了么？」

「那你知道么，奥古斯特，咱们要受什么犒赏？」

人心要从绝望一转而至希望，本是难事；布洛涅的渔夫们还没领会过来：他们就要欢庆作乐了，忧惧的念头得撇下，换成欢畅的心思。

奥古斯特·莫勒从外套那只宽大的口袋里掏出一卷羊皮纸；他摆出最庄严的官府气派，伸直食指点着那羊皮纸，说道：

===Original===
“A general amnesty to all natives of Boulogne who are under arrest at the present moment: a free pardon to all natives of Boulogne who are under sentence of death: permission to all natives of Boulogne to quit the town with their families, to embark on any vessel they please, in or out of the harbour, and to go whithersoever they choose, without passports, formalities or questions of any kind.”

Dead silence followed this announcement. Hope was just beginning to crowd anxiety and sullenness out of the way.

===Chinese===
「凡布洛涅本地人现正被拘押者，一律大赦；凡布洛涅本地人现已被判死刑者，一律特赦；凡布洛涅本地人，均准携家眷离城，任意登船，港内港外悉听尊便，愿往何处便往何处——无须通行证，无须任何手续，不受任何盘问。」

这番宣示过后，是一片死寂。希望正开始把忧惧和阴郁从心底挤将出去。

===Original===
“Then poor André Legrand will be pardoned,” whispered a voice suddenly; “he was to have been guillotined today.”

“And Denise Latour! she was innocent enough, the gentle pigeon.”

“And they’ll let poor Abbé Foucquet out of prison too.”

“And François!”

“And poor Félicité, who is blind!”

===Chinese===
「那么可怜的安德烈·勒格朗（André Legrand）就要获赦了，」忽然有一个声音低低地说；「他原定今天上断头台的。」

「还有德妮丝·拉图尔（Denise Latour）！她够清白无辜的了，那温柔的小鸽子。」

「他们还会把可怜的富凯神父也放出狱来。」

「还有弗朗索瓦（François）！」

「还有可怜的费利西泰（Félicité），她眼睛看不见！」

===Original===
“M. l’Abbé would be wise to leave Boulogne with the children.”

“He will too: thou canst be sure of that!”

“It is not good to be a priest just now!”

“Bah! *calotins* are best dead than alive.”

===Chinese===
「神父先生还是带着孩子们离开布洛涅为妙。」

「他一定会的：这个你尽管放心！」

「眼下当教士可不是好事！」

「呸！『教士佬』（*calotins*）这号东西，死了比活着强。」

===Original===
But some in the crowd were silent, others whispered eagerly.

“Thinkest thou it would be safer for us to get out of the country whilst we can?” said one of the men in a muffled tone, and clutching nervously at a woman’s wrist.

“Aye! aye! it might leak out about that boat we procured for …”

“Sh! … I was thinking of that …”

“We can go to my aunt Lebrun in Belgium …”

===Chinese===
可人群里有不作声的，也有急切交头接耳的。

「你说，趁眼下还走得掉，咱们离了这地方是不是更稳妥？」一个男人闷声闷气地说，一面紧张地攥住一个女人的手腕。

「是啊！是啊！咱们替人弄船那桩事万一走漏了风声……」

「嘘！……我也正想着这个……」

「咱们可以上比利时我勒布伦姨妈（aunt Lebrun）那儿去……」

===Original===
Others talked in whispers of England or the New Land across the seas: they were those who had something to hide, money received from refugee aristocrats, boats sold to would-be émigrés, information withheld, denunciations shirked: the amnesty would not last long, ’twas best to be safely out of the way.

“In the meanwhile, my cabbages,” quoth Auguste sententiously, “are you not grateful to Citizen Robespierre, who has sent this order specially down from Paris?”

“Aye! aye!” assented the crowd cheerfully.

===Chinese===
另一些人则窃窃私议着英格兰，或大洋彼岸的新大陆：这些人个个都有把柄要藏——收过逃亡贵族的钱，卖过船给一心出走的逃亡者，瞒下过消息，推脱过告密：大赦长不了，还是及早走避、躲得远远的为妙。

「话说回来，我的卷心菜们，」奥古斯特煞有介事地说，「罗伯斯庇尔公民特意从巴黎发下这道命令来，你们不感激他么？」

「感激！感激！」人群欢欢喜喜地应道。

===Original===
“Hurrah for Citizen Robespierre!”

“*Viva la République!*”

“And you will enjoy yourselves today?”

“That we will!”

“Processions?”

“Aye! with music and dancing.”

===Chinese===
「罗伯斯庇尔公民万岁！」

「共和国万岁！」（*Viva la République!*）

「那你们今天要好好乐一乐了？」

「那还用说！」

「要游行么？」

「要！又奏乐，又跳舞。」

===Original===
Out there, far away, beyond the harbour, the grey light of dawn was yielding to the crimson glow of morning. The rain had ceased and heavy slaty clouds parted here and there, displaying glints of delicate turquoise sky, and tiny ethereal vapours in the dim and remote distance of infinity, flecked with touches of rose and gold.

The towers and pinnacles of old Boulogne detached themselves one by one from the misty gloom of night. The old bell of the Beffroi tolled the hour of six. Soon the massive cupola of Notre Dame was clothed in purple hues, and the gilt cross on St. Joseph threw back across the square a blinding ray of gold.

The town sparrows began to twitter, and from far out at sea in the direction of Dunkirk there came the muffled boom of cannon.

===Chinese===
那边远处，港外天边，熹微的曙色正让位给清晨的绯红霞光。雨已经停了，沉重的青灰云层处处绽开，露出几抹淡青的碧空，还有渺远天际隐隐约约的缕缕轻云，缀着点点玫瑰红与金黄。

老布洛涅的塔楼尖顶，一座一座从夜雾的幽暗里显出轮廓。贝夫鲁瓦的老钟敲响了六点。不多时，圣母院巨大的穹顶披上了绛紫，圣约瑟教堂（St. Joseph）的镀金十字架往广场上投回一道炫目的金光。

城里的麻雀开始啁啾，从远处海上敦刻尔克的方向，传来一声声沉闷的炮响。

===Original===
“And remember, my pigeons,” admonished Auguste Moleux solemnly, “that in this order which Robespierre has sent from Paris, it also says that from today onwards *le bon Dieu* has ceased to be!”

Many faces were turned towards the East just then, for the rising sun, tearing with one gigantic sweep the banks of cloud asunder, now displayed his magnificence in a gorgeous immensity of flaming crimson. The sea, in response, turned to liquid fire beneath the glow, and the whole sky was irradiated with the first blush of morning.

*Le bon Dieu* has ceased to be!

===Chinese===
「还有，记住，我的鸽子们，」奥古斯特·莫勒郑重其事地告诫道，「罗伯斯庇尔从巴黎发来的这道命令里还说了：从今天起，『好上帝』（*le bon Dieu*）已经不存在啦！」

就在那时，许多脸庞转向了东方，因为初升的太阳猛然一把将重重云堆撕开，正在一大片绚烂的火红中尽显它的壮丽。大海应和着，在霞光底下化作一片流火，整个天空则被清晨最初的红晕照得通明。

好上帝已经不存在啦！

===Original===
“There is only one religion in France now,” explained Auguste Moleux, “the religion of Reason! We are all citizens! We are all free and all able to think for ourselves. Citizen Robespierre has decreed that there is no good God. *Le bon Dieu* was a tyrant and an aristocrat, and, like all tyrants and aristocrats, He has been deposed. There is no good God, there is no Holy Virgin and no Saints, only Reason, who is a goddess and whom we all honour.”

And the townsfolk of Boulogne, with eyes still fixed on the gorgeous East, shouted with sullen obedience:

“Hurrah! for the Goddess of Reason!”

“Hurrah for Robespierre!”

===Chinese===
「如今法兰西只剩下一种宗教了，」奥古斯特·莫勒解释道，「理性的宗教！我们全是公民！我们全自由，全都能自己思考。罗伯斯庇尔公民已经颁令：没有什么好上帝。好上帝是个暴君，是个贵族，跟一切暴君和贵族一样，他已经被废黜了。没有什么好上帝，没有圣母，也没有圣徒，只有理性——她是一位女神，我们都敬奉她。」

于是布洛涅的城里人，眼睛仍盯着那绚烂的东方，怀着阴郁的顺从高呼：

「理性女神万岁！」

「罗伯斯庇尔万岁！」

===Original===
Only the women, trying to escape the town-crier’s prying eyes, or the soldiers’ stern gaze, hastily crossed themselves behind their husbands’ backs, terrified lest *le bon Dieu* had, after all, not altogether ceased to exist at the bidding of Citizen Robespierre.

Thus the worthy natives of Boulogne, forgetting their anxieties and fears, were ready enough to enjoy the national fête ordained for them by the Committee of Public Safety, in honour of the capture of the Scarlet Pimpernel. They were even willing to accept this new religion which Robespierre had invented: a religion which was only a mockery, with an actress to represent its supreme deity.

*Mais, que voulez-vous?* Boulogne had long ago ceased to have faith in God: the terrors of the Revolution, which culminated in that agonizing watch of last night, had smothered all thoughts of worship and of prayer.

The Scarlet Pimpernel must indeed be a dangerous spy that his arrest should cause so much joy in Paris!

===Chinese===
只有女人们，躲着街头公告人那双窥探的眼睛，躲着士兵们严峻的目光，在丈夫的背后匆匆画着十字，惟恐好上帝到头来并没有全然遵照罗伯斯庇尔公民的一声号令，就此不存在了。

就这样，布洛涅这些老实的本地人忘却了忧惧，满心情愿地准备享受公共安全委员会为他们钦定的国家庆典——为庆贺擒获猩红石榴红而设的庆典。他们甚至愿意接受罗伯斯庇尔发明的这门新宗教：一门纯属戏弄的宗教，由一名女演员来充任它的至高神明。

可是，又有什么办法呢？（*Mais, que voulez-vous?*）布洛涅早就不再信上帝了：革命的种种恐怖——到昨夜那场煎熬的守望而登峰造极——早已把人心头一切敬拜与祈祷的念头闷死了。

猩红石榴红想必真是个危险的奸细，要不然，他被捕的消息怎会在巴黎引起这么大的欢腾！

===Original===
Even Boulogne had learned by experience that the Committee of Public Safety did not readily give up a prey, once its vulture-like claws had closed upon it. The proportion of condemnations as against acquittals was as a hundred to one.

But because this one man was taken, scores today were to be set free!

In the evening at a given hour—seven o’clock as Auguste Moleux, the town-crier, understood—the boom of the cannon would be heard, the gates of the town would be opened, the harbour would become a free port.

The inhabitants of Boulogne were ready to shout:

“*Vive* the Scarlet Pimpernel!”

===Chinese===
连布洛涅也从亲身阅历里领教过：公共安全委员会这只秃鹫，利爪一攥住猎物，轻易不肯松开。定罪与开释之比，是一百比一。

可就因为拿住了这一个人，今天便有几十口子要重获自由！

到了晚间指定的时刻——照街头公告人奥古斯特·莫勒的理解，是七点钟——将闻炮响，城门将大开，海港将成为自由港。

布洛涅的居民早已准备好要高呼：

「猩红石榴红万岁！」

===Original===
Whatever he was—hero or spy—he was undoubtedly the primary cause of all their joy.

By the time Auguste Moleux had cried out the news throughout the town, and pinned the new proclamation of mercy up on every public building, all traces of fatigue and anxiety had vanished. In spite of the fact that wearisome vigils had been kept in every home that night, and that hundreds of men and women had stood about for hours in the vicinity of the Gayole Fort, no sooner was the joyful news known than all lassitude was forgotten, and everyone set to with a right merry will to make the great fête-day a complete success.

There is in every native of Normandy, be he peasant or gentleman, an infinite capacity for enjoyment, and at the same time a marvellous faculty for coordinating and systematizing his pleasures.

In a trice the surly crowds had vanished. Instead of these, there were groups of gaily-visaged men pleasantly chattering outside every eating and drinking place in the town. The national holiday had come upon these people quite unawares, so the early part of it had to be spent in thinking out a satisfactory programme for it. Sipping their beer or coffee, or munching their cherries à l’eau-de-vie, the townsfolk of Boulogne, so lately threatened with death, were quietly organizing processions.

There was to be a grand muster on the Place de la Sénéchaussée, then a torchlight and lantern-light march, right round the Ramparts, culminating in a gigantic assembly outside the Town Hall, where the Citizen Chauvelin, representing the Committee of Public Safety, would receive an address of welcome from the entire population of Boulogne.

The procession was to be in costume! There were to be Pierrots and Pierettes, Harlequins and English clowns, aristocrats and goddesses! All day the women and girls were busy contriving travesties of all sorts, and the little tumbledown shops in the Rue de Château and the Rue Frédéric Sauvage—kept chiefly by Jews and English traders—were ransacked for old bits of finery, and for remnants of costumes, worn in the days when Boulogne was still a gay city and Carnivals were held every year.

And then, of course, there would be the Goddess of Reason, in her triumphal car! the apotheosis of the new religion, which was to make everybody happy, rich and free.

Forgotten were the anxieties of the night, the fears of death, the great and glorious Revolution, which for this one day would cease her perpetual demand for the toll of blood.

Nothing was remembered save the pleasures and joys of the moment, and at times the name of that Englishman—spy, hero or adventurer—the cause of all this bounty: the Scarlet Pimpernel.
