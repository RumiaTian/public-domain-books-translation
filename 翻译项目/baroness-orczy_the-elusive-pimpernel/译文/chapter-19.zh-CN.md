## XIX. The Strength of the Weak / XIX. 弱者的力量

===Original===

“M. L’Abbé!﻿ ﻿…” said Marguerite gravely.

“Yes, *mon enfant*.”

The old man looked up from his breviary, and saw Marguerite’s great earnest eyes fixed with obvious calm and trust upon him. She had finished her toilet as well as she could, had shaken up and tidied the paillasse, and was now sitting on the edge of it, her hands clasped between her knees. There was something which still puzzled her, and impatient and impulsive as she was, she had watched the abbé as he calmly went on reading the Latin prayers for the last five minutes, and now she could contain her questionings no longer.

===Chinese===

「神父先生！……」玛格丽特郑重地唤道。

「是的，我的孩子。」

老人从日课经本上抬起眼来，看见玛格丽特那双恳切的大眼睛正安然信靠地凝望着他。她已尽力把自己收拾停当，拍松草垫、略加整理，此刻正坐在垫沿上，双手交握，夹在两膝之间。心里还有一桩疑惑未解，她本是个急性子、又容易冲动，眼看神父安之若素地诵着拉丁经文，一诵便是五分钟，如今再按捺不住满腹的疑问了。

===Original===

“You said just now that they set you to watch over me﻿ ﻿…”

“So they did, my child, so they did﻿ ﻿…” he replied with a sigh, as he quietly closed his book and slipped it back into his pocket. “Ah! they are very cunning﻿ … and we must remember that they have the power. No doubt,” added the old man, with his own, quaint philosophy, “no doubt *le bon Dieu* meant them to have the power, or they would not have it, would they?”

===Chinese===

「您方才说，是他们派您来看管我的……」

「正是，我的孩子，正是……」他叹了口气答道，一面悄然合上经本，掖回衣袋里。「唉！他们狡猾得很……可我们得记着，权柄在他们手里。毫无疑问，」老人又添了一句，带着他那套古雅的处世哲学，「毫无疑问，是好天主（le bon Dieu）有意让他们掌权的，不然他们哪来的权柄呢，您说是不是？」

===Original===

“By ‘they’ you mean the Terrorists and Anarchists of France, M. L’Abbé.﻿ … The Committee of Public Safety who pillage and murder, outrage women, and desecrate religion.﻿ … Is that not so?”

“Alas! my child!” he sighed.

“And it is ‘they’ who have set you to watch over me?﻿ ﻿… I confess I don’t understand﻿ ﻿…”

===Chinese===

「您说的『他们』，指的是法兰西的恐怖党人和无政府党徒吧，神父先生。……那个公共安全委员会，劫掠杀戮，糟践妇女，亵渎宗教。……可是不是？」

「唉！我的孩子！」他喟然叹道。

「派您来看管我的，也正是『他们』？……恕我直言，我实在不明白……」

===Original===

She laughed, quite involuntarily indeed, for in spite of the reassurance in her heart her brain was still in a whirl of passionate anxiety.

“You don’t look at all like one of ‘them,’ M. l’Abbé,” she said.

“The good God forbid!” ejaculated the old man, raising protesting hands up toward the very distant, quite invisible sky. “How could I, a humble priest of the Lord, range myself with those who would flout and defy Him.”

“Yet I am a prisoner of the Republic and you are my jailer, M. l’Abbé.”

===Chinese===

她笑了起来，实在是不由自主——尽管心底已然宽慰，脑子里却仍被炽烈的焦灼搅得团团转。

「您可半点不像『他们』那一号人，神父先生。」她说。

「好天主不容！」老人失声呼道，抗议似的把双手举向那遥不可及、根本望不见的天空。「我本是主的一个卑微教士，怎能与那些嘲弄上主、蔑视上主的人为伍。」

「然而我毕竟是共和国的囚徒，而您毕竟是看守我的狱卒，神父先生。」

===Original===

“Ah, yes!” he sighed. “But I am very helpless. This was my cell. I had been here with François and Félicité, my sister’s children, you know. Innocent lambs, whom those fiends would lead to slaughter. Last night,” he continued, speaking volubly, “the soldiers came in and dragged François and Félicité out of this room, where, in spite of the danger before us, in spite of what we suffered, we had contrived to be quite happy together. I could read the Mass, and the dear children would say their prayers night and morning at my knee.”

He paused awhile. The unshed tears in his mild blue eyes struggled for freedom now, and one or two flowed slowly down his wrinkled cheek. Marguerite, though heartsore and full of agonizing sorrow herself, felt her whole noble soul go out to this kind old man, so pathetic, so high and simple-minded in his grief.

She said nothing, however, and the Abbé continued after a few seconds’ silence.

===Chinese===

「唉，是啊！」他叹道。「可我是心有余而力不足啊。这原是我的牢房。我先前同弗朗索瓦和费利西泰住在这里——我妹妹的两个孩子，您知道。两只无辜的羔羊，那帮恶魔却要把他们牵去宰杀。昨儿夜里，」他絮絮地往下说，「士兵们闯进来，把弗朗索瓦和费利西泰从这间屋里拖走了；在这间屋里，尽管大祸临头，尽管受尽苦楚，我们一家还是想法子过得挺快活的。我可以诵弥撒，亲爱的孩子们早晚就在我膝前念他们的祈祷。」

他停了片刻。那双温和的蓝眼睛里忍了许久的泪水，这时挣扎着要夺眶而出，有一两滴慢慢淌下他布满皱纹的面颊。玛格丽特自己固然心如刀绞、五内俱焚，此刻却把她整个高贵的灵魂都倾向了这位仁厚的老人——他悲哀得那样可怜，又那样高洁而天真。

然而她一言未发，神父默然几秒后又接着说了下去。

===Original===

“When the children had gone, they brought you in here, *mon enfant*, and laid you on the paillasse where Félicité used to sleep. You looked very white, and stricken down, like one of God’s lambs attacked by the ravening wolf. Your eyes were closed and you were blissfully unconscious. I was taken before the governor of the prison, and he told me that you would share the cell with me for a time, and that I was to watch you night and day, because﻿ ﻿…”

The old man paused again. Evidently what he had to say was very difficult to put into words. He groped in his pockets and brought out a large bandana handkerchief, red and yellow and green, with which he began to mop his moist forehead. The quaver in his voice and the trembling of his hands became more apparent and pronounced.

“Yes, M. l’Abbé? Because?﻿ ﻿…” queried Marguerite gently.

===Chinese===

「孩子们被带走之后，他们就把您抬到这里来了，我的孩子，把您放在费利西泰往常睡的草垫上。您面色惨白，像被打垮了似的，活像上帝的一只羔羊遭了饿狼的扑袭。您双眼紧闭，人事不省，倒也幸而免了苦痛。他们把我带到典狱长跟前，典狱长对我说，您要同我合住这间牢房一段时日，还说我必须日夜看守着您，因为……」

老人又顿住了。显然，他要说的那句话实在难以出口。他在衣袋里摸索了半晌，掏出一方红黄绿三色的大花帕，开始擦拭额头上的汗。他嗓音里的颤音、双手的哆嗦，愈发明显、愈发止不住了。

「是吗，神父先生？因为什么呢？……」玛格丽特轻声问道。

===Original===

“They said that if I guarded you well, Félicité and François would be set free,” replied the old man after a while, during which he made vigorous efforts to overcome his nervousness, “and that if you escaped the children and I would be guillotined the very next day.”

There was silence in the little room now. The Abbé was sitting quite still, clasping his trembling fingers, and Marguerite neither moved nor spoke. What the old man had just said was very slowly finding its way to the innermost cells of her brain. Until her mind had thoroughly grasped the meaning of it all, she could not trust herself to make a single comment.

===Chinese===

「他们说，只要我把您看守得好好的，费利西泰和弗朗索瓦就放人，」过了半晌，老人才答道——这半晌里他竭力按捺着心头的慌乱——「可若是让您逃了，第二天一早，我和孩子们就得上断头台。」

小屋里一片沉寂。神父一动不动地坐着，紧攥着自己颤抖的手指；玛格丽特也是纹丝不动，一言不发。老人方才说的那句话，正十分缓慢地渗入她脑海最深处的角落。在心神彻底领会这话的全部含义之前，她不敢开口说半个字。

===Original===

It was some seconds before she fully understood it all, before she realized what it meant not only to her, but indirectly to her husband. Until now she had not been fully conscious of the enormous wave of hope which almost in spite of herself had risen triumphant above the dull, grey sea of her former despair; only now when it had been shattered against this deadly rock of almost superhuman devilry and cunning did she understand what she had hoped, and what she must now completely forswear.

No bolts and bars, no fortified towers or inaccessible fortresses could prove so effectual a prison for Marguerite Blakeney as the dictum which morally bound her to her cell.

“If you escape the children and I would be guillotined the very next day.”

===Chinese===

隔了好几秒，她才算把这一切全部想明白，才算意识到这话不仅关乎她自己，而且间接关乎她的丈夫。直到此刻，她才分明觉出：一股何等巨大的希望的浪潮，早就不由分说地冲破她先前那片死灰色的绝望之海，昂扬而起；也直到此刻，这浪潮撞上这块集近乎超人的凶残与狡诈于一身的致命礁石、摔得粉碎时，她才明白自己曾怀着怎样的指望，而如今又必须把这一切彻底断念。

任凭门闩铁窗，任凭壁垒高塔、插翅难飞的要塞，都远远比不上这一句在道义上把她拴死在牢房里的判词，能成为囚禁玛格丽特·布莱克尼更有效的牢笼。

「若是让您逃了，第二天一早，我和孩子们就得上断头台。」

===Original===

This meant that even if Percy knew, even if he could reach her, he could never set her free, since her safety meant death to two innocent children and to this simple-hearted man.

It would require more than the ingenuity of the Scarlet Pimpernel himself to untie this Gordian knot.

===Chinese===

这就是说，即便珀西知道了，即便他能寻到她跟前，他也永远无法救她脱身，因为她的安然无恙，便意味着两个无辜的孩子和这个心地纯朴的老人要付出性命。

要解开这样一个戈耳狄俄斯之结，光凭猩红石榴红本人的机智恐怕还不够。

===Original===

“I don’t mind for myself, of course,” the old man went on with gentle philosophy. “I have lived my life. What matters if I die tomorrow, or if I linger on until my earthly span is legitimately run out? I am ready to go home whenever my Father calls me. But it is the children, you see. I have to think of them. François is his mother’s only son, the breadwinner of the household, a good lad and studious too, and Félicité has always been very delicate. She is blind from birth and﻿ ﻿…”

“Oh! don’t﻿ ﻿… for pity’s sake, don’t﻿ ﻿…” moaned Marguerite in an agony of helplessness. “I understand﻿ ﻿… you need not fear for your children, M. l’Abbé: no harm shall come to them through me.”

“It is as the good God wills!” replied the old man quietly.

===Chinese===

「我自己倒无所谓，当然，」老人带着他那温厚的达观接着说。「我这一生已经过完了。明天就死也罢，苟延残喘到寿数正当走完也罢，又有什么要紧？天父几时召唤，我便几时预备回家去。可孩子们不一样啊，您瞧。我不能不为他们着想。弗朗索瓦是他母亲唯一的儿子，一家人的顶梁柱，又是个好后生，还肯用功；费利西泰呢，身子素来孱弱。她生下来就瞎，还……」

「哦！别说了……行行好，别说了……」玛格丽特在一阵无可奈何的痛楚中呻吟道。「我明白了……您不必为孩子们担心，神父先生：他们绝不会因我而受半点伤害。」

「一切听从好天主的安排！」老人平静地答道。

===Original===

Then, as Marguerite had once more relapsed into silence, he fumbled for his beads, and his gentle voice began droning the Paters and Aves wherein no doubt his childlike heart found peace and solace.

He understood that the poor woman would not wish to speak, he knew as well as she did the overpowering strength of his helpless appeal. Thus the minutes sped on, the jailer and the captive, tied to one another by the strongest bonds that hand of man could forge, had nothing to say to one another: he, the old priest, imbued with the traditions of his calling, could pray and resign himself to the will of the Almighty, but she was young and ardent and passionate, she loved and was beloved, and an impassable barrier was built up between her and the man she worshipped!

===Chinese===

随后，见玛格丽特重又归于沉默，他便摸出念珠，用他那温厚的嗓音，开始低低诵起《天主经》和《圣母经》来——毫无疑问，他那颗孩童般的心正在其中觅得安宁与慰藉。

他明白这可怜的妇人不愿开口，也同她本人一样清楚，他那无奈的哀恳有着何等不可抗拒的力量。就这样，时间一分一秒飞逝，狱卒与囚徒，被人力所能锻造的最坚固的锁链拴在一处，却相对无言：他，这位老教士，浸染着圣职的传统，尚可祈祷，尚可把自己交托给全能上主的旨意；而她，年轻、热烈、多情，她爱着，也被爱着，一道不可逾越的屏障，却就此横亘在她与她所崇拜的男人之间！

===Original===

A barrier fashioned by the weak hands of children, one of whom was delicate and blind. Outside was air and freedom, reunion with her husband, an agony of happy remorse, a kiss from his dear lips, and trembling held her back from it all, because of François who was the breadwinner and of Félicité who was blind.

Mechanically, now, Marguerite rose again, and like an automaton﻿—lifeless and thoughtless﻿—she began putting the dingy, squalid room to rights. The Abbé helped her demolish the improvised screen; with the same gentle delicacy of thought which had caused him to build it up, he refrained from speaking to her now: he would not intrude himself on her grief and her despair.

===Chinese===

这道屏障，是由孩子们孱弱的手筑成的——其中一个体弱，且双目失明。门外便是空气与自由，便是与丈夫的重逢，便是又幸福又痛楚的悔恨煎熬，便是他亲爱的唇上一吻；然而她战战兢兢，被这一切硬生生拦住，只因弗朗索瓦是一家人的顶梁柱，只因费利西泰双目失明。

这会儿，玛格丽特机械地又站起身来，像个自动木偶似的——无生气，无思虑——动手收拾那间昏暗肮脏的屋子。神父帮她拆去那道临时隔起的屏挡；当初是何等温存体贴的心思促使他把它搭起，此刻他便以同样的温存体贴，避免同她说话：他不肯把自己的存在，硬挤进她的悲伤与绝望里去。

===Original===

Later on, she forced herself to speak again, and asked the old man his name.

“My name is Foucquet,” he replied, “Jean Baptiste Marie Foucquet, late parish priest of the Church of Saint Joseph, the patron saint of Boulogne.”

Foucquet! This was l’Abbé Foucquet! the faithful friend and servant of the de Marny family.

===Chinese===

后来，她强打精神再开了口，问老人尊姓大名。

「我叫富凯，」他答道，「让·巴蒂斯特·马里·富凯，圣约瑟夫教堂的前任本堂神父——圣约瑟夫，正是布洛涅的主保圣人。」

富凯！这就是富凯神父！德马尔尼一家那位忠实的友人兼老仆。

===Original===

Marguerite gazed at him with great, questioning eyes.

What a wealth of memories crowded in on her mind at sound of that name! Her beautiful home at Richmond, her brilliant array of servants and guests, His Royal Highness at her side! life in free, joyous happy England﻿—how infinitely remote it now seemed. Her ears were filled with the sound of a voice, drawly and quaint and gentle, a voice and a laugh half shy, wholly mirthful, and oh! so infinitely dear:

“I think a little sea voyage and English country air would suit the Abbé Foucquet, m’dear, and I only mean to ask him to cross the Channel with me﻿ ﻿…”

Oh! the joy and confidence expressed in those words! the daring, the ambition! the pride! and the soft, languorous air of the old-world garden round her then, the passion of his embrace! the heavy scent of late roses and of heliotrope, which caused her to swoon in his arms!

===Chinese===

玛格丽特睁大一双探询的眼睛，怔怔地望着他。

这名字一入耳，多少往事便潮水般涌上她心头！她在里士满的美丽宅邸，仆从如云、宾客盈门的盛况，亲王殿下在她身畔！自由、欢畅、快活的英格兰生活——此刻想来，已是何等遥远。她耳际充满了那个声音，慢悠悠的，古雅而温柔；那嗓音，那笑声，半带羞怯，却又满含欢畅，哦！亲得叫人心颤：

「我看啊，海上走一遭，再吸吸英格兰的乡间空气，对富凯神父正合适，亲爱的，我不过想请他跟我一道渡过海峡罢了……」

哦！那话语里流露的喜悦与自信！那份胆魄，那份雄心！那份骄傲！还有那时围绕着她的一方古老公馆花园里柔靡温软的空气，他拥抱的热烈！晚玫瑰与天芥菜的浓香，熏得她在他的臂弯里几欲晕去！

===Original===

And now a narrow prison cell, and that pathetic, tender little creature there, with trembling hands and tear-dimmed eyes, the most powerful and most relentless jailer which the ferocious cunning of her deadly enemies could possibly have devised.

Then she talked to him of Juliette Marny.

The Abbé did not know that Mlle. de Marny had succeeded in reaching England safely and was overjoyed to hear it.

===Chinese===

而如今，只剩下一间狭小的牢房，和那边那个楚楚可怜、温厚柔弱的小人儿——一双颤抖的手，一双泪眼昏花的眼睛——竟是她的死敌们以狠毒的狡计所能设计出的最有力、最无情的狱卒。

于是她同他谈起朱丽叶特·马尔尼来。

神父原本并不知道德马尔尼小姐已平安抵达英格兰，闻言不禁大喜过望。

===Original===

He recounted to Marguerite the story of the Marny jewels: how he had put them safely away in the crypt of his little church, until the Assembly of the Convention had ordered the closing of the churches, and placed before every minister of *le bon Dieu* the alternative of apostasy or death.

“With me it has only been prison so far,” continued the old man simply, “but prison has rendered me just as helpless as the guillotine would have done, for the enemies of *le bon Dieu* have ransacked the Church of Saint Joseph and stolen the jewels which I should have guarded with my life.”

===Chinese===

他向玛格丽特讲起马尔尼家珠宝的始末：他如何把它们妥妥地藏进自己那座小教堂的地窖，直到国民公会下令封闭教堂，逼着每一位好天主的仆人，在叛教与死亡之间任择其一。

「轮到我，至今还不过是坐牢罢了，」老人质朴地接着说，「可坐牢已经把我弄得同上断头台一般无能为力了，因为好天主的仇人们洗劫了圣约瑟夫教堂，把我拼着性命也该守护住的珠宝偷走了。」

===Original===

But it was obvious joy for the Abbé to talk of Juliette Marny’s happiness. Vaguely, in his remote little provincial cure, he had heard of the prowess and daring of the Scarlet Pimpernel and liked to think that Juliette owed her safety to him.

“The good God will reward him and those whom he cares for,” added Abbé Foucquet with that earnest belief in divine interference which seemed so strangely pathetic under these present circumstances.

Marguerite sighed, and for the first time in this terrible soul-stirring crisis through which she was passing so bravely, she felt a beneficent moisture in her eyes: the awful tension of her nerves relaxed. She went up to the old man took his wrinkled hand in hers and falling on her knees beside him she eased her overburdened heart in a flood of tears.

===Chinese===

不过，同神父谈起朱丽叶特·马尔尼的幸福，显然是件乐事。在他那偏远的外省小堂区里，他约略听说过猩红石榴红的神勇与胆略，很乐意相信朱丽叶特的平安脱险正是拜他所赐。

「好天主会酬报他，也会酬报他眷顾的人，」富凯神父又添了一句，语气里满是对天意干预的那种恳切信念——在眼下的境地中，这信念显得异样地令人心酸。

玛格丽特叹了口气。在她如此坚毅地经受着的这场惊心动魄的浩劫里，这是头一回，她感到眼中有一种温热的湿意泛起：神经上可怕的紧绷，松弛下来了。她走到老人跟前，把他那双皱缩的手握在自己手里，跪倒在他身旁，任满腔重压在滔滔泪水中一泻而出。
