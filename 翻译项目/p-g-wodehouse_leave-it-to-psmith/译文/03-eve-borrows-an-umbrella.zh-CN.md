## III Eve Borrows an Umbrella / 伊芙借伞

===Original===
What strikes the visitor to London most forcibly, as he enters the heart of that city’s fashionable shopping district, is the almost entire absence of ostentation in the shopwindows, the studied avoidance of garish display. About the front of the premises of Messrs. Thorpe & Briscoe, for instance, who sell coal in Dover Street, there is as a rule nothing whatever to attract fascinated attention. You might give the place a glance as you passed, but you would certainly not pause and stand staring at it as at the Sistine Chapel or the Taj Mahal. Yet at ten-thirty on the morning after Eve Halliday had taken tea with her friend Phyllis Jackson in West Kensington, Psmith, lounging gracefully in the smoking-room window of the Drones Club, which is immediately opposite the Thorpe & Briscoe establishment, had been gazing at it fixedly for a full five minutes. One would have said that the spectacle enthralled him. He seemed unable to take his eyes off it.

===Chinese===
游客一踏进伦敦时髦商业区的腹地，感受最强烈的莫过于：各家橱窗几乎全然不事铺张，刻意回避着花哨扎眼的陈列。譬如多佛街（Dover Street）上卖煤的索普-布里斯科商店（Messrs. Thorpe & Briscoe），其门面照例便无一丝足以令人流连注目之处。路过时你兴许会瞥它一眼，却决计不会像瞻仰西斯廷礼拜堂或泰姬陵那般驻足痴望。然而，在伊芙·哈利迪（Eve Halliday）去西肯辛顿的朋友菲利斯·杰克逊（Phyllis Jackson）家喝过茶的翌日上午十点半，皮史密斯（Psmith）——他正风度优雅地闲倚在「蜂子俱乐部」（the Drones Club）吸烟室的窗边，而俱乐部恰恰与索普-布里斯科商店隔街相望——却已对着那片门面整整凝望了五分钟。简直可以说，这番景象把他彻底迷住了。他似乎连眼睛都无法从上面挪开。

===Original===
There is always a reason for the most apparently inexplicable happenings. It is the practice of Thorpe (or Briscoe) during the months of summer to run out an awning over the shop. A quiet, genteel awning, of course, nothing to offend the eye﻿—but an awning which offers a quite adequate protection against those sudden showers which are such a delightfully piquant feature of the English summer: one of which had just begun to sprinkle the West End of London with a good deal of heartiness and vigour. And under this awning, peering plaintively out at the rain, Eve Halliday, on her way to the Ada Clarkson Employment Bureau, had taken refuge. It was she who had so enchained Psmith’s interest. It was his considered opinion that she improved the Thorpe & Briscoe frontage by about ninety-five percent.

===Chinese===
再貌似离奇的变故，背后也总有缘故。每逢夏季，索普（或者布里斯科）照例要在店门外支出一领遮阳篷。自然是顶素净体面的遮阳篷，绝无半点刺眼之处——但它足可抵挡骤雨，而骤雨，素来是英国夏天里一道别有风味的景致。此刻恰好有一场，正以十足的热忱和干劲，朝伦敦西区噼里啪啦地泼洒下来。而正在去艾达·克拉克森职业介绍所（Ada Clarkson Employment Bureau）路上的伊芙·哈利迪，便躲在这领遮阳篷下，哀怨地凝望着雨。把皮史密斯的兴味如此牢牢锁住的，正是她。依他深思熟审的见解，她给索普-布里斯科商店的门面增色了约莫百分之九十五。

===Original===
Pleased and gratified as Psmith was to have something nice to look at out of the smoking-room window, he was also somewhat puzzled. This girl seemed to him to radiate an atmosphere of wealth. Starting at farthest south and proceeding northward, she began in a gleam of patent-leather shoes. Fawn stockings, obviously expensive, led up to a black crêpe frock. And then, just as the eye was beginning to feel that there could be nothing more, it was stunned by a supreme hat of soft, dull satin with a black bird of Paradise feather falling down over the left shoulder. Even to the masculine eye, which is notoriously to seek in these matters, a whale of a hat. And yet this sumptuously upholstered young woman had been marooned by a shower of rain beneath the awning of Messrs. Thorpe & Briscoe. Why, Psmith asked himself, was this? Even, he argued, if Charles the chauffeur had been given the day off or was driving her father the millionaire to the City to attend to his vast interests, she could surely afford a cab-fare? We, who are familiar with the state of Eve’s finances, can understand her inability to take cabs, but Psmith was frankly perplexed.

===Chinese===
吸烟室窗外有了悦目风景可看，皮史密斯固然欢喜受用，却也颇有几分纳闷。在他看来，这位姑娘周身焕发着一股富贵之气。自最南端起，循北而上：起手是漆皮鞋一抹亮泽；小鹿色的长袜一看便价格不菲，向上接一袭黑色绉纱连衣裙；而正当眼睛渐渐觉得此间再无胜景，一顶登峰造极的帽子又将它震住：软缎制成，柔光内敛，一支黑色天堂鸟翎羽自左肩翩然垂下。即便以男性的眼光——众所周知，男人在这类事上向来不甚灵敏——这也是一顶了不得的帽子。然而这位装潢得富丽堂皇的姑娘，竟被一阵骤雨困在索普-布里斯科商店的遮阳篷底下。这是为何？皮史密斯暗自纳闷。他推想，纵然司机查尔斯今日歇班，或是正开车送她那位百万富翁父亲上金融城去照管他的宏图伟业，她总还付得起一趟车钱吧？我们这些熟知伊芙财务状况的人，自能明白她何以雇不起车，可皮史密斯委实一头雾水。

===Original===
Being, however, both ready-witted and chivalrous, he perceived that this was no time for idle speculation. His not to reason why; his obvious duty was to take steps to assist Beauty in distress. He left the window of the smoking-room, and, having made his way with a smooth dignity to the club’s cloakroom, proceeded to submit a row of umbrellas to a close inspection. He was not easy to satisfy. Two which he went so far as to pull out of the rack he returned with a shake of the head. Quite good umbrellas, but not fit for this special service. At length, however, he found a beauty, and a gentle smile flickered across his solemn face. He put up his monocle and gazed searchingly at this umbrella. It seemed to answer every test. He was well pleased with it.

===Chinese===
不过，皮史密斯素来机敏，又具骑士肝胆，当即看出此刻绝非闲想玄思之时。追问缘由，非他本分；他昭然若揭的义务，是设法搭救落难的美人。他离开吸烟室窗边，以从容的气派踱到俱乐部衣帽间，着手对一排雨伞逐把严加检验。他可不容易满足。有两把他都从架上抽了出来，末了还是摇头放了回去。伞是好伞，只是当不起这趟特别的差事。不过，他终于寻出一把妙品，庄重的脸上掠过一丝温和的浅笑。他戴上单片眼镜，将这把伞细细审视。它似乎经得起一切考验。他大为满意。

===Original===
“Whose,” he inquired of the attendant, “is this?”

===Chinese===
「这把伞，」他向侍者打听，「是哪位的？」

===Original===

===Chinese===
「回先生，是沃尔德威克少爷的。」

===Original===
“Ah!” said Psmith tolerantly.

===Chinese===
「啊！」皮史密斯宽容地说。

===Original===

Meanwhile Eve Halliday, lightening up the sombre austerity of Messrs. Thorpe & Briscoe’s shopfront, continued to think hard thoughts of the English climate and to inspect the sky in the hope of detecting a spot of blue. She was engaged in this cheerless occupation when at her side a voice spoke.

===Chinese===
与此同时，还在替索普-布里斯科商店阴沉肃穆的门面增光添彩的伊芙·哈利迪，继续对英国气候恶狠狠地腹诽，一面检视天空，指望能侦察出一角蓝天。她正埋头于这项凄清的活计，身旁忽然有人开了口。

===Original===
“Excuse me!”

===Chinese===

===Original===
A hatless young man was standing beside her, holding an umbrella. He was a striking-looking young man, very tall, very thin, and very well dressed. In his right eye there was a monocle, and through this he looked down at her with a grave friendliness. He said nothing further, but, taking her fingers, clasped them round the handle of the umbrella, which he had obligingly opened, and then with a courteous bow proceeded to dash with long strides across the road, disappearing through the doorway of a gloomy building which, from the number of men who had gone in and out during her vigil, she had set down as a club of some sort.

===Chinese===
一个未戴帽子的年轻人立在她身旁，手里执一把伞。他是个模样出挑的年轻人，个子极高，身形极瘦，衣着极讲究。他右眼上嵌着单片眼镜，正透过镜片，以庄重而友善的神情俯视她。他再无二话，只牵起她的手指，替它们扣上伞柄——伞已殷勤地撑开——随即谦恭一躬，迈开大步横穿马路，消失在对面一座阴沉沉的楼房门内；凭她守候期间进进出出的男士数目，她早断定那是家什么俱乐部。

===Original===
A good many surprising things had happened to Eve since first she had come to live in London, but nothing quite so surprising as this. For several minutes she stood where she was without moving, staring round-eyed at the building opposite. The episode was, however, apparently ended. The young man did not reappear. He did not even show himself at the window. The club had swallowed him up. And eventually Eve, deciding that this was not the sort of day on which to refuse umbrellas even if they dropped inexplicably from heaven, stepped out from under the awning, laughing helplessly, and started to resume her interrupted journey to Miss Clarkson’s.

===Chinese===
自打来伦敦安身，伊芙经历的稀奇事着实不少，却没有一桩及得上这桩稀奇。她原地纹丝不动站了好几分钟，圆睁双眼呆望着对面那座楼。不过，这段插曲显然已然收场。年轻人没有再露面，甚至连窗口也不曾现身。俱乐部把他整个吞了下去。终于，伊芙认定，今天可不是回绝雨伞的日子——哪怕这伞来得莫名其妙、直如天降——便从遮阳篷下跨出，无可奈何地笑着，重新踏上那趟被打断的旅程，往克拉克森小姐（Miss Clarkson）那里去了。
