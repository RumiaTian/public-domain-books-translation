## Gavon’s Eve / 加文之夜

===Original===
It is only the largest kind of ordnance map that records the existence of the village of Gavon, in the shire of Sutherland, and it is perhaps surprising that any map on whatever scale should mark so small and huddled a group of huts, set on a bare, bleak headland between moor and sea, and, so one would have thought, of no import at all to any who did not happen to live there. But the river Gavon, on the right bank of which stand this half-dozen of chimneyless and windswept habitations, is a geographical fact of far greater interest to outsiders, for the salmon there are heavy fish, the mouth of the river is clear of nets, and all the way up to Gavon Loch, some six miles inland, the coffee-coloured water lies in pool after deep pool, which verge, if the river is in order and the angler moderately sanguine, on a fishing probability amounting almost to a certainty. In any case during the first fortnight of September last I had no blank day on those delectable waters, and up till the fifteenth of that month there was no day on which someone at the lodge in which I was stopping did not land a fish out of the famous Picts’ pool. But after the fifteenth that pool was not fished again. The reason why is here set forward.

===Chinese===
只有那种最大比例尺的军用地图，才会标出萨瑟兰郡（Sutherland）境内加文（Gavon）这个村子的存在；无论何种比例尺的地图都肯标出这样一小簇挤作一团的茅屋，倒也实在令人称奇——它们坐落在荒原与大海之间一片光秃秃、萧瑟的岬地上，照理说，对任何并非恰好住在那里的人而言，都毫无意义可言。然而，这五六幢没有烟囱、终年承受风灌的住处所依附的加文河，对局外人来说却是一个有意思得多的地理事实：河里的鲑鱼都是肥大的鱼，河口没有拦网，从河口一路上行到内陆约六英里处的加文湖（Gavon Loch），那咖啡色的河水一段接一段地汇成一个又一个深潭；只要河水水位合适、垂钓者又不过分悲观，这些深潭几乎可以说是十拿九稳的好钓场。总之，去年九月头两个礼拜，我在那一片乐也融融的水面上没有哪天空手而归；到那个月十五号为止，没有哪一天，我下榻的那间钓屋里没有人从著名的皮克特人潭（Picts' pool）里钓起一条鱼来。可十五号一过，那口潭就再没人去钓过。个中缘由，便记在下面。

===Original===
The river at this point, after some hundred yards of rapid, makes a sudden turn round a rocky angle, and plunges madly into the pool itself. Very deep water lies at the head of it, but deeper still further down on the east side, where a portion of the stream flicks back again in a swift dark backwater towards the top of the pool again. It is fishable only from the western bank, for to the east, above this backwater, a great wall of black and basaltic rock, heaved up no doubt by some fault in strata, rises sheer from the river to the height of some sixty feet. It is in fact nearly precipitous on both sides, heavily serrated at the top, and of so curious a thinness, that at about the middle of it where a fissure breaks its topmost edge, and some twenty feet from the top, there exists a long hole, a sort of lancet window, one would say, right through the rock, so that a slit of daylight can be seen through it. Since, therefore, no one would care to cast his line standing perched on that razor-edged eminence, the pool must needs be fished from the western bank. A decent fly, however, will cover it all.

===Chinese===
河流到了这一段，先是经过百余码的急流，紧接着在一处嶙峋的河角猛地一拐，便疯了似的扎进这口潭里。潭头一带水极深，而东岸再往下些则更深，那里有一股水流又迅速地回旋成一道幽暗的回水，反向朝着潭头涌去。这潭只能从西岸下钩，因为东岸、在这道回水上方，一道巍然的黑色玄武岩峭壁——无疑是由某处地层错动所拱起——从河边笔直地拔起到六十英尺上下。它两面其实都近乎峭立，顶部锯齿嶙峋，而又薄得颇为奇特：在大约正中处，一道裂缝截断了它的顶缘，距顶部约二十英尺的地方，赫然有一个长长的孔洞，直直地贯穿岩壁，形如一扇柳叶窗，一线天光可以从中透过来。因此，既然没有人愿意站在那剃刀般锋利的脊上甩竿，这潭自然就只能从西岸来钓了。不过，一枚像样的飞蝇，足以把整潭都罩住。

===Original===
It is on the western bank that there stand the remains of that which gave its title to the pool, namely, the ruins of a Pict castle, built out of rough and scarcely hewn masonry, unmortared but on a certain large and impressive scale, and in a very well-preserved condition considering its extreme antiquity. It is circular in shape and measures some twenty yards of diameter in its internal span. A staircase of large blocks with a rise of at least a foot leads up to the main gate, and opposite this on the side towards the river is another smaller postern through which down a rather hazardously steep slope a scrambling path, where progress demands both caution and activity, conducts to the head of the pool which lies immediately beneath it. A gate-chamber still roofed over exists in the solid wall: inside there are foundation indications of three rooms, and in the centre of all a very deep hole, probably a well. Finally, just outside the postern leading to the river is a small artificially levelled platform, some twenty feet across, as if made to support some superincumbent edifice. Certain stone slabs and blocks are dispersed over it.

===Original===

But this transit demands a steady head, and at the best is a somewhat giddy passage. Otherwise the road between it and Brora lies in a long detour higher up the moor, passing by the gates of Gavon Lodge, where I was stopping. For some vague and ill-defined reason the pool itself and the Picts’ Castle had an uneasy reputation on the country side, and several times trudging back from a day’s fishing I have known my gillie take a longish circuit, though heavy with fish, rather than make this shortcut in the dusk by the castle. On the first occasion when Sandy, a strapping yellow-bearded viking of twenty-five, did this he gave as a reason that the ground round about the castle was “mossy,” though as a God-fearing man, he must have known he lied. But on another occasion he was more frank, and said that the Picts’ pool was “no canny” after sunset. I am now inclined to agree with him, though, when he lied about it, I think it was because as a God-fearing man he feared the devil also.

===Original===
It would seem, then, that Catrine Gordon, in answer to Sandy’s letter, left Inverness in heavy trouble. What happened afterwards can only be conjectured, but it seems likely she took the shortcut to Gavon, meaning to cross the river on the boulders above the Picts’ pool. But whether she slipped accidentally in her passage, and so was drawn down by the hungry water, or whether, unable to face the future, she had thrown herself into the pool, we can only guess. In any case they sleep together now in the bleak, windswept graveyard at Brora, in obedience to the inscrutable designs of God.

===Chinese===
这么说来，卡特琳·戈登应是接到桑迪的信后，满心愁苦地离开了因弗内斯。其后发生了什么，只能揣测；但她很可能抄近路赶往加文，打算从皮克特人潭上方的乱石上过河。至于她究竟是过河时失足滑倒、被贪婪的河水卷了下去，还是因不堪面对将来、自己纵身跳进了潭里，我们只能各凭猜测。无论如何，此刻他们都一同长眠在布罗拉那片萧瑟、风声呼啸的墓地里——顺服于上帝那不可测度的旨意。
