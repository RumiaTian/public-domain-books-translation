## Endnotes / 尾注

===Original===
1. The cordary berry grows during the winter and is at its best at New Year. The seeds have sweet meats. The berries are bright red, and the seeds dark purple, running over inside with sweet juice which keeps the kernel moist. ↩︎

===Chinese===
1. 科达里莓（cordary berry）在冬天生长，于新年前后最是美好。种子里裹着甘甜的仁肉。莓子鲜红，种子深紫，内里盈溢着甜汁，使核仁保持润泽。 ↩︎

===Original===
2. No books meant more to her, between the ages of six and ten, than *The Three Mulla-Mulgars*, *A Little Boy Lost*, and *The Princess and the Goblin*. ↩︎

===Chinese===
2. 在她六岁到十岁之间，没有哪本书在她心里的分量更重于这三本：《三只穆拉穆尔加》（*The Three Mulla-Mulgars*）、《迷路的小男孩》（*A Little Boy Lost*）与《公主与妖魔》（*The Princess and the Goblin*）。 ↩︎
