## XVII The Stricken Woods / XVII 劫后树林

===Original===
At first they thought they were ruined; then Dick, searching, found the old saw under a tree, and the butcher’s knife near it, as though the knife and saw had been trying to escape in company and had failed.

Bit by bit they began to recover something of their scattered property. The remains of the flannel had been taken by the cyclone and wrapped round and round a slender coconut tree, till the trunk looked like a gaily-bandaged leg. The box of fishhooks had been jammed into the centre of a cooked breadfruit, both having been picked up by the fingers of the wind and hurled against the same tree; and the staysail of the *Shenandoah* was out on the reef, with a piece of coral carefully placed on it as if to keep it down. As for the lugsail belonging to the dinghy, it was never seen again.

There is humour sometimes in a cyclone, if you can only appreciate it; no other form of air disturbance produces such quaint effects. Beside the great main whirlpool of wind, there are subsidiary whirlpools, each actuated by its own special imp.

===Chinese===
起初他们以为自己完了；随后迪克四下寻找，在一棵树下找到了那把旧锯子，屠刀就在近旁，仿佛刀和锯曾想结伴逃走，却没有逃成。

他们一点一点找回了一些四散的家当。法兰绒的残片被气旋卷走，一圈一圈缠在一株细瘦的椰子树上，树干看上去像一条扎得花花绿绿的伤腿。鱼钩盒被楔进一只烤熟的面包果心子里——两样都是被风的手指拈起来，摔在同一棵树上的；「谢南多亚号」的那面三角帆躺在外面的礁石上，上面还仔细压着一块珊瑚，仿佛怕它被吹走。至于小艇的那面斜桁帆，从此再没见过。

气旋有时也带着幽默，只看你欣赏不欣赏；再没有别种空气的骚动能造出这样古怪的效果。在大风的主漩涡之外，还有许多次生的漩涡，每一个都由它自己专属的小恶魔驱动。

===Original===
Emmeline had felt Hannah nearly snatched from her arms twice by these little ferocious gimlet winds; and that the whole business of the great storm was set about with the object of snatching Hannah from her, and blowing him out to sea, was a belief which she held, perhaps, in the innermost recesses of her mind.

The dinghy would have been utterly destroyed, had it not heeled over and sunk in shallow water at the first onset of the wind; as it was, Dick was able to bail it out at the next low tide, when it floated as bravely as ever, not having started a single seam.

But the destruction amidst the trees was pitiful. Looking at the woods as a mass, one noticed gaps here and there, but what had really happened could not be seen till one was amongst the trees. Great, beautiful coconut palms, not dead, but just dying, lay crushed and broken as if trampled upon by some enormous foot. You would come across half a dozen lianas twisted into one great cable. Where coconut palms were, you could not move a yard without kicking against a fallen nut; you might have picked up full-grown, half-grown, and wee baby nuts, not bigger than small apples, for on the same tree you will find nuts of all sizes and conditions.

===Chinese===
这些凶悍的螺钻小风，有两次几乎把汉纳从埃米琳怀里夺走；这场大风暴的整个勾当，为的就是从她怀里抢走汉纳、把他刮到海里去——这个念头，或许就藏在她内心最深的角落里。

若不是风头一动它就倾倒在浅水里沉了下去，小艇本会被毁得干干净净；即便如此，等到下一次退潮，迪克把水舀干，它又像从前一样神气地浮了起来，板缝一条也没有裂。

可树间的毁坏是凄惨的。把树林当作整体望去，只见这里那里缺了口子；真正出了什么事，要走到树间才看得见。高大美丽的椰子树，没有死，只是正在死去，横卧在地，压碎折断，仿佛被某只巨足践踏过。你会撞见五六根藤蔓绞成一根巨缆。在有椰子树的地方，你挪不了一码不踢到落地的椰子；你可以捡到长成的、半大的、还有小得不如苹果的婴孩椰子，因为在同一棵树上，本就结着大小各样、生熟各态的果子。

===Original===
One never sees a perfectly straight-stemmed cocoa-palm; they all have an inclination from the perpendicular more or less; perhaps that is why a cyclone has more effect on them than on other trees.

Artus, once so pretty a picture with their diamond-chequered trunks, lay broken and ruined; and right through the belt of mammee apple, right through the bad lands, lay a broad road, as if an army, horse, foot, and artillery, had passed that way from lagoon edge to lagoon edge. This was the path left by the great forefoot of the storm; but had you searched the woods on either side, you would have found paths where the lesser winds had been at work, where the baby whirlwinds had been at play.

From the bruised woods, like an incense offered to heaven, rose a perfume of blossoms gathered and scattered, of rain-wet leaves, of lianas twisted and broken and oozing their sap; the perfume of newly-wrecked and ruined trees﻿—the essence and soul of the artu, the banyan and cocoa-palm cast upon the wind.

===Chinese===
谁也没见过树干笔直的椰子树；它们多少都有些偏离垂直的倾侧；也许正因如此，气旋对它们比对别的树更见效。

阿尔图树，树干曾经织着菱纹、如画一般好看，如今折断摧折，横陈满地；横贯曼密苹果林带，横贯那片烂地，躺着一条宽阔的路，仿佛一支大军——骑兵、步兵和炮兵——曾从礁湖这头开到那头。这是风暴巨大的前掌踏出的路径；可你若搜索两侧的树林，还会找到那些较小的风干活的地方、那些小小旋风嬉戏的地方留下的路。

从受创的树林里，像一炷献给上天的香，升起一股芬芳：采撷又抛散的花，雨淋的叶子，绞断的藤蔓渗出的汁液；新遭摧折的树木的芬芳——阿尔图、榕树（banyan）和椰子树的精魂，抛在了风上。

===Original===
You would have found dead butterflies in the woods, dead birds too; but in the great path of the storm you would have found dead butterflies’ wings, feathers, leaves frayed as if by fingers, branches of the aoa, and sticks of the hibiscus broken into little fragments.

Powerful enough to rip a ship open, root up a tree, half ruin a city. Delicate enough to tear a butterfly wing from wing﻿—that is a cyclone.

Emmeline, wandering about in the woods with Dick on the day after the storm, looking at the ruin of great tree and little bird, and recollecting the land birds she had caught a glimpse of yesterday being carried along safely by the storm out to sea to be drowned, felt a great weight lifting from her heart. Mischance had come, and spared them and the baby. The blue had spoken, but had not called them.

===Chinese===
在林中你会找到死去的蝴蝶，还有死去的鸟；而在风暴的大道上，你会找到蝴蝶残存的翅膀、羽毛、像被手指撕碎的叶子，阿奥阿树（aoa）的枝条，还有折成小小碎段的木槿枝。

强大得足以撕开一艘船，拔起一棵树，毁掉半座城。又精细得足以把蝴蝶的一翼从另一翼上撕下来——这就是气旋。

风暴次日，埃米琳随迪克在林间走着，看着大树与小鸟的劫毁，想起昨天瞥见的那些陆鸟被风暴安然携走、带到海上淹死，只觉一块大石从心头卸下。厄运来过了，却放过了他们和婴儿。那片蔚蓝开口了，但没有点他们的名。

===Original===
She felt that something﻿—the something which we in civilisation call Fate﻿—was for the present gorged; and, without being annihilated, her incessant hypochondriacal dread condensed itself into a point, leaving her horizon sunlit and clear.

The cyclone had indeed treated them almost, one might say, amiably. It had taken the house﻿—but that was a small matter, for it had left them nearly all their small possessions. The tinder box and flint and steel would have been a much more serious loss than a dozen houses, for, without it, they would have had absolutely no means of making a fire.

If anything, the cyclone had been almost too kind to them; had let them pay off too little of that mysterious debt they owed to the gods.

===Chinese===
她感觉到那东西——就是我们文明人叫作命运的那东西——眼下已经吃饱了；而她那无休止的疑病般的恐惧，虽未消失，也凝缩成了一个点，留给她一片阳光普照、澄澈明朗的地平线。

说起来，气旋待他们几乎算得上仁至义尽。它拿走了房子——可那是小事，因为它几乎把他们的全部小家当都留下了。火绒盒和火石火镰若丢了，可比丢十几座房子严重得多，因为没有它们，他们便绝对再没有生火的法子。

若说有什么不妥，倒是气旋对他们过于宽厚了；欠诸神的那笔神秘的债，让他们还得太少了。
