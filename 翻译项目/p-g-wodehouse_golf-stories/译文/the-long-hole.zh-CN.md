## The Long Hole / 长洞

===Original===
The young man, as he sat filling his pipe in the clubhouse smoking-room, was inclined to be bitter.

===Chinese===

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Chinese===

===Chinese===

===Original===

You know the sort of excrescences. Oh, well, a tick’s a tick, and there’s nothing more to say, I suppose.”

===Chinese===
