## VII / 七

===Original===
At ten o’clock the next morning Dick Martin walked blithely into Lincoln’s Inn Fields. The birds were twittering in the high trees, the square lay bathed in pale April sunshine, and as for Slick, he was at peace with the world, though he had travelled nigh on thirty thousand miles and had failed to report at the end of them.

Messrs. Havelock and Havelock occupied an old Queen Anne house that stood shoulder to shoulder with other mansions of the period. A succession of brass plates on the door announced this as the registered office of a dozen corporations, for Mr. Havelock was a company lawyer, who, though he never appeared in the courts, gave the inestimable benefit of his advice to innumerable and prosperous corporations.

===Chinese===
第二天上午十点，迪克·马丁步履轻快地走进林肯律师学院广场。高高的树梢上鸟雀啁啾，广场沐浴在四月淡淡的阳光里；至于滑头本人，虽说跑了将近三万英里，到头来却落得个无话可报，此刻倒也与这个世界相安无事。

哈夫洛克与哈夫洛克事务所（Messrs. Havelock and Havelock）坐落在一座安妮女王时代的老宅里，与同期营造的几座宅邸比肩而立。门上一块挨着一块的黄铜铭牌昭示着，这里是一打公司的注册办公处，因为哈夫洛克先生是位公司法律师——他虽从不出庭，却把本人高见的莫大裨益，施与数不清的生意兴旺的公司。

===Original===
Evidently the detective was expected, for the clerk in the outer office was almost genial.

“I will tell Mr. Havelock you’re here,” he said, and came back in a few seconds to beckon the wanderer into the private sanctum of the senior partner.

===Chinese===
侦探显然在对方的预料之中，因为外间办公室的那位职员简直称得上和颜悦色。

“我这就去禀报哈夫洛克先生您到了。”他说，不出几秒钟便回来，招手引这位浪迹天涯的归客，走进大合伙人的私密内室。

===Original===
As Dick Martin came in, he was finishing the dictation of a letter, and he smiled a welcome and nodded to a chair. When the dictation was done and the homely stenographer dismissed, he got up from the big writing-table, filling his pipe.

===Chinese===
迪克·马丁进屋时，他正口授完一封信的末尾，含笑相迎，又朝一把椅子点了点头。口授完毕，那位相貌家常的速记员被打发走后，他便从大写字台后起身，往烟斗里装起烟丝来。

===Original===
“So you didn’t see him?” he asked.

“No, sir. I moved fast, but he was quicker. I got into Rio the day he left. I was in Cape Town just three days after he had gone on overland to Beira﻿—and then I had your cable.”

===Chinese===
“这么说，你没见着他？”他问。

“没有，先生。我动作够快，可他更快。我抵达里约（Rio）那天，他刚动身离开。等我赶到开普敦（Cape Town），他取道陆路去了贝拉（Beira）才不过三天——紧接着我就收到了您的电报。”

===Original===
Havelock nodded solemnly, puffing at his pipe.

“The erratic devil!” he said. “You might have come up with him at Beira. He’s there yet.”

===Chinese===
哈夫洛克衔着烟斗吞云吐雾，郑重地点了点头。

“这个行踪不定的鬼东西！”他说。“你在贝拉本来兴许能追上他。他眼下还在那儿呢。”

===Original===
Walking to his desk he pressed a bell, and his secretary made a reappearance.

“Give me the Selford file﻿—the current one,” he said, and waited until she had returned and given him a large blue folder. From this he took a cable form and handed it to his visitor. Slick read:

===Chinese===
他踱到写字台前按了按铃，秘书再度现身。

“把塞尔福德的卷宗拿来——要最新的那份。”他说，随后等她回来、递给他一个大蓝文件夹。他从里面抽出一张电报单，交给来客。滑头读到：

===Original===
Havelock London. Who is this Man Martin chasing after me? Have already mailed power of attorney. Please leave me alone. Shall be in London August.﻿—Pierce.

The cable was dated Cape Town three days before Dick had arrived there.

===Chinese===
哈夫洛克，伦敦。这个叫马丁的人是谁，为何对我穷追不舍？授权书（power of attorney）已寄出。请别再来纠缠。八月回伦敦。——皮尔斯（Pierce）

这份电报发自开普敦，日期在迪克抵达那里的三天之前。

===Original===
“I could hardly do anything else,” said Mr. Havelock, rubbing his nose irritably with his knuckle. “Did you hear anything about him?”

Dick chuckled.

===Chinese===
“我几乎别无选择。”哈夫洛克先生说着，用指关节烦躁地蹭着鼻子。“关于他，你可曾听到过什么消息？”

迪克轻声笑了。

===Original===
“That fellow didn’t stand still long enough for anyone to notice him,” he said. “I’ve talked to hotel porters and reception clerks in seven kinds of broken English, and none of ’em had anything on him. He was in Cape Town the day the new High Commissioner arrived from England.”

===Chinese===
“那家伙从不在一个地方站得够久，让人来得及留意他。”他说。“我操着七种磕磕绊绊的英语，同旅馆搬运工和接待员们逐一攀谈，他们谁也说不出他半点情况。新任高级专员（High Commissioner）从英国抵达的那天，他正在开普敦。”

===Original===
“Well,” asked Havelock after a pause, “what has that to do with it?”

“Nothing.” Another pause, and then: “What do you really suspect?” asked Dick.

===Chinese===
“嗯，”哈夫洛克顿了顿才问，“这跟那件事有什么相干？”

“毫无相干。”又顿了一顿，随后迪克问：“您心里真正怀疑的是什么？”

===Original===
Mr. Havelock pursed his lips.

“I don’t know,” he admitted frankly. “At the worst that he has married, or has become entangled in some way with a lady whom he is not anxious to bring to England.”

===Chinese===
哈夫洛克先生抿紧了嘴唇。

“我说不好。”他坦然承认。“往最坏处想，无非是他已经结婚，或是同某位女士纠缠不清，因而不急于带她回英国。”

===Original===
Dick fingered his chin thoughtfully.

“Have you had much correspondence from him?” And, when the other nodded: “May I see it?”

===Chinese===
迪克若有所思地摩挲着下巴。

“他的来信您收得多吗？”见对方点头，又问：“可以让我看看吗？”

===Original===
He took the portfolio from Mr. Havelock’s hand and turned the leaves. There were cablegrams, addressed from various parts of the world, long and short letters, brief instructions, obviously in reply to some query that Havelock had sent.

===Chinese===
他从哈夫洛克先生手里接过文件夹，一页页翻检。里面有从世界各地发来的电报，有长信短信，还有几条简短的指示——显然是在答复哈夫洛克去函的询问。

===Original===
“Those only take you back for a year. I have one or two cases filled with his letters, if you would like to see them?”

Dick shook his head.

“These are all in his handwriting?”

===Chinese===
“这些只能上溯到一年以前。我还有一两箱他写来的信，您要不要看看？”

迪克摇了摇头。

“这些通通都是他的亲笔？”

===Original===
“Undoubtedly. There is no question that he is being impersonated, if that is what you mean.”

===Chinese===
“毫无疑问。倘若您是想问有没有人冒名顶替，那是绝无可能的事。”

===Original===
The detective handed back the portfolio with a little grimace.

“I wish I’d caught up with him,” he said. “I’d like to see what kind of bird he is, though I know a dozen young fellows whose feet start itching the moment they sit still. I’m sorry I haven’t been more successful Mr. Havelock, but, as I say, this lad is a swift mover. Maybe at some later time I’ll ask to see the whole of these letters; I’d like to study them.”

===Chinese===
侦探把文件夹递了回去，微微做了个苦相。

“真希望我当初追上了他。”他说。“我倒想见识见识这是只什么鸟儿，虽说我也认识十来个小伙子，屁股一沾板凳脚底就发痒。很抱歉我没能办得更漂亮些，哈夫洛克先生，不过正如我所说，这位小哥是个脚底抹油、行动如风的主儿。也许往后什么时候，我会请求把这些信从头到尾看一遍；我很想研读研读。”

===Original===
“You can see them now if you wish,” said the lawyer, reaching for the bell.

The detective stopped him.

===Chinese===
“您若想看，现在就可以看。”律师说着，伸手去够电铃。

侦探拦住了他。

===Original===
“So far as the alliance is concerned, I think you can rest your mind. He was alone in New York and alone in San Francisco. He landed without any encumbrances at Shanghai, and I trailed him through India without there being a hint of a lady in the case. When he comes back in August I’d like to meet him.”

===Chinese===
“就联姻这档子事而言，我想您尽可以把心放进肚里。他在纽约（New York）是只身一人，在旧金山（San Francisco）也是只身一人。他在上海（Shanghai）登岸时未带任何累赘，我一路尾随他横穿印度（India），全程不见半点有女士牵涉其间的影子。等他八月回来，我想同他见上一面。”

===Original===
“You shall.” Mr. Havelock smiled grimly. “If I can nail him down long enough to give you time to get here.”

===Chinese===
“你会见着的。”哈夫洛克先生冷冷一笑。“只要我能把他钉在一个地方钉得够久，好让你来得及赶到这儿。”

===Original===
Dick went home, turning over in his mind two important problems, in his pocket a very handsome cheque for his services. The elderly woman who kept house for him was out marketing when he arrived. Sitting down at his desk, his head in his hands, his untidy hair rumpled outrageously, he went over the last six exciting months of his life, and at the end the question in his mind was not answered. Presently he pulled the telephone towards him and called Havelock.

===Chinese===
迪克回了家，脑中翻来覆去转着两个重大的疑问，口袋里揣着一张酬劳相当可观的支票。他到家时，替他料理家务的老太太出门采买去了。他坐到写字台前，双手抱头，一头乱发揉搓得不成体统，把这六个月惊险刺激的经历从头到尾检视了一遍，到头来，心里的疑问依旧悬而未决。过了一会儿，他把电话拉到面前，拨给了哈夫洛克。

===Original===
“I forgot to ask you, why does he call himself Pierce?”

“Who? Oh, you mean Selford? That is his name. Pierce, John Pierce. I forgot to explain to you that he hated his title. Oh, did I? Have you an idea?”

===Chinese===
“我忘了问您，他为什么自称皮尔斯？”

“谁？哦，您是说塞尔福德？那正是他的本名。皮尔斯，约翰·皮尔斯（John Pierce）。我忘了向您解释，他厌恶自己的爵位。哦，我说过了吗？您有想法了？”

===Original===
“None,” said Slick untruthfully, for he had several ideas.

He had unpacked all but one suitcase, and this he now proceeded to turn on to the table. It was full of documents, hotel bills, notes he had made in the course of his tour; and at the bottom of the case a square sheet of blotting-paper, which he took out carefully and held up to the light. It was the blotted impression left by an envelope:

===Chinese===
“没有。”滑头答道，答得并不老实——他心里的想法多着呢。

行李箱他都已解开，只剩一只，此刻便把它翻个底朝天倒在桌上。箱里塞满了文件、旅馆账单，还有他旅途中所作的笔记；箱底垫着一方吸墨纸（blotting-paper），他小心翼翼地取出来，举到亮处照看。纸上洇着一个信封留下的印迹：

===Original===
Mr. Bertram Cody, Weald House, South Weald, Sussex.

There was no need to refresh his memory, for he had made a very careful note of the name and address. He had found that sheet of blotting-paper in the private sitting-room at the Plaza Hotel in Buenos Aires which had been occupied, forty-eight hours before his arrival, by the restless Mr. Pierce. Nobody had used the room after he had gone until Dick had asked the hotel manager to show him the suite which his quarry had occupied.

===Chinese===
贝特伦·科迪先生，威尔德宅邸，南威尔德，萨塞克斯。

他无需借此唤起记忆，因为这个姓名和地址他早已仔仔细细记下了。那方吸墨纸，是他在布宜诺斯艾利斯（Buenos Aires）广场饭店（Plaza Hotel）的一间私人起居室里找到的——在他抵达的四十八小时之前，坐立不定的皮尔斯先生占用的正是那间屋。皮尔斯走后再无人用过那房间，直到迪克请饭店经理带他去看看这只猎物住过的那套客房。

===Original===
He locked away the blotting-paper in a drawer of his desk, strolled into his bedroom, and stood for a long time looking at himself in the glass.

“Call yourself a detective, eh?” he demanded of his reflection, as his lips curled. “You poor, four-flushing mutt!”

===Chinese===
他把那方吸墨纸锁进写字台的抽屉，踱进卧室，对着镜中的自己端详了老半天。

“你也好意思自称侦探，嗯？”他冲着镜中身影喝问，嘴角一撇。“你这可怜的、只会虚张声势的蠢货！”

===Original===
He spent the remainder of the day learning a new card trick that he had picked up on the voyage over; an intricate piece of work, consisting of palming a card from the top of the pack and passing it so that it became the ninth card of the pack. With a stopwatch before him he practised, until he managed to accomplish the transfer in the fifteenth part of a second. Then he was satisfied. When dusk descended on the world, he got out his car and drove southward leisurely.

===Chinese===
这一天剩下的工夫，他都用来操练在归途上学到的一手新牌戏；那是件精细活儿——从整副牌的顶端掌心藏下一张牌，再移牌换位，使它变成整副牌的第九张。他面前摆着秒表练了又练，直到能在十五分之一秒内完成这一移转，方才心满意足。暮色四合、天光沉入昏暗之际，他开出汽车，从容不迫地向南驶去。
