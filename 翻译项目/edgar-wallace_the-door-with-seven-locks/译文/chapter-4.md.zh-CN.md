## IV / 四

===Original===

There was a silence of a minute. Dick looked at the man, hardly believing his ears.

===Chinese===

沉默了约莫一分钟。迪克望着这个人，简直不敢相信自己的耳朵。

===Original===

“Trying to open a dead man’s tomb?” he repeated. “Now sit down and tell me all about it, Lew.”

“I can’t﻿—yet. I’m scared,” said the other doggedly. “This man is hell, and I’d as soon face the devil as go through another night like I had on Tuesday.”

===Chinese===

“想撬开一个死人的坟墓？”他重复了一遍，“好了，坐下吧，把事情一五一十讲给我听，卢。”

“我还说不得——暂时说不得。我害怕呀。”对方执拗地说，“那人简直是个恶魔，我宁可去直面魔鬼，也不愿再熬一个星期二那样的夜晚。”

===Original===

“Who is the man?”

“I won’t tell you that,” said the other sullenly. “I might at the end, but I won’t tell you now. If I can find a quiet place I’m going to write it all out, and have it on paper in case﻿—anything happens to me.”

===Chinese===

“那人是谁？”

“这我可不会告诉你。”对方闷闷地说，“到最后兴许会说，现在是不说的。我要是能寻个清静地方，就把这一切原原本本写下来，白纸黑字留在纸上，万一——我出了什么事。”

===Original===

He was obviously labouring under a sense of unusual excitement, and Dick, who had known him for many years, both in England and in Canada, was amazed to see this usually phlegmatic man in such a condition of nerves.

He refused to take the dinner that the old housekeeper served, contenting himself with a whisky and soda, and Dick Martin thought it wise not to attempt to question him any further.

===Chinese===

他显然正被一阵异乎寻常的激动折磨着；迪克在英国也好、加拿大也好，同他相识多年，眼见这个平日处变不惊的人落得这般神经紧张，不由得暗暗称奇。

老管家端上晚饭，他不肯动箸，只喝了杯威士忌兑苏打便算了；迪克·马丁觉得，还是别再往下盘问的为妙。

===Original===

“Why don’t you stay here tonight and write your story? I won’t ask you for it, but you’ll be as safe here as anywhere.”

===Chinese===

“今晚你何不就待在这儿，把你的事写下来？我不会跟你讨看，再说你在这儿和在任何地方一样安全。”

===Original===

That idea seemed already to have occurred to the man, for he obeyed instantly, and Dick gathered that he had such a scheme in his mind. Dinner was nearly through when the detective was called away to the phone.

===Chinese===

这个主意那人心目中似乎早就有了，因为他二话不说便依了；迪克这才咂摸出来，他原就存着这份打算。晚饭将近用完时，侦探被人叫去接电话。

===Original===

“Is that Mr. Martin?”

The voice was that of a stranger.

“Yes,” replied Dick.

===Chinese===

“是马丁先生吗？”

说话的是一个陌生的嗓音。

“是我。”迪克答道。

===Original===

“I am Mr. Havelock. The Commissioner sent me a message this evening, and I was expecting you to call at my office. I wonder if you could see me tonight?”

There was anxiety and urgency in the tone.

===Chinese===

“我是哈夫洛克。总监今晚给我捎了个信，我本还指望你到我事务所来一趟。不知今晚你可愿见我一面？”

语气里透着焦灼，还带着急迫。

===Original===

“Why, surely,” said Dick. “Where are you living?”

“907, Acacia Road, St. John’s Wood. I am very near to you; a taxi would get you here in five minutes. Have you dined? I was afraid you had. Will you come up to coffee in about a quarter of an hour?”

===Chinese===

“哦，那自然。”迪克说，“您住在哪儿？”

“圣约翰伍德（St. John's Wood），阿卡西亚路（Acacia Road）907号。我离你很近，坐出租车五分钟就到。你用过晚饭没有？我倒怕你是用过了。一刻钟后上我这儿来喝杯咖啡，可好？”

===Original===

Dick Martin had agreed before he realized that his guest and his strange story had to be considered.

The startling announcement of Lew Pheeney had changed his plans. Yet it might be advisable to leave the man to write his story. He called his housekeeper aside and dismissed her for the night. Pheeney, alone in the flat, might write his story without interruption.

===Chinese===

迪克·马丁应承下来之后，才想起家里那位客人，还有他那离奇的故事，不能不掂量掂量。

卢·菲尼那句耸人听闻的招认已打乱了他的计划。不过，把这人撂下安心写他的故事，倒也不失为稳妥之策。他把老管家唤到一旁，打发她回去歇息。菲尼独自留在公寓里，正好不受打搅地写他的东西。

===Original===

The man readily agreed to his suggestion, seemed, in fact, relieved at the prospect of being alone, and a quarter of an hour later Mr. Martin was ringing the bell of an imposing house that stood in its acre of garden in the best part of St. Johns Wood. An elderly butler took his stick and hat and conducted him into a long dining-room, furnished with quiet taste. Evidently Mr. Havelock was something of a connoisseur, for of the four pictures that hung on the wall, Dick accurately placed one as being by Corot, and the big portrait over the carved mantelpiece was undoubtedly a Rembrandt.

===Chinese===

那人一听这提议便满口应承，看那样子，能得个独处的机会倒像是松了口气。一刻钟之后，马丁先生已站在圣约翰伍德最上等地段一座气派宅邸的门首按铃，宅子连同占地一英亩的花园自成一片天地。一位上了年纪的管家接过他的手杖和帽子，引他走进一间陈设清雅的长餐厅。哈夫洛克先生显然颇有几分鉴赏家的眼力：壁上挂着四幅画，迪克一眼便断定其中一幅出自柯罗（Corot）手笔，雕花壁炉架上方那幅大肖像，则毫无疑问是伦勃朗（Rembrandt）。

===Original===

The lawyer was dining in solitary state at the end of a long, polished table. A glass of red wine stood at his elbow, a long, thin cigar was between his teeth. He was a man between fifty and sixty, tall and rather thin. He had the brow and jaw of a fighter, and his iron-grey side-whiskers gave him a certain ferocious appearance. Dick liked him, for the eyes behind his horn-rimmed spectacles were very attractive.

===Chinese===

律师正独自一人在一张擦得锃亮的长桌尽头用餐，俨然一派威仪。肘边搁着一杯红葡萄酒，齿间衔着一支细长的雪茄。他五十到六十之间年纪，高而偏瘦，生就一副斗士的额头与下颚，铁灰色的连鬓络腮胡子又给他添了几分狰狞气象。迪克却对他颇有好感，因为角质框眼镜后面的那双眼睛，十分惹人喜爱。

===Original===

“Mr. Martin, eh?” He half rose and offered his firm, thin hand. “Sit down. What will you drink? I have a port here that was laid down for princes. Walters, give Mr. Martin a glass.”

===Chinese===

“马丁先生，嗯？”他半抬起身，伸出干瘦而有力的手。“请坐。喝点什么？我这儿有一款波特酒，当年窖下时原是备着款待王公的。沃尔特斯（Walters），给马丁先生斟一杯。”

===Original===

He leaned back in his chair, his lips pursed, and regarded the young man fixedly.

“So you’re a detective, eh?” It sounded reminiscent of an experience he had had that morning, and Dick grinned. “The commissioner says you’re leaving the police force tomorrow, and that you want a hobby. By heavens, I’ll furnish you a hobby that’ll save me a lot of sleepless nights! Walters, serve Mr. Martin and clear out. And I am not to be interrupted. Switch off the phone; I’m not at home to anybody, however important.”

===Chinese===

他往椅背上一靠，抿着嘴唇，定睛打量这个年轻人。

“这么说，你是个侦探，嗯？”这话听着叫他想起当天早晨的一段遭遇，迪克咧嘴笑了。“总监说你明天就要脱离警队，还说你正想寻个消遣。老天在上，我给你寻的这桩消遣，保管能省下我许多个不眠之夜！沃尔特斯，伺候马丁先生用过就都退下。我不见外客，不许打搅。把电话关掉——天大的事，就说我不在家。”

===Original===

When the door had closed behind the butler, Mr. Havelock rose and began a restless pacing of the room. He had a quick, abrupt, almost offensively brusque manner, jerking out his sentences accusatively.

===Chinese===

管家退出、房门合拢之后，哈夫洛克先生便站起身来，焦躁地在屋里踱来踱去。他举止急促生硬，唐突得近乎无礼，一句句话像蹦豆子似的往外迸，字字带着诘问的火气。

===Original===

“I’m a lawyer﻿—you probably know my name, though I’ve never been in a police court in my life. I’m very seldom in any court of law. I deal with companies and estates, and I’m trustee for half a dozen, or maybe a dozen, various charities. I’m the trustee of the Selford estate.” He said this with a certain emphasis, as though he thought that Dick would understand the peculiar significance of this. “I’m the trustee of the Selford estate,” he said again, “and I wish to heaven I wasn’t. Old Lord Selford﻿—not that he was old, except in sin and iniquity, but the late Lord Selford, let me say﻿—left me the sole executor of his property and guardian of his wretched child. The late Lord Selford was a very unpleasant, bad-tempered man, half mad, as most of the Selfords have been for generations. Do you know Selford Manor?”

===Chinese===

“我是个律师——我的名字你多半听说过，虽然我这辈子没进过治安法庭，旁的法庭也难得踏足。我做的是公司和产业方面的业务，还兼着六家、兴许一打各类慈善机构的受托人。我是塞尔福德（Selford）产业的受托人。”他说这一句时特意加重了语气，仿佛认为迪克自会掂量出这话的特别分量。“我是塞尔福德产业的受托人，”他又说了一遍，“我巴不得老天爷当初没把这差事派给我。老塞尔福德勋爵——他也并非真的老，只是论作孽使坏堪称老资格；这么说吧，已故的塞尔福德勋爵——把产业的唯一遗嘱执行人一职丢给了我，连他那可怜孩子的监护之责也一并撂在我肩上。这位已故的塞尔福德勋爵脾气极坏，是个顶讨人嫌的人物，疯了一半——塞尔福德家世世代代大抵都是这路货色。你可知道塞尔福德庄园（Selford Manor）？”

===Original===

Dick smiled.

“Curiously enough, I was on the edge of it today. I didn’t know there was such a place until this afternoon, and I had no such idea there was a Lord Selford﻿—does he live there?”

===Chinese===

迪克莞尔一笑。

“说来也巧，我今天刚打那庄园边上经过。到今天下午为止，我还不知道有这么个地方，也压根没想到世上还有位塞尔福德勋爵——他就住在那儿么？”

===Original===

“He doesn’t.” Havelock snapped the words, his eyes gleaming fiercely from behind his glasses. “I wish to God he did! He lives nowhere. That is to say, he lives nowhere longer than two or three days together. He is a nomad of nomads; his father in his youth was something of the same nature. Pierce﻿—that is his family name, by the way, and he has always been called Pierce﻿—has spent the last ten years wandering from town to town, from country to country, drawing heavily upon his revenue, as he can well afford to do because it is a large one, and returning to England only at the rarest intervals. I haven’t seen him for four years.” He said this slowly.

===Chinese===

“他不住那儿。”哈夫洛克把这几个字咬得斩钉截铁，眼镜后面双眼凶光毕露。“我倒求上天让他住那儿呢！他哪儿也不住。这么说吧，天底下没有一个地方他能连住两三天以上。他是个天字第一号的流浪汉；他父亲年轻时也有几分这样的秉性。皮尔斯（Pierce）——顺便说一句，这是他的家姓，而且大家向来只叫他皮尔斯——这十年间逛了一城又一城，走了一国又一国，大手大脚地支取他的进项——他那份进项着实丰厚，也禁得起他这般花销——难得回英国一遭。我已经四年没见过他了。”这几句话，他说得很慢。

===Original===

“I’ll give you his history, Mr. Martin, so that you will understand it better,” he went on. “When Selford died, Pierce was six. He had no mother, and, curiously enough, no near relations. Selford was an only child, and his wife was also in that position, so that there were no uncles and aunts to whom I could have handed over my responsibility. The boy was delicate, as I found when I sent him to a preparatory school at the age of eight, expecting to be rid of the poor little beggar, but not a day passed that he didn’t send me a note asking to be taken away. Eventually I found a private tutor for him, and he got some sort of education. It was not good enough to enable him to pass the Little Go﻿—that is the entrance examination to Cambridge﻿—and I sent him abroad with his tutor to travel. I wish to heaven I hadn’t! For the travel bug bit deep into his soul, and he’s been moving ever since. Four years ago he came to me in London. He was then on his way to America, where he was studying economic conditions. He had a wild idea of writing a book﻿—one of the delusions from which most people suffer is that other people are interested in their recollections.”

===Chinese===

“我把他这段来历讲给你听，马丁先生，你也好把事情看得更明白。”他接着说，“老塞尔福德去世时，皮尔斯才六岁。他没了母亲，说来也怪，近亲也一个没有。塞尔福德是独子，他妻子也是独女，因此我连个可以移交责任的叔伯姑姨都寻不出。这孩子身子单薄——我送他上预备学校那年他八岁，本指望就此脱开这可怜的小家伙，谁知他没有一天不捎张字条来求我把他领回去。到头来我给他延请了一位家庭教师，他总算受了些教育。可那点教育还不足以叫他通过‘小考’（Little Go）——也就是剑桥大学的入学考试——我便打发他随家庭教师出国游历去了。我真悔不该多此一举！游历这条毒虫深深刻进了他的魂里，打那以后他就再没停过脚。四年前他来伦敦寻我，那时他正要取道赴美，说是考察经济状况。他有个狂想的念头，要写一部书——世人多半都害着一种错觉，总以为旁人对自己的回忆录津津有味。”

===Original===

Dick flushed guiltily, but the lawyer went on, without apparently noticing his embarrassment.

===Chinese===

迪克心虚地红了脸，律师却径自往下说，像是全然不曾留意他的窘态。

===Original===

“Now I’m worried about this boy. From time to time demands come through to me for money, and from time to time I cable him very respectable sums﻿—which, of course, he is entitled to receive, for he is now twenty-four.”

===Chinese===

“如今我正为这孩子悬着心。他隔三差五便递话来支钱，我也隔三差五电汇给他一笔笔数目可观的款项——这自然是他名分上应得的，他如今已二十四岁了。”

===Original===

“His financial position﻿—” began Dick.

“Perfectly sound, perfectly sound,” said Mr. Havelock impressively. “That isn’t the question at all. What is worrying me is, the boy being so long out of my sight. Anything may happen to him; he may have fallen into the worst possible hands.” He hesitated, and added: “And I feel that I should get in touch with him﻿—not directly, but through a third person. In other words, I want you to go to America next week, and, without saying that you came from me, or that I sent you, get acquainted with Lord Selford﻿—he travels, by the way, as Mr. John Pierce. He is a very quick mover, and you’ll have to make careful inquiries as to where he has gone, because I cannot promise that I can keep you as well informed of his movements as I should like. If, in your absence, I have a cable from him, I will, of course, transmit it to you. I want you to find Pierce, but in no circumstances are you to acquaint the police of America that you are following him, or that there is anything suspicious in his movements. All that I want to know is, Has he contracted any undesirable alliance, is he a free agent, is the money I send to him being employed for his own benefit? He tells me, by the way, that he has bought a number of shares in industrial concerns in various parts of the world, and some of these shares are in my possession. A great number, however, I cannot account for, and he has replied to my inquiries by telling me that they are safely deposited with a South African banking corporation. The reason I ask you to keep this matter entirely to yourself is because, you will understand, I can’t have him embarrassed by the attentions of the local authorities. And most earnestly I am desirous that he should not know I sent you. Now, Mr. Martin, how does the idea appeal to you?”

===Chinese===

“他的财务状况——”迪克刚一开腔。

“好端端的，好端端的。”哈夫洛克先生一字一顿地说，“那根本不是问题的要害。叫我悬心的是，这孩子太长久不在我的眼皮底下。什么祸事都轮得上他沾边；说不定他已经落进顶顶糟糕的手掌心里了。”他顿了顿，又补上一句，“我寻思着，该同他通上气——不亲自出面，而是经由第三者。换句话说，我要你下星期去一趟美国，在不透露你从我这儿来、也不提是我差遣的前提下，设法结识塞尔福德勋爵——对了，他出门在外用的名号是约翰·皮尔斯（John Pierce）先生。他行踪飘忽，你得仔细打听他到了何处，因为虽则我巴不得如此，我却不敢担保能把他的行止源源本本地告知于你。你不在期间，我若接到他的电报，当然即刻转给你。我要你找到皮尔斯，但无论如何，不许让美国的警方知道你在追踪他，也不许他们疑心他的行动有什么见不得人。我想要弄清的不过是：他可曾结下什么不相宜的姻缘？他可还是自由之身？我汇给他的钱，可曾用在他自己身上？对了，他告诉我，他在世界各地买下了一批实业股份，其中有一部分股票就存在我手里。可另有一大批我对不上账，我去函查问，他回话说都稳稳当当地存放在南非的一家银行里了。我之所以要你把这件事整个儿烂在肚里，是因为——你自会明白——我不能让他被当地当局的殷勤照拂弄得下不来台。还有一层，我千叮万嘱的是，绝不能叫他知道是我派你去的。怎么样，马丁先生，这个差事合你的心意么？”

===Original===

Dick smiled.

“It looks to me like a very pleasant sort of holiday. How long will this chase last?”

===Chinese===

迪克笑了。

“在我看来，这倒像是一场颇为惬意的度假。这趟追寻要延续多久？”

===Original===

“I don’t know﻿—a few months, a few weeks: it all depends upon the report I receive from you, which, by the way, must be cabled to me direct. I have a very free hand and I can allow you the limit of expenses; in addition to which I will pay you a handsome fee.”

He named a sum which was surprisingly munificent.

===Chinese===

“说不准——几个月，几个星期，全看你给我的报告行事；顺便交代一句，报告必须直接拍电报给我。我手中权限极宽，经费任你花到上限为止；此外我还将付你一笔可观的酬金。”

他报出的那个数目，阔绰得出人意料。

===Original===

“When would you want me to go?”

===Chinese===

“您要我什么时候动身？”

===Original===

The lawyer took out a little pocketbook and evidently consulted a calendar.

“Today is Wednesday; suppose you leave next Wednesday by the Cunarder? At present he is in Boston, but he tells me that he is going to New York, where he will be staying at the Commodore. Boston is a favourite hunting ground of his.” His lips twitched. “I believe he intends sparing a chapter to the American War of Independence,” he said dryly; “and, naturally, Boston will afford him an excellent centre for that study.”

===Chinese===

律师掏出一本小记事簿，显然是查了查日历。

“今天是星期三；就下星期三搭冠达邮轮（Cunarder）动身，如何？他眼下在波士顿，不过他告诉我说要去纽约，届时将下榻科莫多旅馆（the Commodore）。波士顿是他中意的一处猎场。”他嘴唇微微一抽。“我相信，他打算为美国独立战争匀出一章的篇幅。”他干巴巴地说，“而波士顿呢，自然会给这项研究提供一个绝好的根据地。”

===Original===

“One question,” said Dick, as he rose to go. “Have you any reason to suppose that he has contracted, as you say, an undesirable alliance﻿—in other words, has married somebody that he shouldn’t have married?”

===Chinese===

“还有一问。”迪克起身告辞时说，“您可有半点根据，疑心他结下了您所说的不相宜的姻缘——换句话说，娶了不该娶的人？”

===Original===

“No reason at all, except my suspicious mind,” smiled Mr. Havelock. “If you become friendly with him, as I am perfectly sure with an effort you can succeed in doing, there are certain things I would like you to urge upon him. The first of these is that he come back to England and takes his seat in the House of Peers. That is very essential. Then I should like him to have a London season, because it’s high time he was married and off my mind. Selford Manor is going to ruin for want of an occupant.
