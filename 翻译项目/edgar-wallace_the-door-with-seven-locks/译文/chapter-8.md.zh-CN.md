## VIII / 八

===Original===
“Show him in,” said Mr. Bertram Cody.

He was a little bald man with a gentle voice and the habit of redundancy. He required five minutes to say all that any other man could express in three sentences. Of this fault, if fault it was, he was well aware and made a jest of his weakness.
===Chinese===
“让他进来。”贝特伦·科迪先生说。

他是个秃顶的小个子男人，嗓音温和，说起话来却有絮叨的毛病。旁人三句话就能说完的事，他非得用上五分钟不可。这个缺点——倘若算得上缺点的话——他自己心里有数，还时常拿它打趣。

===Original===
Fixing his large gold-rimmed spectacles, he peered at the card again﻿—

Mr. John Rendle,

				194, Collins Street, Melbourne
===Chinese===
他扶正那副金边大眼镜，又端详起那张名片来——

约翰·伦德尔先生（Mr. John Rendle），

				墨尔本，科林斯街194号（194, Collins Street, Melbourne）

===Original===
The name meant nothing to Mr. Cody. He had known a Rendle in the eighties, a highly respectable tea importer, but the acquaintance was so slight a one that it was hardly likely.

He had been studying a small pocket notebook when the visitor was announced; a red morocco case that had, in addition to diary and writing space, a little pocket for cards, slips for stamps, and a tiny flat purse. He pushed the book under a heap of papers at his hand as the stranger entered.
===Chinese===
这名字对科迪先生毫无意义。八十年代他认识过一个姓伦德尔的，是个极体面的茶叶进口商，不过那点交情浅得很，眼前这位多半不会是同一个人。

来客通报之前，他一直在研看一本小小的袖珍记事簿：一只红色摩洛哥皮匣子，除了日记页和书写页之外，还带一个插名片的小袋、一条贴邮票的纸夹，外加一只扁平的小钱袋。生人进来时，他把这本簿子塞进了手边一叠文件底下。

===Original===
“Mr. Rendle,” said a woman’s harsh voice in the shadowy part of the room where the door was, and there came out of the gloom a tall, good-looking young man who certainly bore no resemblance to the long-forgotten China merchant.
===Chinese===
“伦德尔先生到。”房门边幽暗的角落里，一个女人粗厉的嗓音说道；接着从暗影里走出一个高挑俊朗的年轻人，同那位早被淡忘的做中国生意的商人，实在找不出半点相像。

===Original===
“Will you sit down?” said Mr. Cody gently. “And will you please forgive the semidarkness in which I live. I find that my eyes are not as good as they were, and the glare of lights produces a very painful effect. This table-lamp, carefully shaded as it is, supplies my needs adequately, though it is insufficient for my visitors. Fortunately, if you will forgive what may appear to you as a rudeness, I receive most of my callers in the daytime.”
===Chinese===
“您请坐。”科迪先生温声说道。“还请您原谅，我就惯居在这半明半暗里。我发觉自己的眼睛大不如前了，灯光太亮，刺得我极不好受。这盏台灯灯罩遮得严实，尽够我用了，只是对来访的客人来说就嫌暗了。还算走运——您若觉得这话听着失礼，还请包涵——我接见的客人多半都在白天。”

===Original===
The visitor had a quick smile, and was evidently a man to whom the semidarkness of the big, richly-furnished library was in no sense depressing. He groped in the shadows for the chair, which revealed itself by the light on its polished back, and sat down.
===Chinese===
来客脸上霎时绽出笑容，显然，这间陈设华贵的大书房里的昏暗，于他毫无不快之感。他在暗影里摸索那把椅子——椅背擦得锃亮，在灯光下显出了轮廓——便坐了下来。

===Original===
“I’m sorry to come at this hour, Mr. Cody, but I only arrived yesterday by the *Moldavia*.”

“From China,” murmured Mr. Cody.

“From Australia﻿—I transhipped at Colombo.”
===Chinese===
“抱歉这么晚登门，科迪先生，我昨天才乘莫尔达维亚号（Moldavia）到港。”

“是从中国来的吧。”科迪先生喃喃道。

“是从澳大利亚来——我在科伦坡（Colombo）转的船。”

===Original===
“The *Moldavia* did not call at Colombo owing to an outbreak of cholera,” interrupted Mr. Cody, more gently still.

The visitor laughed.
===Chinese===
“莫尔达维亚号因当地霍乱流行，没有在科伦坡停靠。”科迪先生插话道，语气愈发温和。

来客笑了。

===Original===
“On the contrary, it called, and I and some thirty passengers embarked. The outbreak was reported after we left port. You are confusing the *Moldavia* with the *Morania*, which missed the call a week later.”

The colour deepened on Mr. Cody’s plump face. He was deeply wounded, and in his most tender part, for he had been guilty of an error of fact.
===Chinese===
“恰恰相反，靠了岸，我和另外三十来名乘客就是在那儿上的船。疫情是我们出港之后才见报的。您是把莫尔达维亚号同莫拉尼亚号（Morania）弄混了——那班船晚了一个星期，才真的误了停靠。”

科迪先生那张丰满的脸涨得更红了。他深深受了伤害，而且伤在最要害的地方——他竟犯了一桩事实错误。

===Original===
“I beg your pardon,” he said in a hushed and humble voice. “I am humiliated to discover that I have made a mistake. It *was* the *Morania*﻿—I beg your pardon! The *Moldavia* had a smooth voyage?”

“No, sir. We ran into the simoon and had three boats carried away﻿—”

“The two lifeboats on the spar deck and a cutter on the aft deck,” nodded Mr. Cody. “You also lost a lascar﻿—washed overboard. Forgive me for interrupting you, I am an omnivorous reader.”
===Chinese===
“请您原谅。”他压低嗓音，谦卑地说。“发觉自己出了错，我真是无地自容。那是莫拉尼亚号，没错——请您原谅！莫尔达维亚号一路风平浪静吧？”

“不，先生。我们遇上了西蒙风（simoon），给刮走了三条艇——”

“轻甲板上的两条救生艇，外加后甲板上的一条小艇。”科迪先生点头道。“你们还损失了一名印度水手——给冲到海里去了。恕我打断您，我读书极杂，无所不窥。”

===Original===
There was a paused in the conversation here. Mr. Cody, his head on one side, waited expectantly.

“Now, perhaps﻿—?” he suggested, almost timidly.

Again the visitor smiled.
===Chinese===
谈话在这里停顿了片刻。科迪先生歪着头，满怀期待地等着。

“那么，现在——？”他近乎怯生生地提示道。

来客又笑了。

===Original===
“I’ve called on a curious errand,” he said. “I have a small farm near Ten Mile Station﻿—a property which adjoins a station of yours in that part of the world.”

Mr. Cody nodded slowly. He had many properties in the overseas States: they were profitable investments.
===Chinese===
“我这趟登门，是为一桩古怪差事。”他说。“我在十英里牧场（Ten Mile Station）附近有个小农庄——那产业同贵方在那边的一片牧场正好接壤。”

科迪先生缓缓点头。他在海外各地置有不少产业，都是生利的投资。

===Original===
“I have reason to believe that there is gold on your property,” Rendle went on. “And I take this view because I am by training an engineer and I know something about metallurgy. Six months ago I made a discovery which, very naturally, I was not anxious to advertise until I was certain of my facts.”

He talked lucidly of conglomerate and outcrop, and Bertram Cody listened, nodding his head from time to time. In the course of his description, Mr. Rendle unfolded a map on the desk﻿—a small scale map that did not interest Bertram Cody at all.
===Chinese===
“我有理由相信，贵方那片产业里有金子。”伦德尔接着说。“我持这个看法，是因为我是工程师出身，冶金学上也懂得一些。半年前我有了一个发现——这个发现嘛，自然，在事实查实之前我不急于声张。”

他把砾岩、露头讲得条理分明，贝特伦·科迪听着，不时点一点头。讲到中途，伦德尔先生在书桌上摊开一幅地图——一张小比例尺的图，贝特伦·科迪对它毫无兴趣。

===Original===
“My theory is that there is a reef running from here to here﻿—”

When his guest had reached the end of his discourse:
===Chinese===
“我的推断是，有一条矿脉从这里延伸到这里——”

等客人把话讲完：

===Original===
“Yes﻿—I know there is gold at Ten Mile Station: the discovery was made by our agent and duly reported to us, so that the fear you had, Mr.﻿—er﻿—em﻿—Rendle, that he was keeping his﻿—er﻿—find a secret, had no foundation. There is gold﻿—yes. But not in paying quantities. The matter has already been reported in the newspaper press﻿—um﻿—you would not have seen that, of course. Nevertheless, I am grateful to you. Human nature is indeed a frail quality, and I cannot sufficiently thank you for your thoughtfulness and﻿—um﻿—the trouble to which you have been put.”
===Chinese===
“是——我知道十英里牧场有金子：发现的是我们的代理商，也如实呈报给我们了，所以您先前的担心——您贵姓是，呃——唔——伦德尔——担心他把他的——呃——那个发现瞒着不报，是全无根据的。金子是有的——有。可惜没有开采价值。这件事报纸上已经登过了——唔——您自然没瞧见。尽管如此，我仍感激您。人性实在是脆弱的东西，您的体贴周到，还有——唔——您为此费的心力，我怎么谢也谢不尽。”

===Original===
“I understand that you bought this property from Lord Selford,” asserted Mr. Rendle.

The bald man blinked quickly, like a man who was dazzled by a bright light.
===Chinese===
“据我所知，这片产业是您从塞尔福德勋爵手里买下的。”伦德尔先生断言道。

秃顶的男人霎时连眨了几下眼，像叫强光晃了眼似的。

===Original===
“From his﻿—er﻿—agents: an eminent firm of lawyers. I forget their names for the moment. His lordship is abroad, you know. I believe that he is difficult to get at.” He spread out his plump hands in a gesture of helplessness. “It is difficult! This young man prefers to spend his life in travel. His agents hear of him in Africa﻿—they have a letter from the﻿—um﻿—wild pampas of the Argentine﻿—they send him money to China﻿—an adventurous life, my dear young friend, but unnerving to his﻿—um﻿—relations, if he has relations. I am not sure.”
===Chinese===
“是从他的——呃——代理人手里买下：一家大名鼎鼎的律师事务所。名字我一时想不起来了。勋爵大人在国外，您是知道的。依我看，他这人很难寻得着。”他摊开丰满的双手，做了个爱莫能助的手势。“难哪！这位年轻人宁肯把一辈子花在游历上。他的代理人在非洲得到他的音讯——他们收到过一封信，寄自——唔——阿根廷的莽莽荒原——他们往中国给他汇钱——多历险的人生啊，我亲爱的年轻朋友，只是对他的——唔——亲眷们，实在叫人揪心，倘若他有亲眷的话。我还说不准呢。”

===Original===
He shook his head and sighed; then, with a start, as though he were for the first time aware that there was an audience to his perturbations, he rose and held out both his hands.

“Thank you for coming,” he breathed, and Mr. Rendle found his own hand encased in two warm, soft palms. “Thank you for your interest. Life is a brighter place for such disinterestedness.”
===Chinese===
他摇了摇头，叹了口气；随即一怔，仿佛这才头一回发觉，自己这副失魂落魄的模样竟有人旁观，便站起身来，伸出双手。

“多谢您来。”他轻声说。伦德尔先生只觉自己的一只手被两只温软的手掌裹住了。“多谢您这份关切。世上多一分这样的无私，人间也就亮堂一分。”

===Original===
“Do you ever hear from him?” asked the visitor.

“From﻿—um﻿—his lordship? No, no! He is ignorant of my existence. Oh, dear, no!” He took the visitor’s arm and walked with him to the door. “You have a car?” He was almost grateful to his guest for the possession of such an article. “I am glad. It looks like being a stormy night﻿—and it is late. Half past ten, is it not? A safe journey to town!”
===Chinese===
“您可收到过他的音讯？”来客问。

“收到——唔——勋爵大人的信？没有，没有！他压根不知道有我这个人。哎呀，断乎没有！”他挽起来客的胳膊，陪他走到门边。“您有汽车吧？”客人竟有这么一件东西，他几乎要感激不尽。“那我就放心了。夜里怕是有风暴——时候也不早了。十点半了，是不是？祝您平平安安进城！”

===Original===
He stood under the portico until the rear lights of the car had disappeared behind a clump of rhododendrons that bordered the drive, then he went back into the hall.

The stout, hard woman in black silk, who Dick had thought was Mr. Cody’s housekeeper, followed her husband into the study and closed the door behind him.
===Chinese===
他站在门廊底下，直到汽车的尾灯隐没在车道旁那一丛杜鹃花后面，才折回门厅里去。

那个穿黑绸衣裳、精壮厉害的女人——迪克先前只当她是科迪先生的女管家——跟着丈夫走进书房，随手在他身后带上了门。

===Original===
“Who was he?” she asked. Her voice was uneducated, strident, and complaining.

Mr. Cody resumed his place behind the heavy writing-table and smiled blissfully as he lowered himself into the padded chair.
===Chinese===
“方才那人是谁？”她问。她的嗓音粗鄙无文，又尖利，又满含埋怨。

科迪先生回到那张笨重的写字台后面，往软垫椅里坐下去时，脸上漾着一片怡然自得的笑。

===Original===
“His name is Dick Martin,” he said, “and he is a detective.”

Mrs. Cody changed colour.
===Chinese===
“他名叫迪克·马丁，”他说，“是个侦探。”

科迪太太变了脸色。

===Original===
“Good Gawd! Detective! Bertie, what did he come here for?”

She was agitated; the fat, beringed hand that went up to her mouth was trembling.
===Chinese===
“天老爷！侦探！伯蒂（Bertie），他上这儿来干什么？”

她慌了神；那只戴满戒指的胖手举到嘴边，直打哆嗦。

===Original===
“You’re sure?” she quavered.

Mr. Cody nodded.
===Chinese===
“你没弄错？”她颤声问。

科迪先生点了点头。

===Original===
“A clever man﻿—but I expected him. I have at least three photographs of him. I wonder,” said Mr. Cody softly. “I really wonder!”

He slipped his hand under the heap of papers to find the little notebook, and suddenly his face went pale.
===Chinese===
“是个聪明人——不过我早料到他会来。他的照片我手头至少有三张。真是纳闷，”科迪先生柔声说，“我实在纳闷！”

他把手伸进那叠文件底下去摸那本小记事簿，脸色陡然变白了。

===Original===
“It’s gone﻿—my book and the key﻿—my God! the key!”

He reeled to his feet like a drunken man, blank terror in his face.

“It was when he showed me that map!” he muttered hoarsely. “I’d forgotten that the fellow is an expert thief. Shut that damned door. I want to telephone!”
===Chinese===
“不见了——我的簿子，还有钥匙——我的天！钥匙！”

他像醉汉似的踉跄着站起身，脸上是一片茫然惊恐。

“就是他给我看地图的那会儿！”他嗓音嘶哑地咕哝道。“我竟忘了这家伙是个盗窃行家。把那扇该死的门关上。我要打电话！”
