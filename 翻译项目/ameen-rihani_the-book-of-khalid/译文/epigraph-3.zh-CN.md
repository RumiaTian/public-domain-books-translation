### To God / 致上帝

===Original===
In the religious systems of mankind, I sought thee, O God, in vain; in their machine-made dogmas and theologies, I sought thee in vain; in their churches and temples and mosques, I sought thee long, and long in vain; but in the Sacred Books of the World, what have I found? A letter of thy name, O God, I have deciphered in the Vedas, another in the Zend-Avesta, another in the Bible, another in the Koran. Ay, even in the Book of the Royal Society and in the Records of the Society for Psychical Research, have I found the diacritical signs which the infant races of this Planet Earth have not yet learned to apply to the consonants of thy name. The lisping infant races of this Earth, when will they learn to pronounce thy name entire? Who shall supply the Vowels which shall unite the Gutturals of the Sacred Books? Who shall point out the dashes which compound the opposite loadstars in the various regions of thy Heaven? On the veil of the eternal mystery are palimpsests of which every race has deciphered a consonant. And through the diacritical marks which the seers and paleologists of the future shall furnish, the various dissonances in thy name shall be reduced, for the sake of the infant races of the Earth, to perfect harmony.

===Chinese===
在人间种种宗教体系里，主啊，我寻觅你，徒然；在他们那机器制造的教义与神学里，我寻觅你，徒然；在他们的教堂、神庙、清真寺里，我寻觅你良久，寻觅你良久而徒然；可是在世间的诸圣典中，我又寻见了什么呢？主啊，你名号里的一个字母，我在《吠陀经》（Vedas）中辨出；又一个，在《阿维斯塔经》（Zend-Avesta）里；又一个，在《圣经》（Bible）之中；又一个，在《古兰经》（Koran）之内。是啊，甚至在皇家学会（Royal Society）的典册里、在心灵研究协会（Society for Psychical Research）的记录里，我也找见了那些变音符号——而这地球上年幼的诸族，尚不懂得把它们标注到你名号的那些辅音上去。这地球上口齿不清的年幼诸族啊，他们何时才能学会把你名号整个念出来？谁来补上那些元音，把诸圣典里的那些喉音联成一体？谁来指明那些短划，将你诸天不同区域里彼此对立的指路明星合而为一？在永恒奥秘的帷幕之上，有一层层的重写羊皮纸，每一个民族都从其上辨出了一个辅音。而经由未来的先见者与古文书学者所提供的变音符号，你名号里种种不谐之音，为了地球上年幼诸族的缘故，终将归于完美的和鸣。

> 哈立德
