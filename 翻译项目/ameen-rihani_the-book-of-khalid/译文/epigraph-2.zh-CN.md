### To Nature / 致自然

===Original===
O Mother eternal, divine, satanic, all encompassing, all-nourishing, all-absorbing, O star-diademed, pearl-sandaled Goddess, I am thine forever and ever: whether as a child of thy womb, or an embodiment of a spirit-wave of thy light, or a dumb blind personification of thy smiles and tears, or an ignis-fatuus of the intelligence that is in thee or beyond thee, I am thine forever and ever: I come to thee, I prostrate my face before thee, I surrender myself wholly to thee. O touch me with thy wand divine again; stir me once more in thy mysterious alembics; remake me to suit the majestic silence of thy hills, the supernal purity of thy sky, the mystic austerity of thy groves, the modesty of thy slow-swelling, soft-rolling streams, the imperious pride of thy pines, the wild beauty and constancy of thy mountain rivulets. Take me in thine arms, and whisper to me of thy secrets; fill my senses with thy breath divine; show me the bottom of thy terrible spirit; buffet me in thy storms, infusing in me of thy ruggedness and strength, thy power and grandeur; lull me in thine autumn sundowns to teach me in the arts that enrapture, exalt, supernaturalise. Sing me a lullaby, O Mother eternal! Give me to drink of thy love, divine and diabolic; thy cruelty and thy kindness, I accept both, if thou wilt but whisper to me the secret of both. Anoint me with the chrism of spontaneity that I may be ever worthy of thee.﻿—Withdraw not from me thy hand, lest universal love and sympathy die in my breast.﻿—I implore thee, O Mother eternal, O sea-throned, heaven-canopied Goddess, I prostrate my face before thee, I surrender myself wholly to thee. And whether I be tomorrow the censer in the hand of thy High Priest, or the incense in the censer﻿—whether I become a star-gem in thy cestus or a sun in thy diadem or even a firefly in thy fane, I am content. For I am certain that it shall be for the best.

===Chinese===
永恒之母啊，神圣而又魔祟的母亲——你无所不包、无所不养、无所不纳；星辰为冠、珍珠为履的女神啊，我永永远远属于你：或作你子宫中所生的孩子，或作你光中一缕灵波的化身，或作你笑与泪的无言无目的化身，或作你之内、你之外那智慧的鬼火一闪——我永永远远属于你。我来到你前，伏面于你之前，将我自己全然交托于你。啊，求你再以神圣之杖触我；再在你奥秘的蒸馏瓶中搅动我；重新造我，使我合于你群山庄严的寂静、你穹苍绝高的纯净、你林间神秘的清苦、你那缓缓涨起、轻轻滚流的溪水的羞怯、你松树的骄矜、你山涧的野性之美与恒久。将我拥入你怀，向我低语你的奥秘；以你神圣的气息充满我的感官；向我显示你那可畏性灵的底蕴；在你的风暴中击打我，把你那份粗粝与刚强、权能与崇高灌注入我；在秋日的夕暮里抚我入眠，授我以令人迷醉、令人飞升、令人超凡入化的技艺。为我唱一支摇篮曲吧，永恒之母啊！让我饮你的爱——神圣而又魔祟的爱；你的残忍与你的仁慈，我两皆领受，只要你肯向我低语二者共通的奥秘。请以自发性之圣膏膏我，使我永远配得上你。——莫从我这儿撤回你的手，免得博爱与共情在我胸中死去。——我恳求你，永恒之母啊，以海为宝座、以天为华盖的女神，我伏面于你之前，将我自己全然交托于你。明日我无论是你大祭司手中的香炉，还是炉中焚的香烟——无论我化作你腰带上一颗星宝，还是你冠冕上的一轮太阳，甚至只是你圣殿中一只萤火——我都心甘。因为我确知，这一切终归于至善。

> 哈立德
