## Act III / 第三幕

### Scene I / 第一场

===Original===
Time: A month later: dawn.

（**Naaman's** tent, on high ground among the mountains near Samaria: the city below. In the distance, a wide and splendid landscape. **Saballidin** and soldiers on guard below the tent. Enter **Ruahmah** in hunter's dress, with a lyre slung from her shoulder.）

===Chinese===
时间：一个月之后：黎明。

（**乃缦**的营帐，搭在撒马利亚附近群山的高处：城池在下方。远处是一片广阔壮丽的山川。**萨巴利丁**与众兵士守在帐下。**如阿玛**身着猎装上场，肩上斜挂一架七弦琴。）

===Original===
Ruahmah: Peace and good health to you, Saballidin. Good morrow to you all. How fares my lord?

===Chinese===
如阿玛：愿你平安康健，萨巴利丁。你们大家早安。我家主上可好？

===Original===
Saballidin: The curtains of his tent are folded still: They have not moved since we returned, last night, And told him what befell us in the city.

===Chinese===
萨巴利丁：他帐帘依旧垂合：自昨夜我们回来，把城中遭遇禀告了他之后，便再未动过。

===Original===
Ruahmah: Told him! Why did you make report to him. And not to me? Am I not captain here, Entrusted by the King's command with care Of Naaman's life, until he is restored? 'Tis mine to know the first of good or ill In this adventure: mine to shield his heart From every arrow of adversity. What have you told him? Speak!

===Chinese===
如阿玛：禀告了他！你们为何向他禀报，却不禀报于我？我岂不是此处的统领，奉王命受托照看乃缦的性命，直到他痊愈吗？此行是福是祸，我理当最先知晓：替他的心挡下每一支苦难之箭，乃是我的本分。你们对他说了什么？讲！

===Original===
Saballidin: Lady, we feared To bring our news to you. For when the king Of Israel had read our monarch's letter, He rent his clothes, and cried, "Am I a god, To kill and make alive, that I should heal A leper? Ye have come with false pretence, Damascus seeks a quarrel with me. Go!" But when we told our lord, he closed his tent, And there remains enfolded in his grief. I trust he sleeps; 'twere kind to let him sleep! For now he doth forget his misery, And all the burden of his hopeless woe Is lifted from him by the gentle hand Of slumber. Oh, to those bereft of hope Sleep is the only blessing left—the last Asylum of the weary, the one sign Of pity from impenetrable heaven. Waking is strife: sleep is the truce of God! Ah, lady, wake him not. The day will be Full long for him to suffer, and for us To turn our disappointed faces home On the long road by which we must return.

===Chinese===
萨巴利丁：夫人，我们不敢把这消息带到您跟前。因为以色列王读罢我主的御书，便撕裂衣裳，呼道："我是神吗，能杀又能活，使我能医治一个大麻风？你们是借故来此寻衅，大马士革要与我寻仇。去罢！"可我们禀告主上之后，他便合拢了帐帘，仍将自己裹在哀痛之中。但愿他睡着了；让他睡着才是慈悲！因为此刻他忘了自己的苦楚，那一无指望的哀伤重担，被睡眠温柔的手从他身上轻轻卸下。唉，对于一个被夺去希望的人，睡眠是所余唯一的恩赐——是疲惫者最后的避难所，是深不可测的天上唯一下降的怜悯之征。醒来便是争战：睡眠是神的休战！啊，夫人，别叫醒他。这白昼于他将够漫长，够他受苦；于我们也够漫长——我们要转回失望的面孔，沿那条漫长的归路回家。

===Original===
Ruahmah: Return! Who gave you that command? Not I! The King made me the leader of this quest, And bound you all to follow me, because He knew I never would return without The thing for which he sent us. I'll go on Day after day, unto the uttermost parts Of earth, if need be, and beyond the gates Of morning, till I find that which I seek— New life for Naaman. Are ye ashamed To have a woman lead you? Then go back And tell the King, "This huntress went too far For us to follow; she pursues the trail Of hope alone, refusing to forsake The quarry: we grew weary of the chase; And so we left her and retraced our steps, Like faithless hounds, to sleep beside the fire." Did Naaman forsake his soldiers thus When you went forth to hunt the Assyrian Bull? Your manly courage is less durable Than woman's love, it seems. Go, if you will— Who bids me now farewell?

===Chinese===
如阿玛：回去！这是谁下的令？不是我！王命我为这次寻访的首领，命你们所有人都随我而行，因为他知道我绝不空手而归——必将所寻之物带回。我愿一日复一日地走下去，走到地的极处，若有必要，走过晨光之门，直到我寻见我所寻的——乃缦的新生。你们以女子为统领，引以为耻吗？那就回去吧，禀告王说："这位女猎手走得太远，我们追不上了；她独自追寻希望的踪迹，不肯舍弃猎物：我们厌倦了这场追逐；于是我们抛下她，掉头回返，像不忠的猎犬，回到火堆旁去睡。"当初你们出猎亚述之公牛时，乃缦可曾如此抛弃他的兵卒？看来你们的男子气概，竟不如女子的爱情经久。要走便走——现在有谁向我辞行？

===Original===
Soldiers: Not I, not I!

Saballidin: Lady, lead on, we'll follow you forever!

===Chinese===
众兵士：我不辞，我不辞！

萨巴利丁：夫人，领路吧，我们永远追随您！

===Original===
Ruahmah: Why, now you speak like men! Brought you no word Out of Samaria, except that cry Of impotence and fear from Israel's king?

===Chinese===
如阿玛：这回你们才像个男子汉！除了以色列王那无能畏怯的呼号，你们可曾从撒马利亚带回什么话？

===Original===
Saballidin: I do remember while he spoke with us A rustic messenger came in, and cried "Elisha saith, let Naaman come to me At Dothan, he shall surely know there is A God in Israel."

===Chinese===
萨巴利丁：我记得他正与我们说话时，一个乡野来使闯进来，喊道："以利沙说，让乃缦到多坍来见我，他就必知道以色列中有一位神。"

===Original===
Ruahmah: What said the King?

===Chinese===
如阿玛：王怎么说？

===Original===
Saballidin: He only shouted "Go!" more wildly yet, And rent his clothes again, as if he were Half-maddened by a coward's fear, and thought Only of how he might be rid of us. What comfort could there be for him, what hope For us, in the rude prophet's misty word?

===Chinese===
萨巴利丁：他只是越发狂乱地喊"去罢！"又再撕裂衣裳，仿佛被懦夫的恐惧逼得半疯，心里只想着怎样打发我们走。那粗陋先知含糊的话，于他有何安慰，于我们又有何希望？

===Original===
Ruahmah: It is the very word for which I prayed! My trust was not in princes; for the crown, The sceptre, and the purple robe are not Significant of vital power. The man Who saves his brother-men is he who lives His life with Nature, takes deep hold on truth, And trusts in God. A prophet's word is more Than all the kings on earth can speak. How far Is Dothan?

===Chinese===
如阿玛：这正是我祷告所求的那句话！我所信赖的并非君王；因为王冠、权杖与紫袍，并不代表生命的大能。拯救同胞之人，是与自然同其生活、深深握住真理、并信靠神的那一位。一位先知的话，胜过地上众王所能言。多坍离此多远？

===Original===
Soldier: Lady, 'tis but three hours' ride Along the valley northward.

===Chinese===
兵士：夫人，沿山谷向北，不过是三个时辰的骑程。

===Original===
Ruahmah: Near! so near? I had not thought to end my task so soon! Prepare yourselves with speed to take the road. I will awake my lord.

===Chinese===
如阿玛：这么近！竟这么近？我未料到我的使命能这样快了结！速速整备上路。我去叫醒主上。

===Original===
Saballidin: Ruahmah, stay!

Ruahmah: The gratitude Of him we both desire to serve: your friend— My master and my lord.

Saballidin: No more than this?

Ruahmah: Yes, if you will, take all the thanks my hands Can hold, my lips can speak.

Saballidin: I would have more.

===Chinese===
萨巴利丁：如阿玛，且慢！

如阿玛：我们二人都愿尽心侍奉的那位的感激：你的朋友——我的主、我的主上。

萨巴利丁：仅此而已？

如阿玛：是的，若你愿意，尽可收下我双手所能捧、我口唇所能言的一切谢意。

萨巴利丁：我求更多。

===Original===
Ruahmah: My friend, there's nothing more to give to you, My service to my lord is absolute. There's not a drop of blood within my veins But quickens at the very thought of him; And not a dream of mine but he doth stand Within its heart and make it bright. No man To me is other than his friend or foe. You are his friend, and I believe you true!

===Chinese===
如阿玛：我的朋友，再没有什么可以给你了，我对主上的侍奉是毫无保留的。我血管里没有一滴血，不是一念及他便沸腾；我每一个梦里，他都立于其核心，使之焕然生光。世间的男子于我，不是他的友便是他的敌。你是他的友，而我信你忠诚！

===Original===
Saballidin: I have been true to him—now, I am true To you.

===Chinese===
萨巴利丁：我向他是忠诚的——如今，我向你亦然。

===Original===
Ruahmah: And therefore doubly true to him! O let us match our loyalties, and strive Between us who shall win the higher crown! Men boast them of a friendship stronger far Than love of woman. Prove it! I'll not boast, But I'll contend with you on equal terms In this brave race: and if you win the prize I'll hold you next to him: and if I win He'll hold you next to me; and either way We'll not be far apart. Do you accept My challenge?

===Chinese===
如阿玛：因此向他是加倍的忠诚！哦，让我们较量彼此的忠心，在你我之间争一争，看谁能赢得更高的冠冕！男子常自夸他们的情谊远胜女子的爱情。那就证明给我看！我不夸口，但我愿与你在这场英勇的竞逐中以同等条件相争：若你得胜，我便视你仅次于他；若我得胜，他便视你仅次于我；无论哪样，我们都不致相离太远。你可接受我的挑战？

===Original===
Saballidin: Yes! For you enforce my heart By honour to resign its great desire, And love itself to offer sacrifice Of all disloyal dreams on its own altar. Yet love remains; therefore I pray you, think How surely you must lose in our contention. For I am known to Naaman: but you He blindly takes for Tsarpi. 'Tis to her He gives his gratitude: the praise you win Endears her name.

===Chinese===
萨巴利丁：我接受！因为你以尊严迫我的心，令它舍弃那至大的渴望，乃至爱本身，也当在自己的祭坛上献上一切不忠之梦为祭。然而爱犹在；所以我求你思量，在这场较量中你必输无疑。因为乃缦认识我；而你，他却盲目地当作瑟璧。他所有的感激都归于她：你所赢得的称颂，都使她的名字更加可亲。

===Original===
Ruahmah: Her name? Why, what is that? A name is but an empty shell, a mask That does not change the features of the face Beneath it. Can a name rejoice, or weep, Or hope? Can it be moved by tenderness To daily services of love, or feel the warmth Of dear companionship? How many things We call by names that have no meaning: kings That cannot rule; and gods that are not good; And wives that do not love! It matters not What syllables he utters when he calls, 'Tis I who come—'tis I who minister Unto my lord, and mine the living heart That feels the comfort of his confidence, The thrill of gladness when he speaks to me— I do not hear the name!

===Chinese===
如阿玛：她的名字？哦，那算什么？一个名字不过是个空壳，一副面具，换不掉其下容貌的轮廓。一个名字能欢喜、能垂泪、能盼望吗？它能被柔情触动，日日去尽爱的本分，或感受亲密相伴的温暖吗？世间多少事物，我们冠以名号，却毫无意义：不能治国的王；不为善的神；不爱夫的妻子！他呼唤时吐出的是哪几个音节，并不紧要——来的是我——侍奉主上的是我，那颗感受到他信赖之安慰、听他对我说话便悸动欢喜的活心，也是我的——我听不见那名字！

===Original===
Saballidin: And yet, be sure There's danger in this error—and no gain!

===Chinese===
萨巴利丁：但你须明白，这错认里有危险——而且并无益处！

===Original===
Ruahmah: I seek no gain; I only tread the path Marked for me daily by the hand of love. And if his blindness spared my lord one pang Of sorrow in his black, forsaken hour— And if this error makes his burdened heart More quiet, and his shadowed way less dark, Whom do I rob? Not her who chose to stay At ease in Rimmon's House! Surely not him! Only myself? And that enriches me. Why trouble we the master? Let it go— Tomorrow he must know the truth—and then He shall dispose of me e'en as he will!

===Chinese===
如阿玛：我不求益处；我只踏着爱之手为我日日标出的路前行。若他的盲目，能免我主在那幽暗、被弃的时辰里多受一桩痛楚——若这错认能让他负重的心更安宁，让他的暗途少几分晦暗，我夺了谁？不是那选择安卧于临门之家享安逸的她！自然也不是他！只除我自己？而那反倒使我丰盈。何必搅扰主上？随它去罢——明日他必知真相——那时，他可随他的心意处置我！

===Original===
Saballidin: Tomorrow?

Ruahmah: Yes, for I will tarry here, While you conduct him to Elisha's house To find the promised healing. I forebode A sudden danger from the craven king Of Israel, or else a secret ambush From those who hate us in Damascus. Go, But leave me twenty men: this mountain-pass Protects the road behind you. Make my lord Obey the prophet's word, whatever he commands, And come again in peace. Farewell!

===Chinese===
萨巴利丁：明日？

如阿玛：是的，因为我要留在此处，由你护送他到以利沙家中，寻获所应许的医治。我预感到以色列那怯懦之王会骤然加害，又或大马士革恨我们的人会设下暗伏。去吧，但留下二十人：这道山口护卫着你们身后的归路。务必让主上遵从先知的话，无论他有何吩咐，你们都要使主上顺从，再平安归来。别矣！

===Original===
Naaman:（Appearing at the entrance of his tent.） O let me ever wake to music! For the soul Returns most gently then, and finds its way By the soft, winding clue of melody, Out of the dusky labyrinth of sleep, Into the light. My body feels the sun Though I behold naught that his rays reveal. Come, thou who art my daydawn and my sight, Sweet eyes, come close, and make the sunrise mine!

===Chinese===
乃缦：（出现在帐门口。）哦，让我永远在乐声中醒来！因为那时灵魂归来得最温柔，循着旋律柔软蜿蜒的引线，走出睡眠那幽暗的迷宫，来到光中。我的身体感知着太阳，虽则他的光辉所照之物我一概不见。来吧，你是我的晨曦、我的眼目，甜蜜的双眸，来近一些，让这日出也归我所有！

===Original===
Ruahmah:（Coming near.） A fairer day, dear lord, was never born In Paradise! The sapphire cup of heaven Is filled with golden wine: the earth, adorned With jewel-drops of dew, unveils her face A joyful bride, in welcome to her king. And look! He leaps upon the Eastern hills All ruddy fire, and claims her with a kiss. Yonder the snowy peaks of Hermon float Unmoving as a wind-dropt cloud. The gulf Of Jordan, filled with violet haze, conceals The rivers winding trail with wreaths of mist. Below us, marble-crowned Samaria thrones Upon her emerald hill amid the Vale Of Barley, while the plains to northward change Their colour like the shimmering necks of doves. The lark springs up, with morning on her wings, To climb her singing stairway in the blue, And all the fields are sprinkled with her joy!

===Chinese===
如阿玛：（走近。）亲爱的主上，纵在乐园之中，也未曾诞生过比这更美的日子！苍穹这只蓝玉之杯，斟满了金色的美酒：大地缀满露珠的宝石，揭开面庞，如一位喜乐的新娘，迎候她的王。看哪！他跃上东方的群山，通体赤红的火焰，以一吻认取了她。远处黑门山的雪峰漂浮着，静止如一朵风停后的云。约旦河的深谷里弥漫着紫罗兰色的薄霭，以雾气的花环掩起河流蜿蜒的踪迹。我们脚下，撒马利亚头戴大理石冠，端坐于她翠玉的山丘之上，立于大麦谷之中；而北方的平原变幻着色泽，如同鸽子颈羽上闪烁的光彩。云雀腾起，双翼载着清晨，在碧空之中攀援她那歌唱的阶梯，所有田野都洒满了她的喜悦！

===Original===
Naaman: Thy voice is magical: thy words are visions! I must content myself with them, for now My only hope is lost: Samaria's king Rejects our monarch's message—hast thou heard? "Am I a god that I should cure a leper?" He sends me home unhealed, with angry words, Back to Damascus and the lingering death.

===Chinese===
乃缦：你的声音有法力：你的话语便是异象！我唯有以此自慰，因为我唯一的指望已落空：撒马利亚王拒绝了我主的御书——你可曾听说？"我是神吗，能治好一个长大麻风的？"他带着怒语打发我未得医治而归，回到大马士革，回到那拖延的死亡。

===Original===
Ruahmah: What matter where he sends? No god is he To slay or make alive. Elisha bids You come to him at Dothan, there to learn There is a God in Israel.

===Chinese===
如阿玛：他打发你去何处又有何要紧？他不是神，不能杀也不能活。以利沙请你到多坍去见他，好在那里知晓以色列中有一位神。

===Original===
Naaman: I fear That I am grown mistrustful of all gods; Their secret counsels are implacable.

===Chinese===
乃缦：我只怕我已变得不信任所有的神了；他们隐秘的谋断无可挽回。

===Original===
Ruahmah: Fear not! There's One who rules in righteousness High over all.

===Chinese===
如阿玛：不要怕！有一位以公义掌权，高居万有之上。

===Original===
Naaman: What knowest thou of Him?

===Chinese===
乃缦：你对他知道什么？

===Original===
Ruahmah: Oh, I have heard—the maid of Israel— Rememberest thou? She often said her God Was merciful and kind, and slow to wrath, And plenteous in forgiveness, pitying us Like as a father pitieth his children.

===Chinese===
如阿玛：哦，我曾听说——那以色列的使女——你可记得？她常说她的神大有怜悯、满有恩慈，不轻易发怒，赦免丰盛，怜恤我们，正如父亲怜恤他的儿女。

===Original===
Naaman: If there were such a God, I'd worship Him Forever!

===Chinese===
乃缦：若有这样一位神，我愿永远敬拜他！

===Original===
Ruahmah: Then make haste to hear the word His prophet promises to speak to thee! Obey it, my dear lord, and thou shalt lose This curse that burdens thee. This tiny spot Of white that mars the beauty of thy brow Shall melt like snow; thine eyes be filled with light. Thou wilt not need my leading any more— Nor me—for thou wilt see me, all unveiled— I tremble at the thought.

===Chinese===
如阿玛：那么就快去听他先知应许要对你说的话吧！顺从它，我亲爱的主上，你便要卸下这压着你的咒诅。你额上那破坏容颜的一小点白斑，将如雪消融；你的双眼要充满光明。你再也不需要我的引领——也不再需要我——因为你将看见我，毫无遮蔽——我念及此便战栗。

===Original===
Naaman: Why, what is this? Why shouldst thou tremble? Art thou not mine own?

===Chinese===
乃缦：咦，这是怎么说？你为何要战栗？你岂不是属于我的吗？

===Original===
Ruahmah:（Turning to him.） Surely I am! But take me, take me now! For I belong to thee in body and soul; The very pulses of my heart are thine. Wilt thou not feel how tenderly they beat? Wilt thou not lie like myrrh between my breasts And satisfy thy lonely lips with love? Thou art opprest, and I would comfort thee While yet thy sorrow weighs upon thy life. Tomorrow? No, today! The crown of love Is sacrifice; I have not given thee Enough! Ah, fold me in thine arms—take all!

===Chinese===
如阿玛：（转向他。）我确是属于你的！但现在就接纳我，接纳我吧！因为我身我灵皆属于你；我心中每一次搏动都是你的。你难道不觉出它们跳得何等温柔吗？你岂不愿如没药般安卧在我双乳之间，以爱满足你孤独的唇吗？你受苦，我要安慰你，趁你的哀伤仍压在你性命之上。明日？不，就在今日！爱的冠冕乃是牺牲；我给你的还远远不够！啊，将我拥入怀中——尽都收取吧！

===Original===
Naaman: Thou art too dear to injure with a kiss— Too dear for me to stain thy purity, Or leave one touch upon thee to regret! How should I take a gift may bankrupt thee, Or drain the fragrant chalice of thy love With lips that may be fatal? Tempt me not To sweet dishonour; strengthen me to wait Until thy prophecy is all fulfilled, And I can claim thee with a joyful heart.

===Chinese===
乃缦：你太珍贵了，珍贵得我不忍以一吻相伤——太珍贵了，我不可玷污你的纯洁，或在你身上留下一丝让你追悔的触碰！我怎能收下一份或将使你倾家荡产的礼物，又怎能以或许致命的唇，饮尽你那芳香的爱之杯？莫引诱我陷入甜蜜的不义；求你坚固我，使我等待，直到你的预言尽数应验，我便能以一颗欢喜的心来认取你。

===Original===
Ruahmah:（Turning away.） Thou wilt not need me then—and I shall be No more than the faint echo of a song Heard half asleep. We shall go back to where We stood before this journey.

===Chinese===
如阿玛：（转过身去。）那时你便不再需要我了——而我将不过是半睡半醒之间听见的一首歌的微弱回声。我们将回到此行之前我们所立之处。

===Original===
Naaman: Never again! For thou art changed by some deep miracle. The flower of womanhood hath bloomed in thee— Art thou not changed?

===Chinese===
乃缦：再不会回到从前了！因你已被某个深邃的奇迹所改变。女子之花已在你身上盛开——你岂不是变了吗？

===Original===
Ruahmah: Yea, I am changed—and changed Again—bewildered—till there's nothing clear To me but this: I am the instrument In an Almighty hand to rescue thee From death. This will I do—and afterward—

===Chinese===
如阿玛：是的，我变了——并且一变再变——迷茫失措——直到我心中只余一事清楚：我是全能者手中的器皿，为要救你脱离死亡。这事我必做成——至于此后——

### Scene II / 第二场

===Original===
（The house of Elisha, upon a terraced hillside. A low stone cottage with vine-trellises and flowers; a flight of steps, at the foot of which is **Naaman's** chariot. He is standing in it; **Saballidin** beside it. Two soldiers come down the steps.）

===Chinese===
（以利沙的住所，位于层叠的山坡上。一座低矮的石屋，旁有葡萄棚架与花丛；一段石阶，阶下停着**乃缦**的战车。他立于车中；**萨巴利丁**在车旁。两名兵士从阶上走下。）

===Original===
First Soldier: We have delivered my lord's greeting and his message.

Second Solider: Yes, and near lost our noses in the doing of it! For the servant slammed the door in our faces. A most unmannerly reception!

===Chinese===
兵士甲：我们已将主上的问候与口信传到了。

兵士乙：不错，差一点连鼻子都搭进去！那仆人竟当着我们的面把门摔上。好不礼貌的接待！

===Original===
First Soldier: But I take that as a good omen. It is mark of holy men to keep ill-conditioned servants. Look, the door opens, the prophet is coming.

===Chinese===
兵士甲：我倒把这当作好兆头。圣人身边养几个粗鄙仆人，正是其标记。看，门开了，先知出来了。

===Original===
Second Soldier: No, by my head, it's that notable mark of his master's holiness, that same lantern-jawed lout of a servant.

===Chinese===
兵士乙：呸，凭我的头担保，那正是他主人成圣的显著标记——还是那个瘦长脸的粗蠢仆人。

===Original===
Gehazi: My master, the prophet of Israel, sends word to Naaman the Syrian—are you he?—"Go wash in Jordan seven times and be healed."

===Chinese===
基哈西：我主以色列的先知传话给亚兰人乃缦——你就是他吗？——"去在约旦河里沐浴七次，便得痊愈。"

===Original===
Naaman: What insolence is this? Am I a man To be put off with surly messengers? Has not Damascus rivers more renowned Than this rude, torrent Jordan? Crystal streams, Abana! Pharpar! flowing smoothly through A paradise of roses? Might I not Have bathed in them and been restored at ease? Come up, Saballidin, and guide me home!

===Chinese===
乃缦：这是何等的轻慢！我难道是个可以随便用粗暴的信使打发的人吗？大马士革岂没有比这条湍急粗野的约旦河更出名得多的河流吗？水晶般清澈的溪流，亚罢拿！法珥法！平缓地流过一座玫瑰的乐园？我岂不能在其中沐浴，安安逸逸地复原吗？上来吧，萨巴利丁，领我回家！

===Original===
Saballidin: Bethink thee, master, shall we lose our quest Because a servant is uncouth? The road That seeks the mountain leads us through the vale. The prophet's word is friendly after all; For had it been some mighty task he set, Thou wouldst perform it. How much rather then This easy one? Hast thou not promised her Who waits for thy return? Wilt thou go back To her unhealed?

===Chinese===
萨巴利丁：主上三思，难道因为一个仆人粗鲁，我们便要舍弃所求吗？通往山巅的路必先穿过山谷。先知的话终究是出于善意；因为若他所吩咐的是一件艰难的大事，你也会照办。何况这一件如此轻易？你不是已对那等候你归来之人许下诺言吗？你要未得医治就回到她那里去吗？

===Original===
Naaman: No! not for all my pride! I'll make myself most humble for her sake, And stoop to anything that gives me hope Of having her. Make haste, Saballidin, Bring me to Jordan. I will cast myself Into that river's turbulent embrace A hundred times, until I save my life Or lose it!

===Chinese===
乃缦：不！哪怕赔上我全部的骄傲也不！为了她的缘故，我愿变得极其谦卑，俯就任何能给我希望拥有她的事。快，萨巴利丁，带我到约旦河去。我要投身于那条河湍急的怀抱，纵然百次，直到我救回性命，或丧失性命！

===Original===
Elisha:（Interrupting.） As thy soul liveth I will not receive A gift from thee, my son! Give all to Him Whose mercy hath redeemed thee from thy plague.

===Chinese===
以利沙：（插话。）我指着你的性命起誓，我必不接受你的礼物，我的儿！将一切归给那以怜悯救赎你脱离灾病的那位吧。

===Original===
Naaman: He is the only God! I worship Him! Grant me a portion of the blessed soil Of this most favoured land where I have found His mercy; in Damascus will I build An altar to His name, and praise Him there Morning and night. There is no other God In all the world.

===Chinese===
乃缦：唯有他是神！我敬拜他！求你赐我一捧这蒙福之地的圣土，我正是在此地寻得他的怜悯；我要在大马士革为他的名筑一座坛，在那里朝夕颂赞他。普天之下，再无别神。

===Original===
Elisha: Thou needest not This load of earth to build a shrine for Him; Yet take it if thou wilt. But be assured God's altar is in every loyal heart, And every flame of love that kindles there Ascends to Him and brightens with His praise. There is no other God! But evil Powers Make war against Him in the darkened world; And many temples have been built to them.

===Chinese===
以利沙：你并不需要这一捧土来为他筑坛；但你若要，便拿去罢。只是你当确知：神的坛设在每一颗忠诚的心里，在那里点燃的每一缕爱之火焰，都升达于他，因颂赞他而愈发明亮。再无别神！然而邪恶的权势在这幽暗的世界与他争战；已有许多殿宇是为它们而建。

===Original===
Naaman: I know them well! Yet when my master goes To worship in the House of Rimmon, I Must enter with him; for he trusts me, leans Upon my hand; and when he bows himself I cannot help but make obeisance too— But not to Rimmon! To my country's king I'll bow in love and honour. Will the Lord Pardon thy servant in this thing?

===Chinese===
乃缦：这些我深知！然而当主上进入临门之家敬拜时，我必须随他而入；因为他信任我，倚靠我的手搀扶；当他俯伏时，我不得不也一同屈身——但并非拜临门！我向我所爱的、所尊崇的国王屈身。愿主在此事上饶恕你的仆人吗？

===Original===
Elisha: My son, Peace has been granted thee. 'Tis thine to find The only way to keep it. Go in peace.

===Chinese===
以利沙：我的儿，平安已经赐给你了。唯当由你自己去寻得保守这平安的唯一之道。你平平安安地去吧。

===Original===
Naaman: Thou hast not answered me—may I bow down?

===Chinese===
乃缦：你尚未答我——我可否屈身？

===Original===
Elisha: The answer must be thine. The heart that knows The perfect peace of gratitude and love, Walks in the light and needs no other rule. Take counsel with thy heart and go in peace!

===Chinese===
以利沙：这答案须由你自己来给。一颗知晓感恩与爱之全然平安的心，行在光中，无需别的律法。与你自己的心相商，然后平平安安地去吧！
