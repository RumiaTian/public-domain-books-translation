## Act II / 第二幕

===Original===
Time: A week later.

The forecourt of the House of Rimmon. At the back the broad steps and double doors of the shrine: above them the tower of the god, its summit invisible. Enter various groups of citizens, talking, laughing, shouting: Rakhaz, Hazael, Shumakim and others.

===Chinese===
时间：一周之后。

临门之家前庭。后方是神庙宽阔的台阶与双扇大门；门上是神的高塔，塔顶隐没不见。各群百姓上场，交谈、欢笑、呼喊：拉卡兹、哈薛、舒马金及其他人。

===Original===
First Citizen: Great news, glorious news, the Assyrians are beaten!

Second Citizen: Naaman is returning, crowned with victory. Glory to our noble captain!

Third Citizen: No, he is killed. I had it from one of the camp-followers who saw him fall at the head of the battle. They are bringing his body to bury it with honour. O sorrowful victory!

===Chinese===
市民甲：天大的喜讯，辉煌的喜讯，亚述人被打败啦！

市民乙：乃缦凯旋归来，头戴胜利之冠。我军元帅万岁！

市民丙：不，他阵亡了。这是一个随营杂役亲眼见他冲锋时倒下的。他们正把他的遗体运回来厚葬。啊，悲哉此胜！

===Original===
Rakhaz: Peace, my good fellows, you are ignorant, you have not been rightly informed, I will misinform you. The accounts of Naaman's death are overdrawn. He was killed, but his life has been preserved. One of his wounds was mortal, but the other three were curable, and by these the physicians have saved him.

Shumakim: (Balancing himself before RAKHAZ in pretended admiration.) O wonderful! Most admirable logic! One mortal, and three curable, therefore he must recover as it were, by three to one. Rakhaz, do you know that you are a marvelous man?

===Chinese===
拉卡兹：安静，好人们，你们无知得很，消息不确，待我来误导你们。关于乃缦阵亡的说法言过其实。他确曾阵亡，但性命却保住了。他一处伤是致命的，但另外三处都能医治，医师们正是凭这三处救活了他。

舒马金：（装出一脸钦佩，在拉卡兹面前晃来晃去。）妙啊！绝妙的逻辑！一处致命，三处可医，于是他必能痊愈，好比三比一之胜。拉卡兹，你可知自己是个奇人？

===Original===
Rakhaz: Yes, I know it, but I make no boast of my knowledge.

Shumakim: Too modest, for in knowing this you know what is unknown to any other in Damascus!

===Chinese===
拉卡兹：是的，我自知，但我并不夸耀我的见闻。

舒马金：太谦虚了，因为知道这件事，便知道了大马士革任何他人都不知晓之事！

===Original===
Hazael: Here is Saballidin, we'll question him; He was enflamed by Naaman's fiery words, And rode with him to battle. Good, my lord, We hail you as a herald of the fight You helped to win. Give us authentic news Of your great general! Is he safe and well? When will he come? Or will he come at all?

===Chinese===
哈薛：萨巴利丁来了，我们且问他；他被乃缦慷慨激昂的言辞所点燃，与他一同驰骋沙场。好啊，大人，你亲历并襄助了这场胜仗，我们视你为它的信使。请告诉我们关于你那位大将军的确切消息！他可平安无恙？何时归来？还是终归不会回来了？

===Original===
Saballidin: He comes but now, returning from the field Where he hath gained a crown of deathless fame! Three times he led the charge; three times he fell Wounded, and the Assyrians beat us back. Yet every wound was but a spur to urge His valour onward. In the last attack He rode before us as the crested wave That heads the flood; and lo, our enemies Were broken like a dam of river-reeds, Burst by the torrent, scattered, swept away! But look! the Assyrian king in wavering flight Is lodged like driftwood on a little hill, Encircled by his guard, and stands at bay. Then Naaman, followed hotly by a score Of whirlwind riders, hammers through the hedge Of spearmen, brandishing the golden yoke: "Take back this gift," he cries; and shatters it On Shalmaneser's helmet. So the fight Dissolves in universal rout: the king, His chariots and his horsemen melt away; Our captain stands the master of the field, And saviour of Damascus! Now he brings, First to the king, report of this great triumph.

===Chinese===
萨巴利丁：他此刻便到，从那片为他赢得不朽荣冠的战场归来！他三次率军冲锋；三次负伤倒下，亚述人将我们击退。然而每一处伤口都只是一根马刺，催逼他的英勇更进一步。在最后一次进攻中，他策马奔在我们最前，犹如洪流之上浪头高耸的卷浪；看哪，我们的敌军如同一道河苇筑成的堤坝，被洪流冲决、溃散、席卷一空！可是看！那亚述王在飘摇奔逃之中，像一块浮木搁浅在一座小丘上，被卫兵环绕，作困兽之斗。于是乃缦率领二十名旋风骑手紧追不舍，挥舞着那副金轭砸穿了矛兵的阵栅："收回此礼！"他高喊，将金轭在撒缦以色的头盔上砸得粉碎。战局于是化为全线溃败：那国王、他的战车与骑兵尽数消融；我们的元帅屹立战场，成为主宰，大马士革的拯救者！此刻他先来向王禀报这场大捷。

===Original===
Ruahmah: (Coming close to Saballidin,) But what of him who won it? Fares he well? My mistress would receive some word of him.

Saballidin: Hath she not heard?

Ruahmah: But one brief message came: A tablet saying, "We have fought and conquered," No word of his own person. Fares he well?

===Chinese===
如阿玛：（凑近萨巴利丁。）但赢得此胜的人怎样了？他可安好？我家主母想得他一点消息。

萨巴利丁：她未曾听闻吗？

如阿玛：只来了短短一信：一方书版写着"我等已战而胜之"，对他本人只字未提。他可安好？

===Original===
Saballidin: Alas, most ill! For he is like a man Consumed by some strange sickness: wasted, wan— His eyes are dimmed so that scarce can see; His ears are dulled; his fearless face is pale As one who walks to meet a certain doom Yet will not flinch. It is most pitiful— But you shall see.

===Chinese===
萨巴利丁：唉，糟得很！他像一个被某种怪病消磨的人：憔悴、苍白——双目昏花，几乎看不见东西；双耳迟钝；他那无所畏惧的脸惨白如一个走向必定厄运却不肯退缩之人。实在令人心酸——不过你们就要亲眼见到了。

===Original===
Ruahmah: Yea, we shall see a man Who took upon himself his country's burden, dared To hazard all to save the poor and helpless; A man who bears the wrath of evil powers Unknown, and pays the hero's sacrifice.

===Chinese===
如阿玛：是的，我们将见到一个人，他担当起国族的重担，敢于孤注一掷去拯救贫苦无助之人；一个承受着幽冥邪恶权势之怒、付出英雄牺牲的人。

===Original===
Benhadad: Where is my faithful servant Naaman, The captain of my host?

Saballidin: My lord, he comes.

Naaman: (Kneeling.) Where is my King? Master, the bearer of thy sword returns. The golden yoke thou gavest me I broke On him who sent it. Asshur's Bull hath fled Dehorned. The standards of his host are thine! Damascus is all thine, at peace, and free!

===Chinese===
便哈达：我忠仆乃缦、我军元帅在哪里？

萨巴利丁：我主，他来了。

乃缦：（跪下。）我王何在？主上，持你宝剑之人归来了。你赐我的金轭，我已在那差它来的人身上砸碎。亚述之公牛落角而逃。他大军的旗号尽归于你！大马士革尽归于你，太平、自由！

===Original===
Benhadad: (Holding out his arms.) Thou art a mighty man of valour! Come, And let me fold thy courage to my heart.

Rezon: (Lifting his rod.) Forbear, O King! Stand back from him, all men! By the great name of Rimmon I proclaim This man a leper! On his brow I see The death-white seal, the fingerprint of doom! That tiny spot will spread, eating his flesh, Gnawing his fingers bone from bone, until The impious heart that dared defy the gods Dissolves in the slow death which now begins. Unclean! unclean! Henceforward he is dead: No human hand shall touch him, and no home Of men shall give him shelter. He shall walk Only with corpses of the selfsame death Down the long path to a forgotten tomb. Avoid, depart, I do adjure you all, Leave him to god—the leper Naaman!

===Chinese===
便哈达：（伸出双臂。）你是大能的勇士！来，让我将你的勇气拥入怀中。

利汛：（举起他的杖。）且慢，我王！众人皆退后！奉临门之大名我宣告：此人是个长大麻风的！我见他额上有那死白的印记——厄运的指痕！那一点小斑将蔓延，蚕食他的皮肉，啃噬他的指骨直至骨骨分离，直到那胆敢冒犯神明的不敬之心，在如今业已开始的缓慢死亡中消融。不洁！不洁！从今以后他便算死了：人的手不可触他，人的家不可收容他。他只能与同样死状的尸骸同行，走下那条漫长的路，通往一座被遗忘的坟。退避、散开，我令你们众人，将他交给神明——大麻风的乃缦！

===Original===
Benhadad: (Lingering and turning back.) Alas, my son! O Naaman, my son! Why did I let thee go? Thou art cast out Irrevocably from the city's life Which thou hast saved. Who can resist the gods? I must obey the law, and touch thy hand Never again. Yet none shall take from thee Thy glorious title, captain of my host! I will provide for thee, and thou shalt dwell With guards of honour in a house of mine Always. Damascus never shall forget What thou hast done! O miserable words Of crowned impotence! O mockery of power Given to kings, who cannot even defend Their dearest from the secret wrath of heaven! Naaman, my son, my son!

===Chinese===
便哈达：（依依不舍地回头。）唉，我的儿！哦，乃缦，我的儿！我为何放你离去？你已被无可挽回地逐出这座被你所救之城的生活。谁能抗拒神明？我必须遵从律法，再也不得与你握手。但无人能夺去你辉煌的名号——我军元帅！我将供养你，你可以带着荣誉卫兵，永远住在我的一所宅邸里。大马士革永不忘记你的功勋！啊，这戴冠之无能的悲惨言辞！啊，赋予君王的权力的嘲弄——他们竟无法护住至亲之人免遭上天隐秘的震怒！乃缦，我的儿，我的儿！

===Original===
Naaman: (Slowly, passing his hand over his eyes, and looking up.) Am I alone With thee, inexorable one, whose pride Offended takes this horrible revenge? I must submit my mortal flesh to thee, Almighty, but I will not call thee god! Yet thou hast found the way to wound my soul Most deeply through the flesh; and I must find The way to let my wounded soul escape!

===Chinese===
乃缦：（缓缓举手抹眼，抬头上望。）我竟只与你这铁面无情者相对吗——你的骄傲受了冒犯，便施以此等可怖的报复？我必得向你——全能者——降服我这必死的肉身，但我绝不称你为神！然而你已找到最深的途径，借肉身来刺伤我的灵魂；我也必须找到一条路，让我受伤的灵魂得以逃脱！

===Original===
Ruahmah: (At his feet.) Nay, not alone, dear lord, for I am here; And I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee!

Naaman: What voice is that? The silence of my tomb Is broken by a ray of music—whose?

Ruahmah: (Rising.) The one who loves thee best in all the world.

===Chinese===
如阿玛：（伏在他脚边。）不，并不孤单，亲爱的主，因为我在这里；我永不离开你，永不弃绝你！

乃缦：那是谁的声音？我坟墓般的沉寂被一缕乐音划破——是谁？

如阿玛：（起身。）这世上最爱你的人。

===Original===
Naaman: Why that should be—O dare I dream it true? Tsarpi, my wife? Have I misjudged thy heart As cold and proud? How nobly thou forgivest! Thou com'st to hold me from the last disgrace— The coward's flight into the dark. Go back Unstained, my sword! Life is endurable While there is one alive on earth who loves us,

===Chinese===
乃缦：那该是——哦，我敢当真做此梦吗？瑟璧，我的妻？难道我把你的心错看作冰冷高傲？你宽恕得多么高贵！你来是要把我从最后的耻辱——那向黑暗逃窜的懦夫之行——中挽回。回到鞘中吧，我的剑，一尘不染！只要世上还有一个活着的人爱我们，生命便还堪忍受，

===Original===
Ruahmah: My lord—my lord—O listen! You have erred— You do mistake me now—this dream—

Naaman: Ah, wake me not! For I can conquer death Dreaming this dream. Let me at last believe, Though gods are cruel, a woman can be kind. Grant me but this! For see—I ask so little— Only to know that thou art faithful— Only to lean upon the thought that thou, My wife, art near me, though I touch thee not— O this will hold me up, though it be given From pity more than love.

===Chinese===
如阿玛：主上——主上——哦，请听我说！您弄错了——您此刻认错人了——这梦——

乃缦：啊，别叫醒我！因为做此梦时，我能征服死亡。让我终于相信一回吧：纵然神明残酷，女子却能温柔。求你只许我这一桩！因为你看——我所求如此之微——只求知你忠贞——只知你，我的妻，虽不可触碰，却在我身畔——哦，仅此便能撑住我，哪怕它出于怜悯多于爱情。

===Original===
Ruahmah: (Trembling, and speaking slowly.) Not so, my lord! My pity is a stream; my pride of thee Is like the sea that doth engulf the stream; My love for thee is like the sovran moon That rules the sea. The tides that fill my soul Flow unto thee and follow after thee; And where thou goest I will go; and where Thou diest I will die—in the same hour.

===Chinese===
如阿玛：（颤抖着，缓缓说道。）不然，主上！我的怜悯是一条溪流；我对你的骄傲如大海，吞没这条溪流；我对你的爱如那统御大海的至尊之月。充盈我灵魂的潮水都向你涌去、随你而流；你往何处去，我也往何处去；你在何处死，我也在何处死——死于同一时辰。

===Original===
Naaman: O touch me not! Thou shall not share my doom.

Ruahmah: Entreat me not to go. I will obey In all but this; but rob me not of this— The only boon that makes life worth the living— To walk beside thee day by day, and keep Thy foot from stumbling; to prepare thy food When thou art hungry, music for thy rest, And cheerful words to comfort thy black hour; And so to lead thee ever on, and on, Through darkness, till we find the door of hope.

===Chinese===
乃缦：哦，不可碰我！你不可与我共此厄运。

如阿玛：莫劝我离去。万事我都听从，唯独此事不能；但别夺去我这唯一的恩赐——唯一使生命值得活下去的恩赐——日日行在你身旁，使你的脚不致跌倒；你饥饿时为你备食，你歇息时为你奏乐，用欢快的话语抚慰你的至暗时刻；如此引领你一直前行、前行，穿过黑暗，直到我们找到希望之门。

===Original===
Naaman: What word is that? The leper has no hope.

Ruahmah: Dear lord, the mark upon thy brow is yet No broader than my little fingernail. Thy force is not abated, and thy step Is firm. Wilt thou surrender to the enemy Before thy strength is touched? Why, let me put A drop of courage from my breast in thine. There is a hope for thee. The captive maid Of Israel who dwelt within thy house Knew of a god very compassionate, Long-suffering, slow to anger, one who heals The sick, hath pity on the fatherless, And saves the poor and him who has no helper. His prophet dwells nigh to Samaria; And I have heard that he hath brought the dead To life again. We'll go to him. The King, If I beseech him, will appoint a guard Of thine own soldiers and Saballidin, Thy friend, to convoy us upon our journey. He'll give us royal letters to the king Of Israel to make our welcome sure; And we will take the open road, beneath The open sky, tomorrow, and go on Together till we find the door of hope. Come, come with me!

===Chinese===
乃缦：那是什么话？大麻风的人没有希望。

如阿玛：亲爱的主上，你额上的印记尚不及我小指甲那般宽。你的气力未减，步伐依然稳健。你岂可在力气尚未受损之前便向敌人投降？来，让我从胸中取一滴勇气注入你心。你是有一线希望的。那住在你家中的以色列被掳使女，知道一位极富怜悯的神——恒久忍耐、不轻易发怒，医治病人、怜恤孤儿、拯救穷苦和无人救助者。他的先知就住在撒马利亚附近；我曾听说他使死人复活。我们去找他。王若经我恳求，必会派一队你自己的兵卒和你的朋友萨巴利丁护送我们上路。他会给我们致以色列王的御书，确保我们受到礼遇；明日我们便走上去，顶着开阔的天空，踏上大路，一同前行，直到找到希望之门。来，跟我来！

===Original===
Naaman: (Drawing back.) Thou must not touch me!

Ruahmah: (Unclasping her girdle and putting the end in hand.) Take my girdle, then!

Naaman: (Kissing the clasp of the girdle.) I do begin to think there is a God, Since love on earth can work such miracles!

===Chinese===
乃缦：（后退。）你不可碰我！

如阿玛：（解下腰带，将末端放入他手中。）那就握住我的腰带！

乃缦：（亲吻腰带的扣环。）我开始相信有一位神了，既然人间的爱能行此等奇迹！
