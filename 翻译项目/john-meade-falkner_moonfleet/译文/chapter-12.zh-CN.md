## XII A Funeral / 十二 葬礼

===Original===

“How he lies in his rights of a man!
						

						Death has done all death can.”

> Browning

===Chinese===

“他这样安卧着，享有一个人的名分！
死亡所能做的，死亡都已做尽。”

> Browning

===Original===

We stood for a moment holding one another’s hands; then Ratsey spoke. “John, these two months have changed thee from boy to man. Thou wast a child when I turned that morning as we went up Hoar Head with the packhorses, and looked back on thee and Elzevir below, and Maskew lying on the ground. ’Twas a sorry business, and has broken up the finest gang that ever ran a cargo, besides driving thee and Elzevir to hide in caves and dens of the earth. Thou shouldst have come with us that morn; not have stayed behind. The work was too rough for boys: the skipper should have piped the reefing-hands.”

===Chinese===

我们执手相对，站了片刻；随后 Ratsey 开了口。“John，这两个月把你从孩子变成了汉子。那天早晨，我们赶着驮马上 Hoar Head，我回过头，望见你和 Elzevir 在底下，Maskew 躺在地上——那时你还只是个孩子。那是一桩痛心的营生，拆散了天底下跑私货顶好的一伙人，还害得你和 Elzevir 躲进岩洞穴窟里藏身。那天早上你原该跟我们一道走的，不该留在后头。那活计不是孩子干的：船长早该吹响哨子，把收帆的水手全数召集上去。”

===Original===

It was true enough, or seemed to me true then, for I felt much cast down; but only said, “Nay, Master Ratsey, where Master Block stays, there I must stay too, and where he goes I follow.”

===Chinese===

这话不假，或者说当时在我看来不假，因为我心里正低沉得很；只是答道：“不，Ratsey 师傅，Block 师傅在哪儿安身，我就得在哪儿安身；他上哪儿，我就跟到哪儿。”

===Original===

Then I sat down upon the bed in the corner, feeling my leg began to ache; and the storm, which had lulled for a few minutes, came up again all the fiercer with wilder gusts and showers of spray and rain driving into the cave from seaward. So I was scarce sat down when in came a roaring blast, filling even our corner with cold, wet air, that quenched the weakling candle flame.

===Chinese===

随后我在角落的铺上坐下，觉得腿又疼了起来；歇了几分钟的风暴重新卷来，刮得愈发狂暴，一阵紧似一阵的狂风裹着浪沫和雨水，从海那面直灌进洞来。我刚坐下不久，就闯进一股呼啸的狂飙，连我们这个角落也灌满了又冷又湿的空气，把那点弱不禁风的烛火扑灭了。

===Original===

“God save us, what a night!” Ratsey cried.

===Chinese===

“上帝保佑我们，好一个夜晚！”Ratsey 失声喊道。

===Original===

“God save poor souls at sea,” said I.

===Chinese===

“上帝保佑海上那些可怜的人吧。”我说。

===Original===

“Amen to that,” says he, “and would that every ‘amen’ I have said had come as truly from my heart. There will be sea enough on Moonfleet Beach this night to lift a schooner to the top of it, and launch her down into the fields behind. I had as lief be in the Mohune vault as in this fearsome place, and liefer too, if half the tales men tell are true of faces that may meet one here. For God’s sake let us light a fire, for I caught sight of a store of driftwood before that sickly candle went out.”

===Chinese===

“对这话正该说一声‘阿门’，”他说，“但愿我说过的每一声‘阿门’，都这样字字发自我心底才好。今夜 Moonfleet 海滩上的海浪，准能把一艘纵帆船高高举过浪尖，再把她抛进后头的田地里。叫我待在这骇人的地方，我不在乎换成 Mohune 家的墓窖；倘若人们讲的、说这儿的黑暗里会迎面撞见种种脸孔的传闻有半句是真的，那我情愿去墓窖。看在上帝分上，咱们生堆火吧；那病恹恹的蜡烛熄掉之前，我瞥见那边存着好些浮柴呢。”

===Original===

It was some time before we got a fire alight, and even after the flame had caught well hold, the rush of the wind would every now and again blow the smoke into our eyes, or send a shower of sparks dancing through the cave. But by degrees the logs began to glow clear white, and such a cheerful warmth came out, as was in itself a solace and remedy for man’s afflictions.

===Chinese===

我们费了好一阵才把火生着；即便火苗已经烧得牢牢扎住了根，急风仍时不时把烟扑进我们眼里，或是掀起一阵火星，满洞飞舞。渐渐地，柴块烧出了莹白的光，散出一股欢快的暖意，本身就成了人世愁苦的一味慰藉和良药。

===Original===

“Ah!” said Ratsey, “I was shrammed with wet and cold, and half-dead with this baffling wind. It is a blessed thing a fire,” and he unbuttoned his pilot-coat, “and needful now, if ever. My soul is very low, lad, for this place has strange memories for me; and I recollect, forty years ago (when I was just a boy like thee), old lander Jordan’s gang, and I among them, were in this very cave on such another night. I was new to the trade then, as thou might be, and could not sleep for noise of wind and sea. And in the small hours of an autumn morning, as I lay here, just where we lie now, I heard such wailing cries above the storm, ay, and such shrieks of women, as made my blood run cold and have not yet forgot them. And so I woke the gang who were all deep asleep as seasoned contrabandiers should be; but though we knew that there were fellow-creatures fighting for their lives in the seething flood beneath us, we could not stir hand or foot to save them, for nothing could be seen for rain and spray, and ’twas not till next morning that we learned the *Florida* had foundered just below with every soul on board. Ay, ’tis a queer life, and you and Block are in a queer strait now, and that is what I came to tell you. See here.” And he took out of his pocket an oblong strip of printed paper:

===Chinese===

“啊！”Ratsey 说，“我刚才又湿又冷，浑身冻僵，这阵乱搅人的风差点儿要了我的命。火真是桩蒙福的东西。”他解开了领航外套的纽扣，“眼下更是缺不得。孩子，我的心绪低得很，因为这地方于我有一段古怪的旧忆；我想起四十年前——那时我也不过是个像你这样的孩子——老接货人 Jordan 那一伙，我也在其内，就在这么一个夜晚待在这个洞里。那时我初入这行当，正同你如今一样，风声涛声吵得睡不着。那是个秋天的凌晨，天还早，我躺在这儿，就躺在我们如今躺的地方，听见风暴之上响起一阵阵恸哭，唉，还有女人的尖喊，喊得我血液都凉了，到今天也没有忘记。于是我把那伙人叫醒——老练的私贩子本该睡得死沉——可我们明知脚底下沸腾的洪流里，有自己的同类正在为性命挣扎，却抬不了一根手指去救：雨里浪沫里什么也望不见，直到第二天早晨我们才得知，‘Florida’号就在底下失事沉了，船上没有一个魂灵活下来。唉，人生古怪；你如今和 Block 正落在一个古怪的窘境里，这正是我要来告诉你的。瞧这儿。”说着，他从口袋里掏出一张长方形的印刷纸条：

===Original===

G. R.

Whitehall, 15 May 1758

Whereas it hath been humbly represented to the King that on Friday, the night of the 16th of April last, *Thomas Maskew*, a Justice of the Peace, was most inhumanly murdered at Hoar Head, a lone place in the Parish of Chaldron, in the County of Dorset, by one *Elzevir Block* and one *John Trenchard*, both of the Parish of Moonfleet, in the aforesaid County: His Majesty, for the better discovering and bringing to Justice these Persons, is pleased to promise His Most Gracious *pardon* to any of the Persons concerned therein, except the Persons who actually committed the said Murder; and, as a further Encouragement, a *reward of fifty pounds* to any Person who shall furnish such *information* as shall lead to the *apprehension* of the said *Elzevir Block*, and a *reward* of *twenty pounds* to any Person who shall furnish such *information* as shall lead to the *apprehension* of the said *John Trenchard*. Such *information* to be given to *me*, or to the *governor* of *His Majesty’s Gaol* in Dorchester.

Holdernesse.

===Chinese===

G. R.

白厅，1758 年 5 月 15 日

兹经谦恭奏闻国王陛下：本年 4 月 16 日（星期五）夜间，治安法官*Thomas Maskew*在多塞特郡查尔德伦（Chaldron）教区一处名为 Hoar Head 的荒僻之地，遭该郡 Moonfleet 教区之*Elzevir Block*与*John Trenchard*二人极不人道地谋害。为便于查明并严惩此等人犯，国王陛下特予恩允：凡与本案有涉之人，除实际行凶者外，一体颁予最仁慈之*赦免*；此外为示激励，凡有人提供*线索*、据以*缉获*上述*Elzevir Block*者，赏*五十镑*；凡提供*线索*、据以*缉获*上述*John Trenchard*者，赏*二十镑*。此项*线索*，可呈报*本人*，或多切斯特*国王监狱*之*典狱长*。

Holdernesse.

===Original===

“There﻿—that’s the bill,” he said; “and a vastly fine piece it is, and yet I wish that ’twas played with other actors. Now, in Moonfleet there is none that know your hiding-place, and not a man, nor woman either, that would tell if they knew it ten times over. But fifty pounds for Elzevir, and twenty pounds for an empty pumpkin-top like thine, is a fair round sum, and there are vagabonds about this countryside scurvy enough to try to earn it. And some of these have set the Excisemen on *my* track, with tales of how it is I that know where you lie hid, and bring you meat and drink. So it is that I cannot stir abroad now, no, not even to the church o’ Sundays, without having some rogue lurking at my heels to watch my movements. And that is why I chose such a night to come hither, knowing these knaves like dry skins, but never thinking that the wind would blow like this. I am come to tell Block that ’tis not safe for me to be so much in Purbeck, and that I dare no longer bring food or whatnot, or these man-hounds will scent you out. Your leg is sound again, and ’tis best to be flitting while you may, and there’s the *Éperon d’Or*, and Chauvelais to give you welcome on the other side.”

===Chinese===

“喏——就是这张告示，”他说，“还真是一件印得漂漂亮亮的杰作；只可惜我巴不得登台的是别的角儿。如今在 Moonfleet，没有一个人知道你们的藏身之处，而且不论男人女人，就是知道了十遍，也决计没有谁会去告发。可是 Elzevir 五十镑，你那颗空南瓜脑袋二十镑，加起来是一笔足足的好数目；这一带乡下游荡的流氓里，下作得想挣这笔钱的大有人在。他们中间已经有几个把税吏支到*我*的踪迹上，说是知道你们藏在哪儿、给你们送吃送喝的正是我。闹得我如今不敢出门——连礼拜天上教堂都不成——身后不缀着个奸细窥测我的动静。我挑这么个夜晚来，也正为这个：我知道这帮家伙跟干皮子一样怕沾水；只是没料到风会刮成这副样子。我是来告诉 Block：我再在珀贝克（Purbeck）这厢频繁走动就不保险了，吃的喝的等等，我也不敢再送了，否则这些猎犬终究会把你们嗅出来。你的腿已经好利索了，趁还走得了，早挪窝为妙；对岸有‘Éperon d’Or’客栈，还有 Chauvelais 迎接你们。”

===Original===

I told him how Elzevir was gone this very night to Poole to settle with the *Bonaventure*, when she should come to take us off; and at that Ratsey seemed pleased. There were many things I wished to learn of him, and especially how Grace did, but felt a shyness, and durst not ask him. And he said no more for a minute, seeming low-hearted and crouching over the fire. So we sat huddled in the corner by the glowing logs, the red light flickering on the cave roof, and showing the lines on Ratsey’s face; while the steam rose from his drying clothes. The gale blew as fiercely as ever, but the tide had fallen, and there was not so much spray coming into the cave. Then Ratsey spoke again﻿—

===Chinese===

我告诉他，Elzevir 正是今夜去了普尔，同“Bonaventure”号的人敲定船几时来接我们；Ratsey 听了像是高兴。我有许多事想向他打听，尤其是 Grace 的近况，却又害臊，不敢开口。他也有那么一分钟没再作声，闷闷地弓着身子烤火。我们就这样蜷在角落里，守着烧得通红的柴堆；红光在洞顶上摇曳，照亮了 Ratsey 脸上的皱纹，他那正在烘干的衣裳上腾起缕缕水汽。风暴还是刮得那么凶，只是潮水已落，灌进洞来的浪沫少多了。随后 Ratsey 又开了口——

===Original===

“My heart is very heavy, John, tonight, to think how all the good old times are gone, and how that Master Block can never again go back to Moonfleet. It was as fine a lander’s crew as ever stood together, not even excepting Captain Jordan’s, and now must all be broken up; for this mess of Maskew’s has made the place too hot to hold us, and ’twill be many a long day before another cargo’s run on Moonfleet Beach. But how to get the liquor out of Mohune’s vault I know not; and that reminds me, I have something in my pouches for Elzevir an’ thee;” and with that he drew forth either lapel a great wicker-bound flask. He put one to his lips, tilting it and drinking long and deep, and then passed it to me, with a sigh of satisfaction. “Ah, that has the right smack. Here, take it, child, and warm thy heart; ’tis the true milk of Ararat, and the last thou’lt taste this side the Channel.”

===Chinese===

“John，今晚我的心沉得很，想到旧日的好时光全完了，想到 Block 师傅再也不能回 Moonfleet 了。从前那一班卸货的人手，是站得拢的数一数二的好班底，连 Jordan 船长那一伙也不能比，如今却只得散伙；Maskew 搅下的这摊祸事，把这地方烧得容不下我们了，往后许多年，Moonfleet 海滩上再别想卸一船私货。只是 Mohune 家墓窖里那些酒怎么弄出来，我是想不出法子了；这倒提醒我，我两边口袋里各揣着一件东西，是带给 Elzevir 和你的；”说着，他从外衣两边口袋里各掏出一只柳条套着的大酒瓶。他把一只举到嘴边，仰起脖子咕咚咕咚灌了个痛快，然后满足地叹了口气，递给了我。“啊，这味道才叫地道。来，拿着，孩子，暖暖你的心；这是真正的亚拉腊山奶，也是你在这边海峡能尝到的最后一口了。”

===Original===

Then I drank too, but lightly, for the good liquor was no stranger to me, though it was only so few months ago that I had tasted it for the first time in the Why Not? and in a minute it tingled in my fingertips. Soon a grateful sense of warmth and comfort stole over me, and our state seemed not so desperate, nor even the night so wild. Ratsey, too, wore a more cheerful air, and the lines in his face were not so deeply marked; the golden, sparkling influence of the flask had loosed his tongue, and he was talking now of what I most wanted to hear.

===Chinese===

我也喝了，只是浅浅一口；这好酒于我并不陌生，虽说头一回尝到它，不过是几个月前在“Why Not?”客栈里的事。不消一分钟，酒意便麻酥酥地窜到了指尖。很快，一股暖融融的舒坦悄悄漫上全身，我们的处境似乎也不那么绝望了，连这夜晚也不那么狂暴了。Ratsey 也开朗了些，脸上的皱纹不那么深了；瓶中金黄闪亮的魔力松开了他的舌头，此刻他讲起的，正是我顶想听的话。

===Original===

“Yes, yes, it is a sad breakup, and what will happen to the old Why Not? I cannot tell. None have passed the threshold since you left, only the Duchy men came and sealed the doors, making it felony to force them. And even these lawyer chaps know not where the right stands, for Maskew never paid a rent and died before he took possession; and Master Block’s term is long expired, and now he is in hiding and an outlaw.

===Chinese===

“是啊，是啊，散伙是伤心事，那家老‘Why Not?’往后怎样，我也说不上来。你们走后，没人跨进过那道门槛，只有公爵领地的人来封了门，把撬门定成了重罪。可就是那帮讼师爷，也闹不清产权究竟归谁：Maskew 从没付过租金，人死了，又还没正式接过手；Block 师傅的租期早满了，如今他人又在逃，成了法外之徒。”

===Original===

“But I am sorriest for Maskew’s girl, who grows thin and pale as any lily. For when the soldiers brought the body back, the men stood at their doors and cursed the clay, and some of the fishwives spat at it; and old Mother Veitch, who kept house for him, swore he had never paid her a penny of wages, and that she was afear’d to stop under the same roof with such an evil corpse. So out she goes from the Manor House, leaving that poor child alone in it with her dead father; and there were not wanting some to say it was all a judgement; and called to mind how Elzevir had been once left alone with his dead son at the Why Not? But in the village there was not a man that doubted that ’twas Block had sent Maskew to his account, nor did I doubt it either, till a tale got abroad that he was killed by a stray shot fired by the posse from the cliff. And when they took the hue-and-cry papers to the Manor House for his lass, as next of kin, to sign the requisition, she would not set her name to it, saying that Block had never lifted his hand against her father when they met at Moonfleet or on the road, and that she never would believe he was the man to let his anger sleep so long and then attack an enemy in cold blood. And as for thee, she knew thee for a trusty lad, who would not do such things himself, nor yet stand by whilst others did them.”

===Chinese===

“可我最心疼的是 Maskew 的女儿，她一天天瘦下去，白得像一枝百合。士兵们把尸首抬回来那天，男人们站在自家门口咒那具臭皮囊，有几个渔家妇人还朝它啐唾沫；替他管家看屋的 Veitch 老婆婆赌咒说，他连一个铜板的工钱也没付过她，说她不敢同这样一具凶死的尸首待在同一个屋顶底下。于是她卷起包袱，走出了庄园宅邸，撇下那可怜的孩子，独自守着她死去的父亲；也不乏有人说这是天道报应，还记起 Elzevir 当年也曾独自伴着他死去的儿子，待在‘Why Not?’里。只是村子里没有一个人怀疑，送 Maskew 去结总账的就是 Block，起先我也不怀疑，直到后来传开一种说法，说他是叫缉私队从崖上放的一颗流弹打死的。后来他们把缉捕文书送到庄园宅邸，请他女儿以至亲的身份签那缉拿的呈请，她却不肯落笔，说 Block 在 Moonfleet 或是在路上同她父亲相遇时，从没对他抬过一根指头；说她决不相信他是那样一种人——把怒气隐忍这么久，再去冷血地暗算一个仇人。至于你，她知道你是个靠得住的孩子，自己决不做这样的事，别人做的时候，也决不会在旁边站着。”

===Original===

Now what Ratsey said was sweeter than any music in my ears, and I felt myself a better man, as anyone must of whom a true woman speaks well, and that I must live uprightly to deserve such praise. Then I resolved that come what might I would make my way once more to Moonfleet, before we fled from England, and see Grace; so that I might tell her all that happened about her father’s death, saving only that Elzevir had meant himself to put Maskew away; for it was no use to tell her this when she had said that he could never think to do such a thing, and besides, for all I knew, he never did mean to shoot, but only to frighten him. Though I thus resolved, I said nothing of it to Master Ratsey, but only nodded, and he went on﻿—

===Chinese===

Ratsey 这番话，在我听来比任何音乐都悦耳；我觉得自己成了一个更好的人——一位真正的女子说谁的好话，谁都会这样觉得——也觉得必须活得端正，才配得上这样的夸赞。于是我拿定主意：无论遭逢什么，在逃离英格兰之前，我一定要再设法回一趟 Moonfleet，去见 Grace，把有关她父亲之死的前前后后都告诉她，只瞒下一桩：Elzevir 原是打算亲手除掉 Maskew 的；她既已说过，他决计想不到会做这样的事，再把这层告诉她也是枉然；何况据我所知，他也许根本没有开枪的意思，不过是要吓唬吓唬他。主意虽这样定了，我对 Ratsey 师傅却只字不提，只点了点头，他便接着讲下去——

===Original===

“Well, seeing there was no one save this poor girl to look to putting Maskew under ground, I must needs take it in hand myself; roughing together a sound coffin and digging as fair a grave for him as could be made for any lord, except that lords have always vaults to sleep in. Then I got Mother Nutting’s fish-cart to carry the body down, for there was not a man in Moonfleet would lay hand to the coffin to bear it; and off we started down the street, I leading the walleyed pony, and the coffin following on the trolley. There was no mourner to see him home except his daughter, and she without a bit of black upon her, for she had no time to get her crapes; and yet she needed none, having grief writ plain enough upon her face.

===Chinese===

“唉，眼见得除了这可怜的姑娘，再没有一个人操持 Maskew 的下葬，我只好亲自接下这桩事；胡乱拼了一口结实的棺材，又给他挖了一座坟，齐整得不输给哪位老爷——只除开老爷们总有墓窖可睡。我又借了 Nutting 大娘的鱼车来运尸首，因为 Moonfleet 没有一个男人肯搭手抬棺；我们便顺着街出发了，我牵着那匹斜眼的小马，棺材跟在手拉车后头。送他入土的送葬人只有他女儿一个，而且身上连一片黑纱也没戴，她来不及置办孝服；可她也用不着，悲恸早已明明白白写在她脸上了。”

===Original===
