## Endnotes / 尾注

===Original===
1. *The Starvel Hollow Tragedy*, published 1927, in which Inspector French uncovered a crime of extraordinary brutality from a little heap of ashes. ↩︎

2. *The Sea Mystery*, published 1928, in which Inspector French, with no evidence to go by but the naked body of a man washed up from the sea, solved a crime of amazing ingenuity. ↩︎

===Chinese===
1. 《斯塔弗尔谷惨案》（*The Starvel Hollow Tragedy*），1927 年出版，弗伦奇探长从一小堆灰烬中侦破了一桩极端残忍的罪行。↩︎

2. 《海上疑云》（*The Sea Mystery*），1928 年出版，弗伦奇探长凭着一具被海水冲上岸的男性裸体这唯一的线索，破获了一桩手法惊人巧妙的罪行。↩︎
