## III / 三

===Original===
Slowly and cautiously I went my solitary way down the lamplit road and towards the large building I have described. The road itself seemed like a great Alpine pass, skirting rocky mountains of which the one through whose chasms I had descended formed a link. Deep below to the left lay a vast valley, which presented to my astonished eye the unmistakable evidences of art and culture. There were fields covered with a strange vegetation, similar to none I have seen above the earth; the colour of it not green, but rather of a dull and leaden hue or of a golden red.

There were lakes and rivulets which seemed to have been curved into artificial banks; some of pure water, others that shone like pools of naphtha. At my right hand, ravines and defiles opened amidst the rocks, with passes between, evidently constructed by art, and bordered by trees resembling, for the most part, gigantic ferns, with exquisite varieties of feathery foliage, and stems like those of the palm-tree. Others were more like the cane-plant, but taller, bearing large clusters of flowers. Others, again, had the form of enormous fungi, with short thick stems supporting a wide dome-like roof, from which either rose or drooped long slender branches. The whole scene behind, before, and beside me, far as the eye could reach, was brilliant with innumerable lamps. The world without a sun was bright and warm as an Italian landscape at noon, but the air less oppressive, the heat softer. Nor was the scene before me void of signs of habitation. I could distinguish at a distance, whether on the banks of the lake or rivulet, or halfway upon eminences, embedded amidst the vegetation, buildings that must surely be the homes of men. I could even discover, though far off, forms that appeared to me human moving amidst the landscape. As I paused to gaze, I saw to the right, gliding quickly through the air, what appeared a small boat, impelled by sails shaped like wings. It soon passed out of sight, descending amidst the shades of a forest. Right above me there was no sky, but only a cavernous roof. This roof grew higher and higher at the distance of the landscapes beyond, till it became imperceptible, as an atmosphere of haze formed itself beneath.

Continuing my walk, I started﻿—from a bush that resembled a great tangle of seaweeds, interspersed with fern-like shrubs and plants of large leafage shaped like that of the aloe or prickly-pear﻿—a curious animal about the size and shape of a deer. But as, after bounding away a few paces, it turned round and gazed at me inquisitively, I perceived that it was not like any species of deer now extant above the earth, but it brought instantly to my recollection a plaster cast I had seen in some museum of a variety of the elk stag, said to have existed before the Deluge. The creature seemed tame enough, and, after inspecting me a moment or two, began to graze on the singular herbage around undismayed and careless.

===Chinese===
我缓步而行，小心谨慎，独自沿着那条灯火通明的道路，朝我先前描写过的那座高大建筑走去。这条路本身宛如一道宏伟的阿尔卑斯山隘，傍着巉岩群山蜿蜒，而我正是从其中一座山的裂罅间降下来的，那山便是这绵延群山的一环。左边低深处横卧着一片广袤的山谷，向我惊异的目光展示出人工与文明的无可置疑的迹象。那里有田野，覆满奇异的草木，与我在这地上世界见过的任何植物都无一相似；其色并非青绿，而是近乎一种暗滞的铅灰，或是一种金红。

那里有湖泊和溪流，似乎都被修成人工的堤岸；有的是清水，有的闪亮如一泓泓石脑油。在我右手边，岩石间敞开一道道深谷与隘峡，其间通着路径，显然出自人工修筑，两旁列生着树木，多半状似巨大的蕨类，羽状枝叶精妙纷呈、变化万端，茎干则如棕榈之干。另一些更像甘蔗，却更为高挺，顶着大簇大簇的花。还有一些形似硕大无朋的菌类，短而粗的茎干撑起一面穹顶般的阔盖，从那上面或挺出、或垂下细长的枝条。我身后、眼前、两侧，目力所及的整个景象，都被无数的灯照得灿烂辉煌。这没有太阳的世界明亮而温暖，宛如正午的意大利风光，只是空气不那么窒闷，暑气也更为柔和。我面前的景象也并非没有人类居住的迹象。我能辨认出远处——或在湖畔溪边，或半在丘阜之上——掩映于草木之间的建筑，那必定是人类的居所。我甚至还能发现，虽则在很远的地方，一些人形的身影正在景物之间移动。正当我驻足凝望之际，我看见右方有一叶看似小舟的东西，张着翼形的帆，疾速地滑过空中。它不久便隐没不见，降入一片森林的暗影之中。我的正上方没有天空，只有一面洞穴般的穹顶。这穹顶随着远方景物的延伸愈升愈高，终至无从察觉，因为它下面自行形成了一层雾霭濛濛的大气。

我继续前行，忽然吃了一惊——从一丛灌木中，那灌木宛如一大团纠缠的海草，其间又杂生着蕨状的灌丛和叶形如龙舌兰或仙人掌的大叶植物——窜出一只形体大小略似鹿的古怪动物。但它在跳开几步之后，回过头来好奇地凝视我，我这才看出它并不像地上现今尚存的任何鹿种，倒使我立刻想起我在某家博物馆里见过的一具石膏铸型——一种麋鹿的变种，据说曾存在于大洪水之前。这生物似乎相当驯顺，把我端详了一两眼之后，便毫不畏怯、漫不经心地啃食起四周那奇异的草来。
