## X / 十

===Original===
The word *Ana* (pronounced broadly *Arna*) corresponds with our plural *men*; *An* (pronounced *Arn*), the singular, with *man*. The word for woman is *Gy* (pronounced hard, as in *Guy*); it forms itself into *Gy-ei* for the plural, but the *G* becomes soft in the plural like *Jy-ei*. They have a proverb to the effect that this difference in pronunciation is symbolical, for that the female sex is soft in the concrete, but hard to deal with in the individual. The *Gy-ei* are in the fullest enjoyment of all the rights of equality with males, for which certain philosophers above ground contend.

===Chinese===
安-纳（*Ana*）一词（宽读近于 *Arna*），相当于我们的复数“男人们”；其单数安（*An*）（读作 *Arn*），相当于“男人”。女子的称谓是姬（*Gy*）（发硬音，如同 *Guy*）；它变化为姬-埃（*Gy-ei*）即成复数，惟复数中的 G 转为软音，读如 *Jy-ei*。他们有一句谚语，大意说这种读音上的差别具有象征意味：女性就整体而言是柔软的，落到个体身上却难以对付。姬-埃们充分享有与男性平等的一切权利——那正是地上某些哲学家力争的东西。

===Original===
In childhood they perform the offices of work and labour impartially with the boys, and, indeed, in the earlier age appropriated to the destruction of animals irreclaimably hostile, the girls are frequently preferred, as being by constitution more ruthless under the influence of fear or hate. In the interval between infancy and the marriageable age familiar intercourse between the sexes is suspended. At the marriageable age it is renewed, never with worse consequences than those which attend upon marriage. All arts and vocations allotted to the one sex are open to the other, and the *Gy-ei* arrogate to themselves a superiority in all those abstruse and mystical branches of reasoning, for which they say the *Ana* are unfitted by a duller sobriety of understanding, or the routine of their matter-of-fact occupations, just as young ladies in our own world constitute themselves authorities in the subtlest points of theological doctrine, for which few men, actively engaged in worldly business, have sufficient learning or refinement of intellect. Whether owing to early training in gymnastic exercises or to their constitutional organisation, the *Gy-ei* are usually superior to the *Ana* in physical strength (an important element in the consideration and maintenance of female rights). They attain to loftier stature, and amid their rounder proportions are embedded sinews and muscles as hardy as those of the other sex. Indeed they assert that, according to the original laws of nature, females were intended to be larger than males, and maintain this dogma by reference to the earliest formations of life in insects, and in the most ancient family of the vertebrata﻿—viz., fishes﻿—in both of which the females are generally large enough to make a meal of their consorts if they so desire. Above all, the *Gy-ei* have a readier and more concentred power over that mysterious fluid or agency which contains the element of destruction, with a larger portion of that sagacity which comprehends dissimulation. Thus they cannot only defend themselves against all aggressions from the males, but could, at any moment when he least expected his danger, terminate the existence of an offending spouse. To the credit of the *Gy-ei* no instance of their abuse of this awful superiority in the art of destruction is on record for several ages. The last that occurred in the community I speak of appears (according to their chronology) to have been about two thousand years ago. A *Gy*, then, in a fit of jealousy, slew her husband; and this abominable act inspired such terror among the males that they emigrated in a body and left all the *Gy-ei* to themselves. The history runs that the widowed *Gy-ei*, thus reduced to despair, fell upon the murderess when in her sleep (and therefore unarmed), and killed her, and then entered into a solemn obligation amongst themselves to abrogate forever the exercise of their extreme conjugal powers, and to inculcate the same obligation forever and ever on their female children. By this conciliatory process, a deputation despatched to the fugitive consorts succeeded in persuading many to return, but those who did return were mostly the elder ones. The younger, either from too craven a doubt of their consorts, or too high an estimate of their own merits, rejected all overtures, and, remaining in other communities, were caught up there by other mates, with whom perhaps they were no better off. But the loss of so large a portion of the male youth operated as a salutary warning on the *Gy-ei*, and confirmed them in the pious resolution to which they had pledged themselves. Indeed it is now popularly considered that, by long hereditary disuse, the *Gy-ei* have lost both the aggressive and defensive superiority over the *Ana* which they once possessed, just as in the inferior animals above the earth many peculiarities in their original formation, intended by nature for their protection, gradually fade or become inoperative when not needed under altered circumstances. I should be sorry, however, for any *An* who induced a *Gy* to make the experiment whether he or she were the stronger.

===Chinese===
幼年时期，她们与男童一样承担劳作的职分；而且，在那个专用于消灭无可救药之敌兽的早年阶段，女童往往更受偏爱，因为论天性，她们在恐惧或憎恨的影响之下更为狠辣。从幼年到婚龄之间，两性的亲密交往暂告中断；到了婚龄便重新恢复，其后果之坏，从无逾于婚姻本身所伴随者。分派给一种性别的技艺与职业，对另一种性别也一律开放；而姬-埃们自命在一切艰深玄奥的推理学问上高人一等，她们说，安-纳们或因理解力较为迟钝持重、或因实际事务的刻板例行，均不宜问津——恰如我们这个世界的年轻女士们，动辄自居为神学教义中最精微之点的权威，而积极从事世俗事务的男子，罕有具备足够的学识或心智之精细者。无论当归因于体操的早期训练，还是归因于她们的天生体质，姬-埃们在体力上通常胜过安-纳们（这是考虑和维护女性权利的一个重要因素）。她们身量更高，而在较为丰圆的形体之中，蕴嵌着与另一性别同样强健的筋腱与肌肉。她们甚至断言，依照太初的自然法则，雌性本就该比雄性更为高大；并援引昆虫中最早成形的生命形态，以及脊椎动物最古老的家族——即鱼类——来维护这条教义：在这两类生物中，雌性通常都大到足以把她们的配偶当作一餐吃掉，只要她们愿意。尤其是，姬-埃们对那种蕴含毁灭要素的神秘流质或力量，掌握着更为敏捷、更为凝聚的权能，还禀有更大分量的、深谙掩饰之道的机敏。因此，她们不仅能抵御男性的一切侵犯，而且能在丈夫最料不到危险临头的任何时刻，了结一位失礼的配偶的性命。为姬-埃们增光的是，数个世纪以来，未曾有她们滥用这种可畏的毁灭绝技之优势的记载。我所谈及的这个社区中，最后一次此类事件，据他们的纪年推算，大约发生在两千年前。当时一位姬妒火中烧，杀死了自己的丈夫；这一可憎的行径在男性中间激起如此恐怖，以致他们集体出走，把姬-埃们尽数撇下不管。史载那些由此陷入绝望的寡居姬-埃，趁那凶手熟睡之际（因而手无寸铁）扑向她，将她杀死；随后她们彼此缔结了一项庄严的盟约，永远废止她们那极端婚姻权力的行使，并将同一义务世世代代谆谆训诲于她们的女性后代。经此修好之举，派往那些出逃丈夫们那里的使团劝得多人归来；但归来者多为年长者。较年轻的那些，或因对妻子们疑虑太深而过于怯懦，或因对自己优点估价太高，拒绝了所有的劝请，留在别的社区，被当地另外的配偶搭上了钩，而与那些新配偶，他们或许过得也并不更好。但失去了如此大一部分青年男性，对姬-埃们不啻一记有益的警诫，使她们更坚定了自己立誓发愿的那份虔敬决心。的确，如今人们普遍认为，由于世代相传的长期废置，姬-埃们已丧失了她们一度拥有的对安-纳们的攻守优势——恰如地上那些低等动物，其原始构造中许多由自然安排用以护身的特性，在境况改变、不再需要时，便逐渐消退或失其效用。不过，无论哪位安若诱使一位姬来试一试他俩孰强孰弱，我都要为他捏一把汗。

===Original===
From the incident I have narrated, the Ana date certain alterations in the marriage customs, tending, perhaps, somewhat to the advantage of the male. They now bind themselves in wedlock only for three years; at the end of each third year either male or female can divorce the other and is free to marry again. At the end of ten years the *An* has the privilege of taking a second wife, allowing the first to retire if she so please. These regulations are for the most part a dead letter; divorces and polygamy are extremely rare, and the marriage state now seems singularly happy and serene among this astonishing people;﻿—the *Gy-ei*, notwithstanding their boastful superiority in physical strength and intellectual abilities, being much curbed into gentle manners by the dread of separation or of a second wife, and the *Ana* being very much the creatures of custom, and not, except under great aggravation, likely to exchange for hazardous novelties faces and manners to which they are reconciled by habit. But there is one privilege the *Gy-ei* carefully retain, and the desire for which perhaps forms the secret motive of most lady assertors of woman rights above ground. They claim the privilege, here usurped by men, of proclaiming their love and urging their suit; in other words, of being the wooing party rather than the wooed. Such a phenomenon as an old maid does not exist among the *Gy-ei*. Indeed it is very seldom that a *Gy* does not secure any *An* upon whom she sets her heart, if his affections be not strongly engaged elsewhere. However coy, reluctant, and prudish, the male she courts may prove at first, yet her perseverance, her ardour, her persuasive powers, her command over the mystic agencies of *vril*, are pretty sure to run down his neck into what we call “the fatal noose.” Their argument for the reversal of that relationship of the sexes which the blind tyranny of man has established on the surface of the earth, appears cogent, and is advanced with a frankness which might well be commended to impartial consideration. They say, that of the two the female is by nature of a more loving disposition than the male﻿—that love occupies a larger space in her thoughts, and is more essential to her happiness, and that therefore she ought to be the wooing party; that otherwise the male is a shy and dubitant creature﻿—that he has often a selfish predilection for the single state﻿—that he often pretends to misunderstand tender glances and delicate hints﻿—that, in short, he must be resolutely pursued and captured. They add, moreover, that unless the *Gy* can secure the *An* of her choice, and one whom she would not select out of the whole world becomes her mate, she is not only less happy than she otherwise would be, but she is not so good a being, that her qualities of heart are not sufficiently developed; whereas the *An* is a creature that less lastingly concentrates his affections on one object; that if he cannot get the *Gy* whom he prefers he easily reconciles himself to another *Gy*; and, finally, that at the worst, if he is loved and taken care of, it is less necessary to the welfare of his existence that he should love as well as be loved; he grows contented with his creature comforts, and the many occupations of thought which he creates for himself.

===Chinese===
自所述那次事件起，安-纳们便将婚俗上的若干更改纪元于此——这些更改，或许稍稍有利于男方。如今他们缔结婚姻只以三年为期；每满三年，无论男女，任一方都可离弃另一方，自由另嫁另娶。满十年后，安享有再娶一妻的特权，并许原配退位，倘若她愿意。这些规章大抵形同虚设；离婚与重婚极为罕见，婚姻生活在这个惊人的民族中间如今显得格外幸福安宁；——姬-埃们尽管自夸体力与智力上的优越，却因惧怕离异、惧怕再娶之妻，大受约束而变得温婉有礼；而安-纳们又大抵是习惯的造物，除非受到极度的刺激，断不肯拿业已习以为常的面容与举止，去交换吉凶难卜的新奇。但有一项特权，姬-埃们小心保留着；对这项特权的欲望，或许正是地上大多数鼓吹女权的女士们心底的秘密动机。她们要求的这项特权——在地上被男人们僭据着的——便是宣告爱情、展开追求；换言之，是做求婚的一方，而非被求的一方。老处女这种现象，在姬-埃中间并不存在。的确，只要那位安的心不曾另有强系，一位姬凡属心之所属，几乎无有不弄到手的。她所追求的男子起初无论怎样扭捏、怎样推拒、怎样假作端庄，她的坚忍、她的热烈、她的说服之才、她对弗里尔（vril）神秘力量的驾驭，终归十有八九会套上他的脖颈，收紧成我们所谓的“致命绞索”。她们主张颠倒两性间那种由男人的盲目暴政在地上确立的关系，其论据看似有力，提出时又那样坦率，很值得推荐给不偏不倚的考量。她们说，两性之中，雌性天生比雄性更多情——爱情在她的思绪中占据更大的位置，对她的幸福更为攸关，因此她理当做求婚的一方；否则，雄性便是一种羞怯而迟疑不决的造物——他常常出于私心偏爱独身——他常常装作不解温柔的顾盼与精微的暗示——总而言之，必须果断地追逐他、捕获他。她们还说，姬若不能得到她所选中的安，而与一个她从全世界里本不会挑选的人结为配偶，那她便不仅不如原本那样幸福，而且也成不了那样好的人，她心灵的品质便得不到充分的发展；反之，安这种造物，把感情长久专注于一个对象的本领较小；倘若得不到他属意的姬，他很容易便与另一位姬言归于好；而说到底，最不济的情形是，只要有人爱他、照料他，他是否也付出爱，对他生存的福祉便无甚必要；他会心满意足于生活的舒适，满足于他为自己创造出来的种种心智上的消遣。

===Original===
Whatever may be said as to this reasoning, the system works well for the male; for being thus sure that he is truly and ardently loved, and that the more coy and reluctant he shows himself, the more determination to secure him increases, he generally contrives to make his consent dependent on such conditions as he thinks the best calculated to insure, if not a blissful, at least a peaceful life. Each individual *An* has his own hobbies, his own ways, his own predilections, and, whatever they may be, he demands a promise of full and unrestrained concession to them. This, in the pursuit of her object, the *Gy* readily promises; and as the characteristic of this extraordinary people is an implicit veneration for truth, and her word once given is never broken even by the giddiest *Gy*, the conditions stipulated for are religiously observed. In fact, notwithstanding all their abstract rights and powers, the *Gy-ei* are the most amiable, conciliatory, and submissive wives I have ever seen even in the happiest households above ground. It is an aphorism among them, that “where a *Gy* loves it is her pleasure to obey.” It will be observed that in the relationship of the sexes I have spoken only of marriage, for such is the moral perfection to which this community has attained, that any illicit connection is as little possible amongst them as it would be to a couple of linnets during the time they agree to live in pairs.

===Chinese===
这套道理且随人评说，这套制度于男性却运转甚佳；因为既然确知自己被人真心而热烈地爱着，而且自己越是扭捏推拒，对方把他弄到手的决心便越发增长，他便总能设法使自己的应允附带种种条件——他认定那些条件最有助于保障一种纵非极乐、至少安宁的生活。每一位安都有自己的癖好、自己的做派、自己的偏嗜，而无论这些是什么，他都要求对方许诺对其全盘地、毫无拘束地让步。为了追求她的目标，姬总是慨然应允；而这个非凡民族的天性是对真理怀有不容置疑的敬畏，话一出口便永不反悔，连最轻佻的姬也不例外，故而约定的条件无不恪守不渝。事实上，尽管拥有那一切抽象的权利与权能，姬-埃们仍是我所见过的最和蔼、最肯迁就、最柔顺的妻子，即便地上最幸福的家庭也无可比拟。她们中间流传着一句格言：“姬凡有所爱，服从即是她的乐趣。”细心的读者当会注意到，论及两性关系时我只谈了婚姻；因为这个社区在道德上已达于如此完美的境地，任何苟且的结合在他们中间之不可能，恰如一对红雀在双双同居期间之不可能有越轨之举。
