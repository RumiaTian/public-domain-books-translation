## XXVII / 二十七

===Original===
One day, as I sat alone and brooding in my chamber, Taë flew in at the open window and alighted on the couch beside me. I was always pleased with the visits of a child, in whose society, if humbled, I was less eclipsed than in that of Ana who had completed their education and matured their understanding. And as I was permitted to wander forth with him for my companion, and as I longed to revisit the spot in which I had descended into the nether world, I hastened to ask him if he were at leisure for a stroll beyond the streets of the city. His countenance seemed to me graver than usual as he replied, “I came hither on purpose to invite you forth.”

We soon found ourselves in the street, and had not got far from the house when we encountered five or six young *Gy-ei*, who were returning from the fields with baskets full of flowers, and chanting a song in chorus as they walked. A young *Gy* sings more often than she talks. They stopped on seeing us, accosting Taë with familiar kindness, and me with the courteous gallantry which distinguishes the *Gy-ei* in their manner towards our weaker sex.

===Chinese===
有一天，我正独自在房中闷坐出神，塔伊（Taë）从敞开的窗口飞了进来，落在我身旁的长榻上。这孩子的来访总让我高兴；与他做伴，我虽然自惭形秽，却不像在那些已完成学业、心智臻于成熟的安-纳（Ana）中间那样黯然失色。我既蒙准许带着他作伴外出游逛，又一心想重访我当初降入下界的那处地方，便连忙问他可有闲工夫到城里的街衢之外去走走。他答话时，面容在我看来比平日凝重：“我就是特意来邀你外出的。”

不多时我们已到了街上，离家没走出多远，便遇见五六位年轻的*姬-埃*（Gy-ei），她们正从田野归来，提着满篮的花，一面走一面齐声唱着歌。年轻的*姬*（Gy）唱歌的时候多于说话的时候。她们见了我们便停步，以熟不拘礼的亲热向塔伊招呼，而对我，则报以*姬-埃*们对待我们这个较弱性别时独具的那种彬彬有礼的殷勤。

===Original===
And here I may observe that, though a virgin *Gy* is so frank in her courtship to the individual she favours, there is nothing that approaches to that general breadth and loudness of manner which those young ladies of the Anglo-Saxon race, to whom the distinguished epithet of “fast” is accorded, exhibit towards young gentlemen whom they do not profess to love. No: the bearing of the *Gy-ei* towards males in ordinary is very much that of high-bred men in the gallant societies of the upper world towards ladies whom they respect but do not woo; deferential, complimentary, exquisitely polished﻿—what we should call “chivalrous.”

Certainly I was a little put out by the number of civil things addressed to my amour propre, which were said to me by those courteous young *Gy-ei*. In the world I came from, a man would have thought himself aggrieved, treated with irony, “chaffed” (if so vulgar a slang word may be allowed on the authority of the popular novelists who use it so freely), when one fair *Gy* complimented me on the freshness of my complexion, another on the choice of colours in my dress, a third, with a sly smile, on the conquests I had made at Aph-Lin’s entertainment. But I knew already that all such language was what the French call banal, and did but express in the female mouth, below earth, that sort of desire to pass for amiable with the opposite sex which, above earth, arbitrary custom and hereditary transmission demonstrate by the mouth of the male. And just as a high-bred young lady, above earth, habituated to such compliments, feels that she cannot, without impropriety, return them, nor evince any great satisfaction at receiving them; so I, who had learned polite manners at the house of so wealthy and dignified a Minister of that nation, could but smile and try to look pretty in bashfully disclaiming the compliments showered upon me. While we were thus talking, Taë’s sister, it seems, had seen us from the upper rooms of the Royal Palace at the entrance of the town, and, precipitating herself on her wings, alighted in the midst of the group.

===Chinese===
在此我不妨指出：一位未嫁的*姬*向她属意的对象求爱时固然那般坦率，却绝无那种普遍的阔大与喧哗的做派——那是那些博得“轻狂”（fast）这一显赫评号的盎格鲁-撒克逊年轻小姐们，对她们并不自称爱慕的年轻绅士们所表现出来的。不；*姬-埃*们对待寻常男子的态度，大似上界风雅社会里出身高贵的男子对待他们敬重而不欲追求的女士：谦敬有加，称扬备至，温文之极——用我们的话说，便是“骑士风度”。

那些谦和有礼的年轻*姬-埃*对着我的自尊心说了那么多客气话，我自然有几分局促。在我来自的那个世界，倘若一位美丽的*姬*称许我气色新鲜，另一位称许我衣着配色选得精当，第三位面带狡黠的微笑，称许我在阿弗-林（Aph-Lin）家那次宴饮上斩获的种种芳心，换了男人，准会觉得自己受了怠慢、遭了揶揄、被人“打趣”了（这个俚俗的字眼，若蒙那些信笔滥用它的通俗小说家们授权，姑且容我一用）。可我早已明白，这一切言语无非是法国人所谓的老生常谈，不过是由地下的女性之口，道出那种想博异性好感、显得和蔼可亲的心愿罢了；而这心愿在地上，则凭专断的习俗与世代的传承，素来由男性之口表露出来。而且，正如上界一位出身高贵、听惯了这类恭维的小姐，会觉得回敬这些恭维有失体统、受之也不便显得太过惬意一样；我这个在那国度如此富有而尊贵的一位大臣宅邸里学得了礼貌规矩的人，也只得报以微笑，在羞怯地推辞如雨点般落在我身上的赞语时，竭力显出一副姣好的模样。我们正这样交谈时，塔伊的姐姐想来已从城口王宫楼上的房间里望见了我们，只见她振翅疾飞而下，落在人群正中。

===Original===
Singling me out, she said, though still with the inimitable deference of manner which I have called “chivalrous,” yet not without a certain abruptness of tone which, as addressed to the weaker sex, Sir Philip Sydney might have termed “rustic,” “Why do you never come to see us?”

While I was deliberating on the right answer to give to this unlooked-for question, Taë said quickly and sternly, “Sister, you forget﻿—the stranger is of my sex. It is not for persons of my sex, having due regard for reputation and modesty, to lower themselves by running after the society of yours.”

This speech was received with evident approval by the young *Gy-ei* in general; but Taë’s sister looked greatly abashed. Poor thing!﻿—and a **Princess** too!

===Chinese===
她单单挑中了我，开口说话；虽说仍带着我称之为“骑士风度”的那种无与伦比的谦敬，语气中却不无几分突兀——这样对较弱的性别说话，菲利普·锡德尼爵士（Sir Philip Sydney）怕要称之为“村野”了——“你怎么从来不来看看我们？”

我正斟酌着这意想不到的问题该如何作答，塔伊疾言厉色地说：“姐姐，你忘了——异乡人属于我这一性别。我这一性别的人，但凡顾惜名声与廉耻，就不该自贬身份去追逐你们那一性别的交往。”

这番话在年轻的*姬-埃*们中间博得了明白可见的赞许；塔伊的姐姐却显得大为窘惭。可怜的人儿！——何况还是一位**公主**呢！

===Original===
Just at this moment a shadow fell on the space between me and the group; and, turning round, I beheld the chief magistrate coming close upon us, with the silent and stately pace peculiar to the Vril-ya. At the sight of his countenance, the same terror which had seized me when I first beheld it returned. On that brow, in those eyes, there was that same indefinable something which marked the being of a race fatal to our own﻿—that strange expression of serene exemption from our common cares and passions, of conscious superior power, compassionate and inflexible as that of a judge who pronounces doom. I shivered, and, inclining low, pressed the arm of my child-friend, and drew him onward silently. The *Tur* placed himself before our path, regarded me for a moment without speaking, then turned his eye quietly on his daughter’s face, and, with a grave salutation to her and the other *Gy-ei*, went through the midst of the group﻿—still without a word.

===Chinese===
正当此时，一道影子投在我与那群人之间的空地上；我转过身，只见首席长官正向我们走近前来，步态沉静而庄严，正是弗里尔-雅人（the Vril-ya）所特有的。一见到他的面容，我初见他时攫住我的那种恐怖便又袭来。在那额头之上，在那双眼睛之中，有着同样一种难以界说的东西，标示出其人属于一个对我们自身这一族致命的种族——那种奇异的表情：安详地超然于我们共同的忧烦与激情之外，自觉拥有更高的力量，既悲悯又不容转圜，一如宣读厄运的法官。我不寒而栗，深深俯首，紧握住我这位小朋友的手臂，默默牵着他继续前行。*图尔*（Tur）拦到我们面前的路上，一言不发地注视了我片刻，随后平静地把目光移到他女儿的脸上，向她和其他*姬-埃*们郑重地致意，便从人群正中穿行而过——依旧不发一语。
