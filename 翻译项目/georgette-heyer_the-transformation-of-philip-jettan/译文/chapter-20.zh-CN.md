## XX / 二十

===Original===
Mademoiselle de Chaucheron Rings Down the Curtain

===Chinese===
德·肖什龙小姐落下帷幕

===Original===
Sir Maurice Jettan stood in the withdrawing-room of the Hotel Cleone and studied himself in the glass. He smiled a little and straightened his shoulders.

There came a swish of skirts in the passage without, and the door opened. In walked Cleone, a fair vision in a gown of pure white satin and lace.

Sir Maurice turned. He raised his quizzing-glass the better to inspect his daughter-in-law.

“Upon my soul, Cleone!” he ejaculated.

Cleone swept him a curtsey, laughing.

“Is it not ridiculous? Philip insisted. Wait till you see him!” She ran to the mirror. “Do you like the way my hair is dressed, father?”

“I am struck dumb by the whole effect!” answered Sir Maurice. “Yes, I like that white rose in your hair.”

===Chinese===
莫里斯·杰坦爵士站在克莱奥妮公馆的客厅里，对着镜子端详自己。他微微一笑，挺直了肩膀。

门外的走廊传来一阵裙裾的窸窣，房门开了。克莱奥妮走了进来，一身纯白绸缎配蕾丝的衣裙，宛若仙姿。

莫里斯爵士转过身。他举起长柄眼镜，好把这位儿媳妇端详得更清楚。

“我的老天，克莱奥妮！”他脱口而出。

克莱奥妮笑着朝他深深行了个屈膝礼。

“是不是荒唐透顶？菲利普非要这样。您还没瞧见他呢！”她跑到镜子前。“我的头发梳成这样，您喜欢吗，爸爸？”

“这通身的气派把我说得哑口无言！”莫里斯爵士答道。“喜欢，我喜欢你发间那朵白玫瑰。”

===Original===
“Oh, you must tell Philip that! He spent hours and hours trying to place it to his entire satisfaction! It has been terrible, *je t’assure*. Yes, I am beginning to acquire an accent, am I not? Philip nearly tore his beautiful wig in his anxiety!” She rearranged the roses at her breast. “At one time I expected him to summon François to his assistance. But he refrained, and here am I!”

Sir Maurice sat down.

“Has he been dressing you, my dear?”

“Has he—! For the past three hours, sir! He has driven my maid distracted.” She started to count on her fingers. “He spent half an hour superintending my hairdressing and another half an hour placing this rose and the pearls. Then half an hour went to my patches—this is when he nearly tore his wig!—he could not decide where to put them. The arrangement of my gown occupied quite an hour in all. And then he was much put out over my jewels.” She held up her fingers. “I vow they are red and sore, sir! I have had rings pushed on them, and dragged off them, until I was nigh screaming with impatience! But now I am dressed—and I have been told on pain of Philip’s direst wrath to *n’y toucher pas*!” She sat down on the couch beside Sir Maurice and slipped her hand in his. “Is he not absurd? And oh, I am prodigious nervous!”

===Chinese===
“哦，这话您务必说给菲利普听！为了把花别得让他自己十足满意，他折腾了一个钟头又一个钟头！真是遭罪，*je t’assure*（我向你保证）。是啊，我这口音渐渐有点样子了吧？菲利普急得差点把他那顶漂亮的假发扯坏！”她重新理了理胸前的玫瑰。“有一阵我都当他要把弗朗索瓦叫来救场了。好歹他忍住了，于是我成了现在这样！”

莫里斯爵士坐下了。

“他一直在替你打扮，亲爱的？”

“何止是——！足足三个钟头了，爸爸！他把我的女仆都折腾疯了。”她掰起手指头数。“他花了半个钟头督阵我做头发，又花半个钟头安放这朵玫瑰和那些珍珠。然后半个钟头全耗在我的花斑贴上——就是这一遭，他差点扯坏假发！——他拿不定主意该贴在哪儿。我这条裙子的穿缀前前后后占了一个钟头。再后来，我的珠宝又让他大大地烦恼了一场。”她举起手指。“我发誓它们又红又疼，爸爸！戒指一个劲儿往上套、往下褪，褪得我差点不耐烦得尖叫！可如今我总算是穿戴齐整了——而且奉了严令，胆敢*n’y toucher pas*（再碰一下），菲利普最可怕的雷霆之怒就会降临！”她在莫里斯爵士身边的躺椅上坐下，把手滑进他的手里。“他是不是荒唐透了？哦，还有，我紧张得要命！”

===Original===
“Why, my dear? What should make you so?”

“You see, it is my first appearance in Paris—it is to be my first ball—and I am so afraid I shall not understand what is said to me, or—or something mortifying!”

“Not understand? Nonsense, Clo! Why, you have talked hardly any English since you have been married.”

“Yes, but I am not at all fluent. Philip says everyone will be most amiable, but—oh, dear!”

At that moment François darted into the room, a harassed frown on his face.

“Ah, *pardon, madame! Pardon, m’sieu’! Je cherche la tabatière de m’sieu’ Philippe!*”

“*Laquelle?*” asked Cleone. Sir Maurice was amused by her serious air. “The one with the pearls?”

“*Mais oui, madame.* It is this fool of a Jacques who has lost it, *sans doute*! Ah, *la voilà*!” He seized the errant box and skipped out again. Cleone breathed a sigh of relief.

“How terrible if it had been really lost!” she said.

Sir Maurice laughed.

“Would it have been so great a catastrophe?”

“But of course! It matches his dress, you understand.”

“I see.” Sir Maurice smothered another laugh. “My dear, do you know that it is three years since last I was in this city of cities?”

===Chinese===
“这又是为什么，亲爱的？什么事把你紧张成这样？”

“您想，这是我头一回在巴黎露面——是我头一场舞会——我好怕听不懂人家对我说的话，或者——或者出什么丢人的岔子！”

“听不懂？胡说，克洛！嗐，你成婚以来简直就没怎么说过英语。”

“话虽如此，可我说得一点儿不流利。菲利普说人人都会和和气气，可是——哦，天哪！”

正在这时，弗朗索瓦一头冲进屋来，眉头拧成一团。

“*Ah, pardon, madame! Pardon, m’sieu’! Je cherche la tabatière de m’sieu’ Philippe!*（啊，对不起，夫人！对不起，先生！我在找菲利普先生的鼻烟盒！）”

“*Laquelle?*（哪一只？）”克莱奥妮问，她那一本正经的神气把莫里斯爵士逗乐了。“镶珍珠的那只？”

“*Mais oui, madame.*（正是，夫人。）准是雅克那个蠢货给弄丢了，*sans doute*（十有八九）！啊，*la voilà*（在这儿呢）！”他一把抓住那只乱跑的盒子，又蹦跳着出去了。克莱奥妮松了口气。

“要是真丢了，那可怎么得了！”她说。

莫里斯爵士笑了。

“有那么了不得吗？”

“那当然！它同他的衣裳是一套，您是知道的。”

“原来如此。”莫里斯爵士又忍住一笑。“亲爱的，你可知道，我上回到这座万城之城，已是三年前的事了？”

===Original===
“Is it? Don’t you think it is a wonderful place? Philip took me for a walk yesterday, and I was enchanted! And this house—I know I shall never bear to leave it! Philip says that the Hotel Cleone will be the most fashionable one in Paris! I was so surprised when he brought me here! I had no idea that there was a house waiting for me. He and François got all ready the week before our marriage! I’ve never been so happy in my life! And tonight I am to see Philip in what he calls his milieu. He tells me he was never at home in London.”

“Philip in his milieu. Paris.” Sir Maurice smiled down at her. “When I think of what Philip was not quite a year ago. …”

“It seems impossible, doesn’t it? But oh, I am glad now that I sent him away. He is quite, quite perfect!”

“H’m!” said Sir Maurice.

Cleone laughed at him.

“You pretend! I know how proud you are!”

“Minx! I confess I am curious to see Philip in his Parisian Society. No one knows that he is here?”

“Not a soul. He insisted on guarding the secret until he could make a really dramatic appearance at the Duchesse de Sauverin’s ball tonight. He is mad, you know, quite mad! Oh, here he is!”

===Chinese===
“是吗？您不觉得这地方妙极了吗？昨天菲利普带我出去散步，我都看迷了！还有这宅子——我知道我这辈子都舍不得离开它！菲利普说，克莱奥妮公馆将是全巴黎最时髦的宅子！他把我带到这里时，我惊得不得了！我压根不知道有一座宅子等着我。他和弗朗索瓦在我们成婚前那个星期就全收拾妥当了！我这辈子从没这么快活过！今晚我还要见识见识菲利普所谓的他的用武之地。他告诉我，他在伦敦从来就不得其所。”

“菲利普的用武之地。巴黎。”莫里斯爵士含笑俯视着她。“想想不到一年前菲利普还是个什么样子。……”

“简直难以置信，是不是？可是哦，如今我倒庆幸当初把他打发走了。他现在完完全全、十全十美！”

“哼哼。”莫里斯爵士不置可否。

克莱奥妮笑他。

“您就装吧！我知道您骄傲着呢！”

“小狐狸精！我承认，我很好奇想看看菲利普在他那巴黎社交圈里的样子。谁都不知道他到了这儿？”

“一个都没有。他非要瞒到今晚，好在德·索弗林公爵夫人的舞会上风风光光、惊动四座地露面不可。他疯着呢，您是知道的，彻头彻尾地疯！哦，他来了！”

===Original===
Philip came into the room with a rustle of stiff silks. Sir Maurice started at him.

“Good God, Philip, what audacity!”

From head to foot his son was clad in white. The only splash of colour was the red heels of his shoes; his only jewels were pearls and diamonds; on the lapel of his coat he wore a single white rose.

“Isn’t it ridiculous?” said Cleone. “But doesn’t he look beautiful?”

“Stand up, child, and let me see you side by side. … Yes. What audacity! Had I known, I would have attired myself in black—the old man at the ball.”

“’Twould have made an excellent foil,” agreed Philip. “But no matter. Cleone, you have rearranged your roses!”

Cleone backed, warding him off.

“I cry your pardon, sir! Oh no, let me be!”

Philip came to her, and with deft fingers pulled the flowers into position.

“One of them must kiss your skin, so! To show that it is no whiter than the skin. *Voilà, c’est bien!*”

===Chinese===
菲利普走进屋来，硬绸衣窸窣作响。莫里斯爵士看得一怔。

“老天爷，菲利普，好大的胆子！”

他的儿子从头到脚一身白。全身上下唯一的亮色是鞋上的红后跟；仅有的珠宝是珍珠和钻石；外衣翻领上，他别着一朵白玫瑰。

“是不是荒唐透顶？”克莱奥妮说。“可他这样子不俊极了吗？”

“站起来，孩子，让我看看你们俩并排的样子。……嗯。好大的胆子！早知道如此，我就该穿一身黑——舞会上的老头儿。”

“那可是绝好的陪衬。”菲利普附和。“不过无妨。克莱奥妮，你把玫瑰又动过了！”

克莱奥妮直往后退，拦着他。

“求您恕罪，先生！哦不行，别碰我！”

菲利普走到她跟前，用灵巧的手指把花朵重新摆正。

“其中一朵得吻着你的肌肤，这样！好显出花不比肌肤更白。*Voilà, c’est bien!*（看，这就好了！）”

===Original===
“Who is likely to be at the ball tonight, Philip?” asked his father.

“*Tout le monde.* One always goes to Madame de Sauverin’s balls. It is de rigueur.”

“We shall be late!” warned Cleone. “Oh, we are late now!”

“That is also de rigueur,” said Philip.

“Sir Maurice, *M’sieu’, et Madame Jettan*!” announced the lackey.

There was a sudden hush. All eyes turned to the latecomers. In the doorway stood a tall gentleman, at his side two dazzling visions in white.

Madame de Sauverin stared for a moment in wonderment. Then she hurried forward, hands outstretched.

“Philippe!”

“Philippe! *Le petit* Philippe!” A score of voices took up the cry. Nearly everyone there surged forward.

Philip kissed Madame’s hand.

“*Chère madame!* I may present my wife? My father you know.”

Cleone curtseyed low.

“Your—wife!” Madame took Cleone’s hands. “*Voyons, voyons, notre petit Philippe s’est *éspousé*! Et Maurice!*”

===Chinese===
“今晚舞会上估计都会有哪些人，菲利普？”他父亲问。

“*Tout le monde*（全巴黎的人）。德·索弗林夫人的舞会人人必到。这是规矩。”

“我们要迟到了！”克莱奥妮警告。“哦，现在已经迟了！”

“迟到也是规矩的一部分。”菲利普说。

“莫里斯爵士到——杰坦先生及夫人到！”仆人朗声通报。

全场霎时一静。所有的目光都转向迟来的客人。门口立着一位高挑的绅士，他身侧是两位白衣胜雪、光彩夺目的身影。

德·索弗林夫人怔怔瞧了片刻。随即她抢步上前，双手张开。

“菲利普！”

“菲利普！*小*菲利普！”二十来个声音齐声喊了起来。在场的人几乎全涌了上来。

菲利普吻了夫人的手。

“*Chère madame!*（亲爱的夫人！）容我引见我的妻子？家父您是认得的。”

克莱奥妮深深行了个屈膝礼。

“您的——妻子！”夫人握住克莱奥妮的双手。“*Voyons, voyons, notre petit Philippe s’est éspousé! Et Maurice!*（哎呀呀，咱们的小菲利普娶亲啦！还有莫里斯哪！）”

===Original===
Philip and Cleone were at the centre of a welcoming throng. Cleone’s hand was kissed a dozen times. Delighted questions were shot at Philip.

Saint-Dantin grasped his hand.

“*Mon cher petit!* You have returned at last? *Et madame!*” He bowed to the blushing Cleone. “There is no need to ask who is, madame.” He smiled at her. “It is evident that her name is Cleone!”

De Vangrisse pressed forward.

“The mysterious Cleone! *Madame, votre serviteur!* We have all longed to see the lady who so consistently held Philip’s heart!”

“Philippe, how long have you been in Paris?” demanded De Chatelin. “You are going to remain? *Ah bon!*”

“Philippe, have you an ode for the occasion?” asked another laughing voice.

Clothilde de Chaucheron pushed through the ring.

“*Le petit Philippe au cœur perdu!*” she cried.

Philip disengaged himself from the clutches of Saint-Dantin and took his wife’s hand.

“*Mademoiselle de Chaucheron, chérie*,” he said, and bowed.

Clothilde gazed at Cleone for a moment. Then she swept a deep curtsey.

“*Je me trompe*,” she said, smiling. “*Le petit Philippe au cœur trouvé.*”

菲利普和克莱奥妮被欢迎的人群围在当中。克莱奥妮的手被吻了十几次。欢天喜地的问题接连朝菲利普抛来。

圣丹坦一把握住他的手。

“*Mon cher petit!*（我亲爱的小家伙！）您可算回来了？*Et madame!*（这位是夫人吧！）”他朝红着脸的克莱奥妮一躬。“夫人是谁，毋庸动问。”他朝她微笑。“一望便知，她的名字叫克莱奥妮！”

德·旺格里塞挤上前来。

“神秘的克莱奥妮！*Madame, votre serviteur!*（夫人，为您效劳！）我们大伙儿早就盼着见一见这位始终牢牢占着菲利普的心的女士了！”

“菲利普，为这个日子你可有颂诗一首？”另一个带笑的声音问。

克洛蒂尔德·德·肖什龙从人圈里挤了进来。

“*Le petit Philippe au cœur perdu!*（小菲利普，把心丢了！）”她扬声喊道。

菲利普从圣丹坦的拉扯中脱出身来，握住妻子的手。

“*Mademoiselle de Chaucheron, chérie*（亲爱的德·肖什龙小姐）。”他说着，躬身一礼。

克洛蒂尔德盯着克莱奥妮端详了片刻。随即她深深行了个屈膝礼。

“*Je me trompe*（我看错了）。”她微笑道。“*Le petit Philippe au cœur trouvé.*（小菲利普，把心找到了。）”
