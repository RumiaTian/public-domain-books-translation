### VI / VI

===Original===
It is sweet to live. Love atones for every loss; in its joys all else is forgotten. Ineffable music of the heart, thy divine melody fills the soul with an ecstasy of infinite happiness! What illustrious historians have celebrated the heroes of the world’s progress, the glories of war, the conquests of mind and of spirit! Yet after so many centuries of labor and struggle, there remained only two palpitating hearts, the kisses of two lovers. All had perished except love; and love, the supreme sentiment, endured, shining like an inextinguishable beacon over the immense ocean of the vanished ages.

Death! They did not dream of it. Did they not suffice for each other? What if the cold froze their very marrow? Did they not possess in their hearts a warmth which defied the cold of nature? Did not the sun still shine gloriously, and was not the final doom of the world yet far distant? Omegar bent every energy to the maintenance of the marvellous system which had been devised for the automatic extraction by chemical processes of the nutritive principles of the air, water and plants, and in this he seemed to be successful. So in other days, after the fall of the Roman empire, the barbarians had been seen to utilize during centuries the aqueducts, baths and thermal springs, all the creations of the civilization of the Caesars, and to draw from a vanished industry the sources of their own strength.

===Chinese===
活着是甜美的。爱补偿了一切损失；在爱的欢乐里，其余的一切都被忘却。心之无法言说的音乐啊，你神圣的旋律以无尽幸福的狂喜充满灵魂！多少显赫的史家歌颂过世界进步的英雄、战争的荣光、心智与精神的征服！然而，在这么多世纪的劳作与斗争之后，剩下的只有两颗跳动的心，一对恋人的吻。一切都消亡了，唯独爱还在；爱，这至高的情感，依然长存，像一座不灭的灯塔，闪耀在消逝诸纪元的浩瀚海洋之上。

死亡！他们连想都不去想它。他们彼此拥有，难道还不够吗？哪怕严寒冻彻骨髓又算得了什么？他们心中不是怀着一股蔑视天然严寒的暖意吗？太阳不是依然辉煌地照耀，世界最终的劫数不是还很遥远吗？欧米伽尔把全部精力用来维护那套奇妙的系统——它以化学方法自动地从空气、水和植物中提取营养要素——在这上面他似乎还算成功。正如罗马帝国覆亡之后的其他时代，人们曾看见蛮族在几个世纪里享用高架引水渠、浴场和温泉，享用恺撒们文明的一切创造，从一门消失的工业中汲取自身力量的源泉。

===Original===
But one day, wonderful as it was, this system gave out. The subterranean waters themselves ceased to flow. The soil was frozen to a great depth. The rays of the sun still warmed the air within the glass-covered dwellings, but no plant could live longer; the supply of water was exhausted.

The combined efforts of science and industry were impotent to give to the atmosphere the nutritive qualities possessed by those of other worlds, and the human organism constantly clamored for the regenerating principles which, as we have seen, had been derived from the air, water and plants. These sources were now exhausted.

===Chinese===
然而有一天，这系统再奇妙，也耗竭了。连地下水也停止了流动。土壤冻结到了极深处。太阳的光线还能温暖玻璃穹顶居所内的空气，但再也没有植物能活下去；水的供给已经枯竭。

科学与工业合力，也无法赋予这大气以别的世界的大气所具有的营养特性，而人的机体不断渴求那些再生的要素——如我们所见，它们一向取自空气、水和植物。这些源泉如今都枯竭了。

===Original===
This last human pair struggled against these insurmountable obstacles, and recognized the uselessness of farther contest, yet they were not resigned to death. Before knowing each other they had awaited it fearlessly. Now each wished to defend the other, the beloved one, against pitiless destiny. The very idea of seeing Omegar lying inanimate beside her, filled Eva with such anguish that she could not bear the thought. And he, too, vainly longed to carry away his well beloved from a world doomed to decay, to fly with her to that brilliant Jupiter which awaited them, and not to abandon to the Earth the body he adored.

He thought that, perhaps, there still existed, somewhere upon the Earth, a spot which had retained a little of that life-giving water without which existence was impossible; and, although already they were both almost without strength, he formed the supreme resolution of setting out to seek for it. The electric aeronef was still in working order. Forsaking the city which was now only a tomb, the two last survivors of a vanished humanity abandoned these inhospitable regions and set out to seek some unknown oasis.

===Chinese===
这最后的一对人与这些无法逾越的障碍搏斗，认识到再斗下去也是徒劳，但他们不肯向死亡低头。相识之前，他们曾无畏地等待死亡。如今，每个人都想保卫对方、保卫所爱的人，抵御无情的命运。一想到欧米伽尔没有生气地躺在自己身旁，伊娃便充满无法承受的痛楚。而他也徒然渴望把心爱的人从一个注定衰朽的世界带走，同她一起飞向那颗等待着他们的辉煌的木星，不肯让他所崇拜的躯体留给地球。

他想，地球上或许还有什么地方，存留着一点那赋予生命的水——没有它，生存便不可能——于是，尽管两人都几乎没有了力气，他下定了最后的决心：出发去找。电飞行器还能运转。这两位消失了的人类最后的幸存者，抛下那座如今只是坟墓的城市，离开这些不宜居住的地区，启程去寻找某处未知的绿洲。

===Original===
The ancient kingdoms of the world passed under their feet. They saw the remains of great cities, made illustrious by the splendors of civilization, lying in ruins along the equator. The silence of death covered them all. Omegar recognized the ancient city which he had recently left, but he knew that there, also the supreme source of life was lacking, and they did not stop. They traversed thus, in their solitary airship, the regions which had witnessed the last stages of the life of humanity; but death, and silence, and the frozen desert was everywhere. No more fields, no more vegetation; the watercourses were visible as on a map, and it was evident that along their banks life had been prolonged; but they were now dried up forever. And when, at times, some motionless lake was distinguished in the lower level, it was like a lake of stone; for even at the equator the sun was powerless to melt the eternal ice. A kind of bear, with long fur, was still to be seen wandering over the frozen earth, seeking in the crevices of the rocks its scanty vegetable food. From time to time, also, they descried a kind of penguin and sea-cows walking upon the ice, and large, gray polar birds in awkward flight, or alighting mournfully.

===Chinese===
世界古昔的王国从他们脚下掠过。他们看见大城市的残迹——曾因文明的辉煌而显赫——沿着赤道躺卧在废墟中。死亡的寂静笼罩着这一切。欧米伽尔认出了他不久前才离开的那座古城，但他知道那里也同样缺少生命的至高源泉，他们没有停留。他们就这样乘着孤独的飞艇，飞越了目睹人类生命最后阶段的那些地区；然而死亡、沉寂和冰封的荒漠无处不在。再没有田畴，再没有植被；水道像地图上一样清晰可辨，显然沿河两岸生命曾延续得更久；但如今它们已永远干涸。时而，在低洼处还能辨认出一片凝然不动的湖，那湖像一片石头；因为即使在赤道，太阳也无力融化那永恒的冰。还能看见一种长毛的熊在冻土上游荡，在岩石的缝隙里寻找它可怜的植物食粮。他们还不时瞥见一种企鹅和海牛在冰上行走，还有灰色的大极地鸟笨拙地飞着，或凄然栖落。

===Original===
Nowhere was the sought-for oasis found. The Earth was indeed dead.

Night came. Not a cloud obscured the sky. A warmer current from the south had carried them over what was formerly Africa, now a frozen waste. The mechanism of the aeronef had ceased to work. Exhausted by cold rather than by hunger, they threw themselves upon the bearskins in the bottom of the car.

Perceiving a ruin, they alighted. It was an immense quadrangular base, revealing traces of an enormous stone stairway. It was still possible to recognize one of the ancient Egyptian pyramids which, in the middle of the desert, survived the civilization which it represented. With all Egypt, Nubia and Abyssinia, it had sunk below the level of the sea, and had afterwards emerged into the light and been restored in the heart of a new capital by a new civilization, more brilliant than that of Thebes and of Memphis, and finally had been again abandoned to the desert. It was the only remaining monument of the earlier life of humanity, and owed its stability to its geometric form.

===Chinese===
哪里也找不到要找的绿洲。地球确乎死了。

夜来了。天空没有一丝云。一股来自南方的暖气流把他们带到了昔日的非洲上空——如今是一片冰封的荒原。飞行器的机械停止了运转。他们与其说是饿，不如说是冻得筋疲力尽，扑倒在吊舱底部的熊皮上。

望见一处废墟，他们落了下来。那是一个巨大的四方形基座，显出一条庞大石阶的痕迹。还能认出这是一座古埃及金字塔；它在大漠中央，比它所代表的文明活得更久。它同整个埃及、努比亚和阿比西尼亚一起，曾沉入海平面之下，后来又重新露出天日，在一座新都城的心脏被一个比底比斯和孟菲斯更辉煌的新文明修复，最终又被弃还给沙漠。它是人类早期生活仅存的纪念物，其稳固全靠它的几何形态。

===Original===
“Let us rest here,” said Eva, “since we are doomed to die. Who, indeed, has escaped death? Let me die in peace in your arms.”

They sought a corner of the ruin and sat down beside each other, face to face with the silent desert. The young girl cowered upon the ground, pressing her husband in her arms, still striving with all her might against the penetrating cold. He drew her to his heart, and warmed her with his kisses.

“I love you, and I am dying,” she said. “But, no, we will not die. See that star, which calls us!”

===Chinese===
「我们就在这里歇息吧，」伊娃说，「既然我们注定要死。谁又能逃过死亡呢？让我在你的怀抱里安然死去。」

他们在废墟中寻了个角落，并肩坐下，面对沉默的沙漠。少女蜷缩在地上，把丈夫搂在怀中，仍在全力抵御那刺骨的严寒。他把她搂上心头，用吻温暖她。

「我爱你，我快死了，」她说。「可是不，我们不会死。看那颗星，它在召唤我们！」

===Original===
At the same moment they heard behind them a slight noise, issuing from the ancient tomb of Cheops, a noise like that the wind makes in the leaves. Shuddering, they turned, together, in the direction whence the sound came. A white shadow, which seemed to be self-luminous, for the night was already dark and there was no moon, glided rather than walked toward them, and stopped before their astonished eyes.

“Fear nothing,” it said. “I come to seek you. No, you shall not die. No one has ever died. Time flows into eternity; eternity remains.

“I was Cheops, King of Egypt, and I reigned over this country in the early days of the world. As a slave, I have since expiated my crimes in many existences, and when at length my soul deserved immortality I lived upon Neptune, Ganymede, Rhea, Titan, Saturn, Mars, and other worlds as yet unknown to you. Jupiter is now my home. In the days of humanity’s greatness, Jupiter was not habitable for intelligent beings. It was passing through the necessary stages of preparation. Now this immense world is the heir to all human achievement. Worlds succeed each other in time as in space. All is eternal, and merges into the divine. Confide in me, and follow me.”

===Chinese===
就在同一刻，他们听见身后传来一阵轻微的响动，出自基奥普斯（Cheops）的古墓，一种像风拂树叶的声响。他们战栗着，一同朝声音来处转过身去。一个白色的影子，仿佛自行发光——夜已黑，又没有月亮——与其说是走来，不如说是飘到他们跟前，停在他们惊讶的目光之前。

「不要怕，」它说。「我来寻找你们。不，你们不会死。从来没有人死过。时间流入永恒；永恒长存。

「我曾是在埃及王基奥普斯，在世界之初统治过这个国度。此后我做过奴隶，在多次轮回中赎了我的罪；当我的灵魂终于配得上不朽时，我先后生活在海王星、木卫三、土卫五、土卫六、土星、火星以及其他你们还不知道的世界上。如今木星是我的家园。在人类伟大的时代，木星还不宜于智慧生命居住。它正经历必不可少的准备阶段。如今这个浩大的世界是人类一切成就的继承者。诸世界在时间中相续，一如在空间中相续。一切皆永恒，并融入神性。信赖我，跟我来。」

===Original===
And as the old Pharaoh was still speaking, they felt a delicious fluid penetrate their souls, as sometimes the ear is filled with an exquisite melody. A sense of calm and transcendent happiness flowed in their veins. Never, in any dream, in any ecstasy, had they ever experienced such joy.

Eva pressed Omegar in her arms. “I love you,” she repeated. Her voice was only a breath. He touched his lips to her already cold mouth, and heard them murmur: “How I could have loved!”

===Chinese===
老法老还在说话时，他们感到一股甜美的流渗入灵魂，就像耳朵有时被一段精妙的旋律充满。一种平静而超凡的幸福感流进他们的血脉。无论哪个梦、哪次狂喜，他们都不曾体验过这样的欢乐。

伊娃把欧米伽尔紧搂在怀中。「我爱你，」她重复着。她的声音只剩一口气。他把唇贴上她已然冰凉的嘴，听见唇间低语：「我本可以多么爱你！」

===Original===
Jupiter was shining majestically above them, and in the glorious light of his rays their sight grew dim and their eyes gently closed.

The spectre rose into space and vanished. And one to whom it is given to see, not with the bodily eyes, which perceive only material vibrations, but with the eyes of the soul, which perceive psychical vibrations, might have seen two small flames shining side by side, united by a common attraction, and rising, together with the phantom, into the heavens.

===Chinese===
木星在他们头顶庄严地照耀；在它光辉的射线里，他们的视力渐渐模糊，双眼轻轻地阖上了。

幽灵升入太空，消失了。而凡是有幸不用肉眼——那只能感知物质振动——而用灵魂之眼——那能感知心灵振动——去看的人，本可以看见两簇小小的火焰并肩闪耀，被共同的引力联结着，随那幽灵一起升入天宇。
