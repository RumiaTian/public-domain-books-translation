## Epilogue / 尾声

===Original===
> “And the angel lifted up his hand to heaven and sware by Him that liveth forever and ever that there should be time no longer.”
> — Rev. 10:6

===Chinese===
> 「天使向天举起手来，指着那位永永远远活着的起誓说：不再有时日了。」
> ——《启示录》10:6

### Φ / Φ

===Original===
The Earth was dead. The other planets also had died one after the other. The Sun was extinguished. But the stars still shone; there were still suns and worlds.

In the measureless duration of eternity, time, an essentially relative conception, is determined by each world, and even in each world this conception is dependent upon the consciousness of the individual. Each world measures its own duration. The year of the Earth is not that of Neptune. The latter is 164 times the former, and yet is not longer relatively to the absolute. There is no common measure between time and eternity. In empty space there is no time, no years, no centuries; only the possibility of a measurement of time which becomes real the moment a revolving world appears. Without some periodic motion no conception whatever of time is possible.

===Chinese===
地球死了。其他行星也相继死去。太阳熄灭了。但群星仍在闪耀；宇宙中仍有太阳和世界。

在永恒那不可度量的绵延中，时间——一个本质上是相对的概念——由每个世界各自确定；甚至在同一个世界里，这一概念也取决于个体的意识。每个世界量度自己的绵延。地球的一年不同于海王星的一年。后者是前者的164倍，但相对于绝对而言并不更长。时间与永恒之间没有公度。在空虚的空间里没有时间，没有年，没有世纪；只有量度时间的可能性——一旦出现一个旋转的世界，这可能性便成为现实。没有某种周期运动，就不可能有任何时间概念。

===Original===
The Earth no longer existed, nor her celestial companion, the little isle of Mars, nor the beautiful sphere of Venus, nor the colossal world of Jupiter, nor the strange universe of Saturn, which had lost its rings, nor the slow-moving Uranus and Neptune—not even the glorious Sun, in whose fecundating heat these mansions of the heavens had basked for so many centuries. The Sun was a dark ball, the planets also; and still this invisible system sped on in the glacial cold of starry space. So far as life is concerned, all these worlds were dead, did not exist. They survived their past history like the ruins of the dead cities of Assyria which the archaeologist uncovers in the desert, moving on their way in darkness through the invisible and the unknown.

===Chinese===
地球不复存在了，连同它的天侣们：小小的火星之岛、美丽的金星之球、木星那巨大的世界、失去了光环的土星的奇境、迟缓的天王星与海王星——连那颗辉煌的太阳也不在了，天宇中的这些邸宅，曾有多少个世纪沐浴在他赋育生命的热量之中。太阳成了一个黑球，行星也一样；而这看不见的体系，依然在繁星空间的严寒中疾驰。就生命而言，所有这些世界都已死去，都已不存在。它们像考古学家在大漠中发掘出的亚述死城的废墟一样，守着自己的往史，在黑暗中穿行于不可见与未知之中。

===Original===
No genius, no magician could recall the vanished past, when the Earth floated bathed in light, with its broad green fields waking to the morning sun, its rivers winding like long serpents through the verdant meadows, its woods alive with the songs of birds, its forests filled with deep and mysterious shadows, its seas heaving with the tides or roaring in the tempest, its mountain slopes furrowed with rushing streams and cascades, its gardens enameled with flowers, its nests of birds and cradles of children, and its toiling population, whose activity had transformed it and who lived so joyously a life perpetuated by the delights of an endless love. All this happiness seemed eternal. What has become of those mornings and evenings, of those flowers and those lovers, of that light and perfume, of those harmonies and joys, of those beauties and dreams? All is dead, has disappeared in the darkness of night.

===Chinese===
任何天才、任何魔法师都无法召回那消逝的过去——那时地球漂浮着，沐浴在光明之中，宽阔的绿野迎着朝阳苏醒，河流像长蛇蜿蜒穿过青翠的草场，树林里满是鸟儿的歌唱，森林里充满深邃神秘的暗影，海洋随潮涌动或在风暴中咆哮，山坡被奔流的溪涧和瀑布犁出沟壑，花园里缀满如珐琅的花朵，有鸟儿的巢、婴孩的摇篮，还有辛劳的人民——他们的活动改变了地球，他们活得那样欢欣，一种由无尽之爱的欢愉延续着的生命。这一切幸福看似永恒。那些清晨与黄昏、那些花朵与恋人、那光与芳馨、那些和声与欢乐、那些美与梦，如今何在？一切都死了，都消失在夜的黑暗里。

===Original===
The world dead, all the planets dead, the Sun extinguished. The solar system annihilated, time itself suspended.

Time lapses into eternity. But eternity remains, and time is born again.

Before the existence of the Earth, throughout an eternity, suns and worlds existed, peopled with beings like ourselves. Millions of years before the Earth was, they were. The past of the universe has been as brilliant as the present, the future will be as the past, the present is of no importance.

===Chinese===
世界死了，所有的行星死了，太阳熄灭了。太阳系湮灭了，时间本身也悬搁了。

时间流逝入永恒。但永恒长存，时间再度诞生。

在地球存在之前，在整个永恒之中，就存在着太阳和世界，栖息着像我们一样的生命。在地球诞生的几百万年前，它们已在。宇宙的过去与现在一样辉煌，未来将与过去一样，而现在无关紧要。

===Original===
In examining the past history of the Earth, we might go back to a time when our planet shone in space, a veritable sun, appearing as Jupiter and Saturn do now, shrouded in a dense atmosphere charged with warm vapors; and we might follow all its transformations down to the period of man. We have seen that when its heat was entirely dissipated, its waters absorbed, the aqueous vapor of its atmosphere gone, and this atmosphere itself more or less absorbed, our planet must have presented the appearance of those great lunar deserts seen through the telescope (with certain differences due to the action of causes peculiar to the Earth), with its final geographical configurations, its dried-up shores and watercourses, a planetary corpse, a dead and frozen world. It still bears, however, within its bosom an unexpended energy—that of its motion of translation about the Sun, an energy which, transformed into heat by the sudden destruction of its motion, would suffice to melt it and to reduce it, in part, to a state of vapor, thus inaugurating a new epoch; but for an instant only, for, if this motion of translation were destroyed, the Earth would fall into the Sun and its independent existence would come to an end. If suddenly arrested it would move in a straight line toward the Sun, with an increasing velocity, and reach the Sun in sixty-five days; were its motion gradually arrested, it would move in a spiral, to be swallowed up, at last, in the central luminary.

The entire history of terrestrial life is before our eyes. It has its commencement and its end; and its duration, however many the centuries which compose it, is preceded and followed by eternity—is, indeed, but a single instant lost in eternity.

For a long time after the Earth had ceased to be the abode of life, the colossal worlds of Jupiter and Saturn, passing more slowly from their solar to their planetary stage, reigned in their turn among the planets, with the splendor of a vitality incomparably superior to that of our Earth. But they, also, waxed old and descended into the night of the tomb.

===Chinese===
考察地球的既往，我们可以回到这样一段时间：我们的行星在太空中闪耀，是一颗真正的太阳，外观一如现在的木星和土星，裹着饱含暖雾的稠密大气；我们可以追随它的种种变迁，直到人类的时代。我们已经看到，当它的热完全消散、水被吸收、大气中的水汽散尽、大气本身也被或多或少吸收之后，我们的行星想必呈现出的模样，正是望远镜里那些广袤的月球荒漠的样子（由于地球特有因素的作用，略有差异）：带着它最后的地理轮廓、干涸的海岸与水道，一具行星的尸体，一个死寂冰封的世界。然而它的怀抱里还存着一份未耗尽的能量——绕太阳公转的能量；这份能量若因运动的骤然毁灭而转化为热，足以把它熔化，并使它部分化为蒸气，从而开启一个新纪元；但这只能维持一瞬，因为公转一旦被摧毁，地球便会坠入太阳，它的独立存在便告终结。倘若突然被遏止，它将沿直线向太阳坠落，速度越来越快，六十五天抵达太阳；倘若它的运动被逐渐遏止，它将沿螺旋线运行，最终被中央的巨星吞没。

地球生命的全部历史就在我们眼前。它有开端，有终结；它的绵延，无论由多少个世纪组成，前其后后都是永恒——其实不过是迷失在永恒中的一瞬。

在地球不再是生命居所之后的很长时间里，木星和土星这些巨大的世界，较慢地从太阳阶段过渡到行星阶段，轮到它们在诸行星中称雄，焕发着远胜地球的生命力的光辉。但它们也衰老了，坠入了坟墓之夜。

### Χ / Χ

===Original===
Had the Earth, like Jupiter, for example, retained long enough the elements of life, death would have come only with the extinction of the Sun. But the length of the life of a world is proportional to its size and its elements of vitality.

The solar heat is due to two principal causes—the condensation of the original nebula, and the fall of meteorites. According to the best established calculations of thermodynamics, the former has produced a quantity of heat eighteen million times greater than that which the Sun radiates yearly, supposing the original nebula was cold, which there is no reason to believe was the case. It is, therefore, certain that the solar temperature produced by this condensation far exceeded the above. If condensation continues, the radiation of heat may go on for centuries without loss.

===Chinese===
倘若地球，比如像木星那样，把生命的要素保有得足够长久，死亡便只会随太阳的熄灭而来。但一个世界的寿命，与它的大小及其生命力要素成正比。

太阳的热源自两个主要成因——原始星云的凝聚，和陨石的坠落。依照热力学最可靠的计算，前者产生的热量，是太阳每年辐射热量的1800万倍——这是假定原始星云是冷的，而我们没有理由相信情况如此。因此可以肯定，这一凝聚所产生的太阳温度远超上述数字。倘若凝聚继续下去，热的辐射可以历尽世纪而不衰减。

===Original===
The heat emitted every second is equal to that which would result from the combustion of eleven quadrillions six hundred thousand milliards of tons of coal burning at once! The Earth intercepts only one five hundredth millionth part of the radiant heat, and this one five hundredth millionth suffices to maintain all terrestrial life. Of sixty-seven millions of light and heat rays which the Sun radiates into space, only one is received and utilized by the planets.

Well! to maintain this source of heat it is only necessary that the rate of condensation should be such that the Sun’s diameter should decrease seventy-seven meters a year, or one kilometer in thirteen years. This contraction is so gradual that it would be wholly imperceptible. Nine thousand five hundred years would be required to reduce the diameter by one single second of arc. Even if the Sun be actually in a gaseous state, its temperature, so far from growing less, or even remaining stationary, would increase by the very fact of contraction; for if on the one hand the temperature of a gaseous body falls when it condenses, on the other hand the heat generated by contraction is more than sufficient to prevent a fall in temperature, and the amount of heat increases until a liquid state is reached. The Sun seems to have reached this stage.

===Chinese===
太阳每秒发出的热，等于一万一千六百万亿吨煤同时燃烧所产生的热！地球只截获辐射热的两亿分之一，而这两亿分之一就足以维持全部地球生命。太阳向空间辐射的每六千七百万道光与热线中，只有一道被行星接收和利用。

好！要维持这个热源，只需凝聚的速率恰好使太阳的直径每年缩短七十七米，即十三年缩短一公里。这种收缩极其缓慢，将完全无法察觉。要把直径缩小一角秒，需要九千五百年。即使太阳确实处于气态，它的温度非但不会降低、甚至不会保持不变，反而会因收缩而升高；因为气体凝聚时温度固然下降，但另一方面，凝聚所产生的热又足以阻止温度下降，热量不断增加，直到达到液态。太阳似乎已到了这一阶段。

===Original===
The condensation of the Sun, whose density is only one-fourth that of the Earth, may thus of itself maintain for centuries, at least for ten million years, the light and heat of this brilliant star. But we have just spoken of a second source of heat: the fall of meteorites. One hundred and forty-six million meteorites fall upon the Earth yearly. A vastly greater number fall into the Sun, because of its greater attraction. If their mass equals about the one hundredth part of the mass of the Earth, their fall would suffice to maintain the temperature—not by their combustion, for if the Sun itself was being consumed it would not have lasted more than six thousand years, but by the sudden transformation of the energy of motion into heat, the velocity of impact being 650,000 meters per second, so great is the solar attraction.

If the Earth should fall into the Sun, it would make good for ninety-five years the actual loss of solar energy; Venus would make good this loss for eighty-four years; Mercury for seven; Mars for thirteen; Jupiter for 32,254; Saturn for 9652; Uranus for 1610; and Neptune for 1890 years. That is to say, the fall of all the planets into the Sun would produce heat enough to maintain the present rate of expenditure for about 46,000 years.

===Chinese===
太阳的密度仅为地球的四分之一，仅凭它的凝聚，便可以把这颗辉煌星辰的光和热维持若干世纪——至少一千万年。但我们刚才还谈到第二个热源：陨石的坠落。每年有一亿四千六百万颗陨石落到地球上。落入太阳的要多得多，因为太阳的引力更大。倘若陨石的总质量约为地球质量的一百分之一，它们的坠落便足以维持太阳的温度——不是靠燃烧，因为倘若太阳自身在被烧掉，它撑不过六千年——而是靠运动能向热的骤然转化；太阳引力如此之大，撞击速度高达每秒65万米。

倘若地球坠入太阳，可补偿太阳能量现时损失九十五年；金星可补偿此损失八十四年；水星七年；火星十三年；木星32,254年；土星9652年；天王星1610年；海王星1890年。也就是说，所有行星坠入太阳所产生的热，约可维持现今的消耗速率46,000年。

===Original===
It is therefore certain that the fall of meteors greatly lengthens the life of the Sun. One thirty-third millionth of the solar mass added each year would compensate for the loss, and half of this would be sufficient if we admit that condensation shares equally with the fall of meteorites in the maintenance of solar heat; centuries would have to pass before any acceleration of the planets’ velocities would be apparent.

Owing to these two causes alone we may, therefore, admit a future for the Sun of at least twenty million years; and this period cannot but be increased by other unknown causes, to say nothing of an encounter with a swarm of meteorites.

The Sun therefore was the last living member of the system; the last animated by the warmth of life.

===Chinese===
因此可以肯定，陨石的坠落大大延长了太阳的寿命。每年只需补入太阳质量的三千三百万分之一即可补偿损耗；倘若我们承认凝聚与陨石坠落同等分担太阳热的维持，那么一半就够了；行星速度要出现任何可察觉的加快，还得过去许多个世纪。

因此，单凭这两个成因，我们便可承认太阳至少还有两千万年的未来；这一期限还必定会被其他未知原因所延长，更不必说与陨石群相遇的情形了。

于是，太阳是这体系里最后一个活着的成员；最后一个被生命的温暖所激荡的。

===Original===
But the Sun also went out. After having so long poured upon his celestial children his vivifying beams, the black spots upon his surface increased in number and in extent, his brilliant photosphere grew dull, and his hitherto dazzling surface became congealed. An enormous red ball took the place of the dazzling center of the vanished worlds.

For a long time this enormous star maintained a high surface temperature, and a sort of phosphorescent atmosphere; its virgin soil, illumined by the light of the stars and by the electric influences which formed a kind of atmosphere, gave birth to a marvelous flora, to an unknown fauna, to beings differing absolutely in organization from those who had succeeded each other upon the worlds of its system.

But for the Sun also the end came, and the hour sounded on the timepiece of destiny when the whole solar system was stricken from the book of life. And one after another the stars, each one of which is a sun, a solar system, shared the same fate; yet the universe continued to exist as it does today.

===Chinese===
但太阳也熄灭了。在他把赋生的光束如此长久地倾泻给天上的儿女之后，他表面的黑斑在数量和范围上不断增加，他明亮的光球黯淡下去，他一向夺目的表面凝固了。一颗巨大的红球取代了那消逝诸世界曾经夺目的中心。

很长一段时间里，这颗巨大的星辰维持着很高的表面温度，和一种磷光般的大气；它未经开垦的土壤，被星光和电的影响照亮——那些电的影响形成了一种大气——孕育出奇异的植物群、未知的动物群，孕育出与其体系诸世界先后居民的组织截然不同的生命。

但对太阳来说，终结也到来了；整个太阳系从生命之册上被抹去的那一刻，在命运的时计上敲响了。而那些恒星——每一颗都是一个太阳、一个太阳系——也一颗接一颗遭遇了同样的命运；宇宙却一如既往地继续存在。

### Ψ / Ψ

===Original===
The science of mathematics tells us: “The solar system does not appear to possess at present more than the one four hundred and fifty-fourth part of the transformable energy which it had in the nebulous state. Although this remainder constitutes a fund whose magnitude confounds our imagination, it will also some day be exhausted. Later, the transformation will be complete for the entire universe, resulting in a general equilibrium of temperature and pressure.

“Energy will not then be susceptible of transformation. This does not mean annihilation, a word without meaning, nor does it mean the absence of motion, properly speaking, since the same sum of energy will always exist in the form of atomic motion, but the absence of all sensible motion, of all differentiation, the absolute uniformity of conditions, that is to say, absolute death.”

Such is the present statement of the science of mathematics.

===Chinese===
数学科学告诉我们：「太阳系目前所拥有的可转化能量，看来不超过它在星云态时所具有的四百五十四分之一。尽管这一剩余构成一笔其浩瀚令我们想象为难的基金，它也终有一天会耗竭。此后，对整个宇宙而言，转化将告完成，导致温度与压力的普遍平衡。

「那时能量将不再能够转化。这并不意味着湮灭——一个毫无意义的词——也不意味着严格说来运动的消失，因为同样的能量总和将永远以原子运动的形式存在；而是意味着一切可感运动的消失，一切差异的消失，条件的绝对均一，也就是说，绝对的死亡。」

这就是数学科学目前的论断。

===Original===
Experiment and observation prove that on the one hand the quantity of matter, and on the other hand the quantity of energy also, remains constant, whatever the change in form or in position; but they also show that the universe tends to a state of equilibrium, a condition in which its heat will be uniformly distributed.

The heat of the Sun and of all the stars seems to be due to the transformation of their initial energy of motion, to molecular impacts; the heat thus generated is being constantly radiated into space, and this radiation will go on until every sun is cooled down to the temperature of space itself.

If we admit that the sciences of today, mechanics, physics and mathematics, are trustworthy, and that the laws which now control the operations of nature and of reason are permanent, this must be the fate of the universe.

===Chinese===
实验与观测证明，一方面物质的量、另一方面能量的量，无论形式或位置如何变化，都保持恒定；但它们同样表明，宇宙趋于一种平衡态，一种热将均匀分布的状态。

太阳和一切恒星的热，似乎都源自它们初始运动能向分子撞击的转化；由此产生的热不断向空间辐射，这一辐射将持续下去，直到每一颗太阳都冷却到与空间本身相同的温度。

倘若我们承认今日的科学——力学、物理学和数学——是可靠的，承认如今支配着自然与理性运作的法则是恒久的，这便必定是宇宙的命运。

===Original===
Far from being eternal, the Earth on which we live has had a beginning. In eternity a hundred million years, a thousand million years or centuries, are as a day. There is an eternity behind us and before us, and all apparent duration is but a point. A scientific investigation of nature and acquaintance with its laws raises, therefore, the question already raised by the theologians, whether Plato, Zoroaster, Saint Augustine, Saint Thomas Aquinas, or some young seminarist who has just taken orders: “What was God doing before the creation of the universe, and what will he do after its end?” Or, under a less anthromorphic form, since God is unknowable: “What was the condition of the universe prior to the present order of things, and what will it be after this order has passed away?”

Note that the question is the same, whether we admit a personal God, reasoning and acting toward a definite end, or, whether we deny the existence of any spiritual being, and admit only the existence of indestructible atoms and forces representing an invariable sum of energy.

===Chinese===
我们居住的地球远非永恒，它有一个开端。在永恒之中，一亿年、十亿年或多少个世纪，都如同一日。我们身后有永恒，身前也有永恒，一切表观的绵延都不过是一点。因此，对自然的科学考察和对自然法则的熟识，提出了神学家们早已提出的问题——无论是柏拉图、琐罗亚斯德、圣奥古斯丁、圣托马斯·阿奎那，还是某个刚领圣职的年轻修士：「上帝在创造宇宙之前做什么，宇宙终结之后又将做什么？」或者，以较少拟人色彩的形式——既然上帝不可知：「在现今的事物秩序之前，宇宙处于何种状况；这一秩序消逝之后，又将是何种状况？」

请注意，无论我们承认一个人格的上帝，为一个明确的目的而推理、而行动，还是否认任何精神存在的存在、只承认不灭的原子与代表恒定能量总和的力，问题都是同一个。

===Original===
In the first case, why should God, an eternal and uncreated power, remain inactive? Or, having remained inactive, satisfied with the absolute infinity of his nature which nothing could augment, why did he change this state and create matter and force?

The theologian may reply: “Because it was his good pleasure.” But philosophy is not satisfied with this change in the divine purpose. In the second case, since the origin of the present condition of things only dates back a certain time, and since there can be no effect without a cause, we have the right to ask what was the condition of things anterior to the formation of the present universe.

Although energy is indestructible, we certainly cannot deny the tendency toward its universal dissipation, and this must lead to absolute repose and death, for the conclusions of mathematics are irresistible.

Nevertheless, we do not concede this.

Why?

Because the universe is not a definite quantity.

===Chinese===
在第一种情形下，上帝——一种永恒的、非被造的权能——为什么无所作为？或者，既然一直无所作为，满足于他那无可增损的本性之绝对无限，他又为什么要改变这种状态，创造出物质和力？

能量虽不灭，我们却当然不能否认它普遍耗散的趋势，而这必定导致绝对的安息与死亡，因为数学的结论是无法抗拒的。

尽管如此，我们并不承认这一点。

为什么？

因为宇宙不是一个有限的量。

### Ω / Ω

===Original===
It is impossible to conceive of a limit to the extension of matter. Limitless space, the inexhaustible source of the transformation of potential energy into visible motion, and thence into heat and other forces, confronts us, and not a simple, finished piece of mechanism, running like a clock and stopping forever.

The future of the universe is its past. If the universe were to have had an end, this end would have been reached long ago, and we should not be here to study this problem.

===Chinese===
物质广延的界限是无法设想的。无垠的空间——势能转化为可见运动、再转化为热及其他力量的不竭源泉——呈现在我们面前；而不是一座上好发条、走完便永远停摆的简单的成品机器。

Suppress everything, space and time would still remain; that is to say, space which material things may occupy, and the possibility of the succession of events. If this were not so, neither space nor time would be really measurable, not even in thought, since thought would not exist. ===Original===
We do not comprehend or conceive of infinite space or time, because we are incapable of it. But this incapacity does not invalidate the existence of the absolute. In confessing that we do not comprehend infinity, we feel it about us, and that space, as bounded by a wall or any barrier whatever, is in itself an absurd idea. And we are equally incapable of denying the possibility of the existence, at some instant of time, of a system of worlds whose motions would measure time without creating it. Do our clocks create time? No, they do but measure it. In the presence of the absolute, our measures of both time and space vanish; but the absolute remains.

===Original===
The best definition of the universe ever given, to which there was nothing to add, is Pascal’s, “A sphere whose center is everywhere and circumference nowhere.”

===Chinese===
有史以来对宇宙最好的定义、无可增补的，是帕斯卡（Pascal）的定义：「一个球，中心无处不在，圆周无处可寻。」

正是这无限，保证了宇宙的永恒。

===Original===
Stars, systems, myriads, milliards, universes succeed each other without end in every direction.

No bounds to time. Live in imagination through future ages, add centuries to centuries, epoch to epoch, we shall never attain the end, we shall always remain in the vestibule of the eternity which opens before us.

===Chinese===
恒星、体系、万千、亿万、宇宙，在每一个方向上无尽相续。

我们并不生活在某个并不存在的中心附近；地球一如最遥远的恒星，卧于不可测的无限之中。

A body is alive so long as respiration and the circulation of the blood makes it possible for the various organs to perform their functions. When equilibrium and repose are reached, death follows; but after death all the substances of which the body was formed are wrought into other beings. Dissolution is the prelude to recreation. Analogy leads us to believe that the same is true of the cosmos. Nothing can be destroyed.

For this reason there will always be suns and worlds, not like ours, but still suns and worlds succeeding each other through all eternity.
