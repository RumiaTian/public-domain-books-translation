## Grin / 咧嘴一笑

===Original===
If you’re up against a bruiser and you’re getting knocked about﻿—
 

 Grin.
 

 If you’re feeling pretty groggy, and you’re licked beyond a doubt﻿—
 

 Grin.
 

 Don’t let him see you’re funking, let him know with every clout,
 

 Though your face is battered to a pulp, your blooming heart is stout;
 

 Just stand upon your pins until the beggar knocks you out﻿—
 

 And grin.
This life’s a bally battle, and the same advice holds true,
 

 Of grin.
 

 If you’re up against it badly, then it’s only one on you,
 

 So grin.
 

 If the future’s black as thunder, don’t let people see you’re blue;
 

 Just cultivate a cast-iron smile of joy the whole day through;
 

 If they call you “Little Sunshine,” wish that *they’d* no troubles, too﻿—
 

 You may﻿—grin.
Rise up in the morning with the will that, smooth or rough,
 

 You’ll grin.
 

 Sink to sleep at midnight, and although you’re feeling tough,
 

 Yet grin.
 

 There’s nothing gained by whining, and you’re not that kind of stuff;
 

 You’re a fighter from away back, and you *won’t* take a rebuff;
 

 Your trouble is that you don’t know when you have had enough﻿—
 

 Don’t give in.
 

 If Fate should down you, just get up and take another cuff;
 

 You may bank on it that there is no philosophy like bluff
 

 And grin.

===Chinese===
假如你撞上一个打手，正被他揍得七荤八素——
 
 
 咧嘴一笑。
 
 
 假如你觉得晕头转向，败局已定无可怀疑——
 
 
 咧嘴一笑。
 
 
 别让他看出你在怯阵，让他从每一记重击里明白，
 
 
 哪怕你的脸已被捣成烂泥，你这条该死的心还挺结实；
 
 
 只管用两条腿站直了，直到那家伙把你打倒——
 
 
 然后咧嘴一笑。
这生活是场活见鬼的战斗，同样的忠告照样灵验，
 
 
 那就是咧嘴笑。
 
 
 假如你处境糟透了，那也不过是你一个人的难处，
 
 
 所以咧嘴一笑。
 
 
 假如前路黑得像暴风雨，别让人看出你的沮丧；
 
 
 只管整天修炼一副铸铁般快乐的笑容；
 
 
 若人家管你叫「小太阳」，就祝愿*他们*也无灾无难——
 
 
 你尽可以——咧嘴一笑。
早晨起身就抱定主意，无论顺当还是坎坷，
 
 
 都要咧嘴笑。
 
 
 午夜沉入睡梦，尽管你觉得熬得够苦，
 
 
 还是要咧嘴笑。
 
 
 哭哭啼啼捞不着半点好处，你也不是那种料；
 
 
 你向来就是个斗士，*绝不肯*受一回抢白；
 
 
 你的毛病在于不知道什么时候才算受够了——
 
 
 别认输。
 
 
 倘若命运把你击倒，就爬起来再挨一记重拳；
 
 
 你尽管相信，世上没有哪种哲学比得上虚张声势
 
 
 再加咧嘴一笑。
