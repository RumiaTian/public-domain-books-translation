## The Harpy / 鸱枭

===Original===
*There was a woman, and she was wise; woefully wise was she;*
 
 

 
 *She was old, so old, yet her years all told were but a score and three;*
 
 

 
 *And she knew by heart, from finish to start, the Book of Iniquity.*
There is no hope for such as I, on earth nor yet in Heaven;
 

 Unloved I live, unloved I die, unpitied, unforgiven;
 

 A loathèd jade I ply my trade, unhallowed and unshriven.
I paint my cheeks, for they are white, and cheeks of chalk men hate;
 

 Mine eyes with wine I make to shine, that men may seek and sate;
 

 With overhead a lamp of red I sit me down and wait.
Until they come, the nightly scum, with drunken eyes aflame;
 

 Your sweethearts, sons, ye scornful ones﻿—’tis I who know their shame;
 

 The gods ye see are brutes to me﻿—and so I play my game.
For life is not the thing we thought, and not the thing we plan;
 

 And woman in a bitter world must do the best she can;
 

 Must yield the stroke, and bear the yoke, and serve the will of man;
Must serve his need and ever feed the flame of his desire;
 

 Though be she loved for love alone, or be she loved for hire;
 

 For every man since life began is tainted with the mire.
And though you know he love you so, and set you on love’s throne,
 

 Yet let your eyes but mock his sighs, and let your heart be stone,
 

 Lest you be left (as I was left) attainted and alone.
From love’s close kiss to hell’s abyss is one sheer flight, I trow;
 

 And wedding-ring and bridal bell are will-o’-wisps of woe;
 

 And ’tis not wise to love too well, and this all women know.
Wherefore, the wolf-pack having gorged upon the lamb, their prey,
 

 With siren smile and serpent guile I make the wolf-pack pay;
 

 With velvet paws and flensing claws, a tigress roused to slay.
One who in youth sought truest truth, and found a devil’s lies;
 

 A symbol of the sin of man, a human sacrifice:
 

 Yet shall I blame on man the shame? Could it be otherwise?
Was I not born to walk in scorn where others walk in pride?
 

 The Maker marred, and evil-starred I drift upon His tide;
 

 And He alone shall judge His own, so I His judgment bide.
*Fate has written a tragedy; its name is “The Human Heart.”*
 
 

 
 *The theatre is the House of Life, Woman the mummer’s part:*
 
 

 
 *The Devil enters the prompter’s box and the play is ready to start.*

===Chinese===
*有一个女人，她很聪明；聪明得可怜巴巴；*
 
 

 
 *她老了，那么老，可拢共算来她不过二十三岁；*
 
 

 
 *而那部《罪孽之书》，从尾到头她都烂熟于心。*
像我这样的人没有指望，人间没有，天上也没有；
 
 
 无人爱地活着，无人爱地死去，无人怜，也无人宽宥；
 
 
 一个招人嫌的贱货做着她那行当，不圣洁，也没赎过罪。
我往脸颊上抹红，因为它们发白，男人讨厌死灰的脸色；
 
 
 我用酒把眼睛点亮，好让男人们来寻欢、来餍足；
 
 
 头顶挂一盏红灯，我坐下来等着。
直到他们来，那每夜的下等货色，醉眼里烧着火；
 
 
 你们的情郎、你们的儿子，你们这些轻蔑的人——他们的丑事只有我知道；
 
 
 你们眼中的神明，于我是畜生——所以我玩我的把戏。
因为生活不是我们想的那样，也不是我们盘算的那样；
 
 
 女人在这苦涩的世道里，只能怎么好怎么撑；
 
 
 只能挨那一击，戴上轭木，伺候男人的意志；
只能供他的需索，永远添着他欲火的柴；
 
 
 不管她是因爱被爱，还是为钱被买；
 
 
 因为自有生命以来，每个男人都沾着这泥污。
尽管你知道他这样爱你，把你捧上爱的宝座，
 
 
 也要让你的眼睛只当他叹息是笑话，让你的心是石头，
 
 
 免得你被丢下（像我一样被丢下），身败名裂，孤零零一个。
从爱的紧密一吻到地狱深渊，一步就跌到底，我敢说；
 
 
 结婚戒指和婚礼钟声，是引向灾祸的鬼火；
 
 
 爱得太深不聪明，这个道理女人全知道。
所以，狼群既然吞饱了羔羊——它们的猎物，
 
 
 我就用海妖的笑、毒蛇的诈，叫狼群付出代价；
 
 
 长着天鹅绒的软爪和剥皮的利爪，一头被激起来杀戮的母虎。
一个年轻时寻求至真之真、却撞见魔鬼谎言的人；
 
 
 一个男人之罪的象征，一件活人祭品：
 
 
 可我该把这耻辱归罪于男人吗？难道本可以不是这样？
我难道不是生来就在轻蔑中行走，而别人在骄傲中行走？
 
 
 造物主造坏了我，我顶着凶星，在祂的潮水里漂泊；
 
 
 而祂自会审判祂的造物，所以我静候祂的裁决。
*命运写好了一出悲剧；名叫「人心」。*
 
 

 
 *剧场是「人生之厦」，女人演哑角：*
 
 

 
 *魔鬼坐进提词席，大戏就要开场。*
