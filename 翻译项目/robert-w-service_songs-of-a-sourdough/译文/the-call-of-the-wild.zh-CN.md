## The Call of the Wild / 野性的呼唤

===Original===
Have you gazed on naked grandeur where there’s nothing else to gaze on,
 

 Set pieces and drop-curtain scenes galore,
 

 Big mountains heaved to heaven, which the blinding sunsets blazon,
 

 Black canyons where the rapids rip and roar?
 

 Have you swept the visioned valley with the green stream streaking through it,
 

 Searched the Vastness for a something you have lost?
 

 Have you strung your soul to silence? Then for God’s sake go and do it;
 

 Hear the challenge, learn the lesson, pay the cost.
Have you wandered in the wilderness, the sagebrush desolation,
 

 The bunch-grass levels where the cattle graze?
 

 Have you whistled bits of ragtime at the end of all creation,
 

 And learned to know the desert’s little ways?
 

 Have you camped upon the foothills, have you galloped o’er the ranges,
 

 Have you roamed the arid sun-lands through and through?
 

 Have you chummed up with the mesa? Do you know its moods and changes?
 

 Then listen to the wild﻿—it’s calling you.
Have you known the Great White Silence, not a snow-gemmed twig a-quiver?
 

 (Eternal truths that shame our soothing lies.)
 

 Have you broken trail on snowshoes? mushed your huskies up the river,
 

 Dared the unknown, led the way, and clutched the prize?
 

 Have you marked the map’s void spaces, mingled with the mongrel races,
 

 Felt the savage strength of brute in every thew?
 

 And though grim as hell the worst is, can you round it off with curses?
 

 Then hearken to the wild﻿—it’s wanting you.
Have you suffered, starved, and triumphed grovelled, down, yet grasped at glory,
 

 Grown bigger in the bigness of the whole?
 

 “Done things” just for the doing, letting babblers tell the story,
 

 Seeing through the nice veneer the naked soul?
 

 Have you seen God in His splendours, heard the text that nature renders?
 

 (You’ll never hear it in the family pew.)
 

 The simple things, the true things, the silent men who do things﻿—
 

 Then listen to the wild﻿—it’s calling you.
They have cradled you in custom, they have primed you with their preaching,
 

 They have soaked you in convention through and through;
 

 They have put you in a showcase; you’re a credit to their teaching﻿—
 

 But can’t you hear the wild?﻿—it’s calling you.
 

 Let us probe the silent places, let us seek what luck betide us;
 

 Let us journey to a lonely land I know.
 

 There’s a whisper on the night-wind, there’s a star agleam to guide us,
 

 And the wild is calling, calling﻿ ﻿… let us go.

===Chinese===
你可曾凝望过赤裸的壮美，那里再没有别的可望，
 
 
 布景成列，落幕大戏一场接一场，
 
 
 大山高高隆起到天际，被炫目的落日镀上纹章，
 
 
 黑色的峡谷里急流撕扯咆哮？
 
 
 你可曾扫视那梦幻的谷地，一道碧溪蜿蜒穿行，
 
 
 在浩茫之中寻找一样你丢失了的东西？
 
 
 你可曾把灵魂绷进寂静？那么看在上帝份上，快去做了它；
 
 
 听那挑战，学那一课，付出代价。
你可曾漂泊在荒野，那山艾丛生的荒凉地，
 
 
 那牛群吃草的丛生禾草平川？
 
 
 你可曾在造物的尽头吹几段拉格泰姆，
 
 
 又摸熟了沙漠的小脾气？
 
 
 你可曾在山麓扎营，可曾驰骋过重重山脉，
 
 
 可曾把干旱的日头之地走了个遍？
 
 
 你可曾跟方山结成伙伴？可知道它的心性变幻？
 
 
 那就听那荒野吧——它在呼唤你。
你可曾识得那大白寂静，连镶雪的细枝也不颤一下？
 
 
 （永恒的真理，羞煞我们安慰人的谎言。）
 
 
 你可曾踏着雪鞋开出路来？可曾赶着雪橇犬沿河而上，
 
 
 涉险未知，领路向前，攥住了奖赏？
 
 
 你可曾在地图的空白处留下记号，与混杂的种族相往来，
 
 
 在每一条筋腱里感到野兽的蛮力？
 
 
 哪怕最糟的地狱般严酷，你也能用咒骂把它收场？
 
 
 那就倾听荒野吧——它正等着你。
你可曾受苦、挨饿，又凯旋——趴倒过、沉落过，却仍抓住荣光，
 
 
 在整体的浩大里长得更加高大？
 
 
 「做成了事」只为做本身，任闲人去传讲故事，
 
 
 透过漂亮的虚饰看赤裸的灵魂？
 
 
 你可曾在祂的荣光中见过上帝，听过自然宣讲的经文？
 
 
 （在自家的教堂长椅上你永远听不到。）
 
 
 那些简单的事，真实的事，默默做事的人——
 
 
 那就听那荒野吧——它在呼唤你。
他们用习俗把你裹进摇篮，用说教把你填满，
 
 
 他们用成规把你浸了个透；
 
 
 他们把你摆进陈列柜；你是他们教育的光彩——
 
 
 可你难道听不见荒野吗？——它在呼唤你。
 
 
 让我们去探那寂静之地，去碰碰我们的运气；
 
 
 让我们动身去一片我认识的孤寂土地。
 
 
 夜风里有低语，有一颗星闪着光为我们引路，
 
 
 荒野正呼唤着，呼唤着……我们走吧。
