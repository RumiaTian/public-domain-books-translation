## The Three Voices / 三种声音

===Original===
The waves have a story to tell me,
 

 As I lie on the lonely beach;
 

 Chanting aloft in the pine-tops,
 

 The wind has a lesson to teach;
 

 But the stars sing an anthem of glory
 

 I cannot put into speech.
The waves tell of ocean spaces,
 

 Of hearts that are wild and brave,
 

 Of populous city places,
 

 Of desolate shores they lave;
 

 Of men who sally in quest of gold
 

 To sink in an ocean grave.
The wind is a mighty roamer;
 

 He bids me keep me free,
 

 Clean from the taint of the gold-lust,
 

 Hardy and pure as he;
 

 Cling with my love to nature
 

 As a child to the mother-knee.
But the stars throng out in their glory,
 

 And they sing of the God in man;
 

 They sing of the mighty Master,
 

 Of the loom His fingers span;
 

 Where a star or a soul is a part of the whole,
 

 And weft in the wondrous plan.
Here by the campfire’s flicker,
 

 Deep in my blanket curled,
 

 I long for the peace of the pine-gloom
 

 When the scroll of the Lord is unfurled,
 

 And the wind and the wave are silent,
 

 And world is singing to world.

===Chinese===
波浪有一个故事要讲给我，
 
 
 当我躺在孤单的海滩上；
 
 
 在松树顶上高声吟唱的，
 
 
 风有一课要教给我；
 
 
 可星星唱着一首荣耀的颂歌，
 
 
 我没法把它说成话。
波浪讲的是海洋的空阔，
 
 
 讲狂野而勇敢的心，
 
 
 讲人烟稠密的城市，
 
 
 讲它们冲刷着的荒凉海岸；
 
 
 讲那些出征寻金
 
 
 却沉进海洋坟墓的人。
风是个强大的漫游者；
 
 
 他嘱咐我保持自由，
 
 
 洁净而不染黄金的贪欲，
 
 
 像他一样坚忍而纯粹；
 
 
 让我的爱紧贴着自然，
 
 
 像孩子贴着母亲的膝。
可星星们带着荣耀涌出来，
 
 
 它们唱的是人心中的上帝；
 
 
 唱那位大能的主宰，
 
 
 唱祂手指撑开的那张织机；
 
 
 在那里一颗星或一个灵魂都是整体的一部分，
 
 
 都是那奇妙计划里的纬线。
在这里，就着营火的摇曳，
 
 
 我深深蜷在毯子里，
 
 
 我渴望松林的幽暗里那份安宁，
 
 
 当主的书卷展开的时候，
 
 
 风和浪都静了，
 
 
 一个世界正对着另一个世界歌唱。
