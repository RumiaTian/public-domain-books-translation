## XXX The Coming of Too Much Magic / 三十 过多魔法之降临

===Original===
In Erl that had sighed for magic there was indeed magic now. The pigeon-loft and old lumber-lofts over stables were all full of trolls, the ways were full of their antics, and lights bobbed up and down the street at night long after traffic was home. For the will-o’-the-wisps would go dancing along the gutters, and had made their homes round the soft edges of duck-ponds and in green-black patches of moss that grew upon oldest thatch. And nothing seemed the same in the old village.

And amongst all these magical folk the magical half of Orion’s blood, that had slept while he went amongst earthly men, hearing mundane talk each day, stirred out of its sleep and awakened long-sleeping thoughts in his brain. And the elfin horns that he often heard blowing at evening blew with a meaning now, and blew stronger as though they were nearer.

===Chinese===
厄尔曾为魔法而叹息，如今那里确实有了魔法。鸽笼里、马厩上方的旧物阁楼里，全都住满了巨怪；道路上尽是它们嬉闹的作派；入夜之后往来行旅早已归家许久，街上却仍有一盏盏灯火上下浮动。因为鬼火们会沿着街边的沟渠翩翩起舞，又在鸭塘那柔软的塘沿、在那些长在最古老茅草顶上、绿得发黑的苔藓块里安下了家。在这古老的村子里，似乎没有什么还和从前一样了。

在所有这些魔法之族中间，奥利安血脉里那魔法的一半——当他在尘世之人中间行走、日日听着凡俗的言谈时一直沉睡着的那一半——从沉睡里惊醒了，并唤醒了他脑中那些久睡之念。他常在黄昏时分听见的精灵的号角，如今吹来已含着意味，而且吹得更盛，仿佛离得更近了。

===Original===
The folk of the village watching their lord by day saw his eyes turned away towards Elfland, saw him neglecting the wholesome earthly cares, and at night there came the queer lights and the gibbering of the trolls. A fear settled on Erl.

At this time the parliament took counsel again, twelve greybeard quaking men that had come to the house of Narl when their work was ended at evening; and all the evening was weird with the new magic of Elfland. Every man of them as he ran from his own warm house on his way to the forge of Narl had seen lights leaping, or heard voices gibbering, which were of no Christom land. And some had seen shapes prowling which were of no earthly growing, and they feared that all manner of things had slipped through the border of Elfland to come and visit the trolls.

===Chinese===
村里的百姓白天看着他们的领主，见他双目偏向精灵国那一边，见他荒废了那些正当的人世之务；到了夜里，又有那些古怪的灯火和巨怪们的叽咕声前来。一种恐惧笼罩了厄尔。

这时议会又聚议了，十二位白须飘飘、瑟瑟发抖的老人，在傍晚收工之后来到了纳尔的屋子；而整个傍晚都因精灵国的新魔法而显得诡异。他们中每一个人从自己暖和的家中跑出来、赶往纳尔铁匠铺的路上，都看见了跳跃的灯火，或听见了叽咕的声音，那绝非出自基督之土。还有些人看见了潜行的影子，绝非尘世所长育，他们唯恐各色事物已从精灵国的边境溜了过来，前来拜访那些巨怪。

===Original===
They spoke low in their parliament: all told the same tale, a tale of children terrified, a tale of women demanding the old ways again; and as they spoke they eyed window and crevice, none knowing what might come.

===Chinese===
他们在议会中低声说话：众人讲的都是同一番话——孩子被吓坏了，妇人们要求重新奉行旧日之道；他们说话时眼睛盯着窗户和墙缝，谁也不知道会有什么东西闯进来。

===Original===
And Oth said: “Let us folk go to the Lord Orion as we went to his grandfather in his long red room. Let us say how we sought for magic, and lo we have magic enough; and let him follow no more after witchery nor the things that are hidden from man.”

He listened acutely, standing there amongst his hushed comrade neighbours. Was it goblin voices that mocked him, or was it only echo? Who could say? And almost at once the night all round was hushed again.

===Chinese===
奥斯说道：「让我们这些人到奥利安领主那里去，就像当年我们去他祖父那里、进那间长长的红厅一样。让我们告诉他，我们当年如何寻求魔法，而如今瞧，魔法我们已经够多了；让他别再去追随巫术，也别再追随那些对凡人隐藏的事物。」

他站在那些静默的邻里乡亲之间，凝神倾听着。是哥布林的声音在嘲弄他呢，还是不过是回声？谁也说不准。紧接着，四下的夜又归于寂静了。

===Original===
And Threl said: “Nay. It is too late for that.” Threl had seen their lord one evening standing alone on the downs, all motionless and listening to something sounding from Elfland, with his eyes to the east as he listened: and nothing was sounding, not a noise was astir; yet Orion stood there called by things beyond mortal hearing. “It is too late now,” said Threl.

And that was the fear of all.

===Chinese===
斯瑞尔却说：「不。事已太迟。」斯瑞尔曾在一天傍晚看见他们的领主独自立于丘陵之上，一动不动地倾听着某种发自精灵国的声响，倾听之间双目朝向东方：其实并无什么在作响，没有一丝声响骚动；然而奥利安就那样立在那里，被凡人听觉之外的某些事物召唤着。「如今已太迟了。」斯瑞尔说。

而这正是众人共有的恐惧。

===Original===
Then Guhic rose slowly up and stood by that table. And trolls were gibbering like bats away in their loft, and the pale marsh-lights were flickering, and shapes prowled in the dark: the pit-pat of their feet came now and then to the ears of the twelve that were there in that inner room. And Guhic said: “We wished for a little magic.” And a gust of gibbering came clear from the trolls. And then they disputed awhile as to how much magic they had wished in the olden time, when the grandfather of Orion was lord in Erl. But when they came to a plan this was the plan of Guhic.

“If we may not turn our lord Orion,” he said, “and his eyes be turned to Elfland, let all our parliament go up the hill to the witch Ziroonderel, and put our case to her, and ask for a spell which shall be put against too much magic.”

===Chinese===
于是古希克缓缓站起身来，立在那张桌旁。远处的阁楼里，巨怪们像蝙蝠一般叽叽咕咕，苍白的沼泽灯火闪烁不定，有影子在黑暗中潜行：它们那细碎的足音一阵阵传入坐在内室里的那十二个人的耳中。古希克说道：「我们当初所求的，不过是一点点魔法。」一阵叽咕声清清楚楚地从巨怪那边涌了过来。接着他们争论了一阵，当年——奥利安的祖父尚为厄尔领主之时——他们究竟想要多少魔法。待他们终于议出一策时，这便是古希克的计策。

「倘若我们无法让我们的奥利安领主回心转意，」他说，「既然他的双目已转向精灵国，那就让我们整个议会上山，到女巫齐龙德尔泽尔（Ziroonderel）那里去，把我们的苦衷向她陈明，求她赐一道咒，用来对付这过多的魔法。」

===Original===
And at the name of Ziroonderel the twelve took heart again; for they knew that her magic was greater than the magic of flickering lights, and knew there was not a troll or thing of the night but went in fear of her broom. They took heart again and quaffed Narl’s heavy mead, and refilled their mugs and praised Guhic.

===Chinese===
一听见齐龙德尔泽尔的名字，那十二人又壮起了胆来；因为他们知道她的魔法大过那闪烁灯火的魔法，也知道没有一个巨怪或夜间之物不畏惧她的扫帚。他们重又壮起胆来，痛饮纳尔那浓重的蜂蜜酒，再斟满酒杯，盛赞古希克。

===Original===
And late in the night they all rose up together to go back to their homes, and all kept close together as they went, and sang grave old songs to affright the things that they feared; though little the light trolls care, or the will-o’-the-wisps, for the things that are grave to man. And when only one was left he ran to his house, and the will-o’-the-wisps chased him.

===Chinese===
夜深之时，他们一齐起身回各自的家，路上紧紧挤作一处，唱着庄重的古歌来吓退他们所惧之物；虽说那些轻浮的巨怪，还有那些鬼火，对凡人视为庄重的东西并不怎么放在心上。最后只剩一个人时，他便朝自家奔去，鬼火们一路追着他。

===Original===
When the next day came they ended their work early, for the parliament of Erl cared not to be left on the witch’s hill when night came, or even the gloaming. They met outside Narl’s forge in the early afternoon, eleven of the parliament, and they called out Narl. And all were wearing the clothes they were wont to wear when they went with the rest to the holy place of the Freer, though there was scarcely a soul he had ever cursed that was not blessed by her. And away they went with their old stout staves up the hill.

===Chinese===
次日他们早早便收了工，因为厄尔议会的人不愿意被撂在女巫山上挨到夜色降临、甚至连暮霭初起也不愿意。午后不久，议会的十一个人在纳尔的铁匠铺外聚齐，把纳尔喊了出来。他们身上穿的，都是往常和众人一同去教士的圣所时所穿的那身衣裳，虽说教士所曾诅咒过的灵魂，几乎没有一个不曾受过她的祝福。于是他们拄着各自那些老旧结实的粗杖，上山去了。

===Original===
And as soon as they could they came to the witch’s house. And there they found her sitting outside her door gazing over the valley away, and looking neither older nor younger, nor concerned one way or the other with the coming and going of years.

“We be the parliament of Erl,” they said, standing before her all in their graver clothes.

“Aye,” she said. “You desired magic. Has it come to you yet?”

“Truly,” they said, “and to spare.”

“There is more to come,” she said.

===Chinese===
他们尽快赶到了女巫的屋子。只见她坐在门外，向远处的山谷凝望，看上去既不见老、也不见少，对岁月的来去也毫无在意的样子。

「我们是厄尔议会的人。」他们说道，穿着各自身上那身更庄重的衣裳站在她面前。

「是啊，」她说，「你们曾渴望魔法。它可已降临到你们这儿了？」

「千真万确，」他们说，「而且绰绰有余。」

「还有更多要来呢。」她说。

===Original===
“Mother Witch,” said Narl, “we are met here to pray you that you will give us a goodly spell which shall be a charm against magic, so that there be no more of it in the valley, for overmuch has come.”

“Overmuch?” she said. “Overmuch magic! As though magic were not the spice and essence of life, its ornament and its splendour. By my broom,” said she, “I give you no spell against magic.”

===Chinese===
「女巫婆婆，」纳尔说，「我们聚到此处，是求您赐我们一道好咒，作为抗魔的护符，叫这山谷里再没有魔法，因为来的实在太多了。」

「太多？」她说，「魔法太多！说得仿佛魔法不是生命的香料与精髓、不是它的装饰与荣光似的。凭我的扫帚起誓，」她说，「我不会给你们一道抗魔的咒。」

===Original===
And they thought of the wandering lights and the scarce-seen gibbering things, and all the strangeness and evil that was come to their valley of Erl, and they besought her again, speaking suavely to her.

“Oh, Mother Witch,” said Guhic, “there is overmuch magic indeed, and the folk that should tarry in Elfland are all over the border.”

“It is even so,” said Narl. “The border is broken and there will be no end to it. Will-o’-the-wisps should stay in the marshes, and trolls and goblins in Elfland, and we folk should keep to our own folk. This is the thought of us all. For magic, if we desired it somewhat, years ago when we were young, pertains to matters that are not for man.”

===Chinese===
他们想起那些游荡的灯火、那些难得一见的叽咕之物，想起所有降临到他们厄尔之谷的古怪与邪祟，便又恳求起她来，言辞殷勤而恳切。

「哦，女巫婆婆，」古希克说，「魔法确实太多了，那些本该滞留在精灵国的族类，全都越过了边境。」

「正是这样，」纳尔说，「边境一破，这事便没完没了。鬼火本该待在沼泽里，巨怪和哥布林本该待在精灵国里，而我们人应当守着自己的人。这是我们大家的心思。至于魔法，倘若我们当年——多年前年轻之时——曾略略动过一点念头，它所关涉的，也尽是些非为人所宜之事。」

===Original===
She eyed him silently with a catlike glow increasing in her eyes. And when she neither spoke nor moved, Narl besought her again.

“O Mother Witch,” he said, “will you give us no spell to guard our homes against magic?”

“No spell indeed!” she hissed. “No spell indeed! By broom and stars and night-riding! Would you rob Earth of her heirloom that has come from the olden time? Would you take her treasure and leave her bare to the scorn of her comrade planets? Poor indeed were we without magic, whereof we are well stored to the envy of darkness and Space.” She leaned forward from where she sat and stamped her stick, looking up in Narl’s face with her fierce unwavering eyes. “I would sooner,” she said, “give you a spell against water, that all the world should thirst, than give you a spell against the song of streams that evening hears faintly over the ridge of a hill, too dim for wakeful ears, a song threading through dreams, whereby we learn of old wars and lost loves of the spirits of rivers. I would sooner give you a spell against bread, that all the world should starve, than give you a spell against the magic of wheat that haunts the golden hollows in moonlight in July, through which in the warm short nights wander how many of whom man knows nothing. I would make you spells against comfort and clothing, food, shelter and warmth, aye and will do it, sooner than tear from these poor fields of Earth that magic that is to them an ample cloak against the chill of Space, and a gay raiment against the sneers of nothingness.

===Chinese===
她默不作声地盯着他，眼中那猫一般的光芒愈发炽盛起来。见她既不言语、也不动弹，纳尔便再次恳求她。

「哦，女巫婆婆，」他说，「您难道不肯赐我们一道咒，来护佑我们的家宅、抵御魔法么？」

「偏不！」她嘶声道。「偏不！凭扫帚、凭星辰、凭夜骑起誓！你们要夺走大地那自古承袭的传家之宝么？你们要取走她的珍藏，让她赤条条地去受她那些同侪星球的嗤笑么？我们若没有魔法，实在可怜得很；而我们所藏甚丰，连黑暗与太虚都妒羡我们。」她从坐着的地方探身向前，把手杖往地上一顿，以她那凶悍而凝定不瞬的双目仰视着纳尔的脸。「我宁可，」她说，「给你们一道对抗水的咒，叫全世界都口渴，也不愿给你们一道对抗溪流之歌的咒——那溪流的歌，黄昏在山脊之上隐约可闻，对清醒之耳太过微茫，是一缕穿行于梦境之中的歌，借它我们得知河灵（spirits of rivers）们往昔的战争与失落之爱。我宁可给你们一道对抗面包的咒，叫全世界都挨饿，也不愿给你们一道对抗麦之魔法的咒——那魔法盘桓于七月月光之下的金色洼地，在那些温暖的短夜之中，不知有多少人所一无所知的生灵在其中游荡。我可以给你们对抗安逸与衣裳、食物、居所与温暖的咒，我当真愿意这么做，也不愿从大地这些可怜的田亩之上撕去那魔法——那对他们是一道足以抵御太虚之寒的宽大斗篷，是一件足以抵挡虚无之嗤笑的华美衣裳。

===Original===
“Go hence. To your village go. And you that sought for magic in your youth but desire it not in your age, know that there is a blindness of spirit which comes from age, more black than the blindness of eye, making a darkness about you across which nothing may be seen, or felt, or known, or in any way apprehended. And no voice out of that darkness shall conjure me to grant a spell against magic. Hence!”

===Chinese===
「去吧。回你们的村子去。你们这些在年轻时节曾寻求魔法、到了老来却不愿再要它的人，且听着：有一种由年迈而生的灵魂之盲，比双目之盲更黑，在你们四周笼下一片黑暗，越过它，什么也看不见、摸不到、知晓不得、以任何方式也体察不了。而从那片黑暗中发出的任何声音，都休想诱我赐下一道抗魔的咒。去！」

===Original===
And as she said “Hence” she put her weight on her stick and was evidently preparing to rise from her seat. And at this great terror came upon all the parliament. And they noticed at the same moment that evening was drawing in and all the valley darkening. On this high field where the witch’s cabbages grew some light yet lingered, and listening to her fierce words they had not thought of the hour. But now it was manifestly growing late, and a wind roamed past them that seemed to come over the ridges a little way off, from night; and chilled them as it passed; and all the air seemed given over to that very thing against which they sought for a spell.

And here they were at this hour with the witch before them, and she was evidently about to rise. Her eyes were fixed on them. Already she was partly up from her chair. There could be no doubt that before three moments were passed she would be hobbling amongst them with her glittering eyes peering in each one’s face. They turned and ran down the hill.

===Chinese===
当她吐出那个「去」字时，她把身子往手杖上一压，显然正要从座上起身。议会众人无不大惧。与此同时他们又发觉黄昏正在拢来，整个山谷都在暗下去。在这片女巫种着白菜的高地上，尚有一线余光流连；他们方才听她那番凶狠的话听得出了神，竟不曾留意时辰。可此刻分明已越来越晚，一阵风从他们身旁游过，仿佛从不远处那几道山脊之外、从夜中吹来，掠过时冻得他们发寒；而整个空气，似乎都被他们正要求一道咒来对付的那样东西所占据。

此刻他们就在这样的时辰里，女巫就在他们面前，而她显然正要起身。她的双目定定地盯着他们。她已经从椅子上欠起了半个身子。毫无疑问，不出三瞬的工夫，她便会蹒跚着挤到他们中间，用她那双灼灼之目逐一窥看每个人的脸。他们转身，往山下奔去。
