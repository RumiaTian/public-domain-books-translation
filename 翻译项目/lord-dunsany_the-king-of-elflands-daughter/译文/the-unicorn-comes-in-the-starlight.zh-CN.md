## XVII The Unicorn Comes in the Starlight / 十七 独角兽于星光中降临

===Original===
And winter came, and whitened the roofs of Erl, and all the forest and uplands. And when Orion took his hounds afield in the morning the world lay like a book that was newly written by Life; for all the story of the night before lay in long lines in the snow. Here the fox had gone and there the badger, and here the red deer had gone out of the wood; the tracks led over the downs and disappeared from sight, as the deeds of statesmen, soldiers, courtiers and politicians appear and disappear on the pages of history. Even the birds had their record on those white downs, where the eye could follow each step of their treble claws, till suddenly on each side of the track would appear three little scars where the tips of their longest feathers had flicked the snow, and there the track faded utterly. They were like some popular cry, some vehement fancy, that comes down on a page of history for a day, and passes, leaving no other record at all except those lines on one page.

===Chinese===
于是冬日降临，将厄尔的屋宇、一切森林与高地尽皆染白。清晨奥利安带着他的群犬出猎时，整个世界宛如一部由生命新书写就的书卷；因为前夜的全部故事都化作长长的线条留在了雪上。狐狸从这儿走过，獾从那儿走过，赤鹿从这里出了林子；足迹越过丘陵，消失于视线之外，恰如政治家、军人、廷臣与政客的所作所为在历史的书页上出现又消逝。就连飞鸟也在那些白色的丘陵上留下了它们的记录，目光可追随它们三趾爪的每一步，直到足迹两侧忽然现出三道细小的擦痕——那是它们最长羽毛的梢头拂过积雪所留——足迹便在那里彻底隐没了。它们就像某种一时喧嚣的呼号，某种激烈的狂想，在历史的某一页上降临一日，便消逝无踪，除了那一页上的几道线条之外，不留任何别的痕迹。

===Original===
And amongst all these records left of the story of night Orion would choose the track of some great stag not too long gone, and would follow it with his hounds away over the downs until even the sound of his horn could be heard no longer in Erl. And over a ridge with his hounds, he and they all black against red remnants of sunset, the folk of Erl would see him coming home; and often it was not until all the stars were glowing through the frost. Often the skin of a red deer hung over his shoulders and the huge horns bobbed and nodded above his head.

===Chinese===
在夜之故事所留下的这一切记录之中，奥利安会挑出某只离去未久的大牡鹿的足迹，带着群犬循之越过丘陵远去，直追到他的号角声在厄尔也再听不见。厄尔之民会看见他带着群犬翻过一道山脊归家，他与群犬的剪影在落日殷红的余晖映衬之下通体漆黑；而往往已是满天星辰透过寒霜闪烁的时分。常有赤鹿的皮毛披在他肩上，巨大的鹿角在他头顶上下颠动。

===Original===
And at this time there met one day in the forge of Narl, all unknown to Orion, the men of the parliament of Erl. They met after sunset when all were home from their work. And gravely Narl handed to each the mead that was brewed from the clover honey; and when all were come they sat silent. And then Narl broke the silence, saying that Alveric ruled over Erl no more and his son was Lord of Erl, and telling again how once they had hoped for a magic lord to rule over the valley and to make it famous, and saying that this should be he. “And where now,” he said, “is the magic for which we hoped? For he hunts the deer as all his forefathers hunted, and nothing of magic has touched him from over there; and there is no new thing.”

===Chinese===
约莫在这时候，有一天，厄尔议会的众人在纳尔（Narl）的铁匠铺里聚首了，奥利安全然不知此事。他们在日落之后众人做完各自的活计回家时相聚。纳尔郑重地递给每人一杯以三叶草蜂蜜酿成的蜂蜜酒；人到齐之后，众人默然静坐。于是纳尔打破了沉默，说阿尔韦里克已不再治理厄尔，他的儿子作了厄尔领主，又重提他们曾经如何盼望着有一位通魔法的领主来治理这座山谷、使之名扬天下，并说此人当是他。「然而我们曾期盼的魔法，」他说，「如今何在？因为他打猎逐鹿，一如他列祖列宗之所为，那边没有任何魔法之物沾染过他；这里也没有任何新鲜事物。」

===Original===
And Oth stood up to defend him. “He is as fleet as his hounds,” he said, “and hunts from dawn to sunset, and crosses the furthest downs and comes home untired.”

“It is but youth,” said Guhic. And so said all but Threl.

And Threl stood up and said: “He has a knowledge of the ways of the woods, and the lore of the beasts, beyond the learning of man.”

“You taught him,” said Guhic. “There is no magic here.”

“Nothing of this,” said Narl, “is from over there.”

===Chinese===
于是奥斯（Oth）站起来为他辩护。「他迅捷如他的群犬，」他说，「从黎明猎到日落，越过最远的丘陵归来而不疲倦。」

「那不过是年轻罢了。」古希克（Guhic）说。除斯瑞尔（Threl）外众人皆附和。

斯瑞尔站起来说：「他通晓林间之道，熟谙百兽之识，非人力所学能及。」

「那是你教的。」古希克说，「这里没有什么魔法。」

「这些，」纳尔说，「没有一样是从那边来的。」

===Original===
Thus they argued awhile lamenting the loss of the magic for which they had hoped: for never a valley but history touches it once, never a village but once its name is awhile on the lips of men; only the village of Erl was utterly unrecorded; never a century knew it beyond the round of its downs. And now all their plans seemed lost which they made so long ago, and they saw no hope except in the mead that was brewed from the clover honey. To this they turned in silence. Now it was a goodly brew.

===Chinese===
他们如此争辩了一阵，哀叹他们所期盼的魔法已然落空：因为从来没有哪座山谷不被历史触碰过一次，从来没有哪个村子其名字不曾有一时停留在人们唇间；唯独厄尔这个村子全然不见于记载；没有一个世纪知道它，只知道它在自家丘陵的环绕之中。如今他们许久以前所订下的一切计划似乎尽成泡影，除了那以三叶草蜂蜜酿成的蜂蜜酒之外，他们看不到别的指望。他们便默然转向了酒。这确是一等佳酿。

===Original===
And in a while new plans flashed clear in their minds, new schemes, new devices; and debates in the parliament of Erl flowed proudly on. And they would have made a plan and a policy; but Oth arose from his seat. There was in a flint-built house in the village of Erl an ancient chronicle, a volume bound in leather, and in it at certain seasons folk wrote all manner of things, the wisdom of farmers concerning the time to sow, the wisdom of hunters concerning the tracking of stags, and the wisdom of prophets that told of the way of Earth. From this Oth quoted now, two lines that he remembered on one of the aged pages; and all the rest of that page told of hoeing; these lines he said to the parliament of Erl as they sat with the mead before them at their table:

> “Hooded, and veiled with their night-like tresses,
 The Fates shall bring what no prophet guesses.”

And then they planned no more, for either their minds were calmed by a certain awe that they seemed to find in the lines, or it may be the mead was stronger than anything written in books. However it be they sat silent over their mead. And in early starlight while the west still glowed they passed away from Narl’s house back to their own homes grumbling as they went that they had no magic lord to rule over Erl, and yearning for magic, to save from oblivion the village and valley they loved. They parted one by one as they came to their houses. And three or four that dwelt near the end of the village on the side that was under the downs were not yet come to their doors, when, white and clear in the starlight and what remained of the gloaming, they saw hard-pressed and wearied a hunted unicorn coming across the downs. They stopped and gazed and shaded their eyes and stroked their beards and wondered. And still it was a white unicorn galloping wearily. And then they heard drawing nearer the cry of Orion’s hounds.

===Chinese===
过了一会儿，新的计划在他们心中闪现得清晰起来，新的谋略、新的筹策；厄尔议会的辩论豪迈地流淌开去。他们本来就要订出一项计划、一条方略来了；可是奥斯从座上站了起来。在厄尔村中一座燧石造的屋子里，有一部古老的编年史，一卷皮面装订的书册，每逢特定的时节，人们便在其中写下各种各样的事情：农人关于播种时令的智慧，猎人关于追踪牡鹿的智慧，以及先知们讲述人间之道的智慧。奥斯此刻便从中引述了两行——他记得其中一页老旧书页上的句子；而那一页其余的内容讲的尽是锄地之事；他把这两行诵给厄尔议会众人听，其时众人正坐在桌前，面前摆着蜂蜜酒：

> 「兜帽掩覆，夜色般的长发如纱垂面，
 命运女神将携来先知所不能臆测之物。」

此后他们便不再谋划了，因为不知是他们在那两行诗中似乎寻获的某种敬畏使心安定下来，抑或是那蜂蜜酒比书上所写的任何东西都更为烈性。无论如何，他们默然坐着饮酒。在初升的星光下，西方天际犹有余晖，他们离开纳尔的屋子各自回家，一路抱怨着厄尔没有一位通魔法的领主来治理，渴盼着魔法降临，以拯救他们所爱的村与谷免于湮没无闻。他们走到各自家门，便一个接一个地散去。有三四个住在村子尽头、丘陵那一侧的人尚未走到自家门口，忽然在星光与残存的暮色中，洁白而清晰，他们看见一只被追猎的独角兽正疲态尽显、狼狈不堪地越过丘陵而来。他们停下脚步凝目望去，用手遮住眉眼，抚着胡须，暗暗称奇。那始终是一匹白色的独角兽，正疲惫地奔驰着。随后他们听见了奥利安群犬的吠声，渐次逼近。
