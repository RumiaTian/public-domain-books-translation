## XXXI The Cursing of Elfin Things / 三十一 诅咒精灵之物

===Original===
As the parliament of Erl ran down the hill they ran into the dusk of evening. Greyly it lay in the valley above the mist from the stream. But with more than the mystery of dusk the air was heavy. Lights blinking early from houses showed that all the folk were home, and the street was deserted by everything that was human; save when with hushed air and almost furtive step they saw their lord Orion like a tall shadow go by, with will-o’-the-wisps behind him, towards the house of the trolls, thinking no earthly thoughts. And the strangeness that had been growing day by day made all the village eerie. So that with short and troubled breath the twelve old men hurried on.

And so they came to the holy place of the Freer, which lay on the side of the village that was towards the witch’s hill. And it was the hour at which he was wont to celebrate after-bird-song, as they named the singing that they sang in the holy place when all the birds were home. But the Freer was not within his holy place; he stood in the cold night air on the upper step without it, his face turned towards Elfland. He had on his sacred robe with its border of purple, and the emblem of gold round his neck; but the door of his holy place was shut and his back was towards it. They wondered to see him stand thus.

===Chinese===
当厄尔议会的人跑下山时，他们跑进了暮色之中。那暮色灰蒙蒙地笼罩在山谷里，浮于溪流升起的薄雾之上。然而空气中所凝重的，远不止暮色那一重神秘。各家窗户早早地眨起灯火，说明人众都已归家，街上一切属人的踪迹俱已散尽；只偶尔有一次，他们屏着气、脚步近乎鬼祟地，看见他们的领主奥利安（Orion）如一道高大的影子走过，身后跟着些鬼火，朝巨怪们的屋子而去，心里想着的尽是非人世之事。那一日浓似一日的怪异，已使全村笼罩在不安之中。于是那十二位老人呼吸短促而纷乱，加紧了脚步。

他们便这样来到了教士（the Freer）的圣所，它坐落在村子朝向女巫山的那一侧。此刻正是他惯常举行「鸟歌之后」之礼的时辰——他们如此称呼那在众鸟归巢之后于圣所中所唱的颂歌。然而教士并不在圣所之内；他立于其外上级台阶的冷夜之中，面朝精灵国（Elfland）。他身着紫边的神圣法袍，颈间悬着黄金的徽记；但圣所的门是关着的，他的背正对着它。他们见他这样站着，心中诧异。

===Original===
And as they wondered the Freer began to intone, clear in the evening with his eyes away to the east, where already a few of the earliest stars were showing. With his head held high he spoke as though his voice might pass over the frontier of twilight and be heard by the people of Elfland.

===Chinese===
正诧异间，教士开始吟诵起来，声音在暮色中清朗，他的目光远投向东方，那里已有几颗最早的星子露面。他昂首而语，仿佛他的声音可以越过那暮色的边境，被精灵国的子民听见。

===Original===
“Curst be all wandering things,” he said, “whose place is not upon Earth. Curst be all lights that dwell in fens and in marish places. Their homes are in deeps of the marshes. Let them by no means stir from there until the Last Day. Let them abide in their place and there await damnation.

“Cursed be gnomes, trolls, elves and goblins on land, and all sprites of the water. And fauns be accursed and such as follow Pan. And all that dwell on the heath, being other than beasts or men. Cursed be fairies and all tales told of them, and whatever enchants the meadows before the sun is up, and all fables of doubtful authority, and the legends that men hand down from unhallowed times.

“Cursed be brooms that leave their place by the hearth. Cursed be witches and all manner of witcheries.

“Cursed be toadstool rings and whatever dances within them. And all strange lights, strange songs, strange shadows, or rumours that hint of them, and all doubtful things of the dusk, and the things that ill-instructed children fear, and old wives’ tales and things done o’ midsummer nights; all these be accursed with all that leaneth toward Elfland and all that cometh thence.”

===Chinese===
「凡流荡之物，」他说，「其位不在尘世者，皆当受咒。凡居于泥沼泽国、低湿之地的光，皆当受咒。它们的家园在沼泽的深处。任它们千万不得擅离彼处，直至末日审判。让它们留驻于自己的所在，在那里等待永罚。

「地上诸般侏儒（gnomes）、巨怪（trolls）、精灵（elves）、哥布林（goblins），及水中一切精怪，皆当受咒。牧神之从者萨提儿们（fauns）及一切追随潘神（Pan）之物，皆当受咒。凡栖于荒原之上、非兽非人之物，亦皆当受咒。凡精灵（fairies）及一切述说它们的传说皆当受咒，凡在日出之前施法于草甸之物皆当受咒，及一切来路可疑的寓言，和人众自那不洁之世代流传下来的种种传奇，皆当受咒。

「凡擅离炉边之位的扫帚，皆当受咒。凡女巫（witches）及一切巫术，皆当受咒。

「凡菌蕈之圈及其中所舞之物，皆当受咒。凡一切怪光、怪歌、怪影，或暗指于此的流言，凡暮色中一切可疑之物，凡无知的孩童所畏惧之物，及老妪的闲谈，与仲夏之夜所行之事——凡此种种，连同一切倾心于精灵国之物、一切自彼而来之物，皆当受咒。」

===Original===
Never a lane of that village, never a barn, but a will-o’-the-wisp was dancing nimbly above it; the night was gilded with them. But as the good Freer spoke they backed away from his curses, floating further off as though a light wind blew them, and danced again after drifting a little way. This they did both before and behind him and upon either hand, as he stood there upon the steps of his holy place. So that there was a circle of darkness all round him, and beyond that circle shone the lights of the marshes and Elfland.

And within the dark circle in which the Freer stood making his curses were no unhallowed things, nor were there strangenesses such as come of night, nor whispers from unknown voices, nor sounds of any music blowing here from no haunts of men; but all was orderly and seemly there and no mysteries troubled the quiet except such as have been justly allowed to man.

===Chinese===
村中无论哪一条小巷、哪一座谷仓，上头都有一团鬼火（will-o’-the-wisp）在轻捷地跳跃；那夜里到处被它们镀上了一层金。然而当那位善良的教士诵念之时，它们便从他的咒语前退开，越飘越远，仿佛一阵轻风将它们吹去，飘出一程之后便又跳跃如故。它们在他身前身后、在他左右都这般作态，当他这样立于圣所台阶之上时。于是他的四周便成了一团黑暗的圆，而那圆圈之外，则闪着沼泽与精灵国的光。

在那位教士立而诵咒的黑暗圆圈之内，没有任何不洁之物，也没有夜所生的种种怪异，没有不知何来的低语，也没有从非人众所居之处吹送至此的任何乐音；那里一切井然、得体，除了那公正地赐予人众的之外，没有任何奥秘搅扰那片安宁。

===Original===
And beyond that circle whence so much was beaten back by the bright vehemence of the good man’s curses, the will-o’-the-wisps rioted, and many a strangeness that poured in that night from Elfland, and goblins held high holiday. For word was gone forth in Elfland that pleasant folk had now their dwelling in Erl; and many a thing of fable, many a monster of myth, had crept through that border of twilight and had come into Erl to see. And the light and false but friendly will-o’-the-wisps danced in the haunted air and made them welcome.

And not only the trolls and the will-o’-the-wisps had lured these folk from their fabled land through the seldom-traversed border, but the longings and thoughts of Orion, which by half his lineage were akin to the things of myth and of one race with the monsters of Elfland, were calling to them now. Ever since that day by the frontier when he had hovered between Earth and Elfland he had yearned more and more for his mother; and now, whether he willed it or no, his elfin thoughts were calling their kin that dwelt in the elvish fells; and at that hour when the sound of the horns blew through the frontier of twilight they had come tumbling after it. For elfin thoughts are as much akin to the creatures that dwell in Elfland as goblins are to trolls.

===Chinese===
而在那圈外——如此之多之物被那善人咒语的灼灼烈烈所击退之处——鬼火们在喧嚣纵乐，那一夜里自精灵国涌入的无数怪异也在纵乐，哥布林们大张其乐。因为消息已传遍精灵国：可亲的人众如今已在厄尔安身；于是多少传说之物、多少神话之怪，都从那暮色的边境悄悄爬过，来到厄尔一探究竟。那轻飘而虚妄却友善的鬼火们在闹鬼的空气中起舞，欢迎它们的到来。

引诱这些族类从他们那传说的国度、穿过那罕有人行的边境而来的，并不只是巨怪和鬼火；奥利安的思念与心思，如今也在向它们呼唤——他的血脉有一半与那神话之物同源，与精灵国的怪物同属一族。自从他在边境旁的那一日、在人世与精灵国之间徘徊不定以来，他越来越渴望他的母亲；而如今，不论他是否情愿，他那精灵的思念正在呼唤栖居于精灵群山之中的同类；而当号角之声穿过那暮色的边境吹响的那个时辰，它们便随之纷纷翻涌而来。因为精灵的思念之与栖居于精灵国的造物相亲，正如同哥布林之与巨怪相亲。

===Original===
Within the calm and the dark of the good man’s curses the twelve old men stood silent listening to every word. And the words seemed good to them and soothing and right, for they were overweary of magic.

But beyond the circle of darkness, amidst the glare of the will-o’-the-wisps with which all the night flickered, amidst goblin laughter and the unbridled mirth of the trolls, where old legends seemed alive and the fearfullest fables true; amongst all manner of mysteries, queer sounds, queer shapes, and queer shadows; Orion passed with his hounds, eastwards towards Elfland.

===Chinese===
在那善人咒语的安宁与黑暗之内，十二位老人默然伫立，逐字倾听。那些话在他们听来是好的、抚慰的、正当的，因为他们对魔法已厌倦至极。

但在那黑暗之圈外，在使整夜闪烁不定的鬼火的光芒之中，在哥布林的笑声与巨怪那无羁的欢腾之中，在古老的传说似乎活了过来、最骇人的寓言竟成真实之处；在种种奥秘、怪声、怪形、怪影之间；奥利安带着他的群犬走过，向东，朝精灵国而去。
