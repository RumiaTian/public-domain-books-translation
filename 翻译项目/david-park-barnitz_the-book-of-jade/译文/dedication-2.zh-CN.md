### Dedication / 献词

===Original===
These paltry rhymes, which loftier shall pursue

 Than aught America of high or great

 Hath seen since first began her world-wide state,

 I dedicate, my brother, unto you.

===Chinese===
这些微末的诗句，其志趣所追慕的，

 比自 America 开启她那泱泱大国以来

 所曾有过的任何崇高与伟大都更高远，

 我把它们，我的兄弟，献给你。
