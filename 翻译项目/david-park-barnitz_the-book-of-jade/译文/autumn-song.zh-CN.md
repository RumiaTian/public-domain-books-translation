### Autumn Song / 秋歌

===Original===
Weep, far autumnal skies,

 Shrouded in misty air;

 Weep, for thy solemn dearth,

 And for thy chill despair,

 Earth.

===Chinese===
哭泣吧，遥远的天空，

 笼罩在雾气之中；

 哭泣吧，为你庄严的匮乏，

 为你清冷的绝望，

 大地。

===Original===
O stricken forest-trees,

 Dead leaves that falter down

 Solemnly to your sleep,

 Golden, and red, and brown,

 Weep.

===Chinese===
啊，凋伤的林木，

 枯叶摇摇坠下，

 庄重地落入你们的沉眠，

 金色的、红色的、褐色的，

 哭泣吧。
