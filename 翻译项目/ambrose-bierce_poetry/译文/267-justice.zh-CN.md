## Justice / 正义
===Original===

She jilted me. I madly cried:
“The grave at least can hold her!”
Reflecting then that if she died
’Twould stop her growing older,
I pitilessly sheathed the knife
And sternly sentenced her to life!

===Chinese===

她把我甩了。我发狂地嚎：
"坟墓至少能把她困住牢！"
转念一想：她若一命呜呼，
岂不就此再不能变老——
我于是狠心收刀入鞘，
铁面无私，判她终身活到老！
