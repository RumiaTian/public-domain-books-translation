## Vanished at Cock-Crow / 鸡鸣即逝
===Original===

“I’ve found the secret of your charm,” I said,
Expounding with complacency my guess.
Alas! the charm, even as I named it, fled,
For all its secret was unconsciousness.

===Chinese===

"我可找到你魅力的奥秘了，"我说，
得意洋洋地把我的猜测细陈。
唉！那魅力，就在我道破的当口隐没——
它全部的奥秘，原是那份不自知的本真。
