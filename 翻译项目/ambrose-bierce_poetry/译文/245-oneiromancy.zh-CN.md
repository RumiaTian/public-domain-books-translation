## Oneiromancy / 占梦
===Original===

I fell asleep and dreamed that I
Was flung, like Vulcan, from the sky;
Like him was lamed﻿—another part:
His leg was crippled, and my heart.
I woke in time to see my love
Conceal a letter in her glove.

===Chinese===

我睡熟入梦，梦见自己
像伏尔甘一般被抛下天际；
同他一样落了残疾——只是另一处作痛：
他坏的是腿，我碎的是心胸。
我及时醒转，恰好目睹
我的爱人把一封信塞进手套深处。
