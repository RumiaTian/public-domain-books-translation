## Sepulture / 埋斧
===Original===

“Let’s bury the hatchet,” said Miller to Platt;
And Platt said to Miller: “I’ll gladly do that.”
On its grave, Warner Miller, the grasses grow not,
But the wind in your hair whistles over the spot.

===Chinese===

"把战斧埋了吧，"米勒向普拉特说道；
普拉特回米勒："求之不得，愿意效劳。"
华纳·米勒啊，那战斧的坟头寸草不长，
只有呼啸的风掠过那地方、掠过你头上。
