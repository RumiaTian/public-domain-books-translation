## Conversely / 反之亦然
===Original===

There’s grief in Belgrade, for no crown, it is said,
Is found for King Peter in all of the town.
How sad that he’s lacking a crown for his head!
How sweet were he lacking a head for his crown!

===Chinese===

贝尔格莱德一片哀愁——据传，
全城上下竟寻不出一顶王冠
给彼得国王戴。头颅上缺顶王冠，多可哀！
王冠下若能缺个头颅，那才叫痛快！
