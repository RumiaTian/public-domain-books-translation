## Back to Nature / 回归自然
===Original===

Nathaniel, Julian, Hildegardy!
Sure the stock is far from hardy,
And the name once heard with awe
Now provokes the loud guffaw﻿—
“Hawthorne” in the memory dear,
“Haw-haw-hawthorne” in the ear!

===Chinese===

纳撒尼尔、朱利安、希尔迪加蒂！
这一门血胤，实在谈不上争气，
这个曾令人敬畏的姓氏，
如今只招来哄堂大笑不止——
记忆里，是那可敬的"霍桑"；
耳朵里，只剩"哈-哈-霍桑"！
