## Psychographs / 心灵速写
===Original===

Says Gerald Massey: “When I write, a band
Of souls of the departed guides my hand.”
How strange that poems cumbering our shelves,
Penned by immortal parts, have none themselves!

===Chinese===

杰拉德·梅西说："我一动笔，
便有一队亡灵指引我的手迹。"
奇哉怪哉！那些塞满我们书架的诗章，
出自"不朽灵魂"之手，却不见半缕魂光！
