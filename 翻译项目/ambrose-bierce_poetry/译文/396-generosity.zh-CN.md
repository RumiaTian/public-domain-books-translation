## Generosity / 慷慨
===Original===

By seven brave poems the Mikado shows
His royal fitness for the field of prose.
Of bold, bad bards to crown him brother-chief
Dick Watson Gilder spares one laurel leaf.

===Chinese===

凭七首勇敢的诗，日本天皇
证明了自己跻身散文之林的皇家资格。
在拥立他为狂野诗人联盟大首领时，
迪克·沃森·吉尔德（Dick Watson Gilder）分出了一片月桂叶。
