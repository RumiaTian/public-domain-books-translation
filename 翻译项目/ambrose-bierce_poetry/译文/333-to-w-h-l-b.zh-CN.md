## To W. H. L. B. / 致 W. H. L. B.
===Original===

Refrain, dull orator, from speaking out,
For silence deepens when you raise the shout;
But when you hold your tongue we hear, at least,
Your noise in mastering that little beast.

===Chinese===

收声吧，乏味的演说家，别再高谈，
因为你一呐喊，四下反倒更寂静；
可你一旦管住舌头，我们至少还能听见
你驯服那条小兽时费的劲响。
