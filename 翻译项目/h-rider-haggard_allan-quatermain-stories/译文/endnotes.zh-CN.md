## Endnotes / 尾注

===Original===
1. *Isanusi*, witch-finder. ↩︎
2. This of course was written before Mr. Quatermain’s account of the adventures in the newly-discovered country of Zu-Vendis of himself, Sir Henry Curtis, and Capt. John Good had been received in England. —﻿Editor ↩︎
3. I have known a lion carry a two-year-old ox over a stone wall four feet high in this fashion, and a mile away into the bush beyond. He was subsequently poisoned by strychnine put into the carcass of the ox, and I still have his claws. —﻿Editor ↩︎

===Chinese===
1. 伊萨努西（Isanusi），寻巫师。 ↩︎
2. 这段文字当然写于夸特梅因先生关于他本人、亨利·柯蒂斯爵士与约翰·古德船长在新发现之国祖-文迪斯（Zu-Vendis）历险的自述传抵英国之前。——编者 ↩︎
3. 我就见过一头狮子这样叼着一头两岁的牛越过四英尺高的石墙，跑出一英里开外，进了那边的灌木丛。后来它被涂在牛尸上的士的宁毒死了，它的爪子我至今还留着。——编者 ↩︎
