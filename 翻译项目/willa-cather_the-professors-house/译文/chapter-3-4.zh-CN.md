### IV / IV

===Original===
The fall term of the university opened, and now the Professor went to his lectures instead of to the lake. He supposed he did his work; he heard no complaints from his assistants, and the students seemed interested. He found, however, that he wasn’t willing to take the trouble to learn the names of several hundred new students. It wasn’t worth while. He felt that his relations with them would be of short duration.

===Chinese===
大学的秋季学期开学了，如今教授是去上课，而不是去湖边了。他想必还是把活儿干了；他没听到助教们有什么怨言，学生们看上去也颇有兴致。然而他发觉，自己并不情愿费那个劲去记下几百个新生的名字。不值得。他觉得自己和他们之间的缘分不会长久。

===Original===
The McGregors got home from their vacation in Oregon, and Scott was much amused to find the Professor so doggedly anchored in the old house.

“It never struck me, Doctor, that you were a man who would be keeping up two establishments. They’ll be coming home pretty soon, and then you’ll have to decide where you are going to live.”

“I can’t leave my study, Scott. That’s flat.”

“Don’t, then! Darn it, you’ve a right to two houses if you want ’em.”

===Chinese===
麦格雷戈一家从俄勒冈度假回来了，斯科特见教授这样死死地守在老房子里，觉得十分好笑。

「我可真没想到，博士，你是那种会同时维持两处家宅的人。他们过不了多久就要回来了，到那时候，你总得决定自己到底住哪儿。」

「我离不开我的书房，斯科特。就这么回事。」

「那就别搬嘛！见鬼，你要是想要两处房子，你完全有这个权利。」

===Original===
This encounter took place on the street in front of the house. The Professor went wearily upstairs and lay down on the couch, his refuge from this ever-increasing fatigue. He really didn’t see what he was going to do about the matter of domicile. He couldn’t make himself believe that he was ever going to live in the new house again. He didn’t belong there. He remembered some lines of a translation from the Norse he used to read long ago in one of his mother’s few books, a little two-volume Ticknor and Fields edition of Longfellow, in blue and gold, that used to lie on the parlour table:

===Chinese===
这番碰面是在屋前的街上发生的。教授疲惫不堪地上楼，在长沙发上躺了下来——那沙发是他躲避这日甚一日疲倦的栖身之所。他实在看不出，关于住处这桩事自己究竟该怎么办。他没法让自己相信，自己还会再去新宅子里住。他不属于那里。他想起了从前译自古北欧诗的几行诗，那是很久以前他常读的，就在他母亲那寥寥几本书里的一本中——提克诺与菲尔兹出版公司出的那套朗费罗诗集小两卷本，蓝底金字，从前总是搁在客厅的桌子上：

===Original===
> For thee a house was built
 Ere thou wast born;
 For thee a mould was made
 Ere thou of woman earnest.

===Chinese===
> 早在你降生之前，
 一所房子已为你建成；
 早在你出自妇人之前，
 一副模子已为你造就。

===Original===
Lying on his old couch, he could almost believe himself in that house already. The sagging springs were like the sham upholstery that is put in coffins. Just the equivocal American way of dealing with serious facts, he reflected. Why pretend that it is possible to soften that last hard bed?

===Chinese===
躺在他这张旧沙发上，他几乎可以相信自己已经置身那所房子里了。那些塌陷的弹簧，活像棺材里衬的那种假软垫。这就是美国人含糊其辞地应付严肃事实的法子，他想。何必装作那最后一张硬床也能被软化呢？

===Original===
He could remember a time when the loneliness of death had terrified him, when the idea of it was insupportable. He used to feel that if his wife could but lie in the same coffin with him, his body would not be so insensible that the nearness of hers would not give it comfort. But now he thought of eternal solitude with gratefulness; as a release from every obligation, from every form of effort. It was the Truth.

===Chinese===
他还记得有过那样一段时日，死亡的那种孤独曾叫他恐惧万分，那一念简直不堪承受。他从前总觉得，要是妻子能和他同卧一棺，他的身子总不至麻木到感觉不到她的贴近、从中得到几分安慰。可如今他想到永恒的寂灭，心里竟怀着感激；把它当作从一切义务、一切形式的劳碌之中解脱出来。那便是真相。

===Original===
One morning, just as St. Peter was leaving the house to go to his classroom, the postman handed him two letters, one addressed in Lillian’s hand and one in Louie’s. He put them into his pocket. The feel of them disturbed him. They were of a suspicious thinness—as if they didn’t contain amusing gossip, but announced sudden decisions. He set off down the street, sniffing the lake-cooled morning air and trying to overcome a feeling of nervous dread.

===Chinese===
一天早晨，圣彼得正要出门去教室，邮差递给他两封信，一封是莉莲的笔迹，一封是路易的。他把信揣进口袋。信封的触感让他心神不宁。它们薄得可疑——仿佛里头装的不是什么有趣的家长里短，而是宣布某个突然的决定。他沿街走去，一边嗅着被湖水沁凉的晨风，一边想压下心头那阵紧张的惧意。

===Original===
All the morning those two letters lay in his breast pocket. Though they were so light, their effect was to make him drop his shoulders and look woefully tired. The weather, too, had changed, come on suddenly hot and sultry at noon, as if getting ready for a storm. When his classes were over and he was back in his study again, St. Peter felt no interest in lunch. He took out the two letters and ripped them open with his forefinger to have it over. Yes, all plans were changed, and by the happiest of expectations. The family were hurrying home to prepare for the advent of a young Marsellus. They would sail on the sixteenth, on the *Berengaria*.

===Chinese===
整整一上午，那两封信就揣在他胸前的口袋里。它们虽轻，却压得他肩膀耷拉下来，一脸疲惫不堪的神气。天气也变了，正午时分骤然燠热窒闷起来，像是在酝酿一场暴雨。等他上完课回到书房，圣彼得一点吃午饭的心思也没有。他掏出那两封信，用食指一气划开封口，只想早点了结。果然，所有的盘算都变了，而且是因为一桩再可喜不过的期待。一家人正急着赶回家，好为小马塞勒斯的降生做准备。他们将于十六日登船，搭的是「伯伦加丽亚号」。

===Original===
Lillian added a postscript to the effect that by this same mail she was getting off a letter to Augusta, who would come to him for the keys of the new house. She would be the best person to open the house and arrange to have the cleaning done. She would take it entirely off his shoulders and see that everything was properly put in order.

===Chinese===
莉莲还附了一句，说是趁这同一班邮件，她也给奥古斯塔发了一封信，她会来向他取新宅的钥匙。要开宅子、张罗清扫，奥古斯塔是最合适不过的人选。她会把这副担子整个从他肩头接过去，把一切都料理得妥妥帖帖。

===Original===
They were sailing on the sixteenth, and this was the seventeenth; they were already on the water. The *Berengaria* was a five-day boat. St. Peter caught up his hat and light overcoat and started down the stairs. Halfway down, he stopped short, went back to his study, and softly shut the door behind him. He sat down, forgetting to take off his overcoat, though the afternoon was so hot and his face was damp with perspiration. He sat motionless, breathing unevenly, one dark hand lying clenched on his writing-table. There must, he was repeating to himself, there must be some way in which a man who had always tried to live up to his responsibilities could, when the hour of desperation came, avoid meeting his own family.

===Chinese===
他们是十六日启程的，而今天已是十七日；他们早已在海上。「伯伦加丽亚号」是五天的航程。圣彼得抓起帽子和薄外套，便往楼下走。走到一半，他猛地煞住脚，折回书房，轻轻把门带上。他坐下来，竟忘了脱外套，尽管那个下午酷热难当，他脸上早已汗湿。他一动不动地坐着，呼吸不匀，一只黝黑的手攥成拳，搁在书桌上。他一遍遍地在心里念叨：一定有的，一定有某种办法，能让一个一向尽力尽责的人，在绝望来临的那一刻，得以避开他自己的家人。

===Original===
He loved his family, he would make any sacrifice for them, but just now he couldn’t live with them. He must be alone. That was more necessary to him than anything had ever been, more necessary, even, than his marriage had been in his vehement youth. He could not live with his family again—not even with Lillian. Especially not with Lillian! Her nature was intense and positive; it was like a chiselled surface, a die, a stamp upon which he could not be beaten out any longer. If her character were reduced to an heraldic device, it would be a hand (a beautiful hand) holding flaming arrows—the shafts of her violent loves and hates, her clear-cut ambitions.

===Chinese===
他爱他的家人，他愿为他们作出任何牺牲，可此时此刻他没法和他们住在一起。他必须独处。这于他比任何事都更要紧，甚至比他当年那热烈的青春里婚姻本身还更要紧。他再也不能和家人同住了——哪怕是和莉莲也不行。尤其是不能和莉莲！她的天性浓烈而鲜明；那就像一处凿刻过的平面，一枚钢模，一方印章，他已经再也无法被放在上面捶打成形了。倘若把她的性格化为一枚纹章，那该是一只手（一只美丽的手）擎着燃烧的箭矢——那便是她那暴烈的爱与恨、她那轮廓分明的雄心的箭。

===Original===
“In great misfortunes,” he told himself, “people want to be alone. They have a right to be. And the misfortunes that occur within one are the greatest. Surely the saddest thing in the world is falling out of love—if once one has ever fallen in.”

===Chinese===
「人在大不幸之中，」他对自己说，「总想独处。他们有这个权利。而发生在一个人内心深处的不幸，才是最大的不幸。这世上最悲哀的事，莫过于不再爱了——倘若曾经爱过的话。」

===Original===
Falling out, for him, seemed to mean falling out of all domestic and social relations, out of his place in the human family, indeed.

===Chinese===
对于他而言，这种「不再」，似乎意味着从一切家庭与社会的关系里脱落出去，从他在人类这个大家庭里的位置上脱落出去。

===Original===
St. Peter did not go out of the house that afternoon. He did not leave his study. He sat at his desk with bent head, reviewing his life, trying to see where he had made his mistake, to account for the fact that he now wanted to run away from everything he had intensely cared for.

===Chinese===
那天下午圣彼得没有出门。他没有离开书房。他伏在书桌前，低着头，回溯自己的一生，想看清自己究竟在哪一步上走错了，想解释清楚这样一个事实：他如今竟想逃离他曾深爱过的一切。

===Original===
Late in the afternoon the heaviness of the air in the room drove him to the window. He saw that a storm was coming on. Great orange and purple clouds were blowing up from the lake, and the pine-trees over about the Physics laboratory were blacker than cypresses and looked contracted, as if they were awaiting something. The rain broke, and it turned cold.

===Chinese===
临近傍晚，屋里沉重的气压把他逼到了窗前。他看见一场风暴就要来了。大团橘黄与紫色的云从湖上翻卷而来，物理实验室那一带的松树黑得赛过柏树，看上去蜷缩着，仿佛在等候着什么。雨落了下来，天凉了。

===Original===
The rainstorm was over in half an hour, but a heavy blow had set in for the night. The wind would be a protection, he thought. Even Augusta would hardly come plodding up the stairs tonight It seemed strange to be dreading Augusta, but just now he did dread her. He believed he was safe, for tonight. Though it was only five o’clock, the sky was black, and the room was dusky and chilly. He lit the stove and lay down on the couch. The fire made a flickering pattern of light on the wall. He lay watching it, vacantly; without meaning to, he fell asleep. For a long while he slept deeply and peacefully. Then the wind, increasing in violence, disturbed him. He began to be aware of noises—things banging and slamming about. He turned over on his back and slept deeper still.

===Chinese===
暴雨半个钟头就过了，可一夜的大风却刮了起来。这样的风会是一道屏障，他想。今夜就连奥古斯塔也多半不会拖着步子爬上楼来了。怕起奥古斯塔来，似乎很奇怪，可此刻他确确实实怕她来。他相信自己今夜是安全的。虽才五点钟，天已漆黑，屋里又暗又冷。他生了炉子，在沙发上躺下。炉火在墙上投下明明灭灭的光影。他茫然地躺着看那光影；不知不觉便睡着了。有很长一段时间，他睡得又沉又安稳。后来风势愈猛，惊动了他。他开始觉察到种种声响——是什么东西乒乒乓乓地乱响。他翻了个身，仰面朝天，睡得更沉了。

---

===Original===
When St. Peter at last awoke, the room was pitch-black and full of gas. He was cold and numb, felt sick and rather dazed. The long-anticipated coincidence had happened, he realized. The storm had blown the stove out and the window shut. The thing to do was to get up and open the window. But suppose he did not get up—? How far was a man required to exert himself against accident? How would such a case be decided under English law? He hadn’t lifted his hand against himself—was he required to lift it for himself?

===Chinese===
等到圣彼得终于醒来，屋里漆黑一片，满是煤气。他又冷又麻，恶心想吐，脑子昏昏沉沉。他意识到，那桩他早有预感的巧合终于发生了。风暴把炉子吹熄了，又把窗户吹得合上了。该做的事是爬起来去开窗。可要是他偏不起来呢——？一个人在意外面前，到底被要求使多大力气去自救？这样的案子，依照英国法会怎么判？他并没有举手加害自己——难道他被要求举手救助自己吗？
