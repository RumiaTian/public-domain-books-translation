### VII / VII

===Original===
The next morning the whinnying of my saddle-horse in the shed roused me. I took him down to the foot of the trail where I’d left my trunk, and packed my things up to the cabin on his back. I sat up late that night, waiting for Blake, though I knew he wouldn’t come. A few days later I rode into Tarpin for news of him. Bill Hook showed me Roddy’s horse. He had sold him to the barn for sixty dollars. The stationmaster told me Blake had bought a ticket to Winslow, Arizona. I wired the stationmaster and the dispatcher at Winslow, but they could give me no information. Father Duchene came along, on his rounds, and I told him the whole story.

===Chinese===
第二天早上，棚子里我那匹坐骑的嘶鸣声把我惊醒了。我把它牵到小径底下我搁箱子的地方，把我的物件驮在它背上，运上小屋。那天夜里我坐到很晚，等布雷克回来，虽然明知他不会来。过了几天，我骑马去塔平打听他的消息。比尔·胡克让我看了罗迪那匹马。他已经以六十块钱的价钱把它卖给了马房。站长告诉我，布雷克买了一张去亚利桑那州温斯洛（Winslow）的车票。我给温斯洛的站长和调度员都拍了电报，可他们都给不出什么消息。迪谢纳神父巡游路过，我把事情原原本本告诉了他。

===Original===
He thought Blake would come back sometime, that I’d only miss him if I went out to look for him. He advised me to stay on the mesa that summer and get ahead with my studies, work up my Spanish grammar and my Latin. He had friends all along the Santa Fe, and he was sure we could catch Blake by advertising in the local papers along the road; Albuquerque, Winslow, Flagstaff, Williams, Los Angeles. After a few days with him, I went back to the mesa to wait.

===Chinese===
他觉得布雷克迟早会回来，我要是出去找他，反倒会错过他。他劝我那年夏天就留在台地上，把功课赶一赶，把西班牙语语法和拉丁文好好做一做。他在圣菲铁路沿线到处都有朋友，他肯定我们只要在那条路沿线的地方报纸上登广告，就一定能逮到布雷克；阿尔伯克基、温斯洛、弗拉格斯塔夫、威廉斯、洛杉矶。我在他那儿住了几天，就回台地上等着。

===Original===
I’ll never forget the night I got back. I crossed the river an hour before sunset and hobbled my horse in the wide bottom of Cow Canyon. The moon was up, though the sun hadn’t set, and it had that glittering silveriness the early stars have in high altitudes. The heavenly bodies look so much more remote from the bottom of a deep canyon than they do from the level. The climb of the walls helps out the eye, somehow. I lay down on a solitary rock that was like an island in the bottom of the valley, and looked up. The grey sagebrush and the blue-grey rock around me were already in shadow, but high above me the canyon walls were dyed flame-colour with the sunset, and the Cliff City lay in a gold haze against its dark cavern. In a few minutes it, too, was grey, and only the rim rock at the top held the red light. When that was gone, I could still see the copper glow in the piñons along the edge of the top ledges. The arc of sky over the canyon was silvery blue, with its pale yellow moon, and presently stars shivered into it, like crystals dropped into perfectly clear water.

===Chinese===
我永远忘不了回去那晚的情景。我在日落前一个钟头过了河，把马绊在牛峡那宽阔的谷底里。月亮已经升起来了，尽管太阳还没落，它带着一种高海拔处初升星辰才有的那种闪闪发亮的银辉。从一道深峡谷的谷底望上去，那些天体比从平地上望要显得遥远得多。峡谷两侧峭壁的节节攀升，不知怎的，倒帮了眼睛的忙。我在一块孤零零的岩石上躺下来，那岩石好比谷底的一座小岛，我仰面望着。我周围灰色的山艾丛和蓝灰色的岩石已经笼在阴影里了，可是在我头顶高处，峡谷的石壁还染着落日的火红色，而崖城笼在一层金黄的薄霭里，衬着它背后幽暗的岩洞。过了几分钟，连这也变成了灰色，只有崖顶的檐岩还燃着红光。等那红光也熄了，我还能看见崖顶边缘那些矮松里泛着的铜色微光。峡谷上空那一弯天穹是银蓝色的，挂着一轮淡黄的月亮，不一会儿，星辰便纷纷抖落进天幕，就像水晶投进一澄到底的清水里那般。

===Original===
I remember these things, because, in a sense, that was the first night I was ever really on the mesa at all—the first night that all of me was there. This was the first time I ever saw it as a whole. It all came together in my understanding, as a series of experiments do when you begin to see where they are leading. Something had happened in me that made it possible for me to coordinate and simplify, and that process, going on in my mind, brought with it great happiness. It was possession. The excitement of my first discovery was a very pale feeling compared to this one. For me the mesa was no longer an adventure, but a religious emotion. I had read of filial piety in the Latin poets, and I knew that was what I felt for this place. It had formerly been mixed up with other motives; but now that they were gone, I had my happiness unalloyed.

===Chinese===
我记得这些事，因为从某种意义上说，那才是我头一回真真正正待在台地上——头一回我整个的人都到了那儿。这也是我头一回把它当作一个整体来看。这一切在我心中渐渐归拢到了一起，就像一连串实验，等你开始看清它们要引向何处时那样。我内心里起了某种变化，使我得以梳理、提纯，而这个过程在我心头进行着，随之带来了莫大的幸福。那是一种占有。我初次发现它时的那份激动，和这一刻比起来，实在是一种很淡薄的感觉。对我来说，台地不再是一场冒险，而成了一种宗教般的情感。我在拉丁诗人的作品里读到过孝敬这种情感，我知道那正是我对这个地方所怀的心情。从前这种情感还掺杂着别的动机；可如今那些都消失了，我便有了我那毫无掺杂的幸福。

===Original===
What that night began lasted all summer. I stayed on the mesa until November. It was the first time I’d ever studied methodically, or intelligently. I got the better of the Spanish grammar and read the twelve books of the *Aeneid*. I studied in the morning, and in the afternoon I worked at clearing away the mess the German had made in packing—tidying up the ruins to wait another hundred years, maybe, for the right explorer. I can scarcely hope that life will give me another summer like that one. It was my high tide. Every morning, when the sun’s rays first hit the mesa top, while the rest of the world was in shadow, I wakened with the feeling that I had found everything, instead of having lost everything. Nothing tired me. Up there alone, a close neighbour to the sun, I seemed to get the solar energy in some direct way. And at night, when I watched it drop down behind the edge of the plain below me, I used to feel that I couldn’t have borne another hour of that consuming light, that I was full to the brim, and needed dark and sleep.

===Chinese===
那一夜所开启的东西，持续了整整一个夏天。我在台地上一直待到十一月。这是我头一回有条理地、用心地读书。我拿下了西班牙语语法，读完了十二卷《埃涅阿斯纪》。我上午读书，下午就去清理那个德国人打包时弄出的那堆乱摊子——把废墟收拾停当，好让它们也许再等上一百年，等来真正配得上它们的探险家。我几乎不敢指望此生还能再有一个那样的夏天。那是我生命里的高潮。每天清晨，当太阳的光芒头一次落到台地顶上，而世上其余的一切还笼罩在阴影里时，我醒来，只觉得我已经寻得了一切，而不是失去了一切。没有什么能叫我疲倦。独自在那高旷之地，与太阳比邻而居，我仿佛以一种直截了当的方式汲取着太阳的能量。到了夜里，当我望着它沉入我脚下平原的边缘之下时，我常会觉得自己再也承受不住那耗人的光芒再多一个钟头，觉得自己已经满溢到唇边，需要黑暗与睡眠。

===Original===
All that summer, I never went up to the Eagle’s Nest to get my diary—indeed, it’s probably there yet. I didn’t feel the need of that record. It would have been going backward. I didn’t want to go back and unravel things step by step. Perhaps I was afraid that I would lose the whole in the parts. At any rate, I didn’t go for my record.

===Chinese===
整整那一夏，我都没上鹰巢去取我的日记——说真的，那本子多半至今还在那儿呢。我觉得不需要那份记录。那只会是一种倒退。我不想回过头去，把事情一桩一桩地再拆解开。也许我是怕自己会在那些细枝末节里失掉了整体。无论如何，我没去取我的记录。

===Original===
During those months I didn’t worry much about poor Roddy. I told myself the advertisements would surely get him—I knew his habit of reading newspapers. There are times when one’s vitality is too high to be clouded, too elastic to stay down. Hurrying from my cabin in the morning to the spot in the Cliff City where I studied under a cedar, I used to be frightened at my own heartlessness. But the feel of the narrow moccasin-worn trail in the flat rock made my feet glad, like a good taste in the mouth, and I’d forget all about Blake without knowing it. I found I was reading too fast; so I began to commit long passages of Virgil to memory—if it hadn’t been for that, I might have forgotten how to use my voice, or gone to talking to myself. When I look into the *Aeneid* now, I can always see two pictures: the one on the page, and another behind that: blue and purple rocks and yellow-green piñons with flat tops, little clustered houses clinging together for protection, a rude tower rising in their midst, rising strong, with calmness and courage—behind it a dark grotto, in its depths a crystal spring.

===Chinese===
那几个月里，我并没有怎么为可怜的罗迪发愁。我对自己说，那些广告一定能把他找回来——我知道他爱看报纸。一个人生命的元气有时高扬得不容任何阴霾笼罩，太有弹性，压不下去。每天早上我从小屋匆匆赶到崖城里我读书的那棵雪松底下时，我常常会被自己的没心没肺吓一跳。可那平整岩石上被鹿皮鞋踩出来的窄径，踩在脚底有一种叫人欢喜的感觉，就像嘴里尝到的一味好滋味，我便不知不觉地把布雷克忘得一干二净。我发现自己读得太快了；于是我开始大段大段地背诵维吉尔——要不是那样，我或许会忘了怎样使用自己的嗓音，或者干脆自言自语起来。如今我再翻开《埃涅阿斯纪》，总能看见两幅图景：一幅在书页上，另一幅在那背后：蓝紫色的岩石，黄绿色的平顶矮松，一簇簇紧紧挤在一起以求庇护的小屋，一座粗朴的塔楼矗立其间，坚强地矗立着，带着沉静与勇气——塔楼背后是一个幽暗的石洞，洞的最深处有一眼水晶般的泉水。

===Original===
Happiness is something one can’t explain. You must take my word for it. Troubles enough came afterward, but there was that summer, high and blue, a life in itself.

===Chinese===
幸福这东西是没法子解释的。你只能信我的话。后来的烦恼够多的了，可有那样一个夏天，高旷、湛蓝，本身就足以抵得上一生。

===Original===
Next winter I went back to Pardee and stayed with the O’Briens again, working on the section and studying with Father Duchene and trying to get some word of Blake. Now that I was back on the railroad, I thought I couldn’t fail to find him. I went out to Winslow and to Williams, and I questioned the railroad men. We advertised for him in every possible way, had all the Santa Fe operatives and the police and the Catholic missionaries on the watch for him, offered a thousand dollars reward for whoever found him. But it came to nothing. Father Duchene and our friends down there are still looking. But the older I grow, the more I understand what it was I did that night on the mesa. Anyone who requites faith and friendship as I did, will have to pay for it. I’m not very sanguine about good fortune for myself. I’ll be called to account when I least expect it.

===Chinese===
第二年冬天，我回到帕迪，又住在奥布赖恩家，在工段上做工，跟迪谢纳神父读书，还想方设法打听布雷克的下落。既然我又回到了铁路上，我想我总不至于找不到他。我去了温斯洛，去了威廉斯，向那些铁路上的人打听。我们想尽了一切办法登广告找他，把圣菲铁路沿线所有的员工、警察，还有天主教传教士都动员起来留意他，还悬赏一千块钱给找到他的人。可这一切都落了空。迪谢纳神父和我们南边的朋友至今还在找。只是我年纪越长，就越明白那天夜里我在台地上所做的事究竟意味着什么。谁要是像我那样辜负了信赖与情谊，总得为此付出代价。我对自己的福分并不怎么乐观。迟早有一天，会在我最意想不到的时候，被找去清账的。

===Original===
In the spring, just a year after I quarrelled with Roddy, I landed here and walked into your garden, and the rest you know.

===Chinese===
到了春天，就在我和罗迪闹翻的整整一年之后，我到了这儿，走进了你家的花园，往后的事你都知道了。
