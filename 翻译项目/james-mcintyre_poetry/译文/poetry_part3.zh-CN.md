## Witch Stone / 女巫之石

===Original===
At Forres is a large round stone,
A relic of the days by gone;
For here there were two witches burned,
Underneath their ashes urned.
===
===Chinese===
福里斯镇有块大圆石，
乃是往昔岁月的遗物；
此处曾将两名女巫焚烧，
灰烬之下化为尘土。
===Original===
A man with veneration small
Broke stone and built it in his wall,
But the authorities of town
Made him full quickly pull them down.
===
===Chinese===
有个不敬之人不敬畏，
拆石砌入自家墙壁，
但镇上当权者不容此为，
命他速速将其拆除复归。
===Original===
Replace each piece, and it environ
With large bars of good Scottish iron;
May fine old town thrive and adorn
The beauteous banks of the Findhorn.
===
===Chinese===
将每块石头归位还原，
以优质苏格兰铁条围护；
愿这座美好的古镇繁盛兴旺，
装点芬德霍恩河美丽河岸。
===Original===
Great refulgent electric light.
But after all, 'tis children's play,
Compared with the great orb of day,
Or when the moon it doth shine bright,
And stars do beautify the night.
===
===Chinese===
灿烂夺目的电灯光芒。
但说到底不过是儿戏一场，
比起那伟大的日之圆轮，
抑或皓月当空明亮时，
繁星点缀夜色之美时。

## Lines Read at a Ladies' Aid / 妇女援助会上朗诵的诗

===Original===

我们将此称作妇女援助会。

To attend this pleasant social,

===
===Chinese===

===Chinese===
妇人、秀丽的少女

而今我们结束这段短歌，
期盼与诸位再度相会。

## Salford Parsonage / 索尔福德牧师住宅

Lines delivered at housewarming of Salford Parsonage.
索尔福德牧师住宅乔迁宴上朗诵之诗。

===Original===
Your pastor's fame first got abroad
By his success on Culloden Road;
He filled the church so that the fold
No longer it the flock would hold.
===
===Chinese===
你们牧师的名声初扬四方，
源于他在科洛登路上的成功；
他把教堂挤得满满当当，
羊圈已容不下那成群的信众。
===Original===
But soon a larger church did rise,
With fine neat tower points to the skies;
When you o'erwhelmed with ruin dire
Did lose your parsonage by fire.
===
===Chinese===
但不久一座更大的教堂拔地而起，
精美的塔楼直指苍穹；
当你们遭遇可怕的毁灭，
因大火失去了牧师住宅。
===Original===
To his call with generous bounty
You built best parsonage in county,
But some good people in the town
At this idea they might frown.
===
===Chinese===
响应他的号召，慷慨解囊，
你们建成了全县最好的牧师住宅，
但镇上一些善良的人们
对这想法或许会皱眉不满。
===Original===
Unless we made this correction:
The best in a rural section;
Your pastor he doth vigorous push,
He's not afraid to enter bush.
===
===Chinese===
除非我们做此更正：
是乡间地区最好的住宅；
你们的牧师干劲十足，
他不惧深入荒野丛林。
===Original===
And 'tis a fact there's very few
With him can stick of timber hew;
Ready for to superintend,
Or a helping hand to lend.
===
===Chinese===
事实是很少有人
能像他那样伐木砍柴；
他既可亲自监督，
也乐于伸出援手。
===Original===
When bold man's wanted at the front,
You've but to call on Reverend Hunt;
In pulpit he conviction darts,
And points the way to troubled hearts.
===
===Chinese===
当需要勇者冲锋在前，
你们只需召唤亨特牧师（Reverend Hunt）；
他在讲坛上掷地有声，
为忧愁的心灵指明方向。
===Original===
But solemn thoughts we won't employ,
As this is all a scene of joy;
Maids and matrons all charming
Do grace this pleasant housewarming.
===
===Chinese===
但我们不谈庄重之事，
因为此刻尽是欢乐的场面；
迷人的少女与妇人们
为这愉快的乔迁宴增光添彩。
===Original===
Here people come from near and far
To purchase goods at this bazar;
And all is happiness and glee
At festival of this soirée.
===
===Chinese===
人们从四面八方赶来，
在这义卖会上购买商品；
一切都洋溢着幸福与欢愉，
在这晚会的节日庆典上。

## Transformation Scene / 蜕变

A plain building was removed to a pleasant grove, and in a few weeks it was transformed into the neatest frame building in town.
一座朴素的建筑被移至宜人的小树林，数周之内便蜕变为镇上最精致的木构建筑。

===Original===
As westward we of late did rove
We beheld in the maple grove
An old church now owned by Peter;
In town there is no house looks neater,
For those bay windows do look sweeter
Than anything in prose or meter.
===
===Chinese===
我们最近向西漫步时，
在枫树林中望见
一座旧教堂现归彼得所有；
镇上没有比它更整洁的房屋，
那些凸窗看起来如此甜美，
胜过一切散文或诗句。

## Lines on London / 伦敦吟

Uniformed Encampment victorious at St. Thomas and at the great Forester's demonstration in London, August, 1884.
统一着装的露营队在圣托马斯及1884年8月伦敦大型护林员集会上大获全胜。

===Original===
They once in wilderness did ride
On beast with horn and shaggy hide—
A savage goat or unicorn,
But now parade in uniform;
As gay as ancient Knight or Lord,
With their grand plumes, and belt and swords.
===
===Chinese===
他们曾在荒野中骑行，
骑着长角披毛的野兽——
凶猛的山羊或独角兽，
但如今身着制服列队游行；
如古代骑士或贵族般华丽，
佩戴华丽的羽饰、腰带与佩剑。
===Original===
Their graceful movements at their drill
Doth all with admiration fill,
And the chief glory it was won
By the encampment of London;
For they at every tournament
For drill are the chief ornament.
===
===Chinese===
他们操练时优雅的动作，
令所有人满怀敬佩，
而那最高的荣耀
由伦敦露营队赢得；
因为每次比赛中，
他们的队列操练都是最耀眼的点缀。
===Original===
So skillfully they sword do wield,
Victorious on every field;
For victory's graven on their shield
Doth make their rivals for to yield;
Drill companies they out manoeuvre
From Newfoundland to Vancouver.
===
===Chinese===
他们舞剑如此娴熟，
在每片战场上都凯旋而归；
胜利铭刻在他们的盾牌上，
迫使对手俯首称臣；
从纽芬兰到温哥华，
他们的操练远胜所有队伍。

===Original===
It is folly now to aim
Or to seek for distant fame,
But rest content if we can claim
Something of a local name
Oh the pleasant banks of Thame,
Because in simple strains we sung
The glories of this country young.
===
===Chinese===
如今若还奢望
或追寻远方的名声，
实属愚蠢，

## Canada Before the Confederation of the Provinces / 省际联邦之前的加拿大

===Original===
Canadian provinces they lay
Divided by river and by bay,
Many a separate division,
Among them there was no cohesion.
===
===Chinese===
加拿大的各省割据一方，
被河流与海湾所分割，
各自为政互不相属，
彼此之间毫无凝聚力。

But statesmen saw that a great nation
Could be formed by federation,
And soon they led public opinion
To favor forming this Dominion.
===
===Chinese===
但政治家们看到一个伟大的国家
可以通过联邦而诞生，
于是很快他们引导舆论，
支持建立这个自治领。
===Original===
Northwest with its streams and fountains,
With sources in the Rocky Mountains,
It was all a great mystery,
Hunting for furs its history.
===
===Chinese===
西北地区有溪流与泉源，
源头在落基山脉之中，
那里曾是一片巨大的谜，
猎取毛皮便是它的历史。
===Original===
Though Northwest is filling slow
Yet soon there will be mighty flow,
Millions to Northwest will hurry
In last decade of century.
===
===Chinese===
虽然西北人口增长缓慢，
但很快将会有大潮涌来，
数百万人将涌向西北，
在本世纪最后的十年间。
===Original===
For therein is an opening grand
In great fertile prairie land,
For there the choicest wheat it grows
Near where the Saskatchewan flows.
===
===Chinese===
因为那里有广阔的天地，
在肥沃的大草原上，
最上等的小麦在那里生长，
就在萨斯喀彻温河（Saskatchewan）流淌之处。
===Original===
And on many a river's branch
There is found great grazing ranch,
Favoured districts therein abound
Where cattle graze all the year round.
===
===Chinese===
在许多河流的支流旁，
有广袤的牧场，
其间遍布富饶的地区，
牛群四季在此放牧。
===Original===
Protected from the stormy blast
By the Rocky Mountains vast,
Through canon blows no storm terrific,
But balmy breezes from Pacific.
===
===Chinese===
有广袤的落基山脉
为其挡住狂暴的风暴，
峡谷中不闻恐怖的暴风，
唯有太平洋吹来的和煦微风。

## Canada's Future / 加拿大的未来

===Original===
Canada is a young giant,
Has not yet acquired its strength,
On the arts of peace reliant,
Throughout its vast breadth and length.
===
===Chinese===
加拿大是一个年轻的巨人，
尚未完全获得它的力量，
它依靠和平的技艺，
贯穿其辽阔的广度与长度。
===Original===
Though 'tis not famed for orange bowers
Nor for the products of its vines,
Though other lands have fairer flowers,
Yet it to nobler gifts inclines.
===
===Chinese===
虽然它不以橙树林闻名，
也不以葡萄藤的果实著称，
虽然其他土地有更美的花朵，
然而它倾向更高贵的天赋。
===Original===
It doth produce the golden grain
And few lands can with it compete,
They often try but all in vain
To produce such splendid wheat.
===
===Chinese===
它出产金色的谷物，
少有土地能与之匹敌，
他们屡屡尝试却徒劳无功，
无法产出如此出色的小麦。
===Original===
Our geologists divine,
That ere long we will behold
Many a rich glittering mine
Of copper, silver and of gold.
===
===Chinese===
我们的地质学家预言，
不久我们将看到
许多富饶闪闪发光的矿藏，
蕴藏着铜、银与黄金。
===Original===
But we sing more glorious theme,
It is our verdant pasture land,
Where cows produce a flood of cream,
Doth make cheese of the finest brand.
===
===Chinese===
但我们歌唱更辉煌的主题，
那是我们翠绿的牧场，
奶牛产出充沛的奶油，
制成顶级品牌的奶酪。
===Original===
And great thoughts oftentimes awakes
When we reflect on this wondrous land,
With vast rivers and mighty lakes,
All nature here's on scale so grand.
===
===Chinese===
当我们反思这片奇妙的土地，
常会唤醒伟大的思想，
这里有浩瀚的河流与壮阔的湖泊，
万物自然的规模如此恢宏。
===Original===
Young Dominion so gigantic,
Where rail cars run at speed terrific,
Thousands of miles from the Atlantic,
Till in the West you reach Pacific.
===
===Chinese===
年轻的自治领如此巨大，
火车以惊人的速度奔驰，
从大西洋沿岸绵延数千英里，
直到西方抵达太平洋。
===Original===
From balmy breezes of lake Erie
To the far north frozen ocean,
Where it now seems lone and dreary,
All will yet be life and motion.
===
===Chinese===
从伊利湖（Lake Erie）温暖的微风，
到遥远北方冰封的海洋，
那里如今看似寂寞荒凉，
终将充满生机与活力。
===Original===
Though nation's young its powerful fleet
Doth sail on many a distant sea,
For world's commerce to compete
Her sails in all climes flowing free.
===
===Chinese===
虽然国家年轻，她的强大舰队
已在许多遥远的海洋上航行，
为争夺世界贸易，
她的帆船在各气候带自由飘扬。

## Northwest Rebellion / 西北叛乱

1885.

===Original===
Hail Canada our young fair land,
The world's respect it doth command;
How quick her sons at war's alarms
Sprang to her rescue with their arms.
===
===Chinese===
加拿大，我们年轻美丽的国土，向你致敬，
你赢得了全世界的尊重；
当战争的警报响起，
她的儿女们多么迅速地拿起武器前来救援。
===Original===
In Canada the English rose,
The shamrock and the thistle grows,
United garland they combine
Around the maple tree to twine.
===
===Chinese===
在加拿大，英格兰的玫瑰、
三叶草与蓟草共同生长，
它们编成统一的花环，
环绕枫树缠绕交织。
===Original===
They did march a brave gallant host
From the far East Atlantic coast,
Our Canada so proud and free,
Four thousand miles from sea to sea.
===
===Chinese===
他们组成英勇无畏的大军，
从遥远东方大西洋海岸进发，
我们骄傲而自由的加拿大，
从海到海绵延四千英里。
===Original===
Though skilful rebels did entrench,
But their deadly fires our boys did quench,
And victory it soon was won
By our General Middleton.
===
===Chinese===
尽管狡猾的叛军构筑了工事，
但我们的小伙子们浇灭了他们的炮火，
胜利很快便被
我们的米德尔顿将军（General Middleton）所赢得。
===Original===
And Colonel Williams left a name
For Canada's temple of fame,
A kind and a brave hearted man
In hour of danger led the van.
===
===Chinese===
威廉姆斯上校（Colonel Williams）留下了一个名字，
铭刻在加拿大的英名殿中，
一位善良而勇敢的人，
在危急时刻身先士卒。
===Original===
The ninetieth regiment it fought well,
And Winnipeg doth its glories tell,
London boasts of her volunteers,
For she prides in her Fusiliers.
===
===Chinese===
第九十团英勇作战，
温尼伯传颂着它的荣光，
伦敦为她的志愿军自豪，
她以她的燧发枪团（Fusiliers）为骄傲。
===Original===
Toronto troops have gained renown,
And triumph their quick march did crown,
For the relief of Battleford,
And scattering of the Indian horde.
===
===Chinese===
多伦多的部队赢得了声誉，
急行军的胜利为他们加冕，
他们解了巴特尔福德（Battleford）之围，
驱散了印第安部落的军队。
===Original===
Our volunteers took up their arms,
Each left his home and all its charms;
Though many they were tender reared,
No frost nor snow nor foe they feared.
===
===Chinese===
我们的志愿军拿起了武器，
每人离别了家园和一切温馨；
虽然许多人自幼娇生惯养，
他们不惧霜雪，不惧敌人。
===Original===
Alas that youth so true and brave,
So many now do fill a grave,
And others they are maimed for life,
While engaged in glorious strife.
===
===Chinese===
唉，那些如此忠勇的青年，
许多人如今已长眠地下，
另一些人则终身伤残，
在光荣的战斗中负伤。
===Original===
We have sprung from a good brave stock,
Rose, thistle and the shamrock,
Who all in unity agree,
'Neath the shade of the maple tree.
===
===Chinese===
我们出自勇敢优秀的血脉，
玫瑰、蓟草和三叶草，
它们在团结中和谐共生，
在枫树的荫庇之下。
===Original===
The Indians soon came to grief,
Under their great Poundmaker chief,
And Toronto troops gained fame
And Otter glory to his name.
===
===Chinese===
印第安人很快遭遇了失败，
在他们伟大的庞德梅克（Poundmaker）酋长之下，

===Chinese===
愿所有不热爱枫叶之人
早日遭到报应，

庇荫着这片勇敢自由的土地。

## Big Bear the Indian Chief / 印第安酋长大熊

The following impromptu was given at a banquet to one of the captives of Fort Pit after he had related his experience.
以下即兴诗作献给皮特堡（Fort Pit）一位被俘者的宴席上，在他讲述亲身经历之后。

===Original===
Sad memories it doth awake,
The death of those fell at Frog Lake,
And trials of captives of Fort Pit
When savages did capture it.
===
===Chinese===
它唤醒了悲伤的记忆，
那些在蛙湖（Frog Lake）罹难者之死，
以及皮特堡被俘者的磨难，
当野蛮人攻陷了那座堡垒。
===Original===
But soon Generals Strange and Steel
Made savage hordes their power to feel,
And they rescued women fair
From the paws of the Big Bear.
===
===Chinese===
但很快斯特兰奇将军（General Strange）和斯蒂尔将军（General Steel）
让野蛮部落尝到了他们的厉害，
他们将美丽的妇人们
从大熊（Big Bear）的爪牙下解救出来。
===Original===
Captives for days had naught to eat
But steaks of tough and lean dog meat,
In daily danger of their lives
From bullets and from scalping knives.
===
===Chinese===
被俘者连日来无物可食，
只有坚韧精瘦的狗肉排，
每日都在子弹与剥头皮刀下
冒着生命危险。
===Original===
When building big lodge for war dance,
The cry is heard, the troops advance,
To the white captives sounds so sweet,
But savages they quick retreat.
===
===Chinese===
当他们在建造大型战舞棚屋时，
喊杀声传来，军队在推进，
白人俘虏们听来如此悦耳，
而野蛮人则迅速溃退。
===Original===
A thrilling tale our guest did tell,
That close to him fell bursting shell,
This shot it was not fired in vain,
For several savages were slain.
===
===Chinese===
我们的客人讲述了一个惊心动魄的故事，
炮弹在他身旁炸响，
这一炮并非白费，
几个野蛮人被击毙。
===Original===
Our bold troops great danger braved,
So that white captives might be saved,
Who suffered hunger, cold and damp,
'Mong savage hordes in bush and swamp.
===
===Chinese===
我们勇敢的军队冒着重大的危险，
为了解救白人俘虏，
他们饱受饥寒与潮湿之苦，
在野蛮部落出没的丛林与沼泽间。
===Original===
Big Bear now they have pared his claws,
He must atone for broken laws,
Far away from his native lair,
In prison strong they put Big Bear.
===
===Chinese===
如今他们拔去了大熊的利爪，
他必须为违反法律而赎罪，
远离他故乡的巢穴，
他们将大熊关入坚固的牢狱。

===Original===
In the land of woods and lakes,
Pure happiness each one partakes,
Who is sound in body and in mind,
And to industry is inclined.
===

在这片林木与湖泊的土地上，

Here in great lakes we do take pride,

Other lakes seem inferior
In size to great Superior.
===

我们为这里的五大湖而骄傲，
与山姆大叔（Uncle Sam）共享它们，

And Canadians do take pride,
In Huron's wide expanded tide,

Through St. Clair lake and river.
===
===Chinese===
加拿大人也引以为豪的，
是休伦湖（Huron）辽阔的水面，

穿过圣克莱尔湖（St. Clair）与圣克莱尔河。

Into Erie's lake so grand,
Then behold its wondrous charms,
When embraced in Niagara's arms.
===
===Chinese===
但不久它再度扩展开来，
涌入宏伟的伊利湖（Erie），

Then it more blessings doth bestow,
On pure bosom of Ontario,
Round it our towns and cities cluster,
O'er it Toronto sheds her lustre.
===
===Chinese===
然后它将更多的恩赐
倾洒在安大略湖（Ontario）纯洁的胸膛上，
我们的城镇环绕它聚簇，
多伦多（Toronto）在其上投射她的光辉。
===Original===
And Ontario doth awake,
The thought that 'tis our favorite lake;
Several states approach Lake Erie,
Each one claiming it for dearie.
===
===Chinese===
安大略湖令人想起，
它是我们最钟爱的湖泊；
若干州毗邻伊利湖，
每个都声称它是自己的珍宝。
===Original===
But our fires of love do glow,
Alone for Lake Ontario,
Our love for it is so unbounded,
We have almost it surrounded.
===
===Chinese===
但我们爱的火焰在燃烧，
只为安大略湖而燃烧，
我们对它的爱如此无边，
我们几乎将它环绕。
===Original===
And the lands around its beaches,
They are famed for grapes and peaches,
'Mong choicest fruits you ramble on
From Niagara to Hamilton.
===
===Chinese===
而环绕湖岸的土地，
以葡萄和蜜桃而闻名，
在最上等的果园间漫步，
从尼亚加拉到汉密尔顿（Hamilton）。
===Original===
Ontario North is land of pines,
A land of lakes and rocks and mines,
And beneath dark pine tree shade,
How happy is the youth and maid.
===
===Chinese===
安大略北部是松树之乡，
是湖泊、岩石与矿藏的国度，
在幽暗松林的荫蔽下，
少年与少女多么快乐。
===Original===
For here in summer you keep cool,
And fish for trout in sparkling pool,
For pike or salmon you can spear,
And in the season hunt the deer.
===
===Chinese===
因为这里夏日凉爽宜人，
在清澈的水潭中垂钓鳟鱼，
你可以叉到梭子鱼或鲑鱼，
在猎季还可以猎鹿。
===Original===
In great northern hunting ground,
Where both fish and game abound,
And verdant pastures here are seen,
Where cattle graze 'mong sweetest green.
===
===Chinese===
在广袤的北方猎场，
鱼类与猎物都很丰富，
这里可见翠绿的牧场，
牛群在最鲜嫩的青草间放牧。
===Original===
In the far north a land of pines,
And in the south we have the vines,
Where each year adds into the charms,
Surrounds the homesteads on the farms.
===
===Chinese===
远北是一片松林之地，
南方则有我们的葡萄藤，
每年它都增添更多魅力，
环绕着农场上的家园。
===Original===
Nature our province doth endow,
With hardy sons to guide the plow,
In south we have the fruitful soil,
Where nature's bounties on us smile.
===
===Chinese===
大自然赐予我们的省份
坚韧的儿女来驾驭犁铧，
南方有肥沃的土壤，
大自然的恩惠对我们微笑。
===Original===
We have got rich plains and highlands,
Ontario hath thousand islands,
And there is a great array
Of charming isles on Georgian Bay.
===
===Chinese===
我们拥有富饶的平原与高地，
安大略有千岛群岛，
在乔治亚湾（Georgian Bay）上
有一大片迷人的岛屿。
===Original===
And travellers all they do adore,
The lovely isles near Huron's shore,
Superior makes a grand display,
All round her shores to Thunder Bay.
===
===Chinese===
所有旅行者都对它们赞不绝口，
休伦湖岸边那些可爱的岛屿，

环绕她沿岸直到桑德贝（Thunder Bay）。
===Original===
Muskoka's famed for woods and brakes,
For rocks and meadows and clear lakes,

That 'tis a land for fish and game.
===
===Chinese===
马斯科卡（Muskoka）以森林与密林闻名，

===
===Chinese===
甜蜜的芳香飘来，

## Niagara Dry / 干涸的尼亚加拉

===Original===

有一年早春时节，

狂风横扫湖面与河流，

From Pharaoh's bondage to flee.
===

===Chinese===
这好似布尔沃（Bulwer）笔下的传奇，

===Chinese===
