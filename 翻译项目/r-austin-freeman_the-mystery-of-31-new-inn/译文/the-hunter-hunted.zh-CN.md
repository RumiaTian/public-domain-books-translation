## X The Hunter Hunted / 猎者反被猎

===Original===
The omnibus of those days was a leisurely vehicle. Its ordinary pace was a rather sluggish trot, and in a thickly populated thoroughfare its speed was further reduced by frequent stoppages. Bearing these facts in mind, I gave an occasional backward glance as we jogged northward, though my attention soon began to wander from the rather remote possibility of pursuit to the incidents of our late exploration.

===Chinese===
那个年月的公共马车是种从容的车辆。它寻常的步速不过是颇为迟缓的小跑，到了人烟稠密的大街上，又因频频停靠而愈发缓慢。心里存着这几层，马车载着我们徐徐北行，我不时回头张望一眼；不过没过多久，我的注意力便从追踪那桩相当渺茫的可能，游移到了我们方才那番查探的种种情节上去。

===Original===
It had not been difficult to see that Thorndyke was very well pleased with the results of our search, but excepting the letter﻿—which undoubtedly opened up a channel for further inquiry and possible identification﻿—I could not perceive that any of the traces that we had found justified his satisfaction. There were the spectacles, for instance. They were almost certainly the pair worn by Mr. Graves. But what then? It was exceedingly improbable that we should be able to discover the maker of them, and if we were, it was still more improbable that he would be able to give us any information that would help us. Spectacle-makers are not usually on confidential terms with their customers.

===Chinese===
不难看出，桑代克对我们这趟搜查的收获十分满意；只是除那封信外——它无疑为进一步查访乃至查明身份开出了一条门路——我看不出我们寻到的任何其他痕迹当得起他这份满意。就拿那副眼镜来说。它几乎肯定就是格雷夫斯先生戴的那一副。可那又怎样？我们竟能寻出它的制造商，此事微乎其微；纵然寻得出，他能给我们什么帮衬，可能性就更其渺茫。眼镜制造商同主顾之间，向来不至深交到互吐机密。

===Original===
As to the other objects, I could make nothing of them. The little sticks of reed evidently had some use that was known to Thorndyke and furnished, by inference, some kind of information about Weiss, Graves, or Mrs. Schallibaum. But I had never seen anything like them before and they conveyed nothing whatever to me. Then the bottle that had seemed so significant to Thorndyke was to me quite uninforming. It did, indeed, suggest that some member of the household might be connected with the stage, but it gave no hint as to which one. Certainly that person was not Mr. Weiss, whose appearance was as remote from that of an actor as could well be imagined. At any rate, the bottle and its label gave me no more useful hint than it might be worth while to call on Mr. Fox and make inquiries; and something told me very emphatically that this was not what it had conveyed to Thorndyke.

===Chinese===
至于另外几样物件，我便全然参不透了。那几截小芦秆显然自有其用途，只是桑代克心中有数，而且据理推来，它们提供了某种关于韦斯、格雷夫斯或沙利鲍姆太太的消息。可我从前不曾见过类似的东西，它们对我说来毫无意味。再有那只瓶子，在桑代克眼里如此意味深长，在我看来却全无信息可言。它确实暗示这一户人家里或有哪一位同戏剧界有些瓜葛，却点不出究竟是哪一位。当然那人决计不是韦斯先生——他的相貌离一个演员有多远，简直想象不尽。总而言之，那只瓶子和它的标签给我的有用提示，充其量是值得去拜访一趟福克斯先生打听打听；可有什么在斩钉截铁地告诉我，它向桑代克所传递的决不止这一点。

===Original===
These reflections occupied me until the omnibus, having rumbled over London Bridge and up King William Street, joined the converging streams of traffic at the Mansion House. Here I got down and changed to an omnibus bound for Kensington; on which I travelled westward pleasantly enough, looking down into the teeming streets and whiling away the time by meditating upon the very agreeable afternoon that I promised myself, and considering how far my new arrangement with Thorndyke would justify me in entering into certain domestic engagements of a highly interesting kind.

===Chinese===
这些念头一直陪着我，直到公共马车隆隆驶过伦敦桥（London Bridge），上了国王威廉街（King William Street），汇入市长官邸处交汇合流的车马。我在这里下车，换搭一辆开往肯辛顿的公共马车；乘着它一路西行倒也惬意：俯望底下熙熙攘攘的街市，一面盘算着我给自己许下的那个极愉快的下午，一面思量我和桑代克的新安排，够不够叫我放手去缔结某种意味深长的家庭之约。

===Original===
What might have happened under other circumstances it is impossible to tell and useless to speculate; the fact is that my journey ended in a disappointment. I arrived, all agog, at the familiar house in Endsley Gardens only to be told by a sympathetic housemaid that the family was out; that Mrs. Hornby had gone into the country and would not be home until night, and﻿—which mattered a good deal more to me﻿—that her niece, Miss Juliet Gibson, had accompanied her.

===Chinese===
换了别的情形会怎样，既说不得，也犯不着瞎猜；事实是我这一趟以失望收场。我满心热望地赶到恩斯利花园（Endsley Gardens）那所熟悉的宅子，却只听一位好心的女仆说，一家人都出门了；霍恩比太太下乡去了，到夜里才会回来；还有——这一位于我更要紧得多——她的外甥女朱丽叶·吉布森小姐（Juliet Gibson）也随同去了。

===Original===
Now a man who drops into lunch without announcing his intention or previously ascertaining those of his friends has no right to quarrel with fate if he finds an empty house. Thus philosophically I reflected as I turned away from the house in profound discontent, demanding of the universe in general why Mrs. Hornby need have perversely chosen my first free day to go gadding into the country, and above all, why she must needs spirit away the fair Juliet. This was the crowning misfortune (for I could have endured the absence of the elder lady with commendable fortitude), and since I could not immediately return to the Temple it left me a mere waif and stray for the time being.

===Chinese===
一个不先打招呼、也不问问朋友有无空暇就闯去吃午饭的人，撞上一座空宅，是无权跟命运怄气的。我便如此这般以哲人风度自宽自解，怏怏转身离开那宅子，一面泛泛地质问苍天：霍恩比太太何苦偏偏拣我头一个闲暇的日子任性地跑乡下闲逛，更要紧的是，何苦非把美丽的朱丽叶一并摄了去。这才是最要命的一桩（因为老太太不在家，我原可以可嘉的坚忍熬将过去）；何况我又不能即刻回坦普尔去，一时间竟成了个无处栖身的游魂。

===Original===
Instinct﻿—of the kind that manifests itself especially about one o’clock in the afternoon﻿—impelled me in the direction of Brompton Road, and finally landed me at a table in a large restaurant apparently adjusted to the needs of ladies who had come from a distance to engage in the feminine sport of shopping. Here, while waiting for my lunch, I sat idly scanning the morning paper and wondering what I should do with the rest of the day; and presently it chanced that my eye caught the announcement of a matinee at the theatre in Sloane Square. It was quite a long time since I had been at a theatre, and, as the play﻿—light comedy﻿—seemed likely to satisfy my not very critical taste, I decided to devote the afternoon to reviving my acquaintance with the drama. Accordingly as soon as my lunch was finished, I walked down the Brompton Road, stepped on to an omnibus, and was duly deposited at the door of the theatre. A couple of minutes later I found myself occupying an excellent seat in the second row of the pit, oblivious alike of my recent disappointment and of Thorndyke’s words of warning.

===Chinese===
本能——尤其是下午一点钟前后格外活跃的那一种——把我推向布朗普顿路（Brompton Road），末了安置在一家大餐馆的桌旁；那馆子看去专为远道而来、从事购物这项女性运动的女客而设。在这里，我一边等午饭，一边懒懒地翻着晨报，盘算这一天余下的时光如何打发；不一会儿，恰好瞧见一则广告：斯隆广场（Sloane Square）的剧院有一场日场戏。我已经很久没进过剧院了，而这出戏——轻喜剧——想来合得了我那不算挑剔的口味，于是决计把下午用来重温同戏剧的旧交。于是午饭一毕，我便沿布朗普顿路而下，跨上一辆公共马车，顺顺当当地给放在了剧院门口。两分钟后，我已坐进正厅后排第二排的一个上好座位，方才的失意也罢，桑代克的警告之言也罢，一古脑儿都忘了。

===Original===
I am not an enthusiastic playgoer. To dramatic performances I am disposed to assign nothing further than the modest function of furnishing entertainment. I do not go to a theatre to be instructed or to have my moral outlook elevated. But, by way of compensation, I am not difficult to please. To a simple play, adjusted to my primitive taste, I can bring a certain bucolic appreciation that enables me to extract from the performance the maximum of enjoyment; and when, on this occasion, the final curtain fell and the audience rose, I rescued my hat from its insecure resting-place and turned to go with the feeling that I had spent a highly agreeable afternoon.

===Chinese===
我不是个热心看戏的人。对戏剧演出，我至多肯派给它一项本分的功用——供人消遣。我进剧院不为受教，也不为让道德境界升华。不过作为补偿，我也不难取悦。对一出合乎我原始口味的简单戏文，我能拿出几分田园式的鉴赏力，从演出里榨出最大限度的乐趣；所以这一回，终幕落下、观众起立时，我从那搁得不大牢靠的地方抢救出帽子，转身外行，自觉这一下午过得着实愉快。

===Original===
Emerging from the theatre, borne on the outgoing stream, I presently found myself opposite the door of a teashop. Instinct﻿—the five o’clock instinct this time﻿—guided me in; for we are creatures of habit, especially of the tea habit. The unoccupied table to which I drifted was in a shady corner not very far from the pay-desk; and here I had been seated less than a minute when a lady passed me on her way to the farther table. The glimpse that I caught of her as she approached﻿—it was but a glimpse, since she passed behind me﻿—showed that she was dressed in black, that she wore a beaded veil and hat, and in addition to the glass of milk and the bun that she carried, she was encumbered by an umbrella and a small basket, apparently containing some kind of needlework. I must confess that I gave her very little attention at the time, being occupied in anxious speculation as to how long it would be before the fact of my presence would impinge on the consciousness of the waitress.

===Chinese===
随着散场的人流涌出剧院，不多时我便站到了一家茶室门口的对面。本能——这一回是五点钟的本能——引我进去；因为我们都是习惯的造物，尤其是喝茶的习惯。我漂到一张空桌旁；桌子在个背阴的角落，离收银台不远。我在这里坐下还不到一分钟，一位女士打我身边经过，往更靠里的桌子去。她走近时我瞥了那一眼——也不过是瞥一眼，因为她自我背后绕行——看得出她一身黑衣，戴着缀珠的面纱和帽子；除了一杯牛奶和一只小圆面包，手里还累赘着一把雨伞和一只小提篮，篮里瞧着像装着什么针线活计。我必须承认，当时我几乎没把她放在心上，因为正忙着焦灼地揣测：还要多久，我这位来客的事实才能撞进那女侍的意识里去。

===Original===
The exact time by the clock on the wall was three minutes and a quarter, at the expiration of which an anaemic young woman sauntered up to the table and bestowed on me a glance of sullen interrogation, as if mutely demanding what the devil I wanted. I humbly requested that I might be provided with a pot of tea; whereupon she turned on her heel (which was a good deal worn down on the offside) and reported my conduct to a lady behind a marble-topped counter.

===Chinese===
照墙上挂钟的准确计时，历时三分又一刻（十五秒）；这一期限届满时，一个脸色贫血的年轻女人踱到桌前，赏了我一眼忿忿的质问，仿佛不出声地问我到底要什么鬼名堂。我谦卑地请求赏一壶茶；于是她靴跟一转（那只跟子的外侧已磨去了好一截），把我的行径呈报给大理石面柜台后面的一位女士。

===Original===
It seemed that the counter lady took a lenient view of the case, for in less than four minutes the waitress returned and gloomily deposited on the table before me a teapot, a milk-jug, a cup and saucer, a jug of hot water, and a small pool of milk. Then she once more departed in dudgeon.

===Chinese===
看来柜台后那位女士对此案持宽仁之见，因为不出四分钟，女侍回来了，阴沉沉地在我桌上放下茶壶、奶罐、带托的茶杯、一罐热水，和一小汪牛奶。随后她又一次忿然而去。

===Original===
I had just given the tea in the pot a preliminary stir and was about to pour out the first cup when I felt someone bump lightly against my chair and heard something rattle on the floor. I turned quickly and perceived the lady, whom I had seen enter, stooping just behind my chair. It seemed that having finished her frugal meal she was on her way out when she had dropped the little basket that I had noticed hanging from her wrist; which basket had promptly disgorged its entire contents on the floor.

===Chinese===
我刚把壶里的茶先搅了一遭，正要斟头一杯，忽觉有人轻轻撞上我的椅子，又听见什么东西在地板上哗啦啦一响。我急忙回头，看见我进来时见过的那位女士，正俯身在我椅后。原来她用完了那顿清简的饭食，正要出去，却把我先前留意到挂在她腕上的那只小提篮失手掉了；那篮子登时把满腹的内容尽数吐在了地板上。

===Original===
Now everyone must have noticed the demon of agility that seems to enter into an inanimate object when it is dropped, and the apparently intelligent malice with which it discovers, and rolls into, the most inaccessible places. Here was a case in point. This particular basket had contained materials for Oriental bead-work; and no sooner had it reached the floor than each item of its contents appeared to become possessed of a separate and particular devil impelling it to travel at headlong speed to some remote and unapproachable corner as distant as possible from its fellows.

===Chinese===
想来人人都不难留意到：一件无生命的东西一旦落地，就仿佛有股机灵的邪灵附了体，带着那种显然富于心智的歹毒，专会寻着最够不着的旮旯滚进去。眼前正是一例。这只提篮里装的是东方串珠活计的材料；篮子一沾地板，里头的每一件东西便仿佛各各着了一个专门附体的魔鬼，驱着它没命地奔向某个遥远难近的角落，离它的同伴们越远越好。

===Original===
As the only man﻿—and almost the only person﻿—near, the duty of salvage-agent manifestly devolved upon me; and down I went, accordingly, on my hands and knees, regardless of a nearly new pair of trousers, to grope under tables, chairs and settles in reach of the scattered treasure. A ball of the thick thread or twine I recovered from a dark and dirty corner after a brief interview with the sharp corner of a settle, and a multitude of the large beads with which this infernal industry is carried on I gathered from all parts of the compass, coming forth at length (quadrupedally) with a double handful of the treasure-trove and a very lively appreciation of the resistant qualities of a cast-iron table-stand when applied to the human cranium.

===Chinese===
作为近旁唯一的男人——而且几乎是唯一在场的人——打捞代理人之责显然落到了我头上；于是我不顾一条近乎全新的裤子，就此双手双膝着地，在够得着的桌椅和高背长椅底下摸索那散落的珍宝。那团粗线绳是我在一个阴暗肮脏的角落里、同高背长椅的尖角短暂会晤之后捞回来的；至于这门地狱行当所用的那许多大珠子，我从四面八方一一拾拢，最后（四肢着地地）钻将出来，双手捧满这批拾获之物，同时对铸铁桌柱施加于人类颅骨时的抗撞性能，获得了非常鲜活的体会。

===Original===
The owner of the lost and found property was greatly distressed by the accident and the trouble it had caused me; in fact she was quite needlessly agitated about it. The hand which held the basket into which I poured the rescued trash trembled visibly, and the brief glance that I bestowed on her as she murmured her thanks and apologies﻿—with a very slight foreign accent﻿—showed me that she was excessively pale. That much I could see plainly in spite of the rather dim light in this part of the shop and the beaded veil that covered her face; and I could also see that she was a rather remarkable looking woman, with a great mass of harsh, black hair and very broad black eyebrows that nearly met above her nose and contrasted strikingly with the dead white of her skin. But, of course, I did not look at her intently. Having returned her property and received her acknowledgments, I resumed my seat and left her to go on her way.

===Chinese===
这位失物复得之物的物主，为这桩意外和给我添的麻烦深感不安；事实上她的激动全无必要。她一手提着篮子，我往里倾倒那些抢救回来的破烂时，那只手看得见地发着抖；她低声道谢致歉时——带着极轻微的外国口音——我投去的那匆匆一瞥告诉我，她苍白得厉害。尽管店堂这一带光线相当昏暗，又有缀珠面纱罩着脸，这一点我还是看得分明；我还看得出她是个相貌颇为触目的女人，一头浓密粗硬的黑发，两道极宽的黑眉毛几乎在鼻梁上方连成一片，同她死白的皮肤相衬，格外醒目。不过，我自然没有紧盯细看。归还了她的物件、领受了她的谢意之后，我便回到座位上，任她自去。

===Original===
I had once more grasped the handle of the teapot when I made a rather curious discovery. At the bottom of the teacup lay a single lump of sugar. To the majority of persons it would have meant nothing. They would have assumed that they had dropped it in and forgotten it and would have proceeded to pour out the tea. But it happened that, at this time, I did not take sugar in my tea; whence it followed that the lump had not been put in by me. Assuming, therefore, that it had been carelessly dropped in by the waitress, I turned it out on the table, filled the cup, added the milk, and took a tentative draught to test the temperature.

===Chinese===
我重新握住茶壶柄时，有了一个颇为古怪的发现。茶杯底上躺着一块方糖。对多数人来说，这什么也算不上。他们会以为是自己放进去忘了，然后照旧斟茶不误。可偏偏这时候我喝茶是不放糖的；由此推论，那块糖不是我放的。于是我便认定是女侍粗心失手掉的，把它倒在桌上，斟满茶，兑上奶，试探地呷了一口试试温度。

===Original===
The cup was yet at my lips when I chanced to look into the mirror that faced my table. Of course it reflected the part of the shop that was behind me, including the cashier’s desk; at which the owner of the basket now stood paying for her refreshment. Between her and me was a gas chandelier which cast its light on my back but full on her face; and her veil notwithstanding, I could see that she was looking at me steadily; was, in fact, watching me intently and with a very curious expression﻿—an expression of expectancy mingled with alarm. But this was not all. As I returned her intent look﻿—which I could do unobserved, since my face, reflected in the mirror, was in deep shadow﻿—I suddenly perceived that that steady gaze engaged her right eye only; the other eye was looking sharply towards her left shoulder. In short, she had a divergent squint of the left eye.

===Chinese===
茶杯尚在唇边，我碰巧朝我桌上正对的那面镜子里望了一眼。镜中自然映出我身后的那片店堂，包括收银台；提篮的主人此刻正站在台前付她的茶点钱。她与我之间悬着一盏煤气枝形吊灯，灯光照在我的背上，却正正照在她脸上；虽隔着面纱，我看得出她正定定地望着我；实际上是在凝神注视我，带着一种极古怪的神情——一种期待与惊惶交织的神情。还不止于此。当我回敬她那凝注的目光时——我可以这样而不被察觉，因为我的脸映在镜中，正处在深影里——我突然发觉，那道定定的凝视只用她的右眼；另一只眼正锐利地斜向她自己的左肩。一言以蔽之，她的左眼有散开性斜视。

===Original===
I put down my cup with a thrill of amazement and a sudden surging up of suspicion and alarm. An instant’s reflection reminded me that when she had spoken to me a few moments before, both her eyes had looked into mine without the slightest trace of a squint. My thoughts flew back to the lump of sugar, to the unguarded milk-jug and the draught of tea that I had already swallowed; and, hardly knowing what I intended, I started to my feet and turned to confront her. But as I rose, she snatched up her change and darted from the shop. Through the glass door, I saw her spring on to the footboard of a passing hansom and give the driver some direction. I saw the man whip up his horse, and, by the time I reached the door, the cab was moving off swiftly towards Sloane Street.

===Chinese===
我放下杯子，一阵惊愕窜过全身，疑心与警觉也骤然涌起。转念一想便记起：方才片刻之前她同我说话时，那双眼睛是直直望进我眼里的，没有丝毫斜视的痕迹。我的思绪飞回那块方糖，飞向那只无人看守的奶罐和我已经下咽的那口茶；于是我霍地起身，转向她去——自己也几乎不知要做什么。可我刚站起来，她已一把抓起找头，疾奔出店。隔着玻璃门，我看见她跃上一辆路过的双轮马车的踏板，向车夫吩咐了一句去处。我看见那人扬鞭催马；待我赶到门口，马车已朝斯隆街（Sloane Street）飞快驶远了。

===Original===
I stood irresolute. I had not paid and could not run out of the shop without making a fuss, and my hat and stick were still on the rail opposite my seat. The woman ought to be followed, but I had no fancy for the task. If the tea that I had swallowed was innocuous, no harm was done and I was rid of my pursuer. So far as I was concerned, the incident was closed. I went back to my seat, and picking up the lump of sugar which still lay on the table where I had dropped it, put it carefully in my pocket. But my appetite for tea was satisfied for the present. Moreover it was hardly advisable to stay in the shop lest some fresh spy should come to see how I fared.
