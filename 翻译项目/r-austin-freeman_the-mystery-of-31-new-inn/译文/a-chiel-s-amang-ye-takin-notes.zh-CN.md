## III “A Chiel’s Amang Ye Takin’ Notes” / “一个机灵人正在你们中间记笔记”

===Original===
The attitude of the suspicious man tends to generate in others the kind of conduct that seems to justify his suspicions. In most of us there lurks a certain strain of mischief which trustfulness disarms but distrust encourages. The inexperienced kitten which approaches us confidingly with arched back and upright tail, soliciting caresses, generally receives the gentle treatment that it expects; whereas the worldly-wise tomcat, who, in response to friendly advances, scampers away and grins at us suspiciously from the fancied security of an adjacent wall, impels us to accelerate his retreat with a well-directed clod.

===Chinese===
疑心人的态度，往往会在别人身上催生出那类似乎恰好坐实其疑心的举动。我们多数人心里都潜藏着一分促狭，信任能将它化解，猜忌却会助长它。那只毫无阅历的小猫，拱着背、竖着尾，坦然向我们走近，讨要抚爱，通常便得到它所期待的温存对待；而那只深谙世故的老公猫，面对我们的友好亲近却撒腿跑开，躲到邻近墙头那自以为安全的所在，满腹狐疑地冲我们龇牙，倒惹得我们捡起土块，好好瞄它一记，催它快些逃命。

===Original===
Now the proceedings of Mr. H. Weiss resembled those of the tomcat aforesaid and invited an analogous reply. To a responsible professional man his extraordinary precautions were at once an affront and a challenge. Apart from graver considerations, I found myself dwelling with unholy pleasure on the prospect of locating the secret hiding-place from which he seemed to grin at me with such complacent defiance; and I lost no time and spared no trouble in preparing myself for the adventure. The very hansom which bore me from the Temple to Kennington Lane was utilized for a preliminary test of Thorndyke’s little apparatus. During the whole of that brief journey I watched the compass closely, noted the feel and sound of the road-material and timed the trotting of the horse. And the result was quite encouraging. It is true that the compass-needle oscillated wildly to the vibration of the cab, but still its oscillations took place around a definite point which was the average direction, and it was evident to me that the data it furnished were very fairly reliable. I felt very little doubt, after the preliminary trial, as to my being able to produce a moderately intelligible track-chart if only I should get an opportunity to exercise my skill.

===Chinese===
而韦斯先生的种种做派，恰似前述那只老公猫，招来的也是同一类的回报。对一个负责任的专业人士来说，他那套异乎寻常的防范既是侮辱，也是挑衅。撇开更严重的考虑不谈，我发现自己正怀着一种不大光明正大的快意，盘算着有朝一日查明他那秘密巢穴的所在——他仿佛正是从那里，带着那副洋洋得意的挑衅神情朝我龇牙；于是我为这场冒险做起准备来，抓紧一切时机，不辞一切辛劳。载我从坦普尔回肯宁顿巷的那辆双轮马车，就被我用来对桑代克的小器具做了一次初步试验。在那段短短的旅程中，我始终紧盯着罗盘，留意路面材料的脚感与声响，并为小跑的马匹计步计时。结果相当令人鼓舞。诚然，马车的震动引得罗盘指针狂乱摆动，但它的摆动毕竟围绕着一个确定的中心点，即平均方位；在我看来，它所提供的数据已相当可靠。经此初步试验，我几乎不再怀疑：只要有机会施展这番身手，我就能绘出一张大体看得明白的轨迹图。

===Original===
But it looked as if I should not. Mr. Weiss’s promise to send for me again soon was not fulfilled. Three days passed and still he made no sign. I began to fear that I had been too outspoken; that the shuttered carriage had gone forth to seek some more confiding and easygoing practitioner, and that our elaborate preparations had been made in vain. When the fourth day drew towards a close and still no summons had come, I was disposed reluctantly to write the case off as a lost opportunity.

===Chinese===
可看样子我是等不到这个机会了。韦斯先生答应很快再请我出诊，这话并没有兑现。三天过去了，他那边毫无动静。我开始担心，是不是自己话说得太直；那辆封着窗板的马车是不是已转出去，另寻一位更肯轻信、更好说话的医生；我们那番煞费苦心的准备是不是全做了无用功。到了第四天日暮时分，召唤依然未至，我便老大不情愿地打算把这桩案子当作一桩坐失的机会勾销了事。

===Original===
And at that moment, in the midst of my regrets, the bottle-boy thrust an uncomely head in at the door. His voice was coarse, his accent was hideous, and his grammatical construction beneath contempt; but I forgave him all when I gathered the import of his message.

===Chinese===
而恰恰在那一刻，正当我懊恼之际，药童把他那颗不好看的脑袋从门口探了进来。他嗓音粗嘎，口音难听，语法更是不值一哂；可等我明白了他的口信，这一切我统统原谅了他。

===Original===
“Mr. Weiss’s carriage is waiting, and he says will you come as quickly as you can because he’s took very bad tonight.”

===Chinese===
“韦斯先生的马车候着呢，他说请您越快越好，因为今晚他病得怪重的。”

===Original===
I sprang from my chair and hastily collected the necessaries for the journey. The little board and the lamp I put in my overcoat pocket; I overhauled the emergency bag and added to its usual contents a bottle of permanganate of potassium which I thought I might require. Then I tucked the evening paper under my arm and went out.

===Chinese===
我从椅子里一跃而起，匆匆收拾起这趟出诊的必需物件。小木板和灯放进了大衣口袋；我把应急提包翻检了一遍，在惯常的药品之外又添了一瓶高锰酸钾，心想兴许用得着。随后我把晚报往腋下一夹，出了门。

===Original===
The coachman, who was standing at the horse’s head as I emerged, touched his hat and came forward to open the door.

===Chinese===
我出门时，马车夫正站在马头旁边；他碰了碰帽檐，走上前来开车门。

===Original===
“I have fortified myself for the long drive, you see,” I remarked, exhibiting the newspaper as I stepped into the carriage.

===Chinese===
“您瞧，我给自己备足了粮草，好熬这趟远路。”我一面跨进马车，一面亮出那份报纸说。

===Original===
“But you can’t read in the dark,” said he.

===Chinese===
“可黑灯瞎火的，您没法看哪。”他说。

===Original===
“No, but I have provided myself with a lamp,” I replied, producing it and striking a match.

===Chinese===
“是没有，可我自备了一盏灯。”我答道，一面掏出灯来，划了根火柴。

===Original===
He watched me as I lit the lamp and hooked it on the back cushion, and observed:

===Chinese===
我点亮灯，挂到靠背垫上；他瞧着我做完这些，说道：

===Original===
“I suppose you found it rather a dull ride last time. It’s a longish way. They might have fitted the carriage with an inside lamp. But we shall have to make it a quicker passage tonight. Governor says Mr. Graves is uncommon bad.”

===Chinese===
“我琢磨着，上回这一路您怕是闷得慌。路可不近。他们原该给车里装盏随车灯的。不过今晚咱们得把路赶得快些。东家说格雷夫斯先生病得凶。”

===Original===
With this he slammed the door and locked it. I drew the board from my pocket, laid it on my knee, glanced at my watch, and, as the coachman climbed to his seat, I made the first entry in the little book.

===Chinese===
说罢他砰地关上车门，上了锁。我从口袋里掏出木板，放在膝头，瞥了一眼怀表，趁马车夫爬上驭座的当口，在小本子里记下了第一笔。

===Original===
“8:58 W. by S. Start from home. Horse 13 hands.”

===Chinese===
“8:58 西偏南。自家中启程。马高13掌。”

===Original===
The first move of the carriage on starting was to turn round as if heading for Newington Butts, and the second entry accordingly read:

===Chinese===
马车启动后的第一个动作便是掉头，看方向像是朝纽因顿巴茨去，于是本子上的第二笔相应记的是：

===Original===
“8:58:30 E. by N.”

===Chinese===
“8:58:30 东偏北。”

===Original===
But this direction was not maintained long. Very soon we turned south and then west and then south again. I sat with my eyes riveted on the compass, following with some difficulty its rapid changes. The needle swung to and fro incessantly but always within a definite arc, the centre of which was the true direction. But this direction varied from minute to minute in the most astonishing manner. West, south, east, north, the carriage turned, “boxing” the compass until I lost all count of direction. It was an amazing performance. Considering that the man was driving against time on a mission of life and death urgency, his carelessness as to direction was astounding. The tortuousness of the route must have made the journey twice as long as it need have been with a little more careful selection. At least so it appeared to me, though, naturally, I was not in a position to offer an authoritative criticism.

===Chinese===
可这个方向没保持多久。很快我们便转向南，再转向西，又转回南。我坐着，眼睛死死盯住罗盘，勉强追赶它那些迅疾的变化。指针不停地来回摆动，却始终摆在一个确定的弧度之内，弧的中心便是真实方位。但这方位以最惊人的方式一分钟一分钟地变。西、南、东、北，马车一路转个不停，把罗盘上的方位“点了个遍”，转到我彻底数不清了方向。这真是一场令人叹为观止的表演。想想这人是在跟时间赛跑、办着一桩人命关天的急差，他对方向的不经意实在叫人骇然。这路线弯弯绕绕，只要选路时稍多用一分心，路程本不必拉长一倍。至少在我看是这样，不过话说回来，我自然也轮不上发表什么权威的批评。

===Original===
As far as I could judge, we followed the same route as before. Once I heard a tug’s whistle and knew that we were near the river, and we passed the railway station, apparently at the same time as on the previous occasion, for I heard a passenger train start and assumed that it was the same train. We crossed quite a number of thoroughfares with tramlines﻿—I had no idea there were so many﻿—and it was a revelation to me to find how numerous the railway arches were in this part of London and how continually the nature of the road-metal varied.

===Chinese===
据我判断，我们走的还是上回那条路线。有一回我听见拖船的汽笛，知道已行近河畔；我们还经过了那座火车站，看来与上回正是同一时刻，因为我听见一列客车启动，便认定还是同一班车。我们穿过了相当多条敷设电车道的大街——我原先全不知道竟有这么多——而这一带的铁路拱桥竟如此密集、路面铺料的性质又变得如此频繁，着实让我长了一番见识。

===Original===
It was by no means a dull journey this time. The incessant changes of direction and variations in the character of the road kept me most uncommonly busy; for I had hardly time to scribble down one entry before the compass-needle would swing round sharply, showing that we had once more turned a corner; and I was quite taken by surprise when the carriage slowed down and turned into the covered way. Very hastily I scribbled down the final entry (“9:24 S.E. In covered way”), and having closed the book and slipped it and the board into my pocket, had just opened out the newspaper when the carriage door was unlocked and opened, whereupon I unhooked and blew out the lamp and pocketed that too, reflecting that it might be useful later.

===Chinese===
这一回的旅程绝不沉闷。方向不停的变换、路面性质不断的变化，忙得我不亦乐乎；因为一笔记录还没草草记完，罗盘指针往往又猛地一转，说明我们再度拐过了一个街角；待到马车放慢、拐进那条带顶的廊道时，我竟全无防备。我慌忙草草记下最后一笔（“9:24 东南。入带顶廊道。”），合上本子，连同木板一并塞进口袋，刚把报纸抖开，车门便开了锁、被拉开；于是我摘下灯吹灭，也揣进口袋，心想它往后兴许还有用处。

===Original===
As on the last occasion, Mrs. Schallibaum stood in the open doorway with a lighted candle. But she was a good deal less self-possessed this time. In fact she looked rather wild and terrified. Even by the candlelight I could see that she was very pale and she seemed unable to keep still. As she gave me the few necessary words of explanation, she fidgeted incessantly and her hands and feet were in constant movement.

===Chinese===
与上回一样，沙利鲍姆太太端着一支点亮的蜡烛站在敞开的门口。只是这一回，她的自持差得多了。事实上，她看去颇有几分狂乱惊恐。即便借着烛光我也看得出她脸色惨白，而且似乎静不下来。她向我交代那几句必要的说明时，坐立不安，手脚一直在动个不停。

===Original===
“You had better come up with me at once,” she said. “Mr. Graves is much worse tonight. We will wait not for Mr. Weiss.”

===Chinese===
“您最好这就跟我上楼。”她说，“格雷夫斯先生今晚病重多了。我们将不等韦斯先生。”

===Original===
Without waiting for a reply she quickly ascended the stairs and I followed. The room was in much the same condition as before. But the patient was not. As soon as I entered the room, a soft, rhythmical gurgle from the bed gave me a very clear warning of danger. I stepped forward quickly and looked down at the prostrate figure, and the warning gathered emphasis. The sick man’s ghastly face was yet more ghastly; his eyes were more sunken, his skin more livid; “his nose was as sharp as a pen,” and if he did not “babble of green fields” it was because he seemed to be beyond even that. If it had been a case of disease, I should have said at once that he was dying. He had all the appearance of a man in articulo mortis. Even as it was, feeling convinced that the case was one of morphine poisoning, I was far from confident that I should be able to draw him back from the extreme edge of vitality on which he trembled so insecurely.

===Chinese===
她不等回答，便快步上楼，我跟在后面。屋里的情形与上回大致无异。病人却不然。我一进屋，床上传来的一阵轻而规律的咯咯声便给了我一个再明白不过的危险警讯。我抢步上前，低头看那僵卧的人影，警讯愈发确凿。病人那张死人似的脸愈发像死人了；眼窝陷得更深，皮肤青得更透；“鼻尖尖得像一支笔”，而他若还不曾“念叨绿色的田野”，那是因为他似乎连那个也谈不上了。这若是一例病症，我会立刻断言他垂危待死。他浑身上下，全是濒死之际（in articulo mortis）之人的模样。即便如此——我虽确信这是吗啡中毒——也远不敢自信能把他从生机的绝壁边缘拉回来；此刻他正颤颤巍巍、摇摇欲坠地悬在那道边缘上。

===Original===
“He is very ill? He is dying?”

===Chinese===
“他病得很重？他要死了？”

===Original===
It was Mrs. Schallibaum’s voice; very low, but eager and intense. I turned, with my finger on the patient’s wrist, and looked into the face of the most thoroughly scared woman I have ever seen. She made no attempt now to avoid the light, but looked me squarely in the face, and I noticed, half-unconsciously, that her eyes were brown and had a curious strained expression.

===Chinese===
是沙利鲍姆太太的声音；极低，却急切而紧张。我手指还搭在病人腕上，转过脸去，正对上我平生所见被吓得最彻底的一张女人的脸。此刻她再不设法躲避灯光，而是直直地望着我的脸；我还半是不自觉地注意到，她的眼睛是棕色的，带着一种古怪的、绷紧了的神情。

===Original===
“Yes,” I answered, “he is very ill. He is in great danger.”

===Chinese===
“是的，”我答道，“他病得很重。他处境极度危险。”

===Original===
She still stared at me fixedly for some seconds. And then a very odd thing occurred. Suddenly she squinted﻿—squinted horribly; not with the familiar convergent squint which burlesque artists imitate, but with external or divergent squint of extreme near sight or unequal vision. The effect was quite startling. One moment both her eyes were looking straight into mine; the next, one of them rolled round until it looked out of the uttermost corner, leaving the other gazing steadily forward.

===Chinese===
她仍旧目不转睛地瞪了我好几秒。接着便发生了一件非常古怪的事。她突然斜起眼来——斜得怕人；不是滑稽戏艺人爱模仿的那种常见的对眼，而是极深近视或两眼视力不齐者特有的外斜式、即分散性斜视。那效果着实吓人一跳。前一刻，她两眼还直直望进我眼里；后一刻，其中一只已骨碌一转，转到眼眶最外的角落里张望，剩下另一只兀自平视前方。

===Original===

===Chinese===
她显然觉察到了这一变化，因为她赶忙把头扭开，脸也红了几分。可眼下哪是讲究仪容的时候。

“You can save him, doctor! You will not let him die! He must not be allowed to die!”

===Chinese===
“您能救他的，医生！您不会让他死的！绝不能容他死！”

===Original===

===Chinese===
她说这话时的那股热切，倒仿佛他是她在世上最亲爱的朋友；而我疑心实情远非如此。不过她那溢于言表的恐惧自有其用处。

===Original===

===Chinese===
“咖啡！”她失声道，“可家里一点也没有。我把茶沏得浓浓的，不行吗？”

===Original===

===Chinese===
“不行，茶代不得。我要的必须是咖啡，而且必须快。”

===Chinese===

===Original===
“Can’t you send the coachman?” I asked.

===Chinese===
“不能打发马车夫去吗？”我问。

===Original===
She shook her head impatiently. “No, that is no use. I must wait until Mr. Weiss comes.”

===Chinese===
她不耐烦地摇了摇头。“不，那没有用。我必须等到韦斯先生回来。”

===Original===
“That won’t do,” I said, sharply. “He will slip through our fingers while you are waiting. You must go and get that coffee at once and bring it to me as soon as it is ready. And I want a tumbler and some water.”

===Chinese===
“那可不行。”我厉声道，“等你等完这阵，他早从我们指缝里溜掉了。你必须立刻去买咖啡，一煮好就端来给我。我还要一只平底玻璃杯和一些清水。”

===Original===
She brought me a water-bottle and glass from the washstand and then, with a groan of despair, hurried from the room.

===Chinese===
她从脸盆架上给我取来一只水瓶和玻璃杯，然后一声绝望的呻吟，急匆匆出了屋。

===Original===
I lost no time in applying the remedies that I had to hand. Shaking out into the tumbler a few crystals of potassium permanganate, I filled it up with water and approached the patient. His stupor was profound. I shook him as roughly as was safe in his depressed condition, but elicited no resistance or responsive movement. As it seemed very doubtful whether he was capable of swallowing, I dared not take the risk of pouring the liquid into his mouth for fear of suffocating him. A stomach-tube would have solved the difficulty, but, of course, I had not one with me. I had, however, a mouth-speculum which also acted as a gag, and, having propped the patient’s mouth open with this, I hastily slipped off one of the rubber tubes from my stethoscope and inserted into one end of it a vulcanite ear-speculum to serve as a funnel. Then, introducing the other end of the tube into the gullet as far as its length would permit, I cautiously poured a small quantity of the permanganate solution into the extemporized funnel. To my great relief a movement of the throat showed that the swallowing reflex still existed, and, thus encouraged, I poured down the tube as much of the fluid as I thought it wise to administer at one time.

===Chinese===
我毫不耽搁，立即用起手边的救治手段。往玻璃杯里抖进几粒高锰酸钾晶体，兑满水，我便走近病人。他的昏沉已深。我以他衰弱状况下尚称安全的力度，尽量用力摇撼他，却引不起丝毫抗拒或回应的动作。看来他能否吞咽大可怀疑，我不敢冒险把药液直接倒进他嘴里，唯恐把他呛住窒息。若有根胃管，难题便迎刃而解，可我身边自然没有。不过我带着一件兼作开口塞的张口器（mouth-speculum），用它撑住病人的口，我又急忙从听诊器上捋下一截橡皮管，往管的一端插进一只硬橡胶的耳窥器，权作漏斗。然后，把管的另一端顺着长度所及尽量深地送入食管，我小心翼翼地往这只现凑的漏斗里倾入少量高锰酸钾溶液。喉头一动，表明吞咽反射尚存，令我大大松了口气；受了这番鼓励，我便顺着管子，把我认为一次给药尚属稳妥的分量尽量灌了下去。

===Original===
The dose of permanganate that I had given was enough to neutralize any reasonable quantity of the poison that might yet remain in the stomach. I had next to deal with that portion of the drug which had already been absorbed and was exercising its poisonous effects. Taking my hypodermic case from my bag, I prepared in the syringe a full dose of atropine sulphate, which I injected forthwith into the unconscious man’s arm. On arriving home, I looked anxiously at the message-book, and was relieved to find that there were no further visits to be made. Having made up a mixture for Mr. Graves and handed it to the coachman, I raked the ashes of the surgery fire together and sat down to smoke a final pipe while I reflected once more on the singular and suspicious case in which I had become involved. But fatigue soon put an end to my meditations; and having come to the conclusion that the circumstances demanded a further consultation with Thorndyke, I turned down the gas to a microscopic blue spark and betook myself to bed.
