## XVI Necessary Digression / XVI 必要的题外话

===Original===

On board of the ship on which Charlotte and mademoiselle were embarked, was an officer of large, unencumbered fortune and elevated rank, and whom I shall call Crayton.

He was one of those men who, having traveled in their youth, pretend to have contracted a peculiar fondness for everything foreign, and to hold in contempt the productions of their own country; and this affected partiality extended even to the women.

With him, therefore, the blushing modesty and unaffected simplicity of Charlotte passed unnoticed; but the forward pertness of La Rue, the freedom of her conversation, the elegance of her person, mixed with a certain engaging je ne sais quoi, perfectly enchanted him.

The reader, no doubt, has already developed the character of La Rue: designing, artful and selfish, she had accepted the devoirs of Belcour because she was heartily weary of the retired life she led at the school, wished to be released from what she deemed a slavery, and to return to that vortex of folly and dissipation, which had once plunged her into the deepest misery; but her plan, she flattered herself, was now better formed: she resolved to put herself under the protection of no man, till she had first secured a settlement; but the clandestine manner in which she left Madame Du Pont’s prevented her putting this plan in execution, though Belcour solemnly protested he would make her a handsome settlement the moment they arrived at Portsmouth. This he afterward contrived to evade by a pretended hurry of business: La Rue, readily conceiving he never meant to fulfil his promise, determined to change her battery, and attack the heart of Colonel Crayton. She soon discovered the partiality he entertained for her nation; and having imposed on him a feigned tale of distress, representing Belcour as a villain who had seduced her from her friends under promise of marriage, and afterward betrayed her, pretending great remorse for the errors she had committed, and declaring whatever her affection might have been, it was now entirely extinguished, and she wished for nothing more than an opportunity to leave a course of life which her soul abhorred; but she had no friends to apply to, they had all renounced her, and guilt and misery would undoubtedly be her future portion through life.

===Chinese===

夏洛特和小姐所搭的那艘船上，有一位家道殷实、位阶崇高的军官，我姑且叫他克雷顿（Crayton）。

他是这样一种人：年轻时游历过各地，便假装对一切外国东西养成了特别的爱好，又假意蔑视本国的物产；这做作的偏爱甚至波及女人。

因此，在他眼里，夏洛特那泛红晕的羞怯和天然的素朴全然无足挂齿；而拉吕的前卫泼辣、她谈话的随便、她的仪态优雅、再加上某种说不出的动人风致，却把他完全迷住了。

读者想必早已看穿拉吕的为人：她工于心计、狡诈而自私；她之所以接受贝尔库尔的殷勤，是因为她实在厌倦了在学校里过的隐居生活，巴不得从她所谓的奴役中解脱出来，回到那曾经把她卷入深深不幸的愚行与挥霍的漩涡里去；但她自忖如今盘算得更周到了：她决意在首先确保一份财产之前，绝不把自己置于任何男人的保护之下；只是她逃离迪蓬夫人学校的方式鬼鬼祟祟，使她来不及把这计划付诸实行，尽管贝尔库尔郑重许诺，一到朴次茅斯便给她一份可观的财产。事后他却借口事务匆忙，百般推诿：拉吕一眼看穿他根本无意兑现诺言，便决定调转炮口，去攻打克雷顿上校的心。她很快便发觉他对她的国度怀有好感；于是编了一套身世凄惨的假话，把贝尔库尔说成一个以婚约为饵把她从亲友身边诱走、随后又将她抛弃的恶棍，自己则装出对所犯过失痛悔不已的样子，宣称无论旧日情分如何，如今已烟消云散，她只求有个机会脱离这条为她的灵魂所深恶的生活道路；可她投告无门，亲友都已与她断绝，罪恶和苦难必是她此生注定的份。

===Original===

Crayton was possessed of many amiable qualities, though the peculiar trait in his character, which we have already mentioned, in a great measure threw a shade over them. He was beloved for his humanity and benevolence by all who knew him, but he was easy and unsuspicious himself, and became a dupe to the artifice of others.

He was, when very young, united to an amiable Parisian lady, and perhaps it was his affection for her that laid the foundation for the partiality he ever retained for the whole nation. He had by her one daughter, who entered into the world but a few hours before her mother left it. This lady was universally beloved and admired, being endowed with all the virtues of her mother, without the weakness of the father: she was married to Major Beauchamp, and was at this time in the same fleet with her father, attending her husband to New York.

Crayton was melted by the affected contrition and distress of La Rue; he would converse with her for hours, read to her, play cards with her, listen to all her complaints, and promise to protect her to the utmost of his power. La Rue easily saw his character; her sole aim was to awaken a passion in his bosom that might turn out to her advantage, and in this aim she was but too successful, for before the voyage was finished, the infatuated colonel gave her from under his hand a promise of marriage on their arrival at New York, under forfeiture of five thousand pounds.

===Chinese===

克雷顿具有许多可嘉的品质，只是他性格中我们已提到的那一特点，在很大程度上给这些品质蒙上了一层阴影。凡认识他的人都爱他的仁厚与宽慈，但他自己随和而轻信，便做了他人奸计的冤大头。

他很年轻的时候，娶过一位温婉的巴黎女子，也许正是对她的一片深情，奠下了他终身偏爱整个法兰西民族的根子。她给他留下一个女儿，那女儿降生人世不过几个钟头，母亲便离开了人世。这位小姐人见人爱，她兼有母亲的一切美德，却没有父亲的弱点：她嫁给了博尚少校（Major Beauchamp），此刻正随父亲同在这支舰队里，陪丈夫前往纽约（New York）。

拉吕装出的悔恨与凄苦把克雷顿融化了；他肯陪她一谈几个钟头，读书给她听，陪她打牌，听她诉不完的苦，并允诺竭尽所能保护她。拉吕一眼便看穿了他的性格；她惟一的目标，就是在他胸中唤起一种可以为她所用的激情；而这个目标她是太成功了——航程还未结束，这位神魂颠倒的上校便亲笔立据，允诺一到纽约便与她成婚，违者罚金五千镑。

===Original===

And how did our poor Charlotte pass her time during a tedious and tempestuous passage? Naturally delicate, the fatigue and sickness which she endured rendered her so weak as to be almost entirely confined to her bed; yet the kindness and attention of Montraville, in some measure contributed to alleviate her sufferings, and the hope of hearing from her friends soon after their arrival, kept up her spirits, and cheered many a gloomy hour.

But during the voyage a great revolution took place, not only in the fortune of La Rue, but in the bosom of Belcour: whilst in the pursuit of his amour with mademoiselle, he had attended little to the interesting, inobtrusive charms of Charlotte; but when, cloyed by possession, and disgusted with the art and dissimulation of one, he beheld the simplicity and gentleness of the other, the contrast became too striking not to fill him at once with surprise and admiration. He frequently conversed with Charlotte; he found her sensible, well informed, but diffident and unassuming. The langor which the fatigue of her body and perturbation of her mind spread over her delicate features, served only, in his opinion, to render her more lovely: he knew that Montraville did not design to marry her, and he formed a resolution to endeavor to gain her himself, whenever Montraville should leave her.

Let not the reader imagine Belcour’s designs were honorable. Alas! when once a woman has forgot the respect due to herself by yielding to the solicitations of illicit love, she loses all her consequence, even in the eyes of the man whose art has betrayed them, and for whose sake they have sacrificed every valuable consideration.

===Chinese===

那么，我们可怜的夏洛特在漫长而风雨颠簸的航程中是怎么熬过来的呢？她天生娇弱，一路的劳顿和晕船把她折磨得虚弱不堪，几乎整日困在床上；不过蒙特维尔的体贴和照应，多少减轻了她的痛苦，而一到岸便可听到亲友音讯的希望，也支撑着她的精神，给许多阴郁的时辰添了一点亮色。

但就在这段航程中，不仅拉吕的际遇发生了大变化，贝尔库尔的胸怀也起了大革命：当他追逐着与小姐的情事时，对夏洛特那些含蓄不张扬的动人风韵并未留意；可一旦对到手的猎物生了腻，厌恶了一个人的心机与做作，再看另一个的单纯与温婉，这对照便鲜明得叫他登时又惊又慕。他常同夏洛特交谈；发觉她明理而有见识，却又腼腆而不自负。身体的疲惫与心绪的烦乱给她娇柔容貌笼上的那层慵倦，在他看来，只让她更加可爱：他知道蒙特维尔并不打算娶她，便打定主意：一旦蒙特维尔把她撇下，便设法自己把她弄到手。

读者切莫以为贝尔库尔存的是什么光明正大的念头。唉！一个女人一旦屈从于苟且爱情的撺掇，忘了对自己的尊重，她便失去了全部的分量——即便在那个用手段诱她堕落的男人眼里，在她为之牺牲了一切可贵考虑的那个男人眼里，也是如此。
