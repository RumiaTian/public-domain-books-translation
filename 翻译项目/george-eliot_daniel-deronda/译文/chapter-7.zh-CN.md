Anna was in the secret of Rex’s feeling; though for the first time in their lives he had said nothing to her about what he most thought of, and he only took it for granted that she knew it. For the first time, too, Anna could not say to Rex what was continually in her mind. Anna admired her cousin﻿—would have said with simple sincerity, “Gwendolen is always very good to me,” and held it in the order of things for herself to be entirely subject to this cousin; but she looked at her with mingled fear and distrust, with a puzzled contemplation as of some wondrous and beautiful animal whose nature was a mystery, and who, for anything Anna knew, might have an appetite for devouring all the small creatures that were her own particular pets. 「哪儿的话，」雷克斯说，只觉得灵魂在体内像一场急病似的漫开来。「我几乎没伤着什么。你玩得痛快，我真高兴：拿掉一跤来换，我情愿；只可惜磕破了马膝盖。」
格温多琳走到壁炉边站着看火，那站相最不便说话，他只能看到她的侧脸。
