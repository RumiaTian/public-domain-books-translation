## Endnotes / 尾注

===Original===
1. Pronounced by the natives “muggy-yones.” ↩︎

===Chinese===
1. 当地人把它念成「马吉-尤恩斯」（muggy-yones）。 ↩︎

===Original===
2. A famous saloon in West Texas carried this unusual sign. ↩︎

===Chinese===
2. 西得克萨斯一家有名的酒馆，挂过这块不同寻常的招牌。 ↩︎

===Original===
3. Only such cowboys as are in desperate need of employment ever become sheepherders. ↩︎

===Chinese===
3. 只有走投无路、急着找活计的牛仔，才会去当牧羊人。 ↩︎

===Original===
4. Fox is a halfbreed Indian who sent me a lot of verse. Although he had never heard of Walt Whitman, these stanzas suggest that poet. The spelling and punctuation are mine. ↩︎

===Chinese===
4. 福克斯（Fox）是个混血印第安人，寄给我好些诗稿。他虽从未听说过沃尔特·惠特曼（Walt Whitman），这几节诗却叫人想起那位诗人。拼写和标点是我整理的。 ↩︎

===Original===
5. This fragment is not included in Mr. Clark’s poem. ↩︎

===Chinese===
5. 这段零章不在克拉克先生的原诗之内。 ↩︎
