## IX I Break Out of Prison / 第九章 越狱

===Original===
You are now to be ask’d to pass over the next four weeks in as many minutes: as would I had done at the time! For I spent them in a bitter cold cell in the main tower of Bristol keep, with a chair and a pallet of straw for all my furniture, and nothing to stay my fast but the bread and water that the jailer﻿—a sour man, if ever there were one﻿—brought me twice a day.

===Chinese===
诸君此刻须将接下来的四个星期压缩成几分钟读过——我那时何尝不想如此！只因这四个星期全耗在布里斯托尔主塔那间冰冷牢房里：一张椅子、一捆稻草便是全部家当，狱卒——天底下怕再没比他更阴沉的人了——每日两回送来面包和清水，除此之外再无别物果腹。

===Original===
This keep lies in the northwest corner of the outer ward of the castle﻿—a mighty tall pile and strongly built, the walls (as the jailer told me) being a full twenty-five feet thick near the foundations, though by time you ascended to the towers this thickness had dwindled to six feet and no more. In shape ’twas a quadrilateral, a little shorter from north to south than from east to west (in which latter direction it measured sixty feet, about), and had four towers standing at the four corners, whereof mine was five fathoms higher than the rest.

===Chinese===
这塔楼坐落在城堡外庭西北角——楼身极高，筑造坚固，狱卒告诉我，墙基处厚达整整二十五英尺，往上至塔顶便只剩六英尺不到了。整座塔呈四边形，南北略短于东西（东西方向约六十英尺），四角各立一座小塔，我所囚的那座比其余高出五寻。

===Original===
Guess, then, how little I thought of escape, having but one window, a hundred feet (I do believe) above the ground, and that so narrow that, even without the iron bar across it, ’twould barely let my shoulders pass. What concern’d me more was the cold that gnaw’d me continually these winter nights, as I lay thinking of Delia (whom I had not seen since our examination), or gazing out on the patch of frosty heaven that was all my view. ’Twas thus I had heard Bristol bells ringing for Christmas in the town below.

===Chinese===
由此不难想见，我对越狱这事有多不抱指望：整间牢房只有一扇窗，离地怕有百英尺之高，窗口窄得即便拆了那根铁条，我的肩膀也勉勉强强才能挤过去。更叫人难熬的是冬夜的寒气，它整夜整夜地啮噬着我，我躺在草铺上，不是思念德利娅（自从审讯之后就再没见过她），便是凝望那一小块结霜的天空——那便是我全部的风景。布里斯托尔的圣诞钟声便是这样从下方镇子里传进我耳中的。

===Original===
Colonel Essex had been thrice to visit me, and always offer’d many excuses for my treatment; but when he came to question me, why of course I had nothing to tell, so that each visit but served to vex him more. Clearly I was suspected to know a great deal beyond what appear’d in the letter: and no doubt poor Anthony Killigrew had receiv’d some verbal message from His Majesty which he lived not long enough to transmit to me. As ’twas, I kept silence; and the Colonel in return would tell me nothing of what had befallen Delia.

===Chinese===
埃塞克斯上校（Colonel Essex）来看过我三回，每回都对我的待遇百般致歉；可他一开口讯问，我自然无话可说，于是每来一次他便多恼一分。显而易见，他们怀疑我知道的远不止信上所写：可怜的安东尼·基利格鲁（Anthony Killigrew）想必还从国王陛下那里接到了什么口信，只是没来得及转达给我便已不在人世。我缄口不言，上校也从不告诉我德利娅的遭遇。

===Original===
One fine, frosty morning, then, when I had lain in this distress just four weeks, the door of my cell open’d, and there appear’d a young woman, not uncomely, bringing in my bread and water. She was the jailer’s daughter, and wore a heavy bunch of keys at her gird.

===Chinese===
如此熬了整整四个星期，在一个晴朗而霜冻的清晨，牢门开了，走进一个不算难看的年轻女子，手里端着我的面包和水。她是狱卒的女儿，腰带上挂着一大串钥匙。

===Original===
"Oh, good morning!" said I: for till now her father only had visited me, and this was a welcome change.

===Chinese===
"哦，早上好！"我说道——此前一直是她父亲来送饭，换个人来总归是件叫人高兴的事。

===Original===
Instead of answering cheerfully (as I look’d for), she gave a little nod of the head, rather sorrowful, and answered:﻿—

===Chinese===
她并未如我所期待的那样爽快答话，而是略带忧伤地点了点头，答道——

===Original===
"Father’s abed with the ague."

===Chinese===
"父亲卧病在床，发了寒热。"

===Original===
"Now you cannot expect me to be sorry."

===Chinese===
"这你可别指望我会难过。"

===Original===
"Nay," she said; and I caught her looking at me with something like compassion in her blue eyes, which mov’d me to cry out suddenly﻿—

===Chinese===
"不会的，"她说。我瞥见她那双蓝眼睛里带着几分怜悯，便脱口喊道——

===Original===
"I think you are woman enough to like a pair of lovers."

===Chinese===
"我想你是个通情达理的姑娘，总不至于反对成全一对有情人吧。"

===Original===
"Oh, aye: but where’s t’other half of the pair?"

===Chinese===
"哦，那是自然——可另一半在哪儿呢？"

===Original===
"You’re right. The young gentlewoman that was brought hither with me﻿—I know not if she loves me: but this I do know﻿—I would give my hand to learn her whereabouts, and how she fares."

===Chinese===
"你说得对。和我一同被押来的那位年轻小姐——我不知她是否爱我：但有一件事我确知——我愿拿一只手去换她的下落，哪怕只得知她过得好不好。"

===Original===
"Eat thy loaf," put in the girl very suddenly, setting down the plate and pitcher.

===Chinese===
"吃了你的面包吧，"那姑娘突兀地插了一句，放下盘子和水罐。

===Original===
’Twas odd, but I seem’d to hear a sob in her voice. However, her back was toward me as I glanc’d up. And next moment she was gone, locking the iron door behind her.

===Chinese===
说来也怪，我似乎听见她声音里带着一丝哽咽。可我抬头望去时，她已背过身去。转眼间她便出去了，随手锁上铁门。

===Original===
I turn’d from my breakfast with a sigh, having for the moment tasted the hope to hear something of Delia. But in a while, feeling hungry, I pick’d up the loaf beside me, and broke it in two.

===Chinese===
我叹了口气，搁下早餐，方才那一瞬的希望——或许能听到德利娅的消息——转眼便散了。过了一会儿，腹中饥饿，便拿起身边的面包掰成两半。

===Original===
To my amaze, out dropp’d something that jingled on the stone floor.

===Chinese===
令我大吃一惊的是，有什么东西掉出来，叮当一声落在石板地上。

===Original===
’Twas a small file: and examining the loaf again, I found a clasp-knife also, and a strip of paper, neatly folded, hidden in the bread.

===Chinese===
是一把小锉刀！我再翻检面包，又找到一把折叠小刀，以及一条叠得整整齐齐的纸条，都藏在面包里。

===Original===
> Deare Jack,
> Colonel Essex, finding no good come of his interrogatories, hath set me at large; though I continue under his eye, to wit, with a dowager of his acquaintance, a Mistress Finch. Wee dwell in a private house midway down St. Thomas his street, in Redcliffe: and she hath put a dismal dress upon me (Jack, ’tis *hideous*), but otherwise uses me not ill. But take care of thyself, my deare friend: for though the Colonel be a gentilman, he is press’d by them about him, and at our last interview I noted a mischief in his eye. Canst use this file?﻿—(but take care: all the gates I saw guarded with troopers today.) This by one who hath been my friend: for whose sake tear the paper up. And beleeve your cordial, loving comrade
> D. K.

===Chinese===
> 亲爱的杰克：
> 埃塞克斯上校见讯问无果，已将我释放，只是仍在他监视之下——暂住他一位相识的寡妇芬奇太太（Mistress Finch）家中。我们住在雷德克利夫区圣托马斯街中段的一所私宅里。她给我套了一件难看至极的衣裳（杰克，那简直*没法看*），不过旁的倒还过得去。你千万保重，我亲爱的朋友：上校虽是个绅士，却被身边人裹挟着，上次见面我注意到他眼里藏着恶意。这把锉刀你用得上吗？——（但要当心：我今天看见各道城门都有骑兵把守。）写这信的人一直是你的朋友——为了她，请将此信销毁。相信你真诚的、爱你的同伴。
> D. K.

===Original===
After reading this a dozen times, till I had it by heart, I tore the letter into small pieces and hid them in my pocket. This done, I felt lighter-hearted than for many a day, and (rather for employment than with any farther view) began lazily to rub away at my window bar. The file work’d well. By noon the bar was half sever’d, and I broke off to whistle a tune. ’Twas﻿—

===Chinese===
我把信读了十几遍，直到倒背如流，才将它撕成碎片藏进口袋。做完这些，我心头久违地轻松起来，便——与其说有什么打算，不如说是为了打发时间——懒洋洋地锉起窗上的铁条。锉刀很好使。到中午时铁条已断了一半，我停下来吹了支小曲。曲子是——

===Original===
> "Vivre en tout cas
>  C’est le grand soulas"﻿—

===Chinese===
> "无论如何活着
>  这便是莫大的慰藉"——

===Original===
and I broke off to hear the key turning in my lock.

===Chinese===
正吹着，忽听钥匙在锁孔里转动，我便住了口。

===Original===
The jailer’s daughter enter’d with my second meal. Her eyes were red with weeping.

===Chinese===
狱卒的女儿端着第二顿饭进来了。她双眼哭得通红。

===Original===
Said I, "Does your father beat you?"

===Chinese===
我问："你父亲打你了？"

===Original===
"He has, before now," she replied: "but not today."

===Chinese===
"以前是打过，"她答道，"不过今天没有。"

===Original===
"Then why do you weep?"

===Chinese===
"那你为什么哭？"

===Original===
"Not for that."

===Chinese===
"不是为了这个。"

===Original===
"For what then?"

===Chinese===
"那为了什么？"

===Original===
"For you﻿—oh, dear, dear! How shall I tell it? They are going to﻿—to﻿—" She sat down on the chair, and sobb’d in her apron.

===Chinese===
"为了你——哦，天哪，天哪！我该怎么说？他们要——要——"她一屁股坐到椅子上，用围裙捂着脸抽泣。

===Original===
"What is’t they are going to do?"

===Chinese===
"他们要干什么？"

===Original===
"To﻿—to﻿—h‑hang you."

===Chinese===
"要——要——绞——绞死你。"

===Original===
"The devil! When?"

===Chinese===
"见鬼！什么时候？"

===Original===
"Tut‑tut‑tomorrow mo‑horning!"

===Chinese===
"明——明天早——早上！"

===Original===
I went suddenly very cold all over. There was silence for a moment, and then I heard the noise of someone dropping a plank in the courtyard below.

===Chinese===
我浑身骤然冰冷。沉默了片刻，我听见下面院子里有人在放木板的声响。

===Original===
"What’s that?"

===Chinese===
"那是什么？"

===Original===
"The gug‑gug﻿—"

===Chinese===
"绞——绞架——"

===Original===
"Gallows?"

===Chinese===
"绞架？"

===Original===
She nodded.

===Chinese===
她点了点头。

===Original===
"You are but a weak girl," said I, meditating.

===Chinese===
"你不过是个弱女子，"我思忖着说。

===Original===
"Aye: but there’s a dozen troopers on the landing below."

===Chinese===
"是啊：可楼下楼梯口还有十几个骑兵呢。"

===Original===
"Then, my dear, you must lock me up," I decided gloomily, and fell to whistling﻿—

===Chinese===
"那么，亲爱的，你只好把我锁起来了，"我阴郁地做了决定，随即又吹起口哨——

===Original===
> "Vivre en tout cas
>  C’est le grand soulas"﻿—

===Chinese===
> "无论如何活着
>  这便是莫大的慰藉"——

===Original===
A workman’s hammer in the court below chim’d in, beating out the tune, and driving the moral home. I heard a low sob behind me. The jailer’s daughter was going.

===Chinese===
下面院子里一个工匠的锤声应和着，一下一下敲出曲调，仿佛在为这歌词配注脚。身后传来一声低低的抽泣。狱卒的女儿要走了。

===Original===
"Lend me your bodkin, my dear, for a memento."

===Chinese===
"把你的簪子借我做个念想吧，亲爱的。"

===Original===
She pull’d it out and gave it to me.

===Chinese===
她拔下簪子递给我。

===Original===
"Thank you, and now goodbye! Stop: here’s a kiss to take to my dear mistress. They shan’t hang me, my dear."

===Chinese===
"多谢你，再见了！等一下——这一吻请你转交给我的好小姐。他们休想绞死我，亲爱的。"

===Original===
The girl went out, sobbing, and lock’d the door after her.

===Chinese===
那姑娘哭着出去了，随手锁上了门。

===Original===
I sat down for a while, feeling doleful. For I found myself extremely young to be hang’d. But soon the *whang﻿—whang!* of the hammer below rous’d me. "Come," I thought, "I’ll see what that rascal is doing, at any rate," and pulling the file from my pocket, began to attack the window bar with a will. I had no need for silence, at this great height above the ground: and besides, the hammering continued lustily.

===Chinese===
我坐了一会儿，满心凄凉——毕竟年纪轻轻就要被绞死，未免太不值了。可下面那"砰——砰——！"的锤声很快把我惊醒。"好吧，"我心想，"无论如何我得看看那家伙在捣什么鬼。"我从口袋里掏出锉刀，铆足了劲继续对付那根铁条。在这离地百尺的高处，完全不必顾忌声响——何况底下锤声正起劲呢。

===Original===
Daylight was closing as I finish’d my task and, pulling the two pieces of the bar aside, thrust my head out at the window.

===Chinese===
完工时天色已暗，我把断成两截的铁条拨到一旁，将头探出窗外。

===Original===
Directly under me, and about twenty feet from the ground, I saw a beam projecting, about six feet long, over a sort of doorway in the wall. Under this beam, on a ladder, was a carpenter fellow at work, fortifying it with two supporting timbers that rested on the sill of the doorway. He was merry enough over the job, and paused every now and again to fling a remark to a little group of soldiers that stood idling below, where the fellow’s workbag and a great coil of rope rested by the ladder’s foot.

===Chinese===
正下方离地约二十英尺处，有一根约六英尺长的横梁从墙上一扇门洞上方伸出来。一个木匠正站在梯子上忙着给横梁加两根撑木，撑木搁在门洞的石槛上。他干得挺来劲，不时停下来冲下面闲站着的几个士兵说笑几句——他的工具袋和一大盘绳子就搁在梯脚边。

===Original===
"Reckon, Sammy," said one, pulling a long tobacco pipe from his mouth and spitting, " ’tis a long while since thy last job o’ the sort."

===Chinese===
"我说，萨米，"一个士兵从嘴里拔出长烟斗，吐了口唾沫，"你上回干这活儿怕是有年头了吧。"

===Original===
"Aye, lad: terrible disrepair this place has fall’n into. But send us a cheerful heart, say I! Instead o’ the viper an’ owl, shall henceforward be hangings of men an’ all manner o’ diversion."

===Chinese===
"可不是嘛，伙计：这地方破败得不成样子了。不过我说，打起精神来！往后这儿可没有蛇和猫头鹰看了，换成了吊死人，保准比什么都有趣。"

===Original===
I kept my head out of sight and listen’d.

===Chinese===
我把头缩回暗处，继续听着。

===Original===
"What time doth ’a swing?" ask’d another of the soldiers.

===Chinese===
"几点开绞？"另一个士兵问。

===Original===
"I heard the Colonel give orders for nine o’clock tomorrow," answer’d the first soldier, spitting again.

===Chinese===
"我听上校下令，明天九点，"第一个士兵又吐了口唾沫，答道。

===Original===
The clock over the barbican struck four: and in a minute was being answer’d from tower after tower, down in the city.

===Chinese===
城堡门楼上钟敲了四点：片刻之后，城中塔楼的钟声便此起彼伏地应和起来。

===Original===
"Four o’clock!" cried the man on the ladder: "time to stop work, and here goes for the last nail!" He drove it in and prepar’d to descend.

===Chinese===
"四点了！"梯子上那木匠叫道，"该收工了，钉上最后一颗钉子走人！"他把钉子敲进去，准备下来。

===Original===
"Hi!" shouted a soldier, "you’ve forgot the rope."

===Chinese===
"喂！"一个士兵喊道，"你把绳子忘了。"

===Original===
"That’ll wait till tomorrow. There’s a staple to drive in, too. I tell you I’m dry, and want my beer."

===Chinese===
"明天再说。还有个铁环要打呢。实话说我嗓子都冒烟了，得喝杯啤酒去。"

===Original===
He whipp’d his apron round his waist, and gathering up his nails, went down the ladder. At the foot he pick’d up his bag, shoulder’d the ladder, and loung’d away, leaving the coil of rope lying there. Presently the soldiers saunter’d off also, and the court was empty.

===Chinese===
他把围裙往腰上一缠，拾起钉子，走下梯子。到了梯脚，他拎起工具袋，扛起梯子，晃晃悠悠地走了，那盘绳子就丢在原地。不一会儿士兵们也闲逛散去，院子空了。

===Original===
Now up to this moment I had but one idea of avoiding my fate, and that was to kill myself. ’Twas to this end I had borrow’d the bodkin of the maid. Afterward I had a notion of flinging myself from the window as they came for me. But now, as I look’d down on that coil of rope lying directly below, a prettier scheme struck me. I sat down on the floor of my cell and pull’d off my boots and stockings.

===Chinese===
在此之前，我一心想着的逃避厄运的法子不过是一死了之——向那姑娘借簪子便是为此。后来又想过等他们来提人时纵身跳窗。但此刻，当我低头看见那盘绳子就扔在正下方，一个更好的主意忽然冒了出来。我在牢房地板上坐下，脱掉靴子和袜子。

===Original===
’Twas such a pretty plan that I got into a fever of impatience. Drawing off a stocking and picking out the end of the yarn, I began to unravel the knitting for dear life, until the whole lay, a heap of thread, on the floor. I then serv’d the other in the same way: and at the end had two lines, each pretty near four hundred yards in length: which now I divided into eight lines of about a hundred yards each.

===Chinese===
这主意实在太妙，我急得坐立不安。我扯下一只袜子，找到线头，拼命往下拆线，直到整只袜子变成地板上一堆毛线。另一只也照此办理：最终我得到两根各近四百码长的线——再把它们分成八股，每股约一百码。

===Original===
With these I set to work, and by the end of twenty minutes had plaited a rope﻿—if rope, indeed, it could be called﻿—weak to be sure, but long enough to reach the ground with plenty to spare. Then, having bent my bodkin to the form of a hook, I tied it to the end of my cord, weighted it with a crown from my pocket, and clamber’d up to the window. I was going to angle for the hangman’s rope.

===Chinese===
我动手编绳，二十分钟后便搓出一根——姑且叫它绳子吧——虽然肯定不结实，但长度绰绰有余，够垂到地面还有余。然后我把簪子弯成钩状，系在绳头，从口袋里摸出一枚克朗当坠子，爬到窗口。我要去钓那刽子手的绳索了。

===Original===
’Twas near dark by this; but I could just distinguish it on the paving stones below, and looking about the court, saw that no one was astir. I wriggled first my head, then a shoulder, through the opening, and let the line run gently through my hand. There was still many yards left, that could be paid out, when I heard my coin tinkle softly on the pavement.

===Chinese===
此时天色已近全黑；但我还能勉强辨认出地上的绳盘，四下张望，院中无人走动。我先把头，再把一个肩膀挤过窗洞，让绳子从手中缓缓滑出。还有好几码余量时，我听见坠子轻轻叮当落在石板路上。

===Original===
Then began my difficulty. A dozen times I pull’d my hook across the coil before it hitch’d; and then a full three score of times the rope slipped away before I had rais’d it a dozen yards. My elbow was raw, almost, with leaning on the sill, and I began to lose heart and head, when, to my delight, the bodkin caught and held. It had fasten’d on a kink in the rope, not far from the end. I began to pull up, hand over hand, trembling all the while like a leaf.

===Chinese===
