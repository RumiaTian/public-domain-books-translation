## VII I Find a Comrade / 我找到一位同伴

===Original===
But I must go back a little and tell you what befell in my expedition.

===Chinese===
不过我还得倒回去一点儿，把此番探险的经过讲给诸位听。

===Original===
I had scarce trudged out of sight of my friends, down the hill, when it struck me that my footprints in the snow were in the last degree dangerous to them, and might lead Settle and his crew straight to the dingle. Here was a fix. I stood for some minutes nonpluss’d, when above the stillness of the wood (for the wind had dropp’d) a faint sound as of running water caught my ear, and help’d me to an idea.

The sound seem’d to come from my left. Turning aside I made across the hill toward it, and after two hundred paces or so came on a tiny brook, not two feet across, that gush’d down the slope with a quite considerable chatter and impatience. The bed of it was mainly earth, with here and there a large stone or root to catch the toe: so that, as I stepped into the water and began to thread my way down between the banks of snow, ’twas necessary to look carefully to my steps.

===Chinese===
我下山刚走得望不见友人的身影，忽然省悟过来：我留在雪上的脚印，对他们实在是天大的祸患，没准会把塞特尔和他的手下径直引到幽谷里去。这可难办了。我怔怔地立了好几分钟，一筹莫展；忽然，在松林的一片寂静之上（风已经停了），一缕流水似的轻响传进我耳朵，替我解出了个主意。

那声音似乎来自左边。我偏转方向，横过山坡朝它走去，约莫两百步之后，遇见一条小小的溪流，宽不足两英尺，顺着坡面哗哗地冲下来，那份喧腾急躁的劲儿倒颇为可观。溪床大半是泥土，东一处西一处横着大石头或树根，正好绊人的脚尖：因此我一脚踏进水里、开始在两岸积雪之间穿行而下时，便不得不步步留神。

===Original===
Here and there the brook fetch’d a leap down a sharper declivity, or shot over a hanging stone: but, save for the wetting I took in these places, my progress was easy enough. I must have waded in this manner for half a mile, keeping the least possible noise, when at an angle ahead I spied a clearing among the pines, and to the right of the stream, on the very verge, a hut of logs standing, with a wood rick behind it.

’Twas a low building, but somewhat long, and I guess’d it to be, in summer time, a habitation for the woodcutters. But what surpris’d me was to hear a dull, moaning noise, very regular and disquieting, that sounded from the interior of the hut. I listen’d, and hit on the explication. ’Twas the sound of snoring.

===Chinese===
溪水时不时从更陡的坡面纵身一跃，或从一块悬空的石头上激射出去：不过除了在这些地方吃水受湿，我一路走得还算顺当。就这样蹚水而行、尽量不弄出半点声响，约莫走了半英里，前方一处拐角，我发现松林间有片空地，溪流右侧、紧贴着边上立着一座圆木小屋，屋后堆着一垛柴。

那屋子低矮，却有些长，我猜到了夏天，便是伐木工们的住处。可叫我吃惊的是，屋子里面传出一阵沉闷的、呻吟似的声响，节奏均匀，听得人心里发毛。我侧耳细听，恍然悟出了缘故。那是打鼾的声音。

===Original===
Drawing nearer with caution, I noticed, in that end of the hut which stood over the stream, a gap, or window hole. The sound issued through this like the whirring of a dozen looms. “He must be an astonishing fellow,” thought I, “that can snore in this fashion. I’ll have a peep before I wake him.” I waded down till I stood under the sill, put both hands upon it, and pulling myself up quiet as a mouse, stuck my face in at the window﻿—and then very nearly sat back into the brook for fright.

===Chinese===
我小心翼翼地凑近，看见屋子横跨溪流的那一头有个豁口，或者说窗洞。那声音从洞里涌出来，活像十几台织机一齐嗡鸣。「能这样打鼾的，必是位了不得的人物，」我心想，「趁没叫醒他，且先窥上一眼。」我蹚水而下，到窗台底下站定，双手按住台沿，像老鼠一般悄没声儿把身子撑上去，将脸探进窗口——随即吓得差点儿一屁股坐回溪水里去。

===Original===
For I had gazed straight down into the upturn’d faces of Captain Settle and his gang.

===Chinese===
因为我一眼望下去，正对着塞特尔上尉和他那伙人仰起的脸。

===Original===
How long I stood there, with the water rushing past my ankles and my body turning from cold to hot, and back again, I cannot tell you. But ’twas until, hearing no pause in the sleepers’ chorus, I found courage for another peep: and that must have been some time.

There were but six rascals beside the Captain (so that Jacques must have died hard, thought I), and such a raffle of arms and legs and swollen upturn’d faces as they made I defy you to picture. For they were pack’d close as herrings; and the hut was fill’d up with their horses, ready saddled, and rubbing shoulder to loin, so narrow was the room. It needed the open window to give them air: and even so, ’twas not over-fresh inside.

===Chinese===
我在那里立了多久——溪水从我脚踝边奔流而过，我的身子忽而由冷转热、忽而由热转冷——我说不上来。只知道自己一直立着，直到听出酣睡者们的大合唱没有半刻停顿，才鼓起勇气又窥了第二眼：这中间想必过了好一阵子。

上尉之外只有六个恶棍（可见雅克临死之前必曾死战一场，我想），至于他们弄出的那副手脚纠缠、脸膛肿胀朝天的乱象，我倒要请诸君自己想象看。因为他们挤得像桶里的鲱鱼一般紧密；屋子又让他们的马塞满了——鞍辔齐备，肩擦着胯，屋里就是这么窄。全凭敞开的窗口透空气：饶是如此，屋内的气味也说不上多新鲜。

===Original===
I had no mind to stay: but before leaving found myself in the way of playing these villains a pretty trick. To right and left of the window, above their heads, extended two rude shelves that now were heap’d with what I conjectured to be the spoils of the larder of the Three Cups. Holding my breath and thrusting my head and shoulders into the room, I ran my hand along and was quickly possess’d of a boil’d ham, two capons, a loaf, the half of a cold pie, and a basket holding three dozen eggs. All these prizes I filched one by one, with infinite caution.

I was gently pulling the basket through the window hole, when I heard one of the crew yawn and stretch himself in his sleep. So, determining to risk no more, I quietly pack’d the basket, slung it on my right arm, and with the ham grasp’d by the knuckle in my left, made my way up the stream.

===Chinese===
我无意久留：可临走之前，却起意要同这帮恶棍玩一手漂亮的把戏。窗洞两侧、就在他们头顶上方，各架着一条粗陋的搁板，上面堆着的，我猜想必是「三杯」客栈伙房里掳来的吃食。我屏住呼吸，把头和肩膀探进屋内，伸手沿着搁板一路摸过去，顷刻之间便到手了一只煮火腿、两只阉鸡、一条面包、半块冷馅饼，还有一篮子三打鸡蛋。这些战利品，我一件一件、以十二万分的小心逐一偷了出来。

我正轻轻把篮子往窗外拖，忽听那伙人中有一个打着呵欠、在睡梦里伸起懒腰来。于是我不敢再冒风险，悄悄把篮子收拾停当，挎上右臂，左手攥住火腿的关节骨，便顺溪而上了。

===Original===
’Twas thus laden that I enter’d the dingle, and came on the sad sight therein. I set down the ham as a thing to be asham’d of, and bar’d my head. The girl lifted her face, and turning, all white and tragical, saw me.

“My father is dead, sir.”

I stoop’d and pil’d a heap of fresh snow over the blood stains. There was no intent in this but to hide the pity that chok’d me. She had still to hear about her brother, Anthony. Turning, as by a sudden thought, I took her hand. She look’d into my eyes, and her own filled with tears. ’Twas the human touch that loosen’d their flow, I think: and sinking down again beside her father, she wept her fill.

===Chinese===
我就这样满载而归，进了幽谷，撞见谷中那幅伤心的光景。我把火腿放下来，仿佛它是一件叫人羞愧的东西，又摘下帽子。姑娘抬起脸，转过身来——满面苍白，一片惨戚——望见了我。

「我父亲死了，先生。」

我俯下身去，捧起一堆干净的新雪，盖住那些血迹。这样做没有别的用意，只为掩住那哽在我喉头的哀怜。关于她兄弟安东尼的下落，她还有待听闻。我像是蓦然想起什么似的转过身，握住她的手。她望进我的眼睛里，自己的眼里便涨满了泪水。我想，正是这一点人情的触碰，松开了泪水的闸门：她重新在父亲身旁跪坐下去，痛痛快快哭了个够。

===Original===
“Mistress Killigrew,” I said, as soon as the first violence of her tears was abated, “I have still some news that is ill hearing. Your enemies are encamp’d in the woods, about a half mile below this”﻿—and with that I told my story.

“They have done their worst, sir.”

“No.”

She looked at me with a question on her lip.

Said I, “you must believe me yet a short while without questioning.”

Considering for a moment, she nodded. “You have a right, sir, to be trusted, though I know not so much as your name. Then we must stay close in hiding?” she added very sensibly, though with the last word her voice trail’d off, and she began again to weep.

===Chinese===
「基利格鲁小姐，」等她头一阵倾泻的眼泪稍见平息，我便开口道，「我还有一些消息，听着只会叫人难受。你的仇人们就扎营在树林里，离此地约半英里。」——接着我便把自己的经历讲了一遍。

「他们最毒的手段已经使尽了，先生。」

「不。」

她望着我，唇边挂着一个疑问。

我说：「你还得再信我一小会儿，暂且不要问。」

她思忖片刻，点了点头。「你有这个权利，先生，值得人家信任，虽说我连你的名字都还不知道。那么，我们须得紧紧藏起来才是？」她加上这一句，极有见地；只是说到末一个字，声音渐渐低了下去，又重新哭了起来。

===Original===
But in time, having cover’d the dead baronet’s body with sprays of the wither’d bracken, I drew her to a little distance and prevail’d on her to nibble a crust of the loaf. Now, all this while, it must be remembered, I was in my shirt sleeves, and the weather bitter cold. Which at length her sorrow allow’d her to notice.

“Why, you are shivering, sore!” she said, and running, drew my buff-coat from her father’s body, and held it out to me.

“Indeed,” I answer’d, “I was thinking of another expedition to warm my blood.” And promising to be back in half an hour, I follow’d down my former tracks toward the stream.

===Chinese===
不过到得后来，我们用枯萎的蕨叶把已故从男爵的遗体掩好，我把她引到稍远的地方，劝她啃了几口面包皮。诸君须记得，这半天工夫，我身上始终只穿一件衬衫，而天气又冷得砭骨。她的悲伤总算容她注意到了这一点。

「哎呀，瞧你抖得多厉害！」她说着跑过去，从她父亲遗体上取来那件皮甲，递到我面前。

「可不是，」我答道，「我正想着再来一次远征，好叫血脉暖和暖和。」于是我答应半个钟头内回来，便顺着原先的脚印，朝溪边走去。

===Original===
Within twenty minutes I was back, running and well-nigh shouting with joy.

“Come!” I cried to her, “come and see for yourself!”

===Chinese===
不到二十分钟我就回来了，一路奔跑，欢喜得几乎要放声喊叫。

「来！」我朝她喊道，「来亲眼看看！」

===Original===
What had happen’d was this:﻿—Wading cautiously down the brook, I had cause suddenly to prick up my ears and come to a halt. ’Twas the muffled tramp of hoofs that I heard, and creeping a bit further, I caught a glimpse, beyond the hut, of a horse and rider disappearing down the woods. He was the last of the party, as I guess’d from the sound of voices and jingling of bits further down the slope. Advancing on the hut with more boldness, I found it deserted. I scrambled up on the bank and round to the entrance. The snow before it was trampled and sullied by the footmarks of men and horses: and as I noted this, came Settle’s voice calling up the slope﻿—

===Chinese===
事情的经过是这样的：——我蹑手蹑脚顺溪而下，忽然不得不竖起耳朵、收住脚步。我听见的是一阵沉闷的马蹄声；再往前爬了几步，便瞥见小屋那一头，一人一骑正没入林子深处。那是这伙人的殿后，再往坡下去些，那人语声和马嚼子的叮当声便是明证。我胆子大了些，向小屋逼上前去，发现它已人去屋空。我爬上溪岸，绕到门口。门前的积雪被人脚马蹄践踏得一片乌糟：正当我留意到这一点，坡上头传来塞特尔的喊声——

===Original===
“Jerry﻿—Jerry Toy!”

A nearer voice hail’d in answer.

“Where’s Reuben?”

“Coming, Captain﻿—close behind!”

“Curse him for a loitering idiot! We’ve wasted time enough, as ’tis,” called back the Captain. “How in thunder is a man to find the road out of this cursed wood?”

“Straight on, Cap’n﻿—you can’t miss it,” shouted another voice, not two gunshots below.

A volcano of oaths pour’d up from Settle. I did not wait for the end of them: but ran back for Mistress Delia.

===Chinese===
「杰里——杰里·托伊！」

近处一个声音高声应答。

「鲁本呢？」

「来了，上尉——就在后头！」

「这个磨磨蹭蹭的蠢货，真该死！我们已经耽误得够久了。」上尉回喊道。「这该死的林子里，鬼叫人怎么找得出路？」

「一直往前，上尉——错不了。」另一个声音喊道，就在坡下不出两枪远近的地方。

塞特尔那里顿时喷起一座火山似的连篇咒骂。我没有等它喷完，便跑回去接德利娅小姐了。

===Original===
Together we descended to the hut. By this time the voices had faded away in distance. Yet to make sure that the rascals had really departed, we follow’d their tracks for some way, beside the stream; and suddenly came to a halt with cries of joyful surprise.

The brook had led us to a point where, over a stony fall veil’d with brown bracken, it plunged into a narrow ravine. Standing on the lip, where the water took a smoother glide before leaping, we saw the line of the ravine mark’d by a rift in the pines, and through this a slice of the country that lay below. ’Twas a level plain, well watered, and dotted here and there with houses. A range of wooded hills clos’d the view, and toward them a broad road wound gently, till the eye lost it at their base. All this was plain enough, in spite of the snow that cover’d the landscape. For the sun had burst out above, and the few flakes that still fell looked black against his brilliance and the dazzling country below.

===Chinese===
我们一同下到小屋那里。这时人声已在远处消散。不过，为确证这伙恶棍真的去了，我们还是沿着溪边、跟随他们的足迹走了一程；忽然双双止步，发出又惊又喜的欢呼。

溪水把我们引到一处地方：它从一道蒙着褐色蕨衣的石瀑上翻落，一头扎进狭窄的峡谷。我们立在崖唇上——水在这儿先舒成一道平缓的滑流，再纵身跃下——只见峡谷的走向由松林间一道裂隙标出，透过裂隙，望得见下方乡野的一角。那是一片平畴，水道纵横，四处点缀着屋舍。一带林木蓊郁的丘陵收住了视野，一条宽阔的大路朝丘陵蜿蜒而去，走势从容，直到目光在山脚下把它跟丢。尽管积雪覆着整片原野，这一切仍看得清清楚楚。因为太阳已在头顶破云而出，仍在飘落的几片雪花，映着他的光辉和下方耀眼的乡野，看上去竟是黑的。

===Original===
But what caus’d our joy was to see, along the road, a small cavalcade moving away from us, with many bright glances of light and color, as their steel caps and sashes took the sunshine﻿—a pretty sight, and the prettier because it meant our present deliverance.

The girl beside me gave a cry of delight, then sigh’d; and after a minute began to walk back toward the hut: where I left her, and ran up hill for the basket and ham. On my return, I found her examining a heap of rusty tools that, it seem’d, she had found on a shelf of the building. ’Twas no light help to the good fellowship that afterward united us, that from the first I could read her thoughts often without words; and for this reason, that her eyes were as candid as the noonday.

===Chinese===
而令我们欢喜的，是大路上望见的一小队人马正离我们远去，钢盔与肩带迎着日光，闪出点点明亮的光与色——一幅好看的景象，而且加倍好看，因为它意味着我们眼下的解救。

我身旁的姑娘欢叫一声，随即又叹了口气；过了一分钟，便转身朝小屋走去：我把她留在那里，跑上山坡去取篮子和火腿。回来时，见她正在察看一堆生了锈的工具——看那情形，是从屋里搁板上翻出来的。日后把我们联结在一起的那份美好情谊，从一开始便得了这个不小的助力：我常常无须她开口，便能读出她的心思；而所以能够如此，是因为她的眼睛坦白得如同正午的日光。

===Original===
So now I answer’d her aloud﻿—

“This afternoon we may venture down to the plain, where no doubt we shall find a clergyman to sell us a patch of holy ground﻿—”

“Holy ground?” She look’d at me awhile and shook her head. “I am not of your religion,” she said.

“And your father?”

“I think no man ever discovered my father’s religion. Perhaps there was none to discover: but he was no bad father,” she steadied her voice and went on:﻿—“He would prefer the hillside to your ‘holy ground.’ ”

===Chinese===
于是此刻我便出声回答她——

「今天下午我们就可以冒险下到平原上去，在那边，毫无疑问，我们能找到一位牧师，卖给我们一小块圣地——」

「圣地？」她望了我一阵，摇了摇头。「我不奉你们的教。」她说。

「那么你父亲呢？」

「我想，从没有人窥破过我父亲的宗教。也许根本无物可窥破：但他决不是个坏父亲。」她稳住声音，接着说：——「比起你们的『圣地』来，他更中意这片山坡。」

===Original===
So, an hour later, I delv’d his grave in the frosty earth, close by the spot where he lay. Somehow, I shiver’d all the while, and had a cruel shooting pain in my wound that was like to have mastered me before the task was ended. But I managed to lower the body softly into the hole and to cover it reverently from sight: and afterward stood leaning on my spade and feeling very light in the head, while the girl knelt and pray’d for her father’s soul.

And the picture of her as she knelt is the last I remember, till I open’d my eyes, and was amazed to find myself on my back, and staring up at darkness.

===Chinese===
于是，一个钟头之后，我便在他倒卧之处的近旁，从冻土里为他掘墓。不知怎么，我自始至终抖个不住，伤口里更有一阵阵火辣辣的剧痛，险些没等活计干完就把我击垮。但我总算把遗体轻轻放进了穴里，又恭而敬之地掩上土，不叫它再见天日：随后便拄着铁锹立着，只觉头重脚轻，而这时姑娘已跪了下去，为她父亲的灵魂祈祷。

她跪着祈祷的那幅画面，是我记得的最后一幕；待我再睁开眼，惊愕地发现自己仰面躺着，正对着一片黑暗向上凝望。

===Original===
“What has happen’d?”

“I think you are very ill,” said a voice: “can you lean on me, and reach the hut?”

“Why, yes: that is, I think so. Why is everything dark?”

“The sun has been down for hours. You have been in a swoon first, and then talk’d﻿—oh, such nonsense! Shame on me, to let you catch this chill!”

She help’d me to my feet and steadied me: and how we reached the hut I cannot tell you. It took more than one weary hour, as I now know; but, at the time, hours and minutes were one to me.

===Chinese===
「出了什么事？」

「我想你病得不轻，」一个声音说：「能靠着我，走到小屋去吗？」

「唔，能：就是说，我想能吧。怎么四下里一片漆黑？」

「太阳落下去好几个钟头了。你先是昏厥，后来又说胡话——哎呀，好一派胡言！都怨我不好，竟让你招了这场寒气！」

她扶我起身，撑稳了我：我们是怎么到的小屋，我说不上来。如今我才知道，那足足耗了一个多钟头，疲惫不堪的钟头；可在当时，钟头与分钟于我本是一样。

===Original===
In that hut I lay four nights and four days, between ague fit and fever. And that is all the account I can give of the time, save that, on the second day, the girl left me alone in the hut and descended to the plain, where, after asking at many cottages for a physician, she was forced to be content with an old woman reputed to be amazingly well skill’d in herbs and medicines; whom, after a day’s trial, she turn’d out of doors. On the fourth day, fearing for my life, she made another descent, and coming to a wayside tavern, purchased a pint of aqua vitae, carried it back, and mix’d a potion that threw me into a profuse sweat. The same evening I sat up, a sound man.

===Chinese===
