## XX The Adventure of the Ledge; and How I Shook Hands with My Comrade / XX 岩礁历险，以及我如何与同伴握手

===Original===
We had some ado to find the gate: but no sooner were through, and upon the high road, than I lash’d the horses up the hill at a gallop. To guide us between the dark hedges we had only our lantern and the glare ahead. The dishes and cups clash’d and rattled as the hearse bump’d in the ruts, swaying wildly: a dozen times Matt was near being pitch’d clean out of his seat. With my legs planted firm, I flogg’d away like a madman; and like mad creatures the horses tore upward.

On the summit a glance show’d us all—the wild crimson’d sky—the sea running with lines of fire—and against it the inky headland whereon the House of Gleys flar’d like a beacon. Already from one wing—*our* wing—a leaping column of flame whirl’d up through the roof, and was swept seaward in smoke and sparks. I mark’d the coast line, the cliff tracks, the masts and hull of the *Godsend* standing out, clear as day; and nearer, the yellow light flickering over the fields of young corn. We saw all this, and then were plunging down hill, with the blaze full ahead of us. The heavy reek of it was flung in our nostrils as we gallop’d.

===Chinese===
我们费了些周折才寻到那道门：可一出门、上了大路，我便挥鞭赶马，向山上疾驰而去。黑暗的树篱之间为我们引路的，只有那盏灯笼和前方的火光。灵车在车辙里颠簸摇晃，碟盏杯盘叮当乱响：有十几回，马特险些被整个甩出座位。我双腿死死撑住，像个疯子似的连连抽鞭；马儿也如疯魔一般，朝山上狂奔。

到了山顶，一眼望去，万事尽收眼底——漫天烧红的天空——海面上滚动着一条条火线——映衬着这一切的，是黑沉沉的岬角，格莱斯宅邸（House of Gleys）在其上如烽火一般熊熊燃烧。已有一处厢房——正是我们住的那翼——蹿起一根跳跃的火柱，穿透屋顶，卷着浓烟和火星向海的方向扫去。我辨认出海岸线、崖间小径，「天赐号」（*Godsend*）的桅杆与船身，全都清晰如昼；近些处，则是黄澄澄的火光在青青谷田上闪烁。我们望见了这一切，随即朝山下俯冲，烈焰正当头。飞驰之间，那浓重的烟气味直灌进我们的鼻孔。

===Original===
At the bottom we caught up a group of men running. ’Twas a boatload come from the ship to help. As our horses swept past them, one or two came to a terrified halt; but presently were running hard again after us.

The great gate stood open. I drove straight into the bright-lit yard, shouting “Delia!—where is Delia?”

“Here!” call’d a voice; and from a group that stood under the glare of the window came my dear mistress running.

===Chinese===
到了山脚，我们赶上一群奔跑的汉子。那是从船上放下来救援的一艇人。我们的马从他们身边掠过时，有一两个吓得猛地收住脚；但片刻之后，又跟在我们后面拼命跑了起来。

大门敞着。我径直赶车冲进灯火通明的院子，高喊：「德利娅！——德利娅在哪儿？」

「在这儿！」一个声音应道；我亲爱的女主人从窗子亮光下站着的一群人里奔了出来。

===Original===
“All safe, Jack! But what—” She drew back from our strange equipage.

“All in good time. First tell me—how came the fire?”

“Why, foul work, as it seems. All I know is I was sleeping, and awoke to hear the black seaman hammering on my door. Jumping up, I found the room full of smoke, and escap’d. The rooms beneath, they say, were stuff’d with straw, and the yard outside heap’d also with straw, and blazing. Ben Halliday found two oil jars lying there—”

“Are the horses out?”

“Oh, Jack—I do not know! Shame on me to forget them!”

===Chinese===
「都平安，杰克！可是这是什么——」她见了我们这辆古怪的车驾，不由得倒退了一步。

「回头细说不迟。先告诉我——火是怎么起来的？」

「哼，瞧这架势，是桩见不得人的勾当。我只知道自己正睡着，醒来听见那黑人水手擂我的房门。跳起身来，满屋已是烟，我便逃了出来。据说楼下的房间里塞满了干草，外面的院子也堆着干草，烧得正旺。本·哈利迪（Ben Halliday）在那边发现了两只油罐——」

「马牵出来了吗？」

「哎呀，杰克——我不知道！我真不害臊，竟把它们忘了！」

===Original===
I ran toward the stable. Already the roof was ablaze, and the straw yard, beyond, a very furnace. Rushing in, I found the two horses cowering in their stalls, bath’d in sweat, and squealing. But ’twas all fright. So I fetch’d Molly’s saddle, and spoke to her, and set it across her back: and the sweet thing was quiet in a moment, turning her head to rub my sleeve gently with her muzzle: and followed me out like a lamb. The bay gave more trouble; but I sooth’d him in the same manner, and patting his neck, led him, too, into safety.

By this, all hope to save the house was over: for the well in the court yielded but twenty buckets before it ran dry, and after that no water was to be had. Of the wing where the fire burst out only the walls stood, and a few oaken rafters, that one by one came tumbling and crashing. The flames had spread along the roof, and were now licking the ceiling of the hall and spouting around the clock tower. In the roar and hubbub, Billy’s men work’d like demons, dragging out chairs, chests, and furniture of all kinds, which they strew’d in the yard, returning with shouts for more. One was tearing down the portraits in the hall: another was pulling out the great dresser from the kitchen: a third had found a pile of tapestry and came staggering forth under the load of it.

===Chinese===
我朝马厩跑去。厩顶已经着火，后头的草场更成了一座火炉。我冲进去，见两匹马畏缩在各自的厩位里，浑身汗透，嘶鸣不止。但那不过是受惊罢了。于是我取来莫莉的鞍子，一面跟它说话，一面把鞍子搭上它的背：这可爱的牲口顷刻间便安静下来，转过头来，用鼻吻轻轻蹭我的衣袖：又像羔羊一般跟着我走了出去。那匹枣红马费了些周折；但我也照样抚慰它，拍着它的脖子，把它也牵到了安全的地方。

到这时，抢救宅邸已全无指望：院中那口井打了不满二十桶便见了底，此后再也取不出水来。起火的那翼厢房只剩四壁还立着，还有几根橡木椽子，一根接一根轰然塌落。火焰已沿着屋顶蔓延开来，此刻正舔着大厅的天花板，绕着钟楼喷薄。一片轰响与嘈杂之中，比尔的手下干得如群魔一般，往外拖椅子、箱笼、各式家具，满院子乱堆一气，又呼喊着回去搬更多的。一个在大厅里扯下那些肖像：另一个正把厨房的大餐具柜往外拖：第三个寻着一堆花毯，压得摇摇晃晃地扛了出来。

===Original===
I had fasten’d the horses by the gate, and was ready to join in the work, when a shout was rais’d—

“Billy!—Where’s Billy Pottery? Has any seen the skipper?”

“Sure,” I call’d, “you don’t say he was never alarm’d!”

“Black Sampson was in his room—where’s Black Sampson?”

===Chinese===
我已把两匹马拴在门边，正要过去搭把手，忽然有人喊道——

「比尔！——比尔·波特里（Billy Pottery）在哪儿？有谁见过船长没有？」

「不会吧，」我喊道，「你们可别是说，他压根儿没被叫醒啊！」

「黑人桑普森（Black Sampson）就在他房里——黑人桑普森人呢？」

===Original===
“Here I be!” cried a voice. “To be sure I woke the skipper before any o’ ye.”

“Then where’s he hid? Did any see him come out?”

“Now, that we have not!” answer’d one or two.

I stood by the house door shouting these questions to the men inside, when a hand was laid on my arm, and there in the shadow waited Billy himself, with a mighty curious twinkle in his eye. He put a finger up and signed that I should follow.

===Chinese===
「在这儿呢！」一个声音喊道。「我当然叫醒了船长，比你们哪个都早。」

「那他躲到哪儿去了？有谁见他出来过没有？」

「这个，倒是没有！」一两个人应道。

我站在宅门口，朝屋里的人们高声问着这些话，忽觉一只手搭上我的手臂，阴影里候着的正是比尔本人，眼里闪着一种煞是古怪的光。他竖起一根手指，示意我跟他走。

===Original===
We pass’d round the outbuildings where, three hours before, Matt Soames and I had hid together. I was minded to stop and pull on my boots, that were hid here: but (and this was afterward the saving of me) on second thoughts let them lie, and follow’d Billy, who now led me out by the postern gate.

Without speech we stepp’d across the turf, he a pace or two ahead. A night breeze was blowing here, delicious after the heat of the fire. We were walking quickly toward the east side of the headland, and soon the blaze behind flung our shadows right to the cliff’s edge, for which Billy made straight, as if to fling himself over.

But when, at the very verge, he pull’d up, I became enlighten’d. At our feet was an iron bar driven into the soil, and to it a stout rope knotted, that ran over a block and disappeared down the cliff. I knelt and, pulling at it softly, look’d up. It came easy in the hand.

===Chinese===
我们绕过那排杂用屋——三个钟头前，我和马特·索姆斯（Matt Soames）还一起藏在那里面。我本想停下来，穿上藏在此处的靴子：但（这一层后来倒是救了我的命）转念一想，还是由它们躺着，便跟着比尔走了；这时他领我出了那道角门。

我们一言不发地踏上草皮，他走在前头一两步。这里正吹着夜风，经过火场的热浪，这风沁人心脾。我们快步朝岬角的东侧走去，不多时，身后的火光便把我们的影子一直投到崖边——比尔径直朝崖边走去，仿佛要纵身跃下似的。

可到了崖沿，他却收住脚步，我这才恍然大悟。我们脚边立着一根打进土里的铁桩，桩上系着一根粗绳，绳子绕过一只滑轮，隐没在崖下。我跪下来，轻轻拽了拽绳子，抬头望去。绳子一拉便顺顺溜溜，毫不坠手。

===Original===
Billy, with the glare in his face, nodded: and bending to my ear, for once achiev’d a whisper.

“Saw one stealing hither—an’ follow’d. A man wi’ a limp foot—went over the side like a cat.”

I must have appeared to doubt this good fortune, for he added—

“ ’Be a truth speakin’ man i’ the main, Jack—’lay over ’pon my belly, and spied a ledge—fifty feet down or less—’reckon there be a way thence to the foot. Dear, now! what a rampin’, tearin’ sweat is this?”

For, fast as I could tug, I was hauling up the rope. Near sixty feet came up before I reach’d the end—a thick twisted knot. I rove a long noose; pull’d it over my head and shoulders, and made Billy understand he was to lower me.

===Chinese===
比尔脸上映着火光，点了点头：俯到我耳边，这一回竟凑成了一句耳语。

「俺瞧见有人偷偷往这边溜——就跟上去了。是个瘸脚的汉子——像猫一样翻下崖去了。」

想必我脸上露出不信这等好运的神色，他又补了一句——

「杰克，俺这个人平常说实话的时候居多——俺趴下肚皮贴地一瞧，望见一道岩礁——往下五十英尺上下——俺估摸从那儿兴许有路通到崖脚。哎呀，乖乖！这一身汗撒着欢儿、撕扯着往外冒，算是怎么档子事？」

原来，趁我还能使劲拽的当口，我一直在往上收绳。收上来将近六十英尺，才够着绳头——结着一个大粗绳结。我打了一个长长的活套；把它从头和肩上套过，又让比尔明白，他得把我放下去。

===Original===
“Sit i’ the noose, lad, an’ hold round the knot. For sign to hoist again, tug the rope hard. I can hold.”

He paid it out carefully while I stepp’d to the edge. With the noose about my loins I thrust myself gently over, and in a trice hung swaying.

On three sides the sky compass’d me—wild and red, save where to eastward the dawn was paling: on the fourth the dark rocky face seem’d gliding upward as Billy lower’d. Far below I heard the wash of the sea, and could just spy the white spume of it glimmering. It stole some of the heart out of me, and I took my eyes off it.

===Chinese===
「坐在套子里，小伙子，抱住那个绳结。要再拉你上来的时候，就使劲拽绳子。我这头撑得住。」

他小心地放着绳，我走到崖边。绳套兜住腰身，我轻轻把自己送出崖沿，转眼间便悬在半空，晃晃悠悠。

三面是天穹环抱——狂乱而赤红，只有东边天际晨光渐白：第四面是黑沉沉的岩壁，随着比尔放绳，仿佛正从我身旁向上滑去。极深的下方，我听得见海水的涌动，刚刚能辨出白色浪沫的微光。它偷走了我几分胆气，我把目光从那儿挪开了。

===Original===
Some feet below the top, the cliff fetch’d a slant inward, so that I dangled a full three feet out from the face. As a boy I had adventured something of this sort on the north sides of Gable and the Pillar, and once (after a nest of eaglets) on the Mickledore cliffs: but then ’twas daylight. Now, though I saw the ledge under me, about a third of the way down, it look’d, in the darkness, to be so extremely narrow, that ’tis probable I should have call’d out to Billy to draw me up but for the certainty that he would never hear: so instead I held very tight and wish’d it over.

Down I sway’d (Billy letting out the rope very steady), and at last swung myself inward to the ledge, gain’d a footing, and took a glance round before slipping off the rope.

I stood on a shelf of sandy rock that wound round the cliff some way to my left, and then, as I thought, broke sharply away. ’Twas mainly about a yard in width, but in places no more than two feet. In the growing light I noted the face of the headland ribb’d with several of these ledges, of varying length, but all hollow’d away underneath (as I suppose by the sea in former ages), so that the cliff’s summit overhung the base by a great way: and peering over I saw the waves creeping right beneath me.

===Chinese===
离崖顶几英尺处，崖壁往里斜收进去，因此我悬在半空，离崖面足有三英尺。少年时，我曾在盖博山（Gable）和皮勒峰（the Pillar）的北坡上干过这类冒险的营生，还有一回（为掏一窝雏鹰）在米克尔多尔崖（Mickledore）上试过身手：但那都是在白天。此刻，我虽看得见下方约三分之一深度处的岩礁，黑暗中它却显得窄得出奇；若不是断定比尔决计听不见，我很可能早喊他把我拽上去了：于是我只好紧紧抓住，一心盼着这差事快些完结。

我晃晃悠悠往下降（比尔放绳放得极稳），终于向里一荡，荡到岩礁上，立稳了脚跟，先四下望了一眼，才从绳套里脱出身来。

我站的地方是一道沙岩台，绕着崖壁向我左边蜿蜒了一段，随后——我料想——便陡然断掉了。它宽处约有一码，窄处却不过两英尺。渐亮的天光里，我看见岬角的崖面上横着好几道这样的岩礁，如肋骨一般，长短不一，底下却都凹蚀进去（我猜是远古的海水掏的），因此崖顶远远探出于崖基之外：我探头下望，只见海浪正贴着我脚下爬行。

===Original===
Now all this while I had not let Master Tingcomb out of my mind. So I slipp’d off the rope and left it to dangle, while I crept forward to explore, keeping well against the rock and planting my feet with great caution.

I believe I was twenty minutes taking as many steps, when at the point where the ledge broke off I saw the ends of an iron ladder sticking up, and close beside it a great hole in the rock, which till now the curve of the cliff had hid. The ladder no doubt stood on a second shelf below.

I was pausing to consider this, when a bright ray stream’d across the sea toward me, and the red rim of the sun rose out of the waters, outfacing the glow on the headland, and rending the film of smoke that hung like a curtain about the horizon. ’Twas as if by alchemy that the red ripples melted to gold; and I stood watching with a child’s delight.

===Chinese===
这半天里，我心里始终不曾放下廷科姆老爷（Master Tingcomb）。于是我脱开绳子，任它悬着晃荡，自己向前爬去探路，身子紧贴岩壁，落脚万分小心。

我相信，我走了二十分钟，才走了二十来步。走到岩礁断裂的地方，我看见一架铁梯的梯头戳出崖沿，紧挨着是一个岩石上的大洞——直到此刻，弯曲的崖身才把它显露出来。那梯子无疑立在下层另一道岩台上。

我正停下来思量，一道明亮的光线已越过海面射向我这边，太阳的红边冒出水面，盖过了岬角上的火光，也撕裂了如帷幕一般垂在天际四围的薄烟。红色的涟漪仿佛经了点金术，融成一片金黄；我站在那里，怀着孩童般的欢喜望着。

===Original===
I heard the sound of a footstep: and fac’d round.

Before me, not six paces off, stood Hannibal Tingcomb.

He was issuing from the hole with a sack on his shoulder, and sneaking to descend the steps, when he threw a glance behind—and saw me!

Neither spoke. With a face grey as ashes he turn’d very slowly, until in the unnatural light we look’d straight into each other’s eyes. His never blink’d, but stared—stared horribly, while the veins swell’d black on his forehead and his lips work’d, attempting speech. No words came—only a long drawn sob, deep down in his throat.

And then, letting slip the sack, he flung his arms up, ran a pace or two toward me, and tumbled on his face in a fit. His left shoulder hung over the verge; his legs slipp’d. In a trice he was hanging by his arms, his old distorted face turn’d up, and a froth about his lips. I made a step to save him: and then jump’d back, flattening myself against the rock.

===Chinese===
我听得一阵脚步声：便转过身来。

我面前不过六步远的地方，站着汉尼拔·廷科姆（Hannibal Tingcomb）。

他正从那洞里钻出来，肩上扛着一只口袋，蹑手蹑脚要下梯级，忽地回头一瞥——看见了我！

谁也没有说话。他面如死灰，极慢极慢地转过身来，直到在那异样的光线里，我们两双眼睛直直相望。他的一双一眨不眨，只是瞪着——瞪得骇人，额头上的血管胀得发黑，嘴唇抽动着想说话。话没有出来——只有一声长长的、抽丝似的呜咽，深深闷在喉咙里。

===Original===
The ledge was breaking.

I saw a seam gape at my feet. I saw it widen and spread to right and left. I heard a ripping, rending noise—a rush of stones and earth: and, clawing the air, with a wild screech, Master Tingcomb pitch’d backward, head over heels, into space.

Then follow’d silence: then a horrible splash as he struck the water, far below: then again a slipping and trickling, as more of the ledge broke away—at first a pebble or two sliding—a dribble of earth—next, a crash and a cloud of dust. A last stone ran loose and dropp’d. Then fell a silence so deep I could catch the roar of the flames on the hill behind.

===Chinese===
岩礁正在断裂。

我看见脚边裂开一道缝。我看见它向左右展宽、蔓延。我听见一阵撕裂崩解的声响——一阵石块与泥土的倾泻：廷科姆老爷双手向空乱抓，发出一声狂叫，仰面朝天、翻着跟头，栽进了虚空。

===Original===
Standing there, my arms thrown back and fingers spread against the rock, I saw a wave run out, widen, and lose itself on the face of the sea. Under my feet but eight inches of the cornice remain’d. My toes stuck forward over the gulf.

A score of startled gulls with their cries call’d me to myself. I open’d my eyes, that had shut in sheer giddiness. Close on my left the ledge was broke back to the very base, cutting me off by twelve feet from that part where the ladder still rested. No man could jump it, standing. To the right there was no gap: but in one place only was the footing over ten inches wide, and at the end my rope hung over the sea, a good yard away from the edge.

I shut my eyes and shouted.
