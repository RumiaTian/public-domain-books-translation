## Act III / 第三幕

### Scene I / 第一场

===Original===
A room in **Lady Wishfort’s** house.

***Lady Wishfort** at her toilet, **Peg** waiting.*

===Chinese===
威什福特夫人家中一室。

*（**威什福特夫人**正在梳妆，**佩格（Peg）**在旁伺候。）*

===Original===
Lady Wishfort
Merciful! No news of Foible yet?
Peg
No, madam.
Lady Wishfort
I have no more patience.—If I have not fretted myself till I am pale again, there’s no veracity in me. Fetch me the red—the red, do you hear, sweetheart?—An errant ash colour, as I’m a person. Look you how this wench stirs! Why dost thou not fetch me a little red? Didst thou not hear me, Mopus?33

===Chinese===
**威什福特夫人.** 老天慈悲！福伊布尔还没个音信？

**佩格.** 没有，夫人。

**威什福特夫人.** 我一点耐性也不剩了。——我若不是把自己烦得又白了脸，就算我这人没有一句实话。给我拿红——拿红的，听见没有，心肝？——瞧这一脸死灰，我以人格担保。瞧这丫头磨蹭的！怎么还不去给我拿点红来？没听见我说话么，木头脑袋（33）？

===Original===
Peg
The red ratafia, does your ladyship mean, or the cherry brandy?
Lady Wishfort
Ratafia, fool? No, fool. Not the ratafia, fool—grant me patience!—I mean the Spanish paper,34 idiot; complexion, darling. Paint, paint, paint, dost thou understand that, changeling, dangling thy hands like bobbins before thee? Why dost thou not stir, puppet? Thou wooden thing upon wires!
Peg
Lord, madam, your ladyship is so impatient!—I cannot come at the paint, madam: Mrs. Foible has locked it up, and carried the key with her.
Lady Wishfort
A pox take you both.—Fetch me the cherry brandy then.*Exit **Peg**.* I’m as pale and as faint, I look like Mrs. Qualmsick, the curate’s wife, that’s always breeding. Wench, come, come, wench, what art thou doing? Sipping? Tasting?—Save thee, dost thou not know the bottle?

===Chinese===
**佩格.** 夫人是说红的拉塔菲亚甜酒，还是樱桃白兰地？

**威什福特夫人.** 拉塔菲亚，傻瓜？不，傻瓜。不是拉塔菲亚，傻瓜——老天赐我耐心！——我说的是西班牙纸（34），蠢货；是脸色，宝贝。胭脂！胭脂！胭脂！这你懂不懂，个傻丫头，两只手吊在身前，活像两轴线？还不动弹，木偶？你这牵线的木头东西！

**佩格.** 天哪，夫人，您也太急了！——胭脂我够不着，夫人：福伊布尔把它锁起来了，钥匙随身带走了。

**威什福特夫人.** 叫瘟病把你们两个都拿了去。——那就给我拿樱桃白兰地来。（**佩格**下。）我白得发虚，活像副牧师太太阔姆赛克太太，那位常年有孕的。丫头，来呀，来呀，丫头，你磨蹭什么？咂一口？尝一口？——老天保佑你，你认不认得那只瓶子？

===Original===
*Reenter **Peg** with a bottle and china cup.*
Peg
Madam, I was looking for a cup.
Lady Wishfort
A cup, save thee, and what a cup hast thou brought! Dost thou take me for a fairy, to drink out of an acorn? Why didst thou not bring thy thimble? Hast thou ne’er a brass thimble clinking in thy pocket with a bit of nutmeg?35—I warrant thee. Come, fill, fill!—So—again.—*Knocking at the door.* See who that is.—Set down the bottle first!—here, here, under the table.—What, wouldst thou go with the bottle in thy hand like a tapster? As I’m a person, this wench has lived in an inn upon the road, before she came to me, like Maritornes the Asturian in Don Quixote.36—No Foible yet?
Peg
No, madam; Mrs. Marwood.
Lady Wishfort
Oh, Marwood: let her come in.—Come in, good Marwood.

===Chinese===
*（**佩格**持一瓶一盏瓷杯又上。）*

**佩格.** 夫人，我方才在寻一只杯子。

**威什福特夫人.** 杯子！老天保佑你，瞧你端来的是盏什么杯子！你当我是仙女，能就着橡子壳喝酒么？怎么不把你顶针捎来？你兜里就没有一只铜顶针，里头搁一小块肉豆蔻（35），叮叮当当？——我算准了你有。来，斟上，斟上！——好——再斟。——（叩门声。）瞧瞧是谁。——先把瓶子搁下！——这儿，这儿，桌子底下。——怎么，还要攥着瓶子去开门，像个酒馆跑堂的？我以人格担保，这丫头来我这里之前，准在大路上的客栈里待过，活脱《堂吉诃德》（36）里那位阿斯图里亚斯姑娘马利托内斯。——福伊布尔还没信儿？

**佩格.** 没有，夫人；是玛伍德夫人。

**威什福特夫人.** 哦，玛伍德：请她进来。——进来吧，好玛伍德。

===Original===
*Enter **Mrs. Marwood**.*
Mrs. Marwood
I’m surprised to find your ladyship in deshabille at this time of day.
Lady Wishfort
Foible’s a lost thing; has been abroad since morning, and never heard of since.
Mrs. Marwood
I saw her but now, as I came masked through the park, in conference with Mirabell.
Lady Wishfort
With Mirabell!—You call my blood into my face with mentioning that traitor. She durst not have the confidence. I sent her to negotiate an affair, in which if I’m detected I’m undone. If that wheedling villain has wrought upon Foible to detect me, I’m ruined. O my dear friend, I’m a wretch of wretches if I’m detected.

===Chinese===
*（**玛伍德夫人**上。）*

**玛伍德夫人.** 这时辰见夫人还没穿戴齐整，好生意外。

**威什福特夫人.** 福伊布尔成了没影儿的东西：一早出去，至今音信全无。

**玛伍德夫人.** 方才我蒙着面罩穿过公园，还瞧见她，正同米兰芒特密谈。

**威什福特夫人.** 同米兰芒特！——你一提这奸贼，我的血就往脸上撞。她断不敢有这样的胆量。我派她去交涉一桩事，这桩事一旦叫人识破，我就完了。倘若那个油嘴的恶棍说动了福伊布尔来出首我，我就毁了。哦，亲爱的朋友，事情一败露，我就是可怜虫里头顶可怜的那一个。

===Original===
Mrs. Marwood
O madam, you cannot suspect Mrs. Foible’s integrity!
Lady Wishfort
Oh, he carries poison in his tongue that would corrupt integrity itself! If she has given him an opportunity, she has as good as put her integrity into his hands. Ah, dear Marwood, what’s integrity to an opportunity?—Hark! I hear her!—dear friend, retire into my closet, that I may examine her with more freedom—you’ll pardon me, dear friend, I can make bold with you—there are books over the chimney—Quarles and Prynne,37 and the *Short View of the Stage*,38 with Bunyan’s works to entertain you.—*To **Peg**.*—Go, you thing, and send her in.
*Exeunt **Mrs. Marwood** and **Peg**.*
*Enter **Foible**.*

===Chinese===
**玛伍德夫人.** 哦夫人，您总不至于疑心福伊布尔的忠心！

**威什福特夫人.** 哦，他那条舌头上驮着毒，忠心本身也能叫他蚀化！她只要给了他一个机会，就等于把忠心捧进了他的手心。啊，亲爱的玛伍德，忠心算得了什么，敌得过机会么？——听！她回来了！——亲爱的朋友，先到我内室避一避，我好放开了盘问她——您别见怪，亲爱的朋友，我在您跟前放肆惯了——壁炉上头有书——夸尔斯和普林（37），《剧坛短评》（38），连班扬的著作，够您解闷的。——（对**佩格**。）——去，你这个东西，叫她进来。

*（**玛伍德夫人**与**佩格**下。）*

*（**福伊布尔**上。）*

===Original===
Lady Wishfort
O Foible, where hast thou been? What hast thou been doing?
Foible
Madam, I have seen the party.
Lady Wishfort
But what hast thou done?
Foible
Nay, ’tis your ladyship has done, and are to do; I have only promised. But a man so enamoured—so transported! Well, if worshipping of pictures be a sin—poor Sir Rowland, I say.

===Chinese===
**威什福特夫人.** 哦，福伊布尔，你上哪儿去了？干什么去了？

**福伊布尔.** 夫人，那一位，我见着了。

**威什福特夫人.** 可你办成了什么？

**福伊布尔.** 嗳，办成的、要办的，全在夫人您身上；我不过替人应承了一桩。可那一位爱得那个样儿——神魂颠倒！唉，若说拜画像也算罪过——真难为了罗兰爵士，我是说，可怜他一片痴心。

===Original===
Lady Wishfort
The miniature has been counted like. But hast thou not betrayed me, Foible? Hast thou not detected me to that faithless Mirabell? What hast thou to do with him in the park? Answer me, has he got nothing out of thee?
Foible
*Aside.* So, the devil has been beforehand with me; what shall I say?—*Aloud.*—Alas, madam, could I help it, if I met that confident thing? Was I in fault? If you had heard how he used me, and all upon your ladyship’s account, I’m sure you would not suspect my fidelity. Nay, if that had been the worst I could have borne: but he had a fling at your ladyship too, and then I could not hold; but, i’faith I gave him his own.
Lady Wishfort
Me? What did the filthy fellow say?
Foible
O madam, ’tis a shame to say what he said—with his taunts and his fleers, tossing up his nose. Humph! (says he) what, you are a hatching some plot (says he), you are so early abroad, or catering (says he), ferreting for some disbanded officer, I warrant.—Half pay is but thin subsistence (says he), well, what pension does your lady propose? Let me see, (says he), what, she must come down pretty deep now, she’s superannuated (says he) and—

===Chinese===
**威什福特夫人.** 那张袖珍小像，人家原也说画得像。可你没出卖我罢，福伊布尔？你没把我泄露给那个没良心的米兰芒特罢？你在公园里同他有什么好谈的？回话，他可从你嘴里套出什么去没有？

**福伊布尔.**（旁白）糟了，魔鬼抢在我前头了；这可怎么回话？——（高声）——唉呀夫人，我撞上那个放肆的东西，我有什么法子？难道是我的错？您若听见他怎么作践我——桩桩件件，还全冲着夫人您——我保准您就不会疑心我的忠心。嗳，若只冲着我来，我倒担得住；偏偏他又拿夫人您挖苦，那我就忍无可忍了；可说真的，我把他抢白了个够。

**威什福特夫人.** 冲我？那脏嘴里说了什么？

**福伊布尔.** 哦夫人，他那些话我说不出口——又讥又诮，鼻孔朝天。哼！（他说）怎么，你们在孵什么阴谋（他说），起这么个大早，要么就是办酒席（他说），像雪貂似的嗅退役军官，保管没错。——半饷可养不活人（他说），好，你们家太太打算出多少年俸？我瞧瞧，（他说），怎么，如今可得狠狠放血了，她都超龄了（他说）还有——

===Original===
Lady Wishfort
Ods my life, I’ll have him—I’ll have him murdered. I’ll have him poisoned. Where does he eat?—I’ll marry a drawer to have him poisoned in his wine. I’ll send for Robin from Locket’s39 immediately.
Foible
Poison him? Poisoning’s too good for him. Starve him, madam, starve him; marry Sir Rowland, and get him disinherited. Oh, you would bless yourself to hear what he said!
Lady Wishfort
A villain! Superannuated!
Foible
Humph (says he), I hear you are laying designs against me too (says he) and Mrs. Millamant is to marry my uncle (he does not suspect a word of your ladyship); but (says he) I’ll fit you for that, I warrant you (says he) I’ll hamper you for that (says he); you and your old frippery too (says he); I’ll handle you—

===Chinese===
**威什福特夫人.** 我的命！我非——非叫人宰了他不可。我要叫他吃毒药。他在哪儿用饭？——我索性嫁个跑堂的，好往他酒里下毒。我这就打发人去洛克特馆（39），即刻把罗宾叫来。

**福伊布尔.** 毒死他？毒死他还便宜了他。饿死他，夫人，饿死他；嫁给罗兰爵士，叫他丢了继承权。哦，他那些话您要是听见了，保准念佛！

**威什福特夫人.** 恶棍！超龄！

**福伊布尔.** 哼（他说），我听说你们也在打我的算盘（他说），米勒曼特小姐要嫁我叔叔（您老这边，他一个字也没疑心）；不过（他说）这回我叫你们好看，保管没错（他说），我叫你们吃不了兜着走（他说）；连你那身老古董旧货也一块儿（他说）；我要好生收拾你——

===Original===
Lady Wishfort
Audacious villain! Handle me? Would he durst?—Frippery! Old frippery! Was there ever such a foul-mouthed fellow? I’ll be married tomorrow, I’ll be contracted tonight.
Foible
The sooner the better, madam.
Lady Wishfort
Will Sir Rowland be here, say’st thou? When, Foible?
Foible
Incontinently, madam. No new sheriff’s wife expects the return of her husband after knighthood with that impatience in which Sir Rowland burns for the dear hour of kissing your ladyship’s hand after dinner.

===Chinese===
**威什福特夫人.** 胆大包天的恶棍！收拾我？他敢！——旧货！老掉牙的旧货！世上可有过这张臭嘴？我明天就成婚，今晚就订婚约！

**福伊布尔.** 越快越好，夫人。

**威什福特夫人.** 罗兰爵士会来，你说？几时，福伊布尔？

**福伊布尔.** 须臾便到，夫人。新任郡长太太巴望丈夫受了骑士封号荣归，那份心焦，也比不上罗兰爵士心里烧的那团火——他一心盼着饭后亲吻夫人玉手的那个良辰。

===Original===
Lady Wishfort
Frippery! Superannuated frippery! I’ll frippery the villain; I’ll reduce him to frippery and rags, a tatterdemalion! I hope to see him hung with tatters, like a Long Lane penthouse,40 or a gibbet thief. A slander-mouthed railer! I warrant the spendthrift prodigal’s in debt as much as the million lottery,41 or the whole court upon a birthday.42 I’ll spoil his credit with his tailor. Yes, he shall have my niece with her fortune, he shall.
Foible
He! I hope to see him lodge in Ludgate first, and angle into Blackfriars for brass farthings with an old mitten.43
Lady Wishfort
Aye, dear Foible; thank thee for that, dear Foible. He has put me out of all patience. I shall never recompose my features to receive Sir Rowland with any economy of face. This wretch has fretted me that I am absolutely decayed. Look, Foible.
Foible
Your ladyship has frowned a little too rashly, indeed, madam. There are some cracks discernible in the white varnish.

===Chinese===
**威什福特夫人.** 旧货！超龄的旧货！我定要叫这恶棍「旧货」个够；把他作践成旧货加破烂，一个褴褛鬼！我巴望看他浑身上下挂满破布条，活像朗巷（40）旧衣铺的雨棚，或是上过绞架的贼。一张血口喷人的东西！我拿得准，这挥霍的败家子欠的债，抵得上百万彩票（41），抵得上过生日（42）的整座宫廷。我要去坏他在裁缝跟前的信用。对，他就是要娶我外甥女、连人带财产一并娶去，娶定了。

**福伊布尔.** 他！我巴望先看他搬进路德门（Ludgate）监狱去蹲着，捏着一只旧手套（43），往黑衣修士区（Blackfriars）钓铜子儿去。

**威什福特夫人.** 是啊，亲爱的福伊布尔；难为你这话，亲爱的福伊布尔。他把我一肚子耐性全搅光了。我这副眉眼，再也没法省着使，去迎罗兰爵士了。这混蛋烦得我彻底垮了。瞧，福伊布尔。

**福伊布尔.** 夫人方才皱眉，皱得急了些，真的。那层白瓷漆上，已看得见几道裂纹。

===Original===
Lady Wishfort
Let me see the glass.—Cracks, say’st thou? Why, I am arrantly flayed—I look like an old peeled wall. Thou must repair me, Foible, before Sir Rowland comes, or I shall never keep up to my picture.
Foible
I warrant you, madam, a little art once made your picture like you, and now a little of the same art must make you like your picture. Your picture must sit for you, madam.
Lady Wishfort
But art thou sure Sir Rowland will not fail to come? Or will a not fail when he does come? Will he be importunate, Foible, and push? For if he should not be importunate I shall never break decorums—I shall die with confusion if I am forced to advance—oh no, I can never advance—I shall swoon if he should expect advances. No, I hope Sir Rowland is better bred than to put a lady to the necessity of breaking her forms. I won’t be too coy neither—I won’t give him despair—but a little disdain is not amiss; a little scorn is alluring.
Foible
A little scorn becomes your ladyship.

===Chinese===
**威什福特夫人.** 拿镜子我瞧。——裂纹，你说？嗐，我这算整张皮都剥了——瞧着活像一面剥了皮的老墙。你得把我修补修补，福伊布尔，趁罗兰爵士没来，不然我可撑不到我那张画像的水准了。

**福伊布尔.** 三分轻慢，正衬夫人。

===Original===
Lady Wishfort
Yes, but tenderness becomes me best—a sort of a dyingness—you see that picture has a sort of a—ha, Foible! A swimmingness in the eyes—yes, I’ll look so—my niece affects it; but she wants features. Is Sir Rowland handsome? Let my toilet be removed—I’ll dress above. I’ll receive Sir Rowland here. Is he handsome? Don’t answer me. I won’t know; I’ll be surprised. I’ll be taken by surprise.

By storm, madam. Sir Rowland’s a brisk man.

===Chinese===

===Original===
Mrs. Fainall
O Foible, I have been in a fright, lest I should come too late. That devil, Marwood, saw you in the park with Mirabell, and I’m afraid will discover it to my lady.
Foible
