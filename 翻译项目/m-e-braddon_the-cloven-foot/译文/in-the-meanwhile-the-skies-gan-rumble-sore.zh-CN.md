After that portrait of Lady Barker, John Treverton drew no more caricatures. It seemed as if he had laid aside the pen of the caricaturist in deference to his wife’s dislike of that somewhat ill-natured art. But he had not abandoned the higher walks of art, for he had made himself a studio out of one of the spare bedrooms that looked northward, and was engaged on a portrait of his wife, an altogether fanciful and ideal picture, which he worked at for an hour or two daily with infinite delight. He had many pleasant labours and occupations at this period of his life. The farm, the hunting field, the business details of a large property, which he wished to conduct in an orderly manner, not hiding his talents in a napkin, but improving the estate, which Jasper Treverton had considerably increased during his long life, but upon which the old man had been somewhat loth to spend money. It was altogether a full and happy life which John Treverton led with his wife in this first year of their union, and it seemed to both that nothing was wanting to perfect their happiness. And yet, by-and-by, when there came the prospect of a child being born in the grave old house which had so long been undisturbed by the patter of childish feet, the fulfilment of this sweet hope seemed the one thing needed to fill their cup of joy.
While at the Manor House all was bliss, life dawdled on comfortably enough at the Vicarage, where the good, easy-tempered, hardworking vicar had begun to be reconciled to the idea that his only son was to be an idler all his life; until perchance this seemingly barren plant should some day put forth the glorious flower of genius. 「总有一天要爆发的，西莉亚，你记住；那一场爆发，定要把庄馆炸上天——就像达恩利被杀那夜，柯克欧菲德被炸上了天一样。」
他不再多说，虽然西莉亚不肯轻易放过这个话头。说真的，他也有些生自己的气，不该说这么多；不过他向妹妹交这番底，原不是无缘无故。约翰·特雷弗顿的事，凡能打听的，他都要知道；而西莉亚的处境，能听到许多他自己探不到的东西。
「我原本真当你开始喜欢特雷弗顿先生了呢，」姑娘过了一会儿说。「你和他像是处得很好。」
「我是看劳拉的分上对他客气。倘若我想着这样更合她的利益，再虚伪些的勾当我也肯干。」
爱德华叹了口气，又朝靠垫烦躁地把头一甩。他要置约翰·特雷弗顿于死地；可他也明白，他给仇敌的最重一击，于自己捞不到半点好处。一无所得。那伤害却是无可挽回、致命的：名誉与家业一并摧折——说不定是绞架——羞辱深得连恩爱的妻子也熬不过那一击。这一切，都在爱德华·克莱尔心里，作为一种并非不可能的复仇盘算着。而不幸的是，再没有更小一点的复仇可施。他自觉攞着一种致命的权柄；却是一种不能伤而不杀的权柄。他像那条眼镜蛇：毒牙装着一副灵巧的机关，平时收着，等要用时才放出来。两颗带铰链的牙，平时贴卧在蛇口的上膛。它扑咬猎物时，铰链一动，毒牙落下，毒腺受压，剧毒顺牙上的沟槽流下，滴进为它备好的创口。日晷的影子没走完二十秒，快把受伤的肢体斩下来，否则毒液便要发作。那能救活中毒者的解药，医学至今还没有发现。
