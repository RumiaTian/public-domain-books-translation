===Original===
17. Suspicions of murder, in the case of a woman who was found dead, covered with bruises, after a day’s running fight with her husband, in which the beer jug had been the bone of contention, brought me to this house, a ramshackle tenement on the tail-end of a lot over near the North River docks. The family in the picture lived above the rooms where the dead woman lay on a bed of straw, overrun by rats, and had been uninterested witnesses of the affray that was an everyday occurrence in the house. A patched and shaky stairway led up to their one bare and miserable room, in comparison with which a whitewashed prison-cell seemed a real palace. A heap of old rags, in which the baby slept serenely, served as the common sleeping-bunk of father, mother, and children﻿—two bright and pretty girls, singularly out of keeping in their clean, if coarse, dresses, with their surroundings. The father, a slow-going, honest English coal-heaver, earned on the average five dollars a week, “when work was fairly brisk,” at the docks. But there were long seasons when it was very “slack,” he said, doubtfully. Yet the prospect did not seem to discourage them. The mother, a pleasant-faced woman, was cheerful, even lighthearted. Her smile seemed the most sadly hopeless of all in the utter wretchedness of the place, cheery though it was meant to be and really was. It seemed doomed to certain disappointment﻿—the one thing there that was yet to know a greater depth of misery. ↩︎

===Chinese===
17. 一名女人被发现死去、浑身瘀伤——此前她与丈夫整整追逐缠斗了一天，争执的祸根是一只啤酒壶——这些谋杀嫌疑把我引到了这座房子：北河（North River）码头附近一块地皮尾端上一栋摇摇欲坠的破烂廉租公寓。照片里的这家人就住在死者所躺那几个房间的楼上；她躺在一张稻草铺上，老鼠在其上乱窜。这家人对那场斗殴漠不关心地做了目击者——在这座房子里，斗殴是天天上演的事。一条补了又补、摇摇晃晃的楼梯通向他们唯一那间空空荡荡、凄凄惨惨的屋子，同它一比，一间刷得雪白的牢房真算得上宫殿了。一堆旧破布充作父亲、母亲和孩子们共同的床铺，婴儿安睡其中；两个女孩子明媚漂亮，虽穿着粗劣却干净的衣裳，与周围的环境格格不入。父亲是个慢性子、老实的英国卸煤工，「活计相当兴旺的时候」在码头平均每周挣五美元。但也有漫长的时节活计十分「清淡」，他说，语气将信将疑。然而这样的前景似乎并没有叫他们气馁。母亲是个面容和悦的女人，快快活活，甚至无忧无虑。在这地方彻头彻尾的凄惨之中，她的微笑虽是出于欢快、也确是欢快，却似乎是万般事物中最悲哀而无望的一样。它似乎注定要落空——那是这地方唯一尚未领教过更深一层苦难的东西。 ↩︎
