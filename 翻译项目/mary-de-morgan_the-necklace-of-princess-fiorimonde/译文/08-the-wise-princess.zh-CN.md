## The Wise Princess / 聪明的公主

===Original===
Once upon a time lived a King whose wife was dead and who had one little daughter who was named Fernanda. She was very good and pretty, but when she was a child she vexed all her ladies by asking them questions about everything she saw.

===Chinese===
从前有一位国王，王后早已亡故，膝下只留下一个小女儿，名唤费尔南达（Fernanda）。她心地善良，模样俊俏，可还是个孩子的时候，就见什么问什么，把身边的侍女们问得个个发愁。

===Original===
“Your Highness should not wish to know too much,” they told her, whereat Princess Fernanda threw up her little head, and said,

“I want to know everything.”

===Chinese===
“殿下可不该想知道得太多。”她们这样劝她。费尔南达公主听了，把小脑袋一扬，说道：

“我什么都想知道。”

===Original===
As she grew up she had masters and mistresses to teach her, and learnt every language and every science; but still she said, “It is not enough; I want to know more.”

===Chinese===
她渐渐长大，宫里为她延请了各科男女教师；她学遍了各种语言、各门学问，却仍旧说：“这还不够，我还要知道更多。”

===Original===
In a deep cave underground there lived an old Wizard who was so wise that his face was well-nigh black with wrinkles, and his long white beard flowed to his feet. He knew all sorts of magic, and every day and night sat poring over his books till now there seemed to be nothing left for him to learn.

===Chinese===
地底深处的一个洞穴里，住着一位老巫师。他智慧渊深，一张脸叫皱纹几乎积成了黑色，长长的白须飘飘垂到脚边。他懂得种种巫术魔法，日日夜夜伏在他的书本上苦读，到如今，天底下仿佛再没有什么留给他学的了。

===Original===
One night after everyone was asleep, Princess Fernanda rose and slipped softly down the stairs and out of the palace unheard by anyone, and stole away to the Wizard’s cave.

The old man was sitting on his low stool reading out of an immense book by a dim green light, but he raised his eyes as the Princess entered at the low doorway, and looked at her. She wore a blue and silver robe, but her bright hair was unbound, and fell in ripples to her waist.

===Chinese===
一天夜里，待众人都沉沉睡去，费尔南达公主起了身，轻手轻脚溜下楼梯，悄没声息地出了宫殿，偷偷来到巫师的洞穴。

老人正坐在一张矮凳上，就着幽幽的绿光，读着一本硕大无朋的书；公主从低矮的洞门走进来，他抬起眼睛，把她打量了一番。她穿一身蓝银色的长袍，明亮的头发却披散着，波浪似的一直泻到腰间。

===Original===
“Who are you, and what do you want with me?” he asked shortly.

“I am the Princess Fernanda,” she said, “and I wish to be your pupil. Teach me all you know.”

===Chinese===
“你是谁？找我做什么？”他简慢地问。

“我是费尔南达公主，”她说，“我想做你的学生。把你所知道的，都教给我吧。”

===Original===
“Why do you wish for that?” said the Wizard: “you will not be better or happier for it.”

“I am not happy now,” said the Princess sighing wearily. “Teach me and you shall find me an apt pupil, and I will pay you with gold.”

“I will not have your gold,” said the Wizard, “but come to me every night at this hour, and in three years you shall know all I do.”

===Chinese===
“你为什么要这样？”巫师说，“学了它，你不会变得更好，也不会更快乐。”

“我如今就不快乐，”公主疲惫地叹息道，“教我吧，你会发现我是个颖悟的学生，我要用金币来酬谢你。”

“你的金币我不要，”巫师说，“可你每晚这个时辰到我这里来，三年之后，我所知道的你就全知道了。”

===Original===
So every night the Princess went down to the Wizard’s cave while all the court were sleeping. And the people wondered at her more and more, and said, “How much she knows! How wise she is!”

When the three years had gone by the Wizard said to her, “Go! I can teach you no more now. You are as wise as I.” Then the Princess thanked him and went back to her father’s palace.

===Chinese===
于是每天夜里，趁满朝上下都在酣睡，公主便下到巫师的洞穴里去。人们对她越来越惊叹，都说：“她懂得真多啊！她多聪明啊！”

三年过去，巫师对她说：“去吧！我如今再没有什么可教你的了。你已经同我一样聪明了。”公主谢过了他，回到父亲的王宫里去。

===Original===
She was very wise. She knew the languages of all animals. The fishes came from the deep at her call, and the birds from the trees. She could tell when the winds would rise, and when the sea would be still. She could have turned her enemies to stone, or given untold wealth to her friends. But for all that, when she smiled, her lips were very sad, and her eyes were always full of care. She said she was weary, and her father thought she was sick, and would have sent for the physicians, but she stopped him.

===Chinese===
她真是极聪明的。她通晓一切动物的语言：她一召唤，鱼儿便从深处游来，鸟儿便从树上飞来。她说得出风几时起、海几时静；她若愿意，能把仇敌变成石头，也能给朋友数不清的财富。可是尽管这样，她微笑的时候，唇边总带着深深的哀愁，眼睛里总是盛满了忧思。她说她倦了；父王只当她是病了，要传太医来，却被她拦住了。

===Original===
“How should physicians help me, my father,” she said, “seeing that I know more than they?”

===Chinese===
“太医们哪里帮得上我呢，父王，”她说，“我懂得的比他们还多呀。”

===Original===
One night, a year after she had taken her last lesson from the Wizard, she arose and returned to his cave, and he raised his eyes and saw her standing before him as formerly.

“What do you want?” he said. “I have taught you all I know.”

“You have taught me much,” she said, falling on her knees beside him, “yet I am ignorant of one thing—teach me that also—*how to be happy*.”

===Chinese===
上完巫师的最后一课，又过了一年。一天夜里，她起身回到他的洞里；老人抬起眼睛，看见她还像从前一样站在自己面前。

“你还来做什么？”他说，“我所知道的，都已经教给你了。”

“你教了我许许多多，”她说着，在他身旁跪了下来，“可有一件事我还不懂——把这个也教给我吧——*怎样才能够幸福*。”

===Original===
“Nay,” said the Wizard with a very mournful smile; “I cannot teach you that, for I do not know it myself. Go and ask it of them who know and are wiser than I.”

Then the Princess left the cave and wandered down to the seashore. All that night she spent sitting on a rock that jutted out into the sea, watching the wild sky and the moon coming and going behind the clouds. The sea dashed up around her, and the wind blew, but she did not fear them, and when the sun rose the waters were still and the wind fell. A skylark rose from the fields and flew straight up to heaven, singing as though his heart would burst with pure joy.

===Chinese===
“不，”巫师说着，脸上挂一个十分凄凉的微笑，“这个我教不了你，因为我自己也不知道。去找那些懂得它、比我更聪明的人问吧。”

于是公主离开洞穴，一路徘徊到海边。那一整夜，她都坐在一块探进海里的礁石上，望着狂野的天空，望着月亮在云后出没。海浪在她四周飞溅，风呼呼地吹，她却什么都不怕；待太阳升起，水面上风平浪静，风也息了。一只云雀从田野里飞起，直上青天，唱得仿佛那颗小小的心要因纯粹的欢乐而迸裂似的。

===Original===
“Surely that bird is happy,” said the Princess to herself; and she called it in its own tongue.

“Why do you sing?” she asked.

“I sing because I am so happy,” answered the lark.

===Chinese===
“这只鸟儿准是幸福的。”公主自言自语道。于是她用云雀自己的话唤住了它。

“你为什么唱歌？”她问。

“我唱，是因为我太快活了。”云雀答道。

===Original===
“And why are you so happy?” asked the Princess.

“So happy?” said the lark. “God is so good. The sky is so blue, and the fields are so green. Is that not enough to make me happy?”

“Teach me, then, that I may be happy too,” said Princess Fernanda.

===Chinese===
“那你为什么这么快活？”公主问。

“这么快活？”云雀说，“上帝这么仁慈，天这么蓝，田野这么绿——这还不足以让我快活吗？”

“那么教教我吧，好叫我也这样幸福。”费尔南达公主说。

===Original===
“I cannot,” said the lark; “I don’t know how to teach;” and then he rose, singing, into the blue overhead, and Princess Fernanda sighed and turned back towards the palace.

Outside her door she met her little lapdog, who barked and jumped for joy on seeing her.

===Chinese===
“我教不了，”云雀说，“我不知道怎么教。”说罢它便一路唱着，飞升到头顶的碧空里去了。费尔南达公主叹了口气，转身回宫去。

门外，她遇见自己的小哈巴狗，那狗一见她，欢喜得又跳又叫。

===Original===
“Little dog,” she said; “poor little dog, are you so glad to see me? Why are you so happy?”

“Why am I so happy?” said the little dog, surprised. “I have plenty to eat, and a soft cushion to rest upon, and you to caress me. Is not it enough to make me happy?”

“It is not enough for me,” said the Princess, sighing; but the little dog only wagged his tail and licked her hand.

===Chinese===
“小狗儿，”她说，“可怜的小狗儿，见了我你这么欢喜吗？你为什么这样快活？”

“我为什么这样快活？”小狗儿吃了一惊，“我有吃不完的食，有软软的垫子睡，还有你抚爱我——这还不足以让我快活吗？”

“这对我来说可不够。”公主叹息道。可小狗儿只是摇着尾巴，舔她的手。

===Original===
Inside her room was the Princess’s favourite little maid Doris, folding up her dresses.

“Doris,” she said, “you look very merry. Why are you so happy?”

“Please your Royal Highness, I am going to the fair,” answered Doris, “and Luke is to meet me there; only,” she added, pouting a little, “I wish I had a pretty new hat to wear with my new dress.”

===Chinese===
她的房间里，公主最宠爱的小侍女多丽丝（Doris）正在替她叠衣裳。

“多丽丝，”她说，“瞧你一脸欢喜。你为什么这样快活？”

“回殿下的话，我要去赶集，”多丽丝答道，“卢克（Luke）要在那儿跟我碰头。只是——”她微微撅起嘴，又添了一句，“我真希望有顶漂亮的新帽子，配我的新衣裳。”

===Original===
“Then you are not perfectly happy, so you cannot teach me,” said Princess Fernanda, and then she sighed again.

In the evening at sunset she arose, and went out into the village, and at the door of the first cottage to which she came, sat a woman nursing a baby, and hushing it to sleep. The baby was fat and rosy, and the mother looked down at it proudly.

===Chinese===
“那么你算不得十全十美的快活，也就教不了我了。”费尔南达公主说着，又叹了口气。

傍晚日落时分，她起身走到村子里去。她来到的第一家农舍门前，坐着一个女人，正给怀里的婴儿喂奶，哄他睡觉。婴儿白白胖胖、红红润润，做母亲的低头望着他，一脸骄傲。

===Original===
The Princess stopped, and spoke to her.

“You have a fine little child there,” she said. “Surely you must be very happy.”

The woman smiled.

===Chinese===
公主停下脚步，同她搭话。

“你这小宝宝真好，”她说，“你一定非常幸福吧。”

那女人微微一笑。

===Original===
“Yes,” she said, “so I am; only just now my goodman is out fishing, and as he’s rather late, it makes me anxious.”

===Chinese===
“是啊，”她说，“我确实幸福。只是我家那口子这会儿出海打鱼去了，回来得晚了些，叫人心里惦记。”

===Original===
“Then you could not teach me,” said the Princess, sighing to herself as she moved away. She wandered on till she came to a church, which she entered. All was still within, for the church was empty; but before the altar, on a splendid bier, lay the body of a young man, who had been killed in the war. He was dressed in his gay uniform, and his breast was covered with medals, and his sword lay beside him. He was shot through the heart, but his face was peaceful and his lips were smiling. The Princess walked to his side, and looked at the quiet face. Then she stooped and kissed the cold forehead, and envied the soldier. “If he could speak,” she said, “he surely could teach me. No living mouth could ever smile like that.” Then she looked up and saw a white angel standing on the other side of the bier, and she knew it was Death.

===Chinese===
“那你教不了我了。”公主暗自叹息着走开了。她又一路走去，来到一座教堂，便走了进去。堂内一片寂静，因为教堂里空无一人；只有祭坛前一座华丽的灵床上，躺着一位青年的遗体，他是在战争中阵亡的。他穿着一身鲜艳的军装，胸前挂满了勋章，佩剑放在身旁。他被子弹射穿了心脏，面容却安详，唇边还带着微笑。公主走到他身旁，端详着那张宁静的脸，随后俯下身去，吻了吻那冰冷的额头，心里羡慕着这士兵。“他若还能说话，”她说，“一定能教给我；活人的嘴，谁也笑不出那样的笑。”这时她抬起头来，只见灵床另一侧立着一位白衣天使，她知道，那便是死神（Death）。

===Original===
“You have taught him,” she said, holding out her arms. “Will you not teach me to smile like that?”

“Nay,” said Death, pointing to the medals on the dead man’s breast, “I taught him whilst he was doing his duty. I cannot teach you.” And so saying he vanished from her sight.

===Chinese===
“你教过他了，”她张开双臂说，“你不愿也教教我，让我那样微笑吗？”

“不，”死神指着亡人胸前的勋章说，“我是在他尽本分的时候教会他的。你，我教不了。”说罢，他便从她眼前消失了。

===Original===
She went out from the church down to the seashore. There was a high sea, and a great wind, a little child had been playing on a row of rocks, and had slipped off them into the water, and was struggling among the waves, and would soon be drowned, for he was beyond his depth in the water.

When the Princess saw him, she plunged into the water and swam to where the child was, and taking him in her arms, placed him safely on the rocks again, but the waves were so strong that she could scarcely keep above them. As she tried to seize the rocks, she saw Death coming over the water towards her, and she turned to meet him gladly.

===Chinese===
她走出教堂，下到海边。海上浪高风急。方才有个小孩子在一排礁石上玩耍，一失脚滑进了水里，正在浪涛中挣扎，眼看就要淹死了，因为那水早已没过了他的头顶。

公主一看见，便纵身跳进水里，游到孩子那里，把他抱在怀中，稳稳放回礁石上；可浪头实在凶猛，她勉强才浮得出水面。她正想抓住礁石，忽见死神踏着波涛向她走来，便欢欢喜喜地转身迎了上去。

===Original===
“Now,” said he, clasping her in his arms, “I will teach you all you want to know;” and he drew her under the water, and she died.

===Chinese===
“这下好了，”他说着，把她拥进怀里，“你想知道的一切，我都教你。”说着他把她曳入水中，她便死去了。

---

===Original===
The Kings servants found her lying on the shore, with her face white and her lips cold, but smiling as they had never smiled before, and her face was very calm. They carried her home, and she was laid out in great state, covered with gold and silver.

“She was so wise,” sobbed her little maid, as she placed flowers in the cold hand, “she knew everything.”

===Chinese===
国王的仆从们发现她躺在岸上，脸色苍白，嘴唇冰凉，唇边却带着前所未有的微笑，面容安然极了。他们把她抬回宫去，以极隆重的仪节装殓，周身覆着金银。

“她那么聪明，”她的小侍女抽泣着，往那冰冷的手里放上一朵朵花，“她什么都懂啊。”

===Original===
“Not everything,” said the skylark from the window; “for she asked me, ignorant though I am, to teach her how to be happy.”

“That was the one thing I could not teach her,” said the old Wizard, looking at the dead Princess’s face. “Yet I think now she must be wiser than I, and have learned that too. For see how she smiles.”

===Chinese===
“并不是什么都懂，”窗口的云雀说道，“因为她还请教过什么都不知道的我，求我教她怎样得到幸福呢。”

“那正是我唯一教不了她的一样东西，”老巫师望着亡公主的脸说，“不过我想，她如今必定比我更聪明了，连那一样也学会了。你瞧，她笑得多好啊。”
