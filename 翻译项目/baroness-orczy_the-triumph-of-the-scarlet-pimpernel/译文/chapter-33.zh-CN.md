## XXXIII. The Cataclysm / XXXIII. 大灾变

### I / I

===Original===

Forty names! Found on a list in the pocket of Robespierre’s coat!

Forty names! And every one of these that of a known opponent of Robespierre’s schemes of dictatorship: Tallien, Barrère, Vadier, Cambon, and the rest. Men powerful today, prominent Members of the Convention, leaders of the people, too﻿—but opponents!

===Chinese===

四十个名字！是在罗伯斯庇尔（Robespierre）外衣口袋里一份名单上发现的！

四十个名字！而每一个名字，都属于一个众所周知的、与罗伯斯庇尔独裁图谋作对的人：塔利安（Tallien）、巴雷尔（Barrère）、瓦迪耶（Vadier）、康蓬（Cambon），还有其余诸公。这些人今日正有权有势，是国民公会（the Convention）里声名显赫的议员，也是民众的领袖——然而，终究是作对的人！

===Original===

The inference was obvious, the panic general. That night﻿—it was the 8th Thermidor, July the 26th of the old calendar﻿—men talked of flight, of abject surrender, of appeal﻿—save the mark!﻿—to friendship, camaraderie, humanity! Friendship, camaraderie, humanity? An appeal to a heart of stone! They talked of everything, in fact, save of defying the tyrant; for such talk would have been folly.

===Chinese===

结论昭然若揭，恐慌也就无远弗届。那一夜——那是热月八日，旧历的七月二十六日——人人谈论出逃，谈论卑躬屈膝的投降，谈论去求告友谊、求告同志情谊、求告人性——亏说得出口！友谊、同志情谊、人性？向一颗石头做的心去求告！事实上他们什么都谈到了，独独不谈公然对抗暴君；因为那样的空谈，无异于痴人说梦。

===Original===

Defying the tyrant? Ye gods! When with a word he could sway the Convention, the Committees, the multitude, bend them to his will, bring them to heel like any tamer of beasts when he cracks his whip?

===Chinese===

公然对抗暴君？诸神在上！他一句话便能摇撼国民公会、摇撼各委员会、摇撼芸芸大众，把他们扭向自己的意志，把他们治得服服帖帖——恰似驯兽人鞭梢一响，兽群便应声帖耳？

===Original===

So men talked and trembled. All night they talked and trembled; for they did not sleep, those forty whose names were on Robespierre’s list. But Tallien, their chief, was nowhere to be found. ’Twas known that his fiancée, the beautiful Theresia Cabarrus, had been summarily arrested. Since then he had disappeared; and they﻿—the others﻿—were leaderless. But, even so, he was no loss. Tallien was ever pusillanimous, a temporiser﻿—what?

===Chinese===

于是人们一面谈论，一面发抖。他们谈论了一整夜，也发抖了一整夜；因为名字上了罗伯斯庇尔名单的那四十个人，谁也不曾合眼。可是他们的首领塔利安，却哪里也寻不见。事情已经传开：他的未婚妻、美丽的特蕾莎·卡巴吕斯（Theresia Cabarrus）被草草逮捕了。打那以后他便没了踪影；于是他们——其余这些人——成了群龙无首的一盘散沙。不过，就算这样，少了他也算不得什么损失。塔利安向来就是个怯懦之徒、一个遇事只会拖延观望的人——可不是么？

===Original===

And now the hour for temporising is past. Robespierre then is to be dictator of France. He will be dictator of France, in spite of any opposition led by those forty whose names are on his list! He will be dictator of France! He has not said it; but his friends have shouted it from the housetops, and have murmured under their breath that those who oppose Robespierre’s dictatorship are traitors to the land. Death then must be their fate.

===Chinese===

而今，拖延观望的时辰已经过去了。罗伯斯庇尔就要做法兰西的独裁者了。哪怕名单上那四十个人领头作对，他也照样要做法兰西的独裁者！他就要做法兰西的独裁者了！这话他不曾亲口说出；可他的朋友们已经把这话嚷到了屋顶上，又压着嗓子交头接耳：凡与罗伯斯庇尔的独裁为敌者，皆是这片土地的叛徒。那么，死亡便注定是他们的下场。

===Original===

When then, ye gods? What then?

===Chinese===

到那时候可怎么办呢，诸神在上？往后又能怎么样呢？

### II / II

===Original===

And so the day broke﻿—smiling, mark you! It was a beautiful warm July morning. It broke on what is perhaps the most stupendous cataclysm﻿—save one﻿—the world has ever known.

===Chinese===

于是这一天破晓了——您可得留意，它竟是笑脸迎人的！那是一个美丽和暖的七月清晨。而这一天所照临的，或许是世人所曾亲历的最惊天动地的一场大灾变——只除开那一桩之外。

===Original===

Behold the picture! A medley. A confusion. A whirl of everything that is passionate and cruel, defiant and desperate. Heavens, how desperate! Men who have thrown lives away as if lives were in truth grains of sand; men who have juggled with death dealt it and tossed it about like cards upon a gaming table. They are desperate now, because their own lives are at stake; and they find now that life can be very dear.

===Chinese===

请看这幅图画！一团大杂烩。一场大混乱。一切炽烈与残酷、一切狂傲与绝望的大漩涡。老天爷，何等的绝望！这些人，曾把性命随手抛掷，仿佛性命当真不过一把沙粒；这些人，曾把死亡玩弄于股掌之上，将它像赌桌上的纸牌一般发出去、甩出去。如今他们绝望了，因为命悬一线的换成了他们自己；如今他们才发觉，性命原来可以这般金贵。

===Original===

So, having greeted their leader, the forty draw together, watching the moment when humility will be most opportune.

===Chinese===

于是，四十个人见过首领之后，便凑作一群，盯着看哪一个时刻俯首帖耳最为得宜。

===Original===

Robespierre mounts the tribune. The hour has struck. His speech is one long, impassioned, involved tirade, full at first on vague accusations against the enemies of the Republic and the people, and is full of protestations of his own patriotism and selflessness. Then he warms to his own oratory; his words are prophetic of death, his voice becomes harsh﻿—like a screech owl’s, so we’re told. His accusations are no longer vague. He begins to strike.

===Chinese===

罗伯斯庇尔登上讲坛。钟点敲响了。他的演说是一篇冗长、激愤、缠夹不清的宏文，起头满是对共和国之敌、人民之敌的含糊指控，又满纸是他对自己爱国心与无私的剖白。继而，他为自家的辩才鼓荡起来；他的字字句句预兆着死亡，他的嗓音变得刺耳——据说，像鸣角鸮的啼叫一般。他的指控不再含糊。他出手了。

===Original===

Corruption! Backsliding! Treachery! Moderatism!﻿—oh, moderatism above all! Moderatism is treachery to the glorious revolution. Every victim spared form the guillotine is a traitor let loose against the people! A traitor, he who robs the guillotine of her prey! Robespierre stands alone incorruptible, true, faithful unto death!

===Chinese===

贪污！倒退！叛逆！温和主义！——哦，头一条便是温和主义！温和主义便是对光荣革命的叛逆。凡从断头台（guillotine）刀下饶过的牺牲者，都是放到人间来戕害人民的叛徒！凡从断头台口中夺走她猎物的人，便是叛徒！唯有罗伯斯庇尔孑然独立，不可腐蚀，真诚，坚贞，至死不渝！

===Original===

And for all that treachery, what remedy is there? Why, death of course! Death! The guillotine! New power to the sovereign guillotine! Death to all the traitors!

===Chinese===

而对付这一切叛逆，还有什么解药？嘿，当然是死亡！死亡！断头台！给至高无上的断头台再添新威权！一切叛徒，一律处死！

===Original===

And seven hundred faces became paler still with dread, and the sweat of terror rises on seven hundred brows. There were only forty names on that list﻿ ﻿… but there might be others somewhere else!

===Chinese===

七百张面孔骇得愈发惨白，七百个额角上渗出恐怖的冷汗。名单上只有四十个名字……可别处保不齐还有别的名单！

===Original===

And still the voice of Robespierre thunders on. His words fall of seven hundred pairs of ears like on a sounding-board; his friends, his sycophants, echo them; they applaud, rise in wild enthusiasm. ’Tis the applause that is thundering now!

===Chinese===

罗伯斯庇尔的声音兀自雷鸣般滚个不停。他的话语落在七百对耳廓上，恰似落在共鸣板上；他的朋友、他的谄媚之徒随声附和；他们鼓掌，在狂热的激奋中纷纷起立。此刻轰鸣着的，换成掌声了！

===Original===

One of the tyrant’s most abject slaves has put forward the motion that the great speech just delivered shall forthwith be printed, and distributed to every township, every village, throughout France, as a monument to the lofty patriotism of her greatest citizen.

===Chinese===

暴君手下一个最卑贱的奴才提出动议：方才这篇伟大的演说应即刻付印，颁行到法兰西全境的每一个乡镇、每一座村落，作为她最伟大的公民那份崇高爱国心的纪念碑。

===Original===

The motion at one moment looks as if it would be carried with acclamations; after which, Robespierre’s triumph would have risen to the height of deification. Then suddenly the note of dissension; the hush; the silence. The great Assembly is like a sounding-board that has ceased to respond. Something had turned the acclamations to mutterings, and then to silence. The sounding-board has given forth a dissonance. Citizen Tallien has demanded “delay in printing that speech,” and asked pertinently:

===Chinese===

这动议一时之间眼看便要在欢呼喝彩中通过了；果真通过，罗伯斯庇尔的凯旋便要升到封神的高度。忽然间，不谐之音起；继而是屏息；是死寂。宏伟的议会像一块戛然不应的共鸣板。有什么东西把欢呼变成了窃窃低语，又变成了沉默。共鸣板发出了不谐和的音。塔利安公民（citizen Tallien）要求“缓印这篇演说”，并且一针见血地质问道：

===Original===

“What has become of the Liberty of Opinion in this Convention?”

===Chinese===

“这个国民公会里的言论自由，都到哪儿去了？”

===Original===

His face is the colour of ashes, and his eyes, ringed with purple, gleam with an unnatural fire. The coward has become bold; the sheep has donned the lion’s skin.

===Chinese===

他面如死灰，一双眼睛圈着青紫，燃着一股不自然的火。懦夫胆壮了；绵羊披上了狮皮。

===Original===

There is a flutter in the Convention, a moment’s hesitation. But the question is put to the vote, and the speech is not to be printed. A small matter, in truth﻿—printing or not printing﻿ ﻿… Does the Destiny of France hang on so small a peg?

===Chinese===

公会里起了一阵骚动，一刻的踌躇。然而问题交付了表决：演说不予印行。小事一桩，说实在的——印，还是不印……难道法兰西的命运，就悬在这样一枚小小的钉子上？

===Original===

It is a small matter; and yet how full of portent! Like the breath of mutiny blowing across a ship. But nothing more occurs just then. Robespierre, lofty in his scorn, puts the notes of his speech into his pocket. He does not condescend to argue. He, the master of France, will not deign to bandy words with his slaves. And he stalks out of the Hall surrounded by his friends.

===Chinese===

小事一桩；然而又是何等凶兆毕露！仿佛一阵哗变的气流掠过船帆。不过当刻倒没有再生出别的事端。罗伯斯庇尔满腔鄙夷、高高在上，把演说稿揣回了口袋。他不屑于争辩。他，法兰西的主宰，犯不上屈尊同他的奴仆们斗口。随后他在朋友们的簇拥下，昂然阔步出了大厅。

===Original===

There has been a breath of mutiny; but his is still the iron heel, powerful enough to crush a raging revolt. His withdrawal﻿—proud, silent, menacing﻿—is in keeping with his character and with the pose which he has assumed of late. But he is still the Chosen of the People; and the multitude is there, thronging the streets of Paris﻿—there, to avenge the insult put upon their idol by a pack of slinking wolves.

===Chinese===

哗变的气息是掠过了一阵；可他的铁蹄依旧踏在那里，力道足以碾碎一场汹汹的叛乱。他的退场——高傲、沉默、含着威吓——正合他的性情，也正合他近来摆出的那副姿态。但他终究仍是人民的天选之骄子；民众也在那里，把巴黎的条条街道挤得水泄不通——在那里，等着向那一伙鬼鬼祟祟的豺狼，讨还他们偶像所受的侮辱。

### III / III

===Original===

And now the picture becomes still more poignant. It is painted in colours more vivid, more glowing than and again the Hall of the Convention is crowded to the roof, with Tallien and his friends, in a close phalanx, early at their post!

===Chinese===

而今，这幅图画变得愈加锥心刺骨。它的色彩也涂得比先前更鲜明、更炽烈；国民公会的大厅又一次挤到了屋顶，塔利安和他的朋友们结成密不透风的方阵，早早地守在了各自的岗位上！

===Original===

Tallien is there, pale, resolute, the fire of his hatred kept up by anxiety for his beloved. The night before, at the corner of a dark street, a surreptitious hand slipped a scrap of paper into the pocket of his coat. It was a message written by Theresia in prison, and written with her own blood. How it ever came into his pocket Tallien never know; but the few impassioned, agonised words, seared his very soul and whipped up his courage:

===Chinese===

塔利安在那里，面色苍白，心意已决，对心上人的焦灼，把他的仇恨之火煨得正旺。前一天夜里，在一条黑黢黢的街角，一只悄没声息的手把一方纸片塞进了他的外衣口袋。那是特蕾莎（Theresia）在狱中写下的一封短信——用她自己的血写成的。这封信究竟怎么进的他口袋，塔利安始终无从知晓；可那寥寥几句热血激荡、泣血锥心的话，烙穿了他的灵魂，也把他的勇气鞭策了起来：

===Original===

“The Commissary of Police has just left me,” Theresia wrote. “He came to tell me that tomorrow I must appear before the tribunal. This means the guillotine. And I, who thought that you were a *man*﻿ ﻿… !”

===Chinese===

“警务专员（Commissary of Police）刚刚离开我，”特蕾莎写道，“他来告诉我，明天我就得站到法庭跟前去了。这就意味着断头台。而我，还当您是个*男子汉*……！”

===Original===

Not only is his own head in peril, not only that of his friends; but the life of the woman whom he worships hangs now upon the thread of his own audacity and of his courage.

===Chinese===

濒临险境的，不只是他自己这颗头颅，也不仅是他朋友们的；他所奉若神明的那个女人的性命，如今就悬在他自己的胆魄与勇气的丝线上。

===Original===

St. Just on this occasion is the first to mount the tribune; and Robespierre, the very incarnation of lustful and deadly Vengeance, stands silently by. He has spent the afternoon and evening with his friends at the Jacobins’ Club, where deafening applause greeted his every word, and wild fury raged against his enemies.

===Chinese===

这一回，圣茹斯特（St. Just）第一个登上讲坛；罗伯斯庇尔——活脱脱是那贪馋嗜血、致人死命的“复仇”的化身——默然伫立在一旁。整个下午和黄昏，他都是在雅各宾俱乐部（the Jacobins’ Club）的朋友们中间度过的；在那里，他吐出的每一个字都赢得震耳欲聋的掌声，对他的敌人则燃烧着狂暴的怒火。

===Original===

It is then to be a fight to the finish! To your tents, O Israel!

===Chinese===

看样子，这就要有一场血战到底了！以色列人哪，各回各的帐棚去罢！

===Original===

To the guillotine all those who have dared to say one word against the Chosen of the People! St. Just shall thunder Vengeance from the tribune at the Convention, whilst Henriot, the drunken and dissolute Commandant of the Municipal Guard, shall, by the might of sword and fire, proclaim the sovereignty of Robespierre through the streets of Paris. That is the picture as it has been painted in the minds of the tyrant and of his sycophants: a picture of death paramount, and of Robespierre rising like a new Phoenix from out the fire of calumny and revolt, greater, more unassailable than before.

===Chinese===

凡胆敢对人民的天选之骄子道半个不字的人，统统送上断头台！圣茹斯特要在国民公会的讲坛上雷鸣般宣喝复仇，而昂里奥（Henriot）——市政卫队（Municipal Guard）那个酗酒放荡的司令——则要凭刀剑与烈火的威势，在巴黎的条条街道上宣告罗伯斯庇尔的至尊主权。这便是暴君和他的谄媚之徒们心中绘出的图景：死亡君临一切，而罗伯斯庇尔像一只新生的凤凰，从诽谤与叛乱的烈火中腾身而起，比先前更伟大、更无可撼动。

===Original===

And lo! One sweep of the brush, and the picture is changed.

===Chinese===

可是看哪！画笔只一挥，图景就变了。

===Original===

Ten minutes﻿ ﻿… less﻿ ﻿… and the whole course of the world’s history is altered. No sooner had St. Just mounted the tribune than Tallien jumped to his feet. His voice, usually meek and cultured, rises in a harsh crescendo, until it drowns that of the younger orator.

===Chinese===

十分钟……还不到……世界历史的整个进程便改换了河道。圣茹斯特刚一登上讲坛，塔利安便霍地跳了起来。他平日温顺文雅的嗓音，此刻拔成一阵嘶哑的渐强音，直到把那位年轻演说家的声音整个淹没。

===Original===

“Citizens,” he exclaims, “I ask for truth! Let us tear aside the curtain behind which lurk concealed the real conspirators and the traitors!”

===Chinese===

“公民们，”他呼喊道，“我要求真相！让我们把那道帷幕扯下来吧——真正的阴谋家和叛徒们，正潜藏在那后面！”

===Original===

“Yes, yes! Truth! Let us have the truth!” One hundred voices﻿—not forty﻿—have raised the echo.

===Chinese===

“是的，是的！真相！我们要真相！”一百个声音——不止四十个——把这片回声高高扬起。

===Original===

The mutiny is on the verge of becoming open revolt, is that already, perhaps. It is like a spark fallen﻿—who knows where?﻿—into a powder magazine. Robespierre feels it, sees the spark. He knows that one movement, one word, one plunge into that magazine, foredoomed though it be to destruction, one stamp with a sure foot, may yet quench the spark, may yet smother the mutiny. He rushes to the tribune, tries to mount. But Tallien has forestalled him, elbows him out of the way, and turns to the seven hundred with a cry that rings far beyond the Hall, out into the streets.

===Chinese===

哗变正濒于化作公开的叛乱——或者，这已经是叛乱了，也未可知。它像一粒火星，落进了——谁知道落在哪里？——一座火药库。罗伯斯庇尔感觉到了，望见了那粒火星。他知道，只要一个动作、一个字眼、一次奋身向那火药库的猛扑——纵然注定与之偕亡——只要一只笃定的脚稳稳一跺，也许仍能扑熄火星，也许仍能把这场哗变闷死在烟尘里。他抢向讲坛，想要登上去。可塔利安已抢在他前头，一膀子把他挤开，转身面向那七百人，迸出一声呐喊，声浪远远冲出大厅之外，滚进外面的条条街道。

===Original===

“Citizens!” he thunders in his turn. “I begged of you just now to tear aside the curtains behind which lurk the traitors. Well, the curtain is already rent. And if you dare not strike at the tyrant now, then ’tis I who will dare!” And from beneath his coat he draws a dagger and raises it above his head. “And I will plunge this into his heart,” he cries, “if you have not the courage to smite!”

===Chinese===

“公民们！”这回轮到他雷鸣般怒吼了。“方才我恳求过你们，去扯开那道叛徒们潜藏其后的帷幕。好——帷幕已经扯裂了。倘若此刻你们不敢向暴君下手，那么，敢下手的就该是我了！”说着，他从外衣底下抽出一柄匕首，高高举过头顶。“倘若你们没有胆量出击，”他大喊道，“我就把这柄匕首捅进他的心窝！”

===Original===

His words, that gleaming bit of steal, fan the spark into a flame. Within a few seconds, seven hundred voices are shouting, “Down with the tyrant!” Arms are waving, hands gesticulate wildly, excitedly. Only a very few shout: “Behold the dagger of Brutus!” All the others retort with “Tyranny!” and “Conspiracy!” and with cries of “*Vive la Liberté!*”

===Chinese===

他的话语，加上那一小片寒光闪闪的钢铁，把火星扇成了烈焰。不出几秒钟，七百个嗓音齐声呐喊：“打倒暴君！”臂膀挥舞着，双手狂热而亢奋地比画着。只有寥寥几个人高呼：“看哪，布鲁图斯（Brutus）的匕首！”其余所有人都以“暴政！”“阴谋！”回敬，还爆出一片“*自由万岁！（Vive la Liberté!）*”的呼声。

===Original===

At this hour all is confusion and deafening uproar. In vain Robespierre tries to speak. He demands to speak. He hurls insults, anathema, upon the President, who relentless refuses him speech and jingles his bell against him.

===Chinese===

此时此刻，一切尽是混乱与震耳欲聋的鼎沸。罗伯斯庇尔想发言，却是徒劳。他要求发言。他把辱骂与诅咒掷向议长，议长却毫不容情，拒不许他开口，还把铃铛对着他摇个不停。

===Original===

“President of Assassins,” the falling tyrant cries, “I demand speech of thee!”

===Chinese===

“杀人犯们的议长，”那正在倾覆的暴君喊道，“我向你要求发言！”

===Original===

But the bell goes jingling on, and Robespierre, choked with rage and terror, “turns blue” we are told, and his hand goes up to his throat.

===Chinese===

可铃声只是叮铃铃响个不停；罗伯斯庇尔叫狂怒与恐怖一齐噎住，据说“脸都憋青了”，一只手直摸向自己的喉咙。

===Original===

“The blood of Danton chokes thee!” cries one man. And these words seem like the last blow dealt to the fallen foe. The next moment the voice of an obscure Deputy is raised, in order to speak the words that have been hovering on every lip:

===Chinese===

“是丹东（Danton）的血噎住了你！”有人喊道。这句话，仿佛是朝那倒下的仇敌挥去的最后一击。紧接着，一位籍籍无名的议员开口了，道出了那句早已悬在人人唇边的话：

===Original===

“I demand a decree of accusation against Robespierre!”

===Chinese===

“我要求通过对罗伯斯庇尔的控告法令（decree of accusation）！”

===Original===

“Accusation!” comes from seven hundred throats. “The decree of accusation!”

===Chinese===

“控告！”七百条喉咙一齐迸出。“控告法令！”

===Original===

The President jingles his bell, puts the question, and the motion is passed unanimously.

===Chinese===

议长摇了摇铃，将问题交付表决，动议经全场一致通过。

===Original===

Maximilien Robespierre﻿—erstwhile master of France﻿—is decreed *accused*.

===Chinese===

马克西米连·罗伯斯庇尔（Maximilien Robespierre）——法兰西昔日的主宰——经法令宣告为*被告*。
