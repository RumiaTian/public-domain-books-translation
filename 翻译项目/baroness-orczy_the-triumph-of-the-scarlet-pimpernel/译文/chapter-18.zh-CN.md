## XVIII. Night and Morning / XVIII. 夜与晨

### I / I

===Original===

Theresia waited for a moment or two at the turn of the passage, until her keen ear had told her that Bertrand was no longer on the watch and had closed the door behind him. Then she retraced her steps﻿—on tiptoe, lest he should hear.

She found her way to the front door; it was still on the latch. She opened it and peered out into the night. The little porch was deserted, but out there on the quay a few passersby still livened the evening with chatter or song. Theresia was on the point of stepping out of the porch, when a familiar voice hailed her softly by name:

===Chinese===

特蕾莎（Theresia）在过道的拐角上停了一两分钟，直到她那双敏锐的耳朵告诉她，贝特朗（Bertrand）已不再守在那里，已经随手关上了身后的门。于是她转身往回走——踮着脚尖，生怕让他听见。

她一路摸到前门；门仍旧只搭着闩。她推开门，向外窥探夜色。小小的门廊里空无一人，外头码头上却还有几个过路的人，正以谈笑或歌声给这夜晚添着生气。特蕾莎正要跨出门廊，一个熟悉的嗓音轻轻唤出了她的名字：


===Original===

“Citoyenne Cabarrus!”

===Chinese===

“卡巴吕斯女公民！”


===Original===

A man, dressed in dark clothes, with high boots and sugar-loaf hat, came out from the dark angle behind the porch.

“Not here!” Theresia whispered eagerly. “Out on the quay. Wait for me there, my little Chauvelin. I’ll be with you anon. I have so much to tell you!”

===Chinese===

一个男人从门廊后面阴暗的角落里走了出来：一身深色衣裳，蹬着高筒马靴，头戴一顶糖面包式的尖顶高帽（sugar-loaf hat）。

“别在这儿！”特蕾莎急切地悄声道。“到码头上去。在那儿等着我，我的小肖夫兰（Chauvelin）。我一会儿就到。我有好多话要对你讲！”


===Original===

Silently, he did as she desired. She waited for a moment in the porch, watching the meagre figure in the dark cloak making its way across to the quay, then walking rapidly in the direction of the Pent. The moon was dazzlingly brilliant. The harbour and the distant sea glistened like diamond-studded sheets of silver. From afar there came the sound of the castle clock striking ten. The groups of passersby had dwindled down to an occasional amorous couple strolling homewards, whispering soft nothings and gazing enraptured at the moon; or half-a-dozen sailors lolling down the quays arm in arm, on their way back to their ship, obstructing the road, yelling and singing the refrain of the newest ribald song; or perhaps a belated peddler, weary of an unprofitable beat, wending his way dejectedly home.

One of these poor wretches﻿—a cripple with a wooden leg and bent nearly double with the heavy load on his pack﻿—paused for a moment beside the porch, held out a grimy hand to Theresia, with a pitiable cry.

===Chinese===

他一声不响地依了她。她在门廊里站了片刻，望着那黑斗篷下瘦削的身影一路穿到码头那边，随即快步朝潘特（the Pent）的方向去了。月色亮得耀眼。港湾和远处的海面波光闪闪，像缀满钻石的银缎。远处传来城堡的钟敲响十点的声音。成群的行人渐渐散尽，只剩偶尔一两对多情的情侣挽着臂往家走，咬着耳朵说些甜腻的痴话，如痴如醉地望着月亮；或是五六个水手勾肩搭背、懒懒散散地荡过码头回船去，挡住了道，嚷着唱着最新下流小调的副歌；再不然，便是一个跑了一天、赚头寥寥而心生倦怠的晚归小贩，正蔫头耷脑地往家走。

这些可怜虫里头的一个——一个装着木腿的跛子，叫背上沉重的货担压得几乎折成两截——在门廊旁边停了停，向特蕾莎伸出一只脏污的手，发出一声可怜的哀告。


===Original===

“Of your charity, kind sir! Buy a little something from the pore ole man, to buy a bit of bread!”

He looked utterly woebegone, with lank grey hair blown about by the breeze and a colourless face covered with sweat, that shone like painted metal in the moonlight.

===Chinese===

“发发善心，好心的先生！从这可怜的老头子手里买点儿什么吧，好换块面包吃！”

他那副模样愁苦到了极处：稀疏的灰白头发叫风吹得乱蓬蓬的，一张没有血色的脸上汗涔涔，在月光底下亮得像上了漆的金属。


===Original===

“Buy a little something, kind sir!” he went on, in a shrill, throaty voice. “I’ve a sick wife at ’ome, and pore little gran’childen!”

Theresia﻿—a little frightened, and not at all charitably inclined at this hour﻿—turned hastily away and went back into to house, whither the cripple’s vigorous curses followed her.

===Chinese===

“买点儿什么吧，好心的先生！”他接着说，嗓音又尖又哑。“家里有个害病的老婆，还有几个可怜的小孙儿孙女！”

特蕾莎——心里有几分发毛，这个钟点也全无行善的兴致——赶忙扭过身去，回屋里去了；那跛子中气十足的咒骂，一路追着她进了门。


===Original===

“May Satan and all his armies﻿—”

She shut the door on him and hastened up the passage. That cadaverous old reprobate had caused her to shudder as with the presentiment of coming evil.

===Chinese===

“叫撒旦和他满营的兵马——”

她把他那一套关在门外，急忙顺着过道赶了回去。那个死人脸的老无赖，害她打了个寒噤，仿佛大祸将至的预感攫住了她。


### II / II

===Original===

With infinite precaution, Theresia peeped into the room where she had left Bertrand. She saw him lying on the sofa, fast asleep.

===Chinese===

特蕾莎万般小心地朝她撇下贝特朗的那间屋子张了一眼。她看见他躺在那张沙发上，睡得正沉。


===Original===

On the table in the middle of the room there was an old ink-horn, a pen, and few loose sheets of paper. Noiseless as a mouse, Theresia slipped into the room, sat at the table, and hurriedly wrote a few lines. Bertrand had not moved. Having written her missive, Theresia folded it carefully, and still on tiptoe, more stealthily even than before, she slipped the paper between the young man’s loosely clasped fingers. Then, as soundlessly as she had come, she glided out of the room, ran down the passage, and was out in the porch once more, breathless but relieved.

Bertrand had not moved; and no one had seen her. Theresia only paused in the porch long enough to recover her breath, then, without hesitation and with rapid strides, she crossed over to the water’s edge and walked along in the direction of the Pent.

===Chinese===

屋子正中的桌上摆着一只旧式的角制墨壶（ink-horn）、一支笔，还有几张零散的纸。特蕾莎悄没声儿地像只老鼠溜进屋来，在桌边坐下，匆匆写了几行字。贝特朗没有动。写罢手书，特蕾莎细细把它折好，仍旧踮着脚尖——比先前愈发鬼祟——把纸塞进年轻人松松交扣的手指缝里。随后，她像来时一样无声无息地滑出房间，跑下过道，重又站到了门廊里，气喘吁吁，心里却踏实了。

贝特朗没有动；也没有人瞧见她。特蕾莎只在门廊里停到喘匀了气，便毫不迟疑、大步流星地走到水边，沿着潘特的方向去了。


===Original===

Whereupon, the figure of the old cripple emerged from out the shadows. He gazed after the fast retreating figure of Theresia for a moment or two, then threw down his load, straightened out his back, and stretched out his arms from the shoulders with a sigh of content. After which amazing proceedings he gave a soft, inward chuckle, unstrapped his wooden leg, slung it with his discarded load across his broad shoulders, and turning his back upon harbour and sea, turned up the High Street and strode rapidly away.

===Chinese===

就在这时，那老跛子的身影从阴影里钻了出来。他望着特蕾莎飞快远去的背影出了一会儿神，随即撂下货担，直起腰板，双肩一舒，满意地叹了口气。这番惊人的举动过后，他又打喉咙里发出一声低低的轻笑，解下那条木腿，把它连同撂下的货担一齐扛上宽阔的肩膀，背过身去不再看港湾和大海，拐上高街（High Street），大步流星地去了。


### III / III

===Original===

When Bertrand Moncrif woke, the dawn was peeping in through the uncurtained window. He felt cold and stiff. It took him some time to realise where he was, to collect his scattered senses. He had been dreaming﻿ ﻿… here in this room﻿ ﻿… Theresia had been here﻿ ﻿… and she had laid her head against his breast and allowed him to soothe and comfort her. Then she said that she would come back﻿ ﻿… and he﻿ ﻿… like a fool﻿ ﻿… had fallen asleep.

===Chinese===

贝特朗·蒙克里夫（Bertrand Moncrif）醒来时，晨光正从没挂帘子的窗子里窥探进来。他觉得又冷又僵。过了好一会儿，他才醒悟自己身在何处，才收拾起涣散的神思。他方才一直在做梦……就在这间屋里……特蕾莎来过这里……她曾把头靠在他胸前，由他抚慰她、宽解她。后来她说她要回来……而他……像个傻子似的……竟睡着了。


===Original===

He jumped up, fully awake now; and as he did so a folded scrap of paper fell out of his hand. He had not known that it was there when first he woke, and somehow it appeared to be a part of his dream. As it lay there on the sanded floor at his feet, it looked strangely ghostlike, ominous; and it was with a trembling hand that, presently, he picked it up.

Every minute now brought fuller daylight into the room; a grey, cold light, for the window faced the southwest, showing a wide stretch of the tidal harbour and the open sea beyond. The sun, not fully risen, had not yet shed warmth over the landscape, and to Bertrand this colourless dawn, the mysterious stillness which earth assumes just before it wakens to the sun’s kiss, seemed inexpressibly dreary and desolate.

===Chinese===

他一跃而起，这下子全醒了；随着这动作，一小方折起的纸片从手里飘落。初醒时他并不知道它在那里，也不知怎么，它倒像成了他那梦的一部分。它躺在脚边撒着沙的地面上，模样怪异，如鬼影一般透着不祥；过了片刻，他才伸出一只发颤的手，把它拾了起来。

天光一分一寸地照进屋来，越照越满；却是一种灰白清冷的光，因为这扇窗朝向西南，望得见一大片潮汐港，和港外开阔的汪洋。太阳还没有完全升起，尚未把暖意洒向大地上的景物；在贝特朗看来，这没有颜色的黎明，这大地在太阳一吻中醒来之前佯装出的神秘静默，显得说不出的凄凉萧索。


===Original===

He went to the window and threw open the casement. Down below, a kitchen wench was busy scrubbing the flagged steps of the porch; over in the inner harbour, one or two fishing vessels were preparing to put out to sea; and from the tidal harbour, the graceful yacht which yesterday had brought him﻿—Bertrand﻿—and his friends safely to this land of refuge, was majestically gliding out, like a beautiful swan with gleaming wings outspread.

===Chinese===

他走到窗前，推开了窗扉。底下，一个帮厨的丫头正忙着刷洗门廊的石板台阶；那边的内港里，有一两条渔船正准备出海；而那艘昨天把他——贝特朗——和他的朋友们平安送到这片避难国土的秀美游艇，正从潮汐港里雍容地滑将出来，像一只张开闪亮翅膀的美丽天鹅。


===Original===

Controlling his apprehension, his nervousness, Bertrand at last contrived to unfold the mysterious epistle. He read the few lines that were traced with a delicate, feminine hand, and with a sigh of infinite longing and of ardent passion, he pressed the paper to his lips. Theresia had sent him a message. Finding him asleep, she had slipped it into his hand. The marvel was that he did not wake when she stooped over him, and perhaps even touched his forehead with her lips.

===Chinese===

贝特朗按住心头的疑惧与慌乱，终于把那封神秘的书信展开来。他读了那几行以娟秀的女子笔迹写下的字，带着一声无限渴念、炽热痴情的叹息，把那纸按在了唇上。特蕾莎给他捎来了音信。见他睡熟了，她便把它塞进了他手里。奇的是，她俯身向他的时候，他竟没有醒来；说不定她的唇，还碰过他的额头呢。


===Original===

“A kind soul,” so the message ran, “hath taken compassion on me. There was no room for me at the inn, and she has offered me a bed in her cottage, somewhere close by. I do not know where it is. I have arranged with the landlord that you shall be left undisturbed in the small room where you found one another, and where the four walls will whisper to you of me. Good night, my beloved! Tomorrow you will go to London with the de Servals. I will follow later. It is better so. In London you will find me at the house of Mme. de Neafchâteau, a friend of my father’s who lives at No. 54 in the Soho Square, and who offered me hospitality in the days when I thought I might visit London for pleasure. She will receive me now that I am poor and an exile. Come to me there. Until then my heart will feed on the memory of your kiss.”

The letter was signed “Theresia.”

===Chinese===

“一位好心人向我动了恻隐之心，”信上这样写道，“客栈里没有我的床位了，她便让我在她的村舍里过夜，就在这附近什么地方。那究竟是什么地方，我也不知道。我已同店主讲妥，让你留在这间小屋里，不受搅扰——你们便是在这间屋里寻着彼此的，这里的四壁，会向你低声说起我。晚安，我亲爱的人！明天你随德·塞尔瓦尔（de Servals）一家去伦敦（London）。我随后就来。这样更好。到了伦敦，你到德·讷夫沙托夫人（Mme. de Neafchâteau）家里便能找到我——她是我父亲的朋友，住在苏豪广场（Soho Square）54 号；从前我还当自己会为着游乐到伦敦来一趟，她那时便请我上她家做客。如今我落魄了、流亡了，她也还是会收留我的。到那里来找我吧。在那之前，我的心，就以你那一吻的回忆为食粮。”

信的落款是“特蕾莎”。


===Original===

Bertrand pressed it time and again to his lips. Never in his wildest dreams had he hoped for this; never even in those early days of rapture had he tasted such perfect bliss. The letter he hid against his breast. He was immeasurably happy, felt as if he were treading on air. The sea, the landscape, no longer looked grey and dreary. This was England, the land of the free, the land wherein he had regained his beloved. Ah, the mysterious Scarlet Pimpernel, while seeking ignoble vengeance against her, for sins which she never had committed, did in truth render him and her a priceless service. Theresia, courted, adulated, over in Paris, had been as far removed from Bertrand Moncrif as the stars; but here, where she was poor and lonely, a homeless refugee like himself, she turned instinctively to the faithful lover, who would gladly die to ensure her happiness.

===Chinese===

贝特朗把它按在唇上，吻了一遍又一遍。他纵然做最狂野的梦，也不曾指望过这个；便在早先那些心醉神迷的日子里，他也从不曾尝过这样圆满的至福。他把信藏进了贴胸的衣襟。他幸福得没有边际，只觉得脚下像踩着云端。大海和四野的景色，不再显得灰暗凄凉。这就是英格兰（England），自由的土地，他重新赢得心上人的土地。啊，那位神秘的猩红石榴红（Scarlet Pimpernel），为着她从未犯下的罪过而向她寻那卑劣的报复，却当真为他和她两人做成了一件无价的功德。在巴黎（Paris）那边的时日，特蕾莎被人追求、被人奉承，同贝特朗·蒙克里夫之间隔着星辰一般遥远的距离；可是在这里，她贫穷、孤单，是个同他一样无家可归的流亡者，便本能地投向了这个忠实的情人——一个为保她幸福、甘愿赴死的情人。


===Original===

With that letter in his possession, Bertrand felt that he could not remain indoors. He was pining for open spaces, the sea, the mountains, God’s pure air﻿—the air which she too was breathing even now. He snatched up his hat and made his way out of the little building. The kitchen wench paused in her scrubbing and looked up smiling as he ran past her, singing and shouting for joy. For Régine﻿—the tender, loving heart that pined for him and for his love﻿—he had not a thought. She was the past, the dull, drabby past wherein he had dwelt before he knew how glorious a thing life could be, how golden the future, how rosy that horizon far away.

===Chinese===

怀里揣着这封信，贝特朗觉得自己再也待不住屋里了。他渴念着开阔的天地、大海、群山、上帝纯净的空气——这空气，此刻她不也在呼吸着么。他一把抓起帽子，出了那栋小屋。他从帮厨丫头身边跑过去时，又唱又喊、欢天喜地；丫头停下手里擦洗的活计，含笑抬眼望他。至于雷吉娜（Régine）——那颗为他、也为他的爱苦苦思恋着的温柔多情的心——他连一个念头也不曾分给她。她是过去，是那段阴沉暗淡的过去；他还不曾知道生命能何等辉煌、未来能何等金黄、那天边的地平线能何等绯红的时候，就一直住在那段过去里。


===Original===

By the time he reached the harbour, the sun had risen in all its glory. Way out against the translucent sky, the graceful silhouette of the schooner swayed gently in the morning breeze, her outspread sails gleaming like wings that are tinged with gold. Bertrand watched her for awhile. He thought of the mysterious Scarlet Pimpernel and the hideous vengeance which he had wrought against his beloved. And the rage which possessed his soul at the thought obscured for a moment the beauty of the morning and the glory of the sky. With a gesture characteristic of his blood and of his race, he raised his fist and shook it in the direction of the distant ship.

===Chinese===

等他到了港口，太阳已经升起，光华万丈。极远处，衬着澄澈半透的天幕，那艘纵帆船秀美的侧影在晨风里轻轻摇曳，张开的帆帆光闪耀，像染上了金色的翅膀。贝特朗望了它好一会儿。他想起那位神秘的猩红石榴红，想起他对他心上人施行的那场可憎的报复。一念及此，攫住他灵魂的怒火，一时间遮蔽了清晨的秀美与天空的壮丽。他做了一个他血脉与他种族所特有的手势，举起拳头，朝远处那艘船的方向狠狠挥了挥。
