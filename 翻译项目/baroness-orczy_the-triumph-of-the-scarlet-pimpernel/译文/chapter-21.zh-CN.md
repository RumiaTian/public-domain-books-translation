## XXI. Memories / XXI. 追忆

### I / I

===Original===

When Marguerite Blakeney finally recovered consciousness, the sun was low down in the west. She was in a coach﻿—not her own﻿—which was being whisked along the road at terrific speed. She was alone, her mouth gagged, her wrists and her ankles tied with cords, so that she could neither speak nor move﻿—a helpless log, being taken﻿ ﻿… whither?﻿ ﻿… and by whom?

===Chinese===

玛格丽特·布莱克尼（Marguerite Blakeney）终于恢复知觉的时候，太阳已经西垂，落得低低的了。她身在一辆马车里——不是她自己的那辆——车子正以骇人的速度沿着大路飞驰。她孤身一人，嘴里堵着东西，手腕和脚踝都捆着绳索，说不出话，也动弹不得——一段任人摆布的木头，正被载着……载往何方？……又落在谁的手里？


===Original===

Bertrand was not here. Through the front window of the coach she could perceive the vague outline of two men sitting on the driver’s seat, whilst another was riding the off-leader. Four horses were harnessed to the light coach. It flew along in a southeasterly direction, the while the shades of evening were fast drawing in.

===Chinese===

贝特朗（Bertrand）不在这里。透过马车的前窗，她能辨出坐在车夫座上那两个人的模糊轮廓，另有一个则骑在右前头的梢马上。四匹马套着这辆轻便马车，朝东南方向一路飞驰，而暮色正急速地围拢下来。


===Original===

Marguerite had seen too much of the cruelties and barbarities of this world, too much of the hatred that existed between enemy countries, and too much of the bitter rancour felt by certain men against her husband and indirectly against herself, not to realise at once whence the blow had come that had struck her. Something too in the shape of that back which she perceived through the window in front of her, something in the cut of the threadbare coat, the set of the black bow at the nape of the neck, was too familiar to leave her even for a moment in doubt. Here was no ordinary footpad, no daring abduction with a view to reward or ransom. This was the work of her husband’s enemies, who, through her, were once more striving to get at him.

===Chinese===

玛格丽特见过的世间残忍与暴虐太多了，见过的交战敌国之间的仇恨太多了，某些人对她丈夫、又连带着对她本人怀有的那种刻骨怨毒，她也见得太多了——所以她立刻就明白，这一击来自何方。还有，透过面前的车窗她望见的那副背影的模样，那件磨得露线的旧外套的剪裁，后颈上黑蝴蝶结的系法，也都熟悉得叫她连片刻的疑惑都不曾有。这不是寻常的拦路剪径之贼，也不是为了勒取赎金酬赏而干的胆大绑票。这是她丈夫的仇敌们干下的勾当——他们要借着她，再一次去够到他。


===Original===

Bertrand Moncrif had been the decoy. Whence had come the hatred which prompted him to raise his hand against the very man to whom he owed his life, Marguerite was still too dazed to conjecture. He had gone, and taken his secret of rancour with him, mayhap forever. Lying pinioned and helpless as she was, Marguerite had but the one thought: in what way would those fiends who had her a prisoner use her as a leverage against the life and honour of the Scarlet Pimpernel? They had held her once before﻿—not so very long ago﻿—in Boulogne, and he had emerged unscathed, victorious over them all.

===Chinese===

贝特朗·蒙克里夫（Bertrand Moncrif）就是那诱饵。那股仇恨究竟从何而来，竟驱使他对自己的救命恩人举起手来——玛格丽特还昏沉得很，无从揣度。他走了，把他那腔怨毒的秘密随身带走了，说不定是永远带走了。玛格丽特被捆着躺在那里，全然无助，心里只转着一个念头：那些把她握在掌中的恶魔，要怎样拿她作筹码，去撬动猩红石榴红（the Scarlet Pimpernel）的性命与名誉？他们先前也扣押过她一回——就在不算许久以前——在布洛涅（Boulogne），而他毫发无伤地脱了身，把他们尽数斗败了。


===Original===

Marguerite, helpless and pinioned, forced her thoughts to dwell on that time, when his enemies had filled to the brim the cup of humiliation and of dread which was destined to reach him through her hands, and his ingenuity and his daring dashed the cup to the ground ere it reached her lips. In truth, her plight then, at Boulogne, was in no way less terrible, less seemingly hopeless than now. She was a prisoner then, just as she was now; in the power of men whose whole life and entire range of thought had for the past two years been devoted to the undoing and annihilation of the Scarlet Pimpernel. And there was a certain grim satisfaction for the pinioned, helpless woman in recalling the many instances where the daring adventurer had so completely outwitted his enemies, as well as in the memory of those days at Boulogne when the life of countless innocents was to be the price of her own.

===Chinese===

玛格丽特无助地、被捆着，却强逼自己的思绪流连到那个时候：那时，他的仇敌们把那杯羞辱与惊怖斟得满满，那杯酒本注定要经由她的手送到他面前，而他的机变与胆魄，还不等杯子递到她唇边，便已把它摔翻在地。说实在的，她当年在布洛涅的处境，丝毫不比此刻少了凶险，也不比此刻少了看似无望的绝境。她那时是囚徒，正同此刻一样；落在一伙人手里——两年来，这伙人的整副性命、全部的心思，都扑在搞垮猩红石榴红、将他斩尽杀绝上头。而这个被捆着、无助的女人，回想那大胆的冒险家一次次把仇敌们彻底斗败的种种往事，回想在布洛涅的那些日子——那时，无数无辜者的性命，要做她一人性命的抵偿——心底竟有一种冷峻的快意。



### II / II

===Original===

The embarkation took place somewhere on the coast around Birchington. When, at dead of night, the coach came to a halt, and the tang of sea air and salt spray reached Marguerite’s burning cheeks and parched lips, she tried with all her might to guess at her exact position. But that was impossible.

===Chinese===

登船的地方，在伯钦顿（Birchington）一带海岸的某处。万籁俱寂的深夜，马车停了下来，咸冽的海风夹着飞溅的盐沫，扑上玛格丽特滚烫的双颊和干焦的嘴唇。她竭尽全力，想猜出自己确切的方位。可那是办不到的。


===Original===

She was lifted out of the coach, and at once a shawl was thrown over her face, so that she could not see. It was more instinct than anything else that guided her perceptions. Even in the coach she had been vaguely conscious of the direction in which she had been travelling. All that part of the country was entirely familiar to her. So often she had driven down with Sir Percy, either to Dover or more often to some lonely part of the coast, where he took ship for unknown destinations, that in her mind she could, even blinded with tears and half-conscious as she was, trace in her mind the various turnings and side-roads along which she was being borne at unabating speed.

===Chinese===

她被人从马车里抬了出来，随即一条披巾兜头罩下，遮住了她的脸，叫她什么也看不见。为她引路的，与其说是别的什么，不如说是本能。即便在马车里，她对自己一路行进的方向，也始终存着蒙眬的知觉。那一带的乡野，她熟得不能再熟。她随珀西爵士（Sir Percy）驱车南下过多少回啊——不是去多佛尔（Dover），便是更多时候去海岸上某处僻静的所在，他从那里登船出海，驶向不知名的目的地——因此，纵然她泪眼迷离、神智半昏，心里仍能把载着她一路疾驰、片刻不停的那一道道拐弯、一条条岔路，一一描画出来。


===Original===

Birchington﻿—one of the favourite haunts of the smuggling fraternity, with its numberless caves and retreats dug by the sea in the chalk cliffs, as if for the express benefit of ne’er-do-wells﻿—seemed the natural objective of the miscreants who had her in their power. In fact, at one moment she was quite sure that the square tower of old Minister church flitted past her vision through the window of the coach, and that the horses immediately after that sprinted the hill between Minster and Acoll.

===Chinese===

伯钦顿——走私行帮最中意的落脚地之一，大海在白垩峭壁间掏出的那数不清的岩洞与藏身之所，仿佛专为那些不肖之徒备下——在擒住她的这伙恶徒看来，似乎正是顺理成章的去处。事实上，有一刹那她十分确信，古老的明斯特教堂（Minster）那方形的塔楼，曾掠过车窗、映过她的眼帘，随后马匹便撒开四蹄，冲上了明斯特与埃科尔（Acol）之间的那道山坡。


===Original===

Be that as it may, there was no doubt that the coach came to a halt at a desolate spot. The day which had begun in radiance and sunshine, had turned to an evening of squall and drizzle. A thin rain soon wetted Marguerite’s clothes and the shawl on her head through and through, greatly adding to her misery and discomfort. Though she saw nothing, she could trace every landmark of the calvary to the summit of which she was being borne like an insentient log.

===Chinese===

无论怎样，马车停在一处荒凉冷僻的所在，这一点是毫无疑问的了。这一日清晨还光华灿烂、艳阳满目，到入暮竟变作了狂风裹着斜雨的天气。蒙蒙细雨很快把玛格丽特的衣裳、连同她头上的披巾浸了个透湿，把她的凄苦与难受又添了几分。她虽然什么也看不见，可这条苦路（calvary）上的每一处地标，她都能一一辨明——她正像一段无知无觉的木头，被人抬着，抬向那苦路的顶巅。


===Original===

For a while she lay at the bottom of a small boat, aching in body as well as in mind, her eyes closed, her limbs cramped by the cords which owing to the damp were cutting into her flesh, faint with cold and want of food, wet to the skin yet with eyes and head and hands burning hot, and her ears filled with the dreary, monotonous sound of the oars creaking in the rowlocks and the boom of the water against the sides of the boat.

===Chinese===

有一阵子，她躺在一艘小船的船底，身子痛楚，心里更痛楚；双眼阖着；四肢叫绳索捆得抽紧麻木——绳子受了潮气，正勒进她的皮肉；她又冷又饿，虚弱得直欲晕去；浑身湿透，眼睛、脑袋和双手却烧得滚烫；耳朵里灌满了单调凄凉的声响：桨在桨架里吱呀，海水撞着船帮轰轰发闷。


===Original===

She was lifted out of the boat and carried, as she judged, by two men up a companion ladder, then down some steps and finally deposited on some hard boards; after which the wet shawl was removed from her face. She was in the dark. Only a tiny streak of light found its way through a chink somewhere close to the floor. A smell of tar and of stale food gave her a wretched sense of nausea. But she had by now reached a stage of physical and mental prostration wherein even acute bodily suffering counts as nothing, and is endurable because it is no longer felt.

===Chinese===

她被从船里抬出来，据她判断，是让两个男人抬着上了一道舷梯，又下了几级台阶，最后被放到几块硬木板上；随后，那条湿透的披巾从她脸上揭了下来。她身处黑暗之中。只有一线细细的微光，从贴近地板的什么缝隙里钻了进来。一股柏油味混着馊饭的气味，熏得她一阵阵恶心欲呕。可她此刻身心都已委顿到了一个地步——在那地步里，连剧烈的肉体苦痛也算不得什么；熬得住，只因早已感觉不到了。


===Original===

After a while the familiar motion, the well-known sound of a ship weighing anchor, gave another blow to her few lingering hopes. Every movement of the ship now bore her farther and farther from England and home, and rendered her position more utterly miserable and hopeless.

===Chinese===

过了片刻，那熟悉的摇荡，那船只起锚时再熟识不过的声响，又朝她残存无几的希望击了一记。船身每挪动一下，便把她载得离英格兰（England）和家远一分，也把她的处境变得更凄惨、更绝望一分。


===Original===

Far be it from me to suggest even for a moment that Marguerite Blakeney lost either spirit or courage during this terrible ordeal. But she was so completely helpless that instinct forced her to remain motionless and quiescent, and not to engage in a fight against overwhelming odds. In mid-Channel, surrounded by miscreants who had her in their power, she could obviously do nothing except safeguard what dignity she could by silence and seeming acquiescence.

===Chinese===

我万万无意暗示，玛格丽特·布莱克尼在这场可怕的磨难中丧过心气、失过勇气——哪怕一瞬也不曾。只是她实在全然无助，本能驱策着她一动不动、蛰伏隐忍，不去同那压倒一切的强敌较量。在海峡中央，四面围着她的是把她攥在掌中的恶徒，她显然什么也做不成，唯有以沉默和表面上的顺从，守住自己还能守住的那一点尊严。



### III / III

===Original===

She was taken ashore in the early dawn, at a spot not very far from Boulogne. Precautions were no longer taken against her possible calls for help; even the cords had been removed from her wrists and ankles as soon as she was lowered into the boat that brought her to shore. Cramped and stiff though she was, she disdained the help of an arm which was held out to her to enable her to step out of the boat.

===Chinese===

破晓时分，她被送上了岸，登岸的地点离布洛涅不远。他们不再防范她可能的呼救；她刚一被放进送她上岸的小船，腕上踝上的绳索便都解了去。她尽管又麻又僵，却不屑去扶那只伸过来搀她下船的胳膊。


===Original===

All the faces around her were unfamiliar. There were four or five men, surly and silent, who piloted her over the rocks and cliffs and then along the sands, to the little hamlet of Wimereux, which she knew well. The coast at this hour was still deserted; only at one time did the little party meet with a group of buxom young women, trudging along barefooted with their shrimping nets over their shoulders. They stared wide-eyed but otherwise indifferent, at the unfortunate woman in torn, damp clothes, and with golden hair all dishevelled, who was bravely striving not to fall whilst urged on by five rough fellows in ragged jerseys, tattered breeches, and bare-kneed.

===Chinese===

她四周全是陌生的面孔。四五个汉子，阴郁而沉默，领着她翻过礁石与崖壁，又沿着沙滩，一路走到维默罗（Wimereux）那座她熟识的小渔村。这个时辰，海岸上依然渺无人迹；只有一回，这一小队人遇上了一伙丰润健壮的年轻女子，赤着脚、肩上扛着捕虾网，一路跋涉而行。她们睁圆了眼睛，望着这个不幸的女人——衣衫破碎，湿漉漉贴在身上，一头金发纷乱披散，被五个身穿破旧针织衫、褴褛短裤、赤着膝头的粗汉催赶着，兀自竭力撑持、不肯跌倒——可她们也不过是望一望罢了，旁的一概无动于衷。


===Original===

Just for one moment﻿—a mere flash﻿—Marguerite at sight of these girls had the wild notion to run to them, implore their assistance in the name of their sweethearts, their husbands, their songs; to throw herself at their feet and beg them to help her, seeing that they were women and could not be without heart or pity. But it was a mere flash, the wild vagary of an overexcited brain, the drifting straw that mocks the drowning man. The next moment the girls had gone by, laughing and chattering. One of them intoned the “Ça ira!” and Marguerite, fortunately for her own dignity, was not seriously tempted to essay so futile, so senseless an appeal.

===Chinese===

只在那么一瞬——电光石火似的一瞬——玛格丽特一望见这些姑娘，心里便生出一个狂乱的念头：向她们奔过去，凭着她们的心上人、她们的丈夫、她们的歌，恳求她们搭救；扑倒在她们脚前，求她们帮一帮她——念在她们也是女人，总不至于没有心肝、没有怜悯。可那不过是一瞬，是一颗过度亢奋的头脑生出的狂想，是那根漂来漂去、偏要嘲弄溺水者的稻草。转瞬间，姑娘们已经说说笑笑地走了过去。其中一个哼起了《Ça ira!》（“就这样干！”）。幸而——为了她自己的体面——玛格丽特并没有当真受到蛊惑，去作那样徒劳、那样无谓的哀告。


===Original===

Later on, in a squalid little hovel on the outskirts of Wimereux, she was at last given some food which, though of the poorest and roughest description, was nevertheless welcome, for it revived her spirit and strengthened her courage, of which she had sore need.

===Chinese===

再往后，在维默罗村边一间污秽的小破屋里，她总算得了一些吃食。那东西虽然粗劣已极，却依然受欢迎，因为它让她精神一振、勇气一壮——而这份勇气，正是她万分需要的。


===Original===

The rest of the journey was uneventful. Within the first hour of making a fresh start, she had realised that she was being taken to Paris. A few words dropped casually by the men who had charge of her apprised her of the fact. Otherwise they were very reticent﻿—not altogether rough or unkind.

===Chinese===

余下的旅程再无波折。重新上路后头一个钟头里，她便明白了：自己正被押往巴黎（Paris）。看守她的汉子们不经意间漏出的只言片语，向她透露了这一事实。除此之外，他们都缄口不言——倒也并不怎么粗横，也不怎么刻薄。


===Original===

The coach in which she travelled during this stage of the journey was roomy and not uncomfortable, although the cushions were ragged and the leatherwork mildewed. Above all, she had the supreme comfort of privacy. She was alone in the coach, alone during the halts at wayside hostelries when she was allowed food and rest, alone throughout those two interminable nights when, with brief intervals whilst relays of horses were put into the shafts or the men took it in turns to get food or drink in some house unseen in the darkness, she vainly tried to get a snatch or two of sleep and a few moments of forgetfulness; alone throughout that next long day, whilst frequent summer showers sent heavy raindrops beating against the windowpanes of the coach, and familiar landmarks on the way to Paris flitted like threatening ghouls past her aching eyes.

===Chinese===

旅程这一段乘坐的马车，车厢宽敞，坐着也并不难受，虽说座垫已经破烂，皮件上也生了霉斑。最要紧的是，她享有一种至高的安逸：无人搅扰。她独自待在车厢里；在路边客栈歇脚、蒙准进食歇息的时候，独自一人；在那两个没完没了的漫漫长夜里，独自一人——其间只有短短的间歇，或换班的驿马套上车辕，或汉子们轮番摸黑到哪所瞧不见的屋子里去弄点吃喝——她徒然想抓得一两觉浅眠、偷得片刻的忘却；在随后那个漫长的白昼里，仍是独自一人——一阵阵夏日骤雨把沉甸甸的雨点打在车窗玻璃上，通往巴黎一路那些熟悉的地标，像一个个含着凶兆的食尸鬼，从她酸痛的双眼前一晃而过。


===Original===

Paris was reached at dawn of the third day. Seventy-two hours had crept along leaden-footed, since the moment when she had stepped into her own coach outside her beautiful home in Richmond, surrounded by her own servants, and with that traitor Moncrif by her side. Since then, what a load of sorrow, of anxiety, of physical and mental suffering had she borne! And yet, even that sorrow, even those sufferings and that anxiety, seemed as nothing beside the heartrending thoughts of her beloved, as yet ignorant of her terrible fate and of the schemes which those fiends who had so shamefully trapped her were even now concocting for the realisation of their vengeance against him.

===Chinese===

第三天拂晓，一行人抵达了巴黎。自从那一刻——她在里士满（Richmond）美丽宅邸的门外，被自家的仆人们簇拥着、身边伴着那个叛徒蒙克里夫，跨进自己马车的那一刻——七十二个钟头已经拖着铅铸的脚，一步一步爬了过去。打那以后，她担起了怎样一副重担啊——悲哀、忧惧、身心交迫的苦楚！然而，纵是那悲哀、纵是那些苦楚与忧惧，比起她对心上人肝肠寸断的悬想，也算不得什么了——他还一无所知，既不知她可怕的命运，也不知那伙无耻设局陷害她的恶魔，此刻正密谋着何等毒计，要借着她，把对他的报复做成真章。
