## XXVIII. In the Meanwhile / XXVIII. 与此同时

### I / I

===Original===

Chauvelin, who, despite his many failures, was still one of the most conspicuous﻿—since he was one of the most unscrupulous﻿—members of the Committee of Public Saftey, had not attended its sittings for some days. He had been too deeply absorbed in his own schemes to trouble about those of his colleagues. In truth, the coup which he was preparing was so stupendous, and if it succeeded his triumph would be so magnificent, that he could well afford to hold himself aloof. Those who were still inclined to scorn and to scoff at him today would be his most cringing sycophants on the morrow.

===Chinese===

肖夫兰（Chauvelin）尽管屡遭败绩，却依旧是公共安全委员会（Committee of Public Safety）里最惹眼的成员之一——因为他也是最无所顾忌的成员之一——只是已有好些天不曾到会了。他一门心思沉在自己的谋划里，深得再匀不出工夫去过问同僚们的图谋。说实在的，他酝酿中的这桩大事太过骇人，一旦成功，他的凯旋又将太过煊赫，尽可以稳稳当当超然局外。今日还存心贬抑他、讥诮他的人，一到明日，便会换上一副最卑躬屈膝的嘴脸，做他最谄媚的逢迎客。

===Original===

He knew well enough﻿—none better﻿—that during this time the political atmosphere in the Committees and the Clubs was nothing short of electrical. He felt, as everyone did, that something catastrophic was in the air, that death, more self-evident than ever before, lurked at every man’s elbow, and stalked round the corner of every street.

===Chinese===

他心里再明白不过——没有谁比他更明白——这些时日里，各委员会和各俱乐部的政治空气，简直如同充了电，一触即发。他同所有人一样感觉到：某种天崩地裂的大祸正在半空里酝酿；死神比以往任何时候都更显豁，伏在每个人的臂肘边，绕着每条街的拐角潜行游荡。

===Original===

Robespierre, the tyrant, the autocrat whose mere word swayed the multitude, remained silent and impenetrable, absent from every gathering. He only made brief appearances at the Convention, and there sat moody and self-absorbed. Everyone knew that this man, dictator in all but name, was meditating a Titanic attack upon his enemies. His veiled threats, uttered during his rare appearances at the speaker’s tribune, embraced even the most popular, the most prominent, amongst the representatives of the people. Everyone, in fact, who was likely to stand in his way when he was ready to snatch the supreme power. His intimates﻿—Couthon, St. Just, and the others﻿—openly accused of planning a dictatorship for their chief, hardly took the trouble to deny the impeachment, even whilst Tallien and his friends, feeling that the tyrant had already decreed their doom, went about like ghostly shadows, not daring to raise their voice in the Convention lest the first word they uttered brought down the sword of his lustful wrath upon their heads.

===Chinese===

罗伯斯庇尔（Robespierre）——那个暴君，那个只消片言只语便能搅动群氓的独夫——依旧缄默着，莫测高深，一切集会都不见他的踪影。他只在国民公会（the Convention）偶尔露一露面，便坐在那里，郁郁寡欢，兀自出神。人人都知道，这个人——除名分而外一应俱全的独裁者——正盘算着对他的仇敌来一场泰坦式的雷霆总击。他难得在讲坛上现身几回，吐出的威胁字字裹着面纱，锋芒所向，连人民代表里最得民望、最赫赫扬扬的那几位，也一并罩了进去。说穿了，凡在他伸手攫取至高权力之时可能碍他道的人，无一例外。他的亲信——库东（Couthon）、圣茹斯特（St. Just），还有其余几位——被人公开指斥正为首领图谋独裁，他们却连矢口否认这桩弹劾的工夫都懒得费。而另一头，塔利安（Tallien）和他的朋友们，只觉暴君早已判定了他们的死劫，一个个像鬼影似的游荡，在国民公会里连嗓音也不敢抬高，唯恐出口的头一个字，就招得他那柄恣肆狂怒之剑当头劈落。

===Original===

The Committee of Public Safety﻿—now renamed the Revolutionary Committee﻿—strove on the other hand by a recrudescence of cruelty to ingratiate itself with the potential dictator and to pose before the people as alone pure and incorruptible, blind in justice, inexorable where the safety of the Republic was concerned. Thus an abominable emulation of vengeance and of persecution went on between the Committee and Robespierre’s party, wherein neither side could afford to give in, for fear of being accused of apathy and of moderation.

===Chinese===

公共安全委员会——如今已改名革命委员会（Revolutionary Committee）——则反其道而行之，靠变本加厉的残虐，一面去讨那位潜在独裁者的欢心，一面向民众摆出一副唯独自家纯洁、唯独自家廉正的姿态：执法蒙眼，不讲情面；凡关共和国的安危，绝无宽贷。于是，委员会同罗伯斯庇尔一党之间，展开了一场可憎的竞赛，比着谁报复得狠、迫害得凶；双方谁都退让不得——一退让，便要担上冷漠与温和的罪名。

===Original===

Chauvelin, for the most part, had kept out of the turmoil. He felt that in his hands lay the destiny of either party. His one thought was of the Scarlet Pimpernel and of his imminent capture, knowing that, with the most inveterate opponent of revolutionary excesses in his hands, he would within an hour be in a position to link his triumph with one or the other of the parties﻿—either with Robespierre and his herd of butchers, or with Tallien and the Moderates.

===Chinese===

至于肖夫兰，大体上一直置身于这场纷乱之外。他自觉两党沉浮的命运，全系在自己掌中。他一心念兹的，唯有猩红石榴红（the Scarlet Pimpernel），唯有那桩指日可待的擒获。他心里明白：只消把革命暴行最顽固的死敌攥进手里，不出一个钟头，他便能把自家那场凯旋，随意系上这一党或那一党的战车——或是罗伯斯庇尔同他那一群屠夫，或是塔利安同温和派（the Moderates）。

===Original===

He was the mysterious and invisible deus ex machina, who anon, when it suited his purpose, would reveal himself in his full glory as the man who had tracked down and brought to the guillotine the most dangerous enemy of the revolutionary government. And, so easily is a multitude swayed, that that one fact would bring him popularity transcending that of every other man in France. He, Chauvelin, the despised, the derided, whose name had become synonymous with Failure, would then with a word sweep those aside who had mocked him, hurl his enemies from their pedestals, and name at will the rulers of France. All within four days!

And of these, two had gone by.

===Chinese===

他就是那位神秘莫测、无形无影的解围之神（deus ex machina），早晚时机合宜、于己有利，便会以全副荣光现出真身，让世人知道：正是此人，追查出革命政府最危险的敌人，把他送上了断头台。而群氓的心思原是最容易摆布的，单凭这一桩功业，他名望之隆，便足以压倒法兰西（France）任何一人。他，肖夫兰，这个遭人鄙夷、受人奚落、名姓早已同“失败”二字合为一体的家伙，届时只消片言只语，便能叫讥笑过他的人尽数滚开，把仇敌从高座上掀翻在地，随自己的心意点定法兰西的统治者。这一切，全在四天之内！

而这四天，已经有两天过去了。

### II / II

===Original===

These days in mid-July had been more than usually sultry. It seemed almost as if Nature had linked herself with the passions of men, and hand in hand with Vengeance, Lust and Cruelty, had rendered the air hot and heavy with the presage of oncoming storm.

For Marguerite Blakeney these days had gone by like a nightmare. Cut off from all knowledge of the outside world, without news from her husband for the past forty-eight hours, she was enduring mental agony such as would have broken a weaker or less trusting spirit.

===Chinese===

七月中旬的这些日子，闷热得反乎寻常。仿佛造化也同人间的欲念结成了亲，同复仇、淫欲与残虐手挽着手，把空气蒸得又灼又沉，满满蓄着风暴将临的先兆。

对玛格丽特·布莱克尼（Marguerite Blakeney）来说，这些日子像一场梦魇，一寸一寸碾了过去。她与外间隔绝了一切音讯，丈夫的消息也已断绝了四十八个小时；她忍受着的这场精神酷刑——换一个孱弱些、少些信托的灵魂，早被它磨折碎了。

===Original===

Two days ago she had received a message, a few lines hastily scribbled by an unknown hand, and brought to her by the old woman who waited upon her.

“I have seen him,” the message said. “He is well and full of hope. I pray God for your deliverance and his, but help can only come by a miracle.”

The message was written in a feminine hand, with no clue as to the writer.

Since then, nothing.

===Chinese===

两天前，她收到过一封短信——寥寥数行，出自一只不知名的手，仓促涂就，由伺候她的那位老妇人捎到她跟前。

“我见过他了，”信上说，“他安好，而且满怀希望。我祈求上帝解救您，也解救他；只是援手非有奇迹，断然不来。”

信上是一手女人的字迹，写信人是谁，不曾留下半点线索。

自那以后，便杳无音耗。

===Original===

Marguerite had not seen Chauvelin again, for which indeed she thanked Heaven on her knees. But every day at a given hour she was conscious of his presence outside her door. She heard his voice in the vestibule: there would be a word or two of command, the grounding of arms, then some whispered talking; and presently Chauvelin’s stealthy footstep would slink up to her door. And Marguerite would remain still as a mouse that scents the presence of a cat, holding her breath, life almost at a standstill in this agony of expectation.

===Chinese===

玛格丽特再没有见到过肖夫兰——为这个，她实在跪下来谢过了上苍。可是每天到了一定的时辰，她便能觉察出他守在自己门外。门厅（vestibule）里他的嗓音，她听得见：先是一两句吩咐，枪托顿地一声，接着是几阵窃窃私语；不多时，肖夫兰那鬼鬼祟祟的脚步声，便一路蹑到她的门边。玛格丽特便纹丝不动，活像一只嗅出猫就在近旁的老鼠，屏住呼吸，在这场等待的酷刑里，生命几乎凝住不动了。

===Original===

The remainder of the day time hung with a leaden weight on her hands. She was given no books to read, not a needle wherewith to busy herself. She had no one to speak to save old Mother Théot, who waited on her and brought her her meals, nearly always in silence, and with a dour mien which checked any attempt at conversation.

For company, the unfortunate woman had nothing but her own thoughts, her fears which grew in intensity, and her hopes which were rapidly dwindling, as hour followed hour and day succeeded day in dreary monotony. No sound around her save the incessant tramp, tramp of sentries at her door, and every two hours the changing of the guard in the vestibule outside; then the whispered colloquies, the soldiers playing at cards or throwing dice, the bibulous songs, the ribald laughter, the obscene words flung aloud like bits of filthy rag; the life, in fact, that revolved around her jailers and seemed at a standstill within her prison walls.

===Chinese===

一天里剩下的光阴，铅砣似的坠在她手上。没有人给她书读，也没有一根针线供她拈来打发。能同她说话的，唯有老泰奥妈妈（Mother Théot）——伺候她、给她送一日三餐的，都是这位老太太；她几乎总是默默无言，一脸阴沉森然的神气，把任何攀谈的念头都堵死在半路。

这不幸的女人作伴的，只有自己的万千心事，只有一阵烈过一阵的恐惧，和一寸寸枯萎下去的希望；一个钟头接着一个钟头，一天挨着一天，在沉闷的单调里熬磨。四下里没有别的声响，只有门前哨兵无休无止的踏步、踏步，每隔两个钟头，外面门厅里换一班岗；再有便是窃窃的絮语，士兵们打牌、掷骰子，酗酒走调的歌子，下流的哄笑，还有那些高声抛来掷去的淫词秽语，像一片片肮脏的破布。总而言之，那是绕着狱卒们打转的生活；而狱墙之内她这一方，一切都仿佛停摆了。

===Original===

In the late afternoons the air would become insufferably hot, and Marguerite would throw open the window and sit beside it, her gaze fixed upon the horizon far away, her hands lying limp and moist upon her lap.

Then she would fall to dreaming. Her thoughts, swifter than flight of swallows, would cross the sea and go roaming across country to her stately home in Richmond, where at this house the moist, cool air was fragrant with the scent of late roses and of lime blossom, and the murmur of the river lapping the mossy bank whispered of love and of peace. In her dream she would see the tall figure of her beloved coming toward her. The sunset was playing upon his smooth hair and upon his strong, slender hands, always outstretched toward the innocent and the weak. She would hear his dear voice calling her name, feel his arms around her, and her senses swooning in the ecstasy of that perfect moment which comes just before a kiss.

===Chinese===

午后将尽，暑气总是蒸腾得叫人忍受不得；玛格丽特便推开了窗，坐在窗边，目光定定地望着远方的天际线，双手软软地、沁着微汗，搁在膝上。

于是她便坠入梦想。她的思绪，比燕群的疾飞还要迅捷，飞越大海，一路漫游到里士满（Richmond）她那座气派的家宅；此时此刻，那边湿润清凉的空气里，正浮动着晚玫瑰和菩提花的芬芳，河水轻舔着生苔的堤岸，潺潺的低语诉说着爱，诉说着安宁。梦里，她望见心上人颀长的身影向她走来。落日的余晖在他光滑的头发上流连，也在他那双坚强而修长的手上流连——那双手，永远向着无辜的人与弱小的人伸展。她听得见他亲爱的嗓音唤着她的名字，觉出他的手臂环拥着她，她的知觉在销魂的迷醉里悠悠晕去——那是吻落下来之前，最完满不过的一刻。

===Original===

She would dream﻿ ﻿… only to wake up the next moment to hear the church clock of St. Antoine striking seven, and a minute or two later that ominous shuffling footstep outside her door, those whisperings, the grounding of arms, a burst of cruel laughter, which brought her from the dizzy heights of illusive happiness back to the hideous reality of her own horrible position, and of the deadly danger which lay in wait for her beloved.

===Chinese===

她只顾这样梦下去……只在下一刻猛然惊醒，听见圣安托万教堂的钟敲了七下；一两分钟后，门外那阵不祥的、窸窣拖沓的脚步声来了，那窃窃私语来了，枪托顿地来了，还有一声残忍的哄笑——这一声笑，把她从虚幻幸福的眩晕高处，一把掼回狰狞的现实里：她自身处境的可怕，还有那正候在她心上人前路上的杀身之祸。
