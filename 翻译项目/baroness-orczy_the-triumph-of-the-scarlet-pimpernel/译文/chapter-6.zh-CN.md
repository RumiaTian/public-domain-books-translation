## VI. One Crowded Hour of Glorious Life / VI. 光辉人生中浓烈充实的一小时

### I / I

===Original===
And all the while, the people were shouting:

“*Le violà!*”

“Robespierre!”

===Chinese===
这时候，人群一直在喊：

“瞧，他来啦！（Le violà!）”

“罗伯斯庇尔！”

===Original===
The Fraternal Supper was interrupted. Men and women pushed and jostled and screamed, the while a small, spare figure in dark cloth coat and immaculate breeches, with smooth brown hair and pale-ascetic face, stood for a moment under the lintel of a gaping porte-cochère. He had two friends with him; handsome, enthusiastic St. Just, the right hand and the spur of the bloodthirsty monster, own kinsman to Armand St. Just the renegade, whose sister was married to a rich English millor; and Couthon, delicate, half-paralysed, wheeled about in a chair, with one foot in the grave, whose devotion to the tyrant was partly made up of ambition, and wholly of genuine admiration.

===Chinese===
兄弟晚餐会被打断了。男男女女推推搡搡、尖声喊叫，这时，一个清瘦矮小的身影在一扇洞开的车马大门（porte-cochère）的门楣下站了片刻。他身穿深色呢外套，套裤纤尘不染，棕色头发梳得平平整整，一张苦修者般苍白的面孔。他身边带着两个朋友：一个是俊美而狂热的圣茹斯特（St. Just）——那嗜血魔王的右手、策马的马刺，又是变节者阿尔芒·圣茹斯特（Armand St. Just）的嫡亲族人，而阿尔芒的姐姐，嫁给了一位富有的英国爵爷；另一个是库东（Couthon），孱弱多病、半身不遂，坐在椅中由人推着走，一只脚已经踏进了坟墓——他对暴君的赤诚，部分出于野心，而整个儿出于由衷的倾慕。

===Original===
At the uproarious cheering which greeted his appearance, Robespierre advanced into the open, whilst a sudden swift light of triumph darted from his narrow, pale eyes.

“And you still hesitate!” St. Just whispered excitedly in his ear. “Why, you hold the people absolutely in the hollow of your hand!”

“Have patience, friend!” Couthon remonstrated quietly. “Robespierre’s hour is about to strike. To hasten it now, might be courting disaster.”

===Chinese===
他在震耳欲聋的欢呼中出现，朝空地当中走去，一对细窄的苍白眼睛里，蓦地闪过一道迅疾的得意光芒。

“可您还在犹豫！”圣茹斯特在他耳边兴奋地低语。“嗳，人民分明已经稳稳攥在您的掌心里了！”

“要有耐心，朋友！”库东平静地劝道。“罗伯斯庇尔的时辰就要敲响了。此刻强求提早，只怕是自招祸殃。”

===Original===
Robespierre himself would, in the meanwhile, have been in serious danger through the exuberant welcome of his admirers. Their thoughtless crowding around his person would easily have given some lurking enemy or hotheaded, would-be martyr the chance of wielding an assassin’s knife with success, but for the presence amongst the crowd of his “*tappe-durs*”﻿—hit-hards﻿—a magnificent bodyguard composed of picked giants from the mining districts of Eastern France, who rallied around the great man, and with their weighted sticks kept the enthusiastic crowd at bay.

===Chinese===
与此同时，崇拜者们的狂热欢迎，本会把罗伯斯庇尔本人置于严重的危险之中。他们只顾不假思索地簇拥在他身边，本容易让某个潜伏的仇敌，或者哪个头脑发热、一心想当殉道烈士的家伙，得了挥匕行刺、一击得手的机会；幸而人群中有他的那些“打手”（tappe-durs）——一支威风凛凛的卫队，由法兰西东部矿区精选出的巨汉组成。他们聚拢在这位伟人四周，用坠了分量的棍棒，将狂热的人群逼开在圈外。

===Original===
He walked a few steps down the street, keeping close to the houses on his left; his two friends, St. Just and Couthon in his carrying chair, were immediately behind him, and between these three and the mob, the *tappe-durs*, striding two abreast, formed a solid phalanx.

Then, all of a sudden, the great man came to a halt, faced the crowd, and with an impressive gesture imposed silence and attention. His bodyguard cleared a space for him and he stood in the midst of them, with the light of a resin torch striking full upon his spare figure and bringing into bold relief that thin face so full of sinister expression, the cruel mouth and the coldly glittering eyes. He was looking straight across the table, on which the debris of Fraternal Suppers lay in unsavoury confusion.

===Chinese===
他沿街走了几步，紧靠左手边的房屋；他的两位朋友——圣茹斯特和坐在代步椅里的库东——紧随其后；在这三人与乱民之间，“打手”们两人一排、迈着大步，结成一道铁壁似的方阵。

忽然，这位伟人停下脚步，转过身来面向人群，以一个威严的手势压下喧哗，要众人肃静聆听。卫队为他清出一块空地，他立在卫队中央，一支松脂火把的光正正照在他清瘦的身形上，把那张阴鸷毕露的瘦脸、那张冷酷的嘴和那双寒光凛凛的眼睛，映衬得赫然分明。他的目光笔直地越过那张桌子——桌上，兄弟晚餐会的残羹剩肴，正摊成一片不堪入目的狼藉。

===Original===
On the other side of the table, Mme. de Serval with her three children sat, or rather crouched, closely huddled against one another. Joséphine was clinging to her mother, Jacques to Régine. Gone was the eagerness out of their attitude now, gone the enthusiasm that had reviled the bloodthirsty tyrant in the teeth of a threatening crowd. It seemed as if, with that terrific blow dealt by a giant hand to Bertrand who was their leader in this mad adventure, the awesome fear of death had descended upon their souls. The two young faces as well as that of Mme. de Serval appeared distorted and haggard, whilst Régine’s eyes, dilated with terror, strove to meet Robespierre’s steady gaze, which was charged with sinister mockery.

===Chinese===
桌子另一边，德·塞尔瓦尔夫人（Mme. de Serval）带着她的三个孩子坐着——不如说是蜷缩着，紧紧偎作一团。约瑟芬（Joséphine）攀着母亲，雅克（Jacques）攀着雷吉娜（Régine）。此刻他们身上那股昂扬的劲头没了，那种当着威胁的人群痛斥嗜血暴君的豪情也没了。仿佛随着一只巨掌狠狠一击、放倒了他们这场疯狂冒险的首领贝特朗（Bertrand），对死亡的森然恐惧，便已降临到他们的灵魂之上。两张年轻的脸，连同德·塞尔瓦尔夫人那张脸，都显得扭曲而憔悴；雷吉娜那双吓得睁大的眼睛，则竭力迎住罗伯斯庇尔凝定不动的注视——那目光里满含阴毒的嘲弄。

===Original===
And for one short interval of time the crowd was silent; and the everlasting stars looked down from above on the doings of men. To these trembling, terrified young creatures, suddenly possessed with youth’s passionate desire to live, with a passionate horror of death, these few seconds of tense silence must have seemed like an eternity of suffering. Then Robespierre’s thin face lighted up in a portentous smile﻿—a smile that caused those pale cheeks yonder to take on a still more ashen hue.

“And where is our eloquent orator of a while ago?” the great man asked quietly. “I heard my name, for I sat at my window looking with joy on the fraternisation of the people of France. I caught sight of the speaker, and came down to hear more clearly what he had to say. But where is he?”

===Chinese===
有短短一阵子，人群鸦雀无声；永恒的星辰在天上俯瞰着人间的作为。对这几个战战兢兢、魂飞魄散的年轻人来说——青春那股要活下去的炽烈欲望、那股对死亡的炽烈恐惧，此刻突然攫住了他们——这紧张的沉默中的几秒钟，必定像无穷无尽的受难。随后，罗伯斯庇尔那张瘦脸泛起一丝不祥的笑——这一笑，使那边那几张苍白的面颊愈发罩上一层死灰。

“方才我们那位能言善辩的演说家哪儿去了？”这位伟人不动声色地问道。“我听见了自己的名字，因为我正坐在窗前，欣然望着法兰西人民的友爱同乐。我瞧见了那位发言的人，便下来听他要说什么，听个真切。可他在哪儿？”

===Original===
His pale eyes wandered slowly along the crowd; and such was the power exercised by the extraordinary man, so great the terror that he inspired, that everyone there﻿—men, women and children, workers and vagabonds﻿—turned their eyes away, dared not meet his glance lest in it they read an accusation or a threat.

Indeed, no one dared to speak. The young rhetorician had disappeared, and everyone trembled lest they should be implicated in his escape. He had evidently got away under cover of the confusion and the noise. But his companions were still there﻿—four of them; the woman and the boy and the two girls, crouching like frightened beasts before the obvious fury, the certain vengeance of the people. The murmurs were ominous. “Death! Guillotine! Traitors!” were words easily distinguishable in the confused babbling of the sullen crowd.

===Chinese===
他那双苍白的眼睛缓缓扫过人群；这个非凡人物所施的威权如此之大，他身上激发的恐怖如此之深，以致在场每一个人——男女老少、工人和游民——都把眼睛挪开，不敢与他的目光相接，唯恐从那里面读出控诉或者威胁。

的确，谁也不敢作声。那年轻的雄辩家已经无影无踪，人人都发着抖，唯恐自己被牵连进他的逃脱里。他显然是趁着混乱与喧闹脱身的。可他的同伴们还在那儿——一共四个：那妇人、那少年和两个姑娘，像吓坏了的野兽一般蜷缩着，面对人群明摆着的狂怒、笃定要来的报复。低低的嗡嗡声凶险不祥。在阴沉的人群纷乱的嘈杂里，“死！断头台！卖国贼！”这样的字眼清晰可辨。

===Original===
Robespierre’s cruel, appraising glance rested on those four pathetic forms, so helpless, so desperate, so terrified.

“Citizens,” he said coldly, “did you not hear me ask where your eloquent companion is at this moment?”

Régine alone knew that he lay like a log under the table, close to her feet. She had seen him fall, struck by that awful blow from a brutal fist; but at the ominous query she instinctively pressed her trembling lips close together, whilst Joséphine and Jacques clung to her with the strength of despair.

===Chinese===
罗伯斯庇尔那冷酷的、估量般的目光，落在这四个可怜的人影上——那样无助，那样绝望，那样惊惶。

“公民们，”他冷冷地说，“难道没听见我问，你们那位能言善辩的伙伴此刻在哪儿？”

只有雷吉娜一个人知道，他正像段木头似的躺在桌子底下，紧挨着她的脚边。她亲眼看见他倒下，被那一记残暴的重拳打中；可听到这句凶险的问话，她本能地把自己颤抖的双唇紧紧抿住，而约瑟芬和雅克则凭着绝望中的气力，死死攀住她不放。

===Original===
“Do not parley with the rabble, citizen,” St. Just whispered eagerly. “This is a grand moment for you. Let the people of their own accord condemn those who dared to defame you.”

And even Couthon, the prudent, added sententiously:

“Such an opportunity may never occur again.”

===Chinese===
“别跟这帮贱民多费口舌，公民，”圣茹斯特急切地低声道。“这是您的辉煌时刻。让人民自发地惩处那些胆敢诽谤您的人吧。”

连素来谨慎的库东，也拿腔拿调地补了一句：

“这样的机会，往后再也不会有了。”

===Original===
The people, in truth, were over-ready to take vengeance into their own hands.

“*Á la lanterne, les aristos!*”

Gaunt, bedraggled forms leaned across the table, shook begrimed fists in the direction of the four crouching figures. With the blind instinct of trapped beasts, they retreated into the shadows step by step, as those threatening fists appeared to draw closer, clutching at the nearest table and dragging it with them, in an altogether futile attempt at a barricade.

===Chinese===
说实在的，人民早就急不可耐，要亲手报仇。

“把贵族吊上灯杆！（Á la lanterne, les aristos!）”

形容枯槁、蓬头垢面的人影，隔着桌子探过身来，朝那四个蜷缩的身影挥舞污黑的拳头。那四个人凭着落网野兽般的盲目本能，一步步退进暗影里，眼见那些威吓的拳头步步逼近，便抓住离得最近的一张桌子，拖着它一起退——全然徒劳地想拿它当一道街垒。

===Original===
“Holy Mother of God, protect us!” murmured Mme. de Serval from time to time.

Behind them there was nothing but the rows of houses, no means of escape even if their trembling knees had not refused them service; whilst vaguely, through their terror, they were conscious of the proximity of that awful asthmatic creature with the wheezy cough and the hideous, toothless mouth. At times he seemed so close that they shut their eyes, almost feeling his grimy hands around their throat, his huge, hairy arms dragging them down to death.

===Chinese===
“上帝的圣母啊，保佑我们！”德·塞尔瓦尔夫人一遍又一遍地低声祷告。

他们身后只有一排排房屋，就算那两条打颤的腿还肯听使唤，也无路可逃；而在恐惧的迷雾中，他们还隐隐意识到，那个患哮喘的可怖家伙就在近旁——呼哧呼哧地咳着，咧着一张没有牙齿的丑嘴。有时他仿佛近在咫尺，吓得他们闭上眼睛，几乎当真觉出那双污黑的手已经掐上了自己的喉咙，那两条粗大多毛的胳膊正把他们往死里拖。

===Original===
It all happened in the space of a very few minutes, far fewer even than it would take completely to visualise the picture. Robespierre, like an avenging wraith, theatrical yet impassive, standing in the light of a huge resin torch, which threw alternate lights and shadows, grotesque and weird, upon his meagre figure, now elongating the thin, straight nose, now widening the narrow mouth, misshaping the figure till it appeared like some fantastic ghoul-form from the nether world. Behind him, his two friends were lost in the gloom, as were now Mme. de Serval and her children. They were ensconced against a heavy porte-cochère, a rickety table alone standing between them and the mob, who were ready to drag them to the nearest lantern and immolate them before the eyes of their outraged idol.

===Chinese===
这一切前后不过寥寥几分钟，比把这幅图景在心里完整想上一遍还要少。罗伯斯庇尔像个复仇的幽灵，做戏似的，却又漠然无动，立在巨大松脂火把的光里；火光把明明灭灭、光怪陆离的影子投在他单薄的身形上，一时拉长那又细又直的鼻梁，一时撑宽那窄窄的嘴，把整个人形扭曲得仿佛冥府里来的什么离奇食尸鬼。他身后，他的两位朋友已隐入黑暗，德·塞尔瓦尔夫人和她的孩子们此刻也一样。他们背抵着一扇厚重的车马大门，与暴民之间只隔一张摇摇欲坠的桌子；那群暴民已准备把他们拖到最近的灯杆下，在他们被冒犯的偶像眼前，把他们当祭品宰掉。

===Original===
“Leave the traitors alone!” Robespierre commanded. “Justice will deal with them as they deserve.”

“*Á la lanterne!*” the people﻿—more especially the women﻿—demanded insistently.

Robespierre turned to one of his “*tappe-durs*.”

“Take the aristos to the nearest Commissariat,” he said. “I’ll have no bloodshed to mar our Fraternal Supper.”

===Chinese===
“别去动那些叛徒！”罗伯斯庇尔命令道。“正义自会给他们应得的处置。”

“吊上灯杆！（Á la lanterne!）”人们——尤其是女人们——执拗地叫嚷着。

罗伯斯庇尔转向他手下的一名“打手”。

“把这几个贵族押到最近的警务分署（Commissariat）去，”他说。“我不要见血，坏了我们的兄弟晚餐会。”

===Original===
“The Commissariat, forsooth!” a raucous voice positively bellowed. “Who is going to stand between us and our vengeance? Robespierre has been outraged by this rabble. Let them perish in sight of all!”

===Chinese===
“警务分署？亏你说得出口！”一个嘶哑的嗓门简直是吼出来的。“谁来拦着我们报仇？这帮贱民冒犯了罗伯斯庇尔。让他们死在众人眼前！”

===Original===
How it all happened after that, none who were there could in truth have told you. The darkness, the flickering lights, the glow of the braziers, which made the inky blackness around more pronounced, made everything indistinguishable to ordinary human sight. Certain it is that citizen Rateau﻿—who had constituted himself the spokesman of the mob﻿—was at one time seen towering behind the four unfortunates, with his huge arms stretched out, his head thrown back, his mouth wide open, screaming abuse and vituperation, demanding the people’s right to take the law into its own sovereign hands.

At that moment the light of the nearest resin torch threw his hulking person into bold relief against a heavy porte-cochère which was immediately behind him. The mob acclaimed him, cheered him to the echoes, agreed with him that summary justice in such a case was lone satisfying. The next instant a puff of wind blew the flame of the torch in a contrary direction, and darkness suddenly enveloped the ranting colossus and the cowering prey all ready to his hand.

===Chinese===
这之后事情究竟是怎生经过的，在场的人实在谁也说不清。黑暗、摇曳的光、火盆的红焰——红焰把四周墨似的黑衬得愈发浓重——使寻常人的眼睛什么也分辨不清。可以确定的是：公民拉托（Rateau）——他自封为暴民的代言人——曾一度被人瞥见，高塔般耸在那四个倒霉人身后，巨大的双臂张开，头向后仰，嘴巴大张，嘶声叫骂不休，声言人民有权以主权者的身份，亲手执掌法律。

===Original===
“Rateau!” shouted someone.

“Hey, there! citizen Rateau! Where art thou?” came soon from every side.

No answer came from the spot where Rateau had last been seen, and it seemed as if just then a strong current of air had slammed a heavy door to somewhere in the gloom. Citizen Rateau had disappeared, and the four traitors along with him.

It took a few seconds of valuable time ere the mob suspected that it was being robbed of its prey. Then a huge upheaval occurred, a motion of the human mass densely packed in the Rue St. Honoré, that was not unlike the rush of water through a narrow gorge.

“Rateau!” People were yelling the name from end to end of the street.

===Chinese===
“拉托！”有人喊道。

“喂，喂！拉托公民！你在哪儿呀？”转眼间，四面八方都响起了这声呼喊。

拉托最后露面的地方没有半点回应，而黑暗深处，仿佛恰在那时有一股强风，把哪扇沉重的门砰地关上了。拉托公民不见了，那四个叛徒也随他一同消失。

过了宝贵的几秒钟，暴民才疑心到手的猎物被人劫走了。随即掀起一场大骚动——挤挤挨挨塞满圣奥诺雷街（Rue St. Honoré）的人潮猛地一涌，很像洪水灌过狭窄的峡谷。

“拉托！”人们从街头到街尾，扯着嗓子喊这个名字。

### II / II

===Original===
Superstition, which was rampant in these days of carnage and of crime, had possession of many a craven soul. Rateau had vanished. It seemed as if the Evil One, whose name had been so freely invoked during the course of the Fraternal Supper, had in very truth spirited Rateau away.

On the top of the tumult came a silence as complete as that of a graveyard at midnight. The “*tappe-durs*,” who at their chief’s command had been forging their way through the crowd, in order to reach the traitors, ceased their hoarse calls of “Make way there, in the name of the Convention!” whilst St. Just, who still stood close to his friend, literally saw the cry stifled on Robespierre’s lips.

===Chinese===
在这杀戮与罪孽的年月里甚嚣尘上的迷信，攫住了不知多少怯懦的灵魂。拉托消失了。那个在兄弟晚餐会席间被人们随口咒来骂去的恶魔，仿佛当真把拉托摄走了。

大骚动过后，继之而来的是一片死寂，静得如同午夜的坟场。那些“打手”奉首领之命，正在人群中硬开一条路、要去抓那几个叛徒，这时也住了口，不再嘶声喊“以国民公会的名义，让开！”；而仍旧站在朋友身边的圣茹斯特，简直亲眼看见那声呼叱，活生生憋死在了罗伯斯庇尔的唇边。

===Original===
Robespierre himself had not altogether realised what had happened. In his innermost heart he had already yielded to his friends’ suggestion, and was willing to let mob-law run its course. As St. Just had said: what a triumph for himself if his detractors were lynched by the mob! When Rateau towered above the four unfortunates, hurling vituperation above their heads, the tyrant smiled, well satisfied; and when the giant thus incontinently vanished, Robespierre for a moment or two remained complacent and content.

===Chinese===
