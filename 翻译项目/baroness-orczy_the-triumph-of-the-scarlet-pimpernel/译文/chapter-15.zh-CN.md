## XV. The Nest / XV. 爱巢

### I / I

===Original===

No one, save a very few intimates, knew of the little nest wherein Sir Percy Blakeney and his lady hid their happiness on those occasions when the indefatigable Scarlet Pimpernel was only able to spend a few hours in England, and when a journey to their beautiful home in Richmond could not be thought of. The house﻿—it was only a cottage, timbered and creeper-clad﻿—lay about a mile and a half outside Dover, off the main road, perched up high on rising ground over a narrow lane. It had a small garden round it, which in May was ablaze with daffodils and bluebells, and in June with roses. Two faithful servants, a man and his wife, looked after the place, kept the nest cosy and warm whenever her ladyship wearied of fashion, or else, actually expecting Sir Percy, would come down from London for a day or two in order to dream of that elusive and transient happiness for which her soul hungered, even while her indomitable spirit accepted the inevitable.

===Chinese===

除了极少数心腹，没有人知道这座小小的爱巢。每逢那不知疲倦的猩红石榴红（Scarlet Pimpernel）只能在英国消磨几个钟头、根本谈不上赶回里士满（Richmond）美丽宅邸的时节，珀西·布莱克尼爵士（Sir Percy Blakeney）和他的夫人便把他们的幸福藏在这巢里。这座宅子——其实只是一栋小屋，木骨墙上爬满藤蔓——坐落在多佛尔（Dover）城外约一英里半的地方，离开大路，高踞在一条窄巷上方隆起的坡地上。屋子四周围着一座小花园，五月里水仙与风铃草开得一片绚烂，六月里又是满园玫瑰。一对忠仆——一个男仆和他的妻子——照管着这地方：每当夫人厌倦了上流社会的浮华，或是果真盼着珀西爵士要来，从伦敦（London）下来住上一两天，好去梦她灵魂所渴慕的那份缥缈而易逝的幸福——纵使她那不屈的精神早已听天由命——两人总把爱巢收拾得温暖舒适。

===Original===

A few days ago the weekly courier from France had brought her a line from Sir Percy, together with the promise that she should rest in his arms on the 1st of May. And Marguerite had come down to the creeper-covered cottage knowing that, despite obstacles which might prove insuperable to others, Percy would keep his world.

She had stolen out at dawn to wait for him on the pier; and sure enough, as soon as the May-day sun, which had risen today in his glory as if to crown her brief happiness with warmth and radience, had dissipated the morning mist, her yearning eyes had spied the smart white gig which had put off from the *Daydream* leaving the graceful ship to await the turn of the tide before putting into port.

===Chinese===

几天前，每周一班从法兰西来的信使给她捎来珀西爵士的几行字，还捎来一句许诺：5月1日，她准能偎进他的怀抱。于是玛格丽特（Marguerite）来到这座爬满藤蔓的小屋；她知道，纵然那些阻碍在旁人看来或许万难逾越，珀西也必定言而有信。

她天没亮便悄悄溜出门，到码头上去等他；果然，五一节的太阳今天升得光彩夺目，仿佛要用温暖与光辉为她这短暂的幸福加冕；晨雾刚被它驱散，她那双望眼欲穿的眼睛便望见了从“白日梦”号（Daydream）上放下的一艘漂亮白色小艇，那艘秀美的船只得候着转潮，才能进港。

===Original===

Since then, every moment of the day had been one of rapture. The first sight of her husband in his huge caped coat, which seemed to add further inches to his great height, his call of triumph when he saw her, his arms outstretched, there, far away in the small boat, with a gesture of such infinite longing that for a second or two tears obscured Marguerite’s vision. Then the drawing up of the boat against the landing-stage; Percy’s spring ashore; his voice, his look; the strength of his arms; the ardour of his embrace. Rapture, in truth, to which the thought of its brief duration alone lent a touch of bitterness.

But of parting again Marguerite would not think﻿—not today, while the birds were singing a deafening paean of joy; not while the scent of growing grass, of moits, travailing earth, was in her nostrils; not while the sap was in the trees, and the gummy crimson buds of the chestnuts were bursting into leaf. Not while she wandered up the narrow lane between hedges of blackthorn in bloom, with Percy’s arm around her, his loved voice in her ear, his merry laughter echoing through the sweet morning air.

===Chinese===

打那以后，这一天的每一刻都令人心醉神迷。头一眼望见丈夫裹在那件带披肩的硕大外套里——那外套仿佛让他本就魁伟的身量又平添了几寸——他望见她时那一声得意的呼喊，他远远地在那条小艇里张开双臂，那姿势怀着无穷无尽的渴念，教玛格丽特的眼睛有那么一两秒钟被泪水模糊了。接着是小艇拢上栈桥；珀西一跃上岸；他的嗓音，他的眼神；他双臂的力气；他拥抱的热烈。实在是心醉神迷，唯独一想到这陶醉何其短促，才给它添了一丝苦味。

可是再别离的事，玛格丽特偏不去想——今天不想，鸟儿正唱着震耳欲聋的欢乐颂歌；生长着的青草、湿润而劳作不息的大地的气息，正沁在她鼻端；树身浆液正满，栗树上黏润的绛红芽苞正绽出叶来。她正沿着窄窄的小巷、在开花的黑刺李树篱之间漫步，珀西的胳膊搂着她，他可爱的嗓音响在她耳边，他快活的笑声在甜美的晨风里回荡——这样的时候，她不想。

===Original===

After that, breakfast in the low, raftered room﻿—the hot, savoury milk, the home-backed bread, the home-churned butter. Then the long, delicious, intimate talk of love, and of yearnings, of duty and of gallant deeds. Blakeney kept nothing secret from his wife; and what he did not tell her, that she easily guessed. But it was from the members of the League that she learned all there was to know of heroism and selflessness in the perilous adventures through which her husband passed with so lighthearted a gaiety.

===Chinese===

之后，便在那房梁低矮的屋子里用早餐——滚热喷香的牛奶，自家烤的面包，自家打的黄油。再往后，便是那场绵绵长谈，甜蜜而贴心，谈爱情，谈渴念，谈责任，谈侠义功勋。布莱克尼（Blakeney）对妻子无所隐瞒；他不曾告诉她的，她也一猜便着。不过，关于她丈夫以何等轻松欢快的心情穿过的那些险象环生的历险，其中种种英勇与无私，她倒都是从同盟（the League）的成员们那里听来的。

===Original===

“You should see me as an asthmatic reprobate, m’dear,” he would say, with his infectious laugh. “And hear that cough! Lud love you, but I am mightily proud of that cough! Poor old Rateau does not do it better himself; and he is genuinely asthmatic.”

He gave her an example of his prowess; but she would not allow him to go on. The sound was too weird, and conjured up visions which today she would fain forget.

===Chinese===

“你真该瞧瞧我扮成气喘的老无赖那副模样，亲爱的，”他总是带着他那富有感染力的笑声说。“再听听那咳嗽！老天作证，我可着实为那声咳嗽得意呢！可怜的老拉托（Rateau）亲口咳嗽起来，也强不到哪儿去；他是真害着气喘呢。”

他当场给她演示了一番自己的绝技；她却不许他再演下去。那声音太瘆人，勾起的种种景象，她今天巴不得统统忘掉。

===Original===

“Rateau was a real find,” he went on more seriously; “because he is three parts an imbecile and as obedient as a dog. When some of those devils are on my track, lo! the real Rateau appears and yours truly vanishes where no one can find him!”

“Pray God,” she murmured involuntarily, “they never may!”

“They won’t, m’dear, they won’t!” he asserted with lighthearted conviction. “They have become so confused now between Rateau the coalheaver, the mysterious Scarlet Pimpernel, and the problematic English milor, that all three of these personalities can appear before their eyes and they will let ’em all escape! I assure you that the confusion between the Scarlet Pimpernel who was in the antechamber of Mother Théot on that fateful afternoon, and again at the Fraternal Supper in the Rue St. Honoré, and the real Rateau who was at Mother Théot’s while that same exciting supper party was going on, was so great that not one of those murdering reprobates could trust his own eyes and ears, and that we got away as easily as rabbits out of a torn net.”

===Chinese===

“拉托可真是个难得的发现，”他接着说，语气郑重了些；“他十分里倒有七分是低能儿，又像狗一样听话。每逢那帮魔鬼盯上我的踪迹，瞧！真拉托便露面了，而鄙人就此遁形，任谁也找不着！”

“求上帝，”她不由自主地喃喃道，“千万别叫他们找着！”

“找不着，亲爱的，找不着的！”他满心笃定、轻快地断言。“他们如今叫运煤工拉托、神秘的猩红石榴红，还有那个说不清道不明的英国爵爷（milor）搅得晕头转向，这三副面孔就是一齐站到他们眼皮底下，他们也会一个个都放跑！我向你保证，那个凶险的下午身在泰奥妈妈（Mother Théot）候见室里、随后又出现在圣奥诺雷街（Rue St. Honoré）兄弟晚餐会（Fraternal Supper）上的猩红石榴红，同那个当那场热闹晚宴进行时正在泰奥妈妈家的真拉托，两下里把他们搅得天旋地转，那帮杀人不眨眼的恶棍没一个敢信自己的眼睛耳朵，我们脱身便容易得像兔子钻出破网。”

===Original===

Thus did he explain and laugh over the perilous adventure where he had faced a howling mob disguised as Rateau the coalheaver, and with almost superhuman pluck and boldness had dragged Mme. de Serval and her children into the derelict house which was one of the League’s headquarters. That is how he characterised the extraordinary feat of audacity when, in order to give his gallant lieutenants time to smuggle the unfortunates out of the house through a back and secret way, he showed himself on the balcony above the multitude, and hurled dummy figures into the brazier below.

Then came the story of Bertrand Moncrif, snatched half-unconscious out of the apartment of the fair Theresia Cabarrus, whilst Robespierre himself sat not half a dozen yards away, with only the thickness of a wall between him and his arch enemy.

===Chinese===

他便是这样一面讲解、一面笑着回顾那段险象环生的历险：他扮作运煤工拉托，直面一群嚎叫的暴民，凭着近乎神勇的胆魄，硬是把德·塞尔瓦尔夫人（Mme. de Serval）和她的孩子们拖进了那座荒宅——同盟的一处据点。他又是这样讲述那桩胆大包天的奇功：为了给麾下勇悍的副手们争取时间，好把那几个落难的人从宅子后头一条隐秘的路径偷偷送出去，他索性现身在人群头顶的阳台上，朝底下火盆里一个个扔假人。

接下来便是贝特朗·蒙克里夫（Bertrand Moncrif）的故事：他半昏半醒之际，被人从美艳的特蕾莎·卡巴吕斯（Theresia Cabarrus）的寓所里一把抢走，而当时罗伯斯庇尔（Robespierre）本人就坐在不出六码远的地方，同他的死敌之间只隔着一堵墙的厚度。

===Original===

“How the woman must hate you!” Marguerite murmured, with a slight shudder of acute anxiety which she did her best to conceal. “There are things that a woman like the Cabarrus will never forgive. Whether she cares for Bertrand Moncrif or no, her vanity will suffer intensely, and she will never forgive you for taking him out of her clutches.”

===Chinese===

“那女人该多恨你啊！”玛格丽特喃喃道，一阵剧烈的焦灼使她微微一颤，又被她竭力掩饰过去。“有些事，卡巴吕斯那样的女人是永远不会宽恕的。且不论她心里有没有贝特朗·蒙克里夫，她的虚荣心已重重受了伤，你把他从她掌心里夺走，她永远不会饶你。”

===Original===

He laughed.

“Lud, m’dear!” he said lightly. “If we were to take heed of all the people who hate us we should spend our lives pondering rather than doing. And all I want to ponder over,” he added, whilst his glance of passionate earnestness seemed to envelop her like an exquisite warm mantle, “is your beauty, your eyes, the scent of your hair, the delicious flavour of your kiss!”

===Chinese===

他哈哈一笑。

“老天，亲爱的！”他轻描淡写地说。“要是把恨我们的人个个都放在心上，那我们这一辈子就光顾着掂量，什么也别干了。而我满心想掂量的，”他添上一句，那热烈恳切的目光仿佛一件精美的暖披风，把她整个裹住，“是你的美貌，你的眼睛，你发间的香气，还有你那一吻的甜滋味！”

### II / II

===Original===

It was some hours later on that same glorious day, when the shadows of ash and chestnut lay right across the lane and the arms of evening folded the cosy nest in their mysterious embrace, that Sir Percy and Marguerite sat in the deep window-embrasure of the tiny living-room. He had thrown open wide the casements, and hand resting in hand, they watched the last ray of golden light lingering in the west and listened to the twitterings which came like tender “good nights” from the newly-built nests among the trees.

It was one of those perfect spring evenings, rare enough in northern climes, without a breath of wind, when every sound carries clear and sharp through the stillness around. The air was soft and slightly moist, with a tang in it of wakening life and of rising sap, and with the scent of wild narcissus and of wood violets rising like intoxicating incense to the nostrils. It was in truth one of those evening when happiness itself seems rudely out of place, and nature﻿—exquisite, but so cruelly, transient in her loveliness﻿—demands the tribute of gentle melancholy.

===Chinese===

还是在那个光辉灿烂的日子，几小时之后，梣树与栗树的影子已横过小巷，暮色的臂膀把这座温暖的爱巢拢进它神秘的怀抱，珀西爵士和玛格丽特坐在小小起居室深深的窗龛里。他把窗扉推得大开，两人手叠着手，望着西方流连不去的最后一缕金光，听着树间新筑的巢里传来的一声声啁啾，宛如道道温柔的“晚安”。

这是北方难得一遇的完美春夜，没有一丝风，四下静得出奇，每个声响都传得清清楚楚、锐利分明。空气温柔而微潮，带着一丝苏醒的生命、上涨的树液的辛香，野水仙与林中紫罗兰的芬芳如醉人的熏香，冉冉升上鼻端。说实在的，这样的夜晚，连幸福本身都显得莽撞得不合时宜；造化——精妙绝伦，她的美却又残忍地转瞬即逝——此刻索要的，是一缕温柔愁思的贡赋。

===Original===

A thrush said something to its mate﻿—something insistent and tender that lulled them both to rest. After that, Nature became quite still, and Marguerite, with a catch in her throat which she would have given much to suppress, laid her head upon her husband’s breast.

Then it was that suddenly a man’s voice, hoarse but distant, broke in upon the perfect peace around. What it said could not at first be gathered. It took some time ere Marguerite became sufficiently conscious of the disturbing noise to raise her head and listen. As for Sir Percy, he was wrapped in the contemplating of the woman he worshipped, and nothing short of an earthquake would have dragged him back to reality, had not Marguerite raised herself on her knees and quickly whispered:

===Chinese===

一只画眉对它的伴侣说了句什么——一句又执着又温柔的话，把两个人都哄得安恬下来。此后，造化便全然静默了；玛格丽特喉咙里涌上一阵哽咽——她多想把它压下去——把头靠在了丈夫的胸前。

就在此时，忽然一个男人的声音，沙哑而遥远，打破了周遭完美的安宁。起初听不清说的是什么。过了好一会儿，玛格丽特才充分意识到这扰人的声响，抬起头来细听。至于珀西爵士，他正沉浸在对他所膜拜的女人的凝想里，若不是玛格丽特直起身子跪坐着、急促地悄声唤了一句，只怕天塌地陷也拉不回他来：

===Original===

“Listen!”

The man’s voice had been answered by a woman’s raised as if in defiance that seemed both pitiful and futile.

===Chinese===

“听！”

男人的声音得到一个女人声音的回答，那嗓音扬着，像是在抗辩，听来既凄楚又徒劳。

===Original===

“You cannot harm me now. I am in England!”

Marguerite leaned out of the window, tried to peer into the darkness which was fast gathering over the lane. The voices had come from there: first the man’s, then the woman’s, and now the man’s again; both speaking in French, the woman obviously terrified and pleading, the man harsh and commanding. Now it was raised again, more incisive and distinct than before, and Marguerite had in truth some difficulty in repressing the cry that rose to her lips. She had recognised the man’s voice.

===Chinese===

“如今你害不了我了。我是在英格兰！”

玛格丽特探身窗外，想望进那正迅速拢上小巷的夜色里。声音正是从那边来的：先是男人的，继而女人的，如今又是男人的；两个说的都是法语，女人显然吓得魂不附体、声声哀告，男人则粗暴专横、句句命令。这时那男人的声音又扬起来，比先前更尖利、更分明，玛格丽特当真费了好大劲，才压下冲到唇边的惊呼。她听出了那个男人的嗓音。

===Original===

“Chauvelin!” she murmured.

“Aye, in England, citoyenne!” that ominous voice went on drily. “But the arm of justice is long. And remember that you are not the first who has tried﻿—unsuccessfully, let me tell you!﻿—to evade punishment by flying to the enemies of France. Wherever you may hide, I will know how to find you. Have I not found you here, now?﻿—and you but a few hours in Dover!”

===Chinese===

“肖夫兰（Chauvelin）！”她喃喃道。

“是啊，在英格兰，女公民（citoyenne）！”那不祥的嗓音冷冷地接下去。“可是正义的臂膀长着呢。你给我记着，想逃到法兰西的敌人那里躲避惩罚的，你不是头一个——我告诉你，没有一个成了事！随你藏到哪儿，我都有法子把你找出来。我不是此刻就在这儿把你找着了么？——你到多佛尔才几个钟头！”

===Original===

“But you cannot touch me!” the woman protested with the courage of one in despair.

The man laughed.

“Are you really simple enough, citoyenne,” he said, “to be convinced of that?”

===Chinese===

“可你碰不得我！”那女人拿出绝望者的勇气抗辩道。

男人笑了。

“你当真天真到那个地步么，女公民，”他说，“竟真信了这话？”

===Original===

This sarcastic retort was followed by a moment or two of silence, then by a woman’s cry; and in an instant Sir Percy was on his feet and out of the house. Marguerite followed him as far as the porch, whence the sloping ground, aided by flagged steps here and there, led down to the gate and thence on to the lane.

It was close beside the gate that a human-looking bundle lay huddled, when Sir Percy came upon the scene, even whilst, some fifty yards away at the sharp bend of the lane, a man could be seen walking rapidly away, his pace well-nigh at a run. Sir Percy’s instinct was for giving chase, but the huddle-up figure put out a pair of arms and clung to him so desperately, with smothered cries of: “For pity’s sake, don’t leave me!” that it would have been inhuman to go. And so he bent down, raised the human bundle from the ground, and carried it bodily up into the house.

Here he deposited his burden upon the window seat, where but a few moments ago he had been wrapped in the contemplation of Marguerite’s eyelashes, and with his habitual quaint good-humour, said:

“I leave the rest to you, m’dear. My French is too atrocious for dealing with the case.”

Marguerite understood the hint. Sir Percy, whose command of French was nothing short of phenomenal, never used the language save when engaged in his perilous undertakings. His perfect knowledge of every idiom would have set any ill-intentioned evesdropper thinking.

===Chinese===

这句讥诮的反驳之后，是一两秒钟的沉寂，跟着便是一声女人的惊叫；说时迟那时快，珀西爵士已一跃而起，冲出了屋子。玛格丽特跟着他走到门廊那里；从门廊起，坡地借着散落各处的石板台阶一路往下，通到院门，再由院门通到小巷。

珀西爵士赶到出事地点时，紧挨着院门的地方蜷缩着一团人形的东西；与此同时，五十来码开外小巷的急拐弯处，望得见一个男人正快步远去，那步子几乎是在跑。珀西爵士的本能是拔腿去追，可那蜷作一团的人影伸出两条胳膊，死死抱住他，闷着嗓子哀告：“可怜可怜我，别丢下我！”这当口甩手就走，未免太狠心。于是他俯下身，把那团人从地上抱起来，整个儿抱进了屋。

他把这份负担放到窗座上——就在几分钟之前，他还坐在这里痴痴凝望玛格丽特的睫毛呢——随后带着他惯有的那副古雅的好脾气，说道：

“剩下的就交给你了，亲爱的。我那法语太不成话，办不了这桩案子。”

玛格丽特领会了这层深意。珀西爵士的法语功力之深简直骇人，却除非投身那些凶险的勾当，从不肯用它。他对每一个习语都烂熟于胸，这份造诣，足够叫任何居心不良的偷听者起疑。

===Original===
