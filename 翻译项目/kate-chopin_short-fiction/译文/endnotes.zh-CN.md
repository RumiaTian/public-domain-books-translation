## Endnotes / 尾注
===Original===

1. Pronounced Nack-e-tosh ↩︎
2. A term still applied in Louisiana to mulattoes who were never in slavery, and whose families in most instances were themselves slave owners. ↩︎

===Chinese===

1. 读作“纳克-伊-托什”。↩︎
2. 这个称呼至今仍用于路易斯安那，指那些从未沦为奴隶的黑白混血儿——他们的家族在多数情况下自己就拥有奴隶。↩︎
