## XV The Torn Hotel Bill / 十五 撕碎的旅馆账单

===Original===
On reaching the Yard next morning Inspector French began his day by compiling a list of the various points on which obvious investigations still remained to be made. He had already determined that these should be carried through with the greatest possible dispatch, leaving a general consideration of the case over until their results should be available.

===Chinese===
第二天早晨一到苏格兰场，弗伦奇探长头一件事便是开列一张清单，把各项尚待进行的明显侦查要点一一列上。他早已拿定主意：这些侦查要火速办完，等结果出来，再对全案作通盘的考虑。

===Original===
The immediate questions were, of course: Was Joan Merrill alive? And if so, where was she? These must be solved as soon as possible. The further matters relating to the hiding-place and aims of the gang could wait. It was, however, likely enough that if French could find Joan, he would have at least gone a long way towards solving her captors’ secret.

===Chinese===
眼下的问题自然是：琼·梅里尔还活着吗？倘若活着，人在哪里？这两问必须尽快解开。至于团伙的藏身之处与图谋这些较远的事，不妨缓一缓。不过，十有八九，弗伦奇只要找到琼，距离解开掳她那伙人的秘密，至少也就迈进一大步了。

===Original===
Perhaps the most promising of all the lines of inquiry open to him were the detailed searches of Blessington’s and Sime’s houses, and he decided he would begin with these. Accordingly, having called Sergeant Carter and a couple more men, he went out to Earlswood and set to work.

===Chinese===
在他手头可以着手的各条调查路线之中，最有指望的也许莫过于细搜布莱辛顿和赛姆两家的住宅，他决定就从这两处入手。于是他叫上卡特警士和另外两三个人，出动到厄尔斯伍德，干了起来。

===Original===
French was extraordinarily thorough. Nothing in that house, from the water cistern space in the roof to the floors of the pantries and the tool shed in the yard﻿—nothing escaped observation. The furniture was examined, particularly the writing desk and the old escritoire, the carpets were lifted and the floors tested, the walls were minutely inspected for secret receptacles, the pages of the books were turned over, the clothes﻿—of which a respectable wardrobe remained﻿—were gone through, with special attention to the pockets. Nothing was taken for granted: everything was examined. Even the outside of the house and the soil of the garden were looked at, and at the end, some four hours after they had begun, French had to admit that his gains were practically nil.

===Chinese===
弗伦奇细致得出奇。那座宅子，从屋顶的水箱夹层到配膳间的地面，再到院子里的工具棚——没有一样逃过查看。家具逐一检验，尤其是那张写字台和那架旧式写字柜；地毯掀起，地板敲过；墙壁细细察看有无暗格；书页一页一页翻过；留下的衣服——衣橱里还挺像样地存着一批——也统统搜了一遍，口袋格外留心。没有一样想当然：样样都查。连宅子外观和园子里的泥土都看了个遍。到头来，动手约莫四个钟头之后，弗伦奇不得不承认，所获几乎等于零。

===Original===
The reservation was in respect of four objects, from one or more of which he might conceivably extract some information, though he was far from hopeful. The first was the top sheet of Blessington’s writing pad. French, following his usual custom, had examined it through a mirror, but so completely covered was it with inkstains that he was unable to decipher even a single word. However, on chance he tore it off and put it in his pocket, in the hope that a future more detailed examination might reveal something of interest.

===Chinese===
这“几乎”二字之外，尚有四件东西留了余地，从其中一件或几件上，他并非全无挤出一点消息的可能，尽管他全不敢奢望。第一件是布莱辛顿吸墨纸簿最上面的一页。弗伦奇照老习惯对着镜子照看过了，无奈上面墨迹斑斑、密密麻麻，连一个字也辨认不出。不过他还是碰碰运气，把那页纸撕下来放进衣袋，指望日后更细的查验能显出点什么有意思的东西。

===Original===
The second object was a scrap of crumpled paper which he found in the right-hand upper pocket of one of Dangle’s waistcoats. It looked as if it had been crushed to the bottom of the pocket by some other article﻿—such as an engagement book﻿—being thrust down on the top of it. When the pockets had been cleared﻿—as all had been﻿—this small piece of paper had evidently been overlooked.

===Chinese===
第二件是一团皱巴巴的纸屑，是从丹格尔一件马甲的右上袋里找出来的。看那样子，是有别的什么东西——比如一本约会簿——后来压在上面塞进袋里，把它挤到了袋底。口袋清理的时候——所有口袋都清理过了——这一小片纸显然被漏掉了。

===Original===
French straightened it out. It was the bottom portion of what was clearly a bill, apparently a French hotel bill. On the back was a note written in pencil, and as French read it, the thought passed through his mind that he could not have imagined any more unexpected or puzzling contents. It was in the form of a memorandum and read:

===Chinese===
弗伦奇把它抚平。这显然是一张账单的下半截，看样子是法国旅馆的账单。背面有一行铅笔字，弗伦奇一读，心里顿时掠过一个念头：再也想不出比这更出人意料、更叫人纳闷的内容了。那是一份备忘录式的单子，写道：

===Original===
The paper was torn across the first two items, so that only part of the words were legible. What so heterogeneous a collection could possibly refer to French could not imagine, but he put the fragment in his pocket with the blotting paper for future study.

===Chinese===
纸在头两项上被撕过，只剩半截字，认不齐全。这么一锅大杂烩究竟作什么用场，弗伦奇怎么也想不出，只好把这片纸同那页吸墨纸一起收进衣袋，留待日后研究。

===Original===
The other two objects were photographs, and from the descriptions he had received from Cheyne he felt satisfied that one was of Blessington and the other of Dangle. These were of no help in themselves, but might later prove useful for identification purposes.

===Chinese===
另外两件东西是照片，凭切恩向他描述的模样，他认定一张是布莱辛顿，一张是丹格尔。两张照片本身帮不上忙，但日后供辨认之用，也许有其价值。

===Original===
The search of Earlswood complete, French gave his men an hour for lunch, and then started a similar investigation of Sime’s house. He was just as painstaking and thorough here, but this time he had no luck at all. Though Sime had not so carefully destroyed papers and correspondence, he could not find a single thing which seemed to offer help.

===Chinese===
厄尔斯伍德搜完，弗伦奇给手下人一个钟头吃午饭，随后着手对赛姆的住宅作同样的搜查。在这里他同样一丝不苟、同样彻底，可这回运气全无。赛姆的文件信函虽不曾那样细心销毁，他却连一件似乎帮得上忙的东西也找不出来。

===Original===
Sime’s house being so much smaller than Blessington’s, the search was finished in little over an hour. On its completion French sent two of his men back to the Yard, while with Sergeant Carter he drove to Horne Terrace. There he examined Joan Merrill’s rooms, again without result.

===Chinese===
赛姆的宅子比布莱辛顿的小得多，搜查一个钟头多一点便告完事。一完事，弗伦奇打发两名手下回苏格兰场，自己带着卡特警士驱车前往霍恩台。在那里他检查了琼·梅里尔的房间，仍旧一无所获。

===Original===
The work ended about four, and then he and Carter began another job, quite as detailed and a good deal more wearisome than the others. He had determined to question individually every other person living in the house﻿—that is, the inhabitants of no less than nine flats﻿—in the hope that some one of them might have seen or heard Joan returning to her rooms on the night of her disappearance. In a way the point was not of supreme importance, but experience had taught French the danger of neglecting *any* clue, no matter how unpromising, and he had long since made it a principle to follow up every opening which offered.

===Chinese===
这项工作四点钟前后收场，接着他和卡特又干起另一桩活计，细致不让前者，乏味则远过之。他打定主意，要把楼里其余住户逐个单独讯问——也就是说，足足九套公寓的居民——指望其中有人在她失踪那晚看见或听见琼回自己的房间。在某种意义上，这一点并非头等重要，但经验教会了弗伦奇：*任何*线索，哪怕再没有指望，忽视了都有危险；凡有一线门路便追到底，这是他早已立下的信条。

===Original===
For over two hours he worked, and at last, as he was beginning to accept defeat, he obtained just the information he required.

===Chinese===
他一连干了两个多钟头，最后，正当他开始认输的时候，偏偏得到了他所需要的那条消息。

===Original===
It appeared that about a quarter past eleven on the night in question, the fifteen-year-old daughter of a widow living on the third floor was returning home from some small jollification when she saw, just as she approached the door, three persons come out. Two were men, one tall, well built and clean-shaven, the other short and stout, with a fair toothbrush mustache. The third person was Miss Merrill. A street lamp had shone directly on their faces as they emerged, and the girl had noticed that the men wore serious expressions and that Miss Merrill looked pale and anxious, as if all three were sharers in some bad news. They crossed the sidewalk to a waiting motor. Miss Merrill and the taller man got inside, the second man driving. During the time the girl saw them, none of them spoke. She remembered the car. It was a yellow one with a coach body, and looked a private vehicle. Yes, she recognized the photograph the Inspector showed her﻿—Blessington’s. It was that of the driver of the car.

===Chinese===
据她说，出事那晚十一点一刻光景，住在三楼的一位寡妇的十五岁女儿赴过一场小小的欢聚回家来，正走近大门时，看见三个人从楼里出来。两个是男人：一个高挑结实，脸刮得精光；另一个矮胖，蓄一撇浅色的牙刷胡。第三个是梅里尔小姐。三人出来时，一盏路灯正照在他们脸上，姑娘注意到两个男人神情凝重，梅里尔小姐脸色发白、神色不安，仿佛三人都同得着了什么坏消息。他们穿过人行道，走向一辆候着的汽车。梅里尔小姐同高个子男人坐进车里，另一个男人开车。姑娘看见他们那阵子，谁也没有开口。那辆车她记得：黄色的，轿车式车身，看去是辆私家车。对了，探长拿照片给她看，她认出来了——布莱辛顿的那张，正是开车的人。

===Original===
It did not seem worth while to French to try to trace the car, as he fancied he knew where it had gone. From Horne Terrace to Sime’s house in Colton Street was about a ten minute run. Therefore if it left the former about 11:15, it should reach the latter a minute or two before the half-hour. This worked in with the time at which the invalid lady, Mrs. Sproule, had heard the motor stop in the street, and to French it seemed clear that Miss Merrill had been taken direct to Sime’s, and kept there until 1:45 p.m. on the following day. What arguments or threats the pair had used to get her to accompany them French could not tell, but he shrewdly suspected that they had played the same trick on her as on Cheyne. In all probability they had told her that Cheyne had met with an accident and was conscious and asking for her. Once in the cab it would have been child’s play for a powerful man like Sime to have chloroformed her, and having got her to the house, they could easily have kept her helpless and semiconscious by means of drugs.

===Chinese===
在弗伦奇看来，去追查那辆车并不值得，因为他自忖已知它去了哪里。从霍恩台到科尔顿街赛姆的宅子，坐车约莫十分钟。因此，倘若它 11:15 前后离开前者，就该在十一点半差一两分到达后者。这同那位卧病的斯普劳尔太太（Mrs. Sproule）听见汽车在街上停下的时刻正相吻合。在弗伦奇看来，事情很清楚：梅里尔小姐被径直送到赛姆家，一直关到第二天下午 1:45。那两个人使了什么说辞或威吓才让她跟着走，弗伦奇不得而知，但他机灵地疑心，他们对她使的正是对切恩使过的那一手。十之八九，他们对她说，切恩遇了事故，人还清醒，正要见她。一旦进了车，凭赛姆那样一条壮汉，用氯仿把她麻翻不过儿戏一场；弄进宅子之后，用药物叫她浑身无力、半昏半醒地困着，更是易如反掌。

===Original===
French returned on foot to the Yard, thinking over the affair as he walked. It certainly had a sinister look. These men were very much in earnest. They had not hesitated to resort to murder in the case of Cheyne﻿—it was through, to them, an absolutely unforeseen accident that he escaped﻿—and French felt he would not give much for Joan Merrill’s chances.

===Chinese===
弗伦奇步行回苏格兰场，一路把这件事想了又想。此事的兆头实在凶险。这伙人是动了真格的。对切恩他们尚且毫不迟疑地动过杀机——他能脱身，在他们纯属万万料不到的意外——弗伦奇觉得，琼·梅里尔的生机如何，他实在不敢乐观。

===Original===
When he reached his office he found that a piece of news had just come in. A constable who had been on point duty at the intersection of South and Mitchem Streets, near Waterloo Station, had noticed about 2 p.m. on the day of the disappearance of the gang, a yellow motorcar pass close beside him and turn into Hackworth’s garage, a small establishment in the latter street. Though he had not observed the vehicle with more than the ordinary attention such a man will give to the passing traffic, his recollection both of the car and driver led him to the belief that they were those referred to in the Yard circular. The constable was waiting to see French, and made his report with diffidence, saying that though he thought he was right, he might easily be mistaken.

===Chinese===
回到办公室，他发现刚有一条消息送了进来。团伙逃遁那天下午两点前后，一个在滑铁卢车站（Waterloo Station）附近南街与米彻姆街（South and Mitchem Streets）路口定点值勤的警察，注意到一辆黄色汽车贴身驶过，拐进了后一条街上的哈克沃思（Hackworth）车行——那是家小店。他看那车，本没超出这样一个警察对过往车辆照例会给的那份寻常留意，可凭他对车子和司机的记忆，他相信正是苏格兰场通报里说的那车那人。那警察正候着见弗伦奇，怯生生地作了报告，说自己虽然觉得没错，也很容易弄错。

===Original===
“Quite right to let me know anyhow, Wilson,” French said heartily. “If you’ve seen Blessington’s car it may give us a valuable clue, and if you’re mistaken, there’s no harm done. We’ve nothing to lose by following it up.” He glanced at his watch. “It’s past my dinner hour, but I’ll take a taxi and go round to this garage on my way home. You’d better come along.”

===Chinese===
“不拘怎样，来报告总是对的，威尔逊（Wilson），”弗伦奇恳切地说。“倘若你看见的真是布莱辛顿的车，也许能给我们一条宝贵的线索；倘若你看错了，也不碍事。追查下去，我们毫无损失。”他看了看表。“这都过我的饭点了，不过我叫辆出租汽车，回家路上弯到那家车行去看看。你最好也一起来。”

===Original===
Ten minutes later the two men reached Hackworth’s establishment, and pushing open the door of the tiny office, asked if the manager was about.

===Chinese===
十分钟后，两人到了哈克沃思的铺面，推开那间小办公室的门，问经理在不在。

===Original===

===Chinese===
“我就是约翰·哈克沃思。有何贵干，先生？”一个穿寒酸灰色花呢衣服的胖子说。“要车吗？”

===Original===
“I want a word with you, Mr. Hackworth,” said French pleasantly. “Just a small matter of private business.”

===Chinese===
“我想同您讲句话，哈克沃思先生，”弗伦奇和颜悦色地说。“一点小小的私家事务。”

===Original===
Hackworth nodded, and indicated a farther door.

===Chinese===
哈克沃思点点头，朝里面一扇门指了指。

===Original===
“In here,” he invited, and when French and the constable had taken the two chairs the room contained, he briskly repeated: “Yes, sir?”

===Chinese===
“到这儿来谈，”他招呼道；等弗伦奇和那警察坐了屋里仅有的两把椅子，他又利落地问了一遍：“有何贵干，先生？”

===Original===
At this hint not to waste valuable time, French promptly introduced himself and propounded his question. Mr. Hackworth looked impressed.

===Chinese===
对方这是示意莫浪费宝贵时光，弗伦奇当即通了姓名，说明来意。哈克沃思先生听了，神色肃然。

===Original===
“You don’t tell me that gent was a wrong ’un?” he said anxiously, then another idea seeming to strike him, he continued: “Of course it don’t matter to me in a way, for I’ve got the car. I’ll tell you about it.”

===Chinese===
“您不会是说，那位先生是个歹人吧？”他不安地说，随即像又想起一层，接着道：“当然啰，往一边说，这与我不相干——车在我手里呢。我把经过讲给您听。”

===Original===

===Chinese===
弗伦奇取出布莱辛顿的照片。

===Original===

===Chinese===
“先告诉我，是不是这个人。”他说。

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Chinese===

===Chinese===

French, having put a few more questions, got the proprietor to write out a detailed description of the Napier.
