## XVII On the Flood Tide / 十七 涨潮

===Original===
French was astir betimes next morning, and over his coffee and rolls and honey he laid his plans for the day. As to the next step of his investigation he had no doubt. He must begin by finding out what vessels had left the city after 2:00 p.m. on the previous Thursday. That done, he could go into the question of the passengers each carried, in the hope of learning that Dangle was among them.

===Chinese===
第二天一早弗伦奇就起身了，就着咖啡、面包卷和蜂蜜，把这一天的计划定下来。调查下一步该怎么走，他心里毫无疑虑：先得查清上星期四下午 2:00 之后有哪些船只离了港。这一步办完，再去查每条船各载了哪些乘客，指望能查出丹格尔名列其中。

===Original===
At the outset he was faced by the handicap of being a stranger in a strange land. If Antwerp had been an English port he would have known just where to get his information, but here he was unfamiliar with the ropes. He did not know if all sailings were published in any paper or available to the public at any office; moreover, his ignorance of both French and Flemish precluded his mixing with clerks or dock loafers from whom he might pick up information. Of course there were the Belgian police, but he did not wish to apply to them if he could carry out his job by himself.

===Chinese===
一开头他就遇上一重障碍：人地生疏。安特卫普若是座英国港口，他准知道该上哪儿去打听；可在这里，个中门道他一概不熟。他不知道船期是否都登在什么报纸上，也不知道有什么办公处可供公众查询；再说，他法语、佛兰芒语一窍不通，也就没法混到职员或码头闲汉中间去捡消息。当然还有比利时警方，可只要自己办得成这差事，他并不想去求他们。

===Original===
However, this part of his problem proved easier of solution than he had expected. Inquiries at the post office revealed the fact that there was a shipping agency in the Rue des Tanneurs, and soon he had reached the place, found a clerk who spoke English, and put his question.

===Chinese===
不过，问题的这一部分解起来倒比他预想的容易。在邮局一打听，得知制革匠街（Rue des Tanneurs）有一家航运代理行，不多时他便寻到了那里，找到一位会说英语的职员，把问题提了出来。

===Original===
When French wished to be suave, as he usually did, he could, so to speak, have wheedled his best bone from a bulldog. Now, explaining in a friendly and confidential manner who he was and why he wanted the information, he begged the other’s good offices. The clerk, flattered at being thus courteously approached, showed a willingness to assist, with the result that in ten minutes French had the particulars he needed.

===Chinese===
弗伦奇素来待人和气，真要施展柔功的时候，可以说连斗牛犬嘴边的骨头都哄得下来。此刻他亲亲热热、推心置腹地说明了自己是谁、为什么要打听这些消息，恳请对方从中帮忙。那职员受此礼遇，心里受用，欣然愿助一臂之力，结果不出十分钟，弗伦奇便把所需的详情都弄到了手。

===Original===
He turned into a café, and calling for a bock, sat down to consider what he had learned. And of this the very first fact filled him with delight, as it seemed to fit in with the theory he had evolved.

===Chinese===
他拐进一家咖啡馆，要了一杯博克啤酒（bock），坐下来琢磨刚才听来的情况。其中头一件事实就叫他满心欢喜，因为它似乎正合他推演出来的那套理论的路子。

===Original===
On Thursday it had been high water at 2:30 p.m. By 2:30 the dock gates had been opened, and it appeared that, taking advantage of this, several steamers had left shortly after that hour.

===Chinese===
星期四那天下午 2:30 满潮。到 2:30 船闸已然开启，看样子有几艘轮船正是趁这个时机，在那之后不久离的港。

===Original===
This was distinctly encouraging, and French turned to the list of ships with a growing hope that the end of his investigation might be coming into sight. In all, eleven steamers had left the port on the day in question, between the hours of 2:00 and 6:00 p.m., the period he had included in his inquiry.

===Chinese===
这情形着实鼓舞人心。弗伦奇转而研究那份船舶清单，心里越来越存着指望：这桩调查的尽头，也许已经遥遥在望。在他查询的时段——那天下午 2:00 到 6:00 之间——离港的轮船共计十一艘。

===Original===
There was first of all a Canadian Pacific liner, which had sailed from the quays at 3:00 p.m., and at 3:30 a small passenger boat had left for Oslo and Bergen. The remaining boats were tramps. There were four coasters, two for Newcastle, one for Goole, and one for Belfast, a 6,000 tonner for Singapore and the Dutch Islands, another slightly smaller ship for Genoa and Spezia, and another for Boston, U.S.A. Then there was a big five-masted sailing ship, bound with a general cargo for Buenos Aires and the River Platte, and finally there was a small freighter in ballast for Casablanca.

===Chinese===
头一艘是加拿大太平洋轮船公司（Canadian Pacific）的班轮，下午 3:00 从码头起航；3:30 又有一艘小客船开往奥斯陆（Oslo）和卑尔根（Bergen）。余下的都是不定期货船：四艘跑沿海航线，两艘去纽卡斯尔（Newcastle），一艘去古尔（Goole），一艘去贝尔法斯特（Belfast）；一艘六千吨的去新加坡（Singapore）和荷属群岛；一艘略小些的去热那亚（Genoa）和斯佩齐亚（Spezia）；还有一艘去美国波士顿（Boston）。再往下是一艘五桅大帆船，装着杂货开往布宜诺斯艾利斯（Buenos Aires）和拉普拉塔河（River Platte）；末了一艘小货轮，空载压舱，开往卡萨布兰卡（Casablanca）。

===Original===
Of these eleven ships, the windjammer at once attracted French’s attention. Here was a vessel on which, if you took a passage, you might easily require three dozen tins of peaches before you reached your journey’s end. He determined to begin with this, taking the other ships in order according to the position of their offices. Fortunately in each case the clerk had given him the name of the owners or agents.

===Chinese===
这十一艘船里，那艘大帆船一下就勾住了弗伦奇的注意。乘这样一条船搭客远行，只怕还没到旅程的尽头，轻轻松松就得吃掉三打桃子罐头。他决意先从它查起，其余各船，再按各自船东事务所的位置顺路挨家去过。幸而每艘船的船东或代理行的名号，那位职员都给了他。

===Original===
His first call, therefore, was at an old-fashioned office in a small street close to the Steen Museum. There he saw M. Leblanc, the owner of the windjammer, and explained his business. But M. Leblanc could not help him. The old gentleman had never heard of Dangle nor had anyone resembling his visitor’s photograph called or done any business with his firm. Moreover, no passengers had shipped on the windjammer, and the crew that had sailed was unchanged since the previous voyage.

===Chinese===
于是他头一处拜访的，是斯滕博物馆（Steen Museum）近旁小巷里一家老式事务所。他在那里见到了大帆船的船东勒布朗先生（M. Leblanc），说明来意。可是勒布朗先生帮不上忙。这位老先生从没听说过丹格尔其人，也没有任何相貌同来访者那张照片相像的人来过、同这家商行打过交道。再者，大帆船上没有搭载乘客，出海的船员同上一航次一样，原班未换。

===Original===
This was not encouraging, and French went on to the next item on his program, the headquarters of the small freighter which had sailed in ballast for Casablanca. She was owned by Messrs. Merkel & Lowenthal, whose office was farther down the Rue des Tanneurs, and five minutes later he had pushed open the door and was inquiring for the principal.

===Chinese===
这结果不算鼓舞人心。弗伦奇便接着办计划上的下一项：那艘空载开往卡萨布兰卡的小货轮的行址。这船属于默克尔-洛温塔尔商行（Messrs. Merkel & Lowenthal），事务所就在制革匠街更下去一点的地方。五分钟后，他已推开门，求见负责人。

===Original===
This was a more modern establishment than that of M. Leblanc. Though small, the office ran to plate glass windows, teak furniture, polished brass fittings, and encaustic tiles, while the two typists he could envisage through the small inquiry window seemed unduly gorgeous as to raiment and pert as to demeanor.

===Chinese===
这一家的气派比勒布朗先生的事务所新式。铺面虽小，却很舍得下本钱：平板大玻璃窗、柚木家具、擦得锃亮的黄铜配件、彩纹瓷砖；从那扇小小的问询窗望进去，能看见两名打字员，衣着一味艳丽，神气一味轻佻。

===Original===
He was kept waiting for some minutes, then told that M. Merkel, the head of the business, was away, but that M. Lowenthal, the junior partner, would see him.

===Chinese===
他被晾了好几分钟，然后被告知：商行掌班默克尔先生外出了，不过小合伙人洛温塔尔先生可以见他。

===Original===
His first glance told French that M. Lowenthal was a man to be watched. Seldom had he seen so many of the telltale signs of roguery concentrated in the features of one person. The junior partner had a mean, sly look, close-set, shifty eyes which would not meet French’s, and a large mouth with loose, fleshy lips. His manner was in accord with his appearance, now blustering, now almost fulsomely ingratiating. French took an instant dislike to him, and though he remained courteous as ever, he determined not to lay his cards on the table.

===Chinese===
头一眼弗伦奇就看出，这位洛温塔尔先生是个须得提防的人物。他很少见过这么多奸猾的征候集中体现在一张脸上。这位小合伙人一副刻薄相、狡猾相，两眼挤得很近，眼神闪烁游移，不敢同弗伦奇对视，一张大嘴，嘴唇松肥。他的做派也同这副长相相称：忽而咋咋呼呼，忽而近乎谄媚得叫人起腻。弗伦奇顿生厌恶，但表面上照旧客气周到，心里却打定了主意：不亮底牌。

===Original===
“My name,” he began, “as you will have seen from my card, is French, and I carry out the business of a general agent in London. I am trying to obtain an interview with a friend, who has been staying here, off and on, for some time. I came on here from Brussels in the hope of seeing him, but he had just left. I was told that he had sailed with your ship, the *L’Escaut*, on Thursday afternoon, and if so I called to ask at which port I should be likely to get in touch with him. His name is Dangle.”

===Chinese===
“我的姓名，”他开口道，“您从名片上想必已经看到，是弗伦奇，在伦敦做一般代理商的营生。我想见一位朋友一面，他断断续续在此地盘桓了些时日。我从布鲁塞尔赶来指望见他，不巧他刚走。听说他星期四下午搭贵行的船——莱斯考号（*L’Escaut*）——出海了；若是这样，我登门是想请问：到哪个港口最有希望同他取得联系？他的名字是丹格尔。”

===Original===
While French spoke he watched the other narrowly, on his favorite theory that the involuntary replies to unexpected remarks﻿—starts, changes of expression, sudden pallors﻿—were more valuable than spoken answers.

===Chinese===
弗伦奇说话的时候，眼睛紧紧盯着对方。按他那套心爱的理论：人对意外之言不自觉的反应——一惊、变脸、霎时面无血色——远比口头的回答更有价值。

===Original===
But M. Lowenthal betrayed no emotion other than a mild surprise.

===Chinese===
可是洛温塔尔先生除了微微露出一点惊讶之外，什么也没有流露。

===Original===
“That iss a very egstraordinary statement, sir,” he said in heavy guttural tones. “I do not really know who could haf given you such misleading information. Your friend’s name is quite unknown to me, and in any case we do not take passengers on our ships.”

===Chinese===
“这可是句非同寻常的话，先生，”他用浓重的喉音说道。“我实在想不出，是谁给了您这样误导人的消息。您那位朋友的大名，我压根没有听说过；再说，我行的船本来就一概不载客。”

===Original===
This seemed an entirely reasonable and proper reply, and yet to French’s highly developed instincts it did not ring true. However, he could do nothing more, and after a little further conversation containing not a few veiled inquiries, all of which, he noted, were skillfully parried by the other, he apologized for his mistake and withdrew.

===Chinese===
这回答听上去入情入理、无可挑剔，可在弗伦奇那副千锤百炼的直觉听来，总归有点不对味。不过他一时也无可再为。又稍谈了一会儿，话里夹了不少藏头露尾的探问——他注意到，这些全教对方四两拨千斤地挡了回去——于是他为自己的弄错道歉一声，告退出来。

===Original===
Though he was dissatisfied with the interview, he could only continue his program. He recognized that the secret might be located in Canada or the States, and that Dangle might have booked on the C.P.R. liner. Or he might have gone to Norway﻿—indeed, for the matter of that, he might have signed on on any of the ships for any part of the world.

===Chinese===
这一场会面虽叫他不满，他也只能照原计划继续查。他承认，秘密没准儿就在加拿大或美国，丹格尔或许订了加拿大太平洋班轮的铺位；要么就是去了挪威（Norway）——说实在的，退一步讲，他也完全可能随便在哪条船上签了约，去往世界上任何一个角落。

===Original===
But after a tedious morning of calls and interviews, French had to confess defeat. He could get no farther. At none of the offices at which he applied had he obtained the slightest helpful hint. It began to look as if he had been mistaken as to Dangle’s sea expedition, and if so, as he reminded himself with exasperation, he had no alternative theory to follow up.

===Chinese===
可是一个上午跑下来，拜访一处又一处、会面一场又一场，乏味之极，弗伦奇只得承认失败。他再也查不下去了。所叩过的事务所，没有一家给过他一丝一毫有用的暗示。事情渐渐像是：他把丹格尔海上远征这一层想岔了；倘若当真如此——他一面恼火一面提醒自己——手头可再没有别的理论可供追索了。

===Original===
He strolled slowly along the pleasant, sunlit streets, as he reviewed his morning’s work. He was satisfied with all his interviews but the one. Everywhere save in M. Lowenthal’s office he felt he had been told the truth. But instinctively he distrusted the junior partner. That the man had lied to him he had no reason to suspect, but he had no doubt that he would do so if it suited his book.

===Chinese===
他沿着阳光和煦、景致宜人的街道缓步走着，回味一上午的功课。除那一场之外，其余的会面他都满意。除了洛温塔尔先生的事务所，走到哪儿他都觉得听到的是实话。可是对这位小合伙人，他本能地信不过。说他已经对自己撒了谎，倒还没有什么理由可以指摘；可他毫不怀疑：只要合乎此人的盘算，他是撒得出来的。

===Original===
French felt that it was unsatisfactory to leave the matter in this state, and he presently thought of a simple subterfuge whereby it might be cleared up. It was almost the lunch hour, a suitable time for putting his project into operation. He hurried back to the Rue des Tanneurs, and turning into a café nearly opposite Messrs. Merkel & Lowenthal’s premises, ordered a bock and selected a seat from which he could observe the office door.

===Chinese===
弗伦奇觉得，事情撂在这个状态未免不甘，不多时便想出一条简单的小计策，或许能把它弄个水落石出。此刻将近午饭时分，正宜行事。他赶紧回到制革匠街，拐进一家几乎正对默克尔-洛温塔尔商行门面的咖啡馆，要了一杯博克啤酒，拣了个看得住那事务所大门的座位。

===Original===
He was only just in time. He had not taken his place five minutes when he saw M. Lowenthal emerge and walk off towards the center of the town. Three men clerks and the two rapid-looking typists followed, and lastly there appeared the person for whom he was waiting﻿—the sharp-looking office boy who had attended to him earlier in the day.

===Chinese===
他来得恰是时候。落座还不到五分钟，就见洛温塔尔先生出了门，朝市中心方向去了。三名男职员和那两名模样轻佻的打字员鱼贯而出，末了现身的，正是他守候的那位——白天接待过他的那个机灵相的办公室小伙计。

===Original===
The boy turned off in the opposite direction to his principal﻿—towards a quarter inhabited by laborers and artisans, and French, getting up from his table, slipped quietly out of the café and followed him.

===Chinese===
那小伙计同他东家背道而驰，拐向工人和手艺人聚居的一片街区。弗伦奇从座位上起身，悄悄溜出咖啡馆，跟了上去。

===Original===
The chase continued for some ten minutes, when the quarry disappeared into a small house in a back street. French strolled up and down until some half an hour later the young fellow reappeared. As he approached French allowed a look of recognition and slight surprise to appear on his features.

===Chinese===
跟踪持续了约莫十分钟，目标便钻进了后街一座小屋。弗伦奇踱来踱去地守着，约莫半个钟头后，小伙子重新露面。待他走近，弗伦奇让脸上浮出一副认出对方、略带惊讶的神情。

===Original===
“Ah,” he said, pausing with a friendly smile, “you are the clerk who attended to me this morning in Messrs. Merkel & Lowenthal’s office, are you not? A piece of luck meeting you! I wonder if you could give me a piece of information? I forgot to ask it of M. Lowenthal this morning, and as I am in a hurry, it would be worth five francs to me not to have to go back to your office.”

===Chinese===
“啊，”他停住脚，含着友好的笑容说道，“你就是今早在默克尔-洛温塔尔商行事务所里接待过我的那位职员，对不对？碰见你可真巧！不知你能不能给我指点一件事？今早我忘了问洛温塔尔先生，眼下又赶时间，若能省得回贵行跑一趟，五个法郎我是乐意的。”

===Original===
The youth’s eyes had brightened at the suggestion of financial dealings, and French felt he would learn all the other could tell him. He therefore continued without waiting for a reply.

===Chinese===
一听有金钱交易，小伙计的眼睛顿时亮了。弗伦奇觉得，对方能说的，自己全能听得到，于是不等答话，径直往下说。

===Original===
“The thing is this: I am joining my friend, M. Dangle, aboard the *L’Escaut* at the first opportunity. It was arranged between us that one of us should take with him a couple of dozen of champagne. I want to know whether he took the stuff, or whether I am to. Can you help me at all?”

===Chinese===
“事情是这样：一有机会，我就要上莱斯考号去会我的朋友丹格尔先生。我们俩讲定了，由一个人带上两打香槟。我想知道，那批货究竟是他带了，还是该由我来带。你能不能帮个忙？”

===Original===
The clerk’s English, though fairly good, was not quite equal to such a strain, and French had to repeat himself less idiomatically. But the boy grasped his meaning at last, and then at once dashed his hopes by saying he had never heard of any M. Dangle.

===Chinese===
小伙计的英语虽说还不错，应付这样的话却有点吃力，弗伦奇只得换一套少些惯用语的说法重讲一遍。那小伙计总算弄懂了他的意思，可紧接着一句话就教他的指望落了空：他从没听说过有什么丹格尔先生。

===Original===
“There he is,” French went on, producing his photograph. “You must have seen him scores of times.”

===Chinese===
“喏，就是他，”弗伦奇说着，掏出照片。“你准见过他几十回了。”

===Original===
And then French got the reward of his pertinacity. A look of recognition passed over the clerk’s features, and he made a gesture of comprehension.

===Chinese===
这一下，弗伦奇的锲而不舍得了报偿。小伙计脸上掠过一丝认出来的神色，做了一个会意的手势。

===Original===
“*Mais oui, m’sieur*; yes, sir,” he answered quickly, “but that is not M. Danggalle. I know him: it is M. Charles.”

===Chinese===
“*Mais oui, m’sieur*（是的，先生）；对，先生，”他飞快地答道，“可那不是当加勒先生。这个人我认得：他是夏尔先生（M. Charles）。”

===Original===
“That’s right,” French returned, trying to keep the triumph out of his voice. “His name is Dangle Charles. I know him as M. Dangle, because he is one of four brothers at our works. But of course he would give his name here as M. Charles. But now, can you tell me anything about the champagne?”

===Chinese===
“这就对了，”弗伦奇应道，竭力不让话音里透出得意。“他名叫丹格尔·夏尔。我平日叫他丹格尔先生，因为在我们厂里，他是丹格尔家四兄弟之一。不过到了这边，他自然只报夏尔先生的名字。那么现在——香槟的事，你能告诉我些什么吗？”

===Original===
The clerk shook his head. He had not known upon what business M. Charles had called at the office.

===Chinese===
小伙计摇了摇头。夏尔先生为着什么事到事务所来，他并不知道。

===Original===
“Oh, well, it can’t be helped,” French declared. “I thought that perhaps when he was in with you last Wednesday you might have heard something about it. You don’t know what luggage he took aboard the *L’Escaut*?”

===Chinese===
“唉，那也没法子，”弗伦奇说道。“我原想着，上星期三他在你们里间的当口，你们兴许听到过一句半句呢。他带上莱斯考号的行李是些什么，你也不知道么？”

===Original===
The clerk had not been aware that M. Charles had embarked on the freighter, still less did he know of what his luggage had consisted.
