## XVIII A Visitor from India / 十八 印度来的访客

===Original===
When French reached Victoria, the first person he saw on the platform was Maxwell Cheyne.

===Chinese===
弗伦奇到达维多利亚车站时，在月台上看见的第一个人就是麦克斯韦·切恩。

===Original===
“They told me at the Yard that you might be on this train,” the young man said excitedly as he elbowed his way forward. “Any news? Anything about Miss Merrill?”

===Chinese===
“苏格兰场的人告诉我，您可能就在这班车上。”年轻人一边往前挤，一边激动地说。“有消息吗？有梅里尔小姐的音信吗？”

===Original===
He looked old and worn, and it was evident that his anxiety was telling on him. In his eagerness he could scarcely wait for the Inspector to dismount from his carriage, and his loud tones were attracting curious looks from the bystanders.

===Chinese===
他显得苍老憔悴，焦虑分明正折磨着他。他心急火燎，几乎等不及探长走下车厢，大嗓门引得旁边的人纷纷侧目。

===Original===
“Get a taxi,” French answered quietly. “We can talk there.”

===Chinese===
“去叫一辆出租汽车。”弗伦奇轻声答道。“咱们在车上谈。”

===Original===
A few seconds later they found a vehicle, and Cheyne, gripping the other by the arm, went on earnestly:

===Chinese===
几秒钟后两人便找到了车，切恩一把攥住对方的胳膊，恳切地接着说：

===Original===
“Tell me. I can see you have learned something. Is she﻿—all right?”

===Chinese===
“快告诉我。我看得出您打听到消息了。她——她还好吗？”

===Original===
“I got news of her on Thursday last. She was all right then, though still under the influence of a drug. The whole party has gone to sea.”

===Chinese===
“上星期四我得到了她的消息。那时她安然无恙，只是还受着药物的影响。那一伙人全都出海去了。”

===Original===
“To sea?”

===Chinese===
“出海？”

===Original===
“Yes, to sea in a small tramp. I don’t know what they are up to, but there is no reason to suppose Miss Merrill is otherwise than well. Probably they took her with them to prevent her giving them away. They would drug her to get her to go along, but would cease it as soon as she was on board. I wired for inquiries to be made at the different signal stations, and news may be waiting for us at the Yard.”

===Chinese===
“是的，乘一条小货轮出海。他们想干什么勾当，我不知道，可也没有理由认为梅里尔小姐有什么不妥。多半是怕她说出去坏了他们的事，才把她一起带走的。起初要她跟着走，会给她下药；等她一上船，药就会停了。我已经拍电报叫各个信号站去查访，说不定此刻苏格兰场里正有消息等着我们。”

===Original===
A few seconds sufficed to put Cheyne in possession of the salient facts which French had learned, and the latter in his turn asked for news.

===Chinese===
不消几秒钟，切恩便掌握了弗伦奇查明的那些要点，接着就轮到他来讲这边的消息了。

===Original===
“By Jove, yes!” Cheyne cried, “there is news. You remember that Arnold Price had disappeared? Well, yesterday I had a letter from him!”

===Chinese===
“天哪，可不是！”切恩喊道，“真有消息。您还记得阿诺德·普赖斯失踪的事吧？嘿，昨天我收到他一封信！”

===Original===
“You don’t say so?” French rejoined in surprise. “Where did he write from?”

===Chinese===
“竟有这事？”弗伦奇吃惊地应道。“他从哪儿写来的？”

===Original===
“Bombay. He was shortly leaving for home. He expects to be here in about a month.”

===Chinese===
“孟买。他不久就要动身回国，预计一个月左右到这儿。”

===Original===
“And what about his disappearance?”

===Chinese===
“那他失踪是怎么回事？”

===Original===
“He was ill in hospital. He had gone up to Agra on some private business and met with an accident﻿—was knocked down in the street and was insensible for ages. He couldn’t say who he was, and the hospital people in Agra couldn’t find out, and he hadn’t told the Bombay people where he was going to spend his leave.”

===Chinese===
“他病倒进了医院。他为一点私事上阿格拉（Agra）去，出了意外——在街上被撞倒，人事不省了老半天。他说不清自己是谁，阿格拉医院里的人查不出来，他又没告诉孟买的人自己要去哪里休假。”

===Original===
“Did he mention the letter?”

===Chinese===
“他提到那封信了吗？”

===Original===
“Yes, he thanked me for taking charge of it and said that when he reached home he would relieve me of further trouble about it. He little knows!”

===Chinese===
“提了。他谢我代为保管，还说一到家，就把我从这桩麻烦里解脱出去。他还蒙在鼓里呢！”

===Original===
“That’s so,” French assented.

===Chinese===
“可不是。”弗伦奇深以为然。

===Original===
Their taxi had been held up by a block at the end of Westminster Bridge, but now the mass cleared and in a few seconds they reached the Yard.

===Chinese===
他们的车在威斯敏斯特桥桥头被车流堵住，这时前面疏通了，不多几秒钟便到了苏格兰场。

===Original===
French’s first care was to get rid of Cheyne. He repeated what he had learned about Joan Merrill, then, assuring him that the key of the matter lay in the cipher, he advised him to go home and try it once more. Directly any more news came in he would let him know.

===Chinese===
弗伦奇头一件事是把切恩打发走。他把关于琼·梅里尔的消息重述了一遍，然后向他担保，全案的钥匙就在那份密码里，劝他回家再去试它一试。一有新的消息，他就会通知他。

===Original===
Cheyne having reluctantly taken his departure, French made inquiries as to what had been done in reference to his telephone from Antwerp. It appeared that the Yard had not been idle. In the first place an application had been made to the Moroccan Government, who had replied that no ship had been chartered by them for freight at Casablanca, nor was anything known of agricultural samples for the Italian market. Lowenthal’s story must therefore have been an absolute fabrication. He had, however, told it so readily that French suspected it had been made up beforehand, so as to be ready to serve up to any inquisitive policeman or detective who might come along.

===Chinese===
切恩一步三回头地走了。弗伦奇便着手查问：他从安特卫普打来电话之后，这边都办了些什么。看来苏格兰场没有闲着。头一件，已经向摩洛哥政府提出查询，对方答复说，他们并未在卡萨布兰卡租船运货，也不知道有什么运往意大利市场的农业样品。可见洛温塔尔那套说辞纯属捏造。不过他讲得那么现成，弗伦奇倒疑心那套话是事先编好的，专等随便哪个爱刨根问底的警察或侦探找上门来，就端出来应付。

===Original===
Next Lloyd’s had been approached, as to the direction the *L’Escaut* had taken, and a reply had shortly before come in from them. It stated that up to noon on that day, the vessel had not been reported from any of their stations. But this, French realized, might not mean so much. If she had gone south down the English Channel it would have been well on to dark before she reached the Straits of Dover. In any case, had she wished to slip through unseen, she had only to keep out to the middle of the passage, when in ordinary weather she would have been invisible from either coast. On the other hand, had she gone north, she would almost naturally have kept out of sight of land. It was true that in either case she would have been likely to pass some other vessel which would have spoken her, and the fact that no news of such a recognition had come to hand seemed to indicate that she was taking some unusual course out of the track of regular shipping.

===Chinese===
第二件，已经向劳合社（Lloyd’s）查询莱斯考号驶往哪个方向，对方不久前刚作了答复：截至当天正午，他们的各处分站都没有报告见到这艘船。不过弗伦奇明白，这也未必说明什么。她若是沿英吉利海峡南下，驶到多佛海峡时天已擦黑。况且，她真想悄悄溜过去，只消走航道正中，天气平常的日子里，两岸都望不见她。反过来，她若是北上，那也自然会离岸远远的，不叫陆地上望见。诚然，无论南下北上，她都可能遇上别的船，同对方互通名号；而这种相遇的消息一条也没传来，看来倒表明她走的是某条不寻常的路线，避开了正规航线。

===Original===
French wired this information to the Antwerp police, and then, his chief being disengaged, went in and gave him a detailed account of his adventures in Belgium.

===Chinese===
弗伦奇把这些情况电告安特卫普警方，随后趁上司得空，进去把自己在比利时的经历详细报告了一遍。

===Original===
Chief Inspector Mitchell was impressed by the story. He sat back in his chair and treated French to a prolonged stare as the latter talked. At the end of the recital he remained sitting motionless for some moments, whistling gently below his breath.

===Chinese===
米切尔总探长听了这个故事，颇为动容。弗伦奇讲述的当口，他仰靠在椅子里，久久盯着他看。听罢，他又一动不动地坐了片刻，压着嗓子轻轻吹口哨。

===Original===
“Any theories?” he said at last.

===Chinese===
“有什么高见？”他终于开口。

===Original===
French shook his head.

===Chinese===
弗伦奇摇了摇头。

===Original===
“Well, no, sir,” he answered slowly. “It’s not easy to see what they’re after. And it’s not easy to see, either, why the whole gang wanted to go. It looked at first as if they were just clearing out because of Cheyne’s coming to the Yard, but it’s more than that. The arrangements were made too long ago. They have been dealing with that Antwerp firm for several weeks.”

===Chinese===
“唔，没有，先生。”他慢吞吞地答道。“他们图的是什么，不容易看清。还有，为什么全伙人都要走，也不容易看清。起初像是切恩上了苏格兰场，他们索性一走了之，可事情不止如此。那些安排动手得太早了。他们同那家安特卫普商行打交道，已经打了好几个星期。”

===Original===
“The hard copper was all a story?”

===Chinese===
“硬铜那一套全是编的？”

===Original===
“Looks like it, sir. As a matter of fact every single statement those men made that could be tested has been proved false. Even when there didn’t seem any great object in a yarn they pitched it. Lies seemed to come easier to them.”

===Chinese===
“看样子是的，先生。事实上，那伙人说的话，凡是可以查证的，一查全是假的。有时编个瞎话看不出有多大的用处，他们也照样编。撒谎对他们来说，张口就来。”

===Original===
“Well, I’ve known a good few cases of that, and so have you, French. It’s a habit that grows. Now, what’s your next move?”

===Chinese===
“唔，这种情形我见过不少，你也见过，弗伦奇。撒谎这习惯是越养越深的。说吧，下一步你打算怎么办？”

===Original===
French hesitated.

===Chinese===
弗伦奇迟疑了一下。

===Original===
“For the moment the outlook’s not very cheery,” he said at last. “All the same I can’t believe that boat can go away out of the Scheldt and disappear. In my judgment she’s bound to be reported before long, and I’m looking forward to getting word of her within the next day or so. Then I have no doubt that the tracing is some kind of cipher, and if we could read it we should probably get light on the whole affair.”

===Chinese===
“眼下的前景不算乐观。”他终于说道。“可我不信那船能驶出斯海尔德河，就此无影无踪。依我判断，早晚会有人报告她的行踪，我指望这一两天就能得到消息。再者，我毫不怀疑那份描图是某种密码，只要能读通它，全案多半就能见到亮了。”

===Original===
“Why shouldn’t you read it? Try it again.”

===Chinese===
“你怎么就读不通呢？再试试。”

===Original===
“I intend to, sir. But I don’t hope for much result, because I don’t believe we’ve got the genuine document. I don’t believe they would have handed it, nor a copy of it either, to a man they intended to murder, lest it should be found on his body. I’d state long odds they gave him a fake.”

===Chinese===
“我打算再试，先生。可我不抱多大指望，因为我们手里这份，我不信是真件。他们存心要害死一个人，就不会把原件交给他，连抄件也不会——就怕在他尸身上搜出来。要说他们给了他一件假货，我肯押重注。”

===Original===
“I think you’re probably right,” the chief admitted. “Try at all events. You never know your luck.”

===Chinese===
“我想你多半说得对。”上司承认道。“无论如何试一试。运气这东西，谁也说不准。”

===Original===
He bent over his desk, and French, realizing that the interview had come to an end, quietly left the room. Then, seeing there was nothing requiring his attention urgently, and tired after his journey, he went home.

===Chinese===
上司俯身回到案头。弗伦奇看出谈话已经到头，便悄悄退了出去。他见没有急事等着自己办，加之旅途劳顿，就回家去了。

===Original===
But contrary to his expectations, the next day passed without any news of the *L’Escaut*, and the next, and many days after that. Nor could all his efforts with the tracing throw any light on that mysterious document. As time passed he began to grow more and more despondent, and the fear that he was going to make a mess of the case grew steadily stronger. In vain he laid his difficulties before his wife. For once that final source of inspiration failed him. Mrs. French did not take even one illuminating notion. When the third week had gone by, something akin to despair seized upon the Inspector. The only possibility of hope now seemed to lie in the return of Arnold Price, and French began counting the days until his arrival.

===Chinese===
可是与他的期望相反，第二天过去了，莱斯考号毫无消息；第三天又过去，此后一连许多天也是如此。他在描图上使尽浑身解数，也没能让那份神秘文书透出半点亮光。日子一天天过去，他越来越消沉，生怕这桩案子要砸在自己手里，这恐惧也一天强似一天。他把难处摊给妻子看，也是枉然。这最后一处灵感的源头，破天荒头一回没了用场——弗伦奇太太连半点有启发的念头也没能想出来。第三个星期过完，一种近乎绝望的情绪攫住了探长。如今唯一的一点指望，似乎就落在阿诺德·普赖斯回国这件事上，弗伦奇掰着指头数他到来的日子。

===Original===
One night about three weeks after his return from Belgium he settled down with a cigar after dinner, his thoughts running in their familiar groove: What were these people engaged on? Was there any way in which he could find out? Had he overlooked any evidence or any inquiries? Had he neglected any possible line of research?

===Chinese===
从比利时回来约莫三个星期后的一个晚上，他饭后点上一支雪茄坐定下来，思绪又淌进那道熟辙：这伙人究竟在干什么？有什么法子查出来？有没有漏掉什么证据、什么查访？有没有忽略什么可行的研究路子？

===Original===
The more he considered the affair in all its bearings, the more conscious he became of the soundness of the advice he had given to Cheyne, and which in his turn he had received from his chief. Unquestionably in the tracing lay the solution he required, and once again he racked his brains to see if he could not by any means devise a way to read its message.

===Chinese===
这件事的方方面面，他越琢磨，越觉得自己给切恩的那条建议——也就是他得自上司的那条——确有道理。毫无疑问，他要的答案就藏在描图里，于是他再一次绞尽脑汁，看能不能想出什么法子，把其中的信息读出来。

===Original===
On this point he concentrated, going over and over again everything he had learned about it. For perhaps an hour he remained motionless in his chair, while the smoke from his cigar curled up and slowly dissolved into the blue haze with which the room was becoming obscured. And then suddenly he sat up and with a dawning, tremulous eagerness considered an idea which had just leaped into his mind.

===Chinese===
他把心思全集中在这上头，把已知的一切翻来覆去过了又过。约莫一个钟头，他在椅子里一动不动，雪茄的烟袅袅升起，缓缓融进渐渐蒙住全屋的蓝色雾霭里。蓦地，他坐直身子，带着初生的、微微发颤的兴奋，捉住刚刚跳进脑海的一个念头。

===Original===
He had suddenly remembered a statement made by Cheyne when he was giving his first rather incoherent account of his adventures. The young man said that it had been arranged between himself and Joan Merrill that if either were lucky enough to get the tracing into his or her possession, the first thing he or she would do would be to photograph it. Now, in juxtaposition with that statement, French recalled the facts, first, that Joan must have reached her flat on the night of her abduction at least several minutes before Blessington and Sime arrived with their car; and secondly, that during those minutes she had the tracing with her﻿—the genuine tracing, as there was every reason to believe. *Had Joan photographed it?*

===Chinese===
他猛然记起，切恩头一回语无伦次地讲述自己历险时说过的一句话。年轻人说，他和琼·梅里尔事先讲定：两人不管哪一个，只要有幸把描图弄到手，头一件事就是把它拍下来。把这句话同几件事实并到一处，弗伦奇想起来了：第一，琼被劫走的那天夜里，她回到寓所，必定比布莱辛顿和赛姆乘车赶到早至少好几分钟；第二，就在那几分钟里，描图正在她身上——而且正如种种理由所示，那是真描图。*琼把它拍下来了吗？*

===Original===
French was overwhelmed with amazement and chagrin at his failure to think of this point before, nor could he acquit Cheyne of a like astounding stupidity. For himself he felt there was no excuse whatever. He had even specially noticed the girl’s camera and the flashlight apparatus which she used for her architectural details when he was searching her rooms, but he had then, and since then up till this moment, entirely and completely forgotten the arrangement made between the partners.

===Chinese===
竟一直没有想到这一层，弗伦奇又是惊愕又是懊恼；切恩同样糊涂得惊人，他想不出该怎样替他开脱。至于他自己，更没有任何借口。他搜查那姑娘房间的时候，还特别留意过她的照相机，以及她拍建筑细部用的闪光灯设备，可当时——此后直到此刻——他把两位合伙人之间的这个约定忘得干干净净。

===Original===
Late as it was, French decided to go then and there to ascertain the point. The key of Joan’s flat was at the Yard, and twenty minutes later he had obtained it and was in a taxi bowling towards Horne Terrace.

===Chinese===
夜虽然深了，弗伦奇还是决定当即就去查明这一点。琼那套寓所的钥匙存在苏格兰场，二十分钟后，他已领到钥匙，坐上出租汽车，向霍恩台一路疾驰而去。

===Original===
He kept the vehicle while he ran up the ten flights to No. 12 and secured the camera. Then hastening down, he was driven back to the Yard.

===Chinese===
他让车等着，自己一口气跑上十段楼梯到 12 室，取到那架照相机，随即匆匆奔下楼，坐车回到苏格兰场。

===Original===
By a piece of good luck he found a photographer who had been delayed by other important work, and him he pressed into the service forthwith. With some grumbling the man returned to his dark room. French, too eager to await his report, accompanying him.

===Chinese===
也是运气好，他找到一位被别的重要活计耽搁住的摄影师，立刻把这人拉来当差。那人嘟嘟囔囔地回了暗房，弗伦奇等不及听回话，也跟了进去。

===Original===
A few moments sufficed to settle the question. The camera contained a roll of films of which the first seven had been exposed, and a short immersion in the developer showed that numbers 5, 6, and 7 bore the hoped for impress.

===Chinese===
不消片刻，问题就见了分晓。相机里有一卷胶卷，头七张已经曝光，在显影液里略一浸，便看出第 5、6、7 三张正带着盼望中的影像。

===Original===
Gone was French’s despondency and the weariness caused by his heavy day, and instead he was once more the embodiment of enthusiasm and cheery optimism. He had it now! At last the secret was within his grasp! Of his ability to read the message, now that he was sure he had the genuine one, he had no doubt. He had always liked working out ciphers, and since he had succeeded in extracting the hidden meaning from the stock and share list which had been sent to the elusive Mrs. X in the Gething murder case, his belief in his own powers had become almost an obsession. He could hardly restrain his eagerness to get to grips with this new problem until the negatives should be dry and prints made.

===Chinese===
弗伦奇的沮丧不见了，一整天的劳累也烟消云散，他重新变回了热情与快活乐观的化身。到手了！秘密终于攥在他掌心里了！既然确信拿到的是真件，他对自己读通信息的能力毫不怀疑。他素来喜欢破解密码，自从在格辛凶案里从寄给那位遍寻不获的X太太的股票行情表中破出隐藏含义之后，他对自身本领的信心几乎成了一种执念。底片要晾干、照片要印出才能动手，他好不容易才按捺住扑向这个新难题的急切。

===Original===
The photographer was able to promise these for the following day, and till then French had to possess his soul in patience. But on his return from lunch he found on his desk three excellent prints of the document.

===Chinese===
摄影师答应第二天把这些交出来，在那之前，弗伦奇只好按捺着性子苦等。可他吃过午饭回来，就见桌上摆着三张印得极好的文书照片。

===Original===
They were only half-plate size, or about one-third that of the tracing which had been given to Cheyne. He therefore instructed the photographer to prepare enlargements which would bring the document up to more nearly the size of the original. These were ready before it was time for him to leave for home, and he sat down with ill-controlled excitement to compare them with the document at which he had already spent so much time.

===Chinese===
照片只有半版（half-plate）大，约合交给切恩的那份描图的三分之一。于是他吩咐摄影师加印放大的，把这份文书放大到接近原件的尺寸。还没到他该回家的时候，放大件已经备齐，他怀着按捺不住的激动坐下来，把它同那份他已耗去许多工夫的文书细加比对。

===Original===
