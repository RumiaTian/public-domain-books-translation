## XXXVII The Mob / XXXVII 暴民

===Original===
When Emma, Bertie’s child, came home after a two years’ course of study, she had passed from girlhood to young womanhood. She was white, and sandy-haired. She was not beautiful, and she appeared to be fragile; but she also looked sweet and good, with that peculiar innocence which peers out upon the world with calm, round eyes and sees no evil, but does methodically its simple, everyday work. Zora mothered her, Miss Smith found her plenty to do, and Bles thought her a good girl. But Mrs. Cresswell found her perfect, and began to scheme to marry her off. For Mary Cresswell, with the restlessness and unhappiness of an unemployed woman, was trying to atone for her former blunders.

===Chinese===
伯蒂的孩子埃玛，两年学业后回家时，已从少女长成了青年女子。她是白人，沙色头发。她算不上美，看着还显得纤弱；可她也显得甜美、善良，带着那种特有的天真——用一双沉静的圆眼睛望着这世界，看不见邪恶，只本本分分地做着简单日常的活计。佐拉像母亲一样照管她，史密斯小姐给她找了许多事做，布莱斯觉得她是个好姑娘。可克雷斯韦尔太太觉得她完美，开始盘算着把她嫁出去。因为玛丽·克雷斯韦尔，带着一个无所事事的女人的那种烦躁和不快，正设法为自己从前的过失赎罪。

===Original===
Her humiliation after the episode at Cresswell Oaks had been complete. It seemed to her that the original cause of her whole life punishment lay in her persistent misunderstanding of the black people and their problem. Zora appeared to her in a new and glorified light﻿—a vigorous, self-sacrificing woman. She knew that Zora had refused to marry Bles, and this again seemed fitting. Zora was not meant for marrying; she was a born leader, wedded to a great cause; she had long outgrown the boy and girl affection. She was the sort of woman she herself might have been if she had not married.

===Chinese===
橡树庄那一桩之后，她的屈辱可谓到了极点。在她看来，她一生受罚的根源，就在于她一贯地误解了黑人及其问题。佐拉在她眼里显现出一种新的、升华了的光彩——一个生气勃勃、自我牺牲的女子。她知道佐拉拒绝了布莱斯的求婚，而这似乎也恰如其分。佐拉不是为婚姻而生的；她天生是领袖，与一桩大事业结了婚；她早已长过了少男少女的那份情。她正是那种女人——倘若她自己不曾结婚，本可以成为的那种女人。

===Original===
Alwyn, on the other hand, needed a wife; he was a great, virile boy, requiring a simple, affectionate mate. No sooner did she see Emma than she was sure that this was the ideal wife. She compared herself with Helen Cresswell. Helen was a contented wife and mother because she was fitted for the position, and happy in it; while she who had aimed so high had fallen piteously. From such a fate she would save Zora and Bles.
Emma’s course in nurse-training had been simple and short and there was no resident physician; but Emma, in her unemotional way, was a born nurse and did much good among the sick in the neighborhood. Zora had a small log hospital erected with four white beds, a private room, and an office which was also Emma’s bedroom. The new white physician in town, just fresh from school in Atlanta, became interested and helped with advice and suggestions.

===Chinese===
而阿尔文，需要一个妻子；他是个高大、健壮的男子，需要一个简单、深情的伴侣。她一见埃玛，便断定这就是理想的妻子。她拿自己与海伦·克雷斯韦尔相比。海伦是个知足的妻子和母亲，因为她适合那个位置，并在其中得到快乐；而她，目标那样高，却凄惨地跌了下来。这样的命运，她要替佐拉和布莱斯免去。
埃玛受的护士训练简单而短暂，这里也没有驻院医生；可埃玛，以她那不动声色的方式，是个天生的护士，在四邻的病人中做了许多好事。佐拉盖了一座小小的圆木医院，有四张白床、一间单人房、一间办公室——这办公室也是埃玛的卧室。镇上新来的白人医生，刚从亚特兰大的学校毕业，起了兴趣，常常来出主意、提建议。

===Original===
Meantime John Taylor’s troubles began to increase. Under the old political regime it had been an easy matter to avoid serious damage-suits for the accidents in the mill. Much child labor and the lack of protective devices made accidents painfully frequent. Taylor insisted that the chief cause was carelessness, while the mill hands alleged criminal neglect on his part. When the new labor officials took charge of the court and the break occurred between Colonel Cresswell and his son-in-law, Taylor found that several damage-suits were likely to cost him a considerable sum.

===Chinese===
与此同时，约翰·泰勒的麻烦开始增多。在旧的政治体制下，要躲开厂里事故的巨额索赔，本是件容易事。大量童工，加上缺少防护装置，使事故频繁得令人痛心。泰勒坚持说，主要原因是工人不小心，而厂工们则指控他犯了刑事疏忽。当新上台的劳工官员掌管了法庭、克雷斯韦尔上校又与女婿决裂之后，泰勒发现，好几桩索赔官司很可能要叫他破一大笔财。

===Original===
He determined not to let the bad feelings go too far, and when a particularly distressing accident to a little girl took place, he showed more than his usual interest and offered to care for her. The new young physician recommended Zora’s infirmary as the only near place that offered a chance for the child’s recovery.
“Take her out,” Taylor promptly directed.
Zora was troubled when the child came. She knew the suspicious temper of the town whites. The very next day Taylor sent out a second case, a child who had been hurt some time before and was not recovering as she should.

===Chinese===
他决心不让这种恶感闹得太过，因此当一个小女孩出了尤其凄惨的事故时，他表现出超过往常的关切，主动提出要照料她。那位年轻的新医生推荐了佐拉的小医院，说那是附近唯一能让这孩子有康复机会的地方。
『把她送过去，』泰勒当即吩咐。
孩子送来时，佐拉心里不安。她深知镇上白人多疑的脾气。就在第二天，泰勒又送来了第二个病例，一个早先受了伤、却迟迟不见好的孩子。

===Original===
Under the care of the little hospital and the gentle nurse the children improved rapidly, and in two weeks were outdoors, playing with the little black children and even creeping into classrooms and listening. The grateful mothers came out twice a week at least; at first with suspicious aloofness, but gradually melting under Zora’s tact until they sat and talked with her and told their troubles and struggles. Zora realized how human they were, and how like their problems were to hers. They and their children grew to love this busy, thoughtful woman, and Zora’s fears were quieted.

===Chinese===
在小医院和温柔护士的照料下，孩子们好得很快，两周后已能到户外，和黑人小孩们一起玩耍，甚至溜进教室听课。感恩的母亲们至少每周来两次；起初带着多疑的疏离，但在佐拉的圆通之下渐渐融化，直到坐下来跟她聊天，诉说自己的烦恼和挣扎。佐拉意识到她们多么有人情味，她们的难题又与自己的何其相似。她们和她们的孩子，渐渐爱上了这个忙碌、周到的女子，佐拉的忧虑也平息了。

===Original===
The catastrophe came suddenly. The sheriff rode by, scowling and hunting for some poor black runaway, when he saw white children in the Negro school and white women, whom he knew were mill-hands, looking on. He was black with anger; turning he galloped back to town. A few hours later the young physician arrived hastily in a cab to take the women and children to town. He said something in a low tone to Zora and drove away, frowning.
Zora came quickly to the school and asked for Alwyn. He was in the barn and she hurried there.
“Bles,” she said quietly, “it is reported that a Toomsville mob will burn the school tonight.”

===Chinese===
灾难来得突然。警长骑马经过，皱着眉、正搜捕哪个可怜的黑人逃奴，这时他看见黑人学校里有白人孩子，还有他认得是厂工的白人女子在旁观看。他气得脸发黑；一转身，纵马奔回镇上。几小时后，那位年轻医生匆匆乘马车赶来，把那几个女子和孩子接回镇上。他低声对佐拉说了句什么，皱着眉，驾车走了。
佐拉快步赶到学校，找阿尔文。他在谷仓里，她急忙赶过去。
『布莱斯，』她平静地说，『有消息说，图姆斯维尔的暴民今夜要来烧学校。』

===Original===
Bles stood motionless.
“I’ve been fearing it. The sheriff has been stirring up the worst elements in the town lately and the mills pay off tonight.”
“Well,” she said quietly, “we must prepare.”
He looked at her, his face aglow with admiration.
“You wonder-woman!” he exclaimed softly.
A moment they regarded each other. She saw the love in his eyes, and he saw rising in hers something that made his heart bound. But she turned quickly away.
“You must hurry, Bles; lives are at stake.” And in another moment he thundered out of the barn on the black mare.

===Chinese===
布莱斯一动不动地站着。
『我一直担心这个。警长这些日子一直在煽动镇上最坏的那拨人，而今晚厂里发工钱。』
『那么，』她平静地说，『我们得做准备。』
他看着她，满脸敬佩的光彩。
『你这奇女子！』他轻声叫。
他们对视了片刻。她看见他眼里的爱，而他看见她眼里升起某种东西，令他的心猛跳了一下。但她很快转过身。
『你得赶快，布莱斯；人命关天。』转眼间，他便策马冲出谷仓，骑的是那匹黑马母马。

===Original===
Along the pike he flew and up the plantation roads. Across broad fields and back again, over to the Barton pike and along the swamp. At every cabin he whispered a word, and left behind him grey faces and whispering children.
His horse was reeking with sweat as he staggered again into the schoolyard; but already the people were gathering, with frightened, anxious, desperate faces. Women with bundles and children, men with guns, tottering old folks, wide-eyed boys and girls. Up from the swamp land came the children crying and moaning. The sun was setting. The women and children hurried into the school building, closing the doors and windows.

===Chinese===
他沿着大路飞驰，奔上种植园的小径。穿过宽阔的田野又折回来，越过巴顿大路，沿着沼泽。每一座小屋前他都低语一句，留在他身后的是一张张灰白的脸和窃窃低语的孩子。
他的马汗出如浆、摇摇晃晃地又冲进校园时，人们已开始聚拢，一张张惊恐、焦灼、绝望的脸。抱着包袱和孩子的女人，扛枪的男人，颤巍巍的老人，瞪大眼的少男少女。从沼泽地那边上来的是哭号的孩子。太阳正在落下。妇女和孩子急忙涌进校舍，关上门窗。

===Original===
A moment Alwyn stood without and looked back. The world was peaceful. He could hear the whistle of birds and the sobbing of the breeze in the shadowing oaks. The sky was flashing to dull and purplish blue, and over all lay the twilight hush as though God did not care.
He threw back his head and clenched his hands. His soul groaned within him. “Heavenly Father, was man ever before set to such a task?” Fight? God! if he could but fight! If he could but let go the elemental passions that were leaping and gathering and burning in the eyes of yonder caged and desperate black men.

===Chinese===
阿尔文在外面站了一会儿，回头望去。世界一片宁静。他听得见鸟的啼鸣、微风在投下阴影的橡树间的呜咽。天空闪着一种暗淡的、发紫的蓝，暮色的寂静笼罩万物，仿佛上帝并不在乎。
他仰起头，攥紧双拳。他的灵魂在体内呻吟。『天父，人可曾有过被置于这般境地的吗？』战斗？老天！要是能战斗就好了！要是能放开那些原始的激情就好了——它们正在那边那些被困的、绝望的黑人眼中跳跃、聚拢、燃烧。

===Original===
But his hands were tied﻿—manacled. One desperate struggle, a whirl of blood, and the whole world would rise to crush him and his people. The white operator in yonder town had but to flash the news, “Negroes killing whites,” to bring all the country, all the State, all the nation, to red vengeance. It mattered not what the provocation, what the desperate cause.
The door suddenly opened behind him and he wheeled around.
“Zora!” he whispered.
“Bles,” she answered softly, and they went silently in to their people.

===Chinese===

===Original===
A hundred or more shots rang out; then slowly and silently, the mass of women and men were sucked into the streets of the town, leaving but black eddies on the corners to throw backward glances toward the bare, towering pine where swung two red and awful things. The pale boy-face of one, with soft brown eyes glared up sightless to the sun; the dead, leathered bronze of the other was carved in piteous terror.
