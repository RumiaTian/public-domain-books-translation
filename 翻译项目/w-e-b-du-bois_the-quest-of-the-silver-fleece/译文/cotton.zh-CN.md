## VI Cotton / VI 棉花

===Original===
The cry of the naked was sweeping the world. From the peasant toiling in Russia, the lady lolling in London, the chieftain burning in Africa, and the Eskimo freezing in Alaska; from long lines of hungry men, from patient sad-eyed women, from old folk and creeping children went up the cry, “Clothes, clothes!” Far away the wide black land that belts the South, where Miss Smith worked and Miss Taylor drudged and Bles and Zora dreamed, the dense black land sensed the cry and heard the bound of answering life within the vast dark breast. All that dark earth heaved in mighty travail with the bursting bolls of the cotton while black attendant earth spirits swarmed above, sweating and crooning to its birth pains.

===Chinese===
赤身露体之人的呼喊正席卷世界。从俄国劳作的农人、伦敦闲倚的贵妇、非洲烈火中的酋长、阿拉斯加冻僵的爱斯基摩人；从一排排饥饿的汉子、逆来顺受眼神悲苦的妇人、老人和蹒跚的孩子——都发出同一声呼喊：「衣服，衣服！」在遥远的地方，那条环抱南方的辽阔黑土地——史密斯小姐在那儿工作、泰勒小姐在那儿苦熬、布莱斯和佐拉在那儿做梦——那片浓黑的土地感应到了这呼喊，听见了它那广袤幽暗的胸膛里应答的生命搏动。那一整片暗色的大地，因棉铃的迸裂而剧烈阵痛、隆起，黑色的侍奉地灵们在上方成群飞舞，流着汗，哼着歌，为它的分娩之痛低吟。

===Original===
After the miracle of the bursting bolls, when the land was brightest with the piled mist of the Fleece, and when the cry of the naked was loudest in the mouths of men, a sudden cloud of workers swarmed between the Cotton and the Naked, spinning and weaving and sewing and carrying the Fleece and mining and minting and bringing the Silver till the Song of Service filled the world and the poetry of Toil was in the souls of the laborers. Yet ever and always there were tense silent white-faced men moving in that swarm who felt no poetry and heard no song, and one of these was John Taylor.
He was tall, thin, cold, and tireless and he moved among the Watchers of this World of Trade. In the rich Wall Street offices of Grey and Easterly, Brokers, Mr. Taylor, as chief and confidential clerk surveyed the world’s nakedness and the supply of cotton to clothe it. The object of his watching was frankly stated to himself and to his world. He purposed going into business neither for his own health nor for the healing or clothing of the peoples but to apply his knowledge of the world’s nakedness and of black men’s toil in such a way as to bring himself wealth. In this he was but following the teaching of his highest ideal, lately deceased, Mr. Job Grey.

===Chinese===
棉铃迸裂的奇迹发生之后，当大地因堆积如雾的羊毛而最为明亮、当赤身露体之人的呼喊在人们嘴中最为响亮之际，一团突如其来的工蜂般的劳动者涌到了棉花与赤身者之间，纺纱、织布、缝纫、运送羊毛，开矿、铸造、搬运白银，直到「服务的歌」充满世界，「劳作的诗」住进了劳动者的灵魂。然而在这一群里，永远有那么一些神情紧张、默不作声、脸色惨白的男人穿行其间，他们感觉不到诗、听不见歌——约翰·泰勒便是其中之一。
他高挑、消瘦、冷漠、不知疲倦，周旋于这贸易世界的「守望者」之间。在格雷与伊斯特利经纪行（Grey and Easterly, Brokers）那气派的华尔街办公室里，身为首席机要职员的泰勒先生，俯瞰着世人的赤裸与用以蔽体的棉花供给。他这番守望的目的，对自己、对世人都不加掩饰：他投身商界，既非为自身康健，也非为疗救或衣被万民，而是要把自己对世人的赤裸、对黑人之劳作的知识，用一种能给自己带来财富的方式施展出来。这一点上，他不过是秉承了他那位刚去世的最高典范——约伯·格雷先生（Mr. Job Grey）的教诲。

===Original===
Mr. Grey had so successfully manipulated the cotton market that while black men who made the cotton starved in Alabama and white men who bought it froze in Siberia, he himself sat﻿—

> “High on a throne of royal state
 That far outshone the wealth
 Of Ormuz or of Ind.”

Notwithstanding this he died eventually, leaving the burden of his wealth to his bewildered wife, and his business to the astute Mr. Easterly; not simply to Mr. Easterly, but in a sense to his spiritual heir, John Taylor.

> 「高踞于王者威仪的宝座，
 远胜奥尔穆兹与印度
 之富庶。」（注：引弥尔顿《失乐园》卷二，喻撒旦之奢靡）

尽管如此，他终究还是死了，把财富的重担留给不知所措的妻子，把生意留给精明的伊斯特利先生（Mr. Easterly）；不只是留给伊斯特利先生，在某种意义上，也是留给他精神上的继承人——约翰·泰勒。

===Original===
To be sure Mr. Taylor had but a modest salary and no financial interest in the business, but he had knowledge and business daring﻿—effrontery even﻿—and the determination was fixed in his mind to be a millionaire at no distant date. Some cautious fliers on the market gave him enough surplus to send his sister Mary through the high school of his country home in New Hampshire, and afterward through Wellesley College; although just why a woman should want to go through college was inexplicable to John Taylor, and he was still uncertain as to the wisdom of his charity.
When she had an offer to teach in the South, John Taylor hurried her off for two reasons: he was profoundly interested in the cotton-belt, and there she might be of service to him; and secondly, he had spent all the money on her that he intended to at present, and he wanted her to go to work. As an investment he did not consider Mary a success. Her letters intimated very strongly her intention not to return to Miss Smith’s School; but they also brought information﻿—disjointed and incomplete, to be sure﻿—which mightily interested Mr. Taylor and sent him to atlases, encyclopædias, and census-reports. When he went to that little lunch with old Mrs. Grey he was not sure that he wanted his sister to leave the cotton-belt just yet. After lunch he was sure that he did not want her to leave.

===Chinese===
诚然，泰勒先生不过领着一份不算丰厚的薪水，在这桩生意里也没有丝毫股份，可他有知识、有商界的胆识——甚至可以说是胆大妄为——而且下定决心，要在不久的将来成为一名百万富翁。他在市场上谨慎地试了几手，赚得了一点余钱，足够送妹妹玛丽上完新罕布什尔（New Hampshire）老家的高中，之后又读完了韦尔斯利学院；尽管女人究竟为什么要上大学，对约翰·泰勒来说始终是个谜，而他也仍拿不准自己这番慈善之举是否明智。
当她得到去南方教书的差事时，约翰·泰勒催她快去，原因有二：他对产棉区兴趣浓厚，她在那儿或许能为他效劳；其次，他在她身上已经花光了目前打算花的钱，他要她去自食其力。作为一笔投资，他不认为玛丽算成功。她的来信强烈暗示她无意再回史密斯小姐的学校；可这些信也带来了情报——诚然是零散而不完整的——却令泰勒先生极感兴趣，驱使他去翻地图册、百科全书和人口普查报告。他去赴老格雷太太那顿便饭午餐时，他还拿不准是否想让妹妹眼下就离开产棉区。午饭之后，他拿准了：他不想让她走。

===Original===
The rich Mrs. Grey was at the crisis of her fortunes. She was an elderly lady, in those uncertain years beyond fifty, and had been left suddenly with more millions than she could easily count. Personally she was inclined to spend her money in bettering the world right off, in such ways as might from time to time seem attractive. This course, to her husband’s former partner and present executor, Mr. Edward Easterly, was not only foolish but wicked, and, incidentally, distinctly unprofitable to him. He had expressed himself strongly to Mrs. Grey last night at dinner and had reinforced his argument by a pointed letter written this morning.
To John Taylor Mrs. Grey’s disposal of the income was unbelievable blasphemy against the memory of a mighty man. He did not put this in words to Mrs. Grey﻿—he was only head clerk in her late husband’s office﻿—but he became watchful and thoughtful. He ate his soup in silence when she descanted on various benevolent schemes.

===Original===
Mr. Taylor was thinking fast. Yes, the name of Congressman Peter Smith was quite familiar. Mr. Easterly, as chairman of the Republican State Committee of New Jersey, had been compelled to discipline Mr. Smith pretty severely for certain socialistic votes in the House, and consequently his future career was uncertain. ===Original===

But the gods of lower Broadway thought otherwise. Not that Mary Taylor did not believe in Miss Smith’s work, she was too honest not to believe in education; but she was sure that this was not her work, and she had not as yet perfected in her own mind any theory of the world into which black folk fitted.
