## Note / 题记

===Original===
He who would tell a tale must look toward three ideals: to tell it well, to tell it beautifully, and to tell the truth.
The first is the Gift of God, the second is the Vision of Genius, but the third is the Reward of Honesty.
In *The Quest of the Silver Fleece* there is little, I ween, divine or ingenious; but, at least, I have been honest. In no fact or picture have I consciously set down aught the counterpart of which I have not seen or known; and whatever the finished picture may lack of completeness, this lack is due now to the storyteller, now to the artist, but never to the herald of the Truth.
**The Author.**
New York City,
August 15, 1911.

===Chinese===
欲讲述一个故事的人，须仰望三种境界：把它讲好，把它讲美，把它讲真。
第一种是上帝的恩赐，第二种是天才的远见，而第三种，则是诚实的报偿。
在*《银色羊毛的追寻》（The Quest of the Silver Fleece）*一书里，我承认，神圣与巧思均不多见；然而至少，我做到了诚实。无论是事实还是图景，凡是我所未曾亲见、未曾亲知者，我绝未曾有意写下与之相悖的半字；这部完成之作倘若有何不完备之处，那缺失或应归咎于讲故事的人，或应归咎于执笔的艺匠，却绝不该归咎于真理的传信者。
**作者（The Author）**
于纽约城
一九一一年八月十五日
