## XXXVI The Land / XXXVI 土地

===Original===
Colonel Cresswell started all the more grimly to overthrow the new work at the school because somewhere down beneath his heart a pity and a wonder were stirring; pity at the perfectly useless struggle to raise the unraisable, a wonder at certain signs of rising. But it was impossible﻿—and unthinkable, even if possible. So he squared his jaw and cheated Zora deliberately in the matter of the cut timber. He placed every obstacle in the way of getting tenants for the school land. Here Johnson, the “faithful nigger,” was of incalculable assistance. He was among the first to hear the call for prospective tenants.

===Chinese===
克雷斯韦尔上校越发铁了心要搞垮学校那摊新事业，因为在他心底深处，有一种怜悯和一种惊奇正翻涌着；怜悯那想把扶不起的人扶起来、纯属徒劳的努力，惊奇某些确在抬头的迹象。但这是不可能的——即便可能，也是不可想象的。于是他绷紧下巴，在那批砍下的木材上故意坑了佐拉。他为学校那块地招佃户一事设置重重障碍。在这里，那个『忠心的黑鬼』约翰逊，帮了大忙。他是头一批听见招佃风声的人之一。

===Original===
The meeting was in the big room of Zora’s house, and Aunt Rachel came early with her cheery voice and smile which faded so quickly to lines of sorrow and despair, and then twinkled back again. After her hobbled old Sykes. Fully a half-hour later Rob hurried in.
“Johnson,” he informed the others, “has sneaked over to Cresswell’s to tell of this meeting. We ought to beat that nigger up.” But Zora asked him about the new baby, and he was soon deep in child-lore. Higgins and Sanders came together﻿—dirty, apologetic, and furtive. Then came Johnson.

===Chinese===
会开在佐拉家的大屋。蕾切尔大婶来得早，带着她那欢快的嗓音和笑，那笑那样快就褪成悲伤与绝望的纹路，随即又闪烁回来。她身后一瘸一拐地跟着老赛克斯。足足半小时后罗布才匆匆赶到。
『约翰逊，』他告诉大家，『溜到克雷斯韦尔家去告发这次聚会了。我们该把这黑鬼揍一顿。』可佐拉问起他的新生儿，他便很快一头扎进了育儿经。希金斯和桑德斯一同来了——脏兮兮的、赔着笑脸、鬼鬼祟祟。然后约翰逊来了。

===Original===
“How do, Miss Zora﻿—Mr. Alwyn, I sure is glad to see you, sir. Well, if there ain’t Aunt Rachel! looking as young as ever. And Higgins, you scamp﻿—Ah, Mr. Sanders﻿—well, gentlemen and ladies, this sure is gwine to be a good cotton season. I remember﻿—” And he ran on endlessly, now to this one, now to that, now to all, his little eyes all the while dancing insinuatingly here and there. About nine o’clock a buggy drove up and Carter and Simpson came in﻿—Carter, a silent, strong-faced, brown laborer, who listened and looked, and Simpson, a worried nervous man, who sat still with difficulty and commenced many sentences but did not finish them.

===Chinese===
『您好，佐拉小姐——阿尔文先生，能见到您我真高兴，先生。嘿，这不是蕾切尔大婶吗！看着还是那么年轻。还有希金斯，你小子——啊，桑德斯先生——好吧，先生们女士们，今年准是个棉花好年成。我记得——』他滔滔不绝地说下去，一会儿对这个、一会儿对那个、一会儿对所有人，他那双小眼睛始终在这里那里讨好地转个不停。约莫九点钟，一辆轻便马车驶来，卡特和辛普森进来——卡特是个沉默、脸庞坚毅的棕色皮肤劳工，只听只看；辛普森是个忧心忡忡、神经质的人，坐不住，开了许多个头却没一个说完。

===Original===
Alwyn looked at his watch and at Zora, but she gave no sign until they heard a rollicking song outside and Tylor burst into the room. He was nearly seven feet high and broad-shouldered, yellow, with curling hair and laughing brown eyes. He was chewing an enormous quid of tobacco, the juice of which he distributed generously, and had had just liquor enough to make him jolly. His entrance was a breeze and a roar.
Alwyn then undertook to explain the land scheme.
“It is the best land in the county﻿—”
“When it’s cl’ared,” interrupted Johnson, and Simpson looked alarmed.

===Chinese===
阿尔文看看表，又看看佐拉，可她毫无表示，直到他们听见外头一阵喧闹的歌声，泰勒一头闯了进来。他足有七尺高、膀阔肩圆，黄皮肤、鬈头发、一双笑眯眯的棕眼睛。他嘴里嚼着好大一口烟叶，烟汁慷慨地四处乱吐，喝的酒恰到好处，正好叫他快活。他这一进来，就是一阵风、一声吼。
随后阿尔文着手讲解土地方案。
『这是全县最好的地——』
『那是清出来之后，』约翰逊插嘴，辛普森面露惊慌。

===Original===
“It is partially cleared,” continued Alwyn, “and our plan is to sell off small twenty-acre farms﻿—”
“You can’t do nothing on twenty acres﻿—” began Johnson, but Tylor laid his huge hand right over his mouth and said briefly:
“Shut up!”
Alwyn started again: “We shall sell a few twenty-acre farms but keep one central plantation of one hundred acres for the school. Here Miss Zora will carry on her work and the school will run a model farm with your help. We want to centre here agencies to make life better. We want all sorts of industries; we want a little hospital with a resident physician and two or three nurses; we want a cooperative store for buying supplies; we want a cotton-gin and sawmill, and in the future other things. This land here, as I have said, is the richest around. We want to keep this hundred acres for the public good, and not sell it. We are going to deed it to a board of trustees, and those trustees are to be chosen from the ones who buy the small farms.”

===Chinese===
『有一部分已经清出来了，』阿尔文接着说，『我们的方案是，分售二十英亩一块的小农场——』
『二十英亩你能干出个啥——』约翰逊开口，可泰勒一只大手直接捂上了他的嘴，简短地说：
『闭嘴！』
阿尔文重新开始：『我们将分售几块二十英亩的农场，但保留一百英亩的中心种植园给学校。在这里，佐拉小姐将开展她的工作，学校将在你们的帮助下经营一个示范农场。我们要把改善生活的各种机构集中在这里。我们要各行各业；我们要一间小医院，配一位驻院医生和两三个护士；我们要一个买物资的合作商店；我们要一座轧花机和锯木厂，将来还要别的。这里的地，正如我方才所说，是附近最肥的。这一百英亩我们要留作公益，不卖。我们打算把它立契交给一个理事会，而那些理事，从购买小农场的人里选出。』

===Original===
“Who’s going to get what’s made on this land?” asked Sanders.
“All of us. It is going first to pay for the land, then to support the Home and the School, and then to furnish capital for industries.”
Johnson snickered. “You mean youse gwine to git yo’ livin’ off it?”
“Yes,” answered Alwyn; “but I’m going to work for it.”
“Who’s gwine﻿—” began Simpson, but stopped helplessly.
“Who’s going to tend this land?” asked the practical Carter.

===Chinese===
『这块地上出产的东西，归谁？』桑德斯问。
『归我们大家。它先用来付地价，再用来养家舍和学校，然后为各业提供本钱。』
约翰逊窃笑。『你是说，你们打算靠它讨生活？』
『是的，』阿尔文答；『不过我是要干活的。』
『谁要——』辛普森开口，又无助地停住。
『这块地谁来种？』务实的卡特问。

===Original===
“All of us. Each man is going to promise us so many days’ work a year, and we’re going to ask others to help﻿—the women and girls and school children﻿—they will all help.”
“Can you put trust in that sort of help?”
“We can when once the community learns that it pays.”
“Does you own the land?” asked Johnson suddenly.
“No; we’re buying it, and it’s part paid for already.”
The discussion became general. Zora moved about among the men whispering and explaining; while Johnson moved, too, objecting and hinting. At last he arose.

===Chinese===
『我们大家。每个人向我们保证一年干多少天的活，我们还会请别人帮忙——妇女、姑娘和学校里的孩子——他们都会来帮忙。』
『这样的帮工，靠得住吗？』
『等这个社区明白这是划算的，就靠得住了。』
『这地是你们的吗？』约翰逊忽然问。
『不是；我们正在买，已经付了一部分了。』
讨论变得普遍起来。佐拉在众人中间走来走去，低声解释；约翰逊也在动，反对着、暗示着。终于他站了起来。

===Original===
“Brethren,” he began, “the plan’s good enough for talkin’ but you can’t work it; who ever heer’d tell of such a thing? First place, the land ain’t yours; second place, you can’t get it worked; third place, white folks won’t ’low it. Who ever heer’d of such working land on shares?”
“You do it for white folks each day, why not for yourselves,” Alwyn pointed out.
“’Cause we ain’t white, and we can’t do nothin’ like that.”
Tylor was asleep and snoring and the others looked doubtfully at each other. It was a proposal a little too daring for them, a bit too far beyond their experience. One consideration alone kept them from shrinking away and that was Zora’s influence. Not a man was there whom she had not helped and encouraged nor who had not perfect faith in her; in her impetuous hope, her deep enthusiasm, and her strong will. Even her defects﻿—the hard-held temper, the deeply rooted dislikes﻿—caught their imagination.

===Chinese===
『弟兄们，』他开口，『这计划说说是好的，可你们做不成；谁听说过这样的事？头一件，地不是你们的；第二件，你们弄不到人种；第三件，白人不准。谁听说过这样合伙种地的？』
『你们天天替白人这么干，怎么不替自己干，』阿尔文指出。
『因为我们不是白人，我们干不了那样的事。』
泰勒已经睡着了打呼噜，其余的人面面相觑、半信半疑。这提议对他们来说太大胆了一点，离他们的经验太远了一点。只有一件事让他们没有退缩，那就是佐拉的影响力。在场的人，没有一个没受过她的帮助和鼓励，也没有一个不对她有十足的信心；信她那股冲劲勃勃的希望、她那深沉的热忱、她那坚强的意志。连她的缺点——那紧绷着的脾气、那根深蒂固的好恶——都激起了他们的想象。

===Original===
Finally, after several other meetings five men took courage﻿—three of the best and two of the weakest. During the Spring long negotiations were entered into by Miss Smith to “buy” the five men. Colonel Cresswell and Mr. Tolliver had them all charged with large sums of indebtedness and these sums had to be assumed by the school. As Colonel Cresswell counted over two thousand dollars of school notes and deposited them beside the mortgage he smiled grimly for he saw the end. Yet, even then his hand trembled and that curious doubt came creeping back. He put it aside angrily and glanced up.
“Nigger wants to talk with you,” announced his clerk.
The Colonel sauntered out and found Bles Alwyn waiting.

===Chinese===
最后，又开过几次会后，有五个人鼓起了勇气——三个最好的、两个最弱的。整个春天，史密斯小姐为『买下』这五人进行了漫长的谈判。克雷斯韦尔上校和托利弗先生给他们每个人都挂上了一大笔债务，而这些债都得由学校来承担。克雷斯韦尔上校点着两千多块钱的学校欠条、把它们放在抵押文件旁边时，冷酷地笑了，因为他看见了尽头。然而即便那时，他的手也在抖，那古怪的疑虑又悄悄爬了回来。他恼怒地把它甩开，抬起头。
『有个黑鬼要见您，』他的伙计通报。
上校踱出去，看见布莱斯·阿尔文在等。

===Original===
“Colonel Cresswell,” he said, “I have charge of the buying for the school and our tenants this year and I naturally want to do the best possible. I thought I’d come over and see about getting my supplies at your store.”
“That’s all right; you can get anything you want,” said Colonel Cresswell cheerily, for this to his mind was evidence of sense on the part of the Negroes. Bles showed his list of needed supplies﻿—seeds, meat, cornmeal, coffee, sugar, etc. The Colonel glanced over it carelessly, then moved away.
“All right. Come and get what you want﻿—any time,” he called back.

“Oh, they’ll be all right.”

“Cash?”
“Yes, sir.”

===Chinese===
『克雷斯韦尔上校，』他说，『今年学校和佃户们的采购归我管，我自然想把事情办得尽量好。我想过来谈谈，在您铺子里进我们的货。』
『没问题；你想要什么就拿什么，』克雷斯韦尔上校乐呵呵地说，因为在他看来，这是黑人有脑子的证据。布莱斯亮出他需要的物资清单——种子、肉、玉米面、咖啡、糖等等。上校漫不经心地扫了一眼，便走开了。

===Original===
Cresswell thought a while; such a businesslike proposition from Negroes surprised him.
“Well, I’ll let you know,” he said.
It was nearly a week later before Alwyn approached him again.
“Now, see here,” said Colonel Cresswell, “there’s practically no difference between cash and time prices. We buy our stock on time and you can just as well take advantage of this as not. I have figured out about what these things will cost. The best thing for you to do is to make a deposit here and get things when you want them. If you make a good deposit I’ll throw off ten percent, which is all of my profit.”
“Thank you,” said Alwyn, but he looked over the account and found the whole bill at least twice as large as he expected. Without further parley, he made some excuse and started to town while Mr. Cresswell went to the telephone.

===Chinese===
克雷斯韦尔想了一会儿；黑人们竟提出这样一笔正经生意，叫他吃惊。
『好吧，我回头告诉你，』他说。

『谢谢，』阿尔文说，可他一过账，发现整笔账至少比他预想的大一倍。他不再多话，找个借口便动身进城，而克雷斯韦尔先生则走向了电话。

===Original===
In town Alwyn went to all the chief merchants one after another and received to his great surprise practically the same estimate. He could not understand it. He had estimated the current market prices according to the Montgomery paper, yet the prices in Toomsville were fifty to a hundred and fifty percent higher. The merchant to whom he went last, laughed.
“Don’t you know we’re not going to interfere with Colonel Cresswell’s tenants?” He stated the dealers’ attitude, and Alwyn saw light. He went home and told Zora, and she listened without surprise.
“Now to business,” she said briskly. “Miss Smith,” turning to the teacher, “as I told you, they’re combined against us in town and we must buy in Montgomery. I was sure it was coming, but I wanted to give Colonel Cresswell every chance. Bles starts for Montgomery﻿—”

===Chinese===
在镇上，阿尔文挨家挨户地走访所有大商家，大为惊讶地发现，每家给的报价几乎一模一样。他闹不明白。他本是按蒙哥马利报纸上的行情估的价，可图姆斯维尔的价高出五成到一倍半。他最后去的那家商人笑了。
『你不知道吗？我们不会插手克雷斯韦尔上校的佃户。』他点破了商人们的立场，阿尔文恍然大悟。他回家告诉佐拉，她听着，并不惊讶。
『言归正传，』她利落地说。『史密斯小姐，』她转向那位教师，『正如我跟您说的，镇上的人联手对付我们，我们必须去蒙哥马利进货。我料定会有这一天，可我想给克雷斯韦尔上校每一个机会。布莱斯这就动身去蒙哥马利——』

===Original===
Alwyn looked up. “Does he?” he asked, smiling.
“Yes,” said Zora, smiling in turn. “We must lose no further time.”

“But there’s one from Barton in the morning and Barton is only twenty miles away.”

In less than a week the storehouse was full, and tenants were at work. The twenty acres of cleared swamp land, attended to by the voluntary labor of all the tenants, was soon bearing a magnificent crop. Colonel Cresswell inspected all the crops daily with a proprietary air that would have been natural had these folk been simply tenants, and as such he persisted in regarding them.

===Chinese===
阿尔文抬起头。『是吗？』他笑着问。

The cotton now growing was perhaps not so uniformly fine as the first acre of Silver Fleece, but it was of unusual height and thickness.
“At least a bale to the acre,” Alwyn estimated, and the Colonel mentally determined to take two-thirds of the crop. After that he decided that he would evict Zora immediately; since sufficient land was cleared already for his purposes and moreover, he had seen with consternation a herd of cattle grazing in one field on some early green stuff, and heard a drove of hogs in the swamp. Such an example before the tenants of the Black Belt would be fatal. He must wait a few weeks for them to pick the cotton﻿—then, the end. He was fighting the battle of his color and caste.

The crop throughout the land was short that year, for prices had ruled low last season in accordance with the policy of the Combine. This year they started high again. Would they fall? Many thought so and hastened to sell.
Zora and Alwyn gathered their tenants’ crops, ginned them at the Cresswells’ gin, and carried their cotton to town, where it was deposited in the warehouse of the Farmers’ League.
“Now,” said Alwyn, “we would best sell while prices are high.”
Zora laughed at him frankly.
“We can’t,” she said. “Don’t you know that Colonel Cresswell will attach our cotton for rent as soon as it touches the warehouse?”

『黑鬼们需要个教训，』他嘟囔着，步履不稳地向主人告辞。克雷斯韦尔看着他骑马离去，心里很不自在，那种疑虑之感又一次在他心里翻动。他在对自家黑人——世世代代在他和他父辈身边生活了将近三百年的自家黑人——释放出怎样的新力量？他看见警长魁梧的身影，像一道邪灵，在路的高坡上隐现片刻，便沉入夜色。他慢慢转身，回到他那冷清的宅子，进门时，在那阴森的门廊里打了个寒颤。
