## X The British Labour Mission / 十、英国劳工代表团
===Original===

I was glad to learn that Angelica Balabanova arrived in Petrograd to prepare quarters for the British Labour Mission. During my stay in Moscow I had come to know and appreciate the fine spirit of Angelica. She was very devoted to me and when I fell ill she gave much time to my care, procured medicine which could be obtained only in the Kremlin drug store, and got special sick rations for me. Her friendship was generous and touching, and she endeared herself very much to me.
The Narishkin Palace was to be prepared for the Mission, and Angelica invited me to accompany her there. I noticed that she looked more worn and distressed than when I had seen her in Moscow. Our conversation made it clear to me that she suffered keenly from the reality which was so unlike her ideal. But she insisted that what seemed failure to me was conditioned in life itself, itself the greatest failure.
Narishkin Palace is situated on the southern bank of the Neva, almost opposite the Peter-and-Paul Fortress. The place was prepared for the expected guests and a number of servants and cooks installed to minister to their needs. Soon the Mission arrived﻿—most of them typical workingmen delegates﻿—and with them a staff of newspaper men and Mrs. Snowden. The most outstanding figure among them was Bertrand Russell, who quickly demonstrated his independence and determination to be free to investigate and learn at first hand.
In honour of the Mission the Bolsheviki organized a great demonstration on the Uritski Square. Thousands of people, among them women and children, came to show their gratitude to the English labour representatives for venturing into revolutionary Russia. The ceremony consisted of the singing of the “Internationale,” followed by music and speeches, the latter translated by Balabanova in masterly fashion. Then came the military exercises. I heard Mrs. Snowden say disapprovingly, “What a display of military!” I could not resist the temptation of remarking: “Madame, remember that the big Russian army is largely the making of your own country. Had England not helped to finance the invasions into Russia, the latter could put its soldiers to useful labour.”
The British Mission was entertained royally with theatres, operas, ballets, and excursions. Luxury was heaped upon them while the people slaved and went hungry.

得知安热莉卡·巴拉班诺娃到了彼得格勒，为英国工党代表团打点住处，我很高兴。在莫斯科期间，我已了解并敬重安热莉卡的高尚品性。她待我极尽关爱：我病倒时，她花许多时间照料，弄到只有克里姆林宫药房才有的药，还为我申请了特别的病号口粮。她的友情慷慨而感人，令我深深依恋。

纳雷什金宫被定为代表团下榻处，安热莉卡邀我同去。我注意到她比在莫斯科见时更憔悴、更愁苦。交谈使我明白：与理想如此悬殊的现实，令她痛彻。但她坚持说，在我看来是失败的东西，是生活本身使然——生活本身原是最大的失败。

纳雷什金宫坐落在涅瓦河南岸，几乎正对彼得保罗要塞。为贵客们备好了住处，还安置了一班仆役厨子伺候。代表团很快到了——多为典型的工人代表——随行还有一批报人和斯诺登夫人。其中最抢眼的人物是伯特兰·罗素，他很快显出独立不羁的品性，坚持要自由考察、亲获一手材料。

为欢迎代表团，布尔什维克在乌里茨基广场组织了盛大示威。成千上万的人，其中有妇女儿童，前来向敢来革命俄国的英国劳工代表致谢。仪式是唱《国际歌》，随后音乐与演说，演说由巴拉班诺娃出色地翻译。然后是军事操演。我听见斯诺登夫人不以为然地说：「好一场耀武扬威！」我忍不住接了一句：「夫人，请别忘了，俄国这支庞大的军队多半是贵国的手笔。若不是英国出资资助对俄国的入侵，俄国早把它的兵派去干有用的活了。」

英国代表团受到王侯般的款待：剧院、歌剧、芭蕾、游览，一应俱全。奢侈堆在他们身上，而人民在苦役中挨饿。

===Original===

The Soviet Government left nothing undone to create a good impression and everything of a disturbing nature was kept from the visitors. Angelica hated the display and sham, and suffered keenly under the rigid watch placed upon every movement of the Mission. “Why should they not see the true state of Russia? Why should they not learn how the Russian people live?” she would lament. “Yet I am so impractical,” she would correct herself; “perhaps it is all necessary.” At the end of two weeks a farewell banquet was given to the visitors. Angelica insisted that I must attend. Again there were speeches and toasts, as is the custom at such functions. The speeches which seemed to ring most sincere were those of Balabanova and Madame Ravitch. The latter asked me to interpret her address, which I did. She spoke in behalf of the Russian women proletarians and praised their fortitude and devotion to the Revolution. “May the English proletarians learn the quality of their heroic Russian sisters,” concluded Madame Ravitch. Mrs. Snowden, the erstwhile suffragette, had not a word in reply. She preserved a “dignified” aloofness. However, the lady became enlivened when the speeches were over and she got busy collecting autographs.

苏维埃政府为制造好印象无所不用其极，一切可能引起不快的东西都同客人隔绝。安热莉卡憎恶这种做戏与虚假，代表团一举一动都受严密监视，更使她深以为苦。「为什么不能让他们看看俄国的真实境况？为什么不能让他们知道俄国人民怎么生活？」她总这样叹。「可我又这么不切实际，」她又自我更正，「也许这一切都是必要的。」两周期满，为客人举办了告别宴会。安热莉卡坚持要我出席。照例是演说与祝酒。听来最真挚的，是巴拉班诺娃和拉维奇女史的演说。后者请我替她翻译致辞，我照办了。她代表俄国女无产者讲话，赞美她们对革命的坚忍与忠诚。「愿英国无产者学一学她们俄国姐妹的英雄品质。」拉维奇女史这样作结。斯诺登夫人——昔日的女权运动者——竟无一字回应，端着「矜持」的超然。不过演说一结束，这位夫人就活跃起来，忙着收集起签名来。
