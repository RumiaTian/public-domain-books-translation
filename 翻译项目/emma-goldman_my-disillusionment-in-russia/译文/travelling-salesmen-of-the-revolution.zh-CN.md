## XXIX Travelling Salesmen of the Revolution / 二十九、革命的行商
===Original===

Great preparations were being made by the Communists for the Third Congress of the Third International and the First Congress of the Red Trade Union International. A preliminary committee had been organized in the summer of 1920, while delegates from various countries were in Moscow. How much the Bolsheviki depended upon the First Congress of the Red Trade Union International was apparent from a remark of an old Communist. “We haven’t the workers in the Third International,” he said; “unless we succeed in welding together the proletariat of the world into the R.T.U.I., the Third International cannot last very long.”
The Hôtel de Luxe, renovated the previous year, became the foreign guest house of the Third International and was put in festive attire. The delegates began to arrive in Moscow.
During my stay in Russia I came across three classes of visitors who came to “study the Revolution.” The first category consisted of earnest idealists to whom the Bolsheviki were the symbol of the Revolution. Among them were many emigrants from America who had given up everything they possessed to return to the promised land. Most of these became bitterly disappointed after the first few months and sought to get out of Russia. Others, who did not come as Communists, joined the Communist Party for selfish reasons and did in Rome as the Romans do. There were also the Anarchist deportees who came not of their own choice. Most of them strained every effort to leave Russia after they realized the stupendous deception that had been imposed on the world.
In the second class were journalists, newspapermen, and some adventurers. They spent from two weeks to two months in Russia, usually in Petrograd or Moscow, as the guests of the Government and in charge of Bolshevik guides. Hardly any of them knew the language and they never got further than the surface of things. Yet many of them have presumed to write and lecture authoritatively about the Russian situation. I remember my astonishment when I read in a certain London daily that the teachings of Jesus were “being realized in Russia.” A preposterous falsehood of which none but a charlatan could be guilty. Other writers were not much nearer the truth.

共产党人正为第三国际第三次代表大会和红色工会国际第一次代表大会张罗盛大筹备。筹备委员会早在 1920 年夏、各国代表云集莫斯科时就已成立。布尔什维克何等指望红色工会国际的成立大会，从一位老共产党人的话里可见一斑：「第三国际里我们并没有工人，」他说，「除非把全世界无产者熔进红色工会国际，第三国际撑不了多久。」

豪华旅馆——头一年刚翻修过——成了第三国际的外宾馆，装点得喜气洋洋。各国代表陆续抵达莫斯科。

旅俄期间，我遇到过三类来「考察革命」的客人。第一类是诚恳的理想主义者，在他们眼里布尔什维克就是革命的象征。其中有许多从美国回来的侨民，抛下全部家产奔赴应许之地。这些人多数不出几个月便痛感幻灭，想法子离开俄国；另一些本来不是共产党人的，出于私利入了党，入乡随俗去了。还有被迫放逐而来的无政府主义者，一旦认清那场强加给全世界的弥天骗局，便拼命想离开俄国。

第二类是记者、报人和一些冒险家。他们在俄国待两星期到两个月，通常在彼得格勒或莫斯科，做政府的客人，由布尔什维克的向导陪同。几乎没人懂语言，看到的从不超过表层。可其中许多人竟摆出权威的架势著书演讲，大谈俄国局势。记得我在伦敦一家日报上读到「耶稣的教导正在俄国实现」时，何等惊愕。荒谬绝伦的谎言——只有江湖骗子才说得出。别的作者离真相也不更近些。

If they were at all critical of the Bolsheviki they were so at the expense of the whole Russian people, whom they charged with being “crude, primitive savages, too illiterate to grasp the meaning of the Revolution.” According to these writers it was the Russian people who imposed upon the Bolsheviki their despotic and cruel methods. It did not occur to those so-called investigators that the Revolution was made by those primitive and illiterate people, and not by the present rulers in the Kremlin. Surely they must have possessed some quality which enabled them to rise to revolutionary heights﻿—a quality which, if properly directed, would have prevented the wreck and ruin of Russia. But that quality has persistently been overlooked by Bolshevik apologists who sacrifice all truth in their determination to find extenuating circumstances for the mess made by the Bolsheviki. A few wrote with understanding of the complex problems and with sympathy for the Russian people. But their voice was ineffectual in the popular craze that Bolshevism had become.
The third category﻿—the majority of the visitors, delegates, and members of various commissions﻿—infested Russia to become the agents of the ruling Party. These people had every opportunity to see things as they were, to get close to the Russian people, and to learn from them the whole terrible truth. But they preferred to side with the Government, to listen to its interpretation of causes and effects. Then they went forth to misrepresent and to lie deliberately in behalf of the Bolsheviki, as the Entente agents had lied and misrepresented the Russian Revolution.
Nor did the sincere Communists realize the disgrace of the situation﻿—not even Angelica Balabanova. Yet she had good judgment of character and knew how to appraise the people who flocked to Russia. Her experience with Mrs. Clare Sheridan was characteristic. The lady had been smuggled into Russia before Moscow realized that she was the cousin of Winston Churchill. She was obsessed by the desire “to sculp” prominent Communists. She had also begged Angelica to sit for her. “Lenin, Trotsky, and other leaders are going to; aren’t you?” she pleaded. Angelica, who hated sensationalism in any form, resented the presence in Russia of these superficial visitors.

他们即便批评布尔什维克，也拿整个俄国人民垫背，说他们是「粗野、原始的蛮子，文盲得连革命的意义都领会不了」。照这些作者的说法，是俄国人民把专制残暴的手段强加给了布尔什维克。这些所谓的考察者没想到：革命恰恰是那些原始的文盲干出来的，不是克里姆林宫现在的统治者们干的。他们必定有一种品质，使他们能奋起达到革命的高度——这品质若引导得当，本可免掉俄国的覆灭。可布尔什维克的辩护士们执意要为这伙人的烂摊子找开脱，不惜牺牲一切真相，对那种品质视而不见。也有少数作者带着对复杂问题的理解和同情写作，但在布尔什维主义成了流行狂热的年头，他们的声音无济于事。

第三类人——访客、代表和各色委员会成员的大多数——麇集俄国，是要做执政党的代理人。这些人本有充分的机会看清事物的本来面目，走近俄国人民，从他们口中听全那可怕的真相。可他们宁愿站在政府一边，听政府对因果的一套解释，然后出去颠倒黑白、蓄意为布尔什维克撒谎，一如协约国的走狗们当年为诋毁俄国革命而撒谎那样。

就连真诚的共产党人也没意识到这局面的可耻——连安热莉卡·巴拉班诺娃起初也没意识到。其实她看人颇有眼力，深知涌到俄国来的都是些什么货色。她同克莱尔·谢里登夫人的打交就是典型一例。这位夫人在莫斯科方面发现她是温斯顿·丘吉尔的表妹之前，被偷偷带进了俄国。她满脑子想「塑」共产党要人的半身像，也恳求安热莉卡给她当模特。「列宁、托洛茨基和其他领袖都肯的，您还不肯吗？」她劝道。安热莉卡憎恶一切形式的耸动，对这路浅薄的访客深为反感。

===Original===

“I asked her,” she afterward related, “if she would have thought of ‘sculping’ Lenin three years ago when the English Government denounced him as a German spy. Lenin did not make the Revolution. The Russian people made it. I told this Mrs. Sheridan that she would do better to ‘sculp’ Russian workingmen and women who were the real heroes of the Revolution. I know she did not like what I said. But I don’t care. I can’t stand people to whom the Russian struggle is mere copy for poor imitations or cheap display.”
Now the new delegates were beginning to arrive. They were royally welcomed and fêted. They were taken to show schools, children’s homes, colonies, and model factories. It was the traditional Potemkin villages29 that were shown the visitors. They were graciously received and “talked to” by Lenin and Trotsky, treated to theatres, concerts, ballets, excursions, and military parades. In short, nothing was left undone to put the delegates into a frame of mind favourable to the great plan that was to be revealed to them at the Red Trade Union and the Third International Congresses. There were also continuous private conferences where the delegates were subjected to a regular third degree, Lozovsky﻿—prominent Bolshevik labour leader﻿—and his retinue seeking to ascertain their attitude to the Third International, the dictatorship of the proletariat, and similar subjects. Here and there was a delegate who refused to divulge the instructions of his organization on the ground that he was pledged to report only to the Congress. But such naive people reckoned without their host. They soon found themselves ostracized and at the Congress they were given no opportunity to make themselves heard effectively.
The majority of the delegates were more pliable. They learned quickly that pledges and responsibilities were considered bourgeois superstitions. To show their ultra-radicalism they quickly divested themselves of them. They became the echoes of Zinoviev, Lozovsky, and other leaders.
The American delegates to the Red Trade Union International were most conspicuous by their lack of personality. They accepted without question every proposition and suggestion of the Chair.

「我问她，」安热莉卡后来讲，「三年前英国政府骂列宁是德国间谍的时候，她可曾想到去『塑』列宁？列宁没有制造革命，是俄国人民干的。我告诉这位谢里登夫人，她不如去『塑』俄国男女工人——他们才是革命的真英雄。我知道她不爱听。可我不在乎。那些把俄国的挣扎只当作拙劣模仿和廉价展览素材的人，我受不了。」

如今新代表们纷纷抵达，受着王侯般的欢迎与宴请，被领去看「秀」学校、儿童之家、营舍和模范工厂。给客人们看的，是老一套的波将金村29。他们受列宁和托洛茨基亲切接见、「赐谈」，看戏、听音乐会、观芭蕾、游览、观阅兵。总之，无所不用其极，好把代表们放进一种心境，乐于接受即将在红色工会国际和第三国际大会上向他们揭晓的伟大计划。此外还有没完没了的私下谈话，代表们被施以正规的疲劳审讯——罗佐夫斯基——布尔什维克的著名劳工头目——及其一班人，要摸清各人对第三国际、无产阶级专政等问题的态度。偶尔也有代表拒绝泄露本组织的训令，理由是他只对大会负责。可这种天真的人打错了算盘：很快便遭孤立，到了大会上，根本得不到让人听见的机会。

多数代表圆通得多。他们很快学会了：承诺和责任都是资产阶级迷信，为表现自己的超激进，三下两下便抖落干净。他们成了季诺维也夫、罗佐夫斯基和其他领袖的回声。

赴红色工会国际的的美国代表们，最醒目之处就是毫无个性：主席台的一切提议和暗示，他们照单全收。

The most flagrant intrigues and political machinations and brazen suppression of those who would not be cajoled or bullied into blind adherence found ready support by the American Communist crew and the aides they had brought with them.
The Bolsheviki know how to set the stage to produce an impression. In the staging of the two Congresses held in July, 1921, they outdid themselves. The background for the Congress of the Third International was the Kremlin. In the royal halls where once the all-powerful Romanovs had sat, the awed delegates hung with bated breath upon every word uttered by their pope, Lenin, and the other Grand Seigneurs of the Communist Church. On the eve of the Congress a great meeting was held in the big theatre to which only those whose passports had been approved by the All-Russian Cheka were admitted. The streets leading to the theatre were turned into a veritable military camp. Chekists and soldiers on foot and on horseback created the proper atmosphere for the Communist conclave. At the meeting resolutions were passed extending fraternal greetings to “the revolutionists in capitalist prisons.” At that very moment every Russian prison was filled with revolutionists but no greetings were sent to them. So all-pervading was Moscow hypnotism that not a single voice was raised to point out the farce of Bolshevik sympathy for political prisoners.
The Red Trade Union Congress was set on a less pretentious scale in the House of the Trade Unions. But no details were overlooked to get the proper effects. “Delegates” from Palestine and Korea﻿—men who had not been out of Russia for years﻿—delegates from the great industrial centres of Bokhara, Turkestan, and Azerbaijan, packed the Congress to swell the Communist vote and help carry every Communist proposition. They were there to teach the workers of Europe and America how to reconstruct their respective countries and to establish Communism after the world revolution.
The plan perfected by Moscow during the year 1920﻿–﻿21, and which was a complete reversal of Communist principles and tactics, was very skilfully and subtly unrolled﻿—by slow degrees﻿—before the credulous delegates. The Red Trade Union International was to embrace all revolutionary and syndicalist organizations of the world, with Moscow as its Mecca and the Third International as its Prophet.

最露骨的阴谋、政治手腕，以及对不肯被骗不肯被吓而盲从者的无耻压制，都得到那帮美国共产党船员及其随从的现成支持。

布尔什维克深知怎样布景造势。1921 年 7 月那两个大会的舞台，他们布置得登峰造极。第三国际大会的背景是克里姆林宫。在昔日至尊无上的罗曼诺夫们坐过的殿堂里，诚惶诚恐的代表们屏息聆听他们的教皇列宁、聆听共产党教会的其他大领主们的每一句话。大会前夜，大剧院举行了盛大集会，只有经全俄契卡核准护照的人方能入场。通向剧院的街道成了十足的兵营，步行与骑马的契卡分子和士兵，为共产党的密会调足了气氛。会上通过决议，向「资本主义监狱中的革命者」致以兄弟的敬礼——而此时此刻，俄国每座监狱都关满革命者，却无人向他们致礼。莫斯科的催眠术如此弥漫，竟无一人出声，指出布尔什维克同情政治犯这出闹剧。

红色工会大会设在工会大厦，排场略小，但制造效果的手一处没漏。来自巴勒斯坦和朝鲜的「代表」——多年没离开过俄国的人——来自布哈拉、土耳其斯坦、阿塞拜疆等大工业中心（！）的代表，塞满了大会，替共产党凑票数，保证每个共产党提案都通过。他们坐在那里，教欧美工人怎样重建各自的国家、怎样在世界革命后建立共产主义。

莫斯科在 1920–21 年间炮制完善的那套计划——对共产党原则与策略的一次彻底倒转——被极其巧妙、极其隐微地、一层一层地摊开在轻信的代表们面前：红色工会国际将囊括全世界一切革命的与工团的组织，麦加是莫斯科，先知是第三国际。

All minor revolutionary labour organizations were to be dissolved and Communist units formed instead within the existing conservative trade union bodies. The very people who a year ago had issued the famous Bull of twenty-one points, they who had excommunicated every heretic unwilling to submit to the orders of the Holy See﻿—the Third International﻿—and who had applied every invective to labour in the 2nd and the 2½ Internationals, were now making overtures to the most reactionary labour organizations and “resoluting” against the best efforts of the revolutionary pioneers in the Trade Union movement of every country.
Here again the American delegates proved themselves worthy of their hire. Most of them had sprung from the Industrial Workers of the World; had indeed arisen to “fame and glory” on the shoulders of that militant American labour body. Some of the delegates had valiantly escaped to safety, unselfishly preferring the Hôtel de Luxe to Leavenworth Penitentiary, leaving their comrades behind in American prisons and their friends to refund the bonds they had heroically forfeited. While Industrial Workers continued to suffer persecution in capitalistic America, the renegade I.W.W.’s living in comfort and safety in Moscow maligned and attacked their former comrades and schemed to destroy their organization. Together with the Bolsheviki they were going to carry out the job begun by the American Vigilantes and the Ku Klux Klan to exterminate the I.W.W. *Les extrêmes ce touchent.*
While the Communists were passing eloquent resolutions of protest against the imprisonment of revolutionaries in foreign countries, the Anarchists in the Bolshevik prisons of Russia were being driven to desperation by their long imprisonment without opportunity for a hearing or trial. To force the hand of the Government the Anarchists incarcerated in the Taganka (Moscow) decided on a hunger strike to the death. The French, Spanish, and Italian Anarcho-syndicalists, when informed of the situation, promised to raise the question at an early session of the Labour Congress. Some, however, suggested that the Government be first approached on the matter. Thereupon a Delegate Committee was chosen, including the well-known English labour leader, Tom Mann, to call upon the Little Father in the Kremlin. The Committee visited Lenin.

一切较小的革命劳工组织都将解散，改在现有的保守工会内部组建共产党支部。正是这伙人，一年前还颁布那份著名的二十一条通谕，把每一个不肯听命于教廷——第三国际——的异端逐出教门，把第二国际和第二半国际里的劳工骂了个狗血淋头；如今却向最反动的劳工组织频送秋波，「决议」起各国工会运动中革命先驱者的努力来了。

在这件事上，美国代表们又一次证明自己拿钱干活当之无愧。他们多数出身世界产业工人联合会，确实是踩着那个战斗的美国劳工组织的肩膀爬上「荣名」的。有几位代表英勇脱险，大公无私地宁选豪华旅馆不要莱文沃思监狱，把同志们留在美国的监狱里，把保金留给朋友们去「英雄般地」退赔。世界产业工人联合会的会员还在资本主义美国受迫害时，那些变节的 I.W.W. 们在莫斯科养尊处优，诽谤攻击昔日的同志，密谋摧毁他们的组织。他们要同布尔什维克一道，完成美国义勇团和三 K 党开了头的消灭 I.W.W. 的大业。*两极相遇。*

共产党人慷慨激昂地通过决议、抗议外国囚禁革命者的同时，布尔什维克俄国监狱里的无政府主义者，正被漫长的囚禁逼上绝路——不审不判。为迫使政府让步，塔甘卡监狱（莫斯科）在押的无政府主义者决定绝食至死。法国、西班牙、意大利的无政府工团主义者得知后，答应在劳工大会近期的会议上提出这个问题。不过有人建议先去同政府交涉，于是推选了一个代表委员会——包括英国著名劳工领袖汤姆·曼——去克里姆林宫觐见小父亲。委员会见了列宁。

The latter refused to have the Anarchists released on the ground that “they were too dangerous,” but the final result of the interview was a promise that they would be permitted to leave Russia; should they, however, return without permission, they would be shot. The next day Lenin’s promise was substantiated by a letter of the Central Committee of the Communist Party, signed by Trotsky, reiterating what Lenin had said. Naturally the threat of shooting was omitted in the official letter.
The hunger strikers in the Taganka accepted the conditions of deportation. They had for years fought and bled for the Revolution and now they were compelled to become Ahasueruses in foreign lands or suffer slow mental and physical death in Bolshevik dungeons. The Moscow Anarchist groups chose Alexander Berkman and A. Shapiro as their representatives on the Delegates’ Committee to arrange with the Government the conditions of the release and deportation of the imprisoned Anarchists.
In view of this settlement of the matter the intention of a public protest at the Congress was abandoned by the delegates. Great was their amazement when, just before the close of the Congress, Bukharin﻿—in the name of the Central Committee of the Communist Party﻿—launched into a scurrilous attack on the Anarchists. Some of the foreign delegates, outraged by the dishonourable proceeding, demanded an opportunity to reply. That demand was finally granted to a representative of the French delegation after Chairman Lozovsky had exhausted every demagogic trick in a vain attempt to silence the dissenters.
At no time during the protracted negotiations on behalf of the imprisoned Anarchists and the last disgraceful proceedings at the Red Trade Union Congress did the American Communist delegates make a protest. Loudly they had shouted for political amnesty in America, but not a word had they to say in favour of the liberation of the politicals in Russia. One of the group, approached on behalf of the hunger strikers, exclaimed: “What are a few lives or even a few hundred of them as against the Revolution!” To such Communist minds the Revolution had no bearing on justice and humanity.

列宁拒绝释放无政府主义者，理由是「他们太危险」，但会谈的最后结果是一个承诺：准许他们离开俄国；若擅自回来，枪毙。次日，共产党中央委员会的一封信证实了列宁的承诺，签名的是托洛茨基，重述了列宁的话——自然，枪毙的威胁在官方信件里略去了。

塔甘卡的绝食者接受了驱逐的条件。他们为革命战斗流血多年，如今却被迫要么流落异邦做浮士德的流浪汉，要么在布尔什维克的地牢里慢慢烂死。莫斯科的无政府主义团体推举亚历山大·伯克曼和 A. 夏皮罗做他们在代表委员会里的代表，同政府交涉在押无政府主义者获释与出境的条件。

事情既已这样解决，代表们放弃了在大会上公开抗议的打算。不料大会临近闭幕时，布哈林竟以共产党中央委员会的名义，对无政府主义者发起一场下流的攻击。一些外国代表被这无耻行径激怒，要求答辩权。主席罗佐夫斯基使尽煽动的伎俩也压不住异议者之后，这个要求总算批给了法国代表团的一位代表。

在为在押无政府主义者斡旋的全过程中，以及在红色工会大会最后那场丢人的闹剧里，美国共产党代表们自始至终没有一声抗议。他们为美国的政治大赦喊得震天响，却对俄国政治犯的获释无一句好话。有人为绝食者去疏通，这帮人中有一个竟说：「几条命、几百条命，同革命相比算得了什么！」在这种共产党头脑里，革命与正义和人道毫不相干。

In the face of abject want, with men, women, and children hungrily watching the white bread baked for the Luxe Hôtel in its adjoining bakery, one of the American fraternal delegates wrote to a publication at home that “the workers in Russia control the industries and are directing the affairs of the country; they get everything free and need no money.” This noble delegate lived in the palatial home of the former Sugar King of Russia and enjoyed also the hospitality of the Luxe. He indeed needed no money. But he knew that the workers lacked even the basic necessities and that without money they were as helpless in Russia as in any other country, the week’s *payok* not being sufficient for two days’ existence.
