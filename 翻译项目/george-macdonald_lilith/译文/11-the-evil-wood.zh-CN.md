## XI The Evil Wood / 第十一章 恶林
===Original===

I fell fast asleep, and when I woke the sun was rising. I went to the top again, and looked back: the hollow I had crossed in the moonlight lay without sign of life. Could it be that the calm expanse before me swarmed with creatures of devouring greed?
I turned and looked over the land through which my way must lie. It seemed a wide desert, with a patch of a different colour in the distance that might be a forest. Sign of presence, human or animal, was none﻿—smoke or dust or shadow of cultivation. Not a cloud floated in the clear heaven; no thinnest haze curtained any segment of its circling rim.
I descended, and set out for the imaginable forest: something alive might be there; on this side of it could not well be anything!
When I reached the plain, I found it, as far as my sight could go, of rock, here flat and channeled, there humped and pinnacled﻿—evidently the wide bed of a vanished river, scored by innumerable water-runs, without a trace of moisture in them. Some of the channels bore a dry moss, and some of the rocks a few lichens almost as hard as themselves. The air, once “filled with pleasant noise of waters,” was silent as death. It took me the whole day to reach the patch﻿—which I found indeed a forest﻿—but not a rudiment of brook or runnel had I crossed! Yet through the glowing noon I seemed haunted by an aural mirage, hearing so plainly the voice of many waters that I could hardly believe the opposing testimony of my eyes.
The sun was approaching the horizon when I left the riverbed, and entered the forest. Sunk below the treetops, and sending his rays between their pillar-like boles, he revealed a world of blessed shadows waiting to receive me. I had expected a pinewood, but here were trees of many sorts, some with strong resemblances to trees I knew, others with marvellous differences from any I had ever seen. I threw myself beneath the boughs of what seemed a eucalyptus in blossom: its flowers had a hard calyx much resembling a skull, the top of which rose like a lid to let the froth-like bloom-brain overfoam its cup. From beneath the shadow of its falchion-leaves my eyes went wandering into deep after deep of the forest.
Soon, however, its doors and windows began to close, shutting up aisle and corridor and roomier glade. The night was about me, and instant and sharp the cold. Again what a night I found it!

我沉沉睡去，醒来时朝阳初升。我重回峰顶回首望去：昨夜我在月光下穿过的那片凹地死寂一片，毫无生机迹象。难道方才展现在我眼前那片沉静广袤的土地上，当真曾拥塞着无数极具吞噬贪欲的凶顽生灵吗？
我转过身，眺望着前方必经之地的荒原。那看似是一片广袤的沙漠，远方泛着一抹殊异色泽的地块，或许是一片森林。四顾不见任何人迹或兽踪——既无炊烟，亦无尘嚣，更无农耕开垦的痕迹。明净的天穹中未浮一片白云；在周遭环绕的天际线上，亦无哪怕一丝极薄的烟岚遮蔽。
我走下山脊，朝着那片可供想象的森林迈步前行：那里或许还有些活物；而在森林的这一侧，显然不可能有任何生机！
当我踏上平原时，目力所及之处皆是岩石，此处平坦开裂、布满沟壑，彼处隆起凸起、峰峦耸立——显然这是一条早已干涸干涸的宽阔河床，被数不胜数的冲刷水道割裂得沟壑纵横，其中却没有哪怕一滴水汽的痕迹。某些干涸的河道里长着枯干的苔藓，某些岩石上附着少许几乎与岩石本身同样坚硬的地衣。这片曾经“充溢着宜人水声”的空气，如今却如死亡般寂静。我花费了整整一天的时间才抵达那片斑块——我发觉那确是一片森林——然而在这一路上，我竟未曾跨越过哪怕一条溪流或细泉的雏形！然而在酷热炎炎的正午，我脑海中却仿佛始终盘旋着某种听觉上的海市蜃楼，如此真切地听见万顷波涛的轰鸣，以至于几乎无法相信眼前双目所见的确凿证据。
当我脱离河床步入森林时，夕阳正逼近地平线。落日沉入树冠之下，将万道霞光自如石柱般的树干间穿透而过，展露出一个静候接纳我的神圣阴影世界。我本以为会是一片松林，但在此处却生长着形形色色的各色树木，有些与我所熟知的树木极为神似，另一些则与我平生所见过的任何树木皆有着不可思议的霄壤之别。我在一株看似盛开着花朵的桉树枝桠下倒地躺下：它的花朵生着坚硬如骷髅般的花萼，萼顶如盖般掀起，任由那如泡沫般的脑状花蕊漫溢出花杯。自它那如短剑般的叶片阴影下，我的目光在森林一层又一层的幽深处游移流转。
然而不久，森林的门扉与窗棂开始闭合，合拢了林间甬道、长廊以及更为开阔的林间空地。黑夜将我环抱，刺骨的严寒骤然而至。我又将度过何等可怖的一个长夜！

===Original===

How shall I make my reader share with me its wild ghostiness?
The tree under which I lay rose high before it branched, but the boughs of it bent so low that they seemed ready to shut me in as I leaned against the smooth stem, and let my eyes wander through the brief twilight of the vanishing forest. Presently, to my listless roving gaze, the varied outlines of the clumpy foliage began to assume or imitate﻿—say rather *suggest* other shapes than their own. A light wind began to blow; it set the boughs of a neighbour tree rocking, and all their branches aswing, every twig and every leaf blending its individual motion with the sway of its branch and the rock of its bough. Among its leafy shapes was a pack of wolves that struggled to break from a wizard’s leash: greyhounds would not have strained so savagely! I watched them with an interest that grew as the wind gathered force, and their motions life.
Another mass of foliage, larger and more compact, presented my fancy with a group of horses’ heads and forequarters projecting caparisoned from their stalls. Their necks kept moving up and down, with an impatience that augmented as the growing wind broke their vertical rhythm with a wilder swaying from side to side. What heads they were! how gaunt, how strange!﻿—several of them bare skulls﻿—one with the skin tight on its bones! One had lost the under jaw and hung low, looking unutterably weary﻿—but now and then hove high as if to ease the bit. Above them, at the end of a branch, floated erect the form of a woman, waving her arms in imperious gesture. The definiteness of these and other leaf masses first surprised and then discomposed me: what if they should overpower my brain with seeming reality? But the twilight became darkness; the wind ceased; every shape was shut up in the night; I fell asleep.
It was still dark when I began to be aware of a far-off, confused, rushing noise, mingled with faint cries. It grew and grew until a tumult as of gathering multitudes filled the wood. On all sides at once the sounds drew nearer; the spot where I lay seemed the centre of a commotion that extended throughout the forest. I scarce moved hand or foot lest I should betray my presence to hostile things.
The moon at length approached the forest, and came slowly into it: with her first gleam the noises increased to a deafening uproar, and I began to see dim shapes about me.

我该如何让读者与我共同领会这长夜中狂暴荒诞的森森鬼气？
我栖身的树木在抽枝前高高拔地而起，但它的枝桠却垂得极低，当我倚靠在光滑的树干上、任由目光漫游在渐逝森林那短暂的暮色中时，那些枝条仿佛随时准备将我闭锁其中。渐渐地，在我不经意的游移注视下，丛生枝叶变幻多姿的轮廓开始呈现或模仿——或者不如说暗示——某些殊异于自身的形态。一阵微风开始吹拂；它吹得邻近一棵大树的枝柯摇曳不定，所有的枝条皆随之晃荡，每根嫩枝与每片树叶皆将各自细碎的律动融入了整根大枝的摆动与主枝的摇曳之中。在它那如叶般的形态丛中，赫然出现了一群恶狼，正拼力挣脱巫师手中的皮绳：即便是猎犬也绝不会表现得这般凶残狂暴！随着风势渐盛、它们的动势愈发栩栩如生，我凝视着这一切，心头的兴味亦越发浓烈。
另一团更为硕大密实的枝叶，在我的幻想中幻化作了一群马匹的头颅与前躯，宛如披挂齐整自马厩中探身而出。它们的脖颈不断上下攒动，随着渐盛的狂风以狂暴的左右摇曳撕裂了它们垂直的节律，那份不耐烦之态愈演愈烈。那是何等怪异的马头！何等骨瘦如柴，何等诡谲！——其中数副竟是光秃秃的骷髅——另有一副皮紧紧绷在骨骼之上！其中一副失去了下颌骨，低垂耷拉着，显得无可言喻地疲惫——却时不时又猛然扬起，仿佛试图挣脱马嚼子的束缚。在它们上方、一截树枝的末端，傲然浮现出一个妇人的身形，正以专横决绝的姿态挥舞着双臂。这些枝叶团块所呈现出的具象之清晰，起初令我惊诧，随后却令我惴惴不安：倘若它们以逼真的现实感彻底击溃我的理智，该当如何？然而暮色化作了漆黑的长夜；狂风止息；万千形态皆被闭锁在幽冥之中；我昏昏入睡。
天色依然如墨般幽暗，我开始隐约察觉到一阵远方传来的混乱奔腾喧嚣，其间夹杂着隐约的哀号。声响越来越大，直至整片森林被一阵宛如千军万马集结的骚乱所填满。喧嚣声从四面八方同时逼近；我栖身之处仿佛成了席卷整片森林的风暴中心。我甚至不敢动弹一下手脚，唯恐向那些怀有敌意的生灵暴露了自己的存在。
终于，明月逼近了森林，缓缓步入林中：伴随着她的第一抹清辉，那些杂音骤然演变成震耳欲聋的喧嚣咆哮，我开始能看清身旁模糊的身影。

As she ascended and grew brighter, the noises became yet louder, and the shapes clearer. A furious battle was raging around me. Wild cries and roars of rage, shock of onset, struggle prolonged, all mingled with words articulate, surged in my ears. Curses and credos, snarls and sneers, laughter and mockery, sacred names and howls of hate, came huddling in chaotic interpenetration. Skeletons and phantoms fought in maddest confusion. Swords swept through the phantoms: they only shivered. Maces crashed on the skeletons, shattering them hideously: not one fell or ceased to fight, so long as a single joint held two bones together. Bones of men and horses lay scattered and heaped; grinding and crunching them under foot fought the skeletons. Everywhere charged the bone-gaunt white steeds; everywhere on foot or on windblown misty battle-horses, raged and ravened and raved the indestructible spectres; weapons and hoofs clashed and crushed; while skeleton jaws and phantom-throats swelled the deafening tumult with the war-cry of every opinion, bad or good, that had bred strife, injustice, cruelty in any world. The holiest words went with the most hating blow. Lie-distorted truths flew hurtling in the wind of javelins and bones. Every moment someone would turn against his comrades, and fight more wildly than before, **The truth! The truth!** still his cry. One I noted who wheeled ever in a circle, and smote on all sides. Wearied out, a pair would sit for a minute side by side, then rise and renew the fierce combat. None stooped to comfort the fallen, or stepped wide to spare him.
The moon shone till the sun rose, and all the night long I had glimpses of a woman moving at her will above the strife-tormented multitude, now on this front now on that, one outstretched arm urging the fight, the other pressed against her side. “Ye are men: slay one another!” she shouted. I saw her dead eyes and her dark spot, and recalled what I had seen the night before.
Such was the battle of the dead, which I saw and heard as I lay under the tree.
Just before sunrise, a breeze went through the forest, and a voice cried, “Let the dead bury their dead!” At the word the contending thousands dropped noiseless, and when the sun looked in, he saw never a bone, but here and there a withered branch.
I rose and resumed my journey, through as quiet a wood as ever grew out of the quiet earth.

随着明月冉冉上升、越发皎洁，喧嚣声愈发震耳欲聋，那些身形亦越发轮廓分明。一场惨烈狂暴的激战正在我周遭厮杀鏖战。狂野的呐喊与狂怒的咆哮、交锋冲撞的重击、旷日持久的厮杀搏斗，伴随着字句清晰的话语，皆汹涌奔灌入我的耳中。咒骂与信条、低吼与冷笑、嘲弄与嗤笑、神圣的尊号与刻骨的仇恨号叫，杂糅在混沌的交错穿透中蜂拥而至。枯骨与鬼魅在极度癫狂的混乱中自相残杀。利剑挥砍过幽灵的身躯：鬼魅不过微微战栗。铁锤狠狠砸向枯骨，将骨骼碎裂得可怖至极：然而只要哪怕一个关节仍将两截白骨相连，便无一人倒地或止住厮杀。人与战马的残骨横七竖八、层层叠叠堆积如山；枯骨战士们在脚下骨骼的碾压与碎裂声中死命鏖战。四处皆是枯骨森森的雪白骏马在横冲直撞；四处皆是不灭的鬼魅在徒步或骑在随风翻卷的迷雾战马上狂怒、掠夺、咆哮；刀兵与蹄铁在金铁交鸣中撞击碾碎；而白骨的下颌与鬼魅的咽喉则以各种观念的战阵号角——凡在任何世间曾孕育过纷争、不公与残酷的信条，无论善恶——将那震耳欲聋的喧嚣推向极致。最神圣的言辞往往伴随着最恶毒的重击。被谎言扭曲的真理在标枪与白骨的狂风中呼啸穿梭。每一时刻，皆有人反戈相向倒向昔日同袍，比先前更加疯狂地搏杀，“真理！真理！”依旧是他的呐喊。我注意到有一人始终在原地盘旋打转，向四面八方疯狂乱劈。精疲力竭之时，两名战偶会并肩枯坐一分钟，随即又一跃而起重燃激战。无人弯腰抚慰倒地之人，亦无人移步避让予以宽恕。
月光朗照，直至朝日升起，在整整一夜里，我不时瞥见一名妇人凭借自己的意愿穿行游弋在饱受厮杀煎熬的万众之上，时而出现在这一战线，时而又现身于彼处，一只伸出的手臂极力催促着战火，另一只手则紧按在自己肋下。“尔等皆是凡人：自相残杀吧！”她尖声高呼。我望见了她那双死寂的双眸，以及她肋下的黑斑，猛然忆起前夜所见的那一幕。
这便是我躺在树下所亲眼目睹、亲耳所闻的亡灵之战。
就在日出前夕，一阵清风吹拂过森林，一个声音高呼道：“让死人埋葬他们的死人吧！”话音刚落，鏖战的千军万马悄无声息地纷纷跌落倒地，当太阳探头照入林中时，他连一截白骨亦未曾看见，唯见各处零落散布着几根枯枝。
我站起身，继续踏上征程，穿过了一片如从静穆大地中生长出的最宁谧的森林。

For the wind of the morning had ceased when the sun appeared, and the trees were silent. Not a bird sang, not a squirrel, mouse, or weasel showed itself, not a belated moth flew athwart my path. But as I went I kept watch over myself, nor dared let my eyes rest on any forest-shape. All the time I seemed to hear faint sounds of mattock and spade and hurtling bones: any moment my eyes might open on things I would not see! Daylight prudence muttered that perhaps, to appear, ten thousand phantoms awaited only my consenting fancy.
In the middle of the afternoon I came out of the wood﻿—to find before me a second net of dry watercourses. I thought at first that I had wandered from my attempted line, and reversed my direction; but I soon saw it was not so, and concluded presently that I had come to another branch of the same riverbed. I began at once to cross it, and was in the bottom of a wide channel when the sun set.
I sat down to await the moon, and growing sleepy, stretched myself on the moss. The moment my head was down, I heard the sounds of rushing streams﻿—all sorts of sweet watery noises. The veiled melody of the molten music sang me into a dreamless sleep, and when I woke the sun was already up, and the wrinkled country widely visible. Covered with shadows it lay striped and mottled like the skin of some wild animal. As the sun rose the shadows diminished, and it seemed as if the rocks were reabsorbing the darkness that had oozed out of them during the night.
Hitherto I had loved my Arab mare and my books more, I fear, than live man or woman; now at length my soul was athirst for a human presence, and I longed even after those inhabitants of this alien world whom the raven had so vaguely described as nearest my sort. With heavy yet hoping heart, and mind haunted by a doubt whether I was going in any direction at all, I kept wearily travelling “northwest and by south.”

因为当日出破晓之际，清晨的风便已止息，林木静默无声。不见群鸟鸣啭，不见松鼠、田鼠或鼬鼠现身，亦无一只迟暮的飞蛾横掠过我的路径。然而在前行中我始终保持着警惕，不敢让目光在森林的任何特定形态上稍作停留。一路上，我仿佛始终能隐隐听见鹤嘴锄、铁锹以及白骨碰撞掷地的微弱声响：在任何一瞬间，我的双眼皆可能撞见我不愿目睹之物！白昼的审慎私语告诫着我：或许，只需我顺从的幻想稍作配合，万千鬼魅便会再度现身。
午后时分，我走出了森林——却发现眼前横亘着另一张干涸水道编织而成的巨网。起初我以为自己偏离了预定的行进路线，甚至逆转了方向；但我很快看出并非如此，并随即断定自己不过是来到了同一干涸河床的另一条支流前。我当即开始横穿河道，当落日西沉之际，我正置身于一条开阔干涸河槽的底部。
我坐下等待明月升起，随着睡意袭来，我舒展身躯躺在苔藓之上。我的头颅刚一着地，耳畔便响起了奔腾溪流的轰鸣——各式各样甜美动听的潺潺水声。那融化的流水交织出的朦胧旋律伴我步入了一场无梦的沉睡，当我苏醒时，旭日已然高升，沟壑纵横的大地广袤地展现在眼前。在大地各处投下的阴影笼罩下，它呈现出如某种野生兽皮般条纹斑驳的样貌。随着旭日冉冉上升，阴影渐渐消退，仿佛岩石正在重新吸纳昨夜自其体内渗透而出的幽暗。
在此之前，我爱我的阿拉伯母马与我的藏书，恐怕胜过爱世上活着的男女凡人；然而直到此刻，我的灵魂终于开始极度渴望人的存在，我甚至渴望寻觅那些被渡鸦如此含糊地描绘为与我最相近的异邦住民。怀着沉重却满怀期冀的心情，脑海中萦绕着不知自己究竟是否还在朝任何确定方向迈进的犹疑，我疲惫不堪地沿着“西北偏南”的方向跋涉前行。
