It hardly needs to be said that the aristocracy of Homeville and all the summer visitors and residents devoted their time to getting as much pleasure and amusement out of their life as was to be afforded by the opportunities at hand: Boating, tennis, riding, driving; an occasional picnic, by invitation, at one or the other of two very pretty waterfalls, far enough away to make the drive there and back a feature; as much dancing in an informal way as could be managed by the younger people; and a certain amount of flirtation, of course (but of a very harmless sort), to supply zest to all the rest. But it is not intended to give a minute account of the life, nor to describe in detail all the pursuits and festivities which prevailed during the season.

「俺穿俺那套『镀金马具』那回，你可不是这话，」大卫咧嘴说，「你可没说俺一句囫囵好话。」
「哼！俺看是有点儿分别。」比克斯比太太鄙夷地说，她兄弟哈哈一笑。
「你盘算怎么过去？」他望着咱这位朋友的晚礼鞋问。
「我本想走着去，」是回答，「不过我想顺路在罗宾逊家停一停，请他把我送过去。」
「俺看你还是省了这份心，」大卫宣布，「汤姆早套好了，专等你过去；你一预备停当，拉铃就是。」
「您们太好了，」约翰感激地说，「可我说不准什么时候回来。」
「你什么时候尽兴什么时候回。」哈勒姆先生说。

===Original===

“If you keep him an’ the hoss waitin’ a spell, I guess they won’t take cold this weather.”

===Chinese===

「你就是叫他跟马多等上一阵，这样的天气，俺想他们也不会着凉。」
