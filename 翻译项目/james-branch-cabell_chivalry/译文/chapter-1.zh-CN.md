## I / 塞斯蒂纳的故事

===Original===
"Armatz de fust e de fer e d'acier,

Mos ostal seran bosc, fregz, e semdier,

E mas cansos sestinas e descortz,

E mantenrai los frevols contra 'ls fortz."

===Chinese===
"我身披木与铁与钢的铠甲，

我的居所将是森林、寒冷与道路，

我的歌是塞斯蒂纳与争辩曲，

我将护佑弱者对抗强者。"

===Original===
The first novel.——Alianora of Provence, coming in disguise and in adversity to a certain clerk, is by him conducted across a hostile country; and in that troubled journey are made manifest to each the snares which had beguiled them aforetime.

===Chinese===
第一篇传奇。——普罗旺斯的艾丽亚诺拉（Alianora），乔装潦倒来到一位教士面前，由他引领穿越敌国；在这段艰险旅途中，彼此得以洞见从前将他们引入歧途的种种陷阱。

===Original===
In this place we have to do with the opening tale of the Dizain of Queens. I abridge, as afterward, at discretion; and an initial account of the Barons' War, among other superfluities, I amputate as more remarkable for veracity than interest. The result, we will agree at outset, is that to the Norman cleric appertains whatever these tales may have of merit, whereas what you find distasteful in them you must impute to my delinquencies in skill rather than in volition.

===Chinese===
此处所述乃《十女王传》（Dizain of Queens）的开篇故事。我如后文一样，酌情删繁就简；关于男爵战争的初始叙述及其他冗余之处，因所述虽属确凿却乏味可厌，一并割爱。结果，我们在开篇便达成共识：这些故事若有可取之处，当归功于那位诺曼教士；而其中令诸位感到不适之处，务必归咎于我的技艺不精，而非用心不诚。

===Original===
Within the half hour after de Giars' death (here one overtakes Nicolas mid-course in narrative) Dame Alianora thus stood alone in the corridor of a strange house. Beyond the arras the steward and his lord were at irritable converse.

===Chinese===
在德·雅尔（de Giars）死后半小时内（此处叙事追上了尼古拉斯的讲述），艾丽亚诺拉夫人独自伫立于一所陌生宅邸的走廊中。帷幔彼端，管家正与主人恼怒地交谈。

===Original===
First, "If the woman be hungry," spoke a high and peevish voice, "feed her. If she need money, give it to her. But do not annoy me."

===Chinese===
首先传来一个尖厉而不耐烦的声音："若这女人饿了，就喂她。若她需要钱，就给她。但别来烦我。"

===Original===
"This woman demands to see the master of the house," the steward then retorted.

===Chinese===
"这女人要求面见宅邸的主人，"管家反驳道。

===Original===
"O incredible Boeotian, inform her that the master of the house has no time to waste upon vagabonds who select the middle of the night as an eligible time to pop out of nowhere. Why did you not do so in the beginning, you dolt?" The speaker got for answer only a deferential cough, and very shortly continued: "This is remarkably vexatious. *Vox et praeterea nihil*——which signifies, Yeck, that to converse with women is always delightful. Admit her." This was done, and Dame Alianora came into an apartment littered with papers, where a neat and shriveled gentleman of fifty-odd sat at a desk and scowled.

===Chinese===
"噢，不可思议的波奥提亚人（Boeotian）！告诉她，宅邸的主人没有闲暇去搭理那些偏偏选在半夜三更从不知何处冒出来的流浪汉。你当初为何不这样打发她，你这蠢货？"说话者得到的回答不过是一声恭敬的咳嗽，随即他很快又说："这实在令人恼火。*Vox et praeterea nihil*（空有声音，别无他物）——叶克（Yeck），此话的意思是，与女子交谈总是令人愉快的。让她进来。"门开了，艾丽亚诺拉夫人走进一间堆满纸张的房间，一位年过五旬、整洁而清瘦的绅士坐在书桌前，满脸阴云。

===Original===
He presently said, "You may go, Yeck." He had risen, the magisterial attitude with which he had awaited her entrance cast aside. "Oh, God!" he said; "you, madame!" His thin hands, scholarly hands, were plucking at the air.

===Chinese===
他随即说道："你可以退下了，叶克。"他已站起身来，此前等候她进门时那副威严的架势荡然无存。"噢，天哪！"他说，"是您，夫人！"他那双纤细的、书生的手在空中无助地抓挠着。

===Original===
Dame Alianora had paused, greatly astonished, and there was an interval before she said, "I do not recognize you, messire."

===Chinese===
艾丽亚诺拉夫人驻足不动，大为惊异，隔了好一会儿才说："我不认得您，骁骑爵士（Messire）。"

===Original===
"And yet, madame, I recall very clearly that some thirty years ago the King-Count Raymond Bérenger, then reigning in Provence, had about his court four daughters, each one of whom was afterward wedded to a king. First, Meregrett, the eldest, now regnant in France; then Alianora, the second and most beautiful of these daughters, whom troubadours hymned as the Unattainable Princess. She was married a long while ago, madame, to the King of England, Lord Henry, third of that name to reign in these islands."

===Chinese===
"然而，夫人，我记得清清楚楚，大约三十年前，普罗旺斯在位的国王-伯爵雷蒙·贝伦杰（Raymond Bérenger）身边有四位公主，后来每人都嫁给了国王。先是长女梅雷格蕾特（Meregrett），如今是法兰西的王后；继而是次女、诸女中最美丽的艾丽亚诺拉，吟游诗人称颂她为'不可企及的公主'。她很久以前便嫁给了英格兰国王亨利大人（Lord Henry），乃此岛国第三位以亨利为名的君主。"

===Original===
Dame Alianora's eyes were narrowing. "There is something in your voice," she said, "which I recall."

===Chinese===
艾丽亚诺拉夫人的双眸眯了起来。"您的声音里有某种东西，"她说，"我记得。"

===Original===
He answered: "Madame and Queen, that is very likely, for it is a voice which sang a deal in Provence when both of us were younger. I concede with the Roman that I have somewhat deteriorated since the reign of Cynara. Yet have you quite forgotten the Englishman who made so many songs of you? They called him Osmund Heleigh."

===Chinese===
他答道："夫人，也是王后陛下，这极有可能，因为这副嗓子在普罗旺斯唱过不少歌，那时你我都还年轻。我承认，按那位罗马人的说法，自我爱上茜娜拉（Cynara）的时代以来，我已经衰老了许多。然而，您当真忘了那个为您谱了那么多歌的英格兰人？他们叫他奥斯蒙德·黑利（Osmund Heleigh）。"

===Original===
"He made the Sestina of Spring which won the violet crown at my betrothal," the Queen said; and then, with eagerness: "Messire, can it be that you are Osmund Heleigh?" He shrugged assent. She looked at him for a long time, rather sadly, and demanded if he were the King's man or of the barons' party.

===Chinese===
"他在我的订婚礼上谱写了那首《春之塞斯蒂纳》（Sestina of Spring），赢得了紫罗兰花冠，"王后说；继而急切地追问："骁骑爵士，莫非您就是奥斯蒙德·黑利？"他耸肩以示承认。她久久地凝视他，目光中有几分悲凉，然后问他究竟是国王的人还是男爵一派。

===Original===
The nervous hands were raised in deprecation. "I have no politics," Messire Heleigh began, and altered it, gallantly enough, to, "I am the Queen's man, madame."

===Chinese===
那双紧张的手抬起以示否认。"我不涉政事，"黑利骁骑爵士开口道，随即改口——改得颇为殷勤——"我是王后陛下的人，夫人。"

===Original===
"Then aid me, Osmund," she said.

===Chinese===
"那就帮我吧，奥斯蒙德，"她说。

===Original===
He answered with a gravity which singularly became him, "You have reason to understand that to my fullest power I will aid you."

===Chinese===
他以一种与他格外相称的庄重口吻答道："您有理由相信，我将竭尽全力相助。"

===Original===
"You know that at Lewes these swine overcame us." He nodded assent. "Now they hold the King, my husband, captive at Kenilworth. I am content that he remain there, for he is of all the King's enemies the most dangerous. But, at Wallingford, Leicester has imprisoned my son, Prince Edward. The Prince must be freed, my Osmund. Warren de Basingbourne commands what is left of the royal army, now entrenched at Bristol, and it is he who must liberate my son. Get me to Bristol, then. Afterward we will take Wallingford." The Queen issued these orders in cheery, practical fashion, and did not admit opposition into the account, for she was a capable woman.

===Chinese===
"您知道在刘易斯（Lewes），那些猪猡打败了我们。"他点头表示知晓。"如今他们将我的丈夫——国王——囚禁在凯尼尔沃思（Kenilworth）。我倒情愿他留在那儿，因为他是所有国王之敌中最危险的一个。但莱斯特（Leicester）将我的儿子爱德华王子（Prince Edward）囚于沃灵福德（Wallingford）。王子必须获救，我的奥斯蒙德。沃伦·德·贝辛伯恩（Warren de Basingbourne）统率着王军残部，现驻守布里斯托尔（Bristol），由他来解救我的儿子。那么，护送我到布里斯托尔去。之后我们再拿下沃灵福德。"王后以欢快而务实的方式发布了这些命令，不容许任何异议，因为她是个果决的女人。

===Original===
"But you, madame?" he stammered. "You came alone?"

===Chinese===
"可是您呢，夫人？"他结结巴巴地说，"您是独自来的？"

===Original===
"I come from France, where I have been entreating——and vainly entreating——succor from yet another monkish king, the holy Lewis of that realm. Eh, what is God about when He enthrones these whining pieties! Were I a king, were I even a man, I would drive these smug English out of their foggy isle in three days' space! I would leave alive not one of these curs that dare yelp at me! I would——" She paused, anger veering into amusement. "See how I enrage myself when I think of what your people have made me suffer," the Queen said, and shrugged her shoulders. "In effect, I skulked back in disguise to this detestable island, accompanied by Avenel de Giars and Hubert Fitz-Herveis. Tonight some half-dozen fellows——robbers, thorough knaves, like all you English——attacked us on the common yonder and slew the men of our party. While they were cutting de Giars' throat I slipped away in the dark and tumbled through many ditches till I spied your light. There you have my story. Now get me an escort to Bristol."

===Chinese===
"我从法兰西来，在那里我一直在恳求——徒劳地恳求——那个王国里又一位僧侣般国王的援助，即圣路易（Lewis）。唉，上帝高坐云端，面对那些呜呜咽咽的虔信者究竟在做什么！假若我是一国之君，哪怕是堂堂男儿，我三天之内便将这些自鸣得意的英格兰人从他们的雾霭小岛上赶出去！我要叫那些竟敢对我狂吠的杂种一个不留地活不成！我要——"她住了口，怒气转为好笑。"瞧，一想到你们英格兰人让我受的苦，我就忍不住发火，"王后说着，耸了耸肩。"总而言之，我乔装潜回了这座可恨的岛，随行的是阿韦内尔·德·雅尔（Avenel de Giars）和休伯特·菲茨-赫维斯（Hubert Fitz-Herveis）。今晚大约五六个家伙——强盗，彻头彻尾的恶棍，跟你们所有英格兰人一样——在那边的荒地上袭击了我们，杀了我们的人。趁他们割雅尔的喉咙时，我在黑暗中溜走了，跌跌撞撞过了许多沟渠，直到看见了您的灯光。这就是我的经历。现在，给我一支护卫队去布里斯托尔。"

===Original===
It was a long while before Messire Heleigh spoke. Then, "These men," he said——"this de Giars and this Fitz-Herveis——they gave their lives for yours, as I understand it——*pro caris amicis*. And yet you do not grieve for them."

===Chinese===
黑利骁骑爵士过了好一会儿才开口。"这些人，"他说——"这个德·雅尔和这个菲茨-赫维斯——据我所知，他们为你献出了生命——*pro caris amicis*（为了亲爱的朋友）。然而你并不为他们悲伤。"

===Original===
"I shall regret de Giars," the Queen acknowledged, "for he made excellent songs. But Fitz-Herveis?——foh! the man had a face like a horse." Again her mood changed. "Many persons have died for me, my friend. At first I wept for them, but now I am dry of tears."

===Chinese===
"我会怀念德·雅尔的，"王后承认道，"因为他写了极好的歌。但菲茨-赫维斯？——呸！那人的脸跟马一样。"她的情绪又变了。"有许多人曾为我而死，我的朋友。起初我为他们哭泣，如今我的泪已流干。"

===Original===
He shook his head. "Cato very wisely says, 'If thou hast need of help, ask it of thy friends.' But the sweet friend that I remember was a clean eyed girl, joyous and exceedingly beautiful. Now you appear to me one of those ladies of remoter times——Faustina, or Jael, or Artemis, the King's wife of Tauris——they that slew men, laughing. I am somewhat afraid of you, madame."

===Chinese===
他摇了摇头。"加图（Cato）极为明智地说过：'若你需要帮助，就向朋友求助。'但我记忆中那位甜蜜的朋友是个目光清澈的少女，欢快而绝美。如今您在我看来，是那些久远时代的女子——福斯蒂娜（Faustina）或雅亿（Jael）或阿耳忒弥斯（Artemis），陶里斯国王之妻——她们笑着杀人。我有些怕您，夫人。"

===Original===
She was angry at first; then her face softened. "You English!" she said, only half mirthful. "Eh, my God! you remember me when I was a high hearted young sorceress. Now the powers of the Apsarasas have departed from me, and time has thrust that Alianora, who was once the Unattainable Princess, chin deep in misery. Yet even now I am your Queen, messire, and it is not yours to pass judgment upon me."

===Chinese===
她先是愤怒，继而面色柔和下来。"你们英格兰人！"她说，半是打趣。"唉，我的上帝！您记得的还是那个意气风发的年轻女巫。如今阿布萨罗（Apsarasas）的法力已离我而去，时间将那个曾是'不可企及的公主'的艾丽亚诺拉推入齐颈深的苦难。但即便如此，我仍是您的王后，骁骑爵士，由不得您来对我评头论足。"

===Original===
"I do not judge you," he returned. "Rather I cry with him of old, *Omnia incerta ratione*! and I cry with Salomon that he who meddles with the strife of another man is like to him that takes a hound by the ears. Yet listen, madame and Queen. I cannot afford you an escort to Bristol. This house, of which I am in temporary charge, is Longaville, my brother's manor. Lord Brudenel, as you doubtless know, is of the barons' party and——scant cause for grief!——is with Leicester at this moment. I can trust none of my brother's people, for I believe them to be of much the same opinion as those Londoners who not long ago stoned you and would have sunk your barge in Thames River. Oh, let us not blink the fact that you are not overbeloved in England. So an escort is out of the question. Yet I, madame, if you so elect, will see you safe to Bristol."

===Chinese===
"我没有评判您，"他答道。"我不过是像古人那样感叹：*Omnia incerta ratione*（万事皆无定论）！我也像所罗门（Salomon）那样说，插手他人争端的人好比抓住猎犬的耳朵。然而请听我说，夫人兼王后。我无法为您安排一支去布里斯托尔的护卫队。这座宅邸——我暂时代管——是朗阿维尔（Longaville），我兄长的庄园。布鲁德尔勋爵（Lord Brudenel），您想必知道，属男爵一派，而且——没什么好伤心的——此刻正追随莱斯特。我不能信任我兄长的任何手下，因为他们与不久前向您投石、险些将您的船凿沉在泰晤士河里的那些伦敦人想法如出一辙。哦，我们不要讳言您在英格兰并不太受爱戴。因此护卫队是不可能的。然而，夫人，如果您愿意，我将亲自护送您安全抵达布里斯托尔。"

===Original===
"You? Singly?" the Queen demanded.

===Chinese===
"就您一个人？"王后追问道。

===Original===
"My plan is this: Singing folk alone travel whither they will. We will go as jongleurs, then. I can yet manage a song to the viol, I dare affirm. And you must pass as my wife."

===Chinese===
"我的计划是这样的：唯有卖艺之人才能通行无阻。那么，我们就扮作行吟艺人。我敢说，我还能拉着手摇琴唱支歌。而您必须假扮我的妻子。"

===Original===
He said this with simplicity. The plan seemed unreasonable, and at first Dame Alianora waved it aside. Out of the question! But reflection suggested nothing better; it was impossible to remain at Longaville, and the man spoke sober truth when he declared any escort other than himself to be unprocurable. Besides, the lunar madness of the scheme was its strength; that the Queen would venture to cross half England unprotected——and Messire Heleigh on the face of him was a pasteboard buckler——was an event which Leicester would neither anticipate nor on report credit. There you were! these English had no imagination. The Queen snapped her fingers and said: "Very willingly will I be your wife, my Osmund. But how do I know that I can trust you? Leicester would give a deal for me; he would pay any price for the pious joy of burning the Sorceress of Provence. And you are not wealthy, I suspect."

===Chinese===
他说这话时态度坦率。这计划似乎荒诞不经，艾丽亚诺拉夫人起初挥手否决。绝无可能！但思来想去也想不出更好的办法；留在朗阿维尔是不可能的，而他所言——除了他自己，无人可充任护卫——乃不争的事实。况且，这疯狂至极的计划正是其优势所在；王后竟敢只身穿越半个英格兰——而黑利骁骑爵士看上去不过是一面纸板盾牌——此等事件莱斯特既不会预料到，即便听到报告也不会相信。就是这样！这些英格兰人毫无想象力。王后打了个响指，说道："我非常乐意做您的妻子，我的奥斯蒙德。但我怎能确信我信任您呢？莱斯特会为我开出大价钱；为了一把火烧掉普罗旺斯女巫的虔诚快意，他什么都肯付。而且我猜您并不富裕。"

===Original===
"You may trust me, *mon bel esper*,"——his eyes here were those of a beaten child——"because my memory is better than yours." Messire Osmund Heleigh gathered his papers into a neat pile. "This room is mine. Tonight I keep guard in the corridor, madame. We will start at dawn."

===Chinese===
"您大可信任我，*mon bel esper*（我最美好的希望），"——此刻他的眼睛像一个受了委屈的孩子——"因为我的记性比您好。"奥斯蒙德·黑利骁骑爵士将他的纸张归拢成整齐的一摞。"这间房归我。今晚我在走廊里守夜，夫人。我们天亮就出发。"

===Original===
When he had gone, Dame Alianora laughed contentedly. "*Mon bel esper!* my fairest hope! The man called me that in his verses——thirty years ago! Yes, I may trust you, my poor Osmund."

===Chinese===
他走后，艾丽亚诺拉夫人满意地笑了。"*Mon bel esper!* 我最美好的希望！此人在他的诗中这样称呼过我——三十年前！是的，我可以信任你，我可怜的奥斯蒙德。"

===Original===
So they set out at cockcrow. He had procured for himself a viol and a long falchion, and had somewhere got suitable clothes for the Queen; and in their aging but decent garb the two approached near enough to the appearance of what they desired to be thought. In the courtyard a knot of servants gaped, nudged one another, but openly said nothing. Messire Heleigh, as they interpreted it, was brazening out an affair of gallantry before the countryside; and they esteemed his casual observation that they would find a couple of dead men on the common exceedingly diverting.

===Chinese===
于是他们在鸡鸣时分出发。他弄到了一把手摇琴和一柄长弯刀，还从不知何处为王后找到了合适的衣裳；二人穿着虽旧却体面的衣装，看上去与他们希望被认作的身份相去不远。院子里一群仆人目瞪口呆，互相推搡，但谁也不明说。在他们看来，黑利骁骑爵士不过是厚颜无耻地在乡间搞一桩风流韵事；他们觉得他随口说的那句"在荒地上会找到两具死尸"着实有趣。

===Original===
When the Queen asked him the same morning, "And what will you sing, my Osmund? Shall we begin the practise of our new profession with the Sestina of Spring?"——old Osmund Heleigh grunted out: "I have forgotten that rubbish long ago. *Omnis amans, amens*, saith the satirist of Rome town, and with reason."

===Chinese===
那天早上王后问他："您打算唱什么，我的奥斯蒙德？我们践行新行当，是否先来一首《春之塞斯蒂纳》？"——老奥斯蒙德·黑利哼了一声说："那堆废话我老早忘了。*Omnis amans, amens*（每个恋人都是疯子），罗马城的讽刺诗人如是说，此言不虚。"

===Original===
Followed silence.

===Chinese===
继而是一阵沉默。

===Original===
One sees them thus trudging the brown, naked plains under a sky of steel.
