## Endnotes / 尾注

===Original===

For this perplexing matter the curious may consult Paul Verville’s *Notice sur la vie de Nicolas de Caen*, p. 93 et seq. The indebtedness to Antoine Riczi is, of course, conceded by Nicolas in his “Epilogue.” ↩︎

===Chinese===

关于此困惑之处，好奇者可查阅保罗·维尔维尔（Paul Verville）的《尼古拉斯·德·卡昂生平考》（*Notice sur la vie de Nicolas de Caen*）第93页及以下。尼古拉斯在其“尾声”中当然承认了对安托万·里齐（Antoine Riczi）的借鉴。↩︎

===Original===

She was the daughter of King Ferdinand of Leon and Castile, whose conversion to sainthood the inquisitive may find recorded elsewhere. ↩︎

===Chinese===

她是莱昂与卡斯蒂利亚国王费迪南德（Ferdinand）之女，其封圣之事，好奇者可另寻记载。↩︎

===Original===

Not without indulgence in anachronism. But Nicolas, be it repeated, was no Gradgrindian. ↩︎

===Chinese===

不无时代错置之嫌。但须再次强调，尼古拉斯绝非葛擂梗式（Gradgrindian）的刻板之人。↩︎

===Original===

Nicolas gives this ballad in full, but, for obvious reasons, his translator would prefer to do otherwise. ↩︎

===Chinese===

尼古拉斯全文收录了这首民谣，但出于显而易见的原因，其译者宁愿另作处理。↩︎

===Original===

Nicolas unaccountably omits to mention that during the French wars she had ruled England as Regent with signal capacity﻿—although this fact, as you will see more lately, is the pivot of his chronicle. ↩︎

===Chinese===

尼古拉斯莫名地省略了提及，在法兰西战争期间，她曾以摄政王身份卓越地统治英格兰——尽管此事实，如您稍后将见，乃其编年史之关键。↩︎
