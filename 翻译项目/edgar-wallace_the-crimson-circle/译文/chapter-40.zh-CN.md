## XL / 四十

===Original===
The Escape

===Chinese===
越狱

===Original===
The outrage upon Raphael Willings had produced something like a panic in the Cabinet.

Mr. Parr learnt how profound was the concern when he returned to headquarters. And the Prime Minister was justified in his anxiety. The Crimson Circle had not stated when the next blow would fall, or upon whom.

The inspector was sent for to Downing Street, and was closeted with the Prime Minister for two hours. It was the first personal consultation he had had, and it was followed by a meeting of the inner Cabinet, a fact that was duly recorded in the newspapers.

It was said, but without authority, that the life of the Prime Minister had been threatened, and this statement was neither denied nor affirmed.

===Chinese===
对拉斐尔·威林斯下手的这桩暴行，在内阁里掀起了一阵近乎恐慌的风潮。

帕尔先生回到警察总部，才明白这份忧惧有多深重。而首相的焦虑也不无道理。红圈并没有言明，下一击将于何时落下，又将落在谁的头上。

探长奉召前往唐宁街（Downing Street），同首相闭门密谈了两个钟头。这是他头一回与首相当面磋商，随后又举行了内阁核心会议——此事也照例见诸各报。

有传言说，连首相的性命也受到了威胁，不过此说查无实据；而对这一说法，官方既不否认，也不证实。

===Original===
Derrick Yale, returning to his flat that night, found Inspector Parr waiting on the doormat.

“Is anything wrong?” he asked quickly.

“I want your help,” said Parr, and did not speak again until he was sitting in a comfortable chair before the fire in Yale’s sitting room.

===Chinese===
当天夜里，德里克·耶尔回到公寓，发现帕尔探长正候在门前的擦鞋垫上。

“出什么事了？”他急忙问。

“我要你帮个忙。”帕尔说。直到坐进耶尔起居室炉火前一把舒适的椅子里，他才重新开口。

===Original===
“You know, Yale, that I’ve got to go, and the Prime Minister is considering the advisability of my going a little sooner than I had expected. There has been a Cabinet committee appointed, and they are calling into question the methods which headquarters are employing and I have been asked by the Commissioner to attend an informal meeting at the Prime Minister’s house tomorrow evening.”

“What is the idea?” asked Yale.

“I’m to give a sort of lecture,” said Parr gloomily, “and explain to the members of the Cabinet the methods I have employed against the Crimson Circle. You probably know that I have been given unusual powers, and that I have not been asked to tell the Government all I know. I intend doing that on Friday evening, and I want your help.”

“My dear chap, you have it before you ask it,” said Yale warmly, and Parr went on.

===Chinese===
“你是知道的，耶尔，我这身官服总归是要脱下来的，首相正在斟酌，要不要让我走得比原先预想的再早一点。内阁已经指派了一个委员会，正对警察总部采用的种种手段逐一诘问；总监要我出席明晚在首相府举行的一场非正式会议。”

“这是什么名堂？”耶尔问。

“要我来讲一堂课似的东西，”帕尔闷闷不乐地说，“向内阁的成员们讲解我对付红圈用过的种种手段。你大概知道，人家给了我非常的权力，也从没要我把自己知道的一切向政府和盘托出。我打算在星期五晚上就这么办——我要你帮忙。”

“亲爱的老兄，还没等你开口，这忙就已经帮定啦。”耶尔热诚地说。帕尔接着往下讲。

===Original===
“There is still a lot about the Crimson Circle that is a mystery to me, but I am piecing it together. At the moment I am under the impression that there is somebody at police headquarters who is working with them.”

“That is my view, too,” said Yale quickly. “Why do you say that?”

“Well,” said the slow Parr, “I’ll give you an instance. Young Beardmore had a photograph that he found in his father’s papers and this was posted to me. It arrived all right, with the seal of the envelope intact, but when I opened it, there was a blank card. I have since discovered that he gave that card to Thalia Drummond to post; he swears he stood on the doorstep and watched her slip it into the letter-box on the opposite side of the road. If that is the case, the envelope must have been tampered with after it reached headquarters.”

===Chinese===
“关于红圈，还有好些事在我仍是谜，不过我正在一点点把它拼拢来。眼下的印象是：警察总部里有个人，在同他们串通。”

“这也是我的看法。”耶尔应声道，“你为什么这么说？”

“唔，”说话慢条斯理的帕尔说，“我给你举一个例子。小比德莫尔在他父亲的文件里翻出过一张照片，把它寄给了我。东西完好寄到，信封的封口原封未动，可等我拆开一看，里面只有一张空白卡片。我后来查明，那张卡片是他交给塔莉娅·德拉蒙德去寄的；他赌咒发誓，说自己就站在门阶上，眼看着她把它投进了马路对面的邮筒。倘若果真如此，那么这信封必定是在到了警察总部之后，才被人动过手脚的。”

===Original===
“What kind of a photograph?” asked the other curiously.

“It was either a picture of an execution or the condemned man Lightman, for I think it was taken on the occasion when they tried to execute Lightman and failed. It came to old man Beardmore the day before his death﻿—a great number of things seem to have happened to the victims of the Crimson Circle the day before their death﻿—and was found by Jack and, as I say, sent on﻿—”

“By Thalia Drummond!” said Yale significantly. “My view is that you can exonerate the people at headquarters. This girl is deeper in the Crimson Circle than you imagine. I searched her house tonight﻿—that is where I’ve been, and this is what I found.”

===Chinese===
“一张什么样的照片？”对方好奇地问。

“不是一张行刑的相片，就是死刑犯利特曼的相片，因为我想，那是在他们试图处决利特曼而未得手的那一回拍下的。它是在老比德莫尔死的前一天寄到他手里的——红圈的受害者们，似乎总有许许多多的事，偏偏都发生在他们死的前一天——后来让杰克翻了出来，又照我方才说的，寄了过来——”

“偏偏是经塔莉娅·德拉蒙德的手寄的！”耶尔意味深长地说。“依我看，警察总部里那些人倒可以撇清干系了。这姑娘在红圈里陷得比你想象的要深。我今晚搜了她的住处——我方才就是打那儿来——这就是我搜到的东西。”

===Original===
He went out into the hall and returned with a brown paper parcel, opened it, and the inspector stared.

A gauntlet glove and a long bright-bladed knife were exposed when Yale cut the string and stripped away the paper wrapping.

“This glove is a fellow to that which was found in Froyant’s study. The knife is an exact pair to the other.”

===Chinese===
他出门到门厅里，捧回来一个牛皮纸包裹，动手打开，探长不由得瞪直了眼。

耶尔铰断捆绳、揭去纸皮，一只护腕手套和一柄长刃雪亮的刀子露了出来。

“这只手套，同弗鲁瓦扬书房里搜到的那只正是一对；这把刀，也跟另一把一模一样。”

===Original===
Parr took up the gauntlet and examined it.

“Yes, this is the left hand, and the one on Froyant’s desk was the right,” he agreed. “It is a worn motor-glove. Who was the owner? Try your psychometric powers, Yale.”

“I’ve already tried,” said the other, shaking his head, “but the glove has passed through so many hands that the impressions I receive are very confused. At any rate, this discovery confirms the theory that Thalia Drummond is in the business up to her neck. As to the other matter you were speaking about,” he said, as he wrapped the knife and glove carefully in the paper, “I shall be most happy to assist you.”

===Chinese===
帕尔拿起那只护腕手套，细细察看。

“不错，这只是左手的，弗鲁瓦扬书桌上那只是右手的。”他表示赞同。“这是只用旧的驾车手套。物主是谁？耶尔，用你的触物感应试试。”

“我已经试过了。”对方摇着头说，“只是这手套经手的人太多，我接收到的印象乱作一团。无论如何，这一发现坐实了那个推断：塔莉娅·德拉蒙德在这桩买卖里，已经陷到了脖子根。至于你方才提到的另一件事，”他一面把刀和手套仔细包回纸里，一面说，“我极愿效劳。”

===Original===
“What I want from you,” said Parr, “is that you shall fill in the spaces which I cannot fill,” he shook his head. “I only wish mother could be there,” he said regretfully.

“Mother?” said the astonished Yale.

“My grandmother,” said Mr. Parr soberly. “The only detective in England﻿—bar you and I.”

It was the first time that Derrick Yale ever had reason to suspect that Mr. Parr possessed a sense of humour.

===Chinese===
“我要你做的，”帕尔说，“是替我把我填不上的那些空子填上。”他摇了摇头。“我惟愿‘妈妈’也能在场才好。”他怅然说。

“‘妈妈’？”耶尔吃了一惊。

“我的祖母。”帕尔先生一本正经地说。“英格兰独一无二的大侦探——只除了你我两个。”

这是德里克·耶尔生平头一回，有理由疑心帕尔先生竟也有几分幽默感。

===Original===
It was typical of that period of excitement, when the name of the Crimson Circle was on every tongue, that sensation should follow sensation. But probably no incident created so much excitement as that which, in scrawling headlines, greeted Derrick Yale as, sitting in bed sipping his tea, he read the newspaper the following morning!

Thalia Drummond had escaped!

People escape from prison in works of fiction; they have been known to make a temporary getaway from dread Dartmoor, but never before in the history of the prison service had a woman escaped from Holloway. And yet the wardress unlocking the door of Thalia Drummond’s cell in the morning found it empty.

===Chinese===
时局鼎沸，红圈之名挂在人人嘴上，轰动接着轰动，这本是那一时期的常例。可是大概没有哪一桩事情，激起过如此这般的骚动——第二天早晨，德里克·耶尔坐在床上呷着茶读报，一行行歪斜粗大的标题，就这样劈面迎来！

塔莉娅·德拉蒙德越狱了！

小说里的人物越狱，屡见不鲜；也确曾有人从令人闻之色变的达特穆尔（Dartmoor）监狱短暂脱身过。可是在狱政史上，还从来没有一个女人从霍洛韦监狱逃出去过。然而早晨，女看守打开塔莉娅·德拉蒙德的牢门，里面却已空无一人。

===Original===
It took a great deal to shock Derrick Yale, but the news temporarily paralysed him. He read the account of the escape word by word, and in the end he was as mystified as ever.

But there it was in cold print, officially admitted, and communicated to the early morning press by the Government with unnatural haste.

Owing to the unusual importance of the prisoner, and the character of the offence alleged against her, extraordinary precautions were taken to guard her. The patrol which usually visits the ward in which her cell was situated, was doubled, and instead of hourly, half-hourly visits were paid by the officers on duty. It is not customary to look into every cell on these occasions, but at three o’clock this morning the wardress﻿—Mrs. Hardy﻿—looked through the observation hole and saw the prisoner was there. At six o’clock when the cell door was opened, Drummond was missing. The bars of the window were intact, and the door had not been tampered with.

===Chinese===
能叫德里克·耶尔大吃一惊的事本不多，可这条消息竟叫他一时动弹不得。他把那篇越狱纪事逐字逐句读了下去，读到末了，依旧如坠五里雾中。

可是白纸黑字就摆在那里：官方正式承认，并且由政府以一种反常的仓促，通报给了清晨印行的各报。

鉴于该犯身份非同小可，所控罪名的性质又极重大，狱方为看守她采取了非常的防范措施。通常巡查其牢房所在监区的巡班加了一倍，值班狱吏的察看也由每小时一次改为每半小时一次。这类察看望例并不逐间牢房查看，但今日凌晨三点，女看守——哈代（Hardy）太太——曾从观察孔张望，看见犯人还在里面。六点钟打开牢门时，德拉蒙德已经不见踪影。窗上的铁条完好无损，牢门也不曾被人动过手脚。

===Original===
A search of the prison grounds showed no trace of her footsteps, and it is almost impossible that she could have escaped over the wall. It is equally impossible that she could have left by the ordinary means, since it would have necessitated her passing through six separate doors, none of which had been forced, or through the gatekeeper’s lodge, which is occupied throughout the night.

This new proof of the Crimson Circle’s omnipotence and extraordinary powers is very disconcerting, coming, as it does, at a moment when the lives of Cabinet Ministers are threatened by this mysterious gang.

Yale glanced at the clock. It was half-past eleven. And then he looked at the newspaper and saw that his servant had brought him an early edition of one of the evening papers. He was out of bed in a second and, not waiting for breakfast, rushed off to headquarters, to find Inspector Parr in a very good humour, considering all the circumstances.

===Chinese===
搜遍监狱全境，不见她的一丝足迹；要说她是翻墙逃出去的，几乎不可能。说她是走寻常门径出去的，也同样不可能——那就非得穿过六道各自分开的门不可，而没有一道门曾被强行弄开；再不然，就得打整夜有人的门房里经过。

红圈无所不能、神通广大的这一最新明证，实在令人心慌意乱——它偏偏来得正是时候：内阁大臣们的性命，此刻正悬在这个神秘匪帮的威胁之下。

耶尔瞥了一眼时钟：十一点半。接着他再端详那报纸，才看出仆人拿来的是一家晚报的早印版。他转眼间便跳下床来，早饭也顾不得吃，直奔警察总部，却发现帕尔探长——就眼下种种情形而论——心情竟好得很。

===Original===
“But this is incredible, Parr, it is impossible! She must have friends in the prison!”

“That is my idea entirely,” said Parr. “I told the Commissioner in the identical words that she must have friends in the prison. Otherwise,” he said after a pause, “how did she get out?”

Yale looked at him suspiciously. It did not seem the moment or the occasion for flippant talk, and Inspector Parr’s tone was undoubtedly flippant.

===Chinese===
“可这真是难以置信，帕尔，这不可能！她在监狱里一定有自己人！”

“这恰恰正是我的看法。”帕尔说，“我对总监说的，也是一字不差的一句：她在监狱里一定有自己人。要不然，”他顿了一顿，说，“她是怎么出去的呢？”

耶尔狐疑地望着他。无论此时此际，似乎都不是打趣的时候，而帕尔探长的那副口气，分明透着轻佻。
