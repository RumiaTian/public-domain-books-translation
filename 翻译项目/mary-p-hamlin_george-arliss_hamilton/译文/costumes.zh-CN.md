## Costumes / 服装表

===Original===
Act I

**Jefferson**. Cutaway coat of period, dark, heavy material brown corduroy breeches and vest; top boots to knees, plain leather, not polished; stock and plain fall; three-cornered black hat; wig as in plates or paintings.

**Monroe**. Dark green cutaway coat and breeches, light fancy vest; silk stockings, colonial shoes with buckles, stock and lace fall, three-cornered black hat; walking stick. Dark tie wig.

**Giles**. Light brown corduroy, long square cut coat, knee-breeches, snuff-colored plaid vest, stock and fall and hat. Dark tie wig.

**Tallyrand**. Cinnamon cutaway coat; silk vest, black silk stockings and white stripes, black silk knee-breeches, shoes; long black cane with tassel; large Napoleonic hat with black feather; white stock and elaborate lace fall; jewelry, chains, rings, etc. Auburn wig not tied.

**Schuyler**. Wine colored coat and breeches; dark green flowered vest, silk stockings and shoes; stock and lace fall; three-cornered beaver hat trimmed with silk ribbon; walking stick, white tie wig.

**Reynolds**. Prussian blue coat with tails; fancy flowered vest; buff colored riding breeches; top boots, plain leather; stock and fall; gray, three-cornered hat; dark tie wig.

**Melissa**. Buff colored skirt, three-quarters length; short armed, black bodice, laced down centre; fancy apron; shoes and stockings.

**Citizens and Quakers**. Costumes of period.

===Chinese===
第一幕

**杰斐逊**。当时样式的燕尾式外套，深色厚重料子；棕色灯芯绒马裤与背心；及膝长筒靴，素皮，不抛光；领巾配素色胸饰；黑色三角帽；假发照版画或油画中的样式。

**门罗**。深绿色燕尾式外套与马裤，浅色花哨背心；丝袜，带鞋扣的殖民地式鞋子，领巾与蕾丝胸饰，黑色三角帽；手杖。深色束发式假发。

**贾尔斯**。浅棕色灯芯绒，方襟长外套，及膝马裤，鼻烟色格纹背心，领巾、胸饰与帽子。深色束发式假发。

**塔列朗**。肉桂色燕尾式外套；丝绸背心，缀白条纹的黑丝袜，黑色丝质及膝马裤，鞋子；带流苏的黑色长手杖；缀黑羽的大号拿破仑式帽子；白色领巾与繁复的蕾丝胸饰；珠宝、链子、戒指等。赤褐色假发，散披不束。

**斯凯勒**。酒红色外套与马裤；深绿色绣花背心，丝袜与鞋子；领巾与蕾丝胸饰；缀丝带饰边的河狸皮三角帽；手杖，白色束发式假发。

**雷诺兹**。普鲁士蓝带尾外套；花哨的绣花背心；米黄色骑马马裤；素皮长筒靴；领巾与胸饰；灰色三角帽；深色束发式假发。

**梅利莎**。米黄色裙子，长及小腿；黑色短袖紧身胸衣，胸前当中系带收紧；花哨的围裙；鞋子与长袜。

**市民与贵格会教徒**。当时样式的服装。

===Original===
Act II

**Hamilton**. Light gray moire silk coat and knee-breeches, lightish green fancy vest; handsome stock and lace fall, gray silk stockings and colonial shoes with buckles. Medium fair tie wig.

**Schuyler**. As in Act I, with dark colonial cloak.

**Zekiel**. Snuff brown long square coat trimmed with black and steel buttons; breeches of same material; black stockings and shoes.

**Mrs. Whalen**. Long skirt, old dark material; short sleeved bodice of same; headdress, a piece of worn-out lace; shoes and stockings.

**Jefferson**. As in Act I.

**Monroe**. As in Act I.

**Tallyrand**. Rich champagne colored coat with dark red velvet collar; yellow silk breeches; fancy vest of yellow material with pattern; yellow silk stockings; dark shoes; long white cane with tassel; champagne gloves; hat as in Act I.

**Mrs. Reynolds**. Very low coat, cream colored gown of empire style without sleeves and of a very light filmy material with silk petticoat to match; stockings to match and shoes with straps across instep; dark blue cloak of light material with hood, lined with emerald green silk.

===Chinese===
第二幕

**汉密尔顿**。浅灰色云纹绸外套与及膝马裤，淡绿色花哨背心；讲究的领巾与蕾丝胸饰，灰色丝袜与带鞋扣的殖民地式鞋子。中等浅色的束发式假发。

**斯凯勒**。同第一幕，外加深色殖民地式斗篷。

**泽基尔**。鼻烟色方襟长外套，缀黑色与钢质纽扣；同料马裤；黑色长袜与鞋子。

**惠伦太太**。长裙，深色旧料子；同料短袖紧身胸衣；头上一方磨旧的蕾丝头饰；鞋子与长袜。

**杰斐逊**。同第一幕。

**门罗**。同第一幕。

**塔列朗**。华贵的香槟色外套，暗红丝绒领；黄色丝质马裤；带花纹的黄色料子花哨背心；黄色丝袜；深色鞋子；带流苏的白色长手杖；香槟色手套；帽子同第一幕。

**雷诺兹夫人**。领口极低的奶油色帝政式无袖长裙，料子极轻薄通透，配同色丝绸衬裙；同色长袜与足背绊带的鞋子；轻质料子的深蓝色带兜帽斗篷，翡翠绿绸里子。

Act III

**Betsy Hamilton**. Pale pink dress of fine light silk, low cut and without sleeves; shoes and stockings.

**Angelica**. Street costume light brown silk with faint yellow plaid pattern; a dress of pale yellow voile which hangs in a panel down front; bodice with long sleeves and tulle trimming; large picture hat with tulle and marabou trimming; light blue silk shawl with black tassels and jet buttons on corners.

**Hamilton**. Old rose suit of fine silk; fancy vest with delicate pink pattern; black hat and cane.

**Jefferson**. Tight-fitting dark cutaway coat with knee-breeches and buff colored vest; black silk stockings and shoes; stock and lace fall and hat.

**Monroe**. Slate gray coat and knee-breeches; light fancy vest.

**Schuyler**. Dark green coat, flowered waistcoat; black silk breeches.

**Giles**. As in Act I.

**Judge Jay**. Blue coat and breeches, gray vest; blue stockings, shoes; stock and fall; gray tie wig.

===Chinese===
第三幕

**贝特西·汉密尔顿**。浅粉色细薄丝绸长裙，低领无袖；鞋子与长袜。

**安杰莉卡**。外出服为浅棕色丝绸，带隐约的黄色格纹；一幅浅黄色巴里纱垂挂在身前作长条饰片；长袖紧身胸衣，缀网眼纱花边；大号阔边装饰帽，饰以网眼纱与马罗羽毛；浅蓝色丝绸披肩，四角缀黑色流苏与黑玉纽扣。

**汉密尔顿**。暗玫瑰色细绸套装；缀淡雅粉色花纹的背心；黑色帽子与手杖。

**杰斐逊**。贴身的深色燕尾式外套，配及膝马裤与米黄色背心；黑色丝袜与鞋子；领巾、蕾丝胸饰与帽子。

**门罗**。石板灰外套与及膝马裤；浅色花哨背心。

**斯凯勒**。深绿色外套，绣花背心；黑色丝质马裤。

**贾尔斯**。同第一幕。

**杰伊法官**。蓝色外套与马裤，灰色背心；蓝色长袜，鞋子；领巾与胸饰；灰色束发式假发。

===Original===
Act IV

**Betsy**. Street costume of heavy dark blue silk with thin violet stripes; hat with feather, gloves and silk shawl.

**Angelica**. Rich gown of heavy yellow silk with brocaded buff showing a panel in front of pale violet tulle; short sleeves to elbow with lace trimmings.

**Hamilton**. Black velvet suit.

**Colonel Lear**. Military costume of period.

**Jefferson, Monroe, Schuyler and Jay**. As in Act III.

**Mrs. Reynolds**. Long trailing skirt of white messaline with polka dot pattern and red stripe; light gray tight fitting bodice with red tulle shawl; light green bonnet with rose in front on long stem; large feather muff.

===Chinese===
第四幕

**贝特西**。外出服为深蓝色厚绸，带纤细的紫色条纹；饰羽帽子、手套与丝绸披肩。

**安杰莉卡**。华贵的黄色厚绸长裙，米黄色锦纹，身前露出浅紫色网眼纱饰片；短袖及肘，缀蕾丝花边。

**汉密尔顿**。黑色丝绒套装。

**利尔上校**。当时样式的军装。

**杰斐逊、门罗、斯凯勒与杰伊**。同第三幕。

**雷诺兹夫人**。白色梅斯林软缎曳地长裙，带波点花纹与红色条纹；浅灰色贴身紧身胸衣，配红色网眼纱披巾；浅绿色软帽，前面簪一朵长茎玫瑰；大号羽毛手筒。
