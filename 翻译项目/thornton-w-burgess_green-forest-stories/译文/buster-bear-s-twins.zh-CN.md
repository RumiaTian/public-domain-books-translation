## Buster Bear's Twins / 巴斯特熊的双胞胎

## I Mother Bear's Secret / 熊妈妈的秘密

===Original===
> The best kept secret soon or lat, sire
 Will be found out as sure as fate.
> — Mother Bear

Have you ever wanted to be in a number of places at the same time? Then you know exactly how Peter Rabbit felt in the beautiful springtime. You see, there was so much going on everywhere all the time that Peter felt sure he was missing something, no matter how much he saw and heard. In that he was quite right.
But you may be sure Peter did his best not to miss any more than he had to. He scampered lipperty-lipperty-lip this way, lipperty-lipperty-lip that way, and lipperty-lipperty-lip the other way, watching, listening, asking questions and making a nuisance of himself generally. For a while there were so many new arrivals in the Old Orchard and on the Green Meadows, feathered friends returning from the Sunny South and in a great hurry to begin housekeeping, and strangers passing through on their way to the Far North, that Peter hardly gave the Green Forest a thought.

===Chinese===
> 不管秘密藏得多深，终究会被发现，
 就像命运一样躲也躲不过。
> — 熊妈妈

你有没有过想同时出现在好几个地方的时候？那你就一定能体会彼得兔在美丽春天里的感受了。你看，到处都热闹极了，不管彼得兔看到了多少、听到了多少，他总觉得自己错过了什么。这一点他倒是一点没错。
不过你尽可以相信，彼得兔已经尽力不错过任何事情了。他蹦蹦跳跳、蹦蹦跳跳，一会儿往这边跑，一会儿往那边跑，又一会儿往另一个方向跑去，看看、听听、问这问那，总的来说就是到处惹人嫌。有那么一段时间，老果园和绿草地上来了好多新朋友，毛茸茸的朋友们从温暖的南方赶回来，急急忙忙地开始安家，还有路过的陌生客人要往遥远的北方去，以至于彼得兔几乎都想不起绿森林了。

===Original===
But one moonlight night he happened to think of Paddy the Beaver and that he hadn't seen Paddy since before Paddy's pond froze over early in the winter.
"I must run over and pay him my respects," thought Peter.
"I certainly must. I wonder if he is as glad as the rest of us that Sweet Mistress Spring is here."
No sooner did he think of this than Peter started, lipperty-lipperty-lip, through the Green Forest for the pond of Paddy the Beaver. Now the nearest way was past the great windfall where Mrs. Bear made her home. Peter hadn't thought of this when he started. He didn't think of it until he came in sight of it. The instant he saw that old windfall he stopped short. He remembered Mrs. Bear and that he had heard that she had a secret. Instantly curiosity took possession of him. He forgot all about Paddy the Beaver.
For some time Peter sat perfectly still, looking and listening. There was no sign of Mrs. Bear. Was she under that windfall in her bedroom taking a nap, or was she off somewhere? Peter wished he knew. It was such a lovely night that he had a feeling Mrs. Bear was out somewhere.

===Chinese===
但是有一个月光皎洁的夜晚，彼得兔突然想起了海狸帕迪。他想起自打冬天池塘结冰以来，就再也没见过帕迪了。
"我得去拜访拜访他，"彼得兔想，"我一定要去。不知道他是不是和我们一样高兴，甜蜜的春姑娘已经来了。"
他一想到这个，就蹦蹦跳跳地出发了，穿过绿森林，朝海狸帕迪的池塘跑去。最近的路正好要经过熊太太住的那个大倒木堆。彼得兔出发的时候可没想到这一点，直到看见了才想起来。一看到那个旧倒木堆，他猛地站住了。他想起了熊太太，还有他听说过的那个秘密。好奇心一下子就占了上风。他把海狸帕迪忘得一干二净。
彼得兔一动不动地坐了好一会儿，看着、听着。熊太太一点影子都没有。她是在倒木堆底下的卧室里打盹呢，还是去了别的地方？彼得兔真想知道。今晚的月色这么好，他觉得熊太太大概出门去了。

===Original===
A hop at a time, pausing to look and listen between hops, Peter drew nearer to the great windfall. Still there was no sign of Mrs. Bear.
With his heart going pit-a-pat, pit-a-pat, pit-a-pat, Peter drew nearer and nearer to the great windfall, and at last was close to it on the side opposite to Mrs. Bear's entrance. Taking care not to so much as rustle a dry leaf on the ground, Peter stole around the end of the great windfall until he could see the entrance Mrs. Bear always used. No one was in sight. Peter drew a long breath and hopped a little nearer. He felt very brave and bold, but you may be sure that at the same time he was ready to jump and run, as only he can at the least hint of danger.
For a long time Peter sat and stared at that entrance and wished he dared just poke his head inside. If Mrs. Bear really had a secret, it was somewhere inside there. Anyway, that is what old Granny Fox had said. He had almost worked his courage up to the point of taking just one hurried little peek in that entrance when his long ears caught a faint rustling sound under the great windfall.

===Chinese===
彼得兔蹦一下、停一下，看看听听，蹦一下、停一下，看看听听，一点一点地靠近了那个大倒木堆。还是没有熊太太的影子。
彼得兔的心扑通扑通、扑通扑通、扑通扑通地跳着，越靠越近，终于来到了倒木堆的另一边，也就是熊太太入口的对面。彼得兔小心翼翼，连一片干树叶都不让它发出声响，悄悄地绕过倒木堆的尽头，一直绕到熊太太平时使用的入口那里。没有人。彼得兔长长地吸了一口气，又蹦近了一点。他觉得自己又勇敢又大胆，不过你尽可以相信，同时他也做好了随时跳起来逃跑的准备，只要有一点点危险的迹象，他就能做到。
彼得兔在那个入口前坐了很久，盯着看，巴不得自己有胆子把脑袋伸进去看看。如果熊太太真的有秘密，那一定就在里面。不管怎样，老狐狸奶奶就是这么说的。他几乎鼓足了勇气，正要匆匆忙忙往里面偷看一眼的时候，他那双长耳朵捕捉到了倒木堆下面传来的一阵细微的沙沙声。

===Original===
Peter scurried off to a safe distance, then turned and stared at that entrance. He half expected to see Mrs. Bear's great head come poking out and he was ready to take to his heels. Instead a very small head and then another close beside it appeared.
Peter was so surprised he nearly fell over backward. Then in a flash it came to him that he knew Mrs. Bear's secret. It was out at last. Yes, sir, it was out at last. Mrs. Bear had a family! Mrs. Bear and Buster Bear had twins!

## II Peter Scares the Twins / 彼得兔吓坏了双胞胎

> For timid folk no joy is quite
 Like giving other folks a fright.
> — Mother Bear

It isn't often that Peter Rabbit has a chance to scare anyone. You know he is such a timid fellow himself that he is the one who usually gets the fright. So when he does happen to scare someone it always amuses him. Somehow he always has more respect for himself.
When on that moonlight night he discovered Mrs. Bear's secret, he had the most mixed feelings he ever had known. First came surprise, as he saw those two little heads poked out of Mrs. Bear's entrance. He was sitting up very straight and the surprise was so great that he all but tumbled over backwards. You see, there was no mistaking those two little heads for any but those of baby Bears! He knew that those were two Bear cubs, Mrs. Bear's babies, the secret she had kept hidden so long under the great windfall.
And his surprise at seeing those two little heads was only a little greater than his surprise at the smallness of them. So for perhaps two minutes Peter sat motionless, quite overcome with surprise, as he stared at those two funny little heads poked out from the entrance under the great windfall. Then all in a flash he understood the cause of Mrs. Bear's short temper and the reason she drove everybody away from there, and he felt a sudden panic of fright.

===Chinese===
> 胆小的人最开心的事，
莫过于把别人吓一跳。
> — 熊妈妈

彼得兔可不是经常有机会吓唬别人的。你知道他自己就是个胆小鬼，平时总是被吓的那个。所以要是他碰巧吓到了谁，他总是开心得不得了。不知不觉间，他对自己也多了几分敬意。
在一个月光皎洁的夜晚，他发现了熊太太的秘密，那感受可真是复杂极了。一开始是惊讶，因为他看到两个小脑袋从熊太太的入口处探了出来。他坐得笔直，惊讶得差点翻了个跟头。你瞧，那两个小脑袋一看就知道是小熊的！他知道那是两只熊宝宝，是熊太太的宝贝，是她在大倒木堆下藏了那么久的秘密。
他看到那两个小脑袋有多惊讶，发现它们那么小的时候就更惊讶了。于是，彼得兔大概有两分钟一动不动地坐在那里，完全被惊呆了，盯着那两个从大倒木堆底下入口处探出来的滑稽小脑袋。突然之间，他一下子明白了熊太太为什么脾气那么暴躁，为什么要把大家都赶走。一阵突如其来的恐惧涌上了他的心头。

===Original===
"This is no place for me," thought Peter, "and the sooner I get away from here the better." He looked hastily all about. There was no sign of Mrs. Bear. Right then and there curiosity returned in full force.
"I wish those youngsters would come out where I can look at them and just see how big they are," thought Peter. "It seems safe enough here now, and perhaps if I wait a few minutes they will come out."
So Peter waited. Sure enough, in a few minutes the two little cubs did come out. Plainly it was their first glimpse of the Green Forest, and Peter almost laughed right out at the look of wonder on their faces as they stared all about in the moonlight. But not even his first surprise was greater than Peter's surprise now as he saw how small they were.

===Chinese===
"这地方可不适合我，"彼得兔想，"我得赶紧离开这里。"他急急忙忙四处张望。熊太太一点影子都没有。就在这时，好奇心又完全占了上风。
"我真希望那两个小家伙出来让我看看，看看它们到底有多大，"彼得兔想，"这里看起来挺安全的，也许我等上几分钟它们就会出来了。"
于是彼得兔就等着。果然，没过多久，那两只小熊崽真的出来了。显然这是它们第一次看到绿森林，看着它们在月光下到处张望的惊奇表情，彼得兔差点笑出声来。不过他最初的惊讶，还没有现在看到它们有多小来得大。

===Original===
"Why," he exclaimed to himself, "why-ee, they are no bigger than I! I didn't suppose anyone so big as great big Mrs. Bear could have such small children. I wonder how old they are. I wonder how big they were when they were born. I wonder if they will grow fast. I wonder if they will go about with Mrs. Bear. I suppose Buster Bear is their father, and I wonder if he ever comes to see them. They look to me rather wobbly on their legs. I wonder if Mrs. Bear told them they could come out."
And then the imp of mischief whispered to Peter. "I wonder if I can scare them," thought Peter. "It would be great fun to scare a Bear, even if it is nothing but a cub, and to scare two at once would be greater fun."

===Chinese===
"哎呀，"他自言自语道，"哎呀呀，它们跟我差不多大！我真没想到像熊太太那么大的熊居然能生出这么小的孩子。不知道它们有多大了。不知道它们刚出生的时候有多小。不知道它们长得快不快。不知道它们会不会跟着熊太太到处走。我猜巴斯特熊是它们的爸爸，不知道他有没有来看过它们。我看它们的腿还有点晃呢。不知道熊太太是不是告诉它们可以出来了。"
然后那个调皮的小精灵在彼得兔耳边嘀咕起来了。"不知道我能不能吓唬它们，"彼得兔想，"要是能吓到一只熊，哪怕是只小熊崽，那该多好玩啊，一下子吓到两只就更好玩了。"

===Original===
Peter suddenly thumped the ground very hard with his hind feet. It was so still there in the Green Forest that that thump sounded very loud. The two little cubs gave a startled look towards Peter. As he sat up straight in the moonlight, he looked very big. That is, he did to those two little cubs who never had seen him before.
With funny little whimpers of fright they turned and fairly tumbled over each other as they scurried back through the entrance under the great windfall. Peter laughed and laughed until his sides ached. He, Peter Rabbit, actually had frightened two Bears and made them run. Now he would have something to boast about.

## III Peter's Glee Is Short-Lived / 彼得兔的好心情没持续多久

> You'll find it very seldom pays
 To play a joke that works both ways.
> — Mother Bear

As two frightened little cubs ran, whimpering and tumbling over each other, for the safety of the bedroom under the great windfall, Peter Rabbit thumped twice more just by way of adding to their fright. It was most unkind of Peter. Of course. He should have been ashamed of trying to frighten babies, and those two cubs were babies and nothing more. They were baby Bears.
But Peter had so often felt little cold chills of fear chasing each other up and down his backbone in the presence of Buster Bear and Mrs. Bear that it tickled him to be able to scare any Bears, big or little. Truth to tell, it gave him a feeling as if somehow he was getting even with Buster and Mrs. Bear. Of course he wasn't. Certainly not. But he had that feeling, and he didn't once stop to think how cowardly it was to frighten babies, even though they were Bear babies.
After the two cubs had disappeared, he could hear them scrambling along under the great windfall as they hurried for the darkest corner of that dark bedroom where Mother Bear had left them when she went out to look for something to eat. All the way there they whimpered just as if they thought some dreadful enemy was after them. Peter laughed until his sides ached and the tears came to his eyes.

===Chinese===
> 开双向玩笑很少有好下场，
 这个道理你可要记牢。
> — 熊妈妈

两只吓坏了的小熊崽一边呜呜叫，一边跌跌撞撞地跑向大倒木堆底下卧室的安全地带。彼得兔又"咚咚"敲了两下，好让它们更害怕。这可真是太不厚道了。当然啦。他应该为吓唬小宝宝感到羞耻，那两只熊崽就是小宝宝，不过是小熊宝宝罢了。
不过，彼得兔以前在巴斯特熊和熊太太面前，总觉得一股股冷飕飕的恐惧顺着脊梁骨上上下下地窜，所以现在能吓到熊，不管是大熊还是小熊，他都觉得痛快极了。说实话，这让他觉得自己好像在向巴斯特熊和熊太太报仇似的。当然他并没有。绝对没有。但他就是有这种感觉，他根本没有想到吓唬小宝宝——即使是熊宝宝——是多么懦弱的事情。
两只小熊崽消失之后，他还能听到它们在大倒木堆底下慌慌张张地爬行，赶着跑到那个黑乎乎卧室最暗的角落里去——那是熊妈妈出去找吃的之前留它们在那儿的地方。一路上它们呜呜叫个不停，就好像有什么可怕的敌人在追它们似的。彼得兔笑得肚子都疼了，眼泪都流出来了。

===Original===
An angry growl right behind him put a sudden end to Peter's laughter and glee. It was his turn to run headlong and to whimper as he ran. My, what jumps he made! It seemed as if his feet barely touched the ground before he was in the air again. If those little cubs had been scared, Peter was twice as scared. They had run without knowing what they ran from. But Peter knew what he was running from. He was running from an angry mother, and that mother was a Bear. It was enough to make anybody run.
Peter had been so intent on frightening those little cubs and then laughing at them that he had not heard Mother Bear until she had given that angry growl right behind him. Then he hadn't stopped to explain. Peter believes in running first and explaining later. But at the rate he was going now, there wouldn't be any explaining, because by the time he stopped Mother Bear wouldn't be near enough to hear a word he said.
The fact is Mother Bear didn't follow Peter. She simply growled once or twice in her deepest, most grumbly-rumbly voice just to add a little speed to Peter's long legs, if that were possible. Then as she watched Peter run headlong she grinned. Just as Peter had laughed at the fright of the little cubs, Mother Bear grinned at Peter's fright.

===Chinese===
身后传来一声愤怒的吼叫，彼得兔的笑声和好心情一下子全没了。这回轮到他拼命狂奔，边跑边呜咽了。天哪，他跳得可真高！他的脚好像刚碰到地就又飞起来了。如果说那两只小熊崽被吓坏了，那彼得兔的害怕就是它们的两倍。它们不知道自己在跑什么，但彼得兔知道自己在跑什么。他在逃离一个愤怒的妈妈，而这个妈妈是一头熊。这足以让任何人拔腿就跑。
彼得兔刚才一门心思地吓唬那两只小熊崽，又一门心思地笑话它们，以至于熊妈妈就在身后发出那声愤怒的吼叫之前，他根本没听到动静。然后他也没停下来解释。彼得兔相信先跑再说。不过照他现在这速度，根本不会有什么解释的机会了，因为他停下来的时候，熊妈妈恐怕早就听不见他说的任何一个字了。
其实熊妈妈并没有追彼得兔。她不过是最深沉、最沉闷的声音吼了一两声，好让彼得兔的长腿跑得更快一点——如果还有可能更快的话。然后她看着彼得兔拼命逃跑的样子，笑了。就像彼得兔笑话小熊崽的惊恐一样，熊妈妈也笑话彼得兔的惊恐。

===Original===
"I hope that will teach him a lesson," muttered Mrs. Bear, way down in her throat. "I don't want that long-eared bunch of curiosity hanging around here. He got a glimpse of those youngsters of mine, and now my secret will be out. Well, I suppose it would have had to be out soon."
Mrs. Bear turned into the entrance to her bedroom under the windfall, while Peter Rabbit kept on, lipperty-lipperty-lip, lipperty-lipperty-lip, through the Green Forest towards the Green Meadows and the dear Old Briar-patch. He was eager to get there and tell the news of Mrs. Bear's long-kept secret.

## IV Boxer and Woof-Woof / 拳拳和汪汪

> 'Tis sometimes well, it seems to me,
 To see, but appear not to see.
> — Mother Bear

Not in all the Green Forest could two livelier or more mischievous little folks be found than Boxer and Woof-Woof. Boxer was just a wee bit bigger than his sister, but he was no smarter, nor was he the least bit quicker. For more than three months they had lived under the great windfall in the Green Forest without even once poking their funny little noses outside. You see, when they were born they were very small and helpless.
And the first time they had poked their heads out, Peter Rabbit had given them a terrible scare by thumping the ground with his hind feet. Safely back in their bedroom they snuggled together.
"Who do you suppose that terrible fellow was?" whispered Woof-Woof. How that would have pleased Peter could he have heard it!
"I haven't the least idea," replied Boxer. "I guess we are lucky to be safely back here. Did you notice how his ears stood up?"
"We must ask Mother Bear about him," said Woof-Woof. "He was only about our size, and perhaps he isn't so terrible after all. Here she comes now."

===Chinese===
> 有时候我觉得，
看见了装作没看见，也不错。
> — 熊妈妈

在整个绿森林里，你都找不出比拳拳和汪汪更活泼、更调皮的小家伙了。拳拳只比他妹妹大那么一丁点儿，但并不比她聪明，动作也快不到哪里去。他们在绿森林的大倒木堆底下住了三个多月，一次也没有把他们那滑稽的小鼻子伸到外面去。你知道的，他们刚出生的时候非常小，非常无助。
他们第一次探出头来的时候，彼得兔就用后脚使劲跺地，把他们吓得魂飞魄散。他们安全地逃回卧室，紧紧地依偎在一起。
"你说那个可怕的家伙是谁呀？"汪汪小声问。要是彼得兔听到这话，不知道有多开心呢！
"我一点都不知道，"拳拳回答，"我觉得我们能安全地回到这里来就已经很幸运了。你注意到他的耳朵竖得多高了吗？"
"我们得问问熊妈妈，"汪汪说，"他跟我们差不多大，也许他没那么可怕。妈妈来了。"

===Original===
"Let's not say anything about it," whispered Boxer hurriedly. "You know she told us not to go outside. We may see him again sometime and then we can ask her."
So when Mrs. Bear arrived she found Boxer and Woof-Woof curled up with their arms around each other and looking as innocent as it was possible for baby Bears to look. Mother Bear grinned. She knew just what had happened out there, for she had seen it all. You remember that she had frightened Peter Rabbit even more than he had frightened the cubs. But she wisely decided that she would say nothing about it then.
"These cubs have had their first lesson in life," thought she, as she watched them trying so hard to appear to be asleep. "They disobeyed and as a result they got a great fright. I won't tell them that Peter Rabbit is one of the most harmless fellows in all the Great World. They will remember this fright longer if I don't. These scamps are growing like weeds. They went outside tonight while I was away, and that means that it is time to take them out and show them something of the Great World. If I don't, they will try it again while I am away, and something might happen to them. They are still so small that if Old Man Coyote should happen to find one of them alone I am afraid the sly old sinner would make an end of that cub."

===Chinese===
"我们什么都别说，"拳拳急忙小声说道，"你知道她不让我们出去的。说不定以后我们还会碰到他，到那时候再问她吧。"
于是熊太太来了，看到拳拳和汪汪搂在一起蜷缩着，尽量装出一副小熊宝宝最无辜的样子。熊妈妈笑了。她完全知道外面发生了什么，因为她全都看见了。你还记得吧，她把彼得兔吓得比彼得兔吓唬小熊崽还要厉害。但她明智地决定当时什么也不说。
"这两个小家伙上了人生的第一课，"她一边看着他们拼命装睡，一边想，"他们不听话，结果被吓了个够。我才不会告诉他们彼得兔是整个大千世界上最无害的家伙之一。我不说，他们才会把这次惊吓记得更久。这两个小淘气长得可真快。今晚我出去的时候他们偷偷溜了出去，这意味着该带他们出去见见大千世界了。如果我不带他们去，下次我出去的时候他们还会偷偷跑出去，说不定会出什么事。他们还太小了，要是老土狼碰巧找到其中一只落单的，我怕那个狡猾的老家伙会把那只小熊崽给解决了。"

他们睡觉的时候，拳拳必须自己蜷缩着。起初这是最难熬的。但慢慢地他习惯了。他不知道，汪汪也不知道，但熊妈妈知道，这对他是有好处的；这让他越来越自信。每天跟在后面做同样的事也在起同样的作用。他总是寻找熊妈妈和汪汪可能遗漏的东西。所以他学会了比汪汪更好地使用眼睛、鼻子和耳朵，因为她更依赖熊妈妈，就在她脚后跟后面。
日子一天天过去，拳拳的外套上那股可怕的气味越来越淡了。拳拳已经习惯了，根本注意不到，所以他也没有意识到气味什么时候开始变淡的。最后气味淡到几乎察觉不出来了，只有在下雨或非常潮湿的日子里才能注意到。在熊妈妈允许他重新和汪汪待在一起之后很长一段时间里，她仍然在这样的日子里把他赶走。
终于，拳拳的惩罚结束了。熊妈妈好好训了他一顿，说希望这会成为他永远不会忘记的教训。"是的，妈妈，会的，"他非常温顺地回答，他知道这确实会。然后他重新回到了原来的位置，只不过现在他不再跟在熊妈妈脚后跟后面，而是让汪汪去做，他跟在汪汪后面。虽然汪汪没有察觉，但他更喜欢这样。
于是巴斯特熊的双胞胎长大了又长大，直到大家都说他们是绿森林里见过的最好的年轻熊。
比利水貂说这些熊崽已经受到够多的关注了，应该考虑考虑别人了。也许比利是对的，虽然我猜他想的是自己。不管怎样，绿森林系列到此结束，下一本书将是笑池系列的第一本。书名将是《比利水貂》。
