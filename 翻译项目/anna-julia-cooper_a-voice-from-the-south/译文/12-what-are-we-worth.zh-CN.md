### What Are We Worth? / 我们的价值何在？

===Original===
I once heard Henry Ward Beecher make this remark: “Were Africa and the Africans to sink tomorrow, how much poorer would the world be? A little less gold and ivory, a little less coffee, a considerable ripple, perhaps, where the Atlantic and Indian Oceans would come together﻿—that is all; not a poem, not an invention, not a piece of art would be missed from the world.”

===Chinese===
我曾听亨利·沃德·比彻（Henry Ward Beecher）说过这样一句话：「倘若明天非洲和非洲人沉入海底，世界会穷多少？不过是少一点黄金和象牙，少一点咖啡，或许在大西洋与印度洋相合之处泛起一圈可观的涟漪——如此而已；世上不会少一首诗、少一项发明、少一件艺术品。」

===Original===
This is not a flattering statement; but then we do not want flattery if seeing ourselves as others see us is to help us in fulfilling the higher order, “know thyself.” The world is often called cold and hard. I don’t know much about that; but of one thing I am sure, it is intensely practical. Waves of sentiment or prejudice may blur its old eyes for a little while but you are sure to have your bill presented first or last with the inexorable “How much owest thou?” What have you produced, what consumed? What is your real value in the world’s economy? What do you give to the world over and above what you have cost? What would be missed had you never lived? What are you worth? What of actual value would go down with you if you were sunk into the ocean or buried by an earthquake tomorrow? Show up your cash account and your balance sheet. In the final reckoning do you belong on the debit or the credit side of the account? according to a fair and square, an impartial and practical reckoning. It is by this standard that society estimates individuals; and by this standard finally and inevitably the world will measure and judge nations and races.

===Chinese===
这番话并不动听；然而，倘若「以别人眼中的我看自己」有助于我们践行「认识你自己」（know thyself）这条更高的谕令，我们便不想要动听的谀辞。世人常说这个世界冷酷无情。这一点我不甚了然；但有一件事我确信不疑：它极其讲求实际。情感或偏见的波澜，或许会一时模糊它昏花的老眼，但或迟或早，你必定会收到它呈来的账单，附着那句铁面无私的「你欠多少？」（How much owest thou?）你生产了什么，消费了什么？在世界的大经济中，你的真正价值几何？除去你的耗费，你给世界多贡献了什么？倘若你从未活过，会有什么被人怀念？你价值几何？倘若明天你沉入大洋或被地震埋葬，什么实际的价值会随你一同湮灭？亮出你的现金账目和资产负债表吧。在最终的清算里，按一场公平正直、不偏不倚、切合实际的结算，你究竟记在这本账的借方还是贷方？社会正是依这个标准估量个人；而依这个标准，世界最终也必将衡量并裁判各个民族与种族。

===Original===
It may not be unprofitable then for us to address ourselves to the task of casting up our account and carefully overhauling our books. It may be well to remember at the outset that the operation is purely a mathematical one and allows no room for sentiment. The good housewife’s pet chicken which she took when first hatched, fed from her own hand and fondled on her bosom as lovingly as if it were a babe, is worth no more (for all the affection and care lavished on it) when sold in the shambles: and that never-to-be-forgotten black hen that stole into the parlor, flew upon the mantel looking for a nest among those handsome curios, smashed the Sèvres vases and picked the buds from the lovely tea rose﻿—so exasperatingly that the good woman could never again endure the sight of her﻿—this ill-fated bird is worth no less. There are sections of this country in which the very name of the Negro, even in homeopathic doses, stirs up such a storm of feeling that men fairly grow wild and are unfit to discuss the simplest principles of life and conduct where the colored man is concerned; and you would think it necessary for the Ethiopian actually to change his skin before there can be any harmonious living or lucid thinking: there are a few nooks and crannies, on the other hand, in another quarter of the same country, in which that name embodies an idealized theory and a benevolent sentiment; and the black man (the blacker the better) is the petted nursling, the haloed idea, the foregone conclusion. In these Arcadias, it is as good capital as pushing selfishness and aspiring mediocrity need ask, to be advertised as one of the oppressed race and probably born a slave.

===Chinese===
如此说来，着手盘算我们的账目、仔细稽核我们的账簿，于我们未必无益。开宗明义便须记住：这道工序纯粹是数学的，容不得半点感情。善良的主妇那只心爱的小鸡——刚破壳便抱来，亲手喂养，搂在怀里爱抚，宛如疼一个婴孩——待到送进肉档出售时（尽管倾注了那么多疼爱与照料），身价并不因之增加分毫；而那只令人永难忘怀的黑母鸡，溜进客厅，飞上壁炉架，想在那些漂亮古玩中间寻个窝，砸碎了塞夫勒（Sèvres）瓷瓶，又把娇艳的香水月季的花苞啄了个精光——气得那位善良的主妇从此再见不得它——这只倒霉的鸟儿，身价也并不因之减少分毫。这个国家的某些地区，单是「黑人」这个名目，哪怕只消顺势疗法剂量的一星半点，也足以掀起这样一场情感风暴，使人简直发狂，一涉及有色人便连最简单的生活与行为准则也讨论不成：你会以为非等古实人果真换了皮肤，才谈得上和谐的共处与清明的思想；另一方面，在同一个国家的另一隅，也有几处角落旮旯，这个名字在那里凝结成一套理想化的理论与一副仁善的情肠；黑人（越黑越好）是受人宠爱的婴孩、罩着光轮的观念、不证自明的结论。在这些阿卡迪亚（Arcadia）式的世外桃源里，只要标榜自己是受压迫种族的一员、多半生来为奴，便是一份足令急进的自私与攀高的平庸都无可再求的上好资本。

===Original===
But after all sentiment, whether adverse or favorable, is ephemeral. Ever shifting and unreliable, it can never be counted in estimating values. The sentiments of youth are outgrown in age, and we like today what we despised or were indifferent to yesterday. Nine-tenths of the miscalled color prejudice or race prejudice in this country is mere sentiment governed by the association of ideas. It is not color prejudice at all. The color of a man’s face per se has no more to do with his worthiness and companionableness than the color of his eyes or the shades of his hair. You admire the one or think the other more beautiful to rest the gaze upon. But everyone with brains knows and must admit that he must look deeper than this for the man. Mrs. Livermore once said in my hearing: “It is not that the Negro is black; Spaniards, Portuguese, East Indians, enter our parlors, sup at our tables, and, if they have a sufficiently long bank account, they may marry our daughters: but the Negro is weak﻿—and we don’t like weakness.”

===Chinese===
然而，无论厌憎还是偏爱，情感终究朝生暮死。它变幻不定、不可倚恃，估算价值时永远不能计入。青年时的情感到老境便被超越；我们今天喜爱的，正是昨天所鄙夷或漠然的东西。这个国家被误称为肤色偏见或种族偏见的东西，十之八九不过是受观念联想支配的情感。那根本算不得肤色偏见。一个人面孔的颜色本身，与其人之值得敬重、宜于交往与否，关系并不比他眼睛的颜色或头发的深浅更大。你欣赏这一种，或觉得那一种更宜于入眼。但凡有脑筋的人都知道、也必须承认：要找这个人，他必须看得比这更深。利弗莫尔夫人（Mary A. Livermore）有一次当着我的面说：「问题不在于黑人是黑的；西班牙人、葡萄牙人、东印度人照样进我们的客厅、上我们的餐桌，只要他们的银行账户够长，还可以娶我们的女儿：可黑人是软弱的——而我们不喜欢软弱。」

===Original===
Now this dislike it is useless to inveigh against and folly to rail at. We share it ourselves and often carry it to a more unjustifiable extent. For as a rule the narrower the mind and the more circumscribed the experience, the greater will be the exaggeration of accidents over substance, and of circumstance over soul. It does no good to argue with the poor seasick wretch who, even on land after the voyage, is nauseated by the sight of clear spring water. In vain you show the unreason of the feeling. This, you explain, is a different time, a different place, a different stage of progress in the circulation of waters. That was salt, this is fresh, and so on. You might as well be presenting syllogisms to Aetna. “Yes, my dear Fellow,” he cries, “You talk admirably; but you don’t know how I feel. You don’t know how sick I was on that nasty ship!” And so your rhetoric cannot annihilate the association of ideas. He feels; *you know*. But he will outgrow his feeling﻿—and you are content to wait.

===Chinese===
对这种厌恶，痛加抨击无济于事，破口大骂更是愚妄。我们自己同样怀有它，而且往往推到更没有道理的地步。因为大抵心智越狭、阅历越陋，便越是把偶然夸大过本质、把境遇抬高过灵魂。同那个晕船的可怜虫争辩没有用处：即便航程已毕、人已登岸，他一见清冽的泉水仍要作呕。你向他指出这种感受之无理，也是徒然。你解释说：此一时也，彼一时也；此一地也，彼一地也；水的循环已进入另一个阶段。那是咸的，这是淡的，云云。你简直是在向埃特纳火山（Aetna）陈列三段论。「是啊，亲爱的伙计，」他嚷道，「你说得头头是道；可你不知道我是什么感受。你不知道我在那条破船上晕得有多厉害！」于是你的雄辩终究消灭不了观念的联想。他在感受；*你知道*。但他终会长大、脱出这份感受——而你也甘愿等待。

===Original===
Just as impervious to reason is the man who is dominated by the sentiment of race prejudice. You can only consign him to the fatherly hand of Time; and pray that your own mental sight be not thus obscured and your judgment warped in your endeavors to be just and true.

===Chinese===
受种族偏见情感支配的人，同样不为道理所动。你只能把他交托到时间老父的手里；并祈求你自己的心眼，在力求公正与真实之际，不致同样遭翳、你的判断不致同样扭曲。

===Original===
Sentiment and cant, then, both being ruled out, let us try to study our subject as the world finally reckons it﻿—not certain crevices and crannies of the earth, but the cool, practical, businesslike world. What are we worth? not in Georgia nor in Massachusetts; not to our brothers and sisters and cousins and aunts, every one of whom would unhesitatingly declare us worth a great gold-lump; nor to the exasperated neighbor over the way who would be just as ready, perhaps, to write us down a most unmitigated nuisance. But what do we represent to the world? What is our market value. Are we a positive and additive quantity or a negative factor in the world’s elements. What have we cost and what do we come to?

===Chinese===
于是，情感与空论既已双双排除，就让我们按世界最终结算的方式来研究我们的题目——不是按地球这一处那一处的罅隙角落，而是按这个冷静、实际、公事公办的世界。我们价值几何？不是在佐治亚，也不是在马萨诸塞；不是对我们的兄弟姐妹姑舅姨婶——他们每一位都会毫不迟疑地宣称我们值一大块金锭；也不是对马路对面那位怒不可遏的邻人——他或许同样乐于把我们记作一个不折不扣的祸害。而是要问：对世界，我们代表着什么？我们的市价几何？在世界诸元素中，我们是一个正的、可加的量，还是一个负的因子？我们耗费了几何，又折合几许？

===Original===
The calculation may be made in the same way and on the same principle that we would estimate the value of any commodity on the market. Men are not very unlike watches. We might estimate first the cost of material﻿—is it gold or silver or alloy, solid or plated, jewelled or sham paste. Settle the relative value of your raw material, and next you want to calculate how much this value has been enhanced by labor the delicacy and fineness, the honesty and thoroughness of the workmanship; then the utility and beauty of the product and its adaptability to the end and purpose of its manufacture; and lastly is there a demand in the market for such an article. Does it meet a want, *will it go* and *go right*? Is it durable and reliable. How often do you have to wind it before it runs down, how often repair it. Does it keep good time and require but little watching and looking after. And there is no radical difference, after all, between the world’s way of estimating men and our usual way of valuing watches. In both the fundamental item is the question of material, and then the refining and enhancement of that material through labor, and so on through the list.

===Chinese===
这笔账，可以照我们估算市面上任何一件商品之价值的方式与原则来计算。人与表相去不远。我们可以先估材料的成本——是金是银还是合金，是实心是镀层，是镶了宝石还是冒牌料子。定下原材料的相对价值之后，接下来要计算这份价值经由劳动提升了多少——做工的精微与细致、诚实与彻底；再估产品的效用与美观，及其是否合于制造的用途与目的；最后，市面可有对这样一件货品的需求。它是否满足一种需要，*它走得动*，而且*走得准*？它经久可靠么？多久不上弦便会停摆，多久须修一次？它计时准不准，要不要人时时看护照料？说到底，世界估量人的方式，与我们通常估价表的方式，并无根本的不同。两者之中，首要的项目都是材料问题，其次是劳动对材料的精炼与增值，如此逐项推演下去。

===Original===
What then can we say for our raw material?

===Chinese===
那么，关于我们的原材料，我们能说什么呢？

===Original===
Again I must preface an apology for anything unpalatable in our menu. I promised, you remember, to leave out the sentiment﻿—you may stir it in afterwards, mixing thoroughly according to taste. We must discuss facts, candidly and bluntly, without rhetoric or cant if we would have a clear light on our problem.

===Chinese===
这里我得再为菜单上可能难以下咽的东西预致歉意。你总记得，我答应过把感情撇出去——回头你可以再把它搅进来，随口味拌匀。若要让我们的问题得一个清明的亮光，我们就必须坦率而直截地讨论事实，不假辞藻，不说空话。

===Original===
Now whatever notions we may indulge on the theory of evolution and the laws of atavism or heredity, all concede that no individual character receives its raw material newly created and independent of the rock from whence it was hewn. No life is bound up within the period of its conscious existence. No personality dates its origin from its birthday. The elements that are twisted into the cord did not begin their formation when first the tiny thread became visible in the great warp and filling of humanity. When first we saw the light many of the threads undoubtedly were spun and the color and fineness of the weft determined. The materials that go to make the man, the probabilities of his character and activities, the conditions and circumstances of his growth, and his quantum of resistance and mastery are the resultant of forces which have been accumulating and gathering momentum for generations. So that, as one tersely expresses it, in order to reform a man, you must begin with his great grandmother.

===Chinese===
如今，无论我们对进化论、对隔代遗传或遗传的法则作何想法，人人都承认：没有哪一个人的品格，其原材料是凭空新造、可以离弃它被凿出来的那块山岩的。没有哪一重生命，会被封在自己有意识地存在的这段岁月之内。没有哪一个人格，是从它的生日才开始起算的。搓成绳股的纤维，并不等到那根细线初次显现于人类巨大的经纬之中才开始成形。我们初见天光之时，许多线缕无疑早已纺就，纬纱的颜色与粗细也早已注定。构成此人的材料、他品格与活动的可能性、他生长的条件与境遇、他的抵抗力与掌控力，都是种种力量的合力，而这些力量已经积聚增益了世世代代。所以，正如有人言简意赅地说过的：要改造一个人，你必须从他的曾祖母着手。

===Original===
A few years ago a certain social scientist was struck by a remarkable coincidence in the name of a number of convicts in the State prison of New York. There were found thirty-five or forty men, of the same name with but slight modification in the spelling, all convicted of crimes similar in character. Looking into the matter, he traced them every one back to one woman of inferior character who had come from England in one of the first colonial ships. *And that woman had been a convict and charged with pretty nearly the same crime.*

===Chinese===
几年前，某位社会科学家为纽约州立监狱中一批囚犯姓氏的惊人巧合所触动。他发现三十五或四十个男人，姓氏相同、仅拼法上略有出入，全都因性质相似的罪行定罪。追查之下，他把他们一一追溯到一个品行卑下的女人身上——她乘最早的殖民船之一来自英格兰。*而那个女人本身便是个罪犯，所犯的罪名与这批人几乎如出一辙。*

===Original===
Rightly to estimate our material, then, it is necessary to go back of the twenty or thirty years during which we have been in possession, and find out the nature of the soil in which it has been forming and growing.

===Chinese===
因此，要正确估量我们的材料，就必须回溯到我们持有它的这二三十年之前，查明它得以孕育生长的那片土壤的性质。

===Original===
There is or used to be in England a system of entail by which a lot of land was fixed to a family and its posterity forever, passing always on the death of the father to his eldest son. A man may misuse or abuse, he may impoverish, mortgage, sterilize, eliminate every element of value﻿—but he can never sell. He may cut down every tree, burn every fence and house, abstract by careless tillage, or by no tillage, every nutritive element from the soil, encumber it to two or three times its value and destroy forever its beauty and fertility﻿—but he can never rid himself of it. That land with all its encumbrances and liabilities, its barrenness and squalidness, its poverty and its degradation is inexorably, inevitably, inalienably his; and like a shattered and debased personality it haunts him wherever he goes. An heir coming into an estate is thus often poorer than if he had no inheritance. He is chained to a lifelong possession of debt, toil, responsibility, often disgrace. Happier were it for him if he could begin life with nothing﻿—an isolated but free man with no capital but his possibilities, with no past and no pedigree. And so it often is with men. These bodies of ours often come to us mortgaged to their full value by the extravagance, self-indulgence, sensuality of some ancestor. Some man, generations back, has encumbered his estate for strong drink, his descendants coming into that estate have the mortgage to pay off, principal and interest. Another cut down the fences of character by debauchery and vice﻿—and these have to ward off attacks of the enemy without bulwarks or embattlements. They have burnt their houses of purity and integrity, have rendered the soil poor and unproductive by extravagance and folly﻿—and the children have to shiver amid the storms of passion and feed on husks till they can build for themselves a shelter and fertilize their farms. Not very valuable estates, you will say. Well, no﻿—nothing to boast of, perhaps. But an energetic heir can often pay off some of the liabilities and leave the estate to his children less involved than when he received it. At least he can arrest the work of destruction and see to it that no further encumbrances are added through his folly and mismanagement.

===Chinese===
英格兰有一种（或者说曾经有一种）限定继承制：一片土地被永远固定给一个家族及其后裔，父亲一死便总是传给长子。一个人可以滥用、糟蹋，可以使它贫瘠、抵押、荒芜，耗尽其中每一种价值要素——但他永远不能出售。他可以砍光每一棵树，烧掉每一道围篱和每一座房屋，以粗放的耕作或索性全不耕作，把土壤的养分攫取殆尽，使它负上两三倍于其价值的债累，永远毁掉它的秀美与丰饶——但他永远摆脱不掉它。那片土地，连同它全部的债累与责任、荒瘠与腌臜、贫困与堕落，铁面无私地、无可逃遁地、不可让渡地属于他；又如一个破碎堕落的人格，他走到哪里，它便跟到哪里。这样，继承一份产业的人往往比没有继承反倒更穷。他被锁进一份终身的占有：债务、劳苦、责任，往往还有耻辱。他若能白手起家倒更幸福——一个孤零却自由的人，除了自身的可能性之外身无长物，没有过去，也没有家谱。人也往往如此。我们这副身体来到我们手中时，常常已被某位祖先的奢靡、放纵与耽于情欲抵押到了足值。某个人隔了几代，为烈饮而抵押了祖产，子孙承产时便须连本带利偿清那笔抵押。另一位以放浪与邪恶拆毁了品格的围篱——这些人便只得在没有堡垒与壁垒的情况下抵御仇敌的攻击。他们烧掉了纯洁与正直的房舍，以奢靡与愚行把土壤弄得贫瘠不毛——儿女们便只得在激情的风暴里瑟缩，拿豆荚充饥，直到能为自己筑起一个庇护所、为自己的田地施肥。你会说，这不是什么值钱的产业。唉，确实——或许没什么可夸耀的。但一个精力充沛的继承人，往往能偿清一部分负债，把产业交到儿女手中时，比他接手时不那么债台高筑。至少他可以遏止那场破坏，留心别再因自己的愚蠢与管理不善添上新债。
