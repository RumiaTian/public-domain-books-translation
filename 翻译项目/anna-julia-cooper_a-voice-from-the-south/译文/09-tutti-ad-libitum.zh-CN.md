## Tutti ad Libitum / 随意合奏

===Original===
> A *People* is but the attempt of many
 

 To rise to the completer life of one.
> — Robert Browning

===Chinese===
> 一个*民族*，不过是众人的尝试，
 

 力求上升到一个人更完满的生命。
> —— 罗伯特·勃朗宁

===Original===
> The common *Problem*, yours, mine, every one’s
 

 Is﻿—not to fancy what were fair in life
 

 Provided it could be﻿—but, finding first
 

 What may be, then find how to make it fair
 

 Up to our means; a very different thing!
> — Robert Browning

===Chinese===
> 共同的*难题*，你的、我的、每个人的，
 

 不在于悬想人生中何者为美好——
 

 倘若它能够实现——而在于先探明
 

 何者为可能，再寻出如何使之美好，
 

 尽我们之力；这可是截然不同的两回事！
> —— 罗伯特·勃朗宁

===Original===
> The greatest question in the world is how to give every man a man’s share in what goes on in life﻿—we want a freeman’s share, and that is to think and speak and act about what concerns us all, and see whether these fine gentlemen who undertake to govern us are doing the best they can for us.
> — Felix Holt

===Chinese===
> 世上最重大的问题，是如何让每个人在人生的种种事务中都分得一份属「人」的份额——我们要的是一个自由人的份额：也就是就关乎我们大家的事务去思考、去言说、去行动，并看一看，那些揽下治理我们之责的体面绅士们，究竟是否在为我们竭尽所能。
> —— 菲利克斯·霍尔特（Felix Holt）
