### Has America a Race Problem; If So, How Can It Best Be Solved? / 美国有种族问题吗？若有，如何解决为佳？

===Original===
There are two kinds of peace in this world. The one produced by suppression, which is the passivity of death; the other brought about by a proper adjustment of living, acting forces. A nation or an individual may be at peace because all opponents have been killed or crushed; or, nation as well as individual may have found the secret of true harmony in the determination to live and let live.

===Chinese===
世间的和平有两种。一种由压制而生，那是死亡的沉寂；另一种则由种种活生生的、行动中的力量的恰当调适而达成。一个民族或一个人得享太平，可能是因为一切对手都已被杀死或碾碎；但民族也罢、个人也罢，也同样可能在「自己活，也让别人活」的决心之中，寻得真正和谐的奥秘。

===Original===
A harmless looking man was once asked how many there were in his family.

===Chinese===
从前有人问一位长相无害的男子，他家里一共有几口人。

===Original===
“Ten,” he replied grimly; “my wife’s a one and I a zero.” In that family there was harmony, to be sure, but it was the harmony of a despotism﻿—it was the quiet of a muzzled mouth, the smoldering peace of a volcano crusted over.

===Chinese===
「十口，」他阴沉沉地答道，「我老婆是一，我是个零。」那个家里确有和谐，这不假，但那是一个专制之家的和谐——那是被堵住的嘴的沉默，是结了壳的火山闷烧着的和平。

===Original===
Now I need not say that peace produced by suppression is neither natural nor desirable. Despotism is not one of the ideas that man has copied from nature. All through God’s universe we see eternal harmony and symmetry as the unvarying result of the equilibrium of opposing forces. Fair play in an equal fight is the law written in Nature’s book. And the solitary bully with his foot on the breast of his last antagonist has no warrant in any fact of God.

===Chinese===
无须我多言，由压制产生的和平既不自然，也不可取。专制并不是人类从自然摹写来的观念之一。纵观上帝的宇宙，我们处处看见永恒的和谐与匀称，无不是对立力量达于平衡的不变结果。公平较量、机会均等，是写在自然之书上的法则。而那个把脚踏在最后一个对手胸膛上的孤零零的恶霸，在上帝的任何一件事实里都找不到凭据。

===Original===
The beautiful curves described by planets and suns in their courses are the resultant of conflicting forces. Could the centrifugal force for one instant triumph, or should the centripetal grow weary and give up the struggle, immeasurable disaster would ensue﻿—earth, moon, sun would go spinning off at a tangent or must fall helplessly into its master sphere. The acid counterbalances and keeps in order the alkali; the negative, the positive electrode. A proper equilibrium between a most inflammable explosive and the supporter of combustion, gives us water, the bland fluid that we cannot dispense with. Nay, the very air we breathe, which seems so calm, so peaceful, is rendered innocuous only by the constant conflict of opposing gases. Were the fiery, never-resting, all-corroding oxygen to gain the mastery we should be burnt to cinders in a trice. With the sluggish, inert nitrogen triumphant, we should die of inanition.

===Chinese===
行星与太阳在轨道上划出的优美曲线，正是种种相冲突力量的合力。倘若离心力得逞于一瞬，或者向心力倦怠下来、放弃了争斗，无可估量的灾祸便会接踵而至——地、月、日或者循切线飞旋而去，或者无可奈何地坠入其主宰的星球。酸制衡着碱，使之各安其序；负电极制衡着正电极。一种最易燃的炸物与助燃者之间的恰当平衡，给了我们水——那须臾不可离的温和液体。不宁唯是，连我们呼吸的这空气，看似如此平静、如此安宁，也唯有靠种种对立气体的不断冲突才保得无害。倘若那暴烈不歇、无物不蚀的氧气占了上风，我们顷刻间便会被烧成灰烬；倘若那滞重惰怠的氮气得胜，我们便会死于虚竭。

===Original===
These facts are only a suggestion of what must be patent to every student of history. Progressive peace in a nation is the result of conflict; and conflict, such as is healthy, stimulating, and progressive, is produced through the coexistence of radically opposing or racially different elements. Bellamy’s ox-like men pictured in *Looking Backward*, taking their daily modicum of provender from the grandmotherly government, with nothing to struggle for, no wrong to put down, no reform to push through, no rights to vindicate and uphold, are nice folks to read about; but they are not natural; they are not progressive. God’s world is not governed that way. The child can never gain strength save by resistance, and there can be no resistance if all movement is in one direction and all opposition made forever an impossibility.

===Chinese===
这些事实不过略作提示，其道理本是不言而喻的。一个民族进步的和平是冲突的结果；而那种健康的、催人奋发的、促人进步的冲突，则由激烈对立或种族互异的诸成分之共存而生。贝拉姆（Bellamy）在《回顾》（*Looking Backward*）里描绘的那些公牛般的男人，从祖母式的政府那里领取每日定量的草料，无所争取，无冤可平，无改革可推，无权利可昭雪维护——这种人物读来固然可喜，却不自然，也不进步。上帝的世界不是这样治理的。孩子唯有凭藉阻力才能长出气力；倘若一切运动都朝着一个方向，一切反对都永无可能，阻力便无从谈起。

===Original===
I confess I can see no deeper reason than this for the specializing of racial types in the world. Whatever our theory with reference to the origin of species and the unity of mankind, we cannot help admitting the fact that no sooner does a family of the human race take up its abode in some little nook between mountains, or on some plain walled in by their own hands, no sooner do they begin in earnest to live their own life, think their own thoughts, and trace out their own arts, than they begin also to crystallize some idea different from and generally opposed to that of other tribes or families.

===Chinese===
我承认，对于世上种族类型之所以各自成家，我看不出比这更深的缘由。无论我们关于物种起源与人类一体持何种理论，我们都不得不承认这样一个事实：人类的某个家族一旦在山间某个小小的角落里安顿下来，或在一片由自己双手筑垣围起的平原上落脚，一旦他们开始认真地过自己的生活、想自己的思想、开辟自己的技艺，他们也就开始凝结出某种有别于、而且通常对立于其他部落或家族的观念。

===Original===
Each race has its badge, its exponent, its message, branded in its forehead by the great Master’s hand which is its own peculiar keynote, and its contribution to the harmony of nations.

===Chinese===
每个种族都有自己的徽记、自己的代表、自己的音讯，由那位伟大主宰之手烙在它的额上——那徽记便是它独有的基调，是它对诸民族和声的贡献。

===Original===
Left entirely alone﻿—out of contact, that is with other races and their opposing ideas and conflicting tendencies, this cult is abnormally developed and there is unity without variety, a predominance of one tone at the expense of moderation and harmony, and finally a sameness, a monotonous dullness which means stagnation﻿—death.

===Chinese===
倘若全然孤悬——也就是与其他种族及其对立的观念、相冲的倾向不相接触——这种独尊的风气便会畸形膨胀，于是有齐一而无多样，一个音调压倒一切，牺牲了中节与谐和，最终归于一种雷同，一种意味着停滞的单调沉闷——死亡。

===Original===
It is this of which M. Guizot complains in Asiatic types of civilization; and in each case he mentions I note that there was but one race, one free force predominating.

===Chinese===
基佐（Guizot）先生所诟病的亚洲各型文明的病根，正在于此；而且他所举的每一种情形，我注意到都只有一个种族、一种自由的力量在独占优势。

===Original===
In Lect. II. *Hist. of Civ.* he says:

===Chinese===
在《文明史》（*Hist. of Civ.*）第二讲中，他说：

===Original===
“In Egypt the theocratic principle took possession of society and showed itself in its manners, its monuments and in all that has come down to us of Egyptian civilization. In India the same phenomenon occurs﻿—a repetition of the almost exclusively prevailing influence of theocracy. In other regions the domination of a conquering caste; where such is the case the principle of force takes entire possession of society. In another place we discover society under the entire influence of the democratic principle. Such was the case in the commercial republics which covered the coasts of Asia Minor and Syria, in Ionia and Phoenicia. In a word whenever we contemplate the civilization of the ancients, we find them all impressed with *one ever prevailing character of unity*, visible in their institutions, their ideas and manners; *one sole influence seems to govern and determine all things*.﻿ ﻿… In one nation, as in Greece, the unity of the social principle led to a development of wonderful rapidity; no other people ever ran so brilliant a career in so short a time. But Greece had hardly become glorious before she appeared worn out. Her decline was as sudden as her rise had been rapid. It seems as if the principle which called Greek civilization into life was exhausted. No other came to invigorate it or supply its place. In India and Egypt where again only one principle of civilization prevailed (*one race predominant you see*) society became stationary. Simplicity produced monotony. Society continued to exist, but there was no progression. It remained torpid and inactive.”

===Chinese===
「在埃及，神权原则占据了社会，并显露于其风俗、其纪念物，以及传到我们手中的一切埃及文明之中。在印度，同一现象重现——神权几近独一无二的支配性影响再度上演。在其他地区，是一个征服者种姓的统治；凡属这种情形，强力原则便整个占据了社会。在另一处，我们发现社会处于民主原则的全盘影响之下；遍布小亚细亚与叙利亚海岸的商业共和国，爱奥尼亚与腓尼基，便是如此。一言以蔽之，每当我们凝望古人的文明，便发现它们全都打上了*一种恒常独尊的单一性*的印记，在其制度、观念与风俗中历历可见；*仿佛只有一种影响在支配一切、决定一切*……在某个民族，如希腊，社会原则的单一性带来了惊人的迅速发展；没有哪个民族曾在如此短促的时间里跑出如此辉煌的历程。然而希腊几乎还未及辉煌，便已显得衰竭。她的衰落如其兴起一样突然。仿佛那唤起希腊文明使之出生的原则已经耗竭，再无另一个原则来为之提神，或填补它的位置。在印度与埃及——那里同样只有一种文明原则通行（你们看，也就是一个种族独尊）——社会归于静止。单纯产生了单调。社会继续存在，却再无进步。它一直麻木不仁，无所作为。」

===Original===
Now I beg you to note that in none of these systems was a **Race Problem** possible. The dominant race had settled that matter forever. Asiatic society was fixed in cast-iron molds. Virtually there was but one race inspiring and molding the thought, the art, the literature, the government. It was against this shrivelling caste prejudice and intolerance that the zealous Buddha set his face like a flint. And I do not think it was all blasphemy in Renan when he said Jesus Christ was first of democrats, i.e., a believer in the royalty of the individual, a preacher of the brotherhood of man through the fatherhood of God, a teacher who proved that the lines on which worlds are said to revolve are *imaginary*, that for all the distinctions of blue blood and black blood and red blood﻿—*a man’s a man for a’ that*. Buddha and the Christ, each in his own way, wrought to rend asunder the clamps and bands of caste, and to thaw out the ice of race tyranny and exclusiveness. The Brahmin, who was Aryan, spurned a suggestion even, from the Shudra, who belonged to the hated and proscribed Turanian race. With a Pariah he could not eat or drink. They were to him outcasts and unclean. Association with them meant contamination; the hint of their social equality was blasphemous. Respectful consideration for their rights and feelings was almost a physical no less than a moral impossibility.

===Chinese===
现在我要请你们注意：在所有这些体制之中，**种族问题**（Race Problem）都是不可能出现的。占统治地位的种族早已把那件事一劳永逸地料理妥当。亚洲社会浇铸在生铁的模子里。实际上，启发并浇铸着思想、艺术、文学、政府的，只有一个种族。热忱的佛陀硬着脸面好像坚石，所对抗的正是这种令人枯萎的种姓偏执与不宽容。勒南（Renan）曾说，耶稣基督是民主人士中的第一人——也即个人王者尊严的信奉者，借上帝之父性传讲人类手足之谊的宣道者，证明了诸世界据称循以运转的那些界线纯属*虚拟*、因而无论蓝血、黑血、红血之别——*人总是人（a man's a man for a' that）*的教师；我不认为勒南这话全是渎神。佛陀与基督，各以自己的方式，奋力撕开种姓的铁箍与绑带，融化种族专制与排他的坚冰。身为雅利安人的婆罗门，连首陀罗——那属于遭人憎恶、遭人禁绝的图兰种族之人——的一句建言也要嗤之以鼻。与帕利亚（Pariah，贱民）同席饮食，于他是不可能的。在他们看来，这些人是弃民，是不洁。与他们交往意味着污秽；他们社会平等的暗示便是渎神。对他们权利与感受的尊重体察，几乎是道德上的不可能，也同样是肉体上的不可能。

===Original===
No more could the Helots among the Greeks have been said to contribute anything to the movement of their times. The dominant race had them effectually under its heel. It was the tyranny and exclusiveness of these nations, therefore, which brought about their immobility and resulted finally in the barrenness of their one idea. From this came the poverty and decay underlying their civilization, from this the transitory, ephemeral character of its brilliancy.

===Chinese===
同样，也无从说希腊人中间的黑劳士（Helots）对他们时代的运动有过什么贡献。占统治地位的种族把他们牢牢踩在脚跟之下。因此，正是这些民族的专制与排他造成了它们的僵滞，并最终导致其单一观念的贫瘠不毛。他们的文明底层那贫困与衰朽由此而来，其光辉那种转瞬即逝的性格也由此而来。

===Original===
To quote Guizot again: “Society belonged to *one exclusive* power which could bear with no other. Every principle of a different tendency was proscribed. The governing principle would nowhere suffer by its side the manifestation and influence of a rival principle. This character of unity in their civilization is equally impressed upon their literature and intellectual productions. Those monuments of Hindu literature lately introduced into Europe seem all struck from the same die. They all seem the result of one same fact, the expression of one idea. Religious and moral treatises, historical traditions, dramatic poetry, epics, all bear the same physiognomy. The same character of unity and monotony shines out in these works of mind and fancy, as we discover in their life and institutions.” Not even Greece with all its classic treasures is made an exception from these limitations produced by exclusiveness.

===Chinese===
再引基佐的话：「社会属于*某一种排他的*权力，它容不下任何别的权力。凡属倾向不同的原则一概遭到禁绝。统治的原则绝不容许一个敌对原则在它身旁现身并施展影响。他们文明中这种单一性的印记，同样打在他们的文学与智识产品上。新近引入欧洲的那些印度文学巨著，仿佛全都出自同一副铸模。它们仿佛都是同一事实的结果、同一观念的表达。宗教与道德论说、历史传说、剧诗、史诗，全都带着同一副面目。在这些心智与想象的作品中闪耀的，正是我们在他们的生活与制度中发现的同一种单一与单调的品格。」就连希腊，纵有满座经典珍宝，也未能免于这种由排他而生的局限。

===Original===
But the course of empire moves one degree westward. Europe becomes the theater of the leading exponents of civilization, and here we have a *Race Problem*﻿—if, indeed, the confused jumble of races, the clash and conflict, the din and devastation of those stormy years can be referred to by so quiet and so dignified a term as “problem.” Complex and appalling it surely was. Goths and Huns, Vandals and Danes, Angles, Saxons, Jutes﻿—could any prophet foresee that a vestige of law and order, of civilization and refinement would remain after this clumsy horde of wild barbarians had swept over Europe?

===Chinese===
然而帝国的进程向西又移一度。欧洲成了文明主要代言者上演的剧场，于是在这里我们便有了一个*种族问题*——倘若那些风雷激荡的岁月里种族的混杂纠结、撞击与冲突、喧嚣与蹂躏，当真配得上「问题」这样一个安静而体面的字眼的话。它的确既复杂又骇人。哥特人与匈奴人、汪达尔人与丹麦人、盎格鲁人、撒克逊人、朱特人——这样一支笨拙的野蛮游群席卷欧洲之后，还能有哪位先知预见到，法律与秩序、文明与文雅竟会留得一脉残迹？

===Original===
“Where is somebody’ll give me some white for all this yellow?” cries one with his hands full of the gold from one of those magnificent monuments of antiquity which he and his tribe had just pillaged and demolished. Says the historian: “Their history is like a history of kites and crows.” Tacitus writes: “To shout, to drink, to caper about, to feel their veins heated and swollen with wine, to hear and see around them the riot of the orgy, this was the first need of the barbarians. The heavy human brute gluts himself with sensations and with noise.”

===Chinese===
「哪儿有人肯拿白的来，换我这一把黄的？」一个人嚷道，双手捧满了金子——那是刚被他和他部落的人洗劫、捣毁的某座古代宏伟纪念物里掏出来的金子。史家说：「他们的历史就像一部鸢与乌鸦的历史。」塔西佗（Tacitus）写道：「叫喊、狂饮、蹦跳，觉出自己的血管被酒烧得滚热胀起，耳闻目睹身边纵酒的喧嚣——这便是蛮人的第一需要。这沉重的人形兽只求在感官与喧嚣里饱餐一顿。」

===Original===
Taine describes them as follows:

===Chinese===
丹纳（Taine）把他们描写如下：

===Original===
“Huge white bodies, cool-blooded, with fierce blue eyes, reddish flaxen hair; ravenous stomachs, filled with meat and cheese, heated by strong drinks. Brutal drunken pirates and robbers, they dashed to sea in their two-sailed barks, landed anywhere, killed everything; and, having sacrificed in honor of their gods the tithe of all their prisoners, leaving behind the red light of their burning, went farther on to begin again.”

===Chinese===
「高大白皙的身体，冷凉的血液，凶悍的蓝眼睛，红黄的亚麻色头发；饕餮的肠胃，塞满了肉与干酪，又被烈酒烧得滚热。横暴的、醉醺醺的海盗与劫匪，驾着双帆的小船冲向大海，随处登岸，杀戮殆尽；把俘虏的什一拿来祭了他们的神之后，留下身后焚烧的红色火光，再往前行，从头来过。」

===Original===
A certain litany of the time reads: “From the fury of the Jutes, Good Lord deliver us.” “Elgiva, the wife of one of their kings,” says a chronicler of the time, “they hamstrung and subjected to the death she deserved;” and their heroes are frequently represented as tearing out the heart of their human victim and eating it while it still quivered with life.

===Chinese===
当时的一篇连祷文写道：「从朱特人的狂怒中，好主啊，求你拯救我们。」「埃尔吉娃（Elgiva），他们一位国王的妻子，」当时的编年史家说，「被他们砍断腿筋，受了她罪有应得的死刑」；而他们的英雄，还屡屡被描绘成从人牲胸膛里掏出心来，趁它犹自颤动着生命，便吞食下去。

===Original===
A historian of the time, quoted by Taine, says it was the custom to buy men and women in all parts of England and to carry them to Ireland for sale. The buyers usually made the women pregnant and took them to market in that condition to ensure a better price. “You might have seen,” continues the historian, “long files of young people of both sexes and of great beauty, bound with ropes and daily exposed for sale. They sold as slaves in this manner, their nearest relatives and even their own children.”

===Chinese===
一位为丹纳所引的当时史家说，在英格兰各处买进男女、贩往爱尔兰出售，是那时的风气。买主通常先使那些女人怀孕，再带她们上市，以确保卖得好价钱。「你本可以见到，」那位史家接着说，「一队队美貌出众的青年男女，被绳索捆着，天天摆出来售卖。他们就这样把自己的近亲、甚至自己的亲生儿女卖作奴隶。」

===Original===
What could civilization hope to do with such a swarm of sensuous, bloodthirsty vipers? Assimilation was horrible to contemplate. They will drag us to their level, quoth the culture of the times. Deportation was out of the question; and there was no need to talk of their emigrating. The fact is, the barbarians were in no hurry about moving. They didn’t even care to colonize. They had come to stay. And Europe had to grapple with her race problem till time and God should solve it.

===Chinese===
面对这样一大群耽于声色、嗜血成性的蝮蛇，文明还能指望什么？同化之事，思之可怖。当时的文教说道：他们要把我们拖到他们的水准上去。驱逐则无从谈起；更不必侈谈让他们自行迁走。事实是，蛮人根本无意挪窝。他们连殖民都懒得费心。他们是来久住的。于是欧洲只得同它的种族问题扭打周旋，直到时间与上帝把它解决为止。

===Original===
And how was it solved, and what kind of civilization resulted?

===Chinese===
那么，它后来是怎样解决的？结出的又是一种怎样的文明？

===Original===
Once more let us go to Guizot. “Take ever so rapid a glance,” says he, “at modern Europe and it strikes you at once as diversified, confused, and stormy. All the principles of social organization are found existing together within it; powers temporal, and powers spiritual, the theocratic, monarchic, aristocratic, and democratic elements, all classes of society *in a state of continual struggle* without any one having sufficient force to master the others and take sole possession of society.” Then as to the result of this conflict of forces: “Incomparably more rich and diversified than the ancient, European civilization has within it the promise of *perpetual progress*. It has now endured more than fifteen centuries and in all that time has been in a state of progression, not so rapidly as the Greek nor yet so ephemeral.
