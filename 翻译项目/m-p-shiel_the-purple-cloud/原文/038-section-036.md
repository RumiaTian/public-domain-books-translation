When I turned the next foreland, I all at once began to see a number of craft, which increased as I advanced, most of them small boats, with some schooners, sloops, and larger craft, the majority aground: and suddenly now I was conscious that, mingling with that delicious odour of spring-blossoms﻿—profoundly modifying, yet not destroying it﻿—was another odour, wafted to me on the wings of the very faint land-breeze: and “Man,” I said, “is decomposing”: for I knew it well: it was the odour of human corruption.
