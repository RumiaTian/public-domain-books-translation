===Original===

I could not sleep that night: for all the operations of my mind and body seemed in abeyance. Mechanically I turned the ship westward again; and when the sun came up, there, hardly two miles from me, were the cliffs of Dover; and on the crenulated summit of the Castle I spied the Union Jack hang motionless.
I heard eight, nine o’clock strike in the cabin, and I was still at sea. But some mad, audacious whisper was at my brain: and at 10:30, the 2nd September, immediately opposite the Cross Wall Custom House, the *Boreal*’s anchor-chain, after a voyage of three years, two months, and fourteen days, ran thundering, thundering, through the starboard hawsehole.
Ah heaven! but I must have been stark mad to let the anchor go! for the effect upon me of that shocking obstreperous hubbub, breaking in upon all that cemetery repose that blessed morning, and lasting it seemed a year, was most appalling; and at the sudden racket I stood excruciated, with shivering knees and flinching heart, God knows: for not less terrifically uproarious than the clatter of the last Trump it raged and raged, and I thought that all the billion dead could not fail to start, and rise, at alarm so excessive, and question me with their eyes.﻿ ﻿…

===Chinese===

那一夜我睡不着：因为我的心智和身体的一切运转仿佛都停摆了。我机械地把船又转向西行；太阳升起时，离我不到两英里，就是多佛的白垩峭壁；城堡堞垛的顶上，我望见联合杰克旗纹丝不动地垂着。
我听见舱里的钟敲了八点、九点，我还在海上。可有个疯狂大胆的低语在我脑边牵缠：于是9月2日上午10点半，正对着十字墙海关，*博雷尔*号的锚链，历经三年两个月零十四天的航程，轰隆隆、轰隆隆地泻过了右舷锚链孔。
天啊！我放锚的那一刻准是彻底疯了！因为那震耳欲聋的喧闹，撞进那个蒙福的清晨墓园般的寂静里，一响仿佛响了一年，给我的震撼实在骇人；那突如其来的喧嚣里，我站在那里如受酷刑，膝盖发抖，心口畏缩，天知道：因为它翻滚又翻滚，其鼎沸不亚于最后号角的喧腾；我想，十亿死者听到这般惊天动地的警报，必定会一齐惊起、起身，用他们的眼睛质问我。……
