===Original===

Save for a time, near Cairo, I did not once stop on that homeward voyage, but turned into the little harbour at Imbros at a tranquil sunset on the 7th of March (as I reckon), and I moored the *Speranza* to the ring in the little quay, and I raised the battered motor from the hold with the middle air-engine (battered by the typhoon in the mid-Pacific, which had broken it from the rope-fastenings and tumbled it head-over-heels to port), and I went through the windowless village-street, and up through the plantains and cypresses which I knew, and the Nile mimosas, and mulberries, and Trebizond palms, and pines, and acacias, and fig-trees, till the thicket stopped me, and I had to alight: for in those two years the path had finally disappeared; and on, on foot, I made my way, till I came to the board-bridge, and leant there, and looked at the rill; and thence climbed the steep path in the sward toward that rolling tableland where I had built with many a groan; and halfway up, I saw the tip of the crane-arm, then the blazing top of the south pillar, then the shed-roof, then the platform, a blinking blotch of glory to the watery eyes under the setting sun. But the tent, and nearly all that it contained, was gone.

===Chinese===

除了在开罗附近停过一回，那次归航我不曾再停，三月七日（照我的推算）一个宁静的日落时分，我转进了伊姆罗兹的小港，把「斯佩兰扎号」系在小码头的系环上，用中央气压机把那辆撞坏的摩托从底舱吊起（是在中太平洋被台风撞坏的：风把它从绳固里挣脱，翻着筋斗掼向左舷），然后我穿过那条没有窗子的村街，向上穿过我熟识的芭蕉和柏树、尼罗河合欢、桑树、特拉比松棕榈、松树、金合欢和无花果树，直到树丛拦住我，不得不下车：因为那两年里，小路终于湮灭了；我继续徒步前行，直到木桥前，倚在那里，望着那道细流；随后沿着草地里的陡径往上爬，爬向那片起伏的高台——我曾在那里呻吟着建造；爬到半途，我望见了吊臂的尖，接着是南柱灼灼的顶，接着是棚屋的顶，接着是平台：在落日下，对着我这双泪汪汪的眼，一团眨着眼的荣光。可那帐篷，和里面几乎所有的东西，都没有了。
