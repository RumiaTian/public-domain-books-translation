The first thing which I did when the sun was up was to return to that place: and I returned with hard and masterful brow.
Approaching Peters’ house I saw now, what the darkness had hidden from me, that on his balcony was someone﻿—quite alone there. The balcony is a slight openwork wrought-iron structure, connected to a small roof by three slender voluted pillars, two at the ends, one in the middle: and at the middle one I saw someone, a woman﻿—kneeling﻿—her arms clasped tight about the pillar, and her face rather upward-looking. Never did I see aught more horrid: there were the gracious curves of the woman’s bust and hips still well preserved in a clinging dress of red cloth, very faded now; and her reddish hair floated loose in a large flimsy cloud about her; but her face, in that exposed position, had been quite eaten away by the winds to a noseless skeleton, which grinned from ear to ear, with slightly-dropped under-jaw﻿—most horrid in contrast with the body, and frame of hair. I meditated upon her a long time that morning from the opposite pavement. An oval locket at her throat contained, I knew, my likeness: for eight years previously I had given it her. It was Clodagh, the poisoner.
I thought that I would go into that house, and walk through it from top to bottom, and sit in it, and spit in it, and stamp in it, in spite of anyone: for the sun was now high. I accordingly went in again, and up the stairs to the spot where I had been frightened, and had heard the words. And here a great rage took me, for I at once saw that I had been made the dupe of the malign wills that beset me, and the laughingstock of Those for whom I care not a fig. From a little mahogany table there I had knocked sideways to the ground, in my stumble, a small phonograph with a great 25-inch japanned-tin horn, which, the moment that I now noticed it, I took and flung with a great racket down the stairs: for that this it was which had addressed me I did not doubt; it being indeed evident that its clockwork mechanism had been stopped by the volcanic scoriae in the midst of the delivery of a record, but had been started into a few fresh oscillations by the shock of the fall, making it utter those thirteen words, and stop.

太阳升起后我做的头一件事，便是回到那地方：这一回，我眉头硬朗而专横。
走近彼得斯宅子时，我如今看清了黑夜睛过我的东西：他家阳台上有人——独自一个。那阳台是一座轻巧的镂花熟铁建筑，靠三根细而带涡卷的柱子连着一方小屋顶，两根在两头，一根居中：而就在中间那根柱子上，我看见一个人，一个女人——跪着——双臂紧紧搂住柱子，脸微微仰着。我从不曾见过比这更骇人的东西：她胸与臀的柔美曲线，在那条紧身的红布裙里还保存得很好，只是如今褪尽了色；她带红的头发松松地飘成一大片薄云，笼在她四周；可她那张脸，在那样暴露的位置上，已被风吃得只剩一具无鼻的骷髅，咧着嘴，从这只耳咧到那只耳，下颌微微垂着——同那身子、那头发的框子两相对照，可怖至极。那天早晨，我在对面的人行道上，对着她沉思了许久。她喉间那枚椭圆的金匣里装着，我知道，我的小像：因为八年前是我送给她的。那是克洛达赫，那个下毒的女人。
我想我要进那宅子去，从顶到底走一遍，坐在里面，往里面啐唾沫，在里面跺脚，管他什么人不人：因为太阳已经高了。于是我又进去了，上楼梯，走到我受惊、听见那句话的地方。而这一下，一股大怒把我攝住，因为我立刻看出：我一直被那班围困我的恶意意志玩弄于股掌，被那些我毫不在乎的「谁」当了笑柄。原来我绊倒时，侧着碰翻在地的，是那张红木小桌上的一台小留声机，带一只二十五英寸的大黑漆马口铁喇叭；此刻我一看见它，便抓起来，哐啷一声摔下了楼梯：因为向我说话的正是它，这我毫不怀疑；事情显然是：它的发条机括被火山熔渣卡住，停在一支唱片的半中央，却被我摔倒时的震动激起了几下新的摆荡，叫它吐出那十三个字，随即停住。

I was sufficiently indignant at the moment, but have since been glad, for I was thereby put upon the notion of collecting a number of cylinders with records, and have been touched with indescribable sensations, sometimes thrilled, at hearing the silence of this Eternity broken by those singing and speaking voices, so lifelike, yet most ghostly, of the old dead.
