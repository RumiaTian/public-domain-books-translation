In a hundred years, I suppose, I should never succeed in analysing *why* I swooned: but my consciousness still retains the impression of that horrid thrill. I saw nothing distinctly, for my whole being reeled and toppled drunken, like a spinning-top in desperate death-struggle at the moment when it flags, and wobbles dissolutely to fall; but the very instant that my eyes met what was before me, I knew, I knew, that here was the Sanctity of Sanctities, the old eternal inner secret of the Life of this Earth, which it was a most burning shame for a man to see. The lake, I fancy, must be a mile across, and in its middle is a pillar of ice, very low and broad; and I had the clear impression, or dream, or notion, that there was a name, or word, graven all round in the ice of the pillar in characters which I could never read; and under the name a long date; and the fluid of the lake seemed to me to be wheeling with a shivering ecstasy, splashing and fluttering, round the pillar, always from west to east, in the direction of the spinning of the earth; and it was borne in upon me﻿—I can’t at all say how﻿—that this fluid was the substance of a living creature; and I had the distinct fancy, as my senses failed, that it was a creature with many dull and anguished eyes, and that, as it wheeled forever round in fluttering lust, it kept its eyes always turned upon the name and the date graven in the pillar. But this must be my madness.﻿ ﻿…

我想，再过一百年，我也未必能分析出自己*为什么*晕厥：可我的意识里至今留着那阵骇人震颤的印象。我什么也没看清，因为我的整个存在都在眩晕、醉倒、摇摇欲坠，像一只濒死的陀螺，在转速已衰、放纵地摇晃着将要倒下的那一刻；可我的目光一触到眼前的东西，我就知道，我就知道，这里是至圣中的至圣，是这大地生命古老而永恒的内在秘密，人见了它是天大的罪过。那湖，我猜，直径总有一英里，湖心立着一根冰柱，很低很粗；我有一个清晰的印象——或者梦境，或者念头——冰柱四周刻着一个名字，或一个字，用的是我永远无法辨认的字符；名字下面是一长串日期；而那湖中的流体，仿佛带着战栗的狂喜绕柱流转，泼溅着、拍扑着，永远是自西向东，顺着地球自转的方向；我心里被灌进了一个确信——我完全说不上是怎么来的——这流体是一个活物的体质；而且，就在我的感官涣散之际，我分明觉得它是一个长着许多只晦暗而痛苦的眼睛的活物，当它永远怀着扑腾的贪欲绕柱流转时，那许多只眼睛始终盯着柱上刻着的名字和日期。可这必定是我的疯癫。……
