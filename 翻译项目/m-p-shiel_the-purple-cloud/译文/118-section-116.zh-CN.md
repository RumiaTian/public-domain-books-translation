I passed down the Straits of Malacca, and in that short distance between the Andaman Islands, and the S. W. corner of Borneo I was thrice so mauled, that at times it seemed quite out of the question that anything built by man could escape such unfettered cataclysms, and I resigned myself, but with bitter reproaches, to perish darkly. The effect of the third upon me, when it was over, was the unloosening afresh of all my evil passion: for I said: “Since they mean to slay me, death shall find me rebellious”; and for weeks I could not sight some specially happy village, or umbrageous spread of woodland, that I did not stop the ship, and land the materials for their destruction; so that nearly all those spicy lands about the north of Australia will bear the traces of my hand for many a year: for more and more my voyage became dawdling and zigzaged, as the merest whim directed it, or the movement of the pointer on the chart; and I thought of eating the lotus of surcease and nepenthe in some enchanted nook of this bowering summer, where from my hut-door I could see through the pearl-hues of opium the sea-lagoon slaver lazily upon the old coral atol, and the coconut-tree would droop like slumber, and the breadfruit tree would moan in sweet and weary dream, and I should watch the *Speranza* lie anchored in the pale atol-lake, year after year, and wonder what she was, and whence, and why she dozed so deep forever, and after an age of melancholy peace and burdened bliss, I should note that sun and moon had ceased revolving, and hung inert, opening anon a heavy lid to doze and drowse again, and God would sigh “Enough,” and nod, and Being would swoon to sleep: for that any old Chinaman should be alive in Peking was a thing so fantastically maniac, as to draw from me at times sudden fits of wild red laughter that left me faint.
During a space of four months, from the 18th June to the 23rd October, I visited the Fijis, where I saw skulls still surrounded with remnants of extraordinary haloes of stiff hair, women clad in girdles made of thongs fixed in a belt, and, in Samoa near, bodies crowned with coronets of nautilus-shell, and traces of turmeric-paint and tattooing, and in one townlet a great assemblage of carcasses, suggesting by their look some festival, or dance: so that I believe that these people were overthrown without the least foreknowledge of anything.

我驶下马六甲海峡；而就在从安达曼群岛到婆罗洲西南角的这一短短航程里，我接连三次被风暴摧折得那样厉害，以致有时看来，人造的任何东西要想逃过这样肆无忌惮的大灾，全然无望，我也就听天由命，只带着苦苦的怨无，准备黑暗里灭亡。第三次风暴过后，它在我身上的效应，是把我一切的恶欲重新松了绑：因为我说：「他们既然存心杀我，死亡就必看见我反叛」；一连几个星期，只要望见哪个格外安乐的村子，或哪片格外清荫的林地，我必停下船，运下毁它们的材料；以致澳大利亚以北那些香料之国的地面，许多年里都将带着我手的痕迹：因为我的航程越来越游荡，越来越曲折，全凭一时的兴致，或图上指针的一动；我想在这浓荫如盖的仲夏里，寻一处着魔的角落，食忘忧的陀罗与忘忧药：从我茅舍门口，透过鸦片的珠光宝色，望海湖懒懒地淌口水在古老的珊瑚环礁上，椰子树将垂得像睡眠，面包果树将在甜蜜而疲倦的梦里呻吟，我将守望「斯佩兰扎号」年复一年锚在苍白的环礁湖里，纳罕她是什么、从哪里来、为什么永远睡得这样沉，在一段忧郁的安宁与负重的至福之后，我将发现太阳和月亮都停了转，懸着不动，偶尔掀起沉重的眼皮打个盹又睡去，上帝将叹息一声「够了」，点一点头，万有将昏然睡去：因为「北京还有一个活着的中国老头」，这事实在痴狂得离谱，不时从我这里诱出一阵阵狂野的、血红的大笑，笑得我发晕。
从六月十八日到十月二十三日，四个月里，我到访了斐济，在那里看见髑髅四周还留着硬发编成的非凡光轮的残余，女人们系着皮带缀皮条的腰裳；在近旁的萨摩亚，看见尸体上戴着鹦鹉螺壳编的冠，还有姜黄彩绘和文身的痕迹；在一座小镇上看见一大片尸骸，那情状像是逢着什么节庆，或舞会：所以我相信这些人是在对一切毫无预知的情况下被撂倒的。

The women of the Maoris wore an abundance of green-jade ornaments, and I found a peculiar kind of shell-trumpet, one of which I have now, also a tattooing chisel, and a nicely-carved wooden bowl. The people of New Caledonia, on the other hand, went, I should think, naked, confining their attention to the hair, and in this resembling the Fijians, for they seemed to wear an artificial hair made of the fur of some creature like a bat, and also they wore wooden masks, and great rings﻿—for the ear, no doubt﻿—which must have fallen to the shoulders: for the earth was in them all, and made them wild, perverse and various like herself. I went from one to the other without any system whatever, searching for the ideal resting-place, and often thinking that I had found it: but only wearying of it at the thought that there was a yet deeper and dreamier in the world. But in this search I received a check, my God, which chilled me to the marrow, and set me flying from these places.

毛利人的女子戴着大量的绿玉饰物，我还寻见一种特别的螺号，其中一只现在就在我手上；还有一支文身的小凿，和一只雕工精美的木碗。新喀里多尼亚的人呢，我看是光着身子的，心思全用在头发上，这一点像斐济人：因为他们似乎戴一种人造的发，用什么蝙蝠之类的兽毛做成，还戴木制面具，还有大环——想必是耳环——一定垂到肩膀：因为大地就在他们全体里面，把他们造得同她自己一样野蛮、乖张而多态。我从一岛到另一岛，毫无章法，寻那理想的歇脚之地，屡屡以为寻到了：却只在想到世上必有更幽更深的一处时，又厌倦了。可就在这寻觅中，我捱了一记，我的上帝，那把我冷到骨髓里，叫我从这些地方仓皇飞逃。
