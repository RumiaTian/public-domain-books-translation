===Original===

I have been thinking, thinking of my life: there is a something which I cannot understand.
There was a man whom I met once in that dark backward and abysm of time, when I must have been very young﻿—I fancy at some college or school in England, and his name now is far enough beyond scope of my memory, lost in the vast limbo of past things. But he used to talk continually about certain “Black” and “White” Powers, and of their strife for this world. He was a short man with a Roman nose, and lived in fear of growing a paunch. His forehead atop, in profile, was more prominent than the nose-end, he parted his hair in the middle, and had the theory that the male form was more beautiful than the female. I forget what his name was﻿—the dim clear-obscure being. Very profound was the effect of his words upon me, though, I think, I used to make a point of slighting them. This man always declared that “the Black” would carry off the victory in the end: and so he has, so he has.
But assuming the existence of this “Black” and this “White” being﻿—and supposing it to be a fact that my reaching the Pole had any connection with the destruction of my race, according to the notions of that extraordinary Scotch parson﻿—then it must have been the power of “the Black” which carried me, in spite of all obstacles, to the Pole. So far I can understand.
But *after* I had reached the Pole, what further use had either White or Black for me? Which was it﻿—White or Black﻿—that preserved my life through my long return on the ice﻿—and *why*? It *could* not have been “the Black!” For I readily divine that from the moment when I touched the Pole, the only desire of the Black, which had previously preserved, must have been to destroy me, with the rest. It must have been “the White,” then, that led me back, retarding me long, so that I should not enter the poison-cloud, and then openly presenting me the *Boreal* to bring me home to Europe. But his motive? And the significance of these recommencing wrangles, after such a silence? This I do not understand!
Curse Them, curse Them, with their mad tangles! I care nothing for Them! Are there any White Idiots and Black Idiots﻿—*at all*? Or are these Voices that I hear nothing but the cries of my own strained nerves, and I all mad and morbid, morbid and mad, mad, my good God?
This inertia here is *not good* for me! This stalking about the palace!

===Chinese===

我一直在想，想我这一生：有一件事我无法理解。
从前，在那时光幽暗的深渊后景里，我遇见过一个人——那时我一定还很年轻——好像是在英格兰的某所学院或学校里；他如今叫什么名字，早已越出我记忆的范围，失落于既往万物的茫茫混沌里了。可他老是讲什么「黑」势力与「白」势力，讲它们对这个世界的争夺。他是个矮个子，一副罗马鼻，老怕自己长出啤酒肚。侧面看去，他前额顶比鼻尖还突出，头发中分，还有一套理论，说男性形体比女性更美。我忘了他叫什么——那个朦胧的家伙。可他的话在我身上留下了极深的印子，虽然我想我当时总故意怠慢它们。这个人一口咬定，「黑」最终会赢走胜利：果然如此，果然如此。
可倘若这对「黑」与「白」果真存在——倘若照那位非凡的苏格兰牧师的说法，我到达极点同我种族的毁灭真有什么牵连——那么，把我不顾一切阻障送到极点去的，必是「黑」的势力。到这一步我还能理解。
可是到达极点之后，「白」或「黑」还要我何用？在冰原上漫长的归途里保住我性命的，究竟是哪一个——白，还是黑——又是为什么？那绝不可能又是「黑」！因为我很容易想到，从我触到极点那一刻起，先前保全我的「黑」，其唯一的愿望必是同毁灭其余人一样毁灭我。那么引我回来的必是「白」了：它迟迟拖住我，好叫我不进那毒云；随后又把北风号明白地摆在我眼前，把我带回欧洲。可它的用意何在？而经过这许多年的沉默，这些争吵又卷土重来，又是何意？这我就不明白了！
诅咒他们，诅咒他们，和他们那些疯狂的乱麻！我才不把他们放在心上！到底有没有白痴的白和白痴的黑——到底有没有？还是我听见的这些声音，不过是我自己绷断的神经在喊，而我整个人疯病缠身，病疯疯病，疯病，我的老天？
这儿的这种惰性对我没有好处！在这宫殿里晃来晃去！
