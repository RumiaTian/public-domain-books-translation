===Original===

I did not leave Imbros after that during four years, except for occasional brief trips to the coast﻿—to Kilid-Bahr, Gallipoli, Lapsaki, Gamos, Rodosto, Erdek, Erekli, or even once to Constantinople and Scutari﻿—if I happened to want anything, or if I was tired of work: but without once doing the least harm to anything, but containing my humours, and fearing my Maker. And full of peaceful charm were those little cruises through this Levantic world, which, truly, is rather like a light sketch in watercolours done by an angel than like the dun real earth; and full of self-satisfaction and pious contentment would I return to Imbros, approved of my conscience, for that I had surmounted temptation, and lived tame and stainless.
I had set up the southern of the two closed-lotus pillars, and the platform-top was already looking as lovely as heaven, with its alternate two-foot squares of pellucid gold and pellucid jet, when I noticed one morning that the *Speranza*’s bottom was really now too foul, and the whim took me then and there to leave all, and clean her as far as I could. I at once went on board, descended to the hold, took off my *sudeyree*, and began to shift the ballast over to starboard, so as to tilt up her port bottom to the scraper. This was wearying labour, and about noon I was sitting on a bag, resting in the almost darkness, when something seemed to whisper to me these words: You dreamed last night that there is an old Chinaman alive in Peking. Horridly I started: I *had* dreamed something of the sort, but, from the moment of waking, till then, had forgotten it: and I leapt livid to my feet.
I cleaned no *Speranza* that day, nor for four days did I anything, but sat on the cabin-house and mused, my supporting palm among the hairy draperies of my chin: for the thought of such a thing, if it could by any possibility be true, was detestable as death to me, changing the colour of the sun, and the whole aspect of the world: and anon, at the outrage of that thing, my brow would flush with wrath, and my eyes blaze: till, on the fourth afternoon, I said to myself: “That old Chinaman in Peking is likely to get burned to death, I think, or blown to the clouds!”
So, a second time, on the 4th March, the poor palace was left to build itself.

===Chinese===

那以后整整四年，我没有离开伊姆罗兹，只偶尔往海岸作短短的出行——去基利德巴赫尔、加利波利、拉普萨基、加莫斯、罗多斯托、埃尔德克、埃雷克利，甚至有一次去了君士坦丁堡和斯库塔里——若是碰巧要用什么，或是做烦了工：可我不曾再损坏一草一木，只是括着自己的性子，敬畏着我的造主。穿行这黎凡特世界的小小巡游，满是安宁的魅力：这地方，实在说，与其像土褐色的真实大地，不如像天使画的一幅轻盈的水彩速写；而我回到伊姆罗兹时，总是满怀自得与度诚的满足，良心认可我，因为我胜过了试探，活得驯顺而无垢。
我已把两根闭莲柱中南边的那根立了起来，平台顶已开始美如天庭：透明的金与透明的黑玉两英尺见方，彼此相间——就在这时，一天早晨，我发现「斯佩兰扎号」的船底实在积垢太厚，一时兴起，便当场撂下一切，去把她尽可能清理一番。我立刻上船，下到底舱，脱掉「苏代里」，开始把压舱物移向右舷，好把她左舷的船底翘起刮蹭。这是累人的活，约莫中午，我坐在一只袋子上，在近乎全黑中歇息，忽然有什么仿佛向我耳语了这样一句：你昨夜梦见北京还有一个活着的中国老头。我骇然一惊：我确实梦见过类似的东西，可从醒来到方才，一直忘了：我面无人色地跳了起来。
那天我没有再清理「斯佩兰扎号」，一连四天我什么也没干，只是坐在舱棚上沉思，手掌托在我下巴那毛茸茸的须帘里：因为这样一件事，倘若有一丝可能是真的，于我便可憎如死，会改变太阳的颜色、整个世界的面目；而不时地，为着那东西的污辱，我的额头会烧起怒火，双眼冒火：直到第四天下午，我对自己说：「北京那中国老头，我看多半会被烧死，要不就被炸上天！」
于是，第二次，三月四日，那可怜的宫殿又被撇下，自己造自己去了。
