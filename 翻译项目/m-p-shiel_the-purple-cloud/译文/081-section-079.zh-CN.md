===Original===

No private palace being near, I was going to that great hotel in Bloomsbury: but though I knew that numbers of candlesticks would be there, I was not sure that I should find sufficient: for I had acquired the habit within the past few months of sleeping with at least sixty lighted about me, and their form, pattern, style, age, and material was of no small importance I selected ten from the broken shop, eight gold and silver, and two of old ecclesiastical brass, and having made a bundle, went out, found a bicycle at the Metropolitan Station, pumped it, tied my bundle to the handlebar, and set off riding. But since I was too lazy to walk, I should certainly have procured some other means of travelling, for I had not gone ten jolted and creaking yards, when something went snap﻿—it was a front fork﻿—and I found myself half on the ground, and half across the bare knees of a Highland soldier. I flew with a shower of kicks upon the foolish thing: but that booted nothing; and this was my last attempt in that way in London, the streets being in an unsuitable condition.
All that dismal night it blew great guns: and during nearly three weeks, till London was no more, there was a storm, with hardly a lull, that seemed to behowl her destruction.

===Chinese===

附近没有私人的宫殿，我本打算去布卢姆斯伯里那家大饭店：虽然我知道那里会有许多烛台，却不敢保证够数：因为过去几个月里，我已养成习惯，睡觉时身边至少要点六十支蜡烛，而烛台的形制、纹样、风格、年代和材质，都非小事。我从那家被砸开的铺子里挑了十件，八件金银的，两件教会的老黄铜的，打成一个包，走了出去，在大都会车站找到一辆自行车，给它打足气，把包裹绑上龙头，骑车出发。可我既然懒到不愿走路，本来实在该另找一种代步的法子才对：因为车身颠着、响着，还没走出十码，咔嚓一声——是前叉断了——我发觉自己半个身子已坐在地上，半个身子正跨在一个苏格兰高地士兵光溜溜的膝盖上。我对那蠢东西飞起一顿乱踹：可那毫无用处；这也是我在伦敦最后一次作这等尝试，街道的状况实在不合适。
那凄惨的一夜，风刮得像开炮；此后近三个星期，直到伦敦不复存在，风暴几乎没有间歇，仿佛在为它的毁灭嚎丧。
