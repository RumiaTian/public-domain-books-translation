===Original===

For four days I would do nothing, simply lying and watching, shirking a load so huge: but on the fifth morning I languidly began something: and I had not worked an hour, when a fever took me﻿—to finish it, to finish it﻿—and it lasted upon me, with only three brief intervals, nearly seven years; nor would the end have been so long in coming, but for the unexpected difficulty of getting the four flat roofs watertight, for I had to take down half the east one. Finally, I made them of gold slabs one-and-a-quarter inch thick, smooth on both sides, on each beam double gutters being fixed along each side of the top flange to catch any leakage at the joints, which are filled with slaters’-cement. The slabs are clamped to the top flanges by steel clips, having bolts set with plaster-of-Paris in holes drilled in the slabs. These clips are 1½ in. by ³⁄₁₇ in., and are 17 in. apart. The roofs are slightly pitched to the front edges, where they drain into gold-plated copper-gutters on plated wrought-iron brackets, with one side flashed up over the blocks, which raise the slabs from the beam-tops, to clear the joint gutters.﻿ ﻿… But now I babble again of that base servitude, which I would forget, but cannot: for every measurement, bolt, ring, is in my brain, like a burden: but it is past, it is past﻿—and it was vanity.

===Chinese===

一连四天我什么也不做，只是躺着观望，躲避那副重担：可第五天早晨，我懒懒地起了个头：干了不到一个钟头，一阵热病便攝住了我——完成它，完成它——这热病在我身上持续了将近七年，中间只断过三次；若不是四片平顶封水出了意外的难处，结局原不至拖到这样久，因为我不得不拆掉东顶的一半。末了，我把它们做成了厚一又四分之一英寸的金板，两面刨光，每根梁的顶翼两侧各固定着双重的檐槽，接住接缝处的任何渗漏，缝里填满石板瓦工的油灰。金板用钢夹箍定在顶翼上，夹子带螺栓，以熟石膏浇固在板上钻出的孔里。这些夹子一英寸半长、十七分之三英寸宽，彼此相距十七英寸。四个顶都微微朝前缘倾斜，水从那里排入镀金的铜檐槽，槽架在镀饰的熟铁托座上，一侧的泛水翻上垫块——垫块把金板从梁顶垫起，好让开接缝的槽。……可我又絮叨起那卑贱的苦役了，那我想忘却忘不掉的：因为每一处尺寸、每一颗螺栓、每一只环，都在我脑子里，像一副重担：可它过去了，过去了——而且是虚空。
