===Original===

We had all along had good weather: till, suddenly, on the morning of the 13th April, we were overtaken by a tempest from the S. W., of such mighty and solemn volume that the heart quailed beneath it. It lasted in its full power only an hour, but during that time snatched two of our sledges long distances, and compelled us to lie face-downward. We had travelled all the sunlit night, and were gasping with fatigue; so as soon as the wind allowed us to huddle together our scattered things, we crawled into the sleeping-bags, and instantly slept.
We knew that the ice was in awful upheaval around us; we heard, as our eyelids sweetly closed, the slow booming of distant guns, and brittle cracklings of artillery. This may have been a result of the tempest stirring up the ocean beneath the ice. Whatever it was, we did not care: we slept deep.
We were within ten miles of the Pole.

===Chinese===

我们一路天气都好：不料4月13日清晨，西南骤起一场暴风，声势浩大而森严，风声底下，人心都发颤。它全力的肆虐只持续了一个钟头，可在那一个钟头里，它把我们的两架雪橇掳出老远，逼得我们匍匐在地不敢抬头。我们赶了一整夜阳光普照的路，正累得直喘；于是风势刚容我们把四散的东西归拢到一处，我们就爬进睡袋，倒头便睡熟了。
我们知道四周的冰正在可怖地隆起；眼皮甜蜜地合上时，我们还听见远处炮声般的低沉轰鸣，和火炮般的脆响。这也许是暴风搅动了冰下海洋的缘故。管它是什么呢，我们全不在意：我们睡得沉极了。
我们离北极只剩十英里。
