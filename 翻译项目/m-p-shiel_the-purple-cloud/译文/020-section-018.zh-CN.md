===Original===

Well, onward through the desert ice I continued my lonely way, with a baleful shrinking terror in my heart; for very stupendous, alas! is the burden of that Arctic solitude upon one poor human soul.
Sometimes on a halt I have lain and listened long to the hollow silence, recoiling, crushed by it, hoping that at least one of the dogs might whine. I have even crept shivering from the thawed sleeping-bag to flog a dog, so that I might hear a sound.
I had started from the Pole with a well-filled sledge, and the sixteen dogs left alive from the ice-packing which buried my comrades. This was on the evening of the 13th April. I had saved from the wreck of our things most of the whey-powder, pemmican, etc., as well as the theodolite, compass, chronometer, train-oil lamp for cooking, and other implements: I was therefore in no doubt as to my course, and I had provisions for ninety days. But ten days from the start my supply of dog-food failed, and I had to begin to slaughter my only companions, one by one.
Well, in the third week the ice became horribly rough, and with moil and toil enough to wear a bear to death, I did only five miles a day. After the day’s work I would crawl with a dying sigh into the sleeping-bag, clad still in the load of skins which stuck to me a mere filth of grease, to sleep the sleep of a swine, indifferent if I never woke.
Always﻿—day after day﻿—on the southeastern horizon, brooded sullenly that curious stretched-out region of purple vapour, like the smoke of the conflagration of the world. And I noticed that its length constantly reached out and out, and silently grew.

===Chinese===

唉，我便这样穿过荒凉的冰原继续独行，心里怀着一种不祥的、瑟缩的恐怖；因为那极地的孤寂，压在一个可怜的人类灵魂上，那分量，唉！实在大得骇人。
有时中途歇下，我躺着，久久听着那空荡荡的寂静，被它压得退缩、碾碎，巴望至少有一条狗能呜咽一声。我甚至曾打着寒嘇，从暖透了的睡袋里爬出去鞭打一条狗，只为能听见一个声响。
我离开极点时，雪橇装得满满的，还带着从那场埋葬我同件的冰的挤压中活下来的十六条狗。那是四月十三日的傍晚。我从物资的残骸里抢出了大半的乳清粉、干肉饼等等，还有经纬仪、罗盘、天文钟、烧海豹油的炊灯，以及别的器物：因此我对自己的航向毫无疑惑，口粮也还有九十天的。可出发后十天，狗粮告罄，我不得不开始宰杀我仅有的同伴，一条一条来。
唉，到第三个星期，冰面变得可怕地崎岖，那份劳苦足够把一头熊磨死，我一天只走得五英里。一天的活计之后，我总是拖着一口垂死的叹息爬进睡袋，身上还裹着那一身皮衣，黏着我一身油污秽垢，睡猪一般的觉，从此不再醒来也无所谓。
日复一日——东南方的天际上，那一带奇异的、拖长了的紫色蒸汽始终阴沉地盘踞着，像世界大火的烟。我注意到它的长度不断地伸展、伸展，无声地生长着。
