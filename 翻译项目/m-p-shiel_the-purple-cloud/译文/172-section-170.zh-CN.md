The morning after our coming, we had breakfast﻿—our last meal together﻿—on the first floor in a pentagonal room approached from a lower level by three little steps. In it is a ponderous oak table pierced with a multitude of worm eaten tunnels, also three mighty high backed chairs, an old oak desk covered still with papers, arras on the walls, and three dark religious oil paintings, and a grandfathers clock: it is at about the middle of the château, and contains two small, but deep, three faced oriels, in each face four compartments with white stone shafts between, these looking south upon shrubs and the rocky edge of the island, then upon the deep blue lake, then upon another tiny island containing four trees in a jungle of flowers, then upon the shore of the lake interrupted by the mouths of a river which turned out to be the Rhone, then upon a white town on the slopes which turned out to be Villeneuve, then upon the great mountains back of Bouveret and St. Gingolph, all having the surprised air of a resurrection just completed, everything new washed in dyes of azure, ultramarine, indigo, snow, emerald, that fresh morning: so that one had to call it the best and holiest place in the world. These five old room walls, and oak floor, and two oriels, became specially mine, though it was really common ground to us both, and there I would do many little things. The papers on the desk told that it had been the bureau of one R. E. Gaud, *Grand Bailli*, whose residence the place no doubt had been.
She asked me while eating that morning to stay here, and I said that I would see, though with misgiving: so together we went all about the house, and finding it unexpectedly spacious, I consented to stop.

到达后的第二天早晨，我们在二楼吃了早餐——我们同桌的最后一顿——那是一间五角形的屋子，从下面一层上三级小台阶进去。屋里有一张笨重的橡木桌，布满虫蛀的隧道，还有三把高背大椅，一张还摊着纸的旧橡木书桌，墙上是花毯挂毯和三幅幽暗的宗教油画，一座落地大钟：屋子大约在城堡正中，开有两扇小而深的三面凸窗，每面四格，格间是白石窗棂，向南望见灌木和岛的石缘，再望是深蓝的湖，再望是另一座小小的岛，花的丛林里长着四棵树，再望是湖岸，被一条河的几处河口打断——那河原来是罗讷河，再望是坡上一座白镇——原来是维伦纽夫，再望是布弗雷和圣然戈尔夫背后的大山，全都带着一场刚刚完成的复活所特有的惊讶神情，样样都新洗过，染着蔚蓝、群青、靛蓝、雪白、翠绿，那个清新的早晨：叫人不能不称它做世上最好、最圣洁的地方。这五面老屋的墙、橡木地板和两扇凸窗，成了我特别的所在，虽然其实是我们两人共有的地方，我在那儿要做许多小小的事。书桌上的纸表明，这里从前是一位 R·E·戈的办公房，官职大总管（Grand Bailli）——这宅子想必就是他的居所。
那天早晨吃着饭，她求我住在这儿，我说再看看，心里却存着疑虑：于是一起把整座宅子走了一遍，发现它出乎意料地宽敞，我便同意留下。

At both ends are suites, mostly small rooms, infinitely quaint and cosy, furnished with heavy Henri Quatre furniture and bed draperies; and there are separate, and as it were secret, spiral stairs for exit to each: so we decided that she should have the suite overlooking the length of the lake, the mouths of the Rhone, Bouveret and Villeneuve; and I should have that overlooking the spit of land behind and the little drawbridge, shore cliffs, and elmwood which comes down to the shore, giving at one point a glimpse of the diminutive hamlet of Chillon; and, that decided, I took her hand in mine, and I said:
“Well, then, here we stay, both under the same roof﻿—for the first time. Leda, I will not explain why to you, but it is dangerous, so much so that it *may* mean the death of one or other of us: deadly, deadly dangerous, my poor girl. You do not understand, but that is the fact, believe me, for I know it very well, and I would not tell you false. Well, then, you will easily comprehend, that this being so, you must never on any account come near my part of the house, nor will I come near yours. Lately we have been very much together, but then we have been active, full of purpose and occupation: here we shall be nothing of the kind, I can see. You do not understand at all﻿—but things are so. We must live perfectly separate lives, then. You are nothing to me, really, nor I to you, only we live on the same earth, which is nothing at all﻿—a mere chance. Your own food, clothes, and everything that you want, you will procure for yourself: it is perfectly easy: the shores are crowded with mansions, castles, towns and villages; and I will do the same for myself. The motor down there I set apart for your private use: if I want another, I will get one; and today I will set about looking you up a boat and fishing tackle, and cut a cross on the bow of yours, so that you may know yours, and never use mine. All this is very necessary: you cannot dream how much: but I know how much. Do not run any risks in climbing, now, or with the motor, or in the boat﻿ ﻿… little Leda﻿ ﻿…”
I saw her underlip push, and I turned away in haste, for I did not care whether she cried or not. In that long voyage, and in my illness at Venice, she had become too near and dear to me, my tender love, my dear darling soul; and I said in my heart: “I will be a decent being: I will turn out trumps.”

两头各有一套房间，多是小小的屋子，说不出的古雅安逸，摆着笨重的亨利四世式家具和床帷；各自还有单独的、可说是隐秘的螺旋楼梯出入：于是我们商定，她住纵览长湖、罗讷河口、布弗雷和维伦纽夫的那一套；我住俯望屋后那道沙嘴和小吊桥、岸边峭壁和一直伸到水边的榆树林的那一套，从林间一点空隙可以窥见西庸那个小小的村子：这事一定，我把她的手握在我手里，说：
「好，那我们就住下，同一片屋顶底下——头一回。勒达，为什么，我不对你解释，可这很危险，危险到也许意味着我们两人中一个的死：致命地、致命地危险，我可怜的姑娘。你不懂，可事实如此，相信我，我知道得清清楚楚，我不骗你。那么你很容易明白：既然如此，你无论如何不许走近宅子我这一头，我也不走近你那一头。近来我们一直在一起，可那时我们在赶路，满是目标和营生：在这儿我们什么营生也没有，我看得出来。你全不懂——可事情就是这样。我们必须过完全分开的日子。你于我算不得什么，真的，我于你也一样，我们不过同住一个地球，那不值一提——纯属偶然。你自己的吃食、衣裳、要的一切，你自己去弄：这再容易不过：湖岸上满是宅邸、城堡、城镇和村子；我也照样自己弄。底下那辆摩托，我拨给你专用：我若还要，我再弄一辆；今天我就给你找一条船和一套钓具，在你的船头刻一个十字，好叫你认得你的，绝不用我的。这一切都非常必要：你想不到有多必要：可我知道。爬高、开摩托、坐船，都别冒一点险……小勒达……」
我看见她的下唇撅起来，急忙转过身去，因为不在乎她哭不哭。在那漫长的旅途中，在威尼斯我生病的时候，她离我太近、太亲了，我温柔的爱，我亲爱的心肝；我在心里说：「我要做个正派的人：我要出落成个像样的。」
