===Original===

This of city-burning has now become a habit with me more enchaining﻿—and infinitely more debased﻿—than ever was opium to the smoker, or alcohol to the drunkard. I count it among the prime necessaries of my life: it is my brandy, my bacchanal, my secret sin. I have burned Calcutta, Peking, and San Francisco. In spite of the restraining influence of this palace, I have burned and burned. I have burned two hundred cities and countrysides. Like Leviathan disporting himself in the sea, so I have rioted in this earth.

===Chinese===

这烧城的勾当，如今成了我一种戒不掉的习惯——比鸦片之于烟客、酒精之于酒徒更束人，也更无限地更下贱。我把它算作我生命的头等必需：它是我的白兰地，我的酒神祭，我的隐秘之罪。我烧了加尔各答，烧了北京，烧了旧金山。尽管有这座宫殿约東着，我仍旧烧了又烧。我烧了两百座城池和乡野。像利维坦在海里翻腾戏耍，我便这样在大地上纵欲横行。
