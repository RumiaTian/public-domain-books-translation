===Original===

Very benign, I say, and pleasant to see, was sky and sea during all that voyage: but it was at sunset that my sense of the wondrously beautiful was roused and excited, in spite of that great burden which I carried. Certainly, I never saw sunsets resembling those, nor could have conceived of aught so flamboyant, extravagant, and bewitched: for the whole heaven seemed turned into an arena for warring Hierarchies, warring for the universe, or it was like the wild countenance of God defeated, and flying marred and bloody from His enemies. But many evenings I watched with unintelligent awe, believing it but a portent of the unsheathed sword of the Almighty; till, one morning, a thought pricked me like a sword, for I suddenly remembered the great sunsets of the later nineteenth century, witnessed in Europe, America, and, I believe, over the world, after the eruption of the volcano of Krakatoa.
And whereas I had before said to myself: “If now a wave from the Deep has washed over this planetary ship of earth﻿ ﻿… ,” I said now: “A wave﻿—but not from the Deep: a wave rather which she had reserved, and has spouted, from her own un-motherly entrails﻿ ﻿…”

===Chinese===

我说，整个那段航程里，天和海都是极仁慈、极悦目的：可正是在日落时分，我那对奇美的感受才被唤起、被激动，尽管我驮着那副重担。的确，我从没见过那样的日落，也想象不出任何这样火焰般、这样恣肆、这样中魔的东西：因为整个天穹仿佛变成了一座角斗场，供交战的天军厮杀，为宇宙而战；又像上帝那狂野的面容战败了，带着残缺与血污，从祂的仇敌面前奔逃。可许多个傍晚，我只是怀着不明所以的畏怖仰望，以为那不过是全能者出鞘之剑的一个预兆；直到一天早晨，一个念头像剑一样刺了我一下——因为我猛然想起十九世纪末那些伟大的日落：喀拉喀托火山喷发之后，欧洲、美洲，我相信还有全世界，都曾见过。
而先前我对自己说：「倘若此刻，深渊里涌来的一股巨浪漫过了地球这艘行星之船……」如今我则说：「是巨浪——但不是从深渊里来：倒不如说，是她自己蓄下的、从她那全无慈母心肠的脏腑里喷出来的巨浪……」
