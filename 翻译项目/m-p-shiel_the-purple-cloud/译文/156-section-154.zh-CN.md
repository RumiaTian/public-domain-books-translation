===Original===

She must rise very early. I who am now regularly on the palace-roof at dawn, sometimes from between the pavilion-curtains of the galleries, or from the steps of the telescope-kiosk, may spy her far down below, a dainty microscopic figure, generally running about the sward, or gazing up in wonder at the palace from the lake-edge.
It is now three months since she came with me to Imbros.
I left her the first night in that pale-yellow house with the two green jalousies facing the beach, where there was everything that she would need; but I knew that, like all the houses there now, it leaked profusely, and the next day I went down to the curving stair, cut through the rock at the back and south of the village, climbed, and half a mile beyond found that park and villa with gables, which I had noted from the sea. The villa is almost intact, very strongly built of purplish marble, though small, and very like a Western house, with shingles, and three gables, so that I think it must have been the yali of some Englishman, for it contains a number of English books, though the only body I saw there was what looked like an Aararat Kurd, with spiral string wound down his turban, yellow ankle-pantaloons, and flung red shoulder-cloak; and all in the heavily-wooded park, and all about the low rock-steps up the hill, profusions of mandragora; and from the rock-steps to the house a narrow long avenue of acacias, mossy underfoot, that mingle overhead, the house standing about four yards from the edge of the perpendicular sea-cliff, whence one can see the *Speranza*’s main topmast, and broken mizzenmast-head, in her quiet haven. After examining the place I went down again to the village, and her house: but she was not there: and two hours long I paced about among the weeds of these amateur little alleys and flat-roofed windowless houses (though some have terrace-roofs, and a rare aperture), whose once-raw yellows, greens, and blues look now like sunset tints when the last flush is gone, and they fade dun. When at last she came running with open mouth, I took her up the rock-steps, and into the house, and there she has lived, one of the gable-tips, I now find (that overlooking the sea), being just visible from the northeast corner of the palace-roof, two miles from it.
That night again, when I was leaving her, she made an attempt to follow me.

她一定起得很早。我如今照例天一亮就上宫殿屋顶，有时从游廊的亭子帘幕间，有时从望远镜凉亭的台阶上，可以远远窥见底下的她，一个玲珑的、小小的人影，多半在草地上跑来跑去，要么站在湖边，仰着头惊叹地望这宫殿。
她跟我来因姆罗兹，如今已经三个月了。
头一夜我把她安置在那座淡黄色的宅子里——两扇绿百叶窗朝着海滩——屋里她需要的一应俱全；可我知道那屋子同这里如今所有的屋子一样，漏得厉害，第二天我便下到村子后南面凿穿山岩的那道弯梯，爬上去，走过半英里，寻见那座带山墙的园子和别墅，是我从海上就注意到的。别墅几乎完好，用带紫的大理石造得非常坚固，虽然不大，很有点像西方的宅子，木瓦顶，三座山墙，所以我想它多半是哪个英国人的雅宅，因为里面有好多英文书，虽然我在那儿见到的唯一一个人，看样子是个亚拉拉特库尔德人，头巾上螺旋地缠着绳，黄色的扎脚裤，红披肩甩在肩上；浓荫的园子里，上坡的低石阶四周，到处都是一片一片的曼德拉草；从石阶到宅子是一条又窄又长的金合欢林荫道，脚下生着青苔，树冠在头顶交织；宅子离直立的悬崖边约四码，从那儿望得见斯佩兰扎号静泊的港湾里它的中桅桅顶和断掉的后桅桅头。察看过地方，我又下到村里，到她那座宅子：可她不在：我在这些外行的小巷和没有窗的平顶屋之间的荒草里踱了两个钟头（有几座带露台顶，偶尔开个豁口），那些当初生生的黄、绿、蓝，如今像落日褪尽最后一抹红晕的颜色，暗淡成灰褐。末了她张着嘴跑回来，我带她上石阶，进了别墅，从那时起她就住在那儿；那个山墙尖（朝着海的那个），我如今发觉，从宫殿屋顶的东北角恰好看得见——离它两英里。
那天夜里，我离开她的时候，她又想跟我走。
