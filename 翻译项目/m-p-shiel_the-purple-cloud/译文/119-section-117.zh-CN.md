===Original===

One evening, the 29th November, I dined rather late﻿—at eight﻿—sitting, as was my custom in calm weather, cross-legged on the cabin-rug at the port aft corner, a small semicircle of *Speranza* gold-plate before me, and near above me the red-shaded lamp with green conical reservoir, whose creakings never cease in the stillest mid-sea, and beyond the plates the array of preserved soups, meat-extracts, meats, fruit, sweets, wines, nuts, liqueurs, coffee on the silver spirit-tripod, glasses, cruet, and so on, which it was always my first care to select from the storeroom, open, and lay out once for all in the morning on rising. I was late, seven being my hour: for on that day I had been engaged in the occasionally necessary, but always deferred, task of overhauling the ship, brushing here a rope with tar, there a board with paint, there a crank with oil, rubbing a door-handle, a brass-fitting, filling the three cabin-lamps, dusting mirrors and furniture, dashing the great neat-joinered plains of deck with bucketfulls, or, high in air, chopping loose with its rigging the mizzen topmast, which since a month was sprained at the clamps, all this in cotton drawers under loose *quamis*, barefooted, my beard knotted up, the sun ablaze, the sea smooth and pale with the smooth pallor of strong currents, the ship still enough, no land in sight, yet great tracts of seaweed making eastward﻿—I working from 11 a.m. till near 7, when sudden darkness interrupted: for I wished to have it all over in one obnoxious day. I was therefore very tired when I went down, lit the central chain-lever lamp and my own two, washed and dressed in my bedroom, and sat to dinner in the dining-hall corner. I ate voraciously, with sweat, as usual, pouring down my eager brow, using knife or spoon in the right hand, but never the Western fork, licking the plates clean in the Mohammedan manner, and drinking pretty freely. Still I was tired, and went upon deck, where I had the threadbare blue-velvet easy-chair with the broken left arm before the wheel, and in it sat smoking cigar after cigar from the Indian D box, half-asleep, yet conscious.

十一月二十九日那天傍晚，我吃得颇晚——八点才开饭——照无风天气的习惯，盘腿坐在舱室地毯的左舷后角，面前摆着小半圈「斯佩兰扎号」的金盘，头顶近处挂着红灯罩、绿锥形油罐的灯，那灯即使在大洋最静的中夜也吱呀不停；盘子那边列着罐装汤、肉精、肉、果品、甜食、酒、果仁、利口酒，银制酒精三脚架上的咖啡，杯子、调料架等等——这些素来是我清早起床后的头一件操心：从库房里挑出来、开好、一次摆停当。我吃得迟了，平时七点是我的饭点：因为那天我一直在干那偶尔必要、却总是拖延的活计：检修全船，这儿给一根绳刷沥青，那儿给一块板上漆，给一处曲柄上油，擦一擦门把、一件铜活，给三盏舱灯添油，掸镜子和家具的灰，一桶桶水泼甲板那大片刨得光洁的平面，或者高悬半空，连索具一齐砍掉那后桅上桅——它在夹箍处扭伤已有一个月了——这一切都穿着棉布衬裤、罩着宽松的「卡米斯」，赤着脚，胡须打起结，太阳灼灼，海面平滑而苍白，是强流特有的那种平滑的白，船静得很，望不见陆地，却有大片大片的海草向东去——我从上午十一点干到将近七点，突来的夜色才打断：因为我巴不得这讨厌的一天里一气干完。所以下舱时我很累了，点着中链杆的大灯和我自己的两盏，在卧室洗漱更衣，然后在饭厅一角落座用膳。我狼吞虎咽，照例汗如雨下，急切地淌过额头，右手用刀或勺，却从不用西式叉子，照穆罕默德的方式把盘子舔干净，酒也喝得相当放开。可我还是累，便上了甲板；舵轮前摆着我那把磨旧了的蓝丝绒安乐椅，左扶手已断，我坐在里面，一支接一支地抽着印度 D 字盒里的雪茄，半睡着，却还有知觉。

The moon came up into a pretty cloudless sky, and she was bright, but not bright enough to outshine the enlightened flight of the ocean, which that night was one continuous swamp of Jack-o’-lantern phosphorescence, a wild but faint luminosity mingled with stars and flashes of brilliance, the whole trooping unanimously eastward, as if in haste with elfin momentous purpose, a boundless congregation, in the sweep of a strong oceanic current. I could hear it, in my slumbrous lassitude, struggling and gurgling at the tied rudder, and making wet sloppy noises under the sheer of the poop; and I was aware that the *Speranza* was gliding along pretty fast, drawn into that procession, probably at the rate of four to six knots: but I did not care, knowing very well that no land was within two hundred miles of my bows, for I was in longitude 173°, in the latitude of Fiji and the Society Islands, between those two: and after a time the cigar drooped and dropped from my mouth, and sleep overcame me, and I slept there, in the lap of the Infinite.

月亮升上相当无云的夜空，很亮，却亮不过那晚大洋的流光：那一夜的海，是一整片鬼火般磷光的沼泽，一种狂野而幽微的亮，混着星点和骤闪的光华，全体步调一致地向东行军，仿佛怀着精灵的什么重大使命匆匆赶路，一支无边无际的队伍，被一股强劲的洋流裹卷着。在我昏昏欲睡的倦怠里，我听得见它绑死的舵边挣扎着、汩汩作响，在船尾楼虚弧下弄出湿漉漉的咂嘴声；我也知道「斯佩兰扎号」正滑行得相当快，被吸进了那支队伍，速度约莫四到六节：可我并不在意，深知我船头两百英里之内没有陆地，因为我正处西经 173 度，斐济与社会群岛之间的那个纬度上：过了一阵，雪茄从我嘴角垂下、掉落，睡意征服了我，我就睡在了那里，睡在无限的膝上。
