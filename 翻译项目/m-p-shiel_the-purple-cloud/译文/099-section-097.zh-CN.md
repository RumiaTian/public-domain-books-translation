===Original===

I left no quarter unremembered, taking a train, no longer of four, but of eight, vehicles, drawn by an electric motor which I recharged every morning, mostly from the turbine station in St. Pancras, once from a steam-station with very small engine and dynamo, found in the Palace Theatre, which gave little trouble, and once from a similar little station in a Strand hotel. With these I visited West Ham and Kew, Finchley and Clapham, Dalston and Marylebone; I exhausted London; I deposited piles in the Guildhall, in Holloway Gaol, in the new pillared Justice-hall of Newgate, in the Tower, in the Parliament-house, in St. Giles’ Workhouse, in the Crypt and under the organ of St. Paul’s, in the South Kensington Museum, in the Royal Agricultural Society, in Whiteley’s place, in the Trinity House, in Liverpool Street, in the Office of Works, in the secret recesses of the British Museum; in a hundred inflammable warehouses, in five hundred shops, in a thousand private dwellings. And I timed them all for ignition at midnight of the 23rd April.
By five in the afternoon of the 22nd, when I left my train in Maida Vale, and drove alone to the solitary house on high ground near Hampstead Heath which I had chosen, the work was well finished.

===Chinese===

我没有漏掉任何一个角落；我拉的车不再是一列四节，而是一列八节，由电动机车牵引，我每天早晨给它充电，多半在圣潘克拉斯的涡轮电站，有一回在宫殿剧院找到的一座小引擎小电机的蒸汽电站——没添什么麻烦——还有一回在斯特兰德街一家旅馆的小电站。就这样，我跑遍了西哈姆与邱园，芬奇利与克拉彭，达尔斯顿与马里勒本；我把伦敦榨了个遍；我把柴堆进了市政厅、霍洛韦监狱、新门监狱新的列柱法庭、伦敦塔、议会大厦、圣贾尔斯贫民习艺所、圣保罗教堂的地下圣室和管风琴底下、南肯辛顿博物馆、皇家农业协会、怀特利商厦、三一议院、利物浦街、工程局、大英博物馆的幽密库藏；堆进了一百座易燃的货栈、五百家铺子、一千所私宅。我把它们全部定在4月23日午夜一齐点火。
到22日下午五点，我把列车撂在梅达韦尔，独自驱车去汉普斯特德荒原附近高地上我选定的那座孤宅，那时，工程已干得妥妥帖帖。
