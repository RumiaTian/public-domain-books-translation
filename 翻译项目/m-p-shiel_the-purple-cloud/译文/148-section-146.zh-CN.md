===Original===

It seems certain that she never wore, saw, nor knew of, clothes.
I have dressed her, first sousing her thoroughly with sponge and soap in lukewarm rose-water in the silver cistern of the harem-bath, which is a circular marbled apartment with a fountain and the complicated ceilings of these houses, and frescoes, and gilt texts of the Koran on the walls, and pale rose-silk hangings. On the divan I had heaped a number of selected garments, and having showed her how to towel herself, I made her step into a pair of the trousers called *shintiyan* made of yellow-striped white-silk; this, by a running string, I tied loosely round the upper part of her hips; then, drawing up the bottoms to her knees, tied them there, so that their voluminous baggy folds, overhanging still to the ankles, have rather the look of a skirt; over this I put upon her a blue-striped chiffon chemise, or qamis, reaching a little below the hips; I then put on a short jacket or vest of scarlet satin, thickly embroidered in gold and precious stones, reaching somewhat below the waist, and pretty tight-fitting; and, making her lie on the couch, I put upon her little feet little yellow baboosh-slippers, then anklets, on her fingers rings, round her neck a necklace of sequins, finally dyeing her nails, which I cut, with henna. There remained her head, but with this I would have nothing to do, only pointing to the tarboosh which I had brought, to a square kerchief, to some corals, and to the fresco of a woman on the wall, which, if she chose, she might copy. Lastly, I pierced her ears with the silver needles which they used here: and after two hours of it left her.
About an hour afterwards I saw her in the arcade round the court, and, to my great surprise, she had a perfect plait down her back, and over her head and brows a green-silk feredjeh, or hood, precisely as in the picture.

===Chinese===

看来可以断定，衣裳她从来没有穿过，没有见过，也从来没有听说过。
我给她穿戴起来：先在内院浴房的银槽里用海绵和肥皂，就着温热的玫瑰水，把她通身擦洗了一遍——那浴房是一间圆形的大理石屋子，有一眼泉，这些宅子特有的繁复的穹顶，壁画，墙上镀金的古兰经文，和浅玫瑰色的绸帷幔。长榻上我堆了一摞拣好的衣裳；教过她怎么用浴巾擦身之后，我让她迈进去一条叫欣提扬（shintiyan）的裤子，白地黄条纹的绸子做的；我用一根抽绳把它松松系在她胯骨上；再把裤脚挽到膝下扎住，于是那宽宽垮垮、仍旧垂到脚踝的裤褶，倒更像一条裙子；在这外面我给她套上一件蓝条纹的雪纺衬衣，也就是卡米斯（qamis），长到臀下一点；然后穿上一件大红缎的短上衣或曰坎肩，密密绣着金线和宝石，长到腰下一点，相当贴身；再让她在软榻上躺下，给她的小脚套上小小的黄色巴布什拖鞋，再戴上脚镯，手指上套戒指，脖子上挂一串亮片项链，最后把我替她剪好的指甲染上指甲花。剩下那颗头，我可不管了，只指了指我带来的塔布什帽、一方包头巾、几串珊瑚，还有墙上那幅女人的壁画：她要愿意，尽可以照着打扮。末了，我用此地惯用的银针，替她穿了耳洞：这样忙了两个钟头，我丢下她走了。
约莫一个钟头后，我在绕院的拱廊里看见她：叫我大吃一惊的是，她背上垂着一条完好的辫子，头上眉上罩着一方绿绸的费雷杰（feredjeh），也就是兜帽——同画上那个女人一模一样。
