Well, the most of that same day I spent in a high chamber at Woolwich, dusting out, and sometimes oiling, time-fuses: a work in which I acquired such facility in some hours, that each finally occupied me no more than ninety to a hundred seconds, so that by evening I had, with the previous day’s work, close on 600. The construction of these little things is very simple, and, I believe, effective, so that I should have no difficulty in making them myself in large numbers, if it were necessary. Most contain a tiny dry battery, which sends a current along a bell or copper wire at the running-down moment, the clocks being contrived to be set for so many days, hours, and minutes, while others ignite by striking. I arranged in rows in the covered van those which I had prepared, and passed the night in an inn near the Barracks. I had brought candlesticks from London in the morning, and arranged the furniture﻿—a settee, chest-of-drawers, basin-stand, table, and a number of chairs﻿—in three-quarter-circle round the bed, so getting a triple-row altar of lights, mixed with vases of the house containing small palms and evergreens; with this I mingled a smell of ambergris from the scattered contents of some Turkish sachets which I had; in the bed a bottle of sweet Chypre-wine, with bonbons, nuts, and Havannas. As I lay me down, I could not but reflect, with a smile which I knew to be evil, upon that steady, strong, smouldering lust within me which was urging me through all those pains at the Arsenal, I who shirked every labour as unkingly. So, however, it was: and the next morning I was at it again after an early breakfast, my fingers at first quite stiff with cold, for it blew a keen and January gale. By nine I had 820 fuses; and judging those sufficient to commence with, got into the motor, and took it round to a place called the East Laboratory, a series of detached buildings, where I knew that I should find whatever I wanted: and I prepared my mind for a day’s labour.

唉，那一天的大半，我都消磨在伍利奇一间高高的屋子里，给定时引信掸尘，有时上点油：这活计我几个钟头便熟练到这般地步，末了每只只消九十到一百秒，于是到傍晚，连同前一日的活，我已有将近六百只。这些小东西构造很简单，而且我相信很有效，若有必要，我自己动手大批制造也不难。它们大半内装一节小小的干电池，走完时辰的一刻便沿一根电铃线或铜线送出电流，钟机可以拨定几天、几点、几分；另一些则一击即燃。我把备好的引信一排排安在那辆带篷货车里，在兵营附近一家客栈过的夜。早上我从伦敦带来了烛台，把家具——一张软榻、一口五斗橱、脸盆架、一张桌子、若干椅子——绕床摆成四分之三的圆圈，围出一座三层的灯烛之坛，间杂着屋里花瓶中的小小棕榈与常青；我又洒了几只土耳其香囊里的碎屑，掺进一股龙涎香；床上放着一瓶塞浦路斯甜酒，配着糖果、果仁和哈瓦那雪茄。躺下时，我不禁带着一抹自知邪恶的笑，想起我胸中那股稳定、强大、闷燃的欲火——它驱使我吃了兵工厂里那些苦头的，正是我这个把一切劳作都当有失王者身份而推三阻四的人。然而事情就是这样：第二天清晨，我早早吃过饭又干起来，手指起初冻得发僵，因为刮着一月里那种磆骨的大风。到九点，我已有八百二十只引信；估摸着够开局之用，我便上了摩托，开到一处叫「东实验室」的地方——一连串彼此独立的建筑——我知道在那里想要什么有什么：我也定好了心思，来干上一整天。

In this place I found incredible stores: mountains of percussion-caps, more chambers of fuses, small-arm cartridges, shells, and all those murderous explosive mixtures, a-making and made, with which modern savagery occupied its leisure in exterminating itself: or, at least, savagery civilised in its top-story only: for civilisation was apparently from the head downwards, and never once grew below the neck in all those centuries, those people being certainly much more mental than cordial, though I doubt if they were genuinely mental either﻿—reminding one rather of that composite image of Nebuchadnezzar, head of gold, breast brazen, feet of clay﻿—head manlike, heart cannibal, feet bestial﻿—like aegipeds, and mermaids, and puzzling undeveloped births. However, it is of no importance: and perhaps I am not much better than the rest, for I, too, after all, am of them. At any rate, their lyddites, melanites, cordites, dynamites, powders, jellies, oils, marls, and civilised barbarisms and obeahs, came in very well for their own destruction: for by two o’clock I had so worked, that I had on the first cart the phalanx of fuses; on the second a goodly number of kegs, cartridge-cases and cartridge-boxes, full of powder, explosive cottons and gelatines, and liquid nitroglycerine, and earthy dynamite, with some bombs, two reels of cordite, two pieces of tarred cloth, a small iron ladle, a shovel, and a crowbar; the cab came next, containing a considerable quantity of loose coal; and lastly, in the private carriage lay four big cans of common oil. And first, in the Laboratory, I connected a fuse-conductor with a huge tun of blasting-gelatine, and I set the fuse on the ground, timed for the midnight of the twelfth day thence; and after that I visited the Main Factory, the Carriage Department, the Ordnance Store Department, the Royal Artillery Barracks, and the Powder Magazines in the Marshes, traversing, as it seemed to me, miles of building; and in some I laid heaps of oil-saturated coal with an explosive in suitable spots on the ground-floor near woodwork, and in some an explosive alone: and all I timed for ignition at midnight of the twelfth day. Hot now, and black as ink, I proceeded through the town, stopping with perfect system at every hundredth door: and I laid the faggots of a great burning: and timed them all for ignition at midnight of the twelfth day.

在这地方我发现了难以置信的库存：雷管堆成了山，还有一间间屋子的引信、枪弹、炮弹，以及现代野蛮人用来消磨闲暇、自相灭绝的一切杀人的爆炸混合物——或者说，至少是只在头脑那一层文明化了的野蛮人：因为文明显然是自上而下、从头部下延的，那许多世纪里从未长到脖颈以下；那些人确实心智远多于心血，虽然我怀疑他们连心智也未必真有——倒叫人想起尼布甲尼撒那尊复合的像：金头、铜胸、泥足——头似人，心似食人族，足似畜生——像羊足怪、像人鱼、像种种令人费解的未发育全的怪胎。不过这无关紧要：说不定我也比其余的强不到哪儿去，因为我毕竟也是他们中的一个。无论如何，他们的利达炸药、梅拉尼特炸药、柯达火药、达纳炸药、火药、胶质炸药、油类、泥灰，以及种种文明的野蛮和巫蛊，正好拿来毁了他们自己：因为到两点钟，我已干到这个地步：第一辆车上是引信的方阵；第二辆上是相当数量的火药桶、弹壳和弹药箱，满装着火药、火棉和胶质炸药，液态硝化甘油和土状达纳炸药，外加几颗炸弹、两卷柯达火药、两块油毡布、一只小铁勺、一把铲子、一根撬棍；出租马车随后，装着相当多的散煤；最后，私人马车里躺着四大罐寻常的油。先是在实验室里，我把一根引信导线连上一大桶爆破胶质炸药，把引信搁在地上，拨定十二天后那一天的午夜；随后我走遍了总厂、车械所、军械库、皇家炮兵营房和沼地里的火药库，走过的屋宇，仿佛有好几英里；有几处我在底层的木作近旁，堆上浸透了油的煤，配上炸药，择地放好；有几处只放炸药：全部拨定十二天后那一天的午夜引爆。此刻我浑身滚烫，黑得像墨，穿城而过，每第一百户门口便极有章法地停一停：我布下了一场大火的柴堆：全部拨定十二天后那一天的午夜点燃。
