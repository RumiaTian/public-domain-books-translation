===Original===

Soon after midnight there was a sudden and very visible increase in the conflagration. On all hands I began to see blazing structures soar, with grand hurrahs, on high. In fives and tens, in twenties and thirties, all between me and the remote limit of my vision, they leapt, they lingered long, they fell. My spirit more and more felt, and danced﻿—deeper mysteries of sensation, sweeter thrills. I sipped exquisitely, I drew out enjoyment leisurely. Anon, when some more expansive angel of flame would arise from the Pit with steady aspiration, and linger with outspread arms, and burst, I would lift a little from the chair, leaning forward to clap, as at some famous acting; or I would call to them in shouts of cheer, giving them the names of Woman. For now I seemed to see nothing but some bellowing pandemonic universe through crimson glasses, and the air was wildly hot, and my eyeballs like theirs that walk staring in the inner midst of burning fiery furnaces, and my skin itched with a fierce and prickly itch. Anon I touched the chords of the harp to the air of Wagner’s *Walküren-ritt*.
Near three in the morning, I reached the climax of my guilty sweets. My drunken eyelids closed in a luxury of pleasure, and my lips lay stretched in a smile that dribbled; a sensation of dear peace, of almighty power, consoled me: for now the whole area which through streaming tears I surveyed, mustering its ten thousand thunders, and brawling beyond the stars the voice of its southward-rushing torment, billowed to the horizon one grand Atlantic of smokeless and flushing flame; and in it sported and washed themselves all the fiends of Hell, with laughter, shouts, wild flights, and holiday; and I﻿—first of my race﻿—had flashed a signal to the nearer planets.﻿ ﻿…

===Chinese===

午夜刚过，大火骤然又猛又显地旺了起来。四面八方，我看见燃烧的建筑呼啸着、轰然欢呼着腾空而起。三座五座，十座廿座，三十座五十座，在我与视野尽头之间，它们跃起，久久悬停，又颓然倒下。我的灵魂越感越深，翩翩起舞——感觉的秘境更深，快意的震颤更甜。我细细地啜饮，从容地抻长了享受。不一会儿，当某位更恢弘的火焰天使从深渊里挟着沉稳的志向奋然升起，张着双臂悬停，訇然炸开，我便会从椅子里欠身前倾，鼓起掌来，像在看名角的演出；或者朝他们高声喝彩，把女人的名字一个个赐给他们。因为此刻，我隔着绛红的镜片看出去，仿佛只看见一个怒吼的全魔的宇宙；空气灼热得发狂，我的眼球像那些瞪眼行走在燃烧炉膛最深处的人，我的皮肤上起着一阵凶猛刺痛的痒。我不时拨响竖琴的弦，和着瓦格纳《女武神的骑行》的调子。
凌晨近三点，我的罪中之甜达到了顶点。我醉了的双眼在极乐的餍足里阖上，嘴唇咧开一个淌着口水的微笑；一种亲爱安宁之感、一种全能大力之感抚慰着我：因为此刻，我透过簌簌的泪所俯瞰的这整片天地，正集结着它的万钧雷霆，以其南奔的苦痛在星辰之外咆哮，把一片无烟而红透的火焰大西洋翻涌到天边；地狱的群魔全在里面嬉戏、洗浴，大笑，欢呼，狂飞，过节；而我——我种族中的第一人——已向较近的行星们闪出了一道信号。……
