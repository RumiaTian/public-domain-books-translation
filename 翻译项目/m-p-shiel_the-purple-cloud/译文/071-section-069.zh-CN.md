Two days later I was at the mines: and here I first saw that widespread scene of horror with which I have since become familiar. The story of six out of ten of them all is the same, and short: selfish “owners,” an ousted world, an easy bombardment, and the destruction of all concerned, before the arrival of the cloud in many cases. About some of the Durham pit-mouths I have been given the impression that the human race lay collected there; and that the notion of hiding himself in a mine must have occurred to every man alive, and sent him thither.
In these lead mines, as in most vein-mining, there are more shafts than in collieries, and hardly any attempt at artificial ventilation, except at rises, winzes and cul-de-sacs. I found accordingly that, though their depth does not exceed three hundred feet, suffocation must often have anticipated the other dreaded death. In nearly every shaft, both uptake and down-take, was a ladder, either of the mine, or of the fugitives, and I was able to descend without difficulty, having dressed myself in a house at the village in a check flannel shirt, a pair of two-buttoned trousers with circles of leather at the knees, thick boots, and a miner’s hat, having a leather socket attached to it, into which fitted a straight handle from a cylindrical candlestick; with this light, and also a Davy-lamp, which I carried about with me for a good many months, I lived for the most part in the deeps of the earth, searching for the treasure of a life, to find everywhere, in English duckies and guggs, Pomeranian women in gaudy stiff cloaks, the Walachian, the Mameluk, the Khirgiz, the Bonze, the Imam, and almost every type of man.

两天后，我到了矿区：在这里，我头一回见到那幅此后我已司空见惯的、铺展开来的恐怖景象。十处矿区里有六处，故事一模一样，而且简短：自私的『矿主』，一个被夺了位的世界，一场轻易的轰炸，以及相关人等全数毁灭——许多情形下，都发生在云雾抵达之前。达勒姆的几处坑口给我的印象是：整个人类种族仿佛都聚集在那里了；藏身矿井的念头，想必曾浮上每个活人的心头，把他们都赶到那里去了。
这些铅矿同大多数脉状矿一样，井筒比煤矿多，却几乎不设人工通风，只在上升巷、天井和死胡同里偶一为之。我因而发现，虽然井深不过三百英尺，窒息必定常常赶在那另一种可怖的死之前降临。几乎每个井筒里，出风的和进风的，都有一架梯子，或是矿上原有的，或是逃亡者们架的，我不费什么事便下了井；我先在村里一户人家穿戴齐整：格纹法兰绒衬衫，一条膝头缀着皮圆垫的双排扣裤子，厚靴子，还有一顶矿工帽，帽上拴着皮插座，插着一支直柄圆烛台的柄；就凭这点光，外加一盏戴维安全灯——这盏灯此后在我身边带了好几个月——我绝大部分时日都生活在地底深处，寻找一宗名叫性命的宝藏，结果处处找到的是：英国的坑工和矿汉，披着艳丽硬挺斗篷的波美拉尼亚妇女，瓦拉几亚人，马穆鲁克，吉尔吉斯人，和尚，阿訇，几乎人类的所有类型。
