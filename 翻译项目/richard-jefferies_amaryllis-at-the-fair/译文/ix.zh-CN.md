## IX

===Original===
This discordance between her father and mother hurt Amaryllis' affectionate heart exceedingly. It seemed to be always breaking out all the year round.

===Chinese===
父母之间的分歧深深刺痛了阿玛丽莉斯（Amaryllis）那颗多情的心。这种不和似乎整年都在不断地爆发。

===Original===
Of a summer's eve, when the day's work among the hot hay was done, Iden would often go out and sit under the russet apple till the dew had filled the grass like a green sea. When the tide of the dew had risen he would take off his heavy boots and stockings, and so walk about in the cool shadows of eve, paddling in the wet grass. He liked the refreshing coolness and the touch of the sward. It was not for washing, because he was scrupulously clean under the ragged old coat; it was because he liked the grass. There was nothing very terrible in it; men, and women, too, take off their shoes and stockings, and wade about on the sands at the sea, and no one thinks that it is anything but natural, reasonable, and pleasant. But, then, you see, *everybody* does it at the seaside, and Iden alone waded in the dew, and that was his crime—that he alone did it.

===Chinese===
夏日的傍晚，当一天在燥热的干草中劳作完毕后，艾登（Iden）常常会出门坐在那棵褐苹果树下，直到露水盈盈如一片碧绿的海洋铺满草地。待露潮涨起，他便脱去沉重的靴子和袜子，在黄昏清凉的阴影中踩着湿漉漉的草地漫步。他喜欢那沁人心脾的凉意和草皮的触感。他这样做并非为了清洗——他那件破旧外套底下其实整洁得无可挑剔——而是因为他喜欢草地。这本来算不上什么大不了的事：男人也好，女人也罢，都会脱了鞋袜在海边的沙滩上蹚水，没有人觉得这有何不妥，反倒觉得自然、合理、惬意。可是你看，*人人*都在海边这么做，唯独艾登独自在露水中蹚水，这便是他的"罪过"——唯独他一人这么干。

===Original===
The storm and rage of Mrs. Iden whenever she knew he was paddling in the grass was awful. She would come shuffling out—she had a way of rubbing her shoes along the ground when irritated with her hands under her apron, which she twisted about—and pelt him with scorn.

===Chinese===
每当艾登太太（Mrs. Iden）发觉他又在草地上蹚水时，那暴风骤雨般的怒火简直吓人。她会拖着脚蹭出来——她一生气就双手揪着围裙在身前绞来绞去，脚上的鞋子一路在地上蹭着——然后劈头盖脸地丢来一通嘲骂。

===Original===
"There, put your boots on—do, and hide your nasty feet!" (Iden had a particularly white skin, and feet as white as a lady's.) "Disgusting! Nobody ever does it but you, and you ought to be ashamed of yourself! Anything more disgusting I never heard of. Nobody else but you would ever think of such a thing; makes me feel queer to see you."

===Chinese===
"行了，穿上你的靴子吧——快点，把你那双恶心的脚藏起来！"（艾登皮肤特别白，双脚白得跟淑女的一样。）"真恶心！除了你没别人干这种事，你该害臊才对！再没比这更恶心的事了。除了你谁会想出这种事来；看见你我就浑身不自在。"

===Original===
Shuffling about, and muttering to herself, "Nobody else"—that was the sin and guilt of it—by-and-by Mrs. Iden would circle round to where he had left his boots, and, suddenly seizing them, would fling them in the ditch.

===Chinese===
她拖着脚来回踱步，嘴里念叨着"没别人"——这正是他罪孽和过失所在——不一会儿，艾登太太就绕到他脱靴子的地方，猛地抓起靴子，一把扔进了沟里。

===Original===
And I verily believe, in the depth of her indignation, if she had not been afraid to touch firearms, she would have brought out the gun, and had a shot at him.

===Chinese===
我深信不疑，若不是她惧怕碰枪，她盛怒之下准会搬出猎枪来朝他开上一枪。

===Original===
After a time Iden left his old post at the russet apple, and went up the meadow to the horse-chestnut trees that he himself had planted, and there, in peace and quietness and soft cool shadow, waded about in the dew, without anyone to grumble at him.

===Chinese===
过了一阵，艾登离开了褐苹果树下的老地方，走到草场上头他自己种的那些七叶树下，在那儿平平静静地、在柔和清凉的树荫中踏着露水漫步，再没有人来抱怨他了。

===Original===
How crookedly things are managed in this world!

===Chinese===
这世上的事安排得何等歪歪扭扭！

===Original===
It is the modern fashion to laugh at the East, and despise the Turks and all their ways, making Grand Viziers of barbers, and setting waiters in high places, with the utmost contempt for anything reasonable—all so incongruous and chance-ruled. In truth, all things in our very midst go on in the Turkish manner; crooked men are set in straight places, and straight people in crooked places, just the same as if we had all been dropped promiscuously out of a bag and shook down together on the earth to work out our lives, quite irrespective of our abilities and nature. Such an utter jumble!

===Chinese===
如今时兴嘲笑东方人，鄙夷土耳其人及其种种作风——让理发师当上大维齐尔，把侍者安插到高位上，对一切合情合理的事物嗤之以鼻——全都这么不伦不类、听天由命。其实，我们身边的一切也无不如此：歪人被摆到正位上，正人被塞进歪处，仿佛我们全是从一个袋子里胡乱倒出来、撒在地上各奔前程的，全然不顾各自的能力与天性。好一锅大杂烩！

===Original===
Here was Iden, with his great brain and wonderful power of observation, who ought to have been a famous traveller in unexplored Africa or Tibet, bringing home rarities and wonders; or, with his singular capacity for construction, a leading engineer, boring Mont Cenis Tunnels and making Panama Canals; or, with his Baconian intellect, forming a new school of philosophy—here was Iden, tending cows, and sitting, as the old story goes, undecidedly on a stile—sitting astride—and eternally sitting, and unable to make up his mind to get off on one side or the other.

===Chinese===
艾登这人，天资卓越，观察力惊人，本该成为名扬天下的旅行家，深入未探明的非洲或西藏，带回奇珍异宝；或者凭他那超凡的建造才能，当个首席工程师，去凿穿蒙塞尼隧道、开挖巴拿马运河；又或者凭他那培根式的才智，开创一个新哲学流派——可艾登呢，却在放牛，而且正如老故事里说的那样，犹犹豫豫地骑在篱笆上——两腿叉开坐着——永远坐着，下不了决心从哪边跳下来。

===Original===
Here was Mrs. Iden, who had had a beautiful shape and expressive eyes, full in her youth of life and fire, who ought to have led the gayest life in London and Paris alternately, riding in a carriage, and flinging money about in the most extravagant, joyous, and good-natured manner—here was Mrs. Iden making butter in a dull farmhouse, and wearing shoes out at the toes.

===Chinese===
艾登太太年轻时身段曼妙、双眸传神，浑身充满生命的活力与热情，本该在伦敦和巴黎之间过着最欢快的日子，坐马车兜风，以最挥霍、最快活、最和善的方式挥金如土——可艾登太太呢，却在一个死气沉沉的农庄里搅黄油，脚上的鞋子都磨穿了脚趾。

===Original===
So our lives go on, rumble-jumble, like a carrier's cart over ruts and stones, thumping anyhow instead of running smoothly on new-mown sward like a cricket-ball.

===Chinese===
我们的生活就是这样磕磕绊绊、乱七八糟，像运货马车碾过车辙和石块一样颠簸，哪儿都在乱撞，而非像板球在新割的草坪上那样平滑地滚动。

===Original===
It all happens in the Turkish manner.

===Chinese===
一切尽以土耳其式的方式发生。

===Original===
Another time there would come a letter from one of the Flammas in London. Could they spare a little bag of lavender?—they grew such lovely sweet lavender at Coombe Oaks. Then you might see Mr. and Mrs. Iden cooing and billing, soft as turtledoves, and fraternising in the garden over the lavender hedge. Here was another side, you see, to the story.

===Chinese===
另一次，会收到来自伦敦弗拉玛（Flamma）家的一封信。能不能匀一小袋薰衣草给他们？——他们库姆橡树庄园（Coombe Oaks）种的薰衣草又香又甜，可爱极了。这时你就能看到艾登先生和太太像斑鸠一样柔情脉脉，在花园里的薰衣草篱笆旁相亲相爱。你看，这故事还有另外一面呢。

===Original===
Mrs. Iden was very fond of lavender, the scent, and the plant in every form. She kept little bags of it in all her drawers, and everything at Coombe Oaks upstairs in the bedrooms had a faint, delicious lavender perfume. There is nothing else that smells so sweet and clean and dry. You cannot imagine a damp sheet smelling of lavender.

===Chinese===
艾登太太非常喜欢薰衣草，爱它的香气，也爱它的一切形态。她所有抽屉里都放着薰衣草小布袋，库姆橡树庄园楼上卧室里的每样东西都带着一缕淡雅怡人的薰衣草清香。没有别的什么气味能如此甜美、洁净又干爽。你绝想象不出一张潮湿的床单能散发出薰衣草的香气来。

===Original===
Iden himself liked lavender, and used to rub it between his finger and thumb in the garden, as he did, too, with the black-currant leaves and walnut-leaves, if he fancied anything he had touched might have left an unpleasant odour adhering to his skin. He said it cleaned his hands as much as washing them.

===Chinese===
艾登自己也喜欢薰衣草，常在花园里用拇指和食指揉搓它；若是觉得碰过什么沾上了难闻的气味，他还会揉搓黑加仑叶和核桃叶。他说这样洗手跟用水洗一样干净。

===Original===
Iden liked Mrs. Iden to like lavender because his mother had been so fond of it, and all the sixteen carved oak-presses which had been so familiar to him in boyhood were full of a thick atmosphere of the plant.

===Chinese===
艾登乐见艾登太太也喜欢薰衣草，因为他母亲生前就对它情有独钟，而他少年时代无比熟悉的那十六只雕花橡木衣柜里，至今仍弥漫着薰衣草的浓郁气息。

===Original===
Long since, while yet the honeymoon bouquet remained in the wine of life, Iden had set a hedge of lavender to please his wife. It was so carefully chosen, and set, and watched, that it grew to be the finest lavender in all the country. People used to come for it from round about, quite certain of a favourable reception, for there was nothing so sure to bring peace at Coombe Oaks as a mention of lavender.

===Chinese===
很久以前，当蜜月的花束还插在生命之酒中时，艾登就种了一道薰衣草篱笆来取悦妻子。他精心挑选、仔细栽种、悉心看护，终于种出了全乡最好的薰衣草。四面八方的人常来讨要，全然不担心被拒，因为在库姆橡树庄园，没有比提到薰衣草更能确保宾主尽欢的事了。

===Original===
But the letter from the Flammas was the great event—from London, all that way, asking for some Coombe Oaks lavender! Then there was billing and cooing, and fraternising, and sunshine in the garden over the hedge of lavender. If only it could have lasted! Somehow, as people grow older there seems so much grating of the wheels.

===Chinese===
可弗拉玛家的这封信却是那年头的大事——从伦敦那么远的地方写来，竟然想要库姆橡树庄园的薰衣草！于是便是一番卿卿我我、手足情深，薰衣草篱笆上空阳光灿烂。但愿这般情感能长久！然而不知怎的，人一上了年纪，生活的轮轴就像生了锈，到处咯咯作响。

===Original===
In time, long time, people's original feelings get strangely confused and overlaid. The churchwardens of the eighteenth century plastered the fresco paintings of the fourteenth in their churches—covered them over with yellowish mortar. The mould grows up, and hides the capital of the fallen column; the acanthus is hidden in earth. At the foot of the oak, where it is oldest, the bark becomes dense and thick, impenetrable, and without sensitiveness; you may cut off an inch thick without reaching the sap. A sort of scale or caking in long, long time grows over original feelings.

===Chinese===
日久天长，人们最初的感情便渐渐变得迷离而层层覆盖。十八世纪的教堂管事用灰浆涂盖了十四世纪的壁画——将它们埋在黄褐色的灰泥之下。霉菌蔓生，遮住了倒塌柱子的柱头；莨苕叶饰深埋泥土。橡树最古老的底部，树皮变得又密又厚，坚不可摧，毫无知觉；你砍下一寸厚也见不到树汁。一层类似鳞片或硬壳的东西，在漫长的岁月里渐渐长在了最初的感情之上。

===Original===
There was no one in the world so affectionate and loving as Mrs. Iden—no one who loved a father so dearly; just as Amaryllis loved *her* father.

===Chinese===
世间再没有比艾登太太更深情慈爱的人了——再没有谁如此挚爱自己的父亲；正如阿玛丽莉斯深爱*她的*父亲一样。

===Original===
But after they had lived at Coombe Oaks thirty years or so, and the thick dull bark had grown, after the scales or caking had come upon the heart, after the capital of the column had fallen, after the painting had been blurred, it came about that old Flamma, Mrs. Iden's father, died in London.

===Chinese===
然而在库姆橡树庄园住了大约三十年之后——在那层厚重迟钝的树皮长成之后，在那层鳞甲或硬壳覆上心头之后，在柱头倒塌之后，在壁画模糊之后——艾登太太的父亲老弗拉玛在伦敦去世了。

===Original===
After thirty years of absolute quiet at Coombe Oaks, husband and wife went up to London to the funeral, which took place at one of those fearful London cemeteries that strike a chill at one's very soul. Of all the horrible things in the world there is nothing so calmly ghastly as a London cemetery.

===Chinese===
经过库姆橡树庄园三十年的绝对宁静之后，夫妻俩前往伦敦参加葬礼。葬礼在一处令人毛骨悚然的伦敦公墓举行，那地方直往人的灵魂深处灌入寒意。世间种种可怕之物中，再没有比伦敦公墓更阴森惨淡的了。

===Original===
In the evening, after the funeral, Mr. and Mrs. Iden went to the theatre.

===Chinese===
葬礼当晚，艾登先生和太太去了剧院。

===Original===
"How frivolous! How unfeeling!" No, nothing of the sort; how truly sad and human, for to be human is to be sad. That men and women should be so warped and twisted by the pressure of the years out of semblance to themselves; that circumstances should so wall in their lives with insurmountable cliffs of granite facts, compelling them to tread the sunless gorge; that the coldness of death alone could open the door to pleasure.

===Chinese===
"多轻浮！多无情！"不，绝非如此；这恰恰是何等真切的悲凉与人性，因为做人便是悲凉。男人和女人竟被岁月的压力扭曲得面目全非，不复当年模样；环境竟用花岗岩般不可逾越的峭壁将他们的生活层层围堵，逼他们踏入不见天日的峡谷；唯有死亡的冰冷才能打开通向快乐的大门。

===Original===
They sat at the theatre with grey hearts. With the music and the song, the dancing, the colours and gay dresses, it was sadder there than in the silent rooms at the house where the dead had been. Old Flamma alone had been dead *there*; they were dead here. Dead in life—at the theatre.

===Chinese===
他们怀着灰暗的心坐在剧院里。音乐与歌声、舞蹈、色彩与华丽的衣裳，让这里比停放遗体的寂静房间更加悲伤。老弗拉玛只是在*那里*死了；他们却在这里死了。在活着的时候就死了——在剧院里。

===Original===
They had used to go joyously to the theatre thirty years before, when Iden came courting to town; from the edge of the grave they came back to look on their own buried lives.

===Chinese===
三十年前，当艾登进城求爱时，他们曾高高兴兴地去剧院；如今，他们从坟墓的边缘回过头来，看着自己被埋葬的一生。

===Original===
If you will only *think*, you will see it was a most dreadful and miserable incident, that visit to the theatre after the funeral.

===Chinese===
只要你肯*想一想*，你就会明白，葬礼之后去剧院这件事，是多么可怕而悲惨。
