## XIII

===Original===
To one, as it were, in the gallery, it was a delight to see her; her sweet cheeks, fresh as the dawn, reddening with suppressed indignation; her young brow bent; her eyes cast down—don't you think for a moment she would deign to look at them—pride in her heart, and resolute determination to fight for her dear father and mother.

===Chinese===
在一旁旁观，看她真是一种享受：她甜美的双颊如黎明般清新，此刻因压抑的愤慨而泛红；年轻的眉头紧蹙；双眼低垂——别以为她会屈尊看他们一眼——心中满是骄傲，决意为她亲爱的父母挺身而出。

===Original===
But she felt as she sat so unconcerned that there was a crack in her boot unmended, and it seemed as if everyone could see it though under the solid table. She had not had a really sound pair of boots for many, many months; they could not afford her a new pair at home, and the stupid shoemaker, "Established 1697," was such a time repairing her others.

===Chinese===
但她安坐时感到自己靴子上有一道裂口还没补上，尽管被厚实的桌子挡着，似乎每个人都看得见。她已经好多好多个月没有穿过一双真正完好的靴子了；家里给她买不起新的，那个"创立于1697年"的笨鞋匠修她另一双靴子修了那么久。

===Original===
She would not look at them, but she knew that they were all dressed better than she was; there were some of them very poor, and very vulgar, too, but they were all dressed better than her, and without a doubt had sound boots on their feet.

===Chinese===
她不愿看他们，但知道他们所有人都穿得比她好；其中有几个其实也很穷，也很粗俗，可穿的都比她强，而且毫无疑问脚上都蹬着完好的靴子。

===Original===
The cottagers in Coombe hamlet always had sound boots; she never had; nor, indeed, her mother. Her father had a pair, being compelled by the character of his work in the fields to take care of himself so far, though he wore a ragged coat. But neither mother nor daughter ever had a whole pair of boots—whole and sound as the very cottagers had.

===Chinese===
库姆小村（Coombe hamlet）的农户总有一双完好的靴子；她从来没有过；她母亲也没有。父亲有一双，是田间劳作的性质迫使他不得不这样照顾自己，尽管他穿着一件破旧的外套。但母亲和女儿都从来没有过一双完整完好的靴子——像那些农户穿的那样完整而结实。

===Original===
If Amaryllis had sat there with naked feet she would have been prouder than ever, and that is why I always loved her so; she was not to be put down by circumstances, she was above external things.

===Chinese===
如果阿玛丽莉斯光着脚坐在那里，她反而会比任何时候都骄傲——这正是我一直如此深爱她的缘故；她不会被境遇压倒，她超越于外在之物。

===Original===
But as time went on, and the dinner was nearly over—she had scarcely eaten anything—and as she glanced from time to time at her father's empty chair, and knew that he would not come, and that his defection would revive the old quarrel which might so easily have been mended, the Flamma blood began to rise and grow hotter and hotter, and the foot with the worn boot on it began to tap the floor.

===Chinese===
然而随着时间推移，晚餐快要结束了——她几乎什么也没吃——她时不时瞥向父亲那把空椅子，知道他不会来，而他的缺席会重新点燃那场本可以轻易化解的老争吵，弗拉玛（Flamma）的血液开始涌动，越来越滚烫，那只穿着破靴子的脚开始敲打地板。

===Original===
The Flamma blood would have liked to have swept the whole company over a precipice into the Red Sea as the herd of swine in old time. It was either the Red Sea or somewhere; geography is of no consequence.

===Chinese===
弗拉玛的血液恨不得把在座所有人扫下悬崖坠入红海，就像古时候那群猪一样。要么是红海，要么是别的什么地方；地理无关紧要。

===Original===
The Flamma blood would have liked to have seen them all poisoned and dying on their seats.

===Chinese===
弗拉玛的血液恨不得看到他们全都中毒，死在各自的座位上。

===Original===
The Flamma blood would have been glad to stick a knife into each of them—only it would not have touched them with the longest hop-pole in Kent, so utter was its loathing of the crew gloating over that empty chair.

===Chinese===
弗拉玛的血液恨不得往每个人身上捅一刀——只可惜它连肯特郡最长的啤酒花杆子都不愿碰他们，它对这群对着那把空椅子幸灾乐祸的人厌恶到了极点。

===Original===
And for once Amaryllis did not check it, and did not say to herself, "I *will* not be a Flamma."

===Chinese===
这一次阿玛丽莉斯没有克制自己，没有对自己说"我*绝不*做弗拉玛家的人"。

===Original===
Towards the end of the tedious banquet the word was passed round that everyone was to sit still, as Grandfather Iden was coming to look at his descendants.

===Chinese===
这场令人厌烦的宴会行将结束之际，消息传遍了席间：所有人都要坐好别动，艾登爷爷（Grandfather Iden）要来看望他的子孙后代了。

===Original===
There was not the least fear of any of them stirring, for they well knew his custom—to walk round, and speak a few words to everyone in turn, and to put a new golden sovereign into their hands. Thirty-two sovereigns it was in all—one for each—but the thirty-third was always a spade-guinea, which was presented to the individual who had best pleased him during the year.

===Chinese===
根本不用担心有人会动，因为他们对他的习惯了如指掌——他会绕着走一圈，依次跟每个人说几句话，然后往他们手里放一枚崭新的金索维林。总共三十二枚索维林，每人一枚，但第三十三枚总是一枚铲形基尼（spade-guinea），奖给那一年最令他满意的那个人。

===Original===
A genial sort of custom, no doubt, but fancy the emulation and the heartburning over the spade-guinea! For the fortunate winner usually considered himself the nearest to the Will.

===Chinese===
这习俗确实称得上和蔼可亲，但你且想想为了那枚铲形基尼而生出的竞争和嫉恨吧！因为幸运的赢家通常自以为最接近遗嘱的受益人选。

===Original===
Amaryllis' cheeks began to burn at the thought that she should have to take his horrible money. A hideous old monster he was to her at that moment—not that he had done anything to her personally—but he left her dear father to be worried out of his life by petty tradesmen, and her dear mother to go without a pair of decent boots, while he made this pompous distribution among these wretches. The hideous old monster!

===Chinese===
一想到自己竟要拿他那可恶的钱，阿玛丽莉斯的脸颊便灼烧起来。在那一刻，他对她而言就是一个可怕的老怪物——并非他本人对她做了什么——而是他放任她亲爱的父亲被小商贩们逼得走投无路，放任她亲爱的母亲连一双体面的靴子都没有，自己却在这群无耻之徒面前大摆排场分发钱财。这个可怕的老怪物！

===Original===
Out in the town the boys behind his back gave him endless nicknames: Granfer Iden, Floury Iden, My Lord Lardy-Cake, Marquis Iden, His Greasy Grace; and, indeed, with his whims and humours, and patronage, his caprices and ways of going on, if he but had a patent of nobility, Grandfather Iden would have made a wonderfully good duke.

===Chinese===
镇上的男孩们背地里给他起了没完没了的绰号：格兰弗·艾登（Granfer Iden）、面粉艾登（Floury Iden）、猪油蛋糕勋爵（My Lord Lardy-Cake）、艾登侯爵（Marquis Iden）、油腻阁下（His Greasy Grace）；说实在的，以他的性情、兴致、恩庇、反复无常和行事风格，只要有一纸贵族封状，艾登爷爷准能当个了不起的公爵。

===Original===
By-and-by in comes the old Pacha, still wearing his great grey tottery hat, and proceeds from chair to chair, tapping folk on the shoulder, saying a gracious word or two, and dropping his new golden sovereigns in their eager palms. There was a loud hum of conversation as he went round; they all tried to appear so immensely happy to see him.

===Chinese===
不一会儿，老帕夏（Pacha）走了进来，仍戴着那顶摇摇欲坠的大灰帽，挨个走到每把椅子旁，拍拍人们的肩膀，说上两句客套话，然后把崭新的金索维林塞进他们急切的手掌里。他走动时周围响起一片热烈的交谈声；每个人都装出见到他无比高兴的样子。

===Original===
Amaryllis did not exactly watch him, but of course knew what he was about, when suddenly there was a dead silence. Thirty-two people suddenly stopped talking as if the pneumatic brake had been applied to their lips by a sixty-ton locomotive.

===Chinese===
阿玛丽莉斯没有特意看他，但当然知道他在做什么，突然间一片死寂。三十二个人同时停止了说话，仿佛一台六十吨的机车给他们的嘴唇按上了气动刹车。

===Original===
Dead, ominous silence. You could almost hear the cat licking his paw under the table.

===Chinese===
死一般的、不祥的寂静。你几乎能听见桌子底下猫在舔爪子。

===Original===
Amaryllis looked, and saw the old man leaning with both hands on the back of his son's empty chair.

===Chinese===
阿玛丽莉斯望过去，看见老人双手撑在他儿子空椅子的椅背上。

===Original===
He seemed to cling to it as if it was a spar floating on the barren ocean of life and death into which his withered old body was sinking.

===Chinese===
他似乎紧抓着它不放，仿佛那是漂浮在生死荒漠之海上的一根浮木，而他枯瘦的老躯体正在下沉。

===Original===
Perhaps he really would have clung like that to his son had but his son come to him, and borne a little, and for a little while, with his ways.

===Chinese===
也许他的儿子若肯来找他，稍加忍耐他的脾气，哪怕只是一小会儿，他真的会那样紧紧抓住儿子不放。

===Original===
A sorrowful thing to see—the old man of ninety clinging to the back of his son's empty chair. His great grey tottery hat seemed about to tumble on the floor—his back bowed a little more—and he groaned deeply, three times.

===Chinese===
那景象令人心碎——九十岁的老人紧抓着他儿子空椅子的椅背。他那顶摇摇欲坠的大灰帽似乎随时要掉在地上——他的背又弯下去一些——他深深地呻吟了三声。

===Original===
We can see, being out of the play and spectators merely, that there was a human cry for help in the old man's groan—his heart yearned for his son's strong arm to lean on.

===Chinese===
我们作为置身事外的旁观者看得出来，老人的呻吟中有着人类求救的呼声——他的心渴望着儿子强壮的手臂可以依靠。

===Original===
The crowd of relations were in doubt as to whether they should rejoice, whether the groan was a sign of indignation, of anger too deep ever to be forgotten, or whether they should be alarmed at the possibility of reconciliation.

===Chinese===
那群亲戚举棋不定：到底该不该高兴，这呻吟是愤怒的征兆、是永难消解的深重怨怼，还是该为和解的可能性而惊慌？

===Original===
The Flamma blood was up too much in Amaryllis for her to feel pity for him as she would have done in any other mood; she hated him all the more; he was rich, the five-shilling fare was nothing to him, he could hire a fly from the "Lamb Inn," and drive over and make friends with her father in half an hour. Groaning there—the hideous old monster! and her mother without a decent pair of boots.

===Chinese===
阿玛丽莉斯的弗拉玛血性此刻太强烈了，她无法像在其他情绪下那样怜悯他；她反而更加憎恨他；他那么有钱，五先令的宴席费对他不算什么，他完全可以从"羔羊旅馆"（Lamb Inn）雇辆马车，半小时内就过来和她父亲言归于好。却坐在那里呻吟——这个可怕的老怪物！而她的母亲连一双体面的靴子都没有。

===Original===
In a moment or two Grandfather Iden recovered himself, and continued the distribution, and by-and-by Amaryllis felt him approach her chair. She did not even turn to look at him, so he took her hand, and placed two coins in it, saying in his most gracious way that the sovereign was for her father, and the guinea—the spade-guinea—for herself. She muttered something—she knew not what—she could but just restrain herself from throwing the money on the floor.

===Chinese===
片刻之后，艾登爷爷缓过神来，继续分发礼物。不久，阿玛丽莉斯感到他走近了她的椅子。她甚至没有转头看他，于是他拉起她的手，往里面放了两枚硬币，用最亲切的口吻说，索维林是给她父亲的，而基尼——那枚铲形基尼——是给她的。她喃喃说了些什么——自己也不知道说了什么——只是勉强克制住没把钱扔到地上。

===Original===
It was known in a moment that Amaryllis had the guinea. Conceive the horror, the hatred, the dread of the crowd of sycophants! That the Heiress Apparent should be the favourite!

===Chinese===
消息立刻传开了：阿玛丽莉斯拿到了基尼。想想那群谄媚之徒的惊恐、憎恨和惶惧吧！法定继承人竟然是最受宠的那一个！

===Original===
Yet more. Half-an-hour later, just after they had all got upstairs into the great drawing-room, and some were officiously and reverently admiring the peacock-feather in the screen, and some looking out of the bow window at the fair, there came a message for Amaryllis to put on her hat and go for a walk with her grandfather.

===Chinese===
还有更惊人的。半小时后，当他们全都上楼进了大客厅，有人正殷勤而虔诚地赞叹屏风上的孔雀羽毛，有人在凸窗前眺望集市，这时传来消息说阿玛丽莉斯该戴上帽子，陪她祖父去散步。

===Original===
There was not one among all the crowd in the drawing-room who had ever been invited to accompany Iden Pacha.

===Chinese===
客厅里那么多人，从来没有一个被邀请过陪艾登帕夏（Iden Pacha）散步。

===Original===
Three days ago at home, if anyone had told Amaryllis that she would be singled out in this way, first to receive the Iden medal—the spade-guinea stamp of approval—and then, above all things, to be honoured by walking out with this "almighty" grandfather, how delighted she would have been at the thought of the triumph!

===Chinese===
三天前在家里，若有人告诉阿玛丽莉斯她会被这样特别对待——先获颁艾登勋章，也就是那枚铲形基尼的认可印记，然后最重要的是，被荣幸地邀请与这位"万能"的祖父一同散步，想到这份荣耀她该有多么欣喜若狂！

===Original===
But now it was just the reverse. Triumph over these people—pah! a triumph over rats and flies or some such creatures. She actually felt lowered in her own esteem by being noticed at all among them. Honoured by this old horror—she revolted at it. *He* honour her with his approval—she hated him.

===Chinese===
但现在恰恰相反。对这些人的胜利——呸！不过是战胜老鼠苍蝇之类的东西罢了。被他们注意到反而让她觉得自尊受损。被这个老怪物"荣幸"地看待——她对此深感厌恶。*他*用他的认可来"荣幸"她——她恨他。

===Original===
The other day a travelling piano was wheeled through Coombe and set up a tune in that lonesome spot. Though it was but a mechanical piece of music, with the cogs as it were of the mechanism well marked by the thump, thump, it seemed to cheer the place—till she went out to the gate to look at the Italian woman who danced about while the grinding was done, and saw that she had a sound pair of boots on. That very morning her mother in crossing the road had set the Flamma rheumatism shooting in her bones, for the dampness of the mud came through the crack in her boot.

===Chinese===
前几天，一架旅行钢琴被推过库姆（Coombe），在那荒凉的地方奏起了一曲。虽然不过是机械演奏，齿轮转动时发出的咚咚声清晰可辨，却似乎给那地方添了几分生气——直到她出门去大门边看那个在磨坊边跳舞的意大利女人，发现她脚上穿着一双完好的靴子。就在那天早上，她母亲过马路时，靴子裂缝让湿泥渗了进去，引发了弗拉玛家族的风湿老毛病，痛入骨髓。

===Original===
This miserable old Iden Pacha thought to honour her while he let her mother walk about with her stocking on the wet ground!

===Chinese===
这个可恶的老艾登帕夏竟想用他的"荣幸"来抬举她，却任由她母亲穿着湿透的袜子在泥地上走路！

===Original===
The Flamma blood was up in her veins—what did she care for guineas!

===Chinese===
弗拉玛的血液在她血管里沸腾——她才不在乎什么基尼！

===Original===
As she was putting her hat on in the bedroom before the glass she looked round to see that no one was watching, and then stooped down and put the spade-guinea in the dust of the floor under the dressing-table. She would have none of his hateful money. The sovereign she took care of because it was for her father, and he might buy something useful with it; he wanted a few shillings badly enough.

===Chinese===
她在卧室镜子前戴帽子时，环顾四周确认没人注意，然后弯下腰把铲形基尼塞到梳妆台下的地板灰尘里。她才不要他那可恶的钱。索维林她收好了，因为那是给父亲的，他可以用它买些有用的东西；他正缺几先令呢。

===Original===
So the spade-guinea remained in the dust of the floor for a week or two, till it pleased the housemaid to move the dressing-table to brush away the accumulation, when she found the shining one in the fluff. Being over thirty, she held her tongue, the guinea henceforward travelled down the stream of Time fast enough though silently, but she took the first opportunity of examining the iron box under the Pacha's bed, thinking perhaps there might be a chink in it. And it was curious how for some time afterwards a fit of extraordinary industry prevailed in the house; there was not a table, a chair, or any piece of furniture that was not chivvied about under pretence of polishing. She actually had a day's holiday and a cast-off gown given to her as a reward for her labours.

===Chinese===
铲形基尼就这样在地板灰尘里待了一两个星期，直到女仆心血来潮挪开梳妆台清扫积灰，在毛絮中发现了那枚闪闪发亮的基尼。她年过三十，所以守口如瓶，基尼从此在时间的河流中静静流淌，但一有机会她就去检查帕夏床底下的铁盒子，想看看有没有缝隙可趁。说来也怪，此后很长一段时间里，家里突然掀起了一股异常勤快的风气；没有一张桌子、一把椅子或任何一件家具不是以打磨为借口被挪来挪去的。她甚至得到了一天假期和一件旧裙子作为对她辛劳的奖赏。
