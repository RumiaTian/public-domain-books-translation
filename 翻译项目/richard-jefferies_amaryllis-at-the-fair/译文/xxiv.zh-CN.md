## XXIV / 第二十四章

===Original===
But all these shocks and worries and trampling upon her emotions made the pencil tremble in the artist's hand as she worked in the gaunt garret.

===Chinese===
然而这一切打击、忧虑与情感上的践踏，使得那位在寒碜的阁楼里作画的女画师握笔的手直打颤。

===Original===
One day, as she was returning from Woolhorton, Iden's solicitor, from whom he had borrowed money, overtook her, walked his horse, and began to talk to her in his perky, affected, silly way. Of all the fools in Woolhorton town there was none equal in pure idiotcy to this namby-pamby fellow—it was wonderful how a man of Iden's intelligence could trust his affairs to such a man, the more so as there was at least one good lawyer in the place. This is very characteristic of the farming race; they will work like negroes in the field, and practise the utmost penury to save a little, and be as cautious over a groat as the keenest miser, and then go and trust their most important affairs to some perfect fool of a solicitor. His father, perhaps, or his uncle, or somebody connected with the firm, had a reputation about the era of Waterloo, and upon this tradition they carry their business to a man whom they admit themselves "doan't seem up to much, yon." In the same way, or worse, for there is no tradition even in this case, they will consign a hundred pounds' worth of milk to London on the mere word of a milkman's agent, a man of straw for all they know, and never so much as go up to town to see if there is such a milk business in existence.

===Chinese===
一天，阿玛丽莉斯从伍尔霍顿往回走，艾登的律师——他就是从这位律师那儿借的钱——骑马追上了她，放慢马速，用他那副轻浮、做作、愚蠢的腔调跟她攀谈起来。伍尔霍顿镇上所有蠢人之中，论纯粹的白痴程度，没有一个比得上这个扭捏作态的家伙。令人难以置信的是，以艾登那样的精明人，竟会把事务托付给这样的人，况且本地至少还有一位好律师。这就是庄稼人的典型做派：他们在地里像黑奴一样卖力干活，省吃俭用一文钱掰成两半花，对一枚四便士银币精打细算堪比最抠门的守财奴，然后转头就把最要紧的事务交给一个十足的傻瓜律师去办。或许他父亲，或者他叔叔，或者什么跟那事务所有渊源的人，在滑铁卢战役那会儿有点名气，他们就凭这层老关系把生意送到一个连他们自己都承认"那家伙看起来不太行"的人手上。同样地，或者更糟——因为这回连老关系都没有了——他们仅凭牛奶代理人的一句话，就把价值一百英镑的牛奶托付运往伦敦，那人搞不好就是个空架子，他们连伦敦是否有这家牛奶行都没去核实过。

Now, don't you think you could talk to him, and persuade him to be more practical?"

这个小丑开始跟阿玛丽莉斯谈论起她父亲来。"我说，艾登小姐，您看是不是跟您父亲谈谈这些钱财的事儿；您知道他正往困境里钻呢，真的（这小丑装模作样地打猎）；他会被困住的。您看是不是跟他说说，劝他实际一点？"

===Original===
The chattering of this tomtit upset Amaryllis more than the rudeness of the gruff baker who forced his way in, and would not go. That such a contemptible nincompoop should dare to advise her father to be practical! The cleverest man in the world—advise him to be practical; as if, indeed, he was not practical and hardworking to the very utmost.

===Chinese===
这只山雀的聒噪，比那个粗暴的面包匠硬闯进来赖着不走的无礼行径更让阿玛丽莉斯心烦。这样一个可鄙的蠢货居然敢建议她父亲"实际一点"！世界上最聪明的人——劝他实际一点；好像他还不够实际、不够拼命似的。

===Original===
To her it was a bitter insult. The pencil trembled in her hand.

===Chinese===
对她来说，这是一种刻骨的侮辱。她手中的画笔不住颤抖。

===Original===
But what shook it most of all was anxiety about her mother. Ever since the bailiff's intrusion Mrs. Iden had seemed so unsettled. Sometimes she would come downstairs after the rest had retired, and sit by the dying fire for hours alone, till Iden chanced to wake, and go down for her.

===Chinese===
但最令她手抖的，还是对母亲的忧虑。自从法警闯入以来，艾登太太（Mrs. Iden）一直显得心神不宁。有时别人都歇下了，她却下楼来，在将灭的炉火旁独自坐上好几个钟头，直到艾登碰巧醒了，下来接她。

===Original===
Once she went out of doors very late, leaving the front door wide open, and Amaryllis found her at midnight wandering in an aimless way among the ricks.

===Chinese===
有一次，她深夜出了门，连前门都大敞着没关，阿玛丽莉斯在半夜时分发现她正在草垛之间漫无目的地游荡。

===Original===
At such times she had a glazed look in her eyes, and did not seem to see what she gazed at. At others she would begin to cry without cause, and gave indications of hysteria. The nervous Flamma family were liable to certain affections of that kind, and Amaryllis feared lest her mother's system had been overstrained by these continual worries.

===Chinese===
在那些时候，她的双眼呆滞无神，似乎看不见自己凝视的东西。有时她会毫无来由地哭泣，显出歇斯底里的迹象。弗拉玛（Flamma）家族的人天生神经敏感，容易染上这类病症，阿玛丽莉斯担心母亲的身体在这些接二连三的操劳忧虑中已被折腾得吃不消了。

===Original===
Poor woman! she had, indeed, been worried enough to have shaken the strongest; and, having nothing stolid in her nature, it pressed upon her.

===Chinese===
可怜的女人！她确实被折腾得够呛，足以压垮最强壮的人；而她天性里毫无迟钝之处，这些重压便全落在了她身上。

===Original===
After awhile these attacks seemed to diminish, and Amaryllis hoped that nothing would come of it, but it left her in a state of extreme anxiety lest some fresh trouble should happen to renew the strain.

===Chinese===
过了一阵子，这些发作似乎有所减轻，阿玛丽莉斯心存侥幸，希望不会有什么事了，但这使她陷入了一种极度的焦虑之中，唯恐再出什么新的麻烦，让母亲的神经再次崩溃。

===Original===
When she thought of her mother she could not draw—the sound of her shuffling, nervous footstep on the landing or the path outside under the window stopped her at once. These things disheartened her a thousand times more than the returned sketches the postman was always bringing.

===Chinese===
她一想到母亲就画不下去——母亲在楼梯平台上或者窗外小径上那迟疑的、神经质的脚步声会立刻打断她。这些事让她灰心丧气的程度，比邮差不断送回来的退稿要厉害一千倍。

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Original===
The contemptuous allusion to her sketches as fool stuff, contrasted with the benefit and advantage of earning something—something real and solid—hit the artist very hard. That was the thought that troubled her so much, and paralysed her imagination. They were unsaleable—she saw the worthlessness of them far more than Iden. They were less in value than the paper on which they were traced; fool stuff, fit for the fire only.

===Chinese===
那番轻蔑的言辞，把她的画稿贬为"傻东西"，与"挣点钱——实实在在的东西"形成鲜明对比，重重地击中了这位画师。这正是最令她困扰、最令她想象力瘫痪的那个念头。它们卖不出去——她比艾登更清楚它们一文不值。它们连画在上面的纸都不如；不过是傻东西，只配扔进火里。

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Chinese===
