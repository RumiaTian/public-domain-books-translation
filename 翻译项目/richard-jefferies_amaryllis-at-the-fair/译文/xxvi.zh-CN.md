## XXVI

===Original===
They talked on and on, these three, Iden, Amadis Iden, and Alere Flamma, with Amaryllis listening, from the end of April till near the end of May; till "a month passed away," and still they were talking. For there is nothing so good to the human heart as well agreed conversation, when you know that your companion will answer to your thought as the anvil meets the hammer, ringing sound to merry stroke; better than wine, better than sleep, like love itself—for love is agreement of thought—"God listens to those who pray to him; let us eat and drink, and think of nothing," says the Arabian proverb. So they ate and drank—very moderate the drinking—and thought of nothing, and talked, which should be added to complete felicity. Not, of course, all of them always together, sometimes all four, sometimes Alere, Amadis, and Amaryllis, sometimes only the last two.

The round summerhouse was their Parliament House whenever the east winds sank and the flowers shone forth like sunshine; as the sun shines when the clouds withdraw, so when the harsh east winds cease the May flowers immediately bloom and glow.

It was a large round house, properly builded of brick, as a summerhouse should be—put not thy faith in lath work—and therefore dry and warm; to sit in it was like sitting in a shell, warm and comfortable, with a sea of meadow-grass, smooth and coloured, stretching in front, islanded about with oak, and elm, and ash.

===Chinese===
他们三个——艾登（Iden）、阿玛迪斯·艾登（Amadis Iden）和阿莱尔·弗拉玛（Alere Flamma），以及旁听的阿玛丽莉斯（Amaryllis）——从四月底一直聊到五月底将近；"一个月过去了"，他们还在聊。因为对于人心而言，没有什么比志趣相投的交谈更好的了——你知道你的伙伴会像铁砧迎着铁锤那样回应你的思绪，清脆的回响应和着欢快的敲击；这比酒好，比睡眠好，如同爱情本身——因为爱情正是思想的契合——"真主垂听向他祈祷的人；让我们吃喝，不去想任何事"，阿拉伯谚语如是说。于是他们吃吃喝喝——酒喝得极少——什么也不想，只是聊天，这该加到圆满的幸福中去。当然不是始终四人聚在一起，有时四人全在，有时是阿莱尔、阿玛迪斯和阿玛丽莉斯，有时只有后两人。

每当东风停歇、花朵像阳光般灿烂绽放时，那座圆形凉亭便是他们的议会大厅；正如乌云散去阳光普照，当凛冽的东风止息，五月的花朵便立刻绽放发光。

那是一座宽敞的圆形砖房，凉亭本该如此建造——切莫信靠板条工事——因此干燥温暖；坐在里面如同坐在贝壳中，温暖而舒适，眼前是一片平展展、色彩柔和的牧草之海，四周点缀着橡树、榆树和白蜡树。

===Original===
The finches came to the boughs that hung over the ivy-grown thatch, and sang in the sycamore opposite the door, and in the apple-trees, whose bloom hung down almost to the ground.

These apple-trees, which Iden had planted, flung sackfuls of bloom at his feet. They poured themselves out in abandoned, open-armed, spendthrift, wasteful—perfectly prodigal—quantities of rose-tinted petal; prodigal as a river which flows full to the brim, never questioning but what there will be plenty of water to follow.

Flowers, and trees, and grass, seemed to spring up wherever Iden set down his foot: fruit and flowers fell from the air down upon him. It was his genius to make things grow—like sunshine and shower; a sort of Pan, a half-god of leaves and boughs, and reeds and streams, a sort of Nature in human shape, moving about and sowing Plenty and Beauty.

===Chinese===
雀鸟飞到常春藤覆盖的茅草屋顶上方垂挂的树枝上，对着门口对面的悬铃木歌唱，在苹果树上歌唱——苹果花低垂得几乎触到了地面。

这些苹果树是艾登亲手种下的，它们朝他脚下倾撒出一袋袋的花朵。它们恣意地、张开双臂地、挥霍地、浪费地——彻底地挥霍无度——倾泻出无数玫瑰色的花瓣；挥霍得像一条满溢的河流，从不怀疑后续还会有充沛的水源跟上。

花、树和草，仿佛在艾登落脚之处便蓬勃生长：果实和花朵从空中纷纷落到他身上。他有一种让万物生长的天赋——如同阳光和雨露；像是潘神（Pan），一个掌管枝叶、芦苇和溪流的半神，一个行走于人间的自然之灵，到处播撒丰饶与美丽。

===Original===
One side of the summerhouse was a thick holly-bush, Iden had set it there; he builded the summerhouse and set the ivy; and the pippin at the back, whose bloom was white; the copper-birch near by; the great sycamore alone had been there before him, but he set a seat under it, and got woodbine to flower there; the drooping-ash he planted, and if Amaryllis stood under it when the tree was in full leaf you could not see her, it made so complete an arbour; the Spanish oak in the corner; the box hedge along the ha-ha parapet; the red currants against the red wall; the big peony yonder; the damsons and pear; the yellow honey-bush; all these, and this was but one square, one mosaic of the garden, half of it sward, too, and besides these there was the rhubarb-patch at one corner; fruit, flowers, plants, and herbs, lavender, parsley, which has a very pleasant green, growing in a thick bunch, roses, pale sage—read Boccaccio and the sad story of the leaf of sage—ask Nature if you wish to know how many things more there were.

A place to eat and drink, and think of nothing in, listening to the goldfinches, and watching them carry up the moss, and lichen, and slender fibres for their nest in the fork of the apple; listening to the swallows as they twittered past, or stayed on the sharp, high top of the pear tree; to the vehement starlings, whistling and screeching like Mrs. Iden herself, on the chimneys; chaffinches "chink, chink," thrushes, distant blackbirds, who like oaks; "cuckoo, cuckoo," "crake, crake," buzzing and burring of bees, coo of turtledoves, now and then a neigh, to remind you that there were horses, fullness and richness of musical sound; a world of grass and leaf, humming like a hive with voices.

When the east wind ceases, and the sun shines above, and the flowers beneath, "a summer's day in lusty May," then is the time an Interlude in Heaven.

And all this, summerhouse and all, had dropped out of the pocket of Iden's ragged old coat.

一个吃吃喝喝、什么也不想的地方，听金翅雀歌唱，看它们衔着苔藓、地衣和纤细的纤维到苹果树杈上筑巢；听燕子啁啾着飞过，或停在梨树尖尖的高顶上；听激昂的椋鸟——像艾登夫人本人一样吹着口哨、尖声尖叫——在烟囱上；听苍头燕雀"叮叮"叫，听鸫鸟叫，听远处的乌鸫——它们喜欢橡树；"布谷，布谷"，"嘎嘎，嘎嘎"，蜜蜂嗡嗡嘤嘤，斑鸠咕咕低鸣，偶尔一声马嘶提醒你这里还有马匹——满耳是丰沛的乐音；一片草与叶的世界，嗡嗡作响，如蜂巢般充满声响。

当东风止息、阳光照耀、花朵绽放——"五月蓬勃的一天"，那便是天堂里的间奏。

而这一切，连同凉亭在内，全是从艾登破旧老外套的口袋里掉出来的。

===Original===
There was a magic power of healing in the influences of this place which Iden had created. Both Amadis and Alere Flamma had already changed for the better.

That morning when Amaryllis had found them, just arrived, the one with a portmanteau, and the other with a carpetbag, they were both pale to the last degree of paleness.

Three years had gone by since Amadis had stayed at Coombe Oaks before, when Amaryllis was thirteen and he eighteen; fine romps they had then, a great girl, and a great boy, rowing on the water, walking over the hills, exploring the woods; Amadis shooting and fishing, and Amaryllis going with him, a kind of gamekeeper page in petticoats. They were of the same stock of Idens, yet no relations; he was of the older branch, Amaryllis of the younger.

She had grown into a woman; Amadis Iden into a man.

Sadly, indeed, he had altered. Looking at him, she could scarce believe he was the same; so pale, so thin, so drooping, and fireless—the spark of life sunk into the very ashes. He sat at the dinner-table that morning like a ghost. He was convalescent from low fever: that dread disease which has taken the place of ague in the country. At one time it was ague; in these times it is low fever.

===Chinese===
艾登所创造的这个地方有一种神奇的疗愈之力。阿玛迪斯和阿莱尔·弗拉玛都已经好转了许多。

那天早上阿玛丽莉斯发现他们时，他们刚刚到达，一个提着旅行皮箱，一个拿着毛毯旅行袋，两人的面色都苍白到了极点。

自从阿玛迪斯上次住在库姆橡树庄（Coombe Oaks）以来，已经过去了三年——那时阿玛丽莉斯十三岁，他十八岁；那时他们玩得多开心，一个大姑娘，一个大男孩，在水面上划船，翻山越岭，探索树林；阿玛迪斯打猎钓鱼，阿玛丽莉斯跟着他去，像个穿裙子的小猎场看守。他们同属艾登家族的血脉，却没有亲戚关系；他来自较老的支系，阿玛丽莉斯来自较年轻的支系。

她已经长成了一个女人；阿玛迪斯·艾登已经长成了一个男人。

他的变化确实令人伤感。看着他，她几乎无法相信这是同一个人；如此苍白、如此瘦削、如此萎靡、了无生气——生命的火花已经沉入了灰烬深处。那天早上他坐在餐桌旁像个幽灵。他刚从低烧中恢复过来：那是取代了乡间疟疾的可怕疾病。从前是疟疾；如今是低烧。

===Original===
At Coombe Oaks they had heard of his illness in a far-off way, but had received no distinct particulars, for the news came in a roundabout way by word of mouth, country-folk never write. The distance between the two houses was less than ten miles, and might as well have been five hundred for all the communication.

So that the ghastly paleness of his face came upon her as a spectre in daylight. You could see at a glance what was wrong—the vital energy had been sapped; as a tree fades without a branch broken, or bark scored, fades and withers from the lack of the mysterious force which brings forth fresh leaves, so he drooped in his chair. The body—the tree—was there, but the life was not in it.

Alere Flamma, aged forty-nine, or nearly, was pale from other causes, and it was a different kind of paleness; not bloodlessness, like Amadis, but something lacking in the blood, a vitiated state. Too much Fleet Street, in short; too much of the Oracle—Pantagruel's Oracle of the Bottle.

His hands shook as he held his knife and fork—oddly enough, the hands of great genius often do shake; now and then when he put his glass to his lips, his teeth snapped on it, and chinked.

在库姆橡树庄，他们听说过他生病的事，但只是隐隐约约的，并未收到确切详情，因为消息是辗转口耳相传的——乡下人从不写信。两座宅子相距不到十英里，可就通信而言，五百英里也不过如此。
