## Act III / 第三幕

===Original===
Before dawn. A wild rocky place, **Nanny** and **Biddy Lally** squatting by a fire. Rich stuffs, etc., strewn about. **Paudeen** watching by **Martin**, who is lying as if dead, a sack over him.
Nanny
*To **Paudeen**.* Well, you are great heroes and great warriors and great lads altogether, to have put down the Brownes the way you did, yourselves and the Whiteboys of the quarry. To have ransacked the house and have plundered it! Look at the silks and the satins and the grandeurs I brought away! Look at that now! *Holds up a velvet cloak.* It’s a good little jacket for myself will come out of it. It’s the singers will be stopping their songs and the jobbers turning from their cattle in the fairs to be taking a view of the laces of it and the buttons! It’s my far-off cousins will be drawing from far and near!
Biddy
There was not so much gold in it all as what they were saying there was. Or maybe that fleet of Whiteboys had the place ransacked before we ourselves came in. Bad cess to them that put it in my mind to go gather up the full of my bag of horseshoes out of the forge. Silver they were saying they were, pure white silver; and what are they in the end but only hardened iron! A bad end to them! *Flings away horseshoes.* The time I will go robbing big houses again it will not be in the light of the full moon I will go doing it, that does be causing every common thing to shine out as if for a deceit and a mockery. It’s not shining at all they are at this time, but duck yellow and dark.
===Chinese===
天亮之前。一处荒凉多石的地方，**南妮**和**碧蒂·拉利**蹲在一堆火旁。华贵的衣料等物四下散落。**保丁**守在**马丁**身旁，他直挺挺躺着，像死了一样，身上盖着一条麻袋。
南妮
*（对**保丁**。）*你们可真是大英雄、大勇士、了不起的好汉，就这样把布朗家给踏平了，就凭你们自己，加上采石场那伙白衫党。把宅子翻了个底朝天，还洗劫一空！瞧瞧我带回来的绸子、缎子和这些贵重玩意儿！瞧瞧这个！*（举起一件天鹅绒斗篷。）*用它给自己裁件小外套正合适。到了集市上，唱歌的会停了歌，牛贩子会撇下牲口，都来瞧它的花边和纽扣！我那些八竿子打不着的表亲，也得从四面八方赶来！
碧蒂
里头的金子根本不像他们传的那么多。兴许是那伙白衫党先我们一步，早把宅子翻遍了。也怪那起子人，害我动念去铁匠铺里装了满满一袋马掌。他们说那是银的，纯白的银；到头来算什么，不过是淬硬的铁！不得好下场！*（把马掌扔掉。）*下回我再去做大宅子，决不挑月圆的时候去，月光总把每一件俗物照得发亮，像存心骗人、存心嘲弄人似的。这会儿它们一点也不亮了，灰黄灰黄的，暗得很。


===Original===
Nanny
To leave the big house blazing after us, it was that crowned all! Two houses to be burned to ashes in the one night. It is likely the servant-girls were rising from the feathers and the cocks crowing from the rafters for seven miles around, taking the flames to be the whitening of the dawn.
Biddy
It is the lad is stretched beyond you have to be thankful to for that. There was never seen a leader was his equal for spirit and for daring. Making a great scatter of the guards the way he did. Running up roofs and ladders, the fire in his hand, till you’d think he would be apt to strike his head against the stars.
Nanny
I partly guessed death was near him, and the queer shining look he had in his two eyes, and he throwing sparks east and west through the beams. I wonder now was it some inward wound he got, or did some hardy lad of the Brownes give him a tip on the skull unknownst in the fight? It was I myself found him, and the troop of the Whiteboys gone, and he lying by the side of a wall as weak as if he had knocked a mountain. I failed to waken him trying him with the sharpness of my nails, and his head fell back when I moved it, and I knew him to be spent and gone.
===Chinese===
南妮
临走还让大宅子熊熊烧起来，这才叫画龙点睛！一夜之间两座宅子烧成灰。方圆七英里，使女们多半都从鸭绒被里坐起来了，公鸡在房梁上打鸣了，还当那火光是天在放亮呢。
碧蒂
那得谢就谢躺在那边的小伙子。论胆气、论悍勇，从没见过哪个头领赶得上他。把卫兵冲得四散奔逃，就像他干的那样。手擎着火，爬屋顶、上梯子，叫你以为他脑袋快要撞上星星了。
南妮
我早隐约猜到死神离他近了，瞧他那两只眼里古怪的发亮，人还在梁木间东一把西一把地撒火星。我如今纳闷，他是受了什么内伤，还是布朗家哪个愣小子在混战里悄悄给了他脑壳一下？是我自己找到他的，白衫党那伙人都走光了，他躺在墙根边，虚得像搬倒了一座山。我用指甲尖掐他，也没能弄醒他，一挪他的头就往后垂，我知道他不行了、去了。


===Original===
Biddy
It’s a pity you not to have left him where he was lying and said no word at all to Paudeen or to that son you have, that kept us back from following on, bringing him here to this shelter on sacks and upon poles.
Nanny
What way could I help letting a screech out of myself, and the life but just gone out of him in the darkness, and not a living Christian by his side but myself and the great God?
Biddy
It’s on ourselves the vengeance of the red soldiers will fall, they to find us sitting here the same as hares in a tuft. It would be best for us follow after the rest of the army of the Whiteboys.
Nanny
Whisht! I tell you. The lads are cracked about him. To get but the wind of the word of leaving him, it’s little but they’d knock the head off the two of us. Whisht!
===Chinese===
碧蒂
可惜你没把他撂在原地，也别跟保丁、跟你那儿子提一个字；就是他们，把我们拖住了没法追赶，用麻袋和杠子把他抬到这藏身的地方来。
南妮
我怎么忍得住不叫出来？黑地里他的气刚断，身边除了我和伟大的上帝，连一个活人都没有！
碧蒂
红衣兵的报复要落到我们自己头上了，他们要是发现我们蹲在这儿，跟草丛里的兔子一样。我们最好还是去追白衫党大队的人马。
南妮
嘘！我告诉你。小伙子们为他疯魔着呢。只要叫他们听到半点风声，说要把他撂下，他们不把咱俩的脑袋敲掉才怪。嘘！


===Original===
*Enter **Johnny Bacach** with candles.*
Johnny
*Standing over **Martin**.* Wouldn’t you say now there was some malice or some venom in the air, that is striking down one after another the whole of the heroes of the Gael?
Paudeen
It makes a person be thinking of the four last ends, death and judgment, heaven and hell. Indeed and indeed my heart lies with him. It is well I knew what man he was under his by-name and his disguise.
*Sings.*
Oh, Johnny Gibbons, it’s you were the prop to us.
You to have left us, we are put astray!
===Chinese===
*（**瘸子约翰尼**拿着蜡烛上场。）*
约翰尼
*（俯身站在**马丁**上方。）*你难道不觉得，空气里有股什么怨毒、什么毒气，正把盖尔人（the Gael）的英雄一个接一个撂倒？
保丁
这叫人想起四终：死亡、审判、天堂、地狱。实实在在，我的心向着他们。我早认出他那化名底下、那伪装底下是什么人了。
*（唱。）*
啊，约翰尼·吉本斯，你是我们的顶梁柱。
你这一走，我们全没了方向！


===Original===
Johnny
It is lost we are now and broken to the end of our days. There is no satisfaction at all but to be destroying the English, and where now will we get so good a leader again? Lay him out fair and straight upon a stone, till I will let loose the secret of my heart keening him! *Sets out candles on a rock, propping them up with stones.*
Nanny
Is it mould candles you have brought to set around him, Johnny Bacach? It is great riches you should have in your pocket to be going to those lengths and not to be content with dips.
Johnny
It is lengths I will not be going to the time the life will be gone out of your own body. It is not your corpse I will be wishful to hold in honour the way I hold this corpse in honour.
Nanny
That’s the way always, there will be grief and quietness in the house if it is a young person has died, but funning and springing and tricking one another if it is an old person’s corpse is in it. There is no compassion at all for the old.
===Chinese===
约翰尼
我们算是完了，一辈子抬不起头了。唯有收拾英格兰人还有点滋味，可上哪儿再找这么好的头领？把他端端正正放在石头上，等我把心里藏的话哭唱给他听！*（把蜡烛在一块岩石上摆开，用石头抵稳。）*
南妮
你给他四周摆的是模铸蜡烛吗，瘸子约翰尼？你兜里想必揣了大财，才肯这么破费，不将就着用蘸烛。
约翰尼
等你自己咽了气，我可不会这么破费。我才不稀罕像敬重这具遗体这样敬重你的尸首。
南妮
向来就是这样：家里死的是年轻人，满屋悲伤肃静；躺的是老人的尸首，就只顾打趣、蹦跶、互相捉弄。对老人是一点怜惜也没有。


===Original===
Paudeen
It is he would have got leave for the Gael to be as high as the Gall. Believe me, he was in the prophecies. Let you not be comparing yourself with the like of him.
Nanny
Why wouldn’t I be comparing myself? Look at all that was against me in the world. Would you be matching me against a man of his sort, that had the people shouting him and that had nothing to do but to die and to go to heaven?
Johnny
The day you go to heaven that you may never come back alive out of it! But it is not yourself will ever hear the saints hammering at their musics! It is you will be moving through the ages, chains upon you, and you in the form of a dog or a monster. I tell you that one will go through Purgatory as quick as lightning through a thorn-bush.
===Chinese===
保丁
本该是他，替盖尔人争到跟戈尔人（the Gall）一般高的地位。相信我，预言里就有他。你可别拿自己跟他这样的人比。
南妮
我凭什么不能比？瞧瞧这世上跟我作对的有多少。你倒拿我去跟他那种人较量？他那种人有人人喝彩拥护，除了死和上天堂，一辈子什么也不消做。
约翰尼
愿你上天堂那天，永远活着回不来！可你自己是永远听不见圣徒们敲打他们的音乐的！你要一世一世地流转，锁链缠身，变作狗形或怪物。我告诉你，那一位过炼狱，会像闪电穿过荆棘丛一样快。


===Original===
Nanny
That’s the way, that the way.
*Croons.*
Three that are watching my time to run,
The worm, the devil, and my son,
To see a loop around their neck
It’s that would make my heart to lep!
===Chinese===
南妮
是这样，是这样啊。
*（哼唱。）*
有三个在盯着我时辰到头，
蛆虫、魔鬼，还有我儿子，
要看见绳圈套上他们的脖子，
那才真叫我心头一跳！


===Original===
Johnny
Five white candles. I wouldn’t begrudge them to him indeed. If he had held out and held up it is my belief he would have freed Ireland!
Paudeen
Wait till the full light of the day and you’ll see the burying he’ll have. It is not in this place we will be waking him. I’ll make a call to the two hundred Ribbons he was to lead on to the attack on the barracks at Aughanish. They will bring him marching to his grave upon the hill. He had surely some gift from the other world, I wouldn’t say but he had power from the other side.
Andrew
*Coming in very shaky.* Well, it was a great night he gave to the village, and it is long till it will be forgotten. I tell you the whole of the neighbours are up against him. There is no one at all this morning to set the mills going. There was no bread baked in the nighttime, the horses are not fed in the stalls, the cows are not milked in the sheds. I met no man able to make a curse this night but he put it on my head and on the head of the boy that is lying there before us﻿ ﻿… Is there no sign of life in him at all?
===Chinese===
约翰尼
五支白蜡烛。说实话，我舍得给他。他要是撑住了、挺住了，我相信他本可以解放爱尔兰！
保丁
等天大亮了，瞧瞧他会有一场怎样的葬礼。守灵不会在这儿守。我会去传话给他本要带着去攻打奥加尼什（Aughanish）营房的那两百名缎带会弟兄。他们会送他一路走上山间的坟墓。他身上准有另一个世界赐的禀赋，我不敢说他没有那一边来的力量。
安德鲁
*（脚步虚浮地走进来。）*唉，他给了村子一个了不起的夜晚，很久很久都忘不掉。我告诉你，左邻右舍全都跟他过不去。今天早上没有一个人去开磨坊。夜里没人烤面包，栏里的马没喂，棚里的牛没挤奶。这一夜我碰见的每一个人都在咒骂，咒我，也咒躺在我们面前的这个小伙子……他到底还有没有一点活气？


===Original===
Johnny
What way would there be a sign of life and the life gone out of him this three hours or more?
Andrew
He was lying in his sleep for a while yesterday, and he wakened again after another while.
Nanny
He will not waken, I tell you. I held his hand in my own and it getting cold as if you were pouring on it the coldest cold water, and no running in his blood. He is gone sure enough and the life is gone out of him.
Andrew
Maybe so, maybe so. It seems to me yesterday his cheeks were bloomy all the while, and now he is as pale as wood ashes. Sure we all must come to it at the last. Well, my white-headed darling, it is you were the bush among us all, and you to be cut down in your prime. Gentle and simple, everyone liked you. It is no narrow heart you had, it is you were for spending and not for getting. It is you made a good wake for yourself, scattering your estate in one night only in beer and in wine for the whole province; and that you may be sitting in the middle of Paradise and in the chair of the Graces!
===Chinese===
约翰尼
气都断了三个钟头还多，哪儿来的活气？
安德鲁
昨天他还昏睡了一阵子，过不多久又醒过来了。
南妮
他醒不了了，我告诉你。我握过他的手，凉得像往上头浇了最冷的凉水，血里一点儿流动也没有了。他确实去了，气断了。
安德鲁
也许吧，也许吧。我记得昨天他的脸蛋一直是红润的，如今却白得像柴灰。说到底，我们谁躲不过这一步。唉，我白头的宝贝，你原是我们大家头顶的绿荫，却在正当盛年被砍倒。上上下下，人人喜欢你。你的心胸一点也不狭窄，你天生只管散财、不管敛财。你自己给自己备了一场体面的守灵，一夜之间就把全部家产撒在了啤酒和葡萄酒里，撒给了全省的人；愿你端坐在乐园正中，坐上美惠三神的宝座！


===Original===
Johnny
Amen to that. It’s pity I didn’t think the time I sent for yourself to send the little lad of a messenger looking for a priest to overtake him. It might be in the end the Almighty is the best man for us all!
Andrew
Sure I sent him on myself to bid the priest to come. Living or dead I would wish to do all that is rightful for the last and the best of my own race and generation.
Biddy
*Jumping up.* Is it the priest you are bringing in among us? Where is the sense in that? Aren’t we robbed enough up to this with the expense of the candles and the like?
Johnny
If it is that poor starved priest he called to that came talking in secret signs to the man that is gone, it is likely he will ask nothing for what he has to do. There is many a priest is a Whiteboy in his heart.
===Chinese===
约翰尼
阿门。可惜我派人去叫你的时候，没想到再派个小信使去追一位神父。说到底，说不定全能的天主才是我们大家最好的靠山！
安德鲁
我自己已经打发他去请神父了。无论他是活是死，我总要为我这一族、这一辈人里最后也最好的一位，把该尽的本分尽到。
碧蒂
*（跳起来。）*你要把神父带到我们中间来？这讲的是什么道理？蜡烛这一类开销还没把我们搜刮够吗？
约翰尼
要是他叫的正是那位来找死者暗递暗号的穷酸神父，那他多半不会收钱。心里向着白衫党的神父多着呢。


===Original===
Nanny
I tell you, if you brought him tied in a bag he would not say an Our Father for you, without you having a half-crown at the top of your fingers.
Biddy
There is no priest is any good at all but a spoiled priest. A one that would take a drop of drink, it is he would have courage to face the hosts of trouble. Rout them out he would, the same as a shoal of fish from out the weeds. It’s best not to vex a priest, or to run against them at all.
Nanny
It’s yourself humbled yourself well to one the time you were sick in the gaol and had like to die, and he bade you to give over the throwing of the cups.
Biddy
Ah, plaster of Paris I gave him. I took to it again and I free upon the roads.
===Chinese===
南妮
我告诉你，你就是把他装在袋子里捆来，不先在指尖上捏着半克朗，他也懒得起你一声天主经。
碧蒂
顶用的神父，偏偏得是带点坏名声的神父。肯喝上一小盅的那种，才有胆量去迎那麻烦的大军。能把他们轰走，就像把鱼群从水草里轰散一样。最好别去招惹神父，压根别跟他们作对。
南妮
你自己不就在监牢里病得快死的时候，向一位神父服过软吗，他还叫你再也别掷杯占卜了。
碧蒂
啊，我给他的不过是熟石膏。一出了监、上了大路，我照旧干我的。

Nanny
Much good you are doing with it to yourself or any other one. Aren’t you after telling that corpse no later than yesterday that he was coming within the best day of his life?
Johnny
Whisht, let ye. Here is the priest coming.

===Chinese===
南妮
你这才给自己或给别人添了什么好处。不就是昨天才跟那具尸首说，他正走进一生最风光的日子吗？
约翰尼
嘘，都别说了。神父来了。

Biddy

Johnny

约翰神父，天堂不是我们一直相信的那样。它不是安安静静，不是唱诗奏乐、争斗止息。我见过它，我去过那里。相爱者仍在相爱，只是爱得更加炽烈；骑手仍在驰骋，只是马快如风、腾越山脊；战斗永远、永远在进行。那便是天堂之乐：永不停歇的战斗。我原以为战斗在这里，以为欢乐就在这世上寻得，以为人只要把古传说里那片蛮荒大地带回来就够了——不，不是在这里；我们要熄掉感官，熄掉一切看得见摸得着的东西，就像我熄掉这支蜡烛，才能抵达那欢乐、那战斗。*（他熄掉一支蜡烛。）*我们必须熄掉整个世界，就像我熄掉这支蜡烛。*（又熄掉一支。）*我们必须熄掉星辰的光、太阳的光、月亮的光*（熄掉其余的蜡烛）*，直到我们把一切重新归于无。我先前是在残破的异象里看，如今我全看清了。哪里空无一物，哪里空无一物——那里就有上帝！
警察
现在我们抓他了！
