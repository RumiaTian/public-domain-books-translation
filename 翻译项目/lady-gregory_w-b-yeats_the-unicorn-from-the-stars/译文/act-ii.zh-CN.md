## Act II / 第二幕

===Original===
The same workshop. **Martin** seen arranging mugs and bread, etc., on a table. **Father John** comes in, knocking at open door as he comes; his mind intensely absorbed.
Martin
Come in, come in, I have got the house ready. Here is bread and meat﻿—everybody is welcome. *Hearing no answer, turns round.*
Father John
Martin, I have come back. There is something I want to say to you.
Martin
You are welcome, there are others coming. They are not of your sort, but all are welcome.
Father John
I have remembered suddenly something that I read when I was in the seminary.
===Chinese===
同一间工场。**马丁**正在桌上摆开酒杯、面包等物。**约翰神父**进来，进门时敲了敲敞开的门；他心事重重，全然出神。
马丁
进来，进来，我把屋子收拾好了。这儿有面包有肉——谁来都欢迎。*（听无人应答，转过身来。）*
约翰神父
马丁，我回来了。有件事我想跟你说。
马丁
欢迎您，还有别人要来。他们跟您不是一路人，可人人都欢迎。
约翰神父
我忽然想起当年在修院里读到过的一段东西。


===Original===
Martin
You seem very tired.
Father John
*Sitting down.* I had almost got back to my own place when I thought of it. I have run part of the way. It is very important; it is about the trance that you have been in. When one is inspired from above, either in trance or in contemplation, one remembers afterwards all that one has seen and read. I think there must be something about it in St. Thomas. I know that I have read a long passage about it years ago. But, Martin, there is another kind of inspiration, or rather an obsession or possession. A diabolical power comes into one’s body, or overshadows it. Those whose bodies are taken hold of in this way, jugglers, and witches, and the like, can often tell what is happening in distant places, or what is going to happen, but when they come out of that state they remember nothing. I think you said﻿—
Martin
That I could not remember.
Father John
You remembered something, but not all. Nature is a great sleep; there are dangerous and evil spirits in her dreams, but God is above Nature. She is a darkness, but He makes everything clear; He is light.
===Chinese===
马丁
您看上去很疲倦。
约翰神父
*（坐下。）*我差不多已经回到我自己的地方了，忽然想起了它。我一路还跑着回来了一程。这事非常重要；是关于你陷入的那场恍惚。人若受了上天的感召，无论是恍惚之中还是观照之中，事后都能记起自己看过、读过的一切。我想圣托马斯（St. Thomas）的书里必定有相关的论述。我知道多年前我读到过一大段。可是，马丁，还有另一种感召，说得更准确些，是魔缠或附体。一种邪魔的力量进入人的身体，或者笼罩住它。那些身子被这样占据的人，江湖术士、女巫之流，常能说出远方正在发生的事，或将要发生的事，可他们从那种状态里出来后，什么也不记得。我记得你说过——
马丁
说我什么都不记得。
约翰神父
你记起了一些，但不是全部。自然是一场大睡；她的梦里有危险而邪恶的灵，但上帝在自然之上。她是黑暗，他使一切澄明；他是光。


===Original===
Martin

Father John

Martin

Father John
Very likely, very likely, that is the way it happened. Some plain, unnoticed man has sometimes been sent with a command.

*（声色俱厉。）*它被掐断了；就这么完了。我被派到如今这个孤零零的教区，那地方没有一个人能被我引入歧途。他们把我撂在那里。我们必须忍耐；世界已经毁于水，它还等着被火焚尽。


===Original===
Martin

Andrew
Martin says truth, and he says it well. Planing the side of a cart or a shaft, is that life? It is not. Sitting at a desk writing letters to the man that wants a coach, or to the man that won’t pay for the one he has got, is that life, I ask you? Thomas arguing at you and putting you down﻿—“Andrew, dear Andrew, did you put the tyre on that wheel yet?” Is that life? No, it is not. I ask you all, what do you remember when you are dead? It’s the sweet cup in the corner of the widow’s drinking-house that you remember. Ha, ha, listen to that shouting! That is what the lads in the village will remember to the last day they live.
Martin
Why are they shouting? What have you told them?
Andrew
Never you mind; you left that to me. You bade me to lift their hearts and I did lift them. There is not one among them but will have his head like a blazing tar-barrel before morning. What did your friend the beggar say? The juice of the grey barley, he said.
