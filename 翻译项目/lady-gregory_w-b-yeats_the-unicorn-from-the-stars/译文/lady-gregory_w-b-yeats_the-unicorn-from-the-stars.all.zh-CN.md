## Act I / 第一幕

===Original===
Interior of a coachbuilder’s workshop. Parts of a gilded coach, among them an ornament representing a lion and unicorn. **Thomas** working at a wheel. **Father John** coming from door of inner room.
Father John
I have prayed over Martin. I have prayed a long time, but there is no move in him yet.
Thomas
You are giving yourself too much trouble, Father. It’s as good for you to leave him alone till the doctor’s bottle will come. If there is any cure at all for what is on him, it is likely the doctor will have it.
Father John
I think it is not doctor’s medicine will help him in this case.
Thomas
It will, it will. The doctor has his business learned well. If Andrew had gone to him the time I bade him and had not turned again to bring yourself to the house, it is likely Martin would be walking at this time. I am loth to trouble you, Father, when the business is not of your own sort. Any doctor at all should be able and well able to cure the falling sickness.
===Chinese===
马车匠工场的内部。一辆镀金马车的若干部件摆在场中，其中有一件狮子与独角兽造型的饰件。**托马斯**正在修整一只车轮。**约翰神父**从里间的门口走来。
约翰神父
我为马丁祈祷过了。我祈祷了很久，可他到现在还没有一点动静。
托马斯
神父，您这是自讨苦吃。不如由他去，等医生的药瓶送来再说。他这毛病要真有治，多半也得指望医生。
约翰神父
我看这一回，医生的药帮不了他。
托马斯
能帮，能帮。医生的本事学得扎实。当初要是安德鲁按我吩咐的直接去了医生那里，不半路折回来把您请到家里，这会儿马丁多半已经能下地走路了。神父，这桩事本不在您的行当里，我实在不愿劳烦您。随便哪个医生都该治得了癫痫，而且绰绰有余。




===Original===
Father John
It is not any common sickness that is on him now.
Thomas
I thought at the first it was gone to sleep he was. But when shaking him and roaring at him failed to rouse him, I knew well it was the falling sickness. Believe me, the doctor will reach it with his drugs.
Father John
Nothing but prayer can reach a soul that is so far beyond the world as his soul is at this moment.
Thomas
You are not saying that the life is gone out of him!
===Chinese===
约翰神父
他如今得的，可不是什么寻常的病。
托马斯
起先我以为他只是睡过去了。可摇也摇了，吼也吼了，都叫不醒他，我就认定是癫痫了。相信我，医生的药总能治到点子上。
约翰神父
他的魂灵此刻已飘得那样远，出了这人世，除了祈祷，什么也够不着它。
托马斯
您该不是说他的命已经没了吧！




===Original===
Father John
No, no, his life is in no danger. But where he himself, the spirit, the soul, is gone, I cannot say. It has gone beyond our imaginings. He is fallen into a trance.
Thomas
He used to be queer as a child, going asleep in the fields, and coming back with talk of white horses he saw, and bright people like angels or whatever they were. But I mended that. I taught him to recognise stones beyond angels with a few strokes of a rod. I would never give in to visions or to trances.
Father John
We who hold the faith have no right to speak against trance or vision. Saint Elizabeth had them, Saint Benedict, Saint Anthony, Saint Columcille. Saint Catherine of Siena often lay a long time as if dead.
Thomas
That might be so in the olden time, but those things are gone out of the world now. Those that do their work fair and honest have no occasion to let the mind go rambling. What would send my nephew, Martin Hearne, into a trance, supposing trances to be in it, and he rubbing the gold on the lion and unicorn that he had taken in hand to make a good job of for the top of the coach?
Father John
*Taking up ornament.* It is likely it was that sent him off. The flashing of light upon it would be enough to throw one that had a disposition to it into a trance. There was a very saintly man, though he was not of our church, he wrote a great book called *Mysterium Magnum*, was seven days in a trance. Truth, or whatever truth he found, fell upon him like a bursting shower, and he a poor tradesman at his work. It was a ray of sunlight on a pewter vessel that was the beginning of all. *Goes to the door and looks in.* There is no stir in him yet. It is either the best thing or the worst thing can happen to anyone, that is happening to him now.
===Chinese===
约翰神父
不，不，性命没有危险。可他这个人，他的灵、他的魂，去了哪里，我说不上来。那已经超出了我们所能想象。他陷入了一种恍惚。
托马斯
他从小就古怪，在野地里睡着了，回来就讲看见白马啦，还有浑身发亮的人，像天使还是什么别的。可我把这毛病扳过来了。我教他认清石头比天使要紧，不过用戒尺打了几下。我从来不向幻象和恍惚低头。
约翰神父
我们持守信仰的人，无权开口反对恍惚或异象。圣伊利莎白有过，圣本笃有过，圣安东尼有过，圣科伦西尔（Columcille）也有过。锡耶纳的圣凯瑟琳就常常长久地躺着，像死了一样。
托马斯
古时候兴许是这样，可如今那些东西早已出了这个世界。本本分分干活的人，用不着让心思四处乱跑。就算世上真有恍惚这回事，我侄子马丁·赫恩（Martin Hearne）正给车顶那对狮子和独角兽上金——那是他接下手、要干得漂亮的一件活计——什么会叫他陷入恍惚？
约翰神父
*（拿起那件饰物。）*多半就是它把他送走的。它上面闪动的光，足以让有这种禀性的人陷入恍惚。从前有一位非常圣洁的人，虽不属于我们教会，写过一部大书，叫《玄妙奥秘》（*Mysterium Magnum*），曾一连七天陷入恍惚。真理——或他所悟到的无论什么真理——像一场骤雨劈头落在他身上，而他还只是个正在做工的穷匠人。起头不过是锡镴器皿上的一道日光。*（走到门口向里张望。）*他还没有动静。此刻临到他头上的，要么是人所能遇上的最好的事，要么是最坏的事。




===Original===
Thomas
And what in the living world can happen to a man that is asleep on his bed?
Father John
There are some would answer you that it is to those who are awake that nothing happens, and it is they that know nothing. He is gone where all have gone for supreme truth.
Thomas
*Sitting down again and taking up tools.* Well, maybe so. But work must go on and coachbuilding must go on, and they will not go on the time there is too much attention given to dreams. A dream is a sort of a shadow, no profit in it to anyone at all. A coach, now, is a real thing and a thing that will last for generations and be made use of to the last, and maybe turn to be a hen-roost at its latter end.
Father John
I think Andrew told me it was a dream of Martin’s that led to the making of that coach.
Thomas
Well, I believe he saw gold in some dream, and it led him to want to make some golden thing, and coaches being the handiest, nothing would do him till he put the most of his fortune into the making of this golden coach. It turned out better than I thought, for some of the lawyers came looking at it at Assize time, and through them it was heard of at Dublin Castle﻿ ﻿… and who now has it ordered but the Lord Lieutenant! ***Father John** nods.* Ready it must be and sent off it must be by the end of the month. It is likely King George will be visiting Dublin, and it is he himself will be sitting in it yet.
===Chinese===
托马斯
一个人睡在自己床上，这活生生的世上还能有什么事临到他头上？
约翰神父
有人会这样答你：偏偏是醒着的人什么事也碰不上，偏偏是他们什么都不知道。他去了人人为了至高的真理而去的地方。
托马斯
*（重新坐下，拿起工具。）*好吧，也许是这样。可活计总得做下去，造车的事总得做下去；要是太把梦当回事，它们就做不下去了。梦不过是一种影子，对谁都没有半点好处。马车可就不一样了，那是件实实在在的东西，能传上几代人，一直用到最后，末了说不定还能改成个鸡棚。
约翰神父
我记得安德鲁跟我说过，造这辆马车，起头正是马丁的一个梦。
托马斯
是这样，我想他是在哪个梦里见了金子，就想做一件金色的东西；马车最现成，他不把大半家财投进这辆金马车的打造，就怎么也不肯罢休。结果比我预想的强：巡回法庭开庭的时候，有几位律师来看过，靠他们，这车连都柏林城堡都听说了……如今订购它的不是别人，正是总督大人！*（**约翰神父**点头。）*月底前必须完工，必须发出。乔治国王说不定要驾临都柏林，到时候坐在里头的，没准儿正是他本人。




===Original===
Father John
Martin has been working hard at it, I know.
Thomas
You never saw a man work the way he did, day and night, near ever since the time six months ago he first came home from France.
Father John
I never thought he would be so good at a trade. I thought his mind was only set on books.
Thomas
He should be thankful to myself for that. Any person I will take in hand, I make a clean job of them the same as I would make of any other thing in my yard﻿—coach, half-coach, hackney-coach, ass-car, common-car, post-chaise, calash, chariot on two wheels, on four wheels. Each one has the shape Thomas Hearne put on it, and it in his hands; and what I can do with wood and iron, why would I not be able to do it with flesh and blood, and it in a way my own?
Father John
Indeed, I know you did your best for Martin.
Thomas
Every best. Checked him, taught him the trade, sent him to the monastery in France for to learn the language and to see the wide world; but who should know that if you did not know it, Father John, and I doing it according to your own advice?
===Chinese===
约翰神父
我知道，马丁一直在为这辆车拼命干活。
托马斯
你从没见过像他那样干活的，没日没夜，几乎从半年前他头一回从法国回到家起就是这样。
约翰神父
我从没想到他干手艺会这么在行。我还当他的心思全在书本上。
托马斯
这他该谢我。凡是我经手的人，我都能把他收拾得干净利落，就像收拾我场子里任何别的东西一样——大轿式车、半轿式车、出租马车、驴板车、平板货车、邮驿轻车、折叠篷车、双轮的、四轮的轿式轻马车。每一样都带着托马斯·赫恩（Thomas Hearne）给它定下的形，只要它到了他手里；木头上、铁上我做得到的，血肉之躯上为什么做不到？何况从某种意义上说，它也算是我自己的。
约翰神父
的确，我知道你为马丁尽了力。
托马斯
何止尽力，是尽心尽力到了头。管束他，教他手艺，送他去法国的修道院学语言、见世面——这样的事除了您还有谁该知道呢，约翰神父，何况我全是照您自己的主意办的？




===Original===
Father John
I thought his nature needed spiritual guidance and teaching, the best that could be found.
Thomas
I thought myself it was best for him to be away for a while. There are too many wild lads about this place. He to have stopped here, he might have taken some fancies, and got into some trouble, going against the Government maybe the same as Johnny Gibbons that is at this time an outlaw, having a price upon his head.
Father John
That is so. That imagination of his might have taken fire here at home. It was better putting him with the Brothers, to turn it to imaginings of heaven.
Thomas
Well, I will soon have a good hardy tradesman made of him now that will live quiet and rear a family, and be maybe appointed coachbuilder to the Royal Family at the last.
Father John
*At window.* I see your brother Andrew coming back from the doctor; he is stopping to talk with a troop of beggars that are sitting by the side of the road.
Thomas
There, now, is another that I have shaped. Andrew used to be a bit wild in his talk and in his ways, wanting to go rambling, not content to settle in the place where he was reared. But I kept a guard over him; I watched the time poverty gave him a nip, and then I settled him into the business. He never was so good a worker as Martin, he is too fond of wasting his time talking vanities. But he is middling handy, and he is always steady and civil to customers. I have no complaint worth while to be making this last twenty years against Andrew.
===Chinese===

我当时想，他的天性需要属灵的引导和教诲，而且要最好的。
托马斯
我自己也觉得他最好离开一阵子。这一带野小子太多。他要是留在这儿，说不定就染上些痴念，惹出些乱子，没准儿还跟政府作对，就像约翰尼·吉本斯（Johnny Gibbons）那样，如今成了亡命之徒，脑袋上挂着赏格。
约翰神父
是这样。他那想象力要是留在家里，说不定就烧起来了。把他放到修士们当中去更好，把它转成对天国的想象。
托马斯
好啦，我不久就能把他造就成一个结实过硬的匠人，安安分分过日子，养上一家人，到最后说不定还能受聘当王室的御用马车匠。
约翰神父
*（在窗前。）*我看见你弟弟安德鲁从医生那儿回来了；他停下来，正跟路边坐着的一群乞丐说话。
托马斯
瞧，这又是一个我调教出来的人。安德鲁从前说话做事都有点野，总想去四处游荡，不安心待在生养他的地方。可我看牢了他；我瞅准了贫穷掐他一把的时机，就把他安顿进了这一行。他干活从没有马丁那么出色，太爱把时间浪费在闲扯上。但他手艺还过得去，对主顾向来稳当客气。这二十年来，我对安德鲁没有什么值得一提的怨言。

***Andrew** comes in.*
Andrew
Beggars there outside going the road to the Kinvara fair. They were saying there is news that Johnny Gibbons is coming back from France on the quiet; the king’s soldiers are watching the ports for him.
Thomas
Let you keep now, Andrew, to the business you have in hand. Will the doctor be coming himself or did he send a bottle that will cure Martin?
Andrew
The doctor can’t come, for he’s down with the lumbago in the back. Thomas
He had a right to have written it out himself upon a paper. Where is the use of all that?

我怕是把记号弄挪了……喏，这儿，是他自己念给我听的那段。……「主治贴邻脑部各膜层之疾：头痛、眩晕、抽搐、惊厥、麻痹、梦魇压身、卒中、癫痫。」

Andrew
It is queer Father John is sometimes, and very queer. There are times when you would say that he believes in nothing at all.
Thomas

Thomas
Father John said that it was some sort of a vision or a trance, but I would give no heed to what he would say. It is his trade to see more than other people would see, the same as I myself might be seeing a split in a leather car hood that no other person would find out at all.

他疯也罢不疯也罢，我都不会把那遗体留给法律，任他葬得像条狗，或是被抬走吊在树上。把他抬上麻袋，运到采石场去；就在那山坡上，小伙子们会骑马来，手里擎着白杖，为他举行一场盛大的葬礼。*（**南妮**把那件天鹅绒斗篷盖在他身上。）*
*（众人抬起他，唱着歌把遗体运走：）*
我们的希望、我们的心肝，我们的心随你同死，
你一旦撇下我们，我们便成了走散的马驹！
