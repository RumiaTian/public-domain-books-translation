## VIII / 第八章

===Original===
For over two years now had Black-No-More, Incorporated, been carrying on its self-appointed task of turning Negroes into Caucasians. The job was almost complete, except for the black folk in prisons, orphan asylums, insane asylums, homes for the aged, houses of correction and similar institutions. Those who had always maintained that it was impossible to get Negroes together for anything but a revival, a funeral or a frolic, now had to admit that they had cooperated well in getting white. The poor had been helped by the well-to-do, brothers had helped sisters, children had assisted parents. There had been revived some of the same spirit of adventure prevalent in the days of the Underground Railroad. As a result, even in Mississippi, Negroes were quite rare. In the North the only Negroes to be seen were mulatto babies whose mothers, charmed by the beautiful color of their offspring, had defied convention and not turned them white. As there had never been more than two million Negroes in the North, the whitening process had been viewed indifferently by the masses because those who controlled the channels of opinion felt that the country was getting rid of a very vexatious problem at absolutely no cost; but not so in the South.

===Chinese===
两年多来，「黑色终结」公司（Black-No-More, Inc.）一直干着它自领的差事：把黑人变成白人。这项事业眼看大功告成，只剩监狱、孤儿院、精神病院、养老院、感化院之类机构里的黑人还没有动。从前总有人说，黑人除了布道会、葬礼和游乐，干什么都聚不到一块儿，如今也不得不承认，这回为了变白，他们配合得着实不坏。有钱的帮了穷的，兄姊帮了弟妹，儿女帮了爹娘。当年「地下铁路」（南北战争前协助黑奴北逃的秘密网络）盛行时的那股冒险精神，一时间竟重现于世。结果呢，连密西西比都难得见到几个黑人了。在北方，能见到的黑人只剩混血娃娃，他们的母亲被小家伙漂亮的肤色迷住，硬是不顾流俗，没把他们送去变白。北方从来没有超过两百万黑人，对于这场变白风潮，大众本就反应淡漠，因为把持舆论渠道的人觉得，国家这是分文不花就甩掉了一个老大难问题；南方却全然不是这么回事。

===Original===
When one-third of the population of the erstwhile Confederacy had consisted of the much-maligned Sons of Ham, the blacks had really been of economic, social and psychological value to the section. Not only had they done the dirty work and laid the foundation of its wealth, but they had served as a convenient red herring for the upper classes when the white proletariat grew restive under exploitation. The presence of the Negro as an under class had also made of Dixie a unique part of the United States. There, despite the trend to industrialization, life was a little different, a little pleasanter, a little softer. There was contrast and variety, which was rare in a nation where standardization had progressed to such an extent that a traveler didn’t know what town he was in until someone informed him. The South had always been identified with the Negro, and vice versa, and its most pleasant memories treasured in song and story, were built around this pariah class.

===Chinese===
从前，昔年南部邦联的人口有三分之一是那帮饱受诋毁的「含的子孙」，那时候，黑人在这片地方确有经济、社会和心理上的价值。脏活累活是他们干的，此地财富的根基是他们垫下的；而且每逢白人无产阶级在剥削下骚动不平，他们又是上层阶级顺手拈来的现成幌子，把众人的视线引开。黑人作为一个底层阶级的存在，还使得南方成了合众国里独一无二的一角。在这里，尽管工业化大潮涌动，日子总还有点不一样，有点更舒心，有点更绵软。这里有色调的对照，有花样经，这在一个标准化登峰造极的国度里可是稀罕物——在那样的国度，旅人不问人，根本不知道自己身在何城。南方从来同黑人互为表里，那些珍藏于歌谣与传说中的最美好的回忆，也都是围着这个贱民阶级搭建起来的。

===Original===
The deep concern of the Southern Caucasians with chivalry, the protection of white womanhood, the exaggerated development of race pride and the studied arrogance of even the poorest half-starved white peon, were all due to the presence of the black man. Booted and starved by their industrial and agricultural feudal lords, the white masses derived their only consolation and happiness from the fact that they were the same color as their oppressors and consequently better than the mudsill blacks.

===Chinese===
南方白人对骑士精神那般上心，对保护白人妇女那般紧张，种族自豪感膨胀到那个地步，连最穷最饿的白人雇农也摆出一副煞有介事的傲慢——凡此种种，全都是因为有黑人在。被他们工农两界的封建主老爷又踢又饿的白人大众，唯一的安慰和快乐，就来自于这么一个事实：他们跟骑在自己头上的主子同一种肤色，因此总比垫在泥底的黑人高出一头。

===Original===
The economic loss to the South by the ethnic migration was considerable. Hundreds of wooden railroad coaches, long since condemned as death traps in all other parts of the country, had to be scrapped by the railroads when there were no longer any Negroes to Jim Crow. Thousands of railroad waiting rooms remained unused because, having been set aside for the use of Negroes, they were generally too dingy and unattractive for white folk or were no longer necessary. Thousands of miles of streets located in the former Black Belts, and thus without sewers or pavement, were having to be improved at the insistent behest of the rapidly increased white population, real and imitation. Real estate owners who had never dreamed of making repairs on their tumbledown property when it was occupied by the docile Negroes, were having to tear down, rebuild and alter to suit white tenants. Shacks and drygoods boxes that had once sufficed as schools for Negro children, had now to be condemned and abandoned as unsuitable for occupation by white youth. Whereas thousands of school teachers had received thirty and forty dollars a month because of their Negro ancestry, the various cities and counties of the Southland were now forced to pay the standard salaries prevailing elsewhere.

===Chinese===
这场族群大迁徙给南方造成的经济损失着实可观。几百节木制客车车厢——早在国内其他地方就被判定是死亡陷阱——如今再没有黑人可供他们施行吉姆·克劳（种族隔离），铁路公司只好把她们报废了事。几千间火车站候车室闲置下来：这些屋子本是划给黑人用的，一般又脏又寒碜，白人不屑一顾，要不然就是根本用不着了。位于原黑人区（Black Belt）的成千上万英里街道，向来没有下水道、没有路面铺装，如今白人人口——货真价实的和冒名顶替的都算上——迅速膨胀，逼得当局不得不动手整修。那些收着顺从的黑人房客时做梦也不想着维修破烂房产的业主，如今只好拆的拆、盖的盖、改的改，好合白人房客的心意。从前给黑人孩子当学堂都嫌将就的窝棚和杂货木箱，如今也得判定不堪人居、弃置不用了——总不能叫白人少年委屈在那里面。从前成千上万的小学教师因为有黑人血统，月薪只有三十块、四十块；如今南方各地的城镇县治，都被迫按别处通行的标准发薪水了。

===Original===
Naturally taxes increased. Chambers of Commerce were now unable to send out attractive advertising to Northern business firms offering no or very low taxation as an inducement to them to move South nor were they able to offer as many cheap building sites. Only through the efforts of the Grand Exalted Giraw of the Knights of Nordica were they still able to point to their large reserves of docile, contented, Anglo-Saxon labor, and who knew how long that condition would last?

Consequently, the upper classes faced the future with some misgivings. As if being deprived of the pleasure of black mistresses were not enough, there was a feeling that there would shortly be widespread revolt against the existing medieval industrial conditions and resultant reduction of profits and dividends. The mill barons viewed with distaste the prospect of having to do away with child labor. Rearing back in their padded swivel chairs, they leaned fat jowls on well-manicured hands and mourned the passing of the halcyon days of yore.

===Chinese===
税收自然见涨。商会再也没法子向北方企业寄出诱人的广告，吹嘘此地免税或税负极低，招他们南迁；廉价的建房地皮也拿不出那么多块了。多亏北欧骑士团的大吉罗（Grand Exalted Giraw）卖力，他们还能夸口本地蓄有大批温驯知足的盎格鲁-撒克逊劳工储备——只是这样的好光景还能撑多久，天晓得。

于是，上层阶级面对前程，心里直打鼓。失了黑人情妇的乐趣犹在其次，更让他们不安的是一种预感：用不了多久，就会有人群起反抗现下这种中世纪式的工业条件，利润和股息势必跟着缩水。纱厂大王们一想到早晚非取缔童工不可，就满心的不痛快。他们朝后靠在软垫转椅里，肥腮帮搁在修剪齐整的手上，哀悼昔日太平岁月的一去不返。

===Original===
If the South had lost its Negroes, however, it had certainly not lost its vote, and the political oligarchy that ruled the section was losing its old assurance and complacency. The Republicans had made inroads here and there in the 1934 Congressional elections. The situation politically was changing and if drastic steps were not immediately taken, the Republicans might carry the erstwhile Solid South, thus practically destroying the Democratic Party. Another Presidential election was less than two years off. There would have to be fast work to ward off disaster. Farsighted people, North and South, even foresaw the laboring people soon forsaking both of the old parties and going Socialist. Politicians and business men shuddered at the thought of such a tragedy and saw horrible visions of old-age pensions, eight-hour laws, unemployment insurance, workingmen’s compensation, minimum-wage legislation, abolition of child labor, dissemination of birth-control information, monthly vacations for female workers, two-month vacations for prospective mothers, both with pay, and the probable killing of individual initiative and incentive by taking the ownership of national capital out of the hands of two million people and putting it into the hands of one hundred and twenty million.

===Chinese===
话虽如此，南方虽然失去了黑人，选票可是一票没少，而统治这片地方的政治寡头集团，却渐渐失去了往日的自信与自得。1934年的国会选举里，共和党已经在这儿那儿攻下了几处滩头。政治风向变了，倘若不立刻拿出断然手段，共和党人没准就会席卷昔日铁板一块的南方，民主党实际上也就玩完了。距离下届总统大选，已不足两年。要躲过这场大祸，手脚必须快。南北两地的有识之士甚至预言，劳工大众用不了多久就会抛开两大老党，改投社会主义。政客们和生意人一想这幅惨象就浑身发冷，眼前尽是可怖的幻影：养老金啦，八小时工作制啦，失业保险啦，工伤赔偿啦，最低工资立法啦，取缔童工啦，节育知识的推广啦，女工每月带薪假期、准妈妈两个月带薪假期啦——更可怕的是，国家资本的所有权一旦从两百万人手里挪到一亿两千万人手里，个人的进取心和干劲八成也就给扼杀了。

===Original===
Which explains why Senator Rufus Kretin of Georgia, one of the old Democratic war horses, an incomparable Negro-baiter, a faithful servitor of the dominant economic interests of his state and the lusty father of several black families since whitened, walked into the office of Imperial Grand Wizard Givens one day in March, 1935.

“Well, that’s a pretty big order, Colonel,” said Givens.

===Chinese===
