## War No. 81-Q / 第81-Q号战争

===Original===

Tibet and America, each claiming the Radiant Heat Monopoly, applied for a War Permit for 2127, AD.

The Universal War Board granted it, stating, of course, the conditions. It was, after a few compromises and amendments had been effected, accepted by the belligerent nations.

The conditions were:

万国战争委员会（Universal War Board）颁发了许可证，当然，附上了条件。经过几番妥协与修正之后，交战国接受了这些条件。

条件如下：

===Original===
1. Five 22,000-ton aero-ships, combinations of aero and dirigible, were to be the only combatants.

2. They were to be armed with machine-guns firing nonexplosive bullets only.

3. The War Territory of Kerguelen was to be rented by the two nations, the United American Nations and the Mongolian Alliance, for the two hours of the war, which was to begin on January 5, 2127, at noon.

4. The nation vanquished was to pay all the expenses of the war, excepting the War Territory Rent.

5. No human beings should be on the battlefield. The Mongolian controllers must be in Lhasa; the American ones, in the City of Franklin.

1. 只有五艘 22,000 吨的航空舰（aero-ship）——飞机与飞艇的混合体——可以作为交战方。

2. 舰载武器限为机枪，且只许发射不爆炸的子弹。

3. 凯尔盖朗战区（War Territory of Kerguelen）由两国——美利坚合众国联（United American Nations）与蒙古联盟（Mongolian Alliance）——租用作战的两小时；战争于公元 2127 年 1 月 5 日正午打响。

5. 战场上不得有任何人员。蒙古方的操控员必须身在拉萨（Lhasa），美方操控员则须身在富兰克林城（City of Franklin）。
