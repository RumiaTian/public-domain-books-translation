## List of Illustrations / 插图目录

===Original===
1. A diagram of the temple with the salient points labelled with letters. The general layout shows steps to the south leading into the building, flanked by two towers. Inside are two rows of pews, a pulpit in the middle of the temple past the pews to the north, and ancillary functions beyond that.
