## XXIII Justice / XXIII 正义

===Original===
The day had been an unusually busy one.

Five and thirty prisoners, arraigned before the bar of the Committee of Public Safety, had been tried in the last eight hours—an average of rather more than four to the hour; twelve minutes and a half in which to send a human creature, full of life and health, to solve the great enigma which lies hidden beyond the waters of the Styx.

And Citizen-Deputy Foucquier-Tinville, the Public Prosecutor, had surpassed himself. He seemed indefatigable.

===Chinese===
这一天，忙得异乎寻常。

三十五名囚犯被押到救国委员会（Committee of Public Safety）的审判栏前，在刚刚过去的八个钟头里审讯完毕——平均每点钟要审四个有余；也就是说，用十二分半钟，便把一个满蓄着生命与健康的人打发走，去解开那个隐藏在斯提克斯（Styx）冥河水波之外的大谜。

而公会议员（Citizen-Deputy）富基耶-坦维尔（Foucquier-Tinville）——共和国的公诉人（Public Prosecutor）——更是打破了他自己的纪录。他似乎永不知疲倦。

===Original===
Each of these five and thirty prisoners had been arraigned for treason against the Republic, for conspiracy with her enemies, and all had to have irrefutable proofs of their guilt brought before the Committee of Public Safety. Sometimes a few letters, written to friends abroad, and seized at the frontier; a word of condemnation of the measures of the extremists; and expression of horror at the massacres on the Place de la Révolution, where the guillotine creaked incessantly—these were irrefutable proofs; or else perhaps a couple of pistols, or an old family sword seized in the house of a peaceful citizen, would be brought against a prisoner, as an irrefutable proof of his warlike dispositions against the Republic.

Oh! it was not difficult!

Out of five and thirty indictments, Foucquier-Tinville had obtained thirty convictions.

No wonder his friends declared that he had surpassed himself. It had indeed been a glorious day, and the glow of satisfaction as much as the heat, caused the Public Prosecutor to mop his high, bony cranium before he had adjourned for the much-needed respite for refreshment.

===Chinese===
这三十五名囚犯，个个都以背叛共和国、阴谋勾结她的敌人之罪受到提审，而人人非得有不容辩驳的罪证呈到救国委员会面前不可。有时是几封写给国外朋友的信，在边境上被截了下来的；一句谴责极端派措施的话；一声对革命广场（Place de la Révolution）上大屠杀的惊呼——断头台在那广场上吱嘎作响，从无停歇——这些便是不容辩驳的罪证；再不然，或许是一对手枪，或是从一户安分守己的公民家里搜出的一柄祖传旧剑，被拿来指证囚犯，作为他心怀武力反抗共和国之图谋的不容辩驳的罪证。

哦！这有什么难的！

三十五份起诉状里，富基耶-坦维尔拿到了三十份定罪。

难怪他的朋友们宣称，他打破了自己的纪录。这实在是光辉的一天；那心满意足的灼热，同暑气一道，逼得这位公诉人在退庭去享受那急需的小憩与茶点之前，先揩了揩他那高耸的、瘦骨嶙峋的脑壳。

===Original===
The day's work was not yet done.

The "politicals" had been disposed of, and there had been such an accumulation of them recently that it was difficult to keep pace with the arrests.

And in the meanwhile the criminal record of the great city had not diminished. Because men butchered one another in the name of Equality, there were none the fewer among the Fraternity of thieves and petty pilferers, of ordinary cutthroats and public wantons.

And these too had to be dealt with by law. The guillotine was impartial, and fell with equal velocity on the neck of the proud duke and the gutter-born fille de joie, on a descendant of the Bourbons and the wastrel born in a brothel.

===Chinese===
这一天的公务，还没有办完。

那批「政治犯」已经处置完毕；近来这类人积压得如此之多，审讯的脚步简直追不上逮捕的速度。

与此同时，这座大城的罪案簿也并未变薄。人们纵然以平等之名自相屠戮，窃贼与鼠窃狗偷之辈的行帮，寻常的凶手与公然卖笑的娼妇，人数却并不因此减少分毫。

而这些人，也同样得由法律来处置。断头台是不偏不倚的：它以同样的速度落下，砍在骄傲的公爵的颈上，也砍在阴沟里出身的*fille de joie*（烟花女子）的颈上；砍在波旁王室后裔的颈上，也砍在妓院里生下的浪荡儿的颈上。

===Original===
The ministerial decrees favoured the proletariat. A crime against the Republic was indefensible, but one against the individual was dealt with, with all the paraphernalia of an elaborate administration of justice. There were citizen judges and citizen advocates, and the rabble, who crowded in to listen to the trials, acted as honorary jury.

It was all thoroughly well done. The citizen criminals were given every chance.

The afternoon of this hot August day, one of the last of glorious Fructidor, had begun to wane, and the shades of evening to slowly creep into the long, bare room where this travesty of justice was being administered.

===Chinese===
各部的法令偏袒无产者。反对共和国的罪行，无可辩护；而反对个人的罪行，却要摆出精细司法的全部排场来审理。有公民法官，有公民辩护人，还有那蜂拥而来旁听审讯的暴民，权充名誉陪审团。

一切都办得周详到家。公民罪犯们得到了种种机会。

这炎热的八月天——光辉的果月（Fructidor）行将告尽的几日之一——午后已渐渐消残，暮色开始缓缓爬进那间狭长而空荡的屋子；就在那里，这场正义的滑稽摹演，正由法律一板一眼地主持着。

===Original===
The Citizen-President sat at the extreme end of the room, on a rough wooden bench, with a desk in front of him littered with papers.

Just above him, on the bare, whitewashed wall, the words: "*La République: une et indivisible*," and below them the device: "*Liberté, Egalité, Fraternité!*"

To the right and left of the Citizen-President, four clerks were busy making entries in that ponderous ledger, that amazing record of the foulest crimes the world has ever known, the *Bulletin du Tribunal Révolutionnaire*.

At present no one is speaking, and the grating of the clerks' quill pens against the paper is the only sound which disturbs the silence of the hall.

===Chinese===
公民主席（Citizen-President）坐在屋子最里端的一条粗木长凳上，面前一张桌子，摊满乱堆着的文件。

正在他头顶上方，光秃秃的白灰墙上写着一行字：「*La République: une et indivisible*（共和国：统一而不可分割）」；其下是那条信条：「*Liberté, Egalité, Fraternité!*（自由、平等、博爱！）」

在公民主席的左右两侧，四名书记官正忙着往那部沉甸甸的册簿里做记录——那是一部惊人的档案，记着世间所知最污浊的罪行：《革命法庭公报》（*Bulletin du Tribunal Révolutionnaire*）。

此刻没有人说话；书记官们的鹅毛笔刮擦纸面的沙沙声，是搅扰大厅岑寂的唯一声响。

===Original===
In front of the President, on a bench lower than his, sits Citizen Foucquier-Tinville, rested and refreshed, ready to take up his occupation, for as many hours as his country demands it of him.

On every desk a tallow candle, smoking and spluttering, throws a weird light, and more weird shadows, on the faces of clerks and President, on blank walls and ominous devices.

In the centre of the room a platform surrounded by an iron railing is ready for the accused. Just in front of it, from the tall, raftered ceiling above, there hangs a small brass lamp, with a green abat-jour.

===Chinese===
主席前下方，一条比他那条更低些的凳子上，坐着富基耶-坦维尔公民。他歇过了，养足了神，随时准备重操旧业——只要国家向他要，多少个钟头他都奉陪。

每张桌上点着一支牛油烛，冒着烟，毕毕剥剥地爆着灯花，把一层怪诞的光，和更加怪诞的影子，投在书记官们和主席的脸上，投在空白的墙上和那些不祥的信条上。

屋子中央，一座四周围着铁栏杆的高台，已经备好，专候被告。高台正前方，从头顶上那高高的、椽子裸露的天花板下，悬着一盏小小的黄铜灯，配着绿色的*abat-jour*（灯罩）。

===Original===
Each side of the long, whitewashed walls there are three rows of benches, beautiful old carved oak pews, snatched from Notre Dame and from the Churches of St. Eustache and St. Germain l'Auxerrois. Instead of the pious worshippers of medieval times, they now accommodate the lookers-on of the grim spectacle of unfortunates, in their brief halt before the scaffold.

The front row of these benches is reserved for those citizen-deputies who desire to be present at the debates of the Tribunal Révolutionnaire. It is their privilege, almost their duty, as representatives of the people, to see that the sittings are properly conducted.

These benches are already well filled. At one end, on the left, Citizen Merlin, Minister of Justice, sits; next to him Citizen-Minister Lebrun; also Citizen Robespierre, still in the height of his ascendancy, and watching the proceedings with those pale, watery eyes of his and that curious, disdainful smile, which have earned for him the nickname of "the sea-green incorruptible."

===Chinese===
白灰长墙的每一侧，都排着三行长凳，那是一件件精美的雕花老橡木座椅，是从巴黎圣母院（Notre Dame）、圣厄斯塔什教堂（St. Eustache）和圣日耳曼奥塞尔教堂（St. Germain l'Auxerrois）里强行搬来的。中世纪那些虔诚的礼拜者已经不在了；如今坐在上面的，是一出阴惨戏码的看客——戏里的不幸者们，正在奔赴断头台之前，作最后一段短暂的停留。

这些长凳的前排，专为愿意列席革命法庭（Tribunal Révolutionnaire）辩论的公会议员们保留。作为人民的代表，出席旁听是他们的特权，也几乎是他们的职责——好监督开庭是否进行得规规矩矩。

这些长凳已经坐得满满当当。左端坐着司法部长梅尔兰（Merlin）公民；他旁边是部长勒布伦（Lebrun）公民；再是罗伯斯庇尔（Robespierre）公民，此刻正权势鼎盛，用他那一双苍白而水汪汪的眼睛，和那一丝古怪的、倨傲的微笑，注视着庭审的进行——正是这副神情，为他赢得了「海绿色的不可腐蚀者」的绰号。

===Original===
Other well-known faces are there also, dimly outlined in the fast-gathering gloom. But everyone notes Citizen-Deputy Déroulède, the idol of the people, as he sits on the extreme end of a bench on the right, with arms tightly folded across his chest, the light from the hanging lamp falling straight on his dark head and proud, straight brows, with the large, restless, eager eyes.

Anon the Citizen-President rings a hand-bell, and there is a discordant noise of hoarse laughter and loud curses, some pushing, jolting, and swearing, as the general public is admitted into the hall.

Heaven save us! What a rabble! Has humanity really such a scum?

===Chinese===
还有几张知名的面孔，也在迅速聚拢的昏暗里影影绰绰地显出来。但人人都注意到了公会议员德鲁莱德（Déroulède），人民的偶像：他坐在右边一条长凳的最末端，双臂紧紧交叠在胸前；吊灯的光直直照在他那深色的头发、他那两道骄傲而挺直的浓眉上，照着那双不安分的、灼灼渴望的大眼睛。

不多时，公民主席摇响了一只手铃；普通公众被放进大厅，顿时响起一片刺耳的喧哗——嘶哑的哄笑，响亮的咒骂，推推搡搡、跌跌撞撞，还夹着满口的亵渎。

上天救救我们！好一群乌合之众！人类当真出产这样一摊浮渣么？

===Original===
Women with a single ragged kirtle and shift, through the interstices of which the naked, grime-covered flesh shows shamelessly: with bare legs, and feet thrust into heavy sabots, hair dishevelled, and evil, spirit-sodden faces: women without a semblance of womanhood, with shrivelled, barren breasts, and dry, parched lips, that have never known how to kiss. Women without emotion save that of hate, without desire, save for the satisfaction of hunger and thirst, and lust for revenge against their sisters less wretched, less unsexed than themselves. They crowd in, jostling one another, swarming into the front rows of the benches, where they can get a better view of the miserable victims about to be pilloried before them.

And the men without a semblance of manhood. Bent under the heavy care of their own degradation, dead to pity, to love, to chivalry; dead to all save an inordinate longing for the sight of blood.

And God help them all! for there were the children too. Children—save the mark!—with pallid, precocious little faces, pinched with the ravages of starvation, gazing with dim, filmy eyes on this world of rapacity and hideousness. Children who have seen death!

===Chinese===
女人们，身上只挂着一件破烂的外裙和贴身衬衣，布片的缝隙间，污垢蒙身的赤裸皮肉不知羞耻地袒露着：光着两条腿，脚塞在笨重的木鞋里，头发蓬乱，一张张凶恶的、被烧酒泡烂了的脸：这些女人没有一点女人的样子，皱缩的、不生育的乳房，又干又焦的嘴唇，从来不懂得亲吻为何物。她们没有感情，除了恨；没有欲望，除了果腹解渴，和对复仇的渴望——要向那些姐妹们复仇，那些比自己少一分悲惨、还不曾像自己这样被夺尽女性天性的姐妹。她们蜂拥而入，互相推搡着，涌上长凳的前排，好把那班即将被拉到他们面前示众的可怜牺牲者，看得更加真切。

而那些男人，也没有一丝男人的模样。他们被自身堕落这副沉重的心事压弯了腰，对怜悯、对爱、对侠义之道，一概死了心；对一切都死了心，只剩下对亲眼目睹鲜血的一种贪得无厌的渴求。

上帝救救他们所有的人吧！因为还有那些孩子。孩子——恕我出此一言！——一张张苍白早熟的小脸，被饥饿的蹂躏碾得干瘪凹陷，用昏暗浑浊的眼睛，凝望着这个贪暴而狰狞的世界。这些孩子，是见过死亡的！

===Original===
Oh, the horror of it! Not beautiful, peaceful death, a slumber or a dream, a loved parent or fond sister or brother lying all in white amidst a wealth of flowers, but death in its most awesome aspect, violent, lurid, horrible.

And now they stare around them with eager, greedy eyes, awaiting the amusement of the spectacle; gazing at the President, with his tall Phrygian cap; at the clerks wielding their indefatigable quill pens, writing, writing, writing; at the flickering lights, throwing clouds of sooty smoke, up to the dark ceiling above.

Then suddenly the eyes of one little mite—a poor, tiny midget not yet in her teens—alight on Paul Déroulède's face, on the opposite side of the rooms.

===Chinese===
哦，这有多骇人！不是那种美丽的、安详的死亡——一场酣睡，或一个梦，亲爱的父母、慈爱的姐妹或兄弟，一身皆白，躺在繁花锦簇之中——而是死亡最狰狞的面目：暴烈的、血腥的、恐怖的。

而此刻，他们用急切而贪婪的眼睛四下打量，等着这出戏文给他们解闷；呆望着那位头戴高高弗里吉亚帽的主席；呆望着那班挥舞着不知疲倦的鹅毛笔的书记官，写呀，写呀，写个不停；呆望着那摇曳的烛光，把一团一团乌黑的油烟，送向上方昏暗的天花板。

猛可里，一个小不点儿的目光——一个可怜巴巴的、瘦小得还不到十三岁的小人儿——落到了屋子另一头保罗·德鲁莱德（Paul Déroulède）的脸上。

===Original===
"*Tiens!* Papa Déroulède!" she says, pointing an attenuated little finger across at him, and turning eagerly to those around her, her eyes dilating in wishful recollection of a happy afternoon spent in Papa Déroulède's house, with fine white bread to eat in plenty, and great jars of foaming milk.

He rouses himself from his apathy, and his great earnest eyes lose their look of agonised misery, as he responds to the greeting of the little one.

For one moment—oh! a mere fraction of a second—the squalid faces, the miserable, starved expressions of the crowd, soften at sight of him. There is a faint murmur among the women, which perhaps God's recording angel registered as a blessing. Who knows?

===Chinese===
「*Tiens*（瞧）！德鲁莱德爸爸！」她伸出一根瘦棱棱的小指头，朝他那边指着，又急切地转向身边的人；她的眼睛睁得老大，一心神往地回想起在德鲁莱德爸爸家里度过的那个幸福的下午——有上好的白面包，管够地吃，还有一大罐一大罐冒着泡沫的牛奶。

他从麻木中惊醒过来，回应那小东西的招呼；他那一双恳切的大眼睛，一时褪尽了痛楚悲苦的神色。

有一瞬间——哦！不过一秒的零头——人群里那些邋遢污秽的脸，那些悲惨的、饿瘪了的神情，一见着他便柔和下来。女人们中间起了一阵低低的絮语，上帝那司掌记录的天使，或许会把它记作一声祝福。谁知道呢？

===Original===
Foucquier-Tinville suppresses a sneer, and the Citizen-President impatiently rings his hand-bell again.

"Bring forth the accused!" he commands in stentorian tones.

There is a movement of satisfaction among the crowd, and the angel of God is forced to hide his face again.

===Chinese===
富基耶-坦维尔压下一声冷笑，公民主席不耐烦地又把他的手铃摇响。

「把被告带上来！」他用洪钟般的嗓音喝令。

人群里起了一阵快意的骚动，上帝的天使，只得再一次掩起他的脸。
