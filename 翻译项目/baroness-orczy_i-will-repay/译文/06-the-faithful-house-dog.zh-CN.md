## IV The Faithful House-Dog / IV 忠心的看家狗

===Original===
After supper they talked of Charlotte Corday.
Juliette clung to the vision of that heroine, and liked to talk of her. She appeared as a justification of her own actions, which somehow seemed to require justification.
She loved to hear Paul Déroulède talk; liked to provoke his enthusiasm and to see his stern, dark face light up with the inward fire of the enthusiast.

===Chinese===
晚饭过后，他们谈起了夏洛特·科黛（Charlotte Corday）。
朱丽叶特心里紧紧依着那位女杰的影子，喜欢谈论她。在她眼前，那个影子仿佛成了她自己行为的辩护——而她的行为，不知怎的，似乎总需要一点辩护。
她爱听保罗·德鲁莱德说话；爱去挑动他的热情，爱看他那严峻黝黑的脸膛，被狂热者胸中的火焰照亮。

===Original===
She had openly avowed herself as the daughter of the Duc de Marny. When she actually named her father, and her brother killed in duel, she saw Déroulède looking long and searchingly at her. Evidently he wondered if she knew everything: but she returned his gaze fearlessly and frankly, and he apparently was satisfied.
Madame Déroulède seemed to know nothing of the circumstances of that duel. Déroulède tried to draw Juliette out, to make her speak of her brother. She replied to his questions quite openly, but there was nothing in what she said, suggestive of the fact that she knew who killed her brother.
She wanted him to know who she was. If he feared an enemy in her, there was yet time enough for him to close his doors against her.
But less than a minute later, he had renewed his warmest offers of hospitality.
"Until we can arrange for your journey to England," he added with a short sigh, as if reluctant to part from her.

===Chinese===
她已公然表明自己是马尔尼公爵（Duc de Marny）的女儿。当她真正说出父亲的名字、说出那个死于决斗的兄长时，她看见德鲁莱德久久地、探询地凝视着她。显然他是在纳闷，她是否知道全部底细；可她坦然无惧、光明磊落地回望着他，他似乎也就放心了。
至于那场决斗的种种情形，德鲁莱德夫人似乎一无所知。德鲁莱德试探着引朱丽叶特开口，想让她谈谈她的兄长。她十分坦率地回答他的问话，可她话语里没有一丝痕迹，暗示她知道是谁杀死了自己的哥哥。
她要他知道她是什么人。倘若他惧怕她这个仇敌，还来得及对她闭门不纳。
然而不到一分钟，他又重新发出了最热忱的款待之请。
「等我们把您去英格兰的旅程安排好再说，」他补了一句，短短地叹了口气，仿佛舍不得同她分离。

===Original===
To Juliette his attitude seemed one of complete indifference for the wrong he had done to her and to her father: feeling that she was an avenging spirit, with flaming sword in hand, pursuing her brother's murderer like a relentless Nemesis, she would have preferred to see him cowed before her, even afraid of her, though she was only a young and delicate girl.
She did not understand that in the simplicity of his heart, he only wished to make amends. The quarrel with the young Vicomte de Marny had been forced upon him, the fight had been honourable and fair, and on his side fought with every desire to spare the young man. He had merely been the instrument of Fate, but he felt happy that Fate once more used him as her tool, this time to save the sister.
Whilst Déroulède and Juliette talked together Anne Mie cleared the supper-table, then came and sat on a low stool at madame's feet. She took no part in the conversation, but every now and then Juliette felt the girl's melancholy eyes fixed almost reproachfully upon her.

===Chinese===
在朱丽叶特眼里，他的这副态度，等于对他加诸她和她父亲的伤害全然漠然：她自觉是一个复仇的精灵，手执出鞘的利剑，像无情的天罚女神（Nemesis）一般，追踪着杀死她兄长的凶手；她倒宁愿看见他在自己面前畏缩，甚至怕她——尽管她不过是个娇弱年轻的姑娘。
她不明白，他只是怀着一颗质朴的心，想要弥补过失。同年轻的马尔尼子爵的那场口角，是对方强加于他的；那场决斗光明正大，他这一边出手时，处处只想手下留情，饶恕那年轻人。他不过是命运手中的一件工具罢了；可他心里欢喜，因为命运又一次拿他当了器皿——这一次，是去搭救那位妹妹。
德鲁莱德同朱丽叶特交谈的时候，安娜·米收拾了晚餐桌，随后走过来，坐在夫人脚边的一张矮凳上。她不参与谈话，可是朱丽叶特不时觉得，这姑娘那双忧郁的眼睛正盯着自己，目光里几乎带着责备。

===Original===
When Juliette had retired with Pétronelle, Déroulède took Anne Mie's hand in his.
"You will be kind to my guest, Anne Mie, won't you? She seems very lonely, and has gone through a great deal."
"Not more than I have," murmured the young girl involuntarily.
"You are not happy, Anne Mie? I thought—"
"Is a wretched, deformed creature ever happy?" she said with sudden vehemence, as tears of mortification rushed to her eyes, in spite of herself.
"I did not think that you were wretched," he replied with some sadness, "and neither in my eyes, nor in my mother's, are you in any way deformed."

===Chinese===
朱丽叶特随佩特罗内勒退下去以后，德鲁莱德把安娜·米的手握在自己的手里。
「你会好好待我的客人吧，安娜·米？她显得非常孤单，又受过许多苦。」
「不会比我受的苦更多，」年轻姑娘不由自主地喃喃道。
「你不快活吗，安娜·米？我还以为——」
「一个可怜巴巴的残废，还会有快活吗？」她突然激烈地说，羞愤的眼泪不由自主地涌上了眼睛。
「我并没有觉得你可怜，」他有些忧伤地答道，「无论在我眼里，还是在我母亲眼里，你一点也不残废。」

===Original===
Her mood changed at once. She clung to him, pressing his hand between her own.
"Forgive me! I—I don't know what's the matter with me tonight," she said with a nervous little laugh. "Let me see, you asked me to be kind to Mademoiselle Marny, did you not?"
He nodded with a smile.
"Of course I'll be kind to her. Isn't everyone kind to one who is young and beautiful, and has great, appealing eyes, and soft, curly hair? Ah me! how easy is the path in life for some people! What do you want me to do, Paul? Wait on her? Be her little maid? Soothe her nerves or what? I'll do it all, though in her eyes I shall remain both wretched and deformed, a creature to pity, the harmless, necessary house-dog—"

===Chinese===
她的心绪立时转了过来。她偎近他，把他的手紧紧夹在自己两只手中间。
「原谅我！我——我不知道今晚我是怎么了，」她神经质地轻轻一笑。「让我想想，你是要我好好待马尔尼小姐，是不是？」
他含笑点了点头。
「我当然会好好待她。一个人又年轻又美丽，生着一双大大的、惹人怜爱的眼睛，一头柔软的鬈发——谁对她不好呢？唉！有些人的人生道路，走起来多容易啊！你要我做什么呢，保罗？伺候她？当她的贴身小丫头？替她安神，还是怎么的？我都做，尽管在她眼里，我终究还是又可怜又残废，一个只配让人怜悯的东西，一条无害而少不了的看家狗——」

===Original===
She paused a moment: said "Good night" to him, and turned to go, candle in hand, looking pathetic and fragile, with that ugly contour of shoulder, which Déroulède assured her he could not see.
The candle flickered in the draught, illumining the thin, pinched face, the large melancholy eyes of the faithful house-dog.
"Who can watch and bite!" she said half-audibly as she slipped out of the room. "For I do not trust you, my fine madam, and there was something about that comedy this afternoon, which somehow, I don't quite understand."

===Chinese===
她停了片刻：向他道了声「晚安」，便转身要走，一手执烛，样子凄楚而纤弱，肩头带着那副丑陋的轮廓——尽管德鲁莱德一再向她保证，他根本看不见。
烛光在穿堂风里摇曳，照亮了这条忠心的看家狗那瘦削清苦的脸，和她那双忧郁的大眼睛。
「既能守夜，又能咬人！」她溜出房间时，半是自语地低声说。「因为我信不过你，我的漂亮夫人；今天下午那出喜剧里，有点什么名堂，不知怎的，我就是看不透。」
