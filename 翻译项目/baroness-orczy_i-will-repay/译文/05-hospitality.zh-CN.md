## III Hospitality / III 殷勤款待

===Original===
"Is there anything more I can do for you now, mademoiselle?"
The gentle, timid voice roused Juliette from the contemplation of the past.
She smiled at Anne Mie, and held her hand out towards her.
"You have all been so kind," she said, "I want to get up now and thank you all."
"Don't move unless you feel quite well."
"I am quite well now. Those horrid people frightened me so, that is why I fainted."
"They would have half-killed you, if—"

===Chinese===
「小姐，现在还有什么需要我为您做的吗？」
那温柔而怯生生的声音，把朱丽叶特从对往事的沉思中唤醒。
她朝安娜·米（Anne Mie）笑了笑，向她伸出手去。
「你们大家都待我这么好，」她说，「我现在想起来了，要谢谢你们大家。」
「要是觉得不舒服，就别起来。」
「我现在好得很。那些可怕的人把我吓成那样，所以我才晕了过去。」
「要不是——他们差点儿就把您打个半死了。」

===Original===
"Will you tell me where I am?" asked Juliette.
"In the house of M. Paul Déroulède—I should have said of Citizen-Deputy Déroulède. He rescued you from the mob, and pacified them. He has such a beautiful voice that he can make anyone listen to him, and—"
"And you are fond of him, mademoiselle?" added Juliette, suddenly feeling a mist of tears rising to her eyes.

===Chinese===
「能告诉我这是哪儿吗？」朱丽叶特问。
「在保罗·德鲁莱德先生的府上——我该说，德鲁莱德公会议员的府上。是他把您从暴众手里救出来的，还把那些人安抚了下去。他的嗓音那么好听，谁都会听他的，而且——」
「这么说，您很爱他，小姐？」朱丽叶特接了一句，忽然觉得一片泪雾涌上了眼睛。

===Original===
"Of course I am fond of him," rejoined the other girl simply, whilst a look of the most tenderhearted devotion seemed to beautify her pale face. "He and Madame Déroulède have brought me up; I never knew my parents. They have cared for me, and he has taught me all I know."
"What do they call you, mademoiselle?"
"My name is Anne Mie."
"And mine, Juliette—Juliette Marny," she added after a slight hesitation. "I have no parents either. My old nurse, Pétronelle, has brought me up, and—But tell me more about M. Déroulède—I owe him so much, I'd like to know him better."

===Chinese===
「我当然爱他，」另一位姑娘朴实地答道，一张苍白的脸上，泛起最柔情脉脉的挚爱神色，反而显得美了。「是他和德鲁莱德夫人把我养大的；我从没见过我的父母。是他们照管了我，我所知道的一切，都是他教我的。」
「小姐，他们叫您什么？」
「我叫安娜·米。」
「我叫朱丽叶特——朱丽叶特·马尔尼（Juliette Marny），」她略一迟疑，又补了一句。「我也没爹没娘。是佩特罗内勒，我的老奶妈，把我带大的，还有——可是再多跟我讲讲德鲁莱德先生吧——我欠他太多了，我想更了解他一些。」

===Original===
"Will you not let me arrange your hair?" said Anne Mie as if purposely evading a direct reply. "M. Déroulède is in the salon with madame. You can see him then."
Juliette asked no more questions, but allowed Anne Mie to tidy her hair for her, to lend her a fresh kerchief and generally to efface all traces of her terrible adventure. She felt puzzled and tearful. Anne Mie's gentleness seemed somehow to jar on her spirits. She could not understand the girl's position in the Déroulède household. Was she a relative, or a superior servant? In these troublous times she might easily have been both.
In any case she was a childhood's companion of the Citizen-Deputy—whether on an equal or a humbler footing, Juliette would have given much to ascertain.
With the marvellous instinct peculiar to women of temperament, she had already divined Anne Mie's love for Déroulède. The poor young cripple's very soul seemed to quiver magnetically at the bare mention of his name, her whole face became transfigured: Juliette even thought her beautiful then.

===Chinese===
「让我替您把头发理一理，好不好？」安娜·米说，像是故意躲开正面回答。「德鲁莱德先生这会儿正和夫人在客厅里。到时候您就见着他了。」
朱丽叶特不再多问，任凭安娜·米替她理好头发，借给她一条干净的头巾，总之，把她那场可怕经历的一切痕迹都抹去了。她心里又困惑，又想哭。安娜·米的温柔，不知怎的，竟有些刺得她心神不宁。她弄不明白这姑娘在德鲁莱德家是什么身份。是亲眷呢，还是一个上等仆人？在这动乱的年月里，她完全可能两者都是。
无论如何，她是公会议员从小一起长大的伙伴——究竟是平等的地位，还是低人一等，朱丽叶特情愿付出许多，也要弄个明白。
凭着性情中女子所独具的奇妙直觉，她已猜到了安娜·米对德鲁莱德的恋慕。这个可怜的年轻残废，只要一提起他的名字，整个灵魂仿佛都起了磁性的震颤，整张脸都变了样：那时连朱丽叶特都觉得她美。

===Original===
She looked at herself critically in the glass, and adjusted a curl, which looked its best when it was rebellious. She scrutinised her own face carefully; why? she could not tell: another of those subtle feminine instincts perhaps.
The becoming simplicity of the prevailing mode suited her to perfection. The waist line, rather high but clearly defined—a precursor of the later more accentuated fashion—gave grace to her long slender limbs, and emphasised the lissomeness of her figure. The kerchief, edged with fine lace, and neatly folded across her bosom, softened the contour of her girlish bust and shoulders.
And her hair was a veritable glory round her dainty, piquant face. Soft, fair, and curly, it emerged in a golden halo from beneath the prettiest little lace cap imaginable.
She turned and faced Anne Mie, ready to follow her out of the room, and the young crippled girl sighed as she smoothed down the folds of her own apron, and gave a final touch to the completion of Juliette's attire.

===Chinese===
她对着镜子挑剔地端详自己，抚平了一绺鬈发——那鬈发偏是不服帖时最好看。她细细审视自己的脸；为什么呢？她说不上来：也许又是那种微妙的女性直觉在作祟。
时下流行的那种雅致的简朴装束，与她再合适不过。腰线收得偏高，却清晰利落——是后来那种更强调腰身式样的先声——给她修长窈窕的肢体平添了几分秀雅，也衬出了她身段的轻盈柔美。那条镶着细蕾丝的头巾，整整齐齐地折叠在胸前，柔化了她少女的胸廓与肩头的轮廓。
而她的头发，环着她那娇俏动人的脸庞，真可谓一头华彩。柔软、浅淡、蜷曲，从一顶想得出的小小蕾丝帽最美的式样底下透出来，宛如一圈金色的光晕。
她转过身来，面对着安娜·米，准备随她走出房间；那年轻的跛足姑娘叹了一口气，抚平自己围裙的褶皱，最后整理了一下，朱丽叶特的装束才算齐备。

===Original===
The time before the evening meal slipped by like a dream-hour for Juliette.
She had lived so much alone, had led such an introspective life, that she had hardly realised and understood all that was going on around her. At the time when the inner vitality of France first asserted itself and then swept away all that hindered its mad progress, she was tied to the invalid chair of her half-demented father; then, after that, the sheltering walls of the Ursuline Convent had hidden from her mental vision the true meaning of the great conflict, between the Old Era and the New.
Déroulède was neither a pedant nor yet a revolutionary: his theories were Utopian and he had an extraordinary overpowering sympathy for his fellow-men.
After the first casual greetings with Juliette, he had continued a discussion with his mother, which the young girl's entrance had interrupted.
He seemed to take but little notice of her, although at times his dark, keen eyes would seek hers, as if challenging her for a reply.
He was talking of the mob of Paris, whom he evidently understood so well. Incidents such as the one which Juliette had provoked, had led to rape and theft, often to murder, before now: but outside Citizen-Deputy Déroulède's house everything was quiet, half-an-hour after Juliette's escape from that howling, brutish crowd.
He had merely spoken to them, for about twenty minutes, and they had gone away quite quietly, without even touching one hair of his head. He seemed to love them: to know how to separate the little good that was in them, from that hard crust of evil, which misery had put around their hearts.

===Chinese===
对朱丽叶特来说，晚饭前的那段时光，像梦幻般的一小时，悄然溜了过去。
她一向活得那么孤单，过着那么内向自省的日子，以至于对身边发生的一切，几乎无从了解，也无从体会。当法兰西内在的生命力初露锋芒、继而把一切阻挡它疯狂进程的东西统统扫开的时候，她正被拴在半疯癫父亲的病榻旁；再后来，乌尔苏拉会修道院的高墙又把她遮蔽起来，使她的心眼看不见那场新旧时代之间大冲突的真正含义。
德鲁莱德既非迂腐学究，也谈不上革命家：他的理论是乌托邦式的，而对同胞却怀着一种异乎寻常、沛然莫御的同情心。
同朱丽叶特略事寒暄之后，他便接着与母亲继续讨论起来——那场谈话，本是这位年轻姑娘的进门打断的。
他似乎不大留意她，虽然他那一双黑亮的锐眼，不时会去寻她的目光，仿佛在向她挑战，要她答话。
他谈的是巴黎的暴众，显然他对那些人了如指掌。像朱丽叶特惹起的那类事，从前曾酿成过奸淫掳掠，往往还有凶杀；可如今，德鲁莱德公会议员的宅子外面，一片安静——朱丽叶特逃进这里才半个钟头，那群狂嚎的野蛮人已经散尽。
他不过对他们讲了二十来分钟的话，他们就安安静静地走开了，连他一根头发都没碰。他似乎爱着他们：懂得怎样把他们身上那一点点善，从困苦套在他们心外的那层又冷又硬的恶壳里剥出来。

===Original===
Once he addressed Juliette somewhat abruptly: "Pardon me, mademoiselle, but for your own sake we must guard you a prisoner here awhile. No one would harm you under this roof, but it would not be safe for you to cross the neighbouring streets tonight."
"But I must go, monsieur. Indeed, indeed I must!" she said earnestly. "I am deeply grateful to you, but I could not leave Pétronelle."
"Who is Pétronelle?"
"My dear old nurse, monsieur. She has never left me. Think how anxious and miserable she must be, at my prolonged absence."
"Where does she live?"
"At No. 15 Rue Taitbout, but—"

===Chinese===
有一回，他有些突兀地对朱丽叶特开口：「恕我直言，小姐，为了您自己的缘故，我们只得把您在这里当一会儿囚徒看管起来。在这片屋檐底下没人会伤害您，可是今晚您若要穿过附近几条街，就不安全了。」
「可我必须走，先生。真的，真的必须走！」她恳切地说。「我对您感激不尽，可我不能撇下佩特罗内勒。」
「佩特罗内勒是谁？」
「我亲爱的老奶妈，先生。她从来没有离开过我。请想一想，我这么久不回去，她该多么焦急、多么难受。」
「她住在哪儿？」
「泰布街（Rue Taitbout）十五号，可是——」

===Original===
"Will you allow me to take her a message?—telling her that you are safe and under my roof, where it is obviously more prudent that you should remain at present."
"If you think it best, monsieur," she replied.
Inwardly she was trembling with excitement. God had not only brought her to this house, but willed that she should stay in it.
"In whose name shall I take the message, mademoiselle?" he asked.
"My name is Juliette Marny."
She watched him keenly as she said it, but there was not the slightest sign in his expressive face, to show that he had recognised the name.
Ten years is a long time, and everyone had lived through so much during those years! A wave of intense wrath swept through Juliette's soul, as she realised that he had forgotten. The name meant nothing to him! It did not recall to him the fact that his hand was stained with blood. During ten years she had suffered, she had fought with herself, fought for him as it were, against the Fate which she was destined to mete out to him, whilst he had forgotten, or at least had ceased to think.
He bowed to her and went out of the room.
The wave of wrath subsided, and she was left alone with Madame Déroulède: presently Anne Mie came in.

===Chinese===
「容我给她捎个口信，可好？——告诉她，您很平安，就住在我的屋檐下；眼下您留在这里，显然是更稳妥的。」
「您要是觉得这样最好，先生。」她答道。
她心里正激动得发颤。上帝不仅把她领进了这座宅子，而且要她留在这里。
「小姐，口信该以谁的名义捎去呢？」他问。
「我叫朱丽叶特·马尔尼。」
她说这话时，紧紧盯着他，可他那富于表情的脸上，没有一丝一毫的迹象，表明这个名字唤起了什么。
十年是一段漫长的岁月，而那些年里，人人都熬过了太多的事！一股狂怒的巨浪席卷过朱丽叶特的灵魂，因为她明白了：他忘了。这个名字对他毫无意义！它没有让他记起，他的手上曾染着血污。十年来，她在受苦，她在和自己搏斗，仿佛是替他在搏斗，抗拒着她命定要降给他的那个命运；而他，却忘了，至少已不再去想。
他向她一躬身，走出了房间。
怒潮退了下去，屋里只剩下她和德鲁莱德夫人；不多时，安娜·米也进来了。

===Original===
The three women chatted together, waiting for the return of the master of the house. Juliette felt well and, in spite of herself, almost happy. She had lived so long in the miserable, little attic alone with Pétronelle that she enjoyed the well-being of this refined home. It was not so grand or gorgeous of course as her father's princely palace opposite the Louvre, a wreck now, since it was annexed by the Committee of National Defence, for the housing of soldiery. But the Déroulèdes' home was essentially a refined one. The delicate china on the tall chimneypiece, the few bits of Buhl and Vernis Martin about the room, the vision through the open doorway of the supper-table spread with a fine white cloth, and sparkling with silver, all spoke of fastidious tastes, of habits of luxury and elegance, which the spirit of Equality and Anarchy had not succeeded in eradicating.
When Déroulède came back, he brought an atmosphere of breezy cheerfulness with him.
The street was quiet now, and when walking past the hospital—his own gift to the Nation—he had been loudly cheered. One or two ironical voices had asked him what he had done with the aristo and her lace furbelows, but it remained at that and Mademoiselle Marny need have no fear.

===Chinese===
三个女人一起闲谈着，等候一家之主回来。朱丽叶特觉得身子很好，而且不由自主地，几乎感到快活。她同佩特罗内勒在凄凉的小阁楼里相依为命太久了，如今享受着这雅致人家的安乐，格外觉得受用。它自然比不上她父亲那座卢浮宫对面的王侯般的宅邸气派华美——那宅子如今已成一片废墟，因为被国防委员会（Committee of National Defence）征了去驻兵。可德鲁莱德家这个家，骨子里是精致的。高高的壁炉架上陈设的细瓷器，屋里那几件布尔镶嵌与马丁漆（Buhl and Vernis Martin）的家具，敞开的门口望进去，铺着细白桌布、银器闪烁的晚餐桌，处处诉说着主人的考究趣味、奢华与雅致的习惯——平等与无政府的精神，终究没能把这些根除。
德鲁莱德回来时，带着一身轻快爽朗的气息。
街上如今安静了；他走过那座医院——那是他自己献给国家的一份礼——时，还赢得了一片响亮的欢呼。有一两个讥诮的声音问他，把那个「aristo」（贵族婆娘）和她的蕾丝花边裙子弄到哪里去了，但也就到此为止，马尔尼小姐尽可放心。

===Original===
He had brought Pétronelle along with him: his careless, lavish hospitality would have suggested the housing of Juliette's entire domestic establishment, had she possessed one.
As it was, the worthy old soul's deluge of happy tears had melted his kindly heart. He offered her and her young mistress shelter, until the small cloud should have rolled by.
After that he suggested a journey to England. Emigration now was the only real safety, and Mademoiselle Marny had unpleasantly drawn on herself the attention of the Paris rabble. No doubt, within the next few days her name would figure among the "suspect." She would be safest out of the country, and could not do better than place herself under the guidance of that English enthusiast, who had helped so many persecuted Frenchmen to escape from the terrors of the Revolution: the man who was such a thorn in the flesh of the Committee of Public Safety, and who went by the nickname of The Scarlet Pimpernel.

===Chinese===
他是把佩特罗内勒一道带来的：他那豪爽而慷慨的待客之道，倘若朱丽叶特有整套的仆役家当，只怕连她全家老小都要一并举荐收留下来的。
事实上，是那位可敬老人的喜泪如泉，融化了她的仁厚心肠。他向她和她的年轻女主人许下了栖身之所，直到那一小片乌云滚过天际为止。
过后，他又提议去英格兰走一遭。如今流亡国外才是唯一真正的安全，而马尔尼小姐已不干不净地惹上了巴黎暴民的注意。毫无疑问，不出几日，她的名字就会列进「嫌疑犯」的名单。她离了国才最安全，而她最好的出路，莫过于把自己托付给那位英国的狂热侠士——他帮助过许许多多受迫害的法国人逃离革命的恐怖：这个人，是扎在救国委员会（Committee of Public Safety）肉里的一根刺，诨名唤作红花侠（The Scarlet Pimpernel）。
