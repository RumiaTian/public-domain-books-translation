## Preface / 序言
===Original===

In this book I have written about some aspects of the war which, I believe, the world must know and remember, not only as a memorial of men’s courage in tragic years, but as a warning of what will happen again﻿—surely﻿—if a heritage of evil and of folly is not cut out of the hearts of peoples. Here it is the reality of modern warfare not only as it appears to British soldiers, of whom I can tell, but to soldiers on all the fronts where conditions were the same.

What I have written here does not cancel, nor alter, nor deny anything in my daily narratives of events on the western front as they are now published in book form. They stand, I may claim sincerely and humbly, as a truthful, accurate, and tragic record of the battles in France and Belgium during the years of war, broadly pictured out as far as I could see and know. My duty, then, was that of a chronicler, not arguing why things should have happened so nor giving reasons why they should not happen so, but describing faithfully many of the things I saw, and narrating the facts as I found them, as far as the censorship would allow. After early, hostile days it allowed nearly all but criticism, protest, and of the figures of loss.

The purpose of this book is to get deeper into the truth of this war and of all war﻿—not by a more detailed narrative of events, but rather as the truth was revealed to the minds of men, in many aspects, out of their experience; and by a plain statement of realities, however painful, to add something to the world’s knowledge out of which men of goodwill may try to shape some new system of relationship between one people and another, some new code of international morality, preventing or at least postponing another massacre of youth like that five years’ sacrifice of boys of which I was a witness.

===Chinese===

在本书中，我写下了这场战争的一些方面。我相信，世界必须知道并记住它们——不仅作为悲惨岁月里人们勇气的一座纪念碑，也作为一种警告：倘若邪恶与愚昧的遗产不从各民族心中铲除，这样的事情必将——必定——再度发生。这里所写的，是现代战争的真实面貌，不仅是我所能讲述的英国士兵眼中的战争，也是各地条件相同的战线上所有士兵眼中的战争。

我在这里写下的东西，并不取消、更改或否认我那部西部前线事件逐日报道中的任何内容——那些报道如今已结集成书。它们依然成立——我可以真诚而谦卑地如此声称——是一部关于战争年代法国与比利时诸战役的真实、准确而悲惨的记录，就我所见所知，尽量宏阔地勾画了出来。我那时的职责，是做一个编年史家：不去争论事情为何竟会如此发生，也不去论证它们本不该如此发生，而是忠实地描述我目睹的许多事情，把事实按我发现时的样子叙述出来——在书报检查制度容许的范围之内。经过早期那些充满敌意的日子之后，它几乎容许了一切，只除了批评、抗议，以及伤亡的数字。

本书的目的，是更深地探入这场战争、也是一切战争的真相——不是靠更详尽的事件叙述，而是靠真相如何从人们的经历中、在许多方面揭示于他们的心灵；靠对种种现实——无论多么痛苦——的坦率陈述，为世界的知识增添一点东西，好让心怀善意的人们由此试着在民族与民族之间塑造某种新的关系体系、某种新的国际道德准则，以防止、或至少推迟又一场对青年的屠杀——就像那场我亲眼见证的、持续五年的少年牺牲。
