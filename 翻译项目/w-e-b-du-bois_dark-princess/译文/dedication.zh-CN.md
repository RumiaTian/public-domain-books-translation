===Original===

*To*

*Her High Loveliness*

**Titania** XXVII

By her own grace

Queen of Faerie,

Commander of the Bath; Grand Medallion of Merit; Litterarum Humanarum Doctor; Fidei Extensor; etc., etc.

Of whose faith and fond affection

this romance

was surely born

===Chinese===

*献给*

*至美殿下*

**泰坦妮娅**二十七世

凭自身恩典

仙国女王；巴斯勋位司令；功勋大奖章；人文学博士；信仰拓延者；等等，等等

正是凭着她的信念与深情

这部罗曼史

才得以孕育而生
