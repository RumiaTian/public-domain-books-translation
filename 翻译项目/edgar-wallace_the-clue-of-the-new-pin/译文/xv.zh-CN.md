## XV / 十五

===Original===

In the course of his professional duties Tab Holland had been brought into contact with the master of the Golden Roof on two occasions. The first followed a small scandal, which only remotely touched the restaurant (the woman who was the subject of Tab’s investigation had dined there at an important date) and once in connection with a dead-season topic dealing with the nutritive values of food.
He had found the Chinaman reserved to a point of taciturnity, monosyllabic in speech; a most unsatisfactory person.
Tab knew nothing about him except that he was a successful Chinaman who had gravitated into the restaurant business. He asked Jacques for enlightenment, well knowing that if the news editor could not satisfy his curiosity, it was because Yeh Ling was altogether uninteresting. Jacques was one of those rarities, to whom reference is so frequently made that it might be imagined they were as common as straws in a stable. He was a veritable “mine of information.” The genus occurs sometimes in newspaper offices. Jacques knew everybody and everybody’s wife. He knew why they married. He also knew why stars twinkled and the chemical composition of tears. Quote him a line from any classic and he would give you its predecessor and that which followed. He knew the dates of all important earthquakes and was an authority on the Mogul Emperors. He could sketch you with equal facility the position of Frossard’s second corps at Rezonville on August 17th, 1870, or the military situation at Thermopylae﻿—and dates.

===Chinese===

因职务之故，塔布·荷兰曾与“金顶楼”的东家打过两回交道。头一回是在一桩小小的丑闻之后——那桩丑闻同这家餐馆只有极疏远的一点牵连（塔布调查的那位女子，在一个紧要的日子曾在那里用餐）；另一回则是为一篇应付新闻淡季的稿子，谈的是食物的营养价值。
他发觉这个中国人缄默得近于一言不发，开口净是单字片言；是个极令人不得要领的人物。
塔布对他一无所知，只知道他是个发了迹的中国人，自然而然做进了餐馆这一行。他去请教雅克，心里早有盘算：若是这位新闻主编都满足不了他的好奇心，那便只能说明叶凌这个人压根儿无可足谈。雅克属于那种稀罕人物——人们把他们挂在嘴边提得如此频繁，倒仿佛他们像马厩里的干草一样遍地都是。他是货真价实的“活情报库”。这一属人物偶尔会在报馆里出现。雅克谁都认识，连每个人的太太都认识。他们为什么结的婚，他也知道。他还知道星星为什么眨眼、眼泪的化学成分。随便引哪部经典的一行诗给他，他都能报出上一行和下一行。历次大地震的日期他一概记得，论起莫卧儿诸帝来更是权威。他能同样轻巧地给你勾画出 1870 年 8 月 17 日弗罗萨尔第二军在雷宗维尔的方位，也能勾画温泉关的军情——连日子都一并奉告。

===Original===

The only serious students of the *Megaphone* reference library were the reporters who went there to confound Jacques. They never succeeded.
“Yeh Ling? Yes﻿—queer bird. An educated Chink﻿—got a son who is quite a swell scholar by Chinese standards. He ought to make a good story some day; that house he is building at Storford﻿—it is on the way to Hertford; says that one day his son will be the Chinese Ambassador here and he wants him to have a house worthy of his position. That is what he told Stott. Know Stott? He is a dud architect who knows it all. Weird little devil who looks as if he might have been clever with a different kind of brain. Stott laid out the ground work, sort of Chinese temple with two enormous concrete pillars that are going to stand halfway down the drive. The Pillar of Cheerful Memories and the Pillar of Grateful Hearts. That’s what he is going to call them. Stott thought it was heathenish and wondered if the Bishop would like it. Yes, you ought to see that place, Tab. No, it isn’t built. Yeh Ling has nothing but Chink labour. The Secretary of the Builders’ Union went to see him about it. Yeh Ling said his ancestors had a union of their own which put the bar upon non-Taoist labour. Taoism﻿—”

===Chinese===

《扩音器报》资料室里正经用功的学生，只有那些专程去难倒雅克的记者。他们从未得手过。
“叶凌？认识——是个怪家伙。一个受过教育的中国佬——有个儿子，按中国的标准算得上一号响当当的饱学之士。这家伙早晚能写成一条好新闻；他在斯托福德（Storford）造的那座宅子——就在去赫特福德的路上；他说他儿子早晚要当上中国驻此间的大使，他要让儿子有一座配得上那身份的宅子。这话他是跟斯托特说的。认识斯托特么？一个自认无所不晓的蹩脚建筑师。古怪的小魔头，瞧那副样子，若是换一副别样质地的脑子，本可以挺聪明。底子是斯托特布的置，一座半像中国庙宇的宅子，门前车道走到一半，要立两根大得出奇的水泥柱子。一根‘欢愉记忆之柱’，一根‘感戴之心之柱’——他就要这么给它们命名。斯托特觉得这未免异教气，还拿不准主教大人见了喜不喜欢。对了，那地方你真该去看看，塔布。不，还没盖成。叶凌用的清一色是中国佬的劳工。营造业工会的书记为此去见过他。叶凌说，他的祖上自己就有一个行会，立过禁例，把非道教徒的劳工拒之门外。说到道教——”

===Original===

“I hate to wade into the foaming torrent of your eloquence,” said Tab gently, “but how did you come to meet Stott?”
“Same lodge,” said Jacques. “It is not for me to talk down a brother craftsman﻿—are you one of us by-the-way?”
Tab shook his head.
“Ought to be. Get a little respect for authority into your system. As I was saying, I don’t want to knock Stott, but he’s not everybody’s meat. Go and see that temple or whatever it is, Tab. Might be a good story.”

===Chinese===

“我不大舍得蹚进你这片泡沫翻飞的雄辩激流，”塔布温和地说，“可你是怎么认识斯托特的？”
“同一个分会，”雅克说，“贬损一位会中弟兄，轮不到我——顺便问一句，你也是我们会里的么？”
塔布摇了摇头。
“该入的。也好让你那副筋骨里进去一点对权威的敬畏。我方才说了，我并不想说斯托特的坏话，可他不是人人都消受得了的那碟菜。去瞧瞧那座庙吧，不管它究竟是什么，塔布。说不定是条好新闻。”

===Original===

On the first idle day he had, Tab took his motor bicycle and went out to Storford. He was not entirely without hope that he would see Ursula﻿—her house was only seven miles beyond Storford Hill, and he had reason to know that she had withdrawn herself to her country home. In a letter telling this she had told him in so many words that when she wanted him she would send for him.
He saw the building from a distance.
He had noticed it before﻿—it was hardly possible to miss seeing it, for it stood on the crest of one of the few hills the country boasted. The walls were half finished and heavy wooden uprights rose like the palings of a fence above the queerly laid courses. And one of the pillars already lifted its lofty head. It flanked on one side a broad pathway which was half the width of the house, and stood some fifty feet above the ground, being crowned by a small stone dragon.

===Chinese===

赶上头一个空闲日子，塔布骑上摩托车，出城去了斯托福德。他并非全然不存见到乌苏拉的指望——她的宅子过了斯托福德山再走七英里才到，而他也有缘由知道，她已遁居到乡下的家里。她在讲明此事的信里，还明明白白告诉他：等她要见他时，自会遣人来请。
他远远便望见了那座建筑。
他早先就注意过它——想漏看它几乎不可能，因为它立在这一带屈指可数的几座山头之一的顶上。墙砌了一半，粗大的木立柱像栅栏桩一样，从排布古怪的砖层间竖起。两根柱子已有一根昂起了高高的头。它扼守在一条宽阔走道的一侧——那走道足有宅子一半宽——离地约莫五十英尺，顶上蹲着一只小小的石龙。

===Original===

Tab wondered if this was the Pillar of Grateful Hearts or that which stood, or would stand, for cheerful memories.
Its diameter must have been fully five feet. Near at hand was one of the wooden moulds in which it was cast, and a Chinese workman was scraping the interior.
Tab walked through a break in the low hedge which separated Yeh Ling’s new home from the road and now stood regarding with interest the activities of the blue-bloused workmen. Their industry was remarkable. Whether they were running bricks and mortar, or cutting out the garden (already taking shape) or walling up the terraces, they moved quickly, untiringly, wholly absorbed in their occupations. Never once did they stop to lean upon their spades and picks to discuss the chances of the new administration, or to tell one another how Milligan got his black eye.

===Chinese===

塔布拿不准，这是“感戴之心之柱”，还是那根代表着——或者说将要代表——欢愉记忆的柱子。
它的直径足有五英尺。近旁搁着一副浇铸它用的木模，一个中国工人正在刮模子的内壁。
塔布从把叶凌的新宅同大路隔开的矮树篱的一个豁口走进去，站在那儿饶有兴味地打量那些穿蓝布短褂的工人们的活计。他们的勤勉可观得很。无论是递砖运浆，还是开辟园圃（已见雏形），或是给层层台地垒墙，他们都手脚麻利，不知疲倦，整个儿沉浸在各人的活计里。谁也不曾停下来拄着铁锹镐头，议论一番新政府的胜算，或是互相说说米利根那只乌青眼是怎么来的。

===Original===

Nobody seemed to notice Tab. He strolled further into the land and there was none to challenge his right. A gang of men were gravelling and rolling the broad path and one of these said something which sent the others into a fit of that chittering laughter which is peculiar to the East. Tab wondered what was the joke.
Turning to walk back to the road, he saw that a car had stopped at the break in the hedge, and his heart gave a leap, for its occupant was Ursula.
“What do you think of it?” she asked.

===Chinese===

似乎谁也没有留意塔布。他信步往园子深处走，也没有人出来查问他的来路。一伙人正在给那条宽道铺砾石、碾平，其中一个说了句什么，惹得其余人迸出一阵东方特有的那种叽叽喳喳的笑。塔布纳闷，笑料究竟是什么。
转身往大路走回去时，他看见一辆汽车停在树篱的豁口处，他的心猛地一跳——车里坐的是乌苏拉。
“你觉得它怎么样？”她问。

===Original===

“It is going to be rather wonderful﻿—how do you like the idea of having a Chinaman for a neighbour? I forgot﻿—you rather like the Chinese?”
“Yes,” she said shortly. “There could be worse neighbours than Yeh Ling.”
“You know him?”

===Chinese===

“将来会相当堂皇——有个中国人做邻居，这主意你受用么？我倒忘了——你不是对中国人向来有几分好感？”
“是的，”她简短地说，“比叶凌更糟的邻居也多的是。”
“你认识他？”

===Original===

He wondered if she would deny acquaintance or evade the question.
“Very well,” she said calmly, “he is the proprietor of the Golden Roof. I often dine there. You know him too?”
“Slightly,” said Tab, looking back at the unfinished house. “He must be rich.”
“I don’t know. One never really knows what money is required to build a place like this. The labour is cheap and it seems a very simple kind of house.”

===Chinese===

他揣想她会不会否认相识，或者把话岔开。
“很熟，”她平静地说，“他就是‘金顶楼’的老板。我常在那儿吃饭。你也认识他？”
“略有往来，”塔布说，一面回头望那座未完工的宅子，“他必定很有钱。”
“我不知道。盖这样一处宅子究竟需得多少钱，谁也说不出个准数。劳工便宜，房子瞧着也是顶简单的一种式样。”

===Original===

And then with a wave of her hand, she drove on. She might at least have asked him to lunch, he thought indignantly.
A week went past, a drab week for a discontented Tab Holland, for now there was neither a likelihood of, nor an excuse for, a chance meeting.
A sedative week for hiding Walters. References to the murder seldom appeared now in the newspapers, and he had found a man who had offered to get him a job as a steward on an outward bound liner.

===Chinese===

说罢她一挥手，驱车前行。她总该至少请他吃顿午饭吧——他愤愤地想。
一星期过去了。对满腹不平的塔布·荷兰，这是个灰暗的星期，因为如今既无邂逅的可能，也无邂逅的借口。
对藏匿的沃尔特斯，这是一个安神镇静的星期。如今报上已很少提及那桩凶案，他又寻着一个人，答应替他在一艘外航邮轮上谋一份侍应生的差事。

===Original===

A week of drugged sleep for a besotted man, curled up on a mattress at the top of Yo Len Fo’s house.
But for Inspector Carver an exceptionally busy week though there was no newspaper record of his activities.
Tab no longer spent his evenings at home. The flat seemed horribly empty now that the lovesick Rex had gone. He had had a radio from him saying that he was improved in health. The message was cheerful enough, so that Ursula’s refusal could not have bitten very hard.

===Chinese===

对那个烟昏了头的人——蜷在尤连福屋子顶楼一张床垫上的那一个——这是药力昏睡的一星期。
唯独对卡弗探长，这是格外忙碌的一星期，尽管他的种种动作报上一字未记。
塔布不再在家消磨夜晚。害相思的雷克斯一走，这套公寓空得怕人。他收到过他一封无线电报，说身体见好。电文欢快得很，可见乌苏拉的回绝还不曾咬得太狠。

===Original===

By the end of the week, life had become an intolerable dreariness, and to make matters worse, nothing was happening in the great world that called for Tab’s intervention and interest. He was in that condition of utter boredom when there happened the first of those remarkable incidents which, in his official account of the case, Inspector Carver refers to as “The Second Activity.”
The flats, one of which Tab occupied, had originally been apartments in a private house. With little structural alterations they had been turned into self-contained suites. On each of the landings was a door of one of the four flats. Admission to the house was by the front door, and the landlord had so arranged matters that, whilst the key of each flat was different, all keys opened the street-door. It was, therefore, possible to go in and out without observation, unless by chance one of the other tenants happened to be on the stairs or in the passageway at the time.
On Saturday night Tab knew he would be alone in the house; the other three tenants invariably spent the weekend out of town. One was a middle-aged musician who lived on the top floor. Beneath him was a young couple engaged in literary work; then came Tab’s flat, and the ground floor suite was occupied by a man whose profession was unknown, but who was generally believed to be connected with an advertising agency. He was seldom at home, and Tab had only seen him once.

===Chinese===

到了周末，日子已沉闷得令人难耐；更糟的是，大千世界毫无动静，没有什么事需要塔布插手、引他上心。他正处在这种百无聊赖的当口，那一系列离奇事件中的头一桩发生了——在关于本案的官方报告里，卡弗探长把它称作“第二次活动”。
塔布住着其中一套的这幢公寓楼，原是一栋私宅里的几间屋子。房屋结构稍加改动，它们便成了各自成套的独立套间。每段楼梯平台上，都有一扇门通向四套公寓之一。进楼走前门，房东又把事情安排成这样：各套公寓的钥匙互不相同，临街大门却把把钥匙都开得。因此，进出这幢楼可以不惹人耳目——除非碰巧哪位别的房客当时正在楼梯上或过道里。
礼拜六夜里，塔布知道楼里将只剩他一人；另外三位房客照例都出城度周末。一位是住顶楼的中年音乐家；他楼下是一对舞文弄墨的年轻夫妇；再往下便是塔布的套间；底层那套住着一个职业不明的人，不过一般都相信他同一家广告代理行有干系。他难得在家，塔布只见过他一面。

===Original===

The Saturday night happened to be the occasion of an annual dinner of his club, and Tab dressed and went out early, spent a mildly exhilarating evening and returned home at half-past twelve. There was nothing in first appearance to suggest that anything unusual had happened in his absence, except that the lights in his sitting-room were burning and he had switched them off before he went out.
His first impression was that the waste of current was due to his own carelessness, but then he recalled very clearly that he had turned out the light and closed the sitting-room door before he went out. Now the sitting-room door was open, as also was the door of Rex’s old room.

===Chinese===

这个礼拜六夜里，恰逢他所属俱乐部的年度晚宴，塔布穿戴整齐，早早出了门，过了一个微带兴奋的夜晚，十二点半回的家。乍看之下，没有任何迹象表明他不在时发生过什么不寻常的事，只有一桩：他起居室里的灯亮着，而他出门前分明已把灯关了。
他的第一个念头是，这点浪费掉的电得怪自己粗心；可他随即清清楚楚记起，出门前他熄了灯，还关上了起居室的门。此刻起居室的门敞着，雷克斯旧日那间屋的门也同样敞着。
