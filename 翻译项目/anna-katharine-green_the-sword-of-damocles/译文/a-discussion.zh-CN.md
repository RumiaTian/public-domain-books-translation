## II A Discussion / 二 一场讨论

===Original===
> "Young men think old men fools, and old men know young men to be so."

"And you are actually in earnest?"
"I am."

===Chinese===
> 「年轻人觉得老年人是傻瓜，而老年人知道年轻人确实如此。」

「你当真是认真的？」
「当真。」

===Original===
The first speaker, a fine-looking gentleman of some forty years of age, drummed with his fingers on the table before him and eyed the face of the young man who had repeated this assent so emphatically, with a certain close scrutiny indicative of surprise.

"It is an unlooked-for move for you to make," he remarked at length. "Your success as a pianist has been so decided, I confess I do not understand why you should desire to abandon a profession that in five years' time has procured you both competence and a very enviable reputation—for the doubtful prospects of Wall Street, too!" he added with a deep and thoughtful frown that gave still further impressiveness to his strongly marked features.

===Chinese===
先开口的那位是个相貌堂堂的绅士，约莫四十岁，用手指在面前的桌上敲着鼓点，仔细打量着那年轻人——这年轻人把那声应答重复得如此斩钉截铁——目光中带着一种说明他颇为惊异的审视。

「这一着，可真出人意料，」他终于开口道。「你作为钢琴家的成就已是板上钉钉，我承认我不明白你为何要放弃一门短短五年便已使你既获温饱、又赢得令人艳羡声誉的职业——况且还是为了华尔街那般前途未卜的营生！」他深深皱起眉头，若有所思，使他本就轮廓鲜明的面容更显凝重。

===Original===
The young man with a sweep of his eye over the luxurious apartment in which they sat, shrugged his shoulders with that fine and nonchalant grace which was one of his chief characteristics.

"With such a pilot as yourself, I ought to be able to steer clear of the shoals," said he, a frank smile illumining a face that was rather interesting than handsome.

The elder gentleman did not return the smile. Instead of that he remained gazing at the ample coal-fire that burned in the grate before him with a look that to the young musician was simply inexplicable. "You see the ship in haven," he murmured at last; "but do not consider what storms it has weathered or what perils escaped. It is a voyage I would encourage no son of mine to undertake."

===Chinese===
那年轻人目光一扫两人所坐的这间华美厅堂，耸了耸肩，带着那种优雅而漫不经心的风度——这正是他最显著的特点之一。

「有您这样的领航员，我总该能绕开暗礁了吧。」他说，坦率的微笑照亮了一张称不上俊美却颇为耐看的脸。

年长的绅士没有回以微笑。他只是凝视着面前炉栅里熊熊燃烧的旺火，神情对这年轻音乐家而言简直无法理解。「你看见的是船已入港，」他终于低声道；「却不去想它经历过怎样的风暴、逃脱过怎样的险境。这一趟航程，我不会鼓励我的任何一个儿子去尝试。」

===Original===
"Yet you are not the man to shrink from danger or to hesitate in a course you had marked out for yourself, because of the struggle it involved or the difficulties it presented!" the young man exclaimed almost involuntarily as his glance lingered with a certain sort of fascination on the powerful brow and steady if somewhat melancholy eye of his companion.

"No; but danger and difficulty should not be sought, only subdued when encountered. If you were driven into this path, I should say, 'God pity you!' and hold you out my hand to steady you along its precipices and above its sudden quicksands. But you are not driven to it. Your profession offers you the means of an ample livelihood while your good heart and fair talents insure you ultimate and honorable success, both in the social and artistic world. For a man of twenty-five such prospects are not common and he must be difficult to please not to be satisfied with them."

===Chinese===
「可您绝不是那种面对危险会退缩，也不会因为一段路途需要挣扎、布满艰难便在自己标定的方向上踟蹰不前的人！」年轻人几乎是脱口而出，他的目光带着某种着迷停留在同伴那宽阔的额头和沉稳却略带忧郁的眼睛上。

「不错；但危险与艰难不应去主动追寻，只应在遭遇时加以克服。倘若你是被逼上这条路的，我会说：『愿上帝怜悯你！』并伸出手，在悬崖边、在骤然出现的流沙之上扶你一把。可你并非被逼无奈。你的本行已为你提供了丰足的生计，而你善良的心地和不错的才华，也保证你在社交界与艺术界最终都将获得体面的成功。对二十五岁的人来说，这样的前景并不多见，除非极难伺候，否则没有不满足的。」

===Original===
"Yes," said the other rising with a fitful movement but instantly sitting again; "I have nothing to complain of as the world goes, only—Sir," he exclaimed with a sudden determination that lent a force to his features they had hitherto lacked, "you speak of being driven into a certain course; what do you mean by that?"

"I mean," returned the other; "forced by circumstances to enter a line of business to which many others, if not all others are preferable."

===Chinese===
「是啊，」对方说着，一阵冲动似地站起身，却又立刻坐了下去；「就世道而言，我没什么可抱怨的，只是——先生，」他忽然以一种此前不曾有过的决断之态喊道，这决断使他的面貌增添了一种力量，「您说到被逼上某一条路；您这话是什么意思？」

「我的意思是，」另一位答道，「为境况所迫，而投身一门几乎任何别的行当——若非所有别的行当——都比它更可取的生意。」

===Original===
"You speak strongly, speculation evidently has none of your sympathy, notwithstanding the favorable results which have accrued to you from it. But excuse me, by circumstances you mean poverty, I suppose, and the lack of every other opening to wealth and position. You would not consider the desire to make a large fortune in a short space of time a circumstance of a sufficiently determining nature to reconcile you to my entering Wall Street speculation?"

The elder gentleman rose, not as the other had done with a restless impulse quickly subsiding at the first excuse, but forcibly and with a feverish impatience that to appearance was somewhat out of proportion to the occasion. "A large fortune in a short space of time!" he reiterated, pausing where he had risen with an eagle glance at his companion and a ringing tone in his voice that bespoke a deep but hitherto suppressed agitation. "It is the alluring inscription above the pitfall into which many a noble youth has fallen; the battle-cry to a struggle that has led many a strong man the way of ruin; the guidepost to a life whose feverish days and sleepless nights offer but poor compensation for the sudden splendors and as sudden reverses attached to it. I had rather you had accounted for this sudden freak of yours by the strongest aspiration after power than by this cry of the merely mercenary man who in his desire to enjoy wealth, prefers to win it by a stroke of luck rather than conquer it by a life of endeavor."

===Chinese===
「您说得很重，投机显然丝毫得不到您的同情，尽管您自己从中获益匪浅。但恕我直言，所谓境况所迫，我想您指的是贫穷，是除了这一条之外再无别的通往财富与地位的门路。您不至于认为，想要在短时间内发一笔大财这一念头，算得上是一种足以令您调和心境、容忍我投身华尔街投机的决定性境况吧？」

年长的绅士站起身来，他不像对方那样一阵坐立不安的冲动、随即因头一个借口便平息下去，而是带着一股猛力，一种近乎狂躁的焦灼——这焦灼看上去与眼前的场合多少有些不相称。「在短时间内发一笔大财！」他反复念道，停在起身之处，以鹰隼般的一瞥望向同伴，嗓音里有一种铿锵的调子，透露出一种深沉而此前一直被压抑着的激动。「那是写在陷阱之上、诱使多少高贵青年坠落的迷人铭文；是一场厮杀的战吼，这厮杀已把多少强壮的汉子引上了毁灭之路；是一根指向某种人生的路标，那人生狂热的白昼与不眠的夜晚，对于随之而来的骤然辉煌与骤然倾覆，只能提供可怜的补偿。我宁愿你用对权势最强烈的渴求来解释你这一时的心血来潮，也不愿你用那唯利是图之人的呼喊来解释——那人在贪图享受财富之时，宁可凭一时运气去赢取它，也不愿以毕生的奋进去征服它。」

===Original===
He stopped. "I am aware that this tirade against the ladder by which I myself have risen so rapidly, must strike you as in ill-taste. But Bertram, I am interested in your welfare and am willing to incur some slight charge of inconsistency in order to insure it," and here he turned upon his companion with that expression of extreme gentleness which lent such a peculiar charm to his countenance and explained perhaps the almost unlimited power he held over the hearts and minds of those who came within the circle of his influence.

===Chinese===
他停下。「我明白，这番痛斥那正是我自己借以迅速攀升的阶梯的话，在你听来一定很不中听。可是伯特兰，我关心你的福祉，为此甘愿招来一点言行不一的小小非议。」说到这里，他转向同伴，脸上带着那种极度温柔的神情——正是这种神情赋予了他的面容一种独特的魅力，也许也解释了他对那些进入他影响范围之内的人的心智为何拥有几乎不受限制的掌控力。

===Original===
"You are very good, sir," murmured his young friend, who to explain matters at once was in reality the nephew of this Wall Street magnate, though from the fact of his having taken another name on entering the musical profession, was not generally known as such. "No one, not even my father himself, could have been more considerate and kind; but I do not think you understand me, or rather I should say I do not think I have made myself perfectly intelligible to you. It is not for the sake of wealth itself or the éclat attending its possession that I desire an immediate fortune, but that by means of it I may attain another object dearer than wealth, and more precious than my career."

===Chinese===
「您太好了，先生，」他那位年轻的朋友低声道。说穿了一件事：他其实是这位华尔街巨擘的侄子，只因当初踏入音乐这一行时另起了一个姓氏，一般人并不知道这层关系。「没有人，连我父亲本人在内，能比您更体贴、更厚道了；但我想您并不理解我，或者我该说，我想我没能向您把话说得完全明白。我之所以渴望立刻得到一笔财产，并非为了财富本身，也非为了拥有它时所附带的荣耀，而是想借此达到另一个比财富更可贵、比我的事业更珍重的目的。」

===Original===
The elder gentleman turned quickly, evidently much surprised, and cast a sudden inquiring glance at his nephew, who blushed with a modest ingenuousness pleasing to see in one so well accustomed to the critical gaze of his fellow-men.

"Yes," said he, as if in answer to that look, "I am in love."

===Chinese===
年长的绅士迅速转过身来，显然十分惊讶，向侄子投去一道突兀的探询目光；侄子带着一种谦逊而坦诚的神情红了脸——对这样一个早已习惯众人挑剔目光的人来说，这副模样颇令人赏心悦目。

「是的，」他仿佛是在回答那一眼似的说道，「我恋爱了。」

===Original===
A deep silence for a moment pervaded the apartment, a sombre silence almost startling to young Mandeville, who had expected some audible expression to follow this announcement if only the good-natured "Pooh! pooh!" of the matured man of the world in the presence of ardent youthful enthusiasm. What could it mean? Looking up he encountered his uncle's eye fixed upon him with the last expression he could have anticipated seeing there, namely that of actual and unmistakable alarm.

"You are displeased," Mandeville exclaimed. "You have thought me proof against such a passion, or perhaps you do not believe in the passion itself!" Then with a sudden remembrance of the notable if somewhat indolent loveliness of his uncle's wife, blushed again at his unusual want of tact, while his eye with an involuntary impulse sought the large panel at their right where, in the full bloom of her first youth, the lady of the house smiled upon all beholders.

===Chinese===
深沉的寂静一时弥漫了整个房间，一种阴郁的寂静，几乎让年轻的曼德维尔（Mandeville）吃了一惊；他原以为这番宣告之后会听到些什么声响，哪怕只是那位老于世故的成熟之人在面对年轻人的炽热激情时，发出一声好脾气的「嗤！嗤！」。这是什么意思？他抬起头，正遇上叔父的目光定定地落在他身上，那目光中所带的，是他最最意想不到的神情——一种真真切切、不容误解的惊惶。

「您不高兴，」曼德维尔喊道。「您以为我对这样的激情免疫，又或者您根本不信有这种激情！」随即猛然记起他婶母那种虽略显慵懒却颇为出众的美貌，又因自己这一反常态的不知分寸而红了脸，同时他的目光不由自主地投向右边那块巨大的画板——在那里，正值韶华的全盛时期，这宅子的女主人对所有的观者含笑而立。

===Original===
"I do not believe in that passion influencing a man's career," his uncle replied with no apparent attention to the other's embarrassment. "A woman needs be possessed of uncommon excellences to justify a man in leaving a path where success is certain, for one where it is not only doubtful but if attained must bring many a regret and heartache in its train. Beauty is not sufficient," he went on with sterner and sterner significance, "though it were of an angelic order. There must be worth." And here his mind's eye if not that of his bodily sense, certainly followed the glance of his companion.

"I believe there is worth," the young man replied; "certainly, it is not her beauty that charms me. I do not even know if she *is* beautiful," he continued.

"And you believe you love!" the elder exclaimed after another short pause.

===Chinese===
「我并不相信那种激情足以左右一个男人的一生事业，」他的叔父答道，似乎全然没有留意对方的窘迫。「一个女人必须具备非凡的优长，才能叫一个男人有理由离开一条成功在望的路，转而踏上另一条不仅前途未卜、即便侥幸达成也必将带来诸多悔恨与心痛的路。仅仅美貌是不够的，」他越说越严厉，意味越来越深长，「即便那是天使般的美貌。她必须有值得敬重之处。」说到这里，他心中的眼——若不是肉眼的话——必定是追随着他同伴的目光而去了。

「我相信她有值得敬重之处，」年轻人答道；「当然，迷住我的并非她的美貌。我甚至不知道她*是否*称得上美。」他接着说。

「而你还相信你在爱！」稍停片刻，年长的那位失声喊道。

===Original===
There was so much of bitterness in the tone in which this was uttered, that Mandeville forgot its incredulity. "I think I must," returned he with a certain masculine naivete not out of keeping with his general style of face and manner, "else I should not be here. Three weeks ago I was satisfied with my profession, if not enthusiastic over it; today I ask nothing but to be allowed to enter upon some business that in three years' time at least will place me where I can be the fit mate of any woman in this land, that is not worth her millions."

"The woman for whom you have conceived this violent attachment is, then, above you in social position?"

"Yes, sir, or so considered, which amounts to the same thing, as far as I am concerned."

===Chinese===
这话里的苦涩之味那样浓重，以至于曼德维尔反倒忘了其中的不以为然。「我想我必须是了，」他以一种男子气的天真答道——这天真与他整个面貌举止并不相悖，「否则我不会在这里。三周以前，我对自己的本行还算满意，若说不上热烈；而今日，我只求能被允许投身一门生意，使我在至少三年之后，得以成为本国任何一位女子——只要她不是以百万计的身家——的般配伴侣。」

「那么，使你生出这等狂热依恋的女子，在社会地位上高过你？」

「是的，先生，至少人们这样认为，而就我而言，这二者并无分别。」

===Original===
"Bertram, I have lived longer than you and have seen much of both social and domestic life, and I tell you no woman is worth such a sacrifice on the part of a man as you propose. No woman of today, I should say; our mothers were different. The very fact that this young lady of whom you speak, obliges you to change your whole course of life in order to obtain her, ought to be sufficient to prove to you—" He stopped suddenly, arrested by the young man's lifted hand. "She does not oblige you, then?"

"Not on her own account, sir. This lily," lifting a vase of blossoms at his elbow, "could not be more innocent of the necessities that govern the social circle it adorns, than the pure, single-minded girl to whom I have dedicated what is best and noblest in my manhood. It is her father—"

"Ah, her father!"

===Chinese===
「伯特兰，我活得比你久，见过许多社交与家庭生活，我告诉你，没有哪一个女人值得一个男人如你这般牺牲。应当说，当今没有一个女人值得；我们的母亲那一代不同。你所说的这位小姐竟要你改变整个人生道路才能得到她，仅凭这一点，就应当足以向你证明——」他猛地停住，被年轻人举起的手止住了。「那么，她并没有要你这样做？」

「不是为她自己，先生。这株百合，」他举起手边一瓶盛放的花，「对于主宰它所点缀的社交圈的种种规矩不闻不问；而我以男子之身为之奉献一切最善最美之物的那个纯洁、心思单纯的姑娘，其不闻不问也不过如此。是她的父亲——」

「啊，她的父亲！」

===Original===
"Yes, sir," the young man pursued, more and more astonished at the other's tone. "He is a man who has a right to expect both wealth and position in a son-in-law. But I see I shall have to tell you my story, sir. It is an uncommon one and I never meant that it should pass my lips, but if by its relation I can win your sympathy for a pure and noble passion, I shall consider the sacred seal of secrecy broken in a good cause. But," said he, seeing his uncle cast a short and uneasy glance at the door, "perhaps I am interrupting you. You expect someone!"

"No," said his uncle, "my wife is at church; I am ready to listen."

The young man gave a hurried sigh, cast one look at his companion's immovable face, as if to assure himself that the narrative was necessary, then leaned back and in a steady businesslike tone that softened, however, as he proceeded, began to relate as follows:

===Chinese===
「是的，先生，」年轻人继续说，对另一位语调中的异样越来越惊异。「他是一位有权指望女婿既富且贵的人。但我看，我只好把我的故事告诉您了，先生。这是一桩不寻常的故事，我本从未打算让它出口，可若是讲述出来能为您对一份纯朴高贵的激情生出几分同情，我便算是为一个正当理由打破那神圣的秘密之印了。不过，」他说，见叔父朝门那边投去短促而不安的一眼，「也许我打扰您了。您在等人！」

「没有，」他的叔父说，「我妻子去做礼拜了；我愿意听你说。」

年轻人匆匆叹了一口气，朝同伴那张纹丝不动的脸看了一眼，仿佛要确认这一番叙述确有必要，然后向后一靠，以一种沉稳而公事公办般的腔调开始讲述——不过随着讲述的推进，那腔调渐渐柔和了下来。他是这样说的：
