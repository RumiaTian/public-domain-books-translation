## XIV Miss Belinda Has a Question to Decide / 十四 贝琳达小姐有一桩问题要决断

===Original===
> "I pray you in your letters,

 Speak of me as I am; nothing extenuate,

 Nor set down aught in malice."

===Chinese===
> 「我求你在你的信里，

 如实谈论我；不要有所粉饰，

 也不要怀着恶意记下任何事。」

===Original===
Miss Belinda sitting before her bedroom fire on a certain windy night in January, presented a picture of the most profound thought. A year had elapsed since, with heavy heart and moistened eye, she had bidden goodbye to the child of her care, and beheld her drift away with her new friend into a strange and untried life. And now a letter had come from that friend, in which with the truest appreciation for the feelings of herself and sister, he requested their final permission to adopt Paula as his own child and the future occupant of his house and heart.

===Chinese===
一月里一个风声呼啸的夜晚，贝琳达小姐坐在卧室的炉火前，呈现出一副陷入最深沉思索的模样。距她怀着沉重的心情、含着湿润的双眼，向自己一手照料的那个孩子道别，看着她随她的新朋友漂向一种陌生而未经尝试的生活，已过去了一年。如今那位朋友来了一封信，信中以对她和姐姐的心意最为周到的体谅，请求她们最终允准他将宝拉（Paula）收为亲生女儿，使之日后成为他宅邸与心田的女主人。

===Original===
Yes, after a year of increased comfort, Mrs. Sylvester, who would never have consented to receive as her own any child demanding care or attention, had decided it was quite a different matter to give place and position to a lovely girl already grown, whose beauty was sufficiently pronounced to do credit to the family while at the same time it was of a character to heighten by contrast her own very manifest attractions. So the letter, destined to create such a disturbance in the stern and powerful mind of Miss Belinda, had been written and dispatched.

===Original===
And indeed it was matter for the gravest reflection. To accede to this important request was to yield up all control over the dear young girl whose affection had constituted the brightness of this somewhat disappointed life, while to refuse an offer made with such evident love and anxiety, was to bring a pang of regret to a heart she hesitated to wound. The question of advantage which might have swayed others in their decision, did not in the least affect Miss Belinda. Now that Paula had seen the world and gained an insight into certain studies beyond the reach of her own attainments, any wishes in which she might have indulged on that score were satisfied, and mere wealth with its concomitant of luxuriant living, she regarded with distrust, and rather in the light of a stumbling-block to the great and grand end of all existence.

===Chinese===
这的确是件最该郑重思量的事。应下这桩重要的请求，便是放弃对那位亲爱少女的一切掌控——正是她的爱，构成了这略带失意的一生的光彩；而拒绝一份怀着如此明显的爱与焦灼而提出的好意，又会给一颗她不忍伤害的心带来悔恨的刺痛。那些本可左右旁人决断的利益之念，对贝琳达小姐却毫无影响。宝拉既已见过世面，又在若干远非她贝琳达所能传授的学问上开了眼界，那么她在这上头本可抱有的任何心愿也都满足了；至于单凭财富及其带来的奢靡生活，她素来心存戒备，倒更愿把它看作横亘在一切生存之崇高终极目标前的一块绊脚石。

It was full of letters; those same honest epistles, which, as empowered by Mr. Sylvester, she had requested Paula to send her from week to week. Certain passages she marked, and when she had finished the pile, she took up the letters again and reread those passages. They were necessarily desultory in their character, but they all had, in her mind at least, a bearing upon the question on hand, and as such, I give them to my readers.
