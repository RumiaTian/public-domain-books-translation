## Dedication / 题献

===Original===
To

**My father**

I dedicate this book

as expressing some of the principles of

justice and mercy which,

by precept and example, he has

instilled into my

breast

from early childhood.

===Chinese===
谨将本书献给

**我的父亲**

我以此书表达

他藉言传与身教、

自幼年以来

便灌输于

我心中的

那些

正义与仁慈的原则。
