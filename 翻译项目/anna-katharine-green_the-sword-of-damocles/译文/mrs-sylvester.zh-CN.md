## VII Mrs. Sylvester / 七 西尔维斯特夫人

===Original===
> Love is more pleasant than marriage, for the same reason that romances are more amusing than history.

> "He draweth out the thread of his verbosity, finer than the staple of his argument."

===Chinese===
> 恋爱之所以比婚姻更惬意，正与传奇之比历史更饶有趣味，同属一理。

> 「他将自己那番絮叨的线头，抽得比他论点的本料还要细密。」

===Original===
Young Mandeville having finished his story, looked at his uncle. He found him sitting in an attitude of extreme absorption, his right arm stretched before him on the table, his face bent thoughtfully downwards and clouded with that deep melancholy that seemed its most natural expression, "He has not heard me," was the young man's first mortifying reflection. But catching his uncle's eye which at that moment raised itself, he perceived he was mistaken and that he had rather been listened to only too well.

===Chinese===
小曼德维尔把他的故事讲究，便望着他的叔父。只见叔父正坐在那里，一副出神到了极点的姿态——右臂伸在面前的桌上，脸若有所思地俯着，笼着那一重仿佛才是它最自然神情的深深忧郁。「他根本没在听。」这是年轻人头一个叫人难堪的念头。可这当儿他撞上了叔父抬起的目光，这才明白自己错了，倒不如说是对方听得太过仔细了。

===Original===
"You must forgive me if I have seemed to rhapsodize," the young man stammered. "You were so quiet I half forgot I had a listener and went on much as I would if I had been thinking aloud."

His uncle smiled and throwing off the weight of his reflections whatever they might be, arose and began pacing the floor. "I see you are past surgery," quoth he, "any wisdom of mine would be only thrown away."

===Chinese===
「您得原谅我，我好像说着说着便入了狂态。」年轻人结结巴巴地说。「您那样安静，我几乎忘了跟前还有个听客，便一路说下去，跟自己出声想着心事一般无二。」

叔父微微一笑，抖落那不论是什么的心头重负，站起身来，在屋里踱起了步。「我看你已是无药可救的了。」他说，「我这点儿见识，落在你身上也只是白费。」

===Original===
Young Mandeville was hurt. He had expected some token of approval on his uncle's part, or at least some betrayal of sympathy. His looks expressed his disappointment.

"You expected to convert me by this story," continued the elder, pausing with a certain regret before his nephew; "nothing could convert me but—"

"What?" inquired Mandeville after waiting in vain for the other to finish.

===Chinese===
小曼德维尔心里受伤了。他原盼着叔父多少给他几句首肯，至少也该露一点同情之意才好。他面上的神情已把那份失望流露了出来。

「你原指望用这一番话来说服我。」长者接着往下说，带着一种遗憾在他侄子跟前停下，「可什么也说服不了我，除非——」

「除非什么？」曼德维尔等了半晌不见对方说下去，便追问了一句。

===Original===
"Something which we will never find in the whirl of New York fashionable life. A woman with faith to reward and soul to understand such unqualified trust as yours."

"But I believe Miss Preston is such a girl and will be such a woman. Her looks, her last words prove it."

===Chinese===
「一样在纽约时尚圈那股涡流里头，我们永远寻不见的东西。一个既有信义值得回报、又有性灵能懂得你这份全无保留之信托的女子。」

「可我相信普雷斯顿小姐（Miss Preston）正是这样的姑娘，将来也会是这样的女子。她的神色，她最后那几句话，都可为证。」

===Original===
"Nothing proves it but time and as for your belief, I have believed too." Then as if fearing he had said too much, assumed his most businesslike tone and observed, "But we will drop all that; you have resolved to quit music and enter Wall Street, your object money and the social consideration which money secures. Now, why Wall Street?"

"Because I can think of no other means for attaining what I desire, in the space of time I would consent to keep a young lady of Miss Preston's position waiting."

===Chinese===
「除了时间，什么都证明不了；至于你这份信念，我也曾信过。」他像是怕自己说得太多，便摆出他最像办正经事的那副腔调，说道：「可这些我们撇开不提；你已铁了心要丢开音乐、进华尔街（Wall Street）去，求的无非是钱，以及钱财所能换来的人前体面。那么，为何偏偏是华尔街？」

「因为在我肯让普雷斯顿小姐那样身份的小姐等候我的这段时日里，我想不出还有别的什么法子，能叫我在这么短的工夫里得到我所求的东西。」

===Original===
"Humph! and you have money, I suppose, which you propose to risk on the hazard?"

"Some! enough to start with; a small amount to you, but sufficient if I am fortunate."

"And if you are not?"

The young man opened his arms with an expressive gesture, "I am done for, that is all."

===Chinese===
「哼！那么你手头是有钱的了，我料想，预备拿去在这场赌注上碰运气？」

「有一些！够开个头；在您眼里自是小数，可若我走运，也就够了。」

「可你若不走运呢？」

年轻人把两只手一摊，那姿势胜过千言万语：「那我就完了，不过如此。」

===Original===
"Bertram," his uncle exclaimed with a change of tone, "has it ever struck you that Mr. Preston might have as strong a prejudice against speculation as against the musical profession?"

"No, that is, pardon me but I have sometimes thought that even in the event of success I should have to struggle against his inherited instincts of caste and his natural dislike of all things new, even wealth, but I never thought of the possibility of my arousing his distrust by speculating in stocks and engaging in enterprises so nearly in accord with his own business operations."

===Chinese===
「伯特兰。」叔父换了一种口气唤道，「你可曾想过，普雷斯顿先生对于投机一道，也许怀着同他对音乐行当一般强烈的成见？」

「没有；也就是说，恕我直言——我倒时而想过，即便我侥幸得手，也仍要同他那出身门第的天生做派、同他之于一切新事物——哪怕是新近得来的财富——的天生反感去周旋；可我从没想过，自己竟会因炒股、因插手那些与他自己的买卖如此相近的营生，而惹起他的疑忌。」

===Original===
"Yet if I guess aright you would run greater risk of losing the support of his countenance by following the hazardous course you propose, than if you continued in the line of art that now engages you."

"Do you know—"

"I know nothing, but I fear the chances, Bertram."

"Then I am already defeated and must give up my hopes of happiness."

===Chinese===
「然而若我所料不错，你若当真循你所拟的这条险路走下去，便比继续从事你眼下所从事的艺术一道，更可能失去他那一份青眼相看。」

「您莫非知道——」

「我一无所知，伯特兰；我只是怕那些个可能。」

「那么我已然落败，只好舍去我这幸福的指望了。」

===Original===
A smile thin and indefinable crossed the other's face. "No," said he, "not necessarily." And sitting down by his nephew's side, he asked if he had any objections to enter a bank. "In a good capacity," he exclaimed.

"No indeed; it would be an opportunity surpassing my hopes. Do you know of an opening?"

"Well," said he, "under the circumstances I will let you into the secret of my own affairs. I have always had one ambition, and that was to be at the head of a bank. I have not said much about it, but for the last five years I have been working to this end, and today you see me the possessor of at least three-fourths of the stock of the Madison Bank. It has been deteriorating for some time, consequently I was enabled to buy it low, but now that I have got it I intend to build up the concern. I am able to throw business of an important nature in its way, and I dare prophesy that before the year is out you will see it reestablished upon a solid and influential footing."

===Chinese===
一缕又淡又说不清的笑意，掠过了那一位的脸。「不然，」他说，「未必便至此。」他在侄子身旁坐下，问他可有什么不肯进一家银行的想头。「职位要紧的那种。」他补了一句。

「哪儿的话；那便是远过我所望的好机会了。您可知道哪儿有这样一个空缺？」

「也罢，」他说，「到了这步田地，我便把自家这点私事对你略提一提。我一向有一桩心愿，便是做一家银行的头儿。这话我未曾多讲，可这五年来我一直为着这桩心愿用力，到今日，你瞧我已是麦迪逊银行（Madison Bank）至少四分之三股份的主人了。这家银行走下坡路已有一些时日，故而我得以贱价收进；可如今既已到了手，我便有意把它重整一番。我能给它的道上引去几桩要紧的生意，我敢预言，不出年底，你便要看见它重新在一个稳固而有力的根基上立起来。」

===Original===
"I have no doubt of it, sir; you have the knack of success, anything that you touch is sure to go straight."

"Unhappily yes, as far as business operations go. But no matter about that;" as if the other had introduced some topic incongruous to the one they were considering "the point is this. In two weeks time I shall be elected President of the Bank; if you will accept the position of assistant cashier—the best I can offer in consideration of your total ignorance of all details of the business—it is open to you—"

===Chinese===
「这个我毫不怀疑，先生；您自有那马到成功的本领，凡是您手所触的，没有不顺顺当当办成的。」

「那倒也是不幸而言中，就买卖一道而言。可这话不必提了；」仿佛对方把一桩与眼下的议题全不相干的话头扯了进来「要紧的是这一桩：再过两个星期，我便要被举为这家银行的行长了；你若肯就助理出纳员一职——念在你于这门生意的种种细节一概全然不知，这是我所能给你的最好的差使——这职位便是给你留着的——」

===Original===
"Uncle! how generous! I—"

"Hush! your duties will be nominal, the present cashier is fully competent; but the leisure thus afforded will offer you abundant opportunity to make yourself acquainted with all matters connected with the banking system as well as with such capitalists as it would be well for you to know. So that when the occasion comes, I can raise you to the cashier's place or make such other disposal of your talents as will best insure your rapid advance."

===Chinese===
「叔父！您真是慷慨！我——」

「嘘！你那份差使不过是个虚名，眼下那位出纳员完全能担当得起；可这么一来，你所赢得的余暇，便足给你许多机会，去熟悉银行业务种种相关事宜，去结识那些你理当结识的资本家。如此一来，待到时机一到，我便可提拔你做出纳员，或把你这点才干另行安置到一个最能保你飞黄腾达的位子上。」

===Original===
The young man's eyes sparkled; with a sudden impetuous movement he jumped to his feet and grasped his uncle's hand. "I can never thank you enough; you have made me your debtor for life. Now let anyone ask me who is my father, and I will say—"

"He was Edward Sylvester's brother. But come, come, this extreme gratitude is unnecessary. You have always been a favorite with me, Bertram, and now that I have no child, you seem doubly near; it is my pleasure to do what I can for you. But—" and here he surveyed him with a wistful look, "I wish you were entering into this new line from love of the business rather than love of a woman. I fear for you my boy. It is an awful thing to stake one's future upon a single chance and that chance a woman's faith. If she should fail you after you had compassed your fortune, should die—well you could bear that perhaps; but if she turned false, and married someone else, or even married you and then—"

===Chinese===
年轻人的两眼放出光来；他猛地一个情急的动作跳起身，一把攥住了叔父的手。「我就是再谢您也谢不尽；您叫我这辈子都还不清您的情了。如今谁要再问我的父亲是谁，我便答——」

「他是爱德华·西尔维斯特（Edward Sylvester）的兄弟。可得了，得了，这番过分的感激大可不必。你一向是我心头的偏爱，伯特兰；如今我膝下无子，你便显得加倍地亲近。能为你做点什么，原是我所乐意的。只是——」说到这里，他端详着侄子，目光里有一丝怅惘，「我倒宁愿你进这一行，是为着爱这门生意，而不是为着爱一个女子。我替你捏一把汗，我的孩子。把自己的一生，押在一注独一的彩头上，而这彩头又是一个女子的信义——这真是一桩可怕的事。倘若你功成名就之后她辜负了你，若是她死了——唔，这个你或者还受得住；可她若变了心，另嫁了别人，甚或嫁了你，然后——」

===Original===
"What?" came in silvery accents from the door, and a woman richly clad, her trailing velvets filling the air at once with an oppressive perfume, entered the room and paused before them in an attitude meant to be arch, but which from the massiveness of her figure and the scornful carriage of her head, succeeded in being simply imperious.

Mr. Sylvester rose abruptly as if unpleasantly surprised. "Ona!" he exclaimed, hastening, however, to cover his embarassment by a courteous acknowledgement of her presence and a careless remark concerning the shortness of the services that had allowed her to return from church so early. "I did not hear you come in," he observed.

===Chinese===
「然后什么？」一个清脆的声音自门口传来。一个衣着华贵的女子走了进来——那一袭曳地的丝绒，立时教满室弥漫起一股浓得压人的香气——她到了他们跟前停下，原想摆出一副娇俏俏的姿态，可她身量厚重、头颅又是那样一付睥睨的神气，到头来只落得一个凛然不可犯。

西尔维斯特先生猛地站起，仿佛吃了一记不甚愉快的惊。「奥娜（Ona）！」他失声唤道，旋即赶忙以一番客气地迎她进来的礼数，并随口一句关于今日礼拜简短、所以她才能这么早从教堂回来的闲话，来掩饰自己的失态。「我没听见你进来。」他说。

===Original===
"No, I judge not," she returned with a side glance at Mandeville. "But the services were not short, on the contrary I thought I should never hear the last amen. Mr. Turner's voice is very agreeable," she went on, in a rambling manner all her own, "it never interferes with your thoughts; not that I am considered as having any," she interjected with another glance at their silent guest, "a woman in society with a reputation for taste in all matters connected with fashionable living, has no thoughts of course; business men with only one idea in their heads, that of making money, have more no doubt. Do you know, Edward," she went on with sudden inconsequence, which was another trait of this amiable lady's conversation, "that I have quite come to a conclusion in regard to the girl Philip Longtree is going to marry; she may be pretty, but she does not know how to dress. I wish you could have seen her tonight; she had on mauve with old gold trimmings. Now with one of her complexion—But I forget you haven't seen her. Bertram, I think I shall give a German next month, will you come? Oh, Edward!" as if the thought had suddenly struck her, "Princess Louise *is* the sixth child of Queen Victoria; I asked Mr. Turner tonight. By the way, I wonder if it will be pleasant enough to take the horses out tomorrow? Bird has been obliging enough to get sick just in the height of the season, Mr. Mandeville. There are a thousand things I have got to do and I hate hired horses." And with a petulant sigh she laid her prayerbook on the table and with a glance in the mirror near by, began pulling off her gloves in the slow and graceful fashion eminently in keeping with her every movement.

===Chinese===
「是啊，我看你也没听见。」她答道，斜斜地瞟了曼德维尔一眼。「可那礼拜并不短，恰恰相反，我只当那一声末了的『阿们』我永远等不到了。特纳先生（Mr. Turner）的嗓音倒十分悦耳，」她以一种她所独有的、信马由缰的口气接着说，「它从不来搅乱你自己的念头；倒不是说我这人会被当作有什么念头就是了，」她又瞟了那位默然不语的客人一眼，插了这么一句，「一个身在社交场中、素有时尚起居种种雅鉴之名的女子，当然是没有念头的；倒是那些脑子里只有一个念头——赚钱的生意人，想必还多一些。你可知道，爱德华，」她话锋一转，又冒出一句没头没脑的话来——这原也是这位和蔼夫人谈吐的一大脾性——「关于菲利普·朗特里（Philip Longtree）要娶的那位姑娘，我已大抵有了定见；她人或许生得不丑，可她不懂得穿戴。但愿你能见她今夜的打扮；她竟穿了一身雪青色，镶的是古金色的边。以她那样的肤色——罢了，我忘了你还没见过她。伯特兰，下个月我打算办一场德国舞会（German），你来不来？啊，爱德华！」她像是忽然想起什么，「路易丝公主（Princess Louise）果然是维多利亚女王（Queen Victoria）的第六个孩子；我今夜问了特纳先生。对了，不知明日天好不好，能不能把马套出去？伯德（Bird）倒真会凑趣，偏偏在这大忙的当口病了起来，曼德维尔先生。我手头足有上千桩事要办，偏偏我又最恨雇来的马。」她烦躁地叹了口气，把那本祈祷书搁在桌上，又朝近旁的镜子里照了一眼，这才以一种与她一举一动无不相称的、又慢又优雅的做派，开始褪她那双手套。

===Original===
It was as if an atmosphere of worldliness had settled down upon this room sanctified a moment before by the utterances of a pure and noble love. Mr. Sylvester looked uneasy, while Bertram searched in vain for something to say.

"I seem to have brought a blight," she suddenly murmured in an easy tone somewhat at variance with the glance of half veiled suspicion which she darted from under her heavy lids, at first one and then the other of the two gentlemen before her. "No, I will not sit," she added as her husband offered her a chair. "I am tired almost to death and would retire immediately, but I interrupted you I believe in the utterance of some wise saying about matrimony. It is an interesting subject and I have a notion to hear what one so well qualified to speak in regard to it—" and here she made a slow, half lazy courtesy to her husband with a look that might mean anything from coquetry to defiance—"has to say to a young man like Mr. Mandeville."

===Chinese===
这间屋子方才还被一番纯洁而高尚的爱情的言辞所圣化，此刻却仿佛落下了满满一层世俗的浊气。西尔维斯特先生面有不安之色，伯特兰则想找句话来说，却什么也找不出来。

「我好像带来了一阵阴霾似的。」她忽而以一种从容的口气咕哝了一句，那口气与她那双沉甸甸的眼皮下射出来的、半含猜疑的一瞥，颇有几分不相称——她那一瞥先投向这位、又投向她跟前两位先生中的那一位。「不，我不坐，」她丈夫让了她一把椅子，她又补上一句。「我累得要死，这就想回房去了；可我方才打断了你们，记得你们正要说一句什么关于婚姻之道的高论来着。这个题目倒也有趣，我倒想听听，一位在这题目上如此够资格说话的人——」说到这里，她朝她丈夫慢条斯理、有几分慵懒地蹲了一礼，那一眼的神情，从卖弄到挑衅，什么意味都未尝不可——「对一位像曼德维尔先生这样的年轻人，到底要说些什么。」

===Original===
Edward Sylvester who was regarded as an autocrat among men, and who certainly was an acknowledged leader in any company he chose to enter, bowed his head before this anomalous glance with a gesture of something like submission.

"One is not called upon to repeat every inadvertent phrase he may utter," said he. "Bertram was consulting me upon certain topics and—"

"You answered him in your own brilliant style," she concluded. "What did you say?" she asked in another moment in a low unmoved tone which the final act of smoothing out her gloves on the table with hands delicate as white rose leaves but firm as marble, did not either hasten or retard.

===Chinese===
爱德华·西尔维斯特在人丛中向来被视为一个独断专行之人，无论他愿意踏入哪一种交际场合，他都无疑地是一位公认的领袖；可在这一道古怪的注视之下，他却低下了头，那姿态里颇有一点近乎俯首听命。

「一个人并不必把他不慎脱口而出的每一句话，都复述一遍。」他说，「伯特兰不过是在几桩事上同我商量商量，而——」

「而您便用您那一向漂亮动人的做派答了他。」她替他续完了那一句。「您说了什么来着？」过了一会儿她又问，声音低低的、无动于衷的；而她那把双手——娇嫩得像白玫瑰的花瓣、却又坚执得像大理石——把那双手套在桌上一片片抚平的末了一个动作，既不曾把这问话催快一分，也不曾把它拖慢一拍。

===Original===
"Oh if you insist," he returned lightly, "and are willing to bear the reflection my unfortunate remark seems to cast upon the sex, I was merely observing to my nephew, that the man who centered all his hopes upon a woman's faith, was liable to disappointment. Even if he succeeded in marrying her there were still possibilities of his repenting any great sacrifice made in her behalf."

"Indeed!" and for once the delicate cheek flushed deeper than its rouge. "And why do you say this?" she inquired, dropping her coquettish manner and flashing upon them both, the haughty and implacable woman Bertram had always believed her to be, notwithstanding her vagaries and fashion.

===Chinese===
「噢，你既要追根问底，」他轻描淡写地答道，「又愿意担下我那句倒霉话似乎加在贵性别身上的那种指摘，那我不过是对我侄子说：一个男子若把他全部指望都放在一个女子的信义上，便难免要尝失望的滋味。就算他终于娶到了她，为他这一番而作的任何大牺牲，他日后也仍有可能懊悔。」
