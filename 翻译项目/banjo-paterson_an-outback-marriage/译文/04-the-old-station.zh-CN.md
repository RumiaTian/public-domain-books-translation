## IV The Old Station / 老牧场

===Original===
There are few countries in the world with such varieties of climate as Australia, and though some stations are out in the great, red-hot, frying wastes of the Never-Never, others are up in the hills where a hot night is a thing unknown, where snow falls occasionally, and where it is no uncommon thing to spend a summer's evening by the side of a roaring fire. In the matter of improvements, too, stations vary greatly. Some are in a wilderness, with fittings to match; others have telephones between homestead and outstations, the jackeroos dress for dinner, and the station hands are cowed into touching their hats and saying "Sir." Also stations are of all sizes, and the man who is considered quite a big squatter in the settled districts is thought small potatoes by the magnate "out back," who shears a hundred and fifty thousand sheep, and has an overdraft like the National Debt.

===Chinese===
世界上气候如此多样的国家屈指可数，澳大利亚便是其中之一。有些牧场坐落在"Never-Never"——那片炽热灼人的红色荒原——深处，另一些则栖身于山间，那里的夜晚从不闷热，偶尔还会飘雪，人们在盛夏之夜围坐在熊熊燃烧的炉火旁也并非罕事。在设施方面，各牧场也大相径庭。有些荒凉得像片蛮荒，配套也一般；另一些则在主牧场和外围牧场之间装了电话，年轻牧工（jackeroos）要穿正装吃晚餐，牧场雇工被治得服服帖帖，见人就脱帽致敬、口称"先生"。牧场的规模也大小不一，在定居区被视为大牧场主（squatter）的人，到了"内陆"大亨眼里不过是小角色——那位大亨剪着十五万只羊的毛，透支额度堪比国债。

===Original===
Kuryong was a hill-country station of about sixty thousand acres all told; but they were good acres, as no one knew better than old Bully Grant, the owner, of whose history and disposition we heard something from Pinnock at the club. It was a highly improved place, with a fine homestead—thanks to Bully Grant's money, for in the old days it had been a very different sort of place—and its history is typical of the history of hundreds of others.

===Chinese===
库里昂（Kuryong）是一座山地牧场（station），总面积约六万英亩；但这些英亩都是好地，没有人比它的主人老"恶霸"格兰特（Bully Grant）更清楚这一点——我们在俱乐部里从平诺克（Pinnock）那里听说过他的一些事迹和性情。这是一处设施精良的地方，有一座漂亮的主牧场宅邸——全靠"恶霸"格兰特的钱，因为在早年间这里完全是另一副模样——它的历史也是成百上千座牧场的典型缩影。

===Original===
When Andrew Gordon first bought it, it was held under lease from the Crown, and there were no improvements to speak of. The station homestead, so lovingly descanted upon in the advertisement, consisted of a two-roomed slab hut; the woolshed, where the sheep were shorn, was made of gumtree trunks roofed with bark. The wool went down to Sydney, and station supplies came back, in huge wagons drawn by eighteen or twenty bullocks, that travelled nine miles a day on a journey of three hundred miles. There were no neighbours except at the township of Kiley's Crossing, which consisted of two public-houses and a store. It was a rough life for the young squatter, and evidently he found it lonely; for on a visit to Sydney he fell in love with and married a dainty girl of French descent. Refined, well-educated, and fragile-looking, she seemed about the last person in the world to take out to a slab-hut homestead as a squatter's wife. But there is an old saying that blood will tell; and with all the courage of her Huguenot ancestry she faced the roughness and discomforts of bush life. On her arrival at the station the old two-roomed hut was plastered and whitewashed, additional rooms were built, and quite a neat little home was the result. Seasons were good, and the young squatter might have gone on shearing sheep and selling fat stock till the end of his life but for the advent of free selection in 1861.

===Chinese===
安德鲁·戈登（Andrew Gordon）最初买下这里时，它是从王室租来的，几乎谈不上什么改良。广告中被深情讴歌的牧场主宅邸，不过是两间木板搭的小屋；剪羊毛的棚屋是用桉树干做支架、树皮当屋顶盖起来的。羊毛运往悉尼，牧场物资则由十八到二十头公牛拉的大篷车运回，每天走九英里，三百英里的路程要走上好一阵子。除了基利十字镇（Kiley's Crossing）——那里只有两家酒馆和一家杂货铺——附近没有邻居。对这个年轻的牧场主来说，日子过得很艰难，而且他显然觉得孤单；因为去悉尼时，他爱上了一位血统有法国渊源的精致姑娘并娶了她。她文雅、教养良好、看上去弱不禁风，似乎是世界上最不可能被带到一间木板小屋里当牧场主妻子的人。但老话说得好，血统终究会显露；她以胡格诺（Huguenot）祖先传给她的全部勇气，直面了丛林生活的粗粝和不适。她到达牧场后，那间两间的老屋被粉刷一新，又加盖了房间，最终成了一个整洁的小家。风调雨顺，年轻的牧场主本可以就这样剪一辈子羊毛、卖一辈子肥牛，要不是1861年自由选择法（free selection）的实施。

===Original===
In that year the Legislature threw open all leasehold lands to the public for purchase on easy terms and conditions. The idea was to settle an industrious peasantry on lands hitherto leased in large blocks to the squatters. This brought down a flood of settlement on Kuryong. At the top end of the station there was a chain of mountains, and the country was rugged and patchy—rich valleys alternating with ragged hills. Here and there about the run were little patches of specially good land, which were soon snapped up. The pioneers of these small settlers were old Morgan Donohoe and his wife, who had built the hotel at Kiley's Crossing; and, on their reports, all their friends and relatives, as they came out of the "ould country," worked their way to Kuryong, and built little bits of slab and bark homesteads in among the mountains. The rougher the country, the better they liked it. They were a horse-thieving, sheep-stealing breed, and the talents which had made them poachers in the old country soon made them champion bushmen in their new surroundings. The leader of these mountain settlers was one Doyle, a gigantic Irishman, who had got a grant of a few hundred acres in the mountains, and had taken to himself a Scotch wife from among the free immigrants. The story ran that he was too busy to go to town, but asked a friend to go and pick a wife for him, "a fine shtrappin' woman, wid a good brisket on her."

===Chinese===
那一年，立法机关将所有租借地向公众开放，以优惠条件出售。其初衷是让勤劳的农民定居在以往被大牧场主整块租用的土地上。这给库里昂带来了大量移民潮。牧场的尽头是一脉山脉，地形崎岖、参差不齐——肥沃的山谷与嶙峋的山丘交替出现。牧场各处散布着几小块特别好的土地，很快就被抢购一空。这些小定居者的先驱是老摩根·多诺霍（Morgan Donohoe）和他的妻子，他们建了基利十字镇上的那家酒馆；凭借他们的宣传，所有从"老 country"出来的亲友都一路辗转来到库里昂，在山间搭起一间间木板树皮小屋安了家。越是荒凉的地方，他们越是喜欢。他们是偷马偷羊的行家，当年在老家当偷猎者的本事，到了新环境里很快就让他们成了顶尖的丛林好手。这些山地定居者的头领是一个叫多尔（Doyle）的爱尔兰大汉，他在山里弄到了几百英亩土地，还从自由移民中娶了一个苏格兰老婆。传说他忙得没空进城，就让一个朋友去替他挑老婆，"要个高大壮实的女人，胸部要好看。"

===Original===
The Doyles were large, slow, heavy men, with an instinct for the management of cattle; they were easily distinguished from the Donohoes, who were little red-whiskered men, enterprising and quick-witted, and ready to do anything in the world for a good horse. Other strangers and outlanders came to settle in the district, but from the original settlement up to the date of our story the two great families of the Doyles and the Donohoes governed the neighbourhood, and the headquarters of the clans was at Donohoe's "Shamrock Hotel," at Kiley's Crossing. Here they used to rendezvous when they went away down to the plains country each year for the shearing; for they added to their resources by travelling about the country shearing, droving, fencing, tanksinking, or doing any other job that offered itself, but always returned to their mountain fastnesses ready for any bit of work "on the cross" (i.e., unlawful) that might turn up. When times got hard they had a handy knack of finding horses that nobody had lost, shearing sheep they did not own, and branding and selling other people's calves.

===Chinese===
多尔家（Doyles）的人都是身材高大、动作迟缓、体格厚重的家伙，天生会管牛；他们跟多诺霍家（Donohoes）很容易区分——后者都是些红胡子小个子，精力充沛、头脑灵活，为了匹好马什么都肯干。还有其他外来人和异乡人来这一带定居，但从最初的拓荒到我们故事发生的年代，多尔家和多诺霍家这两大望族一直统治着这一带，两族的大本营就设在基利十字镇多诺霍家的"三叶草酒馆"。每年他们去平原地区剪羊毛之前，都会在这里集合；因为他们靠走南闯北做剪羊毛、赶牛、围栏、挖蓄水池或任何找得到的活计来贴补收入，但总是回到他们的山间堡垒，随时准备接任何"来路不正"（即非法）的活儿。日子艰难时，他们有一手绝活——找到没人报失的马、剪不是自己的羊的毛、给别人的牛犊打上自己的印记再卖掉。

===Original===
When they stole stock, they moved them on through the mountains as quickly as possible, always having a brother or uncle, or a cousin—Terry or Timothy or Martin or Patsy—who had a holding "beyant." By these means they could shift stolen stock across the great range, and dispose of them among the peaceable folk who dwelt in the good country on the other side, whose stock they stole in return. Many a good horse and fat beast had made the stealthy mountain journey, lying hidden in gaps and gullies when pursuit grew hot, and being moved on as things quieted down.

===Chinese===
偷了牲口之后，他们就尽快赶着翻山越岭，总有个兄弟、叔叔或表亲——特里、蒂莫西、马丁或帕齐——在"那边"有块地。用这种法子，他们能把赃物赶过大山脉，卖给山那边好地方的安分人，同时又偷那些人的牲口。不知有多少好马和肥牛走过这条隐秘的山路，追查风声紧时就藏在山缝沟壑里，等风头过了再赶路。

===Original===
Another striking feature was the way in which they got themselves mixed up with each other. Their names were so tangled up that no one could keep tally of them. There was a Red Mick Donohoe (son of the old publican), and his cousin Black Mick Donohoe, and Red Mick's son Mick, and Black Mick's son Mick, and Red Mick's son Pat, and Black Mick's son Pat; and there was Gammy Doyle (meaning Doyle with the lame leg), and Scrammy Doyle (meaning Doyle with the injured arm), and Bosthoon Doyle and Omadhaun Doyle—a Bosthoon being a man who never had any great amount of sense to speak of, while an Omadhaun is a man who began life with some sense, but lost most of it on his journey. It was a common saying in the countryside that if you met a man on the mountains you should say, "Good day, Doyle," and if he replied, "That's not my name," you should at once say, "Well, I meant no offence, Mr. Donohoe."

===Chinese===
另一个引人注目的特点是他们彼此之间的名字纠缠不清，根本分不清谁是谁。有红米克·多诺霍（Red Mick Donohoe，老酒馆老板的儿子），他的堂兄弟黑米克·多诺霍（Black Mick Donohoe），红米克的儿子米克（Mick），黑米克的儿子米克，红米克的儿子帕特（Pat），黑米克的儿子帕特；还有跛脚多尔（Gammy Doyle，意思是腿有残疾的多尔）、伤臂多尔（Scrammy Doyle，意思是胳膊受伤的多尔）、糊涂多尔（Bosthoon Doyle）和蠢蛋多尔（Omadhaun Doyle）——"Bosthoon"指一个从来就没什么脑子的人，而"Omadhaun"指一个生来有些脑子、但在人生旅途中把大部分都丢了的人。乡间有句老话：你在山里碰到一个人，就说"你好，多尔"，如果他回答"那不是我的名字"，你就马上说"哦，那恕我冒犯了，多诺霍先生"。

===Original===
One could generally pick which was which of the original stock, but when they came to intermarry there was no telling t'other from which. Startling likenesses cropped up among the relatives, and it was widely rumoured that one Doyle who was known to be in jail, and who was vaguely spoken of by the clan as being "away," was in fact serving an accumulation of sentences for himself and other members of the family, whose sins he had for a consideration taken on himself.

===Chinese===
一般来说，还能分辨出最初的那些人谁是谁谁，但到了他们互相通婚的时候，就彻底分不清了。亲属之间冒出了惊人的相似面孔，而且有传言说，一个众所周知在蹲牢房的多尔——族人含糊地说他"出远门了"——实际上是在替自己和其他家族成员服刑，那些人的罪过他都一并扛了，当然，是有"报酬"的。

===Original===
With such neighbours as these fighting him for every block of land, Andrew Gordon soon came to the end of his resources, and it was then that he had to take in his old manager as a partner. Before Bully Grant had been in the firm long, he had secured nearly all the good land, and the industrious yeomanry that the Land Act was supposed to create were hiding away up the gullies on miserable little patches of bad land, stealing sheep for a living. Bully fought them stoutly, impounded their sheep and cattle, and prosecuted trespassers and thieves; and, his luck being wonderful, he soon added to the enormous fortune he had made in mining, while Andrew Gordon died impoverished. When he died, old Bully gave the management of the stations to his sons, and contented himself with finding fault. But one dimly-remembered episode in his career was talked of by the old hands around Kiley's Hotel, long after Grant had become a wealthy man, and had gone for long trips to England.

===Chinese===
有这样一群为了每一小块土地跟他争抢的邻居，安德鲁·戈登很快就弹尽粮绝了，不得不让他的老经理入伙成为合伙人。"恶霸"格兰特入伙没多久，就几乎把所有的好地都攥在了手里，而土地法本应创造的勤劳自耕农们，却被逼得躲进了沟壑里，在可怜巴巴的一小块劣地上靠偷羊为生。"恶霸"跟他们硬碰硬地斗，扣押他们的羊和牛，起诉侵入者和盗贼；加上他运气好得惊人，很快就把他靠采矿赚来的巨额财富又翻了一番，而安德鲁·戈登却在穷困潦倒中去世了。安德鲁死后，老"恶霸"把牧场的管理权交给了儿子们，自己则以挑毛病为乐。但他在早年生涯中的一段鲜为人知的往事，在他发了大财、远赴英国长住之后很久，仍然在基利酒馆的老雇工们口中流传。

===Original===
Grant, in spite of the judgment and sagacity on which he prided himself, had at various times in his career made mistakes—mistakes in station management, mistakes about stock, mistakes about men, and last, but not least, mistakes about women; and it was to one of these mistakes that the gossips referred.

===Chinese===
格兰特尽管自诩精明睿智，在他生涯的各个阶段都犯过错误——牧场管理上的错误、牲口上的错误、用人上的错误，最后也是最重要的，还有女人上的错误；而那些闲言碎语所指的，正是其中一次错误。

===Original===
When he was a young man working as Mr. Gordon's manager, and living with the horse-breaker and the ration-carrier on the outstation at Kuryong (in those days a wild, half-civilised place), he had for neighbours Red Mick's father and mother, the original Mr. and Mrs. Donohoe, and their family. Their eldest daughter, Peggy—"Carrotty Peg," her relations called her—was at that time a fine, strapping, bush girl, and the only unmarried white woman anywhere near the station. She was as fair-complexioned as Red Mick himself, with a magnificent head of red hair, and the bust and limbs of a young Amazon.

===Chinese===
他年轻时给戈登先生当经理，住在库里昂外围牧场（当时还是个蛮荒半开化的地方）的驯马师和运粮员旁边，邻居是红米克的父母——老多诺霍先生和多诺霍太太——以及他们的家人。他们的大女儿佩吉（Peggy）——亲戚们叫她"胡萝卜佩"（Carrotty Peg）——当时是个高大健壮的丛林姑娘，也是牧场附近唯一一个未婚的白人女性。她跟红米克一样肤色白皙，顶着一头浓密的红发，身段和四肢活脱脱一个年轻的女战士（Amazon）。

===Original===
This young woman, as she grew up, attracted the attention of Billy the Bully, and they used to meet a good deal out in the bush. On such occasions, he would possibly be occupied in the inspiriting task of dragging a dead sheep after his horse, to make a trail to lead the wild dogs up to some poisoned meat; while the lady, clad in light and airy garments, with a huge white sunbonnet for headgear, would be riding straddle-legged in search of strayed cows. When Grant left the station, and went away to make his fortune in mining, it was, perhaps, just a coincidence that this magnificent young creature grew tired of the old place and "cleared out," too. She certainly went away and disappeared so utterly that even her own people did not know what had become of her; to the younger generation her very existence was only a vague tradition. But it was whispered here and muttered there among the Doyles and the Donohoes and their friends and relations, that old Billy the Bully, on one of his visits to the interior, had been married to this undesirable lady by a duly accredited parson, in the presence of responsible witnesses; and that, when everyone had their own, Carrotty Peg, if alive, would be the lady of Kuryong. However, she had never come back to prove it, and no one cared about asking her alleged husband any unpleasant questions.

===Chinese===
这个姑娘长大了之后，引起了"恶霸"比利（Billy the Bully）的注意，两人常在丛林里幽会。这种时候，他可能正忙着拖着死羊跟在马后面——这是个提神的差事——用血迹把野狗引到下了毒的肉那里；而那位女士则穿着轻薄飘逸的衣裳，顶着一顶巨大的白色遮阳帽，叉开两腿骑在马上寻找走失的牛。格兰特离开牧场去采矿发财时，这位光彩照人的年轻姑娘或许只是碰巧也厌倦了这个地方，"走人了"。她确实走了，消失得无影无踪，连她自己家人都不知道她去了哪里；对年轻一代来说，她的存在只是一个模糊的传说。但在多尔家和多诺霍家及其亲友之间，这里有人窃窃私语，那里有人低声议论，说老"恶霸"比利有一次去内陆时，跟这位"不太体面"的女士由一位正式授权的牧师主持了婚礼，有负责的证人在场；还说，要是各得其所的话，胡萝卜佩如果还活着，就是库里昂的女主人了。不过她始终没有回来证明这件事，也没有人愿意去问她那位所谓的丈夫什么不愉快的问题。

===Original===
So much for the history of its owners; now to describe the homestead itself. It had originally consisted of the two-roomed slab hut, which had been added to from time to time. Kitchen, outhouses, bachelors' quarters, saddle-rooms, and storerooms had been built on in a kind of straggling quadrangle, with many corners and unexpected doorways and passages; and it is reported that a swagman once got his dole of rations at the kitchen, went away, and after turning two or three corners, got so tangled up that when Fate led him back to the kitchen he didn't recognise it, and asked for rations over again, in the firm belief that he was at a different part of the house.

===Chinese===
关于牧场主人的故事就说到这里；现在来描述主牧场宅邸本身。它最初只是那间两间的小木板屋，后来不断加盖扩建。厨房、外屋、单身汉宿舍、鞍具室和储藏室被建成了一种参差不齐的四合院式建筑，拐角众多、门道和走廊出其不意；据说有个流浪汉（swagman）在厨房领了口粮后离开，拐了两三个弯就彻底迷了路，等命运又把他带回厨房时，他竟没认出来，以为到了房子的另一处，又去要了一份口粮。

===Original===
The original building was still the principal living-room, but the house had grown till it contained about twenty rooms. The slab walls had been plastered and whitewashed, and a wide verandah ran all along the front. Round the house were acres of garden, with great clumps of willows and acacias, where the magpies sat in the heat of the day and sang to one another in their sweet, low warble.

===Chinese===
最初的那间小屋仍然是主要的起居室，但房子已经扩建到大约二十间房了。木板墙被粉刷一新，一条宽阔的游廊沿着正面延伸。房子周围是几英亩的花园，大丛大丛的柳树和金合欢树下，喜鹊在白天最热的时候栖息其中，用甜美低沉的歌声互相唱和。

===Original===
The house stood on a spur running from the hills. Looking down the river from it, one saw level flats waving with long grasses, in which the solemn cattle waded knee-deep. Here and there clumps of willows and stately poplars waved in the breeze. In the clear, dry air all colours were startlingly vivid, and round the nearer foothills wonderful lights and shadows played and shifted, while sometimes a white fleece of mist would drift slowly across a distant hill, like a film of snowy lace on the face of a beautiful woman. Away behind the foothills were the grand old mountains, with their snow-clad tops gleaming in the sun.

===Chinese===
房子坐落在一条从山上延伸下来的山脊上。从这里俯瞰河流，可以看到平坦的草地在长草中起伏摇曳，庄严的牛群在齐膝深的草丛中涉水而行。各处的柳树丛和挺拔的杨树在微风中摇曳。在干燥清冽的空气中，所有色彩都鲜艳得令人惊叹，近处山麓周围光影变幻、流光溢彩，有时一片白色的薄雾会缓缓飘过远处的山头，像一层雪白的轻纱覆在一位美丽女子的面庞上。山麓之后是雄伟的古老山脉，积雪覆盖的峰顶在阳光下闪耀。

The garden was almost as lacking in design as the house. In some parts of Australia it is difficult to tell summer from winter; but up in this mountain-country each season had its own attractions. In the spring the flats were green with lush grass, speckled with buttercups and bachelors' buttons, and the willows put out their new leaves, and all manner of shy dry-scented bush flowers bloomed on the ranges; and the air was full of the song of birds and the calling of animals. Then came summer, when never a cloud decked the arch of blue sky, and all animated nature drew into the shade of big trees until the evening breeze sprang up, bringing sweet scents of the dry grass and ripening grain.
