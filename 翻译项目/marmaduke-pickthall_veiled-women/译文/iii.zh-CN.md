## III / 三

===Original===

Immediately on his return to his own house, Muhammad Pasha sent a eunuch to announce his coming to the lady Fitnah. He found her lying on a couch in her stateroom. Two slaves, who had been busy fanning her, retired before him. Seeing she lay still with eyes closed as if quite exhausted, he drew near and whispered:

“Now, inshallah, O beloved, thou wilt hear my reasons.”

She opened great brown eyes, bloodshot with wrath, and glared at him a moment.

“Well, what news?” she asked, with studied coldness.

The Pasha then embarked upon his story; but, at mention of the Consul, she sprang up with rage renewed, expectorating:

===Chinese===

穆罕默德·帕夏一回到自己宅中，便打发一名阉人去向菲特娜夫人通报他要来了。他来到她的上房，见她正躺在一张卧榻上。两个原先忙着替她打扇的女奴，在他进来时退了下去。见她阖着双眼、一动不动地躺着，仿佛已精疲力竭，他凑近前去，低声道：

“好了，若安拉意欲（Inshallah），心爱的，你就听我说说其中的缘由吧。”

她睁开一双棕色的大眼睛，眼里布满怒火熬红的血丝，瞪了他片刻。

“嗯，有什么消息？”她问道，故作冷淡。

帕夏于是开始讲述事情原委；可刚一提到领事，她便怒火复燃，霍然跳起，啐骂道：

===Original===

“Curse thy father! ‘She will see the Consul,’ sayest thou? The Consul! May the Consul and his whole race rot with agony! It is simply to evade a duty which is thine and thine alone. Eject her from the house at once, thou paltry coward! She will kill our son. I know thy guile, by Allah! Thou wilt say, ‘The Consul orders her to marry Yûsuf. We must obey the Consul,’﻿—O salvation!﻿—when all the while thyself art father of the mischief. Oh, let her not come here, or, by my fruitfulness! these hands shall cling to her and not leave hold till they have made her so that no man could desire her.”

Expostulation proving vain, her lord retired in great annoyance. He had to fear a scandal in his house, an inquisition by the Consul, ignominy, if Yûsuf’s mother came in contact with the English lady.

In this dilemma, as in every other which concerned the household, he went for counsel to his only love and first of wives. He sent a herald of his coming to Murjânah Khânum, and after a decent interval repaired to her apartments. She received him in a large room, with no other solid furniture than a low desk on which a manuscript of the Koran lay open; but exquisitely clean and sweet, a contrast to those quarters of the house where Fitnah reigned. The windows were constructed of the finest latticework, which made the light within seem rare and delicate. Murjânah, old but stately, fondled her lord’s hand.

===Chinese===

“咒你老子！‘她要去见领事’，你是这么说的？领事！愿领事和他全族老小都在苦痛里烂掉！这不过是推卸一桩本该你一人独担的本分。马上把她撵出宅去，你这卑劣的懦夫！她会害死我们儿子的。你那点诡计我清楚得很，以安拉起誓！你还会说：‘领事命她嫁给优素福，我们只能服从领事’——哦，苍天救命！——可这祸根从头到尾都是你自己种下的。哼，别让她到这儿来；不然，凭我生养儿女之功起誓！我这双手定要死死揪住她，不把她糟蹋得再没有一个男人看得上她，决不松开。”

劝解既然徒劳，她的夫君只得满怀懊恼地退了出来。他不能不怕：优素福的母亲一旦同那位英国女士打了照面，宅中便会闹出丑闻，招来领事的究问，落得一身羞辱。

处此两难之中，正如以往一切关涉家宅的困境一样，他去向自己唯一的心上人、元配夫人讨主意。他先遣人向穆尔贾娜·哈努姆通报驾到，隔了一段合乎体统的工夫，才移步前往她的居所。她在一间宽敞的屋子里接待了他，屋里除了一张矮几，别无其他大件家具；矮几上摊开着一册《古兰经》手抄本。屋子却洁净得精致，甜香满室，与菲特娜当家作主的那几处房舍恰成对照。窗子是用最精细的镂花格窗做成，使屋内的光线显得幽微而柔美。穆尔贾娜人已年老，气度依然庄严，一面轻轻抚弄着她夫君的手。

===Original===

“Thy face is careworn,” she exclaimed, perusing it. “Inshallah, all the news is good.”

“Inshallah,” he replied mechanically. “But Allah knows that I am greatly troubled. I know not what to do.” And he proceeded to describe the madness of the lady Fitnah. At the tale’s conclusion, a light laugh surprised him.

“Thou askest what to do,” exclaimed Murjânah, “when there is danger that a foolish woman, mad with jealousy, may harm a guest of ours! Hear the word of Allah: ‘When ye have cause to fear their disobedience, ye shall reprimand them, ye shall banish them to beds apart, and ye shall beat them.’ Is not that plain? Beat her! It is thy sacred duty. No, no, she will not cry against thee to the Qadi. She will hide her fault. All women look to men for government, and if it is withheld, have cause of grief. Trust me, beloved, there is no good woman who would not rather suffer stripes occasionally than grow for lack of them into a shrieking harridan. Fitnah Khânum is my durrah, and I love her truly, as the mother of our darling children, and for many virtues. Still I say to thee on this occasion: beat her soundly. Bestow on her a perfect beating, O my soul!”

===Chinese===

“你一脸忧色。”她端详着他的脸，说道。“若安拉意欲，一切消息都好。”

“若安拉意欲。”他机械地应道。“可是安拉知道，我烦恼得很。我不知道该怎么办才好。”接着他便讲起菲特娜夫人的疯癫。故事讲完，一声轻笑却令他愕然。

“你问该怎么办，”穆尔贾娜嚷道，“可眼下分明有这般危险：一个嫉妒得发狂的蠢女人，竟要加害咱们的一位客人！听听安拉的话：‘当你们担心她们违逆的时候，应当训诫她们，应当把她们赶到分睡的床上去，应当责打她们。’这还不明白吗？打她！这是你神圣的本分。不，不，她不会到卡迪（Qadi，伊斯兰教法官）那里哭告你。她会把自己的过错遮掩起来。天下女人都指望男人来管教；这管教若被克扣了，她们便有理由悲苦。相信我，心爱的，天下的好女人，宁可偶尔吃几鞭子，也不愿因缺了鞭子，长成一个尖声嚎叫的泼妇。菲特娜·哈努姆是我的杜拉（durrah，贵妇尊称），我真心疼她，念她是咱们心肝宝贝的母亲，也念她许多美德。虽这样说，这一回我还是要对你说：狠狠地打她。赏她一顿十全十美的痛打，我的亲人！”

===Original===

The Pasha kissed his old wife’s hand submissively, and went forth from her presence with a face of awe. The high proceeding needed courage, for a man so kindly. He went to the small chamber where the eunuchs sat when not on duty, and called, “Sawwâb! Meymûn! Bring me a big kurbash. Attend me, both of you!”

The silent, swift obedience of those servants showed the impression made by his unusual sternness. Their help was necessary that the scene to come might wear the aspect of an execution, not a struggle.

Whip in hand, Muhammad Pasha crossed a courtyard and entered a small room remote from others.

===Chinese===

帕夏恭顺地吻了吻老妻的手，面带敬畏之色，从她面前退了出去。这等隆重的程式，对一个如此仁慈的人来说，是颇需要些勇气的。他走进阉人们不当值时闲坐的那间小屋，喊道：“萨瓦布！梅伊蒙！给我拿一条大库尔巴什（kurbash，皮鞭）来。你们两个，都随我来伺候！”

两个奴仆无声而利落的服从，足见他这反常的严厉给人留下的震慑。必须由他们从旁帮衬，接下来那一场才能有个行刑的模样，而不至于沦为一场扭打。

穆罕默德·帕夏手执皮鞭，穿过一方庭院，走进一间远离其他屋舍的僻静小屋。

===Original===

“Bring Fitnah Khânum hither secretly!” he told the eunuchs.

Sawwâb, the fat, was seized with trembling; while Meymûn, a tall, gaunt creature, gave a deathlike grin. They sped, however. Three minutes had not passed before the lady Fitnah, deftly bound and gagged, was borne into the lonely chamber and the door was shut.

Half an hour later, Muhammad Pasha Sâlih sat conversing with the English lady, preparing her intelligence to meet the Consul’s arguments, which he forewarned her would be all misstatements born of blind fanaticism. When married to Yûsuf, he assured her, and himself believed it, she would hardly know the difference from an English home.

===Chinese===

“把菲特娜·哈努姆悄悄带到这儿来！”他吩咐阉人们。

胖萨瓦布浑身打起颤来；瘦长枯干的梅伊蒙则咧出一副死人般的笑。不过两人还是拔腿飞奔而去。不到三分钟，菲特娜夫人已被麻利地捆住手脚、堵上嘴，抬进了那间僻静的小屋，门也随之关上。

半小时之后，穆罕默德·帕夏·萨利赫已坐下来同那位英国女士交谈，调教她如何应对领事的种种说辞——他预先告诫她，那些话全是盲目狂热中生出的不实之词。他向她担保，一旦嫁了优素福，她几乎觉察不出与一个英国家庭有什么两样——这话他自己也信。
