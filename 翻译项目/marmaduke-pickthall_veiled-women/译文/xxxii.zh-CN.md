## XXXII / 三十二
===Original===
At Kafr ed-Dowâr Muhammad was kept drilling conscripts, relieving older officers who were required for actual fighting. Almost every day he heard the boom of cannon, the stirring noises of attack and skirmish; and often in his leisure moments he would perch in some high nook and watch the flashes, the white puffs of smoke, dispersed upon the green of level fields between the seacoast sandhills and the lake﻿—a pretty sight. Beyond the plain of water skimmed by white-winged birds the town of Alexandria basked in sunlit haze. Upon the land-plain doves were wheeling round deserted villages, kites and vultures hovered high in air. Franks from the seaport rode out in the rearward of the English troops, and from the vantage-point of dykes and hillocks watched the operations through their field-glasses. The assaults, as he had told his mother, were not serious; mere “fantaziyeh” the old soldiers called them. The aim of the assailants was to keep a portion of Arâbi’s troops from joining the main army on the banks of the Canal, where war was being waged in bitter earnest. Muhammad fretted at his dull employment. The atmosphere of strife, the bugle-calls, the march of men, no longer satisfied him as at first. He wished to fight, and begged the general-in-chief, who favoured him, being a close friend of his uncle Hamdi, to move him to some post of danger. The great one laughed and patted him upon the back.
“We cannot spare thee yet from the recruits,” he said. “That work is useful, and it must be done. Think, thou hast given us a thousand soldiers, none like them for rigidity and speed of motion.”

===Chinese===
在卡夫尔·达瓦尔，穆罕默德奉命操练新兵，替下须去实战的老军官。几乎每天他都听得见炮声隆隆，听得见进攻和小接仗那些撩动人心的声响；闲暇时分，他常蹲坐在什么高高的角落里，望着火光闪动、白烟一团团散开在滨海沙丘与湖泊之间平畴的绿色上——一幅好看的景象。隔着白翼飞鸟掠过的茫茫水面，亚历山大港浴在阳光的薄霭里。陆地上，鸽群绕着废弃的村庄盘旋，鸢鸟和兀鹫高高悬在空中。从海港来的法兰克人骑马跟在英军后部，占着堤岸和土丘的便利地势，用望远镜观战。那些进攻，正如他告诉过母亲的，并不当真；老兵们管它们叫“方塔齐耶”（fantaziyeh，做戏）。进攻一方的目的，是拖住阿拉比的一部分队伍，不让他们去运河两岸同主力会合——那里正打着殊死的真仗。穆罕默德为这枯燥的差事烦躁。争战的气氛、军号声、队伍的行进，已不像起初那样令他满足了。他想去打仗，便恳求总司令把自己调到某个危险的岗位——总司令是他叔叔哈姆迪的密友，对他颇为眷顾。那位大人物笑着拍了拍他的背。
“我们还不能把你从新兵那儿抽走，”他说。“那差事有用处，总得有人来做。想想吧，你已经给了我们一千名士兵，论举手投足的刻板和动作的迅速，没有一队比得上他们。”

===Original===
Muhammad hated the recruits, who still were driven in by hundreds every day﻿—men past their prime, and boys dragged from the wretched villages, and active rogues caught hiding in some ditch or patch of cane. The land had been already drained; the dregs were called for. And they were stupid, dazed, those fellahin; a flock of sheep has more intelligence! Muhammad, for whom soldiering was a religion and every detail of the drill had sanctity, was driven frantic by their apathy, their foolish stare. Dancing with fury, he reviled their mothers and beat them with his cane about the ears.
“By the Prophet, they are pigs!” he told the son of Ghandûr, who served him in his tent and hung upon his every word. “Here is El Islam in danger; they are Muslims; yet they yawn and gape if asked to hold a gun. Ah! if I had a hundred Turks instead of them!”

===Chinese===
穆罕默德恨透了这些新兵——如今每天仍有成百的人被驱赶进来：过了盛年的汉子，从破败村庄里拖出来的男孩，在什么沟渠或蔗丛里躲藏时被逮个正着的机灵流氓。这片土地早已被榨干；如今是连渣滓也要征来了。而这些费拉农人又蠢又呆，一群羊也比他们多些智性！在穆罕默德，当兵是一门宗教，操练的每个细节都有神圣的意味，他们的麻木、他们的傻瞪，简直把他逼得发狂。他怒得跳脚，咒骂他们的母亲，用手杖抽打他们的耳侧。
“凭先知起誓，他们是猪！”他对甘杜尔的儿子说——那少年在他帐中当差，他说的每一个字都奉如纶音。“伊斯兰教正临着危险；他们是穆斯林；可叫他们拿一拿枪，他们竟又打哈欠又发怔。啊！我若能拿一百个土耳其人换掉他们该多好！”

===Original===
The son of Ghandûr, who to please Muhammad would himself have put his head into a cannon’s mouth, was horrified at the behaviour of the conscripts. That they could fail to see the light of inspiration on Muhammad’s brow was proof sufficient of their utter baseness. For the same reason he despised the generals. Muhammad was more gifted for command than they, and yet they kept him ever at this menial task. Had Muhammad﻿—or his foster-brother even﻿—owned the leadership, Iskenderîyeh would long since have fallen, and all the English have been pushed into the sea. He dared to proffer this opinion to his lord one evening. But Muhammad in his wisdom answered:
“No, we cannot take the town, for this good reason, that a portion of their fleet, unseen from here, commands it, and would pour in shells to our destruction.”
Ali received this information with head bowed and thanks to God. He prayed the Maker of the World to put some mind in the recruits in order that they too might profit by such high instruction.

===Chinese===
甘杜尔的儿子，为了讨穆罕默德欢心，自己把脑袋伸进炮口也情愿；新兵们的行径叫他骇然。他们竟看不出穆罕默德眉宇间的灵光，仅此一端，便足证他们卑劣至极。出于同一个缘故，他也看不起那些将军。穆罕默德比他们更富统帅之才，他们却总派他干这份下贱差事。倘若由穆罕默德——哪怕干脆由他这位奶兄弟——执掌了指挥权，亚历山大城（Iskenderîyeh）早就陷落，英国人也早被统统推下海去了。一天晚上，他斗胆把这层意思进言给主人。穆罕默德却以其睿智答道：
“不，我们拿不下那座城，理由很现成：他们舰队的一部分就在这里望不见的地方控制着它，会把炮弹倾泻进来，置我们于死地。”
阿里垂着头领受了这番道理，向真主致谢。他祈求造物主往新兵们脑子里放进一点心智，好叫他们也能沾益于这般高明的教诲。

===Original===
It was usual at that time for officers to handle soldiers roughly on parade, caning them upon the head and shoulders, kicking them, and heaping on them every species of abuse. Muhammad might be called indulgent as commanders went; but he was overmuch in earnest. His outbreaks lacked the touch of humour which endears. Old soldiers might have borne them with a laugh for the sake of his enthusiasm, which was very evident.
But these were men who had been driven from their homes like cattle, at the goad’s point. For days they had been herded up in pens in a provincial town, and there harangued by holy men and maddened by religious shouting till they lost what little wits remained to them and hardly knew a true believer from an infidel. Arâbi had proclaimed the golden age; yet here they were imprisoned, hounded, driven, and now subjected to the cuffs and insults of a shameless boy. Huddling together, they looked on with lowered brows, too scared to understand what the young Turk was shouting. Arâbi had proclaimed the Turks abolished. Where was reason? They gave forth inarticulate harsh cries like frightened beasts.
Each squad Muhammad handled seemed more stupid than the last﻿—so stupid that one early morning an inspecting general advised him, laughing, to give drill a rest, and take them to the trenches; they were used to digging.

===Chinese===
在那个年头，军官们在操场上粗暴对待士兵是常事：用手杖打头打肩，用脚踢，百般辱骂，无所不至。照带兵将官的通例而论，穆罕默德还称得上宽厚；只是他过于较真。他的发作缺少那一笔讨人喜欢的幽默。老兵们念着他那份显而易见的热忱，原可以一笑置之地忍受。
可这些人，是像牲口一样被刺棒的尖端赶出家门的。一连数日，他们被圈在外省一座城的栏圈里，听神职人员们训话，被宗教的呼号搅得发狂，直到仅存的一点神智也丧失净尽，几乎分不清信士与异教徒。阿拉比宣告过黄金时代；可他们如今被监禁、被追迫、被驱使，还要受一个恬不知耻的少年的拳脚和侮辱。他们挤作一团，低眉蹙额地看着，吓昏了头，听不懂那年轻的土耳其人在嚷些什么。阿拉比宣告过废除土耳其人。道理何在？他们发出受惊牲口般含混粗厉的叫声。
穆罕默德接手的每一队新兵都比前一队更蠢——蠢到有一日清晨，一位前来视察的将军笑着劝他：操练且歇一歇，带他们去挖壕吧；挖土他们是惯会的。

===Original===
Muhammad felt the order as a whip-cut; he was furious. The general despised his work as an instructor, whereas God knew what trouble he had taken. It was all their fault. In the trenches he allowed them to do nothing right, but shrieked out contradictory orders emphasized by slashes of his cane. Slowly it dawned on them that he was quite alone; the place was hidden by high banks from supervision.
The daily pageant of attack was then in progress. The crackle of a volley came from no great distance. Muhammad implored Allah to direct the bullets so as to kill them all, for they were worse than infidels. He did not notice the changed manner of their breathing, nor the new fire which smouldered in their eyes.
At a blow across the face, accompanied by frightful insults, a burly fellow seized Muhammad’s wrists and deftly tripped him. The boy lay on his back bereft of speech. His captor knelt upon his belly, while the others crowded round like cattle interested. He could feel their breath.

===Chinese===
这道命令在穆罕默德感觉如同挨了一鞭；他怒不可遏。将军竟轻视他做教官的功夫，而真主才知道他下过多少苦心。这全是他们的错。在壕沟里，他不许他们做对一件事，只是尖声喊出前后矛盾的口令，还用手杖一顿抽打来加重语气。他们渐渐省悟到，这里只有他孤身一人；那地方被高高的土堤遮蔽，无人督察。
每日例行的进攻排戏正在上演。一排齐射的脆响从不远处传来。穆罕默德祈求安拉指引子弹，把他们统统打死，因为他们比异教徒还要坏。他没有留意他们呼吸变了样子，也没有留意他们眼里阴燃起来的新的火。
一记抽在脸上的鞭打，伴着骇人的辱骂——一个壮实的汉子攥住穆罕默德的双腕，手脚利落地一绊，把他放倒在地。少年仰面躺着，说不出话来。擒他的人跪压在他肚子上，其余的人像一群凑热闹的牲口围拢过来。他感觉得到他们的鼻息。

===Original===
“Hear, O my little son! Swear by the Sayyid Ahmad to be civil. It were best for thee.”
Muhammad, with his pride undaunted, answered: “Sinful hog! I swear to have thee thrashed with the nailed whip and then decapitated. O Muslimîn! Do you not know that this is mutiny, an awful crime?”
“Then we must finish him,” remarked his captor, with a sigh. “With his own sword! Here! Quickly, while I stop his screeching.”

===Chinese===
“听着，我的小少爷！凭赛义德·艾哈迈德（Sayyid Ahmad）起誓，放规矩些。这样于你最好。”
穆罕默德傲气不折，答道：“犯罪的猪！我发誓要叫人用带钉的皮鞭抽你，然后砍你的头。穆斯林们啊！你们不知道这是哗变，是滔天大罪吗？”
“那我们就只好结果了他，”擒他的人叹道。“就用他自己的剑！来！快，趁我堵住他的尖叫。”

===Original===
The speaker pressed his hand down on Muhammad’s mouth, while another drew the sword and plunged it several times into the prostrate form. They watched until the last convulsions ceased; then piously observed: “Our Lord have mercy on him! There is no power nor might save in Allah, the High, the Tremendous!”
“By Allah, he could bite!” observed his first assailant, shaking blood from his right hand. The palm was bitten through. He stopped to bandage it; and then they made a litter with their spades and so conveyed the body back to camp with wailing.
“The darling of our souls is dead,” they chanted. “Slain by the infidels, whom we repulsed. Our brother, Abdul Câder, too, is wounded in the hand.”

===Chinese===
说话的人把手掌往下压紧穆罕默德的嘴，另一人抽出剑来，朝那横陈的身躯连捅数下。他们盯着看，直到最后的抽搐止息；这才虔敬地说道：“愿我们的主怜悯他！除崇高威严的安拉外，别无权能与力量！”
“凭安拉说，他真会咬！”最先动手的那人说道，甩去右手上的血。掌心被咬穿了。他停下来包扎；随后他们用铁锹拼成一副担架，一路哭嚎着把尸身抬回营地。
“我们心尖上的宝贝死了，”他们齐声哭唱。“被异教徒杀死了——那些异教徒已被我们击退。我们的兄弟阿卜杜勒·卡迪尔（Abdul Câder）也伤了手。”

===Original===
The lie was quite transparent, yet it passed unquestioned. The high commanders shrugged and let it go. There were a hundred men concerned, with Allah knew how many sympathizers. They dreaded a stampede of all the conscripts in the camp.
When Ali, mad with grief, demanded justice, he was told to hold his tongue. The general was profoundly grieved; he shed some tears, and swore that every honour should be paid to the remains. A telegram was sent to Yûsuf Pasha announcing that his son had died a martyr, and that the blessed body was upon its way to Cairo. Within an hour of death it had been dressed for burial. It was carried in a fine procession to the railway, where a special train﻿—a locomotive and an open truck﻿—was waiting. The corpse was laid down in the truck, and covered with some tent-cloth; and Ali sat beside it, while the train sped hooting on past empty villages, where only a few children played upon the dust-heaps, a few women stood in doorways with hands shading eyes, past palm-groves and the fields of cotton and of sugarcane until the citadel rose up before him in the evening sky.

===Chinese===
这谎话一望便知，却无人质疑。高级军官们耸耸肩，随它去了。牵涉其中的有一百来人，抱同情的还有多少，唯有安拉知道。他们生怕全营的新兵一齐惊溃。
阿里悲愤欲狂，要求伸冤，却被告知闭嘴。将军深感哀恸；他洒了几点泪，发誓要给遗骸以一切的哀荣。一封电报发往优素福·帕夏，告知他的儿子已列殉道之位，蒙福的遗体正在运往开罗途中。死后不到一个钟头，遗体已穿好安葬的衣服。它被一列体面的行列抬送到铁路跟前，一列专车——一台机车加一节敞篷货车——正候在那里。尸身安放在货车里，盖上几块帐篷布；阿里坐在旁边；列车鸣着汽笛一路疾驰，掠过一座座空村——村里只有几个孩子在土堆上玩耍，几个女人站在门口以手遮眼张望——掠过棕榈林和棉花田、甘蔗田，直到暮色四合的天空里，城堡的轮廓在他面前耸起。
