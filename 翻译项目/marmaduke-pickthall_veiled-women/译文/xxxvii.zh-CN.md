## XXXVII / 三十七

===Original===
Quickly the daylight spread and filled the streets; while overhead successive darts of light pierced the incumbent darkness and dispelled it, till the sun’s first ray reddened the minarets and plunged the streets in azure shade. Men came out from their doorways as from tombs, and went about their business listlessly. Among the lower classes it was quite expected that the English would take vengeance on the town that day. The people did not care; they were in Allah’s hands, and gave Him thanks because the war was ended.

For Barakah the city wore its usual air; the only wretched figure was her own. She was being led back to a life which had become intolerable. After her tragic flight of yesterday, how ignominious was this meek return! Ghandûr, beside her, talked of the extreme anxiety in which her flight had plunged the Pasha’s family.

===Chinese===
天光迅速漫开来，灌满了各条街道；头顶上，一束接一束的光箭刺透沉沉压顶的黑暗，将它驱散，直到太阳的第一缕光线染红了宣礼塔，把街道浸入湛蓝的荫翳里。人们从各自的门口走出来，像从坟墓里走出来一样，没精打采地各办各的事。下层百姓中间，人人都料定英国人当天要向这座城泄愤报复。人们并不在意；他们全在安拉的手里，还感赞他，因为战争已经结束了。

在巴拉卡眼里，这座城仍是寻常那副面目；唯一愁惨的身影是她自己。她正被领回去，去过那种已变得无法忍受的生活。昨日那场惨痛的出走之后，这样温顺地归来，何其丢脸！甘杜尔走在她身旁，絮絮说着她的出逃使帕夏一家陷入了何等极度的焦灼。

===Original===
“O my lady, how hadst thou the heart to cause us such despair? Think of it! One like thee, alone and in the streets at such a time, when all authority is in abeyance, and the English host may come at any moment with the lust of conquerors! A hundred men were searching for thee through the night. My lord the Pasha thought that grief might lead thee to the place of tombs, and he himself went thither with the slaves enjoined to hide our valuables. Praise be to Allah, thou art found at last! Take comfort, O my lady! Often and often have I grieved for thee, alone among us! And when our great calamity befell﻿—alas, that son of mine should bear such evil tidings!﻿—I prayed to Allah to reveal to thee His boundless mercy. For it has no limits. For all who suffer in this world He will redress the balance. Even the unhorned cattle, O my lady! It is written.”

===Chinese===
“夫人啊，您怎么忍心叫咱们这样绝望？您想想看！像您这样身份的人，孤身一人，又赶上那样的时节上街，官府的权威尽皆失效，英国大军随时可能带着征服者的贪欲开进来！上百号人找您找了整整一夜。老爷帕夏想着，悲伤也许会把您引到亡人墓地那儿去，便亲自带着奴仆们去了，吩咐他们把咱们的贵重物件藏起来。感赞安拉，您总算找到了！宽宽心吧，我的夫人！我常常为您心疼，您孤孤单单地处在咱们中间！咱们的大难临头的时候——唉，偏偏是我那儿子去报的凶信！——我祈求安拉，愿他把那无边的慈悲显示给您。因为那慈悲没有边际。这世上一切受苦的人，他都要还他们一个公道。连没有角的牲畜也一样，夫人啊！经上写着呢。”

===Original===
Barakah heard these consolations as a dreary murmur.

“I am taking thee to the late Pasha’s house, to the great lady,” he informed her. “My lord considers it will be less sad for thee.”

The great lady meant no other than Murjânah Khânum. Recalling the authority Murjânah wielded, Barakah imagined she was being led to punishment.

Two eunuchs came forth, bowing, crying, “Praise to Allah!” They helped her to dismount, and both supported her. A minute later she had passed the harem screen. Her brief excursion in the world was ended. She was once more caged.

===Chinese===
巴拉卡听着这些宽慰的话，只当是一片沉闷的絮语。

“我这就带您到先帕夏的宅子里去，到大夫人那儿，”他告诉她。“老爷想着，这样您会少一些伤心。”

这大夫人不是别人，正是穆尔贾娜·哈努姆。想起穆尔贾娜素来执掌的威权，巴拉卡只道自己是被押去受罚了。

两名阉人迎上前来，躬着身，喊道：“感赞安拉！”他们扶她下驴，两人一左一右搀住她。不消一分钟，她已过了内宅的帷屏。她在这世上短短的出走就此告终。她再度被关进了笼子。

===Original===
Imagining her crime to be as great as that of Christian nun in breaking convent, and knowing that Murjânah Khânum could be ruthless, she expected torture; instead of which she was caressed and put to bed.

She had her lodging in Murjânah’s rooms, was dosed by Fitnah, comforted by Leylah Khânum. The younger ladies came as visitors and talked to cheer her. Old Umm ed-Dahak, not to be excluded, crouched by her bed and crooned as to an infant.

“Why are you all so kind to me?” she asked one day. “I tried to flee, I tell you, to escape to Europe﻿—yet you pet me!”

===Chinese===
她想着自己的罪过，重得就像基督教修女私逃出修道院一般，又深知穆尔贾娜·哈努姆下得了狠手，便等着受刑；等来的却是百般爱抚，被人服侍着上了床。

她宿在穆尔贾娜的房间里，由菲特娜给她喂药，莱拉·哈努姆（Leylah Khânum）从旁宽慰。年轻些的太太们像客人似的来串门，陪她说话解闷。老乌姆·埃德-达哈克也不甘被撇下，蹲在她床边，像哄婴孩一样哼着调儿。

“你们为什么都对我这样好？”有一天她问。“我告诉你们，我原本是想逃走，逃到欧洲去——可你们还这样宠着我！”

===Original===
“All things are pardoned to great grief,” replied Murjânah. “It was not thy fault, O poor one! Would to Allah I could show thee what I see more clearly than I see thee in this room﻿—the power of God, His mercy all around us. Fain would I hear thee give Him praise for thy misfortune. He sees and knows; we fancy; it is weak to strive. Think, O my fawn, my lily, thou hast still one child; thou hadst thy boy for thy delight for fifteen years. More fortunate than I who lost all mine in infancy! What peace can come to woman in thy case who does not offer up her will to God? The men have promise of a certain paradise; we have no certitude of what awaits us. Yet are we not dejected, for we know God’s mercy, and leave the future gladly in His hands. We women are not bargainers, we serve for love; and the mercy of the Highest cannot fail. Thou hast been brought up otherwise to prouder thoughts. Humble thy soul if thou wouldst find relief.”

===Chinese===
“巨大的悲伤能赦免一切，”穆尔贾娜答道。“那不是你的过错，可怜的人！但愿安拉容我把那景象指给你看——我看得比在这屋里看你还清楚——真主的权能，和他四面环绕着我们的慈悲。我巴不得听你为自己的不幸称颂他。他看见，他知道；我们不过妄自揣想；挣扎便是软弱。想想吧，我的小鹿，我的百合，你还有一个孩子；你那儿子给过你十五年的欢喜。比我福气多了，我的孩子全在襁褓里就夭折了！似你这般处境的女人，若不把自己的意志献给真主，还能得着什么安宁？男人们有明许给他们的乐园；我们却无从确知等待自己的是什么。但我们并不消沉，因为我们认得真主的慈悲，甘愿把未来托在他手里。我们女人不是讨价还价的人，我们是出于爱而侍奉；至高者的慈悲必不落空。你受的教养是另一路，心思养得更傲。你若要寻得宽解，就谦卑你的灵魂吧。”

===Original===
“I proud?” cried Barakah. “Thou, the proudest woman I have ever known, canst call me so? I am not as thou art﻿—strong and dauntless, cruel in thy resignation. I am feeble and afraid.”

“May Allah strengthen thee and drive out fear!”

Barakah had lost the vision which had come from Tâhir’s singing﻿—a vision which ignored divergences of race and custom. Without her son the harem life was senseless; she held the Muslim faith in secret dread; and longed for sentimental Christian people. Yûsuf, her husband, proved the soul of kindness, yet she had almost hated him in her revolt from all his race.

===Chinese===
“我高傲？”巴拉卡喊道。“你，我所认得的最最高傲的女人，倒能这样说我？我不像你那样——坚强、无畏，连顺命都顺得冷酷。我又软弱，又害怕。”

“愿安拉使你坚强，把恐惧驱走！”

巴拉卡已经失去了塔希尔的歌声带给她的那个异象——一个无视种族与习俗分歧的异象。没有了儿子，深闺生活于她全无意义；她心底暗暗畏惧穆斯林的信仰；一心渴念那些多愁善感的基督徒众人。她的丈夫优素福事实证明仁厚到了骨子里，可她在对他整个族类的反叛之中，几乎恨过他。

===Original===
One day he told the ladies in her presence:

“The English are not bad. They take wise measures for the land’s redemption. They have asked me to take office, and I have a mind to do so.”

It was the first time she had heard the English mentioned since her reimprisonment. In fact, the Turkish pride had suffered cruelly from this intrusion of a European power, the more so that the natives of the land acclaimed it. Though the English arms restored their party in the State, the Turks in Egypt gnawed their lips and could not speak of them.

===Chinese===
有一天，他当着她的面对太太们说：

“英国人不坏。他们为救赎这片土地采取着明智的措施。他们请我出任官职，我有意应承下来。”

这是她再度被禁闭以来头一回听人提起英国人。事实上，欧洲强权的这一闯入，狠狠挫伤了土耳其人的自尊，更兼这片土地的本土百姓对它齐声欢呼。虽然英国人的武力让他们这一党在国内恢复了权位，埃及的土耳其人仍旧咬着嘴唇，绝口不愿谈论他们。

===Original===
A new way of escape appeared to Barakah. She could obtain an audience of the English rulers and announce her longing to return to Christianity. She pined for the ideals of Christian lands, the independent life of women, and their varied interests. Here she had lost her value, having lost her son. She would soon be an old woman, a mere worn-out animal.

Directly she conceived this plan, she grew more cheerful, and even felt some kindness for the harem walls. While making her endeavour to find out from Yûsuf the names of Englishmen of influence, their character and reputation, she wanted to make certain he would be consoled.

“Light of my eyes,” she whispered, nestling to him, “I have quite outgrown my foolish prejudice. I beg thee now to wed another wife. The son I bore to thee is dead, and I grow old.”

===Chinese===
一条新的出路在巴拉卡眼前出现了。她可以设法求见英国的统治者们，宣布自己渴望回归基督教。她渴慕基督教国土的那些理想——女人独立的生活，还有她们林林总总的志趣。在这里她已失了价值，因为儿子没了。她很快就要成为老妇，一头用残了的牲口罢了。

这计划一在心头成形，她便开朗了些，甚至对这深闺的四壁也生出几分好感。她一面设法从优素福口中探听有势力的英国人的姓名、品性与声望，一面又想确定他会得到宽慰。

“我眼里的光啊，”她偎着他，低声说，“我早把那点愚蠢的偏见抛干净了。如今我求你再娶一房吧。我给你生的儿子死了，我也见老了。”

===Original===
“Wallahi, thou art still delicious,” he replied gallantly; but all the same he thanked her, seeming much relieved.

Perceiving that the anguish of her grief was past, the ladies let her go to her own house.

“Remember my advice,” said old Murjânah in farewell. “Behold, my eyes grow dim, my days are numbered. I speak not frivolously like the young. Give up thy will. That is to islam truly. May Allah grant thee resignation, which is strength.”

===Chinese===
“凭安拉，你如今还是这样叫人心醉，”他殷勤地答道；但他仍旧谢了她，显得如释重负。

太太们看出她悲恸的苦楚已经熬了过去，便让她回自己的宅子去了。

“记住我的话，”老穆尔贾娜临别时说。“看哪，我的眼睛昏花了，我的日子有数了。我不像年轻人那样轻嘴薄舌。舍弃你自己的意志，那才是真正的皈依伊斯兰。愿安拉赐你顺命，顺命即是力量。”
