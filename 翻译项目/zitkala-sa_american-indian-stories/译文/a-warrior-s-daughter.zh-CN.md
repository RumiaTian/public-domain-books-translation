## A Warrior's Daughter / 勇士的女儿

===Original===
In the afternoon shadow of a large teepee, with red-painted smoke lapels, sat a warrior father with crossed shins. His head was so poised that his eye swept easily the vast level land to the eastern horizon line.

He was the chieftain's bravest warrior. He had won by heroic deeds the privilege of staking his wigwam within the great circle of teepees.

He was also one of the most generous gift givers to the toothless old people. For this he was entitled to the red-painted smoke lapels on his cone-shaped dwelling. He was proud of his honors. He never wearied of rehearsing nightly his own brave deeds. Though by wigwam fires he prated much of his high rank and widespread fame, his great joy was a wee black-eyed daughter of eight sturdy winters. Thus as he sat upon the soft grass, with his wife at his side, bent over her bead work, he was singing a dance song, and beat lightly the rhythm with his slender hands.

His shrewd eyes softened with pleasure as he watched the easy movements of the small body dancing on the green before him.

===Chinese===
一座高大的梯皮帐篷（teepee）立在午后的日影里，顶上涂着红漆的烟盖（smoke lapel）格外醒目；一位身为父亲的勇士盘着腿坐在帐前阴影里。他的头微微扬起，目光从容扫过辽阔平坦的原野，一直望到东方的地平线。

他是首领麾下最勇猛的勇士。他屡建英雄功勋，赢得了把棚屋（wigwam）扎进众帐篷围成的大圈之内的特权。

他还是对掉了牙的老人们最慷慨赠礼的人之一。凭此，他那圆锥形的居所才配得上涂红的烟盖。他为自己的荣誉自豪，夜夜不倦地历数自己的英勇事迹。虽然在棚屋火边，他常高谈自己的高位和广布的名声，可他最大的欢喜，却是一个黑眼睛的小女儿——她已结结实实地过了八个冬天。这时他坐在柔软的草地上，妻子在身旁低头做着珠饰活计，他唱着一支舞歌，用修长的手轻轻打着拍子。

看着眼前绿草地上小小的身影轻盈起舞，他精明的双眼因愉悦而柔和了。

===Original===
Tusee is taking her first dancing lesson. Her tightly-braided hair curves over both brown ears like a pair of crooked little horns which glisten in the summer sun.

With her snugly moccasined feet close together, and a wee hand at her belt to stay the long string of beads which hang from her bare neck, she bends her knees gently to the rhythm of her father's voice.

Now she ventures upon the earnest movement, slightly upward and sidewise, in a circle. At length the song drops into a closing cadence, and the little woman, clad in beaded deerskin, sits down beside the elder one. Like her mother, she sits upon her feet. In a brief moment the warrior repeats the last refrain. Again Tusee springs to her feet and dances to the swing of the few final measures.

===Chinese===
图西（Tusee）正在上她人生的第一堂舞蹈课。她编得紧紧的发辫贴着两只棕色耳朵弯垂下来，像一对弯弯的小角，在夏日阳光里闪闪发亮。

她穿着合脚的鹿皮鞋（moccasin），双脚并拢，一只小手按在腰带上，拢住从光脖颈垂下的长串珠链，随着父亲歌声的节拍，轻轻屈起膝盖。

如今她胆子大了些，做起认真的动作——微微向上、向侧，绕着圈。终于歌声落入收束的尾调，这个穿着缀珠鹿皮衣的小小人儿，挨着大的那位坐下来。像她母亲一样，她跪坐在自己的脚上。片刻之后，勇士又重复起最后一段副歌，图西再度一跃而起，应和着结尾几小节的旋律舞动。

===Original===
Just as the dance was finished, an elderly man, with short, thick hair loose about his square shoulders, rode into their presence from the rear, and leaped lightly from his pony's back. Dropping the rawhide rein to the ground, he tossed himself lazily on the grass. "Hunhe, you have returned soon," said the warrior, while extending a hand to his little daughter.

Quickly the child ran to her father's side and cuddled close to him, while he tenderly placed a strong arm about her. Both father and child, eyeing the figure on the grass, waited to hear the man's report.

"It is true," began the man, with a stranger's accent. "This is the night of the dance."

"Hunha!" muttered the warrior with some surprise.

===Chinese===
舞刚一跳完，一个上了年纪的男人从后面骑马来到他们跟前，又轻巧地从马背上翻身跃下。他短发浓密，披散在方正的肩头。他把生皮缰绳往地上一丢，懒洋洋地往草地上一躺。「哼嘿（Hunhe），你回来得挺快。」勇士说道，一面把手伸向小女儿。

孩子急忙跑到父亲身边，紧紧偎着他；勇士温存地用一条结实的胳膊搂住她。父女俩望着草地上躺着的那个人，等他开口回话。

「是真的，」那人开口，带着外乡口音，「今晚就跳舞。」

「嗯哈（Hunha）！」勇士低声惊呼。

===Original===
Propping himself upon his elbows, the man raised his face. His features were of the Southern type. From an enemy's camp he was taken captive long years ago by Tusee's father. But the unusual qualities of the slave had won the Sioux warrior's heart, and for the last three winters the man had had his freedom. He was made real man again. His hair was allowed to grow. However, he himself had chosen to stay in the warrior's family.

"Hunha!" again ejaculated the warrior father. Then turning to his little daughter, he asked, "Tusee, do you hear that?"

"Yes, father, and I am going to dance tonight!"

With these words she bounded out of his arm and frolicked about in glee. Hereupon the proud mother's voice rang out in a chiding laugh.

===Chinese===
那人用双肘撑起身子，抬起了脸。他的相貌属于南方部族一型。多年以前，他被图西的父亲从敌营里俘来。但这奴隶的过人品性赢得了这位苏族勇士的心，最近三个冬天，他已恢复了自由。他重新成了真正的人，头发也获准蓄了起来。不过他自己情愿留在勇士家中。

「嗯哈！」勇士父亲又脱口喊了一声。随后他转向小女儿，问道：「图西，听见了吗？」

「听见了，爸爸，今晚我要去跳舞！」

说着，她从父亲怀里蹦出来，欢天喜地地蹦跳嬉闹。这一来，骄傲的母亲发出一声嗔笑。

===Original===
"My child, in honor of your first dance your father must give a generous gift. His ponies are wild, and roam beyond the great hill. Pray, what has he fit to offer?" she questioned, the pair of puzzled eyes fixed upon her.

"A pony from the herd, mother, a fleet-footed pony from the herd!" Tusee shouted with sudden inspiration.

Pointing a small forefinger toward the man lying on the grass, she cried, "Uncle, you will go after the pony tomorrow!" And pleased with her solution of the problem, she skipped wildly about. Her childish faith in her elders was not conditioned by a knowledge of human limitations, but thought all things possible to grownups.

"Hähob!" exclaimed the mother, with a rising inflection, implying by the expletive that her child's buoyant spirit be not weighted with a denial.

===Chinese===
「孩子，为庆贺你的第一支舞，你父亲得送一份像样的礼物。可他的小马都是野性子，放牧在大山那头。你说说，他拿得出什么合适的东西？」她问；小姑娘那双困惑的眼睛定定地望着她。

「马群里挑一匹小马呀，妈妈，马群里挑一匹快腿的小马！」图西灵机一动，大声喊道。

她伸出小小的食指，指着躺在草地上的那人，喊道：「叔叔，明天你就去牵小马！」她对自己想出的办法满心得意，欢跳个不停。孩子对长辈的信赖，还不曾被「人力有限」的见识约束过；在她看来，大人们无所不能。

「哈霍布（Hähob）！」母亲扬着声调惊叹了一声，这感叹词里含着这样的意思：别让一口回绝压住孩子雀跃的心。

===Original===
Quickly to the hard request the man replied, "How! I go if Tusee tells me so!"

This delighted the little one, whose black eyes brimmed over with light. Standing in front of the strong man, she clapped her small, brown hands with joy.

"That makes me glad! My heart is good! Go, uncle, and bring a handsome pony!" she cried. In an instant she would have frisked away, but an impulse held her tilting where she stood. In the man's own tongue, for he had taught her many words and phrases, she exploded, "Thank you, good uncle, thank you!" then tore away from sheer excess of glee.

The proud warrior father, smiling and narrowing his eyes, muttered approval, "Howo! Hechetu!"

===Chinese===
对这桩难办的差事，那人立刻答道：「嗯哪！图西叫我去的，我就去！」

这话叫小家伙欢喜极了，黑眼睛里盛满了光彩。她站到那壮实汉子面前，棕色的小手欢喜地拍个不停。

「这可太好了！我心里真高兴！去吧，叔叔，牵一匹漂亮的小马来！」她喊道。转眼间她就要欢跳着跑开，可一时兴起，又踮着脚站在原地。那人的母语——他教过她许多词句——她张口便是一串：「谢谢你，好叔叔，谢谢你！」说完，乐得没了着落，一溜烟跑走了。

骄傲的勇士父亲眯起眼睛笑了，低声赞许道：「嚯喔（Howo）！正是这样（Hechetu）！」

===Original===
Like her mother, Tusee has finely pencilled eyebrows and slightly extended nostrils; but in her sturdiness of form she resembles her father.

A loyal daughter, she sits within her teepee making beaded deerskins for her father, while he longs to stave off her every suitor as all unworthy of his old heart's pride. But Tusee is not alone in her dwelling. Near the entranceway a young brave is half reclining on a mat. In silence he watches the petals of a wild rose growing on the soft buckskin. Quickly the young woman slips the beads on the silvery sinew thread, and works them into the pretty flower design. Finally, in a low, deep voice, the young man begins:

"The sun is far past the zenith. It is now only a man's height above the western edge of land. I hurried hither to tell you tomorrow I join the war party."

He pauses for reply, but the maid's head drops lower over her deerskin, and her lips are more firmly drawn together. He continues:

===Chinese===
图西像她母亲，眉毛如描画般纤细，鼻翼微微张开；可她结实的身形，却随了父亲。

转眼图西已长大成人。这个孝顺的女儿坐在自己的梯皮帐篷里，为父亲缝制缀珠的鹿皮衣；而父亲呢，一心想挡回每一个求婚者，觉得他们全配不上他这颗老来心头的骄傲。可图西的住处里并不是她一个人。靠近入口的地方，一个年轻的勇士半躺在席子上，默默看着柔软鹿皮（buckskin）上渐渐绽出的一朵野玫瑰的花瓣。年轻女子敏捷地把珠子穿上有银光的腱线，缀成那朵好看的花样。终于，年轻人用低沉的嗓音开口了：

「太阳早过了头顶，如今离大地的西缘只余一人高了。我急匆匆赶来，是要告诉你：明天我就随出征队出发。」

他停下来等她回答，可姑娘的头垂得更低，伏在鹿皮活计上，嘴唇也抿得更紧了。他接着说：

===Original===
"Last night in the moonlight I met your warrior father. He seemed to know I had just stepped forth from your teepee. I fear he did not like it, for though I greeted him, he was silent. I halted in his pathway. With what boldness I dared, while my heart was beating hard and fast, I asked him for his only daughter.

"Drawing himself erect to his tallest height, and gathering his loose robe more closely about his proud figure, he flashed a pair of piercing eyes upon me.

"'Young man,' said he, with a cold, slow voice that chilled me to the marrow of my bones, 'hear me. Naught but an enemy's scalp-lock, plucked fresh with your own hand, will buy Tusee for your wife,' Then he turned on his heel and stalked away."

===Chinese===
「昨夜月光下，我遇见了你那位勇士父亲。他像是知道我刚从你的帐篷里出来。我怕他不乐意，因为虽然我向他问好，他却一言不发。我拦在他的路上，鼓足了平生最大的胆子——心口擂鼓般又急又响——向他求娶他唯一的女儿。

「他直挺挺立起身，把松披的袍子往那副傲岸的身架上拢了拢，一双锐利的眼睛霍地逼视着我。

「『年轻人，』他说，嗓音又冷又慢，寒气直渗进我的骨髓，『听着。除非你亲手新拔下敌人的头皮发绺（scalp-lock），别的什么都换不来图西做你的妻子。』说完他转身便走，昂然去了。」

===Original===
Tusee thrusts her work aside. With earnest eyes she scans her lover's face.

"My father's heart is really kind. He would know if you are brave and true," murmured the daughter, who wished no ill-will between her two loved ones.

Then rising to go, the youth holds out a right hand. "Grasp my hand once firmly before I go, Hoye. Pray tell me, will you wait and watch for my return?"

Tusee only nods assent, for mere words are vain.

===Chinese===
图西把活计推到一边，用恳切的眼光细细端详爱人的脸。

「我父亲的心其实是善良的。他不过是想弄清，你敢不敢、真不真。」女儿喃喃道；她不愿自己爱着的两个人心存芥蒂。

年轻人起身要走，伸出右手。「走之前，用力握一握我的手吧，霍耶（Hoye）。告诉我，你会等着我、盼着我回来吗？」

图西只点头应允——言语在此已是多余。

===Original===
At early dawn the round campground awakes into song. Men and women sing of bravery and of triumph. They inspire the swelling breasts of the painted warriors mounted on prancing ponies bedecked with the green branches of trees.

Riding slowly around the great ring of cone-shaped teepees, here and there, a loud-singing warrior swears to avenge a former wrong, and thrusts a bare brown arm against the purple east, calling the Great Spirit to hear his vow. All having made the circuit, the singing war party gallops away southward.

Astride their ponies laden with food and deerskins, brave elderly women follow after their warriors. Among the foremost rides a young woman in elaborately beaded buckskin dress. Proudly mounted, she curbs with the single rawhide loop a wild-eyed pony.

It is Tusee on her father's warhorse. Thus the war party of Indian men and their faithful women vanish beyond the southern skyline.

===Chinese===
拂晓时分，环形营地醒来，歌声四起。男男女女唱着勇武，唱着凯旋，唱得那些跨在腾跃小马上、浑身彩绘的勇士胸膛起伏；马身上缀满青翠的树枝。

他们绕着圆锥形梯皮帐篷围成的大圈缓缓骑行，到处有高声歌唱的勇士发誓要报仇雪耻，把赤裸的棕色手臂直指紫色的东方，呼喊大神灵（Great Spirit）听见他的誓言。绕行完毕，这支唱着歌的出征队伍纵马向南飞驰而去。

勇敢的老妇人们骑着驮满食物和鹿皮的小马，跟在自家勇士身后。走在最前面的，是一个穿着精工缀珠鹿皮裙的年轻女子。她高傲地跨在马上，只用一根生皮缰环，就勒住了一匹眼神狂野的小马。

那是图西，骑着她父亲的战马。就这样，这支由印第安男人们和他们的忠贞女子组成的出征队伍，消失在南方的天际线外。

===Original===
A day's journey brings them very near the enemy's borderland. Nightfall finds a pair of twin teepees nestled in a deep ravine. Within one lounge the painted warriors, smoking their pipes and telling weird stories by the firelight, while in the other watchful women crouch uneasily about their center fire.

By the first gray light in the east the teepees are banished. They are gone. The warriors are in the enemy's camp, breaking dreams with their tomahawks. The women are hid away in secret places in the long thicketed ravine.

The day is far spent, the red sun is low over the west.

At length straggling warriors return, one by one, to the deep hollow. In the twilight they number their men. Three are missing. Of these absent ones two are dead; but the third one, a young man, is a captive to the foe.

===Chinese===
一天的行程把他们带到敌族边界附近。夜幕降临时，两座成对的梯皮帐篷藏进一道深深的峡谷。一座帐篷里，彩绘的勇士们懒懒地躺着，抽着烟斗，就着火光讲些神奇怪异的故事；另一座里，警醒的妇人们围着中央的火堆，不安地蜷伏着。

东方初露灰白，帐篷便收起了。他们走了。勇士们摸进敌营，用战斧（tomahawk）劈碎敌人的酣梦；妇人们则藏进长满灌木的深谷中隐秘的角落。

白日将尽，红日低垂在西天。

终于，零落的勇士们一个接一个回到深谷。暮色里他们清点人数，少了三个。失踪者中两个已死；第三个是个年轻人，落入了敌人手里做了俘虏。

===Original===
"He-he!" lament the warriors, taking food in haste.

In silence each woman, with long strides, hurries to and fro, tying large bundles on her pony's back. Under cover of night the war party must hasten homeward. Motionless, with bowed head, sits a woman in her hiding-place. She grieves for her lover.

In bitterness of spirit she hears the warriors' murmuring words. With set teeth she plans to cheat the hated enemy of their captive. In the meanwhile low signals are given, and the war party, unaware of Tusee's absence, steal quietly away. The soft thud of pony-hoofs grows fainter and fainter. The gradual hush of the empty ravine whirrs noisily in the ear of the young woman. Alert for any sound of footfalls nigh, she holds her breath to listen. Her right hand rests on a long knife in her belt. Ah, yes, she knows where her pony is hid, but not yet has she need of him. Satisfied that no danger is nigh, she prowls forth from her place of hiding. With a panther's tread and pace she climbs the high ridge beyond the low ravine. From thence she spies the enemy's campfires.

===Chinese===
「咳嘿！」勇士们悲叹着，匆匆吞咽食物。

妇人们个个沉默，迈着大步来回奔忙，把大包大包的行李捆上马背。趁着夜色掩护，出征队伍必须赶紧回程。有个女子在藏身之处一动不动地坐着，垂着头。她在为爱人悲伤。

她怀着满腔苦楚，听着勇士们低低的议论。她咬紧牙关，打定主意要从那可恨的敌人手里骗回他们的俘虏。这当儿，低低的暗号一声声传来，出征队伍悄悄开拔了，谁也没有察觉图西不在。小马蹄的轻响越来越远、越来越弱。空谷渐渐的寂静，在年轻女子的耳中嗡嗡作响。她警惕着近处的任何脚步声，屏息聆听。右手按在腰间的长刀上。啊，不错，她知道自己的小马藏在哪里，只是眼下还用不着它。确信近处没有危险之后，她从藏身之处悄然潜出。她迈着豹子般的步子和速度，攀上低谷之外的高岭，从那里窥望敌人的营火。

===Original===
Rooted to the barren bluff the slender woman's figure stands on the pinnacle of night, outlined against a starry sky. The cool night breeze wafts to her burning ear snatches of song and drum. With desperate hate she bites her teeth.

Tusee beckons the stars to witness. With impassioned voice and uplifted face she pleads:

"Great Spirit, speed me to my lover's rescue! Give me swift cunning for a weapon this night! All-powerful Spirit, grant me my warrior-father's heart, strong to slay a foe and mighty to save a friend!"

===Chinese===
纤细的身影钉在荒凉的高崖上，伫立在夜之绝顶，衬着满天星斗的夜空。清凉的夜风把断续的歌声和鼓声送进她滚烫的耳朵。她咬紧牙关，恨到极处。

图西召唤群星作证。她仰起脸，声音激越地祈求：

「大神灵啊，助我快去救我的爱人！今夜赐我机敏做兵器！全能的神灵啊，赐我勇士父亲的那颗心——杀敌时刚强，救友时豪雄！」

===Original===
In the midst of the enemy's campground, underneath a temporary dance-house, are men and women in gala-day dress. It is late in the night, but the merry warriors bend and bow their nude, painted bodies before a bright center fire. To the lusty men's voices and the rhythmic throbbing drum, they leap and rebound with feathered headgears waving.

Women with red-painted cheeks and long, braided hair sit in a large half-circle against the willow railing. They, too, join in the singing, and rise to dance with their victorious warriors.

Amid this circular dance arena stands a prisoner bound to a post, haggard with shame and sorrow. He hangs his disheveled head.

He stares with unseeing eyes upon the bare earth at his feet. With jeers and smirking faces the dancers mock the Dakota captive. Rowdy braves and small boys hoot and yell in derision.

===Chinese===
敌营中央，一座临时搭起的舞屋（dance-house）下面，是盛装的男男女女。夜已深了，欢腾的勇士们还在明亮的中央火堆前俯仰屈伸，赤裸的彩绘身体起伏舞动。应和着男人们高亢的嗓音和节奏铿锵的鼓点，他们腾跃弹跳，羽冠挥舞。

环形舞场的中央立着一个被绑在柱上的俘虏，因羞辱和悲伤而形容枯槁。他蓬乱的头垂着。
