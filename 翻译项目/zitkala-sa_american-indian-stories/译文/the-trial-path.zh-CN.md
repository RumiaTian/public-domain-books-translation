## The Trial Path / 审判之路

===Original===
It was an autumn night on the plain. The smoke-lapels of the cone-shaped teepee flapped gently in the breeze. From the low night sky, with its myriad fire points, a large bright star peeped in at the smoke-hole of the wigwam between its fluttering lapels, down upon two Dakotas talking in the dark. The mellow stream from the star above, a maid of twenty summers, on a bed of sweetgrass, drank in with her wakeful eyes. On the opposite side of the teepee, beyond the centre fireplace, the grandmother spread her rug. Though once she had lain down, the telling of a story has aroused her to a sitting posture.

Her eyes are tight closed. With a thin palm she strokes her windshorn hair.

“Yes, my grandchild, the legend says the large bright stars are wise old warriors, and the small dim ones are handsome young braves,” she reiterates, in a high, tremulous voice.

“Then this one peeping in at the smoke-hole yonder is my dear old grandfather,” muses the young woman, in long-drawn-out words.

Her soft rich voice floats through the darkness within the teepee, over the cold ashes heaped on the centre fire, and passes into the ear of the toothless old woman, who sits dumb in silent reverie. Thence it flies on swifter wing over many winter snows, till at last it cleaves the warm light atmosphere of her grandfather’s youth. From there her grandmother made answer:

===Chinese===
那是平原上的一个秋夜。圆锥形梯皮帐篷（teepee）的烟盖在微风中轻轻掀动。低垂的夜空缀满万点火光，一颗大而明亮的星星从两扇飘动的烟盖之间，探进棚屋（wigwam）的烟孔，俯望着黑暗中说话的两个达科他（Dakota）人。头顶那颗星星淌下的柔光，被一个二十个夏天的姑娘，就着无眠的眼睛，躺在香草（sweetgrass）铺的床上，一口口饮了进去。帐篷的另一头，中央火堆的那边，祖母铺开她的毯子。她本来已经躺下了，一个故事的讲述又唤得她坐起身来。

她的双眼紧闭着。一只干瘦的手掌抚着被风磨短的头发。

「是啊，孙儿，传说讲，那些又大又亮的星星是睿智的老勇士，那些小小的、暗淡的，是英俊的年轻勇士。」她用高亢而颤抖的嗓音，又说了一遍。

「那么，那边从烟孔往里偷看的这一颗，就是亲爱的老祖父了。」年轻女子拖着长长的声调，出了会儿神。

她那柔润丰美的声音，飘过帐篷里的黑暗，掠过中央火堆上堆着的冷灰，落进掉了牙的老妇人的耳朵——老妇人正默默坐着，静静出神。从那里，这声音又振起更快的翅膀，飞过许多个冬天的雪，终于破开她祖父青年时代那温暖明亮的空气。从那里，老祖母开了腔：

===Original===
“Listen! I am young again. It is the day of your grandfather’s death. The elder one, I mean, for there were two of them. They were like twins, though they were not brothers. They were friends, inseparable! All things, good and bad, they shared together, save one, which made them mad. In that heated frenzy the younger man slew his most intimate friend. He killed his elder brother, for long had their affection made them kin.”

The voice of the old woman broke. Swaying her stooped shoulders to and fro as she sat upon her feet, she muttered vain exclamations beneath her breath. Her eyes, closed tight against the night, beheld behind them the light of bygone days. They saw again a rolling black cloud spread itself over the land. Her ear heard the deep rumbling of a tempest in the west. She bent low a cowering head, while angry thunderbirds shrieked across the sky. “*Heyã! heyã!*” (No! no!) groaned the toothless grandmother at the fury she had awakened. But the glorious peace afterward, when yellow sunshine made the people glad, now lured her memory onward through the storm.

“How fast, how loud my heart beats as I listen to the messenger’s horrible tale!” she ejaculates. “From the fresh grave of the murdered man he hurried to our wigwam. Deliberately crossing his bare shins, he sat down unbidden beside my father, smoking a long-stemmed pipe. He had scarce caught his breath when, panting, he began:

“ ‘He was an only son, and a much-adored brother.’

===Chinese===
「听着！我又年轻了。那是你祖父死去的那一天。我说的是年长的那个，因为他们有两个人。他们像一对双生子，虽然并不是兄弟。他们是朋友，分不开的朋友！什么福祸，他们都同担共享，只除了一样——就是这一样，叫他们发了狂。就在那火热的疯癫里，年轻的那一个杀死了他最亲密的朋友。他杀了他的兄长，因为长久的情谊早已叫他们成了骨肉。」

老妇人的声音哽住了。她跪坐着，佝偻的双肩前后摇晃，低声发出一串含混的感叹。她那紧闭着抵挡黑夜的眼睛，在眼睑后面看见了往昔岁月的光。它们又看见一团滚动的黑云在大地上铺开。她的耳朵听见西边暴风雨深沉的轰鸣。愤怒的雷鸟尖啸着掠过天空时，她低低缩起头。「嘿亚！嘿亚！」（Heyã! heyã!，不！不！）掉了牙的老祖母呻吟着，怪自己惊醒了这场狂怒。可随后那辉煌的安宁——金黄的太阳照得人们欢喜——此刻正引着她的记忆，穿过风暴继续前行。

「听报信人讲那可怕的事，我的心跳得多快、多响啊！」她脱口说道。「他从被害人的新坟上，急匆匆赶到我们家棚屋。他不慌不忙地盘起光腿，也不等人请，就在我父亲身边坐下，抽起一只长杆烟斗。气还没喘匀，他就喘着开了口：

「『他是独子，是人人疼爱的兄弟。』

===Original===
“With wild, suspecting eyes he glanced at me as if I were in league with the man-killer, my lover. My father, exhaling sweet-scented smoke, assented—‘How.’ Then interrupting the ‘*Eya*’ on the lips of the round-eyed talebearer, he asked, ‘My friend, will you smoke?’ He took the pipe by its red-stone bowl, and pointed the long slender stem toward the man. ‘Yes, yes, my friend,’ replied he, and reached out a long brown arm.

“For many heartthrobs he puffed out the blue smoke, which hung like a cloud between us. But even through the smoke-mist I saw his sharp black eyes glittering toward me. I longed to ask what doom awaited the young murderer, but dared not open my lips, lest I burst forth into screams instead. My father plied the question. Returning the pipe, the man replied: ‘Oh, the chieftain and his chosen men have had counsel together. They have agreed it is not safe to allow a man-killer loose in our midst. He who kills one of our tribe is an enemy, and must suffer the fate of a foe.’

“My temples throbbed like a pair of hearts!

“While I listened, a crier passed by my father’s teepee. Mounted, and swaying with his pony’s steps, he proclaimed in a loud voice these words (hark! I hear them now!): ‘Ho-po! Give ear, all you people. A terrible deed is done. Two friends—ay, brothers in heart—have quarreled together. Now one lies buried on the hill, while the other sits, a dreaded man-killer, within his dwelling.’ Says our chieftain: ‘He who kills one of our tribe commits the offense of an enemy. As such he must be tried. Let the father of the dead man choose the mode of torture or taking of life. He has suffered livid pain, and he alone can judge how great the punishment must be to avenge his wrong.’ It is done.

===Chinese===
「他眼里带着狂乱和疑惧，瞟了我一眼，仿佛我同那杀人凶手——我的爱人——是一伙的。我父亲吐出一口香甜的烟，应了一声——『嗯哪。』接着他打断了那瞪圆了眼的报信人嘴边正要出口的『埃亚』（Eya，是呀），问道：『朋友，抽烟吗？』他捏住红石烟斗的斗，把细长的烟杆朝那人递过去。『好，好，朋友。』那人应着，伸出一条长长的棕色胳膊。

「他一口一口喷出蓝烟，烟像云一样悬在我们中间，喷了好多下。可隔着烟雾，我还是看见他那锐利的黑眼睛朝我一闪一闪。我多想问问那年轻的杀人者等着他的是什么下场，却不敢开口，只怕一张嘴，喊出来的会是尖叫。发问的是我父亲。那人把烟斗递还，答道：『哦，首领和他选中的几个人已经商议过了。他们议定，放一个杀人的在大家中间乱走，不是稳妥的事。杀我们族人的，就是仇敌，就该受仇敌的下场。』

「我的两边太阳穴突突地跳，像一对心脏！

「我正听着，一个传令人从我父亲的帐篷前经过。他骑着马，随着小马的步子一颠一颠，大声宣告这些话（听！我如今还听得见！）：『嚯-波！（Ho-po!）全体听着。一件可怕的事做下了。两个朋友——唉，心里的兄弟——吵翻了。如今一个埋在山上，另一个坐在自己屋里，成了人人惧怕的杀人者。』我们首领说：『杀我们族人的，犯的是仇敌之罪。既是仇敌，就必须受审。让死者的父亲挑选用刑或取命的法子。他受了青一块紫一块的痛楚，唯独他能掂量，多大的惩罚才报得了他的冤仇。』就这么定了。

“ ‘Come, everyone, to witness the judgment of a father upon him who was once his son’s best friend. A wild pony is now lassoed. The man-killer must mount and ride the ranting beast. Stand you all in two parallel lines from the centre teepee of the bereaved family to the wigwam opposite in the great outer ring. Between you, in the wide space, is the given trialway. From the outer circle the rider must mount and guide his pony toward the centre teepee. If, having gone the entire distance, the man-killer gains the centre teepee still sitting on the pony’s back, his life is spared and pardon given. But should he fall, then he himself has chosen death.’

“The crier’s words now cease. A lull holds the village breathless. Then hurrying feet tear along, swish, swish, through the tall grass. Sobbing women hasten toward the trialway. The muffled groan of the round campground is unbearable. With my face hid in the folds of my blanket, I run with the crowd toward the open place in the outer circle of our village. In a moment the two long files of solemn-faced people mark the path of the public trial. Ah! I see strong men trying to lead the lassoed pony, pitching and rearing, with white foam flying from his mouth. I choke with pain as I recognize my handsome lover desolately alone, striding with set face toward the lassoed pony. ‘Do not fall! Choose life and me!’ I cry in my breast, but over my lips I hold my thick blanket.

“In an instant he has leaped astride the frightened beast, and the men have let go their hold. Like an arrow sprung from a strong bow, the pony, with extended nostrils, plunges halfway to the centre teepee. With all his might the rider draws the strong reins in. The pony halts with wooden legs. The rider is thrown forward by force, but does not fall. Now the maddened creature pitches, with flying heels. The line of men and women sways outward. Now it is back in place, safe from the kicking, snorting thing.

===Chinese===
「『都来吧，来看看一位父亲，怎样审判他曾是自己儿子最好的朋友的那个人。一匹野马已经套住了。杀人者必须骑上这匹狂跳的牲口。大家站成平行的两列，从丧家中央的帐篷起，到外面大圈对面那座棚屋止。两列之间那片开阔地，就是指定的审判之路。骑手必须从外圈上马，驾驭小马朝中央帐篷走。要是走完全程，杀人者仍旧骑在马背上到达中央帐篷，他的命就留下，赦免也给了。可他要是摔下来，那就是他自己选了死。』

「传令人的话音落了。一阵寂静攥得全村屏住了呼吸。随后急促的脚步声响起，嗖，嗖，撕开高草。呜咽的妇人们奔向审判之路。环形营地那闷闷的哀吟让人受不住。我把脸藏进毯子的褶子里，随人群朝村外圈的空地跑去。转眼间，两长列神色凝重的人，标出了这场公审的路。啊！我看见几个壮实的汉子，正牵着那匹套住的野马，马又尥又立，嘴里喷着白沫。我认出我那俊美的爱人孤零零地立着，绷着脸，大步朝那匹套住的马走去，痛得我喉咙哽住。『别摔下来！选命，选我！』我在心里喊，厚毯子却死死捂住我的嘴。

“The pony is fierce, with its large black eyes bulging out of their sockets. With humped back and nose to the ground, it leaps into the air. I shut my eyes. I can not see him fall.

“A loud shout goes up from the hoarse throats of men and women. I look. So! The wild horse is conquered. My lover dismounts at the doorway of the centre wigwam. The pony, wet with sweat and shaking with exhaustion, stands like a guilty dog at his master’s side. Here at the entranceway of the teepee sit the bereaved father, mother, and sister. The old warrior father rises. Stepping forward two long strides, he grasps the hand of the murderer of his only son. Holding it so the people can see, he cries, with compassionate voice, ‘My son!’ A murmur of surprise sweeps like a puff of sudden wind along the lines.

“The mother, with swollen eyes, with her hair cut square with her shoulders, now rises. Hurrying to the young man, she takes his right hand. ‘My son!’ she greets him. But on the second word her voice shook, and she turned away in sobs.

“The young people rivet their eyes upon the young woman. She does not stir. With bowed head, she sits motionless. The old warrior speaks to her. ‘Shake hands with the young brave, my little daughter. He was your brother’s friend for many years. Now he must be both friend and brother to you,’

“Hereupon the girl rises. Slowly reaching out her slender hand, she cries, with twitching lips, ‘My brother!’ The trial ends.”

“Grandmother!” exploded the girl on the bed of sweetgrass. “Is this true?”

“Tosh!” answered the grandmother, with a warmth in her voice. “It is all true. During the fifteen winters of our wedded life many ponies passed from our hands, but this little winner, Ohiyesa, was a constant member of our family. At length, on that sad day your grandfather died, Ohiyesa was killed at the grave.”

Though the various groups of stars which move across the sky, marking the passing of time, told how the night was in its zenith, the old Dakota woman ventured an explanation of the burial ceremony.

===Chinese===
「听了这话，姑娘站起身。她缓缓伸出纤细的手，嘴唇哆嗦着喊道：『我的哥哥！』审判结束了。」

「祖母！」香草床上那姑娘脱口喊道。「这是真的吗？」

「嗐！」祖母答道，嗓音里透着暖意。「全是真的。我们成亲后的十五个冬天里，经手过好多好多小马，可这匹小功臣，奥希耶萨（Ohiyesa），一直是我们家的一员。最后，你祖父去世的那个悲伤的日子，奥希耶萨在坟前被杀掉了。」

一簇簇星群在天上移动，标着时辰的流逝，说明夜已到了中天，可这位达科他老妇还是壮着胆子，讲起了葬礼的缘由。

“My grandchild, I have scarce ever breathed the sacred knowledge in my heart. Tonight I must tell you one of them. Surely you are old enough to understand.

“Our wise medicine-man said I did well to hasten Ohiyesa after his master. Perchance on the journey along the ghostpath your grandfather will weary, and in his heart wish for his pony.
