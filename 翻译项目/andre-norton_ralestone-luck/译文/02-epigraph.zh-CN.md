===Original===

> *How hold ye Lorne?*
 
 
===Chinese===
> *汝何以守洛恩？*

===Original===
 By the oak leaf,
 

 By the sea wave,
 

 By the broadsword blade,
 

 Thus hold we Lorne!
> *The oak leaf is dust,*
 
 
===Chinese===
以橡树之叶，

以沧海之波，

以阔剑之锋，

如此守我洛恩！
> *橡叶已成尘，*

===Original===
 
 *The sea wave is gone,*
 
 

 
 *The broadsword is rust,*
 
 

 
 *How now hold ye Lorne?*
 
 

 By our Luck, thus hold we Lorne!
===Chinese===
*海波已无痕，*

*阔剑已锈钝，*

*而今汝何以守洛恩？*

以我家运，守我洛恩！
