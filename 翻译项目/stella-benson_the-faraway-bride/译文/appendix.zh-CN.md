### I / 一
===Original===

The book of the words of Tobit, son of Tobiel, the son of Ananiel, the son of Aduel, the son of Gabael, of the seed of Asael, of the tithe of Nephthali; Who in the time of Enemessar king of the Assyrians was led captive out of Thisbe which is at the right hand of that city, which is called properly Nephthali in Galilee above Aser.

I Tobit have walked all the days of my life in the way of truth and justice, and I did many almsdeeds to my brethren, and my nation, who came with me to Nineve, into the land of the Assyrians. And when I was in mine own country, in the land of Israel, being but young, all the tribe of Nephthali my father fell from the house of Jerusalem, which was chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, that all the tribes should sacrifice there, where the temple of the habitation of the most High was consecrated and built for all ages. Now all the tribes which together revolted, and the house of my father Nephthali, sacrificed unto the heifer Baal. But I alone went often to Jerusalem at the feasts, as it was ordained unto all the people of Israel by an everlasting decree, having the firstfruits and tenths of increase, with that which was first shorn; and them gave I at the altar to the priests the children of Aaron. The first tenth part of all increase I gave to the sons of Aaron, who ministered at Jerusalem; another tenth part I sold away, and went, and spent it every year at Jerusalem: And the third I gave to them to whom it was meet, as Debora my father’s mother had commanded me, because I was left an orphan by my father.

Furthermore, when I was come to the age of a man, I married Anna of mine own kindred, and of her I begat Tobias.

托比所记的话——托比是托比勒的儿子，亚拿尼的孙子，亚杜勒的曾孙，加贝拉的后裔，属亚撒利的子孙，拿弗他利支派中十分取一的人。他在亚述王以尼米撒的日子，被掳出了提斯比；那城位于加利利，在称为拿弗他利之地、亚设的上边。

我托比终生行走真理与公义之道，向我的弟兄和我本族的人行了许多善事；他们与我一同被掳到亚述地的尼尼微。我幼年在本国以色列地的时候，我父家拿弗他利全支派离弃了耶路撒冷——那城是从以色列众支派中选出来的，众支派都当在那里献祭，因为至高者圣所的殿在那里被祝圣建立，直到万代。如今那些一同背叛的支派，并我父家拿弗他利，都向牛犊巴力献祭了。惟独我常常在节期上耶路撒冷去，正如给以色列众人所定永久的条例，带着初熟的果子同十分之一的出产，以及初剪的羊毛；我把这些在祭坛前交给亚伦子孙作祭司的。一切出产的第一个十分之一，我给了在耶路撒冷供职的亚伦的子孙；又一个十分之一我变卖了，年年上耶路撒冷去，在那里花用；第三个十分之一我给了当给的人，照我祖母底波拉所吩咐我的，因为我父亲去世，我成了孤儿。

此外，我成年之后，娶了本族的女子亚拿为妻，与她生了多比雅。

And when we were carried away captives to Nineve, all my brethren and those that were of my kindred did eat of the bread of the Gentiles. But I kept myself from eating; Because I remembered God with all my heart. And the most High gave me grace and favour before Enemessar, so that I was his purveyor. And I went into Media, and left in trust with Gabael, the brother of Gabrias, at Rages a city of Media ten talents of silver. Now when Enemessar was dead, Sennacherib his son reigned in his stead; whose estate was troubled, that I could not go into Media. And in the time of Enemessar I gave many alms to my brethren, and gave my bread to the hungry, And my clothes to the naked: and if I saw any of my nation dead, or cast about the walls of Nineve, I buried him. And if the king Sennacherib had slain any, when he was come, and fled from Judea, I buried them privily; for in his wrath he killed many; but the bodies were not found, when they were sought for of the king. And when one of the Ninevites went and complained of me to the king, that I buried them, and hid myself; understanding that I was sought for to be put to death, I withdrew myself for fear. Then all my goods were forcibly taken away, neither was there anything left me, beside my wife Anna and my son Tobias.

And there passed not five and fifty days before two of his sons killed him, and they fled into the mountains of Ararath; and Sarchedonus his son reigned in his stead; who appointed over his father’s accounts, and over all his affairs, Achiarcharus my brother Anael’s son. And Achiarcharus intreating for me, I returned to Nineve. Now Achiarcharus was cupbearer, and keeper of the signet, and steward, and overseer of the accounts: and Sarchedonus appointed him next unto him: and he was my brothers son.

我们被掳到尼尼微之后，我的弟兄和亲族都吃外邦人的饼，我却约束自己不吃；因为我全心记念神。至高者使我在以尼米撒面前蒙恩得宠，我便作了他的采买。我往玛代去，把十他连得银子托付给加布利亚的兄弟加贝拉，存在玛代的城拉吉斯。以尼米撒死了以后，他儿子西拿基立接续作王；他的国动荡不安，我不能往玛代去了。在以尼米撒的日子，我向弟兄们广行施舍，把饼给饥饿的人，把衣服给赤身的人；我若看见我本族的人死了，或被抛在尼尼微城垣四围，我便葬埋他。犹大人逃亡出来的时候，亚述王西拿基立若杀了人，我也悄悄地葬埋；因他在怒中杀了多人，只是王命人搜寻的时候，尸首都寻不见。有尼尼微人去王那里告我埋尸的事；我明白人寻索我、要置我于死，便惧怕逃走了。于是我一切的财物都被强行夺去，除了我妻子亚拿和我儿子多比雅，什么也没有留给我。

过了不到五十五日，他的两个儿子杀了他，逃往亚拉腊山；他儿子撒基多努接续作王，派我兄弟亚拿埃的儿子阿希加管理他父亲的账目和一切事务。阿希加为我求情，我就回了尼尼微。原来阿希加作酒政，掌玺印，又作家宰和账目总管；撒基多努立他在自己身边，仅次于己；他是我兄弟的儿子。

### II / 二
===Original===

Now when I was come home again, and my wife Anna was restored unto me, with my son Tobias, in the feast of Pentecost, which is the holy feast of the seven weeks, there was a good dinner prepared me, in the which I sat down to eat. And when I saw abundance of meat, I said to my son, Go and bring what poor man soever thou shalt find out of our brethren, who is mindful of the Lord; and, lo, I tarry for thee. But he came again and said, Father, one of our nation is strangled, and is cast out in the market place. Then before I had tasted of any meat, I started up, and took him up into a room until the going down of the sun. Then I returned, and washed myself, and ate my meat in heaviness, Remembering that prophecy of Amos, as he said, Your feasts shall be turned into mourning, and all your mirth into lamentation. Therefore I wept: and after the going down of the sun I went and made a grave, and buried him. But my neighbours mocked me, and said, This man is not yet afraid to be put to death for this matter: who fled away; and yet, lo, he burieth the dead again. The same night also I returned from the burial, and slept by the wall of my courtyard, being polluted, and my face was uncovered: And I knew not that there were sparrows in the wall, and mine eyes being open, the sparrows muted warm dung into mine eyes, and a whiteness came in mine eyes; and I went to the physicians, but they helped me not: moreover Achiarcharus did nourish me, until I went into Elymais. And my wife Anna did take women’s works to do. And when she had sent them home to the owners, they paid her wages, and gave her also besides a kid. And when it was in my house, and began to cry, I said unto her, From whence is this kid? is it not stolen? render it to the owners; for it is not lawful to eat anything that is stolen. But she replied upon me, It was given for a gift more than the wages. Howbeit I did not believe her, but bade her render it to the owners: and I was abashed at her. But she replied upon me, Where are thine alms and thy righteous deeds? behold, thou and all thy works are known.

我回到家里，我妻子亚拿和我儿子多比雅都归回我这里；那时正逢五旬节，就是七七的圣节；有人为我备了丰盛的筵席，我便坐下吃喝。我看见肉极多，就对我儿子说：你去，把我们弟兄中凡谨记主的穷人带一个来；看哪，我等着你。他却回来说：父亲啊，我们族中有一个人被勒死了，抛在市场上。我尚未尝任何食物，就跳起来，把尸首抬进一间屋里，直放到日落。随后我回来洗了身，怀着愁苦吃了饭，记起阿摩司的预言，如他所说：你们的节期要变为悲哀，一切欢乐要变为哀哭。我便哭泣；日落之后，我去掘了一座坟，把他葬埋了。邻舍都讥诮我，说：这人还不怕为这事丧命；他从前逃走了，看哪，如今又埋葬死人。当夜我从葬礼回来，睡在我院墙边；我因沾染污秽，脸没有蒙盖；我不知道墙上有麻雀，我睁着眼，麻雀把温热的粪垂进我眼里，我的眼睛就生了白翳；我去求医生，他们却不能帮助我。此外，阿希加供养我，直到我往以利买去。我妻子亚拿做女人的活计。她把活计送还给主人，他们就给她工钱，另外又送她一只小山羊。山羊到了我家里叫起来，我对她说：这山羊是从哪里来的？不是偷来的吧？归还给原主吧；因为吃任何偷来的东西都是不合法的。她却回答我说：这是礼物，是超出工钱送的。我却不信她，吩咐她归还原主；我为这事羞愧。她回答我说：你的施舍和你的义行在哪里呢？看哪，你和你一切的行为，人都知道了。

### III / 三
===Original===

Then I being grieved did weep, and in my sorrow prayed, saying, O Lord, thou art just, and all thy works and all thy ways are mercy and truth, and thou judgest truly and justly forever. Remember me, and look on me, punish me not for my sins and ignorances, and the sins of my fathers, who have sinned before thee: For they obeyed not thy commandments: wherefore thou hast delivered us for a spoil, and unto captivity, and unto death, and for a proverb of reproach to all the nations among whom we are dispersed. And now thy judgements are many and true: deal with me according to my sins and my fathers’: because we have not kept thy commandments, neither have walked in truth before thee. Now therefore deal with me as seemeth best unto thee, and command my spirit to be taken from me, that I may be dissolved, and become earth: for it is profitable for me to die rather than to live, because I have heard false reproaches, and have much sorrow: command therefore that I may now be delivered out of this distress, and go into the everlasting place: turn not thy face away from me.

It came to pass the same day, that in Ecbatane a city of Media Sara the daughter of Raguel was also reproached by her father’s maids; Because that she had been married to seven husbands, whom Asmodeus the evil spirit had killed, before they had lain with her. Dost thou not know, said they, that thou hast strangled thine husbands? thou hast had already seven husbands, neither wast thou named after any of them. Wherefore dost thou beat us for them? if they be dead, go thy ways after them, let us never see of thee either son or daughter.

我忧愁起来便哭泣，在愁苦中祷告，说：主啊，你是公义的，你的一切作为、一切道路都是怜悯与诚实，你审判真实公义，直到永远。求你记念我，眷顾我，不要因我的罪和我列祖在你面前所犯的罪惩罚我；因为他们违背了你的诫命，你便将我们交给人掳掠、被掳、死亡，作我们在其中被赶散的万国中的笑柄。如今你的审判许多、而且真实：求你按我和我列祖的罪待我，因为我们没有遵守你的诫命，没有在你面前行真理。如今求你看怎样好就怎样待我，吩咐收回我的灵，使我可以消解，成为尘土；因为我死了比活着于我更好，因为我听见了虚假的毁谤，又有许多忧愁：求你吩咐救我脱离这困苦，进入那永远的地方；不要向我掩面。

同一天，在玛代的城埃克巴塔纳，拉贵尔的女儿撒拉也受她父亲使女们的羞辱；因为她曾嫁过七个丈夫，恶灵阿斯摩太在他们尚未与她同房之前，就把他们都杀了。使女们说：你不知道你勒死了你的丈夫们么？你已经有七个丈夫了，连一个的名字你也没有得着。你为什么为他们打我们呢？他们若死了，你去跟从他们吧，我们再不要看见你的儿子或女儿。

When she heard these things, she was very sorrowful, so that she thought to have strangled herself; and she said, I am the only daughter of my father, and if I do this, it shall be a reproach unto him, and I shall bring his old age with sorrow unto the grave. Then she prayed toward the window, and said, Blessed art thou, O Lord my God, and thine holy and glorious name is blessed and honourable forever: let all thy works praise thee forever. And now, O Lord, I set mine eyes and my face toward thee, And say, Take me out of the earth, that I may hear no more the reproach. Thou knowest, Lord, that I am pure from all sin with man, And that I never polluted my name, nor the name of my father, in the land of my captivity: I am the only daughter of my father, neither hath he any child to be his heir, neither any near kinsman, nor any son of his alive, to whom I may keep myself for a wife: my seven husbands are already dead; and why should I live? but if it please not thee that I should die, command some regard to be had of me, and pity taken of me, that I hear no more reproach.

So the prayers of them both were heard before the majesty of the great God. And Raphael was sent to heal them both, that is, to scale away the whiteness of Tobit’s eyes, and to give Sara the daughter of Raguel for a wife to Tobias the son of Tobit; and to bind Asmodeus the evil spirit; because she belonged to Tobias by right of inheritance. The selfsame time came Tobit home, and entered into his house, and Sara the daughter of Raguel came down from her upper chamber.

她听见这些话，心里非常忧愁，甚至想要自缢；她说：我是父亲独生的女儿，我若这样行，必成为他的羞辱，我要使他的白发悲悲惨惨地下阴间。于是她朝着窗户祷告，说：主我的神啊，你是应当称颂的，你圣洁荣耀的名配得称颂尊荣，直到永远；愿你一切的作为永远赞美你。主啊，如今我举目仰望你，说：求你使我离开大地，使我不再听见羞辱。主啊，你知道我洁净，未与男人犯罪；我没有污秽我的名，也没有污秽我父亲的名，在我被掳之地；我是父亲独生的女儿，他没有别的孩子作后嗣，也没有近亲，也没有在世的儿子，可以让我为他守身待嫁；我的七个丈夫都死了；我为什么还活着呢？你若不喜悦我死，求你吩咐人看顾我，怜恤我，使我不再听见羞辱。

他们二人的祷告，都在伟大的神威严之前蒙了垂听。拉斐耳被差去医治他们二人，就是除去托比眼睛的白翳，把拉贵尔的女儿撒拉给托比的儿子多比雅为妻，并捆绑恶灵阿斯摩太；因为她按继承之理原属于多比雅。正当那时，托比回到家里进了屋，拉贵尔的女儿撒拉也从她的楼上下来。

他叫了他来，说：我儿，我死了以后，你要葬埋我；不可轻看你的母亲，要终生尊敬她，行她喜悦的事，不可使她忧愁。我儿，要记念我们的主神，终生不可定意犯罪，也不可违背他的诫命；一生一世行正直的事，不可随从不义的道路。因为你若行事诚实，你的事务必顺利成全，凡行义的人也是如此。要按你的财物施舍；施舍的时候，眼不可嫉视，脸也不可转离穷人，神的脸就不转离你。你若富足，就按富足施舍；你若只有少许，也不要怕按那少许施舍：因为你为自己积存美好的财宝，以备急难之日。因为施舍能救人脱离死亡，不容他进入黑暗。施舍在至高者面前，对一切施与的人都是美好的礼物。我儿，要谨防一切淫乱的事，首先要娶本族之女为妻，不可娶不属你父支派的外邦女子为妻：因为我们乃是众先知的儿女——挪亚、亚伯拉罕、以撒、雅各。我儿，要记念我们的列祖从起初都娶本族的女子为妻，在他们儿女身上蒙福，他们的后裔必得那地为业。所以，我儿，要爱你的弟兄，不可在心里轻看你本族的儿女，以致不娶他们中间的女子为妻；因为骄傲里有败坏和许多祸患，淫乱里有衰残和极大的穷乏：淫乱是饥荒之母。凡为你作工之人的工钱，不可在你那里过夜，要亲手当日给他：因为你若服事神，他也必报偿你。我儿，你行的一切事都要谨慎，你一切的谈吐都要有智慧。你所恨恶的，不可行在别人身上；不可喝酒至醉；醉酒也不可与你同行在路上。
