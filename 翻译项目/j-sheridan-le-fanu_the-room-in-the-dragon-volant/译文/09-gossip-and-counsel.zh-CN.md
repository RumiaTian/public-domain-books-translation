## IX Gossip and Counsel / IX 闲话与忠告

===Original===
My eventful journey was over, at last. I sat in my hotel window looking out upon brilliant Paris, which had, in a moment, recovered all its gaiety, and more than its accustomed bustle. Everyone has read of the kind of excitement that followed the catastrophe of Napoleon, and the second restoration of the Bourbons. I need not, therefore, even if, at this distance, I could, recall and describe my experiences and impressions of the peculiar aspect of Paris, in those strange times. It was, to be sure, my first visit. But, often as I have seen it since, I don’t think I ever saw that delightful capital in a state, pleasurably, so excited and exciting.

I had been two days in Paris, and had seen all sorts of sights, and experienced none of that rudeness and insolence of which others complained, from the exasperated officers of the defeated French army.

I must say this, also. My romance had taken complete possession of me; and the chance of seeing the object of my dream, gave a secret and delightful interest to my rambles and drives in the streets and environs, and my visits to the galleries and other sights of the metropolis.

I had neither seen nor heard of Count or Countess, nor had the Marquis d’Harmonville made any sign. I had quite recovered the strange indisposition under which I had suffered during my night journey.

===Chinese===
我这趟多事的旅程总算结束了。我坐在旅馆窗前，眺望着光辉灿烂的巴黎——它转眼间已恢复了全部的欢快，还添了不止平常的喧闹。拿破仑覆亡、波旁王朝第二次复辟之后那种举国骚动的情景，人人都读到过。因此，即便隔着这么多年我还回想得起、描绘得出，我也无须追述我在那些奇异时日里对巴黎特殊面貌的亲历与观感。这的确是我头一回到访。可后来虽屡屡重见，我不记得再见过这座可爱的都城处于这样一种令人愉悦的、如此亢奋而又如此激荡人心的状态。

我到巴黎已经两天，各处景致都看过了，却不曾领教别人抱怨过的那种吃了败仗的法国军官怒气冲冲的粗鲁无礼。

还有一句也得说：我的罗曼史已把我完全占据；只要有可能撞见我梦中的那个人，我在街巷郊野的漫步驱车、在都城画廊名胜的游览，便都平添了一种秘而不宣的、甜滋滋的兴味。

伯爵和伯爵夫人我既没见着，也没听到消息；德·哈蒙维尔侯爵也不曾有半点表示。夜路上害的那场古怪的不适，我已完全复原了。

===Original===
It was now evening, and I was beginning to fear that my patrician acquaintance had quite forgotten me, when the waiter presented me the card of “Monsieur Droqville;” and, with no small elation and hurry, I desired him to show the gentleman up.

In came the Marquis d’Harmonville, kind and gracious as ever.

“I am a night-bird at present,” said he, so soon as we had exchanged the little speeches which are usual. “I keep in the shade, during the daytime, and even now I hardly ventured to come in a close carriage. The friends for whom I have undertaken a rather critical service, have so ordained it. They think all is lost, if I am known to be in Paris. First let me present you with these orders for my box. I am so vexed that I cannot command it oftener during the next fortnight; during my absence, I had directed my secretary to give it for any night to the first of my friends who might apply, and the result is, that I find next to nothing left at my disposal.”

I thanked him very much.

===Chinese===
这时已是傍晚，我正开始担心我那位贵族相识把我忘了个干净，侍者却给我递上了「德罗克维尔先生」的名片；我按捺不住心头的高兴和忙乱，立刻请他把这位先生请上楼来。

进来的是德·哈蒙维尔侯爵，一如往常那般和蔼可亲。

照例的寒暄一完，他便说：「我眼下是只夜鸟儿。白天我藏在不亮的地方，就是现在，也只敢关着马车窗来。托我办这桩相当紧要的差事的朋友们，如此吩咐了下来。他们认为，一旦让人知道我在巴黎，全盘皆输。先让我把这几张我包厢的入场券送给您。真叫我不痛快，往后两个星期我没法更多地支配它；我不在的期间，曾吩咐秘书：无论哪一晚，先来申请的朋友就先给；结果是，我发现留给自己支配的几乎一无所剩。」

我对他千恩万谢。

===Original===
“And now, a word, in my office of Mentor. You have not come here, of course, without introductions?”

I produced half-a-dozen letters, the addresses of which he looked at.

“Don’t mind these letters,” he said. “I will introduce you. I will take you myself from house to house. One friend at your side is worth many letters. Make no intimities, no acquaintances, until then. You young men like best to exhaust the public amusements of a great city, before embarrassing yourself with the engagements of society. Go to all these. It will occupy you, day and night, for at least three weeks. When this is over, I shall be at liberty, and will myself introduce you to the brilliant but comparatively quiet routine of society. Place yourself in my hands; and in Paris remember, when once in society, you are always there.”

I thanked him very much, and promised to follow his counsels implicitly.

===Chinese===
「现在，以我良师（Mentor）的身份说一句话。您当然不会是毫无引荐信就来的吧？」

我掏出半打介绍信来，他看了看信封上的称呼。

「这些信不必在意，」他说，「我来引荐您。我亲自带您挨家挨户去拜会。身边有一位朋友，胜过多少封信。在那之前，切勿深交，也不要结识什么人。你们年轻人最喜欢先把大城市的公共玩乐尝个遍，再来让社交的义务绊住手脚。这些尽管都去。够您日夜消遣，至少三个星期。完了之后，我就得空了，亲自引您进入社交界那华彩而相对清静的常规。把自己交到我手里；还要记住，在巴黎，一旦进了社交界，就终身都在里面了。」

我感激不尽，答应对他的一应指点言听计从。

===Original===
He seemed pleased, and said﻿—

“I shall now tell you some of the places you ought to go to. Take your map, and write letters or numbers upon the points I will indicate, and we will make out a little list. All the places that I shall mention to you are worth seeing.”

In this methodical way, and with a great deal of amusing and scandalous anecdote, he furnished me with a catalogue and a guide, which, to a seeker of novelty and pleasure, was invaluable.

“In a fortnight, perhaps in a week,” he said, “I shall be at leisure to be of real use to you. In the meantime, be on your guard. You must not play; you will be robbed if you do. Remember, you are surrounded, here, by plausible swindlers and villains of all kinds, who subsist by devouring strangers. Trust no one but those you know.”

===Chinese===
他显得很高兴，说道——

「我这就告诉您几个该去的地方。拿出您的地图，在我指出的地点上标上字母或号码，咱们列一张小单子。我向您提到的这些地方，都值得一去。」

就这样有条不紊，又夹着大量妙趣横生、骇人听闻的轶闻，他给我编成了一份目录和一份指南；对一个寻求新奇与享乐的人，这实在是无价之宝。

「过两个星期，也许一个星期，」他说，「我就得空，能真正帮上您的忙了。在此之前，务必警惕。您不可以赌；一赌就要被抢。记住，您在这里，四面八方都是满口圆谎的骗子和各色恶棍，他们靠吞吃外乡人过活。除了相识的人，谁也别信。」

===Original===
I thanked him again, and promised to profit by his advice. But my heart was too full of the beautiful lady of the Belle Étoile, to allow our interview to close without an effort to learn something about her. I therefore asked for the Count and Countess de St. Alyre, whom I had had the good fortune to extricate from an extremely unpleasant row in the hall of the inn.

Alas! he had not seen them since. He did not know where they were staying. They had a fine old house only a few leagues from Paris; but he thought it probable that they would remain, for a few days at least, in the city, as preparations would, no doubt, be necessary, after so long an absence, for their reception at home.

“How long have they been away?”

“About eight months, I think.”

“They are poor, I think you said?”

===Chinese===
我又道了谢，答应从他的忠告里得益。可我心里满盛着美星旅馆那位美丽的夫人，总不能眼看着这次会面收场，而不想法子打听到她的一点消息。于是我便问起居·圣阿利尔伯爵和伯爵夫人来——我曾有幸把他们从客栈门厅一场极不体面的吵闹里解救出来。

唉！他后来没有再见过他们。他不知道他们下落何处。他们在离巴黎只有几里格的地方有一座漂亮的古老宅邸；不过他想，他们多半会在城里至少再待几天，因为出门这么久，家里总得先作些准备，才好迎接他们回去。

「他们出门多久了？」

「大约八个月，我想。」

「我记得您说过，他们不算阔？」

===Original===
“What *you* would consider poor. But, Monsieur, the Count has an income which affords them the comforts, and even the elegancies of life, living as they do, in a very quiet and retired way, in this cheap country.”

“Then they are very happy?”

“One would say they *ought* to be happy.”

“He is jealous.”

“But his wife﻿—she gives him no cause?”

“How, Monsieur?”

“I always thought she was a little too﻿—a *great deal* too﻿—”

“I should wish so much to be presented to the Count: you tell me he’s so﻿—”

“So agreeably married. But they are living quite out of the world. He takes her now and then to the Opera, or to a public entertainment; but that is all.”

“And he must remember so much of the old regime, and so many of the scenes of the revolution!”

“Yes, the very man for a philosopher, like you! And he falls asleep after dinner; and his wife don’t. But, seriously, he has retired from the gay and the great world, and has grown apathetic; and so has his wife; and nothing seems to interest her now, not even﻿—her husband!”

The Marquis stood up to take his leave.

“Don’t risk your money,” said he. “You will soon have an opportunity of laying out some of it to great advantage. Several collections of really good pictures, belonging to persons who have mixed themselves up in this Bonapartist restoration, must come within a few weeks to the hammer. You can do wonders when these sales commence. There will be startling bargains! Reserve yourself for them. I shall let you know all about it. By the by,” he said, stopping short as he approached the door, “I was so near forgetting. 侯爵站起身来告辞。

「别拿您的钱去冒险，」他说，「您很快就有机会把其中一部分花在刀刃上。好几批真正的好画，主人都是卷进这场波拿巴复辟里的人物，不出几个星期就要上拍卖槌。开拍之时，您大可大展身手。会有惊人的便宜货！养精蓄锐等着吧。一切内情我会通知您。对了，」他走到门口忽然站住，「我差点忘了。下星期有一件您准会尽兴的事，因为这种场面在英国难得一见——我指的是一场*化装舞会*（bal masqué）；据说筹办得比往常更加华丽。地点在凡尔赛——全巴黎的人都要去；入场券抢破头！不过我想，我可以给您弄到一张。晚安！再会！」
