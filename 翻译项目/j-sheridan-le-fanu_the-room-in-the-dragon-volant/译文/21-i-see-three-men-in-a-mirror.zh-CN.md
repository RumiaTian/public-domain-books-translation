## XXI I See Three Men in a Mirror / XXI 我在镜中看见三个人

===Original===
I awoke very early next morning, and was too excited to sleep again. As soon as I could, without exciting remark, I saw my host. I told him that I was going into town that night, and thence to ————, where I had to see some people on business, and requested him to mention my being there to any friend who might call. That I expected to be back in about a week, and that in the meantime my servant, St. Clair, would keep the key of my room, and look after my things.

Having prepared this mystification for my landlord, I drove into Paris, and there transacted the financial part of the affair. The problem was to reduce my balance, nearly thirty thousand pounds, to a shape in which it would be not only easily portable, but available, wherever I might go, without involving correspondence, or any other incident which would disclose my place of residence, for the time being. All these points were as nearly provided for as they could be. I need not trouble you about my arrangements for passports. It is enough to say that the point I selected for our flight was, in the spirit of romance, one of the most beautiful and sequestered nooks in Switzerland.

Luggage, I should start with none. The first considerable town we reached next morning, would supply an extemporized wardrobe. It was now two o’clock; *only* two! How on earth was I to dispose of the remainder of the day?

===Chinese===
第二天清晨我醒得很早，兴奋得再睡不着。一等到不惹人注目的时辰，我便去见店主。我告诉他，我当晚要进城，再从那里前往某地，有事要见几个人；并请他转告任何可能来访的朋友，说我在那边。还说预计一周左右回来，此间我的仆人圣克莱尔会保管我房间的钥匙，照看我的东西。

给店主布下这套迷魂阵之后，我便驱车进了巴黎，在那里办妥了这桩事的银钱部分。难题在于把我名下将近三万英镑的存款，换成一个不仅便于携带、而且无论我走到哪里都能随时取用的形制，既不牵涉书信往来，也不出任何会暴露我临时居所的岔子。这些要点都已尽可能周全地办妥了。护照的安排不必赘述，免得烦扰诸位。只需说一句：我为我们出逃选定的地点，本着罗曼蒂克的精神，是瑞士最美丽、最幽静的角落之一。

行李，我将一件不带。明早我们到达的第一座像样的城镇，自会置办出一套应急的行头。这时是两点钟；才两点！这一天的剩余时光，我到底该怎么打发？

===Original===
I had not yet seen the cathedral of Notre Dame; and thither I drove. I spent an hour or more there; and then to the Conciergerie, the Palais de Justice, and the beautiful Sainte Chapelle. Still there remained some time to get rid of, and I strolled into the narrow streets adjoining the cathedral. I recollect seeing, in one of them, an old house with a mural inscription stating that it had been the residence of Canon Fulbert, the uncle of Abelard’s Eloise. I don’t know whether these curious old streets, in which I observed fragments of ancient gothic churches fitted up as warehouses, are still extant. I lighted, among other dingy and eccentric shops, upon one that seemed that of a broker of all sorts of old decorations, armour, china, furniture. I entered the shop; it was dark, dusty, and low. The proprietor was busy scouring a piece of inlaid armour, and allowed me to poke about his shop, and examine the curious things accumulated there, just as I pleased. Gradually I made my way to the farther end of it, where there was but one window with many panes, each with a bull’s-eye in it, and in the dirtiest possible state. When I reached this window, I turned about, and in a recess, standing at right angles with the side wall of the shop, was a large mirror in an old-fashioned dingy frame. Reflected in this I saw, what in old houses I have heard termed an “alcove,” in which, among lumber, and various dusty articles hanging on the wall, there stood a table, at which three persons were seated, as it seemed to me, in earnest conversation. Two of these persons I instantly recognized; one was Colonel Gaillarde, the other was the Marquis d’Harmonville. The third, who was fiddling with a pen, was a lean, pale man, pitted with the smallpox, with lank black hair, and about as mean-looking a person as I had ever seen in my life. The Marquis looked up, and his glance was instantaneously followed by his two companions. For a moment I hesitated what to do. But it was plain that I was not recognized, as indeed I could hardly have been, the light from the window being behind me, and the portion of the shop immediately before me, being very dark indeed.

我还没见过圣母院大教堂；便驱车前往。我在那里流连了一个多钟头；然后去了巴黎古监狱（Conciergerie）、司法宫（Palais de Justice）和美丽的圣礼拜堂（Sainte Chapelle）。仍旧剩下些时光需要打发，我便信步走进大教堂旁边那些窄巷。我记得在其中一条巷子里见到一座老房子，墙上嵌着铭文，说它曾是阿伯拉尔的爱洛依丝的舅舅、教士富尔贝尔的故居。我不知道这些奇妙的老街如今是否还在——我曾看见古老哥特式教堂的残段被改作仓库。在几家昏暗而古怪的铺子之间，我撞上一家，看样子是个经营各色旧装饰品、盔甲、瓷器、家具的经纪铺。我走进店里；铺内昏暗、积尘、低矮。店主正忙着擦拭一件嵌花盔甲，任由我在他店里东翻西看，随意检视那里堆集的种种古怪物事。我渐渐挪到店铺最里头，那儿只有一扇多格的窗子，每块玻璃中央都带一个牛眼形圆泡，脏得不能再脏。走到这扇窗前，我回过身，只见一个凹室里——与店铺侧墙成直角的位置——立着一面老式暗框的大镜子。镜中映出的，是我听人说旧宅里那种「凹室」（alcove）的所在：破烂家什之间，墙上挂着各样蒙尘的物件，立着一张桌子，桌边坐着三个人，看样子正在认真地交谈。其中两人我一眼便认出：一个是盖亚尔上校，另一个是德·哈蒙维尔侯爵。第三个正摆弄着一支笔，是个瘦削苍白的男人，脸上有天花麻子，稀疏的黑发耷拉着，模样之猥琐，是我生平仅见。侯爵抬起头来，他目光一扫，两个同伴立刻随之望来。我一时踌躇不知如何是好。但显然他们没有认出我——事实上也几乎不可能认出：窗光在我背后，而我面前那片店堂实在暗得很。

===Original===
Perceiving this, I had presence of mind to affect being entirely engrossed by the objects before me, and strolled slowly down the shop again. I paused for a moment to hear whether I was followed, and was relieved when I heard no step. You may be sure I did not waste more time in that shop, where I had just made a discovery so curious and so unexpected.

It was no business of mine to inquire what brought Colonel Gaillarde and the Marquis together, in so shabby, and even dirty a place, or who the mean person, biting the feather end of his pen, might be. Such employments as the Marquis had accepted sometimes make strange bedfellows.

I was glad to get away, and just as the sun set, I had reached the steps of the Dragon Volant, and dismissed the vehicle in which I arrived, carrying in my hand a strong box, of marvellously small dimensions considering all it contained, strapped in a leather cover, which disguised its real character.

===Chinese===
看出这一点，我沉得住气，装出一副全然沉溺在眼前物件里的样子，又慢慢向店堂那头踱去。我停了片刻，听听有没有人跟上来，没听见脚步声，才松了口气。你尽可相信，在那家刚刚让我有了如此离奇、如此意外之发现的铺子里，我没有再多耽搁一刻。

盖亚尔上校和侯爵是何缘由聚在这样一处破败、甚至肮脏的地方，那个咬着笔杆羽毛末端的猥琐人物又是谁，都不是我该打听的。侯爵大人接受的这类差事，有时会把八竿子打不着的人凑作一处。

我庆幸自己脱身出来。日落时分，我已到了飞龙旅馆门前的台阶上，打发走来时的车，手里提着一只钱箱——以它装下的东西而论，小得惊人——外面用皮罩束着，掩住了它的真容。

===Original===
When I got to my room, I summoned St. Clair. I told him nearly the same story I had already told my host. I gave him fifty pounds, with orders to expend whatever was necessary on himself, and in payment for my rooms till my return. I then ate a slight and hasty dinner. My eyes were often upon the solemn old clock over the chimneypiece, which was my sole accomplice in keeping tryst in this iniquitous venture. The sky favoured my design, and darkened all things with a sea of clouds.

The innkeeper met me in the hall, to ask whether I should want a vehicle to Paris? I was prepared for this question, and instantly answered that I meant to walk to Versailles, and take a carriage there. I called St. Clair.

“Go,” said I, “and drink a bottle of wine with your friends. I shall call you if I should want anything; in the meantime, here is the key of my room; I shall be writing some notes, so don’t allow anyone to disturb me, for at least half an hour. At the end of that time you will probably find that I have left this for Versailles; and should you not find me in the room, you may take that for granted; and you take charge of everything, and lock the door, you understand?”

St. Clair took his leave, wishing me all happiness and no doubt promising himself some little amusement with my money. With my candle in my hand, I hastened upstairs. It wanted now but five minutes to the appointed time. I do not think there is anything of the coward in my nature; but I confess, as the crisis approached, I felt something of the suspense and awe of a soldier going into action. Would I have receded? Not for all this earth could offer.

===Chinese===
回到房里，我唤来圣克莱尔。我对他说了与对店主所说几乎相同的一套话。我给了他五十英镑，吩咐他该花在自己身上的尽管花，并代付我到回来为止的房钱。然后我匆匆吃了顿简便的晚饭。我的目光不时落在壁炉架上那座神情肃穆的老钟上——在这桩伤天害理的冒险里，它是唯一与我共守约会的东西。天色也成全我的图谋，一片云海把万物都罩暗了。

店主在大厅里迎住我，问我要不要雇车去巴黎？这个问题我早有准备，立刻答道：我打算步行到凡尔赛，在那里搭车。我唤来圣克莱尔。

"去吧，"我说，"同你的朋友们喝瓶酒去。我要什么自会叫你；眼下，这是我房间的钥匙；我要写几封信，所以至少半个钟头之内，别让任何人来打搅我。半个钟头之后，你多半会发现我已经动身去凡尔赛了；你要是发现我不在房里，就这么认定好了；你把一切照管起来，把门锁上，明白吗？"

圣克莱尔告退了，祝愿我万事如意，无疑也盘算着拿我的钱给自己寻点小小的乐趣。我手执蜡烛，赶紧上楼。此刻离约定时间只差五分钟了。我不认为自己天性里有半点怯懦；但我承认，随着关键时刻临近，我尝到了几分士兵临阵时的紧张与肃然。我会退缩吗？把全世界能给的都给我，也不会。

===Original===
I bolted my door, put on my great coat, and placed my pistols, one in each pocket. I now applied my key to the secret locks; drew the wainscot-door a little open, took my strong box under my arm, extinguished my candle, unbolted my door, listened at it for a few moments to be sure that no one was approaching, and then crossed the floor of my room swiftly, entered the secret door, and closed the spring lock after me. I was upon the screw-stair in total darkness, the key in my fingers. Thus far the undertaking was successful.

===Chinese===
我闩上房门，穿上大衣，两支手枪一边口袋里插一支。接着我把钥匙探进暗锁；将壁板门拉开一条缝，钱箱夹在腋下，吹灭蜡烛，拉开房门的闩，在门边听了片刻，确信没有人走近，然后疾步穿过房间地板，钻进暗门，把弹簧锁在身后扣上。我立于全然漆黑的旋梯上，钥匙攥在指间。到这一步为止，行动是成功的。
