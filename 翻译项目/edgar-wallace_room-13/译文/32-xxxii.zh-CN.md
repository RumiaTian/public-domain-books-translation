## XXXII / 第三十二章

===Original===
For a second Legge was paralysed with rage and fear. Then, in the wildness of his despair, he kicked at the man, who had slipped from the bed and was holding him. He heard an exclamation, felt for a second the fingers relax; and, slipping like an eel from the grasp, flew to the door and closed it. He stood, breathless and panting by the doorway, until he heard the sound of steel against the inner keyhole, and in a flash realised that Johnny had secured the passkey. Quick as lightning, he slipped his own key back into the lock and turned it slightly, so that it could not be pushed out from the other side.

===Chinese===
莱格（Jeffrey Legge）先是被愤怒和恐惧惊得呆了一瞬。随即，在绝望的狂乱中，他朝那个人猛踢过去——那人已经从床上滑下来，正抓着他。他听到一声惊呼，感觉到那抓握的手指松了一瞬；他像鳗鱼一样从对方手中挣脱，飞身扑向门口，把门关上。他靠在门框上气喘吁吁，直到听见钢钥匙插进内侧锁孔的声音，这才猛然醒悟——约翰尼（Johnny Gray）已经拿到了万能钥匙。他闪电般地把自己的钥匙插回锁孔，轻轻一转，让另一边的人推不出来。

===Original===
Johnny Gray! How had he got there? He fled up the stairs and hammered on the door of the cell where he thought his prisoner was held safe. A surly voice replied to him.

"You swine!" he howled. "You let him go! You twister! You can stay there and starve, damn you!"

"I didn't let him go. He held me up. Look out, Jeff, he's got a gun."

===Chinese===
约翰尼·格雷！他怎么到那儿去的？他冲上楼梯，使劲锤他认为关着俘虏的牢房门。一个粗暴的声音回了他。

"你这混账！"他咆哮道，"你放了他！你这叛徒！你就待在那儿饿死吧，该死的！"

"我没放他。他拿枪逼住了我。小心，杰弗里，他有枪。"

===Original===
The news staggered the man. The search of Johnny's clothing had been of a perfunctory nature, but he had thought that it was impossible that any kind of weapon could have been concealed.

"Let me out, guv'nor," pleaded the prisoner. "You've got a key."

There was a third key in his house, Jeffrey remembered. Perhaps this man might be of use to him. He was still weak from his wound, and would need assistance.

"All right, I'll get the key. But if you shopped me—"

"I didn't shop you, I tell you. He held me up—"

===Chinese===
这个消息把莱格震住了。搜约翰尼的衣服不过是例行公事，但他本以为绝不可能藏住任何武器。

"放我出去，老板，"牢房里的囚犯哀求道，"你有钥匙。"

杰弗里想起来，自己手头确实还有第三把钥匙。也许这个人能派上用场。他伤口未愈，身子还虚，需要帮手。

"行，我去拿钥匙。但你要是告了密——"

"我没告密，我跟你说。他拿枪逼着我——"

===Original===
Legge went back to his room, found the key, and, taking another stiff dose of whisky, returned and released his man.

"He's got my gun, too," explained Bill. "Where are all the fellows? We'll soon settle with him."

"They've gone," said Jeffrey.

What a fool he had been! If he had had the sense to keep the gang together only for a few hours—But he was safe, unless Johnny found a means of getting through the window.

===Chinese===
莱格回到自己房间，找到钥匙，又灌了一大口威士忌，回来放出了那个人。

"他把我的枪也拿走了，"比尔（Bill）解释道，"其他弟兄呢？咱们很快就能收拾他。"

"都走了，"杰弗里说。

他真是个蠢货！要是他有点脑子，让那帮人多待几个小时——不过他还是安全的，除非约翰尼能找到办法从窗户出去。

===Original===
"In my room you'll find a pistol; it is in the top right-hand corner of my desk," he said quickly. "Take it and get outside Johnny's cell—on the yard side. If he tries to escape that way, shoot. Because, if he escapes, you're going a long journey, my friend."

Inside the cell, a chagrined Johnny Gray sat down on the girl's bed to consider the possibilities of the position.

"My dear, there's going to be serious trouble here, and I don't want you to think otherwise," he said. "I should imagine there were quite a number of men in this prison, in which case, though I shall probably get two or three of them, they'll certainly get me in the end."

She sat by his side, holding his hand, and the pressure of her fingers was eloquent of the faith she had in him.

"Johnny, dear, does it matter very much what happens now? They can't come in, and we can't get out. How long will it take to starve us to death?"

===Chinese===
"我房间里有一把手枪；在我书桌右上角，"他急促地说，"拿着它，守在约翰尼的牢房外面——院子那一侧。他要是想从那边逃，就开枪。因为他要是跑了，你也别想有好日子过，我的朋友。"

牢房里，懊恼的约翰尼·格雷在姑娘的床上坐下，盘算着眼前的局面。

"亲爱的，这里要出大事了，我不希望你想得太轻松，"他说，"这个监狱里恐怕有不少人，那样的话，就算我能放倒两三个，他们最终还是会抓住我。"

她坐在他身旁，握着他的手，指尖的力度诉说着她对他的信任。

"约翰尼，亲爱的，现在发生什么真的那么重要吗？他们进不来，我们也出不去。把我们饿死要多久？"

===Original===
Johnny had already considered that problem.

"About three days," he said, in such a matter-of-fact tone that she laughed. "My only hope, Marney, is that your father, who, as I told you, has escaped, may know more about this place than he has admitted."

"Did you know anything about it?" she asked.

He hesitated.

"Yes, I think I did. I wasn't sure, though I was a fool not to locate it just as soon as Fenner warned me against Keytown Jail. These chaps like to speak in parables, and mystery is as the breath of their nostrils. Besides, I should have been certain that Fenner knew the jail had been taken over from the Government."

===Chinese===
约翰尼早就想过这个问题。

"大约三天，"他说，语气平淡得让她笑了出来。"我唯一的希望，玛妮，是你父亲——我跟你说过他已经逃出去了——对这个地方的了解可能比他承认的要多。"

"你自己知道什么吗？"她问。

他犹豫了一下。

"知道，我想我是知道的。虽然我不确定，不过我真蠢，芬纳（Fenner）一警告我别去基镇监狱（Keytown Jail）的时候，就该查清楚。这帮人说话总爱拐弯抹角，神秘兮兮的是他们的命根子。再说了，我本该想到芬纳肯定知道那座监狱已经从政府手里转出去了。"

===Original===
He made a careful examination of the bars about the window, but without instruments or tools to force them, he knew that escape that way was impossible. When, in the early hours of the morning, he saw the patient figure of Bill, he realised the extent of the impossibility.

"Good morning, William. I see you're out," he greeted the scowling sentry, who immediately jumped to cover, flourishing his long-barrelled weapon.

"Don't you show your nose, or I'll blow it off," he threatened. "We've got you, Mr. Gray."

"They've got you, alas, my poor William," said Johnny sadly. "The busies will be here at nine o'clock—you don't suppose that I should have let myself come into a trap like this? Of course, I didn't. I squeaked! It was my only chance, William. And your only chance is to sneak away at the earliest opportunity, and turn State's evidence. I'm addressing you as a friend."

===Chinese===
他仔细检查了窗上的铁栏，但没有工具可以撬开它们，他知道从这儿逃出去是不可能的。清晨时分，他看到比尔那耐心守候的身影，这才彻底意识到逃跑有多不可能。

"早上好，威廉。看来你出来了，"他向那阴沉的岗哨打招呼。那人立刻跳到掩体后面，挥舞着他那长管武器。

"你敢探出脑袋，我就把它轰掉，"他威胁道，"你跑不掉了，格雷先生。"

"跑不掉的是你们，唉，可怜的威廉，"约翰尼哀伤地说，"条子（busies）九点钟就到——你以为我会让自己钻进这种陷阱吗？当然不会。我告了密（squeaked）！这是我唯一的机会，威廉。而你唯一的机会就是尽早溜走，转做污点证人（State's evidence）。我是拿你当朋友才跟你说这番话的。"

===Original===
"You'll never get away from here alive," said the man. "Jeff's going to fix you."

"Indeed?" the prisoner began politely, when a scream made him turn.

"Johnny!"

The shutter which hid the grille in the door was swung back, and the muzzle of Jeffrey's Browning had been pushed through one of the openings. As Johnny dropped flat on the bed, he was stunned by the deafening sound of an explosion. Something hit the wall, ricochetted to the roof, and fell almost at the girl's feet. Before the pistol could be withdrawn, Johnny Gray had fired. A jagged end of iron showed where his bullet struck.

===Chinese===
"你活着出不去的，"那人说，"杰弗里会收拾你。"

"是吗？"囚犯客客气气地刚开了口，一声尖叫让他转过身去。

"约翰尼！"

遮挡牢门铁栅的挡板被掀开了，杰弗里的勃朗宁手枪枪口已经从一个格子里伸了进来。约翰尼扑倒在床上，一声震耳欲聋的爆炸声几乎把他震懵。有什么东西打在墙上，弹到屋顶，落在姑娘脚边。手枪还没来得及抽回去，约翰尼·格雷已经开了一枪。铁栅上露出一个参差不齐的弹痕。

===Original===
"The time for persiflage," said Johnny cheerfully, "is past. Now you will sit in that corner, young lady, and will not budge without permission." He pointed to the wall nearest the door, which afforded perfect cover, and, dragging up a stool, he seated himself by her side. "Jeffrey's got quite a tough proposition," he said in his conversational tone. "He can't burn the prison, because there's nothing to burn. He can't come in, and he mustn't go out. If he would only for one moment take away that infernal key—"

"There is another door going out from the bathroom," she said suddenly. "I think it leads to an exercise ground. You can just see a little railed-off space through the window."

===Chinese===
"说笑的时间，"约翰尼轻快地说，"已经过了。现在你坐到那个角落去，小姐，没我允许不许动。"他指着离门最近的那面墙，那里掩护最好，然后拖过一把凳子，在她身旁坐下。"杰弗里这回可碰上硬茬了，"他用闲聊的口吻说道，"他烧不了监狱，因为没什么可烧的。他进不来，也不能出去。他只要把那把该死的钥匙拿开哪怕一秒钟——"

"浴室还有另一扇门通出去，"她忽然说，"我想是通到放风场的。透过窗户能看见一小块围栏隔开的空地。"

===Original===
Johnny went into the bathroom and examined the door. Screwing his head, he could see, through a broken pane, ten square yards of space, where in olden times a condemned prisoner took his exercise, removed from the gaze of his fellows. He tried the key, and to his delight, it turned. Another minute and he was in the little paved yard.

Looking round, he saw a high and narrow gateway, which seemed to be the only exit from the courtyard. And on the other side of that gateway was William, the sentry, well armed and sufficiently terrified to be dangerous. Slipping off his boots, Johnny crept to the gate and listened. The sound of the man's footsteps pacing the flagged walk came to him. Stooping, he squinted through the keyhole, and saw Bill standing, his back toward him, some six yards away. There was no time to be lost. He inserted the key, and the gate was opened before the man could turn to face the levelled revolver.

===Chinese===
约翰尼走进浴室检查那扇门。歪过头，他透过一块碎玻璃看见了大约十平方码的空地——旧时死囚就是在这块与世隔绝的地方放风的。他试了试钥匙，惊喜地发现它能转动。再过一分钟，他就站在了那片铺石小院里。

环顾四周，他看到一道高窄的门洞，似乎是这个院子唯一的出口。门洞的另一边站着岗哨威廉，全副武装，足够害怕到铤而走险。约翰尼脱掉靴子，蹑手蹑脚地凑到门边倾听。那人踱步踩在石板路上的脚步声传了过来。他弯下腰，眯着眼从锁孔望过去，看见比尔背对着他站在大约六码开外。时间紧迫。他插入钥匙，在那人转身面对瞄准的左轮手枪之前，门已经打开了。

===Original===
"Don't shout," whispered Johnny. "You're either discreet or dead. Hand over that gun, you unfortunate man." He moved swiftly toward the terrified criminal, and relieved him of his weapon.

With a gesture, Johnny directed him to the exercise yard.

"Get in and stay," he said, and locked the door, and for the second time, Bill (his other name, Johnny never discovered, was Holliss) was a prisoner.

===Chinese===
"别嚷，"约翰尼低声说，"你要么识相，要么死。把枪交出来，你这倒霉蛋。"他迅速走向那个吓坏了的罪犯，缴了他的武器。

约翰尼用手势示意他进放风场。

"进去待着，"他说着锁上了门。比尔（他的另一个姓氏，约翰尼始终没有发现，是霍利斯（Holliss））第二次成了囚犯。

===Original===
Skirting the building, he came to the entrance of the hall. The door was open, and with his hand on the uplifted hammer of the gun, and his finger pressing the trigger, Johnny leapt into the building.

"Hands up!" he shouted.

At the words, Jeffrey Legge spun round. There was a boom of sound, something whistled past Gray's face, and he fired twice. But now the man was running, zigzagging to left and right, and Johnny hesitated to fire. He disappeared through the door at the farther end of the hall, shutting it behind him, and Johnny raced after him.

===Chinese===
他沿着建筑边缘摸到大厅入口。门敞开着，约翰尼手扣扳机、击锤高举，纵身跃入楼内。

"举起手来！"他大喝一声。

话音未落，杰弗里·莱格猛地转身。一声轰响，有什么东西呼啸着掠过格雷的脸颊，他连开两枪。但那人已经在跑了，左躲右闪，约翰尼犹豫着没有再开枪。那人消失在大厅尽头的门后，随手把门关上，约翰尼紧追不舍。

===Original===
He was in the courtyard now, facing the grille-covered archway. As he came into view, Jeffrey disappeared through the lodge-keeper's door. Johnny tried the grille, but in vain, for a passkey operates on all locks save the lock of the entrance gate of a prison. That alone is distinct, and may not be opened save by the key that was cut for it.

Covering the lodge-keeper's door with his gun, Johnny waited, and, waiting, heard a rumbling sound. Something was coming down the centre of the archway. The straight line of it came lower and lower. A hanging gate! He had forgotten that most old country prisons were so equipped. Under the cover of this ancient portcullis, Legge could escape, for it masked the entrance of the lodge.

===Chinese===
他现在站在院子里，面对着铁栅封顶的拱门。杰弗里一看到他出现，就从门房的门里消失了。约翰尼试了试铁栅，但无济于事——万能钥匙能打开所有锁，唯独打不开监狱的大门锁。那是唯一与众不同的锁，只有专门配的钥匙才能打开。

约翰尼用枪指着门房的门等待着。等的时候，他听到一阵隆隆声。有东西正从拱门正中降下来。那条笔直的线条越来越低。吊闸！他忘了大多数老式的乡村监狱都装有这种东西。在这道古老吊闸的掩护下，莱格可以逃走，因为它恰好遮住了门房的入口。

===Original===
He turned back to the girl.

"Keep out of sight. He's got away," he warned her. "This fellow isn't finished yet."

The gate was down… Jeffrey put on the overcoat he had left in the lodge, slipped his pistol into his pocket and opened the great gates. He had at least a dozen hours' start, he thought, as he stepped into the open…

"Please do not put your hand in your pocket, Mr. Jeffrey," said a plaintive voice. "I should so hate to shoot a fellow-creature. It would be a deed utterly repugnant to my finest feelings."

Jeffrey raised his hands to their fullest extent, for Mr. Reeder was not alone. Behind him were four armed policemen, a cordon of mounted constabulary, spread in a semicircle, cutting off all avenues of escape. And, most ominous of all, was the deadly scrutiny of Peter Kane, who stood at Reeder's right hand.

===Chinese===
他转身回到姑娘身边。

"别露面。他跑了，"他提醒她，"这家伙还没完。"

吊闸落下了……杰弗里穿上留在门房里的大衣，把手枪塞进口袋，打开了大门。他至少领先了十二个小时，他想，一边跨出门去……

"请不要把手伸进口袋，杰弗里先生（Mr. Jeffrey），"一个哀伤的声音说道，"我实在不忍心对同胞开枪。那将是一件与我最美好的感情全然相悖的事。"

杰弗里把手高高举起，举到了极限——因为里德先生（Mr. J. G. Reeder）并非独自一人。他身后是四名武装警察，一队骑警呈半圆形展开，切断了所有逃路。而最不祥的，是彼得·凯恩（Peter Kane）那死一般的审视——他就站在里德先生的右手边。
