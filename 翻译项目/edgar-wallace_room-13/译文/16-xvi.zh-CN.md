## XVI / 第十六章

===Original===
A surprise awaited him when he came to the Charlton. Mrs. Floyd had gone—nobody knew whither. Her husband had followed her some time afterwards, and neither had returned. Somebody had called her on the telephone, but had left no name.

===Chinese===
他赶到查尔顿旅馆时，却扑了个空。弗洛伊德太太已经走了——没人知道去了哪里。她的丈夫过了一阵也跟了出去，两人都没回来。有人打电话找过她，但没留名字。

===Original===
"I know all about her husband not returning," said Craig. "But haven't you the slightest idea where the lady is?"

===Chinese===
"她丈夫没回来的事我全知道了，"克雷格说，"可你连一点那位女士的去向都不清楚吗？"

===Original===
"No."

===Chinese===
"不清楚。"

===Original===
The negative reply was uncompromising.

===Chinese===
这回答斩钉截铁，毫无余地。

===Original===
"Her father hasn't been here?"

===Chinese===
"她父亲来过没有？"

===Original===
His informant hesitated.

===Chinese===
对方犹豫了一下。

===Original===
"Yes, sir; he was on Mrs. Floyd's floor when she was missing—in fact, when Major Floyd was down here making inquiries. The floor waiter recognised him, but did not see him come or go."

===Chinese===
"来过，先生；弗洛伊德太太失踪的时候他就在她那一层——事实上，弗洛伊德少校下来打听情况时他也在。楼层服务员认出了他，但没看见他怎么来怎么走的。"

===Original===
Calling up the house at Horsham he learnt, what he already knew, that Peter was away from home. Barney, who answered him, had heard nothing of the girl; indeed, this was the first intimation he had had that all was not well. And a further disappointment lay in store for him. The detective he had sent to find Johnny returned with the news that the quarry had gone. According to the valet, his master had returned and changed in a hurry, and, taking a small suitcase, had gone off to an unknown destination.

===Chinese===
他打电话到霍舍姆的宅子，了解到他早已知道的情况——彼得不在家。接电话的巴尼（Barney）对姑娘的事一无所知；实际上，这是他第一次听说出了状况。而更大的失望还在后面。他派去抓约翰尼的探员回报说，猎物已经跑了。据男仆说，主人回来后匆匆换了衣服，拎了一只小手提箱，不知去了哪里。

===Original===
An inquiry late that night elicited the fact that Jeff was still living, but unconscious. The bullet had been extracted, and a hopeful view was taken of the future. His father had arrived early in the evening, and was half mad with anxiety and rage.

===Chinese===
深夜打听得知，杰弗里还活着，但一直昏迷不醒。子弹已经取出来了，医生对他的恢复持乐观态度。他父亲傍晚时分就赶到了，焦虑和愤怒几乎让他发疯。

===Original===
"And if he isn't quite mad by the morning, I shall be surprised," said the surgeon. "I'm going to keep him here and give him a little bromide to ease him down."

===Chinese===
"要是到早上他还没彻底疯掉，我就觉得奇怪了，"外科医生说，"我把他留在这儿，给他用点溴化物镇定一下。"

===Original===
"Poison him," suggested Craig.

===Chinese===
"毒死他算了。"克雷格提议道。

===Original===
When the old detective was on the point of going home, there arrived a telephone message from the Horsham police, whom he had enlisted to watch Peter's house.

===Chinese===
老侦探正要回家时，霍舍姆警方来了电话——他此前已安排他们监视彼得的宅子。

===Original===
"Mr. Kane and his daughter arrived in separate motorcars at a quarter past twelve," was the report. "They came within a few minutes of one another."

===Chinese===
"凯恩先生和他的女儿分别在十二点一刻乘不同的汽车抵达，"报告说，"两人前后脚到的，相差不过几分钟。"

===Original===
Craig was on the point of getting through to the house, but thought better of it. A fast police car got him to Horsham under the hour, the road being clear and the night a bright one. Lights were burning in Peter's snuggery, and it was he himself who, at the sound of the motor wheels, came to the door.

===Chinese===
克雷格正要拨通宅子的电话，又改了主意。一辆警用快车不到一小时就把他送到了霍舍姆，一路畅通，夜色清朗。彼得书房里亮着灯，听到汽车轮声，他亲自出来开了门。

===Original===
"Who's that?" he asked, as Craig came up the dark drive, and, at the sound of the detective's voice, he came halfway down the drive to meet him. "What's wrong, Craig? Anything special?"

===Chinese===
"谁呀？"克雷格沿着昏暗的车道走上来时，他问道，一听到侦探的声音，便迎着走到半路。"怎么了，克雷格？出什么事了？"

===Original===
"Jeff's shot. I suppose you know who Jeff is?"

===Chinese===
"杰弗里中枪了。我想你知道杰弗里是谁吧？"

===Original===
"I know, to my sorrow," said Peter Kane promptly. "Shot? How? Where?"

===Chinese===
"知道，这让我痛心不已。"彼得·凯恩立刻答道，"中枪？怎么中的？在哪儿？"

===Original===
"He was shot this evening between a quarter to ten and ten o'clock, at the Highlow Club."

===Chinese===
"今晚九点四十五到十点之间，在海洛俱乐部（Highlow Club）。"

===Original===
"Come in. You'd better not tell my girl—she's had as much as she can bear tonight. Not that I'm worrying a damn about Jeff Legge. He'd better die, and die quick, for if I get him—"

===Chinese===
"进来吧。你最好别跟我女儿说——她今晚受的刺激够多了。我可不是心疼杰弗里·莱格那混蛋。他最好早点死，死得越快越好，因为要是让我逮到他——"

===Original===
He did not finish his sentence, and the detective drew the man's arm through his.

===Chinese===
他没把话说完，侦探挽住了他的胳膊。

===Original===
"Now, listen, Peter, you've got to go very slow on this case, and not talk such a darned lot. You're under suspicion too, old man. You were seen in the vicinity of the club."

===Chinese===
"听好了，彼得，这个案子你得沉住气，少啰嗦。你自己也有嫌疑，老伙计。有人看见你在俱乐部附近。"

===Original===
"Yes, I was seen in the vicinity of the club," repeated Peter, nodding. "I was waiting there—well, I was waiting there for a purpose. I went to the Charlton, but my girl had gone—I suppose they told you—and then I went on to the Highlow, and saw that infernal Lila—by the way, she's one of Jeff's women, isn't she?"

===Chinese===
"对，有人看见我在俱乐部附近，"彼得点头重复道，"我在那儿等——嗯，我在那儿是有目的的。我先去了查尔顿旅馆，但我女儿已经走了——我猜他们告诉过你了——然后我去了海洛俱乐部，看见了那个该死的莉拉——对了，她是杰弗里的女人之一，对吧？"

===Original===
"To be exact," said the other quietly, "she's his wife."

===Chinese===
"准确地说，"对方平静地说，"她是他老婆。"

===Original===
Peter Kane stopped dead.

===Chinese===
彼得·凯恩猛地站住了。

===Original===
"His wife?" he whispered. "Thank God for that! Thank God for that! I forgive her everything. Though she is a brute—how a woman could allow—but I can't judge her. That graft has always been dirty to me. It is hateful and loathsome. But, thank God she's his wife, Craig!" Then: "Who shot this fellow?"

===Chinese===
"他老婆？"他低声说，"谢天谢地！谢天谢地！我原谅她的一切。虽然她是个畜生——一个女人怎么能让他——但我没法怪她。那档子事在我眼里一直脏得不行。又可憎又恶心。不过，谢天谢地她是他的老婆，克雷格！"接着又问："谁开的枪？"

===Original===
"I don't know. I'm going to pull Johnny for it."

===Chinese===
"不知道。我打算把约翰尼抓来问。"

===Original===
They were in the hall, and Peter Kane spun round, open-mouthed, terror in his eyes.

===Chinese===
他们已经进了门厅，彼得·凯恩猛地转过身来，嘴巴大张，眼中满是恐惧。

===Original===
"You're going to pull Johnny?" he said. "Do you know what you're saying, Craig? You're mad! Johnny didn't do it. Johnny was nowhere near—"

===Chinese===
"你要抓约翰尼？"他说，"你知道自己在说什么吗，克雷格？你疯了！约翰尼没有干这事。约翰尼压根不在附近——"

===Original===
"Johnny was there. And, what is more, Johnny was in the room, either at the moment of the shooting or immediately after. The elevator boy has spoken what's in his heart, which isn't much, but enough to convict Johnny if this fellow dies."

===Chinese===
"约翰尼当时在场。而且，约翰尼就在那个房间里，要么是在开枪的时候，要么是紧随其后。电梯小工已经把心里话吐出来了，虽然他脑袋里装不了多少东西，但要是杰弗里死了，够把约翰尼定罪了。"

===Original===
"Johnny there!" Peter's voice did not rise above a whisper.

===Chinese===
"约翰尼在那儿！"彼得的声音低得几乎只是耳语。

===Original===
"I tell you frankly, Peter, I thought it was you."

===Chinese===
"我跟你说实话，彼得，我原以为是你干的。"

===Original===
Craig was facing him squarely, his keen eyes searching the man's pallid face. "When I heard you were around, and that you had got to know that this fellow was a fake. Why were you waiting?"

===Chinese===
克雷格正对着他，锐利的目光搜寻着对方苍白的脸。"我听说你在附近转悠，而且已经知道这家伙是冒牌货。你等在那儿干嘛？"

===Original===
"I can't tell you that—not now," said the other, after turning the matter over in his mind. "I should have seen Johnny if he was there. I saw this girl Lila, and I was afraid she'd recognise me. I think she did, too. I went straight on into Shaftesbury Avenue, to a bar I know. I was feeling queer over this—this discovery of mine. I can prove I was there from a quarter to ten till ten, if you want any proof. Oh, Johnny, Johnny!"

===Chinese===
"我不能告诉你——现在不行，"对方把事情在脑子里过了一遍后说，"要是约翰尼在那儿，我应该看见他了。我看见了那个叫莉拉的姑娘，怕她认出我。我觉得她确实认出来了。我直接拐进沙夫茨伯里大道（Shaftesbury Avenue），去了我知道的一家酒吧。我对自己发现的这——这件事心里发毛。你需要的话，我可以证明九点四十五到十点我一直在那儿。哦，约翰尼，约翰尼！"

===Original===
All this went on in the hall. Then came a quick patter of footsteps, and Marney appeared in the doorway.

===Chinese===
这些话全是在门厅里说的。接着传来一阵急促的脚步声，玛妮（Marney）出现在门口。

===Original===
"Who is it—Johnny? Oh, it is you, Mr. Craig? Has anything happened?" She looked in alarm from face to face. "Nothing has happened to Johnny?"

===Chinese===
"是谁——约翰尼？哦，是您，克雷格先生？出什么事了吗？"她惊恐地看看这个又看看那个，"约翰尼没事吧？"

===Original===
"No, nothing has happened to Johnny," said Craig soothingly. He glanced at Peter. "You ought to know this, Marney," he said. "I can call you Marney—I've known you since you were five. Jeff Legge has been shot."

===Chinese===
"没事，约翰尼没出什么事，"克雷格安抚道。他瞥了彼得一眼，"你该知道这事，玛妮，"他说，"我可以叫你玛妮吧——我从你五岁就认识你了。杰弗里·莱格中枪了。"

===Original===
He thought she was going to faint, and sprang to catch her, but with an effort of will she recovered.

===Chinese===
他以为她要晕过去，赶紧伸手去扶，但她凭意志力稳住了自己。

===Original===
"Jeff shot?" she asked shakily. "Who shot him?"

===Chinese===
"杰弗里中枪了？"她颤抖着问，"谁开的枪？"

===Original===
"I don't know. That's just what we are trying to discover. Perhaps you can help us. Why did you leave the hotel? Was Johnny with you?"

===Chinese===
"不知道。我们正在追查。也许你能帮我们。你为什么离开旅馆？约翰尼跟你在一起吗？"

===Original===
She shook her head.

===Chinese===
她摇了摇头。

===Original===
"I haven't seen Johnny," she said, "but I owe him—everything. There was a woman in the hotel." She glanced timidly at her father. "I think she was an hotel thief or something of the sort. She was there to—to steal. A big Welsh woman."

===Chinese===
"我没见到约翰尼，"她说，"但我欠他——一切。旅馆里有个女人，"她怯怯地看了父亲一眼，"我觉得她是个旅馆窃贼，或者是干那行的。她来旅馆是为了——偷东西。一个高大的威尔士女人。"

===Original===
"A Welsh woman?" said Craig quickly. "What is her name?"

===Chinese===
"威尔士女人？"克雷格急忙问道，"叫什么名字？"

===Original===
"Mrs. Gwenda Jones. Johnny knew about her, and telephoned her to tell her to take care of me until he could get to me. She got me out of the hotel, and then we walked down the Duke of York steps into the Mall. And then a curious thing happened—I was just telling daddy when you came. Mrs. Jones—she's such a big woman—"

===Chinese===
"格温达·琼斯太太（Mrs. Gwenda Jones）。约翰尼知道她，打电话给她，让她在我到达之前照顾我。她把我带出旅馆，然后我们顺着约克公爵台阶（Duke of York steps）走到林荫道（Mall）。接着发生了一件怪事——你来的时候我正要跟我爸爸说。琼斯太太——她个子可大了——"

===Original===
"I know the lady," said Craig.

===Chinese===
"我认识那位女士，"克雷格说。

===Original===
"Well, she disappeared. She wasn't exactly swallowed up by the earth," she said with a faint smile, "and she didn't go without warning. Suddenly she said to me: 'I must leave you now, my dear. I don't want that man to see me.' I looked round to find who it was that she was so terribly afraid of, and there seemed to be the most harmless lot of people about. When I turned, Mrs. Jones was running up the steps. I didn't wish to call her back, I felt so ridiculous. And then a man came up to me, a middle-aged man with the saddest face you could imagine. I told you that, daddy?"

===Chinese===
"嗯，她消失了。倒不是说真的钻进地里去了，"她微微笑了笑，"她走也不是没打招呼。她突然对我说：'亲爱的，我得离开你了。我不想让那个男人看见我。'我回头看她那么害怕的人是谁，周围看着都是些人畜无害的人。等我转过头来，琼斯太太已经跑上了台阶。我不好意思叫她回来，觉得自己太可笑了。然后一个男人向我走来，一个中年男人，脸上带着你所能想象的最忧伤的表情。我跟你说过这个吧，爸爸？"

===Original===
He nodded.

===Chinese===
他点了点头。

===Original===
"He took his hat off—his hair was almost white—and asked me if my name was Kane. I didn't tell him the other name," she said with a shiver. "'May I take you to a place of safety, Miss Kane?' he said. 'I don't think you ought to be seen with that rawboned female.' I didn't know what to do, I was so frightened, and I was glad of the company and protection of any man, and, when he called a cab, I got in without the slightest hesitation. He was such a gentle soul, Mr. Craig. He talked of nothing but the weather and chickens! I think we talked about chickens all the way to Lewisham."

===Chinese===
"他脱下帽子——头发已经花白——问我是不是凯恩小姐。我没有告诉他另一个名字，"她打了个寒噤，"'我能带你去一个安全的地方吗，凯恩小姐？'他说，'我觉得你不该跟那个瘦骨嶙峋的女人待在一起。'我不知道该怎么办，太害怕了，巴不得有个男人陪着、保护我，所以他叫了辆出租车，我毫不犹豫就上了车。他真是个温文尔雅的人，克雷格先生。他一路上只聊天气和鸡！我猜从那儿到刘易舍姆（Lewisham）一路上我们聊的全是鸡。"

===Original===
"Are you sure it was Lewisham?"

===Chinese===
"你确定是刘易舍姆？"

===Original===
"It was somewhere in that neighbourhood. What other places are there there?"

===Chinese===
"就在那一带。那里还有什么地方？"

===Original===
"New Cross, Brockley—" began Craig.

===Chinese===
"纽克罗斯（New Cross），布罗克利（Brockley）——"克雷格说。

===Original===
"That's the place—Brockley. It was the Brockley Road. I saw it printed on the corner of the street. He took me into his house. There was a nice, motherly old woman whom he introduced to me as his housekeeper."

===Chinese===
"就是那个地方——布罗克利。是布罗克利路（Brockley Road）。我在街角看到路牌了。他把我带到他家里。有一位和蔼可亲、慈母般的老太太，他介绍说是他的管家。"

===Original===
"And what did he talk about?" asked the fascinated Craig.

===Chinese===
"他都聊了些什么？"克雷格听得入了迷。

===Original===
"Chickens," she said solemnly. "Do you know what chickens lay the best eggs? I'm sure you don't. Do you know the best breed for England and the best for America? Do you know the most economical chickens to keep? I do! I wondered what he was going to do with me. I tried to ask him, but he invariably turned me back to the question of incubators and patent feeds, and the cubic space that a sitting hen requires as compared with an ordinary hen. It was the quaintest, most fantastic experience. It seems now almost like one of Alice's dreams! Then, at ten o'clock, I found a motorcar had come for me. 'I'm sending you home, young lady,' he said."

===Chinese===
"鸡，"她郑重其事地说，"你知道什么鸡下蛋最好吗？你肯定不知道。你知道英国最好的品种和美国最好的品种是哪些吗？你知道养什么鸡最划算吗？我知道！我琢磨着他打算怎么处置我。我试着问他，但他每次都把话题转回孵化器和专利饲料，还有抱窝母鸡比普通母鸡需要多大的立方空间之类的事上。这是我经历过的最奇妙、最匪夷所思的事。现在想起来简直像爱丽丝的一场梦！然后到了十点钟，我发现一辆汽车来接我了。'我送你回家，小姐，'他说。"

===Original===
"Were you with him all the time, by the way?" asked Craig.

===Chinese===
"对了，你一直跟他在一起吗？"克雷格问。

===Original===
She shook her head.

===Chinese===
她摇了摇头。

===Original===
"No, some part of the time I was with his housekeeper, who didn't even talk about chickens, but knitted large and shapeless jumpers, and sniffed. That was when he was telephoning. I knew he was telephoning because I could hear the drone of his voice."

===Chinese===
"没有，有一阵子我跟他的管家在一起，她连鸡都不聊，只顾织那些又大又没形的套头毛衣，还吸鼻子。那是他在打电话的时候。我知道他在打电话，因为能听到他低沉的嗡嗡声。"

===Original===
"He didn't bring you back?"

===Chinese===
"他没送你回来？"

===Original===
"No, he just put me into the car and told me that I should be perfectly safe. I arrived just a few minutes ahead of daddy."

===Chinese===
"没有，他只是把我送上车，说我会安然无恙的。我比爸爸早到了几分钟。"

===Original===
The detective scratched his chin, irritated and baffled.

===Chinese===
侦探挠了挠下巴，又恼火又困惑。

===Original===
"That's certainly got me," he said. "The rawboned lady I know, but the chicken gentleman is mysterious. You didn't hear his name, by any chance?"

===Chinese===
"这可真是把我难住了，"他说，"那个瘦骨嶙峋的女人我知道是谁，可这位养鸡绅士可太神秘了。你碰巧听到他的名字没有？"

===Original===
She shook her head.

===Chinese===
她摇了摇头。

===Original===
"Do you know the number of the house?"

===Chinese===
"你知道那栋房子的门牌号吗？"

===Original===
"Yes," she said frankly, "but he particularly asked me to forget it, and I've forgotten it." Then, in a more serious tone: "Is my—my—"

===Chinese===
"知道，"她坦率地说，"但他特意嘱咐我忘掉它，我也真的忘了。"然后她用更严肃的语气问道："我的——我的——"

===Original===
"Your nothing," interrupted Peter. "The blackguard was married—married to Lila. I think I must have gone daft, but I didn't realise this woman was planted in my house for a purpose. That type of girl wouldn't come at the wages she did if she had been genuine. Barney was always suspicious of her, by the way."

===Chinese===
"你的什么也没有，"彼得打断了她，"那个无赖已经结过婚了——娶了莉拉。我看我是昏了头了，竟没发现这女人被安插到我家是有目的的。那种类型的姑娘，要是真材实料，不会拿那么低的工钱来干活的。对了，巴尼对她一直有戒心。"

===Original===
"Have you seen Johnny?" the girl asked Craig.

===Chinese===
"你见到约翰尼了吗？"姑娘问克雷格。

===Original===
"No, I haven't seen him," said Craig carefully. "I thought of calling on him pretty soon."

===Chinese===
"没有，还没见到他，"克雷格谨慎地说，"我正打算尽快去拜访他。"

===Original===
Then it came to her in a flash, and she gasped.

===Chinese===
她突然明白过来，倒吸了一口气。

===Original===
"You don't think Johnny shot this man? You can't think that?"

===Chinese===
"你不会觉得是约翰尼开的枪吧？你不可能这么想吧？"

===Original===
