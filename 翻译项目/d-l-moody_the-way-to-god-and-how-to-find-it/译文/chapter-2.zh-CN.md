## II — The Gateway Into the Kingdom / II —— 进入国度的门径

> 「人若不重生，就不能见神的国。」
> ——约翰福音 3:3

===Original===
There is no portion of the Word of God, perhaps, with which we are more familiar than this passage. I suppose if I were to ask those in any audience if they believed that Jesus Christ taught the doctrine of the New Birth, nine tenths of them would say: “Yes, I believe He did.”

Now if the words of this text are true they embody one of the most solemn questions that can come before us. We can afford to be deceived about many things rather than about this one thing. Christ makes it very plain. He says, “Except a man be born again, he cannot *see* the Kingdom of God”—much less inherit it. This doctrine of the New Birth is therefore the foundation of all our hopes for the world to come. It is really the A B C of the Christian religion. My experience has been this—that if a man is unsound on this doctrine he will be unsound on almost every other fundamental doctrine in the Bible. A true understanding of this subject will help a man to solve a thousand difficulties that he may meet with in the Word of God. Things that before seemed very dark and mysterious will become very plain.

===Chinese===
神的话语中，也许再没有哪一段比这一段我们更熟悉的了。我想，无论在什么样的会众面前，我若问他们：耶稣基督是不是教导过「重生」这道理？十之八九的人会答：「是的，我相信祂教导过。」

那么，这一节经文若是真的，它就囊括了能摆在我们面前的最严肃的问题之一。我们在许多事上受骗尚可，唯独在这一件事上不可受骗。基督说得很明白。祂说：「人若不重生，就*不能见*神的国」——更不用说承受它了。所以这「重生」的道理，乃是我们对来世一切盼望的根基。它实在是基督教的 A B C。我的经验是——一个人若在这一条道理上站得不稳，他在圣经里几乎每一条基本道理上都会站不稳。真正明白这一题目，能帮助一个人解开他在神的话语中所遇见的千般疑难。从前看着幽暗难明的事，会变得十分明白。

===Original===
The doctrine of the New Birth upsets all false religion—all false views about the Bible and about God. A friend of mine once told me that in one of his after-meetings, a man came to him with a long list of questions written out for him to answer. He said: “If you can answer these questions satisfactorily, I have made up my mind to be a Christian.” “Do you not think,” said my friend, “that you had better come to Christ first? Then you can look into these questions.” The man thought that perhaps he had better do so. After he had received Christ, he looked again at his list of questions; but then it seemed to him as if they had all been answered. Nicodemus came with his troubled mind, and Christ said to him, “Ye must be born again.” He was treated altogether differently from what he expected; but I venture to say that was the most blessed night in all his life. To be “born again” is the greatest blessing that will ever come to us in this world.

===Chinese===
「重生」的道理推翻一切虚假的宗教——推翻一切对圣经、对神的错谬看法。我有一位朋友曾告诉我，有一次散会后，一个人拿着一张写满问题的单子来找他作答。那人说：「你若能把这些题目答得叫我满意，我就决心做基督徒了。」我朋友说：「你想，你不该先到基督这里来吗？来了再看这些问题。」那人想想，也觉得这样更好。他既接受了基督，再回头看那张问题单；可这一回，那些问题仿佛都已自行解答了。尼哥底母（Nicodemus）心怀困扰地来，基督对他说：「你们必须重生。」他所受的对待，与他所预料的截然不同；可我敢说，那一夜是他一生中最蒙福的一夜。「重生」是我们在今世所能得的、最大的福气。

===Original===
Notice how the Scripture puts it. “Except a man be born again,” “born from above,” “born of the Spirit.” From amongst a number of other passages where we find this word “**except**,” I would just name three. “Except ye repent, ye shall all likewise perish.” (Luke 13:3, 5) “Except ye be converted, and become as little children, ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven.” (Matthew 18:3) “Except your righteousness shall exceed the righteousness of the Scribes and Pharisees, ye shall in no case enter into the kingdom of heaven.” (Matthew 5:20) They all really mean the same thing.

I am so thankful that our Lord spoke of the New Birth to this ruler of the Jews, this doctor of the law, rather than to the woman at the well of Samaria, or to Matthew the publican, or to Zaccheus. If He had reserved his teaching on this great matter for these three, or such as these, people would have said: “Oh yes, these publicans and harlots need to be converted: but I am an upright man; I do not need to be converted.” I suppose Nicodemus was one of the best specimens of the people of Jerusalem: there was nothing on record against him.

===Chinese===
请注意圣经是怎样说的。「人若不重生」「从上面生」「从灵而生」。在圣经若干处出现「**若不**」二字的地方，我只举三处。「你们若不悔改，都要如此灭亡。」（路加福音 13:3, 5）「你们若不回转，变成小孩子的样式，断不得进天国。」（马太福音 18:3）「你们的义若不胜于文士和法利赛人的义，断不能进天国。」（马太福音 5:20）它们其实指的都是同一件事。

我何等感谢主，竟把「重生」的道理对这位犹太人的官、这位律法师讲，而不是对撒玛利亚井旁的妇人、对税吏马太、对撒该讲。倘若祂把这大道理只留给这三位、或这一类人，人们就会说：「哦，是啊，这些税吏和娼妓是当悔改；可我是正直的人，我不需要悔改。」我想尼哥底母可算是耶路撒冷人中的佼佼者了：他没有任何案底。

===Original===
I think it is scarcely necessary for me to prove that we need to be born again before we are meet for heaven. I venture to say that there is no candid man but would say he is not fit for the kingdom of God, until he is born of another Spirit. The Bible teaches us that man by nature is lost and guilty, and our experience confirms this. We know also that the best and holiest man, if he turn away from God, will very soon fall into sin.

Now, let me say what Regeneration is not. It is not going to church. Very often I see people, and ask them if they are Christians. “Yes, of course I am; at least, I think I am: I go to church every Sunday.” Ah, but this is not Regeneration. Others say, “I am trying to do what is right—am I not a Christian? Is not that a new birth?” No. What has that to do with being born again? There is yet another class—those who have “turned over a new leaf,” and think they are regenerated. No; forming a new resolution is not being born again.

===Chinese===
我想，我们需重生之后才配进天堂，这一层几乎不必再证明。我敢说，没有一个坦诚的人不肯承认：在他受了另一位圣灵而生之前，他不配进神的国。圣经教导我们：人按本性是失丧、是有罪的，而我们的经验也证实了这一点。我们也晓得：最好最圣洁的人，若转脸不认神，也很快会陷入罪中。

那么，让我说「重生」不是什么。重生不是上教堂。我常常见人，问他们是不是基督徒。「是啊，当然是；至少我以为是：我每个礼拜天都上教堂。」哎，可这不是重生。另一些人说：「我尽力做对的事——难道我不是基督徒？这不就是新生吗？」不是。这与重生何干？还有一类——那些「翻开了新的一页」、就以为自己重生了的。不；立一个新的志向不等于重生。

===Original===
Nor will being baptized do you any good. Yet you hear people say, “Why, I have been baptized; and I was born again when I was baptized.” They believe that because they were baptized into the church, they were baptized into the Kingdom of God. I tell you that it is utterly impossible. You may be baptized into the church, and yet not be baptized into the Son of God. Baptism is all right in its place. God forbid that I should say anything against it. But if you put that in the place of Regeneration—in the place of the New Birth—it is a terrible mistake. You cannot be baptized into the Kingdom of God. “Except a man be **born again**, he cannot see the Kingdom of God.” If anyone reading this rests his hopes on anything else—on any other foundation—I pray that God may sweep it away.

Another class say, “I go to the Lord’s Supper; I partake uniformly of the Sacrament.” Blessed ordinance! Jesus hath said that as often as ye do it ye commemorate His death. Yet, that is not being “born again;” that is not passing from death unto life. Jesus says plainly—and so plainly that there need not be any mistake about it—“Except a man be born of the Spirit, he cannot enter into the Kingdom of God.” What has a sacrament to do with that? What has going to church to do with being born again?

===Chinese===
受洗也不能救你。可你听人说：「咦，我受过洗了；我受洗的时候就是重生了。」他们以为，既然受了洗归入教会，便是受洗归入了神的国。我告诉你，这是万万不能的。你可以受洗归入教会，却仍未归入神之子。洗礼在它该在的位置上没有错。我断不至说反对它的话。可你若把洗礼摆在「重生」的位置上——摆在「新生」的位置上——那就是可怕的错误。你不能受洗进入神的国。「人若不**重生**，就不能见神的国。」倘若读这段话的任何人，把指望寄托在别的事物上——别的根基上——我求神把它扫除。

另一些人说：「我守圣餐；我每次都领圣礼。」蒙福的圣礼啊！耶稣说过，你们每逢这样做，便是记念祂的死。然而，那不是「重生」；那不是出死入生。耶稣说得明明白白——明白到不会有任何误解的地步——「人若不是从灵生的，就不能进神的国。」圣礼与这事何干？上教堂与重生何干？

===Original===
Another man comes up and says, “I say my prayers regularly.” Still I say that is not being born of the Spirit. It is a very solemn question, then, that comes up before us; and oh! that every reader would ask himself earnestly and faithfully: “Have I been born again? Have I been born of the Spirit? Have I passed from death unto life?”

There is a class of men who say that special religious meetings are very good for a certain class of people. They would be very good if you could get the drunkard there, or get the gambler there, or get other vicious people there—that would do a great deal of good. But “we do not need to be converted.” To whom did Christ utter these words of wisdom? To Nicodemus. Who was Nicodemus? Was he a drunkard, a gambler, or a thief? No! No doubt he was one of the very best men in Jerusalem. He was an honorable Councillor; he belonged to the Sanhedrim; he held a very high position; he was an orthodox man; he was one of the very soundest men. And yet what did Christ say to him? “Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God.”

===Chinese===
又一个人走过来说：「我按时祷告。」我还是要说，这不是从灵而生。于是摆在我们面前的，是一个极庄严的问题；哦！但愿每一位读者都诚恳、忠实地问自己：「我重生了吗？我从灵而生了吗？我出死入生了吗？」

有一类人说，那些特别的奋兴聚会，对某一类人很有好处。要是你能把醉汉弄去，把赌徒弄去，把其他作恶的人弄去——那能大有好处。可是「我们用不着悔改。」基督这番智慧之语，是对谁说的？对尼哥底母。尼哥底母是谁？他是醉汉、赌徒，还是窃贼？不！他无疑是耶路撒冷最好的人之一。他是受人尊敬的议士；他属于公会（Sanhedrim）；他地位崇高；他是正统的人；他是最稳健的人之一。然而基督对他说了什么？「人若不重生，就不能见神的国。」

===Original===
But I can imagine someone saying, “What am I to do? I cannot create life. I certainly cannot save myself.” You certainly cannot; and we do not claim that you can. We tell you it is utterly impossible to make a man better without Christ; but that is what men are trying to do. They are trying to patch up this “old Adam” nature. **There must be a new creation**. Regeneration is a new creation; and if it is a new creation it must be the work of God. In the first chapter of Genesis man does not appear. There is no one there but God. Man is not there to take part. When God created the earth He was alone. When Christ redeemed the world He was alone.

“That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit.” (John 3:6) The Ethiopian cannot change his skin, and the leopard cannot change his spots. You might as well try to make yourselves pure and holy without the help of God. It would be just as easy for you to do that as for the black man to wash himself white. A man might just as well try to leap over the moon as to serve God in the flesh. Therefore, “that which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit.”

===Chinese===
可我能想象有人说：「那我该怎么办？我不能创造生命。我当然不能自救。」你当然不能；我们也不说你能够。我们告诉你，没有基督，要让一个人变好，是绝无可能的；可人们正试图这样做的。他们想修补这「老旧亚当」的本性。**必须有一个新的创造**。重生就是新的创造；既是新创造，就必是神的作为。在创世记第一章里，人尚未出场。那里除了神之外没有别人。人不在场，无分于其中。神创造大地时，祂独自一人。基督救赎世界时，祂也是独自一人。

「从肉身生的就是肉身；从灵生的就是灵。」（约翰福音 3:6）古实人不能改变他的肤色，豹子不能改变它的斑点。你想不靠神的帮助而使自己纯洁圣洁，也是同样不可能。你做这事，正如黑人想把自己洗成白人一样难。一个人想凭肉身事奉神，不如说他想一蹦跳过月亮。所以，「从肉身生的就是肉身；从灵生的就是灵。」

===Original===
Now God tells us in this chapter how we are to get into His kingdom. We are not to work our way in—not but that salvation is worth working for. We admit all that. If there were rivers and mountains in the way, it would be well worth while to swim those rivers, and climb those mountains. There is no doubt that salvation is worth all that effort; but we do not obtain it by our works. It is “to him that worketh not, but believeth” (Romans 4:5). We work because we are saved; we do not work to be saved. We work from the cross; but not towards it. It is written, “Work out your own salvation with fear and trembling” (Philippians 2:12). Why, you must have your salvation before you can work it out. Suppose I say to my little boy, “I want you to spend that hundred dollars carefully.” “Well,” he says, “let me have the hundred dollars; and I will be careful how I spend it.” I remember when I first left home and went to Boston; I had spent all my money, and I went to the post-office three times a day. I knew there was only one mail a day from home; but I thought by some possibility there might be a letter for me. At last I received a letter from my little sister; and oh, how glad I was to get it. She had heard that there were a great many pickpockets in Boston, and a large part of that letter was to urge me to be very careful not to let anybody pick my pocket. Now I required to have something in my pocket before I could have it picked. So you must have salvation before you can work it out.

===Chinese===
那么，神在这一章里告诉我们怎样进入祂的国。我们不是凭做工进去的——并不是说救恩不值得一做去求。这一切我们都承认。即便有江河、有高山挡路，泅过那些江河、翻过那些高山，也是值得的。救恩无疑配得上这一切努力；可我们不是靠行为得着它。那是「那不作工的，只信」（罗马书 4:5）。我们做工，是因为已经得救；我们做工，不是为要得救。我们是从十字架出发做工；而不是朝它做工。经上记着：「当恐惧战兢做成你们得救的工夫」（腓立比书 2:12）。哎呀，你必须先有救恩，才能把它做出来。设想我对我的小儿子说：「我要你小心地花掉那一百块。」「好啊，」他说，「先把那一百块给我；我自会小心地花。」我还记得我头一回离家去波士顿（Boston）；钱花光了，我一天跑三趟邮局。我明知家里一天只来一封信；可我总想着也许有我的一封。终于我收到了小妹妹的一封信；哎呀，接到它我多高兴啊。她听说波士顿扒手很多，信里大半都是叮嘱我千万小心别让人扒了口袋。可我得先在口袋里有东西，才能被人扒。所以你必须先有救恩，才能把它做出来。

===Original===
When Christ cried out on Calvary, “It is finished!” He meant what He said. All that men have to do now is just to accept of the work of Jesus Christ. There is no hope for man or woman so long as they are trying to work out salvation for themselves. I can imagine there are some people who will say, as Nicodemus possibly did, “This is a very mysterious thing.” I see the scowl on that Pharisee’s brow as he says, “How can these things be?” It sounds very strange to his ear. “Born again; born of the Spirit! How can these things be?” A great many people say, “You must reason it out; but if you do not reason it out, do not ask us to believe it.” I can imagine a great many people saying that. When you ask me to reason it out, I tell you frankly I cannot do it. “The wind bloweth where it listeth, and thou hearest the sound thereof, but canst not tell whence it cometh and whither it goeth: so is every one that is born of the Spirit.” (John 8). I do not understand everything about the wind. You ask me to reason it out. I cannot. It may blow due north here, and a hundred miles away due south. I may go up a few hundred feet, and find it blowing in an entirely opposite direction from what it is down here. You ask me to explain these currents of wind; but suppose that, because I cannot explain them, and do not understand them, I were to take my stand and assert, “Oh, there is no such thing as wind.” I can imagine some little girl saying, “I know more about it than that man does; often have I heard the wind, and felt it blowing against my face;” and she might say, “Did not the wind blow my umbrella out of my hands the other day? and did I not see it blow a man’s hat off in the street? Have I not seen it blow the trees in the forest, and the growing corn in the country?”

===Chinese===
基督在各各他喊出「成了！」的时候，祂是当真的。如今人所要做的，只是接受耶稣基督所做成的工作。只要人还想靠自己做出来救恩，就毫无指望。我能想象有些人——也许像尼哥底母那样——会说：「这事太奥秘了。」我看见那法利赛人皱起眉头说：「怎能有这事呢？」这话在他听来十分生疏。「重生；从灵而生！怎能有这事呢？」许多人说：「你得把它推理出来；可你要推理不出来，就别叫我们信。」我能想象许多人这样说。你叫我把它推理出来，我坦白告诉你，我做不到。「风随着意思吹，你听见风的响声，却不晓得从哪里来，往哪里去；凡从灵生的，也是如此。」（约翰福音 3:8）。风的事，我不是什么都懂。你叫我推理。我不能。它也许在这儿正往北吹，一百英里外却正往南吹。我也许往上爬几百英尺，便发觉它吹的方向与下面完全相反。你叫我解释这些气流；可设想一下，正因为我不懂、不能解释，便站定脚跟声称：「哦，根本没有风这种东西。」我能想象一位小女孩说：「这事我比那位先生懂得多；我常听见风，感到它吹在我脸上；」她也许还会说：「前些日子不是风把我的伞吹脱了手吗？我不是看见它把街上一位先生的帽子吹掉了吗？我不是看见它吹动林中的树、乡间生长的庄稼吗？」

===Original===
You might just as well tell me that there is no such thing as wind, as tell me there is no such thing as a man being born of the Spirit. I have felt the spirit of God working in my heart, just as really and as truly as I have felt the wind blowing in my face. I cannot reason it out. There are a great many things I cannot reason out, but which I believe. I never could reason out the creation. I can see the world, but I cannot tell how God made it out of nothing. But almost every man will admit there was a creative power.

There are a great many things that I cannot explain and cannot reason out, and yet that I believe. I heard a commercial traveler say that he had heard that the ministry and religion of Jesus Christ were matters of revelation and not of investigation. “When it pleased God to reveal His Son in Me,” says Paul (Galatians 1:15–16). There was a party of young men together, going up the country; and on their journey they made up their minds not to believe anything they could not reason out. An old man heard them; and presently he said, “I heard you say you would not believe anything you could not reason out.” “Yes,” they said, “that is so.” “Well,” he said, “coming down on the train today, I noticed some geese, some sheep, some swine, and some cattle all eating grass. Can you tell me by what process that same grass was turned into hair, feathers, bristles and wool? Do you believe it is a fact?” “Oh yes,” they said, “we cannot help believing that, though we fail to understand it.” “Well,” said the old man, “I cannot help believing in Jesus Christ.” And I cannot help believing in the regeneration of man, when I see men who have been reclaimed, when I see men who have been reformed. Have not some of the very worst men been regenerated—been picked up out of the pit, and had their feet set upon the Rock, and a new song put in their mouths? Their tongues were cursing and blaspheming; and now are occupied in praising God. Old things have passed away, and all things have become new. They are not reformed only, but **regenerated**—new men in Christ Jesus.
