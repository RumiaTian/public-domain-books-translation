## IX — Backsliding / IX ——「退后」

> 「我必医治他们的退后，甘心爱他们，因为我的怒气向他们转消。」
> ——何西阿书 14:4

===Original===
There are two kinds of backsliders. Some have never been converted: they have gone through the form of joining a Christian community and claim to be backsliders; but they never have, if I may use the expression, “slid forward.” They may talk of backsliding; but they have never really been born again. They need to be treated differently from real backsliders—those who have been born of the incorruptible seed, but who have turned aside. We want to bring the latter back the same road by which they left their first love.

Turn to Psalm 85:5. There you read: “Wilt Thou be angry with us forever? wilt Thou draw out Thine anger to all generations? wilt Thou not revive us again: that Thy people may rejoice in Thee? Show us Thy mercy, O Lord; and grant us Thy salvation.” Now look again: “*I will hear what God the Lord will speak:* for He will speak peace unto His people, and to His saints; but let them not turn again to folly” (verse 8).

===Chinese===
退后之人有两种。一种是从未归主的：他们走完了加入基督徒群体的形式，自称是退后之人；可是他们若容我用这个说法——从来就未曾「向前滑」过。他们可以谈论退后；但他们从未真正重生。他们所需的对待，与真正的退后之人不同——后者是藉着不能朽坏的种子而生，却偏离了正路。我们要把后者带回来，循着他们当初离开起初爱心时所走的那条路。

请翻到诗篇 85:5。那里记着：「你要永远向我们发怒吗？你的怒气要延及万代吗？你不再将我们救活，使你的百姓靠你欢喜吗？耶和华啊，求你向我们显出你的慈爱，将你的救恩赐给我们。」再看下面：「*我要听神耶和华所说的话*，因为他必应许将平安赐给他的百姓、他的圣民；他们却不可再转去受愚妄」（8 节）。

===Original===
There is nothing that will do backsliders so much good as to come in contact with the Word of God; and for them the Old Testament is as full of help as the New. The book of Jeremiah has some wonderful passages for wanderers. What we want to do is to get backsliders to hear what God the Lord will say.

Look for a moment at Jeremiah 6:10. “To whom shall I speak, and give warning, that they may hear? behold, their ear is uncircumcised, and they cannot hearken: behold, the word of the Lord is unto them a reproach; they have no delight in it.” That is the condition of backsliders. They have no delight whatever in the word of God. But we want to bring them back, and let God get their ear. Read from the 14th verse: “They have healed also the hurt of the daughter of My people slightly, saying, Peace, peace; when there is no peace. Were they ashamed when they had committed abomination? nay, they were not at all ashamed, neither could they blush: therefore they shall fall among them that fall: at the time that I visit them they shall be cast down, saith the Lord. Thus saith the Lord, Stand ye in the ways, and see, and ask for the old paths, where is the good way, and walk therein; and ye shall find rest for your souls. But they said, We will not walk therein. Also I set watchmen over you, saying, Hearken to the sound of the trumpet. But they said, We will not hearken.”

===Chinese===
没有什么比接触神的话语更能叫退后之人得益处的了；对退后之人而言，旧约与新约一样充满帮助。耶利米书里有一些为流荡者写的奇妙经文。我们所要做的，就是叫退后之人听见神耶和华所要说的话。

请稍看耶利米书 6:10。「我应当对谁说话，应当警告谁呢？看哪，他们的耳朵未受割礼，不能听见；看哪，耶和华的话他们以为是羞辱，不以为喜悦。」这正是退后之人的光景。他们对神的话语毫无喜悦可言。但我们要把他们带回来，让神得着他们的耳朵。从第 14 节读起：「他们轻轻忽忽地医治我百姓的损伤，说：平安了！平安了！其实没有平安。他们行可憎的事觉得惭愧吗？不然，他们毫不惭愧，也不知羞耻。因此，他们必在仆倒的人中仆倒；我向他们察看的时候，他们必跌倒。这是耶和华说的。耶和华如此说：你们当站在路上察看，访问古道，哪是善道，便行在其间；这样，你们心里必得安息。他们却说：我们不行在其间。我设立守望的人警戒他们，说：要听角声。他们却说：我们不听。」

===Original===
That was the condition of the Jews when they had backslidden. They had turned away from the old paths. And that is the condition of backsliders. They have got away from the good old book. Adam and Eve fell by not hearkening to the word of God. They did not believe God’s word; but they believed the tempter. That is the way backsliders fall—by turning away from the word of God.

In Jeremiah 2 we find God pleading with them as a father would plead with a son. “Thus saith the Lord, What iniquity have your fathers found in Me, that they are gone from Me, and have walked after vanity, and are become vain?… Wherefore I will yet plead with you, saith the Lord; and with your children’s children will I plead… For my people have committed two evils: they have forsaken Me, the Fountain of living waters, and hewed them out cisterns, broken cisterns, that can hold no water.”

===Chinese===
犹太人退后之时，正是这光景。他们偏离了古道。退后之人的光景也是如此。他们远离了那本宝贵的古老书卷。亚当和夏娃之所以堕落，是因为不听神的话语。他们不信神的话；反倒信了那诱惑人的。退后之人堕落的方式正是如此——因转离神的话语。

在耶利米书第 2 章，我们看到神像父亲恳求儿子那样恳求他们。「耶和华如此说：你们列祖见我有什么不义，竟远离我，随从虚无的神，自己成为虚妄的呢？……耶和华说：我因此必与你们争辩，也必与你们的子孙争辩……因为我的百姓做了两件恶事，就是离弃我这活水的泉源，为自己凿出池子，是破裂不能存水的池子。」

===Original===
Now there is one thing to which we wish to call the attention of backsliders; and that is, that the Lord never forsook them; but that they forsook Him! The Lord never left them; but they left Him! And this, too, without any cause! He says, “What iniquity have your fathers found in Me, that they are gone far from Me?” Is not God the same today as when you came to Him first? Has God changed? Men are apt to think that God has changed; but the fault is with them. Backslider, I would ask you, “What iniquity is there in God, that you have left Him and gone far from Him?” You have, He says, hewed out to yourselves broken cisterns that hold no water. The world cannot satisfy the new nature. No earthly well can satisfy the soul that has become a partaker of the heavenly nature. Honor, wealth and the pleasures of this world will not satisfy those who, having tasted the water of life, have gone astray, seeking refreshment at the world’s fountains. Earthly wells will get dry. They cannot quench spiritual thirst.

Again in the 32nd verse: “Can a maid forget her ornaments, or a bride her attire? yet My people have forgotten Me, days without number.” That is the charge which God brings against the backslider. They “have forgotten Me, days without number.”

===Chinese===
如今有一件事，我们要请退后之人留意：主从未离弃他们；是他们离弃了祂！主从未离开他们；是他们离开了祂！而且，这是毫无缘由的！祂说：「你们列祖见我有什么不义，竟远离我呢？」神今日岂不与你初次到祂面前时一样吗？神改变了吗？人总以为神改变了；过错却在于他们自己。退后之人啊，我要问你：「神有什么不义，使你离开祂、远远地远离祂？」祂说，你们为自己凿出了破裂不能存水的池子。这世界不能满足那新性情。没有任何属地的井，能满足那有分于属天性情的灵魂。尊荣、财富和这世界的种种享乐，都不能满足那些已经尝过生命之水、却走迷了路、到世界的泉源那里寻求畅快的人。属地的井终必干涸。它们解不了属灵的渴。

再看第 32 节：「处女岂能忘记她的妆饰呢？新妇岂能忘记她的美衣呢？我的百姓却忘记了我无数的日子！」这正是神对退后之人所提出的控告。他们「忘记了我无数的日子」。

===Original===
I have often startled young ladies when I have said to them, “My friend, you think more of your earrings than of the Lord.” The reply has been, “No, I do not.” But when I have asked, “Would you not be troubled if you lost one; and would you not set about seeking for it?” the answer has been, “Well, yes, I think I should.” But though they had turned from the Lord, it did not give them any trouble; nor did they seek after Him that they might find Him.

How many once in fellowship and in daily communion with the Lord now think more of their dresses and ornaments than of their precious souls! Love does not like to be forgotten. Mothers would have broken hearts if their children left them and never wrote a word or sent any memento of their affection; and God pleads over backsliders as a parent over loved ones who have gone astray. He tries to woo them back. He asks: “What have I done that you should have forsaken Me?”

===Chinese===
我常对年轻的女子说：「朋友，你把你的耳环看得比主还重。」这话常叫她们一惊。她们回答说：「不，我没有。」可等我再问：「你若丢了一只，岂不是会着急吗？岂不是会马上设法去找吗？」她们的回答是：「嗯，是的，我想我会的。」然而她们虽然离开了主，心里却一点不觉得为难；她们也不去寻找祂，好寻见祂。

曾有多少人一度与主相交、日日与主亲密，如今却把自己的衣裳饰物看得比自己宝贵的灵魂还重！爱不愿被人遗忘。作母亲的，若儿女离开她们，从不写一句话、也不捎一点记念爱心的物件，她们的心都要碎了；神为退后之人恳切，正如父母为走迷的儿女恳切。祂设法把他们吸引回来。祂问：「我做了什么，使你竟离弃我？」

===Original===
The most tender and loving words to be found in the whole of the Bible are from Jehovah to those who have left Him without a cause. Jeremiah 2:19.

Hear how He argues with such: (Jeremiah 2:19). “Thine own wickedness shall correct thee, and thy backslidings shall reprove thee; know, therefore, and see, that it is an evil thing and bitter, that thou hast forsaken the Lord thy God, and that My fear is not in thee, saith the Lord God of hosts.”

===Chinese===
整本圣经里所能找到的最温柔、最充满爱意的话，正是耶和华对那些无故离开祂之人所说的。耶利米书 2:19。

请听祂怎样与这样的人理论：（耶利米书 2:19）。「你自己的恶必惩治你；你退后的事必责备你。由此可知可见，你离弃耶和华你的神，不存敬畏我的心，乃为恶事，为苦事。这是万军之主耶和华说的。」

===Original===
I do not exaggerate when I say that I have seen hundreds of backsliders come back; and I have asked them if they have not found it an evil and a bitter thing to leave the Lord. You cannot find a real backslider, who has known the Lord, but will admit that it is an evil and a bitter thing to turn away from Him; and I do not know of any one verse more used to bring back wanderers than that very one. May it bring you back if you have wandered into the far country.

Look at Lot. Did not he find it an evil and a bitter thing? He was twenty years in Sodom, and never made a convert. He got on well in the sight of the world. Men would have told you that he was one of the most influential and worthy men in all Sodom. But alas! alas! he ruined his family. And it is a pitiful sight to see that old backslider going through the streets of Sodom at midnight, after he has warned his children, and they have turned a deaf ear.

===Chinese===
我毫不夸张地说，我曾见过成百的退后之人归回；我问他们，离开主是不是一件又恶又苦的事。你找不到一个真正认识主的退后之人，不承认远离祂是又恶又苦的事；据我所知，再没有哪一节经文比这一节更常被用来领流荡者归回。你若已流荡到了远方，愿这节经文把你领回来。

你看罗得。他岂不觉得这是一件又恶又苦的事吗？他在所多玛住了二十年，从未领过一个人归主。他在世人眼前混得不错。人们会告诉你，他是全所多玛最有势力、最体面的人之一。可是哀哉！哀哉！他毁了自己的家。想想那位年迈的退后之人，午夜走在所多玛的街上——他刚刚警告过他的孩子们，他们却充耳不闻——那是一幅多么可悲的景象。

===Original===
I have never known a man and his wife backslide, without its proving utter ruin to their children. They will make a mockery of religion and will deride their parents: “Thine own wickedness shall correct thee; and thy backsliding shall reprove thee!” Did not David find it so? Mark him, crying, “O my son Absalom, my son, my son Absalom! would God I had died for thee; O Absalom, my son, my son!” I think it was the ruin, rather than the death of his son that caused this anguish.

I remember being engaged in conversation some years ago, till past midnight, with an old man. He had been for years wandering on the barren mountains of sin. That night he wanted to get back. We prayed, and prayed, and prayed, till light broke in upon him; and he went away rejoicing. The next night he sat in front of me when I was preaching, and I think that I never saw anyone look so sad and wretched in all my life. He followed me into the enquiry-room. “What is the trouble?” I asked. “Is your eye off the Saviour? Have your doubts come back?” “No; it is not that,” he said. “I did not go to business, but spent all this day in visiting my children. They are all married and in this city. I went from house to house, but there was not one but mocked me. It is the darkest day of my life. I have awoke up to what I have done. I have taken my children into the world; and now I cannot get them out.” The Lord had restored unto him the joy of His salvation; yet there was the bitter consequence of his transgression. You can run through your experience; and you can find just such instances repeated again and again. Many who came to your city years ago serving God, in their prosperity have forgotten Him: and where are their sons and daughters? Show me the father and mother who have deserted the Lord and gone back to the beggarly elements of the world; and I am mistaken if their children are not on the high road to ruin.

===Chinese===
我从未见过一对夫妇退后，而不给他们的儿女带来彻底的毁灭。他们必把信仰当作笑柄，并讥诮父母：「你自己的恶必惩治你；你退后的事必责备你！」大卫岂不也经历了这事吗？请听他喊着说：「我儿押沙龙啊！我儿，我儿押沙龙啊！我恨不得替你死，押沙龙啊，我儿，我儿！」我想，使他如此痛苦的，是儿子的毁灭，而非儿子的死。

我记得几年前，曾与一位老人交谈，直到过了半夜。他多年流荡在罪的无草之山上。那夜他想回来。我们祷告、祷告、再祷告，直到光射进他心里；他便欢欢喜喜地走了。第二天夜里，我讲道时他坐在我面前，我想我一生从未见过谁面容那么悲伤、那么凄惨。他随我进了询道室。「难处在哪里？」我问。「你的眼睛离开救主了吗？疑惑又回来了吗？」「不；不是这个，」他说。「我今天没有去做生意，却花了一整天去探望我的儿女。他们都成了家，住在这城里。我挨家挨户地走，却没有一家不讥诮我。这是我一生中最黑暗的一天。我如今醒悟到我所做的了。我把儿女带进了世界；如今我却无法把他们领出来。」主已将祂救恩之乐恢复给了他；然而他犯罪的苦果仍摆在那里。你可以追溯自己的经历；你能发现这样的实例一遍又一遍地重演。许多年前来到你们城里事奉神的人，在发达之时便忘记了祂：他们的儿女如今在哪里呢？你指给我看一对撇弃了主、回到世界中那懦弱无用的小学之父的父母；若他们的儿女不是正走在大步迈向毁灭的路上，便是我的判断错了。

===Original===
As we desire to be faithful we warn these backsliders. It is a sign of love to warn of danger. We may be looked upon as enemies for a while; but the truest friends are those who lift up the voice of warning. Israel had no truer friend than Moses. In Jeremiah God gave His people a weeping prophet to bring them back to Him; but they cast off God. They forgot the God who brought them out of Egypt, and who led them through the desert into the promised land. In their prosperity they forget Him and turned away. The Lord had told them what would happen. (Deuteronomy 28). And see what did happen. The king who made light of the word of God was taken captive by Nebuchadnezzar, and his children brought up in front of him and every one slain: his eyes were put out of his head; and he was bound in fetters of brass and cast into a dungeon in Babylon. (2 Kings 25:7). That is the way he reaped what he had sown. Surely it is an evil and a bitter thing to backslide, but the Lord would win you back with the message of His Work.

===Chinese===
我们既要忠心，便警告这些退后之人。警告人脱离危险，正是爱的标记。我们一时也许被看作仇敌；但最真实的朋友，正是那些发出警告之声的人。以色列再没有比摩西更真实的朋友了。在耶利米书里，神赐给祂百姓一位流泪的先知，要领他们归向祂；他们却弃绝了神。他们忘记了那领他们出埃及、经过旷野领他们进入应许之地的神。在发达之时，他们便忘记了祂，转离而去。主早已告诉他们将会发生何事。（申命记第 28 章。）看看后来发生了什么。那位轻看神话语的王被尼布甲尼撒掳去，他的众子被带到他面前，一一被杀；他的双眼被剜出；他被铜链锁着，下在巴比伦的监牢里。（列王纪下 25:7。）这便是他自食其果的下场。退后实在是一件又恶又苦的事，但主愿用祂之工的信息把你赢回来。

===Original===
In Jeremiah 8:5, we read: “Why then is this people of Jerusalem slidden by a perpetual backsliding? They hold fast deceit; *They refuse to return*.” That is what the Lord brings against them. “**They refuse to return**.” “I hearkened and heard; but they spake not aright: no man repented him of his wickedness, saying, What have I done? Every one turned to his course, as the horse rusheth into the battle. Yea, the stork in the heaven knoweth her appointed times; and the turtle and the crane and the swallow observe the time of their coming; but My people know not the judgment of the Lord.”

Now look: “I hearkened and heard; but they spake not aright.” No family altar! No reading the Bible! No closet devotion! God stoops to hear; but His people have turned away! If there be a penitent backslider, one who is anxious for pardon and restoration, you will find no words more tender than are to be found in Jeremiah 3:12: “Go, and proclaim these words toward the north, and say, Return, thou backsliding Israel, saith the Lord; and I will not cause Mine anger to fall upon you: for I am merciful, saith the Lord, and I will not keep anger forever.” Now notice: “Only acknowledge thine iniquity, that thou hast transgressed against the Lord thy God, and hast scattered thy ways to the stranger under every green tree, and ye have not obeyed My voice, saith the Lord. Turn, O backsliding children, saith the Lord; for I am married unto you”—think of God coming and saying, “I am married unto you!”—and I will take you one of a city, and two of a family, and I will bring you to Zion.”

===Chinese===
在耶利米书 8:5，我们读到：「这耶路撒冷的百姓，为何长久退后呢？他们持定诡诈，*不肯回头*。」这正是主所控告他们的。「**他们不肯回头**。」「我留心听，听见他们不正直地说：没有人悔改自己的恶行，说：我做了什么呢？他们各人转奔己路，如马直闯战场。空中的鹳鸟知道来的时候；斑鸠、燕子与白鹤也守候当来的时令；我的百姓却不知道耶和华的法则。」

请看：「我留心听，听见他们不正直地说。」——没有家庭祭坛！没有读经！没有内室的灵修！神俯首垂听；祂的百姓却转离而去！若有一个悔悟的退后之人，渴望得着赦免与复兴，你会发现再没有比耶利米书 3:12 更温柔的话了：「你去向北方宣告说：耶和华说：退后的以色列啊，回来吧！我必不怒目看你们；因为我是慈爱的，我必不永远怀怒。这是耶和华说的。」请注意：「只要承认你的罪孽，就是你违背耶和华你的神，在各青翠树下向陌生人分散了你的道路，没有听从我的话。这是耶和华说的。退后的儿女啊，回来吧！因为我是你们的丈夫」——请想想，神竟来对人说：「我是你们的丈夫！」——「我也必从城中取一人，从一族取两人，将你们带到锡安。」

===Original===
“Only acknowledge thine iniquity.” How many times have I held that passage up to a backslider! “Acknowledge” it; and God says I will forgive you. I remember a man asking, “Who said that? Is that there?” And I held up to him the passage, “Only acknowledge thine iniquity;” and the man went down on his knees, and cried, “My God, I have sinned”; and the Lord restored him there and then. If you have wandered, He wants you to come back.

He says in another place, “O Ephraim, what shall I do unto thee? O Judah, what shall I do unto thee? for your goodness is as a morning cloud, and as the early dew it goeth away” (Hosea 6:4). His compassion and His love is wonderful!

===Chinese===
「只要承认你的罪孽。」我曾多少次把这节经文摆在一个退后之人面前！「承认」它；神说，我必赦免你。我记得一个人问：「这是谁说的？真有这话吗？」我便把那段经文指给他看——「只要承认你的罪孽」——那人便跪下去，喊着说：「我的神啊，我犯了罪」；主便当场恢复了他。你若已经流荡远了，祂愿你回来。

在另一处祂又说：「以法莲哪，我可向你怎样行呢？犹大啊，我可向你怎样做呢？因为你们的良善如同早晨的云雾，又如速散的甘露」（何西阿书 6:4）。祂的怜悯和祂的爱何等奇妙！

愿神恢复其他的退后之人，使他们比从前任何时候更被重用于祂的荣耀，胜过千倍。
