## V / V

### A Divine Saviour / 一位神圣的救主

“Thou art **the Christ**, the Son of the living God.” / 「你是**基督**，是永生神的儿子。」

> Matthew 16:1; John 6:69 / 马太福音 16:1；约翰福音 6:69

===Original===
We meet with a certain class of Enquirers who do not believe in the Divinity of Christ. There are many passages that will give light on this subject.

In 1 Corinthians 15:47, we are told: “The first man is of the earth earthy: the second man is the Lord from heaven.”

In 1 John 5:20: “We know that the Son of God is come, and hath given us an understanding, that we may know Him that is true; and we are in Him that is true, even in His Son Jesus Christ. This is the true God, and eternal life.”

Again in John 17:3: “And this is life eternal, that they might know Thee, the only true God; and Jesus Christ, whom Thou hast sent.”

===Chinese===
我们常遇到一类慕道者，不信基督的神性。关于这题目，有许多经文可以赐下亮光。

哥林多前书 15:47 告诉我们：「头一个人是出于地，乃属土；第二个人是出于天。」

约翰一书 5:20：「我们也知道神的儿子已经来到，且将智慧赐给我们，使我们认识那位真实的，我们也在那位真实的里面，就是在他儿子耶稣基督里面。这是真神，也是永生。」

约翰福音 17:3 又说：「认识你独一的真神，并且认识你所差来的耶稣基督，这就是永生。」

===Original===
And then, in Mark 14:60: “The high priest stood up in the midst, and asked Jesus, saying, Answerest Thou nothing? What is it which these witness against thee? But He held His peace, and answered nothing. Again the high priest asked Him, and said unto Him, Art Thou the Christ, the Son of the Blessed? And Jesus said, I am: and ye shall see the Son of Man sitting on the right hand of power, and coming in the clouds of heaven. Then the high priest rent his clothes, and saith, What need we any further witnesses? Ye have heard the blasphemy: what think ye? And they all condemned Him to be guilty of death.”

Now what brought me to believe in the Divinity of Christ was this: I did not know where to place Christ, or what to do with Him, if He were not divine. When I was a boy I thought that He was a good man like Moses, Joseph, or Abraham. I even thought that He was the best man who had ever lived on the earth. But I found that Christ had a higher claim. He claimed to be God-Man, to be divine; to have come from heaven. He said: “Before Abraham was I am” (John 8:58). I could not understand this; and I was driven to the conclusion﻿—and I challenge any candid man to deny the inference, or meet the argument﻿—that Jesus Christ is either an impostor or deceiver, or He is the God-Man﻿—God manifest in the flesh. And for these reasons. The first commandment is, “Thou shalt have no other gods before Me” (Exodus 20:2). Look at the millions throughout Christendom who worship Jesus Christ as God. If Christ be not God this is idolatry. We are all guilty of breaking the first commandment if Jesus Christ were mere man﻿—if He were a created being, and not what He claims to be.

===Chinese===
马可福音 14:60 又记着：「大祭司站起来，问到中间，问耶稣说：你什么都不回答吗？这些人作见证告你的是什么呢？耶稣却不言语，一句话也不答。大祭司又问他说：你是那当称颂者的儿子基督不是？耶稣说：我是。你们必看见人子坐在那权能者的右边，驾着天上的云降临。大祭司就撕开衣服，说：我们何必再用见证人呢？你们已经听见他这僭妄的话了。你们的意见如何？他们都定他该死的罪。」

至于叫我信服基督神性的，是这一点：基督若不是神，我就不知道该把祂摆在何处，也不知道该怎样对待祂。我年幼时，以为祂像摩西、约瑟、亚伯拉罕一样是个好人。我甚至以为祂是地上有史以来最好的人。可我发现基督有更高的宣告。祂宣告自己是神而人（God-Man），是神圣的，是从天上来的。祂说：「还没有亚伯拉罕，就有了我」（约翰福音 8:58）。我无法明白这话；便被推到了一个结论——我敢请任何坦白的人来否认这推论，或答覆这论证——耶稣基督要么是个骗子、是个欺哄人的，要么祂就是神而人——神在肉身显现。理由如下。第一条诫命是：「除了我以外，你不可有别的神」（出埃及记 20:2）。请看整个基督教界千千万万人敬拜耶稣基督为神。基督若不是神，这便是拜偶像。倘若耶稣基督不过是人——倘若祂只是受造之物，而不是祂自己所宣告的那位——我们便都犯了违背第一条诫命的罪。

===Original===
Some people, who do not admit His divinity, say that He was the best man who ever lived; but if He were not Divine, for that very reason He ought not to be reckoned a good man, for He laid claim to an honor and dignity to which these very people declare He had no right or title. That would rank Him as a deceiver.

Others say that He thought He was divine, but that He was deceived. As if Jesus Christ were carried away by a delusion and deception, and thought that He was more than He was! I could not conceive of a lower idea of Jesus Christ than that. This would not only make Him out an impostor; but that He was out of His mind, and that He did not know who He was, or where He came from. Now if Jesus Christ was not what He claimed to be, the Saviour of the world; and if He did not come from heaven, He was a gross deceiver.

===Chinese===
有些人不承认祂的神性，说祂是有史以来最好的人；可祂若不是神，正因这缘故祂便不该算作好人，因为祂自称拥有一种尊荣与威严，而正是这些人声称祂对此毫无权利与资格。那便要算祂为骗子了。

又有人说，祂以为自己有神性，其实是被蒙蔽了。仿佛耶稣基督被一种幻觉与欺骗所惑，自以为比祂所是的更大！我想不出比对耶稣基督更贬低的看法了。这不仅是把祂说成骗子；更是说祂神经错乱，连自己是谁、从哪里来都不晓得。倘若耶稣基督不是祂自己所宣告的那位——世人的救主；倘若祂不是从天上来的，祂便是一个彻头彻尾的骗子。

===Original===
But how can anyone read the life of Jesus Christ and make Him out a deceiver? A man has generally some motive for being an impostor. What was Christ’s motive? He knew that the course He was pursuing would conduct Him to the cross; that His name would be cast out as vile; and that many of His followers would be called upon to lay down their lives for His sake. Nearly every one of the apostles were martyrs; and they were considered as offscouring and refuse in the midst of the people. If a man is an impostor, he has a motive at the back of his hypocrisy. But what was Christ’s object? The record is that “He went about doing good.” This is not the work of an impostor. Do not let the enemy of your soul deceive you.

In John 5:21 we read: “For as the Father raiseth up the dead, and quickeneth them; even so the Son quickeneth whom He will. For the Father judgeth no man, but hath committed all judgment unto the Son: that all men should honor the Son, even as they honor the Father. He that honoureth not the Son, honoureth not the Father which hath sent Him.”

===Chinese===
然而怎能有人读过耶稣基督的生平，竟把祂说成骗子呢？一个人假冒为善，通常总有某种动机。基督的动机是什么？祂知道祂所走的路必把祂引到十字架；祂的名必被当作恶名抛弃；祂的许多跟随者也要为祂的缘故舍命。使徒几乎没有一个不是殉道者；他们被当作万物的渣滓，被人厌弃。一个人若是骗子，他的虚伪背后总有动机。可基督的目的是什么呢？圣经上记着，祂「周流四方，行善事」。这绝不是骗子所为。不要容那灵魂的仇敌欺骗你。

约翰福音 5:21 记着：「父怎样叫死人起来，使他们活着，子也照样随自己的意思使人活着。父不审判什么人，乃将审判的事全交与子，叫人都尊敬子如同尊敬父一样。不尊敬子的，就是不尊敬差子来的父。」

===Original===
Now notice: by the Jewish law if a man were a blasphemer he was to be put to death; and supposing Christ to be merely human if this be not blasphemy I do not know where you will find it. “He that honoureth not the Son, honoureth not the Father.” That is downright blasphemy if Christ be not divine. If Moses, or Elijah, or Elisha, or any other mortal had said, “You must honour me as you honor God;” and had put himself on a level with God, it would have been downright blasphemy.

The Jews put Christ to death because they said that He was not what He claimed to be. It was on that testimony He was put under oath. The high priest said: “I adjure Thee by the living God, that Thou tell us whether Thou be the Christ, the Son of God” (Matthew 26:63). And when the Jews came round Him and said, “How long dost Thou make us to doubt? If Thou be the Christ tell us plainly.” Jesus said, “I and My Father are one.” Then the Jews took up stones again to stone Him. (John 10:24﻿–﻿33). They said they did not want to hear more, for that was blasphemy. It was for declaring Himself to be the Son of God that He was condemned and put to death. (Matthew 26:63﻿–﻿66).

===Chinese===
请留意：按犹太律法，人若亵渎，必被治死；倘若基督不过是人，这若不算亵渎，我就不知道哪里还能找到亵渎了。「不尊敬子的，就是不尊敬差子来的父。」基督若不是神，这便是彻头彻尾的亵渎。摩西、以利亚、以利沙，或任何别的人，若曾说「你们必须像尊敬神那样尊敬我」，又把自己与神并列，那便是彻头彻尾的亵渎。

犹太人把基督处死，因为祂们说祂并不是祂自己所宣告的那位。正是为此祂被起誓质问。大祭司说：「我指着永生神叫你起誓告诉我们，你是神的儿子基督不是」（马太福音 26:63）。犹太人围着祂说：「你叫我们犹疑不定到几时呢？你若是基督，就明明地告诉我们。」耶稣说：「我与父原为一。」犹太人又拿起石头来要打祂（约翰福音 10:24–33）。祂们说不要再听了，因为那是亵渎。祂正是因宣告自己是神的儿子而被定罪、被处死（马太福音 26:63–66）。

===Original===
Now if Jesus Christ were mere man the Jews did right, according to their law, in putting Him to death. In Leviticus 24:16, we read: “And he that blasphemeth the name of the Lord, he shall surely be put to death, and all the congregation shall certainly stone him: as well the stranger, as he that is born in the land, when he blasphemeth the name of the Lord, shall be put to death.”

This law obliged them to put to death everyone who blasphemed. It was making the statement that He was divine that cost Him His life; and by the Mosaic law He ought to have suffered the death penalty. In John 16:15, Christ says, “All things that the Father hath are Mine: therefore said I, that He shall take of Mine, and shall show it unto you.” How could He be merely a good man and use language as that?

No doubt has ever entered my mind on the point since I was converted.

A notorious sinner was once asked how he could prove the divinity of Christ. His answer was, “Why, He has saved me; and that is a pretty good proof, is it not?”

===Chinese===
倘若耶稣基督不过是人，那么犹太人按他们的律法把祂处死，反而是对的。利未记 24:16 记着：「那亵渎耶和华名的，必被治死，全会众总要用石头打死他。不管是寄居的是本地人，他亵渎耶和华名的时候，必被治死。」

这条律法吩咐他们把一切亵渎的人治死。正是因祂说自己有神性，祂才丧了命；按摩西律法，祂该受死刑。约翰福音 16:15，基督说：「凡父所有的，都是我的；所以我说，他要将受于我的，告诉你们。」祂怎能只是个好人，却说这样的话呢？

自从我得救归主以来，这一点上从没有半点疑惑进入我心。

曾有一个声名狼藉的罪人被问到，他怎能证明基督的神性。他回答说：「哎，祂救了我；这岂不是相当好的明证吗？」

===Original===
An infidel on one occasion said to me, “I have been studying the life of John the Baptist, Mr. Moody. Why don’t you preach him? He was a greater character than Christ. You would do a greater work.” I said to him, “My friend, you preach John the Baptist; and I will follow you and preach Christ: and we will see who will do the most good.” “You will do the most good,” he said, “because the people are so superstitious.” Ah! John was beheaded; and his disciples begged his body and buried it: but Christ has risen from the dead; He has “ascended on high; He has led captivity captive; and received gifts for men.” (Psalm 68:18).

Our Christ **lives**. Many people have not found out that Christ has risen from the grave. They worship a dead Saviour, like Mary, who said, “They have taken away my Lord; and I know not where they have laid Him.” (John 20:13). That is the trouble with those who doubt the divinity of our Lord.

===Chinese===
曾有一位不信者对我说：「慕迪先生，我一直在研究施洗约翰的生平。你为何不讲他呢？他是个比基督更伟大的人物。你讲他会作成更大的事。」我对他说：「朋友，你去讲施洗约翰；我跟着你去传基督：我们看看谁作的善工更大。」他说：「你会作成更大的善工，因为百姓是这般迷信。」啊！约翰被斩首；他的门徒求取他的遗体埋葬了：可基督已经从死里复活；祂已经「升上高天的时候，掳掠了仇敌，将各样的恩赐赏给人」（诗篇 68:18）。

我们的基督**活着**。许多人尚未发现基督已经从坟墓里复活。他们敬拜的是一位死了的救主，像马利亚那样说：「他们把我主挪了去，我不知道放在哪里」（约翰福音 20:13）。这便是那些怀疑我们主神性之人难处的所在。

===Original===
Then look at Matthew 18:20. “Where two or three are gathered together in My name, there am I in the midst of them.” “**There am** I.” Well now, if He is a mere man, how can He be there? All these are strong passages.

Again in Matthew 28:18. “And Jesus came and spake unto them, saying, All power is given unto Me in heaven and in earth.” Could He be a mere man and talk in that way? “All power is given unto Me in heaven and in earth!”

Then again in Matthew 28:20. “Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you; and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world.” If He were mere man, how could He be with us? Yet He says, “I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world!”

===Chinese===
再看马太福音 18:20。「因为无论在哪里，有两三个人奉我的名聚会，那里就有我在他们中间。」「那里就有我。」那么，祂若不过是人，怎能同时在那里呢？这些都是有力的经文。

马太福音 28:18 又说：「耶稣进前来，对他们说：天上地下所有的权柄都赐给我了。」祂能只是个人，却这样说吗？「天上地下所有的权柄都赐给我了！」

马太福音 28:20 又说：「凡我所吩咐你们的，都教训他们遵守，我就常与你们同在，直到世界的末了。」祂若不过是人，怎能与我们同在？然而祂说：「我就常与你们同在，直到世界的末了！」

===Original===
Then again in Mark 2:7. “Why doth this Man thus speak blasphemies? who can forgive sins but God only? And immediately when Jesus perceived in His Spirit that they reasoned within themselves, He said unto them, Why reason ye these things in your hearts? Whether is it easier to say to the sick of the palsy, Thy sins be forgiven thee, or to say, Arise and take up thy bed and walk?”

Some men will meet you and say, “Did not Elisha also raise the dead?” Notice that in the rare instances in which men have raised the dead, they did it by the power of God. They called on God to do it. But when Christ was on earth He did not call upon the Father to bring the dead to life. When He went to the house of Jairus He said, “Damsel, I say unto thee, Arise.” (Mark 5:41).

===Chinese===
马可福音 2:7 又记着：「这个人为什么这样说呢？他说僭妄的话了。除了神以外，谁能赦罪呢？耶稣心中知道他们心里这样议论，就说：你们心里为什么这样议论呢？或对瘫子说你的罪赦了，或说起来！拿你的褥子行走，哪一样容易呢？」

有人会遇见你说：「以利沙不也叫死人复活吗？」请留意，人叫死人复活的那些罕见事例中，他们都是靠着神的大能去作的。他们求神去作。可基督在地上时，并没有求父叫死人活过来。祂到了睚鲁的家，便说：「闺女，我吩咐你起来」（马可福音 5:41）。

===Original===
He had power to impart life. When they were carrying the young man out of Nain He had compassion on the widowed mother and came and touched the bier and said, “Young man, I say unto thee, Arise.” (Luke 7:14).

He spake; and the dead arose.

And when He raised Lazarus He called with a loud voice, “Lazarus, come forth!” (John 11:43). And Lazarus heard, and came forth.

Someone has said, It was a good thing that Lazarus was mentioned by name, or all the dead within the sound of Christ’s voice would immediately have risen.

祂有赐人生命的大能。他们把那少年人抬出拿因城时，祂怜悯那寡居的母亲，上前按住杠，说：「少年人，我吩咐你，起来」（路加福音 7:14）。

祂一发声，死人便起来了。

祂叫拉撒路复活时，大声呼喊说：「拉撒路出来」（约翰福音 11:43）。拉撒路听见了，便出来了。

曾有人说，幸亏祂点出了拉撒路的名字，否则凡基督声音所及之处的一切死人，都要立刻复活了。

In John 5:25, Jesus says: “Verily, verily, I say unto you, The hour is coming, and now is, when the dead shall hear the voice of the Son of God; and they that hear shall live.” What blasphemy would this have been, had He not been divine! The proof is overwhelming, if you will but examine the Word of God.

And then another thing﻿—no good man except Jesus Christ has ever allowed anybody to worship him. When this was done He never rebuked the worshiper. In John 9:38, we read that when the blind man was found by Christ he said, “Lord, I believe. And he worshiped Him.” The Lord did not rebuke him.

===Chinese===
约翰福音 5:25，耶稣说：「我实实在在地告诉你们，时候将到，如今就是了，死人要听见神儿子的声音，听见的人就要活了。」祂若不是神，这话便是何等的亵渎！你只要查考神的话语，证据便铺天盖地、无可推诿。

还有一件事——除了耶稣基督，从来没有一个好人容许任何人敬拜自己。人这样作时，祂从未责备那敬拜者。约翰福音 9:38 记着，那瞎子被基督找着时，便说：「主啊，我信。就拜耶稣。」主并未责备他。

===Original===
Then again, Revelation 22:6, runs thus: “And he said unto me, These things are faithful and true; and the Lord God of the holy prophets sent His angel to show unto His servants the things which must shortly be done. Behold, I come quickly: blessed is he that keepeth the sayings of the prophecy of this book. And I John saw these things and heard them. We see here that even that angel would not allow John to worship him. Even an angel from heaven! And if Gabriel came down here from the presence of God it would be a sin to worship him, or any seraph, or any cherub, or Michael, or any archangel.

我们在此看见，连那位天使也不容约翰敬拜他。那还是从天上来的天使！加百列若从神面前降到这里，人敬拜他也是罪；无论哪个撒拉弗，哪个基路伯，米迦勒，或哪位天使长，都是一样。

===Original===
“**Worship God**!” And if Jesus Christ were not God manifest in the flesh we are guilty of idolatry in worshiping Him. In Matthew 14:33, we read: “Then they that were in the ship came and *worshiped* Him, saying, Of a truth Thou art the Son of God.” He did not rebuke them.

And in Matthew 8:2, we also read: “And, behold, there came a leper and *worshiped* Him, saying, Lord, if Thou wilt, Thou canst make me clean.”

In Matthew 15:25: “Then came she, and *worshiped* Him, saying, Lord, help me!”

There are many other passages; but I give these as sufficient in my opinion to prove beyond any doubt the Divinity of our Lord.

类似的经文还有许多；但我所引的这些，在我看来已足以毫无疑问地证明我们主的神性。

使徒行传第 14 章告诉我们，路司得的异教徒拿着花圈，要向保罗和巴拿巴献祭，因为他们治好了一个瘫子；可这两位传道人撕开衣服，告诉这些路司得人，他们不过是人，不配受人敬拜；仿佛那是大罪一般。倘若耶稣基督只是个人，我们敬拜祂便都犯了大罪。

可祂若正如我们所信，是神独生的、蒙爱的儿子，那么让我们顺服祂对我们所宣告的权；让我们安息于祂那全然赎罪之功上，尽我们一生的日子出去事奉祂。
