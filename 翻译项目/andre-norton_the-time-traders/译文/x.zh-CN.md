## X

===Original===
Having been cuffed and battered into submission more quickly than would have been possible three weeks earlier, Murdock now stood sullenly surveying the man who, though he dressed like a Beaker trader, persisted in using a language Ross did not know.

===Chinese===
默多克（Murdock）挨了一顿耳光和拳脚，比三周前更容易地就被打服了，此刻正阴沉沉地打量着眼前这个人——虽然穿着比克尔商人（Beaker trader）的衣服，嘴里说的却是罗斯（Ross）听不懂的语言。

===Original===
"We do not play as children here." At last the man spoke words Ross could understand. "You will answer me or else others shall ask the questions, and less gently. I say to you now—who are you and from where do you come?"

===Chinese===
"我们这里不跟小孩子玩游戏。"那人终于说出了罗斯能听懂的话。"你要么回答我，要么换别人来问，那可就不客气了。我现在问你——你是什么人，从哪里来？"

===Original===
For a moment Ross glowered across the table at him, his inbred antagonism to authority aroused by that contemptuous demand, but then common sense cautioned. His initial introduction to this village had left him bruised and with one of his headaches. There was no reason to let them beat him until he was in no shape to make a break for freedom when and if there was an opportunity.

===Chinese===
罗斯隔着桌子怒视了对方片刻，骨子里对权威的抵触被那番轻蔑的逼问激发出来。但理智随即发出了警告。他初到这个村子时挨了打，脑袋也犯了病。没有理由让他们一直打下去，打到他连趁机逃跑的力气都没有。

===Original===
"I am Rossa of the traders," he returned, eying the man with a carefully measured stare. "I came into this land in search of my kinsmen who were taken by raiders in the night."

===Chinese===
"我是商人罗萨（Rossa），"他回敬道，用一种精心拿捏的目光盯着对方。"我来到这片土地，是为了寻找夜里被劫匪抓走的亲人。"

===Original===
The man, who sat on a stool by the table, smiled slowly. Again he spoke in the strange tongue, and Ross merely stared stolidly back. His words were short and explosive sounding, and the man's smile faded; his annoyance grew as he continued to speak.

===Chinese===
那人坐在桌旁的凳子上，缓缓露出微笑。他又用那种陌生的语言说了起来，罗斯只是面无表情地回望着他。那人的话语短促而猛烈，微笑渐渐消退；随着他继续说下去，恼怒越来越明显。

===Original===
One of Ross's two guards ventured to interrupt, using the Beaker language. "From where did you come?" He was a quiet-faced, slender man, not like his companion, who had roped Murdock from behind and was of the bully breed, able to subdue Ross's wildcat resistance in a very short struggle.

===Chinese===
罗斯的两个看守中有一个壮着胆子用比克尔语插了话。"你从哪里来？"他面容沉静，身材清瘦，不像他的同伴——那个从背后用绳索套住默多克的家伙，天生一副恶霸体格，在很短的搏斗中就把罗斯野猫般的反抗压制住了。

===Original===
"I came to this land from the south," Ross answered, "after the manner of my people. This is a new land with furs and the golden tears of the sun to be gathered and bartered. The traders move in peace, and their hands are raised against no man. Yet in the darkness there came those who would slay without profit, for what reason I have no knowing."

===Chinese===
"我从南方来到这片土地，"罗斯答道，"遵循我们民族的方式。这里有毛皮和太阳的金色泪珠——琥珀——可以采集和交易。商人们以和平方式行商，不对任何人举起武器。然而黑暗中来了那些无端杀戮之人，我不知他们意欲何为。"

===Original===
The quiet man continued the questioning and Ross answered fully with details of the past of one Rossa, a Beaker merchant. Yes, he was from the south. His father was Gurdi, who had a trading post in the warm lands along the big river. This was Rossa's first trip to open new territory. He had come with his father's blood brother, Assha, who was a noted far voyager, and it was an honor to be chosen as donkey-leader for such a one as Assha. With Assha had been Macna, one who was also a far trader, though not as noted as Assha.

===Chinese===
沉静的男人继续盘问，罗斯事无巨细地讲述了罗萨——一个比克尔商人——的身世。是的，他来自南方。他的父亲叫古尔迪（Gurdi），在大河沿岸的温暖地带有一个贸易站。这是罗萨第一次出来开辟新领地。他是跟父亲的结拜兄弟阿莎（Assha）一起来的，阿莎是一位著名的远行航海家，能被选作阿莎的驴队领队是一种荣幸。和阿莎同行的还有麦克纳（Macna），也是一位远行商人，虽然名气不如阿莎。

===Original===
Of a certainty, Assha was of his own race! Ross blinked at that question. One need only to look upon him to know that he was of trader blood and no uncivilized woodsrunner. How long had he known Assha? Ross shrugged. Assha had come to his father's post the winter before and had stayed with them through the cold season. Gurdi and Assha had mingled blood after he pulled Gurdi free from the river in flood. Assha had lost his boat and trade goods in that rescue, so Gurdi had made good his loss this year. Detail by detail he gave the story. In spite of the fact that he provided these details glibly, sure that they were true, Ross continued to be haunted by an odd feeling that he was indeed reciting a tale of adventure which had happened long ago and to someone else. Perhaps that pain in his head made him think of these events as very colorless and far away.

===Chinese===
阿莎当然是自己人！罗斯听到这个问题眨了眨眼。只要看他一眼就知道他是商人血统，不是什么未开化的林中蛮人。他认识阿莎多久了？罗斯耸耸肩。阿莎去年冬天来到他父亲的贸易站，在那里度过了整个寒冬。古尔迪和阿莎在洪水暴发时结下了生死之交——阿莎把古尔迪从暴涨的河水中拉了出来，自己的船和货物都在那次救援中损失了，所以古尔迪今年赔偿了他的损失。他一五一十地讲述着。尽管这些细节他说得滔滔不绝，深信都是真的，罗斯却始终被一种奇怪的感觉缠绕着——他确实是在复述一段很久以前发生在别人身上的冒险故事。也许是头上的疼痛让他觉得这些事件显得苍白而遥远。

===Original===
"It would seem"—the quiet man turned to the one behind the table—"that this is indeed one Rossa, a Beaker trader."

===Chinese===
"看来，"沉静的男人转向桌子后面的那人说，"此人确实是罗萨，一个比克尔商人。"

===Original===
But the man looked impatient, angry. He made a sign to the other guard, who turned Ross around roughly and sent him toward the door with a push. Once again the leader gave an order in his own language, adding a few words more with a stinging snap that might have been a threat or a warning.

===Chinese===
但那人一脸不耐烦，满面怒容。他向另一个看守打了个手势，那人粗暴地把罗斯转了个身，一把推向门口。头领又用自己的语言下了道命令，末尾加了几个字，带着刺耳的锐利语气，像是威胁或警告。

===Original===
Ross was thrust into a small room with a hard floor and not even a skin rug to serve as a bed. Since the quiet man had ordered the removal of the ropes from Ross's arms, he leaned against the wall, rubbing the pain of returning circulation away from his wrists and trying to understand what had happened to him and where he was. Having spied upon it from the heights, he knew it wasn't an ordinary trading station, and he wanted to know what they did here. Also, somewhere in this village he hoped to find Assha and Macna.

===Chinese===
罗斯被推进一间小屋，地面硬邦邦的，连一张兽皮毯子都没有当床用。沉静的男人下令解掉了他胳膊上的绳索，他靠在墙上，揉着麻木的手腕以缓解血液回流的疼痛，同时试图弄清楚自己遭遇了什么、身在何处。他从高处侦察过这里，知道这不是一个普通的贸易站，他想知道他们在这里做什么。而且，他希望在这座村庄的某个角落能找到阿莎和麦克纳。

===Original===
At the end of the day his captors opened the door only long enough to push inside a bowl and a small jug. He felt for those in the dusk, dipping his fingers into a lukewarm mush of meal and drinking the water from the jug avidly. His headache dulled, and from experience Ross knew that this bout was almost over. If he slept, he would waken with a clearer mind and no pain. Knowing he was very tired, he took the precaution of curling up directly in front of the door so that no one could enter without arousing him.

===Chinese===
这一天结束时，看守只开了一下门，塞进一只碗和一个小水壶。他在暮色中摸索到那些东西，把手指伸进温热的面糊里，贪婪地喝着壶里的水。头痛缓和了些，凭经验罗斯知道这一轮快过去了。睡一觉的话，醒来时头脑会更清醒，疼痛也会消失。知道自已非常疲惫，他特意蜷缩在门前——这样谁要进来都得先惊动他。

===Original===
It was still dark when he awoke with a curious urgency remaining from a dream he could not remember. Ross sat up, flexing his arms and shoulders to combat the stiffness which had come with his cramped sleep. He could not rid himself of a feeling that there was something to be done and that time was his enemy.

===Chinese===
他醒来时天还黑着，一场记不清的梦留下了一股莫名的紧迫感。罗斯坐起来，活动手臂和肩膀以对抗蜷缩入睡带来的僵硬感。他怎么也摆脱不了一种感觉——有些事必须去做，而时间是他最大的敌人。

===Original===
Assha! Gratefully he seized on that. He must find Assha and Macna, for the three of them could surely discover a way to get out of this village. That was what was so important!

===Chinese===
阿莎！他感激地抓住了这个念头。他必须找到阿莎和麦克纳，三个人一定能想出办法逃出这个村子。这才是最重要的事！

===Original===
He had been handled none too gently, and they were holding him a prisoner. But Ross believed that this was not the worst which could happen to him here, and he must be free before the worst did come. The question was, How could he escape? His bow and dagger were gone, and he did not even have his long cloak pin for a weapon, since he had given that to Frigga.

===Chinese===
他被对待得一点也不客气，他们把他关了起来。但罗斯相信这还不是最坏的情况，在最坏的事发生之前他必须获得自由。问题是——他怎么逃跑？他的弓和匕首都不在了，连那根长斗篷别针也没有当武器，因为他把它送给了弗里嘉（Frigga）。

===Original===
Running his hands over his body, Ross inventoried what remained of his clothing and possessions. He unfastened the bronze chain-belt still buckled in his kilt tunic, swinging the length speculatively in one hand. A masterpiece of craftsmanship, it consisted of patterned plates linked together with a series of five finely wrought chains and a front buckle in the form of a lion's head, its protruding tongue serving as a hook to support a dagger sheath. Its weight promised a weapon of sorts, which when added to the element of surprise might free him.

===Chinese===
罗斯用手在身上摸索，清点还剩什么衣物和随身物品。他解开了仍扣在短裙束腰上的青铜链腰带，若有所思地拎在手里掂了掂分量。这是一件工艺杰作——由雕花的铜板通过五条精工细作的链子串联而成，前方的扣头是一个狮头造型，突出的舌头充当挂匕首鞘的钩子。它的分量足以当一种武器，再加上出其不意的因素，或许能帮他脱身。

===Original===
By rights they would be expecting him to produce some opposition, however. It was well known that only the best fighters, the shrewdest minds, followed the traders' roads. It was a proud thing to be a trader in the wilderness, a thought that warmed Ross now as he waited in the dark for what luck and Ba-Bal of the Bright Horns would send. Were he ever to return to Gurdi's post, Ba-Bal, whose boat rode across the sky from dawn to dusk, would have a fine ox, jars of the first brewing, and sweet-smelling amber laid upon his altar.

===Chinese===
不过按常理，他们应该料到他会反抗。众所周知，只有最出色的战士和最精明的头脑才会走商人的路。在荒野中做一名商人是值得骄傲的事，这个念头在此刻暗中等待运气和光角巴巴尔（Ba-Bal of the Bright Horns）降下什么安排时，温暖着罗斯的心。如果他能回到古尔迪的贸易站，巴巴尔——他那从黎明到黄昏横渡天空的神舟的主人——将得到一头上好的公牛、几罐新酿的好酒和芳香的琥珀作为供品。

===Original===
Ross had patience which he had learned from the mixed heritage of his two pasts, the real and the false graft. He could wait as he had waited many times before—quiet, and with outward ease—for the right moment to come. It came now with footsteps ringing sharply, halting before his cell door.

===Chinese===
罗斯有耐心，这耐心来自他双重过去的混合遗产——真实的与嫁接的。他能像以前多次做过的那样等待——安静地、外表从容地——等待正确的时机到来。此刻时机来了，伴随着清脆的脚步声，在牢房门前停住。

===Original===
With the noiseless speed of a hunting cat, Ross flung himself from behind the door to a wall, where he would be hidden from the newcomer for that necessary instant or two. If his attack was to be successful, it must occur inside the room. He heard the sound of a bar being slid out of its brackets, and he poised himself, the belt rippling from his right hand.

===Chinese===
罗斯像猎猫一样无声而敏捷地从门后闪到墙边，在新来者进门后那一两秒必要的瞬间里藏好身形。如果突袭要成功，就必须在房间里完成。他听到门闩被抽出卡槽的声音，全身蓄势待发，右手握着的腰带已经甩动起来。

===Original===
The door was opening inward, and a man stood silhouetted against the outer light. He muttered, looking toward the corner where Ross had thrown his single garment in a roll which might just resemble, for the needed second or two, a man curled in slumber. The man in the doorway took the bait, coming forward far enough for Ross to send the door slamming shut as he himself sprang with the belt aimed for the other's head.

===Chinese===
门朝里打开了，一个男人的身形映在门外的光线中。他嘟囔着，朝角落看去——罗斯把唯一的衣物卷成一团扔在那里，在需要的那一两秒钟里看起来就像一个蜷缩沉睡的人。门口那人上了当，向前走了足够远，罗斯猛地把门甩上，同时挥起腰带朝对方的脑袋砸去。

===Original===
There was a startled cry, cut off in the middle as the belt plates met flesh and bone in a crushing force. Luck was with him! Ross caught up his kilt and belted it around him after he had made a hurried examination of the body now lying at his feet. He was not sure that the man was dead, but at any rate he was completely unconscious. Ross stripped off the man's cloak, located his dagger, freed it from the belt hook, and snapped it on his own.

===Chinese===
一声惊叫响起，中途戛然而止——腰带上的铜片以碾压般的力量砸中了皮肉和骨头。运气站在我这边！罗斯匆匆检查了一下倒在地上的身体，不确定那人是否已死，但至少完全失去了知觉。他捡起短裙系好，剥下那人的斗篷，找到他的匕首，从腰带钩上取下来，别到了自己身上。

===Original===
Then inch by inch Ross edged open the door, peering through the crack. As far as he could see, the hall was empty, so he jerked the portal open, and dagger in hand, sprang out, ready for attack. He closed the door, slipping the bar back into its brackets. If the man inside revived and pounded for attention, his own friends might think it was Ross and delay investigating.

===Chinese===
然后罗斯一寸一寸地推开房门，从门缝向外窥探。目力所及，走廊空无一人，他猛地拉开门，手持匕首跳了出来，准备迎战。他关上门，把门闩插回卡槽。如果里面那人醒过来敲门求救，他的同伴可能以为是罗斯在闹，从而拖延来查看的时间。

===Original===
But the escape from the cell was the easiest part of what he planned to do, as Ross well knew. To find Assha and Macna in this maze of rooms occupied by the enemy was far more difficult. Although he had no idea in which of the village buildings they might be confined, this one was the largest and seemed to be the headquarters of the chief men, which meant it could also serve as their prison.

===Chinese===
但逃出牢房不过是他计划中最容易的一步，罗斯心里清楚。在这座敌占区房间迷宫里找到阿莎和麦克纳要困难得多。他虽然不知道他们被关在村中哪栋建筑里，但这栋是最大的，看起来像是头领们的总部，也就意味着它同样可以充当监狱。

===Original===
Light came from a torch in a bracket halfway down the hall. The wood burned smokily, giving off a resinous odor, and to Ross the glow was sufficient illumination. He slipped along as close to the wall as he could, ready to freeze at the slightest sound. But this portion of the building might well have been deserted, for he saw or heard no one. He tried the only two doors opening out of the hall, but they were secured on the other side. Then he came to a bend in the corridor, and stopped short, hearing a murmur of low voices.

===Chinese===
走廊中段的一只支架上插着一支火把，提供着光源。木头燃烧时烟雾缭绕，散发出松脂的气味，但对罗斯来说这光亮已足够照明。他紧贴墙壁潜行，一有响动就准备立刻冻住不动。但这部分建筑似乎已经被弃用了，他没有看到也没有听到任何人。他试了试走廊上仅有的两扇门，但都从里面锁死了。接着他来到走廊的一个拐角，猛地停住了脚步——他听到了压低的说话声。

===Original===
If he had used a hunter's tricks of silent tread and vigilant wariness before, Ross was doubly on guard now as he wriggled to a point from which he could see beyond that turn. Mere luck prevented him from giving himself away a moment later.

===Chinese===
如果说之前他用过猎人的无声脚步和警觉的谨慎，那么现在罗斯加倍警戒，匍匐到能看到拐弯处另一侧的位置。纯粹是运气才让他没有在片刻之后暴露自己。

===Original===
Assha! Assha, alive, well, apparently under no restraint, was just turning away from the same quiet man who had had a part in Ross's interrogation. That was surely Assha's brown hair, his slender wiry body draped with a Beaker's kilt. A familiar tilt of the head convinced Ross, though he could not see the man's face. The quiet man went down the hall, leaving Assha before a door. As he passed through it Ross sped forward and followed him inside.

===Chinese===
阿莎！阿莎，活着，安好，显然没有被捆绑，正从那个参与过罗斯审讯的沉静男人身边转身离开。那棕色的头发，那精瘦有力的身躯裹着比克尔人的短裙——毫无疑问就是阿莎。一个熟悉的偏头角度让罗斯确信了，尽管他看不到那人的脸。沉静的男人沿着走廊走远了，把阿莎留在一扇门前。阿莎推门进去的瞬间，罗斯飞速冲上前跟了进去。

===Original===
Assha had crossed the bare room and was standing on a glowing plate in the floor. Ross, aroused to desperate action by some fear he did not understand, leaped after him. His left hand fell upon Assha's shoulder, turning the man half around as Ross, too, stepped upon the patch of luminescence.

===Chinese===
阿莎穿过空荡荡的房间，站在了地板上一块发光的板面上。罗斯被某种自己也说不清的恐惧驱使着，拼死扑了过去。他的左手抓住阿莎的肩膀，将人扳转了半圈，同时自己也踏上了那片发出荧光的地方。

===Original===
Murdock had only an instant to realize that he was staring into the face of an astonished stranger. His hand flashed up in an edgewise blow which caught the other on the side of the throat, and then the world came apart about them. There was a churning, whirling sickness which griped and bent Ross almost double across the crumpled body of his victim. He held his head lest it be torn from his shoulders by the spinning thing which seemed based behind his eyes.

===Chinese===
默多克只来得及一瞬意识到自己正瞪着一张全然陌生的、同样惊骇的面孔。他的手闪电般挥出一记横劈，击中对方的脖颈侧面，接着整个世界在他们周围碎裂了。一阵翻搅旋转的眩晕袭来，恶心感揪住了罗斯，他弯着腰几乎折成两段，扑倒在那人瘫软的身体上。他抱住脑袋，生怕被那个似乎扎根在他眼窝后面的旋转物从肩膀上扯下来。

===Original===
The sickness endured only for a moment, and some buried part of Ross's mind accepted it as a phenomenon he had experienced before. He came out of it gasping, to focus his attention once more on the man at his feet.

===Chinese===
眩晕只持续了一瞬，罗斯意识深处某个被掩埋的部分将此判定为他此前经历过的现象。他大口喘着气回过神来，注意力重新聚焦到脚下那个人身上。

===Original===
The stranger was still breathing. Ross stooped to drag him from the plate and began binding and gagging him with lengths torn from his kilt. Only when his captive was secure did he begin looking around him curiously.

===Chinese===
那个陌生人还在呼吸。罗斯弯腰把他从板面上拖下来，从自己的短裙上撕下布条，将他捆绑堵嘴。直到俘虏被牢牢制服，他才开始好奇地打量四周。

===Original===
The room was bare of any furnishings and now, as he glanced at the floor, Ross saw that the plate had lost its glow. The Beaker trader Rossa rubbed sweating palms on his kilt and thought fleetingly of forest ghosts and other mysteries. Not that the traders bowed to those ghosts which were the plague of lesser men and tribes, but anything which suddenly appeared and then disappeared without any logical explanation, needed thinking on. Murdock pulled the prisoner, who was now reviving, to the far end of the room and then went back to the plate with the persistence of a man who refused to treat with ghosts and wanted something concrete to explain the unexplainable. ===Chinese===
