## The Gray Wolf’s Ha’nt / 灰狼的鬼魂

===Original===
It was a rainy day at the vineyard. The morning had dawned bright and clear. But the sky had soon clouded, and by nine o’clock there was a light shower, followed by others at brief intervals. By noon the rain had settled into a dull, steady downpour. The clouds hung low, and seemed to grow denser instead of lighter as they discharged their watery burden, and there was now and then a muttering of distant thunder. Outdoor work was suspended, and I spent most of the day at the house, looking over my accounts and bringing up some arrears of correspondence.

===Chinese===
葡萄园里这一天有雨。清晨破晓时分明明晴朗清爽，可天色很快便阴了上来，到九点钟落了一阵小雨，隔不多久又是一阵。到晌午，雨已经下成了一场沉闷的、连绵不绝的倾泻。乌云低低压着，卸下满腹的水，非但不显得轻了，反而愈发浓重，远处还时不时滚过一阵低沉的闷雷。户外的活计全停了，这一天我大半待在屋里，翻检账目，又补写了几封拖欠已久的信。

===Original===
Towards four o’clock I went out on the piazza, which was broad and dry, and less gloomy than the interior of the house, and composed myself for a quiet smoke. I had lit my cigar and opened the volume I was reading at that time, when my wife, whom I had left dozing on a lounge, came out and took a rocking-chair near me.

===Chinese===
将近四点钟，我踱到游廊上。游廊宽敞干爽，不似屋子里面那么阴沉；我安顿下来，准备安安静静抽口烟。刚点上雪茄、翻开我近日在读的那部书，我妻子——方才还把她留在躺椅上打盹——走了出来，在我近旁一张摇椅里坐下。

===Original===
“I wish you would talk to me, or read to me—or something,” she exclaimed petulantly. “It’s awfully dull here today.”

“I’ll read to you with pleasure,” I replied, and began at the point where I had found my bookmark:—

===Chinese===
「我倒巴不得你跟我说说话，或者念点什么给我听——随便什么都行，」她不耐烦地嚷道，「今天这儿可闷死人了。」

「我乐意念给你听，」我答道，便从夹书签的地方念起——

===Original===
“ ‘The difficulty of dealing with transformations so many-sided as those which all existences have undergone, or are undergoing, is such as to make a complete and deductive interpretation almost hopeless. So to grasp the total process of redistribution of matter and motion as to see simultaneously its several necessary results in their actual interdependence is scarcely possible. There is, however, a mode of rendering the process as a whole tolerably comprehensible. Though the genesis of the rearrangement of every evolving aggregate is in itself one, it presents to our intelligence’ ”—

===Chinese===
「『要应对一切存在物所已经历、或正在经历的这许多面的转化，困难之大，几乎使一种完整而演绎的解释成为无望。要把握物质与运动再分配的全过程，从而同时看清其若干必然结果在实际相互依存中的面貌，几乎是不可能的。不过，倒有一种办法，可以把这一过程作为整体弄得大体可以理解。虽然每一个演化中的聚合体，其重排的起源本身是同一的，它呈现于我们智能面前的却是——』」

===Original===
“John,” interrupted my wife, “I wish you would stop reading that nonsense and see who that is coming up the lane.”

I closed my book with a sigh. I had never been able to interest my wife in the study of philosophy, even when presented in the simplest and most lucid form.

===Chinese===
「约翰，」我妻子打断道，「别念那劳什子了，去看看巷子那头来的又是谁。」

我叹了口气，合上书。哪怕摆成最浅显明白的样子，我也从来没能让我妻子对哲学研究生出半点兴致。

===Original===
Someone was coming up the lane; at least, a huge faded cotton umbrella was making progress toward the house, and beneath it a pair of nether extremities in trousers was discernible. Any doubt in my mind as to whose they were was soon resolved when Julius reached the steps and, putting the umbrella down, got a good dash of the rain as he stepped up on the porch.

===Chinese===
巷子里确实有人过来；至少，一把褪了色的大棉布伞正朝屋子这边挪，伞底下隐约辨得出一双装在裤管里的下肢。那双腿是谁的，我本还有几分疑惑；及至朱利叶斯走到台阶跟前，把伞往地上一放，跨步上廊时结结实实兜了一头雨，这疑惑顿时烟消云散。

===Original===
“Why in the world, Julius,” I asked, “didn’t you keep the umbrella up until you got under cover?”

“It’s bad luck, suh, ter raise a’ umbrella in de house, en w’iles I dunno whuther it’s bad luck ter kyar one inter de piazzer er no, I ’lows it’s alluz bes’ ter be on de safe side. I didn’ s’pose you en young missis ’u’d be gwine on yo’ dribe ter-day, but bein’ ez it’s my pa’t ter take you ef you does, I ’lowed I’d repo’t fer dooty, en let you say whuther er no you wants ter go.”

===Chinese===
「你究竟是怎么想的，朱利叶斯，」我问，「怎么不把伞撑到廊子底下再收？」

「在屋里撑伞不吉利，先生。把伞拿上游廊晦气不晦气，俺说不上来；可俺想啊，凡事总以稳当为上。俺原估摸着，今儿个这样的天，您和少太太不会驾车出门了；不过您要真出门，伺候的差事终究落在俺头上，俺想着还是来报个到，听您示下，说去还是不去。」

===Original===
“I’m glad you came, Julius,” I responded. “We don’t want to go driving, of course, in the rain, but I should like to consult you about another matter. I’m thinking of taking in a piece of new ground. What do you imagine it would cost to have that neck of woods down by the swamp cleared up?”

===Chinese===
「你来了正好，朱利叶斯，」我应道，「雨天地里当然出不成门，不过我另有一件事想同你商量。我打算开一片新地。依你看，把沼泽边那片林子清理出来，得费多少钱？」

===Original===
The old man’s countenance assumed an expression of unwonted seriousness, and he shook his head doubtfully.

===Chinese===
老人脸上浮起一层少有的郑重，迟疑地摇了摇头。

===Original===
“I dunno ’bout dat, suh. It mought cos’ mo’, en it mought cos’ less, ez fuh ez money is consarned. I ain’ denyin’ you could cl’ar up dat trac’ er lan’ fer a hund’ed er a couple er hund’ed dollahs—ef you wants ter cl’ar it up. But ef dat ’uz my trac’ er lan’, I wouldn’ ’sturb it, no, suh, I wouldn’; sho ’s you bawn, I wouldn’.”

===Chinese===
「这个俺说不好，先生。单论钱，兴许要多，兴许要少。俺不否认，那块地您花上一百两百块美金，是清理得出来的——只要您当真想清理。可那块地要是俺的，俺说什么也不去动它；不动，先生，说什么也不；老天爷在上，决不。」

===Original===
“But why not?” I asked.

“It ain’ fittin’ fer grapes, fer noo groun’ nebber is.”

===Chinese===
「可这是为什么？」我问。

「那地种不得葡萄；生荒地从来种不得。」

===Original===
“I know it, but”—

“It ain’ no yeathly good fer cotton, ’ca’se it’s too low.”

===Chinese===
「这我知道，可是」——

「种棉花也压根儿不成，那地地势太洼。」

===Original===
“Perhaps so; but it will raise splendid corn.”

“I dunno,” rejoined Julius deprecatorily. “It’s so nigh de swamp dat de ’coons’ll eat up all de cawn.”

===Chinese===
「兴许如此；不过种玉米，那地可是再好不过。」

「难说，」朱利叶斯不以为然地应道，「那地紧挨着沼泽，浣熊要把玉米吃个精光。」

===Original===
“I think I’ll risk it,” I answered.

“Well, suh,” said Julius, “I wushes you much joy er yo’ job. Ef you has bad luck er sickness er trouble er any kin’, doan blame *me*. You can’t say ole Julius didn’ wa’n you.”

===Chinese===
「我想，这点险我还是冒得起的，」我答道。

「那好啊，先生，」朱利叶斯说，「这桩活计，俺先祝您多多受用。往后您要是走了背运、害了病、遭了灾，不管哪一样，可别怪*俺*。到时候您可不能说，老朱利叶斯没提醒过您。」

===Original===
“Warn him of what, Uncle Julius?” asked my wife.

“Er de bad luck w’at follers folks w’at ’sturbs dat trac’ er lan’. Dey is snakes en sco’pions in dem woods. En ef you manages ter ’scape de p’isen animals, you is des boun’ ter hab a ha’nt ter settle wid—ef you doan hab two.”

===Chinese===
「提醒他什么呀，朱利叶斯大叔？」我妻子问。

「提醒他，动了那块地的人，背后跟着的是什么样的背运。那片林子里有蛇，有蝎子。就算您躲开了那些带毒的畜生，也注定躲不开要同一个作祟的鬼魂（ha'nt）算账——弄不好是两个。」

===Original===
“Whose haunt?” my wife demanded, with growing interest.

“De gray wolf’s ha’nt, some folks calls it—but I knows better.”

“Tell us about it, Uncle Julius,” said my wife. “A story will be a godsend today.”

===Chinese===
「谁的鬼魂？」我妻子追问，兴致越来越浓。

「有人管它叫灰狼的鬼魂——可俺心里比他们清楚。」

「给我们讲讲吧，朱利叶斯大叔，」我妻子说，「今天有个故事听，真是老天赏赐。」

===Original===
It was not difficult to induce the old man to tell a story, if he were in a reminiscent mood. Of tales of the old slavery days he seemed indeed to possess an exhaustless store—some weirdly grotesque, some broadly humorous; some bearing the stamp of truth, faint, perhaps, but still discernible; others palpable inventions, whether his own or not we never knew, though his fancy doubtless embellished them. But even the wildest was not without an element of pathos—the tragedy, it might be, of the story itself; the shadow, never absent, of slavery and of ignorance; the sadness, always, of life as seen by the fading light of an old man’s memory.

===Chinese===
只要老人来了追忆往事的兴致，要引他讲个故事并不难。那些旧奴隶年月的故事，他肚子里仿佛真藏着一口取之不竭的井——有的诡谲怪诞，有的粗放逗笑；有的带着真实的印痕，纵然淡薄，却还辨得出来；另一些则一望便知是编造，究竟出自他本人还是别人之口，我们始终无从知晓，虽说十有八九经过了想象的润饰。但就连最荒诞不经的，也不缺一缕悲怆——也许是故事本身的惨烈；是奴隶制与蒙昧投下的、从不缺席的阴影；是一位老人就着记忆中渐暗的余光望出去，人生所固有的那份哀愁。

===Original===
“Way back yander befo’ de wah,” began Julius, “ole Mars Dugal’ McAdoo useter own a nigger name’ Dan. Dan wuz big en strong en hearty en peaceable en good-nachu’d most er de time, but dange’ous ter aggervate. He alluz done his task, en nebber had no trouble wid de w’ite folks, but woe be unter de nigger w’at ’lowed he c’d fool wid Dan, fer he wuz mos’ sho’ ter git a good lammin’. Soon ez eve’ybody foun’ Dan out, dey didn’ many un ’em ’temp’ ter ’sturb ’im. De one dat did would ’a’ wush’ he hadn’, ef he could ’a’ libbed long ernuff ter do any wushin’.

===Chinese===
「老早以前，还没打仗那阵子，」朱利叶斯开了腔，「老杜加尔·麦卡杜（Dugal' McAdoo）老爷手底下有个黑人，名叫丹（Dan）。丹生得高大、结实、壮健；性子平日里和和平平、好脾气，可要把他招恼了，就危险了。他份内的活计从来做得妥妥帖帖，跟白人也从没闹过别扭；可是哪个黑人要是自以为能作弄丹，那他可要遭殃，八成逃不脱一顿好揍。等大伙儿都摸清了丹的底细，就没几个人想去撩他了。真去撩的那一个，只要还能活得够久、来得及懊悔，准会懊悔自己多这一手。」

===Original===
“It all happen’ dis erway. Dey wuz a cunjuh man w’at libbed ober t’ other side er de Lumbe’ton Road. He had be’n de only cunjuh doctor in de naberhood fer lo! dese many yeahs, ’tel ole Aun’ Peggy sot up in de bizness down by de Wim’l’ton Road. Dis cunjuh man had a son w’at libbed wid ’im, en it wuz dis yer son w’at got mix’ up wid Dan—en all ’bout a ’oman.

===Chinese===
「事情就是这么起来的。伦伯顿大道那头，住着一个巫师（conjure man）。多年来，呶，好些个年头了，这一带的巫医一直是他独一份的营生；直到后来佩姬大娘（Aun' Peggy）在威尔明顿大道（Wim'l'ton Road）边上也支起了这门生意。这巫师有个儿子，跟他一块儿过活；偏偏就是这个儿子，跟丹缠到了一处——说到底，全是为着一个女人。」

===Original===
“Dey wuz a gal on de plantation name’ Mahaly. She wuz a monst’us lackly gal—tall en soopl’, wid big eyes, en a small foot, en a lively tongue, en w’en Dan tuk ter gwine wid ’er eve’ybody ’lowed dey wuz well match’, en none er de yuther nigger men on de plantation das’ ter go nigh her, fer dey wuz all feared er Dan.

===Chinese===
「庄园上有个姑娘，名叫马哈利（Mahaly）。她是个顶俊俏的姑娘——高挑个儿，身段柔软，一双大眼睛，一对小脚，口齿又伶俐。丹跟她相好上以后，人人夸这俩人般配；庄园上旁的黑人男人，没一个敢往她跟前凑，因为他们全都怕丹。」

===Original===
“Now, it happen’ dat dis yer cunjuh man’s son wuz gwine ’long de road one day, w’en who sh’d come pas’ but Mahaly. En de minute dis man sot eyes on Mahaly, he ’lowed he wuz gwine ter hab her fer hisse’f. He come up side er her en ’mence’ ter talk ter her; but she didn’ paid no ’tention ter ’im, fer she wuz studyin’ ’bout Dan, en she didn’ lack dis nigger’s looks nohow. So w’en she got ter whar she wuz gwine, dis yer man wa’n’t no fu’ther ’long dan he wuz w’en he sta’ted.

===Chinese===
「偏巧有一天，这巫师的儿子正顺着大路走，迎面过来的不是别人，正是马哈利。这人的眼睛一搭上马哈利，当下就打定主意，要把她弄到手归自己。他凑到她旁边，跟她搭起话来；可她理也不理，因为她心里惦记着丹，再说这黑人的模样，她横竖看不顺眼。等她走到要去的地方，这人还是一步没挪，跟刚开腔时一个样。」

===Original===
“Co’se, atter he had made up his min’ fer ter git Mahaly, he ’mence’ ter ’quire ’roun’, en soon foun’ out all ’bout Dan, en w’at a dange’ous nigger he wuz. But dis man ’lowed his daddy wuz a cunjuh man, en so he’d come out all right in de een’; en he kep’ right on atter Mahaly. Meanw’iles Dan’s marster had said dey could git married ef dey wanter, en so Dan en Mahaly had tuk up wid one ernudder, en wuz libbin’ in a cabin by deyse’ves, en wuz des wrop’ up in one ernudder.

===Chinese===
「当然喽，他既存心要弄到马哈利，便四处打听起来，很快把丹的底细摸了个透，知道那是个惹不得的黑人。可这人一想，自家老子是巫师，末了自有法子保他逢凶化吉；便照旧缠住马哈利不放。这当口，丹的主人发了话，说他们要愿意就许他们成婚；于是丹和马哈利两个正正经经搭伙过起日子，单住一间小屋，成天你缠着我、我缠着你。」

===Original===
“But dis yer cunjuh man’s son didn’ ’pear ter min’ Dan’s takin’ up wid Mahaly, en he kep’ on hangin’ ’roun’ des de same, ’tel fin’lly one day Mahaly sez ter Dan, sez she:—

“ ‘I wush you’d do sump’n ter stop dat free nigger man fum follerin’ me ’roun’. I doan lack him nohow, en I ain’ got no time fer ter was’e wid no man but you.’

===Chinese===
「可这巫师的儿子，丹把马哈利娶到了手，他面上竟像全不在意，照旧在跟前转悠。到后来有一天，马哈利对丹说道——

「『你想法子叫那个自由黑人别再跟着俺转悠了行不行。俺横竖看不上他；俺的工夫，除了你，谁也不配消受。』」

===Original===
“Co’se Dan got mad w’en he heared ’bout dis man pest’rin’ Mahaly, en de nex’ night, w’en he seed dis nigger comin’ ’long de road, he up en ax’ ’im w’at he mean by hangin’ ’roun’ his ’oman. De man didn’ ’spon’ ter suit Dan, en one wo’d led ter ernudder, ’tel bimeby dis cunjuh man’s son pull’ out a knife en sta’ted ter stick it in Dan; but befo’ he could git it drawed good, Dan haul’ off en hit ’im in de head so ha’d dat he nebber got up. Dan ’lowed he’d come to atter a w’ile en go ’long ’bout his bizness, so he went off en lef’ ’im layin’ dere on de groun’.

===Chinese===
「丹听说这人纠缠马哈利，自然火从心起。第二天夜里，他瞧见这黑人顺着大路过来，便迎上去问他：老围着他女人打转，是什么意思。那人的回话不中丹的意，一句赶一句，到后来，这巫师的儿子抽出刀来，就要往丹身上捅；还没等他把刀抽顺当，丹抡圆了胳膊，照他脑袋上一拳砸下去——这一拳，那人再没起来。丹心想，过一会儿他缓过劲儿来，自会爬起来各走各的路，便自顾自去了，撂他直挺挺躺在地上。」

===Chinese===
「第二天一早，那人被发现死了。这事儿惊动了一大片，可丹一个字不吐，旁的黑人又都没瞧见那场打斗，就没了法子断定是谁下的手。再说死的又是个自由黑人，没有哪个白人特别上心，这事也就不了了之；巫师来把儿子领走，驮回去埋了。」

===Chinese===

===Original===

“ ‘Dat cunjuh man is mo’ d’n twice’t ez ole ez I is, en he kin make monst’us powe’ful goopher. You leabe me a couple er ha’rs fum yo’ head, en fetch me a pig ter-morrer night fer ter roas’, en w’en you come I’ll hab de cha’m all ready fer you.’

===Chinese===
「佩姬大娘听他讲完来龙去脉，说道——

「『那巫师的岁数比俺大一倍还多，下得一手顶厉害的咒（goopher）。你缺的是一件护身符（life-cha'm），明天俺给你做一件；也只有这东西顶得了事。你给俺留下两根你头上的头发，明儿夜里再捎一口小猪来，俺好烤着吃；等你来时，俺就把护身符给你备得妥妥帖帖。』」

===Original===
“So Dan went down ter Aun’ Peggy de nex’ night—wid a young shote—en Aun’ Peggy gun ’im de cha’m. She had tuk de ha’rs Dan had lef’ wid ’er, en a piece er red flannin, en some roots en yarbs, en had put ’em in a little bag made out’n ’coon-skin.

===Chinese===
