## V Sir Clinton in the Museum / 第五章 博物馆中的克林顿爵士

===Original===
“There’s the light on again in the museum,” Sir Clinton observed. “I think we’ll go in and have a look round, now, to see if the place suggests anything.”

Mold stood aside to let them pass, and then resumed his watch at the door to prevent anyone else from entering the room. The servant had just finished fitting the new globe in its place and was preparing to remove the steps which he had used, when Sir Clinton ordered him to leave them in position and to await further instructions.

The museum was a room about forty feet square, with a lofty ceiling. To judge by the panelling of the walls, it belonged to the older part of Ravensthorpe; but the parquet of the floor seemed to be much more modern. Round the sides were placed exhibition cases about six feet high; and others of the same kind jutted out at intervals to form a series of shallow bays. In the centre of the room, directly under the lamp, stood a long, flat-topped case; and the floor beside it was littered with broken glass.

“博物馆的灯又亮了，”克林顿爵士（Sir Clinton）说道。“我看咱们这就进去转上一圈，看看这地方能不能给人一点启发。”

莫尔德（Mold）侧身让他们进去，随后回到门口继续守望，防止再有旁人进屋。仆人刚把新灯泡装回原位，正要搬走他方才用过的梯子，克林顿爵士却吩咐他把梯子留在原处，听候下一步指示。

博物馆是间约四十英尺见方的屋子，顶棚很高。从墙上的木护壁来看，它属于雷文索普（Ravensthorpe）较老的那部分建筑；但地板的拼花镶木看上去却新得多。四面墙边摆着约六英尺高的展柜；另有同式的展柜每隔一段向外凸出，围出一连串浅浅的凹室。屋子正中、吊灯正下方，立着一座长形平顶柜；柜旁的地板上散落着砸碎的玻璃。

“Are you looking for fingermarks?” asked Joan, as she came to his side.

Sir Clinton shook his head.
===Chinese===
克林顿爵士踱到被劫的那一格展柜前，对着碎玻璃沉吟察看了一两分钟。

“您在找指纹吗？”琼走到他身边，问道。

克林顿爵士摇了摇头。

===Original===
“Not much use hunting for fingermarks round here. Remember how many people must have leaned on this case at one time or other during the evening, when they were looking at the collection before the robbery. Fingerprints would prove nothing against anyone in particular, I’m afraid, Joan. What I’m really trying to find is some evidence confirming Mold’s notion that he heard two smashes after the light went out. It certainly looks as if he were right. If you look at the way that bit of glass there is cracked, you’ll see two series of lines in it. It might have been cracked here”﻿—he pointed with his finger﻿—“first of all: long cracks radiating from a smash over in this direction. Then there was a second blow﻿—about here﻿—which snapped off the apices of the spears of glass left after the first smash. But that really proves nothing. The same man might easily have hit the pane twice.”
===Chinese===
“在这儿找指纹用处不大。想想看，失窃之前，整个晚上看收藏的时候，必定有多少人或早或晚在这柜子上倚过。只怕指纹落在谁身上都定不了罪，琼。我真正想找的，是能证实莫尔德那个说法的证据——他说灯灭之后听见了两响砸玻璃声。看样子他确实没说错。你细看那边那块玻璃的裂纹，就能看出上面有两套纹路。它可能是先在这儿裂的”——他用手指点了点——“头一记砸在这个方向，留下放射状的长裂纹。接着又挨了第二记——大约在这儿——把头一回砸剩的玻璃尖茬齐齐崩断。不过这其实证明不了什么。同一个人往玻璃上砸两下，也是轻而易举的事。”

===Original===
He turned back to the keeper.

“Can you give me an estimate, Mold, of how long it was between the two crashes you heard?”

Mold considered carefully before replying.

“So far’s I can remember, Sir Clinton, it was about five seconds. But I’ll not take my oath on it.”
===Chinese===
他转回看管人这边。

“莫尔德，你听见的那两声响，中间隔了多久，能估个大概吗？”

莫尔德仔细掂量了一番才作答。

“凭我记着的，克林顿爵士，约莫五秒钟。可我不敢起誓。”

===Original===
“I wish you could be surer,” said the Chief Constable. “If it really was five seconds, it certainly looks like two separate affairs. A man smashing glass with repeated blows wouldn’t wait five seconds between them.”

He scanned the broken glass again.

“There’s a lot of jagged stuff round the edge of the hole but no blood, so far as I can see. The fellow must have worn a thick glove if he got his hand in there in the dark without cutting himself in the hurry.”
===Chinese===
“你要能说得更准些就好了，”郡警察总长（Chief Constable）说道。“倘若真是五秒钟，那就分明是两桩事。一个人抡着胳膊连砸玻璃，两下之间不会歇上五秒钟。”

他又扫视了一遍碎玻璃。

“窟窿边上一圈尽是参差的玻璃茬，可我看没有血迹。那家伙若是摸黑把手伸了进去、又急急慌慌没割着自己，手上必定戴着厚手套。”

===Original===
He turned back to the keeper.

“You can go outside, Mold, and keep people off the doorstep for a minute or two. Perhaps we shall have news of the manhunt soon. If anyone wants to see me on business; let him in; but keep off casual inquirers for the present.”

Obediently Mold unlocked the door and took his stand on the threshold outside, shutting the door behind him as he went. When he had gone, Sir Clinton turned to Joan.
===Chinese===
他又转向看管人。

莫尔德依言开了锁，到门外门槛上站定，出去时随手带上了门。他走后，克林顿爵士转向琼。

===Original===

“The electros weren’t insured for any great amount, I suppose?”

===Original===
Sir Clinton glanced round the room once more.

“I doubt if there’s much more to find out here,” he concluded. “It doesn’t give us much to go on, does it? Perhaps we’ll have better luck when these fellows come in from their hunt. They may have some news for us. But as things stand, we can’t even be sure whether it was two men or two gangs that were at work. One can’t blame Mold for not giving us better information; but what he gave us doesn’t seem to amount to very much at present.”
===Chinese===
