## Acknowledgment / 致谢
===Original===

to

Nancy Virginia Austen
Whose pleasant friendship, energetic spirit, and practical knowledge encouraged me to believe that a little Etsu-bo, with a heart full of love for old Japan, could gather the falling fragments of samurai spirit and weave them into a fragrant chain for the readers of today.

===Chinese===

致

南希·弗吉尼亚·奥斯汀（Nancy Virginia Austen）
她的友情令人愉快，精神富有活力，见识切于实用；是她鼓励我相信：一个小小的津保，怀着一颗热爱旧日本的心，也能拾起武士精神零落的碎片，为今日的读者把它编成一串芬芳的花环。
