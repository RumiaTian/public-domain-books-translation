## VII Why I Did Not Choose to Run / 我为何不再竞选

===Original===

Perhaps I have already indicated some of the reasons why I did not desire to be a candidate to succeed myself.

The Presidential office takes a heavy toll of those who occupy it and those who are dear to them. While we should not refuse to spend and be spent in the service of our country, it is hazardous to attempt what we feel is beyond our strength to accomplish.

I had never wished to run in 1928 and had determined to make a public announcement at a sufficiently early date so that the party would have ample time to choose someone else. An appropriate occasion for that announcement seemed to be the fourth anniversary of my taking office. The reasons I can give may not appear very convincing, but I am confident my decision was correct.

===Chinese===

关于我不愿做候选人接替自己的缘由，或许我已经透露了几分。

总统之职，于居其位者、于他们的至亲，都索取惨重的代价。为国效命，我们固然不该吝惜倾尽所有、耗尽自身，但去做自知力不能胜的事，总是危险的。

1928 年的竞选，我本来就无意参加，并且决意尽早公开宣布，好让本党有充裕的时间另择他人。宣布的合适时机，似乎就选在我就职四周年那天。我所能举出的理由，也许不见得多么有说服力，但我确信自己的决定是正确的。

===Original===

My personal and official relations have all been peculiarly pleasant. The Congress has not always done all that I wished, but it has done very little that I did not approve. So far as I can judge, I have been especially fortunate in having the approbation of the country.

But irrespective of the third-term policy, the Presidential office is of such a nature that it is difficult to conceive how one man can successfully serve the country for a term of more than eight years.

While I am in favor of continuing the long-established custom of the country in relation to a third term for a President, yet I do not think that the practice applies to one who has succeeded to part of a term as Vice President. Others might argue that it does, but I doubt if the country would so consider it.

===Chinese===

我的私谊与公务关系，无不格外融洽。国会未必事事如我所愿，但我所不赞成的事，它做得极少。据我判断，我素来特别幸运，深得全国的赞许。

但撇开第三任的成例不谈，总统之职的性质如此：单凭一人，如何能在超过 8 年的任期里始终成功地报效国家，殊难想象。

关于总统第三任，我国久已相沿的惯例，我赞成继续维持；但我认为这一惯例并不适用于以副总统之身继任了部分任期的人。旁人或许会争辩说适用，但我怀疑举国是否作此想。

===Original===

Although my own health has been practically perfect, yet the duties are very great and ten years would be a very heavy strain. It would be especially long for the Mistress of the White House. Mrs. Coolidge has been in more than usual good health, but I doubt if she could have stayed there for ten years without some danger of impairment of her strength.

A President should not only not be selfish, but he ought to avoid the appearance of selfishness. The people would not have confidence in a man that appeared to be grasping for office.

It is difficult for men in high office to avoid the malady of self-delusion. They are always surrounded by worshipers. They are constantly, and for the most part sincerely, assured of their greatness.

===Chinese===

我自己的健康虽几乎是完美的，但职责极为繁重，10 年将是极沉重的负担。对白宫女主人而言，这年月尤其漫长。柯立芝夫人的健康素来胜于常人，但我不信她在那里再住满 10 年，体力会没有受损之虞。

总统不但不该自私，还应当避免自私的形迹。一个看上去恋栈逐位的人，人民是不会信任的。

居高位的人，要躲过自欺这一病症，是很难的。他们的周围总围着崇拜者。人们不断向他们保证他们如何伟大——而且多半出于真心。

===Original===

They live in an artificial atmosphere of adulation and exaltation which sooner or later impairs their judgment. They are in grave danger of becoming careless and arrogant.

The chances of having wise and faithful public service are increased by a change in the Presidential office after a moderate length of time.

It is necessary for the head of the nation to differ with many people who are honest in their opinions. As his term progresses, the number who are disappointed accumulates. Finally, there is so large a body who have lost confidence in him that he meets a rising opposition which makes his efforts less effective.

===Chinese===

他们活在一种人为的奉承与颂扬的氛围里，这氛围迟早会损害他们的判断。他们大有变得轻率骄纵的危险。

总统之职隔一段适中的时间更替一次，得到贤明而忠勤的公共服务的机会便随之增加。

一国元首，势必要与许多持论诚恳的人意见相左。随着任期推进，失望者的数目不断累积。到最后，失去信心的人积成如此庞大的一群，他遇到的反对日益高涨，努力的成效便日渐减损。

===Original===

In the higher ranges of public service men appear to come forward to perform a certain duty. When it is performed their work is done. They usually find it impossible to readjust themselves in the thought of the people so as to pass on successfully to the solution of new public problems.

An examination of the records of those Presidents who have served eight years will disclose that in almost every instance the latter part of their term has shown very little in the way of constructive accomplishment. They have often been clouded with grave disappointments.

While I had a desire to be relieved of the pretensions and delusions of public life, it was not because of any attraction of pleasure or idleness.

===Chinese===

在公共服务的更高境域，人们走上前来，仿佛是要完成某一桩使命。使命既成，他们的工作便算完毕。他们通常发现，自己已无法在民众的心目中重新安放自身，也就无法顺利转而去解决新的公共问题。

检视那些任满 8 年的总统们的纪录便会发现：几乎每一例中，任期的后半段都少有建设性的成就可言，倒常常蒙着重大的失望。

我固然渴望卸下公共生活的虚饰与迷妄，却不是因为逸乐或清闲有什么吸引力。

===Original===

We draw our Presidents from the people. It is a wholesome thing for them to return to the people. I came from them. I wish to be one of them again.

Although all our Presidents have had back of them a good heritage of blood, very few have been born to the purple. Fortunately, they are not supported at public expense after leaving office, so they are not expected to set an example encouraging to a leisure class.

They have only the same title to nobility that belongs to all our citizens, which is the one based on achievement and character, so they need not assume superiority. It is becoming for them to engage in some dignified employment where they can be of service as others are.

===Chinese===

我们的总统取自人民。让他们重返人民中间，是件有益的事。我出自他们，愿重为他们中的一员。

我们的总统虽然个个都有良好的血统家世作底蕴，真正生于帝王之家的却寥寥无几。所幸他们离任后不由公费供养，人们也就不指望他们做出助长有闲阶级的榜样。

他们与全体公民一样，只拥有同一种高贵的头衔——建立在功业与品格之上的那一种——所以大可不必自命高人一等。他们从事某种体面的职业，与常人一样地服务社会，正是得体的。

===Original===

Our country does not believe in idleness. It honors hard work. I wanted to serve the country again as a private citizen.

In making my public statement I was careful in the use of words. There were some who reported that they were mystified as to my meaning when I said, “I do not choose to run.”

Although I did not know it at the time, months later I found that Washington said practically the same thing. Certainly he said no more in his Farewell Address, where he announced that “choice and prudence” invited him to retire.

===Chinese===

我们的国家不相信怠惰。它敬重辛勤劳作。我想再以一介平民的身份为国效力。

作公开声明时，我在措辞上十分审慎。有人后来表示，当我说出「我不选择竞选」（I do not choose to run）时，他们对我的本意感到困惑。

虽然我当时并不知晓，几个月后我发现，华盛顿说的实际上是同一回事。当然，他在《告别演说》里说的也不比我更多——在那里他宣布，「选择与审慎」邀请他归隐。

===Original===

There were others who constantly demanded that I should state that if nominated I would refuse to accept. Such a statement would not be in accordance with my conception of the requirements of the Presidential office. I never stated or formulated in my own mind what I should do under such circumstances, but I was determined not to have that contingency arise.

I therefore sent the Secretary to the President, Everett Sanders, a man of great ability and discretion, to Kansas City with instructions to notify several of the leaders of state delegations not to vote for me. Had I not done so, I am told, I should have been nominated.

The report that he had talked with me on the telephone after his arrival, and I had told him I would not accept if nominated, was pure fabrication. I had no communication with him of any kind after he left Washington and did not give him any such instruction or message at any time.

===Chinese===

另有一些人不断催逼，非要我声明：一旦获得提名，必将拒绝接受。这样的声明，与我心目中总统职位的分际不合。倘若真到那一步我该怎么做，我从未说过，心里也未尝定过成算；但我决意不让那样的局面发生。

于是我派总统秘书埃弗雷特·桑德斯（Everett Sanders）——一个极有才干而审慎的人——前往堪萨斯城，指示他知会几个州代表团的领袖，请他们不要投我的票。我听说，倘若不如此，我本会被提名。

有传言说，他抵达后曾与我通电话，我告诉他若获提名也不接受——这纯属捏造。他离开华盛顿后，我与他没有任何形式的往来，也从未在任何时候给过他任何这样的指示或口信。

===Original===

I thought if I could prevent being nominated, which I was able to do, it would never be necessary for me to decide the other question. But in order to be perfectly free, I sent this notice, so that if I declined no one could say I had misled him into supposing that I was willing to receive his vote.

I felt sure that the party and the country were in so strong a position that they could easily nominate and elect some other candidate. The events have confirmed my judgment.

In the primary campaign I was careful to make it known that I was not presenting any candidate. The friends of several of them no doubt represented that their candidate was satisfactory to me, which was true as far as it went.

===Chinese===

我想，只要能阻止自己获得提名——这我办得到——另一个问题便永远不必由我来裁决。但为使自己完全置身事外，我还是送出了这份知会：这样，我若谢绝，便没有人能说我误导过他，令他以为我愿意接受他的投票。

我深信本党与国家形势之强，另择他人提名并使其当选，易如反掌。后来的事态证实了我的判断。

初选期间，我着意让人知道，我不推出任何候选人。其中几位候选人的朋友无疑曾表示，他们的候选人是令我满意的——这话在它成立的范围内，倒也不假。

===Original===

I can conceive a situation in which a President might be warranted in exercising the influence of his office in selecting his successor. That condition did not exist in the last primary. The party had plenty of material, which was available, and the candidate really should be the choice of the people themselves. This is especially so now that so many of the states have laws for the direct expression of the choice of the voters.

A President in office can do very much about the nomination of his successor, because of his influence with the convention, but the feeling that he had forced a choice would place the nominee under a heavy handicap.

When the convention assembles it is almost certain that it will look about to see what candidate has made the largest popular showing, and unless some peculiar disqualification develops it will nominate him.

===Chinese===

我能设想这样一种局面：总统行使职权的影响来择定继任者，或属正当。上一次初选并不存在这种局面。本党有的是现成的可用之才，候选人实在应当出自人民自己的选择。如今这么多州都有了直接表达选民意愿的法律，尤其应当如此。

在任总统凭借他对代表大会的影响，在继任者的提名上大有可为；然而「人选是被他强加的」这种感觉，会使获提名者背上沉重的包袱。

代表大会开会时，几乎必先环顾各方，看哪位候选人在民间声势最盛；除非冒出什么特别的欠缺，大会便会提名此人。

===Original===

That was what happened in the last convention, although no one had a majority when the convention assembled.

A strong group of the party in and outside of the Senate made the mistake of undertaking to oppose Mr. Hoover with a large number of local candidates, which finally resulted in their not developing enough strength for any particular candidate to make a showing sufficient to impress the convention.

Although I did not intimate in any way that I would not accept the nomination, when I sent word to the heads of certain unpledged state delegations not to vote for me, they very naturally turned to Mr. Hoover, which brought about his nomination on the first ballot.

===Chinese===

上届大会正是如此，虽然大会开幕时无人握有多数。

本党在参议院内外的一股强势力量犯了一个错误：推出一大批地方候选人去对抗胡佛先生，结果反倒使任何一位特定候选人都积不起足够的声势，无法给大会留下深刻的印象。

我虽然绝无半点表示说我不接受提名，但当我传话给几个未作许诺的州代表团的头面人物、请他们勿投我的票时，他们便很自然地转向了胡佛先生——这便使他在第一轮投票中获得提名。

===Original===

The Presidential office differs from everything else. Much of it cannot be described, it can only be felt. After I had considered the reasons for my being a candidate on the one side and on the other, I could not say that any of them moved me with compelling force.

My election seemed assured. Nevertheless, I felt it was not best for the country that I should succeed myself. A new impulse is more likely to be beneficial.

It was therefore my privilege, after seeing my administration so strongly endorsed by the country, to retire voluntarily from the greatest experience that can come to mortal man. In that way, I believed I could best serve the people who have honored me and the country which I love.

===Chinese===

总统之职异于世间一切。它的许多东西无从描摹，只能意会。当我把赞成与反对我作候选人的种种理由一一掂量过后，我只能说，没有任何一条以不可抗拒的力量打动了我。

我的当选看似万无一失。尽管如此，我仍觉得，由我接替自己，于国家并非上策。一股新的动力更有可能带来裨益。

于是，在亲见本届政府深获全国如此强有力的拥戴之后，自愿从这桩凡人所能遭逢的最伟大的经历中引退，便成了我的荣幸。我相信，唯有如此，我才能最好地报效给予我荣誉的人民，和我所深爱的国家。
