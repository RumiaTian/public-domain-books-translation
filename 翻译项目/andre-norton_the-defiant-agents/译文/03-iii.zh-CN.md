## III / III

===Original===
Travis, one knee braced against the red earth, blinked as he parted a screen of tall rust-brown grass with cautious fingers to look out into a valley where golden mist clouded most of the landscape. His head ached with dull persistence, the pain fostered in some way by his own bewilderment. To study the land ahead was like trying to see through one picture interposed over another and far different one. He knew what ought to be there, but what was before him was very dissimilar.

A buff-gray shape flitted through the tall cover grass, and Travis tensed. *Mba’a*﻿—coyote? Or were these companions of his actually *ga-n*, spirits who could choose their shape at will and had, oddly, this time assumed the bodies of man’s tricky enemy? Were they *ndendai*﻿—enemies﻿—or *dalaanbiyat’i*, allies? In this mad world he did not know.

*Ei’dik’e?* His mind formed a word he did not speak: Friend?

===Chinese===
特拉维斯单膝抵着红土，眨了眨眼，用小心翼翼的手指拨开一道高高的铁锈色草幕，望向山谷——金色雾霭笼住了那里的大部分景物。他的头持续隐隐作痛，那痛楚不知怎的被他的茫然无措滋养着。端详眼前的原野，就像努力看透一张叠印在另一张截然不同画面上的图画。他知道那里本该是什么模样，可眼前的景象却大相径庭。

一道沙黄灰的身影掠过高高的掩护草丛，特拉维斯绷紧了。*Mba’a*（郊狼）？还是说，他这两位同伴其实是*ga-n*（山灵）——那些能随意择形化身的精怪，这一回偏偏怪里怪气地披上了人类那诡诈宿敌的躯壳？他们是*ndendai*（敌人），还是*dalaanbiyat’i*（盟友）？在这个疯狂的世界里，他无从知晓。

*Ei’dik’e*（朋友）？他脑中浮出一个没有出口的词：朋友？

===Original===
Yellow eyes met his directly. Dimly he had been aware, ever since awaking in this strange wilderness with the coming of morning light, that the four-footed ones trotting with him as he walked aimlessly had unbeastlike traits. Not only did they face him eye-to-eye, but in some ways they appeared able to read his thoughts.

He had longed for water to ease the burning in his throat, the ever-present pain in his head, and the creatures had nudged him in another direction, bringing him to a pool where he had mouthed liquid with a strange sweet, but not unpleasant taste.

Now he had given them names, names which had come out of the welter of dreams which shadowed his stumbling journey across this weird country.

===Chinese===
一双黄眼睛直直迎上他的目光。隐隐地，他早已察觉——自晨光初照时在这片陌生荒野中醒来起——那两只随着他漫无目的地行走、一路小跑的四足伙伴，有着不像走兽的品性。它们不但与他四目相对，而且在某种意义上，似乎能读懂他的心思。

他曾渴盼清水，好浇一浇喉咙里的灼烧、头脑中无时不在的疼痛；那两个生灵便用鼻尖把他拱向另一个方向，引到一泓水潭边，他啜饮的液体带着一种奇异的甜味，却并不难喝。

如今他已给它们起了名字——名字是从那团梦的乱流里浮出来的，正是那乱梦，笼着他跌跌撞撞横穿这片奇土的旅程。

===Original===
Nalik’ideyu (Maiden-Who-Walks-Ridges) was the female who continued to shepherd him along, never venturing too far from his side. Naginlta (He-Who-Scouts-Ahead) was the male who did just that, disappearing at long intervals and then returning to face the man and his mate as if conveying some report necessary to their journey.

It was Nalik’ideyu who sought out Travis now, her red tongue lolling from her mouth as she panted. Not from exertion, he was certain of that. No, she was excited and eager﻿ ﻿… on the hunt! That was it﻿—a hunt!

Travis’ own tongue ran across his lips as an impression hit him with feral force. There was meat﻿—rich, fresh﻿—just ahead. Meat that lived, waiting to be killed. Inside him his own avid hunger roused, shaking him farther out of the crusting dream.

===Chinese===
纳利克伊德尤（Nalik’ideyu，「走岭少女」）是那只雌兽，一路牧着他前行，从不离开他身边太远。纳金尔塔（Naginlta，「先行探路者」）是那只雄兽，干的正是名字里那桩事：隔上一阵便消失，随后归来，面对他和自己的伴侣，仿佛在传达旅途必需的某种通报。

此刻寻到特拉维斯跟前的正是纳利克伊德尤，她喘着气，红舌头耷拉在嘴外。不是累的——这一点他很确定。不，她是兴奋而热切……在打猎！就是这样——一场围猎！

一个念头挟着兽性的力量击中他，特拉维斯自己的舌头也舔过嘴唇。前方有肉——肥美、新鲜——活着的肉，正等着被宰杀。他体内那股贪婪的饥饿醒了，把他从结壳的迷梦里又摇醒了一层。

===Original===
His hands went to his waist, but the groping fingers did not find what vague memory told him should be there﻿—a belt, heavy with knife in sheath.

He examined his own body with attention to find he was adequately covered by breeches of a smooth, dull brown material which blended well with the vegetation about him. He wore a loose shirt, belted in at the narrow waist by a folded strip of cloth, the ends of which fluttered free. On his feet were tall moccasins, the leg pieces extending some distance up his calves, the toes turned up in rounded points.

Some of this he found familiar, but these were fragments of memory; again his mind fitted one picture above another. One thing he did know for sure﻿—he had no weapons. And that realization struck home with a thrust of real and terrible fear which tore away more of the bewilderment cloaking his mind.

===Chinese===
他的手摸向腰间，摸索的手指却没有找到模糊记忆中该在那里的东西——一条腰带，坠着入鞘的重刀。

他仔细检视自己的身体，发现身上的遮蔽倒算齐全：一条质地平滑、暗棕色的裤子，与周围的草木融得很好。他穿着宽松的衬衫，窄腰处用一条折起的布带束住，带子的两端随风飘着。脚上是高帮鹿皮软鞋（moccasin），靴筒上延到小腿好一段，鞋头翘起圆圆的尖。

这些物件有些他觉得眼熟，但都只是记忆的碎片；他的头脑又一次把一张画面叠上另一张。有一件事他确凿无疑——他没有武器。这个认识带着一记真切而可怕的恐惧扎进心底，又撕去了裹着他心智的那团迷惘的一层。

===Original===
Nalik’ideyu was impatient. Having advanced a step or two, she now looked back at him over her shoulder, yellow eyes slitted, her demand on him as instant and real as if she had voiced understandable words. Meat was waiting, and she was hungry. Also she expected Travis to aid in the hunt﻿—at once.

Though he could not match her fluid grace in moving through the grass, Travis followed her, keeping to cover. He shook his head vigorously, in spite of the stab of pain the motion cost him, and paid more attention to his surroundings. It was apparent that the earth under him, the grass around, the valley of the golden haze, were all real, not part of a dream. Therefore that other countryside which he kept seeing in a ghostly fashion was a hallucination.

Even the air which he drew into his lungs and expelled again, had a strange smell, or was it taste? He could not be sure which. He knew that hypno-training could produce queer side effects, but﻿ ﻿… this﻿ ﻿…

===Chinese===
纳利克伊德尤不耐烦了。她已先行一两步，此刻回头越过肩膀望他，黄眼睛眯成一线；她对他的催促即刻而真切，仿佛说出了能听懂的人话。肉在等着，她饿了。而且她指望特拉维斯立刻搭手围猎——马上。

虽学不来她穿行草丛时行云流水的身姿，特拉维斯仍跟了上去，借掩护而行。他狠狠摇头，全不顾这一动引来的刺痛，把更多注意力放在周遭环境上。显然，身下的大地、四周的草丛、金雾弥漫的山谷，都真实不虚，并非梦境的一部分。那么，那片他眼里不断幽幽浮现的另一种乡野，便是幻觉了。

就连吸进肺里又吐出来的空气，都带着一种奇异的气味——或者说，是味道？他分不清究竟是哪种。他知道催眠训练（hypno-training）会生出古怪的副作用，可是……这个……

===Original===
Travis paused, staring unseeingly before him at the grass still waving from the coyote’s passage. Hypno-training! What was that? Now three pictures fought to focus in his mind: the two landscapes which did not match and a shadowy third. He shook his head again, his hands to his temples. This﻿—this only was real: the ground, the grass, the valley, the hunger in him, the hunt waiting﻿ ﻿…

He forced himself to concentrate on the immediate present and the portion of world he could see, feel, scent, which lay here and now about him.

The grass grew shorter as he proceeded in Nalik’ideyu’s wake. But the haze was not thinning. It seemed to hang in patches, and when he ventured through the edge of a patch it was like creeping through a fog of golden, dancing motes with here and there a glittering speck whirling and darting like a living thing. Masked by the stuff, Travis reached a line of brush and sniffed.

特拉维斯顿住脚步，视而不见地瞪着前方——郊狼过处，草浪犹自起伏。催眠训练！那是什么？此刻有三幅画面在他脑中争夺焦点：两幅对不上的风景，还有第三幅影影绰绰的。他又摇了摇头，双手抵住太阳穴。这个——只有这个才是真的：脚下的大地、四围的草、山谷、腹中的饥饿、等着开场的围猎……

===Original===
It was a warm scent, a heavy odor he could not identify and yet one he associated with a living creature. Flat to earth, he pushed head and shoulders under the low limbs of the bush to look ahead.

Here was a space where the fog did not hold, a pocket of earth clear under the morning sun. And grazing there were three animals. Again shock cleared a portion of Travis’ bemused brain.

They were about the size, he thought, of antelopes, and they had a general resemblance to those beasts in that they had four slender legs, a rounded body, and a head. But they had alien features, so alien as to hold him in open-mouthed amazement.

===Chinese===
那是一股温热的气味，一种沉重得叫他无从辨认的气味，却又让他联想到某个活物。他贴伏于地，把头和肩膀从灌木低垂的枝条下探进去，向前张望。

===Original===

One of those mental alerts from the coyotes broke his absorption. Nalik’ideyu was not interested in the odd appearance of the grazing creatures; she was intent upon their usefulness in another way﻿—as a full and satisfying meal﻿—and she was again impatient with him for his dull response.

Those nose horns had an ugly look; this thing might be a fighter rather than a runner. But the suggestion which had flashed from coyote to him had taken root. Travis was hungry, he was a hunter, and here was meat on the hoof, queer as it looked.
