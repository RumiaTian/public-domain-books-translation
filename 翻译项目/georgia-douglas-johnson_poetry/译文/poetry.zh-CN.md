## Beautiful Eyes / 美丽的眼睛

===Original===
Eyes like the violet—in them I see
All that is fair, that is holy to me!
Eyes that shed fragrance, so constant, so true,
Pure as a clear drop of morning dew.
Eyes like the violet, gently along
Lead me to vespers—to prayer and to song.
Eyes like the violet, let me I pray
Live within range of thy glances all day!

===Chinese===
紫罗兰般的眼睛——我在其中看见
一切美好之物，一切于我神圣之物！
散发着芬芳的眼睛，如此恒常，如此真挚，
纯净如清晨一滴澄澈的露珠。
紫罗兰般的眼睛，温柔地一路
引我走向晚祷——走向祈祷与颂歌。
紫罗兰般的眼睛，我祈求，让我
整日栖居在你目光所及的地方！

## Fame / 名声

===Original===
Through the land of tribulation,
O’er the river of despair,
When the taut heart snaps with tension,
Fame awaits you, smiling there.

After love has all been wasted,
After every song is sung;
Late—too late, the world will crown you,
Useless! Love and song are done.

===Chinese===
穿过苦难之地，
越过绝望之河，
当绷紧的心因重压断裂，
名声在那里含笑等你。

当爱情已被耗尽，
当每支歌都已唱罢；
迟了——太迟了，世界才为你加冕，
徒劳！爱与歌都已终结。

## The Final Strain / 最后的乐章

===Original===
I climbed the craggy hill of fame,
Heart-sore and wearily,
Stood on her gleaming goal at length,
And sighed in ecstasy.

“O God,” I cried, “what bliss”—when lo!
Came stealing like a pall,
The strains of Life’s Last Symphony,
In Prelude, to—*the call*.

===Chinese===
我攀上名声嶙峋的山丘，
心痛而疲惫，
终于站上她闪光的顶点，
在狂喜中叹息。

“哦上帝，”我喊道，“何等至福”——看哪！
一袭棺衣悄悄袭来，
那是人生最后交响的乐音，
一支序曲，通向——*那声召唤*。

## Heritage / 遗泽

===Original===
Happy youth in joyous laughter,
Wafts me pensively alone
O’er the winding way, where sorrow
Claims the Mantled for her own.

I can hear their voices ringing
Down the corridor of years,
As they lift their twilight faces,
Through a mist of falling tears!

===Chinese===
欢乐笑声中的幸福青春，
把我忧思地独自送远，
送过迂曲的路，那里忧伤
把蒙纱者认作自己的亲眷。

我能听见他们的声音回响
在岁月的长廊里，
当他们仰起暮色的脸，
穿过一片落泪的迷雾！

## The Heart of a Woman / 女人的心

===Original===
The heart of a woman goes forth with the dawn,
As a lone bird, soft winging, so restlessly on,
Afar o’er life’s turrets and vales does it roam
In the wake of those echoes the heart calls home.

The heart of a woman falls back with the night,
And enters some alien cage in its plight,
And tries to forget it has dreamed of the stars
While it breaks, breaks, breaks on the sheltering bars.

===Chinese===
女人的心随黎明出发，
如一只孤鸟，轻拍着翅膀，不安地向前，
远远掠过人生的塔楼与沟谷漫游，
追随那些被心唤作家园的回响。

女人的心随夜幕退落，
陷于困顿，进入某个陌生的牢笼，
它试着忘记自己曾梦见星辰，
当它撞碎、撞碎、撞碎在护庇的栅栏上。

The cry of the heart

The first in nature’s list,
Is fading, ere the rising sun
The world awake has kist.

The purity of morn,
The glint that lies in virgin cheek,

My joy leaps with your ecstasy,
In sympathy divine;
The smiles that wreathe upon your lips.
Find sentinels on mine:

Your lightest sigh I’m echoing,
I tremble with your pain,
And all your tears are falling
In my heart like bitter rain.

===Chinese===
我的欢悦随你的狂喜跳跃，

你唇边绽开的微笑，

===Original===
The breaking dead leaves ’neath my feet
A plaintive melody repeat,
Recalling shattered hopes that lie
As relics of a bygone sky.

Again I thread the mazy past,
Back where the mounds are scattered fast—
Oh! foolish tears, why do you start,
*To break of dead leaves in the heart?*

===Chinese===
脚下碎裂的枯叶
反复吟着一支哀婉的旋律，
唤起那些碎裂的希望，它们

我再次穿行迷宫般的往昔，
回到坟冢密集散布的地方——

*为了心中枯叶的碎裂？*

## Peace / 宁静

===Original===
I rest me deep within the wood,
Drawn by its silent call,
Far from the throbbing crowd of men
On nature’s breast I fall.

My couch is sweet with blossoms fair,
A bed of fragrant dreams,
And soft upon my ear there falls
The lullaby of streams.

The tumult of my heart is stilled,
Within this sheltered spot,
Deep in the bosom of the wood,
Forgetting, and—forgotten!

===Chinese===
我在林中深深安歇，
被它无声的召唤引来，
远离悸动的人群，
我倒进造化的怀抱。

我的卧榻缀满芬芳的花，
一张馨香之梦的床，
而耳畔轻柔地落下
溪流的摇篮曲。

心中的骚动平息了，
在这庇护之地，
在林子深深的怀抱里，
遗忘着，也——被遗忘！

## Whither? / 何往？

===Original===
Minutes swiftly throb and pass,
Shadows cross the dial-glass,
Speeding ever to some call,
Weary world and shadows, all.

Down the closing aisles of day,
Tramping footsteps die away,
But no tidings thread the gloom,
From the hushed and silent tomb.

===Chinese===
分分秒秒悸动着飞逝，
影子掠过表盘玻璃，
一路奔向某个召唤，
疲惫的世界与影子，无一例外。

沿白昼合拢的长廊，
沓沓足音渐渐消逝，
却没有音讯穿过幽暗，
来自那静默无声的坟茔。

Our separate winding ways we trod,

Unbonded by the clasp of hand,
Without a vow—we understand,
Estranged for aye, the fusing kiss,

## Mirrored / 镜像

===Original===
When lone and solitaire within your chamber,
With lamp unlit, as evening shades unroll,
If you reveal the trail your thoughts are taking,
I then may read the riddle of your soul.

For it is then, the tired mind unveiling,
Drifts stark into the holy after-glow,
Within the hour of quiet meditation,
The tidal thoughts, like limpid waters, flow.

===Chinese===
当你独自一人在房中，
灯未点，暮色层层展开，
若你显露出思绪走过的踪迹，
我便能读出你灵魂的谜底。

赤裸裸漂入神圣的余晖，
在静思的时刻里，
潮涌的思绪如澄澈的水流。

## Repulse / 排拒

===Original===
Nobody cares when I am glad,
I beat upon their hearts in glee,
“Drink, drink joy’s brimming cup with me,”
All echoless, my ecstasy—
Nobody cares when I am glad.

Nobody cares when I am sad,
Whene’er I seek compassion’s breast,
I falter wounded from my quest
Back! back into my heart, sore prest—
Nobody cares when I am sad.

===Chinese===
我高兴时无人在意，
我欢快地叩击他们的心，
“来，来同我饮尽欢乐的满杯，”
一片回声寂寥，我的狂喜——
我高兴时无人在意。

我悲伤时无人在意，
每当我寻求怜悯的胸怀，
我带着伤蹒跚退出求索，
退回！退回我心里，备受压逼——
我悲伤时无人在意。

===Chinese===

## Impelled / 驱策

===Original===
Athwart the sky the great sun sails,
Through aeons thus, the daylight trails,
And man, living breath of the sod
Beholding, in his heart knows God.

Throughout the night’s long brooding deep,
Earth’s trustful children die-to-sleep,
But with the whisperings of morn
Awake, unto the day, new-born.

The mystery of earth untold,
The great infinite, none behold,
Forge ever new the spiral chain,
Revolving man to God again.

===Chinese===
巨大的太阳横渡天空，
历经万古，白昼如此拖曳而行，

大地信实的孩子们死去般睡去，

醒来，迎接白昼，如获新生。

大地未曾言说的奥秘，
那伟大的无限，无人得见，
不断锻造新的螺旋之链，
旋转着使人复归于上帝。

迟来的阵雨有何用处，

## Hegira / 大迁徙

===Original===
Oh, black man, why do you northward roam, and leave all the farm lands bare?
Is your house not warm, tightly thatched from storm, and a larder replete your share?
And have you not schools, fit with books and tools the steps of your young to guide?
Then what do you seek, in the north cold and bleak, ’mid the whirl of its teeming tide?

I have toiled in your cornfields, and parched in the sun, I have bowed ’neath your load of care,
I have patiently garnered your bright golden grain, in season of storm and fair,
With a smile I have answered your glowering gloom, while my wounded heart quivering bled,
Trailing mute in your wake, as your rosy dawn breaks, while I curtain the mound of my dead.

Though my children are taught in the schools you have wrought, they are blind to the sheen of the sky,
For the brand of your hand, casts a pall o’er the land, that enshadows the gleam of the eye,
My sons, deftly sapped of the brawn-hood of man, self-rejected and impotent stand,
My daughters, unhaloed, unhonored, undone, feed the lust of a dominant land.

I would not remember, yet could not forget, how the hearts beating true to your own,
You’ve tortured, and wounded, and filtered their blood ’till a budding Hegira has blown.

Unstrange is the pathway to Calvary’s hill, which I wend in my dumb agony,
Up its perilous height, in the pale morning light, to dissever my own from the tree.

And so I’m away, where the sky-line of day sets the arch of its rainbow afar,
To the land of the north, where the symbol of worth sets the broad gates of combat ajar!

===Chinese===
哦，黑人，你为何向北游徙，任田地全都荒芜？
你的房子难道不暖，草苫严实遮着风雨，你的食橱难道不满盈？
你难道没有学校，配着书册与工具，指引你年轻人的脚步？
那你还要寻什么，在北方寒冷萧瑟的地方，在它人潮的漩涡里？

我在你的玉米田里劳作，在烈日下炙烤，我向你重担弯腰，
我耐心收拢你金黄的谷粒，无论风暴或晴和，
我以微笑回应你阴沉的怒容，而我受伤的心颤抖滴血，
默默随在你身后，当你玫瑰色的黎明破开，我正为死者拢起坟头的幕布。

虽然我的孩子在你兴办的学堂里受教，他们却看不见天空的光泽，
因为你手掌的烙印，给大地罩上尸布，遮暗了眼中的光彩，
我的儿子们，被悄然抽去男儿的雄健，自弃而无力地站立，
我的女儿们，无光环、无尊荣、被毁弃，喂饲着一个强横之地的欲焰。

我不愿记起，却无法忘记，那些忠诚应和着你的心，
你如何折磨、戕害，滤取他们的血，直到一场萌芽的大迁徙终于刮起。

去各各他山的路我并不陌生，我在哑然的苦痛中跋涉，
攀上它险峻的高处，在惨淡的晨光里，把我的亲人从十字架上解下。

于是我走了，去那白昼的天际线把彩虹的拱门远远架起的地方，
去北方的国度，那里价值的徽记把战斗的阔门推开一线！

再度唤回它们的狂野，
它们的严酷与芬芳。

## Break, Break My Heart / 碎裂吧，碎裂吧，我的心

===Original===
Break, break my heart
For love is done,
The pale light trails the dying sun—
And night awaits—no hope—no stars
Darkness
Hide my scars!

===Chinese===
碎裂吧，碎裂吧，我的心，
因为爱已终结，
惨白的光尾随垂死的太阳——
而黑夜在候——无望——无星
黑暗啊，
遮住我的伤痕！

## Little King / 小小君王

===Original===
From worshipping I now arise
Stunned and aghast, with open eyes
I see the real, the little you
I thought so gallant, brave and true.

A pity yet is mine, I fear,
Since wherefore comes this falling tear,
For none among your fawning throng
Will love you well, nor love you long.

===Chinese===
我从膜拜中如今站起，
惊愕骇然，睁着眼睛，
我看见了真实的、渺小的你——
我曾以为你那样豪侠、勇毅而真诚。

可我心里还存着怜悯，我怕，
不然这落下的泪从何而来，
因为在你那谄媚的人群中，
没有人会好好爱你，也不会爱你长久。

## Romance / 罗曼史

===Original===
When I was young
I used to say:
Romance *will come* riding by
And I shall surely smile
And play with him awhile.

When I grew older
then I said:
Romance *may come* riding by
I wonder shall I smile
And play with him awhile?

But now—
Alas! I only say:
Romance *never will* come by
And I shall never smile
He has been dead the while!

===Chinese===
当我年轻的时候，
我常常说：
罗曼史*定会*骑马经过，
我一定会微笑，
陪他玩耍片刻。

当我长大些，
那时我说：
罗曼史*或许会*骑马经过，
我不知道还会不会微笑，
陪他玩耍片刻？

但如今——
唉！我只会说：
罗曼史*永远不会*经过，
我也永远不会微笑，
他早已死去多时！

## Falling Gods / 陨落的诸神

===Original===
Confusion, desuetude and gloom,
The travailing of sound,
Fell desolation in my soul,
And agony profound;
The gods are falling heavily
And for all time to be,
And never more my heart shall know
A shrine to Deity!

===Chinese===
混乱、荒废与幽暗，
声音的阵痛，
凄凉的荒芜落进我的灵魂，
还有深切的苦楚；
诸神正沉沉陨落，
一落便直到所有的将来，
我的心再也不会知道
一座敬神的圣殿！

## Armor / 甲胄

===Original===
You cannot hurt me any more
For I am armored now
And I can look into your face
With cool, unfevered brow.

The tranquil river meets the sea,
My life flows on at rest,
Unurged, untorn, but oh, my God!
I love the old way best!

===Chinese===
你再也不能伤害我，
因为我已披上甲胄，
我能直视你的脸，
额头发凉，不再滚烫。

平静的河流汇入大海，
我的生命安然流淌，
不催迫，不撕扯，可是，哦，我的上帝！
我最爱还是从前那样！

## Divide / 阻隔

===Original===
Your lightest breath may fan my cheek
Your whisper stir me when you speak,
And yet—
The teeming planets play
Between your heart—and mine
Today.

===Chinese===
你最轻的呼吸也能拂动我的脸颊，
你的低语在你开口时也能撩动我，然而——
繁密的行星
正在你的心——与我的心
之间运转，
就在今天。

## Return / 归返

===Original===
Now,
Like the pines intoning
Though some solitary gloom,
My errant throughts go pattering
About love’s ancient tomb,
And though no breath of incense rare
Lies round the shattered cup,
A banquet weird, the fragments
Where the ghost of love
May sup.

===Chinese===
此刻，
像松树在一片
孤寂的幽暗里低吟，
我游荡的思绪嗒嗒作响，
绕着爱的古老坟墓，
纵然再没有珍稀的薰香气息
萦绕那破碎的杯盏，
一场怪异的盛宴，那些残片，
爱的幽灵
可以在此就食。

## Song of the Sinner / 罪人之歌

===Original===
Just a bit of ashes
Grey, grey ashes—spent—
God! how fierce the fires burned
Down to this content.

Just a bit of ashes,
Not a single spark
Lives in this residuum
Crumbling cold and dark.

Just a bit of ashes—
To the judgment day,
I go with my memories—
Pray, sweet virgin, pray!

===Chinese===
只是一撮灰烬，
灰白、灰白的灰烬——燃尽了——
上帝！那火曾烧得多么猛烈，
最后只剩这份安然。

只是一撮灰烬，
没有一粒火星
存活在这残余里，
它冷冷地、暗暗地碎裂。

只是一撮灰烬——
走向审判之日，
我带着我的记忆上路——
祈祷吧，甜蜜的圣女，祈祷吧！

## Celibacy / 独身

===Original===
Where is the love that might have been
Flung to the far ends of Earth?
In my body stamping around,
In my body like a hound
Leashed and restless—
Biding time!

===Chinese===
那份本可能有的爱去了哪里，
被抛到大地的尽头？
它在我身体里跺着脚逡巡，
在我身体里像一条猎犬，
拴着皮绳、躁动不宁——
等待着时机！

## Offering / 奉献

===Original===
I seek no tokens of you dear
I only ask to give
The purple flower of my heart
And you will let it live.

I ask no fealty or plight,
I only pray that you
May find earth’s barren places bright
Perhaps, because it grew.

And when for you the final sun
Moves toward the darkening West,
I shall be lingering to place
Love’s flower on your breast.

===Chinese===
我不向你索要信物，亲爱的，
我只请求给予
我心中那朵紫色的花，
而你会让它活下去。

我不求效忠，也不盟誓，
我只祈愿你
会觉得大地荒凉之处明亮，
或许，只因它曾在那里生长。

而当最后一轮太阳为你
移向渐暗的西方，
我会流连着，把爱的花朵
安放在你的胸膛上。

## Estrangement / 疏离

===Original===
Some day I shall be dead, and pride
Which kept me from your feet,
Shall be the burden of the song
My cold lips shall repeat.

And some day when you too shall find
A pillow in the sod,
Would you then spurn an hour with me
Above—where daisies nod?

===Chinese===
总有一天我会死去，而那
阻我到你脚边的骄傲，
将成为我的冷唇
反复吟唱的歌之重负。

而总有一天，当你也
在草皮间寻得枕垫，
那时你可会拒绝与我在天上——
雏菊点头的所在——共度一时辰？

## Recessional / 退场赞美诗

===Original===
Consider me a memory—a dream
That passed away,
Or yet, a flower that has blown and shattered—
In a day;
For passion sleeps, alas, and keeps no vigil
With the years,
And wakens to no conjuring
Of orison or tears.

Consider me a melody
That served its simple turn,
Or but the residue of fire
That settles in the urn,
For love defies pure reasoning
And undeterred flows
Within—without
The vassal heart!

===Chinese===
把我当作一段记忆——一场
逝去的梦，
或者，一朵盛开又碎裂的花——
只在一日之间；
因为激情睡了，唉，它不再与岁月
一同守夜，
再多的祈祷或泪水
也唤不醒它。

把我当作一段旋律，
奏完了它简单的轮回，
或者只是火的余烬，
沉在瓮底，
因为爱蔑视纯粹的推理，
它无拦无阻地流着，
流进——流出
那颗臣服的心！

## Sepulchre / 坟冢

===Original===
I have mounded the corpse of my sorrow
And wreathed it with roses fair
That none who may pass on the morrow
May know what lies buried there.

===Chinese===
我已把我悲伤的尸身堆成坟冢，
再用鲜艳的玫瑰编成花环，
好让明天路过的任何人
都不知道底下埋着什么。

## Curtain / 落幕

===Original===
When one has lived
’Tis not so hard
To fold the hands,
To say, “Good-night,”
And creep away
Behind the dark;

But ’tis not strange
The heart rebels
When sounds of night
Ring down the day
That was a weary, joyless way
From early dawn
To setting sun:
How eagerly we trail the light
For crumbs of happiness we fend,
And struggle, struggle—to the end!

===Chinese===
当一个人已经活过，
那便不难
合拢双手，
道一声「晚安」，
悄悄退到
黑暗背后；

可这又何足为怪，
心会反抗，
当夜的声响
为白昼敲响落幕的钟——
那原是一条疲惫而无欢的路，
从清早的黎明
到西沉的太阳：
我们多么热切地追随光亮，
为几粒幸福的碎屑自谋生计，
挣扎着，挣扎着——直到尽头！

## Afterglow / 余晖

===Original===
Through you I entered heaven and hell,
Knew rapture and despair,
I flitted o’er the plains of earth
And scaled each shining stair:
Drank deep the waters of content,
And drained the cup of gall,
Was regal and was impotent,
Was suzerain and thrall.

Now, by Reflection’s placid pool
On evening’s mellowed brow,
I smile across the backward way
And pledge anew my vow;
For every glancing, golden gleam,
I offer gladly—pain!
And I would give a thousand world
To live it all again!

===Chinese===
经由你，我进入了天堂与地狱，
识得了狂喜与绝望，
我掠过大地上的平原，
也攀过每一道闪亮的阶梯：
深深畅饮知足的泉水，
也饮尽了苦胆之杯，
曾为王尊，也曾为无力，
曾为宗主，也曾为奴仆。

如今，在黄昏温润的眉宇旁，
凭临静思的平湖，
我向着来路微笑，
重新立下我的誓愿；
为每一次流转的金色微光，
我甘愿献上——苦痛！
我愿交出一千个世界，
把这一切再活一遍！
