### V / 五
===Original===

The new Administration Packard, which a little before midnight on the 18th of July carried six men beyond the northern barricades of the Gran Seco city, did not continue more than a few miles on the road which ran to Fort Castor. Suvorin, who drove﻿—he had in the early days of motoring won the Grand Prix in the race from Paris to Marseilles﻿—did not know the country, but there were men with him who did. Obedient to their instructions, he turned to the right at the San Pedro calvary, and for three hours bumped and skidded along sandy tracks and over stony barrens. It was apparent to one who kept an eye on the stars that he was bending south in a wide circuit, and presently the party came to the main highway between the city and the Mines. Since the defence was not holding a continuous line, but only two sectors, the party were no longer in the battle zone. They did not cross the road till it had been carefully reconnoitred, and once beyond it the car was again in a moraine of boulders and banks of shale. The moon had long set, and the headlights were working feebly, so that it was more by good fortune than by skill that it came at last to the respectable road which ran from the Universum Mine to the railway-sidings south of the city. Here it turned west and made better speed, till the dancing pencils of the searchlights revealed the proximity of General Lossberg’s army. The travellers were in fact very near the General’s advanced headquarters, and a rifle-shot over their heads presently brought them to a halt. Among them they must have had adequate passports, for an hour later they were partaking of the General’s hospitality. He was in a good humour, for he had just had word of the evacuation of the city, and believed that now he was pressing hard on the heels of the fleeing and broken rebels.
Of the six occupants of the car only one accompanied the General on his triumphant entry. This was the driver of the car, Peter Suvorin, a tall man with a bony face and skin like old parchment, and hair so pale that he seemed almost an albino. The others prepared to go down country. General Lossberg entertained him and Pasquali to breakfast, and had an interview thereafter with the others in the extremest privacy. They formed a curious contrast to the trim Olifa staff.

七月十八日午夜前不久，那辆行政大楼新配的帕卡德载着六个人驶出大旱原的城的北栅，在通往卡斯特堡的路上只走了几英里便不再继续。驾车的苏沃林——早年汽车时代他曾在巴黎到马赛的赛车中赢过大奖——不认路，但同行中有认路的。他遵照那些人的指示，在圣佩德罗的十字架路口右转，此后三个小时在沙径上颠籸打滑，碾过碎石荒滩。留意星辰的人看得出，他正腋一个大圈向南弯去，不久这一行人就到了城与矿区之间的大道上。由于守方并未连成完整防线，只守两个地段，这行人已不在交战区。他们把道路仔细侦察过才敢穿越，一过公路，车又驶进了巨石的冰碛与页岩坡之间。月亮早已落了，车灯昏昏欲亮，最后能拐上那条从宇宙矿通往城南铁路支线的像样公路，靠的更多是运气而不是本事。到这里它转向西，跑得快了些，直到探照灯舞动的光柱显示出洛斯贝格将军的大军已近。这行人实际已贴近将军的前方指挥部，一枪掠过头顶，随即叫停了他们。他们身上想必带着过硬的通行证件，因为一小时后他们已蒙将军款待。将军心情很好，他刚接到弃城的消息，相信此刻正穷追着溃逃的叛军的脚后跟。
车中六人，只有一位随将军凯旋入城。就是那位司机，彼得·苏沃林——高个子，瘦骨脸，皮肤像旧羊皮纸，头发淡得近乎白化病人。其余人则准备南下。洛斯贝格将军请他和帕斯夸利用了早餐，随后又与其余诸人在绝密中谈了一次。他们与整洁的奥利法参谋们形成了古怪的对照。

The General was very neat in his field-grey uniform, a well-set-up figure which did not look its fifty-eight years, square, tanned face, brisk, grey moustache, steady, competent grey eyes﻿—the whole a little marred by a stupid mouth and a heavy, rather brutal chin. The motorcar party compared to him seemed like a wandering theatrical troupe. Pasquali, he who played Scriabin, was indeed sprucely dressed and wore an expensive fur-coat, but his prognathous jaw was blue and unshaven, and his dark eyes so opaque with weariness that he looked like a sick negro. Radin was a tall fellow, whose high cheekbones suggested Indian blood, and who carried a recent scar above his right eye. Daniel Judson, short, thick, with rabbit teeth and a broken nose, looked like a damaged prizefighter, and thick black eyebrows above a sallow face gave Laschallas the air of a provincial actor imperfectly made up. Trompetter, a Javanese Dutchman with a touch of the tar brush, had a broad, wedge-shaped face as yellow as a guinea, and little sharp pig’s eyes. All, except Pasquali and Suvorin, were dressed in oddments, and, having been in close hiding, looked as unwholesome as the blanched insects below an upturned flowerpot.
Another joined the conference, a man with a light-cavalry figure, wearing a suit of thin tweeds which had been made not a month before in London. He shook hands with Pasquali and Suvorin, bowed to the General, and nodded to the others. Lossberg spoke to him at length, and he appeared to assent.
“You, Señor Romanes,” said Lossberg, “and also Señor Suvorin enter the city with me. I will have you attached to my Intelligence section. These other gentlemen return in half an hour to Olifa, in the charge of Señor Pasquali. They will report to General Bianca and put themselves under his instructions. I must bid you goodbye, gentlemen, for time presses.” And the brisk general not unwillingly left the group to an aide-de-camp.
So the party of five travelled in comfort that day down country, and, after many delays owing to freight trains, reached the city of Olifa on the following morning. They duly reported to General Bianca, but they did not take his instructions. They seemed to be concerned with urgent business of their own, for they disappeared into the underworld of Olifa, meeting every evening at a certain café in a back street.

将军穿着朴素的灰野战服，十分整洁，身姿挺拔，看不出五十八岁的年纪，方脸晒得黝黑，灰胡子精悍，灰眼睛沉稳干练——只是那张蠢嘴和沉重得近乎凶狠的下巴，让整体略损。与他一比，这汽车队活像个跑码头的戏班。帕斯夸利，那位弹斯克里亚宾的，衣着确实齐整，还穿着一件昂贵的皮大衣，但他突出的下巴青青的没刮，黑眼睛倦得浑浊无光，活像个生病的黑人。拉丁是高个子，高颧骨暗示着印第安血统，右眼上方带着一道新疤。丹尼尔·贾德森矮壮，兔牙塌鼻，像个被打残的职业拳手；拉斯查拉斯蜡黄脸上两道浓黑眉毛，让他像个化装没化好的外省演员。特朗佩特是个带黑人血统的爪哇荷人，宽大的楔形脸黄得像几尼金币，一双小而尖的猪眼。除帕斯夸利和苏沃林外，人人穿得七拼八凑，又久藏密室，看上去就像倒扣花盆底下那些见不得天日的白虫子一样病态。
又有一人加入密谈，轻骑兵般的身形，穿一身不到一个月前才在伦敦做的薄花呢西服。他与帕斯夸利和苏沃林握手，向将军鞠躬，对其余的人点头。洛斯贝格对他讲了很久，他看来是应允了。
“您，罗马内斯先生，”洛斯贝格说，“还有苏沃林先生，随我进城。我会把你们编入我的情报科。其余几位先生半小时后回奥利法去，由帕斯夸利先生负责带队。他们要向比安卡将军报到，听他调遣。我得向各位道别了，军情紧急。”于是这位干脆利落的将军不算不情愿地把这群人丢给了一位副官。
于是那五人当天舒舒服服地南下，因货车频频受阻，次日早晨抵达奥利法城。他们如期向比安卡将军报了到，却不听他的调遣。他们似乎忙着自己紧迫的私务，一转眼就消失在奥利法的地下世界，每晚在一条后街的某家咖啡馆碰头。

Yet there was nothing clandestine about their activity, for not a day passed without one or more of the five being closeted with high officials of the Army, the Marine, or the Police.
