Upon which Lucilla rose and kissed Aunt Jemima, though her head was all confused and she had noises in her ears. “I don’t think we are much like each other, you know,” she said. “Did you hear how Mrs. Chiley was? But after Mrs. John had gone away full of wonder at her philosophy, Lucilla drew close to the fire again and took her head between her hands and tried to think what it meant. Could it be true? Instead of the heiress, in a good position, who could go abroad or anywhere, and do anything she liked, was it possible that she was only a penniless single woman with nobody to look to, and nothing to live on? Such an extraordinary incomprehensible revolution might well make anyone feel giddy. The solid house and the comfortable room, and her own sober brain, which was not in the way of being put off its balance, seemed to turn round and round as she looked into the fire. Lucilla was not one to throw the blame upon her father, as Mrs. John had done. On the contrary she was sorry, profoundly sorry for him, and made such a picture to herself of what his feelings must have been, when he went into his room that night and knew that all his hard-earned fortune was gone, that it made her weep the deepest tears for him that she had yet shed. “Poor papa!” she said to herself; and as she was not much given to employing her imagination in this way, and realising the feeling of others, the effect was all the greater now. 随后露西拉把约翰·布朗进一步的细目摊开给她那位吃惊的听众。约翰太太自己靠一份不大不小的进项过活，对这二百镑一年就不那么看不上。“还有宅子，”她说——“宅子还能给你再添一百镑，露西拉。赖德夫妇，我敢说，立刻就会租下，说不定一大半家具也要了。三百镑，对一个单身女人不算坏了。家具的事你们说了没有，亲爱的？”吉米玛姨妈半带惋惜地补了一句，因为她实在觉得，失掉那只小橱柜是要可惜的。

“我想我就守在这宅子里，”露西拉说；“你可以当我傻，吉米玛姨妈，可我生在这里，而且——”

===Original===

“Stay in the house!” Mrs. John said, with a gasp. She did not think it silly, but simple madness, and so she told her niece. If Lucilla could not make up her mind to Elsworthy’s, there was Brighton and Bath and Cheltenham, and a hundred other places where a single woman might be very comfortable on three hundred a year. And to lose a third part of her income for a piece of sentiment was so utterly unlike any conception Aunt Jemima had ever formed of her niece. It *was* unlike Miss Marjoribanks; but there are times of life when even the most reasonable people are inconsistent. Lucilla, though she felt it was open to grave criticism, felt only more confirmed in her resolution by her aunt’s remarks. She heard a voice Aunt Jemima could not hear, and that voice said, Stay!

===Chinese===

“守在宅子里！”约翰太太倒抽一口气说。她不是当它傻，是当它十足的疯，并且这样告诉了她的甥女。露西拉若是横不下心去埃尔斯沃西那里，还有布莱顿、巴斯、切尔滕纳姆，还有一百个地方，单身女人靠三百镑一年可以过得很舒服。为一点情感上的事丢掉三分之一的进项——这全然不像吉米玛姨妈对她甥女从来的一切构想。这*确实*不像马乔里班克斯小姐；可人生有些时节，连最讲理的人也会不循理而行。露西拉虽然觉得这事尽可受人痛责，姨妈的一番话却只叫她的决心更坚定了。她听见一个吉米玛姨妈听不见的声音，那声音说：守住！
