## IV Sortie / IV 出击

===Original===
Five days later they came up from the south so that this time Shann’s view of the Terran camp was from a different angle. At first sight there had been little change in the general scene. He wondered if the aliens were using the Terran dome shelters themselves. Even in the twilight it was easy to pick out such landmarks as the com dome with the shaft of a broadcaster spearing from its top and the greater bulk of the supply warehouse.

“Two of their small flyers down on the landing field﻿ ﻿…” Thorvald materialized from the shadow, his voice a thread of whisper.

===Chinese===
五天后，他们从南边上来，于是这一次，沙恩是从另一个角度望见地球人营地的。乍看之下，整片景象几乎没什么变化。他揣摩着，那些外星人是不是正拿地球人的穹顶住所自用。即便天色已入暮，也不难辨认出那些地标：通讯穹顶——顶上一根广播天线杆直刺天际——还有体量更大的补给仓库。

“他们那两架小型飞行器落在降落场上了……”托尔瓦德从阴影里现出身形，嗓音细若一缕耳语。

===Original===
By Shann’s side the wolverines were moving restlessly. Since Taggi’s attack on the Throg neither beast would venture near any site where they could scent the aliens. This was the nearest point to which the men could urge either animal, which was a disappointment, for the wolverines would have been an excellent addition to the surprise sortie they planned for tonight, halving the danger for the men.

Shann ran his fingers across the coarse fur on the animals’ shoulders, exerting a light pressure to signal them to wait. But he was not sure of their obedience. The foray was a crazy idea, and Shann wondered again why he had agreed to it. Yet he had gone along with Thorvald, even suggested a few modifications and additions of his own, such as the contents of the crude leaf sack now resting between his knees.

===Chinese===
沙恩身边，两只狼獾躁动不安地挪动。自打塔吉扑袭过那个斯罗格人，这两只家伙说什么也不肯再靠近任何嗅得出外星人气味的地方。这里已是两个人能把它们往前劝到的极限——着实叫人失望，因为若能带上狼獾，今晚策划的这场出其不意的突袭必会如虎添翼，两人承担的风险也能减半。

沙恩的手指抚过两只动物肩上的粗毛，轻轻使了点劲儿，示意它们等候。可他没把握它们是否听话。这场奔袭本就是个疯狂的念头，沙恩又一次纳闷，自己当时怎么就答应了。然而他还是跟着托尔瓦德干了起来，甚至主动提出过几处修改和补充——比如此刻搁在他两膝之间的这只粗制叶袋里装的东西。

===Original===
Thorvald flitted away, seeking his own post to the west. Shann was still waiting for the other’s signal when there arose from the camp a sound to chill the flesh of any listener, a wail which could not have come from the throat of any normal living thing, intelligent being or animal. Ululating in ear-torturing intensity, the cry sank to a faint, ominous echo of itself, to waver up the scale again.

The wolverines went mad. Shann had witnessed their quick kills in the wilds, but this stark ferocity of spitting, howling rage was new. They answered that challenge from the camp, streaking out from under his hands. Yet both animals skidded to a stop before they passed the first dome and were lost in the gloom. A spark glowed for an instant to his right; Thorvald was ready to go, so Shann had no time to try and recall the animals.

===Chinese===
托尔瓦德闪身而去，到西边寻找自己的位置。沙恩还在等对方的信号，营地里却升起一个声音，任谁听了都会寒彻肌肤——那是一声哀嚎，绝不可能出自任何寻常生物的喉咙，无论是智慧种族还是野兽。嚎声凄厉刺耳，低伏下去时化作自身一缕微弱而不祥的回响，随即又颤巍巍地攀高。

狼獾疯了。沙恩在荒野里见过它们扑杀猎物的迅疾，可这般喷着涎沫、长嚎暴怒的赤裸凶态却是头一回见。它们应下营地传来的那声挑战，从他手底下一窜而出。然而还没跑过第一座穹顶，两只动物便急急刹住，随即没入昏暗之中。他右侧有一点火星倏然一亮——托尔瓦德已准备动手，沙恩再没工夫去追回它们了。

===Original===
He fumbled for those balls of soaked moss in his leaf bag. The chemical smell from them blotted out that alien mustiness which the wind brought from the campsite. Shann readied the first sopping mess in his sling, snapped his fire sparker at it, and had the ball awhirl for a toss almost in one continuous movement. The moss burst into fire as it curved out and fell.

To a witness it might have seemed that the missile materialized out of the air, the effect being better than Shann had hoped.

A second ball for the sling﻿—spark﻿ ﻿… out﻿ ﻿… down. The first had smashed on the ground near the dome of the com station, the force of impact flattening it into a round splatter of now fiercely burning material. And his second, carefully aimed, lit two feet beyond.

===Chinese===
他在叶袋里摸索出那几团浸透的苔藓球。球上散出的化学气味，压过了风从营地吹来的那股外星霉味。沙恩把第一团湿漉漉的玩意儿装上投石索（sling），打着打火器引燃，几乎一气呵成地抡圆掷出。苔藓球划出一道弧线，坠地时轰然起火。

在旁观者眼里，那枚弹体简直像凭空从空气里凝出来的——效果比沙恩指望的还要好。

投石索装上第二团——点火……掷出……落下。第一团砸在通讯站穹顶近旁的地面上，撞击之力把它摊成一摊圆形的东西，此刻正烧得凶猛。而他小心瞄准的第二团，落在两英尺开外。

===Original===
Another wail tearing at the nerves. Shann made a third throw, a fourth. He had an audience now. In the light of those pools of fire the Throgs were scuttling back and forth, their hunched bodies casting weird shadows on the dome walls. They were making efforts to douse the fires, but Shann knew from careful experimentation that once ignited the stuff he had skimmed from the lip of one of the hot springs would go on burning as long as a fraction of its viscid substance remained unconsumed.

Now Thorvald had gone into action. A Throg suddenly halted, struggled frantically, and toppled over into the edge of a fire splotch, legs looped together by the coils of the curious weapon Thorvald had put together on their first night of partnership. Three round stones of comparable weight had each been fastened at the end of a vine cord, and those cords united at a center point. Thorvald had demonstrated the effectiveness of his creation by bringing down one of the small “deer” of the grasslands, an animal normally fleet enough to feel safe from both human and animal pursuit. And those weighted ropes now trapped the Throg with the same efficiency.

===Chinese===
又一声嚎叫撕扯着人的神经。沙恩掷出第三团、第四团。这会儿他有观众了。在一汪汪火光的照映下，斯罗格人来回疾窜，弓耸的身躯在穹顶壁上投下幢幢怪影。他们正奋力扑火，但沙恩经过细心试验早有数：他从一处温泉边沿撇来的那种东西一经点燃，只要那黏稠浆体还有一小点没烧尽，就会一直烧下去。

这时托尔瓦德也出手了。一个斯罗格人猛地收步，拼命挣扎，一头栽进一片火迹的边缘——几圈绳索缠住了他的腿，那正是托尔瓦德在他们结伙的第一夜拼就的那件古怪武器。三块分量相仿的圆石，各自系在一根藤索的末端，几根藤索再汇结于中心一点。托尔瓦德曾用它放倒草原上的一种小“鹿”（deer），当场验证了这件造物的效力——那种动物四蹄如飞，寻常自信无论人类还是野兽都追它不上。而此刻，这几根系石绳索又以同样的高效困住了这个斯罗格人。

===Original===
Having shot his last fireball, Shann ran swiftly to take up a new position, downgrade and to the east of the domes. Here he put into action another of the primitive weapons Thorvald had devised, a spear hurled with a throwing stick, giving it double range and twice as forceful penetration power. The spears themselves were hardly more than crudely shaped lengths of wood, their points charred in the fire. Perhaps these missiles could neither kill nor seriously wound. But more than one thudded home in a satisfactory fashion against the curving back carapace or the softer front parts of a Throg in a manner which certainly shook up and bruised the target. And one of Shann’s victims went to the ground, to lie kicking in a way which suggested he had been more than just bruised.

Fireballs, spears Thorvald had moved too. And now down into the somewhat frantic melee of the aroused camp fell a shower of slim weighted reeds, each provided with a clay-ball head. The majority of those balls broke on landing as the Terrans had intended. So, through the beetle smell of the aliens, spread the acrid, throat-parching fumes of the hot spring water. Whether those fumes had the same effect upon Throg breathing apparatus as they did upon Terran, the attackers could not tell, but they hoped such a bombardment would add to the general confusion.

===Chinese===
抛完最后一个火球，沙恩飞快跑去抢占新位置——在穹顶群的下坡、偏东一侧。在这里，他启用了托尔瓦德设计的又一件原始武器：借投矛器（throwing stick）掷出的长矛，射程加倍，穿透力也强上一倍。矛本身不过是粗粗削成的木棍，矛尖在火里炙得焦硬。这些武器也许既杀不了命，也造不成重伤。但不止一支矛令人满意地闷闷砸中斯罗格人弓曲的背甲或较软的前身，顿教目标震荡受创、乌青一片。沙恩手下的一个受害者更扑倒在地，躺着乱蹬乱踢——那副样子，可不像只挨了几块淤伤。

火球、长矛——托尔瓦德那边也换上了新招。此刻，一阵细长带坠的苇矛又落进惊起的营地那片乱作一团的混战之中，每根苇杆顶上都安着一枚陶丸。那些陶丸多半一落地便应声碎裂，正合两个地球人的心意。于是，透过外星人身上那股甲虫气味，温泉水辛辣呛喉的烟气四下漫开。这些烟气对斯罗格人的呼吸器官是否也像对地球人那样奏效，进攻者无从知晓，但他们指望这轮“弹幕”能给本已够乱的场面再添几分乱。

===Original===
Shann began to space the hurling of his crude spears with more care, trying to place them with all the precision of aim he could muster. There was a limit to their amount of varied ammunition, although they had dedicated every waking moment of the past few days to manufacture and testing. Luckily the enemy had had none of their energy beams at the domes. And so far they had made no move to lift their flyers for retaliation blasts.

But the Throgs were pulling themselves into order. Blaster fire cut the dusk. Most of the aliens were now flat on the ground, sending a creeping line of fire into the perimeter of the camp area. A dark form moved between Shann and the nearest patch of burning moss. The Terran raised a spear to the ready before he caught a whiff of the pungent scent emitted by a wolverine hot with battle rage. He whistled coaxingly. With the Throgs eager to blast any moving thing, the animals were in danger if they prowled about the scene.

===Chinese===
沙恩开始更讲究地掌握粗矛掷出的间隔，竭尽自己所能集聚的瞄准功夫，力求投投落点精准。他们五花八门的弹药终究存量有限，尽管过去这几天，他们把醒着的每一刻都花在了制造和试验上。所幸敌人没在穹顶上架起能量炮。而且到眼下为止，他们也没有升空飞行器实施报复性轰击的动静。

可斯罗格人正在收拢阵脚。爆能枪的火力切开了暮色。大多数外星人此刻平贴在地，朝营区外围射出一条匍匐推进的火线。一团黑影在沙恩与最近一片燃烧的苔藓之间移动。地球人先端矛待发，随后才嗅到一缕刺鼻的气味——那是一只狼獾战怒正炽时发出的体味。他打出一声哄劝的呼哨。斯罗格人正巴不得轰掉一切会动的东西，动物们若在这一带逡巡，可就危险了。

===Original===
That blunt head moved. Shann caught the glint of eyes in a furred mask; it was either Taggi or his mate. Then a puff of mixed Throg and chemical scent from the camp must have reached the wolverine. The animal coughed and fled westward, passing Shann.

Had Thorvald had time and opportunity to make his planned raid on the supply dome? Time during such an embroilment was hard to measure, and Shann could not be sure. He began to count aloud, slowly, as they had agreed. When he reached one hundred he would begin his retreat; on two hundred he was to run for it, his goal the river a half mile from the camp.

===Chinese===
那颗钝脑袋动了。沙恩瞥见毛面上闪着一点眼光——不是塔吉，便是它的老伴。随后，营地里那股混着斯罗格气味与化学气味的味道，想必随一阵风钻进了狼獾的鼻子。那动物咳了一声，打沙恩身边掠过，向西逃去。

托尔瓦德有没有时间、有没有机会对补给穹顶下手，实施他计划中的那场偷袭？这般混战里的时间难于估量，沙恩说不准。他开始照事先的约定，缓缓地出声计数。数到一百，他便开始撤退；数到两百，就得没命地跑——目标是离营地半英里的那条河。

===Original===
The stream would take the fugitives to the sea where fjords cut the coastline into a ragged fringe offering a wealth of hiding places. Throgs seldom explored any territory on foot. For them to venture into that maze would be putting themselves at the mercy of the Terrans they hunted. And their flyers could comb the air above such a rocky wilderness without result.

Shann reached the count of one hundred. Twice a blaster bolt singed ground within distance close enough to make him wince, but most of the fire carried well above his head. All of his spears were gone, save for one he had kept, hoping for a last good target. One of the Throgs who appeared to be directing the fire of the others was facing Shann’s position. And on pure chance that he might knock out that leader, Shann chose him for his victim.

===Chinese===
这条河将载着逃亡者们入海——海边，一道道峡湾（fjord）把海岸线割成一绺参差的须边，有的是藏身之处。斯罗格人极少徒步勘察任何地界。他们若冒险闯进那座迷宫，无异于把自己交到所追猎的地球人手里任凭摆布。而他们的飞行器在那片礁石莽原上空梳篦来去，也终将一无所获。

沙恩数到了一百。爆能枪的能量束两度灼过近旁的地面，近得让他咧嘴缩身，但大部分火力都远远高过他的头顶飞了过去。他的矛已全部掷完，只留下一支，指望着最后一个好靶子。一个看样子正指挥同伴射击的斯罗格人，恰恰面对着沙恩的方位。沙恩纯赌一线机会——兴许能敲掉那个头目——便选中他当了自己的猎物。

===Original===
The Terran had no illusions concerning his own marksmanship. The most he could hope for, he thought, was to have the primitive weapon thud home painfully on the other’s armored hide. Perhaps, if he were very lucky, he could knock the other from his clawed feet. But that chance which hovers over any battlefield turned in Shann’s favor. At just the right moment the Throg stretched his head up from the usual hunched position where the carapace extended over his wide shoulders to protect one of the alien’s few vulnerable spots, the soft underside of his throat. And the fire-sharpened point of the spear went deep.

Throgs were mute, or at least none of them had ever uttered a vocal sound to be reported by Terrans. This one did not cry out. But he staggered forward, forelimbs up, clawed digits pulling at the wooden pin transfixing his throat just under the mandible-equipped jaw, holding his head at an unnatural angle. Without seeming to notice the others of his kind, the Throg came on at a shambling run, straight at Shann as if he could actually see through the dark and had marked down the Terran for personal vengeance. There was something so uncanny about that forward dash that Shann retreated. As his hand groped for the knife at his belt his boot heel caught in a tangle of weed and he struggled for balance. The wounded Throg, still pulling at the spear shaft protruding above the swelling barrel of his chest, pounded on.

===Chinese===
对自己的枪法，这个地球人从不存幻想。他想，最好的指望，也不过是让这件原始武器闷闷地砸在对方的装甲皮壳上，教它吃痛。运气再好些，或许能把对手从那双带爪的脚上掀翻。可任何战场上空都悬着的那点运气，这回偏向了沙恩。恰在那一瞬，那斯罗格人从惯常的弓伏姿态里探起了头——平日里，背甲覆着他宽阔的双肩，护住这种外星人寥寥几处弱点之一：喉头的软腹——而火中炙出尖锋的矛头，深深扎了进去。

斯罗格人是哑的，至少就地球人所报，从没有哪一个发出过一点嗓音。这一只也没有喊出声。他踉跄着向前，前肢高举，带爪的指头扯着那根洞穿喉咙的木楔——楔子正卡在生着大颚的颔下，把他的脑袋拗成一个不自然的角度。那斯罗格人仿佛全没理会自己的同类，一瘸一拐地跑着直冲过来，直取沙恩，倒像他真能看穿黑暗，已把这个地球人记下，要向他讨还个人的血债。这一路猛冲透着说不出的邪气，沙恩不由得退了。他伸手去摸腰间的刀，靴跟却绊进一团乱草，他趔趄着找平衡。那受伤的斯罗格人仍旧扯着从自己鼓凸的桶状胸膛上戳出来的矛杆，咚咚地步步逼近。

===Original===
Shann sprawled backward and was caught in the elastic embrace of a bush, so he did not strike the ground. He fought the grip of prickly branches and kicked to gain solid earth under his feet. Then again he heard that piercing wail from the camp, as chilling as it had been the first time. Spurred by that, he won free. But he could not turn his back on the wounded Throg, keeping rather a sidewise retreat.

Already the alien had reached the dark beyond the rim of the camp. His progress now was marked by the crashing through low brush. Two of the Throgs back on the firing line started up after their leader. Shann caught a whiff of their odor as the wounded alien advanced with the single-mindedness of a robot.

===Chinese===
沙恩仰面栽倒，被一丛灌木弹性的怀抱兜住，总算没磕在地上。他挣开多刺枝条的纠缠，蹬踏着想找到脚下坚实的地面。恰在这时，营地那边又响起那声穿耳的哀嚎，同第一回一样令人胆寒。受这一激，他挣脱了出来。但他不敢把后背亮给那个受伤的斯罗格人，宁可侧着身子后退。

那外星人已经抵达营地边缘之外的黑暗。他此刻的行进，全凭撞开矮树丛的哗啦声来标记。火力线那头的两个斯罗格人一跃而起，追赶他们的头领。受伤的外星人抱着机器人般的一根筋步步进逼，沙恩又闻到了他们那股气味。

===Original===
It would be best to head for the river. Tall grass twisted about the Terran’s legs as he began to run. In spite of the gloom, he hesitated to cross that open space. At night Warlock’s peculiar vegetation displayed a very alien attribute﻿—ten﻿ ﻿… twenty varieties of grass, plant, and tree emitted a wan phosphorescence, varying in degree, but affording each an aura of light. And the path before Shann now was dotted by splotches of that radiance, not as brilliant as the chemical-born flames the attackers had kindled in the camp, but as quick to betray the unwary who passed within their dim circles. And there had never been any reason to believe that Throg powers of sight were less than human; there was perhaps some evidence to the contrary. Shann crouched, charting the clumps ahead for a zigzag course which would take him to at least momentary safety in the river bed.

===Chinese===
还是奔河边去最妥。高草缠绕着地球人的双腿，他开始奔跑。尽管天色晦暗，要横穿那片开阔地，他仍不免踌躇。入夜之后，沃洛克特有的草木会显出一种十足异星的品性——十来种……二十来种草、灌、树都散发着惨淡的磷光，明暗程度不一，却各自笼出一圈光晕。此刻沙恩面前的路径上，正星星点点布着那种光斑，虽不及进攻者在营地点起的化学火焰那般明亮，却一样会立刻出卖踏入那圈微光的不小心的人。何况，从来就没有理由相信斯罗格人的视力不如人类，说不定还有相反的证据。沙恩伏低身子，把前方一丛丛光点看在眼里，规划出一条之字形的路线，好带他抵达河床中——至少是片刻的安全。

===Original===
Perhaps a mile downstream was the transport the Terrans had cobbled together no earlier than this afternoon, a raft Thorvald had professed to believe would support them to the sea which lay some fifty Terran miles to the west. But now he had to cover that mile.

The wolverines? Thorvald? There was one lure which might draw the animals on to the rendezvous. Taggi had brought down a “deer” just before they had left the raft. And instead of allowing both beasts to feast at leisure, Shann had lashed the carcass to the shaky platform of wood and brush, putting it out to swing in the current, though still moored to the bank.

Wolverines always cached that part of the kill which they did not consume at the first eating, usually burying it. He had hoped that to leave the carcass in such a way would draw both animals back to the raft when they were hungry. And they had not fed particularly well that day.
