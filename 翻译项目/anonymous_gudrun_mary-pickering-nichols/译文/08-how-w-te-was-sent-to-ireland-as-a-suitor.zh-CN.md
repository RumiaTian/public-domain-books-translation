## Tale V How Wâ-te Was Sent to Ireland as a Suitor / 第五诗章 瓦特如何奉命前往爱尔兰求亲

===Original===
Hettel, king of the Hegelings, a suitor for Hilda, summons his vassals, Wâ-te, Horant, and Fru-te, and sends them to obtain her for his wife. They go, purporting to be merchants, carrying seven hundred warriors concealed in the hold of the ship. They send rich gifts to Hagen, and at the request of his daughter, Hilda, they are kindly received at court.

===Chinese===
黑格林人（Hegelings）之王黑特尔（Hettel）向希尔达（Hilda）求亲，召集封臣瓦特（Wâ-te）、霍兰特（Horant）与弗鲁特（Fru-te），派他们前去为他娶她为妻。三人假扮商人前往，船舱中暗藏七百武士。他们向哈根（Hagen）进献厚礼，又应其女希尔达之请，在宫廷受到殷勤接待。

===Original===
Hettel was lord in Daneland; to be its king he rose;
’Twas in the Sturmisch marches, as many a one well knows;
There abode his kindred, who ways of honor taught him.
Ortland also served him. His might and worth high fame with all soon brought him.

===Chinese===
黑特尔本是丹麦兰（Daneland）之主，后来登基为王；
那是在施图尔姆边区，世人多半知晓；
他的亲族世居彼处，以荣誉之道教诲于他。
奥特兰（Ortland）也臣服于他。他的威权与德望，不久便为他赢得众口交誉。

===Original===
One among his kinsfolk the name of Wâ-te bore;
He for his lands and castles fealty to Hettel swore.
As kinsman of his master, he careful teaching gave him
In all things good and worthy, and in his watchful care did ever have him.

===Chinese===
亲族中有一人，名唤瓦特；
他为自己的领地与城堡，向黑特尔宣誓效忠。
身为君主的尊长亲眷，他谆谆施教，
授以一切善良高贵之事，并将他时时置于自己的悉心看顾之下。

===Original===
A landed knight in Daneland was Wâ-te’s sister’s son,
The brave and upright Horant. Later his faith was shown
Unto his lord, King Hettel, who for his worth did crown him.
This to him he grudged not, but ever for a prince was glad to own him.

===Chinese===
丹麦兰有位拥有领地的骑士，是瓦特的外甥，
那勇敢正直的霍兰特。后来他的忠贞
彰显于其君主黑特尔王之前，君主因他的贤德为他授爵加冕。
他对此毫无怨言，反倒终生庆幸得奉这样一位君王。

===Original===
Hettel, rich and mighty, at Hegeling held his seat,
Not far from the lord of Ortland; this is true, I weet.
He there owned many castles, eighty at least or over;
They who these strongholds guarded in truest faith and honor held them ever.

===Chinese===
富有而威武的黑特尔，在黑格林坐拥王座，
离奥特兰之主不远；此话千真万确，我深知晓。
他在那里坐拥众多城堡，至少八十座犹不止；
守卫这些要塞的人们，以最真的忠义与荣誉长久保着它们。

===Original===
Lord he was of Friesland, its waters and its land;
Ditmarsh, as well as Waleis, were swayed by his kingly hand.
Hettel was truly mighty; his kinsmen they were many;
Bold was he and daring, and ’gainst his foes he plotted, well as any.

===Chinese===
他又是弗里西亚（Friesland）之主，统辖其海陆；
迪特马什（Ditmarsh）连同瓦莱斯（Waleis），都受他王者之手的节制。
黑特尔着实权势煊赫；他的亲族为数众多；
他勇武果敢，对付仇敌的谋略，不输当世任何豪杰。

===Original===
Hettel was an orphan, and so he felt the need
That he a wife should find him. To him, at last, were dead
Father as well as mother, who their lands had left him.
He friends in truth had many, yet found he much in life that of bliss bereft him.

===Chinese===
黑特尔是个孤儿，因而深感
有必要为自己觅一妻子。到他那时，父母
皆已亡故，只把领地留给了他。
他的朋友确实很多，却仍发觉人生中颇多事物，使他失去了欢愉。

===Original===
The best of these besought him some maiden’s love to seek,
Who of his birth was worthy. The knight did answer make:
“I here know none who fitly should be o’er the Hegelings seated,
Nor is there any lady who, brought from far, should as my queen be greeted.”

===Chinese===
其中最亲近的朋友们劝他寻访某位少女的爱情，
要与他的出身相称。骑士如此答道：
「我于此间实不知有哪位女子，配君临黑格林人之上，
也没有哪位淑媛，能从远方迎来、被尊为我的王后。」

===Original===
Then spake a knight of Nifland, Morunc, a youthful lord:
“I know of a lovely maiden, of whom I oft have heard;
She in truth is fairer than all on earth now living.
Her will we gladly sue for, that she her troth to you may soon be giving.”

===Chinese===
这时一位尼夫兰（Nifland）骑士开言，莫伦克（Morunc），一位年轻的贵胄：
「我知道一位可爱的少女，常听人说起她；
她实在比世上一切在世之人都更美。
我们乐意代您向她求亲，好让她早日把终身许给您。」

===Original===
Then quoth the king: “Who is she? her name I pray you tell.”
Then said Morunc: “ ’Tis Hilda, in Ireland she doth dwell;
Her father’s name is Hagen; King Ger was her forefather.
If to this land she cometh, your life will then be blissful altogether.”

===Chinese===
国王又道：「她是谁？请把她的名字相告。」
莫伦克答道：「她就是希尔达，居住在爱尔兰；
她父亲名叫哈根；格尔王是她的先祖。
她若来到这片国土，您的一生从此圆满幸福。」

===Original===
Then spake the young King Hettel: “I oft have heard it said,
Whoever woos this maiden her father’s wrath must dread.
Many a worthy suitor his life for her has ended;
But none among my vassals must meet his death for having me befriended.”

===Chinese===
年轻的黑特尔王说道：「我常听人说，
谁若向这位少女求亲，就必畏惧她父亲的震怒。
多少高贵的求亲者，为她断送了性命；
但我的封臣之中，谁也不得因为与我为友而送命。」

===Original===
Morunc quickly answered: “Then send to Horant’s land,
And bid that he come hither; he well doth understand
The ways and moods of Hagen, for often has he seen them.
Unless his help he gives you, ’twill come to nought, howe’er your friends demean them.”

===Chinese===
莫伦克马上答道：「那就遣使去霍兰特的封地，
召他来此；他深谙
哈根的习性与脾性，因为他屡屡见识。
没有他出手相助，任凭您的亲友如何尽力，也是徒劳。」

===Original===
He said: “Your will I follow, since she is so fair;
But if my friends shall seek her, yourself the suit must share;
And if unto your friendship the task I’ve trusted fitly,
Wealth shall you have and honor, when as the Hegeling’s queen she’s greeted rightly.”

===Chinese===
他说：「既然她如此美貌，我愿依从你的主意；
但若由我的友人们去求她，你自己也须分担这桩使命；
我既把这差事托付给你的情谊，
待她被堂堂正正迎为黑格林人的王后，你必得财富与荣耀。」

===Original===
He quickly sent out riders through the Danish land to haste;
By them was the mighty Horant, his nephew, found at last,
And to the court was bidden; to come must he be speedy,
Within seven days, not later, if he to help his lord in truth were ready.

===Chinese===
他火速派出骑士，疾驰遍及丹麦之地；
他们终于寻到豪杰霍兰特，他的外甥，
并宣他进宫；他必须从速前来，
七日之内，不得延误，若他当真愿意相助他的君主。

===Original===
When Horant met the heralds, and did their errand hear,
Then for friendly service himself he would not spare.
Right gladly did he listen to the bidding of his master;
But this, on a day thereafter, to him brought sorrow great, and sore disaster.

===Chinese===
霍兰特迎接信使，听罢他们的使命，
为尽朋友的情谊，他决不惜己身。
他欣然领受君主的吩咐；
然而日后某天，这事却给他带来巨大的悲伤与严酷的灾殃。

===Original===
To the court he soon went riding, with sixty of his men;
Of friends at home young Horant to take his leave was seen.
He then made haste the faster, when now the tale was told him
How he must help his master, if for a faithful knight he now would hold him.

===Chinese===
他很快便骑马赴宫廷，带着六十名部众；
人们看着年轻的霍兰特在家乡辞别亲友。
当来意向他言明，他如何必须帮助君主——
若君主仍视他为忠贞的骑士——他便愈发兼程赶路。

===Original===
Upon the seventh morning he came to Hettel’s land;
Decked in finest clothing was he and all his band.
The king to welcome Horant rode forth, most glad to greet him,
And saw that with him Fru-te, another Danish knight, was there to meet him.

===Chinese===
第七天早晨，他抵达黑特尔的国土；
他与全体随从都身披华服。
国王为迎霍兰特而出驰，喜不自胜，
又看见与他同来的还有弗鲁特，另一位丹麦骑士。

===Original===
Good news it was of their coming, of which all men now spoke;
Glad was the king to see them; from him a share it took
Of the deep and heavy sorrow which his heart was filling.
“Welcome, Cousin Fru-te!” cried he, the while he looked upon him smiling.

===Chinese===
他们到来的喜讯，如今人人传扬；
国王喜见他们；这喜讯也从他心头
卸下了几分深沉而沉重的忧伤。
「欢迎你，弗鲁特贤弟！」他望着来客含笑高呼。

===Original===
When Horant now with Fru-te before the king did stand,
Then he asked for tidings of their home in the Danish land.
Both of them now answered: “Not many days are ended
Since we in stormy battle with many deadly blows our lives defended.”

===Chinese===
当霍兰特与弗鲁特同立于国王面前，
他便询问他们丹麦故乡的消息。
两人齐声答道：「距今没有几天，
我们还在险恶的鏖战中，顶着致命的刀剑护卫性命。」

===Original===
He asked whence they had ridden from off the stormy field.
They said: “It was from Portugal, where the strife was held;
There the mighty ruler from fighting would not spare us;
Daily within our borders he did us wrong, and much ill-will did bear us.”

===Chinese===
他问他们从哪片鏖战之野驰来。
他们答道：「是从葡萄牙，战事就发生在那里；
那强横的君主不肯放过我们，不容我们休战；
他日日侵犯我们的边境，加害于我们，对我们深怀恶意。」

===Original===
The young King Hettel answered: “Now cast all care away;
I know that the aged Wâ-te will never yield the sway
He holds o’er the Sturmisch marches; he of the land is owner;
Who wins from him a castle will earn high praise and long be held in honor.”

===Chinese===
年轻的黑特尔王答道：「如今且把忧心统统抛开；
我知道年迈的瓦特绝不会放弃
他握有的施图尔姆边区的权柄；他是那片土地的主人；
谁能从他手里夺得一座城堡，必赢得盛誉，长久受人尊崇。」

===Original===
Within the roomy palace the guests then took their seats.
Both Horant and Sir Fru-te with thoughtless, merry wits,
Of the loves of high-born ladies began to gossip gaily.
To them the young king listened, and costly gifts he gave unto them freely.

===Chinese===
宽敞的宫殿里，宾客们随即落座。
霍兰特与弗鲁特爵士兴致盎然，妙语连珠，
兴致勃勃地闲谈起高贵淑媛们的情事。
年轻的国王听他们谈笑，慷慨地赠给他们贵重的礼物。

===Original===
Hettel turned to Horant, and thus to ask began:
“If aught hath reached your hearing, then tell me, if you can,
How ’tis with Lady Hilda, King Hagen’s lovely daughter?
To her would I send most gladly, and would that words of love from me were brought her.”

===Chinese===
黑特尔转向霍兰特，如此问道：
「你若有所耳闻，就请告诉我，倘若你能，
希尔达小姐、哈根王的爱女近况如何？
我很乐意遣人去她那里，恨不得把我的爱语捎给她。」

===Original===
The youthful knight then answered: “She is to me well known;
A maid so fair and lovely my eyes ne’er looked upon
As she, that maid of Ireland, Hilda, the rich and stately,
The daughter of wild Hagen; to wear a crown with you would befit her greatly.”

===Chinese===
年轻的骑士答道：「她与我相熟；
如此美丽可爱的少女，我的眼睛从未见过
像她那样：那位爱尔兰少女，希尔达，雍容华贵，
暴烈的哈根之女；她与您共戴王冠，再相称不过。」

===Original===
On this King Hettel asked him: “Now think you, can it be
That ever her lordly father will give this maid to me?
If I deemed he were so friendly, I would seek to win her,
And would reward him ever who gave to me his ready help to gain her.”

===Chinese===
于是黑特尔王问他：「依你看，可有指望，
她那威严的父亲终有一天肯把这少女许给我？
我若认为他这般友善，我便去设法赢得她，
并永远酬谢那肯助我赢得她的人。」

===Original===
“That can never happen,” to him young Horant said:
“No rider with this errand to Hagen need be sped.
To hasten thither boldly I feel, myself, no longing;
The man sent there to seek her is either slain with blows, or dies by hanging.”

===Chinese===
「那绝无可能，」年轻的霍兰特对他说：
「担着这桩差事的骑士，不必被派往哈根那里。
要我奋勇赶去那边，我自己也毫无热望；
被派去求她的人，不是死于乱刃之下，就是被绞死。」

===Original===
Then spake again King Hettel: “Not so for her I care;
To hang my trusty vassal should Hagen ever dare,
Then he, the king of Ireland, himself must death be facing.
Be he ne’er so boastful, he’ll find his rashness is to him no blessing.”

===Chinese===
黑特尔王又说道：「为了她，我不在乎这些；
哈根若敢绞死我忠勇的封臣，
那么他这爱尔兰之王，自己就得面对死亡。
任他如何自夸，他终将发现，鲁莽于他绝非幸事。」

===Original===
Then spake the knightly Fru-te: “If Wâ-te deigns to go
Unto the king of Ireland, to woo this maid for you,
Lucky will be our errand, and we shall bring the lady;
Or wounds throughout our bodies, e’en to the heart, to take shall we be ready.”

===Chinese===
骑士弗鲁特于是说：「倘若瓦特肯屈尊
前往爱尔兰国王那里，为您向这位少女求亲，
我们的差事便算走运，我们定会把小姐迎来；
不然，纵使浑身创痕，直抵心口，我们也甘愿领受。」

===Original===
Then said to him King Hettel: “My men I now will send
With word to the lord of Sturmland; I do not fear the end,
For Wâ-te will hasten gladly wherever I shall bid him.
Bring Irold, too, from Friesland, with all his men, for sorely do I need him.”

===Chinese===
黑特尔王对他说：「我这就派人
传话给施图尔姆兰（Sturmland）之主；我不怕这结局，
因为无论我差遣瓦特去哪里，他都欣然从命。
也把伊罗尔德（Irold）连同他的全部人马从弗里西亚带来，我正急需他。」

===Original===
His riders then went quickly into the Sturmisch land,
Where the brave old Wâ-te they found among his band.
Then the word they gave him, now to the king to betake him;
But Wâ-te felt great wonder, to know for what the Hegeling king did seek him.

===Chinese===
他的骑士们随即疾驰进入施图尔姆之地，
在部众之中寻见了豪勇的老瓦特。
他们传下口信，命他即刻去见国王；
瓦特心中大为诧异，不知黑格林人之王找他所为何事。

===Original===
He asked if it were needful to bring, when he should go,
His breastplate and his helmet, and any followers, too?
One of the heralds answered: “We did not hear it spoken
That he had need of fighters; for you alone did his words a wish betoken.”

===Chinese===
他问此去是否需要
带上胸甲和头盔，还要不要带些随从？
一名信使答道：「我们未曾听说
他需要武士；他的话里，只表达了请您一人前往的意愿。」

===Original===
Wâ-te would be going, but left behind a guard,
To care for lands and castles. Then taking horse, at his word,
Twelve of his followers only with him from home now started;
Wâ-te, the brave old warrior, at once on his way to court in haste departed.

===Chinese===
瓦特准备启程，但留下一支卫队，
照管领地与城堡。随后他一声号令，策马上路，
只有十二名部下随他离家启行；
豪勇的老将瓦特，当即兼程驰向宫廷。

===Original===
He reached the land of the Hegelings. When he now was seen,
As he came near Kampatille, but little sorrow, I ween,
Was felt by the kingly Hettel; with speed he went to greet him,
And thought of the kindly welcome he would give his friend, old Wâ-te, when he met him.

===Chinese===
他抵达黑格林人的国土。当他的身影一现，
行近坎帕蒂勒（Kampatille）时，据我看来，君王黑特尔
心中几乎不存一丝忧烦；他疾趋相迎，
盘算着与老友瓦特相见时，要如何殷勤接待。

===Original===
Right glad was he to see him; with hearty speech he says:
“Sir Wâ-te, thou art welcome; many are the days
Since I have looked upon you, when on our horses sitting,
Side by side together, we proudly met our foes with blows befitting.”

===Chinese===
见到他，国王满心欢喜；他热忱地说道：
「瓦特爵士，欢迎你；距离我上一次见到你，
已过去多少时日；那时我们跨坐马上，
并肩而行，以相称的刀剑骄傲地迎战仇敌。」

===Original===
Then answered him old Wâ-te: “Ever should good friends
Be glad to be together; that fight the better ends
Where, before the foeman, friends as one are fighting.”
Then by the hand he held him, to him his love and friendship warmly plighting.

===Chinese===
老瓦特答道：「好友相聚，
本该永远欢欣；面对仇敌，
众友如一人并力奋战的战斗，才有好的收场。」
说罢他握住国王的手，向他热烈地申明爱与情谊。

===Original===
They took their seats together, nor place to other gave.
Hettel, he was mighty, and Wâ-te, he was brave;
He yet was also haughty, and proud in all his bearing.
Hettel now was thinking how Wâ-te could be brought to Ireland to be faring.

===Chinese===
他们并肩落座，不把位子让给旁人。
黑特尔权势煊赫，瓦特则骁勇非凡；
瓦特却又性子倨傲，举止间处处透着骄矜。
黑特尔此刻思忖着，如何能促成瓦特前往爱尔兰之行。

===Original===
Then spake the knightly Hettel: “For this I bade thee come;
Need have I of riders, to send to Hagen’s home.
Truly I know of no one whom I would send the sooner
Than thee, my good friend Wâ-te, or who in this could bring me greater honor.”

===Chinese===
骑士黑特尔说道：「我召你来，正为此事；
我需要骑士，差往哈根的家邦。
说实话，我不知还有谁比你更让我愿遣，
我的好友瓦特，也没有谁能在此事上为我赢得更大的荣光。」

===Original===
Then said the aged Wâ-te: “Whatever I can do
To show my love and fealty, I’ll gladly do for you.
Herein I may be trusted, to be for you bold-hearted;
And to bring about your wishes, unless in this by death I should be thwarted.”

===Chinese===
年迈的瓦特说：「凡我所能做的、
能表明我的爱与忠忱之事，我都乐为您效劳。
这一点尽可信任我，我必为您奋不顾身，
定要成全您的心愿，除非死亡从中阻挠。」

===Original===
Then quoth the kingly Hettel: “Many friends have said
That if the mighty Hagen will my wooing heed,
And give to me his daughter, she, as my queen, would honor
Me and my kingdom also; my heart is bent as a wife and queen to own her.”

===Chinese===
君王黑特尔又道：「许多朋友说过，
倘若强横的哈根肯垂顾我的求亲，
把女儿许给我，她成为我的王后，
将为我和我的王国增光；我心意已决，要娶她为妻、立她为后。」

===Original===
Angrily spoke Wâ-te: “Whoever this has said
Would truly feel no sorrow if I this day were dead.
’Tis Fru-te, he of Denmark, I know it is no other,
Who to this has stirred you, to send me to the maid, your suit to further.

===Chinese===
瓦特怒道：「说这话的人，
倘若我今天死了，准不会有一丝难过。
那是弗鲁特，丹麦的那位，我知道不是别人，
是他撺掇您这样，派我去那少女那里，好推进您的求亲。

Queen Hilda and her daughter to talk with Morunc’s knights were never weary.

「你一心要做学生，可你其实是我教过的
令仇敌目眩神摇的剑技中最出色的一个。
无论你在何处征战，都必在战场上赢得无上的赞誉。」

===Original===
Then to the king spake Irold: “My lord, the strife is done
That you so well were waging; such fights have we seen won,
In the land of our king and master. Oft, at home, we freely
Try our skill with weapons; knights and squires there meet in matches daily.”

===Chinese===
伊罗尔德对国王说：「主上，这场较量
您打得如此精彩，如今胜负已分；这样的比武，
在我们君王的国土上，我们也见过胜出。在我们家乡，
人们常随意以兵刃切磋；骑士与扈从天天聚场较艺。」

===Original===
Then again spoke Hagen: “Did I this understand,
I never a fighting weapon had taken in my hand.
No youth have I ever met with who was so quick at learning.”
When to these words they listened, the face of many a one to smiles was turning.

===Chinese===
哈根又说道：「早知如此，
我决不会把兵刃握到手里。
这样一点就透的年轻人，我还从未遇见过。」
众人听了这话，许多人脸上都绽开了笑容。

===Original===
Now by the king ’twas granted to his guests to pass the day
As they might all be choosing. Glad of this were they,
The men from out the Northland. When the hours grew weary,
They vied huge stones in hurling; or else in shooting arrows made them merry.

===Chinese===
国王随即恩准他的客人们，
随心所欲地消磨这一天。北方来的人们
听了都很欢喜。等到时辰渐生倦意，
他们便比赛投掷巨石，或是射箭取乐。
