## Tale XXV How Herwic and Ortwin Found Gudrun / 第二十五诗章 赫尔维克与奥特温如何找到古德伦

===Original===
Herwic and Ortwin, coming in a boat, see the maidens washing on the shore, recognize them, and make themselves known. Thinking it dishonorable to take the maids away without winning them in fight, the knights return to bring their army. The maidens go back to Gerlind, and, feeling sure of her speedy rescue, Gudrun pretends to agree to Hartmut’s suit, and is restored to favor.

===Chinese===
赫尔维克（Herwic）与奥特温（Ortwin）乘船而来，看见少女们正在岸边洗衣，认出了她们，并表明了自己的身份。两位骑士认为，不经战斗赢得便将少女们带走有损荣誉，于是回去调集大军。少女们回到格尔林德（Gerlind）处；古德伦（Gudrun）确信自己很快就会获救，便假意应承哈特穆特（Hartmut）的求婚，重新得回了恩宠。

===Original===
After they long had waited,

 Two in a boat fast nearing;
 others were there none.

===Chinese===

此外再无别人。

===Original===
 Then said the maiden, Hildeburg,

 “These two are sailing hither;
 perhaps the friends sent here are come already.”

少女希尔德堡（Hildeburg）随即
