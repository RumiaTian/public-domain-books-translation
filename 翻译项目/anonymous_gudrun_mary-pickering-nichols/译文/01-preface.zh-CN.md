## Preface / 前言

===Original===
The epic poem of *Gudrun* is one of the most important early literary works of the German race. It is attributed to the latter part of the twelfth or the beginning of the thirteenth century, and to a date a little subsequent to that of the *Nibelungenlied*. It was first brought to the notice of the modern world in the year 1817, the only original manuscript now known to exist having been discovered about that time in the castle Ambras in the Tyrol, among other manuscripts which had been collected by the Emperor Maximilian I (1493﻿–﻿1519). The manuscript is now in the Imperial Library at Vienna. It has been several times edited and printed in the original Middle High German, with critical annotations; various translations into modern German have also been published: but so far as I can learn, no complete metrical version in the English language has been made public.

===Chinese===
史诗《古德伦》（Gudrun）是日耳曼民族最重要的早期文学作品之一。其创作年代被断为十二世纪末或十三世纪初，略晚于《尼伯龙根之歌》（Nibelungenlied）。1817 年，这部史诗首次进入现代世界的视野：现今已知存世的唯一原始手稿，约于其时在蒂罗尔（Tyrol）的安布拉斯城（Ambras）被发现，与皇帝马克西米利安一世（Maximilian I，1493–1519）所搜集的其他手稿收存一处。该手稿今藏于维也纳的帝国图书馆。它已多次以中古高地德语原文校勘刊印，并附评注；现代德语的各种译本亦已出版；但据我所知，此前尚无完整的英文韵体译本公之于世。

===Original===
The name of the author is unknown; it is generally thought to have been constructed, in great measure, from earlier legends which had been repeated by wandering singers. According to the late Karl Bartsch, the distinguished critic and editor of Medieval German literature, the tale shows affinity to legends of the Scalds of Norway and Denmark, and to those of the Shetland Isles. Traces of resemblance are said to be found among the relics of Anglo-Saxon literature. The supposition that the poem was constructed from various early legends explains some of the marvellous incidents of the tale, and those chronological inconsistencies where the rude habits and ideas of earlier times are combined with the later knightly usages of the Middle Ages and with Christian belief.

===Chinese===
作者姓名已不可考；一般认为，这部史诗在很大程度上由游吟歌者世代传唱的早期传说缀合而成。据已故的中世纪德语文学杰出批评家兼校勘者卡尔·巴奇（Karl Bartsch）所言，这个故事与挪威、丹麦的斯卡尔徳（Scalds，即吟游诗人）传说以及设得兰群岛的传说均有亲缘；据说在盎格鲁-撒克逊文学的遗存中也能寻得相似的痕迹。认为此诗由多种早期传说缀合而成的假设，既解释了故事中若干神异的情节，也解释了那些年代上的错杂之处：较早时代的粗陋习尚与观念，竟与中世纪晚期的骑士风俗乃至基督教信仰交织并存。

===Original===
The scene of the poem is laid principally on the shores of the North Sea, and includes Ireland and Normandy, as well as Holland, Denmark, and Friesland. Very vague ideas of geography were, however, entertained by the poet. Some names of places are thought to be fabulous, and critics disagree with regard to the modern countries designated by other names used.

===Chinese===
故事的发生地主要设在北海之滨，涵盖爱尔兰与诺曼底，以及荷兰、丹麦和弗里西亚（Friesland）。不过，诗人怀有的地理观念相当模糊。有些地名被认为是凭空虚构，至于另一些地名所指为今天的哪些国家，批评家们则众说纷纭。

===Original===
The poem is founded upon the themes of love and war, and properly consists of three parts. The first portion, embracing four tales, relates the adventures of Hagen, the grandfather of Gudrun; the second part gives the story of the wooing and abduction of Hilda, his daughter, the mother of Gudrun. The proper story of Gudrun begins only with the ninth tale.

===Chinese===
这首诗以爱情与战争为主题，恰当地说由三个部分构成。第一部分共四个诗章，讲述古德伦的外祖父哈根（Hagen）的历险；第二部分叙述其女希尔达（Hilda）——即古德伦之母——被求娶与被劫夺的故事。古德伦本人的正传，到第九诗章方才开场。

===Original===
The narrative gives a vivid picture of the ideas, manners, and customs of the age of the author; of the dwellings, dress, and ornaments in use; of the weapons and warfare; of the ships and sea-life; and of the tournaments and court festivities. From it we see the nature of the intellectual amusement enjoyed by the northern nations, at that period of their mental development when literary entertainment in the modern sense was yet unknown, and its place was supplied by listening to the recitals of wandering bards.

===Chinese===
这首叙事诗生动描绘了作者那个时代的观念、礼仪与风俗；当时的居所、衣着与佩饰；兵器与战事；船舶与海上生活；以及比武大会与宫廷庆典。由此我们得以窥见北方民族当时所享的智性娱乐是何种性质——在他们心智发展的那个阶段，现代意义上的文学娱乐尚付阙如，倾听游吟歌者的吟诵便填补了这一空缺。

===Original===
Modern German critics agree in assigning a high literary value to the poem of *Gudrun*, and compare it not unfavorably with the *Nibelungenlied*. Bartsch, the critic above named, says: “The general impression which the poem gives is one of greater beauty, though not always of equal grandeur with that of the *Nibelungen*; it is a worthy companion-piece. The two are justly compared, as are the *Iliad* and the *Odyssey*. In the *Nibelungen* as in the *Iliad* the fate of a whole people is decided by the sword, and the ruling house, consisting of noble heroes, meets destruction before our eyes; but the conquerors do not fully rejoice in their success. The whole breathes a tragic spirit, even more than the Greek epic. ‘*Nach Freude Leid*’﻿—‘after joy comes sorrow’﻿—is the earnest tone throughout. *Gudrun*, like the *Odyssey*, closes more tenderly and in a spirit of reconciliation. Although pitiless fate has destroyed the happiness of those for whom the poet has awakened our sympathy, and we see a noble being suffer in the most shameful manner, yet we are sustained by hope, and a happy end compensates for woes endured. ‘*Aus Leid Freude*’﻿—‘sorrows end in joy’﻿—is the final conclusion.﻿ ﻿… All the characters are worked out in the most minute and careful manner, and are developed consistently.﻿ ﻿… The best traits of the German nature, fearless bravery, unfaltering fidelity, and unswerving integrity, are presented. The nobility of a feminine soul which, inspired by pure love, in the distress of a hard captivity, preserves its fidelity to its beloved, perhaps in no poem of the German middle ages is so strikingly shown as in the character of Gudrun.﻿ ﻿… The descriptions both of battle and scenery are masterly, and are painted with a few decisive strokes.”

===Chinese===
现代德国批评家一致给予《古德伦》以很高的文学评价，认为它与《尼伯龙根之歌》相比并不逊色。前文提到的批评家巴奇这样说道：「这首诗给人的总体印象是更为优美，虽然气魄未必总与《尼伯龙根》相当；它是一篇当之无愧的姊妹篇。这两部作品被相提并论是有道理的，正如《伊利亚特》与《奥德赛》并称一样。在《尼伯龙根》中，一如在《伊利亚特》中，整个民族的命运由刀剑裁决，由高贵英雄组成的统治王族在我们眼前走向毁灭；而征服者对自己的胜利也无法尽情欢庆。全篇洋溢着悲剧精神，甚至比希腊史诗更为浓重。『Nach Freude Leid』——『乐极生悲』——是贯穿全诗的沉郁基调。《古德伦》则如《奥德赛》一般，收束得较为温柔，满怀和解的精神。尽管无情的命运摧毁了诗人令我们寄予同情的那些人的幸福，我们目睹一个高贵的生命以最屈辱的方式受苦，但我们始终有希望支撑，圆满的结局补偿了所经受的种种伤痛。『Aus Leid Freude』——『苦尽甘来』——是最终的归结。……所有人物都以极细致、极审慎的笔法刻画而出，发展前后一贯。……日耳曼民族性中最优良的品性——无畏的勇武、坚定不移的忠贞、毫不动摇的正直——都得到了展现。一个女性的灵魂，因纯洁的爱而升华，在严酷囚禁的困厄中仍对所爱之人保持忠贞——在中世纪德语诗歌中，或许没有哪一部能像古德伦这一人物形象那样，把这种高贵展现得如此醒目。……无论战斗还是风景的描写皆属大师手笔，寥寥几笔果决的勾勒便宛然如绘。」

===Original===
The metrical form of *Gudrun* is similar to that of the *Nibelungen*. In both, the accentuation is determined by the logical sense, as in prose, and not always by the number of syllables, as in most modern verse. In both, the lines are metrically divided, giving three accents to each half-line of the stanza, except in the fourth line, in which *Gudrun* differs from the *Nibelungen* in having five accents instead of four on the concluding half-line.

===Chinese===
《古德伦》的格律形式与《尼伯龙根》相似。两者的重音都取决于逻辑语义，一如散文，而不像大多数现代诗那样取决于音节的数目。两者的诗行均按韵律划分为半行，诗节中每个半行各得三个重音；唯有第四行不同——《古德伦》的收尾半行带五个重音，而非《尼伯龙根》的四个。

===Original===
The translator has adhered to the original rhythm, and has endeavored in each stanza to convey strictly the ideas of the author, being careful not to introduce anything, in thought or simile, foreign to the poem, and, as far as the verse would permit, to give a verbal rendering.

===Chinese===
译者谨守原诗的节奏，力求在每一诗节中严格传达作者的意思，小心不引入任何在情思或譬喻上与原诗相违的成分，并在诗律容许的范围内尽量贴近字面译述。

===Original===
The translation has been made from Bartsch’s edition of the original Middle High German (Brockhaus, Leipzig, 1874). He, like the other editors, has supplied some omissions in the manuscript, an instance of which may be seen in the sixth line of the facsimile given. The drawings introduced are copied from medieval German printed books.
