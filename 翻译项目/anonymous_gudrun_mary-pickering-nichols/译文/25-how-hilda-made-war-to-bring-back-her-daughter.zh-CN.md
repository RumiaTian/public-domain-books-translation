## Tale XXII How Hilda Made War to Bring Back Her Daughter / 第二十二诗章 希尔达如何兴兵夺回她的女儿

===Original===
Hilda summons her vassals to make an expedition against the Normans. After visiting the graves of their kinsmen on the Wulpensand, they pass in safety the magnetic rocks at Givers, and arrive on the coast of Normandy.

===Chinese===
希尔达（Hilda）召集封臣，出征诺曼人。众人在武尔彭沙洲（Wulpensand）凭吊过亲人的坟茔之后，平安驶过吉弗斯（Givers）的磁岩，抵达诺曼底（Normandy）海岸。

===Original===
We now will speak no longer
 of the toil the maidens bore
 For knights as well as ladies.
 Queen Hilda evermore

===Chinese===
如今我们不再多讲
少女们承受的苦役，
无论对骑士还是贵妇。
王后希尔达却始终

===Original===
 Her thoughts to this had given
 how to win back her daughter,
 Out of the Norman kingdom,
 whither from home the daring Hartmut brought her.

===Chinese===
把心思全倾在这上头：
如何夺回她的女儿，
救出那诺曼人的王国——
胆大妄为的哈特穆特（Hartmut）把她从家中掳到了那里。

===Original===
First were workmen bidden,
 near to the deep sea-flood,
 Of ships to build her seven,
 strong, well made, and good;

===Chinese===
首先她召集工匠，
在深深的海潮近旁，
为她造起七艘战船，
坚固精良，尽善尽美；

===Original===
 With two-and-twenty barges,
 broad, with both ends rounded.
 Whate’er for them was needed
 was quickly brought, and everything abounded.

===Chinese===
外加二十二艘驳船，
船身宽阔，两端浑圆。
凡是所需的物事
都迅速办齐，样样充盈。

===Original===
Forty galleys also
 lay upon the sea;
 On these her eyes were feeding.
 Longing great had she

===Chinese===
还有四十艘桨帆战船
停卧在大海之上；
她望着它们目不转睛。
她怀着炽烈的渴望，

===Original===
 To see the throng of fighters
 who should soon be sailing.
 She their food made ready;
 for this the knights her praise were loudly telling.

===Chinese===
盼见那一群武士
不日扬帆出海。
她亲手替他们备办饮食；
骑士们为此齐声颂扬她。

===Original===
The time was drawing nearer,
 when now to cross the sea
 No more should they be waiting,
 who wished the maids to free,

===Chinese===
时辰渐渐临近，
此刻渡海之事
再不容他们等待——
那些立誓要解救少女的人，

===Original===
 That in a far-off kingdom
 in hardest toil were living.
 Now Hilda sent for her liegemen;
 to those who called them clothes she first was giving.

===Chinese===
少女们正在遥远的国度
受着最严酷的苦役。
希尔达随即召来她的封臣；
又先把衣裳发给前去传召的人们。

===Original===
The day that she had chosen
 was at the Christmas-tide,
 When they must seek the foemen
 by whom King Hettel died.

===Chinese===
她择定的日子
正当圣诞时节，
那时他们要去寻那班仇敌——
正是害死黑特尔（Hettel）国王的凶手。

===Original===
 Forthwith to friends and kinsmen
 Hilda gave her bidding,
 That they to bring her daughter
 back from the Norman land must then be speeding.

===Chinese===
希尔达当即向亲友
颁下她的号令，
命他们速速成行，
去把她的女儿从诺曼底（Normandy）之地接回。

===Original===
Trusty men were bidden
 by Hilda first to go
 To Herwic and his followers,
 that one and all should know

===Chinese===
希尔达先遣心腹
即刻登程，
去见赫尔维克（Herwic）和他的部众，
好教他们人人知晓

===Original===
 Of the inroad on the Normans
 that she had sworn and plotted.
 To many Hegeling children
 this erelong an orphan’s life allotted.

===Chinese===
她已立誓筹谋，
要对诺曼人大举进犯。
不久，许多黑格林人（Hegeling）的孩儿
便由此被派定了孤儿的生涯。

===Original===
The men sent out by Hilda
 to Herwic rode in haste:
 For what they then were coming
 the king full quickly guessed;

===Chinese===
希尔达遣出的使者
策马疾驰去见赫尔维克：
他们此来为了何事，
国王一猜便中；

===Original===
 Then went he forth to meet them,
 soon as he saw them nearing;
 Gladly them he greeted,
 and soon from them Queen Hilda’s wish was hearing.

===Chinese===
他一望见他们临近，
便出门相迎；
他欣然向他们致意，
随即听明了希尔达王后的心愿。

===Original===
“Well you know, Lord Herwic,
 our woe and plight forlorn,
 And how the Hegeling warriors
 to help the queen have sworn.

===Chinese===
「赫尔维克大人，你深知
我们的哀恸与孤绝的苦境，
也知黑格林的武士们
已立誓鼎助王后。

===Original===
 Yourself Queen Hilda trusteth
 more than any other;
 To none Gudrun is dearer﻿—
 the homeless maid, long sundered from her mother.”

===Chinese===
希尔达王后对您信重，
胜过世上任何人；
再没有人比您更疼古德伦（Gudrun）——
这无家可归的少女，与母亲睽违已久。」

===Original===
The well-born knight thus answered:
 “I know in truth too well
 How Hartmut had the boldness
 my fair betrothed to steal,

===Chinese===
那位出身高贵的骑士答道：
「我实在知道得太清楚，
哈特穆特是何等胆大包天，
竟劫走我美丽的未婚妻，

===Original===
 Because his love she slighted,
 and hearkened to my wooing;
 For this Gudrun, my lady,
 her father lost, and still her lot is ruing.

===Chinese===
只因她薄了他的情意，
而俯允了我的求亲；
为此，古德伦，我的夫人，
失去了父亲，至今犹自哀叹命运。

===Original===
“My pledge and hearty greeting
 bear to your lady good;
 No more the Norman Hartmut
 by me shall be allowed

===Chinese===
「请把我的誓言与诚挚的问候
带给你们贤德的王后；
那诺曼人哈特穆特，休想再
得到我的容许，

===Original===
 To hold so long in bondage
 my own betrothèd maiden:
 For me, of all, ’tis fittest
 to bring the lady home, our lives to gladden.

===Chinese===
把我订下婚约的少女
这样久久拘为奴婢：
在众人之中唯我最宜
把这位夫人接回家来，为我们添欢。

===Original===
“To Hilda and her kinsmen
 this answer you may say:
 When Christmas time is over,
 on the sixth-and-twentieth day,

===Chinese===
「请向希尔达和她的亲族
转达这样的答复：
待圣诞时节一过，
到那第二十六日，

===Original===
 I will ride to the Hegelings,
 three thousand fighters taking.”
 Then the men of Hilda
 waited no more, but home their way were making.

===Chinese===
我便驰往黑格林人那里，
带上三千武士。」
希尔达的使者们
不再耽搁，当即踏上归程。

===Original===
Now Herwic made him ready,
 and to the strife gave thought,
 With many faithful liegemen
 who oft had bravely fought.

===Chinese===
于是赫尔维克整备停当，
一心思谋争战，
身边聚着许多忠实的封臣，
都曾屡屡奋勇作战。

===Original===
 Those who to go were willing
 he for war outfitted;
 Though wintry was the weather,
 they to take the field no longer waited.

===Chinese===
凡愿随征的人，
他都披挂武装；
纵然天时正值隆冬，
他们上阵也不再迟延。

===Original===
Of help the widowed Hilda
 sorely felt the need:
 Soon to her friends in Denmark
 she sent her men with speed,

===Chinese===
孀居的希尔达深感
援手之急需：
她随即火速遣人
去往丹麦（Denmark）的友人们那里，

===Original===
 To tell the knights and warriors
 no more at home to tarry;
 For they to the Norman kingdom
 must ride, to free Gudrun from bondage dreary.

===Chinese===
传告骑士与武士们，
不可再滞留家中；
因为他们必须驰赴
诺曼人的王国，救古德伦脱离凄苦的奴役。

===Original===
They bore to the youthful Horant
 this errand from the queen:
 That he and all his kinsmen
 were to her lord of kin,

===Chinese===
他们向年轻的霍兰特（Horant）
传了王后的这桩使命：
说他与他全体的亲族
于她夫君皆为亲属，

===Original===
 And the sorrows of her daughter
 should by them be heeded;
 For death to her were better
 than ever that her child to Hartmut should be wedded.

===Chinese===
她女儿的苦难
理当他们挂怀；
因为对她而言，宁可一死，
也强似女儿终有一日嫁给哈特穆特。

===Original===
Then sent the knight this answer:
 “Unto Queen Hilda say﻿—
 Though yet ’twill cost to women
 many a bitter day,

===Chinese===
那位骑士随即回话：
「请转告希尔达王后——
尽管此事还将教妇人们
捱过许多辛酸的日子，

===Original===
 I still, with all my followers,
 will help be gladly giving;
 For this will be heard the weeping
 of many a mother’s child, in the land now living.

===Chinese===
我仍愿偕我全体的部众，
欣然出力相助；
因为今世还活着的人里，
多少母亲的孩子将为此哀哭。

===Original===
“I bid you now, moreover,
 to say unto the queen﻿—
 Ere many days are ended,
 in her land will I be seen;

===Chinese===
「此外我还要你们
转告王后——
用不了多少时日，
我便会现身在她的国土；

===Original===
 Tell her that my wishes
 all to war are bending,
 And soon ten thousand warriors
 from out the Danish land will I be sending.”

===Chinese===
告诉她，我的心愿
全都倾注在战事上，
不久我将从丹麦之地
遣出一万名武士。」

===Original===
The men sent there by Hilda
 of Horant took their leave:
 They sped to the Waalisch marches,
 and found Morunc the brave

===Chinese===
希尔达遣到那里的使者
向霍兰特辞行：
他们又疾赴瓦尔河（Waalisch）边地，
寻见勇武的莫伦克（Morunc）

===Original===
 With all his men about him,
 a margrave rich and daring.
 He gladly saw them coming,
 and of a loving welcome was not sparing.

===Chinese===
他部下的兵众环侍四周，
是一位豪富而胆壮的边地侯爵（margrave）。
他欣然看着他们到来，
挚切的欢迎毫不吝惜。

===Original===
Then spake the knightly Irold:
 “Since now by me ’tis known
 That into the Hegeling kingdom,
 before seven weeks are gone,

===Chinese===
骑士伊罗尔德（Irold）随即开言：
「既然如今我已得知，
七周未尽之前，就要
驰进黑格林人的王国，

===Original===
 I with all my followers
 am bidden to be riding,
 For this will I be ready,
 whatever luck be there for us betiding.”

===Chinese===
我便要偕全体部众
奉召骑行；
为此我必整装以待，
无论何种命运在前头等着我们。」

===Original===
The news was spread by Morunc,
 within the Holstein land,
 That Hilda now was sending
 for all her friends at hand;

===Chinese===
莫伦克又把消息
传遍荷尔斯泰因（Holstein）之地：
希尔达如今正遣人
召集她四近的亲友；

===Original===
 He said that all good warriors
 must the field be taking.
 To the Danish knight, brave Fru-te,
 they also gave the word, his help bespeaking.

===Chinese===
他说凡是好武士
都必须披挂上阵。
他们又向丹麦骑士、勇武的弗鲁特（Fru-te）
传话，请他出力相助。

===Original===
The worthy knight, then answering,
 his ready will did show:
 “Back to her home will we bring her.
 Thirteen years ago,

===Chinese===
那可敬的骑士当即作答，
表明他慨然效命的心意：
「我们定要把她接回家去。
十三年前，

===Original===
 We swore the land of the Normans
 should with war be wasted;
 ’Twas then the friends of Hartmut
 stole the maid Gudrun, and homeward hasted.”

===Chinese===
我们立誓：定要叫
诺曼人的国土遭兵燹摧残；
正是那时，哈特穆特的同党
劫走了少女古德伦，匆匆奔回了家。」

===Original===
Wâ-te, the knight from Sturmland,
 to this at once gave thought,
 How he might also help her.
 Altho’ he yet knew nought

===Chinese===
施图尔姆兰（Sturmland）的骑士瓦特（Wâ-te）
闻讯立刻动了心思，
盘算自己如何也能相助。
尽管他还不曾听闻

===Original===
 Of the word that Hilda sent him,
 yet he at once bestirred him;
 Of his knights a goodly number
 then in haste he called, who gladly heard him.

===Chinese===
希尔达捎给他的口信，
他却当即振作起来；
他麾下为数可观的骑士
被他匆匆召集，个个欣然听命。

===Original===
All of them were busy
 with care for the coming war;
 Wâ-te the old from Sturmland
 brought from near and far

===Chinese===
众人全都忙着
为将临的战事操劳；
施图尔姆兰的老瓦特
从远近四方招来了

===Original===
 Full a thousand kinsmen,
 for the fight well fitted;
 With these he hoped that Hartmut
 would soon be overcome and be outwitted.

===Chinese===
足足一千名亲族，
个个堪当鏖战；
他指望凭这班人马，
教哈特穆特速速折戟、中计成擒。

===Original===
The sad and homeless women
 in toil and pain were kept
 By the cross and evil Gerlind:
 but fewer wrongs were heaped

===Chinese===
那些悲苦无家可归的妇人，
被乖戾狠毒的格尔林德（Gerlind）
困在苦役与伤痛之中：
只是堆在赫加特（Hergart）夫人

===Original===
 Upon the Lady Hergart;
 (this name to her was given:)
 She loved the king’s high cup-bearer,
 and greatly hoped to be a princess even.

===Chinese===
身上的欺凌要少些；
（这名字便是这样加诸她身的：）
她爱慕国王的高阶司酒官，
满心盼望自己有朝一日也能做王妃。

===Original===
For this fair Hilda’s daughter
 often sorely wept;
 And Hergart, too, yet later
 woe and sorrow reaped,

===Chinese===
为此，美丽的希尔达之女
常常哀哀哭泣；
而赫加特后来也
收获了哀伤与愁苦，

===Original===
 Because she ne’er with others
 would their toils be sharing.
 Whate’er to her might happen,
 Gudrun for all her ills was little caring.

===Chinese===
因为她从不肯与旁人
一同分担苦役。
无论她自己会遭逢什么，
古德伦对自己身受的苦难全不放在心上。

===Original===
Of the Hegelings none were idle,
 as you before have heard:
 Though many for all their toiling
 would find but scant reward,

===Chinese===
黑格林人中无人闲坐，
一如你们此前所闻：
尽管许多人纵然操劳，
所得的报偿也微薄，

===Original===
 Yet all within the kingdom their
 ready help were lending.
 Now the knights were thinking
 for the brother of Gudrun ’twere best they should be sending.

===Chinese===
王国之内的所有人
却仍踊跃效力。
此刻骑士们商议，
最好还是派人去请古德伦的弟弟。

===Original===
Riders then went swiftly
 into the land of the North,
 And found in an open meadow
 the youth of kingly birth,

===Chinese===
骑手们随即疾驰而去，
奔往北方的国土，
在一片开阔的草地上
寻到了那位王家血胤的少年，

===Original===
 Where by the edge of a river
 many birds were flocking:
 There with his trusty falconer
 he showed his skill, and spent his time in hawking.

===Chinese===
那里在一条河的近旁，
群鸟正纷纷翔集：
他与亲信的驯鹰官在那里
炫示身手，以放鹰行猎消磨光阴。

===Original===
As soon as, riding quickly,
 these by him were seen,
 He said: “Those men now coming
 are sent to us by the queen;

===Chinese===
他远远望见那些人
策马疾驰而来，
便说道：「如今来的那些人，
是王后差来见我们的；

===Original===
 They come to give her bidding,
 proudly hither hasting;
 My mother thinketh wrongly
 that we the war forget, and time are wasting.”

===Chinese===
他们奉她的旨意，
意气昂扬地疾驰到此；
我母亲错怪了我们，
只当忘了战事、虚掷光阴。」

===Original===
He set his hawk a-flying,
 and thence at once he rode.
 Very soon thereafter
 darkened was his mood;

===Chinese===
他把猎鹰放上天去，
随即策马离了那地方。
此后不久，
他的心绪便阴沉下来；

===Original===
 For when the men he greeted,
 and they their tale were telling,
 He learned that the queen, his mother,
 ever in tears her loss was aye bewailing.

===Chinese===
因为待他问候过来使，
听他们讲明来意，
他才得知王后，他的母亲，
还在终日以泪哀悼着自己的失丧。

===Original===
She to the youthful warrior
 sent her greeting kind:
 In her wretched lot, she asked him
 what might be his mind;

===Chinese===
她向这位年轻的武士
致以亲切的问候：
在悲惨的命运里，她问他
心中作何打算；

===Original===
 And asked how many followers
 he could to the war be leading;
 For from the Hegeling kingdom
 they all to the Norman land must soon be speeding.

===Chinese===
又问他能率领
多少部众赴战；
因为从黑格林人的王国，
他们全都要速速开赴诺曼底之地。

===Original===
Then Ortwin sent this answer:
 “Me dost thou rightly bid;
 I from hence will hasten,
 and bring from far and wide

===Chinese===
奥特温（Ortwin）随即回了话：
「你这样吩咐我，正合其宜；
我即刻从此地兼程赶路，
从四面八方召集

===Original===
 Twenty thousand fighters﻿—
 men both brave and daring;
 These my steps will follow
 even to death, their lives and homes forswearing.”

===Chinese===
两万名战士——
个个勇武刚胆；
他们将追随我的脚步
直至赴死，不惜舍却性命与家园。」

===Original===
Now from every border
 many warriors went
 Riding to Hilda’s kingdom,
 for whom the queen had sent;

===Chinese===
于是从每一处边陲，
许多武士启程，
驰往希尔达的王国——
他们正是王后所召之人；

===Original===
 They vied with one another,
 to win her praises striving.
 Not less than sixty thousand
 together came, their help for Hilda giving.

===Chinese===
他们你争我赶，
力求博得她的嘉许。
足有不下六万人
济济而至，为希尔达效力。

===Original===
On the river Waal Sir Morunc
 had upon the wave
 Of broad-built ships full sixty,
 strong to bear the brave

===Chinese===
在瓦尔河（Waal）上，莫伦克爵士
备下整整六十艘
宽体巨船浮波而列，
坚牢堪载勇士，

===Original===
 Who with the Hegelings sailing
 would o’er the sea be carried,
 To free Gudrun, the maiden,
 who sadly now among the Normans tarried.

===Chinese===
载他们随黑格林人扬帆，
渡过大海，
去解救少女古德伦——
她此刻正凄凄地滞留在诺曼人中间。

===Original===
From out the Northland also
 finest ships were brought,
 With horses and with clothing,
 as good as could be sought:

===Chinese===
从北方的国土
也运来了上好的船只，
连同战马与衣装，
精良得无可更求：

===Original===
 Decked were all the helmets,
 the weapons glittered brightly,
 Ready for the onset
 bravely they came, in armor fair and knightly.

===Chinese===
头盔顶顶装点得华美，
兵刃闪烁生光，
只待冲锋陷阵，
他们便披挂堂堂骑士甲胄，英武而来。

===Original===
Now by their shields men reckoned
 how many there might be
 Who to the Norman kingdom
 would go the maid to free,

===Chinese===
人们按着盾牌清点
人数究竟几何——
那些要开赴诺曼人的王国
解救少女的战士，

===Original===
 And to the great Queen Hilda
 their help to give were ready;
 They numbered seventy thousand;
 gifts to all were given by the queenly lady.

===Chinese===
并甘愿向伟大的希尔达王后
献上援手的人；
计点得七万之众；
这位王后般的贵妇向众人颁了赏。

===Original===
On all who there were gathered,
 or to court who later came,
 The queen, though ever mournful,
 yet let her kindness beam:

===Chinese===
凡在那里聚集的人，
或是日后赶来宫廷的人，
王后虽然长怀哀恸，
仍让慈惠之光焕发：

===Original===
 She gave them hearty welcome,

把船驶往那里，众人奉命而行。
