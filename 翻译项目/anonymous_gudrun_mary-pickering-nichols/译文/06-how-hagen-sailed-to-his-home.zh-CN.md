## Tale III How Hagen Sailed to His Home / 第三诗章 哈根扬帆归故里

===Original===
The master of the ship inquires of Hagen and the maidens concerning their country, and learns that Hagen is the son of an old enemy. He threatens to keep him as a hostage, but Hagen seizes thirty of the sailors, and flings them into the sea, and compels the others to steer towards his home. There, he sends messengers to announce his arrival to his father and mother, who come down to the shore to meet him.

===Chinese===
船主向哈根（Hagen）与众少女询问她们的故国，从而得知哈根乃旧日仇敌之子。他威胁要将哈根扣为人质；哈根却一把揪住三十名水手，把他们掷入海中，迫使其余众人把船驶向他的家乡。抵达之后，他派使者向父母通报自己归来的消息，二老于是亲赴岸边相迎。

===Original===
Ere they went on shipboard, the pilgrims them besought
Kindly to take the clothing they with them had brought.
However shy were the maidens, to wear them they were ready;
They donned the clothes with blushes, and now their sorrow had an ending speedy.

===Chinese===
登船之前，朝圣的旅人们好意相请，
求她们收下自己随身带来的衣裳；
少女们虽然羞怯，却也愿意穿戴，
她们红晕满颊地换上新衣，忧愁迅即有了收场。

===Original===
Soon as the lovely maidens embarked upon the wave,
They heartily were greeted by knights both good and brave,
Who to the high-born daughters welcome to give were heedful;
Though they at first mistook them, and thought them wicked elves, or mermaids dreadful.

===Chinese===
可爱的少女们刚一登上海浪中的船，
便受到良善勇毅的骑士们热诚的欢迎；
骑士们一心要礼遇这些出身高贵的闺秀，
虽然起初他们错把她们当作邪恶的精灵，或是骇人的美人鱼。

===Original===
That night the maidens rested with friends upon the sea;
So wondrous was their dwelling, from fear they were not free:
Wiser it were in the children to think this home a blessing.
Soon as the earl had bidden, their food upon the maids they all were pressing.

===Chinese===
当夜，少女们在海上与友伴同眠；
这居处如此奇异，恐惧未从心头驱散：
这些孩子若能把此处当作福地，方为明智。
伯爵一声吩咐，众人便争相向少女们殷勤劝餐。

===Original===
After they had eaten, and while with them he sat,
The lord of the land of Garadie the maidens did entreat
To say by whom such fair ones were brought unto that shore.
The children, at his asking, only felt their sorrow grow the more.

===Chinese===
用饭既毕，他还坐在她们近旁，
加拉迪（Garadie）之主便恳请少女们相告，
是何人把这般佳人送到这片海岸。
孩子们经他一问，只觉愁绪愈发滋长。

===Original===
First answered him the eldest of those who with him sat:
“I come from a far-off kingdom, (my lord, now hear my fate;)
I was born in the land of India, a land wherein my father
Was king while he was living, but I, alas! the crown must leave to another.”

===Chinese===
座中年纪最长的少女先答他问：
「我来自一个遥远的国度（吾主，且听我的命运；）
我生在印度之地，在那片国土上，我的父亲
在世时曾为君王；而我，唉！王冠只得让与他人。」

===Original===
Then spake the maid next younger: “I too have come from far;
Erewhile a strong old griffin did me from Portugal bear.
A king in the land was my father; none than he was prouder,
Nor for a mighty ruler, far or near, were ever praises louder.”

===Chinese===
接着次幼的少女开言：「我也来自远方；
昔日一只强壮的老狮鹫将我从葡萄牙攫走。
我父曾是那片土地上的君王；无人比他更傲岸，
无论远近的强大君王，得到的赞誉都不及他响亮。」

===Original===
Then the youngest maiden, who by the earl sat near,
To him spoke low and modestly, and said: “I pray you hear;
From Iserland I was carried, my father there held power;
But from those who hoped to rear me, alas! afar was I borne in an evil hour.”

===Chinese===
最年幼的少女坐在伯爵身旁，
低声而谦恭地对他言道：「请您听我讲：
我被人从伊瑟兰（Iserland）掳走，我父在那里执掌权柄；
可是，唉！凶险的时辰里，我被带离想养育我的人，流落远方。」

===Original===
The high-born knight then answered: “By God ’tis ordered well,
Since you among your kinsfolk not long were left to dwell;
Now, at last, by his kindness you are freed from dangers,
For I within these borders have found you living here, such lovely strangers.”

===Chinese===
高贵的骑士答道：「这是上帝妥善的安排，
你们在亲族之中未得久住；
如今终于蒙他的仁慈，你们脱离了种种凶险，
因为我在这些疆界之内遇见了活着的你们——如此可爱的异乡佳丽。」

===Original===
However much he asked them, they yet to tell were loath,
How unto them it happened grim death had spared them both,
When erewhiles the griffin unto his nest had brought them.
Many had been their sorrows; no more to speak of these the maids bethought them.

===Chinese===
任凭他怎样追问，她们总不愿讲述，
那狰狞的死神如何偏偏放过了她们俩，
当年狮鹫又如何把她们掳回巢中。
她们苦难重重；少女们决意不再提起这些伤痛。

===Original===
Then said the worthy leader, turning to the youth;
“My dearest friend and fellow, now let me hear the truth;
Since unto me these maidens their sorry tale have given,
From you would I hear gladly, and learn the land and kin whence you were riven.”

===Chinese===
尊贵的首领于是转向那少年，说道：
「我最亲爱的朋友和伙伴，请让我听闻实情；
这些少女已向我讲了自己悲惨的遭遇，
我也乐意听你道来：你从哪片国土、哪门亲族中被生生撕离。」

===Original===
To him wild Hagen answered: “That will I tell to you;
One of those dreadful griffins bore me hither too.
Sigeband was my father; in Ireland once was I living;
But long with these lovely maidens I since have dwelt, with many sorrows striving.”

===Chinese===
狂野的哈根答道：「这我愿说与你听；
也是那些可怖狮鹫中的一只，把我掳到了此地。
西格班德（Sigeband）是我的父亲；我从前住在爱尔兰；
但此后许久，我与这些可爱的少女同住，与重重忧患抗争。」

===Original===
Then they all besought him to say how it befell
That, living with the griffins he had come off so well.
To them young Hagen answered: “To God it all was owing;
But now I have cooled my anger; no more for them my heart with hate is glowing.”

===Chinese===
众人于是一齐恳求他讲个分明，
与狮鹫同处，他怎能保全自身。
年轻的哈根回答他们：「这一切全赖上天；
如今我的怒气已消，心中不再为它们燃着仇恨。」

===Original===
Then spake the lord of Garadie: “I fain would learn from you
How you were freed from danger?” He said: “I quickly slew
Both the old and the young ones; not one of those is living
By whom my life was threatened, and who to me such fear were daily giving.”

===Chinese===
加拉迪之主又开口：「我倒想听你说说，
你是怎样脱离险境？」他说：「我手起刀落，
把老的小的一并诛戮；那些家伙无一幸存——
它们威胁我的性命，日日令我惊惶不定。」

===Original===
Then said all the sailors: “Your strength indeed was great;
For every man and woman to praise you it were meet.
A thousand of us truly ’gainst them in, vain had striven,
Nor ever could have slain them; truly to you have blessings great been given.”

===Chinese===
水手们于是齐声道：「你的力量实在超群；
无论男子还是妇人，人人都该将你称颂。
说真的，我们一千人与它们相拼也是枉然，
永远休想杀死它们；上天确实赐了你巨大的鸿恩。」

===Original===
The earl and all his followers were of the boy afraid;
His strength was past all measure, and sorrow for them made.
They would by craft his weapons have taken from him gladly,
But these he sternly guarded, and soon, thro’ him, it ended for them sadly.

===Chinese===
伯爵和他的全体随从都畏惧这少年；
他的膂力超乎一切衡量，令他们忧惧交并。
他们本想用诡计快快夺下他的兵器，
他却厉加守卫；很快，经他之手，事情对他们落得悲惨收场。

===Original===
Then spake the earl yet further: “It now has happened well,
After our toilsome wanderings, and all that us befell.
But since you are a kinsman of my foeman, Sigeband,
And here have come from Ireland, I as a hostage hold you in my hand.

===Chinese===
伯爵又继续说道：「如今事态正巧，
历尽劳苦的漂泊，又遭了这许多变故。
但你既是我仇人西格班德的亲族，
又从爱尔兰来到这里，我就要把你当人质握在手中。

===Original===
“You come to me most fitly, as you shall know ere long,
For many of your kindred have done to me great wrong.
In Garadie’s fair kingdom, which lies too near their border,
In a heavy fight, my warriors were seized upon and murdered by their shameful order.”

===Chinese===
你来得正是再合适不过，不久你自会知晓，
因为你的许多亲族曾对我作下大恶。
在加拉迪美丽的王国——它离他们的边境实在太近——
一场恶战中，我的武士们遭他们无耻下令，被擒住杀害。」

===Original===
Then answered him young Hagen: “Of all the wrongs they did
I am wholly guiltless; if me to them you lead
I their hearts will soften, and so will the strife be ended.
Let hope to me be granted that I on my kinsmen’s shore may soon be landed.”

===Chinese===
年轻的哈根回答：「他们所行的种种不义，
我全然无辜；你若肯把我带到他们那里，
我自会软化他们的心，争斗也就能止息。
请容我怀着一个希望：让我早日登上亲族的海岸之地。」

===Original===
Then said the earl to Hagen: “For a pledge must you abide,
And I shall keep these maidens to live at court by my side;
They will swell my greatness, and I shall be their owner.”
Then thought the youthful Hagen, such words to be to him a wrong and a dishonor.

===Chinese===
伯爵对哈根说：「你必须留下充当抵押，
这些少女我也要留在身边，在宫中生活；
她们会增添我的威势，我将成为她们的主人。」
年轻的哈根闻言心想：这于他是欺辱与羞辱。

===Original===
He quickly said in anger: “No bondsman will I be;
That may no man ask for, who would unscathed go free.
And now, my worthy sailors, you needs to my land must bear me;
I will reward you gladly, and to give you clothes and gold will never spare me.

===Chinese===
他疾言怒道：「我绝不做人的奴仆；
谁若想安然无恙地走脱，便不该提这要求。
好了，我可敬的水手们，你们务必把我送往我的国土；
我会欣然酬谢，给你们衣物黄金，绝不吝惜分毫。

===Original===
“The earl has thought my maidens his own shall ever be;
But they shall yet be happy, and shall of him be free.
Whoe’er is blest with wisdom, let him my bidding follow;
Look to your sails, and turn them, and guide the ship to Ireland, o’er the billow.”

===Chinese===
伯爵满心以为，这些少女将永远归他所有；
但她们终会得到幸福，终将摆脱他的羁束。
凡蒙受智慧眷顾的人，请听从我的吩咐：
照看你们的船帆，转舵换向，驾船破浪，直向爱尔兰国土。」

===Original===
The men, as the earl had bidden, to seize the boy now dared,
But boldly did he meet them, and for their lives they feared.
He by the hair caught thirty, and into the water flung them;
Soon the strength of his body was known to all, and dreaded much among them.

===Chinese===
水手们奉伯爵之命，斗胆上前捉拿少年，
他却昂然迎击，他们个个自惧性命难保。
他揪住三十人的头发，把他们抛进了海水；
顷刻间他神力之名传遍众人，人人胆战心摇。

===Original===
Had not the kindly maidens sought to end the fight,
Soon the earl of Garadie he would have killed outright.
’Gainst neither low nor mighty did his anger falter;
These warriors and sailors now to Ireland’s shores their way must alter.

===Chinese===
若非善良的少女们竭力劝止这场争斗，
加拉迪伯爵早被他当场结果性命。
无论对方卑微还是显赫，他的怒气都不曾动摇；
这些武士和水手如今只得改换航路，驶向爱尔兰的海滨。

===Original===
They began at once to hasten, lest he their lives might take;
For now the wrath of Hagen made them with fear to quake.
For seventeen days the sailors from toiling never rested,
And sorely were they frightened; whene’er he seemed unkind they ills forecasted.

===Chinese===
他们随即兼程疾驶，唯恐他取了他们性命；
因为哈根的雷霆之怒，吓得他们浑身战栗。
整整十七天，水手们操劳不曾有一刻歇息，
他们惊恐万状；只要他稍显不悦，他们便预感大祸将至。

===Original===
When he now drew nearer unto his father’s shore,
He saw the roomy castles he well had known before;
Soon a lofty palace he spied at the edge of the river;
Three hundred towers fully he there beheld, as strong and good as ever.

===Chinese===
此刻他渐渐驶近父亲的国土，
望见了那些他素来熟悉的宽敞城堡；
随即他又望见河边一座巍峨的宫殿，
足足三百座塔楼尽收眼底，一如既往坚固完好。

===Original===
In it dwelt King Sigeband, with his proud and queenly wife.
Again each pilgrim sailor thought to lose his life;
For should the lord of Ireland aught of them be learning,
They feared that he would slay them; but Hagen stood between, his anger turning.

===Chinese===
宫中住着西格班德国王，和他高贵庄严的王后。
朝圣的水手们又一个个自料性命难保；
因为爱尔兰之主若对他们略有风闻，
他们害怕会遭他诛戮；幸有哈根居间，把那怒火扭转消融。

===Original===
Then spake unto the pilgrims that brave and warlike man:
“Your peace will I make gladly, altho’ I do not reign;
I hold no sway in the kingdom, but thither will I be sending,
And ’twixt yourself and my father of the hatred old I soon will make an ending.

===Chinese===
那位英武刚猛的人对朝圣者们说道：
「我乐意促成你们的平安，虽然我并未临朝；
我在王国中并无权柄，但我会向那里遣人传信，
在你与我父亲之间，那份旧日仇恨，我很快便替你们了清。

===Original===
“Would any now be doing what wealth to him will bring,
Let him my errand carry. Whoever to the king
Shall say what I shall bid him, gold will I give him truly;
And also, very gladly, my father and my mother will reward him duly.”

===Chinese===
谁若此刻愿做一桩能带来财富的事情，
就请替我跑这趟差。谁肯对国王
转达我嘱咐的话，我必真心以黄金相赠；
而且，我父亲和母亲也会非常乐意，给他应得的酬劳。」

===Original===
Twelve of the stranded pilgrims he bade to ride away:
“Now ask of the king, my father,” thus the youth did say,
“Whether to see young Hagen, his son, he still is yearning﻿—
Him who erst by the griffin was stolen far away, heart-sorrow learning.

===Chinese===
他吩咐落难的朝圣者中十二人策马先行：
「去问问国王，我的父亲，」少年这样说道，
「他是否还在渴念见到年轻的哈根——他的儿子，
那昔日被狮鹫远远掳走、尝尽心酸悲苦的孩子。

===Original===
“I know that what you tell him the king will not believe;
Then ask you of my mother if she her faith will give,
And if for her child to own me she will at last be willing,
If I upon my bosom will show a golden cross, the proof fulfilling.”

===Chinese===
我知道，你们告诉他的话，国王必不肯信；
那就去问我的母亲，问她可愿立下信约，
问她最终可愿意认我做她的孩子，
只要我当胸亮出那枚金十字，凭证便能圆满应验。」

===Original===
When those he sent had ridden farther into the land,
They found, in the palace seated, Queen U-te and Sigeband.
Then knew the king that the riders from Garadie came thither,
And that they to him were foemen; at this both he and his men were wroth together.

===Chinese===
他差去的使者们策马深入国土，
在宫中见到了端坐的乌特（U-te）王后与西格班德。
国王随即认出，来骑是加拉迪人马，
且与他为敌；为此他与臣下一齐震怒难当。

===Original===
He asked of them how dared they to come within his land?
Then one among them answered: “We are sent here at the hand
Of your son, the youthful Hagen. If any fain would meet him,
He now is here, so near you, that you ere many hours, in truth, may greet him.”

===Chinese===
他问他们：怎敢擅入他的疆土？
其中一人答道：「我们是奉您儿子、
年轻的哈根之命而来。谁若渴望与他相见，
他此刻就在近处；实情如此，不出几个时辰，您便能与他见面。」

===Original===
Then spake the kingly Sigeband: “To cheat there is no need;
The loss of my dear little one, who hath so long been dead,
Still my heart’s deep sorrow doth too oft awaken.”
“Ask, then, the queen, your lady, if for a falsehood should our word be taken?

===Chinese===
王者气度的西格班德说道：「行骗大可不必；
我那亲爱的小宝贝，死去已这么长久，
他的夭折至今仍时时搅动我心底的哀恸。」
「那么请问您的夫人王后：难道我们的话该被当作谎骗？

===Original===
“The little boy so often in her fond care has been,
She knows if on his bosom a golden cross was seen.
And if upon this wanderer be found the selfsame token,
You as your child can own him; you then will grant that truth by us is spoken.”

===Chinese===
「那孩子从前常在王后的慈爱看护之下，
她自然知道他胸前可曾见过一枚金十字。
倘若这位流浪者身上寻得同样的信物，
您便可以认他为您自己的孩子；也当承认我们句句属实。」

===Original===
Then to the Lady U-te the tale was quickly told;
Glad was she of the tidings, yet mourned she as of old.
She said: “Now let us hasten, that the truth no more be hidden.”
Her lord then bade to saddle; and steeds for himself and his bravest knights were bidden.

===Chinese===
这番话很快便传给了乌特夫人；
她闻讯又喜，却仍像往常一样悲戚。
她说：「我们快些动身，莫让真相再被隐匿。」
她的夫君随即吩咐备鞍，为他自己和最勇猛的骑士们备下坐骑。

===Original===
Straightway one of the pilgrims to the fair Queen U-te said:
“I will tell you, if you listen, what now to do you need.
You first must carry clothing for each young lovely maiden
Whose coming does you honor; as followers of your son were they hither bidden.”

===Chinese===
随即一名朝圣者对美丽的乌特王后说道：
「您若肯听，我便告诉您眼下该做什么。
您须先为每一位年轻可爱的少女备好衣装，
她们的到来于您是体面；她们是作为您儿子的随行被请到这地方。」

===Original===
Soon brought they richest clothing, and tiring-women, too;
The queen was also followed by men both brave and true.
They found the youthful Hagen, who on the shore was standing;
And many men from Garadie, who with the wandering boy on the beach were landing.

===Chinese===
很快，人们送来了华贵的衣裳，还有侍奉梳妆的妇人；
跟随王后来的，也尽是忠勇可靠之士。
他们找到了年轻的哈根，他正伫立在岸上；
还有加拉迪的许多随员，正伴着这位流浪归来的少年，在海滩登临。
