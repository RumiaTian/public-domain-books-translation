## Tale XVIII How Ludwig Slew Hettel, and Stole Away in the Night / 第十八诗章 路德维希如何诛杀黑特尔，并趁夜潜逃

===Original===
Ludwig, king of the Normans, slays Hettel, and his army steals away with the maidens in the night. The Hegelings discover their departure, bury the dead, and erect a hospital and cloister in their memory.

===Chinese===
诺曼人之王路德维希（Ludwig）诛杀了黑特尔（Hettel），他的大军趁夜携少女们悄然遁走。黑格林人（Hegelings）发觉他们已去，埋葬死者，并建起一座济贫院与修道院以资纪念。

===Original===
High in hand their weapons
 Hettel and Ludwig bore﻿—
 Well had they been sharpened.
 Soon each knew the more

===Chinese===
黑特尔与路德维希
把兵刃高高擎起——
刀锋磨得锐利，
不多时各自便看清

===Original===
 Who was now his foeman,
 such strength they both were showing.
 Ludwig slew King Hettel;
 and out of this our mournful tale is growing.

===Chinese===
对面的敌手是何等人物，
两人都使出浑身解数。
路德维希诛杀了黑特尔国王；
我们这支哀歌，便由此生发。

===Original===
When the lord of Matelan
 upon the field lay slain,
 Soon ’twas told to his daughter:
 loudly then began

===Chinese===
马特兰（Matelan）之主
阵亡于沙场之上，
死讯不久便传到他女儿耳中：
古德伦（Gudrun）随即放声

===Original===
 Gudrun to mourn her father,
 so did many a maiden;
 Not one could stop her wailing:
 friends and foes alike were sorrow-laden.

===Chinese===
哀悼起自己的父亲，
众多少女也一同悲泣：
没有一人止得住她的哭号：
友与仇，无人不心怀哀恸。

===Original===
Soon as the grim old Wâ-te
 the death of the king did know,
 He cried and roared in anger.
 Like to the evening glow,

===Chinese===
狰狞的老瓦特（Wâ-te）
一得知国王的死讯，
登时狂呼怒吼。
恰似晚霞烧红了天，

===Original===
 Now were helmets blazing,
 beneath the strokes quick given
 By him and all his followers,
 who by their loss were unto madness driven.

===Chinese===
顶顶头盔此刻熊熊生光，
尽在他与全体部众
疾风般挥落的击打之下——
他们都因这一殇而狂乱若痴。

===Original===
However hard their fighting,
 how could it bring them good?
 Drenched was all the island
 with many knights’ hot blood.

===Chinese===
他们打得再苦再狠，
又怎能讨得好处？
整座海岛都浸透了
无数骑士滚烫的热血。

===Original===
 Not yet the Hegeling warriors
 to think of peace were ready;
 Away from the Wulpensand
 they only wished to bring Gudrun, their lady.

===Chinese===
黑格林人的武士
尚无意念及言和；
他们一心只想把女主人古德伦
带离这武尔彭沙洲（Wulpensand）。

===Original===
In stormy fight the Waal men
 bewreked the death of the king;
 To many a fighting Ortlander
 and hard-pressed Hegeling

===Chinese===
苦战中，瓦尔河（Waal）一带的人
为国王之死复了仇；
对多少浴血奋战的奥特兰（Ortland）人
和苦苦支撑的黑格林人，

===Original===
 Those who came from Denmark
 of friendship gave a token:
 Soon these knights so daring
 found in their hands their trusty weapons broken.

===Chinese===
从丹麦来的人
献上了友情的凭证：
不多时，这些剽悍的骑士
便见手中惯用的兵器纷纷折断。

===Original===
Now to avenge his father
 Ortwin bravely strove:
 Faithful to him did Horant
 and all his followers prove.

===Chinese===
如今奥特温（Ortwin）
奋勇要为父亲复仇：
霍兰特（Horant）与他全体部众
都证明对他忠心耿耿。

===Original===
 Night the field had darkened,
 the light of day was failing;
 Then were given to many
 wounds from which the life-blood fast was welling.

===Chinese===
夜幕罩暗了战场，
白昼的光辉正在消逝；
多少人于是身受重创，
创口里生命之血奔涌不止。

===Original===
Soon, in the dark, on Horant
 a Danish follower sprang;
 The sword that he was holding
 loud on the armor rang:

===Chinese===
黑暗里，一名丹麦随从
猛然扑向霍兰特；
只见他手中挥舞的剑
当啷一声击在甲胄上：

===Original===
 Thinking he was a foeman,
 Horant at once upon him
 Wrought most bitter sorrow:
 a deadly wound by that warrior brave was done him.

===Chinese===
霍兰特只当来的是仇敌，
当下便向他痛下杀手，
铸成至悲的悔恨：
一记致命的创伤，落在了这勇敢武士的身上。

===Original===
When Horant saw that his kinsman
 beneath his blow lay dead,
 Then he bade that his banner
 be borne with his own o’erhead.

===Chinese===
待霍兰特看清那亲族
已倒毙在自己剑下，
便传令把他的军旗
与自己的旗一同高擎。

===Original===
 The voice of him who was dying
 told whose life he had taken
 With his hand so rashly;
 sorely he mourned the friend who never would waken.

===Chinese===
那垂死之人开口的声音，
道出了他一时鲁莽
断送的是谁的性命；
他为此痛悼那再不会醒来的朋友。

===Original===
Loudly called out Herwic:
 “Murder here is done!
 Since we can see no longer,
 and daylight now is gone,

===Chinese===
赫尔维克（Herwic）高声呼喊：
「这里在自相残杀！
既然我们再看不见彼此，
天光又已散尽，

===Original===
 We all shall kill each other,
 friends and foes together.
 If this shall last till morning,
 two may be left to fight, but not another.”

===Chinese===
我们终会互相杀死，
朋友与仇敌搅作一处。
倘若厮杀延续到天明，
至多剩得两人还能相斗，再无第三人。」

===Original===
Where’er they saw old Wâ-te
 on the stormy fighting-ground,
 No one there was willing
 near him to be found;

===Chinese===
在鏖战酣烈的原野上，
人们但见老瓦特所在之处，
谁也不肯
靠近他半步；

===Original===
 No welcome, in his madness,
 was he to any giving:
 Many a foe he wounded,
 and laid on the spot that he would ne’er be leaving.

===Chinese===
他在狂怒之中
对谁也不留情面：
多少仇敌被他杀伤，
击倒在地，教他们永远离不开那片地方。

===Original===
’Twas well the foes were sundered
 until the break of day;
 On either side the foemen
 near each other lay,

===Chinese===
幸而敌对两军
就此分开，直到破晓；
两侧的仇敌
彼此相隔不远地卧着，

===Original===
 Wounded to death or slaughtered.
 Fast the light was waning,
 Not yet the moon was risen,
 and the Hegeling foe the field were nowhere gaining.

===Chinese===
或伤重垂死，或已遭屠戮。
天光迅速黯淡，
月亮还没有升起，
黑格林人的仇敌在战场上寸步未得。

===Original===
The warriors grim, unwillingly,
 to the strife now put a stop;
 The hands of all were weary
 ere they gave the struggle up:

===Chinese===
这些剽悍的武士，老大不情愿，
这才把厮杀止住；
到众人撒手放弃苦斗时，
一双手早已困乏不堪：

===Original===
 But, when the fight was over,
 they near each other loitered.
 Wherever fires were burning,
 for each the other’s shields and helmets glittered.

===Chinese===
可战斗一停，
他们仍在彼此近旁逗留。
但凡有火堆燃起之处，
各人都望见对方盾牌与头盔的闪光。

===Original===
Ludwig then and Hartmut,
 lords of the Norman land,
 Talked aside together.
 Then to his faithful band

===Chinese===
随后路德维希与哈特穆特（Hartmut），
这两位诺曼底之主，
避到一旁密谈。
接着，对着他的亲信部众，

===Original===
 Spake the elder warrior:
 “Why be longer staying
 So near the brave old Wâ-te,
 who all of us is madly bent on slaying?”

===Chinese===
年长的那位武士开言：
「何必再滞留在此，
紧挨着剽悍的老瓦特——
他正发了疯，定要把我们斩尽？」

===Original===
The wily king then bade them:
 “Lie low, and be not seen,
 With your heads upon your bucklers:
 you then must make a din;

===Chinese===
狡黠的国王随即吩咐：
「卧倒，别让人看见，
把头伏在圆盾上，
然后你们只管喧闹作声；

===Original===
 And so the men of the Hegelings
 my plan will not be knowing﻿—
 That, if I now can do it,
 I with you all may hence unseen be going.”

===Chinese===
这样，黑格林人
就识不破我的计谋——
只要此刻办得到，
我就带你们大家悄然离去，无人得见。」

===Original===
Ludwig’s men and kinsmen
 did as he had said:
 They upon their sackbuts
 and trumpets loudly played,

===Chinese===
路德维希的部众与亲族
依言而行：
他们在弯号（sackbut）与铜号上
高声吹奏起来，

===Original===
 As if they, by their prowess,
 the land had gained them wholly.
 Ludwig now to his followers
 showed his crafty plot and cunning fully.

===Chinese===
仿佛凭着他们的骁勇，
已把这地方全然赢到手里。
路德维希就此向部众
亮出了他诡计的全貌。

===Original===
Then were heard, on all sides,
 mingled shouts and cries;
 But wailing from the maidens
 was not allowed to rise:

===Chinese===
于是四面八方
响起混杂的呐喊与喧哗；
但少女们的哀泣
却不许扬起：

===Original===
 All who would not stop it
 were threatened death by drowning﻿—
 To be sunk beneath the waters﻿—
 if they were sobbing heard, or loudly moaning.

===Chinese===
凡不肯止住哭号的，
都被以溺死相威胁——
要把她沉入水底——
只要让人听见抽噎或高声呜咽。

===Original===
Whate’er was owned by the Normans
 now to the ships was ta’en;
 The dead were there left lying,
 e’en where they were slain.

===Chinese===
诺曼人所有的财物
这时都搬上了船；
死者被弃在原地，
就倒在各自丧命之处。

===Original===
 Friends were lost to many
 who, seeking, could not find them:
 So few there were still living,
 that many an empty ship was left behind them.

===Chinese===
许多人失去了同伴，
四下寻觅也找不见：
活着的人所剩无几，
以至于身后抛下许多空船。

===Original===
Thus unbeknown and slyly,
 sailed away o’er the main
 The men of the land of Normandy;
 great was the women’s pain

===Chinese===
就这样无人察觉、鬼鬼祟祟，
诺曼底之地的人
扬帆渡过大海；
妇人们何其伤痛——

===Original===
 From kinsfolk to be sundered,
 and yet to hush their weeping.
 Of this the men knew nothing
 who now upon the Wulpensand were sleeping.

===Chinese===
既要与亲族生生离散，
还得强自忍住啼泣。
此刻酣睡在武尔彭沙洲上的人
对此浑然不知。

===Original===
Before the day was dawning,
 well were on their way
 They whom the Danish warriors
 had thought that morn to slay.

===Chinese===
天色尚未破晓，
有一伙人早已上路——
正是丹麦武士们
本以为那天清晨可加诛戮的人。

===Original===
 Then Wâ-te bade that loudly
 his war-horn should be sounded;
 He was in haste to follow,
 and hoped erelong to fell them, deeply wounded.

===Chinese===
瓦特于是传令，
叫把战号大声吹响；
他急欲随后追赶，
指望不久便将他们重创砍翻。

===Original===
On foot and on their horses,
 the men of the Hegeling land
 All were seen together,
 flocking o’er the sand,

===Chinese===
黑格林之地的人，
或徒步或骑马，
只见他们聚作一处，
黑压压涌过沙地，

===Original===
 To fight the fleeing Normans;
 never in this they rested.
 Ludwig with his followers
 already far upon their way had hasted.

===Chinese===
去追击奔逃的诺曼人；
在这上头他们片刻不停。
而路德维希带着部众
早已遁出老远，一路疾行。

===Original===
Many ships lay empty,
 and clothing there was found;
 All about the Wulpensand
 ’twas scattered o’er the ground;

===Chinese===
许多船只空空如也，
还寻见不少衣物；
武尔彭沙洲上下，
衣物抛撒了一地；

===Original===
 Many weapons also
 were seen, with none to bear them.
 They had overslept their going,
 and never to harm their foes could they come near them.

===Chinese===
还有许多兵器
散见各处，无人执掌。
他们一觉睡过了敌人的离去，
从此再不能近身伤敌分毫。

===Original===
When this was told to Wâ-te,
 with anger he was torn:
 How for the death of Hettel
 he bitterly did mourn!

===Chinese===
这消息一报给瓦特，
他就被怒火撕扯：
他为黑特尔之死
悲恸得何等锥心！

===Original===
 And that on Ludwig’s body
 his wrath he was not wreaking!
 Helmets there lay shattered;
 for this must many a woman’s heart be aching.

===Chinese===
又恨这一腔怒火
没能发泄到路德维希身上！
那里头盔碎裂满地；
为此，多少妇人的心必在隐隐作痛。

===Original===
How gloomily and sadly
 now, in angry mood,
 Ortwin was bewailing
 the loss of his warriors good!

===Chinese===
奥特温此时何等阴郁哀伤，
在愤懑之中
悲悼他失去的
那些勇武的部下！

===Original===
 He said: “Rouse up, my fighters!
 we may perhaps o’ertake them
 Before they leave these waters;
 not far from shore we yet in flight may check them.”

===Chinese===
他说：「奋起吧，我的战士们！
我们或许还能追上他们，
赶在他们驶离这片海域之前；
离岸不远，我们尚能把这伙逃敌截住。」

===Original===
Willingly old Wâ-te
 would his bidding do:
 Fru-te the winds was watching,
 to learn which way they blew.

===Chinese===
老瓦特满口应承，
甘愿听从他的号令：
弗鲁特（Fru-te）观测着风，
看它朝哪一边吹。

===Original===
 Then said he to his kinsmen:
 “What helps it though we hasten?
 Mark what now I tell you:
 the thirty miles they’ve gained we ne’er can lessen.

===Chinese===
接着他对亲族们说道：
「纵使我们疾追，又有何益？
记住我此刻告诉你们的话：
他们已抢得的三十英里，我们再也缩短不了。

===Original===
“Moreover, we of fighters
 have not here enough
 That we in aught can harm them,
 e’en should we now set off:

===Chinese===
「再者，说到战士，
我们在此的人手不够，
纵然立即启程，
也伤不得他们分毫：

===Original===
 Scorn me not,” said Fru-te,
 “and to my words give heeding;
 What more to say is needful?
 Your foes you cannot reach, howe’er you’re speeding.

===Chinese===
莫要轻看我，」弗鲁特说，
「且把我的话听进心里：
还有什么多余可讲？
任你们追赶得再快，也够不着你们的仇敌。

===Original===
“Bid that now the wounded
 upon the ships be laid;
 Then on the field of battle
 let search for the dead be made,

===Chinese===
「传令：即刻把伤者
抬放到船上；
再到战场之上
把死者搜寻一番，

===Original===
 And bid that they be buried
 upon this strand forsaken,
 So friends may rest together;
 this good at least from them should not be taken.”

===Chinese===
并吩咐把他们安葬
在这片荒寂的海滨，
好让朋友们同眠一处；
至少这一件善举，不该从他们身上夺去。」

===Original===
All, standing there together,
 wringing their hands were seen.
 For this one sorrow only,
 would their lot have hapless been﻿—

===Chinese===
只见众人伫立一处，
个个绞扭着双手。
倘若只有这一桩悲恸，
他们的命途也已算不幸——

===Original===
 To lose the youthful maiden,
 Hilda’s lovely daughter.
 How, when they saw her mother,
 if home they went, could news so sad be brought her?

===Chinese===
痛失那如花的少女、
希尔达（Hilda）的爱女。
倘若回到家中、面见她的母亲，
这般凄惨的噩耗叫人如何开口？

===Original===
Then to them said Morunc:
 “Would there were nothing more,
 Beyond our own sad losses,
 for which our hearts are sore!

===Chinese===
这时莫伦克（Morunc）开口：
「但愿除了我们自家的
惨痛死丧之外，
再没有别的伤心事！

===Original===
 Small reward will be given
 for the news we home shall carry,
 That Hettel dead is lying:
 far from Hilda fain would I longer tarry.”

===Chinese===
我们回家要捎的音讯——
说黑特尔已横死在此——
断然换不来什么酬谢：
我倒情愿远远躲开希尔达，在此多留些时日。」

===Original===
Then went the warriors searching
 for the dead upon the sand.
 Those they knew were Christians
 who lay upon the strand,

===Chinese===
于是武士们在沙地上
四处搜寻死者。
凡他们认得是基督徒、
又倒在海滩上的，

===Original===
 As the Sturmisch Wâ-te bade them,
 were all together carried;
 Then both the old and the younger
 chose a spot whereon the dead were buried.

===Chinese===
都遵施图尔姆兰（Sturmisch）人瓦特的吩咐，
一并抬放到一处；
随后老少众人
择定了一块地，把死者安葬。

===Original===
Then said the knight, young Ortwin:
 “Let us bury them here;
 And thought must we be taking
 to build a church full near,

===Chinese===
年轻的骑士奥特温说道：
「就把他们安葬在这里；
我们还须费心
在近旁兴建一座教堂，

===Original===
 That they be not forgotten,
 while this their end is showing.
 For it shall all their kinsmen
 give of their wealth, each one his share bestowing.”

===Chinese===
好教他们不致被遗忘，
只要这终局还在向人昭示。
他们的亲族还应
各捐资财，人人献上一份。」

===Original===
Then spake the Sturmisch Wâ-te:
 “In this thou well hast said;
 We now should sell the horses
 and the clothing of the dead,

===Chinese===
施图尔姆兰人瓦特接着说：
「这话你算说对了；
我们该把死者的马匹
连同他们的衣裳变卖，

===Original===
 Who on the shore are lying;
 so, since their life is ended,
 Shall many poor and needy,
 with the wealth they left, be holpen and befriended.”

===Chinese===
他们正陈尸海滨；
既然性命已了，
就让许多贫穷匮乏的人
靠他们留下的财物，得蒙救济与眷顾。」

===Original===
Then asked the warrior Irold,
if foes who there lay dead
 Should also now be buried,
 or if wolves should on them feed,

===Chinese===
这时武士伊罗尔德（Irold）问道：
那些横尸此地的仇敌，
如今是否也该一体安葬，
还是任凭豺狼撕咬、

===Original===
 And hungry ravens tear them,
 that round their bodies hovered?
 Then to the wise they listened;
 none of the dead were left on the field uncovered.

===Chinese===
任凭盘旋在尸身周遭的
饿鸦去啄食？
众人听从了智者的意见：
死者无一被弃置荒野、暴骨无掩。

===Original===
When now the fight was over,
 and all were free from care,
 Hettel, their king, they buried,
 who for his daughter dear,

===Chinese===
此刻战事已了，
众人都卸下重负；
他们安葬了国王黑特尔——
他为着亲爱的女儿，

===Original===
 Upon this barren seashore,
 e’en unto death had striven.
 To others who had fallen,
 whate’er their land and name, was burial given.

===Chinese===
在这片荒瘠的海滨
直厮杀到生命最后一息。
对其他阵亡的人，
无论来自何方何名，都一体安葬。

===Original===
First, the men from Moorland
 each by himself they laid;
 The same was done for the Hegelings
 found among the dead;

===Chinese===
来自摩尔兰（Moorland）的人，
最先被一一分开安放；
对在死者中寻获的黑格林人，
也照此一一安置；

===Original===
 Unto the Normans, also,
 gave they graves allotted:
 Alone was each one buried,
 if Christian he were or heathen, it nothing booted.

===Chinese===
对诺曼人，同样
划定了各自的墓穴：
人人都是独自下葬，
是基督徒还是异教徒，全不相干。

===Original===
Until six days were over,
 busy were they, at their best,
 And never time were finding
 (for the warriors took no rest)

===Chinese===
直到六天期满，
他们都竭尽全力地忙碌，
始终寻不出空暇——
（因为武士们不肯歇息）——

===Original===
 To ask for dead and dying
 the grace of God in heaven,
 For sins of which they were guilty;
 that they for their misdeeds should be forgiven.

===Chinese===
去为死者与垂死之人
祈求天上上帝的恩宠，
赦免他们身负的罪愆，
好教他们的过恶得蒙宽宥。

===Original===
Saying mass and singing
 were later heard on the strand.
 Never was God so worshipped,
 in any other land,

===Chinese===
后来，海滨之上
响起了诵弥撒与唱圣咏之声。
无论在何方国土，
上帝从未受如此崇奉，

===Original===
 For the dead in stormy fighting.
 Wherever men were lying
 With their death-wounds smitten,
 holy priests they brought to shrive the dying.

===Chinese===
这都是为了风暴之战中的亡者。
但凡有人带着致命的创伤
倒卧之处，
他们都请来圣洁的教士，为垂死者行临终告解。

===Original===
Many there did tarry
 to care for the churchly men.
 A deed of gift was written,
 wherein it could be seen

===Chinese===
许多人留在那里，
照料这些教会中人。
一份捐赠文书写定，
从中可以看清：

===Original===
 How of land to the brothers
 three hundred hides was given.
 Far and wide ’twas bruited,
 that well a godly house was builded, and had thriven.

===Chinese===
赠予修士弟兄的土地，
足有三百海得。
于是风声远播四方：
一座敬神的殿宇已然建成，且日渐兴旺。

===Original===
All who there were mourning
 the loss of friends and kin
 Gave of their wealth a tithing,
 women as well as men,

===Chinese===
所有在那里哀悼
亲友沦亡的人，
无论女男，都捐出
自己财货的什一，

===Original===
 For weal of the souls of any
 whose bodies there lay buried.
 The cloister soon was wealthy,
 by the yield of three hundred hides, through toil unwearied.

===Chinese===
愿凡尸身葬在那里的人，
灵魂都得享福泽。
修道院很快富足起来——
凭三百海得的出产，加上不倦的辛劳。

===Original===
Now may God in his keeping
 have those who there lie dead,
 And the holy men there dwelling.
 Those then homeward sped

===Chinese===
愿上帝把长眠于此的死者、
连同居住其间的圣洁之人，
都收进他的庇护。
随后，那些人便启程还乡——

===Original===
 Who still upon the Wulpensand
 were left among the living;
 After all their sorrows,
 they reached their fatherland, no more in warfare striving.

===Chinese===
他们仍在武尔彭沙洲上、
幸存于世间；
历尽重重悲怆之后，
他们终于抵达故国，从此不再兴兵攻战。
