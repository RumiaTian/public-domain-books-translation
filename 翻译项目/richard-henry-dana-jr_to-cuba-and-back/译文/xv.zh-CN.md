## XV / 十五

===Original===
Matanzas﻿—The Cumbre﻿—The Yumurí﻿—The family of Mr. C﻿—﻿—﻿—Ensor’s.

Instead of the posada by the waterside, I take up my quarters at a hotel kept by Ensor, an American, and his sister. Here the hours, cooking, and chief arrangements are in the fashion of the country, as they should be, but there is more of that attention to guests which we are accustomed to at home, than the Cuban hotels usually give.

The objects to be visited here are the Cumbre and the valley of the Yumurí. It is too late for a morning ride, and I put off my visit until afternoon. Gazzaniga and some of the opera troupe are here; and several Americans at the hotel, who were at the opera last night, tell me that the people of Matanzas made a handsome show, and are of opinion that there was more beauty in the boxes than we saw at the Villanueva. It appears, too, that at the *Retreta*, in the Plaza de Armas, when the band plays, and at evening promenades, the ladies walk about, and do not keep to their carriages as in Havana.

===Chinese===
马坦萨斯——昆布雷山——尤穆里河谷——C——先生一家——恩索尔旅馆。

我没有住水边的那家客栈，改在恩索尔（Ensor）和他妹妹开的一家旅馆下榻。这里的作息、烹饪和主要安排都是本地的路数——本该如此——但对客人的那种殷勤，比古巴旅馆通常给的要多，是我们国内惯见的那一种。

此地要游览的去处是昆布雷山和尤穆里河谷。上午出游已来不及，我把行程推到下午。加扎尼加（Gazzaniga）和歌剧团的一些人正在这里；旅馆里几位昨晚去看过歌剧的美国人对我说，马坦萨斯人士衣香鬓影、颇见气象，并且认为包厢里的美人比我们在比利亚努埃瓦剧院（Villanueva）看到的还要多。据说在武器广场（Plaza de Armas）的「夜游」（*Retreta*）——乐队奏乐的时分——以及傍晚散步的时候，女士们是下地步行的，不像在哈瓦那那样只守在马车里。

===Original===
As soon as the sun began to decline, I set off for the Cumbre, mounted on a pacer, with a Negro for a guide, who rode, as I soon discovered, a better nag than mine. We cross the stone bridges, and pass the great hospital, which dominates over the town. A regiment, dressed in seersucker and straw hats, is drilling, by trumpet call, and drilling well, too, on the green in front of the barracks; while we take our winding way up the ascent of the Cumbre.

The bay, town, and shipping lie beneath us; the Pan rises in the distance to the height of some 3,000 feet; the ocean is before us, rolling against the outside base of the hills; and, on the inside, lies the deep, rich, peaceful valley of the Yumurí. On the top of the Cumbre, commanding the noblest view of ocean and valley, bay and town, is the *ingenio* of a Mr. Jenkes, a merchant, bearing a name that would put Spanish tongues to their trumps to sound, were it not that they probably take refuge in the Don Guillermo, or Don Enrique of his Christian name. The estate bears the name of La Victoria, and is kindly thrown open to visitors from the city. It is said to be a model establishment. The house is large, in a classic style, and costly, and the Negro quarters, the storehouses, mechanic shops, and sugar house are of dimensions indicating an estate of the first class.

On the way up from the city, several fine points of sight were occupied by villas, all of one story, usually in the Roman or Grecian style, surrounded by gardens and shade trees, and with every appearance of taste and wealth.

===Chinese===
太阳刚一偏西，我便动身前往昆布雷山，骑一匹走溜蹄马，由一个黑人当向导——我很快就发现，他胯下那匹比我这匹强。我们跨过石桥，经过俯瞰全城的大医院。一个团穿着皱纱军装、戴着草帽，正在兵营前的草地上听号令操练，而且操练得还不错；我们则取蜿蜒小道，登上昆布雷山的上坡路。

海湾、城镇和船只都在脚下；潘山（Pan）在远处耸起，高约三千英尺；海洋在面前，波涛拍打着山冈外侧的山脚；而内侧，便是深邃、丰饶、安宁的尤穆里河谷。昆布雷山顶上，雄踞着海洋与河谷、海湾与城镇最壮丽的景致，是一位詹克斯先生（Mr. Jenkes）——一个商人——的糖坊（*ingenio*）；他那个姓氏，倘若西班牙人的舌头不想办法躲进他教名里的「唐·纪廉」（Don Guillermo）或「唐·恩里克」（Don Enrique），真要把它们难为到极处。庄园名叫拉·维多利亚（La Victoria），好意对城里来的游人敞开。据说是一座模范庄园。宅子宏大，古典式样，造价不菲；黑人住区、库房、工匠作坊和糖坊的规模，都显出第一流庄园的气派。

从城里上山的路上，几处绝佳的观景点都让别墅占了，全是单层，多为罗马式或希腊式，围有花园和荫凉树，处处透出趣味与财富的气象。

===Original===
It is late, but I must not miss the Yumurí; so we dive down the short, steep descent, and cross dry brooks and wet brooks, and over stones, and along bridle paths, and over fields without paths, and by wretched hovels, and a few decent cottages, with yelping dogs and cackling hens and staring children, and between high, overhanging cliffs, and along the side of a still lake, and after it is so dark that we can hardly see stones or paths, we strike a bridle path, and then come out upon the road, and, in a few minutes more, are among the gaslights and noises of the city.

At the hotel, there is a New York company who have spent the day at the Yumurí, and describe a cave not yet fully explored, which is visited by all who have time﻿—abounding in stalactites, and, though much smaller, reminding one of the Mammoth Cave of Kentucky.

I cannot leave Matanzas without paying my respects to the family to whose kindness I owe so much. Mr. C﻿—﻿— lives in a part of the suburbs called Versailles, near the barracks, in a large and handsome house, built after the style of the country. There I spend an agreeable evening, at a gathering of nearly all the family, sons and daughters, and the sons-in-law and daughters-in-law. There is something strangely cosmopolitan in many of the Cuban families﻿—as in this, where are found French origin, Spanish and American intermarriage, education in Europe or the United States, home and property in Cuba, friendships and sympathies and half a residence in Boston or New York or Charleston, and three languages at command.

Here I learn that the Thirty Millions Bill has not passed, and, by the latest dates, is not likely to pass.

My room at Ensor’s is on a level with the courtyard, and a horse puts his face into the grating as I am dressing, and I know of nothing to prevent his walking in at the door, if he chooses, so that the Negro may finish rubbing him down by my looking glass. Yet the house is neatly furnished and cared for, and its keepers are attentive and deserving people.

===Chinese===
天色已晚，但尤穆里河谷不可错过；于是我们冲下又短又陡的坡，蹚过干涸的小溪和有水的小溪，越过石头，沿马道走，穿过没有路的田野，路过破烂的窝棚和几座体面的小屋，一路是狂吠的狗、咯咯叫的母鸡和瞪眼的孩子，穿行于高峻压顶的峭壁之间，沿着一片静水湖的岸边，直到天黑得几乎看不清石头和路径，才踏上一条马道，随后上了大路，再过几分钟，就置身于城市的煤气灯和人声之中了。

旅馆里有一伙纽约人，白天刚游罢尤穆里回来，说起一个尚未探明的岩洞，凡有工夫的人都要去一看——洞里钟乳石丛生，规模虽小得多，却叫人想起肯塔基的猛犸洞。

我不能不对那一家人致敬告别——我受他们的恩惠太多了。C——先生住在近兵营的一片叫凡尔赛（Versailles）的郊外，宅子宽大漂亮，是本地的式样。我在那里度过了一个愉快的夜晚，全家几乎聚齐了，儿子女儿、女婿儿媳济济一堂。许多古巴家庭都有一种奇特的四海为家的气质——这一家便是：法兰西的血统，西班牙人与美国人的联姻，在欧洲或合众国受的教育，家与产业在古巴，友谊与情感、外加半个居所在波士顿、纽约或查尔斯顿，还能自如运用三种语言。

在这里我得知，「三千万法案」没有通过，而且照最新的消息看，多半也通不过。

我在恩索尔旅馆的房间同院子齐平，更衣时一匹马把脸探进铁栅栏，我看不出有什么能拦住它从门口径直走进来——要是它乐意的话——好让那黑人凑着我的穿衣镜把它擦抹完。不过屋子布置得整洁、打理得尽心，店主夫妇也是殷勤可靠的人。
