## Endnotes / 尾注
===Original===

1. To be truthful, this is not my family motto (*nor* crest); but the real motto seemed a little too satirical to share with Mrs. Monnerie; and however overweening its substitute may appear, I have now hopes, and now misgivings, that it is true. ↩︎

===Chinese===

1. 说实话，这并非我家的家训（纹章也*不*是）；只是真正的家训拿去同莫纳里太太分享，未免太带讥诮；而这替代的一条不管显得多么自负，我如今时而怀着指望、时而生出疑惧，但愿它属实。 ↩︎
