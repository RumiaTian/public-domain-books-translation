## XLVI / 四十六

===Original===

Osberne Enters the City of the Sundering Flood

So, as aforesaid, the time wore till Marymass was over, and then came fresh tidings, to wit that the men of the Small Crafts and the lesser commons were risen against the Porte and the King, and had gotten to them the North Gate of the City, and were holding it against their foemen, together with that quarter of the city which lay round about it. The news hereof was sure, for it was brought to Longshaw one night by three of the weavers who had ridden on the spur to tell it to Sir Godrick, and these three men he knew well, and that they were trusty.

Now so it was both that it had not been easy at any time that war should find Longshaw not duly prepared, and also that at this time there was no tidings which Sir Godrick looked for more than this. Speedy therefore was his rede. For he gave into Osberne’s hands fifteen hundreds of his best men, and bade him ride to the City and the North Gate and see what the fields without the City looked like; and the very next morning the Red Lad and his rode out of Longshaw, having with them two of the said weaver-carles, but the third abode with Sir Godrick.

===Chinese===

奥斯伯恩进入洪流之城

且说，如前文所言，日子挨到圣母节过后，便又传来了新的消息，道是小行会的工匠与下层平民起事，反抗市府与国王，已把城中的北门夺到手里，连同北门四周那一坊市区，一并据守着抵御他们的敌人。这消息确凿无疑，因为一天夜里，有三名织工快马加鞭赶到朗肖，把它报知戈德里克爵士；这三人他都相熟，深知他们可靠。

原来事情恰是如此：一来，无论何时，要教战争寻见朗肖不曾妥为戒备，原不是件容易的事；二来，这时戈德里克爵士所盼的消息，再没有比这更切的了。因此他的方略定得极快。他把一千五百名精兵交到奥斯伯恩手里，命他驰往那城与北门，看看城外的原野是何等光景；次日一早，红衣少年便率部骑出朗肖，带着那几名织工汉子中的两个，第三个则留在戈德里克爵士身边。

Now so good were the Red Lad’s wayleaders and knew all the passages and roads so inly, and so diligent was the Red Lad himself and his men so good and trusty, that by the second day about sunset he was but five miles from the North Gate, and he and his covered by some scattering woodland that lay thereabout.

Straightway Osberne sends a half score of spies to get them to the City and see what was toward, and come back, they that were not slain, and tell him thereof. Straightway they went, and had such hap that all they came back unscathed, and this was their story: That the men of the Small Crafts were not by seeming hard pressed, for still their banners hung out of the North Gate and the wall and towers thereabout; but that both within the City had been bitter battle against them all day long, and also a host of men of their foes had come out from the East Gate, and were now lying round the North Gate in no very good order, because they looked for no peril save from them within the North Gate, and deemed that as for them they had enough on their hands to keep them within their walls, and least of all things did they look for any onfall from without.

Thereon the Red Lad called to him his captains and host-leaders and asked them of rede, and to be short therewith. Some said one thing, some another, as to send back news hereof to Sir Godrick, or to array them in the best wise to fall on these men on the morrow. Nay, some were for hanging about till they should have news of Sir Godrick.

红衣少年的引路人这般得力，对条条小径大道无不烂熟于心；红衣少年本人又这般勤勉，他的人马又这般精良可靠，因此到第二天日落时分，他离北门已不过五英里，一行人便借当地的一片疏林掩蔽了身形。

奥斯伯恩随即派出十名斥候，命他们潜进那城，探看动静，凡不曾被杀的，都回来报他。他们即刻去了，又得了好运，一个个毫发无伤地回来，所报如下：小行会的人看去并未被逼得很紧，因为他们的旗帜依旧悬在北门外和那一带的城墙塔楼上；只是城内整日恶战不断，矛头正对着他们；此外还有一大队敌人从东门出城，此刻正散乱地屯在北门四周，因为他们只道危险来自北门里的人，又想那些人光是困守墙内，已自顾不暇，万万料不到会有人从外面杀来。

于是红衣少年把手下的众队长与领兵头目召到跟前，问他们的方略，并要他们说简短些。有说这个的，有说那个的：或主张把这消息回报戈德里克爵士，或主张好生整顿阵伍，明日便向这些人扑杀。甚至还有人主张原地盘桓，等得了戈德里克爵士的消息再动。

But when they were done, spake the Red Lad: “Sirs, many of these things are good to be done, and some not; for sure am I that we be not sent hither to do nothing. But now if ye will, hearken my rede: it is now well-nigh dark, and in two hours or somewhat more it will be pit-mirk, and these men outside the walls will be going to their rest with no watch and ward set outward toward the upland. Wherefore I say, let us leave our horses here and do off so much of our armour as we may go afoot lightly; for if we win we shall soon get other horses and gear, and if we lose, we shall need them not. But meseemeth if we do deftly and swiftly, all these men we shall have at our will.”

Now they all saw that it would do; so there was no more said, but they fell to arraying their men on foot, and in an hour they were on the way; and going wisely and with little noise, in two hours thence they were amidst the foe and doing their will upon them; and when they were well entered in amongst them and had slain many, they fell to the blowing of horns and crying out, The Red Lad! the Red Lad! Longshaw for the Small Crafts! Then both there was no aid to come to the men of the Porte, whereas they were far away from the East Gate, and also they of the North Gate heard the horns and the cries, and guessed what was toward; so they issued out with torches and cressets and fell upon the foe crying their cries, and so it befell that none of that host of the Porte escaped save they who might make the night their cloak. Then was the gate thrown open, and the Red Lad and his entered, and ye may think whether the townsmen were joyous and made much of them. But when the tale of his men was told, Osberne found that but three of his were missing. And so soon as it was light, he sent back a band of his men to bring on their horses and armour. Thuswise first came Osberne to the City of the Sundering Flood.

===Chinese===

待众人说罢，红衣少年开口道：“诸位，这些主意有许多是可行的，也有一些不然；因为我确知，我们不是被派到此地来无所事事的。如今你们若愿意，且听我的方略：眼下天色将黑，再过两个钟头或略多一些，便要黑得伸手不见五指；城外这些人将要安歇，朝着高地那一面并不设一兵一卒的岗哨。因此我说，我们且把马匹留在此地，卸下身上碍事的甲胄，好教步下走得轻快；因为若得胜，我们很快便会有别的马匹和装备；若战败，也用不着它们了。依我看来，只要我们做得干净迅捷，这些人尽可由我们摆布。”

众人都看出此计可行，于是再无多话，各自把人马列为步队；一个钟头后，他们便已上路。他们走得机敏，动静极小，又过两个钟头，便已杀入敌群之中，任意施为；待深深突入敌阵、杀伤众多之后，他们便吹响号角，高声呐喊：“红衣少年！红衣少年！朗肖为小行会！”这一来，市府的人马既远离东门，无从往援；北门里的人又听得号角与呐喊，猜出了外间的情由，便举着火炬与篝灯呐喊杀出，扑向敌人。于是事情的结果是：市府那支人马无一逃脱，除非有谁能拿黑夜当遮身的斗篷。随后城门大开，红衣少年率众入城；城中百姓如何欢欣、如何厚待他们，不消说得。及至清点人马，奥斯伯恩发现自己部下只少了三名。天刚放亮，他便遣一队人回去接应马匹甲胄。奥斯伯恩就这样头一遭来到了洪流之城。
