## XXXI / 三十一

===Original===

They Meet Through Autumn and Winter

So many a time they met that autumn, and Elfhild would ever be asking him some boon; as the next time after this, it was the gifts which he had brought for her from the Cheaping; for in thinking of her he had clean forgotten them. So then was the merry time in talking of them, and shooting and hurling of them over, and the donning of them, and the talking of them again. Another time she prayed him to come clad in that goodly armour of the spoils of Deepdale, and he could no less than yeasay her, and there he was on the trysting-day, striding by the riverbank in the sun, like an heap of glittering ice hurrying before the river when the thaw is warm and the sun shining bright at Candlemas. And over that also went many pretty plays, as taking the pieces off, and naming them, and doing them on again, and the like.

So wore the days into winter, and yet the two saw each other full often even through the frost and snow and ill weather. And when the spring came, then it was dear to them indeed. And by that time had Osberne’s fears about the stealing of Elfhild much worn off; though it is to be said that exceeding oft his heart was weary and sore with the longing to hold her in his arms. Yet the most of these times he kept his grief in his own heart; so much as Elfhild was moved when it brake forth from him, and she might, so to say, see the torments of him before her very eyes. Indeed on one time, when for a long while she might not comfort him, she told him that this was almost as bad as seeing him laid a-dying before her.

But kind and dear they were to each other, and there was nought in them that was not lovely in those first days of their manhood.

===Chinese===

他们自秋徂冬的相会

那个秋天，他们相会了一回又一回，埃尔弗希尔德总有些恩典要向他讨：譬如紧接下来的那一次，她讨的便是他从东集给她捎来的那些礼物——只因他一心念着她，竟把礼物忘了个干净。于是便有一段快活时光：先把这些礼物谈论一番，再将它们或射或掷地送过河去，穿戴起来，接着又谈论一番。又有一回，她求他穿戴起那副得自深谷战利品的华美甲胄前来；他不能不点头应承她。于是到了相会之期那一天，他便在太阳底下沿着河岸昂然阔步，恰似圣烛节前后天气回暖、日光大亮之时，河面上一大团晶莹的浮冰顺流奔涌而去。在这一副甲胄上也有许多有趣的戏耍：把甲件一件件卸下，说出它们的名目，再一一穿戴上，诸如此类。

如此，日子便渐渐磨进了冬天；然而纵然霜冻雨雪、天气恶劣，两人依然频频相见。待到春天来临，那时节于他们才真是亲爱无比。到那时候，奥斯伯恩唯恐埃尔弗希尔德遭人劫夺的忧惧已消磨大半；虽说也须讲一句，他心里渴望将她拥入怀中，那份情思常常熬得他心头又疲又痛。然而这些时候，他大都将苦楚闷在自己心底；以致一旦迸发出来，埃尔弗希尔德便大为动容，简直可以说，他备受煎熬的情状就摆在她眼前。的确有一回，她好半天也宽慰不了他，便对他说，这几乎同眼看着他奄奄一息躺在自己面前一般难当。

只是两人彼此恩爱亲厚，在那成人之初的日子里，他们身上没有一处不是可爱的。
