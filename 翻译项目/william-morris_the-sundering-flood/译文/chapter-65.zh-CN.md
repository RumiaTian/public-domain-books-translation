## LXV / 六十五

===Original===

Osberne and Elfhild Make Themselves Known to Their People

Therewith the Carline sat down, and there was great cheer and rumour in the hall, and folk wondered what was to come next; but it is not to be said but that they had an inkling of what had befallen. Then Elfhild arose and cast off her grey clothes, and was clad thereunder in the finest of fine gear of gold and of green, and surely, said everybody, that never was such beauty seen in hall. And for a while people held their breaths, as they that see a wonder which they fear may pass away. And then a great shout rent the hall, and there it was done. A tall man rose in his place, a grey cloak fell from him, and he was clad all in glittering armour, and there was none that did not know him for Osberne Wulfgrimsson, who has been called the Red Lad. And he said in a bold and free voice: “See, my masters and dear friends, if I have not kept tryst with you; for it is of a sooth five years well told since I departed from Wethermel with little hope in my heart. And now forsooth is no hope in my heart, for all the hope has budded and blossomed and fruited, and I am yours and ye are mine while the days last. And this is the woman that I have won; and O I would that it had been earlier, though God wot I laboured at it. And now I think ye will be good to her as ye will be good to me, and what tale shall there be except of peace and quiet in these faraway upland vales?”

So passed the hours into deep night at Wethermel, and folk went to sleep scarce trowing in the wonders that they had heard and seen. And there were few among them that did not long for the dawn and the daylight, that they might once again cast eyes upon Osberne and his beloved. And hard it were to say which of those twain was the loveliest. But surely about both of them there was then and always a sweet wisdom that never went beyond what was due and meet for the land they lived in or the people with whom they dwelt. So that all round them the folk grew better and not the worser.

===Chinese===

奥斯伯恩与埃尔弗希尔德向自家亲族亮明身份

话音落处，老妪坐了下来，厅中顿时一片欢腾喧嚷，众人纷纷猜想接下来还有什么好戏；但若说他们对已然降临之事毫无所觉，那也不是实话——他们心里多少都有了些影子。这时埃尔弗希尔德站起身来，褪下身上的灰衣，里面穿的竟是金绿二色、精细至极的华服；人人都说，厅堂之上从未见过这般美貌。有一阵子众人都屏住了呼吸，如同眼见一件奇珍，惟恐它转眼便要消逝。随即一声高呼震彻大厅——事情就此成了。一个高大的汉子在座中站起身来，一袭灰色披风从他身上滑落，露出周身灿亮的甲胄，竟没有一个人认不出他就是奥斯伯恩·伍尔夫格里姆森——那位人称红衣少年的。只听他以豪迈爽朗的声音说道：“看哪，诸位主人家、亲爱的朋友们，我这不是践了同你们的相会之约么？说句实话，自我怀着渺茫的指望离开韦瑟梅尔，屈指算来，足足五个年头了。如今我心里当真再无半点指望，因为所有的指望都已抽芽、开花、结出了果实；只要天长日久，我便是你们的，你们便是我的。这位便是我赢得的女子；啊，但愿这一天来得早些才好，虽然老天知道，我为此下了多少力气。如今我想，你们怎样善待我，也会怎样善待她；在这偏远的高地山谷之间，除了安宁清静的日子，还能讲出什么别的故事来呢？”

在韦瑟梅尔，时辰就这样过去，直到深夜；众人上床安歇时，还几乎不敢相信自己亲耳听到、亲眼见到的这些奇事。他们中间，很少有人不盼着破晓天明，好再一睹奥斯伯恩和他的爱人的丰采。要说这两人之中哪一个更可爱，那可难分高下。但千真万确，他们两人身上，从那时起直到永远，都有一种温厚的睿智，从不逾越他们所居的这片土地、所共处的这些乡人所应有的分寸。因此，在他们四周，人们都日渐向善，而非变坏。
