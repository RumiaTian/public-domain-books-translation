## LXVIII / 六十八

===Original===

The Knight of Longshaw Gathereth Force

The Knight of Longshaw abode at Wethermel in much content, and much it pleased him to look upon the beauty of Elfhild and the fairness of the life that men lived in the Dale. At last he said: “Now I must shake off my sloth somewhat, and it will be a case of farewell.” “Will it?” said Osberne. “Yea,” said the Knight, “for I will to Eastcheaping, and there I will set me to gather men, and I look to it that, ere three months are over, I shall have a good host on foot.” “It is well,” said Osberne.

So in two days’ time the Knight went, with his two men that had fled into the Dale with him, to Eastcheaping, and Osberne rode with him. When they came to Eastcheaping the Knight said: “Now is the time for farewell.” “Nay, nay,” said Osberne, “there shall be no farewell this time at least; but I will help thee with the gathering of men, and when we have got an host I will be the leader thereof. This thou must not gainsay me.” Said the Knight: “But gainsay thee I will, for unless thou gettest thee back to thine own people I will break up my whole purpose.” “And why?” said Osberne. “Thou art blind not to see,” said the Knight. “I come and find thee here as happy as any man in the world, wedded to a fair wife, the lord of a stout and stalwart people who love thee above all things. And I have that in me that tells me that if I carry thee away I carry thee away to death. For I have seen thee in a dream of the night and in a dream of the day living at Wethermel and dying on a field near the City of the Sundering Flood.”

朗肖骑士集结兵马

朗肖骑士在韦瑟梅尔住了些时日，甚是安适；眼见埃尔弗希尔德的美貌，又见谷中人过日子那般美好，他心中大感欣悦。末了他说道：“如今我须得稍稍抖落身上的疏懒，这该是道别的时候了。”“该是道别么？”奥斯伯恩道。“正是，”骑士说，“我要去东集，在那里着手聚集人马，我预计不出三个月，便可有一支像样的大军编成。”“这就好了。”奥斯伯恩道。

于是过了两天，骑士便带着当初随他逃进谷里来的那两名部下动身去东集，奥斯伯恩与他同行。到得东集，骑士说道：“如今是话别的时候了。”“不，不，”奥斯伯恩说，“至少这一回，再没有什么话别；聚集人马之事，我要助你一臂之力，待大军聚齐，我便做它的统领。这一点你不许回驳我。”骑士道：“我偏要回驳你，因为除非你回到你自己人身边去，我便把我全盘的打算作罢。”“这是为何？”奥斯伯恩问。“你竟瞧不出来，真是瞎了眼，”骑士道，“我来到这里，见你快活得赛过世上任何人：娶了美貌的妻子，做着一族坚毅壮勇之民的主上，而他们爱你又胜过一切。况且我心里有个声音告诉我，我若把你带走，便是把你往死路上带。因为我曾在夜半的梦里、白昼的梦里见过你：活着住在韦瑟梅尔，却死在洪流之城近旁的一片战场上。”

Said Osberne: “And shall I choose dishonour then?”

“Nay,” he said, “where is the dishonour? Besides, take this for a gibe, that whereas time agone I could do but ill without thee, now I can do without thee well, for I have three or four fellows will come to my call as soon as they know that my banner is in the field again. Wherefore, I tell thee, thou must either be my unfriend, or get thee back home my friend and my lad.” So when Osberne saw it would no better be, he wept and bade farewell to the Knight of Longshaw, and went his ways back home. Six months hence he heard true tidings of the Knight, that he had gathered an host and fallen on his foes, and had fared nowhere save to thrive. And it is not said that he met the Knight of Longshaw face to face again in this life.

It is further to be told that once in every quarter Osberne went into that same dale wherein he first met Steelhead, and there he came to him, and they had converse together; and though Osberne changed the aspect of him from year to year, as for Steelhead he changed not at all, but was ever the same as when Osberne first saw him, and good love there was between those twain.

Now is there no more to say concerning the Sundering Flood and those that dwelt thereby.

奥斯伯恩道：“那么，难道要我拣选耻辱不成？”

“不，”他说，“耻辱又从何说起？此外，你且把这话当作一句戏言：从前没有你，我凡事办不大好；如今没有你，我倒办得好了，因为我有三四个伙伴，只消得知我的大旗重又上了战阵，便会一召即至。所以我告诉你：你要么做我的仇敌，要么回你家里去，做我的朋友、我的孩子。”奥斯伯恩见事情再无别的转圜，便哭了，与朗肖骑士道了别，取路回家去了。过了六个月，他听到骑士的真实音讯：他已聚起一支大军，进击众仇敌，所到之处无不如意兴旺。至于他此生再与朗肖骑士当面相见，书中却没有这样的记载。

此外还有一节要说的：每逢一季，奥斯伯恩总要去一趟他初遇钢首的那条山谷，在那里与钢首相会，二人对坐交谈；尽管奥斯伯恩的容貌一年年变迁，钢首却丝毫不变，始终同奥斯伯恩初见他时一模一样，而那两人之间，情爱深笃。

至于分离洪流，和居住在它两岸的人们，如今再没有别的话可说了。
