### Present at a Hanging / 目睹绞刑

===Original===
An old man named Daniel Baker, living near Lebanon, Iowa, was suspected by his neighbors of having murdered a peddler who had obtained permission to pass the night at his house. This was in 1853, when peddling was more common in the Western country than it is now, and was attended with considerable danger. The peddler with his pack traversed the country by all manner of lonely roads, and was compelled to rely upon the country people for hospitality. This brought him into relation with queer characters, some of whom were not altogether scrupulous in their methods of making a living, murder being an acceptable means to that end. It occasionally occurred that a peddler with diminished pack and swollen purse would be traced to the lonely dwelling of some rough character and never could be traced beyond. This was so in the case of "old man Baker," as he was always called. (Such names are given in the western "settlements" only to elderly persons who are not esteemed; to the general disrepute of social unworth is affixed the special reproach of age.) A peddler came to his house and none went away—that is all that anybody knew.

===Chinese===
一个名叫丹尼尔·贝克（Daniel Baker）的老人住在爱荷华州黎巴嫩附近，被邻居们怀疑谋杀了一个获准在他家借宿一晚的行商。那是1853年，当时行商在西部乡间比现在更为常见，也伴随着相当大的危险。行商背着货包，沿着各种荒僻的道路走村串户，不得不依靠乡民的款待。这使他与一些古怪的人物打交道，其中有些人谋生的手段并非全然正当，谋杀便是达成目的的可接受手段之一。偶尔会有行商——货包瘪了，钱袋鼓了——被人追踪到某个粗人的偏僻住处，然后就再也追踪不下去了。"贝克老头"——人们总是这样称呼他——的情况便是如此。（在西部"拓荒地"，这类称呼只给那些不受敬重的老年人；社会地位低下的普遍声名之上，又加上了年老这一特殊耻辱。）一个行商来到他家，没有人离开——大家所知仅此而已。

===Original===
Seven years later the Rev. Mr. Cummings, a Baptist minister well known in that part of the country, was driving by Baker's farm one night. It was not very dark: there was a bit of moon somewhere above the light veil of mist that lay along the earth. Mr. Cummings, who was at all times a cheerful person, was whistling a tune, which he would occasionally interrupt to speak a word of friendly encouragement to his horse. As he came to a little bridge across a dry ravine he saw the figure of a man standing upon it, clearly outlined against the gray background of a misty forest. The man had something strapped on his back and carried a heavy stick—obviously an itinerant peddler. His attitude had in it a suggestion of abstraction, like that of a sleepwalker. Mr. Cummings reined in his horse when he arrived in front of him, gave him a pleasant salutation and invited him to a seat in the vehicle—"if you are going my way," he added. The man raised his head, looked him full in the face, but neither answered nor made any further movement. The minister, with good-natured persistence, repeated his invitation. At this the man threw his right hand forward from his side and pointed downward as he stood on the extreme edge of the bridge. Mr. Cummings looked past him, over into the ravine, saw nothing unusual and withdrew his eyes to address the man again. He had disappeared. The horse, which all this time had been uncommonly restless, gave at the same moment a snort of terror and started to run away. Before he had regained control of the animal the minister was at the crest of the hill a hundred yards along. He looked back and saw the figure again, at the same place and in the same姿态 as when he had first observed it. Then for the first time he was conscious of a sense of the supernatural and drove home as rapidly as his willing horse would go.

===Chinese===
七年后的一天夜里，当地颇为知名的浸礼会牧师卡明斯先生（Rev. Mr. Cummings）驾车经过贝克的农场。天不算太暗：轻薄的雾霭之上某处悬着一弯残月。卡明斯先生一向是个开朗的人，吹着口哨，偶尔停下来友好地鼓励一下他的马。当他来到横跨一条干涸沟壑的小桥时，看见一个人影站在桥上，在雾蒙蒙的灰色树林背景前轮廓分明。那人背上绑着什么东西，手里拿着一根粗棍——显然是个流动行商。他的姿态有一种出神的味道，像是梦游者。卡明斯先生在那人面前勒住马，愉快地打了招呼，请他上车——"如果你跟我同路的话，"他补充道。那人抬起头，直视他的脸，既没有回答也没有进一步的动作。牧师和善而坚持地重复了他的邀请。这时那人将右手从身侧向前伸出，指向下方，同时站在桥的最边缘。卡明斯先生越过他看向沟壑深处，没发现什么异常，便收回目光准备再跟那人说话。那人已经消失了。马一直异常不安，此刻发出一声惊恐的嘶鸣，开始狂奔。等牧师重新控制住坐骑时，已在百码外的山丘顶上。他回头望去，又看到了那个人影，站在同一个地方，姿态和他第一次看到时一模一样。直到此刻，他才意识到一种超自然的感觉，于是拼命催马往家赶去。

===Original===
On arriving at home he related his adventure to his family, and early the next morning, accompanied by two neighbors, John White Corwell and Abner Raiser, returned to the spot. They found the body of old man Baker hanging by the neck from one of the beams of the bridge, immediately beneath the spot where the apparition had stood. A thick coating of dust, slightly dampened by the mist, covered the floor of the bridge, but the only footprints were those of Mr. Cummings' horse.

===Chinese===
到家后他把这次经历告诉了家人，第二天一早，他在两位邻居约翰·怀特·科威尔（John White Corwell）和阿布纳·雷泽（Abner Raiser）的陪同下返回了那个地方。他们发现贝克老头的尸体被吊在桥的一根横梁上，就在那幻影站立之处的正下方。一层厚厚的灰尘——被薄雾微微浸湿——覆盖着桥面，但唯一的脚印是卡明斯先生的马留下的。

===Original===
In taking down the body the men disturbed the loose, friable earth of the slope below it, disclosing human bones already nearly uncovered by the action of water and frost. They were identified as those of the lost peddler. At the double inquest the coroner's jury found that Daniel Baker died by his own hand while suffering from temporary insanity, and that Samuel Morritz was murdered by some person or persons to the jury unknown.

===Chinese===
在取下尸体时，他们弄松了下方斜坡上松散易碎的泥土，露出了一些人骨——已被水流和霜冻的作用几乎完全暴露出来。经辨认，这些骨头属于那个失踪的行商。在双重验尸中，验尸陪审团裁定丹尼尔·贝克系因暂时性精神错乱而自缢身亡，而塞缪尔·莫里茨（Samuel Morritz）系被陪审团无法确认的某人或某些人谋杀。
