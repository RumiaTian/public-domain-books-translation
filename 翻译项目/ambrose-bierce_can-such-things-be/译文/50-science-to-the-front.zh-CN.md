## Science to the Front / 科学先行

===Original===
In connection with this subject of "mysterious disappearance"—of which every memory is stored with abundant example—it is pertinent to note the belief of Dr. Hern, of Leipzig; not by way of explanation, unless the reader may choose to take it so, but because of its intrinsic interest as a singular speculation. This distinguished scientist has expounded his views in a book entitled *Verschwinden und Seine Theorie*, which has attracted some attention, "particularly," says one writer, "among the followers of Hegel, and mathematicians who hold to the actual existence of a so-called non-Euclidean space—that is to say, of space which has more dimensions than length, breadth, and thickness—space in which it would be possible to tie a knot in an endless cord and to turn a rubber ball inside out without 'a solution of its continuity,' or in other words, without breaking or cracking it."

===Chinese===
关于"神秘失踪"这一话题——每一段记忆中都储存着丰富的案例——有必要提一提莱比锡（Leipzig）的赫恩博士（Dr. Hern）的信念；并非为了解释，除非读者愿意这样看，而是因为它作为一种奇特的猜想具有内在的趣味。这位杰出的科学家在一本题为《消失及其理论》（*Verschwinden und Seine Theorie*）的书中阐述了自己的观点，这本书引起了一些关注，"尤其是，"一位作者说，"在黑格尔的追随者以及坚持所谓非欧空间（non-Euclidean space）实际存在的数学家中——也就是说，空间的维度不止长、宽、厚——在这样的空间里，可以在一根无尽的绳子上打一个结，可以把一个橡皮球翻转过来而不'破坏其连续性'，换句话说，不把它弄裂或弄碎。"

===Original===
Dr. Hern believes that in the visible world there are void places—vacua, and something more—holes, as it were, through which animate and inanimate objects may fall into the invisible world and be seen and heard no more. The theory is something like this: Space is pervaded by luminiferous ether, which is a material thing—as much a substance as air or water, though almost infinitely more attenuated. All force, all forms of energy must be propagated in this; every process must take place in it which takes place at all. But let us suppose that cavities exist in this otherwise universal medium, as caverns exist in the earth, or cells in a Swiss cheese. In such a cavity there would be absolutely nothing. It would be such a vacuum as cannot be artificially produced; for if we pump the air from a receiver there remains the luminiferous ether. Through one of these cavities light could not pass, for there would be nothing to bear it. Sound could not come from it; nothing could be felt in it. It would not have a single one of the conditions necessary to the action of any of our senses. In such a void, in short, nothing whatever could occur. Now, in the words of the writer before quoted—the learned doctor himself nowhere puts it so concisely: "A man enclosed in such a closet could neither see nor be seen; neither hear nor be heard; neither feel nor be felt; neither live nor die, for both life and death are processes which can take place only where there is force, and in empty space no force could exist." Are these the awful conditions (some will ask) under which the friends of the lost are to think of them as existing, and doomed forever to exist?

===Chinese===
赫恩博士相信，在可见世界中存在虚空地带——真空，而且不仅仅是真空——可以说是某种孔洞，有生命和无生命的物体都可能通过这些孔洞坠入不可见的世界，从此再无人能看见或听见。这一理论大致如下：空间充满了光以太（luminiferous ether），这是一种物质性的东西——如同空气或水一样是实体，只不过几乎无限稀薄。一切力、一切形式的能量都必须通过它来传播；凡是发生的过程，都必须在它之中进行。但让我们假设，在这本应无处不在的介质中存在着空洞，就像地球上有洞穴、瑞士奶酪中有气室一样。在这样一个空洞中，绝对空无一物。那将是一种人工无法制造的真空；因为即使我们从容器中抽出空气，光以太仍然存在。光无法穿过这样一个空洞，因为没有什么载体可以承载它。声音也传不出来；其中什么也感觉不到。它不具备我们任何一种感官运作所需的哪怕一个条件。简而言之，在这样的虚空中，什么也不会发生。现在，用前面那位作者的话说——博学的博士本人从未如此简洁地表述过："一个被关在这样一间密室中的人，既不能看见，也不能被看见；既不能听见，也不能被听见；既不能感觉，也不能被感觉；既不能活，也不能死，因为生与死都是只有在有力存在之处才能发生的过程，而在真空中不可能存在任何力。"（有人会问）这就是失踪者的亲友不得不想象他们身处其中、并注定永远身处其中的可怕处境吗？

===Original===
Baldly and imperfectly as here stated, Dr. Hern's theory, in so far as it professes to be an adequate explanation of "mysterious disappearances," is open to many obvious objections; to fewer as he states it himself in the "spacious volubility" of his book. But even as expounded by its author it does not explain, and in truth is incompatible with some incidents of, the occurrences related in these memoranda: for example, the sound of Charles Ashmore's voice. It is not my duty to indue facts and theories with affinity.

===Chinese===
尽管赫恩博士的理论在此表述得粗略而不完善，但就其自称足以解释"神秘失踪"而言，它存在许多明显的缺陷；这些缺陷在他自己书中那"洋洋洒洒的长篇大论"中倒是少了一些。然而即便是作者本人的阐述，也无法解释、而且事实上与这些记录中所述事件的某些情节相矛盾：例如查尔斯·阿什莫尔（Charles Ashmore）的声音。我没有义务去撮合事实与理论使之相容。
