## The Damned Thing / 该死的东西

### I One Does Not Always Eat What Is on the Table / 一 人并非总是吃桌上的东西

===Original===
By the light of a tallow candle which had been placed on one end of a rough table a man was reading something written in a book. It was an old account book, greatly worn; and the writing was not, apparently, very legible, for the man sometimes held the page close to the flame of the candle to get a stronger light on it. The shadow of the book would then throw into obscurity a half of the room, darkening a number of faces and figures; for besides the reader, eight other men were present. Seven of them sat against the rough log walls, silent, motionless, and the room being small, not very far from the table. By extending a arm any one of them could have touched the eighth man, who lay on the table, face upward, partly covered by a sheet, his arms at his sides. He was dead.

===Chinese===
借着放在一张粗糙桌子一端的牛油蜡烛的光亮，一个男人正在读一本写满字的旧簿子。那是一本磨损严重的旧账本；字迹显然不太清晰，因为那人有时把书页凑近烛焰以便看得更清楚。书本的影子随之遮暗了半个房间，使许多张面孔和身影隐入黑暗；因为除了读书人，另外还有八个人在场。其中七人靠着粗原木墙壁坐着，一动不动，默不作声，房间不大，他们离桌子都不远。伸出一只手臂，他们中任何一个人都能触碰到第八个人——他仰面躺在桌子上，部分被一张床单覆盖，双臂搁在身体两侧。他已经死了。

===Original===
The man with the book was not reading aloud, and no one spoke; all seemed to be waiting for something to occur; the dead man only was without expectation. From the blank darkness outside came in, through the aperture that served for a window, all the ever unfamiliar noises of night in the wilderness—the long nameless note of a distant coyote; the stilly pulsing thrill of tireless insects in trees; strange cries of night birds, so different from those of the birds of day; the drone of great blundering beetles, and all that mysterious chorus of small sounds that seem always to have been but half heard when they have suddenly ceased, as if conscious of an indiscretion. But nothing of all this was noted in that company; its members were not overmuch addicted to idle interest in matters of no practical importance; that was obvious in every line of their rugged faces—obvious even in the dim light of the single candle. They were evidently men of the vicinity—farmers and woodsmen.

===Chinese===
读书的人没有朗出声来，也没有人说话；所有人都似乎在等待某事发生；只有死者无所期待。从外面漆黑的虚空中，透过充当窗户的孔洞，传来荒野夜晚那始终令人陌生的声响——远处郊狼悠长而无名的嗥叫；树木中不知疲倦的昆虫那安静而脉动的颤鸣；夜鸟的怪异啼叫，与白日鸟儿的鸣叫截然不同；笨拙的大甲虫的嗡鸣，以及那神秘的小声音合唱——它们似乎总是在戛然而止时才被半意识地听到，仿佛意识到自己失了分寸。但在场的人没有注意到这些；他们对那些无关紧要的闲事并不太感兴趣；这在他们粗犷面孔的每一道线条中都显而易见——即便在一支蜡烛的昏暗光线下也是如此。他们显然是附近的居民——农夫和樵夫。

===Original===
The person reading was a trifle different; one would have said of him that he was of the world, worldly, albeit there was that in his attire which attested a certain fellowship with the organisms of his environment. His coat would hardly have passed muster in San Francisco; his footgear was not of urban origin, and the hat that lay by him on the floor (he was the only one uncovered) was such that if one had considered it as an article of mere personal adornment he would have missed its meaning. In countenance the man was rather prepossessing, with just a hint of sternness; though that he may have assumed or cultivated, as appropriate to one in authority. For he was a coroner. It was by virtue of his office that he had possession of the book in which he was reading; it had been found among the dead man's effects—in his cabin, where the inquest was now taking place.

===Chinese===
读书的人略有不同；人们会说他属于尘世、深谙世故，尽管他的衣着表明他与周遭的生物有着某种亲近。他的外套在旧金山恐怕过不了关；他的鞋履并非城市出身，躺在他身旁地板上的帽子（他是唯一没戴帽子的人）如果仅被视为个人装饰品，那便误解了它的意义。此人面相颇为令人好感，带着一丝威严；尽管那可能是他刻意摆出或培养的，以符合一个掌权者的身份。因为他是一名验尸官（coroner）。正是凭借他的职务之便，他才持有手中这本簿子；它是在死者遗物中找到的——就在他的小屋里，验尸审讯（inquest）正在进行。

===Original===
When the coroner had finished reading he put the book into his breast pocket. At that moment the door was pushed open and a young man entered. He, clearly, was not of mountain birth and breeding: he was clad as those who dwell in cities. His clothing was dusty, however, as from travel. He had, in fact, been riding hard to attend the inquest.

===Chinese===
验尸官读完后把簿子放进胸前口袋。就在这时，门被推开，一个年轻人走了进来。他显然不是在山里出生长大的：衣着像城市里的人。不过他的衣服满是尘土，像是赶了远路。事实上，他一路策马疾驰赶来参加这次验尸审讯。

===Original===
The coroner nodded; no one else greeted him.

===Chinese===
验尸官点了点头；其他人无人招呼。

===Original===
"We have waited for you," said the coroner. "It is necessary to have done with this business tonight."

===Chinese===
"我们一直在等你，"验尸官说，"今晚必须把这件事了结。"

===Original===
The young man smiled. "I am sorry to have kept you," he said. "I went away, not to evade your summons, but to post to my newspaper an account of what I suppose I am called back to relate."

===Chinese===
年轻人微微一笑。"很抱歉让你们久等了，"他说，"我离开不是为了躲避传唤，而是去向我的报社寄一篇关于我猜想自己被叫回来要讲述之事的报道。"

===Original===
The coroner smiled.

===Chinese===
验尸官微微一笑。

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Original===
"That," replied the other, rather hotly and with a visible flush, "is as you please. I used manifold paper and have a copy of what I sent. It was not written as news, for it is incredible, but as fiction. It may go as a part of my testimony under oath."

===Chinese===
"那个，"对方略带激动地回答，脸涨得通红，"悉听尊便。我用的是复写纸，寄出的那份有底稿。我写的不是新闻，因为它令人难以置信，而是小说。它可以作为我宣誓证词的一部分。"

===Original===
"But you say it is incredible."

===Chinese===
"但你说它令人难以置信。"

===Original===
"That is nothing to you, sir, if I also swear that it is true."

===Chinese===

===Original===
The coroner was silent for a time, his eyes upon the floor. The men about the sides of the cabin talked in whispers, but seldom withdrew their gaze from the face of the corpse. Presently the coroner lifted his eyes and said: "We will resume the inquest."

===Chinese===
验尸官沉默了一会儿，目光落在地板上。小屋两侧的人们低声交谈，但很少把目光从死者脸上移开。不久，验尸官抬起眼睛说："我们继续验尸审讯。"

===Original===
The men removed their hats. The witness was sworn.

===Chinese===
众人摘下帽子。证人宣誓。

Some of the jurors laughed. Against a sombre background humor shows high lights. Soldiers in the intervals of battle laugh easily, and a jest in the death chamber conquers by surprise.

===Chinese===
