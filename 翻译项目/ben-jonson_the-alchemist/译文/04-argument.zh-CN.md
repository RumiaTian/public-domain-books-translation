## Argument / 本事

===Original===
The sickness hot, a master quit, for fear, His house in town, and left one servant there; Ease him corrupted, and gave means to know

A Cheater, and his punk; who now brought low, Leaving their narrow practice, were become Cozeners at large; and only wanting some House to set up, with him they here contract, Each for a share, and all begin to act. Much company they draw, and much abuse, In casting figures, telling fortunes, news, Selling of flies, flat bawdry with the stone, Till it, and they, and all in fume are gone.

===Chinese===
瘟疫正炽，主人畏祸离城，撇下城中宅子，只留一仆看守；安逸腐蚀了这仆人，使他得以结识

一个骗子，和骗子同居的娼妇；这两人本已落魄，弃了小打小闹的营生，正想改做大局；只缺一处开张的宅院，便与他当场立约，各占一股，三人同台开演。主顾盈门，骗术百出：排盘起数、算命、卖新闻，兜售灵蝇（flies，侍灵小鬼），借石头公然拉皮条，直到石头、骗子和这一切随烟散尽。
