## V / 第五章

===Original===

How He Brought the Lady Margaret to the Justice-House

===Chinese===

他如何将玛格丽特夫人带往审判厅

===Original===

Through the streets of Belrémy, past staring townsfolk and saluting men-at-arms, Simon rode, a veiled and cloaked lady on a white palfrey beside him, sitting very upright in her saddle. Behind came Edmund with another lady, veiled also, and speaking never a word. To the justice-house they went, walking sedately, and there dismounted.

Out came Malvallet, armour-clad but bareheaded, his dark eyes eager.

“God be thanked, Simon!” he said fervently. Then he saw the tall woman beside Simon, and stepped back a pace. “What’s to do now, lad?”

===Chinese===

西蒙（Simon）策马穿过贝雷米（Belrémy）的街道，两旁尽是张目围观的市民与举手行礼的甲士；他身侧并辔而行的是一位蒙纱着氅的夫人，骑一匹白色驯马，端端正正立在鞍上。埃德蒙（Edmund）殿后，陪着另一位夫人，同样蒙着面纱，自始至终一言不发。一行人缓辔徐行，来到审判厅，在那里下了马。

马尔瓦莱（Malvallet）迎了出来，一身铠甲却未戴盔，一双黑眼睛热切得很。

“感谢上帝，西蒙！”他恳切地说。随即他瞥见西蒙身旁那个高挑的女人，倒退了一步。“这又是唱的哪一出，小子？”

===Original===

Simon did not answer him, for the guard-men were eyeing him cautiously. He bowed stiffly to his charge.

“Enter, madame.”

Marvelling, and all perplexed, Malvallet stood aside to let the lady pass. She swept by him, into the lofty hall where Simon transacted all his affairs, and where sat Bernard of Talmayne, busily writing. Bernard stood up, astonished at the sudden entrance of two ladies in the company of his lord.

===Chinese===

西蒙没有答话，因为卫兵们正警惕地打量着他。他朝自己护送的贵客僵硬地一躬身。

“请进，夫人。”

马尔瓦莱又惊又惑，闪身给夫人让路。她裙裾生风地从他身旁掠过，径入那座高敞的大厅——西蒙在那里料理一切公务，塔尔曼的伯纳德（Bernard of Talmayne）正伏案疾书。伯纳德站起身来，惊见两位夫人竟随他家大人联袂而至。

===Original===

With a quick, impatient movement the Lady Margaret flung back her veil. Geoffrey caught his breath at the sight of her proud beauty. Her companion also unveiled. She was a little lady with brown curls and big blue eyes. Just now those eyes were exceedingly haughty, but at the back of them Geoffrey thought he discerned a twinkle.

“My captain, madame,” said Simon. “Sir Geoffrey of Malvallet. The Countess Margaret, Geoffrey.”

Geoffrey started, and threw Simon an amazed glance. But in a moment he had hidden his surprise and pulled forward a chair.

===Chinese===

玛格丽特夫人（Lady Margaret）动作干脆而不耐烦，一把掀开头纱。杰弗里（Geoffrey）一见她那高傲的美，不由屏住了呼吸。她的同伴也揭了面纱：是位娇小的夫人，棕色鬈发，一双蓝色的大眼睛。此刻那双眼睛傲气逼人，可杰弗里觉得，傲气的深处隐约闪着一星狡黠。

“我的统领，夫人。”西蒙说。“马尔瓦莱的杰弗里爵士。这位是玛格丽特女伯爵（the Countess Margaret），杰弗里。”

杰弗里一怔，朝西蒙投去诧异的一瞥。但转眼间他便掩去了惊色，拉过一把椅子。

===Original===

“Pray, madame, will you not be seated?” he bowed, all his courtier instincts to the fore.

The Countess hesitated a moment, looking at Simon. Then she sat down, allowing her cloak to fall away from her gleaming shoulders. Her foot tapped the ground imperiously.

“Well, sir? What now?”

===Chinese===

“夫人，请坐？”他躬身相让，浑身的朝臣本能尽数使了出来。

伯爵夫人望着西蒙，迟疑了片刻。随后她坐了下来，任斗篷从光华流转的肩头滑落。她的脚尖威仪凛然地叩点着地面。

“怎么样，先生？现在又如何？”

===Original===

The little lady went to her and stood behind her chair. She smiled upon Geoffrey graciously, as if to thank him for his consideration.

Simon clanked to the table behind which Bernard stood, spellbound.

“Go prepare me two rooms above,” he said. “Let Walter of Santoy set a guard of mine own men upon them, so that not a mouse may creep out unseen. Hasten.”

Bernard stammered something unintelligible and hurried out. Simon turned to his prisoner.

===Chinese===

娇小的夫人走到她跟前，立在椅后。她雍容地向杰弗里嫣然一笑，似在谢他这一番周到。

西蒙甲叶铿然，走向桌子；伯纳德还立在桌后，看得出了神。

“上楼替我备两间房，”他说。“叫桑托伊的沃尔特（Walter of Santoy）派我自己的部下看守，不许一只老鼠溜出去没叫人瞧见。快去。”

伯纳德结结巴巴咕哝了几句谁也听不清的话，匆匆去了。西蒙转向他的俘虏。

===Original===

“Madame, rest assured that I shall look well to your housing, that ye may suffer no discomfort during your sojourn here. Alan is safe, Geoffrey.”

Geoffrey nodded. He clapped his hands vigorously, and when a lackey appeared, ordered wine to be brought. This he offered to the Countess, on one knee.

“I need naught,” she said coldly.

Her companion rustled forward, taking the horn from Geoffrey’s hand.

===Chinese===

“夫人，请放宽心，我必妥善安置您的住处，您在此小住，不致受半分委屈。艾伦（Alan）平安无事，杰弗里。”

杰弗里点点头。他用力拍了拍手，待一名侍仆应声而来，便吩咐取酒。酒来了，他单膝点地，把酒奉到伯爵夫人面前。

“我什么也不要。”她冷冷地说。

她的同伴窸窸窣窣抢上前来，从杰弗里手里接过角杯。

===Original===

“Nay, madame, but taste a little!” she coaxed, and whispered something in the Countess’s ear.

The Lady Margaret smiled faintly and took the horn. Geoffrey made haste to fill another for her lady, and was rewarded by a smile, and a curtsey.

“I thank you, m’sieur.”

“My Lord of Beauvallet,” said the Countess coldly, “for how long do ye seek to detain me?”

===Chinese===

“哎呀夫人，您就略尝一口嘛！”她哄劝着，又凑到伯爵夫人耳边低语了几句。

玛格丽特夫人唇边浮起浅笑，接过了角杯。杰弗里赶忙又斟满一杯，献给她那位夫人，换来盈盈一笑，外加一个屈膝礼。

“多谢您，先生。”

“博瓦莱大人（my Lord of Beauvallet），”伯爵夫人冷冷开口，“你打算把我扣留多久？”

===Original===

“Ye shall be within your castle by noon tomorrow, madame,” Simon answered shortly.

The smouldering eyes challenged him.

“As mistress or prisoner, sir?”

“The decision rests with you, madame. Mistress shall ye be if ye will swear allegiance to King Henry.”

“I bend not so easily, milor’,” she sneered.

===Chinese===

“明日午前，您便回到您的城堡里了，夫人。”西蒙简短地答。

那双郁燃的眼睛向他挑衅。

“是以女主人的身份，还是作阶下囚，先生？”

“这由您定夺，夫人。您若肯向亨利国王（King Henry）宣誓效忠，便是女主人。”

“我才不会这么轻易弯折呢，英国老爷。”她讥诮道。

===Original===

Simon’s lips tightened.

“Maybe ye will break then, madame.”

She laughed at him, but her little teeth were clenched.

“Ye know not Margaret of Belrémy, sir!”

“I think it is you who know not Simon of Beauvallet,” Simon said, with the glimmering of a smile.

===Chinese===

西蒙抿紧了嘴唇。

“那也许就得折断了，夫人。”

她朝他笑了，一口细齿却咬得紧紧的。

“你还不识得贝雷米的玛格丽特呢，先生！”

“我看，是你还不识得博瓦莱的西蒙。”西蒙说着，唇边掠过一丝隐约的笑意。

===Original===

Bernard came back into the room.

“It is done, my lord. Santoy was here.”

The Countess rose, drawing her cloak about her. She addressed Simon softly.

“Let there be an understanding between us, sir.”

“I desire naught better, lady.”

===Chinese===

伯纳德回到厅里。

“办妥了，大人。桑托伊来过。”

伯爵夫人立起身来，裹拢斗篷。她柔声对西蒙说道：

“咱们之间，得先立个明白，先生。”

“求之不得，夫人。”

===Original===

“Then mark well what I say. I give no parole, I swear no allegiance. It is war between us to the death, for I am not vanquished yet, nor will be! Ye would do well to beware my vengeance, Lord of Beauvallet!”

“I thank ye for that warning,” Simon retorted. He held back a curtain at the end of the hall. “Go before me, madame.”

When he returned to the hall it was some time later, and he had shed his armour for a long green tunic which fell below his knees and was slit at the sides to give him greater freedom in walking. Heavy spurred boots were upon his feet, but his head was bare, the light hair still clubbed at neck and brow, brushed and smooth. He was frowning, but when he met Geoffrey’s quizzical glance, the shadows went out of his eyes, and they twinkled responsively.

===Chinese===

“那么听仔细我的话。我不予誓诺，也决不宣誓效忠。你我之间，唯有战至死的战争，因为我尚未战败，也永不会败！你还是当心我的复仇吧，博瓦莱大人！”

“多谢你这句提点，”西蒙回敬道。他掀开大厅尽头的一幅帷帘。“您前头请，夫人。”

过了好一阵，他才回到大厅，铠甲已经卸下，换上一件长长的绿袍，袍长过膝，两侧开衩，走动更添自如。脚上蹬着带踢马刺的重靴，头上却不着一物，浅色的头发依旧在颈后与额角绾成发卷，梳理得平整光洁。他眉头微蹙，可一触到杰弗里打趣的眼神，眼底的阴翳便消散了，眸子里也相应地闪起光来。

===Original===

“Oh, Simon, Simon, thou dog!” Geoffrey teased him. “What have ye done?”

The corners of Simon’s mouth turned down ruefully.

“I have brought a wildcat into our midst,” he answered. “Belrémy is not wholly mine yet, though I hold the town and the castle.”

===Chinese===

“哦，西蒙啊西蒙，你这狗东西！”杰弗里打趣他。“你都干了什么好事？”

西蒙嘴角苦着脸耷拉下来。

“我给我们中间带回了一只野猫，”他答道。“贝雷米还不全然归我，虽然城池和城堡都已在我手中。”

===Original===

Geoffrey seized him by the shoulders, pushing him backwards to a chair.

“Sit, thou squire of dames, and tell me what passed within the castle.”

“Little enough. I entered alone, and was led to my lady’s audience-chamber, where she sat amidst her court, with her cousin beside her.”

===Chinese===

杰弗里抓住他双肩，把他往后推到一把椅子跟前。

“坐下吧，淑女们的扈从，跟我讲讲城堡里出了什么事。”

“没多少可讲的。我只身入堡，被引到我夫人的觐见厅，她端坐在满朝侍臣中间，表弟就坐在近旁。”

===Original===

“Cousin?”

“Ay. Him I expected, for his father, the Sire de Galledemaine, spoke of him. A puny creature with a rose. Faugh! So soon as I had set eyes on him I knew what manner of man I had to treat with. They had thought to frighten me with threats, deeming me a fool to walk thus coolly into their trap.”

“Said I not that it was a trap!”

===Chinese===

“表弟？”

“是啊。这个人我早料到了，他父亲加勒德曼老爷（the Sire de Galledemaine）提过他。一个捧着玫瑰的孱弱货色。呸！我一眼看清了他，便知道自己是在跟什么样的人打交道。他们本想拿威胁来吓我，只当我是个傻子，才会这么从容不迫地踏进他们的圈套。”

“我不是说过那是个圈套么！”

===Original===

“Nay, but I knew the workings of it. They would have taken me prisoner, mayhap slain me. I know not.”

“What!” Geoffrey started up. “But it was truce!”

“So I thought. Yet I suspected treachery, so was I not taken unawares. There was some parley at first. My lady was proud enough, and high in her talk. Then they flung veiled threats at me, and I made an end.”

===Chinese===

“那也不要紧，我摸得清它的门道。他们本要拿我作俘虏，或许还会要我的命。天晓得。”

“什么！”杰弗里跳了起来。“可那明明是休战！”

“我也当是休战。但我疑心有诈，才不曾措手不及。起初还有几句交涉。我那位夫人傲气十足，说话口气高得很。随后他们朝我丢来些遮遮掩掩的威胁，我便收了场。”

===Original===

“Simon, thou art like an oyster! How made ye this end?”

“I drew my sword upon the Lady Margaret, and thus held her men at bay.”

“Ye﻿—ye﻿—Oh, *preux chevalier*!” Geoffrey broke into a long laugh. “They would not think that, the Frenchmen!”

===Chinese===

“西蒙，你这人真像只牡蛎！你这收场是怎么个收法？”

“我拔剑指向玛格丽特夫人，就这样逼得她手下人不敢近前。”

“你——你——哦，好一位*侠义骑士*（preux chevalier）！”杰弗里放声大笑了好一阵。“法国人可料想不到这一出！”

===Original===

“Nay. Not that dainty court. After that it was simple. They led me to Alan who lay in a fair chamber in the tower. He is wounded, but I think not badly. Then came I here, with my lady as hostage. The Chevalier hath sworn an oath of submission, but I trust him not. Now I will invest the castle. It shall be my quarters, and thine. The town is quiet?”

“Ay. The people are amazed at thy clemency. All France thinks King Hal an ogre.”

Simon rose.

===Chinese===

“他们自然料不到。那样一个香喷喷的朝廷。那之后就简单了。他们引我去看艾伦，他躺在塔楼里一间雅致的房间里。他受了伤，但我想不重。随后我便到了此地，携夫人作人质。那位骑士已宣誓归降，但我信不过他。眼下我要进驻城堡：它做我的驻地，也做你的。城里安静么？”

“安静。百姓对你的宽仁惊讶不已。全法兰西都当哈尔王（King Hal）是个吃人的妖魔。”

西蒙起身。

===Original===

“I must see Huntingdon. Where is he?”

“By the southern gate. His men bring the baggage into the town. Where wilt thou quarter thy men, Simon?”

“Some here. I make provision for the others this day. Geoffrey, summon a score of thine own men-at-arms, and a score of the men of Beauvallet. I will have thee ride into the castle and make all ready. They will not offer ye resistance, for fear lest I should slay the Countess.”

===Chinese===

“我得去见亨廷登（Huntingdon）。他在哪儿？”

“在南门。他的人正往城里搬运辎重。你打算把人马驻在哪儿，西蒙？”

“一部分驻在这里，其余的今天安排。杰弗里，去召集你自己名下的二十名甲士，再添二十名博瓦莱的人。我要你骑马进堡，把一切备办停当。他们不敢阻拦你，生怕我一剑杀了伯爵夫人。”

===Original===

Geoffrey picked up his cap.

“Thou’lt not ride in thyself?”

“Nay, there is work for me here. Take what arms ye find, Geoffrey, and keep the court under close surveillance. I would confine the Chevalier, but that he submitted. Watch him. I will come later. Take Master Hubert for Alan,” he added. “I trust not their French leech.”

Geoffrey lounged out, yawning.

===Chinese===

杰弗里拾起帽子。

“你不亲自进去？”

“不，这里有事离不开我。见到的兵刃一律收走，杰弗里，再把那一朝的人等严密看管起来。那骑士我本想囚起来，无奈他已归降。盯紧他。我晚些过去。让休伯特老爷（Master Hubert）去照应艾伦，”他补了一句。“他们的法国医士，我信不过。”

杰弗里打着哈欠，懒洋洋地踱了出去。

===Original===

“Heigh-ho! When shall we be quit of this troublesome town, I wonder?” At the door he paused, and looked back at Simon.

“She is lovely enow, lad, but I like not that termagant beauty.”

Simon drew the ink-horn towards him.

“Lovely? Oh, ay!”

===Chinese===

“唉嗨！咱们几时才甩得脱这座麻烦的城池呢，我倒想知道？”到得门口，他停步回头，望了望西蒙。

“她是够美的，小子，可我不喜欢那股子悍泼的艳色。”

西蒙把墨水瓶拉到面前。

“美么？哦，可不是！”

===Original===

“Thou hadst not remarked it?” An impish smile danced across Geoffrey’s mouth. “Take heed lest she slight thee for Alan.”

Simon’s hand travelled slowly across the paper. He laughed.

“Holy Virgin! She would kill Alan with but a look. She will kill me an I watch her not.”

“And dost thou admire the tigress, my brother?”

===Chinese===

“你倒不曾留意？”一丝顽皮的笑意在杰弗里嘴角跳荡。“当心哪，她为了艾伦可要冷落你了。”

西蒙执笔的手在纸上缓缓移过。他笑了。

“圣母在上！她只消一个眼神便能杀了艾伦。我若不留神，她也会要了我的命。”

“这么说，你赏识这头母老虎么，我的兄弟？”

===Original===

“Not I.” He paused in his writing. “She is very brave,” he added reflectively.

“She would have slain thee foully,” Geoffrey said solemnly.

“Ay. She is a woman. Get thee gone, Geoffrey, and summon thy men.”

“Oh, I go, I go! I leave thee to dream of thine Amazon.”

===Chinese===

“才不呢。”他笔下停了一停。“她非常勇敢，”他若有所思地补上一句。

“她本会卑鄙地杀了你，”杰弗里一本正经地说。

“是啊。她是女人。去办你的差去吧，杰弗里，把你的人召集起来。”

“哦，这就走，这就走！留你在这儿，梦你的亚马逊悍女去。”

===Original===

Simon smiled.

“Ye leave me to quarter my men,” he said.

===Chinese===

西蒙一笑。

“你留下我，是要我安顿人马。”他说。
