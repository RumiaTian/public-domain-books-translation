### I / 一

===Original===
I promised the Wife that if anybody ast me what kind of a time did I have at Palm Beach I’d say I had a swell time. And if they ast me who did we meet I’d tell ’em everybody that was worth meetin’. And if they ast me didn’t the trip cost a lot I’d say Yes; but it was worth the money. I promised her I wouldn’t spill none o’ the real details. But if you can’t break a promise you made to your own wife what kind of a promise can you break? Answer me that, Edgar.
I’m not one o’ these kind o’ people that’d keep a joke to themself just because the joke was on them. But they’s plenty of our friends that I wouldn’t have ’em hear about it for the world. I wouldn’t tell you, only I know you’re not the village gossip and won’t crack it to anybody. Not even to your own Missus, see? I don’t trust no women.
It was along last January when I and the Wife was both hit by the society bacillus. I think it was at the opera. You remember me tellin’ you about us and the Hatches goin’ to *Carmen* and then me takin’ my Missus and her sister, Bess, and four of one suit named Bishop to see *The Three Kings*? Well, I’ll own up that I enjoyed wearin’ the soup and fish and minglin’ amongst the high polloi and pretendin’ we really was somebody. And I know my wife enjoyed it, too, though they was nothin’ said between us at the time.
The next stage was where our friends wasn’t good enough for us no more. We used to be tickled to death to spend an evenin’ playin’ rummy with the Hatches. But all of a sudden they didn’t seem to be no fun in it and when Hatch’d call up we’d stall out of it. From the number o’ times I told him that I or the Missus was tired out and goin’ right to bed, he must of thought we’d got jobs as telephone linemen.

===Chinese===
我跟媳妇儿说好了，要是有人问我去棕榈滩（Palm Beach）玩得咋样，我就说玩得美极了。要是人家问我们碰见谁啦，我就说该见的全见了。要是人家问这趟是不是花了不少钱，我就说是；可这钱花得值。我答应她，一点实底儿也不往外漏。可话又说回来了，要是对自家媳妇儿许的愿都不能反悔，那还有啥愿能反悔的？你倒替我答答这句，埃德加（Edgar）。
我这人吧，可不是那种因为笑话戳到自己身上、就把笑话闷在肚里的主儿。可我们那些朋友里头，有不少人是打死也不能让他们听见这事的。我本不打算跟你说，也就是知道你不是个长舌妇，不会往外乱传。连你家那位都不行，懂不？我可不信任何女的。
差不多就是去年一月那会儿，我跟媳妇儿俩人都染上了上社会的菌。我记得是在歌剧院里头。你该记得我跟你说过的吧——我们两口子跟哈奇（Hatch）两口子去看《卡门》（Carmen），后来我又带我媳妇儿、她妹妹贝丝（Bess），还有四个同姓毕晓普（Bishop）的，一块儿去看《三王》（The Three Kings）？嘿，我自个儿承认，穿上那套礼服，混在上流人物里头，装得咱俩也是有头有脸的人物，那滋味我是真受用。我知道我媳妇儿也受用，虽说当时我俩谁也没挑明。
到了下一个阶段呢，就是嫌自家那帮朋友不够格了。从前啊，哪天晚上能跟哈奇两口子搓拉米牌，我们高兴得跟什么似的。可突然之间，就觉得没意思了；哈奇一来电话，我俩就推三阻四。我跟他推说我自个儿或媳妇儿累得要命、马上要上床睡觉，回回都这么推，次数多到——他准以为我俩改行去架电话线了。

===Original===
We quit attendin’ pitcher shows because the rest o’ the audience wasn’t the kind o’ people you’d care to mix with. We didn’t go over to Ben’s and dance because they wasn’t no class to the crowd there. About once a week we’d beat it to one o’ the good hotels downtown, all dressed up like a horse, and have our dinner with the rest o’ the E-light. They wasn’t nobody talked to us only the waiters, but we could look as much as we liked and it was sport tryin’ to guess the names o’ the gang at the next table.
Then we took to readin’ the society news at breakfast. It used to be that I didn’t waste time on nothin’ but the market and sportin’ pages, but now I pass ’em up and listen w’ile the Missus rattled off what was doin’ on the Lake Shore Drive.
Every little w’ile we’d see where So-and-So was at Palm Beach or just goin’ there or just comin’ back. We got to kiddin’ about it.
“Well,” I’d say, “we’d better be startin’ pretty soon or we’ll miss the best part o’ the season.”

===Chinese===
电影我们也不去了，嫌坐一屋子的观众跟我们不是一个圈子的人，没法儿掺和。本（Ben）那儿我们也不去跳舞了，嫌那帮人没档次。大约一个礼拜一回，我俩就打扮得溜光水滑，奔城里一家高档旅馆去，跟其余那些上等人一块儿吃晚饭。除了服务员，没人搭理我们，可我们能可劲儿看哪；猜隔壁桌那帮人姓甚名谁，也挺有意思的。
打那起，吃早饭的时候我俩就看起社交新闻来了。从前我是不把工夫糟蹋在行情版和体育版以外的东西上的，可如今这俩我也略过去了，改听媳妇儿噼里啪啦地念叨湖滨大道（Lake Shore Drive）上都热闹些啥。
隔三差五地，我们就瞧见报上说某某正在棕榈滩哪、正要去哪、刚回来哪。我俩就拿这个逗趣。
「嘿，」我就说，「咱也该动身了，再不走这一季的好时候可就错过了。」

===Original===
“Yes,” the Wife’d say back, “we’d go right now if it wasn’t for all them engagements next week.”
We kidded and kidded till finally, one night, she forgot we was just kiddin’.
“You didn’t take no vacation last summer,” she says.
“No,” says I. “They wasn’t no chance to get away.”
“But you promised me,” she says, “that you’d take one this winter to make up for it.”
“I know I did,” I says; “but it’d be a sucker play to take a vacation in weather like this.”
“The weather ain’t like this everywheres,” she says.
“You must of been goin’ to night school,” I says.

===Chinese===
「可不，」媳妇儿就回我，「要不是下礼拜这一堆应酬，咱这会儿就走了。」
我俩就这么逗着，逗着逗着，到末了，有一天晚上，她忘了这只是逗趣了。
「去年夏天你没歇假，」她说。
「对，」我说，「走不开啊。」
「可你答应过我，」她说，「今年冬天补我一个假。」
「我是答应过，」我说，「可这种天儿去歇假，那是傻蛋才干的事儿。」
「又不是哪儿都这天气，」她说。
「那你一定是上夜校补过课了，」我说。

===Original===
“Another thing you promised me,” says she, “was that when you could afford it you’d take me on a real honeymoon trip to make up for the dinky one we had.”
“That still goes,” I says, “when I can afford it.”
“You can afford it now,” says she. “We don’t owe nothin’ and we got money in the bank.”
“Yes,” I says. “Pretty close to three hundred bucks.”
“You forgot somethin’,” she says. “You forgot them war babies.”
Did I tell you about that? Last fall I done a little dabblin’ in Crucial Steel and at this time I’m tellin’ you about I still had a hold of it, but stood to pull down six hundred. Not bad, eh?
“It’d be a mistake to let loose now,” I says.
“All right,” she says. “Hold on, and I hope you lose every cent. You never did care nothin’ for me.”

===Chinese===
「还有一桩你答应过我的，」她说，「就是等你出得起钱了，领我出去玩一趟像样的蜜月，补我们当年那趟寒酸的。」
「这话还算数，」我说，「等我出得起钱。」
「你现在就出得起，」她说。「咱不欠谁的账，银行里还有存款。」
「是，」我说，「差不多三百块大洋。」
「你忘了一样东西，」她说。「你把那些战时股（war babies）给忘了。」
这事我跟你说过了吗？去年秋天，我在关键钢铁（Crucial Steel）上头小小的倒腾了一下；到我跟你说的这会儿，我还攥着没撒手呢，稳赚六百块。不赖吧，嗯？
「这会儿撒手可就亏了，」我说。
「行，」她说，「你攥着吧，我巴不得你赔个精光。你压根儿就没把我放在心上过。」

===Original===
Then we done a little spoonin’ and then I ast her what was the big idear.
“We ain’t swelled on ourself,” she says; “but I know and you know that the friends we been associatin’ with ain’t in our class. They don’t know how to dress and they can’t talk about nothin’ but their goldfish and their meat bills. They don’t try to get nowheres, but all they do is play rummy and take in the Majestic. I and you like nice people and good music and things that’s worth w’ile. It’s a crime for us to be wastin’ our time with riff and raff that’d run round barefooted if it wasn’t for the police.”
“I wouldn’t say we’d wasted much time on ’em lately,” I says.
“No,” says she, “and I’ve had a better time these last three weeks than I ever had in my life.”
“And you can keep right on havin’ it,” I says.

===Chinese===
接着我俩温存了一阵，我就问她到底打的什么主意。
「咱不是自高自大，」她说，「可你我心里都明白，咱们一直来往的那帮朋友，配不上咱们这个档次。他们不会穿衣裳，张嘴闭嘴就是金鱼啊、肉账啊，别的一概谈不来。他们也不想着往高处走，成天就知道搓拉米牌、看美捷戏院（Majestic）。我跟你就喜欢体面人、好音乐，喜欢那些个值得的东西。咱跟那帮下三滥混在一块儿，纯属瞎耽误工夫；要不是有警察管着，他们能光着脚丫子满街跑。」
「要说最近，我也没见咱在他们身上多费工夫，」我说。
「是没有，」她说，「这三个礼拜我过得比我这辈子哪会儿都痛快。」
「那你接着这么过就是了，」我说。

===Original===
“I could have a whole lot better time, and you could, too,” she says, “if we could get acquainted with some congenial people to go round with; people that’s tastes is the same as ourn.”
“If any o’ them people calls up on the phone,” I says, “I’ll be as pleasant to ’em as I can.”
“You’re always too smart,” says the Wife. “You don’t never pay attention to no schemes o’ mine.”
“What’s the scheme now?”
“You’ll find fault with it because I thought it up,” she says. “If it was your scheme you’d think it was grand.”
“If it really was good you wouldn’t be scared to spring it,” I says.
“Will you promise to go through with it?” says she.
“If it ain’t too ridic’lous,” I told her.

===Chinese===
「要是能结交几个脾气相投的人一起走动走动，人家的趣味跟咱一样，那我还能过得痛快十倍，你也是，」她说。
「要是这种人打电话过来，」我说，「我一定对他们客气到家。」
「你呀，就是嘴皮子太损，」媳妇儿说。「我提的任何主意你都从来不往耳里听。」
「这回又是什么主意？」
「你准挑毛病，因为这是我想出来的，」她说。「要是你想出来的，你就该觉得了不起了。」
「要是真好，你也不至于不敢往外抛啊，」我说。
「你答应我把它办成吗？」她说。
「只要别太离谱，」我跟她说。

===Original===
“See! I knowed that’d be the way,” she says.
“Don’t talk crazy,” I says. “Where’d we be if we’d went through with every plan you ever sprang?”
“Will you promise to listen to my side of it without actin’ cute?” she says.
So I didn’t see no harm in goin’ that far.
“I want you to take me to Palm Beach,” says she. “I want you to take a vacation, and that’s where we’ll spend it.”
“And that ain’t all we’d spend,” I says.
“Remember your promise,” says she.
So I shut up and listened.

===Chinese===
「瞧！我就知道你会这样，」她说。
「别扯淡了，」我说。「要是我把你抛出来的每个主意都办成了，咱俩这会儿在哪儿呢？」
「你答应我，先听我把话说完，别耍花腔，行不？」她说。
我觉得，就答应到这一步也没什么坏处。
「我要你带我去棕榈滩，」她说。「我要你歇个假，咱就上那儿去过。」
「花销的也不止这一处，」我说。
「记着你答应过的，」她说。
于是我就闭了嘴，听她说下去。

===Original===
The dope she give me was along these lines: We could get special round-trip rates on any o’ the railroads and that part of it wouldn’t cost nowheres near as much as a man’d naturally think. The hotel rates was pretty steep, but the meals was throwed in, and just imagine what them meals would be! And we’d be stayin’ under the same roof with the Vanderbilts and Goulds, and eatin’ at the same table, and probably, before we was there a week, callin’ ’em Steve and Gus. They was dancin’ every night and all the guests danced with each other, and how would it feel fox-trottin’ with the president o’ the B. & O., or the Delmonico girls from New York! And all Chicago society was down there, and when we met ’em we’d know ’em for life and have some real friends amongst ’em when we got back home.
That’s how she had it figured and she must of been practisin’ her speech, because it certainly did sound good to me. To make it short, I fell, and dated her up to meet me downtown the next day and call on the railroad bandits. The first one we seen admitted that his was the best route and that he wouldn’t only soak us one hundred and forty-seven dollars and seventy cents to and from Palm Beach and back, includin’ an apartment from here to Jacksonville and as many stopovers as we wanted to make. He told us we wouldn’t have to write for no hotel accommodations because the hotels had an agent right over on Madison Street that’d be glad to do everything to us.

===Chinese===
她给我下的那剂迷魂药，大概是这样的：随便哪条铁路都有特价往返票，那块儿的花销远没有一般人想得那么贵。旅馆的价钱是挺辣手，可饭钱是包在里头的；你想想，那饭菜得是什么档次！而且我俩能跟范德比尔特家（Vanderbilts）、古尔德家（Goulds）住同一个屋檐下，坐同一张桌子吃饭，说不定住不到一个礼拜，就能管人家叫「史蒂夫」「格斯」（Steve、Gus）了。那儿每天晚上都有舞会，客人之间互相做伴跳；你想想，跟巴尔的摩俄亥俄铁路公司（B. & O.）的总裁，或者纽约德尔莫尼科家（Delmonico）的小姐跳上一曲狐步舞，那是什么派头！而且芝加哥（Chicago）整个儿上流圈子都在那儿，我们一结识上，就是一辈子的交情；等回家以后，这里头就有几个像样的朋友了。
她就是这么盘算的，准是事先练过这番话，因为听在我耳朵里确实舒坦。长话短说，我服了软，跟她约好第二天在城里碰头，去拜访那帮铁路强盗。我们见着头一个售票员，他一口咬定自家这条线最好，去棕榈滩一个来回只宰我们一百四十七块七毛钱——从这儿到杰克逊维尔（Jacksonville）一段还是包厢，中途想停几站就停几站。他又跟我们说，订旅馆的事不必写信打听，因为旅馆在麦迪逊街（Madison Street）就设了个代办处，保管把我们招呼得「舒舒服服」。

===Original===
So we says we’d be back later and then we beat it over to the Florida East Coast’s local studio.
“How much for a double room by the week?” I ast the man.
“They ain’t no weekly rates,” he says. “By the day it’d be twelve dollars and up for two at the Breakers, and fourteen dollars and up at the Poinciana.”
“I like the Breakers better,” says I.
“You can’t get in there,” he says. “They’re full for the season.”
“That’s a long spree,” I says.
“Can we get in the other hotel?” ast the Wife.
“I can find out,” says the man.

「另一家进得去吗？」媳妇儿问。
「我可以打听打听，」那人说。

“That’d be more,” says he. “That’d be fifteen dollars or sixteen dollars and up.”

“What’s that got to do with it?” I ast him.
“A whole lot,” he says. “The people that usually goes abroad is all down to Palm Beach this winter.”

「没齐活儿，」她说，「这才把我打发了。可我要你跟我一样光鲜。」
「这事儿老天爷自会操心，」我说。
可跟她没理可讲。她说咱这趟出门是笔投资，能搭上那些值得结交的人。要是不把行头置办齐了，那是一丁点机会都没有。
所以还没等到十号，我俩手里已经多了这么些东西：两件新晚礼服，两套女式运动装，四五双各种颜色的鞋，一件塔式多（Tuxedo）晚宴上衣，三件礼服衬衫，半打别的衬衫，两条透亮的白裤子，一套新常服，还有老天爷才知道多少内衣、多少帽子多少袜子。而我呢，三月十五号之前得把老宅子上这笔抵押还清。
正当我们要出门赶火车的时候，电话响了。是哈奇太太打来的，请我们过去搓两把拉米。我正刮着脸，是媳妇儿接的话。
「你跟她说什么了？」我问。
「我说我们要出门了，」媳妇儿说。
「我打赌你忘了说我们要上哪儿去，」我说。
「掏钱吧你，」她说。
