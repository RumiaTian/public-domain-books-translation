## XVI / 十六

===Original===
The steward departed, chary of words, as though he were on his way to get another rudder. He had no time to talk.

The violent rolling of the ship did not relent. That seemed senseless, when she was crippled. She ought to be let off, now she could not steer. Impossible to think, with that rolling.

Colet, to his great annoyance, found that his knees were shaking. He had not told them to. He did not want them to shake. He damned those quivering members of his body, and would have stiffened them, but that he was flung against a bulkhead, and so brought down some of the master’s pipes from a rack. Something to do, anyway. He could recover tobacco pipes while others found a rudder. Better men had to look after the ship for those who attended to pipe-racks, while waiting.

===Chinese===
服务员走了，惜字如金，仿佛正赶去再弄一个舵来。他没有闲谈的工夫。

船猛烈地横摇，不见一点缓和。她既然残了，这就显得毫无道理。如今她掌不了舵了，就该免了她这份罪才是。摇成这样，什么也没法想。

科利特十分懊恼地发现，自己的两个膝盖在发抖。他没有吩咐它们抖。他也不想让它们抖。他咒骂身上这对哆嗦的部件，本想把它们绷直，却被甩到了舱壁上，顺势又撞落了架子上的几只船长的烟斗。好歹有点事做了。旁人找舵的时候，他可以拾掇烟斗。在等待的当口，更能干的人只好替照看烟斗架的人去照看船。

===Original===
They also serve, who only look after the tobacco pipes. If there was no wind when he first went into that room, something was howling now all right. It was no good waiting for the captain. Hale was not likely to return till﻿—well, he wasn’t likely to return. The best thing to do would be to go and find the men at the centre of things, because that cabin had precious little interest now. It was useless to wait there, at that time of night, when for all he knew they soon might be taking to the boats.

He heard a heavy concussion. The cabin shook. The papers on which he had been working fell to the floor. The boats! Colet watched the papers sprawling and scattering. They had lost their meaning. They were just as well where they were. But there would be no boats for the sea which could make that sound. The cabin reversed, and as it did so a tongue of water shot over the carpet straight for the papers. Colet dived for them and snatched them out of its way. Save the stationery!

He straightened them on the desk. With measured deliberation he sought carefully for the sheet on which he had been working. They were all in a mess, these sheets, but so was everything else. At least the ship’s papers could be put in order. No more water seemed to be coming in. That was only a splash. Not foundering yet. He settled the papers into their sequence, and began again on them at the mark. The captain had said wait.

===Chinese===
只照看烟斗的人，也一样是在效力。他初进那间舱室时要是还没有风，这会儿可是有什么东西在狂啸了。干等船长是没有用的。黑尔多半要到——咳，他多半是不会回来了。顶好的办法是自己去找那些守在事情中心的人，因为那间舱室如今实在没有什么可留恋的了。夜里那种时辰干守在那里毫无用处，因为天知道他们会不会转眼就要放救生艇。

他听见一声沉重的撞击。舱室震了。他方才做着的那几页文件掉到了地板上。救生艇！科利特眼看着那些纸四仰八叉地摊开、散乱。它们已经失了意义。它们待在原地倒也挺好。可是能发出这种声响的海，是不会让人放艇的。舱室倒了个个儿，就在这一倒之间，一条水舌窜过地毯，直奔那几页文件而去。科利特扑过去，把它们从水路上抢了出来。抢救文具！

他把文件在桌上抚平。他不慌不忙、仔仔细细地找出方才做的那一页。这些纸页全乱了套，可旁的哪一样又不是乱套呢。至少船上的文书还能归置整齐。水像是没有再进来。那不过是溅进来的一股。还没有沉。他把文件按次序排好，从做了记号的地方接着做下去。船长说过，等着。

===Original===
It was the only thing to do, but that lad Casabianca deserved a better poem. It would be easy to wait on a deck diminishing in dissolution if one but knew the reason for it. But this was only an idiot joke, dutifully completing a ship’s papers when the mysterious reason was trying to turn the ship over. Now the infernal water was under the desk. Reason! No more reason in it than there was in the hot gas which congealed to a mud ball, on which grew the truth, and crosses and nails for those who dared to mention it. What a joke; and nobody to get a laugh out of it!

Let her roll. He could not stop it. Time for a pipe. Not his affair. Funny, that a man should curse the stars in their courses, when he was beset. Same thing as a rat biting his trap, maybe. Best to call himself lucky. Lucky to have a job to do, if only a job of checking packages of pots and pans, when the heaven itself was cracking. “We are but little children meek.” The tune of this hymn, for some uninvited cause, was running through his head. The movements of the ship kept it going. “Not born in any high estate.” Couldn’t very well call this estate puffed up. “What can we do for Jesus’ sake?” Well, Jesus, I was checking this ship’s manifest when I went down. Sorry it’s wet.

===Chinese===
这是唯一可做的事，可那个守在燃烧的甲板上的少年卡萨比安卡（Casabianca），本该配一首更好的诗。倘若知道个中缘由，守着一片在崩解中不断缩小的甲板等待，原也容易。可眼下这不过是个白痴的笑话：神秘的缘由正竭力要把船掀翻，自己却还在恪尽职守地填一份船舶文书。这会儿，那要命的水已经漫到书桌底下了。理性！这里头的理性，不比那团热气里的多——热气凝成一颗泥球，泥球上长出真理，还有十字架和钉子，留给胆敢说起它的人。多么好笑的一个玩笑；却没有人能笑得出来！

随她摇去。他拦不住。正好抽一斗。不关他的事。有意思，人一到陷于围困，就该咒骂那循轨而行的群星了。这跟老鼠反咬夹住它的夹子，怕是一回事。顶好还是算自己走运。老天爷本身都开裂了的时候，手里还有一件差事，哪怕只是清点一筐一筐的锅碗瓢盆，也算走运。『我们不过是柔顺的小小孩儿。』这首赞美诗的调子，不知受了什么不请自来的缘由驱使，在他脑子里转个不停。船的颠摇让它停不下来。『并非生在什么高门贵第。』总不好说眼下这份家业算得上趾高气扬。『我们能为主耶稣做什么呢？』唉，耶稣，我沉下去的时候，正核对着这条船的舱单呢。抱歉，纸弄湿了。

===Original===
What was the time? To his surprise, the clock said it was another morning again. The skipper’s cabin was in a sorry state. Somebody was attempting the door, but the slant of the ship held it fast. The door rattled, and then Hale entered. He showed no surprise at finding the purser still busy. He took a towel and wiped his face. “I should drop that now,” he remarked.

Colet told him he had just finished it. Hale looked at the clock, and thanked him. The purser modestly waited for the master to give a word on the business without, but Hale merely balanced himself patiently to the movements of his room, and sought for something in a drawer.

“By the way, Colet, you could turn in here. It would save you the run to your own place.”

===Chinese===
几点钟了？叫他吃惊的是，钟上说，又是一个早晨了。船长舱里一派狼藉。有人在弄那扇门，可是船的倾侧把它顶得死死的。门咔咔地响了一阵，随后黑尔走了进来。见事务长还在忙，他并不显出惊讶。他拿了一条毛巾擦脸。「这个现在可以撂下了。」他说。

科利特告诉他，自己刚刚做完。黑尔看了看钟，谢了他。事务长安分地等着船长透一句外头的事情，可黑尔只是耐心地随着他这间舱室的颠簸稳住身子，在抽屉里翻找什么。

「对了，科利特，你可以就在这儿睡下。省得你跑回自己的舱里去。」

===Original===
But Colet felt a sudden dislike of the suggestion of that isolation.

“Oh, thank you. But I’ll make for my own cubbyhole. It’ll do me good, to run for it.”

That was all. Not a word about the rudder. And perhaps it would be better not to ask questions. Perhaps rudders were indelicate. By Hale’s manner, too, it might be only a rather wet night. It would be nobler to assume the night was merely wet.

===Chinese===
可科利特忽然对那份独处的提议生出了反感。

「哦，谢谢。可我还是要回我自己那个小鸽子笼去。跑那一趟，对我有好处。」

话就这么多。舵的事，一个字没提。也许还是不问的好。也许舵是一件不便启齿的东西。再瞧黑尔那副神气，这没准儿只是一个相当潮湿的夜晚。把这一夜只当作下了一场大雨，倒更高贵些。

===Original===
Hale led on to the head of the companion to the quarterdeck.

“Can’t open the lower door,” he explained. The master stood there, with his hand on the upper door, as though listening for someone who would let them out. “Now,” he muttered, opened it on the instant, and they were both in the night.

The night engulfed them with a roar as though it saw them instantly. Colet was separated from Hale. There was no ship. There was but a pealing and a shouting. The darkness was full of driving water. It was hard to breathe. Hale had gone. Colet forgot which was the head and which the stern. A burst of spray raked past. Then he felt a grip on his arm, and a warm mouth sought his ear. Hale was saying something but his words were torn away.

===Chinese===
黑尔领着他，走到通后甲板的那道升降口顶上。

「下面那扇门打不开。」他解释道。船长站在那儿，一只手按着上面那扇门，像是在等一个放他们出去的人。「就是现在。」他低声说，霎时把门推开，两人便都进了黑夜。

黑夜一声咆哮吞了他们，仿佛它一眼就看见了他们。科利特同黑尔走散了。没有船。只有轰鸣与呐喊。黑暗里满是横冲直撞的水。喘不上气来。黑尔不见了。科利特忘了哪一头是船首，哪一头是船尾。一阵浪花扫掠而过。随后，他觉得胳膊上被一把攥住，一张温热的嘴凑向他的耳朵。黑尔在说着什么，可他的话全被撕走了。

===Original===
“What?”

The skipper’s voice was at once superior to the chaos:

“We can do it.”

===Chinese===
「什么？」

船长的嗓音顿时凌驾于这片混沌之上：

「我们能行。」

===Original===
Wanted a bit of doing. Like trying to walk a plank you couldn’t see which was only there sometimes. It was a wonder it ever came back. My God, she was like a balloon, trying to sail out of it. But the water was up there too. Here was the ladder down to the deck. He had got on to its rungs when the warm mouth came near his ear again:

“Wait. Hold tight.”

The thing under their feet heaved, but checked, as if this was too much for it. The night exploded over them and fell in broken thunder. Couldn’t go through that.

===Chinese===
这话可得费一番手脚才兑现得了。就像走一块你看不见的跳板，而且那跳板还时有时无。它居然还在原处，真是个奇迹。我的天，她像一只气球，拼命想从这阵风浪里飘出去。可上头也有水。这就是下到甲板去的那道梯子了。他刚攀上梯级，那张温热的嘴又凑近了他的耳朵：

「等等。抓牢。」

脚下那件庞然大物耸了起来，却又顿住，仿佛这一下连它也受不住了。黑夜在他们头顶炸开，又化作破碎的雷落下来。这样的关口闯不过去。

===Original===
“Go on,” ordered invisible Hale. They reached the deck, and ran. Ran in short lengths. You can’t keep running on a slope which changes direction abruptly in the dark. Together they got to the foot of the ladder to the bridge-deck, and Hale pushed Colet at it, the signal to mount quickly; but when he gripped the iron thing it came at him as though loose. He was pulling the ladder out of the ship. Nothing to stand on. Then the ship fell head first into nothing; the purser’s face was dragged after the retreating ladder and struck it. Colet could hear the sea in behind him and clambered up; yet hesitated.

“Captain?”

The old man was in that below. No good shouting. The purser got down to the deck again, and groped, with the flood at his knees. He found Hale on his hands and knees, rising, and clutched him.

===Chinese===
「上去。」看不见的黑尔下令。他们到了甲板，跑起来。一小段一小段地跑。在黑暗里一道说变向就变向的斜坡上，谁也没法一口气跑下去。两人一同摸到上驾驶台甲板的梯子脚下，黑尔把科利特朝梯子上一推，那是叫他快上的信号；可他一把攥住那铁家伙时，它却朝他撞过来，像是松了。他简直是在把梯子从船身上拔出来。脚下没了站处。随即，船一头栽进了虚空；事务长的脸被那节节退缩的梯子拖过去，撞在上面。科利特听得见海水在他背后灌进来，攀了上去；却还在迟疑。

「船长？」

老头子还在下面那儿。喊也没有用。事务长又下回甲板，趟着齐膝的洪水摸索。他寻见了黑尔，他正手脚并用地往起爬，便一把攥住了他。

===Original===
“Go on, go on,” Hale shouted.

The captain came into Colet’s cabin with him, and stayed there for a moment. He was smiling.

“You would never have believed that, would you, Colet?”

===Chinese===
「上去，上去。」黑尔喊道。

船长随他一同进了科利特的舱室，在里面待了片刻。他脸上带着笑。

「你是怎么也不会相信的，对吧，科利特？」

===Original===
Colet, a little breathless, held to the edge of his bunk. He hinted that the violence seemed a bit unreasonable.

“No. There’s sufficient cause. I’ve seen it before. We must wait for daylight.”

When the master had gone, Colet considered his bunk. No. The settee tonight. No use turning in when things were happening all the time. But it needed very deliberate control to sit there, waiting for light to come, when the world was falling to pieces. Especially, when, the longer you waited, the louder grew the mania of the wind, and the more surprisingly delirious mounted the buoyancy of the ship. Could she stand it? She seemed terrified. Colet remembered a rabbit he had once seen leaping and convulsive in a wire noose. She was desperate, but she was done.

Colet surrendered limply to the anarchy. Once he rose and vomited. Well﻿ ﻿… not long to wait now﻿ ﻿… the day was about due. The wind did all the moaning that was necessary. That moan outside would do for all creation. No need to add to it. It was the antiphon of doom. And it didn’t seem to matter now. Nothing more to do. A sea battered on his door, but Colet did not look up. He would rest while waiting.

===Chinese===
科利特有点上气不接下气，扶着自己铺位的边沿。他婉转地表示，这番狂暴似乎有点不讲道理。

「不。缘由是足够的。我从前见过这种阵仗。我们只好等天亮。」

船长走后，科利特掂量了一下自己的铺位。不。今晚睡舱室长椅。事情时时刻刻都在发生，躺下也没有用处。可是，当这个世界正四分五裂的时候，要坐在那里等天亮，非得有极刻意的自制不可。尤其是，你等得越久，风的疯癫便叫得越响，船那股上腾之势也蹿得越发谵狂得出奇。她顶得住么？她像是吓坏了。科利特记起从前见过的一只兔子，在铁丝活套里又蹦又抽。她在拼死挣扎，可她是完了。

科利特瘫软下来，向这场无政府的暴乱投降了。有一回他起身呕吐了。唔……不用再等多久了……天也该亮了。该呻吟的，风全替世上万物呻吟过了。外头那一声哀号，尽够代表普天万物了。用不着再往上添一份。那是末日的应答轮唱。而这会儿，似乎什么都不要紧了。再没有什么可做的了。一道海浪擂着他的门，科利特却没有抬头。他要趁等着的时候歇一歇。
