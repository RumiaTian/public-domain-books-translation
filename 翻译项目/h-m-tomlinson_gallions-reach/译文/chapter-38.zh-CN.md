## XXXVIII / 三十八

===Original===
Why was Mat standing there looking at him? Colet sat up. The sun was bright on a high buttress across the valley. This was late. Time they were on the move. Mat ought to have called him; but Mat never would. That funny Malay would never waken him, of course; a very dangerous thing to do; the wandering soul might not have time to get back and reenter the body; his soul then would be lost. He smiled, and cheered the morning to the guide. Mat looked grave. Something wrong with him?

===Chinese===
马特为什么站在那儿望着他？科利特坐起身来。山谷对面一道高高的崖壁上，阳光正亮。这可不早了。该动身了。马特本该叫醒他的；可马特决计不会。那个有趣的马来人当然绝不肯唤醒他；那是顶危险的事；在外游荡的魂魄说不定来不及赶回、重新钻进躯体里去；那他的魂就丢了。他笑了，扬声向向导道了声早晨好。马特神色凝重。是出了什么事么？

===Original===
“What news today?”

“An evil thing has happened, Tuan. We cannot see Tuan Parsell. Where is he?”

Where is he? Oh, spirited away, of course. Colet glanced over at the professor’s corner. Why, Parsell never left the camp, unless accompanied. He never had. But his place was empty.

===Chinese===
「今天有什么消息？」

「出了一桩凶事，老爷。我们见不着帕塞尔老爷了。他在哪儿？」

他在哪儿？哦，自然是叫什么精怪摄去了。科利特朝教授那一角扫了一眼。可不是么，帕塞尔从不离开营地，除非有人随行。他从来没有过。可那一角已经空了。

===Original===
Nobody, Colet was told, had heard him go. They supposed that he had gone down to bathe, but no, he had not been to the pool. He had gone downhill, though; that they knew for they had found the signs. If they might say so, the Tuan had walked lightly, but as though he knew where he was going, and yet would not disturb those who slept. It was hard to follow his track below, for truly, Mat explained, he thought the Tuan had been gone many hours. There had been rain. What thing was this?

===Chinese===
据说，谁也没有听见他出去。伙计们原以为他下坡洗澡去了，可是不，他没有到水潭去过。不过他确是往坡下去了；这个他们是知道的，因为寻着了踪迹。若是容他们斗胆说一句，那位老爷走得轻，却像是知道自己往哪里去，却又不愿惊动睡着的人。到了下坡，踪迹就难跟了，因为——马特解释说——他实在以为老爷已经走了许多个钟头。下过雨。这算一桩什么事？

===Original===
Colet was up and at his gear in the first jolt of the misgiving. That old child out alone in such a country﻿ ﻿… he paused. Here, cool off. Getting scared would not help. Parsell could not have gone far.

Had he taken anything with him? What had he taken? What, his pack had gone? So it had. Food, and his compass, too, and his staff. Parsell was moonstruck. Colet began to rave at the impersonal, but caught Mat’s quiet and steady eye, which yet expressed anxiety; and so gazed out at the woods instead, deliberately still, seeking inspiration. He was up against it.

===Chinese===
疑惧初来的一悸之下，科利特已翻身而起，扑到了行装上。那样一个老小孩，独自流落在这样一片地方……他顿住了。喂，冷静。害怕无济于事。帕塞尔走不了多远。

他随身带了东西没有？带了些什么？什么，他的背囊没了？果真没了。食物，还有他的罗盘，还有他的手杖。帕塞尔是中了月魔。科利特对着那无形无相的东西咆哮起来，却碰上马特一双沉静而坚定的眼睛——那里面却也含着焦灼；于是他改而望着外面的树林，故意纹丝不动，指望寻一点灵感。他这下可陷入难局了。

===Original===
They would find him. After all, the old fellow could not travel far. Not alone. Not in that place. But how far need he travel to get lost? You just turned your head in the woods, lost it entirely, and there you were till domesday; and the camp about five minutes away.

He was buckled and taut. “Come,” he called to Mat.

“If Tuan will allow me,” said Mat, “it is not good to begin a journey without food. It is also better to sit awhile, when the liver is sick. It is hard for a man to consider what he should do while he runs.”

===Chinese===
他们会找到他的。说到底，那老先生走不远。一个人走不远。在这种地方走不远。可是，要迷路又用得着走多远呢？你只消在林子里一转头，就彻底迷了，于是就在那儿待到末日审判那一天；而营地不过五分钟的路。

他束紧了扣带，整个人绷得笔直。「走罢。」他朝马特喊道。

「老爷若容我说一句，」马特道，「空着肚子动身赶路是不好的。肝里有病的时候，也最好先坐上一坐。一个人一面跑着，是很难想明白自己该做什么的。」

===Original===
You bet it is. Very well, then. But that old buffer would have to be found if they stayed there till the coconuts were ripe. But Mat was right. It was no good rushing at the forest. The mountains did not care. They had all the time they wanted. All the same, if old Parsell was not in camp before sundown﻿ ﻿… better not think about it.

“I believe he thinks he can find those Sakais.”

“Truly, Tuan, that is what I fear also.”

===Chinese===
可不是嘛。那么，好吧。可那个老家伙非找到不可，哪怕他们在这儿待到椰子熟透。不过马特是对的。朝森林横冲直撞没有用处。群山毫不在意。它们要多少工夫有多少工夫。话虽如此，要是老帕塞尔日落前还回不了营地……最好还是别想它。

「我看，他是自以为寻得着那些塞凯人。」

「实话说，老爷，我所怕的也正是这个。」

===Original===
No other sign of him. Time was getting on. Two o’clock now. Where was Mat?

Colet retraced his steps through the forest a little distance. Hallooed. No answer. Only something like a jackass in the woods. Naturally, it would be a jackass. Colet hurried down and back a little more﻿—damn the rattans. He﻿—damn the rattans, they were everywhere. Put there to stop him, probably.

===Chinese===
再没有他别的踪迹。时间在往前赶。眼下两点钟了。马特在哪儿？

科利特循原路折回林中走了一小段。高声喊叫。没有回应。只有林子里一样像驴叫的东西。想当然耳，那准是头蠢驴。科利特又急急朝下、朝回赶了一小段——该死的藤条。他——该死的藤条，它们无处不在。多半是摆在那儿拦他的。

===Original===
Quite right, too. The thorns knew better than he did. Fancy trying to run to someone in that tangle. He stood, collected himself, and hallooed again. This time not even a jackass. He waited, and watched the sweat trickle down the back of one hand. Not a sound.

Well, they had found Parsell’s helmet at the bottom of that gully, and he was going to get to the head of it. But was this the same valley? Of course it was. He had not been paying much attention, knowing that Mat knew the business better. But it must be the same cleft in the hills. He would have to push on, guide or no guide. Mat could look after himself. He must find Parsell. No point in being there unless he did.

===Chinese===
这倒也拦得对。荆棘比他懂事。想想看，竟想在那片缠结里朝什么人跑过去。他站住，定一定神，又喊了一声。这一回连驴也没有了。他等着，眼看汗珠顺着一只手的手背往下淌。一点声息也没有。

是这么回事，他们已在那条冲沟的沟底找到了帕塞尔的盔式遮阳帽，而他此去，就是要赶到那条沟的源头。可这是同一条山谷么？当然是。先前他没大留心，知道这行当马特比他在行。可这准是山里同一道裂谷。他只得继续往前，管他有没有向导。马特自己照看得住自己。他必须找到帕塞尔。找不着，他待在这儿就没有一点意义。

===Original===
He shouted. “Parsell! Mat!”

Oh, don’t be a damned fool. They’re not on the telephone.

Better get on with it. Some hours yet to sunset. He turned, and ascended the slope. This was his own particular job, anyhow. He had asked for it. He would have to do it alone. This was the same gully, of course. Why doubt it? Parsell could hardly have gone another way without wings. The leeches had made a mess of that leg, by the look of it. Might have been stuck by a bayonet. No time now to see to it, though. When Parsell was found they’d put everything in order, and get out of that country at the double, even if they had to truss up the old boy. He ought to have been trussed up at first. He must have wandered up that valley; to what did it lead?

===Chinese===
他喊出声来。「帕塞尔！马特！」

哦，别当个十足的傻瓜。他们又不在电话机跟前。

还是赶路要紧。离日落还有几个钟头。他转过身，往坡上走。这反正全是他一个人的差事。是他自讨的。他只好独自去做。这自然是同一条冲沟。有什么可疑的？帕塞尔除非生了翅膀，否则走不上另一条路。瞧那样子，蚂蟥已把那条腿糟蹋得不轻。倒像叫刺刀捅过似的。不过眼下没工夫照管它。等找着帕塞尔，一切自会料理妥当，然后一路跑步离开这片地方，哪怕得把老家伙捆起来抬着走。当初原就该把他捆起来的。他必定是晃上那条山谷去了；那山谷又通向哪里呢？

===Original===
Those rocks and tree-trunks were inhuman, as if, in that glassy stillness, they were shapes at the bottom of the sea. No day was there; it was too far below the surface for much to show. Was it getting dark already? More likely the sides of the narrow valley were closing in; they would, of course, towards the head of it. But if you looked up, nothing could be seen. Nothing was there but ropes and wreckage dangling from a ceiling out of sight.

===Chinese===
那些岩石和树干不属于人世，仿佛在那种琉璃般的死寂里，它们是海底的种种形影。那里没有白昼；它沉得太深，显不出多少名堂。天已经黑了么？多半是窄谷的两壁正在合拢；到了谷头一带，它们自然要合拢。可你若抬头上望，什么也看不见。那里什么也没有，只有绳索和残骸，从望不见顶的天花板上垂挂下来。

===Original===
He stopped again and listened. Great lichened hummocks of rock, like grey couched animals, watched him. Sable pillars receded, the endless aisles of an unholy tabernacle. Shouldn’t like to meet its priests. Nobody there, though. Roots were coiled and contorted in an everlasting agony. And not a sound. If Parsell was left there he would die that night﻿—he would go properly mad. No wonder the Malays called it forbidden. He must get the old man out of it.

That fallen tree﻿—he supposed he would have to climb over it. No way round it. But only night was beyond it. What was the good?

Anyway, Parsell’s helmet was at the bottom of that valley. Where else could he have gone? But could he have ascended so far, through that stuff? It wanted some doing. Now, over that tree, and get on with it. He might as well stay there himself as go back without the old ’un.

===Chinese===
他又停下谛听。一座座生满地衣的巨岩，像伏卧的灰色走兽，监视着他。黝黑的柱子节节退远，是一座渎神圣殿的无尽廊道。可别撞见它的祭司才好。不过那里空无一人。树根盘曲扭绞，陷在一种永恒的苦痛里。而一点声息也没有。帕塞尔若被撂在那里，当夜就会死掉——他会彻头彻尾地疯掉。难怪马来人管这地方叫禁地。他必须把老人弄出去。

那棵倒下的树——他想他只好从上面爬过去。没有绕过去的路。可树的另一边只有黑夜。过去又有什么好处？

不管怎么说，帕塞尔的盔式遮阳帽是在那条山谷的谷底。他还能往别处去么？可他竟能穿过那些东西爬上这么高来么？那得费一番功夫。好了，翻过那棵树，继续赶路。与其丢下那老的空手回去，倒不如自己也待在这儿。

===Original===
Colet climbed the prone column, thrusting creepers and a tough raffle from his face, grunted, and was on the top of it. What was beyond? Only more of the eternal rocks and wreckage on the dim slope, in a light which told him of the end of time. The day after the last day would be the same as this. The light dying and the world a wet litter.

===Chinese===
科利特攀上那根倒卧的巨柱，把藤蔓和一把坚韧的乱柴从脸上拨开，哼了一声，登上了顶端。那一边是什么？只是昏暗坡上更多亘古的岩石和残破，浸在一片光里——那光告诉他，时间到了尽头。末日之后的第二天，会同今天一模一样。光在熄灭，世界成了一摊湿漉漉的破烂。

===Original===
The tree collapsed suddenly as he was gazing ahead, and he was dropped kicking into the hollow heart of the trunk, in choking dust. Something struggled with him, and slithered out past him. He shouted when he caught hold of its hard and slippery body. And he was trapped﻿—he couldn’t escape. The desperate stuff broke away in his hands.

Hold hard. It’s only a rotten tree. And oh, by God! The ants in it were like fire all over him.

===Chinese===
他正朝前望，那棵树忽然塌了，他手脚乱蹬，跌进树干空了的心膛，落进呛人的尘土。有什么东西同他挣扎，又贴着他滑了出去。他攥住那坚硬滑溜的身体时，喊出了声。他陷住了——脱不了身。那拼死挣扎的东西在他手里碎裂开来。

稳住。不过是一棵烂树。可是哦，我的老天！树里的蚂蚁像火，爬满了他一身。

===Original===
They helped him out of it. He was soon out then. Now, if he continued in that trembling and mucky sweat, he’d be added to Parsell. By Jove, those little devils could bite. Like the points of red-hot wires. His job was not to get lost, but to find the old man. You are only lost if you think you are.

Parsell must have had insane strength to have struggled up through that lumber of a dead and forgotten time. What possessed him to go alone? Not to have been turned back by the very silence of it? And in the dark, too. If the old man had a faith which could turn the apparition of hell into a forecourt to be walked through with a visiting card, it would be worth knowing. He could do with a pennyworth of it now, to be going on with. Hard luck to have to do it without any.

===Chinese===
倒是它们帮他从树里脱了身。这么说，他很快就出来了。好了，要是再这么抖个不停、淌着泥汗，他就要去同帕塞尔作伴了。好家伙，那些小恶魔真会咬。像烧红的铁丝尖。他的差事不是把自己弄丢，而是找到那老人。你只有自以为迷了路，才算真迷了路。

帕塞尔准是有一股疯劲，才挣扎着穿过了那一堆死去而被遗忘的时代的烂木头。是什么附了他的体，叫他独自前去？那一片沉寂本身竟也没能叫他回头？还是在黑地里。这老人若果真有一种信仰，能把地狱的幻景变作一方前庭，可以手持名片从容步过，那倒值得领教领教。他眼下正用得着那么一便士的这种货色，先撑着再说。半个子儿都没有却非做不可，真是时运不济。

===Original===
It must be getting near night. It was dark enough for it. Perhaps the evening storm was near. That was what it was; for heaven never peeped into that valley. He was in for it, if a storm burst over such a pit. The trees were unusually still. Waiting for something? Surely he was not purblind; his eyes were all right? The trees were filmy in the dusk. They were shades standing about.

This was the head of the valley, perhaps. That was a sheer wall of rock, so he could go no further. That could not be climbed. Useless to look for a little old man when you could hardly see a precipice, and the day was only a trifle paler than the black trees. What were all those shapes waiting for? Standing about? For him to go? No good. He wasn’t going. There was nowhere to go but back, and he could not go back. Not without Parsell. No point in it. To hell with the darkness and the shapes.

===Chinese===
想必临近夜晚了。天色也黑得够了。也许傍晚的暴雨就在近处。就是这么回事；因为上天从不向这条山谷里偷看一眼。倘若一场暴雨砸进这样的深坑，他就有得受了。树木异样地安静。在等什么？总不至于是他半瞎了；他的眼睛还好好的吧？树木在暮色里成了朦胧的薄膜。它们是四下站立的一群幽灵。

这里也许就是山谷的源头了。那是一面笔直的石壁，他没法再往前了。那墙也攀不上去。连悬崖都险些看不清的时候，去找一个小老头是没用的；天光也只比黑色的树木淡那么一丝。那些形影全在等什么？四下站着？等他走？没用的。他不走了。除了回头无处可去，可他又回不了头。不能丢下帕塞尔回头。回去没有意义。让黑暗和这些形影都见鬼去罢。

===Original===
Perhaps the old man had been translated, gone up in a fiery chariot while they were not watching. Anything might happen there. And what had happened to Mat? That was queer. Mat had been behind him﻿—had shown him which was the way to take a second or two before. Turned round, and nothing was there but the leaves watching him. There was more in what the Malays said about this mountain than he knew. Mat hadn’t liked the job. He had been reluctant about it. He said the mountain was guarded. Well, enchantment or not, there he was and there was no way out of it now.

It sounded as if the place was talking to itself, now it had got him; got him all round. Was it safe to wait under the precipice? There was a noise, up above, like the sea breaking. The storm was coming. What about repeating the Paternoster? Colet considered it. Couldn’t remember it. The aisles filled with quivering blue fire; the trees danced. He had laughed at the men chanting the mantras for a safe journey. But he didn’t know any mantras. Too late now to learn prayers and exemptions. Here it came. Poor old Parsell.

===Chinese===
也许那老人已经蒙召升天，趁他们没看着，乘火焰战车去了。在那地方，什么事都发生得出来。那么马特又出了什么事？那才叫蹊跷。马特本来跟在他身后——就在一两秒钟之前，还指给他该走的路。一转身，那里什么也没有，只有叶子在窥看他。马来人讲这座山的那些话，内里的名堂比他知道的多。马特一直不喜欢这桩差事。他本来就不情愿。他说这山是有人看守的。唉，管它着魔不着魔，他人已在这里，如今再没有脱身的路了。

听那声响，这地方像在自言自语，既然已把他拿到手；把他团团围住了。在峭壁底下等，稳妥么？上头有一种声音，像海在崩碎。暴雨来了。念一遍主祷文（Paternoster）怎么样？科利特掂量了一下。记不全了。廊道里注满了颤动的蓝火；树木跳起舞来。他先前还笑话伙计们念保一路平安的曼特罗。可他自己一句曼特罗也不会。如今再学祷告和禳解，太迟了。来了。可怜的老帕塞尔。

===Original===
A rolling of drums, the steady booming of the coming of calamity; the hooting of bony things following the drums. Men? No, no men there. The *hantus*; they had a night out. The mountain was hollow and booming. They were marching out of it up their valley, an army of them. It was their place. He was caught, back against a wall. He strained his eyes on the cellar blackness towards the shouts. They were muttering near him now. If he could only see﻿ ﻿…

His prison opened suddenly, and skeletons of fire were capering round him, arms about the trees, taking the trees with them. The trees had no weight. They leaped. They had no roots. They were on quick feet. The roots were flinging out of the earth, they were lashing near him, serpents of fire.

No. That was the rain. The floods were pouring down. Torrents of romping fire. The valley was going. The mountain was collapsing and running down. He was going with it.

===Chinese===
一阵鼓声滚动，那是灾祸来临的沉沉轰鸣；随着鼓声，是白骨之物呜呜的喧噪。是人么？不，那里没有人。是『汉图』；它们夜里出来撒了一趟野。这山是空心的，嗡嗡作响。它们正开出山来，沿着他们的山谷上来，一支大军。那是它们的地盘。他被逮住了，背贴着墙。他朝喊声的方向睁大双眼，直望进那地窖般的漆黑里。它们如今已到了近旁，喃喃不休。但愿能看得见……

If you asked him, then he’d been out all night. Was this today or yesterday? There was no saying whether this was early morning or afternoon. The place had been deep under water. The earth and the trees were still talking about it. His watch had stopped. He would like to know what had happened. His clothes looked as though he’d slept in the bed of a river.
