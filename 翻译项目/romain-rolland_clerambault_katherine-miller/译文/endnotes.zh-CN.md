## Endnotes / 尾注

===Original===
1. This Introduction was published in the Swiss newspapers in December, 1917, with an episode of the novel and a note explaining the original title, *L’Un contre Tous*. “This somewhat ironical name was suggested—with a difference—by La Boëtie’s *Le Contr’ Un*; but it must not be supposed that the author entertained the extravagant idea of setting one man in opposition to all others; he only wishes to summon the personal conscience to the most urgent conflict of our time, the struggle against the herd-spirit.” ↩︎

2. Leopardi. ↩︎

===Chinese===
1. 本篇导言于 1917 年 12 月发表于瑞士各报，同时刊出的还有小说的一个片段，以及一则说明原书题名《一人抗众人》（L’Un contre Tous）的附注。「这个略带讥讽意味的题名，系受拉博埃西（La Boëtie）的《反独一》（Le Contr’ Un）启发而来——只是略作变化；但切莫以为，作者曾抱有什么让一人与所有其他人对立起来的狂想；他只是想把个人的良知，召入我们这个时代最紧迫的一场冲突——反对羊群精神的斗争。」 ↩︎

2. 莱奥帕尔迪（Leopardi）。 ↩︎

===Original===
3. “Simon and I then understood our hatred of strangers and barbarians, and our egotism, in which we included ourselves and our entire small moral family.—*The first care of him who would wish to live must be to surround himself with high walls; but even in his closed garden he must introduce only those who are guided by the same feelings, and interests analogous to his own*.”
 —*A Free Man*

 In three lines, three times, this “free man” expresses the idea of “shutting-up,” “closing,” and “surrounding with walls.” ↩︎

4. Reference to ʻAbduʻl-Bahá, at present the head of the Babists or Bahaists. He was at that time a prisoner at St. Jean d’Acre. See *Lessons of St. Jean d’Acre*, by ʻAbduʻl-Bahá, collected by Laura Clifford Barney. ↩︎

===Chinese===
3. 「那时，西蒙和我明白了我们对异乡人与蛮族的憎恶，也明白了我们的利己主义——在这利己主义里，我们把我们自己、连同我们整个小小的道德家族，都一并包容在内。——凡愿活下去的人，头一件要紧的事，必是用高墙把自己围起来；但即便在自己封闭的园子里，他也只应接纳那些受同样情感引导、利益与己相近的人。」
 ——《一个自由的人》（A Free Man）

 短短三行之内，这位「自由的人」三度道出同一层意思：「闭门自守」、「封闭」、「筑墙围困」。 ↩︎

4. 指阿博都-巴哈（ʻAbduʻl-Bahá），时任巴布教徒（Babists）或称巴哈伊信徒（Bahaists）之首。他当时正被囚禁于圣让-达克尔（St. Jean d’Acre）。参看阿博都-巴哈所著、劳拉·克利福德·巴尼（Laura Clifford Barney）辑录的《圣让-达克尔讲义》（Lessons of St. Jean d’Acre）。 ↩︎
