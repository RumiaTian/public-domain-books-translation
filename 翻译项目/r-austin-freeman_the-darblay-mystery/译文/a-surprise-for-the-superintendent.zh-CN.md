## XVI A Surprise for the Superintendent / 第十六章 给警司的意外

===Original===
It appeared that Thorndyke was correct in his estimate of the Superintendent’s state of mind, for that officer managed to dispose in a very short space of time of the formalities necessary for the obtaining of an exhumation license from the Home Office. It was less than a week after the interview that I have recorded when I received a note from Thorndyke asking me to join him and Miller at King’s Bench Walk on the following morning at the unholy hour of half-past six. He offered to put me up for the night at his chambers, but I declined this hospitality, not wishing to trouble him unnecessarily; and after a perfunctory breakfast by gaslight, a ride on an early tram, and a walk through the dim, lamp-lit streets, I entered the Temple just as the subdued notes of an invisible clock bell announced a quarter past six. On my arrival at Thorndyke’s chambers I observed a roomy hired carriage drawn up at the entry, and, ascending the stairs, found “the Doctor” and Miller ready to start, each provided with a good-sized handbag.

===Chinese===
看来桑代克（Thorndyke）对警司心境的估量并没有错，因为这位警官竟在极短的工夫里，办妥了从内政部（Home Office）领取掘尸执照所需的一切手续。就在我所记述的那次会面之后不到一星期，我收到桑代克的便条，邀我次日清晨六点半——一个不像话的钟点——到王座法庭步道（King's Bench Walk）与他和米勒警司（Miller）会合。他提出让我当晚在他的寓所留宿，我却辞谢了这份殷勤，不愿无端搅扰他；于是，就着煤气灯草草用过早餐，乘一趟早班电车，再穿过昏暗的、路灯未熄的街巷，待一座看不见的钟楼闷声报出六点一刻，我恰好走进内殿（the Temple）。一到桑代克的寓所，便见入口处停着一辆宽敞的雇用马车；上得楼来，只见“博士”和米勒警司已整装待发，两人各提着一只不小的手提包。

===Original===
“This is a queer sort of function,” I remarked as we took our way down the stairs; “a sort of funeral the wrong way about.”

===Chinese===
“这可真是一场古怪的仪式，”我们下楼时我议论道，“好比一场倒着办的葬礼。”

===Original===
“Yes,” Thorndyke agreed; “it is what Lewis Carroll would have called an unfuneral﻿—and very appropriately, too. I didn’t give you any particulars in my note, but you understand the object of this expedition?”

===Chinese===
“是啊，”桑代克表示同意，“这要是让刘易斯·卡罗尔（Lewis Carroll）来说，准得管它叫‘非葬礼’——而且叫得再贴切不过。便条里我没有细说缘由，可这趟出征的目的，你是明白的吧？”

===Original===
“I assume that we are going to resurrect the late Jonathan Crile,” I replied. “It isn’t very clear to me what I have to do with the business, as I never knew Mr. Crile, though I am delighted to have this rather uncommon experience. But I should have thought that Usher would be the proper person to accompany you.”

===Chinese===
“我猜咱们是去把已故的乔纳森·克赖尔（Jonathan Crile）从土里请出来，”我答道，“我同这位克赖尔先生素昧平生，所以不很明白此事与我何干；不过能捞到这样一段颇不寻常的经历，我也乐意之至。只是照我想，厄舍（Usher）才是陪您同去的正主儿吧。”

===Original===
“So the Superintendent thought,” said Thorndyke, “and quite rightly; so I have arranged to pick up Usher and take him with us. He will be able to identify the body as that of his late patient, and you and I will help the Superintendent to take the fingerprints.”

===Chinese===
“警司也是这么想的，”桑代克说，“而且想得对；所以我已安排顺路接上厄舍，带他一同前往。他认得出那具尸身是不是他那位已故的病人，而你我两个，则帮着警司取指纹。”

===Original===
“I am taking your word for it, Doctor,” said Miller, “that the fingerprints will be recognizable, and that they will be the wrong ones.”

===Chinese===
“博士，我这是把您的话当真了才来的，”米勒说，“信那指纹还认得出来，而且认出来的，准是对不上的那副。”

===Original===
“I don’t guarantee that,” Thorndyke replied, “but still, I shall be surprised if you get the right ones.”

===Chinese===
“这我可不敢打包票，”桑代克答道，“不过话说回来，倘若您取到的是原先那副指纹，我倒要奇怪了。”

===Original===
Miller nodded with an air of satisfaction, and nothing more was said on the subject until we drew up before Dr. Usher’s surgery. That discreet practitioner was already waiting at the open door, and at once took his place in the carriage, watched curiously by observers from adjacent windows.

===Chinese===
米勒满意地点了点头。这个题目此后便无人再提，直到马车停在厄舍博士的诊所门前。这位行事谨慎的医生早已候在敞开的门口，随即上车坐定，惹得邻近几扇窗口里的旁观者们好奇地张望。

===Original===
“This is a rum go,” he remarked, diffusing a vinous aroma into the atmosphere of the carriage. “I really did think I had paid my last visit to Mr. Crile. But there’s no such thing as certainty in this world.” He chuckled softly and continued: “A bit different this journey from the last. No hatbands this time, and no Sunday-school children. Lord! When I think of those kids piping round the open grave, and that our dear departed brother was wanted by the police so badly that they were actually going to dig him up, it makes me smile, it does, indeed.”

===Chinese===
“这可真是桩邪门事，”他说着，一股酒气随之弥散进车厢的空气里。“我原本真以为，对克赖尔先生的出诊，上一回便是末一回了。可这世上压根儿没有‘万无一失’这回事。”他低声窃笑，又接着说：“这一趟同上一趟可有点不一样。这回没有丧帽带，也没有主日学的娃娃们。天哪！一想到那群孩子尖着嗓门围着敞开的墓穴唱圣歌，一想到咱们那位亲爱的亡故弟兄原来被警察追得那么紧，紧到人家竟要把他从土里刨出来，我就直想笑，真的，不骗你。”

===Original===
In effect, it made him cackle; and as Miller had not heard the account of the funeral, it was repeated for his benefit in great detail. Then the anecdotal ball was set rolling in a fresh direction by one or two questions from Thorndyke, with the result that the entire history of Usher’s attendance on the deceased, including the misdeeds of Mrs. Pepper, was retailed with such a wealth of circumstance that the narration lasted until we stopped at the cemetery gate.

===Chinese===
说实在的，这话惹得他咯咯直笑；米勒不曾听过那场葬礼的始末，于是又有人替他详详细细讲了一遍。接着，桑代克的一两个问题又把闲谈的球朝新的方向一滚，结果便是：厄舍为亡者出诊的全份历史，连佩珀太太（Mrs. Pepper）的种种劣迹在内，都被添枝加叶地转述出来，一直讲到马车停在墓园门口才算完。

===Original===
Our arrival was not unexpected, for as we got out of the carriage, two gentlemen approached the entrance, and one of them unlocked a gate to admit us. He appeared to be the official in charge of the cemetery, while the other, to whom he introduced us, was no less a person than Dr. Garroll, the Medical Officer of Health.

===Chinese===
我们的到来并不出人意料，因为刚一下车，便有两位先生迎向入口，其中一位开锁放我们进去。他像是主管墓园的职员；另一位经他引见与我们相识的，不是别人，正是医学卫生官（Medical Officer of Health）加罗尔博士（Dr. Garroll）。

===Original===
“The Home Office license,” the latter explained, “directs that the removal shall be carried out under my supervision and to my satisfaction; very necessary in a populous neighbourhood like this.”

===Chinese===
“内政部的执照，”后者解释道，“规定起棺须在我的监督之下进行，并须办到我满意为止；在这样一个人烟稠密的街区，这一层确属必要。”

===Original===
“Very necessary,” Thorndyke agreed gravely.

===Chinese===
“确属必要，”桑代克郑重地附和。

===Original===
“I have provided a supply of fresh ground lime, according to the directions,” Dr. Garroll continued; “and as a further precaution, I have brought with me a large formalin spray. That, I think, should satisfy all sanitary requirements.”

===Chinese===
“遵照指令，我已备下一批新鲜的生石灰粉，”加罗尔博士接着说，“作为进一步的防范，我还随身带了一只大号福尔马林喷雾器。我想，有此一物，一切卫生要求也就都周全了。”

===Original===
“It should certainly be sufficient,” Thorndyke agreed, “to meet the requirements of the present case. Has the excavation been commenced yet?”

===Chinese===
“应付眼下这桩事，这确乎尽够了，”桑代克表示同意，“挖掘已经开工了吗？”

===Original===
“Oh, yes,” replied the cemetery official. “It was started quite early, and has been carried down nearly to the full depth; but I thought that the coffin had better not be uncovered until you arrived. I have had a canvas screen put up round the grave so that the proceedings may be quite private. We can send the labourers outside before we unscrew the coffin-lid. You said, Superintendent, that you were anxious to avoid any kind of publicity; and I have warned the men to say nothing to anyone about the affair.”

===Chinese===
“哦，开工了，”墓园职员答道，“一早就动了手，已经挖到快够深了；不过我想，棺材还是等各位到了再揭开为好。我已叫人在墓穴四周支起一道帆布围屏，好让这里进行的事宜全然避人耳目。起棺盖之前，我们可以把工人们打发到外头去。警司，您说过务求避免任何形式的张扬；我也已告诫过那些人，此事对谁都不许吐露半个字。”

===Original===
“Quite right,” said Miller. “We don’t want this to get into the papers, in case﻿—well, in any case.”

===Chinese===
“很对，”米勒说，“这事不能见报，万一——嗯，反正无论如何都不能。”

===Original===
“Exactly, sir,” agreed the official, who was evidently bursting with curiosity himself. “Exactly. Here is the screen. If you will step inside, the excavation can be proceeded with.”

===Chinese===
“正是，长官，”职员附和道，他自己分明也好奇得按捺不住。“正是。围屏在这儿。诸位请进，挖掘便可继续。”

===Original===
We passed inside the screen, where we found four men reposefully contemplating a coil of stout rope, a basket, attached to another rope, and a couple of spades. The grave yawned in the middle of the enclosure, flanked on one side by the mound of newly dug earth and on the other by a tub of lime and a Winchester quart bottle fitted with a spray nozzle and a large rubber bellows.

===Chinese===
我们走进围屏，只见四条汉子正安闲地端详着一盘粗绳、一只拴着另一根绳子的吊篮，还有两把铁锹。墓穴在围场中央张着大口，一侧倚着新掘的土山，另一侧是一桶石灰和一只温切斯特夸脱瓶（Winchester quart）——瓶上装着喷雾嘴，连着一只大橡皮风箱。

===Original===
“You can get on with the digging now,” said the official; whereupon one of the men was let down into the grave, together with a spade and the basket, and fell to work briskly. Then Dr. Garroll directed one of the other men to sprinkle in a little lime; which he did, with a pleased smile and so little discretion that the man below was seen to stop digging, and after looking up indignantly, take off his cap, shake it violently and ostentatiously dust his shoulders with it.

===Chinese===
“现在可以接着挖了，”职员说。于是一条汉子带着一把铁锹和那只吊篮被放落穴中，麻利地干起活来。接着，加罗尔博士又指挥另外一人往穴里撒了点石灰；那人含着得色的微笑照办了，只是全无分寸，只见穴底那位停了锹，愤然仰起脸来，摘下帽子狠狠抖了几抖，又拿它大张旗鼓地把肩膀掸了一遍。

===Original===
When about a dozen basketfuls of earth had been hoisted up, a hollow, woody sound accompanying the thrusts of the spade announced that the coffin had been reached. Thereupon more lime was sprinkled in, and Dr. Garroll, picking up the formalin bottle, sprayed vigorously into the cavity until a plaintive voice from below﻿—accompanied by an unnaturally loud sneeze﻿—was heard to declare that “he’d ’ave brought his umbrella if he’d knowed he was goin’ to be squirted at.” A few minutes’ more work exposed the coffin and enabled us to read the confirmatory inscription on the plate. Then the rope slings were let down and with some difficulty worked into position by the excavator below; who, when he had completed his task, climbed to the surface and grasped one end of a sling in readiness to haul on it.

===Chinese===
吊上十几篮土之后，铁锹每一插下去都伴着一种空空的、敲木头似的声响，宣告已经挖到棺材了。于是又撒进些石灰；加罗尔博士拿起福尔马林瓶，朝穴里使劲喷雾，直喷到穴底传来一声哀哀的抱怨——伴着一个响得不自然的喷嚏——宣称“早晓得要挨水滋，俺就该把伞带来”。再干上几分钟，棺材整个露了出来，棺上那方铭牌也可辨认了，足证无误。随后几条绳套被放下去，穴底挖土的汉子费了些周折才把它们套到位；套好之后，他爬上地面，抓住一根绳套的一头，只待出力拉拽。

===Original===
“It’s a good deal easier letting ’em down than hoisting ’em up,” Usher remarked, as the final shower of lime descended and the men began to haul; “but poor old Crile oughtn’t to take much lifting. There was nothing of him but skin and bone.”

===Chinese===
“棺材这东西，放下去可比吊上来省事多喽，”最后一阵石灰撒下、汉子们开始拉拽时，厄舍说道，“不过可怜的老克赖尔也费不了多大吊劲。他整个人除了皮就是骨头。”

===Original===
However this might be, it took the united efforts of the four men to draw the coffin up to the surface and slew it round clear of the yawning grave. But at last this was accomplished, and it was lifted, for convenience of inspection, on to one of the mounds of newly dug earth.

===Chinese===
话虽如此，四条汉子齐心合力，才把棺材拖上地面，挪转开去，避开那张着大口的墓穴。但这一步终究是完成了；为便于查验起见，棺材被抬到了一座新土堆上。

===Original===
“Now,” said the presiding official, “you men had better go outside and wait down at the end of the path until you are wanted again:” an order that was received with evident disfavour and complied with rather sulkily. As soon as they were gone, our friend produced a couple of screwdrivers, with which he and Miller proceeded in a very workmanlike manner to extract the screws, while Dr. Garroll enveloped them in a cloud of spray, and Thorndyke, Usher, and I stood apart to keep out of range. I turned quickly and saw that officer standing with the raised coffin-lid in his hand, staring into the interior with a look of perfectly indescribable amazement. Instantly I rushed forward and looked into the coffin; and then I was no less amazed. For in place of the mortal remains of the late Jonathan Crile, was a portly sack oozing sawdust from a hole in its side, through which coyly peeped a length of thick lead pipe.

===Chinese===
“好了，”主事的职员说，“你们几个最好到外头去，在小路那头等着，等再叫你们：”这道命令显然不受待见，众人黑着脸勉强照办。他们一走，我们这位朋友便摸出两把螺丝刀，同米勒一起，以十足行家的手法起起螺丝来；加罗尔博士则把他俩裹进一团喷雾里，桑代克、厄舍和我站开一旁，免得沾着喷雾。这活计费时不长；说真的，它完事得比我预想的还快，因为我得知大功告成的头一个讯号，便是米勒警司嗓门里迸出的一声大喊（只一个词：“活见鬼！”）。我急忙转身，只见这位警官一手举着掀起的棺盖，正朝棺内凝望，脸上是一种全然没法形容的惊愕。我立刻抢上前去，往棺材里一看——这一看，我也惊得不相上下。原来，在已故的乔纳森·克赖尔遗骸该在的地方，躺着一只肥鼓鼓的麻袋，侧身一个窟窿里正往外渗着锯末，窟窿口还羞答答地探出一截粗铅管。

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Original===

“博士，您原先就知道这口棺材是空的？”他说。

===Chinese===
桑代克摇了摇头。“我要是早知道，”他答道，“早就告诉您了。”

===Chinese===

===Original===

But the more I considered the astonishing event which had just happened the more incomprehensible did it appear. And yet I saw plainly that it could not really be incomprehensible since Thorndyke had actually arrived at its probability in advance. The glaring discrepancies and inconsistencies which chased one another through my mind could not be real. They must be susceptible of reconciliation with the observed facts. ===Chinese===
