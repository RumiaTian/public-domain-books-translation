## I The Pool in the Wood / 第一章 林中水潭

===Original===
There are certain days in our lives which, as we recall them, seem to detach themselves from the general sequence as forming the starting-point of a new epoch. Doubtless, if we examined them critically, we should find them to be but links in a connected chain. But in a retrospective glance their continuity with the past is unperceived, and we see them in relation to the events which followed them rather than to those which went before.
===Chinese===
人的一生中，有些日子在回忆起来时，仿佛会从连续的整体中自行脱离，自成一个新的纪元的起点。毫无疑问，倘若我们细加推究，便会发现它们不过是环环相扣的链条上的一环。但在回望之际，我们察觉不到它们与过去的连贯，只把它们同其后发生的事件、而非先前的事件联系在一起看待。

===Original===
Such a day is that on which I look back through a vista of some twenty years; for on that day I was, suddenly and without warning, plunged into the very heart of a drama so strange and incredible that in the recital of its events I am conscious of a certain diffidence and hesitation.
===Chinese===
我隔着约莫二十年的光阴回望的那一天，便是这样一个日子；因为就在那一天，我毫无预兆地骤然被卷入了一桩奇事的最中心，那事如此离奇、如此令人难以置信，以致如今追述其经过时，我心里还怀着几分怯懦和迟疑。

===Original===
The picture that rises before me as I write is very clear and vivid. I see myself, a youngster of twenty-five, the owner of a brand-new medical diploma, wending my way gaily down Wood Lane, Highgate, at about eight o’clock on a sunny morning in early autumn. I was taking a day’s holiday, the last I was likely to enjoy for some time; for on the morrow I was to enter on the duties of my first professional appointment. I had nothing in view today but sheer, delightful idleness. It is true that a sketchbook in one pocket and a box of collecting-tubes in another suggested a bare hint of purpose in the expedition; but primarily it was a holiday, a pleasure jaunt, to which art and science were no more than possible sources of contributory satisfaction.
===Chinese===
我提笔之际，眼前浮现的那幅图景依然十分清晰、鲜明。我看见自己——一个二十五岁的年轻人，揣着一张崭新的医学文凭——在初秋一个晴朗的早晨约莫八点钟，兴冲冲地走下海格特（Highgate）的木巷（Wood Lane）。我正在享受一天假期，这也是一段时期内我多半所能享受的最后一个假期了；因为明天我就要走马上任，履行我职业生涯中第一个职位的职责。这一天我别无他图，只求纯粹的、惬意的闲散。诚然，一只口袋里装着的写生簿，另一只口袋里装着的一盒采集管，多少暗示此行带有一点正经目的；但这主要还是一次休憩、一场游乐，艺术与科学至多不过是锦上添花的额外乐趣罢了。

===Original===
At the lower end of the lane was the entrance to Churchyard Bottom Wood, then open and unguarded save by a few hurdles (it has since been enclosed and renamed “Queen’s Wood”). I entered and took my way along the broad, rough path, pleasantly conscious of the deep silence and seeming remoteness of this surviving remnant of the primeval forest of Britain, and letting my thoughts stray to the great plague-pit in the haunted wood that gave the place its name. The foliage of the oaks was still unchanged despite the waning of the year. The low-slanting sunlight spangled it with gold and made rosy patterns on the path, where lay a few prematurely fallen leaves; but in the hollows among the undergrowth traces of the night-mists lingered, shrouding tree-bole, bush, and fern in a mystery of gauzy blue.
===Chinese===
小巷的下端是教堂洼林（Churchyard Bottom Wood）的入口，当年那里敞开着，无人看守，只拦着几道栅栏（那片林子后来围了起来，改称“王后林（Queen’s Wood）”）。我走进林中，沿着那条宽阔崎岖的小径走去，惬意地感受着这片英国原始森林残存遗迹的幽深寂静与看似遥远的风貌，任由思绪飘向那片阴森林子里的大瘟疫坑——正是它使这地方得了这个名字。尽管时序渐晚，橡树的叶子依然未改其色。低斜的阳光在树冠上洒下点点金光，又在小径上投出玫瑰色的光影，径上躺着几片早落的树叶；但在灌木丛的洼地里，夜雾的痕迹尚未散尽，给树干、灌木和蕨草都蒙上了一层薄纱似的蓝色迷离。

===Original===
A turn of the path brought me suddenly within a few paces of a girl who was stooping at the entrance to a sidetrack, and seemed to be peering into the undergrowth as if looking for something. As I appeared, she stood up and looked round at me with a startled, apprehensive manner that caused me to look away and pass as if I had not seen her. But the single glance had shown me that she was a strikingly handsome girl﻿—indeed, I should have used the word “beautiful”; that she seemed to be about my own age, and that she was evidently a lady.
===Chinese===
小径一转，我蓦然来到离一个姑娘只有几步远的地方；她正俯身立在一条岔道的道口，似乎在朝灌木丛里张望，像在寻找什么。我一出现，她便直起身来，回头看我，神色惊惶而戒备，弄得我只好移开目光，装作没有看见她似的走了过去。但就这一眼已让我看清：她是个容貌出众的姑娘——说实话，我本该用“美丽”这个词来形容她；她年纪似乎同我相仿，而且显然是位淑女。

===Original===
The apparition, pleasant as it was, set me speculating as I strode forward. It was early for a girl like this to be afoot in the woods, and alone, too. Not so very safe, either, as she had seemed to realize, judging by the start that my approach seemed to have given her. And what could it be that she was looking for? Had she lost something at some previous time and come to search for it before anyone was about? It might be so. Certainly she was not a poacher, for there was nothing to poach, and she hardly had the manner or appearance of a naturalist.
===Chinese===
这次相逢虽然令人愉快，却让我在阔步前行时不禁暗自揣想。像这样的姑娘，这么早就独自在林子里走动，未免太早了些；而且也不大安全——看她那副样子，她自己似乎也明白这一点，我的走近显然吓了她一跳。再说，她找的能是什么呢？莫非她先前丢了什么东西，趁四下无人时回来寻找？也许是吧。她肯定不是偷猎的人，因为这林子里没什么可偷猎的；而她那举止做派，也不大像是个博物学家。

===Original===
A little farther on I struck into a side path which led, as I knew, in the direction of a small pond. That pond I had had in my mind when I put the box of collecting-tubes in my pocket, and I now made my way to it as directly as the winding track would let me; but still it was not the pond or its inmates that occupied my thoughts, but the mysterious maiden whom I had left peering into the undergrowth. Perhaps if she had been less attractive I might have given her less consideration. But I was twenty-five; and if a man at twenty-five has not a keen and appreciative eye for a pretty girl, there must be something radically wrong with his mental makeup.
===Chinese===
再往前走了一小段，我拐上一条小径，据我所知，它通向一个小池塘。当初往口袋里放那盒采集管时，我心里想着的正是那个池塘；此刻我便顺着蜿蜒的小道，尽量径直地朝它走去；但占据我心思的，仍旧不是那池塘或池中的居民，而是那个被我留在灌木丛边张望的神秘姑娘。倘若她没那么动人，我也许就不会这么惦记她了。可我那年二十五岁；一个男人到了二十五岁，若是对漂亮姑娘没有一双敏锐而欣赏的眼睛，那他的心理构造必定出了什么根本的毛病。

===Original===
In the midst of my reflections I came out into a largish opening in the wood, at the centre of which, in a slight hollow, was the pond: a small oval piece of water, fed by the trickle of a tiny stream, the continuation of which carried away the overflow towards the invisible valley.
===Chinese===
正当我出神思索之际，我走出树林，来到一片颇大的林间空地；空地中央一处浅浅的洼地里，便是那池塘：一小片椭圆形的水域，由一条细流的涓涓细水补给，那条细流的延续部分又将溢出的水送往看不见的山谷。

===Original===
Approaching the margin, I brought out my box of tubes and, uncorking one, stooped and took a trial dip. When I held the glass tube against the light and examined its contents through my pocket lens I found that I was in luck. The “catch” included a green hydra, clinging to a rootlet of duckweed, several active water-fleas, a scarlet water-mite, and a beautiful sessile rotifer. Evidently this pond was a rich hunting ground.
===Chinese===
走近池边，我掏出那盒管子，拔开其中一支的塞子，俯身试探着舀了一下。我把玻璃管举到光前，用袖珍放大镜检视里面的东西，发觉自己运气不错。这一“捕获物”里有一只绿色的水螅，附着在一根浮萍的细根上；好几只活泼的水蚤；一只猩红的水螨；还有一只美丽的固着轮虫。显然，这个池塘是一块丰饶的猎场。

===Original===
Delighted with my success, I corked the tube, put it away, and brought out another, with which I took a fresh dip. This was less successful, but the naturalist’s ardour and the collector’s cupidity being thoroughly aroused, I persevered, gradually enriching my collection and working my way slowly round the margin of the pond, forgetful of everything﻿—even of the mysterious maiden﻿—but the objects of my search; indeed, so engrossed was I with my pursuit of the minute denizens of this watery world that I failed to observe a much larger object which must have been in view most of the time. Actually, I did not see it until I was right over it. Then, as I was stooping to clear away the duckweed for a fresh dip, I found myself confronted by a human face, just below the surface and half-concealed by the pondweed.
===Chinese===
这一收获令我喜出望外，我塞好管子把它收起来，又取出一支，重新舀了一次。这次收获不及前次，但博物学家的热忱和收藏家的贪心已被彻底点燃，我便锲而不舍地干下去，一面渐渐充实我的收藏，一面沿着池边缓缓绕行，把一切都忘在了脑后——连那位神秘的姑娘也忘了——满心只有我要寻找的东西；的确，我专心追逐这个水族世界的微小居民，竟至浑然不觉一个远为庞大的物体——它多半一直在我的视野之内。实际上，直到我正好俯在它上方，我才看见了它。当时我正弯下腰去拨开浮萍，准备再舀一次，冷不防发现自己正对着一张人脸，就在水面之下，半掩在水草之间。

===Original===
It was a truly appalling experience. Utterly unprepared for this awful apparition, I was so overcome by astonishment and horror that I remained stooping, with motion arrested, as if petrified, staring at the thing in silence and hardly breathing. The face was that of a man of about fifty or a little more: a handsome, refined, rather intellectual face, with a moustache and Vandyke beard, and surmounted by a thickish growth of iron-grey hair. Of the rest of the body little was to be seen, for the duckweed and water-crowfoot had drifted over it, and I had no inclination to disturb them.
===Chinese===
这真是一次骇人的经历。对这可怕的幽灵般的景象毫无防备，我又惊又怖，竟保持着俯身的姿势僵住了，仿佛化成了石头，一声不吭地盯着那东西，几乎不敢喘气。那是一张五十岁上下、或者略大一些的男人的脸：英俊、清秀、颇有书卷气，唇上蓄着髭须，颌下留着范戴克式的尖须，头顶覆着一头厚密的铁灰色头发。身体其余的部分几乎看不见，因为浮萍和水毛茛已经漂覆在上面，而我也全无心思去拨动它们。

===Original===
Recovering somewhat from the shock of this sudden and fearful encounter, I stood up and rapidly considered what I had better do. It was clearly not for me to make any examination or meddle with the corpse in any way; indeed, when I considered the early hour and the remoteness of this solitary place, it seemed prudent to avoid the possibility of being seen there by any chance stranger. Thus reflecting, with my eyes still riveted on the pallid, impassive face, so strangely sleeping below the glassy surface and conveying to me somehow a dim sense of familiarity, I pocketed my tubes, and, turning back, stole away along the woodland track, treading lightly, almost stealthily, as one escaping from the scene of a crime.
===Chinese===
从这场突然而可怕的相遇的震撼中稍稍定下神来，我站起身，飞快地盘算着下一步该怎么办。显然，不该由我来验看，也不该以任何方式惊动这具尸体；的确，想到时辰尚早、此地又是这般僻静，还是谨慎为妙，免得被哪个偶然路过的陌生人看见我在这里。我一面这样想着，一面目光仍钉在那张苍白、木然的脸——它在玻璃似的水面下奇怪地沉睡着，不知怎的给我一种隐隐的似曾相识之感——一面把管子装进口袋，转身往回走，沿着林中小学蹑足而去，脚步放得极轻，几乎是偷偷摸摸的，像一个人正从犯罪现场逃开。

===Original===
Very different was my mood, as I retraced my steps, from that in which I had come. Gone was all my gaiety and holiday spirit. The dread meeting had brought me into an atmosphere of tragedy, perchance even of something more than tragedy. With death I was familiar enough; death as it comes to men, prefaced by sickness or even by injury. But the dead man who lay in that still and silent pool in the heart of the wood had come there by none of the ordinary chances of normal life. It seemed barely possible that he could have fallen in by mere misadventure, for the pond was too shallow and its bottom shelved too gently for accidental drowning to be conceivable. Nor was the strange, sequestered spot without significance. It was just such a spot as might well be chosen by one who sought to end his life﻿—or another’s.
===Chinese===
我循原路返回时的心境，同来时已是天壤之别。欢乐的心情和假日的心绪一扫而空。那场可怖的相遇把我带进了一种悲剧的气氛里，甚或是比悲剧更甚的某种东西的气氛里。死亡，我是够熟悉的了；那种循着常规来到人身上的死亡，以疾病、甚至以创伤为先导。可是躺在林子深处那一泓沉寂无声的池水里的死人，却不是经由正常生活的任何寻常变故来到那里的。说他纯属失足落水，看来几乎不可能，因为这池塘太浅，池底又太过平缓，意外溺毙实在难以想象。而那处奇僻的所在，也绝非没有意味。那正是一个想结束自己性命的人——或者想结束别人性命的人——很可能挑选的地方。

===Original===
I had nearly reached the main path when an abrupt turn of the narrow track brought me once more face to face with the girl whose existence I had till now forgotten. She was still peering into the dense undergrowth as if searching for something; and again, on my sudden appearance, she turned a startled face towards me. But this time I did not look away. Something in her face struck me with a nameless fear. It was not only that she was pale and haggard, but that her expression betokened anxiety and even terror. As I looked at her I understood in a flash the dim sense of familiarity of which I had been conscious in the pallid face beneath the water. It was her face that it had recalled.
===Chinese===
我快要走到大路时，窄径猛地一转，又把我带到那个姑娘的面前——直到此刻，我竟把她忘了个干净。她仍旧朝茂密的灌木丛里张望，像在搜寻什么；而我再次骤然出现时，她又是一脸惊愕地转向我。但这一次我没有移开目光。她脸上的某种神色使我生出一种莫名的恐惧。不只是她脸色苍白、形容憔悴，更是她的神情流露着焦灼、甚至恐怖。我望着她，霎时间明白了先前在那张水底下的苍白面孔上感到的那种隐隐的似曾相识是什么——那张脸让我想起的，正是她的脸。

===Original===
With my heart in my mouth, I halted and, taking off my cap, addressed her.
===Chinese===
我心都提到了嗓子眼，停下脚步，脱下便帽，向她开了口。

===Original===
“Pray pardon me; you seem to be searching for something. Can I help you in any way or give you any information?”
===Chinese===
“请恕我冒昧；您似乎在寻找什么东西。我能帮上什么忙，或者给您提供点什么消息吗？”

===Original===
She looked at me a little shyly and, as I thought, with slight distrust, but she answered civilly enough, though rather stiffly:
===Chinese===
她略带羞怯地看了看我，而且——我觉得——还带着几分提防，不过她的回答还算客气，只是相当生硬：

===Original===
“Thank you, but I am afraid you can’t help me. I am not in need of any assistance.”
===Chinese===
“谢谢您，只怕您帮不上我的忙。我不需要什么帮助。”

===Original===
This, under ordinary circumstances, would have brought the interview to an abrupt end. But the circumstances were not ordinary, and as she made as if to pass me I ventured to persist.
===Chinese===
这话若在寻常情形下，早把这场攀谈戛然终结了。可眼前的情形并不寻常，所以当她做出要从我身边走过去的样子时，我大着胆子又追问了一句。

===Original===
“Please,” I urged, “don’t think me impertinent, but would you mind telling me what you are looking for? I have a reason for asking, and it isn’t curiosity.”
===Chinese===
“对不起，”我恳切地说，“请别怪我冒失，您可否告诉我，您在找什么？我这样问是有缘故的，并不是出于好奇。”

===Original===
She reflected for a few moments before replying, and I feared that she was about to administer another snub. Then, without looking at me, she replied:
===Chinese===
她沉吟了片刻才作答，我还担心她又要不客气地回我一句。随后，她眼睛并不看我，答道：

===Original===
“I am looking for my father” (and at these words my heart sank). “He did not come home last night. He left Hornsey to come home, and he would ordinarily have come by the path through the wood. He always came that way from Hornsey. So I am looking through the wood in case he missed his way, or was taken ill, or﻿—”
===Chinese===
“我在找我的父亲”（听了这话，我的心往下一沉）。“他昨夜没有回家。他是从霍恩西（Hornsey）动身回家的，照理他通常会取道穿过林子的那条小路。他从霍恩西回来向来走那条路。所以我在林子里一路找过来，怕他迷了路，或是病倒了，或是——”

===Original===
Here the poor girl suddenly broke off, and, letting her dignity go, burst into tears. I huskily murmured a few indistinct words of condolence, but, in truth, I was little less affected than she was. It was a terrible position, but there was no escape from it. The corpse that I had just seen was almost certainly her father’s corpse. At any rate, the question whether it was or not had to be settled now, and settled by me﻿—and her. That was quite clear; but yet I could not screw my courage up to the point of telling her. While I was hesitating, however, she forced the position by a direct question.
===Chinese===
说到这里，可怜的姑娘突然打住了，顾不得体面，失声哭了起来。我沙哑着嗓子含含糊糊地低声劝慰了几句，可说实话，我受的震动也不比她小多少。这是个可怕的处境，却无从逃避。我刚才看见的那具尸体，几乎可以肯定就是她父亲的尸体。无论如何，那究竟是不是，这个问题此刻必须有个了断，而且要由我——和她——来了断。这是明摆着的；可我仍迟迟鼓不起勇气对她开口。正在我迟疑不决的当口，她一句直截了当的问话逼上了门来。

===Original===
“You said just now that you had a reason for asking what I was searching for. Would it be﻿—?” She paused and looked at me inquiringly as she wiped her eyes.
===Chinese===
“您刚才说，您问我找什么是有缘故的。那缘故莫非是——？”她顿住了，一边擦着眼睛，一边用询问的目光望着我。

===Original===
I made a last, frantic search for some means of breaking the horrid news to her. Of course there was none. Eventually I stammered:
===Chinese===
我拼命地做了最后一次搜寻，想找出一个把这可怕的消息对她开口的话头。当然，那样的话头是不存在的。末了我结结巴巴地说：

===Original===
“The reason I asked was﻿—er﻿—the fact is that I have just seen the body of a man lying﻿—”
===Chinese===
“我问这话，是因为——呃——是这样，我刚才看见一个男人的尸体，躺在——”

===Original===
“Where?” she demanded. “Show me the place!”
===Chinese===
“在哪儿？”她急切地问。“带我到那地方去！”

===Original===
Without replying, I turned and began quickly to retrace my steps along the narrow track. A few minutes brought me to the opening in which the pond was situated, and I was just beginning to skirt the margin, closely followed by my companion, when I heard her utter a low, gasping cry. The next moment she had passed me and was running along the bank towards a spot where I could now see the toe of a boot just showing through the duckweed. I stopped short and watched her with my heart in my throat. Straight to the fatal spot she ran, and for a moment stood on the brink, stooping over the weedy surface. Then, with a terrible, wailing cry, she stepped into the water.
===Chinese===
我没有回答，转过身，快步沿着窄径往回走。几分钟我便到了那片有池塘的空地，正要沿着池边绕行，同伴紧随在我身后——就在这时，我听见她发出一声低低的、抽气似的惊呼。转眼间她已越过我，沿着池岸朝一处地方奔去，此时我也看见那里有一只靴尖正从浮萍下微微露出。我猛地站住，心悬在嗓子眼上望着她。她径直朝那处夺命之地奔去，在池边站了片刻，俯身对着那长满水草的水面。随即，她发出一声凄厉哀号的哭喊，跨进了水里。

===Original===
With this, we walked together to the edge of the opening, and proceeded in single file along the track to the main path, and so out into Wood Lane, at the top of which we crossed the Archway Road into Southwood Lane. We walked mostly in silence, for I was unwilling to disturb her meditations with attempts at conversation, which could only have seemed banal or impertinent. For her part, she appeared to be absorbed in reflections the nature of which I could easily guess, and her grief was too fresh for any thought of distraction. But I found myself speculating with profound discomfort on what might be awaiting her at home. It is true that her own desolate state as an orphan without brothers or sisters had its compensation in that there was no wife to whom the dreadful tidings had to be imparted, nor any fellow orphans to have their bereavement broken to them. But there must be someone who cared; or, if there were not, what a terrible loneliness would reign in that house!
===Chinese===
