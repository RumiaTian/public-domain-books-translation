## IV Mr. Bendelow / 第四章 本德洛先生

===Original===
There are certain districts in London the appearance of which conveys to the observer the impression that the houses, and indeed, the entire streets, have been picked up secondhand. There is in this aspect a grey, colourless, mouldy quality, reminiscent, not of the antique shop, but rather of the marine store dealers; a quality which even communicates itself to the inhabitants, so that one gathers the impression that the whole neighbourhood was taken as a going concern.
===Chinese===
伦敦有那么几个街区，它们的样子给观者一个印象：这里的房屋，乃至整条整条的街道，都是二手拣来的。这份观感里有一种灰扑扑、无色泽、发了霉的品相，叫人想起的不是古玩铺，倒更像是水手旧货行；这品相甚至浸染到了住户身上，使人觉得整个街区是当作一家“照常营业”的商行，连人带铺一总盘下来的。

===Original===
It was on such a district that I found myself looking down from the top of an omnibus a few days after the inquest (Dr. Cornish’s brougham being at the moment under repairs and his horse “out to grass” during the slack season), being bound for a street in the neighbourhood of Hoxton﻿—Market Street by name﻿—which abutted, as I had noticed when making out my route, on the Regent’s Canal. The said route I had written out, and now, in the intervals of my surveys of the unlovely prospect, I divided my attention between it and the note which had summoned me to these remote regions.
===Chinese===
验尸审讯过后几天，我便坐在公共马车顶上，俯瞰着这样一片街区（康尼什医生的布鲁厄姆马车当时正送修，他那匹马也在淡季里“放出去吃草”）；我此行的目的地，是霍克斯顿一带的一条街——名叫市场街——我在拟路线时便注意到，它一头紧挨着摄政运河。那条路线我已写在纸上；此刻，在对这片不堪入目的景致来回巡视的间歇，我把注意力一分为二：一半给它，一半给那封把我召到这偏远地界来的便条。

===Original===
Concerning the latter I was somewhat curious, for the envelope was addressed, not to Dr. Cornish, but to “Dr. Stephen Gray.” This was really quite an odd circumstance. Either the writer knew me personally or was aware that I was acting as locum tenens for Cornish. But the name﻿—James Morris﻿—was unknown to me, and a careful inspection of the index of the ledger had failed to bring to light anyone answering to the description. So Mr. Morris was presumably a stranger to my principal also. The note, which had been left by hand in the morning, requested me to call “as early in the forenoon as possible,” which seemed to hint at some degree of urgency. Naturally, as a young practitioner, I speculated with interest, not entirely unmingled with anxiety, on the possible nature of the case, and also on the patient’s reasons for selecting a medical attendant whose residence was so inconveniently far away.
===Chinese===
对这封便条我倒有几分好奇，因为信封上写的收信人不是康尼什医生，而是“斯蒂芬·格雷医生”。这实在是一桩相当蹊跷的事。写信人要么与我本人相识，要么知道我在替康尼什代理行医。可是那个署名——詹姆斯·莫里斯（James Morris）——我却陌生得很，把账簿的索引仔细查了一遍，也没查出个对得上号的人来。如此说来，这位莫里斯先生，想必在我的东家那里也是生人。便条是当天早晨差人送来的，请我“上午尽早”过访，这口气似乎透着几分紧急。作为一个年轻的开业医生，我自然怀着兴趣——也不无几分忐忑——揣度这病案可能的性质，也揣度病人为何偏偏挑了一位住得如此不便之远的医生。

===Original===
In accordance with my written route, I got off the omnibus at the corner of Shepherdess Walk, and pursuing that pastoral thoroughfare for some distance, presently plunged into a labyrinth of streets adjoining it and succeeded most effectually in losing myself. However, inquiries addressed to an intelligent fish-vendor elicited a most lucid direction and I soon found myself in a little, drab street which justified its name by giving accommodation to a row of stationary barrows loaded with what looked like the “throw-outs” from a colossal spring clean. Passing along this kerbside market and reflecting (like Diogenes, in similar circumstances) how many things there were in the world that I did not want, I walked slowly up the street looking for Number 23﻿—my patient’s number﻿—and the canal which I had seen on the map. I located them both at the same instant, for Number 23 turned out to be the last house on the opposite side, and a few yards beyond it the street was barred by a low wall, over which, as I looked, the mast of a sailing-barge came into view and slowly crept past. I stepped up to the wall and looked over. Immediately beneath me was the towing-path, alongside which the barge was now bringing up and beginning to lower her mast, apparently in order to pass under a bridge that spanned the canal some two hundred yards farther along.
===Chinese===
按照写好的路线，我在牧羊女步道（Shepherdess Walk）的拐角下了公共马车，沿着那条名字上带着田园气息的大道走了一程，随即一头扎进它旁边的迷宫般的街巷，而且卓有成效地把自己走丢了。不过，向一位精明的鱼贩子问路，得了个再明白不过的指点，我很快便置身于一条灰扑扑的小街上——这条街倒也名副其实：沿街歇着一排固定的摊车，车上堆的东西，活像一场大扫除清出来的弃物。我沿着这马路牙子旁的市场边走边想（处境相仿的第欧根尼（Diogenes）当年想必也这般想过）：世上我不想要的东西何其多也。我缓步沿街而上，寻找 23 号——我病人的门牌——还有我在地图上见过的那条运河。两样我是同一瞬间找到的：23 号原来是对面最后一家，再过去几码，街面便被一堵矮墙拦住；我正望着，一艘帆驳船的桅杆从墙头探出来，又缓缓移了过去。我走到墙前往下一望。脚底下便是纤道，那艘驳船正靠拢过来，开始放倒桅杆，看样子是要从前面约两百码外横跨运河的一座桥底下通过。

===Original===
From these nautical manoeuvres I transferred my attention to my patient’s house﻿—or, at least, so much of it as I could see; for Number 23 appeared to consist of a shop with nothing over it. There was, however, in a wall which extended to the canal wall a side door with a bell and knocker, so I inferred that the house was behind the shop, and that the latter had been built on a formerly existing front garden. The shop itself was somewhat reminiscent of the stalls down the street, for though the fascia was newly painted (with the inscription “J. Morris, General Dealer”) the stock-in-trade exhibited in the window was in the last stage of senile decay. It included, I remember, a cracked Toby jug, a mariner’s sextant of an obsolete type, a Dutch clock without hands, a snuffbox, one or two plaster statuettes, an invalid punch-bowl, a shiny, dark, and inscrutable oil painting and a plaster mask, presumably the death mask of some celebrity whose face was unknown to me.
===Chinese===
看罢这些水上的调度，我把注意力转到我病人的宅子上——或者说，转到我看得见的那部分；因为 23 号看上去只有一间铺面，上头并无房舍。不过，在一条延伸到运河护墙的边墙上，开着一扇装有门铃和门环的侧门，于是我推断正屋在铺子后头，铺子则盖在原先的前花园上。铺子本身倒同街上那排摊车有几分神似：招牌虽是新漆的（上书“J. Morris, General Dealer”，J. 莫里斯，杂货商），橱窗里陈列的营业货色却已老朽到了最后一程。我记得其中有：一只开裂的托比壶（Toby jug）、一架早已过时的水手六分仪、一座没有指针的荷兰钟、一只鼻烟盒、一两尊石膏小像、一只缺残的潘趣酒碗、一幅乌亮幽深、不可索解的油画，还有一副石膏面模——想必是哪位名人的死亡面具，只是那张脸我不认得。

===Original===
My examination of this collection was brought to a sudden end by the apparition of a face above the half-blind of the glazed shop-door; the face of a middle-aged woman who seemed to be inspecting me with malevolent interest. Assuming﻿—rather too late﻿—a brisk, professional manner, I opened the shop door, thereby setting a bell jangling within, and confronted the owner of the face.
===Chinese===
我对这批藏品的检视戛然而止：玻璃店门那半卷布帘的上方，忽然显出一张脸来；那是个中年妇人的脸，正怀着不怀好意的兴味打量我。我摆出一副利落的职业架势——可惜摆得晚了些——推开店门，震得门里一只铃铛一通乱响，与那张脸的主人打了个照面。

===Original===
“I am Dr. Gray,” I began to explain.
===Chinese===
“我是格雷医生，”我开口解释道。

===Original===
“Side door,” she interrupted brusquely. “Ring the bell and knock.”
===Chinese===
“走侧门，”她粗声粗气地打断我。“按铃，再敲门。”

===Original===
I backed out hastily and proceeded to follow the directions, giving a tug at the bell and delivering a flourish on the knocker. The hollow reverberations of the latter almost suggested an empty house, but my vigorous pull at the bell-handle produced no audible result, from which I inferred﻿—wrongly, as afterwards appeared﻿—that it was out of repair. After waiting quite a considerable time, I was about to repeat the performance when I heard sounds within; and then the door was opened, to my surprise, by the identical sour-faced woman whom I had seen in the shop. As her appearance and manner did not invite conversation, and she uttered no word, I followed her in silence through a long passage, or covered way, which ran parallel to the side of the shop and presumably crossed the site of the garden. It ended at a door which opened into the hall proper; a largish square space into which the doors of the ground-floor rooms opened. It contained the main staircase and was closed in at the farther end by a heavy curtain which extended from wall to wall.
===Chinese===
我慌忙退出来，依言照办：拉了拉门铃，又在门环上叩出一串花哨的响声。门环空空洞洞的回响几乎叫人疑心这是座空宅；可我狠狠一拽铃柄，却听不见半点动静，由此我断定铃坏了——后来才知道，这判断错了。等了好一阵子，我正要重演一遍，忽听得屋里有声响；随即门开了，开门的竟就是我在铺子里见过的那个苦脸妇人，不由我一怔。瞧她的模样做派都无意攀谈，她自己也一言不发，我便默不作声地跟着她，穿过一条长廊——或者说带顶的过道——它与铺子的侧面平行，想必是从旧花园的地皮上横穿而过。廊子尽头是一扇门，通向正屋的门厅；那是方方一块不小的空间，底层各房间的房门都开向这里。厅里设有主楼梯，顶头则被一幅厚重的帘子从这面墙到那面墙严严封住。

===Original===
We proceeded in this funereal manner up the stairs to the first floor on the landing of which my conductress halted and for the first time broke the silence.
===Chinese===
我们便以这种送葬般的架势上了楼，到了二楼的楼梯平台，我的引路人站住脚，头一回开了口。

===Original===
“You will probably find Mr. Bendelow asleep or dozing,” she said in a rather gruff voice. “If he is, there is no need for you to disturb him.”
===Chinese===
“你多半会撞见本德洛先生睡着，或是打盹，”她的嗓音相当粗厉。“若是那样，就不必劳你搅醒他了。”

===Original===
“Mr. Bendelow!” I exclaimed. “I understood that his name was Morris.”
===Chinese===
“本德洛先生！”我失声道。“我原当他姓莫里斯呢。”

===Original===
“Well, it isn’t,” she retorted. “It is Bendelow. My name is Morris and so is my husband’s. It was he who wrote to you.”
===Chinese===
“嘿，才不呢，”她抢白道。“他姓本德洛。我姓莫里斯，我丈夫也姓莫里斯。给你写信的正是他。”

===Original===
“By the way,” said I, “how did he know my name? I am acting for Dr. Cornish, you know.”
===Chinese===
“对了，”我说，“他怎么会知道我的名字？要知道，我这是替康尼什医生代理呢。”

===Original===
“I didn’t know,” said she, “and I don’t suppose he did. Probably the servant told him. But it doesn’t matter. Here you are, and you will do as well as another. I was telling you about Mr. Bendelow. He is in a pretty bad way. The specialist whom Mr. Morris took him to﻿—Dr. Artemus Cropper﻿—said he had cancer of the bi-lorus, whatever that is﻿—”
===Chinese===
“这我倒不知道，”她说，“我看他也不知道。多半是佣人告诉他的。不过这不要紧。人来了就好，你跟换谁来都一样使得。我方才正说本德洛先生呢。他病得不轻。莫里斯先生带他去看的那位专家——阿特穆斯·克罗珀（Artemus Cropper）医生——说他得的是‘双幽门’（bi-lorus）癌——管它是个什么——”

===Original===
“Pylorus,” I corrected.
===Chinese===
“是幽门，”我纠正道。

===Original===
“Well, pylorus, then, if you prefer it,” she said impatiently. “At any rate, whatever it is, he’s got cancer of it; and, as I said before, he is in a pretty bad way. Dr. Cropper told us what to do, and we are doing it. He wrote out full directions as to diet﻿—I will show them to you presently﻿—and he said that Mr. Bendelow was to have a dose of morphia if he complained of pain﻿—which he does, of course; and that, as there was no chance of his getting better, it didn’t matter how much morphia he had. The great thing was to keep him out of pain. So we give it to him twice a day﻿—at least, my husband does﻿—and that keeps him fairly comfortable. In fact, he sleeps most of the time, and is probably dozing now; so you are not likely to get much out of him, especially as he is rather hard of hearing even when he is awake. And now you had better come in and have a look at him.”
===Chinese===
“好吧，幽门就幽门，随你怎么叫，”她不耐烦地说。“反正不管那是什么，他那儿是生了癌了；我方才也说了，他病得不轻。克罗珀医生吩咐了该怎么办，我们照办就是。他把饮食注意事项一条条写了出来——回头我拿给你看——还说本德洛先生要是喊痛，就给他一剂吗啡——他自然是喊痛的；又说，反正他没有好转的指望，吗啡用多少都无所谓。要紧的是别让他受痛。所以我们一天给他用两回——至少我丈夫是这么办的——他也就相当舒坦了。实际上，他一天到晚大半在睡，这会儿多半也在打盹；所以你多半问不出他什么话来，何况他即使醒着，耳朵也背得很。好了，你这就进来瞧瞧他吧。”

===Original===
She advanced to the door of a room and opened it softly, and I followed in a somewhat uncomfortable frame of mind. It seemed to me that I had no function but that of a mere figurehead. Dr. Cropper, whom I knew by name as a physician of some reputation, had made the diagnosis and prescribed the treatment, neither of which I, as a mere beginner, would think of contesting. It was an unsatisfactory, even an ignominious, position from which my professional pride revolted. But apparently it had to be accepted.
===Chinese===
她走到一间房的门前，轻轻推开门，我怀着几分不自在的心思跟了进去。依我看，我在这里除了做个幌子，再没有别的职司。克罗珀医生——他的名字我知道，是位颇有声望的内科——诊断已经下了，治法已经定了，这两样，我一个初出茅庐的新手，想也不必想去置喙。这是个叫人丧气、甚至有失体面的位置，我的职业自尊对它大不服气。可是看样子，也只有认了。

===Original===
Mr. Bendelow was a most remarkable-looking man. Probably he had always been somewhat peculiar in appearance; but now the frightful emaciation (which strongly confirmed Cropper’s diagnosis) had so accentuated his original peculiarities that he had the appearance of some dreadful, mirthless caricature. Under the influence of the remorseless disease, every structure which was capable of shrinking had shrunk to the vanishing-point, leaving the unshrinkable skeleton jutting out with a most horrible and grotesque effect. His great hooked nose, which must always have been strikingly prominent, stuck out now, thin and sharp, like the beak of some bird of prey. His heavy, beetling brows, which must always have given to his face a frowning sullenness, now overhung sockets which had shrunk away into mere caverns. His naturally high cheekbones were now not only prominent, but exhibited the details of their structure as one sees them in a dry skull. Altogether, his aspect was at once pitiable and forbidding. Of his age I could form no estimate. He might have been a hundred. The wonder was that he was still alive; that there was yet left in that shrivelled body enough material to enable its mechanism to continue its functions.
===Chinese===
本德洛先生是个模样极为惹眼的人。也许他长相本来就有些奇特；可如今那骇人的枯瘦（这有力地印证了克罗珀的诊断）把他原有的种种奇特衬托得格外分明，使他看上去活脱脱是一幅狰狞而全无笑意的漫画像。在那毫不容情的病魔摆布之下，凡是能缩的组织都缩到了消于无形的地步，只剩下缩不掉的骨架森然凸出，效果煞是可怖而怪诞。他那鹰钩大鼻——想必一向就扎眼地高耸——如今又薄又尖地戳出来，活像某种猛禽的喙。他那两道浓重压目的眉毛——想必一向给他这张脸添上一层蹙额愠怒之色——如今悬在早已深陷成两个窟窿的眼眶之上。他那天生的高颧骨如今不但凸出，而且骨相毕露，一如干枯头骨上所见的模样。总而言之，他的样子既叫人可怜，又叫人不敢亲近。他的年纪我全然无从估摸。说一百岁也有人信。稀奇的是他居然还活着；那具干瘪的躯壳里，竟还剩下足够的本钱，让这台机器继续运转。

===Original===
He was not asleep, but was in that somnolent, lethargic state that is characteristic of the effects of morphia. He took no notice of me when I approached the bed, nor even when I spoke his name somewhat loudly.
===Chinese===
他没有睡着，而是处于吗啡药力特有的那种昏昏沉沉、懵懂倦怠的状态。我走近床边，他不理会我；我略略提高声音唤他的名字，他也不理会。

===Original===
“I told you you wouldn’t get much out of him,” said Mrs. Morris, looking at me with a sort of grim satisfaction. “He doesn’t have a great deal to say to any of us nowadays.”
===Chinese===
“我说过吧，你从他嘴里问不出多少东西，”莫里斯太太说着，带着一种冷冷的得意望着我。“如今他跟我们谁也谈不上几句话。”

===Original===
“Well,” said I, “there is no need to rouse him, but I had better just examine him, if only as a matter of form. I can’t take the case entirely on hearsay.”
===Chinese===
“好吧，”我说，“用不着弄醒他，不过我总还是检视一下的好，哪怕只是走个过场。总不能全凭传闻接手病案。”

===Original===
“I suppose not,” she agreed. “You know best. Do what you think necessary, but don’t disturb him more than you can help.”
===Chinese===
“那倒也是，”她应道。“你最清楚。该做什么就做什么，只是能不惊动他，就尽量别惊动他。”

===Original===
It was not a prolonged examination. The first touch of my fingers on the shrunken abdomen made me aware of the unmistakable hard mass and rendered further exploration needless. There could be no doubt as to the nature of the case or of what the future held in store. It was only a question of time, and a short time at that.
===Chinese===
这不是一次冗长的检查。我的手指头一搭上那瘪陷的腹部，便触到了那确凿无疑的硬块，再探下去已属多余。病案是什么性质、前景如何，都已无可置疑。只是个时间问题，而且是为时不久的问题。

===Original===
The patient submitted to the examination quite passively, but he seemed to be fully aware of what was going on, for he looked at me in a sort of drunken, dreamy fashion, but without any sign of interest in my proceedings. When I had finished, I looked him over again, trying to reconstitute him as he might have been before this deadly disease fastened on him. I observed that he seemed to have a fair crop of hair of a darkish iron-grey. I say “seemed,” because the greater part of his head was covered by a skull cap of black silk; but a fringe of hair straying from under it on to the forehead suggested that he was not bald. His teeth, too, which were rather conspicuous, were natural teeth and in good preservation. In order to verify this fact, I stooped and raised his lip the better to examine them. But at this point Mrs. Morris intervened.
===Chinese===
病人全然被动地任我检查，但他对眼前的事似乎全然心中有数，因为他用一种醉醺醺、恍惚悠忽的神情望着我，只是对我的举动毫无关注的表示。检查完毕，我又把他通身端详了一遍，想把这致命恶疾缠身之前的他，在想象中复原出来。我注意到他似乎有一头相当多的深铁灰色头发。我之所以说“似乎”，是因为他大半个脑袋扣着一顶黑丝小帽；但从帽檐底下漏到额前的一绺头发看来，他并不秃顶。还有他的牙齿，也相当惹眼，那是天生的原装牙，保存得很好。为要核实这一点，我俯下身去，掀起他的嘴唇，好看个仔细。可就在这时，莫里斯太太出来干预了。

===Original===
“There, that will do,” she said impatiently. “You are not a dentist, and his teeth will last as long as he will want them. If you have finished you had better come with me and I will show you Dr. Cropper’s prescriptions. Then you can tell me if you have any further directions to give.”
===Chinese===
“得了，行了，”她不耐烦地说。“你又不是牙医，他那口牙，他要用多久就撑得了多久。你要是查完了，最好跟我来，我把克罗珀医生的处方拿给你看。然后你再告诉我，可还有什么要另行嘱咐的。”

===Original===

Here she opened the flap of a bureau and from one of the little drawers took an open envelope which she handed to me. ===Chinese===

Although I was in complete agreement with the writer, I was a little puzzled by these documents. They were signed “Artemus Cropper, M.D.,” but they were not addressed to any person by name.
