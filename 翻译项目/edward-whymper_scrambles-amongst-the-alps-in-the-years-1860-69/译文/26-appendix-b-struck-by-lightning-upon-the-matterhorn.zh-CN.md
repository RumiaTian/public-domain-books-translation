## B Struck by Lightning Upon the Matterhorn395 / B 马特洪峰上遭雷击395
===Original===

[Mr. R. B. Heathcote, of Chingford, Essex, whilst attempting to ascend the Matterhorn by the southern route, was unfortunately used as a lightning-conductor, when he was within 500 feet of the summit of the mountain. It may be observed that the Matterhorn (like all isolated Alpine rock summits) is frequently struck by lightning. Signor Giordano has pointed out elsewhere that he found numerous traces of electric discharges upon the top of the mountain.]396
“On July 30, 1869, in company with Peter Perrn, Peter Taugwalder junior, and Jos. Maquignaz, I commenced the ascent. The atmosphere was clear, and the wind southerly. When very near to the summit an extremely loud thunderclap was heard, and we thought it prudent to descend. We commenced the descent in the following order:﻿—Taugwalder first, myself next, then Perrn, and Maquignaz last. On approaching the Col de Felicité397 I received a sharp, stinging blow on the leg, and thought, at first, that a stone had been dislodged; but a loud thunderclap at once told me what it was. Perrn also said that he had been hit on the leg. In a few moments I received a hit on the right arm, which seemed to run along it, and resembled a shock from a galvanic battery. At the same time all the men gave a startled shriek, and exclaimed that they were hit by lightning. The storm continued near us for some little time, and then gradually died away. On arriving at the cabane I found that Perrn had a long sore on his arm; next morning his leg was much swollen and very weak. We descended to Breuil on the following day, and crossed to Zermatt. The same day my hand began to swell, and it continued very weak for about a week. Maquignaz’s neck was much swollen on each side; the lightning hitting him (according to his account) on the back, and upon each side of the neck. Taugwalder’s leg was also slightly swollen. The thunder was tremendous﻿—louder than I have ever heard it before. There was no wind, nor rain, and everything was in a mist.”

===Chinese===

【埃塞克斯郡清福德镇的 R·B·希思科特先生，在试沿南线登马特洪峰时，于距峰顶不出 500 英尺处，不幸被充当了一回避雷针。可以一说的是，马特洪峰（如一切孤立的阿尔卑斯岩石峰顶一样）常遭雷击。乔达诺先生已在别处指出，他在峰顶发现过电放电的众多痕迹。】396
「1869 年 7 月 30 日，我偕彼得·佩恩、小彼得·陶格瓦尔德和约斯·马基尼亚开始登攀。大气清澈，风向偏南。离峰顶很近时，一声极其响亮的霹雳炸响，我们认为还是下去稳妥。我们按如下次序下撤：——陶格瓦尔德第一，我其次，然后佩恩，马基尼亚殿后。临近幸福坳397 时，我腿上挨了狠狠一记刺痛，起初以为是有石头砸落；但一声响亮的霹雳立时告诉了我那是什么。佩恩也说腿上挨了一下。片刻之后我右臂挨了一记，那东西像顺着臂跑，如同伽伐尼电池的一击。同时众人都惊叫起来，喊着遭雷击了。雷暴在我们附近盘桓了一小会儿，渐渐消散。到小屋后，我发现佩恩手臂上有一道长长的灼痕；次日早晨他的腿肿得很厉害，使不上力。第二天我们下到布勒伊，又翻山去了采尔马特。同一天我的手开始肿，此后约一个星期都很无力。马基尼亚脖子两侧肿得很厉害；按他自述，雷打在他背上和脖子两侧。陶格瓦尔德的腿也有些肿。雷声惊天动地——比我此生听过的都响。无风，无雨，四外一片雾。」
