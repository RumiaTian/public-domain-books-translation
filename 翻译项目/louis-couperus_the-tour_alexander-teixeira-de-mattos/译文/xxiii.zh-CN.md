## XXIII / 二十三

===Original===
The travellers had returned to Memphis and Caleb displayed the skin of a lion which had been shot in the desert and told the people in the thalamegus terrible tales of desert ghosts and dread visions. The barge was now gliding up the Nile in the night; the sky was softly blue, like dark byssus; the water was a pale blue, like rippling silk; and the waning moon hung above the palm-clusters and country-mansions on the riverbank like a great, overripe fruit which threatened to burst in the sky and whose juice was already trickling in thick orange drops that flowed far over the Nile.

And, while the rowers’ monotonous chant resounded with the regular beat of the oars, Thrasyllus, sitting beside Lucius, gave way to melancholy and said:

“Egypt is Egypt no longer. Alexandria is a commercial town; Memphis is a decaying greatness; and the priests are venal and no longer know the Hermetic wisdom. I have sought for five days among the dusty papyri of the neglected library in the temple of Ammon; it is as though all that is worth knowing were hiding itself.”

“The priests must be hiding the Hermetic wisdom on purpose,” said Lucius.

“They used to do so in other days for Plato and Pythagoras, when their souls were lofty and incorruptible. Nowadays they show what they have and tell what they know for money. But what they have is not more than we in Rome possess in the temple of Isis; and what they know is not the key to happiness. And yet﻿ ﻿… and yet I believe in a sacred word, handed down in the wisdom of the Kabbala by word of mouth, from father to son. But I have not yet received it from any priest, neither at Memphis nor in the oasis. And yet I have hopes. There is Thebes; and there are the secrets of Ethiopia﻿ ﻿… down to the pillars of Sesostris.”

===Chinese===
旅行者们已回到孟斐斯；卡莱布展示了一张在沙漠里射杀的狮子皮，给泰拉美戈斯游船上的人们讲述沙漠鬼魂和可怕幻象的骇人故事。此刻游船正趁夜溯尼罗河而上；天空是柔和的蓝色，像深色的细麻绢（byssus）；河水是淡淡的蓝，像起了涟漪的绸缎；残月悬在河岸的棕榈丛和乡间宅邸上空，像一颗熟透欲裂的巨大果子，仿佛随时要在天穹里迸开，汁液已经化作浓稠的橙色点滴，汩汩流泻在尼罗河上，流得很远很远。

桨手们单调的号子应和着整齐的桨声回荡着；特拉叙卢斯坐在卢基乌斯身旁，任忧郁涌上心头，说道：

“埃及已不再是埃及了。亚历山大里亚是座商业城市；孟斐斯是正在朽坏的伟业；祭司们见钱眼开，不再通晓赫尔墨斯（Hermetic）的智慧。我在阿蒙神庙那座无人照管的藏书室里，在积尘的纸草卷中寻了五天；仿佛一切值得知道的东西都在躲藏着自己。”

“祭司们一定是故意藏起了赫尔墨斯的智慧，”卢基乌斯说。

“从前，当他们的灵魂还高尚不可收买的时候，他们对柏拉图（Plato）和毕达哥拉斯（Pythagoras）就是这样做的。如今他们拿出自己有的东西，说出自己知道的东西，都为换钱。可他们有的，并不比我们罗马伊西斯神庙里的更多；他们知道的，也不是幸福的钥匙。然而……然而我相信一个神圣的词语，它凭卡巴拉（Kabbala）的智慧口口相传，从父亲传给儿子。可我还没有从任何祭司那里得到它，在孟斐斯没有，在绿洲也没有。但我仍存指望。还有底比斯（Thebes）呢；还有埃塞俄比亚（Ethiopia）的秘密……直到塞索斯特里斯石柱。”

===Original===
Lucius smiled gently:

“The word,” he said, “the secret of happiness﻿ ﻿… Thrasyllus, is happiness not an illusion of the brain? Does happiness not lie in resigning one’s self piously to one’s fate and is the secret word not the proud ‘Be a god unto yourself’?”

The old man started. And he whispered:

“You also? Have you also heard that word, as *I* heard it at Sais? I took no account of it, it did not satisfy me.”

“It satisfied me in the oasis, because it is a proud, strong word and I have needed pride and strength﻿ ﻿… since I have *known*, Thrasyllus.”

“Known what, Lucius?”

“That Carus stole Ilia from me.”

===Chinese===
卢基乌斯温文地微笑：

“那个词，”他说，“幸福的秘密……特拉叙卢斯，幸福难道不是头脑的一种幻觉？幸福难道不在于虔诚地把自己交付给命运，而那个秘密的词，不正是那句傲然的『做自己的神』吗？”

老人一震。他低声道：

“你也？你也听到过那个词，像*我*当年在塞易斯（Sais）听到的那样？我没把它放在心上，它没有满足我。”

“在绿洲它满足了我，因为那是一个高傲、有力的词，我需要骄傲和力量……自从我*知道*了以后，特拉叙卢斯。”

“知道什么了，卢基乌斯？”

“知道卡鲁斯从我身边偷走了伊利娅。”

===Original===
The old man started violently:

“You know?” he exclaimed. “You know? *Who* told you? *Who* betrayed the secret?”

“The voice itself within my own soul, which the oracles caused to speak to me. My own thoughts, tossing this way and that, which the oracles guided. From the sibyl of Rhacotis, who merely guessed my own thoughts, down to the old high-priest of Ammon-Râ, who spoke to me like a father﻿ ﻿… and who said to me the word, ‘Be a god unto yourself!’ ”

“As Nemu-Pha said to me, at Sais. I paid for it in gold.”

“I paid for it in gold in the oasis. But what does that matter, Thrasyllus? The word gave me strength and pride.”

“O my son, if you could be cured of your sorrow, of your grief!”

“They are no longer in me. I no longer have any grief, no longer any sorrow. I am a god unto myself.”

===Chinese===
老人猛地一震：

“你知道了？”他失声道，“你知道了？是*谁*告诉你的？是*谁*泄露了这秘密？”

“是我自己灵魂里的那个声音，是神谕使它对我开口。是我自己翻来覆去的思绪，是神谕引导了它们。从拉科提斯的女巫赫罗菲拉（Herophila）——她不过是猜中了我自己的心思——直到阿蒙-拉那位待我如父亲的老大祭司……他对我说出了那个词：『做自己的神！』”

“就像内穆-法（Nemu-Pha）当年在塞易斯对我说的那样。我为它付了黄金。”

“在绿洲我也为它付了黄金。可那有什么要紧，特拉叙卢斯？这个词给了我力量和骄傲。”

“噢我的孩子，但愿你能从你的伤痛、你的悲哀中痊愈！”

“它们已不在我心里了。我不再有悲哀，不再有伤痛。我是自己的神。”

===Original===
“The gods suffer. Isis suffered because of Osiris. All the gods suffer.”

“I suffer no longer. My grief has departed from me. The world and life are beautiful. See, the colours and the light are beautiful. The sky is softly blue, like dark byssus; the water ripples like blue silk; and the moon is like a great, overripe fruit which bursts in the sky and whose juice trickles over the Nile. Tomorrow the day will bring another beauty. In these successive beauties, Thrasyllus, I will be a god unto myself.”

“O my son, though I did not tell you the word myself, I am so happy that you yourself found the word!”

In the night there sounded the high, rising tones of a harp, followed by Cora’s crystal-clear voice, which was accompanied by other harps and other voices.

“The word of pride, the word of strength, Thrasyllus,” said Lucius, calmly; and the old tutor saw a tranquil smile on his young master’s face as he added, “The word that almost makes me happy.”

===Chinese===
“神明们也在受苦。伊西斯为俄西里斯受过苦。所有的神都受苦。”

“我不再受苦了。我的悲哀已离我而去。世界和生活是美的。你看，这色彩和光是美的。天空是柔和的蓝，像深色的细麻绢；河水像蓝绸般泛着涟漪；月亮像一颗熟透的巨大果子，在天穹里迸裂，汁液淌过尼罗河。明天白昼会带来另一种美。在这一连串的美里，特拉叙卢斯，我要做自己的神。”

“噢我的孩子，虽然不是我亲口把这个词告诉你，你能自己找到它，我多么高兴！”

夜色里响起竖琴高亢悠扬的琴音，接着是科拉水晶般清亮的嗓音，别的竖琴和别的歌声伴和着她。

“骄傲之词，力量之词，特拉叙卢斯，”卢基乌斯平静地说；老太傅望着年轻主人脸上安详的微笑，听他接着说，“那个几乎让我幸福的词。”
