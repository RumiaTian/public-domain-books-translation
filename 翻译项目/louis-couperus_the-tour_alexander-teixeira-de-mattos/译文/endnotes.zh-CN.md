## Endnotes / 译注

===Original===
1. These cyamos-leaves were actually used for kitchen-utensils by the people of Alexandria; and their sale provided a regular livelihood. ↩︎
2. Stone wells on the banks of the Nile, in which the water rose and fell as in the river itself; marked columns indicated the maximum, minimum and middle gauge. Inspectors informed the people beforehand how high the Nile would rise and when the stream would be likely to overflow its banks. ↩︎
3. Caleb’s description of Saba owes very little to the author’s invention. Nearly all these details upon Arabia Felix will be found set forth in Strabo’s Geography. ↩︎

===Chinese===
1. 亚历山大里亚的居民确实用这种基阿莫斯树叶（cyamos-leaves）制作厨房用具；贩卖它们足以维持一份稳定的生计。 ↩︎
2. 尼罗河岸上的石井，井中之水如河水本身一样涨落；柱上标有刻度，指示最高、最低与中间水位。监察人员会预先告知民众尼罗河将涨到多高、河水何时可能漫过堤岸。 ↩︎
3. 卡莱布对萨巴的描述几乎全无作者的虚构。关于幸福阿拉伯（Arabia Felix）的这些细节，几乎都可详见斯特拉波（Strabo）的《地理学》。 ↩︎
