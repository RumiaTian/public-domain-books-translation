===Original===
**To**

			*Colonel Henry W. Shoemaker*

			an old and valued friend, who was promised this dedication, with an entirely different novel in mind, twenty-two years ago.

===Chinese===
**献给**

			*亨利·W·舒梅克上校（Colonel Henry W. Shoemaker）*

			一位多年深交、深受敬重的朋友——这页献词早在二十二年前便已许诺于他，只是当时心中所想的，是一部截然不同的小说。
