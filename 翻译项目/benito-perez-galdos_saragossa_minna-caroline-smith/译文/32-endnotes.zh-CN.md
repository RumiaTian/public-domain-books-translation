## Endnotes / 尾注

===Original===
1. Today the Convent of Jerusalem still exists in a restored condition. Its façade is towards the Hall of Independence. The hospital occupied the place where the Hotel de l’Europe stands. The present Palace of Deputies for the Province was constructed on the site of the Convent of San Francisco. ↩︎

===Chinese===
1. 耶路撒冷修道院（Convent of Jerusalem）至今犹存，业经修缮。其正立面朝向独立厅（Hall of Independence）。当年的医院，就设在今日欧罗巴饭店（Hotel de l’Europe）矗立之处。如今的省议会大厦（Palace of Deputies for the Province），则建于圣方济各修道院（Convent of San Francisco）的旧址之上。 ↩︎
