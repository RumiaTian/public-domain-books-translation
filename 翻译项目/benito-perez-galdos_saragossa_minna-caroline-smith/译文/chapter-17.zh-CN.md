## XVII / 第十七章

===Original===
While the cannons on the Mediodía were throwing bombs into the centre of the city, the cannon on the east side were discharging solid balls upon the weak walls of Las Mónicas, and the fortifications of earth and brick of the oil mill, and the battery of Palafox. Very soon the French opened three great breaches, and an attack was imminent. They defended themselves in the Goicoechea mill, which they had taken the day before, after it had been abandoned and fired by us.

Certain of victory, the French ran forward over the plain, having received orders to attack. Our battalion occupied a house in the Calle de Pabostre, whose walls had been spiked along their whole length. Many peasants and various regiments were keeping watch in the Cortina, fiery of courage and with not the least terror before the almost certain likelihood of death, hopeful of being useful in death also, helping to stay the enemy’s advance.

Long hours passed. The French questioned with the artillery to see if they were driving us out of the suburb; the walls were gradually being destroyed; the houses were being shaken down with the dreadful concussion; and the heroic people, few of whom had broken fast even with a bit of bread, were calling from the walls, saying that the enemy was coming. At last, against the right of the breach in the centre advanced strong columns sustained by others in the rear. We saw that the intention of the French was to possess themselves at all hazards of that line of crumbling bricks which some hundreds of madmen were defending, and to take it at any cost. Death-dealing masses were hurled forward, the living columns passing over the dead.

===Chinese===
梅迪奥迪亚的炮群朝着城市中心投掷炸弹的同时，东面的大炮正向拉斯莫尼卡斯单薄的墙体、榨油磨坊（oil mill）的砖土工事以及帕拉福克斯炮台倾泻着实心弹。没过多久，法军便轰开了三处大缺口，强攻已迫在眉睫。他们据守在戈伊科切亚磨坊里；那座磨坊前一日已在我们弃守纵火之后，落入了他们手中。

法军奉命进攻，自以为稳操胜券，跑步越过了平野。我们营占据着帕博斯特雷街上的一座房屋，沿墙从头至尾凿满了枪眼。许多乡民和各个团队的士兵在科蒂纳城墙（Cortina）上守望，个个热血满腔，面对几乎必死无疑的境况竟无丝毫惶恐，还指望死后也能派上用场，帮着遏止敌人的进逼。

漫长的时辰过去了。法军以炮火试探查问，要看我们是否已被逐出城郊；城墙渐渐被摧毁；房屋在骇人的震荡中纷纷坍摇；而英雄的人民——他们当中连拿一块面包充过饥的都寥寥无几——还在城头上呼喊：敌人上来了。终于，一支支强劲的纵队扑向中央缺口的右翼，后面还有纵队层层支撑。我们看得分明，法军打定了主意，不惜一切险阻也要占据那条几百个亡命之徒守卫着的酥裂砖墙，不惜任何代价也要把它拿下。一团团致人死命的人浪被掷向前去，活人的纵队从死人的身上踏过。

===Original===
Let it not be said to make our merit less that the French were not fighting under cover. Neither were we, for none of us could show his head above the broken wall and keep it on. Masses of men dashed against one another, and bayonets were fed with brutal anger upon the bodies of enemies. From the houses came incessant fire. We could see the French fall in heaps, pierced by lead and steel, at the very foot of the ruins they were seeking to conquer. New columns took the places of the first, and in those who came after, brutalities of vengeance were added to prodigies of valor.

On our side the number of those who fell was enormous. The dead were left by dozens upon the earth along that line which had been a wall, but was now no more than a shapeless mass of earth, bricks, and corpses. The natural, the human thing would have been to abandon such positions, and not try to hold them against such a combination of force and military skill. But there was nothing of the human or the natural here. Instead, the power of defence was extended infinitely, to limits not recognized by scientific calculation, beyond ordinary valor. The Aragonese nature stood forth, and it is one which does not know how to be conquered. The living took the places of the dead with a sublime aplomb. Death was an accident, a trivial detail, a thing of which no notice should be taken.

While this was going on, other columns equally powerful were trying to take the Casa de González, which I have before mentioned. But from the neighboring houses, and the towers of the wall, came such a terrible fire of rifles and cannons that they desisted from their attempt. Other attacks took place, with better results for them, at our right, toward the orchard of Camporeal and the batteries of Los Mártires. The immense force displayed by the besiegers along one line of short extent could not fail to produce results. From the house in the Calle de Pabostre, close to the Molino of the city, we were, as I have said, firing upon the besiegers, when behold the batteries of San José, formerly occupied in demolishing the wall, directed their cannons against that ancient edifice. We felt that the walls were trembling; the beams were cracking like the timbers of a ship tossed by a tempest; the wood of the walls was cracking too in a thousand fragments. In short, the place was tumbling down.

===Chinese===
不要为了贬损我们的功劳，就说法军作战没有掩蔽。我们同样没有，因为我们当中谁也无法把脑袋探出残墙之上，还保得住它。一堆堆人马迎头相撞，刺刀怀着兽性的狂怒，在敌人的躯体上饱餐。房屋里射出连绵不断的火力。我们看得见法军成堆地倒下，被铅弹和钢铁洞穿，恰恰倒在他们竭力要征服的废墟脚下。新的纵队顶替了先前的，而在后来者的身上，惊世的英勇之外又添上了复仇的暴虐。

我们这一边，倒下的人数也多得骇人。阵亡者成十成十地陈尸在那条线上——那里原本是一道墙，如今只剩一团不成形状的泥土、砖块和尸骸。顺乎常理、合乎人情，本该弃守这样的阵地，不必妄想凭它抵挡如此武力与兵法的联手。可是这里没有半分常理，也没有半分人情。恰恰相反，防御的力量被无限延展，伸到了兵学计算不肯承认的界限之外，超越了寻常的勇武。阿拉贡人的本性挺立出来了——那是一种不懂得如何被征服的本性。活人接替死人，带着一种崇高的从容。死亡不过是一桩意外，一个琐碎的细节，一件不必放在心上的事。

正当此时，另外几支同样强劲的纵队正试图夺取我先前提到过的冈萨雷斯之家。但从左近的房屋和城墙的塔楼里，步枪与大炮射出那般可怕的火力，他们只得作罢。另外几处进攻发生在我们右翼，朝着坎波雷亚尔果园（Camporeal）和洛斯·马尔蒂雷斯炮台的方向，法军占了上风。围城者把浩大的兵力施展开在一条短窄的战线上，不可能不结出战果。正如我说过的，我们正在帕博斯特雷街紧挨城中莫利诺磨坊的那座房屋里向围城者射击，忽见圣何塞炮台——先前它们专事轰塌城墙——把炮口转向了那座古老的建筑。我们觉出四壁在颤抖；梁木像暴风中颠簸的船骨一样嘎嘎炸响；墙间的木料也喀喇喇碎成千百片。一句话，这地方正在塌下来。

===Original===
“*Cuerno, recuerno!*” exclaimed Uncle Garcés, “what if the house falls down upon us!”

The smoke of the powder prevented us from seeing what was going on without or within.

“To the street! To the street!” cried Pirli, throwing himself out of a window.

“Augustine! Augustine! where art thou?” I called to my friend. But Augustine did not appear. In that moment of alarm, not finding either doorway or ladder to descend, I ran to a window to throw myself out; and the spectacle which met my eyes obliged me to draw back without strength or breath. While the cannons of San José were essaying on the right to bury us in the ruins of the house, and seemed to be accomplishing it without effort, in front of and towards the gardens of San Augustine, the French infantry had succeeded at last in penetrating the breaches, killing those unhappy creatures scarcely to be called men, and finishing those who were already dying, for indeed their desperate agony could not be called life.

===Chinese===
“*Cuerno, recuerno!*（见鬼，真是见鬼！）”加尔塞斯大叔喊道，“这房子要是塌下来压在咱们头上可怎么办！”

火药的硝烟遮蔽了视线，外面也好，屋里也好，正发生着什么一概看不见。

“到街上去！到街上去！”皮尔利喊着，从一扇窗口纵身跳了出去。

“奥古斯丁！奥古斯丁！你在哪儿？”我呼唤着我的朋友。可是奥古斯丁没有露面。在那惊惶的一刻，我找不到下去的门口，也找不到梯子，便奔向一扇窗口要纵身往外跳；然而撞入眼帘的景象，弄得我气力全无、呼吸皆失，只得缩了回来。圣何塞的大炮正在右面设法把我们埋进这座房屋的废墟里，而且似乎不费吹灰之力就要办到；而在正面、朝圣奥古斯丁园子的方向，法军步兵终于突进了缺口，杀戮那些简直已算不上人的不幸生灵，又把那些行将咽气的人一一了结——他们那绝望的垂死挣扎，实在也谈不上是活着了。

===Original===
From the neighboring alleys came a horrible fire. The cannons of the Calle de Diezma took the place of those of the conquered battery. But the breach taken, the French were securing themselves on the walls. It was impossible for me to feel in my soul a spark of energy on beholding such stupendous disaster.

I fled from the window, terrified﻿—beside myself. A piece of the wall cracked and fell in enormous fragments, and a square window took the shape of an isosceles triangle; through a corner of the roof I could see the sky. Bits of lime and splinters struck me in the face. I ran further in, following others, who were saying, “This way! this way!”

“Augustine! Augustine!” I called again. At last I saw him among those who were running from one room to another, going up a ladder which led to a garret.

===Chinese===
邻近的小巷里射出可怕的火力。迭斯马街上的大炮接替了失守炮台上的炮火。但缺口既已陷落，法军便在城墙上站稳了脚跟。眼见这般惊天动地的惨祸，我心中再难鼓起一丝元气。

我从窗口逃开，吓得魂飞魄散——整个人失了方寸。一块墙体开裂，巨大的碎块纷纷坠落，一扇方窗变成了等腰三角形的口子；从屋顶的一角望得见天空。灰泥碎屑和木片打在我脸上。我跟着别人往里跑，他们喊着：“这边！这边！”

“奥古斯丁！奥古斯丁！”我又喊。终于，我看见他在那群从一间屋子奔到另一间屋子的人当中，正攀上通向阁楼的一道梯子。

===Original===
“Are you alive?” I asked him.

“I do not know; it is not important,” he answered.

In the garret we broke through a partition wall, and passing into another room, we found an outside staircase. We descended and came to another house. Some soldiers followed, looking for a place to get into the street, and others remained there. The picture of that poor little room is indelibly fixed in my memory, with all its lines and colors, and flooded with plentiful light from a large window, opened upon the street. Portraits of the Virgin and of the saints covered the uneven walls. Two or three old trunks covered with goatskin stood on one side. On the other side we saw a woman’s clothes hanging upon hooks and nails, and a very high but poor-looking bed, although the sheets were fresh. In the window were three large flowerpots with plants in them. Sheltered behind them were two women firing furiously upon the French who occupied the breach. They had two guns. One was charging, the other firing. The one who was firing had been stooping to aim from behind the flowerpots. Resting the trigger a minute, she lifted her head a little to look at the field of battle.

“不知道；这不打紧，”他答道。

在阁楼里，我们打通一堵隔墙，进到另一个房间，寻着一座露天楼梯。我们拾级而下，进到另一座房屋。有些士兵跟了下来，寻找通向街上的出路，另一些人则留在了那里。那间寒酸小屋的景象，连同它全部的线条与色彩，不可磨灭地烙在我的记忆里：一扇临街的大窗，把充盈的天光泻满全屋。凹凸不平的墙上挂满圣母与圣徒的画像。一侧立着两三口蒙着山羊皮的旧箱子。另一侧，我们看见女人的衣裳挂在挂钩和钉子上，还有一张很高却显得寒碜的床，虽然床单是新浆换的。窗台上摆着三只大花盆，盆里种着花木。掩在花盆后面的，是两个正朝占据缺口的法军猛烈开火的女人。她们有两支枪：一支装弹，一支施放。开枪的那一个一直俯身在花盆后面瞄准。她把手指歇离扳机片刻，微微抬起头来，望了一眼战场的情形。

===Original===
“Manuela Sancho,” I exclaimed, placing my hand upon the head of the heroic girl, “resistance is no longer of any use. The next house is already destroyed by the batteries of San José, and the balls are already beginning to fall upon the roof of this. Let us go.”

She took no notice, and went on shooting. At last the house, which was even less able than its neighbor to sustain the shock of the projectiles, quivered as if the earth trembled beneath its foundations. Manuela Sancho threw down her gun. She and the woman who was with her ran into an alcove, where I heard them crying bitterly. Entering, we found the two girls embracing an old crippled woman who was trying to get up from her bed.

“Mother, it is nothing,” Manuela said soothingly, covering her with whatever came first to her hand; “we are only going into the street because it seems as if the house is going to fall down.”

The old woman did not speak. She could not speak. The two girls had taken her in their arms; but we took her in ours, charging them to bring our guns and whatever clothing they could save. We passed out into a court which opened into another street where the fire had not yet reached.

“玛努埃拉·桑乔，”我把手按在这位英勇姑娘的头上，喊道，“抵抗已经没有用处了。隔壁的房屋已经被圣何塞的炮台摧毁，炮弹已经开始落到这一座的屋顶上了。走吧。”

她毫不理会，只顾继续射击。终于，这座比左邻更经不住炮弹震荡的房屋颤抖起来，仿佛地基底下的大地也在打颤。玛努埃拉·桑乔撂下了枪。她同与她作伴的那个女人奔进一间内室，我听见她们在里面失声痛哭。我们走进去，只见两个姑娘正搀着一位残废的老妇人，老人挣扎着想从床上起来。

“妈妈，没什么，”玛努埃拉柔声安抚着，随手抓起什么就往她身上盖，“我们不过是到街上去，因为这房子眼看就要塌了。”

老妇人没有说话。她说不出话来。两个姑娘已把她抱在怀里；但我们把她接了过来，嘱咐她们带上我们的枪，还有凡能抢出的衣物。我们出来走进一座小院，院子通向另一条街，炮火还没有波及那里。
