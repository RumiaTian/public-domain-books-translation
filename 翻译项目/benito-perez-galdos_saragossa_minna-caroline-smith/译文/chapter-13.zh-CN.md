## XIII / 第十三章

===Original===
When we had finished carrying out the flour, I went and looked for Augustine; but I could find him nowhere, neither in his father’s house, nor at the headquarters of supplies, nor in the Coso, nor in Santa Engracia. At nightfall I found him in the powder mill near San Juan de los Panetes. I have forgotten to say that the Saragossans had improvised a work shop where were turned out daily nine or ten quintals of powder. I saw Augustine helping the workmen put into sacks and barrels the powder made during the day. He was working with feverish activity.

“Do you see this enormous heap of powder?” he said to me when I approached him. “Do you see those sacks and those barrels all full of the same material? Well, Gabriel, it seems to me very little.”

“I don’t know what you are trying to say.”

===Chinese===
我们把那批面粉搬运完毕之后，我便去找奥古斯丁；可哪儿也找不见他——他父亲的宅子里没有，补给总部没有，科索大街没有，圣恩格拉西亚也没有。挨到暮色四合，我才在圣胡安-德洛斯帕内塔斯近旁的火药磨坊（powder mill）里找到他。我忘了交代：萨拉戈萨人曾临时赶起一座工场，每天能制出九到十公担（quintal）火药。我看见奥古斯丁正帮着工人们把当日制成的火药装进麻袋和木桶。他干得狂热起劲。

我走近他时，他对我说道：“你瞧这一大堆火药？你瞧那些麻袋、那些木桶，装的全是同样的东西？可是加夫列尔，在我看来还是太少了。”

“我不明白你想说什么。”

===Original===
“I say that if this immense quantity of powder were as big as Saragossa I should like it still better. Yes, and in such a case I should like to be the only inhabitant of this great city. What a pleasure! Listen, Gabriel! If it were so, I would myself set fire to it, and fly into the clouds, torn to pieces in the horrible explosion like pieces of rock which a volcano throws to a distance of a hundred leagues. I would be hurled to the fifth heaven, and of my members, scattered everywhere, there would be no memory! Death, Gabriel, death is what I desire! But I desire a death﻿—I do not explain it to you. My desperation is so great that to die of a gunshot or a sabre-thrust would not satisfy me. I long to be rent asunder, and diffused through space in a thousand burning particles. I pant to feel myself in the bosom of a flame-bearing cloud. My spirit yearns, if only for an infinitesimal instant, the delight of seeing this wretched body reduced to powder. Gabriel, I am desperate. Do you see this powder? Imagine within my breast all the flames which this could make. Did you see her when she went out to get her father? Did you see her when she threw the money? I was in a corner where I could see it all. Mariquilla does not know that that man who maltreated her father is my own! Did you see how the boys threw mud at poor Candiola? I realize that Candiola is a wretch. But she, what fault has she? She and I, what fault have we? None, Gabriel. My heart is broken, and thirsts for a thousand deaths. I cannot live. I will run into the place of greatest danger and fling myself into the fire of the French. After what I have seen today, I and the earth on which I dwell may not be together.”

===Chinese===
“我是说，倘若这一大堆火药能有萨拉戈萨城那么大，我倒更要称心几分。是啊，真到那个地步，我就巴望做这座大城里唯一的居民了。那才叫快意！你听着，加夫列尔！果真那样，我就亲手点火烧了它，在那骇人的爆炸中粉身碎骨、飞进云霄，就像火山把一块块岩石抛到百里格开外。我会被掷上第五重天，我的肢体撒遍四方，从此无人记得！死，加夫列尔，我渴求的就是死！可我渴求的那种死——我跟你说不明白。我绝望到了这步田地，一枪毙命、一刀洞穿，都满足不了我。我渴望被撕成碎片，化作千万粒燃烧的微尘弥散到太空里去。我喘息着渴望感到自己置身于一座驮着烈焰的云的怀抱。哪怕只有无穷小的一瞬，我的灵魂也渴念着要看一看这具可悲的躯壳化为齑粉的快意。加夫列尔，我绝望透了。你瞧见这火药了？想象一下它所能生出的全部烈焰，都装在我的胸膛里。她出去接她父亲的时候，你瞧见了吗？她把钱掷出去的时候，你瞧见了吗？我当时正缩在一个角落里，什么都瞧在眼里。玛丽基亚不知道，那个虐待她父亲的人，正是我的父亲！你瞧见那帮顽童朝可怜的坎迪奥拉扔泥巴了吗？我知道坎迪奥拉是个恶棍。可她有什么罪？她和我，我们有什么罪？没有，加夫列尔。我的心已经碎了，一千个死也解不了渴。我活不下去了。我要奔到最危险的地方去，一头扎进法国人的炮火里。今天亲眼见了这一切之后，我与我栖身的这片大地，再不能相容了。”

===Original===
I drew him away from the place, taking him to the walls; and we went to work on the fortifications which were being made in Las Tenerías, the weakest point in the city since the destruction of San José and Santa Engracia. I have already said that from the mouth of the Huerva to San José stretched a line of fifty mouths of fire. Against this formidable line of attack what avail was our fortified circuit?

The quarter of Las Tenerías extended from the eastern part of the city, between the Huerva and the old part of the town, perfectly outlined yet by the wide road which is called the Coso. It was at the beginning of this century a village of mean houses, almost all inhabited by laborers and artisans, and the religious houses there had none of the splendor of the monuments of Saragossa. The general plan of this district is approximately the segment of a circle whose arc curves out to the open country, and whose chord unites it to the city, from the Puerta Quemada to the rise at the Sepulcro.

From that line to the circumference ran several streets, some of them broken, like the Calles de la Diezma, Barrio Verde, de los Clavos, and de Pabostre. Some of these were marked not by rows of houses, but by walls, and lacking sometimes one thing and sometimes another. The streets spread out into formless little squares or yards or barren gardens. I describe badly because in the days I refer to the heaps of ruins left by the first siege had been used to mount batteries and raise barricades in points where the houses did not offer a natural defence.

===Chinese===
我把他从那地方拉走，带他到城墙边去；我们随即投入了特内里亚斯的筑城劳作——自从圣何塞与圣恩格拉西亚被毁之后，那里成了全城最薄弱的一点。我已经说过，从韦尔瓦河口到圣何塞，横亘着一条五十个喷火炮口的战线。面对这条威势汹汹的攻击线，我们那圈环城工事又济得什么事？

特内里亚斯区坐落在城东，夹在韦尔瓦河与老城之间，至今仍被那条唤作科索的宽阔大道清清楚楚地勾出轮廓。本世纪初叶，这里还是一片寒酸屋舍凑成的村落，居民几乎全是劳工与匠人；区里的修道院房舍，也没有半点萨拉戈萨煌煌建筑群的气象。全区的总体格局近似一块弓形地带：弓背向外拱向旷野，弓弦则把它连上城区——从克马达门直到圣墓（Sepulcro）那一带的坡岗。

从那条弓弦到圆周之间，穿行着好几条街巷，有的已破断残缺，如迭斯马街（Calle de la Diezma）、巴里奥贝尔德（Barrio Verde）、克拉沃斯街（Calle de los Clavos）和帕博斯特雷街（Calle de Pabostre）。其中有些地段，界定街道的不是成行的房屋，而是一堵堵院墙，缺这少那，不成体统。街道漫散开去，汇成一块块不成形状的小方场、院落或荒园。我描写得很拙劣，因为在我所说的那些日子里，第一次围城留下的一堆堆废墟，早已被用来设置炮位、垒筑街垒，填在那些房屋不足以天然御敌的地段上了。

===Original===
Near the fortification of the Ebro were some remnants of an ancient wall, with various little towers of masonry which some persons supposed to be from the hands of the Romans, and others judged to be the work of the Moors. In my time﻿—I do not know how it may be now﻿—these pieces of wall seemed to be mortised into the houses, or rather the houses were mortised into them, appearing like props and corners of that ancient work, blackened but not crumbled by the passing of so many centuries. The new had been built in a confused way upon the ruins of the old, as the Spanish people had developed and grown upon the spoils of other peoples of mixed bloods, until they became as they are today.

The general aspect of the district of Las Tenerías brought to the imagination pleasant fancies of all that had taken place during Moorish rule, the abundance of brick, the long gable ends, the irregular fronts, the window lattices with shutters, the complete architectural anarchy, making it impossible to know where one house ended and another began, or of distinguishing whether this had two floors or three, or if that roof served to support the walls of that one over there. The streets at best ended in yards with no ways out. The archways, which gave entrance to a little square, reminded me that here was a vista upon another Spanish people, very different from those now here.

This amalgamation of houses which I have described to you, this suburb built up by many generations of laborers and peasants and tanners, according to the caprice of each, without order or harmony, had prepared itself for the defence on the twenty-fourth and twenty-fifth days of January, at the time when the French began to display their pomp of attack by placing forces on that side. All the families living in the houses of this suburb proceeded to build works according to their own strategic instincts. There were military engineers in petticoats who demonstrated a profound knowledge of war by walling up certain spaces and opening others to the light, and for purposes of firing. The walls of the eastern side were spiked along their length. The turrets of the wall of Caesar Augustus, built to resist arrows and sling stones, now upheld cannon.

===Chinese===
埃布罗河工事近旁，残存着一段古墙，连着几座砖石砌的小塔楼；有人推想出自罗马人之手，也有人断为摩尔人的营造。在我那个年代——如今如何，我不得而知——这几截墙体像是榫接进了两侧的房屋，或者不如说，是房屋榫接进了墙体，看上去恰似那古老工程的支木与墙角，历经数百年而熏黑未酥。新的东西杂乱无章地压盖在旧的废墟上营造起来，正如西班牙这个民族在其他混血诸族的劫余之上发育滋长，终至今天的模样。

特内里亚斯区的总貌，引人悠然想见摩尔人治下的一切旧事：满目砖房，长长的山墙端壁，歪斜不整的临街立面，带木窗板的格子窗，十足的建筑无政府状态——一幢房子在哪里收尾、另一幢从哪里起头，无从分辨；也辨不清这一幢是两层还是三层，那一片屋顶是不是在替远处那一堵墙受力。街道走到尽头，至多通进一座无路可出的院子。那些通向小方场的拱门令我想起：此地曾展现出另一个西班牙民族的图景，与如今居于此地的民族，已判然两样。

我向你们描绘的这片犬牙交错的屋舍，这片由一代又一代劳工、农夫和制革匠各凭一时兴致搭盖起来、毫无章法与谐调的城郊，在一月二十四日、二十五日——正当法军把兵力压向这一侧、开始摆开它堂堂攻势的时候——已把自己整备停当，投入防御。住在这片城郊屋舍里的人家，全都照着各自的战略本能动手修筑工事。这里还有穿裙子的军事工程师，她们封死某些口子，又为采光和开火另辟一些洞口，显出精深的兵法学识。东侧的墙体沿全线插满了尖桩。凯撒·奥古斯都（Caesar Augustus）的城墙上，那些当年为抵御箭矢与投石而筑的小塔楼，如今扛起的是大炮。

===Original===
If any one of these pieces were turned upon one of the neighboring roofs, the roof or the entire house, whatever was there, would be immediately blown to pieces. Many passages had been obstructed, and two of the religious edifices of the suburb, San Augustine and Las Mónicas, were veritable fortresses. The wall had been rebuilt and strengthened; the batteries had been joined together, and our engineers had calculated the positions and the reach of the enemy’s guns very well, in order to accommodate our defences to them.

Our line had two advanced points, the mill of Goicoechea and a house which, because it belonged to a certain Don Victoriano González, has gone into history by the name of the Casa de González. This line, running from the Puerta Quemada, met first the battery of Palafox, then the Molino, the mill, in the city, then the garden of San Augustine; it continued to the mill of Goicoechea, situated a little out of the district, then to the orchard of Las Mónicas, and on to those of San Augustine; further up, a great battery and the house of González. This is all that I remember of Las Tenerías. There was over there a place called the Sepulcro, because of its nearness to a church of that name. More than one portion of the suburb, indeed, deserved the name of sepulchre. I tell you no more in order not to tire you with these descriptive minutiae, unnecessary to one who knows those glorious places, and insufficient for one who has been unable to visit them.

这些炮位里只要有一门调转炮口，对准左近哪片屋顶，那屋顶、或整幢房子连同其中的一切，顷刻便会被轰得粉碎。许多通道已经堵死，区内两座宗教建筑——圣奥古斯丁（San Augustine）与拉斯莫尼卡斯（Las Mónicas）——俨然是两座真正的堡垒。城墙重新砌起、加固了；各座炮台连成了一气，我们的工程师把敌炮的位置与射程估算得头头是道，好叫我们的防御处处对着它们布设。

我方防线有两个前出据点：一是戈伊科切亚磨坊（mill of Goicoechea），二是一座宅子——因宅主是一位唐·维克托里亚诺·冈萨雷斯（Don Victoriano González），它便以“冈萨雷斯之家”（Casa de González）之名载入了史册。这条防线自克马达门起，先经帕拉福克斯炮台，接着是城内的莫利诺（Molino）磨坊，再到圣奥古斯丁的园子；续而延至略出区界的戈伊科切亚磨坊，接拉斯莫尼卡斯的果园，再接圣奥古斯丁的果园；再往上，是一座大炮台和冈萨雷斯之家。关于特内里亚斯，我记得的就这么些了。那边还有一处地方唤作“圣墓”，因毗邻一座同名教堂而得名。说实在的，这城郊不止一处地方，当得起“坟墓”这个名目。我不再多讲了，免得这些描摹的细枝末节把你们讲乏了——对认得那些光荣之地的人，这些用不着；对无缘踏访的人，这些又不够。
