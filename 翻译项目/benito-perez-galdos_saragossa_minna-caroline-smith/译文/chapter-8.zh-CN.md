## VIII / 第八章

===Original===

The following day, the twenty-second, Palafox said to the messenger who came under a flag of truce from Moncey to propose terms of surrender.

“*I do not know how to surrender. After death, we will talk about that.*”

He followed this with a long and eloquent article which was published in the *Gazette*; but, according to general opinion, neither that document nor any of the proclamations which appeared with the signature of the commanding general were his own composition, but that of his friend, Basilio Boggiero, a man of great judgment, who was often seen in situations of danger, in the company of patriots and military leaders.

===Chinese===

次日，二十二日，帕拉福克斯对那位打着休战旗、自蒙塞军中前来商洽投降条款的信使说道：

“*我不会投降。死了以后，再来谈这件事。*”

随后他又写就一篇文辞雄辩的长文，发表在《公报》（*Gazette*）上；但依照众人的看法，无论是那篇文书，还是任何一件以总司令署名发布的公告，都并非出自他本人的手笔，而是出自他的朋友巴西利奥·博希耶罗——此人极有识见，人们常能在险境之中见到他，与爱国志士和军中将领们相伴。

===Original===

It is excusable to say that the army of the defence was very much inspired by the glorious action of the twenty-first. It was necessary to give expression to this ardor, to arrange a sortie, and so in effect it was done; but it happened that all wished to take part in this at the same time, and it was necessary to bury the dead. The sorties, arranged with prudence, were expedient; because the French, extending their lines around the city, were preparing for a regular siege, and had begun upon their outer works.

The district of Saragossa contained many people, which seemed to the common mind a great advantage, but which seemed to the intelligent a great danger, because of the immense destruction of human life which hunger would quickly bring﻿—hunger, that terrible general who is always the conqueror of overcrowded besieged cities. Because of this excess of people, the sorties were timely.

Renovales made one on the twenty-fourth with the troops of the fortress of San José, and cut down an olive grove which hid the works of the enemy.

Don John O’Neill made a sally from the suburb on the twenty-fifth with the volunteers of Aragón and Huesca, taking the chance of advantage from the enemy’s lack of preparation, and killing many of the enemy’s men.

===Chinese===

守城部队深受二十一日那场光荣战斗的鼓舞，这样说是不会错的。这股热忱须得有所抒发，须得安排一场出击，事实上也照办了；不巧的是人人都想同时上阵，而阵亡者还等着安葬。几番出击都安排得谨慎，倒也得当；因为法军正把战线绕着城池四面铺展，准备一场正规的围攻，外围工事已经破土动工了。

萨拉戈萨城区人烟稠密，在寻常人眼里这仿佛是天大的优势，在有识者看来却潜伏着莫大的危险，因为饥饿很快就要带来生命的大批毁灭——饥饿，那位可怕的将军，从来都是人满为患的围城之城的征服者。也正因为人口过剩，这几场出击来得正是时候。

二十四日，雷诺瓦莱斯率圣何塞要塞的部队出城打了一仗，砍倒了一片遮蔽敌军工事的橄榄树林。

二十五日，唐·约翰·奥尼尔（Don John O’Neill）率阿拉贡义勇军和韦斯卡义勇军自城郊出击，趁敌军疏于戒备之机大占便宜，毙敌甚众。

===Original===

On the thirty-first was made the most telling sally of all, striking in two distinct places and with considerable forces. During the early part of the day we had divided to perfection the works of the first French parallel, thrown up about three hundred and twenty yards from the walls. They were working actively, not resting by night, and we could see that they had signals of colored lanterns along the whole line. From time to time we discharged our guns, but we caused very little destruction. If troops were especially needed for a reconnoissance, they were despatched in less than no time.

The morning of the thirty-first arrived, and my battalion was charged to be ready to march upon orders from Renovales to attack the enemy in their centre, from the Torrero to the Muela road, while General Butron did the same by the Bernardona, that is to say, by the French left, sallying with sufficient forces of infantry and cavalry by the gates of Sancho and Portillo.

In order to distract the attention of the French, the general commanded that a battalion should be divided into skirmishing parties by the Tenerías, calling the attention of the enemy in that direction. In the meantime, with some of the soldiers of Olivenza and part of those of Valencia, we advanced by the Madrid road straight towards the French lines. The skirmishing parties were on both sides of the road when the enemy became aware of our presence, and now we were quicker than deer in doing up the first troop of French infantry which came to meet us. Behind a half-ruined country house some fortifications had been thrown up, and they began firing with good aim and much slaughter. For an instant we remained undecided, then some twenty men of us flanked the country house, while the rest followed the high road, pursuing the fugitives; but Renovales dashed forward and led us on, cutting down and bayoneting those who were defending the house. At the moment when we set foot within the first defence I noticed that my rank was thinned out. I saw some of my companions fall, breathing their last sighs. I looked to my right, fearing not to find my beloved friend among the living; but God had preserved him. Montoria and I were unharmed.

===Chinese===

三十一日那天，打得最见成效的一场出击开始了，兵分两处，兵力可观。当日早间，我们已用炮火把法军第一道平行壕的工事切得七零八落——那道壕线就筑在距城墙约三百二十码的地方。法军干得起劲，夜里也不歇工，我们看得出他们沿整条战线都挂起了彩色灯笼作信号。我们不时开炮轰击，收效却甚微。一旦特别需要部队前去侦察，他们转眼之间便能派出。

三十一日的早晨到了，我营奉命整装待发，一得雷诺瓦莱斯号令，便直捣敌军中路，从托雷罗直到穆埃拉大道；与此同时，布特龙将军（Butron）在贝尔纳多纳大道，也就是法军左翼，采取同样的行动，率足够的步骑兵自圣乔门与波蒂略门出城。

为牵制法军的注意，将军下令将一个营分成几队散兵，开到特内里亚斯（Tenerías）一带，把敌军的注意力引向那个方向。与此同时，我们带着一部分奥利文萨（Olivenza）的士兵和一部分瓦伦西亚的士兵，沿马德里大道径直朝法军战线推进。散兵队散在大道两侧，待敌军察觉我们的行踪，我们已比奔鹿还快，把迎面而来的头一队法国步兵收拾掉了。在一座半塌的乡舍后面，敌军仓促垒起了几处工事，开始放枪，打得又准又狠。我们迟疑了片刻，随后我们中间有二十来人从侧翼包抄那座乡舍，其余的人沿大道追击溃兵；雷诺瓦莱斯却向前猛冲，率领我们砍的砍、刺刀捅的捅，把守屋之敌一一了结。就在我们踏进第一道防线的那一刻，我发觉自己这一排的队伍稀落了。我看见几个同伴倒下去，咽了最后一口气。我朝右边望去，生怕在活着的人当中找不到我亲爱的朋友；但上帝保住了他。我和蒙特里亚都毫发无伤。

===Original===

We could not spend much time in communicating to each other the satisfaction that we felt at finding ourselves still alive, because Renovales gave orders to follow on, in the direction of the line of entrenchments that the French were raising. We abandoned the high road and made a deflection, turning to the right with the intention of joining the volunteers of Huesca, who were attacking by the Muela road.

It may be understood by what I have related that the French did not expect that sortie, and that, taken completely unawares, they were holding there, besides the scanty force that kept the works, the engineers occupied in digging the trenches of the first parallel. We attacked them vigorously, turning upon them a murderous fire, improving the minutes well before the dreaded reinforcements should arrive. We took prisoners those whom we met without arms; we shot those who had them. We took the picks and spades﻿—all this with unequalled energy, animating one another with fiery words, exalted above all by the thought that they were watching us from the city.

In this attack we were fortunate, for while we were destroying those at work on the entrenchments, the troops who had made the sortie on the left were carrying on a successful struggle with the detachments which the enemy had in the Bernardona. While the volunteers of Huesca, the grenadiers of Palafox, and the Walloon guards defeated the French infantry, the squadron of Numancia and Olivenza cavalry cautiously emerged through the Puerta de Sancho, and making a wide detour occupied the Alagón road on one side, and the Muela on the other, exactly when the French drew back from the left to the centre, in need of greater auxiliary forces. Finding themselves in their element, our fiery cavalry sprang forward, destroying whatever was encountered in the way, and then the disgraced infantry, who were fleeing towards Torrero, fell, and were trampled underfoot.

===Chinese===

彼此发现都还活着的这份欢喜，我们来不及细诉，因为雷诺瓦莱斯已下令继续前进，直扑法军正在挖掘的堑壕线。我们离开大道，折向右方，打算去同正沿穆埃拉大道进攻的韦斯卡义勇军会合。

从我上面讲述的情形可以明白，法军不曾料到这次出击；他们被杀了个措手不及，那里除看守工事的少量兵力之外，还有正在挖掘第一道平行壕的工兵。我们向他们猛扑过去，泼以一场杀气腾腾的火力，趁着那令人生畏的援军未到，把分分秒秒都用到了十足。遇上手无寸铁的，我们俘虏；手里持枪的，我们枪毙。我们夺下鹤嘴锄和铁铲——这一切都干得劲头无双，彼此用火热的言语互相打气，而最令我们意气飞扬的，是想到城里的人们正望着我们。

这一仗我们打得顺当：我们在这边歼灭掘壕之敌，从左翼出击的部队也在同敌军置于贝尔纳多纳大道的几支分队较量中占了上风。当韦斯卡义勇军、帕拉福克斯的掷弹兵和瓦隆卫队（Walloon guards）击溃法军步兵之际，努曼西亚与奥利文萨的骑兵中队悄然出了圣乔门，兜一个大圈，一面占住阿拉贡（Alagón）大道，一面占住穆埃拉大道——恰恰赶在法军因急需更多援兵、从左翼向中路收缩的当口。我们的骑兵如鱼得水，奋蹄前冲，路上撞见什么便摧毁什么；那些丧尽了体面的步兵朝托雷罗方向逃窜，纷纷倒毙，被踏在马蹄之下。

===Original===

In their dispersion, many fell beneath our bayonets, and if their desire to flee from the horses was great, great also was our anxiety to receive them in manner worthy of our swords. Some ran, throwing themselves into the trenches, not being able to jump over them; others surrendered at discretion, throwing down their arms; some defended themselves with heroism, permitting themselves to be slain before giving up; and at the last there failed not a few who, shutting themselves up in the brick kiln filled up with boughs and timber, set fire to it, preferring to die by roasting, rather than be taken prisoners.

All this which I have related in detail passed in a very short time, while the French commander, having seen enough in this hour, detached sufficient forces to hold back and punish our too audacious expedition. They beat the drum in Monte Torrero, and we saw a great force of cavalry coming against us; but we who were with Renovales had had our desire, the same as those with Butron, and were not obliged to wait for those horsemen who arrived at the end of the action; so we retired, giving them from a distance a “Good day” of the most sharp and pointed phrases in our vocabulary. We still had time to make useless some pieces placed ready for employment on the following day. We took a multitude of tools and spades, and we destroyed in all haste whatever we could of their entrenchments without losing hold upon the dozens of prisoners, of which we had taken up a collection.

Juan Pirli, one of our companions in the battalion, was carrying home to Saragossa the steel helmet of an engineer for the admiration of the public, and also a frying-pan in which were still the remnants of a breakfast begun in camp before Saragossa and ended in the other world.

===Chinese===

溃散之中，许多人倒在我们的刺刀下；他们想躲开马匹的念头有多急，我们想以配得上手中刀剑的方式“接待”他们的心愿就有多切。有的奔逃时跳不过堑壕，一头栽了进去；有的掷下武器，无条件投降；有的拼死抵抗，宁可被杀也不肯放手；到头来也不乏那样一些人，把自己关进那座堆满枝条木料的砖窑，放起火来，宁可被活活烤死，也不愿被俘。

我如此详述的这一切，都在极短的工夫里过去了；与此同时，法军指挥官在这一个钟头里已经看够了，分出足够的兵力来阻截并惩办我们这支过于大胆的远征。托雷罗山上鼓声大作，我们望见一支强大的骑兵朝我们开来；但追随雷诺瓦莱斯的我们已称了心，布特龙那边也是一样，用不着再等那些赶在战事尾声才到的骑兵；于是我们后撤，隔着老远，用我们词汇里最尖酸扎人的字句，送了他们一声“日安”。我们还来得及毁掉几门已经架好、备作次日之用的大炮，掳走大批工具铁铲，又火速捣毁了他们堑壕中一切来得及捣毁的东西，手里始终没放掉收罗来的那几十名俘虏。

营里我们的一个伙伴胡安·皮尔利（Juan Pirli），正把一顶工兵的钢盔带回萨拉戈萨给公众开眼，还捎着一口平底锅，锅里还留着些早餐的残迹——那顿早餐在萨拉戈萨城前的营地里开的头，到另一个世界里才收的尾。

We had had nine killed and eight wounded in our battalion. When Augustine rejoined me near the Carmen gate, I noticed that one of his hands was stained with blood.

“Are you wounded?” I asked, examining the hand. “It is nothing more than a scratch.”

“It is a scratch,” he replied; “but it was not made by ball, lance, nor sabre, but by teeth, because when I gripped that Frenchman who lifted up his pick to brain me, the damned fellow set his teeth into my hand like a dog at bay.”

===Chinese===

我们营里阵亡九人，负伤八人。奥古斯丁在卡门城门附近同我会合时，我看见他一只手上沾着血。

“你受伤了？”我一面察看那只手，一面问道。“不过是擦破点儿皮。”

“是擦破点儿皮，”他答道，“可它不是枪弹、长矛也不是马刀留下的，是牙齿咬的——我揪住那个举起鹤嘴锄要砸碎我脑袋的法国佬时，那该死的家伙像条被逼上绝路的狗，一口咬进了我的手。”

===Original===

When we entered the city, some by the Puerta del Carmen, some by the Portillo, all the pieces of the redoubts and forts of Mediodía poured a fire against the columns which were coming after us. The two sorties combined had done damage enough to the French. In addition to losing many men, a small part of their entrenchments had been made useless to them, and we had possessed ourselves of a considerable number of their tools. Besides this, the official engineers that Butron took with him on that daring venture had had time to examine the works of the besiegers, and measure them, and could give descriptions of them to the commanding general.

The rampart wall was invaded by the people. They had heard within the city the shooting of the skirmishes, and men and women, old people and children had run out to see what glorious action was bulletined on the plaza. We were received with exclamations of rejoicing, and from San José all the way to the Trinitarios, the long line of men and women, looking towards the battlefield, climbed upon the walls, and clapping their hands at our arrival, waving their handkerchiefs, presented a magnificent sight. After the cannon sounded, the redoubts together poured a fire upon the field that we had just abandoned, and their voices seemed a triumphal salvo, as it mingled with the huzzas and shouts of joy.

In the surrounding houses, the windows and balconies were filled with women, and the interest or curiosity of some of those in the streets was such that they went into the hurrying crowd in numbers, and up to the cannons, to congratulate the brave souls and soothe with kind words their nerves, high strung with the noise of artillery, which is unlike anything else in the world. It was necessary to command the multitude to depart from the fortress at the Portillo. The crowd in the Santa Engracia gave that place the aspect of a theatre, of a public festival. The fire of the cannon ceased at last, having no more need to protect our retreat, and the Castle Aljafería alone sent an occasional shot against the works of the enemy.

In reward for our action on that day it was granted us on the next to wear a red ribbon on the breast by way of decoration; in justice to the hazards of that sortie, Father Boggiero told us, among other things uttered by the mouth of our general, “Yesterday you marked the last day of the year with an action worthy of yourselves. At the sound of the bugle your swords leaped from their scabbards and struck to the ground haughty heads humbled by your valor and patriotism. Numantia! Olivenza! I have now seen that your light horse will know how to preserve the honor of this army and the enthusiasm of these sacred walls! Wear these bloodstained swords that are the sign of your glory and the protection of your country.”

===Chinese===

我们进城时，有的走卡门城门，有的走波蒂略门，梅迪奥迪亚（Mediodía）各棱堡与堡垒的火炮一齐朝尾追我们的敌军纵队倾泻炮火。两路出击合在一起，已够法军受的了：除了折损大批人马，他们一小部分堑壕已形同废物，相当数量的工具也落到了我们手里。此外，布特龙带去参加那次冒险的随军工兵还来得及察看围攻者的工事并加以丈量，得以向总司令一一禀报其形势。

城墙被百姓们占领了。城里的人听见了散战的枪声，男男女女、老老少少都跑出来，要看广场上战报里通报的是怎样一场光荣的战斗。我们在一片欢呼之声中受到迎接；从圣何塞直到三一会修道院，长长的男女人流面向战场，爬满城墙，见我们到来便鼓着掌、挥着手帕，蔚为大观。炮声响过之后，各棱堡一齐朝我们刚刚撤离的原野倾泻炮火，那炮声同欢呼声、欢乐的呐喊声交织成一片，恰似一排凯旋的礼炮。

周围人家的窗口和阳台上挤满了妇女；街上有些人出于关切或者好奇，竟成群结队地涌进匆匆的人流，一直挤到大炮跟前，向勇士们道贺，用好言好语抚慰他们被炮声震得紧绷的神经——炮声这东西，世上再没有一样比得上。只得下令让人群撤出波蒂略门的那座炮台。圣恩格拉西亚的人群，使那地方显出一副剧场的气象、一场公众节庆的气象。炮声终于停息，因为再不必掩护我们的撤退，只剩阿尔哈费里亚城堡还不时朝敌军工事发上一炮。

为犒赏我们当日的战功，次日我们获准在胸前佩一条红绶带，以作荣典。为表彰那次出击的艰险，博希耶罗神父向我们转达了出自我们将军之口的种种言辞，其中有这样一段：“昨天，你们用一场无愧于自身的战斗，给这一年的最后一天作了记号。号声一响，你们的剑便跃出剑鞘，把一颗颗高傲的头颅斩落在地——它们已被你们的勇武与爱国心压服。努曼西亚！奥利文萨！如今我亲眼见到了：你们的轻骑兵必能守住这支军队的荣誉和这几堵圣墙的热忱！佩上这些染血的剑吧，它们是你们荣耀的标志，是你们祖国的干城。”
