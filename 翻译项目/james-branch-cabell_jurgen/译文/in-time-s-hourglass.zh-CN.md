## XXVI In Time’s Hourglass / 第二十六章 在时光的沙漏里
===Original===

“Well, well!” said Jurgen, when he had taken off all that foolish ironmongery, and had made himself comfortable in his shirt; “well, beyond doubt, the situation is awkward. I was content enough in Cocaigne, and it is unfair that I should be thus ousted. Still, a sensible person will manage to be content anywhere. But whither, pray, am I expected to go?”
“Into whatever land you may elect, my dear,” said Anaïtis, fondly. “That much at least I can manage for you: and the interpretation of your legend can be arranged afterward.”
“But I grow tired of all the countries I have ever seen, dear Anaïtis, and in my time I have visited nearly all the lands that are known to men.”
“That too can be arranged: and you can go instead into one of the countries which are desired by men. Indeed there are a number of such realms which no man has ever visited except in dreams, so that your choice is wide.”
“But how am I to make a choice without having seen any of these countries? It is not fair to be expecting me to do anything of the sort.”
“Why, I will show them to you,” Anaïtis replied.
The two of them then went together into a small blue chamber, the walls of which were ornamented with gold stars placed helter-skelter. The room was entirely empty save for an hourglass near twice the height of a man.
“It is Time’s own glass,” said Anaïtis, “which was left in my keeping when Time went to sleep.”
Anaïtis opened a little door of carved crystal that was in the lower half of the hourglass, just above the fallen sands. With her fingertips she touched the sand that was in Time’s hourglass, and in the sand she drew a triangle with equal sides, she who was strangely gifted and perverse. Then she drew just such another figure so that the tip of it penetrated the first triangle. The sand began to smoulder there, and vapors rose into the upper part of the hourglass, and Jurgen saw that all the sand in Time’s hourglass was kindled by a magic generated by the contact of these two triangles. And in the vapors a picture formed.
“I see a land of woods and rivers, Anaïtis. A very old fellow, regally crowned, lies asleep under an ash-tree, guarded by a watchman who has more arms and hands than Jigsbyed.”
“It is Atlantis you behold, and the sleeping of ancient Time﻿—Time, to whom this glass belongs﻿—while Briareus watches.”

“可在未曾亲眼得见那些国度之前，我又怎生拿得定主意？指望我盲人摸象般凭空抉择，委实有失公允。”

“哎，我亲自为你逐一展示便了，”阿娜伊蒂斯答道。

二人遂联袂步入一间素雅的蔚蓝密室，室内的四壁零零星星、错落有致地点缀着金光闪闪的星辰。整座殿室空空如也，唯独正中矗立着一座将近两人多高的巍峨沙漏。

“这正是时光之神（Time）自家的本命沙漏，”阿娜伊蒂斯说道，“昔年时光之神陷入沉睡之际，特托付于我手中好生看管。”

阿娜伊蒂斯伸手拉开嵌在沙漏下半截、恰位于积落沙砾上方的一扇雕花水晶小门。她以葱白玉指轻轻拨弄着时光沙漏中的沙砾，在沙面上勾勒出一个三边相等的三角形——她向来禀赋神异且行事乖张。随即她依样画葫芦又勾勒出另一个形状肖似的三角形，令其尖端恰好刺入第一个三角形的心腹。沙砾随即在交接处冒出青烟、隐隐灼烧起来，缕缕轻烟升腾入沙漏的上半截，朱尔根惊异地瞧见，时光沙漏深处的整片沙海，尽皆被这2个等边三角形相交所催生出的神异法力点燃了。在弥漫的轻烟缭绕之中，一幅画卷徐徐幻化成形。

“我瞧见了一片古木参天、千帆竞渡的浩瀚疆土，阿娜伊蒂斯。一位头戴巍峨王冠的龙钟老朽正酣睡在一株梣树之下，身畔伫立着一位比吉格斯比耶德（Jigsbyed）还要生出更多臂膀巨手的守夜守卫。”

“你所窥见的乃是亚特兰蒂斯（Atlantis），以及太古时光之神的安详酣眠——这座沙漏的主人时光之神——百臂巨人布里阿柔斯（Briareus）此刻正忠心耿耿地守望在侧。”

Then Anaïtis showed him Ogygia, and Tryphême, and Sudarsana, and the Fortunate Islands, and Æaea, and Caer-Is, and Invallis, and the Hesperides, and Meropis, and Planasia, and Uttarra, and Avalon, and Tir-nam-Beo, and Thelême, and a number of other lands to enter which men have desired: and Jurgen groaned.
“I am ashamed of my fellows,” says he: “for it appears their notion of felicity is to dwell eternally in a glorified brothel. I do not think that as a self-respecting young Prince I would care to inhabit any of these earthly paradises, for were there nothing else, I would always be looking for an invasion by the police.”
“There remains, then, but one other realm, which I have not shown you, in part because it is an obscure little place, and in part because, for a reason that I have, I shall not assist you to go thither.

“妇道人家的通灵直觉委实无从蒙蔽。千真万确，方才我假装去赫斯珀里得斯抑或去金生之国皆全无所谓之际，我确乎未曾倾吐肺腑之言：这委实是在下的过失，在下向夫人告罪赔罪。我原本自忖借由故作冷淡能更易驾驭拿捏于你。然而你顷刻间便戳穿了我的伎俩，且理所当然地雷霆震怒。既然如此，我索性将手里的底牌一股脑儿摊在桌面上，再不闪烁其词顾左右而言他了。我平生真真切切所倾心苦恋者，乃是科马克之女艾勒（Aillê, the daughter of Cormac），天下谁人又能苛责我的痴心？阿娜伊蒂斯，你平生何曾见过更教人魂不守舍的曼妙身段？——天地良心，我朱尔根平生何曾见过。况且，我适才还留神到——罢啦，体己细处休要再提！可我终究身不由己撞见了。更有那一双秋水明眸！宛若指引我航程的一对并蒂灯塔，足可告慰我痛失夫人之余那深不见底的滔天痛惜。噢，千真万确，我决意转道奔赴金生之国。”

“你究竟该被发配去往何方绝地，我的俊后生，乃是由老娘挑肥拣瘦说了算，轮不着你自作主张！你给老娘滚去留刻岛！”

“心肝肉儿，尊驾行行好发发慈悲，讲点常理吧！方才我们明明一致断定，留刻岛万万去不得。哎，哪怕别无挂碍，留刻岛上压根儿连半个姿色入眼的妇人都没有啊！”

“除了死读书读出的呆气，你脑子里难道全装满了浆糊不成！恰恰因着这层缘由，老娘偏要把你发配去留刻岛！”
