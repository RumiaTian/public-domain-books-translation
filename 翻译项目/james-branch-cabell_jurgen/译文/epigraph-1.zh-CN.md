===Original===

> “Of **Jurgen** eke they maken mencioun,

That of an old wyf gat his youthe agoon,

And gat himselfe a shirte as bright as fyre

Wherein to jape, yet gat not his desire

In any countrie ne condicioun.”

===Chinese===

> “他们亦曾提及朱尔根（Jurgen），
>
> 他从一老妪处重获青春，
>
> 又得一件明丽如火的衬衣，
>
> 穿上它纵情嬉游，却终难如意，
>
> 无论身处何邦何境。”
