### XV / 第十五章

## In Which Hugh Drummond Arrives at Maybrick Hall / 休·德拉蒙德抵达梅布里克庄苑纪

===Original===
Two things saved Drummond from what was practically certain death﻿—the heavy coat he was wearing, and the fact that he rolled sideways clear of the steering wheel as soon as the man let go of him with his hand. Had he remained behind the wheel he must infallibly have gone to the bottom with the car, and at that point where the river narrowed to come through the piers of the bridge the water was over twenty feet deep. He had sufficient presence of mind to take a deep breath as the car shot downwards; then he felt the water close over his head. And if before his struggles to move had been fierce﻿—now that the end seemed at hand they became desperate. The desire to get clear﻿—to give one kick with his legs and come to the surface roused him to one superhuman effort. He felt as if the huge heave he gave with his legs against the floorboards must send him flying to the top; afterwards he realized that this vast effort had been purely mental﻿—the actual physical result had been practically negligible. But not quite, it had done something, and the coat did the rest.

===Chinese===
两样东西救了德拉蒙德的命，使他免遭几乎必死无疑的厄运——一件是他身上那件厚外套，一件是他趁着那人松手的工夫，向侧旁一滚，离开了方向盘。要是他还留在方向盘后面，那他必定连人带车一块儿沉到底去了；而在河道收窄、从桥墩之间穿过的那一带，水深超过二十英尺。汽车朝下坠落之际，他尚有足够的镇定深深吸了一口气；随即他便感到河水没过了头顶。如果说先前他挣扎着要动弹时还只是凶猛——那么此刻末路似已临头，他的挣扎便成了拼命。那股要挣脱出去、要双腿一蹬浮上水面的念头，激发起他一番超乎常人的努力。他觉得自己双腿抵着车底板猛地一撑，那一蹬之力势必要把他送上水面；事后他才明白，这一番巨大的努力纯粹是精神上的——实际产生的身体效果几乎可以不计。但也不尽然，它多少起了点作用，剩下的就由那件外套来完成了。

===Original===
With that one last supreme throw for life his mind had overcome the effects of the poison to the extent of forcing his legs to give one spasmodic little kick. He floated clear of the car, and slowly﻿—how slowly only his bursting lungs could testify﻿—the big coat brought him to the surface. For a moment or two he could do nothing save draw in deep gulps of air; then he realized that the danger was not yet past. For he couldn’t shout, he could do nothing save float and drift, and the current had carried him clear of the bridge out of sight of those on top. And his mind was quite clear enough to realize that the coat which had saved him, once it became sodden would just as surely drown him.

===Chinese===
凭着那最后一记为求生而作的拼力一搏，他的头脑暂时压住了药力，强使双腿痉挛似地小小蹬了一下。他从车身旁浮了开去，慢慢地——究竟慢到什么地步，只有他那双快要胀裂的肺才能作证——那件宽大的外套把他托上了水面。有那么片刻他什么也做不了，只能大口大口地往里吸气；随即他意识到危险尚未过去。因为他喊不出声，除了随波漂浮之外什么也做不了，而水流已把他冲过了桥，桥上的人再也看不见他。他的头脑也清醒得足以明白：那件救了他命的外套，一旦吸饱了水，便会同样确凿地把他淹死。

===Original===
He could see men with lanterns on the bridge; he could hear them shouting and talking. And then he saw a boat come back from the ship that had passed through just before he went over the edge in his car. Surely they’d pull downstream to look for him, he thought in an agony of futile anger; surely they couldn’t be such fools as to go on pulling about just by the bridge when it was obvious he wasn’t there. But since they thought that he was at the bottom in his car, and blasphemous language was already being wafted at them by the skipper of the vessel for the useless delay, with a sinking heart Drummond saw the boat turn round and disappear upstream into the darkness. Men with lanterns still stood on the bridge, but he was far beyond the range of their lights, and he was drifting further every minute. It was just a question of time now﻿—and it couldn’t be very long either. He could see that his legs had gone down well below the surface, and only the air that still remained in the buttoned-up part of his overcoat kept his head out and his shoulders near the top. And when that was gone﻿—the end. He had done all he could; there was nothing for it now but to wait for the inevitable finish. And though he had been credibly informed that under such circumstances the whole of a man’s life passes in rapid review before him, his sole and only thought was an intense desire to get his hands on Peterson again.

===Chinese===
他看得见桥上那些提着风灯的人；也听得见他们嚷嚷交谈的声音。接着他看见一条小船从那艘轮船边折了回来——那艘船正是他连车坠下之前刚刚驶过桥洞的。他们总该往下游划来找他吧，他在一阵徒劳的愤恨中想道；他们总不至于蠢到还在桥边那一带划来划去，明明他并不在那里。可既然他们以为他连车沉到了河底，而那艘船的船长又已经因为这番无谓的耽搁冲他们破口大骂起来，德拉蒙德心一沉，眼睁睁看着那条小船掉头消失在上游的黑暗里。提着风灯的人仍站在桥上，可他早已超出了他们灯光照及的范围，而且每一分钟都在漂得更远。眼下只是个时间问题了——而且这时间也不会太长。他看得出自己的双腿已经沉到水面以下很深，唯有那件扣起来的大衣里仍残存的一点空气，使他的头还露在外面、肩膀还挨着水面。等那点空气也没了——便是末日。他能做的都做了；如今别无他法，只有等着那不可避免的结局降临。虽说人都言之凿凿地告诉他，在这种境地下，人一生的种种会飞快地在眼前一一掠过，可他心里唯一转着的念头，却是一股强烈的、要再用手扼住彼得森（Peterson）的渴望。

===Original===
For a while he pictured the scene with a wealth of pleasant detail, until a sudden change in his immediate surroundings began to take place. At first he could not realize what had happened; then little by little it began to dawn on him what had occurred. Up to date the water in which he floated had seemed motionless to him; he had been drifting in it at exactly the same velocity as the current. And now, suddenly, he saw that the water was going past him. For a moment or two he failed to understand the significance of the fact; then wild hope surged up in his mind. For a time he stared fixedly at the bridge, and the hope became a certainty. He was not drifting any further from it; he was stationary; he was aground. He could feel nothing; he could see nothing﻿—but the one stupendous fact remained that he was aground. Life took on another lease﻿—anything might happen now. If only he could remain there till the morning they would see him from the bridge, and there seemed no reason why he shouldn’t. The water still flowed sluggishly past him, broken with the faintest ripple close to his head. So he reasoned that it must be very shallow where he was, and being an incurable optimist, he resumed, with even fuller details, the next meeting with Peterson.

===Chinese===
有片刻工夫，他用一连串生动悦目的细节，在脑中细细描摹起那幅画面来，直到他周遭的环境忽然起了变化。起初他没明白是怎么回事；继而才一点一点地咂摸出发生了什么。到此为止，他所浮身其中的水在他看来一直是静止的；他始终以与水流完全相等的速度随之漂行。而此刻，忽然之间，他看见水正从他身边流过。有那么一两秒他没能领会这一事实意味着什么；随即一阵狂喜涌上心头。他盯着那座桥凝视了一阵，那希望便成了确凿无疑的事。他不再往远处漂了；他停住了；他搁浅了。他什么也感觉不到；什么也看不见——但有一个了不得的事实摆在那里：他搁浅了。生命又续上了一程——眼下什么事都可能发生。只要他能在这儿待到天亮，桥上的人便会看见他，而且似乎也没有什么理由说他不能待。水仍懒懒地从他身旁流过，紧挨着他脑袋的地方漾起最细微的涟漪。他便推断自己所处的位置水一定很浅，而既然他是个无可救药的乐天派，便又添油加醋地，重新构思起下一次与彼得森的会面来。

===Original===
But not for long. Starting from his waist and spreading downwards to his feet and outwards through his shoulders to his hands there slowly began to creep the most agonising cramp. The torture was indescribable, and the sweat dripped off his forehead into his eyes. And gradually it dawned on him that the effects of the poison were wearing off. Sensation was returning to his limbs; even through his agony he could feel that he was resting against something under the water. Then he heard a strange noise, and realized that it was he himself groaning with the pain. The use of his voice had come back. He spoke a sentence aloud, and made certain.

===Chinese===
但没能高兴多久。从腰部起，缓缓地向脚部蔓延，又透过肩膀向外延展到双手，开始爬上一阵最钻心的抽筋。那种苦楚难以形容，汗水顺着额头淌进了眼睛。渐渐地他明白过来，那药的效力正在退去。四肢正在恢复知觉；纵在剧痛之中，他也感觉得到自己正倚靠在水下某样东西上。接着他听见一个古怪的声音，才意识到那是自己痛得发出的呻吟。嗓子的气力也回来了。他出声说了一句话，便确定了下来。

===Original===
And then Drummond deliberately decided on doing one of those things which Peterson had always failed to legislate for in the past. Ninety-nine men out of a hundred would have shouted themselves hoarse under such circumstances; not so Drummond. Had he done so a message would have reached Peterson in just so long as it took a trunk call to get through; the man called Franz was still assiduously helping the gatekeeper on the bridge. And the Reverend Theodosius Longmoor and his little Janet would have vanished into the night, leaving no traces behind them.

===Chinese===
于是德拉蒙德有意拿定主意，要做一件过去彼得森向来料不到的事。十个人里头有九个，处在这种境地都会喊破嗓子；德拉蒙德却不。他若当真喊了，一个长途电话接通的工夫，消息便会传到彼得森那儿；那个叫弗朗茨（Franz）的人此刻还在桥上殷勤地帮着守桥人呢。而西奥多修斯·朗穆尔牧师（Reverend Theodosius Longmoor）和他那位「小珍妮特」（Janet）便会消失在夜色之中，不留半点痕迹。

===Original===
Which all flashed through Drummond’s mind, as the cramp took and racked him, and the impulse to shout grew stronger and stronger. Twice he opened his mouth to hail the men he could see not three hundred yards away﻿—to give a cry that would bring a boat posthaste to his rescue; twice he stopped himself with the shout unuttered. A more powerful force was at work within him than mere pain﻿—a cold, bitter resolve to get even with Carl Peterson. And it required no great effort of brain to see that that would be more easily done if Peterson believed he had succeeded. Moreover if he shouted there would be questions asked. The police would inevitably come into the matter, demanding to know why he adopted such peculiar forms of amusement as going into twenty feet of water in a perfectly good motorcar. And all that would mean delay, which was the last thing he wanted. He felt tolerably certain, that, for all his apparent confidence, Peterson was not going to stop one minute longer in the country than was absolutely necessary.

===Chinese===
这一层层的盘算，都在抽筋一阵阵折磨他、想呼喊的冲动越来越强烈的当口，从他脑中一闪而过。他两次张开嘴，想招呼那些他看得见、离他不过三百码的人——想大喊一声，叫一条小船火速赶来救他；又两次硬生生把到了嘴边的喊声咽了回去。他体内有一股比单纯的疼痛更有力的力量在起作用——一股冷峻、刻毒的、要与卡尔·彼得森（Carl Peterson）算账的决心。而且不用费多少脑筋就能看出：倘若彼得森以为他得手了，这事办起来便容易得多。再者，他若一喊，便会引来盘问。警察势必要插手，要追问他为何采用这般别致的消遣方式——开着一辆好好的汽车往二十英尺深的水里钻。这一切都将意味着耽搁，而这恰恰是他最不想要的。他颇可以断定：彼得森表面上看去再怎么从容，在这个国家也绝不肯多停留一分一秒，非到万不得已不可。

===Original===
So he stayed where he was, in silence﻿—and gradually the cramp passed away. He could turn his head now, and with eyes that had grown accustomed to the darkness he saw what had happened. On each side of him the river flowed past smoothly, and he realized that by a wonderful stroke of luck he had struck a small shoal. Had he missed it﻿—had he floated by on either side﻿—well, Peterson’s plan would have succeeded.

===Chinese===
于是他原地不动，默不作声——渐渐地抽筋退了下去。这会儿他能转过头来了，借着已经适应了黑暗的双眼，他看清了是怎么回事。两边河水平稳地流过，他意识到自己凭着天大的运气，撞上了一小片浅滩。要是他没撞上——要是他从任何一边漂了过去——那么，彼得森的计谋便得逞了。

===Original===
“Following the extraordinary motor accident reported in our previous issue, we are now informed that the body of the unfortunate driver has been discovered some three miles from the scene of the tragedy. He was drowned, and had evidently been dead some hours.”

===Chinese===
「继本报上期所载那桩离奇车祸之后，现获悉：不幸的驾车人尸体已于距惨剧现场约三英里处被发现。该人溺毙，显然已死亡数小时。」

===Original===
Drummond smiled grimly to himself as he imagined the paragraphs in the papers. His nerves were far too hardened to let his narrow escape worry him for an instant, and he felt an unholy satisfaction in thinking of Peterson searching the early specials and the late extras for that little item of news.

===Chinese===
德拉蒙德暗自冷冷一笑，在脑中想象着报纸上那段消息。他的神经早已磨炼得坚不可摧，断不至于为这死里逃生的一刻担惊受怕；一想到彼得森会去翻那些早出的号外、晚出的增刊，巴巴地找那一条小消息，他心里便涌起一股邪门的快意。

===Original===
“I’d hate you to be disappointed, my friend,” he muttered to himself, “but you’ll have to be content with the coat and hat. The body has doubtless drifted further on and will be recovered later.”

===Chinese===
「我可不忍心叫你失望啊，老兄，」他自言自语地嘟囔道，「可你也只好拿这顶帽子和这件外套将就将就了。尸身么，想必又往下游漂远了一程，迟些自会打捞上来。」

===Original===
He took off his hat, and let it drift away; he unbuttoned his overcoat and sent it after the hat. Then letting himself down into the deep water, he swam noiselessly towards the bank.

===Chinese===
他摘下帽子，任它漂走；解开大衣的扣子，把它也送了过去。然后他把自己放进深水里，悄无声息地朝岸边游去。

===Original===
A little to his surprise he found that his legs and arms felt perfectly normal﻿—a trifle stiff perhaps, but beyond that the effects of the poison seemed to have worn off completely. Beyond being very wet he appeared to have suffered no evil results at all, and after he’d done “knees up” on the bank for five minutes to restore his circulation he sat down to consider his plan of action.

===Chinese===
他有点意外地发觉，自己的手脚感觉完全正常——也许略有些僵硬，但除此之外，那药的效力似乎已经彻底退尽了。除了浑身湿透之外，他看上去竟没受什么不好的影响；他在岸上做了五分钟「高抬腿」把血脉活动开，便坐下来盘算下一步的行动。

===Original===
First, Phyllis at Maybrick Hall. He must get at her somehow, and, even if he couldn’t get her away, he must let her know that she would be all right. After that things must look after themselves; everything would depend on circumstances. Always provided that those circumstances led to the one great goal﻿—Peterson. Once Phyllis was safe, everything was subservient to that.

===Chinese===
首先是菲莉丝，她在梅布里克庄苑（Maybrick Hall）。他无论如何得设法够到她，即便没法把她带走，也得让她知道她会没事的。其余的事只能走着瞧；一切都得看情势。只要那些情势最终指向那一个至高的目标——彼得森——便可。菲莉丝一旦安全，其余一切都要为这件事让路。

A church clock near by began to toll the hour and Drummond counted the strokes. Eleven o’clock﻿—not two hours since he had gone over the bridge﻿—and it felt like six. So much the better; it gave him so many more hours of darkness, and he wanted darkness for his explorations at Maybrick Hall. And it suddenly dawned on him that he hadn’t the faintest idea where the house was.

===Chinese===
附近一座教堂的钟开始报时，德拉蒙德数着钟声。十一点——他冲下桥至今不过两个钟头——感觉却像过了六个钟头。这样正好；夜色还能供他驱遣的好几个钟头，而他正需要黑暗来摸一摸梅布里克庄苑的底。可他猛地省悟过来：那宅子究竟在哪儿，他一点概念都没有。

The first thing to do was to get back to the spot where the car had halted, and to do that he must go across country. Activity was diminishing on the bridge, but he could still see lanterns dancing about, and the sudden appearance of a very wet man might lead to awkward questions. So he struck off in the direction he judged to be right﻿—moving with that strange, catlike silence which was a never-ceasing source of wonderment even to those who knew him best.

===Chinese===
这要换了别人，或许就打了退堂鼓；对德拉蒙德却只惹得他一笑。他既然不知道，那就查出来——哪怕非得把哪一位从被窝里拽起来问个明白也在所不辞。头一桩要做的事，是回到汽车先前停下的那个地点，而要做到这一点，他必须横穿田野。桥上的人已经渐渐少了起来，但他仍看得见风灯在那里晃来晃去，一个浑身湿透的人忽然冒出来，难免要招来尴尬的盘问。于是他朝着自己判断为对的方向走去——脚步带着一种古怪的、猫一般悄无声息，这本事即便是最熟悉他的人，也总是惊叹不已。

===Original===
No man ever heard Drummond coming, and very few ever saw him until it was too late, if he didn’t intend that they should. And now, in utterly unknown country, with he knew not how many undesirable gentlemen about, he was taking no risks. Mercifully for him it was a dark night﻿—just such a night in fact as he would have chosen, and as he passed like a huge shadow from tree to tree, only to vanish silently behind a hedge, and reappear two hundred yards further on, he began to feel that life was good. The joy of action was in his veins; he was going to get his hands on somebody soon, preferably the Italian or the man who called himself Franz. For Bill he had a sneaking regard; Bill at any rate could appreciate a good car when he saw one. ===Chinese===

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Original===

Through it he could see a road along which the two men must have been walking. And then through a gap in the trees he saw a light in the window of a house. So his first difficulty was solved. The man called Franz and his companion could have but one destination in all probability﻿—Maybrick Hall.
