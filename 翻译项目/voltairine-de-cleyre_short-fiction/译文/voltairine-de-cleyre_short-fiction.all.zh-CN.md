## The Individualist and the Communist1 A Dialogue / 个人主义者与共产主义者：一场对话

===Original===
Individualist
Our host is engaged and requests that I introduce myself to﻿—I beg your pardon, sir, but have I not the pleasure of meeting the Communist speaker who addressed the meeting on Blank street last evening?
Communist
Your face seems familiar to me, too.

===Chinese===
个人主义者
我们的主人正有事缠身，请我自行介绍——先生，恕我冒昧，莫非我有幸见到的，正是昨晚在布兰克街集会上演讲的那位共产主义者（Communist）？
共产主义者
你的面孔我似乎也觉得眼熟。

===Original===
Individualist
Doubtless you may have seen me there, or at some kindred place. I am glad at the opportunity to talk with you as your speech proved you to be somewhat of a thinker. Perhaps﻿—
Communist
Ah, indeed, I recognize you now. You are the apostle of capitalistic Anarchism!

===Chinese===
个人主义者
无疑你在那里，或在某个类似的地方见过我。有机会同您交谈我很高兴，因为您的演讲证明您还算是个思想者。也许——
共产主义者
啊，真的，我现在认出您了。您是资本制无政府主义（capitalistic Anarchism）的使徒！

===Original===
Individualist
Capitalistic Anarchism? Oh, yes, if you choose to call it so. Names are indifferent to me; I am not afraid of bugaboos. Let it be so, then, capitalistic Anarchism.
Communist
Well, I will listen to you. I don’t think your arguments will have much effect, however. With which member of your Holy Trinity will you begin: free land, free money, or free competition?

===Chinese===
个人主义者
资本制无政府主义？哦，是的，如果您愿意这么叫的话。名称于我无所谓；我不怕妖魔鬼怪。那就这样吧，资本制无政府主义。
共产主义者
好吧，我愿听您说。不过我不认为您的论点会有多大效力。您想从您那神圣三位一体的哪一位开始：自由土地、自由货币，还是自由竞争（free competition）？

===Original===
Individualist
Whichever you prefer.
Communist
Then free competition. Why do you make that demand? Isn’t competition free now?

===Chinese===
个人主义者
随您所愿。
共产主义者
那就谈自由竞争。您为什么提出这项要求？难道现在的竞争还不自由吗？

===Original===
Individualist
No. But one of the three factors in production is free. Laborers are free to compete among themselves, and so are capitalists to a certain extent. But between laborers and capitalists there is no competition whatever, because through governmental privilege granted to capital, whence the volume of the currency and the rate of interest is regulated, the owners of it are enabled to keep the laborers dependent on them for employment, so making the condition of wage-subjection perpetual. So long as one man, or class of men, are able to prevent others from working for themselves because they cannot obtain the means of production or capitalize their own products, so long those others are not free to compete freely with those to whom privilege gives the means. For instance, can you see any competition between the farmer and his hired man? Don’t you think he would prefer to work for himself? Why does the farmer employ him? Is it not to make some profit from his labor? And does the hired man give him that profit out of pure good nature? Would he not rather have the full product of his labor at his own disposal?
Communist
And what of that? What does that prove?

===Chinese===
个人主义者
不自由。但生产三要素之一是自由的。劳动者之间可以自由竞争，资本家之间在某种程度上也可以。但劳动者与资本家之间却毫无竞争可言，因为通过政府授予资本的特权——通货数量与利率即由此调节——资本的所有者得以使劳动者在就业上依附于自己，从而使雇佣臣服的状况永久化。只要一个人或一个阶级能够因为别人得不到生产资料（the means of production）、无法把自己的产品资本化，而阻止他们为自己劳动，那么这些人在同特权赋予生产手段的那些人竞争时就不可能是自由的。举个例子，您能看出农夫与他雇工之间有什么竞争吗？您不认为雇工更愿意为自己干活吗？农夫为什么雇他？难道不是为了从他的劳动中赚取利润吗？而雇工是出于纯粹的好心才把那份利润让给农夫的吗？他难道不愿意把劳动的全产品归自己支配吗？
共产主义者
那又如何？那证明了什么呢？

===Original===
Individualist
I am coming to that directly. Now, does this relation between the farmer and his man in any way resemble a cooperative affair between equals, free to compete, but choosing to work together for mutual benefit? You know it does not. Can’t you see that since the hired man does not willingly resign a large share of his product to his employer (and it is out of human nature to say he does), there must be something which forces him to do it? Can’t you see that the necessity of an employer is forced upon him by his lack of ability to command the means of production? He cannot employ himself, therefore he must sell his labor at a disadvantage to him who controls the land and capital. Hence he is not free to compete with his employer any more than a prisoner is free to compete with his jailer for fresh air.
Communist
Well, I admit that much. Certainly the employee cannot compete with his employer.

===Chinese===
个人主义者
我马上就要说到那一点。那么，农夫与其雇工之间的这种关系，难道有任何一点像平等者之间自由竞争、却选择为共同利益合作共事的样子吗？您知道并不像。您难道看不出，既然雇工并非自愿把产品的很大一份让与雇主（说他是自愿的，违背人性），那就必有某种东西在强迫他这样做？您难道看不出，雇主之必要之所以强加于他，是因为他没有能力支配生产资料？他无法雇佣自己，因此只能吃亏地把劳动力卖给控制土地与资本的人。所以他同雇主之间的竞争是不自由的，正如囚犯同狱卒争夺新鲜空气时的不自由一样。
共产主义者
好吧，这一点我承认。雇员当然竞争不过雇主。

===Original===
Individualist
Then you admit that there is not free competition in the present state of society. In other words, you admit that the laboring class are not free to compete with the holders of capital, because they have not, and cannot get, the means of production. Now for your “what of that?” It follows that if they had access to land and opportunity to capitalize the product of their labor they would either employ themselves, or, if employed by others, their wages, or remuneration, would rise to the full product of their toil, since no one would work for another for less than he could obtain by working for himself.
Communist
But your object is identical with that of Communism! Why all this to convince me that the means of production must be taken from the hands of the few and given to all? Communists believe that; it is precisely what we are fighting for.

===Chinese===
个人主义者
那么您就承认了，在目前的社会状态下不存在自由竞争。换言之，您承认劳动阶级与资本占有者之间的竞争是不自由的，因为他们没有、也得不到生产资料。现在来回答您那句“那又如何”。由此可知，倘若他们能够取用土地、有机会把自己的劳动产品资本化，他们要么就会自己雇佣自己，要么即便受雇于人，其工资或报酬也会上升到劳动的全产品，因为没有人会以低于为自己劳动所得的报酬去为他人工作。
共产主义者
可是您的目标与共产主义（Communism）的目标一模一样！何必费这么多口舌来说服我，说生产资料必须从少数人手中夺过来交给所有人？共产主义者正是这么相信的；这恰恰就是我们在为之奋斗的。

===Original===
Individualist
You misunderstand me if you think we wish to take from or give to anyone. We have no scheme for regulating distribution. We substitute nothing, make no plans. We trust to the unfailing balance of supply and demand. We say that with equal opportunity to produce, the division of product will necessarily approach equitable distribution, but we have no method of “enacting” such equalization.
Communist
But will not some be strong and skillful, others weak and unskillful? Will not one deprive the other because he is more shrewd?

===Chinese===
个人主义者
如果您以为我们想把什么从谁那里夺走、或给予谁，那您就误解我了。我们没有任何调节分配的方案。我们不代之以任何东西，不做任何计划。我们信赖供求之间万无一失的平衡。我们说，有了平等的生产机会，产品的分配就必然趋近公允，但我们没有任何方法去“制定”这种均等。
共产主义者
可是难道不会有些人强壮娴熟，另一些人羸弱笨拙？难道不会有人因为自己更精明而剥夺别人吗？

===Original===
Individualist
Impossible! Have I not just shown you that the reason one man controls another’s manner of living is because he controls the opportunities to produce? He does this through a special governmental privilege. Now, if this privilege is abolished, land becomes free, and if there is the ability to capitalize products removing interest, even if one man is stronger or shrewder than another, he nevertheless can make no profit from that other’s labor, because he cannot stop him from employing himself. The cause of subjection is then removed.
Communist
You call that equality! That one man shall have more than others simply because he is stronger or smarter? Your system is no better than the present. What are we struggling against but that very inequality in people’s possessions?

===Chinese===
个人主义者
不可能！我不是刚刚向您表明，一个人之所以能支配另一个人的生活方式，就是因为他支配着生产的机会吗？他是通过一项特殊的政府特权做到这一点的。如今，倘若这项特权被废除，土地成为自由的，倘若有了把产品资本化从而消除利息的可能，那么即使一个人比另一个人更强壮或更精明，他也无法从后者的劳动中获取利润，因为他无法阻止后者自己雇佣自己。臣服的根由便由此消除了。
共产主义者
您竟把这个叫作平等！一个人仅仅因为自己更强壮或更聪明，就该比别人占有更多？您的制度并不比现状好到哪里去。我们斗争的对象，不正是人们财产上的这种不平等吗？

===Original===
Individualist
But what is equality? Does equality mean that I shall enjoy what you have produced? By no means. Equality simply means the freedom of every individual to develop all his being, without hindrance from another, be he stronger or weaker.
Communist
What! You will have the weak person suffer because he is weak? He may need as much, or more, than a strong one, but if he is not able to produce it what becomes of his equality?

===Chinese===
个人主义者
可是什么是平等？平等难道意味着我该享受您生产出来的东西吗？绝非如此。平等仅仅意味着每个个体都有自由去发展他的全部存在，不受他人的阻碍，无论那人是强是弱。
共产主义者
什么！您要弱者因为弱而受苦吗？他所需的可能和强者一样多，甚至更多，可他若无能力生产出来，他的平等又到哪里去了？

===Original===
Individualist
I have nothing against your dividing your product with the weaker man if you desire to do so.
Communist
There you are with charity again. Communism wants no charity.

===Chinese===
个人主义者
如果您愿意把自己的产品分给较弱的人，我毫无异议。
共产主义者
瞧，您又搬出慈善来了。共产主义不需要慈善。

===Original===
Individualist
I have often marveled on the singularity of Communistic mathematics. *My* act you call charity, *our* act is not charity. If one person does a kind act you stigmatize it; if one plus one, summed up and called a commune, does the same thing, you laud it. By some species of alchemy akin to the transmutation of metals, the arsenic of charity becomes the gold of justice! Strange calculation! Can you not see that you are running from a bugaboo again? You change the name, but the character of an action is not altered by the number of people participating in it.
Communist
But it is not the same action. For me to assist you out of pity is the charity of superior possession to the inferior. But to base society upon the principle: “From each according to his capacity, and to each according to his needs” is not charity in any sense.

===Chinese===
个人主义者
我常常惊叹于共产主义数学的奇特。我的行为你们称之为慈善，我们的行为就不是慈善。一个人做了善行，你们给它打上污名；一加一，加起来称作公社，做同样的事，你们就大加赞扬。凭着某种近乎金属嬗变的炼金术，慈善的砒霜竟变成了正义的黄金！奇怪的算法！您难道看不出自己又在逃避一个怪物了吗？您改换了名称，可一个行为的性质并不会因参与者人数多寡而改变。
共产主义者
可这不是同一个行为。我出于怜悯帮助您，是占有优越者对低下者的慈善。而把社会建立在这样一条原则之上：“各尽所能，按需分配”，在任何意义上都不是慈善。

===Original===
Individualist
That is a finer discrimination than logic can find any basis for. But suppose that, for the present, we drop the discussion of charity, which is really a minor point, as a further discussion will show.
Communist
But I say it is very important. See! Here are two workmen. One can make five pair of shoes a day; the other, perhaps, not more than three. According to you, the less rapid workmen will be deprived of the enjoyments of life, or at any rate will not be able to get as much as the other, because of a natural inability, a thing not his fault, to produce as much as his competitor.

===Chinese===
个人主义者
这种分辨比逻辑所能找到的任何根据都更精细。不过，且让我们暂时搁下慈善之争——它实在是个枝节问题，进一步的讨论将会表明这一点。
共产主义者
可我说这非常重要。您瞧！这里有两个工人。一个一天能做五双鞋；另一个也许做不过三双。照您的说法，手脚慢些的工人就要被剥夺生活的享受，或者至少得不到另一个那么多，原因仅仅在于一种天生无能——这不是他的过错——使他生产不过他的竞争者。

===Original===
Individualist
It is true that under our present conditions, there are such differences in productive power. But these, to a large extent, would be annihilated by the development of machinery and the ability to use it in the absence of privilege. Today the majority of trade-people are working at uncongenial occupations. Why? Because they have neither the chance for finding out for what they are adapted, nor the opportunity of devoting themselves to it if they had. They would starve to death while searching; or, finding it, would only bear the disappointment of being kept outside the ranks of an already overcrowded pathway of life. Trades are, by force of circumstances, what formerly they were by law, matters of inheritance. I am a tailor because my father was a tailor, and it was easier for him to introduce me to that mode of making a living than any other, although I have no special adaptation for it. But postulating equal chances, that is free access and non-interest bearing capital, when a man finds himself unable to make shoes as well or as rapidly as his co-worker, he would speedily seek a more congenial occupation.
Communist
And he will be traveling from one trade to another like a tramp after lodgings!

===Chinese===
个人主义者
不错，在目前的条件下，生产能力的确存在这样的差异。但在没有特权的情况下，机械的发展和使用机械的能力将在很大程度上消灭这些差异。今天，大多数从业者在从事着不合性情的职业。为什么？因为他们既没有机会弄清自己适合什么，即便弄清了，也没有机会投身其中。他们在寻觅的过程中会饿死；或者找到了，也只会尝到被拒于一条早已人满为患的人生道路之外的失望。行当如今因情势所迫，成了旧日法律之下的世袭之事。我是个裁缝，因为我父亲是裁缝，他把我引进这一谋生方式比引进任何别的行当都容易，尽管我对裁缝并没有特别的禀赋。但假如机会均等——即土地自由取用、资本不生利息——当一个人发现自己做鞋不如同事做得那样好、那样快时，他会迅速另寻更合性情的职业。
共产主义者
那他就要像一个寻宿处的流浪汉一样，从一个行当流浪到另一个行当了！

===Original===
Individualist
Oh no; his lodgings will be secure! When you admitted that competition is not now free, did I not say to you that when it becomes so, one of two things must happen: either the laborer will employ himself, or the contractor must pay him the full value of his product. The result would be increased demand for labor. Able to employ himself, the producer will get the full measure of his production, whether working independently, by contract, or cooperatively, since the competition of opportunities, if I may so present it, would destroy the possibility of profits. With the reward of labor raised to its entire result, a higher standard of living will necessarily follow; people will want more in proportion to their intellectual development; with the gratification of desires come new wants, all of which guarantees constant labor-demand. Therefore, even your trades-tramp will be sure of his existence.
But you must consider further that the business of changing trades is no longer the difficult affair it was formerly. Years ago, a mechanic, or laborer, was expected to serve from four to seven years’ apprenticeship. No one was a thorough workman until he knew all the various departments of his trade. Today the whole system of production is revolutionized. Men become specialists. A shoemaker, for instance, spends his days in sewing one particular seam. The result is great rapidity and proficiency in a comparatively short apace of time. No great amount of strength or skill is required; the machine furnishes both. Now, you will readily see that, even supposing an individual changes his vocation half a dozen times, he will not travel very long before he finds that to which he is adapted, and in which he can successfully compete with others.

===Chinese===
个人主义者
哦不；他的宿处将是有保障的！当您承认竞争现在并不自由时，我不是对您说过，一旦竞争自由了，二者必居其一：要么劳动者自己雇佣自己，要么承包者必须付给他产品的全部价值。其结果将是对劳动需求的增长。能够自己雇佣自己，生产者就将获得其生产的全部份额，无论他是独立干活、订约干活还是合作干活，因为——请容我这样表述——机会之间的竞争将摧毁利润的可能性。随着劳动报酬提升到其全部成果，更高的生活水准必然随之而来；人们的需要将随其智识发展而比例增长；欲望得到满足，新的欲求又起，这一切都保证了对劳动的持续需求。因此，就连您那位行业流浪汉也保得住自己的生计。
但您还须进一步考虑到，改换行当已不再是昔日那样困难的事了。多年以前，一个技工或劳工要当四年到七年的学徒。不把本行当的各个部门全都学会，谁也算不上地道的工人。今天，整个生产体系已经革命化了。人成了专门匠。譬如一个制鞋匠，终日缝的只是某一道特定的线缝。其结果，是在比较短的时期内便获得极高的速度与娴熟。不需要多少体力或技艺；机器把两者都备齐了。那么您不难看出，即使假定一个人改行改上六次，他也不会流浪太久，就能找到适合自己的行当，并在其中成功地与人竞争。

===Original===
Communist
But admitting this, don’t you believe there will always be some who can produce more than their brothers? What is to prevent their obtaining advantages over the less fortunate?
Individualist
Certainly I do believe there are such differences in ability, but that they will lead to the iniquity you fear I deny. Suppose A does produce more than B, does he in anyway injure the latter so long as he does not prevent B from applying his own labor to exploit nature, with equal facilities as himself, either by self-employment or by contract with others?

===Chinese===
共产主义者
但就算承认这一点，您难道不认为总会有一些人生产得比他们的弟兄多吗？有什么能阻止他们占到不太幸运者的上风呢？
个人主义者
我当然相信能力上存在这样的差异，但要说它们会导致您所惧怕的那种不义，我是否认的。假定甲确实比乙生产得多，只要他不阻止乙以与他同等的条件、或靠自雇或靠与他人订约，把自己的劳动用来开发自然，他又在什么方面损害了乙呢？

===Original===
Communist
Is that what you call right? Will that produce mutual fellowship among human beings? When I see that you are enjoying things which I cannot hope to get, what think you will be my feelings toward you? Shall I not envy and hate you, as the poor do the rich today?
Individualist
Why, will you hate a man because he has finer eyes or better health than you? Do you want to demolish a person’s manuscript because he excels you in penmanship? Would you cut the extra length from Samson’s hair, and divide it around equally among all short-haired people? Will you share a slice from the poet’s genius and put it in the common storehouse so everybody can go and take some? If there happened to be a handsome woman in your neighborhood who devotes her smiles to your brother, shall you get angry and insist that they be “distributed according to the needs” of the Commune? The differences in natural ability are not, in freedom, great enough to injure anyone or disturb the social equilibrium. No one man can produce more than three others; and even granting that much, you can see that it would never create the chasm which lies between Vanderbilt and the switchman on his tracks.

===Chinese===
共产主义者
这就是您所谓的公道吗？这能在人与人之间产生同胞情谊吗？当我看到您享受着我无望得到的东西，您以为我对您会是什么感情？我难道不会又嫉妒又怨恨，就像今天穷人对待富人那样？
个人主义者
咦，您会因为一个人眼睛比您好、身体比您健康就恨他吗？您会因为一个人书法胜过您，就想把他的手稿捣毁吗？您要把参孙（Samson）头发多出的长度剪下来，均分给所有短发的人吗？您要从诗人的天才里切下一块放进公共仓库，好让人人都去取一份吗？倘若您邻里碰巧有位美人，把她的笑容都给了您兄弟，您要发怒，坚持那些笑容必须按公社（the Commune）的“需要”来分配吗？天赋能力的差异，在自由之中并没有大到伤害任何人、搅乱社会均衡的地步。没有一个人能生产得比另外三个人更多；即便退一步承认到这个程度，您也可以看出，这绝造不出横在范德比尔特（Vanderbilt）与他铁轨上扳道工之间的那道鸿沟。

===Original===
Communist
But in establishing equal justice, Communism would prevent even the possibility of injustice.
Individualist
Is it justice to take from talent to reward incompetency? Is it justice to virtually say that the tool is not to the toiler, nor the product to the producer, but to others? Is it justice to rob toil of incentive? The justice you seek lies not in such injustice, where material equality could only be attained at the dead level of mediocrity. As freedom of contract enlarges, the nobler sentiments and sympathies invariably widen. With freedom of access to land and to capital, no glaring inequality in distribution could result. No workman rises far above or sinks much below the average day’s labor. Nothing but the power to enslave through controlling opportunity to utilize labor force could ever create such wide differences as we now witness.

===Chinese===
共产主义者
可是共产主义确立了平等的正义，就连不义的可能性也会一并杜绝。
个人主义者

## Some Nihilists I Have Met / 我结识的几位虚无主义者

===Original===
The word nihilist is so generally associated with darkness, secrecy, dynamite, assassination and blood, that had someone whispered five minutes before the encounter, “You are about to meet a genuine Russian nihilist,” I should, no doubt, have hastily retreated to the shelter of law-abiding domiciles, far from the dirty, tortuous, downtown quarter, where, amidst a labyrinth of alleys and deceitful little streets that mockingly led against walls, and then turned back into one another, I found myself one snowy afternoon, picking my way somewhat disgustedly with no very clear idea concerning my exact whereabouts.
One thing, however, was sure, I had been appointed on a committee to secure musical talent for a concert shortly to be given by a certain society, the proceeds of which were to be used for the purchase of turkeys for people who otherwise could not properly celebrate the Christmas feast. Having learned through devious channels of a new violin-wonder whose services were to be had for a reasonable consideration, I had at length obtained his address and gone in pursuit of him. It was indeed a pursuit. For half an hour I chased skulking lampposts, that on being captured mockingly stood forth without the shadow of a letter on them; signs that had grown so old in wind and weather as to be illegible; a few new brick corners that seemed to say, “Here we are, and we don’t need a name to tell you﻿—we’re plain enough without it,” as indeed they were among such heaps of dirt and ruin; and finally, people who smiled vaguely, and answered me in a foreign language. ## The Chain Gang / 锁链苦役队

===Original===

Why must I grind my teeth and sit there helpless, while those beautiful things were crushed and blasted and torn in living fragments? I could take them home, I could give them drink, they would lift up their heads, they would open wide, for days they would make the room sweet, and the pale, soft glory of their inimitable petals would shine like a luminous promise across the winter. Nobody wanted them, nobody cared; this sodden beast in the flare-up of his consciousness wished to be quit of them. *Why* might I not take them? Something sharp bit and burned my eyelids as I glanced at the one on the floor.
