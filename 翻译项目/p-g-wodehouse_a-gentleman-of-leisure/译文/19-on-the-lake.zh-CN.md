## XIX On the Lake / 湖上

===Original===
In making love, as in every other branch of life, consistency is the quality most to be aimed at. To hedge is fatal. A man must choose the line of action which he judges to be best suited to his temperament, and hold to it without deviation. If Lochinvar snatches the maiden up onto his saddlebow he must continue in that vein. He must not fancy that, having accomplished the feat, he can resume the episode on lines of devotional humility. Prehistoric man, who conducted his courtship with a club, never fell into the error of apologising when his bride complained of headache.

Jimmy did not apologise. The idea did not enter his mind. He was feeling prehistoric. His heart was beating fast and his mind was in a whirl, but the one definite thought that came to him during the first few seconds of the journey was that he ought to have done this earlier. This was the right way—pick her up and carry her off and leave uncles and fathers and butter-haired peers of the realm to look after themselves. This was the way—alone together in their own little world of water with nobody to interrupt and nobody to overhear. He should have done it before. He had wasted precious, golden time hanging about while futile men chatted to her of things that could not possibly be of interest. But he had done the right thing at last—he had got her. She must listen to him now. She could not help listening. They were the only inhabitants of this new world.

He looked back over his shoulder at the world they had left. The last of the Dreevers had rounded the clump of laurels, and was standing at the edge of the water, gazing perplexedly after the retreating canoe.

===Chinese===
谈情说爱这一门，同人生的一切行当一样，最要紧的品质是一以贯之。三心二意是致命的。男人必须选定一条自认为最合自己性情的行动路线，然后毫厘不偏地坚持下去。洛钦瓦尔既已把姑娘掳上鞍桥，他就得把这一路数贯彻到底。他万不可痴心妄想，以为伟业一成，便可以换上虔诚谦卑的路数重续这段插曲。史前男人求爱用的是棍棒，可新娘推说头疼的时候，他从来不曾忙不迭地赔过不是。

吉米没有赔不是。这念头压根儿没进过他的脑子。他正觉得自己一身史前气。他的心跳得飞快，脑袋里一团乱麻，可就在动身之后的头几秒钟里，有一个明确无误的念头冒了出来：这事他早该做了。这才是正路——把她抱起来，掳了就走，叔伯也好，老子也好，还有那些一头黄油色头发的贵族老爷，都由他们自己操心去。这才是正路——两个人单独守着属于自己的一方小小水世界，没有人来打岔，也没有人偷听。他早该这么干。多少黄金般的宝贵光阴，他白白耗在旁边干瞪眼，听那些百无一用的男人同她谈天，谈些绝无可能叫她感兴趣的东西。可他终究做了对的事——他把她弄到手了。如今她非听他说话不可。她想不听也不成。这片新天地里，居民只有他们两个。

他回过头去，望了一眼他们撇在身后的那个世界。德里弗家的末代传人刚绕过那丛月桂，正立在水边，满眼惶惑地目送渐渐远去的小舟。

===Original===
“These poets put a thing very neatly sometimes,” said Jimmy, reflectively, as he dug the paddle into the water. “The man who said ‘Distance lends enchantment to the view,’ for instance. Dreever looks quite nice when you see him as far away as this, with a good strip of water in between.”

Molly, gazing over the side of the boat into the lake, abstained from feasting her eyes on the picturesque spectacle.

“Why did you do it?” she said, in a low voice.

===Chinese===
「这些诗人有时候把一句话说得真够俏皮，」吉米一面把桨深深插进水里，一面若有所思地说。「比方说那位说『距离为景色平添魅力』的。离得这么远看德里弗，中间又隔着一大片好水，它瞧着着实不坏。」

莫莉的目光钉在船舷外的湖水上，克制着自己，不去饱览那幅如画景致。

「你为什么要这样做？」她低声说。

===Original===
Jimmy shipped the paddle and allowed the canoe to drift. The ripple of the water against the prow sounded clear and thin in the stillness. The world seemed asleep. The sun blazed down, turning the water to flame. The air was hot with the damp, electric heat that heralds a thunderstorm. Molly’s face looked small and cool in the shade of her big hat. Jimmy, as he watched her, felt that he had done well. This was, indeed, the way.

“Why did you do it?” she said again.

“I had to.”

“Take me back.”

“No.”

===Chinese===
吉米收起桨，任小船随波漂荡。水波拍打船头，在寂静里听来又清又细。世界仿佛睡着了。太阳当空炽晒，把湖水烧成一泓火焰。空气又湿又热，闷着一股雷雨将临的、静电似的燥热。莫莉的脸在大帽檐的荫凉里显得又小又清爽。吉米望着她，觉得自己干得漂亮。这才当真是正路。

「你为什么要这样做？」她又说了一遍。

「我不得不这样做。」

「带我回去。」

「不。」

===Original===
He took up the paddle and placed a broader strip of water between the two worlds, then paused once more.

“I have something to say to you first,” he said.

She did not answer. He looked over his shoulder again. His lordship had disappeared.

“Do you mind if I smoke?”

She nodded. He filled his pipe carefully and lit it. The smoke moved sluggishly up through the still air. There was a long silence. A fish jumped close by, falling back in a shower of silver drops. Molly started at the sound and half turned.

===Chinese===
他重新操起桨，在两个世界之间又隔出更宽的一片水面，然后再次停下。

「我先有句话要对你说，」他说。

她不答话。他又回头望了一眼。勋爵大人不见了。

「我抽烟你介意吗？」

她点点头。他仔细装满烟斗，点着了。烟在滞止的空气里慢腾腾往上升。长长一阵沉默。近旁一条鱼跃出水面，落回去时溅起一天银珠。莫莉被那声响吓了一跳，半转过身来。

===Original===
“That was a fish,” she said, as a child might have done.

Jimmy knocked the ashes out of his pipe.

“What made you do it?” he asked abruptly, echoing her own question.

She drew her fingers slowly through the water without speaking.

===Chinese===
「那是条鱼，」她说，口气活像个小孩子。

吉米磕去烟斗里的烟灰。

「又是什么叫你这样做的？」他冷不丁问道，把她那句问话原样奉还。

她不言语，只把手指缓缓地划着水面。

===Original===
“You know what I mean. Dreever told me.”

She looked up with a flash of spirit, which died away as she spoke.

“What right?” She stopped and looked away again.

“None,” said Jimmy. “But I wish you would tell me.”

She hung her head. Jimmy bent forward and touched her hand.

“Don’t,” he said. “For Heaven’s sake, don’t! You mustn’t.”

===Chinese===
「你明白我的意思。德利弗都告诉我了。」

她抬起头，眸子里闪过一丝倔强的火光，可一开口，那火光便熄了。

「你有什么权利——」她住了口，重新把脸别开。

「没有权利，」吉米说。「可我求你告诉我。」

她垂下头去。吉米俯过身，碰了碰她的手。

「别，」他说。「看在老天分上，别！你不可以这样。」

===Original===
“I must,” she said miserably.

“You shan’t! It’s wicked.”

“I must. It’s no good talking about it—it’s too late.”

“It’s not. You must break it off today.”

She shook her head. Her fingers still dabbled mechanically in the water. The sun was hidden now behind a grey veil, which deepened into a sullen black over the hill behind the castle. The heat had grown more oppressive.

“What made you do it?” he asked again.

===Chinese===
「我非这样不可，」她凄然道。

「你不许！这是造孽。」

「我非这样不可。说什么也没有用了——太迟了。」

「不迟。你今天就该把它退掉。」

她摇了摇头。手指仍机械地在水里拨弄。太阳这会儿藏进了一层灰纱后面；灰纱在城堡背后的山上越沉越暗，闷成一团阴黑。暑热愈发逼人。

「又是什么叫你这样做的？」他又问了一遍。

===Original===
“Don’t let’s talk about it, please!”

He had a momentary glimpse of her face. There were tears in her eyes. At the sight his self-control snapped.

“You shan’t!” he cried. “It’s ghastly. I won’t let you. You must understand now—you must know what you are to me. Do you think I shall let you—”

A low growl of thunder rumbled through the stillness, like the muttering of a sleepy giant. The black cloud which had hung over the hill had crept closer. The heat was stifling. In the middle of the lake, some fifty yards distant, lay the island, cool and mysterious in the gathering darkness.

He broke off and seized the paddle.

On this side of the island was a boathouse—a little creek, covered over with boards, and capable of sheltering an ordinary rowboat. He ran the canoe in just as the storm began, and turned her broadside on so that they could watch the rain, which was sweeping over the lake in sheets.

===Chinese===
「求你别谈这个了！」

他瞥见了她的脸，只一瞬。她眼里噙着泪。一见这个，他的自制力啪地绷断了。

「你不许！」他喊道。「这太可怕了。我不能由你。你现在必须明白——你必须知道你对我是什么。你以为我会容你——」

一声低低的雷滚过寂静，像睡熟了的巨人在咕哝。原先悬在山头的那团黑云已悄悄爬近。热得人透不过气。湖心五十来码开外卧着那座小岛，在渐浓的暝色里透出清凉与神秘。

他话说到一半收住，一把抓起桨来。

岛的这一面有座船坞——一个小小的水湾，顶上覆着木板，容得下一只普通划艇藏身。他恰在暴雨骤起时把船划进湾去，又把船身横过来，好教两人看雨——大雨正一片一片地扫过湖面。

===Original===
He began to speak again, more slowly now.

“I think I loved you from the first day I saw you on the ship—and then I lost you. I found you again by a miracle, and lost you again; I found you here by another miracle, but this time I am not going to lose you. Do you think I am going to stand by and see you taken from me by—by—”

He took her hand.

“Molly, you can’t love him. It isn’t possible. If I thought you did, I wouldn’t try to spoil your happiness—I’d go away. But you don’t—you can’t. He’s nothing. Molly!”

===Chinese===
他重新开口，这一回说得更慢了。

「我想，打在船上头一回见你的那天起，我就爱上你了——后来我失去了你。靠一个奇迹我又找到了你，却又失去了；在这儿又靠另一个奇迹找到了你，可这一回我不会再失去你。你以为我会袖手站着，眼看人家把你从我身边夺走——被——被——」

他握住她的手。

「莫莉，你不可能爱他。绝不可能。倘若我当真以为你爱他，我也不会存心搅扰你的幸福——我走开就是。可你不爱——你不爱他。他算个什么。莫莉！」

===Original===
“Molly!”

She said nothing, but for the first time her eyes met his, clear and unwavering. He could read fear in them, fear—not of himself, of something vague, something he could not guess at. But they shone with a light which conquered the fear as the sun conquers fire; and he drew her to him, and kissed her again and again, murmuring incoherently.

Suddenly she wrenched herself away, struggling like some wild thing. The boat plunged.

“I can’t!” she cried, in a choking voice. “I mustn’t! Oh, I can’t!”

He stretched out a hand and clutched at the rail that ran along the wall. The plunging ceased. He turned. She had hidden her face, and was sobbing, quietly, with the forlorn hopelessness of a lost child.

===Chinese===
「莫莉！」

她什么也没说，可她生平第一次迎住了他的目光，清明而坚定。他看得出那目光里的惧怕——怕的不是他，是某种朦胧的东西，某种他无从猜度的东西。然而那双眼睛又闪着一种光，把惧怕压了下去，如太阳压服火焰；他把她揽到怀里，一遍又一遍地吻她，口中语无伦次地喃喃。

A great wave of relief swept over Jimmy. He had imagined—he hardly knew what he had imagined—some vast, insuperable obstacle, some tremendous catastrophe whirling them asunder. He could have laughed aloud in his happiness. So this was it, this was the cloud that brooded over them—that Mr. McEachern did not like him! Memories of those early days crowded her mind as she spoke, making her voice tremble; half-forgotten trifles, many of them, fraught with the glamour and fragrance of past happiness.

“We have always been together. He trusted me and I trusted him, and we saw things through together. When I was ill he used to sit up all night with me, night after night. Jimmy turned away his head, for fear his face might betray what he was feeling. He was in a hell of unreasoning jealousy. He wanted her, body and soul, and every word she said bit like a raw wound. A moment before and he had felt that she belonged to him; now, in the first shock of reaction, he saw himself a stranger, an intruder, a trespasser on holy ground.

She saw the movement, and her intuition put her in touch with his thoughts.

“No, no!” she cried. “No, Jimmy—not that!”

「我们向来形影不离。他信我，我信他，什么事都是两个人一同扛过去。我生病的时候，他常常整夜整夜守着我。有一回——其实不过发了点低烧，我自己却当病得要死了——我听见他回来得很晚，喊了他一声，他径直进来，坐下握着我的手守了整整一夜；后来我偶然才听说，那晚一直下着雨，他浑身淋得透湿。那是要送他性命的。我们是搭伙的伙伴呀，吉米，亲爱的。如今我再做什么也不能伤他，对不对？那就不够光明磊落了。」

吉米把头偏向一边，生怕脸上的神情泄露了他的心事。他正陷在一座毫无理性可言的妒火地狱里。他要她，灵与肉都要；她说的每一个字，都像咬在新鲜伤口上一样疼。片刻之前，他还觉得她属于他；此刻，在乍一反转的头一阵震荡里，他看见自己成了外人，成了闯入者，成了踏进圣地的僭越之徒。

她看见他偏头的动作，直觉立刻叫她摸到了他的心思。

「不，不！」她喊道。「不，吉米——不是那个意思！」

===Original===
Their eyes met, and he was satisfied.

They sat there silent. The rain had lessened its force and was falling now in a gentle shower; a strip of blue sky, pale and watery, showed through the grey over the hills. On the island close behind them a thrush had begun to sing.

“What are we to do?” she said at last. “What can we do?”

“We must wait,” he said. “It will all come right. It must. Nothing can stop us now.”

The rain had ceased. The blue had routed the grey and driven it from the sky. The sun, low down in the west, shone out bravely over the lake. The air was cool and fresh.

雨停了。蓝色把灰色杀得大败，逐出了天空。西天低处的太阳抖擞精神，勇敢地把光辉洒遍湖面。空气清凉而新鲜。

Jimmy’s spirits rose with a bound. He accepted the omen. This was the world as it really was, smiling and friendly, not grey, as he had fancied it. He had won. Nothing could alter that. What remained to be done was trivial. He wondered how he could ever have allowed it to weigh upon him.

After a while he pushed the boat out of its shelter onto the glittering water, and seized the paddle.

“We must be getting back,” he said. “I wonder what the time is? I wish we could stay out forever. But it must be late. Molly!”

“Yes?”

“Whatever happens, you’ll break off this engagement with Dreever? Shall I tell him? I will if you like.”

“It’s no good,” he said suddenly; “I can’t keep it in. Molly, do you mind if I sing a bar or two? I’ve got a beastly voice, but I’m feeling rather happy. I’ll stop as soon as I can.”

He raised his voice discordantly.

Covertly, from beneath the shade of her big hat, Molly watched him with troubled eyes. The sun had gone down behind the hills, and the water had ceased to glitter. There was a suggestion of chilliness in the air. The great mass of the castle frowned down upon them, dark and forbidding in the dim light.
