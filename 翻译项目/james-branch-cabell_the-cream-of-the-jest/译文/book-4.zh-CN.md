## Book Fourth / 第四卷

===Original===

“But there were dreams to sell
						

						Ill didst thou buy:
						

						Life is a dream, they tell,
						

						Waking to die.
						

===Chinese===

“可是从前有过梦可卖，

你却买得失策：

人生是一场梦，人们说，

醒来便是死。

===Original===

						Dreaming a dream to prize,
						

						Is wishing ghosts to rise;
						

						And, if I had the spell
						

						To call the buried﻿—well,
						

						Which one would I?”

===Chinese===

把一场梦奉为至宝，

无异于盼亡魂起身；

而我若握有那道咒语，

能唤起埋骨之人——那么，

我会唤起哪一个？”
