## Book
					Second

“Whate’er she be﻿—
						

						That inaccessible She
						

						That doth command my heart and me:

“Till that divine
						

						Idea take a shrine
						

						Of crystal flesh, through which to shine:

“Let her full glory,
						

						My fancies, fly before ye;
						

						Be ye my fictions﻿—but her story.”
