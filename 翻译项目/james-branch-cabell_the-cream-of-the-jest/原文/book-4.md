## Book
					Fourth

“But there were dreams to sell
						

						Ill didst thou buy:
						

						Life is a dream, they tell,
						

						Waking to die.
						

						Dreaming a dream to prize,
						

						Is wishing ghosts to rise;
						

						And, if I had the spell
						

						To call the buried﻿—well,
						

						Which one would I?”
