## May 15th / 5月15日

===Original===
How cruel it was of me to put those poor little owls into a cage even for one night! I cannot forgive myself, and shall never pander to the Man of Wrath’s wishes again. This morning I got up early to see how they were getting on, and I found the door of the cage wide open and no owls to be seen. I thought of course that somebody had stolen them﻿—some boy from the village, or perhaps the chastised cowherd. But looking about I saw one perched high up in the branches of the beech tree, and then to my dismay one lying dead on the ground. The third was nowhere to be seen, and is probably safe in its nest. The parents must have torn at the bars of the cage until by chance they got the door open, and then dragged the little ones out and up into the tree. The one that is dead must have been blown off the branch, as it was a windy night and its neck is broken. There is one happy life less in the garden today through my fault, and it is such a lovely, warm day﻿—just the sort of weather for young soft things to enjoy and grow in. The babies are greatly distressed, and are digging a grave, and preparing funeral wreaths of dandelions.

Just as I had written that I heard sounds of arrival, and running out I breathlessly told the Man of Wrath how nearly I had been able to give him the owls he has so often said he would like to have, and how sorry I was they were gone, and how grievous the death of one, and so on after the voluble manner of women.

===Chinese===
我把那些可怜的小猫头鹰关进笼子，哪怕只关一夜，也是何等残忍！我不能原谅自己，今后再也不会一味迎合愤怒之人的心意了。今天早晨我早早起身去看它们怎样了，却发现笼门大开，猫头鹰已不见了踪影。我自然以为是有人把它们偷走了——也许是村里的哪个男孩，也许是那个挨过训斥的牛倌。可是四下一望，我看见一只高高地栖在山毛榉的枝头，接着又惊愕地发现一只直挺挺躺在地上，死了。第三只哪儿也找不到，多半正安稳地待在窝里。一定是猫头鹰的父母扑啄笼子的木条，直到碰巧把笼门弄开，然后把小家伙们拖出去、带上树去。那只死了的准是被风从枝头刮落的，因为夜里风大，它的脖子折断了。由于我的过错，今天的花园里少了一条快活的小生命，而这又是多么可爱、多么温暖的一天——正是年幼娇嫩的小生灵们享受阳光、茁壮生长的好天气。三个宝贝伤心极了，正在挖一座坟墓，并用蒲公英扎葬礼的花环。

刚写完这句话，我就听见了有人到门的动静，于是跑出去，气喘吁吁地对愤怒之人说，我差一点儿就能把那对猫头鹰送给他了——他不是常念叨着想要吗——它们没了我是多么遗憾，其中一只死了又是何等令人痛心，如此等等，尽是女人们那副滔滔不绝的做派。

===Original===
He listened till I paused to breathe, and then he said, “I am surprised at such cruelty. How could you make the mother owl suffer so? She had never done you any harm.”

Which sent me out of the house and into the garden more convinced than ever that he sang true who sang﻿—

> Two paradises ’twere in one to live in Paradise alone.

===Chinese===
他一直听我讲，直到我停下来喘气，然后他说：「如此残忍，实在出乎我的意料。你怎么能让猫头鹰妈妈这样受苦？她可从来没有伤害过你。」

这话把我气出了屋子、送进了花园，也让我比以往任何时候都更加确信，唱出下面这行诗的人唱得千真万确——

> 独自住在乐园里，一个乐园便抵得上两个乐园。
