## XIX · The Last Lap / XIX · 最后一程

===Original===

Next morning Inspector French was early occupied in making the necessary preparations for his great coup. The first of these involved a visit to Messrs. Dashwood and Munce, and the business day had scarcely begun when he presented himself once more at their office.

===Chinese===

次日上午，弗伦奇探长一早便忙着为他那桩大手笔做种种必要的筹备。头一件便是走访达什伍德－芒斯经纪行；营业时间刚开始不多一会儿，他便再度登上了他们的办公室。

===Original===

“I am sorry, Mr. Dashwood, for troubling you so soon again,” he apologised, “but I want to ask you one other question. Can you tell me whether Mr. Whitman saw your partner during his call? In other words, if Mr. Whitman were to meet Mr. Munce, would he recognise him?”

===Chinese===

「达什伍德先生，这么快又来打搅，实在过意不去，」他致歉道，「不过我还想再问您一个问题。您可否告诉我，惠特曼先生那次来访时，可曾见到您的合伙人？换句话说，倘若惠特曼先生遇上芒斯先生，能认出他来吗？」

===Original===

Mr. Dashwood raised his eyebrows, but he answered without hesitation.

===Chinese===

达什伍德先生扬了扬眉毛，但还是毫不迟疑地作了回答。

===Original===

“Mr. Whitman was shown in to me, and so far as I know, he did not meet my partner. But Mr. Munce is in his room. We can ask him.”

===Chinese===

「惠特曼先生是被直接领到我这里来的，据我所知，他没有见过我的合伙人。不过芒斯先生眼下就在他自己房里。我们可以去问问他。」

===Original===

The junior partner was a more good-natured looking man than Mr. Dashwood, and French was sorry he had not had to deal with him throughout.

===Chinese===

这位年轻合伙人看相貌比达什伍德先生和善得多，弗伦奇不禁暗自惋惜，此案从头到尾须要周旋的怎么不是这个人。

===Original===

“No, I didn’t see him,” he said with a pleasant smile. “As a matter of fact I was out at the time you mention. I went over﻿—” he looked at Dashwood﻿—“to see Troughton about eleven and I did not get back till after lunch.”

===Chinese===

「没有，我没有见过他。」他面带和气的微笑说道。「其实，您说的那个当口我恰好外出。我到——」他望了达什伍德一眼——「去看特劳顿（Troughton）了，十一点来钟去的，午饭之后才回来。」

===Original===

French nodded.

===Chinese===

弗伦奇点了点头。

===Original===

“Now, gentlemen,” he went on, “I am obliged for what you have told me and I am going to ask for your further help in this matter. What I want is very simple. If any letter or wire or telephone call comes to you from Whitman will you please advise me before replying? That is all.” He repeated to Mr. Munce what he had already told Mr. Dashwood as to his suspicion of Whitman’s criminality, stating that under the circumstances he felt sure he could count on the assistance of both gentlemen.

===Chinese===

「二位先生，」他接着说，「承蒙见告，我不胜感激，在这件事上我还要求你们再帮一个忙。我的要求非常简单：倘若惠特曼方面有信件、电报或电话寻到你们头上，请在作复之前先知会我。就这些。」接着，他把先前对达什伍德先生说过的、关于怀疑惠特曼犯罪的那些话，又对芒斯先生复述了一遍，并言明在此情形之下，他自信定能仰仗两位先生的协助。

===Original===

Mr. Dashwood hemmed and hawed and was inclined to demur. He was, he pointed out, a stockbroker, not a detective, and he didn’t see why he should be involved in Inspector French’s machinations. If the Inspector wished to make an arrest it was up to him to do it himself. But fortunately for French, Mr. Munce took the opposite view.

===Chinese===

达什伍德先生哼哼哈哈、支支吾吾，颇有推却之意。他申明自己是证券经纪人，不是侦探，不明白凭什么要把他牵进弗伦奇探长的机谋里来。探长若要拿人，尽可自己去拿。所幸于弗伦奇，芒斯先生所持的看法恰恰相反。

===Original===

“Oh, come now, Dashwood, hang it all,” he protested, “we’ll have to do what the Inspector wants. If this Whitman is a murderer we’re pretty well bound to. Besides, Mr. French doesn’t want us to make any move, only to sit tight and not spoil his plans. What do you say, now?”

===Chinese===

「嗳，得啦，达什伍德，真见鬼，」他抗声道，「探长要怎么办，我们就得怎么办。这惠特曼要真是杀人凶手，我们简直义不容辞。何况弗伦奇先生并非要我们有什么举动，只消稳坐不动、别搅坏他的计划就成。你说呢？」

===Original===

Mr. Dashwood made a gesture as if washing his hands of the whole affair, and announced stiffly that if his partner considered such action in accordance with the traditional relations between stockbroker and client he would not press his own views. Mr. Munce thereupon smiled genially at French and assured him that he could count on his wishes being carried out.

===Chinese===

达什伍德先生做了个手势，仿佛要把整桩事从自己手上洗刷干净，然后板着面孔郑重声明：倘若他的合伙人认为此举合乎证券经纪人与客户之间的传统关系，他便不再坚持己见。芒斯先生随即朝弗伦奇和蔼地一笑，向他保证，他的意愿必定照办不误。

===Original===

This was all right so far as it went, and it paved the way for French’s next proceeding. Going to the nearest telegraph office, he saw the postmaster, showed him his credentials, and explained that he wished to send a reply prepaid telegram, the answer to which was not to be delivered at its address, but was to be sent to him at Scotland Yard. Then drawing a form towards him he wrote:﻿—

===Chinese===

这一步尚属顺利，也为弗伦奇的下一步行动铺平了道路。他到最近的一家电报局去见了局长，出示证件，说明他要拍发一封回费预付的复电，回电不必按址投递，须改送苏格兰场交他本人。说罢，他把一张电报表格拉到面前，写道：——

===Original===

“To Whitman, care of Macdonald, 18 Moray Street, Pentland Avenue, Edinburgh.

“Serious fall in Brazilian stocks impending. Advise modification of plans. Would like an interview. Munce travels to Aberdeen by 10:00 a.m. from King’s Cross, Tuesday. Could you see him at Waverley where train waits from 6:15 to 6:33?

“Dashwood & Munce.”

===Chinese===

「惠特曼收，托麦克唐纳转交，爱丁堡，彭特兰大道莫里街 18 号。

「巴西股票行将大跌。建议变更原定计划。盼能面谈。芒斯星期二乘国王十字上午 10 点班车赴阿伯丁（Aberdeen），该车在韦弗利（Waverley）自 6 点 15 分停靠至 6 点 33 分，可否在站上见他一面？

「达什伍德－芒斯。」

===Original===

This, French thought, should draw Roper. Unless the man was extraordinarily well up in Brazilian politics, of which the chances were negligible, he would suspect nothing amiss. And if he did not suspect a trap he would almost certainly turn up. Not only would he really be anxious about his money, but he would see that it would be suspicious not to show such anxiety.

===Chinese===

弗伦奇想，这一封该能把罗珀引出来。除非此人对巴西政情熟到了异乎寻常的地步——而这种可能微乎其微——否则他不会察觉任何异样。而只要他不疑心这是个圈套，他几乎必定会露面。一来他确实放心不下自己的钱；二来他也看得出，不显出这份着急反倒惹人怀疑。

===Original===

All the same French believed that the telegram should be confirmed by a letter. In the ordinary course of business such a letter would necessarily follow, and Roper might notice the omission.

===Chinese===

尽管如此，弗伦奇认为电报之后还须有一封信加以证实。按照正常的业务惯例，这样一封信必然随之而来，罗珀也许会察觉这份缺漏。

===Original===

To ascertain the form of Messrs. Dashwood and Munce’s correspondence French adopted a simple expedient. He wrote confidentially to the firm saying he had just learnt that the man in whom he was interested had particularly small ears, and asking whether Mr. Dashwood had noticed Whitman’s. This letter he sent by hand and in an hour back came an answer. It took a comparatively short time to print a similar letter form, and on this French typed the following with the same coloured ribbon and spacing:﻿—

===Chinese===

为了查明达什伍德－芒斯经纪行往来函件的格式，弗伦奇用了个简单的法子。他写了一封密函给该行，说自己刚获悉他所关注的那个人耳朵特别小，请问达什伍德先生可曾留意惠特曼的耳朵。此信派专人递送，一个钟头后回音便到。仿印一份相仿的信笺没费多少工夫，弗伦奇便用同样颜色的色带、同样的字距，在上面打出下面这封信：——

===Original===

“Dear Sir,﻿—Confirming wire sent you today. We beg to state that we have just had confidential advices from our agents in Brazil, warning us that unsettled conditions are imminent which are likely to depress Government securities considerably. Under these circumstances we feel that we would like to discuss the question of your investments, as we think you would be wiser to modify your original proposals. In such matters a personal interview is more satisfactory than correspondence, and as Mr. Munce happens to be passing through Edinburgh next Tuesday, we thought perhaps it might be convenient to you to see him at the station. The train waits long enough to enable him to explain the situation fully.

Yours faithfully,”

===Chinese===

「敬启者：——兹确认今日所发之电。敬陈者：本公司顷接驻巴西代理人密报，谓动荡之局迫在眉睫，政府公债恐将因之大幅下挫。当此情形，敝行以为台端投资事宜亟宜从长计议，盖依敝见，台端若将原拟方案稍事变通，方为明智。此类事务，当面一谈总较函商为妥；适逢芒斯先生下星期二途经爱丁堡，或可仰烦台端在车站与之一晤。该车停靠甚久，尽够他将情形详加陈明。

「谨启」

===Original===

French copied the “Dashwood and Munce” signature and despatched the letter by the evening mail. He was in hopes that it would allay any suspicion the telegram might have raised in Roper’s mind, while at the same time involving no reply to the stockbrokers other than that of the prepaid wire which would be delivered at the Yard.

===Chinese===

弗伦奇临摹了「达什伍德－芒斯」的签名，把信交由晚间邮班发出。他指望这封信能化解电报可能在罗珀心中引起的疑虑，同时又不致引来对方给经纪行的任何回复——除了那封回费预付、将投到苏格兰场的电报。

===Original===

The next point to be considered was the matter of Roper’s identification. French did not believe he could manage this himself. He had never seen the man. He had, of course, a copy of the photograph on the passport, but he did not consider this sufficient. In a matter of such importance he dared not leave a loophole for mistake. He felt he must have someone who knew Roper there to assist him.

===Chinese===

下一个要考虑的是指认罗珀的问题。弗伦奇觉得自己独力应付不来。他从未见过此人。护照上的照片他固然有一份复印在案，但他认为这还不够。此案干系重大，他不敢留下半点致误的空隙。他觉得必须有个认识罗珀的人在场从旁相助。

===Original===

He thought at once of Ruth Averill. Of all the persons he had come across she probably knew Roper’s appearance best. But he felt the job was not one for a young girl and he cast round for someone else.

===Chinese===

他一下子便想到了露丝·埃弗里尔。在他打过交道的所有人当中，对罗珀的相貌知道的恐怕数她最详。但他觉得这桩差事不宜派给一个年轻姑娘，便四下里另觅人选。

===Original===

No one at Thirsby seemed suitable. Several people there had been acquainted with Roper, but he did not think any had known him sufficiently intimately to penetrate a disguise, should the man still be wearing one. Nor did he believe anyone at Kintilloch would be much better, though for a while he considered getting Sergeant McGregor.

===Chinese===

瑟斯比那边似乎没有合适的人。当地有几位与罗珀相识，但他认为谁都不够熟稔，万一那人依旧带着伪装，未必识得破。基蒂洛赫方面他也认为好不到哪里去，虽然有一阵子他考虑过调麦克格雷戈警士前来。

===Original===

Finally, he decided that he would ask Philpot. Philpot had known Roper intimately at the Ransome and had seen him at intervals up till the tragedy. He was now in Glasgow: nearer than anyone else that French could get. Moreover, Philpot hated Roper and would no doubt be glad to put the final spoke in his wheel. French was sure he would come for the asking.

===Chinese===

最后，他决定去请菲尔波特。菲尔波特在兰瑟姆疗养院时与罗珀过从甚密，此后直到惨案发生，还不时与他见面。他眼下正在格拉斯哥，是弗伦奇所能请到的人里最近便的一个。何况菲尔波特恨透了罗珀，无疑乐意向这家伙的车轮插进最后一根辐条。弗伦奇确信，只消开口一请，他必肯前来。

===Original===

Accordingly he drew a sheet of paper to him and wrote:﻿—

===Chinese===

于是他拉过一张信纸，写道：——

===Original===

“*Strictly private and confidential,*

“New Scotland Yard.

“Dear Dr. Philpot,﻿—You will be surprised to hear from me, and particularly to learn that I believe I have got my hands on the man wanted for the affair I have been working on. I do not wish to give details in a letter, but it is a man whom you know well and whom we all thought to be dead. You can probably guess from this.

“We have found that under an alias he has been transferring his money abroad, and in the name of the stockbrokers concerned I have asked him to meet their junior partner at Waverley Station, Edinburgh, on Tuesday next at 6:15 p.m. on the arrival of the 10:00 a.m. from King’s Cross. The junior partner will not be there, but I shall, and I hope to make the arrest.

“My difficulty is that I cannot myself identify the wanted man. In this I want your kind help. Will you please meet me under Scott’s Monument at 5:00 p.m.? I shall then ask you to accompany me to the station and from some inconspicuous place keep a lookout for him. When you see him you will tell me and I shall do the rest.

“I ask you to assist me in this, and feel sure that when you consider all the circumstances of the case you will agree to do so.

“Will you please wire your decision on receipt of this letter.

“Yours faithfully,

“Joseph French.”

===Chinese===

「*绝密亲启*

「新苏格兰场。

「菲尔波特医生台鉴：——忽接此信，您必感诧异，及至听说我已将手头案件所要捉拿的人控制在握，想必更觉意外。其中详情不便笔之于书，只能奉告：此人您极为熟识，而我们众人都以为他已死去。由此您大概猜得到是谁。

「我们查明，此人正以化名陆续将钱财转往国外。我已借涉事证券经纪行之名，约他于下星期二下午 6 点 15 分——国王十字上午 10 点班车抵达之时——在爱丁堡韦弗利车站与该行年轻合伙人相见。届时那位合伙人并不会到场，我却在场，并指望当场将人拘捕。

「我的难处是，本人无法指认这名通缉之人。这一点须仰仗您鼎力相助。可否请您下午 5 点在司各特纪念碑（Scott's Monument）下与我相会？届时我将请您陪我同赴车站，从一处不惹眼的地方监视来人。您一见到他，便告知于我，其余自有我来料理。

「我求您相助此举，并深信您把全案情由权衡一遍之后，定会慨然应允。

「接信之后，请即电复您的决定。

「您忠实的，

「约瑟夫·弗伦奇。」

===Original===

For the next few hours French was like the proverbial hen on the hot griddle. Every time his telephone bell rang he snatched up the receiver hoping that the caller was the post office from which he had sent his message. Every time the door opened he looked up eagerly to see if it was not an orange coloured telegraph message that was being brought in. He found it hard to settle to work, so much depended on his plans succeeding.

===Chinese===

此后几个钟头，弗伦奇活像俗话说的热锅上的蚂蚁。电话铃每响一回，他都一把抓起听筒，巴望是发出电报的那个邮局打来的。房门每开一回，他都急切抬头，看送进来的莫非正是一纸橙色封皮的电报。他安不下心来做事——成败所系，全在这套计划上。

===Original===

When, therefore, about four in the afternoon a wire was brought to him, he had to exercise real self-control not to snatch the paper from the messenger. And then he could have laughed with delight. The message had been handed in at the General Post Office in Edinburgh, and read:﻿—

===Chinese===

因此，下午四点来钟一封电报送来时，他只得着实克制，才没有从信差手里把电文一把抢过。待到看完，他简直要乐出声来。电报是在爱丁堡邮政总局（General Post Office）交发的，全文如下：——

===Original===

“To Dashwood and Munce,

“Dover House,

					“Gracechurch Street,

“Your wire. Will meet Munce as suggested.

“Whitman.”

===Chinese===

「致达什伍德－芒斯，

「多佛大楼（Dover House），

					「格雷教堂街，

「来电敬悉。谨如所约与芒斯一晤。

「惠特曼。」

===Original===

So far, so very excellent! Here was the major difficulty overcome! On Tuesday evening the public career of John Roper would come to a sudden stop. The end of the case was at last in sight.

===Chinese===

到现在为止，一切都好到了家！最大的难关已经闯过！星期二晚上，约翰·罗珀的公开生涯便将戛然而止。这桩案子的尽头总算望见了。

===Original===

Early the next morning a second telegram was handed to French, which gave him almost equally great satisfaction. It was from Philpot and read:﻿—

===Chinese===

次日一早，第二封电报递到弗伦奇手中，给他的快慰几乎与前一封不相上下。电报是菲尔波特发来的，全文如下：——

===Original===

“Will meet you place and time stated.”

===Chinese===

「谨遵所示地点时间赴约。」

===Original===

There was now just one other point to be settled. Roper was coming to the station to meet Munce. But Munce was not going to Edinburgh. Someone must therefore take his place.

===Chinese===

眼下只剩一个问题还须解决。罗珀要来车站见芒斯，可芒斯并不去爱丁堡。因此总得有人顶替他。

===Original===

It would be better to have someone as like Munce in appearance as possible. In spite of the statement of the partners, Roper might have got a glimpse of Munce or at least have had his description. In view of this very summons he might make it his business to learn what the man was like. French considered his brother officers and he soon saw that Inspector Tanner, with a slight makeup, could present himself as a very passable imitation of the junior partner. The men were about the same build and colouring, and an alteration in the cut of Tanner’s hair, a pair of spectacles, different clothes and a change of manner would do all that was necessary.

===Chinese===

顶替的人外貌最好与芒斯尽量相像。两位合伙人虽有那番话，罗珀仍有可能瞥见过芒斯，至少也曾得到过他的相貌描述。何况冲着这封电召，他很可能特意去打听此人生得什么模样。弗伦奇把同僚们逐一掂量，很快看出坦纳探长（Inspector Tanner）只消略施化装，便可充作那位年轻合伙人的一个相当过得去的替身。两人身量、肤色大致相仿，只要改一改坦纳的发式，添一副眼镜，换一身衣服，再变一变举止神态，便一切齐备了。

===Original===

French went to Tanner’s room and arranged the matter. Tanner was to call and see Munce on some matter of a prospective investment which would afterwards fall through, and while there observe his model. He would then make himself up and travel to Edinburgh by the 10:00 a.m. from King’s Cross. On reaching Waverley he would cooperate with French as circumstances demanded.

===Chinese===

弗伦奇到坦纳房里把事情安排妥当。坦纳要借一桩日后作罢的投资事宜前去拜访芒斯，趁便端详他的原版。随后他便化装停当，乘国王十字上午 10 点的班车前往爱丁堡。一到韦弗利，便视情势之需与弗伦奇合力行事。

===Original===

To enable him to keep his appointment with Philpot, French found he must leave London on the Monday night. He therefore took the 11:35 p.m. from Euston, and about eight o’clock the next morning reached Princes Street Station. He had not been to Edinburgh for years, and emerging from the station, he was struck afresh with the beauty of the gardens and the splendour of the Castle Rock. But Princes Street itself, which he had once thought so magnificent, seemed to have shrunk, and its buildings to have grown smaller and plainer. “Too much foreign travel,” he thought, vaguely regretful of his change of outlook; “the towns abroad certainly spoil one for ours.”

===Chinese===

为了不误与菲尔波特的约会，弗伦奇觉得必须在星期一夜里离开伦敦。于是他搭上尤斯顿（Euston）开出的夜里 11 点 35 分班车，次日早晨八点来钟到了王子街车站（Princes Street Station）。他多年没来爱丁堡了；走出车站，园圃之美、城堡岩（Castle Rock）之壮丽，又叫他耳目一新。只是王子街本身——他当年觉得何等壮观——似乎缩小了，街上的楼房也变得矮小朴素了。「在国外跑得太多啦，」他心中暗想，对自己眼光的改变隐约有些怅惘；「外国的城市真能把人惯坏，回头再看我们自己的就瞧不上眼了。」

===Original===

He spent most of the day in exploring the historic buildings of the old town, then as five o’clock approached he entered the Princes Street Gardens, and strolling towards Scott’s Monument, took his stand in an inconspicuous place and looked around him.

===Chinese===

这一天的大半工夫，他都用来寻访旧城里的历史建筑。将近五点，他走进王子街花园（Princes Street Gardens），信步朝司各特纪念碑踱去，拣了个不惹眼的地方站定，朝四下里观望。

===Original===

Almost immediately he saw Philpot.
