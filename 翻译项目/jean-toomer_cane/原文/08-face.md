## Face
Hair﻿—
 

 silver-gray,
 

 like streams of stars,
 

 Brows﻿—
 

 recurved canoes
 

 quivered by the ripples blown by pain,
 

 Her eyes﻿—
 

 mist of tears
 

 condensing on the flesh below
 

 And her channeled muscles
 

 are cluster grapes of sorrow
 

 purple in the evening sun
 

 nearly ripe for worms.
