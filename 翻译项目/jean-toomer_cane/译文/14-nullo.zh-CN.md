## Nullo / 空无

===Original===
A spray of pine-needles,
 

 Dipped in western horizon gold,
 

 Fell onto a path.
 

 Dry moulds of cow-hoofs.
 

 In the forest.
 

 Rabbits knew not of their falling,
 

 Nor did the forest catch aflame.
===Chinese===
一蓬松针，
 

 蘸了西天际的金色，
 

 落在一条小径上。
 

 干了的牛蹄印。
 

 在林子里。
 

 兔子们不晓得它们落下，
 

 林子也没有烧起来。
