## Carma / 卡玛

===Original===
> Wind is in the cane. Come along.
 

 Cane leaves swaying, rusty with talk,
 

 Scratching choruses above the guinea’s squawk,
 

 Wind is in the cane. Come along.
===Chinese===
> 风在甘蔗林里。来吧。
 

 甘蔗叶摇曳，絮语沙沙如锈，
 

 在珍珠鸡的尖啼之上刮擦出合唱，

===Original===
Carma, in overalls, and strong as any man, stands behind the old brown mule, driving the wagon home. It bumps, and groans, and shakes as it crosses the railroad track. She, riding it easy. I leave the men around the stove to follow her with my eyes down the red dust road. Nigger woman driving a Georgia chariot down an old dust road. Dixie Pike is what they call it. Maybe she feels my gaze, perhaps she expects it. Anyway, she turns. The sun, which has been slanting over her shoulder, shoots primitive rockets into her mangrove-gloomed, yellow flower face. Hi! Yip! God has left the Moses-people for the nigger. “Gedap.” Using reins to slap the mule, she disappears in a cloudy rumble at some indefinite point along the road.
===Chinese===
卡玛穿着工装裤，壮得赛过任何男人，站在那头老棕骡子身后，赶着大车回家。大车过铁轨时颠着、呻吟着、抖着。她稳稳地坐着。我撇下围着炉子的男人们，目光顺着红色的尘土路追她而去。黑鬼婆子赶着一辆佐治亚战车，走在一条破旧的尘土路上。迪克西大道，他们是这么叫它的。兴许她觉出了我的目光，兴许她正盼着。反正，她回过头来。一直在她肩头斜照的太阳，把一支支原始的焰火射进她那张蒙着红树幽影、开着黄花的脸。嗨！呀呼！上帝丢下了摩西的子民，奔黑人来了。“得儿驾。”她用缰绳抽了一下骡子，在一阵云雾似的轰响里，顺着大路上某个说不清的地点消失了。

===Original===
(The sun is hammered to a band of gold. Pine-needles, like mazda, are brilliantly aglow. No rain has come to take the rustle from the falling sweet-gum leaves. Over in the forest, across the swamp, a sawmill blows its closing whistle. Smoke curls up. Marvelous web spun by the spider sawdust pile. Curls up and spreads itself pine-high above the branch, a single silver band along the eastern valley. A black boy﻿ ﻿… you are the most sleepiest man I ever seed, Sleeping Beauty﻿ ﻿… cradled on a gray mule, guided by the hollow sound of cowbells, heads for them through a rusty cotton field. From down the railroad track, the chug-chug of a gas engine announces that the repair gang is coming home. A girl in the yard of a whitewashed shack not much larger than the stack of worn ties piled before it, sings. Her voice is loud. Echoes, like rain, sweep the valley. Dusk takes the polish from the rails. Lights twinkle in scattered houses. From far away, a sad strong song. Pungent and composite, the smell of farmyards is the fragrance of the woman. She does not sing; her body is a song. She is in the forest, dancing. Torches flare﻿ ﻿… juju men, greegree, witch-doctors﻿ ﻿… torches go out﻿ ﻿… The Dixie Pike has grown from a goat path in Africa.
*Night.*
Foxie, the bitch, slicks back her ears and barks at the rising moon.)
===Chinese===
（太阳被锻打成一条金带。松针像马自达灯，灼灼放光。没有雨水来带走枫香落叶的窸窣。那边的林子里，沼泽对面，一座锯木厂吹响收工的哨子。烟卷起来。锯末堆这只蜘蛛织出的奇妙罗网。卷起来，铺散到松树那么高，在东边的山谷上成为孤零零的一条银带。一个黑孩子……你真是我见过的顶顶瞌睡的人了，睡美人……伏在一头灰骡子背上，循着牛铃空洞的声响，穿过一片锈色的棉田朝牛群去。从铁路那头下来，一台汽油发动机突突地响着，宣告养路班收工回家。一个姑娘站在刷白的棚屋院子里唱着歌——那屋子比堆在跟前的旧枕木垛大不了多少。她的嗓门很大。回声像雨，扫过山谷。黄昏磨去了铁轨的光。零落的屋子里灯火眨眼。远处传来一支忧伤而雄浑的歌。农家庭院那股辛辣而混杂的气味，是女人的芬芳。她没有唱歌；她的身体就是一支歌。她在林子里，跳着舞。火把熊熊……巫公们，格里格里符咒，巫医……火把灭了……迪克西大道，是从非洲一条山羊小道长出来的。）
*夜。*
母狗福克西抿平耳朵，冲着升起的月亮吠叫。）

===Original===
Carma’s tale is the crudest melodrama. Her husband’s in the gang. And its her fault he got there. Working with a contractor, he was away most of the time. She had others. No one blames her for that. He returned one day and hung around the town where he picked up week-old boasts and rumors﻿ ﻿… Bane accused her. She denied. He couldnt see that she was becoming hysterical. He would have liked to take his fists and beat her. Who was strong as a man. Stronger. Words, like corkscrews, wormed to her strength. It fizzled out. Grabbing a gun, she rushed from the house and plunged across the road into a canebrake﻿ ﻿… There, in quarter heaven shone the crescent moon﻿ ﻿… Bane was afraid to follow till he heard the gun go off. Then he wasted half an hour gathering the neighbor men. They met in the road where lamplight showed tracks dissolving in the loose earth about the cane. The search began. Moths flickered the lamps. They put them out. Really, because she still might be live enough to shoot. Time and space have no meaning in a canefield. No more than the interminable stalks﻿ ﻿… Someone stumbled over her. A cry went up. From the road, one would have thought that they were cornering a rabbit or a skunk﻿ ﻿… It is difficult carrying dead weight through cane. They placed her on the sofa. A curious, nosey somebody looked for the wound. This fussing with her clothes aroused her. Her eyes were weak and pitiable for so strong a woman. Slowly, then like a flash, Bane came to know that the shot she fired, with averted head, was aimed to whistle like a dying hornet through the cane. Twice deceived, and one deception proved the other. His head went off. Slashed one of the men who’d helped, the man who’d stumbled over her. Now he’s in the gang. Who was her husband. Should she not take others, this Carma, strong as a man, whose tale as I have told it is the crudest melodrama?
===Chinese===
卡玛的故事，是一出再粗糙不过的情节剧。她丈夫进了劳役队。他落到那步田地，得怪她。他跟一个包工头干活，多半时候不在家。她有了别人。这没人怪她。有一天他回来了，在镇上闲晃，捡起个把星期前的牛皮和风闻……班恩数落她。她抵赖。他看不出她正在发作癔症。他真想抡起拳头揍她。揍她这个壮得赛过男人的女人。比男人还壮。话语像开瓶钻，一寸一寸钻向她的力气。力气泄了。她抓起一杆枪冲出屋子，横过大路扎进甘蔗丛……那里，一方天堂里悬着新月……班恩不敢跟进去，直到听见枪响。随后他耗了半个钟头去张罗邻居的男人。他们在路上碰头，灯光照出甘蔗周围松土里渐渐漫漶的脚印。搜寻开始了。飞蛾扑得灯火直晃。他们把灯熄了。说真的，是因为她兴许还活着，还能开枪。时间和空间在甘蔗地里没有意义。跟那没完没了的秸秆一样没意义……有人被她绊了一下。一声喊了起来。从路上听，还当他们围住了一只兔子或一只臭鼬……抬着死沉的分量穿过甘蔗地不容易。他们把她放倒在长沙发上。某个好奇的、鼻子灵的家伙找伤口。这一翻动她的衣裳，把她惊醒了。她的眼睛又弱又可怜，不像这么强壮的女人会有的。慢慢地，随后像一道闪光，班恩终于明白：她那一枪，是偏着头放的，为的是让它像一只垂死的黄蜂，呜地一声穿过甘蔗。他两回受了骗，而一重骗局给另一重作了证。他的脑袋炸了。他砍伤了帮过忙的人中的一个，就是绊倒在她身上的那个。如今他进了劳役队。他，原是她的丈夫。这样的卡玛——壮得赛过男人，她的故事如我所讲是一出再粗糙不过的情节剧——难道不该另找别人吗？
