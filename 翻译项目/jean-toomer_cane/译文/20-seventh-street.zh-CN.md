## Seventh Street / 第七街

===Original===
> Money burns the pocket, pocket hurts,
 

 Bootleggers in silken shirts,
 

 Ballooned, zooming Cadillacs,
 

 Whizzing, whizzing down the street-car tracks.

===Chinese===
> 钱烧着口袋，口袋疼， 

 穿绸衬衫的私酒贩子， 

 鼓胀着、轰鸣着的凯迪拉克， 

 嗖嗖、嗖嗖地掠过电车轨道。

===Original===
Seventh Street is a bastard of Prohibition and the War. A crude-boned, soft-skinned wedge of nigger life breathing its loafer air, jazz songs and love, thrusting unconscious rhythms, black reddish blood into the white and whitewashed wood of Washington. Stale soggy wood of Washington. Wedges rust in soggy wood﻿ ﻿… Split it! In two! Again! Shred it!﻿ ﻿… the sun. Wedges are brilliant in the sun; ribbons of wet wood dry and blow away. Black reddish blood. Pouring for crude-boned soft-skinned life, who set you flowing? Blood suckers of the War would spin in a frenzy of dizziness if they drank your blood. Prohibition would put a stop to it. Who set you flowing? White and whitewash disappear in blood. Who set you flowing? Flowing down the smooth asphalt of Seventh Street, in shanties, brick office buildings, theaters, drug stores, restaurants, and cabarets? Eddying on the corners? Swirling like a bloodred smoke up where the buzzards fly in heaven? God would not dare to suck black red blood. A Nigger God! He would duck his head in shame and call for the Judgment Day. Who set you flowing?

===Chinese===
第七街是禁酒令和大战生下的野种。一块粗骨头、软皮肤的楔子——黑鬼的生活，喘着二流子的空气、爵士歌和爱，把无意识的节律、黑里透红的血，楔进华盛顿刷得雪白的白木头里。华盛顿发潮发朽的木头。楔子会在湿木头里生锈……劈开它！一分为二！再劈！撕碎它！……太阳。楔子在太阳底下亮晃晃的；一条条湿木片干了，随风飘走。黑里透红的血。为这粗骨软皮的生命淌着，是谁让你流起来的？大战的吸血鬼们要是喝了你的血，会晕得天旋地转。禁酒令会来叫停。是谁让你流起来的？白人和白粉漆在血里消失。是谁让你流起来的？顺着第七街平滑的柏油淌，淌进棚屋、砖砌写字楼、剧院、药房、饭馆和卡巴莱？在街角打着旋？像一股血红的烟，旋上秃鹫在天上飞的地方？上帝都不敢吸黑红的血。一个黑鬼上帝！他会羞得低下头，召唤审判日。是谁让你流起来的？
