## Appendix B The “Round Robin” Letter / 附录 B 「联名信」

===Original===
[The following is the report of the Associated Press correspondent of the “round-robin” incident. It is literally true in every detail. I was present when he was handed both letters; he was present while they were being written.]

**Santiago de Cuba**, August 3rd (delayed in transmission).—Summoned by Major-General Shafter, a meeting was held here this morning at headquarters, and in the presence of every commanding and medical officer of the Fifth Army Corps, General Shafter read a cable message from Secretary Alger, ordering him, on the recommendation of Surgeon-General Sternberg, to move the army into the interior, to San Luis, where it is healthier.

As a result of the conference General Shafter will insist upon the immediate withdrawal of the army North.

As an explanation of the situation the following letter from Colonel Theodore Roosevelt, commanding the First Cavalry, to General Shafter, was handed by the latter to the correspondent of the Associated Press for publication:

===Chinese===
［以下是美联社（Associated Press）记者对「联名信」（round-robin）事件的报道。其中每一个细节都千真万确。两封信递交到他手上时，我均在场；两信执笔之际，他也在场。］

**古巴圣地亚哥**，8 月 3 日（拍发延误）。——应沙夫特少将（Major-General Shafter）之召，今晨在军部召开会议，第五军（Fifth Army Corps）全体指挥官与军医官悉数在场，沙夫特将军当众宣读了阿尔杰部长（Secretary Alger）拍来的电报：命他依照军医署署长斯特恩伯格（Surgeon-General Sternberg）的建议，将全军开入内地圣路易斯（San Luis），称那里更有益于健康。

会议的结果是，沙夫特将军将力主全军立即北撤。

为说明当前局势，沙夫特将军把第一义勇骑兵团团长西奥多·罗斯福上校（Colonel Theodore Roosevelt）写给他的下述信函，交给了美联社记者公开发表：

===Original===
> **Sir**: In a meeting of the general and medical officers called by you at the Palace this morning we were all, as you know, unanimous in our views of what should be done with the army. To keep us here, in the opinion of every officer commanding a division or a brigade, will simply involve the destruction of thousands. There is no possible reason for not shipping practically the entire command North at once. Yellow-fever cases are very few in the cavalry division, where I command one of the two brigades, and not one true case of yellow fever has occurred in this division, except among the men sent to the hospital at Siboney, where they have, I believe, contracted it.

> But in this division there have been 1,500 cases of malarial fever. Hardly a man has yet died from it, but the whole command is so weakened and shattered as to be ripe for dying like rotten sheep, when a real yellow-fever epidemic instead of a fake epidemic, like the present one, strikes us, as it is bound to do if we stay here at the height of the sickness season, August and the beginning of September. Quarantine against malarial fever is much like quarantining against the toothache.

> All of us are certain that as soon as the authorities at Washington fully appreciate the condition of the army, we shall be sent home. If we are kept here it will in all human possibility mean an appalling disaster, for the surgeons here estimate that over half the army, if kept here during the sickly season, will die.

> This is not only terrible from the standpoint of the individual lives lost, but it means ruin from the standpoint of military efficiency of the flower of the American army, for the great bulk of the regulars are here with you. The sick list, large though it is, exceeding four thousand, affords but a faint index of the debilitation of the army. Not twenty percent are fit for active work.

===Chinese===
> **长官**：今晨您在总督府召集将官与军医官会议，正如您所知，对于这支军队应当何去何从，我们全体意见一致。在每一位师长、旅长看来，再把我们留在此地，无异于坐视数千人走向毁灭。没有任何理由不把几乎是全军的部队立刻船运北上。骑兵师——我在该师指挥两个旅中的一个——黄热病（yellow fever）病例寥寥无几；全师除送锡博内（Siboney）医院的士兵外，没有发生过一例真正的黄热病，而那些人，我相信正是在医院里染上的。

> 但这个师里的疟疾（malarial fever）患者已有 1,500 例。至今几乎没有人死于此病，可是全军已被它拖得虚弱涣散，恰如一群烂羊，到了成批倒毙的火候；一旦真正的黄热病大流行——而非眼下这场假流行——向我们袭来，我们便只有倒毙一途，而我们只要留在这里，熬过疫病最猖獗的八月和九月初，这场大流行就必然降临。对疟疾实施检疫隔离，同对牙痛实施检疫隔离没有多大分别。

> 我们全都确信：华盛顿当局一旦充分了解这支军队的现状，就会把我们送回国内。倘若继续把我们留在这里，按人事之常理推断，那将意味着一场骇人听闻的大灾难，因为此间军医估计，全军若在疫季滞留于此，将有一半以上的人死去。

> 这场灾祸，不仅就一条条个人生命的丧失而言是惨剧，就美军精华的战斗效率而言更意味着毁灭，因为正规军的主体此刻都随您在此。病员名册虽已逾四千人、堪称庞大，却只是全军虚弱程度的一个淡影。全军适于执行作战任务的，不足百分之二十。

===Original===
> Six weeks on the North Maine coast, for instance, or elsewhere where the yellow-fever germ cannot possibly propagate, would make us all as fit as fighting-cocks, as able as we are eager to take a leading part in the great campaign against Havana in the fall, even if we are not allowed to try Puerto Rico.

> We can be moved North, if moved at once, with absolute safety to the country, although, of course, it would have been infinitely better if we had been moved North or to Puerto Rico two weeks ago. If there were any object in keeping us here, we would face yellow fever with as much indifference as we faced bullets. But there is no object.

> The four immune regiments ordered here are sufficient to garrison the city and surrounding towns, and there is absolutely nothing for us to do here, and there has not been since the city surrendered. It is impossible to move into the interior. Every shifting of camp doubles the sick-rate in our present weakened condition, and, anyhow, the interior is rather worse than the coast, as I have found by actual reconnaissance. Our present camps are as healthy as any camps at this end of the island can be.

> I write only because I cannot see our men, who have fought so bravely and who have endured extreme hardship and danger so uncomplainingly, go to destruction without striving so far as lies in me to avert a doom as fearful as it is unnecessary and undeserved.

===Chinese===
> 比如说，只消在缅因州北部海岸，或在黄热病病菌绝无可能孳生的其他任何地方驻上六个星期，我们就会人人调养得精壮如斗鸡，人人既有热望、也有能力在秋季讨伐哈瓦那（Havana）的大战役中担当重要角色——即便不获准去波多黎各一试也罢。

> 只要立即启运，全军北撤对国内绝无危险；当然，倘若两星期前就把我们北撤或送往波多黎各，那不知要好上多少倍。倘若留驻此地还有什么目的，我们尽可以像当年直面子弹一样漠然直面黄热病。可是，毫无目的可言。

> 奉调来此的四个免疫团（immune regiments）足以守备这座城市和周围各镇，我们在此地绝对无事可做，而且自城市投降以来就一直无事可做。开入内地是不可能的。以我们眼下虚弱的状况，每挪一次营地，病员率就要翻上一番；何况内地比海岸还要糟些，这是我亲自侦察后得知的。我们眼下的营地，已是这座岛这一端的任何营地所能指望的最卫生的营地了。

> 我写这封信，只因不忍眼睁睁看着我们的士兵——他们打得那么英勇，忍受极度的艰辛与险阻又是那么毫无怨言——走向毁灭，而我却不竭尽自己力之所及，去阻止这场可怕、无谓而不应得的厄运。

===Original===
> Yours respectfully,

> Theodore Roosevelt, 

 *Colonel Commanding Second Cavalry Brigade*.

罗斯福上校带头之后，全体美军将官又联署了一封致沙夫特将军的「联名信」。信文如下：

===Original===
> We, the undersigned officers commanding the various brigades, divisions, etc., of the Army of Occupation in Cuba, are of the unanimous opinion that this army should be at once taken out of the island of Cuba and sent to some point on the Northern seacoast of the United States; that can be done without danger to the people of the United States; that yellow fever in the army at present is not epidemic; that there are only a few sporadic cases; but that the army is disabled by malarial fever to the extent that its efficiency is destroyed, and that it is in a condition to be practically entirely destroyed by an epidemic of yellow fever, which is sure to come in the near future.

> We know from the reports of competent officers and from personal observations that the army is unable to move into the interior, and that there are no facilities for such a move if attempted, and that it could not be attempted until too late. Moreover, the best medical authorities of the island say that with our present equipment we could not live in the interior during the rainy season without losses from malarial fever, which is almost as deadly as yellow fever.

> This army must be moved at once, or perish. As the army can be safely moved now, the persons responsible for preventing such a move will be responsible for the unnecessary loss of many thousands of lives.

> Our opinions are the result of careful personal observation, and they are also based on the unanimous opinion of our medical officers with the army, who understand the situation absolutely.

===Chinese===
> 我们，下列联署的驻古巴占领军（Army of Occupation）各旅、各师等部队的指挥官，一致认为：这支军队应立即撤出古巴岛，送往合众国北部沿岸的某一地点；此举办得到，且于合众国人民绝无危险；军中目前的黄热病尚未形成流行疫情，仅有少数散发病例；但全军已因疟疾而陷于瘫废，战斗力尽毁，且以现状而论，一场黄热病大流行——它在不久的将来必将来临——足以把全军实际上断送净尽。

> 我们从称职军官的报告和亲身观察得知：这支军队无力开入内地；纵然尝试，也全无转运设施可用；而且等到能够尝试之日，必已为时太晚。况且，本岛最权威的医学人士断言，以我们现有的装备，雨季期间驻扎内地，疟疾造成的减员在所难免——而疟疾几乎与黄热病同样致命。

> 这支军队必须立即转移，否则唯有灭亡。既然此刻转移尚可确保安全，那么凡阻挠此举的人，就要对成千上万条性命的无谓丧失负责。

> 我们的意见出自审慎的亲身观察，同时也基于随军军医官的一致判断——他们对局势的了解，透彻无疑。

===Original===
Major M. W. Wood, the chief Surgeon of the First Division, said: “The army must be moved North,” adding, with emphasis, “or it will be unable to move itself.”

General Ames has sent the following cable message to Washington:

> This army is incapable, because of sickness, of marching anywhere except to the transports. If it is ever to return to the United States it must do so at once.

===Chinese===
第一师主任军医 M·W·伍德少校（Major M. W. Wood）说：「这支军队必须北撤」，随后又加重语气补上一句：「否则它将连自己都挪不动了。」

埃姆斯将军已向华盛顿发出如下电报：

> 这支军队因病已无力向任何地方行军，唯有朝运输船走去还办得到。它若还打算返回合众国，就必须立刻动身。
