===Original===
On behalf of the Rough Riders

 I dedicate this book

 to the officers and men of the

 five regular regiments

 which together with mine made up the

 cavalry division at Santiago
Theodore Roosevelt
Executive Mansion

 Albany, New York

 May 1, 1899
