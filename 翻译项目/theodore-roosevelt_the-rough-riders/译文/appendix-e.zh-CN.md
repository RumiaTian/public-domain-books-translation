## Appendix E / 附录 E

===Original===
> **Dear Mr. President**: As some discussion has arisen in the public prints regarding the battle of San Juan, Cuba, July 1, 1898, and your personal movements during that day have been the subject of comment, it may not be amiss in me to state some facts coming under my personal observation as Commanding General of the Cavalry Division, of which your regiment formed a part. It will, perhaps, be advisable to show first how I came to be in command, in order that my statement may have due weight as an authoritative statement of facts: I was placed in command of the Cavalry Division on the afternoon of June 30th by General Shafter; the assignment was made owing to the severe illness of General Wheeler, who was the permanent commander of said Division. Brigadier General Young, who commanded the Second Cavalry Brigade, of which your regiment﻿—the First Volunteer Cavalry﻿—formed a part, was also very ill, and I found it necessary to relieve him from command and place Colonel Wood, of the Rough Riders, in command of the Brigade; this change placed you in command of your regiment.

===Chinese===
> **总统先生**：报端就 1898 年 7 月 1 日的古巴圣胡安之战已有若干议论，您当日的一应行止又成为众人评说的题目。那么，由我来陈述若干亲见的事实，或不为无益——我时任骑兵师（Cavalry Division）师长，您的团正是该师所属部队。为使我这篇陈述作为权威事实之谈具有应有的分量，或许宜先说明我如何受命指挥：6 月 30 日下午，沙夫特将军（General Shafter）委我指挥骑兵师；此番委任，缘于该师原任师长惠勒将军（General Wheeler）病重。指挥第二骑兵旅（Second Cavalry Brigade）的扬准将（Brigadier General Young）——您的团，即第一义勇骑兵团（First Volunteer Cavalry），隶属该旅——同样病得不轻，我只得解除他的指挥，改派莽骑兵（Rough Riders）的伍德上校（Colonel Wood）接掌该旅；这一变动，便使您得以执掌您的团。

===Original===
> The Division moved from its camp on the evening of June 30th, and bivouacked at and about El Poso. I saw you personally in the vicinity of El Poso, about 8 a.m., July 1st. I saw you again on the road leading from El Poso to the San Juan River; you were at the head of your regiment, which was leading the Second Brigade, and immediately behind the rear regiment of the First Brigade. My orders were to turn to the right at San Juan River and take up a line along that stream and try and connect with General Lawton, who was to engage the enemy at El Caney. On reaching the river we came under the fire of the Spanish forces posted on San Juan Ridge and Kettle Hill. The First Brigade was faced to the front in line as soon as it had cleared the road, and the Second Brigade was ordered to pass in rear of the first, and face to the front when clear of the First Brigade. This movement was very difficult owing to the heavy undergrowth, and the regiments became more or less tangled up, but eventually the formation was accomplished, and the Division stood in an irregular line along the San Juan River, the Second Brigade on the right. We were subjected to a heavy fire from the forces on San Juan Ridge and Kettle Hill; our position was untenable, and it became necessary to assault the enemy or fall back. Kettle Hill was immediately in front of the Cavalry, and it was determined to assault that hill. The First Brigade was ordered forward, and the Second Brigade was ordered to support the attack; personally, I accompanied a portion of the Tenth Cavalry, Second Brigade, and the Rough Riders were to the right. This brought your regiment to the right of the house which was at the summit of the hill. Shortly after I reached the crest of the hill you came to me, accompanied, I think, by Captain C. J. Stevens, of the Ninth Cavalry. We were then in a position to see the line of intrenchments along San Juan Ridge, and could see Kent’s Infantry Division engaged on our left, and Hawkins’ assault against Fort San Juan. You asked me for permission to move forward and assault San Juan Ridge. I gave you the order in person to move forward, and I saw you move forward and assault San Juan Ridge with your regiment and portions of the First and Tenth Cavalry belonging to your Brigade. I held a portion of the Second Brigade as a reserve on Kettle Hill, not knowing what force the enemy might have in reserve behind the ridge. The First Brigade also moved forward and assaulted the ridge to the right of Fort San Juan. There was a small lake between Kettle Hill and San Juan Ridge, and in moving forward your command passed to the right of this lake. This brought you opposite a house on San Juan Ridge﻿—not Fort San Juan proper, but a frame house surrounded by an earthwork. The enemy lost a number of men at this point, whose bodies lay in the trenches. Later in the day I rode along the line, and, as I recall it, a portion of the Tenth Cavalry was immediately about this house, and your regiment occupied an irregular semicircular position along the ridge and immediately to the right of the house. You had pickets out to your front; and several hundred yards to your front the Spaniards had a heavy outpost occupying a house, with rifle pits surrounding it. Later in the day, and during the following day, the various regiments forming the Division were rearranged and brought into tactical formation, the First Brigade on the left and immediately to the right of Fort San Juan, and the Second Brigade on the right of the First.

===Chinese===
> 6 月 30 日傍晚，骑兵师开拔离营，在埃尔波索（El Poso）及其周边露营。7 月 1 日上午 8 时左右，我在埃尔波索附近亲眼见到您。其后，在由埃尔波索通往圣胡安河（San Juan River）的路上，我又见到您；您走在本团最前面，本团担任第二旅的先头，紧跟在第一旅殿后那个团的身后。我奉到的命令是：在圣胡安河畔右转，沿该河一线展开，并设法与劳顿将军（General Lawton）取得联络——他负责在埃尔卡内（El Caney）与敌交战。抵达河边时，我们便落入据守圣胡安山岭（San Juan Ridge）与水壶山（Kettle Hill）的西班牙军队的火力之下。第一旅一让开道路，随即面向前方列成横队；第二旅奉命从第一旅身后通过，待完全越过第一旅后再面向前方。灌木丛生，这一机动极为困难，各团多少纠缠到了一起，但阵形终告完成，全师沿圣胡安河摆成一条不规则的战线，第二旅居右。我们遭到圣胡安山岭和水壶山上敌军的猛烈射击；我们的阵地无法立足，若不强攻敌军，便只得后撤。水壶山正当骑兵正前方，于是决定强攻此山。第一旅奉命前进，第二旅奉命支援攻击；我本人随第二旅第十骑兵团（Tenth Cavalry）的一部行动，莽骑兵在右。这就把您的团带到了山顶那所房子的右侧。我登上山巅后不久，您来到我处，随行的好像还有第九骑兵团（Ninth Cavalry）的 C·J·史蒂文斯上尉（Captain C. J. Stevens）。我们当时所在的位置，正可望见沿圣胡安山岭展开的壕堑防线，也看得见肯特（Kent）的步兵师在我们左翼接战，看得见霍金斯（Hawkins）对圣胡安堡（Fort San Juan）的强攻。您请求我准许您前进，强攻圣胡安山岭。我当面下达了前进命令，而且亲眼看到您率本团，连同您旅所属第一、第十骑兵团的各一部，向前推进，强攻圣胡安山岭。我把第二旅的一部分留作水壶山上的预备队，因为不知山岭背后敌军还握有多少预备兵力。第一旅也随之推进，强攻圣胡安堡右侧的山岭。水壶山与圣胡安山岭之间有一小湖，您部推进时从湖的右侧绕行。这就把您带到了圣胡安山岭上一所房子的对面——那不是圣胡安堡本堡，而是一所四周环着土工的木板房。敌军在这处阵地折损了若干人，尸身横陈于壕堑之中。当天晚些时候，我沿线骑巡，据我记忆所及，第十骑兵团的一部分就在这所房子近旁，而您的团沿山岭占据一个不规则的半圆形阵地，紧挨在房子右侧。您的前方派有警戒哨（pickets）；您前方数百码处，西班牙人（Spaniards）设有一处坚固前哨，占据一所房子，四下环绕着散兵壕（rifle pits）。当天晚些时候及次日一整天，组成本师的各团重新调整部署，结成战术队形：第一旅居左，紧靠圣胡安堡右侧；第二旅在第一旅之右。

===Original===
> This was the position occupied by the Cavalry Division until the final surrender of the Spanish forces, on July 17, 1898.

> In conclusion allow me to say, that I saw you, personally, at about 8 a.m., at El Poso; later, on the road to San Juan River; later, on the summit of Kettle Hill, immediately after its capture by the Cavalry Division. I saw you move forward with your command to assault San Juan Ridge, and I saw you on San Juan Ridge, where we visited your line together, and you explained to me the disposition of your command.

===Chinese===
> 骑兵师占据这一阵地，直到 1898 年 7 月 17 日西班牙军队最终投降为止。

> 最后请允许我说明：我亲眼见过您——上午 8 时左右，在埃尔波索；其后，在通往圣胡安河的路上；再后，在水壶山顶上，就在骑兵师攻占该山之后不久。我看见您率所部前进，强攻圣胡安山岭；我也在圣胡安山岭上见过您，我们在那里一同巡视了您的战线，您向我说明了您所部的部署。
