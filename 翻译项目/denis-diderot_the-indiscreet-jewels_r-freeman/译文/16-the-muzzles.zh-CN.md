## XVI The Muzzles / 口笼
===Original===

While the Bramins were making Brama speak, airing their Pagodas by processions, and exhorting the people to repentance; others were thinking how to reap benefit by the prattle of Toys.
Great cities swarm with persons, whom misery renders industrious.﻿—They neither rob nor pick pockets: but they are to pickpockets, what pickpockets are to gamblers. They know everything, they do everything. They go up and down, they insinuate themselves. They are found at court, in the city, at Westminster hall, at church, at the play, at ladies toilets, in coffeehouses, at balls, operas and in academies. They are anything that you would have them be. Do you sollicit a pension, they have the minister’s ear. Have you a lawsuit, they will solicit for you. Do you love gaming, they will make a party with you; good cheer, they keep a good kitchen; women, they will introduce you to Amina or Acaris. From which of the two would you please to purchase the distemper? Take your choice, they will undertake your cure. Their chief occupation is to find the ridicules of private persons, and to make advantage of the follies of the public. From them it is, that papers are distributed in the streets, at the gates of temples, at the playhouse doors, and other public places, by which you are advertised gratis, that such an one living at the Louvre, St. John’s, the temple, or the abbey, at such a sign, dupes mankind at home from nine in the morning to noon, and abroad the rest of the day.
Scarce had the Toys began to speak, when one of these sharpers fill’d the houses of Banza with a printed bill of this form and tenor.﻿—Advertisement to the ladies. And underneath in small Italic, by permission of my lord the great Seneschal, and with the approbation of the gentlemen of the royal academy of sciences. And lower down: The Sieur Eolipila of the royal academy of Banza, Member of the royal society of Monoémugi, of the imperial academy of Biafara, of the academy of the curious of Loango, of the society of Camur in Monomotapa, of the institute of Ereeco, and of the royal academies of Beleguanza and Angola; who has for many years given courses of baubles, with the applause of the court, the town, and the province; has, in favour of the fair sex, invented muzzles or portable gags, which deprive Toys of the use of speech, without obstructing their natural functions. They are neat and convenient.

婆罗门们正忙着替婆罗摩传话、抬着宝塔游街薰香、劝百姓悔改，另一些人却在盘算怎样从玩具的闲话里收利。

大都会里挤满了被穷困逼得勤快起来的人。——他们不抬劫，也不扒包：可是同扒手相比，他们正如扒手同赌客相比。他们无所不知，无所不为。他们上蹿下跳，见缝就钻。宫廷里有他们，城里有他们，威斯敏斯特法庭有他们，教堂有他们，戏院有他们，太太的梳妆台边有他们，咖啡馆、舞会、歌剧、学院里都有他们。你要他们是什么，他们就是什么。你想讨一份年俸，他们同大臣说得上话。你有官司，他们替你去疏通。你爱赌，他们搭你的局；爱吃，他们养着一手好厨；爱女人，他们把你引见给阿米娜或阿卡丽丝。两位之中你高兴从哪位染上病，随你挑；治病他们也包。他们的头号营生，是搜刮私人的笑柄，再拿大众的愚蠢卖钱。沿街、庙门口、戏院门口和别的公众场所散发的那些传单，就出自他们之手——单上白告诉你，某某人，住在卢浮宫区、圣约翰区、圣殿区或修道院区，挂着什么幌子，每天上午九点到晌午在家哄人，其余时辰在外头哄人。

玩具们才开口不多时，这路人物中的一个，就把一张印就的招贴贴满了班扎全城，格式与条文如下。——敬告女上班。下面一行小斜体：蒙御前总管大臣恩准，并经王家科学院诸位先生审定。再下面：班扎王家科学院的厄奥利皮拉先生，莫诺埃穆吉王家学会、比亚法拉帝国学院、洛安戈好奇者学会、莫诺莫塔帕卡穆尔学会、埃雷科学会暨贝莱瓜扎与安哥拉王家诸学院会员；多年来开授小玩物课程，誉满宫廷、都城与外省；今为造福女界，发明口笼兼便携塞口器，能夺玩具之言而不碍其天然功用。制工精洁，使用便利。

===Original===

He has some of all sizes, of all prices, and for all ages: and he has had the honour to serve persons of the highest distinction with them.
There is nothing like being a member of a body of men. Be a work ever so ridiculous, it is puff’d into success. Thus it was, that Eolipila’s invention gain’d ground. People flock’d to his house. The gay ladies went thither in their own equipages, prudent women in hackney-coaches; the devouts sent their confessors or footmen, and nuns their doorkeepers. Everybody must have a muzzle, so that not one from the dutchess down to the cobler’s Joan, but had one either for the fashion or for reasons.
The Bramins, who had declared the prattle of Toys to be a divine punishment, and flatter’d themselves with a reformation of manners and other advantages from it, could not without horror see a machine, which eluded the vengeance of heaven and their hopes. Scarcely had they come down from their pulpits, but they mount again, thunder, roar, make the oracles to speak, and pronounce that a muzzle is an infernal machine, and that there is no salvation for those who shall use them. “Carnal women, quit your muzzles; submit,” cried they, “to the will of Brama. Permit the voice of your Toys to awaken that of your consciences, and blush not to acknowledge crimes, which you have not been ashamed to commit.”
But their exclamations were of little avail: the same fate attended muzzles, that had formerly attended the gowns without sleeves. The preachers were left to catch cold in the temples. All the women took gags, and quitted them not, either till they found them useless, or were tired of them.

各尺寸、各价目、各年龄俱全：并曾荣幸地侍奉过多位贵妇名媛。

入会的身分就是值钱。再荒唐的东西，只要众人一吹，也就成了。于是厄奥利皮拉的发明渐渐吃开。人们成群往他宅里涌。轻快的太太们乘自家的车驾去，稳重的太太们雇街车去；虔诚的派忏悔师或听差去，修女派看门人去。人人必得一口笼，上自公爵夫人，下至补鞋匠家的琼姑娘，没有一个不备一口——或为时髦，或为缘故。

婆罗门们既已宣布玩具的聒噪是天谴，又指望借此整饬风俗、另收些好处，如今见一架机器既逃了上天的报复、又破了他们的指望，怎能不骇然。他们才下讲坛，又重新登坛，打雷，吼叫，请神谕发话，断言口笼乃地狱的机关，凡使用者绝无得救。“肉体的女儿们，摘下你们的口笼；顺从，”他们喊，“顺从婆罗摩的旨意。容你们玩具的声音唤醒你们良心的声音吧；去认你们犯下时不曾羞、如今也不必羞的罪吧。”

可他们的吶喊没有多大用处：口笼遭的命运，同当年无袖袍一样。布道的人们只好在庙堂里晾着嗓子。女人们人人上了口笼，再不离口——直到发觉没用，或用淢了为止。
