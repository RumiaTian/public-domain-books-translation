## X Less Learned and Less Tedious Than the Preceding. Continuation of the Academical Sitting / 较前章少些学问也少些沉闷——院会续篇
===Original===

By the difficulties started against Orcotomus, before he could make his experiments, it appeared, that his notions were judged to have less of solidity than ingenuity. “If Toys have the faculty of speech naturally,” say they, “why have they not made use of it till now? If it was an effect of the goodness of Brama, who has been pleased to endow women with so strong a desire of talking, to double the organs of speech in them; it is very strange, that they have so long been ignorant of, or at least neglected this valuable gift of nature. Why has not the same Toy spoke more than once? Why has none of them spoke of aught but the same subject? By what mechanism does it fall out, that one of the mouths is forcibly kept shut, while the other speaks? Moreover,” added they, “if we judge of the prattle of Toys by the circumstances, in which most of them have spoken, and by the things they have told; there is all the reason in the world to believe it involuntary, and that those parts would continue mute, if it had been in the power of their possessors to impose silence on them.”
Orcotomus arose to answer these objections, and insisted that Toys talk’d in all ages; but so low, that what they said was hardly heard, even by those to whom they belonged. That it is not surprising, that they have raised their voice in our days, when the freedom of conversation is carried so high, that one may without impudence and indiscretion converse on those things, which are the most familiar to them: that if they have spoke audibly but once, we are not to infer that that will be the only time. That there is a vast difference between being mute and keeping silence; that if they have talk’d but on one and the same subject only, probably the reason is, that it is the only one, of which they have ideas. That those who have not spoke as yet, will speak. That if they remain silent, it is because they have nothing to say, or that they are ill made, or want ideas and terms.
“In a word,” continued he, “to pretend that it was an effect of the goodness of Brama to grant to women the means of gratifying their strong desire of talking, by multiplying in them the organs of speech; is to grant, that if this benefaction drag’d any inconveniences after it, it was an effect of his wisdom to prevent them: and this he has done, by compelling one of the mouths to keep silence, while the other speaks.

在奥尔科托穆斯做实验之前，众人向他发难的种种难题已表明：他的见解被人评为巧多于实。“玩具若天生有说话的本事，”他们说，“何以到今天才用？这若出于婆罗摩的恩慈——他既乐意给女人那么强的说话欲，便替她们把发音器官添了一倍——那就怪了：她们竟这样久不知道、至少怠慢了这份天赐的珍宝。为什么同一只玩具不说第二回？为什么它们说的净是同一件题目？又是什么机关，叫一张嘴说话时，另一张嘴被死死按住？再者，”他们补上，“若看玩具们开口时的大多情形，再看它们说出的内容，我们有十足的理由相信那是不由自主；主人若真有能力叫它们缄默，这些部件原会继续哑下去的。”

奥尔科托穆斯起身答辩，坚持说玩具历代都在说话；只是声音低，说出的话连它们的主人也几乎听不见。它们今日提高了嗓门，并不奇怪：如今谈话的自由放得那样开，同它们最熟悉的那类事情，人人谈得脸不红心不跳。它们若才叫人听清了一回，不可便推定这是唯一的一回。哑与守口，中间差得远；它们若至今只谈过一个题目，缘故多半是：它们有概念的题目只此一个。还没开口的，将来会开口。它们若仍旧沉默，那是无话可说，或生得不好，或缺概念、缺词汇。

“一言以蔽之，”他接着说，“若说给女人添发音器官、使她们得偿滔滔之欲，是婆罗摩的恩慈；那便得承认：这份恩赐若拖出什么不便，防患于未然便是他的智慧；而他也确已这样做了——叫两张嘴中的一张，在另一张说话时，强制缄默。

It is already but too inconvenient for us, that women change their mind from one instant to another: what then would it have been, if Brama had left them the power of being of two contradictory sentiments at the same time? Further, the gift of speech has been given, purely in order to be understood; but the women, who find it a difficult talk to understand one another with a single mouth each, how could they possibly do it, if they spoke with two at a time?”
Thus Orcotomus answered several things, and thought he had given satisfactory solutions to all difficulties: but he was mistaken. New doubts were raised, and he was on the point of sinking under them; when Cimonazes came to his assistance. Then the dispute became tumultuous. They stray’d wide of the question, they bewilder’d themselves, they return’d, they bewilder’d themselves a second time, they grew angry, they brawled; from brawling they pass’d to injurious words, and the academical sitting ended.

女人转瞬就变卦，这已经够我们受的了；倘若婆罗摩再让她们同时持有两种相反的意见，那还了得？再说，言语之赐，原只为叫人听懂；女人们一人一张嘴，彼此听得懂已是难事，若是同时用两张嘴说话，还怎么听得懂？”

奥尔科托穆斯就这样答了几样，自以为诸难都有了交代：可他想错了。新的疑问又起，眼看要被压垮；幸而西莫纳泽斯赶来接应。于是争端大乱。众人离题万里，把自己绕迷了，绕回来，又绕迷了第二回，恼了，吼了；从吼骂到恶语，院会就此散场。
