## XVIII The Toyman’s Return / 玩具商回来了
===Original===

The Toyman returned, and presented the ladies with two muzzles of the best sort.﻿—“Ah! mercy!” cried Zelida. “What enormous muzzles are these! And who are the unhappy women, whom these will fit? This is an ell long. Indeed, friend, you must certainly have taken measure by the Sultan’s mare.”
“Yes,” said Sophia indolently, after having measured them with her fingers, “you are in the right; and there is but the Sultan’s mare and old Rimosa, for whom these can do.”
“I protest, ladies, that they are of the common size; and that Zelmaida, Zyrphila, Amiana, and a hundred other ladies, have of the same sort.”
“That is impossible,” said Zelida.
“ ’Tis actually so however,” replied Frenicol: “but they all said the same thing as ye have: and as they have, so may you undeceive yourselves by trying them on.”
“Mr. Frenicol may say what he will; but he shall never persuade me that this will fit me,” said Zelida, “nor this me,” said Sophia. “Let him show us others, if he has any.”
Frenicol, who had often experienced that women are not to be converted on that article, show’d them muzzles for the age of thirteen. “Well,” cried both at the same time, “these are such as we want.”
“I wish they may prove so,” whisper’d Frenicol aside.
“How do you sell them?” said Zelida.
“But ten ducats a piece, madam.”
“Ten ducats, you forget yourself, Frenicol.”
“Madam, that is the price in conscience.”
“You make us pay for the novelty.”
“I protest, ladies, ’tis but two sixpences for a shilling.”
“I must own they are neatly made, but ten ducats are a great deal of money.”
“I’ll abate nothing.”
“We will go to Eolipila’s.”
“You may, ladies: but there are workmen and workmen, muzzles and muzzles.” Frenicol held firm, and Zelida came to. She paid for the two muzzles, and the Toyman went back, fully persuaded that they would be too little for them, and would soon be returned on his hands for a fourth part of the price he received for them. He was mistaken. Mangogul not happening to come within reach of turning his ring on those two women, their Toys were not seized with the humor of talking louder than ordinary; and happily for them: for Zelida having tried her muzzle, found it too little by one half. However, she did not part with it, fancying that it would be pretty near as inconvenient to change it, as not to make use of it at all.

===Chinese===

玩具商回来了，呈给两位太太两枚头等口笼。——“啊！天哪！”泽莉达叫道。“这口笼也大得吓人！哪位苦命女人戴得上？足有一厄尔长。说真的，朋友，您准是照苏丹家的母马量的尺寸。”

“是呢，”索菲娅拿手指量了量，懒懒地说，“您说得对；这尺寸，除了苏丹家的母马，就数老莉莫莎了。”

“我担保，太太们，这是寻常尺码；泽尔迈达、泽尔菲拉、阿米安娜，还有一百位太太，用的都是这一号。”

“不可能。”泽莉达说。

“可事实如此，”弗雷尼科尔回道：“不过她们起先也同二位一样说；她们怎样自己醒的，二位一试也就怎样。”

“弗雷尼科尔先生随怎么说，也休想叫我相信这枚我戴得下，”泽莉达说。“这枚我也戴不下，”索菲娅说。“有别的就再拿来看看。”

弗雷尼科尔屡试不爽，深知女人在这一条上是劝不转的，便拿出十三岁用的口笼来。“喏，”两人同时叫道，“这才正是我们要的。”

“但愿真是，”弗雷尼科尔低声自语。

“怎么卖？”泽莉达说。

“一枚才十杜卡多，夫人。”

“十杜卡多，你昏头了，弗雷尼科尔。”

“夫人，凭良心，这就是实价。”

“你这是拿新奇货宰我们。”

“我担保，太太们，这是一先令当两枚六便士使，只赚工钱。”

“我认账，做工是精；可十杜卡多到底是一大笔钱。”

“一个子儿不让。”

“我们上厄奥利皮拉家去。”

“随二位，太太们：不过工匠同工匠，口笼同口笼，可有高下。”弗雷尼科尔咬定不放，泽莉达松了口。她付了两枚口笼的钱；玩具商回去，满心笃定：这尺寸对她们一定太小，过不多久就得退回他手上，折四分之一价收。他猜错了。曼戈古尔碰巧没有转到能把戒指照上这两个女人的地方，她们的玩具没染上高声说话的瘾；这也算她们走运：因为泽莉达把口笼一试，发现小了一半。可她也没有退货，心想：换一枚同不用，横竖一样不方便。

===Original===

These circumstances came to light from one of her women, who told them in confidence to her lover, who related them in confidence to others, who spread them all over Banza under the seal of secrecy. Nor was Frenicol silent: the adventure of the devouts became public, and for sometime afforded employment to the calumniators of Congo.
Zelida became inconsolable upon it. This woman, more to be pitied than blamed, conceived an aversion for her Bramin, quitted her husband, and shut herself up in a convent. As for Sophia, she threw off the mask, despised censures, patch’d and painted, frequented public places, and had adventures.

这些情形，是从她的一个女仆嘴里漏出来的：女仆悄悄告诉了情人，情人悄悄告诉了旁人，旁人贴着“守密”的封条，传遍了班扎。弗雷尼科尔也没闲着：信女的艳闻闹得尽人皆知，一时成了刚果造谣家们的营生。

泽莉达为此一蹶不振。这女人可悯多于可责：她对婆罗门起了厌心，离开丈夫，把自己关进了修院。至于索菲娅，她索性撕下面具，把物议踩在脚下，贴面，抹粉，出入公共场所，有了艳遇。
