## IV / IV

The Episode Called “Sweet Adelais” / 所谓「甜蜜的阿德莱丝」

===Original===
September 29, 1422

“Anoon her herte hath pitee of his wo,

「她的心随即怜悯他的悲苦，

这位王后便这样沉浸在欢愉与喜悦之中。」

===Original===
Meanwhile had old Dome Sylvia returned contentedly to the helpmate whom she had accepted under compulsion, and who had made her a fair husband, as husbands go. It is duly recorded, indeed, on their shared tomb, that their forty years of married life were of continuous felicity, and set a pattern to all Norfolk. The more prosaic verbal tradition is that Lady Vernon retained Sir Robert well in hand by pointing out, at judicious intervals, that she had only herself to blame for having married such a selfish person in preference to a hero of the age and an ornament of the loftiest circles.

I find, on consultation of the Allonby records, that Sylvia Vernon died of a quinsy, in 1419, surviving Sir Robert by some three months. She had borne him four sons and four daughters: of these there remained at Winstead in 1422 only Sir Hugh Vernon, the oldest son, knighted by Henry V at Agincourt, where Vernon had fought with distinction, and Adelais Vernon, the youngest daughter, with whom the following has to do.

===Chinese===
与此同时，老夫人西尔维娅·弗农（Sylvia Vernon）已心满意足地回到她当初被迫接受的那位配偶身边——此人做她的丈夫还算像样，就照一般丈夫的标准。的确，两人合葬的墓碑上郑重铭刻着：他们四十年的婚姻生活美满连绵，为全诺福克（Norfolk）树立了楷模。口头流传的说法则平实得多：弗农夫人之所以把罗伯特爵士管得服服帖帖，靠的是不失时机地提醒他——她当初放着时代的英雄、最高贵圈子的翘楚不嫁，偏挑了这么一个自私自利的人，只能怪她自己。

我查考阿隆比（Allonby）家的记录发现，西尔维娅·弗农死于喉痈，时在1419年，比罗伯特爵士多活了约莫三个月。她为他生了四子四女：到1422年，仍留在温斯特德（Winstead）的，只剩长子休·弗农爵士（Sir Hugh Vernon）——他曾在阿金库尔（Agincourt）奋勇作战，由亨利五世册封为骑士——以及幼女阿德莱丝·弗农（Adelais Vernon），下文要讲的便是她的事。

### I / I

Gruntings at Aeaea / 埃埃亚岛上的哼哼声

===Original===
It was on a clear September day that the Marquis of Falmouth set out for France. John of Bedford had summoned him posthaste when Henry V was stricken at Senlis with what bid fair to prove a mortal distemper; for the marquis was Bedford’s comrade-in-arms, veteran of Shrewsbury, Agincourt and other martial disputations, and the Duke-Regent suspected that, to hold France in case of the King’s death, he would presently need all the help he could muster.

“And I, too, look for warm work,” the marquis conceded to Mistress Adelais Vernon, at parting. “But, God willing, my sweet, we shall be wed at Christmas for all that. The Channel is not very wide. At a pinch I might swim it, I think, to come to you.”

He kissed her and rode away with his men. Adelais stared after them, striving to picture her betrothed rivalling Leander in this fashion, and subsequently laughed. The marquis was a great lord and a brave captain, but long past his first youth; his actions went somewhat too deliberately ever to be roused to the high lunacies of the Sestian amorist. So Adelais laughed, but a moment later, recollecting the man’s cold desire of her, his iron fervors, Adelais shuddered.

===Chinese===
那是一个晴朗的九月天，法尔茅斯侯爵（Marquis of Falmouth）启程赴法。亨利五世在桑利斯（Senlis）染疾、眼看凶多吉少之际，贝德福德的约翰（John of Bedford）火速传召他前去；因为侯爵是贝德福德的袍泽战友，历经什鲁斯伯里、阿金库尔等诸般兵戎论战的老将，而这位摄政公爵心里明白：一旦国王不讳，要保住法兰西，他眼下就得把能搜罗的帮手尽数搜罗齐备。

「我也料到有一场热仗要打，」临别时，侯爵对阿德莱丝·弗农小姐承认道，「不过，天从人愿的话，我的甜心，尽管如此，咱们圣诞便可成婚。海峡不算太宽。事急了，我想我兴许还能游过海来见你。」

他吻了她，带着随从策马而去。阿德莱丝目送他们，努力想象她的未婚夫这般效法利安得（Leander）泅海的模样，随即笑出声来。侯爵是位大贵人、勇将，只是青春年少早成过往；他行事过于四平八稳，断不会被撩起塞斯托斯情种那般的高热狂痴。于是阿德莱丝笑了；可片刻之后，一想到此人对她的欲念何等冷硬、他的热忱何等铁铸，她又打了个寒噤。

===Original===
This was in the courtyard at Winstead. Roger Darke of Yaxham, the girl’s cousin, standing beside her, noted the gesture, and snarled.

“Think twice of it, Adelais,” said he.

Whereupon Mistress Vernon flushed like a peony. “I honor him,” she said, with some irrelevance, “and he loves me.”

Roger scoffed. “Love, love! O you piece of ice! You gray-stone saint! What do you know of love?” Master Darke caught both her hands in his. “Now, by Almighty God, our Saviour and Redeemer, Jesus Christ!” he said, between his teeth, his eyes flaming; “I, Roger Darke, have offered you undefiled love and you have mocked at it. Ha, Tears of Mary! how I love you! And you mean to marry this man for his title! Do you not believe that I love you, Adelais?” he whimpered.

===Chinese===
这是在温斯特德的庭院里。姑娘的表兄、亚克斯姆的罗杰·达克（Roger Darke of Yaxham）立在她身旁，把这一颤看在眼里，从鼻子里哼出一声冷笑。

「阿德莱丝，这事你可得三思。」他说。

弗农小姐闻言登时红得像朵芍药。「我敬重他，」她说，答得有些文不对题，「他爱我。」

罗杰嗤之以鼻。「爱，爱！哦，你这块冰！你这灰石的圣像！你知道什么叫爱？」达克先生一把攥住她的双手。「如今我以全能的天主、我们的救主、救赎者耶稣基督起誓！」他从牙缝里挤出这一句，两眼喷火；「我罗杰·达克献给你的是未曾沾污的爱，你却拿来嘲弄。哈，圣母的眼泪啊！我多么爱你！而你竟要为一个爵位嫁给那个人！阿德莱丝，你难道不信我爱你么？」他呜咽道。

===Original===
Gently she disengaged herself. This was of a pattern with Roger’s behavior any time during the past two years. “I suppose you do,” Adelais conceded, with the tiniest possible shrug. “Perhaps that is why I find you so insufferable.”

Afterward Mistress Vernon turned on her heel and left Master Darke. In his fluent invocation of Mahound and Termagaunt and other overseers of the damned he presently touched upon eloquence.

===Chinese===
她轻轻地抽出身来。两年来，罗杰的行径随便哪一回都是这个路数。「我信。」阿德莱丝让步道，耸了耸肩，耸得几乎看不出来。「也许正因如此，我才觉得你这么叫人受不了。」

随后弗农小姐转身便走，撇下了达克先生。他对着穆罕默德、特尔玛冈特及沉沦者们的诸位监管念念有词，辞气如流水般顺畅，不多时竟念出了几分雄辩的味道。

### II / II

Comes One with Moly / 携摩吕草者来

===Original===
Adelais came into the walled garden of Winstead, aflame now with autumnal scarlet and gold. She seated herself upon a semicircular marble bench, and laughed for no apparent reason, and contentedly waited what Dame Luck might send.

She was a comely maid, past argument or (as her lovers habitually complained) any adequate description. Circe, Colchian Medea, Viviane du Lac, were their favorite analogues; and what old romancers had fabled concerning these ladies they took to be the shadow of which Adelais Vernon was the substance. At times these rhapsodists might have supported their contention with a certain speciousness, such as was apparent today, for example, when against the garden’s hurly-burly of color, the prodigal blazes of scarlet and saffron and wine-yellow, the girl’s green gown glowed like an emerald, and her eyes, too, seemed emeralds, vivid, inscrutable, of a clear verdancy that was quite untinged with either blue or gray. Very black lashes shaded them. The long oval of her face (you might have objected), was of an absolute pallor, rarely quickening to a flush; but her petulant lips burned crimson, and her hair mimicked the dwindling radiance of the autumn sunlight and shamed it. All in all, the aspect of Adelais Vernon was, beyond any questioning, spiced with a sorcerous tang; say, the look of a young witch shrewd at love-potions, but ignorant of their flavor; yet before this the girl’s comeliness had stirred men’s hearts to madness, and the county boasted of it.

===Chinese===
阿德莱丝走进温斯特德的围墙花园，园中此刻秋深似火，猩红与金黄烧成一片。她在一张半圆形的大理石凳上坐下，无缘无故地笑了，然后心满意足地等着命运夫人（Dame Luck）会送来什么。

她是个标致姑娘，这一点无可置辩，也（如她的爱慕者们惯常抱怨的那样）非言语所能描摹。喀耳刻、科尔基斯的美狄亚、湖上的薇薇安，是他们最爱用的比拟；古时传奇作家关于这几位夫人编织的种种奇谈，在他们看来不过是影子，而阿德莱丝·弗农便是那实体。有时这些狂想家倒也能以几分似是而非的理由支撑其论点，譬如今天便是明证：在花园那一片闹嚷嚷的色彩、猩红与橙黄与酒金的泼天烈焰映衬下，姑娘的绿裙熠熠生辉宛如翡翠，她的眼睛也似翡翠，鲜活、深不可测，澄净的翠色里不掺一丝蓝或灰。乌黑的睫毛遮蔽着它们。她那长长的鹅蛋脸（您也许要挑剔了）白得全无血色，难得泛起一阵红潮；可她任性的双唇烧着绯红，她的头发则戏仿着秋阳将残未残的光焰，并使它自惭形秽。总而言之，阿德莱丝·弗农的容态，毫无疑问，是带有一股妖冶的意味的；譬如说，像一个年轻的巫女，调配媚药的手艺精明老练，却不知媚药自己的滋味；只是在此之前，姑娘的美貌早已把男人们的心搅得发狂，全郡也都引以为夸耀。

===Original===
Presently Adelais lifted her small imperious head, and then again she smiled, for out of the depths of the garden, with an embellishment of diverse trills and roulades, came a man’s voice that carolled blithely.

Sang the voice:

“Had you lived when earth was new

What had bards of old to do

Save to sing in praise of you?

“Had you lived in ancient days,

Adelais, sweet Adelais,

You had all the ancients’ praise—

You whose beauty would have won

Canticles of Solomon,

Had the sage Judean king

Gazed upon this goodliest thing

Earth of Heaven’s grace hath got.

“Had you gladdened Greece, were not

All the nymphs of Greece forgot?

“Had you trod Sicilian ways,

Adelais, sweet Adelais,

“You had pilfered all their praise:

Bion and Theocritus

Had transmitted unto us

Honeyed harmonies to tell

Of your beauty’s miracle,

Delicate, desirable,

And their singing skill were bent

You-ward tenderly—content,

While the world slipped by, to gaze

On the grace of you, and praise

Sweet Adelais.”

===Chinese===
不多时，阿德莱丝昂起她那小小的、骄矜的头，又笑了，因为花园深处传来一个男人引吭高歌的嗓音，缀满了各色颤音与花腔。

那嗓音唱道：

「你若生在天地初开之时，
古代的歌人还有什么事可做，
除了歌唱对你的赞颂？

「你若生在远古岁月，
阿德莱丝，甜蜜的阿德莱丝，
古人的赞美便尽归你一身——
你这美人，本该赢得
所罗门的雅歌，
只要那位睿智的犹地阿君王
曾一睹这天恩所赐
人间至美之物。

「你若曾照亮希腊，希腊的
众宁芙岂不尽数被人遗忘？

「你若曾行经西西里的路途，
阿德莱丝，甜蜜的阿德莱丝，

「你便把他们的一切赞美窃取：
比翁与忒奥克里托斯
将遗赠给我们
蜜一样甘美的乐音，
传颂你美的奇迹，
纤巧动人，令人渴慕，
他们的歌艺将殷殷
向你倾注——心满意足，
任世界悄然流逝，只管凝望
你的风姿，赞美
甜蜜的阿德莱丝。」

===Original===
Here the song ended, and a man, wheeling about the hedge, paused to regard her with adoring eyes. Adelais looked up at him, incredibly surprised by his coming.

This was the young Sieur d’Arnaye, Hugh Vernon’s prisoner, taken at Agincourt seven years earlier and held since then, by the King’s command, without ransom; for it was Henry’s policy to release none of the important French prisoners. Even on his deathbed he found time to admonish his brother, John of Bedford, that four of these—Charles d’Orleans and Jehan de Bourbon and Arthur de Rougemont and Fulke d’Arnaye—should never be set at liberty. “Lest,” as the King said, with a savor of prophecy, “more fire be kindled in one day than all your endeavors can quench in three.”

Presently the Sieur d’Arnaye sighed, rather ostentatiously; and Adelais laughed, and demanded the cause of his grief.

===Chinese===
歌声到此为止。一个男人绕过树篱，驻足用倾慕的目光望着她。阿德莱丝抬头看他，对他的到来惊讶得难以置信。

来者是年轻的达尔奈大人（Sieur d’Arnaye），休·弗农的俘虏，七年前在阿金库尔被俘，此后奉国王之命一直囚禁在此，不索赎金；因为亨利的方针是：法国的要俘一个也不放。即便到了临终的病榻上，他还抽空告诫弟弟贝德福德的约翰：其中有四个——查理·德·奥尔良、让·德·波旁、阿蒂尔·德·鲁热蒙和富尔克·达尔奈（Fulke d’Arnaye）——永不得开释。用国王的话说，那口气颇有几分预言的意味：「免得一日之间燃起的大火，叫你竭尽全力三天也扑灭不了。」

不多时，达尔奈大人叹了口气，叹得未免有些做作；阿德莱丝笑了，问他有何伤心事。

===Original===
“Mademoiselle,” he said—his English had but a trace of accent—“I am afflicted with a very grave malady.”

“What is the name of this malady?” said she.

“They call it love, mademoiselle.”

Adelais laughed yet again and doubted if the disease were incurable. But Fulke d’Arnaye seated himself beside her and demonstrated that, in his case, it might not ever be healed.

“For it is true,” he observed, “that the ancient Scythians, who lived before the moon was made, were wont to cure this distemper by bloodletting under the ears; but your brother, mademoiselle, denies me access to all knives. And in Greece—hé, mademoiselle, I might easily be healed of my malady in Greece! For in Greece is the rock, Leucata Petra, from which a lover may leap and be cured; and the well of the Cyziceni, from which a lover may drink and be cured; and the river Selemnus, in which a lover may bathe and be cured: but your brother will not permit that I go to Greece. You have a very cruel brother, mademoiselle; seven long years, no less, he has penned me here like a starling in a cage.”

===Chinese===
「小姐，」他说——他的英语只带一丝口音——「我正害着一场极沉重的病。」

「这病叫什么名字？」她说。

Afterward he laughed. Always this Frenchman found something at which to laugh; Adelais could not remember in all the seven years a time when she had seen him downcast. 富尔克·达尔奈朝她摇头，一脸责备。

Fulke d’Arnaye shrugged his shoulders. “That was my thought, too,” he admitted, with a laugh. “Always I dream of escape, mademoiselle. Have a care of me, sweet enemy! I shall escape yet, it may be.”

可阿德莱丝已把两只手都搭上一副坚实的肩膀，向他俯下身去，直到她的头发拂着他的头发。是的，这个男人于她格外亲爱：他满口关于她的高调浑话，按公道论罪不至死，只该吃一记耳光，她不能眼睁睁看他送命；再说，迁就一下他的愚蠢，她到底也不怎么介意。

「你还不明白么？」她低语道。「啊，我的游侠骑士，你以为我说话是出于怜悯么？我想做贵妇人——是的。可我一直，我想，是爱你的，富尔克，只是直到今夜之前，我一直以为爱情不过是男人的痴愚、女人的消遣。瞧，这是法尔茅斯的戒指。」她把它从手指上褪下，笨拙地掷出去——女人掷东西都是那样笨拙。戒指闪着光，穿过月色落了下去。「是的，我曾渴慕法尔茅斯的权势，可你让我看见了凌驾于一切尘世权势之上的东西。我永远渴求至高之物，富尔克——啊，美好甜蜜的朋友，别拒绝我！」阿德莱丝哀婉地喊道。「带我走吧，富尔克！我要随你出征，大人，做你的扈从；我要做你的妻子、你的奴婢、你的厨下粗使丫头。什么都行，只要不离开你。大人，女儿家这样哀求，本不是分内的事啊！」

富尔克·达尔奈把她温暖顺从的身躯揽向自己，默默立着。随后他举目向天。「亲爱的主上帝，」他朗声喊道，「我恳求您：倘若因我的过错，这女人他日得知悔恨或悲伤，就把我掷入地狱最深处的深渊，直到永永远远！」之后，他吻了她。

不多时，阿德莱丝抬起头来，带着一丝嘲弄的轻笑。「悲伤！」她应声道，「我看这世上根本就没有悲伤这回事。上马吧，大人，上马！瞧，奥利维耶兄弟正在那边等我们呢。」
