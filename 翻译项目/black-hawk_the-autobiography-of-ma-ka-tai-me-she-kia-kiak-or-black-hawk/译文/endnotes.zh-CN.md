## Endnotes / 尾注

===Original===
1. Capt. Stephenson was held in high estimation as a brave and accomplished gentleman, and at the organization of Rock Island county the county commissioners honored his name by calling the county seat Stephenson, which name it retained until after his death, when that of Rock Island was adopted. ↩︎

===Chinese===
1. 斯蒂文森上尉素有勇毅干练之绅士令誉，深受敬重。罗克岛县（Rock Island county）建县之初，县专员们为表彰他的名声，将县治命名为「斯蒂文森」（Stephenson）；此名一直沿用至他身后，方改用「罗克岛」之名。 ↩︎
