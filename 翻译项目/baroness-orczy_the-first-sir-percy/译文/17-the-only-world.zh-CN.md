## XVII The Only World / XVII 唯一的世界

### I / 一

===Original===
Out there, in the lonely molen on the Veluwe, Gilda had remained for a while, half numb with nerve strain, suffering from the reaction after the terrible excitement of the past few hours. Presently her old serving-woman came to her, still raging with choler at the outrage committed against her person by those two abominable rascallions.

With great volubility, she explained to her mistress that they had fallen on her unawares when first she had been sent downstairs by his lordship﻿—whom may God punish!﻿—They had bound and gagged her, and then told her quite cheerfully that this was an act of friendship on their part, to save her from a worse fate and from the temptation of talking when she should remain silent.

She had been thrust into a dark angle of the millhouse, from whence she could see absolutely nothing, and where she had lain all this while, entirely helpless, hearing that awful din which had been going on outside, expecting to be murdered in cold blood at any moment, and tortured with fear as to what was happening to her mistress. Only a few moments ago, the two ruffians had reappeared, running helter-skelter down the steps and thence out through the door into the open. Fortunately, one of them, conscience-stricken no doubt, had thought, before fleeing, to release her from her bonds.

===Chinese===
在外头，费吕韦（the Veluwe）那座孤零零的磨坊（molen）里，吉尔达（Gilda）又逗留了一阵——神经绷得太久，人已半是麻木，正经受着过去几个钟头那场可怕的激动过后的反应。不多时，她的老侍女来到她身边，兀自满腔怒火，恨着那两个可恶的恶棍无赖加诸她本人的暴行。

她滔滔不绝地向女主人诉说：起初她奉勋爵大人之命——愿上帝惩罚他！——头一回被打发下楼时，他们便冷不丁朝她扑了上来；他们把她捆了个结实，堵上了嘴，过后还满脸快活地告诉她，说这是他们俩的一番友爱之举，好救她免遭更坏的命运，也免得她在该缄口的时候，受不住多嘴的诱惑。

她被推进磨坊屋里一处黑黢黢的角落，从那儿什么也绝对瞧不见；方才这一路，她便一直躺在那里，全然无计可施，耳听着外头那阵可怕的喧嚣闹个不停，时时刻刻只当自己就要遭人冷血杀害，心里又为女主人此刻的境遇惊惧煎熬。就在片刻之前，那两个恶徒重新露了面，跌跌撞撞地奔下台阶，夺门而出，跑进旷野里去了。所幸其中一个——想必是良心发现——临逃之前倒还想到替她松了绑。

===Original===
Maria was stupid, uncomprehending and garrulous; but she was loyal, and had a warm and ample bosom, whereon a tired and aching head could find a little rest.

Gilda, her body still shaken by hysterical sobs, her teeth chattering, her senses reeling with the horror of all that she had gone through, found some measure of comfort in the old woman’s ministrations. A mugful of wine, left over from the midday meal, helped her to regain command over her nerves. Holding her young mistress in her arms, Maria, crooning like a mother over her baby, rocked the half-inert young form into some semblance of sleep.

===Chinese===
玛丽亚（Maria）人笨，懵懵懂懂，又絮叨个没完；可她忠心，还有一副温暖宽厚的胸怀，好叫一颗疲惫作痛的脑袋在那里头觅得少许安歇。

吉尔达的身子还叫歇斯底里的抽泣震得发颤，牙齿咯咯打战，神志也仍为她经历过的那一切恐怖晕眩摇晃，却在老妇人的一番照拂里得了几分慰藉。中午饭剩下的一大杯酒下了肚，帮她重新稳住了自己的神经。玛丽亚把年轻的女主人搂在怀里，像母亲哄着婴孩一般低低哼着，将那半瘫软的年轻身子摇啊摇的，摇出了几分入睡的模样。

### II / 二

===Original===
And here Diogenes found her a couple of hours later, curled up like a tired child in the arms of the old woman.

He came up on tiptoe, carrying a lantern, for now it was quite dark. This he placed on the floor, and then, with infinite caution, he slid into Maria’s place and took the beloved form into his own strong arms.

She scarcely moved, just opened her eyes for a second or two, and then nestled closer against his shoulder, with a little sigh, half of weariness, but wholly of content.

===Chinese===
两个钟头之后，第欧根尼（Diogenes）就在这里寻着了她——她蜷在老妇人怀里，活像个困倦透了的孩子。

他提着一盏提灯，蹑手蹑脚地走上前来——这会儿天已经全黑了。他把灯搁在地板上，然后怀着万分的谨慎，悄然挪进玛丽亚空出的位置，把那心爱的身躯接进了自己坚实的臂弯里。

她几乎没动弹，只睁开眼睛一两秒钟，随即往他的肩头偎得更紧了些，轻轻叹出一口气——半是困倦，却整个儿是心满意足。

===Original===
She was just dead-tired after all she had gone through, and now she slept just like a baby in his arms; whilst he was as happy as it is possible for any human being to be, for she was safe and well, and nothing could part her from him now. He was satisfied to watch her as she slept, her dear face against his breast, her soft breath coming and going with perfect evenness through her parted lips.

Once he stooped and kissed her, and then she woke, put her arms around his neck, and both forgot for the time being that there was another world save that of Love.

===Chinese===
经过这一切之后，她实在是累乏到了极点，此刻便像婴孩一样睡在他怀里；而他呢，已然快活到了一个人所能有的极致——因为她平安无恙，如今再没有什么能把她从他身边夺走了。他心满意足地望着她安睡，望着她那可爱的脸庞贴在自己胸膛上，柔和的气息从微启的双唇间匀匀地一进一出。

有那么一回，他俯下身去吻她；她便醒了，伸出双臂搂住他的脖颈。一时间，两人都忘了，除了爱的世界，这世上还有别的世界。
