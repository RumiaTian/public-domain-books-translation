## IV Revolution and Dictatorship / IV 革命与专政
===Original===

The Bolsheviks have a very definite programme for achieving Communism﻿—a programme which has been set forth by Lenin repeatedly, and quite recently in the reply of the Third International to the questionnaire submitted by the Independent Labour Party.
Capitalists, we are assured, will stick at nothing in defence of their privileges. It is the nature of man, in so far as he is politically conscious, to fight for the interests of his class so long as classes exist. When the conflict is not pushed to extremes, methods of conciliation and political deception may be preferable to actual physical warfare; but as soon as the proletariat make a really vital attack upon the capitalists, they will be met by guns and bayonets. This being certain and inevitable, it is as well to be prepared for it, and to conduct propaganda accordingly. Those who pretend that pacific methods can lead to the realization of Communism are false friends to the wage-earners; intentionally or unintentionally, they are covert allies of the bourgeoisie.
There must, then, according to Bolshevik theory, be armed conflict sooner or later, if the injustices of the present economic system are ever to be remedied. Not only do they assume armed conflict: they have a fairly definite conception of the way in which it is to be conducted. This conception has been carried out in Russia, and is to be carried out, before very long, in every civilized country. The Communists, who represent the class-conscious wage-earners, wait for some propitious moment when events have caused a mood of revolutionary discontent with the existing Government. They then put themselves at the head of the discontent, carry through a successful revolution, and in so doing acquire the arms, the railways, the State treasure, and all the other resources upon which the power of modern Governments is built. They then confine political power to Communists, however small a minority they may be of the whole nation. They set to work to increase their number by propaganda and the control of education. And meanwhile, they introduce Communism into every department of economic life as quickly as possible.
Ultimately, after a longer or shorter period, according to circumstances, the nation will be converted to Communism, the relics of capitalist institutions will have been obliterated, and it will be possible to restore freedom.

===Chinese===

布尔什维克拥有一个实现共产主义（Communism）的非常明确的纲领——列宁（Lenin）曾一再阐述这一纲领，最近在第三国际（the Third International）对独立工党（Independent Labour Party）提交的问卷调查的答复中也有所体现。

我们被告知，资本家为了捍卫其特权将不择手段。只要阶级依然存在，为了本阶级的利益而战，就是人类在具备政治意识范围内的本性。当冲突未被推向极端时，妥协和政治欺骗的方法或许比实际的肉体战争更为可取；但一旦无产阶级对资本家发起真正致命的攻击，他们将遭到大炮和刺刀的还击。既然这是确凿且不可避免的，那么最好为此做好准备，并据此开展宣传。那些声称和平方法能够实现共产主义的人，是工薪劳动者的虚假朋友；无论是有意还是无意，他们都是资产阶级的隐蔽盟友。

因此，根据布尔什维克理论，如果要补救当前经济制度的不公，武装冲突迟早必然爆发。他们不仅预设了武装冲突的存在：他们对于武装冲突应当如何进行，还拥有相当明确的设想。这一设想已经在俄国付诸实践，并不久之后将在每一个文明国家付诸实践。代表有阶级意识的（class-conscious）工薪劳动者的共产党人，等待着事态引起对现存政府的革命性不满情绪的某个有利时机。随后，他们置身于这种不满情绪的前列，贯彻一场成功的革命，并在这一过程中夺取武器、铁路、国家财富以及现代政府权力所依仗的所有其他资源。随后，无论他们在全国人口中是多么微小的少数，他们都将政治权力局限于共产党员手中。他们着手通过宣传和控制教育来扩大自身的人数。与此同时，他们尽快将共产主义引入经济生活的每一个部门。

最终，在经历或长或短的一段时期后（视情况而定），全国人民都将皈依共产主义，资本主义制度的残余将被扫除殆尽，那时恢复自由便成为可能。

===Original===

But the political conflicts to which we are accustomed will not reappear. All the burning political questions of our time, according to the Communists, are questions of class conflict, and will disappear when the division of classes disappears. Accordingly the State will no longer be required, since the State is essentially an engine of power designed to give the victory to one side in the class conflict. Ordinary States are designed to give the victory to the capitalists; the proletarian State (Soviet Russia) is designed to give the victory to the wage-earners. As soon as the community contains only wage-earners, the State will cease to have any functions. And so, through a period of dictatorship, we shall finally arrive at a condition very similar to that aimed at by Anarchist Communism.
Three questions arise in regard to this method of reaching Utopia. First, would the ultimate state foreshadowed by the Bolsheviks be desirable in itself? Secondly, would the conflict involved in achieving it by the Bolshevik method be so bitter and prolonged that its evils would outweigh the ultimate good? Thirdly, is this method likely to lead, in the end, to the state which the Bolsheviks desire, or will it fail at some point and arrive at a quite different result? If we are to be Bolsheviks, we must answer all these questions in a sense favourable to their programme.
As regards the first question, I have no hesitation in answering it in a manner favourable to Communism. It is clear that the present inequalities of wealth are unjust. In part, they may be defended as affording an incentive to useful industry, but I do not think this defence will carry us very far. However, I have argued this question before in my book on *Roads to Freedom*, and I will not spend time upon it now. On this matter, I concede the Bolshevik case. It is the other two questions that I wish to discuss.
Our second question was: Is the ultimate good aimed at by the Bolsheviks sufficiently great to be worth the price that, according to their own theory, will have to be paid for achieving it?
If anything human were absolutely certain, we might answer this question affirmatively with some confidence.

===Chinese===

但我们所习以为常的政治冲突将不再重现。按照共产党人的说法，我们时代所有焦灼的政治问题都是阶级冲突的问题，一旦阶级划分消失，这些问题都将烟消云散。因此，国家将不再被需要，因为国家在本质上是一种旨在使阶级冲突中一方获得胜利的权力机器。普通国家旨在让资本家获胜；无产阶级国家（苏维埃俄国）旨在让工薪劳动者获胜。一旦社会只包含工薪劳动者，国家便将停止具有任何职能。于是，通过一段时期的专政，我们最终将达到一种与无政府主义共产主义所追求的目标极其相似的境况。

关于这种通往乌托邦的方法，产生了三个问题。首先，布尔什维克所预示的终极状态本身是否值得追求？其次，通过布尔什维克的方法达成这一状态所涉及的冲突，是否会如此激烈而漫长，以至于其弊端会压倒终极的善？第三，这种方法最终是否有可能引向布尔什维克所期望的状态，抑或它会在某一关头失败并导致截然不同的结果？如果我们要做布尔什维克，我们就必须在有利于其纲领的意义上回答所有这些问题。

关于第一个问题，我毫不犹豫地在有利于共产主义的意义上予以回答。显而易见，当前财富的不平等是不公正的。在某种程度上，它们可以被辩护为对有益实业提供了激励，但我认为这种辩护并不能走得很远。然而，我此前在《通往自由之路》（*Roads to Freedom*）一书中已经讨论过这一问题，现在我不想在此耗费时间。在这个问题上，我承认布尔什维克的论点。我希望讨论的是另外两个问题。

我们的第二个问题是：布尔什维克所追求的终极的善，是否值得付出其方法所必需付出的代价？

===Original===

The benefits of Communism, if it were once achieved, might be expected to be lasting; we might legitimately hope that further change would be towards something still better, not towards a revival of ancient evils. But if we admit, as we must do, that the outcome of the Communist revolution is in some degree uncertain, it becomes necessary to count the cost; for a great part of the cost is all but certain.
Since the revolution of October, 1917, the Soviet Government has been at war with almost all the world, and has had at the same time to face civil war at home. This is not to be regarded as accidental, or as a misfortune which could not be foreseen. According to Marxian theory, what has happened was bound to happen. Indeed, Russia has been wonderfully fortunate in not having to face an even more desperate situation. First and foremost, the world was exhausted by the war, and in no mood for military adventures. Next, the Tsarist regime was the worst in Europe, and therefore rallied less support than would be secured by any other capitalist Government. Again, Russia is vast and agricultural, making it capable of resisting both invasion and blockade better than Great Britain or France or Germany. The only other country that could have resisted with equal success is the United States, which is at present very far removed from a proletarian revolution, and likely long to remain the chief bulwark of the capitalist system. It is evident that Great Britain, attempting a similar revolution, would be forced by starvation to yield within a few months, provided America led a policy of blockade. The same is true, though in a less degree, of continental countries. Therefore, unless and until an international Communist revolution becomes possible, we must expect that any other nation following Russia’s example will have to pay an even higher price than Russia has had to pay.
Now the price that Russia is having to pay is very great. The almost universal poverty might be thought to be a small evil in comparison with the ultimate gain, but it brings with it other evils of which the magnitude would be acknowledged even by those who have never known poverty and therefore make light of it. Hunger brings an absorption in the question of food, which, to most people, makes life almost purely animal.

===Chinese===

共产主义的好处一旦实现，可以预期将是持久的；我们完全有理由希望进一步的改变将走向更加美好的事物，而非复活古老的罪恶。但是，如果我们承认——正如我们必须承认的那样——共产主义革命的结果在某种程度上是不确定的，那么计算代价就变得必不可少了；因为这笔代价的绝大部分几乎是确凿无疑的。

自 1917 年 10 月革命以来，苏维埃政府几乎与全世界都处于交战状态，并且同时不得不面对国内的内战。这绝不能被视为偶然，也不能被视为无法预见的厄运。根据马克思主义理论，已经发生的事情是注定要发生的。事实上，俄国能够免于面对更加绝望的境地，已经是极其幸运的了。首当其冲的是，全世界都被战争消耗得精疲力竭，毫无心思进行军事冒险。其次，沙皇政权是欧洲最糟糕的政权，因此它所能拉拢的支持远少于任何其他资本主义政府所能获得的支持。再者，俄国幅员辽阔且以农业为主，这使得它比英国、法国或德国更能抵抗侵略和封锁。唯一能够以同等成功进行抵抗的其他国家是美国，而美国目前距离无产阶级革命极其遥远，并且很可能长期充当资本主义制度的主要堡垒。显而易见，如果英国尝试类似的革命，只要美国主导封锁政策，英国在数月之内就会因饥荒而被迫屈服。大陆国家亦是如此，尽管程度稍轻。因此，除非并且直到国际共产主义革命成为可能，否则我们必须预料，任何效仿俄国榜样的其他国家都将不得不付出比俄国已经付出的代价还要高昂的代价。

如今俄国正在被迫付出的代价是极其巨大的。与终极收益相比，几乎普遍的贫困或许被认为是一个微不足道的恶，但它带来了其他恶果，这些恶果的严重程度即便是那些从未体会过贫困因而对其轻描淡写的人也不得不承认。饥饿导致人们全神贯注于食物问题，对大多数人而言，这使得生活几乎退化为纯粹的动物状态。

===Original===

The general shortage makes people fierce, and reacts upon the political atmosphere. The necessity of inculcating Communism produces a hothouse condition, where every breath of fresh air must be excluded: people are to be taught to think in a certain way, and all free intelligence becomes taboo. The country comes to resemble an immensely magnified Jesuit College. Every kind of liberty is banned as being “bourgeois”; but it remains a fact that intelligence languishes where thought is not free.
All this, however, according to the leaders of the Third International, is only a small beginning of the struggle, which must become worldwide before it achieves victory. In their reply to the Independent Labour Party they say:

> It is probable that upon the throwing off of the chains of the capitalist Governments, the revolutionary proletariat of Europe will meet the resistance of Anglo-Saxon capital in the persons of British and American capitalists who will attempt to blockade it. It is then possible that the revolutionary proletariat of Europe will rise in union with the peoples of the East and commence a revolutionary struggle, the scene of which will be the entire world, to deal a final blow to British and American capitalism (*The Times*, July 30, 1920).

===Chinese===

普遍的匮乏使人们变得凶狠残暴，并对政治氛围产生反冲作用。灌输共产主义的必要性造成了一种温室般的境况，每一丝新鲜空气都必须被隔绝在外：人们被教导要以某种特定方式思考，所有自由的心智都成了禁忌。这个国家变得宛如一座极大放大的耶稣会学院（Jesuit College）。任何形式的自由都因属于“资产阶级”而遭到取缔；但事实依然是：在思想不自由的地方，智力必然萎靡凋零。

然而，根据第三国际领导人的说法，所有这一切仅仅是这场斗争的微小开端，斗争在取得胜利之前必须走向世界范围。在对独立工党的答复中，他们写道：

> 很可能在挣脱资本主义政府的枷锁之时，欧洲革命无产阶级将遭遇以企图对其实施封锁的英美资本家为代表的盎格鲁-撒克逊资本势力的抵抗。届时，欧洲革命无产阶级很可能会与东方各族人民联合起来，掀起一场以整个世界为舞台的革命斗争，给英美资本主义以致命的打击（《泰晤士报》[*The Times*]，1920 年 7 月 30 日）。

===Original===

The war here prophesied, if it ever takes place, will be one compared to which the late war will come to seem a mere affair of outposts. Those who realize the destructiveness of the late war, the devastation and impoverishment, the lowering of the level of civilization throughout vast areas, the general increase of hatred and savagery, the letting loose of bestial instincts which had been curbed during peace﻿—those who realize all this will hesitate to incur inconceivably greater horrors, even if they believe firmly that Communism in itself is much to be desired. An economic system cannot be considered apart from the population which is to carry it out; and the population resulting from such a world-war as Moscow calmly contemplates would be savage, bloodthirsty and ruthless to an extent that must make any system a mere engine of oppression and cruelty.
This brings us to our third question: Is the system which Communists regard as their goal likely to result from the adoption of their methods? This is really the most vital question of the three.
Advocacy of Communism by those who believe in Bolshevik methods rests upon the assumption that there is no slavery except economic slavery, and that when all goods are held in common there must be perfect liberty. I fear this is a delusion.
There must be administration, there must be officials who control distribution. These men, in a Communist State, are the repositories of power. So long as they control the army, they are able, as in Russia at this moment, to wield despotic power even if they are a small minority. The fact that there is Communism﻿—to a certain extent﻿—does not mean that there is liberty. If the Communism were more complete, it would not necessarily mean more freedom; there would still be certain officials in control of the food supply, and these officials could govern as they pleased so long as they retained the support of the soldiers. This is not mere theory: it is the patent lesson of the present condition of Russia. The Bolshevik theory is that a small minority are to seize power, and are to hold it until Communism is accepted practically universally, which, they admit, may take a long time. But power is sweet, and few men surrender it voluntarily.

===Chinese===

在此所预言的战争一旦爆发，与之相比，最近这场大战将变得宛如一场前哨战。那些认识到最近这场战争的破坏性、认识到浩劫与赤贫、认识到广大地区文明水平的跌落、仇恨与残暴的普遍滋长，以及和平时期受到遏制的兽性本能被释放出来的人——那些认识到所有这一切的人，即便深信共产主义本身是极其值得追求的，也会在承受难以想象的更大恐怖面前退缩不前。一种经济体制不能脱离执行它的民众来单独考察；而从莫斯科泰然自若所构想的这样一场世界大战中产生出来的人口，其野蛮、嗜血与冷酷无情的程度，必将使任何体制都退化为纯粹压迫与残暴的机器。

这就引出了我们的第三个问题：共产党人视作目标的制度，是否有可能通过采纳其方法而产生？这确实是三个问题中最核心的问题。

那些信仰布尔什维克方法的人对共产主义的倡导，是建立在这样一种假定之上的：除了经济奴役之外不存在任何奴役，一旦所有财产共有，就必然享有完美的自由。恐怕这纯属幻觉。

必然需要管理机构，必然需要控制物资分配的官员。在共产主义国家，这些人就是权力的执掌者。只要他们控制着军队，即便他们只是少数人，他们也能够像此时在俄国一样行使专制权力。存在一定程度的共产主义这一事实，并不意味着存在自由。即便共产主义更加彻底，也并不必然意味着更多的自由；依然会有某些官员控制着粮食供应，而且只要这些官员保有士兵的支持，他们就可以为所欲为地进行统治。这不仅仅是理论：这是俄国当前状况昭然若揭的教训。布尔什维克的理论是由一小部分人夺取政权并掌权直到共产主义几乎被普遍接受，他们承认这可能需要很长时间。然而权力是甜美的，很少有人会自愿放弃权力。

===Original===

It is especially sweet to those who have the habit of it, and the habit becomes most ingrained in those who have governed by bayonets, without popular support. Is it not almost inevitable that men placed as the Bolsheviks are placed in Russia, and as they maintain that the Communists must place themselves wherever the social revolution succeeds, will be loath to relinquish their monopoly of power, and will find reasons for remaining until some new revolution ousts them? Would it not be fatally easy for them, without altering economic structure, to decree large salaries for high Government officials, and so reintroduce the old inequalities of wealth? What motive would they have for not doing so? What motive is possible except idealism, love of mankind, non-economic motives of the sort that Bolsheviks decry? The system created by violence and the forcible rule of a minority must necessarily allow of tyranny and exploitation; and if human nature is what Marxians assert it to be, why should the rulers neglect such opportunities of selfish advantage?
It is sheer nonsense to pretend that the rulers of a great empire such as Soviet Russia, when they have become accustomed to power, retain the proletarian psychology, and feel that their class-interest is the same as that of the ordinary working man. This is not the case in fact in Russia now, however the truth may be concealed by fine phrases. The Government has a class-consciousness and a class-interest quite distinct from those of the genuine proletarian, who is not to be confounded with the paper proletarian of the Marxian schema. In a capitalist state, the Government and the capitalists on the whole hang together, and form one class; in Soviet Russia, the Government has absorbed the capitalist mentality together with the governmental, and the fusion has given increased strength to the upper class. But I see no reason whatever to expect equality or freedom to result from such a system, except reasons derived from a false psychology and a mistaken analysis of the sources of political power.
I am compelled to reject Bolshevism for two reasons: First, because the price mankind must pay to achieve Communism by Bolshevik methods is too terrible; and secondly because, even after paying the price, I do not believe the result would be what the Bolsheviks profess to desire.

===Chinese===

对于那些已经习惯于权力的人而言，权力尤其甜美，而在那些依靠刺刀、在缺乏民意支持下进行统治的人身上，这种习惯变得最为根深蒂固。处在布尔什维克在俄国所处的地位上、以及正如他们坚称在社会革命取得成功的任何地方共产党人都必须处于的地位上的人，几乎不可避免地会不愿放弃其权力的垄断，并会寻找理由留任直到某场新的革命将他们驱逐出去，这难道不是近乎必然的吗？他们难道不会致命地轻易做到在不改变经济结构的情况下，为高级政府官员规定巨额薪金，从而重新引入旧有的财富不平等吗？他们有什么动机不去这么做？除了理想主义、对人类的热爱以及布尔什维克所鄙视的那类非经济动机之外，还能有什么可能的动机？由暴力和少数人的强权统治所建立起来的体制，必然容许专制与剥削；而且如果人性真如马克思主义者所断言的那样，统治者为何要忽视这种谋取私利的机会呢？

当像苏维埃俄国这样一个庞大帝国的统治者习惯了权力之后，硬说他们依然保留着无产阶级心理，觉得自己的阶级利益与普通工人是一样的，这完全是一派胡言。无论真理如何被华丽辞藻所掩盖，如今在俄国实际情况绝非如此。政府拥有与真正无产者截然不同的阶级意识和阶级利益，而真正的无产者决不可与马克思主义图式中纸面上的无产者混为一谈。在资本主义国家，政府与资本家总体上穿一条裤子，构成一个阶级；在苏维埃俄国，政府将资本家心态与统治者心态融为一体，这种融合赋予了上层阶级更大的力量。但我完全看不出有什么理由指望这样一种体制能带来平等或自由，除非这些理由源于虚假的心理学以及对政治权力来源的错误分析。

我不得不基于两个理由拒绝布尔什维主义：第一，人类为了通过布尔什维克方法实现共产主义所必须付出的代价太可怕了；第二，即便付出了这一代价，我也不相信结果会是布尔什维克声称所追求的目标。

But if their methods are rejected, how are we ever to arrive at a better economic system? This is not an easy question, and I shall treat it in a separate chapter.

但如果他们的方法遭到摒弃，我们究竟该如何走向更好的经济体制？这不是一个简单的问题，我将在单独的一章中予以论述。
