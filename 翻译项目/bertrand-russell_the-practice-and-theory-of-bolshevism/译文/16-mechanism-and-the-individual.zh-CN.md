## V Mechanism and the Individual / V 机械论与个人
===Original===

Is it possible to effect a fundamental reform of the existing economic system by any other method than that of Bolshevism? The difficulty of answering this question is what chiefly attracts idealists to the dictatorship of the proletariat. If, as I have argued, the method of violent revolution and Communist rule is not likely to have the results which idealists desire, we are reduced to despair unless we can see hope in other methods. The Bolshevik arguments against all other methods are powerful. I confess that, when the spectacle of present-day Russia forced me to disbelieve in Bolshevik methods, I was at first unable to see any way of curing the essential evils of capitalism. My first impulse was to abandon political thinking as a bad job, and to conclude that the strong and ruthless must always exploit the weaker and kindlier sections of the population. But this is not an attitude that can be long maintained by any vigorous and temperamentally hopeful person. Of course, if it were the truth, one would have to acquiesce. Some people believe that by living on sour milk one can achieve immortality. Such optimists are answered by a mere refutation; it is not necessary to go on and point out some other way of escaping death. Similarly an argument that Bolshevism will not lead to the millennium would remain valid even if it could be shown that the millennium cannot be reached by any other road. But the truth in social questions is not quite like truth in physiology or physics, since it depends upon men’s beliefs. Optimism tends to verify itself by making people impatient of avoidable evils; while despair, on the other hand, makes the world as bad as it believes it to be. It is therefore imperative for those who do not believe in Bolshevism to put some other hope in its place.
I think there are two things that must be admitted: first, that many of the worst evils of capitalism might survive under Communism; secondly, that the cure for these evils cannot be sudden, since it requires changes in the average mentality.
What are the chief evils of the present system? I do not think that mere inequality of wealth, in itself, is a very grave evil. If everybody had enough, the fact that some have more than enough would be unimportant.

===Chinese===

是否存在通过布尔什维主义（Bolshevism）之外的其他方法对现存经济制度进行根本变革的可能？回答这一问题的困难，正是将理想主义者主要吸引到无产阶级专政（dictatorship of the proletariat）旗帜下的原因。如果正如我所论证的那样，暴力革命与共产党统治的方法不太可能产生理想主义者所渴望的结果，那么除非我们能在其他方法中看到希望，否则我们将陷入绝望。布尔什维克反对所有其他方法的论点是强有力的。我坦承，当今日俄国的景象迫使我不再相信布尔什维克的方法时，我起初无法看出医治资本主义本质弊端的任何途径。我的第一冲动是将政治思考视为一项无望的事业而予以放弃，并得出结论：强横残暴者注定永远剥削人口中较为弱小和善良的部分。但这并不是任何充满生机且在性情上抱持希望的人能够长期维持的态度。当然，如果这就是事实，人们就不得不默认接受。有些人相信靠酸奶度日可以长生不老。对这种乐观主义者只需予以驳斥即可；没有必要进一步指出逃避死亡的某种其他途径。同样，证明布尔什维主义不会引向千禧盛世的论点，即便能够表明千禧盛世无法通过任何其他道路达成，也依然是有效的。然而社会问题中的真理并不完全像生理学或物理学中的真理，因为它取决于人们的信念。乐观主义往往通过让人们对可以避免的恶行无法容忍来证实自身；而另一方面，绝望则使世界变得如其所认为的那样糟糕。因此，对于那些不相信布尔什维主义的人而言，在它的位置上树立某种其他的希望是势在必行的。

我认为有两点必须承认：首先，资本主义许多最严重的弊端在共产主义（Communism）下完全可能继续存在；其次，根治这些弊端不可能一蹴而就，因为它需要普通人心智观念的转变。

当前制度的主要弊端是什么？我不认为单纯的财富不平等本身是一种非常严重的弊端。如果每个人都丰衣足食，某些人拥有超出所需的财富这一事实便无关紧要。

With a very moderate improvement in methods of production, it would be easy to ensure that everybody should have enough, even under capitalism, if wars and preparations for wars were abolished. The problem of poverty is by no means insoluble within the existing system, except when account is taken of psychological factors and the uneven distribution of power.
The graver evils of the capitalist system all arise from its uneven distribution of power. The possessors of capital wield an influence quite out of proportion to their numbers or their services to the community. They control almost the whole of education and the press; they decide what the average man shall know or not know; the cinema has given them a new method of propaganda, by which they enlist the support of those who are too frivolous even for illustrated papers. Very little of the intelligence of the world is really free: most of it is, directly or indirectly, in the pay of business enterprises or wealthy philanthropists. To satisfy capitalist interests, men are compelled to work much harder and more monotonously than they ought to work, and their education is scamped. Wherever, as in barbarous or semi-civilized countries, labour is too weak or too disorganized to protect itself, appalling cruelties are practised for private profit. Economic and political organizations become more and more vast, leaving less and less room for individual development and initiative. It is this sacrifice of the individual to the machine that is the fundamental evil of the modern world.
To cure this evil is not easy, because efficiency is promoted, at any given moment, though not in the long run, by sacrificing the individual to the smooth working of a vast organization, whether military or industrial. In war and in commercial competition, it is necessary to control individual impulses, to treat men as so many “bayonets” or “sabres” or “hands,” not as a society of separate people with separate tastes and capacities. Some sacrifice of individual impulses is, of course, essential to the existence of an ordered community, and this degree of sacrifice is, as a rule, not regretable even from the individual’s point of view. But what is demanded in a highly militarized or industrialized nation goes far beyond this very moderate degree.

===Chinese===

只要在生产方法上取得非常适度的改良，即便在资本主义制度下，如果废除战争和军备竞赛，确保每个人衣食无忧也是轻而易举的。贫困问题在现存制度内绝非无法解决，除非考虑到心理因素以及权力的不均等分配。

资本主义制度更为严重的弊端，全部源于其权力的不均等分配。资本的拥有者所施加的影响，与其人数或其对社会的贡献完全不成比例。他们控制着几乎全部的教育和报刊；他们决定普通人应当知晓什么或不应当知晓什么；电影为他们提供了一种新的宣传手段，借助这种手段，他们把那些甚至对画报都嫌太严肃轻浮的人拉拢为支持者。世界上极少有真正自由的心智：绝大多数心智直接或间接地受雇于商业企业或富有的慈善家。为了满足资本家的利益，人们被迫从事比理应承担的远为繁重和单调的劳动，他们的教育被敷衍了事。凡是在劳工过于弱小或过于涣散而无法自保的地方——如在野蛮或半文明国家——为了个人私利就会实行骇人听闻的残酷行径。经济和政治组织变得越来越庞大，留给个人发展和首创精神的空间越来越狭窄。正是个人对机器（机械化体制）的这种牺牲，构成了现代世界的根本弊端。

根治这一弊端并非易事，因为在任何特定时刻——尽管从长远来看并非如此——通过为了庞大组织（无论是军事组织还是工业组织）的顺畅运转而牺牲个人，可以促进效率。在战争和商业竞争中，必须控制个人的冲动，将人视为一个个“刺刀”、“马刀”或“劳力”，而非视作由拥有各自品味和才能的独立个体构成的社会。当然，为了有序社会的存在，对个人冲动的某种牺牲是必不可少的，而且这种程度的牺牲通常来说即便从个人的角度来看也是无可遗憾的。但在一个高度军事化或工业化的国家中所要求的，远远超出了这种非常适度的程度。

A society which is to allow much freedom to the individual must be strong enough to be not anxious about home defence, moderate enough to refrain from difficult external conquests, and rich enough to value leisure and a civilized existence more than an increase of consumable commodities.
But where the material conditions for such a state of affairs exist, the psychological conditions are not likely to exist unless power is very widely diffused throughout the community. Where power is concentrated in a few, it will happen, unless those few are very exceptional people, that they will value tangible achievements in the way of increase in trade or empire more than the slow and less obvious improvements that would result from better education combined with more leisure. The joys of victory are especially great to the holders of power, while the evils of a mechanical organization fall almost exclusively upon the less influential. For these reasons, I do not believe that any community in which power is much concentrated will long refrain from conflicts of the kind involving a sacrifice of what is most valuable in the individual. In Russia at this moment, the sacrifice of the individual is largely inevitable, because of the severity of the economic and military struggle. But I did not feel, in the Bolsheviks, any consciousness of the magnitude of this misfortune, or any realization of the importance of the individual as against the State. Nor do I believe that men who do realize this are likely to succeed, or to come to the top, in times when everything has to be done against personal liberty. The Bolshevik theory requires that every country, sooner or later, should go through what Russia is going through now. And in every country in such a condition we may expect to find the government falling into the hands of ruthless men, who have not by nature any love for freedom, and who will see little importance in hastening the transition from dictatorship to freedom. It is far more likely that such men will be tempted to embark upon new enterprises, requiring further concentration of forces, and postponing indefinitely the liberation of the populations which they use as their material.
For these reasons, equalization of wealth without equalization of power seems to me a rather small and unstable achievement.

===Chinese===

一个给予个人充分自由的社会，必须强大到无需担忧国土防卫，温和到克制不进行艰难的对外征服，并且富足到将闲暇与文明的存在看得重于消费品增加的程度。

但是在存在实现这种状态的物质条件的地方，除非权力在整个社会中非常广泛地普及分散，否则心理条件是不太可能存在的。在权力集中于少数人手中的地方，除非这少数人是极其非同寻常的人，否则他们往往会将以贸易或帝国扩张形式呈现的有形成就，看得重于由更好的教育结合更多的闲暇所带来的缓慢且不那么明显的改善。胜利的喜悦对于权力的执掌者而言尤为巨大，而机械化组织的恶果几乎完全落在了影响力较小的人身上。基于这些理由，我不相信任何权力高度集中的社会能够长期避免涉及牺牲个人最宝贵价值的那类冲突。目前在俄国，由于经济和军事斗争的严峻残酷性，个人的牺牲在很大程度上是不可避免的。但在布尔什维克身上，我并未感受到对这一不幸严重性的任何意识，也未看到对个人相对于国家之重要性的任何认识。我亦不相信那些确实认识到这一点的人，在一切都不得不违背个人自由行事的时期，能够取得成功或脱颖而出。布尔什维克理论要求每个国家迟早都要经历俄国目前正在经历的一切。而且在处于这种境况的每一个国家中，我们都可以预料政府将落入生性并不热爱自由的冷酷无情者之手，他们认为加速从专政向自由过渡微不足道。更有可能的是，这些人将被诱惑去从事需要进一步集中力量的新事业，并无限期推迟把他们用作原料的人民的解放。

基于这些理由，在我看来，没有权力均等化的财富均等化是一项相当微小且不稳固的成就。

But equalization of power is not a thing that can be achieved in a day. It requires a considerable level of moral, intellectual, and technical education. It requires a long period without extreme crises, in order that habits of tolerance and good nature may become common. It requires vigour on the part of those who are acquiring power, without a too desperate resistance on the part of those whose share is diminishing. This is only possible if those who are acquiring power are not very fierce, and do not terrify their opponents by threats of ruin and death. It cannot be done quickly, because quick methods require that very mechanism and subordination of the individual which we should struggle to prevent.
But even equalization of power is not the whole of what is needed politically. The right grouping of men for different purposes is also essential. Self-government in industry, for example, is an indispensable condition of a good society. Those acts of an individual or a group which have no very great importance for outsiders ought to be freely decided by that individual or group. This is recognized as regards religion, but ought to be recognized over a much wider field.
Bolshevik theory seems to me to err by concentrating its attention upon one evil, namely inequality of wealth, which it believes to be at the bottom of all others. I do not believe any one evil can be thus isolated, but if I had to select one as the greatest of political evils, I should select inequality of power. And I should deny that this is likely to be cured by the class-war and the dictatorship of the Communist party. Only peace and a long period of gradual improvement can bring it about.
Good relations between individuals, freedom from hatred and violence and oppression, general diffusion of education, leisure rationally employed, the progress of art and science﻿—these seem to me among the most important ends that a political theory ought to have in view. I do not believe that they can be furthered, except very rarely, by revolution and war; and I am convinced that at the present moment they can only be promoted by a diminution in the spirit of ruthlessness generated by the war.

但权力的均等化并非一朝一夕所能实现。它需要相当水平的道德、智力和技术教育。它需要一个没有极端危机的漫长时期，以便宽容与善良的习惯能够走向普及。它需要那些正在获取权力的人充满活力，同时那些份额正在减少的人不至于进行过于绝望的抵抗。唯有在正在获取权力的人不过于凶暴、且不用毁灭和死亡的威胁恐吓对手的前提下，这才是可能的。它不可能迅速达成，因为迅速的方法恰恰需要我们竭力防止的那种机制化（机械论）以及对个人的屈从。

但即便权力的均等化，也并非政治上所需的一切。出于不同目的将人进行恰当的组合也是必不可少的。例如，工业自治是一个美好社会不可或缺的前提。个人或群体的那些对局外人没有重大影响的行为，理应由该个人或群体自由决断。这在宗教领域已为人所承认，但应当在广泛得多的领域中得到承认。

在我看来，布尔什维克理论的谬误在于将注意力集中在单一弊端上，即财富不平等，并认为财富不平等是一切其他弊端的根源。我不相信任何单一弊端可以如此被孤立看待，但如果我必须挑选出最大的政治弊端，我将选择权力的不平等。而且我否认这能够通过阶级战争和共产党的专政得到医治。唯有和平以及漫长时期的逐步改善才能带来这一结果。

人与人之间的良好关系、免于仇恨、暴力和压迫的自由、教育的普遍普及、合理利用的闲暇、艺术与科学的进步——在我看来，这些都属于政治理论理应着眼的极为重要的目标。我不相信革命和战争能够促进这些目标（除极罕见情况外）；而且我深信，在眼下这个关头，唯有通过削弱由战争所激发的冷酷无情精神，才能推进这些目标。
