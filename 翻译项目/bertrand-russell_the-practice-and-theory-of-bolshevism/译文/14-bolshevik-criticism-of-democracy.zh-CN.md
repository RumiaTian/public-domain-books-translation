## III Bolshevik Criticism of Democracy / III 布尔什维克对民主的批判
===Original===

The Bolshevik argument against Parliamentary democracy as a method of achieving Socialism is a powerful one. My answer to it lies rather in pointing out what I believe to be fallacies in the Bolshevik method, from which I conclude that no swift method exists of establishing any desirable form of Socialism. But let us first see what the Bolshevik argument is.
In the first place, it assumes that those to whom it is addressed are absolutely certain that Communism is desirable, so certain that they are willing, if necessary, to force it upon an unwilling population at the point of the bayonet. It then proceeds to argue that, while capitalism retains its hold over propaganda and its means of corruption, Parliamentary methods are very unlikely to give a majority for Communism in the House of Commons, or to lead to effective action by such a majority even if it existed. Communists point out how the people are deceived, and how their chosen leaders have again and again betrayed them. From this they argue that the destruction of capitalism must be sudden and catastrophic; that it must be the work of a minority; and that it cannot be effected constitutionally or without violence. It is therefore, in their view, the duty of the Communist party in a capitalist country to prepare for armed conflict, and to take all possible measure for disarming the bourgeoisie and arming that part of the proletariat which is willing to support the Communists.
There is an air of realism and disillusionment about this position, which makes it attractive to those idealists who wish to think themselves cynics. But I think there are various points in which it fails to be as realistic as it pretends.
In the first place, it makes much of the treachery of Labour leaders in constitutional movements, but does not consider the possibility of the treachery of Communist leaders in a revolution. To this the Marxian would reply that in constitutional movements men are bought, directly or indirectly, by the money of the capitalists, but that revolutionary Communism would leave the capitalists no money with which to attempt corruption. This has been achieved in Russia, and could be achieved elsewhere. But selling oneself to the capitalists is not the only possible form of treachery. It is also possible, having acquired power, to use it for one’s own ends instead of for the people.

===Chinese===

布尔什维克反对将议会民主制作为实现社会主义之手段的论点是强有力的。我对这一论点的回答，主要在于指出我认为在布尔什维克方法中所存在的谬误，并由此得出结论：不存在任何能够迅速建立起任何令人向往的社会主义形态的捷径。但让我们首先看看布尔什维克的论点究竟是什么。

首先，它假定其听众绝对确信共产主义（Communism）是值得追求的，确信到必要时不惜用刺刀将其强加给不情愿的民众。接着它论证道：只要资本主义依然保持着对宣传的控制及其收买腐蚀手段，议会途径就极不可能在下议院（平民院，House of Commons）为共产主义赢得多数席位；即便存在这样的多数，也极不可能带来有效的行动。共产党人指出人民是如何受蒙蔽的，以及他们选出的领袖是如何一次又一次背叛他们的。由此他们论证道：摧毁资本主义必须是突发性且灾难性的；它必须是少数人的作为；而且它不可能通过宪政方式或在没有暴力的情况下达成。因此，在他们看来，资本主义国家的共产党的职责就是准备武装冲突，并采取一切可能措施解除资产阶级的武装，同时武装愿意支持共产党员的那部分无产阶级。

这种立场带有一种现实主义与去幻想化的意味，使其对那些自命愤世嫉俗的理想主义者极具吸引力。但我认为，在很多方面它并不像其所标榜的那样具有现实主义色彩。

首先，它对宪政运动中工党领袖的背叛大做文章，却不考虑革命中共产党领袖背叛的可能性。对此，马克思主义者会反驳说，在宪政运动中，人们直接或间接地被资本家的金钱所收买，但革命共产主义将使资本家没有任何金钱可用于企图收买。这在俄国已经实现，在其他地方也可以实现。然而，向资本家出卖自己并不是背叛的唯一可能形式。在攫取权力之后，将权力用于谋取私利而非造福人民，也同样是可能的。

===Original===

This is what I believe to be likely to happen in Russia: the establishment of a bureaucratic aristocracy, concentrating authority in its own hands, and creating a regime just as oppressive and cruel as that of capitalism. Marxians never sufficiently recognize that love of power is quite as strong a motive, and quite as great a source of injustice, as love of money; yet this must be obvious to any unbiased student of politics. It is also obvious that the method of violent revolution leading to a minority dictatorship is one peculiarly calculated to create habits of despotism which would survive the crisis by which they were generated. Communist politicians are likely to become just like the politicians of other parties: a few will be honest, but the great majority will merely cultivate the art of telling a plausible tale with a view to tricking the people into entrusting them with power. The only possible way by which politicians as a class can be improved is the political and psychological education of the people, so that they may learn to detect a humbug. In England men have reached the point of suspecting a good speaker, but if a man speaks badly they think he must be honest. Unfortunately, virtue is not so widely diffused as this theory would imply.
In the second place, it is assumed by the Communist argument that, although capitalist propaganda can prevent the majority from becoming Communists, yet capitalist laws and police forces cannot prevent the Communists, while still a minority, from acquiring a supremacy of military power. It is thought that secret propaganda can undermine the army and navy, although it is admittedly impossible to get the majority to vote at elections for the programme of the Bolsheviks. This view is based upon Russian experience, where the army and navy had suffered defeat and had been brutally ill used by incompetent Tsarist authorities. The argument has no application to more efficient and successful States. Among the Germans, even in defeat, it was the civilian population that began the revolution.
There is a further assumption in the Bolshevik argument which seems to me quite unwarrantable. It is assumed that the capitalist governments will have learned nothing from the experience of Russia. Before the Russian Revolution, governments had not studied Bolshevik theory.

===Chinese===

这正是我认为很可能在俄国发生的事情：建立起一个官僚特权阶层，将权力集中于自己手中，并创造出一个与资本主义政权一样压迫和残酷的政权。马克思主义者从未充分认识到，对权力的热爱正如对金钱的热爱一样，是一种同样强烈的动机，也是同样巨大的不公正根源；然而对任何客观公正的政治研究者来说，这必然是不言自明的。同样显而易见的是，通过暴力革命导致少数人专政的方法，是一种极其容易养成专制习惯的方法，而这种习惯将在产生它们的危机过去之后依然长期存在。共产党政客很可能会变得与其他党派的政客别无二致：少数人是诚实的，但绝大多数人将仅仅专注于巧言令色，以诱使人民将权力托付给他们。改善政客阶层的唯一可行途径是对人民进行政治与心理教育，使他们能够识破骗局。在英国，人们已经达到了对善辩者产生怀疑的地步，但如果一个人拙于言辞，他们就认为他必然是诚实的。遗憾的是，美德的普及程度并不像这一理论所暗示的那么广泛。

其次，共产党人的论点假定，尽管资本主义的宣传能够阻止多数人成为共产党员，但资本主义的法律和警察力量却无法阻止仍然处于少数地位的共产党人获得军事力量上的优势。人们认为秘密宣传能够瓦解陆军和海军，尽管人们公认无法让多数人在选举中投票支持布尔什维克的纲领。这种观点是基于俄国的经验，在俄国，陆海军遭遇了战败，并受到了无能沙皇当局的残酷虐待。这一论点并不适用于更具效率且更成功的国家。

And defeat in war created a revolutionary mood throughout Central and Eastern Europe. But now the holders of power are on their guard. There seems no reason whatever to suppose that they will supinely permit a preponderance of armed force to pass into the hands of those who wish to overthrow them, while, according to the Bolshevik theory, they are still sufficiently popular to be supported by a majority at the polls. Is it not as clear as noonday that in a democratic country it is more difficult for the proletariat to destroy the Government by arms than to defeat it in a general election? Seeing the immense advantages of a Government in dealing with rebels, it seems clear that rebellion could have little hope of success unless a very large majority supported it. Of course, if the army and navy were specially revolutionary, they might effect an unpopular revolution; but this situation, though something like it occurred in Russia, is hardly to be expected in the Western nations. This whole Bolshevik theory of revolution by a minority is one which might just conceivably have succeeded as a secret plot, but becomes impossible as soon as it is openly avowed and advocated.
But perhaps it will be said that I am caricaturing the Bolshevik doctrine of revolution. It is urged by advocates of this doctrine, quite truly, that all political events are brought about by minorities, since the majority are indifferent to politics. But there is a difference between a minority in which the indifferent acquiesce, and a minority so hated as to startle the indifferent into belated action. To make the Bolshevik doctrine reasonable, it is necessary to suppose that they believe the majority can be induced to acquiesce, at least temporarily, in the revolution made by the class-conscious minority. This, again, is based upon Russian experience: desire for peace and land led to a widespread support of the Bolsheviks in November 1917 on the part of people who have subsequently shown no love for Communism.
I think we come here to an essential part of Bolshevik philosophy. In the moment of revolution, Communists are to have some popular cry by which they win more support than mere Communism could win. Having thus acquired the State machine, they are to use it for their own ends. But this, again, is a method which can only be practised successfully so long as it is not avowed.

===Chinese===

而且在战争中的失败在整个中欧和东欧造成了革命的情绪。但如今当权者已经提高了警惕。没有任何理由假设他们会听任武装力量的优势落入那些意图推翻他们的人手中，而根据布尔什维克理论，他们此时在选票上依然广受欢迎并拥有多数人的支持。难道这不是如白昼般清晰的道理吗：在一个民主国家，无产阶级通过武力推翻政府，远比在大选中击败政府更加困难？鉴于政府在对付叛乱分子方面拥有的巨大优势，显而易见，除非有极其广大之多数人的支持，否则叛乱几乎毫无成功的希望。当然，如果陆军和海军特别具有革命性，他们可能会促成一场不受欢迎的革命；但这种局面——尽管在俄国曾发生过类似情况——在西方国家是难以指望的。布尔什维克这整套由少数人发动革命的理论，作为一种秘密阴谋或许勉强可以想象成功，但一旦被公开宣告和倡导，就变得彻底不可能了。

但也许有人会说我是在歪曲布尔什维克的革命学说。这一学说的拥护者相当真实地指出，一切政治事件都是由少数人促成的，因为多数人对政治漠不关心。但是在为漠不关心者所默认的少数，与那种令人憎恶以至于惊醒漠不关心者仓促行动的少数之间，存在着本质区别。要使布尔什维克的学说显得合理，就必须假定他们相信，能够诱导多数人至少暂时默认由有阶级意识的（class-conscious）少数人所发动的革命。这同样是基于俄国的经验：对和平与土地的渴望，导致了 1917 年 11 月广大民众对布尔什维克的广泛支持，而这些人随后并未表现出对共产主义的热爱。

我认为我们在此触及了布尔什维克哲学的核心部分。在革命关头，共产党人必须提出某种顺应民意的口号，以此赢得单凭纯粹共产主义所无法赢得的更多支持。在以此方式攫取国家机器之后，他们便将其用于自身的目的。但同样，这是一种唯有在其不公开言明的前提下才能成功施展的方法。

===Original===

It is to some extent habitual in politics. The Unionists in 1900 won a majority on the Boer War, and used it to endow brewers and Church schools. The Liberals in 1906 won a majority on Chinese labour, and used it to cement the secret alliance with France and to make an alliance with Tsarist Russia. President Wilson, in 1916, won his majority on neutrality, and used it to come into the war. This method is part of the stock-in-trade of democracy. But its success depends upon repudiating it until the moment comes to practise it. Those who, like the Bolsheviks, have the honesty to proclaim in advance their intention of using power for other ends than those for which it was given them, are not likely to have a chance of carrying out their designs.
What seems to me to emerge from these considerations is this: That in a democratic and politically educated country, armed revolution in favour of Communism would have no chance of succeeding unless it were supported by a larger majority than would be required for the election of a Communist Government by constitutional methods. It is possible that, if such a Government came into existence, and proceeded to carry out its programme, it would be met by armed resistance on the part of capital, including a large proportion of the officers in the army and navy. But in subduing this resistance it would have the support of that great body of opinion which believes in legality and upholds the constitution. Moreover, having, by hypothesis, converted a majority of the nation, a Communist Government could be sure of loyal help from immense numbers of workers, and would not be forced, as the Bolsheviks are in Russia, to suspect treachery everywhere. Under these circumstances, I believe that the resistance of the capitalists could be quelled without much difficulty, and would receive little support from moderate people. Whereas, in a minority revolt of Communists against a capitalist Government, all moderate opinion would be on the side of capitalism.
The contention that capitalist propaganda is what prevents the adoption of Communism by wage-earners is only very partially true. Capitalist propaganda has never been able to prevent the Irish from voting against the English, though it has been applied to this object with great vigour.

在某种程度上，这在政治中是屡见不鲜的。统一党人在 1900 年凭借布尔什战争（Boer War）赢得了多数席位，并用其资助酿酒商和教会学校。自由党人在 1906 年凭借华工问题（Chinese labour）赢得了多数，并用其巩固了同法国的秘密结盟以及同沙皇俄国的结盟。威尔逊总统（President Wilson）在 1916 年凭借中立立场赢得了多数，并用其参与了战争。这种方法是民主政治现成手段的一部分。但它的成功取决于在付诸实践的时机成熟之前矢口否认。像布尔什维克那样坦诚地提前宣告他们打算将权力用于授予他们权力的目的之外的人，是不太可能有机会实现其企图的。

从这些考量中，在我看来所浮现出的结论是：在一个民主且受过政治教育的国家，支持共产主义的武装革命要想取得成功，所需要的多数必须比通过宪政方式选举共产主义政府所需的多数更为庞大，否则绝无成功的机会。如果这样一个政府诞生并着手执行其纲领，它可能会遭到资本势力的武装抵抗，包括陆海军中相当大一部分军官的抵抗，这是完全可能的。但在平定这种抵抗时，它将获得信仰合法性并捍卫宪法的广大民意群体的支持。此外，在假定已经争取到全国多数人支持的情况下，共产主义政府可以确信能够获得广大工人的忠诚协助，而不会像俄国的布尔什维克那样被迫处处怀疑叛徒。在这些情况下，我相信资本家的抵抗可以毫不费力地被扑灭，并且几乎得不到温和人士的支持。反之，在共产党员针对资本主义政府的少数人叛乱中，所有温和派的舆论都将站在资本主义一边。

所谓资本主义宣传阻碍了工薪劳动者接受共产主义的说法，只是非常局部的真理。资本主义宣传从未能阻止爱尔兰人投票反对英国人，尽管它曾极其卖力地运用于这一目标。

It has proved itself powerless, over and over again, in opposing nationalist movements which had almost no moneyed support. It has been unable to cope with religious feeling. And those industrial populations which would most obviously benefit by Socialism have, in the main, adopted it, in spite of the opposition of employers. The plain truth is that Socialism does not arouse the same passionate interest in the average citizen as is roused by nationality and used to be roused by religion. It is not unlikely that things may change in this respect: we may be approaching a period of economic civil wars comparable to that of the religious civil wars that followed the Reformation. In such a period, nationalism is submerged by party: British and German Socialists, or British and German capitalists, will feel more kinship with each other than with compatriots of the opposite political camp. But when that day comes, there will be no difficulty, in highly industrial countries, in securing Socialist majorities; if Socialism is not then carried without bloodshed, it will be due to the unconstitutional action of the rich, not to the need of revolutionary violence on the part of the advocates of the proletariat. Whether such a state of opinion grows up or not depends mainly upon the stubbornness or conciliatoriness of the possessing classes, and, conversely, upon the moderation or violence of those who desire fundamental economic change. The majority which Bolsheviks regard as unattainable is chiefly prevented by the ruthlessness of their own tactics.
Apart from all arguments of detail, there are two broad objections to violent revolution in a democratic community. The first is that, when once the principle of respecting majorities as expressed at the ballot-box is abandoned, there is no reason to suppose that victory will be secured by the particular minority to which one happens to belong. There are many minorities besides Communists: religious minorities, teetotal minorities, militarist minorities, capitalist minorities. Any one of these could adopt the method of obtaining power advocated by the Bolsheviks, and any one would be just as likely to succeed as they are. What restrains these minorities, more or less, at present, is respect for the law and the constitution.

事实一次又一次地证明，面对几乎没有任何金钱支持的民族主义运动，这种宣传是软弱无力的。它无法抗衡宗教情感。而那些从社会主义中获益最明显的工业人口，尽管受到雇主的反对，大体上依然采纳了社会主义。显而易见的实情是，社会主义并未激起普通公民像面对民族问题所激起的、以及过去面对宗教问题所曾激起的同样热烈的激情。在这方面情况并非不可能发生改变：我们可能正在走向一个经济国内战争的时期，可与宗教改革（Reformation）之后的宗教国内战争相提并论。在这样一个时期，民族主义被党派所淹没：英国与德国的社会主义者，或者英国与德国的资本家，彼此感受到的亲缘关系将超过他们同相反政治阵营的同胞之间的关系。但当那一天到来时，在高度工业化的国家确保社会主义多数席位将毫无困难；如果到那时不能在不流血的情况下推行社会主义，那将归因于富人的违宪行动，而非由于无产阶级拥护者需要采取革命暴力。这样一种舆论状态是否会形成，主要取决于占有阶级的顽固或和解态度，反过来也取决于渴望根本经济变革之人的温和或暴力程度。布尔什维克视为无法达成的多数，主要是被他们自己策略的冷酷无情所阻碍的。

抛开所有细节论点不谈，在民主社会中反对暴力革命有两个宏观理由。首先，一旦放弃尊重选票箱所体现之多数原则，就没有理由假设胜利一定会属于某人碰巧归属的那个特定少数派。除了共产党员之外，还有许许多多的少数派：宗教少数派、禁酒少数派、军国主义少数派、资本家少数派。其中任何一个都可以采纳布尔什维克所倡导的夺权方法，且任何一个都有与他们同样成功的可能。目前或多或少制约这些少数派的，是对法律和宪法的尊重。

Bolsheviks tacitly assume that every other party will preserve this respect while they themselves, unhindered, prepare the revolution. But if their philosophy of violence becomes popular, there is not the slightest reason to suppose that they will be its beneficiaries. They believe that Communism is for the good of the majority; they ought to believe that they can persuade the majority on this question, and to have the patience to set about the task of winning by propaganda.
The second argument of principle against the method of minority violence is that abandonment of law, when it becomes widespread, lets loose the wild beast, and gives a free rein to the primitive lusts and egoisms which civilization in some degree curbs. Every student of medieval thought must have been struck by the extraordinarily high value placed upon law in that period. The reason was that, in countries infested by robber barons, law was the first requisite of progress. We, in the modern world, take it for granted that most people will be law-abiding, and we hardly realize what centuries of effort have gone to making such an assumption possible. We forget how many of the good things that we unquestionably expect would disappear out of life if murder, rape, and robbery with violence became common. And we forget even more how very easily this might happen. The universal class-war foreshadowed by the Third International, following upon the loosening of restraints produced by the late war, and combined with a deliberate inculcation of disrespect for law and constitutional government, might, and I believe would, produce a state of affairs in which it would be habitual to murder men for a crust of bread, and in which women would only be safe while armed men protected them. The civilized nations have accepted democratic government as a method of settling internal disputes without violence. Democratic government may have all the faults attributed to it, but it has the one great merit that people are, on the whole, willing to accept it as a substitute for civil war in political disputes. Whoever sets to work to weaken this acceptance, whether in Ulster or in Moscow, is taking a fearful responsibility. Civilization is not so stable that it cannot be broken up; and a condition of lawless violence is not one out of which any good thing is likely to emerge.

布尔什维克默认所有其他党派都将保持这种尊重，而他们自己则毫无阻碍地准备革命。但如果他们的暴力哲学流行开来，没有任何丝毫理由假设他们会是其受益者。他们相信共产主义是为了多数人的利益；他们理应相信自己能够在这个问题上说服多数人，并有耐心着手进行通过宣传赢得多数的任务。

反对少数人暴力方法的第二个原则性论据是：法律的废弃一旦走向普遍，就会释放野兽，让文明在某种程度上予以遏制的原始欲望与利己主义肆意横行。每一位研究中世纪思想的学者，必然都会被那个时期对法律赋予的极高价值所震撼。原因在于，在盗匪贵族横行的国家，法律是进步的首要前提。我们在现代世界把大多数人遵纪守法视为理所当然，几乎没有意识到为了使这种假设成为可能付出了多少个世纪的努力。我们忘记了：如果谋杀、强奸和暴力抢劫成为司空见惯之事，我们在生活中毫无疑问所期盼的许多美好事物都将荡然无存。而我们更忘记了这种情况是何等容易发生。第三国际所预示的普遍阶级战争，紧随着最近这场战争所产生的约束松弛而来，再伴随着对法律和宪政政府不尊重的蓄意灌输，可能——且我相信必定——会产生这样一种局面：为了争夺一块面包皮而杀人成为常态，而妇女唯有在武装人员的保护下才能获得安全。文明国家已经接受了民主政府作为不诉诸暴力解决内部争端的方法。民主政府或许存在被归咎于它的所有缺点，但它拥有一个巨大的优点，那就是人们总体上愿意接受它作为政治争端中内战的替代物。无论是在乌尔斯特（Ulster）还是在莫斯科（Moscow），任何人只要着手削弱这种接受度，就背负着可怕的责任。文明并非稳固到不可瓦解；而一种无法无天的暴力状态，是绝不可能从中产生任何美好事物的。
