## XVIII Odds and Ends / 零零碎碎

===Original===
The Spring-in-rock, or, as it was sometimes, by a curious perversion, called, the “rock-in-spring,” was a spring running out of a cave-like fissure in a high limestone cliff. Here the old man sheltered himself on that dreary Christmas evening, until Bud brought his roan colt to the top of the cliff above, and he and Ralph helped the old man up the cliff and into the saddle. Ralph went back to bed, but Bud, who was only too eager to put in his best licks, walked by the side of old John Pearson the six miles over to Buckeye Run, and at last, after eleven o’clock, he deposited him in a hollow sycamore by the road, there to wait the coming of the mail-wagon that would carry him into Jackson County.

“Goodbye,” said the basket-maker, as Bud mounted the colt to return. “Ef I’m wanted jest send me word, and I’ll make a forrard movement any time. I don’t like this ’ere thing of running off in the nighttime. But I reckon General Winfield Scott would a ordered a retreat ef he’d a been in my shoes. I’m lots obleeged to you. Akordin’ to my tell, we’re all of us selfish in everything; but I’ll be dog-on’d ef I don’t believe you and one or two more is exceptions.”

===Chinese===
「石中泉」——有时也被古怪地倒了过来，叫成「泉中石」——是一眼泉水，从高高的石灰岩崖壁上一道洞穴般的裂缝里流出来。那个凄凉的圣诞夜，老人就躲在这里，直到巴德把他的沙毛马驹牵到崖顶上，他和拉尔夫两人把老人扶上崖、扶上鞍。拉尔夫回床接着躺，巴德呢，正巴不得卖尽力气，便步行六英里，一路陪着老约翰·皮尔逊到七叶树溪（Buckeye Run），十一点过后，才把他安顿在路边一棵空心梧桐树里，在那儿等送他去杰克逊县（Jackson County）的邮车。

「再会了，」巴德翻身上马要回去时，编筐匠说。「要是用得着俺，捎个话来，俺随时来个向前推进。俺不稀罕这种夜里开溜的勾当。不过俺寻思，温菲尔德·斯科特将军（General Winfield Scott）要是换了俺这双鞋，也得下令撤退。多谢你啦。照俺的说法，咱们事事都自私；可要是俺不信你和另外一两位是例外，俺就不是人。」

===Original===
Whether it was that the fact that Pete Jones had got consid’able shuck up demoralized his followers, or whether it was that the old man’s flight was suspected, the mob did not turn out in very great force, and the tarring was postponed indefinitely, for by the time they came together it became known somehow that the man with a wooden leg had outrun them all. But the escape of one devoted victim did not mollify the feelings of the people toward the next one.

By the time Bud returned his arm was very painful, and the next day he went under Dr. Small’s treatment to reduce the fracture. Whatever suspicions Bud might have of Pete Jones, he was not afflicted with Ralph’s dread of the silent young doctor. And if there was anything Small admired it was physical strength and courage. Small wanted Bud on his side, and least of all did he want him to be Ralph’s champion. So that the silent, cool, and skillful doctor went to work to make an impression on Bud Means.

===Chinese===
不知是因为皮特·琼斯挨的那一下够呛、弄得他的党羽泄了气，还是因为老头儿的出逃走漏了风声，那伙暴民没凑起多大的阵仗，涂柏油的事无限期推了下去——因为他们聚到一块儿的时候，不知怎么都听说了：装木腿的那位把他们全都甩在了后头。可一位忠心的受害者脱了身，并没有让人们对下一位心软半分。

巴德回来时胳膊已疼得厉害，第二天便去找斯莫尔医生诊治复位。不管巴德对皮特·琼斯有几分疑心，他却没染上拉尔夫对这位沉默的年轻医生的那种畏惧。而斯莫尔要是还钦佩什么，那就是体力和胆气。斯莫尔想把巴德拉到自己一边，最不愿见到的就是他当拉尔夫的卫士。于是这位沉默、冷静、手艺高明的医生，动起手来给巴德·米恩斯留个好印象。

===Original===
Other influences were at work upon him also. Mrs. Means volleyed and thundered in her usual style about his “takin’ up with a one-legged thief, and runnin’ arter that master that was a mighty suspicious kind of a customer, akordin’ to her tell. She’d allers said so. Ef she’d a been consulted he wouldn’t a been hired. He warn’t fit company fer nobody.”

And old Jack Means ’lowed Bud must want to have *their* barns burnt like some other folkses had been. Fer his part, he had sense enough to know they was some people as it wouldn’t do to set a body’s self agin. And as fer him, he didn’t butt his brains out agin a buckeye-tree. Not when he was sober. And so they managed, during Bud’s confinement to the house, to keep him well supplied with all the ordinary discomforts of life.

But one visit from Martha Hawkins, ten words of kindly inquiry from her, and the remark that his broken arm reminded her of something she had seen at the East and something somebody said the time she was to Bosting, were enough to repay the champion a thousand fold for all that he suffered. Indeed, that visit, and the recollection of Ralph’s saying that Jesus Christ was a sort of a Flat Creeker himself, were manna in the wilderness to Bud.

===Chinese===
别的力量也在他身上使劲。米恩斯太太拿出她惯常的那套连珠炮，轰骂他「跟一个一条腿的贼混在一块儿，还追着那个老师跑——照俺的说法，那老师就是个顶顶可疑的主儿。俺从来就这么说。当初要是问了俺，压根儿就不会雇他。他跟谁都不配做伴。」

老杰克·米恩斯则一口咬定，巴德准是想让*他们自家*的谷仓也像别人家那样烧掉。就他自己而言，他还算有点脑子，知道世上有些人，是万万不能跟他对着干的。至于他嘛，他可不会拿自己的脑壳往七叶树上撞。脑子清醒的时候不会。就这样，趁巴德困在屋里养伤的工夫，一家人把他人生在世的种种寻常苦楚，供应得足足的。

可玛莎·霍金斯来探望了一回，问了十来句关切的话，又说他的断臂让她想起她在东部见过的什么，还有她去波士顿那阵子听谁说过的一句什么——光是这些，就够报答这位卫士千倍，抵过他受的一切苦。真的，那一趟探望，再加上想起拉尔夫说过的那句「耶稣基督自己也算是个弗拉特溪人」，对巴德简直就是荒野里的吗哪。

===Original===
Poor Shocky was sick. The excitement had been too much for him, and though his fever was very slight it was enough to produce just a little delirium. Either Ralph or Miss Martha was generally at the cabin.

“They’re coming,” said Shocky to Ralph, “they’re coming. Pete Jones is a-going to bind me out for a hundred years. I wish Hanner would hold me so’s he couldn’t. God’s forgot all about us here in Flat Creek, and there’s nobody to help it.”

And he shivered at every sudden sound. He was never free from this delirious fright except when the master held him tight in his arms. He staggered around the floor, the very shadow of Shocky, and was so terrified by the approach of darkness that Ralph stayed in the cabin on Wednesday night and Miss Hawkins stayed on Thursday night. On Friday, Bud sent a note to Ralph, askin him to come and see him.

===Chinese===
可怜的肖基病倒了。这一场惊吓对他来说太过火了；虽说烧得不重，也够引起一点轻微的谵语。小屋里通常总有拉尔夫或玛莎小姐守着。

「他们来了，」肖基对拉尔夫说，「他们来了。皮特·琼斯要把我卖身帮工一百年。我恨不得汉娜抱住我，他就带不成了。上帝把咱们弗拉特溪的人全忘光了，没人帮得了这个忙。」

每一声突然的响动都叫他一哆嗦。只有老师把他紧紧搂在怀里时，他才从这谵语的惊惶里解脱出来。他在地板上摇摇晃晃地走来走去，活脱脱成了肖基的影子；他那样害怕天黑下来，礼拜三夜里拉尔夫便留在小屋，礼拜四夜里霍金斯小姐留下。礼拜五，巴德给拉尔夫捎了张条子，请他来见一面。

“You see, Mr. Hartsook, I ha’n’t forgot what was said about puttin’ in our best licks for Jesus Christ. I’ve been a-trying to read some about him while I set here. And I read where he said somethin about doing fer the least of his brethren being as the same like as if it was done fer Jesus Christ his-self. Now there’s Shocky. I reckon, p’r’aps, as anybody is a little brother of Jesus Christ, it is that Shocky. Pete Jones and his brother Bill is determined to have him back there to-morry. Bekase you see, Pete’s one of the County Commissioners and to-morry’s the day that they bind out. He wants to bind out that boy jes’ to spite ole Pearson and you and me. You see, the ole woman’s been helped by the neighbors, and he’ll claim Shocky to be a pauper, and they a’n’t no human soul here as dares to do a thing con*tra*ry to Pete. Couldn’t you git him over to Lewisburg? I’ll lend you my roan colt.”

Ralph thought a minute. He dared not take Shocky to the uncle’s where he found his only home. But there was Miss Nancy Sawyer, the old maid who was everybody’s blessing. He could ask her to keep him. And, at any rate, he would save Shocky somehow.

As he went out in the dusk, he met Hannah in the lane.

「你瞧，哈茨克先生，俺没忘了咱们说过的，要为耶稣基督卖尽力气。俺坐在这儿，一直想法子读点写他的书。俺读到他说过的话：为他的弟兄中最小的一个做的，就跟做在耶稣基督本人身上一个样。如今就有个肖基。俺寻思，要说谁够得上是耶稣基督的小兄弟，那就是肖基了。皮特·琼斯和他兄弟比尔铁了心，明儿个要把他弄回去。因为你知道，皮特是县委专员之一，明儿个正是他们办卖身帮工的日子。他把那孩子抓去卖身，就是要气死老皮尔逊，气死你俺。你瞧，那老婆子一直靠邻居接济过活，他就会一口咬定肖基是贫民；这一带没有一个人敢做一件违*背*皮特的事。你能不能把他送到刘易斯堡去？俺把俺的沙毛马借给你。」

拉尔夫想了一分钟。他不敢把肖基带到叔叔家去——那是他唯一的栖身之处。可还有南希·索耶小姐，那位人人沾她福气的姑娘。他可以求她收留。无论如何，他总要把肖基救下来。

他趁暮色出门时，在小巷里遇见了汉娜。
