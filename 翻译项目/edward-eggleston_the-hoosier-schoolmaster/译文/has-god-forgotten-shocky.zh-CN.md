## IX Has God Forgotten Shocky? / 上帝忘掉肖基了吗？

===Original===
“Pap wants to know ef you would spend to-morry and Sunday at our house?” said one of Squire Hawkins’s girls, on the very next evening, which was Friday. The old Squire was thoughtful enough to remember that Ralph would not find it very pleasant “boarding out” all the time he was entitled to spend at Pete Jones’s. For in view of the fact that Mr. Pete Jones sent seven children to the school, the “master” in Flat Creek district was bound to spend two weeks in that comfortable place, sleeping in a preoccupied bed, in the “furdest corner,” with insufficient cover, under an insufficient roof, and eating floating islands of salt pork fished out of oceans of hot lard. Ralph was not slow to accept the relief offered by the hospitable justice of the peace, whose principal business seemed to be the adjustment of the pieces of which he was composed. And as Shocky traveled the same road, Ralph took advantage of the opportunity to talk with him. The master could not dismiss Hannah wholly from his mind. He would at least read the mystery of her life, if Shocky could be prevailed on to furnish the clue.

===Chinese===
「俺爹想问问你，明儿个和礼拜天，肯不肯上俺家去过？」第二天傍晚——那是礼拜五——霍金斯乡绅（Squire Hawkins）家的一位姑娘这么问。老乡绅心思周到，想到拉尔夫在皮特·琼斯（Pete Jones）家该搭伙的那段日子若全靠「外宿」打发，怕不会好过。因为皮特·琼斯先生送了七个孩子上学，弗拉特溪学区的「老师」按规定就得在那户舒坦人家住上两星期：睡一张被人先睡过的床，睡在「顶里头的角落」，盖的不够厚，房顶也不严实，吃的是从滚烫猪油的大洋里捞出来的咸肉浮岛。这位好客的治安法官递来解围的好意，拉尔夫一点也没有迟疑就接下了——这位法官的主要营生，似乎就是把他浑身上下的零件逐一调摆妥帖。而肖基走的是同一条路，拉尔夫便趁这个机会跟他说话。老师的心里放不下汉娜。哪怕肖基肯提供线索，让他至少读懂她生命里的谜也好。

===Original===
“Poor old tree!” said Shocky, pointing to a crooked and gnarled elm standing by itself in the middle of a field. For when the elm, naturally the most graceful of trees, once gets a “bad set,” it can grow to be the most deformed. This solitary tree had not a single straight limb.

“Why do you say ‘poor old tree’?” asked Ralph.

===Chinese===
「可怜的老树！」肖基指着田中央一棵孤零零、虬曲多节的榆树说。榆树本是树中最婀娜多姿的一种，可一旦「落了坏地」，也能长成最歪扭的模样。这棵孤零零的树，没有一根枝条是直的。

「你怎么说它『可怜的老树』？」拉尔夫问。

===Original===
“ ’Cause it’s lonesome. All its old friends is dead and chopped down, and there’s their stumps a-standin’ jes like gravestones. It *must* be lonesome. Some folks says it don’t feel, but I think it does. Everything seems to think and feel. See it nodding its head to them other trees in the woods? and a-wantin’ to shake hands! But it can’t move. I think that tree must a growed in the night.”

“Why, Shocky?”

“ ’Cause it’s so crooked,” and Shocky laughed at his own conceit; “must a growed when they was no light so as it could see how to grow.”

===Chinese===
「因为它孤零零的呀。它的老朋友们都死了、都砍倒了，只剩那些树桩立在那儿，跟墓碑一个样。它*准是*孤单的。有人说树没有感觉，我看它有。样样东西好像都会想事儿、都有感觉。你瞧它朝林子那边的树点头呢！还想跟人家握手！可它动不了。我看呀，这棵树准是在黑夜里长出来的。」

「怎么见得，肖基？」

「它长得多歪呀，」肖基为自己的妙想笑了，「长的时候准是没有光，看不见该怎么长。」

===Original===
And then they walked on in silence a minute. Presently Shocky began looking up into Ralph’s eyes to get a smile. “I guess that tree feels just like me. Don’t you?”

“Why, how do you feel?”

===Chinese===
随后两人默默走了一阵。不一会儿，肖基仰起眼睛看拉尔夫的脸色，想讨一个笑。「我猜那棵树心里的滋味跟我一模一样。你说是不是？」

「哦？你是什么滋味？」

===Original===
“Kind o’ bad and lonesome, and like as if I wanted to die, you know. Felt that way ever sence they put my father into the graveyard, and sent my mother to the poorhouse and Hanner to ole Miss Means’s. What kind of a place is a poorhouse? Is it a poorer place than Means’s? I wish I was dead and one of them clouds was a-carryin’ me and Hanner and mother up to where father’s gone, you know! I wonder if God forgets all about poor folks when their father dies and their mother gits into the poorhouse? Do you think He does? Seems so to me. Maybe God lost track of my father when he come away from England and crossed over the sea. Don’t nobody on Flat Creek keer fer God, and I guess God don’t keer fer Flat Creek. But I would, though, ef he’d git my mother out of the poorhouse and git Hanner away from Means’s, and let me kiss my mother every night, you know, and sleep on my Hanner’s arm, jes like I used to afore father died, you see.”

===Chinese===
「就是有点难受、有点孤单，就像想死掉算了，你知道的。打从他们把我爹埋进墓地、把我娘送进贫民院、把汉娜送到老米恩斯太太家那天起，我就一直是这个滋味。贫民院是个什么样的地方？比米恩斯家还穷的地方吗？我恨不得自己死了，有一朵云把我、汉娜和娘一起接到爹去了的地方，你知道的！我不知道，穷人家的爹死了、娘进了贫民院，上帝是不是就把这家人忘得一干二净？你说他会不会？我瞧着像。兴许我爹当年离开英国、漂洋过海过来的时候，上帝就跟丢了他。弗拉特溪上没有一个人把上帝放在心上，我猜上帝也不把弗拉特溪放在心上。可我还是愿意把上帝放在心上，只要他把娘从贫民院里弄出来，把汉娜从米恩斯家弄出来，让我每天夜里亲亲娘，睡在我汉娜的胳膊上，就像爹没死的时候那样，你瞧。」

===Original===
Ralph wanted to speak, but he couldn’t. And so Shocky, with his eyes looking straight ahead, and as if forgetting Ralph’s presence, told over the thoughts that he had often talked over to the fence-rails and the trees. “It was real good in Mr. Pearson to take me, wasn’t it? Else I’d a been bound out tell I was twenty-one, maybe, to some mean man like Ole Means. And I a’n’t but seven. And it would take me fourteen years to git twenty-one, and I never could live with my mother again after Hanner gets done her time. ’Cause, you see, Hanner’ll be through in three more year, and I’ll be ten and able to work, and we’ll git a little place about as big as Granny Sanders’s, and﻿—”

Ralph did not hear another word of what Shocky said that afternoon. For there, right before them, was Granny Sanders’s log-cabin, with its row of lofty sunflower stalks, now dead and dry, in front, with its rainwater barrel by the side of the low door, and its ash-barrel by the fence. In this cabin lived alone the old and shriveled hag whose hideousness gave her a reputation for almost supernatural knowledge. She was at once doctress and newspaper. She collected and disseminated medicinal herbs and personal gossip. She was in every regard indispensable to the intellectual life of the neighborhood. In the matter of her medical skill we cannot express an opinion, for her “yarbs” are not to be found in the pharmacopoeia of science.

What took Ralph’s breath was to find Dr. Small’s fine, faultless horse standing at the door. What did Henry Small want to visit this old quack for?

===Chinese===
拉尔夫想说话，却说不出来。于是肖基眼睛望着正前方，像是忘了拉尔夫就在身边，把那些他常常讲给篱笆桩子和树木听的念头，一一数了出来。「皮尔逊先生肯收留我，真是天大的好心，是不是？不然我兴许要被卖身帮工到二十一岁，给老米恩斯那样刻薄的人干活。我今年才七岁。到二十一岁还得熬十四年；那样的话，等汉娜满期之后，我就再也不能跟娘一块儿过日子了。因为，你瞧，汉娜再有三年就满期，那时我十岁，能干活了，我们就能置一个小地方，跟桑德斯婆婆（Granny Sanders）家差不多大，还有——」

那天下午肖基后来还说了些什么，拉尔夫一个字也没听进去。因为就在他们眼前，立着桑德斯婆婆的圆木小屋：屋前立着一排高高的向日葵秆，如今枯死了；矮门边放着一口接雨水的大桶，篱笆旁是一炉灰桶。屋里独住着那个干瘪的老妖婆，她那副丑相，倒让她得了个近乎通晓神鬼的名声。她一身兼作医生和报纸：采集并散布草药，也采集并散布家长里短。无论从哪方面说，她都是这一带精神生活里少不了的人物。至于她的医道，我们不便置评，因为她那些「草头药」（yarbs），在科学的药典里是查不到的。

叫拉尔夫倒吸一口凉气的是，斯莫尔医生那匹漂亮无瑕的马正拴在门前。亨利·斯莫尔（Henry Small）来看这个老巫婆做什么？
