## A Toy Princess / 玩具公主

===Original===
More than a thousand years ago, in a country quite on the other side of the world, it fell out that the people all grew so very polite that they hardly ever spoke to each other. And they never said more than was quite necessary, as “Just so,” “Yes indeed,” “Thank you,” and “If you please.” And it was thought to be the rudest thing in the world for anyone to say they liked or disliked, or loved or hated, or were happy or miserable. No one ever laughed aloud, and if anyone had been seen to cry they would at once have been avoided by their friends.

===Chinese===
一千多年前，在世界的另一头有一个国家，那里的人们变得彬彬有礼过了头，结果彼此之间几乎绝口不说话。他们绝不多说一句必要之外的话，无非是“正是如此”“的确如此”“谢谢”和“劳驾”这么几句。谁要是说出自己喜欢什么、讨厌什么，爱什么、恨什么，是快活还是难过，都被认为是天下最失礼的事。谁也不放声大笑；要是叫人瞧见谁哭了，朋友们立刻就会躲开他。

===Original===
The King of this country married a Princess from a neighbouring land, who was very good and beautiful, but the people in her own home were as unlike her husband’s people as it was possible to be. They laughed, and talked, and were noisy and merry when they were happy, and cried and lamented if they were sad. In fact, whatever they felt they showed at once, and the Princess was just like them.

===Chinese===
这个国家的国王娶了一位邻国公主。公主又善良又美丽，只是她娘家的人同她丈夫的臣民，恰恰是天下最不像的两路人：他们高兴时又说又笑、热热闹闹、欢欢喜喜，悲伤时就放声痛哭、哀哀悲叹。总之，心里有什么感触，脸上就立刻现出什么，公主也正是这脾气。

===Original===
So when she came to her new home, she could not at all understand her subjects, or make out why there was no shouting and cheering to welcome her, and why everyone was so distant and formal. After a time, when she found they never changed, but were always the same, just as stiff and quiet, she wept, and began to pine for her own old home.

===Chinese===
于是到了新家，她全然不能理解她的臣民，也弄不明白为什么没有欢呼喝彩来迎接她，为什么人人都这样疏远、这样拘谨。过了一阵子，她见他们从不变通，永远是老样子，永远那样呆板沉默，便哭了，思念起自己的故土来。

===Original===
Every day she grew thinner and paler. The courtiers were much too polite to notice how ill their young Queen looked; but she knew it herself, and believed she was going to die.

===Chinese===
她一天比一天消瘦，一天比一天苍白。廷臣们礼数太重，谁也不肯点破年轻王后病成了什么样子；可她自己心里明白，觉得自己是活不成了。

===Original===
Now she had a fairy godmother, named Taboret, whom she loved very dearly, and who was always kind to her. When she knew her end was drawing near she sent for her godmother, and when she came had a long talk with her quite alone.

===Chinese===
她有一位仙女教母，名叫塔博蕾（Taboret），她非常敬爱教母，教母也一向待她慈爱。她知道自己大限将至，便派人去请教母来；教母到了以后，两人单独密谈了许久。

===Original===
No one knew what was said, and soon afterwards a little Princess was born, and the Queen died. Of course all the courtiers were sorry for the poor Queen’s death, but it would have been thought rude to say so. So, although there was a grand funeral, and the court put on mourning, everything else went on much as it had done before.

===Chinese===
没有人知道她们说了些什么。不久，一位小公主降生了，王后便去世了。廷臣们自然都哀怜可怜的王后之死，但说出来便会被认为失礼。所以，尽管举行了盛大的葬礼，宫廷也挂了孝，其余一切照旧，同从前没什么两样。

===Original===
The little baby was christened Ursula, and given to some court ladies to be taken charge of. Poor little Princess! She cried hard enough, and nothing could stop her.

===Chinese===
小婴儿行了洗礼，取名厄休拉（Ursula），交给几位宫廷女官照料。可怜的小公主！她哭得可凶了，怎么也止不住。

===Original===
All her ladies were frightened, and said that they had not heard such a dreadful noise for a long time. But, till she was about two years old, nothing could stop her crying when she was cold or hungry, or crowing when she was pleased.

===Chinese===
女官们都吓坏了，说这么可怕的声音，她们许久没有听见过。可是直到两岁上下，她冷了饿了照旧哭个不停，高兴了照旧咯咯直笑，谁也拿她没法子。

===Original===
After that she began to understand a little what was meant when her nurses told her, in cold, polite tones, that she was being naughty, and she grew much quieter.

===Chinese===
打那以后，乳母们用冷冰冰、客客气气的声调说她不听话，她渐渐能听懂一点了，也就安静了许多。

===Original===
She was a pretty little girl, with a round baby face and big merry blue eyes; but as she grew older, her eyes grew less and less merry and bright, and her fat little face grew thin and pale. She was not allowed to play with any other children, lest she might learn bad manners; and she was not taught any games or given any toys. So she passed most of her time, when she was not at her lessons, looking out of the window at the birds flying against the clear blue sky; and sometimes she would give a sad little sigh when her ladies were not listening.

===Chinese===
她是个漂亮的小姑娘，圆圆的娃娃脸，一双快活的蓝色大眼睛。可她越长越大，那双眼睛的快活和神采却越来越少，胖乎乎的小脸也渐渐消瘦、苍白。不许她同别的孩子一起玩，怕她学坏规矩；也没人教她游戏，没人给她玩具。于是，不上课的时候，她多半是望着窗外，看鸟儿在晴朗的碧空里飞翔；有时趁女官们不留神，她会小小地、伤心地叹一口气。

===Original===
One day the old fairy Taboret made herself invisible, and flew over to the King’s palace to see how things were going on there. She went straight up to the nursery, where she found poor little Ursula sitting by the window, with her head leaning on her hand.

===Chinese===
一天，老仙女塔博蕾隐去了身形，飞到国王的宫殿，看看那边情形如何。她径直上了育婴室，只见可怜的小厄休拉坐在窗边，一手托着腮。

===Original===
It was a very grand room, but there were no toys or dolls about, and when the fairy saw this, she frowned to herself and shook her head.

===Chinese===
那是一间极华丽的屋子，可四下里没有一件玩具、一个布娃娃。仙女见了，暗暗皱眉，摇了摇头。

===Original===
“Your Royal Highness’s dinner is now ready,” said the head nurse to Ursula.

===Chinese===
“殿下的晚饭已经备好了。”保姆长对厄休拉说。

===Original===
“I don’t want any dinner,” said Ursula, without turning her head.

===Chinese===
“我不想吃饭。”厄休拉说，头也不回。

===Original===
“I think I have told your Royal Highness before that it is not polite to say you don’t want anything, or that you don’t like it,” said the nurse. “We are waiting for your Royal Highness.”

===Chinese===
“殿下，我记得早禀告过了，说自己不想吃什么、不喜欢什么，都是不礼貌的。”保姆说，“我们都候着殿下呢。”

===Original===
So the Princess got up and went to the dinner-table, and Taboret watched them all the time. When she saw how pale little Ursula was, and how little she ate, and that there was no talking or laughing allowed, she sighed and frowned even more than before, and then she flew back to her fairy home, where she sat for some hours in deep thought.

===Chinese===
于是公主起身走向餐桌，塔博蕾在一旁看着这一切。她见小厄休拉脸色那样苍白，吃得那样少，席间又不许说笑，不由得叹了口气，眉头皱得更紧了。随后她飞回仙府，坐在那里沉思了好几个钟头。

===Original===
At last she rose, and went out to pay a visit to the largest shop in Fairyland.

===Chinese===
最后她站起身，动身去拜访仙境（Fairyland）最大的一家铺子。

===Original===
It was a queer sort of shop. It was neither a grocer’s, nor a draper’s, nor a hatter’s. Yet it contained sugar, and dresses, and hats. But the sugar was magic sugar, which transformed any liquid into which it was put; the dresses each had some special charm, and the hats were wishing-caps. It was, in fact, a shop where every sort of spell or charm was sold.

===Chinese===
那是一家古怪的铺子。它既不是杂货铺，也不是绸缎庄，更不是帽子店，可铺子里又有糖，有衣裳，有帽子。只是那糖是魔法糖，放进什么液体里，什么液体就要变形；那些衣裳每一件都带着特别的魔力，帽子则全是许愿帽。其实，这是一家出售各式咒语法术的铺子。

===Original===
Into this shop Taboret flew; and as she was well known there as a good customer, the master of the shop came forward to meet her at once, and bowing, begged to know what he could get for her.

===Chinese===
塔博蕾飞进这家铺子。她是这儿有名的老主顾，店主一见她便迎上前来，躬着身问她要点什么货。

===Original===
“I want,” said Taboret, “a Princess.”

===Chinese===
“我要，”塔博蕾说，“一位公主。”

===Original===
“A Princess!” said the shopman, who was in reality an old wizard. “What size do you want it? I have one or two in stock.”

===Chinese===
“一位公主！”店伙说——他其实就是个老巫师。“您要多大尺寸的？我手头现成一两位都有。”

===Original===
“It must look now about six years old. But it must grow.”

===Chinese===
“现在看上去要有六岁上下。可她还必须会长。”

===Original===
“I can make you one,” said the wizard, “but it’ll come rather expensive.”

===Chinese===
“我可以给您做一个，”巫师说，“不过价钱可不便宜。”

===Original===
“I don’t mind that,” said Taboret. “See! I want it to look exactly like this,” and so saying she took a portrait of Ursula out of her bosom and gave it to the old man, who examined it carefully.

===Chinese===
“那我不在乎，”塔博蕾说，“瞧！我要她同这个一模一样。”说着，她从怀里取出一张厄休拉的画像，递给老人，老人细细端详了一番。

===Original===
“I’ll get it for you,” he said. “When will you want it?”

===Chinese===
“我给您办妥。”他说，“您几时要？”

===Original===
“As soon as possible,” said Taboret. “By tomorrow evening if possible. How much will it cost?”

===Chinese===
“越快越好，”塔博蕾说，“最好明晚就要。要多少钱？”

===Original===
“It’ll come to a good deal,” said the wizard, thoughtfully. “I have such difficulty in getting these things properly made in these days. What sort of a voice is it to have?”

===Chinese===
“那可得花一大笔，”巫师若有所思地说，“如今这年月，要把这种东西做得像样，实在不容易。她要一副什么样的嗓子？”

===Original===
“It need not be at all talkative,” said Taboret, “so that won’t add much to the price. It need only say, ‘If you please,’ ‘No, thank you,’ ‘Certainly,’ and ‘Just so.’ ”

===Chinese===
“用不着多话，”塔博蕾说，“这倒加不了几个钱。她只要会说‘劳驾’‘不，谢谢’‘当然’和‘正是如此’就够了。”

===Original===
“Well, under those circumstances,” said the wizard, “I will do it for four cat’s footfalls, two fish’s screams, and two swan’s songs.”

===Chinese===
“好吧，既然这样，”巫师说，“四声猫的脚步、两声鱼的尖叫，再加两支天鹅的歌，我就给您做。”

===Original===
“It is too much,” cried Taboret. “I’ll give you the footfalls and the screams, but to ask for swans’ songs!”

===Chinese===
“太贵了！”塔博蕾叫道，“脚步和尖叫我都依你，居然还要天鹅的歌！”

===Original===
She did not really think it dear, but she always made a point of trying to beat tradesmen down.

===Chinese===
她其实并不觉得贵，只是她向来有个规矩，总要跟做买卖的讲讲价。

===Original===
“I can’t do it for less,” said the wizard, “and if you think it too much, you’d better try another shop.”

===Chinese===
“少一个子儿也不行，”巫师说，“您嫌贵，就请上别家铺子问去。”

===Original===
“As I am really in a hurry for it, and cannot spend time in searching about, I suppose I must have it,” said Taboret; “but I consider the price very high. When will it be ready?”

===Chinese===
“我实在是急等着用，没工夫挨家去寻，”塔博蕾说，“看来只好依你了；可我认为这价钱实在太高。几时能做好？”

===Original===
“By tomorrow evening.”

===Chinese===
“明晚。”

===Original===
“Very well, then, be sure it is ready for me by the time I call for it, and whatever you do, don’t make it at all noisy or rough in its ways;” and Taboret swept out of the shop and returned to her home.

===Chinese===
“那么好，我来取的时候，千万要备妥了。还有，无论如何，别把她做得爱吵闹、毛手毛脚。”说罢，塔博蕾一甩身子出了铺子，回家去了。

===Original===
Next evening she returned and asked if her job was done.

===Chinese===
第二天晚上她又来了，问她的活计做好了没有。

===Original===
“I will fetch it, and I am sure you will like it,” said the wizard, leaving the shop as he spoke. Presently he came back, leading by the hand a pretty little girl of about six years old﻿—a little girl so like the Princess Ursula that no one could have told them apart.

===Chinese===
“我去把她领来，保管您中意。”巫师说着便出了铺子。不多时他回来了，手里牵着一个六岁模样的漂亮小姑娘——那小姑娘同厄休拉公主像得叫人分辨不出彼此。

===Original===
“Well,” said Taboret, “it looks well enough. But are you sure that it’s a good piece of workmanship, and won’t give way anywhere?”

===Chinese===
“嗯，”塔博蕾说，“模样倒还周正。可你保准这活计做得结实，哪儿都不会散架吗？”

===Original===
“It’s as good a piece of work as ever was done,” said the wizard, proudly, striking the child on the back as he spoke, “Look at it! Examine it all over, and see if you find a flaw anywhere. There’s not one fairy in twenty who could tell it from the real thing, and no mortal could.”

===Chinese===
“这活计做得天下少有，”巫师骄傲地说，一边在那孩子背上拍了一下，“您瞧瞧！上上下下仔细验一验，看能不能挑出一处毛病。二十个仙女里也挑不出一个能认出她是假的，凡人就更是想都别想了。”

===Original===
“It seems to be fairly made,” said Taboret, approvingly, as she turned the little girl round. “Now I’ll pay you, and then will be off;” with which she raised her wand in the air and waved it three times, and there arose a series of strange sounds.

===Chinese===
“做工还算过得去。”塔博蕾说着，赞许地把小姑娘转过身来看了看。“现在付钱，付完就走。”说着她把魔杖向空中一举，挥了三下，顿时响起一串奇怪的声音。

===Original===
The first was a low tramping, the second shrill and piercing screams, the third voices of wonderful beauty, singing a very sorrowful song.

===Chinese===
头一声是低低的踏步声，第二声是尖利刺耳的尖叫，第三声是美妙无比的歌喉，唱着一支十分哀伤的歌。

===Original===
The wizard caught all the sounds and pocketed them at once, and Taboret, without ceremony, picked up the child, took her head downwards under her arm, and flew away.

===Chinese===
巫师把那些声音一一接住，立刻装进了口袋。塔博蕾也不客气，拎起那孩子，头朝下往腋下一夹，飞走了。

===Original===
At court that night the little Princess had been naughty, and had refused to go to bed. It was a long time before her ladies could get her into her crib, and when she was there, she did not really go to sleep, only lay still and pretended, till everyone went away; then she got up and stole noiselessly to the window, and sat down on the window-seat all curled up in a little bunch, while she looked out wistfully at the moon. She was such a pretty soft little thing, with all her warm bright hair falling over her shoulders, that it would have been hard for most people to be angry with her. She leaned her chin on her tiny white hands, and as she gazed out, the tears rose to her great blue eyes; but remembering that her ladies would call this naughty, she wiped them hastily away with her nightgown sleeve.

===Chinese===
这天夜里，宫里的小公主不听话，闹着不肯睡觉。女官们费了好大的劲才把她哄进小床，可她上了床也没有真睡，只静静躺着装睡，等人都走了，她就爬起来，蹑手蹑脚地溜到窗前，蜷成小小一团坐在窗座上，怅然望着月亮。她是那样一个娇软可爱的小东西，一头暖色明亮的头发披散在肩上，多数人见了都很难对她生气。她把下巴搁在白白的小手上，望着望着，泪珠涌上了蓝色的大眼睛；可她想起女官们会说这是淘气，便赶紧用睡衣的袖子把泪擦掉了。

===Original===
“Ah moon, pretty bright moon!” she said to herself, “I wonder if they let you cry when you want to. I think I’d like to go up there and live with you; I’m sure it would be nicer than being here.”

===Chinese===
“啊，月亮，又亮又美的月亮！”她自言自语道，“不知道你要哭的时候，他们让不让你哭。我想，我情愿上你那儿去，同你一起住；那一定比待在这儿好。”

===Original===
“Would you like to go away with me?” said a voice close beside her; and looking up she saw a funny old woman in a red cloak, standing near to her. She was not frightened, for the old woman had a kind smile and bright black eyes, though her nose was hooked and her chin long.

===Chinese===
“愿意跟我走吗？”她身旁一个声音说道。她抬起头，看见一个滑稽的老婆婆站在近旁，披着红斗篷。她并不害怕，因为老婆婆虽然长着鹰钩鼻子、长下巴，笑容却很慈祥，一双眼又黑又亮。

===Original===
“Where would you take me?” said the little Princess, sucking her thumb, and staring with all her might.

===Chinese===
“你要带我去哪儿？”小公主嘬着大拇指，使足了劲盯着她问。

===Original===
“I’d take you to the seashore, where you’d be able to play about on the sands, and where you’d have-some little boys and girls to play with, and no one to tell you not to make a noise.”

===Chinese===
“我带你到海边去，你可以在沙滩上跑来跑去地玩儿，还有几个小男孩小女孩陪你玩儿，谁也不会叫你别吵闹。”

===Original===
“I’ll go,” cried Ursula, springing up at once.

===Chinese===
“我愿意去！”厄休拉喊道，一下子跳了起来。

===Original===
“Come along,” said the old woman, taking her tenderly in her arms and folding her in her warm red cloak. Then they rose up in the air, and flew out of the window, right away over the tops of the houses.

===Chinese===
“那就走吧。”老婆婆说着，温柔地把她抱进怀里，用暖和的红斗篷把她裹住。随后两人腾空而起，飞出窗外，越过一重重屋顶，一直飞远了。

===Original===
The night air was sharp, and Ursula soon fell asleep; but still they kept flying on, on, over hill and dale, for miles and miles, away from the palace, towards the sea.

===Chinese===
夜里的空气冷冽，厄休拉很快就睡着了；可她们还在不停地飞呀飞，翻山越谷，飞了一程又一程，离宫殿越来越远，向着大海飞去。

===Original===
Far away from the court and the palace, in a tiny fishing village, on the sea, was a little hut where a fisherman named Mark lived with his wife and three children. He was a poor man, and lived on the fish he caught in his little boat. The children, Oliver, Philip, and little Bell, were rosy-cheeked and bright-eyed. They played all day long on the shore, and shouted till they were hoarse. To this village the fairy bore the still sleeping Ursula, and gently placed her on the doorstep of Mark’s cottage; then she kissed her cheeks, and with one gust blew the door open, and disappeared before anyone could come to see who it was.

===Chinese===
远离宫廷的地方，海边有一座小小的渔村，村里一间小屋里住着渔夫马克（Mark）和他的妻子、三个孩子。他家境贫寒，全靠小船捕来的鱼过活。三个孩子奥利弗（Oliver）、菲利普（Philip）和小贝尔（Bell），个个脸颊红润、眼睛明亮，整天在海滩上玩耍，喊叫得嗓子都哑了。仙女抱着熟睡未醒的厄休拉来到这村子，轻轻把她放在马克家的门槛上，亲了亲她的脸颊，又一口气把门吹开，趁人还没出来看清是谁，就隐去了身影。

===Original===
The fisherman and his wife were sitting quietly within. She was making the children clothes, and he was mending his net, when without any noise the door opened and the cold night air blew in.

===Chinese===
渔夫和妻子正安安静静坐在屋里。她在给孩子们缝衣裳，他在补鱼网，忽听得门悄没声儿地开了，夜里的寒气灌了进来。

===Original===
“Wife,” said the fisherman, “just see who’s at the door.”

===Chinese===
“孩子他妈，”渔夫说，“去看看门口是谁。”

===Original===
The wife got up and went to the door, and there lay Ursula, still sleeping soundly, in her little white nightdress.

===Chinese===
妻子起身走到门口，只见厄休拉躺在那儿，穿着小小的白睡裙，还睡得正香。

===Original===
The woman gave a little scream at sight of the child, and called to her husband.

===Chinese===
