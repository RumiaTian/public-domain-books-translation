## The Hair Tree / 发之树

===Original===
Many years ago there lived a young Queen who was said to be the most beautiful woman in the world. Her skin was white and smooth as ivory, and her eyes bright as stars. But her greatest beauty was her hair. It was neither black nor gold, but exactly halfway between, just the colour of a dead beech-leaf It was so long that it trailed behind her on the ground, and so thick that it took three maids to plait and arrange it every morning. Every day it was scented and washed by the court hairdresser, who examined it carefully to see that it was not growing thinner or falling off. The Queen’s husband, the King, was as proud of it as his wife, and gave her all sorts of lovely jewellery with which to dress it—diamond pins and golden combs—and by his special command the court gardener always kept the best flowers for the Queen to place in her hair.

===Chinese===
许多年以前，有一位年轻王后，据说她是天下最美的女子。她的肌肤白皙光滑如象牙，眼睛明亮如星辰。然而她最美的，是那一头头发。那头发既不乌黑，也不金黄，颜色恰在两者之间，正像一片枯萎的山毛榉叶。它长得拖曳到身后地上，浓密得每天早晨要三个侍女才编得过来、理得过来。宫廷理发师天天给它熏香、洗濯，还细细查验，唯恐它日渐稀疏或脱落。王后的丈夫国王同她一样为这头发自豪，赐给她各式各样美丽的首饰来装扮它——钻石发针、金梳——还特谕宫廷花匠，总是把最好的花留给王后簪发。

===Original===
And not only the King and Queen, but all the courtiers and court ladies—indeed, everyone in the country—praised and admired this beautiful hair; and although some of the court ladies were rather jealous of it, yet all agreed that it would be a real national misfortune if any harm came to it.

===Chinese===
不但国王和王后，满朝的廷臣命妇——实在说，全国上下——都称赞、艳羡这头美发；虽有几位宫廷命妇对它颇为嫉妒，可众口一词：这头发若有闪失，实在是举国的大不幸。

===Original===
One morning the Queen was sitting at her window at work, when a big bird flew past. It was much like an eagle, with a hooked beak and ugly fierce eyes. It hovered around the window for some time, and at last settled on a tree outside and watched the Queen, who did not look up till she was surprised by hearing the bird say in a croaking voice—

===Chinese===
一天早晨，王后正坐在窗前做活计，一只大鸟飞掠而过。那鸟样子很有些像鹰，弯弯的喙，一双凶恶的丑眼睛。它在窗边盘桓了好一阵，最后落在外头一棵树上，盯着王后瞧。王后起初没有抬头，直到那鸟沙哑着嗓子开了口，才把她吓了一跳——

===Original===
“Good day, Queen; you’ve plenty of hair.”

===Chinese===
“日安，王后；您的头发真多啊。”

===Original===
The Queen laughed, well pleased that even a common eagle should notice her beautiful tresses.

===Chinese===
王后笑了，心想连一只寻常的鹰也留意到她的美发，好不得意。

===Original===
“Yes,” she said, shaking it around her. “I’ve more than any other lady in the land.”

===Chinese===
“不错，”她说着，把头发往四下一抖。“全国没有哪位夫人比我的多。”

===Original===
“Then you’ve enough to give me a little,” said the bird. “I can’t find anything soft to line my nest with, and some of your hair would do nicely.”

===Chinese===
“那您尽可以分我一点儿，”鸟说，“我找不到柔软的东西垫窝，您的头发正合适。”

===Original===
“My hair!” cried the Queen, staring at the bird in astonishment; “my beautiful hair to line your common nest with! You must be mad. Do you know that I’m the Queen, and that I value my hair more than anything on earth?”

===Chinese===
“我的头发！”王后喊起来，惊愕地瞪着那鸟；“拿我美丽的头发去垫你那寒酸的窝！你准是疯了。你可知道我是王后，我的头发比世上什么都金贵？”

===Original===
“Nevertheless, it would do very nicely for my nest, and I advise you to give me some of it,” croaked the eagle.

===Chinese===
“话虽如此，它垫我的窝实在再好不过，我劝你还是给我一些罢。”鹰哑着嗓子说。

===Original===
“Indeed, I shall do nothing of the kind,” said the Queen, eagerly. “I never heard such impertinence in my life. Fly away immediately, or I will send out some of the soldiers to shoot you.”

===Chinese===
“哼，我偏不。”王后急赤白脸地说。“我平生没听见过这样无礼的话。快飞走，不然我就叫士兵出来开枪打你。”

===Original===
“They couldn’t do it,” said the bird, with a low laugh; “and if you tell them to try, you will be sorry for it afterwards. Now, I shall only give you one more chance. Queen, will you give me some of your hair for my nest?”

===Chinese===
“他们打不着，”鸟低声一笑，“你若叫他们来试，日后有得后悔。如今我只再给你一次机会。王后，你肯不肯给我一些头发垫窝？”

===Original===
“No, I shall not,” answered the Queen, half crying with anger; “it is very presumptuous of you to ask for such a thing.”

===Chinese===
“不肯，就是不肯。”王后答道，气得半带哭腔。“你讨这样的东西，好大的胆子。”

===Original===
The bird said no more, but rising into the air began to fly slowly round and round the tree on which he had been perched, and as he flew he sang these words in a low voice—

===Chinese===
鸟不再言语，腾身空中，绕着方才栖过的那棵树缓缓盘旋，一面飞，一面低声唱道——

===Original===
> “As the wind blows this tree’s twigs bare,
 

 So shall the proud Queen lose her hair;
 

 The leaves shall come back with the first spring rain,
 

 But when shall the Queen find her hair again?”

===Chinese===
> “风吹得这树的细枝精光，
 

 高傲的王后头发也要落光；
 

 初春一降雨，叶子还会回来，
 

 王后的头发，何时才能回来？”

===Original===
When he had done, the eagle gave a shrill cry and disappeared, leaving the Queen in astonishment at his conduct.

===Chinese===
唱罢，鹰长唳一声，霎时不见了，撇下王后，为它这番行径惊愕不已。

===Original===
It was autumn, and she heard the wind whistling in the branches, and presently a number of leaves dropped from the cherry-tree, and fluttered to the ground, and, at the same moment, a handful of her curls fell out into her lap.

===Chinese===
时值秋天，她听得风在枝头呼啸。不多时，樱桃树上飘下片片叶子，打着旋儿落了地；就在同一刻，她鬓边一把卷发也脱落下来，散在她的裙兜里。

===Original===
The Queen started up in alarm, and ran with tears in her eyes to the King to tell him what had happened. The King laughed at her fears, assuring her the bird could do her no harm, and that it was only by chance that her hair fell out just then.

===Chinese===
王后惊慌地跳起来，含着眼泪跑去把经过告诉国王。国王笑她多虑，叫她放心，说那鸟害不了她，头发恰好那时脱落，不过碰巧罢了。

===Original===
Yet the Queen could not feel comfortable, and that night, when her ladies were brushing her hair, a quantity came out on the brushes and two or three locks fell to the ground. Next morning when she awoke she found some long soft brown tresses lying loose on her pillow, and when she got out of bed, a whole shower fell from her head to the floor.

===Chinese===
可是王后心里总不踏实。当夜侍女们替她梳头，梳子上挂下来好些头发，还有两三绺落在地上。第二天早晨醒来，她见枕上散着几绺又长又软的褐色头发；待她下得床来，满头头发簌簌而落，像下了一阵雨。

===Original===
The Queen wrung her hands in despair, and at once sent for the court hairdresser, who came, bringing with him a number of bottles, all containing lotions to make the hair grow; but all the lotions in the world were of no use. There was no denying the fact that the Queen’s hair was falling out in a most alarming manner. There was scarcely enough for her ladies to plait, and a bald spot was beginning to show on the top of her head. She dared not look at the cherry-tree to see if its branches were beginning to look bare also, but at every gust she heard the leaves falling, and trembled as she thought how soon they would all be gone. At last one morning, after the wind had been very fierce and strong, she ran to the window, and, looking out, saw that the cherry-tree’s branches were quite bare—not a leaf to be seen anywhere. Then she turned to her looking-glass, and, dreadful to behold, she was quite bald also. Her last lock of hair had fallen off and left her head as smooth and white as an egg. At this fearful sight the Queen screamed and fainted, and when her ladies came to help her they were all so shocked that they could scarcely speak. As for the King, his grief was so great that he sobbed aloud.

===Chinese===
王后绝望地绞着双手，立刻宣宫廷理发师进见。理发师来了，带来一大堆瓶子，瓶瓶都装着生发药水；可天下的药水全不管用。无可讳言：王后的头发正以骇人的速度脱落。剩下的几乎不够侍女们编辫子了，头顶上也渐渐显出一块秃斑。她不敢去看那棵樱桃树，不敢看它的枝条是否也开始光秃；可每一阵风起，她都听得见叶子簌簌落下，一想它们转眼就要落尽，便浑身发抖。终于有一天早晨，狂风大作过后，她奔到窗前朝外一望——樱桃树的枝条已经精光，一片叶子也寻不见了。她转过身来照镜，天哪，可怕的是：她自己也全秃了。最后一绺头发已经脱落，她的头光光白白，滑得像一枚鸡蛋。见这可怕的景象，王后尖叫一声，晕了过去；侍女们赶来救她，个个惊得说不出话来。至于国王，他悲痛得放声哭了。

===Original===
The Queen was laid on her bed, and the court physicians and hairdressers held a great consultation as to what was to be done to make her hair grow, and it was agreed that her head should go through a regular course of treatment. In the meantime she said she would keep her room, and it was given out that she was very ill, in order that the common people might not know what a dreadful thing had happened. But all the doctors and hairdressers could do nothing. Not a single hair reappeared on the Queen’s bare white head, and it was impossible to conceal the fact any longer. There was great sorrow all over the country when it became known, and a general mourning was proclaimed till the Queen’s hair should have grown again. In the meantime she wore a little lace cap trimmed with jewels. It was a very pretty little cap, and very becoming—so, at least, all the courtiers said. But it was not so beautiful as her own hair, and the Queen felt this so much that the first time she appeared in public in her cap she could scarcely help crying.

===Chinese===
王后被安放到床上，御医和理发师们会诊良久，商议如何叫头发重新长出，议定要给她的头按部就班地调治一番。这期间她说要闭门静养，宫里便放出话来，只说王后病得厉害，免得百姓知道出了这等可怕的事。可是医生理发师们百般施为，全然无用。王后那光白的头上，连一根头发也没再冒出来，事实再也瞒不住了。消息传开，举国哀伤，全国挂孝志哀，直到王后的头发重新长出为止。这期间她戴一顶镶宝石的花边小帽。那是顶挺好看的小帽，戴着也颇相宜——至少廷臣们个个这么说。可它终究不及她自己的头发美，王后深以为苦，头一回戴着小帽露面时，几乎忍不住要哭出来。

===Original===
So things went on for a little time, and the doctors and hairdressers were all racking their brains to find something to make hair grow. But the Queen fretted so much that she lost her appetite and really fell ill, and was obliged to keep to her bed. The first night after her illness she dreamt a dream, which she thought about all day long. She dreamt that she was sitting in the palace garden, when there came up a little man, who was the strangest object she had ever seen. He was no bigger than a big spider, and all dressed in green. When he saw her he began to dance and sing this rhyme:—

===Chinese===
这样过了些时日，医生理发师们个个绞尽脑汁，要寻出生发的东西来。可是王后忧思过甚，茶饭无心，竟真的病倒了，只得卧床。病后头一夜，她做了一个梦，白天整整想了一天。她梦见自己坐在御花园里，走来一个小人儿，那是她平生所见最古怪的东西。他不过一只大蜘蛛那么大，周身穿着绿衣裳。他一见她，便跳起舞来，唱起这支歌谣：——

===Original===
> “When the grass is thin, you must mow it, mow it, mow it,
 

 But when the ground is bare, you must sow it, sow it, sow it.”

===Chinese===
> “草儿稀了，就得割它、割它、割它，
 

 地皮光了呢，就得种它、种它、种它。”

===Original===
And as he sang these words he took out a handful of tiny seeds from his pocket, and began to scatter them about. Directly there sprang up a little plant, which grew and grew and grew until it became a big tree, and then it put forth buds, and the buds burst, and there came out not leaves, but tiny locks of hair, which grew and grew until they touched the ground, and covered the tree all over.

===Chinese===
他一面唱，一面从口袋里掏出一把细小的种子，四下撒开。登时冒出一株小苗，长啊，长啊，长啊，长成了一棵大树；接着树上生出蓓蕾，蓓蕾绽开，出来的不是叶子，而是一绺绺细小的头发，又长啊长啊，一直垂到地上，把整棵树罩得严严实实。

===Original===
When the Queen awoke she could think of nothing but the strange Hair Tree; and that night when she went to sleep she dreamt exactly the same thing. On the third night she had the same dream again, and when she awoke she called to her husband, and told him of what she had dreamt three nights running.

===Chinese===
王后一觉醒来，满脑子只有那棵奇异的发之树（Hair Tree）；当夜睡去，梦见的分毫不差又是它。第三夜还是同一个梦。她醒来便唤丈夫，把接连三夜梦见的情景讲给他听。

===Original===
“And now, my dear husband,” she continued, “I am sure that the only thing which can ever make my hair grow is some seeds from the Hair Tree. I entreat you to spare no effort to discover it, and to offer a reward to anyone who will bring you news of it. For I feel”—here the Queen’s voice grew very husky—“that if I do not find it I shall never recover my hair, and if not, I shall die, for I cannot live without it.” So saying she fell back on the pillow and closed her eyes.

===Chinese===
“如今，亲爱的夫君，”她接着说，“我确信，唯一能使我头发重新长出的，就是发之树上的种子。我求你竭尽全力去寻它，悬赏购求它的消息，凡带来音讯的人都给重赏。因为我觉着——”说到这里，王后的声音哽住了——“若寻不着它，我的头发就永远好不了；头发好不了，我也活不成了，没有它我万难苟活。”说罢，她跌回枕上，闭上了眼睛。

===Original===
The King at once called a great meeting of his lords together, and told them the Queen’s dream, and a herald was sent round to proclaim that one hundred thousand pounds would be given to anyone who could bring some seeds from the Hair Tree, or even tell where it was to be found. And great placards were pasted up all over the kingdom, offering the reward. So everyone began to talk of this strange Hair Tree, which no one had ever heard of before, and to wonder where it could be—for all would have liked to have earned the reward—but all agreed that the Hair Tree was nowhere in that country.

===Chinese===
国王立刻召集众勋贵大开朝议，把王后的梦讲了一遍，又派出传令官四处宣谕：凡能取来发之树种子、哪怕只是说出它下落的人，赏十万英镑。王国各处张贴了悬赏的大幅告示。于是人人都在谈论这棵奇异的发之树——先前谁也不曾听说过——都在猜想它究竟在哪里，因为谁都盼着赚那笔赏金；只是众口一词：发之树断不会在本国之内。

===Original===
Now it chanced that a poor sailor named Rupert saw one of the placards, and stopped to read it. He was a strong young man, but he had neither father nor mother, nor sisters nor brothers, and he felt so lonely that he did not care what became of him.

===Chinese===
且说有个穷水手，名叫鲁珀特（Rupert），碰巧看见一张告示，便站住读了起来。他是个结实的小伙子，却无父无母，无姐无妹，孤零零一个人，觉着自己落到什么地步都无所谓。

===Original===
So when he saw the placard and the large reward that was offered to anyone who could bring news of the Hair Tree, he began to wonder where it was, and if he could not find it.

===Chinese===
他见了告示，见到那笔给寻得发之树音讯之人的巨额赏金，便琢磨起它到底在哪里，自己能不能把它找到。

===Original===
“One hundred thousand pounds is a lot of money,” he said to himself. “There is no reason why I should not find it as well as another man. I have a good mind to go and look for it, though it seems rather a wild-goose chase.”

===Chinese===
“十万英镑可不是小数目，”他自言自语，“旁人找得，凭什么我找不得？我倒真想动身去找找，虽说这事瞧着多半像大海捞针。”

===Original===
So he packed up his things, and took a little boat, and sailed away towards the north; for it was there, he knew, was a strange country where animals speak like men and women, and plants have hands and eyes; and he thought that there he might find the wonderful Hair Tree.

===Chinese===
于是他收拾好行李，驾了一条小船，朝北边驶去；因为他知道，北方有一片奇异的国度，那里的走兽像男女一样开口说话，草木生着手和眼睛；他想，兴许在那儿能寻见那奇妙的发之树。

===Original===
In the meantime half the young men in the kingdom had started off to search, hoping to win the reward. Some went east, some went west, some north, some south; and they sought in every imaginable place and country. Some gave it up very soon, and returned home, saying that there was no such tree. Others went on and on, asking everyone they met if they had heard of or seen it, and finding no trace of it, yet, being determined not to give it up and return home empty-handed, went still farther and farther. And so months passed away, and the Queen’s head still remained bald.

===Chinese===
这时候，王国里半数年轻人已经动身寻树去了，都巴望赢得那笔赏金。有的往东，有的往西，有的往北，有的往南；凡是想得到的地方和国家，他们统统寻了个遍。有的不久便灰了心，回家说根本没这么棵树。有的则一程又一程地走，逢人便打听可曾听说过、可曾见过它，虽不见半点踪迹，却又打定主意不肯罢休、不肯空手回家，于是越走越远。就这样几个月过去，王后的头依旧光着。

===Original===
In order that her Majesty might not be annoyed by the sight of other women’s hair when her own was gone, the King ordered that they should all wear little caps which quite covered the head and came down on the forehead; and because no one liked these caps, and all thought them very unbecoming, the court ladies were almost as anxious as the Queen that the tree should be found, and her hair grow again.

===Chinese===
为了不让王后陛下瞧见别人的头发烦心，国王下旨：全国妇女一律戴上小帽，把头包严，直压到额前。这些帽子谁也不喜欢，人人都说不像样子，于是宫廷命妇们几乎同王后一样急切，只盼那棵树快快寻到，王后的头发快快长回。

===Original===
Rupert sailed and sailed to the north till he began to think he must be coming to the magic country of which he had heard. For a long time he went on without seeing land anywhere, but at last he came in sight of a little island standing quite alone in the middle of the water. On it there grew neither grass nor flowers of any sort, but three solitary trees. One was much like a common nut-tree, only all the nuts were of a bright red colour; the boughs of the second were laden with precious stones of all kinds, diamonds and rubies, emeralds and pearls; but the third tree was the strangest of all, for its boughs were quite bare, and looked as if they were made of polished brass, and on the centre at the top grew an enormous pod, which pointed straight at the sky. It looked like a great brass drum.

===Chinese===
鲁珀特朝北驶啊驶啊，驶到后来，他想自己准是快到传闻中的那个魔法国度了。好长时间里他四望不见陆地，末了终于望见汪洋正中孤零零一座小岛。岛上寸草不生，一朵花也没有，只立着三棵孤零零的树。一棵很有些像寻常的坚果树，只是满树的坚果都是鲜红色；第二棵的枝头缀满各色宝石——钻石、红宝石、祖母绿、珍珠；第三棵却顶顶古怪：枝条精光，看去像擦得锃亮的黄铜，树顶正中结着一个巨大的豆荚，直指青天，模样像一面大黄铜鼓。

===Original===
Rupert pulled up his boat, and, springing on to the island, filled his pockets full of precious stones and common nuts, and then stood still looking at the enormous pod and wondering what it could contain. While he watched it there was a noise like a clap of thunder, and the pod burst asunder, and twelve round shining golden nuts fell to the ground. At the same time the tree withered as if it had been struck by lightning, and the brass branches all fell away.

===Chinese===
鲁珀特把船拢上岸，跳上小岛，往口袋里塞满了宝石和红坚果，然后站定下来，望着那只巨大的豆荚出神，猜它里头究竟装着什么。正看着，忽听一声巨响如雷，豆荚轰然裂开，十二颗圆滚滚、亮闪闪的金坚果落了一地。与此同时，那树立刻枯萎，像遭了雷殛，铜枝纷纷坠落。

===Original===
Rupert was so frightened by the noise the pod made in bursting that he had buried his face in the ground, but when he found all was quiet again, he slowly raised himself, and picked up one of the gold nuts to examine it. As they were pretty and bright, he thought he would take them all for curiosities, and he placed them on the seat of his little boat. But as he rowed away from the island he began to feel sure he was coming to the enchanted land.
