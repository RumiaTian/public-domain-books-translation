## XV / 十五、斯塔恩夜谈

===Original===
At Starn he was refreshed again with contacts from outside. There was an unusual number of tourists, two and two on the hillpaths, swarming the square. They did him so much good that he crossed to the station to meet a down-train that held more. Not many get out at evening, where there was nothing to do but stare. All he noticed in particular was an old gentleman.

===Chinese===
在斯塔恩（Starn），他又从与外界的接触里恢复了精神。游客多得反常，一对一对走在山径上，把广场挤得满满的。这些人让他受用极了，他索性踱到车站去，迎一列载着更多游客的下行车。傍晚下车的人不多——在这地方，除了干瞪眼无事可做。他特别留意的，只有一位老先生。

===Original===
He was beginning to enjoy the country. Enjoy Starn. Would have liked to know more, its history and contemporary life. He thought the old man with a red face, in grey flannels, a local landowner; thought him back to some dignified house, and was surprised later to see him dining at the inn.

===Chinese===
他开始喜欢上这片乡野了。喜欢上斯塔恩。本想多了解一些——它的历史，还有眼下的生活。他以为这位红脸膛、穿灰法兰绒的老者是位本地乡绅；以为他回某座体面宅邸去了，后来却吃了一惊——竟看见他在客栈里用晚餐。

===Original===
He was not known there, it seemed. There was a difficulty about a room for him. Carston felt that he should leave graciously, suggested that the old man should have his room, and he a bed anywhere. There was consultation, hesitation, acceptance, thanks. After an interval, they had settled down to coffee together.

===Chinese===
看样子，那儿没人认识他。给他腾房间出了点难处。卡斯顿（Carston）觉得此刻应当体面地告退，便提议老者住他这间房，他自己随便哪儿有张床就行。一番商量，一番迟疑，一番接受，一番道谢。过了一阵，两人已安顿下来，对坐共饮咖啡。

===Original===
It was easy to be charming to him. An obvious number of right things to say. In a flash that, too, had passed.

===Chinese===
讨他欢心很容易。现成的得体话有一大堆。一眨眼，这一套也过完了。

===Original===
“So you’ve been staying over at Gault House with the Taverners. Did you meet my son there﻿—Picus, I think they call him? My name’s Tracy.”

===Chinese===
“这么说，你一直住在高尔特宅（Gault House），和塔弗纳家（Taverners）的人做伴。你在那边见过我儿子吗——我记得他们管他叫皮库斯（Picus）？我姓特雷西（Tracy）。”

===Original===
Carston thought: Be cautious, be very cautious indeed. Don’t tell lies. The old man will be going there. His eyes are the colour of his flannels. What we call stone, and never is. Stone takes light.

===Chinese===
卡斯顿心想：要谨慎，千万要谨慎。别撒谎。老头儿是要往那边去的。他的眼睛，正是他那身法兰绒的颜色。我们管那叫石头色——其实从来不是。石头是会收光的。

===Original===
“Yes,” he said, “he is staying there with his friends.”

===Chinese===
“是的，”他说，“他正和朋友们一起住在那儿。”

===Original===
It became suddenly necessary to observe with every faculty he had. He had no idea why; not for distraction, not with reference to himself. The impression was that he was opposite someone very old﻿—not particularly in years, but in something built by centuries of experience, and now no longer in flower. The same could be said of his son and his son’s friends, only that they were in flower, and might not cease flowering once their bodies’ bloom was done. Centuries had gone to his construction. Carston was surprised at his attention to this, until he noticed that their setting outside the inn was the setting of a play.

===Chinese===
他忽然必须调动全部心力去观察。他说不清为什么；不为解闷，也不关他自己。得到的印象是：对面坐着的是一个极其苍老的人——苍老倒不在年岁上，而在某种由几个世纪的经验筑成的东西上，而那东西如今花期已过。他儿子和儿子的朋友们也可以这么说，只不过他们正在花期，而且等身体的盛放一过，也未必就停止开花。好几个世纪都花在了他的构造上。卡斯顿自己对这份专注也觉诧异，直到他看出：他们在客栈外的这片景象，正是一出戏的布景。

===Original===
Before he had wished for drama, and had not found his role. Now he was too much of a man to take himself off.

===Chinese===
从前他巴望过戏剧性，却没有找到自己的角色。如今他已是条十足的汉子，不至于就此抽身退场。

===Original===
The stage surpassed all romantic expectations, a town with towers, in hills high enough and low enough to set and display it equally. A fleece of stars over it, thick as the flocks on the downsides, turning, turning with the earth.

===Chinese===
这座舞台胜过一切浪漫的期待：一座有塔楼的小镇，四围的山不高不低，恰好把它安放得妥帖，又陈列得分明。镇子上空覆着一层繁星，厚得像山坡草场上的羊群，随大地转动，转动。

===Original===
“Do you like this country?” the old man said.

===Chinese===
“你喜欢这片乡野吗？”老者说。

===Original===
“I like it immensely. I am sorry to leave. I only came down for the weekend.”

===Chinese===
“喜欢极了。要走了，真遗憾。我本只下来度个周末。”

===Original===
“Still, you prolonged it. This is Friday.”

===Chinese===
“可你把假期延长了。今天是星期五。”

===Original===
Carston thought: A nice slip to start off with. I’d better be a bit more frank right away. “To tell you the truth, I’m a stranger, and I found things a bit difficult over there.”

===Chinese===
卡斯顿心想：一开场就露了个漂亮的破绽。我还是干脆多坦白些的好。“不瞒您说，我是外乡人，在那边觉得有些难处。”

===Original===
“Would it surprise you to hear that I’ve heard that said before?”

===Chinese===
“这话我从前也听人说过——你听了会奇怪吗？”

===Original===
“Not at all.”

===Chinese===
“一点也不。”

===Original===
They laughed together. Carston began to think backwards. The eighteenth century had produced this type, had set him in culture and conviction that Nature had appointed certain old men to approve and modestly direct her arrangements of air and fire. The Renaissance had kicked off that ball, now frozen into the marble and stucco that he was sure adorned his park somewhere. Behind that there was the matrix; the Middle Ages, feudalism, Christendom. Faith in a childishly planned universe as one thing. *The earth one great city of gods and men.* His history lessons were taking life at last.

===Chinese===
两人一同笑了。卡斯顿开始朝回想。是十八世纪造出了这一型人，把他安置进一种文化和信念里：相信造化委派了某些老人，去认可并谦逊地督导她对气与火的种种安排。那颗球是文艺复兴一脚开出的，如今冻进了大理石和灰泥——他敢肯定，那一类东西正陈设在庄园里的哪个角落。再往后是母体：中世纪、封建制、基督教世界。对一座按孩童心性规划出来的宇宙所抱的信仰，也算其中一项。*大地，神与人同栖的一座伟大城邦。*他的历史课总算活过来了。

===Original===
“I detest impertinence,” the old man said﻿—“I don’t think it will be necessary to go into that; but other people have found my son difficult. Was it anything to do with him?”

===Chinese===
“我最恨无礼放肆，”老者说，“我想这一层不必细究；不过别人也觉得我儿子难处。是为了他吗？”

===Original===
Carston thought: Picus again. In for some more. No good pretending. He said:

===Chinese===
卡斯顿心想：又是皮库斯。躲不过了。装是装不成啦。他说：

===Original===
“I reckon. Perhaps I didn’t catch on﻿—that he was fooling me.”

===Chinese===
“我想是的。也许我没回过味来——没看出他在耍我。”

===Original===
Like talking to an old stone idol; live stone idol; stone idol that walks: after something. I ate their bread, and I was rude to them. Be careful, be very careful, indeed.﻿ ﻿…

===Chinese===
像在对一尊古老的石偶像说话；活的石偶像；会走路的石偶像：在追逐着什么。我吃了他们的面包，却对他们出言无礼。当心，千万当心，真得当心。……

===Original===
“People, I hear, have left the house before, after what I could only wish was my son’s sense of humour had come into play.”

===Chinese===
“我听说，从前也有人离开过那座宅子——在我只肯认作我儿子的幽默感发作之后。”

===Original===
Carston saw that it was a question which was going to lure the other on. And he longed to tell someone something.

===Chinese===
卡斯顿看出，这问题是个饵，要钓他接着往下说。而他也正渴望向什么人讲点什么。

===Original===
“Who are down there now?”

===Chinese===
“眼下都有谁住在那边？”

===Original===
He gave the names. No harm in that.

===Chinese===
他报了名字。这没什么坏处。

===Original===
The old man meditated: “Ah! the heart of the band?”

===Chinese===
老者沉吟：“啊！那一伙的核心人物？”

===Original===
Why a band? More news.

===Chinese===
为什么说“一伙”？又多一条新闻。

===Original===
“My son has a bad habit. He is fond of other people’s property which may never be his. In this instance I am speaking for myself. He has a book of mine that I want. Also, it interests me to know why he should want it. And I may say that your quarrel interests me, whatever it was.”

===Chinese===
“我儿子有个坏习惯。他贪恋别人的财物——那些也许永远到不了他手里的东西。这一回，我是替自己说话。他拿了我一本书，我想要回来。再说，他为什么想要它，这层也让我感兴趣。还可以说一句：你们那场口角，不管因何而起，也让我感兴趣。”

===Original===
“Don’t call it a quarrel. I just didn’t like his way of going about things.”

===Chinese===
“别叫它口角。我只是不喜欢他行事的路数。”

===Original===
“And the rest of my family? We are all more or less related.”

===Chinese===
“那我家里其他人呢？我们多多少少都沾亲带故。”

===Original===
“I was to blame in part. Lost my temper and said more than I should, and they let me go.”

===Chinese===
“我也有一部分不是。发了脾气，说了过头的话，他们就放我走了。”

===Original===
“Very characteristic; of England, I mean. I am sorry. I suppose, by the way, you didn’t see about the house a book on early Church vessels? If you had, it would be easier for me to call my son to order.”

===Chinese===
“很有典型性——我是说，很有英格兰的典型性。我很遗憾。对了，我想问一句：你在那宅子里，没见到过一本论早期教会器皿的书吧？你要是见过，我要申诫儿子就更有话可说了。”

===Original===
Say no; say no; say no. A fool I shall look. He’s seen I hesitated. What in hell does it matter?

===Chinese===
说没有；说没有；说没有。不然我会像个傻子。他已经看出我犹豫了。这他妈的究竟有什么要紧？

===Original===
“I sort of remember a book like that in the library.”

===Chinese===
“我依稀记得，书房里是有那么一本书。”

===Original===
“Not in my son’s room?”

===Chinese===
“没在我儿子房里？”

===Original===
“No, I was never there.”

===Chinese===
“没有，我从没进过他的房间。”

===Original===
The old man did not seem pleasant; silent after he had been told a lie. Then he began to speak fast.

===Chinese===
老者显得不痛快；听了一句谎之后，沉默下来。随后他开始说得飞快。

===Original===
“As you have acknowledged a difficulty, I feel that I might as well tell you why I have come down﻿—at least to find out whether that book is there or not. Why should he want it? He’s quite illiterate. Only if he has it, I shall be on the track of what may be cropping up again. You know what I mean﻿—romantic ideas, now that we know they are lies, which are liable to fall into very silly and very evil practices. Excuses for perversions.”

===Chinese===
“既然你承认在那边待得不顺，我不妨告诉你我下来的缘由——至少是要查明那本书在不在。他要它做什么？他大字不识几个。可只要书在他手里，我就能循迹追下去，追那可能重新冒头的东西。你懂我的意思——浪漫的念头，如今我们已知是谎话，却动辄堕入极愚蠢、极邪恶的行径。为种种倒错张目的借口。”

===Original===
Carston thought: It’s coming out. The old man has a drink in him. *In vino veritas:* good old Montparnasse. Again his curiosity, he said:

===Chinese===
卡斯顿心想：要露底了。老头儿肚里有酒。*In vino veritas*（酒中见真言）：好一个老蒙帕纳斯（Montparnasse）。好奇心又占了上风，他说：

===Original===
“There was nothing like that down there.”

===Chinese===
“那边没有那样的东西。”

===Original===
The old man said: “My book’s gone﻿—and if he has taken that he may have taken something else. There’s his cousin, Scylla, there﻿—”

===Chinese===
老者说：“我的书不见了——他既然拿了那本，就还可能拿了别的。他那表妹斯库拉（Scylla）不就在那儿——”

===Original===
“She is beautiful﻿—”

===Chinese===
“她很美——”

===Original===
“I am glad to hear she is up to your new-world standards. But an affair with her mixed up with superstition and theft.”

===Chinese===
“听她够得上你们新世界的标准，我很高兴。可是同她纠缠的这桩事，掺和着迷信与偷窃。”

===Original===
“What is superstition over here?”

===Chinese===
“在你们这边，‘迷信’指什么？”

===Original===
“A disgusting relic of non-understood natural law.”

===Chinese===
“一种没弄懂的自然法则，留下的令人作呕的遗物。”

===Original===
“I’m at sea.”

===Chinese===
“我如坠五里雾中。”

===Original===
“Of course, you are, and I’m glad to hear it, and that you saw nothing objectionable. In spite of your little difficulty, whatever it was.”

===Chinese===
“你当然在雾里，我很高兴听你这么说——也高兴你没见到任何可指摘的东西。尽管你有过那么一点小小的不痛快，不管那是什么。”

===Original===
“Tell me,” said Carston, “what do you expect me to have seen?”

===Chinese===
“告诉我，”卡斯顿说，“您料想我会见到过什么？”

===Original===
The old man considered: “A strained, shall we say, morbid situation between my son and Scylla Taverner. Repetition in another key with Clarence Lake. Remember, the idea of the first comes from you. The latter, I have frequently observed with disgust. So long as there has been no mention of a cup﻿—”

===Chinese===
老者斟酌道：“一种紧张的、不妨说病态的局面，在我儿子和斯库拉·塔弗纳之间。换一个调子，在克拉伦斯·莱克（Clarence Lake）身上重演。记住，头一桩的说法出自你。后一桩，我可是屡屡冷眼看着，心里作呕。只消还没人提起过一只杯——”

===Original===
Cup. My God! And I’m half in mine. Carston heard a noise like bells he distinguished for the blood in his ears. Then there rang over Starn a variation on three notes, flood-tide pouring into the hill circle, passing out down the valleys, striking and hushed at once on the grass cloth of the hills.

===Chinese===
杯。我的天！而我自己也已半只身子泡在我的杯里了。卡斯顿听见一阵如铃的声响，他辨出那是自己耳中的血声。随后，三个音符的一组变奏鸣响在斯塔恩上空，像涨潮涌进群山的环抱，又顺着山谷流泻而出，一碰到群山铺开的草绿帷布，便霎时敲响又归于沉寂。

===Original===
“Don’t tell,” said Starn bells. “Don’t tell. Don’t tell!”

===Chinese===
“别讲出去，”斯塔恩的钟声说。“别讲。别讲！”

===Original===
He thought: I must tell something, I need to. There must be something I can tell. Not tell on them.

===Chinese===
他想：我必须讲点什么，我需要讲。总该有些东西是我能讲的。但不是告他们的状。

===Original===
The old man was talking with something in his voice of a stallion’s scream:

===Chinese===
老者正说着话，嗓音里带着一种东西，像种马的嘶鸣：

===Original===
“My son’s after Scylla Taverner with a piece out of my collection. As if I didn’t know where I got it, and all about it. And what put him up to it? And what’ll that neurotic hussy make of it? But if he did it, I’ve got a surprise for them. Its story’ll be the surprise, if he doesn’t mind being turned out with his fancy girl﻿—”

===Chinese===
“我儿子揣着我藏品里的一件东西，正去追斯库拉·塔弗纳。仿佛我不知道它的来历，不知道它的全部底细似的。是谁怂恿他干的？那个神经质的骚女人又会拿它搞出什么名堂？可他要是真干了，我有份惊喜等着他们。那东西的来历就是惊喜——只要他不嫌带着他那俏丽的小情人，一道被扫地出门——”

===Original===
Not tell on them.

===Chinese===
不告他们的状。

===Original===
Carston said: “It isn’t what you think, at all, Mr. Tracy. I’ve nothing against your son in general. I reckon now that I was jealous. You see, I’m in love with Miss Taverner, and his easy ways angered me. That’s why I left.”

===Chinese===
卡斯顿说：“事情根本不是您想的那样，特雷西先生。总的说来，我对令郎没有任何意见。我现在想，我是嫉妒了。您瞧，我爱上了塔弗纳小姐，而他那副熟不拘礼的随和劲儿惹恼了我。我走，是为这个。”

===Original===
At once he ceased to be an object of interest. But he was believed. Fooled the old man who was down there, up to less good than anyone else. The bells stopped. There was a feeling that the air had been emptied forever. A cow mooed. Life started again. He went on easily:

===Chinese===
他顿时不再是让人感兴趣的对象了。但他被相信了。骗过了这老头儿——老头儿自己不也下来了嘛，憋的坏水比谁都多。钟声停了。人只觉得空气被永远抽空了。一头牛哞了一声。生活重新开始。他不紧不慢地接着说：

===Original===
“Funny how a love you feel is hopeless spoils your judgment. Goodness knows I never noticed anything of what you suggest. I just couldn’t get inside their life, and I wanted to get into hers﻿—”

===Chinese===
“真怪，一场自觉无望的爱情，竟能这样败坏人的判断。老天知道，您影射的事我一件也没留心到。我只是进不到他们的生活里去，而我又想进到她的生活里去——”

===Original===
“There was that book in the library,” said the old man.

===Chinese===
“书房里确实有过那么一本书。”老者说。

===Original===
Inspiration came lighdy.

===Chinese===
灵感翩然而至。

===Original===
“You half talked me into that. Now I remember it had a bookplate in it, Felix Taverner’s.” If I fool him too much, he’ll go to bed. And I ought to warn them. Warn them of what? That the old man knows the cup’s gone. Certainly. Picus is the sort to take it out on Scylla. The Sanc-Grail theory’s bust anyhow. Tell ’em that. He listened to a theory of the rights of owners to their property which sounded exaggerated even in the mouth of an elderly English collector.

===Chinese===
“那话有一半是您引我说的。对了，我想起来了，那本书里贴着一张藏书票——菲利克斯·塔弗纳（Felix Taverner）的。”我若骗他太过，他就要起身去睡了。再说，我也该给他们递个信。递什么信？就说老头儿知道杯子丢了。一定得递。皮库斯是那号人，会拿斯库拉出气。圣杯（Sanc-Grail）之说反正已经砸了。就这么告诉他们。他听着一整套物主对其财产之权利的理论——那套理论即便出诸一位年迈的英国收藏家之口，也显得夸张。

===Original===
The old red lips moved unpleasantly in their thatch of dead-white hair:

===Chinese===
两片老而红润的嘴唇，在死白须发织成的茅草里讨厌地嚅动着：

===Original===
Prupperty: prupperty: prupperty.

===Chinese===
财——产：财——产：财——产。

===Original===
*The earth one great city of gods and men.*

===Chinese===
*大地，神与人同栖的一座伟大城邦。*

===Original===
He began to live again in moments of insight. They were exceedingly unlike the flashes by which they are generally described, more like obstructions removed, revealing a landscape that had always been there.

===Chinese===
他又开始在一次次洞见里活了过来。那些洞见，同人们惯常描写的那种灵光乍现极不一样，倒更像搬开了什么障碍物，露出一片本就一直在那儿的风景。

===Original===
The old man seemed to have come out of the Roman world. That was difficult to understand, except on a theory that times are grouped otherwise than in sequences. What had his kind been doing at the time of the Roman world? When they had been pouring out of Britain, who had been pouring in? The ancestors of the peasants Carston had seen; but it was not a question of ancestors. There had been a story then of a king, a *comitatus* called Arthur, whose business had been divided between chasing barbarians and looking for a cup. A kind of intermezzo in history, in a time called the Dark Ages, which had produced a story about starlight. Suns of centuries had succeeded it, while the story had lived obscurely in some second-rate literature, and more obscurely, and as an unknown quality, in the imaginations of men like Picus and Scylla, Felix, Clarence, Ross. A very bad old man was putting an unpleasant finger into that pie. Carston was sure of one thing, that he disliked him more than his son. The old man was studying him, with coldness fired by brandy.

===Chinese===
老者像是直接从罗马世界里走出来的人。这很难理解，除非依一种理论：时间的编组，并不按先后次序。罗马世界当令之时，他这一族人在做什么？当他们从布列颠倾泻而出，又是谁在涌入？是卡斯顿见过的那些庄稼人的祖先；可这又不是祖先的问题。那时有过一个王的故事，一支号称亚瑟（Arthur）的*comitatus*（扈从战团），其营生分作两半：驱逐蛮族，寻找一只杯。那算是历史中的一段幕间曲，落在一个号称黑暗时代的时期，产出了一个关于星光的故事。此后，一个个世纪的太阳轮番照临，而那故事晦暗地活在某些二流文字里，又更晦暗地、作为一种未知的质，活在皮库斯和斯库拉、菲利克斯、克拉伦斯、罗斯（Ross）这类人的想象里。如今，一个非常坏的老头儿，正把一根叫人不快的手指伸进这块馅饼。卡斯顿确信一件事：他厌恶这老头儿，胜过厌恶他儿子。老者正打量着他，白兰地把那份冷峻烧得发亮。

===Original===
“I can’t exactly promise to avenge your wrongs, Mr. Carston. But I assure you my son will regret it if he has tampered with my collection. If he has with him a small jade cup, quite ageless in appearance, and slightly ornamented, and if he has persuaded himself that it has some superstitious history, my visit may afford you some satisfaction.”

===Chinese===
“替你雪冤，我可不能确切应承，卡斯顿先生。但我向你保证：他若动了我的藏品，必会后懊。假使他身边带着一只小小的玉杯，看去全然无年代可考，略饰花纹；又假使他说服了自己，信它有什么迷信的来历——那么我这一趟，或许能让你出口气。”

===Original===
Carston thought: He is mad about property, and he hates his son. And his son’s lover, and youth and imagination, and all there is to love over there. He believes in something too. In the thing which he accuses in his son. Whatever that is. Something I can no more imagine in Picus than that Picus doesn’t wash. The devils believe backwards. I can’t grudge the man a trick or two with that behind him. Now I know the father, I can’t hate the son any more.

===Chinese===
卡斯顿心想：他对财产中了魔，他恨他儿子。恨他儿子的情人，恨青春，恨想象，恨那边一切值得爱的东西。他自己也信着点什么。信的正是他指控在他儿子身上的那桩事。不管那究竟是什么。那东西我没法想象会应在皮库斯身上，正如没法想象皮库斯竟会不洗澡。魔鬼也信——不过是倒着信。他背后既然站着那一套，我也不便怪他耍一两手花招。如今认得了父亲，我再也没法恨那儿子了。

===Original===
He noticed the bad moral that if he had stayed over there and behaved himself, he would not have had this interesting insight into his late hosts’ private lives. Another brandy went down. He wanted to go for the old man on their behalf, and excited with drink he needed to talk. So long as he did not say the word cup.

===Chinese===
他留意到一条糟糕的教训：倘若当初他留在那边、行为检点些，就绝不会对前东道主们的私生活获得这份有趣的洞见。又一杯白兰地下了肚。他想替那家人出头，去治一治老头儿，而且酒后亢奋，正需要说话。只消不说出“杯”这个字。

===Original===
“I assure you, sir, you won’t find anything a father would object to in that house.” He knows why I am saying this, because I’ve seen him.

===Chinese===
“我向您保证，先生，在那座宅子里，您不会找到任何当父亲的会反对的东西。”他知道我为什么说这话，因为我已见识过他这号人。

===Original===
“They strike me as people who have loved and suffered a great deal. That purifies.”

===Chinese===
“依我看，他们是一些爱得很深、也受苦很深的人。受苦能净化人。”

===Original===
“From what?”

===Chinese===
“从什么里净化？”

===Original===
“From being like what you say﻿—From only thinking of yourself.” It is not agreeable to be dismissed like a baby. He had to remember that the old man hadn’t seen through him.

===Chinese===
“从变成您说的那种人里——从只想着自己里。”被人当娃娃似的打发掉，可不是滋味。他得记着：老头儿并没有看穿他。

===Original===
“If you were to ask me, I should say that they were looking for something. Miss Taverner told me one day that what they wanted had been lost out of the world.”

===Chinese===
“您要是问我，我会说：他们在找一样东西。塔弗纳小姐有一天对我说过，他们想要的东西，已经从这世界上失落了。”

===Original===
“When and what?” said the old man.

===Chinese===
“什么时候？什么东西？”老者说。

===Original===
“I don’t quite get their dates. Might have been any time, the Middle Ages, or the day before yesterday﻿—a thing that’s been lost﻿—”

===Chinese===
“他们的年代我摸不太准。可能是任何时候——中世纪，也可能是前天——一样失落了的东西——”

===Original===
“There was only one thing lost of a symbolic value in the Middle Ages,” said the old man.

===Chinese===
“中世纪失落的、具有象征价值的东西，只有一件。”老者说。

===Original===
Then Carston’s cups influenced him to obvious caution﻿—then to dreams. He saw Picus making pretty things, Felix laughing, Ross painting, Clarence sleeping, Scylla running away into thunder over burnt grass: running in, in love, through a rain wall.

===Chinese===
随后，卡斯顿的几杯酒引他转入摆在明面上的谨慎——又引他坠入梦境。他看见皮库斯在制造漂亮物件，菲利克斯在笑，罗斯在作画，克拉伦斯在酣睡，斯库拉在焦草之上、雷声之中奔跑远去：又怀着爱，穿过一道雨墙奔了回来。

===Original===
The energy that had got him away must get him back, and damned early. He arranged to be called, arranged for a car, without question that the morning would find him in the same mind.

===Chinese===
当初把他拽开的那股劲头，现在必须把他拽回去，而且要快得要命。他安排了叫早，安排了汽车，毫不怀疑明早一觉醒来，自己心意依旧。
