## XIX / 十九、鲱鱼天

===Original===
It was perceptibly cooler, stale-cool, uneasy air-threads stirring in the straight wood drive. No sun since three o’clock, but a glaring grey gauze overhead. Outside the wood, below the little cliff, a small scoop of bay protected the fisherman’s boats. From the edge they could have stepped on to the roof of his hut, whose tarred shingles were frosted with salt. Set on a ledge out of storms’ reach, brambles padded it from the cliffs side, and it was reached down wooden balks, steep-set and built into the clay. It surprised Carston to see the egg-blue and peacock water changed to the colour of a gun-barrel. Little wind, but the sea was twitching, slapping against the rocks; the colours inland, neither light-veiled or shining, but off a new palette.

===Chinese===
天明显地凉了，一种闷坏的凉；笔直的林中车道上，一缕缕不安的气流在轻轻搅动。三点钟过后就没了太阳，头顶只罩着一层刺眼的灰纱。林子外面、小崖底下，一湾浅浅的凹湾护着渔人的船。从崖沿上，他们抬脚就能踏上他那棚屋的屋顶——屋面上焦油板瓦结了一层盐霜。棚屋安在一道风暴够不着的石台上，悬崖那一面有荆棘丛给它垫护着；人下去要走几条木跳，陡陡地嵌在黏土里。卡斯顿惊讶地看到，那蛋壳蓝、孔雀绿的海水换成了枪管的颜色。风不大，海却在抽搐，一下一下拍打着礁石；内陆的颜色，既不像蒙了层轻纱，也不见光亮，倒像才从一块新调色板上取下来的。

===Original===
Scylla was staring out to sea, and her head lifted in profile made her look at the sky, where it seemed as if some mathematical monster had risen out of the west. For where the sun was turning down-Channel, a ball glared, surrounded by ranks of rose bars, and out from these clouds radiated that reached over to the eastern heavens, across whose spokes strayed loose flakes dipped in every variety of flame, the triangles of empty sky stained all the greens between primrose and jade.

===Chinese===
斯库拉正凝望着外海。她抬起头，侧影一扬，目光便落到天上——那西天，仿佛升起了某种几何学怪物。就在太阳正沉向海峡下游的地方，一团光球灼灼刺目，四周环列着一排排玫瑰色的光杠；从这些云里辐散出一道道云，直铺到东天；辐条之间，游散着一片片蘸过百样火色的碎云，空当里一块块三角形的天，染遍了报春花色与翡翠绿之间的种种绿。

===Original===
“Herring sky,” she said.

===Chinese===
“鲱鱼天。”她说。

===Original===
“What does that mean?”

===Chinese===
“这是什么意思？”

===Original===
She laughed with a confident joy he understood the first time.

===Chinese===
她笑了，笑出一种笃定的欢喜，他头一回听见就懂了。

===Original===
“Southwest wind. Listen!” He heard his heart beating, a hair in his ear, and a trans-finite length away, the stirring that had made uneasy the sea.

===Chinese===
“西南风。听！”他听见自己的心在跳，耳朵里一根发丝的窸窣，还听见在超限遥远的远处，那阵搅得大海不宁的骚动。

===Original===
“A big storm,” she said, turning on him eyes full of an animal’s pleasure. Round the point where the day before he arrived they had played at Aphrodites, a boat came dipping back, the bowsprit dancing and dripping, where over the deep-travelling reef the sea had begun to coil under and over.

===Chinese===
“一场大风暴。”她说着，转过一双蓄满兽性欢愉的眼睛看他。绕过那个岬角——就在他抵达的前一天，他们曾在那儿扮过阿芙洛狄忒（Aphrodites）——一条船颠簸着回来了，船首斜桅又舞又滴水；在那道深水暗礁上，海水已经开始上下盘卷。

===Original===
“Harris is back in time. We’re in for it. No more fish.” A note in her brain about the fish problem. More too freshly killed meat. Picus and Felix on the subject. Then the only good poem a bad poet ever wrote, anticipating jazz:

===Chinese===
“哈里斯（Harris）回来得正是时候。我们要摊上一场了。再没有鱼啦。”她脑子里就鱼的难题记下一笔。还得添些现宰的鲜肉。这题目留给皮库斯和菲利克斯。接着，是那位坏诗人一生写下的唯一一首好诗，早早地预示了爵士乐：

===Original===
> “When descends on the Atlantic
 

 The gigantic
 

 Storm-wind of the equinox.”

===Chinese===
> “当那浩大的
 

 二分日风暴
 

 降临到大西洋之上。”

===Original===
He took her hands and they rocked, saying with her:

===Chinese===
他拉起她的手，两人摇着身子，跟着她一起念：

===Original===
> “From Bermuda’s reefs and edges
 

 Of sunken ledges
 

 On some far-off bright Azore
 

 From Bahama and the dashing
 

 Silver-flashing
 

 Surges of San Salvador.”

===Chinese===
> “从百慕大的礁脉与边沿，
 

 从沉没的礁台
 

 远方明媚的亚速尔，
 

 从巴哈马，和那奔腾
 

 银光飞闪
 

 圣萨尔瓦多的巨浪。”

===Original===
Fallen into nature with her. For the first time in his life. Good old Gulf Stream.

===Chinese===
同她一道坠进了自然。平生头一遭。好样的老墨西哥湾流（Gulf Stream）。

===Original===
Harris the fisherman had downed his mainsheet, flung out an anchor and was rushing a dinghy to shore.

===Chinese===
渔夫哈里斯已经落了主帆索，抛出一枚锚，正驾着小艇抢着往岸上赶。

===Original===
“Take your fish, Madame Taverner, it’s the last you’ll see. I’ve got my pots to get in.”

===Chinese===
“把鱼拿去吧，塔弗纳夫人，这是您能见着的最后一批了。我还得把龙虾笼收回来。”

===Original===
“We’ll help.”

===Chinese===
“我们来帮忙。”

===Original===
Two minutes later Carston was in a dinghy in a slightly resentful scoop of sea, and spent an ungrateful hour hauling up lobster-cages filled with kicking, pinching sea cardinals, ink blue; and the humanitarian protest came from Scylla when they were tossed into a cauldron bubbling on the stones, in an angle of the little secret cliff where England rose out of the harvested sea.

===Chinese===
两分钟后，卡斯顿已经坐在一条小艇里，漂在一小片微含怨气的湾水上，费了毫无谢意可言的一个钟头，拽起一笼笼又蹬又夹、一身墨蓝的海中红衣主教；而当这些龙虾被倒进一口在石上滚沸的大锅——锅安在小隐崖的一角，英格兰便是从那儿、从收割过的海里升起来的——发出人道主义抗议的，却是斯库拉。

===Original===
The wood was darkish, fearful and sad, until they saw the windows shut and already lit, a fire leaping up made out of the driftwood he had helped haul up. Blue sparks and white and green, wind shifting about outside in the trees, until with a scream the up-Channel gale was loosed and they became creatures couched under a stone that quivered in the uproar and mounted to bed with candles streaming.

===Chinese===
林子黑沉沉的，可怖而悲凉，直到他们望见宅子的窗都已关严、亮起了灯，一炉火正蹿着苗，烧的是他帮忙拖上岸的浮木。火花蓝的、白的、绿的，风在屋外的树间四处打转；随后，一声尖啸，上溯海峡的大风放了出来，他们便成了伏在一块石头底下的生灵，石头在喧嚣中震颤；他们端着火苗乱窜的蜡烛上了床。

===Original===
Carston lay in bed and heard above the thunder a gull repeating itself. “*Ai, ai*,” it said, up somewhere in the tumbling sky, a little noise laid delicately upon the universal roar of air. The house was strong, he thought, its stone thrilling but not a window that rattled, tapped by their climbing roses.

===Chinese===
卡斯顿躺在床上，听见雷声之上有一只鸥鸟在反复叫着同一声。“*唉，唉*（*Ai, ai*），”它说，在翻腾天空的高处某个地方，一点细巧的声响，轻轻覆在空气的普遍怒吼之上。宅子结实，他想——石头在发颤，却没有一扇窗格格作响，只有满墙攀上来的玫瑰在轻叩窗。

===Original===
There were pockets in the wind when he could hear the sea. A crash, then under-roar and scream of pebbles, the ravelled water dragged. A light-patch fell on his floor, a piece of the late moon racing apparently from a cloud whipped off her, and behind her a star or so, unhurried, observant and indifferent on a night when everything was out and about. He looked out to sea, surprised that it appeared no more than a bright silver lacquer, when water mountains should have been moving in.

===Chinese===
风里有一阵阵空当，那时他听得见海。轰然一响，接着是卵石的闷吼与尖叫，绞散的海水被拖了回去。一方光斑落在他地板上，一弯迟月，显然正从一朵被抽走的云后赶出来，身后跟着一两颗星，不慌不忙，冷眼看着，事不关己——在这样一个万物都出动了乱窜的夜里。他朝外海望去，惊讶它看上去不过是一层亮银的漆，尽管按说该有水的群山正推进来。

===Original===
Scylla alone wondered how the wood below Gault was bearing it, flooded with salt water, heaped with seaweed, the little stream choked with wrack.

===Chinese===
只有斯库拉独自挂念着，高尔特崖下那片树林怎么受得了——咸水漫了进去，海草堆了满地，小溪被冲上来的海藻噎得死死的。

===Original===
Dark again in Carston’s room. A rain-flaw drummed on the panes. Then with a shriek the wind sprang again. He could not hear the gull, but a few seconds later a crack and a long crash, knew it was a tree gone, and looking out in the next moon-interval, saw something altered in the outline of the wood. It disturbed him to think how hopeless the dawn would be, the “dew silky” quiet changed for grey air, spray-salted on the lips. Would the others enjoy it? Would it blow the nonsense out of them? They might be housebound for days. Oh, God! He was wondering how to prevent this when he fell asleep.

===Chinese===
卡斯顿房里又黑了下来。一阵斜雨敲着窗玻璃。随后风又尖啸着跳起来。他听不见那只鸥了，可几秒钟后，一声脆响，接着一声长长的轰塌——他知道是一棵树倒了；趁下一次月光间歇望出去，他看见树林的轮廓变了些样。他心烦地想，明天的黎明该多么无望：“露水如丝”的静谧换成了灰蒙蒙的天，唇上带着浪沫的咸味。旁人会觉得有意思吗？这场风会把他们身上的胡闹劲儿刮跑吗？他们说不定得困在屋里好几天。哦，上帝！他正琢磨着怎么防止这一层，就睡着了。
