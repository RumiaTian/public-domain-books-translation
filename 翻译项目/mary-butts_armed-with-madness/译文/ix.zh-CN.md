## IX / 九、荒原迷途

===Original===
At a choice of tracks apparently parallel and similar, Picus had led them to the left. He had trooped them through a wood and sweated them over a crest, to drop them again in a sunk road, made for running contraband a century before. He was asking: “Where are the others?”

===Chinese===
几条小径看上去平行而相似，一时无从挑拣，皮库斯（Picus）却领着大家往左去了。他领着这队人穿过一片树林，又让他们汗涔涔地翻过一道山脊，再把他们重新丢进一条洼路——一条百年前走私客跑出来的路。他问：“其他人呢？”

===Original===
“I want my mead,” said Felix. “The farm’s ahead on the water’s edge, where the heath runs out in a point.”

===Chinese===
“我要喝我的蜂蜜酒（mead），”菲利克斯（Felix）说。“农庄就在前头水边上，荒原伸出个尖角的地方。”

===Original===
Sunk roads are filled with loose sand. They go from nowhere to nowhere now. They trap the sun and keep out air. Their banks curl over in a fringe of heather wire. Adders bask on their striped sides. From this one Starn was invisible, and the eventual blue water bright as dew.

===Chinese===
洼路里积满浮沙。如今它们从无处通向无处。它们把太阳困在里面，把风挡在外面。路坡的边沿蜷起一圈石楠铁丝似的流苏。蝰蛇在条纹斑驳的坡壁上晒太阳。从这一条里望不见斯塔恩（Starn），也望不见尽头那片蓝色水面，亮得如同露水。

===Original===
Carston plodded beside Clarence, his feet chafing with sand. They left the wood and crossed another ridge, and saw on their right a creek of yellow grass running inland behind them. They walked another mile till they came to the base of the point, and saw cottages under a crest of dark land. Felix cried out: “We were wrong. Look over there!” They looked across the creek, now filled with water, and only bordered with its grass. Two miles on the farther side, on a patch of green land reclaimed from the heath, was a noble group of trees round a white farm.

===Chinese===
卡斯顿（Carston）挨着克拉伦斯（Clarence）一步步苦走，双脚叫沙子磨得生疼。他们出了树林，又翻过一道山脊，看见右边一条生满黄草的水湾，在他们身后折向内陆。又走了一英里，到了那尖角的根上，望见一道黑土地的坡顶下有几座村舍。菲利克斯喊了起来：“咱们走错了。看那边！”他们朝水湾对岸望去——湾里这会儿已涨满了水，只剩两岸镶着那条黄草边。对岸两英里开外，一片从荒原里垦出来的绿地上，一簇气派的树木环着一座白色的农庄。

===Original===
“We should have gone there,” said Felix﻿—“this comes of Picus’s swank about knowing the heath. This leads only to a place we called Misery, because they starved their dogs.”

===Chinese===
“咱们本该往那边去，”菲利克斯说——“这都是皮库斯吹嘘自己认得荒原的下场。这条路只通到一个我们管它叫‘苦命庄’的地方，因为那儿的人把自家的狗都饿得半死。”

===Original===
“I saw no tracks in the sand,” said Carston.

===Chinese===
“我在沙地上可没看见什么岔道，”卡斯顿说。

===Original===
“I supposed you knew the way.” He heard them screaming like gulls over dead fish and would have preferred the birds’ company. Abusing each other for what they would forgive in two minutes. Consider rather a joke. Forget. They none of them saw that Picus had done it on purpose. Then Carston admitted that he might not have thought of it himself if the night before Picus had not slept with the woman he wanted. Why had he done it? He had separated himself from the woman, to mislead them.

===Chinese===
“我还以为你认得路呢。”他听着他们吵，像一群围着死鱼尖叫的海鸥，倒宁愿有那群鸟做伴。为着两分钟后就会彼此原谅的事，互相臭骂。不如当它个笑话。忘了它。他们谁都没看出来，皮库斯是故意干的。接着卡斯顿承认，倘若头天夜里皮库斯没有跟他想要的那个女人睡，他自己未必想得到这一层。他为什么要这么干？他把自己从那女人身边分开，为的是误导他们。

===Original===
They were lurching back through the sand the way they had come. But they had forgotten where they had entered the sunk road that led nowhere in particular; followed it as it wound into exactly the middle of everywhere, and looking over its bank found the airy roofs of Starn lost behind a line of high black trees.

===Chinese===
他们在沙里跌跌撞撞往回走，循着来路。可他们忘了当初从哪儿拐进这条洼路的——它哪儿也不特别通向；只好跟着它七拐八绕，绕到恰恰是四方的正中央，从路坡上探头一望，斯塔恩那些轻盈的屋顶已经丢在一排高高的黑树后面了。

===Original===
Forgiveness woke in them and fatigue. Picus had been mocked for vanity, had mocked back, and set them off laughing. They were nervous, sweating, flushed. Felix shrieked when a snake flicked across his shoe. But it was all part of a game. Hardly able to see more than the sky, they trudged the road the sun had made into Danae’s chimney, down which God came in a shower of gold. Clarence led them. Picus followed. He had deprecated Carston’s suggestion that they should try and cut across the creek and make for the farm. “Bad bog,” he said. “Marsh-king’s sons,” said Felix, “that would be the end of us,” and Clarence that he had seen a pony lost there. At this choice of pleasures, Carston had followed them; and the sunk road, having come to the middle, stopped in a circular sandbank round a foul pond bordered with marsh-grass where there was no way out. They flung themselves down.

===Chinese===
他们心里醒来了宽恕，也醒来了疲乏。皮库斯先前因虚荣受人嘲弄，回敬了嘲弄，又把他们逗得大笑起来。他们神经紧张，汗流浃背，满脸通红。一条蛇嗖地掠过菲利克斯的鞋面，他尖叫了一声。可这一切都不过是游戏的一部分。天上之外几乎什么也看不见，他们吃力地走在那条太阳已烤成达那厄（Danae）烟囱的路上——当年神明就是化作一阵金雨，顺着它降下来的。克拉伦斯领头。皮库斯跟随。卡斯顿提议试着横穿水湾、直奔农庄，他不以为然。“烂泥沼，”他说。“沼泽王的儿子们，”菲利克斯说，“那咱们就全交待在那儿了。”克拉伦斯则说，他见过一匹小马陷死在那里。在这两般乐事之间，卡斯顿跟了他们；而那条洼路走到正中央，便到了头——一圈环形的沙堤，围着一只污浊的池塘，塘边镶满沼草，没有出路。他们一头栽倒在地。

===Original===
It occurred to Carston that it was all nonsense. They had only a few miles to go. Keeping towards the long hills, they must strike a road and eventually, Starn. At Starn there was water, tea, lime-juice, gin, champagne. The liverish grass and thick water displeased him. “We can’t stay forever in this cup,” he said.

===Chinese===
卡斯顿忽然觉得这一切荒唐透了。他们不过还有几英里路。朝着长长的山峦走下去，总能撞上一条大路，最终撞上斯塔恩。斯塔恩有水，有茶，有青柠汁，有杜松子酒，还有香槟。这肝色的草、稠浊的水，他看着就生厌。“咱们总不能永远泡在这只杯里，”他说。

===Original===
Picus was asleep, Clarence bending over him. An odd sort of pietà. Felix was sinking in the mud, collecting a plant he said was for Ross. All careful of each other’s needs and pleasures. He heard Clarence call softly: “Wet a handkerchief, Felix, I want it for his head.” Saw it brought over, and Picus jump up in a flash. “None of that sort of water for me, thanks.” To spite the men who took care of him? Probably. Carston felt that he was being given an opportunity to hate and that was good. “Hadn’t you better try and get us out of this?” The tall creature looked at him, sadly. “I’ll try; but you’ll have to leave it to me again, you know.” He sprang up the hollowed bank, kicking mouthfuls of sand into Carston’s face, and vanished. Carston crawled up to watch, hiding behind the heather rim. He saw Picus run a few yards and fling himself down on his face. He waited and saw him get up, heard him call “It’s this way,” saw him glance round and make off. Carston called back to the other three, heaved himself over, and they set off on a slight track towards the belt of wood.

===Chinese===
皮库斯睡着了，克拉伦斯俯在他身上。一幅古怪的圣殇图。菲利克斯正陷在泥里，采一株他说要带给罗斯（Ross）的草。人人都在照料彼此的需要和乐趣。他听见克拉伦斯轻声唤道：“把手绢打湿，菲利克斯，我要给他敷头。”看着手绢递过去，皮库斯一闪身跳了起来。“这种水给我免了，多谢。”存心气一气照顾他的人？多半是。卡斯顿觉得自己正被赐予一个恨的机会，而这很好。“你不如想法子带我们出去吧？”那高个子的生灵悲伤地望了他一眼。“我试试；可你们又得把事情全交给我了，这你们知道。”他一纵身蹿上塌陷的路坡，踢起一口口的沙子，扬了卡斯顿一脸，人便没了影。卡斯顿爬上去张望，躲在石楠镶边后面。他看见皮库斯跑出几码，一头扑倒在地。他等着，又见他爬起来，听见他喊“是这边”，看见他四下一瞥，拔腿去了。卡斯顿回头招呼另外三个，翻过坡去，一行人便循着一条浅浅的小径，朝那带树林进发。

===Original===
They went in. Pine-needles are not easy to walk on, like a floor of red glass. It is not cool under them, a black scented life, full of ants, who work furiously and make no sound. Something ached in Carston, a regret for the cool brilliance of the wood they had left, the other side of the hills, on the edge of the sea. This one was full of harp-noises from a wind when there was none outside. He saw Picus ahead, a shadow shifting between trunk and trunk. Some kind of woodcraft he supposed, and said so to Felix who said sleepily: “Somebody’s blunt-faced bees, dipping under the thyme-spray”; a sentence which made things start living again. Would they never have enough of what they called life? There was no kind of a track over the split vegetable glass. A place that made you wonder what sort of nothing went on there, year in year out.

===Chinese===
他们走了进去。松针不好走，像一层红玻璃铺就的地。针叶底下并不凉快，是一种黑色的、芬芳的生命，爬满蚂蚁，它们发疯似的忙碌，却不作一点声响。卡斯顿心里有什么东西在发疼，一阵懊悔，懊悔山那边、大海边上那片被他们丢下的清凉明澈的树林。这一片满是竖琴般的鸣响，来自一股外面根本没有的风。他看见皮库斯在前头，一道影子在树干与树干之间挪移。他想那总归是某种林中行路的本事，便这么对菲利克斯说了；菲利克斯睡意蒙眬地答：“谁家的钝脸蜜蜂，一头扎到百里香枝子底下去了。”这句话让万物重新活了起来。他们所谓的“生活”，他们就永远要不够么？在那碎开的草木玻璃上，没有半条路。这样一个地方，让你不由得琢磨：年复一年，这里进行着的究竟是哪一种“无”。

===Original===
The end of the wood was a little cliff, pitted with rabbit holes; and where the hills opened, Starn towering, not too far.

===Chinese===
树林的尽头是一面小崖，布满兔洞；山峦豁开的地方，斯塔恩高高耸峙，不算太远。

===Original===
“We get down here,” said Carston, trying the loose lip of the cliff.

===Chinese===
“咱们从这儿下去，”卡斯顿说着，探了探崖口那松动的唇沿。

===Original===
“Into what?” said Felix. Then they saw that the wood was surrounded again by marsh, the end of the creek which had separated them from Ross and Scylla, curled round on itself. Cutting them off from Starn this time. Clarence said: “Where’s Picus?” and Carston hoped that young man was about to get what was coming to him. Only he had gone. They began to think of their sins. Except Carston, who did not think of sins at all. There was raw heath on the other side, but the marsh was wide, and Carston was assured deadly. They left the little cliff’s edge, and reentered the wood. He heard Felix saying: “Where has Picus gone?” Gone to find Scylla? Carston wondered. By a quick turn which would bring him suddenly face to face. And at the folly of three strong young men, tramping about, lost and sapped.

===Chinese===
“下到哪儿去？”菲利克斯说。这时他们才看见，树林四周又是沼泽——正是先前把他们和罗斯、斯库拉（Scylla）隔开的那条水湾的尽头，此刻蜷了回来，兜住自身。这一回隔断的是斯塔恩。克拉伦斯说：“皮库斯呢？”卡斯顿巴望那小子马上领受他活该领受的东西。只是他已经走了。他们开始思量起自己的罪过。卡斯顿除外，他压根不思量罪过。对面是生荒原，可沼泽宽，而卡斯顿确信它致命。他们离开小崖的边沿，重新钻回树林。他听见菲利克斯说：“皮库斯上哪儿去了？”找斯库拉去了？卡斯顿寻思。抄个急转弯，猛地同她打照面。还有这份荒唐——三个强壮的年轻人，走来走去，迷了路，泄空了劲。

===Original===
“Call for him,” he said. “He’d not answer,” said Clarence﻿—“he may have had one of his bad pains again.” Carston wished one on him, making an image of him in his anger, until he thought he saw him, walking towards them, transparent and powerful, malicious and shy; his hat perched, to remind them of hooded birds. Remembered his magic, and forgave him. Grew angry again and dazed. Envious also, because he had no part in Picus’s dance. “Leave him alone,” he said, “he’s gone to find a way.” Clarence put a hand on his shoulder; thanks, he supposed, for taking it like that.

===Chinese===
“喊他一声吧，”他说。“他不会应的，”克拉伦斯说——“他没准儿又犯他那阵剧痛了。”卡斯顿巴不得他真犯上一犯，怒气里给他造出一幅像来，造到后来，竟像真看见了他：正朝他们走来，透明而有力，恶毒而羞怯；帽子翘在头顶上，提醒人想起那些戴兜帽的鸟。想起了他的魔法，便原谅了他。跟着又恼起来，又发昏。还嫉妒，因为皮库斯的那支舞里没有他的份。“别管他了，”他说，“他找路去了。”克拉伦斯把一只手搭上他的肩；他猜这是道谢，谢他把这口气咽得这么顺当。

===Original===
At last, from the extreme end of the wood, in the least propitious place, they saw across the marsh a clear causeway of stone.

===Chinese===
终于，从树林最尽头、最不像有路的地方，他们望见沼泽对面一道清晰的石头堤道。

===Original===
They scrambled down, crossed it, found a path which led them under the hills by a white road into Starn.

===Chinese===
他们攀下去，过了堤道，找到一条小径，引着他们贴着山根，沿一条白色大路走进斯塔恩。

===Original===
About the end of the walk, the less said the better, until at the inn the landlord answered their faint cries for drink. He wanted to know why they had left six and come back three.

===Chinese===
这最后一段路怎么走完的，还是少说为妙——直到客栈里，店主人应了他们讨水喝的微弱呼喊。他想知道，为什么他们六个出去，只回来了三个。

===Original===
“Lost ’em,” said Felix.

===Chinese===
“丢了，”菲利克斯说。

===Original===
“You should have stuck to Mr. Tracy. He’s the one for the heath. Knows all the paths, and the people who bide out there.”

===Chinese===
“你们该咬住特雷西先生（Mr. Tracy）不放的。论荒原，他是头一份的人物。条条小路都认得，连住在那上头的人也都认得。”

===Original===
The talk petered out. The evening marched gravely down. The two undoubtable shops put up shutters. The tourists had gone. A man came through with a bunch of sheep. The bar of the labourer’s inn turned on a gramophone. From time to time, a huge man came in from the fields, making for his beer. The cries of children stopped. Mothers came out. A fast touring-car shot across the square. A mist rose, an intangible gauze, refreshing them. They dined in the inn garden. When Carston saw in the west a large star watching, he though of the lights on Broadway. Felix saluted the star. Clarence worried, but without, for once, worrying them.

===Chinese===
谈话渐渐泄了气。暮色庄重地行军下来。那两家不容置疑的铺子上起了门板。游客们已经走了。一个汉子赶着一小群羊打广场穿过。佣工客栈的酒吧间拧开了留声机。不时有个五大三粗的汉子从地里进来，直奔他的啤酒。孩子们的哭喊停了。母亲们走了出来。一辆高速旅行车射过广场。雾气升起来，一层不可捉摸的轻纱，让他们为之一爽。他们在客栈花园里用了晚餐。卡斯顿望见西天一颗大星守望着，想起了百老汇（Broadway）的灯火。菲利克斯向那颗星行了个礼。克拉伦斯在发愁，只是这一回没有让他们跟着愁。

===Original===
They set out to watch the square again.

===Chinese===
他们又出来守望广场。

===Original===
Then Scylla appeared, and Picus and Ross.

===Chinese===
接着斯库拉出现了，还有皮库斯和罗斯。

===Original===
“We found him, sitting on a stone, saying he had lost you: that you ran away from him on Hangar’s ridge.”

===Chinese===
“我们找到了他，坐在一块石头上，说自己把你们丢了：说你们在哈恩加山脊（Hangar’s ridge）上甩下他跑了。”

===Original===
“He lost you first,” said Ross. “I don’t blame you.”

===Chinese===
“是他先把你们弄丢的，”罗斯说。“我不怪你们。”

===Original===
“He did,” said Felix, “and cleared off when he’d made a final mess of it. I know his ways. Have you got the mead?”

===Chinese===
“他就是，”菲利克斯说，“等把一切搅成个彻底的烂摊子，便一溜了之。他那套我清楚。蜂蜜酒你们拿到了吗？”

===Original===
“Bottles,” said Scylla.

===Chinese===
“成瓶的，”斯库拉说。

===Original===
They were surprised when Picus, who had thrown himself into a chair, got up and said: “That’s how you take it. It was not my fault.”

===Chinese===
皮库斯本来一头栽进一把椅子，这时竟站起来说：“原来你们是这么看这件事的。那不是我的错。”众人都吃了一惊。

===Original===
Clarence sprang beside him.

===Chinese===
克拉伦斯一跃到了他身边。

===Original===
As he went out, Scylla touched his sleeve with her fingers. He took no notice, but Clarence did.

===Chinese===
他往外走时，斯库拉用手指碰了碰他的衣袖。他没理会，克拉伦斯却看在了眼里。

===Original===
Carston’s fatigue had passed into charity. They were all overtired, of course. The developments of over-fatigue were caprice and anger. What had Picus been up to? Helping himself with accidents, of course. But exasperating himself.

===Chinese===
卡斯顿的疲惫已经化成了慈悲。他们全都过累了，这是自然的。过累往下发展，便是反复无常和火气。皮库斯一直在弄什么名堂？当然是借着一桩桩意外给自己取利。可也把他自己气了个半死。

===Original===
“There were no bones broken,” he said, “you’ve got the mead, and we’ve all got back.” He was furious when Clarence ignored him, and went out into the half-light after the man who seemed to combine all the elements of a family curse. Felix began:

===Chinese===
“又没伤筋断骨，”他说，“蜂蜜酒也到手了，咱们也全都回来了。”克拉伦斯只当他不存在，他火透了，便走进半明半暗里，去追那个仿佛把一份家族诅咒的所有成分集于一身的男人。菲利克斯开了腔：

===Original===
“God! I’m sick of this. Why can’t we be at Biarritz leading a reasonable life?”

===Chinese===
“天哪！我烦透了。咱们干嘛不能去比亚里茨（Biarritz），过一点讲道理的日子？”

===Original===
A negro song came into Carston’s head: Bear your burden in the heat of the day: but it did not occur to him to tell it to the boy, whom it would have helped.

===Chinese===
一支黑人歌谣浮上卡斯顿心头：在白昼的酷热里背负你的重担。可他没想到把它说给那男孩听，虽然这歌本可以帮到他。

===Original===
Clarence came in again. “I’ve ordered the car. We ought to get back. I’ll tell the others.” Carston saw him go into the dining-room and heard Scylla call:

===Chinese===
克拉伦斯又进来了。“我叫了车。我们该回去了。我去告诉其他人。”卡斯顿看见他走进餐室，听见斯库拉喊：

===Original===
“Well, we want to eat. Tell it to wait.”

===Chinese===
“呃，我们还要吃饭呢。叫它等着。”

===Original===
“We must go home.”

===Chinese===
“我们必须回家。”

===Original===
“Take it home then, and send it back for us.”

===Chinese===
“那就让它回家去，再打发它回来接我们好了。”

===Original===
