## XIII / 十三、一日两爱

===Original===
Scylla went upstairs, and lying on her bed in her shift felt her elation and clean fatigue replaced by shabby weariness and fear.

===Chinese===
斯库拉（Scylla）上了楼，穿着衬裙往床上一躺，觉得那份兴奋和干净的疲乏，换成了寒碜的倦怠和恐惧。

===Original===
Picus had played that trick on Carston. Picus had spoiled her pride in him. Why had he played a spiteful joke? She had not begun to think it possible that he had arranged the story of the cup. Only the trick on Carston was ill-mannered, a little cruel. Also irrelevant. It had made the business seem empty, like the effect got at séances where the interesting, the decisive, the clear is always on the point of arrival, and invariably fades out before the point is reached.

===Chinese===
皮库斯（Picus）对卡斯顿（Carston）玩了那一手。皮库斯败坏了她为他感到的骄傲。他为什么要开一个恶意的玩笑？她还没来得及往那上面想——想到杯的那段故事可能就是他一手安排的。只是对卡斯顿的这一手失了分寸，有点狠。而且不相干。它把整桩事情衬得空落落的，像降神会上得到的那种效果：有趣的、决定性的、明晰的东西永远眼看就要到场，却总在到场之前消隐。

===Original===
Like the mass of Celtic art. Like, now she considered it, the whole Grail story, the saga story *par excellence* that has never come off, or found its form or its poet. Not like the Golden Fleece or Odysseus at Circe’s house. There was something in their lives spoiled and inconclusive like the Grail story. It would be her turn next for Picus to insult, as he had played a pointless joke on a foreign guest. A number of unpleasant emotions followed that thought, chiefly disturbed sexual vanity which sets the earth by the ears. Life was ice-bright, and disagreeable as flint. It was a maudlin dream. Chance couplings, little minds setting to partners. Victory of ants over the sphinx in flesh: over birds.

===Chinese===
像凯尔特艺术的大部分。像——如今她细想了——整个圣杯的故事，那篇*绝无仅有*的萨迦故事，从未圆满演成，从未找到它的形式或它的诗人。不像金羊毛，不像喀耳刻（Circe）家的奥德修斯（Odysseus）。他们的生活里有什么东西也是这样被败坏了、悬而不决，像圣杯的故事一样。下一个轮到她受皮库斯的羞辱，正像他对一位外国客人开了一个无谓的玩笑。这个念头引出一串不快的情绪，主要是被搅乱的性的虚荣——天下大乱，正是拜它所赐。生活亮得像冰，又硬得讨厌，像燧石。它是一场自伤自怜的梦。偶然的偶合，小小的心智各自配对起舞。蚂蚁对血肉斯芬克斯的胜利：对鸟的胜利。

---

===Original===
She watched the flies flashing across the window, a bee searching a flower head in ajar of mixed wild stalks Felix had put there. Then to detach herself she played an old game, that she was lying out on the wood’s roof: translating the stick and leaf that upheld her into herself: into sea: into sky. Sky back again into wood, flesh and sea.

===Chinese===
她望着苍蝇一道道掠过窗玻璃，一只蜜蜂在菲利克斯（Felix）插在那儿的一罐混扎的野花枝里搜寻一朵花头。接着，为把自己从自己身上拔出来，她玩起一个旧游戏：想象自己正躺在树林的顶上：把托着她的枝条和叶子翻译成她自己：翻译成海：翻译成天。再把天翻译回树林、血肉和海。

===Original===
It did not work, as it was meant to, to deliver her from herself, but it made her see Picus’s proceedings diabolic. Why so? Parody of a mystery. A mystery none of them believed. That reduced it to a bad taste. They did not quite disbelieve. Dangerous fooling then? Parodied also in her bed. Very cruel and so wrong.

===Chinese===
这游戏没有像它本来的用意那样奏效，把她从她自己那里解救出来，倒是让她看出了皮库斯那番作为的魔鬼底色。为什么这么说？是对一场奥秘的戏仿。一场他们谁也不信的奥秘。这就把它降成了趣味低劣。可他们又不全然不信。那么，是危险的胡闹？连她的床笫也遭了戏仿。非常残忍，大错特错。

===Original===
But under Gault Cliff there had been no parody. That she had to love Picus by, as much of a creation as any growth in nature. Or ritual, or rite produced by the imagination. As little symbolic as the result of any mystery propitiously performed.

===Chinese===
可在高尔特崖下，没有过任何戏仿。凭着那个，她才爱得下皮库斯——那同自然界里任何一株生长一样，是一件造化。或者说是一场仪轨，一次由想象生出的祭礼。同任何一场顺遂施行的奥秘的结果一样，毫不含什么象征。

===Original===
As she attended to what she was thinking, she laughed, her immense vitality racing back. Her entry had made his trick glorious. Dinner would be difficult. What had been wrong with Carston? She would go and talk to him. What about? She would propitiate Felix. How? They will all hate me. Without whom Picus would not have turned creator. Woman’s place indeed. Clarence wanted that job. He did the work, and I wear the crown. Not my fault. Chances of the sacred game.

===Chinese===
她留意到自己正在想的这些，笑了，那股巨大的活力奔驰着涌了回来。她那一进屋，把他的恶作剧衬得辉煌。晚饭不好办。卡斯顿到底是哪儿不对了？她要去找他谈谈。谈什么？她要安抚菲利克斯。怎么安抚？他们都会恨我。没有我，皮库斯成不了造物者。这可真是「女人的位置」了。克拉伦斯（Clarence）倒想要这份差事。活是他干的，王冠却由我来戴。不怪我。神圣游戏的机缘。

===Original===
Swept off into stadium of the game; which is the pleasure in actions for their own sake. Done for the love of playing. Done for the fun of it. Done for no pompous end.

===Chinese===
一头被卷进这游戏的竞技场；这游戏的乐趣，就在为行动而行动。为爱玩而做。为好玩而做。不为任何装腔作势的目的而做。

===Original===
That Felix was just a little nervous about.

===Chinese===
只这一样，让菲利克斯微微发怵。

===Original===
Played by Alexander, and young Cleopatra in a bundle at Caesar’s feet.

===Chinese===
亚历山大（Alexander）玩过它，年轻的克娄巴特拉（Cleopatra）裹成一团偎在恺撒（Caesar）脚边时也玩过它。

===Original===
Played by that demon Picus, when he had whistled up mystery with what was now undoubtedly a Victorian finger-bowl.

===Chinese===
那个魔鬼皮库斯也玩过——他用一只如今确凿无疑是维多利亚时代的洗指碗，吹哨召来了一场奥秘。

===Original===
Played by Malatesta having Isotta sculpted for the Madonna; and the man who broke the bank at Monte Carlo.

===Chinese===
马拉泰斯塔（Malatesta）玩过——他把伊索塔（Isotta）雕成了圣母；还有那个在蒙特卡洛（Monte Carlo）大破赌庄的人也玩过。

===Original===
Played by Chaucer who loved everything for what it was. A sword for being a sword, or a horse. And they for what they were, the “gentle girls and boys.”

===Chinese===
乔叟（Chaucer）玩过，他爱每一样东西，爱它的本然。剑之为剑，或者马之为马。他们也因本然而受爱，那些“温柔的女孩和男孩”。

===Original===
Good thinking, good eating. All things taking care of themselves. Each thing *accordynge to its kynde*.

===Chinese===
好好地想，好好地吃。万事各安其位。每样东西都*各从其类*（*accordynge to its kynde*）。

===Original===
Would there be a train for Carston to go away by? Good idea of Picus to say it would be better for him to be slept with than visit that wood.

===Chinese===
会有让卡斯顿走的那班火车吗？皮库斯那个主意出得好——说与其让他闯进那片树林，不如陪他睡。

===Original===
Was there anything to eat for dinner, anyhow? She jumped up and went out discreetly through the kitchen. In the scullery there was Felix, cleaning a basket of fish. Too much fish. Enough for half to go bad, and the rest infuriate Picus, who would say he had been given it to encourage his brain. Felix said:

===Chinese===
不管怎么说，晚饭可有东西吃？她一跃而起，蹑手蹑脚从厨房那头出去。洗碗间里是菲利克斯，正收拾一篮鱼。鱼太多了。多到足够放坏一半，再让另一半激怒皮库斯——他会说，给他这么多鱼，是给他补脑子用的。菲利克斯说：

===Original===
“I thought I might as well help. Nanna and Janet are at it all day. Nothing like getting in a stock. Had a good walk with Picus?”

===Chinese===
“我想我不妨搭把手。娜娜（Nanna）和珍妮特（Janet）整天都在忙这个。什么都比不上先囤下一批存货。跟皮库斯散步散得痛快吧？”

===Original===
The basket, full offish-shapes, was wet, black-ribboned inside, a shell sticking here and there, a live whelk walking up. Sea-smelling, almost living food, still running with the live sea. She took a knife and a fish, and cut down on the slab of dark blue slate used for cooling butter. Felix had covered it with scales and blood.

===Chinese===
那只篮子盛满了鱼的形体，湿漉漉的，内壁一道道黑缎带似的纹路，处处粘着贝壳，一只活蛾螺正往上爬。带着海腥的、几乎是活的食物，还流淌着活的海。她取过一把刀、一条鱼，在那块平日镇黄油的深蓝板岩案板上落刀剖开。菲利克斯已经在上面铺满了鳞片和血。

===Original===
“We’ll do it together,” she said.

===Chinese===
“咱们一起弄。”她说。

===Original===
“You’re not very good at it,” he said.

===Chinese===
“你干这个可不大在行。”他说。

===Original===
She hesitated, testing the contact with him.

===Chinese===
她迟疑了一下，试探着同他的这份接触。

===Original===
“We’ll do other things together, then.”

===Chinese===
“那就一起干点别的。”

===Original===
“Don’t we pretty well always?”﻿—His knife scraped down on a bone﻿—“I mean, it’s only half the time I don’t understand.”

===Chinese===
“咱们不差不多一直这样吗？”——他的刀顺着一根骨头刮下去——“我是说，我有一半时间闹不懂罢了。”

===Original===
She thought: So he went and gutted fish for me. She said: “What, my dearest dear, did you understand today?” He answered: “When you came in with Picus I saw your beauty. After Carston had been talking, and surprised me, rather. After the things which have happened lately. It was a kind of answer. A sudden opposite to what I was thinking. To what the world is usually, I suppose. You see, I would sooner have you or even Picus in the right. Only, I haven’t faith.”

===Chinese===
她想：原来他是为我跑去开膛剖肚收拾鱼来了。她说：“那么，我最亲最爱的，你今天懂了什么？”他答道：“你跟皮库斯一块儿进来的时候，我看见了你的美。是在卡斯顿说完那些话之后——说实话，他让我吃了一惊。是在近来发生的那些事之后。那算是一种回答。同我心里正想着的猛然相反。我想，也同这世界平日的样子猛然相反。你要知道，我宁愿是你对，甚至宁愿是皮库斯对。只是，我没有信心。”

===Original===
She thought: Try and have faith. No. Don’t try and have anything. Be with me.

===Chinese===
她想：试着去有信心吧。不。别试着去有无论什么。陪着我。

===Original===
And in answer, she told him about the wood. The bird, Picus. *O lady, be good.* Everything. That she could not have told the others. She heard a thought in his head: I shan’t be able to keep this up, but today I am my sister’s.

===Chinese===
作为回答，她把那片树林讲给他听。那只鸟，皮库斯。*哦，女士，待我好些。*一切。那些她没法讲给别人听的东西。她听见他脑子里的一个念头：这我撑不长久的，可今天，我是属于我妹妹的。

===Original===
More love for her now, handed back through Carston’s spite; peace in the scullery with her flesh and blood. Fish blood and flesh on a stone between them. In one day, two kinds of perfect love. Life with Picus. Life with him. (He had understood love for Picus. Picus would not understand love for him.) Life without Picus? Life without him? She remade Antigone’s discovery that you can have more lovers and more children. Not another brother, once your people’s bearing days are gone.

===Chinese===
此刻给她的爱更多了，经卡斯顿的怨毒转手递了回来；洗碗间里，同她的骨肉至亲享着安宁。鱼的血和肉在一块石头上，横在他们中间。一天之内，两种圆满的爱。与皮库斯的生活。与他的生活。（他懂得了那份对皮库斯的爱。皮库斯却不会懂得那份对他的爱。）没有皮库斯的生活？没有他的生活？她重新发现了安提戈涅（Antigone）的发现：恋人可以更多，孩子也可以更多。兄弟却不能再有——一旦你的族人能生育的岁月一去不返。

===Original===
Life with the two gone. Life with Clarence, Carston, Ross? She thought she heard a voice saying: “You will soon be left alone with them. You will be without Felix. Because there is coming to you the opposite of what you’ve had. Must come to you. More than separation; avoidance, treachery. Equal to what you’ve had. At one point, life without them will mean that.”

===Chinese===
两个人都离去之后的生活。同克拉伦斯、卡斯顿、罗斯（Ross）一起的生活？她仿佛听见一个声音说：“你很快就要被单独留下，同他们在一起。你将失去菲利克斯。因为正在向你走来的，是你所得一切的反面。必得向你走来。比分离更甚：是躲避开，是背弃。与你所得的一切相等。到某一点上，没有他们的生活，指的就是那个。”

===Original===
“Not if I can help it!”

===Chinese===
“只要我拦得住，就不会！”

===Original===
Behind this somewhere was an immense discovery, a huge principle which made it immaterial if she could help it or not. She rested in the knowledge that it was there. Their nurse came in, and they thought at once of washing and going away to change. Mounting the stairs, their arms round each other’s shoulders, Carston saw them from his room, and was inexpressibly shocked, unable to understand how Scylla had persuaded her brother that her relations with Picus had been misunderstood.

===Chinese===
这话背后某个地方，有一个巨大的发现，一条庞大的原理，她拦得住拦不住，在它面前都成了无关紧要。她安然栖止于「它就在那里」的认知里。他们的老保姆进来了，两人立刻想到洗漱，各自回房更衣。上楼时两人互相搂着肩，卡斯顿从自己房里望见，受到难以言喻的震动，怎么也想不明白：斯库拉是怎么让她哥哥相信，她与皮库斯的关系只是一场误会。
