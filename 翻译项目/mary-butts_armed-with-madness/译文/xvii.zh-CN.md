## XVII / 十七、幻影游荡

===Original===
Clarence figured it out. Picus had done this to get away from him, falsifying the devotion of years, flaunt a pretty cousin, marry a pretty cousin: because she had some money: because she was a bird and bee woman: because Carston was after her, must be after her, or he would not have come back from Starn. Or why should any man run back after such an exit, to help a woman, a mime, a baggage, a bag of excrement? There is a great difference between a sportsman, a painter, a man that feels the earth, between Ross and Scylla and his terrible green Sdi creature, and Clarence’s feeling for decoration best served by cities, a blasted heath no more than a site for his palace. He was on Carston and Felix’s side, never satisfied with the earth sacrifice the others munched, wanting *fausse route* with his friend, because his education was insufficient for his abilities. Not for the first time he did not try to correct himself, thought about his wounds and his wishes until they took phantom shape and he slipped off uneasily with his gun down the wood. Scylla made a face after him. Ross shook his head. Carston had an impulse to follow.

===Chinese===
克拉伦斯想明白了。皮库斯干出这桩事，就是为了摆脱他：把多年的深情篡改成谎言，炫示一个漂亮的表妹，娶一个漂亮的表妹：因为她有几个钱：因为她是个鸟儿蜜蜂一样的女人：因为卡斯顿在追她，必定在追她，不然他不会从斯塔恩回来。要不然，一个男人那样拂袖而去之后，为什么还要跑回来，去帮一个女人、一个做戏的、一件贱货、一袋粪土？一个运动家、一个画家、一个摸得到大地的人——罗斯、斯库拉，和他那条可怕的绿色斯迪（Sdi）怪物——跟克拉伦斯的趣味之间，隔着天大的差别：他的趣味在装饰，而装饰最好由城市来伺候；一片遭天谴的荒原，在他不过是替他的宫殿挑好的一块宅基。他站在卡斯顿和菲利克斯一边，从不满足于旁人嚼得起劲的那种大地祭品，一心想同他那位朋友去走*歧路*（*fausse route*），因为他的教养配不上他的才能。他没有设法纠正自己——这也不是头一回了——只管反刍自己的创伤和欲念，直到它们长出幻影的形状；随后他挟着枪，浑身不自在地溜下树林去了。斯库拉冲他的背影做了个鬼脸。罗斯摇了摇头。卡斯顿一时冲动，想跟上去。

===Original===
“Gone off to invent excuses for Picus,” said Felix, “for you to listen to﻿—It’s the occupation down here.”

===Chinese===
“跑掉去给皮库斯编借口了，”菲利克斯说，“好留给你们听——在这地方，这就是正经营生。”

===Original===
“Come and pose,” said Ross. “I want a model.”

===Chinese===
“过来摆姿势吧，”罗斯说。“我缺个模特。”

===Original===
Carston was alone with Scylla. He said:

===Chinese===
卡斯顿单独同斯库拉待在了一处。他说：

===Original===
“I think I’ve an excuse now to say ‘explain a bit.’ ” It was parching hot, gritty as if a storm of microscopic dust had filled up the holes in the leaves, in the grass-blades, in the skin.

===Chinese===
“我想这会儿我总算有借口说一句‘解释一下吧’了。”天气燥热灼人，满空气的砂砾，仿佛一场微尘的风暴填满了叶子里的孔、草叶里的孔、皮肤里的孔。

===Original===
“Reassure me, at least,” he said, “that this would have happened without me.”

===Chinese===
“至少宽宽我的心，”他说，“告诉我就算没有我，这一切也照样会发生。”

===Original===
“Of course. Much worse if it hadn’t been for you.” They stopped talking.

===Chinese===
“当然。要不是有你，还会糟得多。”两人住了口。

===Original===
“I tried to help you,” he said, “it is your turn.” Saw the effort she made, thought how easy these people were to spur.

===Chinese===
“我出过力要帮你，”他说，“这回轮到你了。”他瞧见她费了多大的劲，心想这些人多么一策就动。

===Original===
“Let’s go up and tackle Picus,” she said﻿—“there is one thing about staying in bed, it runs to earth.”

===Chinese===
“咱们上楼去会一会皮库斯，”她说——“赖在床上有一桩好处：等于自己入了穴，一找一个准。”

===Original===
“No,” said Carston, “you must excuse me. I’ve had about enough of that chap.”

===Chinese===
“不，”卡斯顿说，“恕我失陪。那位仁兄，我受得差不多了。”

===Original===
“So,” said Scylla, “for the moment, have I.”

===Chinese===
“说的是，”斯库拉道，“眼下我也受够了。”

===Original===
“Seems to me he played a mean trick on you all﻿—What I don’t see is why. Or why it should have got you.”

===Chinese===
“依我看，他是对你们大伙使了个损招——我不明白的是图什么。也不明白这事怎么偏偏就伤着了你。”

===Original===
The other side of the house Ross was seeing Felix flung in a chair, hearing the nervous sobbing his own cool voice could not control. Nor could he control in himself his aversion to speak or to help.

===Chinese===
在房子另一头，罗斯看着菲利克斯瘫进椅子里，听着那阵神经质的抽泣——自己冷冷的嗓音管束不住它。他心里那份嫌恶，自己也管束不住：既不想开口，也不想伸手。

===Original===
“What in hell do we come here for? I told Scylla to sell those shares and we’d have been at Biarritz.”

===Chinese===
“我们到底下这儿来干什么？我早叫斯库拉把那些股票卖掉，这会儿我们人已经在比亚里茨（Biarritz）了。”

===Original===
“It would have been the same at Biarritz.”

===Chinese===
“到了比亚里茨，还不是一样。”

===Original===
“You might be. I should be different there. You’re looking for something. I’m not. And I hope when you get it you’ll like it. Looking for the Sanc-Grail. It’s always the same story. The Golden Fleece or the philosopher’s stone, or perpetual motion, or Atlantis or the lost tribes or God. All ways of walking into the same trap. And Scylla gets into bed with old Tracy’s son.”

===Chinese===
“你也许一样。我在那儿就会是另一个人。你在找什么东西。我不找。等你到手了，我还盼你能喜欢它呢。找什么圣所圣杯。永远是同一套老故事。金羊毛啦，贤者之石啦，永动机啦，亚特兰蒂斯啦，失落的支派啦，要不就是上帝。条条路数，通的是同一口陷阱。而斯库拉呢，跟老特雷西的儿子上了床。”

===Original===
“That is not the point. What is it about a trap?”

===Chinese===
“那不是要紧的。陷阱又怎么说？”

===Original===
The boy got up and looked a little madly and very insolently at Ross, the blue eyes cold between lids red with weeping. Ross was surprised to find himself edging away, like a man who is to be shot at.

===Chinese===
那少年站起身来，有点发狂、又极其无礼地瞪着罗斯，哭红了的眼睑中间，一双蓝眼睛冷冰冰的。罗斯惊讶地发现自己正一点点往旁边蹭，像个等着挨枪子的人。

===Original===
“We’re through with the baby-brother business.”

===Chinese===
“当小弟弟的营生，我们收摊了。”

===Original===
Upstairs, Picus had finished shaving, his body worked on as delicately and scrupulously as a cat. Whistling to himself while Felix was sobbing, whistling back his power as their idol, like a god summoning an element or in confidence like prayer. He set his tie for the last time, shook himself, laid himself down on the window seat, and drew a ring with a pearl on to his atrociously powerful hand.

===Chinese===
楼上，皮库斯刮完了脸，一身皮肉侍弄得像猫一样精细讲究。菲利克斯在底下抽泣，他自顾自吹着口哨，把身为众人偶像的威能一声声吹回来，像一位神明召唤一种元素，又像祷告那样私下交心。他末了又扶正一回领带，抖擞了一下，往窗座上一躺，将一枚带珍珠的戒指套上那只力道骇人的手。

===Original===
And Clarence out on the high turf was not looking at the sea or the terrible crest of Gault suspended in the haze. Or at the small enamel floor he trod on, flower and leaf stars and bars and rings and crosses: or at a dozen rabbits hurrying: or at one hawk not hurrying, until he dropped faster than the eye and there would be one rabbit the less. He walked slowly, inside himself, petting his phantoms, especially a phantom of Picus, the body up at the house was behaving more and more unlike. He wondered also why Scylla had called him “medievalist,” because she said he assumed a form from inside and made things fit it, instead of compelling what is to do his construction for him. Hadn’t Picus invented a lying fancy to please her, to get off with her? Lying and lecherous his bird was, for a woman who had snapped him up for her body’s sake and her vanity. This went on until he saw the names he called her take body and walk to meet him out of the wood. Vanity, lechery, falsehood, and malice lolled along together across the grass, out of the trees. And because she called him medievalist, he saw them in archaic dress.

===Chinese===
而克拉伦斯在外头高处的草皮上，既没有看海，也没有看雾霭里悬着的高尔特崖那道可怖的顶脊。也没有看脚下那片珐琅般的小地面，花与叶缀成的星、条、环、十字：没有看十几只匆匆赶路的兔子：也没有看那只不慌不忙的鹰——直到它快过眼珠地一坠，兔子便从此少掉一只。他慢慢走着，人缩在自己心里，抚弄着他的幻影，尤其是皮库斯的一个幻影——楼上屋里那具肉身，行径却越来越不像了。他还想不通斯库拉干吗叫他“中世纪主义者”，因为她说过：他是先从心里取定一个形，再逼万事万物去凑合它，而不是强令实存替他把构造造出来。皮库斯难道不是编了个骗人的瞎话去讨她欢心，好把她弄到手？他那只鸟儿本就又撒谎又好色，图的是一个为了自己那副身子骨和虚荣、把他一把抢走的女人。这样想着想着，直到他看见自己加给她的那些名目生出肉身，从树林里走出来迎他。虚荣、淫欲、谎骗和恶意，懒洋洋结伴荡过草地，钻出树丛。又因为她叫他中世纪主义者，他看它们一身穿的都是古装。

===Original===
Scylla said to Carston on the lawn:

===Chinese===
草坪上，斯库拉对卡斯顿说：

===Original===
“So, you see, what sounded romantic excitement about the Sanc-Grail cup was real. And unfortunate?”

===Chinese===
“所以你瞧，当初关于圣所圣杯那杯子、听着像罗曼蒂克兴头的那些话，是真的。而且不幸，是不是？”

===Original===
Carston wondered, deplored and detested the European faculty for taking the skeleton out of the cupboard. Rattling it, airing it, lecturing on it. She was winding up a discourse without enquiry into his feelings. On what he supposed was the skeleton, the world skeleton. He heard:

===Chinese===
卡斯顿又是诧异、又是悲叹、又是憎恶这门欧洲本领——把柜子里的骷髅拖将出来。摇响它，晾晒它，拿它开讲。她正给一篇宏论收尾，全不过问他的感受。论题——据他猜想——正是那具骷髅，世界的那具骷髅。他听见：

===Original===
“If the materialist’s universe is true, not a working truth to make bridges with and things, we are a set of blind factors in a machine. And no passion has any validity and no imagination. They are just little tricks of the machine. It either is so, or it isn’t. If you hold that it isn’t, you corrupt your intellect by denying certain facts. If you stick to the facts as we have them, life is a horror and an insult. Nothing has any worth, but to tickle our sensations and oil the machine. There is no value in our passions and perceptions, or final differences between a life full of design and adventure and a life crawled out in a palace or a slum. The life of Plato or Buddha, apart from the kick of the illusion, was as futile as the lives of the daughters of Louis XV. Old talk, you say, and remember *In Memoriam*. But notice what is happening now people have become used to the idea. Any little boy in a Paris bar, who never heard of physics knows. Everyone gets the age’s temper. With results on their conduct﻿—‘Why be good any more’ they say, and the youngest ones not that. And it’s not intellectual beauty the culture-camp admire. It’s themselves for having such fine subconsciousnesses. Such an elegant sublimation of their infant interests. Watch the world with the skeleton acclimatised! Even when I was new we tried the bad to see if it might not be good. But the new lot aren’t interested. Don’t give a button for the good any more.

===Chinese===
“如果唯物主义者的宇宙是真的——不是拿来造桥派用场的那种好用之真——那我们便是一台机器里的一套瞎眼因子。一切激情皆无有效性，一切想象亦然。它们不过是机器的几手小把戏。要么如此，要么不然。你若咬定不然，便是否认某些事实，荼毒了自己的智力。你若坚守我们手头的这些事实，人生便是一场恐怖、一桩侮辱。万事皆无价值，除了撩拨我们的感官、给机器上油。我们的激情与知觉里没有价值；一座充满构想与冒险的人生，同一场在宫殿或贫民窟里爬完的人生，也没有了终极的差别。柏拉图或佛陀的一生，除去幻觉那一记兴奋，同路易十五的女儿们的一生一样无谓。你会说：老生常谈，还想得起《悼念集》（*In Memoriam*）。可是请注意，如今人们惯用了这个观念之后，正出着什么事。巴黎酒吧里随便哪个没听说过物理的小小子都懂得。人人都沾上时代的脾气。结果落在他们的品行上——他们说：‘干吗还要学好？’顶年轻的一辈，连这句都省了。文化营里的人景仰的又不是智性之美。是他们自己，景仰自己竟有这样出色的下意识。把他们婴儿期的兴致升华得何其雅致。瞧瞧这骷髅已服水土的世界吧！就连我还是新的时候，我们也拿恶试过，看它会不会其实是善。可新的一拨人不感兴趣了。对善，再不肯出一枚纽扣。”

===Original===
“And there is no evading it by any ‘service of humanity’ game. Unless you’re one of the people who get sensual kick out looking after things, why help humanity? Think of Wells’s Utopias. Birth-control, and peace and drains. And nothing left to do but report on the fauna of a further star. Our visiting-list extended to super-birds, or intellectually developed spiders. *Nothing* but physical adventure. Especially as we’ve picked up one priceless truth off the road, that every action brings with it its toxin and its antitoxin. If, instead of becoming cynical or scared, we started enquiry again from that﻿—”

===Chinese===
“而且，靠什么‘服务人类’的把戏也绕不开它。除非你是那号照管起东西来能照管出肉体快感的人，凭什么去帮人类？想想威尔斯（Wells）笔下的那些乌托邦吧。节育，和平，下水道。往后便没有事可做，只剩给更远一颗星球的动物志写报告。把拜客名单拉长，添上超级鸟类，或者智力发达的蜘蛛。*无非*是肉身的冒险罢了。何况我们还在路面上白捡了一条无价的真理：每一个行动，都随身带着它的毒素和它的抗毒素。倘若我们不去变愤世嫉俗、不去变惶恐，反倒从这一点上重新发问——”

===Original===
Felix shouted melodiously:

===Chinese===
菲利克斯朗声喊道，倒带着曲调：

===Original===

===Chinese===
“我是战后人。我这才刚从你们手里脱开身。我承认你们吓唬得住我，可实际上你们叫我厌烦。我什么都不在乎了。我兴许是一团抑制，可我从此只为自己——”

===Original===
Through the grilling haze, Luxury, Malice, and Untruth strolled over the grass to Clarence. In Ross’s heart there twisted ache and dislike. Carston gave in to spiritual upset, while his body lay in a garden chair. Scylla ended:

===Chinese===
烤人的雾霭里，奢靡、恶意与虚谎荡过草地，朝克拉伦斯去了。罗斯心里绞着疼与厌。卡斯顿听任心境翻腾，身子却摊在花园椅上。斯库拉收了尾：

===Original===
“So even the memory of a great magic turned out to be a bird’s joke.”

===Chinese===

===Original===
Picus thought how he would appear downstairs and bewilder them again. Had enough of Scylla. Wished now he hadn’t given her that cup. Caught out he’d been by the old man, but that wasn’t over yet. Get right out and come in by another door. Make Clarence, Scylla, and the lot of ’em quite happy always. Play round his way and their way forever. *And I’ll give you leave to play till doomsday.* Not mother. Too late to do that. Sort of the old man’s prisoner. ===Chinese===

===Original===
Scylla saw that Carston had had enough, and felt stifled and alone. Clarence returned in agony up the doubling wood-path, not the straight. Ross withdrew into his picture, and Carston hung on tight to a thought: We’ve all got to get out of here for a bit.
