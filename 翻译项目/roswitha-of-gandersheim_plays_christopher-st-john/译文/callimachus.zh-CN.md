## Callimachus / 卡利马科斯

### Argument / 剧情梗概

===Original===
The resurrection of Drusiana and Callimachus.
Callimachus cherishes a guilty passion for Drusiana, not only while she is alive but after she has died in the Lord. He dies from the bite of a serpent, but, thanks to the prayers of Saint John the Apostle, he is restored to life, together with Drusiana, and is born again in Christ.

===Chinese===
德鲁西娅娜（Drusiana）与卡利马科斯（Callimachus）的复活。
卡利马科斯对德鲁西娅娜怀着有罪的情欲，不仅在她生前，甚至在她安逝于主之后。他死于毒蛇之咬，但多亏使徒圣约翰的祈祷，他与德鲁西娅娜一同复生，在基督里重获新生。

### Callimachus / 卡利马科斯

#### Scene I / 第一场

===Original===
Callimachus
My friends, a word with you.

Friends
We are at your service as long as you please.

Callimachus
I should prefer to speak with you apart from the crowd.

Friends
What pleases you, pleases us.

Callimachus
Then we will go to some quieter place where no one will interrupt us.

Friends
Just as you like.

===Chinese===
卡利马科斯
朋友们，跟你们说句话。

朋友们
但凭吩咐，我们随时候命。

卡利马科斯
我想避开人群，同你们单独谈。

朋友们
你乐意的，我们也乐意。

卡利马科斯
那我们去个清静些的地方，没人打扰。

朋友们
悉听尊便。

#### Scene II / 第二场

===Original===
Callimachus
For a long time now I have been in great trouble. I hope that by confiding in you I shall find relief.

Friends
When a man tells his friends of his sufferings it is only fair that they should try to share them.

Callimachus
I would to heaven that you could lighten this load upon my heart!

Friends
Well, tell us precisely what is wrong. We will grieve with you, if we must. If not, we can do our best to distract your mind.

Callimachus
I love—

Friends
What do you love?

Callimachus
A thing of beauty, a thing of grace!

===Chinese===
卡利马科斯
许久以来，我深陷苦恼。希望向你们倾吐之后，我能得到解脱。

朋友们
一个人向朋友诉说苦楚，朋友理当设法分担。

卡利马科斯
但愿你们真能卸下我心头的重负！

朋友们
那就把实情原原本本告诉我们。若是难免，我们与你同悲；若还来得及，我们尽力为你解忧。

卡利马科斯
我爱——

朋友们
你爱什么？

卡利马科斯
一件美的东西，一件优雅的东西！

===Original===
Friends
That is too vague! How can we tell from this what is the object of your love?

Callimachus
Woman.

Friends
Ah, now you say “woman” we all understand!

Callimachus
By woman, I mean a woman.

Friends
Clearer still! But it is impossible to give an opinion on a subject until the subject is defined. So name the woman.

Callimachus
Drusiana.

===Chinese===
朋友们
这太含糊了！这样我们怎么知道你爱的对象是什么？

卡利马科斯
女人。

朋友们
啊，你一说“女人”，我们全明白了！

卡利马科斯
我说女人，意思是某个女人。

朋友们
更清楚了！可事情不点明，就没法发表意见。说出她的名字吧。

卡利马科斯
德鲁西娅娜。

===Original===
Friends
What? The wife of Prince Andronicus?

Callimachus
Yes.

Friends
Nothing can come of that. She has been baptized.

Callimachus
What do I care, if I can win her love?

Friends
You cannot.

Callimachus
What makes you say so?

Friends
You are crying for the moon.

===Chinese===
朋友们
什么？亲王安德罗尼库斯的妻子？

卡利马科斯
是。

朋友们
那不会有结果的。她已受洗。

卡利马科斯
只要能赢得她的爱，我管那些做什么？

朋友们
你赢不了。

卡利马科斯
何出此言？

朋友们
你是在痴人说梦。

===Original===
Callimachus
Am I the first to do so? Have I not the example of many others to encourage me?

Friends
Now listen. This woman you sigh for is a follower of the holy Apostle John, and has devoted herself entirely to God. They say she will not even go to the bed of Andronicus although he is a devout Christian. Is it likely that she will listen to you?

Callimachus
I came to you for consolation, and instead you drive me to despair!

Friends
We should be poor friends if we consoled and flattered you at the expense of the truth.

===Chinese===
卡利马科斯
难道我是头一个？不是有许多先例给我打气吗？

朋友们
听着。你为之叹息的这个女人，是圣使徒约翰的追随者，已把自己全然献给上帝。据说连安德罗尼库斯的床她都不肯上，尽管他是虔诚的基督徒。她可能听你的吗？

卡利马科斯
我来求安慰，你们却把我往绝望里逼！

朋友们
若为了安慰奉承你而牺牲真相，那我们就不配为友了。

===Original===
Callimachus
Since you refuse to advise me, I will go to her and pour out my soul in words that would melt a heart of stone!

Friends
Fool! It is hopeless!

Callimachus
I defy the stars!

Friends
We shall see.

===Chinese===
卡利马科斯
既然你们不肯为我出主意，我就去找她，用能熔化顽石的话语倾吐我的灵魂！

朋友们
蠢货！没指望的！

卡利马科斯
我偏要逆着星命干！

朋友们
走着瞧。

#### Scene III / 第三场

===Original===
Callimachus
Drusiana, listen to me! Drusiana, my deepest heart’s love!

Drusiana
Your words amaze me, Callimachus. What can you want of me?

Callimachus
You are amazed?

Drusiana
I am astounded.

Callimachus
First I want to speak of love!

Drusiana
Love! What love?

===Chinese===
卡利马科斯
德鲁西娅娜，听我说！德鲁西娅娜，我心底最深的爱！

德鲁西娅娜
你的话让我惊愕，卡利马科斯。你能要我什么呢？

卡利马科斯
你惊愕？

德鲁西娅娜
我骇然。

卡利马科斯
我要先谈谈爱！

德鲁西娅娜
爱！什么爱？

===Original===
Callimachus
That love with which I love you above all created things.

Drusiana
Why should you love me? You are not of my kin. There is no legal bond between us.

Callimachus
It is your beauty.

Drusiana
My beauty?

Callimachus
Yes.

Drusiana
What is my beauty to you?

===Chinese===
卡利马科斯
我以此爱爱你，胜过爱一切受造之物。

德鲁西娅娜
你为什么爱我？你非我亲族，我们之间没有法定姻缘。

卡利马科斯
是你的美貌。

德鲁西娅娜
我的美貌？

卡利马科斯
是。

德鲁西娅娜
我的美貌与你何干？

===Original===
Callimachus
But little now—it is that which tortures me—but I hope that it may be much before long.

Drusiana
Not a word more. Leave me at once, for it is a sin to listen to you now that I understand your devilish meaning.

Callimachus
My Drusiana, do not kill me with your looks. Do not drive away one who worships you, but give back love for love.

Drusiana
Wicked, insidious words! They fall on deaf ears. Your love disgusts me. Understand I despise you!

Callimachus
You cannot make me angry, because I know that you would own my passion moves you if you were not ashamed.

===Chinese===
卡利马科斯
眼下还只是些许——正是它折磨着我——但我希望不久它便会增多。

德鲁西娅娜
不许再说一个字。立刻离开我，如今我明白了你魔鬼般的用意，再听下去便是罪。

卡利马科斯
我的德鲁西娅娜，别用你的眼色杀了我。别赶走一个崇拜你的人，请以爱还爱。

德鲁西娅娜
邪恶、阴险的话！它们落进聋子的耳朵。你的爱令我憎恶。明白告诉你，我鄙夷你！

卡利马科斯
你激不起我的怒气，因为我知道，若不是羞怯作祟，你会承认我的激情打动了你。

===Original===
Drusiana
It moves me to indignation, nothing else.

Callimachus
That feeling will not last.

Drusiana
I shall not change, be sure of that.

Callimachus
I would not be too sure.

Drusiana
You frantic, foolish man! Do not deceive yourself! Why delude yourself with vain hopes? What madness leads you to think that I shall yield? I have renounced even what is lawful—my husband’s bed!

Callimachus
I call heaven and earth to witness that if you do not yield I will never rest from the fight for you. I will be as cunning as the serpent. I will use all my skill and strength to trap you.

===Chinese===
德鲁西娅娜
它打动我的是义愤，仅此而已。

卡利马科斯
那种感觉长不了。

德鲁西娅娜
我不会变，你放明白些。

卡利马科斯
我看未必。

德鲁西娅娜
你这狂乱愚蠢的人！别自欺了！何必用虚妄的希望迷惑自己？什么疯狂让你以为我会屈服？连合法的东西——我丈夫的床——我都弃绝了！

卡利马科斯
我请天地作证：你若不屈服，我为你而战绝不停歇。我要像蛇一样狡猾，用尽全部技巧与力量来诱捕你。

#### Scene IV / 第四场

===Original===
Drusiana
O Lord Jesus, what use is my vow of chastity? My beauty has all the same made this man love me. Pity my fears, O Lord. Pity the grief which has seized me. I know not what to do. If I tell anyone what has happened, there will be disorder in the city on my account; if I keep silence, only Thy grace can protect me from falling into the net spread for me. O Christ, take me to Thyself. Let me die swiftly. Save me from being the ruin of a soul!

Andronicus
Drusiana, Drusiana! Christ, what blow has fallen on me! Drusiana is dead. Run one of you and fetch the holy man John.

===Chinese===
德鲁西娅娜
主耶稣啊，我的守贞之誓有什么用？我的美貌终究还是让这个男人爱上了我。主啊，怜悯我的恐惧，怜悯攫住我的忧苦。我不知所措。若把我遇到的事告诉旁人，城里将因我而起骚乱；若我保持沉默，唯有你的恩典能护佑我不落入为我张开的网。基督啊，接我到你那里去吧。让我速死。救我免于毁灭一个灵魂！

安德罗尼库斯
德鲁西娅娜，德鲁西娅娜！基督啊，何等打击落在我身上！德鲁西娅娜死了。你们谁快跑去把圣者约翰请来。

#### Scene V / 第五场

===Original===
John
Why do you weep, my son?

Andronicus
Oh, horrible! O Lord, that life should suddenly become so hateful!

John
What troubles you?

Andronicus
Drusiana, your disciple, Drusiana—

John
She has passed from the sight of men?

Andronicus
Yes. And I am desolate.

John
It is not right to mourn so bitterly for those whose souls we know rejoice in peace.

===Chinese===
约翰
我儿，你为何哭泣？

安德罗尼库斯
啊，可怕！主啊，生命竟突然变得如此可憎！

约翰
什么事困扰你？

安德罗尼库斯
德鲁西娅娜，你的门徒，德鲁西娅娜——

约翰
她已从人眼前逝去了？

安德罗尼库斯
是。我孤苦无依了。

约翰
明知逝者的灵魂正安享平安，如此恸哭是不应当的。

===Original===
Andronicus
God knows I do not doubt that her soul is in eternal joy, and that her incorrupt body will rise again. What grieves me so sorely is that in my presence just now she prayed for death. She begged she might die.

John
You know her reason?

Andronicus
I know it, and will tell you when I am less sick with grief.

John
Come. We must celebrate the funeral rites with proper ceremony.

Andronicus
There is a marble tomb near here in which the body shall be laid, and our steward Fortunatus shall guard her grave.

John
It is right that she should be interred with honour. God rest her soul in peace.

===Chinese===
安德罗尼库斯
上帝明鉴，我不怀疑她的灵魂在永恒喜乐之中，她那不朽的身体必将复活。令我如此锥心的，是方才她当着我的面祈求死亡。她恳求让她死去。

约翰
你知道缘故吗？

安德罗尼库斯
我知道，等我悲痛稍缓再告诉你。

约翰
来吧。我们须以应有的仪轨举行葬礼。

安德罗尼库斯
附近有一座大理石墓，可将遗体安放其中，我们的管家福尔图纳图斯会看守她的坟墓。

约翰
她理应体面下葬。愿上帝使她的灵魂安息。

#### Scene VI / 第六场

===Original===
Callimachus
Fortunatus, Fortunatus, what is to become of me? Death itself cannot quench my love for Drusiana!

Fortunatus
Poor wretch!

Callimachus
I shall die if you do not help me.

Fortunatus
How can I help you?

===Chinese===
卡利马科斯
福尔图纳图斯，福尔图纳图斯，我会落得什么下场？连死亡也无法熄灭我对德鲁西娅娜的爱！

福尔图纳图斯
可怜虫！

卡利马科斯
你不帮我，我就活不成了。

福尔图纳图斯
我怎么帮你？

===Original===
Callimachus
In this. You can let me look on her, dead.

Fortunatus
Up to now the body is sound and whole, I reckon because it was not wasted with disease. As you know she was taken in a moment by a fever.

Callimachus
Oh, how happy I should be if I might see for myself.

Fortunatus
If you are willing to pay me well, you can do what you like.

===Chinese===
卡利马科斯
就这样：你让我看她一眼，死了的她。

福尔图纳图斯
到现在遗体还完好无损，我想是因为没有疾病的损耗。你知道，她是一场热病瞬间夺去的。

卡利马科斯
啊，若能亲眼见到，我该多么幸福。

福尔图纳图斯
只要你肯出大价钱，想干什么都行。

===Original===
Callimachus
Here, take all I have with me, and be sure that I will give you more, much more, later.

Fortunatus
Quick, then! We’ll go to the tomb.

Callimachus
You cannot go quickly enough for me.

===Chinese===
卡利马科斯
拿着，我身上带的都给你，放心，日后我还会给你更多，多得多。

福尔图纳图斯
那就快点！我们去墓室。

卡利马科斯
再快也不嫌快。

#### Scene VII / 第七场

===Original===
Fortunatus
There lies the body. The face is not like the face of a corpse. The limbs show no sign of decay. You can take her to your heart.

Callimachus
O Drusiana, Drusiana, I worshipped you with my whole soul! I yearned from my very bowels to embrace you! And you repulsed me, and thwarted my desire. Now you are in my power, now I can wound you with my kisses, and pour out my love on you.

Fortunatus
Take care! A monstrous serpent! It is coming towards us!

Callimachus
A curse on me! And on you, Fortunatus, who led me on and urged me to this infamy. Wretch, may you die from the serpent’s bite! Terror and remorse are killing me.

===Chinese===
福尔图纳图斯
遗体在那儿躺着。那脸不像死人的脸，四肢没有腐烂的迹象。你可以把她搂进怀里。

卡利马科斯
啊，德鲁西娅娜，德鲁西娅娜，我以整个灵魂崇拜你！我五内如焚地渴望拥抱你！你却拒绝我，挫败我的欲望。如今你在我掌中，如今我可以用亲吻刺伤你，把我的爱倾倒在你身上。

福尔图纳图斯
小心！一条巨蛇！它朝我们来了！

卡利马科斯
我遭了天谴！也咒你，福尔图纳图斯，是你引诱我、怂恿我干这桩丑事。恶棍，愿你死于蛇咬！恐惧和悔恨正在杀死我。

John

Andronicus

John

Andronicus
*To the Spectators.* Tremble.8

安德罗尼库斯
不忘怀信赖您的人，正见您的圣德。

约翰
看哪！那不可见的上帝向我们显现，取了一位俊美青年的可见形貌。

安德罗尼库斯
（对观众）战栗吧。8

John

God
To raise Drusiana from the dead, and with her him who lies outside her tomb, have I come, that in them My Name may be glorified.

Andronicus
How swiftly He was caught up again into heaven!

John

Andronicus
Let us go on to the tomb. It may be that there what is now obscure will become clear.

===Chinese===

主耶稣，你为何屈尊在此地向你的仆人显现？

上帝
我来，是要使德鲁西娅娜从死里复活，并使躺在她墓外的那人一同复活，好让我的名在他们身上得荣耀。

安德罗尼库斯

约翰

安德罗尼库斯

#### Scene IX / 第九场

===Original===
John
In Christ’s name, what miracle is this? The sepulchre is open, and Drusiana’s body has been cast forth. And near it lie two other corpses enlaced in a serpent’s coils.

Andronicus
I begin to understand. This is Callimachus, who while he lived was consumed with an unholy passion for Drusiana. It troubled her greatly and her distress brought on a fever. She prayed that she might die.

John
Such was her love of chastity.

Andronicus
After her death the wretched man, crazed with love, and stung by the defeat of his wicked plan, was still more inflamed by desire.

John
Pitiable creature!

Andronicus
I have no doubt that he bribed this unworthy servant to give him the opportunity for committing a detestable crime.

===Chinese===
约翰
凭基督之名，这是何等奇迹？墓穴敞开着，德鲁西娅娜的遗体被抛在外头。近旁还躺着两具尸体，缠在蛇的盘绕之中。

安德罗尼库斯
我开始明白了。这是卡利马科斯，生前对德鲁西娅娜怀着不圣洁的情欲，备受煎熬。这令她极为不安，忧苦引发了热病，她才祈求一死。

约翰
她对贞洁的爱竟至于此。

安德罗尼库斯
她死后，这可怜人爱得发狂，又因邪恶计划受挫而心痛如刺，欲火反而烧得更旺。

约翰
可悲的人！

安德罗尼库斯
我毫不怀疑，他买通了这不配称仆人的仆人，为他制造机会去犯那可憎的罪行。

===Original===
John
It is not to be believed!

Andronicus
But death struck both of them down before the deed was accomplished.

John
They met their deserts.

Andronicus
What astonishes me most is that the Divine Voice should have promised the resurrection of him who planned the crime, and not of him who was only an accomplice. Maybe it is because the one, blinded by the passion of the flesh, knew not what he did, while the other sinned of deliberate malice.

John
With what wonderful exactness the Supreme Judge examines the deeds of men! How even the scales in which He weighs the merits of each individual man! None can understand, none explain. Human wisdom cannot grasp the subtlety of the divine judgment.

Andronicus
So we should be content to marvel at it, as it is not in our power to attain a precise knowledge of the causes of things.

===Chinese===
约翰
令人难以置信！

安德罗尼库斯
但死亡在罪行得逞之前，就把两人双双击倒了。

约翰
他们罪有应得。

安德罗尼库斯
