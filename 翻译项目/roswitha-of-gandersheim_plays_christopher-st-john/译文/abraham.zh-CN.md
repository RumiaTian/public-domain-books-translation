## Abraham / 亚伯拉罕

### Argument / 剧情

===Original===
The fall and repentance of Mary, the niece of the hermit Abraham, who, after she has spent twenty years in the religious life as a solitary, abandons it in despair, and, returning to the world, does not shrink from becoming a harlot. But two years later Abraham, in the disguise of a lover, seeks her out and reclaims her. For twenty years she does penance for her sins with many tears, fastings, vigils, and prayers.

===Chinese===
隐修士亚伯拉罕（Abraham）的侄女玛利亚（Mary）的堕落与悔改：她在独修的修道生活中度过二十年后，因绝望而弃之不顾，重返尘世，不惜沦为娼妓。但两年之后，亚伯拉罕乔装成寻欢客寻访到她，将她挽回。此后二十年，她以无数眼泪、斋戒、守夜与祈祷，为自己的罪做补赎。

### Abraham / 亚伯拉罕

#### Scene I / 第一场

===Original===
Abraham
Brother Ephrem, my dear comrade in the hermit life, may I speak to you now, or shall I wait until you have finished your divine praises?

Ephrem
And what can you have to say to me which is not praise of Him Who said: “Where two or three are gathered together in My Name, I am with them”?

Abraham
I have not come to speak of anything which He would not like to hear.

Ephrem
I am sure of it. So speak at once.

Abraham
It concerns a decision I have to make. I long for your approval.

Ephrem
We have one heart and one soul. We ought to agree.

===Chinese===
亚伯拉罕（Abraham）
厄弗冷（Ephrem）弟兄，我隐修生活中的亲爱同伴，我现在可以同你说话吗，还是该等你颂完神圣的赞歌？

厄弗冷
你所能对我说的，除了赞美那位曾言「哪里有两三个人奉我的名聚集，我就在他们中间」的主之外，还能是什么呢？

亚伯拉罕
我此来要说的话，绝无一句是祂不愿听的。

厄弗冷
我确信如此。那就请即刻说吧。

亚伯拉罕
事关我须作的一个决定。我渴望得到你的赞同。

厄弗冷
我们同心同魂，本该彼此相合。

===Original===
Abraham
I have a little niece of tender years. She has lost both her parents, and my affection for her has been deepened by compassion for her lonely state. I am in constant anxiety on her account.

Ephrem
Ought you who have triumphed over the world to be vexed by its cares!

Abraham
My only care is her radiant beauty! What if it should one day be dimmed by sin.

Ephrem
No one can blame you for being anxious.

Abraham
I hope not.

===Chinese===
亚伯拉罕
我有一个年幼的小侄女。她双亲俱亡，我对她的怜惜之情，又因她孤苦无依而愈发深笃。我为她日夜悬心。

厄弗冷
你既已战胜了世界，岂该再被世界的牵挂所烦扰！

亚伯拉罕
我唯一的牵挂便是她那焕发的美貌！倘有一日它被罪玷污而失色，那可如何是好。

厄弗冷
你为此忧心，无人能责怪你。

亚伯拉罕
但愿如此。

===Original===
Ephrem
How old is she?

Abraham
At the end of this year she will be eight.

Ephrem
She is very young.

Abraham
That does not lessen my anxiety.

Ephrem
Where does she live?

===Chinese===
厄弗冷
她多大了？

亚伯拉罕
到今年年底她就八岁了。

厄弗冷
她还很小。

亚伯拉罕
这并不能减轻我的忧虑。

厄弗冷
她住在哪里？

===Original===
Abraham
At my hermitage now; for at the request of her other kinsfolk I have undertaken to bring her up. The fortune left her ought, I think, to be given to the poor.

Ephrem
A mind taught so early to despise temporal things should be fixed on heaven.

Abraham
I desire with all my heart to see her the spouse of Christ and devoted entirely to His service.

Ephrem
A praiseworthy wish.

Abraham
I was inspired by her name.

===Chinese===
亚伯拉罕
如今住在我的隐修庐；因她其他亲眷之请，我承担了抚养她之责。我想，她名下遗留的财产应当分给穷人。

厄弗冷
一颗这么早便学会轻看尘世之物的心，理当系于上天。

亚伯拉罕
我全心渴望见她成为基督的净配，全然献身于事奉祂。

厄弗冷
可嘉的心愿。

亚伯拉罕
我是受了她的名字的启发。

===Original===
Ephrem
What is she called?

Abraham
Mary.

Ephrem
Mary! Such a name ought to be adorned with the crown of virginity.

Abraham
I have no fear that she will be unwilling, but we must be gentle.

Ephrem
Come, let us go, and impress on her that no life is so sweet and secure as the religious one.

===Chinese===
厄弗冷
她叫什么名字？

亚伯拉罕
玛利亚。

厄弗冷
玛利亚！这样的名字理当戴上童贞的冠冕。

亚伯拉罕
我不怕她不愿意，只是我们须以温柔相待。

厄弗冷
来，我们同去，让她谨记：没有任何生活比修道生活更甘美、更安稳。

#### Scene II / 第二场

===Original===
Abraham
Mary, my child by adoption, whom I love as my own soul! Listen to my advice as to a father’s, and to Brother Ephrem’s as that of a very wise man. Strive to imitate the chastity of the holy Virgin whose name you bear.

Ephrem
Child, would it not be a shame if you, who through the mystery of your name are called to mount to the stars where Mary the mother of God reigns, chose instead the low pleasures of the earth?

Mary
I know nothing about the mystery of my name, so how can I tell what you mean?

Ephrem
Mary, my child, means “star of the sea”—that star which rules the world and all the peoples in the world.

Mary
Why is it called the star of the sea?

===Chinese===
亚伯拉罕
玛利亚（Mary），我收养的孩子，我爱你就如爱自己的灵魂！请听我的劝告，如同听父亲的；也请听厄弗冷弟兄的劝告，如同听一位极有智慧之人的。要努力效法你所承其名的神圣童贞女的纯洁。

厄弗冷
孩子，你既借你名字的奥秘蒙召，升往群星——上帝之母玛利亚在那里为王——你若转而拣选尘世低贱的逸乐，岂不是奇耻大辱？

玛利亚
我对我名字的奥秘一无所知，又怎能明白您的意思呢？

厄弗冷
玛利亚，我的孩子，意即“海上之星”——那是统辖世界与天下万民的一颗星。

玛利亚
为什么称它为海上之星？

===Original===
Ephrem
Because it never sets, but shines always in the heavens to show mariners their right course.

Mary
And how can such a poor thing as I am—made out of slime, as my uncle says—shine like my name?

Ephrem
By keeping your body unspotted, and your mind pure and holy.

Mary
It would be too great an honour for any human being to become like the stars.

===Chinese===
厄弗冷
因为它永不落下，常在天上照耀，为水手指明正路。

玛利亚
可像我这样卑贱之物——如我叔父所说，是泥污里造出来的——怎能像我名字那样发光呢？

厄弗冷
只要保持你身体无瑕，你心思纯洁神圣。

玛利亚
对任何人来说，变得像星辰一样，都是太大的尊荣。

===Original===
Ephrem
If you choose you can be as the angels of God, and when at last you cast off the burden of this mortal body they will be near you. With them you will pass through the air, and walk on the sky. With them you will sweep round the zodiac, and never slacken your steps until the Virgin’s Son takes you in His arms in His mother’s dazzling bridal room!

Mary
Who but an ass would think little of such happiness! So I choose to despise the things of earth, and deny myself now that I may enjoy it!

Ephrem
Out of the mouths of babes and sucklings! A childish heart, but a mature mind!

===Chinese===
厄弗冷
只要你愿意，你可以像上帝的天使一样；待你最终卸下这必死身体的重担时，他们必在你身旁。你将与他们一同穿越空气，行走于天空；与他们一同环游黄道诸宫，脚步不停，直到童贞女之子在祂母亲那光华夺目的洞房里，将你拥入怀中！

玛利亚
除了蠢驴，谁会小看这样的福分！所以我选择轻看世上之物，现在就克制自己，好叫我得享那福分！

厄弗冷
真是“从婴孩和吃奶的口中”发出了赞美！一颗孩童的心，却有一个成熟的心智！

===Original===
Abraham
God be thanked for it!

Ephrem
Amen to that.

Abraham
But though by God’s grace she has been given the light, at her tender age she must be taught how to use it.

Ephrem
You are right.

Abraham
I will build her a little cell with a narrow entrance near my hermitage. I can visit her there often, and through the window instruct her in the psalter and other pages of the divine law.

Ephrem
That is a good plan.

Mary
I put myself under your direction, Father Ephrem.

Ephrem
My daughter! May the Heavenly Bridegroom to Whom you have given yourself in the tender bud of your youth shield you from the wiles of the devil!

===Chinese===
亚伯拉罕
为此感谢上帝！

厄弗冷
诚心所愿。

亚伯拉罕
然而她虽蒙上帝恩典得了光照，在这稚龄，还须教她如何运用。

厄弗冷
你说得对。

亚伯拉罕
我要在我的隐修庐旁，为她造一间入口窄小的小室。我可以常去探望她，隔着窗子教她诵读圣咏集（psalter）与神圣律法的其他篇章。

厄弗冷
这是个好主意。

玛利亚
厄弗冷神父，我把自己置于您的指导之下。

厄弗冷
我的女儿！你在青春的嫩芽之际将自己献给的天上新郎，愿祂保守你免遭魔鬼的诡计！

#### Scene III / 第三场

===Original===
Abraham
Brother Ephrem, Brother Ephrem! When anything happens, good or bad, it is to you I turn. It is your counsel I seek. Do not turn your face away, brother—do not be impatient, but help me.

Ephrem
Abraham, Abraham, what has come to you? What is the cause of this immoderate grief? Ought a hermit to weep and groan after the manner of the world?

Abraham
Was any hermit ever so stricken? I cannot bear my sorrow.

Ephrem
Brother, no more of this. To the point; what has happened?

Abraham
Mary! Mary! my adopted child! Mary, whom I cared for so lovingly and taught with all my skill for ten years! Mary—

Ephrem
Well, what is it?

===Chinese===
亚伯拉罕
厄弗冷弟兄，厄弗冷弟兄！无论何事，或福或祸，我总转向你。我所求的正是你的忠告。弟兄，不要转脸不顾我——不要不耐烦，请帮帮我。

厄弗冷
亚伯拉罕，亚伯拉罕，你怎么了？这般无节制的哀恸，缘何而起？隐修士岂可效法世人的样式哭泣哀号？

亚伯拉罕
何曾有隐修士遭此大难？我的忧伤承当不起。

厄弗冷
弟兄，不要再这样了。说正题；出了什么事？

亚伯拉罕
玛利亚！玛利亚！我收养的孩子！十年来我如此疼爱她、倾尽所能教导她的玛利亚！玛利亚——

厄弗冷
到底怎么了？

===Original===
Abraham
Oh God! She is lost!

Ephrem
Lost? What do you mean?

Abraham
Most miserably. Afterwards she ran away.

Ephrem
But by what wiles did the ancient enemy bring about her undoing?

Abraham
By the wiles of false love. Dressed in a monk’s habit, the hypocrite went to see her often. He succeeded in making the poor ignorant child love him. She leapt from the window of her cell for an evil deed.

===Chinese===
亚伯拉罕
哦上帝！她完了！

厄弗冷
完了？你是什么意思？

亚伯拉罕
惨极了。后来她逃走了。

厄弗冷
可那亘古的仇敌是用什么诡计害她堕落的？

亚伯拉罕
用假爱情的诡计。那伪君子身披修士会衣，频频前去见她。他终于使那可怜无知的孩子爱上了他。她为着一桩罪恶的勾当，从小室的窗口跳了出去。

===Original===
Ephrem
I shudder as I listen to you.

Abraham
When the unhappy girl knew that she was ruined, she beat her breast and dug her nails into her face. She tore her garments, pulled out her hair. Her despairing cries were terrible to hear.

Ephrem
I am not surprised. For such a fall a whole fountain of tears should rise.

Abraham
She moaned out that she could never be the same—

Ephrem
Poor, miserable girl!

===Chinese===
厄弗冷
我听着便不寒而栗。

亚伯拉罕
那不幸的姑娘知道自己毁了之后，捶打胸膛，指甲抠进脸庞。她撕裂衣裳，扯下头发。她绝望的哀号惨不忍闻。

厄弗冷
我不意外。遭此堕落，当涌出一整泉眼泪。

亚伯拉罕
她呻吟着说，她再也不能像从前一样了——

厄弗冷
可怜、悲惨的姑娘！

===Original===
Abraham
And reproached herself for having forgotten our warning.

Ephrem
She might well do so.

Abraham
She cried that all her vigils, prayers, and fasts had been thrown away.

Ephrem
If she perseveres in this penitence she will be saved.

Abraham
She has not persevered. She has added worse to her evil deed.

Ephrem
Oh, this moves me to the depths of my heart!

===Chinese===
亚伯拉罕
又责备自己忘了我们的训诫。

厄弗冷
她理当如此。

亚伯拉罕
她哭喊说，她一切的守夜、祈祷与斋戒都付诸东流了。

厄弗冷
她若持守这份悔改，必能得救。

亚伯拉罕
她没有持守。她在恶行之上又添了更糟的。

厄弗冷
哦，这叫我心深深震动！

===Original===
Abraham
After all these tears and lamentations she was overcome by remorse, and fell headlong into the abyss of despair.

Ephrem
A bitter business!

Abraham
She despaired of being able to win pardon, and resolved to go back to the world and its vanities.

Ephrem
I cannot remember when the devil could boast of such a triumph over the hermits.

Abraham
Now we are at the mercy of the demons.

Ephrem
I marvel that she could have escaped without your knowledge.

===Chinese===
亚伯拉罕
在这一切眼泪与哀叹之后，她被懊悔压倒，一头栽进了绝望的深渊。

厄弗冷
苦事啊！

亚伯拉罕
她绝望于能得到宽赦，便决意回到世界和它的虚浮中去。

厄弗冷
我不记得魔鬼何曾能在隐修士身上夸耀这样的胜利。

亚伯拉罕
如今我们任凭魔鬼摆布了。

厄弗冷
她竟能在你毫不知情中逃走，令我称奇。

===Original===
Abraham
If I had not been so blind! I ought to have paid more heed to that terrible vision. Yes, I see now that it was sent to warn me.

Ephrem
What vision?

Abraham
I dreamed I was standing at the door of my cell, and that a huge dragon with a loathsome stench rushed violently towards me. I saw that the creature was attracted by a little white dove at my side. It pounced on the dove, devoured it, and vanished.

Ephrem
There is no doubt what this vision meant.

Abraham
When I woke I turned over in my mind what I had seen, and took it as a sign of some persecution threatening the Church, through which many of the faithful would be drawn into error. I prostrated myself in prayer, and implored Him Who knows the future to enlighten me.

Ephrem
You did right.

===Chinese===
亚伯拉罕
我若不是那样瞎眼就好了！我本该更留心那可怕的异象。是的，我如今明白，那是派来警诫我的。

厄弗冷
什么异象？

亚伯拉罕
我梦见自己站在小室门口，一条巨龙带着令人作呕的恶臭向我猛扑过来。我看见那怪兽是被我身旁一只小白鸽引来的。它扑向鸽子，将它吞吃，随即消失。

厄弗冷
这异象的含义毋庸置疑。

亚伯拉罕
醒来后我反复思量所见之景，把它当作某种迫害将临教会的征兆，许多信众将由此被引入歧途。我俯伏祈祷，恳求那知道未来的主光照我。

厄弗冷
你做得对。

===Original===
Abraham
On the third night after the vision, when for weariness I had fallen asleep, I saw the beast again, but now it was lying dead at my feet, and the dove was flying heavenwards safe and unhurt.

Ephrem
I am rejoiced to hear this, for to my thinking it means that some day Mary will return to you.

Abraham
I was trying to get rid of the uneasiness with which the first vision had filled me by thinking of the second, when my little pupil in her cell came to my mind. I remembered, although at the time I was not alarmed, that for two days I had not heard her chanting the divine praises.

Ephrem
You were too tardy in noticing this.

===Chinese===
亚伯拉罕
异象之后的第三个夜晚，我因疲惫沉睡过去，又见了那兽，只是这一次它已横死在我脚前，那鸽子正向天上飞去，安然无伤。

厄弗冷
听闻此讯我甚欣慰，因为依我之见，这意味着玛利亚终有一日会回到你身边。

亚伯拉罕
我正想借思想第二个异象，排遣第一个异象带给我的不安，忽然想起小室里我那小小的学生。我记起——虽然当时并未惊觉——我已有两天没听见她诵唱神圣的赞歌了。

厄弗冷
你察觉得太迟了。

===Original===
Abraham
I admit it. I went at once to her cell, and, knocking at the window, I called her again and again, “Mary! My child! Mary!”

Ephrem
You called in vain?

Abraham
“Mary,” I said. “Mary, my child, what is wrong? Why are you not saying your office?” It was only when I did not hear the faintest sound that I suspected.

Ephrem
What did you do then?

===Chinese===
亚伯拉罕
我承认。我立刻去到她的小室，敲着窗，一遍又一遍地唤她：“玛利亚！我的孩子！玛利亚！”

厄弗冷
你唤了也是徒然？

亚伯拉罕
“玛利亚，”我说，“玛利亚，我的孩子，怎么了？你为何不诵你的日课？”直到连最微弱的声息也听不见，我才起了疑心。

厄弗冷
那你怎么办？

===Original===
Abraham
When I could no longer doubt that she had gone, I was struck with fear to my very bowels. I trembled in every limb.

Ephrem
I do not wonder, since I, hearing of it, find myself trembling all over.

Abraham
Then I wept and cried out to the empty air, “What wolf has seized my lamb? What thief has stolen my little daughter?”

Ephrem
You had good cause to weep! To lose her whom you had cherished so tenderly!

===Chinese===
亚伯拉罕
当我再不能怀疑她已经离去时，恐惧直透我的脏腑。我四肢战栗。

厄弗冷
这我并不奇怪，因为我仅是听你说起，便已浑身发抖。

亚伯拉罕
于是我哭泣，向着空茫呼喊：“是什么豺狼叼走了我的羔羊？是什么盗贼偷走了我的小女儿？”

厄弗冷
你大有理由哭泣！失去你如此温柔珍爱的她！

===Original===
Abraham
At last some people came up who knew what had happened. From them I learned that she had gone back to the world.

Ephrem
Where is she now?

Abraham
No one knows.

Ephrem
What is to be done?

Abraham
I have a faithful friend, who is searching all the cities and towns in the country. He says he will never give up until he finds her.

Ephrem
And if he finds her—what then?

Abraham
Then I shall change these clothes, and in the guise of a worldling seek her out. It may be that she will heed what I say, and even after this shipwreck turn again to the harbour of her innocence and peace.

Ephrem
And suppose that in the world they offer you flesh meat and wine?

Abraham
If they do, I shall not refuse; otherwise I might be recognized.

Ephrem
No one will blame you, brother. It will be but praiseworthy discretion on your part to loosen the bridle of strict observance for the sake of bringing back a soul.

Abraham
I am the more eager to try now I know you approve.

Ephrem
He Who knows the secret places of the heart can tell with what motive every action is done. That scrupulous and fair Judge will not condemn a man for relaxing our strict rule for a time and descending to the level of weaker mortals if by so doing he can make more sure of rescuing an errant soul.

Abraham
Help me with your prayers. Pray that I may not be caught in the snares of the devil.

Ephrem
May He Who is supreme good itself, without Whom no good thing can be done, bless your enterprise and bring it to a happy end!

===Chinese===
亚伯拉罕
终于有些人走上前来，知道发生了何事。我才从他们口中得知，她已回到世上去了。

厄弗冷
她如今在哪里？

亚伯拉罕
无人知晓。

厄弗冷
如今该怎么办？

亚伯拉罕
我有一位忠实的友人，正寻遍全国的城镇。他说找不到她决不罢休。

厄弗冷

Mary
With all my strength I will persevere, and though my flesh may fail, my spirit never will.

Abraham

Mary
If His will is made perfect in me it will be because of your merits.

Abraham
Come, let us hasten on our way.

Mary

Abraham

#### Scene IX / 第九场

===Original===
Ephrem
Well, brother! If I am not mistaken, you bring good news.

Abraham
The best in the world.

Ephrem
You have found your lost lamb?

Abraham
I have, and, rejoicing, have brought her back to the fold.

Ephrem
Truly this is the work of divine grace.

Abraham
That is certain.

Ephrem
How is she spending her days? I should like to know how you have ordered her life. What does she do?
