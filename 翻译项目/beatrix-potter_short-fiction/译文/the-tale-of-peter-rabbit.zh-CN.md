## The Tale of Peter Rabbit / 彼得兔的故事

===Original===
Once upon a time there were four little Rabbits, and their names were Flopsy, Mopsy, Cottontail, and Peter.

They lived with their Mother in a sandbank, underneath the root of a very big fir-tree.

===Chinese===
从前，有四只小兔子，他们的名字分别叫弗洛普西、莫普西、棉尾巴，还有彼得。

他们和妈妈一起住在一个沙土坡里，就在一棵大枞树（fir-tree）的树根底下。

===Original===
“Now my dears,” said old Mrs. Rabbit one morning, “you may go into the fields or down the lane, but don’t go into Mr. McGregor’s garden.”

“Your Father had an accident there; he was put in a pie by Mrs. McGregor.”

“Now run along, and don’t get into mischief. I am going out.”

===Chinese===
“好了，孩子们，”有一天早上，兔妈妈说道，“你们可以到田野里去玩，也可以顺着小路去散步，可千万别进麦格雷戈先生（Mr. McGregor）的花园。”

“你们的爸爸就是在那儿出了事——他被麦格雷戈太太做成了馅饼。”

“好了，快去吧，别淘气闯祸。我要出门了。”

===Original===
Then old Mrs. Rabbit took a basket and her umbrella, and went through the wood to the baker’s. She bought a loaf of brown bread and five currant buns.

Flopsy, Mopsy, and Cottontail, who were good little bunnies, went down the lane to gather blackberries;

But Peter, who was very naughty, ran straight away to Mr. McGregor’s garden.

And squeezed under the gate!

===Chinese===
说完，兔妈妈挎上篮子，撑起她的小伞，穿过树林到面包房去了。她买了一条黑面包，还有五个葡萄干小面包。

弗洛普西、莫普西和棉尾巴都是乖乖的小兔子，他们顺着小路去采黑莓了。

可彼得呢，偏偏调皮得很，撒腿就直奔麦格雷戈先生的花园去了。

哧溜一下，从栅栏门底下钻了进去！

===Original===
First he ate some lettuces and some French beans; and then he ate some radishes;

And then, feeling rather sick, he went to look for some parsley.

But round the end of a cucumber frame, whom should he meet but Mr. McGregor!

===Chinese===
他先吃了一些生菜，又吃了一些法国豆；接着又啃起萝卜来。

吃着吃着，肚子有些不舒服了，他便去找香菜压一压。

谁知，绕过黄瓜棚架的一头，他迎面撞上的，竟是麦格雷戈先生！

===Original===
Mr. McGregor was on his hands and knees planting out young cabbages, but he jumped up and ran after Peter, waving a rake and calling out, “Stop thief!”

Peter was most dreadfully frightened; he rushed all over the garden, for he had forgotten the way back to the gate.

He lost one of his shoes among the cabbages, and the other shoe amongst the potatoes.

===Chinese===
麦格雷戈先生正跪在地上移栽卷心菜苗，可他一跃而起，挥舞着一把耙子追赶彼得，一边追一边大喊：“抓小偷啊！”

彼得吓得魂飞魄散；他在花园里到处乱窜，因为回栅栏门的路，他已经全忘了。

他的一只鞋子掉在了卷心菜地里，另一只鞋子丢在了土豆丛中。

===Original===
After losing them, he ran on four legs and went faster, so that I think he might have got away altogether if he had not unfortunately run into a gooseberry net, and got caught by the large buttons on his jacket. It was a blue jacket with brass buttons, quite new.

Peter gave himself up for lost, and shed big tears; but his sobs were overheard by some friendly sparrows, who flew to him in great excitement, and implored him to exert himself.

Mr. McGregor came up with a sieve, which he intended to pop upon the top of Peter; but Peter wriggled out just in time, leaving his jacket behind him.

===Chinese===
鞋子丢掉以后，他四条腿一齐跑，跑得更快了。要不是他倒了大霉，一头撞进了一张醋栗网，被夹克上那几颗大扣子挂住，我说不定他已经溜之大吉了呢。那可是一件蓝色的夹克，钉着黄铜扣子，还是崭新的。

彼得自以为这下全完了，吧嗒吧嗒掉下大颗大颗的眼泪；可他抽抽搭搭的哭声，被几只好心的小麻雀听见了。它们激动地飞到他身边，苦苦劝他再使把劲儿。

麦格雷戈先生拿着一把筛子赶了过来，想一下子扣在彼得头顶上；可彼得就在这千钧一发之际扭着身子挣脱了，只留下那件小夹克。

===Original===
And rushed into the tool-shed, and jumped into a can. It would have been a beautiful thing to hide in, if it had not had so much water in it.

Mr. McGregor was quite sure that Peter was somewhere in the tool-shed, perhaps hidden underneath a flowerpot. He began to turn them over carefully, looking under each.

Presently Peter sneezed—“Kertyschoo!” Mr. McGregor was after him in no time.

===Chinese===
他一头钻进工具棚，跳进了一只大水罐里。这倒是个藏身的好地方，只可惜里面装了太多的水。

麦格雷戈先生十分肯定，彼得就藏在工具棚里的某个角落，说不定就躲在哪只花盆底下。他开始小心翼翼地把花盆一只只翻过来，挨个往底下瞧。

不一会儿，彼得打了个喷嚏——“阿嚏！”麦格雷戈先生一下子就追了过来。

===Original===
And tried to put his foot upon Peter, who jumped out of a window, upsetting three plants. The window was too small for Mr. McGregor, and he was tired of running after Peter. He went back to his work.

Peter sat down to rest; he was out of breath and trembling with fright, and he had not the least idea which way to go. Also he was very damp with sitting in that can.

After a time he began to wander about, going lippity—lippity—not very fast, and looking all round.

===Chinese===
他一脚踏下去，想踩住彼得，彼得却从一扇窗户跳了出去，撞翻了三棵菜苗。那窗户对麦格雷戈先生来说太小了，他也追彼得追得累了，便回去接着干活了。

彼得坐下来歇口气；他气喘吁吁，吓得直发抖，全然不知该往哪边走才好。再说，在那水罐里坐了半天，他浑身上下都湿漉漉的。

过了一会儿，他开始四处溜达，一蹦——一跳——走得并不快，一双眼睛四下里张望着。

===Original===
He found a door in a wall; but it was locked, and there was no room for a fat little rabbit to squeeze underneath.

An old mouse was running in and out over the stone doorstep, carrying peas and beans to her family in the wood. Peter asked her the way to the gate, but she had such a large pea in her mouth that she could not answer. She only shook her head at him. Peter began to cry.

Then he tried to find his way straight across the garden, but he became more and more puzzled. Presently, he came to a pond where Mr. McGregor filled his water-cans. A white cat was staring at some goldfish, she sat very, very still, but now and then the tip of her tail twitched as if it were alive. Peter thought it best to go away without speaking to her; he had heard about cats from his cousin, little Benjamin Bunny.

===Chinese===
他在一堵墙上找到了一扇门；可门锁着，门下的缝又太窄，一只胖乎乎的小兔子根本钻不过去。

一只老田鼠正在石头门槛上进进出出，把豆子和豌豆搬给住在树林里的一家老小。彼得向她打听回栅栏门该怎么走，可她嘴里衔着一颗大豌豆，话也说不出来，只能冲他摇摇头。彼得哭了起来。

后来他想干脆横穿过花园，找到出路，可他越走越迷糊。不一会儿，他来到一个池塘边，麦格雷戈先生常在这儿给水罐打水。一只白猫正盯着几条金鱼看，她一动不动地坐着，坐得稳稳的，可时不时的，尾巴尖会猛地一抽，像是活的一样。彼得觉得，还是不跟她搭话、悄悄走开为妙；关于猫的厉害，他早听表弟小本杰明兔（Benjamin Bunny）说起过了。

===Original===
He went back towards the tool-shed, but suddenly, quite close to him, he heard the noise of a hoe—scr‑r‑ritch, scratch, scratch, scritch. Peter scuttered underneath the bushes. But presently, as nothing happened, he came out, and climbed upon a wheelbarrow and peeped over. The first thing he saw was Mr. McGregor hoeing onions. His back was turned towards Peter, and beyond him was the gate!

Peter got down very quietly off the wheelbarrow; and started running as fast as he could go, along a straight walk behind some black-currant bushes.

Mr. McGregor caught sight of him at the corner, but Peter did not care. He slipped underneath the gate, and was safe at last in the wood outside the garden.

Mr. McGregor hung up the little jacket and the shoes for a scarecrow to frighten the blackbirds.

===Chinese===
他转身朝工具棚那边走回去，可就在他跟前，突然响起了锄头的声音——嚓-嚓-嚓-嚓-嚓。彼得慌忙钻进了灌木丛底下。可过了一阵，什么动静也没有，他便探出头来，爬上一辆手推车，悄悄往外张望。他头一眼瞧见的，是麦格雷戈先生正在锄洋葱。他的背正朝着彼得，而在他身后，就是那扇栅栏门！

彼得蹑手蹑脚地从手推车上溜下来，沿着黑加仑灌木丛后面的一条笔直小路，撒开腿拼命地跑。

麦格雷戈先生在拐角处一眼瞥见了他，可彼得这会儿什么都不顾了。他从栅栏门底下哧溜一钻，总算平安地逃进了花园外头的树林里。

麦格雷戈先生把那件小夹克和两只小鞋子挂起来，扎成一个稻草人，好吓唬那些黑鸟。

===Original===
Peter never stopped running or looked behind him till he got home to the big fir-tree.

He was so tired that he flopped down upon the nice soft sand on the floor of the rabbit-hole and shut his eyes. His mother was busy cooking; she wondered what he had done with his clothes. It was the second little jacket and pair of shoes that Peter had lost in a fortnight!

I am sorry to say that Peter was not very well during the evening.

His mother put him to bed, and made some camomile tea; and she gave a dose of it to Peter!

“One tablespoonful to be taken at bedtime.”

But Flopsy, Mopsy, and Cottontail had bread and milk and blackberries for supper.

===Chinese===
彼得一路跑个不停，头也不回，直到钻进大枞树底下的家，才停住脚。

他累坏了，一进门就扑通一声瘫倒在兔子洞里松软的沙地上，闭上了眼睛。妈妈正忙着做饭，心里纳闷他那身衣裳都到哪儿去了。这已经是彼得在半个月里丢掉的第二件小夹克、第二双小鞋子啦！

说来遗憾，那天晚上，彼得觉得浑身不大舒坦。

妈妈把他安顿到床上，煮了一些甘菊茶；她给彼得灌下了一大勺！

“睡前服用，一汤匙。”

至于弗洛普西、莫普西和棉尾巴呢，他们的晚餐是面包泡牛奶，还有黑莓。
