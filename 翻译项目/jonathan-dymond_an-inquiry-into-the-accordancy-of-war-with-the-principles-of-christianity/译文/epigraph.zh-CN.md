===Original===

Contempt prior to examination, however comfortable to the mind which entertains it, or however natural to great parts, is extremely dangerous; and more apt than almost any other disposition, to produce erroneous judgments both of persons and opinions.

> Paley

===Chinese===

未经审视便先行藐视，不论对抱持此种态度的心智而言是何等惬意，亦或对才智出众者而言是何等自然，皆极其危险；并且比几乎任何其他秉性都更容易对人与观点产生错误的评判。

> 佩利（Paley）
