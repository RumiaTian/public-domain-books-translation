## II The High Hat / 二 高顶帽

===Original===
John Quincy Winterslip walked aboard the ferry at Oakland feeling rather limp and weary. For more than six days he had been marooned on sleepers—his pause at Chicago had been but a flitting from one train to another—and he was fed up. Seeing America first—that was what he had been doing. And what an appalling lot of it there was! He felt that for an eternity he had been staring at endless plains, dotted here and there by unesthetic houses the inmates of which had unquestionably never heard a symphony concert.

Ahead of him ambled a porter, bearing his two suitcases, his golf clubs and his hatbox. One of the man’s hands was gone—chewed off, no doubt, in some amiable frontier scuffle. In its place he wore a steel hook. Well, no one could question the value of a steel hook to a man in the porter’s profession. But how quaint—and western!

The boy indicated a spot by the rail on the forward deck, and the porter began to unload. Carefully selecting the man’s good hand, John Quincy dropped into it a tip so generous as to result in a touching of hook to cap in a weird salute. The object of this attention sank down amid his elaborate trappings, removed the straw hat from his perspiring head, and tried to figure out just what had happened to him.

===Chinese===
约翰·昆西·温特斯利普（John Quincy Winterslip）在奥克兰登上渡轮时，只觉得浑身疲软乏力。一连六天多，他都被困在卧铺车厢里——在芝加哥那一停，也不过是从一趟车匆匆跳上另一趟车——他实在受够了。「先看美国」——他这些日子干的就是这个。可美国也实在多得吓人！他觉得自己仿佛已生生世世凝望着无边无际的大平原，原野上东一幢西一幢地点缀着毫不见美的房子，住在里头的人无疑从未听过一场交响音乐会。

前头一个搬运工慢悠悠地走着，替他扛着两口衣箱、高尔夫球杆和帽盒。那人的一只手没了——十有八九是在边疆某场和和气气的斗殴里被咬掉的。原处装着一只钢钩。唔，对干搬运工这一行的人来说，钢钩有多要紧，谁也不能不服气。可这是多么古雅——多么西部风啊！

小伙子指了指前甲板栏杆旁的一处地方，搬运工便动手卸行李。约翰·昆西小心挑了那人还完好的那只手，把一笔可观的小费放进掌心，结果那只钢钩以一种古怪的敬礼姿势碰了碰帽檐。受此厚遇的主角在他那一大堆繁复的行头中间坐了下来，从汗涔涔的脑袋上摘下草帽，努力想弄明白自己究竟遇上了什么事。

===Original===
Three thousand miles from Beacon Street, and two thousand miles still to go! Why, he inquired sourly of his usually pleasant self, had he ever agreed to make this absurd expedition into heathen country? Here it was late June, Boston was at its best. Tennis at Longwood, long mild evenings in a single shell on the Charles, weekends and golf with Agatha Parker at Magnolia. And if one must travel, there was Paris. He hadn’t seen Paris in two years and had been rather planning a quick run over, when his mother had put this preposterous notion into his head.

Preposterous—it was all of that. Traveling five thousand miles just as a gentle hint to Aunt Minerva to return to her calm, well-ordered life behind purple windowpanes on Beacon Street. And was there any chance that his strong-minded relative would take the hint? Not one in a thousand. Aunt Minerva was accustomed to do as she pleased—he had an uncomfortable, shocked recollection of one occasion when she had said she would do as she damn well pleased.

John Quincy wished he was back. He wished he was crossing Boston Common to his office on State Street, there to put out a new issue of bonds. He was not yet a member of the firm—that was an honor accorded only to Winterslips who were bald and a little stooped—but his heart was in his work. He put out a bond issue with loving apprehension, waiting for the verdict as a playwright waits behind the scenes on a first night. Would those First Mortgage Sixes go over big, or would they flop at his feet?

===Chinese===
离灯塔街（Beacon Street）已经三千英里，前头还有两千英里的路！他没好气地问他那个平日温和的自己：当初怎么会答应这趟闯进异教徒之地的荒唐远行？眼下是六月下旬，波士顿正是最好的时光。朗伍德（Longwood）的网球，查尔斯河上单人赛艇里一个又一个悠长温柔的黄昏，还有在玛格诺利亚（Magnolia）同阿加莎·帕克（Agatha Parker）共度的高尔夫周末。倘若人当真要旅行，还有巴黎呢。他两年没见巴黎了，本来正盘算着飞快地跑一趟，他母亲却把这么个荒唐念头塞进了他的脑子。

荒唐透顶——可不正是。足足跑上五千英里，只为委婉示意米纳瓦姑婆：请回到灯塔街紫色窗玻璃后面那种平静而有条理的生活里去吧。可这位意志坚定的亲戚领会这层暗示的机会有没有？千中无一。米纳瓦姑婆向来是想怎样就怎样——他有个至今叫他既震惊又不自在的记忆：有一回她竟说，她高兴怎么做就他妈的怎么做。

约翰·昆西恨不得已经回去了。他恨不得正穿过波士顿公园（Boston Common）走向州街（State Street）上的办公室，去发行新的一期债券。他还不是公司的合伙人——那份荣誉只留给秃了顶、有点驼背的温特斯利普家的人——但他把心放在工作上。他发行一期债券，怀着爱意也怀着忐忑，像剧作家首演之夜守在幕后等待评判。那批第一抵押六厘债券会一鸣惊人呢，还是栽倒在他脚边一败涂地？

===Original===
The hoarse boom of a ferry whistle recalled John Quincy to his present unbelievable location on the map. The boat began to move. He was dimly conscious of a young person of feminine gender who came and sat at his side. Away from the slip and out into the harbor the ferry carried John Quincy, and he suddenly sat up and took notice, for he was never blind to beauty, no matter where he encountered it.

And he was encountering beauty now. The morning air was keen and dry and bright. Spread out before him was that harbor which is like a tired navigator’s dream come true. They passed Goat Island, and he heard the faint echo of a bugle, he saw Tamalpais lifting its proud head toward the sparkling sky, he turned, and there was San Francisco scattered blithely over its many hills.

The ferry plowed on, and John Quincy sat very still. A forest of masts and steam funnels—here was the water front that had supplied the atmosphere for those romantic tales that held him spellbound when he was a boy at school—a quiet young Winterslip whom the gypsy strain had missed. Now he could distinguish a bark from Antwerp, a great liner from the Orient, a five-masted schooner that was reminiscent of those supposedly forgotten stories. Ships from the Treaty Ports, ships from coconut islands in southern seas. A picture as intriguing and colorful as a back drop in a theater—but far more real.

===Chinese===
渡轮汽笛沙哑的一声轰鸣，把约翰·昆西唤回到他此刻在地图上这处难以置信的位置。船开动了。他隐约觉察到一个年轻女子走来坐在他身边。渡轮载着约翰·昆西驶离泊位、进了港湾，他忽然坐直身子留起神来——因为无论在哪儿遇见美，他从不曾视而不见。

此刻他遇见的正是美。清晨的空气凛冽、干燥而明亮。在他眼前铺展开的，是那座像疲惫的航海者美梦成真的港湾。船过山羊岛（Goat Island），他听见军号隐约的回声；他望见塔马尔帕斯山（Tamalpais）向着闪闪发亮的天空昂起骄傲的头；他一转身，旧金山正欢欢喜喜地撒落在它重重叠叠的山丘上。

渡轮破浪前行，约翰·昆西端坐不动。桅杆和蒸汽烟囱的森林——这就是那道滨水风光，当年在学校里，正是那些以此为背景的浪漫故事让他听得神魂颠倒——他这个安静的温特斯利普家少年，偏偏没沾上家里那吉普赛血统的边。如今他分得出一艘来自安特卫普的三桅帆船、一艘来自东方的大邮轮，还有一艘五桅帆船，教人想起那些据说早已忘却的故事。来自通商口岸的船，来自南洋椰子岛的船。一幅像剧院画幕一样引人入胜、色彩斑斓的图景——只是远比画幕真实。

===Original===
Suddenly John Quincy stood up. A puzzled look had come into his calm gray eyes. “I—I don’t understand,” he murmured.

He was startled by the sound of his own voice. He hadn’t intended to speak aloud. In order not to appear too utterly silly, he looked around for someone to whom he might pretend he had addressed that remark. There was no one about—except the young person who was obviously feminine and therefore not to be informally accosted.

John Quincy looked down at her. Spanish or something like that, blue-black hair, dark eyes that were alight now with the amusement she was striving to hide, a delicate oval face tanned a deep brown. He looked again at the harbor—beauty all about the boat, and beauty on it. Much better than traveling on trains!

The girl looked up at John Quincy. She saw a big, broad-shouldered young man with a face as innocent as a child’s. A bit of friendliness, she decided instantly, would not be misunderstood.

===Chinese===
约翰·昆西忽然站起来。他平静的灰眼睛里浮起困惑的神色。「我——我不明白，」他喃喃道。

他被自己的声音吓了一跳。他本没打算说出声来。为了不显得太傻，他四下里张望，想找个人好假装那句话是对他说的。周围一个人也没有——只有那位显然身为女性的年轻人，因此不便随随便便搭讪。

“I’m from Boston,” he said.
