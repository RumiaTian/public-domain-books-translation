## XXI The Stone Walls Crumble / 二十一 石墙崩塌

===Original===
The stone walls were crumbling and the light streaming through﻿—but only for Chan. John Quincy was still groping in the dark, and his reflections were a little bitter as he returned to the house at Waikiki. Chan and he had worked together, but now that they approached the crisis of their efforts, the detective evidently preferred to push on alone, leaving his fellow-worker to follow if he could. Well, so be it﻿—but John Quincy’s pride was touched.

He had suddenly a keen desire to show Chan that he could not be left behind like that. If only he could, by some inspirational flash of deductive reasoning, arrive at the solution of the mystery simultaneously with the detective. For the honor of Boston and the Winterslips.

===Chinese===

他陡然生出一股强烈的欲望，要向陈查理证明，自己可不是能这样随随便便被甩下的人。但愿他能凭推理演绎的一道灵光，与这位侦探同时抵达谜底。为了波士顿的荣誉，为了温特斯利普家的荣誉。

Frowning deeply, he considered all the old discarded clues again. The people who had been under suspicion and then dropped﻿—Egan, the Compton woman, Brade, Kaohla, Leatherbee, Saladine, Cope. He even considered several the investigation had not touched. Presently he came to Bowker. What did Bowker’s reappearance mean?

For the first time in two weeks he thought of the little man with the fierce pompadour and the gold-rimmed eyeglasses. Bowker with his sorrowful talk of vanished barrooms and lost friends behind the bar. How was the steward on the *President Tyler* connected with the murder of Dan Winterslip? He had not done it himself, that was obvious, but in some way he was linked up with the crime. John Quincy spent a long and painful period seeking to join Bowker up with one or another of the suspects. It couldn’t be done.

他住了口，那方案的种种不利已在他心里了然。他的右手倏地伸向口袋。约翰·昆西灵机一动，抓起一只灌满水的水瓶，朝那人头上掷去。詹尼森一闪身；水瓶砸穿了一扇舷窗。玻璃的哗啦声划破夜空，却没有一个人露面。约翰·昆西看见詹尼森朝他扑来，手里有什么东西寒光一闪。他侧身让过，整个人扑上那人的后背，把他压得跪了下去。他死死攥住詹尼森握着自动手枪的右腕。两人这样僵持了片刻，随后詹尼森开始缓缓站起身来。握枪的那只手开始挣脱。约翰·昆西咬紧牙关，拼命想守住这一攥。可他面对的是比那红头发水手强悍得多的对手，他相形见绌，这认知带着叫人发晕的力量，一寸寸爬上他的心头。

詹尼森这会儿已经站直了，右手眼看就要挣脱。再过一刻——下一步呢？约翰·昆西暗想。这个人根本没打算放他上岸；话一出口，那方案就已在他心里作废。一声闷响的枪声，然后夜里晚些时候，等船远远开到太平洋上——约翰·昆西想到了波士顿，想到他的母亲。想到正等他归来的卡洛塔。他鼓起全身的力气，要作最后一搏，把那一攥重新扣死。
