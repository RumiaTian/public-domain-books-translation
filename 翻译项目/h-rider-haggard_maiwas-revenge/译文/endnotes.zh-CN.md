## Endnotes / 尾注

===Original===
1. The Editor would have been inclined to think that in relating this incident Mr. Quatermain was making himself interesting at the expense of the exact truth, did it not happen that a similar incident has come within his knowledge. —﻿Editor ↩︎

===Chinese===
1. 若不是恰巧有一桩类似的遭遇，也落在编者的见闻之内，编者本来要倾向于认为，夸特梅因先生讲述这件事，是为了把自己说得引人入胜，而不惜牺牲一点事实的确凿。——编者 ↩︎

===Original===
2. For the satisfaction of any who may be so disbelieving as to take this view of Mr. Quatermain’s story, the Editor may state that a gentleman with whom he is acquainted, and whose veracity he believes to be beyond doubt, not long ago described to him how he chanced to kill *four* African elephants with four consecutive bullets. Two of these elephants were charging him simultaneously, and out of the four three were killed with the head shot, a very uncommon thing in the case of the African elephant. —﻿Editor ↩︎

===Chinese===
2. 为叫凡是疑心到这步田地、对夸特梅因先生的故事作此想法的人心服，编者可以说明：有一位编者相识的绅士——其诚实，编者信得过，无可置疑——不久前向他描述过，自己如何碰巧用连发四弹，打死了*四头*非洲象。其中两头同时向他猛冲；而四头之中，有三头是头部一枪毙命——对非洲象来说，这是极罕见的事。——编者 ↩︎

===Original===
3. The largest elephant tusk of which the Editor has any certain knowledge scaled one hundred and fifty pounds. ↩︎

===Chinese===
3. 编者确切知道的、最大的一支象牙，称重一百五十磅。↩︎

===Original===
4. About one hundred and twenty miles. —﻿Editor ↩︎

===Chinese===
4. 约一百二十英里。——编者 ↩︎
