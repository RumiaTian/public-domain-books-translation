## Dedication / 献词

===Original===
Dedicated to

**My Mother**

And to the Men Whose Confidence and Foresight Made Possible the Flight of the

*Spirit of St. Louis*
- Mr. Harry H. Knight
- Major William B. Robertson
- Major Albert Bond Lambert
- Mr. J. D. Wooster Lambert
- Mr. Harold M. Bixby
- Mr. Earl C. Thompson
- Mr. Harry F. Knight
- Mr. E. Lansing Ray
