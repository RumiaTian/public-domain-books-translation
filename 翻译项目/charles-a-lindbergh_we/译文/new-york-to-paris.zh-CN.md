## X New York to Paris / 十 从纽约到巴黎

===Original===
At New York we checked over the plane, engine and instruments, which required several short flights over the field.

===Chinese===
在纽约，我们对飞机、发动机和仪表做了检查，为此在机场上空试飞了几个短程。

===Original===
When the plane was completely inspected and ready for the transatlantic flight, there were dense fogs reported along the coast and over Nova Scotia and Newfoundland, in addition to a storm area over the North Atlantic.

===Chinese===
飞机全部查验完毕、可以投入跨大西洋飞行时，沿岸以及新斯科舍和纽芬兰上空却报有浓雾，北大西洋上还有一个风暴区。

===Original===
On the morning of May 19th, a light rain was falling and the sky was overcast. Weather reports from land stations and ships along the great circle course were unfavorable and there was apparently no prospect of taking off for Paris for several days at least. In the morning I visited the Wright plant at Paterson, New Jersey, and had planned to attend a theatre performance in New York that evening. But at about six o'clock I received a special report from the New York Weather Bureau. A high pressure area was over the entire North Atlantic and the low pressure over Nova Scotia and Newfoundland was receding. It was apparent that the prospects of the fog clearing up were as good as I might expect for some time to come. The North Atlantic should be clear with only local storms on the coast of Europe. The moon had just passed full and the percentage of days with fog over Newfoundland and the Grand Banks was increasing so that there seemed to be no advantage in waiting longer.

===Chinese===
5 月 19 日晨，天上下着小雨，天空阴沉。大圆航线沿线陆上台站和船舶发来的天气报告都不利，至少几天之内显然没有飞往巴黎的希望。上午我参观了新泽西州帕特森（Paterson）的莱特工厂，本打算当晚去纽约看一场戏。可大约六点钟，我收到纽约气象局的一份特别报告：整个北大西洋为高压所控制，新斯科舍和纽芬兰上空的低压正在退去。显然，雾消云散的前景已是相当长一段时间里所能指望的最好情形了。北大西洋应当是晴朗的，只有欧洲沿岸有局部风暴。月亮刚过望，纽芬兰和大浅滩（Grand Banks）出现雾天的日数比例正在上升，再等下去似乎没有任何好处。

===Original===
We went to Curtiss Field as quickly as possible and made arrangements for the barograph to be sealed and installed, and for the plane to be serviced and checked.

===Chinese===
我们尽快赶到柯蒂斯机场，安排人封好并装上自记气压计，同时给飞机整备、检查。

===Original===
We decided partially to fill the fuel tanks in the hangar before towing the ship on a truck to Roosevelt Field, which adjoins Curtiss on the east, where the servicing would be completed.

===Chinese===
我们决定先在机库里给油箱加一部分油，再用卡车把飞机拖到罗斯福机场——它在柯蒂斯机场以东、与之紧邻——在那里完成全部整备。

===Original===
I left the responsibility for conditioning the plane in the hands of the men on the field while I went into the hotel for about two and one-half hours of rest; but at the hotel there were several more details which had to be completed and I was unable to get any sleep that night.

===Chinese===
我把整备飞机的责任交给场上的人员，自己回旅馆去休息大约两个半小时；可到了旅馆还有几件细节必须办完，那一夜我一点儿也没能合眼。

===Original===
I returned to the field before daybreak on the morning of the twentieth. A light rain was falling which continued until almost dawn; consequently we did not move the ship to Roosevelt Field until much later than we had planned, and the takeoff was delayed from daybreak until nearly eight o'clock.

===Chinese===
二十日拂晓前，我回到机场。小雨一直下到将近天明；因此我们把飞机挪到罗斯福机场的时间比计划晚了许多，起飞也从黎明推迟到将近八点。

===Original===
At dawn the shower had passed, although the sky was overcast, and occasionally there would be some slight precipitation. The tail of the plane was lashed to a truck and escorted by a number of motorcycle police. The slow trip from Curtiss to Roosevelt was begun.

===Chinese===
黎明时分阵雨已经过去，虽然天仍阴着，偶尔还飘点零星小雨。飞机尾部拴在一辆卡车上，由一批摩托警察护送，从柯蒂斯到罗斯福的缓慢转运开始了。

===Original===
The ship was placed at the extreme west end of the field heading along the east and west runway, and the final fueling commenced.

===Chinese===
飞机被安放在机场最西端，机头对着东西向跑道，最后的加油开始了。

===Original===
About 7:40 a.m. the motor was started and at 7:52 I took off on the flight for Paris.

===Chinese===
上午 7 点 40 分左右，发动机启动；7 点 52 分，我起飞，踏上了飞往巴黎的航程。

===Original===
The field was a little soft due to the rain during the night and the heavily loaded plane gathered speed very slowly. After passing the halfway mark, however, it was apparent that I would be able to clear the obstructions at the end. I passed over a tractor by about fifteen feet and a telephone line by about twenty, with a fair reserve of flying speed. I believe that the ship would have taken off from a hard field with at least five hundred pounds more weight.

===Chinese===
夜里下过雨，场地有些松软，重载的飞机加速非常缓慢。不过一过半程标记，我就看得出能够越过跑道尽头的障碍物了。我以相当充裕的速度余量，从一台拖拉机上方约十五英尺、一条电话线上方约二十英尺处掠过。我相信，倘若是从坚硬场地上起飞，这架飞机还能再多载至少五百磅。

===Original===
I turned slightly to the right to avoid some high trees on a hill directly ahead, but by the time I had gone a few hundred yards I had sufficient altitude to clear all obstructions and throttled the engine down to 1,750 rpm. I took up a compass course at once and soon reached Long Island Sound where the Curtiss Oriole with its photographer, which had been escorting me, turned back.

===Chinese===
我稍稍右转，避开正前方一座山丘上的几棵大树；不过飞出几百码后，高度已足以越过一切障碍，我把发动机转速收到 1,750 转/分。我随即转入罗盘航向，很快到达长岛海峡（Long Island Sound），一直为我护航的那架载着摄影师的柯蒂斯"黄鹂"式在那里折返了。

===Original===
The haze soon cleared and from Cape Cod through the southern half of Nova Scotia the weather and visibility were excellent. I was flying very low, sometimes as close as ten feet from the trees and water.

===Chinese===
轻雾很快散去，从科德角到新斯科舍南半部，天气和能见度都极好。我飞得很低，有时离树木和水面只有十英尺。

===Original===
On the three hundred mile stretch of water between Cape Cod and Nova Scotia I passed within view of numerous fishing vessels.

===Chinese===
在科德角与新斯科舍之间三百英里的水面上，无数渔船进入我的视野。

===Original===
The northern part of Nova Scotia contained a number of storm areas and several times I flew through cloudbursts.

===Chinese===
新斯科舍北部有好几片风暴区，我数次穿越倾盆骤雨。

===Original===
As I neared the northern coast, snow appeared in patches on the ground and far to the eastward the coastline was covered with fog.

===Chinese===
临近北部海岸时，地面出现斑斑残雪，而远远的东方，海岸线被雾笼罩着。

===Original===
For many miles between Nova Scotia and Newfoundland the ocean was covered with caked ice but as I approached the coast the ice disappeared entirely and I saw several ships in this area.

===Chinese===
新斯科舍与纽芬兰之间的海面上，许多英里都覆着板结的浮冰；但一接近海岸，冰完全消失了，我在这片海域看见了几艘船。

===Original===
I had taken up a course for St. Johns, which is south of the great Circle from New York to Paris, so that there would be no question of the fact that I had passed Newfoundland in case I was forced down in the north Atlantic.

===Chinese===
我取的是飞往圣约翰斯（St. Johns）的航向，它在纽约—巴黎大圆航线以南——这样，万一我被迫降落在北大西洋，"已飞过纽芬兰"这一事实便无可置疑。

===Original===
I passed over numerous icebergs after leaving St. Johns, but saw no ships except near the coast.

===Chinese===
离开圣约翰斯后，我飞越了无数冰山，但除近岸处外没有看见一艘船。

===Original===
Darkness set in about 8:15 and a thin, low fog formed over the sea through which the white bergs showed up with surprising clearness. This fog became thicker and increased in height until within two hours I was just skimming the top of storm clouds at about ten thousand feet. Even at this altitude there was a thick haze through which only the stars directly overhead could be seen.

===Chinese===
大约 8 点 15 分夜幕降临，海面上腾起一层薄薄的低雾，白色的冰山在雾中反而显出惊人的清晰。雾越来越浓、越来越高，两小时之内，我已在一万英尺左右的高度贴着风暴云的顶飞行。即便在这个高度，仍有浓重的霾，只有头顶正上方的星星看得见。

===Original===
There was no moon and it was very dark. The tops of some of the storm clouds were several thousand feet above me and at one time, when I attempted to fly through one of the larger clouds, sleet started to collect on the plane and I was forced to turn around and get back into clear air immediately and then fly around any clouds which I could not get over.

===Chinese===
没有月亮，天非常黑。有些风暴云的云顶高出我几千英尺；有一次我试图从一朵较大的云中穿过去，飞机上开始积起冻雨，我被迫立即掉头回到净空之中，此后凡飞不过去的云就绕着飞。

===Original===
The moon appeared on the horizon after about two hours of darkness; then the flying was much less complicated.

===Chinese===
黑暗持续了大约两个小时后，月亮升上了地平线，飞行随之简单多了。

===Original===
Dawn came at about 1 a.m. New York time and the temperature had risen until there was practically no remaining danger of sleet.

===Chinese===
纽约时间约凌晨 1 点，天亮了。气温已经回升，结冻雨的危险实际上已不存在。

===Original===
Shortly after sunrise the clouds became more broken although some of them were far above me and it was often necessary to fly through them, navigating by instruments only.

===Chinese===
日出后不久，云层变得较为破碎，不过有些云仍远在我上方，常常必须穿云而过，全凭仪表导航。

===Original===
As the sun became higher, holes appeared in the fog. Through one the open water was visible, and I dropped down until less than a hundred feet above the waves. There was a strong wind blowing from the northwest and the ocean was covered with white caps.

===Chinese===
太阳升高后，雾中现出一个个空洞。从一个洞里看到了开阔的水面，我随即下降到离浪顶不足一百英尺的高度。西北风刮得很猛，海面上白浪滔滔。

===Original===
After a few miles of fairly clear weather the ceiling lowered to zero and for nearly two hours I flew entirely blind through the fog at an altitude of about 1,500 feet. Then the fog raised and the water was visible again.

===Chinese===
飞了几英里相当晴好的天气之后，云幕高度降到零，在约 1,500 英尺的高度上我全然盲飞，穿雾近两个小时。随后雾升高了，水面重新显露出来。

===Original===
On several more occasions it was necessary to fly by instrument for short periods; then the fog broke up into patches. These patches took on forms of every description. Numerous shorelines appeared, with trees perfectly outlined against the horizon. In fact, the mirages were so natural that, had I not been in mid-Atlantic and known that no land existed along my route, I would have taken them to be actual islands.

===Chinese===
又有几次不得不短暂改飞仪表；随后雾碎成一片一片。这些雾片形态千奇百怪，无数条海岸线浮现出来，树木衬着地平线轮廓分明。说实话，这些幻景逼真至极，倘若我不是身处大西洋中部、明知航线上没有任何陆地，准会把它们当成真正的岛屿。

===Original===
As the fog cleared I dropped down closer to the water, sometimes flying within ten feet of the waves and seldom higher than two hundred.

===Chinese===
雾一散，我就下降得更贴近水面，有时在离浪十英尺以内飞行，很少高于二百英尺。

===Original===
There is a cushion of air close to the ground or water through which a plane flies with less effort than when at a higher altitude, and for hours at a time I took advantage of this factor.

===Chinese===
贴近地面或水面有一层气垫，飞机在其中飞行比在高空省力，我一连几个小时都在利用这个有利条件。

===Original===
Also, it was less difficult to determine the wind drift near the water. During the entire flight the wind was strong enough to produce white caps on the waves. When one of these formed, the foam would be blown off, showing the wind's direction and approximate velocity. This foam remained on the water long enough for me to obtain a general idea of my drift.

===Chinese===
另外，贴近水面时也较容易判断风致偏流。整个飞行途中，风力之强足以在浪尖掀起白沫。白帽浪一形成，泡沫便被吹离浪头，显出风向和大概的风速。这些泡沫在水面上留存的时间，足够我对自己的偏流形成一个大致的概念。

===Original===
During the day I saw a number of porpoises and a few birds but no ships, although I understand that two different boats reported me passing over.

===Chinese===
白天我看见了许多鼠海豚和几只鸟，却没有看见船——虽然我听说有两艘不同的船报告我从它们上空飞过。

===Original===
The first indication of my approach to the European Coast was a small fishing boat which I first noticed a few miles ahead and slightly to the south of my course. There were several of these fishing boats grouped within a few miles of each other.

===Chinese===
我逼近欧洲海岸的第一个迹象，是一条小小的渔船，最初出现在前方几英里、航线路偏南的地方。这样的渔船有好几条，彼此相距不过几英里，聚在一处。

===Original===
I flew over the first boat without seeing any signs of life. As I circled over the second, however, a man's face appeared, looking out of the cabin window.

===Chinese===
我飞过第一条船，没有见到任何人的迹象。可当我绕着第二条船盘旋时，一张人脸出现了，正从舱窗里向外张望。

===Original===
I have carried on short conversations with people on the ground by flying low with throttled engine, shouting a question, and receiving the answer by some signal. When I saw this fisherman I decided to try to get him to point towards land. I had no sooner made the decision than the futility of the effort became apparent. In all likelihood he could not speak English, and even if he could he would undoubtedly be far too astounded to answer. However, I circled again and closing the throttle as the plane passed within a few feet of the boat I shouted, "Which way is Ireland?" Of course the attempt was useless, and I continued on my course.

===Chinese===
我以前曾用这样的办法与地上的人作过简短的交谈：收小油门低飞，喊出问题，再靠某种信号收到回答。见到这个渔夫时，我决定试试让他指一指陆地的方向。可这个念头刚起，其徒劳便显而易见了。十有八九他不会讲英语；即便会，也必定惊得目瞪口呆，无从答起。不过我还是又绕了一圈，当飞机在离船几英尺处掠过时，我收了油门，喊道："爱尔兰在哪个方向？"这番尝试当然毫无用处，我只好继续赶路。

===Original===
Less than an hour later a rugged and semi-mountainous coastline appeared to the northeast. I was flying less than two hundred feet from the water when I sighted it. The shore was fairly distinct and not over ten or fifteen miles away. A light haze coupled with numerous local storm areas had prevented my seeing it from a long distance.

===Chinese===
不到一小时之后，一条嶙峋而半多山的海岸线出现在东北方。望见它时，我正飞在离水面不足二百英尺的高度。海岸相当清晰，距离不过十或十五英里。一层薄霾加上多处局部风暴区，使我无法从远处望见它。

===Original===
The coastline came down from the north, curved over towards the east. I had very little doubt that it was the southwestern end of Ireland but in order to make sure I changed my course towards the nearest point of land.

===Chinese===
海岸线从北方延伸下来，向东弯去。我几乎不怀疑它就是爱尔兰的西南端，但为了确证，我朝最近的陆地点改了航向。

===Original===
I located Cape Valentia and Dingle Bay, then resumed my compass course towards Paris.

===Chinese===
我辨认出瓦伦西亚角（Cape Valentia）和丁格尔湾（Dingle Bay），随后恢复飞往巴黎的罗盘航向。

===Original===
After leaving Ireland I passed a number of steamers and was seldom out of sight of a ship.

===Chinese===
离开爱尔兰后，我飞越过许多艘轮船，很少有看不见船的时候。

===Original===
In a little over two hours the coast of England appeared. My course passed over Southern England and a little south of Plymouth; then across the English Channel, striking France over Cherbourg.

===Chinese===
两个多小时后，英格兰的海岸出现了。我的航线飞越英格兰南部，在普利茅斯（Plymouth）略偏南处过海；随后横穿英吉利海峡，在瑟堡（Cherbourg）一带进入法国。

===Original===
The English farms were very impressive from the air in contrast to ours in America. They appeared extremely small and unusually neat and tidy with their stone and hedge fences.

===Chinese===
与我们在美国的农场相比，英国的农场从空中看去令人印象深刻。它们显得极小，却又异常整洁，石砌的围篱和树篱齐齐整整。

===Original===
I was flying at about a fifteen hundred foot altitude over England and as I crossed the Channel and passed over Cherbourg, France, I had probably seen more of that part of Europe than many native Europeans. The visibility was good and the country could be seen for miles around.

===Chinese===
我以约一千五百英尺的高度飞越英格兰；等我渡过海峡、飞过法国瑟堡上空时，我对欧洲这一带的见识，恐怕已超过许多土生土长的欧洲人。能见度很好，四野之景尽收眼底，绵延数英里。

===Original===
People who have taken their first flight often remark that no one knows what the locality he lives in is like until he has seen it from above. Countries take on different characteristics from the air.

===Chinese===
初次飞行的人常常感叹：不从空中看一眼，谁也不知道自己住的地方究竟是什么模样。国家也从空中显出别样的面貌。

===Original===
The sun went down shortly after passing Cherbourg and soon the beacons along the Paris-London airway became visible.

===Chinese===
飞过瑟堡不久，太阳便落了，很快，巴黎—伦敦航线沿线的信标灯亮了起来，清晰可见。

===Original===
I first saw the lights of Paris a little before ten p.m., or five p.m. New York time, and a few minutes later I was circling the Eiffel Tower at an altitude of about four thousand feet.

===Chinese===
晚上将近十点、即纽约时间下午五点，我第一次望见巴黎的灯火；几分钟后，我在约四千英尺的高度绕着埃菲尔铁塔盘旋。

===Original===
The lights of Le Bourget were plainly visible, but appeared to be very close to Paris. I had understood that the field was farther from the city, so continued out to the northeast into the country for four or five miles to make sure that there was not another field farther out which might be Le Bourget. Then I returned and spiralled down closer to the lights. Presently I could make out long lines of hangars, and the roads appeared to be jammed with cars.

===Chinese===
勒布尔热机场的灯火清晰可见，但看去离巴黎非常近。我原以为机场离城更远，于是继续朝东北飞出城郊四五英里，确认更远处没有另一个可能是勒布尔热的机场。然后我折回来，朝灯光盘旋下降。不一会儿，我便认出一排排长长的机库，公路上看去堵满了汽车。

===Original===
I flew low over the field once, then circled around into the wind and landed.

===Chinese===
我在机场上空低掠了一遭，然后绕到迎风方向，降落到地面。

===Original===
After the plane stopped rolling I turned it around and started to taxi back to the lights. The entire field ahead, however, was covered with thousands of people all running towards my ship. When the first few arrived, I attempted to get them to hold the rest of the crowd back, away from the plane, but apparently no one could understand, or would have been able to conform to my request if he had.

===Chinese===
飞机停稳后，我把它转过头，开始朝灯光方向滑行。然而前方整个机场都布满了人，成千上万，全都朝我的飞机跑来。最先赶到的那几个人一到，我就试图让他们拦住后面的人群，让他们离飞机远些，但显然谁也听不懂；即便听得懂，也没人办得到。

===Original===
I cut the switch to keep the propeller from killing someone, and attempted to organize an impromptu guard for the plane. The impossibility of any immediate organization became apparent, and when parts of the ship began to crack from the pressure of the multitude I decided to climb out of the cockpit in order to draw the crowd away.

===Chinese===
我切断了电门，免得螺旋桨伤人性命，又试图临时组织一支飞机护卫队。当场组织之不可能旋即显露：人群挤压之下，机身部件开始噼啪开裂，我决定爬出座舱，把人群引开。

===Original===
Speaking was impossible; no words could be heard in the uproar and nobody apparently cared to hear any. I started to climb out of the cockpit, but as soon as one foot appeared through the door I was dragged the rest of the way without assistance on my part.

===Chinese===
说话是不可能的；喧嚣之中什么话也听不见，而且显然也没人想听。我开始往座舱外爬，可我的脚刚从舱门口露出来，就身不由己地被拽了出去。

===Original===
For nearly half an hour I was unable to touch the ground, during which time I was ardently carried around in what seemed to be a very small area, and in every position it is possible to be in. Everyone had the best of intentions but no one seemed to know just what they were.

===Chinese===
