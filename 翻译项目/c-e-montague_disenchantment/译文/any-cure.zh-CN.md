## XV Any Cure? / 十五 可有良方？

### I / 一

===Original===
How shall it all be set right? For it must be, of course. A people that did not wait to be pushed off its seat by the Kaiser is not likely now to turn its face to the wall and die inertly of shortage of faith and general moral debility. Some day soon we shall have to cease squatting among the potsherds and crabbing each other, and give all the strength we have left to the job of regaining the old control of ourselves and our fate which, in the days of our health, could only be kept by putting forth constantly the whole force of the will. “Not to be done,” you may say. And, of course, it will be a miracle. But only the everyday miracle done in somebody’s body, or else in his soul. When the skin shines white and tight over the joints, and the face is only a skull with some varieties of expression, and the very flame flickers and jumps in the lamp, the body will bend itself up to expel a disease that it could not, in all its first splendour of health, keep from the door. In all the breeds of cowardly livers﻿—drunkards, thieves, liars, sorners, drug-takers, all the kinds that have run from the enemy, throwing away as they ran every weapon that better men use to repel him﻿—you will find some that turn in the end and rend with their bare hands the fiend that they could not face with their bow and their spear.

===Chinese===
这一切该如何拨回正轨？因为那当然是必须做到的。一个当初不曾坐等德皇（Kaiser）来把自己推下座位的民族，如今也不至于转身面壁，因信仰匮乏与全面的道德衰弱而惰怠地死去。不久之后的某一天，我们就得停止蹲坐在碎瓦之间互相怨詈，把仅剩的全部气力都投入那件工作：重新赢回我们对自身与命运的旧日掌控——在健康的日子里，这份掌控唯有靠不断使出意志的全部力量方能维持。“办不到，”你也许会说。当然，那会是一个奇迹。但不过是发生在某人身体里、或者他灵魂里的那种日日常见的奇迹。当皮肤在关节处绷得发白发亮，当面孔只剩一具尚有几种表情的骷髅，当连灯里的火苗本身也摇曳跳动起来，身体却会奋然弓起，把一种疾病驱逐出去——想当年它以全盛的健康，尚且未能把这种病挡在门外。在一切懦怯度日的品类——酒徒、窃贼、说谎者、吃白食的、吞麻醉药的，所有那些临敌而逃、一路把较好的人用以御敌的件件武器都抛却的人——之中，你总会发现有一些到了最后掉转头来，赤手空拳，撕碎那个他们当年持弓仗矛也不敢面对的恶魔。

===Original===
But these recoveries only come upon terms: no going back to heaven except through a certain purgatorial passage. There, while it lasts, the invalid must not expect to enjoy either the heady visions of the fever that is now taking its leave or the more temperate beatitude of the health that may presently come. He lies reduced to animal, almost vegetable, matter, quite joyless and unthrilled, and has to abide in numb passivity, like an unborn child’s, whatever may come of the million minute molecular changes going on unseen in the enigmatic darkness of his tissues, where tiny cell is adding itself to tiny cell to build he knows not what. And then some day the real thing, the second birth as wonderful as the first, comes of itself and the stars are singing together all right and the sons of God shouting for joy. The same way with the spirit, except that the body faints, and so is eased, at some point in any rising scale of torment: the spirit has to go on through the mill without such anaesthetics as fainting. So the man who has gone far off the rails in matters of conduct, and tries to get back to them, has such hells of patience to live through, and out of, as no liquid fire known to the war chemists could make for the flesh. To possess your soul in patience, with all the skin and some of the flesh burnt off your face and hands, is a job for a boy compared with the pains of a man who has lived pretty long in the exhilarating world that drugs or strong waters seem to create and is trying to live now in the first bald desolation created by knocking them off, the time in which

> The dulled heart feels
> That somewhere, sealed with hopeless seals,
> The unmeaning heaven about him reels,
> And he lies hurled
> Beyond the roar of all the wheels
> Of all the world.

===Chinese===
但这些康复都是附有条件的：不经一段炼狱般的历程，便无路重回天国。在那段历程持续期间，病人既别指望享受正在告退的高烧所赐的醉人幻象，也别指望领略或许不久将至的健康那种较为平和的至福。他卧着，被削减为动物性、几乎植物性的物质，全无欢愉，全无悸动，只能麻木而被动地承受——像未出世的胎儿那般——任凭千百万次微细的分子变化在他组织幽暗难测的深处悄然进行，小小的细胞正把自己叠加到小小的细胞之上，建造着连他自己也无从知晓的东西。然后某一天，真正的康复——与第一次诞生同样奇妙的第二次诞生——自行降临，晨星确然一同歌唱，神的众子也都欢呼。灵魂的康复也是同一回事，只除了一点：在任何步步升级的酷刑之中，身体到了某个节点便会昏厥，并由此得到宽解；灵魂却必须在没有昏厥这类麻醉的情况下，硬生生闯过磨坊的全程。所以，一个在行为上脱轨太远而试图归轨的人，必得活着熬过并且活着走出一座座忍耐的地狱——战时化学家所知的任何液火（liquid fire），都无法为肉体造出这样的地狱。与那样一种人的疼痛相比，带着烧掉了全部皮肤和几分皮肉的脸与手而“常存忍耐，保全灵魂”，不过是小孩子的活计：那种人在麻醉品或烈酒（strong waters）似乎营造的令人亢奋的世界里活了颇久，如今正努力活在戒掉它们之后最初那片光秃秃的荒凉里，活在那样的一段时日里——

> 迟钝的心只觉得：
> 在某处，盖着无望的封印，
> 无意义的天穹绕着他晕眩，
> 他被远远掷倒，
> 抛在这世界一切车轮的
> 轰鸣之外。

===Original===
And yet no other way out. Disease and imbecility and an early and ignoble death, or else that stoic facing, through interminable days, of an easily escapable dullness that may be anything from an ache up to an agony.

===Chinese===
然而此外别无出路。要么是疾病、痴愚，加一场早至而不体面的死亡；要么便是斯多噶式的直面——在一个个无尽的白昼里，直面一种本可轻易脱身的沉闷，它轻则如隐痛，重则如剧痛。

### II / 二

===Original===
That is about where we stand as a nation. Of course, a few fortunates mailed in a happy, indefeasible genius of wonder and delight at everything round them are all right. And so are a few clods of whole-hog insensibility. Most of us, on the whole, find that effort is less fun than it was, and many things somewhat dull that used to sparkle with interest; the salt has lost, not all, but some of its savour; the grasshopper is a bit of a burden; old hobbies of politics, social causes, liberal comradeships, the loves and wars of letters and art, which used to excite, look at times as if they might only have been, at the best, rather a much ado about nothing; buzzing about our heads there come importunate suspicions that much of what we used to do so keenly was hardly worth doing, and that the dim, far goals we used to struggle towards were only possibly worth trying for and are, anyhow, out of reach now. That is the somewhat sick spirit’s condition. The limp apathy that we see at elections, the curious indifference in presence of public wrongs and horrors, the epidemic of sneaking pilferage, the slackening of sexual self-control﻿—all these are symptomatic like the furred tongue, subnormal heat, and muddy eye.

===Chinese===
作为一个民族，我们的大致处境便是如此。当然，少数幸运儿身披一层快乐而不可剥夺的天才甲胄，对周遭一切都能惊奇与欣喜，他们安然无恙；少数感觉彻底迟钝的木头人，也安然无恙。而我们当中的大多数人，总的来看，发觉出力已不如从前有趣，许多旧日闪着兴味之光的事物多少显得乏味了；盐失了几分味——不是全失；蚱蜢也成了几分重担；政治、社会事业、自由的同道之谊、文字与艺术的爱憎与论战，这些曾令人振奋的旧日爱好，有时看上去充其量不过是一场无事忙；绕着我们头顶嗡嗡盘旋的，是种种纠缠不去的疑心：我们从前做得那么起劲的许多事，恐怕本不值得做；我们曾奋力以求的那些朦胧遥远的目标，至多是或许值得一试，而且无论如何，如今已够不着了。这便是一个抱恙灵魂的境况。选举中我们看到的有气无力的冷漠，面对公共的罪错与惨祸时那种古怪的漠然，偷偷摸摸的小偷小摸蔚成风气，性方面自我约束的松弛——这一切都是症候，好比厚腻的舌苔、偏低的体温、浑浊的眼神。

===Original===
Like the hard drinker next morning, we suffer a touch of Hamlet’s complaint, the malady of the dyspeptic soul, of indolent kings and of pampered youth before it has found any man’s work to try itself on﻿—

> How weary, stale, flat, and unprofitable
> To me are all the uses of the world!

===Chinese===
像豪饮者次日清晨那样，我们也染上了几分哈姆雷特（Hamlet）的宿疾——消化不良的灵魂的痼疾，怠惰君王的痼疾，也是尚未寻得任何男子汉的事业来一试身手的娇惯青春的痼疾——

> 在我看来，人世间的一切行止
> 是何等可厌、陈腐、乏味而无聊！

===Original===
Not the despair of the battered, vanquished, or oppressed, but the moping of the relaxed, the surfeited, or the morbid. Glad as we all were to be done with the war, its ends left even the strongest of us a little let down, as the ends of other long and intense excitements, good or bad, do. As Ibsen’s young woman out in search of thrills would have said, there were harps in the air during the war. Many of them were disagreeable in their timbre, but still they were harps. Since the war a good many of the weaker vessels have somehow failed to find harps in the air, though there are really plenty of them in full vibration. So they have run about looking for little pick-me-ups and nips of something mildly exciting to keep up to par their sagging sense of the adventuresomeness of life. Derby sweeps never had such a vogue; every kind of gamble has boomed; dealers in public entertainment have found that the rawest sensationalism pays better than ever﻿—anything that will give a fillip, any poor new-whisky fillip, to jaded nerves.

===Chinese===
这不是被摧折者、战败者或受压迫者的绝望，而是松弛者、饫足者或病态者的悒悒寡欢。我们人人都乐于同战争作个了断，但战争的收场，正如其他或善或恶的漫长而剧烈的亢奋的收场一样，也使我们当中最坚强的人感到几分颓落。用易卜生（Ibsen）笔下那位出门寻刺激的年轻女子的话来说：战争期间，空中有竖琴。其中许多架音色刺耳，但终究是竖琴。停战以来，相当一部分意志薄弱者，不知怎的再也寻不见空中的竖琴了，尽管满架竖琴分明都在全声振动。于是他们四处奔走，寻些小小的提神饮子和轻度兴奋的浅酌，好让他们下垂的人生历险感维持在及格线上。德比抽彩（Derby sweeps）从未这般风行；形形色色的赌事一派兴旺；公众娱乐的经营者们发现，最生猛的耸动噱头比以往任何时候都更能生财——什么都行，只要能给疲惫的神经来一下刺激，哪怕像新出的威士忌那样蹩脚的一下。

### III / 三

===Original===
Of course, life itself is all right. It never grows dull. All dullness is in the mind; it comes out thence and diffuses itself over everything round the dull person, and then he terms everything dull, and thinks himself the victim of the impact of dull things. In stupid rich people, in boys and girls deadeningly taught at dead-alive schools, in all disappointed weaklings and in declining nations, this loss of power to shed anything but dullness upon what one sees and hears is common enough. Second-rate academic people, Victorian official art, the French Second Empire drama, late Latin literature exhibit its ravages well. In healthy children, in men and women of high mental vitality, in places where any of the radioactivity of gifted teaching breaks out for a while, and in swiftly and worthily rising nations the mind is easily delighted and absorbed by almost any atom of ordinary experience and its relation to the rest. The wonder and beauty and humour of life go on just the same as ever whether Spain or Holland or Italy feel them or miss them; youth would somewhere hear the chimes at midnight with the stir they made in Shakespeare’s wits although all England were peopled for ages with dullards whose pastors and masters had trained them to find the divine Falstaffiad as dull as a thaw.

===Chinese===
当然，生活本身是没有问题的。它从不变得乏味。一切乏味都在心智里；它从那里生出来，弥散到乏味之人四周的万事万物上，于是他便把一切都称作乏味，还自命为乏味之物撞击之下的受害者。在愚蠢的富人身上，在死气沉沉的学校里被教得日渐麻木的男孩女孩身上，在一切失意的弱者身上，在衰颓中的民族身上，这种能力的丧失——只往所见所闻之上敷设乏味、再也敷不出别样东西的能力的丧失——相当常见。二流学人、维多利亚时代的官方艺术、法兰西第二帝国的戏剧、晚期拉丁文学，都很好地陈列着这场浩劫的创痕。而在健康的儿童身上，在心智活力充沛的男女身上，在天才教学的放射性得以短时喷发的地方，在迅速而体面地崛起的民族身上，心智几乎能被寻常经验的任何一个原子、以及它与其余一切的关联所愉悦、所摄住。生活的奇妙、美与幽默一如既往地进行着，无论西班牙、荷兰或意大利感受到它们，还是错失了它们；纵使整个英格兰千秋万代住满蠢人，他们的牧师与师长把他们训练得把神圣的“福斯塔夫剧”（Falstaffiad）看得像化雪天气一般乏味，青春总会在什么地方听见午夜的钟声，并感到那钟声曾在莎士比亚的机敏头脑里激起的悸动。

===Original===
It need not come to that. Sick as we are, we have still in reserve the last resource of the sick, that saving miracle of recuperative force with which I have bored you. To let the sick part of our soul just be still and recover; to make our alcoholized tissues just do their work long enough on plain water﻿—that, if we can but do it, is all the sweeping and garnishing needed to make us possible dwelling-places again for the vitalizing spirit of sane delight in whatever adventure befalls us. How, then, to do it? Not, I fancy, by any kind of powwow or palaver of congress, conference, general committee, subcommittee, or other expedient for talking in company instead of working alone. This is an individual’s job, and a somewhat lonely one, though a nation has to be saved by it. To get down to work, whoever else idles; to tell no lies, whoever else may thrive on their uses; to keep fit, and the beast in you down; to help any who need it; to take less from your world than you give it; to go without the old drams to the nerves﻿—the hero stunt, the sob story, all the darling liqueurs of war emotionalism, war vanity, war spite, war rant and cant of every kind; and to do it all, not in a sentimental mood of self-pity like some actor mounting in an empty theatre and thinking what treasures the absent audience has lost, but like a man on a sheep-farm in the mountains, as much alone and at peace with his work of maintaining the world as God was when he made it.

===Chinese===
事情未必就会落到那步田地。我们虽然病了，却还保有病人的最后一项资源，那救命的康复力奇迹——这一层我已经说得你们生厌了。让我们灵魂中患病的那部分只管静下来，自行复原；让我们酒精浸透的组织只在清水上勉力履职足够长的一段时间——只要我们做得到，这便是全部所需的洒扫与陈设，足以使我们重新成为合宜的居所，迎回那种赋生活力的、神志清明的欢愉精神，无论遭遇什么际遇都能乐在其中。那么，该怎么做呢？我想，决不是靠任何大会、会议、全体委员会、小组委员会式的帕瓦聚谈或冗长空议，或任何借群处清谈以逃避独自劳作的法子。这是个人的活计，而且是相当孤独的活计，尽管一个民族的得救正系于它。别人尽可闲荡，自己埋头苦干；别人尽可靠说谎得利，自己一句谎也不说；保持康健，压住体内的兽；帮助任何需要帮助的人；从你的世界取得的，少于你给予它的；不再给神经灌那些旧日的烈酒——英雄戏码、苦情戏，以及战争感伤主义、战争虚荣、战争怨毒、种种战争的狂言与伪善所酿的全部心爱甜酒；而且做这一切时，不要怀着自怜的感伤情绪，像某个登上空无一人的剧场舞台、暗想缺席的观众错失了多少瑰宝的演员；而要像山间牧羊场上的汉子，那样孤独，又那样与自己的工作相安——他维系着这个世界，一如上帝创造它时那般。

===Original===
You remember the little French towns which the pestle and mortar of war had so ground into dust, red and white, that each separate brick went back at last, dust to dust, to mix with the earth from which it had come. The very clay of them has to be put into moulds and fired again. To some such remaking of bricks, some shaping and hardening anew of the most elementary, plainest units of rightness in action, we have to get back. Humdrum decencies, patiently practised through millions of undistinguished lives, were the myriad bricks out of which all the advanced architecture of conduct was built﻿—the solemn temples of creeds, gorgeous palaces of romantic heroism, cloud-capped towers of patriotic exaltation. And now, just when there seems to be such a babble as never before about these grandiose structures, bricks have run short.

Something simple, minute, and obscure, wholly good and not pulled up at all, something almost atomic﻿—a grain of wheat, a thread of wool, a crystal of clean salt, figures best the kind of human excellence of which our world has now most need. We would seem to have plunged on too fast and too far, like boys who have taken to spouting six-syllabled words until they forget what they had learnt of the alphabet. The moral beauty of perfect contrition is preached to a beaten enemy by our Press while the vitals of England are rotting with unprecedented growths of venereal disease: an England of boundlessly advertised heroes and saints has ousted the England in which you would never, wherever you travelled, be given wrong change on a bus.

===Chinese===
你们还记得那些法国小镇吧：战争的杵与臼把它们碾磨得那样碎，红的白的，尽成尘土，以致每一块砖最终都尘归于尘，回到它所从来的泥土里去。连它们的泥料，也须重新入模，重新烧制。我们必须回到某种这样的重新制砖的工作上来，回到对行为之正当性那些最基本、最朴素单元的重新塑形与重新硬化上来。千百万默默无闻的生命耐心践行的平凡德行，曾是亿万块砖，一切高级的行为建筑皆由它们砌成——信条的庄严神殿，浪漫英雄主义的瑰丽宫殿，爱国豪情的入云高塔。而此刻，正当这些宏构巨制似乎前所未有地被众口喧腾地议论着，砖却短缺了。

某种简单的、细小的、不显眼的东西，全然的好，全然不事拔高，某种近乎原子性的东西——一粒麦子，一缕羊毛，一粒洁净的盐的结晶——最好地写照了今日我们的世界最需要的那种人的卓越。我们似乎猛进得太快太远，像一些惯于吐诵六音节大词的男孩，直到把字母表上学过的东西忘了个干净。我国的报界正向一个被打垮的敌人宣讲完美痛悔的道德之美，而英格兰的脏腑正因前所未见的性病赘瘤而溃烂：一个英雄与圣徒被无限炒作的英格兰，已经逐走了那个你无论旅行到哪里、都不会在公共汽车上被找错零钱的英格兰。

===Original===
The wise man saved his little city, “yet no man remembered that same poor man,” and no one had better take to this way of saving England if what he wants is public distinction. It will be a career as undistinguished as that of one of the extra corpuscles formed in the blood to enable a lowland man to live on Himalayan heights. Our best friends for a long time to come will not be any of the standing cynosures of reporters’ eyes; they will find a part of their satisfaction in being nobodies; assured of the truth of the saying that there is no limit to what a man can do so long as he does not care a straw who gets the credit for it. Working apart from the whole overblown world of war valuations, the scramble for honours earned and unearned, the plotting and jostling for front places on the stage and larger letters on the bill, the whole life that is commonly held up to admiration as great and enviable, they will live in a kind of retreat almost cloistral; plenty of work for the faculties, plenty of rest for the nerves, control for desire and atrophy for conceit. Hard?﻿—yes, but England is worth it.

===Chinese===
那智慧人救了他的小城，“却没有人记念那穷人”；谁若图的是公众的显名，就最好不要拣这条拯救英格兰的路。那将是一份同样无声无闻的履历，一如低地人血液中为使其能居于喜马拉雅高处而生出的那些额外红血球的履历。往后很长一段时日，我们最好的朋友，将不会是记者眼中那些常年的瞩目焦点；他们将在做无名之辈之中寻得一部分满足；他们确信那句格言的真确：只要一个人对功劳归于何人毫不在乎，他能做成的事便没有限度。置身于整个被吹胀的战争估值世界之外——那争抢挣得的与未挣得的荣衔、为台上的前排座位和海报上更大的字母而钩心斗角推推搡搡的世界，那整套通常被推尊为伟大而可羡的生活——之外，他们将过着一种近乎隐修的退隐生活：才干不乏工作，神经不乏歇息，欲望得其节制，自负归于萎缩。难吗？——难，但英格兰值得。

### IV / 四

===Original===
Among the mind’s powers is one that comes of itself to many children and artists. It need not be lost, to the end of his days, by anyone who has ever had it. This is the power of taking delight in a thing, or rather in anything, everything, not as a means to some other end, but just because it is what it is, as the lover dotes on whatever may be the traits of the beloved object. A child in the full health of his mind will put his hand flat on the summer turf, feel it, and give a little shiver of private glee at the elastic firmness of the globe. He is not thinking how well it will do for some game or to feed sheep upon. That would be the way of the wooer whose mind runs on his mistress’s money. The child’s is sheer affection, the true ecstatic sense of the thing’s inherent characteristics. No matter what the things may be, no matter what they are good or no good for, there they are, each with a thrilling unique look and feel of its own, like a face; the iron astringently cool under its paint, the painted wood familiarly warmer, the clod crumbling enchantingly down in the hands, with its little dry smell of the sun and of hot nettles; each common thing a personality marked by delicious differences.

===Chinese===
在心智的种种能力之中，有一种会自行来到许多儿童和艺术家身上。凡是曾经拥有过它的人，直到生命的尽头都不必将它失去。这便是对一件事物、或者毋宁说对任何事物、一切事物感到欢喜的能力——不是把事物当作通往其他目的的手段，而仅仅因为它就是它自己，一如恋爱中人疼爱着所爱对象的无论哪一样品性。一个心智全然健康的孩子会把手掌平摊在夏日的草皮上，去感觉它，为这颗星球弹性而坚实的触感而微微打一个只有自己知道的欢颤。他并不在想这块草地拿来做哪种游戏有多合适，或在上面牧羊有多合适。那样想的话，便是那种满脑子惦着情人钱财的追求者的路数。孩子的欢喜是纯粹的情感，是对事物固有禀性的真正出神的感知。无论事物可能是什么，无论它们有用无用，它们就在那里，各各带着一种令人战栗的、独一无二的模样与触感，像一张脸：铁器在漆皮下沉着一股清冽的凉，上过漆的木头则亲切地更暖几分；土块在掌心里迷人地散碎，散出阳光和烫荨麻那一小股干燥的气味；每一件寻常之物都是一个人格，以种种可意的差异为标记。

===Original===
This joy of an Adam new to the garden and just looking round is brought by the normal child to the things that he does as well as those that he sees. To be suffered to do some plain work with the real spade used by mankind can give him a mystical exaltation: to come home with his legs, as the French say, reentering his body from the fatigue of helping the gardener to weed beds sends him to sleep in the glow of a beatitude that is an end in itself. Then the paradoxes of conduct begin to twinkle into sight; sugar is good, but there is a time to refrain from taking it though you can; a lie will easily get you out of a scrape, and yet, strangely and beautifully, rapture possesses you when you have taken the scrape and left out the lie. Divine unreason, as little scrutable and yet as surely a friend as the star that hangs a lamp out from the Pole to show you the way across gorse-covered commons in Surrey. So he will toe the line of a duty, not with a mere release from dismay, but exultantly, with the fire and lifting of heart of the strong man and the bridegroom, feeling always the same secret and almost sensuous transport, while he suppresses a base impulse, that he felt when he pressed the warm turf with his hand or the crumbling clay trickled warm between his fingers.

===Chinese===
这种初入园林、方才举目四顾的亚当式的欢喜，正常的孩子不但带到他所见的事物上，也带到他所做的事情上。获准用人类真正使用的那种铁锹做一点平凡的活计，能给他一种神秘的昂扬：正如法国人所说，帮着园丁锄完草畦里的草，带着两条累得直往身体里缩的腿回到家，他会在一种自为其目的的至福光辉中入睡。于是行为的种种悖论开始闪烁着显形：糖是好的，但纵然拿得到，也有应当克制不去拿的时候；一句谎话能轻易把你从窘境里捞出来，然而，奇妙而美丽的是，当你把窘境吞下、把谎话撇开时，狂喜会占据你。神圣的非理性——它像从天极悬出一盏灯、为你照亮穿越萨里郡（Surrey）长满金雀花的公共荒地之路的那颗星一样难于参透，却也同样确然是朋友。于是他会去踏上一条责任的准绳，不是仅仅如释重负，而是意气昂扬，带着大力者与新郎的心火与心扬；在克制一个卑下冲动时，他始终怀着那同一种隐秘的、近乎肉感的销魂，一如他当初把手掌压上温热的草皮、或看着酥散的黏土在指间温热地簌簌流泻时所感到的。

===Original===
The right education, if we could find it, would work up this creative faculty of delight into all its branching possibilities of knowledge, wisdom, and nobility. Of all three it is the beginning, condition, or raw material. At present it almost seems to be the aim of the commonplace teacher to take it firmly away from any pupil so blessed as to possess it. How we all know the kind of public school master whose manner expresses breezy comradeship with the boys in facing jointly the boredom of admittedly beastly but still unavoidable lessons! And the assumption that life out of school is too dull to be faced without the aid of infinitely elaborated games! And the girl schools where it seems to be feared that evil must come in any space of free time in which neither a game nor a dance nor a concert nor a lecture with a lantern intervenes to rescue the girls from the presumed tedium of mere youth and health! Everywhere the assumption that simple things have failed; that anything like hardy mental living and looking about for oneself, to find interests, is destined to end ill; that the only hope is to keep up the full dose of drugs, to be always pulling and pushing, prompting and coaxing and tickling the youthful mind into condescending to be interested. You know the effects: the adolescent whose mind seems to drop when taken out of the school shafts, or at least to look round, utterly at a loss, with a plaintive appeal for a suggestion of something to do, some excitement to come, something to make it worth while to be alive on this dull earth. We saw the effects in our hapless brain work in the war.

===Chinese===
正当的教育，倘若我们寻得着它，本可把这种欢愉的创造能力，加工发展为知识、智慧与高贵的一切枝繁叶茂的可能。它是三者的开端、条件，或曰原料。而在今天，平庸教师的目标，几乎恰恰是把它从任何有幸拥有它的学生身上坚定地拿走。我们都认得那种公学（public school）的教师：他的做派透着与男孩们爽朗的同志情谊，合力共同面对那些公认乏味至极、却终究无可逃免的功课！还有那项假定：校门之外的生活太沉闷了，若无无限繁复的运动游戏之助，便无从面对！还有那些女子学校：似乎唯恐在任何一段自由时间里，若没有一场比赛、一次舞会、一场音乐会或一场带幻灯的讲课居间搭救，把女孩们从仅凭青春与健康便想当然会有的沉闷里解救出来，邪恶便必乘虚而入！到处都是这样的假定：简单的事物已经失灵；任何强健的心智生活、任何自己放眼寻觅兴味的做法，都注定落得糟糕下场；唯一的指望，是把足量的药物一直灌下去，永远连拉带推、又提示又哄劝又挠痒地撩动年轻的心智，求它屈尊俯就地对什么发生兴趣。效果如何，你们是知道的：那样养出来的青少年，一旦卸出学校的辕杆，心智便仿佛一落千丈，至少也会四下环顾、全然无措，哀声恳求有人提示一件可做的事、一场将至的兴奋、一样能让他在这颗沉闷的地球上活着还觉得值的东西。战争期间我们那些不幸的脑力劳作，已经让我们领教过这些效果了。

===Original===
But if we were to wait to save England till thousands of men and women brought up in this way see what they have lost and insist on a better fate for their children we might as well write England off as one with Tyre and Sidon already. Her case is too pressing. She cannot wait for big, slowly telling improvements in big institutions, although improvements must come. She has to be saved by a change in the individual temper. We each have to fall back, with a will, on the only way of life in which the sane simplicity of joy in plain things and in common rightness of action can be generated. Health of mind or body comes of doing wholesome things﻿—perhaps for a long time without joy in doing them, as the sick man lies chafing, eating the slops that are all he is fit for, or as the dipsomaniac drinks in weariness and depression the insipid water that is to save him. Then, on some great day, self-control may cease to be merely the sum of many dreary acts of abstention; it may take life again as an inspiriting force, both a warmth and a light, such as makes nations great.

===Chinese===
但是，倘若要等到成千上万这样教养出来的男女看清自己失去了什么、坚持要给子女一个较好的命运，才去拯救英格兰，那我们大可干脆把英格兰一笔勾销，与推罗（Tyre）和西顿（Sidon）一同销账了事。她的病情太紧迫了。她等不起庞大机构里那些收效迟缓的庞大改良，尽管改良终须到来。她的得救，只能靠个人心性的改变。我们每个人都得发愿退回那唯一的生活方式上去——唯有在其中，方能再生出对平凡事物、对行为之寻常正当的那种神志清明而单纯的欢愉。心或身的健康，来自做有益健康之事——也许有很长一段时间做得并无欢愉，正如病人一面烦躁地卧着，一面吃着仅堪其受用的稀糊；又如嗜酒狂在疲惫与抑郁中，喝下那终将救他的寡淡清水。然后，在某个盛大的日子，自我节制或许会不再只是许多可厌的克己行为的总和；它或许会重新活转过来，成为一种鼓舞人心的力量，既是暖又是光——使民族伟大的，正是这样的东西。
