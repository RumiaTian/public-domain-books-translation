## Foreword / 前言

===Original===
This book is not written with the idea of adding to or improving on the Conservative philosophy. Or of “bringing it up to date.” The ancient and tested truths that guided our Republic through its early days will do equally well for us. The challenge to Conservatives today is quite simply to demonstrate the bearing of a proven philosophy on the problems of our own time.

===Chinese===
本书的写作，无意于对保守主义（Conservative）哲学有所增补或改进，也无意于使之“与时俱进”。那些在共和国早期指引我们一路走来的古老而经过检验的真理，对我们同样适用。今天保守主义者面临的挑战，说到底就是证明一种已被验证的哲学与我们时代诸问题之间的关联。

===Original===
I should explain the considerations that led me to join in this effort. I am a politician, a United States Senator. As such, I have had an opportunity to learn something about the political instincts of the American people, I have crossed the length and breadth of this great land hundreds of times and talked with tens of thousands of people, with Democrats and Republicans, with farmers and laborers and businessmen. I find that America is fundamentally a Conservative nation. The preponderant judgment of the American people, especially of the young people, is that the radical, or Liberal, approach has not worked and is not working. They yearn for a return to Conservative principles.

===Chinese===
我应当说明，是什么考虑促使我参与这项工作。我是一名政治人物，一名美国参议员。正因如此，我有机会了解美国人民的政治直觉；我数百次纵横穿越这片伟大的国土，与数以万计的人交谈过——与民主党人和共和党人，与农民、劳工和商人。我发现，美国从根本上说是一个保守主义的国家。美国人民、尤其是年轻人的压倒性判断是：激进的路子——也就是自由派（Liberal）的路子——过去行不通，现在也行不通。他们渴望回归保守主义原则。

===Original===
At the same time, I have been in a position to observe firsthand how Conservatism is faring in Washington. And it is all too clear that in spite of a Conservative revival among the people the radical ideas that were promoted by the New and Fair Deals under the guise of Liberalism still dominate the councils of our national government.

===Chinese===
与此同时，我一直有机会就近观察保守主义在华盛顿的境遇。而再清楚不过的是：尽管人民中间出现了保守主义的复兴，但在自由主义旗号下由新政（New Deal）和公平政策（Fair Deal）推行的种种激进观念，仍然主宰着我们国家政府的议事决策。

===Original===
In a country where it is now generally understood and proclaimed that the people’s welfare depends on individual self reliance rather than on state paternalism, Congress annually deliberates over whether the *increase* in government welfarism should be small or large.

===Chinese===
在一个人民福祉系于个人自立而非国家家长主义（state paternalism）这一点已被普遍理解并宣示的国家里，国会却年复一年地讨论：政府福利主义（welfarism）的*增长*幅度应当小一些还是大一些。

===Original===
In a country where it is now generally understood and proclaimed that the federal government spends too much, Congress annually deliberates over whether to raise the federal budget by a few billion dollars or by many billion.

===Chinese===
在一个联邦政府花销过大这一点已被普遍理解并宣示的国家里，国会却年复一年地讨论：联邦预算该增加几十亿美元，还是增加几百亿美元。

===Original===
In a country where it is now generally understood and proclaimed that individual liberty depends on decentralized government, Congress annually deliberates over whether vigorous or halting steps should be taken to bring state government into line with federal policy.

===Chinese===
在一个个人自由有赖于权力分散的政府这一点已被普遍理解并宣示的国家里，国会却年复一年地讨论：应当采取大刀阔斧的行动还是迟疑踌躇的步骤，使州政府与联邦政策保持一致。

===Original===
In a country where it is now generally understood and proclaimed that Communism is an enemy bound to destroy us, Congress annually deliberates over means of “coexisting” with the Soviet Union.

===Chinese===
在一个共产主义是注定要毁灭我们的敌人这一点已被普遍理解并宣示的国家里，国会却年复一年地讨论与苏联“共存”的办法。

===Original===
And so the question arises: Why have American people been unable to translate their views into appropriate political action? Why should the nation’s underlying allegiance to Conservative principles have failed to produce corresponding deeds in Washington?

===Chinese===
于是问题来了：为什么美国人民一直无法把自己的看法转化为恰当的政治行动？这个国家对保守主义原则根深蒂固的信奉，为什么未能在华盛顿产生与之相称的作为？

===Original===
I do not blame my brethren in government, all of whom work hard and conscientiously at their jobs. I blame Conservatives﻿—ourselves﻿—myself. Our failure, as one Conservative writer has put it, is the failure of the Conservative demonstration. Though we Conservatives are deeply persuaded that our society is ailing, and know that Conservatism holds the key to national salvation﻿—and feel sure the country agrees with us﻿—we seem unable to demonstrate the practical relevance of Conservative principles to the needs of the day. We sit by impotently while Congress seeks to improvise solutions to problems that are not the real problems facing the country, while the government attempts to assuage imagined concerns and ignores the real concerns and real needs of the people.

===Chinese===
我不怪政府里的同僚，他们人人都在自己的岗位上勤勉尽责地工作。我要责怪的是保守主义者——我们自己——我自己。正如一位保守主义作家所言，我们的失败，是保守主义论证的失败。尽管我们保守主义者深信这个社会病了，深知保守主义掌握着国家得救的钥匙，也确信这个国家站在我们一边，我们却似乎无法证明保守主义原则与当下需要之间的切实关联。我们只能束手枯坐，眼看国会给一些并非国家真正面临的问题临时拼凑解决方案，眼看政府设法抚慰想象出来的忧虑，却无视人民真实的忧虑和真实的需要。

===Original===
Perhaps we suffer from an over-sensitivity to the judgments of those who rule the mass communications media. We are daily consigned by “enlightened” commentators to political oblivion: Conservatism, we are told, is out-of-date. The charge is preposterous and we ought boldly to say so. The laws of God, and of nature, have no dateline. The principles on which the Conservative political position is based have been established by a process that has nothing to do with the social, economic and political landscape that changes from decade to decade and from century to century. These principles are derived from the nature of man, and from the truths that God has revealed about His creation. Circumstances do change. So do the problems that are shaped by circumstances. But the principles that govern the solution of the problems do not. To suggest that the Conservative philosophy is out of date is akin to saying that the Golden Rule, or the Ten Commandments or Aristotle’s *Politics* are out of date. The Conservative approach is nothing more or less than an attempt to apply the wisdom and experience and the revealed truths of the past to the problems of today. The challenge is not to find new or different truths, but to learn how to apply established truths to the problems of the contemporary world. My hope is that one more Conservative voice will be helpful in meeting this challenge.

===Chinese===
或许我们是对大众传播媒体主宰者的评判过于敏感了。我们天天被“开明的”评论员打入政治遗忘的冷宫：保守主义，我们被告知，已经过时了。这种指控荒谬绝伦，我们理应大胆地这样说。上帝的律法和自然的律法没有时效期限。保守主义政治立场所依据的各项原则，其确立过程与那十年一变、百年一改的社会、经济、政治图景毫无关系。这些原则源自人的本性，源自上帝关于其造化所启示的真理。环境会变，由环境塑成的问题也会变，但支配这些问题之解决的原则不变。说保守主义哲学已经过时，无异于说金律、十诫或亚里士多德的《政治学》已经过时。保守主义的主张，无非就是试图把过去的智慧、经验和启示的真理应用于今天的问题。挑战不在于寻找新的或不同的真理，而在于学会如何把确立已久的真理应用于当代世界的问题。我的希望是，多一个保守主义的声音，将有助于应对这一挑战。

===Original===
This book is an attempt to bridge the gap between theory and practice. I shall draw upon my speeches, the radio and television broadcasts and the notes I have made over the years in the hope of doing what one is often unable to do in the course of a harried day’s work on the Senate floor: to show the connection between Conservative principles so widely espoused, and Conservative action, so generally neglected.

===Chinese===
本书就是弥合理论与实践之间鸿沟的一次尝试。我将利用我的演讲、广播电视讲话和多年积累的笔记，希望做到一件在参议院繁忙的日常工作中往往无暇去做的事：把人们广泛信奉的保守主义原则，与普遍受到忽视的保守主义行动，联系起来。

## I The Conscience of a Conservative / I 一个保守主义者的良心

===Original===
I have been much concerned that so many people today with Conservative instincts feel compelled to apologize for them. Or if not to apologize directly, to qualify their commitment in a way that amounts to breast-beating. “Republican candidates,” Vice President Nixon has said, “should be economic conservatives, but conservatives with a heart.” President Eisenhower announced during his first term, “I am conservative when it comes to economic problems but liberal when it comes to human problems.” Still other Republican leaders have insisted on calling themselves “progressive” Conservatives.1 These formulations are tantamount to an admission that Conservatism is a narrow, mechanistic *economic* theory that may work very well as a bookkeeper’s guide, but cannot be relied upon as a comprehensive political philosophy.

===Chinese===
我一直深感不安：今天有那么多人怀着保守主义的本能，却觉得不得不为此道歉。或者即使不直接道歉，也要以某种近乎捶胸自责的方式给自己的信念打折扣。「共和党候选人，」副总统尼克松说过，「在经济上应当是保守派，但要做有良心的保守派。」艾森豪威尔总统在第一届任期内宣布：「在经济问题上我是保守派，但在人的问题上我是自由派。」还有一些共和党领袖则坚称自己是「进步主义的」保守主义者1。这些说法无异于承认：保守主义是一种狭隘的、机械论的*经济*理论，或许可以充作簿记员的指南，却不能倚为一种完整的政治哲学。

===Original===
The same judgment, though in the form of an attack rather than an admission, is advanced by the radical camp. “We liberals,” they say, “are interested in *people*. Our concern is with human beings, while you Conservatives are preoccupied with the preservation of economic privilege and status.” Take them a step further, and the Liberals will turn the accusations into a class argument: it is the little people that concern us, not the “malefactors of great wealth.”

===Chinese===
同样的判断也出自激进阵营之口，只是采取的是攻击而非承认的形式。「我们自由派，」他们说，「关心的是*人*。我们关注的是活生生的人，而你们保守主义者念念不忘的只是维护经济特权和经济地位。」再进一步，自由派就会把这些指责变成一套阶级论调：我们关心的是小人物，而不是那些「家财万贯的恶棍」。

===Original===
Such statements, from friend and foe alike, do great injustice to the Conservative point of view. Conservatism is *not* an economic theory, though it has economic implications. The shoe is precisely on the other foot: it is Socialism that subordinates all other considerations to man’s material well-being. It is Conservatism that puts material things in their proper place﻿—that has a structured view of the human being and of human society, in which economics plays only a subsidiary role.

===Chinese===
这类说法，无论出自朋友还是敌人之口，都对保守主义观点极不公道。保守主义*并非*一种经济理论，尽管它有经济上的意涵。事实恰恰相反：把其余一切考量都隶属于人的物质福祉之下的，正是社会主义。把物质事物安放在其恰当位置的，才是保守主义——它对人和人类社会有一种成体系的看法，在这一看法中，经济只扮演从属性的角色。

===Original===
The root difference between the Conservatives and the Liberals of today is that Conservatives take account of the *whole* man, while the Liberals tend to look only at the material side of man’s nature. The Conservative believes that man is, in part, an economic, an animal creature; but that he is also a spiritual creature with spiritual needs and spiritual desires. What is more, these needs and desires reflect the *superior* side of man’s nature, and thus take precedence over his economic wants. Conservatism therefore looks upon the enhancement of man’s spiritual nature as the primary concern of political philosophy. Liberals, on the other hand﻿—in the name of a concern for “human beings”﻿—regard the satisfaction of economic wants as the dominant mission of society. They are, moreover, in a hurry. So that their characteristic approach is to harness the society’s political and economic forces into a collective effort to *compel* “progress.” In this approach, I believe they fight against Nature.

===Chinese===
今日保守主义者与自由派的根本分歧在于：保守主义者把人当作*完整的人*来考量，而自由派往往只看人的本性中的物质一面。保守主义者相信，人在一定程度上是一种经济性的、动物性的存在；但他同时是一种灵性的存在，有灵性的需要和灵性的渴望。更重要的是，这些需要与渴望反映的是人本性中*更高*的一面，因而优先于他的经济欲求。所以，保守主义把提升人的灵性本性视为政治哲学的首要关切。自由派则相反——打着关心「人」的旗号——把满足经济欲求当作社会压倒一切的使命。而且他们操之过急。于是他们的典型做法，就是驱使社会的政治力量与经济力量汇成一场集体的努力，去*强制*促成「进步」。我认为，在这条路上，他们是在与自然作对。

===Original===
Surely the first obligation of a political thinker is to understand the nature of man. The Conservative does not claim special powers of perception on this point, but he does claim a familiarity with the accumulated wisdom and experience of history, and he is not too proud to learn from the great minds of the past.

===Chinese===
政治思想家的第一要务，当然是理解人的本性。保守主义者并不自诩在这一点上有特别的洞察力，但他确实谙熟历史上积累的智慧与经验，而且他并不傲慢到不肯向往昔的伟大心灵学习。

===Original===
The first thing he has learned about man is that each member of the species is a unique creature. Man’s most sacred possession is his individual soul﻿—which has an immortal side, but also a mortal one. The mortal side establishes his absolute differentness from every other human being. *Only a philosophy that takes into account the essential differences between men, and, accordingly, makes provision for developing the different potentialities of each man can claim to be in accord with Nature.* We have heard much in our time about “the common man.” It is a concept that pays little attention to the history of a nation that grew great through the initiative and ambition of uncommon men. The Conservative knows that to regard man as part of an undifferentiated mass is to consign him to ultimate slavery.

===Chinese===
他关于人认识到的第一件事是：这个物种的每一个成员都是独一无二的存在。人最神圣的所有物是他个人的灵魂——灵魂有一面不朽，也有一面速朽。速朽的一面确立了他与任何其他人的绝对差异。*唯有一种哲学，它把人与人的本质差异考虑在内，并从而为发展每个人的不同潜能作出安排，才有资格宣称自己合乎自然。*我们这个时代听得太多的，是「普通人」这个概念。这个概念几乎无视一个国家的历史——这个国家正是靠不普通的人的进取心和抱负才变得伟大。保守主义者知道：把人视为无差别群体的一部分，就是把他交付给终极的奴役。

===Original===
Secondly, the Conservative has learned that the economic and spiritual aspects of man’s nature are inextricably intertwined. He cannot be economically free, or even economically efficient, if he is enslaved politically; conversely, man’s political freedom is illusory if he is dependent for his economic needs on the State.

===Chinese===
第二，保守主义者认识到，人的本性中经济的一面与灵性的一面盘根错节、不可分割。人若在政治上受奴役，就不可能有经济上的自由，甚至不可能有经济上的效率；反过来，人若在经济需要上依附于国家，其政治自由也只是幻影。

===Original===
The Conservative realizes, thirdly, that man’s development, in both its spiritual and material aspects, is not something that can be directed by outside forces. Every man, for his individual good and for the good of his society, is responsible for his *own* development. The choices that govern his life are choices that *he* must make: they cannot be made by any other human being, or by a collectivity of human beings. If the Conservative is less anxious than his Liberal brethren to increase Social Security “benefits,” it is because he is more anxious than his Liberal brethren that people be free throughout their lives to spend their earnings when and as they see fit.

===Chinese===
第三，保守主义者认识到：人的发展，无论其灵性方面还是物质方面，都不是外力所能指挥的东西。每一个人，为了他自己的利益，也为了他的社会的利益，要为他*自己的*发展负责。支配他一生的种种选择，必须由*他本人*作出：任何别的人不能代作，任何人的集体也不能代作。如果说保守主义者不像他的自由派同仁那样热衷于增加社会保障「福利」，那是因为他比自由派同仁更急切地希望：人们一生都能自由地按自己认为恰当的时机和方式支配自己的收入。

===Original===
So it is that Conservatism, throughout history, has regarded man neither as a potential pawn of other men, nor as a part of a general collectivity in which the sacredness and the separate identity of individual human beings are ignored. Throughout history, true Conservatism has been at war equally with autocrats and with “democratic” Jacobins. The true Conservative was sympathetic with the plight of the hapless peasant under the tyranny of the French monarchy. And he was equally revolted at the attempt to solve that problem by a mob tyranny that paraded under the banner of egalitarianism. The conscience of the Conservative is pricked by *anyone* who would debase the dignity of the individual human being. Today, therefore, he is at odds with dictators who rule by terror, and equally with those gentler collectivists who ask our permission to play God with the human race.

===Chinese===
正因如此，历史上的保守主义从来不把人视为他人潜在的棋子，也不把人视为某种笼统集体的一部分——在那样的集体里，个人神圣而独立的人格身份被置之不顾。纵观历史，真正的保守主义对独裁者和「民主的」雅各宾派同样地斗争不休。真正的保守主义者同情不幸的农民在法国君主暴政下的苦难，也同样憎恶借一场打着平均主义旗号招摇过市的暴民专制来解决那个问题的企图。凡有贬低个人人格尊严者，无论*何人*，都刺痛保守主义者的良心。因此，他今天既与靠恐怖统治的独裁者势不两立，也同样与那些温文尔雅、请求我们准许他们以上帝自居来摆布人类的集体主义者格格不入。

===Original===
With this view of the nature of man, it is understandable that the Conservative looks upon politics as the art of achieving the maximum amount of freedom for individuals that is consistent with the maintenance of social order. The Conservative is the first to understand that the practice of freedom requires the establishment of order: it is impossible for one man to be free if another is able to deny him the exercise of his freedom. But the Conservative also recognizes that the political power on which order is based is a self-aggrandizing force; that its appetite grows with eating. He knows that the utmost vigilance and care are required to keep political power within its proper bounds.

===Chinese===
基于这种对人性的看法，就不难理解：保守主义者把政治视为一门艺术——在维护社会秩序的前提下，为个人争得最大限度的自由。保守主义者比谁都清楚，自由的实践需要秩序的建立：如果一个人能够剥夺另一个人行使自由的权利，后者就不可能自由。但保守主义者也认识到，作为秩序之基础的政治权力是一种自我膨胀的力量；它的胃口越吃越大。他知道，要把政治权力约束在其正当界限之内，需要最大的警觉与审慎。

===Original===
In our day, order is pretty well taken care of. The delicate balance that ideally exists between freedom and order has long since tipped against freedom practically everywhere on earth. In some countries, freedom is altogether down and order holds absolute sway. In our country the trend is less far advanced, but it is well along and gathering momentum every day. Thus, for the American Conservative, there is no difficulty in identifying the day’s overriding political challenge: it is *to preserve and extend freedom*. As he surveys the various attitudes and institutions and laws that currently prevail in America, many questions will occur to him, but the Conservative’s first concern will always be: *Are we maximizing freedom?* I suggest we examine some of the critical issues facing us today with this question in mind.

===Chinese===
在我们这个时代，秩序已经相当有保障了。自由与秩序之间理想存在的微妙平衡，在地球上几乎每一个地方都早已向不利于自由的一侧倾斜。在某些国家，自由已完全倒下，秩序占据绝对主宰。在我们国家，这一趋势走得没那么远，但已行之颇深，且日日加速。因此，对美国保守主义者来说，认清当今压倒一切的政治挑战毫无困难：那就是*维护并扩展自由*。当他审视当今美国流行的种种观念、制度和法律时，许多问题会浮上他的心头，但保守主义者的第一关切永远是：*我们是在把自由最大化吗？*我建议我们怀着这个问题，来考察今天摆在我们面前的一些关键议题。

## II The Perils of Power / II 权力的危险

===Original===
The New Deal, Dean Acheson wrote approvingly in a book called *A Democrat Looks at His Party*, “conceived of the federal government as the whole people organized to do what had to be done.” A year later Mr. Larson wrote *A Republican Looks at His Party*, and made much the same claim in his book for Modern Republicans. The “underlying philosophy” of the New Republicanism, said Mr. Larson, is that “if a job has to be done to meet the needs of the people, and no one else can do it, then it is the proper function of the federal government.”

===Chinese===
迪安·艾奇逊曾在一本题为《一个民主党人看他的党》（*A Democrat Looks at His Party*）的书中表示赞许地写道：新政「把联邦政府设想为组织起来去做必须做之事的全体人民」。一年后，拉森先生写了《一个共和党人看他的党》，在书中为现代共和党人（Modern Republicans）提出了大体相同的主张。拉森先生说，新共和主义的「根本哲学」是：「如果一件事必须完成以满足人民的需要，而没有其他人能做，那么它就是联邦政府的正当职能。」

===Original===
Here we have, by prominent spokesmen of both political parties, an unqualified repudiation of the principle of limited government. There is no reference by either of them to the Constitution, or any attempt to define the legitimate functions of government. The government can do whatever *needs* to be done; note, too, the implicit but necessary assumption that it is the government itself that determines *what* needs to be done. We must not, I think underrate the importance of these statements. They reflect the view of a majority of the leaders of one of our parties, and of a strong minority among the leaders of the other, and they propound the first principle of totalitarianism: that the State is competent to do all things and is limited in what it actually does only by the will of those who control the State.

===Chinese===
这便是两党的显赫发言人同声作出的对有限政府原则的无保留否定。两人都没有提宪法（the Constitution），也没有尝试界定政府的正当职能。政府可以做任何*需要*做的事；还请注意其中隐含却必然的假定：究竟什么事*需要*做，由政府自己决定。我认为，我们切不可低估这些言论的分量。它们反映着一个党的大多数领袖、以及另一个党内一个强大少数派的观点，它们道出的是极权主义的第一原则：国家有能力做一切事情，其实际所为仅受掌国者意志的限制。

===Original===
It is clear that this view is in direct conflict with the Constitution which is an instrument, above all, for *limiting* the functions of government, and which is as binding today as when it was written. But we are advised to go a step further and ask why the Constitution’s framers restricted the scope of government. Conservatives are often charged, and in a sense rightly so, with having an overly mechanistic view of the Constitution: “It is America’s enabling document; we are American citizens; therefore,” the Conservatives’ theme runs, “we are morally and legally obliged to comply with the document.” All true. But the Constitution has a broader claim on our loyalty than that. The founding fathers had a *reason* for endorsing the principle of limited government; and this reason recommends defense of the constitutional scheme even to those who take their citizenship obligations lightly. The reason is simple, and it lies at the heart of the Conservative philosophy.

===Chinese===
显然，这种观点与宪法直接冲突——宪法首先是一件*限制*政府职能的工具，而且它在今天一如成文之日般有效约束着我们。但有人建议我们再进一步，问一问：宪法的起草者们为什么要限制政府的权力范围？人们常常指责保守主义者——从某种意义上说并非无理——对宪法抱有一种过于机械的看法：「宪法是美国的立国文件；我们是美国公民；因此，」保守主义者的论式是，「我们在道德上和法律上都有义务遵守这份文件。」说得都对。但宪法对我们忠诚的要求不止于此。开国先贤们赞成有限政府原则是有*理由*的；这个理由，甚至对那些并不把公民义务当回事的人，也为捍卫宪政体制提供了论据。理由很简单，它就在保守主义哲学的核心处。

===Original===
Throughout history, government has proved to be the chief instrument for thwarting man’s liberty. Government represents power in the hands of some men to control and regulate the lives of other men. And power, as Lord Acton said, *corrupts* men. “Absolute power,” he added, “corrupts absolutely.”

===Chinese===
纵观历史，政府已被证明是挫败人的自由的首要工具。政府意味着一部分人手中的权力，用以控制和规约另一部分人的生活。而权力，正如阿克顿勋爵所说，会*腐蚀*人。「绝对权力，」他补充道，「导致绝对腐败。」

===Original===
State power, considered in the abstract, need not restrict freedom: but absolute state power always does. The *legitimate* functions of government are actually conducive to freedom. Maintaining internal order, keeping foreign foes at bay, administering justice, removing obstacles to the free interchange of goods﻿—the exercise of these powers makes it possible for men to follow their chosen pursuits with maximum freedom. But note that the very instrument by which these desirable ends are achieved *can* be the instrument for achieving undesirable ends﻿—that government can, instead of extending freedom, restrict freedom. And note, secondly, that the “can” quickly becomes “will” the moment the holders of government power are left to their own devices. This is because of the corrupting influence of power, the natural tendency of men who possess *some* power to take unto themselves *more* power. The tendency leads eventually to the acquisition of *all* power﻿—whether in the hands of one or many makes little difference to the freedom of those left on the outside.

===Chinese===
抽象地看，国家权力未必限制自由：但绝对的国家权力必定如此。政府的*正当*职能实际上有助于自由。维持内部秩序、抵御外敌、执行司法、扫除商品自由流通的障碍——行使这些权力，使人得以在最大自由中从事自己选定的事业。但请注意，达成这些可欲目标的工具，*同样可能*成为达成不可欲目标的工具——政府能够不扩展自由，反而限制自由。其次还要注意，一旦政府权力的执掌者可以为所欲为，这个「能够」很快就变成「将会」。这是因为权力有腐蚀作用，因为握有*某些*权力的人自然而然地倾向于为自己攫取*更多*权力。这种倾向最终导致*一切*权力的集中——权力握于一人之手还是多人之手，对被排除在外者的自由来说没什么两样。

===Original===
Such, then, is history’s lesson, which Messrs. Acheson and Larson evidently did not read: release the holders of state power from any restraints other than those they wish to impose upon themselves, and you are swinging down the well-travelled road to absolutism.

===Chinese===
这便是历史的教训——艾奇逊和拉森两位先生显然没有读过：解除对国家权力执掌者的一切约束，只留下他们愿意加于自身的约束，你就是在沿着那条通往专制主义的老路疾驰而下。

===Original===
The framers of the Constitution had learned the lesson. They were not only students of history, but victims of it: they knew from vivid, personal experience that freedom depends on effective restraints against the accumulation of power in a single authority. And that is what the Constitution is: *a system of restraints against the natural tendency of government to expand in the direction of absolutism*. We all know the main components of the system. The first is the limitation of the federal government’s authority to specific, delegated powers. The second, a corollary of the first, is the reservation to the States and the people of all power not delegated to the federal government. The third is a careful division of the federal government’s power among three separate branches. The fourth is a prohibition against impetuous alteration of the system﻿—namely, Article V’s tortuous, but wise, amendment procedures.

===Chinese===
宪法的起草者们学到了这个教训。他们不仅是历史的学生，更是历史的受害者：他们从切身的鲜活经验中知道，自由有赖于对权力向单一权威集中的有效约束。宪法正是这样一件东西：*一套约束机制，用以对抗政府朝专制主义方向膨胀的天性*。这套机制的主要构成，我们都清楚。第一，把联邦政府的权限定在若干明确授予的权力之内。第二，作为第一条的推论，凡未授予联邦政府的权力，一概保留给各州和人民。第三，把联邦政府的权力审慎地分配给三个相互独立的部门。第四，禁止对这套体制的轻率变更——也就是宪法第五条那套曲折而明智的修宪程序。

===Original===
Was it then a *Democracy* the framers created? Hardly. The system of restraints, on the face of it, was directed not only against individual tyrants, but also against a tyranny of the masses. The framers were well aware of the danger posed by self-seeking demagogues﻿—that they might persuade a majority of the people to confer on government vast powers in return for deceptive promises of economic gain. And so they forbade such a transfer of power﻿—first by declaring, in effect, that certain activities are outside the natural and legitimate scope of the public authority, and secondly by dispersing public authority among several levels and branches of government in the hope that each seat of authority, jealous of its own prerogatives, would have a natural incentive to resist aggression by the others.

===Chinese===
那么，起草者们创立的是一个*民主政体*吗？很难说是。这套约束机制，就其表面而言，不仅针对个体的暴君，也针对多数人的暴政。起草者们深知营私煽动家的危险——他们可能以经济利益的虚假许诺为交换，说服人民中的多数授予政府巨大的权力。因此他们禁止了这种权力转移——首先，实际上是宣告某些活动处在公共权威的自然而正当的范围之外；其次，把公共权威分散到政府的若干层级和部门，指望每一处权威出于对自身特权的珍视，天然有抵制他人侵权的动力。

===Original===
But the framers were not visionaries. They knew that rules of government, however brilliantly calculated to cope with the imperfect nature of man, however carefully designed to avoid the pitfalls of power, would be no match for men who were determined to disregard them. In the last analysis their system of government would prosper only if the governed were sufficiently determined that it should. “What have you given us?” a woman asked Ben Franklin toward the close of the Constitutional Convention. “A Republic,” he said, “*if you can keep it*!”

===Chinese===
但起草者们并非空想家。他们知道，政府的规则，无论设计得多么精巧以应对人性之不完美，无论构思得多么周密以避开权力的陷阱，在执意无视规则的人面前都不堪一击。归根结底，他们的政府体制能否长存，取决于被治者是否有足够的决心让它长存。「你们给了我们什么？」制宪会议临近结束时，一位妇女问本·富兰克林。「一个共和国，」他说，「*只要你们能守住它*！」

===Original===
We have not kept it. The Achesons and Larsons have had their way. The system of restraints has fallen into disrepair. The federal government has moved into every field in which it believes its services are needed. The state governments are either excluded from their rightful functions by federal preemption, or they are allowed to act at the sufferance of the federal government. Inside the federal government both the executive and judicial branches have roamed far outside their constitutional boundary lines. And all of these things have come to pass without regard to the amendment procedures prescribed by Article V. The result is a Leviathan, a vast national authority out of touch with the people, and out of their control. This monolith of power is bounded only by the will of those who sit in high places.

===Chinese===
我们没有守住它。艾奇逊们和拉森们得逞了。约束机制已年久失修。联邦政府侵入了它认为需要其服务的每一个领域。各州政府要么被联邦的优先立法挤出其正当职能，要么只能仰联邦政府之鼻息行事。在联邦政府内部，行政和司法两个部门都已远远越出各自的宪法边界。而这一切的发生，全然无视第五条规定的修宪程序。结果便是一个利维坦（Leviathan）——一个庞大、脱离人民亦不受人民控制的全国性权力。这个庞然权力所受的唯一限制，只剩下高居其位者的意志。

===Original===
There are a number of ways in which the power of government can be measured.

===Chinese===
衡量政府权力的大小，有若干种办法。

===Original===
One is the size of its financial operations. Federal spending is now approaching a hundred billion dollars a year (compared with three and one-half billion less than three decades ago.)

===Chinese===
一是其财政活动的规模。联邦开支如今正逼近每年一千亿美元（而不到三十年前只有三十五亿美元）。

===Original===
Another is the scope of its activities. A study recently conducted by the *Chicago Tribune* showed that the federal government is now the “biggest land owner, property manager, renter, mover and hauler, medical clinician, lender, insurer, mortgage broker, employer, debtor, taxer and spender in all history.”

===Chinese===
二是其活动的范围。《芝加哥论坛报》（*Chicago Tribune*）最近的一项研究表明：联邦政府如今是「有史以来最大的土地所有者、物业管理者、承租人、搬运与运输承揽者、临床医疗者、放贷者、保险人、按揭经纪人、雇主、债务人、征税者和花钱者」。

===Original===
Still another is the portion of the peoples’ earnings government appropriates for its own use: nearly a third of earnings are taken every year in the form of taxes.

===Chinese===
三是政府为自身用途而征用的民财份额：每年有将近三分之一的收入以税收形式被拿走。

===Original===
A fourth is the extent of government interference in the daily lives of individuals. The farmer is told how much wheat he can grow. The wage earner is at the mercy of national union leaders whose great power is a direct consequence of federal labor legislation. The businessman is hampered by a maze of government regulations, and often by direct government competition. The government takes six percent of most payrolls in Social Security Taxes and thus compels millions of individuals to postpone until later years the enjoyment of wealth they might otherwise enjoy today. Increasingly, the federal government sets standards of education, health and safety.

===Chinese===
四是政府干预个人日常生活的程度。农民被告知可以种多少小麦。工薪者的命运操在全国性工会领袖手里，而后者的大权正是联邦劳工立法的直接产物。工商业者被政府规章的重重迷宫所困，还常常遭遇政府的直接竞争。政府以社会保障税的形式拿走多数工薪总额的百分之六，从而迫使千百万个人把本可今天享有的财富推迟到日后年份去享受。联邦政府还越来越多地为教育、卫生和安全设定标准。

===Original===
How did it happen? How did our national government grow from a servant with sharply limited powers into a master with virtually unlimited power?

===Chinese===
这是怎么发生的？我们的全国政府如何从一个权力受到严格限制的仆人，长成了一个权力几乎不受限制的主人？

===Original===
In part, we were swindled. There are occasions when we have elevated men and political parties to power that promised to restore limited government and then proceeded, after their election, to expand the activities of government. But let us be honest with ourselves. Broken promises are not the major causes of our trouble. *Kept* promises are. All too often we have put men in office who have suggested spending a little more on this, a little more on that, who have proposed a new welfare program, who have thought of another variety of “security.” We have taken the bait, preferring to put off to another day the recapture of freedom and the restoration of our constitutional system. We have gone the way of many a democratic society that has lost its freedom by persuading itself that if “the people” rule, all is well.

===Chinese===
在一定程度上，我们是被骗了。我们有时把许诺恢复有限政府的人和政党推上台，而他们当选后却变本加厉地扩张政府活动。但我们也要对自己诚实。背弃的诺言并不是我们困境的主因。*兑现的*诺言才是。我们太经常地把这样的人选进公职：他们建议在这上面多花一点、在那上面多花一点，他们提出新的福利计划，他们又想出一种新的「保障」。我们咬了钩，宁愿把夺回自由、恢复宪政体制的日子一天天往后推。我们走上了许多民主社会的老路——它们说服自己：只要「人民」在统治，一切就都好。

===Original===
The Frenchman, Alexis de Tocqueville, probably the most clairvoyant political observer of modern times, saw the danger when he visited this country in the 1830s. Even then he foresaw decay for a society that tended to put more emphasis on its democracy than on its republicanism. He predicted that America would produce, not tyrants but “guardians.” And that the American people would “console themselves for being in tutelage by the reflection that they have chosen their own guardians. Every man allows himself to be put in lead-strings, because he sees that it is not a person nor a class of persons, but the people at large that hold the end of his chain.”

===Chinese===
法国人阿历克西·德·托克维尔——大概是现代最有先见之明的政治观察家——在 19 世纪 30 年代（1830s）访问这个国家时就看出了危险。即使在那时，他已预见：一个偏重民主甚于共和的社会终将衰朽。他预言美国产生的将不是暴君，而是「监护者」。而美国人民会「因为想到监护者是自己选的，便为处于受监护状态而自宽自解。每个人都任由自己被套上绳索，因为他看到握着绳索另一端的不是某个人、也不是某个阶级，而是全体人民」。

===Original===
Our tendency to concentrate power in the hands of a few men deeply concerns me. We can be conquered by bombs or by subversion; but we can also be conquered by neglect﻿—by ignoring the Constitution and disregarding the principles of limited government. Our defenses against the accumulation of unlimited power in Washington are in poorer shape, I fear, than our defenses against the aggressive designs of Moscow. Like so many other nations before us, we may succumb through internal weakness rather than fall before a foreign foe.

===Chinese===
我们把权力日益集中于少数人之手的倾向，令我深感忧虑。我们可能被炸弹征服，被颠覆征服；但我们也可能被漠视征服——无视宪法，漠然置有限政府的原则于不顾。我担心，我们抵御华盛顿无限权力集聚的防线，比抵御莫斯科侵略图谋的防线更加不济。像我们之前的许多国家一样，我们可能亡于内弱，而非败于外敌。

===Original===
I am convinced that most Americans now want to reverse the trend. I think that concern for our vanishing freedoms is genuine. I think that the people’s uneasiness in the stifling omnipresence of government has turned into something approaching alarm. But bemoaning the evil will not drive it back, and accusing fingers will not shrink government.

===Chinese===
我确信，如今大多数美国人都想让这一趋势逆转。我认为，对我们正在消逝的自由的忧虑是真实的。我认为，人民在政府令人窒息、无所不在的氛围中的不安，已接近于惊恐。但是哀叹邪恶并不能把它赶回去，指摘的手指并不能让政府缩小。

===Original===
*The turn will come when we entrust the conduct of our affairs to men who understand that their first duty as public officials is to divest themselves of the power they have been given.* It will come when Americans, in hundreds of communities throughout the nation, decide to put the man in office who is pledged to enforce the Constitution and restore the Republic. Who will proclaim in a campaign speech: “I have little interest in streamlining government or in making it more efficient, for I mean to reduce its size. I do not undertake to promote welfare, for I propose to extend freedom. My aim is not to pass laws, but to repeal them. It is not to inaugurate new programs, but to cancel old ones that do violence to the Constitution, or that have failed in their purpose, or that impose on the people an unwarranted financial burden. I will not attempt to discover whether legislation is ‘needed’ before I have first determined whether it is constitutionally permissible. And if I should later be attacked for neglecting my constituents’ ‘interests,’ I shall reply that I was informed their main interest is liberty and that in that cause I am doing the very best I can.”

===Chinese===
*当我们把治理国事托付给这样的人——他们明白，公职人员的第一要务是交出已赋予他们的权力——转折点就会到来。*当全国各地成百上千个社区的美国人决定把誓言维护宪法、光复共和国的人送入公职时，它就会到来。这个人将在竞选中宣告：「我对精简政府或提高政府效率没多大兴趣，因为我要缩小它的规模。我不承担促进福利之责，因为我要扩展的是自由。我的目标不是通过法律，而是废除法律。不是开创新的计划，而是取消旧的计划——那些践踏宪法的计划，那些未能实现其目的的计划，那些把无正当理由的财政负担强加于人民的计划。在首先判定一项立法是否合乎宪法之前，我不会去探究它是否『必要』。倘若日后有人攻击我漠视选民的『利益』，我将回答：据我所知，他们的主要利益是自由，而在这项事业上，我正竭尽所能。」

## III States’ Rights / III 州权

===Original===
The Governor of New York, in 1930, pointed out that the Constitution does not empower the Congress to deal with “a great number of﻿ ﻿… vital problems of government, such as the conduct of public utilities, of banks, of insurance, of business, of agriculture, of education, of social welfare, and a dozen other important features.” And he added that “Washington must not be encouraged to interfere” in these areas.

===Chinese===
1930 年，纽约州州长指出：宪法并未授权国会处理「大量……至关重要的治理问题，例如公用事业、银行、保险、商业、农业、教育、社会福利的运营，以及其他十来个重要领域」。他还补充说，在这些领域，「绝不可鼓励华盛顿进行干预」。

===Original===
Franklin Roosevelt’s rapid conversion from Constitutionalism to the doctrine of unlimited government, is an oft-told story. But I am here concerned not so much by the abandonment of States’ Rights by the national Democratic Party﻿—an event that occurred some years ago when that party was captured by the Socialist ideologues in and about the labor movement﻿—as by the unmistakable tendency of the Republican Party to adopt the same course. The result is that today *neither* of our two parties maintains a meaningful commitment to the principle of States’ Rights. Thus, the cornerstone of the Republic, our chief bulwark against the encroachment of individual freedom by Big Government, is fast disappearing under the piling sands of absolutism.

===Chinese===
富兰克林·罗斯福从宪政主义迅速转向无限政府学说，这段旧事人们已经讲滥了。而我在这里感到忧虑的，与其说是全国性民主党对州权（States' Rights）的背弃——那是几年前该党被劳工运动内外的社会主义意识形态分子俘获时发生的事——不如说是共和党正采取同一路线的那种明白无误的趋势。结果是：今天我们的两党*都*不再对州权原则作有意义的坚持。于是，共和国的这块基石、我们抵御大政府（Big Government）侵犯个人自由的主要堡垒，正在专制主义层层堆积的流沙之下迅速消失。

===Original===
The Republican Party, to be sure, gives lip-service to States’ Rights. We often *talk* about “returning to the States their rightful powers”; the Administration has even gone so far as to sponsor a federal-state conference on the problem. But deeds are what count, and I regret to say that in actual practice, the Republican Party, like the Democratic Party, summons the coercive power of the federal government whenever national leaders conclude that the States are not performing satisfactorily.

===Chinese===
诚然，共和党对州权口惠而实不至。我们经常*空谈*「把正当权力归还给各州」；本届政府甚至郑重其事地就这个问题发起了一次联邦—州会议。但要紧的是行动，而我遗憾地说，在实际做法上，一旦全国领袖们认定各州表现不能令人满意，共和党便同民主党一样，随时召唤联邦政府的强制权力。

===Original===
Let us focus attention on one method of federal interference﻿—one that tends to be neglected in much of the public discussion of the problem. In recent years the federal government has continued, and in many cases has increased, federal “grants-in-aid” to the States in a number of areas in which the Constitution recognizes the exclusive jurisdiction of the States. These grants are called “matching funds” and are designed to “stimulate” state spending in health, education, welfare, conservation or any other area in which the federal government decides there is a need for national action. If the States agree to put up money for these purposes, the federal government undertakes to match the appropriation according to a ratio prescribed by Congress. Sometimes the ratio is fifty-fifty; often the federal government contributes over half the cost.

===Chinese===
让我们把注意力集中于联邦干预的一种手段上——在公众对这一问题的诸多讨论中，它往往被忽视。近年来，联邦政府在若干领域继续推行、且在许多情况下加码了对各州的联邦「补助拨款」（grants-in-aid），而这些领域是宪法承认由各州专属管辖的。这类拨款名为「对等资金」（matching funds），旨在「刺激」各州在卫生、教育、福利、资源保育或任何联邦政府认定需要全国行动的领域增加开支。如果各州同意为这些目的出钱，联邦政府便承诺按国会规定的比例配拨款额。比例有时是对半开；联邦政府出的钱常常超过成本的一半。

===Original===
There are two things to note about these programs. The first is that they are *federal* programs﻿—they are conceived by the federal government both as to purpose and as to extent. The second is that the “stimulative” grants are, in effect, a mixture of blackmail and bribery. The States are told to go along with the program “or else.” Once the federal government has offered matching funds, it is unlikely, as a practical matter, that a member of a State Legislature will turn down his State’s fair share of revenue collected from all of the States. Understandably, many legislators feel that to refuse aid would be political suicide. This is an indirect form of coercion, but it is effective nonetheless.

===Chinese===
关于这些计划，有两点需要注意。第一，它们是*联邦*的计划——目的与规模都由联邦政府构想。第二，这些「刺激性」拨款实际上是敲诈与贿赂的混合物。各州被告知：要么跟着计划走，「否则后果自负」。联邦政府一旦亮出对等资金，从实际政治看，州议会的议员不太可能拒绝本州从各州共同缴纳的收入中分得的那一份。可以理解，许多议员觉得拒绝援助无异于政治自杀。这是一种间接的强制，但照样有效。

===Original===
A more direct method of coercion is for the federal government to *threaten* to move in unless state governments take action that Washington deems appropriate. Not so long ago, for example, the Secretary of Labor gave the States a lecture on the wisdom of enacting “up-to-date” unemployment compensation laws. He made no effort to disguise the alternative: if the States failed to act, the federal government would.

===Chinese===
更直接的强制办法，是联邦政府*威胁*要亲自下场，除非州政府采取华盛顿认为恰当的行动。例如不久前，劳工部长给各州上了一课，讲授制定「最新式」失业补偿法的明智之处。他对另一条出路毫不掩饰：各州若不行动，联邦政府就会行动。

===Original===
Here are some examples of the “stimulative” approach. Late in 1957 a “Joint Federal-State Action Committee” recommended that certain matching funds programs be “returned” to the States on the scarcely disguised grounds that the States, in the view of the Committee, had learned to live up to their responsibilities. These are the areas in which the States were learning to behave: “vocational education” programs in agriculture, home economics, practical nursing, and the fisheries trade; local sewage projects; slum clearance and urban renewal; and enforcement of health and safety standards in connection with the atomic energy program.

===Chinese===
下面是这种「刺激」路数的几个例子。1957 年末，一个「联邦—州联合行动委员会」建议把某些对等资金计划「归还」各州，理由几乎是明说的：在委员会看来，各州已经学会尽到自己的责任。各州「学会守规矩」的领域计有：农业、家政、实用护理和渔业方面的「职业教育」计划；地方排污工程；贫民窟清理与城市更新；以及原子能计划相关的卫生与安全标准的执行。

===Original===
Now the point is not that Congress failed to act on these recommendations, or that the Administration gave them only halfhearted support; but rather that the federal government had no business entering these fields in the first place, and thus had no business taking upon itself the prerogative of judging the States’ performance. The Republican Party should have said this plainly and forthrightly and demanded the immediate withdrawal of the federal government.

===Chinese===
现在的问题不在于国会没有按这些建议行动，也不在于政府只给了它们半心半意的支持；而在于联邦政府当初就无权进入这些领域，因而也无权把评判各州表现的特权揽到自己身上。共和党本应把这一点明明白白、直截了当地说出来，并要求联邦政府立即撤出。

===Original===
We can best understand our error, I think, by examining the theory behind it. I have already alluded to the book, *A Republican Looks at His Party*, which is an elaborate rationalization of the “Modern Republican” approach to current problems. (It does the job just as well, I might add, for the Democrats’ approach.) Mr. Larson devotes a good deal of space to the question of States’ Rights. He contends that while there is “a general presumption” in favor of States’ Rights, thanks to the Tenth Amendment, this presumption must give way whenever it appears to the federal authorities that the States are not responding satisfactorily to “the needs of the people.” This is a paraphrase of his position but not, I think, an unjust one. And if this approach appears to be a high-handed way of dealing with an explicit constitutional provision, Mr. Larson justifies the argument by summoning the concept that “for every right there is a corresponding duty.” “When we speak of States’ Rights,” he writes, “we should never forget to add that there go with those rights the corresponding States’ responsibilities.” Therefore, he concludes, if the States fail to do their duty, they have only themselves to blame when the federal government intervenes.

===Chinese===
我想，检视我们错误背后的理论，最能帮助我们理解错误所在。我已经提到过《一个共和党人看他的党》这本书，它是对「现代共和党人」处理当前问题之路线的精心辩护。（我可以补一句：拿它来为民主党的路线辩护，同样称职。）拉森先生用相当篇幅讨论州权问题。他主张：多亏第十修正案（Tenth Amendment），存在一种有利于州权的「一般推定」，但只要联邦当局认为各州对「人民的需要」回应得不能令人满意，这一推定就必须让路。这是对他立场的转述，但我认为并非不公允的转述。如果说这种做法显得是对一条明文宪法条款的专横处理，拉森先生则搬出「凡权利皆有相应义务」的观念来为此说辩护。「当我们谈论州权时，」他写道，「我们永远不应忘记加上一句：与这些权利相伴的，是各州相应的责任。」因此他断言：各州若失职，联邦政府出面干预时，只能怪它们自己。

===Original===
The trouble with this argument is that it treats the Constitution of the United States as a kind of handbook in political theory, to be heeded or ignored depending on how it fits the plans of contemporary federal officials. The Tenth Amendment is *not* “a general assumption,” but a prohibitory rule of law. The Tenth Amendment recognizes the States’ *jurisdiction* in certain areas. States’ Rights means that the States have a right to act or *not to act*, as they see fit, in the areas reserved to them. The States may have duties corresponding to these rights, but the duties are owed to the people of the States, not to the federal government. Therefore, the recourse lies not with the federal government, which is not sovereign, but with the people who are, and who have full power to take disciplinary action. If the people are unhappy with say, their State’s disability insurance program, they can bring pressure to bear on their state officials and, if that fails, they can elect a new set of officials. And if, in the unhappy event they should wish to divest themselves of this responsibility, they can amend the Constitution. The Constitution, I repeat, draws a sharp and clear line between federal jurisdiction and state jurisdiction. The federal government’s failure to recognize that line has been a crushing blow to the principle of limited government.

===Chinese===
这种论证的毛病在于：它把美利坚合众国宪法当成一本政治理论手册，当代联邦官员的计划合用则理之，不合用则弃之。第十修正案*不是*什么「一般推定」，而是一条禁止性的法律规则。第十修正案承认的是各州在某些领域的*管辖权*。州权的意思是：在保留给各州的领域里，各州有权行动，也有权*不行动*，悉听其便。各州或许有与这些权利相应的义务，但这些义务是欠各州人民的，不是欠联邦政府的。因此，求全责备之权不属于联邦政府——它并非主权者——而属于人民，人民才是主权者，并且拥有采取惩戒行动的全权。如果人民对比如说本州的残障保险计划不满，他们可以向州官员施压；施压不成，还可以改选一批新官员。倘若他们竟想卸掉这份责任——那将是不幸的——他们还可以修宪。我再说一遍：宪法在联邦管辖权与州管辖权之间划了一条锋利而清晰的界线。联邦政府拒不承认这条界线，已给有限政府原则以毁灭性一击。

===Original===
But again, I caution against a defensive, or apologetic, appeal to the Constitution. There is a *reason* for its reservation of States’ Rights. Not only does it prevent the accumulation of power in a central government that is remote from the people and relatively immune from popular restraints; it also recognizes the principle that essentially local problems are best dealt with by the people most directly concerned. Who knows better than New Yorkers how much and what kind of publicly-financed slum clearance in New York City is needed and can be afforded? Who knows better than Nebraskans whether that State has an adequate nursing program? Who knows better than Arizonans the kind of school program that is needed to educate their children? The people of my own State﻿—and I am confident that I speak for the majority of them﻿—have long since seen through the spurious suggestion that federal aid comes “free.” They know that the money comes out of their own pockets, and that it is returned to them minus a broker’s fee taken by the federal bureaucracy. They know, too, that the power to decide how that money shall be spent is withdrawn from them and exercised by some planning board deep in the caverns of one of the federal agencies. They understand this represents a great and perhaps irreparable loss﻿—not only in their wealth, but in their priceless liberty.

===Chinese===
但我还是要提醒：不可用一种防御性的、辩解式的姿态诉诸宪法。宪法保留州权是有*理由*的。它不仅防止权力集聚于一个远离人民、相对免受民众约束的中央政府；它还确认了一条原则：本质上属于地方的问题，最好由与之关系最直接的人民来处理。纽约市需要多少、需要什么样的公帑资助的贫民窟清理，财力又负担得起多少，还有谁比纽约人更清楚？那个州的护理项目够不够，还有谁比内布拉斯加人更清楚？教育他们的孩子需要什么样的学校计划，还有谁比亚利桑那人更清楚？我自己那个州的人民——我确信我是在替他们中的多数说话——早就看穿了所谓联邦援助是「免费」的伪善说法。他们知道那笔钱出自他们自己的口袋，转一圈回到他们手里时，已被联邦官僚机构抽走了一笔经纪费。他们也知道，如何花这笔钱的决定权已被从他们手中拿走，交给了深藏在某个联邦机构洞穴深处的某个规划委员会。他们明白，这意味着一场巨大的、或许无法弥补的损失——损失的不仅是财富，还有他们无价的自由。

===Original===
Nothing could so far advance the cause of freedom as for state officials throughout the land to assert their rightful claims to lost state power; and for the federal government to withdraw promptly and totally from every jurisdiction which the Constitution reserved to the states.

===Chinese===
若要最有力地推进自由事业，莫过于全国各地的州官员起而主张收回失落的州权力的正当权利；也莫过于联邦政府迅即而彻底地退出宪法保留给各州的每一处管辖领域。

## IV And Civil Rights / IV 兼论公民权利

===Original===
An attempt has been made in recent years to disparage the principle of States’ Rights by equating it with defense of the South’s position on racial integration. I have already indicated that the reach of States’ Rights is much broader than that﻿—that it affects Northerners as well as Southerners, and concerns many matters that have nothing to do with the race question. Still, it is quite true that the integration issue is affected by the States’ Rights principle, and that the South’s position on the issue is, today, the most conspicuous expression of the principle. So much so that the country is now in the grips of a spirited and sometimes ugly controversy over an imagined conflict between States’ Rights, on the one hand, and what are called “civil rights” on the other.

===Chinese===
近年来，有人试图把州权原则等同于为南方在种族融合（integration）问题上的立场辩护，以此贬损州权原则。我已经指出，州权的涵盖范围远不止于此——它同样关系到北方人和南方人，所涉的许多事务与种族问题毫无关系。不过，融合问题的确受州权原则影响，南方在这个问题上的立场也确实是今天该原则最引人注目的表现。以致全国如今陷入一场激烈而时有恶相的争论：一边是州权，一边是所谓「公民权利」（civil rights），两者被想象成势不两立。

===Original===
I say an imagined conflict because I deny that there *can* be a conflict between States’ Rights, properly defined﻿—and civil rights, properly defined. If States’ “Rights” are so asserted as to encroach upon individual rights that are protected by valid federal laws, then the exercise of state power is a nullity. Conversely, if individual “rights” are so asserted as to infringe upon valid state power, then the assertion of those “rights” is a nullity. The rights themselves do not clash. The conflict arises from a failure to define the two categories of rights correctly, and to assert them lawfully.

===Chinese===
我之所以说这是想象出来的冲突，是因为我否认：界定得当的州权与界定得当的公民权利之间*可能*存在冲突。如果各州的「权利」被主张到侵入有效联邦法律所保护的个人权利的地步，那么州权力的行使归于无效。反过来，如果个人的「权利」被主张到侵犯有效州权力的地步，那么这些「权利」的主张也归于无效。权利本身并不冲突。冲突源于未能正确界定这两类权利、未能合法地主张它们。

===Original===
States’ Rights are easy enough to define. The Tenth Amendment does it succinctly: “The powers not delegated to the United States by the Constitution nor prohibited by it to the States are reserved to the States respectively, or to the people.”

===Chinese===
州权的界定并不难。第十修正案说得简明扼要：「宪法未授予合众国、也未禁止各州行使的权力，保留给各州各自享有，或保留给人民。」

===Original===
Civil rights should be no harder. In fact, however﻿—thanks to extravagant and shameless misuse by people who ought to know better﻿—it is one of the most badly understood concepts in modern political usage. Civil rights is frequently used synonymously with “human rights”﻿—or with “natural rights.” As often as not, it is simply a name for describing an activity that someone deems politically or socially desirable. A sociologist writes a paper proposing to abolish some inequity, or a politician makes a speech about it﻿—and, behold, a new “civil right” is born! The Supreme Court has displayed the same creative powers.

===Chinese===
公民权利的界定本应同样不难。然而实际上——多亏那些本应更明白事理的人毫无廉耻的滥用——它已成为现代政治用语中被误解得最厉害的概念之一。「公民权利」经常被当作「人权」（human rights）的同义词使用，或与「自然权利」混用。更多的时候，它不过是给某人认为在政治上或社会上可取的某种活动命名的一种说法。社会学家写一篇论文，提议废除某项不公；或者政客就此发表一次演讲——瞧，一项新的「公民权利」诞生了！联邦最高法院也展示了同样的创造力。

===Original===
A *civil* right is a right that is asserted and is therefore protected by some valid law. It may be asserted by the common law, or by local or federal statutes, or by the Constitution; *but unless a right is incorporated in the law, it is not a civil right and is not enforceable by the instruments of the civil law*. There may be some rights﻿—“natural,” “human,” or otherwise﻿—that *should* also be civil rights. But if we desire to give such rights the protection of the law, our recourse is to a legislature or to the amendment procedures of the Constitution. We must not look to politicians, or sociologists﻿—or the courts﻿—to correct the deficiency.

===Chinese===
*公民*权利是这样一种权利：它被主张，并因此受某项有效法律的保护。它可以由普通法确立，也可以由地方或联邦的制定法确立，或由宪法确立；*但一项权利除非载入法律，否则就不是公民权利，不能借助民事法律的工具来强制执行*。或许存在一些权利——「自然的」「人的」或别的什么——它们*也应当*成为公民权利。但我们若想让这类权利获得法律的保护，正途在于立法机关，或宪法的修宪程序。我们切不可指望政客、社会学家——或法院——来弥补这一缺憾。

===Original===
In the field of racial relations, there are some rights that are clearly protected by valid laws and are therefore “civil” rights. One of them is the right to vote. The Fifteenth Amendment provides that no one shall be denied the franchise on account of race, color or previous condition of servitude. Similarly with certain legal privileges enforced by the Fourteenth Amendment. The legislative history of that amendment makes it clear (I quote from the Civil Rights Act of 1866 which the Amendment was designed to legitimize) that people of all races shall be equally entitled “to make and enforce contracts, to sue, be parties, and give evidence, to inherit, to purchase, lease, sell, hold and convey real and personal property and to full and equal benefit of all laws and proceedings for the security of persons and property.” After the passage of that Act and the Amendment, all persons, Negroes included, had a “civil” right to these protections.

===Chinese===
在种族关系领域，有一些权利明确受有效法律保护，因而属于「公民」权利。其中之一是投票权。第十五修正案规定：任何人不得因种族、肤色或先前的奴役状态而被剥夺选举权。第十四修正案所实施的某些法律特权也是如此。该修正案的立法史表明（我引自修正案意在使其合法化的 1866 年《公民权利法》）：一切种族的人应一律平等地有权「订立和执行契约、提起诉讼、充当当事人和提供证据、继承、购买、租赁、出售、持有和转让不动产与动产，并平等地充分享有为保障人身和财产而设的一切法律与程序的利益」。该法案与该修正案通过之后，所有人，包括黑人在内，都享有受这些保护的「公民」权利。

===Original===
It is otherwise, let us note, with education. For the federal Constitution does *not* require the States to maintain racially mixed schools. Despite the recent holding of the Supreme Court, I am firmly convinced﻿—not only that integrated schools are not required﻿—but that the Constitution does not permit any interference whatsoever by the federal government in the field of education. It may be just or wise or expedient for negro children to attend the same schools as white children, but they do not have a civil right to do so which is protected by the federal constitution, or which is enforceable by the federal government.

===Chinese===
但请注意，教育却是另一回事。因为联邦宪法*并没有*要求各州设立种族混合的学校。尽管联邦最高法院近来作出了那样的判决，我仍坚信——不仅种族融合的学校并非宪法所要求——而且宪法根本不允许联邦政府对教育领域作任何干涉。黑人儿童与白人儿童同校就读，或许是公正的、明智的、权宜的，但他们并不因此拥有一项受联邦宪法保护、可由联邦政府强制执行的公民权利。

===Original===
The intentions of the founding fathers in this matter are beyond any doubt: *no powers regarding education were given the federal government*. Consequently, under the Tenth Amendment, jurisdiction over the entire field was reserved to the States. The remaining question is whether the Fourteenth Amendment﻿—concretely, that amendment’s “equal protection” clause﻿—modified the original prohibition against federal intervention.

===Chinese===
开国先贤们在这件事上的意图毋庸置疑：*没有把任何关于教育的权力授予联邦政府*。因此，依照第十修正案，整个教育领域的管辖权保留给各州。剩下的问题是：第十四修正案——具体说，是该修正案的「平等保护」条款——是否修改了当初对联邦干预的禁止。

===Original===
To my knowledge it has never been seriously argued﻿—the argument certainly was not made by the Supreme Court﻿—that the authors of the Fourteenth Amendment intended to alter the Constitutional scheme with regard to education. Indeed, in the famous school integration decision, *Brown v. Board of Education* (1954), the Supreme Court justices expressly acknowledged that they were not being guided by the intentions of the amendment’s authors. “*In approaching this problem*,” Chief Justice Warren said “*we cannot turn the clock back to 1868 when the amendment was adopted﻿ ﻿… We must consider public education in the light of its full development and in its present place in American life throughout the nation.*” In effect, the Court said that what matters is not the ideas of the men who wrote the Constitution, but the *Court’s* ideas. It was only by engrafting its own views onto the established law of the land that the Court was able to reach the decision it did.

===Chinese===
据我所知，从未有人认真论证过——最高法院当然也没有论证过——第十四修正案的起草者们有意改变宪法在教育方面的安排。事实上，在那宗著名的学校融合判决，即布朗诉教育委员会案（*Brown v. Board of Education*，1954）中，最高法院的大法官们明确承认，他们并没有以修正案起草者们的意图为准绳。「*在处理这个问题时，*」沃伦首席大法官说，「*我们不能把时钟拨回 1868 年修正案通过的那个时代……我们必须根据公共教育的充分发展、及其今天在美国全国生活中的地位来考虑它。*」这实际上是说：要紧的不是宪法起草者们的想法，而是*法院*的想法。最高法院正是靠把自己的观点嫁接到这个国家的既定法律之上，才得出它所作的判决。

===Original===
The intentions of the Fourteenth Amendment’s authors are perfectly clear. Consider these facts. 1. During the entire congressional debate on the Fourteenth Amendment it was never once suggested by any proponent of the amendment that it would outlaw segregated schools. 2. At the same time that it approved the Fourteenth Amendment, Congress established schools in Washington in Georgetown “for the sole use of﻿ ﻿… colored children.” 3. In all the debates on the amendment by the State Legislatures there was only one legislator, a man in Indiana, who thought the amendment would affect schools. 4. The great majority of the States that approved the amendment permitted or required segregated schools at the very time they approved the amendment. There is not room here for exhaustive treatment of this evidence, but the facts are well documented, and they are all we have to know about the Fourteenth Amendment’s bearing on this problem. The amendment was not intended to, and therefore it did not outlaw racially separate schools. It was not intended to, and therefore it did not, authorize *any* federal intervention in the field of education.

===Chinese===
第十四修正案起草者们的意图一清二楚。请看这些事实。1. 在国会关于第十四修正案的整个辩论过程中，没有任何一位修正案的支持者哪怕一次暗示它将取缔种族隔离的学校。2. 国会通过第十四修正案的同时，还在华盛顿的乔治敦创办了学校，「专供……有色人种儿童」使用。3. 各州议会就该修正案进行的一切辩论中，只有一位议员——印第安纳州的一个人——认为修正案会影响学校。4. 批准该修正案的各州中，绝大多数在批准之时仍允许或要求学校实行种族隔离。这里没有篇幅穷尽地论述这些证据，但事实俱在，记载详明，而关于第十四修正案与此问题的关系，我们只需知道这些就够了。该修正案无意取缔按种族分开的学校，因此它没有取缔。该修正案无意授权联邦干预教育领域，因此它没有授权——*任何*授权都没有。

===Original===
I am therefore not impressed by the claim that the Supreme Court’s decision on school integration is the law of the land. *The Constitution, and the laws “made in pursuance thereof,” are the “supreme law of the land.”* The Constitution is what its authors intended it to be and said it was﻿—not what the Supreme Court says it is. If we condone the practice of substituting our own intentions for those of the Constitution’s framers, we reject, in effect, the principle of Constitutional Government: we endorse a rule of men, not of laws.

===Chinese===
因此，「最高法院关于学校融合的判决就是本国最高法律」的说法打动不了我。*宪法，以及「依宪法制定」的法律，才是「本国的最高法律」。*宪法就是其起草者们意图它成为、并宣称它是的那个东西——而不是最高法院说它是什么就是什么。倘若我们容忍用自己的意图偷换宪法起草者们的意图这种做法，我们实际上就抛弃了宪政政府的原则：我们认可的是人治，不是法治。

===Original===
I have great respect for the Supreme Court as an institution, but I cannot believe that I display that respect by submitting abjectly to abuses of power by the Court, and by condoning its unconstitutional trespass into the legislative sphere of government. The Congress and the States, equally with the Supreme Court, are obliged to interpret and comply with the Constitution according to their own lights. I therefore support all efforts by the States, excluding violence of course, to preserve their rightful powers over education.

===Chinese===
作为一个机构，我对联邦最高法院怀有极大的敬意，但我无法相信，卑屈地屈从于法院的滥权、纵容它违宪地侵入政府的立法领域，就是表达敬意的方式。国会和各州与最高法院一样，有义务按照自己的理解去解释并遵守宪法。因此，我支持各州为保住其对教育的正当权力而作的一切努力——当然，暴力除外。

===Original===
As for the Congress, I would hope that the national legislature would help clarify the problem by proposing to the States a Constitutional amendment that would reaffirm the States’ exclusive jurisdiction in the field of education. This amendment would, in my judgment, assert what is already provided unmistakably by the Constitution; but it would put the matter beyond any further question.

===Chinese===
至于国会，我希望全国立法机构能向各州提出一项宪法修正案，重申各州在教育领域的专属管辖权，以此帮助澄清这个问题。依我的判断，这项修正案所申明的，本就是宪法已作了毫不含糊之规定的东西；但它可以把这件事彻底钉死，不再生疑。

===Original===
It so happens that I am in agreement with the *objectives* of the Supreme Court as stated in the *Brown* decision. I believe that it is both wise and just for negro children to attend the same schools as whites, and that to deny them this opportunity carries with it strong implications of inferiority. I am not prepared, however, to impose that judgment of mine on the people of Mississippi or South Carolina, or to tell them what methods should be adopted and what pace should be kept in striving toward that goal. That is their business, not mine. I believe that the problem of race relations, like all social and cultural problems, is best handled by the people directly concerned. Social and cultural change, however desirable, should not be effected by the engines of national power. Let us, through persuasion and education, seek to improve institutions we deem defective. But let us, in doing so, respect the orderly processes of the law. Any other course enthrones tyrants and dooms freedom.

===Chinese===
说来凑巧，《布朗案》判决所申明的最高法院的*目标*，我是赞成的。我相信黑人儿童与白人儿童同校就读既明智又公正，我也相信，拒绝给他们这个机会，带有强烈的暗示他们低人一等的意味。然而我并不准备把我这一判断强加给密西西比或南卡罗来纳的人民，也不准备告诉他们：朝那个目标努力应当采用什么方法、保持什么节奏。那是他们的事，不是我的事。我相信，种族关系问题同一切社会文化问题一样，最好由直接相关的人民自己处理。社会与文化的变迁，无论多么可取，都不应借助全国性权力的机器来实现。让我们通过劝说和教育，努力改进我们认为有缺陷的制度。但在这样做的时候，让我们尊重法律的正当程序。任何别的路都会把暴君送上王座，把自由送入绝境。

## V Freedom for the Farmer / V 还农民以自由

===Original===
> “… supervision of agriculture and other concerns of a similar nature﻿ ﻿… which are proper to be provided for by local legislation, can never be desirable cares of a general jurisdiction. It is therefore improbable that there should exist a disposition in the federal councils to usurp the powers with which they are connected; because the attempt to exercise those powers would be as troublesome as they were nugatory.”

===Chinese===
> 「……对农业的监督以及其他类似性质的事务……本宜由地方立法处理，它们永远不宜成为一般性管辖权的分内之事。因此，联邦议事机构中不太可能存在篡夺与之相关权力的倾向；因为行使那些权力的尝试，将既徒劳无益，又麻烦缠身。」

===Original===
Hamilton was wrong in his prediction as to what men would do, but quite right in foreseeing the consequences of their foolhardiness. Federal intervention in agriculture has, indeed, proved “troublesome.” Disregard of the Constitution in this field has brought about the inevitable loss of personal freedom; and it has created economic chaos. Unmanageable surpluses, an immense tax burden, high consumer prices, vexatious controls﻿—I doubt if the folly of ignoring the principle of limited government has ever been more convincingly demonstrated.

===Chinese===
汉密尔顿预言人们会做什么，预言错了；但预见他们鲁莽行事的后果，却预见得相当准。联邦对农业的干预，确实已被证明「麻烦缠身」。在这一领域无视宪法，带来了个人自由不可避免的丧失，也造成了经济上的混乱。无法收拾的过剩、沉重的税负、高企的消费品价格、恼人的管制——我不信无视有限政府原则的愚蠢还曾有过比这更有说服力的展示。

===Original===
We have blundered on so grand a scale that even our critical faculties seem to have been damaged in the process. No man who is familiar with the subject will deny that the policy of price supports and production controls has been a colossal failure. Yet, today, some of our best minds have no better solution to the problem than to raise the supports and increase the controls!

===Chinese===
我们的败绩规模如此浩大，以致连我们的批判力似乎都在这个过程中受到了损伤。熟悉这一题目的人都不会否认：价格支持与生产管制的政策已一败涂地。然而今天，我们一些最优秀的头脑拿出的解决办法，竟然还是提高支持、加紧管制！

===Original===
The teaching of the Constitution on this matter is perfectly clear. *No power over agriculture was given to any branch of the national government.* The sponsors of the first Agriculture Adjustment Act, passed in 1933, tried to justify the law under the so-called general welfare clause of the Constitution. The Supreme Court promptly struck down that legislation on the grounds that the phrase, “general welfare,” was simply a qualification of the taxing power and did not give Congress the power to *control* anything. “The regulation (of agricultural production),” the Court said in *United States v. Butler* (1936) “is not in fact voluntary. The farmer, of course, may refuse to comply [a privilege not given him under present legislation], but the price of such refusal is loss of benefits﻿ ﻿… the power to confer or withhold unlimited benefits is the power to coerce or destroy﻿ ﻿…”

===Chinese===
宪法在这件事上的训示一清二楚。*全国政府的任何一个部门都未被授予管辖农业的权力。*1933 年通过的第一部《农业调整法》，其发起人试图援引宪法所谓「公共福利」条款为该法辩护。最高法院随即击落了那部立法，理由是：「公共福利」一语只是对征税权的一项限定，并未授予国会*控制*任何东西的权力。「（对农业生产的）管制，」法院在《合众国诉巴特勒案》（*United States v. Butler*，1936）中说，「并非真正出于自愿。农场主当然可以拒绝照办［现行立法并未给他这项特权］，但拒绝的代价是丧失好处……给予或扣留无限好处的权力，就是强制或摧毁的权力……」

===Original===
The New Deal Congress replied by enacting substantially identical legislation, the second A.A.A., and now sought to justify the program as a “regulation of interstate commerce.” This was a transparent evasion of the *Butler* case; but the Supreme Court, which by this time was under heavy political fire for having thwarted the “Roosevelt Revolution,” made one of its celebrated about-faces and upheld the new act. The federal government has usurped many powers under the guise of “regulating commerce,” but this instance of distorting the plain meaning of the Constitution’s language is perhaps the most flagrant on record.

===Chinese===
新政国会以牙还牙，制定了实质相同的立法——第二部《农业调整法》（A.A.A.），并转而把该计划辩护为一种「州际商业的管制」。这是对《巴特勒案》判决公然的规避；而此时因挫败「罗斯福革命」而备受政治围攻的最高法院，作出了它那几次著名的掉头转向之一，维持了新法。联邦政府假「管制商业」之名僭取的权力不可胜数，但这一桩对宪法条文明白含义的歪曲，大概是记录在案的最明目张胆的一例。

===Original===
In the case that upheld the second A.A.A., *Wickard v. Filburn*, (1942), a farmer had been fined for planting 23 acres of wheat, instead of the eleven acres the government had allotted him﻿—notwithstanding that the “excess” wheat had been consumed *on his own farm*. Now how in the world, the farmer wanted to know, can it be said that the wheat I feed my own stock is in interstate commerce? That’s easy, the Court said. If you had *not* used your own wheat for feed, you might have bought feed from someone else, and that purchase might have affected the price of wheat that *was* transported in interstate commerce! By this bizarre reasoning the Court made the commerce clause as wide as the world and nullified the Constitution’s clear reservation to the States of jurisdiction over agriculture.

===Chinese===
在维持第二部《农业调整法》的《威卡德诉菲尔伯恩案》（*Wickard v. Filburn*，1942）中，一位农场主因种了 23 英亩小麦而被罚款——政府分给他的配额是十一英亩——尽管那些「超额」小麦全部是在*他自己的农场*上消耗掉的。这位农场主想知道：我喂自家牲口的小麦，究竟凭什么能说成是州际商业？这还不容易，法院说。你如果*没有*用自己的小麦做饲料，你可能就会向别人购买饲料，而那笔购买就可能影响*确实*在州际商业中运输的小麦的价格！凭这套匪夷所思的推理，法院把商业条款撑得同世界一样宽，把宪法对各州农业管辖权的明确保留一笔勾销。

===Original===
The tragedy, of course, is that the federal government’s unconstitutional intrusion into Agriculture has not brought us any closer to a solution of the “farm problem.” The problem, when federal intervention began, was declining farm incomes. Today, many farm incomes are still low. But now we have additional problems﻿—production controls that restrict freedom, high consumer prices, huge crop surpluses and a gigantic tax bill that is running close to six billion dollars a year. No matter what variant of the price support-production control approach we adopt, the solution to these problems continues to elude us.

===Chinese===
当然，悲剧在于：联邦政府违宪闯入农业，并没有使我们离「农业问题」的解决更近一步。联邦干预开始时，问题是农场收入下滑。今天，许多农场收入仍然低迷。但现在我们又添了新问题——限制自由的生产管制、高企的消费品价格、庞大的作物过剩，以及一年将近六十亿美元的巨额税单。无论我们采用价格支持—生产管制路数的哪个变种，这些问题的解决依旧遥遥无期。

===Original===
The reason government intervention has created more problems than it has solved is quite simple. *Farm production, like any other production is best controlled by the natural operation of the free market.* If the nation’s farmers are permitted to sell their produce freely, at price consumers are willing to pay, they will, under the law of supply and demand, end up producing roughly what can be consumed in national and world markets. And if farmers, in general, find they are not getting high enough prices for their produce, some of them will move into other kinds of economic activity. The result will be reduced agricultural production and higher incomes for those who remain on the farms. If, however, the government interferes with this natural economic process, and pegs prices higher than the consumer is willing to pay, the result will be, in Hamilton’s phrase, “troublesome.” The nation will pay exorbitant prices for work that is not needed and for produce that cannot be consumed.

===Chinese===
政府干预制造的问题多于解决的问题，原因很简单。*农业生产，同任何其他生产一样，最好由自由市场的自然运作来调节。*如果全国的农场主获准按消费者愿意支付的价格自由出售产品，那么在供求规律之下，他们最终的产量大致就是国内和世界市场所能消费的量。而如果农场主们普遍发现产品卖不出足够高的价格，其中一部分就会转营其他经济活动。结果将是农业产出减少、留在土地上的人收入提高。然而，如果政府插手这一自然的经济过程，把价格钉在消费者不愿支付的水平之上，其结果，用汉密尔顿的话说，就会「麻烦缠身」。国家将为不需要的劳动、为消费不掉的产品付出高昂的代价。

===Original===
In recent years, the government has sought to alleviate the problem of overproduction by the soil bank and acreage retirement programs. Actually, these programs are simply a modern version of the hog-killing and potato-burning schemes promoted by Henry Wallace during the New Deal. And they have been no more successful in reducing surpluses than their predecessors. But there is also a positive evil in these programs: in effect, they reward people for *not producing*. For a nation that is expressing great concern over its “economic growth,” I cannot conceive of a more absurd and self-defeating policy than one which subsidizes non-production.

===Chinese===
近年来，政府试图以「土壤银行」和退耕计划来缓解生产过剩问题。其实，这些计划不过是亨利·华莱士在新政时期推行的杀猪焚薯方案的现代翻版。它们在削减过剩上的成效，同其前辈一样不彰。而这些计划还有一层实实在在的恶：它们实际上是奖励人们*不生产*。对于一个正在为「经济增长」忧心忡忡的国家来说，我想不出还有比补贴不生产更荒唐、更自拆台脚的政策了。

===Original===
The problem of surpluses will not be solved until we recognize that technological progress and other factors have made it possible for the needs of America, and those of accessible world markets, to be satisfied by a far fewer number of farmers than now till the soil. I cannot believe that any serious student of the farm problems fails to appreciate this fact. What has been lacking is not an understanding of a problem that is really quite impossible not to understand, but the political courage to do something about it.

===Chinese===
过剩问题要到我们承认下面这一点才会解决：技术进步等因素，已使美国本土和可及的世界市场的需要，能够由比现在耕地者少得多的农场主来满足。我不相信任何认真研究农业问题的人会看不到这个事实。我们所缺的，不是对一个其实根本不可能不理解的问题的理解，而是对此有所作为的政治勇气。

===Original===
Doing something about it means﻿—and there can be no equivocation here﻿—*prompt and final termination of the farm subsidy program*. The only way to persuade farmers to enter other fields of endeavor is to stop paying inefficient farmers for produce that cannot be sold at free market prices. Is this a cruel solution? Is it heartless to permit the natural laws of economics to determine how many farmers there shall be in the same way that those laws determine how many bankers, or druggists, or watchmakers there shall be? It was never considered so before the subsidy program began. Let us remember that the movement *from* the farm *to* other fields of endeavor has been proceeding in this country since its beginning﻿—and with good effects, not ill.

===Chinese===
有所作为，意味着——在这件事上不容含糊其辞——*迅速而最终地终止农业补贴计划*。要说服农场主转营他业，唯一的办法就是不再为卖不出自由市场价格的农产品向低效的农场主付钱。这是残忍的解决办法吗？容许经济自然规律决定该有多少农场主——正如这些规律决定该有多少银行家、药剂师或钟表匠——就是冷酷无情吗？在补贴计划开始之前，从来没有人这样认为。请不要忘记：*离开*农场、*转向*其他行业的流动，在这个国家立国以来就一直在进行——其效果是好的，不是坏的。

===Original===
I cannot believe that this course will lose politicians as many votes as some of them seem to fear. Most farmers want to stand on their own feet. They are prepared to take their chances in the free market. They have a more intimate knowledge than most of us of the consequences of unlimited government power, and so, it would seem, a greater interest than most in returning agriculture to freedom and economic sanity.

===Chinese===
我不相信这条路会让政客们丢掉他们有些人似乎担心的那么多选票。大多数农场主想靠自己的双脚站立。他们准备在自由市场里碰自己的运气。比起我们大多数人对无限政府权力之后果的了解，他们有更切身的体会，因此，让农业回归自由与经济清明，他们的关切似乎也比大多数人都更深切。

## VI Freedom for Labor / VI 还劳工以自由

===Original===
If I had to select the vote I regard as the most important of my Senate career it would be the one I cast on the Kennedy-Ervin “Labor Reform” Bill of 1959. The Senate passed the measure 90﻿–﻿1; the dissenting vote was mine. The measure had been advertised as a cure-all for the evils uncovered by the McClellan Committee investigation. I opposed it because I felt certain that legislation which pretended to respond to the popular demand for safeguards against union power, but actually did not do so, would preclude the possibility of meaningful legislation for some time to come.

===Chinese===
如果一定要我选出参议员生涯中我认为最重要的一票，那就是 1959 年我对肯尼迪—欧文「劳工改革」法案投的那一票。参议院以 90 对 1 通过该案；那张反对票是我的。这项法案被宣传成麦克莱伦委员会调查所揭露种种弊病的万灵药。我反对它，因为我确信：一部假装回应民众对防范工会权力之保障的诉求、实际上却并未做到的立法，将在今后一段时期内堵死有意义的立法的可能。

===Original===
That opinion was vindicated later on. The House of Representatives rejected Kennedy-Ervin, and substituted in its place a much better measure, the Landrum-Griffin bill. The ensuing conference between representatives of the two houses made only minor changes in the House version; I would guess that 90% of the original Landrum-Griffin bill survived in the conferees’ report. The Senate adopted the report with only two dissenting votes﻿—proof to me that my initial protest had been wise.

===Chinese===
这个判断后来得到了证实。众议院否决了肯尼迪—欧文案，代之以一部好得多的法案——兰德勒姆—格里芬法案。随后两院代表的协商会议只对众议院版本作了微小改动；我估计原兰德勒姆—格里芬法案的 90% 在协商委员会的报告里存活了下来。参议院仅以两票反对通过了该报告——在我看来，这证明我当初的抗议是明智的。

===Original===
But the protest still holds: though the Landrum-Griffin Bill was an improvement over the Kennedy measure, Congress has still to come to grips with the real evil in the Labor field. Graft and corruption are symptoms of the illness that besets the labor movement, not the cause of it. *The cause is the enormous economic and political power now concentrated in the hands of union leaders.*

===Chinese===
但抗议依然成立：尽管兰德勒姆—格里芬法案比肯尼迪的法案有改进，国会仍须正视劳工领域的真正病灶。贪腐与舞弊是困扰劳工运动的那场疾病的症状，不是病因。*病因，是如今集中于工会领袖之手的巨大经济权力与政治权力。*

===Original===
Such power hurts the nation’s economy by forcing on employers contract terms that encourage inefficiency, lower production and high prices﻿—all of which result in a lower standard of living for the American people.

===Chinese===
这种权力伤害国民经济：它把助长低效、压低产量、推高价格的合同条款强加给雇主——这一切的结果，便是美国人民生活水平的下降。

===Original===
It corrupts the nation’s political life by exerting undue influence on the selection of public officials.

===Chinese===
它败坏国家的政治生活：对公职人员的遴选施加不当影响。

===Original===
It gravely compromises the freedom of millions of individual workers who are able to register a dissent against the practice of union leaders only at the risk of losing their jobs.

===Chinese===
它严重侵害千百万个别工人的自由：他们若想对工会领袖的做法表示异议，就只得冒着丢掉饭碗的风险。

===Original===
All of us have heard the charge that to thus criticize the power of Big Labor is to be anti-labor and anti-union. This is an argument that serves the interest of union leaders, but it does not usually fit the facts, and it certainly does not do justice to my views. I believe that unionism, kept within its proper and natural bounds, accomplishes a positive good for the country. Unions *can* be an instrument for achieving economic justice for the working man. Moreover, they are an alternative to, and thus discourage State Socialism. Most important of all, they are an expression of freedom. Trade unions properly conceived, are an expression of man’s inalienable right to associate with other men for the achievement of legitimate objectives.

===Chinese===

===Original===
One way to check the unions’ power is for the government to dictate through compulsory arbitration, the terms of employment throughout an entire industry. I am opposed to this course because it simply transfers economic power from the unions to the government, and encourages State Socialism. The other way is to disperse union power and thus extend freedom in labor-management relations.

===Chinese===
遏制工会权力的一个办法，是由政府通过强制仲裁规定整个行业的雇佣条件。我反对这条路，因为它不过是把经济权力从工会转到政府手里，而且助长国家社会主义。另一个办法是分散工会权力，从而扩展劳资关系中的自由。

===Original===
Eighty years ago the nation was faced with a comparable concentration of economic power. Large corporations, by gaining monopoly control over entire industries, had nullified the laws of competition that are conducive to freedom. We responded to that challenge by outlawing monopolies through the Sherman Act and other antitrust legislation. *These laws, however, have never been applied to labor unions.* And I am at a loss to understand why. If it is wrong for a single corporation to dictate prices throughout an entire industry, it is also wrong for a single union﻿—or, as is the actual case, a small number of union leaders﻿—to dictate wages and terms of employment throughout an entire industry.

===Chinese===
八十年前，这个国家曾面对过类似的经济权力集中。大公司靠垄断整个行业，废掉了有利于自由的竞争法则。我们的回应是通过《谢尔曼法》和其他反托拉斯立法把垄断定为非法。*然而这些法律从未适用于工会。*我不明白这是为什么。如果一家公司独断整个行业的价格是错误的，那么一个工会——或者如实际情形那样，一小撮工会领袖——独断整个行业的工资和雇佣条件，同样是错误的。

===Original===
*The evil to be eliminated is the power of unions to enforce industry-wide bargaining.* Employees have a right, as we have seen, to select a common agent for bargaining with *their* employer but they do not have a right to select a national agent to bargain with all employers in the industry. If a union has the power to enforce uniform conditions of employment throughout the nation its power is comparable to that of a Socialist government.

===Chinese===
*要铲除的弊病，是工会强制推行全行业谈判的权力。*如前所见，雇员有权选择一个共同代理人与*他们的*雇主谈判，但无权选择一个全国性代理人与该行业的所有雇主谈判。如果一个工会有权把划一的雇佣条件强加于全国，它的权力就堪比一个社会主义政府。

===Original===
Employers are forbidden to act collusively for sound reasons. The same reasons apply to unions. Industry-wide price-fixing causes economic dislocations. So does industry-wide wage-fixing. A wage that is appropriate in one part of the country may not be in another area where economic conditions are very different. Corporate monopolies impair the operation of the free market, and thus injure the consuming public. So do union monopolies. When the United Automobile Workers demand a wage increase from the auto industry, a single monolith is pitted against a number of separate, competing companies. The contest is an unequal one, for the union is able to play off one company against another. The result is that individual companies are unable to resist excessive wage demands and must, in turn, raise their prices. The consumer ultimately suffers for he pays prices that are fixed not by free market competition﻿—the law of supply and demand﻿—but by the arbitrary decision of national union leaders. Far better if the employees of Ford were required to deal with Ford, and those of Chrysler with Chrysler and so on. The collective bargaining process will work for the common good in all industries if it is confined to the employers and employees directly concerned.

===Chinese===
雇主被禁止串通行事，理由是充分的。同样的理由也适用于工会。全行业的定价造成经济失衡，全行业的定薪亦然。在一国某地合适的工资，放到经济条件迥异的另一地区未必合适。公司垄断损害自由市场的运转，从而伤害消费大众；工会垄断同样如此。当联合汽车工人工会向汽车业要求加薪时，是一整块巨石对垒若干各自分立、相互竞争的公司。这场较量是不对等的，因为工会能够拉一家公司打另一家。结果，各公司抵挡不住过分的工资要求，只能转而涨价。最终受害的是消费者：他支付的价格，不是由自由市场竞争——供求法则——决定，而是由全国性工会领袖的专断决定。倘若福特公司的雇员只能与福特谈，克莱斯勒的雇员只能与克莱斯勒谈，以此类推，那要好得多。集体谈判程序只要限定于直接相关的雇主与雇员之间，就能在所有行业中为共同利益服务。

===Original===
Let us henceforth make war on all monopolies﻿—whether corporate or union. The enemy of freedom is unrestrained power, and the champions of freedom will fight against the concentration of power wherever they find it.

===Chinese===
让我们从此向一切垄断开战——无论那是公司的垄断还是工会的垄断。自由之敌是不受约束的权力，自由的捍卫者在权力集中的地方发现它，就会在那里与之战斗。

## VII Taxes and Spending / VII 税收与开支

===Original===
We all have heard much throughout our lifetimes, and seen little happen, on the subject of high taxes. Where is the politician who has not promised his constituents a fight to the death for lower taxes﻿—and who has not proceeded to vote for the very spending projects that make tax cuts impossible? There are some the shoe does not fit, but I am afraid not many. Talk of tax reduction has thus come to have a hollow ring. The people listen, but do not believe. And worse: as the public grows more and more cynical, the politician feels less and less compelled to take his promises seriously.

===Chinese===
关于高税收，我们一生中听得太多，见到的行动却太少。哪一位政客不曾向选民许诺为减税血战到底——随后又不曾投票赞成那些恰恰使减税化为泡影的开支项目？确实有几位不合此例，但恐怕不多。于是，减税的空谈渐渐有了空洞的回声。人民在听，却不信。更糟的是：公众越玩世不恭，政客就越觉得犯不着把自己的诺言当真。

===Original===
I suspect that this vicious circle of cynicism and failure to perform is primarily the result of the Liberals’ success in reading out of the discussion the moral principles with which the subject of taxation is so intimately connected. We have been led to look upon taxation as merely a problem of public financing: How much money does the government need? We have been led to discount, and often to forget altogether, the bearing of taxation on the problem of individual freedom. We have been persuaded that the government has an unlimited claim on the wealth of the people, and that the only pertinent question is what portion of its claim the government should exercise. The American taxpayer, I think, has lost confidence in *his* claim to his money. He has been handicapped in resisting high taxes by the feeling that he is, in the nature of things, obliged to accommodate whatever need for his wealth government chooses to assert.

===Chinese===
我怀疑，这种玩世不恭与自食其言的恶性循环，主要是自由派得逞的结果：他们把与税收问题血脉相连的道德原则从讨论中剔除出去了。我们被引导着把税收仅仅看成一个公共财政问题：政府需要多少钱？我们被引导着轻视、甚至常常完全遗忘税收对个人自由问题的影响。我们被说服：政府对人民的财富拥有无限的要求权，唯一值得讨论的问题只是政府该行使这一要求权的多大份额。我想，美国纳税人已经对*自己本人*对金钱的要求权失去了信心。他觉得按照事物的本性，政府无论主张什么样的财富需要，他都有义务满足——这份感觉束缚了他抵抗重税的手脚。

===Original===
The “nature of things,” I submit, is quite different. Government does *not* have an unlimited claim on the earnings of individuals. One of the foremost precepts of the natural law is man’s right to the possession and the use of his property. And a man’s earnings are his property as much as his land and the house in which he lives. Indeed, in the industrial age, earnings are probably the most prevalent form of property. It has been the fashion in recent years to disparage “property rights”﻿—to associate them with greed and materialism. This attack on property rights is actually an attack on freedom. It is another instance of the modern failure to take into account the *whole* man. How can a man be truly free if he is denied the means to exercise freedom? How can he be free if the fruits of his labor are not his to dispose of, but are treated, instead, as part of a common pool of public wealth? Property and freedom are inseparable: to the extent government takes the one in the form of taxes, it intrudes on the other.

===Chinese===
而我敢说，「事物的本性」完全是另一回事。政府对个人的收入*并不*拥有无限的要求权。自然法的首要诫命之一，就是人对财产的占有权与使用权。一个人的收入，正如他的土地和他居住的房子一样，是他的财产。的确，在工业时代，收入大概是财产最普遍的形态。近年来贬损「财产权」成了时髦——把它同贪婪和物质主义挂钩。这种对财产权的攻击，实际上是对自由的攻击。它是现代人无视*完整的人*的又一例证。一个人若被剥夺了行使自由的手段，他怎么可能是真正自由的？如果他劳动的果实不归他支配，反被当作公共财富蓄水池的一部分，他怎么可能自由？财产与自由不可分离：政府以税收形式取走前者到什么程度，它对后者的侵入就到什么程度。

===Original===
Here is an indication of how taxation currently infringes on our freedom. A family man earning $4,500 a year works, on the average, twenty-two days a month. Taxes, visible and invisible, take approximately 32% of his earnings. This means that one-third, or seven whole days, of his monthly labor goes for taxes. The average American is therefore working one-third of the time for government: a third of what he produces is not available for his own use but is confiscated and used by others who have not earned it. Let us note that by this measure the United States is already one-third “socialized.” The late Senator Taft made the point often. “You can socialize,” he said, “just as well by a steady increase in the burden of taxation beyond the 30% we have already reached as you can by government seizure. The very imposition of heavy taxes is a limit on a man’s freedom.”

===Chinese===
这里可以约略看出今天的税收如何侵害我们的自由。一个年收入 4,500 美元的养家者，平均每月工作二十二天。有形的和无形的税收大约拿走他收入的 32%。这就是说，他每月的劳动有三分之一——整整七天——是为税而干。可见普通美国人已有三分之一的时间在为政府工作：他生产的东西有三分之一不能归他自己支配，而是被没收，被那些并未挣得它的人使用。请注意，以此衡量，美国已经「社会主义化」了三分之一。已故的塔夫脱参议员常讲这一点。「你尽可以，」他说，「通过把我们现已达到的 30% 的税负再不断提高来实现社会主义化，其效果同政府直接没收一样。重税的课征本身就是对人的自由的限制。」

===Original===
But having said that each man has an inalienable right to his property, it also must be said that every citizen has an obligation to contribute his fair share to the legitimate functions of government. Government, in other words, has *some* claim on our wealth, and the problem is to define that claim in a way that gives due consideration to the property rights of the individual.

===Chinese===
但说了每个人对自己的财产拥有不可让渡的权利之后，还必须说：每个公民都有义务为政府的正当职能公平地出一份力。换言之，政府对我们的财富拥有*某种*要求权，问题在于如何界定这份要求权，使个人的财产权得到应有的尊重。

===Original===
The size of the government’s rightful claim﻿—that is, the total amount it may take in taxes﻿—will be determined by how we define the “legitimate functions of government.” With regard to the federal government, the Constitution is the proper standard of legitimacy: its “legitimate” powers, as we have seen are those the Constitution has delegated to it. Therefore, if we adhere to the Constitution, the federal government’s total tax bill will be the cost of exercising such of its *delegated* powers as our representatives deem necessary in the national interest. But conversely, when the federal government enacts programs that are *not* authorized by its delegated powers, the taxes needed to pay for such programs *exceed* the government’s rightful claim on our wealth.

===Chinese===
政府正当要求权的规模——也就是它可以通过税收取得的总额——取决于我们如何界定「政府的正当职能」。就联邦政府而言，宪法正是正当性的准绳：如前所见，它的「正当」权力就是宪法授予它的那些权力。因此，只要我们恪守宪法，联邦政府的税单总额就应当是行使那些*授予*权力中经我们的代表认定符合国家利益所必需者的成本。反过来说，当联邦政府推行*并未*经其授予权力授权的计划时，为这些计划筹措的税款就*越出了*政府对我们财富的正当要求权。

===Original===
The distribution of the government’s claim is the next part of the definition. What is a “fair share?” I believe that the requirements of justice here are perfectly clear: *government has a right to claim an equal percentage of each man’s wealth, and no more*. Property taxes are typically levied on this basis. Excise and sales taxes are based on the same principle﻿—though the tax is levied on a transaction rather than on property. *The principle is equally valid with regard to incomes, inheritances and gifts.* The idea that a man who makes $100,000 a year should be forced to contribute ninety percent of his income to the cost of government, while the man who makes $10,000 is made to pay twenty percent is repugnant to my notions of justice. I do not believe in punishing success. To put it more broadly, I believe it is contrary to the natural right to property to which we have just alluded﻿—and is therefore immoral﻿—to deny to the man whose labor has produced more abundant fruit than that of his neighbor the opportunity of enjoying the abundance he has created. As for the claim that the government *needs* the graduated tax for revenue purposes, the facts are to the contrary. The total revenue collected from income taxes beyond the twenty percent level amounts to less than $5 billion﻿—less than the federal government now spends on the one item of agriculture.

===Chinese===
政府要求权如何分配，是界定的下一部分。什么是「公平的一份」？我相信，正义在此处的要求一清二楚：*政府有权对每个人的财富抽取相同的百分比，且仅止于此*。财产税通常就是按这个基础课征的。货物税和销售税基于同一原则——只是课税的对象是交易而非财产。*这一原则对收入、遗产和赠与同样有效。*让年收入 100,000 美元的人把九成收入交给政府开销，而年收入 10,000 美元的人只交两成——这种观念与我的正义观格格不入。我不相信惩罚成功。说得再宽一点，我认为：拒绝让劳动果实比邻居更丰硕的人享受自己创造的丰裕，有悖于我们刚刚提到的财产自然权利——因而是不道德的。至于说政府出于岁收目的*需要*累进税，事实恰恰相反。从税率超过百分之二十那一段征得的所得税总额不足 50 亿美元——还不及联邦政府如今花在农业这一项上的钱。

===Original===
The graduated tax is a *confiscatory* tax. Its effect, and to a large extent its aim, is to bring down all men to a common level. Many of the leading proponents of the graduated tax frankly admit that their purpose is to redistribute the nation’s wealth. Their aim is an egalitarian society﻿—an objective that does violence both to the charter of the Republic and the laws of Nature. We are all equal in the eyes of God but we are equal *in no other respect*. Artificial devices for enforcing equality among unequal men must be rejected if we would restore that charter and honor those laws.

===Chinese===
累进税是一种*没收性*的税。它的效果——很大程度上也是它的目的——是把所有人都拉低到同一个水平。累进税的许多头号鼓吹者坦率承认，他们的目的就是重新分配国民财富。他们追求的是一个平均主义的社会——这个目标既践踏共和国的立国宪章，也践踏自然法则。在上帝眼中我们人人平等，但*除上帝眼中之外，我们无一处平等*。强使不平等的人们划一的人工手段必须抛弃，否则我们便无从恢复那份宪章、尊崇那些法则。

===Original===
One problem with regard to taxes, then, is to enforce justice﻿—to abolish the graduated features of our tax laws; and the sooner we get at the job, the better.

===Chinese===
因此，税收的一个问题就是实现正义——废除我们税法中的累进条款；而且动手越早越好。

===Original===
The other, and the one that has the greatest impact on our daily lives, is to reduce the volume of taxes. And this takes us to the question of government spending. While there is something to be said for the proposition that spending will never be reduced so long as there is money in the federal treasury, I believe that as a practical matter spending cuts must come before tax cuts. If we reduce taxes before firm, principled decisions are made about expenditures, we will court deficit spending and the inflationary effects that invariably follow.

===Chinese===
另一个问题，也是对我们日常生活影响最大的问题，是缩减税收的总量。这就把我们带到政府开支的问题上。虽然「只要国库还有钱，开支就永远减不下来」这种说法不无道理，但我认为就实际操作而言，必须先削减开支，再削减税收。倘若在尚未对支出作出坚定而有原则的决定之前就减税，我们将招来赤字开支，以及必然随之而来的通胀恶果。

===Original===
It is in the area of spending that the Republican Party’s performance, in its seven years of power, has been most disappointing.

===Chinese===
正是在开支领域，共和党执政七年来的表现最令人失望。

===Original===
In the Summer of 1952, shortly after the Republican Convention, the two men who had battled for the Presidential nomination met at Morningside Heights, New York, to discuss the problem of taxes and spending. After the conference, Senator Taft announced: “General Eisenhower emphatically agrees with me in the proposal to reduce drastically overall expenses. Our goal is about $70 billion in fiscal 1954 (President Truman had proposed $81 billion) and $60 billion in fiscal 1955﻿ ﻿… Of course, I hope we may do better than that and that the reduction can steadily continue.” Thereafter, the idea of a $60 billion budget in 1955, plus the promise of further reductions later on, became an integral part of the Republican campaign.

===Chinese===
1952 年夏，共和党全国代表大会后不久，那两位角逐过总统候选人提名的人在纽约州莫宁赛德高地会晤，讨论税收与开支问题。会谈之后，塔夫脱参议员宣布：「艾森豪威尔将军完全赞同我提出的大幅削减总开支的方案。我们的目标是 1954 财年约 700 亿美元（杜鲁门总统提出的是 810 亿美元），1955 财年 600 亿美元……当然，我希望我们能做得更好，并使削减稳步持续下去。」此后，1955 年 600 亿美元预算的构想，加上日后进一步削减的承诺，成了共和党竞选纲领不可分割的一部分。

===Original===
Now it would be bad enough if we had simply failed to redeem our promise to reduce spending; the fact, however, is that federal spending has greatly *increased* during the Republican years. Instead of a $60 billion budget, we are confronted, in fiscal 1961, with a budget of approximately $80 billion. If we add to the formal budget figure disbursements from the so-called trust funds for Social Security and the Federal Highway Program﻿—as we must if we are to obtain a realistic picture of federal expenditures﻿—total federal spending will be in the neighborhood of *$95 billion*.

===Chinese===
如果我们仅仅是没有兑现削减开支的诺言，那已经够糟了；而事实是，共和党执政这些年，联邦开支大幅*增加*了。我们迎来的不是 600 亿美元的预算，而是 1961 财年大约 800 亿美元的预算。倘若把所谓信托基金——社会保障和联邦公路计划——的支出也加进正式预算数字（要得到联邦开支的真实图景，我们就必须这样做），联邦开支总额将在 *950 亿美元*上下。

===Original===
We are often told that increased federal spending is simply a reflection of the increased cost of national defense. This is untrue. In the last ten years purely *domestic* expenditures have increased from $15.2 billion, in fiscal 1951, to a proposed $37.0 billion in fiscal 19612﻿—*an increase* of 143%! Here are the figures measured by a slightly different yardstick: during the last five years of the Truman Administration the average annual federal expenditure for domestic purposes was $17.7 billion; during the last five years of the Eisenhower Administration it was $33.6 billion, an increase of 89%.

===Chinese===
我们常听说，联邦开支的增长不过反映了国防费用的上涨。这不是实情。过去十年，纯*国内*开支已从 1951 财年的 152 亿美元增至 1961 财年拟议的 370 亿美元2——*增长*了 143%！换一把稍有不同的尺子来量：杜鲁门政府最后五年，联邦国内开支年均 177 亿美元；艾森豪威尔政府最后五年为 336 亿美元，增长 89%。

===Original===
Some allowance must be made, of course, for the increase in population; obviously the same welfare program will cost more if there are more people to be cared for. But the increase in population does not begin to account for the increase in spending. During the ten-year period in which federal spending will have increased by 143%, our population will have increased by roughly 18%. Nor does inflation account for the difference. In the past ten years the value of the dollar has decreased less than 20%. Finally, we are often told that the government’s *share* of total spending in the country is what is important and consequently we must take into account the increase in gross national product. Again, however, the increase in G.N.P., which was roughly 40% over the past ten years, is not comparable to a 143% increase in federal spending. The conclusion here is inescapable﻿—that far from arresting federal spending and the trend toward Statism we Republicans have kept the trend moving forward.

===Chinese===
当然，人口增长要打一点折扣：要照顾的人多了，同样的福利计划自然要花更多的钱。但人口增长远远解释不了开支的增长。在联邦开支增长 143% 的那十年里，我们的人口大约只增长 18%。通货膨胀也解释不了这个差额。过去十年，美元的币值下降不足 20%。最后，我们还常听说，要紧的是政府在全国总开支中的*份额*，因此必须考虑国民生产总值的增长。然而，过去十年国民生产总值增长约 40%，与联邦开支增长 143% 相比，不可同日而语。结论无法回避：我们共和党人非但没有遏止联邦开支和国家主义的趋势，反而推着这趋势继续向前。

===Original===
I do not mean to suggest, of course, that things would have been different under a Democratic Administration. Every year the Democratic national leadership demands that the federal government spend *more* than it is spending, and that Republicans propose to spend. *And this year, several weeks before President Eisenhower submitted his 1961 budget, The Democratic National Advisory Council issued a manifesto calling for profligate spending increases in nearly every department of the federal government; the demands for increases in domestic spending alone could hardly cost less than $20 billion a year.*

===Chinese===
我当然不是说，换民主党执政情况就会不同。民主党的全国领导层年年都要求联邦政府花得比正在花的、比共和党提议花的*更多*。*而且就在今年，在艾森豪威尔总统提交 1961 年度预算之前几周，民主党全国咨询委员会发表了一份宣言，要求联邦政府几乎每个部门都大手笔增加开支；仅国内开支一项的增加要求，一年就少不了 200 亿美元。*

===Original===
I do mean to say, however, that *neither* of our political parties has seriously faced up to the problem of government spending. The recommendations of the Hoover Commission which could save the taxpayer in the neighborhood of $7 billion a year have been largely ignored. Yet even these recommendations, dealing as they do for the most part with extravagance and waste, do not go to the heart of the problem. The root evil is that the government is engaged in activities in which it has no legitimate business. As long as the federal government acknowledges responsibility in a given social or economic field, its spending in that field cannot be substantially reduced. As long as the federal government acknowledges responsibility for education, for example, the amount of federal aid is bound to increase, at the very least, in direct proportion to the cost of supporting the nation’s schools. *The only way to curtail spending substantially, is to eliminate the programs on which excess spending is consumed.*

===Chinese===
我要说的是：我们的两个政党*都*没有严肃正视政府开支问题。胡佛委员会的建议本可为纳税人每年节省约 70 亿美元，却大体遭到忽视。然而，即便是这些建议——它们处理的多半是奢靡与浪费——也未能触及问题的核心。病根在于：政府正在从事它没有正当理由插手的活动。只要联邦政府承认自己对某一社会或经济领域负有责任，它在该领域的开支就无法实质削减。例如，只要联邦政府承认对教育负有责任，联邦援助的数额就必然至少随全国办学成本的增加而等比例增加。*要实质地缩减开支，唯一的办法是砍掉吞噬超额开支的那些计划。*

===Original===
The government must begin to *withdraw* from a whole series of programs that are outside its constitutional mandate﻿—from social welfare programs, education, public power, agriculture, public housing, urban renewal and all the other activities that can be better performed by lower levels of government or by private institutions or by individuals. I do not suggest that the federal government drop all of these programs overnight. But I do suggest that we establish, by law, a rigid timetable for a staged withdrawal. We might provide, for example, for a 10% spending reduction each year in all of the fields in which federal participation is undesirable. It is only through this kind of determined assault on the principle of unlimited government that American people will obtain relief from high taxes, and will start making progress toward regaining their freedom.

===Chinese===
政府必须开始从一整批越出其宪法授权的计划中*退出*——从社会福利计划、教育、公共电力、农业、公共住房、城市更新，以及所有其余可由较低层级政府、私人机构或个人办得更好的活动中退出。我不是说联邦政府应在一夜之间砍掉所有这些计划。我确实要说的是：我们应当以法律确定一张刚性的、分阶段撤出的时间表。比如可以规定：在联邦参与并不可取的所有领域，开支每年削减 10%。唯有以这种坚定的攻势冲击无限政府原则，美国人民才能从重税下获得解脱，并开始朝着重获自由的方向前进。

===Original===
And let us, by all means, remember the *nation’s* interest in reducing taxes and spending. The need for “economic growth” that we hear so much about these days will be achieved, not by the government harnessing the nation’s economic forces, but by emancipating them. By reducing taxes and spending we will not only return to the individual the means with which he can assert his freedom and dignity, but also guarantee to the nation the economic strength that will always be its ultimate defense against foreign foes.

===Chinese===
而且，务必让我们记住减税减支对*国家*的利益。这些天我们听得太多的「经济增长」，其实现之道不在政府驾驭全国的经济力量，而在解放这些力量。减税减支，不仅能把个人得以主张自身自由与尊严的手段还给他，还将保证国家拥有那种永远是对外敌的最终防御的经济力量。

## VIII The Welfare State / VIII 福利国家

===Original===
> **Washington**﻿—The President estimated that the expenditures of the Department of Health, Education and Welfare in the fiscal year 1961 (including Social Security payments) would exceed $15,000,000,000. Thus the current results of New Deal legislation are Federal disbursements for human welfare in this country second only to national defense.

===Chinese===
> **华盛顿**——总统估计，卫生、教育与福利部 1961 财年的支出（含社会保障付款）将超过 15,000,000,000 美元。可见新政立法的今日成果，是联邦在这个国家为人民福利所作的支出已仅次于国防。

===Original===
For many years it appeared that the principal domestic threat to our freedom was contained in the doctrines of Karl Marx. The collectivists﻿—non-Communists as well as Communists﻿—had adopted the Marxist objective of “socializing the means of production.” And so it seemed that if collectivization were imposed, it would take the form of a State owned and operated economy. I doubt whether this is the main threat any longer.

===Chinese===
多年来，对我国自由的主要国内威胁，似乎包含在卡尔·马克思的学说里。集体主义者——共产党人和非共产党人——都接受了马克思主义「生产资料社会化」的目标。因此，倘若集体化真的强加于我们，似乎将采取国家占有并经营经济的形式。我怀疑如今这还是不是主要威胁。

===Original===
The collectivists have found, both in this country and in other industrialized nations of the West, that free enterprise has removed the economic and social conditions that might have made a class struggle possible. Mammoth productivity, wide distribution of wealth, high standards of living, the trade union movement﻿—these and other factors have eliminated whatever incentive there might have been for the “proletariat” to rise up, peaceably or otherwise, and assume direct ownership of productive property. Significantly, the bankruptcy of doctrinaire Marxism has been expressly acknowledged by the Socialist Party of West Germany, and by the dominant faction of the Socialist Party of Great Britain. In this country the abandonment of the Marxist approach (outside the Communist Party, of course) is attested to by the negligible strength of the Socialist Party, and more tellingly perhaps, by the content of left wing literature and by the programs of left wing political organizations such as the Americans For Democratic Action.

===Chinese===
集体主义者在本国和西方其他工业化国家都发现：自由企业制度已经铲除了可能使阶级斗争成为现实的经济社会条件。庞大的生产率、广泛的财富分配、高水平的生活、工会运动——这些以及其他因素，已消磨掉「无产阶级」起来——无论以和平还是其他方式——直接夺取生产资料所有权的一切动力。值得注意的是，教条式马克思主义的破产，已由西德社会党和英国社会党的主导派系明确承认。在这个国家，马克思主义路线的抛弃（当然，共产党除外）可以从社会党的微不足道得到证明，而更有说服力的，也许是左翼出版物的内容和美国人争取民主行动组织（Americans For Democratic Action）等左翼政治组织的纲领。

===Original===
The currently favored instrument of collectivization is the Welfare State. The collectivists have not abandoned their ultimate goal﻿—to subordinate the individual to the State﻿—but their strategy has changed. They have learned that Socialism can be achieved through Welfarism quite as well as through Nationalization. They understand that private property can be confiscated as effectively by taxation as by expropriating it. They understand that the individual can be put at the mercy of the State﻿—not only by making the State his employer﻿—but by divesting him of the means to provide for his personal needs and by giving the State the responsibility of caring for those needs from cradle to grave. Moreover, they have discovered﻿—and here is the critical point﻿—that *Welfarism is much more compatible with the political processes of a democratic society*. Nationalization ran into popular opposition, but the collectivists feel sure the Welfare State can be erected by the simple expedient of buying votes with promises of “free” federal benefits﻿—“free” housing, “free” school aid, “free” hospitalization, “free” retirement pay and so on﻿ ﻿… The correctness of this estimate can be seen from the portion of the federal budget that is now allocated to welfare, an amount second only to the cost of national defense.3

===Chinese===
眼下得宠的集体化工具，是福利国家（Welfare State）。集体主义者并没有放弃他们的终极目标——使个人从属于国家——但战略变了。他们已经懂得：通过福利主义（Welfarism）实现社会主义，同通过国有化一样有效。他们明白，没收私有财产，靠征税同靠剥夺一样干净利落。他们明白，把个人置于国家掌心——不仅可以让国家当他的雇主——还可以剥夺他自行满足个人需要的手段，把从摇篮到坟墓照料这些需要的责任交给国家。何况他们还发现——这正是要害——*福利主义与民主社会的政治进程相容得多*。国有化曾遭民众反对，但集体主义者确信，福利国家只凭一个简单的权宜之计就能建立起来：用许诺「免费」联邦好处——「免费」住房、「免费」助学补助、「免费」住院医疗、「免费」退休金等等——来收买选票……这一估计是否正确，看看如今联邦预算中拨给福利的那一份便知：其数额仅次于国防开支。3

===Original===
I do not welcome this shift of strategy. Socialism-through-Welfarism poses a far greater danger to freedom than Socialism-through-Nationalization precisely because it is more difficult to combat. The evils of Nationalization are self-evident and immediate. Those of Welfarism are veiled and tend to be postponed. People can understand the consequences of turning over ownership of the steel industry, say, to the State; and they can be counted on to oppose such a proposal. But let the government increase its contribution to the “Public Assistance” program and we will, at most, grumble about excessive government spending. The effect of Welfarism on freedom will be felt later on﻿—after its beneficiaries have become its victims, after dependence on government has turned into bondage and it is too late to unlock the jail.

===Chinese===
我并不乐见这一战略转变。经由福利主义实现的社会主义，对自由的威胁远大于经由国有化实现的社会主义，恰恰因为它更难对抗。国有化的祸害不言自明、立竿见影。福利主义的祸害则遮遮掩掩，且往往延后发作。人们能够理解，比如，把钢铁业的所有权交给国家意味着什么；而且可以指望他们反对这种提案。但让政府多往「公共援助」计划里投点钱，我们至多抱怨一句政府开销太大而已。福利主义对自由的影响要到日后再被感受到——那时它的受益者已变成它的受害者，对政府的依赖已变成枷锁，而开锁已经太迟。

===Original===
But a far more important factor is Welfarism’s strong emotional appeal to many voters, and the consequent temptations it presents the average politician. It is hard, as we have seen, to make out a case for State ownership. It is very different with the rhetoric of humanitarianism. How easy it is to reach the voters with earnest importunities for helping the needy. And how difficult for Conservatives to resist these demands without appearing to be callous and contemptuous of the plight of less fortunate citizens. Here, perhaps, is the best illustration of the failure of the Conservative demonstration.

===Chinese===
但更重要的一个因素，是福利主义对许多选民强大的情感号召力，以及由此给普通政客带来的诱惑。如前所见，为国家所有权张目是难的。人道主义的辞令就完全是另一回事了。用恳切的哀告去打动选民、号召帮助穷苦人，何其容易。而保守主义者要抵制这些要求又不显得冷酷无情、不显得蔑视不幸同胞的困境，又何其困难。这里，或许就是保守主义论证失败的最佳例证。

===Original===
I know, for I have heard the questions often. Have you no sense of social obligation? the Liberals ask. Have you no concern for people who are out of work? for sick people who lack medical care? for children in overcrowded schools? Are you unmoved by the problems of the aged and disabled? Are you *against* human welfare?

===Chinese===
我知道这些，因为这些问题我听得太多。你们就没有社会责任感吗？自由派问。你们就不关心失业的人吗？不关心缺医少药的病人吗？不关心挤在超员学校里的孩子吗？老人和残障者的种种问题，你们无动于衷吗？你们*反对*人类福利吗？

===Original===
The answer to all of these questions is, of course, no. But a simple “no” is not enough. I feel certain that Conservatism is through unless Conservatives can demonstrate and communicate the difference between being concerned with these problems and believing that the federal government is the proper agent for their solution.

===Chinese===
所有这些问题的答案当然都是否定的。但一句简单的「不是」并不够。我确信，除非保守主义者能够论证并讲清下面这个区别——关心这些问题，与相信联邦政府是解决它们的合适代理人，是两回事——否则保守主义就完了。

===Original===
The long range political consequences of Welfarism are plain enough: as we have seen, the State that is able to deal with its citizens as wards and dependents has gathered unto itself unlimited political and economic power and is thus able to rule as absolutely as any oriental despot.

===Chinese===
福利主义的长期政治后果够清楚了：如前所见，一个能把公民当作被监护人和依附者来对待的国家，已经把无限的政治经济权力集于一身，因而能够像任何东方专制君主一样绝对地统治。

===Original===
Let us, however, weigh the consequences of Welfarism on the individual citizen.

===Chinese===
不过，让我们来掂量掂量福利主义加于个别公民的后果。

===Original===
Consider, first, the effect of Welfarism on the donors of government welfare﻿—not only those who pay for it but also the voters and their elected representatives who decide that the benefits shall be conferred. Does some credit redound on them for trying to care for the needs of their fellow citizens? Are they to be commended and rewarded, at some moment in eternity, for their “charity?” I think not. Suppose I should vote for a measure providing for free medical care: I am unaware of any moral virtue that is attached to my decision to confiscate the earnings of X and give them to Y.

===Chinese===
首先，看看福利主义对政府福利的给予方——不仅包括出钱的人，还包括决定授予这些好处的选民及其当选代表——的影响。他们为照顾同胞的需要出了力，就有什么功德落在他们头上吗？在永恒的某个时刻，他们会因这份「慈善」而受称许、得报偿吗？我看不会。假如我投票赞成一项免费医疗的法案：我看不出，我决定没收 X 的收入转交给 Y 的这一决定，附带着什么道德上的美德。

===Original===
Suppose, however, that X approves of the program﻿—that he has voted for welfarist politicians with the idea of helping his fellow man. Surely the wholesomeness of his act is diluted by the fact that he is voting not only to have his own money taken but also that of his fellow citizens who may have different ideas about their social obligations. Why does not such a man, instead, contribute what he regards as his just share of human welfare to a private charity?

===Chinese===
然而，假定 X 赞成这个计划——他投票给福利主义政客，是抱着帮助同胞的念头。他这一行为的纯正，无疑要打折扣：他投票不仅是要拿出自己的钱，还要拿出他的同胞们的钱，而那些人对自己的社会义务可能有完全不同的想法。这样的人为什么不把他认为自己应付的那份人类福利，捐给私人慈善机构呢？

===Original===
Consider the consequences to the recipient of welfarism. For one thing, he mortgages himself to the federal government. In return for benefits﻿—which, in the majority of cases, he pays for﻿—he concedes to the government the ultimate in political power﻿—the power to grant or withhold from him the necessities of life as the government sees fit. Even more important, however, is the effect on him﻿—the elimination of any feeling of responsibility for his own welfare and that of his family and neighbors. A man may not immediately, or ever, comprehend the harm thus done to his character. Indeed, this is one of the great evils of Welfarism﻿—that it transforms the individual from a dignified, industrious, self-reliant *spiritual* being into a dependent animal creature without his knowing it. There is no avoiding this damage to character under the Welfare State. Welfare programs cannot help but promote the idea that the government *owes* the benefits it confers on the individual, and that the individual is entitled, by right, to receive them. Such programs are sold to the country precisely on the argument that government has an *obligation* to care for the needs of its citizens. Is it possible that the message will reach those who vote for the benefits, but not those who receive them? How different it is with private charity where both the giver and the receiver understand that charity is the product of the humanitarian impulses of the giver, not the due of the receiver.

===Chinese===
再看看福利主义加于接受者的后果。其一，他把自己抵押给了联邦政府。作为好处的回报——这些好处在多数情形下本是他自己付的——他让渡给政府一种终极的政治权力：由政府酌情赐予或扣留他的生活必需品。然而更重要的是对他本人的影响：他对自己的福利、对其家人和邻人的福利的责任感被消除了。这种对他品格的戕害，一个人可能不会立刻明白，甚至永远不明白。这确是福利主义的大恶之一：它把人从一个有尊严的、勤勉的、自立的*灵性*存在，不知不觉地变成一头依附性的动物性存在。在福利国家之下，这种品格损伤无从避免。福利计划不可避免地助长一种观念：政府给予个人的好处是政府*欠*下的，个人*有权*领取。这些计划向全国推销时，打的恰恰是这个旗号：政府有关怀公民需要的*义务*。难道这讯息会传到投票赞成好处的人耳中，却传不到领取好处的人耳中？私人慈善就全然不同：给予者和接受者都明白，慈善是给予者人道情怀的产物，不是接受者应得的款项。

===Original===
Let us, then, not blunt the noble impulses of mankind by reducing charity to a mechanical operation of the federal government. Let us, by all means, encourage those who are fortunate and able to care for the needs of those who are unfortunate and disabled. But let us do this in a way that is conducive to the spiritual as well as the material well-being of our citizens﻿—and in a way that will preserve their freedom. Let welfare be a private concern. Let it be promoted by individuals and families, by churches, private hospitals, religious service organizations, community charities and other institutions that have been established for this purpose. If the objection is raised that private institutions lack sufficient funds, let us remember that every penny the federal government does *not* appropriate for welfare is potentially available for private use﻿—and without the overhead charge for processing the money through the federal bureaucracy. Indeed, high taxes, for which government Welfarism is so largely responsible, is the biggest obstacle to fund raising by private charities.

===Chinese===
那么，让我们不要把慈善降格为联邦政府的机械操作，从而磨钝人类的高尚冲动。让我们务必鼓励幸运而有力者去照顾不幸与残障者的需要。但让我们以一种既增进公民灵性福祉又增进其物质福祉的方式来做——以一种保全他们自由的方式来做。让福利成为私人的事务。让它由个人与家庭、教会、私立医院、宗教服务组织、社区慈善机构以及其他为此目的而设的机构去推动。如果有人反对说，私人机构资金不足，那么请记住：联邦政府*不*征作福利的每一个铜板，都潜在地可供私人使用——而且不必支付把钱经由联邦官僚体系过一道手的管理费。的确，政府福利主义要负极大责任的重税，正是私人慈善募款的最大障碍。

===Original===
Finally, if we deem public intervention necessary, let the job be done by local and state authorities that are incapable of accumulating the vast political power that is so inimical to our liberties.

===Chinese===
最后，倘若我们认定公共干预必不可少，那就让地方和州当局来做这件事——它们无力积聚那足以危害我们自由的庞大政治权力。

===Original===
The Welfare State is *not* inevitable, as its proponents are so fond of telling us. There is nothing inherent in an industrialized economy, or in democratic processes of government that *must* produce de Tocqueville’s “guardian society.” Our future, like our past, will be what we make it. And we can shatter the collectivists’ designs on individual freedom if we will impress upon the men who conduct our affairs this one truth: that the material and spiritual sides of man are intertwined; that it is impossible for the State to assume responsibility for one without intruding on the essential nature of the other; that if we take from a man the personal responsibility for caring for his material needs, we take from him also the will and the opportunity to be free.

===Chinese===
福利国家*并非*不可抗拒，尽管它的鼓吹者最爱这么告诉我们。工业化经济也好，民主的政治程序也好，其中没有任何东西*必定*造就托克维尔所说的「监护者社会」。我们的未来一如我们的过去，将是我们自己造出来的。只要我们把下面这条真理铭刻在主政者心上，我们就能粉碎集体主义者对个人自由的图谋：人的物质一面与灵性一面相互交织；国家要为其中一面承担责任，就不可能不侵入另一面的本质；我们若夺走一个人照料自身物质需要的个人责任，也就夺走了他自由的意志与自由的机会。

## IX Some Notes on Education / IX 教育杂议

===Original===
I agree with lobbyists for federal school aid that education is one of the great problems of our day. I am afraid, however, that their views and mine regarding the nature of the problem are many miles apart. They tend to see the problem in *quantitative* terms﻿—not enough schools, not enough teachers, not enough equipment. I think it has to do with *quality*: How good are the schools we have? Their solution is to spend more money. Mine is to raise standards. Their recourse is to the federal government. Mine is to the local public school board, the private school, the individual citizen﻿—as far away from the federal government as one can possibly go. And I suspect that if we knew which of these two views on education will eventually prevail, we would know also whether Western civilization is due to survive, or will pass away.

===Chinese===
我同意联邦助学拨款说客的一点：教育是我们时代的大问题之一。然而，他们对问题性质的看法与我的看法，恐怕相距十万八千里。他们倾向于从*数量*上看问题——学校不够、教师不够、设备不够。我认为问题出在*质量*上：我们现有的学校到底有多好？他们的解决办法是多花钱。我的办法是提高标准。他们求告的对象是联邦政府。我求告的对象是地方公立学校董事会、私立学校、公民个人——离联邦政府越远越好。而且我怀疑：倘若我们能知道这两种教育观最终哪一种会占上风，我们也就知道了西方文明是注定存续，还是注定消亡。

===Original===
To put this somewhat differently, I believe that our ability to cope with the great crises that lie ahead will be enhanced in direct ratio as we recapture the lost art of learning, and will diminish in direct ratio as we give responsibility for training our children’s minds to the federal bureaucracy.

===Chinese===
换一种说法：我相信，我们应对未来重大危机的能力，将随着我们重拾那门失传的学习技艺而成正比地增强，也将随着我们把训练孩子头脑的责任交给联邦官僚体系而成正比地衰减。

===Original===
But let us put these differences aside for the moment and note four reasons why federal aid to education is objectionable even if we grant that the problem is primarily quantitative.

===Chinese===
不过，让我们暂且把这些分歧放一放，来指出联邦教育拨款之所以不可取的四条理由——即便我们承认问题主要出在数量上。

===Original===
The first is that federal intervention in education is unconstitutional. It is the fashion these days to say that responsibility for education “traditionally” rests with the local community﻿—as a prelude to proposing an exception to the tradition in the form of federal aid. This “tradition,” let us remember, is also the *law*. It is sanctioned by the Constitution of the United States, for education is one of the powers reserved to the States by the Tenth Amendment. Therefore, any federal aid program, however desirable it might appear, must be regarded as illegal until such time as the Constitution is amended.

===Chinese===
第一条：联邦干预教育违宪。如今时兴说教育责任「历来」归属地方社区——这话通常是把联邦拨款作为这条惯例之例外提出来的铺垫。但我们应当记住，这条「惯例」同时也是*法律*。它得到美利坚合众国宪法的确认，因为教育是第十修正案保留给各州的权力之一。因此，任何联邦拨款计划，无论表面多么可取，在宪法修正之前都必须视为非法。

===Original===
The second objection is that the alleged need for federal funds has never been convincingly demonstrated. It all depends, of course, on how the question is put. If you ask, Does State X need additional educational facilities? the answer may be yes. But if you ask, Does State X require additional facilities that are beyond the reach of its own financial means? the answer is invariably no. The White House Conference on Education in 1955 was, most of us will remember, an elaborate effort to demonstrate popular support for federal aid. As expected, the “consensus” of the conference was that more federal aid was needed. However, the conferees reached another conclusion that was hardly noticed by the press. “No state represented,” the Conference report stated, “has a demonstrated financial incapacity to build the schools they will need during the next five years.” What is lacking, the report went on, *is not money, but a “political determination powerful enough to overcome all the obstacles.”*

===Chinese===
第二条反对理由：所谓需要联邦资金，从来未被有说服力地证明过。当然，一切取决于问题怎么问。如果你问：X 州需要增加教育设施吗？答案也许是肯定的。但如果你问：X 州所需的额外设施，超出了它自身财力的承受范围吗？答案一律是否定的。我们多数人都记得，1955 年的白宫教育会议是一场精心策划，旨在显示民众支持联邦拨款。不出所料，会议的「共识」是需要更多的联邦拨款。然而与会者还达成了另一项结论，新闻界几乎无人注意。会议报告说：「与会各州无一被证明在财力上无力修建未来五年所需的学校。」报告接着说，*缺的不是钱，而是一种「足以克服一切障碍的强大政治决心」。*

===Original===
Through the succeeding five years, congressional committees have listened to hundreds of hours of testimony in favor of federal aid, but they have never heard that 1955 finding successfully contradicted. What the White House conferees were saying in 1955, and what proponents of federal aid to education have been saying ever since, is that because a few States have not seen fit to take care of their school needs, it is incumbent upon the federal government to take up the slack. My view is that if State X possesses the wealth to educate its children adequately, but has failed to utilize its wealth for that purpose, it is up to the people of State X to take remedial action through their local and state governments. The federal government has neither the right nor the duty to intervene.

===Chinese===
此后的五年里，国会各委员会听了几百个小时支持联邦拨款的证词，却从未听到 1955 年那项结论被成功地驳倒。1955 年白宫与会者所说的、以及联邦教育拨款鼓吹者从此一直说的，无非是：因为少数几个州没有照顾好自己的学校需要，联邦政府就有责任来填这个缺口。我的看法是：倘若 X 州拥有足以让子女受到充分教育的财力，却没有把这份财力用到这上面，那么采取补救行动是 X 州人民的事，该由他们通过自己的地方和州政府去做。联邦政府既无干预的权利，也无干预的义务。

===Original===
Let us, moreover, keep the problem in proper perspective. The national school system is *not* in distress. Shortly before the Senate debate this year on increased federal aid, I asked Mr. Arthur Flemming the Secretary of Health, Education and Welfare, how many of the Nation’s school districts were in actual trouble﻿—how many, that is, had reached their bonded limit. His answer was approximately 230. Now there are roughly 42,000 school districts in America. Thus, proponents of federal aid are talking about a problem that affects only one-half of one percent of our school districts! I cannot believe that the state governments responsible for those areas are incapable of making up whatever deficiencies exist. It so happens that the same deficiency figure﻿—one-half of one percent﻿—applies to my own state of Arizona. And Arizona proudly turned down federal funds under the 1958 National Defense Education Act on the grounds that Arizonans, themselves, were quite capable of closing the gap.

===Chinese===
而且，让我们把问题放到恰当的比例来看。全国的学校系统*并没有*陷入困境。今年参议院辩论增加联邦拨款前不久，我问卫生、教育与福利部部长阿瑟·弗莱明先生：全国有多少学区真正陷入了困境——也就是有多少学区已达举债上限。他的回答是大约 230 个。而全美学区大约有 42,000 个。可见，联邦拨款的支持者谈论的，是一个只影响我们学区百分之零点五的问题！我无法相信，负责那些地区的州政府无力弥补现存的任何亏空。巧的是，同一个亏空比例——百分之零点五——恰好适用于我自己的州亚利桑那。而亚利桑那自豪地拒绝了 1958 年《国防教育法》下的联邦资金，理由是亚利桑那人自己完全有能力填平缺口。

===Original===
This may be the place, while we are speaking of need, to lay to rest the notion that the American people have been niggardly in support of their schools. Since the end of World War II, Americans have built 550,000 classrooms at a cost of approximately $19 billion﻿—almost all of which was raised at the local level. This new construction provided space for over 15 million pupils during a period when the school population increased by only 10 million pupils. It is evident, therefore, that increased school expenditures have more than kept pace with increased school needs.

===Chinese===
趁着谈「需要」，这里或许正可以埋葬一种说法：美国人民对自己学校的支持一直很吝啬。自第二次世界大战结束以来，美国人修建了 550,000 间教室，耗资约 190 亿美元——其中几乎全部是在地方层面筹措的。这些新建校舍为超过 1,500 万学生提供了座位，而同期在校生只增加了 1,000 万。由此可见，学校开支的增长非但没有落后于学校需要的增长，反而超过了它。

===Original===
Here are some of the figures. In the school year 1949﻿–﻿50 there were 25 million students enrolled in various education institutions in the United States. In the year 1959﻿–﻿60 there were 34.7 million﻿—an increase of 38%. During the same period revenues for school use, raised largely at the local level, increased from 5.4 billion to 12.1 billion﻿—an increase of 124%. When school expenditures increase three and a half times as fast as the school population, I do not think that the adequacy of America’s “traditional” approach to education is open to serious question.

===Chinese===
这里有一些数字。1949–50 学年，美国各类教育机构注册学生为 2,500 万。1959–60 学年为 3,470 万——增长 38%。同期教育经费——主要在地方层面筹措——从 54 亿美元增至 121 亿美元——增长 124%。当学校开支的增长速度三倍半于在校人口的增长速度时，我认为，美国「传统的」教育之道是否充足，已无认真争论的余地。

===Original===
The third objection to federal aid is that it promotes the idea that federal school money is “free” money, and thus gives the people a distorted picture of the cost of education. I was distressed to find that five out of six high school and junior college students recently interviewed in Phoenix said they favored federal aid because it would mean more money for local schools and ease the financial burden on Arizona taxpayers.

===Chinese===
第三条反对理由：联邦拨款助长了「联邦的教育钱是白来的钱」的观念，从而给人民一幅关于教育成本的扭曲图景。令我痛心的是，最近在菲尼克斯受访的中学生和初级学院学生中，六分之五的人说他们赞成联邦拨款，因为那意味着地方学校有更多的钱，还能减轻亚利桑那纳税人的负担。

===Original===
The truth, of course, is that the federal government has no funds except those it extracts from the taxpayers who reside in the various States. The money that the federal government pays to State X for education has been taken from the citizens of State X in federal taxes and comes back to them, minus the Washington brokerage fee. The less wealthy States, to be sure, receive slightly more than they give, just as the more wealthy States receive somewhat less. But the differences are negligible. For the most part, federal aid simply substitutes the tax-collecting facilities of the federal government for those of local governments. This fact cannot be stressed often enough; for stripped of the idea that federal money is free money, federal aid to education is exposed as an act of naked compulsion﻿—a decision by the federal government to force the people of the States to spend more money than they choose to spend for this purpose voluntarily.

===Chinese===
真相当然在于：联邦政府除了从各州纳税人身上抽取的钱之外，并没有别的资金。联邦政府付给 X 州办教育的钱，本是从 X 州公民那里以联邦税收拿走的，如今转一圈回到他们手里，扣掉了华盛顿的经纪费。较穷的州所得确实略多于所出，正如较富的州所得略少于所出。但差额微不足道。在极大程度上，联邦拨款不过是让联邦政府的征税机器代替地方政府的征税机器而已。这一事实再怎么强调也不为过；因为一旦剥掉「联邦的钱是白来的钱」这层想法，联邦教育拨款就暴露为赤裸裸的强制——联邦政府作出决定，强迫各州人民为这个目的花掉比他们自愿选择的更多的钱。

===Original===
The fourth objection is that federal aid *to* education inevitably means federal control *of* education. For many years, advocates of federal aid denied that aid implies control, but in the light of the National Defense Education Act of 1958 they cannot very well maintain their position. Federal aid under the act is conditioned upon compliance by the States and local educational institutions with various standards and specifications laid down by the Department of Health, Education and Welfare. *There are no less than twelve direct controls of this kind in the act.* Moreover, the acknowledged purpose of the act is to persuade local educational institutions to put greater emphasis on the physical sciences and other subjects directly related to national defense. I do not question the desirability of encouraging increased proficiency in the physical sciences, but when the federal government does the encouraging through the withholding and granting of funds, I do not see how it can be denied that the federal government is helping to determine the *content* of education; and influencing content is the last, not the first, stage of control.

===Chinese===
第四条反对理由：联邦*对*教育的资助，不可避免地意味着联邦*对*教育的控制。多年来，联邦拨款的鼓吹者否认资助意味着控制，但在 1958 年《国防教育法》面前，他们很难再坚持这个立场了。该法之下的联邦拨款，以各州和地方教育机构遵从卫生、教育与福利部规定的各项标准与规格为条件。*这类直接控制，该法中不下十二处。*此外，该法公开承认的宗旨，就是劝说地方教育机构更加重视自然科学及其他与国防直接相关的科目。我不怀疑提高自然科学造诣是可取的，但当联邦政府的「鼓励」是靠资金的给予与扣留来实现时，我看不出怎么能否认：联邦政府正在参与决定教育的*内容*；而影响内容，是控制的最后一步，不是第一步。

===Original===
Nobody should be surprised that aid has led to controls. It could, and *should not* be otherwise. Congress cannot be expected to appropriate the people’s money and make no provision for how it will be spent. Congress would be shirking its responsibilities to the taxpayer if it distributed his money willy-nilly, without regard to its use. Should Congress permit the use of federal funds to subsidize Communist schools and thus promote the cause of our enemies? Of course not. But a prohibition of such use is clearly an exercise of federal control. Congress will always feel impelled to establish conditions under which people’s money is to be spent, and while some controls may be wise we are not guaranteed against unwise controls any more than we are guaranteed against unwise Congressmen. The mistake is not the controls but appropriating the money that requires controls.

===Chinese===
资助带来了控制，谁都不该惊讶。事情只能如此，也*理应*如此。不能指望国会拨出人民的钱，却对钱怎么花不置一词。倘若国会漫无目标地撒钱，不问用途，那才是对纳税人失职。难道国会该允许联邦资金去补贴共产党人的学校、从而助长我们敌人的事业吗？当然不该。但禁止这种用途，分明就是行使联邦控制。国会将永远忍不住为人民的金钱设定使用条件；有些控制或许是明智的，但正如没有人能保证我们不会遇上不明智的议员，也没有人能保证我们不会遇上不明智的控制。错误不在控制，而在拨出那笔需要控制的巨款。

===Original===
So much for the evils and dangers of federal aid. Note that I have not denied that many of our children are being inadequately educated, or that the problem is nationwide. I have only denied that it is the kind of problem that requires a solution at the national level. To the extent the problem is quantitative﻿—to the extent we have too few classrooms and pay some of our teachers too little money﻿—the shortages can be taken care of by the localities concerned. But more: to the extent the problem is qualitative﻿—which in my opinion it mainly is﻿—it is manifestly one that lends itself to correction at the local level. There is no place where deficiencies in the *content* of an educational system can be better understood than locally where a community has the opportunity to view and judge the product of its own school system.

===Chinese===
联邦拨款的弊害与危险，说得够多了。请注意，我并没有否认我们的许多孩子所受的教育是不充分的，也没有否认问题遍及全国。我否认的只是：这是那种需要在国家层面解决的问题。在问题属于数量性的范围内——教室太少、给某些教师的薪水太低——缺额可由有关地方自行解决。但更进一步：在问题属于质量性的范围内——依我看问题主要是质量性的——它显然更适合在地方层面纠正。一个教育制度*内容*上的缺陷，再没有比地方更清楚它的了：在地方，社区有机会审视和评判自己学校系统的产出。

===Original===
In the main, the trouble with American education is that we have put into practice the educational philosophy expounded by John Dewey and his disciples. In varying degrees we have adopted what has been called “progressive education.”

===Chinese===
大体而言，美国教育的毛病在于：我们把约翰·杜威及其门徒阐发的教育哲学付诸了实践。我们在不同程度上采用了所谓的「进步主义教育」。

===Original===
Subscribing to the egalitarian notion that every child must have the same education, we have neglected to provide an educational system which will tax the talents and stir the ambitions of our best students and which will thus insure us the kind of leaders we will need in the future.

===Chinese===
由于信奉每个孩子必须受同样教育的平均主义观念，我们忽略了去建立一种能磨砺最优秀学生的才智、激发其抱负、从而确保我们未来得到所需领袖的教育制度。

===Original===
In our desire to make sure that our children learn to “adjust” to their environment, we have given them insufficient opportunity to acquire the knowledge that will enable them to *master* their environment.

===Chinese===
由于一心要确保孩子学会「适应」环境，我们没有给他们足够的机会去获得那种能让他们*驾驭*环境的知识。

===Original===
In our attempt to make education “fun,” we have neglected the academic disciplines that develop sound minds and are conducive to sound characters.

===Chinese===
由于力图把教育变得「有趣」，我们荒疏了那些砥砺健全心智、有助于健全品格的学术训练。

===Original===
Responding to the Deweyite attack on methods of teaching, we have encouraged the teaching profession to be more concerned with *how* a subject is taught than with *what* is taught. Most important of all: in our anxiety to “improve” the world and insure “progress” we have permitted our schools to become laboratories for social and economic change according to the predilections of the professional educators. We have forgotten that the proper function of the school is to transmit the cultural heritage of one generation to the next generation, and to so train the minds of the new generation as to make them capable of absorbing ancient learning and applying it to the problem of its own day.

===Chinese===
由于迎合杜威派对教学方法的攻击，我们纵容教师行业更关心一门课*怎么*教，而不是*教什么*。最重要的：由于急欲「改善」世界、确保「进步」，我们听任学校按照职业教育家的偏好，变成社会经济改造的实验室。我们忘记了学校的正当职能：把一代人的文化遗产传给下一代，并这样训练新一代的头脑，使他们能够吸收古老的学问，将其应用于自身时代的问题。

===Original===
The fundamental explanation of this distortion of values is that we have forgotten that purpose of education. Or better: we have forgotten for *whom* education is intended. The function of our schools is not to educate, or elevate, *society*; but rather to educate *individuals* and to equip them with the knowledge that will enable them to take care of society’s needs. We have forgotten that a society progresses only to the extent that it produces leaders that are capable of guiding and inspiring progress. And we cannot develop such leaders unless our standards of education are geared to excellence instead of mediocrity. We must give full rein to individual talents, and we must encourage our schools to enforce the academic disciplines﻿—to put preponderant emphasis on English, mathematics, history, literature, foreign languages and the natural sciences. We should look upon our schools﻿—not as a place to train the “whole character” of the child﻿—a responsibility that properly belongs to his family and church﻿—but to train his *mind*.

===Chinese===
这种价值扭曲的根本原因，在于我们忘记了教育的目的。或者更准确地说：忘记了教育是为*谁*而设的。我们学校的职能不是教育或提升*社会*，而是教育*一个个的人*，用知识武装他们，使他们能够照料社会的需要。我们忘记了：一个社会只有培养出能够指引并激励进步的领袖，才会进步。而唯有把教育标准对准卓越而非平庸，我们才能造就这样的领袖。我们必须让个人才能尽情驰骋，必须鼓励我们的学校厉行学术训练——把压倒性的重点放在英语、数学、历史、文学、外语和自然科学上。我们应当把学校看作——不是训练孩子「整个品格」的场所——那责任本属于他的家庭和教会——而是训练他的*头脑*的场所。

===Original===
Our country’s past progress has been the result, not of the mass mind applying average intelligence to the problems of the day, but of the brilliance and dedication of wise individuals who applied their wisdom to advance the freedom and the material well-being of all of our people. And so if we would improve education in America﻿—and advance the fortunes of freedom﻿—we will not rush to the federal treasury with requests for money. We will focus attention on our local community, and make sure that our schools, private and public, are performing the job the Nation has the right to expect of them.

===Chinese===
我们国家昔日的进步，不是群体心智以平均智力应付当世问题的结果，而是睿智的个人以其光辉与献身、运用自己的智慧推进全体人民的自由与物质福祉的结果。因此，倘若我们想改善美国的教育——并增进自由的气运——我们就不会一头冲向联邦国库伸手要钱。我们将把注意力放到我们的地方社区，确保我们的学校，无论私立公立，都在履行国家有权期望它们完成的那份工作。

## X The Soviet Menace / X 苏联的威胁

===Original===
And still the awful truth remains: We can establish the domestic conditions for maximizing freedom, along the lines I have indicated, and yet become slaves. We can do this by losing the Cold War to the Soviet Union.

===Chinese===
而可怕的事实依然摆在那里：我们可以按照我在前文指明的路线，为自由的极大化确立种种国内条件，却照样沦为奴隶。实现这一点的途径，就是把冷战输给苏联。

===Original===
American freedom has always depended, to an extent, on what is happening beyond our shores. Even in Ben Franklin’s day, Americans had to reckon with foreign threats. Our forebearers knew that “keeping a Republic” meant, above all, keeping it safe from foreign transgressors; they knew that a people cannot live and work freely, and develop national institutions conducive to freedom, except in peace and with independence. In those early days the threat to peace and independence was very real. We were a fledgling-nation and the slightest misstep﻿—or faint hearts﻿—would have laid us open to the ravages of predatory European powers. It was only because wise and courageous men understood that defense of freedom required risks and sacrifice, as well as their belief in it, that we survived the crisis of national infancy. As we grew stronger, and as the oceans continued to interpose a physical barrier between ourselves and European militarism, the foreign danger gradually receded. Though we always had to keep a weather eye on would-be conquerors, our independence was acknowledged and peace, unless we chose otherwise, was established. Indeed, after the Second World War, we were not only master of our own destiny; we were master of the world. With a monopoly of atomic weapons, and with a conventional military establishment superior to any in the world, America was﻿—in relative and absolute terms﻿—the most powerful nation the world had ever known. American freedom was as secure as at any time in our history.

===Chinese===
美国的自由，向来在某种程度上取决于我们海岸之外发生的事情。即便在本·富兰克林（Ben Franklin）的时代，美国人也不得不提防外来的威胁。我们的先辈懂得，「守护共和」首先意味着使它免遭外国侵犯者的荼毒；他们懂得，一个民族唯有在和平与独立之中，才能自由地生活与劳作，才能发展出有助于自由的全国性制度。在那些早年岁月，和平与独立所受的威胁是实实在在的。我们彼时还是个羽毛未丰的国家，稍有失足——或稍露怯意——就会把门户敞给贪得无厌的欧洲列强去蹂躏。正是因为有明智而勇敢的人士懂得捍卫自由需要冒险与牺牲，并且怀着对自由的信念，我们才渡过了国家婴儿期的危机。随着我们日渐强大，随着各大洋继续在我们与欧洲军国主义之间横亘一道天然屏障，外来的危险逐渐消退。尽管我们始终要对那些潜在的征服者保持警惕，我们的独立已获承认，和平——除非我们自己另作选择——已经确立。事实上，第二次世界大战之后，我们不仅掌握着自己的命运，而且掌握着世界的命运。凭借原子武器的独家垄断，加上举世无匹的常规军事建制，美国——无论相对而言还是绝对而言——是世上有史以来最强大的国家。美国的自由，其稳固程度不减于我国历史上的任何时期。

===Original===
Now, a decade and half later, we have come full circle and our national existence is once again threatened as it was in the early days of the Republic. Though we are still strong physically, we are in clear and imminent danger of being overwhelmed by alien forces. We are confronted by a revolutionary world movement that possesses not only the will to dominate absolutely every square mile of the globe, but increasingly the capacity to do so: a military power that rivals our own, political warfare and propaganda skills that are superior to ours, an international fifth column that operates conspiratorially in the heart of our defenses, an ideology that imbues its adherents with a sense of historical mission; and all of these resources controlled by a ruthless despotism that brooks no deviation from the revolutionary course. This threat, moreover, is growing day by day. And it has now reached the point where American leaders, both political and intellectual, are searching desperately for means of “appeasing” or “accommodating” the Soviet Union as the price of national survival. The American people are being told that, however valuable their freedom may be, it is even more important to live. A craven fear of death is entering the American consciousness; so much so that many recently felt that honoring the chief despot himself was the price we had to pay to avoid nuclear destruction.

===Chinese===
十五年过去了，我们兜了一个大圈，国家的生存重新像共和国早年那样受到威胁。尽管我们在实体力量上依然强大，却已明显而迫近地面临被异己力量压倒的危险。我们面对的是一场革命性的世界运动：它不仅有意志要支配全球的每一平方英里土地，而且日益具备这样做的能力——一支堪与我国匹敌的军事力量，胜过我们的政治战与宣传技能，一支在我们防御腹地以阴谋方式活动的国际第五纵队，一种使其信徒满怀历史使命感的意识形态；而所有这些资源，都掌控在一个不容革命航向稍有偏离的冷酷专制政权手里。何况，这一威胁正与日俱增。事态如今已发展到这般地步：美国的政界与思想界领袖都在不顾一切地寻找「绥靖」或「迁就」苏联的办法，把那当作国家生存的代价。美国人民被告知：无论你们的自由何等宝贵，活下去更要紧。一种怯懦的怕死心理正在渗入美国人的意识；以至于不久之前，许多人还觉得向那位头号专制暴君本人致敬，是我们为免遭核毁灭不得不付出的代价。

===Original===
The temptation is strong to blame the deterioration of America’s fortunes on the Soviet Union’s acquisition of nuclear weapons. But this is self-delusion. The rot had set in, the crumbling of our position was already observable, long before the Communists detonated their first Atom Bomb. Even in the early 1950s, when America still held unquestioned nuclear superiority, it was clear that we were losing the Cold War. Time and again in my campaign speeches of 1952 I warned my fellow Arizonans that “American Foreign Policy has brought us from a position of undisputed power, in seven short years, to the brink of possible disaster.” And in the succeeding seven years, that trend, because its cause remains, has continued.

===Chinese===
人们很想把美国国运的衰败归咎于苏联获得了核武器。但这是自欺。早在共产党人引爆第一颗原子弹之前，溃烂就已开始，我们地位的崩塌已清晰可见。即便在 1950 年代初，当美国还握有无可争议的核优势之时，我们正在输掉冷战已是一目了然。1952 年竞选演讲中，我曾一再告诫我的亚利桑那同胞：「美国的外交政策，在短短七年之内，就把我们从无可争议的强国地位带到了可能覆亡的边缘。」而其后七年，这一趋势因其根源未除而延续至今。

===Original===
The real cause of the deterioration can be simply stated. Our enemies have understood the nature of the conflict, and we have not. They are determined to win the conflict, and we are not.

===Chinese===
这一衰败的真正原因，一句话就能说清。我们的敌人懂得这场冲突的性质，我们不懂。他们决心赢得这场冲突，我们不。

===Original===
I hesitate to restate the obvious﻿—to say again what has been said so many times before by so many others: that the Communists’ aim is to conquer the world. I repeat it because it is the beginning and the end of our knowledge about the conflict between East and West. I repeat it because I fear that however often we have given lip-service to this central political fact of our time, very few of us have *believed* it. If we had, our entire approach to foreign policy over the past fourteen years would have been radically different, and the course of world events radically changed.

===Chinese===
对于这种明摆着的事，我本迟疑是否还要重述——是否还要把别人已经说过无数遍的话再说一遍：共产党人的目标就是征服世界。我之所以重提，是因为它是我们对东西方冲突全部认识的起点与终点。我之所以重提，是因为我担心：尽管我们对这个时代的核心政治事实口头上承认过无数次，真正*相信*它的却寥寥无几。倘若我们真信，过去十四年我们外交政策的整个路数就会截然不同，世界事变的进程也会彻底改观。

===Original===
If an enemy power is bent on conquering you, and proposes to turn all of his resources to that end, he is at war with you; and you﻿—unless you contemplate surrender﻿—are at war with him. Moreover﻿—unless you contemplate treason﻿—your objective, like his, will be victory. Not “peace,” but victory. Now, while traitors (and perhaps cowards) have at times occupied key positions in our government, it is clear that our national leadership over the past fourteen years has favored neither surrender nor treason. It is equally clear, however, that our leaders have not made *victory* the goal of American policy. And the reason that they have not done so, I am saying, is that they have never believed deeply that the Communists are in earnest.

===Chinese===
如果一个敌对国家一心要征服你，并打算倾其全部资源用于这个目的，那它就是在同你交战；而你——除非你打算投降——也是在同它交战。进而，除非你打算叛国，你的目标就和它的一样，是胜利。不是「和平」，而是胜利。诚然，叛国者（或许还有懦夫）有时曾窃据我们政府中的要职；但显而易见，过去十四年我们的国家领导层既不倾向于投降，也不倾向于叛国。然而同样显而易见的是，我们的领导人从未把*胜利*定为美国政策的目标。而我要说的是，他们之所以没有这样做，是因为他们从未深切相信：共产党人是认真的。

===Original===
Our avowed national objective is “peace.” We have, with great sincerity, “waged” peace, while the Communists wage war. We have sought “settlements,” while the Communists seek victories. We have tried to pacify the world. The Communists mean to own it. Here is why the contest has been an unequal one, and why, essentially, we are losing it.

===Chinese===
我们公开宣告的国家目标是「和平」。我们怀着十足的诚意去「推行」和平，共产党人却在进行战争。我们寻求「和解」，共产党人寻求胜利。我们试图安抚世界，共产党人却要把世界据为己有。这场较量之所以不对等、我们之所以在实质上节节失利，症结就在这里。

===Original===
Peace, to be sure, is a proper goal for American policy﻿—as long as it is understood that peace is not all we seek. For we do not want the peace of surrender. We want a peace in which freedom and justice will prevail, and that﻿—given the nature of Communism﻿—is a peace in which Soviet power will no longer be in a position to threaten us and the rest of the world. A tolerable peace, in other words, must *follow* victory over Communism. We have been fourteen years trying to bury that unpleasant fact. It cannot be buried and any foreign policy that ignores it will lead to our extinction as a nation.

===Chinese===
诚然，和平是美国政策的正当目标——只要人们明白，和平并非我们追求的全部。因为我们不要投降换来的和平。我们要的是自由与公义得以伸张的和平，而鉴于共产主义的本性，那样的和平只能是苏维埃势力再也无法威胁我们和世界其他地区的和平。换言之，一种可以容忍的和平，必须*在*战胜共产主义*之后*方能到来。十四年来，我们一直想把这一令人不快的事实埋葬掉。但它埋葬不掉；任何无视这一事实的外交政策，都将把我们引向亡国。

===Original===
We do not, of course, want to achieve victory by force of arms. If possible, overt hostilities should always be avoided; especially is this so when a shooting war may cause the death of many millions of people, including our own. But we cannot, for that reason, make the avoidance of a shooting war our chief objective. If we do that﻿—if we tell ourselves that it is more important to avoid shooting than to keep our freedom﻿—we are committed to a course that has only one terminal point: surrender. We cannot, by proclamation, make war “unthinkable.” For it is not unthinkable to the Communists: naturally, they would prefer to avoid war, but they are prepared to risk it, in the last analysis, to achieve their objectives. We must, in our hearts, be equally dedicated to our objectives. If war is unthinkable to us but not to them, the famous “balance of terror” is not a balance at all, but an instrument of blackmail. U.S.-Soviet power may be in balance; but if we, and not they, rule out the possibility of using that power, the Kremlin can create crisis after crisis, and force the U.S., because of our greater fear of war, to back down every time. And it cannot be long before a universal Communist Empire sits astride the globe.

===Chinese===
我们当然不想靠武力赢得胜利。只要有可能，公开的战事总应避免；当一场枪炮之战可能夺去千百万人的生命——其中也包括我们自己的生命——时，尤其应当如此。但我们不能因此就把避免开战当作首要目标。倘若我们那样做——倘若我们对自己说，避免开火比保住自由更要紧——我们就把自己押上了一条只有一个终点站的路：投降。我们无法凭一纸声明就宣布战争「不可想象」。因为在共产党人看来，战争并非不可想象：他们自然也宁愿避免战争，但归根到底，为了实现其目标，他们准备冒战争的风险。我们也必须在内心同样矢志于自己的目标。如果战争对我们不可想象、对他们却不然，那么那道著名的「恐怖平衡」根本不是什么平衡，而是一件讹诈的工具。美苏力量或许势均力敌；但把动用这份力量之可能性排除在外的，是我们而不是他们，克里姆林宫就能一场接一场地制造危机，并利用我们更怕战争这一点，逼我们每次都退让。用不了多久，一个普天的共产主义帝国就会骑踞全球之上。

===Original===
The rallying cry of an appeasement organization, portrayed in a recent novel on American politics, was “I would rather crawl on my knees to Moscow than die under an Atom bomb.” This sentiment, of course, repudiates everything that is courageous and honorable and dignified in the human being. We must﻿—as the first step toward saving American freedom﻿—affirm the contrary view and make it the cornerstone of our foreign policy: that we would rather die than lose our freedom. There are ways which I will suggest later on﻿—not easy ways, to be sure﻿—in which we may save both our freedom *and* our lives; but all such suggestions are meaningless and vain unless we first understand what the objective is. We want to stay alive, of course; but more than that we want to be free. We want to have peace; but before that we want to establish the conditions that will make peace tolerable. “Like it or not,” Eugene Lyons has written, “the great and inescapable task of our epoch is not to end the Cold War but to win it.”

===Chinese===
在最近一部描写美国政治的小说里，一个绥靖组织的鼓动口号是：「我宁肯跪着爬向莫斯科，也不愿死在原子弹底下。」这种心绪，自然把人身上一切有勇气、有荣誉感、有尊严的东西全盘否弃。我们必须——作为挽救美国自由的第一步——申明相反的立场，并把它立为我们外交政策的基石：我们宁死也不丧失自由。下文我会提出一些办法——诚然不是容易的办法——凭此我们或许能既保住自由，*又*保住性命；但除非我们先弄清目标是什么，一切这类提议都毫无意义、终归落空。我们当然想活下去；但更要紧的，我们要自由。我们要和平；但在那之前，我们要先确立使和平变得可以容忍的条件。尤金·莱昂斯（Eugene Lyons）写道：「无论你喜欢与否，我们这个时代伟大而无可回避的任务，不是结束冷战，而是打赢冷战。」

===Original===
I suggest that we look at America’s present foreign policy, and ask whether it is conducive to victory. There are several aspects of this policy. Let us measure each of them by the test: Does it help defeat the enemy?

===Chinese===
我提议我们检视美国当前的外交政策，问一问它是否有助于取得胜利。这项政策有若干侧面。让我们用这把检验尺逐一衡量：它是否有助于击败敌人？

Defensive Alliances / 防御性同盟

===Original===
Through NATO, SEATO and the Central Treaty Organization in mid-Asia, we have served notice on the Kremlin that overt Communist aggression in certain areas of the world will be opposed by American arms. It is likely that the existence of these alliances has helped discourage military adventurism by the Communists.

===Chinese===
通过北约（NATO）、东南亚条约组织（SEATO）以及设在亚洲中部的中央条约组织（Central Treaty Organization），我们向克里姆林宫下了通告：共产党在世界某些地区的公开侵略，必将遭到美国武力的抵抗。这些同盟的存在，很可能有助于遏制共产党人的军事冒险主义。

===Original===
Still, we should not overestimate the value of the alliances. Though they play a significant role in safeguarding American freedom, there are a number of reasons why it is a limited role.

===Chinese===
尽管如此，我们不应高估这些同盟的价值。它们在捍卫美国自由方面的作用固然可观，但这一作用之所以有限，有数条理由。

===Original===
First, the alliance system is not coextensive with the line that must be held if enemy expansion is to be prevented. There are huge areas of the non-Communist world that the alliances do not touch. Nor﻿—even assuming America is strong enough to guard a worldwide defense perimeter﻿—is there any prospect of bringing these areas into the system. The so-called neutral countries of the Middle East, Africa and Southern Asia have refused to align themselves with the anti-Communist cause, and it is in those areas, as we might expect, that the Communists are making significant strides. This is a critical weakness. If all of those areas should fall under Communist rule, the alliances would be outflanked everywhere: the system would be reduced to a series of outposts, and probably indefensible ones at that, in a wholly hostile world.

===Chinese===
第一，同盟体系并不与那道必须守住的防线同其广狭；不守住这道防线，敌人的扩张便无从遏止。非共产主义世界有大片地区是同盟体系触及不到的。而且——即便假定美国强大到足以守卫一道环球防御圈——把这些地区纳入体系也毫无前景。中东、非洲和南亚那些所谓「中立」的国家拒绝同反共事业站在一边；而正如我们所料，共产党人正是在那些地区大步推进。这是一个严重的弱点。倘若那些地区全部落入共产党统治之下，同盟就将处处被侧翼包抄：整个体系将沦为一系列前哨据点——而且多半是守不住的前哨——置身于一个全然敌对的世界。

===Original===
Secondly, the alliance system does not protect even its members against the most prevalent kind of Communist aggression: political penetration and internal subversion. Iraq is a case in point. We had pledged ourselves to support the Iraqi against overt Soviet aggression﻿—not only under the Baghdad Pact of which Iraq was the cornerstone, but also under the Eisenhower Doctrine. Iraq fell victim to a pro-Communist coup without an American or Russian shot being fired. Cuba is another example. If the Red Army had landed in Havana, we would have come to Cuba’s aid. Castro’s forces, however, were native Cubans; as a result, a pro-Communist regime has become entrenched on our very doorstep through the technique of internal subversion. And so it will always be with an enemy that lays even more emphasis on political warfare than on military warfare. So it will be until we learn to meet the enemy on his own grounds.

===Chinese===
第二，同盟体系甚至保护不了自己的成员免遭共产党最常见的那种侵略：政治渗透与内部颠覆。伊拉克便是一例。我们曾承诺支持伊拉克人抵御苏联的公开侵略——不仅依据以伊拉克为基石的巴格达条约，也依据艾森豪威尔主义。结果伊拉克一枪未发——美方俄方皆然——便沦为一场亲共政变的牺牲品。古巴是另一个例子。倘若红军在哈瓦那登陆，我们本会驰援古巴。然而卡斯特罗（Castro）的武装是古巴本地人；结果，一个亲共政权凭内部颠覆之术，就在我们自家的门口扎下了根。对一个把政治战看得比军事战还重的敌人来说，今后仍将如此。除非我们学会在敌人自己的地面上迎击它，这种局面还会一直继续下去。

===Original===
But thirdly, the alliance system cannot adequately protect its members even against *overt* aggression. In the past, the Communists have been kept in check by America’s strategic air arm. Indeed, in the light of the weakness of the allied nations’ conventional military forces, our nuclear superiority has been the alliances’ only real weapon. But as the Soviet Union draws abreast of us in nuclear strength, that weakness could prove our undoing. In a nuclear stalemate, where neither side is prepared to go “all out” over local issues, the side with the superior conventional forces has an obvious advantage. Moreover, it is clear that we cannot hope to match the Communist world man for man, nor are we capable of furnishing the guns and tanks necessary to defend thirty nations scattered over the face of the globe. The long-overdue answer, as we will see later on, lies in the development of a nuclear capacity for limited wars.

===Chinese===
但第三，同盟体系甚至不足以保护其成员抵御*公开的*侵略。过去，共产党人之所以被钳制，靠的是美国的战略空军。诚然，鉴于各盟国常规军事力量的薄弱，我们的核优势是这些同盟唯一真正的武器。但随着苏联的核力量与我们并驾齐驱，这一薄弱之处可能招致我们的覆灭。在核僵局之中，双方都不肯为局部争端倾尽全力，常规力量占优的一方就握有显而易见的好处。况且，显然我们无法指望在人力上同共产党世界逐一相抵，我们也没有能力为散布全球的三十个国家提供防御所需的枪炮坦克。这个迟迟未曾采取的答案，下文将会看到，在于发展用于有限战争的核力量。

===Original===
Finally﻿—and I consider this the most serious defect of all﻿—the alliance system is completely defensive in nature and outlook. This fact, in the light of the Communists’ dynamic, offensive strategy, ultimately dooms it to failure. No nation at war, employing an exclusively defensive strategy, can hope to survive for long. Like the boxer who refuses to throw a punch, the defense-bound nation will be cut down sooner or later. As long as every encounter with the enemy is fought on his initiative, on grounds of his choosing and with weapons of his choosing, we shall keep on losing the Cold War.

===Chinese===
最后——我认为这是全部缺陷中最严重的一条——同盟体系就其本性与取向而言是全然防御性的。鉴于共产党人进取的进攻战略，这一事实最终注定它要失败。处于战争之中的国家，若采取纯防御的战略，休想指望长久存活。正如拒绝出拳的拳击手迟早要被击倒，一味设防的国家迟早要被击溃。只要同敌人的每一次遭遇都依对方的主动、在对方选定的场地、用对方选定的武器来进行，我们就会把冷战一直输下去。

Foreign Aid / 对外援助

===Original===
Another aspect of our policy is the Foreign Aid program. To it, in the last fourteen years, we have committed over eighty billions of American treasure﻿—in grants, loans, material, and technical assistance. I will not develop here what every thinking American knows about this Gargantuan expenditure﻿—that it has had dire consequences, not only for the American taxpayer, but for the American economy; that it has been characterized by waste and extravagance both overseas and in the agencies that administer it; and that it has created a vast reservoir of anti-Americanism among proud peoples who, however irrationally, resent dependence on a foreign dole. I would rather put the question, Has the Foreign Aid program, for all of its drawbacks, made a compensating contribution toward winning the Cold War?

===Chinese===
我们政策的另一个侧面是对外援助计划。十四年来，我们为它投入了八百多亿美元的美国资财——有赠款、贷款、物资与技术援助。这笔挥霍无度的巨额开支意味着什么——它不仅给美国纳税人、也给美国经济带来了严重后果；它无论在海外还是在其主管机构里，都以浪费与奢靡著称；它在那些骄傲的民族中间蓄起了一大池反美情绪，这些民族不管多么不合情理，总怨恨仰赖外国的施舍——凡此种种，每个肯思考的美国人都心中有数，我在此不想展开。我倒愿意提出这样一个问题：对外援助计划撇开它的一切弊病不谈，究竟有没有为赢得冷战作出补偿性的贡献？

===Original===
And this test, let me say parenthetically, is the only one under which the Foreign Aid program can be justified. It cannot, that is to say, be defended as a charity. The American government does not have the right, much less the obligation, to try to promote the economic and social welfare of foreign peoples. Of course, all of us are interested in combating poverty and disease wherever it exists. *But the Constitution does not empower our government to undertake that job in foreign countries*, no matter how worthwhile it might be. Therefore, except as it can be shown to promote America’s national interests, the Foreign Aid program is unconstitutional.

===Chinese===
让我顺带说一句：这项检验，是对外援助计划唯一可以据以自证正当的标准。也就是说，它不能以慈善的名义来辩护。美国政府没有权利——更谈不上义务——去设法增进外国人民的经济与社会福利。当然，凡有贫困与疾病之处，我们都乐于与之斗争。*但是宪法并没有授权我们的政府去外国承担那项工作*，无论它多么有价值。因此，除非能证明它有助于促进美国的国家利益，对外援助计划就是违宪的。

===Original===
It can be argued, but not proved, that American aid helped prevent Western Europe from going Communist after the Second World War. It is true, for example, that the Communist parties in France and Italy were somewhat weaker after economic recovery than before it. But it does not follow that recovery *caused* the reduction in Communist strength, or that American aid caused the recovery. It is also true, let us remember, that West Germany recovered economically at a far faster rate than France or Italy, and received comparatively little American aid.

===Chinese===
可以说——但无法证明——美国的援助曾在第二次世界大战之后帮助西欧免于投向共产主义。例如，法国和意大利的共产党在经济复苏之后确实比复苏之前有所削弱。但由此并不能推出，是复苏*造成*了共产党力量的削弱，也不能推出，是美国的援助造成了复苏。我们还应记得，西德的经济复苏远比法国或意大利迅速，而它得到的美国援助相对而言却很少。

===Original===
It also can be argued that American military aid has made the difference between friendly countries having the power to fight off or discourage Communist aggression, and not having that power. Here, however, we must distinguish between friendly countries that were *not* able to build their own military forces, and those that were. Greece, Turkey, Free China, South Korea and South Vietnam needed our help. Other countries, England and France, for example, were able to maintain military forces with their own resources. For many years now, our allies in Western Europe have devoted smaller portions of their national budgets to military forces than we have. The result is that the American people, in the name of *military* aid, have been giving an *economic* handout to these nations; we have permitted them to transfer to their domestic economy funds which, in justice, should have been used in the common defense effort.

===Chinese===
还可以辩称：美国的军事援助，正是一些友好国家有力量击退或吓阻共产党的侵略、与没有这种力量两者之间的分野。但在这一点上，我们必须区分当时*无力*建立本国军事力量的友好国家，与*有此力量*的国家。希腊、土耳其、自由中国、南韩和南越需要我们的帮助。另一些国家，例如英国和法国，是能够靠自己的资源维持军事力量的。多年来，我们在西欧的盟友拨给军事力量的国家预算份额，一直比我们低。结果就是：美国人民以*军事*援助之名，一直在向这些国家发放*经济*施舍；我们竟容许它们把依公义本应用在共同防务大业上的资金，转移去充实它们本国的民生经济。

===Original===
Now let us note a significant fact. In each of the situations we have mentioned so far﻿—situations where some evidence exists that Foreign Aid has promoted American interests﻿—there is a common denominator: *in every case, the recipient government was already committed to our side*. We *may* have made these nations, on balance, stronger and more constant allies, though even that is debatable. But we did not cause them to alter their basic political commitments. This brings us to the rest of the Foreign Aid program﻿—and to the great fallacy that underlies it.

===Chinese===
现在让我们留意一个重要的事实。在我们迄今提到的每一种情形里——也就是多少有些证据表明对外援助曾增进美国利益的那些情形里——都有一个公分母：*在每一种情形中，受援政府本来就已站到了我们这一边*。我们*或许*使这些国家总体上成了更强、更恒久的盟友，尽管连这一点也尚可争论。但我们并没有促使它们改变基本的政治归属。由此便谈到对外援助计划的其余部分——以及支撑它的那个大谬误。

===Original===
Increasingly, our foreign aid goes not to our friends, but to professed neutrals﻿—and even to professed enemies. We furnish this aid under the theory that we can buy the allegiance of foreign peoples﻿—or at least discourage them from “going Communist”﻿—by making them economically prosperous. This has been called the “stomach theory of Communism,” and it implies that a man’s politics are determined by the amount of food in his belly.

===Chinese===
而今，我们的对外援助日益不去资助我们的朋友，而是去资助自称中立的国家——甚至自称与我们为敌的国家。我们发放这种援助，所依据的理论是：只要让外国人民在经济上富足起来，就能买得他们的忠诚——至少也能劝阻他们「投向共产党」。有人把这称作「共产主义的肠胃理论」，它意味着，一个人的政治立场是由他肚子里的食物多寡决定的。

===Original===
Everything we have learned from experience, and from our observation of the nature of man, refutes this theory. A man’s politics are, primarily, the product of his mind. Material wealth can help him further his political goals, but it will not change them. The fact that some poor, illiterate people have “gone Communist” does not prove that poverty caused them to do so any more than the fact that Alfred K. and Martha D. Stern are Communists proves that great wealth and a good education make people go Communist. Let us remember that Communism is a political movement, and that its weapons are primarily political. The movement’s effectiveness depends on small cadres of political activists, and these cadres are, typically, composed of literate and well-fed people. We are not going to change the minds of such political activists, or impede their agitation of the masses by a “war on poverty,” however worthy such an effort might be on humanitarian grounds.

===Chinese===
我们从经验中学到的一切，以及我们对人性的观察所得，全都驳斥这套理论。一个人的政治立场，首先是他的头脑的产物。物质财富可以帮助他推进政治目标，却改变不了目标。一些贫穷的、不识字的人「投向了共产党」，这个事实并不能证明是贫穷使他们如此；正如阿尔弗雷德·K·斯特恩和玛莎·D·斯特恩夫妇是共产党人，并不能证明巨大的财富和良好的教育会使人投向共产党。让我们记住：共产主义是一场政治运动，它的武器首先是政治性的。这场运动的效力，系于一小批一小批的政治骨干，而这些骨干通常正是识文断字、衣食无忧的人。无论「向贫困开战」在人道立场上多么值得称道，我们都无法靠它改变这类政治骨干的头脑，也无法靠它阻挠他们对群众的煽动。

===Original===
It thus makes little sense to try to promote anti-Communism by giving money to governments that are not anti-Communist, that are, indeed, far more inclined to the Soviet-type society than to a free one. And let us remember that the foreign policies of many of the allegedly neutral nations that receive our aid are not “neutral” at all. Is Sukarno’s Indonesia neutral when it encourages Red Chinese aggression? Or Nehru’s India when it censures the Western effort to recover Suez but refuses to censure the Soviet invasion of Hungary? Or Nasser’s United Arab Republic which equips its armed forces with Communist weapons and Communist personnel? Is American aid likely to make these nations less pro-Communist? Has it?

===Chinese===
因此，想靠把钱送给那些并非反共的政府来促进反共事业，实在没有多少道理；而事实上，那些政府宁向苏式社会倾斜，远甚于向自由社会倾斜。我们还应记得，许多自称中立、接受我们援助的国家，其外交政策根本谈不上「中立」。苏加诺（Sukarno）的印度尼西亚鼓励红色中国进行侵略，它中立吗？尼赫鲁（Nehru）的印度谴责西方收复苏伊士的行动，却拒绝对苏联入侵匈牙利加以谴责，它中立吗？纳赛尔（Nasser）的阿拉伯联合共和国用共产党的武器和共产党的人员装备自己的军队，它中立吗？美国的援助有可能使这些国家少一点亲共吗？它做到过吗？

===Original===
But let us, for the moment, concede the validity of the “stomach theory,” and ask a further question: Is our foreign aid program the kind that will bring prosperity to underdeveloped countries? We Americans believe﻿—and we can cite one hundred and fifty years of experience to support the belief﻿—that the way to build a strong economy is to encourage the free play of economic forces: free capital, free labor, a free market. Yet every one of the “neutral” countries we are aiding is committed to a system of State Socialism. Our present policy of government-to-government aid strengthens Socialism in those countries. We are not only perpetuating the inefficiency and waste that always attends government-controlled economies; by strengthening the hand of those governments, we are making it more difficult for free enterprise to take hold. For this reason alone, we should eliminate all government-to-government capital assistance and encourage the substitution of American private investment.

===Chinese===
不过，让我们姑且承认「肠胃理论」成立，再问一个问题：我们的对外援助计划，属于那种能给不发达国家带来繁荣的计划吗？我们美国人相信——并且可以援引 150 年的经验来支持这个信念——建立强健经济之道，在于让各种经济力量自由地发挥作用：自由的资本、自由的劳力、自由的市场。然而我们援助的每一个「中立」国家，奉行的都是国家社会主义（State Socialism）制度。我们现行的政府对政府援助政策，正在壮大这些国家的社会主义。我们不仅在延续政府统制经济素来的低效与浪费；由于加强了那些政府之手，我们还在使自由企业更难在那里立足。单凭这一条理由，我们就应当取消一切政府对政府的资金援助，鼓励代之以美国的私人投资。

===Original===
Our present Foreign Aid program, in sum, is not only ill-administered, but ill-conceived. It has not, in the majority of cases, made the free world stronger; it has made America weaker; and it has created in minds the world over an image of a nation that puts prime reliance, not on spiritual and human values, but on the material things that are the stock-in-trade of Communist propaganda. To this extent we have adopted Communist doctrine.

===Chinese===
总而言之，我们现行的对外援助计划不仅管理不善，而且构想失当。在多数情形下，它并没有使自由世界更强，而是使美国更弱；并且在全世界人们心中塑造了一个国家的形象：这个国家首要倚靠的，不是精神与人的价值，而是共产党宣传赖以兜售的那些物质东西。在这个程度上，我们采纳了共产党的教条。

===Original===
In the future, if our methods are to be in tune with our true objectives, we will confine foreign aid to military and technical assistance to those nations that need it and that are committed to a common goal of defeating world Communism.

===Chinese===
今后，倘若要让我们的方法同真正的目标合拍，我们将把对外援助限于军事与技术援助，只提供给那些需要它、并矢志于击败世界共产主义这一共同目标的国家。

Negotiations / 谈判

===Original===
As I write, the world is waiting for another round of diplomatic conferences between East and West. A full scale summit meeting is scheduled for Spring; later on, President Eisenhower and Premier Khrushchev will have further talks in the Soviet Union. And we are told that this is only the beginning of a long-range American policy to try to settle world problems by “negotiation.”

===Chinese===
在我写下这些文字的时候，全世界正在等待东西方之间又一轮外交会议。一场全面的首脑会议定于春季举行；稍后，德怀特·艾森豪威尔（Dwight D. Eisenhower）总统还将在苏联同尼基塔·赫鲁晓夫（Nikita Khrushchev）总理举行进一步的会谈。而人们告诉我们，这不过是一项试图以「谈判」解决世界问题的美国长远政策的开端。

===Original===
As the preparations for the Spring meetings go forward, I am struck by a singular fact: no one on our side claims﻿—let alone believes﻿—that the West will be stronger after these new negotiations than it is today. The same was true last Summer. We agreed to “negotiate” about Berlin﻿—not because we hoped to gain anything by such talks﻿—but because the Communists had created a “crisis,” and we could think of nothing better to do about it than go to the conference table. After all, we assured ourselves, there is no harm in talking.

===Chinese===
随着春季会议的筹备工作向前推进，一个非同寻常的事实令我心惊：我方没有一个人宣称——更谈不上相信——经过这些新的谈判，西方会比今天更强。去年夏天也是如此。我们同意就柏林问题「谈判」——不是因为我们指望从这种会谈中得到什么——而是因为共产党人制造了一场「危机」，而我们除了一头扎到谈判桌前，想不出更好的应对之策。我们对自己说：毕竟，谈谈总没有害处。

===Original===
I maintain there *is* harm in talking under present conditions. There are several reasons why this is so. First of all, Communists do not look upon negotiations, as we do, as an effort to reach an agreement. For them, negotiations are simply an *instrument* of political warfare. For them, a summit meeting is another battle in the struggle for the world. A diplomatic conference, in Communist language, is a “propaganda forum from which to speak to the masses over the heads of their leaders.”

===Chinese===
我要指出：在目前的条件下，谈话*确有*害处。何以如此，有数条理由。首先，共产党人不象我们这样，把谈判看作达成协议的努力。对他们来说，谈判不过是政治战的一种*工具*。在他们眼里，一次首脑会议，不过是争夺世界的斗争中的又一场战斗。用共产党的话说，一次外交会议，是一个「越过各国领导人直接向群众讲话的宣传讲坛」。

===Original===
Of course, if the Communists can obtain a formal agreement beneficial to them, so much the better. But if not the negotiations themselves will provide victory enough. For example, when the Soviets challenged our rights in West Berlin, we handed them a victory by the mere act of sitting down at the conference table. By agreeing to negotiate on that subject, we agreed that our rights in Berlin were “negotiable”﻿—something they never were before. Thus we acknowledged, in effect, the inadequacy of our position, and the world now expects us to adjust it as proof of our good faith. Our answer to Khrushchev’s ultimatum should have been that the status of West Berlin concerns only West Berliners and the occupying powers, and is therefore not a matter that we are prepared to discuss with the Soviet Union. That would have been the end of the Berlin “crisis.”

===Chinese===
当然，倘若共产党人能获得一份于己有利的正式协议，那就更好。但即便得不到，谈判本身就足以让他们收获一场胜利。举例来说，当苏联人挑衅我们在西柏林的权利时，我们单是坐到谈判桌前的举动，就把一场胜利拱手奉送。同意就那个题目谈判，就是同意我们在柏林的权利是「可以谈判的」——而从前它们从来不是。于是我们实际上承认了我方地位的不足，如今全世界都等着我们调整这种地位，以证明我们的诚意。对赫鲁晓夫的最后通牒，我们的答复本应当是：西柏林的地位只关涉西柏林居民和各占领国，因此不是我们准备同苏联讨论的事项。那样，柏林的「危机」早就终结了。

===Original===
The Berlin situation illustrates another reason why the West is at an inherent disadvantage in negotiating with the Communists. The central strategic fact of the Cold War, as it is presently fought, is that the Communists are on the offensive and we are on the defensive. The Soviet Union is always moving ahead, always trying to get something from the free world; the West endeavors, at best, to hold what it has. Therefore, the focal point of negotiations is invariably somewhere in the non-Communist world. Every conference between East and West deals with some territory or right belonging to the free world which the Communists covet. Conversely, since the free world does not seek the liberation of Communist territory, the possibility of Communist concessions never arises. Once the West did attempt to use the conference table for positive gain. At Geneva, in 1955, President Eisenhower told the Soviets he wanted to discuss the status of the satellite nations of Eastern Europe. He was promptly advised that the Soviet Union did not consider the matter a legitimate subject for negotiation, and that was that. Now since we are not permitted to talk about what *we* can get, the only interesting question at an East-West conference is what the Communists can get. Under such conditions, we can never win. At best we can hope for a stalemate that will place us exactly where we started.

===Chinese===
柏林的局势还说明了西方在同共产党人谈判时先天居于下风的另一条理由。就目前的打法而言，冷战的核心战略事实是：共产党人处于攻势，我们处于守势。苏联总是在向前推进，总是设法从自由世界那里攫取点什么；西方充其量是竭力守住自己已有的东西。因此，谈判的焦点照例落在非共产主义世界的某处。东西方之间的每一次会议，谈的都是共产党人觊觎的自由世界的某块领土或某项权利。反过来，由于自由世界并不谋求共产党领土的解放，共产党让步的可能从来不会出现。西方倒是有一回曾想借助谈判桌谋取实际的收益。1955 年在日内瓦，德怀特·艾森豪威尔总统告诉苏联人，他想讨论东欧卫星国的地位问题。对方旋即告知，苏联不认为这个问题是正当的谈判题目，事情就此作罢。既然不许我们谈*我们*能得到什么，东西方会议上唯一有意思的问题，便只剩共产党人能得到什么。在这种条件下，我们永远赢不了。充其量只能指望一个僵局，把我们原封不动地送回出发点。

===Original===
There is still another reason for questioning the value of negotiations. Assume that somehow we achieve an agreement we think advances our interests. Is there any reason for supposing the Communists will keep it one moment longer than suits their purpose? We, and they, are different in this respect. We keep our word. The long and perfidious Communist record of breaking agreements and treaties proves that the Soviet Union will not keep any agreement that is not to its advantage to keep. It follows that the only agreement worth making with the Soviets is one that will be self-enforceable﻿—which means one that is in the Kremlin’s interest to keep. But if that is the case, why bother to “negotiate” about it? If an action is in the interest of the Soviet Union, the Kremlin will go ahead and perform it without feeling any need to make it the subject of a formal treaty.

===Chinese===
对谈判的价值提出疑问，还有另一条理由。假定我们设法达成了一项我们认为有利于自身利益的协议。有什么理由假定，共产党人会把它信守得比合乎其目的的时限多出一分钟？在这一方面，我们和他们不同。我们言而有信。共产党人毁弃协定与条约的长久而背信弃义的记录证明：苏联绝不会信守任何守之无利、于它不利的协议。由此可以推知，同苏联人唯一值得缔结的协议，是可以自行强制执行的协议——也就是说，是守住它才符合克里姆林宫利益的协议。可如果情形本就如此，又何必费神去「谈判」？凡是符合苏联利益的事，克里姆林宫径直去办就是，绝不会觉得有必要把它立为正式条约的标的。

===Original===
The next time we are urged to rush to the conference table in order to “relax world tensions,” let our reaction be determined by this simple fact: the only “tensions” that exist between East and West have been created, and deliberately so, by the Communists. They can therefore be “relaxed” by the Kremlin’s unilateral act. The moment we decide to relax tensions by a “negotiated compromise” we have decided to yield something of value to the West.

===Chinese===
下一次再有人敦促我们赶赴谈判桌去「缓和国际紧张局势」时，让我们用这个简单的事实来定夺反应：东西方之间仅有的那些「紧张」，都是共产党人制造的，而且是蓄意制造的。因此，凭克里姆林宫的单方面行动，它们就可以被「缓和」。而一旦我们决定用「谈判达成的妥协」来缓和紧张，我们就已经决定让西方交出某种有价值的东西。

The "Exchange" Program / 「交流」计划

===Original===
In recent months, the so-called exchange program has become an increasingly prominent feature of American foreign policy. The program began modestly enough in 1955 at the Geneva Summit Meeting, when we agreed with the Soviets to promote “cultural exchanges” between the two countries. Since then we have exchanged everything from opera companies and basketball teams to trade exhibitions and heads of governments. We are told that these exchanges are our best hope of peace﻿—that if only the American and Russian peoples can learn to “understand” each other, they will be able to reconcile their differences.

===Chinese===
近几个月来，所谓的交流计划日益成为美国外交政策中显眼的一项内容。这个计划的开端相当低调：1955 年在日内瓦首脑会议上，我们同苏联人商定促进两国之间的「文化交流」。自那以后，从歌剧团、篮球队到贸易展览会、政府首脑，我们交换了各式各样的东西。人们告诉我们，这些交流是我们和平的最大指望——只要美国人民和俄国人民学会彼此「了解」，他们就能调和彼此的分歧。

===Original===
The claim that the conflict between the Soviets and ourselves stems from a “lack of understanding” is one of the great political fables of our time. *Whose* lack of understanding?

===Chinese===
说苏联人同我们的冲突源于「缺乏了解」，这是当今一大政治寓言。*究竟是谁*缺乏了解？

===Original===
Are the American people ill-informed as to the nature of Communism and of the Soviet state? True, some Americans fail to grasp how evil the Soviet system really is. But a performance by the Bolshoi Ballet, or a tour of the United States by Nikita Khrushchev, is certainly not calculated to correct *that* deficiency.

===Chinese===
美国人民对共产主义的性质和苏维埃国家的本质消息闭塞吗？诚然，有些美国人认识不到苏维埃制度究竟邪恶到何种地步。可是，莫斯科大剧院芭蕾舞团的一场演出，或者尼基塔·赫鲁晓夫在美国的巡游，断然治不了*那个*毛病。

===Original===
What of the Soviet leaders? Are *they* misled? All of the evidence is that the men in the Kremlin have a greater knowledge of America than many of our own leaders. They know about our political system, our industrial capacity, our way of life﻿—and would like to destroy it all.

===Chinese===
那么苏联的领导人呢？*他们*受了蒙蔽吗？一切证据都表明，克里姆林宫里那些人对美国的了解，胜过我们自己的许多领导人。他们熟知我们的政治制度、我们的工业能力、我们的生活方式——并且一心想把这一切统统毁掉。

===Original===
What about the Russian people? We are repeatedly told that the Russian man-on-the-street is woefully ignorant of the American way, and that our trade exhibition in Moscow, for example, contributed vastly to his knowledge and thus to his appreciation of America. Assume this is true. Is it relevant? As long as the Russian people do not control their government, it makes little difference whether they think well of us or ill. It is high time that our leaders stopped treating the Russian people and the Soviet government as one and the same thing. The Russian people, we may safely assume, are basically on our side (whether or not they have the opportunity to listen to American musicians); but their sympathy will not help us win the Cold War as long as all power is held firmly in the hands of the Communist ruling class.

===Chinese===
俄国人民又如何？人们一再对我们说，俄国的街头百姓对美国的生活方式可悲地无知，譬如我们在莫斯科举办的贸易展览会，就大大增进了他对美国的了解，从而增进了他对美国的好感。就算这是真的，它又有什么相干？只要俄国人民不能支配他们的政府，他们对我们的观感是好是坏，都无足轻重。我们的领导人早就不该再把俄国人民和苏维埃政府当作同一样东西来对待了。我们可以放心地假定，俄国人民基本上是站在我们一边的（无论他们有没有机会听美国音乐家的演奏）；但只要全部权力仍牢牢握在共产党统治阶级手中，他们的同情就帮不了我们赢得冷战。

===Original===
The exchange program, in Soviet eyes, is simply another operation in Communist political warfare. The people the Kremlin sends over here are, to a man, trained agents of Soviet policy. Some of them are spies, seeking information; all of them are trusted carriers of Communist propaganda. Their mission is not cultural, but political. Their aim is not to inform, but to mislead. Their assignment is not to convey a true image of the Soviet Union, but a false image. The Kremlin’s hope is that they will persuade the American people to forget the ugly aspects of Soviet life, and the danger that the Soviet system poses to American freedom.

===Chinese===
在苏联人眼里，交流计划不过是共产党政治战的又一次行动。克里姆林宫派到这边来的人，无一不是训练有素的苏联政策特工。他们中间有刺探情报的间谍；而所有的人都是共产党宣传的可托信使。他们的使命不是文化性的，而是政治性的。他们的目的不是传播实情，而是引人受骗。他们的任务不是传达苏联的真实形象，而是虚假的形象。克里姆林宫的指望是：这些人能劝使美国人民忘掉苏联生活的丑恶方面，忘掉苏维埃制度对美国自由构成的威胁。

===Original===
It is a mistake to measure the success of this Communist operation by the extent to which it converts Americans to Communism. By that test, of course, the operation is almost a complete failure. But the Kremlin’s aim is not to make Americans *approve* of Communism, much as they would like that; it is to make us *tolerant* of Communism. The Kremlin knows that our willingness to make sacrifices to halt Communist expansion varies in direct ratio as we are *hostile* to Communism. They know that if Americans regard the Soviet Union as a dangerous, implacable enemy, Communism will not be able to conquer the world. The Communists’ purpose, then, is to show that Khrushchev does not have horns﻿—that he is fundamentally a nice fellow; that the Soviet people are﻿—“ordinary people” just like ourselves; that Communism is just another political system.

===Chinese===
用这场共产党行动使多少美国人皈依共产主义来衡量它的成败，是一个错误。按这个标准衡量，这项行动当然近乎彻底失败。但克里姆林宫的目标并不是叫美国人*赞成*共产主义——尽管他们求之不得；它是要使我们*容忍*共产主义。克里姆林宫知道：我们为遏止共产主义扩张而甘愿牺牲的程度，同我们对共产主义的*敌视*程度成正比地消长。他们知道，如果美国人把苏联看作一个危险的、毫不妥协的敌人，共产主义就不可能征服世界。于是，共产党人的用意就是要表明：赫鲁晓夫头上并没有长角——他根本上是个好伙计；苏联人民同我们一样，是「寻常百姓」；共产主义不过是又一种政治制度罢了。

===Original===
It would not have made sense, midway in the Second World War, to promote a Nazi-American exchange program or to invite Hitler to make a state visit to the United States. Unless we cherish victory less today than we did then, we will be equally reluctant to treat Communist agents as friends and welcome guests. The exchange program is a Communist confidence game. Let us not be taken in by it. Let us remember that American confidence in the Soviet government is the very last thing we want.

===Chinese===
在第二次世界大战正酣之际，去推行一项纳粹—美国交流计划，或者邀请希特勒对美国进行国事访问，是讲不通的。除非我们今天对胜利的珍视不如当年，否则我们将同样不愿把共产党代理人当作朋友和贵宾来款待。交流计划是一场共产党的骗局。我们不要上这个当。让我们记住：美国人对苏联政府的信任，恰恰是我们最不该有的东西。

===Original===
Many people contend that a “normalization” of Soviet-American relations, as envisaged by the exchange program, is only a logical extension of granting diplomatic recognition to Communist governments. I agree. Accordingly, I think it would be wise for the United States to reexamine the question of its diplomatic relations with Communist regimes. We often hear that recognition permits us to gather information in Communist countries. I am unaware, however, of any advantage that our diplomatic mission in Moscow confers along these lines that does not doubly accrue to the Soviet Union from its diplomatic spy corps in Washington and other American cities. Espionage possibilities aside, I am quite certain that our entire approach to the Cold War would change for the better the moment we announced that the United States does not regard Mr. Khrushchev’s murderous claque as the legitimate rulers of the Russian people or of any other people. Not only would withdrawal of recognition stiffen the American people’s attitude toward Communism; it would also give heart to the enslaved peoples and help them to overthrow their captors. Our present policy of not recognizing Red China is eminently right, and the reasons behind that policy apply equally to the Soviet Union and its European satellites. If our objective is to win the Cold War, we will start now by denying our moral support to the very regimes we mean to defeat.

===Chinese===
许多人主张，交流计划所设想的美苏关系「正常化」，不过是给予共产党政权外交承认这一做法在逻辑上的延伸。我同意。既然如此，我认为美国重新审视它同共产党政权的外交关系问题，才是明智之举。我们时常听到有人说，承认使我们得以在共产党国家收集情报。然而我不知道，我们在莫斯科的外交使团在这方面带来的任何一点好处，有哪一点不会加倍地归到苏联头上——凭它派驻华盛顿及美国其他城市的外交间谍班子。撇开间谍活动的可能不谈，我相当确信：一旦我们宣布美利坚合众国不承认赫鲁晓夫先生那伙凶残的捧场者是俄国人民或任何别国人民的合法统治者，我们处理冷战的整个路数就会立刻好转。撤销承认不仅会使美国人民对共产主义的态度更趋强硬；它还会给被奴役的人民以勇气，帮助他们推翻囚禁他们的人。我们不承认红色中国的现行政策是完全正确的，这项政策背后的理由同样适用于苏联及其欧洲卫星国。如果我们的目标是赢得冷战，我们现在就着手，拒绝把我们道义上的支持给予那些我们决意击败的政权。

Disarmament / 裁军

===Original===
For many years, our policy-makers have paid lip-service to the idea of disarmament. This seems to be one of the ways, in modern diplomacy, of proving your virtue. Recently, however﻿—under strong Communist propaganda pressure﻿—we have acted as though we mean this talk to be taken seriously. I cite our government’s momentous decision to suspend nuclear tests.

===Chinese===
多年以来，我们的政策制定者一直把裁军挂在嘴上。这似乎是现代外交中证明自己有德行的方式之一。然而近来——在共产党宣传的强大压力之下——我们的行事仿佛真要人把这种话当真。我举我国政府暂停核试验这一重大决定为例。

===Original===
Students of history have always recognized that armament races are a symptom of international friction﻿—not a cause of it. Peace has never been achieved, and it will not in our time, by rival nations suddenly deciding to turn their swords into plowshares. No nation in its right mind will give up the means of defending itself without first making sure that hostile powers are no longer in a position to threaten it.

===Chinese===
治史的学者一向明白，军备竞赛是国际摩擦的症候，而不是它的原因。和平从来不曾靠敌对国家忽然决定化剑为犁而达成，在我们这个时代也不会。任何神志清醒的国家，都不会在尚未确知敌对强国已无力威胁自己之前，就放弃自卫的手段。

===Original===
The Communist leaders are, of course, in their right minds. They would not dream of adopting a policy that would leave them, on balance, relatively weaker than before they adopted such a policy. They might preach general disarmament for propaganda purposes. They also might seriously promote mutual disarmament in certain weapons in the knowledge that their superior strength in other weapons would leave them, on balance, decisively stronger than the West. Thus, in the light of the West’s weakness in conventional weapons, it might make sense for the Communists to seek disarmament in the nuclear field; if all nuclear weapons suddenly ceased to exist, much of the world would immediately be laid open to conquest by the masses of Russian and Chinese manpower.

===Chinese===
共产党领导人当然是神志清醒的。他们做梦也不会去采纳一项使其综合地位在总体上反而弱于奉行之前的政策。为了宣传，他们也许会鼓吹普遍裁军。他们也可能会认真地推动在某些武器上实行相互裁军，因为他们心中有数：他们在另一些武器上的优势，将使他们总体上比西方强大得多。这样，鉴于西方在常规武器上的弱势，共产党人谋求核领域的裁军或许是说得通的；倘若所有核武器顷刻之间不复存在，世界上的大片地方将立时门户洞开，听凭俄国和中国的庞大人力前去征服。

===Original===
American leaders have not shown a comparable solicitude for our security needs. After the Second World War, the United States had a conventional military establishment rivaling the Soviet Union’s, and an absolute monopoly in nuclear power. The former weapon we hastily and irresponsibly dismantled. The latter we failed to exploit politically, and then we proceeded to fritter away our lead by belated entry into the hydrogen bomb and guided missile fields. The result is that we are outclassed in the conventional means for waging land warfare; regarding nuclear weapons, we are approaching the point, if it has not already been reached, where Communist power is equal to our own.

===Chinese===
美国领导人对于我们安全的需要，却没有表现出同样一份关切。第二次世界大战之后，美国拥有堪与苏联匹敌的常规军事建制，并在核力量上握有绝对的垄断。前者，我们仓促而不负责任地把它拆散了。后者，我们未能在政治上加以运用，随后又因迟迟才进入氢弹与导弹领域，把我们的领先地位一点点挥霍掉。结果，在陆战的常规手段上我们已被比了下去；至于核武器，我们正趋近——纵然尚未抵达——共产党力量与我们旗鼓相当的那一点。

===Original===
To the impending physical parity in nuclear weapons must be added a psychological factor assiduously cultivated by Communist propaganda. The horrors of all-out warfare are said to be so great that no nation would consider resorting to nuclear weapons unless under direct attack by those same weapons. Now the moment our leaders really accept this, strategic nuclear weapons will be neutralized and Communist armies will be able to launch limited wars without fear of retaliation by our Strategic Air Command. I fear they are coming to accept it, and thus that a military and psychological situation is fast developing in which aggressive Communist forces will be free to maneuver under the umbrella of nuclear terror.

===Chinese===
在核武器行将形成的实力均势之外，还须加上共产党宣传苦心培植的一个心理因素。据说，全面战争的恐怖如此巨大，以致除非首先遭到这些武器本身的直接攻击，任何国家都不会考虑动用核武器。我们的领导人一旦真正接受这一点，战略核武器就等于被解除效用，共产党军队将可以发动有限战争而无须惧怕我国战略空军司令部的报复。我担心他们正在开始接受这一点；果真如此，一种军事与心理态势正在迅速形成：好战的共产党军队将可以在核恐怖的保护伞下自由行动。

===Original===
It is in this context that we must view the Communist propaganda drive for a permanent ban on the testing of nuclear weapons, and the inclination of our own leaders to go along with the proposal. There are two preliminary reasons why such proposals ought to be firmly rejected. First, there is no reliable means of preventing the Communists from secretly breaking such an agreement. Our most recent tests demonstrated that underground atomic explosions can be set off without detection. Secondly, we cannot hope to maintain even an effective *strategic* deterrent unless we keep our present nuclear arsenal up to date; this requires testing. But the main point I want to make is that tests are needed to develop *tactical* nuclear weapons for possible use in limited wars. Our military experts have long recognized that for limited warfare purposes we must have a weapons superiority to offset the Communists’ manpower superiority. This means we must develop and perfect a variety of small, clean nuclear weapons; and this in turn means: testing. The development of such a weapons system is the only way in which America will be able to fight itself out of the dilemma﻿—one horn of which is superior Communist manpower, the other, the impending neutralization of strategic nuclear weapons.

===Chinese===
我们必须在这样的背景之下来看待共产党人为永久禁止核试验而掀起的宣传攻势，以及我国领导人附和这项提议的意向。这类提议应当坚决拒绝，先说两条初步的理由。第一，没有什么可靠的手段能防止共产党人暗中破坏这样一份协议。我们最近的试验证明，地下的原子爆炸可以在不被探测的情况下引爆。第二，除非使我们现有的核武库不断更新，我们连一个有效的*战略*威慑也休想维持；这就需要试验。但我想说的要点是：为了研制可能用于有限战争的*战术*核武器，试验是必需的。我们的军事专家早已认识到：为了有限战争的目的，我们必须拥有一种武器上的优势，以抵消共产党的人力优势。这意味着我们必须研制并完善多种小型「干净」核武器；而这又意味着：试验。发展这样一套武器系统，是使美国得以从困境中打出一条活路的唯一办法——这困境的一角是共产党的人力优势，另一角是战略核武器行将失效。

===Original===
Our government was originally pushed into suspending tests by Communist-induced hysteria on the subject of radioactive fallout. However one may rate that danger, it simply has no bearing on the problem at hand. The facts are that there is practically no fallout from tests conducted above the earth’s atmosphere, and none at all from underground tests. Therefore, the only excuse for suspending tests is that our forbearance somehow contributes to peace. And my answer is that I am unable to see how peace is brought any nearer by a policy that may reduce our relative military strength. Such a policy makes sense only under the assumption that Communist leaders have given up their plan for world revolution and will settle for peaceful coexistence﻿—an assumption we make at the risk of losing our national life.

===Chinese===
我国政府当初被推入暂停试验，是共产党人在放射性沉降物问题上煽起的歇斯底里所致。无论人们怎样评估那种危险，它同眼前的问题毫不相干。事实是：在地球大气层之上进行的试验几乎不产生沉降物，地下试验则完全没有。因此，暂停试验的唯一借口，是说我们的克制多少有助于和平。而我的回答是：我看不出，一项可能减损我们相对军事力量的政策，怎么会把和平拉近一步。这样的政策，只有在一种假设之下才说得通：共产党领导人已放弃其世界革命的图谋，甘愿满足于和平共处——我们作出这个假设，就是拿国家的生命去冒险。

===Original===
If our objective is victory over Communism, we must achieve superiority in all of the weapons﻿—military, as well as political and economic﻿—that may be useful in reaching that goal. Such a program costs money, but so long as the money is spent wisely and efficiently, I would spend it. I am not in favor of “economizing” on the nation’s safety. As a Conservative, I deplore the huge tax levy that is needed to finance the world’s number-one military establishment. But even more do I deplore the prospect of a foreign conquest, which the absence of that establishment would quickly accomplish.

===Chinese===
如果我们的目标是战胜共产主义，我们就必须在一切可能有助于达成这一目标的武器——军事的，以及政治的和经济的——上都取得优势。这样一项计划要花钱；但只要钱花得明智而有效率，我就愿意花。我不赞成为国家的安全「节省」。作为一个保守主义者（Conservative），我痛惜供养世界上头号军事建制所需要的那笔巨额税款。但我更加痛惜外国征服得逞的前景——而一旦失去那个军事建制，这种征服顷刻便能实现。

United Nations / 联合国

===Original===
Support of the United Nations, our leaders earnestly proclaim, is one of the cornerstones of American foreign policy. I confess to being more interested in whether American foreign policy has the support of the United Nations.

===Chinese===
我国领导人郑重宣告：支持联合国是美国外交政策的基石之一。我承认，我更关心的是美国外交政策是否得到联合国的支持。

===Original===
Here, again, it seems to me that our approach to foreign affairs suffers from a confusion in objectives. Is the perpetuation of an international debating forum, for its own sake, the primary objective of American policy? If so, there is much to be said for our past record of subordinating our national interest to that of the United Nations. If, on the other hand, our primary objective is victory over Communism, we will, as a matter of course, view such organizations as the U.N. as a possible *means* to that end. Once the question is asked﻿—Does America’s participation in the United Nations help or hinder her struggle against world Communism?﻿—it becomes clear that our present commitment to the U.N. deserves reexamination.

===Chinese===
在这里，我们的外交之道在我看来又犯了目标混淆的毛病。让一个国际辩论讲坛长久存在，并以其自身为目的，是美国政策的首要目标吗？如果是，那么我们过去使国家利益从属于联合国利益的记录，就大有可称道之处。反过来，如果我们首要的目标是战胜共产主义，那么我们理所当然要把联合国这样的组织看作达到那个目标的一种可能的*手段*。一旦有人发问——美国参加联合国，究竟有助于还是妨碍她同世界共产主义的斗争？——那么显而易见，我们目前对联合国的承诺值得重新检讨。

===Original===
The United Nations, we must remember, is in part a Communist organization. The Communists always have at least one seat in its major policy-making body, the Security Council; and the Soviet Union’s permanent veto power in that body allows the Kremlin to block any action, on a substantial issue, that is contrary to its interests. The Communists also have a sizeable membership in the U.N.’s other policy-making body, the General Assembly. Moreover, the U.N.’s working staff, the Secretariat, is manned by hundreds of Communist agents who are frequently in a position to sabotage those few U.N. policies that *are* contrary to Communist interests. Finally, a great number of non-Communist United Nations are sympathetic to Soviet aims﻿—or, at best, are unsympathetic to ours.

===Chinese===
我们必须记住，联合国的组成在相当程度上是一个共产党组织。在其主要的决策机构安全理事会里，共产党人至少握有一个席位；而苏联在这个机构里的常任否决权，使克里姆林宫得以在一切实质问题上封阻任何违背其利益的行动。在联合国的另一个决策机构联合国大会里，共产党人也占有可观的席位。此外，联合国的工作班子秘书处，被数百名共产党代理人盘踞，他们时常有能力暗中破坏联合国那少数*确实*与共产党利益相抵触的政策。最后，为数众多的非共产党会员国同情苏联的目标——或者充其量，对我们的目标没有同情。

===Original===
We therefore should not be surprised that many of the policies that emerge from the deliberations of the United Nations are not policies that are in the best interest of the United States. United Nations policy is, necessarily, the product of many different views﻿—some of them friendly, some of them indifferent to our interests, some of them mortally hostile. And the result is that our national interests usually suffer when we subordinate our own policy to the U.N.’s. In nearly every case in which we have called upon the United Nations to do our thinking for us, and to make our policy for us﻿—whether during the Korean War, or in the Suez crisis, or following the revolution in Iraq﻿—we have been a less effective foe of Communism than we otherwise might have been.

===Chinese===
因此，联合国审议产生的许多政策，并非最符合美国利益的政策，对此我们不必惊讶。联合国的政策必然是众多不同观点的产物——其中一些对我们友好，一些对我们的利益漠然置之，一些则抱着致命的敌意。其结果便是：当我们使自己的政策从属于联合国的政策时，我们的国家利益通常要蒙受损失。在我们请联合国代我们思考、代我们制定政策的几乎每一桩事例中——无论在朝鲜战争期间、在苏伊士危机中，还是在伊拉克革命之后——比起我们自己拿定主意的情形，我们作为共产主义的对手都要逊色几分。

===Original===
Unlike America, the Communists do not respect the U.N. and do not permit their policies to be affected by it. If the “opinion of mankind,” as reflected by a U.N. resolution, goes against them, they﻿—in effect﻿—tell mankind to go fly a kite. Not so with us; we would rather be approved than succeed, and so are likely to adjust our own views to conform with a United Nations majority. This is not the way to win the Cold War. I repeat: Communism will not be beaten by a policy that is the common denominator of the foreign policies of 80-odd nations, some of which are our enemies, nearly all of which are less determined than we to save the world from Communist domination. Let us, then, have done with submitting major policy decisions to a forum where the opinions of the Sultan of Yeman count equally with ours; where the vote of the United States can be cancelled out by the likes of “Byelorussia.”

===Chinese===
共产党人不像美国，他们并不尊重联合国，也不容许自己的政策受它的左右。如果联合国一项决议所反映的「人类舆论」于他们不利，他们实际上就是对人类说：你一边玩去。我们却不然；我们宁要称许，不要成功，于是往往把自己的见解调整得去顺应联合国多数。这不是赢得冷战的办法。我再说一遍：一项由 80 多个国家的外交政策折中调和而成的政策，是打不垮共产主义的——这些国家有的是我们的敌人，而且几乎全部都不如我们这样有决心把世界从共产主义统治下解救出来。那么，就让我们同这种做法一刀两断吧：不再把重大的政策决定提交给这样一个论坛——在那里，也门苏丹的意见同我们的意见平起平坐；在那里，美国的表决可以被「白俄罗斯」之流的选票一笔抵销。

===Original===
I am troubled by several other aspects of our U.N. commitment. First﻿—and here again our Cold War interests are damaged﻿—the United Nations provides a unique forum for Communist propaganda. We too, of course, can voice our views at the U.N.; but the Communists’ special advantage is that their lies and misrepresentations are elevated to the level of serious international debate. By recognizing the right of Communist regimes to participate in the U.N. as equals, and by officially acknowledging them as “peace-loving,” we grant Communist propaganda a presumption of reasonableness and plausibility it otherwise would not have.

===Chinese===
我们对联合国的承诺，还有其他几个方面令我忧虑。第一——在这里我们的冷战利益再度受损——联合国为共产党宣传提供了一个独一无二的讲坛。诚然，我们同样可以在联合国陈述我们的见解；但共产党人的特殊便宜在于：他们的谎言与歪曲被抬升到严肃国际辩论的层面。由于承认共产党政权有权以平等身份参加联合国，并由于以官方身份确认它们「爱好和平」，我们给了共产党宣传一种它本来不会享有的合情合理、可信可从的推定。

===Original===
Second, the U.N. places an unwarranted financial burden on the American taxpayer. The Marxist formula, “from each according to his ability﻿ ﻿…”﻿—under which contributions to the U.N. and its specialized agencies are determined﻿—does not tally with the American concept of justice. The United States is currently defraying roughly a third of all United Nations expenses. That assessment should be drastically reduced. The U.N. should not operate as a charity. Assessments should take into account the benefits received by the contributor-nation.

===Chinese===
第二，联合国把一笔不正当的财政负担压在美国纳税人身上。「各尽所能……」这一马克思主义公式——联合国及其专门机构的会费就是依它分摊的——同美国人的公义观念全不相合。美国目前承担着联合国全部开支的大约三分之一。这笔摊款应当大幅削减。联合国不应办成一家慈善机构。会费的分摊应当计入缴费国所获得的益处。

===Original===
Finally, I fear that our involvement in the United Nations may be leading to an unconstitutional surrender of American sovereignty. Many U.N. activities have already made strong inroads against the sovereign powers of Member Nations. This is neither the time nor place to discuss the merits of yielding sovereign American rights﻿—other than to record my unequivocal opposition to the idea. It is both the time and place, however, to insist that any such discussion take place within the framework of a proposed constitutional amendment﻿—and not, clandestinely, in the headquarters of some U.N. agency.

===Chinese===
最后，我担心我们卷入联合国，可能导致对美国主权的违宪让渡。联合国的许多活动，早已对各会员国的主权权力造成严重侵蚀。让渡美国主权权利究竟利弊如何，此时此地不是讨论的地方——我只在此记下我对这一观念斩钉截铁的反对。但此时此地正该坚持的是：任何这类讨论都必须在拟议中的宪法修正案框架之内进行——而不是在某个联合国机构的总部里偷偷摸摸地进行。

===Original===
Withdrawal from the United Nations is probably not the answer to these problems. For a number of reasons that course is unfeasible. We should make sure, however, that the nature of our commitment is such as to advance American interests; and that will involve changes in some of our present attitudes and policies toward the U.N. Let the U.N. firsters﻿—of whom there are many in this country﻿—put their enthusiasm for “international cooperation” in proper perspective. Let them understand that victory over Communism must come *before* the achievement of lasting peace. Let them, in a word, keep their eyes on the target.

===Chinese===
退出联合国，恐怕不是解决这些问题的办法。出于若干理由，那条路并不可行。但我们应该确保：我们所承担义务的性质，必须是足以增进美国利益的；而这将要求改变我们目前对联合国的某些态度和政策。让那些「联合国至上」派——此辈在我国为数不少——把他们对「国际合作」的热情放到恰当的位置上。让他们明白：战胜共产主义必须*先于*持久和平的实现。一句话，让他们盯住目标不放。

Aid to Communist Governments / 对共产党政府的援助

===Original===
There is one aspect of our policy that *is* offensive-minded﻿—in the minds of its authors, anyway. Its effect, unfortunately, is exactly opposite to the one intended.

===Chinese===
我们的政策确有一个侧面*是*以进攻为念的——至少在其设计者的心目中如此。可惜它的效果同设计者的用意恰恰相反。

===Original===
Some time ago our leaders advanced the theory that Communist satellite regimes would, with our help, gradually break their ties with the Soviet Union and “evolve” political systems more in keeping with our notions of freedom and justice. Accordingly, America adopted the policy of giving aid to Communist governments whose relations with Moscow seemed to be strained. And that policy gave birth to a slogan: “America seeks the liberation of enslaved peoples﻿—not by revolution﻿—but through evolution.” Under the aegis of this slogan, we are sending hundreds of millions of dollars to the Communist government of Poland, having already given more than a billion dollars to the Communist government of Yugoslavia.

===Chinese===
前些时候，我们的领导人提出一种理论：共产党的卫星政权在我们的帮助之下，将逐渐斩断同苏联的纽带，「演变」出比较合乎我们自由与公义观念的政治制度。于是美国采取了一项政策：向那些同莫斯科的关系似乎失和的共产党政府提供援助。这项政策还催生了一句口号：「美国谋求被奴役人民的解放——不靠革命——而靠演进。」在这句口号的庇护之下，我们正向波兰的共产党政府送去数以亿计的美元，而对南斯拉夫的共产党政府，我们已经送出了超过十亿美元。

===Original===
In my view, this money has not only been wasted; it has positively promoted the Communist cause. It has *not* made Communist governments less Communist. It has *not* caused Communist governments to change sides in the Cold War. It *has* made it easier for Communist governments to keep their subjects enslaved. And none of these results should have come as a surprise.

===Chinese===
在我看来，这笔钱不仅被糟蹋了，还实实在在助长了共产主义的事业。它*没有*使共产党政府减弱一丝共产主义的成色。它*没有*促使共产党政府在冷战中改换阵营。它*倒是*使共产党政府更容易把治下的臣民维持在奴役之中。这些结果，没有一样本应使人意外。

===Original===
One does not have to take the view that a Communist regime will never “evolve” into a non-Communist one (though I tend to it) in order to see that this is practically impossible as long as the Soviet Union possesses the military and political power to prevent it. The Kremlin may, for its own purposes, permit certain “liberalization” tendencies in satellite countries; it may even permit small deviations from the approved Soviet foreign policy line. It will do so sometimes to confuse the West, sometimes as a prudent means of relieving internal pressures. But it will never let things go too far. Hungary proved that. The moment a Communist government threatens to become a non-Communist one, or threatens to align itself with the West against the Soviet Union, the Kremlin will take steps to bring the defecting government into line.

===Chinese===
人们即使不必持「共产党政权永远不会『演变』成非共产党政权」的看法（我本人倒倾向于这种看法），也能看出：只要苏联握有足以阻止此事的军事与政治力量，这种演变实际上就是不可能的。出于自身的盘算，克里姆林宫也许会容许卫星国出现某些「自由化」的倾向；甚至会容许对既定的苏联外交路线作些许偏离。它有时以此迷惑西方，有时以此作为缓解内部压力的稳妥手段。但它绝不会听任事情走得太远。匈牙利证明了这一点。一旦某个共产党政府露出要变成非共产党政府的苗头，或者露出要同西方联合起来反对苏联的苗头，克里姆林宫就会采取措施，把这个背离的政府重新拉回队列。

===Original===
Hungary proved this truth, and Poland has proved that dissident Communists learned it. Western leaders, unfortunately, were much less perceptive. In the Fall of 1956, there appeared to be a breach between Gomulka’s government and the Kremlin. Many Westerners joyfully proclaimed that Poland was pulling away from Communism, and hoping to hasten this movement, our government began to send the Gomulka regime American aid. The succeeding years witnessed two facts: 1. Our money made it easier for Gomulka’s regime to deal with its economic problems; 2. Gomulka moved into an even closer relationship with the Soviet government. Gomulka knew, as American policy-makers ought to have known, that the price of abandoning Communism is a Budapest-type blood bath. This, of course, need not be the case were America prepared to come to the aid of people who want to strike out for freedom. But as long as we give Soviet military forces a free hand in Eastern Europe, it is the height of folly to try to bribe Communist governments into becoming our friends.

===Chinese===
匈牙利证明了这个真理，而波兰则证明持异见的共产党人领受了它。可惜，西方领导人的悟性差得多。1956 年秋天，哥穆尔卡（Gomulka）政府同克里姆林宫之间似乎出现了一道裂痕。许多西方人欣喜地宣告：波兰正在挣脱共产主义。我们的政府指望加速这一进程，开始向哥穆尔卡政权提供美国援助。其后数年见证了两个事实：一、我们的钱使哥穆尔卡政权更容易应付它的经济难题；二、哥穆尔卡同苏联政府的关系反而走得更近。哥穆尔卡心里明白——美国的政策制定者本也应当时刻明白——背弃共产主义的代价，就是一场布达佩斯式的血洗。当然，倘若美国准备驰援那些决意为自由一搏的人民，情形本可以不是这样。但只要我们还让苏联军事力量在东欧放手行事，企图收买共产党政府使之变成我们的朋友，就是愚蠢透顶。

===Original===
We must realize that the captive *peoples* are our friends and potential allies﻿—not their rulers. A truly offensive-minded strategy would recognize that the captive peoples are our strongest weapon in the war against Communism, and would encourage them to overthrow their captors. A policy of strengthening their captors can only postpone that upheaval within the Communist Empire that is our best hope of defeating Communism without resorting to nuclear war.

===Chinese===
我们必须认识到：被奴役的*人民*是我们的朋友和潜在的同盟者——而不是他们的统治者。一个真正以进攻为念的战略，会承认被奴役的人民是我们在反共战争中最强大的武器，并且会鼓励他们推翻囚禁他们的人。强化那些囚禁者的政策，只会把共产主义帝国内部的那场大震荡往后拖延——而那场震荡，正是我们不诉诸核战争而击败共产主义的最大指望。

Toward Victory / 迈向胜利

===Original===
By measuring each aspect of our foreign policy against the standard﻿—Is it helpful in defeating the enemy?﻿—we can understand why the past fourteen years have been marked by frustration and failure. We have not gotten ahead because we have been travelling the wrong road.

===Chinese===
把我们外交政策的每一个侧面放到这把尺子之下——它是否有助于击败敌人？——我们就能懂得，为什么过去的十四年满是挫折与失败。我们之所以寸步未进，是因为走的是一条错路。

===Original===
It is less easy to stake out the right road. For in terms of our own experience it is a new road we seek, and one therefore that will hold challenges and perils that are different (though hardly graver) from those with which we are now familiar. Actually, the “new” road is as old as human history: it is the one that successful political and military leaders, having arrived at a dispassionate “estimate of the situation,” always follow when they are in a war they mean to win. From our own estimate of the situation, we know the *direction* we must take; and our standard﻿—Is it helpful in defeating Communism?﻿—will provide guideposts all along the way. There are some that can be observed even now:

===Chinese===
要划出那条正确的路，就没那么容易了。因为就我们自身的经验而论，我们寻求的是一条新路，因而它将承载的挑战与凶险，同我们如今熟知的那些不同——尽管未必更为凶险。其实，这条「新」路同人类历史一样古老：凡属成功的政治领袖与军事统帅，在冷静作出「态势估计」之后，只要身处一场他们决意打赢的战争，走的一向就是这条路。从我们自己对态势的估计出发，我们知道必须采取的*方向*；而我们的尺子——它是否有助于战胜共产主义？——将一路为我们立下指路的标桩。有几根标桩，现在就可以立起：

===Original===
1. The key guidepost is the Objective, and we must never lose sight of it. It is not to wage a struggle against Communism, but to win it.

===Chinese===
1. 关键的标桩是目标，我们绝不可让它离开视线。目标不是同共产主义进行一场斗争，而是打赢这场斗争。

Our Goal Must Be Victory / 我们的目标必须是胜利

===Original===
2. Our strategy must be primarily offensive in nature. Given the dynamic, revolutionary character of the enemy’s challenge, we cannot win merely by trying to hold our own. In addition to parrying his blows, we must strike our own. In addition to guarding our frontiers, we must try to puncture his. In addition to keeping the free world free, we must try to make the Communist world free. To these ends, we must always try to engage the enemy at times and places, and with weapons, of our own choosing.

===Chinese===
2. 我们的战略必须以进攻为根本。鉴于敌人挑战的进取性与革命性，我们不可能单靠力保自存而取胜。除了挡开对方的打击，我们必须打出自己的打击。除了守卫我们的边界，我们必须设法洞穿对方的边界。除了保住自由世界的自由，我们必须设法使共产主义世界获得自由。为了这些目的，我们必须始终设法在*我们*选定的时间和地点、用我们选定的武器同敌人交手。

===Original===
3. We must strive to achieve and maintain military superiority. Mere parity will not do. Since we can never match the Communists in manpower, our equipment and weapons must more than offset his advantage in numbers. We must also develop a limited war capacity. For this latter purpose, we should make every effort to achieve decisive superiority in small, clean nuclear weapons.

===Chinese===
3. 我们必须力争取得并保持军事优势。仅仅打平是不够的。既然我们在人力上永远赶不上共产党人，我们的装备与武器就必须绰绰有余地抵消其数量上的优势。我们还必须发展有限战争的能力。为后一目的，我们应当竭尽全力，在小型「干净」核武器上取得决定性的优势。

===Original===
4. We must make America economically strong. We have already seen why economic energy must be released from government strangulation if individual freedom is to survive. Economic emancipation is equally imperative if the nation is to survive. America’s maximum economic power will be forged, not under bureaucratic direction, but in freedom.

===Chinese===
4. 我们必须使美国在经济上强盛。我们已经看到：倘若个人自由要存续，经济的活力为什么必须从政府的扼制中释放出来。同样，倘若国家要存续，经济的解放也刻不容缓。美国最大的经济力量，不是在官僚的指挥之下，而是在自由之中锻造出来的。

===Original===
5. In all of our dealings with foreign nations, we must behave like a great power. Our national posture must reflect strength and confidence and purpose, as well as good will. We need not be bellicose, but neither should we encourage others to believe that American rights can be violated with impunity. We must protect American nationals and American property and American honor﻿—everywhere. We may not make foreign peoples love us﻿—no nation has ever succeeded in that﻿—but we can make *them respect us*. And *respect* is the stuff of which enduring friendships and firm alliances are made.

===Chinese===
5. 在同外国的一切交往中，我们必须表现得像个大国。我们的国家姿态必须显示出力量、信心与宗旨，也要显示出善意。我们不必好斗，但也不该让旁人相信，侵犯美国的权利可以逍遥无事。我们必须保护美国的侨民、美国的财产和美国的荣誉——在一切地方。我们未必能使外国人民爱我们——从来没有一个国家在这上面成功过——但我们能使他们*敬重我们*。而*敬重*，正是持久的友谊与牢固的同盟赖以构成的那种材料。

===Original===
6. We should adopt a discriminating foreign aid policy. American aid should be furnished only to friendly, anti-Communist nations that are willing to join with us in the struggle for freedom. Moreover, our aid should take the form of loans or technical assistance, not gifts. And we should insist, moreover, that such nations contribute their fair share to the common cause.

===Chinese===
6. 我们应当采取有甄别的对外援助政策。美国的援助，只应提供给友好的、反共的、愿意同我们并肩投入自由之战的国家。而且，我们的援助应当采取贷款或技术援助的形式，而不是馈赠。此外，我们还应坚持：这些国家必须为共同的事业贡献它们应尽的一份。

===Original===
7. We should declare the world Communist movement an outlaw in the community of civilized nations. Accordingly, we should withdraw diplomatic recognition from all Communist governments including that of the Soviet Union, thereby serving notice on the world that we regard such governments as neither legitimate nor permanent.

===Chinese===
7. 我们应当宣布世界共产主义运动为文明国家共同体中的法外之徒。为此，我们应当撤销对一切共产党政府的外交承认，包括苏联政府在内，从而昭告世界：我们认为这样的政府既不合法，也难长久。

===Original===
8. We should encourage the captive peoples to revolt against their Communist rulers. This policy must be pursued with caution and prudence, as well as courage. For while our enslaved friends must be told we are anxious to help them, we should discourage premature uprisings that have no chance of success. The freedom fighters must understand that the time and place and method of such uprisings will be dictated by the needs of an overall world strategy. To this end we should establish close liaison with underground leaders behind the Iron Curtain, furnishing them with printing presses, radios, weapons, instructors: the paraphernalia of a full-fledged Resistance.

===Chinese===
8. 我们应当鼓励被奴役的人民起来反抗他们的共产党统治者。推行这项政策，既要有勇气，也要审慎周密。一方面必须让我们身受奴役的朋友知道，我们切望帮助他们；另一方面，对于那些毫无成功希望的仓促起义，我们应当劝阻。自由的战士必须明白：这类起义的时间、地点与方式，将由一项总体世界战略的需要来决定。为此，我们应当同铁幕后面的地下领袖建立密切的联络，向他们提供印刷机、无线电、武器、教官：一场全面抵抗运动的全套行装。

===Original===
9. We should encourage friendly peoples that have the means and desire to do so to undertake offensive operations for the recovery of their homelands. For example, should a revolt occur inside Red China, we should encourage and support guerrilla operations on the mainland by the Free Chinese. Should the situation develop favorably, we should encourage the South Koreans and the South Vietnamese to join Free Chinese forces in a combined effort to liberate the enslaved peoples of Asia.

===Chinese===
9. 对于那些既有手段又有意愿的友好民族，我们应当鼓励他们发起收复故土的攻势行动。例如，倘若红色中国境内发生暴动，我们应当鼓励并支持自由中国人在大陆上展开游击作战。倘若形势发展有利，我们应当鼓励南韩人和南越人同自由中国的军队联合作战，合力解放亚洲被奴役的人民。

===Original===
10. We must﻿—ourselves﻿—be prepared to undertake military operations against vulnerable Communist regimes. Assume we have developed nuclear weapons that can be used in land warfare, and that we have equipped our European divisions accordingly. Assume also a major uprising in Eastern Europe, such as occurred in Budapest in 1956. In such a situation, we ought to present the Kremlin with an ultimatum forbidding Soviet intervention, and be prepared, if the ultimatum is rejected, to move a highly mobile task force equipped with appropriate nuclear weapons to the scene of the revolt. Our objective would be to confront the Soviet Union with superior force in the immediate vicinity of the uprising and to compel a Soviet withdrawal. An actual clash between American and Soviet armies would be unlikely; the mere threat of American action, coupled with the Kremlin’s knowledge that the fighting would occur amid a hostile population and could easily spread to other areas, would probably result in Soviet acceptance of the ultimatum. The Kremlin would also be put on notice, of course, that resort to long-range bombers and missiles would prompt automatic retaliation in kind. On this level, we would invite the Communist leaders to choose between total destruction of the Soviet Union, and accepting a local defeat﻿ ﻿… Had we the will and the means for it in 1956, such a policy would have saved the Hungarian Revolution.

===Original===
This is hard counsel. But it is hard, I think, not for what it says, but for saying it openly. Such a policy involves the risk of war? Of course; but any policy, short of surrender, does that. Any policy that successfully frustrates the Communists’ aim of world domination runs the risk that the Kremlin will choose to lose in a kamikaze-finish. It is hard counsel because it frankly acknowledges that war may be the price of freedom, and thus intrudes on our national complacency. But is it really so hard when it goes on to search for the most likely means of safeguarding both our lives *and* our freedom? Is it so hard when we think of the risks that were taken to create our country?﻿—risks on which our ancestors openly and proudly staked their “lives, fortunes, and sacred honor.” Will we do less to *save* our country?

===Chinese===
这是逆耳的主张。但我想，它之所以逆耳，不在于它说了什么，而在于把话公开说了出来。这样的政策包含战争的风险吗？当然；但投降之外的任何政策都包含这种风险。任何成功挫败共产党支配世界之图谋的政策，都冒着一重风险：克里姆林宫可能宁肯以「神风式」的同归于尽来收场。它之所以逆耳，是因为它坦率承认战争可能是自由的代价，从而触犯了我们举国的自满自足。但它接着又去探寻那最有希望同时保全我们性命*与*自由的手段，这当真还那么逆耳吗？当我们想起缔造这个国家所冒过的风险——我们的祖先曾公开而自豪地把「生命、财产和神圣的荣誉」押作赌注的风险——它还那么逆耳吗？为了*拯救*我们的国家，难道我们会做得更少吗？

===Original===
The risks I speak of are risks on our terms, instead of on Communist terms. *We*, not they, would select the time and place for a test of wills. *We*, not they, would have the opportunity to bring maximum strength to bear on that test. *They*, not we, would have to decide between fighting for limited objectives under unfavorable circumstances, or backing down. And these are immense advantages.

===Chinese===
我所说的风险，是按我们的条件去冒的风险，而不是按共产党的条件。由*我们*而不是他们，来选定这场意志较量试锋的时间与地点。由*我们*而不是他们，来获得把最大力量压上这场较量的机会。由*他们*而不是我们，去决定是在不利的形势下为有限的目标而战，还是退让。而这些都是莫大的便宜。

===Original===
The future, as I see it, will unfold along one of two paths. Either the Communists will retain the offensive; will lay down one challenge after another; will invite us in local crisis after local crisis to choose between all-out war and limited retreat; and will force us, ultimately, to surrender or accept war under the most disadvantageous circumstances. Or *we* will summon the will and the means for taking the initiative, and wage a war of attrition against them﻿—and hope, thereby, to bring about the internal disintegration of the Communist empire. One course runs the risk of war, and leads, in any case, to probable defeat. The other runs the risk of war, and holds forth the promise of victory. For Americans who cherish their lives, but their freedom more, the choice cannot be difficult.

===Chinese===
在我看来，未来将沿着两条路中的一条展开。或者，共产党人保住攻势，接二连三地发出挑战，在一场又一场局部危机中邀我们「全面战争还是有限退却」二者择一，并最终在最不利的形势下逼我们投降或者应战。或者，*我们*唤起夺取主动的意志、备齐相应的手段，对他们展开一场消耗战——并指望由此促成共产主义帝国的内部解体。前一条路冒战争的风险，而且无论如何多半以失败告终；后一条路同样冒战争的风险，却预示着胜利的许诺。对于珍爱自己的生命、但更珍爱自由的美国人来说，这个选择不难作出。
