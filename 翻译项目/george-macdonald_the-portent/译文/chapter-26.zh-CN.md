## XXVI. Escape / XXVI. 出逃

===Original===
It was time, however, to lay some plan, and make some preparations, for our departure. The first thing to be secured was a convenient exit from the house. I searched in all directions, but could discover none better than that by which I had entered. Leaving the house one evening, as soon as Lady Alice had retired, I communicated my situation to Wood, who entered with all his heart into my projects. Most fortunately, through all her so-called madness, Lady Alice had retained and cherished the feeling that there was something sacred about the diamond-ring and the little money which had been intended for our flight before; and she had kept them carefully concealed, where she could find them in a moment. I had sent the ring to a friend in London, to sell it for me; and it produced more than I expected. I had then commissioned Wood to go to the county town and buy a light gig for me; and in this he had been very fortunate. My dear old Constancy had the accomplishment, not at all common to chargers, of going admirably in harness; and I had from the first enjoined upon Wood to get him into as good condition as possible. I now fixed a certain hour at which Wood was to be at a certain spot on one of the roads skirting the park, where I had found a crazy door in the plank-fence—with Constancy in the dogcart, and plenty of wraps for Alice.

“And for Heaven’s sake, Wood,” I concluded, “look to his shoes.”

===Chinese===
然而，是时候为我们的出走定出些计划、做些准备了。头一件要安排妥当的，是从宅子里寻一个便利的出口。我四处搜寻，却再找不到比我当初进来那条路更合适的。一天傍晚，爱丽丝小姐刚一退下歇息，我便离开宅子，把我的处境告知了伍德，他全心全意地附和了我的谋划。最为幸运的是，任凭所谓的疯病缠身，爱丽丝小姐始终留存并珍守着一种感觉：那枚钻石戒指和那一小笔先前为我们的出逃而备下的钱款，是某种不可亵渎的神圣之物；她把它们小心地藏着，藏在一伸手就能摸到的地方。我已把那枚戒指寄给伦敦的一位朋友托他代售；所得之款比我所预料的还多。我又托伍德到郡城去替我买一辆轻便双轮马车；这一趟他也极走运。我那亲爱的老伙伴忠贞（Constancy），有一桩在战马里颇为罕见的本事——套上挽具走得极其出色；我从一开始就叮嘱伍德，务必把它调养到最佳的膘情。当下我定好一个钟点，让伍德届时在环绕园林的一条大路边某个地点候着——那处木板栅栏上有一扇摇摇欲坠的小门——赶着那辆双轮轻马车，带着忠贞，并为爱丽丝备下充足的御寒衣物。

“还有，看在老天的分上，伍德，”我最后叮嘱道，“留神它的蹄铁。”

===Original===
It may seem strange that I should have been able to go and come thus without detection; but it must be remembered that I had made myself more familiar with the place than any of its inhabitants, and that there were only a very few domestics in the establishment. The gardener and stableman slept in the house, for its protection; but I knew their windows perfectly, and most of their movements. I could watch them all day long, if I liked, from some loophole or other of my quarter; where, indeed, I sometimes found that the only occupation I could think of.

===Chinese===
我竟能这样来去自如而不被人发觉，或许叫人觉得奇怪；但须记得，我对这地方已比宅中任何一个住客都更为熟稔，况且府里统共只有寥寥几个仆人。园丁和马夫为了守护宅子，夜里就睡在屋中；可他们的窗子、他们大半的行止，我都摸得一清二楚。只要我愿意，从我所在这头的某个窥孔里，便能整日盯着他们；说实在的，那有时竟成了我所能想起的唯一营生。

===Original===
The next evening I said, “Alice, I must leave the house: will you go with me?”

“Of course I will, Duncan. When?”

“The night after tomorrow, as soon as everyone is in bed and the house quiet. If you have anything you value very much, take it; but the lighter we go the better.”

“I have nothing, Duncan. I will take a little bag—that will do for me.”

“But dress as warmly as you can. It will be cold.”

“Oh, yes; I won’t forget that. Good night.”

She took it as quietly as going to church.

===Chinese===
次日傍晚，我说：“爱丽丝，我必须离开这宅子了：你可愿随我同去？”

“我自然愿意，邓肯。什么时候？”

“后天夜里，待众人都睡下、宅子静下来就走。你若有什么格外看重的物件，就带上；只是咱们走得越轻便越好。”

“我没什么物件，邓肯。我带一只小提袋就够了——于我便足矣。”

“可你要尽量穿得暖和些。夜里会冷。”

“噢，晓得；这个我不会忘的。晚安。”

她把这事看得平静极了，就像要去教堂那般从容。

===Original===
I had not seen Mrs. Blakesley since she had told me that the young earl and countess were expected in about a month; else I might have learned one fact which it was very important I should have known, namely, that their arrival had been hastened by eight or ten days. The very morning of our intended departure, I was looking into the court through a little round hole I had cleared for observation in the dust of one of the windows, believing I had observed signs of unusual preparation on the part of the household, when a carriage drove up, followed by two others, and Lord and Lady Hilton descended and entered, with an attendance of some eight or ten.

There was a great bustle in the house all day. Of course I felt uneasy, for if anything should interfere with our flight, the presence of so many would increase whatever difficulty might occur. I was also uneasy about the treatment my Alice might receive from the newcomers. Indeed, it might be put out of her power to meet me at all. It had been arranged between us that she should not come to the haunted chamber at the usual hour, but towards midnight.

===Chinese===
自从布莱克斯利太太告诉我那位年轻的伯爵和伯爵夫人约莫一月之后便要到来那天起，我就再没见过她；否则我本可得知一桩对我而言至关紧要的事实——他们的行期已提前了八到十天。就在我们约定出走的那个清晨，我正从一扇窗户积灰上被我捅出的一个小圆孔里朝院中张望——我已察觉府中上下有异乎寻常的忙碌迹象——这时一辆马车驶到门前，后头又跟着两辆，希尔顿勋爵和希尔顿夫人下了车进了门，随行约有八到十人之众。

整整一天，宅子里一片忙乱。我心里自然不安，因为倘若有什么碍了我们的出逃，这许多人手在场，只会把任何可能冒出来的难处放大。我也为我那爱丽丝在新来这些人手下会受怎样的对待而忧心。说实在的，他们说不定会让她根本无法前来见我。我们俩事先已商定：她不照往常的时辰到那间闹鬼的房间来，而是挨近午夜时分再来。

===Original===
I was there waiting for her. The hour arrived; the house seemed quiet; but she did not come. I began to grow very uneasy. I waited half an hour more, and then, unable to endure it longer, crept to her door. I tried to open it, but found it fast. At the same moment I heard a light sob inside. I put my lips to the keyhole, and called “Alice.” She answered in a moment:—

“They have locked me in.”

===Chinese===
我在那里等着她。时辰到了；宅子似乎静了下来；可她没有来。我开始惴惴不安起来。我又等了半个钟头，终于再忍不住，便摸索到她的门前。我试着推门，却发现门已闩住。就在这时，我听见屋里传来一声轻轻的抽泣。我把嘴唇贴到锁孔上，唤了一声“爱丽丝”。她立刻应道：——

“他们把我锁在里头了。”

===Original===
The key was gone. There was no time to be lost. Who could tell what they might do tomorrow, if already they were taking precautions against her madness? I would try the key of a neighbouring door, and if that would not fit, I would burst the door open, and take the chance. As it was, the key fitted the lock, and the door opened. We locked it again on the outside, restored the key, and in another moment were in the haunted chamber. Alice was dressed, ready for flight. To me, it was very pathetic to see her in the shapes of years gone by. She looked faded and ancient, notwithstanding that this was the dress in which I had seen her so often of old. Her stream had been standing still, while mine had flowed on. She was a portrait of my own young Alice, a picture of her own former self.

===Chinese===
钥匙不见了。一刻也耽搁不得。既然他们已经在提防她的疯病，谁知道明天还会对她做出什么来？我打算试一试隔壁那扇门的钥匙，若是不合，我就把门撞开，听天由命。结果那把钥匙恰好对得上锁眼，门开了。我们重新从外头把门锁好，将钥匙放回原处，转眼间便已置身于那间闹鬼的房间。爱丽丝已换好衣裳，整装待发。在我眼中，看见她穿着多年以前那身打扮，实在叫人心酸。她显得憔悴而苍老，尽管这正是我往昔曾无数次见她穿过的那身衣裳。她那道溪流已凝滞不前，而我这一道却已奔流向前。她是我那年轻爱丽丝的一幅画像，是她自己旧时模样的一帧写照。

===Original===
One or two lights glancing about below detained us for a little while. We were standing near the window, feeling now very anxious to be clear of the house; Alice was holding me and leaning on me with the essence of trust; when, all at once, she dropped my arm, covered her face with her hands, and called out: “The horse with the clanking shoe!” At the same moment, the heavy door which communicated with this part of the house flew open with a crash, and footsteps came hurrying along the passage. A light gleamed into the room, and by it I saw that Lady Alice, who was standing close to me still, was gazing, with flashing eyes, at the door. She whispered hurriedly:

“I remember it all now, Duncan. My brain is all right. It is come again. But they shall not part us this time. You follow me for once.”

===Chinese===
楼下有一两束灯光晃来晃去，把我们耽搁了片刻。我们站在窗边，此刻一心只盼着能赶紧脱出这宅子；爱丽丝以十足的信赖紧紧挽着我、依偎着我；忽然间，她松开我的手臂，双手掩面，喊出声来：“那匹挂着叮当作响的马蹄铁的马！”就在同一瞬间，连通宅子这一侧的那扇厚重的大门砰的一声撞开，脚步声沿着过道急急赶来。一道光射进屋里，借着那光我看见爱丽丝小姐——她仍紧挨着我站立——正目光灼灼地盯着那扇门。她急促地低语道：

“我现在全都记起来了，邓肯。我的脑子清爽了。它又回来了。可这一回，他们休想再把咱们拆散。这一回，换你跟着我走。”

===Original===
As she spoke, I saw something glitter in her hand. She had caught up an old Malay creese that lay in a corner, and was now making for the door, at which half a dozen domestics were by this time gathered. They, too, saw the glitter, and made way. I followed close, ready to fell the first who offered to lay hands on her. But she walked through them unmenaced, and, once clear, sped like a bird into the recesses of the old house. One fellow started to follow. I tripped him up. I was collared by another. The same instant he lay by his companion, and I followed Alice. She knew the route well enough, and I overtook her in the great hall. We heard pursuing feet rattling down the echoing stair. To enter my room and bolt the door behind us was a moment’s work; and a few moments more took us into the alley of the kitchen-garden. With speedy, noiseless steps, we made our way to the park, and across it to the door in the fence, where Wood was waiting for us, old Constancy pawing the ground with impatience for a good run.

===Chinese===
她说话间，我看见她手里有什么东西一闪。她抄起了角落里一把旧的马来短剑（Malay creese），此刻正朝门口走去，那门前这时已聚了六七个仆人。他们也看见了那一道寒光，纷纷让出路来。我紧跟其后，谁敢上手拿她，我便立时把谁放倒。可她从他们中间穿行而过，无人敢拦，一脱出身来，便像一只鸟儿似的飞入了这座老宅的深处。有个家伙拔腿要追。我一个绊子把他撂倒。另一个揪住了我的衣领。同一瞬间他也躺到了同伴身旁，而我继续追赶爱丽丝。她对路径熟得很，我在大厅里便追上了她。我们听见追赶的脚步声在回声隆隆的楼梯上咚咚而下。闪进我的房间、把门闩死，不过是眨眼的工夫；再过片刻，我们便已置身于菜园的夹道之中。我们迈着疾速而无声的步子，奔到园林，横穿而过，来到栅栏的那扇门前——伍德正等在那里，老忠贞已等不及要痛痛快快跑上一程，正用蹄子焦躁地刨着地。

===Original===
He had had enough of it before twelve hours were over.

Was I not well recompensed for my long years of despair? The cold stars were sparkling overhead; a wind blew keen against us—the wind of our own flight; Constancy stepped out with a will; and I urged him on, for he bore my beloved and me into the future life. Close beside me she sat, wrapped warm from the cold, rejoicing in her deliverance, and now and then looking up with tear-bright eyes into my face. Once and again I felt her sob, but I knew it was a sob of joy, and not of grief. The spell was broken at last, and she was mine. I felt that not all the spectres of the universe could tear her from me, though now and then a slight shudder would creep through me, when the clank of Constancy’s bit would echo sharply back from the trees we swept past.

不出十二个钟头，它可就跑了个够。

我那漫长的绝望岁月，岂不是得到了完满的酬报？清冷的星辰在头顶闪烁；一阵寒风迎面劲吹——那是我们自身逃亡所招来的风；忠贞奋蹄而行，意气风发；我催它再快些，因它载着我和我的心爱之人，正驶入未来的岁月。她就坐在我身侧，裹得暖暖的抵御寒冷，为自己的获救而满心欢喜，不时抬眼，用那泪光莹莹的眼睛望向我的脸。我一次又一次感到她在哽咽，可我知道那是喜极而泣，不是悲伤。那道咒语终于解开了，她是我的了。我感到纵是天地间所有的鬼魅加在一起，也休想再把她从我身边夺走——尽管每当我掠过一排排树木、忠贞嚼子的叮当声从树间清脆地回响过来时，仍会有一阵轻轻的战栗，沿着我的脊背悄然爬过。

===Original===
We rested no more than was absolutely necessary; and in as short a space as ever horse could perform the journey, we reached the Scotch border, and before many more hours had gone over us, Alice was my wife.

===Chinese===
