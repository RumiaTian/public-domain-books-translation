## IV. Hilton Hall / IV. 希尔顿庄园

===Original===
As my father accompanied me to the door, where the gig, which was to carry me over the first stage of my journey, was in waiting, a large target of hide, well studded with brass nails, which had hung in the hall for time unknown﻿—to me, at least﻿—fell on the floor with a dull bang. My father started, but said nothing; and, as it seemed to me, rather pressed my departure than otherwise. I would have replaced the old piece of armour before I went, but he would not allow me to touch it, saying, with a grim smile﻿—

“Take that for an omen, my boy, that your armour must be worn over the conscience, and not over the body. Be a man, Duncan, my boy. Fear nothing, and do your duty.”

===Chinese===
父亲陪着我走到门口，那辆要载我走过旅程头一段路的轻便马车已候在那里。就在此时，一面宽大的兽皮圆盾——其上密密钉着黄铜圆钉，在大厅里已挂了不知几多年头，至少在我是不知其始——“咚”的一声闷响，落到了地上。父亲猛地一惊，却什么也没说；而且在我看来，他倒像是更急着催我动身。我本想临行前把这件老甲胄重新挂回去，他却不许我碰它，只带着一丝冷峻的笑说道——

“孩子，便把这事当作一个预兆吧：你的甲胄，当穿在良心之上，不可穿在身体之上。要做个顶天立地的男子汉，邓肯，我的孩子。什么都不必怕，尽你的本分就是。”

===Original===
A grasp of the hand was all the goodbye I could make; and I was soon rattling away to meet the coach for Edinburgh and London. Seated on the top, I was soon buried in a reverie, from which I was suddenly startled by the sound of tinkling iron. Could it be that my adversary was riding unseen alongside of the coach? Was that the clank of the ominous shoe? But I soon discovered the cause of the sound, and laughed at my own apprehensiveness. For I observed that the sound was repeated every time that we passed any trees by the wayside, and that it was the peculiar echo they gave of the loose chain and steel work about the harness. The sound was quite different from that thrown back by the houses on the road. I became perfectly familiar with it before the day was over.

I reached London in safety, and slept at the house of an old friend of my father, who treated me with great kindness, and seemed altogether to take a liking to me. Before I left he held out a hope of being able, some day or other, to procure for me what I so much desired﻿—a commission in the army.

===Chinese===
紧握了一握，这便是我所能道出的全部道别；不多时我便哐啥上路，去赶那班驶往爱丁堡与伦敦的驿车。我坐在车顶，不多久便沉入一阵遐想，忽被一阵铁器的叮当声所惊起。莫非我那对头正隐着身形，与驿车并辔而行？莫非那是那不祥马蹄铁的叮当声？但我很快便弄清了那声响的来历，不禁自笑这般疑神疑鬼。因为我留意到，每逢车行经过路旁的树木，那声响便复响一回；原来那不过是松垂的链条与马具上的钢铁件，在树间所激起的一种独特的回声。这与路旁房屋抛回的回声截然不同。未等这一日过完，我已对它再熟不过。

我平安抵达伦敦，在父亲一位老友的宅中歇宿。这位老友待我极为厚道，且似乎整体上对我颇生好感。临行之前，他给我留下了一线指望：说不定哪一天，他能为我谋得我那样渴盼的东西——军中的一纸委任状。

===Original===
After spending a day or two with him, and seeing something of London, I climbed once more on the roof of a coach; and, late in the afternoon, was set down at the great gate of Hilton Hall. I walked up the broad avenue, through the final arch of which, as through a huge Gothic window, I saw the hall in the distance. Everything about me looked strange, rich, and lovely. Accustomed to the scanty flowers and diminutive wood of my own country, what I now saw gave me a feeling of majestic plenty, which I can recall at will, but which I have never experienced again. Behind the trees which formed the avenue, I saw a shrubbery, composed entirely of flowering plants, almost all unknown to me. Issuing from the avenue, I found myself amid open, wide, lawny spaces, in which the flowerbeds lay like islands of colour. A statue on a pedestal, the only white thing in the surrounding green, caught my eye. I had seen scarcely any sculpture; and this, attracting my attention by a favourite contrast of colour, retained it by its own beauty. It was a Dryad, or some nymph of the woods, who had just glided from the solitude of the trees behind, and had sprung upon the pedestal to look wonderingly around her. A few large brown leaves lay at her feet, borne thither by some eddying wind from the trees behind. As I gazed, filled with a new pleasure, a drop of rain upon my face made me look up. From a grey, fleecy cloud, with sun-whitened border, a light, gracious, plentiful rain was falling. A rainbow sprang across the sky, and the statue stood within the rainbow. At the same moment, from the base of the pedestal rose a figure in white, graceful as the Dryad above, and neither running, nor appearing to walk quickly, yet fleet as a ghost, glided past me at a few paces, distance, and, keeping in a straight line for the main entrance of the hall, entered by it and vanished.

===Chinese===
与他盘桓了一两日，又略略观览了一番伦敦，我再次攀上驿车的车顶；及至将近黄昏时分，我在希尔顿庄园（Hilton Hall）的大门前下了车。我沿那条宽阔的林荫道前行，穿过最后一道拱门——那拱门宛如一扇巨大的哥特式窗——便远远望见了那座宅邸。我四下里所见的一切，都显得陌生、丰饶而可爱。看惯了故土那般稀疏零落的花、那般矮小的林地，此刻眼前所见，给了我一种堂皇丰足之感——此感我至今能随心唤回，却再不曾重新体味过。在构成林荫道的那两排树后面，我看见一片灌木丛，全由花木组成，其中几乎无一是我所识得。走出林荫道，我发觉自己置身于一片开阔、宽敞、草坪般的空地之中，其间花床错落，宛如一座座色彩的岛屿。一座立在台座上的雕像——周遭碧绿中唯一一点白——攫住了我的目光。我从前几乎不曾见过什么雕塑；这一座，先是借一种我所偏爱的色调反差引我注目，复又凭它自身之美把我留住。那是一尊德律阿得斯（Dryad），或说是某位林中仙女，方才从身后树丛的幽寂里溜将出来，跃上台座，正惊奇地四下打量。她足下散落着几片棕色的大叶，是被身后某阵回旋的风自树间卷来的。我正满怀一种崭新的欣悦凝望着，一滴雨落在我脸上，引我抬头。一朵灰白如絮的云，云边被日头照得发白，正落下一阵轻柔、温婉、丰沛的雨。一道彩虹跃过天际，那雕像便立在彩虹之中。也就在同一刻，台座底部升起一道白色的身影，与头顶那尊德律阿得斯一般曼妙，既不似奔跑，也不似疾走，却快如鬼魅，在我数步开外一掠而过，径直朝宅邸正门奔去，入门而没。

===Original===
I followed in the direction of the mansion, which was large, and of several styles and ages. One wing appeared especially ancient. It was neglected and out of repair, and had in consequence a desolate, almost sepulchral look, an expression heightened by the number of large cypresses which grew along its line. I went up to the central door and knocked. It was opened by a grave, elderly butler. I passed under its flat arch, as if into the midst of the waiting events of my story. For, as I glanced around the hall, my consciousness was suddenly saturated, if I may be allowed the expression, with the strange feeling﻿—known to everyone, and yet so strange﻿—that I had seen it before; that, in fact, I knew it perfectly. But what was yet more strange, and far more uncommon, was, that, although the feeling with regard to the hall faded and vanished instantly, and although I could not in the least surmise the appearance of any of the regions into which I was about to be ushered, I yet followed the butler with a kind of indefinable expectation of seeing something which I had seen before; and every room or passage in that mansion affected me, on entering it for the first time, with the same sensation of previous acquaintance which I had experienced with regard to the hall. This sensation, in every case, died away at once, leaving that portion such as it might be expected to look to one who had never before entered the place.

===Chinese===
我朝那座宅邸的方向走去。宅子很大，兼具几个时代、几种风格的面貌。其中一翼显得格外古旧。它无人照管、年久失修，因而一副荒凉、几乎阴森如墓的神气；沿它一线所植的众多高大柏树，更把这神情衬得浓重。我走到正门前叩了叩门。来开门的是一位神情庄重的年长男管家。我从那道平拱下走过，仿佛走进了我这段故事里那正等候上演的种种事件的正中。因为，就在我环顾大厅之际，我的意识忽然被一种奇异之感所浸透——容我姑且这样形容——那感觉人人都曾体味，却又如此离奇：我仿佛从前见过它；事实上，我对它了如指掌。然而更离奇、也远为少见的却是：尽管关于大厅的这种感觉顷刻便淡去、消散，尽管我对于即将被引见的任何一个去处将是什么模样全然无从揣想，我仍带着一种说不清道不明的期待，随在男管家身后，仿佛预着自己又要见到什么曾见过的东西；而那宅子里每一间屋、每一条廊，头一遭踏入时，都给了我同样的“似曾相识”之感——正如我在大厅所经历的那般。而这感觉，回回都是一现即隐，把那一处地方，还作一个从未踏足其中者所能料想的那般模样。

===Original===
I was received by the housekeeper, a little, prim, benevolent old lady, with colourless face and antique headdress, who led me to the room prepared for me. To my surprise, I found a large wood-fire burning on the hearth; but the feeling of the place revealed at once the necessity for it; and I scarcely needed to be informed that the room, which was upon the ground floor, and looked out upon a little solitary grass-grown and ivy-mantled court, had not been used for years, and therefore required to be thus prepared for an inmate. My bedroom was a few paces down a passage to the right.

===Chinese===
接待我的是女管家——一位矮小、拘谨整洁、面善心慈的老太太，面色寡淡，头饰古旧——她把我领到为我备好的那间屋子。出乎我意料，我看见壁炉里正燃着一炉旺旺的柴火；然而这屋子给人的感觉，当即就显出了生这炉火的必要；几乎不待旁人相告，我也猜得到：这间位于底层的屋子，窗对着一个冷清的、长满荒草、爬满常春藤的小庭院，已多年无人居住，所以须得如此一番布置，方能住进人来。我的卧房就在右手边一条走廊下去几步路的地方。

===Original===
Left alone, I proceeded to make a more critical survey of my room. Its look of ancient mystery was to me incomparably more attractive than any show of elegance or comfort could have been. It was large and low, panelled throughout in oak, black with age, and worm-eaten in many parts﻿—otherwise entire. Both the windows looked into the little court or yard before mentioned. All the heavier furniture of the room was likewise of black oak, but the chairs and couches were covered with faded tapestry and tarnished gilding, apparently the superannuated members of the general household of seats. I could give an individual description of each, for every atom in that room, large enough for discernable shape or colour, seems branded into my brain. If I happen to have the least feverishness on me, the moment I fall asleep, I am in that room.

===Chinese===
剩下我独自一人，我便着手把这间屋子更细细地打量一番。它那古老而幽秘的神气，于我而言，比任何雅致或舒适的排场都更令我倾心——简直无可比拟。屋子又大又低，四壁通体镶着橡木护板，年深日久已呈黑色，许多地方更被虫蛀——除此之外，仍算完好。两扇窗都对着前述的那个小院或庭院。屋里所有较沉重的家具，也同样都是黑橡木所制；然而椅子和长榻上蒙着的，却是褪了色的织锦与失了光的描金，看去显然是全宅众座椅具里那批“告老退休”的成员。我大可对它们逐一细描，因为那间屋子里每一个大到足以辨清形色的小物件，仿佛都烙进了我的脑中。只要我身上略起半点烧热，一合眼入睡，我便又回到了那间屋子里。
