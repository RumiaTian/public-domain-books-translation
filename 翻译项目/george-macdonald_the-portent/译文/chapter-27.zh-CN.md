## XXVII. Freedom / XXVII. 自由

===Original===
Honest Wood joined us in the course of a week or two, and has continued in my service ever since. Nor was it long before Mrs. Blakesley was likewise added to our household, for she had been instantly dismissed from the countess’s service on the charge of complicity in Lady Alice’s abduction.

===Chinese===
伍德在一两周之内便来与我们会合，自此一直在我门下效力。没过多久，布莱克斯利太太也加入了我们一家——她被指控同谋劫走爱丽丝小姐，当即遭伯爵夫人辞退。

===Original===
We lived for some months in a cottage on a hillside, overlooking one of the loveliest of the Scotch lakes. Here I was once more tutor to my Alice. And a quick scholar she was, as ever. Nor, I trust, was I slow in my part. Her character became yet clearer to me, every day. I understood her better and better.

===Chinese===
我们在山坡上的一座小屋里住了数月，从那里可俯瞰苏格兰最秀美的湖泊之一。在这里，我又一次给我的爱丽丝当起了先生。她一如既往，是个极其聪敏的学生；而我相信，自己这一头也并不迟钝。她的性情在我眼中日渐清明，一天胜似一天；我对她，也懂得愈来愈深。

===Original===
She could endure marvellously; but without love and its joy she could not *live*, in any real sense. In uncongenial society, her whole mental faculty had frozen; when love came, her mental world, like a garden in the spring sunshine, blossomed and budded. When she lost me, the Present vanished, or went by her like an ocean that has no milestones; she caring only for the Past, living only in the Past, and that reflection of it in the dim glass of her hope, which prefigured the Future.

===Chinese===
她极能隐忍；然而若没有爱，以及爱所带来的欢愉，从任何真正的意义上讲，她都算不上是在*活着*。在气味不投的人群中，她整个的心智能力会尽数冻结；可一旦爱降临，她的精神世界便如同春日阳光下的花园，抽芽、绽放。当她失去我时，“现在”便消失了，又或者像一片没有里程标的汪洋，从她身旁浩荡流逝——她只在乎“过去”，只活在“过去”，以及“过去”映在她那希望的幽暗镜中的那个倒影里；而那倒影，正预示着“未来”。

===Original===
We have never again heard the clanking shoe. Indeed, after we had passed a few months in the absorption of each other’s society, we began to find that we doubted a great deal of what seemed to have happened to us. It was as if the gates of the unseen world were closing against us, because we had shut ourselves up in the world of the present. But we let it go gladly. We felt that love was the gate to an unseen world infinitely beyond that region of the psychological in which we had hitherto moved; for this love was teaching us to love all men, and live for all men. In fact, we are now, I am glad to say, very much like other people; and wonder, sometimes, how much of the story of our lives might be accounted for on the supposition that unusual coincidences had fallen in with psychological peculiarities. Dr. Ruthwell, who is sometimes our most welcome guest, has occasionally hinted at the sabre-cut as the key to all the mysteries of the story, seeing nothing of it was at least recorded before I came under his charge. But I have only to remind him of one or two circumstances, to elicit from his honesty and immediate confession of bewilderment, followed by silence; although he evidently still clings to the notion that in that sabre-cut lies the solution of much of the marvel. At all events, he considers me sane enough now, else he would hardly honour me with so much of his confidence as he does. Having examined into Lady Alice’s affairs, I claimed the fortune which she had inherited. Lord Hilton, my former pupil, at once acknowledged the justice of the claim, and was considerably astonished to find how much more might have been demanded of him, which had been spent over the allowance made from her income for her maintenance. But we had enough without claiming that.

===Chinese===
自那以后，我们再也没有听见过那叮当作响的马蹄铁。说实在的，在我们彼此相得、沉浸于对方的陪伴中度过几个月之后，我们竟开始发觉：那些仿佛发生在我们身上的事，有相当一部分我们已将信将疑。仿佛那不可见世界的大门正对我们阖上，只因我们已把自己禁闭在了眼前这个现世之中。可我们欣然由它如此。我们感到，爱本身就是通往一个不可见世界的门，那世界远在我们迄今所游走的那片心灵领域之上，不可测度；因为这份爱，正教我们去爱一切人，为一切人而活。说起来令人欣慰，我们如今和旁人已没什么两样；有时也不免诧异：我们这一生的种种，倘若假定是异乎寻常的巧合同心理上的特异禀赋不期而遇，究竟有多少能由此得到解释。鲁思韦尔医生有时是我们座上最受欢迎的客人，他偶尔会暗示：那道刀伤，正是解开这故事全部谜团的钥匙——因为至少在我落到他照管之前，这些事并无半点记载。可我只需向他提醒一两个情由，便能引得他凭着那份诚实，当即坦然承认自己也百思不解，继之便是沉默；尽管他显然仍抱着这样的念头：这许多奇事的解答，就藏在那道刀伤之中。无论如何，他如今总算认为我的心智够清醒了，否则也不至于像现在这般，赏脸给我那样多的信赖。我查明了爱丽丝小姐的产业之后，便主张领回她所继承的那笔财产。希尔顿勋爵——我从前的学生——当即承认这一要求正当合理；而当他发现原本还可以向他索要远不止此——因为从她收入中拨作她生活费的津贴之外，还另有款项被支用了——他相当惊讶。但即使不去讨这笔，我们已有的也已足够。

===Original===
My wife purchased for me the possession of my forefathers, and there we live in peace and hope. To her I owe the delight which I feel every day of my life in looking upon the haunts of my childhood as still mine. They help me to keep young. And so does my Alice’s hair; for although much grey now mingles with mine, hers is as dark as ever. For her heart, I know that cannot grow old; and while the heart is young, man may laugh old Time in the face, and dare him to do his worst.

===Chinese===
我的妻子为我买回了先祖的那处产业，我们便在那里过着安宁而满怀希望的日子。我此生每一天，望着童年时流连的那些所在、视它们仍归我所有时，心中所涌起的那份欣悦——都是拜她所赐。是它们助我青春常驻。我的爱丽丝的那头秀发，也同样如此；因为虽然我的发间如今已掺入了不少灰白，她的秀发却仍一如既往地乌黑。至于她的心，我知道那是绝不会老的；而只要这颗心还年轻，人便可当面嗤笑那老迈的时光，敢放言任他使出最狠的手段。
