## VIII. The Somnambulist / VIII. 梦游者

===Original===
One night I was sitting in my room, devouring an old romance which I had brought from the library. It was late. The fire blazed bright; but the candles were nearly burnt out, and I grew sleepy over the volume, romance as it was.

Suddenly I found myself on my feet, listening with an agony of intention. Whether I had heard anything I could not tell; but I felt as if I had. Yes; I was sure of it. Far away, somewhere in the labyrinthine pile, I heard a faint cry. Driven by some secret impulse, I flew, without a moment’s reflection, to the closet door, lifted the tapestry within, unfastened the second door, and stood in the great waste echoing hall, amid the touches, light and ghostly, of the cobwebs set afloat in the eddies occasioned by my sudden entrance.

===Chinese===
一天夜里，我坐在自己房中，正贪婪地读着一本从图书室借来的旧传奇。夜已深了。炉火熊熊，亮堂堂地燃着；可两支蜡烛都已快燃到尽头，我虽读的是传奇，也不禁对着书卷昏然欲睡。

忽然间，我发觉自己已霍然起立，正以一种几近痛苦的专注侧耳聆听。我究竟听见了什么没有，自己也说不清；但我感觉仿佛听见了。是的；我对此确信无疑。在远处，在那迷宫般层叠错综的宅邸深处的某个地方，我听见了一声微弱的啼叫。受某种隐秘的冲动所驱使，我不假思索地飞奔到套间门前，掀起门内的挂毯，解开第二道门，便站在了那空旷而回声四起的大厅之中——蛛网被我这突如其来的闯入所搅起的气流卷得飘浮起来，如鬼魅般轻轻拂过我的周身。

===Original===
A faded moonbeam fell on the floor, and filled the place with an ancient dream-light, which wrought strangely on my brain, and filled it, as if it, too, were but a deserted, sleepy house, haunted by old dreams and memories. Recollecting myself, I went back for a light; but the candles were both flickering in the sockets, and I was compelled to trust to the moon. I ascended the staircase. Old as it was, not a board creaked, not a banister shook﻿—the whole felt solid as rock. Finding, at length, no more stair to ascend, I groped my way on; for here there was no direct light from the moon﻿—only the light of the moonlit air. I was in some trepidation, I confess; for how should I find my way back? But the worst result likely to ensue was, that I should have to spend the night without knowing where; for with the first glimmer of morning, I should be able to return to my room. At length, after wandering into several rooms and out again, my hand fell on a latched door. I opened it, and entered a long corridor, with many windows on one side. Broad strips of moonlight lay slantingly across the narrow floor, divided by regular intervals of shade.

===Chinese===
一道黯淡的月光落在地板上，给整个地方笼上一层古老的梦之光，怪异地作用于我的脑际，将它填满，仿佛我的脑子也不过是座荒废、慵倦的宅子，有旧日的梦与记忆在其中作祟。我回过神来，转身回去取一盏灯；可两支蜡烛都在烛台上明灭不定，我只得仰仗月光。我登上楼梯。楼梯虽古旧，却无一块木板吱呀作响，无一截扶手摇颤——整体坚实如岩。终于，发现再没有阶梯可上了，我便摸索着往前走去；因为这里已无月光直接照入——唯有被月光照亮的那一片空气所泛出的光。我心中不免有些惶恐，我承认；因为我该如何寻路而归呢？但可能招致的最坏结果，不过是我得在不知何处的地方将就一夜；因为只要晨光初现，我便能回到自己的房间。终于，在误入又退出几间屋子之后，我的手触到了一扇带门闩的门。我推开它，走进一条长廊，廊的一侧开着许多扇窗。一道道宽阔的月光斜斜地铺在狭窄的廊道上，被等距的阴影一一隔开。

===Original===
I started, and my heart swelled; for I saw a movement somewhere﻿—I could neither tell where, nor of what: I was only aware of motion. I stood in the first shadow, and gazed, but saw nothing. I sped across the light to the next shadow, and stood again, looking with fearful fixedness of gaze towards the far end of the corridor. Suddenly a white form glimmered and vanished. I crossed to the next shadow. Again a glimmer and vanishing, but nearer. Nerving myself to the utmost, I ceased the stealthiness of my movements, and went forward, slowly and steadily. A tall form, apparently of a woman, dressed in a long white robe, appeared in one of the streams of light, threw its arms over its head, gave a wild cry﻿—which, notwithstanding its wildness and force, had a muffled sound, as if many folds, either of matter or of space, intervened﻿—and fell at full length along the moonlight. Amidst the thrill of agony which shook me at the cry, I rushed forward, and, kneeling beside the prostrate figure, discovered that, unearthly as was the scream which had preceded her fall, it was the Lady Alice. I saw the fact in a moment: the Lady Alice was a somnambulist. Startled by the noise of my advance, she had awaked; and the usual terror and fainting had followed. She was cold and motionless as death. What was to be done? If I called, the probability was that no one would hear me; or if anyone should hear﻿—but I need not follow the course of my thought, as I tried in vain to recover the poor girl. Suffice it to say, that both for her sake and my own, I could not face the chance of being found, in the dead of night, by common-minded domestics, in such a situation.

===Chinese===
我一惊，心头一紧；因为我看见某处有一丝动静——既说不清在何处，也说不清是什么：我只觉知到有东西在动。我立在第一片阴影中凝望，却什么也没看见。我疾步穿过光带，闪入下一片阴影，再次站定，怀着恐惧而凝定的目光望向长廊的尽头。忽然，一个白色的身影一闪便消失了。我移到下一片阴影。又是一闪即逝，却更近了。我极力镇定心神，不再悄悄掩行，而是缓缓地、稳稳地向前走去。一个高挑的身影——看模样是个女子——披着长长的白袍，出现在其中一道光带里，将双臂猛地举过头顶，发出一声狂乱的喊叫——那叫声尽管狂野有力，听来却发闷，仿佛隔着许多重褶层，或属于物质，或属于空间——随后便直挺挺地倒在那片月光里。那声叫喊令我一阵痛苦的战栗透遍全身，我冲上前去，跪倒在那个倒卧的身影身旁，发现这倒下前发出那声非人尖叫的，竟是爱丽丝小姐（Lady Alice）。我当即明白了是怎么回事：爱丽丝小姐是个梦游者（somnambulist）。被我上前的脚步声所惊，她醒了过来；随之而来的便是惯有的惊恐与昏厥。她冰冷不动，如同死人。该怎么办呢？倘若我呼救，多半无人听得见；纵使有人听见——但我不必细述我心中辗转的念头，因为我正徒劳地设法使这可怜的姑娘苏醒。只消这样说便够了：无论是为她着想还是为我自己，我都无法面对在深夜里、在这般境况之下，被一干庸俗的仆佣撞见的可能。

===Original===
I was kneeling by her side, not knowing what to do, when a horror, as from the presence of death suddenly recognized, fell upon me. I thought she must be dead. But at the same moment, I hear, or seemed to hear, (how should I know which?) the rapid gallop of a horse, and the clank of a loose shoe.

===Chinese===
我跪在她身旁，正不知所措，忽然一阵恐惧袭来——那是猝然觉察到死亡就在近前时所生的那种恐惧。我想她一定已经死了。可就在同一瞬间，我听见——又似乎是听见，我怎辨得真切？——一阵急促的马蹄奔驰声，以及一只松脱马蹄铁的叮当声。

===Original===
In an agony of fear, I caught her up in my arms, and, carrying her on my arms, as one carries a sleeping child, hurried back through the corridor. Her hair, which was loose, trailed on the ground; and, as I fled, I trampled on it and stumbled. She moaned; and that instant the gallop ceased. I lifted her up across my shoulder, and carried her more easily. How I found my way to the stair I cannot tell: I know that I groped about for some time, like one in a dream with a ghost in his arms. At last I reached it, and descending, crossed the hall, and entered my room. There I placed Lady Alice upon an old couch, secured the doors, and began to breathe﻿—and think. The first thing was to get her warm, for she was cold as the dead. I covered her with my plaid and my dressing-gown, pulled the couch before the fire, and considered what to do next.

===Chinese===
在极度的恐惧之中，我将她一把抱起，托在双臂之上，如同抱一个酣睡的孩子那般，匆匆穿过长廊往回赶。她那散开的长发拖在地上；奔逃之间，我踩住了它，一个踉跄。她发出一声呻吟；就在那一瞬，那奔驰声戛然而止。我把她举起，横搁在肩上，抱起来便轻松了些。我是怎样找到楼梯的，自己也无法说清：我只知道我摸索了一阵，仿佛一个怀中抱着鬼魂的梦中人。终于我走到了楼梯口，下楼穿过大厅，回到自己房中。我把爱丽丝小姐安置在一张旧长榻上，闩好各道门，才开始喘过气来——并开始思索。头一件事是要让她暖过来，因为她冷得如同死人。我用我的格子呢披肩和晨衣把她盖好，把长榻拉到炉火跟前，然后思量下一步该怎么办。
