## XIX / 十九

Between Father and Son / 父子之间

===Original===

“Well, Skinner, my man,” said Jimmy, “how goes it?”

Mr. Crocker looked about him cautiously. Then his priestly manner fell from him like a robe, and he bounded forward.

“Jimmy!” he exclaimed, seizing his son’s hand and shaking it violently. “Say, it’s great seeing you again, Jim!”

Jimmy drew himself up haughtily.

“Skinner, my good menial, you forget yourself strangely! You will be getting fired if you mitt the handsome guest in this chummy fashion!” He slapped his father on the back. “Dad, this is great! How on earth do you come to be here? What’s the idea? Why the buttling? When did you come over? Tell me all!”

Mr. Crocker hoisted himself nimbly onto the writing-desk, and sat there, beaming, with dangling legs.

===Chinese===

「喂，斯金纳，我的好伙计，」吉米说，「日子过得怎么样啊？」

克罗克先生小心翼翼地朝四下里张望了一圈。随后，他那一身管家祭司般的威仪像一袭长袍似的从他身上滑落，他一个箭步蹿上前去。

「吉米！」他嚷道，一把攥住儿子的手，使劲摇晃。「哎呀，又见到你真是太好啦，吉姆！」

吉米傲然挺直了身子。

「斯金纳，我的好奴仆，你可真是忘乎所以得离奇！要是你跟这位英俊的客人这么亲热地动拳碰掌，可就要被辞退啦！」他拍了拍父亲的背。「爸，这可真妙！你到底是怎么跑到这儿来的？打的什么主意？怎么当起管家来了？什么时候渡海过来的？一五一十全告诉我！」

克罗克先生轻捷地一撑，坐上了写字台，两条腿悬空晃着，满面红光。

===Original===

“It was your letter that did it, Jimmy. Say, Jim, there wasn’t any need for you to do a thing like that just for me.”

“Well, I thought you would have a better chance of being a peer without me around. By the way, dad, how did my stepmother take the Lord Percy episode?”

A shadow fell upon Mr. Crocker’s happy face.

“I don’t like to do much thinking about your stepmother,” he said. “She was pretty sore about Percy. And she was pretty sore about your lighting out for America. But, gee! what she must be feeling like now that I’ve come over, I daren’t let myself think.”

“You haven’t explained that yet. Why did you come over?”

===Chinese===

「全是你那封信闹出来的事，吉米。哎，吉姆，你犯不着光为了我干这种事。」

「呃，我想没有我在跟前碍眼，你当贵族的机会总要大些。对了，爸，我那继母对珀西勋爵那桩公案作何感想？」

一片阴云掠上了克罗克先生那张欢喜的脸。

「关于你继母，我不愿意多想，」他说，「珀西那件事，她已经气得够呛。你拔腿溜到美国来，她又气得够呛。可是，老天爷！如今连我也跑了过来，她心里不定成了什么滋味，我可不敢让自己去想。」

「这个你还没交代呢。你到底为什么要过来？」

===Original===

“Well, I’d been feeling homesick﻿—I always do over there in the baseball season﻿—and then talking with Pett made it worse﻿—”

“Talking with Pett? Did you see him, then, when he was in London?”

“See him? I let him in!”

“How?”

“Into the house, I mean. I had just gone to the front door to see what sort of a day it was﻿—I wanted to know if there had been enough rain in the night to stop my having to watch that cricket game﻿—and just as I got there the bell rang. I opened the door.”

“A revoltingly plebeian thing to do! I’m ashamed of you, dad! They won’t stand for that sort of thing in the House of Lords!”

===Chinese===

「呃，我一直害着思乡病——每逢棒球季，我在那边总是这样——后来又跟佩特聊了一通，病势越发沉重——」

「跟佩特聊了一通？这么说，他在伦敦那阵子你见到他了？」

「见到他？是我放他进的门！」

「怎么讲？」

「我是说，放他进屋。我当时刚走到前门口，想看看天气如何——我想知道夜里雨下得够不够大，能不能免了我去看那场板球赛——我刚走到门口，门铃就响了。我便开了门。」

「这事儿干得可真是俗不可耐！我都替你臊得慌，爸！上议院（House of Lords）里可容不下这种行径！」

===Original===

“Well, before I knew what was happening they had taken me for the butler. I didn’t want your stepmother to know I’d been opening doors﻿—you remember how touchy she was always about it so I just let it go at that and jollied them along. But I just couldn’t help asking the old man how the pennant race was making out, and that tickled him so much that he offered me a job here as butler if I ever wanted to make a change. And then your note came saying that you were going to New York, and﻿—well, I couldn’t help myself. You couldn’t have kept me in London with ropes. I sneaked out next day and bought a passage on the *Carmantic*﻿—she sailed the Wednesday after you left﻿—and came straight here. They gave me this job right away.” Mr. Crocker paused, and a holy light of enthusiasm made his homely features almost beautiful. “Say, Jim, I’ve seen a ballgame every darned day since I landed! Say, two days running Larry Doyle made home-runs! But, gosh! that guy Klem is one swell robber! See here!” Mr. Crocker sprang down from the desk, and snatched up a handful of books, which he proceeded to distribute about the floor. “There were two men on bases in the sixth and What’s-his-name came to bat. He lined one out to centre-field﻿—where this book is﻿—and﻿—”

“Pull yourself together, Skinner! You can’t monkey about with the employer’s library like that.” Jimmy restored the books to their places. “Simmer down and tell me more. Postpone the gossip from the diamond. What plans have you made? Have you considered the future at all? You aren’t going to hold down this buttling job forever, are you? When do you go back to London?”

The light died out of Mr. Crocker’s face.

===Chinese===

「唉，我还没弄明白是怎么回事，他们已经把我当成管家了。我可不想让你继母知道我开过门——你总记得，她一向为这个多么动气——所以我就将错就错，顺着他们打哈哈。可我到底没憋住，问了老佩特一句锦旗赛进行得怎么样。这话把他逗得那么开心，他当场拍板：哪天我想换个东家，就上这儿来当管家。接着你的信到了，说你就要来纽约——这下，我实在把持不住了。你就是拿绳子也休想把我拴在伦敦。第二天我悄悄溜出门，买了张*卡曼提克号*（Carmantic）的船票——它在你动身后那个星期三启的航——然后径直奔了这儿。他们二话不说就把这份差事给了我。」克罗克先生顿了顿，一股神圣的狂热之光映亮了他那其貌不扬的眉眼，几乎使它们俊美起来。「嘿，吉姆，自打上岸，我天天都看一场球赛，一天没落！嘿，拉里·多伊尔（Larry Doyle）连着两天打出本垒打！可是，天哪！克莱姆（Klem）那家伙真是个头号大盗！你瞧！」克罗克先生从写字台上一跃而下，抓起一把书，便朝地板四下里分发起来。「第六局两人上垒，那个谁谁谁上场打击。他打出一记平直球，直飞中外野——就是搁这本书的地方——接着——」

「振作起来，斯金纳！主人家的书房可不许你这么折腾。」吉米把书归回原位。「冷静冷静，再跟我往下说。球场上那些闲话先搁一搁。你作了什么打算？到底考虑过将来没有？这份管家的差事你总不至于干一辈子吧？什么时候回伦敦？」

那道光芒从克罗克先生脸上熄灭了。

===Original===

“I guess I shall have to go back some time. But how can I yet, with the Giants leading the league like this?”

“But did you just light out without saying anything?”

“I left a note for your stepmother telling her I had gone to America for a vacation. Jimmy, I hate to think what she’s going to do to me when she gets me back!”

“Assert yourself, dad! Tell her that woman’s place is the home and man’s the ballpark! Be firm!”

Mr. Crocker shook his head dubiously.

“It’s all very well to talk that way when you’re three thousand miles from home, but you know as well as I do, Jim, that your stepmother, though she’s a delightful woman, isn’t the sort you can assert yourself with. Look at this sister of hers here. I guess you haven’t been in the house long enough to have noticed, but she’s very like Eugenia in some ways. She’s the boss all right, and old Pett does just what he’s told to. I guess it’s the same with me, Jim. There’s a certain type of man that’s just born to have it put over on him by a certain type of woman. I’m that sort of man and your stepmother’s that sort of woman. No, I guess I’m going to get mine all right, and the only thing to do is to keep it from stopping me having a good time now.”

===Chinese===

「我想迟早总得回去。可巨人队正这么领跑全联盟，眼下我怎么走得出？」

「可你就这么一声不吭地溜了？」

「我给你继母留了张条子，说我到美国度假去了。吉米，一想到等她把我弄回去之后要怎么发落我，我就不寒而栗！」

「拿出丈夫气概来，爸！告诉她：女人安身的地方是家里，男人安身的地方是球场！坚决一点！」

克罗克先生将信将疑地摇了摇头。

「离家三千里的时候，说说这种漂亮话自然容易。可你跟我一样清楚，吉姆，你那位继母虽说是个讨人喜欢的女人，却不是那种你能硬气得起来的对象。瞧瞧她这位妹妹吧。我猜你在这宅子里住得还短，没来得及看出来，她有些地方跟尤金妮娅像极了。她才是当家的没错，老佩特让干什么就干什么。我想我这情形也差不多，吉姆。世上有一种男人，天生就注定要吃某一种女人的亏。我正是那种男人，你继母正是那种女人。唉，到头来我那一份横竖是逃不掉的，眼下唯一的办法，就是别让它搅了我眼前的快活日子。」

===Original===

There was truth in what he said, and Jimmy recognised it. He changed the subject.

“Well, never mind that. There’s no sense in worrying oneself about the future. Tell me, dad, where did you get all the ‘dinner-is-served, madam’ stuff? How did you ever learn to be a butler?”

“Bayliss taught me back in London. And, of course, I’ve played butlers when I was on the stage.”

Jimmy did not speak for a moment.

“Did you ever play a kidnapper, dad?” he asked at length.

===Chinese===

他这话不无道理，吉米也听出来了。他换了个话题。

「好了，那个不谈。为将来操心毫无意义。告诉我，爸，你那一套『晚饭备好了，夫人』的功夫是从哪儿学来的？你到底是怎么学会当管家的？」

「在伦敦的时候贝利斯教的。再说，我从前在台上也演过管家。」

吉米一时没有作声。

「爸，你演过绑匪吗？」他终于开口问道。

===Original===

“Sure. I was Chicago Ed. in a crook play called *This Way Out*. Why, surely you saw me in that? I got some good notices.”

Jimmy nodded.

“Of course. I knew I’d seen you play that sort of part some time. You came on during the dark scene and﻿—”

“﻿—switched on the lights and﻿—”

“﻿—covered the bunch with your gun while they were still blinking! You were great in that part, dad.”

===Chinese===

「当然演过。我在一出叫《由此出去》（This Way Out）的盗贼戏里演过芝加哥·埃德（Chicago Ed）。怎么，你肯定看过吧？我还得了几篇漂亮剧评呢。」

吉米点了点头。

「当然。我就说我什么时候看过你演这一路角色。你在暗场的时候上场，接着——」

「——把灯一开，再——」

「——乘他们还在眨眼的当儿，一杆枪把满屋子人都罩住了！那个角色你演得棒极了，爸。」

===Original===

“It was a good part,” said Mr. Crocker modestly. “It had fat. I’d like to have a chance to play a kidnapper again. There’s a lot of pep to kidnappers.”

“You *shall* play one again,” said Jimmy. “I am putting on a little sketch with a kidnapper as the star part.”

“Eh? A sketch? You, Jim? Where?”

“Here. In this house. It is entitled ‘Kidnapping Ogden’ and opens tonight.”

Mr. Crocker looked at his only son in concern. Jimmy appeared to him to be rambling.

===Chinese===

「那是个好角色，」克罗克先生谦虚地说，「油水足。我很想再有机会演一回绑匪。绑匪这个行当，来劲儿得很。」

「你*一定*会再演一回的，」吉米说。「我正在排一出小短剧，绑匪正是头牌角色。」

「就在这儿。就在这座宅子里。剧名叫《绑走奥格登》，今晚开演。」

克罗克先生忧心忡忡地望着他的独生子。在他看来，吉米说话已经开始颠三倒四了。

===Original===

“Amateur theatricals?” he hazarded.

“In the sense that there is no pay for performing, yes. Dad, you know that kid Ogden upstairs? Well, it’s quite simple. I want you to kidnap him for me.”

Mr. Crocker sat down heavily. He shook his head.

“I don’t follow all this.”

“Of course not. I haven’t begun to explain. Dad, in your rambles through this joint you’ve noticed a girl with glorious red-gold hair, I imagine?”

===Chinese===

「就演出不拿薪水这一点而言，是的。爸，楼上那个小子奥格登你知道吧？呃，事情非常简单。我想请你替我把他绑走。」
