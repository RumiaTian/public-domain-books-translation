## XXXII / 第三十二章

===Original===
The deacons appointed Wednesday night, for Mary to come and give in her experience and they invited all the people to hear them decide if her vision meant that her sins were forgiven, or if Satan had sent a dream to deceive her.

Before first dark she dressed herself carefully in a new black and white checked homespun dress and a large white apron which covered the whole front of her skirt. She bound her head with a fresh white cloth instead of a colored headkerchief, then put on a wide-brimmed white straw hat. The hat was old, its crown was broken and its brim flopped down at one side, but it was white, and she felt it was better for tonight than her new black sailor.

She had not worn her earrings lately, for the babies liked them and tried to snatch them out of her ears. They were in her trunk wrapped up with her love-charm. She slowly untied the cloth that held them and put the earrings on, then the charm.

Instead of tarnishing, the earrings had got brighter with the years. They glittered as gaily as on the first day she ever saw them. Should she wear her charm? Would it be sinful to wear it tonight? She looked in the glass and asked the question to herself. One of her minds said, “Take it off.” The other mind said, “Don’t be a fool, keep it on.” The earrings shook and twinkled. The deacons were men who needed to be ruled in her favor tonight. She would wear the charm too.

===Chinese===
执事们定下星期三晚上，让玛丽来陈述她的属灵经历，还邀请众人都来，听他们裁断：她见的那异象，究竟是罪已蒙赦的凭据，还是撒旦打发来诓她的一个梦。

天还没擦黑，她就仔仔细细地穿戴起来：换上一条新的黑白格子家织布裙子，系上一条雪白的大围裙，把裙子的整个前襟都遮住了。她头上不扎花头巾，改用一块簇新的白布裹头，又戴上一顶白草阔边帽。帽子是旧的，帽顶塌了，一边的帽檐耷拉下来，可它是白的，她觉得今晚戴它，比那顶新的黑水手帽更合适。

近来她没戴过耳环，因为宝宝们喜欢，总想伸手把她耳朵上的耳环抓下来。耳环收在她的箱子里，和她的爱情符咒包在一处。她慢慢解开裹着它们的布，先戴上耳环，再戴上符咒。

耳环非但没有发乌，反倒随着岁月一年亮过一年，闪闪烁烁，还像她头一回见着它们那天一样欢快。符咒要不要戴呢？今晚戴着它算不算罪呢？她照着镜子，暗暗问自己。一个心思说：「摘下来。」另一个心思说：「别犯傻，戴着。」耳环颤颤地闪着光。今晚这些执事，是须得笼络得偏向她的人。她便连符咒也一并戴上了。

===Original===
When she reached Maum Hannah’s house, the place was packed and jammed with people. The news of her finding peace had traveled fast, the crowd had come to hear her experience and to see how the deacons dealt with her. She held her head high and her eyes straight in front of her as she walked through the crowd and took her seat on the candidate’s bench at one side of the fireplace facing the five deacons who sat on the other side. Andrew was dressed in his Sunday clothes and his face had a solemn look; Doll sat high up in front with the members, with her sharp little eyes on her husband.

Doll was nervous. Her hands kept clasping and unclasping, her eyelids batted fast now and then, and she kept whispering to Maum Hannah, who sat with her eyes closed, praying.

Brer Dee whispered to Andrew, who nodded his head and stroked his chin and gazed around at the assembled crowd. The time had come. He got up and took the cowbell off the mantelpiece and made a few hoarse tinkles, then he began lining out the hymn to be sung, two lines at a time:

===Chinese===
她走到汉纳婆婆家时，屋里已经挤得满满当当、水泄不通。她寻得平安的消息传得飞快，众人都赶了来，要听她的经历，也要看看执事们怎么发落她。她高高昂着头，眼睛直望着前面，从人群中间走过去，在炉床一边的候教凳上落了座，正对着坐在另一边的五位执事。安德鲁穿着礼拜天的衣裳，脸上一副庄重的神情；多莉跟教友们坐在前排高处，一双尖利的小眼睛盯住自己的丈夫。

多莉心里发慌。她两只手不停地握拢又松开，眼皮不时急促地眨动，还一个劲儿地跟汉纳婆婆嘀咕，汉纳婆婆却闭着眼睛坐着祷告。

迪老哥跟安德鲁咬了咬耳朵，安德鲁点点头，摸着下巴，环视聚拢来的人群。时候到了。他站起身，从壁炉台上取下牛铃，摇出几声喑哑的叮当，然后开始逐句领唱要唱的赞美诗，一次领出两句：

===Original===
“My soul be on thy guard.
					

					Ten thousand foes arise.”

Brer Dee raised it, all the people joined in singing it; high treble voices mingled with deep basses, but Mary stood dumb. If she cracked her teeth and tried to sing she would break down and cry like a child.

“The hosts of sin are pressing hard,
					

					To draw thee from the skies.”

Andrew’s voice rose high and his eyes stared at something away above Mary’s head.

===Chinese===
「我的魂啊，要警醒，

万千仇敌正兴起；」

迪老哥起了调，众人都跟着唱起来；尖亮的高音和深沉的低音交融在一处，玛丽却哑然站着。她只要一张口跟着唱，准得垮下来，哭得像个孩子。

「罪恶大军重重地紧逼，

要把你拽离天际。」

安德鲁的嗓音高扬上去，眼睛直瞪着玛丽头顶上方遥远的什么东西。

===Original===
When the hymn was sung, Brer Dee read out of the Book how the angels in Heaven rejoice over one sinner who repents; then he prayed a short prayer asking God and Jesus to guide the deacons tonight; to help them know whether the sister who was a candidate here before them was truly saved or if she had only been deceived by a vision from Satan.

Mary felt a vague dread of standing up trying to tell all these people what she had seen; of trying to make them understand how her misery and mourning had been changed to joy. Her knees were shaking and when the prayer was over she sat with downcast eyes, while Andrew announced that on the night after her son was buried, Mary Pinesett, a fallen member, had experienced a deep conviction of sin. She did not wait to seek forgiveness, but started to pray that very night, and had prayed on without a drop of water to drink, without a crumb of bread to eat until a vision had come before her mortal eyes. She was here tonight to tell the deacons this vision.

When she got to her feet to speak, the room grew so quiet she could hear her own quickened breath. She was very hoarse and her first words were low and trembling, but as soon as they cleared the way, others that were steady rushed in and loosened her tongue. She was back in the pine woods, seeking, praying, crying in the night. She could hear Unex speaking again, but now his voice was no voice from the grave but an angel’s voice from Heaven.

===Chinese===
赞美诗唱完，迪老哥从圣经里诵读天使怎样在天上为一个悔改的罪人欢喜；接着他做了个简短的祷告，求上帝和耶稣今夜指引执事们，帮助他们分辨：眼前这位候教的姊妹到底是真正得救了，还是不过叫撒旦的一个幻象哄骗了一场。

玛丽心头笼着一层说不清的畏惧：要站起来，把她看见的讲给这么多人听；要叫他们明白，她的苦楚和哀恸是怎么变成欢喜的。她两膝直打颤。祷告完了，她垂着眼坐着，听安德鲁宣布：她儿子下葬后的那个晚上，被革出教门的教友玛丽·派恩塞特深深知罪。她没有先去求赦免，当晚就祷告起来，一路祷告下去，不喝一滴水、不吃一口面包，直到一个异象来到她凡人的眼前。她今晚到这里来，就是要把这异象讲给执事们听。

她站起身要说话时，屋里静得连她自己急促的呼吸声都听得见。她的嗓子哑得厉害，起初几个字又低又颤，可这几个字一开出路来，后头稳稳当当的话便一拥而进，松开了她的舌头。她又回到了那片松林里，求救恩、祷告、在黑夜里哭泣。她听得见小意外又在说话，可如今他的声音不再是坟里发出的声音，而是从天上来的天使的声音。

===Original===
A strange power seized her and held her and she spoke with a new, quickened tongue. Instead of looking first at one deacon, then at another she hardly took her eyes from Andrew’s face and he gazed back at her spellbound, with his eyes as bright as stars.

She could feel how eagerly the people listened, how they leaned forward trying not to miss a word she said. Some of them smiled kindly, others wiped tears away.

Brer Dee listened eagerly too, but when she finished, he got to his feet and said solemnly, “Brudders an’ sisters, Si May-e has been a turrible sinner. ’E has sent many mens to Hell. His soul might be clean but his body ought to be baptized again befo we receives ’em into Heaven’s Gate Church.”

===Chinese===
一种奇异的力量攫住了她、托住了她，她用一条崭新的、灵快的舌头说话。她不再先看看这个执事、再看看那个执事，眼睛几乎离不开安德鲁的脸，他也出神地回望着她，眼睛亮得像星星。

她感觉得到众人听得多么急切，怎样把身子往前探，生怕漏掉她说的半个字。有的人慈和地微笑，有的人抹去了眼泪。

迪老哥也听得入神，可她一讲完，他就站起身来，郑重地说：「弟兄们，姊妹们，玛丽妹子从前是个不得了的罪人。她把好些男人送进了地狱。她的魂灵兴许干净了，可她的身子该再受一回洗，然后我们才好把她接进天门教堂。」

===Original===
Brer Dee’s words stung. The blood burned Mary’s face. A flutter of whispers ran through the room. Andrew rose and cleared his throat. A frown was on his face, and his rumbling voice was deep as he said that Brer Dee was the head deacon, and besides, Brer Dee was a wise man. But Brer Dee was sometimes so anxious to safeguard the church that it made him fall into mistakes. Andrew had never heard of anybody who was baptized more than once. No matter how much they sinned or how many times they fell, one baptizing was enough.

He sat down, but Brer Dee got up and held his ground. “You ain’ never hear-ed of no sinner wicked as Si May-e, Brer Andrew. Evybody in dis house tonight knows as good as me, if Gawd didn’t take Si May-e’s son to break ’em heart, ’e would be a sinnin right on till yet. I think ’e needs a new baptizin.”

Mary felt bewildered. Brer repeated what he said as if she had not understood him. “I say you needs to be baptized again, Si May-e.”

===Chinese===
迪老哥的话扎人。热血烧红了玛丽的脸。一阵窸窸窣窣的低语掠过全屋。安德鲁站起来，清了清嗓子。他皱着眉头，低沉的嗓音隆隆地说：迪老哥是首席执事，再说，迪老哥是个明智的人。可是迪老哥有时一心想保全教会，倒叫自己出了差错。安德鲁从没听说过有谁受洗受过两回的。不论人犯下多少罪、跌倒多少回，受一回洗就尽够了。

他坐下了，迪老哥却站起来，寸步不让。「你从来没听说过有哪个罪人坏得赶上玛丽妹子，安德鲁老哥。今晚这屋里的人个个都跟我一样清楚：要不是上帝收走玛丽妹子的儿子、好击碎她的心，她这会儿还得一个劲儿地罪下去呢。我看她得重新受一回洗。」

玛丽听糊涂了。迪老哥把话又说了一遍，像是她没听懂似的。「我说的是，你得再受一回洗，玛丽妹子。」

===Original===
Doll got up and said that Brer Dee was right, exactly right. Another baptizing would do well if it rid Mary of all the sin she had.

Mary’s earrings quivered in her ears for all the grudges she had ever felt against Doll rose and rankled in her heart for a bitter second. Then she glanced up and met Andrew’s eyes, and her hatred suddenly melted. Pity for poor short-necked, short-waisted, short-winded Doll took its place.

The deacons talked on and on, the members said what they thought. Most of them agreed that Brer Dee was right; Mary should be baptized again.

===Chinese===
多莉站起来说，迪老哥说得对，一点不差。再受一回洗倒也好，只要能把玛丽身上带的罪统统洗个干净。

玛丽耳上的耳环颤了颤——她对多莉积下的种种旧怨一时间全涌上心头，苦苦地刺痛了一霎。随后她抬眼正碰上安德鲁的目光，那股恨意忽然融化了，换成对可怜的多莉的怜悯——脖子短、腰身短、气也短的多莉。

执事们说了又说，教友们也把各人的想法说了。多数人赞成迪老哥的话：玛丽该再受一回洗。

===Original===
She stood waiting for the decision, listening, thinking, looking at Doll. Maybe, Doll was not to blame for being fat and mean and deceitful. No woman would be so heavy and ugly and common if she could help it. God made her what she was. He had fixed Doll so she knew nothing of pleasure or sorrow, and spent all her days on foolish tasteless half-baked things. Poor duck-legged Doll.

The discussion was done at last. Mary bowed her head meekly and said she was willing to be baptized again. She felt cool and steady; as she bowed she felt her earrings dangle. They were not downhearted, but gay and bold and shiny.

Meeting was over and the people came up to welcome Mary back into the fold. They shook her hand until it was numb, her arm ached with weariness, but her heart was warmed through with so much kindliness.

===Chinese===
她站着等候裁断，听着，想着，望着多莉。多莉生得又肥又刻薄又奸猾，这兴许怪不得她。哪个女人要是有法子，也不肯让自己这样臃肿、这样丑、这样俗气。是上帝把她造成这样。上帝把多莉造得不识欢乐、也不识忧愁，一辈子把日子全花在愚蠢无味、半生不熟的事情上。可怜的鸭子腿多莉。

议论总算到了头。玛丽温顺地低下头，说她愿意再受一回洗。她心里又清凉又安稳；低头的时候，她觉出耳环在耳边晃荡。它们一点也不垂头丧气，反倒欢欢喜喜、胆气十足、亮亮闪闪。

聚会散了，人们走上前来，欢迎玛丽重回羊圈。他们握她的手，握得她的手都麻了，胳膊也乏得发酸，可她的心被这许多善意暖了个透。

===Original===
Old Daddy Cudjoe came last, after most of the others had gone and only Andrew waited outside to see Mary home. He took Mary’s hand and shook it, then he cut his eyes all around to be certain Maum Hannah could not hear him when he whispered:

“If you gwine to quit wid mens now, Si May-e, do gi’ me you conjure rag. ’E’s de best charm I ever made.”

Mary looked straight into his eyes and smiled as she shook her head.

“I’ll lend ’em to you when you need em, Daddy, but I couldn’ gi’ way my love-charm. ’E’s all I got now to keep me young.”

===Chinese===
库乔老爹来得最末，那时旁的人差不多走光了，只剩安德鲁在外头等着送玛丽回家。他握住玛丽的手摇了摇，然后眼珠子朝四下里溜了一圈，确准汉纳婆婆听不见了，才低声说：

「要是你这会儿要跟男人们断干净了，玛丽妹子，就把你的符咒布包给我吧。那是我做过的最好的符咒。」

玛丽直直望进他的眼睛，一边摇头一边笑了。

「你要用的时候，我借给你，老爹，可我不能把我的爱情符咒白白送人。它是我如今用来保住年轻的全部家当了。」
