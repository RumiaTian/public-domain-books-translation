## XXIV / 第二十四章

===Original===

“Lawd, we had a time!” Mary said to Maum Hannah when all was over. Then she looked at her two newborn sons and said gently, “Dat picture o’ Hell scared me so bad I couldn’ wait for de moon to change, enty? An’ de pain hit me so hard, I couldn’ pray not to save my life! No, Jedus!”

Maum Hannah did not go to bed, but sat by the fire and smoked. She never slept in any bed but her own. Whenever she stayed from home at night she sat by the fire and nodded and smoked without even taking off her shoes.

Mary and the newborn babies were in the big room bed. Mary was too tired even to talk to Seraphine who had unexpectedly come to the landing on the boat that afternoon but could not get home until after the storm. Maum Hannah was mortified for the girl to find Mary like this. She had been away with fine town people trying to make something of herself, while her mother lived in sin. Mary ought to quit her ways. She had a daughter almost grown now. Maum Hannah talked until the storm had passed and the night was still. She sat close to the hearth, for the rain had chilled the air and the flickering fire gave out a grateful warmth. Mary was glad to see her rest, for hurrying around so fast must have made her tired. She smoked a pipeful and laid her pipe on the hearth and settled herself with folded arms to sleep a little. She had hardly dozed when something roused Mary. She opened her eyes and peered through the dim light at her two babies. They were sound asleep. They did not move the slightest bit. Yet she heard a baby’s faint cry. She called Maum Hannah, who hurried to the bed and leaned over and looked. Both the babies were asleep. Sound asleep. But she certainly had heard a baby’s voice. One had waked her.

「主啊，咱们这一场可真够呛！」一切都过去之后，玛丽对汉纳婆婆说。随后她望着自己两个新添的儿子，温声说道：「那张地狱图把我吓得那么厉害，我等不得月亮变了，对吧？疼又来得那么凶，疼得我连祷告都念不下去，命都快顾不上了！不，耶稣！」

汉纳婆婆没上床去睡，只坐在火边抽烟。她除了自家的床，从不睡别人的床。夜里只要在外头过夜，她就那么坐在火边，点头打盹儿、抽烟，连鞋也不脱。

玛丽和刚出生的娃娃们躺在大屋的床上。玛丽累得连话都懒得说，就连塞拉芬也顾不上搭理——那天下午塞拉芬出人意料地坐船到了船埠，却直到暴风雨过去才进得了家门。让这姑娘撞见玛丽这副模样，汉纳婆婆臊得慌。她一直在外头跟着城里的体面人做事，想挣出个人样来，她的娘却活在罪里。玛丽真该收了她的那一套了。她如今都已有个快长成大姑娘的女儿了。汉纳婆婆絮絮叨叨，一直说到暴风雨过去、夜色静定。她紧挨着炉床坐着，因为雨水把空气浸得寒浸浸的，跳动的火苗送出一股暖人的热。玛丽乐得看她歇着，东奔西跑忙得那么急，准把她累乏了。她抽完一烟斗，把烟斗搁在炉床上，交叠起胳膊安顿好，想眯一小会儿。她才打上盹儿，就有什么把玛丽惊醒了。她睁开眼，在昏暗的光里细细看她的两个娃娃。他们睡得正香，一动也不动。可她分明听见一声婴儿微弱的啼哭。她唤汉纳婆婆，婆婆赶紧走到床边，俯身瞧了瞧。两个娃娃都睡着。睡得死死的。可她方才确实听见了婴儿的声音。有一个把她哭醒了。

===Original===

A dream maybe.

Maum Hannah laughed and went back to her chair by the fire. Mary was getting foolish. Waking herself up with dreams.

Maum Hannah sighed, then turned in her chair suddenly. She heard a baby too and she was not dreaming. She hobbled to the bed. No sound came from there. What was it she heard? Where did that cry come from? Not from Mary’s boys. Both of them slept. What could it be?

The room was almost dark for the fire burned low. Maum Hannah said she felt nervish. A ghost must be walking around under the house. Mary was too brazen. Something was bound to happen to her. Maybe a plat-eye was outside; screech owls and whippoorwills had both been crying. She had put the shovel in the fire to stop them.

She added a light wood knot to the fire and a blaze flared up bright. At the same time a feeble wail sounded. Mary was sure it was in the room, but Maum Hannah waited to hear it again. Maybe a little goat had gone under the house out of the storm. There it was again. What was it? The old woman went to the corner; the sound seemed there, behind the organ. She didn’t feel comfortable close to that organ. Why did Mary have it? Organs were tools of the devil. The idea of Reverend Duncan wanting one in the church.

Could that sound like a baby crying come out of the organ? Satan might be playing it. Mary had talked a lot of impudent talk lately. It made her uneasy.

兴许是个梦吧。

汉纳婆婆笑了笑，回到火边自己的椅子上。玛丽渐渐犯糊涂了。做个梦就把自己惊醒。

汉纳婆婆叹了口气，忽然在椅子里一转身。她也听见了婴儿声，而且她可不是在做梦。她挪着步子一瘸一拐地到床边去。那边没有一点声响。她听见的是什么？那哭声是从哪儿来的？不是从玛丽的两个小子那儿来的。两个都睡着。那能是什么呢？

炉火烧得矮了，屋里差不多黑了。汉纳婆婆说她心里直发毛。准是有鬼在屋子底下打转。玛丽脸皮太厚了。她是注定要出事的。没准儿外头有个普拉特眼（plat-eye）妖；鸣角鸮和三声夜鹰都啼叫过了。她已把火铲插进火里，好止住它们的啼声。

她往火里添了一个引火松木疙瘩，火苗腾地蹿起来，亮晃晃的。就在这当口，一声细弱的啼哭响了起来。玛丽拿准那声音就在屋里，汉纳婆婆却还要等它再响一回。兴许是只小山羊躲暴风雨钻进了屋子底下。又来了。那是什么？老太太走到屋角去；声音像是在那儿，风琴后头。挨那架风琴近了，她浑身不自在。玛丽干吗要这物件？风琴是魔鬼的家什。邓肯牧师竟异想天开，要在教堂里摆上一架。

这种像婴儿啼哭的声音，能从风琴里出来吗？没准儿是撒旦在弹它。玛丽近来放肆话说得太多了。这叫她心里不安生。

Mary lay back down, and Maum Hannah covered her up with the three babies! Mary never had seen three babies but once before, and one of those was a little runty thing. All three of these were fine big children.

“Auntie, go in de shed room an’ see if Seraphine is wake,” Mary whispered.

“Lawd﻿—I been forgot bout em,” Maum Hannah said with a wise smile.

She opened the shed-room door. It was too dark to see much, but she called, “Is yunnuh all sleep in here?”
