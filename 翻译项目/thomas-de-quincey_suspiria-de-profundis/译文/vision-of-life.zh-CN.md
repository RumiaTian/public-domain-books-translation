## Vision of Life / 生命的幻象

===Original===
Upon me, as upon others scattered thinly by tens and twenties over every thousand years, fell too powerfully and too early the vision of life. The horror of life mixed itself already in earliest youth with the heavenly sweetness of life; that grief which one in a hundred has sensibility enough to gather from the sad retrospect of life in its closing stage for *me* shed its dews as a prelibation upon the fountains of life whilst yet sparkling to the morning sun. I saw from afar and from before what I was to see from behind. Is this the description of an early youth passed in the shades of gloom? No; but of a youth passed in the divinest happiness. And, if the reader has (which so few have) the passion without which there is no reading of the legend and superscription upon man’s brow, if he is not (as most are) deafer than the grave to every *deep* note that sighs upwards from the Delphic caves of human life, he will know that the rapture of life (or anything which by approach can merit that name) does not arise, unless as perfect music arises, music of Mozart or Beethoven, by the confluence of the mighty and terrific discords with the subtle concords. Not by contrast, or as reciprocal foils, do these elements act—which is the feeble conception of many—but by union. They are the sexual forces in music: “male and female created he them”; and these mighty antagonists do not put forth their hostilities by repulsion, but by deepest attraction.

===Chinese===
同每一千年间仅以十数、二十数之数零星散布于世的少数人一样，生命的幻象（the vision of life）也降临于我——降临得过于强猛，也过于早了。生命的恐怖，早在稚龄之初，便已与生命那属天的甘甜相互掺杂；那种悲怆——一百人之中仅有一人具备足够的敏感，能从生命垂暮之境的凄然回望里将它采撷到手——之于*我*，却早在生命之泉还迎着朝阳熠熠闪烁的时节，便已把它的露滴当作先尝的祭酒（prelibation）洒落泉上。凡我日后须自后方回望方能得见的种种，我从远方、自前方，便已预先看见。这是在描述一段于阴翳幽暗之中度过的早年么？不是；它描述的，是一段在最神圣的幸福之中度过的青春。而读者倘具有那份激情（具此者何其稀少）——没有它，便无从读出人类额上的铭文与题字；倘他不似大多数人那样，比坟墓更其沉聋，听不见从人类生活的德尔斐洞窟（Delphic caves）中向上叹息的每一个*深沉*的音符——他便会懂得：生命的极乐（或任何凭其近似而差可配得上这名称的东西）之得以生发，恰如完美的音乐之得以生发——莫扎特（Mozart）或贝多芬（Beethoven）的音乐——必赖雄浑骇人的不协和音与精微的和协音二者之交汇。这些元素并非借对比、或作为相互的陪衬而发生作用——那是许多人的浅薄之见——而是借合一。它们正是音乐中的两性之力：「有男有女，是他造了他们」；而这些强大的对峙者并不以排斥施展它们的敌意，而是以最深的吸引。

===Original===
As “in today already walks tomorrow,” so in the past experience of a youthful life may be seen dimly the future. The collisions with alien interests or hostile views of a child, boy, or very young man, so insulated as each of these is sure to be—those aspects of opposition which such a person *can* occupy—are limited by the exceedingly few and trivial lines of connection along which he is able to radiate any essential influence whatever upon the fortunes or happiness of others. Circumstances may magnify his importance for the moment; but, after all, any cable which he carries out upon other vessels is easily slipped upon a feud arising. Far otherwise is the state of relations connecting an adult or responsible man with the circles around him as life advances. The network of these relations is a thousand times more intricate, the jarring of these intricate relations a thousand times more frequent, and the vibrations a thousand times harsher which these jarrings diffuse. This truth is felt beforehand, misgivingly and in troubled vision, by a young man who stands upon the threshold of manhood. One earliest instinct of fear and horror would darken his spirit if it could be revealed to itself and self-questioned at the moment of birth: a second instinct of the same nature would again pollute that tremulous mirror if the moment were as punctually marked as physical birth is marked which dismisses him finally upon the tides of absolute self-control. A dark ocean would seem the total expanse of life from the first; but far darker and more appalling would seem that inferior and second chamber of the ocean which called him away forever from the direct accountability of others. Dreadful would be the morning which should say, “Be thou a human child incarnate”; but more dreadful the morning which should say, “Bear thou henceforth the sceptre of thy self-dominion through life, and the passion of life!” Yes, dreadful would be both; but without a basis of the dreadful there is no perfect rapture. It is in part through the sorrow of life, growing out of dark events, that this basis of awe and solemn darkness slowly accumulates. *That* I have illustrated. But, as life expands, it is more through the *strife* which besets us, strife from conflicting opinions, positions, passions, interests, that the funereal ground settles and deposits itself which sends upward the dark lustrous brilliancy through the jewel of life, else revealing a pale and superficial glitter. Either the human being must suffer and struggle, as the price of a more searching vision, or his gaze must be shallow and without intellectual revelation.

===Chinese===
正如「明日已行走于今日之中」，在青春生涯的既往经验之中，也可朦胧窥见未来。孩童、少年或极年轻的男子，与异己的利害或敌对的观点之间所发生的碰撞——此三者中的每一个都注定是如此隔绝的——其*所能*占据的对抗形态，实为大受局限：他赖以向他人的际遇或幸福辐射任何实质影响的联结线路，稀少而微末之至。境遇或许会一时夸大他的重要性；但归根结底，他系向他船的任何缆索，一旦衅端骤起，都极易被解开脱弃。及至生命向前推进，一个成年人或身负重责的人与其周身诸圈层相维系的关系状态，便全然是另一番光景。这张关系之网的繁复要胜过千倍，这些繁复关系之间的龃龉要频繁千倍，而这些龃龉所扩散开的震颤，也要刺耳千倍。伫立于成年门槛上的年轻人，对此真理早有预感——怀着不祥的疑虑，在纷乱的视象之中预先感到。有一种最原初的恐惧与惊骇之本能，倘若在降生之顷它得向自身显露、得自审自问，便会使其灵魂蒙上阴影；而同类的第二种本能，倘若那个时刻也如肉身的降生一般被精确地标出——那便是将他最终遣送上绝对自制之潮流的时刻——也将再度玷污那面颤动的明镜。从一开始，一片黑暗的海洋便仿佛是生命的全部幅员；而那海洋更深一层的第二重内室——正是它召唤他永远离去，不复直接受他人的问责——便显得更暗、也更可怖。那宣告「愿你降生为一个血肉的人间孩童」的清晨，已然可怖；而那宣告「愿你自今而后，终生荷负你自我主宰的权杖，并荷负生命的激情！」的清晨，则更为可怖。是的，二者皆可怖；但没有可怖者作根基，便没有完美的极乐。这份敬畏与肃穆之暗的根基，一部分正是经由生命的悲怆——从幽暗的事变中生长出来的悲怆——缓缓积聚而成。*这一点*，我已有所阐发。然而，随着生命的展开，更多地倒是经由那困扰着我们的*纷争*——意见、立场、激情、利害彼此冲突的纷争——那葬仪般的地层方始沉降、淀积，也正是它透过生命这块宝石，向上透出幽暗而莹泽的光华；若无它，宝石所显露的便只是苍白而浮浅的闪光。人要么必须受苦、挣扎，以此为代价换取一种更深透的视界；要么，他的目光便必得肤浅，而无从获得智性的启示。
