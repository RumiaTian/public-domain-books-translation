## V The Family / 五 家人

===Original===
My grandfather, Maco, had been our chief. I never saw him, but my father often told me of the great size, strength, and sagacity of this old warrior. Their principal wars had been with the Mexicans. They had some wars with other tribes of Indians also, but were seldom at peace for any great length of time with the Mexican towns.

===Chinese===
我的祖父马科（Maco）曾是我们部落的酋长。我从未见过他，但父亲常对我讲起这位老武士魁伟的身躯、过人的膂力和睿智。他们主要的战事都是同墨西哥人打的。他们同其他印第安部落也有些战争，而同墨西哥人的城镇，却难得有太太平平的长久日子。

===Original===
Maco died when my father was but a young warrior, and Mangus-Colorado9 became chief of the Bedonkohe Apaches. When I was but a small boy my father died, after having been sick for some time. When he passed away, carefully the watchers closed his eyes, then they arrayed him in his best clothes, painted his face afresh, wrapped a rich blanket around him, saddled his favorite horse, bore his arms in front of him, and led his horse behind, repeating in wailing tones his deeds of valor as they carried his body to a cave in the mountain. Then they slew his horses, and we gave away all of his other property,10 as was customary in our tribe, after which his body was deposited in the cave, his arms beside him. His grave is hidden by piles of stone. Wrapped in splendor he lies in seclusion, and the winds in the pines sing a low requiem over the dead warrior.

===Chinese===
父亲还只是个年轻武士时，马科去世了，曼加斯-科罗拉多（Mangus-Colorado）9 继任贝东科黑（Bedonkohe）阿帕奇人的酋长。我还年幼时，父亲病了些时日，便去世了。他咽气后，守灵的人轻轻合上他的双眼，为他穿上最好的衣裳，重新绘饰了他的脸，用一床华美的毯子裹住他，给他心爱的马备上鞍鞯，把他的兵器捧在他身前，牵着他的马跟在后面，一路用哀恸的调子诵念着他的英勇事迹，把他的遗体抬进山中的一个洞穴。然后他们宰死了他的马，我们把他其余的财物尽数散给了别人，10 这是部落的习俗；随后他的遗体安放进洞穴，兵器放在他身旁。他的坟茔由石堆掩藏。他裹着华彩，长眠幽处，松间的风为这位亡故的武士低唱着安魂曲。

===Original===
After my father’s death I assumed the care of my mother. She never married again, although according to the customs of our tribe she might have done so immediately after his death. Usually, however, the widow who has children remains single after her husband’s death for two or three years; but the widow without children marries again immediately. After a warrior’s death his widow returns to her people and may be given away or sold by her father or brothers. My mother chose to live with me, and she never desired to marry again. We lived near our old home and I supported her.

===Chinese===
父亲死后，我担起了奉养母亲的责任。她没有再嫁，尽管按我们部落的习俗，她在父亲死后本可立即改嫁。不过一般说来，有子女的寡妇在丈夫死后独身两三年；没有子女的寡妇则立即再嫁。武士死后，他的寡妻回到娘家，可由她的父亲或兄弟把她许配或卖掉。我的母亲选择同我一起生活，从没有再嫁的念头。我们住在老屋近旁，由我奉养她。

===Original===
In 1846, being seventeen years of age, I was admitted to the council of the warriors. Then I was very happy, for I could go wherever I wanted and do whatever I liked. I had not been under the control of any individual, but the customs of our tribe prohibited me from sharing the glories of the warpath until the council admitted me. When opportunity offered, after this, I could go on the warpath with my tribe. This would be glorious. I hoped soon to serve my people in battle. I had long desired to fight with our warriors.

===Chinese===
1846 年，我十七岁，被接纳进武士的议事会。那时我满心欢喜，因为从此我想去哪里就去哪里，想做什么就做什么。我本就不受任何个人的辖制，但部落的习俗不许我分享出征的光荣，直到议事会接纳我。此后一有机会，我就能随本族出征了。这将无比荣耀。我盼望早日为族人在战场上效力。我早就渴望同我们的武士一起并肩作战。

===Original===
Perhaps the greatest joy to me was that now I could marry the fair Alope, daughter of No-po-so. She was a slender, delicate girl, but we had been lovers for a long time. So, as soon as the council granted me these privileges I went to see her father concerning our marriage. Perhaps our love was of no interest to him; perhaps he wanted to keep Alope with him, for she was a dutiful daughter; at any rate he asked many ponies for her. I made no reply, but in a few days appeared before his wigwam with the herd of ponies and took with me Alope. This was all the marriage ceremony necessary in our tribe.

===Chinese===
而对我来说，最大的快乐或许在于：如今我可以娶美丽的阿洛佩（Alope）了，她是诺波索（No-po-so）的女儿。她是个苗条娇弱的姑娘，但我们相爱已久。于是，议事会一授予我这些权利，我便去见她的父亲，商议我们的婚事。也许我们的爱情在他看来无足轻重；也许他想把阿洛佩留在身边，因为她是个孝顺的女儿；总之，他索要了许多匹矮种马。我没有答话，过了几天，赶着一群矮种马来到他的棚屋前，带走了阿洛佩。在我们部落，这就是全部必要的婚礼。

===Original===
Not far from my mother’s teepee I had made for us a new home. The teepee was made of buffalo hides and in it were many bear robes, lion hides, and other trophies of the chase, as well as my spears, bows, and arrows. Alope had made many little decorations of beads11 and drawn work on buckskin, which she placed in our teepee. She also drew many pictures on the walls of our home. She was a good wife, but she was never strong. We followed the traditions of our fathers and were happy. Three children came to us﻿—children that played, loitered, and worked as I had done.

===Chinese===
在离母亲梯皮帐篷（teepee）不远的地方，我为我们筑了一个新家。帐篷用野牛皮制成，里面有许多熊皮褥子、狮皮，还有别的狩猎纪念物，以及我的矛、弓和箭。阿洛佩做了许多珠饰11 和鹿皮抽纱的小饰物，摆在我们的帐篷里。她还在我们家的壁上画了许多画。她是个好妻子，只是身子一直单薄。我们遵行父辈的传统，日子过得幸福。我们有了三个孩子——他们像当年的我一样玩耍、闲逛、劳作。
