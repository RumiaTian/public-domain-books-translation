## II Subdivisions of the Apache Tribe / 二 阿帕奇部落的分支

===Original===
The Apache Indians are divided into six sub-tribes. To one of these, the Be-don-ko-he, I belong.

===Chinese===
阿帕奇印第安人分为六个分支部落。我属于其中的一支——贝东科黑（Be-don-ko-he）。

===Original===
Our tribe inhabited that region of mountainous country which lies west from the east line of Arizona, and south from the headwaters of the Gila River.

===Chinese===
我们部落居住的那片山区，在亚利桑那（Arizona）东界以西，希拉河（Gila River）源头以南。

===Original===
East of us lived the Chi-hen-ne (Ojo Caliente), (Hot Springs) Apaches. Our tribe never had any difficulty with them. Victoria, their chief, was always a friend to me. He always helped our tribe when we asked him for help. He lost his life in the defense of the rights of his people. He was a good man and a brave warrior. His son Charlie now lives here in this reservation with us.

===Chinese===
在我们以东，住着奇亨内（Chi-hen-ne，即温泉阿帕奇）人。我们部落同他们从无纠纷。他们的酋长维多利亚（Victoria）一直是我的朋友。我们部落请他相助时，他总是出手。他在保卫族人权利的战斗中失去了性命。他是个好人，一个勇敢的武士。他的儿子查理（Charlie）如今同我们一起住在这片保留地上。

===Original===
North of us lived the White Mountain Apaches. They were not always on the best of terms with our tribe, yet we seldom had any war with them. I knew their chief, Hash-ka-ai-la, personally, and I considered him a good warrior. Their range was next to that of the Navajo Indians, who were not of the same blood as the Apaches. We held councils with all Apache tribes, but never with the Navajo Indians. However, we traded with them and sometimes visited them.

===Chinese===
在我们以北，住着白山阿帕奇（White Mountain Apaches）人。他们同我们部落的关系并不总是最融洽，但我们之间很少动干戈。我同他们的酋长哈什卡艾拉（Hash-ka-ai-la）本人相识，我认为他是个好武士。他们的活动区域紧邻纳瓦霍印第安人（Navajo），而纳瓦霍人与阿帕奇人并非同一种血统。我们同所有阿帕奇部落都开议事会，同纳瓦霍人却从不。不过我们同他们做买卖，有时也互相走访。

===Original===
To the west of our country ranged the Chi-e-a-hen Apaches. They had two chiefs within my time, Co-si-to and Co-da-hoo-yah. They were friendly, but not intimate with our tribe.

===Chinese===
在我们国土的西面，活动着奇埃阿亨（Chi-e-a-hen）阿帕奇人。在我的年代里，他们有过两位酋长：科西托（Co-si-to）和科达胡亚（Co-da-hoo-yah）。他们对我们部落友好，但并不亲密。

===Original===
South of us lived the Cho-kon-en (Chiricahua) Apaches, whose chief in the old days was Cochise, and later his son, Naiche. This tribe was always on the most friendly terms with us. We were often in camp and on the trail together. Naiche, who was my companion in arms, is now my companion in bondage.

===Chinese===
在我们以南，住着乔科嫩（Cho-kon-en，即奇里卡瓦）阿帕奇人，从前的酋长是柯奇斯（Cochise），后来是他的儿子纳切（Naiche）。这个部落同我们的关系一向最为亲密。我们常常一同扎营，一同上路。纳切曾是我并肩作战的伙伴，如今是我同受拘囚的伙伴。

===Original===
To the south and west of us lived the Nedni Apaches. Their chief was Whoa, called by the Mexicans Capitan Whoa. They were our firm friends. The land of this tribe lies partly in Old Mexico and partly in Arizona.3 Whoa and I often camped and fought side by side as brothers. My enemies were his enemies, my friends his friends. He is dead now, but his son Asa is interpreting this story for me.

===Chinese===
在我们的西南面，住着内德尼（Nedni）阿帕奇人。他们的酋长是沃阿（Whoa），墨西哥人称他沃阿上尉（Capitan Whoa）。他们是我们坚定的朋友。这个部落的土地一部分在老墨西哥（Old Mexico），一部分在亚利桑那。3 沃阿和我常常像兄弟一样并肩扎营、并肩作战。我的敌人就是他的敌人，我的朋友就是他的朋友。他如今已经故去，他的儿子阿萨（Asa）正在为我口译这个故事。

===Original===
Still the four tribes (Bedonkohe, Chokonen, Chihenne, and Nedni), who were fast friends in the days of freedom, cling together as they decrease in number. Only the destruction of all our people would dissolve our bonds of friendship.

===Chinese===
当年在自由岁月里是挚友的这四个部落——贝东科黑、乔科嫩、奇亨内和内德尼——如今人丁日减，仍旧紧紧相依。唯有我们全体人民的毁灭，才能解开我们友谊的纽带。

===Original===
We are vanishing from the earth, yet I cannot think we are useless or Usen would not have created us. He created all tribes of men and certainly had a righteous purpose in creating each.

===Chinese===
我们正在从大地上消逝，然而我不能认为我们是无用之民，否则乌森（Usen）就不会创造我们。他创造了人间的所有部落，创造每一个部落，必定都有他正直的用意。

===Original===
For each tribe of men Usen created He also made a home. In the land created for any particular tribe He placed whatever would be best for the welfare of that tribe.

===Chinese===
乌森创造的每一个部落，他都同时为之安了一个家园。在为某个部落造出的土地上，他放置了最有利于那个部落福祉的一切。

===Original===
When Usen created the Apaches He also created their homes in the West. He gave to them such grain, fruits, and game as they needed to eat. To restore their health when disease attacked them He made many different herbs to grow. He taught them where to find these herbs, and how to prepare them for medicine. He gave them a pleasant climate and all they needed for clothing and shelter was at hand.

===Chinese===
乌森创造阿帕奇人时，也在西方创造了他们的家园。他赐给他们所需的谷物、果物和猎物，供他们食用。为了在疾病袭来时使他们恢复健康，他让许多种不同的药草生长。他教他们在哪里找到这些药草，教他们怎样炮制入药。他赐给他们宜人的气候，他们穿衣蔽体、安身栖身所需的一切，都近在手边。

===Original===
Thus it was in the beginning: the Apaches and their homes each created for the other by Usen himself. When they are taken from these homes they sicken and die. How long4 will it be until it is said, there are no Apaches?

===Chinese===
起初就是这样：阿帕奇人与他们的家园，是乌森亲手为彼此创造的。一旦被人从这些家园里带走，他们便害病，便死去。还要多久4，人们就会说，世上再没有阿帕奇人了？
