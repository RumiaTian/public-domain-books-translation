## VIII Raids That Were Successful / 八 得手的袭掠

===Original===
In the summer of 1862 I took eight men and invaded Mexican territory. We went south on the west side of the Sierra Madre Mountains for five days; then in the night crossed over to the southern part of the Sierra de Sahuaripa range. Here we again camped to watch for pack trains. About ten o’clock next morning four drivers, mounted, came past our camp with a pack-mule train. As soon as they saw us they rode for their lives, leaving us the booty. This was a long train, and packed with blankets, calico, saddles, tinware, and loaf sugar. We hurried home as fast as we could with these provisions, and on our return while passing through a canyon in the Santa Catilina range of mountains in Arizona, met a white man driving a mule pack train. When we first saw him he had already seen us, and was riding at full tilt up the canyon. We examined his train and found that his mules were all loaded with cheese. We put them in with the other train and resumed our journey. We did not attempt to trail the driver and I am sure he did not try to follow us.

===Chinese===
1862 年夏天，我带八个人侵入墨西哥境内。我们沿马德雷山脉（Sierra Madre Mountains）西麓南行五天，然后趁夜翻到萨瓦里帕山脉（Sierra de Sahuaripa）的南段。我们在这里又扎下营，守候驮队。第二天上午十点来钟，四个赶骡人骑着马，赶着一队骡驮子经过我们营地。他们一看见我们，便没命地打马逃了，把辎重丢给了我们。这驮队很长，满载着毯子、印花布、马鞍、洋铁器皿和方糖。我们带着这批物资拼命往家赶；归途穿过亚利桑那（Arizona）圣卡蒂利纳山脉（Santa Catilina）的一道峡谷时，又遇上一个白人赶着一队骡驮子。我们刚看见他，他也早已看见我们，正没命地往峡谷上方逃。我们查看他的驮队，发现骡子驮的全是干酪。我们把这些骡子并进先前那队，继续赶路。我们没有去追那赶骡人，我敢说他也绝不敢来追我们。

===Original===
In two days we arrived at home. Then Mangus-Colorado, our chief, assembled the tribe. We gave a feast, divided the spoils, and danced all night. Some of the pack mules were killed and eaten.

===Chinese===
两天后我们到了家。我们的酋长曼加斯-科罗拉多（Mangus-Colorado）随即召集全族。我们设宴庆功，分了战利品，跳了一整夜的舞。有几匹驮骡宰了吃肉。

===Original===
This time after our return we kept out scouts so that we would know if Mexican troops should attempt to follow us.

===Chinese===
这一回回来以后，我们派出了侦察兵，墨西哥部队若想追来，我们就能察觉。

===Original===
On the third day our scouts came into camp and reported Mexican cavalry dismounted and approaching our settlement. All our warriors were in camp. Mangus-Colorado took command of one division and I of the other. We hoped to get possession of their horses, then surround the troops in the mountains, and destroy the whole company. This we were unable to do, for they, too, had scouts. However, within four hours after we started we had killed ten troopers with the loss of only one man, and the Mexican cavalry was in full retreat, followed by thirty armed Apaches, who gave them no rest until they were far inside the Mexican country. No more troops came that winter.

===Chinese===
第三天，侦察兵回营报告：墨西哥骑兵下马步行，正向我们居地逼近。我们的武士都在营中。曼加斯-科罗拉多统领一路，我统领另一路。我们本想先夺下他们的马，再在山里把这队人马围而歼之。这一着没能实现，因为他们也放出了侦察兵。不过，出发后四个钟头之内，我们只折了一人，就杀了他们十名骑兵，墨西哥骑兵全线溃逃，三十名带械的阿帕奇人衔尾追击，一直追到他们退入墨西哥腹地深处才罢休。那年冬天，再没有部队来犯。

===Original===
For a long time we had plenty of provisions, plenty of blankets, and plenty of clothing. We also had plenty of cheese and sugar.

===Chinese===
此后好长一段日子，我们的粮食充裕，毯子充裕，衣裳也充裕。干酪和糖也尽有尽用。

===Original===
Another summer (1863) I selected three warriors and went on a raid into Mexico. We went south into Sonora, camping in the Sierra de Sahuaripa Mountains. About forty miles west of Casa Grande is a small village in the mountains, called by the Indians “Crassanas.” We camped near this place and concluded to make an attack. We had noticed that just at midday no one seemed to be stirring; so we planned to make our attack at the noon hour. The next day we stole into the town at noon. We had no guns, but were armed with spears and bows and arrows. When the war-whoop was given to open the attack the Mexicans fled in every direction; not one of them made any attempt to fight us.

===Chinese===
又一个夏天（1863 年），我挑了三名武士，再次袭入墨西哥。我们南下进入索诺拉（Sonora），在萨瓦里帕山里扎营。卡萨格兰德（Casa Grande）以西约四十英里的山里，有一座小村，印第安人管它叫「克拉桑纳斯」（Crassanas）。我们在村子附近扎营，决意动手。我们留意到正午时分村里似乎没有人走动，便定在晌午进攻。第二天中午，我们悄悄摸进了村。我们没有枪，兵器是长矛和弓箭。进攻的战嚎一响，墨西哥人四散奔逃；没有一个人想同我们交手。

===Original===
We shot some arrows at the retreating Mexicans, but killed only one. Soon all was silent in the town and no Mexicans could be seen.

===Chinese===
我们朝奔逃的墨西哥人射了几箭，只射死一个。不久，全村一片死寂，一个墨西哥人也看不见了。

===Original===
When we discovered that all the Mexicans were gone we looked through their houses and saw many curious things. These Mexicans kept many more kinds of property than the Apaches did. Many of the things we saw in the houses we could not understand, but in the stores we saw much that we wanted; so we drove in a herd of horses and mules, and packed as much provisions and supplies as we could on them. Then we formed these animals into a pack train and returned safely to Arizona. The Mexicans did not even trail us.

===Chinese===
等我们察觉墨西哥人跑光了，便进他们的屋子查看，见到许多新奇的物件。这些墨西哥人家的财物种类，比阿帕奇人的多得多。屋里许多东西我们看不明白，但店铺里有许多我们要的；于是我们赶拢一群马和骡子，把尽可能多的粮食杂货驮在它们背上，编成一支驮队，平安返回了亚利桑那。墨西哥人连我们的踪迹都没有追。

===Original===
When we arrived in camp we called the tribe together and feasted all day. We gave presents to everyone. That night the dance began, and it did not cease until noon the next day.

===Chinese===
回到营地，我们把全族召齐，欢宴了一整天。我们给每个人都送了礼。当夜舞会开场，一直跳到第二天晌午才停。

===Original===
This was perhaps the most successful raid ever made by us into Mexican territory. I do not know the value of the booty, but it was very great, for we had supplies enough to last our whole tribe for a year or more.

===Chinese===
这大概是我们历次深入墨西哥境内最得手的一次袭掠。那批掳获值多少钱，我说不上，但一定很多，因为那些物资够我们全族吃用一年还有余。

===Original===
In the fall of 1864 twenty warriors were willing to go with me on another raid into Mexico. These were all chosen men, well armed and equipped for battle. As usual we provided for the safety of our families before starting on this raid. Our whole tribe scattered and then reassembled at a camp about forty miles from the former place. In this way it would be hard for the Mexicans to trail them and we would know where to find our families when we returned. Moreover, if any hostile Indians should see this large number of warriors leaving our range they might attack our camp, but if they found no one at the usual place their raid would fail.

===Chinese===
1864 年秋，有二十名武士愿意随我再去墨西哥袭掠一次。这都是精选的人，兵器齐全，装备停当。同往常一样，出发之前我们先安顿了家眷的安全。全族四散开去，再在离原营地约四十英里的地方重新聚齐。这样一来，墨西哥人便难以追踪他们，而我们回来时也知道去哪里找家人。再者，倘若有敌对的印第安人望见这么多武士离开我们的猎场，他们可能来犯我们的营地；但若他们扑到老地方一个人也找不到，这一趟也就白来了。

===Original===
We went south through the Chokonen Apaches’ range, entered Sonora, Mexico, at a point directly south of Tombstone, Arizona, and went into hiding in the Sierra de Antunez Mountains.

===Chinese===
我们南行穿过乔科嫩阿帕奇人的猎场，在亚利桑那墓碑镇（Tombstone）正南一处进入墨西哥索诺拉，随后隐进安图内斯山脉（Sierra de Antunez）里藏身。

===Original===
We attacked several settlements in the neighborhood and secured plenty of provisions and supplies. After about three days we attacked and captured a mule pack train at a place called by the Indians “Pontoco.” It is situated in the mountains due west, about one day’s journey19 from Arispe.

===Chinese===
我们袭击了附近几处聚落，掳获了大量粮食物资。约莫三天后，我们在一处印第安人唤作「蓬托科」（Pontoco）的地方，袭击并夺下了一支骡驮队。那地方在正西的山里，离阿里斯佩（Arispe）约一天的路程。19

===Original===
There were three drivers with this train. One was killed and two escaped. The train was loaded with mescal,20 which was contained in bottles held in wicker baskets. As soon as we made camp the Indians began to get drunk and fight each other. I, too, drank enough mescal to feel the effect of it, but I was not drunk. I ordered the fighting stopped, but the order was disobeyed. Soon almost a general fight was in progress. I tried to place a guard out around our camp, but all were drunk and refused to serve. I expected an attack from Mexican troops at any moment, and really it was a serious matter for me, for being in command I would be held responsible for any ill luck attending the expedition. Finally the camp became comparatively still, for the Indians were too drunk to walk or even to fight. While they were in this stupor I poured out all the mescal, then I put out all the fires and moved the pack mules to a considerable distance from camp. After this I returned to camp to try to do something for the wounded. I found that only two were dangerously wounded. From the leg of one of these I cut an arrowhead, and from the shoulder of another I withdrew a spear point. When all the wounds had been cared for, I myself kept guard till morning. The next day we loaded our wounded on the pack mules and started for Arizona.

===Chinese===
这支驮队有三个赶骡人。一个被打死，两个逃了。驮子满载着梅斯卡尔酒（mescal），20 装在柳条筐里护着的瓶子里。我们刚一扎营，印第安人便喝起了酒，醉了，彼此斗殴起来。我也喝了些，喝到身上起了酒力，但没有醉。我下令住手，没有人听。不多时几乎演成一场全营混战。我想在营地四周布哨，可人人都醉了，谁也不肯当值。我时刻提防墨西哥部队来攻，这对我实在非同小可——我是统领，此行若有闪失，罪责都在我身上。最后营里总算安静下来，因为印第安人醉得走不动，连打架也不能够了。趁他们昏醉，我把梅斯卡尔酒统统倒掉，然后熄灭所有的火，把驮骡赶到离营地很远的地方。办完这些，我回到营里照料伤者。我发现只有两个伤得重。我从其中一人的腿里剜出一枚箭镞，又从另一人的肩头拔出一支矛尖。所有伤口都处置妥当后，我便亲自守夜到天明。第二天，我们把伤员驮上驮骡，动身回亚利桑那。

===Original===
The next day we captured come cattle from a herd and drove them home with us. But it was a very difficult matter to drive cattle when we were on foot. Caring for the wounded and keeping the cattle from escaping made our journey tedious. But we were not trailed, and arrived safely at home with all the booty.

===Chinese===
第二天，我们从一群牛里捉了些牛，赶着一同回家。可我们步行赶牛，实在吃力。又要照料伤员，又要看住牛不跑散，一路走得又慢又烦。但没有被人追蹑，我们带着全部掳获平安到了家。

===Original===
We then gave a feast and dance, and divided the spoils. After the dance we killed all the cattle and dried the meat. We dressed the hides and then the dried meat was packed in between these hides and stored away. All that winter we had plenty of meat. These were the first cattle we ever had. As usual we killed and ate some of the mules. We had little use for mules, and if we could not trade them for something of value, we killed them.

===Chinese===
随后我们设宴、跳舞，分了战利品。舞罢，我们把牛全部宰了，把肉晒干。牛皮鞣制好，干肉就夹在这些皮子中间包好收藏。那个冬天我们肉食充足。这是我们头一回拥有牛。同往常一样，有几匹骡子宰了吃掉。骡子对我们用处不大，若换不来值钱的东西，我们就宰了它。

===Original===
In the summer of 1865, with four warriors, I went again into Mexico. Heretofore we had gone on foot; we were accustomed to fight on foot; besides, we could more easily conceal ourselves when dismounted. But this time we wanted more cattle, and it was hard to drive them when we were on foot. We entered Sonora at a point southwest from Tombstone, Arizona, and followed the Sierra de Antunez Mountains to the southern limit, then crossed the country as far south as the mouth of Yaqui River. Here we saw a great lake21 extending beyond the limit of sight. Then we turned north, attacked several settlements, and secured plenty of supplies. When we had come back northwest of Arispe we secured about sixty head of cattle, and drove them to our homes in Arizona. We did not go directly home, but camped in different valleys with our cattle. We were not trailed. When we arrived at our camp the tribe was again assembled for feasting and dancing. Presents were given to everybody; then the cattle were killed and the meat dried and packed.

===Chinese===
1865 年夏天，我又带着四名武士进入墨西哥。此前我们都是步行前往；我们惯于步战；况且不骑马更容易藏身。但这一回我们想要更多的牛，步行赶牛太不容易。我们从亚利桑那墓碑镇的西南方向进入索诺拉，沿安图内斯山脉走到山系尽头，再横穿乡野，一直南到雅基河（Yaqui River）河口。在这里，我们望见一片大湖，21 湖面一望无际。然后我们折向北行，袭击了几处聚落，掳获了大量物资。回程走到阿里斯佩西北时，我们又得了约六十头牛，赶着它们回亚利桑那的家。我们没有直接回家，而是赶着牛在各处谷地里轮流扎营。没有人追踪我们。回到营地时，全族又聚齐设宴、跳舞。人人都有馈赠；随后牛都宰了，肉晒干装好。
