## I Origin of the Apache Indians / 一 阿帕奇印第安人的起源

===Original===
In the beginning the world was covered with darkness. There was no sun, no day. The perpetual night had no moon or stars.

===Chinese===
起初，世界笼罩在黑暗之中。没有太阳，没有白昼。永恒的黑夜里，没有月亮，也没有星辰。

===Original===
There were, however, all manner of beasts and birds. Among the beasts were many hideous, nameless monsters, as well as dragons, lions, tigers, wolves, foxes, beavers, rabbits, squirrels, rats, mice, and all manner of creeping things such as lizards and serpents. Mankind could not prosper under such conditions, for the beasts and serpents destroyed all human offspring.

===Chinese===
然而，世上已有各种各样的走兽和飞禽。走兽之中有许多狰狞的无名怪物，还有龙、狮、虎、狼、狐、河狸、兔、松鼠、大鼠、小鼠，以及蜥蜴和蛇之类的种种爬虫。在这样的境况下，人类无法繁衍生息，因为走兽和蛇会残害人类的一切后裔。

===Original===
All creatures had the power of speech and were gifted with reason.

===Chinese===
万物都有言语的能力，也都被赋予了理智。

===Original===
There were two tribes of creatures: the birds or the feathered tribe and the beasts. The former were organized under their chief, the eagle.

===Chinese===
世上的生灵分为两族：飞禽，即披羽的一族，与走兽。飞禽一族由他们的酋长——鹰——统领。

===Original===
These tribes often held councils, and the birds wanted light admitted. This the beasts repeatedly refused to do. Finally the birds made war against the beasts.

===Chinese===
这些部族时常召开议事会，飞禽们想让光明进入世界，走兽们却屡屡不肯。最后，飞禽向走兽开战。

===Original===
The beasts were armed with clubs, but the eagle had taught his tribe to use bows and arrows. The serpents were so wise that they could not all be killed. One took refuge in a perpendicular cliff of a mountain in Arizona, and his eye (changed into a brilliant stone) may be seen in that rock to this day. The bears, when killed, would each be changed into several other bears, so that the more bears the feathered tribe killed, the more there were. The dragon could not be killed, either, for he was covered with four coats of horny scales, and the arrows would not penetrate these. One of the most hideous, vile monsters (nameless) was proof against arrows, so the eagle flew high up in the air with a round, white stone, and let it fall on this monster’s head, killing him instantly. This was such a good service that the stone was called sacred. (A symbol of this stone is used in the tribal game of Kah.1) They fought for many days, but at last the birds won the victory.

===Chinese===
走兽们手持木棒作战，鹰却已教会本族使用弓箭。蛇极为狡黠，无法被斩尽杀绝。有一条蛇躲进了亚利桑那（Arizona）一座山的垂直崖壁之中，它的眼睛（已化作一颗璀璨的石头）至今仍可在那块岩石上看到。熊被杀死之后，每一只能变作好几只别的熊，于是披羽一族杀的熊越多，熊反倒越多。龙也无法被杀死，因为它周身覆盖着四层角质的鳞甲，箭射不透。还有一个最狰狞、最邪恶的无名怪物，箭也伤不了它；于是鹰衔着一块圆形的白石飞上高天，让石头落在这怪物头上，立时把它砸死了。这一功劳太大，那块石头从此被奉为圣石。（部落的赌戏卡戏（Kah）1 所用的图形，便是这石头的象征。）这一仗打了许多天，最终飞禽赢得了胜利。

===Original===
After this war was over, although some evil beasts remained, the birds were able to control the councils, and light was admitted. Then mankind could live and prosper. The eagle was chief in this good fight: therefore, his feathers were worn by man as emblems of wisdom, justice, and power.

===Chinese===
战争结束之后，虽然还残留着一些邪恶的走兽，飞禽已能掌控议事会，光明得以进入世界。人类从此能够生存繁衍。鹰是这场正义之战的首领，因此人类佩戴鹰羽，作为智慧、公正与权力的象征。

===Original===
Among the few human beings that were yet alive was a woman who had been blessed with many children, but these had always been destroyed by the beasts. If by any means she succeeded in eluding the others, the dragon, who was very wise and very evil, would come himself and eat her babes.

===Chinese===
在当时尚存的少数人类之中，有一个蒙受许多子女的女人，可这些孩子总是被走兽们害死。即便她想尽办法躲过了别的怪物，那条既狡诈又邪恶的龙也会亲自前来，吃掉她的婴孩。

===Original===
After many years a son of the rainstorm was born to her and she dug for him a deep cave. The entrance to this cave she closed and over the spot built a campfire. This concealed the babe’s hiding place and kept him warm. Every day she would remove the fire and descend into the cave, where the child’s bed was, to nurse him; then she would return and rebuild the campfire.

===Chinese===
许多年后，她生下了一个暴风雨之子，为他挖了一个深洞。她把洞口封住，在洞口上面生起篝火。这样既藏住了婴孩的藏身之处，又使他温暖。每天她移开火堆，下到洞里孩子睡卧的地方给他哺乳，然后再回到上面，重新生起篝火。

===Original===
Frequently the dragon would come and question her, but she would say, “I have no more children; you have eaten all of them.”

===Chinese===
龙常常前来盘问她，她总是说：「我再也没有孩子了；他们全被你吃掉了。」

===Original===
When the child was larger he would not always stay in the cave, for he sometimes wanted to run and play. Once the dragon saw his tracks. Now this perplexed and enraged the old dragon, for he could not find the hiding place of the boy; but he said that he would destroy the mother if she did not reveal the child’s hiding place. The poor mother was very much troubled; she could not give up her child, but she knew the power and cunning of the dragon, therefore she lived in constant fear.

===Chinese===
孩子长大些后，不再总待在洞里，因为他有时想跑动玩耍。有一次，龙看见了孩子的脚印。这下老龙又困惑又震怒，因为它找不到男孩藏身的地方；它便说，如果母亲不肯说出孩子的藏身之处，就要把她毁掉。可怜的母亲苦恼极了；她不能交出自己的孩子，可她深知龙的神通与狡诈，于是日夜生活在恐惧之中。

===Original===
Soon after this the boy said that he wished to go hunting. The mother would not give her consent. She told him of the dragon, the wolves, and the serpents; but he said, “Tomorrow I go.”

===Chinese===
此后不久，男孩说他想去打猎。母亲不肯应允。她把龙、狼和蛇的事情讲给他听；他却说：「明天我就去。」

===Original===
At the boy’s request his uncle (who was the only man then living) made a little bow and some arrows for him, and the two went hunting the next day. They trailed the deer far up the mountain and finally the boy killed a buck. His uncle showed him how to dress the deer and broil the meat. They broiled two hind quarters, one for the child and one for his uncle. When the meat was done they placed it on some bushes to cool. Just then the huge form of the dragon appeared. The child was not afraid, but his uncle was so dumb with fright that he did not speak or move.

===Chinese===
应男孩的请求，他的舅舅（当时世上唯一的男人）为他做了一张小弓和几支箭，第二天两人便去打猎。他们循着鹿的踪迹远远追上山去，最后男孩射死了一头公鹿。舅舅教他怎样剥制鹿肉、怎样炙烤。他们烤了两条后腿，一条给孩子，一条给舅舅。肉烤熟之后，他们把它搁在几丛灌木上晾凉。就在这时，龙的庞大身躯出现了。孩子并不害怕，他舅舅却吓得呆若木鸡，说不出话，也动弹不得。

===Original===
The dragon took the boy’s parcel of meat and went aside with it. He placed the meat on another bush and seated himself beside it. Then he said, “This is the child I have been seeking. Boy, you are nice and fat, so when I have eaten this venison I shall eat you.” The boy said, “No, you shall not eat me, and you shall not eat that meat.” So he walked over to where the dragon sat and took the meat back to his own seat. The dragon said, “I like your courage, but you are foolish; what do you think you could do?” “Well,” said the boy, “I can do enough to protect myself, as you may find out.” Then the dragon took the meat again, and then the boy retook it. Four times in all the dragon took the meat, and after the fourth time the boy replaced the meat he said, “Dragon, will you fight me?” The dragon said, “Yes, in whatever way you like.” The boy said, “I will stand one hundred paces distant from you and you may have four shots at me with your bow and arrows, provided that you will then exchange places with me and give me four shots.” “Good,” said the dragon. “Stand up.”

===Chinese===
龙拿走男孩那包肉，走到一旁。它把肉搁在另一丛灌木上，在旁边坐下。然后它说：「这正是我一直在寻找的孩子。小子，你又肥又嫩，等我吃完了这鹿肉，就吃你。」男孩说：「不，你不许吃我，也不许吃那肉。」说着他走到龙坐的地方，把肉拿回自己的座位。龙说：「我喜欢你的胆量，可你太愚蠢了；你以为自己能做什么？」「哼，」男孩说，「保护我自己还是绰绰有余的，你不妨试试。」于是龙又把肉拿走，男孩又把它夺了回来。龙一共夺了四次，第四次之后，男孩把肉放回原处，说道：「龙，你敢跟我比试吗？」龙说：「敢，随你挑什么方式。」男孩说：「我站在离你一百步远的地方，你用弓箭向我射四箭；条件是之后你我互换位置，也让我射四箭。」「好，」龙说，「站好吧。」

===Original===
Then the dragon took his bow, which was made of a large pine tree. He took four arrows from his quiver; they were made of young pine tree saplings, and each arrow was twenty feet in length. He took deliberate aim, but just as the arrow left the bow the boy made a peculiar sound and leaped into the air. Immediately the arrow was shivered into a thousand splinters, and the boy was seen standing on the top of a bright rainbow over the spot where the dragon’s aim had been directed. Soon the rainbow was gone and the boy was standing on the ground again. Four times this was repeated, then the boy said, “Dragon, stand here; it is my time to shoot.” The dragon said, “All right; your little arrows cannot pierce my first coat of horn, and I have three other coats﻿—shoot away.” The boy shot an arrow, striking the dragon just over the heart, and one coat of the great horny scales fell to the ground. The next shot another coat, and then another, and the dragon’s heart was exposed. Then the dragon trembled, but could not move. Before the fourth arrow was shot the boy said, “Uncle, you are dumb with fear; you have not moved; come here or the dragon will fall on you.” His uncle ran toward him. Then he sped the fourth arrow with true aim, and it pierced the dragon’s heart. With a tremendous roar the dragon rolled down the mountain side﻿—down four precipices into a canyon below.

===Chinese===
于是龙取过它的弓——那弓是用一棵大松树做的。它从箭囊里抽出四支箭；箭是用幼松树苗做的，每支足有二十英尺长。它仔细瞄准，可就在箭离弦的一刹那，男孩发出一声奇异的喊叫，腾身跃入空中。那支箭立刻碎裂成千百片木屑，只见男孩站在一道明亮的彩虹顶上，恰在龙所瞄准之处的上方。不久彩虹消散，男孩又落回地面。这样重复了四次，然后男孩说：「龙，站到这边来；该我射了。」龙说：「好啊；你的小箭连我第一层角甲都穿不透，我还有三层呢——尽管射吧。」男孩射出一箭，正中龙的心口上方，一大片角质鳞甲应声落地。第二箭又落下一层，接着又是一层，龙的心脏便暴露出来。这时龙浑身发抖，却动弹不得。射第四箭之前，男孩说：「舅舅，你吓傻了；你一直没动；快过来，不然龙倒下来会砸着你。」舅舅朝他跑来。于是他瞄准了射出第四箭，箭穿透了龙的心脏。龙发出一声震天的怒吼，从山坡上翻滚而下——跌过四道峭壁，落进下面的峡谷。

===Original===
Immediately storm clouds swept the mountains, lightning flashed, thunder rolled, and the rain poured. When the rainstorm had passed, far down in the canyon below, they could see fragments of the huge body of the dragon lying among the rocks, and the bones of this dragon may still be found there.

===Chinese===
顷刻间，风暴的乌云席卷群山，电光闪闪，雷声滚滚，大雨倾盆。暴风雨过后，在下面深深的峡谷里，他们能看见龙庞大的尸体碎块散落在岩石之间，这龙的骸骨至今仍可在那里找到。

===Original===
This boy’s name was Apache. Usen2 taught him how to prepare herbs for medicine, how to hunt, and how to fight. He was the first chief of the Indians and wore the eagle’s feathers as the sign of justice, wisdom, and power. To him, and to his people, as they were created, Usen gave homes in the land of the west.

===Chinese===
这个男孩名叫阿帕奇（Apache）。天神乌森（Usen）2 教会了他如何采药治病、如何狩猎、如何作战。他是印第安人的第一任酋长，佩戴鹰羽，作为公正、智慧与权力的标志。对于他，以及随他一同被创造的族人，乌森在西方的土地上赐下了家园。
