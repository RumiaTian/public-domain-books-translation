## XIX A Prisoner of War / 十九 战俘

===Original===
When I had given up to the Government they put me on the Southern Pacific Railroad and took me to San Antonio, Texas, and held me to be tried by their laws.

===Chinese===
我向政府投降之后，他们把我押上南太平洋铁路（Southern Pacific Railroad）的火车，解到得克萨斯（Texas）的圣安东尼奥（San Antonio），关押起来，要按他们的法律审判我。

===Original===
In forty days they took me from there to Fort Pickens (Pensacola), Florida. Here they put me to sawing up large logs. There were several other Apache warriors with me, and all of us had to work every day. For nearly two years we were kept at hard labor in this place and we did not see our families until May, 1887. This treatment was in direct violation of our treaty made at Skeleton Canyon.

===Chinese===
四十天后，他们把我从那里解往佛罗里达（Florida）的皮肯斯堡（Fort Pickens，彭萨科拉 Pensacola）。在那里，他们派我锯大圆木。还有几名阿帕奇（Apache）武士同我在一起，我们全都得天天做工。我们在这个地方服了近两年的苦役，直到 1887 年 5 月才见到家人。这样的待遇，直接违背了我们在骷髅峡谷（Skeleton Canyon）立下的条约。

===Original===
After this we were sent with our families to Vermont, Alabama, where we stayed five years and worked for the Government. We had no property, and I looked in vain for General Miles to send me to that land of which he had spoken; I longed in vain for the implements, house, and stock that General Miles had promised me.

===Chinese===
此后，我们连同家眷被送往亚拉巴马（Alabama）的弗蒙特（Vermont），在那里住了五年，为政府做工。我们一无所有；我指望迈尔斯将军（General Miles）把我送到他所说过的那片土地上去，指望落了空；我渴望他许给我的农具、房屋和牲畜，渴望也落了空。

===Original===
During this time one of my warriors, Fun, killed himself and his wife. Another one shot his wife and then shot himself. He fell dead, but the woman recovered and is still living.

===Chinese===
在这期间，我的一名武士芬（Fun）杀死了自己和妻子。另一名武士开枪打了妻子，又开枪打自己。他当场死了，那女人却活了下来，至今还活着。

===Original===
We were not healthy in this place, for the climate disagreed with us. So many of our people died that I consented to let one of my wives go to the Mescalero Agency in New Mexico to live. This separation is according to our custom equivalent to what the white people call “divorce,” and so she married again soon after she got to Mescalero. She also kept our two small children, which she had a right to do. The children, Lenna and Robbie, are still living at Mescalero, New Mexico. Lenna is married. I kept one wife, but she is dead now and I have only our daughter Eva with me. Since my separation from Lenna’s mother I have never had more than one wife at a time. Since the death of Eva’s mother I married another woman (December, 1905) but we could not live happily and separated. She went home to her people—that is an Apache divorce.

===Chinese===
在这个地方我们身子不健，因为气候与我们不合。族人死得太多，我便应允让我的一个妻子去新墨西哥（New Mexico）的梅斯卡莱罗事务署（Mescalero Agency）那边生活。按我们的习俗，这样的分离就相当于白人所说的「离婚」；所以她到梅斯卡莱罗不久便另嫁了。她把我们的两个年幼的孩子留在了自己身边，这是她的权利。这两个孩子，蕾娜（Lenna）和罗比（Robbie），如今仍住在新墨西哥的梅斯卡莱罗。蕾娜已经出嫁。我留下了一位妻子，可她如今也已去世，身边只剩我们的女儿伊娃（Eva）陪伴我。自从同蕾娜的母亲分开以来，我从未同时有过一个以上的妻子。伊娃的母亲去世后，我又娶了一个女人（1905 年 12 月），但我们过不到一处，便分开了。她回她族人那里去了——这就是阿帕奇式的离婚。

===Original===
Then,46 as now, Mr. George Wratton superintended the Indians. He has always had trouble with the Indians, because he has mistreated them. One day an Indian, while drunk, stabbed Mr. Wratton with a little knife. The officer in charge took the part of Mr. Wratton and the Indian was sent to prison.

===Chinese===
那时 46 也和现在一样，是乔治·拉顿（George Wratton）先生管辖印第安人。他同印第安人始终处不好，因为他虐待他们。有一天，一个印第安人喝醉了，用一把小刀捅了拉顿先生。负责的军官偏袒拉顿先生，那印第安人便被送进了监狱。

===Original===
When47 we first came to Fort Sill, Captain Scott was in charge, and he had houses built for us by the Government. We were also given, from the Government, cattle, hogs, turkeys and chickens. The Indians did not do much good with the hogs, because they did not understand how to care for them, and not many Indians even at the present time keep hogs. We did better with the turkeys and chickens, but with these we did not have as good luck as white men do. With the cattle we have done very well, indeed, and we like to raise them. We have a few horses also, and have had no bad luck with them.

===Chinese===
我们 47 初到西尔堡（Fort Sill）时，是斯科特上尉（Captain Scott）负责管理，他让政府为我们盖了房子。政府还给了我们牛、猪、火鸡和鸡。印第安人养猪没什么成效，因为他们不懂怎么照料猪，直到如今养猪的印第安人也不多。火鸡和鸡我们养得好些，可比起白人来，运气还是不如。养牛我们确实干得很好，也喜欢养牛。我们还有几匹马，养马也没出过什么岔子。

===Original===
In the matter of selling48 our stock and grain there has been much misunderstanding. The Indians understood that the cattle were to be sold and the money given to them, but instead part of the money is given to the Indians and part of it is placed in what the officers call the “Apache Fund.” We have had five different officers in charge of the Indians here and they have all ruled very much alike—not consulting the Apaches or even explaining to them. It may be that the Government ordered the officers in charge to put this cattle money into an Apache fund, for once I complained and told Lieutenant Purington49 that I intended to report to the Government that he had taken some of my part of the cattle money and put it into the Apache Fund, he said he did not care if I did tell.

===Chinese===
在出售 48 我们牲畜和谷物的事情上，有过许多误会。印第安人原以为牛卖掉之后，钱会交到他们手里；可实际上，钱只有一部分发给印第安人，另一部分放进了军官们所谓的「阿帕奇基金」（Apache Fund）。先后有五位不同的军官主管过这里的印第安人，他们的做法都十分相似——不同阿帕奇人商量，甚至不向他们说明。也许这是政府命令主管军官把卖牛的钱放进阿帕奇基金的；因为有一次我抱怨，对普林顿中尉（Lieutenant Purington）49 说，我要向政府告发，他把卖牛钱里属于我的那份拿走了一些、放进了阿帕奇基金，他却说，我就是去告发，他也不在乎。

===Original===
Several years ago the issue of clothing ceased. This, too, may have been by the order of the Government, but the Apaches do not understand it.

===Chinese===
几年前，发放衣物的供应停止了。这也可能是政府的命令，但阿帕奇人不明白这是为什么。

===Original===
If there is an Apache Fund, it should some day be turned over to the Indians, or at least they should have an account of it, for it is their earnings.

===Chinese===
倘若真有阿帕奇基金，总有一天应当交还印第安人，至少也该给他们一份账目，因为这本来就是他们挣来的钱。

===Original===
When General Miles last visited Fort Sill I asked to be relieved from labor on account of my age. I also remembered what General Miles had promised me in the treaty and told him of it. He said I need not work any more except when I wished to, and since that time I have not been detailed to do any work. I have worked a great deal, however, since then, for, although I am old, I like to work50 and help my people as much as I am able.

===Chinese===
迈尔斯将军上次来西尔堡时，我请求他念我年老，免了我的劳役。我还记得迈尔斯将军在条约里许过我的话，也对他说了。他说，往后我不必再做工，除非我自己愿意；从那时起，再没有派过我什么活。不过从那以后我仍干了许多活，因为人虽老了，我喜欢干活 50，愿意尽力帮助我的族人。
