## VII Fighting Under Difficulties / 七 艰难奋战

===Original===
All the other Apaches were satisfied after the battle of Kaskiyeh, but I still desired more revenge. For several months we were busy with the chase and other peaceful pursuits. Finally I succeeded in persuading two others warriors, Ah-koch-ne and Ko-deh-ne, to go with me to invade the Mexican country.

===Chinese===
卡斯基耶一仗打完，别的阿帕奇人都满足了，我却仍渴望更多的复仇。此后几个月，我们忙于狩猎和别的营生。最后我说动了两名武士——阿科奇内（Ah-koch-ne）和科德内（Ko-deh-ne）——随我杀入墨西哥人的地界。

===Original===
We left our16 families with the tribe and went on the warpath. We were on foot and carried three days’ rations. We entered Mexico on the north line of Sonora and followed the Sierra de Antunez Mountains to the south end of the range. Here we decided to attack a small village. (I do not know the name of this village.) At daylight we approached from the mountains. Five horses were hitched outside. We advanced cautiously, but just before we reached the horses the Mexicans opened fire from the houses. My two companions were killed. Mexicans swarmed on every side; some were mounted; some were on foot, and all seemed to be armed. Three times that day I was surrounded, but I kept fighting, dodging, and hiding. Several times during the day while in concealment I had a chance to take deliberate aim at some Mexican, who, gun in hand, was looking for me. I do not think I missed my aim either time. With the gathering darkness I found more time to retreat toward Arizona. But the Mexicans did not quit the chase. Several times the next day mounted Mexicans tried to head me off; many times they fired on me, but I had no more arrows; so I depended upon running and hiding, although I was very tired. I had not eaten since the chase began, nor had I dared to stop for rest. The second night I got clear of my pursuers, but I never slackened my pace until I reached our home in Arizona. I came into our camp without booty, without my companions, exhausted, but not discouraged.

===Chinese===
我们把家眷16 留给部落，踏上了征途。我们步行前往，带了三天的口粮。我们从索诺拉北界进入墨西哥，沿着安图内斯山脉（Sierra de Antunez）走到山系南端。在这里我们决定袭击一座小村庄。（这村子叫什么名字，我不知道。）天亮时分，我们从山上摸近村边。村外拴着五匹马。我们小心前进，可就在快到马跟前时，墨西哥人从屋里开了火。我的两个同伴都被打死了。墨西哥人四面围拢；有的骑马，有的步行，个个似乎都有武器。那天我三次被围，但我且战、且躲、且藏。白天有几回，我在暗处得着机会，从容瞄准那些持枪搜寻我的墨西哥人。我自信没有一回打空。天色渐黑，我得了空子向亚利桑那（Arizona）且战且退。但墨西哥人不肯罢休。第二天，骑马的墨西哥人几次想抄到我前头拦住去路；他们朝我开了许多枪，而我的箭早已用尽；只好凭着奔跑和躲藏脱身，尽管我已疲惫不堪。自被追捕起我没有吃过一口东西，也不敢停下来歇息。第二夜我才甩脱追兵，可我一直不敢放慢脚步，直到回到亚利桑那的家。我回到营地，没有缴获，没有同伴，精疲力竭，却没有灰心。

===Original===
The wives and children of my two dead companions were cared for by their people. Some of the Apaches blamed me for the evil result of the expedition, but I said nothing. Having failed, it was only proper that I should remain silent. But my feelings toward the Mexicans did not change﻿—I still hated them and longed for revenge. I never ceased to plan for their punishment, but it was hard to get the other warriors to listen to my proposed raids.

===Chinese===
我那两个死去同伴的妻子儿女，由他们的亲人照管。有些阿帕奇人怪我此行招致恶果，我一言不发。既然败了，我合该沉默。但我对墨西哥人的心肠没有变——我依旧恨他们，依旧渴望复仇。我从未停止谋划惩罚他们，只是很难让别的武士听我讲那些突袭的打算。

===Original===
In a few months after this last adventure I persuaded two other warriors to join me in raiding the Mexican frontier. On our former raid we had gone through the Nedni Apaches’ range into Sonora. This time we went through the country of the Cho-kon-en and entered the Sierra Madre Mountains. We traveled south, secured more rations, and prepared to begin our raids. We had selected a village near the mountains which we intended to attack at daylight. While asleep that night Mexican scouts discovered our camp and fired on us, killing one warrior. In the morning we observed a company of Mexican troops coming from the south. They were mounted and carried supplies for a long journey. We followed their trail until we were sure that they were headed for our range in Arizona; then we hurried past them and in three days reached our own settlement. We arrived at noon, and that afternoon, about three o’clock, these Mexican troops attacked our settlement. Their first volley killed three small boys. Many of the warriors of our tribe were away from home, but the few of us who were in camp were able to drive the troops out of the mountains before night. We killed eight Mexicans and lost five﻿—two warriors and three boys. The Mexicans rode due south in full retreat. Four warriors were detailed to follow them, and in three days these trailers returned, saying that the Mexican cavalry had left Arizona, going southward. We were quite sure they would not return soon.

===Chinese===
上次冒险之后又过了几个月，我说动了另外两名武士，同我一起去袭掠墨西哥边地。前一次袭掠，我们是取道内德尼阿帕奇人的猎场进入索诺拉；这一回，我们走乔科嫩（Cho-kon-en）的地界，进入了马德雷山脉（Sierra Madre Mountains）。我们一路南行，补充了口粮，准备动手。我们选定了山边一座村庄，打算天亮时进攻。夜里我们睡熟的时候，墨西哥的侦察兵发现了我们的营地，向我们开枪，打死了一名武士。早晨，我们望见一队墨西哥军队从南边开来。他们骑着马，驮着长途的给养。我们缀着他们的踪迹走，直到确信他们的目的地正是我们在亚利桑那的猎场；于是我们抢在他们前面赶路，三天后回到了自己的居地。我们是中午到的，当天下午约莫三点钟，这队墨西哥军就袭击了我们的居地。他们第一排枪就打死了三个小男孩。我们部落许多武士不在家，但营中仅有的这几个人，赶在天黑前把他们逐出了山。我们杀死八个墨西哥人，折了五个——两名武士和三个孩子。墨西哥人纵马朝正南仓皇退去。我们派了四名武士跟踪追蹑，三天后追踪的人回来，说墨西哥骑兵已离开亚利桑那，往南去了。我们很确信，他们一时半会儿不会再来。

===Original===
Soon after this (in the summer of 1860) I was again able to take the warpath against the Mexicans, this time with twenty-five warriors. We followed the trail of the Mexican troops last mentioned and entered the Sierra de Sahuaripa Mountains. The second day in these mountains our scouts discovered mounted Mexican troops. There was only one company of cavalry in this command, and I thought that by properly surprising them we could defeat them. We ambushed the trail over which they were to come. This was at a place where the whole company must pass through a mountain defile. We reserved fire until all of the troops had passed through; then the signal was given. The Mexican troopers, seemingly without a word of command, dismounted, and placing their horses on the outside of the company, for breastworks, made a good fight against us. I saw that we could not dislodge them without using all our ammunition, so I led a charge. The warriors suddenly pressed in from all sides and we fought hand to hand. During this encounter I raised my spear to kill a Mexican soldier just as he leveled his gun at me; I was advancing rapidly, and my foot slipping in a pool of blood, I fell under the Mexican trooper. He struck me over the head with the butt of his gun, knocking me senseless. Just at that instant a warrior who followed in my footsteps killed the Mexican with a spear. In a few minutes not a Mexican soldier was left alive. When the Apache war-cry had died away, and their enemies had been scalped, they began to care for their dead and wounded. I was found lying unconscious where I had fallen. They bathed my head in cold water and restored me to consciousness. Then they bound up my wound and the next morning, although weak from loss of blood and suffering from a severe headache, I was able to march on the return to Arizona. I did not fully recover for months, and I still wear the scar given me by that musketeer. In this fight we had lost so heavily that there really was no glory in our victory, and we returned to Arizona. No one seemed to want to go on the warpath again that year.

===Chinese===
此后不久（1860 年夏天），我又能出征讨伐墨西哥人了，这回带了二十五名武士。我们循着上一队墨西哥军的行迹，进了萨瓦里帕山脉（Sierra de Sahuaripa）。进山第二天，我们的侦察兵发现了一支骑马的墨西哥部队。这支队伍只有一个骑兵连，我心想只要出其不意，定能吃掉他们。我们在他们必经的路上设了埋伏。那地方是条山间隘口，全队人马非从中间过不可。我们按兵不动，等他们全部穿过隘口，才发出信号。那些墨西哥骑兵似乎没等口令就纷纷下马，把马牵到队伍外围当作胸墙，同我们打得有板有眼。我看出不用尽弹药赶不走他们，便率众冲锋。武士们从四面猛然压上，我们短兵相接。混战中，我正举矛要刺一个墨西哥士兵，恰在那人端枪向我瞄准；我进身太快，一脚踩进血泊滑倒，摔在那墨西哥骑兵身下。他抡起枪托砸在我的头上，把我砸得不省人事。就在那一瞬，紧随我身后的一名武士一矛结果了那墨西哥人。几分钟后，活着的墨西哥士兵一个不剩。等阿帕奇的战嚎止息、敌人的头皮被剥下之后，大家才开始照料自己死伤的人。我被人发现时，正倒在原地人事不省。他们用冷水浸洗我的头，把我救醒过来。随后他们替我包扎好伤口；第二天早晨，我虽因失血而虚弱、头疼得厉害，仍能随队启程返回亚利桑那。过了好几个月我才完全复原，那个火枪手留给我的伤疤，至今还在。这一仗我们损失惨重，胜了也实在说不上光彩，我们便回了亚利桑那。那一年，似乎谁也不想再出征了。

===Original===
In the summer (1861) with twelve warriors I again went into Mexico. We entered Chihuahua and followed south on the east side of the Sierra Madre Mountains four days’ journey; then crossed over to the Sierra de Sahuaripa range, not far east of Casa Grande. Here we rested one day, and sent out scouts to reconnoiter. They reported pack trains camped five miles west of us. The next morning just at daybreak, as these drivers were starting with their mule pack train, we attacked them. They rode away for their lives, leaving us the booty. The mules were loaded with provisions, most of which we took home. Two mules were loaded with side-meat or bacon;17 this we threw away. We started to take these pack trains home, going northward through Sonora, but when near Casita, Mexican troops overtook us. It was at daybreak and we were just finishing our breakfast. We had no idea that we had been pursued or that our enemies were near until they opened fire. At the first volley a bullet struck me a glancing lick just at the lower corner of the left eye and I fell unconscious. All the other Indians fled to cover. The Mexicans, thinking me dead, started in pursuit of the fleeing Indians. In a few moments I regained consciousness and had started at full speed for the woods when another company coming up opened fire on me. Then the soldiers who had been chasing the other Indians turned, and I stood between two hostile companies, but I did not stand long. Bullets whistled in every direction and at close range to me. One inflicted a slight flesh wound on my side, but I kept running, dodging, and fighting, until I got clear of my pursuers. I climbed up a steep canyon, where the cavalry could not follow. The troopers saw me, but did not dismount and try to follow. I think they were wise not to come on.

===Chinese===
夏天（1861 年），我又带着十二名武士进入墨西哥。我们进入奇瓦瓦（Chihuahua），沿马德雷山脉东麓南行四天的路程；随后翻越到萨瓦里帕山系，在卡萨格兰德（Casa Grande）以东不远的地方。我们在这里歇了一天，派出侦察兵四下探查。他们回报说，西面五英里外驻着骡马驮队。第二天一早，天刚破晓，赶驮队的人正要驱骡启程，我们扑了上去。他们逃命要紧，把辎重全丢给了我们。骡子驮的多是粮食，大部分被我们运回了家。有两头骡子驮的是腌肋肉，也就是培根；17 这些我们扔了。我们赶着缴获的驮队北上，取道索诺拉回程，可是快到卡西塔（Casita）时，墨西哥部队追上了我们。那也是天刚亮，我们刚好吃完早饭。直到对方开火，我们才知道自己被追了、敌人就在眼前。第一排枪里，一颗子弹擦过我的左眼角下沿，我应声昏倒。其余的印第安人全都奔向掩蔽处。墨西哥人只当我死了，便去追那些奔逃的印第安人。片刻后我苏醒过来，正拔腿朝树林飞奔，另一支赶来的连队又朝我开火。先前追我同伴的士兵也回过头来，我夹在两股敌军之间，好在没有夹多久。子弹四下呼啸，贴着我的身子飞。有一颗在我胁边擦开一道轻伤，我却仍是又跑、又躲、又打，直到甩脱追兵。我爬上一条陡峭的峡谷，骑兵上不来。那些骑兵望见了我，却没有下马来追。我想他们不追是聪明的。

===Original===
It had been understood that in case of surprise with this booty, our place of rendezvous should be the Santa Bita Mountains in Arizona. We did not reassemble in Mexico, but traveled separately and in three days we were encamped in our place of rendezvous. From this place we returned home empty-handed. We had not even a partial victory to report. I again returned wounded, but I was not yet discouraged. Again I was blamed by our people, and again I had no reply.

===Chinese===
原先有约在先：倘若带着这批辎重遭遇不测，我们的会合地点是亚利桑那的圣比塔山（Santa Bita Mountains）。我们没有在墨西哥重新聚齐，而是各自分头赶路，三天后，大家已在会合点扎了营。从那里我们两手空空地回了家。我们连一场小胜都无可禀报。我又一次带伤而归，但我还没有灰心。族人们又怪我，我又无言以对。

===Original===
After our return many of the warriors had gone on a hunt and some of them had gone north to trade for blankets from the Navajo Indians. I remained at home trying to get my wounds healed. One morning just at daybreak, when the squaws were lighting the campfires to prepare breakfast, three companies of Mexican troops who had surrounded our settlement in the night opened fire. There was no time for fighting. Men, women, and children fled for their lives. Many women and children and a few warriors were killed, and four women were captured. My left eye was still swollen shut, but with the other I saw well enough to hit one of the officers with an arrow, and then make good my escape among the rocks. The troopers burned our teepees and took our arms, provisions, ponies, and blankets. Winter was at hand.

===Chinese===
我们回来以后，许多武士外出打猎去了，有些人北上找纳瓦霍印第安人换毯子。我留在家里养伤。一天清早，天刚放亮，妇人们正生起篝火预备早饭，夜里已把我们居地团团围住的三个连墨西哥军队开了火。根本来不及应战。男女老少四散逃命。许多妇女孩子和几名武士被杀，四个女人被掳走。我的左眼还肿得睁不开，但靠另一只眼，我还看得清，一箭射中他们的一名军官，随即钻进乱石堆里脱了身。那些骑兵烧了我们的梯皮帐篷，掠走了我们的武器、粮食、矮种马和毯子。冬天眼看就要来了。

===Original===
There were not more than twenty warriors in camp at this time, and only a few of us had secured weapons during the excitement of the attack. A few warriors followed the trail of the troops as they went back to Mexico with their booty, but were unable to offer battle. It was a long, long time before we were again able to go on the warpath against the Mexicans.

===Chinese===
那时营里的武士不到二十人，混战之中抢到武器的只有寥寥几个。有几个武士缀着那队满载掳获、退回墨西哥去的敌军的踪迹追了一段，却无力求战。过了很久很久，我们才重新有能力出征讨伐墨西哥人。

===Original===
The four women who were captured at this time by the Mexicans were taken into Sonora, Mexico, where they were compelled to work for the Mexicans. After some years they escaped to the mountains and started to find our tribe. They had knives which they had stolen from the Mexicans, but they had no other weapons. They had no blankets; so at night they would make a little teepee by cutting brush with their knives, and setting them up for the walls. The top was covered over with brush. In this temporary teepee they would all sleep. One night when their campfire was low they heard growling just outside the teepee. Francisco, the youngest woman of the party (about seventeen years of age), started to build up the fire, when a mountain lion crashed through the teepee and attacked her. The suddenness of the attack made her drop her knife, but she fought as best she could with her hand. She was no match for the lion, however; her left shoulder was crushed and partly torn away. The lion kept trying to catch her by the throat; this she prevented with her hands for a long time. He dragged her for about 300 yards, then she found her strength was failing her from loss of blood, and she called to the other women for help. The lion had been dragging her by one foot, and she had been catching hold of his legs, and of the rocks and underbrush, to delay him. Finally he stopped and stood over her. She again called her companions and they attacked him with their knives and killed him. Then they dressed her wounds and nursed her in the mountains for about a month. When she was again able to walk they resumed their journey and reached our tribe in safety.

===Chinese===
这时被墨西哥人掳走的四个女人，被带到墨西哥的索诺拉，被迫替墨西哥人做工。过了几年，她们逃进山里，动身寻找我们的部落。她们带着从墨西哥人那里偷来的刀，却没有别的武器。她们没有毯子；夜里便用刀砍下树枝，支起来作墙，搭成一个小小的帐篷，顶上再盖上枝叶。就在这临时的帐篷里，她们挤在一起睡。一天夜里，篝火将熄，她们听见帐篷外传来低吼。弗朗西斯科（Francisco），一行中最年轻的女人（约莫十七岁），起身去添火，一头美洲狮破帐而入，向她扑来。事起突然，她的刀脱了手，只好徒手拼命招架。可她哪里是狮子的对手；她的左肩被咬得粉碎，还撕掉了一块。狮子一次次想咬住她的喉咙，她用双手死死撑住了很久。狮子拖着她走了约 300 码，她渐渐因失血而力气不支，便呼喊其余的女人来救。狮子一直咬着她的脚往回拖，她就抓着狮子的腿，抓着石头和灌木，拖延它。最后狮子停下来，站在她身上。她再呼同伴，同伴们持刀齐上，把它杀死了。随后她们替她裹伤，在山里护理了约莫一个月。等她重新能走路了，一行人再启程，平安到了我们部落。

===Original===
This woman (Francisco) was held as a prisoner of war with the other Apaches and died on the Fort Sill Reservation in 1892. Her face was always disfigured with those scars and she never regained perfect use of her hands. The three older women died before we became prisoners of war.

===Chinese===
这个女人（弗朗西斯科）后来同别的阿帕奇人一起被当作战俘拘押，1892 年死在西尔堡保留地（Fort Sill Reservation）。她的脸上永远留着那些疤痕，双手也再没能恢复如初。另外三个年长的女人，都在我们沦为战俘之前去世了。

===Original===
Many women and children were carried away at different times by Mexicans. Not many of them ever returned, and those who did underwent many hardships in order to be again united with their people. Those who did not escape were slaves to the Mexicans, or perhaps even more degraded.

===Chinese===
许许多多妇女和孩子，在不同的时候被墨西哥人掳走。回来的人寥寥无几；回来的那些，也是历尽艰辛才得以同族人重聚。逃不出来的，就给墨西哥人做奴隶，甚或落到更下贱的地步。

===Original===
When warriors were captured by the Mexicans they were kept in chains. Four warriors who were captured once at a place north of Casa Grande, called by the Indians “Honas,” were kept in chains for a year and a half, when they were exchanged for Mexicans whom we had captured.

===Chinese===
武士被墨西哥人捉去，就要戴上镣铐。有四名武士曾在卡萨格兰德以北一个被印第安人唤作「霍纳斯」（Honas）的地方被俘，戴了一年半的镣铐，才用我们俘获的墨西哥人换回来。

===Original===
We never chained prisoners or kept them in confinement, but they seldom got away. Mexican men when captured were compelled to cut wood and herd horses. Mexican women and children18 were treated as our own people.

===Chinese===
我们从不给俘虏戴镣铐，也不把他们关押起来，但他们很少能逃掉。墨西哥男人被俘后，就被派去砍柴、牧马。墨西哥的妇女和孩子，18 我们则待之如自己的族人。
