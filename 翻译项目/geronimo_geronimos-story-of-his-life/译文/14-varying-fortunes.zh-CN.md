## IX Varying Fortunes / 九 胜败无常

===Original===
In the fall of 1865 with nine other warriors I went into Mexico on foot. We attacked several settlements south of Casa Grande, and collected many horses and mules. We made our way northward with these animals through the mountains. When near Arispe we made camp one evening, and thinking that we were not being trailed, turned loose the whole herd, even those we had been riding. They were in a valley surrounded by steep mountains, and we were camped at the mouth of this valley so that the animals could not leave without coming through our camp. Just as we had begun to eat our supper our scouts came in and announced Mexican troops coming toward our camp. We started for the horses, but troops that our scouts had not seen were on the cliffs above us, and opened fire. We scattered in all directions, and the troops recovered all our booty. In three days we reassembled at our appointed place of rendezvous in the Sierra Madre Mountains in northern Sonora. Mexican troops did not follow us, and we returned to Arizona without any more fighting and with no booty. Again I had nothing to say, but I was anxious for another raid.

===Chinese===
1865 年秋，我同另外九名武士徒步进入墨西哥。我们袭击了卡萨格兰德（Casa Grande）以南的几处聚落，收拢了许多马和骡子。我们赶着这些牲口取山间小道北返。一天傍晚，快到阿里斯佩（Arispe）时，我们扎下营，自忖没有被人追踪，便把整群牲口都放开，连自己骑的也放了。牲口在一处四面陡山的谷地里，我们的营扎在谷口，牲口要出去，非得穿过我们的营地不可。我们刚端起晚饭，侦察兵进营报告：墨西哥部队朝我们营地来了。我们起身奔向马匹，可我们侦察兵没发现的另一股部队已占了头顶的悬崖，开了火。我们四散奔逃，部队把我们的掳获全数夺了回去。三天后，我们在索诺拉北部马德雷山脉（Sierra Madre Mountains）中的老会合点重新聚齐。墨西哥部队没有追来，我们没有再交战，两手空空地回了亚利桑那（Arizona）。我又一次无话可说，却更急切地想再去袭掠一回。

===Original===
Early the next summer (1866) I took thirty mounted warriors and invaded Mexican territory. We went south through Chihuahua as far as Santa Cruz, Sonora, then crossed over the Sierra Madre Mountains, following the river course at the south end of the range. We kept on westward from the Sierra Madre Mountains to the Sierra de Sahuripa Mountains, and followed that range northward. We collected all the horses, mules, and cattle we wanted, and drove them northward through Sonora into Arizona. Mexicans saw us at many times and in many places, but they did not attack us at any time, nor did any troops attempt to follow us. When we arrived at our homes we gave presents to all, and the tribe feasted and danced. During this raid we had killed about fifty Mexicans.

===Chinese===
第二年夏初（1866 年），我带了三十名骑马的武士侵入墨西哥境内。我们南下穿过奇瓦瓦（Chihuahua），直抵索诺拉的圣克鲁斯（Santa Cruz），然后翻过马德雷山脉，沿着山系南端的河道走。我们离开马德雷山脉继续向西，到萨瓦里帕山脉（Sierra de Sahuripa），再沿这条山系北行。我们收齐了想要的马、骡和牛，赶着它们取道索诺拉北上，进入亚利桑那。墨西哥人多次多处望见我们，却始终没有来攻，也没有部队试图追赶。到了家，我们给所有人分送了礼物，全族设宴跳舞。这次袭掠，我们总共杀了约五十个墨西哥人。

===Original===
Next year (1867) Mangus-Colorado led eight warriors on a raid into Mexico. I went as a warrior, for I was always glad to fight the Mexicans. We rode south from near Tombstone, Arizona, into Sonora, Mexico. We attacked some cowboys, and after a fight with them, in which two of their number were killed, we drove all their cattle northward. The second day we were driving the cattle, but had no scouts out. When we were not far from Arispe, Mexican troops rode upon us. They were well armed and well mounted, and when we first saw them they were not half a mile away from us. We left the cattle and rode as hard as we could toward the mountains, but they gained on us rapidly. Soon they opened fire, but were so far away from us that we were unable to reach them with our arrows; finally we reached some timber, and, leaving our ponies, fought from cover. Then the Mexicans halted, collected our ponies, and rode away across the plains toward Arispe, driving the cattle with them. We stood and watched them until they disappeared in the distance, and then took up our march for home.

===Chinese===
第二年（1867 年），曼加斯-科罗拉多（Mangus-Colorado）率八名武士前往墨西哥袭掠。我以武士的身份同行，因为同墨西哥人打仗，我向来甘愿。我们从亚利桑那墓碑镇（Tombstone）附近南下，进入墨西哥索诺拉。我们袭击了一伙牧牛人，交手之后，他们死了两个，我们把他们的牛统统往北赶。第二天我们赶着牛走，却没有放出侦察兵。离阿里斯佩不远时，墨西哥部队策马扑来。他们武器精良，马也雄健；我们刚望见他们时，他们离我们还不到半英里。我们丢下牛，没命地往山里逃，他们却追得飞快。不久他们开了火，但距离尚远，我们的箭够不着他们；我们总算赶到了一片树林，撇下矮种马，凭掩护还击。这时墨西哥人停了下来，收拢我们的矮种马，赶着牛，穿过平原往阿里斯佩去了。我们站在原地，望着他们消失在远方，然后动身步行回家。

===Original===
We arrived home in five days with no victory to report, no spoils to divide, and not even the ponies which we had ridden into Mexico. This expedition was considered disgraceful.

===Chinese===
我们五天后到家，既无胜仗可报，又无掳获可分，连骑进墨西哥的那些矮种马也没能带回来。这一趟被众人视为奇耻大辱。

===Original===
The warriors who had been with Mangus-Colorado on this last expedition wanted to return to Mexico. They were not satisfied, besides they felt keenly the taunts of the other warriors. Mangus-Colorado would not lead them back, so I took command and we went on foot, directly toward Arispe in Sonora, and made our camp in the Sierra de Sahuripa Mountains. There were only six of us, but we raided several settlements (at night), captured many horses and mules, and loaded them with provisions, saddles and blankets. Then we returned to Arizona, traveling only at night. When we arrived at our camp we sent out scouts to prevent any surprise by Mexicans, assembled the tribe, feasted, danced, and divided the spoils. Mangus-Colorado would not receive any of this booty, but we did not care. No Mexican troops followed us to Arizona.

===Chinese===
随曼加斯-科罗拉多走过这一趟的武士们想再回墨西哥去。他们心有不甘，又实在咽不下其他武士的讥嘲。曼加斯-科罗拉多不愿再领他们去，我便接了统领。我们徒步出发，直奔索诺拉的阿里斯佩，在萨瓦里帕山里扎营。我们只有六个人，却（在夜里）袭掠了好几处聚落，掳得许多马骡，给它们驮上粮食、马鞍和毯子。随后我们回亚利桑那，只在夜里赶路。回到营地，我们先放出侦察兵，防墨西哥人偷袭，然后召齐全族，设宴、跳舞、分战利品。曼加斯-科罗拉多不肯受分这批掳获，我们也不在意。墨西哥部队没有追进亚利桑那。

===Original===
About a year after this (1868) Mexican troops rounded up all the horses and mules of the tribe not far from our settlement. No raids had been made into Mexico that year, and we were not expecting any attacks. We were all in camp, having just returned from hunting.

===Chinese===
此后约一年（1868 年），墨西哥部队在我们居地不远处把部落的马骡洗劫一空。那一年我们没有进墨西哥袭掠过，也就没有提防任何来犯。我们都在营里，刚打猎回来。

===Original===
About two o’clock in the afternoon two Mexican scouts were seen near our settlement. We killed these scouts, but the troops got under way with the herd of our horses and mules before we saw them. It was useless to try to overtake them on foot, and our tribe had not a horse left. I took twenty warriors and trailed them. We found the stock at a cattle ranch in Sonora, not far from Nacozari, and attacked the cowboys who had them in charge. We killed two men and lost none. After the fight we drove off our own stock and all of theirs.

===Chinese===
下午两点来钟，我们居地附近出现了两个墨西哥侦察兵。我们杀了他们，可没等我们望见大队，部队已赶着我们那群马骡上路了。徒步追赶无济于事，我们部落又一匹马也不剩。我带了二十名武士，一路追踪。我们在索诺拉一座牧牛场找到了牲口，那地方离纳科萨里（Nacozari）不远；我们袭击了看管牲口的牧牛人，杀了两个，自己无一伤亡。战罢，我们赶走了自己的牲口，连他们的也一并赶走了。

===Original===
We were trailed by nine cowboys. I sent the stock on ahead and with three warriors stayed in the rear to intercept any attacking parties. One night when near the Arizona line we discovered these cowboys on our trail and watched them camp for the night and picket their horses. About midnight we stole into their camp and silently led away all their horses, leaving the cowboys asleep. Then we rode hard and overtook our companions, who always traveled at night instead of in the daytime. We turned these horses in with the herd and fell back to again intercept anyone who might trail us. What these nine cowboys did next morning I do not know, and I have never heard the Mexicans say anything about it; I know they did not follow us, for we were not molested. When we arrived in camp at home there was great rejoicing in the tribe. It was considered a good trick to get the Mexicans’ horses and leave them asleep in the mountains.

===Chinese===
九个牧牛人追踪我们。我让牲口先走，自己带三名武士殿后，拦截可能的追兵。一天夜里，快到亚利桑那边界时，我们发现这些牧牛人缀在我们后面，便看他们扎营过夜、拴好马。约莫半夜，我们摸进他们的营地，悄悄牵走了他们所有的马，让那些牧牛人兀自酣睡。然后我们打马疾驰，追上了总是昼伏夜行的同伴。我们把这些马并入牲口群，又退回后队，防备再有人追踪。这九个牧牛人第二天早上如何，我不知道，也从未听墨西哥人提起过；我只知道他们没有再追来，因为我们再没受到搅扰。我们回到家里的营地时，全族欢腾。夺得墨西哥人的马、把他们留在山里沉睡，被大家看作一桩妙计。

===Original===
It was a long time before we again went into Mexico or were disturbed by the Mexicans.

===Chinese===
此后很久，我们既没有再去墨西哥，也没有再受墨西哥人搅扰。
