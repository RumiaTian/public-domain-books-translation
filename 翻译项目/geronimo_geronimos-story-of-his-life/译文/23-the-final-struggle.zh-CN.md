## XVII The Final Struggle / 十七 最后的抗争

===Original===
We started with all our tribe to go with General Crook back to the United States, but I feared treachery and decided to remain in Mexico. We were not under any guard at this time. The United States troops marched in front and the Indians followed, and when we became suspicious, we turned back. I do not know how far the United States army went after myself, and some warriors turned back before we were missed, and I do not care.

===Chinese===
我们全部落的人起程，要随克鲁克将军（General Crook）回美国去，可我怕其中有诈，便决意留在墨西哥。这时并没有人看守我们。美国部队行在前面，印第安人跟在后面，待我们起了疑心，便折了回去。美国军队追了我多远，我不知道；有些武士趁人未察觉也折了回去；这些我都不放在心上。

===Original===
I have suffered much from such unjust orders as those of General Crook. Such acts have caused much distress to my people. I think that General Crook’s death35 was sent by the Almighty as a punishment for the many evil deeds he committed.

===Chinese===
像克鲁克将军下的这类不公的命令，我吃过许多苦头。这样的行径给我的族人带来了深重的苦难。我想，克鲁克将军之死35，是全能的神降下的惩罚，罚他生前作下的诸多恶事。

===Original===
Soon General Miles was made commander of all the western posts, and troops trailed us continually. They were led by Captain Lawton, who had good scouts. The Mexican36 soldiers also became more active and more numerous. We had skirmishes almost every day, and so we finally decided to break up into small bands. With six men and four women I made for the range of mountains near Hot Springs, New Mexico. We passed many cattle ranches, but had no trouble with the cowboys. We killed cattle to eat whenever we were in need of food, but we frequently suffered greatly for water. At one time we had no water for two days and nights and our horses almost died from thirst. We ranged in the mountains of New Mexico for some time, then thinking that perhaps the troops had left Mexico, we returned. On our return through Old Mexico we attacked every Mexican found, even if for no other reason than to kill. We believed they had asked the United States troops to come down to Mexico to fight us.

===Chinese===
不久，迈尔斯将军（General Miles）受命统领西部各哨所，部队便不停地循迹追捕我们。带队的是劳顿上尉（Captain Lawton），他手底下有些好侦察兵。墨西哥36士兵也变得更加活跃，人数也更多了。我们几乎天天有小仗要打，于是最后决意分散成小股。我带着六个男人、四个女人，直奔新墨西哥温泉（Hot Springs）附近的山岭而去。我们经过许多牧牛场，却没有同牛仔起过纠纷。缺吃的时候我们就宰牛充饥，可常常为水吃大苦头。有一回，我们两天两夜没有水喝，马匹几乎渴死。我们在新墨西哥的群山里往来盘桓了一些时日，后来想着部队也许已经离开墨西哥，便折了回去。归途穿行老墨西哥（Old Mexico）时，我们逢着墨西哥人就杀，哪怕别无缘故，单为杀戮也要杀。我们认定，是他们请了美国部队南下墨西哥来打我们。

===Original===
South of Casa Grande, near a place called by the Indians Gosoda, there was a road leading out from the town. There was much freighting carried on by the Mexicans over this road. Where the road ran through a mountain pass we stayed in hiding, and whenever Mexican freighters passed we killed them, took what supplies we wanted, and destroyed the remainder. We were reckless of our lives, because we felt that every man’s hand was against us. If we returned to the reservation we would be put in prison and killed; if we stayed in Mexico they would continue to send soldiers to fight us; so we gave no quarter to anyone and asked no favors.

===Chinese===
在卡萨格兰德（Casa Grande）以南，靠近一处印第安人唤作戈索达（Gosoda）的地方，有一条从镇上通出去的路。墨西哥人在这条路上经营着繁忙的货运。在道路穿山口而过的地方，我们潜伏下来，每逢墨西哥运货的人经过，我们就杀了他们，取走所要的物资，把余下的尽数毁掉。我们把自己的性命置之度外，因为我们觉得，人人的手都在与我们作对。回到保留地，就要被投进监狱、遭杀害；留在墨西哥，他们又会不断派兵来打我们；所以我们对谁都不留情，也不向谁乞恩。

===Original===
After some time we left Gosoda and soon were reunited with our tribe in the Sierra de Antunez Mountains.

===Chinese===
过了些时日，我们离开戈索达，不久便在安图内斯山脉（Sierra de Antunez）同自己的部落会合了。

===Original===
Contrary to our expectations the United States soldiers had not left the mountains in Mexico, and were soon trailing us and skirmishing with us almost every day. Four or five times they surprised our camp. One time they surprised us about nine o’clock in the morning, and captured all our horses37 (nineteen in number) and secured our store of dried meats. We also lost three Indians in this encounter. About the middle of the afternoon of the same day we attacked them from the rear as they were passing through a prairie﻿—killed one soldier, but lost none ourselves. In this skirmish we recovered all our horses except three that belonged to me. The three horses that we did not recover were the best riding horses we had.

===Chinese===
出乎我们的意料，美国士兵并没有离开墨西哥的山区，很快又循迹追来，几乎天天同我们打小仗。他们有四五次偷袭了我们的营地。有一回，早晨九点钟光景，他们袭了我们，把我们全部的马匹37（共十九匹）掳了去，还夺走了我们贮存的干肉。这一遭我们还折了三个印第安人。同一天下午过了一半，趁他们正穿过一片草原，我们从背后掩杀过去——杀了一名士兵，自己一个没折。这场小仗里，我们把马匹全数夺了回来，只除了属于我的三匹。没能夺回的那三匹，是我们最善骑的好马。

===Original===
Soon after this we made a treaty with the Mexican troops. They told us that the United States troops were the real cause of these wars, and agreed not to fight any more with us provided we would return to the United States. This we agreed to do, and resumed our march, expecting to try to make a treaty with the United States soldiers and return to Arizona. There seemed to be no other course to pursue.

===Chinese===
这之后不久，我们同墨西哥部队立了约。他们对我说，这些战事真正的根由在美国部队；只要我们肯回美国去，他们便答应不再同我们交战。我们应允了，便重新上路，指望试着同美国士兵立约，回亚利桑那（Arizona）去。除此之外，似乎再没有别的路可走。

===Original===
Soon after this scouts from Captain Lawton’s troops told us that he wished to make a treaty with us; but I knew that General Miles was the chief of the American troops, and I decided to treat with him.

===Chinese===
此后不久，劳顿上尉部队里的侦察兵来告诉我们，说他想同我们立约；可我知道迈尔斯将军才是美国部队的主帅，便决意同他去谈。

===Original===
We continued to move our camp northward, and the American troops also moved northward,38 keeping at no great distance from us, but not attacking us.

===Chinese===
我们继续拔营北上，美国部队也向北移动，38 与我们相距不远，却不来攻打。

===Original===
I sent my brother Porico (White Horse) with Mr. George Wratton on to Fort Bowie to see General Miles, and to tell him that we wished to return to Arizona; but before these messengers returned I met two Indian scouts﻿—Kayitah, a Chokonen Apache, and Marteen, a Nedni Apache. They were serving as scouts for Captain Lawton’s troops. They told me that General Miles had come and had sent them to ask me to meet him. So I went to the camp of the United States troops to meet General Miles.

===Chinese===
我派我的兄弟波里科（Porico，意为「白马」）同乔治·拉顿（George Wratton）先生前往鲍伊堡（Fort Bowie）去见迈尔斯将军，告诉他我们想回亚利桑那去；可这几位信使还没回来，我先遇见了两个印第安侦察兵——乔科嫩（Chokonen）阿帕奇人凯伊塔（Kayitah），和内德尼（Nedni）阿帕奇人马丁（Marteen）。他们正在劳顿上尉的部队里当侦察兵。他们告诉我，迈尔斯将军已经来了，差他们来请我去见他。于是我前往美国部队的营地，去见迈尔斯将军。

===Original===
When I arrived at their camp I went directly to General Miles and told him how I had been wronged, and that I wanted to return to the United States with my people, as we wished to see our families, who had been captured39 and taken away from us.

===Chinese===
到了他们的营地，我径直去见迈尔斯将军，向他诉说我受了怎样的冤屈，说我愿意带我的人回美国去，因为我们想见自己的家人，他们已被掳走39、被带离了我们身边。

===Original===
General Miles said to me: “The President of the United States has sent me to speak to you. He has heard of your trouble with the white men, and says that if you will agree to a few words of treaty we need have no more trouble. Geronimo, if you will agree to a few words of treaty all will be satisfactorily arranged.”

===Chinese===
迈尔斯将军对我说：「合众国总统差我来对你说话。他听闻了你同白人之间的纷争，说只要你应下寥寥数语的条约，便不必再起纷扰。杰罗尼莫，只要你应下寥寥数语的条约，一切都会得到妥善的安排。」

===Original===
So General Miles told me how we could be brothers to each other. We raised our hands to heaven and said that the treaty was not to be broken. We took an oath not to do any wrong to each other or to scheme against each other.

===Chinese===
就这样，迈尔斯将军对我讲了我们两人可以怎样结为兄弟。我们举手向天，言明这条约永不毁弃。我们起誓，彼此不做亏负对方的事，也不暗算对方。

===Original===
Then he talked with me for a long time and told me what he would do for me in the future if I would agree to the treaty. I did not greatly believe General Miles, but because the President of the United States had sent me word I agreed to make the treaty, and to keep it. Then I asked General Miles what the treaty would be. General Miles said to me:40 “I will take you under Government protection; I will build you a house; I will fence you much land; I will give you cattle, horses, mules, and farming implements. You will be furnished with men to work the farm, for you yourself will not have to work. In the fall I will send you blankets and clothing so that you will not suffer from cold in the winter time.

===Chinese===
随后他同我谈了许久，对我说，倘若我应下这条约，日后他会怎样待我。我并不十分相信迈尔斯将军，可合众国总统既给我捎了话来，我便应承立约，并守约不渝。于是我问迈尔斯将军，条约将是怎样的。迈尔斯将军对我说：40「我要把你们置于政府的保护之下；我要给你们盖一所房子；我要给你们圈出大片土地；我要给你们牛、马、骡子和农具。还会拨给人手替你们种地，因为你自己将不必劳作。入秋我会给你们送去毯子和衣裳，好叫你们冬天不受寒冻。

===Original===
“There is plenty of timber, water, and grass in the land to which I will send you. You will live with your tribe and with your family. If you agree to this treaty you shall see your family within five days.”

===Chinese===
「我要送你们去的那地方，木材、水和草都很充足。你们将同自己的部落、自己的家人住在一起。你若应下这条约，五天之内就能见到你的家人。」

===Original===
I said to General Miles: “All the officers that have been in charge of the Indians have talked that way, and it sounds like a story to me; I hardly believe you.”

===Chinese===
我对迈尔斯将军说：「凡是管治过印第安人的军官，个个都是这样说的，在我听来就像一段故事；我很难相信你。」

===Original===
He said: “This time it is the truth.”

===Chinese===
他说：「这一回是实话。」

===Original===
I said: “General Miles, I do not know the laws of the white man, nor of this new country where you are to send me, and I might break their laws.”

===Chinese===
我说：「迈尔斯将军，我不懂白人的法律，也不懂你要送我去的那片新地方的法律，我说不定会触犯他们的法律。」

===Original===
He said: “While I live you will not be arrested.”

===Chinese===
他说：「只要我活在世上一日，就不会有人逮捕你。」

===Original===
Then I agreed to make the treaty. (Since I have been a prisoner of war I have been arrested and placed in the guardhouse twice for drinking whisky.)

===Chinese===
于是我应承立约。（自从做了战俘，我已两次因喝威士忌被逮捕，关进禁闭室。）

===Original===
We stood between his troopers and my warriors. We placed a large stone on the blanket before us. Our treaty was made by this stone, and it was to last until the stone should crumble to dust; so we made the treaty, and bound each other with an oath.

===Chinese===
我们站在他的骑兵和我的武士之间。我们在面前的毯子上安放了一块大石头。我们的条约就凭着这块石头订立，要延续到石头碎为尘土的那一日；就这样，我们立了约，相互起誓为凭。

===Original===
I do not believe that I have ever violated that treaty; but General Miles41 never fulfilled his promises.

===Chinese===
我不认为自己曾违犯过那条约；可迈尔斯将军41却从未履行他的诺言。

===Original===
When we had made the treaty General Miles said to me: “My brother, you have in your mind how you are going to kill men, and other thoughts of war; I want you to put that out of your mind, and change your thoughts to peace.”

===Chinese===
立约之后，迈尔斯将军对我说：「我的兄弟，你心里还存着怎样去杀人的念头，还有别的战争的念头；我要你把这些从心里撇开，把心思转到和平上。」

===Original===
Then I agreed and gave up my arms. I said: “I will quit the warpath and live at peace hereafter.”

===Chinese===
于是我应承了，交出了武器。我说：「我从此不再出征，往后安享太平。」

===Original===
Then General Miles swept a spot of ground clear with his hand, and said: “Your past deeds shall be wiped out like this and you will start a new life.”

===Chinese===
随后，迈尔斯将军用手把一小块地面扫净了，说：「你过往的行事，就要这样被抹去，你将开始新的生活。」
