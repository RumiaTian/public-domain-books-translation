## II The Girl Who Cried / 二 哭泣的姑娘
===Original===
The northern express had deposited its passengers at King’s Cross on time. All the station approaches were crowded with hurrying passengers. Taxicabs and “growlers” were mixed in apparently inextricable confusion. There was a roaring babble of instruction and counter-instruction from policemen, from cab drivers, and from excited porters. Some of the passengers hurried swiftly across the broad asphalt space and disappeared down the stairs toward the underground station. Others waited for unpunctual friends with protesting and frequent examination of their watches.
One alone seemed wholly bewildered by the noise and commotion. She was a young girl not more than eighteen, and she struggled with two or three brown paper parcels, a hatbox, and a bulky handbag. She was among those who expected to be met at the station, for she looked helplessly at the clock and wandered from one side of the building to the other till at last she came to a standstill in the center, put down all her parcels carefully, and, taking a letter from a shabby little bag, opened it and read.
Evidently she saw something which she had not noticed before, for she hastily replaced the letter in the bag, scrambled together her parcels, and walked swiftly out of the station. Again she came to a halt and looked round the darkened courtyard.

===Chinese===
北方快车正点把旅客卸在了国王十字车站。车站各个出口都挤满了行色匆匆的旅客，出租汽车和老式四轮马车搅作一团，乱得似乎再也解不开。警察、马车夫和兴奋的搬运夫扯着嗓子此呼彼应，吵嚷成一片。有的旅客快步穿过宽阔的沥青广场，消失在通往地铁站的楼梯下面；有的等候着不守时的朋友，一边频频看表，一边满肚子抗议。
唯独有一个人，似乎被这喧嚣搅得全然不知所措。她是个不到十八岁的年轻姑娘，手里艰难地拎着两三个牛皮纸包、一只帽盒和一只鼓鼓囊囊的手提包。她也属于等着有人到车站来接的那类人，只见她束手无策地望望大钟，从车站这边转悠到那边，最后在大厅当中站定，小心放好手里的大小包裹，从一只寒酸的小提包里取出一封信，拆开来读。
显然她看到了先前没留意的东西，因为她慌忙把信塞回提包，拢起大小包裹，快步走出了车站。到了外头她又站住，环顾暮色四合的站前广场。

“You are a stone around my neck,” said the man bitterly. “I have to hide you, and all the time I’m in a fret as to whether you will give me away or not. I am going to keep you under my eye now,” he said. “You know a little too much about me.”

“出去，”他粗声说，“这屋子我要用。”
她顺从地照办了。他反手锁上门，把一只手提箱搬上床，打开来，取出一个小小的日本漆盒。他从盒里拿出一副小小的玻璃研杵研钵、六只小药瓶、一支皮下注射针，还有一盏小酒精灯。接着他又从口袋里掏出烟盒，抽出两支香烟，小心地并排放在梳妆台上。这一小时的绝大部分，他都在忙碌。
至于那姑娘，这段时间就一直待在冰冷的饭厅里，缩在椅子上，独自轻轻啜泣。
