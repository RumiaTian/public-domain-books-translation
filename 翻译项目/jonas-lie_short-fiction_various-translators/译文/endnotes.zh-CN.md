## Endnotes / 尾注

===Original===

1. *Evje* signifies a stream with a slow current. ↩︎

2. Norwegian peasants bear the same name as their estates. As there are no nobles in Norway, the farmers or owners of estates occupy a much higher social position than in Germany. ↩︎

3. The *Skydo-Bauern* along the principal roads supply travellers with beds and food. ↩︎

4. A district in northern Norway. ↩︎

===Chinese===

1. *Evje*（埃维）意为一条水流缓慢的小河。 ↩︎

2. 挪威农民与自己的田庄同名。挪威没有贵族，因此农民亦即田庄主人所占的社会地位，远比德国的农民为高。 ↩︎

3. 大路沿线的斯基多农户（*Skydo-Bauern*）为旅客提供床铺和饮食。 ↩︎

4. 北挪威的一个地区。 ↩︎

===Original===

7. A large boat with five oars on each side, used for winter fishing in northern Norway. ↩︎

8. The chief port in those parts. ↩︎

===Original===

9. *Hin Karen* = “the devil.” “Karen” is the Danish “Karl.” ↩︎

10. The *Klör*, or clews, were rings in the corner of the sail to fasten it down by in a strong wind. *Setja ei Klo* = “take in the sail a clew.” *Setja tvo*, or *tri Klör* = “take it in two or three clews,” I.e., diminish it still further as the wind grew stronger. ↩︎

11. A demon peculiar to the north Norwegian coast. It rides the seas in a half-boat. Compare Icelandic *draugr*. ↩︎

12. See note 6. ↩︎

===Chinese===

9. *Hin Karen*＝「魔鬼」。「Karen」即丹麦语的「Karl」。 ↩︎

10. *Klör* 即帆角环，是帆角上的环扣，风大时用来把帆收紧扣牢。*Setja ei Klo*＝「收一个帆角」。*Setja tvo* 或 *tri Klör*＝「收两三个帆角」，也就是说，风势愈猛，帆面便愈收愈小。 ↩︎

11. 北挪威海岸特有的一种恶魔，驾着半条船在海上游弋。参较冰岛语 *draugr*。 ↩︎

12. 见注 6。 ↩︎

15. The northernmost province of Norway, right within the Arctic circle. ↩︎
