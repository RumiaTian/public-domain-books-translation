## Foreword / 前言

===Original===
This edition of Jonas Lie’s *Short Fiction* was produced from two translations. “The Grey” was translated by *Littel’s Living Age* and published in 1879. “The Fisherman and the Draug,” “Jack of Sjöholm and the Gan-Finn,” “Tug of War,” “ ‘The Earth Draws,’ ” “The Cormorants of Andvaer,” “Isaac and the Parson of Brönö,” “The Wind-Gnome,” “The Huldrefish,” “Finn-Blood,” “The Homestead Westward in the Blue Mountains” and “ ‘It’s Me’ ” were translated by Robert Nisbet Bain and published in *Weird Tales from Northern Seas* in 1893.

===Chinese===
本版约纳斯·利（Jonas Lie）的《短篇集》由两种译本编成。《灰马》一篇由《利特尔氏活时代》（Littel’s Living Age）译出，1879 年发表。《渔夫与德劳格》《舍霍尔姆的杰克与甘-芬恩》《拔河》《“大地在拉扯”》《安德沃尔的鸬鹚》《艾萨克与布伦讷的牧师》《风精》《哈尔德鱼》《芬恩之血》《蓝山以西的农庄》与《“是我”》诸篇，均由罗伯特·尼斯贝特·贝恩（Robert Nisbet Bain）译出，1893 年收录于《北方诸海怪谈》（Weird Tales from Northern Seas）出版。
