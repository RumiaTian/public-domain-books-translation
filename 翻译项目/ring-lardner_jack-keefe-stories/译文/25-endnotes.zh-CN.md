## Endnotes / 尾注
===Original===

1. “Ee-yah!” was an exclamation frequently shouted by Detroit Tigers manager Hughey Jennings. It was a phrase so widely associated with him that fans in attendance at games would often yell it when he entered the field. —﻿S.E. Editor ↩︎

===Chinese===

「伊—呀！」是底特律老虎队经理休伊·詹宁斯（Hughey Jennings）常喊的一句口号。这句话同他关联太深，以致他每回进场，到场的球迷们也常常跟着齐声大喊。——S.E. 编者 ↩︎
