## XI / 十一

He Postures Among Chimney-Pots / 他在烟囱丛中装腔作势

### I / 一

===Original===
I met the Charterises in Genoa, just as I had planned. Anne’s first exclamation was, “Heavens, child, how dissipated you look! I would scarcely have known you.”

Charteris said nothing. But he and I lunched at the Isotta the following day, and at the conclusion of the meal the little man leaned back and lighted a cigarette.

===Chinese===
我按原定的计划，在热那亚（Genoa）同查特里斯夫妇碰了头。安妮开口第一声便是：「天哪，孩子，你瞧着多么一副放荡相！我险些儿认不得你了。」

查特里斯什么也没说。可第二天，他同我在伊索塔（Isotta）吃了午饭；饭罢，这小个子男人往椅背上一靠，点起一支香烟。

===Original===
“You must overlook my wife’s unfortunate tendency toward the most unamiable of virtues. But, after all, you are clamantly not quite the boy I left at Liverpool last October. Where are your Hardresses now?”

“In London for the season. And why is your wife rushing on to Paris, John?”

===Chinese===
「我妻子有个不幸的癖性，偏爱种种德行里最不讨人喜欢的那一种，你得多多包涵。话说回来，你如今分明已不大是我去年十月留在利物浦（Liverpool）的那个男孩子了。你那两位哈德雷斯眼下都在哪儿？」

「在伦敦过社交季呢。还有，约翰，你妻子干吗急着赶去巴黎？」

===Original===
“Shopping, as usual. Yes, I believe I did suggest it was as well to have it over and done with. Anne is very partial to truisms. Besides, she has an aunt there, you know. Take my advice, and always marry a woman who is abundantly furnished with attractive and visitable relations, for this precaution is the true secret of every happy marriage. We may, then, regard the Hardress incident as closed?”

“Oh, Lord, yes!” said I, emphatically.

===Chinese===
「买东西呗，照例。是啊，我想起来，确实是我提议的：这桩事不如趁早办完早了。安妮顶偏爱老生常谈。再说，她在那儿有位姑母，你是知道的。听我一句劝，娶妻总要娶一位备有大批讨人喜欢、又随时可以登门走动的亲戚的女人，因为这一层防备，才是一切美满婚姻的真正秘诀。那么，哈德雷斯这段公案，我们便可以看作了结了？」

「哦，老天，了结了！」我加重了语气说。

===Original===
“Well, after all, you have been sponging off them for a full year. The adjective is not ill-chosen, from what I hear. I fancy Mrs. Hardress has found you better company after she had mixed a few drinks for you, and so—But a truce to moral reflections! for I am desirous once more to hear the chimes at midnight. I hear Francine is in Milan?”

“There is at any rate in Milan,” said I, “a magnificent Gothic Cathedral of international reputation; and upon the upper gallery of its tower, as my guidebook informs me, there is a watchman with an efficient telescope. Should I fail to meet that watchman, John, I would feel that I had lived futilely. For I want both to view with him the Lombard plain, and to ask him his opinion of Cino da Pistoia, and as to what was in reality the middle name of Cain’s wife.”

===Chinese===
「嗯，话说回来，你到底在他们家白吃白住了整整一年。就我听来的情形说，这个形容词挑得并不算不当。我料想，哈德雷斯太太给你调过几杯酒下肚之后，便发觉你是个更可人意的小伙伴了，于是——罢了罢了，道德训诫到此为止！因为我不免又想再去听听午夜的钟声了。听说弗朗辛（Francine）眼下在米兰（Milan）？」

「不管怎样，米兰总归有一座名闻国际的宏伟哥特式大教堂；据我的旅行指南说，教堂塔身的上层平台上，有一名配着得力望远镜的守望人。约翰，我若见不上那位守望人一面，便会觉得自己这一辈子活得徒劳。因为我既要同他一道眺望伦巴第（Lombard）平原，又要请教他对皮斯托亚的奇诺（Cino da Pistoia）的看法，还要问一问该隐（Cain）的妻子中间名究竟是什么。」

### II / 二

===Original===
Francine proved cordial; but John Charteris was ever fickle, and not long afterward an Italian countess, classic in feature, but in coloring smacking of an artistic renaissance, had drawn us both to Switzerland, and thence to Liége. It was great fun, knocking about the Continent with John, for he knew exactly how to order a dinner, and spoke I don’t know how many languages, and seemed familiar with every side-street and back-alley in Europe. For myself, my French as acquired in Fairhaven appeared to be understood by everybody, but in replying very few of the natives could speak their own foolish language comprehensibly. I could rarely make head or tail out of what they were jabbering about.

I was alone that evening, because Annette’s husband had turned up unexpectedly; and Charteris had gone again to hear Nadine Neroni, the new prima donna, concerning whom he and his enameled Italian friend raved tediously. But I never greatly cared for music; besides, the opera that night was *Faust*; the last act of which in particular, when three persons align before the footlights and scream at the top of their voices, for a good half hour, about how important it is not to disturb anybody, I have never been able to regard quite seriously.

===Chinese===
弗朗辛待我们果然殷勤热络；无奈约翰·查特里斯素来没个长性，不多几时，一位眉眼古典、肤色却透着一股艺术复兴派气息的意大利伯爵夫人，便把我们两个一齐勾到了瑞士（Switzerland），又从那里转往列日（Liége）。同约翰一道在欧陆各地东游西荡，实在是件妙事：他点起菜来炉火纯青，会说天知道多少种语言，欧洲的每一条横街窄巷，他仿佛都熟门熟路。至于我自己，我在费尔黑文学来的那点法语，说出去人人听得懂；可轮到对方作答，本地人里却没有几个能把他们自己那门愚蠢的语言说得叫人明白。他们叽里咕噜讲些什么，我难得听出个头绪。

那天晚上我独自一个，因为安妮特（Annette）的丈夫出人意料地回来了；查特里斯呢，又去捧娜丁·内罗尼（Nadine Neroni）——新来的那位头牌女歌剧演员——的场去了；为了此人，他同他那位搪瓷脸的意大利朋友赞叹个没完，絮絮得腻死人。可我对音乐向来兴味不大；再说，那晚的戏码是《浮士德》（*Faust*）；尤其是最后一幕——三个人在脚灯前排成一列，扯开嗓门足足吼上半点钟，吼的无非是「切莫惊扰旁人」这件事何等要紧——这一幕，我从来没法子正眼相看。

===Original===
So I was spending this evening sedately in my own apartments at the Continental; and meanwhile I lisped in numbers that (or I flattered myself) had a Homeric tang; and at times chewed the end of my pencil meditatively. “From present indications,” I was considering, “that Russian woman is cooking something on her chafing-dish again. It usually affects them that way about dawn.”

I began on the next verse viciously, and came a cropper over the clash of two sibilants, as the distant clamour increased. “Brutes!” said I, disapprovingly. “Sere, clear, dear—Now they have finished, ‘*Jamais, monsieur*,’ and begun crying, ‘Fire!’ Oh, this would draw more than three souls out of a weaver, you know! Mere, near, hemisphere—no, but the Greeks thought it was flat. By Jove! I do smell smoke!”

===Chinese===
于是这天晚上，我便安安分分待在大陆旅馆（Hôtel Continental）自己的房间里消磨，一面咿呀吟哦着——至少我自命如此——颇有几分荷马（Homer）风味的诗行，一面时时若有所思地啃着铅笔头。「从种种迹象看，」我思忖道，「隔壁那个俄国女人又在她的暖锅上煮起什么东西来了。这一路人闹到天快亮时，照例总要这样来上一口。」

我恶狠狠地动手写下节，却因两个咝音相撞栽了跟头，这时远处的喧嚷愈闹愈高。「畜生！」我不以为然地说。「Sere、clear、dear——得，这会儿他们那声『绝无此事，先生』（*Jamais, monsieur*）总算吵完了，又改口喊起『着火啦！』哦，这阵仗，可真不止能把织布匠身上的三魂吵出窍来，你知道的！Mere、near、hemisphere——不对，希腊人倒以为大地是扁的。哎呀！我真闻见烟味了！」

===Original===
Wrapping my dressing-gown about me—I had afterward reason to thank the kindly fates that it was the green one with the white fleurs-de-lis, and not my customary, unspeakably disreputable bathrobe, scorched by the cigarette ashes of years—I approached the door and peeped out into the empty hotel corridor. The incandescent lights glimmered mildly through a gray haze which was acrid and choking to breathe; little puffs of smoke crept lazily out of the lift-shaft just opposite; and downstairs all Liége was shouting incoherently, and dragging about the heavier pieces of hotel furniture.

===Chinese===
我裹了裹晨衣——事后我有充分的理由感激好心的命运女神，亏得这是那件绣白鸢尾花的绿晨衣，而不是我那件常年被烟灰烫得疤疤瘌瘌、说不出的邋遢的旧浴袍——走到房门前，朝旅馆空荡荡的走廊里窥探出去。白炽灯的光透过一片灰蒙蒙的浓雾温和地闪烁，那雾又呛又噎人；一小股一小股的烟，正从正对面的电梯井里懒洋洋地往外爬；楼下，全列日的人都在语无伦次地喊叫，拖着旅馆里一件件沉重的家具乱转。

===Original===
“By Jove!” said I, and whistled a little disconsolately as I looked downward through the bars about the lift-shaft.

“Do you reckon,” spoke a voice—a most agreeable voice—“we are in any danger?”

===Chinese===
「哎呀！」我说，一面从电梯井四周的护栏间朝下张望，一面有点儿怏怏地吹了声口哨。

「依你看，」一个声音开口道——一个顶悦耳的声音——「咱们眼下有什么危险么？」

===Original===
The owner of the voice was tall; not even the agitation of the moment prevented my observing that, big as I am, her eyes were almost on a level with my shoulder. They were not unpleasant eyes, and a stray dream or two yet lingered under their heavy lids. The owner of the voice wore a strange garment that was fluffy and pink—pale pink like the lining of a seashell—and billows of white and the ends of various blue ribbons peeped out about her neck. I made mental note of the fact that disordered hair is not necessarily unbecoming; it sometimes has the effect of an unusually heavy halo set about the face of a half-awakened angel.

===Chinese===
这声音的主人身量很高；哪怕当此慌乱之际，我也不曾漏掉这一层观察：像我这样的大个子，她的眼睛却几乎同我的肩膀齐平。那并不是一双叫人讨厌的眼睛，沉沉的眼睑底下，还滞着一两个未醒尽的梦。声音的主人穿着一件毛茸茸的粉红色怪衣裳——淡粉色的，像贝壳的衬里——颈边探出一波一波的白纱和几根蓝缎带的梢头。我心里暗暗记下一桩事实：蓬乱的头发未必有损颜色；它有时竟像一圈格外厚重的光晕，环在一位半醒未醒的天使脸旁。

===Original===
“It would appear,” said I, meditatively, “that, in consideration of our being on the fifth floor, with the lift-shaft drawing splendidly, and the stairs winding about it—except the two lower flights, which have just fallen in—and in consideration of the fire department’s probable incompetence to extinguish anything more formidable than a tar-barrel—yes, it would appear, I think, that we might go further than ‘dangerous’ and find a less appropriate adjective to describe the situation.”

===Chinese===
「看起来，」我沉吟着说，「考虑到我们身在五层楼，电梯井拔风拔得正旺，楼梯绕着它盘旋——只是底下两段刚刚塌了——再考虑到消防队多半连柏油桶再威风些的东西都扑不灭——是的，我想，看起来，我们尽可以比『危险』更进一层，另找一个更不贴切的形容词来形容眼下的局面。」

===Original===
“You mean we cannot get down?” The beautiful voice was tremulous.

And my silence made reply.

“Well, then,” she suggested, cheerfully, after due reflection, “since we can’t go down, why not go up?”

===Chinese===
「你是说，我们下不去了？」那美妙的声音发起颤来。

而我的沉默替我作了回答。

「那么好吧，」她经过充分的斟酌之后，快快活活地提议道，「既然下不去，何不往上走呢？」

===Original===
As a matter of fact, nothing could be more simple. We were on the top floor of the hotel, and beside us, in the niche corresponding to the stairs below, was an iron ladder that led to a neatly-whitewashed trapdoor in the roof. Adopting her suggestion, I pushed against this trapdoor and found that it yielded readily; then, standing at the top of the ladder, I looked about me on a dim expanse of tiles and chimneys; yet farther off were the huddled roofs and gables of Liége, and just a stray glimpse of the Meuse; and above me brooded a clear sky and the naked glory of the moon.

===Chinese===
事实上，再没有比这更简单的了。我们正在旅馆顶层，身旁一个同楼下楼梯间上下相对的壁龛里，立着一道铁梯，直通屋顶一扇刷得雪白齐整的活动天窗。依着她的提议，我推了推那扇天窗，发觉它一推便开；随后我站在梯顶，环顾四周一片朦胧的瓦顶和烟囱；更远处，是列日挤挤挨挨的屋脊和山墙，还有偶然一瞥的默兹河（Meuse）；头顶上，则笼罩着一泓晴空，和月亮赤裸裸的荣光。

### III / 三

===Original===
I lowered my head with a distinct sigh of relief.

“I say,” I called, “it is infinitely nicer up here—superb view of the city, and within a minute’s drop of the square! Better come up.”

===Chinese===
我低下头来，分明舒了一口长气。

「我说，」我朝下喊，「这上头可惬意多了——全城景致一览无余，而且只消纵身一跳，不出一分钟便能落进广场！还是上来的好。」

===Original===
“Go first,” said she; and subsequently I held for a moment a very slender hand—a ridiculously small hand for a woman whose eyes were almost on a level with my shoulder—and we two stood together on the roof of the Hôtel Continental. We enjoyed, as I had predicted, an unobstructed view of Liége and of the square, wherein two toy-like engines puffed viciously and threw impotent threads of water against the burning hotel beneath us, and, at times, on the heads of an excited throng erratically clad.

But I looked down moodily, “That,” said I, as a series of small explosions popped like pistol shots, “is the café; and, oh, Lord! there goes the only decent Scotch in all Liége!”

===Chinese===
「你先上。」她说；随后我攥了一会儿一只十分纤细的手——对一个眼睛几乎同我肩膀齐平的女人来说，这手小得未免可笑——于是我们两个便并肩站在了大陆旅馆的屋顶上。正如我所预言，列日城和广场尽收眼底，毫无遮拦：广场上两台玩具似的救火机凶狠地噗噗喷着汽，朝我们脚下燃烧的旅馆泼去一股股绵软无力的细水线，时不时还浇在一片衣衫凌乱、群情激昂的人堆的头顶上。

可我闷闷地朝下望着。「那一处，」一串小爆炸噼啪作响、如同手枪开火时，我说，「是咖啡馆；还有，哦，老天！全列日唯一像样的苏格兰威士忌，这会儿也跟着遭殃了！」

===Original===
“There is Mamma!” she cried, excitedly; “there!” She pointed to a stout woman, who, with a purple shawl wrapped about her head, was wringing her hands as heartily as a birdcage, held in one of them, would permit. “And she has saved Bill Bryan!”

“In that case,” said I, “I suppose it is clearly my duty to rescue the remaining member of the family. You see,” I continued, in bending over the trapdoor and tugging at the ladder, “this thing is only about twenty feet long; but the kitchen wing of the hotel is a little less than that distance from the rear of the house behind it; and with this as a bridge I think we might make it. In any event, the roof will be done for in a half-hour, and it is eminently worth trying.” I drew the ladder upward.

===Chinese===
「妈妈在那儿呢！」她激动地喊道；「在那儿！」她指着一个健硕的妇人。那妇人头上裹着条紫披巾，正绞着双手，绞得那般起劲，全不顾其中一只手里还提着一只鸟笼。「她还救出了比尔·布莱恩（Bill Bryan）呢！」

「既然如此，」我说，「那把这一家剩下的成员救出来，分明便是我的本分了。」「你瞧，」我接着说，一面俯身探进天窗口，去拽那道梯子，「这玩意儿不过二十英尺上下；旅馆厨房那一翼，同它背后那座房子的后墙，相距比这还短那么一点儿；拿它当桥，我想我们过得去。横竖再过半个钟头，这屋顶就要完蛋，所以极值得一试。」我把梯子往上抽。

===Original===
Then I dragged this ladder down the gentle slant of the roof, through a maze of ghostly chimneys and dim skylights, to the kitchen wing, which was a few feet lower than the main body of the building. I skirted the chimney and stepped lightly over the eaves, calling, “Now then!” when a muffled cry, followed by a crash in the courtyard beneath, shook my heart into my mouth. I turned, gasping; and found the girl lying safe, but terrified, on the verge of the roof.

“It was a bucket,” she laughed, “and I stumbled over it—and it fell—and—and I nearly did—and I am frightened!”

===Chinese===
随后我拖着这梯子，顺着屋顶徐缓的斜坡往下走，穿过一片迷宫似的灰白烟囱和暗色天窗，一直走到厨房那一翼——那里比楼的主体低了几英尺。我绕过烟囱，轻步跨过屋檐，口中喊一声「这就来！」——便在此时，一声闷住的惊叫，紧跟着底下方院里一声重物砸落的脆响，把我的一颗心直掀进了嗓子眼。我回过身，直喘气；只见那姑娘好端端地伏在屋顶边上，人却吓了个半死。

「是一只水桶，」她笑道，「我让它绊了一跤——它掉下去了——我——我也差一点儿——我吓坏了！」

===Original===
And somehow I was holding her hand in mine, and my mouth was making irrelevant noises, and I was trembling. “It was close, but—look here, you must pull yourself together!” I pleaded; “because we haven’t, as it were, the time for airy badinage and repartee—just now.”

“I can’t,” she cried, hysterically. “Oh, I am so frightened! I can’t!”

“You see,” I said, with careful patience, “we must go on. I hate to seem too urgent, but we *must*, do you understand?” I waved my hand toward the east. “Why, look!” said I, as a thin tongue of flame leaped through the open trapdoor and flickered wickedly for a moment against the paling gray of the sky.

===Chinese===
也不知怎么的，我已把她的手握在自己手里，嘴上说着不相干的胡话，浑身发着抖。「方才真是千钧一发，可是——听着，你必须振作起来！」我央告道；「因为眼下的当口，咱们可以说还没工夫来轻飘飘地打趣斗嘴——至少现在没有。」

「我振作不了，」她歇斯底里地喊道。「哦，我吓坏了！我做不到！」

「你瞧，」我拿出十二分的小心与耐心，说，「我们必须继续走。我不愿显得逼人太甚，可是我们*必须*走，你明白么？」我朝东边挥了挥手。「喏，看！」我说，恰有一线细细的火舌从敞开的天窗口蹿出来，在渐渐泛白的灰色天幕上，恶狠狠地摇曳了一瞬。

===Original===
She saw and shuddered. “I’ll come,” she murmured, listlessly, and rose to her feet.

===Chinese===
她看见了，打了个寒战。「我这就来。」她恹恹地低声说，随即站起了身。

### IV / 四

===Original===
I heaved another sigh of relief, and waving her aside from the ladder, dragged it after me to the eaves of the rear wing. As I had foreseen, this ladder reached easily to the eaves of the house behind the rear wing, and formed a passable though unsubstantial-looking bridge. I regarded it disapprovingly.

“It will only bear one,” said I; “and we will have to crawl over separately after all. Are you up to it?”

===Chinese===
我又舒了一口长气，挥手让她从梯子边让开，自己拖着梯子，走到后面一翼的檐口。正如我所预料的，这梯子轻轻松松便搭上了后翼背后那座房子的屋檐，架成一座说得过去、只是瞧着不甚牢靠的桥。我朝它皱着眉打量。

「它一次只吃得住一个人的分量，」我说；「到头来我们还是得一个一个分开爬过去。你撑得住么？」

===Original===
“Please go first,” said she, very quiet. And, after gazing into her face for a moment, I crept over gingerly, not caring to look down into the abyss beneath.

Then I spent a century in impotence, watching a fluffy, pink figure that swayed over a bottomless space and moved forward a hair’s breadth each year. I made no sound during this interval. In fact, I do not remember drawing a really satisfactory breath from the time I left the hotel-roof, until I lifted a soft, faint-scented, panting bundle to the roof of the Councillor von Hollwig.
