## XII / 十二

He Faces Himself and Remembers / 他直面自己，忆起往事

### I / 一

===Original===
Into the lobby of the Hôtel d’Angleterre strolled, an hour later, a tall young man, in a green dressing-gown, and inquired for Charteris. The latter, in evening dress, was mournfully breakfasting in his new quarters.

===Chinese===
一个钟头之后，一个高个子青年踱进了英吉利旅馆（Hôtel d’Angleterre）的门厅，身上裹着一件绿色晨衣，开口打听查特里斯的下落。后者正一身晚礼服，在他新迁的下处愁眉苦脸地用着早餐。

===Original===
Charteris sprang to his feet. I saw, with real emotion, that he had been weeping; but now he was all flippancy. “My dear boy! I have just torn my hair and the rough drafts of several cablegrams on your account! Sit down at once, and try the bacon, since, for a wonder, it is not burnt—and, in passing, I had thought of course that you were.”

Instead, I took a drink, and went to sleep upon the nearest sofa.

===Chinese===
查特里斯一跃而起。我怀着真切的感动看出，他方才哭过；可这会儿他已满嘴轻佻。「亲爱的孩子！我刚刚为了你，扯断了自己的头发，还撕掉好几份电报的草稿！快坐下，尝尝这培根——难得地，居然没有烤糊——顺便说一句，我先前还理所当然地以为，你倒已经糊了呢。」

可我没有照办，只灌了一杯酒，便倒在最近的一张沙发上睡着了。

### II / 二

===Original===
I was very tired, but I awakened about noon and managed to procure enough clothes to make myself not altogether unpresentable to the public eye. Charteris had gone already about his own affairs, and I did not regret it, for I meant, without delay, to follow up my adventure of the night before.

===Chinese===
我疲惫不堪，可中午前后还是醒了过来，并且设法弄到了足够的衣物，让自己在世人眼里不至于全然不成体统。查特里斯早已出去料理他自己的事务，我并不引以为憾，因为我打算毫不耽搁，去追索昨夜那场奇遇的下文。

===Original===
But when I had come out of the Rue de la Casquette, and was approaching the statue of Gretry, I came upon a very ornately-dressed woman, who was about to enter an open carriage. I stared; and preposterous as it was, I knew that I was not mistaken. And I said aloud, “Signorina!”

===Chinese===
可当我从卡斯凯特街（Rue de la Casquette）出来，正走近格雷特里（Gretry）的雕像时，迎面撞见一位衣饰极为华艳的女人，正要登上一辆敞篷马车。我死死盯住她；尽管此事荒唐透顶，我却知道自己没有认错。于是我出了声：「小姐！」

===Original===
It was a long while before she said, “Don’t—don’t ever call me that again!” And since the world in general appeared just then to be largely flavoured with the irresponsibility of dreams, it did not surprise me that we were presently alone in somebody’s sitting-room.

===Chinese===
隔了好半晌，她才开口：「别——千万别再那样叫我了！」而由于那时候的整个世界，仿佛大体上都浸染着一股梦里才有的不负责任的滋味，所以没过多久，我们便已双双单独坐在某间客厅里，我竟也不觉得奇怪。

===Original===
“I have seen you twice in Liége,” she said. “I suppose this had to come about. I would have preferred to avoid it, though. Well! *che sarà!* You don’t care for music, do you? No—otherwise you would have known earlier that I am Nadine Neroni now.”

===Chinese===
「我在列日（Liége）见过你两回了，」她说。「我想，这一遭总是躲不过的。不过，我原本宁愿避开它。唉！*che sarà*——该怎样就怎样吧！你对音乐不上心，对不对？是啊——不然的话，你早该知道，我如今是娜丁·内罗尼（Nadine Neroni）了。」

===Original===
“Ah!” I said, very quietly. I had heard, as everybody had, a deal concerning the Neroni. “I think, if you will pardon me, I will not intrude upon Baron von Anspach’s hospitality any longer,” I said.

===Chinese===
「啊！」我说，说得非常平静。关于内罗尼其人其事，我同所有人一样，早听说过不少。「倘若你见谅，」我说，「我想我不便再叨扰冯·安斯巴赫男爵的殷勤款待了。」

===Original===
“That is unworthy of you—no, I mean it would have been unworthy of a boy we knew of.” There was a long pier-glass in these luxurious rooms. She led me to it now. “Look, Bobbie. We have altered a little, haven’t we? I at least, am unmistakable. ‘Their eyes are different, somehow,’ you remember. You haven’t changed as much—not outwardly. I think you are like Dorian Gray. Yes, as soon—as soon as I could afford it, I read every book you ever talked about, I think. It was damnably foolish of me. For I’ve heard things. And there was a girl I tried to help in London—an Agnes Faroy—”

===Chinese===
「这话有失你的身份——不，我是说，换作我们从前认识的那个男孩，是不屑说这句话的。」这几间奢华的屋子里立着一面长长的穿衣镜。她此刻便把我领到镜前。「看啊，博比。我们都有几分变了样，是不是？至少我这个人，如今是一眼便知了。『她们的眼睛总有些不一样，』你还记得吧。你变得没有我厉害——至少外表上没有。我看你像道林·格雷（Dorian Gray）。是啊，一等我——一等我付得起书钱，我就把你谈起过的每一本书都找来读了，我想一本不落。我这人真是蠢得该死。因为我听说了种种事情。在伦敦还有个姑娘，我试图帮衬过她——一个阿格尼丝·法罗伊——」

===Original===
“Ah!” I said.

===Chinese===
「啊！」我说。

===Original===
“She had your picture even then, poor creature. She kissed it just before she died. She didn’t know that I had ever heard of you. She never knew. Oh, how *could* you!” the Neroni said, with something very like a sob, “Or were you always—just that, at bottom?”

===Chinese===
「那时候她就已经藏着你的画像了，可怜的人。咽气之前，她还吻了它。她不知道我听说过你。她始终不知道。哦，你*怎么能*这样！」内罗尼说，嗓音里带着一样极似呜咽的东西，「还是说，你从头到尾——骨子里，便是那么一个人？」

===Original===
“And have you ever noticed, Mademoiselle Neroni, that every one of us is several people? In consequence I must confess to have been wondering—?”

===Chinese===
「内罗尼小姐，你可曾留意过，我们每一个人，其实都是好几个人合成的？因此我不得不承认，我方才一直在纳闷——？」

===Original===
“Well! I wasn’t. You won’t believe it now, perhaps. And it doesn’t matter, anyhow.” Her grave voice lifted and upon a sudden was changed. “Bobbie, when you had gone I couldn’t stand it! I couldn’t let you ruin your life for me, but I could not go on as I had done before—Oh, well, you’ll never understand,” she added, wearily. “But Von Anspach had always wanted me to go with him. So I wrote to him, at the Embassy. And after all, what is the good of talking—now!”

===Chinese===
「哼！我就不是好几个人。如今你多半不肯信了。不过横竖也不要紧。」她庄重的嗓音扬了起来，倏忽变了腔调。「博比，你走了以后，我受不住了！我不能眼睁睁看你为我毁掉一生，可我又无法再像从前那样过下去——唉，罢了，你永远不会懂的，」她疲惫地补了一句。「可是冯·安斯巴赫，他一向是要我跟他走的。于是我给他写了信，寄到使馆。说到底，事到如今，还谈这些有什么用！」

===Original===
We two were curiously quiet. “No, I suppose there is no good in talking now.” We stood there, as yet, hand in hand. The mirror was candid. “Oh, Signorina, I want to laugh as God laughs, and I cannot!”

===Chinese===
我们两个出奇地沉默。「是啊，我看事到如今，谈什么都是枉然。」我们至今还手牵着手站在那里。镜子是坦白的。「哦，小姐，我多想像上帝那样大笑一场，却怎么也笑不出！」

### III / 三

===Original===
But I lack the heart to set down all that brief and dreary talk of ours. How does it matter what we said? We two at least knew, even as we talked, that all we said meant in the outcome, nothing. Yet we talked awhile and spoke, I think, quite honestly.

===Chinese===
可是我狠不下心，把我们那番简短而凄凉的谈话一一记下来。我们说了些什么，又有什么要紧？至少我们两个，就连说着的时候也明白：我们说的一切，到头来都算不得数。可我们还是谈了一阵，而且谈得，我想，相当坦白。

===Original===
She was not unhappy; and there were inbred Lichfeldian traditions which prompted me to virtuous indignation over her defects in remorse and misery. There were my memories, too.

===Chinese===
她并不显得不幸；而利奇菲尔德那些生在骨血里的传统，又怂恿我义正词严地愤慨一番——愤慨她竟不知悔恨、也不知愁苦。何况，我还有自己的那些记忆呢。

===Original===
“I don’t sing very well, of course, but then I’m not dependent on my singing, you know. Oh, why not be truthful? And Von Anspach always sees to it I get the tendered of criticism—in print. And, moreover, I’ve a deal put by. I’m a miser, *he* says, and I suppose I am, because I know what it is to be poor. So when the rainy day comes—as of course it will—I’ll have quite enough to purchase a serviceable umbrella. Meanwhile, I have pretty much everything I want. People talk of course, but it is only on the stage they ever drive you out into a snowstorm. Besides, they don’t talk to *me*.”

===Chinese===
「当然，我唱得算不得顶好，不过你也知道，我又不指着嗓子糊口。唉，干吗不说实话呢？反正冯·安斯巴赫自有安排，叫我到手的批评永远是最温柔的——还见诸白纸黑字。再说，我也攒下了不小的一笔。他叫我守财奴，*他*就是这么叫的，我想我确实是，因为我尝过穷的滋味。所以等雨天来——它当然要来的——我攒的钱，足够买一把结实顶用的伞了。眼下呢，我想要的东西差不多都已到手。人们自然要说闲话，可是把你赶进暴风雪里去的那种事，只有戏台上才有。再说，他们也犯不着当着*我*的面讲。」

===Original===
In fine, I found that the Neroni was a very different being from Miss Montmorenci. …

===Chinese===
总而言之，我发觉内罗尼与当年的蒙特莫伦西小姐相比，已是判然两种生命。……

### IV / 四

===Original===
Then I left her. I had not any inclination just now to pursue my fair Elena. Rather I sat alone in my new bedroom, thinking, confusedly, first of Amelia Van Orden, and how I danced with her a good eight years ago; of that woman who had come to me in remote Fairhaven, coming through the world’s gutter, unsullied—because that much I yet believe, although I do not know. … She may have been always the same, even in the old days when Lichfield thought her “fast,” and she was more or less “compromised,”—and years before I met her, a blind, inexperienced boy. Only she may then have been a better actress than I suspected. … I thought, in any event, of those execrable rhymes that likened her to the Lady in *Comus*, moving serene and unafraid among a rabble of threatening bestial shapes; and I thought of the woman who would, by this time, be with Von Anspach.

===Chinese===
随后我便离开了她。此刻我毫无兴致去追我那位美丽的埃莱娜（Elena）。我倒是独自坐在自己新居的卧室里，心绪纷乱地想着，先想起阿梅莉亚·范·奥登，想起足足八年前我同她共舞的光景；想起那个来到偏远的费尔黑文、寻到我面前的女人——她从世间的阴沟里一路走来，却不曾被玷污——因为这一层我至今相信，尽管我并不能确知。……说不定她从来就是这般人物，早在利奇菲尔德当年议论她「轻佻」、她多少已算「坏了名声」的岁月里就是——而那还是许多年之前，我遇见她的时候，不过是个瞎了眼的、毫无阅历的男孩。只不过，她那时的做戏功夫，兴许比我所疑心的还要高明。……不管怎样，我想起了那些把她比作《科玛斯》中那位贵妇的拙劣歪诗——说她安详自若、了无惧色地穿行在一群面目狰狞的兽形怪物中间；我又想起了这个女人——到这个时辰，她该已依在冯·安斯巴赫身边了。

===Original===
For here again were inbred Lichfieldian traditions of the sort I rarely dare confess to, even to myself, because they are so patently hidebound and ridiculous. These traditions told me that this woman, whom I had loved, was Von Anspach’s harlot. I might—and I did—endeavor to be ironical and to be broadminded and to be up-to-date about the whole affair, and generally to view the matter through the sophisticated eyes of the author of *The Apostates*, that Robert Etheridge Townsend who was a connoisseur of ironies and human foibles; but these futilities did no good at all. Lichfield had got at and into me when I was too young to defend myself; and I could no more alter the inbred traditions of Lichfield, that were a part of me, than a carpet could change its texture. My traditions merely told me that the dear woman whom I remembered had come—in fleeing from discomforts which were unbearable, if that mattered—to be Von Anspach’s harlot: and finding her this, my traditions declined to be the least bit broadminded. In Lichfield such women were simply not respectable; nor could you get around that fact by going to Liége.

===Chinese===
因为这里又冒出了利奇菲尔德那些生在骨血里的传统——这一路货色，我连对自己都极少敢承认，因为它们明摆着又顽固又可笑。这些传统告诉我：这个我爱过的女人，是冯·安斯巴赫的姘妇。我尽可以——我也确实——竭力就这整桩事端冷嘲几句、放达几分、摩登一点，总而言之，搬出《背教者》作者那双阅尽世故的眼睛来看待此事——那位罗伯特·埃瑟里奇·汤森（Robert Etheridge Townsend），一位反讽与人情弱点的鉴赏家；可这些无用功半点用处也没有。利奇菲尔德在我年纪尚幼、无力自卫的时候，便俘获了我、也钻进了我的体内；我再也改变不了利奇菲尔德那些与生俱来的传统——它们已是我身上的一部分——正如一张地毯改不了自己的质地。我的传统只是告诉我：我记得的那个可亲的女人，如今沦落成了冯·安斯巴赫的姘妇——她原是为躲避那不堪忍受的不适才走到这一步的，倘若这也算得一个缘由——而见她落到这般田地，我的传统断然拒绝拿出半分宽宏。在利奇菲尔德，这样的女人干脆就是不体面；你就是跑到列日去，也绕不开这个事实。

===Original===
There was in the room a *Matin*, which contained a brief account of the burning of the Continental, and a very lengthy one of the Neroni’s appearance the night before. Drearily, to keep from thinking, I read a deal concerning *la gracieuse cantatrice américaine*. Whether or not she had made a fool of me with histrionics in Fairhaven, there was no doubt that she had chosen wisely in forsaking Lethbury, and the round of village “Opera Houses.” She had chosen, after all, and precisely as I had done, to make the most of youth while it lasted; and she appeared, just now, to harvest prodigally.

===Chinese===
房间里有一份《晨报》（*Matin*），上头有一则关于大陆旅馆（the Continental）焚毁的简讯，还有一大篇关于内罗尼前夜登台的报道。为了不去思想，我恹恹地读了好多关于那位「风姿曼妙的美国女歌唱家」（*la gracieuse cantatrice américaine*）的文字。她在费尔黑文是否曾用做戏的本事耍弄过我，姑且不论；但她舍弃莱思伯里、舍弃乡间「歌剧院」的巡回演出，无疑选择得聪明。说到底，她选的正是我选的那条路：趁青春尚在，把青春用足；而此时此刻，她看来正在丰盛得近乎挥霍地坐收其成。

===Original===
“*On jouait Faust*,” I read, “*et jamais le célèbre personnage de Goethe n’adore plus exquise Gretchen. Miss Nadine Neroni est, en effet, une idéale Marguerite à la taille bien prise, au visage joli éclairé des deux yeux grands et doux. Et lorsqu’elle commença à chanter, ce fut un véritable ravissement: sa voix se fit l’interprète rêvée de la divine musique de Gounod, tandis que sa personne et son coeur incarnaient physiquement et moralement l’héroine de Goethe*.” …

===Chinese===
「当晚演的是《浮士德》（*Faust*），」我读到，「歌德笔下那位千古名角，从来不曾爱慕过比这更精致的格蕾琴（Gretchen）。娜丁·内罗尼小姐确是一位理想的玛格丽特（Marguerite）：身段娉婷匀称，一张俏脸被那双又大又温柔的眼睛映照得熠熠生辉。而当她启口歌唱，那真是一场名副其实的销魂：她的嗓音成了古诺（Gounod）神妙音乐梦寐以求的诠释者，而她的身与心，又在形骸与灵魂两面，双双化作了歌德笔下的女主人公。」……

===Original===
And so on, for Von Anspach had “seen to it,” prodigally. And “Oh, well!” I thought; “if everybody else is so extravagantly pleased, what in heaven’s name is the use of my being squeamish? Besides, she is only doing what I am doing, and getting all the pleasure out of life that is possible. She and I are very sensible people. At least, I suppose we are. I wonder, though? Meanwhile, I had better go and look for that preposterously beautiful Elena. And a fig for the provincial notions of Lichfield, that are poisoning me with their nonsense! and for the notions of Fairhaven, too, I suppose—”

===Chinese===
诸如此类，不一而足——因为冯·安斯巴赫早已「打点妥当」，而且打点得挥霍无比。「唉，也罢！」我想；「既然旁人个个都这般兴高采烈，我又何苦偏要假正经，这算图的哪一门呢？再说，她干的不过是我在干的事，不过把人生里捞得着的快活都捞个干净。她和我都是顶顶明白的人。至少，我猜是吧。可到底是不是，难说得很！眼前呢，我还是去找那位美得不像话的埃莱娜为妙。利奇菲尔德那套外省见识，见它的鬼去吧——净拿些胡说八道来毒害我！费尔黑文的见识，我想也一并见鬼去吧——」

### V / 五

===Original===
Then Charteris came into the room. “John,” said I, “this is a truly remarkable world, and only hypercriticism would venture to suggest that it is probably conducted by an inveterate humourist. So lend me that pocket-piece of yours, and we will permit chance to settle the entire matter. That is the one intelligent way of treating anything which is really serious. You probably believe I am Robert Etheridge Townsend, but as a matter of fact, I am Hercules in the allegory. So! the beautiful lady or America? Why, the eagle flutters uppermost, and from every mountain side let praises ring. Accordingly I am off.”

===Chinese===
随后查特里斯进了屋。「约翰，」我说，「这真是个了不起的世界，只有吹毛求疵之徒，才敢冒昧进言，说它多半是由一位积习难改的幽默家掌管着的。所以，把你那枚随身硬币借我，咱们让运气来裁决这整桩事情。对待任何真正严肃的事，这是唯一聪明的法子。你多半以为我是罗伯特·埃瑟里奇·汤森，可事实上，我是那则寓言里的赫拉克勒斯。来吧！美人，还是美利坚？——瞧，鹰徽翱翔在上，愿颂歌从每一面山崖上响起来。于是我动身在即。」

===Original===
“And you will cross half the world,” said Charteris, “in the green dressing-gown, or in the coat which Byam borrowed for you this morning? I do not wish to seem inquisitive, you understand—”

===Chinese===
「那么你打算，」查特里斯说，「就穿着这件绿色晨衣、或者今早拜亚姆（Byam）替你借来的这件外衣，横跨半个世界喽？我并不想显得多管闲事，你明白的——」

===Original===
“No, I believe I am through with borrowed coats—as with yours, for instance. But I am quite ready to go in my own dressing-gown if necessary—”

===Chinese===
「不，我相信我从此不穿借来的外衣了——包括你那一件，比如说。不过真到必要的时候，我穿自己的晨衣照样动身——」

===Original===
I wheeled at the door.

“By the way, I am done with you, John. I am fond of you, and all that, and I sincerely admire my chimney-pot coquette—of whom you haven’t heard—but, after all, there are real people yonder. And by God, even after two years of being pickled in alcohol and chasing after women that are quite used to being chased—well, even now I am one of those real people. So I am done with you and this perpetual making light of things—!”

===Chinese===
我在门口转过身来。

「顺便说一句，我同你也到此为止了，约翰。我喜欢你，诸如此类的话自然还有；我也真心倾慕我那位大礼帽底下卖弄风情的姑娘（chimney-pot coquette）——这人你还没听说过——可是说到底，大洋那头有的是活生生的人。而且老天在上，哪怕在酒里泡了两年、追了两年早被人追惯了的女人——喏，即便到如今，我也还是那活生生的人里头的一个。所以我同你、同这套没完没了的游戏人间，一并到此为止——！」

===Original===
“The Declaration of Independence,” Charteris observed, “is undoubtedly the best thing in imaginative literature that we Americans have as yet accomplished; but I am sufficiently familiar with it, thank you, and I find, with age, that only the more untruthful platitudes are endurable. Oh, I predicted for you, at our first meeting, a life without achievements but of gusto! Now, it would appear, you plan to prance among an interminable saturnalia of the domestic virtues. So be it! but I warn you that the house of righteousness is but a wayside inn upon the road to being a representative citizen.”

===Chinese===
「《独立宣言》，」查特里斯评道，「无疑是我们美国人迄今在想象文学中成就的最高杰作；只是我对它已经熟得不能再熟了，谢谢，而且我发觉，人上了年纪，只有那些更不老实的陈词滥调还受得住。哦，我们头一回见面，我就为你预言过一生：没有功业，只有兴致！如今看来，你是打算到那场没完没了的家常德行的狂欢里去扬蹄撒欢了。由它去吧！只是我警告你：正义之宅，不过是通往做一个模范公民的路上，一家借宿的路边客栈。」

===Original===
“You are talking nonsense,” I rapped out—“and immoral nonsense.”

“It is very strange,” John Charteris complained, “how so many of us manage to reduce everything to a question of morality—that is, to the alternative of being right or wrong. Now a man’s personality, as somebody or other very properly observes, has many parts besides the moral area; and the intelligent, the artistic, even the religious part, need not necessarily have anything to do with ethics—”

===Chinese===
「你在胡说八道，」我厉声道——「而且是不道德的胡说八道。」

「真是咄咄怪事，」约翰·查特里斯抱怨道，「我们这么许多人，怎么总有本事把一切事情都归结成一个道德问题——也就是说，归结成对与错的两难抉择。要知道，一个人的个性，正如某位什么人很中肯地说过的，除了道德这一区，还有许多别的区域；而智识的那一区、艺术的那一区、甚至宗教的那一区，未必就非得同伦理沾亲带故——」

===Original===
“Ah, yes,” said I, “so there is a train at noon—”

“And a virtuous man,” continued Charteris, amicably, “is no more the perfect type of humanity than an intellectual man. In fact, the lowest and certainly the most disagreeable type of all troublesome people is that which combines an immaculate past with a limited understanding. The religious tenets of this class consist of an unshakable belief that the Bible was originally written in English, and contains nothing applicable to any of the weekdays. And in consequence—”

===Chinese===
「啊，对了，」我说，「中午正好有一班火车——」

「再说，有德之士，」查特里斯和颜悦色地接着说，「也并不比智识之士更够格当人类的完美典型。事实上，在一切招人头疼的角色里头，最低劣、无疑也最讨人嫌的一种，便是把一尘不染的过去，配上一副狭隘有限的见识。这一流人的宗教信条，不外是一条不可动摇的信念：圣经原文是用英文写成的，而且书中没有一个字，适用于一个礼拜里任何一个平日的言行。因此之故——」

===Original===
I left him mid-course in speech. “Words, words!” said I; and it appeared to me for the moment that words were of astonishingly trivial import, however carefully selected, which was in me a wholesome, although fleet, apostacy of yesterday’s creed. And I sent a cablegram to Bettie Hamlyn.

===Chinese===
他话说到一半，我便撇下了他。「字眼，字眼！」我说；那一刻我忽然觉得，字眼这东西——无论挑选得多么精心——分量也轻薄得惊人；这在我身上，是对昨日信条的一场有益的、尽管转瞬即逝的叛教。随后，我给贝蒂·哈姆林发了一封电报。

### VI / 六

===Original===
It was on the trip homeward I first met with Celia Reindan. I then considered her a silly little nuisance. …

===Chinese===
正是在归途之上，我头一回遇见了塞莉娅·赖因丹（Celia Reindan）。那时我把她看作一个傻乎乎的小讨嫌。……

===Original===
For I crossed the Atlantic in a contained fury of repentance for the wasted months. I had achieved nothing that was worthy of me, and presently I would be dead. Why, I might die within the five minutes! I might never see the lagging minute-hand of my little traveling clock pass that next numeral, say! The thought obsessed me, especially at night. Once, in a panic, I rose from my berth, and pushed the minute-hand forward a half-hour. “Now, I have tricked You!” I said, aloud; for nervously I was footing a pretty large bill. At twenty-three one has the funds wherewith to balance these accounts. …

===Chinese===
因为我横渡大西洋的整整一路，都憋着一腔按捺住的悔恨，为那白白虚掷的月份。我不曾成就一件配得上我本人的事，而不久我就要死了。嗐，说不定五分钟之内我就要死了！说不定我再也看不见我那只旅行小钟上磨磨蹭蹭的分针，爬过下一个数字了，你瞧！这个念头缠住了我，尤其是在夜里。有一回，我在一阵恐慌中从铺位上爬起来，把分针往前拨了半个钟头。「这下，我可骗过你了！」我出声说道；因为我在神经上正偿付着一笔相当可观的账单。二十三岁的人，手里还有的是本钱，来平衡这些账目。……

===Original===
I wanted to live normally—to live as these persons thick about me, who seemed to grow up, and mate, and beget, and die, in the incurious fashion of oxen. I wanted to think only from hand to mouth, to think if possible not at all, and to be guided always in the conduct of my life by gross and obvious truisms, so that I must be judged at last but as one of the herd. “And what is accustomed—what holds of familiar usage—had come to seem the whole essence of wisdom, on all subjects”; for I wanted just the sense of companionship, irrevocable and eternal and commonly shared with every one of my kind. And yonder was Bettie Hamlyn. … “Oh, make a man of me, Bettie! just a common man!”

===Chinese===
我要过正常的日子——像密密围在我四周的这些人那样过活，他们仿佛就是以牛群那种不闻不问的方式，长大，配偶，生养，然后死去。我只想今天不想明天地活着，能不想就索性不想，一生的行止一律听凭几条粗浅现成的大道理指引，好叫我到头来，只被当作畜群里寻常的一头。「凡属习以为常的——凡属相沿惯熟的——便是一切事情上智慧的全部精髓」；因为我想要的，不过是一种有人作伴之感，它不可反悔、地久天长，与我同类的每一个人所共有。而大洋那头，就有个贝蒂·哈姆林。……「哦，把我做成人吧，贝蒂！一个普普通通的人就成！」

===Original===
And Bettie might have done it, one considers, even then, for I was astir with a new impetus. Now, with a grin, the Supernal Aristophanes slipped the tiniest temptation in my way; to reach Fairhaven I was compelled to spend some three hours of an April afternoon in Lichfield, where upon Regis Avenue was to be met, in the afternoon, everyone worth meeting in Lichfield; and Stella drove there on fine afternoons, under the protection of a trim and preternaturally grave tiger; and the afternoon was irreproachable.

===Chinese===
而贝蒂兴许当真做得到这一点，细想起来，即便在那时也做得到，因为我正蠢动着一股新的劲头。这不，天上那位阿里斯托芬咧嘴一笑，往我路上塞来一枚顶细小的诱惑：要到费尔黑文去，我就不得不在利奇菲尔德消磨一个四月下午的三个来钟头；而在利奇菲尔德，一到下午，凡是值得结识的人物，在里吉斯大道（Regis Avenue）上保准遇得着；晴好的午后，斯特拉也总在那里兜风，由一名装束利落、老成得超乎自然的小马夫随车护卫；何况那天下午，又是一个无可指摘的下午。

### VII / 七

===Original===
By the way she looked back over her shoulder, I knew that Stella had not recognized me. I stood with a yet lifted hat, irresolute.

===Chinese===
从她回头越过肩头望过来的那副神气，我知道斯特拉没有认出我。我举着尚未放下的帽子，立在原地，踌躇不决。

===Original===
“By Jove!” said I, in my soul, “then the Blagdens are in Lichfield! Why, of course! they always come here after Lent. And Bettie would not mind; to call on them would be only courteous; and besides, Bettie need not ever know. And moreover, I was always very fond of Peter.”

===Chinese===
「天哪！」我在心里说，「这么说，布拉格登一家到利奇菲尔德来了！嗐，可不是嘛！每年大斋期一过，他们总要上这儿来的。再说贝蒂也不会见怪；登门拜访一回，不过是尽个礼数；何况，贝蒂永远不必知道。再者说，我一向顶喜欢彼得来着。」

===Original===
So the next afternoon but four, Stella was making tea for me. …

===Chinese===
于是，四天之后的那个下午，斯特拉便已在为我沏茶了。……
