## XVI / 十六

He Seeks for Copy / 他寻觅写作素材

### I / 一

===Original===
I had aforetime ordered Mr. George Bulmer to read *The Apostates*, and, as the author of this volume explained, from motives that were purely well-meaning. Tonight I was superintending the process.

===Chinese===
我早先便吩咐过乔治·布尔默先生研读《背教者》——照本书作者的申说，这纯粹出于一片好意。今晚，我正在监督这一工程。

===Original===
“For the scene of the book is the Green Chalybeate,” said I; “and it may be my masterly rhetoric will so far awaken your benighted soul, Uncle George, as to enable you to perceive what the more immediate scenery is really like. Why, think of it! what if you should presently fall so deeply in love with the adjacent mountains as to consent to overlook the deficiencies of the more adjacent café! Try now, nunky! try hard to think that the right verb is really more important than the right vermouth! and you have no idea what good it may do you.”

===Chinese===
「因为这书的场景便是绿铁泉，」我说；「没准儿我这一手雄辩的说辞，足以把您那蒙昧的灵魂唤醒到这般田地，乔治叔叔，教您看清近在眼前的这片风景究竟是什么模样。嗐，想想看！万一您转眼之间便爱上了近旁的群山，爱到肯宽宥那更近一旁的咖啡馆的种种欠缺，那该多好！来试试吧，老叔！使劲想一想：挑对一个动词，当真比挑对一味味美思更要紧！这对您能有多大的好处，您可想不出来。」

===Original===
Mr. Bulmer read on, with a bewildered face, while I gently stirred the contents of my tall and delectably odored glass. It was “frosted” to a nicety. We were drinking “Mamie Taylors” that summer, you may remember; and I had just brought up a pitcherful from the bar.

===Chinese===
布尔默先生满脸茫然，只管往下读；我则轻轻搅动着高脚杯里香气袭人的内容。杯身的「霜」结得恰到好处。你或许记得，那年夏天我们喝的是「玛米·泰勒」（Mamie Taylor）；我刚从酒吧提了满满一壶上来。

===Original===
“Oh, I say, you know!” observed Uncle George, as he finished the sixth chapter, and flung down the book.

===Chinese===
「哦，我说，你可知！」乔治叔叔读罢第六章，把书一摔，如此评曰。

===Original===
“Rot, utter rot,” I assented pleasantly; “puerile and futile trifling with fragments of the seventh commandment, as your sturdy common sense instantly detected. In fact,” I added, hopefully, “I think that chapter is trivial enough to send the book into a tenth edition. In *Afield*, you know, I tried a different tack. Actuated by the noblest sentiments, the heroine mixes prussic acid with her father’s whiskey and water; and ‘Old-Fashioned’ and ‘Fair Play’ have been obliging enough to write to the newspapers about this harrowing instance of the deplorably low moral standards of today. Uncle George, do you think that a real lady is ever justified in obliterating a paternal relative? You ought to meditate upon that problem, for it is really a public question nowadays. Oh, and there was a quite lovely clipping last week I forgot to show you—all about Electra, as contrasted with Jonas Chuzzlewit, and my fine impersonal attitude, and the survival of the fittest, and so on.”

===Chinese===
「胡扯，一派胡扯，」我愉快地附和道；「拿第七诫的残片拼小儿科的无用文章，正如您那副健全的常识当场识破的那样。事实上，」我满怀希冀地补上一句，「我觉得那一章无聊得足以送这本书进第十版了。在《旷野》里，你知道，我试的是另一条路数。女主人公在一腔最崇高的情操驱使之下，往她父亲的掺水威士忌里调了氢氰酸；『老古板』（Old-Fashioned）和『公道人』（Fair Play）两位还承情肯写，投书报纸，就这桩今日道德水准低落到可叹的骇人实例大发议论。乔治叔叔，您以为一位真正的淑女，究竟可有道理把一位父系亲眷抹除掉？您真该把这道题目参上一参，因为它如今当真是个公共问题。哦，对了，上礼拜还有一段顶可爱的剪报，我忘了给您看——通篇讲的是厄勒克特拉（Electra），拿来同乔纳斯·朱述尔维特（Jonas Chuzzlewit）两相对照，还讲我那套漂亮的不偏不倚的态度，讲适者生存，等等等等。」

===Original===
But Uncle George refused to be comforted. “Look here, Bob!” said he, pathetically, “why don’t you brace up and write something—well! we’ll put it, something of the sort you *can* do. For you can, you know.”

===Chinese===
可乔治叔叔不肯领受这份宽慰。「听我说，鲍勃！」他可怜巴巴地说，「你干吗不振作起来，写一点——嗯！我们姑且这么措辞吧——写一点你*办得到*的那类东西。因为你是办得到的，你知道。」

===Original===
“Ah, but is not a judicious nastiness the market-price of a second edition before publication?” I softly queried. “I had no money. I was ashamed to beg, and I was too well brought up to steal anything adroitly enough not to be caught. And so, in view of my own uncle’s deafness to the prayers of an impecunious orphan, I have descended to this that I might furnish butter for my daily bread.” I refilled my glass and held the sparkling drink for a moment against the light. “This time next year,” said I, as dreamily, “I shall be able to afford cake; for I shall have written *As the Coming of Dawn*.”

===Chinese===
「啊，可是书未出版，一番斟酌得宜的下流笔墨，难道不正是确保第二版的市场行情么？」我轻声问道。「我当时没有钱。乞讨，我羞于启齿；偷窃，我受的教养又太好，偷得不够利落，免不了束手就擒。于是，念及亲叔叔对一个不名一文的孤儿的祷告充耳不闻，我便堕落到这一步，好歹给我每日的面包挣出涂的黄油来。」我重新斟满酒杯，把那杯泛泡的酒对着灯光端详了片刻。「明年今日，」我如入梦境似的说，「我便享用得起蛋糕了；因为到那时，我已写成《恰似黎明降临》（*As the Coming of Dawn*）。」

===Original===
Mr. Bulmer sniffed, and likewise refilled his glass. “You catch me lending you any money for your—brief Biblical words!” he said.

===Chinese===
布尔默先生嗤了一声，也同样斟满自己的杯子。「休想叫我再借钱给你，供你去干那号——用圣经里简短的字眼来讲——的事！」他说。

===Original===
“For the reign of subtle immorality,” I sighed, “is well-nigh over. Already the augurs of the pen begin to wink as they fable of a race of men who are evilly scintillant in talk and gracefully erotic. We know that this, alas, cannot be, and that in real life our peccadilloes dwindle into dreary vistas of divorce cases and the police-court, and that crime has lost its splendour. We sin very carelessly—sordidly, at times—and artistic wickedness is rare. It is a pity; life was once a scarlet volume scattered with misty-coated demons; it is now a yellow journal, wherein our vices are the hackneyed formulas of journalists, and our virtues are the not infrequent misprints. Yes, it is a pity!”

===Chinese===
「因为精微不道德的统治，」我叹道，「已近尾声了。执笔的占卜官们一面编着神话，说有那么一族男人，谈吐邪恶而流光溢彩、情致旖旎而不失风雅，一面已经开始彼此递眼色了。我们知道，唉，这等人物是断乎不能有的；在真实生活里，我们那些小小的罪过，缩成了离婚案子与治安法庭的一幅幅阴郁远景，罪恶也失掉了它的华彩。我们犯罪犯得非常漫不经心——有时还失之肮脏——艺术性的邪恶已成罕物。真可惜；生活曾是一部猩红的书卷，散落着雾衣幢幢的魔鬼；如今它成了一份黄色小报，我们的恶行是报人们用滥的套语，我们的美德倒是不时出现的排印错误。是啊，真可惜！」

===Original===
“Dearest Robert!” remonstrated Mr. Bulmer, “you are sadly passé: that pose is of the Beardsley period and went out many magazines ago.”

===Chinese===
「最亲爱的罗伯特！」布尔默先生规劝道，「你落伍得可悲：这副做派是比亚兹莱（Beardsley）时期的产物，早在好几本杂志之前就过时了。」

===Original===
“The point is well taken,” I admitted, “for our life of today is already reflected—faintly, I grant you—in the best-selling books. We have passed through the period of a slavish admiration for wickedness and wide margins; our quondam decadents now snigger in a parody of primeval innocence, and many things are forgiven the latter-day poet if his botany be irreproachable. Indeed, it is quite time; for we have tossed over the contents of every closet in the ménage à trois. And I—*moi, qui vous parle*—I am wearied of hansom-cabs and the flaring lights of great cities, even as so alluringly depicted in *Afield*; and henceforth I shall demonstrate the beauty of pastoral innocence.”

===Chinese===
「此言有理，」我承认道，「因为我们今日的生活，已经——我姑且认了，淡淡地——映现在那些畅销书里了。对邪恶与宽大留白的奴隶式崇拜，那个时期我们已然度过；昔日那些颓废派，如今顶着一脸太初无邪的滑稽模仿相嗤嗤窃笑；而晚近的诗人，只要他的植物学无懈可击，许多事情都可获宽宥。的确，也到时候了；因为三人共居之家（ménage à trois）里每一口衣柜的底细，我们都已抖搂净尽。而我——在下（*moi, qui vous parle*）——我已腻烦了双轮马车和大都市灼灼的灯火，正如《旷野》里所描画得那般诱人的一切；从今往后，我要去阐扬田园无邪之美了。」

===Original===
“Saul among the prophets,” Uncle George suggested, helpfully.

“Quite so,” I assented, “and my first prophecy will be *As the Coming of Dawn*.”

===Chinese===
「扫罗也列在先知中了，」乔治叔叔凑趣地进言。

「正是，」我应道，「而我的第一道预言，便是《恰似黎明降临》。」

===Original===
Mr. Bulmer tapped his forehead significantly. “Mad, quite mad!” said he, in parenthesis.

===Chinese===
布尔默先生意味深长地敲了敲自己的额头。「疯了，彻底疯了！」他说，权作一句插批。

===Original===
“I shall be idyllic,” I continued, sweetly; “I shall write of the ineffable glory of first love. I shall babble of green fields and the keen odours of spring and the shamefaced countenances of lovers, met after last night’s kissing. It will be the story of love that stirs blindly in the hearts of maids and youths, and does not know that it is love—the love which manhood has half forgotten and that youth has not the skill to write of. But I, at twenty-four, shall write its story as it has never been written; and I shall make a great book of it, that will go into thousands and thousands of editions. Yes, before heaven, I will!”

===Chinese===
「我要写田园牧歌，」我甜甜地接着说；「我要写初恋那无可言说的荣光。我要絮絮叨叨，写绿色的田野，写春日清冽的气息，写情人们昨夜一吻之后、今朝重逢时那一张张羞赧的脸。那将是一个爱情的故事——那在少女少男心底盲目躁动、自己却不知是爱情的爱情——成年男子已把它忘掉了一半，年轻人又没有本事写它。可我，二十四岁，要来写它的故事，写得前无古人；我要把它做成一部大书，一部要印上千版万版的书。是的，皇天在上，我一定办到！」

===Original===
I brought my fist down, emphatically, on the table.

===Chinese===
我斩钉截铁地一拳捶在桌上。

===Original===
“H’m!” said Mr. Bulmer, dubiously; “going back to renew associations with your first love? I have tried it, and I generally find her grandchildren terribly in the way.”

===Chinese===
「唔！」布尔默先生将信将疑地说；「要回头去同你的初恋重续旧谊么？我试过，十有八九会发现她的孙儿孙女们碍事碍得要命。」

===Original===
“It is imperative,” said I—“yes, imperative for the scope of my book, that I should view life through youthful and unsophisticated eyes. I discovered that, upon the whole, Miss Jemmett is too obviously an urban product to serve my purpose. And I can’t find anyone who will.”

===Chinese===
「这是万万必要的，」我说——「是的，就我这部书的格局而言，万万必要：我必须透过一双年轻的、不谙世故的眼睛去观察人生。我发现，总的说来，杰米特小姐太明显是一件城市产物，合不上我的用途。而合用的人，我又一个也寻不着。」

===Original===
Uncle George whistled softly. “ ‘Honourable young gentleman,’ ” he murmured, as to himself, “ ‘desires to meet attractive and innocent young lady. Object: to learn how to be idyllic in three-hundred pages.’ ”

===Chinese===
乔治叔叔轻轻打了个呼哨。「『一位体面的青年绅士，』」他自言自语似的低声道，「『欲结识迷人而天真的年轻淑女一名。目的：学会如何在三百页篇幅之内写成田园牧歌。』」

===Original===
There was no commentary upon his text.

===Chinese===
对他这段高文，无人作评。

===Original===
“I say,” queried Mr. Bulmer, “do you think this sort of thing is fair to the girl? Isn’t it a little cold-blooded?”

===Chinese===
「我说，」布尔默先生问道，「你觉不觉得这种事对那姑娘不大公道？这岂不有几分冷血么？」

===Original===
“Respected nunky, you are at times very terribly the man in the street! Anyhow, I leave the Green Chalybeate tomorrow in search of *As the Coming of Dawn*.”

===Chinese===
「受人敬重的老叔，您有时候实在可怕得很，活脱脱是个街头巷尾的寻常俗人！不管怎样，我明天就要离开绿铁泉，去寻访《恰似黎明降临》了。」

===Original===
“Look here,” said Mr. Bulmer, rising, “if you start on a tour of the country, looking for assorted dawns and idylls, it will end in my abducting you from some rustic institution for the insane. You take a liver-pill and go to bed! I don’t promise anything, mind, but perhaps about the first I can manage a little cheque if only you will make oath on a few Bibles not to tank up on it in Lichfield. The transoms there,” he added unkindlily, “are not built for those full rich figures.”

===Chinese===
「你听着，」布尔默先生站起身来，「你若当真要周游全国，四处搜罗五花八门的黎明与田园诗，到头来免不了由我把你从哪家乡下疯人院里搭救出来。快吃一粒肝药丸，上床睡觉去！你给我记住，我什么也不应承；不过到一号前后，兴许我能设法对付出一张小小的支票——只要你肯对几部圣经起誓，不用这笔钱在利奇菲尔德痛饮一场。那儿的门楣横窗，」他又刻薄地添了一句，「可不是照着您这样富态丰满的身材建造的。」

### II / 二

===Original===
Next morning, I notified the desk-clerk, and, quite casually, both the newspaper correspondents, that the Green Chalybeate was about to be bereft of the presence of a distinguished novelist. Then, as my train did not leave till night, I resolved to be bored on horseback, rather than on the golf-links, and had Guendolen summoned, from the stable, for a final investigation of the country roads thereabouts.

===Chinese===
第二天早晨，我知会了柜台接待员，又以全然若无其事的姿态知会了那两位报纸记者：绿铁泉即将痛失一位杰出小说家的驾临。随后，由于火车要到夜里才开，我决意把这份烦闷改在马背上去消受，而不去高尔夫球场，便吩咐从马厩里唤出格温多琳（Guendolen），对这一带的乡间道路做最后一次勘察。

===Original===
Guendolen this afternoon elected to follow a new route; and knowing by experience that any questioning of this decision could but result in undignified defeat, I assented. Thus it came about that we circled parallel to the boardwalk, which leads uphill to the deserted Royal Hotel, and passed its rows of broken windows; and went downhill again, always at Guendolen’s election; and thus came to the creek, which babbled across the roadway and was overhung with thick foliage that lisped and whispered cheerfully in the placid light of the declining sun. It was there that the germ of *As the Coming of Dawn* was found.

===Chinese===
这天下午，格温多琳裁定取道一条新路线；经验告诉我，对这项裁决提出任何质询，结局都只能是一场不体面的败仗，我便应允了。于是我们兜的圈子恰好与那条木板步道平行——步道一路朝山上通去，直通那座被废弃的皇家旅馆（Royal Hotel）——路过了它一排排破碎的窗子；随后又一路下坡，处处循着格温多琳的裁决；就这样来到了那条小溪边。溪水淙淙地横过路面，头顶浓荫四合，在西斜太阳恬静的光里，欢快地咬着舌子窸窣低语。《恰似黎明降临》的胚芽，便是在那里寻获的。

===Original===
For I had fallen into a reverie over the deplorable obstinacy of my new heroine, who declined, for all my labours, to be unsophisticated; and taking advantage of this, Guendolen had twitched the reins from my hand and proceeded to satisfy her thirst in a manner that was rather too noisy to be quite good form. I sat in patience, idly observing the sparkling reflection of the sunlight on the water. I was elaborating a comparison between my obstinate heroine and Guendolen. Then Guendolen snorted, as something rustled through the underbrush, and turning, I perceived a Vision.

===Chinese===
原来我正陷入出神，为我那位新女主角可叹的固执发愁——任凭我百般经营，她始终不肯变得天真未凿；格温多琳趁这个空子，从我手里一扯扯走了缰绳，径自打起水来，那动静未免太响，算不得十分得体。我耐心坐着，闲看日光在水面闪烁的粼粼倒影，心中正细细推敲一个比拟，把我这位固执的女主角同格温多琳相提并论。忽然灌木丛中窸窣一响，格温多琳打了个响鼻；我回过头去，便看见了一位幻象（Vision）。

===Original===
The Vision was in white, with a profusion of openwork. There were blue ribbons connected with it. There were also black eyes, of the almond-shaped, heavy-lidded variety that I had thought existed only in Lely’s pictures, and great coils of brown hair which was gold where the chequered sunlight fell upon it, and two lips that were inexpressibly red. I was filled with pity for my tired horse, and a resolve that for this once her thirst should be quenched.

===Chinese===
那幻象一身白衣，镂空轻纱层叠繁复。周身还连缀着几条蓝缎带。还有一双黑眼睛——杏核形、眼帘沉沉的那一种，我原以为只存在于莱利（Lely）的画里；还有一大盘一大盘的棕色头发，斑驳的日光落上去，便泛出金色；还有两片嘴唇，红得无从言说。我心里霎时充满对我这匹疲惫坐骑的怜悯，并立下决断：就这一回，定要叫她饮个畅快。

===Original===
Thereupon, I lifted my cap hastily; and Guendolen scrambled to the other bank, and spluttered, and had carried me well past the Iron Spring, before I announced to the evening air that I was a fool, and that Guendolen was describable by various quite picturesque and derogatory epithets. And I smiled.

===Chinese===
于是我匆匆掀了掀帽檐；格温多琳蹬蹬爬上对岸，甩着水沫，一路把我驮过了铁泉（Iron Spring）老远，我才对着暮色里的空气宣布：我是个傻瓜；而格温多琳呢，尽可以用各色相当传神又相当不客气的形容词来描摹她。于是我笑了。

===Original===
“Now, Robert Etheridge Townsend, you writer of books, here is a subject made to your hand!” And then:

“Only ’twixt the light and shade
Floating memories of my maid
Make me pray for Guendolen.”

After this we retraced our steps. I was peering anxiously about the roadway.

===Chinese===
「好啊，罗伯特·埃瑟里奇·汤森，你这写书的人，现成有个题目送到你手上来了！」接着便是：

「唯有在光与影之间，
我那姑娘飘浮的忆念，
教我为格温多琳祈愿。」

这之后，我们循原路折回。我一路焦灼地打量着路面。

===Original===
“Pardon me,” said I, subsequently; “but *have* you seen anything of a watch—a small gold one, set with pearls?”

“Heavens!” said the Vision, sympathetically, “what a pity! Are you sure it fell here?”

“I don’t seem to have it about me,” I answered, with cryptic, but entire veracity. I searched about my pockets, with a puckered brow. “And as we stopped here—”

===Chinese===
「恕我冒昧，」稍后我开口道；「您*可曾*见到一块怀表——金质的小表，镶着珍珠的？」

「天哪！」幻象满怀同情地说，「多可惜！您敢肯定它是掉在这儿了么？」

「我身上似乎不见它的踪影，」我答道，话玄而句句属实。我皱着眉头，浑身上下摸索口袋。「方才我们又恰好在这里停过——」

===Original===
I looked inquiringly into the water.

“From this side,” observed the Vision, impersonally, “there is less glare from the brook.”

===Chinese===
我探询地往水里望去。

「从这一边看，」幻象以事不关己的口吻评点道，「溪水的反光要小一些。」

===Original===
Having tied Guendolen to a swinging limb, I sat down contentedly in these woods. The Vision moved a little, lest I be crowded.

“It might be further up the road,” she suggested.

===Chinese===
我把格温多琳拴上一条摇曳的横枝，便心满意足地在这些树中间坐了下来。幻象往旁边挪了挪，惟恐挤着了我。

「表兴许掉在更靠前头的路上，」她提示道。

===Original===
“Oh, I must have left it at the hotel,” I observed.

“You might look—” said she, peering into the water.

“Forever!” I assented.

===Chinese===
「哦，那我准是把它落在旅馆里了，」我说。

「您可以找找看——」她说着，眼睛往水里端详。

「找上一辈子！」我欣然应道。

===Original===
The Vision flushed, “I didn’t mean—” she began.

“But I did,” quoth I—“and every word of it.”

===Chinese===
幻象红了脸。「我不是那个意思——」她开口道。

「我可是那个意思，」我道——「而且字字当真。」

===Original===
“Why, in that case,” said she, and rose to her feet, “I’d better—” A frown wrinkled her brow; then a deep, curved dimple performed a similar office for her cheek. “I wonder—” said she.

===Chinese===
「嗐，既然如此，」她说着，站起身来，「我还是——」一道蹙纹拢上她的眉心；随即一记深深的、弯弯的酒涡，又给她面颊当起了同样的差。「我纳闷——」她说。

===Original===
“Why, you would be a boldfaced jig,” said I, composedly; “but, after all there is nobody about. And, besides—for I suspect you of being one of the three dilapidated persons in veils who came last night—we are going to be introduced right after supper, anyway.”

===Chinese===
「嗐，您这岂不成了个厚脸皮的轻佻丫头，」我从容自若地说；「不过话说回来，四下里一个人也没有。再说——因为我疑心您正是昨儿夜里那三位蒙面纱、形容狼狈的人物之一——横竖晚饭一过，自会有人把我们引见。」

===Original===
The Vision sat down. “You mentioned your sanatorium?” quoth she.

“The Asylum of Love,” said I; “discharged—under a false impression—as cured, and sent to paradise.

===Chinese===
幻象坐了回去。「您方才提到您的疗养院？」她道。

「爱情疯人院，」我说；「业已获准出院——出于一场误会——认定为痊愈，并发往天堂。」

===Original===
“Oh!” said I, defiant, “but it *is*!”

===Chinese===
「哦！」我说，语气不服，「可这里*就是*！」

===Original===
She looked about her. “The woods *are* rather beautiful,” she conceded, softly.

“They form a quite appropriate background,” said I. “It is a veritable Eden, before the coming of the snake.”

===Chinese===
她环顾四周。「这林子*确实*秀美，」她柔声让步道。

「它们构成一方再相宜不过的背景，」我说。「这里是一座十足地道的伊甸园，时在蛇到来之前。」

===Original===
“Before?” she queried, dubiously.

“Undoubtedly,” said I, and felt my ribs, in meditative wise. “Ah, but I thought I missed something! We participate in a historic moment. This is in Eden immediately after the creation of—Well, but of course you are acquainted with that famous bull about Eve’s being the fairest of her daughters?”

===Chinese===
「之前？」她将信将疑地问。

「毫无疑问，」我说，一面若有所思地摸了摸自己的肋骨。「啊，我说怎么方才觉得缺了点什么！我们正躬逢一个历史性的时刻。此刻身在伊甸园中，正是那位——嗯，不过，『夏娃是她女儿们当中最美的一位』，这句著名的悖谬妙语，您自然是听说过的？」

===Original===
“It is *quite* time,” said she, judicially, “for me to go back to the hotel, before—since we are speaking of animals—your presence here is noticed by one of the squirrels.”

===Chinese===
「*实在*到时候了，」她以法官断案的口吻说，「我该回旅馆去了，赶在——既然我们正谈论动物——您在此地的行藏，被哪一只松鼠察觉之前。」

===Original===
“It is not good,” I pleaded, “for man to be alone.”

“I have heard,” said she, “that—almost anyone can cite scripture to his purpose.”

===Chinese===
「那人独居不好，」我央告道。

「我听说过，」她说，「——几乎随便什么人，都能把圣经引得合乎自己的目的。」

===Original===
I thrust out a foot for inspection. “No suggestion of a hoof,” said I; “and not the slightest odour of brimstone, as you will kindly note; and my inoffensive name is Robert Townsend.”

===Chinese===
我伸出一只脚来，供人查验。「毫无蹄子的嫌疑，」我说；「也绝无一丝硫磺的气味，敬希明鉴；至于在下这无害的姓名，乃是罗伯特·汤森。」

===Original===
“Of course,” she submitted, “I could never think of making your acquaintance in this irregular fashion; and, therefore, of course, I could not think of telling you that my name is Marian Winwood.”

“Of course not,” I agreed; “it would be highly improper.”

===Chinese===
「自然，」她顺水推舟地说，「我决计想不到以这样不合规矩的方式同您结交；因此呢，我自然也决计想不到告诉您，我的名字叫玛丽安·温伍德（Marian Winwood）。」

「自然想不到，」我表示赞同；「那可大大有失体统。」

===Original===
“—And it is more than time for me to go to supper,” she concluded again, with a lacuna, as it seemed to me, in the deduction.

===Chinese===
「——而且，我去用晚饭的时辰也早已过了头了，」她再度作结道——依我看，这番推论里缺了一环。

===Original===
“Look here!” I remonstrated; “it isn’t anywhere near six yet.” I exhibited my watch to support this statement.

“Oh!” she observed, with wide, indignant eyes.

“I—I mean—” I stammered.

She rose to her feet.

===Chinese===
「您听我说！」我抗议道；「眼下离六点还早得很呢。」我出示怀表，以佐此说。

「哦！」她说，眼睛睁得溜圆，满是嗔意。

「我——我是说——」我结结巴巴。

她站起身来。

===Original===
“—I will explain how I happened to be carrying two watches—”

===Chinese===
「——我愿解释解释，我怎么恰好随身带着两块怀表——」

===Original===
“I do not care to listen to any explanations. Why should I?”

===Chinese===
「我不想听什么解释。凭什么要听？」

===Original===
“—upon,” I firmly said, “the third piazza of the hotel. And this very evening.”

===Chinese===
「——就在，」我斩钉截铁地说，「旅馆三楼的游廊上。就在今天晚上。」

===Original===
“You will not.” And this was said even more firmly. “And I hope you will have the kindness to keep away from these woods; for I shall probably always walk here in the afternoon.” Then, with an indignant toss of the head, the Vision disappeared.

===Chinese===
「您休想。」这一句说得越发斩钉截铁。「还望您行个好，从此躲开这片树林；因为我往后每天下午，多半总要在此地散步。」说罢，幻象嗔然把头一甩，倏忽不见了。
