## XVII / 十七

He Provides Copy / 他供出了素材

### I / 一

===Original===
So it was Uncle George Bulmer who presently left the Green Chalybeate, to pursue Mrs. Chaytor with his lawless arts. I stayed out the season.

===Chinese===
于是，不多几日便离开绿铁泉的，是乔治·布尔默叔叔——他追着查托太太去了，随身带去他那套无法无天的手段。我则留了下来，把整个避暑季住满。

===Original===
Now I cannot conscientiously recommend the Green Chalybeate against your next vacation. Once very long ago, it was frequented equally for the sake of gaiety and of health. In the summer that was Marian’s the resort was a beautiful and tumbledown place where invalids congregated for the sake of the nauseous waters—which infallibly demolish a solid column of strange maladies I never read quite through, although it bordered every page of the writing-paper you got there from the desk-clerk—and a scanty leaven of persons who came thither, apparently, in order to spend a week or two in lamenting “how very dull the season is this year, and how abominable the fare is.”

===Chinese===
如今，要我凭良心举荐绿铁泉作你下一个度假的去处，我办不到。很久很久以前，人们来此，为寻欢与为养身各占一半。到了属于玛丽安的那个夏天，这处胜地是一派美丽而颓败的光景：病人们为那令人作呕的矿泉水汇聚于此——那水包治不误，能一举摧毁长长一栏稀奇古怪的病症，那一栏我从来不曾读完过，尽管它印满了你在那边从账房领班手里领来的每一页信纸——另有寥寥一撮人，瞧那意思，跑到这里来，专为花上一两个礼拜，哀叹「今年这避暑季多么乏味，伙食又多么可憎」。

===Original===
But for one I praise the place, and I believe that Marian Winwood also bears it no ill-will. For we two were very happy there. We took part in the “subscription euchers” whenever we could not in time devise an excuse which would pass muster with the haggard “entertainer.” We danced conscientiously beneath the pink and green icing of the ballroom’s ceiling, with all three of the band playing “Hearts and Flowers”; and with a dozen “chaperones”—whom I always suspected of taking in washing during the winter months—lined up as closely as was possible to the door, as if in preparation for the hotel’s catching fire any moment, to give us pessimistic observal. And having thus discharged our duty to society at large, we enjoyed ourselves tremendously.

===Chinese===
可是说到我自己，我却要夸这地方一句，而且我相信，玛丽安·温伍德（Marian Winwood）对它也并无恶感。因为我们两个在那里过得非常快活。每逢来不及编出一项哄得过那位形容枯槁的「司仪」的托辞，我们便去参加「认购尤克牌赛」。我们在舞厅顶棚那粉绿相间的糖霜底下，一丝不苟地跳舞，乐队全体三位乐师奏着《心与花》；一打「陪媪」——我一直疑心她们入了冬便靠替人浆洗度日——排得离门越近越好，挨成一列，仿佛随时预备着旅馆起火，好对我们作悲观的检阅。如此这般，对大社会尽完了本分，我们便纵情作乐起来。

===Original===
For instance, we would talk over the book I was going to write in the autumn. That was the main thing. Then one could golf, or drive, or—I blush to write it even now—croquet. Croquet, though, is a much maligned game, as you will immediately discover if you ever play it on the rambling lawn of the Chalybeate, about six in the afternoon, say, when the grass is greener than it is by ordinary, and the shadows are long, and the sun is well beneath the treetops of the Iron Bank, and your opponent makes a face at you occasionally, and on each side the old, one-storied cottages are builded of unusually red bricks and are quite ineffably asleep.

===Chinese===
比如，我们会把我打算在秋天动笔的那部书细细谈论一番。这才是头等大事。然后呢，可以打高尔夫，可以驱车兜风，或者——即便此刻落笔，我还要脸红——打槌球。不过，槌球是一门蒙冤已深的游戏，只要你在绿铁泉那片漫展无际的草坪上打过一回，你立刻便会承认——譬如说，下午六点光景，草色比寻常更绿，影子拖得老长，太阳已经落到铁岸（Iron Bank）的树冠底下去了，你的对手时不时朝你做个鬼脸，两旁那些老式的单层小屋，砌着出奇红的砖，睡得说不出的酣沉。

===Original===
Or again there is always the creek to divert yourself in. Once I caught five crawfishes there, while Marian waited on the bank; and afterward we found an old tomato-can and boiled them in it, and they came out a really gorgeous crimson. This was the afternoon that we were Spanish Inquisitors. … Oh, believe me, you can have quite a good time at the Chalybeate, if you set about it in the proper way.

===Chinese===
再不然，那条小溪也永远可供你消遣。有一回，我在那儿捉到五只小龙虾，玛丽安在岸上守候；随后我们寻着一只旧番茄罐头，把它们煮在里面，煮出来真是一派华丽丽的绯红。那正是我们扮西班牙宗教裁判官的那个下午。……哦，相信我，只要你按正当的路子来办，在绿铁泉也尽可以过上一段好时光。

### II / 二

===Original===
Only it is true that sometimes, when it rained, say, with that hopeless insistency which, I protest, is unknown anywhere else in the world; and when Marian was not immediately accessible, and cigarettes were not quite satisfactory, because the entire universe was so sodden that matches had to be judiciously coaxed before they would strike; and when if you happened to be writing a fervid letter to Rosalind Jemmett, let us say, the ink would not dry for ever so long:—why, it is true that in these circumstances you would feel a shade too like the wicked Lord So-and-So of a melodrama to be comfortable.

===Chinese===
只不过，有时候确也难免如此——譬如说，碰上下雨，那种没救的缠绵劲儿，我敢声辩，普天之下再无第二处见识过；又碰上玛丽安一时寻不着人，烟抽得又不甚惬意，因为整个宇宙湿得那样通透，火柴须得好生哄劝、循循善诱，方肯着火；又碰上——比方说——你正给罗瑟琳·杰米特写一封炽热的情书，那墨水偏生许久不干：——呃，确也难免，处在这般境况里，你会觉得自己活脱脱是情节剧里那位作恶的某某勋爵，像得叫人不大自在。

===Original===
Yet even in these circumstances, reason told me that the Book was the main thing, that the girl would be thoroughly over the affair by November at latest, and that at the cost of a few inconsequent tears, she would have meanwhile immeasurably obliged posterity. And I knew that no man may ever write in perdurable fashion save by ruthlessly converting his own life into “copy,” since of other persons’ lives he can, at most, reproduce but the blurred and misinterpreted by-ends, by reason of almost any author’s deplorable lack of omniscience. Yes, the Book was the main thing; and yet the girl—knowingly to dip my pen into her heart as into an inkstand was not, at best, chivalric. …

===Chinese===
然而即便在这般境况里，理智仍旧告诉我：那部书才是头等大事；到十一月，至迟到十一月，这姑娘准会把这段情事彻底熬过去；况且只消赔上几滴无关宏旨的眼泪，她便同时给后世帮了一个无可估量的大忙。我也知道，人要想写出传之久远的文字，唯有狠起心肠，把自己的生活炼成「稿件素材」，因为旁人的生活，他至多只能复现其中模糊的、遭人曲解的枝节——缘由在于，几乎任何一位作家，都可悲地缺乏全知全能。是啊，那部书才是头等大事；可是那位姑娘——存心把笔尖浸进她的心，如同浸进一只墨水瓶，这无论往好里说，也算不得骑士风范。……

===Original===
“But the Book!” said I. “Why, I must be quite idiotically in love to think of letting that Book perish!” And I viciously added: “Confound the pretty simpleton!” …

===Chinese===
「可是那部书！」我说。「嗐，我准是害了失心疯的相思，才会起念头让那部书夭折！」接着我又恶狠狠补上一句：「这漂亮的小傻瓜，真该死！」……

### III / 三

===Original===
So the book was builded, after all, a little by a little. Hardly an evening came when after leaving Marian I had not at least one excellent and pregnant jotting to record in my notebook. Now it would be just an odd turn of language, or a description of some gesture she had made, or of a gown she had worn that day; and now a simile or some other rather good figure of speech which had popped into my mind when I was making love to her.

===Chinese===
于是，那部书终究一点一点地垒了起来。几乎夜夜如此：离开玛丽安之后，我的笔记本里总添得上至少一条精彩而富于孕意的札记。这一回，也许只是她说话的一个别致腔调，或是描下她某个手势、她当天穿的某条裙子；下一回，又是一个明喻，或旁的什么颇为高妙的修辞，是我在向她求爱时冷不丁蹦进脑子里的。

===Original===
Nor had I any difficulty in preserving nearly all she said to me, for Marian was never a chatterbox; yet her responses had, somehow, that long-sought tang it wasn’t in me to invent for any imaginary young woman who must be, for the sake of my new novel, quite heels over head in love.

===Chinese===
她对我说的话，我也几乎句句留得住，毫不费力，因为玛丽安从来不是个碎嘴子；可她那些应对，不知怎的，自有一股我寻觅已久的韵味——要我凭空捏造，是万万捏不出的，哪怕对象是我那部新小说里那位务须爱得神魂颠倒的虚构小姐。

===Original===
And I began to see that Bettie was right, as usual. I had portrayed Gillian Hardress pretty well in *Afield*; but by and large, I had always written about women as though they were “pterodactyls or some other extinct animal, which you had never seen, but had read a lot about.”

And now, in looking over my notes, I knew, and my heart glowed to know, that I was not about to repeat the error.

===Chinese===
我也渐渐看出，贝蒂说得对，一如既往地对。在《旷野》（*Afield*）里，我把吉莲·哈德雷斯描摹得还算像样；可大体而论，我写起女人来，向来只当她们是「翼手龙，或是旁的什么绝了种的动物，你从来没见过，只读过许多关于它们的书」。

而此刻，翻检着我的札记，我知道——知道得心头发热——我眼看不会再重蹈这个覆辙了。

===Original===
So the Book was builded, after all, a little by a little. And a little by a little the summer wore on; and in the lobby of the Main Hotel was hung the beautiful Spirit of the Falls poster of the Buffalo Exposition; and we talked of Oom Paul Krüger, and Shamrock II, and the Nicaragua Canal, and lanky Bob Fitzsimmons, and the Boxer outrages; and we read *To Have and to Hold* and *The Cardinal’s Snuff Box*, and thought it droll that the King of England was not going to call himself King Albert, after all.

===Chinese===
于是，那部书终究一点一点地垒了起来。夏天也一寸一寸地磨蚀下去；大旅馆（Main Hotel）的门厅里，挂起了布法罗博览会那张漂亮的《瀑布之灵》海报；我们谈论保罗·克吕格尔大叔（Oom Paul Krüger），谈论「三叶草二号」，谈论尼加拉瓜运河、瘦长条儿的鲍勃·菲茨西蒙斯，还有义和团的暴行；我们读《执手偕老》（*To Have and to Hold*）和《红衣主教的鼻烟盒》（*The Cardinal’s Snuff Box*），还觉得英国国王到头来不打算自称阿尔伯特国王，这事怪有趣味。

===Original===
And then came the news of how the President had been shot, “with a poisoned bullet,” and a week of contradictory bulletins from the Milburn House in Buffalo. And there were panicky surmises raised everywhere as to “what these anarchists may do next,” so that Maggio was mobbed in Columbus, and Emma Goldman in Chicago; and Colonel Roosevelt was found, after days of search, on Mt. Marcy in the Adirondacks, and was told in the heart of a forest that tomorrow he would be at the head of a nation. And the country’s guidance was entrusted to a mere lad of forty-three, with general uneasiness as to what might come of it; and the dramatic tale of Colonel Roosevelt’s taking of the oath of office was in that morning’s paper; and Marian and I were about to part.

===Chinese===
随后便传来了总统遇刺的消息——「中的是一颗毒弹」——接着是布法罗米尔本宅（Milburn House）里发出的一整个礼拜自相矛盾的病情公告。四面八方都掀起了惶惶的揣测，猜「这帮无政府主义者下一步会干出什么」，于是马焦（Maggio）在哥伦布市遭了围攻，爱玛·戈德曼（Emma Goldman）在芝加哥也遭了围攻；寻了多日，人们才在阿迪朗达克山中的马西峰上寻着罗斯福上校，在密林深处告诉他：明天，他便要执掌一个国家了。国家的舵柄，就这样交到一位才四十三岁的小子手里，举国惴惴，不知往后会闹出什么来；罗斯福上校宣誓就职的那段富于戏剧性的报导，就印在那天的晨报上；而我同玛丽安，也就要分手了。

### IV / 四

===Original===
“It will be dreadful,” sighed she; “for we have to stay a whole week longer, and I shall come here every afternoon. And there will be only ghosts in the woods, and I shall be very lonely.”

“Dear,” said I, “is it not something to have been happy? It has been such a wonderful summer; and come what may, nothing can rob us now of its least golden moment. And it is only for a little.”

===Chinese===
「那可真要命，」她叹道；「因为我们还得再待上整整一个礼拜，而我天天下午都会到这儿来。树林里就只剩些鬼魂了，我会非常孤单的。」

「亲爱的，」我说，「曾快活过一场，这难道不算一桩收获？这个夏天何其美妙；而且不管怎样，如今谁也抢不走它最微小的一寸黄金时光了。再说，分别不过短短一晌。」

===Original===
“You will come back?” said she, half-doubtingly.

“Yes,” I said. “You wonderful, elfin creature, I shall undoubtedly come back—to your real home, and claim you there. Only I don’t believe you do live in Aberlin—you probably live in some great, gnarled oak hereabouts; and at night its bark uncloses to set you free, and you and your sisters dance out the satyrs’ hearts in the moonlight. Oh, I know, Marian! I simply *know* you are a dryad—a wonderful, laughing, clear-eyed dryad strayed out of the golden age.”

===Chinese===
「你会回来的吧？」她说，将信将疑。

「会的，」我说。「你这奇妙的、小精灵似的人儿，我准定会回来——回到你真正的家，在那儿把你认领。只是我不信你当真住在阿伯林（Aberlin）——你多半就住在这一带某棵虬结的老橡树里；夜里，树皮张开，放你出来，你同你的姐妹们在月光底下跳舞，把萨堤洛斯们的心都跳丢了。哦，我知道，玛丽安！我*就是知道*，你是一位德律阿得斯（dryad）——一位了不起的、爱笑的、目光澄澈的树中仙女，从黄金时代里走散出来的。」

===Original===
“What a boy it is!” she said. “No, I am only a really and truly girl, dear—a rather frightened girl, with very little disposition to laughter, just now. For you are going away—Oh, my dear, you have meant so much to me! The world is so different since you have come, and I am so happy and so miserable that—that I am afraid.” An infinitesimal handkerchief went upward to two great, sparkling eyes, and dabbed at them.

“Dear!” said I. And this remark appeared to meet the requirements of the situation.

===Chinese===
「这孩子气的一个人啊！」她说。「不，我不过是一个如假包换的姑娘，亲爱的——一个此刻相当害怕的姑娘，眼下简直没什么心思发笑。因为你要走了——哦，亲爱的，你于我的意义何其重大！自从你来了，世界全然换了个样，我又这样快活、又这样痛苦，快活得——痛苦得——叫我害怕。」一方小得不能再小的手帕升上去，凑到两只盈盈闪光的大眼睛上，轻轻沾了沾。

「亲爱的！」我说。而这一句，看来正好应付了眼前的场面。

===Original===
There was a silence now. We sat in the same spot where I had first encountered Marian Winwood. Only this was an autumnal forest that glowed with many gem-like hues about us; and already the damp odour of decaying leaves was heavy in the air. It was like the Tosti thing translated out of marine terms into a woodland analogue. The summer was ended; but *As the Coming of Dawn* was practically complete.

It was not the book that I had planned, but a far greater one which was scarcely mine. There was no word written as yet. But for two months I had viewed life through Marian Winwood’s eyes; day by day, my half-formed, tentative ideas had been laid before her with elaborate fortuitousness, to be approved, or altered, or rejected, just as she decreed; until at last they had been welded into a perfect whole that was a Book, bit by bit, we had planned it, I and she; and, as I dreamed of it as it would be in print, my brain was fired with exultation, and I defied my doubt and I swore that the Book, for which I had pawned a certain portion of my self-respect, was worth—and triply worth—the price which had been paid. … This was in Marian’s absence.

===Chinese===
这时是一阵沉默。我们坐在我初遇玛丽安·温伍德的那个老地方。只是此刻，四下里已是一座秋日的森林，闪烁着种种宝石般的色泽；腐叶的潮气已然沉沉浮在空中。这就像托斯蒂（Tosti）的那支歌，从海洋的辞句里译出，换成了树林里的对应物。夏天结束了；而《恰似黎明降临》（*As the Coming of Dawn*）也差不多大功告成。

它不是我原先谋划的那部书，而是一部远为伟大的书——伟大得几乎算不得我的。书里还一个字没写。可是整整两个月，我一直透过玛丽安·温伍德的眼睛观看人生；我那些半成形、试着步的构想，日复一日，煞费苦心地装作出于偶然，摆到她面前，听凭她发落：认可，修改，或者否决；直到它们终于锻造成一个完美的整体——一部书。一点一点地，我们把这部书谋划了出来，我，和她；而当我梦想着它印成铅字的模样，我的头脑便燃起一阵狂喜，我向我的疑心叫阵，我赌咒说，这部书——我为它典当了自己自尊里的一份——值这笔代价，值三倍这笔代价。……——而这，是在玛丽安不在跟前的时候。

===Original===
“Dear!” said she. …

Her eyes were filled with a tender and unutterable confidence that thrilled me like physical cold. “Marian,” said I, simply, “I shall never come back.”

The eyes widened a trifle, but she did not seem to comprehend.

===Chinese===
「亲爱的！」她说。……

她的眼里盛满一种温柔而不可名状的信任，叫我浑身一激灵，像受了实打实的寒气。「玛丽安，」我平平常常地说，「我永远不会回来了。」

那双眼睛微微睁大了些，可她似乎并没有听懂。

===Original===
“Have you not wondered,” said I, “that I have never kissed you, except as if you were a very holy relic or a cousin or something of that sort?”

“Yes,” she answered. Her voice was quite emotionless.

===Chinese===
「你难道不曾纳闷过么，」我说，「我从来不曾吻过你——除了那样一种吻法，仿佛你是一件极神圣的圣髑，或者一位表亲，或者诸如此类的什么？」

「纳闷过，」她答道。她的声音平静得没有一丝波澜。

===Original===
“And yet—yet—” I sprang to my feet. “Dear God, how I have longed! Yesterday, only yesterday, as I read to you from the verses I had made to other women, those women that are colourless shadows by the side of your vivid beauty—and you listened wonderingly and said the proper things and then lapsed into dainty boredom—*how* I longed to take you in my arms, and to quicken your calm blood a little with another sort of kissing. You knew—you must have known! Last night, for instance—”

“Last night,” she said, very simply, “I thought—And I hoped you would.”

===Chinese===
「可是——可是——」我霍地跳了起来。「亲爱的上帝啊，我是怎样地渴望过！就在昨天，就在昨天，我为你诵读我写给别的女子的诗篇——比起你鲜活的明艳，那些女子只是些褪了色的影子——你听着，满脸惊奇，说着得体的话，随后便陷入一种精致的倦怠——我*何等*渴望把你搂进怀里，用另一种吻，让你平静的血液稍稍加快一点。你知道的——你一定知道的！就拿昨夜来说——」

「昨夜，」她说，说得非常简单，「我想过——我也盼过你会的。」

===Original===
“What a confession for a nicely brought up girl! Well! I didn’t. And afterward, all night, I tossed in sick, fevered dreams of you. I am mad for love of you. And so, once in a while I kiss your hand. Dear God, your hand!” My voice quavered, effectively.

“Yes,” said she; “still, I remember—”

===Chinese===
「一位家教良好的姑娘，竟作这样的自白！好啦！我没有。而之后整整一夜，我在关于你的病态而滚烫的梦里翻来覆去。我为对你的爱发了狂。所以，我只是隔三差五，吻一吻你的手。亲爱的上帝啊，你的手！」我的嗓音颤抖起来，颤得恰如其分。

「是啊，」她说；「不过，我还记得——」

===Original===
“I have struggled; and I have conquered this madness—for a madness it is. We can laugh together and be excellent friends; and we can never, never be anything more. Well! we have laughed, have we not, dear, a whole summer through? Now comes the ending. Ah, I have seen you puzzling over my meaning before this. You never understood me thoroughly; but it is always safe to laugh.”

She smiled; and I remember now it was rather as Mona Lisa smiles.

===Chinese===

### V / 五

===Original===

I spent six months in this. ===Original===

===Original===

I ought to have been content with this. But I had somehow contracted an insane notion that a novel is the more enjoyable when it is adroitly written. ===Chinese===

### VI / 六

===Original===

### VIII / 八

===Original===

“And to cap it all, she has assigned her hero every pretty speech I ever made to her! I honestly believe the rogue took shorthand jottings on her cuffs. “There is a land where lovers may meet face to face, and heart to heart, and mouth to mouth”—why, that’s the note I wrote her on the day she wasn’t feeling well!”

Presently, however, I began to laugh, and presently sitting there alone, I began to applaud as if I were witnessing a play that took my fancy.

“Oh, the adorable jade!” I said; and then: “George Glock, forsooth! *George Dandin, tu l’ as voulu.*”

我读着读着，火气越读越旺。瞧啊，我那宏大而姣好的构想，被「乔治·格洛克」——这是那女人愚蠢的笔名——点化成了一出相当机巧的情节剧，而且信笔写来，那副东一榔头西一棒槌的笔路，直看得我浑身扭动。那一刻，我简直乐意亲手掐死玛丽安·温伍德——罪名不取偷窃，而取屠戮。

「最气人的是，她把我从前对她说过的每一句漂亮话，全数派给了她笔下的男主角！我实实在在相信，这泼皮把速记记在了自己的袖口上。『有一方土地，情人们得以面对面、心贴心、唇对唇地相会』——嗐，这正是她那天身子不爽利时，我写给她的字条！」

然而，过了一忽儿，我笑了起来；又过一忽儿，独自坐在那儿的我，竟鼓起掌来，仿佛看的是一出正中下怀的戏。

「哦，这可人心疼的小滑头！」我说；接着又道：「亏她还是『乔治·格洛克』！真是『乔治·当丹，你自找的』（*George Dandin, tu l’as voulu*）。」

===Original===
Naturally I put the entire affair into a short story. And—though even to myself it seems incredible—Miss Winwood wrote me within three days of the tale’s appearance, a very indignant letter.

For she was furious, to the last exclamation point and underlining, about my little magazine tale. … “Why don’t you stop writing, and try plumbing or butchering or traveling for scented soap? *You can’t write!* If you had the light of creation you wouldn’t be using my material.” …

—Which caused me to reflect forlornly that I had wasted a great deal of correct behavior upon Marian, since any of the more intimately amorous advances which I might have made, and had scrupulously refrained from making, would very probably have been regarded as raw “material,” to be developed rather than shocked by. …
