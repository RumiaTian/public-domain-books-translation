## Epilogue / 尾声
===Original===

Note by J. R. Bickley, M.R.C.S.

Within about six months of the date on which he wrote the last words of this history of our joint adventures, my dear friend, Humphrey Arbuthnot, died suddenly, as I had foreseen that probably he would do, from the results of the injury he received in the island of Orofena.

He left me the sole executor to his will, under which he divided his property into three parts. One third he bequeathed to me, one third (which is strictly tied up) to Bastin, and one third to be devoted, under my direction, to the advancement of Science.

His end appears to have been instantaneous, resulting from an effusion of blood upon the brain. When I was summoned I found him lying dead by the writing desk in his library at Fulcombe Priory. He had been writing at the desk, for on it was a piece of paper on which appear these words: “*I have seen her. I*﻿—” There the writing ends, not stating whom he thought he had seen in the moments of mental disturbance or delusion which preceded his decease.

Save for certain verbal corrections, I publish this manuscript without comment as the will directs, only adding that it sets out our mutual experiences very faithfully, though Arbuthnot’s deductions from them are not always my own.

I would say also that I am contemplating another visit to the South Sea Islands, where I wish to make some further investigations. I dare say, however, that these will be barren of results, as the fountain of Life-water is buried forever, nor, as I think, will any human being stand again in the Hades-like halls of Nyo. It is probable also that it would prove impossible to rediscover the island of Orofena, if indeed that volcanic land still remains above the waters of the deep.

Now that he is a very wealthy man, Bastin talks of accompanying me for purposes quite different from my own, but on the whole I hope he will abandon this idea. I may add that when he learned of his unexpected inheritance he talked much of the “deceitfulness of riches,” but that he has not as yet taken any steps to escape their golden snare. Indeed he now converses of his added “opportunities of usefulness,” I gather in connection with missionary enterprise.

J. R. Bickley.

J. R. 比克利（皇家外科医师公会会员）附记

这部我们共同冒险的历史的末一句写定之后，不出六个月，我的挚友汉弗莱·阿巴思诺特突然去世——照我预料的可能——死于他在奥罗费纳岛上所受的伤的后患。

他立我为遗嘱的唯一执行人；照遗嘱，他把产业分作三份。一份赠我，一份（严格地锁定了的）赠巴斯廷，一份在我的指导下用于推进科学。

他的结局仿佛是刹那间的，起于脑溢血。我被召去时，发现他倒在富尔科姆修道院旧宅藏书室的写字台旁，已经死去。他方才正在写字，因为桌上有一页纸，上面写着这几个字："*我看见她了。我——*"文字便到此为止，没有说明他在去世之前那阵心智的纷乱或错觉的时刻里，以为自己看见了谁。

除若干文字上的订正外，我照遗嘱的指示发表这部手稿，不加评论，只补一句：它极其忠实地记述了我们共同的经历，虽然阿巴思诺特从中引出的推论，未必尽同于我。

我还要说，我正打算再到南海诸岛去一趟，想作一些进一步的考察。不过我敢说，这些考察不会有什么结果，因为生命之水的泉源已经永远埋葬，而照我看，也再不会有任何人重进尼俄那冥府般的殿堂。多半也不可能再寻见奥罗费纳岛，倘若那片火山的土地当真还浮在深海的水面上的话。

如今他已是很富的人，巴斯廷谈着要陪我同去，目的却同我的全然两样；总的说，我希望他打消这个主意。我可以补一句：他得知这笔意外的遗产时，谈了许多"钱财的迷惑"，可他至今没有采取任何步骤，逃出那黄金的罗网。实在说他如今谈的是他添了的"行善的机会"，我领会，那是同传教事业连在一起说的。

J. R. 比克利

P.S.﻿—I forgot to state that the spaniel Tommy died within three days of his owner. The poor little beast was present in the room at the time of Arbuthnot’s passing away, and when found seemed to be suffering from shock. From that moment Tommy refused food and finally was discovered quite dead and lying by the body on Marama’s feather cloak, which Arbuthnot often used as a dressing-gown. As Bastin raised some religious objections, I arranged without his knowledge that the dog’s ashes should rest not far from those of the master and mistress whom it loved so well.

J. R. B.

又及——我忘了交代：那只小獚犬汤米死在它主人之后三天。阿巴思诺特咽气的时候，这可怜的小畜生正在屋里，被发现时仿佛受了惊。从那一刻起汤米拒绝进食，末了人们发觉它已经死了，躺在遗体旁边，躺在马拉玛那件羽毛斗篷上——阿巴思诺特常拿它当晨衣用。因为巴斯廷提出若干宗教上的异议，我便瞒着他安排了：把这狗的骨灰安放在它深爱的主人夫妇的骨灰不远处。

J. R. B.
