## I Thery’s Trade / 一 特里的营生
===Original===
Four men sat about a table on the sidewalk in front of the Café of the Nations in the High Street of Cádiz and talked business.

Leon Gonsalez was one, Poiccart was another, George Manfred was a notable third, and one Thery, or Saimont, was the fourth. Of this quartet, only Thery requires no introduction to the student of contemporary history. In the Bureau of Public Affairs you will find his record. As Thery, alias Saimont, he is registered and to all students of Criminology and Physiognomy, he must need no introduction.

===Chinese===
四个人围坐在加的斯（Cádiz）大街万国咖啡馆（Café of the Nations）门前人行道的一张桌旁，谈着一桩买卖。

莱昂·冈萨雷斯（Leon Gonsalez）是一个，波伊卡特（Poiccart）是另一个，乔治·曼弗雷德（George Manfred）是名声在外的第三个，还有一个名叫特里（Thery）——又叫赛蒙特（Saimont）——的是第四个。这四人当中，唯有特里无须向研究当代史的学子多做介绍。在公共事务局的档案里，你自会查到他的记录。作为特里，化名赛蒙特，他早已登记在案，凡是治犯罪学与相貌学的学者，对他必不陌生。

===Original===
He sat at the little table, this man, obviously ill at ease, pinching his fat cheeks, smoothing his shaggy eyebrows, fingering the white scar on his unshaven chin, doing all the things that the lower classes do when they suddenly find themselves placed on terms of equality with their betters.

For although Gonsalez, with the light blue eyes and the restless hands, and Poiccart, heavy, saturnine, and suspicious, and George Manfred, with his grey shot beard and single eyeglass, were less famous in the criminal world, each was a great man, as you shall learn.

===Chinese===
此刻这人坐在小桌旁，显然浑身不自在，捏捏肥嘟嘟的脸颊，抹抹浓乱的眉毛，又摸摸没刮的下巴上那道白疤——下层人骤然同体面人平起平坐时，多半都这副做派。

因为，尽管长着浅蓝色眼睛、一双手总不安生的冈萨雷斯，还有阴沉、忧郁、多疑的波伊卡特，以及灰白胡须、单片眼镜的乔治·曼弗雷德，在罪案世界里名气都算小，可个个都是了不起的人物——这一点下文自见分晓。

===Original===
Manfred laid down the *Heraldo de Madrid*, removed his eyeglass, rubbed it with a spotless handkerchief, and laughed quietly.

“These Russians are droll,” he commented.

Poiccart frowned and reached for the newspaper. “Who is it﻿—this time?”

“A Governor of one of the Southern Provinces.”

“Killed?”

Manfred’s moustache curled in scornful derision.

“Bah! Who ever killed a man with a bomb! Yes, yes; I know it has been done﻿—but so clumsy, so primitive, so very much like undermining a city wall that it may fall and slay﻿—amongst others﻿—your enemy.”

===Chinese===
曼弗雷德放下《马德里先驱报》（*Heraldo de Madrid*），摘下单片眼镜，用一方一尘不染的手帕擦拭，轻声笑了。

「这些俄国人真是滑稽。」他评了一句。

波伊卡特皱起眉头，伸手去拿报纸。「这回——是谁？」

「南方某省的一个省长。」

「死了？」

曼弗雷德的髭须轻蔑地往上一撇。

「呸！谁用炸弹杀成过人！是，是，我知道有过先例——可那多笨拙，多原始，活像挖墙脚叫城墙塌下来，砸死一大片人——你的仇人——也捎带在其中。」

===Original===
Poiccart was reading the telegram deliberately and without haste, after his fashion.

“The Prince was severely injured and the would-be assassin lost an arm,” he read, and pursed his lips disapprovingly. The hands of Gonsalez, never still, opened and shut nervously, which was Leon’s sign of perturbation.

“Our friend here”﻿—Manfred jerked his head in the direction of Gonsalez and laughed﻿—“our friend has a conscience and﻿—”

“Only once,” interrupted Leon quickly, “and not by my wish; you remember, Manfred; you remember, Poiccart”﻿—he did not address Thery﻿—“I advised against it. You remember?” He seemed anxious to exculpate himself from the unspoken charge. “It was a miserable little thing, and I was in Madrid,” he went on breathlessly, “and they came to me, some men from a factory at Barcelona. They said what they were going to do, and I was horror-stricken at their ignorance of the elements of the laws of chemistry. I wrote down the ingredients and the proportions, and begged them, yes, almost on my knees, to use some other method. ‘My children,’ I said, ‘you are playing with something that even chemists are afraid to handle. If the owner of the factory is a bad man, by all means exterminate him, shoot him, wait on him after he has dined and is slow and dull, and present a petition with the right hand and﻿—with the left hand﻿—so’!”

===Chinese===
波伊卡特照他一贯的做派，不慌不忙地读着那封电讯。

「亲王受了重伤，行刺者丢了一条胳膊。」他念道，抿嘴不以为然。冈萨雷斯那双从不安生的手紧张地一张一合——这是莱昂心绪不宁的标志。

「咱们这位朋友」——曼弗雷德朝冈萨雷斯那边一甩头，笑了——「咱们这位朋友有副良心，而且——」

「就那么一回，」莱昂赶忙插嘴，「还非我本意；您记得的，曼弗雷德；您记得的，波伊卡特」——他没有对特里说——「我那时是反对的。记得吧？」他一副急于洗清这桩没说出口的罪责的模样。「那是桩不起眼的小事，我当时在马德里，」他上气不接下气地往下说，「有人来找我，是巴塞罗那一家工厂的几个工人。他们说了打算干的事，他们连化学定律的入门都一无所知，吓得我目瞪口呆。我把原料和配比写下来，求他们——不错，几乎是跪着求他们——换个别的主意。『孩子们，』我说，『你们摆弄的这个东西，连化学家都不敢碰。要是厂主是个恶人，尽可以消灭他，枪毙他，等他吃完饭、又迟又钝的时候伺候他，右手递上一份请愿书，左手——这么一下』！」

===Original===
Leon twisted his knuckles down and struck forward and upward at an imaginary oppressor. “But they would listen to nothing I had to say.”

Manfred stirred the glass of creamy liquid that stood at his elbow, and nodded his head with an amused twinkle in his grey eyes.

“I remember﻿—several people died, and the principal witness at the trial of the expert in explosives was the man for whom the bomb was intended.”

===Chinese===
莱昂攥起指节朝下一拧，朝着假想中的暴徒向前、向上一击。「可我说的话他们一个字也听不进。」

曼弗雷德搅了搅手肘边那杯乳白色的酒，灰眼睛里闪着被逗乐的光，点了点头。

「我记得——死了好几个人，那位爆破专家受审的时候，主要证人正是炸弹原本要炸的那个人。」

===Original===
Thery cleared his throat as if to speak, and the three looked at him curiously. There was some resentment in Thery’s voice.

“I do not profess to be a great man like you, señors. Half the time I don’t understand what you are talking about﻿—you speak of governments and kings and constitutions and causes. If a man does *me* an injury I smash his head”﻿—he hesitated, “I do not know how to say it﻿ ﻿… but I mean﻿ ﻿… well, you kill people without hating them, men who have not hurt you. Now, that is not my way.﻿ ﻿…” He hesitated again, tried to collect his thoughts, looked intently at the middle of the roadway, shook his head and relapsed into silence.

===Chinese===
特里清了清嗓子，像要开口，三人好奇地望着他。特里的嗓音里带着几分怨气。

「我不敢冒充诸位先生（señores）这样的伟人。你们说的话我一半听不懂——你们讲政府、国王、宪法，还有种种主义。谁要是伤了*我*，我就敲碎他的脑袋」——他迟疑了一下，「我不知道该怎么说……可我是说……唉，你们杀人不带恨，杀的还是从没招惹过你们的人。这可不是我的路数……」他又迟疑起来，努力收拢思绪，直勾勾盯着路中央，摇了摇头，重新归于沉默。

===Original===
The others looked at him, then at one another, and each man smiled. Manfred took a bulky case from his pocket, extracted an untidy cigarette, rerolled it deftly and struck a government match on the sole of his boot.

“Your﻿—way﻿—my﻿—dear﻿—Thery”﻿—he puffed﻿—“is a fool’s way. You kill for benefit; we kill for justice, which lifts us out of the ruck of professional slayers. When we see an unjust man oppressing his fellows; when we see an evil thing done against the good God”﻿—Thery crossed himself, “and against man﻿—and know that by the laws of man this evildoer may escape punishment﻿—we punish.”

===Chinese===
其余三人望望他，又彼此相望，各自莞尔。曼弗雷德从口袋里摸出一只鼓鼓的烟盒，抽出一支皱巴巴的烟，麻利地重新卷紧，拿一根官制火柴在靴底上一划。

「你的——路数——我——亲爱——的——特里，」——他吐了口烟——「是蠢人的路数。你为私利杀人，我们为正义杀人，这一点便使我们高出职业凶手的芸芸之辈。当我们看见一个不义之徒欺压同胞；当我们看见一件恶事冒犯了好上帝」——特里画了个十字——「也冒犯了世人——又明知按人间的法律，这作恶的人可能逃脱惩罚——我们就施以惩罚。」

===Original===
“Listen,” interrupted the taciturn Poiccart; “once there was a girl, young and beautiful, up there”﻿—he waved his hand northward with unerring instinct﻿—“and a priest﻿—a priest, you understand﻿—and the parents winked at it because it is often done﻿ ﻿… but the girl was filled with loathing and shame, and would not go a second time, so he trapped her and kept her in a house, and then when the bloom was off turned her out, and I found her. She was nothing to me, but I said, ‘Here is a wrong that the law cannot adequately right.’ So one night I called on the priest with my hat over my eyes and said that I wanted him to come to a dying traveller. He would not have come then, but I told him that the dying man was rich and was a great person. He mounted the horse I had brought, and we rode to a little house on the mountain.﻿ ﻿… I locked the door and he turned round﻿—so! Trapped, and he knew it. ‘What are you going to do?’ he said with a gasping noise. ‘I am going to kill you, señor,’ I said, and he believed me. I told him the story of the girl.﻿ ﻿… He screamed when I moved towards him, but he might as well have saved his breath. ‘Let me see a priest,’ he begged; and I handed him﻿—a mirror.”

===Chinese===
「听着，」寡言少语的波伊卡特插话，「从前有个姑娘，年轻，漂亮，就在北边」——他凭着分毫不差的直觉朝北一挥手——「还有一个神父——一个神父，你懂吗——做父母的对此睁一只眼闭一只眼，这种事向来多得很……可那姑娘满心憎恶与羞耻，不肯去第二次，他便设局困住她，把她关在一座宅子里，等花容凋残又把她赶出门，是我找到了她。她与我素不相识，可我说：『这里有一桩冤屈，法律没法给它足够的昭雪。』于是一天夜里，我压着帽檐去拜访那位神父，说想请他去见一个垂死的旅人。他本不肯来，我告诉他那垂死的人有钱，是个大人物。他骑上我牵来的马，我们一路骑到山上一座小屋。……我锁上门，他转过身来——就这样！落入陷阱，他心里明白。『你要干什么？』他喘着气问。『我要杀了你，先生（señor）。』我说。他信了。我把那姑娘的事讲给他听……我朝他走去时他尖叫起来，可他不如省省力气。『让我见一位神父。』他哀求道；我便递给他——一面镜子。」

===Original===
Poiccart stopped to sip his coffee.

“They found him on the road next day without a mark to show how he died,” he said simply.

“How?” Thery bent forward eagerly, but Poiccart permitted himself to smile grimly, and made no response.

===Chinese===
波伊卡特停下来，呷了一口咖啡。

「第二天，人们在路上发现了他，周身上下找不到一点致命的痕迹。」他平平淡淡地说。

「怎么弄的？」特里急切地探身向前，波伊卡特却只冷冷一笑，不置一词。

===Original===
Thery bent his brows and looked suspiciously from one to the other.

“If you can kill as you say you can, why have you sent for me? I was happy in Jerez working at the wine factory﻿ ﻿… there is a girl there﻿ ﻿… they call her Juana Samarez.” He mopped his forehead and looked quickly from one to the other. “When I received your message I thought I should like to kill you﻿—whoever you were﻿—you understand I am happy﻿ ﻿… and there is the girl﻿—and the old life I have forgotten﻿—”

===Chinese===
特里皱起眉，满腹狐疑地把三人挨个瞧过去。

「你们要真像嘴上说的那样能杀人，干吗还派人叫我？我在赫雷斯（Jerez）的酒厂干活，本来快快活活……那边有个姑娘……人家管她叫胡安娜·萨马雷斯（Juana Samarez）。」他抹了抹额头，飞快地把三人挨个瞧了一遍。「接到你们口信那会儿，我想的是干脆把你们杀了——不管你们是谁——你们明白，我快快活活的……又有那姑娘……还有那种我已经忘掉的旧日子——」

===Original===
Manfred arrested the incoherent protests.

“Listen,” said he imperiously; “it is not for you to inquire the wherefore and the why; we know who you are and what you are; we know more of you even than the police know, for we could send you to the garotte.”

Poiccart nodded his head in affirmation, and Gonsalez looked at Thery curiously, like the student of human nature that he was.

“We want a fourth man,” went on Manfred, “for something we wish to do; we would have wished to have had one animated by no other desire than to see justice done. Failing that, we must have a criminal, a murderer if you like.”

===Chinese===
曼弗雷德止住了这番语无伦次的抱怨。

「听着，」他不容分说，「不该你刨根问底；我们知道你是谁、是干什么的；我们对你的底细比警察知道的还多，因为我们能送你上西班牙绞刑（garotte）。」

波伊卡特点头称是，冈萨雷斯则一副天生的研究人性的学者做派，好奇地打量着特里。

「我们要办一件事，缺个第四人，」曼弗雷德接着说，「本来巴不得能找到一个除了眼见正义伸张、别无他念的人。这样的人寻不着，我们就得用一个罪犯——你要愿意，说凶手也行。」

===Original===
Thery opened and shut his mouth as if about to speak.

“One whom we can at a word send to his death if he fails us; you are the man; you will run no risk; you will be well rewarded; you may not be asked to slay. Listen,” went on Manfred, seeing that Thery had opened his mouth to speak. “Do you know England? I see that you do not. You know Gibraltar? Well, this is the same people. It is a country up there”﻿—Manfred’s expressive hands waved north﻿—“a curious, dull country, with curious, dull people. There is a man, a member of the Government, and there are men whom the Government have never heard of. You remember one: Garcia, Manuel Garcia, leader in the Carlist movement; he is in England; it is the only country where he is safe; from England he directs the movement here, the great movement. ===Chinese===
特里张了张嘴，像要说话。

「一个坏了我们的事、我们一句话就能打发去死的人；你就是这个人；你不会担任何风险；你会得到重赏；也未必就叫你动手杀人。听着，」见特里张嘴要说话，曼弗雷德继续道，「你了解英国吗？我看你不了解。你知道直布罗陀？唔，那边就是同一族人。那是个北边的国家」——曼弗雷德那双富于表情的手朝北一挥——「一个古怪、乏味的国家，住着一群古怪、乏味的人。那里有一个人，是政府的一员；还有些人，是政府从来没有听说过的。有一个你记得：加西亚，曼努埃尔·加西亚，卡洛斯派运动的领袖；他就在英国；那是唯一能容他安身的国家；他在英国遥控着这边的运动，那场大运动。你知道我说的是什么？」

===Original===
Thery nodded.

“This year as well as last there has been a famine, men have been dying about the church doors, starving in the public squares; they have watched corrupt Government succeed corrupt Government; they have seen millions flow from the public treasury into the pockets of politicians. This year something will happen; the old regime must go. The Government know this; they know where the danger lies, they know their salvation can only come if Garcia is delivered into their hands before the organization for revolt is complete. When that is passed, Garcia is as good as dead. You must help us to prevent that from ever becoming law; that is why we have sent for you.”

===Chinese===
特里点头。

「今年和去年一样闹了饥荒，教堂门口倒毙了人，公共广场上有人活活饿死；他们眼看一届腐败政府接着一届腐败政府；眼看千百万公款流进政客的腰包。今年要出大事；旧政权非倒不可。政府知道这一点；他们知道危险在哪儿，也知道唯有赶在叛乱组织大功告成之前，把加西亚交到他们手里，他们才有活路。可加西亚眼下安然无恙，若不是英国政府里有一位成员要提出并通过一项法案，他会一直安然无恙下去。那法案一旦通过，加西亚就等于死定了。你必须帮我们阻止它变成法律；这就是我们叫你来的缘故。」

===Original===
Thery looked bewildered. “But how?” he stammered.

Manfred drew a paper from his pocket and handed it to Thery. “This, I think,” he said, speaking deliberately, “is an exact copy of the police description of yourself.” Thery nodded. Manfred leant over and, pointing to a word that occurred halfway down the sheet, “Is that your trade?” he said.

Thery looked puzzled. “Yes,” he replied.

“Do you really know anything about that trade?” asked Manfred earnestly; and the other two men leant forward to catch the reply.

“I know,” said Thery slowly, “everything there is to be known: had it not been for a﻿—mistake I might have earned great money.”

“Then,” he said briskly, “the English minister is a dead man.”

===Chinese===
「该知道的，」特里缓缓地说，「我全知道：要不是出了——一桩岔子，我这会儿早挣了大钱了。」

「那么，」他干脆利落地说，「那位英国大臣已经是个死人了。」
