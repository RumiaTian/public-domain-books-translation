## Jackrabbit and Cottontail / 长耳兔与棉尾兔
===Original===

Anciently the Jackrabbit lived in a sage plain, and the Cottontail rabbit lived in a cliff hard by. They saw the clouds gather, so they went out to sing. The long-legged Jackrabbit sang for snow, thus:

> “U pi na wi sho, U pi na wi sho,

U kuk uku u kuk!”

But the short-legged Cottontail sang for rain, like this:

> “Hatchi ethla ho na an saia.”

That’s what they sung﻿—one asking for snow, the other for rain; hence to this day the Pókʻia (Jackrabbit) runs when it snows, the Âʻkshiko (Cottontail) when it rains.
Thus shortens my story.

===Chinese===

古时候，长耳兔（Jackrabbit）住在一片鼠尾草平原上，棉尾兔（Cottontail）住在近旁的峭壁里。它们看见云聚拢起来，便出去唱歌。长腿的长耳兔唱的是求雪，这样唱：

> “U pi na wi sho, U pi na wi sho,

U kuk uku u kuk!”

短腿的棉尾兔唱的是求雨，这样唱：

> “Hatchi ethla ho na an saia.”

它们唱的就是这个——一个求雪，一个求雨；所以直到今天，下雪时长耳兔 Pókʻia 就奔跑，下雨时棉尾兔 Âʻkshiko 就奔跑。
我的故事就此收短。
