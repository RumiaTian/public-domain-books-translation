## Endnotes / 尾注
===Original===

1. “Tommied to death”: Subjected to the “tommy” or “truck” system of wages. See “tommy” below. —﻿Editor ↩︎
2. “Tommy”: The iniquitous system of paying workers with vouchers rather than money. These vouchers were only redeemable at the employer’s own stores, where goods were often of inferior quality and comparatively high in price. The system kept down employer’s actual wages bills but meant that their workers often lived in abject poverty. —﻿Editor ↩︎
3. “Butty”: A butty in the mining districts is a middleman: a doggy is his manager. The butty generally keeps a tommy or truck shop and pays the wages of his labourers in goods. When miners and colliers strike they term it, “going to play.” —﻿B. D. ↩︎
4. “At play”: on strike. —﻿Editor ↩︎
5. “Hachis”: in cooking, an ingredient which is minced or chopped. Here it indicates just “a mess.” —﻿Editor ↩︎
6. “Charter”: The People’s Charter of 1838, demanding universal manhood suffrage (the right to vote) and other democratic reforms. —﻿Editor ↩︎
7. “Paixhans rockets”: Paixhans was a French general who developed artillery guns which fired explosive shells. The implication here is that the barons cannot stand against modern artillery in the hands of the people. —﻿Editor ↩︎
8. “Five Points”: This refers to the demands of the People’s Charter demanding universal manhood suffrage (the right to vote) and other democratic reforms. —﻿Editor ↩︎
9. “Congo”: black tea imported from China (despite the name, not from the Congo). —﻿Editor ↩︎
10. “Biggin”: a kind of coffee-pot with a strainer, named for its inventor. —﻿Editor ↩︎

===Chinese===

1. 「被汤米害死」（Tommied to death）：指受「汤米」即「以货代饷」工资制的盘剥。参见下文「汤米」条。——编者 ↩︎
2. 「汤米」（Tommy）：用代价券而非现钱支付工人工资的罪恶制度。这种代价券只能在雇主自开的铺子里兑换，货物往往质次价高。该制度压低了雇主实际支付的工资账，却意味着工人往往生活在赤贫之中。——编者 ↩︎
3. 「包工头」（Butty）：矿区里的包工头就是中间人；「监工」（doggy）是他的管家事者。包工头通常开一家汤米铺即代饷店，用货物支付手下工人的工资。矿工煤夫罢工时，他们称之为「去玩耍」。——本作者 ↩︎
4. 「玩耍」（At play）：即罢工。——编者 ↩︎
5. 「杂碎」（Hachis）：烹调中指切碎剁细的配料。此处意为「一团乱糟糟」。——编者 ↩︦
6. 「宪章」（Charter）：即一八三八年《人民宪章》，要求实行男子普选权（投票权）及其他民主改革。——编者 ↩︎
7. 「佩克桑火箭弹」（Paixhans rockets）：佩克桑是一位法国将军，发明了发射爆炸弹的火炮。此处暗示：男爵们抵挡不住人民手中的现代炮火。——编者 ↩︎
8. 「五条」（Five Points）：指《人民宪章》的各项要求，包括男子普选权（投票权）及其他民主改革。——编者 ↩︎
9. 「工夫茶」（Congo）：从中国进口的红茶（虽然叫这个名字，却并非来自刚果）。——编者 ↩︎
10. 「比金壶」（Biggin）：一种带滤网的咖啡壶，以其发明者命名。——编者 ↩︎
