## IX / 第九章

===Original===
Hugh was by no means continuously depressed; as a matter of fact, most of the time he was agog with delight, especially over the rallies that were occurring with increasing frequency as the football season progressed. Sometimes the rallies were carefully prepared ceremonies held in the gymnasium; sometimes they were entirely spontaneous.

===Chinese===
休的心情绝没有一直低落下去；说实在的，他大部分时间都欢喜得心痒难耐，尤其是对那一场场助威大会（rallies）——随着橄榄球赛季的推进，它们开得一次比一次勤。这些大会有时是在体育馆里精心筹备的仪式，有时又完全是自发的。

===Original===
A group of men would rush out of a dormitory or fraternity house yelling, “Peerade, peerade!” Instantly everyone within hearing would drop his books﻿—or his cards﻿—and rush to the yelling group, which would line up in fours and begin circling the campus, the line ever getting longer as more men came running out of the dormitories and fraternity houses. On, on they would go, arm in arm, dancing, singing Sanford songs, past every dormitory on the campus, past every fraternity house﻿—pausing occasionally to give a cheer, always, however, keeping one goal in mind, the fraternity house where the team lived during the football season. Then when the cheerleaders and the team were heading the procession, the mob would make for the football field, with the cry of “Wood, freshmen, wood!” ringing down the line.

===Chinese===
常常会有那么一群人，从哪栋宿舍楼或兄弟会宅子里冲出来，高喊着“游行喽！游行喽！”顷刻之间，凡是听得见的人都会丢下手里的书——或者牌——朝喊声奔去；那群人便四人一排排好队伍，开始绕着校园行进，随着宿舍楼和兄弟会宅子里不断有人跑出来，队伍越拉越长。他们就这么走下去，走下去，臂挽着臂，边舞边唱着桑福德的歌，走过校园里的每一栋宿舍楼，走过每一座兄弟会宅子——时不时停下来喊上一通欢呼，心里却始终惦着同一个目标，也就是橄榄球赛季期间球队住的那座兄弟会宅子。等到啦啦队长们和球队领头走在队伍前头，人群便朝橄榄球场进发，“柴火，新生们，柴火！”的喊声顺着队伍一路传下去。

===Original===
Hugh was always one of the first freshmen to break from the line in his eagerness to get wood. In an incredibly short time he and his classmates had found a large quantity of old lumber, empty boxes, rotten planks, and not very rotten gates. When a light was applied to the clumsy pile of wood, the flames leaped up quickly﻿—someone always seemed to have a supply of kerosene ready﻿—and revealed the excited upper-classmen sitting on the bleachers.

===Chinese===
抢柴火的时候，休总是最先脱队的几个新生之一。在短得叫人难以置信的时间里，他和同学们就搜罗来一大堆旧木料、空箱子、烂木板，还有不算太烂的栅栏门。火苗一触到那堆笨重的柴垛，火头便腾地蹿起——总好像有谁早就备下了一桶煤油——照出看台上坐得兴奋不已的高年级学生。

===Original===
“Dance, freshmen, dance!”

===Chinese===
“跳起来，新生们，跳起来！”

===Original===
Then the freshmen danced around the fire, holding hands and spreading into an ever widening circle as the fire crackled and the flames leaped upward. Slowly, almost impressively, the upper-classmen chanted:

===Chinese===
于是新生们手拉着手，围着火堆跳起舞来；柴火噼啪作响，火苗向上蹿腾，他们的圈子越扩越大。高年级学生缓缓地、近乎肃穆地吟唱起来：

===Original===
> “Round the fire, the freshmen go,
 

 Freshmen go,
 

 Freshmen go;
 

 Round the fire the freshmen go
 

 To cheer Sanford.”

===Chinese===
> “绕着篝火，新生走，
 

 新生走，
 

 新生走；
 

 绕着篝火新生走
 

 为桑福德欢呼。”

===Original===
The song had a dozen stanzas, only the last line of each being different. The freshmen danced until the last verse was sung, which ended with the Sanford cheer:

===Chinese===
这首歌共有十二段，每段只有末一行不同。新生们一直跳到最后一遍唱完，那一段以桑福德的欢呼收尾：

===Original===
> “Closer now the freshmen go,
 

 Freshmen go,
 

 Freshmen go;
 

 Closer now the freshmen go
 

 To cheer﻿—

> *Sanford!*
 
 

 Sanford! Rah, rah!
 

 Sanford! Sanford!
 

 San﻿—San﻿—San﻿—
 

 San﻿—ford, San﻿—ford﻿—San﻿—*ford*!”

===Chinese===
> “越凑越近，新生走，
 

 新生走，
 

 新生走；
 

 越凑越近新生走
 

 来欢呼——

> *桑福德！*
 
 

 桑福德！啦，啦！
 

 桑福德！桑福德！
 

 桑——桑——桑——
 

 桑——福德，桑——福德——桑——*福德*！”

===Original===
While the upper-classmen were singing the last stanza the freshmen slowly closed in on the dying fire. At the first word of the cheer, they stopped, turned toward the grand stand, and joined the cheering. That over, they broke and ran for the bleachers, scrambling up the wooden stands, shoving each other out of the way, laughing and shouting.

===Chinese===
高年级学生唱最后一段的时候，新生们朝着渐渐熄灭的火堆慢慢围拢过去。欢呼的第一个字一出口，他们便停下脚步，转向大看台，跟着喝彩起来。那一通完了，他们一哄而散，朝看台奔去，手脚并用地爬上木头看台，你推我搡，又笑又喊。

===Original===
The football captain usually made a short and very awkward speech, which was madly applauded; perhaps the coach said a few words; two or three cheers were given; and finally everyone rose, took off his hat if he wore one﻿—nearly everyone but the freshmen went bareheaded﻿—and sang the college hymn, simply and religiously. Then the crowd broke, straggling in groups across the campus, chatting, singing, shouting to each other. Suddenly lights began to flash in the dormitory windows. In less than an hour after the first cry of “Peerade!” the men were back in their rooms, once more studying, talking, or playing cards.

===Chinese===
橄榄球队队长照例来一篇简短而十分笨拙的讲话，博得疯狂的掌声；教练或许也说上几句；再喊上两三通欢呼；最后全体起立，戴帽子的——除了新生，几乎人人光着脑袋——把帽子摘下，朴素而虔敬地唱起学院圣歌。随后人群散开，三三两两、零零落落地穿过校园，一路聊天、唱歌、彼此喊话。蓦然间，宿舍楼的窗户里灯光一盏接一盏亮了起来。从第一声“游行喽！”喊响算起，不出一个钟头，大家已经回到各自的房里，重新念书的念书，聊天的聊天，打牌的打牌。

===Original===
It was the smoker rallies, though, that Hugh found the most thrilling, especially the last one before the final game of the season, the “big game” with Raleigh College. There were 1123 students in Sanford, and more than 1000 were at the rally. A rough platform had been built at one end of the gymnasium. On one side of it sat the band, on the other side the Glee Club﻿—and before it the mass of students, smoking cigarettes, corncob pipes, and, occasionally, a cigar. The “smokes” had been furnished free by a local tobacconist; so everybody smoked violently and too much. In half an hour it was almost impossible to see the ceiling through the dull blue haze, and the men in the rear of the gymnasium saw the speakers on the platform dimly through a wavering mist.

===Chinese===
不过，最让休觉得惊心动魄的，还是“烟会”助威大会（smoker rallies），尤其是赛季最后一场比赛——同罗利学院（Raleigh College）的“大赛”——之前的那最后一次。桑福德共有 1123 名学生，到会的超过 1000 人。体育馆的一端搭起了一座粗陋的主席台。台的一侧坐着乐队，另一侧坐着男声合唱团（Glee Club）——台前则是黑压压的一大片学生，抽着香烟、玉米芯烟斗，偶尔还有人抽一支雪茄。“烟”是镇上一家烟商免费供应的；于是人人抽得起劲，抽过了量。不到半个钟头，暗蓝色的烟霭已浓得几乎望不见天花板，体育馆后排的人看台上的演讲者，得透过一层晃动的烟雾，只见影影绰绰的影子。

===Original===
The band played various Sanford songs, and everybody sang. Occasionally Wayne Gifford, the cheerleader, leaped upon the platform, raised a megaphone to his mouth, and shouted, “A regular cheer for Sanford﻿—a regular cheer for Sanford.” Then he lifted his arms above his head, flinging the megaphone aside with the same motion, and waited tense and rigid until the students were on their feet. Suddenly he turned into a mad dervish, twisting, bending, gesticulating, leaping, running back and forth across the platform, shouting, and finally throwing his hands above his head and springing high into the air at the concluding “San﻿—*ford*!”

===Chinese===
乐队奏起一支又一支桑福德的歌，人人放声齐唱。啦啦队长韦恩·吉福德（Wayne Gifford）时不时跃上主席台，举起扩音喇叭喊道：“给桑福德来一支正彩——给桑福德来一支正彩。”随即双臂举过头顶，顺势把喇叭甩向一旁，绷紧了身子直挺挺地候着，直到学生们全体起立。刹那间，他化作一个疯魔的旋转托钵僧，拧腰、屈身、比画、腾跃，在台上来回疾走，高声呐喊，末了随着收尾那声“桑——*福德*！”双手抛过头顶，高高蹦起。

===Original===
The Glee Club sang to mad applause; a tenor twanged a ukulele and moaned various blues; a popular professor told stories, some of them funny, most of them slightly off color; a former cheerleader told of the triumphs of former Sanford teams﻿—and the atmosphere grew denser and denser, bluer and bluer, as the smoke wreathed upward. The thousand boys leaned intently forward, occasionally jumping to their feet to shout and cheer, and then sinking back into their chairs, tense and excited. As each speaker mounted the platform they shouted: “Off with your coat! Off with your coat!” And the speakers, even the professor, had to shed their coats before they were permitted to say a word.

===Chinese===
男声合唱团的演唱博得狂热的掌声；一位男高音拨响尤克里里，呜呜咽咽地唱了几段布鲁斯；一位广得人心的教授讲了几个故事，有的好笑，多数稍稍带了点颜色；一位前任啦啦队长历数历届桑福德球队的赫赫战功——烟雾袅袅上升，空气越来越浓，越来越蓝。上千个男孩全神贯注地前倾着身子，不时跳起来喊叫喝彩，随即又跌回椅中，绷得紧紧的，亢奋不已。每逢有人登台演讲，他们便齐声大喊：“脱掉外衣！脱掉外衣！”于是演讲的人，连教授也在内，都得先把外衣脱了，才被允许开口说一个字。

===Original===
When the team entered, bedlam broke loose. Every student stood on his chair, waved his arms, slapped his neighbor on the back or hugged him wildly, threw his hat in the air, if he had one﻿—and, so great was his training, keeping an eye on the cheerleader, who was on the platform going through a series of indescribable contortions. Suddenly he straightened up, held his hands above his head again, and shouted through his megaphone: “A regular cheer for the team﻿—a regular cheer for the team. Make it big﻿—*big*! Ready﻿—!” Away whirled the megaphone, and he went through exactly the same performance that he had used before in conducting the regular cheer. Gifford looked like an inspired madman, but he knew exactly what he was doing. The students cheered lustily, so lustily that some of them were hoarse the next day. They continued to yell after the cheer was completed, ceasing only when Gifford signaled for silence.

===Chinese===
球队一进场，全场顿时闹翻了天。每个学生都站到椅子上，挥舞手臂，拍邻座的背，或是忘情地搂住他，把帽子抛向空中——如果头上有帽子的话——同时，到底是训练有素，谁都不忘盯住台上的啦啦队长，他正在那里做出一连串难以言状的扭曲动作。突然，他直起身来，重新把双手举过头顶，对着喇叭喊道：“给球队来一支正彩——给球队来一支正彩。喊响亮些——*响亮*！预备——！”喇叭嗖地飞开，他又分毫不差地把方才指挥正彩的那一套从头演了一遍。吉福德看上去像个神灵附体的疯子，可他对自己在做什么知道得一清二楚。学生们纵情欢呼，卖力到第二天有些人嗓子都哑了。欢呼结束后喊声仍不停歇，直到吉福德示意肃静，才渐渐止住。

===Original===
Then there were speeches by each member of the team, all enthusiastically applauded, and finally the speech of the evening, that of the coach, Jack Price. He was a big, compactly built man with regular features, heavy blond hair, and pale, cold blue eyes. He threw off his coat with a belligerent gesture, stuck his hands into his trousers pockets, and waited rigidly until the cheering had subsided. Then he began:

===Chinese===
接着，球队的每名队员都讲了话，个个博得热烈的掌声；最后是当晚压轴的讲话——教练杰克·普赖斯（Jack Price）的演讲。他生得高大敦实，五官端正，一头浓密的金发，一双淡蓝色的冷眼睛。他带着挑衅的姿态甩掉外衣，双手插进裤袋，直挺挺地立着，直等到欢呼平息下去才开口：

===Original===
“Go ahead and yell. It’s easy as hell to cheer here in the gym; but what are you going to do Saturday afternoon?”

===Chinese===
“喊吧，尽管喊。在体育馆里喊两嗓子，容易得要死；可到星期六下午，你们打算干什么？”

===Original===
His voice was sharp with sarcasm, and to the shouts of “Yell! Fight!” that came from all over the gymnasium, he answered, “Yeah, maybe﻿—maybe.” He shifted his position, stepping toward the front of the platform, thrusting his hands deeper into his pockets.

===Chinese===
他的嗓音尖利，满是讥诮；体育馆四面八方涌来“喊呀！拼呀！”的喊声，他只应了一句：“是啊，也许——也许。”他换了个姿势，朝台前跨出一步，双手往裤袋里插得更深。

===Original===
“I’ve seen a lot of football games, and I’ve seen lots of rooters, but this is the goddamndest gang of yellow-bellied quitters that I’ve ever seen. What happened last Saturday when we were behind? I’m asking you; what happened? You quit! Quit like a bunch of whipped curs. God! you’re yellow, yellow as hell. But the team went on fighting﻿—and it won, won in spite of you, won for a bunch of yellow pups. And why? Because the team’s got guts. And when it was all over, you cheered and howled and serpentined and felt big as hell. Lord Almighty! you acted as if you’d done something.”

===Chinese===
“橄榄球赛我见得多了，看台上呐喊助威的我也见得多了，可你们这帮家伙，是我见过的最他妈没种、最爱撂挑子的一群孬种。上星期六咱们落后的时候，出了什么事？我问你们；到底出了什么事？你们撂了挑子！像一帮被打怕了的癞皮狗似的撂了挑子。天哪！你们这些胆小鬼，胆小得没治了。可球队照样在拼——而且赢了，不顾你们赢的，替一帮胆小的狗崽子赢的。为什么？因为球队有种。等到一切收了场，你们倒又欢呼、又吼叫、又拉起长蛇阵扭了个痛快，觉得自己了不起得要命。全能的主啊！瞧你们那副样子，倒像真立了什么大功似的。”

===Original===
His right hand came out of his pocket with a jerk, and he extended a fighting, clenched fist toward his breathless audience. “I’ll tell you something,” he said slowly, viciously; “the team can’t win alone day after tomorrow. *It can’t win alone!* You’ve got to fight. Damn it! *You’ve got to fight!* Raleigh’s good, damn good; it hasn’t lost a game this season﻿—and we’ve got to win, *win*! Do you hear? We’ve got to win! And there’s only one way that we can win, and that’s with every man back of the team. Every goddamned mother’s son of you. The team’s good, but it can’t win unless you fight﻿—*fight*!”

===Chinese===
他的右手猛地从裤袋里抽了出来，朝屏息无声的听众伸出一只攥紧的、好斗的拳头。“我告诉你们一件事，”他说得又慢又狠；“后天，球队孤军奋战是赢不了的。*它孤军奋战是赢不了的！*你们得去拼。妈的！*你们必须去拼！*罗利很强，强得要命；这个赛季它一场都没输——而我们必须赢，*赢*！听见没有？我们必须赢！我们要赢，只有一条路：每一个人都站在球队身后撑腰。你们中间每一个人，他妈的一个都不能少。球队是好，可你们不拼，它就赢不了——*拼*！”

===Original===
Suddenly his voice grew softer, almost gentle. He held out both hands to the boys, who had become so tense that they had forgotten to smoke. “We’ve got to win, fellows, for old Sanford. Are you back of us?”

===Chinese===
突然，他的嗓音柔和了下来，几乎带着温情。他向男孩们伸出双手——他们早已紧张得忘了抽烟。“咱们必须赢，伙计们，为了老桑福德。你们给我们撑腰吗？”

===Original===
“Yes!” The tension shattered into a thousand yells. The boys leaped on the chairs and shouted until they could shout no more. When Gifford called for “a regular cheer for Jack Price” and then one for the team﻿—“Make it the biggest you ever gave”﻿—they could respond with only a hoarse croak.

===Chinese===
“愿意！”满场紧绷的张力哗然碎作千百声呐喊。男孩们跳上椅子，喊到再也喊不动为止。等到吉福德号召“给杰克·普赖斯来一支正彩”、接着再给球队来一支——“喊出你们从来没有过的大动静”——他们能回应的，只剩一声嘶哑的嘎叫。

===Original===
Finally the hymn was sung﻿—at least, the boys tried loyally to sing it﻿—and they stood silent and almost reverent as the team filed out of the gymnasium.

===Chinese===
最后，圣歌也唱了——至少，男孩们忠心耿耿地试着唱了——球队鱼贯走出体育馆时，他们肃立无言，近乎虔敬。

===Original===
Hugh walked back to Surrey Hall with several men. No one said a word except a quiet good night as they parted. Carl was in the room when he arrived. He sank into a chair and was silent for a few minutes.

===Chinese===
休同几个人一路走回萨里楼。分手时除了轻轻道一声晚安，谁也没有说话。他回到房里时，卡尔已经在屋里。他一屁股陷进椅子，好几分钟没有作声。

===Original===
Finally he said in a happy whisper, “Wasn’t it wonderful, Carl?”

===Chinese===
末了，他喜不自胜地悄声说：“简直妙极了，卡尔，是不是？”

===Original===
“Un-huh. Damn good.”

===Chinese===
“嗯呐。真他妈棒。”

===Original===
“Gosh, I hope we win. We’ve *got* to!”

===Chinese===
“天哪，真希望咱们能赢。咱们*非*赢不可！”

===Original===
Carl looked up, his cheeks redder than usual, his eyes glittering. “God, yes!” he breathed piously.

===Chinese===
卡尔抬起头来，脸颊比平时更红，眼睛闪闪发亮。“上帝啊，是啊！”他像做祷告似的低声说。
