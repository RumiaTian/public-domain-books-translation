## XXVII / 第二十七章

===Original===
The next morning Hugh’s mother and father arrived in the automobile. He was to drive them back to Merrytown the day after commencement. At last he stood in the doorway of the Nu Delta house and welcomed his father, but he had forgotten all about that youthful dream. He was merely aware that he was enormously glad to see the “folks” and that his father seemed to be withering into an old man.

===Chinese===
第二天早晨，休的母亲和父亲坐汽车到了。他定于毕业典礼的次日开车送他们回梅里敦。他终于站在“纽德尔塔”宅子的门口，迎接了父亲，只是那青春年代的梦，他早已忘了个一干二净。他只是觉得，见到“家里人”他快活极了；也只觉得，父亲仿佛正在枯萎成一个老人。

===Original===
As the underclassmen departed, the alumni began to arrive. The “five year” classes dressed in extraordinary outfits﻿—Indians, Turks, and men in prison garb roamed the campus. There were youngsters just a year out of college, still looking like undergraduates, still full of college talk. The alumni ranged all the way from these one-year men to the fifty-year men, twelve old men who had come back to Sanford fifty years after their graduation, and two of them had come all the way across the continent. There had been only fifty men originally in that class; and twelve of them were back.

===Chinese===
低年级学生陆续离校，校友们开始到来。过“五年”重聚的各届穿着稀奇古怪的行头﻿——扮印第安人的、扮土耳其人的，还有一身囚服打扮的，在校园里游荡。有些小伙子出校门才一年，看着还像本科生，满口还是学院里的言谈。校友的阵容，从这些出校一年的年轻伙计，一直到五十年的老届友——十二位老人在毕业五十年后回到了桑福德，其中两位是一路横穿了整个大陆赶来的。他们那一届当年拢共只有五十个人；如今回来了十二个。

===Original===
What brought them back? Hugh wondered. He thought he knew, but he couldn’t have given a reason. He watched those old men wandering slowly around the campus, one of them with his grandson who was graduating this year, and he was awed by their age and their devotion to their alma mater. Yes, Henley had been right. Sanford was far from perfect, far from it﻿—a child could see that﻿—but there was something in the college that gripped one’s heart. What faults that old college had; but how one loved her!

===Chinese===
是什么把他们带回来的？休纳闷。他觉得自己知道，可又说不出个理由。他望着那些老人在校园里缓缓地转悠，有一位身边还带着今年毕业的孙子；他们的高龄，他们对母校的一片忠爱，叫他心生敬畏。是啊，亨利说得对。桑福德远非完美，远非﻿——小孩子都看得出来﻿——可这学院里有一种东西，能把人的心攥住。这所老学院毛病何其多；可人们对它爱得又是何其深！

===Original===
Thousands of Japanese lanterns had been strung around the campus; an electric fountain sparkled and splashed its many-colored waters; a band seemed to be playing every hour of the day and night from the bandstand in front of the Union. It was a gay scene, and everybody seemed superbly happy except, possibly, the seniors. They pretended to be happy, but all of them were a little sad, a little frightened. College had been very beautiful﻿—and the “world outside,” what was it? What did it have in store for them?

===Chinese===
成千上万盏日式灯笼挂满了校园；一座电光喷泉波光闪烁，喷洒着五彩的水花；学生会堂前的音乐台上，乐队仿佛白天黑夜没有一刻不在演奏。场面一派欢腾，人人似乎都快活到了十分——可能只有毕业班的人例外。他们装出快活的样子，可个个都有几分伤感，有几分惶恐。大学是那样美丽﻿——而“外面的世界”呢，它是什么？它给他们预备下了什么？

===Original===
There were mothers and fathers there to see their sons receive their degrees, there were the wives and children of the alumni, there were sisters and fiancées of the seniors. Nearly two thousand people; and at least half of the alumni drunk most of the time. Very drunk, many of them, and very foolish, but nobody minded. Somehow everyone seemed to realize that in a few brief days they were trying to recapture a youthful thrill that had gone forever. Some of the drunken ones seemed very silly, some of them seemed almost offensive; all of them were pathetic.

===Chinese===
母亲和父亲们来观看儿子们领取学位，校友的妻子儿女来了，毕业班同学的姐妹和未婚妻们也来了。将近两千人；校友至少有一半多半时间都醉着。其中许多人醉得很厉害，也很糊涂，可谁也不介意。不知怎么，人人似乎都明白：他们是在用短短几天工夫，追回一种永远逝去了的青春震颤。有些醉汉显得非常愚蠢，有些几乎叫人反感；可他们个个都令人心酸。

===Original===
They had come back to Sanford where they had once been so young and exuberant, so tireless in pleasure, so in love with living; and they were trying to pour all that youthful zest into themselves again out of a bottle bought from a bootlegger. Were they having a good time? Who knows? Probably not. A bald-headed man does not particularly enjoy looking at a picture taken in his hirsute youth; and yet there is a certain whimsical pleasure in the memories the picture brings.

===Chinese===
他们回到了桑福德——当年在这里，他们曾那样年轻、那样蓬勃，寻欢作乐不知疲倦，那样热爱生活；如今，他们想靠一瓶从私酒贩子手里买来的酒，把那股青春的兴头重新灌回自己身体里。他们玩得快活吗？谁知道呢？多半不。一个秃顶的男人看着自己毛发浓密的青年时代拍下的照片，并不会有特别的兴致；然而照片勾起的回忆里，自有一种忽发奇想的乐趣。

===Original===
For three days there was much gaiety, much singing of class songs, constant parading, dances, speechmaking, class circuses, and endless shaking of hands and exchanging of reminiscences. The seniors moved through all the excitement quietly, keeping close to their relatives and friends. Graduation wasn’t so thrilling as they had expected it to be; it was more sad. The alumni seemed to be having a good time; they were ridiculously boyish: only the seniors were grave, strangely and unnaturally dignified.

===Chinese===
一连三天，欢庆不断：一遍遍唱着班歌，不停地游行、跳舞、发表演说，还有各年级的杂耍会，握手握个没完，叙旧叙个没完。毕业班的同学静静地穿行在这一片喧腾里，紧紧挨着自己的亲友。毕业并不像他们原先指望的那样令人激动；它更叫人伤感。校友们似乎玩得很快活；他们孩子气得可笑：只有毕业班的人神情庄重，庄重得古怪，庄重得不自然。

===Original===
Most of the alumni left the night before the graduation exercises. The parents and fiancées remained. They stood in the middle of the campus and watched the seniors, clad in caps and gowns, line up before the Union at the orders of the class marshal.

===Chinese===
大多数校友在毕业典礼仪式的前夜离去了。父母们和未婚妻们留了下来。他们站在校园中央，看着身穿方帽学袍的毕业班同学，在毕业班领队的号令下，于学生会堂前列队。

===Original===
Finally, the procession, the grand marshal, a professor, in the lead with a wand in his hand, then President Culver and the governor of the State, then the men who were to receive honorary degrees﻿—a writer, a college president, a philanthropist, a professor, and three politicians﻿—then the faculty in academic robes, their many-colored hoods brilliant against their black gowns. And last the seniors, a long line of them marching in twos headed by their marshal.

===Chinese===
终于，游行队伍出场了：总领队是一位教授，手执一根权杖走在最前头；随后是卡尔弗校长和州长；再后是将要接受荣誉学位的先生们﻿——一位作家、一位大学校长、一位慈善家、一位教授，外加三位政客﻿——然后是身着学袍的教员们，五彩的垂布衬着黑袍，鲜亮夺目。最后是毕业班，由他们的领队带头，两人一排，长长的一列。

===Original===
The visitors streamed after them into the chapel. The seniors sat in their customary seats, the faculty and the men who were to receive honorary degrees on a platform that had been built at the altar. After they were seated, everything became a blur to Hugh. He hardly knew what was happening. He saw his father and mother sitting in the transept. He thought his mother was crying. He hoped not.﻿ ﻿… Someone prayed stupidly. There was a hymn.﻿ ﻿… What was it Cynthia had said? Oh, yes: “I can’t marry a stranger.” Well, they weren’t exactly strangers.﻿ ﻿… He was darn glad he had gone to New York.﻿ ﻿… The president seemed to be saying over and over again, “By the power invested in me﻿ ﻿…” and every time that he said it, Professor Blake would slip the loop of a colored hood over the head of a writer or a politician﻿—and then it was happening all over again.

===Chinese===
来宾们潮水般跟着涌进礼拜堂。毕业班同学坐了他们惯坐的座位，教员们和将要接受荣誉学位的先生们坐在圣坛前搭起的一座平台上。落座之后，在休眼里一切都化作了一片模糊。他几乎不知道正在发生什么。他看见父亲和母亲坐在耳堂里。他以为母亲在哭。但愿不是。﻿……有人愚不可及地做了祷告。唱了一首圣歌。﻿……辛西娅那时说过什么来着？哦，对了：“我不能嫁给一个陌生人。”唉，他们也说不上完全是陌生人。﻿……他真是庆幸自己去了一趟纽约。﻿……校长好像在一遍又一遍地说：“以授予我的权力﻿……”每说一遍，布莱克教授就把一圈彩色的垂布套上某位作家或政客的头﻿——然后这一切又从头再来一遍。

===Original===
Suddenly the class marshal motioned to the seniors to rise. They put on their mortarboards. The president said once more, “By the power invested in me.﻿ ﻿…” The seniors filed by the president, and the grand marshal handed each of them a roll of parchment tied with blue and orange ribbons. Hugh felt a strange thrill as he took his. He was graduated; he was a bachelor of science.﻿ ﻿… Back again to their seats. Someone was pronouncing benediction.﻿ ﻿… Music from the organ﻿—marching out of the chapel, the surge of friends﻿—his father shaking his hand, his mother’s arms around his neck; she *was* crying.﻿ ﻿…

===Chinese===
毕业班领队忽然示意毕业班同学起立。他们戴上学位方帽。校长又说了一遍：“以授予我的权力。﻿……”毕业班同学从校长面前鱼贯而过，总领队递给每人一卷用蓝橙两色丝带扎着的羊皮纸文凭。休接过自己那一卷时，感到一阵奇异的震颤。他毕业了；他是理学士了。﻿……又回到座位上。有人在念祝福文。﻿……管风琴奏起乐来﻿——队伍走出礼拜堂，朋友们潮水般涌上来﻿——父亲握着他的手，母亲的胳膊搂着他的脖子；她*确实*哭了。﻿……

===Original===
Graduation was over, and, with it Hugh’s college days. Many of the seniors left at once. Hugh would have liked to go, too, but his father wanted to stay one more day in Haydensville. Besides, there was a final senior dance that night, and he thought that Hugh ought to attend it.

===Chinese===
毕业典礼结束了，休的大学生涯也随之结束。许多毕业班同学当即离校。休也想走，可父亲想在海登斯维尔再待一天。再说，当晚还有最后一场毕业班舞会，他觉得休理当出席。

===Original===
Hugh did go to the dance, but somehow it brought him no pleasure. Although it was immensely decorous, it reminded him of Cynthia. He thought of her tenderly. The best little girl he’d ever met.﻿ ﻿… He danced on, religiously steering around the sisters and fiancées of his friends, but he could not enjoy the dance. Shortly after eleven he slipped out of the gymnasium and made one last tour of the campus.

===Chinese===
休确实去了舞会，可不知怎么，它没给他带来半点快乐。尽管这场舞会规矩得体到了极点，它还是让他想起了辛西娅。他想起她，心里一片温柔。他平生遇见过的最好的姑娘。﻿……他一支接一支跳下去，一丝不苟地绕开朋友们的姐妹和未婚妻，可他没法从跳舞里得到快乐。十一点刚过，他就溜出体育馆，把校园巡了最后一圈。

===Original===
It was a moonlight night, and the campus was mysterious with shadows. The elms shook their leaves whisperingly; the tower of the chapel looked like magic tracery in the moonlight. He paused before Surrey Hall, now dark and empty. Good old Carl.﻿ ﻿… Carl and Cynthia? He wondered.﻿ ﻿… Pudge had roomed there, too. He passed on. Keller Hall, Cynthia and Norry.﻿ ﻿… “God, what a beast I was that night. How white Norry was﻿—and Cynthia, too,” Cynthia again. She’d always be a part of Sanford to him. On down to the lake to watch the silver path of the moonlight and the heavy reflections near the shore. Swimming, canoeing, skating﻿—he and Cynthia in the woods beyond.﻿ ﻿… On back to the campus, around the buildings, every one of them filled with memories. Four years﻿—four beautiful, wonderful years.﻿ ﻿… Good old Sanford.﻿ ﻿…

===Chinese===
那是个有月光的夜晚，校园因满地暗影而显得神秘。榆树簌簌地摇着叶子；礼拜堂的塔楼在月光里宛如一幅魔幻的镂花细纹。他在萨里楼前驻足——楼里如今黑着，空着。好一个老卡尔。﻿……卡尔和辛西娅？他纳闷。﻿……帕吉当初也住在那儿的。他继续往前走。凯勒楼，辛西娅和诺里。﻿……“天哪，那天夜里我是头十足的畜生。诺里多够意思﻿——辛西娅也是。”又是辛西娅。对他来说，她将永远是桑福德的一部分。再往下走到湖边，看月光铺出的银色水路，看近岸处浓重的倒影。游泳、划独木舟、滑冰﻿——还有他和辛西娅在湖那头的树林里。﻿……再折回校园，绕着各座楼走，每一座楼里都盛满了回忆。四年﻿——四年美丽的、美妙的年华。﻿……好一个老桑福德。﻿……

===Original===
Midnight struck. Someone turned a switch somewhere. The Japanese lanterns suddenly lost their colors and faded to gray balloons in the moonlight. Some men were singing on the Union steps. It was a few seniors, Hugh knew; they had been singing for an hour.

===Chinese===
午夜钟声敲响。不知哪儿有人拉了一下电闸。日式灯笼霎时褪尽了颜色，在月光里化成一只只灰色的气球。学生会堂的台阶上有几个人在唱歌。休知道，那是几个毕业班同学；他们已经唱了一个钟头了。

===Original===
He stood in the center of the campus and listened, his eyes full of tears. Earnestly, religiously, the men sang, their voices rich with emotion:

===Chinese===
他站在校园中央听着，眼里满是泪水。那些人唱得恳切，唱得虔诚，嗓音里饱含深情：

===Original===
> “Sanford, Sanford, mother of men,
 

 Love us, guard us, hold us true.
 

 Let thy arms enfold us;
 

 Let thy truth uphold us.
 

 Queen of colleges, mother of men﻿—
 

 Alma mater﻿—Sanford﻿—hail!
 

 Alma-mater﻿—Hail!﻿—Hail!”

===Original===
Hugh walked slowly across the campus toward the Nu Delta house. He was both happy and sad﻿—happy because the great adventure was before him with all its mystery, sad because he was leaving something beautiful behind.﻿ ﻿…

===Chinese===
休缓缓穿过校园，朝“纽德尔塔”宅子走去。他既快活又伤感﻿——快活，是因为那场大冒险连同它全部的神秘就在眼前；伤感，是因为他正把一样美丽的东西留在身后。﻿……
