## XXI / 第二十一章

===Original===
For some minutes after Claire had left him Bill remained where he was, motionless. He felt physically incapable of moving. All the strength that was in him he was using to throw off the insidious poison of her parting speech, and it became plainer to him with each succeeding moment that he would have need of strength.

It is part of the general irony of things that in life’s crises a man’s good qualities are often the ones that help him least, if indeed they do not actually turn treacherously and fight against him. It was so with Bill. Modesty, if one may trust to the verdict of the mass of mankind, is a good quality. It sweetens the soul and makes for a kindly understanding of one’s fellows. But arrogance would have served Bill better now. It was his fatal habit of self-depreciation that was making Claire’s words so specious, as he stood there trying to cast them from his mind. Who was he, after all, that he should imagine that he had won on his personal merits a girl like Elizabeth Boyd?

He had never been able to look on himself, after the manner of many of the men he knew, as a tremendous fellow, the center of a wondering world, Nature’s supreme handiwork. He was conscious﻿—too conscious﻿—of his shortcomings. From boyhood the sort of man he had admired was the capable, dashing, quick-witted, debonair fellow, the man you could never take without his answer, whether you attacked him with a sword or with an epigram, and the realization of the gulf that separated himself from this ideal male had engendered humility, increasing with the years. He had the not very common type of mind that perceives the merit in others more readily than their faults and in himself the faults more readily than the merit. Time and the society of a great number of men of different ranks and natures had rid him of the outer symbol of this type of mind, which is shyness, but it had left him still unconvinced that he amounted to anything very much as an individual.

克莱尔（Claire）离去之后，比尔（Bill）在原地待了好几分钟，一动不动。他只觉得浑身瘫软，动弹不得。他拿出了周身的气力，去驱除她临别那番话里暗中蚀人的毒；而一刻一刻过去，他益发分明地感到，这份气力往后还少不得要用。

世事通盘的嘲弄之中有一桩是：人生危急关头，一个人的种种美德往往最帮不上他的忙，有时甚至当真倒戈相向，反过来同他作对。比尔正是如此。谦逊，倘若可以信从世人的公断，是一种美德。它润泽灵魂，使人对自己的同类怀一份厚道的体谅。可此刻，狂妄自大反倒更能给比尔帮上忙。他站在那里，竭力想把那些话从心里撵出去，而叫克莱尔那些话显得如此似是而非的，正是他那要命的自贬习气。说到底，他是何等样人，竟敢妄想凭自身的长处赢得伊丽莎白·博伊德（Elizabeth Boyd）这样的姑娘？

他从来没能像他认识的许多男人那样，把自己看作一位了不得的人物——万众惊叹的世界以他为中心，他是大自然至高无上的手笔。他对自己的短处心知肚明——太心知肚明了。打从男童时代起，他所敬慕的便是那一路精明干练、骁捷争先、机敏过人、风流倜傥的角色——不论你用剑向他进击，还是用隽语向他讥刺，都休想制住他而听不到他还口；而一旦悟到自己与这位理想男子之间隔着一道鸿沟，谦卑便在他心里生了根，并且逐年滋长。他那种头脑属于不太多见的一型：看别人的长处比看别人的短处来得容易，看自己的短处又比看自己的长处来得容易。岁月，加上同许许多多出身不同、性情各异的男子交游往还，早已使他褪尽了这类头脑的外在表征——羞怯；却仍没能叫他相信，自己作为一个体，究竟算得上多重要的人物。

This was the thought that met him every time he tried to persuade himself that what Claire had said was ridiculous, the mere parting shaft of an angry woman. With this thought as an ally her words took on a plausibility hard to withstand. Plausible! That was the devil of it. By no effort could he blind himself to the fact that they were that. In the light of Claire’s insinuations what had seemed coincidences took on a more sinister character. It had seemed to him an odd and lucky chance that Nutty Boyd should have come to the rooms which he was occupying that night, seeking a companion. Had it been chance? Even at the time he had thought it strange that, on the strength of a single evening spent together, Nutty should have invited a total stranger to make an indefinite visit at his home. Had there been design behind the invitation?

Bill began to walk slowly to the house. He felt tired and unhappy. He meant to go to bed and try to sleep away these wretched doubts and questionings. Daylight would bring relief.

As he reached the open front door he caught the sound of voices, and paused for an instant, almost unconsciously, to place them. They came from one of the rooms upstairs. It was Nutty speaking now, and it was impossible for Bill not to hear what he said, for Nutty had abandoned his customary drawl in favor of a high, excited tone.

每逢他竭力说服自己——克莱尔那番话荒唐无稽，不过是一个恼怒的女人临别掷来的暗箭——总有这个念头迎面拦住他。有了这念头充当盟友，她的话便显出一种难以抵挡的貌似有理。貌似有理！坏就坏在这里。任凭他怎样使劲，他都无法闭上眼睛不承认：那些话确乎如此。经克莱尔那番含沙射影一映照，原先似乎只是巧合的种种事情，便染上了更不祥的色彩。那一晚，纳蒂·博伊德（Nutty Boyd）会跑到他落脚的那几间屋里来找伴儿，他向来觉得是一桩古怪而走运的偶然。那当真是偶然吗？单凭同处一晚的交情，纳蒂便邀请一个素昧平生的人到他家里不限时日地住下去，当时他就已经觉得蹊跷。这份邀请的背后，莫非另有布置？

比尔开始缓步朝屋子走去。他觉得又疲倦又难受。他打算上床睡下，想法子把这些凄惶的疑虑和诘问都在睡乡里睡化了。天光一亮，自会松快。

走到敞开着的前门口，他听见屋里有说话的声音，便几乎不由自主地驻足片刻，想辨出那是谁的声音。声音从楼上一个房间里传来。此刻说话的是纳蒂，而比尔想不听见他的话也办不到，因为纳蒂已经抛下惯常的拖腔，换上了又高又激动的调门。

That was all that Bill heard, for he had stumbled across the hall and was in his room, sitting on the bed and staring into the darkness with burning eyes. The door banged behind him.

So it was true!

这么说，那是真的！
