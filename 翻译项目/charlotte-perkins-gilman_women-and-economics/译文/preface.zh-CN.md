## Preface / 序言

===Original===
This book is written to offer a simple and natural explanation of one of the most common and most perplexing problems of human life﻿—a problem which presents itself to almost every individual for practical solution, and which demands the most serious attention of the moralist, the physician, and the sociologist﻿—

===Chinese===
本书旨在就人类生活中最常见、最令人困惑的问题之一，提出一个简单而自然的解释——这个问题几乎向每一个个人呈现，要求其加以实际的解决，又要求道德家、医生与社会学家予以最严肃的关注——

===Original===
To show how some of the worst evils under which we suffer, evils long supposed to be inherent and ineradicable in our natures, are but the result of certain arbitrary conditions of our own adoption, and how, by removing those conditions, we may remove the evils resultant﻿—

===Chinese===
要表明：我们所承受的一些最深重的祸害——那些长久以来被认定与生俱来、根植于我们本性而无法根除的祸害——不过是我们自行采纳的某些武断条件所致的结果；而只要除去那些条件，我们便可能除去随之而生的祸害——

===Original===
To point out how far we have already gone in the path of improvement, and how irresistibly the social forces of today are compelling us further, even without our knowledge and against our violent opposition﻿—an advance which may be greatly quickened by our recognition and assistance﻿—

===Chinese===
要指出我们在改良之路上已经走出多远，指出今日的种种社会力量正如何不可抗拒地驱策我们继续前行——哪怕我们并不自知，哪怕我们激烈反对——而这一进程，若得我们的认识与协助，便可大大加快——

===Original===
To reach in especial the thinking women of today, and urge upon them a new sense, not only of their social responsibility as individuals, but of their measureless racial importance as makers of men.

===Chinese===
要特别触及今日有思想的妇女，敦促她们树立一种新的意识：不仅意识到自己作为个体的社会责任，更意识到自己作为「人的造就者」（makers of men）所具有的无从估量的种族重要性。

===Original===
It is hoped also that the theory advanced will prove sufficiently suggestive to give rise to such further study and discussion as shall prove its error or establish its truth.

===Chinese===
同时也希望，本书所提出的理论足以发人深思，足以引出更进一步的研究与讨论，从而证明其误，或确立其真。

===Original===
Charlotte Perkins Stetson

===Chinese===
夏洛特·珀金斯·斯泰森（Charlotte Perkins Stetson）
