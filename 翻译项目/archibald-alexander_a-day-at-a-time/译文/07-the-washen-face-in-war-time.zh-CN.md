## VII The “Washen Face” in War Time / VII 战时的「洗净的脸」

===Original===
> “And he washed his face, and went out, and refrained himself, and said, Set on bread.”
> — Genesis 43:31

===Chinese===
> 「他洗了脸出来，勉强隐忍，吩咐人摆饭。」
> — Genesis 43:31

===Original===
That is what Joseph did when his feelings nearly overmastered him at the sight of his brother Benjamin standing before him, all unconscious of who he was. He “sought where to weep,” says the record with quaint matter-of-factness, for of course he did not want his brothers to see him weeping just yet. So “he entered into his chamber and wept there.” But Joseph’s secret affections being thus recognised and allowed their expression, he had a duty to perform. He put a curb upon his feelings. He took a firm grip of himself. He “washed his face and went out, and refrained himself, and said, Set on bread.” One cannot help admiring that. It was a fine thing to do.

And there are two classes of people in our own time in whom one sees this same attitude, and never without a strange stirring of heart.

===Chinese===
弟弟便雅悯站在他面前，全然不知他是谁；约瑟一见之下，情感几乎不能自持，而他所做的正是这样一件事。记载以古朴的平实口吻说，他「急忙寻找可哭之地」，因为他自然还不愿意让哥哥们看见他流泪。于是他「进入自己的屋里，哭了一场」。但约瑟这份深藏的手足之情既已得到承认、得到抒发，他就还有当尽的本分要做。他给情感勒上了缰绳，紧紧地把握住自己。他「洗了脸出来，勉强隐忍，吩咐人摆饭」。这一切叫人不能不钦佩。这实在是一件美事。

而在我们这个时代，有两类人身上正可以见到这种同样的态度，并且每次都免不了令人心头起一种奇异的激动。

===Original===
The first and most honourable are those who have already tasted of the sorrows of war and lost some dear one in the service of King and country. We speak of the courage and sacrifice of our men, and we cannot speak too highly or too gratefully about that. But there is something else that runs it very close, if it does not exceed it, and that is the quiet heroism and endurance of many of those who have been bereaved. Time and again one sees them facing up to all life’s calls upon them with a marvellous spirit of self-restraint. God only knows how sad and sore their loss is. And upon what takes place when they enter into their chamber and shut the door and face their sorrow alone with God, it does not beseem us to intrude. Such sorrow is a sacred thing, but at least we know, and are glad to know, that God Himself is there as He is nowhere else. It is never wrong and never weak to let the tears come before Him. As a father understands, so does He know all about it. As a mother comforteth, so does the touch of His Hand quieten and console.

===Chinese===
第一类也是最高贵的一类，是那些已经尝过战争之苦、在效命君王与国家时失去了至亲的人。我们讲论我们将士的勇气与牺牲，无论说得多么崇高、多么感恩都不为过。可是另有一件事与之紧紧相随，即便不曾超过，至少也不相上下，那就是许多丧亲者身上那种安静的英雄气概与坚忍。一次又一次，你会看见他们以一种令人惊叹的自我克制精神，迎对生活向他们提出的一切要求。他们的损失何等悲伤、何等惨痛，唯有上帝知道。至于他们走进自己的屋子、关上门、独自与上帝面对悲伤时发生了什么，我们不便闯入窥探。这样的悲哀是神圣的事；但我们至少知道，并且乐于知道，上帝亲自在那里，非任何别处可比。在他面前让眼泪流出来，从来不是错，也从不软弱。人间的父亲怎样体察，他也照样尽知这一切；人间的母亲怎样安慰，他手的抚摸也照样使人心平、得安慰。

===Original===
But what fills one with reverent admiration is that so many of those whose hearts we know have been so cruelly wounded have set up a new and noble precedent in the matter of courage and self-control. They are not shirking any of the duties of life. They are claiming no exemptions on the ground of their sorrow, and they excuse themselves from no duty merely because it would hurt. They wear their hurt gently like a flower in the breast. They carry their sorrow like a coronet. Out from their secret chambers they come, with washen face and brave lips to do their duty and refrain themselves. How beautiful it is! What a fine thing to see! The sorrowing mother of a noble young fellow I am proud to have known, said to a friend recently who was marvelling at her fortitude, “My boy was very brave and I must try to be brave, too, for his sake.” Dear, gentle mother! One cannot speak worthily about a spirit so sweet and gracious as that. One can only bow the head and breathe the inward prayer, “God send thee peace, brave heart!” But, surely, to accept sorrow in that fashion is to entertain unawares an angel of God! The feeling which underlies this new etiquette of sorrow with the washen face is not very easily put into words. But it rests, I think, upon the dim sense that the death which ends those young lives on this noble field of battle is something different from the ordinary bleak fact of mortality. If death is ever glorious, it is when it comes to the soldier fighting for a pure and worthy cause. There is something more than sorrow, there is even a quiet and reverent pride in the remembrance that the beloved life was given as “a ransom for many.” When one thinks what we are fighting for, one can hardly deny to the fallen the supreme honour of the words “for Christ’s sake.” And it is not death to fall so. Rather is it the finding of life larger and more glorious still. It is that that marks the war-mourners of today as a caste royal and apart. It is that that moves so many of them by an inward instinct to wear their sorrow royally. Hidden in the heart of their grief is a tender and wistful pride. Lowell has put this feeling into very fine words:

===Chinese===
但最令人肃然起敬的是：我们知道这些人的心曾受何等残酷的创伤，而其中竟有这么多的人，在勇气与自制的问题上立下了一个崭新而高贵的典范。他们不逃避生活的任何本分。他们不因悲伤而要求任何豁免，也绝不因为尽某项本分会痛就推托不做。他们把伤痛温柔地佩戴着，像胸前的一朵花；他们把悲伤承担起来，像一顶冠冕。他们从隐秘的内室走出来，带着洗净的脸（washen face）与坚毅的嘴唇，去尽他们的本分，去克制他们自己。这是何等美丽！看在眼里是何等美好！有一位高贵的青年，我以认识他为荣；他那哀伤的母亲近来对一位惊叹她坚毅的朋友说：「我的孩子非常勇敢，为了他的缘故，我也必须努力勇敢。」亲爱而温柔的母亲啊！对这样一种甜美、仁厚的精神，人的言语是不配的；只能低下头来，在心中默祷一句：「愿上帝赐你平安，勇敢的心！」然而，以这样的方式领受悲哀，岂不正是不知不觉间接待了上帝的天使！这种以洗净的脸为表征的新哀悼礼节，其底下的情感并不容易用言语道出。但我想，它立足于一种朦胧的感觉：在这片高贵的战场上终结那些年轻生命的死亡，与寻常那种冰冷无情的死亡事实，是有所不同的。倘若死亡也有荣耀可言，那便是在它临到一个为纯洁而高贵的事业作战的士兵身上的时候。在追念之中，有比悲伤更多的东西，甚至还有一种安静而虔敬的骄傲——那亲爱的生命是献上作了「多人赎价」的。当人想到我们是在为何而战，就几乎不能不把「为基督的缘故」这一至高尊荣加给那些倒下的人。这样倒下，并不是死；毋宁说，那是发现了更宽广、更荣耀的生命。正是这一点，使今日战争中的居丧者成为一个尊贵而独特的族类；正是这一点，凭着一种内在的本能，打动他们中间如此多的人，叫他们以王者之风佩戴自己的悲伤。在他们悲伤的内里，藏着一种温柔而怅惘的骄傲。洛威尔（Lowell）已把这种情感化作了极美的诗句：

===Original===
> “I, with uncovered head,
> Salute the sacred dead,
> Who went and who return not—
> Say not so.
> ’Tis not the grapes of Canaan that repay,
> But the high faith that fails not by the way.
> Virtue treads paths that end not in the grave;
> No bar of endless night exiles the brave,
> And, to the saner mind,
> We rather seem the dead that stayed behind.”

===Chinese===
> 「我，摘帽露顶，
> 向神圣的亡者致意，
> 他们一去，不复回还——
> 切莫这样说。
> 酬报我们的，并非迦南的葡萄，
> 乃是那途中不挫的高信。
> 德行踏出的路，不终于坟墓；
> 无尽长夜的门闩，放逐不了勇者；
> 而在较清醒的心智看来，
> 我们反倒像是滞留在后的死者。」

===Original===
The other class who are teaching us a new and better way to bear burdens are the friends at home of those who are on active service. Men, with sons in the trenches, are going about our streets these days almost as if nothing were happening, making it a point of honour not to let the lurking fear in their hearts have any outward expression. Wives and mothers and sisters are filling their hands and their hearts full of duties, and putting such a brave face on life that you would never suspect they have a chamber that could tell a different tale. It is absolutely splendid. There is no other word for it. I walked a street-length with a young wife recently whose man has been ill and out of the fight for a while. She hoped that he might have been sent home, and who can blame her? but he has gone back to the trenches instead. And how bravely and quietly she spoke of it! Pride, a true and noble pride in her beloved soldier, a resolute endeavour to do her difficult bit as uncomplainingly and willingly as he—it seemed to me that I saw all that in her brave smile. And I said to myself, “Here is the cult of the washen face! And a noble cult too! Britain surely deserves to win when her women carry their crosses so!”

===Chinese===
另一类正在教我们以更新、更好的方式背负重担的人，是现役军人留在家中的亲人们。儿子们身在战壕的父亲们，这些日子照常在我们的街上来来往往，几乎像什么事都没有发生，他们把不让心中潜伏的恐惧有丝毫外露，当作荣誉攸关之事。妻子、母亲和姊妹们，双手与心头都装满了当尽的本分，对生活摆出如此勇敢的面容，你绝不会疑心她们还有一间内室，能讲出另一番光景。这实在壮美无比——再没有别的字眼可以形容。前不久，我陪一位年轻的妻子走了一程街路；她的丈夫病了，已有一阵子离开战斗。她曾盼望他会被送回家来，这又怎能怪她？可他却已重回战壕。而她说起这件事来，是何等勇敢、何等安静！自豪，是对她那亲爱的士兵的一份真切而高贵的自豪；是一种坚定的努力，要像他一样毫无怨言、甘心乐意地尽她自己那份艰难的本分——在我看来，这一切都在她那勇敢的微笑里。我对自己说：「这就是洗净脸的风尚！而且是一种高贵的风尚！英国的妇女这样背负她们的十字架，英国实在配得胜利！」

===Original===
It is easy, of course, to read the thought in their minds. Our men, they say, are splendid, why should we be doleful and despondent? They have made a new virtue of cheerfulness; let us try to learn it too. They have offered everything in a cause which it is an honour to help in any degree; let us lay beside theirs the worthy sacrifice of the washen face and a brave restraint. Such, I imagine, is the unconscious kind of reasoning which results in the resolute and cheerful bearing you may see on all sides of you every day.

And wherever it is seen, it carries its blessing with it. Others with their own private burdens and anxieties are encouraged to hold on to that hope and cheerfulness which are just the homely side of our faith in God and in the righteousness of our cause.

===Chinese===
当然，他们心里的想法不难读出。他们说：我们的人那样出色，我们又何必哭丧着脸、垂头丧气？他们已把开朗愉快造成一种新的美德，让我们也试着学会它。他们已在一项但凡出力分毫都属光荣的事业中献上了一切；让我们把洗净的脸与勇敢的克制这份配得上的祭，摆放在他们的祭旁。我想，正是这种不知不觉的推理，造就了你每天在四围都能见到的那种坚毅而欢欣的风度。

而这种风度无论在哪里出现，都带着它自己的祝福。那些自己也有私人的重担与忧虑的人受到激励，得以持守住那份盼望与乐观——那正是我们对上帝、对我们事业之正义的信心最家常的一面。

===Original===
The cult of the washen face is contagious. It spreads like a beneficent stain. And since it is entirely praiseworthy, we can but wish it to spread more and more. Those who come out from the chambers where they have kept company with sorrow or anxiety, to face life and duty with shining face and mastered feelings, are not only proving their faith in the Divine Strength, they are making a precious contribution to the moral steadfastness of the nation.

“And he washed his face and went out and refrained himself.” Good man!

===Chinese===
洗净脸的风尚是有感染性的。它像一种有益的染渍一般蔓延开来。既然它全然值得称赞，我们只能愿它越传越广。那些从曾与悲伤或忧虑相伴的内室中走出来，以光亮的脸和制伏了的情感去面对生活与本分的人，不但在证明他们对神圣能力的信心，也是在为这个国家道德上的坚定作出宝贵的贡献。

「他洗了脸出来，勉强隐忍。」好样的！

Prayer

We bless Thee, O God, for the assurance that Thine ear is ever open to our cry, that it is never wrong to take our sorrows and our cares to Thee. But help us also, endowed with Thy strength in our secret chambers, to bear our burdens bravely in the sight of men. For Thy Name’s sake. Amen.
