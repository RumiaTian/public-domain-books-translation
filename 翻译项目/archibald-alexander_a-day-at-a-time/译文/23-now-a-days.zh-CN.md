## XXIII Now-a-Days / XXIII 如今

===Original===
> “There be many servants nowadays that break away every man from his master.”
> — 1 Samuel 25:10

===Chinese===
> 「近来悖逆主人奔逃的仆人甚多。」
> — 1 Samuel 25:10

===Original===
Nabal, says the Bible, was a churl. When David sent his men to request some provender, in return for services rendered, this ill-mannered sheep-farmer broke out, “Who is David? There be many servants nowadays that break away every man from his master.” It was a singularly rude and ungracious reply, all things considered. But it is not about Nabal’s truculence I wish to speak. I want you to think about that phrase he used, and the tone in which it was said. “Nowadays.” The implication, of course, is that servants did not break away from their masters in *his* young days. Things were different in the times *he* could remember.

You will recognise this peculiar intonation of “Nowadays” as something fairly familiar. You hear it yet, quite often. Nowadays the Church has lost caste. Nowadays the Bible is a neglected book. Nowadays faith is on the wane, and most people don’t believe anything at all. There are many such sentences, beginning with the word “nowadays” and sounding like a chant on a minor key.

This pessimistic philosophy is difficult to fight, for it is unsubstantial, and dissolves like mist whenever you come to close quarters. But there are three queries I have noted in my Bible opposite that “Nowadays” of Nabal.

===Chinese===
圣经说，拿八是个凶恶愚顽的人。大卫打发人去向他要些粮草，作为已往效劳的回报，这个无礼的牧羊场主冲口喊道：「大卫是谁？近来悖逆主人奔逃的仆人甚多。」平心而论，这是一个出奇粗鲁、毫无恩义的答复。但我要说的并不是拿八的凶横。我要你们想一想他用的那个字眼，以及他说这话时的语气。「如今」。言下之意当然是：在*他*年轻那会儿，仆人是不会悖逆主人奔逃的。在*他*记得起的时代里，事情可不是这样。

你们会听出来，「如今」这种特别的腔调，是相当耳熟的。你们如今还时常听见它。如今教会已经失势。如今圣经成了一本无人理会的书。如今信仰日渐衰落，大多数人对什么都不信了。这样的句子很多，都用「如今」一词开头，听起来像用小调吟出的哀歌。

这种悲观的哲学很难驳斥，因为它虚而无物，你一逼近，它就像雾一样消散。但在我的圣经里，拿八那句「如今」旁边，我记下了三个问句。

===Original===
And the first is—What about the man himself? Judge his philosophy by his actions. Nabal apparently believed that servants were getting entirely out of hand, and he speaks as if he remembered something very different in his own early days. Very good. What was he doing to maintain the old standards? Nothing, less than nothing. His personal manners and behaviour were such that servants would be very ready to break away on that farm, I should think. Now, what business has Nabal to go whining, in general terms, mark you, about servants nowadays, when he behaves like a boor to his own? For any declension which he may see about him, he is himself largely responsible.

I think that it is a perfectly fair line of argument, and it disposes of quite a number of pious “inexactitudes.” When I hear a man talking about the lost influence of the Church nowadays, I am always tempted to inquire what his own relation to it is, whether he is loyally supporting it and working in its interests, for experience has taught me that a very great deal of exaltation of the Church’s past records, at the expense of its position today, comes from men who are themselves doing absolutely nothing to help it on its way. There are exceptions, of course, but, as a rule, it is not the active workers in any worthy cause who are lamenting its failure. The men who think the country is going to the dogs are themselves to be found, for the most part, lolling in the clubs. It is not the pledged and active member of Christ’s kingdom who thinks it is disappearing from the earth. And to those who are fond of the nowadays type of complaint, I would suggest the inquiry—What about yourself? Are you helping to keep up the old standards as you say you remember them? Or is your influence also tending to set this ball of the earth rolling in the very direction you deplore, namely, down the hill?

===Chinese===
第一个问句是——这人自己怎么样？要用他的行为来判断他的哲学。拿八显然相信仆人们已经全然管束不住，他说话的口吻，仿佛他记得自己早年完全是另一番光景。很好。那么，他为维护旧日的水准做了些什么呢？什么也没做，比什么也没做还少。以他个人的举止品行而论，我想，在他那座农场上，仆人们巴不得早早走脱。请注意：拿八对待自家的仆人举止像个粗汉，却去泛泛地——请注意，是泛泛地——哀叹什么「如今的仆人」，他哪里来的资格呢？他在四围所见到的任何衰败，他自己要负大半的责任。

我认为这是一条完全公允的论证思路，它足以打发掉不少虔敬而欠精确的说法。每当我听见有人谈论如今教会失去了影响力，我总想问一句：他自己与教会的关系如何？他是否忠诚地支持教会、为教会的利益出力？因为经验告诉我，凡是抬高教会往昔的记录、却贬损它今日地位的话，十有八九出自这样一些人——他们自己丝毫不出力，帮教会行进一程。例外自然有，但一般而论，为一桩有价值的事业哀叹其失败的人，绝不是它那些积极做工的人。认为国家正走向败亡的人，多半可以在俱乐部里懒洋洋地躺着找到。认为基督的国正从地上消失的，也绝不会是那立了誓约、积极尽力的国民。对那些偏爱「如今」式怨言的人，我愿提议这样一个自问——你自己怎么样？你在出力维护你口称还记得的旧日水准么？还是你自己的影响，也正把这地球之球朝着你所痛惜的方向——就是下山坡的方向——推着滚动呢？

===Original===
The second query on Nabal’s “Nowadays” is—Can his memory be relied upon? It is an instinct with us all to idealise the past, and gild it in memory with all sorts of romance. We quietly drop all the shadows from the picture as time goes on. Were ever summer days since so long and fine and sunny as they were when we were boys? Never! We are all agreed about that. Yet when we were boys, men who were then grey were using exactly the same words about summer days years before! We are all apt to praise the past just because it is the past, and because it has a way of turning rosy as it recedes. The wise man recognises that, and allows for it. The foolish man begins many sentences with “Nowadays,” and ends with a shake of the head and a sigh.

But there is something that does not forget nor gild the past with false romance, and that is history. Turn back its pages a hundred years or more; read such a book as H. G. Graham’s *Social Life in Scotland in the Eighteenth Century*; and you will soon discover what a fine word “nowadays” really is.

As far as humanity and civilisation, brotherly charity, and true religion are concerned, the man who in pessimistic mood contrasts nowadays with the good old times a hundred years ago, simply does not know what he is talking about. Changes there have been, many and radical, but change is not necessarily a sign either of declension or decay.

===Chinese===
在拿八那句「如今」旁边的第二个问句是——他的记性靠得住么？把过去理想化，在记忆里给它镀上种种浪漫的色彩，是我们人人的天性。随着时间流逝，我们悄悄把画面上的阴影统统抹去。自从我们做孩子以来，可曾有过像当年那样悠长、晴美、阳光明媚的夏日？从来没有！这一点我们都同意。然而当我们做孩子的时候，那些当时已经白发苍苍的人，多年前谈起他们儿时的夏日，用的恰恰也是同样的话！我们都容易赞美过去，只因为它是过去，只因为它在渐行渐远时自有一种泛出玫瑰色的本领。智慧人认得这一层，并且把它估计在内。愚昧人则用「如今」起头说了许多句子，末了摇一摇头，叹一口气。

但有一样东西，既不遗忘过去，也不给它镀上虚假的浪漫，那就是历史。把它的书页翻回去一百年或更久；读一读 H. G. 格雷厄姆（H. G. Graham）的《十八世纪苏格兰社会生活》（*Social Life in Scotland in the Eighteenth Century*）这样的书；你很快就会发现，「如今」这个词究竟是个多么美妙的字眼。

就人道与文明、弟兄之爱与真信仰而论，那以悲观心绪把如今与一百年前的好旧时光相比的人，简直不知道自己在说什么。变化确曾有过，既多且剧，但变化未必就是衰退或败坏的征兆。

===Original===
I can partly understand a man without faith in God giving his vote for a general falling off in human progress, but I cannot understand a man who believes in God, and in the presence in the world of a living spirit of Christ, being a pessimist. No one affirms, of course, that we are progressing everywhere, and all the time. Setbacks here and there, there are in human history just as in a successful campaign. But that, on the whole, the world grows better, the Kingdom comes, and earth draws nearer to Heaven, seems to me to be simply a corollary from the fact that God reigns, and has blessed us with knowledge of Himself.

I grant you that the war is a disappointing revelation of how far mankind still has to travel. But, as far as we are concerned, I am not disposed to counsel undue humiliation and self-condemnation on account of it. A people that for the sake of unseen eternal realities like honour and righteousness will make the sacrifices which we are making, can hardly be said to be degenerating, especially when we remember some of the causes for which we have drawn the sword in years and generations gone by. But even though the clock of progress be set back awhile—and that does not seem so likely now as when the war began—it is simply not possible that, in this world of God’s, evil should ultimately vanquish good, that the Spirit of Christ should finally be crushed by the forces that oppose it. That can never be. As soon might the germs of disease which the sun destroys turn round upon it and quench its blessed light.

===Chinese===
一个对上帝没有信心的人投票赞成人类进步总体在衰退，我还多少能够理解；但我不能理解，一个信上帝、信基督的灵正活在世上的人，怎么会是个悲观主义者。当然，没有人断言我们在处处时时都在进步。人类历史中也处处有挫折，正如一场得胜的战局中也难免挫折。但就总体而言，世界在变得更好，神的国在来临，地在向天靠近——在我看来，这不过是「上帝在掌权，并施恩使我们认识祂自己」这一事实的必然结论。

我承认，这场大战令人失望地显明，人类要走的路还何等遥远。但就我们自己而论，我不愿为此劝人过度羞愧、自责。一个民族，为了荣誉与公义这类眼不能见的永恒实在，甘愿作出我们正在作出的牺牲，很难说它是在堕落退化——何况我们还记得，在已往的年岁与世代里，我们拔剑所向的曾是怎样一些缘由。但纵然进步的时钟被拨回片刻——如今看来这已不像开战之初那样可能——在这属于上帝的世界上，邪恶终究战胜良善、基督的灵终将被敌对的势力压碎，是断然不可能的。那是永不会有的事。这就像日头所歼灭的疾病病菌，倒转身来把它那有福的光熄灭一样可能。

===Original===
The third query opposite Nabal’s “Nowadays” is—Does he truly discern the present time? Does he know “nowadays” even as well as he knows the past? As a matter of fact, David was not just a servant who had broken away from his master, and if Nabal had only lived a little longer he would have seen how completely he had misread the signs of the times.

That is worth remembering when you are tempted to say, Nowadays things are out of joint. Maybe you don’t clearly see these very days you are disparaging. When Jesus preached in Nazareth, the village where He had been brought up, the people said, Is not this the Carpenter? and in their anger at His presumption, as they thought it, they wanted to make away with Him. If they had only known!

It is not enough to recognise that we cannot see the future. We cannot even see the present. Think what it would be like if we could see the great men, the prophets, poets, reformers, leaders, who are at this present moment in our nurseries and schools, or if we were able to recognise in the—at present—small shoot of a cause, the great tree into which in God’s providence it is destined to grow!

===Chinese===
在拿八那句「如今」旁边的第三个问句是——他真能辨明当下的时候么？对于「如今」，他就算与对过去一样了解么？事实上，大卫并不是一个单只悖逆主人奔逃的仆人；拿八倘若多活了一些时日，就会看见他把时代的征兆看错得何等彻底。

当你想说「如今事事都不成体统」的时候，这一点值得记住。也许你并没有看清你正加以贬低的这些日子。耶稣在拿撒勒——祂长大的村庄——讲道的时候，众人说：「这不是那木匠么？」他们恼怒祂的僭妄——照他们所想的——竟要下手除灭祂。倘若他们知道就好了！

认识到我们看不见未来，还不够；我们连现在也看不见。想一想，倘若我们能看见那些此刻正在我们的育婴室和学校里的伟人——先知、诗人、改革家、领袖——那会是什么光景；或者倘若我们能够在一桩事业——就目前而论——细小的嫩枝里，认出那在上帝眷护中命定要长成的大树！

Nowadays; nowadays! What a delusion it is for anybody to think he knows “nowadays” well enough to call it names! It is not with observation that the Kingdom comes. God rings no bell when He has a new and gracious purpose afoot in the world. And the thing for you and me to do is to rest confidently in the faith that, in His own good way and time, God is redeeming the world to Himself, and to do all that we can to help Him, and to make our little corner of it a brighter and a better place. But do not let us imagine that we can see all that is going on about us. There is far, far more of God and of goodness in the world than we suspect. The woods and hedges look very bleak and bare today.1 It is a dead and barren aspect that Nature wears nowadays. Yet *even now* the sap is mounting quickly in every living stem, and Spring is getting ready while we sleep.

So, let us have the courage to believe—so is it with every worthy cause of God and man.

如今；如今！谁若自以为把「如今」认识得够了，可以对它破口辱骂，这是何等的自欺！神的国来到，不是眼所能见的。上帝在世上有一个新的、施恩的计划进行时，并不鸣钟预告。你我当做的事，是安然笃信：上帝正以祂自己美好的方式、在祂自己美好的时间，把世界救赎归向祂自己；并且竭尽所能去帮助祂，使我们这小小的一角成为一个更光明、更美好的地方。但不要以为我们能看明四围正在发生的一切。这世界里的上帝与良善，远比我们猜度的多得多。今天的树林和树篱看上去十分荒凉光秃。1 这就是「如今」大自然披戴的枯死荒凉的面貌。然而*就在此刻*，浆液正在每一个活着的茎干里迅速上升；当我们沉睡时，春天正在预备。

所以，让我们有勇气相信——上帝与人的每一桩有价值的事业，情形都是如此。

Prayer
Almighty God, Ruler and Disposer of all events, we would remember that this world of ours is, first of all, Thine. We believe that, though Thy Kingdom comes not with observation yet it does come more and more. We believe that, with Thee, the best is yet to be. And we pray that, with that faith in our hearts, we may leave the large campaign with quietness and confidence to Thee, and seek rather to discharge the duties of that post Thou hast assigned to us, with loyalty and good hope. Amen.
