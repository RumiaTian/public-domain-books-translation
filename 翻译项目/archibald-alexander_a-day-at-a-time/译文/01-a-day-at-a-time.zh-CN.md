## I A Day at a Time / I 一天一天地过

===Original===
> "As thy days, so shall thy strength be."
> — Deuteronomy 33:25

===Chinese===
> 「你的日子如何，你的力量也必如何。」
> — Deuteronomy 33:25

===Original===
If any one of us knows a word of hope or has picked up a message of comfort anywhere, it is his plain duty to share it, these days. We owe it to each other to cherish as exceeding precious, and to pass on to others, every brave and helpful word or thought we come across.

Well, here is a splendid one for us all, and especially for those who have most at stake in this great conflict, and are looking anxiously ahead and fearing what the weeks may have in store—"As thy days, so shall thy strength be." It is a great and glorious promise. And just a couple of verses further on, it is caught up and included in one greater still—"The eternal God is thy refuge and underneath are the everlasting arms." Fathers and mothers, with a boy, or more than one, perhaps, away on active service for King and country, this promise is for you, to take to your heart and hide there, like some precious secret between you and God—As thy days, so shall thy strength be.

===Chinese===
在这些日子里，我们中间若有谁晓得一句盼望的话语，或从何处拾得一条安慰的信息，把它分享出来，便是他明白的本分。凡遇见勇敢而有益的言语或思想，都当视作极贵重的珍宝加以珍惜，并传递给他人——这是我们彼此相欠的债。

那么，这里就有一句极好的话，给我们每一个人，尤其给那些在这场大冲突中利害攸关最深的人，他们正焦灼地朝前张望，害怕往后的几个星期里不知藏着什么——「你的日子如何，你的力量也必如何。」这是一个伟大而荣耀的应许。再往下不过两节经文，它便被接续起来，包纳在一个更伟大的应许之中——「永生的神是你的居所，他永久的膀臂在你以下。」做父亲、做母亲的，你们或许有一个儿子，或不止一个，正为王与国家奔赴战地服役——这应许就是给你们的，要把它领到心里珍藏起来，像你们与神之间的一个宝贵秘密——你的日子如何，你的力量也必如何。

===Original===
Notice carefully, however, how the promise runs. Not, mark you, as your life is, not as your years are, not even as your weeks are, but as your days, so shall your strength be. For each day as it comes, God's promise is that strength will be given you, but just for a day at a time. The way to live under any circumstances, but especially in these hard weeks, is just a day at a time. Leave tomorrow with God, my brother, until it comes. That is what the Word of God lays upon you as a duty. Live this day at your best and bravest, trusting that God's help will not fail you. And for the duties and trials of tomorrow, however hard and heavy, believe that strength for that day also will be given you, when it comes.

You cannot have failed to observe what an important place this way of living had in the teaching of Jesus Christ. He was always trying to get men to trust the coming days to God, and to live fully worthily and nobly today. He was dead against the practice of adding to the burdens of today fears and forebodings for tomorrow. It is in love to us, in His desire to save us unnecessary pain, that He bids us remember that "sufficient unto the day is the evil thereof."

===Chinese===
然而，请仔细留意这应许是怎么说的。请注意，不是照你的一生如何，不是照你的年岁如何，甚至不是照你的星期如何，而是照你的日子如何，你的力量也必如何。神的应许是：每一天来到之时，必有力量赐给你，但一次只给一天。无论身处何种境况，尤其在这艰难的几个星期里，过日子的法门就是一天一天地过。我的弟兄，把明天留给神，直到它来到。这是神的道加在你身上的本分。尽你最大的勇敢活好今天，深信神的帮助必不致叫你落空。至于明天的职责与试炼，无论多么艰难沉重，要相信等到那一天来到，那一天的力量也必赐给你。

你们必定早已看出，这种活法在耶稣基督的教导中占着何等要紧的位置。他总是设法叫人把将来的日子交托给神，而使今天活得完全配得上、完全高贵。对于把对明天的恐惧与不祥预感加在今天重担上的做法，他是断然反对的。他吩咐我们记念「一天的难处一天当就够了」，正是出于他对我们的爱，出于他要救我们免受不必要之苦的心意。

===Original===
In one of R. D. Blackmore's fine open-air stories, there is a character who talks at length about horses. After comparing good ones and bad ones in their behaviour the first time they breast a hill with a load behind them, he sums the matter up thus: "Howsoever good a horse be, he longeth to see over the top of the hill before he be halfway up it." The man who is listening to him confesses that he has often felt that way himself! And I do not know that there are many of us who can claim to be guiltless in this respect. Yet it is perfectly plain that the men and women who are living the bravest and most successful lives around us, and are proving towers of strength to others, are those who have learned the art of living just a day at a time, and of depending upon God for strength for that day in the simplest and most trustful fashion.

Why, my brothers, if God our Father had meant us to carry on our backs the fears and anxieties of the coming days, He would surely have told us more about them! If we were meant to bear today what next week holds, surely we should have been permitted to see into next week. But we cannot. We cannot see a single second ahead. God gives us Now, and Tomorrow He keeps to Himself. Is there anything wiser or better we can do with our tomorrows than just to leave them quietly and trustfully with Him?

===Chinese===
在 R. D. 布莱克莫尔（R. D. Blackmore）一部精彩的露天小说里，有一个人物对马大谈特谈。他把好马与劣马头一回驮着重负爬坡时的表现比较了一番之后，这样总括道：「无论一匹马多么好，它没爬到半坡，就巴望能望见山顶那边的光景。」听他说话的人承认，他自己也常有这般心情！而我们中间，敢说在这方面毫无亏欠的，恐怕也没有几个。然而有一件事再明白不过：在我们周围活得最勇敢、最有成就、并成为他人力量之柱石的男男女女，正是那些学会了「一天一天地过」这门艺术、并以最单纯最信靠的方式仰赖神赐下当天之力的人。

我的弟兄们，想一想吧，倘若天父本意是要我们把将来日子的恐惧与忧虑背在自己背上，他必定会把关于这些日子的更多事告诉我们！倘若今天就该担起下星期所要发生的事，我们理当蒙容许窥见下星期才是。但我们不能。往前一秒钟我们都看不见。神把「现在」赐给我们，把「明天」留在他自己手中。对于我们的种种明天，除了安安静静、满心信靠地交在他手中，我们还能做什么更智慧、更美好的事呢？

===Original===
The habit of living ahead, as so many of us do, prevents us from getting the full taste and flavour of the happiness and blessing that are ours today. I defy any man to be adequately grateful for this day's sunshine if he is worrying all the time about the chance of a bad day tomorrow. Mark Rutherford, merciless self-critic as he was, takes himself severely to task for this habit in his *Autobiography*. "I learned, alas! when it was almost too late," he says, "to live in each moment as it passed over my head, believing that the sun as it is now rising, is as good as it ever will be." Yes, in great things as well as in little things, that is true. If we are to live our lives at the full, and anywhere on the Christian level, the only way is to live one day at a time.

Our forefathers in the pulpit were fond of reminding their hearers to live each day as if it were their last. And in solemn truth, without being in the least morbid, that is the way to live. If a man knew that after today, he would not smell the sea again, how fully and gratefully would he fill his lungs with its ozone today! If he knew he were not to enter God's House again, how earnestly and sincerely and reverently he would join in its worship today! Yes, but the point is, why should his hope, that he has other days to come, prevent him taking out of this day all that he possibly can? Why should this day be any less prized, because others in all probability will follow it?

===Chinese===
像我们许多人那样「预支着生活」的习惯，使我们尝不到今日幸福与福分的十足滋味。我要说，一个人若时时刻刻都在担心明天也许是个坏日子，他就绝不可能对今天的阳光有充分的感恩。马克·拉瑟福德（Mark Rutherford）这位毫不留情的自我批评者，在《自传》（Autobiography）里就这习惯严厉地责备了自己。「唉！等到几乎太迟，我才学会，」他说，「活在每一个掠过我头顶的瞬间，相信此刻正在升起的太阳，与它日后任何时刻一样美好。」是的，无论大事小事，这话都真。我们若要活得丰满，活得有一点基督徒的样式，唯一的路就是一天一天地活。

从前站在讲坛上的先辈们喜欢提醒会众：要把每一天当作最后一天来活。而这确是庄重的真理，没有半分病态，这原是对的活法。一个人若知道今天过后就再闻不到海的气息，今天他该会何等饱满、何等感激地让肺腑充满海上清新的空气！他若知道自己再不能踏进神的殿，今天他该会何等恳切、真诚、虔敬地投入殿中的敬拜！是啊，但关键在于：为什么他「还有别的日子可活」的盼望，就该拦阻他尽力从今天汲取一切呢？为什么因为别的日子多半会接踵而至，今天就该少受珍重呢？

===Original===
But the great value of this word is the comfort of it to those who are anxious and fear the coming days. And which of us is not in that category? I do not suppose there is one of my readers upon whom, somehow or other, the war has not levied its tax. Nearly everyone has somebody belonging to him or her who is in this gigantic struggle, and whose welfare is a matter of real concern. And, closer still, there are fathers and mothers, sisters and brothers, whose very dearest are "in it" or are getting ready to do their share. They have joined, and we are proud that they have joined, for this is a cause that ennobles every mother's son who fights for it. But who shall say what the mother's thoughts are, these days? How proud, and justly proud, the father is that his boy has played the man, and offered himself to his King and for his country! But only God, who made the father—and the mother—heart, knows what the surrender costs. And only God knows how eagerly and anxiously they look ahead to try to see what the future may hold.

And, knowing that, He sends His comfort to you, fathers and mothers. The comfort of His promise—As thy days, so shall thy strength be. Just a day at a time, my friend! Do not take fears for next month on your shoulders now. You will get strength given you for today, certain and sure, and when next month comes, the strength and comfort for that day will come too, as certain and as sure. Be not overanxious about the morrow. Leave your tomorrow, and your soldier-son, in God's hands. You can do nothing more at the best, and this is the best. But it is such a mistake to do anything less. Leave all your tomorrows with God—it is what He wants you to do—and humbly and gratefully take from His hands His gift of Today, and the strength that comes with it. If that be not enough—and it is not enough for God has said more—when that is not enough, still your heart a moment, and listen! And you will hear, beneath that promise for today, like the grand deep tones of an organ, the magnificent diapason of the Father's constant love and mindfulness—"The eternal God is thy refuge, and underneath are the everlasting arms." And surely that is enough!

===Chinese===
然而这话最大的价值，在于它对那些忧虑而惧怕将来日子之人的安慰。而我们哪一个不在其列呢？我想，我的读者当中不会有一个人，不曾以这样那样的方式被这场大战征收过它的税。几乎人人都有一位亲人正陷身这场巨大的搏斗，他或她的平安实在令人挂心。再近一层，还有那些做父母、做兄弟姐妹的，他们至亲至爱的人正「在其中」，或正准备尽自己的一份。他们已投身其中，我们也为他们投身而自豪，因为这是一项使每一个为它战斗的儿子都得尊荣的事业。可是这些日子里，谁能道尽一位母亲心中的念头呢？儿子已经作了大丈夫，把自己献给了他的王、献给了他的国家，做父亲的是何等自豪，而且自豪得有理！但唯有造了父亲的心、也造了母亲的心的神，知道这一舍出代价几何。也唯有神知道，他们是怎样急切而焦灼地朝前张望，想看一看未来究竟藏着什么。

而神知道这一切，他把他的安慰差给你们，做父母的人们。他应许的安慰——你的日子如何，你的力量也必如何。一次只过一天，我的朋友！现在不要把下个月的恐惧扛上肩头。今天的力量必赐给你，确定无疑；到了下个月，那一天的力量与安慰也必照样确定无疑地来到。不要为明天过分忧虑。把你的明天，把你从军的儿子，都交在神的手中。尽你所能，此外再无可做，而这已是最好的；做得再少，便是大错。把你一切的明天都留给神——这正是他要你做的——再谦卑而感激地从他的手中领受他那「今日」的礼物，和随之而来的力量。若这还不够——那的确还不够，因为神说得更多——当这还不够时，让你的心静默片刻，侧耳而听！你就要听见，在那今日应许的下面，仿佛管风琴宏大深沉的音调，回荡着天父恒久眷爱与看顾的雄浑谐音——「永生的神是你的居所，他永久的膀臂在你以下。」而这必定够了！

Prayer
O Lord our God, who dost appoint the way for each of us, give us the grace to trust that as Thou hast helped us hitherto, so, in Thy great mercy, Thou wilt bless us still. We do not ask to see the distant scene. Keep us, and our beloved, this day; and in quietness and confidence teach us to leave tomorrow with Thee, our Father. Through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.
