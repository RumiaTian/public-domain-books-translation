## XVIII The Equipment of Joy / XVIII 喜乐的装备

===Original===
> “The joy of the Lord is your strength.”
> — Nehemiah 8:10

===Chinese===
> 「靠耶和华而得的喜乐是你们的力量。」
> — Nehemiah 8:10

===Original===
Let us talk about joy, and especially that kind of it of which Nehemiah was thinking when he said, “The joy of the Lord is your strength.” It is strange that while practically everybody would agree as to the wholesomeness and the duty of joy in the ordinary sense of the term, to add the words “of the Lord” to it, seems, to some, completely to alter its character and in fact to spoil it, to turn it into an unreal sort of joy which is not true joy at all.

===Chinese===
让我们来谈谈喜乐，特别是尼希米说「靠耶和华而得的喜乐是你们的力量」时心中所想的那一种。说来奇怪：就这词平常的意义而言，喜乐于人身心有益，也是当尽的本分——这一点几乎人人都同意；可只要再添上「主的」几个字，在有些人看来，似乎就完全改变了它的性质，甚至把它败坏了，使它变成一种虚而不实的喜乐，根本算不得真喜乐。

===Original===
I wish emphatically to protest against such a conception of religious joy as an injustice to the Father Love of God. The joy of the Lord, as I understand it, is not different in quality from wholesome human gladness, it is, in fact, just that gladness deepened and sanctified by the sense of God, and the knowledge of Him brought to us by Jesus Christ our Lord. There is not a single innocent and pure source of gladness open to men and women on this earth but is made to taste sweeter when they have opened their hearts to the love of God. It is the very crown of happy living that is reached when a man can say, “My Lord and my God.” Once I have dared to accept the wonderful truth that even for me the Eternal Father has His place and His plan and His care, every simplest happiness, every common joy of living, every delight in the beauty of the world and the pleasures of home and work and friendship—every one of these takes on a keener edge. It is a pestilent heresy to declare that a Christian ought to walk through life like a man with a hidden sickness. On the contrary, there is no one who has a better right to be joyous and happy-hearted. Do you think it is for nothing that the “joy of our salvation” is a Bible phrase? And shall we believe that that salvation is ours and not be mighty glad about it all the time? What is the good of translating “Gospel” as “good news” and at the same time living as if religion were a bondage and a burden grievous to be borne? Of all the strange twists of human convention, it is surely the strangest to allow ordinary human joy to be happy and cheerful, and to insist that those whose joy is in the Lord should pull a long face, and forswear laughter, and crawl along dolefully as if to the sound of some dirge! The “morning face and the morning heart” belong of right to the truly religious, and no one ought to be gladder, come what may, than the man who has made the highest and best disposal of his little life that anyone can make, namely, surrendered it in faith and obedience to his Lord.

===Chinese===
对于这种宗教喜乐观，我要郑重地提出抗议：这是对上帝为父之爱的不公。靠主而得的喜乐，照我的领会，其性质与健全的人间欢愉并无二致；事实上，它正是那欢愉本身，因着对上帝的意识、因着我们主耶稣基督所带给我们的对祂的认识，而更深、而成圣了。世上男男女女所能享有的每一样清白纯正的喜乐之源，没有一样不在他们向上帝的爱敞开心扉之后变得更加甘甜。人生的快乐所抵达的真正冠冕，正是当一个人能说「我的主，我的神」的时候。我一旦敢于接受这奇妙的真理——永恒的天父连对我也有祂的位置、祂的计划、祂的眷顾——那么，每一份至简的幸福，每一种寻常的生活之乐，每一份对世界之美的喜悦、对家庭与工作与友谊之趣的喜悦——这一切便无一不添上更锐利的锋芒。宣称基督徒走过一生，应当像个身怀隐疾的人，这是贻害无穷的异端邪说。恰恰相反，再没有人比他更有权利欢欢喜喜、心中快乐。你以为「救恩之乐」成为圣经里的成语，是徒然的么？我们既相信那救恩是我们的，难道还能不时时为此大大欢喜么？把「福音」译作「好消息」，同时却活得仿佛信仰是一种奴役、一副难受的重担，这有什么益处呢？在人间习俗种种怪诞的扭曲之中，最怪诞的大概莫过于：听任寻常人的喜乐欢畅愉快，却坚称那些在主里喜乐的人必须拉长了脸、弃绝欢笑，凄凄惨惨地爬行，仿佛一路踏着哀乐的调子！「朝晨的面容与朝晨的心」按本分原属于真正敬虔的人；无论遭遇什么，没有人应当比那人更快乐——他已经对自己的小小一生作出了人所能作出的最高、最好的处置，就是凭着信心与顺服，把它交托给了他的主。

===Original===
A gloomy, ponderous, stiff religion which looks askance at innocent merriment and is afraid to pull a long breath of enjoyment has the mark of “damaged goods” on it somehow, and no one will take it off your hands. It is not catching, and certainly your children will never catch it. It is said to be a good test of a religion that it can be preached at a street corner. But I know a better test than that. Preach it to a child. Set him in the midst of those who profess it. If their religion frightens him, freezes the smiles on his lips, and destroys his happiness, depend upon it, whatever sort of religion it be, it lacks the essential winsomeness of the religion of Jesus Christ.

===Chinese===
一种阴郁、沉闷、僵硬的信仰，对无伤大雅的欢笑皱眉侧目，连畅快地深吸一口气享受一番都不敢，它身上不知怎的总带着「受损货物」的印记，没有人肯从你手里接过去。它不会传染，至少你的孩子肯定传染不上。有人说，一种信仰能够在街角宣讲，便是检验它的好法子。可我知道一个更好的法子。把它讲给一个孩子听。把他安置在那些信奉它的人中间。倘若这信仰叫他害怕，叫他唇边的笑容凝结，毁掉他的快乐，那你就尽管确信：无论它是哪一种信仰，它总缺少耶稣基督的信仰那根本的动人可爱之处。

===Original===
I need not say, of course, that I am not pleading for a more hilarious religious life. And, equally of course, empty frivolity, and the cult of the continual grin are insufferable things to endure either in the name of religion or anything else. Not by a single word would I lessen the condemnation which such aberrations deserve. But I do say, and with all my heart I believe that a deep, abiding wellspring of happiness—which our author calls the “joy of the Lord”—is of the very essence of true religion, and is indeed, what he asserts it, actually our strength. Actually our strength. Let us be quite clear about that.

===Chinese===
当然，不必说，我并不是在为一种更加喧闹狂欢的信仰生活辩护。同样当然，空洞的轻浮，与那把终日咧嘴而笑奉为圭臬的风气——无论打着宗教的名义还是别的什么名义——都是叫人忍无可忍的东西。这类乖张行径所当受的谴责，我一个字也不愿减轻。但我确实要说，并且全心相信：一股深邃长流的幸福泉源——也就是我们这位作者所称的「靠主而得的喜乐」——正是真信仰的本质所在；而且诚如他所断言的，它实实在在地就是我们的力量。实实在在，是我们的力量。这一点，让我们看得清清楚楚。

===Original===
The man in whose heart there dwells this best of all joys is a strength to other people. We don’t need anyone to prove that to us, I imagine. We have all been helped and revived many a time merely by contact with some hearty cheerful soul. Who, for example, that had his choice, would elect for his family physician a man with a doleful air? Have we not all found that a doctor’s cheery manner was as potent a medicine as any drug that he called by a Latin name? Ay, and even when we are in trouble, and our hearts are sad and sore, I think we would all rather see the friend whose faith in God showed in a brave and buoyant outlook than one whose religion was of the dowie and despondent sort.

===Chinese===
心里住着这诸般喜乐中之至善的人，是旁人的力量。我想，这一点无须谁来向我们证明。我们人人都曾单单因为接近某个热诚欢畅的灵魂，便屡次得了帮助、重新振作起来。譬如，谁若能自行挑选，肯选一个满面愁容的人做自己全家的医生呢？我们岂不都曾发现，医生那欢快的神态，同他口中用拉丁文念出名字的任何一剂药，是同样有效的良药么？是啊，甚至当我们身处患难、心中忧伤伤痛的时候，我想我们也都宁愿见那位朋友——他对上帝的信心显现在勇敢而开朗的眼光里——而不愿见那信仰属于阴郁沮丧一类的人。

===Original===
I have heard it said of an employee who had the gift of the joyous heart that the twinkle of his eyes was worth £100 a year to his firm. I could easily believe it, though the money value might well have been set at any figure, seeing that the thing itself is really priceless. Did not the most famous modern apostle of the duty of happiness—himself a signal proof that joy is something more than the mere easy overflow of health and animal spirits—did not Stevenson declare that “by being happy we sow anonymous benefits,” and that “the entrance of such a person into a room is as if another candle had been lighted?” I take it the proof is ample that a joyous heart is a strength to others.

===Chinese===
我听说有一位雇员，天生怀着喜乐的心肠，有人这样说他：单是他眼中那闪烁的光彩，对他的公司来说，一年就值 100 英镑。这话我很容易相信，尽管那金钱价值原本尽可以定作任何数目，因为这东西本身就实在无价。那位论快乐之本分的现今最著名的使徒——他本人便是一个显著的明证，证明喜乐远不止是健康与活泼天性那轻易的洋溢——史蒂文森（Stevenson）岂不曾宣告：「借着快乐，我们播下无名的恩惠」，又说过「这样的人一走进屋子，就仿佛又点上了一支蜡烛」么？我想，喜乐的心是旁人的力量，证据已经够充足了。

===Original===
But more, it is a strength to oneself. That may not be so obvious, and yet the result here is even more certain. Ordinary experience tells us that joy is good for us, that depression and gloom work us bodily harm. But from one province of scientific study especially there has come a wonderful array of evidence that makes it as certain as any fact can be that the happy states of mind do literally add to our strength in quite measurable directions. There is, in strict fact, no tonic in all the world like gladness.

===Chinese===
不但如此，它更是自己本人的力量。这一点也许不那么显豁，结果却更加确实。平常的经验告诉我们：喜乐于我们有益，抑郁阴沉则损害我们的身体。而尤其从一个科学研究的领域，涌来了蔚为可观的一系列证据，使我们能够像确信任何事实一样确信：快乐的心境当真在颇可测度的方向上加添我们的力量。说一句严格属实的话：普天之下，再没有一味补药比得上欢愉。

===Original===
That being so, joy, and especially the best kind of it of which Nehemiah speaks, is not a luxury, not a condition you may legitimately cherish if you are fortunate enough to possess it. It is a sheer necessity. You can’t do without it. Even to meet your sorrows, even to gird you for service, even to run your race without fainting, you need the joy of the Lord, which is strength. And since the Father has stored up such an abundant supply of it in this world of His, since it is knocking at our doors every day, and only our distrust and suspicion keep it outside, we know what to do to secure this good gift of God. We have only to open our doors to let it in, and give it room.

===Chinese===
既然如此，喜乐——尤其是尼希米所说那最好的一种——就并不是奢侈品，并不是一种你若侥幸拥有了，便可以名正言顺地守着的心境。它是十足的必需品。你少不了它。哪怕只为迎接你的忧伤，只为束上腰去服事，只为奔跑你的路程而不困倦，你也需要靠主而得的喜乐——那便是力量。天父既在祂自己的这个世界里积存了如此丰盈的供应，它既天天叩我们的门，只是我们的不信与猜疑把它关在外面，那么，要得着上帝这份美好的恩赐该怎么办，我们已经知道了。我们只须把门打开，放它进来，给它地方。

===Original===
> “So take Joy home
> And make a place in thy great heart for her,
> And give her time to grow, and cherish her,
> Then will she come and oft will sing to thee
> When thou art working in the furrows—ay,
> Or weeding in the sacred hour of dawn.
> It is a comely fashion to be glad—
> Joy is the grace we say to God.”

===Chinese===
> 「那么，就把喜乐领回家去，
> 在你宽广的心里为她备一个位置，
> 给她时间生长，好生珍惜她，
> 那时她必来到，屡屡向你歌唱——
> 当你在垄沟间劳作——是啊，
> 或在黎明那圣洁的时辰里除草。
> 欢欢喜喜，原是美好的风尚——
> 喜乐，是我们向上帝说的谢恩。」

===Original===
Prayer
Help us, O God, beyond our poor and forgetful thanksgiving, to show forth the praise of Thy loving kindness by our joy and gladness. For Thy great grace and mercy toward us, and for all the gifts of Thy sleepless Providence, we offer Thee the joy of our hearts. Accept our offering, we beseech Thee; forgive its scant measure, and teach us to be glad in Thee. For Thy Name’s sake. Amen.

===Chinese===
祈祷
上帝啊，求祢帮助我们，超越我们那贫乏而健忘的感谢，借我们的喜乐与欢愉，显出祢慈爱当得的赞美。为了祢向我们所施的大恩与大怜悯，为了祢那不眠之眷护的一切恩赐，我们把心中的喜乐献给祢。求祢悦纳我们的奉献；赦免它的微薄，教导我们在祢里面欢喜。为祢名的缘故。阿们。
