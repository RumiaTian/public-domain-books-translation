## Endnotes / 尾注
===Original===

1. Ah, my friend, from whom I have borrowed this scion of the nobility! Had he been left with us he would have forgiven me my little theft, and now that he has gone I will not change the name. ↩︎

===Chinese===

1. 啊，我的朋友，这位贵族的小枝，就是从你那里借来的！他若还留在我们这里，多半会饶我这桩小小的窃取；如今他既已去了，这个名字我便不改了。 ↩︎
