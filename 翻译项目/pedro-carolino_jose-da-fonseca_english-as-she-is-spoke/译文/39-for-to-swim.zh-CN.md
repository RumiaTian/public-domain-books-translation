# For to Swim / 为了去游泳

===Original===
- I row upon the belly on the back and between two waters.

===Chinese===
- 我趴在肚子上划，仰在背上划，还在两水之间划。

===Original===
- I am not so dexte rous that you.

===Chinese===
- 我不及您那样灵巧。

===Original===
- Nothing is more easy than to swim; it do not what don’t to be afraid of.

===Chinese===
- 没有什么比游泳更容易的了；它不有什么好害怕的。
