# For to Wish the Good Morning / 为了去祝早安

===Original===
- How does your father do?

===Chinese===
- 您的父亲可好？

===Original===
- He is very well.

===Chinese===
- 他很好。

===Original===
- I am very delight of it. Were is it?

===Chinese===
- 我对此是十分的欣喜。他是在哪里？

===Original===
- I shall come back soon, I was no came that to know how you are.

===Chinese===
- 我很快就回来；我此来不为别的，正是要知道你们可好。
