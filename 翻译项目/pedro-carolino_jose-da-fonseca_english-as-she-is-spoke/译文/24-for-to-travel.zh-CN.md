# For to Travel / 为了去旅行

===Original===
- Where you go so?

===Chinese===
- 您这样是要往哪里去？

===Original===
- I am going to Cadiz.

===Chinese===
- 我到加的斯去。

===Original===
- Have you already arrested a coach?

===Chinese===
- 您已经逮捕了一辆马车吗？

===Original===
- Yes, sir, and very cheap.

===Chinese===
- 是的，先生，而且非常便宜。

===Original===
- There is it some danger on the highway?

===Chinese===
- 在那公路上是有一些危险吗？

===Original===
- It is not spoken that.

===Chinese===
- 它没有被说起。

===Original===
- They speak not that may have some robbers on the woods?

===Chinese===
- 他们不说在那些树林里可能有一些强盗吗？

===Original===
- It have nothing to fear, or in day neither the night.

===Chinese===
- 它没有什么可害怕的，或在白天也不在夜里。

===Original===
- Don’t we does pass for a﻿—﻿—?

===Chinese===
- 我们不确实经过一处﻿—﻿—吗？

===Original===
- No, sir, they leave it to left.

===Chinese===
- 不，先生，他们把它留在左边了。

===Original===
- Let us take patience, still some o’clock, and we shall be in the end of our voyage.

===Chinese===
- 让我们拿出耐心来，还有几个钟点，我们就将到达我们旅途的尽头了。
