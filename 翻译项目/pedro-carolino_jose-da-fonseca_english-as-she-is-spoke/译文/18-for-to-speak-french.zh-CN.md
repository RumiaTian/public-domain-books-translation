# For to Speak French / 为了去讲法语

===Original===
- How is the french? Are you too learned now?

===Chinese===
- 法语学得怎样了？您如今已经很有学问了么？

===Original===
- I could to tell some word’s that I know by heart.

===Chinese===
- 我还能够讲说出一些词语，是我用心记熟的。

===Original===
- Not apprehend you, the french language is not difficult.

===Chinese===
- 您别疑惧，法国语言是不难的。

===Original===
