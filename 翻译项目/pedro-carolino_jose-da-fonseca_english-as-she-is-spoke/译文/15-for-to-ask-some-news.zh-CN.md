# For to Ask Some News / 为了去打听些新闻

===Original===
- It is true what is told of master M﻿—﻿—?

===Chinese===
- 人家所说的关于M————老爷的事，是真的么？

===Original===
- Then what is told of him?

===Chinese===
- 那么，人家都说了他些什么？

===Original===
- I have heard that he is hurt mortally.

===Chinese===
- 我听说他受了致命伤了。

===Original===
- I shall be sowow of it, because he is a honestman.

===Chinese===
- 我会为此很悲伤的，因为他是一个正直的人。

===Original===
- Which have wounden him?

===Chinese===
- 是哪一个伤了他？

===Original===
- Do know it why?

===Chinese===
- 可晓得为着什么么？

===Original===
- The noise run that is by to have given a box on the ear to a of them.

===Chinese===
- 谣言跑传着，说是他给了他们之中的一个一记耳光。
