# For to Dress Him Self / 为了去给他自己穿衣

===Original===
- John, make haste, lighted the fire and dress-me.

===Chinese===
- 约翰，快些，把火点着了，给我穿衣。

===Original===
- Give me my shirt.

===Chinese===
- 把我的衬衫给我。

===Original===
- There is it sir.

===Chinese===
- 它在这儿，先生。

===Original===
- Is it no hot, it is too cold yet.

===Chinese===
- 它不热么？它还太冷呢。

===Original===
- If you like, I will hot it.

===Chinese===
- 如果您愿意，我把它热一热。

===Original===
- No, no, bring me my silk stocking’s.

===Chinese===
- 不，不，把我的丝长袜拿来。

===Original===
- Its are make holes.

===Chinese===
- 它们已是做成洞了。

===Original===
- Make its a point, or make to mend them.

===Chinese===
- 给它们做个织补，或是把它们补一补。

===Original===
- Comb me, take another comb. Give me my handkarchief.

===Chinese===
- 给我梳头，另拿一把梳子。把我的手帕给我。

===Original===
- There is a clean, sir.

===Chinese===
- 有一块干净的，先生。

===Original===
- What coat dress you to day?

===Chinese===
- 今天您要穿什么外衣？

===Original===
- Those that I had yesterday.

===Chinese===
- 穿我昨天那件。

===Original===
- The tailor do owe to bring soon that of cloth.

===Chinese===
- 裁缝应当快些把那件呢料的送来。

===Original===
- Have you wexed my shoes?

===Chinese===
- 您给我的鞋上过蜡了么？

===Original===
- I go wex its now.

===Chinese===
- 我这就去给它们上蜡。

===Original===
- It must that I may wash my hands, the mouth and my face.

===Chinese===
- 我必须洗一洗我的手、嘴巴和我的脸。
