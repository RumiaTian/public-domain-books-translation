# With the Tailor / 与裁缝

===Original===
- Can you do me a coat?

===Chinese===
- 您能给我做一件外衣么？

===Original===
- What cloth will you do to?

===Chinese===
- 您愿意做什么料子？

===Original===
- From a stuff what be of season.

===Chinese===
- 用一块正当令的料子。

===Original===
- How much wants the ells for coat, waist coat, and breeches?

===Chinese===
- 做外衣、背心和长裤，要多少厄尔的布？

===Original===
- Six ells.

===Chinese===
- 六厄尔。

===Original===
- What will you to double the coat?

===Chinese===
- 外衣您要配什么里子？

===Original===
- From some thing of duration. I believe to you that.

===Chinese===
- 要些能经久的料子。这个我是信您的。

===Original===
- When do you bring me my coat?

===Chinese===
- 您几时把我的外衣送来？

===Original===
- The rather that be possible.

===Chinese===
- 越快越好，只要是可能的。

===Original===
- Bring you my coat?

===Chinese===
- 您把我的外衣带来了么？

===Original===
- Yes, sir, there is it.

===Chinese===
- 带来了，先生，它在这儿。

===Original===
- You have me done to expect too.

===Chinese===
- 您让我等候得太过分了。

===Original===
- I did can’t to come rather.

===Chinese===
- 我确实是没能早些来。

===Original===
- It don’t are finished?

===Chinese===
- 它不是已经做好了么？

===Original===
- The lining war not sewd.

===Chinese===
- 里子还没有缝上。

===Original===
- It is so that do one’s now.

===Chinese===
- 如今人家就是这么做的。

===Original===
- Button me.

===Chinese===
- 替我扣上扣子。

===Original===
- It pinches me too much upon stomack.

===Chinese===
- 它在肚子上把我夹得太紧了。

===Original===
- The sleeves have not them great deal wideness?

===Chinese===
- 袖子们不是有着它们的许多宽大么？

===Original===
- No, sir, they are well.

===Chinese===
- 不是的，先生，它们很好。
