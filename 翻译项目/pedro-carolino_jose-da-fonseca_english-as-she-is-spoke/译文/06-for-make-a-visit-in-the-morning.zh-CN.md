# For Make a Visit in the Morning / 为了做一次早晨的拜访

===Original===
- Is your master at home?

===Chinese===
- 您的主人可在家？

===Original===
- Yes, sir.

===Chinese===
- 在的，先生。

===Original===
- Is it up.

===Chinese===
- 他起来了没有。

===Original===
- No, sir, he sleep yet.

===Chinese===
- 还没有，先生，他还睡着呢。

===Original===
- I go make that he get up.

===Chinese===
- 我去把他弄起来。

===Original===
- It come in one’s? How is it, you are in bed yet?

===Chinese===
- 进来一位么？怎么，您还睡在床上呢？

===Original===
- Yesterday at evening, I was to bed so late that I may not rising me soon that morning.

===Chinese===
- 昨天到了晚上，我上床太迟，以至于今天早上没法把我自己早早起来。

===Original===
- Well! what you have done after the supper?

===Chinese===
- 好啊！晚饭之后你们做了些什么？

===Original===
- We have sung, danced, laugh and played.

===Chinese===
- 我们唱了，跳了，笑，还玩耍了。

===Original===
- What game?

===Chinese===
- 耍的什么？

===Original===
- To the picket.

===Chinese===
- 打皮克。

===Original===
- Whom I am sorry do not have know it!

===Chinese===
- 我何等地遗憾，不曾会得它！

===Original===
- Who have prevailed upon?

===Chinese===
- 谁占得了上风？

===Original===
- I had gained ten lewis.

===Chinese===
- 我赢了十个路易。

===Original===
- Till at what o’clock its had play one?

===Chinese===
- 直玩到了几点钟呢？

===Original===
- Un till two o’clock after mid night.

===Chinese===
- 直到半夜以后两点钟。

===Original===
- At what o’clock are you go to bed.

===Chinese===
- 您是几点钟去睡的。

===Original===
- Half pass three.

===Chinese===
- 半过三点。

===Original===
- I am no astonished if you get up so late.

===Chinese===
- 我毫不惊讶，既然您起得这般迟。

===Original===
- What o’clock is it?

===Chinese===
- 现在是什么点钟了？

===Original===
- What o’clock you think is it?

===Chinese===
- 您想现在是什么点钟？

===Original===
- I think is not yet eight o’clock.

===Chinese===
- 我想还尚未八点钟。

===Original===
- How is that, eight ’clock! it is ten ’clock struck.

===Chinese===
- 怎么会，八点钟！十点钟已经敲过了。

===Original===
- It must then what I rise me quickly.

===Chinese===
- 那我必须把我自己快快起来了。

===Original===
- Adieu, my deer, I leave you. If can to see you at six clock to the hotel from —﻿—﻿—, we swill dine togetter.

===Chinese===
- 再会了，我的亲爱的，我告辞了。若能够在六点钟于————旅馆相见，我们便将一同用饭。

===Original===
- Willingly. Good by.

===Chinese===
- 十分乐意。再会。
