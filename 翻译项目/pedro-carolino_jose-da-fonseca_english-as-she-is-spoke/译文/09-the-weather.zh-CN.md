# The Weather / 天气

===Original===
- We shall have a fine weather to day.

===Chinese===
- 今天我们将有一个好天气。

===Original===
- There is some foggy.

===Chinese===
- 有一些雾蒙蒙。

===Original===
- I fear of the thunderbolt.

===Chinese===
- 我害怕那霹雳。

===Original===
- The sun rise on.

===Chinese===
- 太阳升上来。

===Original===
- The sun lie down.

===Chinese===
- 太阳躺下了。

===Original===
- It is light moon’s.

===Chinese===
- 这是月亮的光亮。
