# From the House-Keeping / 从家务

===Original===
- I don’t know more what I won’t with they servants.

===Chinese===
- 我不再知道我要拿这些仆人们怎么样了。

===Original===
- I tell the same, it is not more some good servants. Any one take care to sweep neither to make fire at what I may be up.

===Chinese===
- 我也这么说，如今再没有一些好仆人了。谁也不肯费心去扫地，也不肯在我该起身的时候生火。

===Original===
- How the times are changed! Anciently I had some servants who were divine my thought. The duty was done at the instant, all things were cleanly hold one may look on the furnitures now as you do see. It is too different, whole is covered from dust; the pier-glasses, side-boards, the pantries, the chests of drawers, the walls selves, are changed of colours.

===Chinese===
- 时代变得多么厉害啊！古时候我有一些能占卜我的念头的仆人。差事顷刻之间就办妥，一切东西都被干干净净地保持着——您可以亲眼看看如今这些家具，正如您所见的那样。这可是太不一样了，整个都盖满了灰尘；大壁镜、餐具柜、食品柜、五斗橱，还有那些墙壁本身，全都变了颜色。

===Original===
- Believe me, send again whole the people; I take upon my self to find you some good servants for to succeed them.

===Chinese===
- 相信我，把这些人全都打发走吧；我亲自负责替您找一些好仆人，为了去接替他们。

===Original===
- Ah! what I shall be oblige to you of it!

===Chinese===
- 啊！我将为这件事多么感激您啊！
