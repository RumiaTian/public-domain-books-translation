8. The reference here is to a famous folktale﻿—best known in Pushkin’s version, “The Little Gold Fish.” A fisherman, having caught a little gold fish, is promised the fulfillment of all his wishes for its release. All his demands, instigated by his wife, are granted, beginning with a substitution of a new trough for a broken one, and up to the attainment of rank and wealth; but finally the wife insists upon the fulfillment of a wish so insolent that, upon his return from interviewing his benefactress, the fisherman finds his wife sitting at the same old broken trough, before his former humble hut. —﻿B. G. G. ↩︎

8. 此处所指是一则著名民间故事——以普希金的版本《小金鱼》最为人熟知。渔夫捕到一条小金鱼，金鱼许诺只要放生，便满足他的一切愿望。在妻子的怂恿下，他的要求一一得到满足，从换掉破损的旧木盆开始，直到取得地位与财富；但最后妻子坚持要实现一个无礼至极的愿望，以致渔夫面见恩人归来时，发现妻子又坐回那只破木盆前，守着原先那座寒酸的小屋。——B. G. G. ↩︎

12. 圣湖。——B. G. G. ↩︎
