## XXXIV Given a Chance / XXXIV 得到一个机会

===Original===
"Come in, Mr. Bagley," I said, "and bring the children. I want to talk with you all. Merton, you go home with Junior."

"But, papa—" he objected.

"Do as I bid you," I said, firmly, and I entered the squalid abode.

The man and the children followed me wonderingly. I sat down and looked the man steadily in the eye for a moment.

===Chinese===
“进来吧，巴格利先生（Mr. Bagley），”我说，“把孩子们都带上。我要跟你们大家谈一谈。默顿（Merton），你跟小琼斯（Junior）回家去。”

“可是，爸爸——”他提出异议。

“照我吩咐的做。”我口气坚决地说，随即走进了那所又脏又乱的屋子。

那男人和孩子们满心纳闷地跟了进来。我坐下，目不转睛地直望着他的眼睛，盯了片刻。

===Original===
"Let us settle one thing first," I began. "Do you think I am afraid of you?"

"S'pose not, with sich backin' as yer got," was the somewhat nervous reply.

"I told Mr. Jones after I came home last night that I should fight this thing alone if no one stood by me. But you see that your neighbors have reached the limit of forbearance. Now, Mr. Bagley, I didn't remain to threaten you. There has been enough of that, and from very resolute, angry men, too. I wish to give you and yours a chance. You've come to a place where two roads branch; you must take one or the other. You can't help yourself. You and your children won't be allowed to steal or prowl about any more. That's settled. If you go away and begin the same wretched life elsewhere, you'll soon reach the same result; you and your son will be lodged in jail and put at hard labor. Would you not better make up your mind to work for yourself and family, like an honest man? Look at these children. How are you bringing them up?—Take the road to the right. Do your level best, and I'll help you. I'll let bygones be bygones, and aid you in becoming a respectable citizen."

The man showed his emotion by a slight tremor only, and after a moment's thoughtful hesitation he took my hand and said, in a hoarse, choking voice: "You've got a claim on me now which all the rest couldn't git, even if they put a rope around my neck. I s'pose I have lived like a brute, but I've been treated like one, too."

"If you'll do as I say, I'll guarantee that within six months you'll be receiving all the kindness that a self-respecting man wants," I answered.

Then, turning to his wife, I asked, "What have you in the house to eat?"

"Next to nothin'," she said, drying her eyes with her apron, and then throwing open their bare cupboard.

"Put on your coat, Bagley, and come with me," I said.

He and his wife began to be profuse with thanks.

A few minutes later, some of the neighbors were much surprised to see Bagley and myself going up the road together.

My wife, Merton, and tenderhearted Mousie were at the head of the lane watching for me. Reassured, as we approached, they returned wonderingly to the house, and met us at the door.

===Original===
Winifred gave me a pleased, intelligent look, and said, "Come in, Mr. Bagley;" while Merton and I hastened away to catch up with neglected work.

"Your husband's been good to me," said the man, abruptly.

"That's because he believes you are going to be good to yourself and your family," was her smiling reply.

"Will you come and see my wife?" he asked.

===Chinese===
“你今天干得很好，我的孩子。现在去看看你的鸡吧，大的小的都照看一遍。这样你一天的活儿就完了，随你想玩什么都行。”说罢，我又动身往草甸去了。

到六点钟，谷仓里已经收进三车干草，默顿也装好了四箱浆果，只等上市。这时的鲍勃西又满地乱跑了，欢蹦得像只蟋蟀；温妮带着孩子的那股弹跳劲儿，也差不离地撒起欢来。巴格利把那半个钟头的工夫补足以后，扛着搂草耙顺着车道走上来了，跟前一个傍晚不一样，没有跟着他那条丑狗。过了一会儿，他帮我把箱子抬上赶集马车；然后，有些笨拙地踌躇了一阵，开了口：

“我说，达勒姆先生，您就不能自个儿给老弟派个活儿？我跟您交个实底，我是真心想撑起来的；可我知道，去了罗林斯（Rollins）那儿会是什么光景。他会凶得跟剁肉的斧子似的，他的伙计们还要百般奚落我。您自个儿雇我吧，我从工钱里省出钱来赔他的鸡，要么您就先扣下够数的一截，替我赔上。”

我思量了片刻，随即爽快地说：“只要罗林斯肯答应，我就照这个办。今晚我去见他。”

===Original===
"Did yer wife go to see my wife?"

"Yes, and she says she has hopes of you all. You've earned your bread today as honestly as I have, and you've more than paid for what my wife gave you this morning. Here's a quarter to make the day square, and here's a couple of baskets of raspberries left over. Take them to the children." "Well, yer bring me right to the mark," he said, emphasizing his words with a slap on his thigh. "I've got an uphill row to hoe, and it's good ter have some human critters around that'll help a feller a bit."

I laughed as I clapped him on the shoulder, and said: "You're going to win the fight, Bagley. I'll see Rollins at once, for I find I shall need another man awhile."

"Give me the job then," he said, eagerly, "and give me what you think I'm wuth;" and he jogged off home with that leaven of all good in his heart—the hope of better things.

===Chinese===
“您太太去看过我太太了吗？”

“去了，她还说，她对你们一家都抱着指望。今天你挣面包，跟我挣得一样正正当当，我太太早上给你的那些东西，你也早已赔过了。这里有两角五分钱，好把今天的账扯平；还有多出来的两篮树莓，拿去给孩子们吧。”“好哇，您这一下真把我带到点儿上了，”他说着，在大腿上猛拍一记来加重话头，“我这垄地是得往坡上犁的，身边能有几个拿人当人的肯帮老弟一把，真是难得。”

我笑着在他肩上拍了一记，说：“这一仗你准能打赢，巴格利。我这就去找罗林斯，因为我盘算下来，手头还需再添一个人手，用上一阵子。”

“那这活儿就给我吧，”他急切地说，“您觉得我值多少就给多少。”说罢，他便颠颠地往家去了，心里揣着那份使一切变好的酵头：指望往后的日子。
