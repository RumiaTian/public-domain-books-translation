## XLIII Thanksgiving Day / XLIII 感恩节

===Original===
November weather was occasionally so blustering and stormy that I turned schoolmaster in part, to relieve my wife. During the month, however, were bright, genial days, and others softened by a smoky haze, which gave me opportunity to gather and store a large crop of turnips, to trench in my celery on a dry knoll, and to bury, with their heads downward, all the cabbages for which I could not find a good market. The children still gave me some assistance, but, lessons over, they were usually permitted to amuse themselves in their own way. Winnie, however, did not lose her interest in the poultry, and Merton regularly aided in the care of the stock and in looking after the evening supply of firewood. I also spent a part of my time in the wood lot, but the main labor there was reserved for December. The chief task of the month was the laying down and covering of the tender raspberries; and in this labor Bagley again gave me his aid.

===Chinese===
十一月的天气偶尔狂暴得风雨大作，我便客串起半个教书先生，替妻子分劳。不过这个月里也有晴朗温和的日子，还有些日子被烟霭般的天色烘得柔和，这就给了我机会，收贮下一大茬芜菁，在一处干燥的小丘上开沟壅埋芹菜，又把所有卖不上好价钱的白菜一棵棵头朝下埋进土里。孩子们照旧给我搭把手，不过功课一完，通常就由着他们自寻乐趣去了。但温妮（Winnie）没有丢开她对家禽的兴趣，默顿（Merton）也照例帮着照料牲口，并经管晚间的柴火。我自己也把一部分时间花在林地里，只是那里主要的活计留待十二月。这个月的主要任务，是把娇嫩的树莓枝压倒、覆盖起来；这项活计巴格利（Bagley）又来帮了我一把。

===Original===
Thanksgiving Day was celebrated with due observance. In the morning we all heard Dr. Lyman preach, and came home with the feeling that we and the country at large were prosperous. Mr. and Mrs. Jones, with Junior, dined with us in great state, and we had our first four-course dinner since arriving in Maizeville, and at the fashionable hour of six in the evening. I had protested against my wife’s purpose of staying at home in the morning, saying we would “browse around during the day and get up appetites, while in the afternoon we could all turn cooks and help her.” Merton was excepted, and, after devouring a hasty cold lunch, he and Junior were off with their guns. As for Bobsey, he appeared to browse steadily after church, but seemed in no wise to have exhausted his capacity when at last he attacked his soup, turkey drumstick, and the climax of a pudding. Our feast was a very informal affair, seasoned with mirth and sauced with hunger. The viands, however, under my wife’s skill, would compare with any eaten in the great city, which we never once had regretted leaving. Winifred looked after the transfers from the kitchen at critical moments, while Mousie and Winnie were our waitresses. A royal blaze crackled in the open fireplace, and seemed to share in the sparkle of our rustic wit and unforced mirth, which kept plump Mrs. Jones in a perpetual quiver, like a form of jelly.

===Chinese===
感恩节过得一应如仪。上午我们全家去听莱曼博士（Dr. Lyman）讲道，回到家时心里都觉得，我们自家连同举国上下，日子都过得兴旺。琼斯先生和琼斯太太带着小琼斯（Junior），极隆重地来与我们共进晚餐；这是我们到梅兹维尔（Maizeville）之后头一回吃四道菜的宴席，而且开席赶的还是时兴的钟点——傍晚六点。我原先还抗议过妻子上午守在家里的打算，说我们要“白天四处觅食游逛，养出好胃口来，下午再一齐改行当厨子，给她搭把手”。默顿是例外，他三口两口吞下一顿匆匆的冷饭，便和小琼斯挎着枪出发了。至于鲍勃西（Bobsey），做完礼拜之后他好像就一直在稳稳当当地“啃食”个没停，可到最后对汤、火鸡腿和压轴的布丁发起总攻时，那份肚量似乎丝毫不曾告罄。我们的宴席极不拘礼数，以欢笑调味，以饥饿佐汁。不过菜肴经妻子妙手调理，足可与大城里吃到的任何一餐媲美——而对于离开那座大城，我们从来没有后悔过一回。温妮弗蕾德（Winifred）在要紧关头照管着从厨房到餐桌的转运，小耗子（Mousie）和温妮则充当我们的侍者。敞开的壁炉里一大蓬旺火噼啪作响，似乎也来分享我们乡野妙语与天然欢笑的光彩；那欢笑逗得富态的琼斯太太像一具果冻似的颤个不停。

===Original===
Her husband came out strong in his comical resume of the past year’s experience, concluding: “Well, we owe you and Mrs. Durham a vote of thanks for reforming the Bagley tribe. That appears to me an orthodox case of convarsion. First we gave him the terrors of the law. Tell yer what it is, we was a-smokin’ in wrath around him that mornin’, like Mount Sinai, and you had the sense to bring, in the nick of time, the gospel of givin’ a feller a chance. It’s the best gospel there is, I reckon.”

“Well,” I replied, becoming thoughtful for a moment with boyish memories, “my good old mother taught me that it was God’s plan to give us a chance, and help us make the most of it.”

“I remembered the Bagleys today,” Mrs. Jones remarked, nodding to my wife. “We felt they ought to be encouraged.”

“So did we,” my wife replied, sotto voce.

===Chinese===
她丈夫把过去一年的经历作了一番滑稽的回顾，讲得格外卖力，末了说道：“呃，为着把巴格利一家子改造过来，我们真该给你和达勒姆太太（Mrs. Durham）投上一票谢意。照我看，这就是一桩正正经经的皈依案例。起先，我们叫他领教了律法的可怕。不瞒你说，那天早晨，我们一伙人满腔怒火，围着他烟气腾腾，活像西奈山；亏得你有见识，赶在节骨眼上，把‘给人一个机会’的福音带了来。要我说，这是天底下顶好的福音。”

“呃，”我答道，一时让儿时的回忆勾得出了神，“我那位善良的老母亲教导过我：给我们机会，再帮我们把机会用到十足，这正是上帝的安排。”

“今天我想起了巴格利家，”琼斯太太朝我妻子点点头，说道。“我们觉得，他们应当得到鼓励。”

“我们也是这么想的，”我妻子低声应道。

===Original===
We afterward learned that the Bagleys had been provisioned for nearly a month by the goodwill of neighbors, who, a short time since, had been ready to take up arms against them.

By eight o’clock everything was cleared away, Mrs. Jones assisting my wife, and showing that she would be hurt if not permitted to do so. Then we all gathered around the glowing hearth, Junior’s *rat-a-tat-snap*! proving that our final course of nuts and cider would be provided in the usual way.

How homely it all was! how free from any attempt at display of style! yet equally free from any trace of vulgarity or ill-natured gossip. Mousie had added grace to the banquet with her blooming plants and dried grasses; and, although the dishes had been set on the table by my wife’s and children’s hands, they were daintily ornamented and inviting. All had been within our means and accomplished by ourselves; and the following morning brought no regretful thoughts. Our helpful friends went home, feeling that they had not bestowed their kindness on unthankful people whose scheme of life was to get and take, but not to return.

===Chinese===
我们后来才知道，巴格利一家靠着街坊们的好意，已经备下了差不多够吃一个月的口粮；而不久之前，这些街坊还恨不得抄起家伙来对付他们呢。

到八点钟，席面全收拾停当——琼斯太太帮着我妻子的忙，而且分明表示，若不许她插手，她是要伤心的。随后我们全都围拢到红彤彤的炉火旁；小琼斯那里*嗒嗒——咔*！地响成一片，证明我们最后一道坚果和苹果酒，正照老法子张罗出来。

这一切多么家常！全然没有一点摆阔炫排场的企图，却也全然没有一丝粗俗或恶意闲话的影子。小耗子用她那些开花的盆栽和干草穗给宴席添了几分雅致；菜盘虽是妻子和孩子们亲手端上桌的，却点缀得精致诱人。一切都量力而行，一切都亲手办成；第二天早晨也没有带来半点追悔的念头。我们那些古道热肠的朋友回家去了，心里觉得，这份好意并没有错施给不知感恩的人——那种人的活法只知捞取和领受，从想不到回报。
