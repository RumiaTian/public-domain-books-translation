## VII Mr. Jones Shows Me the Place / VII 琼斯先生带我去看那地方

===Original===
The next day, according to appointment, I went to Maizeville. John Jones met me at the station, and drove me in his box sleigh to see the farm he had written of in his laconic note. I looked at him curiously as we jogged along over the melting snow. The day was unclouded for a wonder, and the sun proved its increasing power by turning the sleigh tracks in the road into gleaming rills. The visage of my new acquaintance formed a decided contrast to the rubicund face of the beef-eating marketman. He was sandy even to his eyebrows and complexion. His scraggy beard suggested poverty of soil on his lantern jaws. His frame was as gaunt as that of a scarecrow, and his hands and feet were enormous. He had one redeeming feature, however—a pair of blue eyes that looked straight at you and made you feel that there was no "crookedness" behind them. His brief letter had led me to expect a man of few words, but I soon found that John Jones was a talker and a good-natured gossip. He knew everyone we met, and was usually greeted with a rising inflection, like this, "How are you, John?"

We drove inland for two or three miles.

"No, I didn't crack up the place, and I ain't a-goin' to," said my real estate agent. "As I wrote you, you can see for yourself when we get there, and I'll answer all questions square. I've got the sellin' of the property, and I mean it shall be a good bargain, good for me and good for him who buys. I don't intend havin' any neighbors around blamin' me for a fraud;" and that is all he would say about it.

===Chinese===
第二天，我如约前往梅兹维尔（Maizeville）。约翰·琼斯（John Jones）到车站来接我，驾着他那辆箱式雪橇，载我去看他信上简短提到的那座农场。雪橇载着我们在渐消的残雪上一路颠颠地慢行，我便好奇地打量起他来。难得这天万里无云，太阳渐增的威力，把路上的雪橇辙印都照成了一道道闪亮的细流。我这位新相识的面相，同那位吃牛肉的市场贩子红润润的脸膛，恰成鲜明的对照：他连眉毛带肤色都是沙黄色；灯笼似的瘦长脸颊上，那把稀稀拉拉的胡子，叫人想起长不出好庄稼的瘠土；身架干瘦得如同稻草人，手脚却又大得出奇。不过他有一处长处聊为补救——一双蓝眼睛端端地直望着你，叫你觉得那背后没有半点“歪门歪道”。他那封短信原使我料想此人寡言少语，可不久我就发现，约翰·琼斯不仅健谈，还是个和和气气的闲话篓子。我们一路遇见的人他无有不识，人家招呼他，调子总是往上一挑的，就像这样：“约翰，你好哇？”

我们又朝内陆赶了两三英里。

“没有，我没夸过那地方，往后也不打算夸，”我的地产经纪人说。“信上我写过，到了那儿你自己一看便知，问什么我都老老实实照直回答。这产业的卖权在我手里，我一心要把它办成一桩公道的好买卖，对我有好处，对买主也有好处。我可不想叫左邻右舍日后戳着脊梁骨，骂我使诈；”关于这地方，他肯说的话就这么多。

===Original===
On we went, over hills and down dales, surrounded by scenery that seemed to me beautiful beyond all words, even in its wintry aspect.

"What mountain is that standing off by itself?" I asked.

"Schunemunk," he said. "Your place—well, I guess it will be yours before plantin' time comes—faces that mountain and looks up the valley between it and the main highlands on the left. Yonder's the house, on the slope of this big round hill, that'll shelter you from the north winds."

===Chinese===
我们继续赶路，翻过一坡又一坡，下了一谷又一谷；四下的景致，纵然是冬日模样，在我眼里也美得难以言表。

“那座孤零零立在一旁的是什么山？”我问。

“舒内芒克山（Schunemunk），”他说。“你那地方——唔，依我看，赶不到栽种时节，它就是你的了——正对着那座山，抬眼顺着山谷望进去，左边就是那道主山梁。喏，那头就是房子，坐落在这座又圆又大的山包的坡上，北风叫它一挡，你就不用怕了。”

===Original===
I shall not describe the place very fully now, preferring that it should be seen through the eyes of my wife and children, as well as my own.

"The dwelling appears old," I said.

"Yes; part of it's a good deal more'n a hundred years old. It's been added to at both ends. But there's timbers in it that will stand another hundred years. I had a fire made in the livin'-room this mornin', to take off the chill, and we'll go in and sit down after we've looked the place over. Then you must come and take potluck with us."

===Chinese===
这地方的景致，这会儿我不打算细讲，宁可留到日后，让它借我妻子和孩子的眼睛、也借我自己的眼睛来看。

“这房子看着旧了。”我说。

“是旧；有一截子远不止一百年了，两头都接建出去过。不过屋里的木料，再撑一百年也不碍事。今儿一早我叫人在起居室里生了火，好去去寒气；等咱们把这地方看遍了，就进去坐下歇脚。回头你务必留下来，跟我们吃顿便饭——有什么吃什么。”

===Original===
At first I was not at all enthusiastic, but the more I examined the place, and thought it over, the more it grew on my fancy. When I entered the main room of the cottage, and saw the wide, old-fashioned fireplace, with its crackling blaze, I thawed so rapidly that John Jones chuckled. "You're amazin' refreshin' for a city chap. I guess I'll crack on another hundred to the price."

"I thought you were not going to crack up the place at all."

"Neither be I. Take that old armchair, and I'll tell you all about it. The place looks rather run down, as you have seen. Old Mr. and Mrs. Jamison lived here till lately. Last January the old man died, and a good old man he was. His wife has gone to live with a daughter. By the will I was app'inted executor and trustee. I've fixed on a fair price for the property, and I'm goin' to hold on till I get it. There's twenty acres of plowable land and orchard, and a five-acre woodlot, as I told you. The best part of the property is this. Mr. Jamison was a natural fruit grower. He had a heap of good fruit here and wouldn't grow nothin' but the best. He was always a-speerin' round, and when he come across something extra he'd get a graft, or a root or two. So he gradually came to have the best there was a-goin' in these parts. Now I tell you what it is, Mr. Durham, you can buy plenty of new, bare places, but your hair would be gray before you'd have the fruit that old man Jamison planted and tended into bearing condition; and you can buy places with fine shade trees and all that, and a good show of a garden and orchard, but Jamison used to say that an apple or cherry was a pretty enough shade tree for him, and he used to say too that a tree that bore the biggest and best apples didn't take any more room than one that yielded what was fit only for the cider press. Now the p'int's just here. You don't come to the country to amuse yourself by developin' a property, like most city chaps do, but to make a livin'. Well, don't you see? This farm is like a mill. When the sun's another month higher it will start all the machinery in the apple, cherry, and pear trees and the small fruits, and it will turn out a crop the first year you're here that will put money in your pocket."

===Chinese===
起初我毫无热忱，可这地方越看、越琢磨，越叫我动心。等我走进农舍的正屋，望见那座老式宽大的壁炉里火苗噼啪作响，我这一颗心化得飞快，化得那样快，逗得约翰·琼斯呵呵直乐。“你这个城里人，倒真叫人痛快。我看哪，我还得给价钱再吹上一百块。”

“我还道你压根儿不打算吹嘘那地方的呢。”

“我本来也没吹。你坐那把旧扶手椅，我原原本本讲给你听。这地方瞧着相当破败，方才你也看见了。老贾米森（Jamison）先生和太太一向住到这里，直到不久前。今年一月老头儿过世了——他可是位顶好的老人。老伴儿如今跟一个闺女过活去了。遗嘱上委派我做了遗嘱执行人和受托人。这产业的价钱我已经定了公道的一口数，不到这个数我不撒手。能耕的地带果园一共二十英亩，另有五英亩的林子，先前我同你讲过。这产业顶要紧的好处还在这里：贾米森先生天生是块种果树的料。他这里好果子攒了一大堆，除了顶好的，他啥也不种。他老在四下里踅摸，一碰上出挑的品种，就讨一枝接穗，或是弄它一两株根苗。这么着，这一带顶好的果木，便渐渐都归了他。达勒姆（Durham）先生，如今我把话给你挑明：新的、光秃秃的地方，你尽可以买上一大把，可要把老贾米森栽下、侍弄到挂果的那些果树等出来，只怕你的头发都等白了；带着漂亮遮荫树啦、花园果园都像模像样的地方，你也买得着，可贾米森老爱说，苹果树、樱桃树给他当遮荫树就够体面的了；他还常说，结的苹果又大又好的树，占的地方并不比那等只配送进榨汁机的树多。如今，话就说到这个点子上。你到乡下来，不是像多数城里人那样，拿置产业、办产业当消遣，你是来谋生计的。喏，你可明白？这座农场就是一盘磨。等太阳再爬高一个月，苹果树、樱桃树、梨树和小果树上那一整套机器就要开动起来；你来的头一年，它就能转出一茬收成，把钱装进你的腰包。”

===Original===
Then he named the price, half down and the rest on mortgage, if I so preferred. It was within the limit that my means permitted.

I got up and went all over the house, which was still plainly furnished in part. A large woodhouse near the back door had been well filled by the provident old man. There was ample cellar room, which was also a safeguard against dampness. Then I went out and walked around the house. It was all so quaint and homely as to make me feel that it would soon become homelike to us. There was nothing smart to be seen, nothing new except a barn that had recently been built near one of the oldest and grayest structures of the kind I had ever seen. The snow-clad mountains lifted themselves about me in a way that promised a glimpse of beauty every time I should raise my eyes from work. Yet after all my gaze lingered longest on the orchard and fruit trees that surrounded the dwelling.

"That's sensible," remarked Mr. Jones, who followed me with no trace of anxiety or impatience. "Paint, putty, and pine will make a house in a few weeks, but it takes a good slice out of a century to build up an orchard like that."

===Chinese===
接着他说出了价钱：先付一半，余下的如我愿意可作抵押。这数目在我财力许可的范围之内。

我起身把房子里里外外走了一遍；屋里还有几分是原先那副家常简朴的陈设。后门近旁有间宽大的柴房，亏得那位老人过日子有算计，里面填得满满当当。地窖也很宽敞，还能防潮气。随后我出门，绕着屋子走了一圈。一切都那么古拙而家常，叫我觉得，用不了多久，它在我们眼里便是一个家了。四下见不到一点时髦气，也没有什么新东西，只有一座新近盖起的谷仓，紧挨着我平生所见最古老、最灰白的一座同类屋舍。披雪的群山在我四围耸立，仿佛许下诺言：往后我每回从活计上抬眼，都能望见一角美景。可绕到最后，我凝望得最久的，还是宅子四围那片果园和那些果树。

“这话在理，”琼斯先生（Mr. Jones）说。他一路跟着我，不见半点焦躁或不耐烦。“油漆、油灰，再添上松木，几个星期就能变出一幢房子；可要养成这么一片果园，得从一个世纪的年头里实实在在割下一大块来。”

===Original===
"That was just what I was thinking, Mr. Jones."

"Oh, I knowed that. Well, I've got just two more things to say, then I'm done and you can take it or leave it. Don't you see? The house is on a slope facing the southeast. You get the morning sun and the southern breeze. Some people don't know what they're worth, but I, who've lived here all my life, know they're worth payin' for. Again, you see the ground slopes off to the crick yonder. That means good drainage. We don't have any malary here, and that fact is worth as much as the farm, for I wouldn't take a section of the garden of Eden if there was malary around."

"On your honor now, Mr. Jones, how far is the corner around which they have the malaria?"

===Chinese===
“我心里想的正是这个，琼斯先生。”

“嗐，我知道你想着什么。好，我还有两句话，说完就完，买不买随你。你瞧见没有？房子坐落在一面朝东南的坡上，早晨的太阳、南来的风，你都占着。有人不晓得这两样值几个钱，可我在这里住了一辈子，知道它们值得花钱买。还有，你看见地势朝着那边小溪往下溜。这就是说，泄水痛快。我们这儿从来没有疟疾，单凭这一条就顶得上整座农场；周围要是有疟疾，就是把伊甸园（Eden）割一块给我，我也不要。”

“琼斯先生，如今你拿人格担保告诉我，他们得疟疾的那个拐角，离这儿有多远？”

"Yes, if my wife is as well satisfied as I am. I want you to give me the refusal of it for one week at the price you named."

"Agreed, and I'll put it in black and white."

"Now, Mr. Jones," I began with an apologetic little laugh, "you grow one thing up here in all seasons, I fancy—an appetite. As I feel now, your potluck means good luck, no matter what is in it."

"Now you talk sense. I was a-hankerin' myself. I take stock right off in a man or a critter with an appetite. They're always improvin'. Yes, sir; Maizeville is the place to grow an appetite, and what's more we can grow plenty to satisfy it."

Mrs. Jones made a striking contrast to her husband, for she first impressed me as being short, red, and round; but her friendly, bustling ways and hearty welcome soon added other and very pleasant impressions; and when she placed a great dish of fricasseed chicken on the table she won a goodwill which her neighborly kindness has steadily increased.

===Chinese===
“要，只要我妻子也像我一样中意。请你给我一个星期的优先权，价钱照你开定的算。”

“成了，这事我要白纸黑字写下来。”

“嗳，琼斯先生，”我带着点儿告罪的笑开口说，“依我看，你们这地方一年四季只长一样东西——胃口。照我这会儿的感觉，你那顿便饭就意味着好运，锅里炖的是什么全不相干。”

“这才叫说话。我自己这会儿也正馋着呢。人也好，牲口也好，只要有胃口，我立马就信得过——这样的人见天儿都有长进。可不是嘛，梅兹维尔是顶会长胃口的地方；不光如此，填这胃口的东西，我们也种得出足足的。”

琼斯太太同她丈夫恰成鲜明的对照：头一眼的印象，她又矮、又红润、又圆墩墩的；可她那热络忙活的做派和真挚热忱的待客，很快又添上别的一些、而且是非常可喜的印象；待她把满满一大盘烩鸡端上桌来，她便赢得了我的好感——而她那待邻里的厚道情谊，又使这份好感有增无已。
