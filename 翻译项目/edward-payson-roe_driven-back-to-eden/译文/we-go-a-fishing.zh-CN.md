## XXIX We Go A-Fishing / XXIX 我们去钓鱼

===Original===
The following day, happily, proved all that we could desire. The children were up with the dawn, and Junior was not long in joining us. By eight o’clock we had finished breakfast and the morning work, our lunch basket was packed, and the market wagon stood at the door. Mr. Jones had good-naturedly promised to take a look at the premises occasionally to see that all was right. I had put but one seat in the wagon for my wife and myself, since the young people decided that a straw ride to the river would be “more fun than a parlor car.”

My wife entered into the spirit of this little outing with a zest which gave me deep content. Her face indicated no regretful thoughts turning toward the Egypt of the city; her mother love was so strong that she was happy with the children. The robins, of which there seemed no end about the house, gave us a tuneful and hilarious sendoff; the grown people and children whom we met smiled and cheered, following us with envious eyes. Each of the children held a pole aloft, and Merton said that “the wagon looked as if our lima bean patch was off on a visit.”

===Chinese===
第二天，真个天遂人愿，一切正如我们所盼。孩子们天一亮就起了床，小琼斯（Junior）也没过多久就来同我们会合。到八点钟，早饭和晨间的活计都已做完，午饭篮子装好了，赶集马车也停在了门口。琼斯先生（Mr. Jones）和和气气地应承下来，不时照看一眼宅子，看看一切是否如常。车上我只给妻子和我自己安了一个座位，因为年轻人认定，坐干草车到河边去，要比头等客厅车厢“更好玩”。

妻子把这次小小出游的兴味领会得十足，那份兴致让我深感快慰。她脸上看不出丝毫回望城市——那座“埃及”——的怅惘；她的母爱如此深厚，同孩子们在一起便觉快乐。宅子四周的知更鸟似乎多得没有尽头，啭鸣欢腾地给我们送行；一路遇见的大人小孩都笑着欢呼，用艳羡的目光目送我们。每个孩子都高高举着一根钓竿，默顿（Merton）说，这车看上去“就像我们那片利马豆地出门做客去了”。

===Original===
In the village we increased our stock of lines and hooks, and bought a few corks for floats. We soon reached the mouth of the Moodna Creek, where stood a weather-beaten boathouse, with a stable adjoining, in which old Bay could enjoy himself in his quiet, prosaic way. A good-sized boat was hired, and, as the tide was in, we at first decided to go up the creek as far as possible and float down with the ebb. This, to the children, was like a voyage of discovery, and there was a general airing of geography, each little bay, point, and gulf receiving some noted name. At last we reached a deep, shaded pool, which was eventually dubbed “Bobsey’s Luck;” for he nearly fell into it in his eagerness to take off a minnow that had managed to fasten itself to his hook.

Merton and Junior, being more experienced anglers, went ashore to make some casts on the ripples and rapids of the stream above, and secured several fine “winfish.” The rest of us were content to take it easy in the shade and hook an occasional cat and sun fish. At last the younger children wanted variety, so I permitted them to land on the wooded bank, kindle a little fire, and roast some clams that we had bought at the boathouse. The smoke and the tempting odors lured Merton and Junior, who soon proved that boys’ appetites can always be depended upon.

===Chinese===
在村子里，我们添置了几副钓线和鱼钩，又买了几只做浮子的软木塞。不多时就到了穆德纳溪（Moodna Creek）的溪口；那里立着一座饱经风霜的船屋，旁边连着马厩，好让老枣红马（old Bay）在里头照它那安安静静、平平常常的性子自得其乐。我们雇了一条不小的船；正赶上涨潮，起初大家决定尽量往溪的上游划，再趁退潮漂下来。在孩子们看来，这简直就是一场发现新大陆的航行，人人卖弄起地理学问来，每一处小湾、小岬、小汊都被安上某个响当当的名号。最后我们到了一泓荫翳的深潭，这潭后来得了个“鲍勃西的运气”（Bobsey’s Luck）的名号；因为他急着去摘一条死死咬在钩上的小鱼，险些一头栽进潭里。

默顿和小琼斯是老练些的钓手，便上了岸，到上游波纹荡漾、水流湍急的地方甩了几竿，钓着了好几条上好的“白鲦”。我们其余的人安于在树荫下悠然自得，偶尔钓起一条鲇鱼或太阳鱼，也就心满意足。到后来，小些的孩子想换换花样，我便许他们登上林木葱茏的岸坡，生起一小堆火，烤几只在船屋买的蛤蜊吃。炊烟和馋人的香气把默顿和小琼斯也勾了过去；这两位随即证明，男孩子的胃口任何时候都靠得住。

===Original===
Time passed rapidly, and I at last noticed that the tide had fallen to such a degree as to fill me with alarm.

“Come, youngsters,” I cried, “we must go back at once, or we shall have to stay here till almost night.”

They scrambled on board, and we started downstream, but soon came to shallow water, as was proved by the swift current and the ripples. A moment later we were hard aground. In vain we pushed with the oars; the boat would not budge. Then Junior sat down and coolly began to take off shoes and stockings. In a flash Merton followed his example. There was no help for it, and we had no time to lose. Over they splashed, lightening the boat, and taking the “painter,” or tie rope, at the bow, they pulled manfully. Slowly at first, but with increasing progress, the keel grated over the stones, and at last we were again afloat. A round of applause greeted the boys as they sprung back into the boat, and away we went, cautiously avoiding shoals and sandbars, until we reached Plum Point, where we expected to spend the remainder of the day. Here, for a time, we had excellent sport, and pulled up sunfish and white perch of a very fair size. Bobsey caught so large a specimen of the former variety that he had provided himself with a supper equal even to his capacity.

===Chinese===
时光过得飞快，我终于察觉，潮水已退得叫我暗暗心惊。

“喂，小家伙们，”我喊道，“咱们必须马上往回走，不然就得困在这儿，一直待到天快黑了。”

他们连爬带跳地上了船，我们便顺流而下；可是不久水流湍急、波纹乍起，表明水浅了。转眼间，船就结结实实地搁了浅。我们用桨推了又推，全然白费，船纹丝不动。这时小琼斯坐了下来，不动声色地开始脱鞋褪袜。默顿一转眼也照办起来。事已至此别无他法，而且一刻也耽搁不得。两个孩子噗通噗通蹚进水里，给船减了载，又抓住船头的“painter”——就是系船的缆绳——使出全身力气往前拖。龙骨起先慢慢蹭着石子挪动，越走越顺，我们终于重新浮了起来。两个孩子纵身跳回船上时，迎来一片喝彩；我们随即继续下行，小心翼翼地避开浅滩和沙洲，来到普拉姆角（Plum Point）——我们打算在这里消磨这天余下的时光。这地方有一阵子鱼汛极佳，我们拉上来的太阳鱼和白鲈个头都相当可观。鲍勃西钓着的那条太阳鱼尤其大，单是这一条，就为他备下了一顿连他那副肚量也对付得了的晚饭。

===Original===
The day ended in unalloyed pleasure, and never had the old farmhouse looked so like home as when it greeted us again in the evening glow of the late spring sun. Merton and Junior divided the finny spoils to their satisfaction, while Winnie and I visited the chicken coops and found that there had been no mishaps during our absence. I told my boy that I would milk the cow while he cleaned the fish for supper, and when at last we sat down we formed a tired, hilarious, and hungry group. Surely, if fish were created to be eaten, our enjoyment of their browned sweetness must have rounded out their existence completely.

“O papa!” exclaimed Merton, at the breakfast table, on Monday morning; “we haven’t planted any musk and water melons!”

“That is true,” I replied. “I find that I overlooked melons in making out my list of seeds. Indeed, I passed them over, I imagine, as a luxury that we could dispense with the first year.”

===Chinese===
这一天在毫无杂质的欢乐中收了尾；当老农舍在暮春夕阳的余晖里再度迎候我们时，它从来没有显得这么像个家。默顿和小琼斯把那一堆鳞光闪闪的渔获分得两下称心，温妮（Winnie）则随我去看了鸡舍，见我们不在的这段工夫没出一点儿岔子。我对儿子说，他去收拾晚饭吃的鱼，我来挤牛奶；等末了大家坐下来，凑成的是又疲惫、又欢腾、又饥饿的一群。说真的，倘若鱼生来就是要给人吃的，那么我们品尝它们烤得焦黄的鲜美的这番享受，想必也把它们的鱼生成全得十全十美了。

“哎呀，爸爸！”星期一早晨的早饭桌上，默顿嚷了起来，“咱们还没种香瓜和西瓜呢！”

“这话不假，”我答道，“我发觉拟种子单子的时候把瓜漏掉了。说来，我想当时是把它们当成了奢侈品，寻思头一年不种也过得去，就这么放过去了。”

===Original===
“I’ll take care of ’em if you will only let us have some,” persisted the boy; and the other children joined in his request.

“But the garden is all filled up,” I said, thoughtfully; “and I fear it is too late to plant now.”

Looks of disappointment led me to think further and I got one of my seed catalogues.

“Here are some early kinds named and perhaps they would mature; but where shall we put them?”

===Chinese===
“只要您肯让我们种点儿，往后就都归我来照管，”男孩缠着不放；其余的孩子也一齐帮他央求。

“可园子已经种得满满的了，”我沉吟着说，“而且我怕现在下种已经太迟。”

一张张失望的小脸促使我接着往下想，我便取来一册种子目录。

“这儿列着几个早熟品种，也许还赶得上成熟；可咱们把它们种到哪儿去呢？”

===Original===
“Seems to me we had better have a little less corn, if room can be made for melons,” was Merton’s suggestion.

“I’ll tell you what we’ll do,” I continued. “We’ve had such good fortune in accomplishing our early work, and you have helped so nicely, that you shall try your hand at melons. Drive your mother and Mousie down to the village this morning, and get some seeds of the nutmeg muskmelon and Phinney’s early watermelon. I’ll take two rows in the early corn on the warm garden slope, pull up every third hill, and make, in their places, nice, warm, rich beds for the seed which we will plant as soon as you come back. I don’t believe the corn will shade the melon vines too much; and as soon as we have taken off the green ears we will cut away the stalks. Thus we shall get two crops from the same ground.”

This plan was carried out, and the melon seed came up in a very promising way.

===Chinese===
“要我说，要是能腾出地方种瓜，玉米少种点儿也使得，”这是默顿出的主意。

“我看这么办，”我接着说。“咱们早先的活计办得这样顺当，你们又帮得这样像样，这一回就由你们试着种瓜吧。今天上午赶车送你妈妈和小耗子（Mousie）到村里去，买些肉豆蔻香瓜（nutmeg muskmelon）和菲尼早熟西瓜（Phinney’s early watermelon）的种子。我在菜园向阳坡的早玉米里要上两行，每隔两穴拔掉一穴，在原处弄成又暖和又肥沃的好苗床，等你们一回来就把种子播下去。我不信玉米会给瓜藤遮太多荫；再说一等掰下嫩玉米棒子，我们就把秸秆砍掉。这样，同一块地就能收两茬。”

这个计划照办了，瓜种出苗的情形也十分喜人。
