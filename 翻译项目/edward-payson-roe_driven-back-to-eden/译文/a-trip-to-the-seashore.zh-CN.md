## XXXIX A Trip to the Seashore / XXXIX 海滨一日游

===Original===
In the comparative leisure which the children enjoyed during August, they felt amply repaid for the toil of the previous months. We also managed to secure two great gala days. The first was spent in a trip to the seashore; and this was a momentous event, marred by only one slight drawback. The *Mary Powell*, a swift steamer, touched every morning at the Maizeville Landing. I learned that, from its wharf, in New York, another steamer started for Coney Island, and came back in time for us to return on the *Powell* at 3:30 p.m. Thus we could secure a delightful sail down the river and bay, and also have several hours on the beach. My wife and I talked over this little outing, and found that if we took our lunch with us, it would be inexpensive. I saw Mr. Jones, and induced him and his wife, with Junior, to join us. Then the children were told of our plan, and their hurrahs made the old house ring. Now that we were in for it, we proposed no halfway measures. Four plump spring chickens were killed and roasted, and to these were added so many ham sandwiches and hard-boiled eggs, that I declared that we were provisioned for a week. My wife nodded at Bobsey, and said, “Wait and see!”

Whom do you think we employed to mount guard during our absence? No other than Bagley. Mr. Jones said that it was like asking a wolf to guard the flock, for his prejudices yielded slowly; but I felt sure that this proof of trust would do the man more good than a dozen sermons.

===Chinese===
八月里，孩子们得享一段比较清闲的日子，觉得先前几个月的辛劳得到了丰厚的报偿。我们也设法安排出了两个盛大的欢庆日子。头一个便消磨在海滨之游上；这是一桩了不得的大事，只有一点小小的缺憾略损其美。行驶飞快的轮船「玛丽·鲍威尔」号（Mary Powell）每天早晨都停靠梅兹维尔码头（Maizeville Landing）。我打听到，在纽约，从「鲍威尔」号停靠的那座码头，另有一班轮船开往康尼岛（Coney Island），返航的时刻也正赶得上，好让我们搭下午三点半的「鲍威尔」号回家。这样，我们既能沿河顺湾享一程畅快的航行，又能在海滩上盘桓几个钟头。我和妻子把这桩小小的出游合计了一番，发现只要自带午餐，花费并不大。我去见琼斯先生（Mr. Jones），说动了他和他的太太，带着小琼斯（Junior），同我们一道去。随后把计划告诉了孩子们，一片欢呼声震得老屋嗡嗡作响。既然横竖是去定了，我们备办起来就绝不含糊。四只肥嫩的童子鸡宰了烤上，又添上许许多多的火腿三明治和煮鸡蛋，多得我直说，这份储备够我们吃上一个礼拜。妻子朝鲍勃西（Bobsey）点了点头，说：“等着瞧吧！”

你们猜，我们出门期间雇了谁来看家守卫？不是别人，正是巴格利（Bagley）。琼斯先生说，这无异于请一头狼来看守羊群，因为此人的成见消解得很慢；可我确信，这份信任的明证，比十几场布道对这个人更有益处。

===Original===
Indeed, he did seem wonderfully pleased with his task, and said, “Ye’ll find I’ve ’arned my dollar when ye git back.”

The children scarcely slept in their glad anticipation, and were up with the sun. Mr. and Mrs. Jones drove down in their light wagon, while Junior joined our children in another straw ride, packed in between the lunch baskets. We had ample time after reaching the landing to put our horses and vehicles in a safe place, and then we watched for the *Powell*. Soon we saw her approaching Newtown, four miles above, then speeding toward the wharf, and rounding into it, with the ease and grace of a swan. We scrambled aboard, smiled at by all. I suppose we did not form, with our lunch baskets, a very stylish group, but that was the least of our troubles. I am satisfied that none of the elegant people we brushed against were half so happy as we were.

===Chinese===
说真的，他看来对这趟差事喜欢得不得了，还说：“等你们回来就知道了，我这块钱没白挣。”

孩子们欢欢喜喜地盼着，简直没怎么合眼，太阳一露头就全起来了。琼斯先生夫妇驾着他们的轻便马车下来，小琼斯则挤在几只午餐篮子中间，同我们的孩子们一道，坐上另一辆铺着干草的车子。到了码头，时间还很宽裕，我们把马匹车辆安置在稳妥的地方，随后便守候「鲍威尔」号进港。不大工夫，便望见它驶近上游四英里处的纽敦（Newtown），接着朝码头疾驰而来，随即一个回旋靠上泊位，从容娴雅，宛如一只天鹅。我们连爬带跨地上了船，人人望着我们直笑。我料想，挎着午餐篮子的我们这一伙，未必显得多么时髦体面，可那原是我们最不当回事的事。我敢肯定，同我们摩肩接踵的那些雅致人物，快活程度没有一个是及得上我们一半的。

===Original===
We stowed away our baskets and then gave ourselves up to the enjoyment of the lovely Highland scenery, and to watching the various kinds of craft that we were constantly passing. Winnie and Bobsey had been placed under bonds for good behavior, and were given to understand that they must exercise the grace of keeping moderately still. The sail down the river and bay was a long, grateful rest to us older people, and I saw with pleasure that my wife was enjoying every moment, and that the fresh salt breeze was fanning color into her cheeks. Plump Mrs. Jones dozed and smiled, and wondered at the objects we passed, for she had never been much of a traveller; while her husband’s shrewd eyes took in everything, and he often made us laugh by his quaint remarks. Junior and Merton were as alert as hawks. They early made the acquaintance of deckhands who good-naturedly answered their numerous questions. I took the younger children on occasional exploring expeditions, but never allowed them to go beyond my reach, for I soon learned that Bobsey’s promises sat lightly on his conscience.

At last we reached the great Iron Pier at Coney Island, which we all traversed with wondering eyes.

===Chinese===
我们把篮子安顿好，便一头沉入高地秀色的享受之中，一路观赏着不断从身旁掠过的种种船只。温妮（Winnie）和鲍勃西先前已被“具结画押”，保证循规蹈矩，他们也心里有数：多少得拿出点功夫，安分些，别闹腾。对我们这些年纪大些的人来说，顺河入湾的这一程，是一场绵长而惬意的歇息；我高兴地看到，妻子每一刻都自得其乐，带着咸味的新鲜海风阵阵拂过，把红晕扇回了她的面颊。丰腴的琼斯太太（Mrs. Jones）打着盹儿，含着微笑，对沿途掠过的景物样样称奇，因为她向来算不得爱旅行的人；她丈夫那双精明的眼睛却把什么都收进眼底，言语古怪俏皮，时常逗得我们大笑。小琼斯和默顿（Merton）机灵得像两只小鹰，早早便同甲板上的水手们混熟了，那些人一团和气，把他们数不清的问题一一作答。我不时带着几个小的四处探险，却从不许他们跑到我伸手可及的范围之外，因为我很快便发现，鲍勃西许下的诺言，在他良心上是搁不住的。

终于，我们抵达了康尼岛那座大铁码头，一家人满眼惊奇地把它从头走了一遍。

===Original===
We established ourselves in a large pavilion, fitted up for just such picnic parties as ours. Beneath us stretched the sandy beach. We elderly people were glad enough to sit down and rest, but the children forgot even the lunch baskets, so eager were they to run upon the sand in search of shells.

All went well until an unusually high wave came rolling in. The children scrambled out of its way, with the exception of Bobsey, who was caught and tumbled over, and lay kicking in the white foam. In a moment I sprang down the steps, picked him up, and bore him to his mother.

He was wet through; and now what was to be done?

===Chinese===
我们在一座大凉棚里安顿下来，那棚子正是专为我们这样的野餐聚会备置的。脚下，沙滩绵延铺展。我们几个上了年纪的乐得坐下歇歇腿，孩子们却连午餐篮子都忘到了脑后，一心只急着奔上沙地去捡贝壳。

起初一切顺当，直到一排异乎寻常的大浪滚滚卷来。孩子们连躲带闪地避开，唯独鲍勃西没跑脱，被浪头逮个正着，翻倒在地，仰躺在白色的泡沫里乱蹬腿。我一步跳下台阶，把他抱起来，送到他母亲跟前。

他浑身透湿；这下可如何是好？

===Original===
After inquiry and consultation, I found that I could procure for him a little bathing dress which would answer during the heat of the day, and an old colored woman promised to have his clothing dry in an hour. So the one cloud on our pleasure proved to have a very bright lining, for Bobsey, since he was no longer afraid of the water, could roll in the sand and the gentle surf to his heart’s content.

Having devoured a few sandwiches to keep up our courage, we all procured bathing dresses, even Mrs. Jones having been laughingly compelled by her husband to follow the general example. When we all gathered in the passageway leading to the water, we were convulsed with laughter at our ridiculous appearance; but there were so many others in like plight that we were scarcely noticed. Mrs. Jones’s dress was a trifle small, and her husband’s immensely large. He remarked that if we could now take a stroll through Maizeville, there wouldn’t be a crow left in town.

===Chinese===
打听商议了一番，我得知可以给他弄到一件小小的游泳衣，在一天里顶热的时辰将就着穿正合适；还有一位上了年纪的有色人种老妇人答应，一个钟头之内就把他的衣服烘干。于是，罩在我们乐子上的唯一一朵乌云，竟镶上了一道明亮的银边：鲍勃西既然不再怕水，尽可以在沙堆里、在轻柔的浪花里痛痛快快地打滚了。

我们先狼吞虎咽地咽下几块三明治壮了壮胆，随后便人人都去领了游泳衣；连琼斯太太也被丈夫笑呵呵地硬劝着，随了大伙的例。等我们一伙人聚在通往海水的过道里，瞧着彼此那副滑稽相，个个笑得前仰后合；好在同样打扮的人比比皆是，几乎没有人来留意我们。琼斯太太的泳衣略微小了些，她丈夫那件却又大得没有边。他说，咱们这会儿要是能上梅兹维尔街上遛一圈，镇上的乌鸦准会吓得一只不剩。

===Original===
Mrs. Jones could not be induced to go beyond a point where the water was a foot or two deep, and the waves rolled her around like an amiable porpoise. Merton and Junior were soon swimming fearlessly, the latter wondering, meanwhile, at the buoyant quality of the salt water. My wife, Mousie, and Winnie allowed me to take them beyond the breakers, and soon grew confident. In fifteen minutes I sounded recall, and we all emerged, lank Mr. Jones now making, in very truth, an ideal scarecrow. Bobsey’s dry garments were brought, and half an hour later we were all clothed, and, as Mr. Jones remarked, “For a wonder, in our right minds.”

The onslaught then made on the lunch baskets was never surpassed, even at that place of hungry excursionists. In due time we reached home, tired, sleepy, yet content with the fact that we had filled one day with enjoyment and added to our stock of health.

===Chinese===
怎么劝也没法让琼斯太太再往前挪一步，她只站在水深一二英尺的地方，让海浪推来拥去，活像一头好性儿的鼠海豚。默顿和小琼斯转眼便毫无惧色地游开了，后者一边游，一边还惊叹海水浮力之大。我妻子、小耗子（Mousie）和温妮由着我带她们游过碎浪，不多时便游得胆壮心定。一刻钟后，我发出召回信号，大家一齐上了岸；又高又瘦的琼斯先生浑身湿衣紧贴在身上，这一回真真做成了一个活脱脱的标准稻草人。鲍勃西的干衣服取了回来，半个钟头后，人人都穿戴整齐；用琼斯先生的话说，“难得这一回，咱们个个又都心智如常了。”

随后对午餐篮子发动的那场猛攻，便是在那个饿汉云集的游览胜地，也从未有人超出其右。到了时候，我们回到了家，又乏又困，心里却很满足：我们把整整一天填满了欢乐，还给健康的积蓄上又添了一笔。

===Original===
The next morning proved that Bagley had kept his word. Everything was in order, and the amount of work accomplished in the garden showed that he had been on his mettle. Hungry as we had been, we had not emptied our lunch baskets, and my wife made up a nice little present from what remained, to which was added a package of candy, and all was carried to the Bagley cottage.

Juvenile experiences had not exactly taught the Bagley children that “the way of the transgressor is hard,”—they had not gone far enough for that—and it certainly was our duty to add such flowers as we could to the paths of virtue.

===Chinese===
第二天一早便证明，巴格利果然说到做到。一切井井有条，园子里干出的活计之多，足见他把那股劲头足足拿了出来。我们虽然饿，午餐篮子却终究没有吃空；妻子把剩下的吃食包成一份漂漂亮亮的小礼，又添上一包糖果，一并送到了巴格利家的小屋。

巴格利家孩子们的切身体验，还谈不上教会了他们“背道者的路必至艰难”的道理——他们还没有走到那一步——那么，尽力往德行的小路上添些鲜花，自然就是我们义不容辞的本分了。

===Original===
The month of August was now well advanced. We had been steadily digging the potatoes in the field and selling them in their unripened condition, until half the acre had been cleared. The vines in the lower half of the patch were now growing very yellow, and I decided to leave them, until the tubers should thoroughly ripen, for winter use. By the 20th of the month we had all the space that had been cleared, that is, half an acre, filled with Duchess and Wilson strawberries; and the plants first set were green and vigorous, with renewed running tendencies. But the runners were promptly cut off, so that the plants might grow strong enough to give a good crop of fruit in the following June.

I now began to tighten the reins on the children, and we all devoted more hours to work.

===Chinese===
这时八月已过了大半。我们一直在地里稳扎稳打地刨土豆，趁没长熟便卖出去，直到整块薯地清出了一半。地势低的那半边薯秧，如今黄得越来越深，我决定先留着不动，等块茎彻底长熟，好留作冬天的吃食。到了本月二十号，凡腾出来的空地——也就是那半英亩——我们已经全栽上了公爵（Duchess）草莓和威尔逊（Wilson）草莓；头一批栽下的秧苗青翠茁壮，又萌发出抽匍匐茎的势头。不过匍匐茎一露头便被及时剪去，好叫秧苗长得足够壮实，来年六月好结出一季丰收的果子。

这时我开始把勒在孩子们身上的缰绳收紧了些，大伙儿每天拨给活计的时辰也多了。

===Original===
During the month we gathered a few bushels of plums on the place. My wife preserved some, and the rest were sold at the boardinghouses and village stores, for Mr. Bogart had written that when I could find a home market for small quantities of produce, it would pay me better than to send them to the city. I kept myself informed as to city prices, and found that he had given me good and disinterested advice. Therefore, we managed to dispose of our small crop of early pears and peaches as we had done with the plums. Every day convinced me of the wisdom of buying a place already stocked with fruit; for, although the first cost was greater, we had immediately secured an income which promised to leave a margin of profit after meeting all expenses.

During the last week of August the potatoes were fully ripe, and Merton, Winnie, Bobsey, and I worked manfully, sorting the large from the small, as they were gathered. The crop turned out very well, especially on the lower side of the field, where the ground had been rather richer and moister than in the upper portion.

===Chinese===
这个月里，我们在庄上收了几蒲式耳李子。妻子把一部分做成了蜜饯，其余的卖给膳宿公寓和村中的店铺，因为博加特先生（Mr. Bogart）曾来信说，倘能使小批量的出产在家门口找到买主，那比运进城去卖对我更为合算。我一直留心城里的行情，发觉他给我的指点既高明，又无私心。于是，早梨和桃子那一小茬收成，我们也照李子的办法脱了手。一天又一天，我越发相信，买下一座原本就挂满果实的地方是何等明智；因为纵然当初价钱贵些，我们却即刻便有了一笔进项，而且看光景，付清各项开销之后还能有盈余。

八月最后一个星期，土豆已完全熟透，默顿、温妮、鲍勃西和我干得虎虎有生气，一边收，一边把大的小的分开拣。这季收成着实不坏，尤其地势低的那半边地，土质比上头那半边更肥，也更润。

===Original===
I did not permit Merton to dig continuously, as it was hard work for him; but he seemed to enjoy throwing out the great, smooth, white-coated fellows, and they made a pretty sight as they lay in thick rows behind us, drying, for a brief time, in the sun. They were picked up, put into barrels, drawn to the dry, cool shed, and well covered from the light. Mr. Jones had told me that as soon as potatoes had dried off after digging, they ought to be kept in the dark, since too much light makes them tough and bitter. Now that they were ripe, it was important that they should be dug promptly, for I had read that a warm rain is apt to start the new potatoes to growing, and this spoils them for table use.

So I said: “We will stick to this task until it is finished, and then we shall have another outing. I am almost ready to begin rebuilding the barn; but before I do so, I wish to visit Houghton Farm, and shall take you all with me. I may obtain some ideas which will be useful, even in my small outlay of money.”

===Chinese===
我没有让默顿一个劲儿连轴刨，那活儿对他实在太重；可他似乎挺享受把那些个大个溜圆、白皮白肉的家伙往外抛的那份乐子，土豆在我们身后密密地排成一行行，在太阳底下短暂地晾晒一阵，煞是好看。随后把它们拾起来，装进木桶，拉到干燥阴凉的棚屋里，遮得严严实实，不让见光。琼斯先生早同我讲过，土豆刨出来晾干表皮之后，就该存放在暗处，因为光照太多，土豆会变得又硬又苦。如今既已熟透，就得赶紧下地刨收，这一点很是紧要，因为我读到过，一场暖雨往往催得新土豆重新发芽，那样就不堪上桌了。

于是我说：“咱们要把这桩活儿咬住不放，干完为止，然后再出去郊游一回。我差不多就要动手翻建谷仓了；不过动工之前，我想先去一趟霍顿农场（Houghton Farm），把你们全都带上。说不定能得些有用的主意，即便在我这点小小的花销上，也派得上用场。”
