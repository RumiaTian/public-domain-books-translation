## XVII Mr. Newton’s Dilemma / 十七 牛顿先生的两难

===Original===
The most carefully guided streaks of luck may, in spite of all precautions, overflow into the wrong channel, and this had happened to Mr. Montague Newton, producing an evening that was financially disastrous and a night from which sleep was almost banished. He had had one of his little card parties; but whether it was the absence of Joan, and the inadequacy of her fluffy-haired substitute, or whether the wine had disagreed with one of the most promising victims, the result was the same. They had played chemin de fer, and the gilded pigeon, whose feathers seemed already to be ornamenting the headdress of Monty Newton, had been successful, and when he should have been signing cheques for large amounts, he was cashing his counters with a reluctant host.

===Chinese===
纵是引导得最细心周到的鸿运，任你百般提防，也难免漫出堤岸、灌错沟渠；蒙塔古·牛顿先生正撞上了这样的事，招来了一个破财遭灾的夜晚，和一夜几乎被撵走睡眠的黑暗。他照例办了一场小小的牌局；可不知是琼不在场、她那满头蓬松头发的替手不够格呢，还是酒水同一位最有指望宰到手的「肥羊」犯了冲，结果反正是一样。他们玩的是「铁路」牌（chemin de fer）；那只镀金的鸽子——瞧那光景，它的羽毛俨然已在装点蒙蒂·牛顿的头冠了——居然大获全胜；本该在这个时辰签出一张张大额支票的人，这会儿正向着一位一百个不情愿的东道主兑取筹码。

===Original===
The night started wrong with Joan’s substitute, whose name was Lisa. She had guided to the establishment, via an excellent dinner at Mero’s, the son of an African millionaire. Joan, of course, would have brought him alone, but Lisa, less experienced, had allowed a young-looking friend of the victim to attach himself to the party, and she had even expected praise for her perspicacity and enterprise in producing two birds for the stone which Mr. Newton so effectively wielded, instead of one.

===Chinese===
这一晚，打从琼的替手起就走了岔道，那替手名叫丽莎。她先在梅罗餐厅安排了一顿上好的晚餐，借这一餐把一位非洲百万富翁的儿子引进了那处场子。若换作琼，自然只会把他一个人带来；丽莎历练不足，竟容许「肥羊」的一个面相年轻的朋友自行黏上了牌局，她甚至还有意讨赏——只道自己独具眼力、锐意进取，给牛顿先生那块使得出神入化的石头，送来的鸟儿不是一只，倒是两只。

===Original===
Monty did not resent the presence of the newcomer, and rather took the girl’s view, until he learnt that Lisa’s “find” was not, as she had believed, an officer of the Guards, but a sporting young lawyer with a large criminal practice, and one who had already, as a junior, conducted several prosecutions for the Crown. The moment his name was mentioned, Monty groaned in spirit. He was, moreover, painfully sober. His friend was not so favourably situated.

===Chinese===
蒙蒂起先并不嫌这位生客在场，反倒颇以为那姑娘的见识有理；直到听说丽莎「淘来」的这位，并非她以为的禁卫军军官，而是一个手里挂着一摊大宗刑案生意、性子又好赌的年轻律师——此人早年还以初级出庭律师的身份，替王室经办过好几桩公诉——名字一报出来，蒙蒂心里便暗暗叫苦。何况这人清醒得叫人绝望。他那位朋友的处境，倒没有这么「有利」了。

===Original===
That was the first of the awkward things to happen. The second was the bad temper of the player, who, when the bank was considerably over £3,000, had first of all insisted upon the cards being reshuffled, and then had gone banquo—the game being baccarat. Even this contretemps might have been overcome, but after he had expressed his willingness to “give it,” the card which Monty had so industriously palmed, slipped from his hand to the table, and though the fact was unnoticed by the players, the lawyer’s attention being diverted at the moment, it was impossible to recover that very valuable piece of pasteboard. And Monty had done a silly thing. Instead of staging an artistic exhibition of annoyance at remarks which the millionaire’s son had made, he decided to take a chance on the natural run of the cards. And he had lost. On top of that, the slightly inebriated player had decided that when a man had won a coup of £3,000 it was time to stop playing. So Monty experienced the mortification of paying out money, and accompanying his visitor to the door with a smile that was so genial and so full of good-fellowship that the young gentleman was compelled to apologize for his boorishness.

===Chinese===
这是当晚头一件别扭事。第二件，出在那位牌客的坏脾气上：庄上的赌本早已大大过了 £3,000，他却先一口咬定要把牌重洗一遍，随后又喊了一声「班科」（banco）——他们玩的这牌戏正是巴卡拉（baccarat）。就连这个岔子，原本也还过得去；可待他表示愿意「接这一注」，蒙蒂费了老大功夫藏进掌心的那张牌，竟从他手里滑脱，掉在了桌面上。牌客们谁也没瞧见——那位律师的注意力恰好一时岔开——可这张身价贵重的纸板，是无论如何也收不回来了。蒙蒂接着又干了件蠢事：百万富翁的儿子说了几句闲话，他本可以借题发挥、煞有介事地表演一场恼怒、就此散局，他却偏要赌一赌运气，任凭牌路自然而然地走下去。于是他输了。雪上加霜的是，那位带着三分醉意的牌客认定：一个人赢下一注 £3,000，就该见好就收。蒙蒂只得忍着满心屈辱往外掏钱，还满面春风地送客到门口，那笑容亲切至极、交情十足，倒逼得那位年轻绅士为自己的粗野赔了不是。

===Original===
“Come along some other night and give me my revenge,” said Monty.

“You bet I will! I’m going to South Africa tomorrow, but I shall be back early next year, and I’ll look you up.”

Monty watched him going down the steps and hoped he would break his neck.

===Chinese===
「改天晚上再来，让我报这一箭之仇。」蒙蒂说。

「那还用说！我明天就要去南非，不过明年一开年头就回来，到时候一定来找你。」

蒙蒂望着他走下台阶，心里巴望他摔断脖子。

===Original===
He was worried about Joan—more worried than he thought it possible for him to be about so light a girl. She was necessary to him in many ways. Lisa was a bungling fool, he decided, though he sent her home without hurting her feelings. She was a useful girl in many ways, and nothing spoils a tout quicker than constant nagging.

===Chinese===
他为琼心烦——烦心的程度，超出了他自料能为这么个轻浮姑娘所动的那条界线。她于他，许多方面都少不得。丽莎是个笨手笨脚的蠢货，他如此断定；不过送她回家时，倒半点没让她觉出委屈。这姑娘许多方面都得用，而要论毁掉一个揽客的托儿，什么都快不过成天的数落。

===Original===
He felt very lonely in the house, and wandered from room to room, irritated with himself that the absence of this featherbrained girl, who had neither the education nor the breed of his own class, should make such a big difference. And it did; he had to admit as much to himself. He hated the thought of that underground room. He knew something of her temperament, and how soon her experience would get on her nerves. In many respects he wished he did not feel that way about her, because she had a big shock coming, and it was probably because he foresaw this hurt, that he was anxious to make the present as happy as he could for her.

===Chinese===
他在这宅子里觉得说不出的孤单，从一间屋子踱到另一间屋子；叫他恼火的是他自己：不过是个既无他那一阶层的教养、也无那份血统的没头脑姑娘，不在跟前，竟会造成这么大的差别。差别确确实实存在；这一点他不能不向自己承认。他一想到那间地下室便觉得厌恶。她的性子他略知一二，也知道这段遭遇要多快就有多快地磨蚀她的神经。在许多方面，他倒但愿自己对她的心思不是这样；因为一场大刺激正在前头候着她，多半正因为预见了这份伤害，他才急着想让她眼下的日子过得尽量快活。

===Original===
After he had done what he was to do, there was no reason in the world why they should be bad friends, and he would give her a big present. Girls of that class soon forget their miseries if the present is large enough. Thus he argued, tossing from side to side in his bed, and all the time his thoughts playing about that infernal cellar. What she must be feeling! He did not worry at all about Mirabelle, because—well, she was a principal in the case. To him, Joan was the real victim.

===Chinese===
等他把该办的事办完，天底下再没有半点理由叫他们做不成朋友，他还要送她一份厚礼。那个阶层的姑娘，只要礼够重，转眼便把苦楚忘个干净。他躺在床上翻来覆去，就这样一条一条替自己圆着理；心思却始终绕着那座该死的地窖打转。她这会儿心里不知是什么滋味！至于米拉贝尔，他倒一丝儿也不挂心，因为——呃，她到底是本案的正主。在他看来，真正的受害人是琼。

===Original===
Sleep did not come until daybreak, and he woke in his most irritable frame of mind. He had promised the girl he would call and see her, though he had privately arranged with Oberzohn not to go to the house until the expiry of the five days.

===Chinese===
直到天蒙蒙亮他才睡着；一觉醒来，心境恶劣到了无以复加的地步。他答应过那姑娘要来看她，虽然他私下里同欧伯佐恩讲定，五天期满之前不进那所宅子。

===Original===
By lunchtime he could stand the worry no longer, and, ordering his car, drove to a point between New Cross and Bermondsey, walking on foot the remainder of the distance. Mr. Oberzohn expected the visit. He had a shrewd knowledge of his confederate’s mental outfit, and when he saw this well-dressed man picking a dainty way across the littered ground, he strolled out on the steps to meet him.

===Chinese===

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Original===

===Chinese===
蒙蒂觉得这个问题不劳他回答。

===Original===
