## The Little Brawl at Allen / 阿伦的小械斗

### I / I

===Original===
“I think,” said Cairell Whiteskin, “that although judgement was given against Fionn, it was Fionn had the rights of it.”

===Chinese===
“我看，”白皮卡雷尔（Cairell Whiteskin）说，“裁决虽然判了芬恩（Fionn）输，理却在芬恩这边。”

===Original===
“He had eleven hundred killed,” said Conán amiably, “and you may call that the rights of it if you like.”

===Chinese===
“他可是折了一千一百条人命，”柯南（Conán）和颜悦色地说，“你乐意的话，尽管管那叫理好了。”

===Original===
“All the same﻿—” Cairell began argumentatively.

===Chinese===
“话虽如此——”卡雷尔摆出抬杠的架势开了腔。

===Original===
“And it was you that commenced it,” Conán continued.

===Chinese===
“再说，先开头的是你。”柯南接着说。

===Original===
“Ho! Ho!” Cairell cried. “Why, you are as much to blame as I am.”

===Chinese===
“嚯！嚯！”卡雷尔嚷道，“怎么着，你我的过错一样大。”

===Original===
“No,” said Conán, “for you hit me first.”

===Chinese===
“不一样，”柯南说，“是你先动的手。”

===Original===
“And if we had not been separated﻿—” the other growled.

===Chinese===
“还有，要不是旁人把咱们拉开——”那一位低吼道。

===Original===
“Separated!” said Conán, with a grin that made his beard poke all around his face.

===Chinese===
“拉开！”柯南说，咧嘴一笑，笑得满脸的胡茬朝四面八方炸了开去。

===Original===
“Yes, separated. If they had not come between us I still think﻿—”

===Chinese===
“对，拉开。他们要不上来隔在中间，我到如今还觉得——”

===Original===
“Don’t think out loud, dear heart, for you and I are at peace by law.”

===Chinese===
“别把想的说出声来，亲爱的，你我已经依法讲和了。”

===Original===
“That is true,” said Cairell, “and a man must stick by a judgement. Come with me, my dear, and let us see how the youngsters are shaping in the school. One of them has rather a way with him as a swordsman.”

===Chinese===
“这话不假，”卡雷尔说，“裁决定下的事，男人就得守住。跟我来吧，亲爱的，去看看小的们在学校里练得怎么样了。有一个耍起剑来，颇有一手。”

===Original===
“No youngster is any good with a sword,” Conán replied.

===Chinese===
“小的们使剑都不成。”柯南答道。

===Original===
“You are right there,” said Cairell. “It takes a good ripe man for that weapon.”

===Chinese===
“这话你说着了，”卡雷尔说，“那件兵器，得一个熟透了的好汉才使得。”

===Original===
“Boys are good enough with slings,” Conán continued, “but except for eating their fill and running away from a fight, you can’t count on boys.”

===Chinese===
“玩投石索，小子们倒还凑合，”柯南接着说，“可除了填饱肚皮和临阵开溜，小子们是靠不住的。”

===Original===
The two bulky men turned towards the school of the Fianna.

===Chinese===
两条魁梧大汉转过身，朝芬尼亚勇士团（Fianna）的学校走去。

---

===Original===
It happened that Fionn mac Uail had summoned the gentlemen of the Fianna and their wives to a banquet. Everybody came, for a banquet given by Fionn was not a thing to be missed. There was Goll mor mac Morna and his people; Fionn’s son Oisín and his grandson Oscar. There was Dermod of the Gay Face, Caelte mac Ronán﻿—but indeed there were too many to be told of, for all the pillars of war and battle-torches of the Gael were there.

===Chinese===
正赶上尤尔之子芬恩（Fionn mac Uail）传召勇士团的众位绅士偕妻子前来赴宴。人人都到了，因为芬恩摆下的宴席，是任谁也不肯错过的事。到场的有戈尔·麦克·莫尔纳（Goll mor mac Morna）和他的人马；有芬恩的儿子奥辛（Oisín）和孙子奥斯卡（Oscar）；有快活脸迪尔梅德（Dermod of the Gay Face）、卡尔特·麦克·罗南（Caelte mac Ronán）——其实人多得数也数不尽，因为盖尔人的战争支柱、战斗火炬，一个不落全到场了。

===Original===
The banquet began.

===Chinese===
宴会开始了。

===Original===
Fionn sat in the Chief Captain’s seat in the middle of the fort; and facing him, in the place of honour, he placed the mirthful Goll mac Morna; and from these, ranging on either side, the nobles of the Fianna took each the place that fitted his degree and patrimony.

===Chinese===
芬恩坐在堡垒正中统领的首席上；他把那位快活的戈尔·麦克·莫尔纳安置在迎面的上座；再从这两位向两侧依次排开，勇士团的贵族们各就各位，位位都合着自家的身份与世业。

===Original===
After good eating, good conversation; and after good conversation, sleep﻿—that is the order of a banquet: so when each person had been served with food to the limit of desire the butlers carried in shining, and jewelled drinking-horns, each having its tide of smooth, heady liquor. Then the young heroes grew merry and audacious, the ladies became gentle and kind, and the poets became wonders of knowledge and prophecy. Every eye beamed in that assembly, and on Fionn every eye was turned continually in the hope of a glance from the great, mild hero.

===Chinese===
吃过美馔，是妙谈；妙谈过后，是安眠——宴会就是这个次序。于是待人人都吃到了心满意足，司酒们便捧进亮闪闪、镶珠宝的饮酒号角，每一只里都涨着一泓滑润上头的酒浆。年轻的好汉们欢畅胆大起来，淑女们温柔可亲起来，诗人们则一个个成了知识与预言的奇观。满座的眼波都在放光；满座的眼睛又不住地转向芬恩，巴望那位宽和的大英雄回赠一瞥。

===Original===
Goll spoke to him across the table enthusiastically.

===Chinese===
戈尔隔着桌子，兴冲冲地对他说起话来。

===Original===
“There is nothing wanting to this banquet, O Chief,” said he.

===Chinese===
“啊，统领，这场宴会缺什么都不缺了。”他说。

===Original===
And Fionn smiled back into that eye which seemed a well of tenderness and friendship.

===Chinese===
芬恩便含笑回望那只眼睛——那眼睛好似一口温柔与情谊的深井。

===Original===
“Nothing is wanting,” he replied, “but a well-shaped poem.”

===Chinese===
“样样都有，”他答道，“只缺一首模样周正的诗。”

===Original===
A crier stood up then, holding in one hand a length of coarse iron links and in the other a chain of delicate, antique silver. He shook the iron chain so that the servants and followers of the household should be silent, and he shook the silver one so that the nobles and poets should hearken also.

===Chinese===
这时一名传令官站起身来，一手提着一截粗粝的铁链，一手提着一串精巧的古银链。他抖响铁链，是要宅里的仆役和随从肃静；他抖响银链，是要贵族和诗人们也侧耳来听。

===Original===
Fergus, called True-Lips, the poet of the Fianna-Finn, then sang of Fionn and his ancestors and their deeds. When he had finished Fionn and Oisín and Oscar and mac Lugac of the Terrible Hand gave him rare and costly presents, so that every person wondered at their munificence, and even the poet, accustomed to the liberality of kings and princes, was astonished at his gifts.

===Chinese===
于是，人称真唇的费格斯（Fergus True-Lips）——芬尼亚勇士团（Fianna-Finn）的诗人——唱起了芬恩、他的列祖列宗和他们的功业。他唱罢，芬恩、奥辛、奥斯卡和可怕之手麦克·卢加克（mac Lugac of the Terrible Hand）纷纷赠他珍奇贵重的礼物，人人惊叹他们的豪阔，连这位见惯了君王公子出手大方的诗人，也不免为自己的所得咋舌。

===Original===
Fergus then turned to the side of Goll mac Morna, and he sang of the Forts, the Destructions, the Raids, and the Wooings of clann-Morna; and as the poems succeeded each other, Goll grew more and more jovial and contented.

===Chinese===
随后费格斯转向戈尔·麦克·莫尔纳那一席，唱起莫尔纳氏族（clann-Morna）的《堡寨》《焚毁》《袭掠》《求婚》；诗一首接着一首，戈尔越听越欢畅，越听越受用。

===Original===
When the songs were finished Goll turned in his seat.

===Chinese===
歌唱完了，戈尔在座上转过身来。

===Original===
“Where is my runner?” he cried.

===Chinese===
“我的信使呢？”他喊道。

===Original===
He had a woman runner, a marvel for swiftness and trust.

===Chinese===
他有一名女信使，论脚程、论可靠，都是一桩奇观。

===Original===
She stepped forward.

===Chinese===
她走上前来。

===Original===
“I am here, royal captain.”

===Chinese===
“我在这里，尊贵的统领。”

===Original===
“Have you collected my tribute from Denmark?”

===Chinese===
“丹麦的贡赋，你收上来了吗？”

===Original===
“It is here.”

===Chinese===
“就在这里。”

===Original===
And, with help, she laid beside him the load of three men of doubly refined gold. Out of this treasure, and from the treasure of rings and bracelets and torques that were with him, Goll mac Morna paid Fergus for his songs, and, much as Fionn had given, Goll gave twice as much.

===Chinese===
于是在旁人帮衬下，她把三个男子才扛得动的双重精炼之金卸放在他身旁。戈尔·麦克·莫尔纳便从这批财宝里，再添上随身带来的戒指、手镯和颈环，付给费格斯歌酬；不论芬恩给了多少，戈尔给的都是双倍。

===Original===
But, as the banquet proceeded, Goll gave, whether it was to harpers or prophets or jugglers, more than anyone else gave, so that Fionn became displeased, and as the banquet proceeded he grew stern and silent.

===Chinese===
可是宴席往下进行，无论赏竖琴师、赏先知还是赏要把戏的，戈尔出手都比谁都重，芬恩见了心中不快；宴席越往后，他脸色越沉，话也越少。

### II / II

===Original===
The wonderful gift-giving of Goll continued, and an uneasiness and embarrassment began to creep through the great banqueting hall.

===Chinese===
戈尔那一场好精彩的赠礼还在继续，一阵不安与尴尬开始在大宴会厅里悄悄爬行。

===Original===
Gentlemen looked at each other questioningly, and then spoke again on indifferent matters, but only with half of their minds. The singers, the harpers, and jugglers submitted to that constraint, so that every person felt awkward and no one knew what should be done or what would happen, and from that doubt dullness came, with silence following on its heels.

===Chinese===
绅士们面面相觑，眼含探问，随即又拣些不相干的闲话来说，心却只用上了一半。歌者、竖琴师和要把戏的也都受着这份拘束，于是人人觉得别扭，谁也不知道该做什么、又会出什么事；疑虑一生，沉闷便至，寂静又紧跟在沉闷的脚后跟上来。

===Original===
There is nothing more terrible than silence. Shame grows in that blank, or anger gathers there, and we must choose which of these is to be our master.

===Chinese===
再没有比寂静更可怕的了。羞愧在那片空白里生长，或是怒气在那片空白里聚积，我们必须挑出其中一个来做自己的主人。

===Original===
That choice lay before Fionn, who never knew shame.

===Chinese===
这道选择就摆在芬恩面前——他从来不知道羞愧是什么。

===Original===
“Goll,” said he, “how long have you been taking tribute from the people of Lochlann?”

===Chinese===
“戈尔，”他说，“你从洛克兰（Lochlann）人那里收贡，收了多久了？”

===Original===
“A long time now,” said Goll.

===Chinese===
“有些年头了。”戈尔说。

===Original===
And he looked into an eye that was stern and unfriendly.

===Chinese===
他抬眼望去，望进的那只眼睛冷峻而不友善。

===Original===
“I thought that my rent was the only one those people had to pay,” Fionn continued.

===Chinese===
“我原以为那些人要缴的租，只我这一份。”芬恩接着说。

===Original===
“Your memory is at fault,” said Goll.

===Chinese===
“你记岔了。”戈尔说。

===Original===
“Let it be so,” said Fionn. “How did your tribute arise?”

===Chinese===
“就算记岔了吧，”芬恩说，“你这份贡是怎么起的头？”

===Original===
“Long ago, Fionn, in the days when your father forced war on me.”

===Chinese===
“早了，芬恩，就在你父亲硬把战争塞给我的那些日子。”

===Original===
“Ah!” said Fionn.

===Chinese===
“啊！”芬恩说。

===Original===
“When he raised the High King against me and banished me from Ireland.”

===Chinese===
“他搬动至高王来对付我、把我逐出爱尔兰的那些日子。”

===Original===
“Continue,” said Fionn, and he held Goll’s eye under the great beetle of his brow.

===Chinese===
“讲下去。”芬恩说，同时用他那甲虫般隆起的大眉，把戈尔的眼睛牢牢罩在底下。

===Original===
“I went into Britain,” said Goll, “and your father followed me there. I went into White Lochlann (Norway) and took it. Your father banished me thence also.”

===Chinese===
“我去了不列颠，”戈尔说，“你父亲追到不列颠。我去了白洛克兰（White Lochlann，即挪威），把它占了，你父亲又把我从那儿逐走。”

===Original===
“I know it,” said Fionn.

===Chinese===
“这我知道。”芬恩说。

===Original===
“I went into the land of the Saxons and your father chased me out of that land. And then, in Lochlann, at the battle of Cnocha your father and I met at last, foot to foot, eye to eye, and there, Fionn!”

===Chinese===
“我去了撒克逊人的地界，你父亲把我从那片土地上也撵了出去。后来在洛克兰的克诺卡（Cnocha）之战中，你父亲和我终于碰上了——脚对着脚，眼对着眼，就在那儿，芬恩！”

===Original===
“And there, Goll?”

===Chinese===
“就在那儿怎么样，戈尔？”

===Original===
“And there I killed your father.”

===Chinese===
“就在那儿，我杀了你父亲。”

===Original===
Fionn sat rigid and unmoving, his face stony and terrible as the face of a monument carved on the side of a cliff.

===Chinese===
芬恩僵坐着，一动不动，那张脸冷硬怕人，像凿在崖壁上的石像的面孔。

===Original===
“Tell all your tale,” said he.

===Chinese===
“把你的事从头讲完。”他说。

===Original===
“At that battle I beat the Lochlannachs. I penetrated to the hold of the Danish king, and I took out of his dungeon the men who had lain there for a year and were awaiting their deaths. I liberated fifteen prisoners, and one of them was Fionn.”

===Chinese===
“那一仗，我打垮了洛克兰人。我一路打进丹麦王的堡垒，从他的地牢里放出那些躺了一年、正等死期的人。我救出十五名囚徒，其中一个就是芬恩。”

===Original===
“It is true,” said Fionn.

===Chinese===
“是真的。”芬恩说。

===Original===
Goll’s anger fled at the word.

===Chinese===
这话一出，戈尔的怒气逃走了。

===Original===
“Do not be jealous of me, dear heart, for if I had twice the tribute I would give it to you and to Ireland.”

===Chinese===
“别嫉妒我，亲爱的，这贡赋若是有双份，我全都给你，给爱尔兰。”

===Original===
But at the word jealous the Chief’s anger revived.

===Chinese===
可“嫉妒”二字一出，统领的怒气又活了过来。

===Original===
“It is an impertinence,” he cried, “to boast at this table that you killed my father.”

===Chinese===
“放肆，”他喊道，“在这张桌子上夸口说你杀了我父亲！”

===Original===
“By my hand,” Goll replied, “if Fionn were to treat me as his father did I would treat Fionn the way I treated Fionn’s father.”

===Chinese===
“凭我的手起誓，”戈尔答道，“芬恩若照他父亲待我的法子待我，我就照我待芬恩父亲的法子待芬恩。”

===Original===
Fionn closed his eyes and beat away the anger that was rising within him. He smiled grimly.

===Chinese===
芬恩闭上眼睛，把心头正在升起的怒气打发了回去。他狞笑了一下。

===Original===
“If I were so minded, I would not let that last word go with you, Goll, for I have here an hundred men for every man of yours.”

===Chinese===
“我若真存了这个心，你最后那句话就带不出这座大厅，戈尔，因为你的手下一个，我这里就有一百个。”

===Original===
Goll laughed aloud.

===Chinese===
戈尔放声大笑。

===Original===
“So had your father,” he said.

===Chinese===
“你父亲当年，人手也一样多。”他说。

===Original===
Fionn’s brother, Cairell Whiteskin, broke into the conversation with a harsh laugh.

===Chinese===
芬恩的兄弟白皮卡雷尔一声刺耳的大笑，打断了话头。

===Original===
“How many of Fionn’s household has the wonderful Goll put down?” he cried.

===Chinese===
“好一个了不得的戈尔，放倒过芬恩家里多少条汉子？”他喊道。

===Original===
But Goll’s brother, bald Conán the Swearer, turned a savage eye on Cairell.

===Chinese===
可戈尔的兄弟、秃头的起誓者柯南（Conán the Swearer），把一只凶眼转向了卡雷尔。

===Original===
“By my weapons,” said he, “there were never less than an hundred-and-one men with Goll, and the least of them could have put you down easily enough.”

===Chinese===
“凭我的兵器起誓，”他说，“跟着戈尔的人从来没有少过一百零一个，最不济的那一个，也能把你轻轻松松放倒。”

===Original===
“Ah!” cried Cairell. “And are you one of the hundred-and-one, old scaldhead?”

===Chinese===
“啊哈！”卡雷尔喊道，“那你也是那一百零一里的一个喽，老癞头？”

===Original===
“One indeed, my thick-witted, thin-livered Cairell, and I undertake to prove on your hide that what my brother said was true and that what your brother said was false.”

===Chinese===
“正是其中一个，我这脑瓜笨、胆子薄的卡雷尔。我预备就在你那张皮上证明：我兄弟说的是真话，你兄弟说的是假话。”

===Original===
“You undertake that,” growled Cairell, and on the word he loosed a furious buffet at Conán, which Conán returned with a fist so big that every part of Cairell’s face was hit with the one blow. The two then fell into grips, and went lurching and punching about the great hall. Two of Oscar’s sons could not bear to see their uncle being worsted, and they leaped at Conán, and two of Goll’s sons rushed at them. Then Oscar himself leaped up, and with a hammer in either hand he went battering into the melee.

===Chinese===
“你预备着尝吧。”卡雷尔咬牙低吼，话音未落，一记狂怒的掌击已抽向柯南；柯南回敬的一拳大得出奇，只这一拳，卡雷尔脸上处处都挨到了。两人随即扭作一团，跌跌撞撞、拳脚翻飞，在大厅里滚打。奥斯卡的两个儿子见自家长辈落了下风，忍无可忍，朝柯南扑去；戈尔的两个儿子冲上来迎住。跟着奥斯卡本人一跃而起，两手各抡一柄铁锤，砸进了混战。

===Original===
“I thank the gods,” said Conán, “for the chance of killing yourself, Oscar.”

===Chinese===
“多谢众位神明，”柯南说，“给了我一个亲手宰你的机会，奥斯卡。”

===Original===
These two encountered then, and Oscar knocked a groan of distress out of Conán. He looked appealingly at his brother Art og mac Morna, and that powerful champion flew to his aid and wounded Oscar. Oisín, Oscar’s father, could not abide that; he dashed in and quelled Art Og. Then Rough Hair mac Morna wounded Oisín and was himself tumbled by mac Lugac, who was again wounded by Gara mac Morna.

===Chinese===
这两位随即交手，奥斯卡一击，从柯南身上敲出一声痛苦的呻吟。柯南满眼哀告地望向自家兄弟阿特·奥格·麦克·莫尔纳（Art og mac Morna），那位雄健的猛士飞身来援，伤了奥斯卡。奥斯卡的父亲奥辛哪里看得下去，冲进去降服了阿特·奥格。跟着，乱发麦克·莫尔纳（Rough Hair mac Morna）伤了奥辛，自己却被麦克·卢加克掀翻在地；麦克·卢加克转身又挨了加拉·麦克·莫尔纳（Gara mac Morna）一下，也挂了彩。

===Original===
The banqueting hall was in tumult. In every part of it men were giving and taking blows. Here two champions with their arms round each other’s necks were stamping round and round in a slow, sad dance. Here were two crouching against each other, looking for a soft place to hit. Yonder a big-shouldered person lifted another man in his arms and threw him at a small group that charged him. In a retired corner a gentleman stood in a thoughtful attitude while he tried to pull out a tooth that had been knocked loose.

===Chinese===
