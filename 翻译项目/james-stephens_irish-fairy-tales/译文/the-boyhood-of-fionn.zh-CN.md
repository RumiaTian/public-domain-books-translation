## The Boyhood of Fionn / 芬恩的少年时代

===Original===
> He was a king, a seer and a poet. He was a lord with a manifold and great train. He was our magician, our knowledgable one, our soothsayer. All that he did was sweet with him. And, however ye deem my testimony of Fionn excessive, and, although ye hold my praising overstrained, nevertheless, and by the King that is above me, he was three times better than all I say.
> — Saint Patrick

===Chinese===
> 他是王者，是先知，是诗人。他是一位扈从如云的君主。他是我们的术士，我们的智者，我们的预言者。他所做的一切，在他都是甜美的。无论你们怎样觉得我给芬恩作的证言过了头，无论你们怎样认为我的称颂太夸张，然而，凭着我头上的那位王起誓，他比我所说的一切还要好上三倍。
> ——圣帕特里克（Saint Patrick）

### I / I

===Original===
Fionn1 got his first training among women. There is no wonder in that, for it is the pup’s mother teaches it to fight, and women know that fighting is a necessary art although men pretend there are others that are better. These were the women druids, Bovmall and Lia Luachra.

===Chinese===
芬恩1（Fionn）最早是在女人中间受的训。这没什么可奇怪的，因为教小狗崽打架的本是狗妈妈，女人也明白：打架是一门少不了的手艺，尽管男人偏要装出还有别样的手艺更了不起。教他的，是两位女德鲁伊：波芙玛尔（Bovmall）和丽娅·卢阿赫拉（Lia Luachra）。

===Original===
It will be wondered why his own mother did not train him in the first natural savageries of existence, but she could not do it. She could not keep him with her for dread of the clann-Morna. The sons of Morna had been fighting and intriguing for a long time to oust her husband, Uail, from the captaincy of the Fianna of Ireland, and they had ousted him at last by killing him. It was the only way they could get rid of such a man; but it was not an easy way, for what Fionn’s father did not know in arms could not be taught to him even by Morna. Still, the hound that can wait will catch a hare at last, and even Mananánn sleeps.

===Chinese===
人们会奇怪，他自己的母亲为什么不亲口传授他生存最初的那几课天然的野性。她是没法子。她不敢把他留在身边，因为怕莫尔纳部族（clann-Morna）。莫尔纳的儿子们明争暗斗了许久，要把她的丈夫尤尔（Uail）从爱尔兰芬尼亚勇士团大统领的位子上挤下去，最后他们挤掉了他——用的法子是杀掉他。要除掉那样一个男人，这是唯一的法子；可这法子也来之不易，因为在兵器这一门上，芬恩的父亲不知道的东西，连莫尔纳也教不了他。不过，等得起的那只猎狗，终究逮得着野兔；就连玛纳南（Mananánn），也有打盹的时候。

===Original===
Fionn’s mother was beautiful, long-haired Muirne: so she is always referred to. She was the daughter of Teigue, the son of Nuada from Faery, and her mother was Ethlinn. That is, her brother was Lugh of the Long Hand himself, and with a god, and such a god, for brother we may marvel that she could have been in dread of Morna or his sons, or of anyone. But women have strange loves, strange fears, and these are so bound up with one another that the thing which is presented to us is not often the thing that is to be seen.

===Chinese===
芬恩的母亲，是美丽的长发的穆尔娜——人们提起她，永远是这样称呼。她是泰格（Teigue）的女儿；泰格是来自仙境（Faery）的努阿达（Nuada）之子；她的母亲是埃丝琳（Ethlinn）。这就是说，她的哥哥不是别人，正是长臂卢格（Lugh of the Long Hand）本人。有这样一位神做哥哥，而且是这样一位神，她居然还会怕莫尔纳、怕他的儿子们、怕什么人，我们尽可以为之称奇。可是女人的爱是奇异的，怕也是奇异的，而且两者彼此纠缠得这样紧，摆在我们眼前的那个东西，往往并不是看得见的那个。

===Original===
However it may be, when Uail died Muirne got married again to the King of Kerry. She gave the child to Bovmall and Lia Luachra to rear, and we may be sure that she gave injunctions with him, and many of them. The youngster was brought to the woods of Slieve Bloom and was nursed there in secret.

===Chinese===
不管怎么说，尤尔死后，穆尔娜改嫁了凯里王（King of Kerry）。她把孩子交给波芙玛尔和丽娅·卢阿赫拉抚养，而且我们可以拿得稳：她随孩子附了一道又一道叮嘱，多得数不清。这小家伙被带到了斯利夫·布卢姆（Slieve Bloom）的林子里，在那里秘密地养大。

===Original===
It is likely the women were fond of him, for other than Fionn there was no life about them. He would be their life; and their eyes may have seemed as twin benedictions resting on the small fair head. He was fair-haired, and it was for his fairness that he was afterwards called Fionn; but at this period he was known as Deimne. They saw the food they put into his little frame reproduce itself lengthways and sideways in tough inches, and in springs and energies that crawled at first, and then toddled, and then ran. He had birds for playmates, but all the creatures that live in a wood must have been his comrades. There would have been for little Fionn long hours of lonely sunshine, when the world seemed just sunshine and a sky. There would have been hours as long, when existence passed like a shade among shadows, in the multitudinous tappings of rain that dripped from leaf to leaf in the wood, and slipped so to the ground. He would have known little snaky paths, narrow enough to be filled by his own small feet, or a goat’s; and he would have wondered where they went, and have marvelled again to find that, wherever they went, they came at last, through loops and twists of the branchy wood, to his own door. He may have thought of his own door as the beginning and end of the world, whence all things went, and whither all things came.

===Chinese===
两位女人多半是疼他的，因为除了芬恩，她们身边再没有别的活物。他就是她们的命；她们的眼睛落在他那颗小小的、金发的脑袋上，就像两道祝福。他是金头发，日后得了“芬恩”这个名字，正是为着他的金发美秀；不过在这个年纪，他的名字是代姆内（Deimne）。她们眼看着喂进他那小小身架的饭食，一寸一寸结结实实地顺着长、横着宽，化成一股股蹦跳的活力与劲头——先是爬，随后摇摇晃晃地迈，再后来就跑了。他有鸟儿做游伴，而凡是在林中栖身的生灵，想必都做过他的伙伴。小芬恩有过长长的、孤零零的晒太阳的时辰，那时候，世界好像只是阳光和一片天。也有同样长的时辰，在雨的千敲万点里——雨从林中一片叶滴到另一片叶，又这样滑落到地面——生命像影子中间的一道影子，悄悄经过。他认得那些蛇一样细的小径，窄得刚好填满他自己那双小脚，或是山羊的一双蹄子；他会纳闷这些小径通到哪里去，又会再吃一惊：原来无论通到哪里，绕着枝桠丛生的林子兜兜转转，最后都回到他自家的门口。他多半把自家的门口想成世界的起点和尽头，万物从这里出发，万物又回到这里来。

===Original===
Perhaps he did not see the lark for a long time, but he would have heard him, far out of sight in the endless sky, thrilling and thrilling until the world seemed to have no other sound but that clear sweetness; and what a world it was to make that sound! Whistles and chirps, coos and caws and croaks, would have grown familiar to him. And he could at last have told which brother of the great brotherhood was making the noise he heard at any moment. The wind too: he would have listened to its thousand voices as it moved in all seasons and in all moods. Perhaps a horse would stray into the thick screen about his home, and would look as solemnly on Fionn as Fionn did on it. Or, coming suddenly on him, the horse might stare, all a-cock with eyes and ears and nose, one long-drawn facial extension, ere he turned and bounded away with manes all over him and hoofs all under him and tails all round him. A solemn-nosed, stern-eyed cow would amble and stamp in his wood to find a flyless shadow; or a strayed sheep would poke its gentle muzzle through leaves.

===Chinese===
也许他有很久都没见过云雀，可他一定听见过它，在望不到顶的无尽高天里，一声连一声地颤鸣，直到整个世界仿佛再没有别的声音，只剩那一缕清亮的甜；能发出这声音的，又是怎样一个世界啊！哨音、啁啾、鸠语、鸦噪、蛙鸣，他都听熟了。随便哪一刻听见哪一声响，他到后来都能说出，那偌大一个弟兄会里，是哪一位弟兄在出声。还有风：他听过它的一千种嗓音，在四季里走，在种种心情里走。也许会有一匹马闯进他家四周厚厚的屏障，一本正经地望着芬恩，跟芬恩望它一样正经。再不然，冷不丁撞见他，那马会猛一愣，眼睛耳朵鼻孔一齐竖起，整张脸拉成狭长的一条，随即掉头蹦开去了——鬃毛披了一身，蹄子踏了一地，尾巴甩了一圈。一头鼻息沉沉、目光严峻的母牛，会在他的林子里踱步、跺蹄，找一片没有苍蝇的荫凉；或是有一只走散的绵羊，把温柔的鼻子从叶子中间拱过来。

===Original===
“A boy,” he might think, as he stared on a staring horse, “a boy cannot wag his tail to keep the flies off,” and that lack may have saddened him. He may have thought that a cow can snort and be dignified at the one moment, and that timidity is comely in a sheep. He would have scolded the jackdaw, and tried to out-whistle the throstle, and wondered why his pipe got tired when the blackbird’s didn’t.

===Chinese===
“一个男孩子，”他瞪着一匹瞪着他的马，也许会这样想，“一个男孩子不会摇尾巴赶苍蝇。”缺了这一样本事，他也许还闷闷不乐呢。他也许想到，一头母牛能够一边喷着鼻子一边保持尊严；想到怯生生长在绵羊身上是很好看的。他会骂寒鸦多嘴，会跟画眉比赛吹口哨，还会纳闷：为什么自己的哨子吹累了，乌鸫的却不累。

===Original===
There would be flies to be watched, slender atoms in yellow gauze that flew, and filmy specks that flittered, and sturdy, thick-ribbed brutes that pounced like cats and bit like dogs and flew like lightning. He may have mourned for the spider in bad luck who caught that fly.

===Chinese===
还有苍蝇要看：有披着黄纱的细身子的，飞着；有薄雾似的虚影一点，飘着；也有筋粗骨壮的壮实家伙，扑起来像猫，咬起来像狗，飞起来像闪电。

===Original===
There would be much to see and remember and compare, and there would be, always, his two guardians. The flies change from second to second; one cannot tell if this bird is a visitor or an inhabitant, and a sheep is just sister to a sheep; but the women were as rooted as the house itself.

===Chinese===
要看的、要记的、要比较的多着呢，而且永远有那两位保护人在。苍蝇一秒一秒地变；这只鸟是过客还是住客，谁也说不准；一只绵羊跟另一只绵羊，不过是姊妹；可那两位女人，是跟房子一样扎了根的。

### II / II

===Original===
Were his nurses comely or harsh-looking? Fionn would not know. This was the one who picked him up when he fell, and that was the one who patted the bruise. This one said:

===Chinese===
他的看护们是俊是丑？芬恩不会知道。这一位，是摔倒时把他扶起来的；那一位，是揉他乌青块的。这一位说：

===Original===
“Mind you do not tumble in the well!”

===Chinese===
“小心别掉进井里！”

===Original===
And that one:

===Chinese===
那一位则说：

===Original===
“Mind the little knees among the nettles.”

===Chinese===
“小心小膝盖，别碰着荨麻。”

===Original===
But he did tumble and record that the only notable thing about a well is that it is wet. And as for nettles, if they hit him he hit back. He slashed into them with a stick and brought them low.

===Chinese===
可他偏偏就掉了进去，还记下一笔：井这东西唯一与众不同的地方，就是它是湿的。至于荨麻，它们要是碰了他，他就还手。他抡起棍子砍进去，把它们砍得伏伏帖帖。

===Original===
There was nothing in wells or nettles, only women dreaded them. One patronised women and instructed them and comforted them, for they were afraid about one.

===Chinese===
井也罢荨麻也罢，里头其实什么都没有，只有女人自己怕。对女人呢，就得端出点长辈的宽容来教导她们、宽慰她们，因为她们老是替一个人提心吊胆。

===Original===
They thought that one should not climb a tree!

===Chinese===
她们竟然认为，一个人不该爬树！

===Original===
“Next week,” they said at last, “you may climb this one,” and “next week” lived at the end of the world!

===Chinese===
“下个星期，”她们终于说，“这棵就许你爬了。”可“下个星期”住在世界的尽头呢！

===Original===
But the tree that was climbed was not worth while when it had been climbed twice. There was a bigger one near by. There were trees that no one could climb, with vast shadow on one side and vaster sunshine on the other. It took a long time to walk round them, and you could not see their tops.

===Chinese===
可是爬过的树，爬上两回就不值一爬了。近旁总有一棵更大的。还有谁也爬不上去的树，一面铺着浩大的荫凉，另一面铺着更大的太阳。绕它们走一圈要好久好久，抬头也望不见它们的树顶。

===Original===
It was pleasant to stand on a branch that swayed and sprung, and it was good to stare at an impenetrable roof of leaves and then climb into it. How wonderful the loneliness was up there! When he looked down there was an undulating floor of leaves, green and green and greener to a very blackness of greeniness; and when he looked up there were leaves again, green and less green and not green at all, up to a very snow and blindness of greeniness; and above and below and around there was sway and motion, the whisper of leaf on leaf, and the eternal silence to which one listened and at which one tried to look.

===Chinese===
站在一根摇荡弹跳的枝上，是快活的；死死盯住那一层钻不透的叶子顶棚，然后一头钻进去，也是快活的。那上头的孤寂，多妙啊！往下看，是一片起伏的叶子地，绿了又绿、绿了更绿，一直绿到发黑；往上看，又是叶子，绿的、不太绿的、全然不绿的，一直淡到发白、绿得叫人眼盲；上上下下、四围八方，全是摇曳和晃动，叶子挨着叶子的低语，还有那永恒的寂静——人朝它听，也朝它看。

===Original===
When he was six years of age his mother, beautiful, long-haired Muirne, came to see him. She came secretly, for she feared the sons of Morna, and she had paced through lonely places in many counties before she reached the hut in the wood, and the cot where he lay with his fists shut and sleep gripped in them.

===Chinese===
他六岁那年，母亲——美丽的长发的穆尔娜——来看他了。她是悄悄来的，因为怕莫尔纳之子们；她穿过了一个又一个郡的荒僻去处，才走到林中那座小屋，走到他躺着的那张小床边——两只小拳头攥得紧紧的，拳心里攥着睡眠。

===Original===
He awakened to be sure. He would have one ear that would catch an unusual voice, one eye that would open, however sleepy the other one was. She took him in her arms and kissed him, and she sang a sleepy song until the small boy slept again.

===Chinese===
他自然是醒了的。他总有一只耳朵能捕捉陌生的嗓音，总有一只眼睛会睁开，不管另一只多困。她把他搂进怀里亲吻，唱起一支催眠的歌，直唱到小男孩重新睡着。

===Original===
We may be sure that the eye that could stay open stayed open that night as long as it could, and that the one ear listened to the sleepy song until the song got too low to be heard, until it was too tender to be felt vibrating along those soft arms, until Fionn was asleep again, with a new picture in his little head and a new notion to ponder on.

===Chinese===
我们拿得稳：那只能撑着的眼睛，那一晚撑到了撑不住为止；那只耳朵一直听着那支催眠的歌，听到歌声低得听不见了，听到它轻得连在那双柔软臂膀上的颤动也感觉不到了，听到芬恩重新睡着——小小的脑袋里装进了一幅新的画，还多了一桩要想一想的事。

===Original===
The mother of himself! His own mother!

===Chinese===
他的母亲！他自己的母亲！

===Original===
But when he awakened she was gone.

===Chinese===
可等他醒来，她已经走了。

===Original===
She was going back secretly, in dread of the sons of Morna, slipping through gloomy woods, keeping away from habitations, getting by desolate and lonely ways to her lord in Kerry.

===Chinese===
她正悄悄往回赶，怕着莫尔纳之子们，溜过阴沉沉的林子，避开有人烟的地方，拣着荒凉冷清的小路，回到她在凯里的夫君那里去。

===Original===
Perhaps it was he that was afraid of the sons of Morna, and perhaps she loved him.

===Chinese===
也许，怕莫尔纳之子们的是他；也许，她爱他。

### III / III

===Original===
The women Druids, his guardians, belonged to his father’s people. Bovmall was Uail’s sister, and, consequently, Fionn’s aunt. Only such a blood-tie could have bound them to the clann-Baiscne, for it is not easy, having moved in the world of court and camp, to go hide with a baby in a wood; and to live, as they must have lived, in terror.

===Chinese===
两位女德鲁伊，他的保护人，是他父亲族里的人。波芙玛尔是尤尔的姊妹，也就是说，是芬恩的姑母。唯有这样一条血缘，才拴得住她们去依附巴斯克内部族（clann-Baiscne）；因为在宫廷与军营里走动过的人，再带着一个婴儿躲进林子里去，可不是容易的事；还得照她们必定经历过的那样，在恐惧里过活。

===Original===
What stories they would have told the child of the sons of Morna. Of Morna himself, the huge-shouldered, stern-eyed, violent Connachtman; and of his sons﻿—young Goll Mor mac Morna in particular, as huge-shouldered as his father, as fierce in the onset, but merry-eyed when the other was grim, and bubbling with a laughter that made men forgive even his butcheries. Of Conán Mael mac Morna his brother, gruff as a badger, bearded like a boar, bald as a crow, and with a tongue that could manage an insult where another man would not find even a stammer. His boast was that when he saw an open door he went into it, and when he saw a closed door he went into it. When he saw a peaceful man he insulted him, and when he met a man who was not peaceful he insulted him. There was Garra Duv mac Morna, and savage Art Og, who cared as little for their own skins as they did for the next man’s, and Garra must have been rough indeed to have earned in that clan the name of the Rough mac Morna. There were others: wild Connachtmen all, as untameable, as unaccountable as their own wonderful countryside.

===Chinese===
她们会给这孩子讲多少莫尔纳之子们的故事啊。讲莫尔纳本人，那个宽肩膀、狠眼睛、暴脾气的康诺特（Connacht）人；讲他的儿子们——尤其是年轻的戈尔·麦克·莫尔纳（Goll Mor mac Morna），肩膀跟他父亲一样宽，冲杀起来一样凶猛，只是那一个满面阴沉的时候，这一双眼睛却亮着笑意，肚子里咕嘟咕嘟直冒笑，那份笑叫男人们连他的屠杀都能宽恕。讲他的兄弟柯南·梅尔·麦克·莫尔纳（Conán Mael mac Morna），獾子一样粗声粗气，野猪一样满脸鬃须，乌鸦一样秃着头，还有一条舌头，换个人连结巴都结不上的地方，它也能张口就骂出一句漂亮话。他的自夸是：见到开着的门，他进去；见到关着的门，他也进去。见到和气的人，他骂；遇到不和气的人，他也骂。还有加拉·杜夫·麦克·莫尔纳（Garra Duv mac Morna），还有凶悍的阿特·奥格（Art Og），这两位自己的皮和旁人的皮一样不当回事；而加拉得粗野到什么地步，才在那个部族里挣得“粗暴的麦克·莫尔纳”这个名号，也可想而知了。还有别的人：清一色狂野的康诺特人，跟他们家乡那片奇妙的山水一样，驯不服，也捉摸不透。

===Original===
Fionn would have heard much of them, and it is likely that he practised on a nettle at taking the head off Goll, and that he hunted a sheep from cover in the implacable manner he intended later on for Conán the Swearer.

===Chinese===
这些人的事，芬恩听得多了；很可能，他对着一棵荨麻练过砍下戈尔的脑袋，也从掩蔽处扑出过，追猎过一只绵羊，用的正是他日后打算用在起誓者柯南身上的那股不死不休的狠劲。

===Original===
But it is of Uail mac Baiscne he would have heard most.

===Chinese===
可他听得最多的，还是尤尔·麦克·巴斯克内的事。

===Original===
With what a dilation of spirit the ladies would have told tales of him, Fionn’s father. How their voices would have become a chant as feat was added to feat, glory piled on glory. The most famous of men and the most beautiful; the hardest fighter; the easiest giver; the kingly champion; the chief of the Fianna na h-Eirinn. Tales of how he had been waylaid and got free; of how he had been generous and got free; of how he had been angry and went marching with the speed of an eagle and the direct onfall of a storm; while in front and at the sides, angled from the prow of his terrific advance, were fleeing multitudes who did not dare to wait and scarce had time to run. ===Chinese===

===Original===
He listened to the retreating footsteps until they could be heard no more, and the one sound that came to his tense ears was the beating of his own heart.

===Chinese===
他听着那远去的脚步声，听到再也听不见；这时进到他紧绷的耳朵里的唯一声响，是他自己心的跳动。

===Original===
Even the wind had ceased, and there seemed to be nothing in the world but the darkness and himself. In that gigantic blackness, in that unseen quietude and vacancy, the mind could cease to be personal to itself. It could be overwhelmed and merged in space, so that consciousness would be transferred or dissipated, and one might sleep standing; for the mind fears loneliness more than all else, and will escape to the moon rather than be driven inwards on its own being.

===Chinese===
连风也停了。天地间仿佛只剩黑暗和他。在那份浩大的漆黑里，在那看不见的静与空里，心智可以不再专属它自己。它会被吞没、会融进太虚，知觉随之前移或者散掉，人可以站着睡去；因为心智怕孤独怕过一切，宁可逃到月亮上去，也不肯被赶回自己的深处。

===Original===
But Fionn was not lonely, and he was not afraid when the son of Midna came.

===Chinese===
可芬恩不孤独。米德纳之子到来的时候，他也不怕。

===Original===
A long stretch of the silent night had gone by, minute following minute in a slow sequence, wherein as there was no change there was no time; wherein there was no past and no future, but a stupefying, endless present which is almost the annihilation of consciousness. A change came then, for the clouds had also been moving and the moon at last was sensed behind them﻿—not as a radiance, but as a percolation of light, a gleam that was strained through matter after matter and was less than the very wraith or remembrance of itself; a thing seen so narrowly, so sparsely, that the eye could doubt if it was or was not seeing, and might conceive that its own memory was recreating that which was still absent.

===Chinese===
静夜的漫长一截过去了，一分钟跟着一分钟，缓缓相续；其中没有变化，便也没有时间；没有过去，也没有将来，只有一段叫人麻木的、无尽的长今——几乎是意识的寂灭。随后变化来了：云也一直在走，月亮终于能在云后觉出来了——不是一团光亮，而是一线渗漏的光，一缕从一层又一层物质里滤出来的微芒，比它自己的残魂、比它自己的记忆还要淡；那东西被看得这样窄、这样稀薄，眼睛竟拿不准自己是看见了还是没有看见，甚至会疑心，是自己的记忆正在再造那尚且缺席的东西。

===Original===
But Fionn’s eye was the eye of a wild creature that spies on darkness and moves there wittingly. He saw, then, not a thing but a movement; something that was darker than the darkness it loomed on; not a being but a presence, and, as it were, impending pressure. And in a little he heard the deliberate pace of that great being.

===Chinese===
可芬恩的眼睛，是野生生灵窥伺黑暗、并在其中了然行动的眼睛。于是他看见的不是一件东西，而是一段移动；某种比它所隐现的那片黑暗更黑的东西；不是一个形体，而是一股临在，一种仿佛正在压来的重压。不多时，他听见了那位伟大存在的、不慌不忙的脚步。

===Original===
Fionn bent to his spear and unloosed its coverings.

===Chinese===
芬恩俯身向他的枪，解开了枪上的包裹。

===Original===
Then from the darkness there came another sound; a low, sweet sound; thrillingly joyous, thrillingly low; so low the ear could scarcely note it, so sweet the ear wished to catch nothing else and would strive to hear it rather than all sounds that may be heard by man: the music of another world! the unearthly, dear melody of the Shí! So sweet it was that the sense strained to it, and having reached must follow drowsily in its wake, and would merge in it, and could not return again to its own place until that strange harmony was finished and the ear restored to freedom.

===Chinese===
随后，黑暗里传来另一个声音；一个低而甜的声音；欢畅得叫人颤栗，也低得叫人颤栗；低得耳朵几乎捕捉不到，甜得耳朵再不想听别的，宁可竭力去听它，也不想听人间一切听得见的声响：那是另一个世界的音乐！是仙丘那不属于人世的、无比可亲的旋律！它甜到这个地步，感官便绷紧了朝它去；一经到达，就只得昏昏然随它而行，就要融进它里头，再回不了自己的原地——直到那奇异的和声奏完，耳朵才恢复了自由。

===Original===
But Fionn had taken the covering from his spear, and with his brow pressed close to it he kept his mind and all his senses engaged on that sizzling, murderous point.

===Chinese===
可芬恩已经拆了枪上的包裹，额头紧贴着枪，把心思和全部感官都钉在那个嘶嘶作响、杀气腾腾的枪尖上。

===Original===
The music ceased and Aillen hissed a fierce blue flame from his mouth, and it was as though he hissed lightning.

===Chinese===
音乐停了，艾伦从嘴里嘶地喷出一道凶猛的蓝火——他这一嘶，仿佛嘶出的是闪电。

===Original===
Here it would seem that Fionn used magic, for spreading out his fringed mantle he caught the flame. Rather he stopped it, for it slid from the mantle and sped down into the earth to the depth of twenty-six spans; from which that slope is still called the Glen of the Mantle, and the rise on which Aillen stood is known as the Ard of Fire.

===Chinese===
看到这里，倒像是芬恩用了法术：他张开镶着流苏的披篷，把火焰接住了。说接住，不如说截住——火从披篷上一滑，直钻进地里，钻下去有二十六拃深；从此，那道坡至今还叫斗篷峡谷（Glen of the Mantle），艾伦站过的那道高冈，就叫火之高地（Ard of Fire）。

===Original===
One can imagine the surprise of Aillen mac Midna, seeing his fire caught and quenched by an invisible hand. And one can imagine that at this check he might be frightened, for who would be more terrified than a magician who sees his magic fail, and who, knowing of power, will guess at powers of which he has no conception and may well dread.

===Chinese===
想象得出艾伦·麦克·米德纳的惊愕：他的火，竟被一只看不见的手接住、掐灭了。也想象得出，这一挫之下他也许害怕了；一个眼睁睁看着自己法术失灵的术士，还有谁比他更惊恐？他既然识得权能，就必然会揣想出他全无概念的种种权能，而且多半会怕。

===Original===
Everything had been done by him as it should be done. His pipe had been played and his timpan, all who heard that music should be asleep, and yet his fire was caught in full course and was quenched.

===Chinese===
他该做的都按部就班做足了。管笛吹了，蒂姆潘琴也奏了，听见那音乐的本该都睡着，可他的火偏偏正在半途被截住，被掐灭。

===Original===
Aillen, with all the terrific strength of which he was master, blew again, and the great jet of blue flame came roaring and whistling from him and was caught and disappeared.

===Chinese===
艾伦使出他掌握的全部可怕气力，又喷了一次；那股蓝火的大喷流吼着、啸着从他体内冲出，又被接住，消失了。

===Original===
Panic swirled into the man from Faery; he turned from that terrible spot and fled, not knowing what might be behind, but dreading it as he had never before dreaded anything, and the unknown pursued him; that terrible defence became offence and hung to his heel as a wolf pads by the flank of a bull.

===Chinese===
一阵恐慌灌进了这位仙境来客。他掉头逃离那个可怕的地点，不知道背后可能是什么，却怕它怕得超过了平生所怕的一切；那未知在后头追他；那可怕的守备变成了攻杀，咬住他的脚跟，像狼贴着公牛的侧腹一路轻步追赶。

===Original===
And Aillen was not in his own world! He was in the world of men, where movement is not easy and the very air a burden. In his own sphere, in his own element, he might have outrun Fionn, but this was Fionn’s world, Fionn’s element, and the flying god was not gross enough to outstrip him. Yet what a race he gave, for it was but at the entrance to his own Shí that the pursuer got close enough. Fionn put a finger into the thong of the great spear, and at that cast night fell on Aillen mac Midna. His eyes went black, his mind whirled and ceased, there came nothingness where he had been, and as the Birgha whistled into his shoulder-blades he withered away, he tumbled emptily and was dead. Fionn took his lovely head from its shoulders and went back through the night to Tara.

===Chinese===
何况艾伦已不在自己的世界！他这是在人的世界，在这里行动不爽利，连空气都是重负。在他自己的疆界、自己的元素里，他说不定跑得过芬恩；可这是芬恩的世界，芬恩的元素，这位飞逃的神祇血肉不够浊重，在这样的世界里施展不开，甩不掉他。饶是这样，他也跑出了一场好竞逐——直到他自己仙丘的入口，追击者才逼得够近。芬恩把一根手指扣进大枪的皮索，就在那一掷里，黑夜落到了艾伦·麦克·米德纳身上。他的眼睛黑了，他的心智旋了一下，停了；他所在的地方来了一场空无；比尔加呼啸着钉进他的肩胛，他枯萎下去，空空地跌倒，死了。芬恩从他肩上取下那颗可爱的头颅，穿夜回塔拉去了。

===Original===
Triumphant Fionn, who had dealt death to a god, and to whom death would be dealt, and who is now dead!

===Chinese===
得胜的芬恩啊——他曾把死亡送给一位神，死亡也将被送给他，而如今，他已经死了！

===Original===
He reached the palace at sunrise.

===Chinese===
日出时分，他到了王宫。

===Original===
On that morning all were astir early. They wished to see what destruction had been wrought by the great being, but it was young Fionn they saw and that redoubtable head swinging by its hair.

===Chinese===
那天早晨，人人起得早。他们本想看看那位伟大存在造成了怎样的毁灭，可他们看见的，是年轻的芬恩，和那颗提在头发里悠荡的狰狞头颅。

===Original===
“What is your demand?” said the Ard-Rí.

===Chinese===
“你有什么要求？”至高王说。

===Original===
“The thing that it is right I should ask,” said Fionn: “the command of the Fianna of Ireland.”

===Chinese===
“问我该问的那一件，”芬恩说，“爱尔兰芬尼亚勇士团的统率之权。”

===Original===
“Make your choice,” said Conn to Goll Mor; “you will leave Ireland, or you will place your hand in the hand of this champion and be his man.”

===Chinese===
“你自己挑，”康恩对戈尔·麦克·莫尔纳说，“要么离开爱尔兰，要么把手放进这位勇士的手里，做他的人。”

===Original===
Goll could do a thing that would be hard for another person, and he could do it so beautifully that he was not diminished by any action.

===Chinese===
别人做来千难万难的事，戈尔做得来；而且做得那样漂亮，无论做什么，都不折损他一分。

===Original===
“Here is my hand,” said Goll.

===Chinese===
“我的手在这儿。”戈尔说。

===Original===
And he twinkled at the stern, young eyes that gazed on him as he made his submission.

===Chinese===
他俯首称臣的时候，朝那双瞪视着他的严厉的年轻眼睛，眨了眨。
