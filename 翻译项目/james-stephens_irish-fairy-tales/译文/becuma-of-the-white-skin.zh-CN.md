## Becuma of the White Skin / 白肤贝库玛

### I / I

===Original===
There are more worlds than one, and in many ways they are unlike each other. But joy and sorrow, or, in other words, good and evil, are not absent in their degree from any of the worlds, for wherever there is life there is action, and action is but the expression of one or other of these qualities.

===Chinese===
世界不止一个，它们在许多方面互不相同。可无论在哪个世界里，欢乐与悲哀——换句话说，善与恶——都从不缺席，只是各有各的分量；因为凡有生命处即有行动，而行动，无非是这两种性质之一的表露。

===Original===
After this Earth there is the world of the Shí. Beyond it again lies the Many-Coloured Land. Next comes the Land of Wonder, and after that the Land of Promise awaits us. You will cross clay to get into the Shí; you will cross water to attain the Many-Coloured Land; fire must be passed ere the Land of Wonder is attained, but we do not know what will be crossed for the fourth world.

===Chinese===
尘世之外，有仙丘（the Shí）的世界。再往外，横着斑斓之国（the Many-Coloured Land）。接下来是奇境（the Land of Wonder），再过去，应许之地（the Land of Promise）正等着我们。进仙丘，须跨越泥土；到斑斓之国，须跨越水；要抵达奇境，必先穿越火；至于第四个世界须跨越什么，我们就不得而知了。

===Original===
This adventure of Conn the Hundred Fighter and his son Art was by the way of water, and therefore he was more advanced in magic than Fionn was, all of whose adventures were by the path of clay and into Faery only, but Conn was the High King and so the arch-magician of Ireland.

===Chinese===
「百战」康恩（Conn the Hundred Fighter）和他儿子阿特（Art）的这场奇遇走的是水路；因此论法术，他比芬恩（Fionn）更为高深——芬恩的种种奇遇走的都是土路，而且仅止于仙境（Faery）；何况康恩身为至高王（the High King），也就顺理成章，是爱尔兰的首席大法师。

===Original===
A council had been called in the Many-Coloured Land to discuss the case of a lady named Becuma Cneisgel, that is, Becuma of the White Skin, the daughter of Eogan Inver. She had run away from her husband Labraid and had taken refuge with Gadiar, one of the sons of Manannán mac Lir, the god of the sea, and the ruler, therefore, of that sphere.

===Chinese===
斑斓之国里召开了一场议事会，审理一位女士的案子：她名叫贝库玛·克尼斯格尔（Becuma Cneisgel），意即白肤贝库玛，是埃奥甘·因弗（Eogan Inver）的女儿。她抛下丈夫拉布拉德（Labraid）私逃，投奔了加迪亚尔（Gadiar）——海神玛纳南·麦克·利尔（Manannán mac Lir）的儿子之一；海神既是海之神，自然也是那一界的统治者。

===Original===
It seems, then, that there is marriage in two other spheres. In the Shí matrimony is recorded as being parallel in every respect with earth-marriage, and the desire which urges to it seems to be as violent and inconstant as it is with us; but in the Many-Coloured Land marriage is but a contemplation of beauty, a brooding and meditation wherein all grosser desire is unknown and children are born to sinless parents.

===Chinese===
这样看来，此外两界也是有婚姻的。据记载，仙丘的婚配与尘世的婚姻事事相当，促人成婚的那股欲望，看来也同我们这里的一样，狂热而善变；可在斑斓之国，婚姻不过是对美的观照，是一场孵育般的沉思冥想，其间全然不知更粗鄙的欲望为何物，孩子生下来，父母皆无罪。

===Original===
In the Shí the crime of Becuma would have been lightly considered, and would have received none or but a nominal punishment, but in the second world a horrid gravity attaches to such a lapse, and the retribution meted is implacable and grim. It may be dissolution by fire, and that can note a destruction too final for the mind to contemplate; or it may be banishment from that sphere to a lower and worse one.

===Chinese===
贝库玛这桩罪若在仙丘，本会被看得很轻，或不加惩罚，或只施一个名义上的发落；可在这第二重世界里，这样的失足却系着一种可怕的严肃，量出的报应无情而阴冷。或是以火销形——那意味着一种心智无法凝想的、彻底到头的毁灭；或是从那一界放逐到更低、更坏的一界去。

===Original===
This was the fate of Becuma of the White Skin.

===Chinese===
这便是白肤贝库玛的命运。

===Original===
One may wonder how, having attained to that sphere, she could have carried with her so strong a memory of the earth. It is certain that she was not a fit person to exist in the Many-Coloured Land, and it is to be feared that she was organised too grossly even for life in the Shí.

===Chinese===
人们也许要纳罕：她既然升到了那一界，怎么还能随身带着对尘世如此强烈的记忆。可以肯定，她本就不是宜居于斑斓之国的人；只怕她的禀性粗浊得太厉害，连仙丘里的生活也容不下她。

===Original===
She was an earth-woman, and she was banished to the earth.

===Chinese===
她是个尘世的女人，于是被放逐到了尘世。

===Original===
Word was sent to the Shís of Ireland that this lady should not be permitted to enter any of them; from which it would seem that the ordinances of the Shí come from the higher world, and, it might follow, that the conduct of earth lies in the Shí.

===Chinese===
旨令传遍爱尔兰的诸仙丘：不许这位女士进入其中任何一座。由此看来，仙丘的法度似出自更高的世界；而依此推论，尘世的行止善恶，怕也就握在仙丘手里。

===Original===
In that way, the gates of her own world and the innumerable doors of Faery being closed against her, Becuma was forced to appear in the world of men.

===Chinese===
就这样，自己世界的大门与仙境的无数门扉一齐对她关死了，贝库玛只得现身于人类的世界。

===Original===
It is pleasant, however, notwithstanding her terrible crime and her woeful punishment, to think how courageous she was. When she was told her sentence, nay, her doom, she made no outcry, nor did she waste any time in sorrow. She went home and put on her nicest clothes.

===Chinese===
然而，尽管她罪孽可怕、惩罚堪哀，想到她何等勇敢，总还是件叫人愉快的事。判决宣下来的时候——不，是她的劫数（Doom）宣下来的时候——她没有喊一声冤，也没有把光阴枉费在悲伤里。她回家去，穿上了自己最漂亮的衣裳。

===Original===
She wore a red satin smock, and, over this, a cloak of green silk out of which long fringes of gold swung and sparkled, and she had light sandals of white bronze on her thin, shapely feet. She had long soft hair that was yellow as gold, and soft as the curling foam of the sea. Her eyes were wide and clear as water and were grey as a dove’s breast. Her teeth were white as snow and of an evenness to marvel at. Her lips were thin and beautifully curved: red lips in truth, red as winter berries and tempting as the fruits of summer. The people who superintended her departure said mournfully that when she was gone there would be no more beauty left in their world.

===Chinese===
她穿一袭红缎衬袍，外面罩一件绿丝斗篷，斗篷上长长的金穗摇曳生辉；一双纤秀的脚上，趿着白铜打的轻便凉鞋。她一头长发柔软，黄得像金子，软得像海上卷起的泡沫。她的眼睛又阔又清，像一泓水，灰得像鸽子的胸脯。她的牙齿白得像雪，齐整得令人称奇。她的嘴唇薄薄的，弧线优美：那是真正的红唇，红得像冬天的浆果，诱人如夏天的果实。监送她离去的人们哀哀地说，她一走，他们这个世界里，美也就一无所剩了。

===Original===
She stepped into a coracle, it was pushed on the enchanted waters, and it went forward, world within world, until land appeared, and her boat swung in low tide against a rock at the foot of Ben Edair.

===Chinese===
她跨进一只柳条小舟（coracle），小舟被推上有魔法的海面，一路前行，一重世界套着一重世界，直到陆地出现，她的船才顺着落潮，悠悠荡在本·埃代尔（Ben Edair）山脚的一块岩石旁。

===Original===
So far for her.

===Chinese===
她的事，至此说罢。

### II / II

===Original===
Conn the Hundred Fighter, Ard-Rí of Ireland, was in the lowest spirits that can be imagined, for his wife was dead. He had been Ard-Ri for nine years, and during his term the corn used to be reaped three times in each year, and there was full and plenty of everything. There are few kings who can boast of more kingly results than he can, but there was sore trouble in store for him.

===Chinese===
爱尔兰的至高王（Ard-Rí）「百战」康恩，正陷在人所能想象的最低落的情绪里，因为他的王后死了。他做至高王已有九年，任内谷物一年收割三茬，样样丰足充盈。能把王道业绩夸到他这个地步的国王没有几位，可等着他的，却是钻心的祸事。

===Original===
He had been married to Eithne, the daughter of Brisland Binn, King of Norway, and, next to his subjects, he loved his wife more than all that was lovable in the world. But the term of man and woman, of king or queen, is set in the stars, and there is no escaping Doom for anyone; so, when her time came, Eithne died.

===Chinese===
他娶的王后叫埃丝妮（Eithne），是挪威国王布里斯兰·宾（Brisland Binn）的女儿；除了子民之外，他爱王后胜过世上一切可爱之物。然而男人与女人、国王与王后的期限都写在星辰上，谁的劫数也逃不脱；于是她的时限一到，埃丝妮便死了。

===Original===
Now there were three great burying-places in Ireland﻿—the Brugh of the Boyne in Ulster, over which Angus Og is chief and god; the Shí mound of Cruachan Ahi, where Ethal Anbual presides over the underworld of Connacht, and Tailltin, in Royal Meath. It was in this last, the sacred place of his own lordship, that Conn laid his wife to rest.

===Chinese===
爱尔兰原有三大葬地：一是阿尔斯特博因河畔的布鲁格（the Brugh of the Boyne），由安格斯·奥格（Angus Og）在那里主宰为神；二是克鲁亨·阿伊（Cruachan Ahi）的仙丘土冢，埃塔尔·安布阿尔（Ethal Anbual）在那里统掌康诺特（Connacht）的冥府；三是王土米斯（Royal Meath）的塔尔廷（Tailltin）。康恩便把王后安葬在最后这一处——他自己领地内的圣地。

===Original===
Her funeral games were played during nine days. Her keen was sung by poets and harpers, and a cairn ten acres wide was heaved over her clay. Then the keening ceased and the games drew to an end; the princes of the Five Provinces returned by horse or by chariot to their own places; the concourse of mourners melted away, and there was nothing left by the great cairn but the sun that dozed upon it in the daytime, the heavy clouds that brooded on it in the night, and the desolate, memoried king.

===Chinese===
她的葬礼竞技进行了九天。诗人和竖琴手唱起她的挽歌，一座方圆十英亩的石冢堆上了她的遗骸。随后哭唱止息，竞技收场；五省的诸侯们或骑马或驾车，各回各的封地；吊唁的人潮渐渐散尽，大石冢旁什么都不剩了，只剩下白天在冢上打盹的太阳，夜里在冢上盘踞的重重乌云，还有那个凄凄惶惶、满怀追忆的王。

===Original===
For the dead queen had been so lovely that Conn could not forget her; she had been so kind at every moment that he could not but miss her at every moment; but it was in the Council Chamber and the Judgement Hall that he most pondered her memory. For she had also been wise, and lacking her guidance, all grave affairs seemed graver, shadowing each day and going with him to the pillow at night.

===Chinese===
因为死去的王后是那样可爱，康恩忘不了她；她是时时刻刻那样温存，他也时时刻刻不能不想念她；而最教他反复追想的地方，是在议事厅里，在审判厅里。因为她还很有智慧；失了她的指引，一切重大的政务显得愈发重大，白天给每一天蒙上阴影，夜里又随他一同上枕。

===Original===
The trouble of the king becomes the trouble of the subject, for how shall we live if judgement is withheld, or if faulty decisions are promulgated? Therefore, with the sorrow of the king, all Ireland was in grief, and it was the wish of every person that he should marry again.

===Chinese===
国王的烦恼便成了臣民的烦恼，因为裁决若迟迟不下，谬断若连连颁行，我们还怎么活命？于是，随着国王的哀伤，整个爱尔兰都悲恸起来，人人心里都盼着他再娶。

===Original===
Such an idea, however, did not occur to him, for he could not conceive how any woman should fill the place his queen had vacated. He grew more and more despondent, and less and less fitted to cope with affairs of state, and one day he instructed his son Art to take the rule during his absence, and he set out for Ben Edair.

===Chinese===
这样的念头他却从未起过，因为他想不出天下有哪个女人填得上王后空出的位子。他越来越消沉，越来越无力应付国务；有一天，他吩咐儿子阿特在他离开期间执掌朝政，自己动身往本·埃代尔去了。

===Original===
For a great wish had come upon him to walk beside the sea; to listen to the roll and boom of long, grey breakers; to gaze on an unfruitful, desolate wilderness of waters; and to forget in those sights all that he could forget, and if he could not forget then to remember all that he should remember.

===Chinese===
因为一股强烈的心愿攫住了他：要到海边去走一走；去听长长的灰色巨浪翻滚轰鸣；去凝望那不结果实的、荒凉的水的荒野；在那些景象里，能忘掉的都忘掉；若是忘不掉，那就把该记住的都记住。

===Original===
He was thus gazing and brooding when one day he observed a coracle drawing to the shore. A young girl stepped from it and walked to him among black boulders and patches of yellow sand.

===Chinese===
他正这样望着、想着，有一天，忽见一只柳条小舟向岸边驶来。一位年轻女子从舟中步出，踏着黑色的巨砾和一片片黄沙，朝他走来。

### III / III

===Original===
Being a king he had authority to ask questions. Conn asked her, therefore, all the questions that he could think of, for it is not every day that a lady drives from the sea, and she wearing a golden-fringed cloak of green silk through which a red satin smock peeped at the openings. She replied to his questions, but she did not tell him all the truth; for, indeed, she could not afford to.

===Chinese===
身为国王，他有权发问。于是康恩把自己想得出的问题问了个遍——毕竟不是天天都有女士从海上来，何况她披着金穗绿丝斗篷，开口处还露出里头红缎衬袍的一角。对他的问题她一一作答，却没有把实情和盘托出；因为，说实在的，她实话不起。

===Original===
She knew who he was, for she retained some of the powers proper to the worlds she had left, and as he looked on her soft yellow hair and on her thin red lips, Conn recognised, as all men do, that one who is lovely must also be good, and so he did not frame any inquiry on that count; for everything is forgotten in the presence of a pretty woman, and a magician can be bewitched also.

===Chinese===
她知道他是谁，因为她还保有几分离开的那些世界所固有的神通；而康恩望着她柔软的黄发、薄薄的红唇，也像天下所有男人一样认定：长得可爱的人必定心地良善，于是在这一条上便一句也没有追问；须知在漂亮女人面前，一切都会被忘掉，法师也是会被迷住的。

===Original===
She told Conn that the fame of his son Art had reached even the Many-Coloured Land, and that she had fallen in love with the boy. This did not seem unreasonable to one who had himself ventured much in Faery, and who had known so many of the people of that world leave their own land for the love of a mortal.

===Chinese===
她告诉康恩，他儿子阿特的名声连斑斓之国都传到了，她已经爱上了这个少年。这话在康恩听来并不离奇，因为他本人在仙境历过不少险，也见惯了那个世界的许多人为着爱一个凡人而离开自己的国土。

===Original===
“What is your name, my sweet lady?” said the king.

===Chinese===
“你叫什么名字，我可爱的女士？”国王问。

===Original===
“I am called Delvcaem (Fair Shape) and I am the daughter of Morgan,” she replied.

===Chinese===
“人们叫我德尔夫卡姆（Delvcaem），意即‘美好身形’，我是摩根（Morgan）的女儿。”她答道。

===Original===
“I have heard much of Morgan,” said the king. “He is a very great magician.”

===Chinese===
“摩根其人，我听到过不少，”国王说，“他是一位非常伟大的法师。”

===Original===
During this conversation Conn had been regarding her with the minute freedom which is right only in a king. At what precise instant he forgot his dead consort we do not know, but it is certain that at this moment his mind was no longer burdened with that dear and lovely memory. His voice was melancholy when he spoke again.

===Chinese===
这场谈话之间，康恩一直以那种唯有国王才使得的细致入微的自由打量着她。他究竟在哪一瞬忘掉了亡妻，我们无从知道；但可以肯定，此刻他心里已不再压着那段亲爱可爱的记忆。再开口时，他的声音忧郁了。

===Original===
“You love my son!”

===Chinese===
“你爱我的儿子！”

===Original===
“Who could avoid loving him?” she murmured.

===Chinese===
“谁能不爱他呢？”她喃喃道。

===Original===
“When a woman speaks to a man about the love she feels for another man she is not liked. And,” he continued, “when she speaks to a man who has no wife of his own about her love for another man then she is disliked.”

===Chinese===
“一个女人对一个男人谈起她对另一个男人的爱，她就不讨人喜欢了。而且，”他继续说道，“要是她谈的这个男人自己又没有妻子，那她就要招人嫌了。”

===Original===
“I would not be disliked by you,” Becuma murmured.

===Chinese===
“我可不愿招你嫌。”贝库玛喃喃道。

===Original===
“Nevertheless,” said he regally, “I will not come between a woman and her choice.”

===Chinese===
“话虽如此，”他以王者的气度说，“我总不至于横在一个女人和她的选择中间。”

===Original===
“I did not know you lacked a wife,” said Becuma, but indeed she did.

===Chinese===
“我原本不知道你缺一位妻子。”贝库玛说。其实她知道得清清楚楚。

===Original===
“You know it now,” the king replied sternly.

===Chinese===
“你如今知道了。”国王严厉地答道。

===Original===
“What shall I do?” she inquired, “am I to wed you or your son?”

===Chinese===
“那我该怎么办？”她问，“我是嫁你呢，还是嫁你儿子？”

===Original===
“You must choose,” Conn answered.

===Chinese===
“你必须自己选。”康恩答道。

===Original===
“If you allow me to choose it means that you do not want me very badly,” said she with a smile.

===Chinese===
“你若容我自己选，那说明你要我要得不那么急。”她微笑着说。

===Original===
“Then I will not allow you to choose,” cried the king, “and it is with myself you shall marry.”

===Chinese===
“那我就不容你选，”国王喊道，“你要嫁的，是我本人。”

===Original===
He took her hand in his and kissed it.

===Chinese===
他把她的手握在自己手里，吻了吻。

===Original===
“Lovely is this pale thin hand. Lovely is the slender foot that I see in a small bronze shoe,” said the king.

===Chinese===
“多么可爱，这只苍白纤细的手。多么可爱，这只小铜鞋里的纤秀的脚。”国王说。

===Original===
After a suitable time she continued:

===Chinese===
过了合宜的时分，她继续说道：

===Original===
“I should not like your son to be at Tara when I am there, or for a year afterwards, for I do not wish to meet him until I have forgotten him and have come to know you well.”

===Chinese===
“我在塔拉（Tara）的时候，不愿你的儿子也在；此后一年也不愿。因为我要先忘掉他，先同你相熟了，才愿意见他。”

===Original===
“I do not wish to banish my son,” the king protested.

===Chinese===
“我不想放逐我的儿子。”国王抗议道。

===Original===
“It would not really be a banishment,” she said. “A prince’s duty could be set him, and in such an absence he would improve his knowledge both of Ireland and of men. Further,” she continued with downcast eyes, “when you remember the reason that brought me here you will see that his presence would be an embarrassment to us both, and my presence would be unpleasant to him if he remembers his mother.”

===Chinese===
“那其实算不得放逐，”她说，“可以派给王子一桩职分；趁这样的外出，他既能增进对爱尔兰的见识，也能增进对人的见识。再说，”她垂下眼睛继续道，“等你想起我到这里来的缘由，你就会明白：他在场，你我两人都要难堪；他若还记得他母亲，我在场，他也要不痛快。”

===Original===
“Nevertheless,” said Conn stubbornly, “I do not wish to banish my son; it is awkward and unnecessary.”

===Chinese===
“话虽如此，”康恩执拗地说，“我还是不想放逐我的儿子；这既别扭，又没有必要。”

===Original===
“For a year only,” she pleaded.

===Chinese===
“只一年。”她恳求道。

===Original===
“It is yet,” he continued thoughtfully, “a reasonable reason that you give and I will do what you ask, but by my hand and word I don’t like doing it.”

===Chinese===
“不过，”他若有所思地接着说，“你给的理由倒也成立，我就依你所请；可是凭我的手、凭我的话，我不喜欢这么办。”

===Original===
They set out then briskly and joyfully on the homeward journey, and in due time they reached Tara of the Kings.

===Chinese===
于是两人轻快欢畅地踏上了归途，适时抵达了诸王的塔拉（Tara of the Kings）。

### IV / IV

===Original===

一到塔拉，德尔夫卡姆——她的法术神通比贝库玛更为高强——便命贝库玛离开，贝库玛便走了。

===Original===
She left the king’s side. She came from the midst of the counsellors and magicians. She did not bid farewell to anyone. She did not say goodbye to the king as she set out for Ben Edair.

===Chinese===
她离开了国王身边。她从谋臣和法师们的中间走出来。她没有向任何人道别。她动身往本·埃代尔去的时候，也没有对国王说一声再见。

===Original===
Where she could go to no man knew, for she had been banished from the Many-Coloured Land and could not return there. She was forbidden entry to the Shí by Angus Og, and she could not remain in Ireland. She went to Sasana and she became a queen in that country, and it was she who fostered the rage against the Holy Land which has not ceased to this day.

===Chinese===
她还能到哪里去，没有人知道：她已被逐出斑斓之国，回不去了；安格斯·奥格禁止她进入仙丘；爱尔兰，她又留不得。她去了萨萨纳（Sasana），在那个国家做起了王后；那场针对圣地的仇恨，至今不曾止息——当初滋养它的，正是她。
