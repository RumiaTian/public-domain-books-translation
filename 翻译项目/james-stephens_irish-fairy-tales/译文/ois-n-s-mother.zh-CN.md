## Oisín’s Mother / 奥辛的母亲

### I / I

===Original===
Evening was drawing nigh, and the Fianna-Finn had decided to hunt no more that day. The hounds were whistled to heel, and a sober, homeward march began. For men will walk soberly in the evening, however they go in the day, and dogs will take the mood from their masters.

===Chinese===
夜色渐渐降临，芬尼亚勇士团（Fianna-Finn）决定这一天不再行猎了。呼哨一响，猎犬都被唤回脚边，一行人马踏上沉稳的归途。因为人不论白天怎样奔走，到了傍晚总会走得沉稳，而狗呢，主人是什么心绪，它们便是什么心绪。

===Original===
They were pacing so, through the golden-shafted, tender-coloured eve, when a fawn leaped suddenly from covert, and, with that leap, all quietness vanished: the men shouted, the dogs gave tongue, and a furious chase commenced.

===Chinese===
他们正这样走着，穿过道道金光、色彩柔嫩的黄昏，忽然一头小鹿从隐蔽处跃出——随着这一跃，一切安宁霎时无踪：人们呼喊，群犬出声，一场狂烈的追逐开始了。

===Original===
Fionn loved a chase at any hour, and, with Bran and Sceólan, he outstripped the men and dogs of his troop, until nothing remained in the limpid world but Fionn, the two hounds, and the nimble, beautiful fawn. These, and the occasional boulders, round which they raced, or over which they scrambled; the solitary tree which dozed aloof and beautiful in the path, the occasional clump of trees that hived sweet shadow as a hive hoards honey, and the rustling grass that stretched to infinity, and that moved and crept and swung under the breeze in endless, rhythmic billowings.

===Chinese===
芬恩（Fionn）无论什么时辰都爱一场追逐。他带着布兰（Bran）和斯科兰（Sceólan），把队伍里的人和狗远远甩在身后，直到那澄明的天地间只剩下芬恩、两只猎犬，和那头轻捷美丽的小鹿。再有，便是偶尔的巨石，供他们绕着疾驰，或攀上翻越；是一棵孤零零的树，在路旁独自打盹，美丽而清远；是一簇簇的树丛，像蜂巢贮蜜一般贮满甜荫；还有那窸窣作响的青草，铺展到无穷无尽，在微风里涌动、蔓爬、摇荡，化作无休无止、一浪逐一浪的起伏。

===Original===
In his wildest moment Fionn was thoughtful, and now, although running hard, he was thoughtful. There was no movement of his beloved hounds that he did not know; not a twitch or fling of the head, not a cock of the ears or tail that was not significant to him. But on this chase whatever signs the dogs gave were not understood by their master.

===Chinese===
芬恩哪怕在最狂野的时刻，心里也存着思量；此刻他虽然拼命奔跑，心里仍存着思量。他心爱的猎犬，没有一个动作是他不认得的：脑袋每一次抽动、每一次扬甩，耳朵或尾巴每一次竖立，对他都有含义。可这一场追逐里，狗儿们无论递出什么信号，主人却都读不懂了。

===Original===
He had never seen them in such eager flight. They were almost utterly absorbed in it, but they did not whine with eagerness, nor did they cast any glance towards him for the encouraging word which he never failed to give when they sought it.

===Chinese===
他从没见过它们追得这样急切。它们几乎全然忘我地扑在这追逐上，却不因急切而呜咽，也不朝他递来一眼，讨那句鼓劲的话——往常它们一讨，他从没有不给的。

===Original===
They did look at him, but it was a look which he could not comprehend. There was a question and a statement in those deep eyes, and he could not understand what that question might be, nor what it was they sought to convey. Now and again one of the dogs turned a head in full flight, and stared, not at Fionn, but distantly backwards, over the spreading and swelling plain where their companions of the hunt had disappeared.

===Chinese===
它们确实望了他，可那是他读不懂的一望。那双深邃的眼睛里有一个问题、一句陈说，他既不明白那问题会是什么，也不明白它们想传达的是什么。疾奔之中，两只狗偶尔有一颗回过头来，凝望的不是芬恩，而是远远的后方，望着同伴们消失于其上的那片铺展起伏的平原。

===Original===
“They are looking for the other hounds,” said Fionn.

===Chinese===
“它们是在找别的猎犬。”芬恩说。

===Original===
“And yet they do not give tongue! Tongue it, a Vran!” he shouted, “Bell it out, a Heólan!”

===Chinese===
“可它们竟不出声！叫起来呀，布兰！”他喊道，“吼出声来，斯科兰！”

===Original===
It was then they looked at him, the look which he could not understand and had never seen on a chase. They did not tongue it, nor bell it, but they added silence to silence and speed to speed, until the lean grey bodies were one pucker and lashing of movement.

===Chinese===
正是在这时，它们望了他——那望不透的一望，是他在追逐场上从未见过的。它们不叫，也不吼，只是把沉默叠给沉默，把速度叠给速度，直到那精瘦的灰色身躯，化作一团拧紧了又抽掠的疾动。

===Original===
Fionn marvelled.

===Chinese===
芬恩惊异不已。

===Original===
“They do not want the other dogs to hear or to come on this chase,” he murmured, and he wondered what might be passing within those slender heads.

===Chinese===
“它们不想让别的狗听见，不想让它们加入这场追逐。”他喃喃道，心里纳罕，那两颗修长的头颅里，究竟转着什么念头。

===Original===
“The fawn runs well,” his thought continued. “What is it, a Vran, my heart? After her, a Heólan! Hist and away, my loves!”

===Chinese===
“这小鹿跑得真好，”他的念头接着转，“怎么回事，布兰，我的心肝？追上她，斯科兰！嗬——追吧，我的宝贝们！”

===Original===
“There is going and to spare in that beast yet,” his mind went on. “She is not stretched to the full, nor half stretched. She may outrun even Bran,” he thought ragingly.

===Chinese===
“这畜生还有的是脚力，”他的心思继续道，“她还没使出全力，连一半也没使出。她说不定连布兰都能跑赢。”他这样想着，心头冒火。

===Original===
They were racing through a smooth valley in a steady, beautiful, speedy flight when, suddenly, the fawn stopped and lay on the grass, and it lay with the calm of an animal that has no fear, and the leisure of one that is not pressed.

===Chinese===
他们正以平稳、优美、迅疾的疾驰穿过一道平缓的山谷，小鹿却突然收步，卧在了草地上。它卧得那样安静，像一头毫无恐惧的兽；卧得那样从容，像一只不曾被逼迫的生灵。

===Original===
“Here is a change,” said Fionn, staring in astonishment. “She is not winded,” he said. “What is she lying down for?”

===Chinese===
“这可变了样了，”芬恩瞪着眼，惊诧不已。“她连气都不喘，”他说，“她卧下做什么？”

===Original===
But Bran and Sceólan did not stop; they added another inch to their long-stretched easy bodies, and came up on the fawn.

===Chinese===
布兰和斯科兰却没有停；它们舒展的长身子又拉长了一寸，朝小鹿逼了过去。

===Original===
“It is an easy kill,” said Fionn regretfully. “They have her,” he cried.

===Chinese===
“这一下唾手可得了，”芬恩惋惜地说。“它们逮住她了。”他喊道。

===Original===
But he was again astonished, for the dogs did not kill. They leaped and played about the fawn, licking its face, and rubbing delighted noses against its neck.

===Chinese===
可他又一次惊住了：狗没有扑杀。它们绕着小鹿又蹦又耍，舔它的脸，把欢喜的鼻子往它颈子上蹭。

===Original===
Fionn came up then. His long spear was lowered in his fist at the thrust, and his sharp knife was in its sheath, but he did not use them, for the fawn and the two hounds began to play round him, and the fawn was as affectionate towards him as the hounds were; so that when a velvet nose was thrust in his palm, it was as often a fawn’s muzzle as a hound’s.

===Chinese===
芬恩随即赶了上来。他拳中攥着的长矛已压低待刺，利刀却还留在鞘里，可他一样也没有用——因为小鹿和两只猎犬绕着他耍闹起来，小鹿待他的亲热，竟不输猎犬；于是每当一只天鹅绒似的鼻子伸进他的掌心，那鼻子有时是猎犬的，有时便是小鹿的。

---

===Original===
In that joyous company he came to wide Allen of Leinster, where the people were surprised to see the hounds and the fawn and the Chief and none other of the hunters that had set out with them.

===Chinese===
他就在这支欢欢喜喜的队伍陪伴下，回到了伦斯特（Leinster）广阔的阿伦（Allen）。那里的人们看见猎犬、小鹿与首领同归，同他们一道出发的猎人却一个也不见，都惊奇不已。

===Original===
When the others reached home, the Chief told of his chase, and it was agreed that such a fawn must not be killed, but that it should be kept and well treated, and that it should be the pet fawn of the Fianna. But some of those who remembered Brah’s parentage thought that as Bran herself had come from the Shí so this fawn might have come out of the Shí also.

===Chinese===
其余人回到家后，首领讲了他的追逐，众人议定：这样的小鹿万万杀不得，须得好好收养、好好相待，让它做勇士团的宝贝小鹿。不过，一些记得布兰出身来历的人心想：布兰既然本是从仙丘（the Shí）里来的，这头小鹿，说不定也是从仙丘里出来的。

### II / II

===Original===
Late that night, when he was preparing for rest, the door of Fionn’s chamber opened gently and a young woman came into the room. The captain stared at her, as he well might, for he had never seen or imagined to see a woman so beautiful as this was. Indeed, she was not a woman, but a young girl, and her bearing was so gently noble, her look so modestly high, that the champion dared scarcely look at her, although he could not by any means have looked away.

===Chinese===
深夜，他正预备歇息，芬恩寝室的门轻轻开了，一位年轻女子走进屋来。统领直望着她，这也难怪，因为他从未见过、也从未想象过能见到这样美的女子。说实在的，她还算不得女子，而是一位少女；她的仪态那样温婉高贵，她的神情那样矜持清华，这位大勇士几乎不敢看她，却又无论如何也移不开目光。

===Original===
As she stood within the doorway, smiling, and shy as a flower, beautifully timid as a fawn, the Chief communed with his heart.

===Chinese===
她立在门内，含着笑，像一朵花那样羞怯，像一头小鹿那样美得惹怜；首领便与自己的心商量起来。

===Original===
“She is the Sky-woman of the Dawn,” he said. “She is the light on the foam. She is white and odorous as an apple-blossom. She smells of spice and honey. She is my beloved beyond the women of the world. She shall never be taken from me.”

===Chinese===
“她是黎明的天上女子，”他说，“她是浪沫上的光。她像苹果花一样洁白芬芳。她身上带着香料和蜂蜜的气息。普天之下的女子，我最爱的是她。谁也别想把她从我身边夺走。”

===Original===
And that thought was delight and anguish to him: delight because of such sweet prospect, anguish because it was not yet realised, and might not be.

===Chinese===
这个念头于他是欢喜，也是煎熬：欢喜，是因为有这样甜美的指望；煎熬，是因为它还未成真，而且说不定成不了真。

===Original===
As the dogs had looked at him on the chase with a look that he did not understand, so she looked at him, and in her regard there was a question that baffled him and a statement which he could not follow.

===Chinese===
正如追逐场上猎犬曾用他读不懂的目光望他，此刻她也这样望他；她的凝注里有一个难住他的问题，有一句他无从领会的话。

===Original===
He spoke to her then, mastering his heart to do it.

===Chinese===
于是他按住心潮，开口对她说话。

===Original===
“I do not seem to know you,” he said.

===Chinese===
“我好像不认识你。”他说。

===Original===
“You do not know me indeed,” she replied.

===Chinese===
“你确实不认识我。”她答道。

===Original===
“It is the more wonderful,” he continued gently, “for I should know every person that is here. What do you require from me?”

===Chinese===
“这就更奇了，”他温和地接着说，“这里的人我本该个个认得。你有什么要求我的？”

===Original===
“I beg your protection, royal captain.”

===Chinese===
“我恳求您的保护，尊贵的大统领。”

===Original===
“I give that to all,” he answered. “Against whom do you desire protection?”

===Chinese===
“这保护我给每一个人，”他答道，“你要提防的是谁？”

===Original===
“I am in terror of the Fear Doirche.”

===Chinese===
“我怕的是费尔·多尔切（Fear Doirche）。”

===Original===
“The Dark Man of the Shí?”

===Chinese===
“仙丘的黑暗之人？”

===Original===
“He is my enemy,” she said.

===Chinese===
“他是我的仇人。”她说。

===Original===
“He is mine now,” said Fionn. “Tell me your story.”

===Chinese===
“从今往后，他就是我的仇人。”芬恩说，“把你的来历讲给我听。”

===Original===
“My name is Saeve, and I am a woman of Faery,” she commenced. “In the Shí many men gave me their love, but I gave my love to no man of my country.”

===Chinese===
“我叫赛芙（Saeve），是仙境（Faery）的一位女子，”她开口道，“在仙丘，许多男子把爱给我，可我的爱，没有给我族中的任何男子。”

===Original===
“That was not reasonable,” the other chided with a blithe heart.

===Chinese===
“这可说不过去，”那一位嗔怪道，心里却轻快。

===Original===
“I was contented,” she replied, “and what we do not want we do not lack. But if my love went anywhere it went to a mortal, a man of the men of Ireland.”

===Chinese===
“我本是知足的，”她答道，“我们不想望的东西，便也不欠缺。可若说我的爱情去了哪里，它去给了一个凡人，一个爱尔兰的男人。”

===Original===
“By my hand,” said Fionn in mortal distress, “I marvel who that man can be!”

===Chinese===
“凭我的手起誓，”芬恩痛苦万分地说，“我真想知道那男人是谁！”

===Original===
“He is known to you,” she murmured. “I lived thus in the peace of Faery, hearing often of my mortal champion, for the rumour of his great deeds had gone through the Shí, until a day came when the Black Magician of the Men of God put his eye on me, and, after that day, in whatever direction I looked I saw his eye.”

===Chinese===
“那人是您认得的，”她低声道，“我便这样住在仙境的安宁里，时常听到我那位凡人勇士的消息，因为他伟大功业的传闻，早已传遍了仙丘。直到有一天，众神之民的那位黑魔法师，把他的眼睛放到了我身上；从那天起，无论我朝哪个方向看，我总看见他的眼睛。”

===Original===
She stopped at that, and the terror that was in her heart was on her face.

===Chinese===
讲到这儿她停住了，心里的恐怖，全涌到了脸上。

===Original===
“He is everywhere,” she whispered. “He is in the bushes, and on the hill. He looked up at me from the water, and he stared down on me from the sky. His voice commands out of the spaces, and it demands secretly in the heart. He is not here or there, he is in all places at all times. I cannot escape from him,” she said, “and I am afraid,” and at that she wept noiselessly and stared on Fionn.

===Chinese===
“他无处不在，”她低声说，“他在灌木丛里，在山冈上。他从水里仰起头看我，他从天上俯下眼盯我。他的声音从虚空里发号施令，又在人心里暗暗索求。他不在东也不在西，他时时处处都在。我逃不脱他，”她说，“我好怕。”说到这里，她无声地哭了起来，眼睛直直望着芬恩。

===Original===
“He is my enemy,” Fionn growled. “I name him as my enemy.”

===Chinese===
“他是我的仇敌，”芬恩低吼道，“我指名他做我的仇敌。”

===Original===
“You will protect me?” she implored.

===Chinese===
“您会保护我的，是吗？”她恳求道。

===Original===
“Where I am let him not come,” said Fionn. “I also have knowledge. I am Fionn, the son of Uail, the son of Baiscne, a man among men and a god where the gods are.”

===Chinese===
“有我在，便容不得他来，”芬恩说，“我也有我的智识。我是芬恩，尤尔（Uail）之子、巴斯克内（Baiscne）之子的芬恩；在人们中间，是人中丈夫；在神们中间，便是神。”

===Original===
“He asked me in marriage,” she continued, “but my mind was full of my own dear hero, and I refused the Dark Man.”

===Chinese===
“他向我求过婚，”她接着说，“可我心里装着我自己的亲爱英雄，我便回绝了那黑暗之人。”

===Original===
“That was your right, and I swear by my hand that if the man you desire is alive and unmarried he shall marry you or he will answer to me for the refusal.”

===Chinese===
“那是你的权利。凭我的手起誓：你属意的男人若还活着、还未成婚，他就得娶你；不然，他为这次回绝，得向我交代。”

===Original===
“He is not married,” said Saeve, “and you have small control over him.”

===Chinese===
“他没有成婚，”赛芙说，“可您对他，管束不了多少。”

===Original===
The Chief frowned thoughtfully.

===Chinese===
首领蹙眉沉思。

===Original===
“Except the High King and the kings I have authority in this land.”

===Chinese===
“在这片土地上，除了至高王和诸位国王，我都有权柄。”

===Original===
“What man has authority over himself?” said Saeve.

===Chinese===
“哪个男人，又管得住自己呢？”赛芙说。

===Original===
“Do you mean that I am the man you seek?” said Fionn.

===Chinese===
“你是说，你找的男人就是我？”芬恩说。

===Original===
“It is to yourself I gave my love,” she replied.

===Chinese===
“我把爱情给的，正是您自己。”她答道。

===Original===
“This is good news,” Fionn cried joyfully, “for the moment you came through the door I loved and desired you, and the thought that you wished for another man went into my heart like a sword.”

===Chinese===
“这是天大的好消息！”芬恩欢喜地喊道，“你一进门，我便爱了你，恋了你；一想到你属意的是另一个男人，那念头就像一柄剑，插进了我的心。”

===Original===
Indeed, Fionn loved Saeve as he had not loved a woman before and would never love one again. He loved her as he had never loved anything before. He could not bear to be away from her. When he saw her he did not see the world, and when he saw the world without her it was as though he saw nothing, or as if he looked on a prospect that was bleak and depressing. The belling of a stag had been music to Fionn, but when Saeve spoke that was sound enough for him. He had loved to hear the cuckoo calling in the spring from the tree that is highest in the hedge, or the blackbird’s jolly whistle in an autumn bush, or the thin, sweet enchantment that comes to the mind when a lark thrills out of sight in the air and the hushed fields listen to the song. But his wife’s voice was sweeter to Fionn than the singing of a lark. She filled him with wonder and surmise. There was magic in the tips of her fingers. Her thin palm ravished him. Her slender foot set his heart beating; and whatever way her head moved there came a new shape of beauty to her face.

===Chinese===
确实，芬恩爱赛芙，是他从前不曾爱过一个女子那样爱，往后也不会再这样去爱一个。他爱她，胜过他此前爱过的一切。与她分开，他一时一刻也受不得。他望着她，便望不见世界；没有她而望着世界，便仿佛什么也没有见，仿佛望着一片荒寒败落的景象。牡鹿的吼鸣，在芬恩听来原是音乐，可赛芙一开口，那声音于他便尽够了。他原爱听春天里杜鹃在树篱最高枝头的啼叫，爱听秋丛里乌鸫欢快的哨音，爱听云雀直窜上高空、唱到望不见影、静默的田野侧耳听它歌唱时，浮上心头的那一缕又细又甜的迷醉——可他妻子的声音，在芬恩听来比云雀的歌唱还甜。她让他心中盈满惊奇与悬想。她的指尖上有魔法。她薄薄的掌心令他销魂。她纤细的脚，叫他的心怦然跳动；她的头无论怎样一转，脸上便有一种新的美生出来。

===Original===
“She is always new,” said Fionn. “She is always better than any other woman; she is always better than herself.”

===Chinese===
“她永远是新的，”芬恩说，“她永远比任何别的女子都好；她永远比她自己还好。”

===Original===
He attended no more to the Fianna. He ceased to hunt. He did not listen to the songs of poets or the curious sayings of magicians, for all of these were in his wife, and something that was beyond these was in her also.

===Chinese===
勇士团的事，他不再操心了。他不再行猎。诗人的歌、术士的奇言，他也都不听了，因为这一切都在他妻子身上，而且她身上，还有超出这一切的东西。

===Original===
“She is this world and the next one; she is completion,” said Fionn.

===Chinese===
“她就是这个世界，也是下一个世界；她就是圆满。”芬恩说。

### III / III

### VI / VI

===Original===
