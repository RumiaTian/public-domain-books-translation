## Endnotes / 尾注

===Original===
1. Pronounce Fewn to rhyme with “tune.” ↩︎
2. Pronounced Usheen. ↩︎
3. This version of the death of Uail is not correct. Also Cnocha is not in Lochlann but in Ireland. ↩︎
4. The Hill of Howth. ↩︎

===Chinese===
1. 芬恩（Fionn）读作 Fewn，与 tune 押韵。↩︎
2. 奥辛（Oisín）读作 Usheen。↩︎
3. 这一版本所载尤尔（Uail）之死并不确实。另外，克诺卡（Cnocha）并不在洛克兰（Lochlann），而是在爱尔兰。↩︎
4. 即豪斯山（the Hill of Howth）。↩︎
