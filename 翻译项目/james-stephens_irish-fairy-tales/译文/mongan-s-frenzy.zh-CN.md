## Mongan’s Frenzy / 蒙根的狂怒

### I / I

===Original===
The abbot of the Monastery of Moville sent word to the storytellers of Ireland that when they were in his neighbourhood they should call at the monastery, for he wished to collect and write down the stories which were in danger of being forgotten.

===Chinese===
莫维尔修道院（Monastery of Moville）的院长传话给爱尔兰各地的说书人：凡行经修道院左近的，务必进院一叙，因为他想把那些濒临失传的故事搜集起来，笔录成书。

===Original===
“These things also must be told,” said he.

===Chinese===
“这些事也必须有人讲述。”他说。

===Original===
In particular he wished to gather tales which told of the deeds that had been done before the Gospel came to Ireland.

===Chinese===
他尤其想搜集那类故事，讲的是福音传到爱尔兰之前所行的种种事迹。

===Original===
“For,” said he, “there are very good tales among those ones, and it would be a pity if the people who come after us should be ignorant of what happened long ago, and of the deeds of their fathers.”

===Chinese===
“因为，”他说，“那些故事里有极好的篇章；倘若后人竟对远古发生过什么一无所知，对先辈的功业一无所知，那岂不可惜。”

===Original===
So, whenever a storyteller chanced in that neighbourhood he was directed to the monastery, and there he received a welcome and his fill of all that is good for man.

===Chinese===
于是，只要有说书人碰巧行经那一带，人们便给他指点修道院的路。到了那里，他必受款待，凡于人有益的美善之物，尽他受用。

===Original===
The abbot’s manuscript boxes began to fill up, and he used to regard that growing store with pride and joy. In the evenings, when the days grew short and the light went early, he would call for some one of these manuscripts and have it read to him by candlelight, in order that he might satisfy himself that it was as good as he had judged it to be on the previous hearing.

===Chinese===
院长的手稿箱渐渐满了起来，他望着那与日俱增的珍藏，又得意，又欢喜。每到日短天黑的傍晚，他便命人取来其中一卷，就着烛光读给他听，好让自己确信：这故事确如他上一回听到时判定的那样好。

===Original===
One day a storyteller came to the monastery, and, like all the others, he was heartily welcomed and given a great deal more than his need.

===Chinese===
一天，有个说书人来到修道院。他与所有来客一样受到热诚欢迎，得到的款待远超所需。

===Original===
He said that his name was Cairidè, and that he had a story to tell which could not be bettered among the stories of Ireland.

===Chinese===
他说自己名叫凯里德（Cairidè），还说随身带着一个故事，在爱尔兰所有的故事里，再没有比它更好的。

===Original===
The abbot’s eyes glistened when he heard that. He rubbed his hands together and smiled on his guest.

===Chinese===
院长一听，眼睛顿时亮了。他搓着双手，朝客人堆起满脸笑。

===Original===
“What is the name of your story?” he asked.

===Chinese===
“你的故事叫什么名字？”他问。

===Original===
“It is called ‘Mongan’s Frenzy.’ ”

===Chinese===
“它叫作《蒙根的狂怒》。”

===Original===
“I never heard of it before,” cried the abbot joyfully.

===Chinese===
“我从来没有听说过它。”院长欢喜得喊了起来。

===Original===
“I am the only man that knows it,” Cairidè replied.

===Chinese===
“如今世上只有我一个人知道它。”凯里德答道。

===Original===
“But how does that come about?” the abbot inquired.

===Chinese===
“这又是怎么一回事？”院长问。

===Original===
“Because it belongs to my family,” the storyteller answered. “There was a Cairidè of my nation with Mongan when he went into Faery. This Cairidè listened to the story when it was first told. Then he told it to his son, and his son told it to his son, and that son’s great-great-grandson’s son told it to his son’s son, and he told it to my father, and my father told it to me.”

===Chinese===
“因为这故事是我家传的。”说书人答道，“蒙根（Mongan）当年走进仙境（Faery）时，我族中有一位凯里德随行在侧。故事头一回被讲出来的时候，就是这位凯里德听的。后来他讲给他的儿子，他的儿子又讲给儿子，那儿子的玄孙之子又讲给了他儿子的儿子，那人再讲给我的父亲，我父亲讲给了我。”

===Original===
“And you shall tell it to me,” cried the abbot triumphantly.

===Chinese===
“那你就讲给我听吧。”院长得胜似的喊道。

===Original===
“I will indeed,” said Cairidè.

===Chinese===
“我正要讲给您听。”凯里德说。

===Original===
Vellum was then brought and quills. The copyists sat at their tables. Ale was placed beside the storyteller, and he told this tale to the abbot.

===Chinese===
随即取来犊皮纸和羽笔，誊写生各就各位。麦芽酒摆在说书人手边，他便向院长讲起这个故事。

### II / II

===Original===
Said Cairidè:

===Chinese===
凯里德讲道：

===Original===
Mongan’s wife at that time was Brótiarna, the Flame Lady. She was passionate and fierce, and because the blood would flood suddenly to her cheek, so that she who had seemed a lily became, while you looked upon her, a rose, she was called Flame Lady. She loved Mongan with ecstasy and abandon, and for that also he called her Flame Lady.

===Chinese===
那时蒙根的妻子是布罗蒂亚娜（Brótiarna），人称“火焰夫人”（the Flame Lady）。她性子火热，为人泼烈；热血会突然涌上她的脸颊——方才望她还像一朵百合，就在你注视的这一会儿，她已变成一朵玫瑰——所以人们叫她火焰夫人。她爱蒙根爱得如痴如狂、不管不顾；为这个，他也叫她火焰夫人。

===Original===
But there may have been something of calculation even in her wildest moment, for if she was delighted in her affection she was tormented in it also, as are all those who love the great ones of life and strive to equal themselves where equality is not possible.

===Chinese===
可即便在她最狂放的时刻，兴许也藏着几分盘算：她在爱恋中欢喜，也在爱恋中受煎熬，一如世上所有爱上人中龙凤的人——偏要在无法比肩的地方，硬争一个比肩。

===Original===
For her husband was at once more than himself and less than himself. He was less than himself because he was now Mongan. He was more than himself because he was one who had long disappeared from the world of men. His lament had been sung and his funeral games played many, many years before, and Brótiarna sensed in him secrets, experiences, knowledges in which she could have no part, and for which she was greedily envious.

===Chinese===
因为她的丈夫，既多于他自己，又少于他自己。说他少于他自己，是因为他如今是蒙根；说他多于他自己，是因为他是一位早已从人世间消逝的人。为他唱的哀歌早已唱过，为他办的葬礼竞技也早已办过许多许多年；布罗蒂亚娜感觉到他身上藏着秘密、经历和学问，那是她无从有份的东西，为此她嫉妒得心痒难熬。

===Original===
So she was continually asking him little, simple questions apropos of every kind of thing.

===Chinese===
于是她没完没了地就各式各样的事，问他一些小小的、听着天真的问题。

===Original===
She weighed all that he said on whatever subject, and when he talked in his sleep she listened to his dream.

===Chinese===
无论他谈什么题目，她都要掂量他说的每一句话；他睡梦里说话时，她便凑近去听他的梦。

===Original===
The knowledge that she gleaned from those listenings tormented her far more than it satisfied her, for the names of other women were continually on his lips, sometimes in terms of dear affection, sometimes in accents of anger or despair, and in his sleep he spoke familiarly of people whom the storytellers told of, but who had been dead for centuries. Therefore she was perplexed, and became filled with a very rage of curiosity.

===Chinese===
从那些偷听里拾掇来的零碎，与其说满足了她，不如说更煎熬了她：别的女人的名字不断挂在他嘴边，有时是亲昵怜惜的口吻，有时是愤怒或绝望的声调；他睡梦里还亲亲热热地念叨一些人——那些人只活在说书人的故事里，人已死了好几百年了。因此她满心惶惑，渐渐被一种狂怒般的好奇填满了。

===Original===
Among the names which her husband mentioned there was one which, because of the frequency with which it appeared, and because of the tone of anguish and love and longing in which it was uttered, she thought of oftener than the others: this name was Duv Laca. Although she questioned and cross-questioned Cairidè, her storyteller, she could discover nothing about a lady who had been known as the Black Duck. But one night when Mongan seemed to speak with Duv Laca he mentioned her father as Fiachna Duv mac Demain, and the storyteller said that king had been dead for a vast number of years.

===Chinese===
丈夫念叨过的名字里头，有一个她比谁都想得多：它出现得最勤，吐出来时又总带着痛楚、爱恋与渴慕的声气——这个名字就是杜夫·拉卡（Duv Laca）。她一遍又一遍盘问她家的说书人凯里德，却怎么也打听不出那位人称“黑鸭”（Black Duck）的夫人的来历。可有一夜，蒙根像是正同杜夫·拉卡说话，提到了她的父亲菲亚赫纳·杜夫·麦克·德曼（Fiachna Duv mac Demain），说书人却说，那位王已经死去不知多少年了。

===Original===
She asked her husband then, boldly, to tell her the story of Duv Laca, and under the influence of their mutual love he promised to tell it to her some time, but each time she reminded him of his promise he became confused, and said that he would tell it some other time.

===Chinese===
于是她大起胆子，求丈夫给她讲杜夫·拉卡的故事；趁着两人情浓，他答应改日讲给她听。可她每回一提这个约，他就变得讪讪的，说还是另找时候再讲吧。

===Original===
As time went on the poor Flame Lady grew more and more jealous of Duv Laca, and more and more certain that, if only she could know what had happened, she would get some ease to her tormented heart and some assuagement of her perfectly natural curiosity. Therefore she lost no opportunity of reminding Mongan of his promise, and on each occasion he renewed the promise and put it back to another time.

===Chinese===
日子一久，可怜的火焰夫人对杜夫·拉卡的醋意越来越浓，也越来越认定：只要能知道当年到底出了什么事，她那颗受煎熬的心就能得些安宁，她那份再自然不过的好奇也能得些抚慰。于是她逮着一切机会提醒蒙根践约，而他每一回都重申前约，再把它推到下一回。

### III / III

===Original===
In the year when Ciaran the son of the Carpenter died, the same year when Tuathal Maelgariv was killed and the year when Diarmait the son of Cerrbel became king of all Ireland, the year 538 of our era in short, it happened that there was a great gathering of the men of Ireland at the Hill of Uisneach in Royal Meath.

===Chinese===
且说木匠之子基兰（Ciaran）去世的那一年，图阿尔·梅尔加里夫（Tuathal Maelgariv）被杀的同一年，瑟尔贝尔之子迪尔梅德（Diarmait）登基为全爱尔兰之王的那一年——总而言之，公元538年——爱尔兰人在王土米斯（Royal Meath）的乌斯尼奇山（Hill of Uisneach）举行了一场盛大的集会。

===Original===
In addition to the Council which was being held, there were games and tournaments and brilliant deployments of troops, and universal feastings and enjoyments. The gathering lasted for a week, and on the last day of the week Mongan was moving through the crowd with seven guards, his storyteller Cairidè, and his wife.

===Chinese===
会议之外，还有竞技比武，有焕然耀眼的军阵操演，还有普天同庆的宴饮欢愉。集会一连开了七天；到了最后一天，蒙根正带着七名卫士、他的说书人凯里德和他的妻子在人群中走动。

===Original===
It had been a beautiful day, with brilliant sunshine and great sport, but suddenly clouds began to gather in the sky to the west, and others came rushing blackly from the east. When these clouds met the world went dark for a space, and there fell from the sky a shower of hailstones, so large that each man wondered at their size, and so swift and heavy that the women and young people of the host screamed from the pain of the blows they received.

===Chinese===
那本是个艳阳高照、赛事正酣的好天。可忽然间，西边天上聚起了云，又有乌云从东边黑压压地涌来。两路云一相会，天地霎时昏暗了一阵，天上落下好一阵冰雹——雹子大得人人称奇，又急又沉，打得人群里的妇人和后生一个个疼得尖叫起来。

===Original===
Mongan’s men made a roof of their shields, and the hailstones battered on the shields so terribly that even under them they were afraid. They began to move away from the host looking for shelter, and when they had gone apart a little way they turned the edge of a small hill and a knoll of trees, and in the twinkling of an eye they were in fair weather.

===Chinese===
蒙根的随从用盾牌搭起一片顶。冰雹砸在盾上砰砰作响，凶猛骇人，躲在底下的人也心惊胆战。他们开始离开人群寻处躲避，才走出去不多远，刚绕过一座小山的山肩和一丛树冈，眨眼之间，已是朗朗晴日。

===Original===
One minute they heard the clashing and bashing of the hailstones, the howling of the venomous wind, the screams of women and the uproar of the crowd on the Hill of Uisneach, and the next minute they heard nothing more of those sounds and saw nothing more of these sights, for they had been permitted to go at one step out of the world of men and into the world of Faery.

===Chinese===
前一刻，他们还听得见冰雹的劈啪轰击、毒风的呼啸、妇人的尖叫，以及乌斯尼奇山上人群的鼎沸；后一刻，那些声响统统听不见了，那些景象也统统看不见了——因为他们蒙了恩准，一步之间，跨出世人的世界，跨进了仙界的疆土。

### IV / IV

===Original===
There is a difference between this world and the world of Faery, but it is not immediately perceptible. Everything that is here is there, but the things that are there are better than those that are here. All things that are bright are there brighter. There is more gold in the sun and more silver in the moon of that land. There is more scent in the flowers, more savour in the fruit. There is more comeliness in the men and more tenderness in the women. Everything in Faery is better by this one wonderful degree, and it is by this betterness you will know that you are there if you should ever happen to get there.

===Chinese===
这个世界与仙境的世界是有分别的，只是那分别不能一眼看破。这边有的东西，那边样样都有；只是那边的东西，样样都比这边的更好。这边明亮的，那边更加明亮。那片土地上的太阳含金更多，月亮含银更多。花更香，果更甜。男人更俊雅，女人更温存。仙境里的万事万物，都恰好奇妙地胜出一筹；你若有朝一日当真到了那里，凭这一筹之胜，你便知道自己到了何处。

===Original===
Mongan and his companions stepped from the world of storm into sunshine and a scented world. The instant they stepped they stood, bewildered, looking at each other silently, questioningly, and then with one accord they turned to look back whence they had come.

===Chinese===
蒙根一行从风雨的世界一步踏进阳光里，踏进一个芬芳的世界。脚跟才落地，他们便怔怔站住，默默相顾，满眼疑问；随即不约而同地回过头去，望向来路。

===Original===
There was no storm behind them. The sunlight drowsed there as it did in front, a peaceful flooding of living gold. They saw the shapes of the country to which their eyes were accustomed, and recognised the well-known landmarks, but it seemed that the distant hills were a trifle higher, and the grass which clothed them and stretched between was greener, was more velvety: that the trees were better clothed and had more of peace as they hung over the quiet ground.

===Chinese===
身后并没有风雨。那边的阳光同前方一样，慵懒地铺展着，是一片安详的、活金般的流光。他们望见自己看惯了的山川形胜，认出了熟稔的地标；只是远山仿佛略高了些，覆在山上、铺在山间的青草更绿了，更如天鹅绒了；树木也披挂得更丰美，静静垂在安宁的土地上，透出更多的静谧。

===Original===
But Mongan knew what had happened, and he smiled with glee as he watched his astonished companions, and he sniffed that balmy air as one whose nostrils remembered it.

===Chinese===
蒙根却知道出了什么事。他看着一脸惊愕的同伴们，咧嘴直乐，又深深嗅着那温软的空气，像一个鼻孔还记得它的人。

===Original===
“You had better come with me,” he said.

===Chinese===
“你们最好跟我来。”他说。

===Original===
“Where are we?” his wife asked.

===Chinese===
“我们这是在哪儿？”妻子问。

===Original===
“Why, we are here,” cried Mongan; “where else should we be?”

===Chinese===
“嗨，我们在这儿呀，”蒙根喊道，“还能在哪儿？”

===Original===
He set off then, and the others followed, staring about them cautiously, and each man keeping a hand on the hilt of his sword.

===Chinese===
于是他动身前行，众人跟着走，小心翼翼地四下张望，每人都有一只手扣在剑柄上。

===Original===
“Are we in Faery?” the Flame Lady asked.

===Chinese===
“我们是在仙境吗？”火焰夫人问。

===Original===
“We are,” said Mongan.

===Chinese===
“正是。”蒙根说。

===Original===
When they had gone a little distance they came to a grove of ancient trees. Mightily tall and well grown these trees were, and the trunk of each could not have been spanned by ten broad men. As they went among these quiet giants into the dappled obscurity and silence, their thoughts became grave, and all the motions of their minds elevated as though they must equal in greatness and dignity those ancient and glorious trees. When they passed through the grove they saw a lovely house before them, built of mellow wood and with a roof of bronze﻿—it was like the dwelling of a king, and over the windows of the Sunny Room there was a balcony. There were ladies on this balcony, and when they saw the travellers approaching they sent messengers to welcome them.

===Chinese===
走了一程，他们来到一片古树的林子。这些树生得高大雄伟，每一株的树干，十个彪形大汉手拉手也围不拢。穿行在这些沉静的巨人之间，走进斑驳的幽暗与寂静里，众人的心思庄重起来，胸中意气也随之高远，仿佛非要与这些古老光荣的树木比一比伟岸与尊严。穿过树林，眼前现出一座可爱的宅子，以温润的木料建成，覆着青铜的屋顶——那像是一位王的居所；向阳厅（the Sunny Room）的窗子上方有一道露台。露台上立着几位女子，望见来客渐近，便遣了使者前去相迎。

===Original===
Mongan and his companions were then brought into the house, and all was done for them that could be done for honoured guests. Everything within the house was as excellent as all without, and it was inhabited by seven men and seven women, and it was evident that Mongan and these people were well acquainted.

===Chinese===
蒙根一行随即被请进屋中，贵客该受的礼遇，一样不缺地都给了他们。屋里屋外一般精美；宅中住着七男七女，而蒙根与这些人分明是旧相识。

===Original===
In the evening a feast was prepared, and when they had eaten well there was a banquet. There were seven vats of wine, and as Mongan loved wine he was very happy, and he drank more on that occasion than anyone had ever noticed him to drink before.

===Chinese===
入夜备下了盛宴，酒饭用罢，又开华筵。席上有七大桶酒。蒙根素来嗜酒，快活非常，那一晚喝下肚的酒，比任何人以往见过的都多。

===Original===
It was while he was in this condition of glee and expansion that the Flame Lady put her arms about his neck and begged he would tell her the story of Duv Laca, and, being boisterous then and full of good spirits, he agreed to her request, and he prepared to tell the tale.

===Chinese===
正当他酒酣兴发、心花怒放的当口，火焰夫人搂住他的脖颈，恳求他讲一讲杜夫·拉卡的故事。他那时正豪兴滔滔，满心畅快，一口应允，便准备开讲。

===Original===
The seven men and seven women of the Fairy Palace then took their places about him in a half-circle; his own seven guards sat behind them; his wife, the Flame Lady, sat by his side; and at the back of all Cairidè his storyteller sat, listening with all his ears, and remembering every word that was uttered.

===Chinese===
仙宫的七男七女便围着他坐成半圈；自家的七名卫士坐在他们身后；妻子火焰夫人坐在他身旁；最后头坐着他的说书人凯里德——他竖起两只耳朵听着，说出的每一个字都记在心里。

### V / V

===Original===
Said Mongan:

===Chinese===
蒙根讲道：

===Original===
In the days of long ago and the times that have disappeared forever, there was one Fiachna Finn the son of Baltan, the son of Murchertach, the son of Muredach, the son of Eogan, the son of Neill. He went from his own country when he was young, for he wished to see the land of Lochlann, and he knew that he would be welcomed by the king of that country, for Fiachna’s father and Eolgarg’s father had done deeds in common and were obliged to each other.

===Chinese===
在那久远的岁月、早已永远消逝的年代里，有一位菲亚赫纳·芬（Fiachna Finn），他是巴尔坦（Baltan）之子，穆尔切塔赫（Murchertach）之孙，穆雷达克（Muredach）、埃奥甘（Eogan）、尼尔（Neill）一脉相承的后人。他年轻时离开故土，想去看看洛克兰（Lochlann）之地；他知道那国的王必定欢迎他，因为菲亚赫纳的父亲与埃奥尔加格的父亲曾共过事，彼此欠着情分。

===Original===
