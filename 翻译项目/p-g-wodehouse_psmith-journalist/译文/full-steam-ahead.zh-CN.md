## IX Full Steam Ahead / 全速前进
===Original===
When Psmith returned to the office, he found Billy Windsor in the doorway, just parting from a thickset young man, who seemed to be expressing his gratitude to the editor for some good turn. He was shaking him warmly by the hand.

Psmith stood aside to let him pass.

“An old college chum, Comrade Windsor?” he asked.

“That was Kid Brady.”

“The name is unfamiliar to me. Another contributor?”

“He’s from my part of the country—Wyoming. He wants to fight anyone in the world at a hundred and thirty-three pounds.”

===Chinese===
普史密斯回到报社时，看见比利·温莎正站在门口，与一个敦实的青年道别——那人似乎在为某桩好事向主编表达感激，正热情地握着他的手。

普史密斯侧身让他过去。

「大学时代的老哥们儿，温莎同志？」他问。

「那是小子布雷迪。」

「这名字我不熟。又一位撰稿人？」

「他跟我同乡——怀俄明来的。他想在一百三十三磅量级上跟全世界随便谁干一架。」

===Original===
“We all have our hobbies. Comrade Brady appears to have selected a somewhat exciting one. He would find stamp collecting less exacting.”

“It hasn’t given him much excitement so far, poor chap,” said Billy Windsor. “He’s in the championship class, and here he has been pottering about New York for a month without being able to get a fight. It’s always the way in this rotten East,” continued Billy, warming up as was his custom when discussing a case of oppression and injustice. “It’s all graft here. You’ve got to let half a dozen brutes dip into every dollar you earn, or you don’t get a chance. If the kid had a manager, he’d get all the fights he wanted. And the manager would get nearly all the money. I’ve told him that we will back him up.”

“You have hit it, Comrade Windsor,” said Psmith with enthusiasm. “*Cosy Moments* shall be Comrade Brady’s manager. We will give him a much-needed boost up in our columns. A sporting section is what the paper requires more than anything.”

===Chinese===
「人各有好。布雷迪同志选的这一样似乎略嫌刺激。集邮会让他觉得省力些。」

「到眼下为止还没什么刺激可说呢，可怜的家伙。」比利·温莎说。「他是有冠军级身手的，可他却在纽约瞎转悠了一个月，连一场比赛都捞不着。这个烂透的东部向来就是这副德行。」比利接着说，一讨论起压迫和不公的案例，他照例火气就往上冒。「这儿到处是敲竹杠。你每挣一块钱，都得让六七个狠角色伸手抽头，不然你连机会都没有。这小子要是有个经纪人，想要多少场比赛就有多少场。而钱也几乎全进了经纪人的腰包。我跟他说了，我们给他撑腰。」

「你算说对了，温莎同志。」普史密斯热情地说。「《舒适时光》就来当布雷迪同志的经纪人。我们要在版面上给他来一场久旱逢甘霖的力捧。这份报纸最缺的，就是体育版。」

===Original===
“If things go on as they’ve started, what it will require still more will be a fighting editor. Pugsy tells me you had visitors while I was out.”

“A few,” said Psmith. “One or two very entertaining fellows. Comrades Asher, Philpotts, and others. I have just been giving them a bite of lunch at the Knickerbocker.”

“Lunch!”

“A most pleasant little lunch. We are now as brothers. I fear I have made you perhaps a shade unpopular with our late contributors; but these things must be. We must clench our teeth and face them manfully. If I were you, I think I should not drop in at the house of Comrade Asher and the rest to take potluck for some little time to come. In order to soothe the squad I was compelled to curse you to some extent.”

“Don’t mind me.”

“I think I may say I didn’t.”

===Chinese===
「照眼下这个开局闹下去，它更缺的还会是一位战斗编辑。普格西告诉我，我出去那会儿来了几位访客。」

「来了几位。」普史密斯说。「一两位非常有意思的人物。阿舍同志、菲尔波茨同志，还有其他几位。我刚在尼克博克饭店请他们吃了顿便饭。」

「便饭！」

「一顿极其愉快的小宴。我们现在亲如兄弟了。恐怕我让诸位前撰稿人对你生了几分嫌恶；但事已至此，别无他法。我们必须咬紧牙关，拿出男子汉气概去面对。我要是你，往后一小段日子里，就不会顺道去阿舍同志和其他诸位府上赶饭吃。为了安抚那队人马，我不得不在一定程度上把你咒骂了一番。」

「不用顾着我。」

「我想我可以这么说：我确实没顾着。」

===Original===
“Say, look here, you must charge up the price of that lunch to the office. Necessary expenses, you know.”

“I could not dream of doing such a thing, Comrade Windsor. The whole affair was a great treat to me. I have few pleasures. Comrade Asher alone was worth the money. I found his society intensely interesting. I have always believed in the Darwinian theory. Comrade Asher confirmed my views.”

===Chinese===
「我说，听我说，那顿饭的钱你必须记到报社账上。必要开销嘛，你懂的。」

「这种事我想都不会想，温莎同志。整件事于我是莫大的享受。我的乐趣本就不多。光是阿舍同志就值回票价。我发现与他为伴妙趣无穷。我一向信奉达尔文学说。阿舍同志印证了我的观点。」

===Original===
They went into the inner office. Psmith removed his hat and coat.

“And now once more to work,” he said. “Psmith the *flaneur* of Fifth Avenue ceases to exist. In his place we find Psmith the hardheaded subeditor. Be so good as to indicate a job of work for me, Comrade Windsor. I am champing at my bit.”

Billy Windsor sat down, and lit his pipe.

“What we want most,” he said thoughtfully, “is some big topic. That’s the only way to get a paper going. Look at *Everybody’s Magazine*. They didn’t amount to a row of beans till Lawson started his ‘Frenzied Finance’ articles. Directly they began, the whole country was squealing for copies. *Everybody’s* put up their price from ten to fifteen cents, and now they lead the field.”

===Chinese===
两人走进里间办公室。普史密斯摘下帽子，脱了外衣。

「好了，重新开工。」他说。「第五大道的闲逛者（flaneur）普史密斯就此退场。顶替他上场的，是头脑冷静的副主编普史密斯。劳驾给我指派一件活儿吧，温莎同志。我嚼着嚼子，早已按捺不住。」

比利·温莎坐下，点了烟斗。

「我们现在最需要的，」他若有所思地说，「是某个大题目。这是把一张报纸办活的唯一法门。瞧瞧《人人杂志》（Everybody’s Magazine）。在劳森（Lawson）开始写他的《疯狂金融》（Frenzied Finance）系列文章之前，他们不值一排豆子。文章一开张，全国都嗷嗷叫着抢购。《人人》把定价从十分涨到十五分，如今领跑整个行业。」

===Original===
“The country must squeal for *Cosy Moments*,” said Psmith firmly. “I fancy I have a scheme which may not prove wholly scaly. Wandering yesterday with Comrade Jackson in a search for Fourth Avenue, I happened upon a spot called Pleasant Street. Do you know it?”

Billy Windsor nodded.

“I went down there once or twice when I was a reporter. It’s a beastly place.”

“It is a singularly beastly place. We went into one of the houses.”

===Chinese===
「全国也必须为《舒适时光》嗷嗷叫。」普史密斯语气坚定。「我自忖有一个方案，未必全然寒碜。昨天我和杰克逊同志寻摸第四大道时，误打误撞撞见一个叫普莱森特街的地方。你知道那儿吗？」

比利·温莎点了点头。

「我当记者那会儿下去过一两回。那是个糟糕透顶的地方。」

「那是个格外糟糕透顶的地方。我们进了其中一栋楼。」

===Original===
“They’re pretty bad.”

“Who owns them?”

“I don’t know. Probably some millionaire. Those tenement houses are about as paying an investment as you can have.”

“Hasn’t anybody ever tried to do anything about them?”

“Not so far as I know. It’s pretty difficult to get at these fellows, you see. But they’re fierce, aren’t they, those houses!”

===Chinese===
「那些楼都够呛。」

「产业归谁？」

「不知道。多半是哪位百万富翁。那些廉租公寓楼，算得上数一数二赚钱的投资。」

「就从来没有人想法子治治它们？」

「据我所知没有。你要明白，很难揪住这些家伙的把柄。不过那些楼也真够吓人的，是不是！」

===Original===
“What,” asked Psmith, “is the precise difficulty of getting at these merchants?”

“Well, it’s this way. There are all sorts of laws about the places, but anyone who wants can get round them as easy as falling off a log. The law says a tenement house is a building occupied by more than two families. Well, when there’s a fuss, all the man has to do is to clear out all the families but two. Then, when the inspector fellow comes along, and says, let’s say, ‘Where’s your running water on each floor? That’s what the law says you’ve got to have, and here are these people having to go downstairs and out of doors to fetch their water supplies,’ the landlord simply replies, ‘Nothing doing. This isn’t a tenement house at all. There are only two families here.’ And when the fuss has blown over, back come the rest of the crowd, and things go on the same as before.”

===Chinese===
「那么，」普史密斯问，「揪住这帮买卖人的确切难处何在？」

「嗐，是这么回事。关于这些地方的法规多得很，可谁想绕，都易如反掌。法律规定，住两家以上人家的房子才算廉租公寓。于是风声一紧，房主只消把两家之外的人家统统清走。等督察员来了，说——比如这么说吧——『你们每层的自来水呢？法律明文规定必须有，可这些人却得下楼、出屋去打水』，房东只消回一句：『没的事。这压根不是廉租公寓。这儿就住了两家人。』等风头一过，其余人马杀回来，一切照旧。」

===Original===
“I see,” said Psmith. “A very cheery scheme.”

“Then there’s another thing. You can’t get hold of the man who’s really responsible, unless you’re prepared to spend thousands ferreting out evidence. The land belongs in the first place to some corporation or other. They lease it to a lessee. When there’s a fuss, they say they aren’t responsible, it’s up to the lessee. And he lies so low that you can’t find out who he is. It’s all just like the East. Everything in the East is as crooked as Pearl Street. If you want a square deal, you’ve got to come out Wyoming way.”

===Chinese===
「我明白了。」普史密斯说。「一套非常欢快的机制。」

「还有一桩。你抓不到真正担责任的人，除非你舍得花几千块钱去挖证据。地皮首先归某个什么公司所有。他们把地转租给一个二房东（lessee）。风声一紧，他们就说自己概不负责，有事找二房东。而那位藏得深的，你连他是谁都查不出来。这整套就跟东部一个样。东部的一切都像珍珠街（Pearl Street）一样七拐八绕。想要公道，你就得奔怀俄明那边去。」

===Original===
“The main problem, then,” said Psmith, “appears to be the discovery of the lessee, lad? Surely a powerful organ like *Cosy Moments*, with its vast ramifications, could bring off a thing like that?”

“I doubt it. We’ll try, anyway. There’s no knowing but what we may have luck.”

“Precisely,” said Psmith. “Full steam ahead, and trust to luck. The chances are that, if we go on long enough, we shall eventually arrive somewhere. After all, Columbus didn’t know that America existed when he set out. All he knew was some highly interesting fact about an egg. What that was, I do not at the moment recall, but it bucked Columbus up like a tonic. It made him fizz ahead like a two-year-old. The facts which will nerve us to effort are two. In the first place, we know that there must be someone at the bottom of the business. Secondly, as there appears to be no law of libel whatsoever in this great and free country, we shall be enabled to haul up our slacks with a considerable absence of restraint.”

===Chinese===
「那么，」普史密斯说，「主要问题看来就是查明那位二房东了，老弟？以《舒适时光》这样一台势力雄厚、爪牙四布的机关，总不至于办不成这点小事吧？」

「我看悬。不过总要试试。没准儿咱们运气好呢。」

「正是。」普史密斯说。「全速前进，听天由命。只要走得够久，我们多半终能抵达什么地方。毕竟，哥伦布启航时也不知道美洲的存在。他知道的只是关于一只鸡蛋的某个极有趣的事实。那到底是什么，我眼下回忆不起来了，但它像一剂强心针，给哥伦布提了气，让他像匹两岁小马似的喷着响鼻一路飞奔。为我们鼓劲的事实有两条。其一，我们知道这桩生意背后必定有个主使。其二，这个伟大而自由的国家看来压根没有诽谤法，我们便可以毫无顾忌、甩开膀子大干一场。」

===Original===
“Sure,” said Billy Windsor. “Which of us is going to write the first article?”

“You may leave it to me, Comrade Windsor. I am no hardened old journalist, I fear, but I have certain qualifications for the post. A young man once called at the office of a certain newspaper, and asked for a job. ‘Have you any special line?’ asked the editor. ‘Yes,’ said the bright lad, ‘I am rather good at invective.’ ‘Any special kind of invective?’ queried the man up top. ‘No,’ replied our hero, ‘just general invective.’ Such is my own case, Comrade Windsor. I am a very fair purveyor of good, general invective. And as my visit to Pleasant Street is of such recent date, I am tolerably full of my subject. Taking full advantage of the benevolent laws of this country governing libel, I fancy I will produce a screed which will make this anonymous lessee feel as if he had inadvertently seated himself upon a tin-tack. Give me pen and paper, Comrade Windsor, instruct Comrade Maloney to suspend his whistling till such time as I am better able to listen to it; and I think we have got a success.”

===Chinese===
「那是。」比利·温莎说。「头一篇文章由咱们哪个来写？」

「这就交给我吧，温莎同志。我算不上久经沙场的老报人，但对这个职位，我自有几分资质。从前有个年轻人到某家报社登门求职。『你有什么专长吗？』主编问。『有，』那个机灵的小伙子说，『我相当擅长骂人。』『骂人有什么专门的门类吗？』上头那位追问。『没有，』咱们的主人公答道，『就是泛泛地骂人。』我的情况正是如此，温莎同志。我是个相当不错的供货商，供应上好的、泛用的骂人文章。加之我造访普莱森特街尚在新鲜头上，肚子里这个题目装得满满的。趁势把这国关于诽谤的仁慈法律用到十足，我想我能写出一篇雄文，让那位隐名二房东感觉像一屁股坐在了图钉上。给我纸笔，温莎同志；吩咐马洛尼同志暂停吹口哨，等我腾出耳朵好生欣赏；然后我想，咱们就大功告成了。」
