## II Billy Windsor / 比利·温莎
===Original===
Billy Windsor had started life twenty-five years before this story opens on his father’s ranch in Wyoming. From there he had gone to a local paper of the type whose Society column consists of such items as “Pawnee Jim Williams was to town yesterday with a bunch of other cheapskates. We take this opportunity of once more informing Jim that he is a liar and a skunk,” and whose editor works with a revolver on his desk and another in his hip-pocket. Graduating from this, he had proceeded to a reporter’s post on a daily paper in a Kentucky town, where there were blood feuds and other Southern devices for preventing life from becoming dull. All this time New York, the magnet, had been tugging at him. All reporters dream of reaching New York. At last, after four years on the Kentucky paper, he had come East, minus the lobe of one ear and plus a long scar that ran diagonally across his left shoulder, and had worked without much success as a freelance. He was tough and ready for anything that might come his way, but these things are a great deal a matter of luck. The cub reporter cannot make a name for himself unless he is favoured by fortune. Things had not come Billy Windsor’s way. His work had been confined to turning in reports of fires and small street accidents, which the various papers to which he supplied them cut down to a couple of inches.

Billy had been in a bad way when he had happened upon the subeditorship of *Cosy Moments*. He despised the work with all his heart, and the salary was infinitesimal. But it was regular, and for a while Billy felt that a regular salary was the greatest thing on earth. But he still dreamed of winning through to a post on one of the big New York dailies, where there was something doing and a man would have a chance of showing what was in him.

The unfortunate thing, however, was that *Cosy Moments* took up his time so completely. He had no chance of attracting the notice of big editors by his present work, and he had no leisure for doing any other.

All of which may go to explain why his normal aspect was that of a caged eagle.

===Chinese===
故事开场之前二十五年，比利·温莎生在他父亲的怀俄明牧场。从那里他进了一家本地报纸——那类报纸的社会新闻栏里登的都是诸如「波尼·吉姆·威廉姆斯昨日与一帮小气鬼进城。我们借此机会再次通知吉姆：他是个骗子加臭虫」的货色，编辑办公时桌上摆一支左轮手枪，屁股兜里还揣着一支。从这家报纸毕业之后，他又到肯塔基一座城镇的日报谋了个记者职位——那地方血仇械斗成风，还有各式南方特色的消遣，日子绝不至于无聊。这些年里，纽约像磁石一般一直牵着他。天下的记者都做着进纽约的梦。终于在肯塔基那张报纸干满四年之后，他东来了——少了一只耳朵的耳垂，左肩上斜添了一道长长的疤——靠自由撰稿糊口，却没什么起色。他筋骨结实，来什么接什么，可这种事多半要看运气。初出茅庐的记者若不受命运眷顾，就休想闯出名号。好运一直没有落到比利·温莎头上。他干的活计不外乎报道火灾和小型街头事故，稿子交给各家报纸后，都被删得只剩两三英寸。

比利碰上《舒适时光》（*Cosy Moments*）助理编辑这个位子时，正处在穷途潦倒的境地。他从心眼里瞧不起这份活计，薪水也少得可怜。但好歹是固定的，有一阵子比利觉得，一份固定薪水真是天底下顶了不起的东西。不过他仍旧梦想着打入纽约某家大日报——那里才有正经干头，一个人也才有机会显显自己肚里的本事。

可惜的是，《舒适时光》把他的时间占得满满当当。靠眼下的工作他引不起大编辑们的注意，却又挤不出工夫去干别的。

这些原委，或许足以说明他平日的神情何以总像一头关在笼里的鹰。

===Original===
Billy Windsor, like most men of the plains, combined the toughest of muscle with the softest of hearts. He was always ready at any moment to become the champion of the oppressed on the slightest provocation. His alliance with Pugsy Maloney had begun on the occasion when he had rescued that youth from the clutches of a large negro, who, probably from the soundest of motives, was endeavouring to slay him. Billy had not inquired into the rights and wrongs of the matter: he had merely sailed in and rescued the office-boy. And Pugsy, though he had made no verbal comment on the affair, had shown in many ways that he was not ungrateful.

“Bully for you, Pugsy!” he cried. “You’re a little sport. Here”—he produced a dollar-bill—“go out and get some milk for the poor brute. She’s probably starving. Keep the change.”

“Sure thing,” assented Master Maloney. He strolled slowly out, while Billy Windsor, mounting a chair, proceeded to chirrup and snap his fingers in the effort to establish the foundations of an *entente cordiale* with the rescued cat.

By the time that Pugsy returned, carrying a five-cent bottle of milk, the animal had vacated the bookshelf, and was sitting on the table, washing her face. The milk having been poured into the lid of a tobacco tin, in lieu of a saucer, she suspended her operations and adjourned for refreshments. Billy, business being business, turned again to Luella Granville Waterman, but Pugsy, having no immediate duties on hand, concentrated himself on the cat.

===Chinese===
比利·温莎和平原上长大的人大多一样，筋骨最硬，心肠最软。哪怕只有一点由头，他随时都肯挺身充当受压迫者的保护人。他与普格西·马洛尼的交情，始于他把那少年从一个高大黑人的魔爪下救出来的那次——那位黑人多半出于最纯正的动机，正努力要他的命。比利没有追究事情的是非曲直：他只是一头冲进去，把听差救了出来。普格西呢，虽然嘴上对此事一字不提，却处处表现出自己并非忘恩负义之辈。

「好样的，普格西！」他叫道。「你真是个小好汉。给」——他掏出一张一元钞票——「出去给这可怜的小东西买点儿牛奶。它多半饿坏了。找头你留着。」

「没错儿。」马洛尼少爷应道。他慢悠悠地踱了出去，比利·温莎则踩上一把椅子，啧啧唤猫、打着响指，努力与这只获救的猫打下「真诚友好关系」（*entente cordiale*）的基础。

等普格西拎着一瓶五分钱的牛奶回来时，那猫已经离开书柜，正蹲在桌上洗脸。牛奶倒进一只烟盒盖里权当碟子，它便中止了梳洗，移驾进餐。比利呢，公事终归是公事，又回头对付卢埃拉·格兰维尔·沃特曼去了；普格西手头没有急差，便一门心思看住了猫。
