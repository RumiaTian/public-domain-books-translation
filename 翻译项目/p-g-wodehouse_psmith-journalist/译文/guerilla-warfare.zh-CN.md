## XVII Guerilla Warfare / 游击战
===Original===
Thus ended the opening engagement of the campaign, seemingly in a victory for the *Cosy Moments* army. Billy Windsor, however, shook his head.

“We’ve got mighty little out of it,” he said.

“The victory,” said Psmith, “was not bloodless. Comrade Brady’s ear, my hat—these are not slight casualties. On the other hand, surely we are one up? Surely we have gained ground? The elimination of Comrade Repetto from the scheme of things in itself is something. I know few men I would not rather meet in a lonely road than Comrade Repetto. He is one of Nature’s sandbaggers. Probably the thing crept upon him slowly. He started, possibly, in a merely tentative way by slugging one of the family circle. His nurse, let us say, or his young brother. But, once started, he is unable to resist the craving. The thing grips him like dram-drinking. He sandbags now not because he really wants to, but because he cannot help himself. To me there is something consoling in the thought that Comrade Repetto will no longer be among those present.”

===Chinese===
战役的第一场交锋就此收场，表面上看是《舒适时光》大军的胜利。然而比利·温莎摇了摇头。

「咱们从中捞到的好处少得可怜。」他说。

「这场胜利，」普史密斯说，「并非不流血。布雷迪同志的耳朵，我的帽子——这些可不是轻微的伤亡。不过话说回来，咱们总归是领先一分了吧？总归是推进了一步吧？雷佩托同志从万物格局中被除名这件事，本身就算一项收获。比起雷佩托同志，我认识的人里没几个是我更不愿在僻静小路上遇见的。他是造化亲手打造的沙袋客。这毛病多半是慢慢缠上他的。起初，或许只是试探性地敲自家圈子里某一位的闷棍。比方说他的奶妈，或者他的小弟弟。可一旦开了头，他就再也抗拒不了这种瘾。这毛病像贪杯烈酒一样攫住他。他如今抡沙袋，不是因为真心想抡，而是身不由己。想到雷佩托同志从此不会再出现在场面上，我心底便涌起一丝安慰。」

===Original===
“What makes you think that?”

“I should imagine that a benevolent Law will put him away in his little cell for at least a brief spell.”

“Not on your life,” said Billy. “He’ll prove an alibi.”

Psmith’s eyeglass dropped out of his eye. He replaced it, and gazed, astonished, at Billy.

“An alibi? When three keen-eyed men actually caught him at it?”

“He can find thirty toughs to swear he was five miles away.”

===Chinese===
「你凭什么这么想？」

「我料想，仁慈的法律会把他请进那间小号房里，至少小住一阵。」

「想都别想。」比利说。「他会拿出不在场证明。」

普史密斯的单片眼镜从眼眶里掉了下来。他把它装回去，惊愕地瞪着比利。

「不在场证明？三个眼尖的人可是当场逮住他干的？」

「他能找来三十个打手，赌咒发誓说他当时在五英里外。」

===Original===
“And get the court to believe it?” said Psmith.

“Sure,” said Billy disgustedly. “You don’t catch them hurting a gangsman unless they’re pushed against the wall. The politicians don’t want the gangs in gaol, especially as the Aldermanic elections will be on in a few weeks. Did you ever hear of Monk Eastman?”

“I fancy not, Comrade Windsor. If I did, the name has escaped me. Who was this cleric?”

“He was the first boss of the East Side gang, before Kid Twist took it on.”

“Yes?”

===Chinese===
「还能让法庭信以为真？」普史密斯说。

「当然能。」比利厌恶地说。「不逼到墙角，你别想见他们动黑帮分子一根毫毛。政客们不想让黑帮蹲大牢，何况再过几个星期就要进行市议员选举了。你听说过蒙克·伊斯曼（Monk Eastman）吗？」

「我想没有，温莎同志。就算听过，这名字也从我记忆里溜走了。这位教士是何许人？」

「他是下城东区帮的头一任龙头，后来帮派归了小子特威斯特（Kid Twist）。」

「哦？」

===Original===
“He was arrested dozens of times, but he always got off. Do you know what he said once, when they pulled him for thugging a fellow out in New Jersey?”

“I fear not, Comrade Windsor. Tell me all.”

“He said, ‘You’re arresting me, huh? Say, you want to look where you’re goin’; I cut some ice in this town. I made half the big politicians in New York!’ That was what he said.”

“His small talk,” said Psmith, “seems to have been bright and well-expressed. What happened then? Was he restored to his friends and his relations?”

===Chinese===
「他被抓过几十回，可回回都脱了身。你知道有一回他在新泽西地界上行凶揍了人、被警方逮住时，说过什么吗？」

「恐怕不知道，温莎同志。细细讲来。」

「他说：『你们要抓我，是吧？我说，你们走路得看看脚下；我在这个城里是有斤两的。纽约一半的大政客都是我造出来的！』他就是这么说的。」

「他的寒暄，」普史密斯说，「看来既风趣又得体。后来呢？他被送还亲友身边了吗？」

===Original===
“Sure, he was. What do you think? Well, Jack Repetto isn’t Monk Eastman, but he’s in with Spider Reilly, and the Spider’s in with the men behind. Jack’ll get off.”

“It looks to me, Comrade Windsor,” said Psmith thoughtfully, “as if my stay in this great city were going to cost me a small fortune in hats.”

Billy’s prophecy proved absolutely correct. The police were as good as their word. In due season they rounded up the impulsive Mr. Repetto, and he was haled before a magistrate. And then, what a beautiful exhibition of brotherly love and auld-lang-syne camaraderie was witnessed! One by one, smirking sheepishly, but giving out their evidence with unshaken earnestness, eleven greasy, wandering-eyed youths mounted the witness stand and affirmed on oath that at the time mentioned dear old Jack had been making merry in their company in a genial and law-abiding fashion, many, many blocks below the scene of the regrettable assault. The magistrate discharged the prisoner, and the prisoner, meeting Billy and Psmith in the street outside, leered triumphantly at them.

===Chinese===
「当然是。不然还能怎样？嗐，杰克·雷佩托不是蒙克·伊斯曼，但他跟蜘蛛莱利是一伙的，蜘蛛又跟幕后那班人是一伙的。杰克会脱身的。」

「依我看，温莎同志，」普史密斯若有所思地说，「我在这座伟大城市的居留，看来要让我在帽子上破一小笔财了。」

比利的预言分毫不差。警方说到做到。时机一到，他们便将那位冲动的雷佩托先生缉拿归案，押到了治安法官面前。接下来，人们目睹了一场何等美好的表演——手足之爱，加上「友谊地久天长」式的袍泽情谊！十一个油头垢面、贼眼溜溜的青年挨个儿登上证人席，讪讪地笑着，却以毫不动摇的诚恳态度宣誓作证：在所述时刻，亲爱的老杰克正在他们的陪伴下，以和蔼守法的方式寻欢作乐，地点距那起令人遗憾的行凶现场隔了不知多少个街区。治安法官当庭开释了被告，而这位被告在街面上撞见比利和普史密斯时，得意扬扬地朝他们狞笑。

===Original===
Billy stepped up to him. “You may have wriggled out of this,” he said furiously, “but if you don’t get a move on and quit looking at me like that, I’ll knock you over the Singer Building. Hump yourself.”

Mr. Repetto humped himself.

So was victory turned into defeat, and Billy’s jaw became squarer and his eye more full of the light of battle than ever. And there was need of a square jaw and a battle-lit eye, for now began a period of guerilla warfare such as no New York paper had ever had to fight against.

===Chinese===
比利逼上前去。「这回算你滑脱了，」他怒气冲冲地说，「可你要是不赶紧挪窝、还敢这么瞪着我，我就把你打飞过辛格大厦（Singer Building）。滚。」

雷佩托先生赶紧滚了。

胜利就这样变成了失败，而比利的下颌比以往更见方正，眼里也比以往更燃着战斗的光。这副方正的下颌和燃着战火的眼睛确乎派得上用场，因为一段游击战时期就此开场——纽约还从没有哪家报纸不得不与这样的阵仗搏斗过。

===Original===
It was Wheeler, the gaunt manager of the business side of the journal, who first brought it to the notice of the editorial staff. Wheeler was a man for whom in business hours nothing existed but his job; and his job was to look after the distribution of the paper. As to the contents of the paper he was absolutely ignorant. He had been with *Cosy Moments* from its start, but he had never read a line of it. He handled it as if it were so much soap. The scholarly writings of Mr. Wilberfloss, the mirth-provoking sallies of Mr. B. Henderson Asher, the tender outpourings of Luella Granville Waterman—all these were things outside his ken. He was a distributor, and he distributed.

A few days after the restoration of Mr. Repetto to East Side Society, Mr. Wheeler came into the editorial room with information and desire for information.

He endeavoured to satisfy the latter first.

===Chinese===
头一个把这事端到编辑部面前的，是惠勒（Wheeler），本报经营部门的瘦高经理。惠勒这个人，在上班时间里除了本职便目中无他；而他的本职，就是料理报纸的发行。至于报纸的内容，他一窍不通。他从《舒适时光》创刊便效力至今，却从没读过它一行字。他经手它，就像经手一批肥皂。威尔伯弗洛斯先生的学术文章，B·亨德森·阿舍先生逗人发笑的妙语，卢埃拉·格兰维尔·沃特曼的绵绵倾诉——这一切都在他的见识之外。他是发行人，他发行。

雷佩托先生荣归下城东区社交界几天之后，惠勒先生走进编辑室，带来一条消息，也想打听一桩事。

他先设法满足后者。

===Original===
“What’s doing, anyway?” he asked. He then proceeded to his information. “Someone’s got it in against the paper, sure,” he said. “I don’t know what it’s all about. I ha’n’t never read the thing. Don’t see what anyone could have against a paper with a name like *Cosy Moments*, anyway. The way things have been going last few days, seems it might be the organ of a blamed mining-camp what the boys have took a dislike to.”

“What’s been happening?” asked Billy with gleaming eyes.

“Why, nothing in the world to fuss about, only our carriers can’t go out without being beaten up by gangs of toughs. Pat Harrigan’s in the hospital now. Just been looking in on him. Pat’s a feller who likes to fight. Rather fight he would than see a ballgame. But this was too much for him. Know what happened? Why, see here, just like this it was. Pat goes out with his cart. Passing through a low-down street on his way uptown he’s held up by a bunch of toughs. He shows fight. Half a dozen of them attend to him, while the rest gets clean away with every copy of the paper there was in the cart. When the cop comes along, there’s Pat in pieces on the ground and nobody in sight but a Dago chewing gum. Cop asks the Dago what’s been doing, and the Dago says he’s only just come round the corner and ha’n’t seen nothing of anybody. What I want to know is, what’s it all about? Who’s got it in for us and why?”

===Chinese===
「到底出什么事了？」他问。接着才说到他的消息。「有人在跟这报纸过不去，肯定的。」他说。「我不知道到底为啥。这玩意儿我一行都没读过。反正我看不出，谁会跟一张名叫《舒适时光》的报纸过不去。照这几天的情形，它倒像是某座该死矿区营地的机关报，惹得哥们儿嫌弃上了。」

「到底出了什么事？」比利目光灼灼地问。

「嗐，天大的事也算不上，不过就是我们的送报员一出门就挨帮派流氓的揍。帕特·哈里根（Pat Harrigan）这会儿正躺在医院里。我刚去看了他。帕特是个爱打架的主儿。让他挑，他宁可打架也不看球赛。可这回也太过分了。知道怎么回事吗？嗐，你听着，事情是这样式的。帕特推着车出去。穿一条下三滥街上城的时候，被一伙流氓拦住了。他摆开架势打。六个人招呼他一个，其余的把车上的报纸一张不剩地席卷一空。等警察赶到，帕特已经七零八碎躺在地上，四下里只剩一个嚼着口香糖的南欧佬。警察问那南欧佬出了什么事，南欧佬说他刚从拐角那边过来，什么人也没瞧见。我就想知道，这到底是怎么一回事？谁跟我们过不去，图什么？」

===Original===
Mr. Wheeler leaned back in his chair, while Billy, his hair rumpled more than ever and his eyes glowing, explained the situation. Mr. Wheeler listened absolutely unmoved, and, when the narrative had come to an end, gave it as his opinion that the editorial staff had sand. That was his sole comment.

“It’s up to you,” he said, rising. “You know your business. Say, though, someone had better get busy right quick and do something to stop these guys roughhousing like this. If we get a few more carriers beat up the way Pat was, there’ll be a strike. It’s not as if they were all Irishmen. The most of them are Dagoes and such, and they don’t want any more fight than they can get by beating their wives and kicking kids off the sidewalk. I’ll do my best to get this paper distributed right and it’s a shame if it ain’t, because it’s going big just now—but it’s up to you. Good day, gents.”

===Chinese===
惠勒先生往椅背上一靠，听比利——头发比平时更乱、双眼放光——说明情由。惠勒先生听着，纹丝不动，等叙述终了，发表见解：编辑部有胆量。这就是他的全部评语。

「瞧你们的了。」他起身说。「你们懂行。不过我说，最好赶紧有人忙活起来，想法子治治这帮家伙这么胡闹。要是再有几个送报员落得帕特这下场，就要罢工了。好在他们也不是个个都爱尔兰人。多半是南欧佬之流，他们想要的打斗，无非是打老婆、把小孩踢下人行道，再多的就不要了。我会尽力把报纸发行到位——发行不到位就可惜了，眼下正卖得红火——不过，瞧你们的了。回见，各位。」

He went out. Psmith looked at Billy.

“As Comrade Wheeler remarks,” he said, “it is up to us. What do you propose to do about it? This is a move of the enemy which I have not anticipated. I had fancied that their operations would be confined exclusively to our two selves. If they are going to strew the street with our carriers, we are somewhat in the soup.”

Billy said nothing. He was chewing the stem of an unlighted pipe. Psmith went on.

===Chinese===
他走了出去。普史密斯看着比利。

「正如惠勒同志所言，」他说，「得瞧我们的了。你打算如何应对？敌人这一手我未曾料到。我原以为他们的行动只会局限于我们两个本人。他们若是打算拿我们的送报员铺满街道，咱们可就陷进汤锅里了。」

比利没说话。他嚼着一支没点着的烟斗的柄。普史密斯继续说下去。

===Original===
“It means, of course, that we must buck up to a certain extent. If the campaign is to be a long one, they have us where the hair is crisp. We cannot stand the strain. *Cosy Moments* cannot be muzzled, but it can undoubtedly be choked. What we want to do is to find out the name of the man behind the tenements as soon as ever we can and publish it; and, then, if we perish, fall yelling the name.”

Billy admitted the soundness of this scheme, but wished to know how it was to be done.

“Comrade Windsor,” said Psmith. “I have been thinking this thing over, and it seems to me that we are on the wrong track, or rather we aren’t on any track at all; we are simply marking time. What we want to do is to go out and hustle round till we stir up something. Our line up to the present has been to sit at home and scream vigorously in the hope of some stout fellow hearing and rushing to help. In other words, we’ve been saying in the paper what an outsize in scugs the merchant must be who owns those tenements, in the hope that somebody else will agree with us and be sufficiently interested to get to work and find out who the blighter is. That’s all wrong. What we must do now, Comrade Windsor, is put on our hats, such hats as Comrade Repetto has left us, and sally forth as sleuthhounds on our own account.”

===Chinese===
「这当然意味着，我们必须在一定程度上振作起来。这场战役若是打成长期的，他们就把我们攥在脆处了。我们熬不住这份消耗。《舒适时光》套不上口套，却无疑可以被掐住喉咙。我们要做的，就是尽早查出廉租公寓背后那个人的名字，公之于众；然后，即便覆灭，也要喊着那个名字倒下。」

比利承认此计甚稳妥，但想知道如何落实。

===Original===
“Yes, but how?” demanded Billy. “That’s all right in theory, but how’s it going to work in practice? The only thing that can corner the man is a commission.”

“Far from it, Comrade Windsor. The job may be worked more simply. I don’t know how often the rents are collected in these places, but I should say at a venture once a week. My idea is to hang negligently round till the rent-collector arrives, and when he has loomed up on the horizon, buttonhole him and ask him quite politely, as man to man, whether he is collecting those rents for himself or for somebody else, and if somebody else, who that somebody else is. Simple, I fancy? Yet brainy. Do you take me, Comrade Windsor?”

Billy sat up, excited. “I believe you’ve hit it.”

Psmith shot his cuffs modestly.

===Chinese===
「是，可是怎么干？」比利追问。「理论上是好的，实际操作起来怎么弄？能把这个逼到墙角的，只有专门委员会。」

比利兴奋地坐直了。「我看你说到点子上了。」

普史密斯谦虚地抖了抖衬衫袖口。
