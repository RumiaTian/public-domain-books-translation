## Bird-Songs / 鸟之歌
===Original===

I will sing a song,
Said the owl.
You sing a song, sing-song
Ugly fowl!
What will you sing about,
Night in and day out?

All about the night,
When the gray
With her cloak smothers bright,
Hard, sharp day.
Oh, the moon! the cool dew!
And the shadows!﻿—tu-whoo!

I will sing a song,
Said the nightingale.
Sing a song, long, long,
Little Neverfail!
What will you sing about,
Day in or day out?

All about the light
Gone away,
Down, away, and out of sight:
Wake up, day!
For the master is not dead,
Only gone to bed.

I will sing a song,
Said the lark.
Sing, sing, Throat-strong,
Little Kill-the-dark!
What will you sing about,
Day in and night out?

I can only call!
I can’t think!
Let me up, that’s all!
I see a chink!
I’ve been thirsting all night
For the glorious light!

===Chinese===

我要唱一支歌，
猫头鹰说。
你唱吧，唱吧，唱个不停，
丑陋的鸟！
你要唱什么呢，
整日整夜地唱？

我要唱那黑夜，
唱那灰色
用她的斗篷闷熄明亮、
坚硬尖利的白昼。
哦月亮！哦清凉的露！
还有那些影子！——吐—呼！

我要唱一支歌，
夜莺说。
唱吧，唱吧，长长的，
小「从不失败」！
你要唱什么呢，
日日夜夜地唱？

我要唱那光，
它去了远方，
向下、向远方，看不见了：
醒来吧，白昼！
因为主人没有死，
只是上床睡了。

我要唱一支歌，
云雀说。
唱吧，唱吧，大嗓门，
小「杀死黑暗」！
你要唱什么呢，
整日整夜地唱？

我只会叫！
我不会想！
让我上去，就这一件事！
我看见了一道裂缝！
我渴了一整夜，
渴慕那荣耀的光！
