## A Make-Believe / 一场假装游戏
===Original===

I will think as thinks the rabbit:﻿—

Oh, delight
In the night
When the moon
Sets the tune
To the woods!
And the broods
All run out,
Frisk about,
Go and come,
Beat the drum﻿—
Here in groups,
There in troops!
Now there’s one!
Now it’s gone!
There are none!
And now they are dancing like chaff!
I look, and I laugh,
But sit by my door, and keep to my habit﻿—
A wise, respectable, clean-furred old rabbit!

Now I’m going,
Business calls me out﻿—
Going, going,
Very knowing,
Slow, long-heeled, and stout,
Loping, lumbering,
Nipping, numbering,
Head on this side and on that,
Along the pathway footed flat,
Through the meadow, through the heather,
Through the rich dusky weather﻿—
Big stars and little moon!
Dews are lighting down in crowds,
Odours rising in thin clouds,
Night has all her chords in tune﻿—
The very night for us, God’s rabbits,
Suiting all our little habits!
Wind not loud, but playful with our fur,
Just a cool, a sweet, a gentle stir!
And all the way not one rough bur,
But the dewiest, freshest grasses,
That whisper thanks to every foot that passes!

I, the king the rest call Mappy,
Canter on, composed and happy,
Till I come where there is plenty
For a varied meal and dainty.
Is it cabbage, I grab it;
Is it parsley, I nab it;
Is it carrot, I mar it;
The turnip I turn up
And hollow and swallow;
A lettuce? Let us eat it!
A beetroot? Let’s beat it!
If you are juicy,
Sweet sir, I will use you!
For all kinds of corn-crop
I have a born crop!
Are you a green top?
You shall be gleaned up!
Sucking and feazing,
Crushing and squeezing
All that is feathery,
Crisp, not leathery,
Juicy and bruisy﻿—
All comes proper
To my little hopper
Still on the dance,
Driven by hunger and drouth!
All is welcome to my crunching,
Finding, grinding,
Milling, munching,
Gobbling, lunching,
Fore-toothed, three-lipped mouth﻿—
Eating side way, round way, flat way,
Eating this way, eating that way,
Every way at once!

Hark to the rain!﻿—
Pattering, clattering,
The cabbage leaves battering,
Down it comes amain!﻿—
Home we hurry
Hop and scurry,
And in with a flurry!
Hustling, jostling
Out of the airy land
Into the dry warm sand;
Our family white tails,
The last of our vitals,
Following hard with a whisk to them,
And with a great sense of risk to them!

===Chinese===

我要像兔子那样去想事情：——

哦，夜里多欢畅，
当月亮
给树林
定下曲调！
一窝窝的
全都跑出来，
蹦跳嬉闹，
来了又去，
敲起鼓点——
这里一小群，
那里一大伙！
瞧，有一只！
转眼不见了！
一只也不剩了！
这会儿它们又像谷壳般跳舞！
我看着，笑着，
却守在门边，保持我的老习惯——
一只明智、体面、皮毛干净的老兔子！

现在我要出门了，
有正事唤我出去——
去呀，去呀，
心里有数，
慢吞吞、长后腿、壮实实，
蹦跳着、笨拙地挪，
小口啃着，一路数着，
脑袋这边偏偏，那边偏偏，
沿着踩得平平的小径，
穿过草场，穿过石楠，
穿过浓郁昏暗的天气——

大星星和小月亮！
露水成群地轻轻降落，
香气在薄云中升起，
夜晚把她所有的琴弦都调准了——
这正是为我们、为上帝的兔子们设的夜，
合我们一切小小的习性！
风不喧哗，只跟我们的皮毛嬉戏，
只是一阵清凉、甜美、温柔的拂动！
一路没有一颗扎人的芒刺，
只有挂满露水、最新鲜的青草，
对每一双经过的脚低声道谢！

我，被大伙称作玛皮王的那位，
从容而快乐地小跑向前，
直到来到一处丰盛的地方，
可以吃一顿花样繁多、精致的美餐。
是卷心菜？我抓住它；
是欧芹？我抢来它；
是胡萝卜？我啃坏它；
芜菁我把它翻出来，
掏空、吞下；
莴苣？咱们吃掉它！
甜菜根？咱们「打敗」它！
你若是多汁的，
甜美的先生，我就享用你！
对每一种谷物的收成，
我都天生自备一副嗉囊！
你是青绿的缨子？
你必被我拾掇干净！
吮吸着，撕扯着，
碾碎着，挤压着
一切毛茸茸、
脆嫩而不韧、
多汁而易碰伤的——
一切都正合适
我这小小的跳跃者，
还在跳着舞，
被饥渴驱赶着！
一切都欢迎进我咔嚓作响、
寻寻觅觅、碾碾磨磨、
吞吞嚼嚼、
狼吞虎咽、边吃边尝、
门牙在前、三瓣唇的嘴里——
横着吃，转着吃，平着吃，
这样吃，那样吃，
一口气样样都吃！

听那雨声！——
噼噼啪啪，哗哗啦啦，
砸得卷心菜叶乒乓作响，
大雨倾盆而下！——
我们急忙往家赶，
连蹦带窜，
一阵风钻进去！
推推搡搡，
挤出那天空下的世界，
钻进干燥温暖的沙土；
我们一家的白尾巴，
我们身上最后的要害，
紧跟着一甩，跟了进去，
还提心吊胆，怕被夹住！

===Original===

Hear to it pouring!
Hear the thunder roaring
Far off and up high,
While we all lie
So warm and so dry
In the mellow dark,
Where never a spark,
White or rosy or blue,
Of the sheeting, fleeting,
Forking, frightening,
Lashing lightning
Ever can come through!

Let the wind chafe
In the trees overhead,
We are quite safe
In our dark, yellow bed!
Let the rain pour!
It never can bore
A hole in our roof﻿—
It is waterproof!
So is the cloak
We always carry,
We furry folk,
In sandhole or quarry!
It is perfect bliss
To lie in a nest
So soft as this,
All so warmly drest!
No one to flurry you!
No one to hurry you!
No one to scurry you!
Holes plenty to creep in!
All day to sleep in!
All night to roam in!
Gray dawn to run home in!
And all the days and nights to come after﻿—
All the to-morrows for hind-legs and laughter!

Now the rain is over,
We are out again,
Every merry, leaping rover,
On his right leg and his wrong leg,
On his doubled, shortened long leg,
Floundering amain!
Oh, it is merry
And jolly﻿—yes, very!

But what﻿—what is that?
What can he be at?
Is it a cat?
Ah, my poor little brother,
He’s caught in the trap
That goes-to with a snap!
Ah me! there was never,
Nor will be for ever﻿—
There was never such another,
Such a funny, funny bunny,
Such a frisking, such a whisking,
Such a frolicking brother!
He’s screeching, beseeching!
They’re going to﻿—

Ah, my poor foot,
It is caught in a root!
No, no! ’tis a trap
That goes-to with a snap!
Ah me, I’m forsaken!
Ah me, I am taken!
I am screeching, beseeching!
They are going to﻿—

No more! no more! I must stop this play,
Be a boy again, and kneel down and pray
To the God of sparrows and rabbits and men,
Who never lets any one out of his ken﻿—
It must be so, though it be bewild’ring﻿—
To save his dear beasts from his cruel children!

===Chinese===

听那倾盆大雨！
听那雷声轰鸣，
在远处，在高空，
而我们全都躺着，
又暖和又干燥，
在醇厚的黑暗里，
在那里，无论白色、
玫瑰色还是蓝色的火花——
那片状飞掠、
枝杈分叉、
骇人鞭挞的闪电——
永远穿不进来！

任风在头顶
的树间发怒，
我们十分安全，
在我们幽黄暗色的床里！

任雨倾盆！
它永远钻不透
我们的屋顶——
它是防水的！
我们毛茸茸一族
无论在沙洞还是采石场，
身上常年披的
那件斗篷也是防水的！

躺在这样
柔软的窝里，
裹得暖暖和和，
真是十足的福分！

没有谁来惊扰你！
没有谁来催逼你！
没有谁把你吓得乱窜！
洞多得钻不完！
白天睡个够！
整夜任你游荡！
灰蒙蒙的黎明跑回家去！
往后还有那么多的日日夜夜——
那么多的明天，留给后腿和欢笑！

如今雨过天晴，
我们又出来了，
每个欢快蹦跳的游荡者，
迈着对的腿、错的腿，
迈着蜷起的、缩短的长腿，
一路踉跄飞奔！
哦，真是快活，
开心——对，非常开心！

可是什么——那是什么？
它在干什么？
是一只猫吗？
啊，我可怜的小兄弟，
他落进了陷阱，
咔嗒一声合上了！
唉！从来没有过，
往后也永远不会有——
从来没有这样的一个，
这样一只滑稽又滑稽的小兔子，
这样蹦蹦跳跳、窜来窜去、
这样嬉戏玩闹的兄弟！
他在尖叫，在哀求！
他们要来——

啊，我可怜的脚，
被树根夹住了！
不，不！是陷阱，
咔嗒一声合上了！
唉，我被撇下了！
唉，我被抓住了！
我在尖叫，在哀求！
他们要来——

不要了！不要了！我必须停止这场游戏，
重新做回一个男孩，跪下祷告，
向那麻雀、兔子与人的上帝——
他从不让任何一个离开他的视野——
必定如此，虽然令人困惑——
求他救他亲爱的兽类，脱离他残忍的孩子们！
