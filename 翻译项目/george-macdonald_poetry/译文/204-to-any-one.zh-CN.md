## To Any One / 致任何人
===Original===

Go not forth to call Dame Sorrow
From the dim fields of Tomorrow;
Let her roam there all unheeded,
She will come when she is needed;
Then, when she draws near thy door,
She will find God there before.

===Chinese===

不要出去，从「明日」昏暗的田野里
把忧愁夫人唤来；
任她在那里游荡，无人理会，
需要她时她自会来；
而当她走近你的门，
会发现上帝早已在那儿等着。
