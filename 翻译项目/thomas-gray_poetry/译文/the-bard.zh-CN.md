## The Bard A Pindaric Ode / 吟游诗人 一首品达体颂歌

===Original===
The following Ode is founded on a tradition current in Wales, that Edward the First, when he completed the conquest of that country, ordered all the bards that fell into his hands to be put to death.

===Chinese===
下面这首颂歌取材于威尔士流传的一个传说：爱德华一世在完成对该国的征服之后，下令将落入他手中的吟游诗人全部处死。

===Original===
I. 1.

"Ruin seize thee, ruthless King!

Confusion on thy banners wait,

Though fanned by Conquest's crimson wing

They mock the air with idle state.16

Helm, nor hauberk's twisted mail,17

Nor even thy virtues, Tyrant, shall avail

To save thy secret soul from nightly fears,

From Cambria's curse, from Cambria's tears!"

Such were the sounds, that o'er the crested pride18

Of the first Edward scattered wild dismay,

As down the steep of Snowdon's shaggy side19

He wound with toilsome march his long array.

Stout Glo'ster stood aghast in speechless trance;20

"To arms!" cried Mortimer, and couched his quivering lance.21

===Chinese===
I. 1.

「愿毁灭抓住你，无情的国王！

愿溃乱守候在你的旌旗之上，

纵然胜利以猩红的翅膀扇动，

它们也只是以虚张的威仪嘲弄空气。16

头盔，或锁环盘绕的锁子甲，17

甚至你的种种德行，暴君，都无济于事，

救不了你隐秘的灵魂免于夜夜的恐惧，

免于坎布里亚的诅咒、坎布里亚的眼泪！」

这样的声音，在第一位爱德华冠顶

骄横的头上18撒开慌乱的惊惶，

当他拖着艰难行军的长队，

沿斯诺登乱石嶙峋的陡坡19盘旋而下。

刚毅的格洛斯特呆立当场，哑然失魂；20

「拿起武器！」莫蒂默大喊，挺起他颤动的长矛。21

===Original===
I. 2.

On a rock, whose haughty brow,

Frowns o'er old Conway's foaming flood,

Robed in the sable garb of woe,

With haggard eyes the Poet stood;

(Loose his beard, and hoary hair22

Streamed, like a meteor, to the troubled air)23

And with a Master's hand, and Prophet's fire,

Struck the deep sorrows of his lyre.

"Hark, how each giant-oak, and desert cave,

Sighs to the torrent's awful voice beneath!

O'er thee, oh King! their hundred arms they wave,

Revenge on thee in hoarser murmurs breathe;

Vocal no more, since Cambria's fatal day,

To high-born Hoel's harp, or soft Llewellyn's lay.

===Chinese===
I. 2.

在一块岩石上，它倨傲的眉宇

俯临古老的康威翻沫的激流，

身披乌黑的丧服，

诗人形容枯槁地站着；

（披散的胡须与霜白的头发22

如一颗流星，飘向骚动的天空）23

而他以大师之手、先知之火，

拨响他里拉琴深处的悲怆。

「听，每一株巨橡、每一个荒洞，

都在向脚下激流可畏的轰声叹息！

在你上方，哦国王！他们挥动百条手臂，

以更嘶哑的低语呼出对你的复仇；

它们不再歌唱，自坎布里亚遭难之日起，

不再应和高贵的霍埃尔的竖琴，或温柔的卢埃林的歌。

===Original===
I. 3.

Cold is Cadwallo's tongue,

That hushed the stormy main;

Brave Urien sleeps upon his craggy bed;

Mountains, ye mourn in vain

Modred, whose magic song

Made huge Plinlimmon bow his cloud-topped head.

On dreary Arvon's shore they lie,24

Smeared with gore, and ghastly pale;

Far, far aloof th' affrighted ravens sail;

The famished Eagle screams, and passes by.25

Dear lost companions of my tuneful art,

Dear, as the light that visits these sad eyes,26

Dear, as the ruddy drops that warm my heart,

Ye died amidst your dying country's cries—

No more I weep. They do not sleep.

On yonder cliffs, a grisly band,

I see them sit, they linger yet,

Avengers of their native land;

With me in dreadful harmony they join,

And weave with bloody hands the tissue of thy line."27

===Chinese===
I. 3.

卡德瓦洛的舌已冰冷，

它曾使风暴的大海平息；

勇武的乌里安睡在他嶙峋的床上；

群山啊，你们徒然哀悼

莫德雷德，他魔力的歌

曾使庞大的普林利蒙俯下云遮的头颅。

在凄凉的阿尔文海岸，他们躺着，24

血污满身，惨白骇人；

受惊的渡鸦远远、远远地飞开；

饥饿的鹰尖啸着，掠过。25

亲爱的、我音乐之艺的失落的伙伴，

亲如探望这双愁眼的光，26

亲如温暖我心的殷红血滴，

你们在垂死祖国的哭喊声中死去——

我不再哭泣。他们并未安睡。

在那边悬崖上，一伙狰狞的人影，

我看见他们坐着，他们仍在逗留，

是他们故土的复仇者；

他们与我汇成可怖的和声，

以染血的手织出你世系的经纬。」27

===Original===
II. 1.

"Weave the warp, and weave the woof,

The winding-sheet of Edward's race.

Give ample room, and verge enough

The characters of hell to trace.

Mark the year, and mark the night,

When Severn shall re-echo with affright28

The shrieks of death, thro' Berkley's roofs that ring,

Shrieks of an agonizing King!

She-Wolf of France, with unrelenting fangs,29

That tear'st the bowels of thy mangled Mate,

From thee be born, who o'er thy country hangs30

The scourge of Heaven. What Terrors round him wait!

Amazement in his van, with Flight combined,

And sorrow's faded form, and solitude behind."

===Chinese===
II. 1.

「织经线，织纬线，

织爱德华家族的裹尸布。

留足空间、足够的边幅，

好描出地狱的文字。

记住那年，记住那夜，

当塞文河将惊惶地回响28

死亡的尖叫，响彻伯克利的屋顶，

一位垂死挣扎的国王的尖叫！

法兰西的母狼，以毫不留情的獠牙，29

撕扯你残破配偶的脏腑，

从你将生出那把天罚之鞭30

高悬于你祖国之上。何等的恐怖在他四周守候！

惊惶在前开路，与奔逃相联，

还有忧伤褪色的形影，与孤寂殿后。」

===Original===
II. 2.

"Mighty Victor, mighty Lord!

Low on his funeral couch he lies!31

No pitying heart, no eye, afford

A tear to grace his obsequies.

Is the sable Warrior fled?32

Thy son is gone. He rests among the Dead.

The Swarm, that in thy noontide beam were born?

Gone to salute the rising Morn.

Fair laughs the Morn, and soft the Zephyr blows,33

While proudly riding o'er the azure realm

In gallant trim the gilded Vessel goes;

Youth on the prow, and Pleasure at the helm;

Regardless of the sweeping Whirlwind's sway,

That, hushed in grim repose, expects his evening-prey."

===Chinese===
II. 2.

「威严的胜利者，威严的君王！

他低低地躺在灵床上！31

没有怜悯的心，没有一只眼，

肯滴一滴泪来装点他的葬仪。

那黑甲的武士已遁走？32

你的儿子去了。他在死者中间安息。

那在你正午光辉里生养的一群？

都去朝拜初升的晨光。

晨光笑得明媚，西风轻轻吹拂，33

当那艘鎏金的船，装束煊赫，

骄傲地驶过蔚蓝的疆域；

青春立在船头，欢愉掌着舵；

全不顾那横扫的旋风之威力，

它正狞然蛰伏，静候它傍晚的猎物。」

===Original===
II. 3.

"Fill high the sparkling bowl,34

The rich repast prepare,

Reft of a crown, he yet may share the feast;

Close by the regal chair

Fell Thirst and Famine scowl

A baleful smile upon their baffled Guest.

Heard ye the din of battle bray,35

Lance to lance, and horse to horse?

Long Years of havoc urge their destined course,

And thro' the kindred squadrons mow their way.

Ye Towers of Julius, London's lasting shame,36

With many a foul and midnight murther fed,

Revere his Consort's faith, his Father's fame,3738

And spare the meek Usurper's holy head.39

Above, below, the rose of snow,40

Twined with her blushing foe, we spread;

The bristled Boar in infant-gore41

Wallows beneath the thorny shade.

Now, Brothers, bending o'er th' accursed loom

Stamp we our vengeance deep, and ratify his doom."

===Chinese===
II. 3.

「把闪亮的酒盏斟满，34

备好丰盛的宴席，

纵然失去王冠，他尚可共享此宴；

紧挨着王座，

狞恶的干渴与饥荒

朝他们失意的客人投来凶险的笑。

可听见战阵的喧嚣嘶鸣，35

长矛对长矛，战马对战马？

浩劫的漫长岁月推动注定的进程，

在骨肉的队列间刈出自己的路。

尤利乌斯之塔啊，伦敦永远的耻辱，36

以多少卑污的午夜谋杀喂肥了自己，

且敬重他王后的忠贞、他父亲的名声，3738

饶过那谦卑篡位者的圣洁头颅。39

上方下方，雪白的玫瑰，40

与她含羞的仇敌相缠，我们铺展；

那鬃毛倒竖的野猪，在婴儿的血污里41

于多刺的浓荫下打滚。

现在，兄弟们，俯向这受诅咒的织机，

把我们的复仇钤印钤深，批准他的末日。」

===Original===
III. 1.

"Edward, lo! to sudden fate

(Weave we the woof. The thread is spun.)

Half of thy heart we consecrate.42

(The web is wove. The work is done.)

Stay, oh stay! nor thus forlorn

Leave me unblessed, unpitied, here to mourn;

In yon bright track, that fires the western skies,

They melt, they vanish from my eyes.

But oh! what solemn scenes on Snowdon's height

Descending slow their glitt'ring skirts unroll?

Visions of glory, spare my aching sight,

Ye unborn Ages, crowd not on my soul!

No more our long-lost Arthur we bewail.43

All hail, ye genuine Kings, Britannia's Issue, hail!"44

===Chinese===
III. 1.

「爱德华，看哪！突至的命运

（我们织纬线。线已纺好。）

你心的一半，我们奉为祭献。42

（布已织就。工已完成。）

留下，哦留下！别这样孤零零

把我无福无怜地撇在这里哀恸；

在那点亮西天的明亮轨迹里，

他们消融了，从我眼前消逝。

但是啊！斯诺登之巅上是何等庄严的景象，

缓缓降下，展开它们闪烁的裙裾？

荣耀的幻象，饶过我作痛的目光，

未生的世代啊，别簇拥上我的灵魂！

我们不再哀恸久已失落的亚瑟。43

万岁，真正的君王们，不列颠的苗裔，万岁！」44

===Original===
III. 2.

"Girt with many a Baron bold

Sublime their starry fronts they rear;

And gorgeous Dames, and Statesmen old

In bearded majesty, appear.

In the midst a Form divine!

Her eye proclaims her of the Briton-Line;

Her lion-port, her awe commanding face,45

Attempered sweet to virgin-grace.

What strings symphonious tremble in the air,

What strains of vocal transport round her play!

Hear from the grave, great Taliessin, hear;46

They breathe a soul to animate thy clay.

Bright Rapture calls, and soaring, as she sings,

Waves in the eye of Heaven her many-coloured wings."

===Chinese===
III. 2.

还有华贵的命妇、老成的政治家，

===Original===
III. 3.

"The verse adorn again

Fierce War, and faithful Love,47

And Truth severe, by fairy Fiction drest.

In buskined measures move48

Pale Grief, and pleasing Pain,

With Horror, Tyrant of the throbbing breast.

A Voice as of the Cherub-Choir,49

Gales from blooming Eden bear;

And distant warblings lessen on my ear,50

That lost in long futurity expire.

Fond impious Man, think'st thou, yon sanguine cloud,

Raised by thy breath, has quenched the Orb of day?

To-morrow he repairs the golden flood,

And warms the nations with redoubled ray.

Enough for me. With joy I see

The different doom our Fates assign.

Be thine Despair, and sceptred Care,

To triumph, and to die, are mine."

He spoke, and headlong from the mountain's height

Deep in the roaring tide he plunged to endless night.

「让诗行再度装点

惨烈的战争与忠贞的爱情，47

还有严峻的真实——以仙灵的虚构为衣。

以悲剧的高靴步律行进，48

苍白的忧伤、悦人的痛楚，

与恐怖同行，它是悸动胸膛的暴君。

一个声音，仿佛基路伯的合唱，49

由繁花盛开的伊甸吹来的和风载来；

远处的歌声在我耳畔渐轻，50

终于消散在悠远的未来。

痴妄不虔的人啊，你以为，那片被你气息

扬起的血色云，已熄灭了日轮？

明天他将重整金色的光流，

以加倍的光焰温暖万国。

于我足矣。我欣喜地看到

命运分派给我们的不同结局。

愿你得到绝望，与执杖的忧劳，

凯旋与赴死，属于我。」

他说罢，从山巅一头

扎进咆哮的激流，坠入无尽的长夜。
