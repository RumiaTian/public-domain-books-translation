## The Descent of Odin An Ode from the Norse Tongue / 奥丁的降临 译自古诺语的一首颂歌

===Original===
In Bartholinus, de causis contemnendae mortis; Hafniae, 1689

Uprose the King of Men with speed,

And saddled strait his coal-black steed;

Down the yawning steep he rode,

That leads to Hela's drear abode.72

Him the Dog of Darkness spied,

His shaggy throat he opened wide,

While from his jaws, with carnage filled,

Foam and human gore distilled;

Hoarse he bays with hideous din,

Eyes that glow, and fangs that grin;

And long pursues, with fruitless yell,

The father of the powerful spell.

===Chinese===
载于巴托林《论轻视死亡之因》（de causis contemnendae mortis）；哥本哈根（Hafniae），1689 年

众人之王霍然起身，迅捷无比，

立刻备好他煤黑的骏马；

他骑马驰下张口的陡崖，

那路通往赫拉凄凉的居所。72

黑暗之犬发现了他，

它张开毛茸茸的咽喉，

从它塞满杀戮的双颚，

滴下白沫与人血；

它哑着嗓狂吠，声浪骇人，

眼冒凶光，獠牙狞笑；

又长久追赶，徒劳地嚎叫，

追赶那强咒之父。

===Original===
Onward still his way he takes,

(The groaning earth beneath him shakes,)

Till full before his fearless eyes

The portals nine of hell arise.

===Chinese===
他仍旧一路向前，

（脚下呻吟的大地震颤，）

直到他无畏的眼前后

耸起地狱的九重门。

===Original===
Right against the eastern gate,

By the moss-grown pile he sate;

Where long of yore to sleep was laid

The dust of the prophetic Maid.

Facing to the northern clime,

Thrice he traced the runic rhyme;

Thrice pronounced, in accents dread,

The thrilling verse that wakes the dead;

Till from out the hollow ground

Slowly breathed a sullen sound.

===Chinese===
正对着东门，

他在青苔丛生的石堆旁坐下；

很久以前，预言的少女，

她的尘骨就被安放在那里长眠。

他面朝北方的天域，

三度描画如尼的韵文；

三度以可畏的声调，

念出那唤醒死者的震颤诗行；

直到从空洞的地底，

缓缓呼出一声阴沉的响动。

===Original===
Prophetess

What call unknown, what charms presume

To break the quiet of the tomb?

Who thus afflicts my troubled sprite,

And drags me from the realms of night?

Long on these mould'ring bones have beat

The winter's snow, the summer's heat,

The drenching dews, and driving rain!

Let me, let me sleep again.

Who is he, with voice unblest,

That calls me from the bed of rest?

===Chinese===
女预言者

是何等的召唤，何等的符咒胆敢

惊扰坟墓的安宁？

谁这样折磨我烦乱的魂灵，

把我从黑夜的国度拖出？

冬雪与夏暑早已在这朽骨上

轮番击打，

还有浸骨的露与横扫的雨！

让我，让我再睡去吧。

他是谁，带着不祥的声音，

把我从安息之榻唤起？

===Original===
Odin

A Traveller, to thee unknown,

Is he that calls, a Warrior's son.

Thou the deeds of light shalt know;

Tell me what is done below,

For whom yon glitt'ring board is spread,

Drest for whom yon golden bed.

===Chinese===
奥丁

一个旅人，你不认识他，

唤你的人是一个武士之子。

你终将知晓光明的作为；

告诉我下面发生了什么，

那闪闪的筵席为谁而设，

那张金床为谁铺陈。

===Original===
Prophetess

Mantling in the goblet see

The pure beverage of the bee,

O'er it hangs the shield of gold;

'Tis the drink of *Balder* bold;

*Balder's* head to death is given.

Pain can reach the sons of Heaven!

Unwilling I my lips unclose;

Leave me, leave me to repose.

===Chinese===
女预言者

看那杯中泛起泡沫的，

是蜜蜂纯净的饮品，

金盾悬在杯上；

那是勇敢的*巴尔德尔*（Balder）的酒；

*巴尔德尔*的头颅已交给了死亡。

痛苦竟能及于天神之子！

我不情愿地张开嘴唇；

走开，让我，让我安息。

===Original===
Odin

Once again my call obey.

Prophetess, arise, and say,

What dangers *Odin's* child await,

Who the Author of his fate.

===Chinese===
奥丁

再一次听从我的呼唤。

女预言者，起来，说说，

什么危险在等待*奥丁*的孩子，

谁是他命运的执笔人。

===Original===
Prophetess

In *Hoder's* hand the Hero's doom;

His brother sends him to the tomb.

Now my weary lips I close;

Leave me, leave me to repose.

===Chinese===
女预言者

英雄的劫数握在*霍德尔*（Hoder）手中；

他的兄弟送他进坟墓。

如今我疲惫的双唇合上；

走开，让我，让我安息。

===Original===
Odin

Prophetess, my spell obey,

Once again arise, and say,

Who th' Avenger of his guilt,

By whom shall *Hoder's* blood be spilt.

===Chinese===
奥丁

女预言者，遵从我的咒语，

再一次起来，说说，

谁是他罪愆的复仇者，

*霍德尔*的血将溅在谁手。

===Original===
Prophetess

In the caverns of the west,

By *Odin's* fierce embrace comprest,

A wond'rous Boy shall *Rinda* bear,

Who ne'er shall comb his raven-hair,

Nor wash his visage in the stream,

Nor see the sun's departing beam,

Till he on *Hoder's* corse shall smile

Flaming on the fun'ral pile.

Now my weary lips I close;

Leave me, leave me to repose.

===Chinese===
女预言者

在西边的洞窟里，

被*奥丁*猛烈的拥抱压过，

*琳达*（Rinda）将生下一个奇异的男孩，

他永不梳理乌黑的头发，

永不以流水洗脸，

永不看太阳西沉的余光，

直到他对着*霍德尔*的尸身微笑——

尸身正燃烧在火葬堆上。

如今我疲惫的双唇合上；

走开，让我，让我安息。

===Original===
Odin

Yet a while my call obey.

Prophetess, awake, and say,

What Virgins these, in speechless woe,

That bend to earth their solemn brow,

That their flaxen tresses tear,

And snowy veils, that float in air.

Tell me whence their sorrows rose;

Then I leave thee to repose.

===Chinese===
奥丁

再稍待片刻，听从我的呼唤。

女预言者，醒来，说说，

这些少女是谁，怀着无声的悲苦，

把庄严的眉宇垂向大地，

扯着她们亚麻色的发辫，

还有那飘在空中的雪白面纱。

告诉我她们的忧伤从何而起；

说完我就留你安息。

===Original===
Prophetess

Ha! no Traveller art thou,

King of Men, I know thee now;

Mightiest of a mighty line—

===Chinese===
女预言者

哈！你不是什么旅人，

众人之王，我认出你了；

你是强大世系中最强大者——

===Original===
Odin

No boding Maid of skill divine

Art thou, nor Prophetess of good;

But Mother of the giant-brood!

===Chinese===
奥丁

你并非预卜吉凶、身怀神技的少女，

也不是报佳音的女预言者；

你是巨人一族之母！

===Original===
Prophetess

Hie thee hence, and boast at home,

That never shall enquirer come

To break my iron-sleep again;

Till *Lok* has burst his tenfold chain.73

Never, till substantial Night

Has reassumed her ancient right;

Till wrapped in flames, in ruin hurled,

Sinks the fabric of the world.

===Chinese===
女预言者

快滚吧，回去自夸，

说再不会有问询者前来

打破我铁一般的沉睡；

直到*洛基*（Lok）挣断他的十重锁链。73

绝不会有，直到实体化的黑夜

重新取回她古老的权利；

直到这世界的构造裹着烈焰、在毁灭中

被抛掷着沉沦。
