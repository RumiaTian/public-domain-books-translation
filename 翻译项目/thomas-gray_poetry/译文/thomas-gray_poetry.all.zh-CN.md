## A Long Story / 一个漫长的故事

===Original===
In Britain's Isle, no matter where,

An ancient pile of building stands;

The Huntingdons and Hattons there

Each panel in achievements clothing,

And passages, that lead to nothing.

### The Argument / 剧情梗概

===Original===
The drama opens with the indignation of Agrippina at receiving her son's orders from Anicetus to remove from Baiae, and to have her guard taken from her. At this time Otho having conveyed Poppaea from the house of her husband Rufus Crispinus, brings her to Baiae, where he means to conceal her among the crowd; or, if his fraud is discovered, to have recourse to the Emperor's authority; but, knowing the lawless temper of Nero, he determines not to have recourse to that expedient but on the utmost necessity. In the meantime he commits her to the care of Anicetus, whom he takes to be his friend, and in whose age he thinks he may safely confide. Nero is not yet come to Baiae; but Seneca, whom he sends before him, informs Agrippina of the accusation concerning Rubellius Plancus, and desires her to clear herself, which she does briefly, but demands to see her son, who, on his arrival, acquits her of all suspicion, and restores her to her honours.

In the mean while, Anicetus, to whose care Poppaea had been entrusted by Otho, contrives the following plot to ruin Agrippina; he betrays his trust to Otho, and brings Nero, as it were by chance, to the sight of the beautiful Poppaea. The Emperor is immediately struck with her charms, and she, by a feigned resistance, increases his passion; though, in reality, she is from the first dazzled with the prospect of empire, and forgets Otho. She therefore joins with Anicetus in his design of ruining Agrippina, soon perceiving that it will be for her interest. Otho hearing that the Emperor had seen Poppaea, is much enraged; but not knowing that this interview was obtained through the treachery of Anicetus, is readily persuaded by him to see Agrippina in secret, and acquaint her with his fears that her son Nero would marry Poppaea. Agrippina, to support her own power, and to wean the Emperor from the love of Poppaea, gives Otho encouragement, and promises to support him. Anicetus secretly introduces Nero to hear their discourse; who resolves immediately on his mother's death, and, by Anicetus's means, to destroy her by drowning. A solemn feast, in honour of their reconciliation, is to be made; after which she being to go by sea to Bauli, the ship is so contrived as to sink or crush her; she escapes by accident, and returns to Baiae.

In this interval Otho has an interview with Poppaea; and being duped a second time by Anicetus and her, determines to fly with her into Greece, by means of a vessel which is to be furnished by Anicetus; but he, pretending to remove Poppaea on board in the night, conveys her to Nero's apartment; she there encourages and determines Nero to banish Otho, and finish the horrid deed he had attempted on his mother. Anicetus undertakes to execute his resolves; and, under pretence of a plot upon the Emperor's life, is sent with a guard to murder Agrippina, who is still at Baiae in imminent fear, and irresolute how to conduct herself. The account of her death, and the Emperor's horror and fruitless remorse, finishes the drama.

在此间隙，奥托与波培娅会面；他被她和阿尼塞图斯再度愚弄，决意借助阿尼塞图斯备办的船只，与她一同逃往希腊；但阿尼塞图斯假称夜里把波培娅送上船，却把她送进了尼禄的寝宫；她在那里鼓动尼禄、使他下定决心放逐奥托，并完成他先前对母亲未遂的可怕罪行。阿尼塞图斯领命去执行他的决断；他借有人谋刺皇帝之名，奉命带卫队去杀仍在巴伊埃、身处极度恐惧、进退失据的阿格里皮娜。她的死讯，以及皇帝的惊怖与徒然的悔恨，为全剧作结。

## Act I. Scene I. / 第一幕 第一场

===Original===
*Scene—The Emperor's villa at Baiae.*

Agrippina

'Tis well, begone! your errand is performed,

The message needs no comment. Tell your master,

His mother shall obey him. Say you saw her

Yielding due reverence to his high command;

Alone, unguarded and without a lictor,

As fits the daughter of Germanicus.

Say, she retired to Antium; there to tend

Her household cares, a woman's best employment.

What if you add, how she turned pale and trembled;

You think, you spied a tear stand in her eye,

And would have dropped, but that her pride restrained it?

(Go! you can paint it well) 'twill profit you,

And please the stripling. Yet 'twould dash his joy

To hear the spirit of Britannicus

Yet walks on earth; at least there are who know

Without a spell to raise, and bid it fire

A thousand haughty hearts, unused to shake

When a boy frowns, nor to be lured with smiles

To taste of hollow kindness, or partake

His hospitable board; they are aware

Of the unpledged bowl, they love not aconite.

*Speaks as to Anicetus entering.*

## Ode on the Pleasure Arising from Vicissitude A Fragment / 咏变迁之乐 残篇

===Original===
Now the golden Morn aloft

Waves her dew-bespangled wing,

With vermeil cheek and whisper soft

She woos the tardy spring;

Till April starts, and calls around

The sleeping fragrance from the ground;

And lightly o'er the living scene

Scatters his freshest, tenderest green.

===Chinese===
此刻金色的清晨在高天

挥动她缀满露珠的翅膀，

以朱红的脸颊与轻柔的低语

追求着迟来的春天；

直到四月惊起，四下唤醒

沉睡在地底的芬芳；

又轻盈地在鲜活的景色上

撒下他最新鲜、最柔嫩的绿。

===Original===
New-born flocks, in rustic dance,

Frisking ply their feeble feet;

Forgetful of their wintry trance,

The birds his presence greet;

But chief, the sky-lark warbles high

His trembling thrilling ecstasy

And, lessening from the dazzled sight,

Melts into air and liquid light.

===Chinese===
新生的羊群，跳着乡土的舞蹈，

蹦蹦跳跳迈着柔弱的蹄子；

忘却了冬日里的昏沉恍惚，

鸟儿们向他问好；

而最要紧的是云雀高高啭鸣，

唱着颤抖而震颤心弦的狂喜，

渐渐缩小，退出眩目的视野，

消融进空气与流动的光。

===Original===
Rise, my soul! on wings of fire,

Rise the rapturous choir among;

Hark! 'tis Nature strikes the lyre,

And leads the general song.

Yesterday the sullen year

Saw the snowy whirlwind fly;

Mute was the music of the air,

The Herd stood drooping by;

Their raptures now that wildly flow,

No yesterday, nor morrow know;

'Tis man alone that joy descries

With forward and reverted eyes.

===Chinese===
起来吧，我的灵魂！乘着火焰之翼，

升入那狂喜的合唱队中；

听哪！是自然拨响了里拉琴，

领起了大合唱。

昨日，阴郁的季节

还见雪白的旋风飞卷；

空中的音乐喑哑无声，

畜群垂头伫立一旁；

他们如今奔涌的狂喜，

既不知昨日，也不知明朝；

唯有人，才能以瞻前顾后的眼睛

辨认出欢乐。

===Original===
Smiles on past Misfortune's brow

Soft Reflection's hand can trace;

And o'er the cheek of Sorrow throw

A melancholy grace;

While Hope prolongs our happier hour,

Or deepest shades, that dimly lower

And blacken round our weary way,

Gilds with a gleam of distant day.

===Chinese===
温柔的「回忆」之手，能在昔日厄运的

眉宇间描出微笑；

又能在忧伤的面颊上

披一缕忧郁的优雅；

当「希望」延长我们较幸福的时辰，

或以远方天光的一线微明，

镀亮那阴沉压顶、把疲惫之路

四下涂黑的至深暗影。

===Original===
Still, where rosy Pleasure leads,

See a kindred Grief pursue;

Behind the steps that Misery treads,

Approaching Comfort view;

The hues of Bliss more brightly glow,

Chastised by sabler tints of woe;

And blended form, with artful strife,

The strength and harmony of Life.

===Chinese===
而且，玫瑰色的欢乐所引领之处，

总见同源的忧伤紧随；

在苦难踏出的脚步后面，

看那正走近的安慰；

至福的色调因忧伤

更暗沉色调的调剂而愈见明艳；

它们以精巧的争衡交织成

生命的力量与和谐。

===Original===
See the Wretch, that long has tost

On the thorny bed of Pain,

At length repair his vigour lost,

And breathe and walk again;

The meanest flowret of the vale,

The simplest note that swells the gale,

The common Sun, the air, the skies,

To him are opening Paradise.

===Chinese===
看那在多刺的痛楚之床上

辗转已久的可怜人，

终于恢复了他耗损的元气，

又能呼吸，又能行走；

谷中最卑微的一朵小花，

随风扬起的最简单的一个音符，

寻常的太阳、空气与天空，

于他都是开启的乐园。

===Original===
Humble Quiet builds her cell,

Near the source whence Pleasure flows;

She eyes the clear crystalline well,

And tastes it as it goes.

===Chinese===
谦逊的静谧筑起她的小室，

就在欢乐流出的泉源近旁；

她凝望着清澈晶莹的井泉，

在泉水流过时细细品尝。

## Ode on the Spring / 春颂

===Original===
Lo! where the rosy-bosomed Hours,

Fair Venus' train, appear,

Disclose the long-expecting flowers,

And wake the purple year!

The Attic warbler pours her throat,

Responsive to the cuckoo's note,

The untaught harmony of spring;

While, whispering pleasure as they fly,

Cool Zephyrs thro' the clear blue sky

Their gathered fragrance fling.

===Chinese===
看哪！玫瑰色胸怀的时序女神，

美神维纳斯的扈从，现身了，

绽开久候的花朵，

唤醒紫红的年华！

阿提卡的歌鸟倾泻歌喉，

应和着杜鹃的啼鸣，

唱出春天未经传授的和鸣；

而清凉的西风一边飞一边低语着欢愉，

掠过澄澈的蓝天，

把他们攒集的芬芳抛洒。

===Original===
Where'er the oak's thick branches stretch

A broader browner shade,

Where'er the rude and moss-grown beech

O'er-canopies the glade,4

Beside some water's rushy brink

With me the Muse shall sit, and think

(At ease reclined in rustic state)

How vain the ardour of the crowd,

How low, how little are the proud,

How indigent the great!

===Chinese===
凡橡树稠密的枝桠张开

更宽广、更棕褐浓荫之处，

凡粗野多苔的山毛榉

为林间空地张起顶篷之处，4

在流水灯芯草丛生的岸边，

缪斯将与我同坐，思索

（悠然斜倚，一派村野的气度）：

人群的热望多么虚妄，

骄傲者多么卑琐、多么渺小，

显贵者多么贫乏！

===Original===
Still is the toiling hand of Care;

The panting herds repose;

Yet hark, how through the peopled air

The busy murmur glows!

The insect youth are on the wing,

Eager to taste the honied spring,

And float amid the liquid noon;5

Some lightly o'er the current skim,

Some show their gaily-gilded trim

Quick-glancing to the sun.6

===Chinese===
忧劳的操劳之手歇了；

喘息的畜群安歇了；

可听哪，在那挤满生灵的空中，

忙碌的嗡鸣多么灼热！

昆虫的青春正振翅，

急欲品尝蜜甜的春天，

漂浮在流动的正午之中；5

有的轻轻掠过水流，

有的炫耀它们艳丽镀金的衣裳，

迅疾地朝太阳闪烁。6

===Original===
To Contemplation's sober eye7

Such is the race of Man;

And they that creep, and they that fly,

Shall end where they began.

Alike the Busy and the Gay

But flutter through life's little day,

In fortune's varying colours drest;

Brushed by the hand of rough Mischance,

Or chilled by age, their airy dance

They leave, in dust to rest.

===Chinese===
在沉思冷静的眼里，7

人类正是这般光景；

爬行的也好，飞翔的也好，

都将终结于开始之地。

忙碌者与欢乐者全都一样，

只是扑腾过生命短促的一日，

穿着命运变换不定的彩衣；

被横逆之掌拂过，

或被年岁冻僵，他们轻盈的舞

就此停歇，归于尘土安息。

===Original===
Methinks I hear in accents low

The sportive kind reply:

Poor moralist! and what art thou?

A solitary fly!

Thy joys no glittering female meets,

No hive hast thou of hoarded sweets,

No painted plumage to display;

On hasty wings thy youth is flown

Thy sun is set, thy spring is gone—

We frolic, while 'tis May.

===Chinese===
我仿佛听见那嬉戏的一族

以低低的语声回答：

可怜的道学家！你又是什么？

一只孤零零的苍蝇！

你的欢乐没有闪光的雌性来分享，

你没有贮存甘甜的蜂巢，

没有彩羽可以炫耀；

凭仓促的翅膀你的青春已飞逝，

你的太阳落了，你的春天去了——

我们且尽欢，趁此五月。

## Satire on the Heads of Houses Or, Never a Barrel the Better Herring / 讽各学院院长 或者说，满桶鲱鱼一个样

===Original===
O Cambridge, attend

To the Satire I've penned

On the Heads of thy Houses,

Thou Seat of the Muses!

Know the Master of Jesus

Does hugely displease us;

The Master of Maudlin

In the same dirt is dawdling;

The Master of Sidney

Is of the same kidney;

The Master of Trinity

To him bears affinity;

As the Master of Keys

Is as like as two peas,

So the Master of Queen's

Is as like as two beans;

The Master of King's

Copies them in all things;

The Master of Catherine

Takes them all for his pattern;

The Master of Clare

Hits them all to a hair;

The Master of Christ

By the rest is enticed;

But the Master of Emmanuel

Follows them like a spaniel;

The Master of Benet

Is of the like tenet;

The Master of Pembroke

Has from them his system took;

The Master of Peter's

Has all the same features;

The Master of St. John's

Like the rest of the Dons.

As to Trinity Hall

We say nothing at all.

===Chinese===
哦剑桥，且听

我写下的这首讽刺诗，

讽的是你的各学院院长，

你这座缪斯的居城！

要知道耶稣学院的院长

大大地叫我们不痛快；

莫德林学院的院长

在同一摊泥里磨蹭；

西德尼学院的院长

与他们一丘之貉；

三一学院的院长

与他气味相投；

而凯斯学院的院长

像得如两颗豌豆；

王后学院的院长

也一样像两颗豆子；

国王学院的院长

事事都照搬他们；

凯瑟琳学院的院长

把他们全当作样板；

克莱尔学院的院长

与他们分毫不差；

基督学院的院长

被其余人勾了魂；

而伊曼纽尔学院的院长

像条西班牙猎犬般跟着他们；

贝内特学院的院长

信奉同样的信条；

彭布罗克学院的院长

那套体系学自他们；

圣彼得学院的院长

眉眼全都一个模子；

圣约翰学院的院长

同其余的学监一样。

至于三一大厅学堂，

我们什么也不讲。

## Song / 歌

===Original===
Thyrsis, when we parted, swore

Ere the spring he would return—

Ah! what means yon violet flower!

And the buds that deck the thorn!

'Twas the lark that upward sprung!

'Twas the nightingale that sang!

===Chinese===
西尔西斯，分别时曾发誓

不待春天他便归来——

啊！那边那朵紫罗兰是什么意思！

还有缀满山楂的嫩芽！

那是云雀直冲上天！

那是夜莺婉转啼鸣！

===Original===
Idle notes! untimely green!

Why this unavailing haste?

Western gales and skies serene

Speak not always winter past.

Cease, my doubts, my fears to move,

Spare the honour of my love.

===Chinese===
无谓的鸟语！不合时宜的青翠！

为何这般徒劳的匆忙？

西来的和风与晴朗的天空

未必总说明冬天已过。

停下吧，别再搅动我的疑惧，

饶过我爱人的名誉。

## Sonnet on the Death of Richard West / 悼理查德·韦斯特之死的十四行诗

===Original===
In vain to me the smiling Mornings shine,

And reddening Phoebus lifts his golden fire;

The birds in vain their amorous descant join;

Or cheerful fields resume their green attire;

These ears, alas! for other notes repine,

A different object do these eyes require;

My lonely anguish melts no heart but mine;

And in my breast the imperfect joys expire.

Yet Morning smiles the busy race to cheer,

And new-born pleasure brings to happier men;

The fields to all their wonted tribute bear;

To warm their little loves the birds complain;

I fruitless mourn to him that cannot hear,

And weep the more because I weep in vain.

At Stoke, Aug., 1742.

===Chinese===
含笑的晨光对我徒然闪耀，

泛红的福玻斯徒然举起他的金焰；

鸟儿们徒然加入恋歌的变奏；

欢畅的田野徒然重新披上绿装；

这对耳朵，唉！正为别的音符哀叹，

这双眼睛渴求的是别样的景象；

我孤独的悲苦只融化我自己的心；

不圆满的欢愉在我胸中熄灭。

清晨仍微笑着为忙碌的人群打气，

为更幸福的人们带来新生的欢愉；

田野向所有人呈上惯常的贡赋；

鸟儿们为温热小小的爱而啼怨；

我向他——他已听不见——徒然哀悼，

且因哭泣无益而愈发哭泣。

书于斯托克，1742 年 8 月。

## Stanzas to Mr. Bentley / 致本特利先生

===Original===
In silent gaze the tuneful choir among,

Half pleased, half blushing, let the Muse admire,

While Bentley leads her sister-art along,

And bids the pencil answer to the lyre.

See, in their course, each transitory thought

Fixed by his touch a lasting essence take;

Each dream, in fancy's airy colouring wrought,

To local symmetry and life awake!

===Chinese===
在和谐的合唱队中间默然凝望，

半是欣喜，半是赧然，任缪斯惊叹，

当本特利携她的姊妹艺术同行，

命画笔应答里拉琴。

看，每个转瞬即逝的思绪在流转中

经他一触便凝成不朽的本质；

每个以想象轻盈色彩织成的梦，

都醒转为具体的匀称与生命！

===Original===
The tardy rhymes that used to linger on,

To censure cold, and negligent of fame,

In swifter measures animated run,

And catch a lustre from his genuine flame.

Ah! could they catch his strength, his easy grace,

His quick creation, his unerring line;

The energy of Pope they might efface,

And Dryden's harmony submit to mine.

===Chinese===
那些惯于迟迟流连的诗行，

冷淡于责难、疏懒于声名，

如今以更轻快的节律欢畅奔流，

并从他真诚的火焰中借得光辉。

啊！但愿它们能得其笔力、他从容的优雅，

他迅捷的创造、他毫厘不爽的线条；

那蒲柏的气力或可被它们抹去，

德莱顿的和声也要向我称臣。

===Original===
But not to one in this benighted age

Is that diviner inspiration given,

That burns in Shakespeare's or in Milton's page,

The pomp and prodigality of heaven.

As, when conspiring in the diamond's blaze,

The meaner gems, that singly charm the sight,

Together dart their intermingled rays,

And dazzle with a luxury of light.

===Chinese===
但在这蒙昧的时代，那种更神圣的

灵感不曾赋予任何一人，

它燃烧在莎士比亚或弥尔顿的书页上，

那是天国的排场与豪奢。

正如，当诸光在钻石的辉焰中合谋，

那些单看已足悦目的次等宝石，

一齐射出交相混杂的光芒，

以光的豪奢炫人眼目。

===Original===
Enough for me, if to some feeling breast

My lines a secret sympathy …

And as their pleasing influence …

A sigh of soft reflection. …

===Chinese===
于我足矣，倘若我的诗行

能把一种隐秘的同情……送抵某颗善感的心

而当它们令人愉悦的感化……

一声轻柔回想的叹息。……

## The Alliance of Education and Government A Fragment / 论教育与政府之同盟 残篇

===Original===
> … πόταγ ̕ ὦ ̕γαθέ· τὰν γὰρ ἀοιδὰν
 Οὕτι πα εἰς Αΐδάν γε τὸν ἐκλελάθοντα φυλαξεῖς.
> — Theocritus, *Id*. I 63

===Chinese===
> ……πόταγ ̕ ὦ ̕γαθέ· τὰν γὰρ ἀοιδὰν（……唱吧，亲爱的；因为歌声）
 Οὕτι πα εἰς Αΐδάν γε τὸν ἐκλελάθοντα φυλαixeῖς.（你带不到哈得斯，那里的人一旦忘却，便无从寻回。）
> ——忒奥克里托斯，《田园诗》I 63

===Original===
Essay I.

As sickly plants betray a niggard earth,

Whose barren bosom starves her generous birth,

Nor genial warmth, nor genial juice retains

Their roots to feed, and fill their verdant veins;

And as in climes, where Winter holds his reign,

The soil, though fertile, will not teem in vain,

Forbids her gems to swell, her shades to rise,

Nor trusts her blossoms to the churlish skies,

So draw mankind in vain the vital airs,

Unformed, unfriended, by those kindly cares,

That health and vigour to the soul impart,

Spread the young thought, and warm the opening heart.

===Chinese===
第一篇。

正如病弱的植物暴露了吝啬的泥土，

它贫瘠的胸膛饿着自己慷慨诞下的生命，

既留不住和暖的温度，也留不住滋养的汁液

去喂养它们的根，注满它们青翠的脉络；

也正如在冬神君临的地带，

土壤纵然肥沃，也不肯白白孕育，

不许芽苞鼓胀，不许绿荫拔高，

不敢把花朵托付给粗暴的天空，

人类也这样徒然呼吸生命的空气，

若缺那些亲切的照料为之成形、与之相伴——

那把健康与活力注入灵魂的照料，

铺展稚嫩的思想，温热初启的心。

===Original===
So fond Instruction on the growing powers

Of Nature idly lavishes her stores,

If equal Justice with unclouded face

Smile not indulgent on the rising race,

And scatter with a free though frugal hand

Light golden showers of plenty o'er the land.

But Tyranny has fixed her empire there,

To check their tender hopes with chilling fear,

And blast the blooming promise of the year.

===Chinese===
溺爱的教化对自然

生长中的力量白白倾洒她的储藏，

倘若平等的正义不以无云的容颜

纵容地微笑看待这成长的世代，

不以自由而节俭的手

把轻盈的金色丰饶之雨撒遍大地。

而暴政已把帝国安扎在那里，

用彻骨的恐惧遏阻他们娇嫩的希望，

并摧折这一年绽放的期许。

===Original===
This spacious animated scene survey

From where the rolling orb, that gives the day,

His sable sons with nearer course surrounds,

To either pole, and life's remotest bounds.

How rude so e'er th' exterior form we find,

Howe'er Opinion tinge the varied mind,

Alike to all the kind impartial Heaven

The sparks of truth and happiness has given;

With sense to feel, with memory to retain,

They follow pleasure, and they fly from pain;

Their judgment mends the plan their fancy draws,

Th' event presages, and explores the cause.

The soft returns of gratitude they know,

By fraud elude, by force repel the foe,

While mutual wishes, mutual woes endear

The social smile, and sympathetic tear.

===Chinese===
请纵览这浩瀚而有生气的舞台，

从那赐予白昼的旋转天球

以较近的轨道环抱其黝黑子民之处，

到两极，到生命最遥远的边界。

无论我们见到怎样粗陋的外形，

无论成见如何染出各异的心智，

仁慈无偏的上天同样把

真理与幸福的火花赐给了所有人；

他们有感官去感觉，有记忆去留存，

他们追逐快乐，逃避痛苦；

判断力修正想象画出的图样，

预断结果，探究原因。

他们懂得感恩的温柔回报，

以智计避开、以强力击退仇敌，

而共同的愿望、共同的苦难

使社交的微笑与同情的泪水愈发亲切。

===Original===
Say then, thro' ages by what fate confined

To different climes seem different souls assigned?

Here measured laws and philosophic ease

Fix and improve the polished arts of peace;

There Industry and Gain their vigils keep,

Command the winds, and tame th' unwilling deep.

Here Force and hardy deeds of blood prevail.

There languid Pleasure sighs in every gale.

Oft o'er the trembling nations from afar

Has Scythia breathed the living cloud of war;

And, where the deluge burst, with sweepy sway

Their arms, their kings, their gods were rolled away.

As oft have issued, host impelling host,

The blue-eyed myriads from the Baltic coast.

The prostrate South to the destroyer yields

Her boasted titles and her golden fields.

With grim delight the brood of Winter view

A brighter day, and heavens of azure hue;

Scent the new fragrance of the breathing rose,

And quaff the pendent vintage as it grows.

===Chinese===
那么说吧，千百年来是何等命运限定，

不同的灵魂被分派到不同的风土？
