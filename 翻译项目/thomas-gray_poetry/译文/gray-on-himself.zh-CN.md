## Gray on Himself Written in 1761, and Found in One of His Pocketbooks / 格雷自题 作于 1761 年，见其一本随身记事簿

===Original===
Too poor for a bribe, and too proud to importune;

He had not the method of making a fortune;

Could love, and could hate, so was thought somewhat odd;

No very great wit, he believed in a God.

A place or a pension he did not desire,

But left church and state to Charles Townshend and Squire.

===Chinese===
穷得没法行贿，又傲得不肯钻营；

他不懂那发财的门径；

会爱，也会恨，便被认为有几分古怪；

才气平平，却相信上帝。

官职或年金他都无所欲求，

把教会与国家留给查尔斯·汤森和乡绅老爷。
