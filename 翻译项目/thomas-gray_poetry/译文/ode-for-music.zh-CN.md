## Ode for Music Performed at the Installation of the Chancellor of the University of Cambridge, 1769 / 音乐颂 一七六九年剑桥大学校长就职典礼演出之作

===Original===
Air

"Hence, avaunt, ('tis holy ground)

Comus, and his midnight crew,

And Ignorance with looks profound,

And dreaming Sloth of pallid hue,

Mad Sedition's cry profane,

Servitude that hugs her chain,

Nor in these consecrated bowers

Let painted Flatt'ry hide her serpent train in flowers."

Chorus

"Nor Envy base, nor creeping Gain,

Dare the Muse's walk to stain,

While bright-eyed Science watches round;

Hence, away, 'tis holy ground!"

===Chinese===
咏叹调

「去吧，滚开，（这里是圣地，）

科穆斯，和他午夜的那一伙，

还有满眼高深的愚昧，

还有面色苍白的酣梦惰怠，

疯狂的煽动那渎神的呼号，

还有紧抱锁链的奴性，

也别让涂脂抹粉的谄媚

在这些圣洁的殿堂里把蛇队列藏进花丛。」

合唱

「卑劣的嫉妒、匍匐的贪利，都

不敢玷污缪斯漫步之处，

因为明眸的科学在四周守望；

去吧，滚开，这里是圣地！」

===Original===
Recitative

From yonder realms of empyrean day

Bursts on my ear th' indignant lay;

There sit the sainted Sage, the Bard divine,

The few, whom Genius gave to shine

Thro' every unborn age, and undiscovered clime.

===Chinese===
宣叙调

从那边最高天的白昼之境，

一支义愤的歌在我耳畔迸发；

那里坐着圣化的智者、神圣的诗人，

那寥寥数人，天才赐他们光辉，

照彻每个未生的世代、未曾发现的地方。

===Original===
Accompanied

Rapt in celestial transport they,

Yet hither oft a glance from high

They send of tender sympathy

To bless the place, where on their opening soul

First the genuine ardor stole.

'Twas Milton struck the deep-toned shell,

And, as the choral warblings round him swell,

Meek Newton's self bends from his state sublime,

And nods his hoary head, and listens to the rhyme.

===Chinese===
伴奏吟唱

他们沉醉于天界的狂喜，

却还常自高处向这里

投来温柔怜惜的一瞥，

祝福这地方——在这里，他们初启的灵魂

第一次悄悄燃起真挚的热忱。

是弥尔顿敲响了低沉的琴，

当合唱的和鸣在他四周涌起，

温驯的牛顿自己也俯下他崇高的尊位，

颔着他霜白的头，倾听这诗韵。

===Original===
Air

"Ye brown o'er-arching groves,

That Contemplation loves,

Where willowy Camus lingers with delight!

Oft at the blush of dawn

I trod your level lawn,

Oft wooed the gleam of Cynthia silver-bright

In cloisters dim, far from the haunts of Folly,

With Freedom by my side, and soft-eyed Melancholy."

===Chinese===
咏叹调

「你们这些苍翠如盖的树丛，

沉思冥想所钟爱的地方，

柳影摇曳的卡穆斯在此流连忘返！

常在黎明的红晕里，

我踏过你们平整的草坪，

常在昏暗的回廊中追求辛西娅银亮的光，

远离愚行出没之境，身边是

自由相伴，还有目含柔光的忧郁。」

===Original===
Recitative

But hark! the portals sound, and pacing forth,

With solemn steps and slow,

High potentates, and dames of royal birth,

And mitred fathers in long order go;

Great Edward, with the lilies on his brow

From haughty Gallia torn,

And sad Chatillon, on her bridal morn

That wept her bleeding Love, and princely Clare,

And Anjou's Heroine, and the paler Rose,

The rival of her crown and of her woes,

And either Henry there,

The murthered saint, and the majestic lord

That broke the bonds of Rome.

===Chinese===
宣叙调

但听！门户訇然作响，缓步走出，

以庄严而缓慢的步履，

显赫的权贵、王族出身的命妇，

还有戴主教冠的教父们鱼贯而行；

伟大的爱德华，额上带着

从高傲的高卢折来的百合，

忧伤的夏蒂隆，在她的婚礼之晨

为她流血的爱人而泣，还有尊贵的克莱尔，

安茹的女杰，还有那朵更苍白的玫瑰——

她王冠与苦难的对手——

两位亨利也在这里，

被害的圣者，和那位雄主，

他挣断了罗马的枷锁。

===Original===
Accompanied

(Their tears, their little triumphs o'er,

Their human passions now no more,

Save Charity, that glows beyond the tomb.)

All that on Granta's fruitful plain

Rich streams of regal bounty poured,

And bad these awful fanes and turrets rise,

To hail their Fitzroy's festal morning come;

And thus they speak in soft accord

The liquid language of the skies:—

===Chinese===
伴奏吟唱

（他们的眼泪、他们细小的凯旋都已过去，

他们人间的情欲已不复存，

唯有仁爱，在坟墓之外仍灼灼生辉。）

所有那些在格兰塔丰饶的平原上

倾注帝王恩泽之洪流的人，

是他们下令竖起这些可畏的殿宇与角塔，

此刻都来迎接他们的菲茨罗伊节庆之晨的来临；

于是他们以柔和的和声，

说著天空流丽的语言：——

===Original===
Quartetto

"What is grandeur, what is power?

Heavier toil, superior pain.

What the bright reward we gain?

The grateful memory of the good.

Sweet is the breath of vernal shower,

The bee's collected treasures sweet,

Sweet music's melting fall, but sweeter yet

The still small voice of gratitude."

===Chinese===
四重唱

「什么是显赫，什么是权力？

是更重的劳苦，更大的伤痛。

我们赢得的光明回报是什么？

是善人们感激的怀念。

春日阵雨的气息甘甜，

蜜蜂采聚的宝藏甘甜，

音乐消融的旋律甘甜，但更甜的

是那宁静微小的感恩之声。」

===Original===
Recitative

Foremost and leaning from her golden cloud

The venerable Margaret see!

"Welcome, my noble son, (she cries aloud)

To this, thy kindred train, and me;

Pleased in thy lineaments we trace

A Tudor's fire, a Beaufort's grace."

===Chinese===
宣叙调

最前面，自她金色的云端俯身，

看那可敬的玛格丽特！

「欢迎你，我高贵的儿子，（她高声喊道，）

来到这里，来到你亲族的行列，来到我身边；

我们欣喜地在你眉宇间辨认出

都铎的火焰、博福特的优雅。」

===Original===
Air

"Thy liberal heart, thy judging eye,

The flower unheeded shall descry,

And bid it round heaven's altars shed

The fragrance of its blushing head;

Shall raise from earth the latent gem

To glitter on the diadem."

===Chinese===
咏叹调

「你宽厚的心，你明鉴的眼，

将发现那无人留意的花朵，

命它在天国的祭坛四周

散吐它红颜的芬芳；

将把深藏的宝石从土中唤起，

让它闪耀在王冠之上。」

===Original===
Recitative

"Lo! Granta waits to lead her blooming band,

Not obvious, not obtrusive, she

No vulgar praise, no venal incense flings;

Nor dares with courtly tongue refined

Profane thy inborn royalty of mind;

She reveres herself and thee.

With modest pride to grace thy youthful brow,

The laureate wreath, that Cecil wore, she brings,

And to thy just, thy gentle hand

Submits the fasces of her sway,

While spirits blest above and men below

Join with glad voice the loud symphonious lay."

===Chinese===
宣叙调

「看哪！格兰塔等着引领她如花的一队前来，

她不张扬，不冒进，

不掷庸俗的颂词，不焚买好的香；

也不敢以雅致的宫廷之舌

亵渎你与生俱来的心灵王者之气；

她敬重她自己，也敬重你。

她怀着谦逊的骄傲，为装点你青春的眉宇，

带来塞西尔戴过的桂冠，

并把她统辖的束棒，

交与你公正而温和的手，

当上界蒙福的精灵与下界的众人

都以欢声汇入这响亮的和谐之歌。」

===Original===
Grand Chorus

"Thro' the wild waves as they roar,

With watchful eye and dauntless mien,

Thy steady course of honour keep,

Nor fear the rocks, nor seek the shore;

The Star of Brunswick smiles serene,

And gilds the horrors of the deep."

===Chinese===
大合唱

「穿过狂野咆哮的波涛，

以警觉的眼、无畏的神色，

守住你荣誉的稳定航向，

莫惧礁石，也莫寻海岸；

不伦瑞克之星安详微笑，

把深渊的恐怖镀上金光。」
