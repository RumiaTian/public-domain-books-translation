## Lines Written at Burnham / 伯纳姆题句

===Original===
And, as they bow their hoary Tops, relate

In murm'ring Sounds the dark Decrees of Fate;

While Visions, as Poetic eyes avow,

Cling to each Leaf and swarm on ev'ry Bough:

===Chinese===
而当它们俯下霜白的树冠，便以

喃喃的低语诉说命运的幽暗敕令；

幻象呢，正如诗人的眼睛所断言，

攀附在每片叶上，群聚在每个枝头：
