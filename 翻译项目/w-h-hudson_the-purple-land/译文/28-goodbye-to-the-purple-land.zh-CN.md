## XXVIII Goodbye to the Purple Land / 第二十八章 别了，紫色国土
===Original===

I was soon back in Montevideo after that. When I bade Demetria goodbye she appeared reluctant to part with me, retaining my hand in hers for an unusual time. For the first time in her life, probably, she was about to be left in the company of entire strangers, and for many days past we had been much to each other, so that it was only natural she should cling to me a little at parting. Once more I pressed her hand and exhorted her to be of good courage, reminding her that in a very few days all trouble and danger would be over; still, however, she did not release my hand. This tender reluctance to lose me was affecting and also flattering, but slightly inopportune, for I was anxious to be in the saddle and away. Presently she said, glancing down at her rusty habiliments, “Richard, if I am to remain concealed here till I go to join you on board, then I must meet your wife in these poor garments.”
“Oh, *that* is what you are thinking about, Demetria!” I exclaimed.
At once I called in our kind hostess, and when this serious matter was explained to her she immediately offered to go to Montevideo to procure the necessary outfit, a thing I had thought nothing about, but which had evidently been preying on Demetria’s mind.
When I at length reached the little suburban retreat of my aunt (by marriage), Paquíta and I acted for some time like two demented persons, so overjoyed were we at meeting after our long separation. I had received no letters from her, and only two or three of the score I had written had reached their destination, so that we had ten thousand questions to ask and answers to make. She could never gaze enough at me or finish admiring my bronzed skin and the respectable moustache I had grown; while she, poor darling! looked unusually pale, yet withal so beautiful that I marvelled at myself for having, after possessing her, considered any other woman even passably good-looking. I gave her a circumstantial account of my adventures, omitting only a few matters I was in honour bound not to disclose.

此后不久，我便轻车熟路折回了蒙得维的亚（Montevideo）。当我向德梅特里亚（Demetria）依依惜别之际，她显得极不舍得同我分开，将我的手紧紧攥在她的掌心之中久久不肯松开。平生大概还是头一遭，她即将被独自留在举目无亲的全然陌生的人家之中，而过去这些天里我们朝夕相处甘苦与共，离别关头她对我难免生出几分依恋不舍，倒也合情合理。我再次紧握了握她的手，温言勉励她务必鼓起勇气，叮嘱她用不了两三天（2到3天）光景一切苦难与凶险都将烟消云散；然而即便如此，她依旧迟迟不肯松手。这份恋恋不舍的深情款款固然教我心头大为感动，亦教我平添了几分自豪，但眼下未免有些不合时宜，皆因我心急如焚迫不及待想要纵马启程进城。片刻之后，她垂首打量着自己周身那一袭破旧褪色的寒酸布裙，轻声细语道：“理查德（Richard），设若我必须在此隐匿避难直到登船同你汇合，那届时我岂不是只能穿着这身寒碜邋遢的旧衣裳去拜见尊夫人了么？”
“哎呀，原来你在为了这档子事犯愁啊，德梅特里亚！”我忍不住哑然失笑失声惊呼起来。
我当即把我们那位热心肠的女东道主唤进屋内，将这桩关乎颜面的重大心事向她交代清楚；女主人听罢毫不含糊，当即自告奋勇应承亲自进一趟蒙得维的亚城，替她置办全套齐整体面的新行头——这等细微关窍我此前压根未曾顾及，然而显而易见，这心结早已在德梅特里亚心头沉甸甸盘踞了良久。
当我终于赶抵我那位（姻亲）姨妈在城郊僻静幽雅的小宅院时，我和帕基塔（Paquíta）简直高兴得宛如两个彻底失心疯的狂人一般，历经这番漫长坎坷的生离死别之后骤然重逢，胸中的狂喜之情简直无可名状。久别多日我未曾收到过她的一字片纸，而我鸿雁传书寄出的二十来封（20来封）厚厚家信里，辗转投递到她手里的也不过两三封（2到3封）罢了，于是我们心头积攒了上万个（10000个）滔滔不绝的问题急待发问，有着千言万语急待倾吐交代。她宛如看不够似的一遍又一遍深情凝视着我的面庞，对我晒成古铜色的黝黑肌肤和我嘴唇上蓄起的一撇威风凛凛的浓密胡髭赞不绝口；而我可怜的小心肝娇妻呢，面容虽透着少有的苍白柔弱，却依旧娇艳绝伦不可方物，教我暗自惊诧错愕：在坐拥这等倾城绝色之后，漫游途中我怎会一度觉着其他凡俗女子哪怕称得上姿色尚可呢。我将自己这一路的离奇历险巨细靡遗向她原原本本倾囊相告，唯独隐瞒过滤掉了几桩恪守骑士荣誉而断不可对外泄露的闺阁私密。

Thus, when I told her the story of my sojourn at the estancia Peralta, I said nothing to betray Demetria’s confidence; nor did I think it necessary to mention the episode of that wicked little sprite, Cleta; with the result that she was pleased at the chivalrous conduct I had displayed throughout the whole of that affair, and was ready to take Demetria to her heart.
I had not been back twenty-four hours in Montevideo before a letter from the Lomas de Rocha storekeeper came to justify my caution in having left Demetria at some distance from the town. The letter informed me that Don Hilario had quickly guessed that I had carried off his unhappy master’s daughter, and that no doubt was left in his mind when he discovered that, on the day I left the estancia, a person answering to my description in every particular had purchased a horse and sidesaddle and had ridden off towards the estancia in the evening. My correspondent warned me that Don Hilario would be in Montevideo even before his letter, also that he had discovered something about my connection with the late rebellion, and would be sure to place the matter in the hands of the government, so as to have me arrested, after which he would have little difficulty in compelling Demetria to return to the estancia.
For a moment this intelligence dismayed me. Luckily, Paquíta was out of the house when it came, and fearing that she might return and surprise me while I was in that troubled state, I rushed out; then, skulking through back streets and narrow lanes, peering cautiously about in fear of encountering the minions of the law, I made my escape out of the town. My only desire just then was to get away into some place of safety where I would be able to think over the position quietly, and if possible devise some plan to defeat Don Hilario, who had been a little too quick for me. Of many schemes that suggested themselves to my mind, while I sat in the shade of a cactus hedge about a mile from town, I finally determined, in accordance with my old and well-tried rule, to adopt the boldest one, which was to go straight back to Montevideo and claim the protection of my country. The only trouble was that on my way thither I might be caught, and then Paquíta would be in terrible distress about me, and perhaps Demetria’s escape would be prevented.

因此，当我向她娓娓道来我在佩拉尔塔大庄园（estancia Peralta）做客借宿的奇闻轶事之际，我守口如瓶，未曾吐露半句有违德梅特里亚信任托付的话语；同样我也觉着压根毫无必要向她招认关于克莱塔（Cleta）那个刁钻古怪小妖精的荒唐小插曲；结果帕基塔对我在这场风波里自始至终所展现出的堂堂骑士君子操守大为欢喜赞赏，甚至迫不及待张开双臂要将德梅特里亚拥入怀中视作亲姐妹相待。
然而我返回蒙得维的亚还不足二十四小时（24小时），罗查丘陵（Lomas de Rocha）杂货铺掌柜飞鸽传书送来的一封急信，便全然印证了我将德梅特里亚安置在远离城郭的乡野是何等明智周全的审慎之举。信中火急火燎通报我：唐·希拉里奥（Don Hilario）早已心明眼亮，猜出正是我横插一杠、强行拐跑了他那位失心疯老东家的千金小姐；而当他查实就在我告辞离去的那一天，一个与我体貌特征丝毫不差的英挺汉子在左近买下了一匹骏马和一具女式侧鞍、且在夜半三更并辔策马直奔庄园折返之际，他心头最后一丝疑窦便彻底荡然无存了。我那位通报信讯的挚友严厉告诫我：唐·希拉里奥脚程极快，甚至可能抢在这封密信送达之前便已然杀入了蒙得维的亚城内，不仅如此，这恶棍还设法刺探掌握了我曾卷入近期白党叛乱的风声把柄，必定会毫不留情将这些罪证呈递给官府衙门，借官军之手将我缉拿归案投入大牢，随后他便能顺理成章、毫不费力地威逼胁迫德梅特里亚乖乖屈从跟他折返老庄园去了。
初初闻听这等大祸临头的凶讯，一时间真教我心惊胆战乱了方寸。万幸的是，信使叩门之际帕基塔恰好外出未在寓所，唯恐她折返回家撞见我这副心惊肉跳的惶恐失态模样，我当机立断拔腿冲出门去；随后我鬼祟地穿行在荒僻狭窄的偏僻后街与阴暗小巷之中，战战兢兢四下张望警惕，唯恐迎头撞上荷枪实弹的官府鹰犬爪牙，一路仓皇逃出了城门关隘。那一刻我心头唯一的执念，便是尽快遁入一处稳妥安全的绝密避难所，好教我平心静气从容权衡眼下的危局，竭尽所能谋划出一条挫败反制唐·希拉里奥阴谋的制胜良策——这个阴险的家伙显然比我料想的要机敏老辣得多。正当我蜷缩在距离城池约莫一英里（1英里）开外的一处仙人掌树篱阴凉下沉思之际，脑海中走马灯般闪现过无数条应对方案，最终依照我平生久经沙场考验的一贯铁律，我毅然决然采纳了其中最为大胆冒险的一条绝策——那便是光明正大昂首阔步折回蒙得维的亚城，公开向大英帝国领事馆寻求我堂堂母邦的官方庇护。唯一的凶险顾虑在于，设若在进城的半道上不幸被巡捕捕获就擒，帕基塔必将陷入万劫不复的绝望痛苦之中，而德梅特里亚的潜逃大计也将随之彻底功亏一篑。

While I was occupied with these thoughts I saw a closed carriage pass by, driven towards the town by a tipsy-looking coachman. Coming out of my hiding-place, I managed to stop him and offered him two dollars to drive me to the British Consulate. The carriage was a private one, but the two dollars tempted the man, so after securing the fare in advance, he allowed me to get in, and then I closed the windows, leant back on the cushion, and was driven rapidly and comfortably to the house of refuge. I introduced myself to the Consul, and told him a story concocted for the occasion, a judicious mixture of truth and lies, to the effect that I had been unlawfully and forcibly seized and compelled to serve in the Blanco army, and that, having escaped from the rebels and made my way to Montevideo, I was amazed to hear that the government proposed arresting me. He asked me a few questions, looked at the passport which he had sent me a few days before, then, laughing good-humouredly, put on his hat and invited me to accompany him to the War Office close by. The secretary, Colonel Arocena, he informed me, was a personal friend of his, and if we could see him it would be all right. Walking by his side I felt quite safe and bold again, for I was, in a sense, walking with my hand resting on the superb mane of the British Lion, whose roar was not to be provoked with impunity. At the War Office I was introduced by the Consul to his friend, Colonel Arocena, a genial old gentleman with a bald head and a cigarette between his lips. He listened with some interest and a smile, slightly incredulous I thought, to the sad story of the ill-treatment I had been subjected to at the hands of Santa Coloma’s rebellious rascals. When I had finished he pushed over a sheet of paper on which he had scrawled a few words to me, with the remark, “Here, my young friend, take this, and you will be safe in Montevideo. We have heard about your doings in Florida, also in Rocha, but we do not propose going to war with England on your account.”
At this speech we all laughed; then when I had pocketed the paper, which bore the sacred seal of the War Office on the margin and requested all persons to refrain from molesting the bearer in his lawful outgoings and incomings, we thanked the pleasant old Colonel and retired. I spent half an hour strolling about with the Consul, then we separated.

听闻这番幽默风趣的机锋对白，屋内的众人全都忍不住哄堂大笑起来；随后我将那份加盖着陆军部神圣朱印大印、严令各方军警宵小在本人一切合法往来出入关隘之际严禁擅自骚扰羁押的特赦公文小心揣入怀中，向这位慈祥风趣的老上校千恩万谢辞行告退。我陪同领事先生在阳光明媚的林荫大道上悠然踱步闲逛了半个小时（0.5小时），随后彼此握手作别。

I had noticed two men in military uniform at some distance from us when we were together, and now, returning homewards, I found that they were following me. By and by they overtook me, and politely intimated their intention of making me their prisoner. I smiled, and, drawing forth my protection from the War Office, handed it to them. They looked surprised, and gave it back, with an apology for having molested me, then left me to pursue my way in peace.
I had, of course, been very lucky throughout all this adventure; still, I did not wish to attribute my easy escape entirely to luck, for I had, I thought, contributed a good deal towards it by my promptness in acting and in inventing a plausible story on the spur of the moment.
Feeling very much elated, I strolled along the sunny streets, gaily swinging my cane, when, turning a corner near Doña Isidora’s house, I suddenly came face to face with Don Hilario. This unexpected encounter threw us both off our guard, he recoiling two or three paces backward and turning as pale as the nature of his complexion would allow. I recovered first from the shock. So far I had been able to baffle him, and knew, moreover, many things of which he was ignorant; still, he was there in the town with me and had to be reckoned with, and I quickly resolved to meet him as a friend, affecting entire ignorance of his object in coming to Montevideo.
“Don Hilario﻿—you here! Happy the eyes that behold you,” I exclaimed, seizing and shaking his hand, pretending to be overjoyed at the meeting.
In a moment he recovered his usual self-possessed manner, and when I asked after Doña Demetria he answered after a moments hesitation that she was in very good health.
“Come, Don Hilario,” I said, “we are close to my aunt Isidora’s house, where I am staying, and it will give me great pleasure to present you to my wife, who will be glad to thank you for your kindness to me at the estancia.”
“Your wife, Don Ricardo! Do you tell me that you are married?” he exclaimed in amazement, thinking probably that I was already the husband of Demetria.
“What, did I not tell you before!” I said. “Ah, I remember speaking to Doña Demetria about it. Strange that she has not mentioned it to you. Yes, I was married before coming to this country﻿—my wife is an Argentine. Come with me and you shall see a beautiful woman, if that is an inducement.”

当我们并肩同行之际，我便早已警觉地留心到不远处的街角处戳立着两名身着军服甲胄的官军密探，眼下我独自掉头折返寓所，果然察觉到这两个尾巴正不紧不慢紧随尾行在我的身后。不多时他们便快步追赶了上来，彬彬有礼却不容置疑地宣布要将我逮捕缉拿归案。我嘴角泛起一抹从容浅笑，从怀中摸出那份热气腾腾的陆军部特赦公文，气定神闲递到了他们的眼皮底下。二人定睛一瞧大惊失色，连忙毕恭毕敬双手将公文奉还，因平白冒犯了上谕而连连鞠躬赔罪告饶，随后便灰溜溜闪身退去，由得我在大街上大摇大摆安稳前行。
整场历险之中，我固然可谓吉星高照洪福齐天；然而我却绝不愿将这番顺风顺水的全身而退全然归功于瞎猫碰上死耗子的虚妄运气，皆因我深知，这主要得益于我当机立断的凌厉行动魄力，以及在千钧一发之际随机应变编织出天衣无缝说辞的过人机智。

这样一种所谓至善纯洁的僵死国度，是违背天理大自然法则的，是令人作呕、窒息反胃的无间炼狱：对于一颗生机勃勃、意气风发且血肉丰满的健康心灵而言，哪怕万劫不复长眠于毫无梦魇的冰冷荒坟黄土之下，也远比苟活在那样一种行尸走肉般的所谓乌托邦圣境里要舒坦惬意一千倍。设若那位凭借着对大自然玄妙造化奥秘的通天彻地智慧而长生不老的高登西奥·迪·卢卡先生（Signor Gaudentio di Lucca），此刻当真奇迹般显圣降临在这座山巅之上矗立在我面前，郑重其事昭告我他在中非（Central Africa）腹地所隐居修行的那个所谓神圣纯洁无瑕的世外桃源并非虚妄幻梦，且盛情相邀亲自引渡我前往那方神圣乐土，我也定会毫不犹豫拱手婉拒他的美意。我宁愿生生世世扎根滞留在乌拉圭东岸（Banda Orientál）这片泥泞野蛮的热土旷野之上，哪怕任由这方水土的狂暴风霜最终将我同化得同此地任何一个亡命徒一般凶悍桀骜，哪怕落得有朝一日也不惜“踩着尸山血海的滔滔杀戮”一路搏杀问鼎至高无上的总统宝座也在所不惜。皆因即便在我自己的母邦英格兰故土——那个平心而论远算不上如古秘鲁亦或中非波法尔（Pophar）圣境那般尽善尽美的人间帝国——漫长的工业文明早已将我与大自然母亲割裂阻隔得太久太久；而如今恰恰是在这片东岸国度的大地之上，在这片其政治罪愆污点在纯洁傲慢的英格兰与虚伪肮脏的巴西帝国眼中同样沦为荒唐丑闻笑柄的狂放蛮荒热土之上，我终于同生我养我的大自然母亲重修旧好、水乳交融重新紧紧相拥在一处。正因如此，纵然她千般不是万种缺陷，我依旧毫无保留深沉挚爱着她周身的一切。在此刻，且让我效仿当年的圣科洛马（Santa Coloma），双膝跪倒在山巅的大地之上，神圣地亲吻脚下这块坚硬的岩石，就宛如初生的赤子婴儿贪婪亲吻着赐予他生命琼浆的母亲甘甜乳房一般；在此刻，且让我摒弃一切对于尘垢泥泞的虚伪洁癖，效仿狂放不羁的约翰·卡里克弗格斯（John Carrickfergus），将我的一双肉掌深深插进脚下松软肥沃的深褐沃土之中，去紧紧扣合握紧久别重逢的大自然慈母那双温暖有力的粗粝大手。
别了，在这暴风骤雨与灿烂艳阳交织翻腾的绝美造化热土，在这一半是圣洁美德、一半是滔天罪孽的传奇国度！但愿未来一切胆敢踏足侵犯这片疆土的狂妄侵略者，其下场皆如过往那些折戟沉沙的折翼旧敌一般，到头来唯有丢盔弃甲，留得这片土地上的儿女自主打理掌控自身的造化宿命；但愿圣科洛马那铭刻在骨髓里的豪迈骑士侠骨，多洛雷斯那一腔焚身熔铁的炽烈奔放深情，以及坎德拉里亚（Candelaria）那润物细无声的博大仁爱温存，能够生生世世在你们代代相传的子孙后代血脉中奔涌流淌，以无上的罗曼蒂克与绝代之美点亮照耀他们的壮美人生；但愿我们那自诩高人一等的傲慢外邦文明毒瘤，永无机会玷污摧残你们在原野上自由绽放的烂漫山花，亦或将我们那冰冷所谓的沉重文明枷锁套在你们的高乔牧羊汉子脖颈之上——那群如苍穹飞鸟般无拘无束、优雅洒脱且痴迷乐韵长歌的旷野赤子——断断莫要将他们规训改造成旧大陆那群神情麻木阴郁、摇尾乞怜的可悲农奴苦力！
