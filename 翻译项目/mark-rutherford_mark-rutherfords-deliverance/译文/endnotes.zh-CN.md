## Endnotes / 尾注

===Original===
1. This was written many years ago, but is curiously pertinent to the discussions of this year. —﻿Editor, 1884 ↩︎

===Chinese===
1. 此文写于多年以前，却与今年的种种讨论出奇地切题。——编者，1884 ↩︎

===Original===
2. Not exactly untrue, but it sounds strangely now when socialism, nationalisation of the land, and other projects have renewed in men the hope of regeneration by political processes. The reader will, however, please remember the date of these memoirs. —﻿Editor, 1884 ↩︎

===Chinese===
2. 说它全然不对倒也不是，只是如今听来颇为奇异——如今，社会主义、土地国有化以及其他种种方案，已重新唤起人们借政治程序获得新生的希望。不过，请读者记住这些回忆录的写作年代。——编者，1884 ↩︎
