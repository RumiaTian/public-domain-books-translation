## Looking-Glass River / 镜面河

===Original===
Smooth it slides upon its travel,
Here a wimple, there a gleam—
O the clean gravel!
O the smooth stream!

Sailing blossoms, silver fishes,
Paven pools as clear as air—
How a child wishes
To live down there!

We can see our coloured faces
Floating on the shaken pool
Down in cool places,
Dim and very cool;

Till a wind or water wrinkle,
Dipping marten, plumping trout,
Spreads in a twinkle
And blots all out.

See the rings pursue each other;
All below grows black as night,
Just as if mother
Had blown out the light!

Patience, children, just a minute—
See the spreading circles die;
The stream and all in it
Will clear by-and-by.
===Chinese===
河水悠悠向前流淌，
这儿泛起微波，那儿闪一道光——
啊，多么干净的卵石！
啊，多么平顺的河面！

飘浮的花瓣，银色的小鱼，
铺底的水潭清澈得像空气——
小孩子多么向往
能住到那底下去！

我们看得见自己彩色的脸庞
漂浮在荡漾的水潭上
在那凉凉的深处，
朦胧而又清凉；

直到一阵风或一道水纹，
潜水的燕子、扑跃的鳟鱼，
一眨眼把它揉皱
又把一切都抹掉。

看那一圈圈水环彼此追逐；
水底的一切变黑得像夜晚，
就仿佛是妈妈
把灯吹灭了一般！

孩子们，再耐心等一会儿——
看那一圈圈波纹正慢慢消散；
这条河，还有河里的一切，
不一会儿又会变清。

## From a Railway Carriage / 火车车厢里

===Original===
Faster than fairies, faster than witches,
Bridges and houses, hedges and ditches;
And charging along like troops in a battle,
All through the meadows the horses and cattle:
All of the sights of the hill and the plain
Fly as thick as driving rain;
And ever again, in the wink of an eye,
Painted stations whistle by.

Here is a child who clambers and scrambles,
All by himself and gathering brambles;

Here is a tramp who stands and gazes;
And there is the green for stringing the daisies!

Here is a cart run away in the road
Lumping along with man and load;
And here is a mill, and there is a river:
Each a glimpse and gone forever!
===Chinese===
比仙子还快，比女巫还快，
桥梁和房屋，篱笆和水沟；
又像战场上的大军冲锋，
草地上到处是牛群马群：
山岗和平原上所有的景象
像暴雨一样密密地飞过；
一次又一次，一眨眼的工夫，
漆着彩色的车站呼啸而过。

这儿有个孩子爬上又爬下，
独自一个，在摘那野莓的刺藤；

这儿有个流浪汉站着发呆；
那边是一片草地，好串起雏菊花环！

这儿有辆大车在路上失控逃窜，
连人带货一路颠颠簸簸；
这边是一座磨坊，那边是一条河：
每一眼都只一瞥，便永远不见！

## Winter-Time / 冬日

===Original===
Late lies the wintry sun a-bed,
A frosty, fiery sleepy-head;
Blinks but an hour or two; and then,
A blood-red orange, sets again.

Before the stars have left the skies,
At morning in the dark I rise;
And shivering in my nakedness,
By the cold candle, bathe and dress.

Close by the jolly fire I sit
To warm my frozen bones a bit;
Or with a reindeer-sled explore
The colder countries round the door.

When, to go out, my nurse doth wrap
Me in my comforter and cap,
The cold wind burns my face, and blows
Its frosty pepper up my nose.

Black are my steps on silver sod;
Thick blows my frosty breath abroad;
And tree and house, and hill and lake,
Are frosted like a wedding-cake.
===Chinese===
冬天的太阳赖着不起床，
一个结着霜、红通通的瞌睡虫；
眨一两个钟头的眼；然后，
像一个血红的橘子，又落了下去。

星星还没离开天空，
清早天没亮我就起了床；
我光着身子，冷得打哆嗦，
就着冰冷的蜡烛，洗澡、穿衣。

我挨着欢快的炉火坐着，
把我冻僵的骨头暖一暖；
要不就驾着驯鹿雪橇去探险，
去探访门外更冷的地方。

等到要出门，保姆就给我
裹上围巾、戴好帽子，
冷风烧灼着我的脸，又把
那霜做的胡椒粉吹进我鼻子。

在银色的草地上，我的脚印黑黑；
我呼出的霜气一股股地往外冒；
那树和房子，那山和湖，
全结了霜，就像一只结婚蛋糕。

## The Hayloft / 干草阁

===Original===
Through all the pleasant meadow-side
The grass grew shoulder-high,
Till the shining scythes went far and wide
And cut it down to dry.

These green and sweetly smelling crops
They led in wagons home;
And they piled them here in mountain tops
For mountaineers to roam.

Here is Mount Clear, Mount Rusty-Nail,
Mount Eagle and Mount High;—
The mice that in these mountains dwell
No happier are than I!

O what a joy to clamber there,
O what a place for play,
With the sweet, the dim, the dusty air,
The happy hills of hay.
===Chinese===
在那整片宜人的草坡上，
草长得齐肩高，
直到亮闪闪的镰刀四处挥舞，
把它割倒晒干。

这些绿油油、香喷喷的庄稼，
被装在大车上拉回了家；
在这儿堆成了一座座山头，
好让登山家们来攀爬。

这儿是"清山"、"锈钉山"、
"鹰山"和"高山"；——
住在这群山里的小老鼠，
也不见得比我更快乐！

啊，在那儿攀爬多开心，
啊，那是个多好的玩耍地，
空气又香、又暗、又带着尘，
那些快活的干草堆成的山。

## Farewell to the Farm / 别了，农庄

===Original===
The coach is at the door at last;
The eager children, mounting fast
And kissing hands, in chorus sing:
Goodbye, goodbye, to everything!

To house and garden, field and lawn,
The meadow-gates we swang upon,
To pump and stable, tree and swing,
Goodbye, goodbye, to everything!

And fare you well forevermore,
O ladder at the hayloft door,
O hayloft where the cobwebs cling,
Goodbye, goodbye, to everything!

Crack goes the whip, and off we go;
The trees and houses smaller grow;
Last, round the woody turn we swing:
Goodbye, goodbye, to everything!
===Chinese===
马车终于停在了门口；
急切的孩子，飞快地爬上车，
挥着小手，齐声唱起来：
再见啦，再见啦，所有的一切！

再见了房子和花园、田野和草坪，
再见了我们荡来荡去的草场栅门，
再见了水泵和马厩、大树和秋千，
再见啦，再见啦，所有的一切！

永远地别了啊，
干草阁门前的那架梯子，
挂着蜘蛛网的干草阁，
再见啦，再见啦，所有的一切！

鞭子啪的一响，我们出发了；
树和房子变得越来越小；
最后，我们绕过那树木茂密的拐角：
再见啦，再见啦，所有的一切！

## North-West Passage / 西北航程

### I / 一

#### Good Night / 晚安

===Original===
When the bright lamp is carried in,
The sunless hours again begin;
O'er all without, in field and lane,
The haunted night returns again.

Now we behold the embers flee
About the firelit hearth; and see
Our faces painted as we pass,
Like pictures, on the window-glass.

Must we to bed indeed? Well then,
Let us arise and go like men,
And face with an undaunted tread
The long black passage up to bed.

Farewell, O brother, sister, sire!
O pleasant party round the fire!
The songs you sing, the tales you tell,
Till far tomorrow, fare ye well!
===Chinese===
明亮的灯端进屋里的时候，
没有太阳的时光又重新开始；
在外面，在田野和小径上，
鬼魅出没的黑夜又回来了。

此刻我们看见余烬四散逃窜，
在炉火照亮的壁炉边；又看见
我们走过时，自己的脸
像画像一样，映在窗玻璃上。

我们真非得上床不可吗？那么好吧，
让我们像男子汉一样站起身、走出去，
迈着无所畏惧的步子，
面对那条通往床边的又长又黑的走道。

别了，我的兄弟、姐妹、父亲！
围在炉火边的快乐伙伴！
你们唱的歌、你们讲的故事，
在遥远的明天到来之前，且好好道别吧！

### II / 二

#### Shadow March / 影子行军

===Original===
All round the house is the jet-black night;
It stares through the window-pane;
It crawls in the corners, hiding from the light,
And it moves with the moving flame.

Now my little heart goes a-beating like a drum,
With the breath of the Bogie in my hair;
And all round the candle the crooked shadows come
And go marching along up the stair.

The shadow of the balusters, the shadow of the lamp,
The shadow of the child that goes to bed—
All the wicked shadows coming, tramp, tramp, tramp,
With the black night overhead.
===Chinese===
屋子四周是漆黑的夜；
它隔着窗玻璃往里张望；
它在角落里爬行，躲着光，
又随着晃动的火苗而移动。

此刻我小小的心儿咚咚跳得像擂鼓，
觉得妖怪的鼻息就喷在我头发上；
蜡烛四周，那些弯弯曲曲的影子走来，
一路列队行军，上了楼梯。

栏杆的影子，灯的影子，
那个去上床的孩子的影子——
所有那些邪恶的影子走来了，嚓、嚓、嚓，
头顶上是黑沉沉的夜。

### III / 三

#### In Port / 入港

===Original===
Last, to the chamber where I lie
My fearful footsteps patter nigh,
And come from out the cold and gloom
Into my warm and cheerful room.

There, safe arrived, we turned about
To keep the coming shadows out,
And close the happy door at last
On all the perils that we passed.

Then, when mamma goes by to bed,
She shall come in with tip-toe tread,
And see me lying warm and fast
And in the land of Nod at last.
===Chinese===
最后，我那害怕的脚步踢踢踏踏，
走近我躺卧的卧房，
从那又冷又阴的地方，
走进我那温暖明亮的房间。

到了那儿，安全到达，我们转过身，
把追来的影子挡在门外，
终于把这扇快乐的房门关上，
把一路上经过的险情都关在外面。

然后，等妈妈路过去就寝，
她会踮着脚尖走进来，
看见我暖暖和和地躺得死死的，
终于到了瞌睡乡里头。

## The Unseen Playmate / 看不见的玩伴

===Original===
When children are playing alone on the green,
In comes the playmate that never was seen.
When children are happy and lonely and good,
The Friend of the Children comes out of the wood.

Nobody heard him and nobody saw,
His is a picture you never could draw,
But he's sure to be present, abroad or at home,
When children are happy and playing alone.

He lies in the laurels, he runs on the grass,
He sings when you tinkle the musical glass;
Whene'er you are happy and cannot tell why,
The Friend of the Children is sure to be by!

He loves to be little, he hates to be big,
'Tis he that inhabits the caves that you dig;
'Tis he when you play with your soldiers of tin
That sides with the Frenchmen and never can win.

'Tis he, when at night you go off to your bed,
Bids you go to your sleep and not trouble your head;
For wherever they're lying, in cupboard or shelf,
'Tis he will take care of your playthings himself!
===Chinese===
孩子们独自在草地上玩耍的时候，
那位从未露面的玩伴就走了进来。
当孩子们快乐、孤单又乖巧的时候，
孩子们的朋友就从树林里出来。

没有人听见过他，也没有人看见过他，
他的模样是一幅你永远画不出的画，
可他准会到场，无论是在户外还是在家，
只要孩子们快乐地独自玩耍。

他躺在月桂树丛里，他在草地上奔跑，
你敲响会唱歌的玻璃杯，他就放声高歌；
每当你快乐得说不出是为什么，
孩子们的朋友就一定在你身侧！

他喜欢自己小小的样子，讨厌长大，
你挖的那些洞穴，住进去的就是他；
当你玩起你的锡兵玩具，
总是站在法国人那边、永远赢不了的，也是他。

就是他，夜里你去上床的时候，
叫你快去睡，别再胡思乱想；
因为不管你的玩具躺在哪儿，碗橱里还是架子上，
都是他亲自替你照看你的宝贝！

## My Ship and I / 我的小船和我

===Original===
O it's I that am the captain of a tidy little ship,
Of a ship that goes a-sailing on the pond;
And my ship it keeps a-turning all around and all about;
But when I'm a little older, I shall find the secret out
How to send my vessel sailing on beyond.

For I mean to grow as little as the dolly at the helm,
And the dolly I intend to come alive;
And with him beside to help me, it's a-sailing I shall go,
It's a-sailing on the water, when the jolly breezes blow
And the vessel goes a divie-divie-dive.

O it's then you'll see me sailing through the rushes and the reeds,
And you'll hear the water singing at the prow;
For beside the dolly sailor, I'm to voyage and explore,
To land upon the island where no dolly was before,
And to fire the penny cannon in the bow.
===Chinese===
啊，我就是一艘漂亮小船的船长，
这艘船呀，在池塘上扬帆远航；
我的小船老是转来转去、兜着圈子；
可等我再长大一点，我就能弄清这个秘密——
怎么让我的小船一直驶向前方。

因为我打算变得像舵手娃娃那样小，
我还打算让那娃娃活过来；
有他在旁边帮我，我就可以扬帆起航，
在水面上扬帆远航，当快活的风儿吹起，
小船就一蹦一蹦地往下栽。

啊，到那时你就会看见我穿过灯心和芦苇，
你就会听见水在船头唱歌；
因为在那娃娃水手身旁，我要去远航、去探险，
登上一个娃娃从未到过的岛屿，
再从船头放响那一分钱的小炮。

## My Kingdom / 我的王国

===Original===
Down by a shining water well
I found a very little dell,
No higher than my head.
The heather and the gorse about
In summer bloom were coming out,
Some yellow and some red.

I called the little pool a sea;
The little hills were big to me;
For I am very small.
I made a boat, I made a town,
I searched the caverns up and down,
And named them one and all.

And all about was mine, I said,
The little sparrows overhead,
The little minnows too.
This was the world, and I was king;
For me the bees came by to sing,
For me the swallows flew.

I played there were no deeper seas,
Nor any wider plains than these,
Nor other kings than me.
At last I heard my mother call
Out from the house at even-fall,
To call me home to tea.

And I must rise and leave my dell,
And leave my dimpled water well,
And leave my heather blooms.
Alas! and as my home I neared,
How very big my nurse appeared,
How great and cool the rooms!
===Chinese===
在一口波光闪闪的水井旁，
我发现了一个很小很小的山谷，
还没有我的头那么高。
四周的石楠花和荆豆，
在夏天正开着花，
有些黄，有些红。

我把这小水洼叫做大海；
那些小山丘，在我眼里很大；
因为我实在很小。
我做了一只船，我建了一座城，
我把那些洞穴上上下下搜了个遍，
把它们一个不落地都起了名字。

我说，这一带都是我的，
头顶上的那些小麻雀，
还有那些小鱼苗。
这就是世界，而我是国王；
蜜蜂是特意为我飞来唱歌，
燕子也是为我飞翔。

我玩着，假装没有更深的海洋，
也没有比这更宽广的平原，
更没有别的国王，只有我一个。
终于，我听见妈妈在呼唤，
那是黄昏时分，从屋子里传出来，
叫我回家喝茶去。

我只好站起来，离开我的山谷，
离开我那带着小酒窝的水井，
离开我的石楠花。
哎呀！当我快走到家门口，
保姆看起来多么高大，
那些房间多么宽敞、多么凉快！

## Picture-Books in Winter / 冬日的图画书

===Original===
Summer fading, winter comes—
Frosty mornings, tingling thumbs,
Window robins, winter rooks,
And the picture story-books.

Water now is turned to stone
Nurse and I can walk upon;
Still we find the flowing brooks
In the picture story-books.

All the pretty things put by
Wait upon the children's eye,
Sheep and shepherds, trees and crooks,
In the picture story-books.

We may see how all things are,
Seas and cities, near and far,
And the flying fairies' looks,
In the picture story-books.

How am I to sing your praise,
Happy chimney-corner days,
Sitting safe in nursery nooks,
Reading picture story-books?
===Chinese===
夏天消退了，冬天来到——
结霜的早晨，冻麻的拇指，
窗台上的知更鸟，冬日里的秃鼻鸦，
还有那些图画故事书。

如今水都变成了石头，
保姆和我都能在上面走；
我们却仍能找到潺潺的小溪，
就在那些图画故事书里。

收起来的所有好玩意儿，
都在等候孩子们的眼睛，
绵羊和牧羊人、树木和牧杖，
就在那些图画故事书里。

我们可以看见万物是什么模样，
海洋和城市，无论远近，
还有那飞舞的仙子们的容颜，
就在那些图画故事书里。

我要怎样才能歌颂你们呢，
那些围着炉角的快乐时光，
安安稳稳坐在育婴室的角落里，
读着图画故事书？

## My Treasures / 我的宝贝

===Original===
These nuts, that I keep in the back of the nest
Where all my lead soldiers are lying at rest,
Were gathered in autumn by nursie and me
In a wood with a well by the side of the sea.

This whistle we made (and how clearly it sounds!)
By the side of a field at the end of the grounds.
Of a branch of a plane, with a knife of my own,
It was nursie who made it, and nursie alone!

The stone, with the white and the yellow and grey,
We discovered I cannot tell *how* far away;
And I carried it back, although weary and cold,
For, though father denies it, I'm sure it is gold.

But of all of my treasures the last is the king,
For there's very few children possess such a thing;
And that is a chisel, both handle and blade,
Which a man who was really a carpenter made.
===Chinese===
这些坚果，我藏在窝的里头，
我的铅兵们都在那儿好好地躺着，
是秋天里保姆和我一起捡来的，
就在海边一口井旁的树林里。

这只哨子，是我们做的（吹起来多响亮！）
就在场地尽头那片田边做的。
用的是一截梧桐树枝，用我自己的小刀，
可全是保姆一个人做的，就保姆一个！

这块石头，带着白、黄、灰三种颜色，
是在多远的地方发现的，我说*不上来*；
我把它背了回来，虽然又累又冷，
因为虽说爸爸不承认，我肯定它是金子。

但我所有的宝贝里，最后这一件是国王，
因为很少有孩子能拥有这样的东西；
那是一把凿子，把手和刀刃俱全，
是一个真正的木匠亲手做的。

## Block City / 积木城

===Original===
What are you able to build with your blocks?
Castles and palaces, temples and docks.
Rain may keep raining, and others go roam,
But I can be happy and building at home.

Let the sofa be mountains, the carpet be sea,
There I'll establish a city for me:
A kirk and a mill and a palace beside,
And a harbour as well where my vessels may ride.

Great is the palace with pillar and wall,
A sort of a tower on the top of it all,
And steps coming down in an orderly way
To where my toy vessels lie safe in the bay.

This one is sailing and that one is moored:
Hark to the song of the sailors on board!
And see, on the steps of my palace, the kings
Coming and going with presents and things!

Now I have done with it, down let it go!
All in a moment the town is laid low.
Block upon block lying scattered and free,
What is there left of my town by the sea?

Yet, as I saw it, I see it again,
The kirk and the palace, the ships and the men,
And as long as I live, and where'er I may be,
I'll always remember my town by the sea.

## The Land of Story-Books / 故事书的国度

===Original===
At evening when the lamp is lit,
Around the fire my parents sit;
They sit at home and talk and sing,
And do not play at anything.

Now, with my little gun, I crawl
All in the dark along the wall,
And follow round the forest track
Away behind the sofa back.

There, in the night, where none can spy,
All in my hunter's camp I lie,
And play at books that I have read
Till it is time to go to bed.

These are the hills, these are the woods,
These are my starry solitudes;
And there the river by whose brink
The roaring lions come to drink.

I see the others far away
As if in firelit camp they lay,
And I, like to an Indian scout,
Around their party prowled about

So, when my nurse comes in for me,
Home I return across the sea,
And go to bed with backward looks
At my dear land of Story-books.
