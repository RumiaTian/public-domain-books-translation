## System / 规矩

===Original===
Every night my prayers I say,
And get my dinner every day;

And every day that I've been good,
I get an orange after food.

The child that is not clean and neat,
With lots of toys and things to eat,

He is a naughty child, I'm sure—
Or else his dear papa is poor.
===Chinese===
每天晚上我把祷告念，
天天都有三顿饭来填；

只要当天我乖乖听话，
饭后橘子就奖给我啦。

孩子若是不干又不净，
玩具零食数也数不清；

他准是个淘气小坏蛋，
不然就是他爹穷没钱。

## A Good Boy / 好孩子

===Original===
I woke before the morning, I was happy all the day,
I never said an ugly word, but smiled and stuck to play.

And now at last the sun is going down behind the wood,
And I am very happy, for I know that I've been good.

My bed is waiting cool and fresh, with linen smooth and fair,
And I must off to sleepsin-by, and not forget my prayer.

I know that, till tomorrow I shall see the sun arise,
No ugly dream shall fright my mind, no ugly sight my eyes,

But slumber hold me tightly till I waken in the dawn,
And hear the thrushes singing in the lilacs round the lawn.
===Chinese===
天还没亮我就醒来，一整天都高高兴兴，
我从不讲半句粗话，只是笑着玩个不停。

此刻太阳正慢慢落下，躲进那树林的背后，
我心里真是好快乐，因为知道自己乖得够。

我的小床正等着我，又凉又爽自自在在，
我得赶紧上床睡，可不能把祷告忘怀。

我知道，直到明天，我看见太阳再升起，
恶梦吓不坏我的心，丑象闯不进我眼底。

只等睡眠将我紧紧抱，直到我在黎明苏醒，
听见草坪四周丁香丛，鸫鸟唱得好听好听。

## Escape at Bedtime / 临睡前的逃奔

===Original===
The lights from the parlour and kitchen shone out
Through the blinds and the windows and bars;
And high overhead and all moving about,
There were thousands of millions of stars.

There ne'er were such thousands of leaves on a tree,
Nor of people in church or the Park,
As the crowds of the stars that looked down upon me,
And that glittered and winked in the dark.

The Dog, and the Plough, and the Hunter, and all,
And the star of the sailor, and Mars,
These shone in the sky, and the pail by the wall
Would be half full of water and stars.

They saw me at last, and they chased me with cries,
And they soon had me packed into bed;
But the glory kept shining and bright in my eyes,
And the stars going round in my head.
===Chinese===
客厅和厨房里的灯光透了出来，
穿过百叶窗、窗户和一道道铁栏；
高高的头顶上满天空摇摇摆摆，
千千万万颗星星闪亮在那半。

树上从没长出过那么多的叶片，
教堂或公园里也没这么多人丁；
像那成群结队俯身看我的星点，
在黑夜里眨着眼、亮亮又晶晶。

大犬、北斗、猎户，还有全部星官，
水手的那颗星，还有火星也在；
它们在天上闪耀，墙边水桶看看，
会盛上满满半桶水、半桶星海。

它们终于发现了我，叫嚷着来把我追赶，
没多会儿就把我打发上了小床；
可那辉煌的光芒还在我眼里闪亮好看，
满脑袋的星星团团转、好不忙。

## Marching Song / 行军歌

===Original===
Bring the comb and play upon it!
Marching, here we come!
Willie cocks his Highland bonnet,
Johnnie beats the drum.

Mary Jane commands the party,
Peter leads the rear;
Feet in time, alert and hearty,
Each a Grenadier!

All in the most martial manner
Marching double-quick;
While the napkin like a banner
Waves upon the stick!

Here's enough of fame and pillage,
Great commander Jane!
Now that we've been round the village,
Let's go home again.
===Chinese===
拿来梳子当琴弹一弹！
行军啦，我们来啦！
威利歪戴高地帽看一看，
约翰尼把鼓敲打。

玛丽·简来指挥这一队，
彼得殿后压阵脚；
步子合拍，精神抖擞不畏，
个个都是掷弹兵好！

全用最最神气的军姿，
飞快地向前行军；
餐巾像旗帜一面挂起，
在棍子上面飞舞！

名气和战利品都已足够，
伟大的指挥官简！
既然我们已绕村子走够，
我们这就回家转。

## The Cow / 奶牛

===Original===
The friendly cow, all red and white,
I love with all my heart:
She gives me cream with all her might,
To eat with apple-tart.

She wanders lowing here and there,
And yet she cannot stray,
All in the pleasant open air,
The pleasant light of day;

And blown by all the winds that pass,
And wet with all the showers,
She walks among the meadow grass
And eats the meadow flowers.
===Chinese===
那友善的奶牛，红白相间，
我打心眼里爱她：
她拼尽全力给我奶油鲜，
配苹果馅饼吃下。

她这儿那儿游荡，哞哞叫唤，
可她却走不了多远，
都在这宜人的开阔天，
这宜人的日光灿烂；

被所有路过的风儿吹拂，
被所有的阵雨淋湿，
她走在草地青青的小路，
吃着草地上的花枝。

这世界装满了各种各样好东西，

## The Wind / 风

===Original===
I saw you toss the kites on high
And blow the birds about the sky;
And all around I heard you pass,
Like ladies' skirts across the grass—
O wind, a-blowing all day long,
O wind, that sings so loud a song!

I saw the different things you did,
But always you yourself you hid.
I felt you push, I heard you call,
I could not see yourself at all—
O wind, a-blowing all day long,
O wind, that sings so loud a song!

O you that are so strong and cold,
O blower, are you young or old?
Are you a beast of field and tree,
Or just a stronger child than me?
O wind, a-blowing all day long,
O wind, that sings so loud a song!
===Chinese===
我看见你把风筝抛上天，
又把鸟儿吹得满天转；
我四处听见你走过身边，
像淑女的裙摆掠过草面——
风啊，整整一天吹个不住，
风啊，唱着这么响亮的歌谱！

我看见你做的种种事情，
可你总是把自己藏隐。
我感到你推，我听见你唤，
我却怎么也看不见你的脸——
风啊，整整一天吹个不住，
风啊，唱着这么响亮的歌谱！

你这般强劲又这般冰凉，
风啊，你年老还是年幼长？
你是田野林木里的一只兽，
还是个比我更强的小孩头？
风啊，整整一天吹个不住，
风啊，唱着这么响亮的歌谱！

## Keepsake Mill / 纪念磨坊

===Original===
Over the borders, a sin without pardon,
Breaking the branches and crawling below,
Out through the breach in the wall of the garden,
Down by the banks of the river, we go.

Here is the mill with the humming of thunder,
Here is the weir with the wonder of foam,
Here is the sluice with the race running under—
Marvellous places, though handy to home!

Sounds of the village grow stiller and stiller,
Stiller the note of the birds on the hill;
Dusty and dim are the eyes of the miller,
Deaf are his ears with the moil of the mill.

Years may go by, and the wheel in the river,
Wheel as it wheels for us, children, today,
Wheel and keep roaring and foaming forever,
Long after all of the boys are away.

Home from the Indies, and home from the ocean,
Heroes and soldiers we all shall come home;
Still we shall find the old mill-wheel in motion,
Turning and churning that river to foam.

You with the bean that I gave when we quarrelled,
I with your marble of Saturday last,
Honoured and old and all gaily apparelled,
Here we shall meet and remember the past.
===Chinese===
越过了边界，是饶恕不了的罪过，
折断了树枝，从底下匍匐穿行，
钻出花园墙上的那一个缺口，
顺着河边堤岸，我们一路前进。

这儿是磨坊，轰鸣如阵阵雷响，
这儿是堰坝，泛起奇妙的白沫，
这儿是水闸，水流在底下奔淌——
离家虽近，却是神奇的好场所！

村子里的声响越来越静悄悄，
山上鸟儿的啼鸣也越来越轻；
磨坊主的眼睛又灰暗又粗糙，
耳朵被磨坊的劳作震得听不清。

一年年过去，河里那只水轮，
仍像今天为我们孩子般转动，
永远不停地轰鸣、翻着水花飞奔，
在所有男孩都已离去之后许久。

从东印度群岛归来，从大洋归来，
英雄和士兵，我们都要回故乡；
我们仍会看见那老水轮转得欢，
翻着搅着，把河水搅成白沫浪。

你带着我吵架时给的那颗豆，
我带着你上星期六的那颗弹珠，
庄重又老旧，一身盛装锦绣，
我们要在这儿相见，把往事故重温述。

## Good and Bad Children / 好孩子和坏孩子

===Original===
Children, you are very little,
And your bones are very brittle;
If you would grow great and stately,
You must try to walk sedately.

You must still be bright and quiet,
And content with simple diet;
And remain, through all bewild'ring,
Innocent and honest children.

Happy hearts and happy faces,
Happy play in grassy places—
That was how, in ancient ages,
Children grew to kings and sages.

But the unkind and the unruly,
And the sort who eat unduly,
They must never hope for glory—
Theirs is quite a different story!

Cruel children, crying babies,
All grow up as geese and gabies,
Hated, as their age increases,
By their nephews and their nieces.
===Chinese===
孩子们啊，你们还小小的，
骨头也都还脆又易折的；
你们想要长得高大体面，
走路就得稳稳当当、不乱颠。

你们还要活泼又安静，
粗茶淡饭也要心满意；
还要经过一切迷乱境，
做个天真诚实的好孩子。

快乐的心、快乐的脸，
草地上快乐地游玩——
古时候就是这么样的，
孩子们才长成了君王圣贤。

可那凶巴巴、不听话的，
还有贪吃没有节制的，
他们别想有什么光荣命——
他们的故事可是大不同！

残忍的孩子、爱哭的娃娃，
长大都成了蠢鹅和傻瓜，
年纪越大越招人厌弃，
被侄儿侄女们所嫌弃。

## Foreign Children / 外国的小孩

===Original===
Little Indian, Sioux or Crow,
Little frosty Eskimo,
Little Turk or Japanee,
O! don't you wish that you were me?

You have seen the scarlet trees
And the lions over seas;
You have eaten ostrich eggs,
And turned the turtles off their legs.

Such a life is very fine,
But it's not so nice as mine;
You must often, as you trod,
Have wearied not to be abroad.

You have curious things to eat,
I am fed on proper meat;
You must dwell beyond the foam,
But I am safe and live at home.

Little Indian, Sioux or Crow,
Little frosty Eskimo,
Little Turk or Japanee,
O! don't you wish that you were me?
===Chinese===
小小的印第安人，苏族或克劳族，
小小的冻坏了的爱斯基摩人，
小小的土耳其人或日本人，
哦！你不想变成我这样的人？

你见过那火红的大树，
还有海那边的狮子出没；
你吃过鸵鸟的蛋，
还把海龟翻得四脚朝天。

这样的日子倒是挺好，
可不如我的日子妙；
你一路走一路想，
一定厌倦了不在家乡。

你有稀奇古怪的东西可吃，
我吃的是正经体面的肉食；
你得住在大洋的浪花外，
我可安安稳稳住在家里头。

小小的印第安人，苏族或克劳族，
小小的冻坏了的爱斯基摩人，
小小的土耳其人或日本人，
哦！你不想变成我这样的人？

## The Sun's Travels / 太阳的旅行

===Original===
The sun is not a-bed when I
At night upon my pillow lie;
Still round the earth his way he takes,
And morning after morning makes.

While here at home, in shining day,
We round the sunny garden play,
Each little Indian sleepy-head
Is being kissed and put to bed.

And when at eve I rise from tea,
Day dawns beyond the Atlantic Sea,
And all the children in the West
Are getting up and being dressed.
===Chinese===
太阳并没有上床睡觉，
当夜里我枕着枕头躺倒；
他仍绕着地球一路走，
把一个又一个早晨造就。

当我们在家，在这亮堂的天，
绕着阳光满满的花园游玩，
每一个印第安困困小脑袋，
正被亲一亲、抱上了床铺睡。

而当傍晚我喝完茶起身，
大西洋那边已拂晓清晨，
西方所有的孩子们一个个，
正在起床、把衣裳穿妥。

## The Lamplighter / 点灯人

===Original===
My tea is nearly ready and the sun has left the sky;
It's time to take the window to see Leerie going by;
For every night at tea-time and before you take your seat,
With lantern and with ladder he comes posting up the street.

Now Tom would be a driver and Maria go to sea,
And my papa's a banker and as rich as he can be;
But I, when I am stronger, and can choose what I'm to do,
O Leerie, I'll go round at night and light the lamps with you!

For we are very lucky, with a lamp before the door,
And Leerie stops to light it as he lights so many more;
And O! before you hurry by with ladder and with light,
O Leerie, see a little child and nod to him tonight!
===Chinese===
我的茶点快好了，太阳也已下了天；
是时候趴到窗前，看利里路过这边；
因为每天晚上喝茶时，你还没坐上座，
他就提着灯、扛着梯，匆匆从街上走过。

如今汤姆想赶车，玛丽亚想下海洋，
我爸爸是个银行家，富得不能再富足；
可我呀，等我长大了，能自己选做的事，
哦利里，我夜里要跟你去，把一盏盏灯点起！

因为我们真有福气，门前有一盏灯立，
利里停下来把它点亮，像点亮许多盏一样；
哦！在你扛着梯、提着灯匆匆走过之前，
哦利里，看看这小孩，今晚对他点点头眼！

## My Bed Is a Boat / 我的床是条小船

===Original===
My bed is like a little boat;
Nurse helps me in when I embark;
She girds me in my sailor's coat
And starts me in the dark.

At night, I go on board and say
Good night to all my friends on shore;
I shut my eyes and sail away
And see and hear no more.

And sometimes things to bed I take,
As prudent sailors have to do:
Perhaps a slice of wedding-cake,
Perhaps a toy or two.

All night across the dark we steer:
But when the day returns at last,
Safe in my room, beside the pier,
I find my vessel fast.
===Chinese===
我的床像一条小小的船；
保姆扶我上去，当我登船；
她给我系上水手的外套，
就让我出发进了黑天。

到了夜里，我上了船，说道：
向岸上所有朋友道晚安；
我闭上眼睛驶向远方，
再也看不见、听不见。

有时候我把东西带上床，
像精明的水手必须做的：
也许一片结婚的喜糕，
也许一两个玩具小宝贝。

整夜我们在黑暗中掌舵：
可当白天终于又转回头，
我安安稳稳在房间里，码头边，
发现我的船儿系得牢牢不丢。

## The Moon / 月亮

===Original===
The moon has a face like the clock in the hall;
She shines on thieves on the garden wall,
On streets and fields and harbour quays,
And birdies asleep in the forks of the trees.

The squalling cat and the squeaking mouse,
The howling dog by the door of the house,
The bat that lies in bed at noon,
All love to be out by the light of the moon.

But all of the things that belong to the day
Cuddle to sleep to be out of her way;
And flowers and children close their eyes
Till up in the morning the sun shall arise.
===Chinese===
月亮有一张脸，像门厅里的钟一样；
她照着花园墙上的小偷、过往，
照着街道、田野和港口的码头，
还有树杈里睡着的小鸟儿头。

那尖叫的猫、吱吱叫的老鼠，
房门前嚎叫的那条狗子，
正午还躺在床上睡觉的蝙蝠，
都爱在月光底下出门走动。

可所有属于白昼的那些生灵，
都蜷缩着睡去，躲开她的行踪；
花儿和孩子们都闭上了眼睛，
直到早晨太阳再升上天空。

## The Swing / 秋千

===Original===
How do you like to go up in a swing,
Up in the air so blue?
Oh, I do think it the pleasantest thing
Ever a child can do!

Up in the air and over the wall,
Till I can see so wide,
Rivers and trees and cattle and all
Over the countryside—

Till I look down on the garden green,
Down on the roof so brown—
Up in the air I go flying again,
Up in the air and down!
===Chinese===
你喜不喜欢荡上秋千去，
高高飞进那蓝天里？
哦，我真是觉得这最最有趣，
是孩子能做的最美事体！

高高飞起，飞过了院墙，
直到我能看得那么宽广，
河流、树木、牛群，样样都在
那广阔田野的一大方——

直到我俯看那青青的花园，
俯看那褐色的屋檐——
我又飞上了高高的天，
高高飞起，又落下来！
