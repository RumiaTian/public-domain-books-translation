## XVIII Manuel Chooses / 十八 曼努埃尔的抉择

===Original===
“But I cannot understand,” said Freydis, on a fine day in September, “how it is that, now the power of Schamir is in your control, and you have the secret of giving life to your images, you do not care to use either the secret or the talisman. For you make no more images, you are always saying, ‘No, we will let that wait a bit,’ and you do not even quicken the ten caricatures of the image-makers which you have already modeled.”

“Life will be given to these in due time,” said Manuel, “but that time is not yet come. Meanwhile, I avoid practice of the old Tuyla mystery for the sufficing reason that I have seen the result it has on the practitioner. A geas was upon me to make a figure in the world, and so I modeled and loaned life to such a splendid gay young champion as was to my thinking and my desire. Thus my geas, I take it, is discharged, and a thing done has an end. Heaven may now excel me by creating a larger number of living figures than I, but preeminence in this matter is not a question of arithmetic﻿—”

“Ah, yes, my squinting boy has all the virtues, including that of modesty!”

“Well, but I have seen my notion embodied, seen it take breath, seen it depart from Morven in all respects, except for a little limping﻿—which, do you know, I thought rather graceful?﻿—in well-nigh all respects, I repeat, quite indistinguishable from the embodied notions of that master craftsman whom some call Ptha, and others Jahveh, and others Abraxas, and yet others Koshchei the Deathless. In fine, I have made a figure more admirable and significant than is the run of men, and I rest upon my laurels.”

===Chinese===
「可是我不明白，」九月里一个晴朗的日子，弗蕾迪丝说道，「如今沙米尔的法力已握在你手中，你又掌握了赋泥像以生命的秘诀，你怎么就无意动用这秘诀，也无意动用那法宝呢。因为你如今再不造像了，你总是说：『不，这件事我们且缓一缓。』就连造像者们的那十个漫画像——你已经捏好了的那些——你也还不肯给它们生命。」

「到时候它们自会被赋予生命，」曼努埃尔说，「只是那个时辰还没有来。这期间我不再修习那古老的图伊拉秘法，理由也够充分：我亲眼见过它落在修习者身上的下场。曾有一道誓约加在我身上，要我在世上立起一个形象，于是我便照着自己的心思与愿望，捏出了那样一位堂堂而快活的少年英雄，把生命借给了他。这样看来，我的誓约已经卸除，而做成的事自有完结。上天如今尽可以造出比我更多的活人像来胜过我，但在这一桩上，出类拔萃原不是个算术问题——」

「啊，是了，我的斜眼小子样样美德俱全，连谦虚也不例外！」

「喏，可我已经见过我的构想化作形体，见过它呼吸，见过它离开莫尔文；它在一切方面——只除了一点微跛，这个你可知道，我还觉得跛得相当优雅？——我再说一遍，它在几乎一切方面，都同那位大匠师化出的诸般构想难以分辨：那位匠师，有人称他普塔（Ptha），有人称他雅赫维，有人称他阿布拉克萨斯（Abraxas），还有人管他叫不死的科谢伊（Koshchei the Deathless）。总而言之，我造出了一具人像，比寻常世人更可钦佩、更具分量，我便安享我的桂冠了。」

===Original===
“You have created a living being somewhat above the average, that is true: but then every woman who has a fine baby does just as much﻿—”

“The principle is not the same,” said Manuel, with dignity.

“And why not, please, big boy?”

“For one thing, my image was an original and unaided production, whereas a baby, I am told, is the result of more or less hasty collaboration. Then, too a baby is largely chance work, in that its nature cannot be exactly foreplanned and predetermined by its makers, who, in the glow of artistic creation, must, I imagine, very often fail to follow the best aesthetic canons.”

===Chinese===
「你造出了一个略高于平均水准的活人，这倒是真的：可随便哪个生下漂亮娃娃的女人，做到的不也是同一样的事么——」

「这道理可不一样，」曼努埃尔庄重地说。

「那么请问，又有什么不一样呢，大孩子？」

「其一，我的泥像是独出心裁、独力完成的造物；而婴孩呢，据我所知，是几分仓促的合作之果。再者，婴孩大体上是碰运气的活计，因为它的天性无法由它的造者们精确地预先设计、预先注定——这些造者身处艺术创作的热狂之中，我想，多半常常顾不上遵循最上乘的美学法度。」

===Original===
“As for that, nobody who makes new and unexampled things can make them exactly to the maker’s will. Even your image limped, you remember﻿—”

“Ah, but so gracefully!”

“﻿—No, Manuel, it is only those necromancers who evoke the dead, and bid the dead return to the warm flesh, that can be certain as to the results of their sorcery. For these alone of magic-workers know in advance what they are making.”

“Ah, this is news! So you think it is possible to evoke the dead in some more tangible form than that of an instructive ghost? You think it possible for a dead girl﻿—or, as to that matter, for a dead boy, or a defunct archbishop, or a deceased ragpicker﻿—to be fetched back to live again in the warm flesh?”

“All things are possible, Manuel, at a price.”

===Chinese===
「说到这个，凡造出前所未有之新事物的人，谁也不能把它造得同造者的意愿分毫不差。就连你的泥像不也跛了么，你记得的——」

「啊，可跛得多么优雅！」

「——不，曼努埃尔，唯有那些召唤亡者、命亡者回到温热血肉里去的死灵法师，才能对自己法术的结果有十足的把握。因为在一切施法者之中，唯独他们预先知道自己造的是什么。」

「啊，这可是新闻！这么说，你认为亡者可以召作一种比有益世道的鬼魂更切实可触的形相？你认为一个死去的姑娘——或者，把话说开去，一个死去的少年、一位已故的大主教、一个亡故的拾破烂的——也能被领回来，重新活在温热的血肉里？」

「万事皆有可能，曼努埃尔，只要出得起代价。」

===Original===
Said Manuel:

“What price would be sufficient to repurchase the rich spoils of Death? and whence might any bribe be fetched? For all the glowing wealth and beauty of this big round world must show as a new-minted farthing beside his treasure chests, as one slight shining unimportant coin which﻿—even this also!﻿—belongs to earth, but has been overlooked by him as yet. Presently this hour, and whatever is strutting through this hour, is added to the heaped crypts wherein lie all that was worthiest in the old time.

“Now there is garnered such might and loveliness and wisdom as human thinking cannot conceive of. An emperor is made much of here when he has conquered some part of the world, but Death makes nothing of a world of emperors: and in Death’s crowded storerooms nobody bothers to estimate within a thousand thousand of how many emperors, and tzars and popes and pharaohs and sultans, that in their day were adored as omnipotent, are there assembled pellmell, along with all that was worthiest in the old time.

===Chinese===
曼努埃尔说道：

「什么样的代价，才赎得回死神那丰厚的掠获呢？又能从哪里取来贿赂呢？因为这浑圆大地上一切灼灼生辉的财富与美色，摆到他的藏宝箱旁边，也只能像一枚新铸的法新（farthing），一枚单薄发亮、无足轻重的小钱——而连这一枚，也是属于尘世的！——只不过他至今还不曾放在眼里罢了。转眼之间，眼前这个时辰，连同在这个时辰里趾高气扬走过的一切，都要添进那一座座堆叠的地窖——窖中正躺着往昔一切至可珍贵之物。

「如今那里已经囤聚起这样的人力、美色与智慧，非凡人的思虑所能想见。在这里，一位皇帝征服了世界的某一部分，便受尽抬举；死神却不把整整一个世界的皇帝当回事：在死神拥挤的库房里，谁也懒得费神去估算那里乱纷纷堆聚着多少皇帝、多少沙皇、教皇、法老与苏丹——连百万之内的约数都不肯估——这些人在各自的当世都被奉作全能，同往昔一切至可珍贵之物堆作一处。

===Original===
“As touches loveliness, not even Helen’s beauty is distinguishable among those multitudinous millions of resplendent queens whom one finds yonder. Here are many pretty women, here above all is Freydis, so I do not complain. But yonder is deep-bosomed Semiramis, and fair-tressed Guenevere, and Magdalene that loved Christ, and Europa, the bull’s laughing bride, and Lilith, whose hot kiss made Satan ardent, and a many other ladies by whose dear beauty’s might were shaped the songs which cause us to remember all that was worthiest in the old time.

“As wisdom goes, here we have prudent men of business able to add two and two together, and justice may be out of hand distinguished from injustice by an impanelment of the nearest twelve fools. Here we have many Helmases a-cackling wisely under a goose-feather. But yonder are Cato and Nestor and Merlin and Socrates, Abelard sits with Aristotle there, and the seven sages confer with the major prophets, and yonder is all that was worthiest in the old time.

“All, all, are put away in Death’s heaped storerooms, so safely put away that opulent Death may well grin scornfully at Life: for everything belongs to Death, and Life is only a mendicant scratching at his sores so long as Death permits it. No, Freydis, there can be no bribing Death! For what bribe anywhere has Life to offer which Death has not already lying disregarded in a thousand dusty coffers along with all that was worthiest in the old time?”

===Chinese===
「论到美色，就是在那边亿兆煌煌的后妃之中，连海伦（Helen）的姿容也认不出来了。这里有的是漂亮女人，这里更坐着冠绝一切的弗蕾迪丝，所以我没什么好抱怨的。可那边有胸乳丰隆的塞弥拉弥斯（Semiramis），有美发如云的桂妮薇儿（Guenevere），有爱过基督的抹大拉的马利亚（Magdalene），有公牛那含笑的新娘欧罗巴（Europa），有莉莉丝（Lilith）——她炽热的一吻曾把撒旦烧得情热如焚——还有许许多多别的贵妇，正是凭着她们亲爱美貌的伟力，才锤炼出那些歌谣，教我们至今还记得往昔一切至可珍贵之物。

「论到智慧，我们这里有精明的生意人，会把二加二加到一起；要把公道同不公道分开，也只消就地凑齐最近的十二个傻瓜列名陪审，便可立见分晓。我们这里有许多位黑尔马斯，正握着一管鹅毛，嘎嘎地大发睿智之论。可那边有加图（Cato）、涅斯托尔（Nestor）、梅林（Merlin）与苏格拉底（Socrates），那边阿伯拉尔（Abelard）与亚里士多德（Aristotle）同坐，七贤同大先知们商谈，那边还有往昔一切至可珍贵之物。

「一切，一切，都收进了死神堆叠的库房，收得那样稳妥，以致阔绰的死神尽可以冲着生命鄙夷地咧嘴一笑：因为万事万物都属于死神，而生命不过是个叫花子，趁死神容许，才抓挠着自己的疮疤。不，弗蕾迪丝，死神是贿赂不动的！生命还能拿出什么贿赂，是死神不曾早已同往昔一切至可珍贵之物一起、丢在他一千只积尘的钱柜里正无人理会地躺着的呢？」

===Original===
Freydis replied: “One thing alone. Yes, Manuel, there is one thing only which all Death’s ravishings have never taken from Life, and which has not ever entered into Death’s keeping. It is through weighing this fact, and through doing what else is requisite, that the very bold may bring back the dead to live again in the warm flesh.”

“Well, but I have heard the histories of presumptuous men who attempted to perform such miracles, and all these persons sooner or later came to misery.”

“Why, to be sure! to whom else would you have them coming?” said Freydis. And she explained the way it was.

===Chinese===
弗蕾迪丝答道：「只有一样。是的，曼努埃尔，天下只有一样东西，是死神一切的劫掠从不曾从生命手里夺走、也从不曾收进死神掌管的。正是把这一层掂量分明，再把其余该做的事一一做到，那极大胆的人才能把亡人带回来，叫他重新活在温热的血肉里。」

「可是，我听说过那些妄自尊大之辈的事迹，他们试图行出这样的奇迹，而这些人个个迟早都归了苦难。」

「可不是嘛！不然你还要他们归到谁那儿去呢？」弗蕾迪丝说。接着，她把其中的道理讲了。

===Original===
Manuel put many questions. All that evening he was thoughtful, and he was unusually tender with Freydis. And that night, when Freydis slept, Dom Manuel kissed her very lightly, then blinked his eyes, and for a moment covered them with his hand. Standing thus, the tall boy queerly moving his mouth, as though it were stiff and he were trying to make it more supple.

Then he armed himself. He took up the black shield upon which was painted a silver stallion. He crept out of their modest magic home and went down into Bellegarde, where he stole him a horse, from the stables of Duke Asmund.

And that night, and all the next day, Dom Manuel rode beyond Aigremont and Naimes, journeying away from Morven, and away from the house of jasper and porphyry and violet and yellow breccia, and away from Freydis, who had put off immortality for his kisses. He travelled northward, toward the high woods of Dun Vlechlan, where the leaves were aglow with the funereal flames of autumn: for the summer wherein Dom Manuel and Freydis had been happy together was now as dead as that estranged queer time which he had shared with Alianora.

===Chinese===
曼努埃尔提了许多问题。那一晚他始终若有所思，待弗蕾迪丝也异乎寻常地温存。那天夜里，弗蕾迪丝睡熟之后，曼努埃尔大人极轻地吻了她一下，随后眨了眨眼，又用手把眼睛遮了片刻。他这样站着，这高个子少年古怪地嚅动着嘴，仿佛那张嘴发僵，正想把它揉得活络一些。

于是他披挂起来。他拿起那面黑盾，盾上绘着一匹白银骏马。他悄悄溜出他们那座朴素的魔法居所，下到贝勒加德，在阿斯蒙德公爵的马厩里给自己偷了一匹马。

那一夜，再加上第二天一整天，曼努埃尔大人骑马走过了艾格雷蒙（Aigremont）与奈梅斯（Naimes），一路远离莫尔文，远离那座碧玉、斑岩、紫黄二色角砾岩筑成的宅子，也远离弗蕾迪丝——她为了他的吻，早已卸下了不朽。他向北而行，朝敦·弗莱赫兰的高林赶去，那里满林秋叶正被秋天丧葬的火焰照得通明：因为曼努埃尔大人同弗蕾迪丝一同欢度的那个夏天，如今已经死了，死得就像他与阿莉亚诺拉共同消磨的那段疏远而古怪的时光一样。
