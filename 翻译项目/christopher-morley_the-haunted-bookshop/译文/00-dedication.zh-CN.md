## To the Booksellers / 致书商们

===Original===
Be pleased to know, most worthy, that this little book is dedicated to you in affection and respect.

===Chinese===
诸位可敬的书商，敬请知晓，这本小书谨以深情与敬意献给你们。

===Original===
The faults of the composition are plain to you all. I begin merely in the hope of saying something further of the adventures of *Roger Mifflin*, whose exploits in *Parnassus on Wheels* some of you have been kind enough to applaud.

===Chinese===
此书的瑕疵，各位一目了然。我动笔之初，不过是想再多说几句罗杰·米夫林（Roger Mifflin）的冒险故事——他当年在《车轮上的帕纳索斯》（*Parnassus on Wheels*）中的种种壮举，承蒙诸位不吝喝彩。

===Original===
But then came Miss Titania Chapman, and my young advertising man fell in love with her, and the two of them rather ran away with the tale.

===Chinese===
后来却来了蒂塔尼亚·查普曼（Titania Chapman）小姐，我那个年轻的广告人爱上了她，这两位一来二去，反倒把故事给带跑了。

===Original===
I think I should explain that the passage in Chapter VIII, dealing with the delightful talent of Mr. Sidney Drew, was written before the lamented death of that charming artist. But as it was a sincere tribute, sincerely meant, I have seen no reason for removing it.

===Chinese===
我应当说明：第八章中论及西德尼·德鲁（Sidney Drew）先生迷人天赋的那段文字，写于那位可敬的艺术家不幸辞世之前。然而既是一片赤诚的敬意，我实在找不到理由将它删去。

===Original===
Chapters I, II, III, and VI appeared originally in *The Bookman*, and to the editor of that admirable magazine I owe thanks for his permission to reprint.

===Chinese===
第一、二、三、六章最初刊于《书人》（*The Bookman*）杂志，承蒙该刊主编惠允转载，在此谨致谢忱。

===Original===
Now that Roger is to have ten Parnassuses on the road, I am emboldened to think that some of you may encounter them on their travels. And if you do, I hope you will find that these new errants of the Parnassus on Wheels Corporation are living up to the ancient and honourable traditions of our noble profession.

===Chinese===
如今罗杰将在路上安置十座帕纳索斯（Parnassus），我斗胆揣测，诸位在旅途中或许会与它们不期而遇。倘若果真如此，但愿你们会发现，这些帕纳索斯车轮公司的新流浪者们，正在不辱我们这一崇高行当古老而光荣的传统。

===Original===
Christopher Morley.
Philadelphia,
April 28, 1919.

===Chinese===
克里斯托弗·莫利
费城
一九一九年四月二十八日
