### I / 一
===Original===

On the evening of that very afternoon, Thursday, I again collapsed. I was coming home in the dusk through a whispering world. All over the streets, everywhere on the broad shining snow, under a blaze of stars so sharp and piercing that the sky seemed strangely close and intimate, the talk went on. Groups everywhere and groups irrespective of all class distinction﻿—a well-to-do woman in rich furs, a peasant woman with a shawl over her head, a wild, bearded soldier, a stout, important officer, a maidservant, a cabdriver, a shopman﻿—talking, talking, talking, talking.﻿ ﻿… The eagerness, the ignorance, the odd fairytale world spun about those groups, so that the coloured domes of the churches, the silver network of the stars, the wooden booths, the mist of candles before the icons, the rough painted pictures on the shops advertising the goods sold within﻿—all these things shared in that crude idealistic, cynical ignorance, in that fairytale of brutality, goodness, cowardice, and bravery, malice and generosity, superstition and devotion that was so shortly to be offered to a materialistic, hard-fighting, brave and unthinking Europe!﻿ ﻿…
That, however, was not now my immediate business﻿—enough of that presently. My immediate business, as I very quickly discovered, was to pluck up enough strength to drag my wretched body home. The events of the week had, I suppose, carried me along. I was to suffer now the inevitable reaction. I felt exactly as though I had been shot from a gun and landed, suddenly, without breath, without any strength in any of my limbs in a new and strange world. I was standing, when I first realised my weakness, beside the wooden booths in the Sadovaya. They were all closed of course, but along the pavement women and old men had baskets containing sweets and notepaper and red paper tulips offered in memory of the glorious Revolution. Right across the Square the groups of people scattered in little dusky pools against the snow, until they touched the very doors of the church.﻿ ﻿… I saw all this, was conscious that the stars and the church candles mingled﻿ ﻿… then suddenly I had to clutch the side of the booth behind me to prevent myself from falling. My head swam, my limbs were as water, and my old so well-remembered friend struck me in the middle of the spine as though he had cut me in two with his knife. How was I ever to get home?

就在那个下午——星期四——的晚上，我又垮了。暮色里我穿过一个窃窃私语的世界往家走。满街到处，在宽阔发亮的雪地上，在锐利刺骨、使天空显得奇异地逼近而亲昵的繁星底下，谈话在进行。到处是一群一群的人，全不分阶级——穿名贵皮草的阔太太，包披巾的农妇，胡子拉碴的狂野士兵，神气十足的胖军官，女仆，马车夫，店伙——说呀，说呀，说呀，说呀。……那份热切，那份无知，那个缠绕着人群的奇怪的童话世界，叫教堂的彩色圆顶、星星的银网、木头货棚、圣像前烛火的迷蒙、铺子上粗糙的商品广告画——这一切都分享着那粗砺的理想主义、玩世不恭的无知，分享着那个残忍与善良、怯懦与勇猛、恶毒与慷慨、迷信与虔诚的童话，而它转眼就要端给一个唯利是图、狠斗敢打、勇敢而无脑的欧洲了！……
然而那不是我眼前的急务——那个且待下文。我的急务，我很快就发现，是攒足力气，把我这副可怜的身子拖回家。这一个星期的事，想来是把我一路裹挟着走的。如今该轮到我受那不可避免的反弹了。我的感觉就像被人从炮筒里打出去，猛然落地，气也没了，四肢一丝力气也没有，落在一个陌生的新世界里。我头一回意识到自己的虚弱时，正站在萨多瓦亚街的木头货棚旁。货棚自然全关着，可人行道上，女人和老头们提着篮子，卖糖果、信纸和红纸做的郁金香，纪念那场光荣的革命。广场那头，一群群人散成一小摊一小摊暗影，贴在雪上，一直摊到教堂门口。……这一切我都看见了，也意识到星星同教堂的烛光交融在一起……随后我忽然不得不抓住身后货棚的边，免得栽倒。我的头发晕，四肢软得像水，我那位记忆犹新的老朋友一拳打在我脊梁正中，仿佛拿刀把我拦腰斩成了两截。我可怎么回得了家？

===Original===

No one noticed me﻿—indeed they seemed to my sick eyes to have ceased to be human. Ghosts in a ghostly world, the snow gleaming through them so that they only moved like a thin diaphanous veil against the wall of the sky﻿ ﻿… I clutched my booth. In a moment I should be down. The pain in my back was agony, my legs had ceased to exist, and I was falling into a dark, dark pool of clear jet-black water, at the bottom of which lay a star.﻿ ﻿…
The strange thing is that I do not know who it was who rescued me. I know that someone came. I know that to my own dim surprise an *isvostchick* was there and that very feebly I got into it. Someone was with me. Was it my black-bearded peasant? I fancy now that it was. I can even, on looking back, see him sitting up, very large and still, one thick arm holding me. I fancy that I can still smell the stuff of his clothes. I fancy that he talked to me, very quietly, reassuring me about something. But, upon my word, I don’t know. One can so easily imagine what one wants to be true, and now I want, more than I would then ever have believed to be possible, to have had actual contact with him. It is the only conversation between us that can ever have existed: never, before or after, was there another opportunity. And in any case there can scarcely have been a conversation, because I certainly said nothing, and I cannot remember anything that he said, if indeed he said anything at all. At any rate I was there in the Sadovaya, I was in a cab, I was in my bed. The truth of the rest of it anyone may decide for himself.﻿ ﻿…

===Chinese===

没有人留意我——在我病恹恹的眼里，他们简直已不再是人。鬼世界里的鬼魂，雪光透过他们的身子，他们只是贴在天墙上挪动的一层薄薄的轻纱……我抓住货棚。再过一刻我就要倒下了。背上的疼像刀绞，两条腿已经不存在了，我正跌进一潭黑黑的水，清亮而墨黑，潭底躺着一颗星。……
怪的是，我不知道救我的是谁。我知道有人来了。我知道，连我自己都迷迷糊糊地吃惊，一辆马车已在跟前，我有气无力地爬了上去。有人同我在一起。是不是我那个黑胡子农民？我现在想是的。回头看，我甚至看得见他坐在车上，高大而安静，一条粗壮的胳膊搂着我。我仿佛还闻得出他衣裳的料子味。我仿佛记得他对我说话了，很轻，安慰着我什么。可是说实在的，我不知道。人要信什么是真的，太容易把它想出来了；而如今我比当年料想不到地更盼着，我真的同他接触过。那是我们之间可能存在过的唯一一次交谈：从前没有、往后也再没有别的机会。可无论如何算不上什么交谈，因为我分明一句话也没说，他说过什么我也一句记不得，倘若他当真说过。总之，我是在萨多瓦亚街，我在马车上，我在我的床上。其余的真相，谁爱怎么断就怎么断吧。……
