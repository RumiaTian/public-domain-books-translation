## XVII / 第十七章

===Original===
The Hôtel de Ville at Orange still stands, as it did then, in the newly-named Place de la République; and if the tourist of today mounts its steps, enters the building through its central portal, crosses the wide vestibule and finally turns down a long corridor on his right, he will, almost at the end of this, come to a door which bears the legend: “*Travaux Publics.*”

===Chinese===
奥朗日的市政厅（Hôtel de Ville）至今还立在原地，一如当年，就在新命名的共和国广场（Place de la République）上。今天的游客若是拾级而上，从正中的大门走进楼里，穿过宽阔的门厅，再向右拐进一条长廊，差不多走到廊子尽头，就会来到一扇门前，门上钉着一块牌子，写着：「公共工程（*Travaux Publics*）」。

===Original===
Should he be bold enough to push open the door, he will find himself in a perfectly banal room, with whitewashed walls covered with maps and plans that are of no interest to him, a large desk at one end, and a few wooden chairs. There is a thin carpet in the middle of the red-tiled floor and faded green rep curtains temper the glaring light of the afternoon sun. But on this day of May 1794 there were no curtains to the window, and not even a strip of carpet on the floor. There was no desk either, only a long trestle table covered with a tattered green cloth, behind which, on wooden chairs, sat three men, dressed alike in dark blue coats tightly buttoned across the chest, drab breeches and high topped boots, and wearing tricolour sashes around their waist.

===Chinese===
他若有胆量推开这扇门，便会发现自己站在一间再平常不过的屋子里：四壁粉刷过白，墙上挂满他毫无兴趣的地图和图纸，屋子一头摆着一张大书桌，另有几把木椅。红砖地正中铺着一块薄地毯，褪了色的绿梭纹布窗帘，调和着午后烈日的强光。可是在 1794 年 5 月的这一天，窗口没有窗帘，地上连一条地毯也没有。也没有书桌，只有一张条凳架起的长桌，蒙着一块破破烂烂的绿桌布，桌后坐着三个人，坐在木椅上，装束一模一样：深蓝色上衣，胸前扣得严严实实，土黄色马裤，长统马靴，腰间都围着三色绶带。

===Original===
The one who sat in the centre and who appeared to be in supreme authority rested his elbow on the table, and his chin was supported in his hand. He was gazing intently on a man who stood before him, in the centre of the room, the other side of the table; a man who looked footsore and weary and who wore a military uniform all tattered and covered with slime and dust.

===Chinese===
坐在正中、看样子握有至高权威的那一个，肘支在桌上，用手托着下巴，正目不转睛地盯着一个人；那人站在他面前、屋子中央、桌子的另一头，一脸风尘困顿，身上的军装破破烂烂，糊满了泥浆和尘土。

===Original===
The two others also kept their eyes fixed on this man. They were listening with rapt interest to the story which he was relating. Early this morning he and a dozen others also attired in tattered uniforms had come into Orange in a state bordering on collapse. They had made their way to the barracks where the officer in command had mercifully given them food and drink. As soon as they had eaten and drunk, they tried to tell their story; but this was so amazing, not to say incredible, that the officer in command had thought it prudent to send for the superintendent of gendarmerie, who in turn had the men conveyed to the Hôtel de Ville, there to be brought before the Representative of the Convention on special mission who sat with the Committee of Public Safety. And now Lieutenant Godet stood alone to face the Committee; the others had been handed back to the gendarmerie to be dealt with later on. The representative on special mission who sat with the two other Members of the Committee at the table covered with the tattered green cloth, had questioned Godet, and he thereupon embarked upon the story of this amazing adventure. He began by relating the events which three days ago had set the quiet little commune of Laragne seething with excitement. He told of the arrival of the squad of soldiers in magnificent uniforms, under the command of an officer more superb than anything that had ever been seen in the countryside before. He told of the perquisition in the house of Citizen Colombe the grocer, by those magnificent soldiers, of the finding there by them of certain valuables belonging to the ci-devant Frontenacs, valuables which he himself had vainly searched for in the château, the evening before. He told of the arrest of young Colombe: of the high-handed manner in which the superb officer had relieved him, Godet, of his command, and ordered him and his men, together with the ci-devant Frontenac, to join his squad, and to march with him out of Laragne. He had told it all with a wealth of detail, and the members of the Committee had listened in silence and with rapt interest.

===Chinese===
另外两个人也把目光钉在这人身上，凝神屏息地听他讲述自己的遭遇。今天清晨，他同另外十来个人——同样一个个衣衫褴褛——走进了奥朗日，几乎到了垮掉的地步。他们摸到兵营，营里管带的军官发了善心，给了他们吃喝。刚一吃饱喝足，他们便要讲自己的经历；可这经历离奇得吓人，简直可以说是叫人没法信，管带的军官觉得稳妥起见，派人去请宪兵队总监，宪兵队总监又把这一行人押到市政厅，送到同公安委员会一同办公的国民公会特派代表面前。如今单剩戈岱中尉一个人面对委员会；其余的人已经交还宪兵队，听候发落。同另外两位委员坐在那张蒙着破绿布的桌后的特派代表审问了戈岱，戈岱随即讲起这段惊人冒险的始末。他先从三天前那桩把安安静静的拉拉涅小公社搅得群情鼎沸的事件讲起。他讲到那队军装华丽的士兵开进城来，带队军官的气派，乡下人见所未见。讲到那些神气的士兵如何搜查杂货商公民科隆布的家宅，如何在他家搜出旧贵族弗朗特纳克家的几件值钱物件——头天晚上，他本人还曾在庄园里为这些东西白白搜了一场。讲到小科隆布被逮捕；讲到那位神气十足的军官如何蛮横地解除了他戈岱的指挥权，命令他和他的人，连同旧贵族弗朗特纳克，一并编入他的队伍，随他开出拉拉涅。他讲得巨细靡遗，几位委员默默听着，听得入了神。

===Original===
But now the man at the table who was the representative on special mission, and who appeared chief in authority, broke in with an exclamation that was almost one of rage.

===Chinese===
可这时，桌旁那位特派代表——看样子是掌总的一个——猛地插进来一声断喝，几乎怒不可遏。

===Original===
“And do you mean to tell me, citizen lieutenant,” he said in a harsh, rasping voice, “that you could mistake a lot of English spies﻿—for that is what they were, you may take it from me﻿—that you could mistake them, I say, for soldiers of our army. Where were your eyes?”

===Chinese===
「那么你是想告诉我，公民中尉，」他声音又粗又刺耳地说道，「你竟会把一伙英国奸细——他们就是奸细，你只管信我的话——你竟会把他们，我说，当成了我们军队的士兵。你的眼睛长到哪儿去了？」

===Original===
Lieutenant Godet gave a shrug which he hoped would pass for unconcern. In reality he felt physically sick; a prey to overwhelming terror. At first, when he and his men had come in sight of the city, they had felt nothing but relief to see the end of what had been almost martyrdom. It was only afterwards, when he found himself in this narrow room, with its whitewashed walls and its silence, and face to face with those three men, that fear had entered his heart. He felt like an animal in a cage﻿—a mouse looking into the pale, piercing eyes of a cat. He passed his tongue once or twice over his parched lips before he gave reply.

===Chinese===
戈岱中尉耸了耸肩，指望这一下能装出满不在乎的样子。实际上他只觉得浑身发虚，整个人被一阵压倒一切的恐怖攥住了。起初，他和他的人望见这座城的时候，心里只有如释重负——那场几乎算得上受难的苦差总算到了头。只是后来，等他发觉自己置身于这间四壁刷白的窄屋里，四下死寂，同那三个人面对面，恐惧这才钻进了他的心。他觉得自己像关在笼子里的畜生——一只正望着猫那苍白刺人的眼睛的老鼠。他舔了一两下干得冒烟的嘴唇，才开口回答。

===Original===
“I was not the only one, citizen,” he said sullenly, “who was deceived. The whole commune of Laragne was at the heels of those soldiers. My own men were mustered before the pseudo-captain and heard him give words of command.”

===Chinese===
「上当的又不是我一个，公民，」他闷闷地说，「受骗的是整个拉拉涅公社，全都追在那伙士兵屁股后头跑。我手下的人就在那位假上尉面前列队，听他发号施令。」

===Original===
“But Englishmen, citizen lieutenant,” the man at the table argued; “Englishmen! Their appearance! Their speech!”

===Chinese===
「可他们是英国人哪，公民中尉，」桌旁那人反驳道，「英国人！瞧他们那副模样！听他们那口谈吐！」

===Original===
“They spoke as you and I would, Citizen Chauvelin,” Godet retorted, still sullenly, “As for appearance, one man is like another. I could not be expected to know every officer of our army by sight.”

===Chinese===
「他们说话，同您同我一个腔调，公民肖维兰，」戈岱反唇相讥，依旧闷声闷气，「至于模样嘛，人跟人还能差到哪里去。总不能指望我把我们军队里的军官一个个都认得脸熟。」

===Original===
“But you said they were splendidly dressed!”

===Chinese===
「可你说过，他们穿得漂漂亮亮！」

===Original===
“They were. I knew the uniform well enough. Had there been a doubtful button or a galloon wanting I should have spotted it.

===Chinese===
「他们是穿得漂亮。那种军装我熟得很。哪怕有一颗纽扣可疑、少一根饰绦，我也瞧得出来。

===Original===
“But so clean!” one of the others at the table remarked with a sigh, that might have been of envy, “so magnificent!”

===Chinese===
「可他们那么齐整！」桌旁另一个叹了口气说，那口气里没准儿还带着艳羡，「那么神气！」

===Original===
“I knew that there were some *compagnies d’élite*,” the lieutenant rejoined, “attached to certain regiments. How could I guess?”

===Chinese===
「我知道有几个团队附设着精锐连（*compagnies d’élite*），」中尉接道，「我哪里猜得到呢？」

===Original===
“It might have been better for you, if you had,” the man in the centre remarked dryly.

===Chinese===
「你若是猜得到，」正中那一位冷冷地说，「兴许倒好了。」

===Original===
Godet’s wan face took on a more ashen hue; again he passed his tongue over his parched lips.

===Chinese===
戈岱那张蜡黄的脸愈发灰败了；他又舔了舔干裂的嘴唇。

===Original===
“Haven’t we had enough of this?” one of the others at the table now put in impatiently. “We are satisfied that those English spies, or whatever they were, acted with amazing effrontery, which makes me think that perhaps they are a part of that gang that we all know of, and of which Citizen Chauvelin spoke just now. We are also satisfied that Citizen Lieutenant Godet did not show that acumen which an officer in his responsible position should have done. What we want to know now is, what happened after the pseudo-captain of the so-called 33rd division had arrested that young Colombe and marched out of Laragne?”

===Chinese===
「这一套我们还没听够吗？」桌旁另一人不耐烦地插了进来。「那伙英国奸细——不管他们究竟是什么人——干起事来脸皮厚得骇人，这一点我们已经信了；这倒叫我想起，他们兴许就是那一个人人心里有数的匪帮里的角色，正是公民肖维兰方才提到的那一伙。公民中尉戈岱，没能拿出一名身居其职的军官本该有的敏锐来，这一点我们也信了。现在我们要知道的是：那位自称第三十三分队的假上尉，抓了小科隆布、开出拉拉涅之后，又出了什么事？」

===Original===
“And in your interest, citizen lieutenant,” the man in the centre rejoined sternly, “I advise you to make a statement that is truthful in every detail.”

===Chinese===
「而且我劝你，公民中尉，」正中那人严厉地接道，「供词要字字属实，这于你自身有利。」

===Original===
“Had I wished to tell lies,” the soldier retorted sullenly, “I shouldn’t be here now. I should have﻿—”

===Chinese===
「我若是有心扯谎，」那士兵倔头倔脑地顶了回去，「这会儿就不会在这儿了。我本来可以——」

===Original===
“No matter,” the other broke in curtly, “what you would have done. The State desires to know what you did.”

===Chinese===
「不必说，」对方干脆打断了他，「你本来可以怎么怎么，无关紧要。国家要知道的是你干了什么。」

===Original===
“Well!” Lieutenant Godet began after a moment or two during which he appeared to collect his thoughts. “We marched out of Laragne in the direction of Serres. The captain﻿—I still, of course, looked upon him as a captain﻿—had so disposed us that I and my own men were between two squads of his. We were footsore, all of us, because we had had three days’ tramping in the dust, one day battling against hard wind, another with long hours spent in scouring the château of those traitors Frontenacs; we were also very hungry. Remember that we had been dragged out of our beds in the early morning, and not given a chance of getting a bite or drink before starting on the march. But they, the others, were fresh as if they had just come out of barracks with their bellies full.﻿ ﻿… They marched along at a swinging pace, and it was as much as we could do to keep step with them.”

===Chinese===
「好吧！」戈岱中尉沉吟了一两刻，像是先把思路收拢了，这才开了腔。「我们开出拉拉涅，朝塞尔方向走。那位上尉——我自然还把他当作上尉——把队伍布得很妙：我同我的人，正夹在他两拨队伍中间。我们个个脚底都走烂了，因为已经在尘土里连踩了三天：一天顶着硬邦邦的大风赶路，又花一整天的工夫去搜弗朗特纳克那伙叛徒的庄园；我们还饿得慌。别忘了，我们是一清早被人从被窝里拖出来的，上路前连一口吃的、一口喝的都没捞着。可他们，那一伙人，却个个精神抖擞，仿佛刚吃饱喝足从兵营里开出来。……他们迈开大步，一摇一摆地往前赶，我们拼了命，才跟得上他们的步点。」

===Original===
The man’s voice became somewhat more steady as he talked. The note of terror which had been so conspicuous in it at first had given place to one of dull resentment. Encouraged by the obvious interest which his story had evoked in his hearers, he resumed more glibly:

===Chinese===
讲着讲着，这人的嗓音渐渐稳了些。起初那股扎眼的惶恐腔调，已经换成了一腔沉甸甸的怨气。眼见自己的故事分明勾起了听者的兴味，他胆子一壮，口齿也顺溜起来：

===Original===
“About half a league north of Laragne, a bridle-path branches off the high road; into this the captain ordered his company to turn, and we continued to plod along through the dust and in the midday heat, till we came to a tumbledown cottage by the roadside; a cottage flanked by a dilapidated shed, and a bit of garden all overgrown with weeds. Here a halt was called, and the prisoners were ordered out of the wagon. A moment or two later a woman appeared at the cottage door, some words were exchanged between her and the captain, and subsequently, when order to march was given, the prisoners marched along with us; the wagon and horses having been left behind at the cottage.”

===Chinese===
「拉拉涅以北约莫半里格，有一条马道从大路上岔出去；上尉命令队伍转上这条道，我们便继续在尘土里、在正午的暑气里一步步往前蹭，直蹭到路边一座东倒西歪的小屋跟前；屋子一侧搭着一间破棚子，还有一小片荒草没膝的园子。在这儿传令停下，犯人被押下大车。过了片刻，屋门口出现一个女人，她同上尉交谈了几句；随后开拔令一下，犯人便同我们一道步行；大车和马都撂在那座小屋了。」

===Original===
“Didn’t you think this very strange, citizen lieutenant?” one of the men at the table asked; “a wagon and horses which you would naturally presume belonged to the State, being thus left at a tumbledown roadside cottage?”

===Chinese===
「你不觉得这事蹊跷吗，公民中尉？」桌旁一人问道，「一辆大车、几匹马，你顺理成章该认定是国家的财物，就这么撂在路边一座破屋跟前？」

===Original===
“Whatever I may have thought,” the lieutenant replied, “it was not my place to make observations to my superior officer.”

===Chinese===
「我心里想什么姑且不论，」中尉答道，「对上级军官评头论足，可不归我管。」

===Original===
“Superior officer!” the man in the centre remarked, with a gesture of contemptuous wrath.

===Chinese===
「上级军官！」正中那人应了一声，愤然做一个鄙夷的手势。

===Original===
“I think, Citizen Chauvelin,” the accused now put in a little more firmly, “that you are unnecessarily hard on me. There was really nothing to indicate﻿—”

===Chinese===
「我看哪，公民肖维兰，」受审的这一位这时插了话，口气硬了些许，「您对我未免太苛了。实在是什么迹象也没有——」

===Original===
But the other broke in with a vicious snarl:

===Chinese===
可对方一声恶狠狠的咆哮，打断了他：

===Original===
“Nothing to indicate﻿—? Nothing? The eyes of a patriot should be sharp enough to detect a spy or a traitor through any disguise﻿—”

===Chinese===
「什么迹象也没有——？没有？爱国者的眼睛就该尖利，任凭什么伪装，也该一眼看穿奸细和叛徒——」

He paused abruptly, and cast a quick, inquisitorial glance at his two colleagues first, then at the soldier before him. Had he detected a trace, a sign, a flicker of the eyelid that betrayed knowledge of his own past? of the times﻿—numberless now﻿—that he too had been hoodwinked by those bold adventurers who called themselves the League of the Scarlet Pimpernel, and by their chief whose prowess in the art of disguise had marked some of the most humiliating hours in Chauvelin’s career? Calais, Boulogne, Nantes, Paris; each of those great cities had a record of the Terrorist’s discomfiture when brought face to face with that mysterious and elusive Scarlet Pimpernel. Even, now crushed in the hot palm of his hand, he held a scrap of paper which had revealed the author of the plot to which that fool Lieutenant Godet had fallen a victim﻿—just as he, himself, Chauvelin, had done﻿—just like that﻿—and so many times﻿—. The penalty for him had always been more humiliation, a further fall from the original high place which he had once occupied in Paris: and with it the knowledge that one day the masters of France would tire of his failures. Ah! he knew that well enough, he knew that they would tire, and then they would crush him as they had crushed others, whose only crime, like his, had been failure.

===Chinese===
他猛地顿住，先朝两位同僚、又朝面前的士兵飞快扫了一眼，那目光像审讯一般探人。他可曾捕捉到一丝痕迹、一点征兆、一下泄露天机的眨眼皮，表明对方知道他的老底？知道那一回回——如今已数不清了——他自己也叫那伙胆大包天、自号猩红繁笺花同盟的冒险家们蒙蔽过？也叫他们的首领蒙蔽过——那人一手易容改扮的绝技，在肖维兰的生涯里烙下了几段最见不得人的时辰？加来、布洛涅、南特、巴黎；这几座大城，每一座都留着记录：这位恐怖党人一跟那位神秘莫测、来去无踪的猩红繁笺花打了照面，便栽了跟头。甚至此刻，他的手心里还滚烫地攥着一小张纸——就是这张纸，泄露了那桩骗局的始作俑者，害得蠢货戈岱中尉做了冤大头——正像他肖维兰自己栽过的那样——正是那样——还不止多少回——。落到他头上的惩罚，向来是又添一层羞辱，从他在巴黎原先占着的高位上再朝下跌一截；随之而来的，还有那份心知肚明：终有一日，法兰西的主子们要腻烦他的连番败绩。啊！这个他再清楚不过了，他清楚他们终有腻烦的一天，到那时他们就会像碾死旁人一样把他碾死——那些人同他一样，唯一的罪名，就是败绩。

His only claim to immunity, so far, had been the fact that he alone, of all the members of the National Assembly, of all the members of Committees, or of the Executive, knew who the Scarlet Pimpernel really was; he had seen him without disguise; he knew him by name, not only him but some of his more important followers; and when some of the ferocious tyrants, who for the time being were the masters of France, did at times loudly demand the suppression of Citizen Chauvelin, for incompetence that amounted to treason, there were always others who pleaded for him because of that knowledge. Many felt that with the death of Chauvelin, the last hope of capturing that band of English spies would have to be abandoned; and so they pleaded for his retention and their fellow-tyrants allowed him another few months’ grace so that he might accomplish that which they knew was the great purpose of his life. And whenever in the opinion of those bloodthirsty tigers, who held France under their domination, some outlying provincial districts had need of what they called “purging from the pestilence of traitors,” whenever wholesale arrests, perquisitions, wholesale death-sentences or brutal massacres were the order of the day, Citizen Chauvelin was sent down with special powers, always in the vain hope that the Scarlet Pimpernel, emboldened by success, would fall into the trap perpetually set for him.
