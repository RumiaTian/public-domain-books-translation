## XXXVIII / 第三十八章

===Original===
An immense lassitude had held Fleurette in a kind of semiconsciousness, a dreamless sleep from which she woke at intervals, only to open her eyes for a moment, and immediately let the lids, heavy with sleep, fall over them again. It was the reaction insisted on by health and youth against the terrible nerve-strain of that awful day.

===Chinese===
一股极度的倦怠把弗勒蕾特困在一种半昏迷的状态里，像一场无梦的酣睡，她时时从中醒转，却只把眼睛睁开片刻，随即又让沉甸甸载满睡意的眼皮阖落下来。这正是健康与青春执意讨还的一口气，为的是抵住那可怕的一日加在神经上的骇人重负。

===Original===
During the brief intervals while she had a certain consciousness of things about her, she found herself nestling against chéri Bibi’s shoulder! and when, with half-dimmed eyes she looked up at him, and tried to smile between two yawns, she always saw his pale, grave face turned away in profile, gazing straight out before him into the dark recess of the post-chaise, in which apparently they were travelling. She called softly to him once or twice, but he never turned to look at her, only his hand, which felt cold and clammy, would gently stroke her hair.

===Chinese===
在那些她还多少晓得周遭事物的短短间隙里，她发觉自己正偎在亲爱的比比的肩头；每当她睁着半昏的倦眼仰望他，想在两个哈欠之间挤出一丝笑意时，她总看见他那张苍白、凝重的脸侧向一旁，直望着自己前方驿车深处的暗影——看样子，他们正坐着这辆车赶路。她有一两次柔声唤他，可他从不回过头来看她，只有他那只摸上去又冷又黏湿的手，会轻轻抚弄她的头发。

===Original===
How long all this lasted, what happened to her in the intervals of sleep, Fleurette never knew, but there came a time when the chaise rattled unpleasantly over the cobblestones of a city, and lights darted to and fro through the darkness as the vehicle lumbered along through fitfully lighted streets. Fleurette sat up straight; all the sleep suddenly gone out of her eyes.

===Chinese===
这一切持续了多久，睡去的间隙里她又经历了什么，弗勒蕾特一概不知；只记得后来有那么一阵子，驿车喀啦喀啦碾过一座城里的鹅卵石路面，颠得人好不难受；车身顺着时明时暗的街巷隆隆前行，一团团灯火在黑暗中来去穿梭。弗勒蕾特一下子坐直了身子；睡意霎时从眼中退得干干净净。

===Original===
“Where are we going, chéri Bibi?” she asked. “Do you know?”

===Chinese===
「我们要上哪儿去呀，亲爱的比比？」她问，「你知道吗？」

===Original===
“No! I do not,” Bibi replied, and his voice sounded hoarse and hollow. “Would to God I did.”

===Chinese===
「不！我不知道。」比比答道，他的嗓音听来沙哑而空洞。「但愿天主让我知道。」

===Original===
Fleurette had never heard him invoke *le bon Dieu* before, and she tried through the gloom to peer into his face.

===Chinese===
从前弗勒蕾特从不曾听他呼求过好天主（*le bon Dieu*），她便竭力透过昏暗，想看清他的脸。

===Original===
“But we are out of danger now, chéri?” she asked wide-eyed, the old terror which had caused her to lose consciousness in that awful courthouse, once more clutching at her heart.

===Chinese===
「可我们已经脱险了，对不对，亲爱的比比？」她睁大眼睛问；那股曾叫她在一座可怕的法庭里昏死过去的老恐惧，又一次攫住了她的心。

===Original===
“I do not know,” he murmured mechanically; “would to God I did.”

===Chinese===
「我不知道。」他机械地喃喃道，「但愿天主让我知道。」

===Original===
And then as if recalled to himself by the half-drawn sigh of terror from Fleurette, he seized hold of her, and pressed her head against his breast.

===Chinese===
接着，仿佛是弗勒蕾特那半声惊惧的叹息把他唤回了神，他一把将她抓住，把她的头按在自己胸口。

===Original===
“No! No!” he said hastily, “they cannot harm you whilst I’m here to guard you.”

===Chinese===
「不！不！」他慌忙说道，「只要我在这儿护着你，他们就伤不着你。」

===Original===
Just then the coach came to a halt, and a moment later the door was thrown open and a gruff voice said:

===Chinese===
正在这时，驿车停了下来；片刻之后，车门被猛地拉开，一个粗哑的嗓音说道：

===Original===
“Will you descend, citizeness?”

===Chinese===
「您下车吗，女公民？」

===Original===
Fleurette, frightened, clung to Bibi. She made no attempt to move. Whereupon the gruff voice resumed:

===Chinese===
弗勒蕾特吓坏了，紧紧贴住比比。她一动也不动。于是那粗哑的嗓音又开了腔：

===Original===
“If you don’t come willingly, I shall have to send someone to fetch you.”

===Chinese===
「你要是不肯乖乖下来，我就只好叫人来带你下去了。」

===Original===
Fleurette buried her face against Bibi’s coat. His arms held her tightly. A minute, perhaps less, went by, and then﻿—suddenly﻿—she heard a voice﻿—a very gentle, very timid voice this time, saying:

===Chinese===
弗勒蕾特把脸深深埋进比比的外衣。他的双臂紧紧搂住她。一分钟过去了，兴许还不到一分钟，然后——蓦地——她听见一个声音——这一回，是个非常温柔、非常怯生生的声音，正说：

===Original===
“Mam’zelle Fleurette! Oh, Mam’zelle Fleurette, I pray you to turn to me. It is I﻿—Amédé.”

===Chinese===
「弗勒蕾特小姐！哦，弗勒蕾特小姐，求您转过脸来看我。是我——阿梅代（Amédé）。」

===Original===
What had happened? Was she dreaming? Or had she died of fright and gone straight up to heaven? Certain it is that she felt a timid hand upon her shoulder, whilst Bibi’s hold upon her relaxed.

===Chinese===
这是怎么了？她是在做梦？还是她已经吓死过去、魂儿径直升了天？可以肯定的是，她觉出一只怯生生的手落在了自己肩头，而比比搂着她的手臂松了下来。

===Original===
“Hold up the lantern, man,” the gruff voice now broke in upon the delicious silence that ensued, “and let her see that she is not dreaming.”

===Chinese===
「把灯举起来，伙计，」那粗哑的嗓音此刻打断了随之而来的一片妙不可言的静默，「让她瞧瞧，她可不是在做梦。」

===Original===
The light of a lantern flashed across Fleurette’s eyes, she opened them and turned her head, and found herself gazing on M’sieu’ Amédé’s pink and moist face, into his kind eyes full of anxiety and of tenderness, upon his mouth which had taught her how to kiss. Gently, slowly, she extricated herself from Bibi’s embrace. Gently, slowly she seemed to glide into Amédé’s arms.

===Chinese===
一盏提灯的光在弗勒蕾特眼前一闪。她睁开眼，转过头，发现自己正望着阿梅代先生那张红扑扑、汗津津的脸，望进他那双盛满焦灼与温柔的慈和眼睛里，望着那张曾经教会她怎样亲吻的嘴。轻轻地，缓缓地，她从比比的怀抱里挣出身来。轻轻地，缓缓地，她仿佛滑进了阿梅代的臂弯。

===Original===
He carried her whither she knew not. All she knew was that presently she found herself snuggling in a deep, cosy armchair, and that Amédé was kneeling beside her, with his eyes fixed ecstatically upon her as if she were *la sainte Vierge* herself.

===Chinese===
他抱着她去了哪儿，她并不知道。她只知道，不多时她便窝进了一把又深又暖和的扶手椅，阿梅代跪在她身旁，一双眼睛出神地凝望着她，仿佛她就是圣母（*la sainte Vierge*）本人。

===Original===
“Where am I, dear M’sieu’ Amédé?” she asked.

===Chinese===
「我这是在哪儿呀，亲爱的阿梅代先生？」她问。

===Original===
“At Ste. Césaire, Mam’zelle Fleurette,” he replied.

===Chinese===
「在圣塞泽尔（Ste. Césaire），弗勒蕾特小姐。」他答道。

===Original===
“Where is that?”

===Chinese===
「那是什么地方？」

===Original===
“Just outside Nîmes. Your chaise passed through the streets of Nîmes.”

===Chinese===
「就在尼姆（Nîmes）城外。你们的驿车方才穿过了尼姆的街道。」

===Original===
“I daresay,” she said with a tired little sigh. “I was so sleepy; I didn’t know where we were.”

===Chinese===
「多半是吧，」她带着一丝倦倦的轻叹说，「我困得厉害；都不知道咱们到了哪儿。」

===Original===
“We are under the protection of the bravest men that ever lived,” Amédé said slowly. “They saved me from death. They have saved you, Mam’zelle Fleurette.”

===Chinese===
「咱们如今在天底下最勇敢的人们的保护之下。」阿梅代缓缓说道，「他们把我从死亡里救了出来。他们也把您救出来了，弗勒蕾特小姐。」

===Original===
A shudder went through her. She closed her eyes as if to try and shut away the awful visions which his words had conjured up. But his kind, strong arms encircled her closer, and she nestled against him and once again felt comforted and safe. He told her the entire odyssey of his rescue, from the hour when the mock soldiers entered his father’s shop at Laragne, until when his brave rescuers took leave of him outside the derelict cottage by the banks of the Drôme, and he, seeing the pale crescent of the moon rise above the snow-capped crest of La Lance, had solemnly bowed nine times, praying for that joy which today was his at last.

===Chinese===
一阵战栗传遍她的全身。她合上眼睛，仿佛想把他的话唤起的种种可怕景象关在眼外。可他那双慈和而有力的臂膀把她搂得更紧了，她偎在他怀里，又一次感到宽心而安全。他把自家获救的整套历险打头至尾讲给她听：从那些假扮的士兵走进他父亲在拉拉涅（Laragne）的铺子的那一刻起，直到他那些勇敢的救命恩人在德龙河（Drôme）岸边那座荒废小屋外与他作别——他望着一弯淡淡的新月升上拉朗斯峰（La Lance）积雪盖顶的峰巅，庄严地鞠了九个躬，祈求那份欢喜，而今这欢喜终究落到了他的身上。

===Original===
He had spent a few very lonely days in the cottage after that, devoured with anxiety as to the fate of his beloved. He could not eat, he could not sleep. For hours he would watch the filmy crescent of the moon, whose pale light mayhap illumined the window behind which his own Fleurette would also be watching and praying. And three days ago he received the message which he was waiting for. It appeared mysteriously early one morning outside the cottage door. A missive, with a stone put upon it to prevent its being blown away by the wind. How it got there Amédé never knew. It came from the leader of that gallant little league of Englishmen who devoted their lives to helping those in distress. In it he, Amédé, was ordered to walk as far as Crest, to the house of Citizen Marcor the farrier, where he could hire a horse. And then to hie him straightway hither to Ste. Césaire, not sleeping in any wayside inn, but rather in the fields, under shelter of hedges or forest trees, getting food for himself and his horse as best he could. The missive further directed him, on arriving at Ste. Césaire, to seek out an empty house situated in the Rue Basse, and there to wait, for of a surety within two days he would hold his beloved Fleurette in his arms. Amédé had obeyed these commands to the letter. This very morning he had arrived at Ste. Césaire and found the house in the Rue Basse. It was neither empty nor uninhabited. There was furniture in the house, and what’s more there were two friends, two fine English heroes, who had been expecting Amédé and who made him welcome when he arrived. Oh! and didn’t Mam’zelle Fleurette think that these Englishmen were the finest and bravest men that ever lived? As for their chief who was known amongst them as the Scarlet Pimpernel (*le mouron rouge*, M’sieu’ Amédé called it), he surely was more like one of the mythological gods rather than a mere mortal.

===Chinese===
此后，他在那座小屋里独自熬过了好些天孤寂的日子，为他心爱之人的命运熬煎得五内俱焚。他吃不下，睡不着。一连几个钟头，他呆望着那轮薄纱似的弯月，心想这月儿的清辉，兴许也正照着那扇小窗——他自家的弗勒蕾特，多半也在窗后守望着、祈祷着。三天前，他盼来了他等候的消息。一天清早，它神秘地出现在小屋门外：一封信，上面压着块石头，免得叫风吹跑。这信是怎么到的，阿梅代始终无从知晓。它出自那支豪侠的英格兰人小同盟首领之手——这些英国人甘把性命搭上，专门搭救落难之人。信里命令他——阿梅代本人——徒步走到克雷斯特（Crest），到蹄铁匠公民马科尔（Citizen Marcor）家中，在那儿雇一匹马；然后即刻兼程直奔圣塞泽尔，沿途不在任何客栈投宿，宁可露宿田野，借树篱或林间树木遮身，人吃马嚼，都自己设法张罗。信里还吩咐他，一到圣塞泽尔，便去寻下街（Rue Basse）的一座空房子，在那儿候着，因为不出两日，他准能把他心爱的弗勒蕾特搂进怀里。阿梅代把这些吩咐一字不差地照办了。就在今天早晨，他抵达圣塞泽尔，找到了下街的那座房子。房子既不空，也并非无人居住。屋里有家具，更有两位朋友、两位好样儿的英国英雄正候着阿梅代，他一进门便受到热诚的欢迎。哦！弗勒蕾特小姐难道不觉得，这些英国人是开天辟地以来最出色、最勇敢的男子汉吗？至于他们的首领——在他们中间，他以猩红繁笺花之名为人所知（阿梅代先生管它叫 *le mouron rouge*，法语即「猩红繁笺花」）——他实在更像神话里的一位天神，而非区区一个凡人。

===Original===
M’sieu’ Amédé seemed very anxious to know what Mam’zelle Fleurette thought about all these marvellous adventures, but how could she tell him, how could she talk at all when every time she raised her blue eyes to him, he broke off in the midst of a most exciting narrative in order to ask her in a voice vibrating with passion: “*Tu m’aimes* Fleurette?”

===Chinese===
阿梅代先生似乎急切地想知道，弗勒蕾特小姐对这些奇妙的冒险有何想法；可她怎么告诉他呢？她又怎么开得了口呢？——每当她抬起那双蓝眼睛望他，他总要把一段正讲到最来劲处的叙述拦腰打断，用激动得发颤的声音问她：「你爱我吗（*Tu m'aimes*），弗勒蕾特？」
