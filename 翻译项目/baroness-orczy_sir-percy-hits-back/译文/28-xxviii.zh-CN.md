## XXVIII / 第二十八章

===Original===
Ever afterwards to Chauvelin, it seemed as if the Scarlet Pimpernel had heard his challenge, and come in response to his thoughts: for hardly had a couple of days gone before the first rumour reached him of the nearness of his arch-enemy. Twenty-four hours later the hue and cry was all over Orange after a gang of English spies who, it was averred, made it their business to cheat Madame la Guillotine out of her dues.

===Chinese===
在此后的岁月里，在肖维兰看来，事情始终仿佛是猩红繁笺花听见了他的挑战，应着他的心念赶了来：因为前后不出两天，他的宿敌已然近在咫尺的头一阵风声，便传到了他的耳中。二十四小时之后，缉拿的喧嚣已闹遍整个奥朗日——追捕一伙英国奸细，据称这伙人干的营生，就是专叫断头夫人（Madame la Guillotine）收不着她应得的那一份。

===Original===
Citizen Pochart brought Representative Chauvelin the news which already was all over the town, namely that Architect Caristie and his family, consisting of his wife and the small son now aged ten, who was destined one day to become one of Orange’s most distinguished citizens, had unaccountably disappeared from their tumbledown lodgings in the Rue de la République, where they had taken refuge after their house had been requisitioned by the State and turned into a prison-house.

===Chinese===
公民波夏尔（Pochart）给肖维兰代表带来了那桩早已传遍全城的新闻：建筑师卡里斯蒂（Caristie）携家眷——太太，外加那个如今已十岁、注定有朝一日要成为奥朗日最杰出公民之一的小儿子——已经从共和国街（Rue de la République）的破旧寓所里莫名其妙地不见了踪影；自打自家的宅子被国家征用、改成拘押所之后，他们便一直躲在那处寓所里安身。

===Original===
For some time the Sectional Members of the Committee of Public Safety, Citizens Pochart and Danou, ardent patriots, had had their eyes on the Caristie family. Aristos, what? Architect Caristie had designed and built houses in the past for tyrants and ci-devants. The arrest of the entire family had been decided on. It was to have taken place that very evening. Orders to that effect were out, their place of incarceration fixed in the very house where they had once sat in luxury, whilst patriots had starved outside their gates.

===Chinese===
有好一阵子，公安委员会的区委员们——公民波夏尔和公民达努（Danou），两位热忱的爱国志士——早就把眼睛盯在了卡里斯蒂一家身上。贵族佬，还用说吗？建筑师卡里斯蒂从前专为暴君和旧贵族（ci-devant）设计建造宅第。满门逮捕的事已经定了案，原定就在当天晚上动手。相应的命令已经发出，关押的处所也已定妥，恰恰便在那一座宅子里——想当年他们在里头锦衣玉食，爱国志士们却在大门外挨饿。

===Original===
And suddenly the news had spread like wildfire through the town that Architect Caristie, his wife and son had disappeared. Disappeared? Where? asked every patriot. But no one knew. One evening they had still been seen, as usual, taking walking exercise on the riverbank, and the next day when the soldiers of the revolutionary army presented themselves at the door of their lodgings in the Rue de la République and demanded admittance, lo! they received no answer: the lodgings were deserted, the birds had flown from their nests. Nor could the guard at any of the gates of the city throw light upon this mysterious occurrence. No one had passed the gates without duly authenticated passes. Pochart was at his wits’ end and asked counsel of Representative Chauvelin. What was to be done in face of this mystery? Exercise strict supervision at the gates, Chauvelin advised. All passes in future to be signed by himself as well as by the Sectional Members of the Committee of Public Safety.

===Chinese===
突然之间，建筑师卡里斯蒂携妻儿失踪的消息像野火一般传遍了全城。失踪？失到哪儿去了？每一个爱国志士都这样问。可是谁也不知道。头一天傍晚，还有人照旧瞧见他们在河岸边散步消食；第二天，革命军的士兵们来到他们共和国街寓所门前要求进屋，瞧！无人应门：寓所里人去楼空，鸟儿早已飞离了巢穴。城里各处城门的卫兵，也没一个能对这桩蹊跷事说出个所以然来。凡出城的，没有一个不是凭着一应查验妥当的通行证过去的。波夏尔一筹莫展，只得向肖维兰代表讨个主意。面对这个谜团，该怎么办？肖维兰出的主意是：在各城门厉行督察。今后凡通行证，一律除公安委员会区委员签署外，还须经他本人一并签署。

===Original===
The news of the presence of the Scarlet Pimpernel in Orange had acted upon his nerves like a whiplash. Fate, it seemed, was hitting at him from every side: and he felt like a fighter who has been downed once, twice, and then suddenly feels the strength of giants in his blood; the agility of a cat spurring him to a new and stupendous effort. In a vague, fatalistic kind of way the safety of Fleurette and the destruction of the Scarlet Pimpernel appeared to him as inextricably involved. If he allowed his arch-enemy to baffle him now and here, in this city, then Fleurette was doomed and he himself must perish.

===Chinese===
猩红繁笺花现身奥朗日的消息，像一记鞭子抽在他的神经上。命运仿佛正从四面八方朝他打来：他觉得自己像个拳手，被击倒过一回、两回，忽然间浑身血液里涌起巨人的膂力，猫一般的敏捷催逼着他再做一番惊天动地的努力。弗勒蕾特的平安和猩红繁笺花的毁灭，在他心里以一种模糊而宿命般的方式纠缠在一起，解也解不开。倘若他听任宿敌此时此地、就在这座城里把他挫败，那么弗勒蕾特便注定没命，他自己也非灭亡不可。

===Original===
This was the immediate state of mind into which the news of the nearness of the Scarlet Pimpernel had thrown him. A wild desire to link the destruction of his enemy with the safety of his child, to deserve so well of the State, in fact, that the life of Fleurette would be ceded to him as a reward. A drowning man will catch at a straw, and so did Chauvelin catch at this hope, cling to it, turn the thought over and over in his mind. With feverish activity then he spurred those about him into additional vigilance, combated that superstitious terror with which every official these days regarded the gang of English spies and their mysterious chief. He brought to every man’s notice the handsome reward offered by the Revolutionary Government for the capture of the Scarlet Pimpernel, described the Englishman’s appearance, his methods, his motives, worked up every man in Orange, aye, and every woman too, into a state of enthusiasm for the possible capture of this inveterate and daring enemy of France.

===Chinese===
猩红繁笺花逼近的消息，当即将他抛入的，便是这样一副心境：一种狂热的欲望——把仇敌的毁灭同孩子的平安拴在一处；说得再实一点，就是为国家立下大大的功劳，好叫弗勒蕾特的性命当作犒赏发还给他。溺水的人连一根稻草也要抓；肖维兰也正是这样抓住了这个希望，牢牢攥住，把这个念头在心里翻来覆去地掂量。于是他以一股热病似的劲头，鞭策左右的人加倍警戒，全力抗击时下每位官员看待英国奸细团伙及其神秘头目的那种迷信般的恐惧。他把革命政府为缉拿猩红繁笺花而悬出的那笔可观赏金，摆到每个人的眼前，又把那英国人的相貌、手段、动机一一描绘，把奥朗日的每一个男人——可不，连每一个女人也在内——都鼓动得群情亢奋，一心巴望着能捉住这个积仇既久、胆大包天的法兰西之敌。

===Original===
But this particular frame of mind was not destined to endure. Soon memory got to work, recalled unpleasant moments in Calais, Boulogne, in Paris, in Nantes. What if here too, in Orange, the Scarlet Pimpernel should triumph and he Chauvelin once more be forced to eat the bread of humiliation? What if baffled once more, he should lose, at one terrible swoop, both his revenge and his last hope of saving Fleurette? And then it was that first the insidious, the stupendous thought penetrated his brain. Was it Satan himself who had whispered it into his ear? or some army of mocking imps intent upon torturing him to madness? But heavens above, what a thought! The Scarlet Pimpernel and Fleurette! Was that going to be the solution of this terrible impasse? The thought feverishly driven back at first, returned more insistent. Why not? And then again, why not? A young girl, sweet, pretty, innocent, was she not one to arouse those instincts of chivalry which Chauvelin had hitherto affected to despise?

===Chinese===
可是这一种心境注定持久不了。记忆很快就开了工，唤回了加来、布洛涅（Boulogne）、巴黎、南特（Nantes）那些不痛快的旧事。倘若在这里，在奥朗日，猩红繁笺花又一次得胜，而他肖维兰又得再吞一回屈辱的面包，那可怎么好？倘若再一次遭挫，他在可怕的一击之下就要同时输个精光——既赔上他的复仇，又赔上他营救弗勒蕾特的最后一线指望，那可怎么好？就在这时，头一个钻进他脑子里的，便是那个阴险的、骇人的念头。是撒旦亲自把它凑在他耳边说的？还是一小队存心把他折磨到发疯的讥诮小鬼？老天爷在上，这算个什么念头！猩红繁笺花和弗勒蕾特！难道这竟要成为这个可怕僵局的解法么？这个念头起初被他狠命驱开，卷土重来时却愈发咄咄逼人。为什么不呢？接着又问一遍，为什么不呢？一个年轻姑娘，甜美、秀丽、天真无邪，难道不正是能唤起骑士侠义本能的人物么——而这侠义本能，肖维兰向来只是装作鄙夷罢了。

===Original===
What a possibility! Heavens above, what a possibility! His very senses reeled now at the thought. But he allowed his mind to dwell upon it, to weigh its possibilities: to familiarize itself more and more with it. At first it had seemed like madness, but no longer now! His Fleurette! Already Amédé Colombe was far away, under the protection of the Scarlet Pimpernel, what more likely than that﻿—No! no! it could not be! His daughter! His, Chauvelin’s! And in a swift vision he saw himself luring Marguerite Blakeney, the beloved and beautiful wife of the Scarlet Pimpernel to her death, holding her as hostage, threatening her, torturing her. His enemy’s wife! What agonies she had endured at his hands! And now Fleurette! Would not the Scarlet Pimpernel, triumphant and revengeful, gloat over her death, rather than raise a finger to save her life? Would he not gaze with joy on the misery endured by his bitterest foe?

===Chinese===
多么大的一个可能啊！老天爷在上，多么大的一个可能！如今光是想上一想，便叫他神魂摇荡。可他偏偏放任心思在这上头流连，掂量它的种种可能，同它一点一点地厮熟起来。起初它还像是一句疯话，可如今再也不像了！他的弗勒蕾特！阿梅代·科隆布早已走远，正在猩红繁笺花的庇护之下，还有什么比这更顺理成章的呢——不！不！断乎不能！他的女儿！他的，肖维兰亲生的女儿！一霎眼的幻象里，他看见自己正把玛格丽特·布莱克尼（Marguerite Blakeney）——猩红繁笺花那位受尽爱慕的美丽妻子——诱往死地，扣作人质，威吓她，折磨她。他仇敌的妻子！她在他手里忍受过怎样的苦刑！而如今，是弗勒蕾特！得胜而挟着私怨的猩红繁笺花，眼看她死去难道不会幸灾乐祸，倒肯抬一抬手指救她的性命？他难道不会欢欢喜喜地观赏他不共戴天的仇敌所忍受的煎熬？

===Original===
And then once more torturing thoughts would assail him: torturing fears and torturing hopes, hopes? Yes, hopes! “Why should you not hope, man?” whispered an insidious demon in his ear: “the Scarlet Pimpernel does not know, cannot know that Fleurette is your daughter; the daughter of his enemy Armand Chauvelin. To him she is just the sweet, pretty, innocent victim of a system of government which he hates and which he combats. Then why not hope?” And the floating, racking visions of Juliette Marny, and Yvonne de Kernogan, of the Abbé Foucquet and Madeleine Lanoy, would once more haunt the daydreams of this man already steeped in misery, and hope insidious, ever-living hope, would whisper in its turn: “To that long list of innocents snatched from prison and from death by the insolent adventurer whom you hate, why should not the name of Fleurette be added? Fleurette of unknown parentage, just a sweet girl dwelling at Lou Mas, with old Louise and a father known as Armand? Why not?”

===Chinese===
接着，煎熬人的念头便又来袭击他：煎熬人的恐惧，还有煎熬人的希望。希望？不错，正是希望！「我说你呀，为什么就不该指望呢？」一个阴险的魔鬼凑在他耳边低语，「猩红繁笺花并不知道，也不可能知道弗勒蕾特是你的女儿，是他仇人阿尔芒·肖维兰的女儿。在他看来，她不过是个甜美、秀丽、天真无邪的牺牲品，牺牲于那套他既憎恨又抗击的统治之下。那么为什么不该指望呢？」于是，朱丽叶特·马尼（Juliette Marny）、伊冯娜·德·克尔诺甘（Yvonne de Kernogan）、富凯神父（Abbé Foucquet）和玛德琳·拉努瓦（Madeleine Lanoy）那些飘忽萦回的身影，便又来出没于这个早已浸透苦水的人的白日梦；而希望——阴险的、永远不死的希望——也轮到它凑上前低语：「你所恨的那个目中无人的冒险家，从牢狱和死亡手里抢出了一长串无辜者，在这份名单上，为什么就不该添上弗勒蕾特的名字？出身不明的弗勒蕾特，不过是个住在庐庄（Lou Mas）的甜美姑娘，身边伴着老路易丝和一位人称阿尔芒的父亲。为什么不呢？」

===Original===
And day after day, whilst presiding, self-appointed over a tribunal of infamy, Chauvelin’s mind became more and more familiarized with the vision of his Fleurette snatched out of the jaws of death by the man with the lazy eyes and the mocking lips, the demmed, elusive Pimpernel of his daydreams and his sleepless nights.

===Chinese===
就这样过了一天又一天：他自封为主，坐在一座恶名昭彰的法庭上主持审判，与此同时，他心里那个幻象也愈来愈厮熟——他的弗勒蕾特，被那个眼睛慵懒、嘴角含讥的男人从死亡的巨口中夺了回来，正是他白日梦里、无眠长夜中那个「该死的」神出鬼没的繁笺花。
