## VII / 第七章

===Original===
Fleurette had walked very fast. She was still tremendously excited and would have run all the way, only that the road for the most part led sharply uphill and that her heart was beating and pumping wildly with agitation.

===Chinese===
弗勒蕾特一路走得飞快。她还处在极度的兴奋之中，本来真想一路跑着去的，只是这条路大半截陡峭上坡，而且她的心正激动得怦怦狂跳，不受使唤。

===Original===
Strangely enough the gates of the park were wide open, which was very unusual, as they were always kept closed for fear of the footpads and vagabonds. Old Pierre, who was in charge of the gate, was nowhere to be seen. Fleurette ran along the sanded avenue which, bordered by bosquets of acacia and elder, led in sharp curves up to the house. Twilight was slowly fading into evening, but even through the gathering darkness Fleurette noticed that the avenue, usually so beautifully raked and tidy, was all trampled and knocked about as if by the weight of many heavy feet. A minute later the main block of the château stood out before her, like a solid mass silhouetted against a jade-coloured sky. Just above the pointed roof of the turret at the furthest angle of the façade, a star shone with a cold, silvery radiance.

===Chinese===
说来奇怪，园子的大门竟四敞大开——这可是少有的事，平日里为了防备拦路劫匪和流浪汉，大门总是关得严严实实的。看门的老皮埃尔（Pierre）也不见踪影。弗勒蕾特沿着铺沙的林荫道一路跑上去，道旁夹着洋槐和接骨木的树丛，几记急弯，一路通到宅子跟前。暮色正缓缓沉入黑夜，可即便透过愈聚愈浓的昏暗，弗勒蕾特也看得出，这条平日搂耙得漂漂亮亮、整整洁洁的林荫道，如今满遭践踏、凌乱不堪，像被许多沉重的脚碾踏过一般。一分钟后，庄园的正宅已经耸立在她眼前，宛如一大团结实的黑影，衬在玉色的天上。就在宅子正面最远一角那座塔楼的尖顶上方，一颗星正吐着清冷的银辉。

===Original===
The entrance into the main building was under a broad archway which intersected the façade and led into the great farmyard and to the sheds and farm buildings. Fleurette felt vaguely conscious that something unusual had occurred at the château; though the place looked peaceful enough, it appeared strangely deserted, at this hour, when usually men and maids were still about their work. She slipped quickly under the archway, and turning sharply to the left, she came to the great paved hall where servants and farm hands sat at meals.

===Chinese===
正宅的入口开在一道宽阔的拱门底下，拱门横贯宅子正面，通向大场院和一应棚舍、农房。弗勒蕾特隐隐觉出庄园里出了什么不寻常的事；这地方看上去尽管够安静，此时却冷清得出奇——往常这个时辰，男女仆役们本该还在各忙各的活计。她飞快地溜进拱门，猛地向左一拐，来到那座铺石的大厅，仆人和雇工们平日就是在那里用饭的。

===Original===
She found the place in a strange state of confusion: the men﻿—they were all old men these days, as all the young ones had had to go and join the army and fight the English﻿—the men were standing about in groups, talking and gesticulating with their arms, after the manner of the people of Dauphiné who are glib of speech and free with their gestures; the maids were gathered together in the dark corner of the room, holding their aprons to their eyes. The oil lamp which hung from the whitewashed ceiling had not yet been lit: only one or two tallow-candles on the table guttered in their pewter sconces.

===Chinese===
她只见这地方乱作一团，透着古怪：男仆们——如今尽是老头儿了，年轻力壮的都不得不投军去打英国人——正三五成群地站着，挥着胳膊谈说比划，一派多菲内人的做派，那地方的人本来就口齿伶俐、手势豪放；女仆们则挤在屋子昏暗的角落里，围裙掩着眼睛。刷白的天花板上悬着的那盏油灯还没有点：只有桌上一两支牛油蜡烛，在锡镴烛台上淌着烛泪，摇曳欲熄。

===Original===
Old Mathieu, who was the acknowledged father of the staff and who was affectionately called Papa by the maids, was the first to spy Fleurette, who stood disconcerted in the doorway.

===Chinese===
老马蒂厄（Mathieu）是阖府仆役公认的老前辈，女仆们都亲亲热热唤他「老爹」；头一个望见怔在门口的弗勒蕾特的，正是他。

===Original===
“Ah! Mam’zelle Fleurette! Mam’zelle Fleurette!” he exclaimed and lifted his hands and cast up his eyes with an expression of woe: “*Quel malheur! Mon Dieu, quel malheur!*”

===Chinese===
「啊！弗勒蕾特小姐！弗勒蕾特小姐！」他失声叫道，双手一扬，两眼上翻，满脸悲苦：「*Quel malheur! Mon Dieu, quel malheur!*」（多大的祸事啊！我的天，多大的祸事啊！）

===Original===
He had on his bottle-green coat, his buckled shoes, and the white cotton gloves which he wore when he served the family at meals upstairs. They had just finished dinner, it seems, when the awful calamity occurred.

===Chinese===
他身上还穿着那件瓶绿色上衣、带扣的鞋子，戴着他上楼伺候主人用饭时才戴的白棉纱手套。看来那场可怕的灾祸降临时，主子们才刚用罢晚餐。

===Original===
“But what is it, papa?” Fleurette asked, feeling quite ready to cry in sympathy. “What has happened?”

===Chinese===
「可是到底怎么了，老爹？」弗勒蕾特问，一颗心已经酸酸的，直想陪着掉泪。「出了什么事？」

===Original===
“The soldiers, mam’zelle!” papa replied, and a fresh groan went the round of men and women alike, and one or two of the girls sobbed aloud.

===Chinese===
「来大兵了，小姐！」老爹答道。男女仆役间又是一阵新的哀叹，有一两个姑娘索性放声抽泣起来。

===Original===
Now as far as Fleurette was concerned, as recently as this very morning, the inner meaning of these words “the soldiers!” would not perhaps have had much significance. In her own little home, by Bibi’s strict orders, politics and social questions were never discussed. Fleurette was not supposed to know anything of the conflicts that were raging in the great cities, in the name of liberty and of fraternity. The horrors of summary arrests, of perquisitions, of sentences without trial, of wholesale executions, of hatred and revenge and lust were supposed to be beyond her ken; and knowing Bibi’s abhorrence of those subjects being broached, she kept her counsels and her knowledge to herself. But Fleurette was not brainless, and she had a large heart. With her brain she had noted many things which were wilfully kept away from her, and her kind heart had often been filled with pity at many of the tales which she had heard in the village, tales of suffering under this new kind of tyranny, wielded, it seems, in the name of liberty and of the brotherhood of man. She had heard many things and had forgotten nothing; but somehow until this morning these things had seemed remote, like the tales of ogres and demons which are told to frighten children. She had not disbelieved them, but vaguely she felt that nothing of the sort could possibly happen to people whom she knew and loved.

===Chinese===
在弗勒蕾特这方面，就在今天早上以前，「来大兵了！」这几个字的内里分量，她或许还体会不深。在她自己的小家里，按比比的严令，政治和社会问题是从不许谈论的。大城市里以自由和博爱之名闹得沸反盈天的那些纷争，原不该让弗勒蕾特知道分毫。肆意逮捕、抄家搜查、不经审讯的判罪、成批的处决，还有仇恨、报复与贪欲的种种恐怖，本该都在她的见闻之外；她也深知比比最忌讳这些话头被提起，便把自家的见识揣在心里，不出一声。可弗勒蕾特并非没有脑子，她还有一副宽厚的心肠。她用那颗脑袋，早把旁人存心瞒她的事记下了许多；她那副好心肠，也常常为村里听来的许多传闻而满怀怜悯——说的尽是这种新式暴虐之下的苦难，而那暴虐，据说还在以自由和人类博爱的名义行使着。她听过许多事，一件也不曾忘；只是不知怎么，直到今天早上，这些都还显得遥远，像讲来吓唬孩子的妖魔鬼怪的故事。她并非不信，只是隐隐觉得，这一类事万万不会落到她认识和亲爱的人身上。

===Original===
But since this morning many things had occurred which had widened her range of vision. Amédé, who did not want to go away, was being dragged from home in order to be made into a soldier and to fight the English. She had actually seen some of those soldiers, ragged, uncouth and unkempt, with their officer, like a great bully, speaking to her, as if she were a mere slut out of the streets. He had jeered when she told him that she was Citizen Armand’s daughter, and the soldiers had nudged one another and seemed to mock her when she met their glance. Then again she had heard mysterious voices and seen something in the person of a decrepit old faggot-carrier that had thrilled and puzzled her. All these things had worked a subtle change in Fleurette. The tales of ogres and demons no longer appeared quite so remote. The fact that there were evil and sorrow in the world had in a vague kind of way been brought home to her, and also that the spectre of death and misery of which she had only heard was actually lurking in this peaceful corner of Dauphiné and had already knocked at this very door.

===Chinese===
可打今天早上起，桩桩件件的事情接连发生，把她的眼界拓宽了。阿梅代本不愿离家，却硬生生要被拖去当兵，去打英国人。她还亲眼见过那样一些兵：褴褛、粗野、邋遢，那军官像个大恶霸似的对她说话，只当她是个街头拉来的贱货。她说自己是公民阿尔芒的女儿，他反倒嗤笑；士兵们彼此用胳膊肘你捅我、我捅你，待她迎上他们的目光，分明是在嘲弄她。再后来，她又听见了那神秘的嗓音，又在那衰朽的背柴老人身上瞧出了叫她心头一颤、百思不解的东西。这一切，在弗勒蕾特身上起了悄然的变化。妖魔鬼怪的故事，再不像先前那样遥远了。世上原有邪恶与苦难这回事，已隐隐约约落到了她心上；还有那她只听人说起过的死亡与凄惨的幽灵，原来当真潜伏在多菲内这个安谧的角落里，而且已经敲响了这一家的门。

===Original===
“The soldiers!” meant something to her now.

===Chinese===
「来大兵了！」这几个字，此刻对她有了实实在在的分量。

===Original===
“What happened?” she asked, and a dozen tongues were ready to embark on the telling of the tragic event. It was just after dinner. Madame and Mademoiselle had retired to the boudoir, as usual, and Monsieur was sipping his wine in the dining-room, when the great bell at the gate clanged loudly. Pierre, who was still at work in the stables, ran to open the gate: he was almost knocked down by two men on horseback who, without a word or question rode past him along the avenue up to the house followed by a dozen men or more in tattered uniforms and wearing dirty red caps on their heads. The sound of horses and of men stamping the ground brought some of the maids and farmhands out into the yard. The soldiers had come to a halt under the archway, the two riders then dismounted and ordered André to take their horses round to the stables. André, of course, did not dare disobey. Then, as the entrance door was closed, one of the soldiers knocked loudly against it with the butt-end of his musket, whilst one of those who had been on horseback and who appeared to be in authority called out summarily:

===Chinese===
「出了什么事？」她问。十几张嘴顿时都抢着要讲这桩惨祸。事情出在晚餐刚过。夫人和小姐照例退进闺房去了，先生还在饭厅里啜着他的酒，大门上那口大钟忽然当啷啷响得震天。还在马厩里干活的皮埃尔跑去开门：险些被两个骑马的人撞翻在地，那两人一言不发、一句不问，纵马从他身边掠过，径直沿林荫道奔向宅子，身后跟着十来个穿破烂军装、头上扣着脏红帽的兵。马蹄和人踏地的声响，把几个女仆和雇工引到了场院里。士兵们在拱门底下停住，两名骑手随即翻身下马，吩咐安德烈（André）把马牵去马厩。安德烈自然不敢不依。接着，见正门关着，一个士兵便抡起枪托，咚咚地把门砸得山响；骑马人里像是掌权的那个，不容分说地厉声喝道：

===Original===
“Open in the name of the Republic!”

===Chinese===
「以共和国的名义，开门！」

===Original===
Old Mathieu, who was upstairs clearing away the dinner things, terribly scared, ran down to open the door. Again without a word or question, the soldiers pushed past him until they came to the vestibule where they demanded to know where were the ci-devant Frontenacs. Old Mathieu here paused in his narrative and once more threw up his hands and cast up his eyes in horror.

===Chinese===
老马蒂厄正在楼上收拾杯盘，吓得魂不附体，跑下楼来开门。士兵们又是一言不发、一句不问，从他身边挤了过去，直来到前厅，在那里厉声盘问旧贵族（ci-devant）弗朗特纳克一家人的下落。讲到这里，老马蒂厄停了下来，再一次双手一摊、两眼上翻，满脸惊恐。

===Original===
“Ci-devant, Mam’zelle!” he exclaimed. “I ask you! Just as those devils up in Paris talked of our poor martyred King and Queen!”

===Chinese===
「旧贵族，小姐！」他嚷道，「您听听！这不正跟巴黎那帮魔鬼称呼我们可怜的殉难国王和王后一个样吗！」

===Original===
Of course he tried to stop the brigands from going up to see Madame like that, in their dusty shoes and dirty clothes. But what could he do alone among so many? Ah! if only Baptiste and Jean, Achille and Henri had been there, as in the good old days, fine sturdy fellows of the Dauphiné: they would soon have got the better of these down-at-heel bandits, and if it was a case of protecting Madame and Mademoiselle, why! there would have been some broken heads, and the soldiers of the Republic would have sung another song than they were singing now﻿—the muckworms! But there! Henri and André and the lot of the young ones had all been taken for cannon-fodder, to fight against the English, and there were only a few fogies left now like he﻿—Mathieu﻿—and the women.

===Chinese===
他当然想拦住那帮强盗，不让他们就那么蹬着满脚尘土的鞋、裹着一身脏衣裳上楼去见夫人。可他孤身一个，对方人多势众，他有什么办法？唉！要是巴普蒂斯特（Baptiste）和让（Jean）、阿希尔和亨利（Henri）都还在就好了，像从前的好年月那样，个个都是多菲内响当当的壮实汉子：这几个鞋跟磨塌的匪类，早叫他们收拾得服服帖帖；既是保护夫人和小姐的事，哼！保管要打破几颗脑袋，共和国的兵也唱不成眼下这支歌了——一群粪堆里的蛆！可是有什么法子！亨利、安德烈，还有那一拨年轻人，全都当了炮灰，拉去打英国人了；如今只剩下几个老朽——他，马蒂厄——和这帮娘儿们。

===Original===
Anyway, poor old papa was helpless. All he could do was to precede those hellhounds upstairs, so that he might at least warn Monsieur of what was coming. But even this they would not let him do; as soon as he had reached the upstairs landing, the same man who had ordered him to open the front door in the name of the Republic, and who wore a tricolour sash round his middle, this same man grabbed him by the shoulder and thrust him aside as if he were a bundle of faggots. And without more ado, he just walked into the dining-room where Monsieur was still quietly sipping his last glass of wine.

===Chinese===
总而言之，可怜的老爹是无能为力了。他能做的，只有抢在那群地狱恶狗前头上楼，好歹先给先生通个信。可连这个他们也不容他做；他刚上到楼梯口，就是先前喝令他以共和国名义开大门的那个、腰里缠着三色绶带的家伙，一把攥住他的肩膀，把他往旁边一搡，仿佛搡的是一捆柴火。那人二话不说，径直闯进饭厅——先生还在那里安安静静啜着他最后的一杯酒。

===Original===
From seeing Monsieur sitting there, the beautiful long-stemmed wineglass in his hand, his face quite serene, you would have thought that he had heard nothing of the turmoil on the stairs. But he had heard everything, the tramping of feet, the rough voices, the curt command to open in the name of the Republic. He knew what was coming. Perhaps he had expected it long ago. It was well to be prepared for anything these days. Anyway, there he sat, glass in hand, his elbow resting on the table, where Mathieu had but a few minutes ago been engaged in clearing away the dessert. At the rude entry made by all those ragamuffins into his beautifully ordered dining-room, he just turned his head and looked at the men.

===Chinese===
瞧先生坐在那里的模样——手中端着那只杯脚细长的漂亮酒杯，一脸安详——你会以为楼梯上那场喧嚷他一字也没听见。其实他什么都听见了：杂沓的脚步、粗厉的人声、那句以共和国名义开门的简短喝令。他知道来的是什么。兴许他早就料到有这一天了。如今这世道，无论什么都该防个备。总之，他就那么坐着，杯在手中，臂肘支在桌上——几分钟之前，马蒂厄还在那张桌上收拾餐后果点。眼见那群破衣烂衫的小无赖鲁鲁莽莽闯进他收拾得漂漂亮亮的饭厅，他只是回过头去，朝那些人望了望。

===Original===
“In the name of the Republic,” the man with the sash said curtly.

===Chinese===
「以共和国的名义。」缠绶带的那人简短地说。

===Chinese===

===Chinese===

===Chinese===

Upstairs they went, and downstairs; in Madame’s room and in Mademoiselle’s, in the kitchen, the stables, the offices. They interrogated the men, they bullied the women; they turned everything topsy-turvy; they raked about in the hay and the straw of the stables, they scoured the park, they glued their ugly, dirty noses to the sanded paths, to try and find the imprint of footsteps. ===Original===
But Fleurette was silent now. Already she had repented of having said so much. Discretion would have been so much wiser. That was the worst of her: she always allowed her tongue to run away with her. She looked eagerly from one anxious face to the other: well she knew that the little she had said would be talked over and commented on and be made the subject of gossip until it reached the village and possibly even Serres and Sisteron; and God only knew what harm this might do to Madame and Mademoiselle. She bit the tip of her tongue hard just to punish it for having wagged too freely, and seized with a sudden impulse, which she found irresistible, she snatched up a candle from the table, and incontinently turned and fled out of the hall, leaving the others to gape and stare after her, to scratch their heads, and to conjecture.

===Chinese===
