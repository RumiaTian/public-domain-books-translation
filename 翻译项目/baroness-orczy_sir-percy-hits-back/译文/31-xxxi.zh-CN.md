## XXXI / 第三十一章

===Original===
To say that Fleurette had in the past few days become familiarized with the grim mummeries that went on in the common room, would be putting it rather strongly. But she certainly had no longer the same horror of them as she had had at first. The presentment of the mock guillotine still harrowed her, it is true, but she could not help laughing at the antics of the mock Satan and his satellites when they seized the President of the Tribunal and the Public Prosecutor by the feet and dragged them off to an imaginary hell. There was that one man in particular whom she had sometimes noticed before and who was aide to one of the warders, he was very diverting. She used to watch him turning and wriggling his huge body, which he had painted all over with soot and draped in bits of red rags. He made an ideal Satan with tail and horns complete, and sometimes it seemed to Fleurette as if he went through all his antics for the sole purpose of bringing a smile upon her lips. Moreover, in a vague kind of way, she associated him with that lovely letter from Amédé, which she had found inside the folds of her kerchief one evening.

===Chinese===
要说弗勒蕾特在这几天里已经看惯了那间公共大厅里上演的一出出阴森的假面闹戏，未免说得过重了些。可她对这些闹戏，确实已不复起初那般的恐惧了。诚然，演断头台戏的那一幕仍旧剜着她的心，可是看到假撒旦和他那群小鬼揪住庭长和公诉人的双脚、把他们一路拖往一座凭空想象的地狱时的那副滑稽样子，她又禁不住要发笑。其中尤其有一个男人，她先前就不时留意过——他是给一名看守当帮手的——这人非常逗趣。她常常望着他扭动、摆撼那具庞大的身躯：那身子他从头到脚涂满了煤烟，又披挂着一条条红布条。尾巴犄角一应俱全，他扮起撒旦来真是再理想不过；有时弗勒蕾特简直觉得，他把那套把戏从头耍到尾，就只为引她唇边浮起一丝笑意。再说，朦朦胧胧地，她总把他同阿梅代那封可爱的信联想到一处——那封信，是一天傍晚她从头巾的褶缝里发现的。

===Original===
The death of so many who had been her prison-companions at first, especially that of Claire de Châtelard, had deeply affected her. The want of fresh air, of exercise, and above all of love and joy, had begun to affect her health: her cheeks had lost their freshness, her eyes their lustre, her lips their smile.

===Chinese===
起初与她同监的那些人，死了那么多，尤其是克莱尔·德·沙特拉尔（Claire de Châtelard）的死，深深刺痛了她。缺少新鲜空气，缺少活动，更缺少爱与欢乐，她的身子开始吃了亏：脸颊失去了红润，眼睛失去了光彩，嘴唇失去了笑意。

===Original===
It was only in the recreation hour that she would smile sometimes. Always when that big, clumsy, hideous-looking fellow who was some kind of aide to one of the warders, set himself the task of fooling for her benefit. She came to look upon him as a friend, and remembering how mysteriously that letter from Amédé had come inside her kerchief, she would look up whenever he came near her, wondering if he had another such welcome message for her. And one evening﻿—she really had not the least idea how it happened﻿—she found a sealed letter inside her workbasket. And the letter was from chéri Bibi. Oh! the joy of it! The joy! She read, and reread it, and kissed the paper whereon his dear hand had rested. How she had missed Bibi all these days! How she longed to reassure him that she was well and that she trusted and believed in him! As to obeying him in all things, of course she would do it. To begin with, she was not afraid, not the least bit in the world. He was watching over her, and he was so great and powerful that no danger could possibly assail her while he cared for her. She would indeed obey him in all things, hold her peace while that wicked Adèle tried to do her harm; she would hold her peace before the Tribunal just as le bon Jésus had done when he was questioned by his judges.

===Chinese===
只有到了消遣的钟点，她才偶尔会笑一笑。而她的笑，总是落在那个大块头、笨手笨脚、长相凶丑的家伙——就是那个给哪名看守当什么帮手的——存心插科打诨、专为她逗乐的当口。她渐渐把他看作一位朋友；想起阿梅代那封信当初是怎样神不知鬼不觉地进了她的头巾，每逢他走到近旁，她总要抬起眼来，心里琢磨着，他是不是又给她捎来了这样一封叫人欢喜的信。一天傍晚——她实在半点也想不明白这是怎么一回事——她在针线篮（workbasket）里发现了一封封了口的信。信是亲爱的比比（chéri Bibi）写来的。哦！多么欢喜！多么欢喜啊！她读了又读，又亲吻那张纸——他那亲爱的手曾在上面停歇。这些天来，她多么想念比比啊！她多么盼着让他放心：她好好的，她信赖他、笃信他。至于凡事都听他的话，那自然是不消说的。头一条，她不怕，一点儿也不怕，天底下一点儿也不怕。他正守护着她呢；他是那样伟大、那样有力量，只要他惦着她、护着她，无论什么危险都休想近她的身。她真的要凡事都听他的：那歹毒的阿黛尔存心害她，她就缄默不语；到了法庭上，她也照样缄默，就像好耶稣（le bon Jésus）当年受审判官们盘问时那样。

===Original===
Oh! it was a dear, a comforting, an infinitely precious letter. And beside it Fleurette had found a tiny little slip of paper on which were scribbled the words: “Let me have something to take back to the writer, to let him know that you are well. Leave it in your work basket, and I will see to it that he gets it.” And so Fleurette had written a few lines to chéri Bibi; told him that she was well, and assured him that she was not afraid and would obey his commands in all things. She would hold her peace and trust in him. This little note she had hidden that evening in her workbasket and by noon on the following day it had gone.

===Chinese===
哦！这是一封可爱的信，一封暖人的信，一封无限珍贵的信。信的旁边，弗勒蕾特还发现了一小片纸条，上面潦潦草草地涂着几句话：「给我点东西带回去交给写信的人，好叫他知道你平安。把东西留在你的针线篮里，我自有法子送到他手上。」于是弗勒蕾特便给亲爱的比比写了几行字；告诉他她很好，还向他担保：她不怕，凡事都听他的吩咐。她会缄默，会信赖他。这张小字条，她当晚就藏进了针线篮；到第二天晌午，它已经不见了。
