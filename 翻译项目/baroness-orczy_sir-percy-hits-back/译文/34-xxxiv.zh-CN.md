## XXXIV / 第三十四章

===Original===
The first thing that struck Fleurette’s perceptions when she entered that huge room, was that up at the further end of it﻿—upon a raised platform and behind a tall desk, sat Bibi chéri himself. Two other men sat there with him, but Fleurette hardly saw them. It was on Bibi that she looked. She had slept very little during the night. Excitement had kept her awake, as well as the tears and lamentations of two of her roommates who were to appear with her this day before the tribunal.

===Chinese===
弗勒蕾特走进那间大厅，头一件触动她知觉的事便是：大厅顶里头的高台上，一张高高的案桌后面，端坐着亲爱的比比（Bibi chéri）本人。另有两个人同他坐在那里，弗勒蕾特却几乎没有看见。她一双眼睛只顾望着比比。夜里她睡得极少。兴奋使她睡不着，同室两位室友的眼泪和哀叹也叫她睡不着——这一天，她们也要同她一道上革命法庭（Revolutionary Tribunal）受审。

===Original===
And it was Bibi who was to be her judge. Well then obviously she had nothing to fear. One of some fifteen of her fellow-prisoners, she was bustled with them across the room to a wooden bench where they were roughly ordered to sit down. As they crossed the room boos and hisses, and one or two louder cries of execration, greeted them. A few remarks, all of them malevolent, rose above the murmurs.

===Chinese===
而将要给她当法官的，正是比比。那么，明摆着她没有什么可怕的了。她是大致十五名难友中的一个，同他们一道被推推搡搡地拥过大厅，来到一条木长凳前，被粗声粗气地喝令坐下。穿过大厅时，迎接他们的是一片嘘声倒彩，还有一两嗓子更响的咒骂。几句议论从嗡嗡的人语上方冒出来，句句恶毒。

===Original===
“That old man there, I knew him once. Old tyrant. He’s getting his deserts at last.”

===Chinese===
「那边那个老头子，我从前认得他。老恶霸。他到底遭上报应来了。」

===Original===
“Do you see the woman next to him? Five freeborn Frenchwomen she had at a time once, to wait on her and do her hair. Aristo, *va*! It won’t take long to do thy hair tomorrow. One snick with the scissors, what?”

===Chinese===
「你瞧见他旁边那个女人了吗？她从前一度同时使唤着五个自由出身的法国女人，伺候她，替她梳头。贵族佬，哼！明儿再替她梳头，可就用不了多大工夫啦。剪刀咔嚓一剪，是不是？」

===Original===
“That young wench too. Not much more than eighteen, I warrant.”

===Chinese===
「还有那个小丫头。我敢保，至多不过十八岁出头。」

===Original===
“I hear she is a thief as well as a traitor.”

===Chinese===
「我听说她又偷东西，又当叛贼。」

===Original===
“Pity they should have abolished the whipping-post. That would have done the young traitors a world of good.”

===Chinese===
「可惜他们竟把鞭笞柱（whipping-post）给废了。那玩意儿本可以叫这些年轻的叛贼们受益不浅呢。」

===Original===
“Me, I prefer the guillotine; quickest work, eh?”

===Chinese===
「我嘛，宁可要断头台；干起活来最麻利，是不是？」

===Original===
Fleurette had blushed with shame to the roots of her hair. She tried not to look in the direction whence these voices, harsh and coarse, had come. She tried to think of all the prayers which M. le Curé had taught her long ago. She tried to think of M’sieu’ Amédé and of the joy she would have when she saw him again. But she could not shut the gates of her consciousness against all these people who had gathered here for the sole purpose of seeing their fellow-creatures suffer. Men and women and even little children. The women for the most part had brought their knitting, for everyone was knitting socks these days for the brave soldiers who were fighting against the enemies of France, and through the murmur of voices, the monotonous click-click of the needles acted as an irritant upon the nerves.

===Chinese===
弗勒蕾特羞得连头发根都红了。她竭力不去看这些又刺耳又粗鄙的声音传来的方向。她竭力去想本堂神父很久以前教过她的那些祷告。她竭力去想阿梅代先生，想她再见到他时将有多么欢喜。可是她关不上自己意识的大门，挡不住这一群人——他们聚集到这里来，唯一的目的就是要看自己的同类受苦。男人、女人，甚至连小孩子都有。女人们多半带来了毛线活计，因为这些日子，人人都在为同法兰西的敌人作战的勇敢士兵们织袜子；而在一片嗡嗡的人语底下，织针那单调的咔嗒咔嗒声不住地磨着人的神经。

===Original===
All around there appeared to be a sea of faces. And eyes. Innumerable eyes that glared, and mouths that grinned and derided. And above the faces, a sea of red caps with tricolour cockades. Fleurette tried hard not to look. She closed her eyes and tried to murmur the prayers she and M’sieu’ Amédé used to say together when M. le Curé prepared them for their first communion.

===Chinese===
四下里望去，只见一片脸的海洋。还有眼睛。数不清怒瞪着的眼睛，还有一张张咧着讪笑嘲弄的嘴。脸海之上，又是一片缀着三色帽徽的红帽子的海洋。弗勒蕾特竭力不去看。她闭上眼睛，试着低声念诵那些祷告——当年本堂神父为他们俩预备初领圣体时，她同阿梅代先生常在一块儿念的。

===Original===
Bibi wore a hat with feathers. He had a bell in front of him, and this he often tinkled, when the noise from the crowd all around became too great. Once or twice he was addressed as “Citizen President.” Fleurette had never seen him look so stern. The words which he spoke to the accused were not only bitter but terribly cruel. He seemed so unlike her real chéri Bibi, that she caught herself marvelling whether her fancy was not playing her aching eyes some strange and horrible trick.

===Chinese===
比比戴着一顶插羽毛的帽子。他面前摆着一只铃，四周人群的喧声闹得太大时，他便常常把铃摇得叮当响。有一两次，人们称呼他『公民庭长』。弗勒蕾特从来没有见过他神色这样严厉。他对被告们说的话，不但刻毒，而且狠得可怕。他同她真正的、亲爱的比比如此不相像，弄得她不由暗自诧异：莫不是她的幻觉，正对着她酸痛的眼睛耍什么奇怪而可怕的花招？

===Original===
One after the other the names of her fellow-prisoners were called, and one by one they were made to stand up and then walk to the centre of the room and up a couple of shallow steps to a small raised platform round which there was a wooden railing. In every instance as soon as the prisoner mounted this platform, and became as it were the centre of attraction for all these innumerable eyes, he or she would be greeted with groans and hisses and cat’s calls, until Bibi tinkled his bell and loudly demanded silence.

===Chinese===
难友们的名字一个接一个地被点到，他们便一个个被迫站起身，走到大厅中央，再登上两级浅浅的台阶，上一座小小的高台——台子四周围着木栏杆。每逢有囚犯登上这座高台，俨然成了这无数只眼睛齐齐注视的中心，迎接他或她的，便是一片哼声、嘘声和倒彩，直到比比摇响铃铛，大声喝令肃静。

===Original===
A man in a red cap who sat just below Bibi’s desk then stood up and read something out aloud, which Fleurette never understood, but which the crowd apparently did, for the reader was frequently interrupted by more boos and hisses and often cries of execration. After this reading Bibi, or one of the two men who sat beside him, asked the prisoner questions. These were sometimes replied to, but not always. The crowd invariably threw in loud comments on both questions and answers, and Bibi was then forced to tinkle his bell in order to demand silence. And through the noise, the cries and the hisses, the questions and answers, the one sound that was never drowned, and never was still, was the click-click of hundreds of knitting needles.

===Chinese===
随后，一个坐在比比案桌正下方的红帽子男人站起来，高声宣读一件什么东西。弗勒蕾特从来听不懂那是什么，可人群显然听得懂，因为宣读的人不时被打断——又是一片嘘声倒彩，还常常夹着咒骂。读完之后，比比，或者坐在他身旁那两个人中的一个，便向囚犯发问。这些问话有时有人回答，有时没有。人群照例要对问话和答话都高声插上一番议论，于是比比又不得不摇响铃铛，喝令肃静。而在这一切喧闹、呼喊与嘘声、问话与答话之中，唯有一种声音从不曾被淹没，也从来不曾停歇——那便是几百根织针咔嗒咔嗒的声响。

===Original===
The first batch of prisoners to face the Tribunal, were men and women almost unknown to Fleurette. They had not long been brought into the Caristie house, had replaced others who had been Fleurette’s early companions in prison. She had seen them in the common room, acting in the grim farces that were the fashion there, but she had not made friends with them as she had done with Claire de Châtelard or Madame de Mornas. But when came the turn of a woman who had actually been her roommate, who had sat next to her on the bench of the accused, and squeezed her hand ere she was led up to the raised platform with the wooden railing, then, Fleurette felt all her resolution of bravery and trust in Bibi, giving way.

===Chinese===
头一批被提上革命法庭的囚犯，是些弗勒蕾特几乎不认识的男男女女。他们进卡里斯蒂（Caristie）宅子不久，顶替了另一些人——那些人原是弗勒蕾特入狱之初的同伴。她在公共大厅里见过他们，在那些那里时兴的阴惨闹戏里扮演角色；可她不曾同他们结下交情，不像同克莱尔·德·沙特拉尔（Claire de Châtelard）或莫尔纳夫人（Madame de Mornas）那样。可是，当轮到一个真正同她合住一室的女人时——那女人方才还同她并排坐在被告席上，在被带上那座围木栏杆的高台之前，还紧紧握过她的手——弗勒蕾特便觉得，自己那一腔勇敢的决心，还有对比比的信任，全都垮了下去。

===Original===
The heat in the room had become unbearable. The stench of dank and grimy clothes, of perspiring humanity, of hot breaths charged with hate, acted as a pungent soporific. Fleurette’s head fell forward once or twice, her eyes involuntarily closed. For a time she lost consciousness. It was her own name spoken in a stentorian voice that brought her back to reality.

===Chinese===
大厅里的热气已变得叫人受不了。那股恶臭——阴潮垢腻的衣裳、汗涔涔的人群、饱含仇恨的火热呼吸——像一味刺鼻的催眠药，熏得人昏昏欲睡。弗勒蕾特的头有一两次垂了下去，眼睛不由自主地合上了。有一阵子，她失去了知觉。把她拉回现实中来的，是一个洪钟般的嗓音喊出了她自己的名字。

===Original===
“Fleur Chauvelin, *nommée* Armand.”

===Chinese===
「弗勒尔·肖维兰（Fleur Chauvelin），*nommée*（又名）阿尔芒。」

===Original===
Someone nudged her elbow. An impatient voice rasped out a sharp: “*Allons! allons!*” and she found herself dragged to her feet and led by the arm to the raised platform, amidst a din which fortunately was too great to allow her ears to catch individual sounds.

===Chinese===
有人用胳膊肘捅了捅她。一个不耐烦的嗓音厉声甩出一句尖利的「得啦！得啦！（*Allons! allons!*）」，她便觉着自己已被拽起身来，被人抓着胳膊带上那座高台，耳边一片鼎沸——幸而那喧闹大得使她的耳朵捕捉不到任何单个的声音。

===Original===
She looked straight across to Bibi, who was as pale as a waxen image.

===Chinese===
她直直地望向比比，比比的脸色白得像一尊蜡像。

===Original===
“Fleur Chauvelin, *nommée* Armand.”

===Chinese===
「弗勒尔·肖维兰，*nommée*（又名）阿尔芒。」
