## II / 第二章

===Original===
The perambulating army had now gone to rest: some in the cart-shed, some along the benches and tables or floor of the inn. The lieutenant in a bed. Is he not the officer commanding this score of ardent patriots? Therefore must he lie in a bed﻿—old Portal’s bed﻿—whilst old Portal himself and his wife, older and more decrepit than he, can lie on the floor, or in the dog’s kennel for aught Lieutenant Godet cares.

===Chinese===
这支流动大军如今歇下了：有的睡在车棚里，有的横在客栈的长凳上、餐桌上或地板上。中尉嘛，睡在床上。他岂不是统领着这二十名热血爱国者的军官？所以他就非躺在床上不可——老波塔尔的床——至于老波塔尔本人，还有他那位比他更老迈衰朽的老婆，睡地板去，或者睡狗窝去，戈岱中尉（Lieutenant Godet）才不管呢。

===Original===
The two woodcutters﻿—or shall we call them charcoal-burners?﻿—were among the last to leave. They had petitioned for work among the worthies here present: but money was very scarce these days and each man did what work he could for himself, and did not pay another to do it for him. But Papa Tronchet, who was a carpenter by trade and owned a little bit of woodland just by the bridge, close to Armand’s cottage, he promised one of the men﻿—not both﻿—a couple of hours’ work tomorrow: wood-cutting at the rate of two sous an hour, and then he thought it dear.

===Chinese===
那两个伐木工——还是叫他们烧炭工好？——属于最晚离开的那批人。他们曾向在场诸位体面人物讨过活计：无奈这些日子银钱短少，人人都是自己的活自己干，不肯花钱雇旁人代劳。唯独特龙谢老爹（Papa Tronchet）——他本行是木匠，在桥边、紧挨阿尔芒（Armand）小屋的地方有一小片林地——答应给两人中的一个——不是两个——明天几个钟头的活干：砍柴，工钱每小时两苏，就这样他还嫌贵呢。

===Original===
And so the company had dispersed: each man to his home. The two vagabonds﻿—woodcutters or charcoal-burners, they were anyhow vagabonds﻿—found their way into the town. Wearily they trudged, for one of them was very old and the other lame, till they reached a narrow lane at right angles to the riverbank. The lane was made up of stone houses that had overhanging eaves, between which the sun could never penetrate. It was invariably either as damp as the bottom of a well, or as dry and windswept as an iron stovepipe. Tonight it was dry and hot: broken-down shutters, innocent of paint, creaked upon rusty hinges. A smell of boiled cabbage, of stale water and garlic hung beneath the eaves; it came in great gusts down pitch-dark stairways, under narrow doors, oozing with sticky moisture.

===Chinese===
于是这伙人便散了：各自回家去。那两个流浪汉——管他伐木工还是烧炭工，反正就是个流浪汉——一路摸进了镇子。他们拖着疲惫的步子艰难前行，因为其中一个已经老态龙钟，另一个瘸着腿，一直走到一条与河岸成直角的窄巷跟前。巷子两旁全是石屋，屋檐前探，太阳从来照不进屋檐之间。这里向来不是潮得像井底，就是干得像铁烟囱管似的又干又灌风。今晚又干又热：破破烂烂的百叶窗上一星油漆也没有，在生锈的铰链上嘎吱作响。煮圆白菜、馊水和大蒜的气味悬在屋檐底下，一阵一阵地从漆黑的楼梯井里、从渗着黏腻潮气的窄门底下涌出来。

===Original===
The two vagabonds turned into one of these doors and by instinct seemingly, for it was pitch dark, they mounted the stone stairs that squelched with grease and dirt underneath their feet. They did not speak a word until they came to the top of the house, when one of them with a kick of his boot threw open a door; it groaned and creaked under the blow. It gave on an attic-room with sloping ceiling, black with the dirt of ages, and with dormer window masked by a tattered rag that had once been a curtain. There was a wooden table in the centre of the room, and three chairs, with broken backs and ragged rush-seats, dotted about. On the table a couple of tallow candles guttered in pewter sconces.

===Chinese===
两个流浪汉拐进了其中一扇门，似乎是全凭本能，因为里面漆黑一片；他们蹬上石楼梯，脚底下的油腻污垢被踩得咯吱作响。一路上他们一言不发，直到登上房子顶层，其中一个抬脚一靴子踢开一扇门；门在这一击之下呻吟着吱嘎大开。门内是一间阁楼房，天花板倾斜，被经年累月的污垢熏得乌黑，屋顶的老虎窗上一条破布遮掩着，那布从前想必是幅窗帘。房间中央摆着一张木桌，另有三把椅子散在各处，椅背断裂，灯芯草坐垫破烂不堪。桌上两支牛油蜡烛插在锡制烛台里，烛泪淋漓，摇曳欲熄。

===Original===
One of the chairs was drawn close up to the table and on it sat a young man dressed in a well-worn travelling-coat with heavy boots on his feet, and a shabby tricorne hat on the top of his head. His arms were stretched out over the table and his face was buried in them. He had obviously been asleep when the door was so unceremoniously thrown open. At the sound he raised his head and blinked drowsily in the dim light at the newcomers.

===Chinese===
其中一把椅子拉得紧挨着桌子，上面坐着个年轻人，身穿一件穿旧了的旅行外套，脚蹬沉重的靴子，头上扣着一顶寒酸的三角帽。他双臂摊在桌上，脸埋在臂弯里。门被这样不由分说地踹开时，他显然正睡得香。听到响动，他抬起头，就着昏暗的烛光，睡眼惺忪地朝来人眨了眨。

===Original===
Then he stretched out his arms, yawned and gave himself a shake like a sleepy dog, and finally exclaimed in English! “Ah! at last!”

===Chinese===
随后他伸了个懒腰，打个哈欠，像条瞌睡的狗似的抖了抖身子，终于用英语喊道：「啊！总算回来了！」

===Original===
One of the vagabonds﻿—the one namely who at Les Amandiers had appeared with bent shoulders and a hacking cough, now straightened out what proved to be a magnificent athletic figure, and gave a pleasant laugh.

===Chinese===
两个流浪汉中的一个——就是在杏树园（Les Amandiers）里驼背弯腰、咳嗽连连露面的那一位——此刻挺直了身躯，显出一副魁伟矫健的好身材，发出一阵愉快的笑声。

===Original===
“Tony, you lazy dog!” he said, “I’ve a mind to throw you downstairs. What say you, Ffoulkes? While you and I have been breaking our backs and poisoning our lungs with the scent of garlic, I verily believe that this villain Tony has been fast asleep.”

===Chinese===
「托尼（Tony），你这个懒骨头！」他说，「我真想把你扔下楼去。你说呢，福尔克斯（Ffoulkes）？你我在这儿累断了腰杆、让大蒜味熏毒了肺脏，我却深深疑心，这个恶棍托尼倒一直在呼呼大睡。」

===Original===
“By all means let’s throw him downstairs,” assented the second vagabond, now no longer lame, whom his friend had addressed as Ffoulkes.

===Chinese===
「扔吧，干脆把他扔下楼去。」第二个流浪汉附和道，他如今一点也不瘸了——他的伙伴方才称他为福尔克斯。

===Original===
“What would you have me do but sleep?” Tony broke in with a laugh. “I was told to wait, and so I waited. I’d far rather have been with you.”

===Chinese===
「不睡觉还能叫我干什么？」托尼笑着抢白，「人家吩咐我等着，我就等着呗。我宁可跟你们俩在一块儿。」

===Original===
“No, you wouldn’t,” Ffoulkes demurred, “for then you would have been dirtier than I, and almost as filthy as Blakeney. Look at him; did you ever see such a disgusting object?”

===Chinese===
「你才不会呢。」福尔克斯不服，「要是真跟我们在一起，你会比我更脏，脏得差不多赶上布莱克尼（Blakeney）了。瞧瞧他；你生平见过这么恶心的物件吗？」

===Original===
“By Gad!” rejoined Blakeney, surveying his own slender hands coated with coal-dust, grease and grime, “I don’t know when I have been quite so dirty. Soap and water!” he commanded with a lofty gesture, “or I perish.”

===Chinese===
「天哪！」布莱克尼应道，一面端详自己那双修长的手——上头糊满了煤灰、油垢和污泥，「我都记不起什么时候这么脏过了。肥皂加水！」他姿态高华地一挥手，发号施令道，「不然我就要完蛋了。」

===Original===
But Tony gave a rueful shrug.

===Chinese===
可托尼只是懊丧地耸了耸肩。

===Original===
“I have a bit of soap in my pocket,” he said, and diving into the capacious pocket of his coat he produced an infinitesimal remnant of soap which he threw upon the table. “As for water, I can’t offer you any. The only tap in the house is in the back kitchen which Madame, our worthy landlady, has locked up for the night. She won’t have anything wasted, she tells me, not even water.”

===Chinese===
「我口袋里倒是有一小块肥皂。」他说着，把手伸进外套那只宽大的口袋，掏出一小片可怜巴巴的肥皂头，扔在桌上。「至于水，我可没法奉上。全屋唯一的水龙头在后厨房里，咱们那位可敬的房东太太已经锁上过夜了。她告诉我，什么东西都不许浪费，连水也不例外。」

===Original===
“Fine, thrifty people, your Dauphinois,” commented Blakeney, wisely shaking his head. “But did you try bribery?”

===Chinese===
「好极了，真是节俭人家，你们多菲内（Dauphiné）人。」布莱克尼煞有介事地摇着头评点道，「不过，你试过贿赂没有？」

===Original===
“Yes! But Madame﻿—I beg your pardon, Citizeness Martot﻿—immediately called me a cursed aristo, and threatened me with some committee or other. I couldn’t argue with her, she reeked of garlic.”

===Chinese===
「试了！可那位夫人——恕我改口，女公民马尔托（Citizeness Martot）——当即骂我是什么可恶的贵族佬，还抬出这个那个委员会来威胁我。我没法跟她讲理，她满身大蒜味。」

===Original===
“And you, Tony, are an arrant coward,” Blakeney rejoined, “where garlic is concerned.”

===Chinese===
「而你呢，托尼，是个不折不扣的胆小鬼，」布莱克尼回敬道，「一碰到大蒜就露怯。」

===Original===
“I am,” Tony was willing to admit. “That’s why I am so terrified of you both at this moment.”

===Chinese===
「我正是啊。」托尼倒也爽快，「所以这会儿我对你们两位才怕成这样。」

===Original===
They all laughed, and since water was not obtainable, Sir Percy Blakeney, one of the most exquisite dandies of his time, and his friend Sir Andrew Ffoulkes, sat down on rickety chairs, in clothes sticky with dirt, their faces and hands masked by a thick coating of grime. Down the four walls of the small attic-room fillets of greasy moisture trickled and mingled with the filth that lay in cakes upon the floor.

===Chinese===
三个人都放声笑了。既然弄不到水，珀西·布莱克尼爵士——他那个年代最讲究入时的纨绔子弟之一——和他的朋友安德鲁·福尔克斯爵士便在摇摇欲坠的椅子上落了座，衣裳上黏满污垢，脸和手都罩着一层厚厚的油泥。小阁楼的四壁上，一道道油腻的水痕淌下来，同地板上结成块的秽物混作一处。

===Original===
“I can’t bear to look at Tony,” Blakeney said with a mock sigh, “he is too demned clean.”

===Chinese===
「我简直不忍心看托尼，」布莱克尼装模作样地叹了口气说，「他实在太——太干净了。」

===Original===
“We’ll soon remedy that,” was Ffoulkes’s dry comment.

===Chinese===
「这个毛病马上就能治过来。」福尔克斯干巴巴地评论道。

===Original===
And behold Sir Andrew Ffoulkes at close grips with Lord Anthony Dewhurst, and this in silence for fear of disturbing the rest of the house, and bringing attention on themselves. It was a sparring match in the best style, Blakeney acting as referee, its object﻿—to transfer some of the grime that coated the clothes and hands of Sir Andrew on to the immaculate Lord Tony. They were only boys after all, these men, who even now were risking their lives in order to rescue the innocent from the clutches of a bloody tyranny. They were boys in their love of adventure, and in their hero worship, and men in the lighthearted way in which they were prepared for the supreme sacrifice, should luck turn against them.

===Chinese===
且看安德鲁·福尔克斯爵士与安东尼·杜赫斯特勋爵（Lord Anthony Dewhurst）扭作一团，而且全程一声不吭，生怕惊动这屋里旁人的安歇、招人耳目。这是一场最上乘路数的拳斗练习，布莱克尼充任裁判，其宗旨——是把安德鲁爵士衣上手上的一些污垢，转移到一尘不染的托尼勋爵身上。说到底，这帮人都不过是群孩子，此时此刻正拿性命去冒险，好把无辜者从血腥暴政的魔爪底下救出来。在对冒险的酷爱上，在对英雄的崇拜上，他们是孩子；而一旦时运不济，他们又能以一身轻松豁达，随时准备最大的牺牲，在这上头，他们是男子汉。

===Original===
The sparring match ended in a call for mercy on the part of Lord Tony. His face was plastered with grime, his hands as dirty as those of his friends.

===Chinese===
这场拳斗练习以托尼勋爵讨饶告终。他的脸糊满了污垢，一双手也跟两位朋友的一样脏了。

===Original===
“Tony,” Blakeney said finally, when he called a halt, “if her ladyship were to see you now, she would divorce you.”

===Chinese===
「托尼，」布莱克尼终于喊停的时候说道，「尊夫人这会儿要是瞧见你这副模样，非跟你离婚不可。」

===Original===
Vent having been given to unconquerable animal spirits, there was a quick return to the serious business of the day.

===Chinese===
那股压不服的血气发泄过了，众人便迅速回到当天正事上来。

===Original===
“What is the latest?” Lord Tony asked.

===Chinese===
「有什么最新消息？」托尼勋爵问。

===Original===
“Just this,” Sir Percy replied: “That those hellhounds have sent out detachments of soldiers all over the country to ferret out what they are pleased to call treason. We all know what that means. Since their iniquitous ‘Law of the Suspect,’ no man, woman or child is safe from denunciation: now with this perambulating army, summary arrests occur by the thousand. It seems that at any moment any of those brigands can seize you by the coat-collar and drag you before one of their precious committees, who promptly sends you to the nearest guillotine.”

===Chinese===
「就是这样，」珀西爵士答道，「那些地狱恶犬已经往全国各地派出一支支士兵小队，去搜捕他们美其名曰『叛国』的勾当。我们都清楚那意味着什么。自从他们那条伤天害理的『嫌疑犯法令』出笼，无论男女老幼，谁也保不齐被人告发：如今再加上这支流动大军，不经审讯的逮捕动辄上千。看那光景，那帮匪徒里随便哪一个，随时都能揪住你的衣领，把你拖到他们那些宝贝委员会跟前，委员会一转身就把你送上最近的断头台。」

===Original===
“And you came across a detachment of those brigands, I suppose.”

===Chinese===
「这么说，你们是撞上那帮匪徒的一支分队了。」

===Original===
“We have; Ffoulkes and I spent a couple of hours in their company, in the midst of fumes of garlic that would have reduced you, Tony, to a drivelling coward. I vow the smell of it has even infested my hair.”

===Chinese===
「撞上了；我和福尔克斯在他们中间泡了两个来钟头，泡在能把托尼你熏成淌口水的懦夫那样的大蒜味里。我敢起誓，那股味儿连我的头发都钻进去了。」

===Original===
“Anything to be done?” Tony asked simply. He knew his chief well enough to perceive the vein of grim earnestness through all this flippancy.

===Chinese===
「有什么要办的吗？」托尼直截了当地问。他对自家首领知之甚深，听得出这份插科打诨底下那股冷峻的认真劲儿。

===Original===
“Yes!” Blakeney replied. “The squad of brigands who are scouring this part of France are principally after a family named Frontenac, which consists of father, mother and an invalid daughter. I had already found out something about them in the course of the day, whilst I carted some manure for a farmer close by. Beastly stuff, manure, by the way! I tried to get into touch with Monsieur, who is a stubborn optimist, and does not believe that any man could mean harm to him or to his family. I went to him in the guise of a royalist agent, supposed to have inside information of impending arrests. He simply refused to believe me. Well! we’ve met that type of man before. He will have a terrible awakening tomorrow.”

===Chinese===
「有！」布莱克尼答道。「正在法国这一带搜捕的那队匪徒，主要冲着一户姓弗朗特纳克（Frontenac）的人家去，一家三口：父亲、母亲，还有个缠绵病榻的女儿。白天里我给附近一个农夫运肥，已经摸到他们一些底细。顺带说一句，肥料这玩意儿，真够呛！我设法去接触那位先生，他是个顽固不化的乐天派，不信天下竟有人会存心害他、害他的家小。我乔装成一个保王党密使去见他，只说手握即将大逮捕的内线消息。他干脆就是不肯信我。唉！这种人咱们从前也领教过。明天，他就要挨一场骇人的大梦初醒了。」

===Original===
Sir Percy paused for a moment or two, a deep frown between his brows. His keen intellect, alive to all those swift tragedies which he had devoted his life to countermine, was already at work envisaging the immediate future, the personages of the coming drama, husband, wife, invalid daughter; then the perquisition, the arrest, summary condemnation and slaughter of three helpless innocents.

===Chinese===
珀西爵士顿了一两秒钟，眉心拧出深深的皱纹。那些倏忽而至的惨剧，他已将毕生心血用来与之周旋，此刻他敏锐的头脑已经在运作，预演着迫在眉睫的未来、这出即将开场的戏文里的各色人物：丈夫、妻子、病弱的女儿；接着便是搜查、逮捕、草草定罪，以及三个手无寸铁的无辜者惨遭屠戮。

===Original===
“I can’t help being sorry for the man,” he said after awhile, “though he is an obstinate fool! but it is the wife and daughter whom we cannot allow those savage beasts to capture and to kill. I caught sight of them. The girl is pathetic, frail and crippled. I couldn’t bear﻿—”

===Chinese===
「我不由得替那男人难过，」过了一会儿他说道，「虽说他是个顽固的蠢货！可那位太太和小姐，我们决计不能让那群野兽捉去杀了。我望见过她们母女一眼。那姑娘真叫人心疼，孱弱，又有残疾。我不忍——」

===Original===
He broke off abruptly. No need to say more, of course; they understood one another these men who had braved death so often together for love of humanity and for love of sport. Blakeney silent, one firm, slender hand clutched upon the table, was working out a problem of how to rescue three helpless people from that certain deathtrap which was already laid for them. The other two waited in equal silence for orders. The League of the Scarlet Pimpernel! pledged to help the innocent and to save the helpless! One to command, nineteen to obey: the two who were here in this filthy, dark attic-room were the chief’s most trusted officers; but the others were not far away!

===Chinese===
他蓦地收住了话头。自然，不必再多说一句；这几个人为了对人类的爱、也为了对这场游戏的爱，早已一次次共赴死地，彼此心照不宣。布莱克尼沉默着，一只坚定修长的手紧按在桌上，正在推演一道难题：如何把三个束手无策的人，从那个已经为他们布下的必死陷阱里救出来。另外两人也同样沉默地等着号令。猩红繁笺花同盟！誓扶无辜、誓救无助！一人发令，十九人听命：此刻在这间污秽昏暗阁楼里的两位，正是首领最信任的左膀右臂；而其余的人，也就在不远处！

===Original===
Seventeen others! scattered about the countryside, disguised, doing menial work in order to keep in touch with the population, spying, hiding in woods or huts; all of them under orders from their chiefs, and prepared for the call from him.

===Chinese===
还有十七个！散布在四乡，乔装改扮，干着下等杂役以便与百姓声气相通，刺探消息，藏身林间或棚屋；所有人一律听候诸位首领的调遣，随时准备应他一声召唤。

===Original===
“Tony,” Blakeney said at last, “you’d better find Hastings and Stowmaries at once and they must pass the word round to the others. I want three of them﻿—they can draw lots for that﻿—to go to the Four Oaks and there to remain until I can send Ffoulkes to them with full instructions. When you’ve done that, I want you and Ffoulkes to spend the night in and about Les Amandiers, and gather what you can of the projects of those brigands by keeping your ears open.
