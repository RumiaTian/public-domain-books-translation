## XXXIII / 第三十三章

===Original===
The stage was now set for the last act of the tragedy, which the chief actor himself knew could only end one way. He had schemed and planned until he felt that his reason would give way, until he feared that he would lose the nerve and the power of which he had such sore need. He had thought of everything, weighed every possibility from the bribing of prison warders, to the suppression﻿—by murder if need be﻿—of the two witnesses Godet and Adèle. He had thought of turning the tables on Pochart and Danou, by launching accusations against them. But all these plans had to be rejected one by one. Fleurette liberated today through the success of one or the other of these schemes would only be re-arrested on the morrow. The suppression of the witnesses, the arrest of his more powerful enemies, would only rouse more bitter antagonism against himself and, failing in the end to save his Fleurette, would end in precipitating her doom.

===Chinese===
悲剧的最后一幕如今已经搭好了台，而这出戏只能有一种收场，主角本人对此心知肚明。他运筹，设谋，直谋得觉得自己的理性快要撑不住了，直怕自己会失掉那万分急需的胆魄与力量。他什么都想到了，把各种可能一一掂量过：从贿赂监狱看守，到除掉戈岱（Godet）和阿黛尔这两名证人——必要时不妨诉诸杀人。他还想过反咬波夏尔（Pochart）和达努（Danou）一口，对他们发动控告，来个反客为主。然而所有这些计划，只得一个接一个地否弃。靠其中哪一条计谋得手，弗勒蕾特今天获了释，明天也只会再度被捕。除掉证人也好，逮捕权势更大的仇敌也罢，都只会激起对他本人更刻毒的敌意，到头来救不得他的弗勒蕾特，只会加速她的毁灭。

===Original===
Driven by despair, he had at one time pinned his hopes of salvation for the child on the possible interference of the Scarlet Pimpernel, but even that fond and foolish hope had been shattered by his betrayal of his jealously guarded secret. What was there left to hope for? That his power was great enough at the Tribunal to force an acquittal in spite of the witnesses, in spite of Pochart and Danou and all the mob whom they had already gathered round them. The Public Prosecutor, a man of his own making, would not dare to side against him. But there was the populace, the rabble, the swinish multitude, who, now that even the worst type of venal and corrupt jury had been abolished, were judges and jury, advocate and prosecutor all in one. The last word always rested with them, and Pochart and Danou, egged on by envy and revenge, would know how to sway the rabble.

===Chinese===
绝望驱使之下，他一度把搭救这孩子的指望，寄托在猩红繁笺花可能出手干预上头；可就连这点又痴又傻的希望，也随着他泄露了自己严密守护的秘密而化为了泡影。还有什么可指望的呢？无非是指望他在革命法庭上的权势足够大，大到任凭那两名证人、任凭波夏尔和达努以及他们已然纠集在身边的那伙暴民作梗，也能强行断出一个无罪开释。公诉人是他一手扶植起来的人，不敢与他作对。可是还有平民，有贱民，有那帮猪猡一般的乌合之众——如今连最贪赃、最腐败的那一路陪审团都已废除，他们便一身兼任法官与陪审员、辩护人与公诉人了。最后的一锤定音，永远操在他们手里；而波夏尔和达努受着嫉妒与复仇心的撺掇，自有一套法子左右这帮贱民。

===Original===
Chauvelin was not the man to indulge in illusions. He knew well enough﻿—none better﻿—that the passions of hatred and of spite which he himself had engendered and fostered in the hearts of his fellow-men, were turned against him, as they had been turned on all the makers of this bloody revolution, on your Brissots and your Carriers, your Philippe d’Orléans, and your great Danton. They would destroy his exquisite Fleurette as effectually as they had destroyed thousands of others, equally innocent.

===Chinese===
肖维兰不是那种耽于幻象的人。他再清楚不过——天底下没有谁比他更清楚——那股他自己亲手在同胞心里播下又培育起来的仇恨与怨毒的激情，如今调转头来对准了他，正如它们当年也曾对准这场血腥革命的一切缔造者，对准你们那些布里索（Brissot）呀、卡里埃（Carrier）呀，对准你们的菲利普·德·奥尔良（Philippe d’Orléans）、你们那位了不起的丹东。他们会把他的娇美的弗勒蕾特毁掉，毁得同他们毁掉成千上万同样清白无辜的人一样彻底。

===Original===
And now the end had come. No longer could the day be put off. President Legrange and Public Prosecutor Isnard might be arriving in Paris any hour when the new aerial telegraph might be set in motion, or a courier sent down to Orange posthaste and burst the bubble of Chauvelin’s machinations.

===Chinese===
而如今，结局到了。这一天再也无法往后拖了。勒格朗热（Legrange）庭长和公诉人伊斯纳尔（Isnard）说不定哪个钟点就会抵达巴黎——一到那时，新式的空中电报（aerial telegraph）可能随之开动，或是急差十万火急地打发下来，直奔奥朗日，把肖维兰机关算尽织成的那只肥皂泡一捅而破。

===Original===
And then on that afternoon of the 15th of June two things occurred. To begin with when the Public Prosecutor placed before him the printed forms of accusation with the names left in blank, and with them a list of the names of those awaiting trial, Chauvelin with a hand that appeared quite steady, wrote in one of the blank spaces the name of Fleur Chauvelin, *nommée* Armand. Secondly when, an hour later, the captain of the guard stood in the courtyard of the Caristie house reading out the names of those who were to stand their trial on the morrow, Fleurette heard the sound of her own name.

===Chinese===
于是，六月十五日下午，发生了两件事。头一件：公诉人把姓名留白的印制控告书摆到他面前，随同还附了一份候审者的名单，肖维兰用一只看上去相当平稳的手，在其中一处空白里写下了一个名字：弗勒尔·肖维兰（Fleur Chauvelin），*nommée*（又名）阿尔芒。第二件：一个钟头之后，卫兵上尉站在卡里斯蒂（Caristie）宅的院子里，宣读次日即将出庭受审者的名单，弗勒蕾特听见了自己的名字。

===Original===
She was not frightened, nor did she weep. Tears were a thing of the past for her. Twenty days had gone by since she had been happy, more than a fortnight since she had been brought into this house and deprived of air and sunlight and joy. One by one those who had been kind to her in this prison house had gone: Claire de Châtelard, Madame de Mornas, poor Eugénie Blanc, and kind M. de Bollène. Their names had been on the roll-call. The next day they were gone, and Fleurette never saw them again. Lately she had been lonely too. No one had taken the place in her unsophisticated heart of Claire de Châtelard. The only friend she had left was the warder’s aide, the rough scavenger who had brought her the two welcome letters, Amédé’s and Bibi’s. He still continued his antics, joined in the gruesome mummeries which still went on in the common room, and Fleurette somehow had a sense of reassurance when he was nigh. But this night of all nights, after she had heard the captain of the guard read her name upon the roll-call, her grimy friend was not there. Fleurette missed him, and disappointment over his absence was the only sorrowful feeling of which she felt conscious, when she realized that her fate would be decided on the morrow.

===Chinese===
她没有害怕，也没有哭。眼泪于她已是过去的东西了。从她上一次快活算起，二十天已经过去了；从她被带进这座宅子、被夺去空气、阳光和欢乐算起，也有两个多星期了。在这座牢狱般的宅子里，待她好过的人一个接一个都走了：克莱尔·德·沙特拉尔（Claire de Châtelard）、莫尔纳夫人（Madame de Mornas）、可怜的欧仁妮·布朗（Eugénie Blanc），还有和善的德·博莱讷先生（M. de Bollène）。他们的名字上了点名（Roll-call）的名单。第二天人就不在了，弗勒蕾特从此再没能见到他们。近来她还添了一层孤单：在她那颗质朴未凿的心里，谁也没能填补克莱尔·德·沙特拉尔的空位。她仅剩的朋友，便是那个看守的帮手——粗手粗脚的打扫夫，正是他给她送来过那两封受欢迎的信，一封是阿梅代的，一封是比比的。他照旧耍他的把戏，照旧加入公共大厅里还在上演的那一出出阴森森的假面闹戏；不知怎么的，只要他在近旁，弗勒蕾特心里便觉得踏实。可是偏偏在今天这个夜里，在她听过卫兵上尉在点名名单上念出自己的名字之后，她那位满身油泥的朋友却不在。弗勒蕾特惦记他；待她明白自己的命运明天就要见分晓时，因他不在而生出的那点失望，竟是她唯一觉察得到的愁绪。

===Original===
She was not afraid. Had not Bibi enjoined her, begged her to trust him and not to be afraid? She wondered when she would be allowed to see Bibi, whether he would be there tomorrow, at her trial encouraging her with his presence and with his glance when she was made to stand before the judge. She knew that in a sense she had done wrong. She had taken Madame’s valuables and handed them over to Amédé. This she had no right to do, and since Adèle had seen her with M’sieu’ Amédé that evening, and spoken ill of her because of that, she supposed that she would be punished. It was only vaguely that she marvelled what the punishment would be. But she was not afraid because she trusted Bibi. Nor did she regret her actions. If it had all to be done over again, she would act in precisely the same way. The mysterious voice often rang in her ear even now. She had obeyed the commands of *le bon Dieu*, and it was *le bon Dieu* who had chosen a still more mysterious way for saving M’sieu’ Amédé from the consequences of her actions.

===Chinese===
她不害怕。比比不是叮嘱过她、恳求过她，叫她信他、不要害怕么？她盘算着什么时候能获准见上比比一面，盘算着他明天会不会到场——在她的庭审上，当她被带到法官跟前站着的时候，用他的身影、用他的目光给她打气。她知道，从某种意义上讲，她是做下了错事。她拿了夫人的值钱物件，交到了阿梅代手里。这事她原是无权做的；而既然那天傍晚阿黛尔撞见她同阿梅代先生在一起，还为了这个说她的坏话，她料想自己是免不了要受罚的。至于那惩罚会是个什么样子，她也只是朦朦胧胧地有点儿好奇罢了。可她不害怕，因为她信比比。她也不懊悔自己的所作所为。倘若这一切能重来一遍，她还是会分毫不差地照原样再做一回。即使到如今，那神秘的声音仍时常在她耳畔回响。她遵从了好天主（*le bon Dieu*）的诫命，而恰恰是好天主，择定了一条更加玄妙的路子，把阿梅代先生从她那些行为招来的后果里搭救了出去。

===Original===
Thus did Fleurette envisage the day that was to come, with love and trust in her heart for Bibi, and the certainty after all these trials and tabulations of a happy reunion with him and old Louise at Lou Mas.

===Chinese===
弗勒蕾特便是这样，心里怀着对比比的爱与信任，怀着历尽这一切磨难与波折之后、定能同他和老路易丝在庐庄（Lou Mas）欢然团圆的笃定，展望着即将到来的那一天。

===Original===
Not to mention the reunion with M’sieu’ Amédé.

===Chinese===
更不必说同阿梅代先生的团圆了。
