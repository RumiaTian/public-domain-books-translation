**A1. `they-talk-of-divers-matters-in-the-hall.md` / `译文/they-talk-of-divers-matters-in-the-hall.zh-CN.md` 第 13 块**

- 定位：译文第 13 块末尾。EN 块止于 `…then he lifted up his voice and sang:`；CN 块止于「…他便放开喉咙唱道：」
- 缺失：epub `chapter-3.xhtml` 第 25–50 段，共 **26 行 / 24 段（≥25 字）**——雷德斯曼所唱全曲，含舞台指示 `The Minstrel saith:` / `The Maidens answer:`
