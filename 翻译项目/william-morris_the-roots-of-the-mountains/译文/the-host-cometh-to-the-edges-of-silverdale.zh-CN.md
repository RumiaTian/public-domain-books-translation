## XLII The Host Cometh to the Edges of Silverdale / 四十二 大军抵达银谷之缘

===Original===
So wore the night, and when the dawn was come were the two captains afoot, and they went from band to band to see that all was ready, and all men were astir betimes, and by the time that the sun smote the eastern side of Shield-broad ruddy, they had broken their fast and were dight for departure. Then the horns blew up beside the banners, and rejoiced the hearts of men. But by the command of the captains this was the last time that they should sound till they blew for onset in Silverdale, because now would they be drawing nigher and nigher to the foemen, and they wotted not but that wandering bands of them might be hard on the lips of the pass, and might hear the horns’ voice, and turn to see what was toward.

===Chinese===
夜就这样渐渐过去。黎明一到，两位首领便已起身，自一队队人马之间穿行巡视，查看一切是否齐备；众人也都早早起身忙碌。待到朝阳将盾阔（Shield-broad）东坡照得一片通红之时，他们已用罢朝食，整装待发。于是号角在旌旗之畔嘹然响起，振奋了众人之心。然首领们有令：此乃最后一次鸣角，非至银谷吹响进攻之号，不得再作；因为如今他们离敌人将愈逼愈近，谁知那些四散游荡的敌队会不会正紧挨隘口之唇，闻得角声，便掉头来窥探动静。

===Original===
Forth then went the banners of the Wolf, and the men of the vanward fell to threading the rock-maze toward the north, and in two hours’ time were clear of the Dale under Shield-broad. All went in the same order as yesterday; but on this day the Sun-beam would bear her hauberk, and had a sword girt to her side, and her heart was high and her speech merry.

When they left the Dale under Shield-broad the way was easy and wide for a good way, the river flowing betwixt low banks, and the pass being more like a string of little valleys than a mere gap, as it had been on the other side of the Dale. But when one third of the day was past, the way began to narrow on them again, and to rise up little by little; and at last the rock-walls drew close to the river, and when men looked toward the north they saw no way, and nought but a wall. For the gap of the Shivering Flood turned now to the east, and the Flood came down from the east in many falls, as it were over a fearful stair, through a gap where there was no path between the cliffs and the water, nought but the boiling flood and its turmoil; so that they who knew not the road wondered what they should do.

===Chinese===
于是狼之旌旗向前开拔，前队之人开始鱼贯穿过那片乱石迷阵向北进发，两个时辰之后，便已出了盾阔之下的山谷。一切行进次序皆照昨日之旧；只是这一日日光（the Sun-beam）非要亲身披挂锁子甲不可，腰间又悬了一柄剑，她意气高昂，谈笑风生。

离了盾阔之下那座山谷之后，好长一段路都宽阔平易：河水在低矮的两岸之间流淌，这处隘道与其说是一道山豁，倒不如说像一串小山谷连缀而成，与山谷那一边的形势截然不同。然一日已过三分之一之时，道路又渐渐向他们逼窄起来，且一寸一寸往上攀升；末了石壁直逼到河岸，人朝北一望，再无路可寻，唯见一堵石墙。盖因战栗川（the Shivering Flood）的豁口至此折向东去，川水自东方层层跌落，仿佛沿一道可畏的阶梯倾泻而下，那道豁口之中，绝壁与水之间并无路径，唯有翻滚沸腾的洪流与其轰鸣；故不知此路之人，都不免犯愁，不知如何是好。

===Original===
But Folk-might led the banners to where a great buttress of the cliffs thrust itself into the way, coming well-nigh down to the water, just at the corner where the river turned eastward, and they got them about it as they might, and on the other side thereof lo! another gap exceeding strait, scarce twenty foot over, wall-sided, rugged beyond measure, going up steeply from the great valley: a little water ran through it, mostly filling up the floor of it from side to side; but it was but shallow. This was now the battle-road of the Host, and the vanward entered it at once, turning their backs upon the Shivering Flood.

Full toilsome and dreary was that strait way; often great stones hung above their heads, bridging the gap and hiding the sky from them; nor was there any path for them save the stream itself; so that whiles were they wading its waters to the knee or higher, and whiles were they striding from stone to stone amidst the rattle of the waters, and whiles were they stepping warily along the ledges of rock above the deeper pools, and in all wise labouring in overcoming the rugged road amidst the twilight of the gap.

===Chinese===
然而民强将旌旗引至一处：那里一道巨大的崖壁支脉突入路中，几乎直抵水边，正当河道折向东去之拐角处；众人尽力从它旁边绕过，待到了它的另一面——看哪！又是一道极为狭窄的豁口，宽不过二十尺，两壁如墙，崎岖难行之极，自那大山谷中陡然上升：一道细流从中穿过，几乎将豁底的地面从这边到那边漫满；所幸水并不深。这便是大军此刻的征战之路了，前队当即踏入其中，将战栗川抛在身后。

这条狭窄之路既劳苦，又凄凉：时常有巨石悬在头顶之上，如桥一般横跨豁口，将天光遮蔽；他们又别无路可走，唯有那溪流本身而已；故而他们时而涉水及膝、乃至更深，时而在哗然水声之中从一石跨到另一石，时而小心翼翼地沿着深潭上方石壁的岩边踩踏而行，千方百计地在这豁口半明半暗的光影里，征服这条崎岖之路。

===Original===
Thus they toiled till the afternoon was well worn, and so at last they came to where the rock-wall was somewhat broken down on the north side, and great rocks had fallen across the gap, and dammed up the waters, which fell scantily over the dam from stone to stone into a pool at the bottom of it. Up this breach, then, below the force they scrambled and struggled, for rough indeed was the road for them; and so came they up out of the gap on to the open hillside, a great shoulder of the heath sloping down from the north, and littered over with big stones, borne thither belike by some ice-river of the earlier days; and one great rock was in special as great as the hall of a wealthy goodman, and shapen like to a hall with hipped gables, which same the men of the Wolf called House-stone.

There then the noise and clatter of the vanward rose up on the face of the heath, and men were exceeding joyous that they had come so far without mishap. Therewith came weaponed men out from under House-stone, and they came toward the men of the vanward, and they were a half-score of the forerunners of the Wolf; therefore Folk-might and Face-of-god fell at once into speech with them, and had their tidings; and when they had heard them, they saw nought to hinder the host from going on their road to Silverdale forthright; and there were still three hours of daylight before them. So the vanward of the host tarried not, and the captains left word with the men from under House-stone that the rest of the Host should fare on after them speedily, and that they should give this word to each company, as men came up from out the gap. Then they fared speedily up the hillside, and in an hour’s wearing had come to the crest thereof, and to where the ground fell steadily toward the north, and hereabout the scattered stones ceased, and on the other side of the crest the heath began to be soft and boggy, and at last so soft, that if they had not been wisely led, they had been bemired oftentimes. At last they came to where the flows that trickled through the mires drew together into a stream, so that men could see it running; and thereon some of the Woodlanders cried out joyously that the waters were running north; and then all knew that they were drawing nigh to Silverdale.

===Chinese===
他们这般劳顿，直至午后已深，终于来到一处：北面的石壁稍有坍塌，巨石横亘豁口之上，将水拦住，水从坝上自一石至一石稀稀落下，坠入坝底的一处深潭。于是他们就在这瀑布之下，自那豁口攀爬挣扎而上，因那条路实在崎岖难行；就这样，他们从豁口爬了上来，到了开阔的山坡之上——那是一片自北面斜斜而下的大荒原山肩，遍地散布着巨石，大概是远古某条冰河搬运到此的；其中有一块巨石格外惹眼，竟有殷实富户整座厅堂那么大，形状也像一座带斜脊山墙的厅堂，狼之民将这块石头唤作屋石（House-stone）。

于是在那荒原坡面之上，前队的喧腾嘈杂之声扬起，众人因一路无灾无难地行到此地，都喜不自胜。正这时，有持械之人从屋石之下走出来，朝前队之人迎上前来——他们正是狼族十名打前站的先遣；于是民强与面神当即上前与他们交谈，听取他们的消息；听罢，二人觉出并无什么能阻挡大军径直向银谷进发；而此刻距天黑尚有三个时辰的天光。于是前队并不耽搁，首领们给屋石下之人留下话：大军其余各队须立即跟上，每有一队自豁口出来，便将此话传与那一队。然后他们便快步上了山坡，行了一个时辰光景，已到山脊之上，到了地势稳稳向北倾斜之处；这一带散乱的石头已尽，过了山脊另一面，荒原开始变得松软泥泞，末了竟软到这般地步：若非有人明智引路，他们早就要屡屡陷进泥潭之中了。终于他们来到一处，那从各处泥沼中渗出的涓流在此汇成一道溪水，可以看见水在流淌了；当下林中之民里有几人欢然高呼：水是向北流的！于是众人都知道，他们已经逼近银谷了。

===Original===
No man they met on the road, nor did they of Shadowy Vale look to meet any; because the Dusky Men were not great hunters for the more part, except it were of men, and especially of women; and, moreover, these hill-slopes of the mountain-necks led no-whither and were utterly waste and dreary, and there was nought to be seen there but snipes and bitterns and whimbrel and plover, and here and there a hill-fox, or the great erne hanging over the heath on his way to the mountain.

===Chinese===
一路上他们未遇见一人，幽谷之人也不指望遇见什么人；因为黝暗人多半并非好猎手——除非所猎的是人，尤其是女人；况且这些山颈上的山坡哪儿也不通，乃是彻底荒凉凄寂之地，所能见到的不过是鹬鸟、苇鳽、杓鹬、鸻鸟之属，间或一只山狐，或是一只硕大的海雕，正掠过荒原，飞往山中去。

===Original===
When sunset came, they were getting clear of the miry ground, and the stream which they had come across amidst of the mires had got clearer and greater, and rattled down between wide stony sides over the heath; and here and there it deepened as it cleft its way through little knolls that rose out of the face of the mountain-neck. As the Host climbed one of these and was come to its topmost (it was low enough not to turn the stream), Face-of-god looked and beheld dark-blue mountains rising up far off before him, and higher than these, but away to the east, the snowy peaks of the World-mountains. Then he called to mind what he had seen from the Burg of the Runaways, and he took Folk-might by the arm, and pointed toward those far-off mountains.

“Yea,” said Folk-might, “so it is, War-leader. Silverdale lieth between us and yonder blue ridges, and it is far nigher to us than to them.”

But the Sun-beam came close to those twain, and took Face-of-god by the hand and said: “O Gold-mane, dost thou see?” and he turned about and beheld her, and saw how her cheeks flamed and her eyes glittered, and he said in a low voice: “Tomorrow for mirth or silence, for life or death.”

But the whole vanward as they came up stayed to behold the sight of the mountains on the other side of Silverdale, and the banners of the Folk hung over their heads, moving but little in the soft air of the evening: so went they on their ways.

===Chinese===
日落时分，他们正将那片泥泞之地抛在身后；他们在泥沼中央所遇的那道溪水，此时已变得更清、更大，在荒原上宽阔的石质两岸之间哗然奔泻；它一路劈开山颈坡面上隆起的一座座小阜，时而在此处冲出深槽。大军攀上其中一座小阜、来到它顶上时（此阜矮小，并未使溪水改道），面神举目望去，只见远方前方，深蓝色的群山拔地而起，比这些更远处、却偏在东边的，是世界之山的雪峰。他想起在逃奴堡（the Burg of the Runaways）之上所见之景，便拉住民强的手臂，朝那些远山指去。

"是啊，"民强说，"正是如此，战首。银谷就在我们与那蓝色山脊之间，它离我们可比离那些山脊近多了。"

然而日光走到那二人近前，拉住面神的手，说："啊，金鬃，你看见了么？"他转过身来望着她，见她双颊绯红如火，双目闪闪生辉，便低声道："明日，非欢即寂，非生即死。"

可是整个前队上来之时，都驻足观赏银谷那一边群山的景象，族人的旌旗就悬在他们头顶上方，在傍晚柔和的空气里微微飘动、几乎不动：他们便这样继续上路。

===Original===
The sun sank, and dusk came on them as they followed down the stream, and night came, and was clear and starlit, though the moon was not yet risen. Now was the ground firm and the grass sweet and flowery, and wind-worn bushes were scattered round about them, as they began to go down into the ghyll that cleft the wall of Silverdale, and the night-wind blew in their faces from the very Dale and place of the Battle to be. The path down was steep at first, but the ghyll was wide, and the sides of it no longer straight walls, as in the gaps of their earlier journey, but broken, sloping back, and (as they might see on the morrow) partly of big stones and shaly grit, partly grown over with bushes and rough grass, with here and there a little stream trickling down their sides. As they went, the ghyll widened out, till at last they were in a valley going down to the plain, in places steep, in places flat and smooth, the stream ever rattling down the midst of it, and they on the west side thereof. The vale was well grassed, and oak-trees and ash and holly and hazel grew here and there about it; and at last the Host had before it a wood which filled the vale from side to side, not much tangled with undergrowth, and quite clear of it nigh to the stream-side. Thereinto the vanward entered, but went no long way ere the leaders called a halt and bade pitch the banners, for that there should they abide the daylight. Thus it had been determined at the Council of the Hall of the Wolf; for Folk-might had said: “With an Host as great as ours, and mostly of men come into a land of which they know nought at all, an onslaught by night is perilous: yea, and our foes should be overmuch scattered, and we should have to wander about seeking them. Let us rather abide in the wood of Wood-dale till the morning, and then display our banners on the hillside above Silverdale, so that they may gather together to fall upon us: in no case shall they keep us out of the Dale.”

===Chinese===
太阳落下，他们沿溪下行，暮色渐渐袭来；夜降临了，天宇澄澈，星光满天，虽然月亮尚未升起。此时地面已坚实，芳草甘美，遍生野花，四下散布着为风所摧的灌木丛——他们开始步入那道劈开银谷石墙的深壑了；夜风从那正要交战的谷地、从那将来的战场之地，迎面吹来。下行之路起初颇陡，但深壑宽阔，壑的两壁不再是先前一路那些豁口中的笔直石墙，而是破碎的、向后倾斜的（他们翌日便可看清）：一部分是巨石与页状砂砾，一部分为灌木和粗草所覆，间或一道细流沿壁淌下。他们一路前行，深壑愈展愈宽，终于他们已身处一条下通平原的山谷之中——谷地或陡或缓、或崎岖或平坦，那条溪水始终在谷中央哗然奔泻，而他们走在溪的西岸。谷中芳草丰美，柞、梣、冬青、榛树处处散生；末了，大军面前出现了一片树林，将山谷从这边到那边满满占满，林下灌丛并不甚纠缠，近溪一带更是全无杂木。前队便踏入林中，没走多远，几位首领便下令暂停，吩咐立起旌旗——就在此处等候天明。此乃狼之厅议事之时早已定下的；因为民强曾说过："以我们这样一支大军，又多是初入一无所知之地的人，夜间突袭实为凶险：况且我们的敌人未免过于分散，我们还得四处游荡去寻觅他们。不如在林谷的树林中歇到天明，然后在银谷上方的山坡上展开旌旗，好让他们聚拢来攻我们：无论如何，他们休想挡住我们进谷。"

===Original===
There then they stayed, and as each company came up to the wood, they were marshalled into their due places, so that they might set the battle in array on the edge of Silverdale.

===Chinese===
于是他们便在此驻扎下来；每有一队人马来到林中，便被引至各自应分的位置，以便在银谷之缘列阵布战。
