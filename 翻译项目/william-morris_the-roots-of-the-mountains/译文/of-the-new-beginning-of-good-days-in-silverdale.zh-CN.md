## LII Of the New Beginning of Good Days in Silverdale / 五十二 银谷佳日之新始

===Original===
On the third day there was high-tide and great joy amongst all men from end to end of the Dale; and the delivered thralls were feasted and made much of by the kindreds, so that they scarce knew how to believe their own five senses that told them the good tidings.

For none strove to grieve them and torment them; what they would, that did they, and they had all things plenteously; since for all was there enough and to spare of goods stored up for the Dusky Men, as corn and wine and oil and spices, and raiment and silver. Horses were there also, and neat and sheep and swine in abundance. Withal there was the good and dear land; the waxing corn on the acres; the blossoming vines on the hillside; and about the orchards and alongside the ways, the plum-trees and cherry-trees and pear-trees that had cast their blossom and were overhung with little young fruit; and the fair apple-trees a-blossoming, and the chestnuts spreading their boughs from their twisted trunks over the green grass. And there was the goodly pasture for the horses and the neat, and the thymy hill-grass for the sheep; and beyond it all, the thicket of the great wood, with its unfailing store of goodly timber of ash and oak and holly and yoke-elm. There need no man lack unless man compelled him, and all was rich enough and wide enough for the waxing of a very great folk.

===Chinese===
到了第三日，谷中自这头到那头，人人皆大庆，欢声载道；被解救的奴虏（thralls）受诸氏族设宴款待、倍加优礼，几乎不敢相信自家五官所传来的喜讯。

因再无人存心折磨他们、苦待他们；他们随心所欲，一无所缺，万物丰足；盖因当初为黝暗人（the Dusky Men）所蓄之物——麦、酒、油、香料，以及衣帛与银——堆积如山，人人皆取用不尽。马匹、牛、羊、猪，亦所在多有。此外还有那肥美可爱之地土；田亩间日渐长成的麦子；山坡上正盛放的葡萄藤；果园四周及道路两旁，李树、樱桃树、梨树花已落尽，低垂着累累嫩果；佳美的苹果树正当花期，栗树自其虬曲之干上舒展枝柯，荫覆青草。又有肥美之牧地以养马与牛，长满麝香草的山坡之草以饲羊；这一切之外，便是那片大林的密灌丛，其中梣、柞、冬青、枷榆（yoke-elm）之良材，取之不竭。此间无人会感匮乏，除非有人强逼于他；此间一切既丰饶且广袤，足以成就一个极盛之族的繁衍壮大。

===Original===
Now, therefore, men betook them to what was their own before the coming of the Dusky Men; and though at first many of the delivered thrall-folk feasted somewhat above measure, and though there were some of them who were not very brisk at working on the earth for their livelihood; yet were the most part of them quick of wit and deft of hand, and they mostly fell to presently at their cunning, both of husbandry and handicraft. Moreover, they had great love of the kindreds, and especially of the Woodlanders, and strove to do all things that might pleasure them. And as for those who were dull and listless because of their many torments of the last ten years, they would at least fetch and carry willingly for them of the kindreds; and these last grudged them not meat and raiment and houseroom, even if they wrought but little for it, because they called to mind the evil days of their thralldom, and bethought them how few are men’s days upon the earth.

Thus all things throve in Silverdale, and the days wore on toward the summer, and the Yuletide rest beyond it, and the years beyond and far beyond the winning of Silverdale.

===Chinese===
于是众人各归本业，重操黝暗人来犯之前的旧业；起初虽有许多被解救的奴虏饮宴稍过度量，虽则其中有些人于耕作谋生并不甚勤勉；然他们大多心思灵巧、双手娴熟，不久便各自归了本行，无论农耕、百工，皆上手极快。再者，他们深为敬爱诸氏族，尤其敬爱林中汉子（the Woodlanders），凡能使他们欢心之事，无不竭力为之。至于那些因过去十年所受百般折磨而变得迟钝萎靡之人，至少也甘心为诸氏族之人奔走执役；而诸氏族也不吝给他们饭食、衣物、住所，哪怕他们干活甚少，因为诸氏族念及他们昔日为奴的苦日子，又想到人活于世，岁月本自无多。

于是银谷（Silverdale）万物兴旺；日子一天天推向盛夏，推向盛夏之后岁末冬至节（Yuletide）的休歇，推向夺取银谷之后的一年又一年，乃至久远的将来。
