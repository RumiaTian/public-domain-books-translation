## III They Talk of Divers Matters in the Hall / 三 众人于厅中谈论诸事

===Original===
Now Face-of-god, who is also called Gold-mane, rose up to meet the newcomers, and each of them greeted him kindly, and the Bride kissed him on the cheek, and he her in likewise; and he looked kindly on her, and took her hand, and went on up the hall to the dais, following his father and the old man; as for him, he was of the kindred of the House, and was foster-father of Iron-face and of his sons both; and his name was Stone-face: a stark warrior had he been when he was young, and even now he could do a man's work in the battlefield, and his understanding was as good as that of a man in his prime. So went these and four others up on to the dais and sat down before the thwart-table looking down the hall, for the meat was now on the board; and of the others there were some fifty men and women who were deemed to be of the kindred and sat at the endlong tables.

===Chinese===
面神——亦即人称金鬃者——起身迎接新到的客人，众人皆亲切地与他见礼，新娘在他颊上亲了一下，他也同样回亲；他和善地看着她，牵起她的手，随父亲与那位老人继续向厅堂高台走去。至于那位老人，他是此宅宗族中人，乃是铁面及其诸子的养父；他名叫石面：年轻时是一名猛将，至今在战场上仍能做壮汉之事，其心智见识也不减盛年之人。于是这几位连同另外四人登上高台，在横案前坐定，居高临下望着整座厅堂——此时肉食已陈于案上；此外还有约五十名男女，皆被视为同族之人，坐在两侧的纵长案旁。

===Original===
So then the Alderman stood up and made the sign of the Hammer over the meat, the token of his craft and of his God. Then they fell to with good hearts, for there was enough and to spare of meat and drink. There was bread and flesh (though not Gold-mane's venison), and leeks and roasted chestnuts of the grove, and red-cheeked apples of the garth, and honey enough of that year's gathering, and medlars sharp and mellow: moreover, good wine of the western bents went up and down the hall in great gilded copper bowls and in mazers girt and lipped with gold.

===Chinese===
于是长老起身，在肉食之上画了锤符（the sign of the Hammer）——那是他手艺与所奉之神的标记。众人便兴致勃勃地大吃起来，盖因酒肉丰足，尽够食用。席上有面包、有肉（虽不是金鬃的鹿肉），有韭菜，有林中烤熟的栗子，有园圃里红颊的苹果，有当年采得的丰足蜂蜜，还有酸甜参半的欧楂：更有西面草坡所产的佳酿，盛在镀金的大铜碗与镶金包口的枫木碗中，于厅堂里来回传递不绝。

===Original===
But when they were full of meat, and had drunken somewhat, they fell to speech, and Iron-face spake aloud to his son, who had but been speaking softly to the Bride as one playmate to the other: but the Alderman said: "Scarce are the wood-deer grown, kinsman, when I must needs eat sheep's flesh on a Thursday, though my son has lain abroad in the woods all night to hunt for me."

And therewith he smiled in the young man's face; but Gold-mane reddened and said: "So is it, kinsman, I can hit what I can see; but not what is hidden."

===Chinese===
众人吃饱喝足、略带酒意之后，便攀谈起来。铁面高声对儿子说话——方才那儿子不过是在和新娘低声细语，如同一对玩伴一般；只听这位长老说道：「族亲啊，林中的鹿何时竟这般稀少了，竟教我在星期四非得吃起羊肉来——虽说我的儿子为替我打猎，在林中野外宿了一整夜。」

说着，他朝年轻人的脸露出笑意；金鬃却红了脸，说道：「确是如此，族亲；我射得中我看得见的，却射不中那藏起来的。」

===Original===
Iron-face laughed and said: "Hast thou been to the Woodland-Carles? are their women fairer than our cousins?"

Face-of-god took up the Bride's hand in his and kissed it and laid it to his cheek; and then turned to his father and said: "Nay, father, I saw not the Wood-carles, nor went to their abode; and on no day do I lust after their women. Moreover, I brought home a roebuck of the fattest; but I was over-late for Kettel, and the flesh was ready for the board by then I came."

"Well, son," quoth Iron-face, for he was merry, "a roebuck is but a little deer for such big men as are thou and I. But I rede thee take the Bride along with thee the next time; and she shall seek whilest thou sleepest, and hit when thou missest."

===Chinese===
铁面笑道：「你莫不是去了林中汉子（the Woodland-Carles）那里？他们的女子比咱们的表姐妹更美么？」

面神将新娘的手握在自己掌中，吻了一下，贴在脸颊上；然后转向父亲说道：「不曾，父亲，我没见那些林中汉子，也没去他们的住处；我也从不贪慕他们的女子。况且，我还带了一只最肥的獐子回来；只是回来时已误了下锅的时辰，等我到家，案上肉食早已备妥了。」

「也好，儿子，」铁面说道，他心情甚好，「獐子对于像你我这般壮汉来说，不过是只小小的鹿罢了。不过我劝你下次带着新娘同去；她可以在你睡觉时搜寻猎物，你射不中时她来射。」

===Original===
Then Face-of-god smiled, but he frowned somewhat also, and he said: "Well were that, indeed! But if ye must needs drag a true tale out of me: that roebuck I shot at the very edge of the wood nigh to the Motestead as I was coming home: harts had I seen in the wood and its lawns, and boars, and bucks, and loosed not at them: for indeed when I awoke in the morning in that wood-lawn ye wot of, I wandered up and down with my bow unbent. So it was that I fared as if I were seeking something, I know not what, that should fill up something lacking to me, I know not what. Thus I felt in myself even so long as I was underneath the black boughs, and there was none beside me and before me, and none to turn aback to: but when I came out again into the sunshine, and I saw the fair dale, and the happy abode lying before me, and folk abroad in the meads merry in the eventide; then was I full fain of it, and loathed the wood as an empty thing that had nought to give me; and lo you! all that I had been longing for in the wood, was it not in this House and ready to my hand?—and that is good meseemeth."

===Chinese===
面神笑了笑，却也微微皱了皱眉，说道：「那确实再好不过！但你们若非要逼我说出实话不可：那只獐子，是我在归途中、在议事坪（the Motestead）附近的林子边上射的：我在林中和林间草地上见过雄鹿，见过野猪，见过牡鹿，都没有放箭：因为当日清早，我在你们知道的那片林间草地上醒来时，便拿着未上弦的弓走来走去。那时我走着，仿佛在寻找什么——我不知道是什么——要来填补我心中所缺的什么——我也不知道是什么。我在黑色枝叶之下行走时，心中一直怀着这种感觉：身旁无人，前路无人，也没有人可以回头去看：但当我重新走进阳光之中，看见那美丽的山谷、那快乐的家园就在眼前，草地上的人们在傍晚中快活地走动——那时我满心欢喜，只觉得那片林子空空洞洞，什么也给不了我；你们看！我在林中所渴望的一切，岂不都在这座宅子里、都唾手可得么？——在我看来，那便是好的。」

===Original===
Therewith he drank of the cup which the Bride put into his hand after she had kissed the rim, but when he had set it down again he spake once more:

"And yet now I am sitting honoured and well-beloved in the House of my Fathers, with the holy hearth sparkling and gleaming down there before me; and she that shall bear my children sitting soft and kind by my side, and the bold lads I shall one day lead in battle drinking out of my very cup: now it seems to me that amidst all this, the dark cold wood, wherein abide but the beasts and the Foes of the Gods, is bidding me to it and drawing me thither. Narrow is the Dale and the World is wide; I would it were dawn and daylight, that I might be afoot again."

And he half rose up from his place.

===Chinese===
说着，他接过新娘递来的杯子饮了一口——新娘递杯之前，先在杯沿上亲了一下——但他放下杯子后，又开口说道：

「然而此刻，我坐在列祖列宗之宅中，受人敬重、被人深爱；神圣的灶火在下面熠熠生辉；将来要为我生儿育女的她，温柔和善地坐在我身旁；我将来要在战场上率领的那些勇猛少年，正用我这只杯子饮酒：可是在这一切之中，我却觉得，那片幽暗阴冷的林子——其中不过住着野兽与众神之敌——正在召唤我、吸引我前去。山谷太窄，而天地甚宽；我恨不得此刻便是拂晓天明，好让我重新上路。」

他说着，半欠身子要从座上站起来。

===Original===
But his father bent his brow on him and said: "Kinsman, thou hast a long tongue for a half-trained whelp: nor see I whitherward thy mind is wandering, but if it be on the road of a lad's desire to go further and fare worse. Hearken then, I will offer thee somewhat! Soon shall the West-country merchants be here with their winter truck. How sayest thou? hast thou a mind to fare back with them, and look on the Plain and its Cities, and take and give with the strangers? To whom indeed thou shalt be nothing save a purse with a few lumps of gold in it, or maybe a spear in the stranger's band on the stricken field, or a bow on the wall of an alien city. This is a craft which thou mayst well learn, since thou shalt be a chieftain; a craft good to learn, however grievous it be in the learning. And I myself have been there; for in my youth I desired sore to look on the world beyond the mountains; so I went, and I filled my belly with the fruit of my own desires, and a bitter meat was that; but now that it has passed through me, and I yet alive, belike I am more of a grown man for having endured its grip. Even so may it well be with thee, son; so go if thou wilt; and thou shalt go with my blessing, and with gold and wares and wain and spearmen."

===Chinese===
但他父亲对他沉下脸来，说道：「族亲啊，你不过是只半调教的幼犬，舌头倒长得长了：我也看不出你的心思跑到哪里去了，除非是顺着少年人'走得越远、日子越糟'的那条老路去了。那么听着，我来给你出个主意！西地的商人们很快就要带着他们过冬的货色到了。你觉得如何？你可愿意跟他们回去，去看看那平原（the Plain）和它的诸城，和陌生人做些买卖交涉？在他们眼里，你不过是一只装了几块金子的钱袋，或者是在战场上异邦队列里的一杆长矛，又或者是异邦城墙上一张挂着的弓罢了。这是一门你该学的手艺，因为你将来要做首领；这门手艺虽然学起来辛苦，却值得一学。我自己也去过；年轻时我极其渴望去看看山外的世界；于是我便去了，把自己欲望的果实饱餐了一顿，那是一块苦涩的肉；可如今它既已从我身上过去了，而我尚且活着，也许正因为忍受了它的钳制，我反倒更像一个成年的汉子了。你也未尝不能如此，儿子；所以你若要去，便去罢；你可以带着我的祝福去，带着金子、货物、车马和枪兵一同去。」

===Original===
"Nay," said Face-of-god, "I thank thee, for it is well offered; but I will not go, for I have no lust for the Plain and its Cities; I love the Dale well, and all that is round about it; therein will I live and die."

Therewith he fell a-musing; and the Bride looked at him anxiously, but spake not. Sooth to say her heart was sinking, as though she foreboded some new thing, which should thrust itself into their merry life.

===Chinese===
「不，」面神说，「多谢你，这番好意我心领了；但我不去，因为我并不向往那平原与它的诸城；我爱这条山谷，也爱它周围的一切；我要在此生、在此死。」

说罢他便陷入沉思；新娘不安地望着他，却没有说话。说老实话，她的心正在下沉，仿佛预感到有什么新的事情将要发生，要闯入他们快乐的生活中来。

===Original===
But the old man Stone-face took up the word and said:

"Son Gold-mane, it behoveth me to speak, since belike I know the wildwood better than most, and have done for these threescore and ten years; to my cost. Now I perceive that thou longest for the wood and the innermost of it; and wot ye what? This longing will at whiles entangle the sons of our chieftains, though this Alderman that now is hath been free therefrom, which is well for him. For, time was this longing came over me, and I went whither it led me: overlong it were to tell of all that befell me because of it, and how my heart bled thereby. So sorry were the tidings that came of it, that now meseemeth my heart should be of stone and not my face, had it not been for the love wherewith I have loved the sons of the kindred. Therefore, son, it were not ill if ye went west away with the merchants this winter, and learned the dealings of the cities, and brought us back tales thereof."

===Chinese===
这时老人石面接过话头，说道：

「金鬃吾儿，我不得不开口说几句，因为这片野林（the Wildwood），多半我比旁人都更熟悉——这七十年间深有体会，也付出了代价。如今我看得出，你渴望着那片林子、渴望着林子的最深处；你们知道么？这种渴望有时会缠上首领们的子孙——虽然如今这位长老幸而未曾被它缠上，这于他倒是好事。因为，曾几何时，这渴望也曾袭上我心头，我便随它指引而去：因它而遭遇的种种、我的心又如何因此而流血——都不必细说了，太长了。由此传回的消息是如此凄惨，以至于我如今常想：若非出于我对宗族子孙们的这份爱，我的心倒真该是石头做的，而不只是脸罢了。所以，吾儿，你若趁今年冬天跟那些商人往西去，去学学诸城的经营之道，回来给我们讲讲那里的故事，那也未尝不是好事。」

===Original===
But Gold-mane cried out somewhat angrily, "I tell thee, foster-father, that I have no mind for the cities and their men and their fools and their whores and their runagates. But as for the wood and its wonders, I have done with it, save for hunting there along with others of the Folk. So let thy mind be at ease; and for the rest, I will do what the Alderman commandeth, and whatso my father craveth of me."

===Chinese===
金鬃却有些恼怒地叫道：「养父，我告诉你，我对那些城池毫无兴趣——不稀罕城里的男人、蠢货、娼妓和逃徒。至于那片林子和它的奇景，我已经跟它了结了，往后不过是随族人一同去打打猎罢了。所以你大可安心；至于其余的事，长老吩咐什么我便做什么，父亲要我怎样我便怎样。」

===Original===
"And that is well, son," said Stone-face, "if what ye say come to pass, as sore I misdoubt me it will not. But well it were, well it were! For such things are in the wood, yea and before ye come to its innermost, as may well try the stoutest heart. Therein are Kobbolds, and Wights that love not men, things unto whom the grief of men is as the sound of the fiddle-bow unto us. And there abide the ghosts of those that may not rest; and there wander the dwarfs and the mountain-dwellers, the dealers in marvels, the givers of gifts that destroy Houses; the forgers of the curse that clingeth and the murder that flitteth to and fro. There moreover are the lairs of Wights in the shapes of women, that draw a young man's heart out of his body, and fill up the empty place with desire never to be satisfied, that they may mock him therewith and waste his manhood and destroy him. Nor say I much of the strong-thieves that dwell there, since thou art a valiant sword; or of them who have been made Wolves of the Holy Places; or of the Murder-Carles, the remnants and off-scourings of wicked and wretched Folks—men who think as much of the life of a man as of the life of a fly. Yet happiest is the man whom they shall tear in pieces, than he who shall live burdened by the curse of the Foes of the Gods."

===Chinese===
「那很好，吾儿，」石面说，「倘若你说到做到的话——尽管我深怕你做不到。但那样才好啊，才好啊！因为林中有那样一些东西，是的，在你到达林子的最深处之前就会遇上，足以考验最坚强的心。那里有地精（Kobbolds），有不喜爱人类的妖灵（Wights）——在它们看来，人的悲伤不过如同琴弓拉出的声响之于我们。那里栖息着不得安息的鬼魂；那里游荡着矮人和山民，他们是贩卖奇术之人，是赐予毁灭宗族之礼物的施与者；他们锻造那缠绵不去的诅咒、那飞来飞去的杀戮。此外，那里还有以女子之形出现的妖灵的巢穴，她们能将一个年轻人的心从身体里勾出来，再用永不能满足的欲望填满那空处，好以此来嘲弄他、消损他的丈夫气概、将他毁灭。至于那些盘踞在林中的强人，我不必多说什么——因为你手中有一把利剑；至于那些被变成圣地之狼的人，我也不多说了；至于那些凶徒（Murder-Carles）——那些邪恶悲惨的族群残留下来的渣滓——这些人看一条人命，和看一只苍蝇的命没什么两样。然而被他们撕成碎片的人，还算是最幸运的呢；比起那背负着众神之敌的诅咒、苟活在世的人，要好得多了。」

===Original===
The housemaster looked on his son as the old carle spake, and a cloud gathered on his face a while; and when Stone-face had made an end he spake:

"This is long and evil talk for the end of a merry day, O fosterer! Wilt thou not drink a draught, O Redesman, and then stand up and set thy fiddle-bow a-dancing, and cause it draw some fair words after it? ===Chinese===

"Here I drink a double health to Face-of-god and the Bride, and the love that lieth between them, and the love betwixt them twain and us."

While the song was a-singing Face-of-god took the Bride's hand in his and caressed it, and was soft and blithe with her; and she reddened and trembled for pleasure, and called to mind wedding feasts that had been, and fair brides that she had seen thereat, and she forgot her fears and her heart was at peace again.

And Iron-face looked well-pleased on the two from time to time, and smiled, but forbore words to them.

But up and down the hall men talked with one another about things long ago betid: for their hearts were high and they desired deeds; but in that fair Dale so happy were the years from day to day that there was but little to tell of. So deepened the night and waned, and Gold-mane and the Bride still talked sweetly together, and at whiles kindly to the others; and by seeming he had clean forgotten the wood and its wonders.

Then at last the Alderman called for the cup of good night, and men drank thereof and went their ways to bed.

歌声唱起之时，面神握住新娘的手，轻轻抚弄，对她温柔体贴；她红了脸，欢喜得颤抖起来，想起从前参加过的婚宴，想起在那些婚宴上见过的美丽新娘，便忘了方才的忧惧，心中重归安宁。

铁面不时满怀欣慰地看着这两人，面带微笑，却不与他们说话。

厅堂上下众人彼此谈论着古时候发生的事：因为人心气高，渴望着壮举；但在那条美丽的山谷中，年年岁岁如此幸福安宁，竟没有多少事情可讲。夜越来越深，金鬃和新娘仍在甜蜜地低语，偶尔也与旁人和善地攀谈几句；看他的样子，似乎已把那片林子和它的奇景忘得一干二净了。

最后，长老终于吩咐斟上晚安酒，众人饮罢，便各自回房安寝去了。
