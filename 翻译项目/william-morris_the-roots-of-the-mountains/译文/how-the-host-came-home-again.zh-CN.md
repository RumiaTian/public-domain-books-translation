## LVII How the Host Came Home Again / 五十七 大军归乡

===Original===
It was fourteen days before they came to Rosedale; for they had much baggage with them, and they had no mind to weary themselves, and the wood was nothing loathsome to them, whereas the weather was fair and bright for the more part. They fell in with no mishap by the way. But a score and three of runaways joined themselves to the Host, having watched their goings and wotting that they were not foemen. Of these, some had heard of the overthrow of the Dusky Men in Silverdale, and others not. The Burgdalers received them all, for it seemed to them no great matter for a score or so of newcomers to the Dale.

But when the Host was come to Rosedale, they found it fair arid lovely; and there they met with those of their folk who had gone with Dallach. But Dallach welcomed the kindreds with great joy, and bade them abide; for he said that they had the less need to hasten, since he had sent messengers into Burgdale to tell men there of the tidings. Albeit they were mostly loth to tarry; yet when he lay hard on them not to depart as men on the morrow of a gild-feast, they abode there three days, and were as well guested as might be, and on their departure they were laden with gifts from the wealth of Rosedale by Dallach and his folk.

===Chinese===
他们走了十四日方才抵达玫瑰谷；因随军辎重繁多，又无意赶路劳累，且一路天气多半晴朗明丽，林莽于他们并不令人生厌。途中未遭任何祸患。但有二十三名逃亡之人归附了大军——他们一路窥伺其行迹，已知这些人并非敌寇。这些人中，有的已听闻黝暗人在银谷覆灭之事，有的则未有所闻。堡谷之民将他们尽数收留，因多来二十来个新人入谷，在他们看来并非什么了不起的大事。

及至大军抵达玫瑰谷，只见其地秀美可爱；在那里他们遇见了先前随达拉赫同去的本族之人。达拉赫满心欢喜地迎接诸宗族，坚请他们驻留；因他说他们无须急于赶路，他早已派出信使前往堡谷，向彼处之人通报消息。他们虽多半不愿久留，然达拉赫苦苦相劝，谓不可如行会盛宴翌晨之人那般匆匆散去；他们遂在此盘桓三日，受尽上宾之待，临行之际，达拉赫及其族人又将玫瑰谷的珍宝礼物装了他们满载。

===Original===
Before they went their ways Dallach spake with Face-of-god and the chiefs of the Dalesmen, and said:

“Ye have given me much from the time when ye found me in the wood a naked wastrel; yet now I would ask you a gift to lay on the top of all that ye have given me.”

Said Face-of-god: “Name the gift, and thou shalt have it; for we deem thee our friend.”

===Chinese===
临行之前，达拉赫与面神及谷民诸首领交谈，说道：

「自尔等于林中寻得我这赤身露体的流浪汉之时起，尔等已赐我良多；然今我仍欲向尔等再求一桩馈赠，置于尔等所赐一切之上。」

面神道：「说出此馈赠为何，你便可得着；因我们视你为友。」

===Original===
“I am no less,” said Dallach, “as in time to come I may perchance be able to show you. But now I am asking you to suffer a score or two of your men to abide here with me this summer, till I see how this folk newborn again is like to deal with me. For pleasure and a fair life have become so strange to them, that they scarce know what to do with them, or how to live; and unless all is to go awry, I must needs command and forbid; and though belike they love me, yet they fear me not; so that when my commandment pleaseth them, they do as I bid, and when it pleaseth them not, they do contrary to my bidding; for it hath got into their minds that I shall in no case lift a hand against them, which indeed is the very sooth. But your folk they fear as warriors of the world, who have slain the Dusky Men in the Marketplace of Silverstead; and they are of alien blood to them, men who will do as their friend biddeth (think our folk) against them who are neither friends or foes. With such help I shall be well holpen.”

===Chinese===
达拉赫道：「我所求不亚于此，来日我或许能向尔等证之。然今我所求者，是请尔等容许二三十名部下于此留驻一夏，直至我看清这群重获新生之民将如何与我相处。因欢乐与美好的生活于他们已变得如此陌生，他们几乎不知如何消受，不知如何度日；除非任一切尽归败坏，我势必发号施令、有所禁断；然他们虽多半爱戴我，却不畏惧我；故我所发命令合其心意时，他们便照办，不合心意时，便反其道而行之；因他们心中已认定我绝不会向他们动手，而这确是实情。然尔等之人，他们则视作当世武士而畏惧之——这些人曾在银镇的市集上斩杀黝暗人；且与他们血统殊异，是些（我们的人如此想）会照朋友之命行事、去对付那些对他们而言既非友亦非敌之人。有此臂助，我便大有依靠了。」

===Original===
In such wise spake Dallach; and Face-of-god and the chiefs said that so it should be, if men could be found willing to abide in Rosedale for a while. And when the matter was put abroad, there was no lack of such men amongst the younger warriors, who had noted that the dale was fair amongst dales and its women fairer yet amongst women.

===Chinese===
达拉赫如此说道；面神与众首领皆言此事当允，只要能寻得愿意暂留玫瑰谷之人。此事一传开，年轻武士之中便不乏应募者——他们早已留意到，此谷于诸谷之中最为秀美，而其女子于众女之中更为姣好。

===Original===
So two score and ten of the Burgdale men abode in Rosedale, no one of whom was of more than twenty and five winters. Forsooth divers of them set up house in Rosedale, and never came back to Burgdale, save as guests. For a half score were wedded in Rosedale before the year’s ending; and seven more, who had also taken to them wives of the goodliest of the Rosedale women, betook them the next spring to the Burg of the Runaways, and there built them a stead, and drew a garth about it, and dug and sowed the banks of the river, which they called Inglebourne. And as years passed, this same stead throve exceedingly, and men resorted thither both from Rosedale and Burgdale; for it was a pleasant place; and the land, when it was cured, was sweet and good, and the wood thereabout was full of deer of all kinds. So their stead was called Inglebourne after the stream; and in latter days it became a very goodly habitation of men.

===Chinese===
于是堡谷之人中有五十名留驻玫瑰谷，无一超过二十五冬之龄。其中确有数人在玫瑰谷安家立业，此后除作客外再未回归堡谷。因岁末之前，便有十人在玫瑰谷成婚；又有七人，亦娶了玫瑰谷女子中最姣好者为妻，于次年春前往逃奴堡，在那里筑起一处宅院，围以院墙，并于河两岸掘土播种——他们称此河为炉火溪（Inglebourne）。随着岁月流逝，这处宅院日渐繁盛，玫瑰谷与堡谷之人皆来聚居；因此地宜人；其土一经整治，便肥美丰饶，周围林中百兽群游。故其宅院因溪而得名，亦唤作炉火溪；日后成为一处极其美好的人居之所。

===Original===
Moreover, some of the once-enthralled folk of Rosedale, when they knew that men of their kindred from Silverdale were going home with the men of Burgdale to dwell in the Dale, prayed hard to go along with them; for they looked on the Burgdalers as if they were new Gods of the Earth. The Burgdale chiefs would not gainsay these men either, but took with them three score and ten from Rosedale, men and women, and promised them dwelling and livelihood in Burgdale.

===Chinese===
此外，玫瑰谷昔日为奴之人中，有知其在银谷的同族之人将随堡谷之人归去、居于谷中者，苦苦恳求同往；因他们视堡谷之人如同新生的地神一般。堡谷诸首领也不忍回绝这些人，便从玫瑰谷带走七十人，男女皆有，许他们在堡谷安身立命。

===Original===
So now with good hearts the Host of Burgdale turned their faces toward their well-beloved Dale; and they made good diligence, so that in three days’ time they were come anigh the edge of the woodland wilderness. Thither in the eventide, as they were making ready for their last supper and bed in the wood, came three men and two women of their folk, who had been abiding their coming ever since they had had the tidings of Silverdale and the battles from Dallach. Great was the joy of these messengers as they went from company to company of the warriors, and saw the familiar faces of their friends, and heard their wonted voices telling all the story of battle and slaughter. And for their part the men of the Host feasted these stay-at-homes, and made much of them. But one of them, a man of the House of the Face, left the Host a little after nightfall, and bore back to Burgstead at once the tidings of the coming home of the Host. Albeit since Dallach’s tidings of victory had come to the Dale, the dwellers in the steads of the countryside had left Burgstead and gone home to their own houses; so that there was no great multitude abiding in the Thorp.

===Chinese===
于是堡谷大军满怀欣悦，转身奔赴他们挚爱的山谷；他们赶路甚勤，三日之后便已抵近林莽荒野之边缘。彼处暮色之中，他们正备办林中最后一顿晚餐、安最后一次床铺之际，来了他们族中三男二女——这些人自打从达拉赫处闻知银谷及诸战消息之后，便一直在此守候大军驾临。这些信使穿行于一队一队武士之间，看见故友熟识的面容，听见他们熟悉的声音讲述着厮杀战伐的全本故事，心中之喜无以言表。而大军这边也将这些居家之人奉为上宾，殷勤款待。其中一人，是面神之宅的人，入夜不久便离了大军，连夜将大军归来的消息送回堡镇。然自达拉赫的捷报传至谷中之后，乡间农舍之人已纷纷离开堡镇、各回自家；故屯中所留之人已无多。

===Original===
So early on the morrow was the Host astir; but ere they came to Wildlake’s Way, the Shepherd-folk turned aside westward to go home, after they had bidden farewell to their friends and fellows of the Dale; for their souls longed for the sheepcotes in the winding valleys under the long grey downs; and the garths where the last year’s ricks shouldered up against the old stone gables, and where the daws were busy in the tall unfrequent ash-trees; and the green flowery meadows adown along the bright streams, where the crowfoot and the paigles were blooming now, and the harebells were in flower about the thorn-bushes at the down’s foot, whence went the savour of their blossom over sheepwalk and water-meadow.

===Chinese===
次日清晨，大军便已起身赶路；然未至野湖径，牧羊之民便辞别谷中之亲友同伴，折向西行归家去了；因他们的心魂早已向往那蜿蜒山谷中、绵长灰冈之下的羊栏；向往那去年草垛挨挤在旧石山墙边、高大疏落的白蜡树上寒鸦忙碌的院落；向往那沿着明丽溪流而下的一片片绿茵繁花之甸——此时毛茛与黄花九轮草正当盛开，冈脚荆棘丛畔蓝铃花正吐蕊，花香弥漫过牧羊之地与水边草甸。

===Original===
So these went their ways with many kind words; and two hours afterwards all the rest of the Host stood on the level ground of the Portway; but presently were the ranks of war disordered and broken up by the joy of the women and children, as they fell to drawing goodman or brother or lover out of the throng to the way that led speediest to their homesteads and halls. For the War-leader would not hold the Host together any longer, but suffered each man to go to his home, deeming that the men of Burgstead, and chiefly they of the Face and the Steer, would suffice for a company if any need were, and they would be easily gathered to meet any hap.

===Chinese===
这些人说着无数温言话语，各自上路；两个时辰之后，大军余众尽数立于港路之平地之上；然顷刻之间，战阵便被妇人孩童之喜乐冲散打乱——她们纷纷从人群里将自己的男人、或兄弟、或情郎拉出来，拽向通往自家农舍厅堂最快捷的那条路。因战首不愿再将大军拘作一处，任凭众人各自归家；他以为堡镇之人，尤其是面族与犍牛族之人，若遇何需用，足可成一队之用，且容易召集以应对任何变故。

===Original===
So now the men of the Middle and Lower Dale made for their houses by the road and the lanes and the meadows, and the men of the Upper Dale and Burgstead went their ways along the Portway toward their halls, with the throng of women and children that had come out to meet them. And now men came home when it was yet early, and the long day lay before them; and it was, as it were, made giddy and cumbered with the exceeding joy of return, and the thought of the day when the fear of death and sundering had been ever in their hearts. For these new hours were full of the kissing and embracing of lovers, and the sweetness of renewed delight in beholding the fair bodies so sorely desired, and hearkening the soft wheedling of longed-for voices. There were the cups of friends beneath the chestnut trees, and the talk of the deeds of the fighting-men, and of the heavy days of the home-abiders; many a tale told oft and o’er again. There was the singing of old songs and of new, and the beholding the well-loved nook of the pleasant places, which death might well have made nought for them; and they were sweet with the fear of that which was past, and in their pleasantness was fresh promise for the days to come.

===Chinese===
于是中谷与下谷之人沿大路、小径、草甸奔自家屋舍而去，上谷与堡镇之人则携着一群群出来迎接的妇人孩童，沿港路归向各自的厅堂。此时众人归家尚早，漫长一日仍铺展于前；这一日仿佛因归来之极度喜乐而令人目眩神摇、不胜负荷，又因忆起那段死亡离散之忧常驻心头的日子而百感交集。因这些崭新的时辰里满是情人们的亲吻与拥抱，满是重新得见那朝思暮想的美好身躯所涌起之甜蜜欢愉，满是聆听那梦萦魂牵之温柔嗓音所唤起之欣慰。栗树之下，故交举杯；谈说武士之功勋、居家者之艰难岁月；一段故事，反复传讲，不厌其烦。有旧曲新歌的吟唱，有重睹那些心爱角落、宜人之地——那些死亡本可轻易使之化为乌有之处；往昔之恐惧反使眼前愈发甘甜，而这甘美之中，又蕴着来日崭新的期许。

===Original===
So amid their joyance came evening and nightfall; and though folk were weary with the fullness of delight, yet now for many their weariness led them to the chamber of love before the rest of deep night came to them to make them strong for the happy life to be begun again on the morrow.

House by house they feasted, and few were the lovers that sat not together that even. But Face-of-god and the Sun-beam parted at the door of the House of the Face; for needs must she go with her new folk to the House of the Steer, and needs must Face-of-god be amongst his own folk in that hour of high-tide, and sit beside his father beneath the image of the God with the ray-begirt head.

===Chinese===
于是在他们的欢庆之中，暮色降临，夜幕四合；众人虽因欢愉之满溢而疲惫，然此时对许多人而言，这份疲惫反将他们引向了爱之密室——待到深夜酣眠来临，使他们养足精神，好在明日重启那幸福的生活。

他们挨家挨户地设宴欢庆，那一晚情人们鲜有不并肩而坐者。然面神与日光在面神之宅门前依依惜别；因她必须随她的新族人前往犍牛之宅，而面神也必须在这欢庆高潮之时刻与自家族人同在，坐在父亲身旁，在那尊首级环绕光芒的神像之下。
