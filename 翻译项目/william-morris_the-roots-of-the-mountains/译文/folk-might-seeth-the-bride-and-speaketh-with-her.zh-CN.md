## L Folk-Might Seeth the Bride and Speaketh with Her / 五十 民强见新娘并与之交谈

===Original===
Now tells the tale of Folk-might, that he went his ways from the Hall to the house where the Bride lay; and the swain who had brought the message went along with him, and he was proud of walking beside so mighty a warrior, and he talked to Folk-might as they went; and the sound of his voice was irksome to the chieftain, but he made as though he hearkened. Yet when they came to the door of the house, which was just out of the Place on the Southern road (for thereby had the Bride fallen to earth), he could withhold his grief no longer, but turned on the threshold and laid his head on the doorjamb, and sobbed and wept till the tears fell down like rain. And the boy stood by wondering, and wishing that Folk-might would forbear weeping, but durst not speak to him.

In a while Folk-might left weeping and went in, and found a fair hall sore befouled by the felons, and in the corner on a bed covered with furs the wounded woman; and at first sight he deemed her not so pale as he looked to see her, as she lay with her long dark-red hair strewed over the pillow, her head moving about wearily. A linen cloth was thrown over her body, but her arms lay out of it before her. Beside her sat the Alderman, his face sober enough, but not as one in heavy sorrow; and anigh him was another chair as if someone had but just got up from it. There was no one else in the hall save two women of the Woodlanders, one of whom was cooking some potion on the hearth, and another was sweeping the floor anigh of bran or some such stuff, which had been thrown down to sop up the blood.

===Chinese===
今且说民强之事：他自厅中而出，往新娘所卧之屋而去；那传信的少年与他同行，因能与如此威武之勇士并肩而行，颇以为荣，一路上向民强絮絮叨叨；其声之于这位首领，本甚聒噪，他却装作倾听之状。然待行至那屋门前——其屋恰在广场（the Place）之外、南行大路旁（盖新娘即于彼处倒地也）——他再也按捺不住心中之痛，便于门槛上回过身来，将头抵住门柱，抽噎哀泣，泪落如雨。那少年立在一旁，心中诧异，唯愿民强莫再哭泣，却不敢开口劝他。

过了一会儿，民强止泪入内，只见一座本甚华美之厅堂，被那些凶徒糟蹋得不成样子；角落里一张盖着毛皮之床上，卧着那位受伤之妇人；初看之下，她并不如他所预想的那般苍白——她卧在那里，一头深红长发披散枕上，头颅无力地来回晃动。她身上搭着一块亚麻布，双臂却伸在布外，垂于身前。长老（the Alderman）坐于她身旁，面色颇为凝重，却不像怀有深哀之人；他近旁另有一椅，似乎方才有人刚刚起身离座。厅中除两名林中汉子（the Woodlanders）部族之妇人外，再无他人——一名在炉前煎熬药汤，另一名正在扫地，扫的是麸皮之类物事，乃撒在地上以吸渍血迹。

===Original===
So Folk-might went up to the Bride, sorely dreading the image of death which she had grown to be, and sorely loving the woman she was and would be.

He knelt down by the bedside, heeding Iron-face little, though he nodded friendly to him, and he held his face close to hers; but she had her eyes shut and did not open them till he had been there a little while; and then they opened and fixed themselves on his without surprise or change. Then she lifted her right hand (for it was in her left shoulder and side that she had been hurt) and slowly laid it on his head, and drew his face to hers and kissed it fondly, as she both smiled and let the tears run over from her eyes. Then she spake in a weak voice:

===Chinese===
于是民强走向新娘，心中既深惧她如今已成的那副将死之相，又深爱她此刻所是、将来所仍是的那位女子。

他在床前跪下，对铁面（Iron-face）几乎未加理会——虽则向他友善地颔首——便将自己的脸凑近她的脸；然她双目紧闭，直至他在那里待了一阵之后方才睁开；那双眼一睁开，便直直凝注于他脸上，既无惊讶之色，亦无变化之容。于是她抬起右手（盖受伤之处乃在左肩与左胁），缓缓置于他头上，引他的脸贴向自己，深情一吻，面上既是微笑，眼中又任泪水盈盈而下。随即她以虚弱之声说道：

===Original===
"Thou seest, chieftain and dear friend, that I may not stand by thy victorious side today. And now, though I were fain if thou wouldst never leave me, yet needs must thou go about thy work, since thou art become the Alderman of the Folk of Silverdale. Yea, and even if thou wert not to go from me, yet in a manner should I go from thee. For I am grievously hurt, and I know by myself, and also the leeches have told me, that the fever is a-coming on me; so that presently I shall not know thee, but may deem thee to be a woman, or a hound, or the very Wolf that is the image of the Father of thy kindred; or even, it may be, someone else—that I have played with time agone."

===Chinese===
「首领，挚友啊，尔今可见，今日我已不能立于尔凯旋之侧了。如今，虽我甚愿尔永不离我，然尔既已成银谷（Silverdale）之民的长老，自须去料理尔之事务。便算尔不离我而去，在某种意义上，亦将是我要离尔而去。盖我伤势沉重，我自己心知，医师们也已言明，热度即将发作；故不多时我便要认不得尔了，或将尔当作一名妇人、一条猎犬，甚至当作那头狼（the Wolf）——那乃是尔族之祖（the Father of thy kindred）的形相；甚或，也许，当作别的什么人——是我昔日曾一同嬉戏过的。」

===Original===
Her voice faltered and faded out here, and she was silent a while; then she said:

"So depart, kind friend and dear love, bearing this word with thee, that should I die, I call on Iron-face my kinsman to bear witness that I bid thee carry me to bale in Silverdale, and lay mine ashes with the ashes of thy Fathers, with whom thine own shall mingle at the last, since I have been of the warriors who have helped to bring thee aback to the land of thy folk."

===Chinese===
她说至此处，声音断续渐低，终至不可闻，遂沉默了一阵；然后又道：

「故去罢，仁慈之友、亲爱之人，携此言而去：倘若我死，我请我之族人铁面作证——我嘱尔扶柩至银谷行火葬之礼，将我之骨灰与尔先祖之骨灰合葬，尔自己之骨灰最终亦必与之相融——因我亦是助尔重返尔族故土之诸战士中的一员。」

===Original===
Then she smiled and shut her eyes and said: "And if I live, as indeed I hope, and how glad and glad I shall be to live, then shalt thou bring me to thy house and thy bed, that I may not depart from thee while both our lives last."

And she opened her eyes and looked at him; and he might not speak for a while, so ravished as he was betwixt joy and sorrow. But the Alderman arose and took a gold ring from off his arm, and spake:

===Chinese===
她随即一笑，闭上了眼睛，说道：「若我活下来——我亦正如此想，活下来我当何等欢欣、何等欢欣——那时尔便须引我入尔之宅、尔之榻，使我于我二人一生之期内，再不与尔分离。」

她又睁开双眼，望着他；他半晌说不出话来，盖其心正为悲喜二者所攫。此时长老起身，自臂上褪下一枚金戒，开口说道：

===Original===
"This is the gold ring of the God of the Face, and I bear it on mine arm betwixt the Folk and the God in all man-motes, and I bore it through the battle today; and it is as holy a ring as may be; and since ye are plighting troth, and I am the witness thereof, it were good that ye held this ring together and called the God to witness, who is akin to the God of the Earth, as we all be. Take the ring, Folk-might, for I trust thee; and of all women now alive would I have this woman happy."

===Chinese===
「此乃面之神（the God of the Face）之金戒；凡遇民人大会（man-motes），我皆戴之于臂，以介于民众与神明之间；今日血战之中，我亦戴着它。此戒乃世间至圣之戒；尔等既在订盟立誓，而我又是见证之人，尔等便当同握此戒，请此神作证——此神与地神（the God of the Earth）同源一体，正如我众人皆然。民强，将戒拿去罢，我信得过尔；当今在世之诸妇人之中，我最愿此女得享安乐。」

===Original===
So Folk-might took the ring and thrust his hand through it, and took her hand, and said:

"Ye Fathers, thou God of the Face, thou Earth-god, thou Warrior, bear witness that my life and my body are plighted to this woman, the Bride of the House of the Steer!"

===Chinese===
于是民强接过那戒，将手穿过其中，又握住她的手，说道：

「列位先祖！面之神！地神！武士（the Warrior）！请共鉴之：我之性命、我之身躯，已许此女——犍牛之宅（the House of the Steer）之新娘！」

===Original===
His face was flushed and bright as he spoke, but as his words ceased he noted how feebly her hand lay in his, and his face fell, and he gazed on her timidly. But she lay quiet, and said softly and slowly:

"O Fathers of my kindred! O Warrior and God of the Earth! bear witness that I plight my troth to this man, to lie in his grave if I die, and in his bed if I live."

===Chinese===
说话之时，他面色潮红、神采焕发；然言语既毕，他便察觉她的手何等无力地躺在自己掌中，脸色随之一黯，怯怯地望着她。她却静静地卧着，轻而缓地说道：

「噫，我族之列位先祖！武士与地神啊！请共鉴之：我以此身许此人——死则同其坟茔，生则同其卧榻。」

===Original===
And she smiled on him again, and then closed her eyes; but opened them presently once more, and said:

"Dear friend, how fared it with Gold-mane today?"

===Chinese===
她又对他一笑，随又合上双目；然不多时复又睁开，说道：

「挚友，金鬃（Gold-mane）今日战况如何？」

===Original===
Said Folk-might: "So well he did, that none might have done better. He fared in the fight as if he had been our Father the Warrior: he is a great chieftain."

She said: "Wilt thou give him this message from me, that in no wise he forget the oath which he swore upon the finger-ring as it lay on the sundial of the Garden of the Face? And say, moreover, that I am sorry that we shall part, and have between us such breadth of wildwood and mountain-neck."

===Chinese===
民强答道：「他战得极好，无人能出其右。他在阵中厮杀，宛如我等之父、那位武士亲临：他诚然是位大首领。」

她道：「尔可愿替我传一句话给他——千万莫要忘了他在面神之园（the Garden of the Face）日晷之上、对着那枚指环所立之誓？再告诉他，我甚憾我们将要分离，你我之间隔着如此辽阔之林莽山颈。」

===Original===
"Yea, surely will I give thy message," said Folk-might; and in his heart he rejoiced, because he heard her speak as if she were sure of life. Then she said faintly:

"It is now thy work to depart from me, and to do as it behoveth a chieftain of the people and the Alderman of Silverdale. Depart, lest the leeches chide me: farewell, my dear!"

===Chinese===
「是，我必替尔传话，」民强道；他心中暗喜，盖听她说话之态，似已确信自己能活。她遂又虚弱地说道：

「尔今当去作尔之事，行一民之首领、银谷之长所当为之事。去罢，免得医师们怪罪于我：再会了，我亲爱的！」

===Original===
So he laid his face to hers and kissed her, and rose up and embraced Iron-face, and went his ways without looking back.

But just over the threshold he met old Hall-ward of the House of the Steer, who was at point to enter, and he greeted him kindly. The old man looked on him steadily, and said: "Tomorrow or the day after I will utter a word to thee, O Chief of the Wolf."

===Chinese===
他便把脸贴上她的脸，吻了她，起身与铁面相拥，径自去了，不再回顾。

然方出门槛，便撞见犍牛之宅的老厅卫（Hall-ward）正欲入门，民强殷勤向他致意。老人定定地端详着他，说道：「明日或后日，我有一言要告尔，狼之首领（O Chief of the Wolf）。」

===Original===
"In a good hour," said Folk-might, "for all thy words are true." Therewith he gat him away from the house, and came to Face-of-god, where he sat before the altar of the Crooked Sword; and now were the chiefs come back from their meat, and were sitting with him; there also were Wood-father and Wood-wont; but Bow-may was with the Sun-beam, who was resting softly in the fair meadow after all the turmoil.

So men made place for Folk-might beside the War-leader, who looked upon his face, and saw that it was sober and unsmiling, but not heavy or moody with grief. So he deemed that all was as well as it might be with the Bride, and with a good heart fell to taking counsel with the others; and kindly and friendly were the redes which they held there, with no gainsaying of man by man, for the whole folk was glad at heart.

===Chinese===
「正逢吉时，」民强道，「盖尔所言皆是真话。」说罢他便离了此屋，来到面神（Face-of-god）跟前——面神正坐于曲剑祭坛（the altar of the Crooked Sword）之前；此时众首领已用罢饭食回来，与他同坐；在座者尚有林父（Wood-father）与林习（Wood-wont）；然弓姝（Bow-may）却在日光（Sun-beam）之处——日光历经那一番惊扰之后，正于那片芳草地之上恬然歇息。

众人为民强在战首（the War-leader）身侧让出一席；战首观其面色，见其凝重不笑，却并未为悲愁所压、郁郁不乐。他遂以为新娘之事已是不幸中之大幸，便欣然与众计议；他们在那里所议之事，皆甚和气友善，无人彼此驳难，盖全族上下心怀欢欣。

===Original===
So there they ordered all matters duly for that present time, and by then they had made an end, it was past sunset, and men were lodged in the chief houses about the Marketstead.

Albeit, though they ate their meat with all joy of heart, and were merry in converse one with the other, the men of the Wolf would by no means feast in their Hall again till it had been cleansed and hallowed anew.

===Chinese===
他们便在那里将当下诸事一一安排妥帖；待到议毕，日已西沉，众人皆被安顿歇宿于市坪（the Marketstead）四周那些主要屋宇之中。

然则，他们虽满怀欢欣地用饭，彼此交谈甚乐，狼族（the Wolf）之人却无论如何不肯再于他们那厅中设宴，须得等那厅重新洒扫洁净、重新祝圣之后方可。
