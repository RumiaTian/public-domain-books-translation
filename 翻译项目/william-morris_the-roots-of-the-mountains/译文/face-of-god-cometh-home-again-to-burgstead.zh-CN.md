## VIII Face-of-God Cometh Home Again to Burgstead / 八 面神重归堡镇

===Original===
Face-of-god went back through the wood by the way he had come, paying little heed to the things about him. For whatever he thought of strayed not one whit from the image of the Fair Woman of the Mountainside.

He went through the wood swiftlier than yesterday, and made no stay for noon or aught else, nor did he linger on the road when he was come into the Dale, either to speak to any or to note what they did. So he came to the House of the Face about dusk, and found no man within the hall either carle or queen. So he cried out on the folk, and there came in a damsel of the house, whom he greeted kindly and she him again. He bade her bring the washing-water, and she did so and washed his feet and his hands. She was a fair maid enough, as were most in the Dale, but he heeded her little; and when she was done he kissed not her cheek for her pains, as his wont was, but let her go her ways unthanked. But he went to his shut-bed and opened his chest, and drew fair raiment from it, and did off his wood-gear, and did on him a goodly scarlet kirtle fairly broidered, and a collar with gems of price therein, and other braveries. And when he was so attired he came out into the hall, and there was old Stone-face standing by the hearth, which was blazing brightly with fresh brands, so that things were clear to see.

===Chinese===
面神循来路穿过林莽归去，沿途事物全不留心。盖因他心之所思，丝毫不能偏离那山颜美妇人（the Fair Woman of the Mountainside）的影像。

这一日穿林较昨日更为迅疾，正午亦不停歇，余事更不必论；及至入了堡谷，路上亦不逗留，既不与任何人搭话，亦不察看他人所务。约近黄昏时分，他回到面神之宅，厅中空无一人，无论男女。他便高声呼唤仆从，有一侍女入内，他和颜以待，侍女亦还礼。他命她取盥洗之水来，侍女依言，为他洗足、洗手。此女容貌颇为姣好——堡谷中女子多半如此——他却几乎未曾留意；侍女侍奉既毕，他竟不如往常那般吻她脸颊以酬其劳，连谢也不谢一声，便任她自去。他自己却走到封闭木榻卧间，打开箱箧，自内取出华美衣裳，脱下林中行装，换上一件绣工精美之大红长袍，又戴上一条嵌有珍贵宝石之项圈，并其他诸般华饰。装束既毕，他步出厅堂，只见老石面正立于炉畔，炉中新柴烈焰熊熊，照得满室通明。

===Original===
Stone-face noted Gold-mane's gay raiment, for he was not wont to wear such attire, save on the feasts and high days when he behoved to. So the old man smiled and said:

"Welcome back from the Wood! But what is it? Hast thou been wedded there, or who hath made thee Earl and King?"

Said Face-of-god: "Foster-father, sooth it is that I have been to the wood, but there have I seen nought of manfolk worse than myself. Now as to my raiment, needs must I keep it from the moth. And I am weary withal, and this kirtle is light and easy to me. Moreover, I look to see the Bride here again, and I would pleasure her with the sight of gay raiment upon me."

"Nay," said Stone-face, "hast thou not seen some woman in the wood arrayed like the image of a God? and hath she not bidden thee thus to worship her tonight? For I know that such wights be in the wood, and that such is their wont."

Said Gold-mane: "I worship nought save the Gods and the Fathers. Nor saw I in the wood any such as thou sayest."

===Chinese===
石面留心到金鬃这一身华服——他平日并不穿这等衣裳，唯有节庆大日理当穿着之时方才一著。于是老人含笑说道：

"自林中归来，欢迎欢迎！可这是怎么一回事？你在那边成了亲么，还是有谁封你作了伯爵与国王？"

面神道："义父，我确是去了林中，但在那边并未遇见什么比我更险恶之人。至于这身衣裳嘛，总须拿出来晾一晾，免为蠹虫所蛀。况且我亦疲倦，这件长袍于我轻便舒适。再者，我盼着新娘今夜再来此间，愿以一身华服博她一欢。"

"不然，"石面道，"你莫非在林中遇见了什么女子，衣饰如同神像一般？她岂不是命你今夜如此供奉她么？盖因我深知林中确有这等精怪，而此乃其惯技。"

金鬃道："我所崇奉者，唯诸神与列祖而已。林中亦未见你所言那等人物。"

===Original===
Therewith Stone-face shook his head; but after a while he said:

"Art thou for the wood tomorrow?"

"Nay," said Gold-mane angrily, knitting his brows.

"The morrow of tomorrow," said Stone-face, "is the day when we look to see the Westland merchants: after all, wilt thou not go hence with them when they wend their ways back before the first snows fall?"

"Nay," said he, "I have no mind to it, fosterer; cease egging me on hereto."

Then Stone-face shook his head again, and looked on him long, and muttered: "To the wood wilt thou go tomorrow or next day; or some day when doomed is thine undoing."

===Chinese===
说罢石面摇了摇头；过一会儿又道：

"你明日还要去林中么？"

"不去，"金鬃怒道，双眉紧皱。

"明日之后那一日，"石面道，"便是我们盼望西土商人（Westland merchants）到来之日：说到底，待他们初雪降前启程归去之时，你难道不与他们一同离此而去么？"

"不，"他道，"我无意于此，义父；休要再怂恿我。"

于是石面又摇了摇头，久久凝视着他，喃喃道："明日也好，后日也好，你总要再去林中；要不然，便是你劫数已到的那一日。"

===Original===
Therewith entered the service and torches, and presently after came the Alderman with Hall-face; and Iron-face greeted his son and said to him: "Thou hast not hit the time to do on thy gay raiment, for the Bride will not be here tonight; she bideth still at the Feast at the Apple-tree House: or wilt thou be there, son?"

"Nay," said Face-of-god, "I am overweary. And as for my raiment, it is well; it is for thine honour and the honour of the name."

So to table they went, and Iron-face asked his son of his ways again, and whether he was quite fixed in his mind not to go down to the Plain and the Cities: "For," said he, "the morrow of tomorrow shall the merchants be here, and this were great news for them if the son of the Alderman should be their faring-fellow back."

But Face-of-god answered without any haste or heat: "Nay, father, it may not be: fear not, thou shalt see that I have a good will to work and live in the Dale."

And in good sooth, though he was a young man and loved mirth and the ways of his own will, he was a stalwarth workman, and few could mow a match with him in the hay-month and win it; or fell trees as certainly and swiftly, or drive as straight and clean a furrow through the stiff land of the lower Dale; and in other matters also was he deft and sturdy.

===Chinese===
正说间，仆役执火炬鱼贯而入，俄顷长老偕厅面亦至；铁面问候其子，对他说道："你这身华服穿得不是时候，新娘今夜不来此间；她尚在苹果树之宅（the Apple-tree House）的宴席上未散：儿啊，你莫非也要去那边么？"

"不去，"面神道，"我太过疲倦。至于我这身衣裳，倒也无妨；这是为你的体面，也是为家族名声的体面。"

于是众人入席。铁面又问其子近来行止，问他是否当真下定决心不下平原、不入诸城："盖因，"他说，"明日之后那一日商人便到此地，若长老之子肯做他们归程的同路人，于他们实乃天大的喜讯。"

面神却不慌不忙、不愠不怒地答道："父亲，不可如此：你放心，你将看到我有心于堡谷之中劳作、安居。"

且说句实在话，他虽年少之人、性好游乐任性，却是一把干活的好手：割草之月能与他比试镰刀而取胜者寥寥无几；伐木亦少有人能如他那般稳当利落；在堡谷下端那片硬土上犁田，也少有人能如他那般犁出又直又净的沟垄；至于旁的活计，他亦无一不巧、无一不强健。
