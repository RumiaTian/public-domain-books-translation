## XLVII The Kindreds Win the Mote-House / 四十七 诸宗族攻克议事堂

===Original===
The din and tumult still came from the north side of the Marketplace, so that all the air was full of noise; and Face-of-god deemed that the thralls had gotten weapons into their hands and were slaying their masters.

===Chinese===
集市坪北面仍旧传来喧闹鼎沸之声，满空为之震动；面神心想必是那些奴隶已夺得兵刃在手，正在诛杀其主。

===Original===
Now he lifted up his face, and put his hand on Folk-might’s shoulder, and said in a loud voice:

“Kinsmen, it were well if our brother were to bid the banners into the Mote-house of the Wolf, and let all the Host set itself in array before the said house, and abide till the chasers of the foe come to us thither; for I perceive that they are now become many, and are more than those of our kindred.”

Then Folk-might looked at him with kind eyes, and said:

“Thou sayest well, brother; even so let it be!”

And he lifted up his sword, and Face-of-god cried out in a loud voice: “Forward, banners! blow up horns! fare we forth with victory!”

===Chinese===
他于是扬起脸来，一手按在民强肩上，朗声说道：

"诸位宗亲，若由我们这位兄弟传令诸旗进狼之议事堂，令全军于该堂前列阵待命，等候追击来敌之我众到此会合，便甚妥当；因我见那些追兵如今已成大群，反比我等此处宗族之人更多。"

民强遂以亲切之目视之，说道：

"兄弟说得是；便如此办吧！"

他举起剑来，面神朗声高呼："旗帜前行！号角齐鸣！我军奏凯而进！"

===Original===
So the Host drew its ranks together in good order, and they all set forward, and old Stone-face took the Sun-beam by the hand and led on behind Folk-might and the War-leader. But when they came to the Hall, then saw they how the steps that led up to the door were high and double, going up from each side without any railing or fool-guard; and crowding the stairs and the platform thereof was a band of the Dusky Men, as many as could stand thereon, who shot arrows at the host of the kindreds, howling like dogs, and chattering like apes; and arrows and spears came from the windows of the Hall; yea, and on the very roof a score of these felons were riding the ridge and mocking like the trolls of old days.

===Chinese===
于是大军整队成列，井然向前开拔；老石面携日光之手，随在民强与战首之后同行。既至厅前，只见通向大门之台阶高峻而分作两股，自两侧各自拾级而上，并无栏干或防跌之护；阶梯与其上平台尽被一伙黝暗人挤满，能立几人便立几人，向宗族大军放箭，嗥如犬吠、噪如猿啼；箭与矛自厅堂窗中飞出；乃至屋脊之上，亦有二十名恶徒跨坐其上，如古时所传山魈恶鬼一般戏谑嘲笑。

===Original===
Now when they saw this they stayed a while, and men shielded them against the shafts; but the leaders drew together in front of the Host, and Folk-might fell to speech; and his face was very pale and stern; for now he had had time to think of the case of the Bride, and fierce wrath, and grief unholpen filled his soul. So he said:

“Brothers, this is my business to deal with; for I see before me the stair that leadeth to the Mote-house of my people, and now would I sit there whereas my fathers sat, when peace was on the Dale, as once more it shall be tomorrow. Therefore up this stair will I go, and none shall hinder me; and let no man of the host follow me till I have entered into the Hall, unless perchance I fall dead by the way; but stand ye still and look on.”

===Chinese===
众既见此，停步不前，举起盾牌遮挡箭矢；众首领聚于大军之前，民强开言；其面极为苍白严厉；盖此时他已得暇思及新娘之遭际，一腔怒火与无助之悲充满其心。遂说道：

"兄弟们，此事当由我来料理；我眼前所见，正是通向我族议事堂之阶，而今我欲坐于我父祖所坐之处——彼时谷中尚是太平，一如明日必将再归太平。故此我必登此阶，无人可挡；在我未入厅堂之前，军中任何人不得随我同上，除非我中途倒毙；尔等且伫立旁观。"

===Original===
“Nay,” said Face-of-god, “this is partly the business of the War-leader. There are two stairs. Be content to take the southern one, and I will take the northern. We shall meet on the plain stone at the top.”

But Hall-face said: “War-leader, may I speak?”

“Speak, brother,” said Face-of-god.

Said Hall-face: “I have done but little today, War-leader. I would stand by thee on the northern stair; so shall Folk-might be content, if he doeth two men’s work who are not little-hearted.”

Said Face-of-god: “The doom of the War-leader is that Folk-might shall fall on by the southern stair to slake his grief and increase his glory, and Face-of-god and Hall-face by the northern. Haste to the work, O brothers!”

===Chinese===
"不然，"面神道，"此亦是战首之分内事。台阶有两道。你且取南面一道，我取北面一道。我们当在阶顶那方平石之上相会。"

厅面却说："战首，我可否进一言？"

面神道："兄弟，但说无妨。"

厅面道："战首，我今日所建功业甚少。我愿随你立于北阶之上；如此则民强亦可遂其愿——他一人当两员不怯之将的活计。"

面神道："战首裁定如下：民强自南阶冲杀，以舒其悲而增其荣；面神与厅面自北阶冲杀。兄弟们，速速动手！"

===Original===
And he and Hall-face went to their places, while all looked on. But the Sun-beam, with her hand still in Stone-face’s, she turned white to the lips, and stared with wild eyes before her, not knowing where she was; for she had deemed that the battle was over, and Face-of-god saved from it.

===Chinese===
他与厅面遂各归其位，众人尽皆注视。而日光——一手仍握在石面手中——嘴唇都白了，双目发直、茫然前视，不知身在何处；盖她原以为战事已了、面神已安然而归。

===Original===
But Folk-might tossed up his head and laughed, and cried out, “At last, at last!” And his sword was in his hand, the Sleep-thorn to wit, a blade of ancient fame; so now he let it fall and hang to his wrist by the leash, while he clapped his hands together and uttered the Wolf-whoop mightily, and all the men of the Wolf that were in the host, and the Woodlanders withal, uttered it with him. Then he put his shield over his head and stood before the first of the steps, and the Dusky Men laughed to see one man come against them, though there was death in their hearts. But he laughed back at them in triumph, and set his foot on the step, and let Sleep-thorn’s point go into the throat of a Dusky lord, and thrust amongst them, hewing right and left, and tumbling men over the edge of the stair, which was to them as the narrow path along the cliff-side that hangeth over the unfathomed sea. They hewed and thrust at him in turn; but so close were they packed that their weapons crossed about him, and one shielded him from the other, and they swayed staggering on that fearful verge, while the Sleep-thorn crept here and there amongst them, lulling their hot fury. For, as desperate as they were, and fighting for death and not for life, they had a horror of him and of the sea of hatred below them, and feared where to set their feet, and he feared nought at all, but from feet to sword-point was but an engine of slaughter, while the heart within him throbbed with fury long held back as he thought upon the Bride and her wounding, and all the wrongs of his people since their Great Undoing.

===Chinese===
民强却仰头大笑，高呼道："终有此时，终有此时！"他手中之剑——即睡刺（Sleep-thorn），一柄古来驰名之刃——此刻被他一松，凭系于腕间的剑绳垂下；他一面鼓掌，一面厉声发出狼吼（the Wolf-whoop），军中一切狼族之人、并林中之民，皆随之同吼。继而他将盾顶在头上，立于第一级阶前，众黝暗人见一人独来攻，皆大笑，虽然其心已存必死之志。他却以凯旋之笑回敬，一脚踏上石阶，让睡刺之尖刺入一名黝暗贵人之咽喉，闯入人群，左右劈砍，将人纷纷自阶沿翻落下去——那阶沿于他们，不啻悬于无底海上的崖畔狭径。他们回身劈砍刺击；但他们挤得太密，兵刃在他四周相互交错，一人反替他挡了另一人之击，在那可怖的阶沿上摇晃踉跄；而睡刺则在人群中四处游走，将其狂暴一一催入长眠。盖他们虽已绝望、虽为求死而战非为求生而战，却对这个人、对脚下那片仇恨之海心存恐惧，不知何处下脚；而他毫无所惧，自足至剑尖无非一具杀戮之器，其心搏动不已，满腔积压已久之怒火——一念及新娘与其所受之伤、一念及自大倾覆以来其族所受之种种冤屈。

===Original===
So he smote and thrust, till him-seemed the throng of foes thinned before him: with his sword-pommel he smote a lord of the Dusky Ones in the face, so that he fell over the edge amongst the spears of the kindred; then he thrust the point of Sleep-thorn towards the Hall-door through the breast of another, and then it seemed to him that he had but one before him; so he hove up the edges to cleave him down, but ere the stroke fell, close to his ears exceeding loud rang out the cry, “For the Burg and the Face! for the Face, for the Face!” and he drew aback a little, and his eyes cleared, and lo! it was Hall-face the tall, his long sword all reddened with battle; and beside him stood Face-of-god, silent and panting, his face pale with the fierce anger of the fight, and the weariness which was now at last gaining upon him. There stood those three with no other living man upon the plain of the stairs.

===Chinese===
他一路劈砍刺杀，直杀到眼前敌锋渐稀：以剑首击中一名黝暗贵人之面，那人翻落阶沿，坠入宗族众矛之中；继而又将睡刺之尖透过另一人胸膛，直指向厅门；此时他只觉眼前仅剩一人；遂高举双刃欲将其劈倒，然那一击尚未落下，耳畔忽闻大声疾呼："为堡与面！为面、为面！"他略退半步，双目一清，看哪！却是高大的厅面，长剑之上尽染战血；其旁立着面神，默然喘息，面色苍白——一则因战斗之烈怒，二则因终于袭来之疲倦。三人立于那片阶顶平地之上，再无第四个活人。

===Original===
Then Face-of-god turned shouting to the Folk, and cried:

“Forth now with the banners! For now is the Wolf come home. On into the Hall, O Kindred of the Gods!”

Then poured the Folk up over the stairs and into the Hall of the Wolf, the banners flapping over their heads; and first went the War-leader and Folk-might and Hall-face, and then the three delivered thralls, Wolf-stone, God-swain, and Spear-fist, and Dallach with them, though both he and Wolf-stone had been hurt in the battle; and then came blended together the Men of the Face along with them of the Wolf who had entered the Marketstead with them, and with these were Stone-face and Wood-wont and Bow-may, leading the Sun-beam betwixt them; and now was she come to herself again, though her face was yet pale, and her eyes gleamed as she stepped across the threshold of the Hall.

===Chinese===
面神遂转向众人高呼，喊道：

"旗帜前行！狼已归家。诸神之宗族啊，杀入厅堂去！"

众人遂如潮水般涌上台阶、涌入狼之厅堂，旗帜在众人头顶猎猎飘扬；当先者是战首与民强、厅面；其次是三名得释之奴——狼石（Wolf-stone）、神仆（God-swain）、矛拳（Spear-fist）——达拉赫也与之同在，虽他与狼石俱已在战中受伤；继之是面族之人与初入集市坪时同来的狼族人混作一处，其中尚有石面、林习、弓娥，三人搀扶着日光走在中间；此时她已神魂初定，面色犹白，双目却在迈过厅堂门槛之时闪闪生光。

===Original===
But when a many were gotten in, and the first-comers had had time to handle their weapons and look about them, a cry of the utmost wrath broke from Folk-might and those others who remembered the Hall from of old. For wretched and befouled was that well-builded house: the hangings rent away; the goodly painted walls daubed and smeared with wicked tokens of the Alien murderers: the floor, once bright with polished stones of the mountain, and strewn with sweet-smelling flowers, was now as foul as the den of the man-devouring troll of the heaths. From the fair-carven roof of oak and chestnut-beams hung ugly knots of rags and shapeless images of the sorcery of the Dusky Men. And furthermore, and above all, from the last tie-beam of the roof over the dais dangled four shapes of men-at-arms, whom the older men of the Wolf knew at once for the embalmed bodies of their four great chieftains, who had been slain on the day of the Great Undoing; and they cried out with horror and rage as they saw them hanging there in their weapons as they had lived.

===Chinese===
及至大批人众涌入，先入者得暇整理兵刃、环顾四周，民强与那些还记得此厅旧貌之人，尽皆发出一腔震天之怒。盖那座营建精良之屋，已被糟蹋得不堪入目：帷幕尽被撕裂；彩绘之美墙为外族凶手种种邪恶魔记涂抹污损：其地本以磨光山石铺就、香花满布，如今却秽如荒原食人山魈之巢。橡与栗木所雕之美栋之下，悬着一团团丑陋之破布结、一尊尊黝暗人巫术所制之无形怪像。更有甚者、最为可怖者——高台之上屋顶最后一道横梁之上，悬着四具甲士之躯；狼族老者一眼便认出，那是他们四大首领经防腐处理的尸身——皆是当年大倾覆之日被杀之人；众人见他们身着生前甲胄兵刃、如生时一般悬在那里，皆以惊骇与愤怒相呼。

===Original===
There was the Hostage of the Earth, his shield painted with the green world circled with the worm of the sea. There was the older Folk-might, the uncle of the living man, bearing a shield with an oak and a lion done thereon. There was Wealth-eker, on whose shield was done a golden sheaf of wheat. There was he who bore a name great from of old, Folk-wolf to wit, bearing on his shield the axe of the hewer. There they hung, dusty, befouled, with sightless eyes and grinning mouths, in the dimmed sunlight of the Hall, before the eyes of that victorious Host, stricken silent at the sight of them.

===Chinese===
其一为地之质（the Hostage of the Earth），其盾上绘着绿色的大地，外绕以海中之巨蟒。其二为老民强，即今在世民强之叔，盾上绘着一株橡树与一头雄狮。其三为富积（Wealth-eker），其盾上绘着一束金色的麦穗。其四乃自古声名赫赫之人，名唤族狼（Folk-wolf），盾上绘着樵夫之斧。四人悬于该处，尘封垢污，目盲口龇，在厅堂昏暗的日光里，悬于那支见之默然无声的得胜大军眼前。

===Original===
Underneath them on the dais stood the last remnant of the battle of the Dusky Men; and they, as men mad with coming death, shook their weapons, and with shrieking laughter mocked at the overcomers, and pointed to the long-dead chiefs, and called on them in the tongue of the kindreds to come down and lead their dear kinsmen to the high-seat; and then they cried out to the living warriors of the Wolf, and bade them better their deed of slaying, and set to work to make alive again, and cause their kinsmen to live merry on the earth.

With that last mock they handled their weapons and rushed howling on the warriors to meet their death; nor was it long denied them; for the sword of the Wolf, the axe of the Woodland, and the spear of the Dale soon made an end of the dreadful lives of these destroyers of the Folks.

===Chinese===
其下高台之上，立着黝暗人战阵中所余的最后一批；他们如将死而发狂之人，挥舞兵刃，以尖笑嘲弄战胜之人，指着那些死去已久的首领，用宗族之语唤他们下来、引导亲爱的族亲登上高座；继而又向活着的狼族武士大喊，叫他们胜过杀人之举，动手使死人复生，让其族亲在世间重新欢乐度日。

说完这最后一番嘲讽，他们握紧兵刃，嗥叫着扑向众武士，前去受死；其死也不久即如愿以偿：盖狼之剑、林之斧、谷之矛，顷刻之间便了结了这些屠族者可怖的一生。
