## Endnotes / 尾注
===Original===

1. It has been told me for a fact that when the exiled Khedive Ismaìl Pasha (known to London street-boys of the period as old Ishmel Parker) was at Naples, one of the officers in attendance on him challenged an Italian in a café for having dared to insult a Prophet of his (the Egyptian’s) religion. The man had been blaspheming, it appeared, as only a Neapolitan or a Tuscan knows how to blaspheme, heaping foul epithets on the name of his Saviour and the Blessed Virgin. A duel, my informant assures me, actually took place on these grounds. ↩︎
2. All the territory successively annexed to the rising of the Ottoman Empire was classed either as forming part of the *dar ul Islâm*, the house of Islâm, or as belonging to the *dar ul harb*, or the house of war, according as it was inhabited by Mohammedans or by Christians. In the latter case the new subjects of the Sultàn were called *rayahs*, and they were personally assessed to ransom their lives, which were forfeited by defeat, and as an equivalent for military service from which they were exempted, or rather, which they did not enjoy the privilege of rendering. This capitation-tax received the name of *haratsh*, and its payment entitled each Christian to keep his head on his shoulders for the space of one year. (Skene: *Anadol, or the Last Home of the Faithful*.) ↩︎
3. It was predicted in the beginning of the present century by a much-revered sheikh that when the first of the sevens falls the ruin of Islâm will commence, and when the second falls it will have been completed. We are now in the year of the Hegira 1277; the year about to open will invert the first of the two Arabic sevens read from right to left﻿—V becoming Ʌ; that is, 7 becoming 8, and in the year 1280 of the Hegira the second 7 will also be inverted. This prophecy, supported as it is by the reality of the troubles now arising in various quarters, has naturally exercised a great influence on the fatalist tendencies of the Mussulmans and increased their ill-will towards other sects. (Skene: *Rambles in Syrian Deserts*.) ↩︎
4. Ahmed Pasha sent some troops under the command of two colonels into the streets. They soon applied to him for instructions, under the impossibility of keeping the peace without resorting to violence. He ordered them in writing to fire upon the people.

1. 有人确切地告诉过我：当年被放逐的赫迪夫伊斯梅尔·帕夏（Khedive Ismaìl Pasha，那时伦敦街头的顽童们管他叫「老伊什梅尔·帕克」〔old Ishmel Parker〕）侨居那不勒斯时，他随行的一名军官曾在一家咖啡馆里向一个意大利人提出决斗，因为那人竟敢侮辱他（这位埃及人）所奉宗教的一位先知。据说那人当时正以一种唯有那不勒斯人或托斯卡纳人才精通的方式亵渎神明，将种种污言秽语加在他救主和圣母的名号上。我的消息提供者向我保证，一场决斗确曾因此事而发生。 ↩︎
2. 奥斯曼帝国崛起过程中陆续兼并的一切疆土，或被划入 *dar ul Islâm*（伊斯兰之家），或被归为 *dar ul harb*（战争之家），视其居民为穆斯林或基督徒而定。在后一种情形下，苏丹（Sultàn）的新臣民被称为 *rayahs*（赖亚，非穆斯林顺民），他们须以人头纳税来赎回自己的性命——这些性命本已因战败而被剥夺——并以此代替兵役；更确切地说，代替他们无福享受的那份服役「特权」。这种人头税名为 *haratsh*（哈拉吉），完税之后，每一名基督徒便有权让脑袋在自己的肩膀上再多留一年。（斯基恩：《阿纳多尔，或信士最后的家园》〔Anadol, or the Last Home of the Faithful〕。） ↩︎
3. 本世纪初，一位备受尊崇的谢赫曾预言：当第一个七倾倒时，伊斯兰的毁灭将随之开始；当第二个七倾倒时，毁灭便已完结。我们如今正处在希吉来历 1277 年；即将开启的一年，将把两个阿拉伯数字的七当中从右往左数的第一个倒转过来——V 变成 Ʌ，亦即 7 变成 8；到希吉来历 1280 年，第二个 7 也将随之倒转。这一预言，又有当今各地乱象的现实为佐证，自然对穆斯林的宿命论倾向产生了巨大影响，并加深了他们对其他教派的恶感。（斯基恩：《叙利亚沙漠漫游记》〔Rambles in Syrian Deserts〕。） ↩︎
4. 艾哈迈德·帕夏（Ahmed Pasha）派两名上校率部上街。由于不动用武力便无法维持治安，他们很快向他请示机宜。他以书面命令他们向民众开枪。

One of the colonels in command of the *regulars* obeyed his order and dispersed the mob, proving thus that the evil might have been checked. The other colonel, who had charge of the *irregulars*, was won over by a Mussulman sheikh, who adjured him in the name of the Prophet and their common religion to join them and clear the holy city of Damascus of infidels. He went over to the insurgents with his troops. (Skene, as above.) For further particulars of the massacre, see Skene, already quoted, Churchill: *Druzes and Maronites*, and *Ten Years in Mount Lebanon*, and the newspapers of the latter half of 1860. ↩︎
