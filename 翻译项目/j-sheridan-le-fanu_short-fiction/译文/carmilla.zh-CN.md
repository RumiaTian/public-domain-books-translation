### Prologue / 编者前言

===Original===
Upon a paper attached to the narrative which follows, Doctor Hesselius has written a rather elaborate note, which he accompanies with a reference to his essay on the strange subject which the MS. illuminates.

===Chinese===
在附于下文这份记述之上的一纸笺记里，赫塞利乌斯医生（Doctor Hesselius）写下了一段相当详备的按语，并援引了他本人就这一手稿所阐明的离奇主题所撰的一篇论文。

===Original===
This mysterious subject, he treats, in that essay, with his usual learning and acumen, and with remarkable directness and condensation. It will form but one volume of the series of that extraordinary man's collected papers.

===Chinese===
这一神秘的主题，他在那篇论文中以他惯有的学识与敏锐加以探讨，行文之直捷凝练，颇堪瞩目。该文将来只不过是这位非凡人物全集系列中的一卷而已。

===Original===
As I publish the case, in these volumes, simply to interest the laity, I shall forestall the intelligent lady, who relates it, in nothing; and, after due consideration, I have determined, therefore, to abstain from presenting any précis of the learned Doctor's reasoning, or extract from his statement on a subject which he describes as "involving, not improbably, some of the profoundest arcana of our dual existence, and its intermediates."

===Chinese===
我在本丛书中公布这桩医案，无非是为了引起一般读者的兴趣，因此，对于那位讲述此案的聪慧女士，我无意抢先置喙任何内容；经过慎重考量，我遂决定，对这位博学医生的推论不作任何摘要，对其论述亦不作任何摘引——他所探讨的这一主题，他本人形容为"所涉者，颇有可能牵动我们二元存在及其居间层次的若干最深奥秘"。

===Original===
I was anxious, on discovering this paper, to reopen the correspondence commenced by Doctor Hesselius, so many years before, with a person so clever and careful as his informant seems to have been. Much to my regret, however, I found that she had died in the interval.

===Chinese===
发现这纸笺记之时，我本急欲重启赫塞利乌斯医生在多年以前与那位情报提供者所开始的通信——其人观其所述，显得那般聪敏而审慎。然而令我深感惋惜的是，我得知她已在此期间故去了。

===Original===
She, probably, could have added little to the narrative which she communicates in the following pages, with, so far as I can pronounce, such a conscientious particularity.

===Chinese===
推想起来，对于她在以下篇页中所传达的那份记述——以我所能判断的而言，她已怀着那般一丝不苟的周详将其备述——她恐怕也再难有所增补了。
