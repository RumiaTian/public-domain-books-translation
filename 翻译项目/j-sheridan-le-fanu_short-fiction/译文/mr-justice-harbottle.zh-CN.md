### Prologue / 前言

===Original===
On this case, Doctor Hesselius has inscribed nothing more than the words, "Harman's Report," and a simple reference to his own extraordinary essay on "The Interior Sense, and the Conditions of the Opening Thereof."

The reference is to Vol. I, Section 317, Note Za. The note to which reference is thus made, simply says: "There are two accounts of the remarkable case of the Honourable Mr. Justice Harbottle, one furnished to me by Mrs. Trimmer of Tunbridge Wells (June, 1805); the other at a much later date, by Anthony Harman, Esq. I much prefer the former; in the first place, because it is minute and detailed, and written, it seems to me, with more caution and knowledge; and in the next, because the letters from Doctor Hedstone, which are embodied in it, furnish matter of the highest value to a right apprehension of the nature of the case. It was one of the best declared cases of an opening of the interior sense, which I have met with. It was affected, too, by the phenomenon, which occurs so frequently as to indicate a law of these eccentric conditions; that is to say, it exhibited, what I may term, the contagious character of this sort of intrusion of the spirit-world upon the proper domain of matter. So soon as the spirit-action has established itself in the case of one patient, its developed energy begins to radiate, more or less effectually, upon others. The interior vision of the child was opened; as was, also, that of its mother, Mrs. Pyneweck; and both the interior vision and hearing of the scullery-maid, were opened on the same occasion. After-appearances are the result of the law explained in Vol. II. Section 17 to 49. The common centre of association, simultaneously recalled, unites, or *re*unites, as the case may be, for a period measured, as we see, in Section 37. The *maximum* will extend to days, the *minimum* is little more than a second. We see the operation of this principle perfectly displayed, in certain cases of lunacy, of epilepsy, of catalepsy, and of mania, of a peculiar and painful character, though unattended by incapacity of business."

关于此案，赫塞利乌斯医生（Doctor Hesselius）只写下了"哈曼报告（Harman's Report）"几个字，并附了一处简单的引见，指向他自己那篇非凡的论文——论"内在感官（the interior sense）及其开启之条件"。

该引见指向第一卷第三百一十七节，注 Z a。被如此援及的那条注释，只这样写道："尊敬的哈博特尔法官大人（the Honourable Mr. Justice Harbottle）这一桩奇案，现存有两份记述，一份由坦布里奇韦尔斯（Tunbridge Wells）的特里默夫人（Mrs. Trimmer）提供（一八〇五年六月）；另一份时日甚晚，出自安东尼·哈曼先生（Anthony Harman, Esq.）。两相比较，我远为偏取前者；其一，因其细致而详尽，据我看来，下笔更为审慎、更见学识；其二，因其中所收录的赫德斯通医生（Doctor Hedstone）的几封书信，为正确领会此案的性质，提供了至为珍贵的材料。这是我所曾寓目的、内在感官开启之症候最为明晰的案例之一。此案且受一种现象所累，而该现象出现之频繁，已足可表明它是此类乖戾病况下的一条规律；换言之，它呈现出——姑且如此称之——灵异世界这般侵入物质本然之领域时所带的那种传染性特征。灵异之作用一旦在某一病人身上确立，其已然展开的势能，便开始向外辐射，或多或少地波及旁人。那孩子的内在视觉得以开启；其母派因内克夫人（Mrs. Pyneweck）的内在视觉亦然；而洗碗女佣的内在视觉与内在听觉，也于同一情形之下双双开启。此后种种再現之象，则是第二卷第十七至四十九节所阐释的那条定律所致。那被同时唤起的共同联想之核心，视情况而合、或复合，其历时之久暂，一如我们在第三十七节中所见。最长者可延及数日，最短者不过一瞬。这一原理的运作，在某些疯症、癫痫、僵症及一种特殊而痛苦的躁狂之症中——虽则并不妨碍患者经办事务——我们可见其展露无遗。"

The memorandum of the case of Judge Harbottle, which was written by Mrs. Trimmer of Tunbridge Wells, which Doctor Hesselius thought the better of the two, I have been unable to discover among his papers. I found in his escritoire a note to the effect that he had lent the Report of Judge Harbottle's case, written by Mrs. Trimmer, to Doctor F. Heyne. To that learned and able gentleman accordingly I wrote, and received from him, in his reply, which was full of alarms and regrets on account of the uncertain safety of that "valuable MS.," a line written long since by Doctor Hesselius, which completely exonerated him, inasmuch as it acknowledged the safe return of the papers. The narrative of Mr. Harman, is, therefore, the only one available for this collection. The late Dr. Hesselius, in another passage of the note that I have cited, says, "As to the facts (non-medical) of the case, the narrative of Mr. Harman exactly tallies with that furnished by Mrs. Trimmer." The strictly scientific view of the case would scarcely interest the popular reader; and, possibly, for the purposes of this selection, I should, even had I both papers to choose between, have preferred that of Mr. Harman, which is given, in full, in the following pages.

Heyne）。于是我便致函那位博学干练的先生，收到他的回函；回函之中，因那部"珍贵手稿"之安危未卜而满纸惊惶与憾惜，并附有一行赫塞利乌斯医生多年前所写的字迹，此行字迹将他开脱得干干净净——其中明言承认，那些文件早已安然归还。因此，哈曼先生的记述，便成了可供本集使用的唯一一份。已故的赫塞利乌斯医生，在我所援引的那张便笺的另一处说道："至于此案（非医学方面）的事实，哈曼先生的记述与特里默夫人所提供的那份，恰相吻合。"此案严格科学一面的看法，怕是难以引起普通读者的兴趣；而且，就算两份记述都摆在我眼前任我取舍，就本选集的用意而言，我多半也仍会偏取哈曼先生这一份。该份记述，全文具载于以下诸页。
