## Laura Silver Bell / 劳拉·银铃

===Original===
In the five Northumbrian counties you will scarcely find so bleak, ugly, and yet, in a savage way, so picturesque a moor as Dardale Moss. The moor itself spreads north, south, east, and west, a great undulating sea of black peat and heath.
What we may term its shores are wooded wildly with birch, hazel, and dwarf-oak. No towering mountains surround it, but here and there you have a rocky knoll rising among the trees, and many a wooded promontory of the same pretty, because utterly wild, forest, running out into its dark level.
Habitations are thinly scattered in this barren territory, and a full mile away from the meanest was the stone cottage of Mother Carke.

===Chinese===
在诺森伯兰那五个郡里，你只怕再难找到一片如此荒凉、如此丑陋，却又带着几分蛮荒的如画意味的荒野——达德尔沼（Dardale Moss）。这沼地本身向北、南、东、西四面铺展，如同一片起伏不定的茫茫黑海，尽是泥炭与石楠。
我们姑且称之为它的"岸"的地方，杂乱地长着桦树、榛树与矮橡。四周并无巍峨群山环抱，只是林木之间这里那里隆起几座石丘，又有同样的森林——因了十足的荒蛮反添了几分可爱——伸出一道道林木葱茏的岬角，探入它那阴沉的平地之上。
这一带穷乡僻壤，人烟稀疏，居所寥落；而距那最寒伧的一处茅屋也足有一英里之遥的，便是卡克老妈（Mother Carke）的石屋。

===Original===
Let not my southern reader who associates ideas of comfort with the term "cottage" mistake. This thing is built of shingle, with low walls. Its thatch is hollow; the peat-smoke curls stingily from its stunted chimney. It is worthy of its savage surroundings.
The primitive neighbours remark that no rowan-tree grows near, nor holly, nor bracken, and no horseshoe is nailed on the door.
Not far from the birches and hazels that straggle about the rude wall of the little enclosure, on the contrary, they say, you may discover the broom and the ragwort, in which witches mysteriously delight. But this is perhaps a scandal.

===Chinese===
请我那南方的读者莫要见"茅屋"二字便联想到什么舒适安逸，因而生出误会。这物件是用木瓦搭成的，墙矮矮的。屋顶的草茬塌陷下去；泥炭的青烟吝啬地从那矮小的烟囱里袅袅升起。它与它那蛮荒的周遭倒正相宜。
那些淳朴的邻里都说，近处既不长花楸，也没有冬青，没有蕨丛，门上更没有钉着马蹄铁。
倒是那小院粗陋的墙边东倒西歪的桦树与榛树近旁，他们说道，你能寻见金雀花与千里光——正是巫婆们神秘所好的那两种草。不过这或许是诋毁之辞。

===Original===
Mall Carke was for many a year the *sage femme* of this wild domain. She has renounced practice, however, for some years; and now, under the rose, she dabbles, it is thought, in the black art, in which she has always been secretly skilled, tells fortunes, practises charms, and in popular esteem is little better than a witch.
Mother Carke has been away to the town of Willarden, to sell knit stockings, and is returning to her rude dwelling by Dardale Moss. To her right, as far away as the eye can reach, the moor stretches. The narrow track she has followed here tops a gentle upland, and at her left a sort of jungle of dwarf-oak and brushwood approaches its edge. The sun is sinking bloodred in the west. His disk has touched the broad black level of the moor, and his parting beams glare athwart the gaunt figure of the old beldame, as she strides homeward stick in hand, and bring into relief the folds of her mantle, which gleam like the draperies of a bronze image in the light of a fire. For a few moments this light floods the air—tree, gorse, rock, and bracken glare; and then it is out, and gray twilight over everything.

===Chinese===
马尔·卡克（Mall Carke）多年来一直是这一片蛮荒之地的接生婆（sage femme）。不过她已歇业数年；如今私下里——人们暗中传说——摆弄起黑巫术来，这一样本事她向来精通，又替人算命、行那驱神役鬼的小术，在乡人眼里，她与一个巫婆也相差无几了。

===Original===

===Original===
