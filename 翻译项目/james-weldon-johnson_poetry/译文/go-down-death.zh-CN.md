## Go Down Death A Funeral Sermon / 下去吧，死亡：一篇葬礼布道

===Original===
Weep not, weep not,
She is not dead;
She's resting in the bosom of Jesus.
Heart-broken husband—weep no more;
Grief-stricken son—weep no more;
Left-lonesome daughter—weep no more;
She's only just gone home.

Day before yesterday morning,
God was looking down from his great, high heaven
Looking down on all his children,
And his eye fell on Sister Caroline,
Tossing on her bed of pain.
And God's big heart was touched with pity,
With the everlasting pity.

===Chinese===
不要哭，不要哭，
她没有死；
她正在耶稣的怀抱里安息。
心碎的丈夫——别再哭了；
悲恸的儿子——别再哭了；
被撇下而孤单的女儿——别再哭了；
她不过是刚刚回了家。

前天早晨，
上帝从他崇高浩大的天国俯望，
俯望着他所有的儿女，
他的目光落在卡罗琳姊妹身上，
她正在病痛的床上翻腾。
上帝那博大的心被怜悯触动，
被那永远的怜悯触动。

===Original===
And God sat back on his throne,
And he commanded that tall, bright angel standing at his right hand:
Call me Death!
And that tall, bright angel cried in a voice
That broke like a clap of thunder:
Call Death!—Call Death!
And the echo sounded down the streets of heaven
Till it reached away back to that shadowy place,
Where Death waits with his pale, white horses.

And Death heard the summons,
And he leaped on his fastest horse,
Pale as a sheet in the moonlight.
Up the golden street Death galloped,
And the hoofs of his horse struck fire from the gold,
But they didn't make no sound.
Up Death rode to the Great White Throne,
And waited for God's command.

===Chinese===
上帝在他的宝座上坐直了身子，
吩咐站在他右手边的那位高大光亮的天使：
去把死亡给我叫来！
那位高大光亮的天使便高声喊道，
嗓音像一声炸雷：
叫死亡来！——叫死亡来！
回声顺着天国的街道一路响下去，
一直传到那幽暗的远方，
死亡正带着他苍白如雪的马匹在那里等候。

死亡听见了召唤，
他跃上他最快的马，
月光里苍白得像一页素纸。
死亡沿着金色的街道疾驰，
马蹄踏得金地迸出火星，
却没有发出一点声响。
死亡一路驰到那大白宝座前，
等候上帝的吩咐。

===Original===
And God said: Go down, Death, go down,
Go down to Savannah, Georgia,
Down in Yamacraw,
And find Sister Caroline.
She's borne the burden and heat of the day,
She's labored long in my vineyard,
And she's tired—
She's weary—
Go down, Death, and bring her to me.

And Death didn't say a word,
But he loosed the reins on his pale, white horse,
And he clamped the spurs to his bloodless sides,
And out and down he rode,
Through heaven's pearly gates,
Past suns and moons and stars;
On Death rode,
And the foam from his horse was like a comet in the sky;
On Death rode,
Leaving the lightning's flash behind;
Straight on down he came.

===Chinese===
上帝说：下去吧，死亡，下去，
下到佐治亚的萨凡纳，
下到亚马克罗，
去找到卡罗琳姊妹。
她担过了白日的重担和炎热，
她在我的葡萄园里劳作了许久，
她累了——
她乏了——
下去吧，死亡，把她带到我这里来。

死亡一句话也没说，
只把他那匹苍白如雪的马的缰绳放开，
把马刺夹向他那毫无血色的马腹，
他纵马冲出，一路向下，
穿过天国的珍珠城门，
掠过一个个太阳、月亮和星辰；
死亡一路奔驰，
马身上溅起的白沫像天上一颗彗星；
死亡一路奔驰，
把闪电的光甩在身后；
他径直向下降落。

===Original===
While we were watching round her bed,
She turned her eyes and looked away,
She saw what we couldn't see;
She saw Old Death. She saw Old Death
Coming like a falling star.
But Death didn't frighten Sister Caroline;
He looked to her like a welcome friend.
And she whispered to us: I'm going home,
And she smiled and closed her eyes.

And Death took her up like a baby,
And she lay in his icy arms,
But she didn't feel no chill.
And Death began to ride again—
Up beyond the evening star,
Out beyond the morning star,
Into the glittering light of glory,
On to the Great White Throne.
And there he laid Sister Caroline
On the loving breast of Jesus.

===Chinese===
我们正围在她的床边守望，
她转过头去，望向别处，
她看见了我们所看不见的；
她看见了死亡老爹。她看见死亡老爹
像一颗陨落的星降落。
可是死亡没有吓着卡罗琳姊妹；
在她眼里，他就像一位受欢迎的朋友。
她低声对我们说：我要回家了，
然后她笑了笑，合上了眼睛。

死亡像抱婴儿一样把她抱起，
她躺在他冰冷的臂弯里，
却没有感到一丝寒意。
死亡重新策马奔驰——
向上越过黄昏星，
再越过晨星，
奔进荣耀的璀璨光辉，
直奔那大白宝座。
在那里，他把卡罗琳姊妹
安放在耶稣慈爱的胸前。

===Original===
And Jesus took his own hand and wiped away her tears,
And he smoothed the furrows from her face,
And the angels sang a little song,
And Jesus rocked her in his arms,
And kept a-saying: Take your rest,
Take your rest, take your rest.

Weep not—weep not,
She is not dead;
She's resting in the bosom of Jesus.

===Chinese===
耶稣亲手拭去她的眼泪，
抚平她脸上的皱纹，
天使们唱起一支小歌，
耶稣把她搂在怀里轻轻摇着，
一遍又一遍地说：安息吧，
安息吧，安息吧。

不要哭——不要哭，
她没有死；
她正在耶稣的怀抱里安息。
