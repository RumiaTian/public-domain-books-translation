## Fifty Years (1863–1913) / 五十年（1863–1913）

===Original===
On the Fiftieth Anniversary of the Signing of the Emancipation Proclamation

O brothers mine, today we stand
Where half a century sweeps our ken,
Since God, through Lincoln's ready hand,
Struck off our bonds and made us men.

Just fifty years—a winter's day—
As runs the history of a race;
Yet, as we look back o'er the way,
How distant seems our starting place!

Look farther back! Three centuries!
To where a naked, shivering score,
Snatched from their haunts across the seas,
Stood, wild-eyed, on Virginia's shore.

This land is ours by right of birth,
This land is ours by right of toil;
We helped to turn its virgin earth,
Our sweat is in its fruitful soil.

===Chinese===
谨以此纪念《解放宣言》签署五十周年

啊，我的兄弟，今天我们伫立之处，
半个世纪的岁月扫过我们的眼界，
自从上帝借林肯那甘愿的手，
斩断我们的枷锁，使我们成为真正的人。

不过五十年——如一个冬日的长度——
以一个种族的历史来度量；
可当我们回望来路，
那起点显得多么遥远！

再往回看！整整三个世纪！
看到那一群赤裸、瑟瑟发抖的人，
从海那边的家园被掳掠而来，
睁着狂乱的眼睛，站在弗吉尼亚的海岸。

这片土地因出生的权利属于我们，
这片土地因劳作的权利属于我们；
我们曾帮着翻垦它处子的泥土，
我们的汗水浸在它丰饶的土壤里。

===Original===
Where once the tangled forest stood—
Where flourished once rank weed and thorn—
Behold the path-traced, peaceful wood,
The cotton white, the yellow corn.

To gain these fruits that have been earned,
To hold these fields that have been won,
Our arms have strained, our backs have burned,
Bent bare beneath a ruthless sun.

That Banner which is now the type
Of victory on field and flood—
Remember, its first crimson stripe
Was dyed by Attucks' willing blood.

And never yet has come the cry—
When that fair flag has been assailed—
For men to do, for men to die,
That we have faltered or have failed.

===Chinese===
在那曾经荆棘缠绕的密林所在——
在那曾经疯长野草和荆棘的地方——
看吧，小径纵横、宁静安逸的树林，
雪白的棉花，金黄的玉米。

为了收获这些挣来的果实，
为了守住这些赢得的田地，
我们的手臂绷得酸痛，脊背被灼烤，
赤裸着弯向无情的烈日。

那面如今象征着
战场与洪流之上胜利的旗帜——
记住，它第一道绯红的条纹，
是阿塔克斯（Attucks）甘愿洒下的热血染成。

而每当那美好的旗帜遭受攻击，
号召人们去干、去死的呐喊响起——
我们从来没有一次
畏缩过，或退缩过。

===Original===
We've helped to bear it, rent and torn,
Through many a hot-breath'd battle breeze
Held in our hands, it has been borne
And planted far across the seas.

And never yet—O haughty Land,
Let us, at least, for this be praised—
Has one black, treason-guided hand
Ever against that flag been raised.

Then should we speak but servile words,
Or shall we hang our heads in shame?
Stand back of new-come foreign hordes,
And fear our heritage to claim?

No! stand erect and without fear,
And for our foes let this suffice—
We've bought a rightful sonship here,
And we have more than paid the price.

===Chinese===
我们曾扛着它，纵然它已被撕破扯碎，
穿过一场场热风灼人的战地硝烟，
它曾被我们擎在手中，远渡重洋，
插到大海的彼岸。

而且从没有——哦，高傲的国度，
至少这一点请容我们受到称许——
从没有一只受叛逆驱使的黑人的手
曾经举起来反对那面旗帜。

难道我们只该说卑躬屈膝的话，
或者羞愧地垂下我们的头？
退到新来的异族人群后面，
不敢主张我们生来承继的权利？

不！挺直站着，无所畏惧，
对仇视我们的人只须说这一句——
我们已在这里买下了名正言顺的儿子名分，
而且我们付出的代价早已超出。

===Original===
And yet, my brothers, well I know
The tethered feet, the pinioned wings,
The spirit bowed beneath the blow,
The heart grown faint from wounds and stings;

The staggering force of brutish might,
That strikes and leaves us stunned and dazed;
The long, vain waiting through the night
To hear some voice for justice raised.

Full well I know the hour when hope
Sinks dead, and 'round us everywhere
Hangs stifling darkness, and we grope
With hands uplifted in despair.

Courage! Look out, beyond, and see
The far horizon's beckoning span!
Faith in your God-known destiny!
We are a part of some great plan.

===Chinese===
然而我的兄弟，我深知
那被拴住的双脚，被缚住的翅膀，
深知重击之下低垂的精神，
被创伤与蜇刺磨得衰弱的心；

深知蛮横强权那叫人踉跄的力量，
打得我们目瞪神迷；
深知漫漫长夜里徒劳的等待，
等着听见有人为正义发声。

我深知那样的时刻：希望
死沉沉地坠落，我们四周
笼罩着令人窒息的黑暗，我们伸出
高举的手，在绝望中摸索。

鼓起勇气！放眼望去，看
遥远地平线招手的辽阔！
信奉上帝昭示给你们的命运！
我们是某个宏大计划的一部分。

===Original===
Because the tongues of Garrison
And Phillips now are cold in death,
Think you their work can be undone?
Or quenched the fires lit by their breath?

Think you that John Brown's spirit stops?
That Lovejoy was but idly slain?
Or do you think those precious drops
From Lincoln's heart were shed in vain?

That for which millions prayed and sighed,
That for which tens of thousands fought,
For which so many freely died,
God cannot let it come to naught.

===Chinese===
不要因为加里森（Garrison）
和菲利普斯（Phillips）的舌头已在死亡中冷却，
就以为他们的事业可以被推翻？
就以为他们呼出的火种可以被扑灭？

你们以为约翰·布朗的精神会止息？
以为洛夫乔伊（Lovejoy）只是白白被杀？
或者你们以为从林肯心中
洒下的那些珍贵的血滴是白流？

那千百万人祈祷过、叹息过的心愿，
那几万人为之战斗过的事业，
那么多人甘愿为之牺牲，
上帝不会容它化为泡影。
