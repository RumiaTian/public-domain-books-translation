## The Word of an Engineer / 工程师的一句话

===Original===
"She's built of steel
From deck to keel,
And bolted strong and tight;
In scorn she'll sail
The fiercest gale,
And pierce the darkest night.

"The builder's art
Has proved each part
Throughout her breadth and length;
Deep in the hulk,
Of her mighty bulk,
Ten thousand Titans' strength."

The tempest howls,
The Ice Wolf prowls,
The winds they shift and veer,
But calm I sleep,
And faith I keep
In the word of an engineer.

===Chinese===
「她用钢建成，
从甲板到龙骨，
用螺栓铆得坚固严实；
她会傲然驶过
最猛烈的暴风，
刺破最漆黑的夜。

「建造者的技艺
检验过她的每一部分，
从舷到舷，从首到尾；
在她那庞大船身
的深处，
藏着一万个泰坦的神力。」

风暴嗥叫，
冰狼潜行，
风向变幻莫测，
可我安然入睡，
怀着信念，
信着工程师的一句话。

===Original===
Along the trail
Of the slender rail
The train, like a nightmare, flies
And dashes on
Through the black-mouthed yawn
Where the cavernous tunnel lies.

Over the ridge,
Across the bridge,
Swung twixt the sky and hell,
On an iron thread
Spun from the head
Of the man in a draughtsman's cell.

And so we ride
Over land and tide,
Without a thought of fear—
*Man never had*
*The faith in God*
*That he has in an engineer!*

===Chinese===
沿着那细细铁轨
铺出的道，
列车像一场噩梦般飞驰，
一头冲进
那黑洞洞张开的
深隧道的巨口。

越过山脊，
跨过桥梁，
悬在天与地狱之间，
靠一根铁线，
它从那制图室里
一个人头脑中抽出。

就这样我们乘车奔驰，
越过陆地和潮水，
心里没有一丝恐惧——
*人从没有对上帝*
*有过像对工程师*
*那样的信心！*
