## 'Possum Song / 负鼠之歌

===Original===
(A Warning)
'Simmons ripenin' in de fall,
You better run,
Brudder 'Possum, run!
Mockin' bird commence to call,
You better run, Brudder 'Possum, git out de way!
You better run, Brudder 'Possum, git out de way!
Run some whar an' hide!
Ole moon am sinkin'
Down behin' de tree.
Ole Eph am thinkin'
An' chuckelin' wid glee.
Ole Tige am blinkin'
An' frisky as kin be,
Yo' chances, Brudder 'Possum,
Look mighty slim to me.

Run, run, run, I tell you,
Run, Brudder 'Possum, run!
Run, run, run, I tell you,
Ole Eph's got a gun.
Pickaninnies grinnin'
Waitin' fu' to see de fun.
You better run, Brudder 'Possum, git out de way!
Run, Brudder 'Possum, run!

===Chinese===
（一则警告）
柿子在秋天熟透了，
你快跑，
负鼠大哥，快跑！
模仿鸟开始叫了，
你快跑，负鼠大哥，躲开这条道！
你快跑，负鼠大哥，躲开这条道！
找个地方藏好！
老月亮
正沉到树后头。
老伊弗心里盘算着，
乐得直偷笑。
老泰杰眨着眼，
撒欢得不得了，
你的指望啊，负鼠大哥，
我瞧着可实在渺茫。

跑，跑，跑，我告诉你，
跑，负鼠大哥，快跑！
跑，跑，跑，我告诉你，
老伊弗手里有枪。
黑娃娃们咧着嘴，
等着看好戏呢。
你快跑，负鼠大哥，躲开这条道！
跑，负鼠大哥，快跑！
