2. **形近字/IME 别字 1 处（专项⑤）**
   - 文件：`译文/trooping-with-crows.zh-CN.md` 第 12 行
   - 英文块摘录：`…and would tumble over the girl’s eyes whenever she turned up her face to meet yours`
