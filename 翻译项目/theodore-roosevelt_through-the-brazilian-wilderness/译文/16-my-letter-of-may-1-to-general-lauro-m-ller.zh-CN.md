## Appendix C My Letter of May 1 to General Lauro Müller / 附录C 我5月1日致劳罗·米勒将军的信
===Original===

The first report on the expedition, made by me immediately after my arrival at Manaos, and published in Rio de Janeiro upon its receipt, is as follows:

===Chinese===

我抵达马瑙斯后立即写就的关于考察队的首份报告，到件后即在里约热内卢发表，全文如下：

===Original===

> I wish first to express my profound acknowledgments to you personally and to the other members of the Brazilian Government whose generous courtesy alone rendered possible the Expedição Scientifica Roosevelt-Rondon. I wish also to express my high admiration and regard for Colonel Rondon and his associates who have been my colleagues in this work of exploration. In the third place I wish to point out that what we have just done was rendered possible only by the hard and perilous labor of the Brazilian Telegraphic Commission in the unexplored western wilderness of Matto Grosso during the last seven years. We have had a hard and somewhat dangerous but very successful trip. No less than six weeks were spent in slowly and with peril and exhausting labor forcing our way down through what seemed a literally endless succession of rapids and cataracts. For forty-eight days we saw no human being. In passing these rapids we lost five of the seven canoes with which we started and had to build others. One of our best men lost his life in the rapids. Under the strain one of the men went completely bad, shirked all his work, stole his comrades’ food and when punished by the sergeant he with cold-blooded deliberation murdered the sergeant and fled into the wilderness. Colonel Rondon’s dog running ahead of him while hunting, was shot by two Indians; by his death he in all probability saved the life of his master. We have put on the map a river about 1,500 kilometres in length running from just south of the 13th degree to north of the 5th degree and the biggest affluent of the Madeira. Until now its upper course has been utterly unknown to everyone, and its lower course although known for years to the rubbermen utterly unknown to all cartographers. Its source is between the 12th and 13th parallels of latitude south, and between longitude 59° and longitude 60° west from Greenwich. We embarked on it about at latitude 12° 1′ south and longitude 60° 18 west. After that its entire course was between the 60th and 61st degrees of longitude approaching the latter most closely about in latitude 8° 15′. The first rapids were at Navaïté in 11° 44′ and after that they were continuous and very difficult and dangerous until the rapids named after the murdered sergeant Paishon in 11° 12′. At 11° 23′ the river received the Rio Kermit from the left.

===Chinese===

> 首先，我谨向您本人及巴西政府其他成员致以深切的谢忱——唯有诸位慷慨的礼遇，罗斯福-龙东科学考察队（Expedição Scientifica Roosevelt-Rondon）方得以成行。我还要向隆东上校及其同仁表达崇高的敬佩与问候——他们是我此次勘察工作中并肩的同事。第三，我要指出：我们刚刚完成的事业，唯有仰仗巴西电报委员会过去七年间在马托格罗索西部未勘荒野中的艰苦而凶险的劳动，方才成为可能。
> 我们的旅程艰辛而略带凶险，但非常成功。我们足足花了六个星期，冒着危险、耗尽力气，缓缓闯过一道接一道仿佛无穷无尽的险滩瀑布。整整四十八天，我们没有见到一个人影。过这些险滩时，出发时的七条独木舟折损了五条，只得另造新舟。我们一名最好的伙计在险滩中丧了命。在重压之下，有一个人彻底堕落：逃避一切劳作，偷窃同伴的食物；中士惩罚他时，他竟蓄意冷血地杀害了中士，逃入荒野。隆东上校打猎时跑在前面的狗，被两个印第安人射杀；它一死，多半救了主人的命。
> 我们已在地图上标出一条约1500公里长的河：起自南纬十三度以南不远处，止于南纬五度以北，是马代拉河最大的支流。迄今为止，其上游对所有人全然未知；下游虽为采胶人熟知多年，却对一切制图者全然未知。其源头在南纬12度与13度之间、格林尼治西经59度与60度之间。我们登船处约在南纬12°1′、西经60°18′。此后全程河道都在西经60度与61度之间，以南纬8°15′处最逼近后者。第一处险滩在纳瓦伊泰，南纬11°44′；此后险滩连绵不断，艰难凶险，直到以遇害的派雄中士命名的派雄滩，南纬11°12′。南纬11°23′处，克米特河自左汇入。

===Original===

At 11° 22′ the Marciano Avila entered it from the right. At 11° 18′ the Taunay entered from the left. At 10° 58′ the Cardozo entered from the right. At 10° 24′ we encountered the first rubber man. The Rio Branco entered from the left at 9° 38′. We camped at 8° 49′ or approximately the boundary line between Matto Grosso and Amazonas. The confluence with the upper Aripuanan, which entered from the right, was in 7° 34′. The mouth where it entered the Madeira was in about 5° 30′. The stream we have followed down is that which rises farthest away from the mouth and its general course is almost due north.
> My dear Sir, I thank you from my heart for the chance to take part in this great work of exploration.
> With high regard and respect, believe me
> Very sincerely yours,
> Theodore Roosevelt.

===Chinese===

> 南纬11°22′处，马尔恰诺·阿维拉河自右汇入。南纬11°18′处，陶奈河自左汇入。南纬10°58′处，卡多佐河自右汇入。南纬10°24′处，我们遇到第一个采胶人。布兰科河于南纬9°38′自左汇入。我们在南纬8°49′处扎营，约当马托格罗索州与亚马逊州的州界。与自右来汇的阿里普阿南河上游的合流处，在南纬7°34′。它注入马代拉河的河口，约在南纬5°30′。我们循流而下的这一支，正是离河口最远的源头；其大流向几乎正北。
> 亲爱的先生，我从心底感谢您给我参与这项伟大勘察事业的机会。
> 谨致崇高的敬意与问候，请相信我是
> 您最诚挚的，
> 西奥多·罗斯福。
