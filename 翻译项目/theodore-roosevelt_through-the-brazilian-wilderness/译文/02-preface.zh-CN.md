## Preface / 序言
===Original===

This is an account of a zoo-geographic reconnaissance through the Brazilian hinterland.
The official and proper title of the expedition is that given it by the Brazilian Government: Expedição Scientifica Roosevelt-Rondon. When I started from the United States, it was to make an expedition, primarily concerned with mammalogy and ornithology, for the American Museum of Natural History of New York. This was undertaken under the auspices of Messrs. Osborn and Chapman, acting on behalf of the Museum. In the body of this work I describe how the scope of the expedition was enlarged, and how it was given a geographic as well as a zoological character, in consequence of the kind proposal of the Brazilian Secretary of State for Foreign Affairs, General Lauro Müller. In its altered and enlarged form the expedition was rendered possible only by the generous assistance of the Brazilian Government. Throughout the body of the work will be found reference after reference to my colleagues and companions of the expedition, whose services to science I have endeavored to set forth, and for whom I shall always feel the most cordial friendship and regard.
Theodore Roosevelt.
Sagamore Hill, September 1, 1914

===Chinese===

本书记述的是一次穿越巴西腹地的动物地理勘察（zoo-geographic reconnaissance）。
本次考察队的正式名称由巴西政府所定：罗斯福-龙东科学考察队（Expedição Scientifica Roosevelt-Rondon）。我从美国启程时，原计划是为纽约美国自然历史博物馆（American Museum of Natural History）进行一次以哺乳动物学（mammalogy）和鸟类学（ornithology）为主要任务的考察。此行承蒙奥斯本先生与查普曼先生代表博物馆鼎力主持。在本书正文中，我记述了考察范围如何扩大，又如何因巴西外交部长劳罗·米勒将军（General Lauro Müller）的善意提议，从单纯的动物学考察扩展为兼具地理学性质的考察。考察队经此扩充改组之后，唯有依靠巴西政府的慷慨协助方能成行。正文各处屡屡提及考察队的诸位同僚与旅伴，我已尽力阐扬他们为科学所做的贡献，而对他们，我将永怀最诚挚的友情与敬意。
西奥多·罗斯福
萨加莫尔山（Sagamore Hill），1914年9月1日
