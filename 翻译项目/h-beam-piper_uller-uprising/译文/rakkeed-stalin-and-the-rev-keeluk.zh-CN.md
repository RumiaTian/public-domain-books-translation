## II Rakkeed, Stalin, and the Rev. Keeluk / 第二章 拉基德、斯大林与基卢克神父

===Original===
Von Schlichten, in a fresh uniform, sat at the end of the table in Sidney Harrington’s office; Harrington and Eric Blount, the Lieutenant-Governor, faced each other across it, over the three-foot disc of an Ulleran chessboard. Harrington had the white, or center, position. Blount, sandy-haired and considerably younger, was playing black, and his pieces were closing in relentlessly from the outer rim.

“Well, then what?” Harrington asked.

Von Schlichten dropped ash from his cigarette into the tray that served all three of them.
