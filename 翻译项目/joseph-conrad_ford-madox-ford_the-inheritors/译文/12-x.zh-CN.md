## X / 第十章

===Original===
Before noon of the next day I was ascending the stairs of the new house in which the Duc had his hermitage. There was an air of secrecy in the broad publicity of the carpeted stairs that led to his flat; a hush in the atmosphere; in the street itself, a glorified cul de sac that ran into the bustling life of the Italiens. It had the sudden sluggishness of a backwater. One seemed to have grown suddenly deaf in the midst of the rattle.

===Chinese===
第二天晌午之前，我已经在登那幢新楼的楼梯——公爵把他的隐居之所设在了里面。通往他那套公寓的楼梯铺着地毯，宽宽绰绰地敞在明处，却透着一股隐秘气；空气里有一种屏息的静；那条街本身是一条装点得体面的死胡同，一头扎进意大利人剧院街区（the Italiens）喧腾的生活。那地方有一种回水死湾般突如其来的滞缓。人在那片喧响当中，仿佛骤然间聋了。

===Original===
There was an incredible suggestion of silence﻿—the silence of a private detective﻿—in the mien of the servant who ushered me into a room. He was the English servant of the theatre﻿—the English servant that foreigners affect. The room had a splendour of its own, not a cheaply vulgar splendour, but the vulgarity of the most lavish plush and purple kind. The air was heavy, killed by the scent of exotic flowers, darkened by curtains that suggested the voluminous velvet backgrounds of certain old portraits. The Duc de Mersch had carried with him into this place of retirement the taste of the New Palace, that showplace of his that was the stupefaction of swarms of honest tourists.

===Chinese===
那个引我进房间的仆人，气度间有一种令人难以置信的静默——私家侦探式的静默。他是戏台上的那种英国仆人——外国人装模作样学来的那种英国仆人。这房间自有一种豪华，不是廉价俗气的豪华，而是最挥霍的长毛绒与紫绛那一类的俗。空气滞重，被异国花香熏得死气沉沉，又被窗帘压得昏暗——那些窗帘令人想起某些旧肖像画里厚重层叠的天鹅绒背景。德·默施公爵把新宫（the New Palace）的趣味也随身带进了这处退隐之地——新宫是他那座炫耀之所，一向令成群结队的老实游客看得目瞪口呆。

===Original===
I remembered soon enough that the man was a philanthropist, that he might be an excellent man of heart and indifferent of taste. He must be. But I was prone to be influenced by things of this sort, and felt depressed at the thought that so much of royal excellence should weigh so heavily in the wrong scale of the balance of the applied arts. I turned my back on the room and gazed at the blazing white decorations of the opposite house-fronts.

===Chinese===
我很快就想起，此人是个慈善家，他兴许心地极好，只是趣味低劣。想必如此。可我素来容易被这类事物左右，一想到这么多帝王家的卓异品质，竟沉甸甸地压在实用艺术这杆秤错了的一侧秤盘上，便觉得抑郁。我转过身去，背对房间，凝望对面一排房屋立面那白得晃眼的装饰。

===Original===
A door behind me must have opened, for I heard the sounds of a concluding tirade in a high-pitched voice.

===Chinese===
我背后准是有一扇门开了，因为我听见一个尖利的嗓音，正嚷到一段长篇斥骂的收梢。

===Original===
“*Et quant à un duc de farce, je ne m’en fiche pas mal, moi*,” it said in an accent curiously compounded of the foreign and the *coulisse*. A muttered male remonstrance ensued, and then, with disconcerting clearness:

===Chinese===
“Et quant à un duc de farce, je ne m’en fiche pas mal, moi（至于一个滑稽戏里的公爵，我嘛，可一点儿也不放在心上），”那声音说，口音古怪地掺和着外国腔与后台（coulisse）腔。接着是一阵男人含混的低声劝阻，随后，字字清晰得叫人难堪：

===Original===
“*Gr-r-rangeur﻿—Eschingan﻿—eh bien﻿—il entend. Et moi, j’entends, moi aussi. Tu veux me jouer contre elle. La Grangeur﻿—pah! Consoles-toi avec elle, mon vieux. Je ne veux plus de toi. Tu m’as donné de tes sales rentes Groenlandoises, et je n’ai pas pu les vendre. Ah, vieux farceur, tu vas voir ce que j’en vais faire.*”

===Chinese===
“Gr-r-rangeur—Eschingan—eh bien—il entend. Et moi, j’entends, moi aussi. Tu veux me jouer contre elle. La Grangeur—pah! Consoles-toi avec elle, mon vieux. Je ne veux plus de toi. Tu m’as donné de tes sales rentes Groenlandoises, et je n’ai pas pu les vendre. Ah, vieux farceur, tu vas voir ce que j’en vais faire.（格兰——若——埃辛冈——好嘛——他听得见。我呢，我也听得见。你想拿我当筹码去对付她。勒·格兰若——呸！老东西，你找她去彼此安慰吧。我不要再要你了。你把你那些臭格陵兰债券塞给我，我一张都脱不了手。啊，老滑头，你等着瞧我拿它们怎么办。）”

===Original===
A glorious creature﻿—a really glorious creature﻿—came out of an adjoining room. She was as frail, as swaying as a garden lily. Her great blue eyes turned irefully upon me, her bowed lips parted, her nostrils quivered.

===Chinese===
隔壁房间里走出来一位光彩照人的造物——一位真正光彩照人的造物。她纤弱而摇曳，像园中一株百合。她那双蓝色的大眼睛怒冲冲地转向我，噘起的双唇分开了，鼻翼翕动。

===Original===
“*Et quant à vous, M. Grangeur Eschingan*,” she began, “*je vais vous donner mon idée à moi﻿ ﻿…*”

===Chinese===
“Et quant à vous, M. Grangeur Eschingan（至于您，格兰若·埃辛冈先生），”她开口道，“je vais vous donner mon idée à moi …（我这就把我的主意告诉您……）”

===Original===
I did not understand the situation in the least, but I appreciated the awkwardness of it. The world seemed to be standing on its head. I was overcome; but I felt for the person in the next room. I did not know what to do. Suddenly I found myself saying:

===Chinese===
我全然不明白这是怎样一种局面，却掂得出其中的尴尬。世界像是倒了个个儿。我狼狈不堪；可心里又同情隔壁房里的那位。我不知道该怎样才好。忽然间，我发觉自己开口说道：

===Original===
“I am extremely sorry, madam, but I don’t understand French.” An expression of more intense vexation passed into her face﻿—her beautiful face. I fancy she wished﻿—wished intensely﻿—to give me the benefit of her “*idée à elle*.” She made a quick, violent gesture of disgusted contempt, and turned toward the half-open door from which she had come. She began again to dilate upon the little weaknesses of the person behind, when silently and swiftly it closed. We heard the lock click. With extraordinary quickness she had her mouth at the keyhole: “*Peeg, peeg*,” she enunciated. Then she stood to her full height, her face became calm, her manner stately. She glided halfway across the room, paused, looked at me, and pointed toward the unmoving door.

===Chinese===
“非常抱歉，夫人，我不懂法语。”一种更强烈的恼怒掠过她的脸——她那张美丽的脸。我想她本是极想——极其想——让我领教她的“idée à elle（她自己的主意）”的。她迅疾而暴烈地做了个嫌恶轻蔑的手势，转身朝她出来的那扇半开的门走去。她又开始铺陈门后那人的种种小毛病，那门却无声而疾速地关上了。我们听见锁里咔哒一响。她以惊人的敏捷把嘴贴上锁眼：“Peeg, peeg（猪猡，猪猡）”，她一字一顿地念。随后她直起身子站定，脸色平静下来，仪态庄严。她翩然滑行到房间中央，停住，望着我，朝那扇纹丝不动的门指了一指。

===Original===
“*Peeg, peeg*,” she explained, mysteriously. I think she was warning me against the wiles of the person behind the door. I gazed into her great eyes. “I understand,” I said, gravely. She glided from the room. For me the incident supplied a welcome touch of comedy. I had leisure for thought. The door remained closed. It made the Duc a more real person for me. I had regarded him as a rather tiresome person in whom a pompous philanthropism took the place of human feelings. It amused me to be called *Le Grangeur*. It amused me, and I stood in need of amusement. Without it I might never have written the article on the Duc. I had started out that morning in a state of nervous irritation. I had wanted more than ever to have done with the thing, with the *Hour*, with journalism, with everything. But this little new experience buoyed me up, set my mind working in less morbid lines. I began to wonder whether de Mersch would funk, or whether he would take my non-comprehension of the woman’s tirades as a thing assured.

===Chinese===
“Peeg, peeg，”她神秘地解释道。我想她是在提醒我提防门后那人的诡诈。我凝视进她那双大眼睛。“我明白了。”我郑重地说。她翩然滑出了房间。这一幕于我恰好添了一笔受欢迎的喜剧色彩。我得了余暇可以思想。那扇门始终关着。它使公爵在我这里成了一个更真实的人物。此前我只把他看作一个相当讨厌的人，一身浮夸的慈善心肠顶替了人的感情。被人称作“勒·格兰若（Le Grangeur）”让我觉得好笑。这让我觉得好笑，而我正需要消遣。若没有这一笔，我或许根本写不出那篇关于公爵的文章。那天早晨出门时，我正处于一种神经质的烦躁之中。我前所未有地想同这件事一刀两断——同《时辰报》，同新闻行当，同一切。可这段小小的新经历让我振作起来，思路也转到不那么阴郁的轨道上去了。我开始琢磨，德·默施会不会临阵怯场，或者，他会不会把“我听不懂那女人的长篇斥骂”当作一件确凿无疑的事。

===Original===
The door at which I had entered, by which she had left, opened.

===Chinese===
我进来时走的那扇门——也是她出去的那扇——开了。

===Original===
He must have impressed me in some way or other that evening at the Churchills. He seemed a very stereotyped image in my memory. He spoke just as he had spoken, moved his hands just as I expected him to move them. He called for no modification of my views of his person. As a rule one classes a man so-and-so at first meeting, modifies the classification at each subsequent one, and so on. He seemed to be all affability, of an adipose turn. He had the air of the man of the world among men of the world; but none of the unconscious reserve of manner that one expects to find in the temporarily great. He had in its place a kind of sub-sulkiness, as if he regretted the pedestal from which he had descended.

===Chinese===
那天晚上在丘吉尔府上，他想必多少给过我一点印象。他在我的记忆里似乎是一幅非常刻板的图像。他说话正是先前那种说法，双手的动作也正是我料想中的那几动。他丝毫不需要我修正对自己这个人的看法。通常，人初次见面便把对方归入某类，此后每见一次修正一次分类，如此等等。他似乎满身和气，带着发福的势头。他有那种见惯世面的人置身于见惯世面者们中间的气派；却没有人们预期能在一时显贵身上看到的那种不自觉的矜持。取而代之的是一种隐隐的愠色，仿佛在惋惜他已经走下来的那座台座。

===Original===
In his slow commercial English he apologised for having kept me waiting; he had been taking the air of this fine morning, he said. He mumbled the words with his eyes on my waistcoat, with an air that accorded rather ill with the semblance of portentous probity that his beard conferred on him. But he set an eyeglass in his left eye immediately afterward, and looked straight at me as if in challenge. With a smiling “Don’t mention,” I tried to demonstrate that I met him halfway.

===Chinese===
他用他那口缓慢的商务英语道歉，说让我久等了；他说，方才他正趁着这晴好的早晨透透空气。他两眼盯着我的马甲咕哝出这几句话，那副神气，同他的胡须赋予他的俨然诚信的表象颇不相称。但他随即往左眼里架上一副单片眼镜，直勾勾地看着我，像是在下战书。我微笑着应了声“哪里的话”，想表明我是半路相迎的。

===Original===
“You want to interview me,” he said, blandly. “I am only too pleased. I suppose it is about my Arctic schemes that you wish to know. I will do what I can to inform you. You perhaps remember what I said when I had the pleasure of meeting you at the house of the Right Honourable Mr. Churchill. It has been the dream of my life to leave behind me a happy and contented State﻿—as much as laws and organisation can make one. This is what I should most like the English to know of me.” He was a dull talker. I supposed that philanthropists and state founders kept their best faculties for their higher pursuits. I imagined the low, receding forehead and the pink-nailed, fleshy hands to belong to a new Solon, a latter-day Aeneas. I tried to work myself into the properly enthusiastic frame of mind. After all, it was a great work that he had undertaken. I was too much given to dwell upon intellectual gifts. These the Duc seemed to lack. I credited him with having let them be merged in his one noble idea.

===Chinese===
“你是要来采访我，”他温和地说，“我高兴还来不及呢。我想，你要了解的是我的北极各项计划。我愿尽力让你知情。你也许还记得，我有幸在尊敬的丘吉尔先生阁下府上见到你时说过的话。在我身后留下一个幸福而知足的国家——尽法律与组织所能造就的那样一个——是我毕生的梦想。这是我最想让英国人了解的关于我的一切。”他是个乏味的谈客。我想，慈善家和建国者们总把最好的才具留给他们更崇高的追求。我想象着那低平后缩的额头和粉红指甲的厚实的手，属于一位新的梭伦（Solon）、一位当代的埃涅阿斯（Aeneas）。我努力使自己进入恰如其分的热情心境。毕竟，他着手的是一项伟大的事业。我太惯于计较智识上的禀赋了。这些，公爵似乎欠缺。我宁可相信，他已经让它们溶进了自己那唯一的高贵理想里。

===Original===
He furnished me with statistics. They had laid down so many miles of railways, used so many engines of British construction. They had taught the natives to use and to value sewing-machines and European costumes. So many hundred of English younger sons had gone to make their fortunes and, incidentally, to enlighten the Eskimo﻿—so many hundreds of French, of Germans, Greeks, Russians. All these lived and moved in harmony, employed, happy, free labourers, protected by the most rigid laws. Man-eating, fetish-worship, slavery had been abolished, stamped out. The great international society for the preservation of Polar freedom watched over all, suggested new laws, modified the old. The country was unhealthy, but not to men of clean lives﻿—*hominibus bonae voluntatis*. It asked for no others.

===Chinese===
他向我提供统计数字。他们铺了多少英里的铁路，用了多少台英国造的机车。他们教会土著使用并珍视缝纫机和欧式服装。多少百名英国人家的次子们到那里去发家，顺带教化爱斯基摩人——又有多少百名法国人、德国人、希腊人、俄国人。所有人都和睦地生活、活动，是受雇的、快乐的、自由的劳工，受着最严苛的法律保护。食人、拜物、奴隶制都已废除，被扑灭干净。那个保护极地自由的伟大国际社团监护着一切，提议新的法律，修改旧的。那地方有损健康，但只对不清白度日的人如此——对善意的人们（hominibus bonae voluntatis）则不然。它也不要别样的人。

===Original===
“I have had to endure much misrepresentation. I have been called names,” the Duc said.

===Chinese===
“我不得不忍受许许多多的曲解。我被人辱骂过。”公爵说。

===Original===
The figure of the lady danced before my eyes, lithe, supple﻿—a statue endued with the motion of a serpent. I seemed to see her sculptured white hand pointing to the closed door.

===Chinese===
那位女士的身影在我眼前舞动，轻盈、柔韧——一尊被赋予了蛇行动作的雕像。我仿佛看见她那只雕塑般的白手，正指着那扇关着的门。

===Original===
“Ah, yes,” I said, “but one knows the people that call you names.”

===Chinese===
“啊，是啊，”我说，“不过是什么样的人在骂您，大家心里都有数。”

===Original===
“Well, then,” he answered, “it is your task to make them know the truth. Your nation has so much power. If it will only realise.”

===Chinese===
“那么，”他答道，“让他们知道真相，就是你的任务了。贵国有的是力量。只要它肯明白过来。”

===Original===
“I will do my best,” I said.

===Chinese===
“我一定尽力。”我说。

===Original===
I saw the apotheosis of the Press﻿—a Press that makes a State Founder suppliant to a man like myself. For he had the tone of a deprecating petitioner. I stood between himself and a people, the arbiter of the peoples, of the kings of the future. I was nothing, nobody; yet here I stood in communion with one of those who change the face of continents. He had need of me, of the power that was behind me. It was strange to be alone in that room with that man﻿—to be there just as I might be in my own little room alone with any other man.

===Chinese===
我看见了报刊的神化——一种竟能让一位建国者向我这号人屈身相求的报刊。因为他的口气，正是一个低声下气的哀求者的口气。我立在他与一个民族之间，是万民的仲裁者，未来诸王的仲裁者。我什么也不是，是个无名之辈；可我竟站在这里，同一位改换大陆面貌的人物心意相通。他有求于我，有求于我背后的力量。同那个人单独待在那间屋子里——就像我可能在自己那间小屋里同随便哪个人单独待着一样——真是奇怪。

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Original===
“I will do my best,” I said.

===Chinese===
“我一定尽力。”我说。

===Original===
“Ah, yes, do,” he said, “Mr. Churchill told me how nicely you can do such things.”

===Chinese===
“啊，是的，务必，”他说，“丘吉尔先生告诉过我，这类事情你能办得多漂亮。”

===Original===

===Chinese===
我说丘吉尔先生真是费心了。谈话的紧张松弛了下来。公爵问我，可曾去见过我的姑母。

===Original===

===Original===
I went straight home to my little room above the roofs. I began at once to write my article, working at high pressure, almost hysterically. I remember that place and that time so well. In moments of emotion one gazes fixedly at things, hardly conscious of them. Afterward one remembers.

===Chinese===

I wrote furiously, anxious to be done with it. Once I went and leaned over the balcony, trying to hit on a word that would not come. Miles down below, little people crawled over the cobbled street, little carts rattled, little workmen let down casks into a cellar. It was all very grey, small, and clear.

===Chinese===

One of them was tall and blond, with a heavy, bow-shaped red moustache﻿—Irish in type; the other of no particular height, excellently groomed, dark, and exemplary. I knew he was exemplary from some detail of costume that I can’t remember﻿—his gloves or a strip of silk down the sides of his trousers﻿—something of the sort. The blond was saying something that I did not catch. I heard the words “de Mersch” and “*Anglaise*,” and saw the dark man turn his attention to the little group below. Then I caught my own name mispronounced and somewhat of a stumbling-block to a high-pitched contemptuous intonation. The little correspondent, who was on my other arm, started visibly and moved swiftly behind my back.
