## XIX / 第十九章

===Original===
I wanted to see her, to finish it one way or another, and, at my aunt’s house, I found her standing in an immense white room; waiting for me. There was a profusion of light. It left her absolutely shadowless, like a white statue in a gallery; inscrutable.

===Chinese===
我想见她，好歹把这事情作个了断；在老姑母的宅子里，我见到她站在一间阔大的白色房间里；正等着我。满室是丰沛的光。那光使她全然无影，像画廊里一尊白色的雕像；莫测高深。

===Original===
“I have come,” I said. I had it in my mind to say: “Because there is nothing for me to do on earth.” But I did not, I looked at her instead.

===Chinese===
“我来了，”我说。我心里本想说的是：“因为我在这个世上已无事可做。”但我没有说出口，只是望着她。

===Original===
“You have come,” she repeated. She had no expression in her voice, in her eyes. It was as if I were nothing to her; as if I were the picture of a man. Well, that was it; I was a picture, she a statue. “I did it,” I said at last.

===Chinese===
“你来了，”她重复道。她的嗓音里、眼睛里，都没有表情。仿佛我于她什么也不是；仿佛我是一幅男人的画像。可不是嘛，正是如此；我是一幅画，她是一尊雕像。“是我干的，”我终于说。

===Original===
“And you want?” she asked.

===Chinese===
“而你想要？”她问。

===Original===

It made an effect as if the Venus of Milo had turned its head toward me. She began to speak, as if the statue were speaking, as if a passing bell were speaking; recording a passing passionlessly.

===Chinese===
“你知道的，”我答道，“我想要我的……”我想不出那个词。它既可以指一份酬报，也可以指一笔正当的应得。她十分突然地望向我。那情形，就像米洛的维纳斯（Venus of Milo）朝我转过头来一般。她开始说话，仿佛是雕像在说话，仿佛是报丧钟在说话；不动声色地记录着一桩亡故。

===Original===
“You have done nothing at all,” she said. “Nothing.”

===Chinese===
“你根本什么也没有做成，”她说，“什么也没有。”

===Original===
“And yet,” I said, “I was at the heart of it all.”

===Chinese===
“可是，”我说，“我曾在这一切的中心啊。”

===Original===

I seemed suddenly to see an immense machine﻿—unconcerned, soulless, but all its parts made up of bodies of men: a great mill grinding out the dust of centuries; a great winepress. She was continuing her speech.

===Chinese===

===Original===
“As for you﻿—you are only a detail, like all the others; you were set in a place because you would act as you did. It was in your character. We inherit the earth and you, your day is over.﻿ ﻿… You remember that day, when I found you﻿—the first day?”

===Chinese===
“至于你——你只是一个零件，同所有别的人一样；你被安放在一个位置上，因为你势必会照你做的那样去做。那本来就在你的性子里。我们承受大地，而你们，你们的日子过完了。……你记得那一天吗，我找到你的那一天——最初的那天？”

===Original===
I remembered that day. It was on the downland, under the immense sky, amid the sound of larks. She had explained the nature of things. She had talked expressionlessly in pregnant words; she was talking now. I knew no more of her today, after all these days, after I had given up to her my past and my future.

===Chinese===
我记得那一天。在丘陵原野上，在浩浩长天之下，在云雀的颤鸣里。她讲解了事物的本性。她曾经面无表情地说着意味深长的话；此刻她又在说了。到了今天，经过这许多日子，经过我已把自己的过去和未来都交给了她，我对她的了解并不比那时更多一分。

===Original===
“You remember that day. I was looking for such a man, and I found you.”

===Chinese===
“你记得那一天。我当时要找的正是这样一个人，而我找到了你。”

===Original===
“And you﻿ ﻿…” I said, “you have done this thing! Think of it!﻿ ﻿… I have nobody﻿—nothing﻿—nowhere in the world. I cannot look a man in the face, not even Churchill. I can never go to him again.” I paused, expecting a sign of softening. None came. “I have parted with my past and you tell me there is no future.”

===Chinese===
“而你……”我说，“你做下了这样的事！想想吧！……我在这世上没有人——没有东西——没有去处。我没法正眼看任何一个人，连丘吉尔也不能。我再也不能上他那里去了。”我顿了顿，指望一点软化的表示。没有来。“我舍了我的过去，而你对我说，没有未来。”

===Original===
“None,” she echoed. Then, coldly, as a swan takes the water, she began to speak:

===Chinese===
“没有，”她应声道。随后，冷冷地，像天鹅入水一般，她开始说：

===Original===

===Original===
“And now I stand alone. Yesterday that fabric was everything to you; it seemed solid enough. And where is it today? What is it to you more than to me? There stood Virtue﻿ ﻿… and Probity﻿ ﻿… and all the things that all those people stood for. Well, today they are gone; the very belief in them is gone. Who will believe in them, now that it is proved that their tools were people﻿ ﻿… like de Mersch? And it was I that did it. That, too, is to be accounted to me for virtue.

===Chinese===
“而如今我独自站着。昨天，那个构造于你还是一切；它看上去足够坚实。今天它又在哪里？它于你，比于我更多出什么吗？那里立着德性……正派……还有所有那些人所代表的一切。好，今天它们去了；对它们的信念本身也去了。既然已经证明，它们的工具竟是人……像德·默施那样的人，谁还会相信它们？而做成这件事的是我。这也要记在我的德性账上。

===Original===
“Well, I have inherited the earth. I am the worm at the very heart of the rose of it. You are thinking that all that I have gained is the hand of Gurnard. But it is more than that. It is a matter of a chessboard; and Gurnard is the only piece that remains. And I am the hand that moves him. As for a marriage; well, it is a marriage of minds, a union for a common purpose. If for your sake I had swerved one jot from my allotted course, I should have been untrue. There was a danger, once, for a minute.﻿ ﻿… But I stood out against it. What would you have had me do? Go under as Fox went under? Speak like him, look as he looks now.﻿ ﻿… Me? Well, I did not.

===Chinese===
“好，我承受了大地。我就是那朵玫瑰花心最深处的蛀虫。你在想，我赢得的一切不过是格纳德的婚姻。可远不止如此。这是一盘棋的事；格纳德是盘上仅剩的一枚棋子。而我，是走这枚棋子的那只手。至于婚姻；那是一场心智的联姻，一个为共同目的结成的结合。可占主导的心智是我的。至于你。好吧，你舍了你的过去……而你没有未来。这是真的。你无处可去；什么也没剩下，这世上什么也没有了。这也是真的。可那于我算什么？一组事实罢了——你舍了你的过去，你没有未来。那件工作总得有人做；我总得叫你去做。我挑中你，因为你势必会做。如此而已。……我了解你；了解你的隐秘角落，你的弱点。那就是我的力量。我代表那必然之势，代表一路行进的未来；你，代表躺在路旁的过去。倘若为了你，我偏离了自己命定的路线一丝一毫，我便是不忠于自己了。曾经有过一次危险，一分钟之久。……可我顶住了。你要我怎么办？像福克斯那样沉下去？像他那样说话，长成他如今那副模样。……我吗？好吧，我没有。

===Original===
