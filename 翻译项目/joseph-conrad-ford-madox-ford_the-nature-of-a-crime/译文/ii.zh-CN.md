## II / 二

===Original===
Last night it was very late and I grew tired, so I broke off my letter. Perhaps I was really afraid of seeing that mouse again. Those minute superstitions are curious things. I noticed, when I looked at the enumeration of these pages tonight, I began to write upon the thirteenth sheet—and that gives me a vague dissatisfaction. I read, by the by, a paragraph in a newspaper: it dealt with half-mad authors. One of these, the writer said, was Zola; he was stated to be half mad because he added together the numbers on the backs of cabs passing him in the street. Personally, I do that again and again—and I know very well that I do it in order to dull my mind. It is a sort of narcotic. Johnson, we know, touched his street-posts in a certain order: that, too, was to escape from miserable thoughts. And we all know how, as children, we have obeyed mysterious promptings to step upon the lines between the paving-stones in the street.… But the children have their futures: it is well that they should propitiate the mysterious Omnipotent One. In their day, too, Johnson and Zola had their futures. It was well that Johnson should "touch" against the evil chance; that Zola should rest his mind against new problems. In me it is mere imbecility. For I have no future.

===Chinese===
昨夜实在很晚了，我疲倦起来，便中断了这封信。也许，我其实是怕再看见那只老鼠。那些细微的迷信，真是古怪的东西。我注意到，今夜我数了数这些稿纸的页码，发现自己开始写在第十三张上——这让我隐隐感到不快。顺便说一句，我在报纸上读到过一段文字：讲的是半疯的作家。其中一位，那作者说，是左拉；据说他之所以半疯，是因为他要把街头经过他身边的马车背上的号码一一加起来。就我个人而言，我也一遍又一遍地这样做——而且我十分清楚，我这样做是为了麻痹自己的心智。那是一种麻醉剂。我们知道，约翰逊以某种特定的顺序去触摸街边的路灯柱：那同样是为了逃避痛苦的念头。我们也都知道，小时候我们如何遵从那些神秘的冲动，专门去踩街上铺路石之间的接缝。……但孩子们还有他们的未来：他们去讨好那神秘的全能者，是应当的。在他们那个年代，约翰逊和左拉也各有各的未来。约翰逊去"触摸"以避邪祟，左拉以新的算题安顿心神，都是应当的。而在我就纯属愚蠢了。因为我没有未来。

===Original===
Do you find it difficult to believe that? You know the Burdens, of course. But I think you do not know that, for the last nine years, I have administered the Burden estates all by myself. The original trustees were old Lady Burden and I; but nine years ago Lady Burden gave me a power of attorney and since then I have acted alone. It was just before then that I had bought the houses in Gordon Square—the one I live in, the one you live in, and the seven others. Well, rightly speaking, those houses have been bought with Burden money, and all my pictures, all my prints, all my books, my furniture, my reputation as a connoisseur, my governorship of the two charities—all the me that people envy—have been bought with the Burden money. I assure you that at times I have found it a pleasurable excitement.… You see, I have wanted you sometimes so terribly—so terribly that the juggling with the Burden accounts has been as engrossing a narcotic as to Zola was the adding up of the numbers upon the backs of cabs. Mere ordinary work would never have held my thoughts.

===Chinese===
你觉得这难以置信吗？你当然认识伯登一家。但我想你并不知道，过去九年来，一直是我独自一人打理着伯登家的产业。最初的受托人是老伯登夫人和我；但九年前伯登夫人给了我一份委托书，从那以后便由我一人行事。正是在那之前不久，我买下了戈登广场的那些宅邸——我住的那一幢、你住的那一幢，以及另外七幢。说到底，那些宅邸是用伯登家的钱买的，而我所有的画、所有的版画、所有的藏书、我的家具、我作为鉴赏家的名声、我在那两家慈善机构的理事职位——所有人们所羡慕的那个"我"——都是用伯登家的钱买来的。我向你保证，有时我觉得这还是一种令人愉悦的刺激。……你看，我有时对你的渴望是那样可怕——可怕到摆弄伯登家的账目，对我而言便如左拉把马车背上的号码加起来一样，是一种令人全神贯注的麻醉剂。寻常的工作从来拢不住我的心思。

===Original===
Under old Burden's will young Edward Burden comes of age when he reaches the age of twenty-five or when he marries with my consent. Well, he will reach the age of twenty-five and he will marry on April 5. On that day the solicitors of his future wife will make their scrutiny of my accounts. It is regarded, you understand, as a mere formality. But it amuses me to think of the faces of Coke and Coke when they come to certain figures! It was an outlaw of some sort, was it not, who danced and sang beneath the gallows? I wonder, now, what sort of traitor, outlaw, or stealthy politician I should have made in the Middle Ages. It is certain that, save for this one particular of property, I should be in very truth illustrious. No doubt the state shall come at last in which there shall no more be any property. I was born before my time.

===Chinese===
按照老伯登的遗嘱，小爱德华·伯登年满二十五岁、或经我同意而婚配时，便算成年。好啦，他将满二十五岁，并且将于四月五日成婚。届时他未来妻子的律师们将审查我的账目。你知道，这被视为不过是一道例行手续。但想象一下科克律师事务所（Coke and Coke）那些人看到某些数字时的表情，倒令我颇觉有趣！在绞刑架下手舞足蹈、引吭高歌的，好像是个什么亡命之徒吧？我不禁好奇，倘若生在中世纪，我会成为怎样的叛逆、亡命之徒或是暗中行事的政客。可以肯定的是，若非在财产这一桩上，我本会真正功成名就。毫无疑问，那个不再有财产可言的时代终将到来。我生不逢时。

===Original===
For it is certain that I am illustrious save in that one respect. Today young Edward Burden came here to the office to introduce me to his fiancée. You observe that I have robbed her. The Burden property is really crippled. They came, this bright young couple, to get a cheque from me with which to purchase a motorcar. They are to try several cars in the next three weeks. On the day before the wedding they are to choose one that will suit them best—and on the wedding-day in the evening they are to start for Italy. They will be coming towards you.… Then no doubt, too, a telegram will reach them, to say that in all probability motorcars will be things not for them for several years to come. What a crumbling of their lives!

===Chinese===
因为可以肯定的是，除了那一桩之外，我确实是功成名就的。今天小爱德华·伯登来这间办公室，把他未婚妻介绍给我。你看到了，我掠夺了她。伯登家的产业其实已被掏空。这一对容光焕发的年轻人来，是为了向我领一张支票去买一辆汽车。未来三周里他们要试好几辆车。婚礼前一天他们将挑出一辆最称心的——婚礼当天傍晚便动身去意大利。他们将是朝你那个方向去的。……届时无疑也会有一封电报追上他们，说在今后好些年里，汽车这东西恐怕都与他们无缘了。他们的人生将怎样地崩塌啊！

===Original===
It was odd how I felt towards *her*. You know his pompous, high forehead, the shine all over him, the grave, weighty manner. He held his hat—a wonderful shiny, "good" hat—before his mouth, for all the world as if he had been in church. He made, even, a speech in introducing Miss Averies to me. You see, in a sense, he was in a temple. My office enshrined a deity, a divinity: the law, property, the rights of man as maintained by an august constitution. I am for him such a wonderfully "safe" man. My dear one, you cannot imagine how I feel towards him: a little like a deity, a little like an avenging Providence. I imagine that the real Deity must feel towards some of His worshippers much as I feel towards this phoenix of the divines.

===Chinese===
我对*她*的感觉，很是奇特。你知道他那副神气活现的高额头，他通体的光泽，那庄重沉甸的派头。他把帽子——一顶亮闪闪的、很"体面"的帽子——举在嘴前，活脱脱像在教堂里一般。他居然还致了一番辞，把艾弗里丝小姐介绍给我。你看，在某种意义上，他是置身于一座神庙之中。我的办公室供奉着一位神明、一种神性：法律、财产、由一部庄严的宪法所维护的人之权利。在他眼中我是那样一个不可思议地"可靠"的人。我亲爱的，你无法想象我对他的感觉：有一点像一位神明，有一点像一位降罚的天意。我想，真正的神明对祂的某些信众的感觉，大概一如我对这位圣徒模范的感觉。

===Original===
The Deity is after all the supreme Artist—and the supreme quality of Art is surprise.

Imagine then the feeling of the Deity towards some of those who most confidently enter His temple. Just imagine His attitude towards those who deal in the obvious platitudes that "honesty is the best policy," or "genius the capacity for taking pains." So for days the world appears to them. Then suddenly: honesty no longer pays; the creature, amassing with his infinite pains, data for his Great Work, is discovered to have produced a work of an Infinite Dullness. That is the all-suffering Deity manifesting Himself to His worshippers. For assuredly a day comes when two added to two no longer results in four. That day will come on April 5 for Edward Burden.

===Chinese===
神明终究是至高的艺术家——而艺术至高的品质，便是出人意料。

那么，请想象神明对某些满怀自信地踏入祂殿堂之人的感觉。请想象祂对那些满口"诚实是上策"、"天才是下苦功的能力"之类陈词滥调之人的态度。一连许多天，世界在他们眼中便是如此。然后忽然之间：诚实不再划算；那个以无尽的辛劳为他的"伟大作品"搜集资料的家伙，被发现产出的竟是一部"无尽的沉闷"之作。那便是那位忍受一切的神明向祂的信众显圣。因为二加二不再等于四的那一天，终究会来的。对爱德华·伯登而言，那一天将在四月五日到来。

===Original===
After all he has done nothing to make two and two become four. He has not even checked his accounts: well: for some years now I have been doing as much as that. But with his fiancée it is different. She is a fair, slight girl with eyes that dilate under all sorts of emotion. In my office she appears not a confident worshipper but a rather frightened fawn led before an Anthropomorphic Deity. And, strangely enough, though young Burden who trusts me inspires me with a sardonic dislike, I felt myself saying to this poor little thing that faced me: "Why: I have wronged you!" And I regretted it.

===Chinese===
毕竟，二加二之所以等于四，并不是他促成的。他连自己的账目都未曾核对过：这些年来，倒是我在替他做这件事。但他的未婚妻则不同。她是个白皙纤弱的姑娘，一双眼睛在任何情绪之下都会睁得大大的。在我这间办公室里，她不像一个自信的信徒，倒像一头惊惶的小鹿，被牵到了一位人形神明跟前。而奇怪的是，尽管信任我的小伯登令我生出一种讥讽的厌恶，我却发觉自己对面前这个可怜的小东西说出了口："哎呀，我对不住你！"而我随即后悔了。

===Original===
She, you see, has after all given something towards a right to enjoy the Burden estates and the Burden wealth; she has given her fragile beauty, her amiability, her worship, no doubt, of the intolerable Edward. And all this payment in the proper coin: so she has in a sense a right.…

Good night, dear one, I think you have it in your power—you *might* have it in your power—to atone to this little creature. Tomorrow I will tell you why and how.

===Chinese===
你看，她毕竟付出了某些东西，换取享用伯登家产业与财富的资格；她付出了她那易碎的美貌、她的温顺、她对那个叫人无法忍受的爱德华的——无疑是——崇拜。而这一切都是以恰当的筹码支付的：所以在某种意义上，她是有权利的……

晚安，亲爱的，我想你是有这个能力的——你*或许*有这个能力——来补偿这个小生灵。明日我再告诉你为何、又如何。
