## III / 三

===Original===
I wrote last night that you have something in your power. If you wished it you could make me live on. I am confident that you will not wish it: for you will understand that capriciously or intolerably I am tired of living this life. I desire you so terribly that now, even the excitement of fooling Burden no longer hypnotizes me into an acceptance of life without you. Frankly, I am tired out. If I had to go on living any longer I should have to ask you to be mine in one form or other. With that and with my ability—for of course I have great ability—I could go on fooling Burden forever. I could restore: I could make sounder than ever it was that preposterous "going concern" the Burden Estate. Unless I like to let them, I think that the wife's solicitors will not discover what I have done. For, frankly, I have put myself out in this matter in order to be amusing to myself and ingenious. I have forged whole builder's estimates for repairs that were never executed: I have invented whole hosts of defaulting tenants. It has not been latterly for money that I have done this: it has been simply for the sheer amusement of looking at Edward Burden and saying to myself:

"Ah: you trust me, my sleek friend. Well.…"

===Chinese===
昨夜我写道，有一件事在你权限之内。只要你愿意，你能让我继续活下去。我确信你不会愿意：因为你会明白，无论是出于任性还是出于再也无法忍受，我已厌倦了过这种日子。我对你的渴望可怕到这般地步，以至于如今，即便是愚弄伯登所带来的刺激，再也无法催眠我、让我甘心接受一个没有你的生活。坦白说，我已精疲力竭。倘若我还得继续活下去，我就不得不要求你以这种或那种方式成为我的人。有了那个，再加上我的能力——因为我当然颇有才能——我便可以永无止境地继续愚弄伯登。我可以把亏空补回去：我可以把伯登产业那桩荒唐的"经营中产业"做得比从前任何时候都更牢靠。只要我不愿让他们发现，我想他妻子的律师们是查不出我做了什么的。因为坦白讲，在这件事上我是费了一番心思，好让自己觉得有趣、显得精巧的。我伪造过整份整份的营造商估算单——为那些从未动工的修缮；我凭空捏造过一大批拖欠租金的租户。近来我做这些已不是为了钱：纯粹是为了看着爱德华·伯登时那种自得其乐：

"啊：你信任我，我油光水滑的朋友。好嘛……"

===Original===
But indeed I fancy that I am rich enough to be able to restore to them all that I have taken. And, looking at Edward Burden's little fiancée, I was almost tempted to set upon that weary course of juggling. But I am at the end of my tether. I cannot live without you longer. And I do not wish to ask you. Later I will tell you. Or No—I will tell you now.

===Chinese===
但说真的，我想我已足够富有，足以把他们的一切都归还。而看着爱德华·伯登那位小小的未婚妻，我几乎动了念头，想重新踏上那条令人厌倦的弄虚作假之路。但我已到了山穷水尽的地步。我没有你，便活不下去，再多一刻也不行。而我不愿向你开口。我本想晚些再告诉你。或者，不——我现在就告诉你。

===Original===
You see, my dear thing, it is a question of going one better. It would be easy enough to deceive your husband: it would be easier still to go away together. I think that neither you nor I have ever had any conscientious scruples. But, analysing the matter down to its very depths, I think we arrive at this, that without the motives for self-restraint that other people have we are anxious to show more self-restraint than they. We are doing certain work not for payment but for sheer love of work. Do I make myself clear? For myself I have a great pride in your image. I can say to myself: "Here is a woman, my complement. She has no respect for the law. She does not value what a respect for the law would bring her. Yet she remains purer than the purest of the makers of law." And I think it is the converse of that feeling that you have for me.

===Chinese===
你看，我亲爱的，这是一桩"更胜一筹"的事。欺骗你丈夫很容易：私奔就更易如反掌。我想你我二人从来不曾有过什么良心上的顾忌。但是，把这事一直剖析到底，我想我们得出的结论是：我们没有旁人那种自我克制的动机，却急于表现出比他们更强的克制。我们做某些事不是为了报酬，而是纯粹出于对做事本身的热爱。我说清楚了吗？就我而言，我对你的形象怀有一种极大的骄傲。我可以对自己说："这里有一位女子，是我的另一半。她不敬重法律。她不在乎敬重法律会给她带来什么。然而她仍比那些最纯洁的立法者更为纯洁。"而我想，你对我的感情，正是这种感情的对应。

===Original===
If you desire me to live on, I will live on: I am so swayed by you that if you desire me to break away from this ideal of you, the breath of a command will send me round to your side.

I am ready to give my life for this Ideal: nay more, I am ready to sacrifice you to it, since I know that life for you will remain a very bitter thing. I know, a little, what renunciation means.

And I am asking you to bear it—for the sake of my ideal of you. For, assuredly, unless I can have you I must die—and I know that you will not ask me to have you. And I love you: and bless you for it.

===Chinese===
你若要我活下去，我便活下去：我被你所左右，到了这般地步——你若要我打破我心中你这个理想，你一声命令的气息便足以把我唤到你身边。

我已准备好为这个理想献出生命：不仅如此，我已准备好为此牺牲你，因为我知道，对你而言，活下去仍将是一件十分痛苦的事。我多少知道一点，放弃意味着什么。

而我正要求你承受它——为了我心中你的那个理想。因为可以肯定，除非能得到你，我必死——而我知道你不会要求我得到你。而我爱你：并为此祝福你。
