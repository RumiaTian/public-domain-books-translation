## IX In Which He Has a Near Shave / 第九章　他险些送命
### I / 一
===Original===
“Captain, you have me guessing.” The American bit the end off another cigar, and leaned back in his chair. “You say that swell Frenchman with the waiters hovering about like fleas round a dog’s tail is the reason you came to Paris. Is he kind of friendly with Hiram C. Potts?”

===Chinese===
「上尉，您可真把我考住了。」那美国人咬掉又一支雪茄的烟头，朝椅背上一靠。「您是说，那位气派的法国人——侍者围着他团团转，活像狗尾巴上的一群跳蚤——就是您来巴黎的缘由。他跟海勒姆·C·波茨是不是有点儿交情？

===Original===
Drummond laughed.

===Chinese===
德拉蒙德哈哈一笑。

===Original===
“The first time I met Mr. Potts,” he remarked, “that swell Frenchman was just preparing to put a thumbscrew on his second thumb.”

===Chinese===
「我头一回见到波茨先生的时候，」他说道，「那位气派的法国人正张罗着要往他第二根拇指上安夹拇指的螺丝夹。」

===Original===
“Second?” The detective looked up quickly.

===Chinese===
「第二根？」侦探霍地抬起头来。

===Original===
“The first had been treated earlier in the evening,” answered Drummond quietly. “It was then that I removed your millionaire pal.”

===Chinese===
「头一根当晚早些时候已经伺候过了，」德拉蒙德不动声色地答道，「我就是那会儿把您那位百万富翁老兄弄走的。」

===Original===
The other lit his cigar deliberately.

===Chinese===
对方不紧不慢地点上了雪茄。

===Original===
“Say, Captain,” he murmured, “you ain’t pulling my leg by any chance, are you?”

===Chinese===
「我说，上尉，」他低声道，「您该不是拿我寻开心吧？」

===Original===
“I am not,” said Drummond shortly. “I was told, before I met him, that the gentleman over there was one of the boys.﻿ ﻿… He is, most distinctly. In fact, though up to date such matters have not been much in my line, I should put him down as a sort of super-criminal. I wonder what name he is passing under here?”

===Chinese===
「没有，」德拉蒙德答得干脆。「见着他之前，就有人告诉我，那边那位先生是他们一伙的。……他分明就是，千真万确。老实讲，这类勾当虽说到如今都不大归我行内，我也要给他记上一笔：此人是某种超级罪犯。不知道他在这儿顶着个什么名字？」

===Original===
The American ceased pulling at his cigar.

===Chinese===
美国人不再抽他的雪茄。

===Original===
“Do they vary?”

===Chinese===
「名字还会变？」

===Original===
“In England he is clean-shaven, possesses a daughter, and answers to Carl Peterson. As he is at present I should never have known him, but for that little trick of his.”

===Chinese===
「在英格兰，他脸刮得干干净净，身边有个女儿，名字叫卡尔·彼得森。若不是他那个小习惯，光凭他眼下这副模样，我是断断认不出来的。」

===Original===
“Possesses a daughter!” For the first time the detective displayed traces of excitement. “Holy Smoke! It can’t be him!”

===Chinese===
「身边有个女儿！」侦探头一回显出几分激动。「我的老天！该不会就是他吧！」

===Original===
“Who?” demanded Drummond.

===Chinese===
「谁？」德拉蒙德追问。

===Original===
But the other did not answer. Out of the corner of his eye he was watching three men who had just joined the subject of their talk, and on his face was a dawning amazement. He waited till the whole party had gone into the restaurant, then, throwing aside his caution, he turned excitedly on Drummond.

===Chinese===
对方却没有回答。他斜着眼角，打量着刚走到他们话题主角身旁的三个男人，脸上渐渐透出惊讶。他等那一行人统统进了餐厅，才抛开顾忌，激动地转向德拉蒙德。

===Original===
“Are you certain,” he cried, “that that’s the man who has been monkeying with Potts?”

===Chinese===
「您敢肯定，」他嚷道，「跟波茨捣鬼的就是那个人？」

===Original===
“Absolutely,” said Hugh. “He recognised me; whether he thinks I recognised him or not, I don’t know.”

===Chinese===
「十足肯定，」休说，「他认出我了；至于他以为我认没认出他，我可不知道。」

===Original===
“Then what,” remarked the detective, “is he doing here dining with Hocking, our cotton trust man; with Steinemann, the German coal man; and with that other guy whose face is familiar, but whose name I can’t place? Two of ’em at any rate, Captain, have got more millions than we’re ever likely to have thousands.”

===Chinese===
「那么，」侦探说道，「他在这儿跟霍金——咱们棉花托拉斯的那位——跟德国煤炭商斯坦内曼，还有那个脸熟名字却叫不出的家伙，一道吃饭算是怎么回事？至少他们里头有两位，上尉，人家手里的百万，比咱们这辈子有指望到手的几千块还多。」

===Original===
Hugh stared at the American.

===Chinese===
休直勾勾地盯着那美国人。

===Original===
“Last night,” he said slowly, “he was foregathering with a crowd of the most atrocious ragged-trousered revolutionaries it’s ever been my luck to run up against.”

===Chinese===
「昨天夜里，」他慢悠悠地说，「他正跟一群顶顶不像话的穿破裤子的革命家厮混——我这辈子撞上的各路货色里，数他们最恶劣。」

===Original===
“We’re in it, Captain, right in the middle of it,” cried the detective, slapping his leg. “I’ll eat my hat if that Frenchman isn’t Franklyn﻿—or Libstein﻿—or Baron Darott﻿—or any other of the blamed names he calls himself. He’s the biggest proposition we’ve ever been up against on this little old earth, and he’s done us every time. He never commits himself, and if he does, he always covers his tracks. He’s a genius; he’s the goods. Gee!” he whistled gently under his breath. “If we could only lay him by the heels.”

===Chinese===
「咱们撞上大事了，上尉，正撞在正当中，」侦探一拍大腿，嚷道。「那法国人要不是富兰克林——就是利布斯坦——再不就是达罗特男爵——反正是他自报的那一长串混账名字里的随便哪一个，我把帽子吃给您看。他是咱们在这颗小小地球上碰见过的头一号对手，回回都叫他把咱们耍了。他从不授人以柄，万一授了，也必定把踪迹抹得干干净净。他是天才；他是真家伙。嚯！」他压着嗓子轻轻打了个呼哨。「要是能把他就地锁拿，该有多好。」

===Original===
For a while he stared in front of him, lost in his dream of pleasant anticipation; then, with a short laugh, he pulled himself together.

===Chinese===
他直勾勾望着前面出了一会儿神，沉浸在那份美滋滋的憧憬里；随即短短一笑，振作起来。

===Original===
“Quite a few people have thought the same, Captain,” he remarked, “and there he is﻿—still drinking highballs. You say he was with a crowd of revolutionaries last night. What do you mean exactly?”

===Chinese===
「这么想的人可不止一两个，上尉，」他说，「可他不是好端端在那儿——照样喝他的高杯酒。您说他昨儿晚上跟一群革命家混在一起，究竟是怎么个意思？」

===Original===
“Bolshevists, Anarchists, members of the Do-no-work-and-have-all-the-money Brigade,” answered Hugh. “But excuse me a moment. Waiter.”

===Chinese===
「布尔什维克，无政府主义者，还有『不干活儿、全捞钱』旅的弟兄们，」休答道，「失陪一下。侍者。」

===Original===
A man who had been hovering round came up promptly.

===Chinese===
一个一直在近旁打转的人应声快步上前。

===Original===
“Four of ’em, Ted,” said Hugh in a rapid undertone. “Frenchman with a beard, a Yank, and two Boches. Do your best.”

===Chinese===
「四位，特德，」休急促地低声说，「留胡子的法国人，一个美国佬，两个德国佬。看你的了。」

===Original===
“Right-o, old bean!” returned the waiter, “but don’t hope for too much.”

===Chinese===
「好嘞，老伙计！」那侍者应道，「不过可别指望太高哟。」

===Original===
He disappeared unobtrusively into the restaurant, and Hugh turned with a laugh to the American, who was staring at him in amazement.

===Chinese===
他不显山不露水地进了餐厅。休笑着转向那美国人，对方正目瞪口呆地望着他。

===Original===
“Who the devil is that guy?” asked the detective at length.

===Chinese===
「那家伙究竟是哪路神仙？」侦探终于开口问。

===Original===
“Ted Jerningham﻿—son of Sir Patrick Jerningham, Bart., and Lady Jerningham, of Jerningham Hall, Rutland, England,” answered Hugh, still grinning. “We may be crude in our methods, Mr. Green, but you must admit we do our best. Incidentally, if you want to know, your friend Mr. Potts is at present tucked between the sheets at that very house. He went there by aeroplane this morning.” He waved a hand towards Jerry. “He was the pilot.”

===Chinese===
「特德·杰宁汉——英国拉特兰郡杰宁汉庄园的帕特里克·杰宁汉爵士从男爵偕杰宁汉太太的公子，」休答道，脸上仍堆着笑，「我们的手段兴许土气，格林先生，可您得承认我们尽力了。顺带奉告一句，您要是想知道的话：您那位朋友波茨先生，这会儿正钻在那座宅子的被窝里。他是今天早上坐飞机去的。」他朝杰里一摆手。「飞行员就是他。」

===Original===
“Travelled like a bird, and sucked up a plate of meat-juice at the end,” announced that worthy, removing his eyes with difficulty from a recently arrived fairy opposite. “Who says that’s nothing, Hugh: the filly across the road there, with that bangle affair round her knee?”

===Chinese===
「一路稳当得像只鸟儿，落地还舔了一盘子肉汁，」那位活宝宣布道，一双眼睛好不容易才从对面新落座的小仙女身上挪开。「谁说这不算稀奇，休：瞧路对过那匹小母马，膝盖上还箍着个镯子似的玩意儿呢？」

===Original===

===Chinese===
「我得替他赔个不是，格林先生，」休说道，「他离开学堂还没几天，不知深浅。」

===Original===
But the American was shaking his head a little dazedly.

===Chinese===
那美国人却微微发着懵直摇头。

===Original===
“Crude!” he murmured, “crude! If you and your pals, Captain, are ever out of a job, the New York police is yours for the asking.” He smoked for a few moments in silence, and then, with a quick hunch of his shoulders, he turned to Drummond. “I guess there’ll be time to throw bouquets after,” he remarked. “We’ve got to get busy on what your friend Peterson’s little worry is; we’ve then got to stop it﻿—some old how. Now, does nothing sort of strike you?” He looked keenly at the soldier.

===Chinese===
「土气！」他喃喃道，「土气！上尉，哪天您和您那帮哥们儿要是失了业，纽约警察局的差事，您开口就是。」他默默抽了几口烟，随后肩膀猛一耸，转向德拉蒙德。「我看道贺的话往后有的是工夫说，」他说，「咱们得赶紧忙活起来，弄清您那位朋友彼得森操的是哪门子小心事，然后想法子把它搅了——不管使什么法子。怎么样，您就一点儿没咂摸出味儿来？」他锐利地望着这位军人。

===Original===
“Revolutionaries, Bolshevists, paid agitators last night: international financiers this evening. Why, the broad outline of the plan is as plain as the nose on your face; and it’s just the sort of game that man would love.﻿ ﻿…” The detective stared thoughtfully at the end of his cigar, and a look of comprehension began to dawn on Hugh’s face.

===Chinese===
「昨天夜里是革命家、布尔什维克、拿钱的鼓动家；今天晚上是国际金融家。嗐，这盘棋的大轮廓明摆着，就像您脸上那只鼻子一样；而这恰恰是那人最爱玩的一手把戏。……」侦探若有所思地盯着雪茄烟头，休的脸上也渐渐透出领悟的神情。

===Original===

===Chinese===
「好家伙！格林先生，」他说，「我有点儿明白您的意思了。先前叫我犯难的是：彼得森和拉金顿这样两个人，怎么会跟昨晚那路人搅在一块儿。」

===Original===
“Lakington! Who’s Lakington?” asked the other quickly.

===Chinese===
「拉金顿！拉金顿是谁？」对方急急问道。

===Original===
“Number Two in the combine,” answered Hugh, “and a nasty man.”

===Chinese===
「他们那个团伙里的二号人物，」休答道，「一个狠毒的角色。」

===Original===

===Chinese===
「那么波茨究竟插在哪一杠子上？」休说，美国人这一席话他一字不落地听着。「还有兰普郡公爵夫人的珍珠呢？」

===Chinese===
「珍珠！」美国人刚起了个头，餐厅的门突然开了，特德·杰宁汉钻了出来。他像是急着赶路，休在椅子上半直起了身。随即他又坐了回去——只见一群怒气冲冲的领班侍者和其他大人物，以不可思议的速度不知打哪儿冒了出来，把杰宁汉围了个水泄不通。

### II / 二
===Original===
But on this particular evening the detective proved wrong. They reached Maxim’s without mishap, they enjoyed an excellent dinner, during which the American showed himself to be a born conversationalist, as well as a shrewd man of the world. And over the coffee and liqueurs Hugh gave him a brief outline of what had taken place since he first got mixed up in the affair. The American listened in silence, though amazement shone on his face as the story proceeded. The episode of the disappearing body especially seemed to tickle his fancy, but even over that he made no remark.
