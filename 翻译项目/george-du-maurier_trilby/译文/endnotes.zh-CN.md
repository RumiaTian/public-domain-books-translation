## Endnotes / 尾注
===Original===

1. Glossary.﻿—*Pioupiou* (alias *pousse-caillou*, alias *tourlourou*)﻿—a private soldier of the line. *Zouzou*﻿—a Zouave. *Nounou*﻿—a wet-nurse with a pretty ribboned cap and long streamers. *Toutou*﻿—a nondescript French lapdog, of no breed known to Englishmen (a regular little beast!) *Loulou*﻿—a Pomeranian dog﻿—not much better. ↩︎

===Chinese===

1. 词汇表。——*Pioupiou*（亦称 *pousse-caillou*，亦称 *tourlourou*）——步兵线列步兵大兵。*Zouzou*——祖阿夫轻步兵。*Nounou*——戴着饰有漂亮丝带和长飘带便帽的奶妈。*Toutou*——一种难以名状的法国哈巴狗，英国人从未见识过的杂种犬（纯粹是一只招人嫌的小畜生！）。*Loulou*——博美犬——也没好到哪儿去。↩︎
