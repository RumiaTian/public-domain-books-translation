Glossary.﻿—*Pioupiou* (alias *pousse-caillou*, alias *tourlourou*)﻿—a private soldier of the line. *Zouzou*﻿—a Zouave. *Nounou*﻿—a wet-nurse with a pretty ribboned cap and long streamers. *Toutou*﻿—a nondescript French lapdog, of no breed known to Englishmen (a regular little beast!) *Loulou*﻿—a Pomeranian dog﻿—not much better. ↩︎
