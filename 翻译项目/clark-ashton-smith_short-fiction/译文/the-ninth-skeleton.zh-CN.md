## The Ninth Skeleton / 第九具骷髅

===Original===
It was beneath the immaculate blue of a morning in April that I set out to keep my appointment with Guenevere. We had agreed to meet on Boulder Ridge, at a spot well known to both of us, a small and circular field surrounded with pines and full of large stones, midway between her parents’ home at Newcastle and my cabin on the northeastern extremity of the Ridge, near Auburn.

===Chinese===
我启程去赴桂妮薇儿（Guenevere）之约，其时正逢四月清晨，穹苍澄碧、纤尘不染。我们相约在巨石岭（Boulder Ridge）碰头——那是一处我俩都熟稔的地方：一方为松林所环抱、满布巨石的小小圆形旷野，恰好坐落于她双亲定居的纽卡斯尔（Newcastle），与我那间在岭之东北端、邻近奥本（Auburn）的木屋之间。

===Original===
Guenevere is my fiancée. It must be explained that at the time of which I write, there was a certain amount of opposition on the part of her parents to the engagement﻿—an opposition since happily withdrawn. In fact, they had gone so far as to forbid me to call, and Guenevere and I could see each other only by stealth, and infrequently,

===Chinese===
桂妮薇儿是我的未婚妻。这里须得说明一笔：在我笔下所叙的那个时辰，她双亲对我们的婚约多少有些反对——不过这层阻力，后来总算欣然撤去了。事实上，他们当时竟至于不准我登门；我与桂妮薇儿只能偷偷相会，且聚少离多。

===Original===
The Ridge is a long and rambling moraine, heavily strewn in places with boulders, as its name implies, and with many outcroppings of black volcanic stone. Fruit-ranches cling to some of its slopes, but scarcely any of the top is under cultivation, and much of the soil, indeed, is too thin and stony to be arable. With its twisted pines, often as fantastic in form as the cypresses of the California coast, and its gnarled and stunted oaks, the landscape has a wild and quaint beauty, with more than a hint of the Japanesque in places.

===Chinese===
这道山岭是一道漫长而逶迤的冰碛（moraine），顾名思义，处处散布着漂砾，并多有黑色火山岩露出地表。果园攀附于它几处山坡之上，但岭顶几乎全无开垦；事实上，许多地方的土层既薄且石砾丛生，根本无可耕作。此处松树盘曲虬结，姿态往往奇诡如加利福尼亚海岸的丝柏，橡树亦多疙瘩节疤、矮小蜷缩，整个景致透着一种荒蛮而古拙的美，有些地方更隐隐流露出几分日本画的意趣。

===Original===
It is perhaps two miles from my cabin to the place where I was to meet Guenevere. Since I was born in the very shadow of Boulder Ridge, and have lived upon or near it for most of my thirty-odd years, I am familiar with every rod of its lovely and rugged extent, and, previous to that April morning, would scarcely have refrained from laughing if anyone had told me I could possibly lose my way﻿… Since then﻿—well, I assure you, I should not feel inclined to laugh﻿…

===Chinese===
从我的木屋到与桂妮薇儿相约之处，约莫两英里光景。我既生于巨石岭的浓阴之下，三十余载的岁月里又大半栖息于岭上或岭畔，故而这可爱而崎岖的山岭每一寸土地，我都了如指掌；在那个四月的清晨之前，倘若有人对我说我竟会在岭上迷路，我恐怕难免要哑然失笑……可自那以后——唉，我敢向你保证，我再笑不出来了……

===Original===
Truly, it was a morning made for the trysts of lovers. Wild bees were humming busily in the patches of clover and in the ceanothus bushes with their great masses of white flowers, whose strange and heavy perfume intoxicated the air. Most of the spring blossoms were abroad: cyclamen, yellow violet, poppy, wild hyacinth, and woodland star; and the green of the fields was opalescent with their colouring. Between the emerald of the buckeyes, the grey-green of the pines, the golden and dark and bluish greens of the oaks, I caught glimpses of the snow-white Sierras to the east, and the faint blue of the Coast Range to the west, beyond the pale and lilac levels of the Sacramento valley. Following a vague trail, I went onwards across open fields where I had to thread my way among clustering boulders.

===Chinese===
诚然，那是一个天生为恋人幽会而设的清晨。野蜂在三叶草丛间、在缀满团团白花的美洲茶（ceanothus）丛中忙碌地嗡嗡低吟，那些白花所散发的奇异而馥郁的香气，使整片空气都醺醺欲醉。春日的大部分花朵都已纷纷露面：仙客来、黄色的堇菜、罂粟、野风信子，以及林星花；原野的青绿因它们的色彩而泛出蛋白石般的柔光。在七叶树的翡翠、松树的灰绿、橡树那金黄、幽暗与泛蓝的种种绿意之间，我偶尔窥见东面雪白的内华达山脉（Sierras），以及西面、在萨克拉门托（Sacramento）谷地那苍白而带丁香紫色的平畴之外，海岸山脉（Coast Range）那一抹淡蓝。我循着一条隐约的小径，继续向前穿过开阔的旷野，须在成簇的漂砾之间穿行而过。

===Original===
My thoughts were all of Guenevere, and I looked only with a casual and desultory eye at the picturesqueness and vernal beauty that environed my path. I was halfway between my cabin and the meeting-place, when I became suddenly aware that the sunlight had darkened, and glanced up, thinking, of course, that an April cloud, appearing unobserved from beyond the horizon, had passed across the sun. Imagine, then, my surprise when I saw that the azure of the entire sky had turned to a dun and sinister brown, in the midst of which the sun was clearly visible, burning like an enormous round red ember. Then, something strange and unfamiliar in the nature of my surroundings, which I was momentarily at a loss to define, forced itself upon my attention, and my surprise became a growing consternation. I stopped and looked about me, and realized, incredible as it seemed, that I had lost my way; for the pines on either hand were not those that I had expected to see. They were more gigantic, more gnarled, than the ones I remembered; and their roots writhed in wilder and more serpentine contortions from a soil that was strangely flowerless, and where even the grass grew only in scanty tufts. There were boulders large as druidic monoliths, and the forms of some of them were such as one might see in a nightmare. Thinking, of course, that it must all be a dream, but with a sense of utter bewilderment which seldom if ever attends the absurdities and monstrosities of nightmare, I sought in vain to orient myself and to find some familiar landmark in the bizarre scene that lay before me.

===Chinese===
我满心所想的全是桂妮薇儿，对沿途那如画的风光与春日的明媚，不过走马观花地随意一瞥。走到木屋与会面之处一半路程时，我忽然察觉阳光暗淡了下来，便抬头望去，自然以为是天边不知何时飘来一片四月的云，遮住了日头。那么，请想象我当时是何等惊愕——只见整片穹苍的蔚蓝已化作一种阴沉而不祥的褐色，日头仍清晰可见，却燃烧得如同一枚巨大浑圆的赤红余烬。紧接着，周遭景物之中有某种陌生而诡异之处，我一时竟说不出所以然来，却硬生生撞进我的意识，于是惊愕逐渐化作日益加深的惶恐。我停下脚步，四下张望，终于意识到——尽管这听起来匪夷所思——我竟迷失了方向；因为两旁的松树，已不是我料想中应见的那几株。它们比我记忆中的更为高大、更为盘虬；它们的根须从一片奇异地了无花开的泥土里，以更狂乱、更如蛇形盘曲的姿态扭绞而出，那地上甚至连青草也只是稀稀落落地长成几簇。还有些漂砾大如德鲁伊的独块巨石，其中几尊的形状，简直只有在梦魇之中才能见到。我当然以为这必然只是一场梦，可那种彻底的茫然之感，却绝非梦魇中种种荒诞怪异之物所能带来的（纵然有之，也极为罕见）——我徒然地试图辨清方位，试图在这片展现在我眼前的诡异景象中，寻出某个熟悉的标识。

===Original===
A path, broader than the one I had been following, but running in what I judged to be the same direction, wound on among the trees. It was covered with a grey dust, which, as I went forward, became deeper and displayed footprints of a singular form﻿—footprints that were surely too attenuate, too fantastically slender, to be human, despite their five toe-marks. Something about them, I know not what, something in the nature of their very thinness and elongation, made me shiver. Afterwards, I wondered why I had not recognized them for what they were; but at the time, no suspicion entered my mind﻿—only a vague sense of disquietude, an indefinable trepidation.

===Chinese===
一条小径，比我先前所循的那条更宽，却依我判断朝着同一个方向，在林间蜿蜒而去。它覆着一层灰色的尘土，我愈向前行，那尘土愈深，并显出一串形貌奇特的脚印——这些脚印未免太过细窄、太过纤长得荒诞离奇了，纵然各有五个足趾的印记，也断不可能是人所留下的。它们身上有某种东西，我说不出究竟是什么——那种极度的单薄与修长——令我不寒而栗。事后回想，我不明白自己当时为何竟没认出它们究竟是什么；可在当时，我心里并无半分猜疑，只有一缕隐约的不安，一种难以名状的战栗。

===Original===
As I proceeded, the pines amid which I passed became momentarily more fantastic and more sinister in the contortions of their boughs and boles and roots. Some were like leering hags; others were obscenely crouching gargoyles; some appeared to writhe in an eternity of hellish torture; others were convulsed as with a satanic merriment. All the while, the sky continued to darken slowly, the dun and dismal brown that I had first perceived turning through almost imperceptible changes of tone to a dead funereal purple, wherein the sun smouldered like a moon that had risen from a bath of blood. The trees and the whole landscape were saturated with this macabre purple, were immersed and steeped in its unnatural gloom. Only the rocks, as I went on, grew strangely paler; and their forms were somehow suggestive of headstones, of tombs and monuments. Beside the trail, there was no longer the green of vernal grass﻿—only an earth mottled by drying algae and tiny lichens the colour of verdigris. Also there were patches of evil-looking fungi with stems of a leprous pallor and blackish heads that drooped and nodded loathsomely.

===Chinese===
我一路前行，身旁经过的松树，其枝、其干、其根的扭曲，愈来愈显得奇诡、显得凶险。有的状如挤眉弄眼的老巫婆；有的则是形容猥亵、蹲伏蜷缩的兽形石像；有的仿佛在地狱般的酷刑之中永世扭动挣扎；有的则浑身抽搐，似被一种撒旦式的狂欢所攫住。与此同时，天色持续缓缓地暗下去，我最初所见到的那种阴沉惨淡的褐色，经过几近难以察觉的色调推移，转成一种死寂的、丧礼般的紫色；日头在这片紫色里闷燃，如同一轮自血池之中升起的月亮。树木与整片景致都被这阴森可怖的紫所浸透，都被它那悖逆常理的幽暗所浸泡、所浸渍。唯有岩石，随着我走下去，竟变得异样地苍白；而它们的形状，隐隐叫人联想起墓碑，联想起坟茔与纪念碑。小径两旁，再不见春草的青绿——只有一片被渐干的藻类与铜绿一般的细小地衣所斑驳的泥土。此外还有些形容可憎的菌类，一丛丛地生长着，茎秆透出麻风病般的惨白，黑乎乎的菌盖低垂着、厌嫌地一点一点。

===Original===
The sky had now grown so dark that the whole scene took on a semi-nocturnal aspect, and made me think of a doomed world in the twilight of a dying sun. All was airless and silent; there were no birds, no insects, no sighing of the pines, no lisping of leaves: a baleful and preternatural silence, like the silence of the infinite void.

===Chinese===
此时天色已暗得令整片景象都染上了一种半似夜晚的况味，使我联想起一个命定覆亡的世界，沉沦在一轮垂死日头的暮色之中。万物都了无气息、寂然无声；没有鸟雀，没有虫豸，没有松涛的叹息，没有树叶的窸窣：那是一种邪祟的、超乎自然的寂静，一如无穷虚空之中的寂静。

===Original===
The trees became denser, then dwindled, and I came to a circular field. Here, there was no mistaking the nature of the monolithal boulders﻿—they were headstones and funeral monuments, but so enormously ancient that the letterings or figures upon them were well-nigh effaced; and the few characters that I could distinguish were not of any known language. About them there the hoariness and mystery and terror of incomputable Eld. It was hard to believe that life and death could be as old as they. The trees around them were inconceivably gnarled and bowed as with an almost equal burden of years. The sense of awful antiquity that these stones and pines all served to convey increased the oppression of my bewilderment, confirmed my disquietude. Nor was I reassured when I noticed on the soft earth about the headstones a number of those attenuate footprints of which I have already spoken. They were disposed in a fashion that was truly singular, seeming to depart from and return to the vicinity of each stone.

===Chinese===
树木渐趋茂密，继而又稀疏起来，我终于来到一方圆形的旷野。在这里，那些独石般的漂砾究竟是什么，便再无疑问了——它们乃是墓碑与丧葬的纪念碑，只是古老得太过惊人，上面的刻字或图形几乎已被磨灭殆尽；而我勉强辨认出的寥寥几个字符，又绝非任何已知的语言。它们周身弥漫着一种不可计量的洪荒（Eld）所特有的苍古、神秘与恐怖。简直叫人难以相信，生与死竟能古老至此。环绕它们的树木，则盘虬得难以想象，佝偻低垂，仿佛也扛着与之几乎相等的岁月之重。这些石头与松树所共同传达出的那种可怖的苍古之感，加重了我惶乱之中的窒息，坐实了我的不安。而当我注意到墓碑四周松软的泥土上那一串串我已说过的瘦长脚印时，我心头的疑惧也并未稍减。它们的排列方式实在奇特，似是从每块石头近旁离去、又返回每块石头近旁。

===Original===
Now, for the first time, I heard a sound other than the sound of my own footfalls in the silence of this macabre scene. Behind me, among the trees, there was a faint and evil rattling. I turned and listened; there was something in these sounds that served to complete the demoralization of my unstrung nerves; and monstrous fears, abominable fancies, trooped like the horde of a witches’ sabbat through my brain.

===Chinese===
就在此时，在这片阴森景象的死寂之中，我头一回听见了除自己足音之外的声响。在我身后，林间，传来一阵若有若无、邪气森森的磕碰之声。我回转身去凝神倾听；这些声音里有某种东西，将我那早已紧绷欲断的神经彻底击溃；种种畸形的恐惧、种种可憎的臆想，如巫魔夜会（witches' sabbat）上蜂拥的群魔一般，成群结队地涌入我的脑中。

===Original===
The reality that I was now to confront was no less monstrous! There was a whitish glimmering in the shadow of the trees, and a human skeleton, bearing in its arms the skeleton of an infant, emerged and came towards me! Intent as on some ulterior cryptic purpose, some charnel errand not to be surmised by the living, it went by with a tranquil pace, an effortless and gliding tread in which, despite my terror and stupefaction, I perceived a certain horrible and feminine grace. I followed the apparition with my eyes as it passed among the monuments without pausing and vanished in the darkness of the pines on the opposite side of the field. No sooner had it gone, than a second, also bearing in its arms an infant skeleton, appeared, and passed before me in the same direction and with the same abominable and loathsome grace of movement.

===Chinese===
我此刻所要面对的实情，同样骇人听闻！树影之中隐隐泛着一抹惨白的微光，一具人的骷髅，怀中抱着一具婴孩的骷髅，自阴影中现身，朝我走来！它神态凝定，仿佛专注于某种别有所图、晦暗难明的目的，专注于某种活人所无从揣度的骸骨堂差使，以从容的步调从我面前经过——那步伐轻巧而不费力，恍如滑行；尽管我惊恐万状、目瞪口呆，却仍从中辨出一种可怖的、属于女性的优雅。我目送这幽灵般的身影穿过一座座墓碑而不作片刻停留，隐没在旷野对面的松林深处。它前脚刚走，第二具骷髅便已现身，怀中同样抱着一具婴孩的骷髅，从我面前朝同一方向走过，举手投足之间，是同样的可憎、同样的令人作呕的优雅。

===Original===
A horror that was more than horror, a fear that was beyond fear, petrified all my faculties, and I felt as if I were weighted down by some ineluctable and insupportable burden of nightmare, Before me, skeleton after skeleton, each precisely like the last, with the same macabre lightness and ease of motion, each carrying its pitiful infant, emerged from the shadow of the ancient pines and followed where the first had disappeared, intent as on the same cryptic errand. One by one they came, till I had counted eight! Now I knew the origin of the bizarre footprints whose attenuation had disturbed and troubled me.

===Chinese===
一种甚于恐惧的恐惧，一种凌驾于惊骇之上的惊骇，将我全部的神志都化作了顽石；我只觉得自己仿佛被某种无可逃避、不堪承受的梦魇重负所压伏。在我面前，一具又一具骷髅，每一具都与前一具毫无二致，以同样阴森可怖的轻盈、同样从容不迫的步态，每一具都抱着它那可怜的婴孩，自古松的阴影中现身，循着第一具消失的方向鱼贯而去，神态凝定，仿佛专注于同一桩晦暗难明的差使。它们一个接一个地走来，直至我数出了八个！直到这时，我才明白那些令我心神不宁的怪异脚印，究竟从何而来。

===Original===
When the eighth skeleton had passed from sight, my eyes were drawn as by some irresistible impulsion to one of the nearer headstones, beside which I was amazed to perceive what I had not noticed before: a freshly opened grave, gaping darkly in the soft soil. Then, at my elbow, I heard a low rattling, and the fingers of a fleshless hand plucked lightly at my sleeve. A skeleton was beside me, differing only from the others through the fact that it bore no infant in its arms. With a lipless and ingratiating leer, it plucked again at my sleeve, as if to draw me towards the open grave, and its teeth clicked as if it were trying to speak. My senses and my brain, aswirl with vertiginous terror, could endure no more: I seemed to fall and fall through deeps of infinite eddying blackness with the clutching terror of those fingers upon my arm, till consciousness was left behind in my descent.

===Chinese===
待到第八具骷髅也走出视野，我的目光被某种不可抗拒的冲动所牵引，落到了近旁的一块墓碑上；令我骇然的是，在它旁边，我竟看见一桩先前未曾留意的事物：一座新掘开的坟茔，在松软的泥土里黑沉沉地洞张着口。紧接着，就在我肘畔，我听见一阵低低的磕碰声，一只无肉之手的手指，轻轻地扯了扯我的衣袖。一具骷髅立在我身旁，与其余诸具唯一的不同，便是它怀中并未抱着婴孩。它以一种没有嘴唇的、讨好献媚的狞笑，又扯了扯我的衣袖，似要把我拽向那座敞开的坟茔，它的牙齿嗒嗒作响，仿佛正竭力想要开口说话。我的感官与头脑，已被令人眩晕的恐惧搅得团团乱转，再也承受不住：我仿佛不断地坠落、坠落，穿过无穷回旋的漆黑深渊，而那只手手指的紧攥仍死死扣在我的臂上——直坠到我把意识都抛在了身后。

===Original===
When I came to, Guenevere was holding me by the arm, concern and puzzlement upon her sweet oval face, and I was standing among the boulders of the field appointed for our rendezvous.

“What on earth is the matter with you, Herbert?” she queried anxiously. “Are you ill? You were standing here in a daze when I came, and didn’t seem to hear or see me when I spoke to you. And I really thought you were going to faint when I touched your arm.”

===Chinese===
待我苏醒过来，桂妮薇儿正挽着我的手臂，那张甜美的鹅蛋脸上满是关切与困惑；而我，正站在我们约定相会的那片旷野的漂砾之间。

“你到底怎么了，赫伯特（Herbert）？”她焦急地探问道。“你是病了吗？我来的时候，你就这样恍恍惚惚地站在这里，我跟你说话，你仿佛既听不见、也看不见我。我碰到你手臂的时候，我真以为你就要晕过去了。”
