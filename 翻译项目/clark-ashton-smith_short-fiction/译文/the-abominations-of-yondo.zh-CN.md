## The Abominations of Yondo / 雍多的可憎之物

===Original===
The sand of the desert of Yondo is not as the sand of other deserts; for Yondo lies nearest of all to the world's rim; and strange winds, blowing from a pit no astronomer may hope to fathom, have sown its ruinous fields with the gray dust of corroding planets, the black ashes of extinguished suns. The dark, orb-like mountains which rise from its wrinkled and pitted plain are not all its own, for some are fallen asteroids half-buried in that abysmal sand. Things have crept in from nether space, whose incursion is forbid by the gods of all proper and well-ordered lands; but there are no such gods in Yondo, where live the hoary genii of stars abolished and decrepit demons left homeless by the destruction of antiquated hells.

===Chinese===
雍多（Yondo）荒漠的沙，不同于他处荒漠的沙；因为雍多在一切地域之中，距大地的边缘最近；自那连天文学家也无从测度的深渊里吹来的怪风，将朽蚀行星的灰败尘埃、熄灭恒星的黑色余烬，遍播于它那倾败的原野之上。自它那皱缩、坑洼的平原上耸起的暗色球状群山，并非尽是本土所生，其中有些，乃是半埋于那无底荒沙之中的坠落陨星。有种种异物自幽冥的太虚潜入此地，它们的入侵，为一切端正有序的疆土上的诸神所不容；然而雍多并无此等神祇，栖居其间的，乃是那些被废黜的星辰的苍老巨灵，以及因陈旧地狱之毁灭而无家可归的衰颓恶魔。

===Original===
It was noon of a vernal day when I came forth from that interminable cactus-forest in which the Inquisitors of Ong had left me, and saw at my feet the gray beginnings of Yondo. I repeat, it was noon of a vernal day; but in that fantastic wood I had found no token or memory of a spring; and the swollen, fulvous, dying and half-rotten growths through which I had pushed my way, were like no other cacti, but bore shapes of abomination scarcely to be described. The very air was heavy with stagnant odors of decay; and leprous lichens mottled the black soil and russet vegetation with increasing frequency. Pale-green vipers lifted their heads from prostrate cactus-boles and watched me with eyes of bright ochre that had no lids or pupils. These things had disquieted me for hours past; and I did not like the monstrous fungi, with hueless stems and nodding heads of poisonous mauve, which grew from the sodden lips of fetid tarns; and the sinister ripples spreading and fading on the yellow water at my approach were not reassuring to one whose nerves were still taut from unmentionable tortures. Then, when even the blotched and sickly cacti became more sparse and stunted, and rills of ashen sand crept in among them, I began to suspect how great was the hatred my heresy had aroused in the priests of Ong and to guess the ultimate malignancy of their vengeance.

===Chinese===
那是一个春日的正午，我从昂（Ong）的审判官们所弃置我的那片连绵无际的仙人掌森林中走出，望见雍多那灰败的起始，就在我脚下。我再说一遍，那是一个春日的正午；可是在那诡异的林莽之中，我寻不见任何春天的征兆或记忆；我一路推开那些肿胀、黄褐、垂死而半腐的草木向前行去，它们与一切别的仙人掌都判然不同，却生着几乎难以描摹的可憎形状。连空气都沉滞着腐臭的、停滞的气息；而那癞病般的苔藓，愈来愈频繁地将黑土与赭色的草木斑驳地点染。淡绿的小蝰蛇自倾倒的仙人掌躯干上抬起头来，以那既无眼睑亦无瞳孔的明亮赭石色眸子凝视着我。这些物事已使我惴惴不安了数个时辰；我更不喜那些畸怪的蕈类，它们生着无色的菌柄与低垂的毒紫色菌冠，自恶臭山潭那浸饱水汽的唇边长出；而当我走近时，那在黄水上扩散又消褪的阴森涟漪，对一个神经尚因不可言说的酷刑而紧绷如弦的人而言，也并无半分宽慰。后来，当连那些斑驳而病态的仙人掌也变得愈发稀疏、愈发矮小，一缕缕灰烬般的细沙悄悄潜入它们之间，我才开始揣度我的异端在那昂的祭司心中唤起了何等深切的仇恨，并揣测出他们报复之中那终极的恶毒。

===Original===
I will not detail the indiscretions which had led me, a careless stranger from far-off lands, into the power of those dreadful magicians and mysteriarchs who serve the lion-headed Ong. These indiscretions, and the particulars of my arrest, are painful to remember; and least of all do I like to remember the racks of dragon-gut strewn with powdered adamant, on which men are stretched naked; or that unlit room with six-inch windows near the sill, where bloated corpse worms crawled in by hundreds from a neighboring catacomb. Sufficient to say that, after expending the resources of their frightful fantasy, my inquisitors had borne me blindfolded on camel-back for incomputable hours, to leave me at morning twilight in that sinister forest. I was free, they told me, to go whither I would; and in token of the clemency of Ong, they gave me a loaf of coarse bread and a leathern bottle of rank water by way of provision. It was at noon of the same day that I came to the desert of Yondo.

===Chinese===
我无意详述那些轻率的行径——正是它们使我这来自远方、不慎疏虞的异乡人，落入那些侍奉狮首之神昂的骇人术士与秘仪教长（mysteriarchs）之手。这些行径，连同我被捕的种种详情，回想起来都痛楚难堪；而我最不愿忆起的，莫过于那些以龙筋编成、撒满金刚砂的刑架——人被赤身裸体地张开其上；或是那间六英寸小窗紧贴窗台的幽暗囚室，成百上千臃肿的尸虫自邻近的地下墓穴爬入其中。只消说，我的审判官们在穷尽了他们那可怖想象的一切手段之后，将我蒙上双眼、缚于驼背，驮行了不可胜计的时辰，于晨曦微明之时将我弃于那片不祥的森林之中。他们对我说，我自由了，可往任何想去之处；作为昂之宽宥的凭证，他们给了我一块粗面包、一只盛着腥臭脏水的皮瓶，权作路资。正是同一天的正午，我来到了雍多的荒漠。

===Original===
So far, I had not thought of turning back, for all the horror of those rotting cacti, or the evil things that dwelt among them. Now, I paused knowing the abominable legend of the land to which I had come; for Yondo is a place where few have ventured wittingly and of their own accord. Fewer still have returned—babbling of unknown horrors and strange treasure; and the lifelong palsy which shakes their withered limbs, together with the mad gleam in their starting eyes beneath whitened brows and lashes, is not an incentive for others to follow. So it was that I hesitated on the verge of those ashen sands, and felt the tremor of a new fear in my wrenched vitals. It was dreadful to go on, and dreadful to go back, for I felt sure that the priests had made provision against the latter contingency. So after a little I went forward, singing at each step in loathly softness, and followed by certain long-legged insects that I had met among the cacti. These insects were the color of a week-old corpse and were as large as tarantulas; but when I turned and trod upon the foremost, a mephitic stench arose that was more nauseous even than their color. So, for the nonce, I ignored them as much as possible.

===Chinese===
在此之前，尽管那些腐烂的仙人掌何等可怖，其间栖居的恶物何等可憎，我都不曾起过折返之念。而如今我却踌躇了，因为我知道自己所至之地有着怎样可憎的传说；雍多这地方，少有人明知而甘愿涉足。归来者更是寥寥——他们语无伦次地念叨着未知的恐怖与奇异的珍宝；而那终生不愈、震颤他们枯萎四肢的瘫疾，以及斑白眉睫之下那双暴凸眼眸中狂乱的光芒，绝非引诱他人效尤的榜样。于是我在那片灰烬之沙的边缘踌躇，在我那被折磨得衰败的脏腑中，感到一种新的恐惧的颤栗。向前是可怖，折返亦是可怖，因为我深知，祭司们对后一种情形必已预作防备。过了一会儿，我向前走去，每一步都发出令人作呕的、轻柔的鸣响，几只我在仙人掌间遇见的长腿昆虫尾随其后。这些昆虫的颜色如同死去一周的尸首，体型则与狼蛛相仿；可当我转身一脚踩住最前面那只时，一股恶臭腾起，比它们的颜色更令人作呕。于是，暂且之间，我只得尽可能不去理会它们。

===Original===
Indeed, such things were minor horrors in my predicament. Before me, under a huge sun of sickly scarlet, Yondo reached interminable as the land of a hashish-dream against the black heavens. Far-off, on the utmost rim, were those orb-like mountains of which I have told; but in between were awful blanks of gray desolation, and low, treeless hills like the backs of half-buried monsters. Struggling on, I saw great pits where meteors had sunk from sight; and divers-colored jewels that I could not name glared or glistened from the dust. There were fallen cypresses that rotted by crumbling mausoleums, on whose lichen blotted marble fat chameleons crept with royal pearls in their mouths. Hidden by the low ridges, were cities of which no stela remained unbroken—immense and immemorial cities lapsing shard by shard, atom by atom, to feed infinities of desolation. I dragged my torture-weakened limbs over vast rubbish-heaps that had once been mighty temples; and fallen gods frowned in rotting psammite or leered in riven porphyry at my feet. Over all was an evil silence, broken only by the satanic laughter of hyenas, and the rustling of adders in thickets of dead thorn or antique gardens given to the perishing nettle and fumitory.

===Chinese===
诚然，这些于我的困境而言，不过是些微末的可怖。在我面前，一轮病态猩红的巨日之下，雍多向着黑色的苍穹无尽地延展，宛如一场哈希什迷梦（hashish-dream）中的疆域。极远之处，在最外的边缘，便是我所说过的那些球状群山；可两者之间，却是一处处骇人的、灰败荒芜的空白，以及低矮、无树的丘陵，形似半埋于地的怪物的脊背。我艰难前行，望见一个个巨坑，那是陨星沉没消失之处；我无以名之的各色宝石，在尘埃中灼灼生光或熠熠闪烁。有倾倒的柏树在倾圮的陵墓旁腐烂，那生满苔藓斑迹的大理石上，肥硕的变色龙口衔皇室珍珠缓缓爬行。隐于低矮山脊之后的，是一座座无一碑完整的城池——广袤而无从追忆的古城，正一碎片一碎片、一微粒一微粒地崩颓，去喂养那无尽的荒凉。我拖曳着因酷刑而衰弱的肢体，攀过一堆堆曾为雄伟神庙的浩瀚瓦砾；陨落的诸神，或于腐朽的砂质岩（psammite）中蹙眉，或于我脚下破裂的斑岩（porphyry）中狞笑。笼罩一切的，是一片邪祟的寂静，唯有鬣狗那魔鬼般的狞笑，以及蝰蛇在枯荆棘丛中、在任由将死的荨麻与烟堇（fumitory）侵占的古旧园林里的窸窣爬行，偶尔将它打破。

===Original===
Topping one of the many mound-like ridges, I saw the waters of a weird lake, unfathomably dark and green as malachite, and set with bars of profulgent salt. These waters lay far beneath me in a cup-like hollow; but almost at my feet on the wave-worn slopes were heaps of that ancient salt; and I knew that the lake was only the bitter and ebbing dregs of some former sea. Climbing down, I came to the dark waters, and began to lave my hands; but there was a sharp and corrosive sting in that immemorial brine, and I desisted quickly, preferring the desert dust that had wrapped me about like a slow shroud.

===Chinese===
翻过那许许多多坟冢般的山脊之一，我望见了一片怪湖的水面——它幽深难测、暗而绿如孔雀石，其间横亘着一道道辉耀的盐带。那片水域远远地卧在我脚下的一个杯状洼地之中；可几乎就在我脚边、那些被波浪侵蚀的斜坡上，堆着一簇簇那古老的盐；我便明白，这湖不过是某片往昔之海那苦涩而退落的残液。我攀援而下，来到那暗水之畔，开始濯洗双手；可那太古的盐水里有一种尖锐而腐蚀的蜇刺，我连忙缩手，宁可任由那早已如一袭缓缓裹尸的殓衣般裹缠我的荒漠尘埃。

===Original===
Here I decided to rest for a little; and hunger forced me to consume part of the meager and mocking fare with which I had been provided by the priests. It was my intention to push on if my strength would allow and reach the lands that lie to the north of Yondo. These lands are desolate, indeed, but their desolation is of a more usual order than that of Yondo; and certain tribes of nomads have been known to visit them occasionally. If fortune favored me, I might fall in with one of these tribes.

===Chinese===
我决意在此稍作歇息；饥饿迫使我吞下祭司们所给我的那份少得可怜、形同嘲弄的口粮之一部分。我本打算，若体力尚可，便继续北行，抵达雍多以北的那些疆土。那些疆土诚然也是荒凉的，可它们的荒凉，比之雍多的荒凉，尚属较为寻常的一类；而且据说有些游牧部族偶尔会涉足其地。若命运眷顾，我或可偶遇其中一族。

===Original===
The scant fare revived me, and, for the first time in weeks of which I had lost all reckoning, I heard the whisper of a faint hope. The corpse-colored insects had long since ceased to follow me; and so far despite the eeriness of the sepulchral silence and the mounded dust of timeless ruin, I had met nothing half so horrible as those insects. I began to think that the terrors of Yondo were somewhat exaggerated. It was then that I heard a diabolic chuckle on the hillside above me. The sound began with a sharp abruptness that startled me beyond all reason, and continued endlessly, never varying its single note, like the mirth of an idiotic demon. I turned, and saw the mouth of a dark cave fanged with green stalactites, which I had not perceived before. The sound appeared to come from within this cave.

===Chinese===
那点微薄的口粮使我稍稍苏复；在我已全然忘却其数目的数周以来，我头一次听见了一缕微弱希望的絮语。那尸色的昆虫早已不再尾随我；而至此，尽管四周是墓穴般寂静的阴森，以及无时间之朽败那坟冢般的尘埃，我所遇之物，竟无一及得上那些昆虫一半可怖。我开始以为，雍多的诸般恐怖未免有些言过其实。正在那时，我听见头顶的山坡上传来一声恶魔般的低笑。那声音以一种令我莫名惊骇的尖锐突兀而起，继而无穷无尽地延续，单调不变，犹如一个白痴恶魔的欢愉。我转身望去，看见一个暗洞的洞口，满口獠牙般地生满了绿色的钟乳石——这是我先前未曾察觉的。那声音，似乎便是从这洞穴之中传出。

===Original===
With a fearful intentness I stared at the black opening. The chuckle grew louder, but for awhile I could see nothing. At last I caught a whitish glimmer in the darkness; then, with all the rapidity of nightmare, a monstrous Thing emerged. It had a pale, hairless, egg-shaped body, large as that of a gravid she-goat; and this body was mounted on nine long wavering legs with many flanges, like the legs of some enormous spider. The creature ran past me to the water's edge; and I saw that there were no eyes in its oddly sloping face; but two knife-like ears rose high above its head, and a thin, wrinkled snout hung down across its mouth, whose flabby lips, parted in that eternal chuckle, revealed rows of bats' teeth. It drank acidly of the bitter lake then, with thirst satisfied, it turned and seemed to sense my presence, for the wrinkled snout rose and pointed toward me, sniffing audibly. Whether the creature would have fled, or whether it meant to attack me, I do not know; for I could bear the sight no longer but ran with trembling limbs amid the massive boulders and great bars of salt along the lakeshore.

===Chinese===
我怀着惊惧的专注，凝视着那黑色的洞口。低笑声愈发响亮，但好一阵我什么也看不见。终于，我在黑暗中捕捉到一缕惨白的微光；随即，以噩梦般的迅疾，一个畸怪之物涌了出来。它有一个苍白、无毛、蛋形的身躯，大如一头怀胎的母山羊；这身躯装在九条长而摇摆、生着许多翼片的腿上，恰如某种巨型蜘蛛的腿。那生物从我身旁跑过，直奔水边；我看见它那奇异地倾斜的脸上并没有眼睛；可两只刀刃般的耳朵高耸于其头顶之上，一条瘦皱的长吻垂过它的嘴，那松弛的唇永远咧在那不绝的低笑里，露出层层叠叠的蝙蝠般的牙齿。它酸涩地饮着那苦湖的水，随后，渴既得解，它转过身来，似是察觉了我的存在，因为那条皱缩的长吻抬了起来，直直指向我，发出清晰可闻的嗅探之声。这生物本欲逃遁，抑或意欲攻我，我不得而知；因为我再也无法忍受这景象，遂以颤抖的四肢，在湖滨那巨硕的卵石与一道道巨大的盐柱之间奔逃。

===Original===
Utterly breathless I stopped at last, and saw that I was not pursued, I sat down, still trembling, in the shadow of a boulder. But I was to find little respite, for now began the second of those bizarre adventures which forced me to believe all the mad legends I had heard.

===Chinese===
终于，我上气不接下气地停了下来，发觉自己并未被追及，便在一块巨石的阴影里坐下，兀自颤抖。可我注定难得喘息，因为此刻，那迫使我信服我所听闻的一切疯狂传说的第二桩诡诞奇遇，已然开端。

===Original===
More startling even than that diabolic chuckle was the scream that rose at my very elbow from the salt-compounded sand—the scream of a woman possessed by some atrocious agony, or helpless in the grip of devils. Turning, I beheld a veritable Venus, naked in a white perfection that could fear no scrutiny, but immersed to her navel in the sand. Her terror-widened eyes implored me and her lotus hands reached out—with beseeching gesture. I sprang to her side—and touched a marble statue, whose carven lids were drooped in some enigmatic dream of dead cycles, and whose hands were buried with the lost loveliness of hips and thighs. Again I fled, shaken with a new fear; and again I heard the scream of a woman's agony. But this time I did not turn to see the imploring eyes and hands.

===Chinese===
比那恶魔般的低笑更令人惊心的，是一声从我手肘边、那掺盐的沙中升起的尖叫——那是一个女人被某种穷凶极恶的苦痛所附、或无助地落入群魔之掌时的尖叫。我转过身，看见一位真正的维纳斯，赤裸着一身不怕任何审视的洁白完美，却自腰脐以下没入沙中。她那因恐惧而圆睁的双眸向我哀求，她那莲瓣般的双手伸出——作着恳求的姿态。我扑到她身侧——却触到了一座大理石雕像，它那雕出的双睑低垂着，沉入某种属于已死纪元的玄奥迷梦，它的双手，连同那已逝的臀与股的秀美，一齐被埋没了。我再次奔逃，因一种新的恐惧而战栗；我再次听见一个女人苦痛的尖叫。可这一次，我没有转身去看那哀求的双眼与双手。

===Original===
Up the long slope to the north of that accursed lake stumbling over boulders of basanite and ledges that were sharp with verdigris-covered metals; floundering in pits of salt, on terraces wrought by the receding tide in ancient aeons. I fled as a man flies from dream to baleful dream of some cacodemoniacal night. At whiles there was a cold whisper in my ear, which did not come from the wind of my flight; and looking back as I reached one of the upper terraces, I perceived a singular shadow that ran pace by pace with my own. This shadow was not the shadow of man nor ape nor any known beast; the head was too grotesquely elongated, the squat body too gibbous; and I was unable to determine whether the shadow possessed five legs, or whether what appeared to be the fifth was merely a tail.

===Chinese===
我攀上那受诅咒之湖以北的长坡，在玄武岩（basanite）的巨石上踉跄，在那些因覆满铜绿的金属而锋利如刃的岩架上跌撞；我陷落于盐坑之中，陷落于远古纪元里退去的潮汐所凿出的层阶之上。我如一个在某个邪魔之夜从一重噩梦逃向另一重噩梦的人那般奔逃。不时，我耳畔有一缕冰冷的低语，那并非来自我奔逃时所带起的风；当我抵达其中一道上层阶地回首张望时，我察觉到一个奇异的影子，与我亦步亦趋。这影子，既非人也非猿、更非任何已知野兽的影子；它的头颅诡怪地过分拉长，矮胖的身躯又过于隆凸；我甚至无从断定，这影子究竟生着五条腿，还是那看似第五条的，不过是一条尾巴。

===Original===
Terror lent me new strength, and I had reached the hilltop when I dared to look back again. But still the fantastic shadow kept pace by pace with mine; and now I caught a curious and utterly sickening odor, foul as the odor of bats who have hung in a charnel-house amid the mold of corruption.
