## XI. An Affair with the Barbarous Fishers / 十一　与野蛮渔人的事端
===Original===

So this mighty Empress chose to be jealous of a mere woman prisoner!

===Chinese===

瞧这位权势熏天的女皇，竟偏偏要吃一个区区女囚的醋！

===Original===

Now my mind has been trained to work with a soldierly quickness in these moments of stress, and I decided on my proper course on the instant the words had left her lips. I was sacrificing myself for Atlantis by order of the High Council of the Priests, and, if needful, Nais must be sacrificed also, although in the same flash a scheme came to me for saving her.

===Chinese===

我的头脑素来训练有素，在这等危急关头能以军人的迅捷运转；她话音才离唇，我当下便定下了应走的路数。我既奉祭司最高议事会之命为亚特兰蒂斯牺牲自己，若有必要，娜伊丝也须一并牺牲——虽说就在同一瞬，一条救她的计策已在我心里闪现。

===Original===

So I bowed gravely before the Empress, and said I, “In this, and in all other things where a mere human hand is potent, I will carry out your wishes, Phorenice.” And she on her part patted my arm, and fresh waves of feeling welled up from the depths of her wondrous eyes. Surely the Gods won for her half her schemes and half her battles when they gave Phorenice her shape, and her voice, and the matters which lay within the outlines of her face.

===Chinese===

于是我向女皇郑重一躬，说道：「在这件事上，以及在凡人一只手能奏效的一切别的事情上，我都愿遂你的心愿，福伦尼刻。」她呢，则拍了拍我的手臂，那双妙目深处又涌起新的层层情澜。诸神赋予福伦尼刻这身段、这嗓音、这眉目轮廓之间蕴藏的万般意态时，想必已替她赢下了她一半的图谋、一半的战局。

===Original===

By this time the merchants, and the other dwellers adjacent to this part of the harbour, where the royal quay stands, had come down, offering changes of raiment, and houses to retire into. Phorenice was all graciousness, and though it was little enough I cared for mere wetness of my coat, still that part of the harbour into which we had been thrown by the mammoth was not over savoury, and I was glad enough to follow her example. For myself, I said no further word to Nais, and refrained even from giving her a glance of farewell. But a small sop like this was no meal for Phorenice, and she gave the port-captain strict orders for the guarding of his prisoner before she left him.

===Chinese===

这时，商贾们和毗邻王家码头一带而居的其他住户都已赶了下来，献上替换的衣裳，还请出屋子让我们歇息。福伦尼刻满身雍容和悦。衣裳湿了这点小事我本不大在意，只是我们被猛犸甩进去的那一角海港，实在说不上气味雅致，我便也乐得效仿她。至于我自己，再没对娜伊丝多说半句话，连一道道别的眼色也克制着不曾给。可这样一小片面包心，是填不饱福伦尼刻的；临走前，她又给港务长官下了严令，命他看守好他的囚徒。

===Original===

At the house into which I was ushered they gave me a bath, and I eased my host of the plainest garment in his store, and he was pleased enough at getting off so cheaply. But I had an hour to spend outside on the pavement listening to the distant din of bombardment before Phorenice came out to me again, and I could not help feeling some grim amusement at the face of the merchant who followed. The fellow was clearly ruined. He had a store of jewels and gauds of the most costly kind, which were only in fraction his own, seeing that he had bought them (as the custom is) in partnership with other merchants. These had pleased Phorenice’s eye, and so she had taken all and disposed them on her person.

===Chinese===

我被引进的那户人家给我备了浴汤，我顺手把主人库中最素朴的一件衣裳「减轻」到他身上，他只破费这么点便脱了身，已是喜出望外。可我在门外的街面上枯等了一个钟头，听着远处炮轰的喧响，福伦尼刻才重新出来见我；瞧着跟在她身后出来的那商人的脸色，我不由得生出一丝冷然的兴味。这家伙分明是毁了。他藏着一批极贵重的珠宝饰物，却只小半是自家财产，因为（照通行的规矩）他是与其他商人合伙置办的。这些东西入了福伦尼刻的眼，于是她尽数取走，披挂了一身。

===Original===

“Are they not pretty?” said she, showing them to me. “See how they flash under the sun. I am quite glad now, Deucalion, that the mammoth gave us that furious ride and that spill, since it has brought me such a bonny present. You may tell the fellow here that some day when he has earned some more, I will come and be his guest again. Ah! They have brought us litters, I see. Well, send one away and do you share mine with me, sir. We must play at being lovers today, even if love is a matter which will come to us both with more certainty tomorrow. No; do not order more bearers. My own slaves will carry us handily enough. I am glad you are not one of your gross, overfed men, Deucalion. I am small and slim myself, and I do not want to be husbanded by a man who will overshadow me.”

===Chinese===

「这些不漂亮吗？」她把它们展示给我看。「瞧它们在日头底下多么闪亮。德乌卡利翁，如今我倒真高兴猛犸带我们疯了那一程、摔了那一跤，因为它给我挣来这么一件好礼。你可以告诉这儿这家伙，等哪天他又挣下一些，我再来做他的客人。啊！我看他们给我们抬轿子来了。好，打发掉一乘，你与我合乘我这一乘吧，先生。今天我们得扮一扮情人，哪怕恋爱这桩事，要等到明天才更有把握落到你我头上。不；不用多叫轿夫。我自己的奴隶抬我们绰绰有余。我很高兴你不是那种粗蠢痴肥的男人，德乌卡利翁。我自己生得娇小苗条，可不想嫁一个把我整个罩住的丈夫。」

===Original===

“Back to the royal pyramid?” I asked.

===Chinese===

「回王家金字塔去？」我问。

===Original===

“No, nor to the walls. I neither wish to fight nor to sit as Empress today, sir. As I have told you before, it is my whim to be Phorenice, the maiden, for a few hours, and if someone I wot of would woo me now, as other maidens are wooed, I should esteem it a luxury. Bid the slaves carry us round the harbour’s rim, and give word to these starers that, if they follow, I will call down fire upon them as I did upon the sacrifice.”

===Chinese===

「不，也不回城墙去。今天我既不想打仗，也不想端坐为女皇，先生。我从前便对你说过，我这会儿的兴致，是做几个时辰的少女福伦尼刻；倘若有那么一位我心知肚明的人，此刻肯像追求别的少女一般追求我，那在我便是极奢侈的享受了。吩咐奴隶们抬着我们沿港湾绕行，再给那些盯梢的递句话：谁若跟着，我便引天火烧他，就像方才烧那祭牲一般。」

===Original===

Now, I had seen something of the unruliness of the streets myself, and I had gathered a hint also from the officer at the gate of the royal pyramid that night of Phorenice’s welcoming banquet. But as whatever there was in the matter must be common knowledge to the Empress, I did not bring it to her memory then. So I dismissed the guard which had come up, and drove away with a few sharp words the throng of gaping sightseers who always, silly creatures, must needs come to stare at their betters; and then I sat in the litter in the place where I was invited, and the bearers put their heads to the pole.

===Chinese===

说到街头的不驯，我自己已见识过一二；福伦尼刻接风宴那晚，王家金字塔门前那位军官的话，也让我听出几分端倪。但此事无论有什么内情，女皇必定了然，我便没有在当时向她提起。于是我遣散了赶上来的卫队，又用几句厉声呵斥赶走了那群张口呆看的闲人——这些蠢货，见了体面人总忍不住要凑上来盯瞧。随后我在受让的位子上坐进轿中，轿夫们把头颈拱进了轿杠。

===Original===

They swung away with us along the wide pavement which runs between the houses of the merchants and the mariner folk and the dimpling waters of the harbour, and I thought somewhat sadly of the few ships that floated on that splendid basin now, and of the few evidences of business that showed themselves on the quays. Time was when the ships were berthed so close that many had to wait in the estuary outside the walls, and memorials had been sent to the King that the port should be doubled in size to hold the glut of trade. And that, too, in the old days of oar and sail, when machines drawing power from our Lord the Sun were but rarely used to help a vessel speedily along her course.

===Chinese===

轿夫们抬着我们悠悠而行，沿着商贾水手人家与微漾港水之间那条宽阔的石道；我想起此刻浮在那片壮丽港池上的寥寥几艘船、码头上稀见的生意迹象，心头不免生出几分怅然。想当年，船只停得密密匝匝，许多船只好在外墙外的河口候泊，请愿文书一封封递到老国王面前，求他把港口扩充一倍，以容那泛滥的贸易。何况那还是桨帆用力的旧时光，借我们太阳神主之力驱动的机械，还极少用来帮船只快捷地走完航程。

===Original===

The Egypt voyage and a return was a matter of a year then, as against a brace of months now, and of three ships that set out, one at least could be reckoned upon succumbing to the dangers of the wide waters and the terrible beasts that haunt them. But in those old days trade roared with lusty life, and was ever growing wider and more heavy. Your merchant then was a portly man and gave generously to the Gods. But now all the world seemed to be in arms, and moreover trade was vulgar. Your merchant, if he was a man of substance, forgot his merchandise, swore that chaffering was more indelicate than blasphemy and curled his beard after the new fashion, and became a courtier. Where his father had spent anxious days with cargo tally and ship-master, the son wasted hours in directing sewing men as they adorned a coat, and nights in vapouring at a banquet.

===Chinese===

那时跑一趟埃及再返回，是足足一年的事，如今不过两个月；而每三艘出海的船，至少可料定有一艘要葬身在茫茫大水的凶险和出没其间可怕巨兽的口腹之中。但在那旧日时光，贸易怀着旺盛的生命力轰然前行，地盘越做越宽，分量越压越重。那时的商人是个脑满肠肥的体面人，向诸神的供奉也出手大方。可如今，普天之下似乎都在兵戈相见，况且经商也已成了俗气事。你的商人，但凡有点家底，便把货物抛在脑后，赌咒说讨价还价比亵渎神明更失体面，把胡须按新式样卷起来，摇身成了朝臣。他的父亲当年为货单和船主愁白了多少个白天，这儿子却把时辰虚掷在指点裁缝装点一件外袍上，把夜晚浪掷在宴席的大话里。

===Original===

Of the smaller merchants who had no substance laid by, taxes and the constant bickerings of war had well-nigh ground them into starvation. Besides, with the country in constant uproar, there were few markets left for most merchandise, nor was there aught made now which could be carried abroad. If your weaver is pressed as a fire-tube man he does not make cloth, and if your farmer is playing at rebellion, he does not buy slaves to till his fields. Indeed, they told me that a month before my return, as fine a cargo of slaves had been brought into harbour as ever came out of Europe, and there was nothing for it but to set them ashore across the estuary, and leave them free to starve or live in the wild ground there as they chose. There was no man in all Atlantis who would hold so much as one more slave as a gift.

===Chinese===

至于那些没有积蓄的小商人，赋税和连绵不断的战事摩擦，几乎把他们碾到了饿死的边缘。再者，国内动乱不休，大多数货物已剩不下几个市场，如今也造不出什么可以运销海外的物件。你的织工若被拉去充了火管兵，他便织不成布；你的农夫若在忙于谋反，他便不买奴隶耕田。可不是么——他们对我说，我返回前一个月，港里曾运进一批上好奴隶，品质之佳不亚于历来从欧罗巴贩来的任何一批，却也毫无办法，只好把他们卸在河口对岸的滩上，任他们自便，或饿死，或在那片荒地里讨生活。整个亚特兰蒂斯，再没有一个人肯多收留一个奴隶，白送也不要。

===Original===

But though I was grieved at this falling away, all schemes for remedy would be for afterwards. It would only make ill worse to speak of it as we rode together in the litter. I was growing to know Phorenice’s moods enough for that. Still, I think that she too had studied mine, and did her best to interest me between her bursts of trifling. We went out to where the westernmost harbour wall joins the land, and there the panting bearers set us down. She led me into a little house of stone which stood by itself, built out on a promontory where there is a constant run of tide, and when we had been given admittance, after much unbarring, she showed me her new gold collectors.

===Chinese===

这些衰败景象虽然令我痛心，但一切补救方略都只好留待日后。同乘一轿之时提起这些，只会使病情更重。对此，我已渐渐摸得透福伦尼刻的性子。不过，我想她也研读过我的心思，在嬉闹玩笑的间隙，总尽力找些让我感兴趣的话头。我们一路行到最西端的港湾隔墙与陆地相接之处，气喘吁吁的轿夫在那里把我们放下。她领我走进一座孤零零的小石屋；石屋筑在一处海岬上向外伸出，那里潮流终日奔涌不休。等一道道门闩陆续卸下、我们获准进屋之后，她领我看她的新式集金所（gold collectors）。

===Original===

In the dry knowledge taught in the colleges and groves of the Sacred Mountain it had been a common fact to us that the metal gold was present in a dissolved state in all sea water, but of plans for dragging it forth into yellow hardness, none had ever been discussed. But here this field-reared upstart of an Empress had stumbled upon the trick as though it had been written in a book.

===Chinese===

在圣山的学院和林苑里传授的枯燥学问告诉我们，金属黄金以溶解之态存在于一切海水之中，这原是个人尽皆知的事实；但要说有什么法子能把它拖拽出来、凝成黄色的坚硬之物，却从未有人讨论过。可在这儿，这位田垄间养大的暴发户女皇，竟一头撞破了这诀窍，仿佛它是白纸黑字写在一本书里似的。

===Original===

She patted my arm laughingly as I stared curiously round the place. “I tell all others in Atlantis that only the Gods have this secret,” said she, “and that They gave it to me as one of themselves. But I am no Goddess to you, am I, Deucalion? And, by my face! I have no other explanation of how this plan was invented. We’ll suppose I must have dreamed it. Look! The seawater sluices in through that culvert, and passes over these rough metal plates set in the floor, and then flows out again yonder in its natural course. You see the yellow metal caught in the ridges of the plates? That is gold. And my fellows here melt it with fire into bars, and take it to my smith’s in the city. The tides vary constantly, as you priests know well, as the quiet moon draws them, and it does not take much figuring to know how much of the sea passes through these culverts in a month and how much gold to a grain should be caught in the plates. My fellows here at first thought to cheat me, but I towed two of them in the water once behind a galley till the cannibal fish ate them, and since then the others have given me credit for﻿—for what do you think?”

===Chinese===

我好奇地四下打量这地方时，她笑着拍我的手臂。「我对亚特兰蒂斯所有其余的人都说，这秘密唯有诸神才有，」她道，「还说他们把它给了我，把我当诸神中的一位。可在你眼里我并不是女神，对吧，德乌卡利翁？再说，凭我这张脸起誓！这法子是怎么想出来的，我自己也拿不出别的解释。我们就假定我准是梦来的吧。瞧！海水从那条暗渠里冲进来，淌过铺在地面上的这些粗糙金属板，再顺着天然的流向从那边流出去。你看见板槽里兜住的黄色金属了吗？那就是黄金。我这些伙计在这儿用火把它熔成金条，送到城里的金匠作坊去。潮汐起落不定，这一点你们祭司最清楚——宁静的月亮牵引着它们——稍稍一算便知道，一个月里有多少海水流过这些暗渠，板里按理该兜住多少黄金、精确到几厘几毫。我这儿的手下起先还想欺瞒我，可我有一回把他们两个拴在水里，拖在一条桨船后面，直到食人鱼（cannibal fish）把他们吃了个干净。打那以后，其余的人便信了我——你猜信了我什么？」

===Original===

“More divinity.”

===Chinese===

「更多的神性。」

===Original===

“I suppose it is that. But I am letting you see how it is done. Just have the head to work out a little sum, and see what an effect can be gained. You will be a God yet yourself, Deucalion, with these silly Atlanteans, if only you will use your wit and cleverness.”

===Chinese===

「我猜也就是这么回事。不过我这是让你瞧瞧它是怎么弄成的。只消动动脑子，算一笔小小的账，看看能收多大的成效。德乌卡利翁，只要你肯使出你的机智与聪明，在这些蠢笨的亚特兰蒂斯人当中，你自己迟早也会成一位神。」

===Original===

Was she laughing at me? Was she in earnest? I could not tell. Sometimes she pointed out that her success and triumphs were merely the reward of thought and brilliancy, and next moment she gave me some impossible explanation and left me to deduce that she must be more than mortal or the thing could never have been found. In good truth, this little woman with her supple mind and her supple body mystified me more and more the longer I stayed by her side; and more and more despairing did I grow that Atlantis could ever be restored by my agency to peace and the ancient Gods, even after I had carried out the commands of the High Council, and taken her to wife.

===Chinese===

她是在嘲弄我吗？还是当真的？我说不准。有时她指出，她的成功与凯旋不过是思想与才思的报偿；下一刻，她又给我一个根本讲不通的解释，让我自己去推想：她必定非止凡人，否则这法子绝无从发现。说实在的，这个心智柔韧、身段也柔韧的小女人，我在她身边待得越久，就越发被她弄得云山雾罩；而想凭我之手让亚特兰蒂斯重归太平、重回古老诸神之怀，我也越发灰心——哪怕在我执行了祭司最高议事会的训令、娶她为妻之后，也是如此。

===Original===

Only one plan seemed humanly possible, and that was to curb her further mischievousness by death and then leave the wretched country naturally to recover. It was just a dagger-stroke, and the thing was done. Yet the very idea of this revolted me, and when the desperate thought came to my mind (which it did ever and anon), I hugged to myself the answer that if it were fitting to do this thing, the High Gods in Their infinite wisdom would surely have put definite commands upon me for its carrying out.

===Chinese===

人力可行的方略看来只有一条：用死亡遏住她进一步的为祸，然后让这可怜的国家自然而然地休养生息。不过一匕首下去，事情便告完成。可这念头本身就令我憎恶；每当这孤注一掷的想法又冒上心头（它总是不时冒上来），我便抱着这样一句答复自我宽慰：倘若此举当真合宜，至上诸神以其无穷的智慧，必定早已给我下达明令，命我执行。

===Original===

Yet, such was the fascination of Phorenice, that when presently we left her gold collectors, and stumbled into such peril, that a little withholding of my hand would have gained her a passage to the nether Gods, I found myself fighting when she called upon me, as seldom I have fought before. And though, of course, some blame for this must be laid upon that lust of battle which thrills even the coldest of us when blows begin to whistle and war-cries start to ring, there is no doubt also that the pleasure of protecting Phorenice, and the distaste for seeing her pulled down by those rude, uncouth fishers put special nerve and vehemence into my blows.

===Chinese===

然而福伦尼刻的魔力就是如此：随后我们离开她的集金所，一头撞进了那样的险境——只要我稍稍袖一袖手，便可送她去冥间诸神那里——可当她召唤我时，我竟奋勇厮杀，狠过我平生少有的几回。当然，这其中有一部分得归咎于那股嗜战的血热：兵刃开始呼啸、喊杀声开始震响时，连我们中间最冷静的人也会为之激颤；但同样毫无疑问的是，护卫福伦尼刻的快意，和眼看她要被那些粗野笨拙的渔人拖倒的憎厌，给我的击打添了额外的狠劲与猛力。

===Original===

The cause of the matter was the unrest and the prevalency to street violence which I have spoken of above, and the desperate poverty of the common people, which led them to take any risk if it showed them a chance of winning the wherewithal to purchase a meal. We had once more mounted the litter, and once more the bearers, with their heads beneath the pole, bore us on at their accustomed swinging trot. Phorenice was telling me about her new supplies of gold. She had made fresh sumptuary laws, it appeared.

===Chinese===

事情的起因，便是我上文说过的那种动荡与街头暴乱的盛行，再加上平民赤贫已极——只要能见到一线挣得一顿饭钱的指望，他们便肯冒任何风险。我们又上了轿，轿夫们又把头颈拱在杠下，照他们惯常那种摇摆的小跑抬着我们前行。福伦尼刻正对我讲她黄金的新进项。看样子，她又新订了几条奢侈禁令（sumptuary laws）。

===Original===

“In the old days,” said she, “when yellow gold was tediously dredged up grain by grain from river gravels in the dangerous lands, a quill full would cost a rich man’s savings, and so none but those whose high station fitted them to be so adorned could wear golden ornaments. But when the seawater gave me gold here by the double handful a day, I found that the price of these river hoards decreased, and one day﻿—could you credit it?﻿—a common fellow, who was one of my smiths, came to me wearing a collar of yellow gold on his own common neck. Well, I had that neck divided, as payment for his presumption; and as I promised to repeat the division promptly on all other offenders, that special species of forwardness seems to be checked for the time. There are many exasperations, Deucalion, in governing these common people.”

===Chinese===

「旧时，」她道，「黄澄澄的金子，要人们在险地的河沙里一粒一粒烦闷地淘出来，一小羽毛管的分量便要吃掉一个富人的积蓄，所以唯有多少高门显位、配得上这般装点的人，才佩戴黄金饰物。可自从海水在这儿一天给我两捧两捧地送上黄金，我便发觉这些河藏的价钱跌了下来。后来有一天——你能信么？——一个下等人，我金匠作坊里的一个匠人，竟在自己那根下等脖颈上戴着一圈黄金领圈来见我。好啊，我便叫人把那根脖子分了家，算作对他僭越的偿价；我又许下话来，其余犯规的人一律照此即刻分家，于是这一路冒进的歪风，眼下总算是刹住了。德乌卡利翁，治理这些小民，气人的事可多着呢。」

===Original===

She had other things to say upon the matter, but at this point I saw two clumsy boats of fishers paddling to us from over the ripples, and at the same time amongst the narrow lanes which led between the houses on the other side of us, savage-faced men were beginning to run after the litter in threatening clusters.

===Chinese===

这事她还有别的话要说，可就在这时，我看见两条渔人的笨拙小船拨开涟漪朝我们划来；与此同时，在我们另一侧房屋之间的窄巷里，凶相毕露的汉子们正成群结伙、气势汹汹地追赶着轿子。

===Original===

“With permission,” I said, “I will step out of the conveyance and scatter this rabble.”

===Chinese===

「还请恕我自专，」我说，「我这就下轿，把这群乌合之众轰散。」

===Original===

“Oh, the people always cluster round me. Poor ugly souls, they seem to take a strange delight in coming to stare at my pretty looks. But scatter them. I have said I did not wish to be followed. I am taking holiday now, Deucalion, am I not, whilst you learn to woo me?”

===Chinese===

「哦，百姓们总要围拢到我身边来。这些可怜的丑八怪，来一睹我的花容月貌，于他们仿佛是一桩奇异的乐事。不过轰散就轰散吧。我说过我不愿被人跟着。我这会儿是在休假，德乌卡利翁，不是吗——趁着这工夫，你正好学学怎么向我求爱？」

===Original===

I stepped to the ground. The rough fishers in the boats were beginning to shout to those who dodged amongst the houses to see to it that we did not escape, and the numbers who hemmed us in on the shore side were increasing every moment. The prospect was unpleasant enough. We had come out beyond the merchants’ quarters, and were level with those small huts of mud and grass which the fishing population deem sufficient for shelter, and which has always been a spot where turbulence might be expected. Indeed, even in those days of peace and good government in the old King’s time, this part of the city had rarely been without its weekly riot.

===Chinese===

我跨到地上。船里那些粗野的渔人开始朝房屋间躲闪的同伙吆喝，叫他们看住我们，别让我们逃脱；从岸上一面围拢过来的人数，每一刻都在增加。前景相当不妙。我们已行出商贾聚居的地界，到了与那些泥墙草顶小屋平齐的地方——打渔人家觉得这样的栖身之屋已尽够用——而这一带从来就是个可能闹乱子的所在。说真的，即便在老国王治下太平善政的那些年月，城中的这一隅也很少有哪个星期不闹上一场暴乱。

===Original===

The life of the fisherman is the most hard that any human toilers have to endure. Violence from the wind and waves, and pelting from firestones out of the sky are their daily portion; the great beasts that dwell in the seas hunt them with savage persistence, and it is a rare day when at least someone of the fishers’ guild fails to come home to answer the tally.

===Chinese===

渔人的生活，是人间一切劳作者当中最苦的一种。风浪的暴虐、天上火石（firestones）的砸击，是他们每日的定份；栖居海中的巨兽怀着凶残的执拗猎食他们，渔人行会（fishers' guild）里难得有一天不至少短少一个人回来应点。

===Original===

Moreover, the manner which prevails of catching fish is not without its risks.

===Chinese===

再者，时下通行的捕鱼之法，也并非全无风险。

===Original===

To each man there is a large sea-fowl taken as a nestling, and trained to the work. A ring of bronze is round its neck to prevent its swallowing the spoil for which it dives, and for each fish it takes and flies back with to the boat, the head and tail and inwards are given to it for a reward, the ring being removed whilst it makes the meal.

===Chinese===

每个渔人手里都有一只雏鸟时便捉来、训练熟了干这活计的大海鸟（sea-fowl）。它颈上箍着一圈青铜环，免得它把潜下水去捉的渔获吞进自己肚里；它每衔起一条鱼飞回船边，人们便把鱼头、鱼尾和内脏赏给它作犒劳，让它进餐时取下铜环。

===Original===

The birds are faithful, once they have got a training, and are seldom known to desert their owners; but, although the fishers treat them more kindly than they do their wives, or children of their own begetting, the life of the birds is precarious like that of their masters. The larger beasts and fish of the sea prey on them as they prey on the smaller fish, and so whatever care may be lavished upon them, they are most liable to sudden cutting off.

===Chinese===

这些鸟一旦受了训练，便很忠心，鲜有背弃主人的名声；不过，渔人待它们虽比待自己的妻儿还亲厚，鸟的性命却和主人一样朝不保夕。海中更大的巨兽和大鱼捕食它们，正如它们捕食更小的鱼；所以无论人们把怎样的疼爱倾注在它们身上，它们总是极易骤然丧命。

===Original===

And here is another thing that makes the life of the fisher most precarious: if his fishing bird be slain, and the second which he has in training also come by ill fortune, he is left suddenly bereft of all utensils of livelihood, and (for aught his guild-fellows care) he may go starve. For these fishers hold that the Gods of the sea regulate their craft, and that if one is not pleasing to Them They rob him of his birds; after which it would be impious to have any truck or dealing with such a fellow; and accordingly he is left to starve or rob as he chooses.

===Chinese===

还有一桩事，把渔人的性命愈发悬在一线：他的渔鸟（fishing bird）若被杀了，正在训练的第二只又遭了横祸，他便骤然丧尽了一切谋生的家伙什，（行会的同伙是不管的）便只好挨饿去。因为这些渔人认定，海中诸神掌管着他们这门营生；谁若不讨诸神的欢心，诸神便夺走他的鸟；此后再与这等人有半点瓜葛往来，便是渎神；于是他就被撇下，或饿死，或为盗，任凭他自选。

===Original===

All of which circumstances tend to make the fishers rude, desperate men, who have been forced into the trade because all other callings have rejected them. They are fellows, moreover, who will spend the gains of a month on a night’s debauch, for fear that the morrow will rob them of life and the chance of spending; and, moreover, it is their one point of honour to be curbed in no desire by an ordinary fear of consequences. As will appear.

===Chinese===

凡此种种，都把渔人陶冶成粗野亡命之徒——他们是被一切别的行当拒之门外，才被迫吃上这碗饭的。再说，这帮家伙肯把一个月的进项花在一夜的荒唐上，生怕明朝夺走他们的性命和花钱的机会；而且，不受寻常的后怕拘束，正是他们唯一讲究的荣誉点。下文自见分晓。

===Original===

I went quickly towards the largest knot of these people, who were skulking behind the houses, leaving the litter halted in the path behind me, and I bade them sharply enough to disperse. “For an employment,” I added, “put your houses in order, and clean the fish offal from the lanes between them. Tomorrow I will come round here to inspect, and put this quarter into a better order. But for today the Empress (whose name be adored) wishes for a privacy, so cease your staring.”

===Chinese===

我快步走向人堆最密的一团——他们正躲在房屋后面探头探脑——把轿子留在身后的小径上停着，厉声喝令他们散开。「给你们派个正经活计，」我添了一句，「把各自的屋子收拾利落，把巷子里的鱼肚鱼肠清扫干净。明天我会来这一带巡查，把这地方整顿得像样些。至于今天，女皇（愿她的圣名受万世敬仰）要图个清静，所以收起你们那副贼眼。」

===Original===

“Then give us money,” said a shrill voice from amongst the huts.

===Chinese===

「那就给钱，」小屋之间一个尖利的嗓门嚷道。

===Original===

“I will send you a torch in an hour’s time,” I said grimly, “and rig you a gallows, if you give me more annoyance. To your kennels, you!”

===Chinese===

「一个钟头后，我就给你们送一支火把，」我冷冷道，「你们再敢搅扰，我还给你们搭一座绞架。滚回你们的狗窝去！」

===Original===

I think they would have obeyed the voice of authority if they had been left to themselves. There was a quick stir amongst them. Those that stood in the sunlight instinctively slipped into the shadow, and many dodged into the houses and cowered in dark corners out of sight. But the men in the two hide-covered fisher-boats that were paddling up, called them back with boisterous cries.

===Chinese===

我想，倘若任其自便，他们是会服从这威严的声口的。人群里起了一阵快速的骚动。站在日头底下的那些人本能地溜进了阴影，许多人闪身躲进屋里，缩在看不见的黑暗角落里。可是那两条蒙着兽皮的渔船划上来，船里的汉子们又用粗声大气的呼喊把他们召了回去。

===Original===

I signed to the litter-bearers to move on quickly along their road. There was need of discipline here, and I was minded to deal it out myself with a firm hand. I judged that I could prevent them following the Empress, but if she still remained as a glittering bait for them to rob, and I had to protect her also, it might be that my work would not be done so effectively.

===Chinese===

我打手势叫轿夫们顺路赶紧前行。此地需要一顿教训，我打算亲手用铁腕发放。我料定自己能够拦住他们不追女皇；可她若还留在那里，当着一件亮闪闪的诱人赃物，我又得分神护她，那我的活计兴许就干不那么利索了。

===Original===

But it seems I was presumptuous in giving an order which dealt with the person of Phorenice. She bade the bearers stand where they were, and stepped out, and drew her weapons from beneath the cushions. She came towards me strapping a sword on to her hip, and carrying a well-dinted target of gold on her left forearm. “An unfair trick,” cries she, laughing. “If you will keep a fight to yourself now, Deucalion, where will your greediness carry you when I am your shrinking, wistful little wife? Are these fools truly going to stand up against us?”

===Chinese===

可看来，我竟擅自发号施令，管到福伦尼刻头上，是僭越了。她吩咐轿夫原地站住，自己跨出轿来，从坐垫底下抽出兵器。她朝我走来，一面把一柄剑挂上胯骨，左小臂上挎着一面瘢痕累累的金圆盾。「不地道呀，」她笑着嚷道，「德乌卡利翁，你现在就把一场厮杀独吞了，等我成了你那位娇怯怯、意切切的小娘子，你的贪心还不知要涨到哪一步去呢？这些蠢货当真要跟我们动手吗？」

===Original===

I was not coveting a fight, but it seemed as if there would be no avoidance of it now. The robe and the glittering gauds of which Phorenice had recently despoiled the merchant, drew the eyes of these people with keen attraction. The fishers in the boats paddled into the surf which edged the beach, and leaped overside and left the frail basket-work structures to be spewed up sound or smashed, as chance ordered. And from the houses, and from the filthy lanes between them, poured out hordes of others, women mixed with the men, gathering round us threateningly.

===Chinese===

我并不贪求一场厮杀，可眼下属实已无从避开了。福伦尼刻新近从那商人身上剥来的衣袍与亮闪闪的饰物，把这干人眼里的贪火勾得炽旺。船上的渔人把船划进镶在滩边的浪沫里，翻身越过船舷跳下，任那两副脆弱的柳条骨架凭浪头摆布，或完好吐上岸，或砸个粉碎。而从那些房屋里、从房屋之间污秽的窄巷里，又涌出一伙伙旁的人，女的混在男的当中，气势汹汹地朝我们围拢过来。

===Original===

“Have a care,” shouted one on the outskirts of the crowd. “She called down fire for the sacrifice once today, and she can burn up others here if she chooses.”

===Chinese===

「留神，」人群外圈有一人高喊，「她今天已为祭牲引过一回天火了；她要是乐意，在这儿也能把其余的人烧成灰。」

===Original===

“So much the more for those that are left,” retorted another. “She cannot burn all.”

===Chinese===

「那剩下的人不正好多得些，」另一人反唇相讥，「她又烧不尽所有人。」

===Original===

“Nay, I will not burn any,” said Phorenice, “but you shall look upon my swordplay till you are tired.”

===Chinese===

「不，我一个也不烧，」福伦尼刻道，「只是我的剑法，你们要看到腻烦为止。」

===Original===

I heard her say that with some malicious amusement, knowing (as one of the Seven) how she had called down the fires of the sky to burn that cloven-hoofed horse offered in sacrifice, and knowing too, full well, that she could bring down no fire here. But they gave us little enough time for wordy courtesies. Their Empress never went far unattended, and, for aught the wretches knew, an escort might be close behind. So what pilfering they did, it behoved them to get done quickly.

===Chinese===

听她这话，我心里怀着几分恶意的兴味——我深知她是怎样引下天火，烧掉那匹献作祭牲的偶蹄马的，也深知她在这里半点天火也引不下来。可他们没给我们多少工夫来斗口客套。他们的女皇出行从不会走远而无随侍，这些可怜虫拿不准扈从是不是就跟在近处。所以，要偷要抢，便得赶紧下手。

===Original===

They closed in, jostling one another to be first, and the reek of their filthy bodies made us cough. A grimy hand launched out to seize some of the jewels which flashed on Phorenice’s breast, and I lopped it off at the elbow, so that it fell at her feet, and a second later we were engaged.

===Chinese===

他们围逼上来，你推我搡抢在前头，那一身身脏臭皮囊的气味熏得我们直咳嗽。一只污黑的手探出来，要抓福伦尼刻胸前闪亮的珠宝，我一剑把它从肘部剁了下来，那手便落在她脚边；下一瞬，我们便交上了手。

===Original===

“Your back to mine, comrade,” cried she, with a laugh, and then drew and laid about her with fine dexterity. Bah! but it was mere slaughter, that first bout.

===Chinese===

「你的背贴上我的背，伙计，」她笑着喊道，随即拔剑在手，身手伶俐地四下里挥砍。呸！头一回合，简直就是一面倒的屠戮。

===Original===

The crowd hustled inwards with such greediness to seize what they could, that none had space to draw back elbow for a thrust, and we two kept a circle round us by sheer whirling of steel. It is necessary to do one’s work cleanly in these bouts, as wounded left on the ground unnoticed before one are as dangerous as so many snakes. But as we circled round in our battling I noted that all of Phorenice’s quarry lay peaceful and still. By the Gods! but she could play a fine sword, this dainty Empress. She touched life with every thrust.

===Chinese===

人群贪婪地向里挤，人人都想抢捞一把，结果谁也没有抽肘送剑的空当，我们两个全凭一片翻飞的刀光剑影，在身周守住了一个圆圈。这等混战里，活必须干得干净利落，因为落在身前地上、无人留意的伤者，就像一条条毒蛇一样危险。可我在厮杀中转着圈时，留意到福伦尼刻剑下的猎物个个躺得安安静静。诸神啊！这位玲珑娇艳的女皇，一手剑耍得真叫漂亮。她每一刺都点到命门。

===Original===

Yes, it was plain to see, now an example was given, that the throne of Atlantis had been won, not by a lovely face and a subtle tongue alone; and (as a fighter myself) I did not like Phorenice the less for the knowledge. I could but see her out of the corner of my eye, and that only now and again, for the fishers, despite their ill-knowledge of fence, and the clumsiness of their weapons, had heavy numbers, and most savage ferocity; and as they made so confident of being able to pull us down, it required more than a little hard battling to keep them from doing it. Ay, by the Gods! it was at times a fight my heart warmed to, and if I had not contrived to pluck a shield from one fool who came too vain-gloriously near me with one, I could not swear they would not have dragged me down by sheer ravening savageness.

===Chinese===

是的，如今有了实例摆在眼前，谁都看得分明：亚特兰蒂斯的御座，并非单凭一张俏脸和一条巧舌赢来的；而我自己也是习武之人，知道了这一点，对福伦尼刻的喜欢非但不减，反而更增。我只能用眼角余光瞥她，还只能是偶尔一瞥，因为这些渔人虽然剑术拙劣、兵器粗笨，人数却压得死沉，凶暴至极；他们既那般自信能把我们扑倒，要叫他们扑不成，就少不了一场硬仗。是啊，凭诸神起誓！这一场厮杀有时打得我心头发热；倘若我没有从一个过于逞英雄、举着盾逼得太近的蠢货手里夺下一面盾，我可不敢起誓说，他们那种饿狼般的凶蛮就不会把我生生拖倒。

===Original===

And always above the burly uproar of the fight came very pleasantly to my ears Phorenice’s cry of “Deucalion!” which she chose as her battle shout. I knew her, of course, to be a past-mistress of the art of compliment, and it was no new thing for me to hear the name roared out above a battle din, but it was given there under circumstances which were peculiar, and for the life of me I could not help being tickled by the flattery.

===Chinese===

而在那场打斗粗粝的喧腾之上，福伦尼刻一声声「德乌卡利翁！」总是悦耳地传进我耳中——她把它选作了自己的战场呐喊。我自然知道，她是谀颂之道的一位宗师；在战阵的喧嚣之上听人吼出这个名字，于我也并非新鲜事；可它此刻响起的情境实在别致，任我怎样克制，也禁不住被这记奉承搔得心痒。

===Original===

Condemn my weakness how you will, but I came very near then to liking the Empress of Atlantis in the way she wished. And as for that other woman who should have filled my mind, I will confess that the stress of the moment, and the fury of the engagement, had driven both her and her strait completely out beyond the marches of my memory. Of such frail stuff are we made, even those of us who esteem ourselves the strongest.

===Chinese===

随你们怎样谴责我的软弱吧，可在那一刻，我已险些照福伦尼刻指望的那般，当真喜欢上这位亚特兰蒂斯的女皇了。至于那位本该占满我心智的另一个女人，我供认不讳：那一刻的紧张和接战的狂烈，早把她和她的困境一并逐出了我记忆的边疆之外。我们就是用这样脆弱的材料做成的，连我们中间那些自诩最强的人，也不例外。

===Original===

Now it is a temptation few men born to the sword can resist, to throw themselves heart and soul into a fight for a fight’s sake, and it seems that women can be bitten with the same fierce infection. The attack slackened and halted. We stood in the middle of a ring of twisted dead, and the rest of the fishers and their women who hemmed us in shrank back out of reach of our weapons.

===Chinese===

为打而打、全身心扑进一场厮杀，这份诱惑，天生佩剑之人少有抵挡得住的；看来女人也同样会被这凶猛的热病咬中。攻势松懈了下来，停住了。我们站在一圈横七竖八的尸首中央，其余的渔人和他们的女人把我们团团围住，却缩回了我们兵器够不着的地方。

===Original===

It was the moment for a truce, and the moment when a few strong words would have sent them back cowering to their huts, and given us free passage to go where we chose. But no, this Phorenice must needs sing a hymn to her sword and mine, gloating over our feats and invulnerability; and then she must needs ask payment for the bearers of her litter whom they had killed, and then speak balefully of the burnings, and the skinnings, and the sawings asunder with which this fishers’ quarter would be treated in the near future, till they learned the virtues of deportment and genteel manners.

===Chinese===

这正是讲和的时刻，也是几句斩钉截铁的话便可把他们赶回小屋瑟缩、给我们让出随便去哪儿的通路的时刻。可是不，我们这位福伦尼刻偏要对着她和我两柄剑唱一首颂歌，夸耀我们的武功和刀枪不入；接着又偏要向他们讨还轿夫的人命钱——轿夫已叫他们杀了；然后又恶狠狠地历数这渔人区不久将受的待遇：烧的烧，剥皮的剥皮，锯成两截的锯成两截，直到他们学会举止规矩与斯文礼数的种种美德。

===Original===

“It makes your backs creep, does it?” said Phorenice. “I do not wonder. This severity must have its unpleasant side. But why do you not put it beyond my power to give the order? Either you must think yourselves Gods or me no Goddess, or you would not have gone on so far. Come now, you nasty-smelling people, follow out your theory, and if you make a good fight of it, I swear by my face I will be lenient with those who do not fall.”

===Chinese===

「听得脊背发凉了，是吗？」福伦尼刻道，「我不奇怪。这等严刑总免不了有叫人不痛快的一面。可你们为什么不干脆叫我下不了这道命令呢？要不是你们把自己当成诸神，或把我当成假女神，断不至于闹到这般地步。来吧，你们这些浑身臭气的人，把你们的道理行到底；要是你们打得像样，我凭我这张脸起誓，对倒不下的人一定从宽发落。」

===Original===

But there was no pressing up to meet our swords. They still ringed us in, savage and sullen, beyond the ring of their own dead, and would neither run back to the houses, nor give us the game of further fight. There was a certain stubborn bravery about them that one could not but admire, and for myself I determined that next time it became my duty to raise troops, I would catch a handful of these men, and teach them handiness with the utensils of war, and train them to loyalty and faithfulness. But presently from behind their ranks a stone flew, and though it whizzed between the Empress and myself, and struck down a fisher, it showed that they had brought a new method into their attack, and it behoved us to take thought and meet it.

===Chinese===

可是并没有人上来迎战我们的剑。他们仍旧把我们围在圈里，凶悍而阴沉，隔着自家死人的那道环，既不肯逃回屋去，也不肯再给我们一场厮杀的戏乐。他们身上有一股子倔强的胆气，令人不能不佩服；就我自己而言，我已打定主意，下回轮到我奉命募兵，定要抓来一小撮这样的人，教他们熟练运用战争的器械，把他们训得忠贞可靠。可不多时，一石块从他们队伍后面飞来，虽则它嗖地从女皇和我之间穿过去，放倒了一个渔人，却表明他们已给进攻添了新招，我们不得不掂量掂量、想个对策。

===Original===

I looked round me up and down the beach. There was no sign of a rescue. “Phorenice,” I said in the court tongue, which these barbarous fishers would know little enough of, “I take it that a whiff of the sea-breeze would come very pleasant after all this warm play. As you can show such pretty sword work, will you cut me a way down to the beach, and I will do my poor best to keep these creatures from snapping at our heels?”

===Chinese===

我朝滩头滩尾环顾了一遍。全无救兵的踪影。「福伦尼刻，」我用宫廷语（court tongue）说——这些野蛮渔人听不懂几句——「我想，经过这一场热身的戏耍，来一口海风必定十分受用。你的剑招这般漂亮，可否替我砍开一条通到滩头的路？我也好尽我菲薄之力，防着这些畜生咬我们的脚跟？」

===Original===

“Oh!” cried she. “Then I am to have a courtier for a husband after all. Why have you kept back your flattering speeches till now? Is that your trick to make me love you?”

===Chinese===

「哦！」她叫起来，「这么说，我到底还是要嫁一个朝臣做丈夫了。你怎么把奉承话扣到现在才说？这就是你哄我爱你的手段吗？」

===Original===

“I will think out the reason for it another time.”

===Chinese===

「这缘由，我改日再想。」

===Original===

“Ah, these stern, commanding husbands,” said she, “how they do press upon their little wives!” and with that leaped over the ring of dead before her, and cut and stabbed a way through those that stood between her and the waters which creamed and crashed upon the beach. Gods! what a charge she made. It made me tingle with admiration as I followed sideways behind her, guarding the rear. And I am a man that has spent so many years in battling, that it takes something far out of the common to move me to any enthusiasm in this matter.

===Chinese===

「啊，这些威风凛凛、发号施令的丈夫们，」她道，「他们压起自家小娘子来可真够呛！」话音未落，她已跃过身前的死人环，又劈又刺，从拦在她与那拍岸翻腾的浪头之间的人堆里杀出一条路来。诸神啊！她那一冲何等了得。我在她侧后方跟着、断后，佩服得浑身发麻。我这个人，戎马半生，要在这上头激起半点热忱，非得有些远超寻常的东西不可。

===Original===

There were two boats creaking and washing about in the edge of the surf, but in one, happily, the wickerwork which made its frame was crushed by the weight of the waves into a shapeless bundle of sticks, and would take half a day to replace. So that, let us but get the other craft afloat, and we should be free from further embroiling. But the fishers were quick to see the object of this new manoeuvre. “Guard the boat,” they shouted. “Smash her; slit her skin with your knives! Tear her with your fingers! Swim her out to sea! Oh, at least take the paddles!”

===Chinese===

浪沫边上还有两条船在吱嘎摇晃，幸而其中一条的柳条骨架已被浪头的分量压成一捆不成形的柴棒，修好它得费半天工夫。这样一来，只要我们把另一条弄下水，便可脱开这场纠缠。可渔人们眼尖，一眼看穿这新调度的用意。「守住船，」他们大喊，「砸烂它！拿刀子割开它的皮！用手指撕了它！把它拖到海里去！哦，至少把桨抢走！」

===Original===

But, if these clumsy fishers could run, Phorenice was like a legged snake for speed. She was down beside the boat before any could reach it, laughing and shouting out that she could beat them at every point. Myself, I was slower of foot; and, besides, there was some that offered me a fight on the road, and I was not wishful to baulk them; and moreover, the fewer we left clamouring behind, the fewer there would be to speed our going with their stones. Still I came to the beach in good order, and laid hands on the flimsy boat and tipped her dry.

===Chinese===

不过，这些笨手笨脚的渔人固然能跑，比起脚程，福伦尼刻却像一条长了腿的蛇。谁都还没挨着船边，她已先到了，一路笑着嚷着，说样样都要赢过他们。我自己脚头慢些；再者，路上还有人主动要陪我过招，我也不愿拂了他们的好意；何况，留在身后叫嚣的越少，用石块给我们的离场助兴的也就越少。饶是如此，我仍好整以暇地到了海滩边，一把攥住那条单薄的小船，把里面的水倒干。

===Original===

“Fighting is no trade for me,” I cried, “whilst you are here, Phorenice. Guard me my back and walk out into the water.”

===Chinese===

「有你在这儿，福伦尼刻，厮杀这行当我就不干了，」我喊道，「替我守住背后，往水里走吧。」

===Original===

I took the boat, thrusting it afloat, and wading with it till two lines of the surf were past. The fishers swarmed round us, active as fish in their native element, and strove mightily to get hands on the boat and slit the hides which covered it with their eager fingers. But I had a spare hand, and a short stabbing-knife for such close-quarter work, and here, there, and everywhere was Phorenice the Empress, with her thirsty dripping sword. By the Gods! I laughed with sheer delight at seeing her art of fence.

===Chinese===

我推着船，一边趟水，直到越过两道浪线。渔人们围着我们蜂拥打转，在水里灵活得如同游鱼归了本行，拼命要把手搭上船舷，用急切的指头割开蒙船的兽皮。好在我还腾得出一只手，还有一柄专用于这等贴身近斗的短刺刀；而女皇福伦尼刻东一剑西一剑，无处不在，那柄饮血滴水的剑从不停歇。凭诸神起誓！看她这身剑术，我由衷地笑出了声。

===Original===

But the swirl of a great fish into the shallows, and the squeal of a fisher as he was dragged down and home away into the deep, made me mindful of foes that no skill can conquer, and no bravery avoid. Without taking time to give the Empress a word of warning, I stooped, and flung an arm round her, and threw her up out of the water into the boat, and then thrust on with all my might, driving the flimsy craft out to sea, whilst my legs crept under me for fear of the beasts which swam invisible beneath the muddied waters.

===Chinese===

可是一条大鱼搅着水花卷进浅滩，一个渔人尖叫着被拖下去、拖进深水老巢，这景象提醒了我：还有技能无从战胜、勇力无从躲开的对头。我来不及给女皇一句警告，便俯下身，一把搂住她，把她从水里抛进船中，随即拼尽全力往前推，把这条单薄的小船直往外海送去；与此同时，我的两条腿在身子底下直打软——唯恐那些看不见的巨兽正在搅浑的水底游弋。

===Original===

To the fishers, inured to these horrid perils by daily association, the seizing of one of their number meant little, and they pressed on, careless of their dull lives, eager only to snatch the jewels which still flaunted on Phorenice’s breast. Of the vengeance that might come after they recked nothing; let them but get the wherewithal for one night’s good debauch, and they would forget that such a thing as the morning of a morrow could have existence.

===Chinese===

渔人们日日与这类可怖的凶险厮混，早已处之泰然，同类被卷走一个，在他们算不得什么；他们继续扑上来，把自家那条贱命置之度外，眼里只惦着福伦尼刻胸前仍招摇闪亮的珠宝。事后会遭什么报复，他们全不理会；只要捞到够痛痛快快荒唐一夜的盘缠，他们连「明天还有早晨」这种事的存在，都会忘个一干二净。

===Original===

Two fellows I caught and killed that, diving down beneath, tried to slit the skin of the boat out of sight under the water; and Phorenice cared for all those that tried to put a hand on the gunwales. Yes, and she did more than that. A huge long-necked turtle that was stirred out of the mud by the turmoil, came up to daylight, and swung its great horn-lipped mouth to this side and that, seeking for a prey. The fishers near it dodged and dived. I, thrusting at the stern of the boat, could only hope it would pass me by and so offered an easy mark. It scurried towards me, champing its noisy lips, and beating the water into spray with its flippers.

===Chinese===

有两个家伙潜到水下，想在水底人不知鬼不觉地割开船皮，叫我逮住杀了；凡是想往船舷上搭一搭手的，福伦尼刻都一一打发了。是的，她干的不止这些。一头被这场骚乱从泥里搅起的巨型长颈龟浮上亮水，那张角质阔嘴左右摆荡，寻觅猎物。它近旁的渔人闪转腾挪、扎水逃避。我在船尾推着船，只能指望它放我过去，因为我正是一个现成的易袭之靶。它咂着那张聒噪的嘴朝我冲来，鳍肢把水拍成飞沫。

===Original===

But Phorenice was quick with a remedy and a rescue. She passed her sword through one of the fishers that pressed her, and then thrust the body towards the turtle. The great neck swooped towards it; the long slimy feelers which protruded from its head quivered and snuffled; and then the horny green jaws crunched on it, and drew it down out of sight.

===Chinese===

可福伦尼刻救急解围来得飞快。她一剑捅穿一个逼近她的渔人，随即把那尸首朝巨龟推了过去。那巨颈倏地俯冲下来；头上探出的黏滑长须颤动着四下嗅探；随即那两片角质的绿颚咔嚓一合，把它拖下水去，没了踪影。

===Original===

The boat was in deep water now, and Phorenice called upon me to come in over the side, she the while balancing nicely so that the flimsy thing should not be overset. However, Phorenice crouched in the stern, holding the two shields﻿—her own golden target, and the rough hide buckler I had won﻿—and so protected both of us whilst I paddled, and though many stones clattered against the shields, and hit the hide covering of the boat, so that it resounded like a drum, none of them did damage, and we drew quickly out of their range.

===Chinese===

船已进了深水，福伦尼刻唤我从船舷翻进去，她则细心地稳着重心，免得这单薄的玩意儿翻掉。渔人们已放弃了追赶——他们发现，只要进入女皇这柄可怕长剑的挥及范围，挣到的便只有断臂和开花的脸——于是便改为成阵投掷尖棱的石块，指望砸晕我们，或把船砸裂。好在福伦尼刻蹲在船尾，高举那两面盾——她自己那面金圆盾，和我夺来的那面粗皮盾——在我划桨时把我们两个都护住了；许多石块哐哐当当地砸在盾上，砸在蒙船的兽皮上，震得船皮咚咚如鼓，却没有一块伤得了我们，我们很快便驶出了他们的射程。
