## XII. The Drug of Our Lady the Moon / 十二　月神夫人的迷药
===Original===

Our Lord the Sun was riding towards the end of His day, and the smoke from a burning mountain fanned black and forbidding before His face. Phorenice wrung the water from her clothes and shivered. “Work hard with those paddles, Deucalion, and take me in through the water-gate and let me be restored to my comforts again. That merchant would rue if he saw how his pretty garments were spoiled, and I rue, too, being a woman, and remembering that he at least has no others I can take in place of these.” She looked at me sidelong, tossing back the short red hair from her eyes. “What think you of my wisdom in coming where we have come without an escort?”

===Chinese===

太阳神主正驰向祂这一日的尽头，一座燃烧的山喷出的浓烟，黑沉沉地在祂面前扇展开来，大煞风景。福伦尼刻拧着衣裳上的水，打了个寒噤。「德乌卡利翁，好好划你的桨，带我进水门，让我重新回到我的安逸里去。那商人若是瞧见他那身漂亮衣裳糟蹋成了什么样子，准会懊悔不迭；我是个女人，也懊悔——何况我还记得，他家里再没有旁的衣裳，可供我拿来替换这一身。」她斜眼看我，把挡眼的短红发甩了回去。「我们这样不带一名护卫就跑到此地来，你对我这份明智作何评价？」

===Original===

“The Empress can do no wrong,” I quoted the old formula with a smile.

===Chinese===

「女皇是不会做错的。」我微笑着引了那句老套话。

===Original===

“At least I have shown you that I can fight. I caught you looking your approval of me quite pleasantly once or twice. You were a difficult man to thaw, Deucalion, but you warm perceptibly as you keep on being near me. La, sir, we shall be a pair of rustic sweethearts yet, if this goes on. I am glad I thought of the device of going near those smelly fishers.”

===Chinese===

「至少我已经向你表明，我是能打仗的。有一两回，我还撞见你目光里颇为愉快地流露出对我的赞许。你这人很难焐热，德乌卡利翁，可你在我身边待得久了，也明显地回暖了。哎呀，先生，再这样下去，我们真要变成一对乡下情侣了。幸亏我灵机一动，想出到那些臭烘烘的渔人跟前去的妙计。」

===Original===

So she had taken me out in the litter unattended for the plain purpose of inviting a fight, and showing me her skill at arms, and perhaps, too, of seeing in person how I also carried myself in a moment of stress. Well, if we were to live on together as husband and wife, it was good that each should know to a nicety the other’s powers; and also, I am too much of an old battler and too much enamoured with the glorious handling of arms to quarrel very deeply with anyone who offers me a tough upstanding fight. Still for the life of me, I could not help comparing Phorenice with another woman. With a similar chance open before us, Nais had robbed me of the struggle through a sheer pity for those squalid rebels who did not even call her chieftain; whilst here was this Empress frittering away two score of the hardiest of her subjects merely to gratify a whim.

===Chinese===

如此说来，她不带随从，用轿子把我抬出去，明摆着就是要招惹一场厮杀，好向我炫耀她的武艺，或许还要亲眼看看我在危急关头又是如何自处的。唉，倘若我们要以夫妻之实长相厮守，那么彼此把对方的本事摸个一清二楚，倒也不坏；何况我这人本就是个老于行伍的，又太迷恋兵刃交手的荣光，谁若肯与我痛痛快快打一场硬仗，我便与谁深怨不起来。可我拼了命也忍不住，总要把福伦尼刻同另一个女人相比。当年同样的机缘摆在我们面前，娜伊丝纯出于怜悯，生生剥夺了我那一场争斗——怜悯那帮连酋长都不肯认她的腌臜叛军；而这边这位女皇，却只为满足一时兴致，便糟蹋掉四十名她麾下最硬朗的臣民。

===Original===

Yet, loyal to my vow as a priest, and to the commands set upon me by the high council on the Sacred Mountain, I tried to put away these wayward thoughts and comparisons. As I rowed over the swingings of the waves towards the forts which guard the harbour’s mouth, I sent prayers to the High Gods to give my tongue dexterity, and They through Their love for the country of Atlantis, and the harassed people, whom it was my deep wish to serve, granted me that power of speech which Phorenice loved. Her eyes glowed upon me as I talked.

===Chinese===

然而，我既忠于祭司的誓愿，又忠于圣山上祭司最高议事会加于我的训令，便竭力摈除这些任性的念头与比较。我划桨越过荡漾的波涛，向扼守港口入口的堡垒驶去，一路向上天祷告，求至上诸神赋予我舌头以机变；祂们出于对亚特兰蒂斯国土的眷爱，对那些我心深处渴望效劳的困顿黎民的眷爱，赐予了我那种福伦尼刻所喜爱的口才。我说话的时候，她的双眼灼灼放光地望着我。

===Original===

This beach of the fishers where we had had our passage at arms is safe from ship attack from without, by reason of a chain of jagged rocks which spring up from the deep, and run from the harbour side to the end of the city wall. The fishers know the passes, and can oftentimes get through to the open water beyond without touching a stone; or if they do see a danger of hitting on the reef, leap out and carry their light boats in their hands till the water floats them again. But here I had neither the knowledge nor the dexterity, and, thought I, now the High Gods will show finally if They wish this woman who has defiled them to reign on in Atlantis, and if also They wish me to serve as her husband.

===Chinese===

我们刚才交过手的这片渔人滩头，外面的船只攻打不到，因为有一列嶙峋的礁石从深水中拔起，从港口一侧一直延到城墙尽头。渔人们熟悉水道，往往一路蹭不到一块石头，便穿出到外面的开阔水面；倘若眼见要撞上礁石，他们便跳下水，把轻巧的小船扛在手里，走到水再度浮得起来船的地方。可我于此道既无知识，也无巧技，我心想：如今至上诸神就要最终显明，祂们究竟愿不愿意让这个玷辱了祂们的女人继续君临亚特兰蒂斯，也愿不愿意让我做她的丈夫。

===Original===

I cried these things in my heart, and waited to receive the omen. There was no half-answer. A great wave rose in the lagoon behind us, a wave such as could have only been caused by an earth tremor, and on its sleek back we were hurled forward and thrown clear of the reefs with their seaweeds licking round us, without so much as seeing a stone of the barrier. I bowed my head as I rowed on towards the harbour forts. It was plain that not yet would the High Gods take vengeance for the insults which this lovely woman had offered Them.

===Chinese===

我在心里如此呼告，静候征兆降临。回答没有半点含糊。环礁湖里在我们身后腾起一道巨浪——这样的浪，只能是地皮震颤所致——浪头光滑的脊背把我们猛地掀向前去，抛过了礁石群，海藻在我们周身舔拂，而我们竟连那道屏障的一块石头也没瞧见。我朝港口的堡垒继续划去，一路垂着头。很明显，对于这个可爱女人加于祂们的种种侮辱，至上诸神还不到报仇的时候。

===Original===

The sentries in the two forts beat drums at one another in their accustomed rotation, and in the growing dusk were going to pay little enough attention to the fishingboat which lay against the great chain clamouring to have it lowered. But luckily a pair of officers were taking the air of the evening in a stone-dropping turret of the roof of the nearer fort, and these recognised the tone of our shouts. They silenced the drums, torches were lowered to make sure of our faces, and then with a splash the great chain was dropped into the water to give us passage.

===Chinese===

两座堡垒里的哨兵按他们惯常的轮次对击着鼓，暮色渐浓，对那条贴着大铁链、嚷嚷着要放链的小渔船，本不会怎么理会。所幸有一对军官正在较近那座堡垒屋顶的投石塔楼里乘晚凉，听出了我们喊话的腔调。他们叫停了鼓，火把放下来照清了我们的脸，随后哗啦一声，大铁链沉入水中，放我们通行。

===Original===

A galley lay inside, nuzzling the harbour wall, and presently the ladder of ropes was let down from the top of the nearest fort, and a crew came down to man the oars. There were the customary changes of raiment too, given as presents by the officers of the fort, and these we put on in the cabin of the galley in place of the sodden clothes we wore. There are fevers to be gained by carrying wet clothes after sunset, and though from personal experience I have learned that these may be warded off with drugs, I noticed with some grim amusement that the Empress had sufficiently little of the Goddess about her to fear very much the ailments which are due to frail humanity.

===Chinese===

港内泊着一条桨船，正偎着港湾的堤壁，不多时，绳梯从最近那座堡垒顶上放了下来，一队桨手下船来操桨。照例还有替换的衣裳，是堡垒的军官们赠送的礼物，我们便在桨船的舱里换上，替下身上湿透的衣裳。日落之后还穿着湿衣，是会招热病的；我虽凭切身经验晓得这些病可以用药物挡开，却也带着几分冷冷的兴味注意到：女皇身上女神的成分实在够少的，竟非常害怕这些源于凡人弱质的疾病。

===Original===

The galley rowed swiftly across the calm waters of the harbour, and made fast to the rings of gold on the royal quay, and whilst we were waiting for litters to be brought, I watched a lantern lit in the boat which stood guard over Phorenice’s mammoth. The huge red beast stood shoulder-deep in the harbour water, with trunk upturned. It was tamed now, and the light of the boat’s lantern fell on the little ripples sent out by its tremblings. But I did not choose to intercede or ask mercy for it. If the mammoth sank deeper in the harbour mud, and was swallowed, I could have borne the loss with equanimity.

===Chinese===

桨船飞快地划过平静的港面，系牢在王家码头的金环上。等轿子抬来的当口，我望着守看福伦尼刻那头猛犸的小船上点起了一盏灯笼。那头红色巨兽立在港水中，水深及肩，长鼻上扬。它如今驯服了，船上灯笼的光，正落在它微微战栗时荡出的一圈圈细涟上。可我不打算替它求情，也不打算替它讨饶。哪怕这猛犸陷进港湾的泥里越陷越深，整个被吞没，我也能心平气和地承受这损失。

===Original===

To tell the truth, that ride on the great beast’s back had impressed me unfavourably. In fact, it put into me a sense of helplessness that was well-nigh intolerable. Perhaps circumstances have made me unduly self-reliant: on that others must judge. But I will own to having a preference for walking on my own proper feet, as the Gods in fashioning our shapes most certainly intended. On my own feet I am able to guard my own head and neck, and have done on four continents, throughout a long and active life, and on many a thousand occasions. But on the back of that detestable mammoth, pah! I grew as nervous as a child or a dastard.

===Chinese===

说实话，在那巨兽背上骑的那一程，给我的印象很坏。它着实叫我生出一种几乎无法忍受的无助之感。或许是种种际遇把我造就得过分倚仗自己了：这要由旁人来评断。但我愿意承认，我偏爱用自己的脚走路——诸神塑造我们这副身形时，无疑正是这个用意。凭自己的一双脚，我能护住自己的头颅脖颈，在四大洲上，在漫长而活跃的一生里，在千百回的场合，我都做到了。可在那头可憎的猛犸背上——呸！我变得像个孩子、一个懦夫一样心惊胆战。

===Original===

However, I had little enough leisure for personal megrims just then. Whilst we waited, Phorenice asked the port-captain (who must needs come up officiously to make his salutations) after the disposal of Nais, and was told that she had been clapped into a dungeon beneath the royal pyramid, and the officer of the guard there had given his bond for her safekeeping.

===Chinese===

不过，那时节我哪有多少闲工夫顾自己的小性子。等候间，福伦尼刻向港务长官（他少不得又凑上来殷勤请安）问起娜伊丝的发落，得知她已被关进王家金字塔底下的地牢，那里的卫戍官已立下保状，担保她万无一失。

===Original===

“It is to be hoped he understands his work,” said the Empress. “That pretty Nais knows the pyramid better than most, and it may be he will be sent to the tormentors for putting her in a cell which had a secret outlet. You would feel pleasure if the girl escaped, Deucalion?”

===Chinese===

「但愿他懂行，」女皇说，「那个标致的娜伊丝对金字塔的熟稔胜过大多数人，说不定就因为把她关进了一间带暗出口的牢房，他自己倒要被送去见施刑的人了。这姑娘若是逃了出去，你会高兴吧，德乌卡利翁？」

===Original===

“Assuredly,” said I, knowing how useless it would be to make a secret of the matter. “I have no enmity against Nais.”

===Chinese===

「当然高兴，」我说，心里明白这件事瞒也是白瞒。「我对娜伊丝并无仇怨。」

===Original===

“But I have,” said she viciously, “and I am still minded to lock your faith to me by that wedding gift you know of.”

===Chinese===

「可我有，」她恶狠狠地说，「而且我仍旧存心要用你知道的那件婚礼赠礼，把你的忠诚锁死在我身上。」

===Original===

“The thing shall be done,” I said. “Before all, the Empress of Atlantis.”

===Chinese===

「这件事定会办到，」我说。「亚特兰蒂斯的女皇，高于一切。」

===Original===

“Poof! Deucalion, you are too stiff and formal. You ought to be mightily honoured that I condescend to be jealous of your favours. Your hand, sir, please, to help me into the litter. And now come in beside me, and keep me warm against the night air. Ho! you guards there with the torches! Keep farther back against the street walls. The perfume you are burning stifles me.”

===Chinese===

「啧！德乌卡利翁，你太僵硬、太拘板了。我肯屈尊来吃你那些恩宠的醋，你该感到无上的荣幸才是。先生，劳你的手，扶我上轿。现在进来坐到我身边来，替我挡挡夜里的寒气。喂！你们那些打火把的卫兵！退远些，靠到街墙那边去。你们熏的这香，闷死我了。」

===Original===

Again there was a feast that night in the royal banqueting-hall; again I sat beside Phorenice on the raised dais which stands beneath the symbols of the snake and the outstretched hand. What had been taken for granted before about our forthcoming relationship was this time proclaimed openly; the Empress herself acknowledged me as her husband that was to be; and all that curled and jewelled throng of courtiers hailed me as greater than themselves, by reason of this woman’s choice. There was method, too, in their salutation. Some rumour must have got about of my preference for the older and simpler habits, and there was no drinking wine to my health after the new and (as I considered) impertinent manner. Decorously, each lord and lady there came forward, and each in turn spilt a goblet at my feet; and when I called any up, whether man or woman, to receive titbits from my platter, it was eaten simply and thankfully, and not kissed or pocketed with any extravagant gesture.

===Chinese===

当夜，王家宴厅里又开了一场盛宴；我又坐在福伦尼刻身旁，坐在蛇徽与张手徽记之下的高台上。先前我们之间那层将成未成的关系，人们原本只是心照不宣，这一回却公然宣告了出去；女皇亲口认了我做她未来的丈夫；满堂鬈发油亮、浑身珠玉的朝臣，因着这个女人的拣选，都敬我为高于他们自身的人。他们的礼数中也自有章法。想是有些风声传了出去，说我不喜新巧、偏爱旧简，于是无人再按那种新派的（在我看来实属放肆的）方式为我健康举杯。在场的勋贵与命妇个个端端正正地上前，依次在我脚边泼洒一盏酒；我无论唤谁上来——男人也好，女人也好——从我的盘里领些珍馐，他们都只朴朴实实地领受致谢，再没有吻食、纳袖那一套夸张的姿态。

===Original===

The flaring jets of earth-breath showed me, too, so I thought, a plainer habit of dress, and a more sober mien amongst this thoughtless mob of banqueters. And, indeed, it must have been plain to notice, for Phorenice, leaning over till the ruddy curls on her shoulder brushed my face, chided me in a playful whisper as having usurped her high authority already.

===Chinese===

地息喷出的烈焰也让我看出——至少我自以为看出——这班不动脑子的宴客中间，衣着的习尚素净了些，神情也收敛了些。而且这变化必定显而易见，因为福伦尼刻俯身过来，肩头的红鬈发直拂到我的脸上，嬉笑着低声嗔我，说我已经僭用了她的至高权威。

===Original===

“Oh, sir,” she pleaded mockingly, “do not make your rule over us too ascetic. I have given no orders for this change, but tonight there are no perfumes in the air; the food is so plain and I have half a mind to burn the cook; and as for the clothes and gauds of these diners, by my face! they might have come straight from the old King’s reign before I stepped in here to show how tasteful could be colours on a robe, or how pretty the glint of a jewel. It’s done by no orders of mine, Deucalion. They have swung round to this change by sheer courtier instinct. Why, look at the beards of the men! There is not half the curl about many of them today that they showed with such exquisiteness yesterday. By my face! I believe they’d reap their chins tomorrow as smooth as yours, if you go on setting the fashions at this prodigious rate and I do not interfere.”

===Chinese===

「哦，先生，」她装腔作势地央告道，「你治理我们，可别太过苦行。这场变化我并未下过什么旨意，可是今夜空气里没有一丝香气；饭菜素成这个样子，我都想一把火烧了厨子；至于这些赴宴人的衣裳首饰——凭我这张脸起誓！倒像是从老国王的时代直接穿来的，那时节我还没进宫来示范：袍子上的色彩可以何等雅致，宝石的光芒可以何等明媚。这可不是奉我的旨意办的，德乌卡利翁。他们是全凭朝臣的本能，一下扭转到这变化上来的。瞧瞧这些男人的胡子！今天其中好些人胡子上的鬈曲，还抵不上昨天他们妆出的那般精妙的一半。凭我这张脸起誓！你要照这个惊人的速度继续领袖风尚、我又不出手干预的话，我相信明天他们就要把下巴刮得同你一样光了。」

===Original===

“Why hinder them if they feel more cleanly shaven?”

===Chinese===

「他们若觉得刮了胡子更清爽，何苦拦着？」

===Original===

“No, sir. There shall be only one clean chin where a beard can grow in all Atlantis, and that shall be carried by the man who is husband to the Empress. Why, my Deucalion, would you have no sumptuary laws? Would you have these good folk here and the common people outside imitate us in every cut of the hair and every fold of a garment which it pleases us to discover? Come, sir, if you and I chose to say that our sovereignty was marked only by our superior strength of arm and wit, they would hate us at once for our arrogance; whereas, if we keep apart to ourselves a few mere personal decorations, these become just objects to admire and pleasantly envy.”

===Chinese===

「不，先生。全亚特兰蒂斯，凡是长得出胡子的下巴，只许有一个是光的，那就是女皇的丈夫其人。再说，我的德乌卡利翁，难道你要连奢侈禁令也一并废了不成？难道你要让这里的好人们、让外面的平民百姓，连我们兴之所至发明的每一种发式、每一道衣褶都一一仿效？得了，先生，倘若你我宣布，我们的君权只以臂力与心智的过人作标记，他们会立刻恨我们的傲慢；反过来，我们若把区区几样贴身的饰物留给自己专用，这些东西便恰成众人钦慕、又可以艳羡得舒舒服服的对象了。」

===Original===

“You show me that there is more in the office of a ruler than meets the eye.”

===Chinese===

「你让我看到，为君之道，远不止眼睛看到的这些。」

===Original===

“And yet they tell me, and indeed show me, that you have ruled with some success.”

===Chinese===

「可他们跟我说，也确实做给我看过，你治国还颇有几分成效呢。」

===Original===

“I employed the older method. It requires a Phorenice to invent these nicer flights.”

===Chinese===

「我用的是更古老的法子。这般精微的花样，须得一个福伦尼刻才发明得出。」

===Original===

“Flatterer!” said she, and smote me playfully with the back of her little fingers on my arm. “You are becoming as great a courtier as any of them. You make me blush with your fine pleasantries, Deucalion, and there is no fan-girl here tonight to cool my cheek. I must choose me another fan-girl. But it shall not be Ylga. Ylga seems to have more of a kindness for you than I like, and if she is wise she will go live in her palace at the other side of the city, and there occupy herself with the ordering of her slaves, and the makings of embroideries. I shall not be hard on Ylga unless she forces me, but I will have no woman in this kingdom treat you with undue civility.”

===Chinese===
