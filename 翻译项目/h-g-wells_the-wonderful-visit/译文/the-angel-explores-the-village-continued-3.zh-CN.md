## XXVII The Angel Explores the Village (Continued) / 二十七 天使探访村庄（续）

那场风波过后，天使一路走过磨坊，绕到教堂后面，去细看那些墓碑。

“这儿似乎就是他们安放破残零件的地方，”天使一面读着碑文说。“古怪的词儿——relict（遗孀）！Resurgam（我必复活）！这么说来，他们并没有彻底了结。要镇住她，得堆上好大一堆石头啊。……她倒真有股子劲儿。”

“霍金斯？”天使轻声念道，……“*霍金斯？*这名字我陌生得很。……这么说，他没有死。……明明白白写着呢——归入天使军，1863 年 5 月 17 日。他想必同我在这下界一样，觉得格格不入。只是我不明白，他们为什么在这碑顶上搁那么个小罐子似的东西。真古怪！四下里还有好几个——都是小石罐，上头还搭着一幅硬邦邦的石布。”

Just then the boys came pouring out of the National School, and first one and then several stopped agape at the Angel’s crooked black figure among the white tombs. “Ent ’e gart a baak on en!” remarked one critic.

“ ’E’s got ’air like a girl!” said another.

The Angel turned towards them. He was struck by the queer little heads sticking up over the lichenous wall. He smiled faintly at their staring faces, and then turned to marvel at the iron railings that enclosed the Fitz-Jarvis tomb. “A queer air of uncertainty,” he said. “Slabs, piles of stone, these railings.﻿ ﻿… Are they afraid?﻿ ﻿… Do these Dead ever try and get up again? There’s an air of repression﻿—fortification﻿—”

===Chinese===

正在这时，男孩子们从国民学校里涌了出来，先是有一个、接着又有好几个，目瞪口呆地停在白色墓碑之间天使那黑黢黢的驼背身影前。“俺说，他背上还长着个包哩！”一位批评家发了话。

“他头发跟闺女家似的！”另一个说。

天使朝他们转过身去。一颗颗小脑袋从长满地衣的墙头上探出来，让他觉得稀奇。他对那些瞪视的小脸淡淡一笑，随即又转过去惊叹那圈围着菲茨-贾维斯（Fitz-Jarvis）家坟墓的铁栏杆。“一股古怪的、拿不准的气味，”他说，“石板，一堆堆石头，还有这些栏杆。……他们是在害怕吗？……这些死人有时还想再爬起来？有一股压制——设防——的气派”

===Original===

“Get yer *’air* cut, Get yer *’air* cut,” sang three little boys together.

“Curious these Human Beings are!” said the Angel. “That man yesterday wanted to cut off my wings, now these little creatures want me to cut off my hair! And the man on the bridge offered to take the ‘paint’ off me. They will leave nothing of me soon.”

“Where did you get that *’at*?” sang another little boy. “Where did you get them clo’es?”

===Chinese===

“去把*头发*铰铰，去把*头发*铰铰，”三个小男孩齐声唱道。

“这些人真是古怪！”天使说。“昨天那个人要剪掉我的翅膀，如今这些小东西又要我铰掉头发！桥上那个人还非要给我把‘漆’褪下来不可。要不了多久，他们就要把我身上弄得一件不剩了。”

“你那顶*帽子*哪儿弄来的？”另一个小男孩唱道。“你那身衣裳哪儿弄来的？”

===Original===

“They ask questions that they evidently do not want answered,” said the Angel. “I can tell from the tone.” He looked thoughtfully at the little boys. “I don’t understand the methods of Human intercourse. These are probably friendly advances, a kind of ritual. But I don’t know the responses. I think I will go back to the little fat man in black, with the gold chain across his stomach, and ask him to explain. It is difficult.”

He turned towards the lych gate. “*Oh!*” said one of the little boys, in a shrill falsetto, and threw a beechnut husk. It came bounding across the churchyard path. The Angel stopped in surprise.

This made all the little boys laugh. A second imitating the first, said “*Oh!*” and hit the Angel. His astonishment was really delicious. They all began crying “*Oh!*” and throwing beechnut husks. One hit the Angel’s hand, another stung him smartly by the ear. The Angel made ungainly movements towards them. He spluttered some expostulation and made for the roadway. The little boys were amazed and shocked at his discomfiture and cowardice. Such sawney behaviour could not be encouraged. The pelting grew vigorously. You may perhaps be able to imagine those vivid moments, daring small boys running in close and delivering shots, milder small boys rushing round behind with flying discharges. Milton Screever’s mongrel dog was roused to yelping ecstasy at the sight, and danced (full of wild imaginings) nearer and nearer to the angelic legs.

===Chinese===

“他们问的问题，分明并不想要回答，”天使说，“我从那口气里听得出来。”他若有所思地望着这群小男孩。“我不懂人类交往的法子。这些多半是友好的表示，一种仪式。可我不知道该怎样回礼。我想我还是回去找那个穿黑衣裳、肚子上横一条金链子的小胖子，请他解说解说。实在太难了。”

他朝墓园门楼走去。“*噢！*”一个小男孩用尖利的假嗓子喊了一声，扔过来一颗山毛榉果壳。果壳蹦蹦跳跳地滚过墓园小径。天使诧异地站住了。

这下子所有的小男孩都笑了。第二个学着第一个的样子，喊一声“*噢！*”，正好打中天使。他那副吃惊的模样实在太逗了。他们全都喊起“*噢！*”来，山毛榉果壳雨点般扔出。一颗打中天使的手，一颗狠狠掹在他耳朵边上。天使朝他们迈开笨拙的步子，气急败坏地咕哝了几句抗议，向大路退去。小男孩们见他这般狼狈、这般脓包，又是惊又是愤。这样的怂包行径断不可纵容。弹雨愈发猛烈。你也许想象得出那些活生生的时刻：胆大的孩子跑得贴近了投弹，斯文些的孩子绕到背后远远放箭。弥尔顿·斯克里弗（Milton Screever）家的那条杂种狗见了这光景，兴奋得狂吠不止，（满脑子狂想地）朝着那双天使的腿越舞越近。

The Angel seemed quite upset. “I don’t understand,” he said. “These Human ways.﻿ ﻿…”

“Yes, of course. Unusual to you. How’s your excrescence?”

“My what?” said the Angel.

“Bifid limb, you know. How is it? Now you’re down this way, come in. Come in and let me have a look at it again. You young roughs! And meanwhile these little louts of ours will be getting off home. They’re all alike in these villages. *Can’t* understand anything abnormal. See an odd-looking stranger. Chuck a stone. No imagination beyond the parish.﻿ ﻿… (I’ll give you physic if I catch you annoying strangers again.)﻿ ﻿… I suppose it’s what one might expect.﻿ ﻿… Come along this way.”

So the Angel, horribly perplexed still, was hurried into the surgery to have his wound redressed.

就这样，天使仍旧满心惶惑，被匆匆领进诊所，重新包扎伤口。
