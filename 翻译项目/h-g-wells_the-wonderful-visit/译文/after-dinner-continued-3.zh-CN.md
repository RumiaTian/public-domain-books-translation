## XXI After Dinner (Continued) / 二十一 餐后（续）

===Original===

And thus in the little bedroom over the gable we reach a first resting place in this story. And as we have been hard at it, getting our story spread out before you, it may be perhaps well to recapitulate a little.

Looking back you will see that much has been done; we began with a blaze of light “not uniform but broken all over by curving flashes like the waving of swords,” and the sound of a mighty harping, and the advent of an Angel with polychromatic wings.

Swiftly, dexterously, as the reader must admit, wings have been clipped, halo handled off, the glory clapped into coat and trousers, and the Angel made for all practical purposes a man, under a suspicion of being either a lunatic or an impostor. You have heard too, or at least been able to judge, what the Vicar and the Doctor and the Curate’s wife thought of the strange arrival. And further remarkable opinions are to follow.

===Chinese===

就这样，在山墙之上那间小小的卧室里，我们抵达了本故事的第一处歇脚地。我们一路忙着把这故事在你面前铺展开来，如今或许不妨稍稍回顾一番。

回头望去，你会看到已经发生了许多事；我们是从一道光的闪耀开始的——“并不均匀，而是满布着蜿蜒的闪光，宛如剑影翻飞”——还有洪亮的竖琴之声，以及一位背生五彩翅膀的天使的降临。

正如读者必定承认的那样，我们手脚麻利：翅膀剪了，光环摘了，那身荣光被塞进了上衣和长裤，天使就一切实际效用而言被造成了一个凡人，还背着要么是疯子、要么是骗子的嫌疑。你也已经听到——或者至少足以自行判断——教区牧师、医生和副牧师太太对这位古怪来客的看法。而更多的高见还在后头。

===Original===

The afterglow of the summer sunset in the northwest darkens into night and the Angel sleeps, dreaming himself back in the wonderful world where it is always light, and everyone is happy, where fire does not burn and ice does not chill; where rivulets of starlight go streaming through the amaranthine meadows, out to the seas of peace. He dreams, and it seems to him that once more his wings glow with a thousand colours and flash through the crystal air of the world from which he has come.

So he dreams. But the Vicar lies awake, too perplexed for dreaming. Chiefly he is troubled by the possibilities of Mrs. Mendham; but the evening’s talk has opened strange vistas in his mind, and he is stimulated by a sense as of something seen darkly by the indistinct vision of a hitherto unsuspected wonderland lying about his world. For twenty years now he has held his village living and lived his daily life, protected by his familiar creed, by the clamour of the details of life, from any mystical dreaming. But now interweaving with the familiar bother of his persecuting neighbour, is an altogether unfamiliar sense of strange new things.

===Chinese===

西北方向夏日落日的余晖暗成夜色，天使睡着了，梦中回到了那个奇妙的世界：那里永远光明，人人幸福，火烧不伤人，冰寒不了人；一道道星光的小溪流淌过不朽的花草原，汇入安宁之海。他做着梦，恍惚间自己的翅膀又焕发出千种色彩，闪过他来自的那个世界的澄澈天空。

他就这样做梦。教区牧师却醒着躺在床上，心事重重，全无睡意。最叫他烦心的是门德姆太太的种种可能；可晚间的谈话已在他头脑里打开一扇扇奇异的远景，刺激着他，仿佛借着模糊的目力幽幽瞥见：就在他的世界四周，躺着一个从未被疑心过的仙境。二十年来，他领着这个村子的圣俸，过着日常的日子，靠熟稔的信条、靠生活琐事的喧嚣护佑着，与一切神秘的梦想隔绝。可如今，同那位逼迫他的老对头的惯常烦扰交织在一起的，是一种全然陌生的、对新奇事物的感觉。

===Original===

There was something ominous in the feeling. Once, indeed, it rose above all other considerations, and in a kind of terror he blundered out of bed, bruised his shins very convincingly, found the matches at last, and lit a candle to assure himself of the reality of his own customary world again. But on the whole the more tangible trouble was the Mendham avalanche. Her tongue seemed to be hanging above him like the sword of Damocles. What might she not say of this business, before her indignant imagination came to rest?

And while the successful captor of the Strange Bird was sleeping thus uneasily, Gully of Sidderton was carefully unloading his gun after a wearisome blank day, and Sandy Bright was on his knees in prayer, with the window carefully fastened. Annie Durgan was sleeping hard with her mouth open, and Amory’s mother was dreaming of washing, and both of them had long since exhausted the topics of the Sound and the Glare. Lumpy Durgan was sitting up in his bed, now crooning the fragment of a tune and now listening intently for a sound he had heard once and longed to hear again. As for the solicitor’s clerk at Iping Hanger, he was trying to write poetry about a confectioner’s girl at Portburdock, and the Strange Bird was quite out of his head. But the ploughman who had seen it on the confines of Siddermorton Park had a black eye. That had been one of the more tangible consequences of a little argument about birds’ legs in the “Ship.” It is worthy of this passing mention, since it is probably the only known instance of an Angel causing anything of the kind.

===Chinese===

这感觉里有种不祥的意味。有一回，它当真压倒了一切别的念头，他怀着几分惊恐，跌跌撞撞地下了床，结结实实磕破了小腿，好不容易摸到火柴，点起一支蜡烛，好让自己重新确信这个习常世界的实在。但总的说来，更要紧、更具体的麻烦还是门德姆太太这场雪崩。她的舌头仿佛悬在他头顶，就像达摩克利斯之剑。在她那义愤的想象力平息下来之前，这桩事她什么样的话说不出来呢？

怪鸟的猎获者这般不安地睡着，与此同时，锡德顿的加利（Gully）在熬过乏味的一无所获的一天之后，正细心地给他的枪退膛；桑迪·布莱特（Sandy Bright）跪着祷告，窗子关得严严实实。安妮·德根（Annie Durgan）张着嘴酣睡，阿莫里的母亲梦见洗衣裳，她们俩早把那声响和那光亮的话题聊了个精光。“块头”德根（Lumpy Durgan）在床上坐起来，一会儿哼着几节曲子，一会儿凝神细听一个他听过一回、巴望再听一回的声音。至于伊平亨格（Iping Hanger）那位律师的书记员，他正琢磨着为波特伯多克（Portburdock）一位糖果店姑娘写诗，怪鸟早被他忘到九霄云外了。倒是那个在锡德莫顿园（Siddermorton Park）边界上见过怪鸟的庄稼汉挂了彩，眼睛乌青——那是他在“船”字号酒馆里为鸟腿问题小小争辩了一场留下的、比较实在的后果之一。此事值得一提带过，因为它多半是有史可考的唯一一例：一位天使竟惹出了这等事端。
