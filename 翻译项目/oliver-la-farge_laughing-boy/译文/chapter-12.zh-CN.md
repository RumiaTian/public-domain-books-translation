## Chapter XII / 第十二章

### I / I

===Original===
During the greetings, Laughing Boy took stock. With entire confidence in his wife’s ability to win over these people, he carried himself as though he had no faintest idea that there might be strained relations between them, but in his mind he was calling the roll of his family. Wounded Face, sitting apart in his blanket like a sleepy eagle, was against him. Spotted Horse, the younger uncle, was waiting; meantime he intended to be cordial. Spotted Horse would follow somebody’s lead, whoever spoke most commandingly; afterwards, if the issue were unpleasant, he would mildly deplore it. His mother was against him, but she too waited, not declaring herself, not closing her judgment yet. His sisters took no position, but welcomed the stranger and did their best to make her at home because she was his wife. Bow’s Son, his brother, and Bay Horse, his brother-in-law, both evidently thought he had made a fool of himself, and felt hostile towards her. Bay Horse could be discounted; he did not belong to the clan, and the taboo which forbids a man’s looking upon his mother-in-law kept him away from the family circle most of the time.

===Chinese===
寒暄问安的当口，笑娃（Laughing Boy）在心里掂量各人。他全然信得过妻子笼络这些人的本事，举手投足便装得丝毫不知彼此之间或许存着嫌隙，心里却把自家的名册一个个点过去。疤脸（Wounded Face）独自裹在毯子里坐着，像一只打盹的鹰，是反对他的。小舅舅花斑马（Spotted Horse）还在观望；眼底下他打算热络相待。花斑马总要跟从什么人的主张，看谁的话口气最硬；事后倘若结局不愉快，他也只会温温和和地叹息一番。他母亲反对他，可她也在等，不亮明态度，评断还没有落槌。几个姐妹不站边，只管欢迎这远来的客人，尽力让她安顿得像在自己家里，只因她是他的妻子。弟弟弓之子（Bow’s Son）和姐夫枣红马（Bay Horse）显然都认定他干了件糊涂事，对她怀着敌意。枣红马可以撇开不算；他不属于这个氏族，再说那桩忌讳——做男人的不得瞧见自己的岳母——平日里就把他挡在家门圈子外头。

===Original===
Out of the corner of his eye, without seeming to pay attention, he watched Slim Girl with relish as she said and did exactly the right things, giving an excellent impersonation of just any attractive woman.

===Chinese===
他用眼角余光瞟着，装出并不留意的样子，饶有兴味地看瘦姑娘（Slim Girl）一言一语、一举一动都拿捏得恰到好处，活脱脱扮出一个招人喜欢的寻常女子。

===Original===
Now Jesting Squaw’s Son dismounted before the hogan and stood beside him, looking anxious and hesitant. It was plain to see that he was concerned only lest there should be some estrangement between them, lest an alien life might have made a gulf. Friendship stood firm and true. So they embraced and wrestled and spoke loudly to each other.

===Chinese===
这时打趣婆娘之子（Jesting Squaw’s Son）在霍根屋前下了马，站到他身边，一脸的忐忑迟疑。看得出他挂心的只有一桩：生怕两人之间生了分，生怕外头那段日子在当中劈出一道鸿沟。友情依旧坚实如初。于是两人搂抱摔跤，大声说起话来。

===Original===
He faced his father last, and most anxiously. Two Bows had held back from the others; his was an awkward position in this matter. Long days of teaching the jeweller’s craft, hunts together, lessons in the trail and the bow, work shared, had brought them very near to each other. They were father and son, and they were close friends. Laughing Boy admired and emulated the old warrior, and he could confide in him. Two Bows saw some of himself bearing fruit anew in the young brave. And yet, in a matter like this, his rights were only those of courtesy﻿—to Laughing Boy’s own clansmen, to his mother and her brothers, was the decision. He could only watch for the time when his purely personal influence might turn the scales. Now, he said nothing. His son could feel fondness and sympathy there, but whether approval went with it he could not tell.

===Chinese===
他最后才去面对父亲，也是顶提心吊胆的一回。双弓（Two Bows）先前一直避开众人；在这件事上，他的处境左右为难。多少个日夜教儿子银匠手艺，一同打猎，传授辨路和弓箭的功夫，同担共扛的活计，把爷儿俩拉得极近。他们是父子，也是知交。笑娃敬慕这位老战士，处处学他，有什么心事也能向他吐露。双弓则看着自己身上的某些东西，又在年轻的勇士身上重新开花结果。然而在这一类事情上，他的权利只剩客气二字——作数的，是笑娃自家的氏族亲人，是他母亲和舅舅们。他只能瞅准时机，让自己这份纯属私人的情分去拨动天平。此刻他一言不发。儿子在他身上感觉得到疼爱和体恤，只是这疼爱里头带不带首肯，就说不准了。

### II / II

===Original===
Laughing Boy had been half-afraid lest, like Friend of the Eagles, or Reared in a Mountain, he would find that his own people seemed dirty and smelled badly when he returned to them. Secretly, even a little shamefacedly, he considered the life that he was living perhaps not so far removed from that of ordinary Earth People as the Eagles’ home in the sky, or the mother-of-pearl and turquoise dwellings of the Divine Ones, but still something apart, like the magic country at the end of Old Age River. He had waited somewhat anxiously for his first impression, and found that his home was delightfully as he had imagined it. Everything was the same; it seemed a miracle. That which had been intimate and dear was so still, only now nothing was taken for granted, but every commonest detail leapt to him with new vividness.

===Chinese===
笑娃原先还有几分担心，怕自己像鹰之友（Friend of the Eagles），或是山中养大（Reared in a Mountain）那样，回到自己人中间，会觉得他们又腌臜、气味又难闻。他心里暗暗地、甚至有点儿难为情地想，自己眼下过的日子，也许不像鹰在天上的家、不像神圣之灵（Divine Ones）珠母和绿松石的宫室那样，同寻常地上之民（Earth People）的日子隔得那么远，却终究自成一重天地，就像暮年河（Old Age River）尽头的奇境。他怀着几分焦灼等那第一眼的印象，结果发现家里喜滋滋地正同他想象的一模一样。什么都没变样；简直是桩奇迹。原本亲昵珍贵的东西依旧亲昵珍贵，只是如今没有一样是理所当然的了，顶平常琐碎的细节，也带着崭新的鲜明朝他扑面而来。

===Original===
There were constant little surges of delight in his heart over trivial, minor things﻿—a shadow across a cliff, the bend of a cottonwood, the sheep coming in at evening, their silly, solemn faces all about the hogan﻿—why should they have changed? A man does not realize that he has changed himself, or only partially recognizes it, thinking that the world about him is different; a familiar dish has become no longer enjoyable, a fundamental aphorism no longer true; it is a surprise, then, when his eyes and ears report unchanged, familiar impressions. So the wonderful sameness of things, the unfailing way in which expectation was fulfilled, were proofs of something beautiful in the order of the world. It was glorious to pick up the threads of talk where he had dropped them, discussing the old, well-worn subjects casually and in detail, as though they were still inlaid in his life, with just a little seasoning of the attitude of one who has been farther and seen more.

===Chinese===
一些琐碎细微的小事，总在他心里激起一阵一阵小小的欢喜——一道掠过岩壁的影子，一株棉白杨弯下的身姿，傍晚归圈的羊群，那些又傻气又一本正经的脸挤满霍根屋四周——它们凭什么要变呢？一个人并不知道自己已经变了，或者只认出一半，倒以为是周遭的世界不同了；一道熟悉的吃食不再有味，一条顶要紧的老话不再灵验；于是等到耳目照旧报来熟悉的印象，反倒成了意外。万物这般美妙地如常，期望桩桩不落空地应验，便成了世界秩序里某种美好之物的凭证。把先前撂下的话头从撂下的地方接起来，随口而又细致地谈论那些谈旧了的老题目，仿佛它们依然镶嵌在自己的日子里，再稍稍带上一点走得更远、见得更多的人才有的口气——这真是痛快极了。

===Original===
One could see that his family had expected some outlandishness. Now they were puzzled; some disappointed, and some pleased to see how normal and Navajo were Laughing Boy and his wife. Her blankets spoke for them with many tongues, and the solid evidences of their prosperity, all Navajo, nothing bizarre or American, but good honest silver, turquoise, coral﻿—“hard goods”﻿—and handsome Indian ponies.

===Chinese===
看得出，他家里原是备着要看些洋怪事的。如今他们反倒纳闷了；有的失望，有的见笑娃两口子这样本分、这样纳瓦霍，心里欢喜。她的毯子替他们说了千言万语，还有那份殷实家业的种种实证，全是纳瓦霍的物事，没有一样古怪美国派，只有地道厚实的银子、绿松石、珊瑚——“硬货”——外加几匹体面的印第安小马。

===Original===
He watched Slim Girl, seeing the shutters closed behind her eyes, correct, sure, in hand, doing just the perfect thing. He was swept by constantly recurring waves of pleasure in her, and felt, as he sometimes did, a faint fear of that detached self-command. Slowly they were being forced to accept her as really belonging to the People. It pleased his dramatic instinct, as well as the strong sense of privacy he had concerning their relationship, to play up, being very normal, and letting no look or gesture suggest that they two came from a land of enchantment.

===Chinese===
他看着瘦姑娘，看见她眼睛后面落下了一道道百叶窗，妥帖，笃定，一切拿捏在手，做的桩桩件件恰到好处。一阵接一阵的欢喜在他心里漫过；同时，如同他偶尔会有过的那样，对那份置身事外的自制，他生出一丝隐隐的惧意。家人们正被逼着一点点承认：她当真算是迪内（The People）的人了。他把戏做足——一派寻常样子，不让一个眼色、一个手势透露出他们俩来自一片施了魔法的土地——这既满足他天生的戏子本能，也合乎他把两人关系捂得严严实实的那份私心。

===Original===
Knowing her well, he could see that she was at high tension, and secretly watchful. He had no idea that that strain, that painful vigilance, was above all for himself.

===Chinese===
他深知她的为人，看得出她绷得紧紧的，暗地里时时提防。他却万万想不到，那份紧张、那份苦楚的警醒，归根结底是为了他自己。

===Original===
When he was alone with his father, he showed him the silver-mounted bridle and some of his other jewelry. Two Bows turned over the harness, feeling the surface with his fingertips.

===Chinese===
趁单独同父亲在一处，他把镶银的笼头和另外几件首饰拿给父亲看。双弓把那副辔头翻来覆去，用指尖细摸表面的手工。

===Original===
“I have nothing more to teach you﻿—that is well done.” He tapped the cheek-strap. “I should not have thought of using that design that way.”

===Chinese===
“我没有什么可再教你的了——这活儿做得好。”他敲了敲颊革。“我就想不到能把那个花样这么用。”

===Original===
From Two Bows, such praise made it hard to keep a quiet, modest face.

===Chinese===
从双弓嘴里听来这样的夸赞，要维持一张沉静谦和的脸，可真不容易。

===Original===
Jesting Squaw’s Son came back in the late afternoon. They drifted off together, with arms over each other’s shoulders, until they came to rest under the scrub oaks behind the peach trees. They discussed this and that, vaguely, trailing off into silence, playing with twigs and pebbles, running their fingers through the sand, occupying their hands. At length Laughing Boy looked at his friend and spoke:

===Chinese===
傍晚时分，打趣婆娘之子回来了。两人搭着肩膀晃出去，一路晃到桃树后面那些矮栎底下才安顿下来。他们有一搭没一搭地说些闲话，说着说着就沉进了沉默里，摆弄树枝石子，手指在沙里划来划去，让手上有事可做。末了，笑娃望着朋友开了口：

===Original===
“I do not talk to those people. Some of them have their minds made up, some of them will not understand. I do not think you will know what I am talking about, but you understand me. I want you to know.

===Chinese===
“我不同那些人讲。他们有的主意已定，有的不会懂。我想你听不明白我要说的是什么，可你懂得我这个人。我要你知道。

===Original===
“I have been down Old Age River in the log, with sheet-lightning and rainbows and soft rain, and the gods on either side to guide me. The Eagles have put lightning snakes and sunbeams and rainbows under me; they have carried me through the hole in the sky. I have been through the little crack in the rocks with Red God and seen the homes of the Butterflies and the Mountain Sheep and the Divine Ones. I have heard the Four Singers on the Four Mountains. I mean that woman.

===Chinese===
“我乘着木头顺暮年河而下，一路片闪、彩虹、软雨，两岸都有神明引导。众鹰把闪电蛇、阳光和彩虹铺在我身下；他们驮着我穿过了天上的洞。我跟着红神（Red God）钻过岩石间的小裂缝，见过蝴蝶、大角羊和神圣之灵的家园。我在四座圣山上听见过四位颂唱师唱。我说的是那个女人。

===Original===
“It sounds like insane talk. It is not. It is not just because I am in love. It is not what I feel when I am near her, what happens to my blood when she touches me. I know about that. I have thought about that. It is what goes on there. It is all sorts of things, but you would have to live there to see it.

===Chinese===
“这些话听着像疯话。不是疯话。也不单是因为我坠了情网。不在我挨着她时心里是什么滋味，不在她碰我时我血里翻起什么。那个我清楚。那个我想过。我说的是那边过的日子本身。那是种种的一切，可你得住到那儿去才看得见。

===Original===
“I know the kind of thing my uncle says. It is not true. We are not acting out here, we are pretending. We have masks on, so they will not see our real faces. You have seen her blankets and my hard goods. Those are true. Those are just part of it.”

===Chinese===
“我舅舅说的那套话，我知道。那不是真的。我们在这儿不是本来面目，是假扮。我们戴着面具，好叫他们看不见我们真正的脸。她的毯子、我的硬货你都见过了。那些是真的。那些不过是其中一角。”

===Original===
Jesting Squaw’s Son answered, “I have seen the blankets and the hard goods; they sing. I am happy about you.”

===Chinese===
打趣婆娘之子答道：“毯子和硬货我都见了；它们会唱歌。我为你欢喜。”

===Original===
He felt better after that, he cared that his friend should know, and, in contradistinction to the others, telling him did not lessen the rare quality of the thing described. He returned to the hogans feeling better able to act his part.

===Chinese===
这以后他觉得松快了些；他在乎让朋友知道，而且恰恰同对旁人相反，说给他听，并不折损那件被说的事的稀罕成色。他回到霍根屋那边，觉得这出戏更有本事演下去了。

===Original===
He found the evening meal most enjoyable as he watched the good ways and mannerisms of his family. Among them he could make out a growing perplexity. What had that old man told them to expect? A word slipped from his brother to his younger uncle gave him the cue, filling his heart with glee. When he got a chance he whispered to Slim Girl,

===Chinese===
晚饭桌上，他看着家里众人那些可爱做派和积年的习惯，吃得津津有味。他听得出众人中间有一种渐渐加深的困惑。那老头子到底嘱咐他们等着看什么来着？弟弟对小舅舅漏出的半句话给了他提示，乐得他心花怒放。得了个空子，他悄声对瘦姑娘说：

===Original===
He lolled back on the sheepskins, laughing inside himself. A smile shadowed the corners of his wife’s mouth.

===Chinese===
他歪在羊皮上，肚子里暗笑。他妻子的嘴角浮起一丝若隐若现的笑影。

### III / III

===Original===
Slim Girl watched the ceremony with interest, feeling in a pronounced fashion the mixed emotion she had towards so much that was going on about her. It might be a weapon to destroy her, for the very reason that it was a summation and a visible expression of many things in her people’s life that mattered to her. She had a sneaking suspicion that the family had gone to the expense of a full Night Chant largely because of the effect they thought it might have on the erring member.

===Chinese===
瘦姑娘怀着兴味观看仪式，心里分外清楚地觉出自己对周遭种种事体的百般滋味。这仪式也可能成为毁掉她的兵器，正因为它集其大成、活生生摆出了她族人生活中许多于她要紧的东西。她心里藏着一丝疑影：这家人肯破费办一场全本的夜颂仪（Night Chant），多半是看中了它或许能在那个走岔了路的人身上起的作用。

===Original===
It was sometimes absurd and sometimes quite beautiful. The masked dancers were grotesque, but there were moments by firelight when their shapeless heads and painted bodies, their rhythmic, intent movements, became grand and awe-inspiring. The long and repetitious prayers were often monotonous, chanted to dull, heavy music, but in the worst of them there were flashes of poetic feeling. Her American education had dulled her sensibility to the quick, compact imagery of a single statement, leaving to the hearer, the evocation of the picture intended that forms the basis of Navajo poetry. Still, she caught it sometimes,

===Chinese===
它有时荒唐，有时又美得很。戴面具的舞者样子怪诞，可火光里有那么几个瞬间，他们那没有形状的头脸、彩绘的身子、专注而合着节拍的动作，会忽然变得雄浑庄严，令人敬畏。冗长重复的祷词往往单调，配着沉滞的重乐唱诵，可就在最沉闷的段落里，也闪过诗意的火光。她受的美国教育磨钝了她对那种一语道破、以少胜多的意象的感受力——只把一句话撂给听的人，让他自己在心里唤出那幅图景，纳瓦霍诗歌的根基正在于此。尽管如此，她有时还是接得住，

===Original===
“By red rocks the green corn grows,
						

						Beautifully it grows﻿ ﻿…”

===Chinese===
“红岩旁边，绿玉米生长，

						美美地生长……”

===Original===
She saw it, and the terse implication that takes for granted all that the Indians feel about corn, contenting itself with merely calling forth that feeling.

===Chinese===
她看见了那幅图景，也看见那话里短促的余意——它把印第安人对玉米怀有的一切视作当然，自己只管把那份情唤起来，再不多添一字。

===Original===

She could sympathize with their spirit, but not enter into it. A door had been closed to her, and at times, even standing here among the other spectators, in the heart of the Navajo country, she was swept again by a hopeless nostalgia for the country and the people, forever lost, of her dim childhood.

===Chinese===
她试着把这些东西当作生来就有、贴着自己心窝的，却发现只能客观地看着它们。她如今是外人了。她能体谅其中的精神，却进不去了。有一扇门对她关死了；有时候，即便站在这里，站在看客中间，站在纳瓦霍乡土的腹地，她也会重新被一种无望的乡愁卷走——为那片土地和那群人，她蒙昧童年里那片永远失掉了的土地和人。

===Original===
When she had been a very little girl, she had trembled with terror and awe at the sight of the very gods coming into the circle of people. Out in the darkness one heard their distant call, repeated as they came nearer, until with the fourth cry they entered the firelight. They danced and sang there, majestic and strange; then they vanished again to return to their homes in the sacred places. Now they were just Indians whom she knew, dressed up in a rather silly way. Like many unreligious people, she kept slipping into the idea that these worshippers were pretending to be taken in by the patently absurd. Most of the adult spectators had been through the Night Chant initiation; all of them knew that the gods were no more than men in masks; how could they be so reverent? What was her devout husband’s ecstasy, or his devoutness, when he himself put on the painted rawhide bag trimmed with spruce and feathers, pretending to be Talking God?

===Chinese===
她还是个小丫头的时候，一见真真切切的神明走进人群的圈子，就会又惊又惧地发抖。黑暗深处听得见他们遥远的呼喊，一声一声应和着近前来，到第四声呐喊，他们便踏进了火光。他们在那里歌舞，威严而奇异；随后又隐没不见，回圣地的家去了。如今他们不过是她认得的印第安人，穿戴得有几分可笑。同许多没有信仰的人一样，她总是不由自主地滑进一个念头：这些礼拜的人是在假装被那些明摆着的荒唐玩意儿哄住。看客里的成年人多半行过夜颂仪的入仪礼；他们全知道神明不过是戴面具的人；他们怎么能这样虔敬？她那虔敬的丈夫，等他自己套上那只缀着云杉枝和羽毛的彩绘生皮袋子、假扮说话神（Talking God）的时候，他的忘形，他的虔敬，又算什么？

She had known that the wine came from the vineyard of an Italian who was a Catholic﻿—something vaguely wicked﻿—and that the bread was just bread. She knew the minister for a nice man whose wife rather bullied him.
