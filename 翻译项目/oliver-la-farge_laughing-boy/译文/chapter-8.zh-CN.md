## Chapter VIII / 第八章

### I / I

===Original===
She was still asleep inside the house. He stood looking down upon her in the half-light. She seemed frail, childish, and sweet, with the shadow under the eyelids, her mouth faintly drooping, her figure reduced to almost nothing beneath the blanket. He thought of that drama of strength and weakness, of conquering and being conquered, fitting it to this small person, soft in sleep. Now that he was looking at her, he had no reservations; it only seemed a miracle that she should be his. He wondered at the mere chance it was; Slender Hair speaking to him of the dance and the racing, coming to Tsé Lani, this little incident and that, until out of nowhere that which might never have been entered and became the core of his life.

===Chinese===
她还睡在屋里。他站在半明半暗的光里，低头望着她。她显得纤弱、稚气而甜美，眼睑下罩着一层暗影，嘴角微微下垂，毯子底下的身形几乎缩得若有若无。他想起那出关于强与弱、关于征服与被征服的戏文，把它套在这个睡得软软的小人儿身上。此刻这样望着她，他心里再无半点保留；只觉得她竟能归他所有，这本身就像个奇迹。他惊叹这纯乎是偶然：细发（Slender Hair）同他谈起舞会和赛马，来到特塞拉尼（Tsé Lani），这一桩小事，那一桩小事，直到那件原本也许永远不会成真的事凭空而至，闯了进来，成了他生命的核心。

===Original===
The sun would be up soon. He went to meet it.

===Chinese===
太阳快升起来了。他出去迎接它。

===Original===
She woke happy, watching him under lazy eyelids as he stood outside the door, naked save for his breechclout, with the level sunlight touching the edges of his flanks and ribs, making a golden reflection where his upraised arms bunched the muscles at his shoulders. She thanked God and the gods indiscriminately. Whatever happened now, this could not be taken away. She shifted the blanket, closed her eyes, and assumed sleep.

===Chinese===
她带着欢喜醒来，惺忪的眼帘底下望着他：他站在门外，只系一条遮裆布，周身赤裸，平直的阳光抚着他侧腰和肋骨的棱线，高举的双臂把肩头的肌肉束起来，映出一层金光。她不分彼此，上帝和诸神一概谢过了。往后无论出什么事，这个是夺不走的了。她动了动毯子，闭上眼睛，装出睡着的样子。

===Original===
He sat down beside her, a little nervous about her awakening. Her eyelids quivered, she yawned deliciously, she stretched her arms like a kitten playing. She sat up and smiled at him, seeing his face brighten as he responded.

===Chinese===
他在她身旁坐下，想到她就要醒来，心里微微发紧。她的眼皮颤了颤，惬意地打个哈欠，又像只玩耍的小猫似的伸了伸胳膊。她坐起身来朝他微笑，看见他回应时，脸上亮堂起来。

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Original===

===Chinese===
他摸了摸那头乱发，辫子歪挂在一旁，随即朝她咧嘴一笑。“你自己的也好不到哪儿去；上泉水边照照你自己去。”

===Original===
She reached over to a shelf and took down a small mirror, which she handed to him. He looked at himself in it; this was fascinating but a little disappointing. Finally she took it from him.

===Chinese===
她探身到架子那边，取下一面小镜子递给他。他对着镜子照自己；这东西迷人，却又有点儿叫人失望。末了她把它从他手里收了回去。

===Original===
“Come, now, dress, and do up my hair.”

===Chinese===
“来吧，快些，穿上衣裳，给我把头发编起来。”

===Original===
He had often exchanged that service with his brothers and sisters; it was a pleasant and friendly act. He had watched his mother and father together at it, one leaning against the other’s knees, laughing when the brush pulled too hard, and he had seen that they extracted some pleasure from it which he did not know. Now he understood that, and the sheer domesticity of it delighted him. He felt really married, settled, a man who would soon have children, and speak as one of established position, no more a boy.

===Chinese===
这种互相梳头的活计，他从前常和弟弟妹妹们换着做；那是一桩愉快而亲热的举动。他见过母亲和父亲在一起梳头，一个倚着另一个的膝头，刷子拽得太重时便笑出声来，他那时便看出来，两人从里头得了某种他所不知道的乐趣。如今他懂了，这份十足的居家过日子的滋味叫他心花怒放。他觉得自己真正成了亲、落了户，是个不久就会有孩子的人了，开口也像个立住了地位的人，再不是个孩子。

===Chinese===
早饭端上来时，正合心意。

===Chinese===

===Chinese===

===Chinese===

===Chinese===
“谁来经管它们？你有你的马、你的银饰活计；那尽够了。我还得三天两头往镇里跑，给那个女传教士做工。”

===Chinese===

===Chinese===
“没有，我没存坏心思。可是婆娘一走，屋子就空了。”

Tomorrow, when I go in, I shall bring you Mexican silver. And those days you can go tend to the horses. By and by, when we have made much money with your jewelry and horses and my weaving and work, we shall go back to your country. We shall go back rich. Is it not a good plan?”

===Chinese===
“我从前天天都给她做工；如今只是偶尔去做。她给的钱不薄。你往后有银子打东西，托的正是她的福。明天我进城，就给你捎墨西哥银回来。那几天你正好去照看马。往后，等咱们靠你的银饰和马、我的织布和做工攒下许多钱，咱们就回你的家乡去。咱们风风光光地回去。这计划还不好么？”

“You have spoken well. Here, I do not know about these things, but you know, and your words are good. It is enough for me that I have you. It is not just that we are married, but we are married all through; there is not any part of us left out.”

===Chinese===
“你说得在理。这些事在这儿我不懂，可你懂，你的话是好的。有你在，我尽够了。不光是咱们成了亲，咱们是从头到脚、彻里彻外结的亲；咱们俩，没有哪一星半点是落在外头的。”

===Chinese===

===Original===
Quickly she kissed him. He felt embarrassed, and loved it.

===Chinese===
她飞快地吻了他一下。他觉着难为情，又偏偏受用。

===Original===
He had to teach her the ritual and song of purification, but, with faint childhood memories to aid her, she was quick to learn. Her close attention pleased him, and it was a pleasure to hear her sing. He was only sorry for her, that she had for so long been denied these things, and angry and puzzled at the schools and the American life that had forbidden them. In the end, he taught her songs that she had no business knowing, quite aware of what he did, as a kind of unavowed tribute and token of the special quality he felt in her.

===Chinese===
洁净的仪轨和歌，他得一样样教她，好在有儿时淡薄的记忆帮衬着，她学得快。她那份专注叫他欢喜，听她唱歌也是一桩享受。他只是替她难受，这些东西竟被隔绝了这么久；对禁止这些东西的学堂和美国生活，他又气又困惑。到后来，他索性把一些她按理无份知晓的歌也教给了她——他对自己做的事心知肚明——算是一份未尝说出口的敬意，也是一枚凭信，记着他在她身上觉出的那种特别的品性。

### II / II

===Original===
After the steam bath and the water and the foaming yucca-suds, it was good to lie with hair spread out, drying, and talk vaguely of things to be done, and now and again to touch her. Great achievements completed themselves in a phrase. He drew the design of a bracelet in the sand; he braided his hair and mimicked the nasal speech of a Ute. They fell to talking of the ways of different tribes, the old wars, and the present semi-hostility between the Navajo and the Pah-Utes.

===Chinese===
蒸气浴、清水，再浇上泡沫腾腾的丝兰皂液，洗过之后，摊开头发躺着晾干，有一搭没一搭地商量要办的事，时不时碰一碰她，真是好滋味。宏图大业，一言半语间便自己成了。他在沙地上画手镯的图样；他编起辫子，学尤特人（Ute）那鼻音浓重的腔调说话。两人渐渐谈起各部族的习性、昔年的战事，还有眼下纳瓦霍人和派尤特人（Pah-Utes）之间那种半敌对的形势。

===Original===
“There is not often trouble with them,” he told her, “but we do not like them. They live wild up in that country beyond Oljeto, where they are hard to catch, and they steal things. Mostly they trouble the Mormons; the Mormons are afraid of them, they say. Since I was a little boy, only once we had real trouble with them. Then one time we went on the warpath for them. I went on the warpath that time.”

===Chinese===
“跟他们不常起纠纷，”他告诉她，“可我们不喜欢他们。他们在奥尔赫托（Oljeto）再过去那片地界里撒野过活，那儿难逮着他们，他们又手脚不干净。他们多半去搅扰摩门人（Mormons）；摩门人怕他们，据说是这样。打我是个小娃娃起，同他们真刀真枪的乱子只闹过一回。就是那一回，我们为他们出了征。那回出征我也在数。”

===Original===
He felt proud of the part he had played, and wanted to tell her about it.

===Chinese===
他为自己当时出的力感到自豪，想把那件事讲给她听。

===Original===
“That was three years ago; I was just about full grown. Blunt Nose, he made the trouble, that one. He was chief of a band of them; he lived up beyond Naesjé canyon, near Tsé Nanaazh. That is wild country, almost in the mountains. He was bad.

===Chinese===
“那是三年前；我刚差不多长足了个子。钝鼻子（Blunt Nose），惹祸的就是那一个。他是他们一伙人的头目；住在纳埃谢（Naesjé）峡谷再上去、特塞纳纳兹（Tsé Nanaazh）近旁。那是片荒僻地界，差不多算进山里了。那家伙坏。

===Original===
“He used to kill Mormon cattle all the time, a cow, and a cow, and a cow, here and there. He needed to keep no sheep. He did the way he pleased. He wore two pistols, and had a gun on his saddle, they say. He would ride down the middle of the trail, and not turn out for anyone.

===Chinese===
“他一年到头宰摩门人的牲口，一头牛，又一头牛，又一头牛，东一头西一头。他犯不着养羊。他爱怎么干就怎么干。他身上挎两支手枪，鞍子上还架着一杆枪，据说这样。他常打小路正当中骑过去，给谁也不让道。

===Original===
“One time he heard the Mormons had sent for soldiers, so he left their country alone. Then he sat quiet for a while, but his people got hungry. A Pah-Ute will eat almost anything, but there is very little up there. That is why the Navajos leave them alone; there is nothing in that country but a few Pah-Utes and a few antelopes. You cannot make anything out of the skins of either, so we let them alone.

===Chinese===
“有一回他听说摩门人派人去叫大兵了，便不去动他们的地界了。他消停了一阵，可他的人饿慌了。派尤特人差不多什么都吃得下，可那上头实在出产寥寥。所以纳瓦霍人不招惹他们；那片地界除了不多几个派尤特人、不多几只羚羊，什么也没有。这两样的皮子哪样也做不成物件，所以我们由他们去。

===Original===
“Well, now Blunt Nose decided to try mutton. He came down by Jahai Spring where Hungry Man lived. He had all his braves with him. They started to run off Hungry Man’s sheep. Then The Doer came along. You have heard of him? He is the one who killed those two Americans; his father was Generous Chief, the one who never was captured when The People went into exile. So The Doer came along; he saw that man’s wife, where she came running. He rode up, he started shooting at those men. They shot back at him. They were too many for him; he rode away and they chased him. But he killed one.

===Chinese===
“好，这下钝鼻子打定主意尝尝羊肉。他下到贾海泉（Jahai Spring）那一带，饿汉（Hungry Man）就住在那儿。他把手下的勇士全带了来。他们动手赶跑饿汉的羊。恰巧干事汉（The Doer）打那儿经过。你听说过他吧？就是杀了那两个美国人的那一个；他父亲是慷慨酋长（Generous Chief），迪内（The People）遭流放那年月始终没被拿住的那一个。就这样，干事汉打那儿经过；他望见那人的婆娘奔跑而来。他策马赶上去，朝那些人开了火。他们回敬他。他们人多，他吃不消；他拨马便走，他们追他。可他撂倒了一个。

===Original===
“Then Blunt Nose was angry. He killed Hungry Man, and his two children. He ran off the sheep. He went back into the Naesjé country.

===Chinese===
“钝鼻子这就恼了。他杀了饿汉，还有他的两个孩子。他把羊赶跑了。他回纳埃谢地界去了。

===Original===

===Chinese===

A lot of people came together and went after him, so that he had to make a big circle, around by Oljeto, to get back. When he went past the trading post, he shot into it. ===Chinese===
“没过多久他又下来了。可这边有人望风，打起了烟讯号。许多人聚齐去追他，逼得他兜了个大圈子，绕道奥尔赫托那一边，才脱身回去。他打交易站前经过时，朝站里开枪。没伤着人，可打坏了些铁皮罐头，震碎了些窗玻璃。随后在回家路上，他过了特奥·阿齐西（T’o Atsisi）溪上的桥。那是座大木桥，骑马也过得去；是瓦辛顿（Washindon）叫人修的。他们派了两个美国人来指点该怎么修。

===Original===
“Well, he came by this bridge. He was angry, so he burnt it, that man. And he got some sheep, and went home.

===Chinese===
“好嘛，他打这桥上过。他正一肚子火，就把它烧了，那家伙。他又捎上些羊，回家去了。

===Original===

So American Chief wrote a paper saying that he was getting up a war-party of Americans who would do the fighting. He said to have good horses ready for them, and that the Navajos must not start fighting or there would be soldiers. That was what he said.

===Chinese===
“随后交易商又写了一纸文书，一纸口气硬的，送给美国酋长。这当口干事汉正在纠合一支出征队。迪内人怒了；他们要把那些派尤特人一个不留全杀了。美国酋长便回文说，他要纠合一支美国人的出征队，由他们来动刀兵。他说要给他们备好脚力好的马，还说纳瓦霍人不许先开衅，不然就要开来大兵。他是这么说的。

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Original===

===Chinese===
“那骑手打戈穆利·特奥（Gomulli T’o）交易站经过。他的马瘸了，人也乏透了。我正在那儿，带着一匹好马，一匹沙毛马。黄胡子（Yellow Mustache）是那儿的交易商；他叫我把文书带上去。他叫我豁出命赶路。他说会有赏钱。

===Original===

The sun was about halfway up when I saw Oljeto. And right then the Pah-Utes saw me. They started after me, and I went as hard as I could for the trading post.

===Chinese===
“我跑了一整天。夜里也跑，跑到月亮落山。天全黑之后我又跑了一程，可我怕鬼。这才扎营歇下。第二天清早我望见一匹闲散的壮马，便捉住它接着赶路。太阳升到一半高的光景，我望见了奥尔赫托。也正在那时，派尤特人瞧见了我。他们追了上来，我豁出一切朝交易站飞奔。

===Original===
“I could hear them shooting at me; I could hear their bullets. I was very much afraid, but there was nothing to do except ride hard.

===Chinese===
“我听得见他们朝我放松；听得见子弹飕飕地过。我怕极了，可没有别的法子，只有拼命地跑。

===Original===
“Then I felt something hit me. It made a dull thump; it did not hurt. I thought, ‘I have been hit in the bottom of my spine. In a minute it will hurt. Probably I shall die, I do not want to die.’ That way I thought.

===Chinese===
“接着我觉得什么东西撞上了我。闷闷一声钝响；并不疼。我想：‘我脊梁根上中了弹。过一分钟就要疼起来。多半我要死了；我不想死。’我那样一路想着。

===Original===
“I thought all that all at once, then I felt behind, but there was nothing. Then people began shooting from the windows of the trading post, and the Pah-Utes went away. I rode up to the door and got off, wondering if I should fall. But I was all right. There was a bullet in the cantle of my saddle; that was what I had felt.

===Chinese===
“这些念头一眨眼全过了一遍，我往身后摸去，却什么也没有。这时交易站的窗口里有人开起枪来，派尤特人便撤了。我骑到门口下马，心里拿不准自己会不会栽倒。可我毫发无损。马鞍后桥里嵌着一颗子弹；我方才觉着的就是它。

===Original===
“We waited five days for the American war-party; meantime they got some good horses together. Blunt Nose was around all the time. People gathered together in groups of ten to twelve families, or more, like in the days when we were always at war. Then those men came.

===Chinese===
“我们等了五天，等那支美国人的出征队；这其间大家凑齐了几匹好马。钝鼻子那阵子一直在左近出没。人们十个、十二个家户一伙地聚拢，也有更多的，就像从前我们长年交兵的那些年月。后来那些人到了。

===Original===
“There were eight of them. They all had badges on their shirts, like policemen, only not quite the same. One of them was a fat man; we did not see why they brought him.

===Chinese===
“他们一共八个。衬衫上都别着徽章，像警察，只是不大一样。其中有一个胖子；我们看不出带他来做什么用。

===Original===
“They said they wanted four Navajos to be trackers. The Doer was to be head tracker. There were two other young men, and then they took me. They said they would pay me a dollar a day. That was a new idea to me, to be paid for hunting Pah-Utes. I thought you just hunted them.

===Chinese===
“他们说要用四个纳瓦霍人当追踪手。干事汉领追踪手。另有两位年轻人，随后又挑中了我。他们说一天付我一块钱。追猎派尤特人还能拿钱，这在我是个新道理。我还道追猎他们就只是追猎罢了。

===Original===
“We tracked them for three days without seeing them. They tried to make us go at night, but we pretended not to be able to follow the trail. When The Doer told them that, they believed him. It was not an easy trail. Their horses were not shod, and they went a great deal over bald rock, they turned and doubled, they dragged branches behind them.

===Chinese===
“我们缀着踪迹走了三天，没见着他们的人。他们要我们夜里也赶路，我们只装作缀不住踪迹。干事汉替我们一说，他们便信了。那踪迹也实在难跟。他们的马没钉蹄铁，净走光秃秃的石面，又绕圈子又折返，还在身后拖着树枝扫踪。

===Original===
“We did not get much to eat, we could not make a fire. Those Americans brought a brown, sweet candy, and a little, dried-up black candy in boxes, something sticky. These we ate; you could go a long time on them. Then we had some dried corn. We started at first light; we went till it was quite dark. During the day it was hot among those rocks. We were hungry. It did not seem that we were following Pah-Utes; we were just following tracks in the sand, or little scratches on the rock, and some day we should come to the end of them, and something important would happen. Now the only thing in the world was those marks. When you saw one where you had not expected it, it seemed to shout at you.

===Chinese===
“我们没什么进嘴的，又生不得火。那些美国人带来一种褐色的甜糖块，还有小盒里一种干缩发黑、黏糊糊的糖。我们就吃这些；吃了能顶好一程。后来我们又有些干玉米。天刚放亮就动身；走到天黑透才歇。白天在那片石头窝里热得厉害。我们饿着。倒不像是在追什么派尤特人；只是在追沙地上的蹄印，或是石面上细细的刮痕，总有一天我们会追到这些印子的尽头，那时便要出一件大事。这会儿世上独一无二的东西，就是那些印子。在你意想不到的地方猛见着一个，它就像冲你叫喊。

===Original===
“But we were gaining on them. On the fourth day they were much fresher, those tracks. We were close to them. Then we saw a couple of them, on lame horses, and we chased them. They had to cross a deep arroyo; when they went down into it we raced, hoping to catch them at the bottom of it. But they got up the other side; they just came up that side when we got to the near edge. Right away they started shooting. They shot at that fat man.

===Chinese===
“可我们步步逼近了。到第四天，那些蹄印新鲜多了。我们离他们近了。这时我们望见他们两个，骑着瘸马，我们便追。他们非穿过一条很深的旱谷不可；他们下谷的时候我们放马疾驰，指望在谷底截住他们。可他们上了对岸；我们赶到近沿时，他们恰从那一边冒上头来。立刻就开了火。他们冲那胖子打。

===Original===
“Right away he fell off his horse. He landed on his stomach, and as soon as he landed he began to shoot. He shot between his horse’s legs. He hit one Pah-Ute in the leg and one in the arm. Then we knew why he had been brought along, that fat man.

===Chinese===
“他应声落马。他摔了个嘴啃地，一落地就开了火。他从马腿中间打枪。他打中一个派尤特人的腿，又打中一个的胳膊。这下我们明白带那胖子来是干什么用的了。

===Original===
“We wanted to kill them, but the chief American said that they had to go to T’o Nanasdési to be punished. Their horses were no good, so they put them on those two young men’s horses, and sent them back with them and one American to Oljeto. That left me and The Doer. I had never seen him before, that man, but everybody has heard about him. I was anxious to do well in his eyes.

===Chinese===
“我们想杀了他们，可那领头的美国人说，他们得押到托纳纳斯代西去伏法。他们那两匹马不中用了，便把俘虏安在那两个年轻人的马上，打发他们同一个美国人押送回奥尔赫托。这就剩下我和干事汉。那个人我从前没见过，可谁没听说过他。我一心想在他眼里挣个好看。

===Original===
“In the afternoon we came to the mouth of Yotatséyi Canyon. The trail was fresh and clear. The Doer told them that the other end of the canyon was halfway up Napani Mountain, to our right. It went in a big curve, he said. If they got out there, we could never catch them, he said. So he told them to send three men with him, and he would take them straight across there. He would reach it by nightfall, he said. Then, in the morning, we could start in from both ends and catch them, he said. So they did that. I stayed with the four at the lower end. I thought about there being still ten Pah-Utes, but I did not say anything. I did not want to seem afraid, if The Doer was not.

===Chinese===
“下午我们到了约塔塞伊（Yotatséyi）峡谷口。踪迹又新又清楚。干事汉告诉他们，峡谷那一端在纳帕尼山（Napani Mountain）半腰，在我们右手。那峡谷兜一个大弯，他说。他们若打那头出去，我们就永远逮不着了，他说。于是他说，拨三个人跟他走，他带他们照直横插过去。天黑前就能赶到那头，他说。然后明儿一早，我们两头一齐往里攻，就能堵住他们，他说。他们便照办了。我同那四个人留在下头这端。我心里念着派尤特人还剩十个呢，可我一声不吭。干事汉都不显怕，我也不想显出怕来。

===Original===
“We went a little way into the canyon and made camp. The chief American lent me a pair of magic tubes that he had, that you put to your eyes, and they made everything far away look near. I watched with them until I saw The Doer’s smoke signals. He signalled that they had had a fight, and the enemy were coming towards us. So we watched, but we saw no sign of them. It was dark soon after. We watched all night.

===Chinese===
“我们往峡谷里走了一小截，扎下营。那领头的美国人借给我一副神筒，他随身的物件，举到眼前，远处的东西便看着近了。我拿它瞭望，直到望见干事汉的烟讯号。他报的信是：他们交过手了，敌人正朝我们这边来。我们便警戒起来，却没见他们一点影子。天不久就黑透了。我们瞭望了一整夜。

===Original===
“When it was getting near dawn, but no light as yet, we started on foot. As soon as there was a little light, they sent me along the top of the talus along one wall, to look out for ambushes. The canyon was about five hundred paces wide; the walls were at least that high. The chief American watched me with those tubes. I would wave my hand, as a sign that I saw no one, and they would come forward, strung out across the canyon.

===Chinese===
“天临近破晓、还没亮的时候，我们步行出发。刚见一丝亮，他们就派我沿着一面崖壁下碎石坡的坡顶走，提防埋伏。峡谷约有五百步宽；两壁至少也有那么高。那领头的美国人拿神筒望着我。我挥一挥手，表示没见着人，他们便排开一线，横贯峡谷朝前推进。

===Original===
“It was full light when we got near where the canyon turned the corner. I went on ahead again, very slowly. I could look down into the sand and see the tracks that we had been following so long. I felt very much alone. I knew that man was watching me as I crept; that made me feel a little better. My father told me that the warpath was like stalking mountain sheep, only more exciting. I thought that there was more to it than that. Mountain sheep do not shoot back at you: mountain sheep are not people who are thinking about you just as hard as you are thinking about them.

===Chinese===
“大天亮时，我们挨近了峡谷拐角的地方。我又赶到前头去，走得极慢。我低头便望得见沙地，望见我们缀了这么久的那些蹄印。我感到说不出的孤单。我知道那个人正望着我潜行；这教我好受了些。我父亲说过，走征途就像潜近大角羊，只是更教人心跳。我想这里头的名堂还不止这些。大角羊不会朝你还枪：大角羊不是那样的人，一面苦苦算计你，正如你苦苦算计着他。

===Original===
“Then I saw them. They must have made camp up in the middle of Yotatséyi; now they were coming to try to get out this way. There were nine of them, in single file, riding carefully and watching. So I signalled to the Americans, and saw them take cover. I made myself as hard to see as I could. Suddenly I thought that I was enjoying myself a great deal.

===Chinese===
“接着我望见他们了。他们准是在约塔塞伊峡谷中段扎了营；这会儿正要打这一头夺路出去。他们一共九个，一路纵队，骑得小心，四下里张望。我便向美国人发信号，望见他们各自隐伏。我把身子藏得教人尽量瞧不见。忽然间我想：这滋味真是受用极了。

===Original===
“They came around the corner; now I looked right down on them, from behind a bush. One of them, looking around, looked straight at my bush. He was searching for people hiding. I thought that that bush was not nearly thick enough. His eyes passed on, and for just a moment I felt weak.

===Chinese===
“他们绕过拐角；这下我正从一丛灌木后头，居高临下望着他们。其中一个四下张望的，目光直直落在我这丛灌木上。他正在搜寻藏匿的人。我心想那丛灌木实在算不得密。他的目光移了过去，有一霎我只觉浑身发软。

===Original===
“Then the Americans started shooting. So I stood up and gave the war-cry and started. As I was shouting, I thought, they will hear the Coyote howl and know he’s a Wolf. There was a brave just below me in a buckskin shirt with beadwork on it, and a hat with a silver hatband. I noticed all about him while I shot at him. I wanted to kill him; I wanted it to be known that I had killed one. I saw my arrow in the air a long time; I saw it strike. He fell off his horse. Then I saw that he was not dead, and I was glad. I do not know why.

===Chinese===
“这时美国人开火了。我便站起身，发出战吼，冲了出去。我一面喊，一面想：他们听见郊狼嗥叫，就会知道那是一条狼。我下方正有一个勇士，穿一件缀珠绣的鹿皮衬衫，帽子上箍着银帽箍。我朝他放箭的当口，把他周身打量了个透。我要杀他；我要叫人知道我杀过一个。我的箭在空中飞了好久好久；我看见它中的。他从马上栽了下去。后来我见他没死，我心里高兴。我不知道为什么。

===Original===
“There were five of them down now, and the others put up their hands, so the Americans took them prisoners. One had been killed over in the other end of the canyon.

===Chinese===
“这时他们倒下了五个，其余的举起手来，美国人便把他们俘了。还有一个，是在峡谷那一头被打死的。

===Original===
“We went back to Oljeto, and they paid us as they said. Then we had ourselves purified from the blood, and we spent several dollars on canned food. I bought a big paper bag, as big as a hat, full of all different kinds of candy, and went home, and we all ate it.

===Chinese===
“我们回到奥尔赫托，他们如数付了钱。随后我们行了洁净礼，涤去血污，又花了几块钱买罐头吃。我买了一大纸袋糖果，帽子那么大的一袋，各式各样都有，带回家去，大家一块儿分着吃了。

===Original===
“When I was telling about what happened, I got to the part about thinking I was shot, and I started to laugh. I could not stop, I just laughed. So they started calling me by that name. That is all.”

===Chinese===
“每逢我讲起这段事，讲到自以为中了枪那一段，我就笑起来。止也止不住，光是笑。他们便拿那个名字叫起我来。就是这么回事。”

===Original===
Slim Girl said, “I am glad you are a man like that.” She thought to herself, he is a warrior. That was worth ten thousand schools. He must have been about eighteen. Where is basketball against that?

===Chinese===
瘦姑娘（Slim Girl）说：“我真高兴，你是这样一条汉子。”她心里想：他是个战士。单这一条，抵得上一万个学堂。他那时想必十八岁上下。拿篮球跟这个比，又算得了什么？
