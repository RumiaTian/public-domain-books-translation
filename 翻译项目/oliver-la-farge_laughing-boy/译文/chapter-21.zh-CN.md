## Chapter XXI / 第二十一章

### I / I

===Original===
Now began the four days of waiting. But just waiting was not enough; there had been no women to wail for her, no outcry of bereaved relatives; he would make it a vigil, all the four days should be one prayer. This was not an ordinary death.

===Chinese===
如今开始的是四天的等待。可光是等待还不够；没有女人们为她哭丧，没有亲眷们丧亲的哭喊；他要把它做成一场守夜，四天统统合成一篇祷告。这不是一场寻常的死亡。

===Original===
It was quite dark, and the snow still drifted down like waterlogged leaves falling through water. He rebuilt the fire till it blazed, arranged the saddle-blanket and his saddle for a reclining place, pulled the trade blanket about him, and began the vigil, staring at the distant blacker place in the blackness of the cliffs behind the snowflakes that marked the niche.

===Chinese===
天已全黑，雪还在飘落，像浸透水的叶子在水中沉降。他把火重新架起，直烧得熊熊，铺开鞍毯、放好马鞍，权作倚卧之处，把那条买卖来的毯子往身上一裹，开始守夜，眼睛盯着雪片后面崖壁的黑影里那处更黑的地方——龛室就在那儿。

===Original===
He tried to pray, but his mind kept wandering, reviewing incidents of their life together, happy and unhappy, but so full of life, so charged with her personality. He would forget that she was dead, he would just be thinking about her. The cold coming through his clothing would wash along his skin, a flake would touch his face, and he would remember.

===Chinese===
他试着祷告，心思却总往外跑，一遍遍过着他俩共同生活的种种情景，有甜有苦，却那样满含生气，那样浸透她的性子。他会忘了她已经死了，只顾想着她。透进衣裳的寒气漫过皮肤，一片雪落在脸上，他才又想起来。

===Original===
Now it is all over. Let it be altogether so. That horse is lucky; well, we shall go with her, too.

===Chinese===
如今一切都完了。就让它整个儿这样吧。那匹马有福气；好，咱们也随她去。

===Original===
He got his pony, took his saddle in his hand, and went back into the niche. The animal was nervous and wild with the darkness and the cold and the smell of death. It would not stand still. Later it was to occur to him as part of the remarkableness that he went unhesitatingly into that place after dark, but now he thought nothing of it. Now he was not a Navajo terrified of the dead, not an Indian, not an individual of any race, but a man who had buried his own heart.

===Chinese===
他牵过小马，一手提着马鞍，回到龛室里去。那牲口又惊又野，是被黑暗、寒冷和死亡的气味闹的，怎么也不肯站定。后来他想起，天黑之后他竟毫不犹豫地走进那个地方，也是这件事的奇处之一，可眼下他全没在意。此刻他不是一个怕死人的纳瓦霍，不是一个印第安人，不是任何族类的一分子，只是一个亲手埋掉了自己心的人。

===Original===
He selected his arrow.

===Chinese===
他挑出一支箭。

===Original===
“… It has not been in vain. You will remember me, I shall live in you.”

===Chinese===
“……这一切不算白费。你会记着我，在你身上，我还将活着。”

===Original===
Wind God had spoken her words in his mind. She would not like this. He put back the arrow in his quiver, and led the horse out to the fire. There he took off its rope, and hobbled it.

===Chinese===
她的这些话，是风神在他心里说出来的。她不会喜欢这样。他把箭放回箭袋，把马牵到火边。到了那里，他解下缰绳，给它绊上了腿。

===Original===
“Go see if you can find food and water, little brother. Go away and be happy.”

===Chinese===
“去找找草料和水吧，小兄弟。走远些，快快活活地过。”

===Original===
He returned to his vigil, collecting a large pile of dead wood for the fire, and making himself as comfortable as possible with his blankets and his saddle. He began to feel some fear, conscious of the nearness of her tomb, being so very much alone in that narrow canyon. He set himself to the task of realizing what had happened, and conceiving a continuing life without Slim Girl. It was not easy; he spent a long time in rebellion, or in a mere thronging of bitter emotions that made him throw his shoulders from side to side.

===Chinese===
他回到守夜的地方，给火堆拢起一大堆枯柴，又拿毯子和马鞍把自己安顿得尽量舒坦。他开始觉出些恐惧来——意识到她的墓近在旁边，又独自一人处在这条窄峡谷里。他给自己立下功课：认清已经发生的事，构想一个没有瘦姑娘、却仍要继续的人生。这不容易；他久久陷在抗拒里，或者说陷在一阵蜂拥而至的苦涩情绪里，搅得他把肩膀来回直摆。

===Original===
Jesting Squaw’s Son had been lucky. But in the end he was better off, because there had been that year and a half. Not for anything would he lose that. He began remembering again﻿—it was a kind of anodyne﻿—until he came back to the inevitable starting point. Then it was worse.

===Chinese===
打趣婆娘之子算是幸运的。可到头来还是他境遇更好，因为有过那一年半。拿什么都换，他也不肯失去它。他又开始回忆——回忆是一种止痛的东西——直到绕回那个绕不开的起点。那时节就更难熬了。

===Original===
After some hours he grew calmer, partly because of fatigue. The disaster was accepted and familiar; he told himself that he could see the life ahead growing, in a way, from what had gone before. Nothing could ever make him forget; what he was and always would be, what he did and thought, would always be conditioned by Slim Girl. The remainder of his life would be a monument to her. All this could not be changed or taken from him, he would never lose its mark. That was a comfort.

===Chinese===
过了几个钟头，他平静了些，一半是因为乏了。这场灾祸已经认下、变得熟悉；他对自己说，往后的日子，也算是从先前的一切里生长出来的。什么也不能叫他忘掉；他如今是什么人、永远是什么人，他做什么、想什么，都将永远由瘦姑娘塑成。他的余生将是献给她的一座碑。这一切改变不了，也夺不走，那印记他永远不会丢。这是一种安慰。

===Original===
He was thinking this way with his intellect, it did not really go inside of him. It was still just platitudes.

===Chinese===
他这些念头都是脑子在转，并没有真进到心里去。仍旧不过是些空话。

===Original===
He became more aware of things about him, the cold, the fire, the snow. Flakes fell into the flames with little hisses, and he remembered his dream.

===Chinese===
他对身周的事物更有了知觉，寒冷、火、雪。雪片落进火焰，嘶地一声轻响，他想起了自己的梦。

===Original===
“Slayer of Enemy Gods” she called me. But Slayer of Enemy Gods spared the Cold Woman and Old Age Woman and Poverty People and Hunger People. She tried to kill the Hunger People; I thought she could. If we had not tried to do that, we should have been living happily within the Navajo country long ago. She was too daring, she wanted perfection.

===Chinese===
她管我叫过“诛敌神者”。可诛敌神者饶过了冷女人（Cold Woman）、老年女人（Old Age Woman）、贫穷之民（Poverty People）和饥饿之民。她却要去杀饥饿之民；我原以为她办得到。倘若我们不曾起意去做那件事，我们早就在纳瓦霍的地界里乐乐和和地过活了。她太胆大，她要的是十全十美。

===Original===
By whatever means she got it, we had perfection. But it could not have gone on. We are not divine. Or I am not; she had made herself above Earth People. What has happened to me, it is like what happened to many people long ago. It happened to Taught Himself and the Magician’s Daughter, to Reared in a Mountain when he went through the homes of the gods, to Eagles’ Friend when he went to the sky. They went away and saw something better than they had ever known. They did not try to bring something too good for earth back to earth. But they did not﻿—lose﻿—Slim Girl.

===Chinese===
不论她用的是何等法子，我们有过十全十美了。可它长不了。我们不是神。至少我不是；她已把自己抬到地上之民之上。我遭逢的这件事，就像很久以前许多人遭逢过的。自学汉（Taught Himself）和巫师之女（Magician’s Daughter）遭逢过，山中养大走遍诸神居所的时候遭逢过，鹰之友上天去的时候也遭逢过。他们走了远路，见着了比自己从来所知的更好的东西。他们不曾想把好得不宜人间的东西带回人间来。可他们不曾——失去——瘦姑娘。

===Original===
His head fell forward on his knees and he stopped thinking. He was exhausted, and shortly fell asleep in that position. He woke when he began to fall over, very cold, and thought he must have drowsed. The fire was low. The snowfall ceased and dawn came limping.

===Chinese===
他的头垂到膝上，他不再想了。他筋疲力尽，不一会儿就那样睡着了。身子开始歪倒时他醒了，冷极了，心想方才准是打了盹。火塌得低了。雪住了，黎明一瘸一拐地来。

===Original===
The first day was sparkling, crisp, and sunny. The first day was one of stunned, dull realization. He wandered about uncertainly and drugged himself with detailed, long-drawn-out memories. And in the end, he would return to the beginning of his circle and stand or sit motionless and groan. It was a long day and a strange one; later he did not remember it clearly.

===Chinese===
第一天清朗、干冷、明亮。第一天却是懵懵的、迟钝的省悟。他没个定准地转来转去，拿细碎的、拖得长长的回忆麻醉自己。到末了，他总会兜回那个圈子的起点，站着或坐着不动，呻吟。那是一段漫长而古怪的日子；后来他记不真切了。

===Original===
The second night he tried hard not to sleep, but it was hard, with cold, hunger, fatigue, and the fire. He dozed a good deal, and his memories became very accurate dreams into which slowly would creep a sense of horror without reason, until he woke, not knowing he had slept, continuing the thought and the mood. He tried to pray, but it was chiefly ejaculations and the names of the gods. It was an endless and terrible night.

===Chinese===
第二夜他极力不睡，可是难，又是冷，又是饿，又是乏，还有那火。他打了好多回盹，回忆变得毫厘不爽，成了梦，一种无缘无故的恐怖慢慢渗进去，直到他惊醒——不觉得自己睡着过——接着先前的念头和心绪往下走。他试着祷告，出口的却多半是呼喊和众神的名字。那是一个无尽而可怕的夜。

===Original===
Daylight, when at last it came, was a release. He shook himself, thinking, I must be calm, I must think clearly. This is no time for wandering without getting anywhere. He quieted himself for a time, achieving a state of apparent resignation which enabled him to pray, but the oft-repeated *hozoji* sounded hollow. He did not really think there was anything beautiful; he was just acting as he thought he ought.

===Chinese===
天光终于来了，是一种解脱。他抖擞一下，想道：我得镇静，我得想清楚。这可不是瞎转悠而一无所获的时候。他让自己安分了一阵，做出一副貌似认命的样子，得以祷告，可那念了又念的霍卓吉听着空洞。他心里并不真觉得有什么美的东西；他只是在照自己以为应该的样子做。

===Original===
Plenty of people had died in his neighbourhood; there had been mourning and grief, when everyone had stayed close to the hogans for four days. But this was different. He had seen the bereaved, he had seen real sorrow upon them, but he could not believe that they had felt as he did.

===Chinese===
他那一带死过不少人；有过哀哭和悲伤，那时人人紧挨着霍根屋守足四天。可这一回不同。他见过死了亲人的人，见过他们脸上真切的哀恸，但他不能相信他们尝过他这样的滋味。

===Original===
He was alone in more than the physical sense. No one, not even Jesting Squaw’s Son, could come near him. All his life, wherever he was, however long he lived, he would remain alone. It would always be like this. The one companionship in the world had gone; when the sun has been destroyed for a man, what comfort is there in a world of moonlight?

===Chinese===
他的孤独不止是身子上的。没有一个人——连打趣婆娘之子也算上——能走近他。他这一辈子，无论在哪里，无论活多久，都是孤身一人。永远都会是这样。世上唯一的那份相伴去了；一个人的太阳既已毁掉，月光的世界里还有什么安慰可言？

===Original===
He had nothing to do in the canyon save tend his fire and think. He would get hold of a thought, work it over and over until he lost all sense of proportion towards it, and finally put it in a phrase or a simile, so that it obtained substance and could not be dismissed.

===Chinese===
在峡谷里他无事可做，只有守火、想事。他会抓住一个念头，翻来覆去地掂弄，直到对它全然失了分寸，末了把它凝成一句话或一个比方，这样它便有了实体，再撵不走了。

===Original===
He hated to watch it grow dark; he felt afraid of the night. He did not want to be shut into that little space of firelight with all the things he was thinking. Alone, alone, all life alone, all life carrying this pain inside himself. He might as well die. But she wanted him to live. It was the third night, and he was approaching the stage of visions. Outlines of things dimly seen in the starlight changed and assumed startling forms. He became the audience listening to unseen people arguing as to whether he ought to kill himself or not. He knew he ought to live, but he could not control which side might win.

===Chinese===
他不忍看天黑下去；他怕夜。他不想被关进那一小圈火光里，同满脑子的念头关在一处。孤独，孤独，一生孤独，一生把这份疼揣在自己心里。不如死了倒好。可她要他活着。这是第三夜，他临近了见异象的地步。星光里影影绰绰的轮廓变了样，显出骇人的形状。他成了听众，听着看不见的人们争论他该不该自尽。他知道该活着，可哪一边占上风，他管不住。

===Original===
He couldn’t always follow very well what was going on. Extraneous things intruded themselves. There were people all around, pitying him. It was being insisted upon that loneliness and pain were not worth enduring for a whole lifetime, without purpose.

===Chinese===
他并不总能跟得很上眼前的光景。不相干的东西自己闯进来。四下里全是人，都在怜悯他。有人一味主张：这孤独和疼痛既然没有个目的，犯不着忍上一辈子。

===Original===
“But I have to live for her,” he said aloud, and thought hard about her.

===Chinese===
“可我得为她活着，”他出声说，一面苦苦地想她。

===Original===
Then he saw her, standing on the other side of the fire. He started to his feet, choking with all a Navajo’s terror of the walking dead. He was dissolved in fear, but she was gone. He was alone, the voices were gone, the people. He sat down, trembling, and quite wide awake. Evidently he did not want to die, but he had no will to live; he did not know himself, it would be wrong to make a decision now. Little by little he grew drowsy, and dozed in snatches. Perhaps her coming was a good thing; one would not expect her ghost to be like other people’s.

===Chinese===
随后他看见她了，站在火堆那一边。他霍地站起，纳瓦霍人对行走死人的那种恐惧把他噎得透不过气。他吓得整个儿化掉了，她却已经不见。他又是一个人了，声音没有了，人也没有了。他坐下来，发着抖，人全然醒透。看来他并不想死，可他也没有求生的意志；他不认得自己了，这时候做决定是不对的。一点一点地他又困了，断断续续打起盹来。也许她的来是件好事；本来也不该指望她的鬼魂跟旁人的一样。

===Original===
This, too, became a discussion outside himself. The spirits of the dead are bad; if they walk, it is for destruction. She is different, she would come to protect him. She would lead him to some frightful end. But no one could imagine blue fire coming out of her eyes and mouth. It went on and on. There was an outline of something he had not seen before, it moved and he felt his scalp crawl. Then he let out a deep sigh and related. It was a bush, some little distance away. Dawn was coming.

===Chinese===
这也成了身外的一场争论。死人的魂是凶的；它们若行走，是为了毁灭。她不同，她会来护着他。她会把他引到什么可怕的结局里去。可谁也想像不出她的眼睛和嘴里会喷出蓝火来。争了又争。有个他从未见过的东西的轮廓，它一动，他觉出头皮发麻。随后他深深叹了口气，定下神来。原来是一丛灌木，隔着一小段路。天快亮了。

===Original===
A little water and the clear sunshine revived him for a time, but soon he was tired and miserable again, listlessly occupying himself with gathering more firewood, bringing a branch at a time, setting aside without interest those that would do for making a sweat-bath. Later, he thought of that day as the day of treason. He protracted his occupation, being meticulous about finding all the wood in one section before moving to the next. He thought of the eventual bath, which should wash him clean of the taint of death; he wished it might wash out his mind. He thought that if he never had met her, he would be happy now, remembering Slender Hair riding by and telling him of the dance that was to be, remembering himself riding, singing, down to Tsé Lani, the firelight and that girl, remembering what chance, what meeting of eyes in a crowd, his uncle’s tactlessness, had put an end to a young man who had no cares.

===Chinese===
一点水，清朗的日头，让他振作了一阵，可不久他又疲惫、凄惶起来，无精打采地忙着多拾些柴火，一次抱一根枝子，顺手把够做汗浴的撂在一边，心不在焉。后来他把那一天想成背叛的日子。他把手里的活计拉得老长，细细致致，一块地段的柴拾尽了才挪到下一块。他想着最后那一场浴，它该把他身上死亡的秽气洗净；但愿也能把他的心思洗空。他想，倘若从未遇见她，他如今是有福的——记着细发骑马路过、告诉他要有一场舞；记着他自己骑着马、唱着歌下特塞拉尼去，那火光，那一个姑娘；记着是怎样的机缘、怎样人堆里目光的一遇，再加上舅舅那不知轻重的一句，结果了一个无忧无虑的年轻人。

===Original===
The thought stayed with him as he wandered about kicking up the snow for bits of dead wood. He knew that it was an entity in itself, and saw it as a tall old man who leered as he walked beside and slightly behind him, a bad, strong old man. The old man kept at him about it, that he had been a fool, that he should have avoided all this. And he thought, in answer, that it was too late now, why couldn’t he be left alone? He tried to explain to himself, to the old man, that he couldn’t have helped doing what he did. He himself, as a third person, repeated that all the suffering was worth while for the happy months, but the old man only sneered. He tried to get himself in hand, and think of a new design for a belt, but that was useless. He would walk around for a long time without looking for wood at all. Picking up his pony’s tracks, he followed them out into the main canyon until he saw the horse in a sheltered place under the east wall, then, realizing how far he had gone from the place of vigil, hurried back. Everything had gone to pieces, he did everything wrong. The old man had waited for him, he was triumphant over this breach of observance.

===Chinese===
他四下里踢开积雪找枯柴，那念头一路跟着他不放。他知道它自身已成了一件实体，便把它看成一个高个子老头，贼忒兮兮地笑着，走在他身旁、稍稍靠后的地方，一个又凶又壮的老头。老头拿这件事数落他个没完，说他是个傻瓜，说他本该躲开这一切。他心里回嘴：如今已经晚了，为什么就不能叫他清静？他试着对自己、也对老头解释：他做下的事，是躲也躲不开的。他自己又站作第三个人，重说一遍：为了那些快活的月份，这些苦都值得；老头只是冷笑。他想把自己管住，琢磨一条腰带的新花样，可是没用。他常常转悠上好一阵，压根儿没找柴。踏着小马的蹄印，他一路跟出去，进了主峡谷，才望见马儿待在东壁下一处背风的地方，这才醒悟自己离守夜的地方已走出多远，急忙赶回去。一切都散了架，他事事都做岔了。老头一直在等他，为这一遭失守得意洋洋。

===Original===
Nightfall was at least a change. Having plenty of wood, he built the fire up high, and went to some trouble to make himself comfortable. This was the fourth night, he was more or less out of his head. The old man had long ceased to be a personification and become a reality; he got in under the same blanket and hammered, hammered at him about the unfortunate past. Laughing Boy saw an empty, drifting future, always with this old man. He saw himself a long time from now, and the dead boy who had ridden down to Tsé Lani crying across a gap full of darkness to the empty husk of a man who had destroyed him. He tried to call on the gods, but there came only Hunger People, Old Age, and Cold Woman. Yellow Singer and his wife were there, looking sorry for him in that unpleasant, understanding way, like the day he was married. They all looked at him that way.
