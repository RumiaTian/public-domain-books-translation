## Chapter XIII / 第十三章

### I / I

===Original===
They rode away from T’o Tlakai in gay company﻿—Bay Horse and Bow’s Son, Tall Brave from T’ies Napornss and his wife, and half a dozen others, men and women, returning towards T’o Tlikahn, Tsébitai, and Seinsaidesah. It had turned sharply cold, the ponies went well; they played and raced, showing off their jewelry and best clothes and horsemanship﻿—all young people. Bay Horse smoked on a dead twig, blowing out clouds of breath.

===Chinese===
他们一伙人欢欢喜喜地骑马离开特奥·特莱凯（T’o Tlakai）——枣红马（Bay Horse）、弓之子（Bow’s Son）、从蒂斯纳波恩斯（T’ies Napornss）来的高勇（Tall Brave）两口子，还有半打别的男女，一路朝着特奥·特莱凯、特塞比泰（Tsébitai）和塞恩赛德萨（Seinsaidesah）各自回去。天气骤然冷了下来，小马们脚下走得正欢；众人一路玩耍赛马，炫耀着自己的首饰、最好的衣裳和骑术——全是年轻人。枣红马叼着一截枯枝当烟抽，一口一口喷出大团白气。

===Original===
Gomulli T’o trading post stood on a flat, bare shoulder of sand and rock, a level space of half a dozen acres, rising to the west, falling to the east. There was a corral with sides six feet high, and the L-shaped one-story house of stone and adobe with a corrugated iron roof. Around it was nothing green, nothing varied, just sand and rocks, some tin cans, part of a rotted blue shirt. There was no relief. In summer the drenching sun searched out its barren walls; in winter the wind was bleak around it. It was just something dumped there, a thing made by man, contributing nothing, in the midst of majestic desolation. Beyond its level were red-brown cliffs, dull orange bald-rock, yellow sand, leading away to blend into a kind of purplish brown with blue clouds of mountains for background. This did not belong; it was crushed and empty.

===Chinese===
戈穆利·特奥交易站立在一面平坦、光秃的沙石肩坡上，一片方圆六来英亩的平地，向西缓缓抬升，向东缓缓沉降。有一座围栏六英尺高的畜栏，还有一栋L形的单层石坯屋子，屋顶铺着瓦楞铁皮。四下里没有一点绿色，没有一点变化，只有沙子和石头，几只空铁皮罐，半件糟烂了的蓝衬衫。毫无调剂。夏天，倾泻而下的烈日把它光秃秃的墙垣搜刮个遍；冬天，寒风在它周遭刮得凄清。它不过是随手倾倒在那儿的一件东西，一件人造出来的物件，什么也添补不上，兀立在一片雄浑的荒凉正中。出了这片平地，是红褐色的峭壁、暗橙色的秃岩、黄沙，一路引开去，融成一种泛紫的褐色，背后衬着蓝色云霭般的群山。它与此地格格不入；被压得灰溜溜的，空落落的。

===Original===
Besides the ones belonging to his own party, Laughing Boy noted two other ponies hitched by the corral. He made his fast with a bowknot, the animal being rather unenterprising and not having learned how to untie them. He looped the reins over the saddle-horn and sauntered to the door of the store, trailing the bridle carelessly, and adjusting his recently acquired hat. It was a stiff-brimmed felt, with the crown undented and the string tied under the chin, Indian fashion, becoming him well. He gave it a wicked slant.

===Chinese===
除了自己一伙的马，笑娃还留意到畜栏边拴着另外两匹小马。他把自己的马打了个蝴蝶结拴牢——这牲口颇不求上进，还没学会怎么解这样的结。他把缰绳盘上鞍角，晃晃悠悠朝店门走去，手里漫不经心地拖着那副笼头，一面正了正新近置办的帽子。那是一顶硬檐毡帽，帽顶没压过凹痕，帽带照印第安人的做派系在下巴底下，衬他衬得很好。他把帽檐斜出一副促狭的角度。

They fooled him and exasperated him until he strove frantically to outcheat them, and that didn’t work either. He had no idea of how to attract their trade, nor of how to circumvent their sharpness. It was always like this. Two men had been there since he opened the store in the morning, making one nickel purchase, and now none of these others wanted to buy. They just wanted to talk.
