## V Sid Insists on Ghostgirls / 西德坚持要幽灵少女

===Original===
> Whenever I take up a newspaper and read it, I fancy I see ghosts creeping between the lines. There must be ghosts all over the world. They must be as countless as the grains of the sands, it seems to me.

===Chinese===
> 每当我拿起一份报纸来读，我总仿佛看见幽灵在字里行间爬动。全世界一定到处都是幽灵。依我看，它们必定多得数不清，像沙粒一样。（译注：易卜生《当我们死而复苏时》。）

---

===Original===
My Elizabethan boy friend put his fists on his hips and laid down the law to us as if we were a lot of nervous children who’d been playing too hard.

“Look you, masters, this is a Recuperation Station and I am running it as such. A plague of all operations! I care not if the frame of things disjoints and the whole Change World goes to ruin, but you, warrior maid, are going to rest and drink more wine slowly before you tell your tale and your colleagues are going to be properly companioned. No questions, anyone. Beau, and you love us, give us a lively tune.”

Kaby relaxed a little and let him put his hand carefully against her back in token of support and she said grudgingly, “All right, Fat Belly.”

Then, so help me, to the tune of the Muskrat Ramble, which I’d taught Beau, we got girls for those two E.T.s and everybody properly paired up.

===Chinese===
我那位伊丽莎白时代的男朋友叉起双拳抵在胯上，对我们发号施令，仿佛我们是一群玩过了头、神经紧张的娃娃。

「都听着，各位师傅，这里是疗养站，我就按疗养站的规矩来办。一切行动都见鬼去吧！哪怕乾坤的架子散了套、整个变更世界塌了台，我也不管；可是你，女战士，得先歇着，慢慢再喝点酒，然后才讲你的故事；你的两位同僚呢，也得有人好好作陪。谁都不许提问。博，你要还爱我们，就给咱们来支热闹的曲子。」

卡比松泛了一点，任他小心翼翼把手贴上她的背，以示扶持，她勉勉强强地说：「行吧，胖肚皮。」

接着——老天作证——伴着《麝鼠漫步》（Muskrat Ramble，我教给博的曲子）的调子，我们给那两位 E.T. 各配上了姑娘，人人得到了妥帖的配对。

===Original===
Right here I want to point out that a lot of the things they say in the Change World about Recuperation Stations simply aren’t so—and anyway they always leave out nine-tenths of it. The Soldiers that come through the Door are looking for a good time, sure, but they’re hurt real bad too, every one of them, deep down in their minds and hearts, if not always in their bodies or so you can see it right away.

Believe me, a temporal operation is no joke, and to start with, there isn’t one person in a hundred who can endure to be cut from his lifeline and become a really wide-awake Doubleganger—a Demon, that is—let alone a Soldier. What does a badly hurt and mixed-up creature need who’s been fighting hard? *One individual* to look out for him and feel for him and patch him up, and it helps if the one is of the opposite sex—that’s something that goes beyond species.

There’s your basis for the Place and the wild way it goes about its work, and also for most other Recuperation Stations or Entertainment Spots. The name Entertainer can be misleading, but I like it. She’s got to be a lot more than a good party girl—or boy—though she’s got to be that too. She’s got to be a nurse and a psychologist and an actress and a mother and a practical ethnologist and a lot of things with longer names—and a reliable friend.

===Chinese===
我正好要在这儿指出来：变更世界里流传的那些关于疗养站的闲话，多半根本不是那么回事——再说，他们讲起来总漏掉十之八九。打门里进来的士兵是想寻个痛快，这没错，可他们个个也伤得很重，伤在头脑和心灵的深处，哪怕不一定伤在身上、也不一定一眼看得出来。

相信我，时间行动可不是闹着玩的；头一条，一百个人里也挑不出一个，受得了从自己的生命线上被割下来、变成一个真正彻底清醒的二重身——也就是魔鬼——更别提当士兵了。一个刚恶战过、伤得厉害又晕头转向的生灵，需要什么？需要*单独一个人*来看顾他、体贴他、把他修补好；而这一个人若是异性，效果更佳——这是超出了物种界限的事。

这就是场所的根基，也是它那套疯疯癫癫办事路数的根基，还是大多数别的疗养站或娱乐站的根基。「招待员」这名目容易叫人误会，可我喜欢。她得远远不止是个好派的派对女郎——或男孩——虽说这一条她也得够格。她得同时是护士、心理学家、女演员、母亲、实务民俗学家，外加一长串名头更长的行当——还要是个靠得住的朋友。

---

===Original===
None of us are all those things perfectly or even near it. We just try. But when the call comes, Entertainers have to forget grudges and gripes and envies and jealousies—and remember, they’re lively people with sharp emotions—because there isn’t any time then for anything but *help and don’t ask who*!

And, deep inside her, a good Entertainer doesn’t care who. Take the way it shaped up this time. It was pretty clear to me I ought to shift to Illy, although I wasn’t quite easy in my mind about leaving Erich, because the Lunan was a long time from home and, after all, Erich was among anthropoids. Ilhilihis needed someone who was *simpatico*.

===Chinese===
我们谁也没法把这些角色样样演到满分，连边都沾不上。我们只是尽力。可号令一到，招待员就必须忘掉宿怨、牢骚、眼红和醋意——别忘了，他们是情感锋利的活泼人——因为那时候容不下任何别的事，只容得下*救人，别问是谁*！

而且，一个出色的招待员，心底深处根本不在乎那「谁」是谁。就拿这一回的格局来说吧。我心里明明白白，我该转去照应伊尔利，虽说撇下埃里希让我多少有点不踏实——因为这位月球人离家太久太远，而埃里希好歹身处类人猿的窝里。伊尔希利希斯需要一个*意气相投*（*simpatico*）的人。

===Original===
I like Illy and not just because he is a sort of tall cross between a spider monkey and a persian cat—though that is a handsome combo when you come to think of it. I like him for himself. So when he came in all lopped and shaky after a mean operation, I was the right person to look out for him. Now I’ve made my little speech and know-nothings in the Change World can go on making their bum jokes. But I ask you, how could an arrangement between Illy and me be anything but Platonic?

We might have had some octopoid girls and nymphs in stock—Sid couldn’t be sure until he checked—but Ilhilihis and Sevensee voted for real people and I knew Sid saw it their way. Maud squeezed Mark’s hand and tripped over to Sevensee (“Those are sharp hoofs you got, man”—she’s picked up some of my language, like she has everything else), though Beau did frown over his shoulder at Lili from the piano, maybe to argue that she ought to take on the E.T., as Mark had been a real casualty and could use live nursing. But it was plain as day to anybody but Beau that Bruce and Lili were a big thing and the last to be disturbed.

===Chinese===
我喜欢伊尔利，倒不光因为他像蜘蛛猴和波斯猫杂交出的一副高挑身段——虽说这么一想，这组合也真叫俊。我喜欢的是他本人。所以，当他经历了一场卑劣的行动、缺着触手、浑身发颤地进来时，照应他的正该是我。好，我的小小演说讲完了，变更世界里那些一无所知的家伙尽可以继续编他们的烂笑话。可我倒要问问你：我和伊尔利之间的安排，除了柏拉图式的，还能是别的什么？

库房里兴许还存着几位章鱼姑娘和宁芙——西德不查一查也不敢说死——可伊尔希利希斯和七观都投了真人一票，我也看得出西德随他们的意。莫德捏了捏马克的手，蹦蹦跳跳奔七观去了（「你这蹄子可够尖利的，伙计」——她捡了我一些口头语，就像她捡起其他一切那样快），只是博从钢琴那边回头朝莉莉皱了皱眉，兴许是想争一句：该她来接待这位 E.T.，因为马克是货真价实的伤员，用得上活人护理。可除了博谁都看得出，布鲁斯和莉莉正是一对天雷地火，最碰不得。

===Original===
Erich acted stiffly hurt at losing me, but I knew he wasn’t. He thinks he has a great technique with Ghostgirls and he likes to show it off, and he really is pretty slick at it, if you go for that sort of thing and—yang my yin!—who doesn’t at times?

===Chinese===
埃里希装出一副失去我而僵僵的委屈样，可我知道他才不委屈。他自认对付幽灵少女有一套绝技，又爱显摆，而且他那手确实相当溜——如果你好这一口的话——而且——给我的阴来点阳！——谁又没有好这一口的时候呢？

===Original===
And when Sid formally wafted the Countess out of Stores—a real blonde stunner in a white satin hobble skirt with a white egret swaying up from her tiny hat, way ahead of Maud and Lili and me when it comes to looks, though transparent as cigarette smoke—and when Erich clicked his heels and bowed over her hand and proudly conducted her to a couch, black Svengali to her Trilby, and started to German-talk some life into her with much head cocking and toothy smiling and a flow of witty flattery, and when she began to flirt back and the dream look in her eyes sharpened hungrily and focused on him—well, then I knew that Erich was happy and felt he was doing proud by the Reichswehr. No, my little commandant wasn’t worrying me on that score.

===Chinese===
再说，当西德郑重其事地把伯爵夫人（the Countess）从库房区里请出来——一位真正的金发尤物，白色缎面窄摆裙，小帽上摇曳着一枝白鹭羽，论容貌把莫德、莉莉和我甩出几条街，虽说透明得像香烟的烟——当埃里希碰响靴跟，吻她的手，风风光光把她引到一张躺椅上，当她的黑斯文加利，配他那特丽尔比（译注：Svengali 与 Trilby，杜·莫里耶小说《软帽子》里的催眠大师与受其摆布的歌女），歪着脑袋、龇着牙笑，用滔滔不绝的机智恭维外加德语，给她嘴里说进一点生气，而她也回抛起媚眼、眼里那副梦茫茫的神色饿津津地聚焦到他身上——好吧，那时候我就知道，埃里希是快活的，还觉得他给国防军争了光。不，我那位小指挥官在那一头上不叫我操心。

---

===Original===
Mark had drawn a Greek hetaera, name of Phryne; I suppose not the one who maybe still does the famous courtroom striptease back in Athens, and he was waking her up with little sips of his scotch and soda, though, from some looks he’d flashed, I got the idea Kaby was the kid he really went for. Sid was coaxing the fighting gal to take some high-energy bread and olives along with the wine, and, for a wonder, Doc seemed to be carrying on an animated and rational conversation with Sevensee and Maud, maybe comparing notes on the Northern Venusian Shallows, and Beau had got on to Panther Rag, and Bruce and Lili were leaning on the piano, smiling very appreciatively, but talking to each other a mile a minute.

Illy turned back from inspecting them all and squeaked, “Animals with clothes are so refreshing, dahling! Like you’re all carrying banners!”

===Chinese===
马克抽到的是一位希腊赫泰拉，名叫弗里妮（Phryne）；想必不是雅典那边也许还在重演法庭脱衣名场面的那一位。他正用他的威士忌苏打一小口一小口把她唤醒，不过，从他抛过去的几记眼风判断，卡比才是他真正相中的妞。西德正哄着那位女斗士就着酒吃几口高能面包和橄榄；而说来稀奇，大夫似乎正同七观和莫德进行一场热烈而条理清楚的谈话，没准在切磋金星北浅海的心得；博已经弹到了《黑豹拉格泰姆》（Panther Rag）；布鲁斯和莉莉倚着钢琴，笑得满怀欣赏，嘴里却互相说个不停，一分钟一里地。

伊尔利把众人巡视了一圈，回过头吱吱道：「穿衣裳的动物们真让人耳目一新，达令！就像你们个个都举着旗！」

===Original===
Maybe he had something there, though my banners were kind of Ash Wednesday, a charcoal gray sweater and skirt. He looked at my mouth with a tentacle to see how I was smiling and he squeaked softly, “Do I seem dull and commonplace to you, Greta girl, because I haven’t got banners? Just another Zombie from a billion years in your past, as gray and lifeless as Luna is today, not as when she was a real dreamy sister planet simply bursting with air and water and feather forests. Or am I as strangely interesting to you as you are to me, girl from a billion years in my future?”

“Illy, you’re sweet,” I told him, giving him a little pat. I noticed his fur was still vibrating nervously and I decided the heck with Sid’s orders, I’m going to pump him about what he was doing with Kaby and the satyr. Couldn’t have him a billion years from home and bottled up, too. Besides, I was curious.

===Chinese===
这话也许有点道理，虽说我的旗有点「圣灰星期三」的味道：炭灰色的毛衣加裙子。他用一条触手端详我的嘴，看我笑得如何，然后轻轻地吱吱道：「就因为我没打旗号，我在你眼里就显得乏味、平常吗，格蕾塔姑娘？不过是来自你那十亿年过去的又一个僵尸，灰扑扑、没生气，跟今天的月亮一样——可不是当年那颗名副其实、如梦似幻的姐妹行星，满得要溢出空气、海洋和羽毛森林。又或者，我在你眼里，也像你在我眼里一样，是那样一种奇异的有趣吗，来自我这十亿年未来的姑娘？」

「伊尔利，你真贴心。」我对他说，轻轻拍了拍他。我注意到他的毛还在紧张地颤，我便打定主意：西德的命令见鬼去吧，我要套一套他跟卡比和半人羊到底在干什么。总不能让他离家十亿年，心里的事还闷着罐。再说，我也好奇。
