## XVII The Summer Palace and Its Grounds / 第十七章 颐和园及其苑囿

===Original===
The Summer Palace, the Empress Dowager’s favorite residence, is really a superb domain. Its naturally picturesque situation among the beautiful Western Hills, sixteen miles from Peking, has been improved wherever possible, by the devices of art. The many buildings that constitute an Oriental Palace have been most picturesquely grouped on the banks of its great lake. The eminences and natural undulations have all been made the most of as sites for Palaces and temples, and the grounds are laid out with all the art the Chinese landscape artist has at his command.

===Chinese===
颐和园是皇太后最为钟爱的行宫，实为一处极壮丽的苑囿。其地坐落於秀美的西山之中，距北京十六英里，天然形胜本已绝胜，复经匠心巧思，凡可增益之处无不锦上添花。构成一座东方宫苑的众多殿宇楼阁，错落有致地布列于其大湖之畔。山丘高地、地势起伏之处，皆被尽善利用，以为宫苑寺观之基址；而整座苑囿的布局，则尽显中国造园家胸中的全部匠心。

===Original===
The buildings of the Palace proper, where Their Majesties and their suites live, are all massed in one great town-like group at the southeastern end of the lake. In this group are the Theater, with its courts, and the Great Audience Hall. Palaces, temples, summerhouses, tea-booths, dot the whole surface of the great park, and all the vantage-points have been utilized for constructions.

===Chinese===
正宫的一群建筑——即皇上、皇太后及随扈人等所居之处——悉数集中成一片城镇般的庞大建筑群，坐落於湖的东南端。这片建筑群之中，有戏楼及其院落，亦有正大光明殿。宫苑、寺观、凉亭、茶肆，点缀着整座广阔御园的各处，凡属形胜之地，皆已用以兴筑。

===Original===
A beautiful white marble terrace runs the length of the southern side of the lake. Pavilions at intervals vary the monotony of this line or give accent to the natural indentations of the banks. Picturesque landing-places, with their marble steps lapped by the waters of the lake, also lend their variety to this terrace surmounted by its beautiful lotus balustrade.

===Chinese===
一道秀美的汉白玉石台沿湖的南岸绵延。台之上每隔一段便有一座亭阁，或打破这道线条的单调，或恰好烘托出湖岸天然凹入的轮廓。一座座如画的码头，其大理石的阶矶被湖水轻轻拍打，也为这道石台平添了几分变化，台之上更环以秀美的莲花栏杆。

===Original===
The highest of the hills in the park of the Summer Palace is crowned by the Great Temple of the Ten Thousand Buddhas. This is approached by hundreds of steps, which lead up from the broadest part of the marble terrace over the lake. Beautiful Palaces are built along this terraced height. Picturesque *pai-lou* (memorial arches) are built at such beautiful points, that Nature herself seems to have designed these positions for them.

===Chinese===
颐和园园内最高的那座山，山顶上坐落着大万佛寺。由大理石台最宽处跨湖而上，需登数百级石阶方能抵达。沿着这一带层叠的高地，建有秀美的宫苑。那如画的牌楼（纪念性的牌坊）便建在这些绝胜之处，仿佛大自然亲手为它们择定了位置。

===Original===
A fair, verdure-clad island lies peacefully on the bosom of the lake, and the Palace and temple built thereon seem a part of the natural formation of stone out of which they rise. A graceful seventeen-arched bridge of white marble connects this island with the northern bank of the lake.

===Chinese===
一座苍翠欲滴的秀丽小岛静谧地偎依於湖上，岛上所建的宫苑与寺观，仿佛便是其下岩层自然生长而出的一部分。一座姿态优雅的十七孔汉白玉桥，将这座小岛与湖的北岸连接起来。

===Original===
The canal from Peking, which feeds the lake, winds in and out of the grounds in such graceful meanderings as to seem some fair mountain stream. The outlets to the lakes are spanned by the graceful camel-backed bridges that only the Chinese architects build. Nature and art are everywhere so blended, so harmonized, it is difficult to tell which is which. The simple lines and beautiful proportions and harmonious colors of the one-storied Chinese buildings make even these seem almost a part of the landscape.

===Chinese===
自北京而来的运河——湖水即由其补给——蜿蜒穿行於苑囿之间，那柔和的回环曲折，竟仿佛一道秀美的山涧。各处湖水出口，皆有姿态优美的驼背石桥飞架其上，此种桥唯中国的匠人方能造得。自然与人工处处交融，处处和谐，竟令人难以分辨孰为天成、孰为人巧。中国的一层建筑，线条简洁，比例秀美，色彩调和，便使这些殿宇也几乎成了这片山水的一部分。

===Original===
Chinese architecture—and one grows to admire it very much when studied in its own environment—is tent-building, carried to its greatest perfection and made enduring by the use of materials that last. The grouping of the Chinese buildings is on the same order as the congregation of tents of some roving tribe of Nomads. The downward curve and upturned tilt of their roofs is but the natural slope of the canvas and its uplifting by the tent-poles. These slender tent-poles have developed into the supporting pillars of the verandah, and the raised canvas door of the tent has grown into its buoyantly curved roof. The ornamental eaves are but the solidified silken fringes and embroidered valances of the tents of old. The curious roof ornamentations of the modern Chinese house replace the weights that held the tent-canvas steady. These weights, from rough stones, have now become carven images, cunningly wrought. The Chinese even erect their houses as their ancestors raised their tents. The builder places the columns and puts on the roof before the walls are built. Except the pagoda—and this even seems like so many superposed tents—the Chinese building remains today, in spite of its elaborate roofs, its lacquered pillars, and elaborate ornamentation, like some splendid tent, grown into greater fixity and beautified by some magician’s wand. It is admirably suited to the calm pastoral landscapes in which it rests, and seems a part of Nature itself, and is never out of keeping with its surroundings!

===Chinese===
中国建筑——若在其本来的环境中细细观摩，便会逐渐深为欣赏——实乃帐幕之建筑，发展至极致，并以经久的材料使之坚固长存。中国建筑的群组布局，与游牧部落帐幕的鳞次栉比，遵循的乃是同一种章法。屋顶向下弯垂、两端翘起的弧线，不过是篷布自然的坡垂以及立柱将其撑起之状。那细长的帐幕立柱，已演化为游廊下支撑的柱列；而帐幕门口撩起的篷布，则长成了那轻盈上扬的弧形屋顶。华美的檐头，不过是旧时帐幕上的丝质流苏与绣幔凝固而成的模样。今日中国民居屋顶上那些奇特的饰物，所取代的正是当年压住篷布使其稳固的重物。这些重物由粗陋的石块，已变为精雕细琢的造像。中国人盖房子，至今仍如其先辈之竖立帐幕。工匠先立柱、上屋顶，而后才砌墙。除却宝塔——便连宝塔也仿佛是一层层叠摞起来的帐幕——中国建筑时至今日，尽管有了繁复的屋顶、漆柱和华美的装饰，仍像一顶华美的帐幕，只是被某位术士的魔杖点化得更加稳固、更加华美罢了。它与所安处的宁静田园山水异常相宜，仿佛是自然本身的一部分，与其周遭的环境绝无一丝违和！

===Original===
Wherever available, in the grounds of the Summer Palace, flowers are planted, and they succeed each other almost the whole year round, for the Chinese are wonderful gardeners. The extensive grounds are, however, not given up entirely to flowers and beautiful constructions; there are great fields of grain. Wheat and millet, and even vegetables, are raised in these pleasure grounds. It was curious to me to see how picturesque so prosaic a thing as a field of turnips might become, when properly placed in a large pleasure domain. By the planting of these useful crops, a great deal of fertile land is utilized, without any detriment to the landscape, and the utilitarian spirit, so strong in the Chinese, is satisfied.

===Chinese===
凡是可以利用之处，颐和园的苑囿里都栽满了花，且几乎一年到头此花谢而彼花开，前后相继，因中国人是极高明的园艺家。然而这片广阔的苑囿并非全然献与花卉与华美的建筑——其间还有大片的庄稼地。这些游乐之苑中也种着麦、粟，甚至蔬菜。在我看来颇为新奇的是：像萝卜地这般乏味的事物，若布置得宜、置於一片广阔的御苑之中，竟也能显出如画的意趣。藉着栽种这些实用的庄稼，大片肥沃的土地得以利用，於风景并无丝毫损伤，而中国人素来强烈的务实之心，也由此得到满足。

===Original===
There is one terraced hillside in the grounds of the Summer Palace, called the “Flowery Mountain.” In the season of the peonies, which the Chinese call “The King of Flowers,” this is really a flowery mountain—one mass of blooms of exquisitely blended colors and faint evanescent perfume. The China Aster is also brought to great perfection by the Chinese gardeners, and in the time of the chrysanthemum the grounds fairly blaze with this autumnal glory. The Chinese do not go in for the cultivation of the chrysanthemum of extraordinary size. Her Majesty does not care much for these; but her gardeners arrive at some wonderful combinations of colors and some most curious shapes. The year I was in the Palace, Her Majesty was delighted with a beautiful green variety, that the gardeners had succeeded in getting, and that year there was also a new variety whose petals were like threads, they were so thin and hairlike.

===Chinese===
颐和园苑囿中有一处呈阶状的山坡，名为「花山」。牡丹盛放之时——中国人称之为「花中之王」——这确是一座花山——一片繁花之海，色彩调配精妙，香气幽淡而转瞬即逝。中国的园艺家所培植的翠菊也十分出色，至菊开时节，苑中简直为这秋日的盛景所点燃。中国人并不着意培植特大花朵的菊花。皇太后并不甚喜此等大花；但她的园艺家却能造就若干奇妙的色彩搭配与极其别致的形态。我在宫中的那一年，皇太后对一种秀美的绿色品种尤为欢喜，那是园艺家们培植而成的新品；那年另有一种新品，其花瓣细如游丝，薄如发缕。

===Original===
Old Ruins in the Summer Palace

===Chinese===
颐和园中的旧日遗迹

===Original===
The Temple of the Ten Thousand Buddhas is so-called from its being built in glazed yellow tiles, each representing a niche, in which is seated a Buddha, of which there are many more than ten thousand. The interior is composed of three chapels. In the central one thrones the Great Buddha. There was another famous Buddha in this temple, which was invested with peculiarly sacred qualities, but it was hurled into the lake below and broken into a thousand pieces when the foreign troops were in possession in 1900. Her Majesty seemed to feel the depredations to the temples, by the foreigners, more keenly than anything else. The Chinese are so perfectly tolerant in matters of religion, they cannot understand our attitude toward any other religion but our own, and our contempt for any other kind of worship except that in which we ourselves indulge.

===Chinese===
万佛寺之所以得名，是因其以黄色的琉璃砖所建，每一块砖代表一个佛龛，龛中各坐一尊佛像，其数远多於一万。寺内分为三座佛堂。正中一间高踞其上的，是大佛。寺中本还有另一尊著名的佛像，被视为具有特别的神圣特质，但 1900 年外国军队占领此地时，它被抛入山下湖中，摔得粉碎。对於外国人劫毁寺庙一事，皇太后似比对其余任何事都更为痛心。中国人於宗教之事最为宽容，他们全然不能理解我们对自身所奉宗教之外其他宗教的态度，也不能理解我们对自身所行之外其他礼拜方式的轻蔑。

===Original===
The Chinese are said to hate the foreigner. They certainly have not much reason to like him; nor to admire our much vaunted civilization. The European Christian soldier in China has burned, destroyed, and killed with as much barbarity as the heathen, and in many instances has given the latter points in cruelty.

===Chinese===
据说中国人憎恶外国人。他们确也无多少理由喜欢外国人，亦无理由仰慕我们自诩的文明。在中国，信奉基督教的欧洲士兵烧杀毁掠，其野蛮并不亚於他们所称的「异教徒」，在残忍一事上，许多时候反倒给后者作出了榜样。

===Original===
On the slope behind the terraced hill of the Ten Thousand Buddhas are the ruins of the old Summer Palace, destroyed by the European troops fifty years ago. After this, the site of the dwelling Palaces was changed, and they were massed on the southern side of the lake. Her Majesty has nearly hidden all trace of the 1900 devastations to the Summer Palace, but these old ruins of the former Palace still remain, and they are not a blot upon the landscape. On the contrary, they have become picturesque with time, and give the one note of somberness to this smiling demesne that is needed to accentuate its charm. There is a small lake not far from these old ruins, built around with smiling pavilions and a curious tower-like construction which is used as a private temple. There are landing-places and small boats. It looks like a charming bit of old Venice. We never went here, however, but once. There are some unhappy associations connected with this beautiful spot, and Her Majesty did not seem to care to visit it. The promenade in the direction of the old Palace also seemed to sadden her, for she had passed the early years of her married life in these now crumbling ruins.

===Chinese===
在万佛寺那层叠的山后山坡上，是旧日夏宫的遗迹，五十年前为欧洲军队所毁。自此以后，居住宫苑的基址便改换了，集中建於湖的南岸。颐和园 1900 年所遭的毁坏，皇太后已几乎将其痕迹全数掩去；但那座旧宫的遗迹犹存，且并非风景上的瑕疵。相反，岁月使之别具画意，为这一片明媚的苑囿添上了一抹所需的沉郁色调，反倒使其风致更为突出。距这些旧迹不远，有一座小湖，湖周环以明丽的亭阁，另有一座奇特的塔形建筑，用作私人的庙宇。湖边有码头，湖上有小舟。它仿佛是一小片迷人的旧日威尼斯。然而我们只去过那里一次。这处胜景牵连着若干不快的回忆，皇太后似乎并不愿往游。朝旧宫方向的散步似乎也令她神伤，因为她婚后的早年岁月，便是在这些如今已倾颓的废墟之中度过的。

===Original===
From the highest elevations in the grounds of the Summer Palace, we could see the road from Peking! Sometimes Her Majesty and the Ladies would watch from some of the summerhouses, the carts and chairs and vehicles as they passed along. Several times we saw the Emperor and his suite returning from some ceremony in Peking, over the road cleared for his passage. Her Majesty, herself, would be the first to descry him, and she would say, “The Emperor comes.” Then the Empress and Ladies would all look, for it was not against the Proprieties for them to look at His Majesty at such a distance. These views of the high road from the eminences of the Summer Palace were all Her Majesty and the young Empress ever saw of the outside world and common humanity; for neither at the Winter nor Sea Palaces could they get any views from a distance, nor was there any opportunity of seeing beyond the walls. When Their Chinese Majesties go abroad—and this is generally only from one Palace to another—quaint, triangular flags are placed along the Imperial route, warning the people that Their Sacred Majesties are to pass, and that the road will be reserved for them between certain hours. No vehicles or pedestrians are allowed for some time before and after the Imperial passage. In the City of Peking, the inhabitants, even on the streets where the Imperial cortège is to pass, are shut into their houses and not allowed to go out of their doors during the time, and at the intersection of the transversal streets huge curtains are hung, shutting them off from the Imperial way. For these progresses of Their Majesties, the roads are covered with yellow sand.

===Chinese===
自颐和园园内最高的几处眺望，可以望见自北京来的那条路！有时皇太后与命妇们会在某处凉亭里，眺望大车、轿子和各色车辆在路上来往。我们曾数次望见皇上及其随扈，自北京某处典礼归来，沿着为他清出的道路行进。皇太后总是头一个望见他，便会说：「皇上回来了。」於是皇后与众命妇便都去看，因为隔着那般距离，望一望皇上并不算违礼。自颐和园高处所见的这条大道，便是皇太后与皇后所能见到的外部世界与凡俗人世的全部；因为在紫禁城与西苑两处宫苑，她们都无法远眺，也没有任何机会望见墙外之物。皇上、皇太后出行时——大抵只是由一处宫苑移往另一处宫苑——沿途御道上便会插起古雅的三角旗，告示百姓「两宫圣驾将行」，道路将在特定时辰内为他们清出。圣驾经过前后的一段时间内，一应车马行人皆不得通行。在北京城内，即便是在御驾所要经过的街道上，居民也被禁闭於家中，在此期间不得出门；於横向街口的交汇处，则悬挂起巨大的帷幕，将这些路口与御道隔开。凡皇上、皇太后出行，所过道路皆铺以黄沙。
