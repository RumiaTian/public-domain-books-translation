## Dedication / 题献

===Original===
**To Sir Robert Hart**

To whose helpful encouragement I owe so much, I affectionately dedicate this account of my experiences at the Court of the country he has so long and faithfully served.

Katherine A. Carl.
New York, May, 1905.

===Chinese===
**献给罗伯特·赫德爵士（Sir Robert Hart）**

他出于好意的鼓励使我受惠良多，我满怀感激地将这份宫廷经历记述奉献给他——那个国家，他已长久而忠实地效力多年。

凯瑟琳·A·卡尔
纽约，一九〇五年五月
