## XIX A Garden Party at the Summer Palace / 第十九章 颐和园的游园会

===Original===
Not long after this, Her Majesty gave a garden party for the ladies and gentlemen of the Legations. These garden parties occupy two days, for ladies and gentlemen are not received at the same time by Their Majesties of China. The Corps Diplomatique and attachés were entertained the first day, and the ladies of the Legations the following day. The entertainment was the same for each. The gentlemen were formally received in the Great Audience Hall by Their Majesties, after which a repast was served them in a pavilion near. When this was finished, they were taken for a tour of the gardens and lakes, and they left the Palace about two o’clock. None of the Ladies of the Court, except, of course, the Empress Dowager, were present at the receptions of the gentlemen of the Corps Diplomatique. The ladies of the Legations were received the following day.

===Chinese===
此后不久，皇太后为各国公使馆的夫人们和先生们举办了一场游园会。这种游园会要占去两天时间，因为中国的皇上与皇太后从不同时接见男女宾客。第一天招待的是外交使节团及其随员，第二天则招待各公使馆的夫人。两日的款待内容相同。先生们先在正大光明殿由皇上与皇太后正式接见，随后在附近的一座亭阁内赐宴。宴毕，由人引着游览园中的亭台湖山，约莫午后两点便离宫。在招待外交使节团先生们的场合上，宫中的命妇们一概不出席，自然，皇太后是例外。各公使馆的夫人们则在次日被接见。

===Original===
I was rather embarrassed as to what I should do, at this first formal reception, for the ladies of the Legations, since my arrival in the Palace. Being a foreigner, I thought it looked incongruous for me to receive with the Chinese Ladies. My uneasiness seemed to be divined by Her Majesty (she was always wonderful for her tact); she said, as I had been presented first in private Audience, it would be well for me to be presented also in public Audience. She suggested that I should go to the Foreign Office, meet Mrs. Conger on her arrival, and come into the Throne-room with her. When the eunuchs announced that the ladies had arrived at the Foreign Office, Her Majesty ordered my red Palace chair to take me there.

===Chinese===
自从入宫以来，在这次为公使馆夫人举办的首次正式觐见上，我颇为尴尬，不知自己应当如何行事。身为外国人，我觉着自己与中方命妇一同接宾，未免有些不相宜。皇太后似乎看出了我的局促（她向来以分寸得宜著称），说道：既然我此前已先行私下入觐，那么此番也当于大庭广众之下被正式引见。她提议我到外务部去，待康格夫人抵达时迎候她，与她一同入宝座殿。待太监们通报夫人一行已抵外务部，皇太后便吩咐抬我那顶红色的宫轿，送我前去。

===Original===
The Foreign Office is only a few hundred yards to the left of the Imperial entrance to the Palace. Mrs. Conger was one of the first ladies to arrive. When the other ladies came, all walked over to the gate of the Palace, and, after entering, went to a pavilion at the right of the Audience Hall, where they arranged themselves in the order in which they were to be presented.

===Chinese===
外务部距宫中御道入口的左侧不过几百码之遥。康格夫人是最早抵达的夫人之一。待其他夫人陆续到齐，众人便步行至宫门，入宫之后，先到觐见殿右侧的一座亭阁中，按引见的次序排定位置。

===Original===
The verandah and large marble platform of approach to the Audience Hall were shaded with tent-like silken awnings and covered, for the day, with red carpets, the latter a concession to foreign taste; for Her Majesty, though having many beautiful carpets stored up, has none in use, and only in winter and for certain functions are the courts carpeted. She never uses them in the interior.

===Chinese===
通往觐见殿的游廊和那方宽阔的大理石月台，都用帐幔般的丝绸天篷遮起，又在当日铺上了红地毯——此举乃是为了迁就外宾的口味；因皇太后虽然库中收着许多精美的地毯，平日却一概不用，唯在冬日或某些特定典礼时，庭院中方才铺毯。殿内则从不铺设。

===Original===
A double line of Princesses, led by the Princess Imperial, descended the steps of the Audience Hall and met the ladies on the marble platform. The Princesses then turned and preceded them into the Audience Hall. Here they separated and stood in a picturesque group on either side of the Throne dais.

===Chinese===
两列王妃格格，由大公主领头，自觐见殿的台阶上拾级而下，到那大理石月台上迎接夫人们。王妃格格们随即转身，在前引导众人入殿。进殿之后，她们分立两旁，在宝座阶陛的两侧各成一组，如画般侍立。

===Original===
Here, in the dim obscurity, sat the Empress Dowager on the Dynastic Throne, with the Emperor seated at her left. In front of Her Majesty stood the official table, with its cover of Imperial yellow reaching to the floor. To the ladies standing below the dais only the heads and shoulders of the Empress Dowager were visible above the table, with its pyramids of fruits and flowers.

===Chinese===
就在这幽暗的光影之中，皇太后端坐于御座之上，皇上则坐于其左侧。皇太后面前摆着御案，那明黄色的案帷直垂至地。立于阶下的夫人们望去，御案上堆叠如塔的果品花卉之上，只能看见皇太后的头部与双肩。

===Original===
The ladies made three reverences on entering, and each advanced and went up on the dais at her presentation. Her Majesty’s interpreter, the elder Miss Yu, stood at her right, a little behind, and repeated the Chinese name and title of each lady presented. Her Majesty, who has a royal memory for faces, recognized each lady who had been presented before, but treated all with equal cordiality. This cordiality was sometimes construed by the ladies, on their first presentation, as a special mark of interest in themselves; but it was the Empress Dowager’s invariable position toward all the foreigners at these diplomatic receptions. Like all well-bred hostesses, she was most particular to show no difference even to those ladies she liked best.

===Chinese===
夫人们入内时三行礼，引见时则依次上前，登临阶陛。皇太后的通译——年长的裕小姐——立于其右侧稍后处，将每一位被引见的夫人的中文姓名及称谓逐一陈奏。皇太后记人的本事绝佳，凡曾引见过的夫人无不认得，然对待众人同样殷勤。这种殷勤有时会被初次引见的夫人误作是对自己格外垂青的表示；但此乃皇太后在外交宴会上待一切外国人的不变之态。正如一切教养良好的女主人，她最是讲究一视同仁，即便是她最钟意的夫人，也绝不稍有差等。

===Original===
When all had been presented, the eunuchs removed the official table behind which the Empress Dowager received the formal presentations, and she descended from the dais. One of her yellow satin chairs was brought and she sat down at the right side of the Audience Hall. The ladies were then, collectively, presented by Her Majesty to the young Empress and the Princess Imperial, and tea was ordered. While the ladies were drinking tea, standing around the Empress Dowager’s chair, she said a few words to each, informally.

===Chinese===
待全部引见完毕，太监们便撤去皇太后接受正式引见时所凭的那张御案，皇太后遂从阶陛上下来。一顶明黄缎的轿椅抬了过来，她在觐见殿的右侧落座。随后，皇太后将夫人们一并引见给皇后与大公主，并吩咐赐茶。夫人们围着皇太后的轿椅立着饮茶之际，皇太后不拘礼数地与每人闲谈了几句。

===Original===
When the tea was finished, the ladies, conducted by the eunuchs and accompanied by the Princesses, went through the court of the Theater, past the Palace of the young Empress, through Her Majesty’s court to her Throne-room, where luncheon was served. This was in alternate courses of foreign and Chinese food. There were foreign wines and table waters, as well as Chinese, and quantities of sweet champagne, without which, the Chinese imagine, no foreigner can eat.

===Chinese===
茶毕，夫人们在太监引导及王妃格格们陪同下，穿过戏楼庭院，经过皇后的寝宫，再过皇太后的庭院，来到她的宝座殿中，于此赐宴。席间中西菜肴交替上桌，洋酒、矿泉水与中土饮品一并俱备，更有大量的甜香槟——中国人以为，无此物则洋人便不能下咽。

===Original===
After luncheon, at which the Imperial Princess and Princesses acted as hostesses, the visiting ladies went to the marble terrace overlooking the lake. Here they were met by the young Empress and the secondary wife of the Emperor, for they were never present at the table when the foreign ladies were entertained, any more than Her Majesty herself.

===Chinese===
宴毕——席间由大公主与诸王妃格格充作主人——来宾夫人们便移步至俯瞰昆明湖的大理石台榭。在此迎候她们的是皇后与皇上的侧室，因为款待外国夫人时，她们与皇太后本人一样，是从不入席的。

===Original===
The Empress Dowager’s barge did not lead the Palace fleet that day. There were three big houseboats, each of which ponderous affairs had a large cabin with a yellow-covered seat for Her Majesty, which, though she never used, was never occupied by anyone else. Anything covered with yellow is sacred to Their Majesties, and is never used except by them.

===Chinese===
那一日，皇太后的御舟并未领航宫中的船队。共有三艘大型楼船，每一艘这等笨重的舟船上都设有一座宽敞的舱房，其中备有一张铺着明黄帷垫的座位，是专为皇太后而设的——她虽从不使用，却也无人敢擅坐。凡覆以明黄之物，皆为皇上与皇太后所独尊，除他们之外，任何人不得使用。

===Original===
The Secondary Wife of the Emperor

In Summer Coiffure

===Chinese===
皇上的侧室

夏日发髻

===Original===
We were rowed across the lake, first to the island, where the Palace and small temple adjacent were visited, after which the ladies took the boats again and continued the tour of the lake to the Marble Boat. This Marble Boat was built over the lake as a summerhouse for one of the Emperors, and is on the plan of the Palace houseboat, but with an upper, as well as lower deck. It is one of the things in the Summer Palace most talked about by foreigners, and it is a curiosity, though not a thing of beauty. It was never made the objective point of any of Her Majesty’s promenades, nor visited, except when foreigners were invited to the Palace. On the lower deck of the Marble Boat, where was the best view of the lake, light refreshments, sweets, and fruits were served. When the tour of the lake was finished, the ladies made their adieus to Their Majesties and the young Empress and Princesses, and left the Palace grounds for the Foreign Office, where they took their own chairs and carriages for Peking.

===Chinese===
众人荡舟渡湖，先至湖中岛，游历了岛上的宫殿与邻近的小庙；随后夫人们复又登舟，续游湖景，直至石舫。这座石舫建于湖上，原是某位皇上消夏的别业，其式样仿照宫中的楼船，却有上下两层甲板。这是外国人在颐和园中谈论最多的景物之一，也算得上一桩奇观，却并不算美。皇太后平时散步，从不以此为目的地，也从不游览此处，唯有邀请外国人入宫时方才一至。在石舫下层甲板——此处观湖景最佳——供奉了些许茶点、糖果与时鲜果品。湖上游历既毕，夫人们便向皇上、皇太后、皇后及诸王妃格格辞行，离宫返回外务部，在那里各乘自家的轿子和马车回北京。

===Original===
In spite of Her Majesty’s cordiality and the efforts of the Princesses and Ladies, these garden parties were not always as pleasant as they might be. There seemed an absolute lack of harmony among the ladies of the Legation. Each seemed to watch the other with a jealous eye, in constant fear that someone might overstep her place. Some did not hesitate, even, to show their private animosities on the steps of the Throne, or before their hostesses at the table. They seemed to act on the principle that the Chinese, not understanding the language, would not understand anything else. It was unfortunate that this most punctilious of people, the Chinese, should have had this apparent lack of friendliness to judge the European ladies by. They received all with equal favor and perfect etiquette, and it was a pity that the lack of harmony among the foreign ladies should have led them to commit what seemed to be breaches of etiquette, which the Chinese could not have failed to observe. I was astonished to see how observant the latter were and how accurately they gauged our standing.

===Chinese===
尽管皇太后待人以诚，王妃格格与命妇们也费心周旋，这些游园会却未必都尽如人意。公使馆的夫人之间，似有一种绝对的不睦。人人皆以妒忌的目光打量旁人，生怕有人僭越了自己的位次。有的甚至毫不顾忌，竟在宝座阶陛之前，或在席间女主人面前，公然流露私怨。她们似乎抱着这样的心思：中国人既听不懂她们的话，自然也看不出别的来。可叹这最讲礼仪的中国人，竟要凭此等显然不睦之举，来评判欧洲的夫人。中方以同等礼遇和完美的礼节接待众人，而外国夫人之间这份失睦，竟使她们做出若干看似失礼的举动，中国人又怎能看不出来呢。后者洞察之细、品评我们身分之准，着实令我惊讶。

===Original===
Their comments on our costumes were also very interesting. Her Majesty seemed to like foreign dress, especially when in pretty colors, for she reveled in color. She said the foreign costume was very becoming to well-made and well-proportioned people; but she thought, while it showed off to advantage a good figure, it was unfortunate for anyone who was not so blessed. She thought the Chinese costume, falling in straight lines from the shoulder, was more becoming to stout people, for it hid many defects. One universal comment, among these Chinese ladies, on us, was that we look old for our years. The well-bred Chinese repress, from early childhood, all outward evidences of emotion. They lead such simple, wholesome lives—“Early to bed and early to rise”—that there are rarely any lines visible in their faces until they reach an advanced age, when they seem to go suddenly from ripe womanhood into extreme old age.

===Chinese===
她们对我们服饰的评点，也颇有意味。皇太后似乎喜欢洋装，尤喜色彩鲜丽者，因她本就嗜好色彩。她说洋装穿在体态匀称、风姿绰约之人身上十分相宜；但她也以为，洋装虽能衬出好身段，于那些天资未逮者却不免苛刻。她认为中式的衣裳自双肩笔直垂落，于丰腴之人反更相宜，因它能遮掩诸多瑕疵。这些中国夫人对我们有一句普遍的评语——便是我们看上去比实际年龄显老。教养良好的中国人，自幼便压抑一切情绪的外露。他们过着那样简朴而健康的生活——「早睡早起」——以致脸上极少见皱纹，直到年事已高，才仿佛由丰韵的中年遽然步入极老之态。

===Original===
They have a particular aversion to blond hair. They did not tell me so, as I have blond hair; but on the stage all the demons are represented with blond hair, and the more blond it is, the more wicked the demon. One day, one of the Ladies suggested to me that there were some very fine vegetable hair dyes for turning the hair black without injuring it; in fact, the growth was increased thereby. She said if I used this, my hair “might in time become black; at least, it would grow much darker.”

===Chinese===
她们对金发有种特别的厌恶。这一点她们并未明说与我，因我自己便是金发；但在戏台上，一切妖魔鬼怪都作金发扮相，金发越浅，则其妖越甚。一日，有位命妇向我建言，说有种极好的植物染发剂，可使头发变黑而无伤发质，反能使头发更易生长。她说我若用了此物，头发「假以时日或可变黑；至少也会深得多」。
