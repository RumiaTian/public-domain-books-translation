## XXI A European Circus at the Palace / 二十一 宫中一场西洋马戏

===Original===
Chinese ceremonies and celebrations were not all I was destined to enjoy while at the Summer Palace. There began to be talk of some "foreign entertainment" soon to be given, and when I found this foreign entertainment was to be a circus, a real European circus, I was delighted. I had been out in China two years, and had not had much European entertainment during that time, and—shall I confess it?—I dearly loved a circus if the horses and animals were fine. This circus was then in Tientsin, and someone had suggested to Their Majesties it would be an interesting thing to see. A young Manchu was sent to Tientsin to investigate. When he returned, flaming posters were submitted to Their Majesties by the Chief Eunuchs. When the Empress Dowager saw the vulgarly colored picture of a summarily clad young woman of the show, I was watching her face and I saw a look of contemptuous scorn pass over it. She brightened up, however, when she saw the pictures of the animals at their tricks, and the men on horses, and it was decided that the circus should be brought up from Tientsin! The animals and performers were to be domiciled in one of the parks near by, but the tent was to be stretched within the enclosure of the Palace.

Sites for the ring were discussed, and it was finally decided to have the tents pitched at the extreme western end of the lake. There was a large open field here, planted in turnips! As the turnips were ready to be gathered, it was decided that the crop should be pulled up and this place prepared for the tents.

===Chinese===
在颐和园（Summer Palace）小住期间，我命中注定要领略的，远不止中国的种种典礼与庆贺。不久便有了风声，说是要办一场什么「西洋玩意儿」；待我得知这西洋玩意儿竟是一场马戏——一场地地道道的西洋马戏——我真是喜不自胜。我来中国已满两年，这段时日里西洋的消遣见得不多，而——容我招认么？——只要马匹与兽类出色，我是顶爱看马戏的。这马戏班其时正在天津（Tientsin），有人便向两位陛下进言，说看一看倒也有趣。宫里便派了一位年轻的满族（Manchu）官员前去天津察看。他回来后，几位大太监便将那些花花绿绿的海报呈给两位陛下御览。皇太后瞧见海报上那个衣着简陋、颜色俗艳的女演员像，我正留神端详她的面容，只见一丝鄙夷不屑的神色从她脸上掠过。然而待她看见兽类做把戏、汉子骑马的图画，神色便为之一展，于是便定下：将这马戏班从天津接进京来！兽类与演戏的人就近安顿在附近一处园子里，而帐篷则要搭在宫禁之内。

场地的位置议来议去，末了定下：将帐篷搭在湖最西头的那一端。那里有一大片开阔地，地里正种着萝卜！萝卜既已长成、到了收成的时候，便议定将这一茬萝卜尽数拔起，腾出地方来搭帐篷。

===Original===
One day we went out into the turnip field, and the Empress Dowager herself pulled the first turnip; then the Empress and all the Princesses pulled some, and when they found a curiously shaped one, it was given to Her Majesty. It was a strange sight to see the Great Empress Dowager, sitting there at the side of the field, on her yellow campstool, smiling and interested, with the turnips piled around her, and the gaily dressed Empress and Princesses in their silken gowns flitting in and out of the field, apparently enjoying, to its utmost, the simple task of pulling these prosaic vegetables. The eunuchs and attendants stood in crowds around to take the turnips when pulled. They were not allowed, however, to pull any themselves. When a small square was denuded, Her Majesty and the Ladies returned to the Palace, and an army of workmen came and pulled up the whole field and began to prepare the ground for the circus tents.

As the performance of the circus was to be on the first day of the month, the Imperial players were at the Theater. When the morning Audience was finished, Their Majesties and the Empress and Ladies went to the Theater and listened to two or three plays. After luncheon, taken in the Imperial loge, Their Majesties started for the landing-place, followed by the young Empress and Ladies. The lake was gay with beautiful barges, great houseboats, and numbers of flat boats for the eunuchs. The barges and houseboats were picturesquely decorated with flying banners, pennants, and tasseled wands. Two steam-launches, puffing away, gave an air of modernity to this most Oriental fleet. One of the steam-launches was splendidly decorated with yellow banners, with gorgeous yellow silk scarfs festooned around the cabin and the Imperial flag flying above it. The Empress Dowager and the Emperor descended the marble steps to this gaily decked launch, and started off alone for the other end of the lake, the Imperial banners and colors flying.

===Chinese===
一日我们到那萝卜地里去，皇太后亲手拔下头一棵萝卜；随后皇后与众位福晋格格也都动手去拔，谁若拔得一棵形状稀奇的，便呈给太后。这光景着实奇特：堂堂大清皇太后，坐在地头那张黄色折凳上，面带笑意、兴致勃勃，身边堆满萝卜；衣饰鲜丽的皇后与众位福晋格格身着绸缎袍褂，在田间穿来穿去——拔这种平淡无奇的蔬菜这样一桩简简单单的活计，她们似乎都乐在其中，享到极致。太监与侍从们则围成一群一群地站着，萝卜一拔出来，便上前接过去。只是不许他们自己动手拔。待一小方地的萝卜都拔尽了，太后与诸位宫眷便回宫去了，一队工匠便来将整片地都拔尽，开始平整土地，预备搭马戏班的帐篷。

马戏演期定在初一，宫里的伶人其时正在戏台上。早间的觐见散后，两位陛下携皇后与众位宫眷到戏台去，听了两三出戏。在御用看阁里用了午膳之后，两位陛下便动身往湖边的船坞去，皇后与众位宫眷随行。湖上十分热闹：华美的画舫、宽敞的游船，还有许多供太监们乘坐的平底船。画舫与游船上装饰得如画一般，旌旗飞扬，幡带飘舞，还有那缀着流苏的长竿。两艘小火轮，噗噗地冒着烟，给这支东方气息十足的船队添了几分时新气象。其中一艘小轮船装饰得金碧辉煌，挂满黄色幡旗，船舱四周悬着华美的黄绸帷，桅顶上飘着龙旗。皇太后与皇上沿着石级下到这艘装饰一新的小轮船上，独自先行往湖的那一端驶去，船上御用旗帜飞扬。

===Original===
The Empress and Princesses went in the Empress's State boat; the visiting ladies followed in another of these ponderous but picturesque affairs. In size they are as large as an ordinary Chinese pavilion. The Empress's cabin was carpeted and splendidly upholstered in cloth of gold, with the usual tea-tables and lounges. It had one of those gallery-like prows with silken awnings, where the Princesses stood. The young Empress sat within, on one of the gold-covered couches. As she had been brought up with several of the Princesses as playmates, the young Empress generally waived ceremony with them; but she knew how, when necessary, to maintain a sweet dignity that was charming and perfectly in accord with her exalted position. Today was a State occasion. She sat alone, and the Ladies remained outside on the prow. She asked me to come in and showed me the interior and some of the curiously inlaid tables. She knew I was interested in all these things. She made me sit at her side, and when I demurred she said she knew it was not the foreign custom to sit on cushions on the floor, as was the habit of the Ladies when in her presence, and that I must sit beside her. This was the consideration they always showed me at the Palace, which I fully realized was not due to any special liking for me, but simply to their exquisite breeding—their desire to make me feel comfortable and at home.

When we arrived at the other side of the lake, the Empress and Ladies stood while Their Majesties landed. They were welcomed by a great burst of music from the bands. A number of Princes and Officials stood waiting to receive them and conduct them to the handsome loges that had been prepared for them.

===Chinese===
皇后与众位福晋格格上了皇后的仪船；来做客的女眷们则登上另一艘这样笨重却别致的大船。这种船的体量，足有普通中国亭子那般大。皇后的船舱里铺着地毯，椅榻都用金线锦缎装点得金碧辉煌，照例设有茶几与卧榻。船头有一处廊台一般的所在，张着绸篷，福晋格格们便站在那里。皇后却坐在舱里一张蒙着金线锦缎的榻上。因皇后自幼与好几位福晋格格一同长大，是儿时的玩伴，是以她寻常并不与她们拘礼；然而必要之时，她也懂得如何端出一份和悦而端庄的气度来——这气度颇为动人，又与她崇高的位分十分相称。这一日的场面，乃是仪礼之典。她独自坐着，宫眷们都留在舱外的船头。她唤我进去，给我看舱中的陈设，还有几张镶嵌得十分精巧的桌子。她知我对这些物事都留心。她要我坐在她身旁，我逊谢之时，她便说，她知道按洋人规矩，并不像宫眷们到了她跟前那般，习惯坐在地下的坐垫上，是以我定要坐在她身边才是。这便是我在宫中向来所蒙的体恤；我心里十分明白，这并非由于对我有什么特别的厚爱，只不过是出于他们那份深厚的教养——出于他们要我感到舒适自在的那份心意。

我们一行到了湖的对岸，皇后与众位宫眷都站起身来，等候两位陛下登岸。两班乐队的乐声响成一片，迎候着他们。一班王公大臣立在那里候着，迎上来将两位陛下引到为他们预备的华美看阁里。

===Original===
It was a picturesque procession that started from the landing-place—the Empress Dowager and the Emperor, under the big, embroidered, yellow silk, State umbrellas, preceded and surrounded by gorgeously attired attendants and splendidly gowned officials, the young Empress and Ladies, in gala attire, following after, with their eunuchs and attendants. The day was perfect, and glorious sunshine added to the brilliant effect. The side of the tent toward the Imperial loges was open. There was a railed platform before the pavilions that had been erected as loges. These pavilions were luxuriously fitted up: Their Majesties' loges were hung with the Imperial yellow. A yellow satin chair (with a smaller one at its left) was placed in the center of the raised platform, under the silken awning, and Their Majesties could sit here or within as they chose. The Empress and Ladies stood in groups on either side of this platform.

About two hundred officials had been invited to see the circus, and, contrary to the usual custom, there was no screen between them and the Imperial party. On the right were two bands of foreign music, or rather of Chinese musicians who played foreign music on European instruments. These were the bands of Yuan-Shih-Kai, Viceroy of Tientsin, and of Sir Robert Hart, the Inspector-General of Imperial Customs. Sir Robert's band was formed about eighteen years since, when, as music is his hobby, he decided to try to have some Chinese taught European music on European instruments. He has now a well-equipped band of twenty trained Chinese musicians under a competent European conductor. They play on both brass and stringed instruments. His efforts have been so successful that his example has lately been followed by several high Chinese Officials, first among whom was Yuan-Shih-Kai. The latter's band is military, with fifty musicians, who play only on brass instruments. The two bands played alternately during the intervals of the performance.

===Chinese===
从船坞起步的仪仗，实在是一幅如画的景象：皇太后与皇上撑着那绣花大黄绸御用伞盖，前后左右簇拥着服饰华美的侍从与袍褂鲜明的官员；皇后与众位宫眷盛装相随，身后跟着她们的太监与随从。这一日天色绝好，灿烂的日光更添了几分辉煌气象。帐篷朝向御用看阁的那一面敞开着。充作看阁的几座亭子前，搭着一处带栏杆的平台。这几座亭阁里陈设奢靡：两位陛下的看阁，悬的是御用明黄帷幔。平台正中、绸篷之下，设着一张黄缎宝座（左侧另有一张小些的），两位陛下或坐此处，或入阁内，悉听尊便。皇后与众位宫眷三五成群，立于平台两侧。

受邀来观马戏的官员约二百人；与寻常惯例不同，此次他们与御前之间并不设帷幔。右首是两支西乐乐队，更确切地说，是两班中国乐工，操着西洋乐器奏西洋乐曲。一支是直隶总督袁世凯（Yuan-Shih-Kai）的乐队，一支是大清海关税务司罗伯特·赫德爵士（Sir Robert Hart）的乐队。赫德爵士这一班乐队，约莫十八年前便已组建——音乐乃是他素所爱好，那时他便决意试试看，教些中国人学西洋乐器、奏西洋乐曲。如今他已有了一支编制齐全的乐队，二十名受过训练的中国乐工，由一位称职的西洋指挥统率。铜管、弦乐，一应俱能。他这一番用心成效斐然，近来几位中国大员也起而效法，头一个便是袁世凯。袁世凯那支乐队是军乐队，五十名乐工，单奏铜管乐。演出的间隙，两班乐工便轮流奏乐。

===Original===
For the first time during my residence at the Palace, I now had an opportunity of seeing the Imperial Princes and many of the great nobles and officials. Though they were often asked to the Theater at the Palace, the screen between them and the Imperial loge was never removed, except at the end of the performance, when they bowed their thanks and when the Ladies retired to their own loge. The gentlemen, however, could be well seen at the circus; and though the Chinese Ladies did not glance in their direction, I took advantage of being a foreigner, and when I was behind the others, and could do so without being seen, I closely scanned their faces and attire. Several of the Princes of the Imperial Family came up to the platform where Their Majesties sat and made their bows to them, afterward slightly saluting their relations among the Ladies and Princesses.

Among these young Princes at the circus was a son of Prince Kung and an adopted son of the Imperial Princess. This young man not only had a remarkably fine figure, tall and slender, with broad shoulders, but his face was very handsome. His bow, on coming up to pay his respects to Their Majesties, was as graceful as that of a young chevalier. His regard was so ingenuous, his expression so clever and withal so modest, his whole demeanor so gracious, I was much struck with him. His dress was elegant, and his jewels chosen with discretion. There was none of that overloading of belt ornamentation that the young dandies of the Imperial set were then affecting. His father was one of the great Princes of China, and if this young man develops and carries out the promise of his youth (he was then only seventeen), I fancy he will be heard of also. Like most of the young Manchu Princes, he held a position in the Imperial household, such as Master of the Horse or Captain of the Archers.

自从我入宫居住以来，这是头一遭有机会得见宗室王公，以及许多显贵大臣。王公们虽也常被请入宫中听戏，然而他们与御用看阁之间所张的帷幔，除却散戏之时——其时他们要行礼谢恩、女眷们也要退回自家的看阁——是从不撤去的。然而看马戏之时，这些男宾却可以看得清清楚楚；中国的夫人们虽不朝他们那边瞥一眼，我却借了自己是外国人的便宜，待到落在人后、不至于被人瞧见之时，便细细打量他们的容貌衣着。宗室里有几位王公走到两位陛下所坐的平台前行礼，礼毕又微微颔首，向人丛里与自己有亲的宫眷和福晋格格致意。

这一日到场的小王公当中，有恭亲王（Prince Kung）的一位公子，还有一位大公主（the Imperial Princess）的养子。这位青年不但身段极好——修长匀称，肩背宽阔——容貌也甚是俊美。他走上前去向两位陛下行礼，那姿态之潇洒，犹如一位年轻的骑士。他目光率真，神情聪慧而又谦逊有加，举手投足温文可亲，我对他颇为留意，留下深刻印象。他的服饰雅致，所佩珠宝也挑选得极有分寸。那时宫中一干时髦少年所好似的、腰带上琳琅满目堆叠的饰物，他身上全无此弊。他的父亲乃是中国数一数二的大王公，这位青年若能如其少年时这份气象那般发展、有所作为（其时他不过十七岁），我想日后人们也定会听到他的名字。与大多数年轻的满族王公一样，他在内廷当差，担任的职位或是御马总管，或是弓箭侍卫统领之类。

It was not much of a circus, but none of the Imperial party had ever seen one before, and the setting was so gorgeous, it was unique as a circus performance even to me. The Empress Dowager and the Emperor had splendidly jeweled opera glasses, which a eunuch held ready for their use. The Emperor, disliking to be looked at, held his own glasses before his face most of the time. It seemed to me he used them principally for the purpose of screening himself. The animals pleased both Their Majesties; but aside from the dwarfs, of which there were two, the rest of the performers seemed to have but a mediocre interest for them. Her Majesty was particularly interested in the dogs and trained animals, and His Majesty in the horses and fancy riding. I was standing near him, and he looked keenly at me several times to see how the performance struck me; and one of his head eunuchs asked me in English—the Emperor would not try it—whether I thought it was "good or bad." Their Majesties sat through the performance, the Empress Dowager only retiring to her loge once during the time, which was while one of the summarily clad young ladies was gyrating on a trapeze. There was a magnificent tigress which the circus master had trained, and which was his pièce de résistance. The Empress Dowager would not allow this to be taken out of its cage, and though it was brought out in front of the Imperial platform, it was too catlike to interest her. She has a great antipathy to anything feline. When the performance was finished, the Imperial party left in the same state in which it had arrived, Their Majesties accompanied to the launch by the Princes and high Officials, the music of the two bands playing simultaneously. The Ladies of the Palace and Their Majesties, themselves, have so little novelty in their lives, I think, on the whole, the innovation of the circus was generally appreciated.

这马戏班子算不得怎样出色，只是御前上下从前从未见过马戏，而此间的排场又如此华美，便是我，也觉得这场马戏别开生面、独一无二。皇太后与皇上都有镶珠嵌宝的观剧镜，由一名太监捧着，随时听候取用。皇上不愿被人注视，大半时间都将观剧镜举在面前。我瞧他那模样，戴这观剧镜，多半是用来遮掩面目的。兽类倒博得两位陛下同声叫好；只是除了那两名侏儒而外，其余的演员似乎都引不起他们多大兴致。皇太后于狗与受过训练的兽类兴致最浓，皇上则爱看马匹与花样骑术。我正站在皇上近旁，他几次三番凝神望着我，要看这演出在我眼中如何；他身边一位首领太监便用英语问我——皇上自己却不肯开口——我以为这演出「好还是不好」。两位陛下从开场坐到散场，整场之中，皇太后仅退入看阁一次——那正是一位衣着简陋的女演员在空中秋千上盘旋之时。还有一头雄壮的母虎，是马戏班主亲手驯出来的，乃是他压轴的看家本领。皇太后不许将它放出笼来，那虎虽被抬到御前平台外面，到底太像猫了，引不起太后的兴致。太后于一切猫科之物，都抱有一种极深的反感。演出既毕，御前一行人便依着来时那般仪仗离去，王公大臣们一直送到小轮船边，两班乐工同时奏起乐来。宫眷们与两位陛下，平生所历新奇之事如此之少，是以总而言之，这场马戏之举，倒也算是皆大欢喜的一桩新鲜事。
