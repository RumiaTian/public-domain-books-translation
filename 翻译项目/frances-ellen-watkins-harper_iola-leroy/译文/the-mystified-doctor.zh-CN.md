## VIII The Mystified Doctor / 第八章 满腹疑团的医生

===Original===
“Colonel,” said Dr. Gresham to Col. Robinson, the commander of the post, “I am perfectly mystified by Miss Leroy.”

“What is the matter with her?” asked Col. Robinson. “Is she not faithful to her duties and obedient to your directions?”

“Faithful is not the word to express her tireless energy and devotion to her work,” responded Dr. Gresham. “She must have been a born nurse to put such enthusiasm into her work.”

“Why, Doctor, what is the matter with you? You talk like a lover.”

A faint flush rose to the cheek of Dr. Gresham as he smiled, and said, “Oh! come now, Colonel, can’t a man praise a woman without being in love with her?”

“Of course he can,” said Col. Robinson; “but I know where such admiration is apt to lead. I’ve been there myself. But, Doctor, had you not better defer your lovemaking till you’re out of the woods?”

===Chinese===
“上校，”格雷沙姆医生（Dr. Gresham）对驻地指挥官罗宾逊上校（Col. Robinson）说，“勒罗伊小姐（Miss Leroy）实在让我满腹疑团。”

“她怎么啦？”罗宾逊上校问。“她难道不是恪尽职守、事事遵从你的嘱咐吗？”

“‘恪尽职守’还不足以形容她那不知疲倦的劲头和对工作的忘我投入，”格雷沙姆医生应道。“能把这样的热忱倾注到工作里，她准是个天生的看护。”

“哎哟，医生，你这是怎么啦？说起话来倒像个谈情说爱的人。”

格雷沙姆医生的脸颊上泛起一抹淡淡的红晕，他含笑说道：“哦，得啦，上校，难道一个男人夸奖一个女人，就非得爱上她不成？”

“夸当然是可以夸的，”罗宾逊上校说；“可这样的倾慕容易把人引到什么地方去，我心里清楚。我自己就是从那条路上过来的。不过，医生，你把求爱的事推迟到出了这片林子再办，岂不更好？”

===Original===
“I assure you, Colonel, I am not thinking of love or courtship. That is the business of the drawing-room, and not of the camp. But she did mystify me last night.”

“How so?” asked Col. Robinson.

“When Tom was dying,” responded the doctor, “I saw that beautiful and refined young lady bend over and kiss him. When she found that he was dead, she just cried as if her heart was breaking. Well, that was a new thing to me. I can eat with colored people, walk, talk, and fight with them, but kissing them is something I don’t hanker after.”

“And yet you saw Miss Leroy do it?”

“Yes; and that puzzles me. She is one of the most refined and ladylike women I ever saw. I hear she is a refugee, but she does not look like the other refugees who have come to our camp. Her accent is slightly Southern, but her manner is Northern. She is self-respecting without being supercilious; quiet, without being dull. Her voice is low and sweet, yet at times there are tones of such passionate tenderness in it that you would think some great sorrow has darkened and overshadowed her life. Without being the least gloomy, her face at times is pervaded by an air of inexpressible sadness. I sometimes watch her when she is not aware that I am looking at her, and it seems as if a whole volume was depicted on her countenance. When she smiles, there is a longing in her eyes which is never satisfied. I cannot understand how a Southern lady, whose education and manners stamp her as a woman of fine culture and good breeding, could consent to occupy the position she so faithfully holds. It is a mystery I cannot solve. Can you?”

“I think I can,” answered Col. Robinson.

===Chinese===
“我向你保证，上校，我没有在想什么爱情或求婚的事。那是客厅里的事，不是军营里的事。可昨天夜里，她着实让我摸不着头脑。”

“此话怎讲？”罗宾逊上校问。

“汤姆（Tom）咽气的时候，”医生应道，“我看见那位美丽文雅的年轻小姐俯下身去吻他。她发现人已经死了，就哭起来，哭得好像心都要碎了。唉，这在我可是件新鲜事。我可以同有色人种一起吃饭、走路、说话、并肩打仗，可要说到亲吻他们，那可不是我心向往之的事。”

“可你不是亲眼看见勒罗伊小姐这么做了吗？”

“看见了；而这正是让我想不通的地方。她是我平生所见最娴雅、最有大家闺秀风范的女子之一。听说她是逃难来的，可她又不像到我们营里来的其他逃难者。她的口音略带南方腔调，举止做派却是北方的。她自尊而不倨傲，娴静而不呆板。她的嗓音低柔悦耳，可有时那声音里又透出那样炽烈的柔情，你会以为有什么莫大的哀伤遮暗了、罩住了她的一生。她的面容虽无半点阴郁，却时而弥漫着一种难以言喻的哀愁。我有时趁她不觉得我在看她的时候打量她，只觉得她那张脸上仿佛写着一整部书。她微笑的时候，眼睛里含着一种永远得不到满足的渴望。我不明白，一位南方小姐，教养与风度都表明她是位极有文化、极有教养的女子，怎么会甘心守着她如今这样尽心尽力担任的职位。这是我一猜不透的谜。你猜得透吗？”

“我想我猜得透，”罗宾逊上校答道。

===Original===
“Will you tell me?” queried the doctor.

“Yes, on one condition.”

“What is it?”

“Everlasting silence.”

“I promise,” said the doctor. “The secret between us shall be as deep as the sea.”

“She has not requested secrecy, but at present, for her sake, I do not wish the secret revealed. Miss Leroy was a slave.”

===Chinese===
“愿意讲给我听吗？”医生问道。

“愿意，不过有个条件。”

“什么条件？”

“永远守口如瓶。”

“我答应，”医生说。“咱们俩之间的这个秘密，要藏得像海一样深。”

“她本人并没有要求保密，只是眼下为了她的缘故，我不愿这个秘密泄露出去。勒罗伊小姐做过奴隶。”

===Original===
“Oh, no,” said Dr. Gresham, starting to his feet, “it can’t be so! A woman as white as she a slave?”

“Yes, it is so,” continued the Colonel. “In these States the child follows the condition of its mother. This beautiful and accomplished girl was held by one of the worst Rebels in town. Tom told me of it and I issued orders for her release.”

“Well, well! Is that so?” said Dr. Gresham, thoughtfully stroking his beard. “Wonders will never cease. Why, I was just beginning to think seriously of her.”

“What’s to hinder your continuing to think?” asked Col. Robinson.

“What you tell me changes the whole complexion of affairs,” replied the doctor.

===Chinese===
“哦，不，”格雷沙姆医生霍地站起身来，“不可能是这样！像她这样白皙的女子，会是个奴隶？”

“是的，就是这样，”上校接着说。“在这些州，孩子的身分随母亲而定。这位才貌双全的姑娘，被城里最死硬的叛党之一霸占着。这事是汤姆告诉我的，我下了释放她的命令。”

“哎呀，哎呀！当真如此？”格雷沙姆医生说着，若有所思地捋着胡须。“真是奇事迭出，没有个完。唉，我正刚开始对她认真起来呢。”

“那又有什么妨碍你继续想下去的？”罗宾逊上校问。

“你告诉我的这件事，把整桩事情的面目都改变了，”医生答道。

===Original===
“If that be so I am glad I told you before you got head over heels in love.”

“Yes,” said Dr. Gresham, absently.

Dr. Gresham was a member of a wealthy and aristocratic family, proud of its lineage, which it could trace through generations of good blood to its ancestral isle. He had become deeply interested in Iola before he had heard her story, but after it had been revealed to him he tried to banish her from his mind; but his constant observation of her only increased his interest and admiration. The deep pathos of her story, the tenderness of her ministrations, bestowed alike on black and white, and the sad loneliness of her condition, awakened within him a desire to defend and protect her all through her future life. The fierce clashing of war had not taken all the romance out of his nature. In Iola he saw realized his ideal of the woman whom he was willing to marry. A woman, tender, strong, and courageous, and rescued only by the strong arm of his Government from a fate worse than death. She was young in years, but old in sorrow; one whom a sad destiny had changed from a lighthearted girl to a heroic woman. As he observed her, he detected an undertone of sorrow in her most cheerful words, and observed a quick flushing and sudden paling of her cheek, as if she were living over scenes that were thrilling her soul with indignation or chilling her heart with horror. As nurse and physician, Iola and Dr. Gresham were constantly thrown together. His friends sent him magazines and books, which he gladly shared with her. The hospital was a sad place. Mangled forms, stricken down in the flush of their prime and energy; pale young corpses, sacrificed on the altar of slavery, constantly drained on her sympathies. Dr. Gresham was glad to have some reading matter which might divert her mind from the memories of her mournful past, and also furnish them both with interesting themes of conversation in their moments of relaxation from the harrowing scenes through which they were constantly passing. Without any effort or consciousness on her part, his friendship ripened into love. To him her presence was a pleasure, her absence a privation; and her loneliness drew deeply upon his sympathy. He would have merited his own self-contempt if, by word or deed, he had done anything to take advantage of her situation. All the manhood and chivalry of his nature rose in her behalf, and, after carefully revolving the matter, he resolved to win her for his bride, bury her secret in his Northern home, and hide from his aristocratic relations all knowledge of her mournful past. One day he said to Iola:﻿—

===Chinese===
“倘若当真如此，我很庆幸趁你还没一头栽进爱河，就把这话告诉了你。”

“是啊，”格雷沙姆医生心不在焉地说。

格雷沙姆医生出身于一个富贵的贵族门第，这个家族为自家的世系自豪——凭着世代相承的好血统，可以一直追溯到祖居的岛屿。还没有听到她的身世之前，他已对伊奥拉（Iola）深感兴趣；待身世向他揭开之后，他曾试着把她从心头排遣开去；可他日复一日地注视她，反而只让他对她的兴趣和倾慕有增无减。她身世里深沉的哀婉，她不分黑人白人一视同仁的温柔看护，以及她处境中那份凄凉孤单，都在他心里唤起了一个愿望：要在她今后的一生里守护她、卫护她。战争的激烈冲杀并没有把他天性里的浪漫气质扫荡干净。在伊奥拉身上，他看到自己愿意迎娶的那个女子的理想成了现实——一个温柔、坚强而勇敢的女子，全凭他的政府强有力的臂膀，才从比死亡更悲惨的命运中被解救出来。她年华正少，却历尽忧患；一场悲哀的命运，把这个无忧无虑的姑娘造就成了一个英勇的女子。他观察她时，听得出她最快活的话语底下也压着一缕哀愁，看得出她的脸颊忽而泛红、忽而转白，仿佛她正一幕幕重新经历着某些旧景——那些旧景或以义愤激荡她的灵魂，或以恐怖冷却她的心。身为看护和医生，伊奥拉和格雷沙姆医生时时聚在一处。他的朋友们给他寄来杂志和书籍，他都乐意与她分享。医院是个伤心之地。血肉模糊的躯体，正在血气方刚、精力旺盛的年华里被击倒；一具具苍白的年轻尸体，作为献在奴隶制祭坛上的牺牲，时时刻刻都在消耗着她的怜悯。格雷沙姆医生很高兴手头有些读物，既可引开她对伤心往事的记忆，也可在他们从一幕幕惨烈景象中抽身歇息的时刻，为两人提供饶有趣味的话题。她全不经意、也毫无所觉，他的友谊却成熟成了爱情。对他来说，她在眼前是一种快乐，不在眼前是一种缺憾；她的孤单深深地牵动他的怜惜。他若有只言片语、一举一动趁她处境之危占了她的便宜，那便辜负了他的自重，连他自己都要瞧不起自己。他天性中全部的男子气概与侠义精神都为她奋然而起；他把这件事仔细盘算了一番之后，决意娶她为妻，把她的秘密埋进他北方的家中，瞒住他那些贵族亲戚，不叫他们对她伤心的过去知道分毫。一天，他对伊奥拉说道：

===Original===
“This hospital life is telling on you. Your strength is failing, and although you possess a wonderful amount of physical endurance, you must not forget that saints have bodies and dwell in tabernacles of clay, just the same as we common mortals.”

“Compliments aside,” she said, smiling; “what are you driving at, Doctor?”

“I mean,” he replied, “that you are running down, and if you do not quit and take some rest you will be our patient instead of our nurse. You’d better take a furlough, go North, and return after the first frost.”

“恭维话且收起，”她含笑说；“您到底想说什么，医生？”

===Original===
“Doctor, if that is your only remedy,” replied Iola, “I am afraid that I am destined to die at my post. I have no special friends in the North, and no home but this in the South. I am homeless and alone.”

There was something so sad, almost despairing in her tones, in the drooping of her head, and the quivering of her lip, that they stirred Dr. Gresham’s heart with sudden pity, and, drawing nearer to her, he said, “Miss Leroy, you need not be all alone. Let me claim the privilege of making your life bright and happy. Iola, I have loved you ever since I have seen your devotion to our poor, sick boys. How faithfully you, a young and gracious girl, have stood at your post and performed your duties. And now I ask, will you not permit me to clasp hands with you for life? I do not ask for a hasty reply. Give yourself time to think over what I have proposed.”

===Chinese===
“医生，要是这就是您唯一的药方，”伊奥拉答道，“我怕我是注定要死在自己的岗位上了。我在北方没有特别亲近的朋友，在南方除了这里也没有别的家。我无处安身，孤身一人。”

她的声调里、她低垂的头颅上、她颤抖的嘴唇间，有一种那样悲哀、近乎绝望的东西，霎时间激起了格雷沙姆医生满心的怜惜；他向她走近一步，说道：“勒罗伊小姐，你不必这样孤孤单单。请让我享有让你一生明亮幸福的权利吧。伊奥拉，自从看见你这样看护我们可怜的伤病员，我就爱上你了。你这样一位年轻温雅的姑娘，坚守岗位、履行职责，何等忠心。如今我问你：可否允许我与你执手相守，共度此生？我不求你立刻答复。给你自己一点时间，好好思量我的提议。”
