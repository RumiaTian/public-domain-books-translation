## V · Inspector Tanner Becomes Convinced / V · 坦纳探长深信不疑

===Original===
After dinner that same evening Inspector Tanner, having lit his pipe, and selected the most comfortable armchair he could find, set himself to take stock of his position, and see just where he stood with his new case.

===Chinese===
当晚用罢晚餐，坦纳探长点燃烟斗，又拣了所能寻见的最舒服的一张扶手椅坐下，着手盘点自己的处境，看看这桩新案子里自己究竟走到了哪一步。

===Original===
He realised that the lawyer’s communication contained food for thought. Certainly a lot of the information he had gained seemed to point in a rather unmistakable way to Austin. That the latter had murdered his father he felt it hard to believe, and yet he had known men to be convicted on slighter evidence than that he already held. Absentmindedly pressing down the tobacco in his pipe, he closed his eyes, and tried to view the facts he had learnt in a proper perspective.

===Chinese===
他意识到，律师那一番话里大有值得深思之处。的确，他到手的种种信息，有不少似乎明白无误地指向奥斯汀。要说后者竟谋害了亲生父亲，他实觉难以置信；然而他见过有人被定罪，凭的证据比他手中这些还要单薄。他心不在焉地按了按斗钵里的烟丝，闭上眼睛，力图把已知的种种事实放到恰如其分的远近来看。

===Original===
Here was a son who had never been able to get on with his father, so much so that they could not live in the same house. To the father he had been a continual disappointment, and no doubt that irritation would show in the father’s manner, and could not but increase the bad blood between them. It was true they had agreed to differ, and Sir William had allowed Austin £1,000 a year, but agreeing to differ did not necessarily prevent very unpleasant feelings on both sides, and as for the money, though it seemed handsome at first sight, it was very small compared to what Sir William might have paid without missing it.

===Chinese===
瞧瞧这么一个儿子：从来与父亲处不来，闹到连一幢屋子都住不进同一扇门。在父亲眼里，他是一而再、再而三的失望；那份懊恼必定形于做父亲的言谈举止，也就免不了加深父子间的嫌隙。诚然，父子俩讲好了各执己见、互不相犯，威廉爵士也允给奥斯汀每年 1,000 英镑；可是「各执己见」未必就能挡住双方心里那股很不痛快的滋味。至于钱，乍看固然出手豪阔，可比起威廉爵士就是拿了出来也不觉得肉痛的数目，实在算不得什么。

===Original===
From what Tanner had seen of Austin and his villa, he thought the latter must be living at the rate of well on to a thousand a year. That was to say, nearly at the rate of his income. Under these circumstances he falls in love and decides to marry. The lady would have but a small dot, if any. The two of them must therefore live on what had before been enough for Austin only. What does Austin do? He sees his father the day after the engagement, probably to tell him the news, and possibly to ask for an increased allowance. As in the ordinary course of nature a large portion of Sir William’s money would soon become his in any case, this would not be an unreasonable demand. But what does Sir William answer? Tanner could only surmise, but from what the dead man had said to Mr. Arbuthnot it was probable he had not only refused the increase, but had threatened altering his will adversely to Austin if the marriage took place. If, as appeared possible from the words overheard by Innes, Sir William had said anything derogatory to the lady, Austin’s feelings, already considerably aroused, would probably reach white heat. At all events, whether the interview between father and son had or had not taken this course, it was bedrock fact that they had quarrelled about some woman.

===Chinese===
就坦纳对奥斯汀本人和他那幢小宅的观察来看，此人过活的花销怕是每年要近千镑。换句话说，几乎把进项花到了头。偏在这个当口，他恋爱了，决意成婚。那位小姐纵有嫁妆也必定微薄，两口人今后要靠先前只够奥斯汀一人花销的款项度日。那么奥斯汀怎么行事？订婚次日他便去见父亲，多半是报喜信，也可能顺带请求增加津贴。照常理，威廉爵士的财产早晚有大半要归他所有，这要求原不算过分。可威廉爵士怎么回答的？坦纳只能揣度；但据死者对阿巴思诺特先生说过的话，他多半非但回绝了增贴，还撂下话来：倘若这桩婚事当真办了，就改遗嘱，叫奥斯汀吃大亏。倘若——从英尼斯无意中听来的那几句看，不无这种可能——威廉爵士还说了什么有辱那位小姐的话，奥斯汀本已相当激愤的情绪，只怕要烧到白热。无论如何，父子那场会面有没有走到这一步且不论，他们曾为某个女人吵过一架，却是凿凿的实情。

===Original===
Tanner continued his surmises. Assuming he was correct so far, Austin would inevitably be faced with a very terrible temptation. If his father should die without altering his will, it would mean £150,000 to himself. £150,000! Quite a respectable sum of money! There would then be no question of love in a cottage with Miss Drew. He could give her all those things which men delight to give women. And the irritation of the constant unpleasantness with Sir William would be gone. The more Tanner considered the matter, the more powerful he felt this temptation would have been. Many a stronger man than Austin seemed to be had succumbed to less.

===Chinese===
坦纳继续往下揣想。倘若到此为止他的推断不差，奥斯汀就无可避免地要面对一种极其可怕的诱惑。父亲若是不改遗嘱便一命归西，对他就意味着 150,000 英镑。150,000 英镑！好一笔像样的巨款！到那时，同德鲁小姐「茅屋里的爱情」便无从谈起，男人们乐于奉献给女人的种种，他尽可以一一奉献给她；而同威廉爵士无休无止怄气斗气的那份煎熬，也一并烟消云散。这件事坦纳越琢磨，越觉得这诱惑的力量只怕大得很。多少瞧着比奥斯汀更硬气的人，栽在比这更小的诱惑上也不稀奇。

===Original===
Then coming down to details. The murderer had unquestionably known his way about Luce Manor. He had either gone to the library by the side door, and there committed the crime, carrying the body down to the boathouse, or, more probably, he had devised some scheme to make Sir William go there of his own accord. He had also taken the particular oars belonging to the boat used. All the knowledge necessary for this Austin of course possessed.

===Chinese===
再往细处看。凶手对卢斯庄园的路径无疑了如指掌。他要么是打侧门进了书房，就在那里行凶，再把尸体搬下船屋；要么——可能性更大——是设了个什么圈套，让威廉爵士自己走到那地方去。他还取走了那条船配套的那对桨。而干这一切所需要的那点知识，奥斯汀自然样样不缺。

===Original===
But more suspicious than that, Austin by his own showing had actually taken a boat down the river on that night, and the hours of leaving and returning were such that he would just have had time to reach the Luce Manor boathouse, commit the murder, and return the boat to the club. Of course, he had claimed to have an alibi for this period, but alibis could be faked, and the very readiness and apparent completeness of this seemed to Tanner slightly indicative of prearrangement.

===Chinese===
可比这更可疑的是：据奥斯汀自己供述，当晚他确曾驾船顺流而下，而离镇与回镇的钟点掐得恰好——刚好够他赶到卢斯庄园船屋、行凶杀人、再把船送回俱乐部。当然，他声称这段时间自己另有不在场证明；可不在场证明也能伪造，何况这一份来得那样现成、瞧上去又那样完密，在坦纳看来，倒隐隐透出几分预先布置的痕迹。

===Original===
There was then the question of the oars. This also had a suspicious look. At first Tanner had accepted without question the boatman’s theory that the boat and oars had become separated in the upper channel and had passed down to the falls through different arches of the road bridge. Indeed, his own inspection of the place had led him to the same conclusion. And this losing of the oars had seemed to explain adequately enough how the accident happened.

===Chinese===
然后是船桨的问题。这一点也透着蹊跷。起初，船工的那套说法坦纳照单全收：船和桨在上游河道里失散，又各自穿过干道大桥不同的桥孔，漂到了瀑布下头。说实在的，他亲自察看那地方之后，得出的也是同一个结论。而这失桨一事，似乎也把这场「意外」如何发生解释得够圆的了。

===Original===
But when it was shown that the affair was not an accident at all, the matter of the oars took on a very different complexion. Evidently it was part of the plant﻿—deliberately arranged to create the very impression it had produced. And Tanner now saw that the murderer had overreached himself. Sir William might easily have lost one oar, but he would not have let both go. And it was very unlikely, even if he had dropped both, that they would have separated far enough from the boat to have taken the other archway. Here again one might have done so, but not both. No, Tanner was driven to the conclusion that the boat must have been sent adrift close to the Luce Manor bank of the river, while the oars were taken over to the opposite side in another boat, and there thrown overboard. And Austin had had such a boat down the river that night.

===Chinese===
然而一旦查明此事压根儿不是意外，船桨问题便全然换了副面孔。它显然是整个布局中的一环——蓄意安排出来，好制造它果真制造出的那种印象。而且坦纳如今看明白了：凶手这一手玩过了头。威廉爵士失手落一支桨，大有可能；两支一齐落水，他决计不容。退一步说，纵然两支都脱了手，它们也不大可能漂离船身那么远，各自钻进另一个桥孔去。这一层也一样：一支或许办得到，两支断不能都办到。不，坦纳不得不认定的只能是：那条船是在紧挨卢斯庄园一侧的河岸边被放开漂走的，而那对桨则被人用另一条船载到对岸，抛进了水里。偏偏当晚奥斯汀就有这么一条船到过下游。

===Original===
Lastly, and most suggestive of all was Austin’s start and look of fear, when the Coroner had announced the adjournment of the inquest. It was true he had accounted for his apprehension. An investigation might bring up the question of the alibi in which, as he was unable to prove its truth, no credence might be placed, leaving him in an awkward position. But was this a satisfactory explanation? Were these consequences not too remote to be seen with such immediate clearness as to cause an actual start? Did it not involve too instantaneous a perception of the facts? The Inspector thought so.

===Chinese===
最后，也最耐人寻味的一点是：验尸官宣布验尸审讯延期时，奥斯汀猛然一惊，面露惧色。不错，他对自己的惶恐是解释过的——调查可能翻出不在场证明的问题，而他既无法证实其真伪，人家也许就无从采信，弄得他处境尴尬。可这个解释站得住么？这些后果未免太迂远了，哪能一眼望得那样分明，分明到教人当真吓一跳？这岂不太需要一种对事实的瞬间顿悟了么？探长觉得是。

===Original===
But he saw as he still smoked and pondered, that it was equally possible that his whole conclusions were erroneous. Austin unquestionably might be, as he pictured himself, the innocent victim of the real murderer’s plant.

===Chinese===
但他一边抽烟一边盘算，也看出了另一种同样可能的事：他的全部结论也许压根儿错了。奥斯汀未尝不像他自己刻画的那样，是真凶设局陷害的无辜牺牲品。

===Original===
His thoughts turned to Cosgrove Ponson. Here was another man who had an interest in Sir William’s death. And from what Mr. Arbuthnot had said, his interest might be quite as strong, or even stronger, than Austin’s. When a man about town who had been living a rather fast life got into financial difficulties, he was not usually very squeamish as to how he extricated himself. £75,000 to a man perhaps faced with ruin would be worth taking a risk for. And if Cosgrove had become hardened enough to commit the crime, he would probably not have jibbed at making the plant to throw the suspicion on Austin. Tanner concluded that if Austin’s alibi held, his next work must be the investigation of Cosgrove’s life as well as his movements on the night of the murder.

===Chinese===
他的思绪又转到科斯格罗夫·庞森身上。这是另一个能从威廉爵士之死中得好处的人。而且照阿巴思诺特先生的话来看，他的利害未必弱于奥斯汀，甚至可能更强。一个浪荡城中、日子过得相当放浪的人，一旦陷入财务困境，为求脱身，通常是不大讲究手段的。对一个也许面临破产的人，75,000 英镑值得为之冒一回险。倘若科斯格罗夫已经狠到下得去手作案，那么再设个局把嫌疑栽到奥斯汀身上，他多半也不会缩手。坦纳得出结论：如果奥斯汀的不在场证明站得住，他下一步的功夫，就得花在彻查科斯格罗夫的履历及其案发当晚的行踪上。

===Original===
His whole cogitations therefore simply brought him back to the point from which he had started. Before he did anything else, every point of Austin’s alibi must be gone into with the most meticulous care.

===Chinese===
于是他这一番冥思苦想，兜了个圈子，又把他送回了出发点。在做任何别的事情之前，奥斯汀不在场证明的每一个环节，都必须以最一丝不苟的细心查验到底。

===Original===
Accordingly, having refilled his pipe, he set himself to go over in detail the story Austin Ponson had told him of his movements on the night of the murder. He wished not only to be clear in his own mind as to the sequence of events, but to make a list of the points upon which he could reasonably hope to obtain confirmatory evidence.

===Chinese===
他重新装满烟斗，随即着手把奥斯汀·庞森讲给他听的案发当晚行踪，细细过了一遍。他不仅要让自己对事件的先后次序了然于心，还要开一张单子，列出那些他有望合理取得佐证的要点。

===Original===
Austin, according to his own statement, left the pavilion of the Halford boat club at about ten minutes before nine. He rowed to the Old Ferry, sat there for about ten minutes, until the maid arrived with the note, then walked to the Abbey and spent a few minutes searching for Miss Drew and her friends. Failing to find them, he went to the Franklyns’ house, satisfied himself it was empty, returned to the Old Ferry, and rowed back to Halford, arriving at the pavilion about quarter before eleven. There he had waked the caretaker to get the boat in. He had walked to the Drews’, stayed with them some ten minutes, going on to his own house, which he reached about twenty minutes past eleven. Now, first, as to the checking of these hours.

===Chinese===
照奥斯汀自己的陈述：九点差十分左右，他离开哈尔福德划船俱乐部的会所；划船到老渡口，在那里坐了约十分钟，直到那女仆把字条送到；随后步行到修道院，花几分钟寻找德鲁小姐和她的朋友们；没有找到，他又走到弗兰克林家的宅子，弄确实了屋里空无一人，这才回到老渡口，划船返回哈尔福德，约十一点差一刻回到俱乐部会所。在那里他唤起看门人，才把船收进去。之后他步行到德鲁家，坐了约十分钟，再回自己的住处，到家约十一点二十分。好，先来核对这些钟点。

===Original===
Tanner took a sheet of paper, and looking up his note of the times it had taken him to walk the various distances, and estimating for the rowing, he made a statement something like a railway time table. When he had finished he found that the total estimated time which the journeys should have taken worked out at practically that mentioned by Austin. So far, then, the story hung together.

===Chinese===
坦纳取过一张纸，翻出自己记下的徒步各段路程所需时间的笔记，再估出划船的用时，编成一份类似铁路时刻表的东西。编完一看，各段行程估算出的总耗时，同奥斯汀所说的几乎分毫不差。如此说来，这故事到目前为止是自洽的。

===Original===
Next with regard to outside testimony. What confirmation would it be reasonable to expect?

===Chinese===
其次是旁证。可以合理指望什么样的佐证呢？

===Original===
From the attendant at the boat club pavilion he should be able to learn if Austin did take out a boat, and if so, at what hour. Possibly other persons in the club might have been present, and could substantiate this point. The attendant would also know the hour at which he was called out on Austin’s return. The Drews would certainly have remarked the time of Austin’s call, and lastly, the latter’s own servants would be sure to remember the hour of his return. There should be ample﻿—indeed overwhelming﻿—evidence of the times at which Austin set out with his boat and returned.

===Chinese===
从俱乐部会所的看门人那里，他应当能问出奥斯汀当晚是否果真取船外出，若果真取了，又是什么时辰。俱乐部里或许还有旁人在场，能证实这一点。奥斯汀回来时看门人被唤起身，那时刻看门人也必定记得。德鲁一家肯定留意过奥斯汀来访的时刻；最后，奥斯汀自家的仆人也一定记得他回来的钟点。关于奥斯汀携船出发与归来的时刻，证据应当是充足的——简直是压倒性的。

===Original===
So far so good, but it was clear to Tanner that if he could get no further corroboration than this, the whole alibi was worthless. One point﻿—but that a vital point﻿—would remain unsubstantiated. Austin might have left and returned at the hours he stated, but where was he between them? Did he go to the Abbey and the Franklyns’? If he did, he could not have been to Luce Manor, and necessarily was innocent. How was this to be ascertained?

===Chinese===
到这一步还算顺当；但坦纳心里明白，倘若再得不到更进一层的佐证，整个不在场证明便一文不值。有一个要点——而且是性命攸关的要点——将无从证实。奥斯汀固然可以在他所说的钟点离家与回家，可这两个钟点之间，他人又在哪里？他果真去了修道院和弗兰克林家么？若是去了，他便到不了卢斯庄园，必然清白无辜。可这一层又如何查实呢？

===Original===
There were, of course, the footprints. That these were made by Austin’s shoes was beyond question. The fact that the latter were new prevented the marks showing the individuality that might otherwise have been expected, but against that, at one point on the edge of the left sole there was a slight dint﻿—caused possibly by striking a stone﻿—and this dint was faithfully reproduced in the prints. It was too much to suppose that two separate pairs of similar shoes should have similar dints.

===Chinese===
当然，还有那几枚脚印。脚印是奥斯汀那双鞋留下的，这一点无可置疑。鞋是新的，印痕上便显不出本来或许可以指望的那种个人特征；但反过来说，左鞋掌边缘有一处小小的凹痕——多半是磕在石头上磕出来的——而这凹痕，在脚印里也如实再现。要说两双各自独立的同款鞋上都带一模一样的凹痕，未免太巧了。

===Original===
But two other points were by no means beyond question. Though it was certain that Austin’s shoes had passed over that ground, how could it be proved that Austin was then wearing them? How, again, could it be known that they were made at the time stated? Could not Austin really have been at Luce Manor on that evening, and have made the tracks to the Abbey at some other time? Tanner was puzzled. He did not see how this point was to be cleared up.

===Chinese===
但另外两点却决谈不上无可置疑。奥斯汀的鞋固然走过那片地面，可怎么能证明当时穿鞋的正是奥斯汀本人？又怎么能知道脚印就是在所说的那个时辰留下的？难道奥斯汀就不能当晚真去了卢斯庄园，而在别的什么时间踩出通向修道院的那串脚印？坦纳犯了难，看不出这一层该怎么弄个水落石出。

===Original===
Next morning he began his investigation at the pavilion of the Halford boat club. It was quite a large place, situated on a kind of bay on the river, just below the town. The house was built of red brick, with heavy overhanging eaves, and a kind of piazza in front. Before the piazza and stretching a good way past it in each direction, was the wharf wall, with several broad flights of steps leading to the river. Out on the water were moored some dozen or more boats, and others were in the railed-off space surrounding the house. Tanner pushed open the gate of this railing and entered.

===Chinese===
次日早晨，他从哈尔福德划船俱乐部的会所着手查起。那是个相当大的场子，坐落在镇子下游一处河湾里。房子红砖砌成，屋檐沉沉外探，房前带一方游廊。游廊前面是码头护墙，朝两头各延伸出去好一段，其间几道宽阔的石阶下到水边。水上系着十几条或更多的船，另有一些搁在房子四周用栏杆围出的场地上。坦纳推开栏杆的门，走了进去。

===Original===
At first he could see no one about, then, as he walked round the house, he came on a youngish man in a cap and a blue jersey, who was washing out one of the boats. He hailed him, and they got into conversation in a leisurely way. Tanner praised the house and general appearance of the place, and then gradually came to the object of his call.

===Chinese===
起初他四下看不见一个人影；待绕着房子走到一侧，撞见一个戴便帽、穿蓝色针织衫的年轻人，正在冲洗一条船。他招呼一声，两人便不紧不慢地攀谈起来。坦纳称赞了那房子和整个场子的气象，随后渐渐转到此行的正题上。

===Original===
He had no trouble in obtaining the required information. The caretaker remembered Austin taking out the boat on the Wednesday night. He confirmed the latter’s statement on all points, and was quite certain of the hours he had left and returned.

===Chinese===
他要打听的情况不费吹灰之力便到了手。看门人记得奥斯汀星期三晚上取船的事，后者陈述的各点他都一一证实，对他离所与返回的钟点也一口咬定，毫不含糊。

===Original===

「偏偏就是这个我办不到，」他答道，「鞋不在这儿了。星期五我发现鞋不见了，告诉了奥斯汀先生。他说那双鞋不合脚，送去撑一撑了。」

===Original===
“They were new, then?”

===Chinese===
「那么，鞋是新的？」

===Original===
“Yes, he got them in town on Monday.”

===Chinese===
「是新的，星期一他在城里买的。」

===Original===
“It was curious he should wear them that night if they weren’t comfortable.”

===Chinese===
「既然不合脚，那晚他还偏穿它们，倒奇怪了。」

===Original===
“I suppose he hadn’t found it out. That evening was the first time he had had them on.”

===Chinese===
「我猜他自己还没觉出来。那天晚上是他头一回上脚。」

===Original===
“I see.” Tanner nodded his head, then continued, “What time did he go out, Mr. Lewis?”

===Chinese===
「明白了。」坦纳点了点头，接着又问：「刘易斯先生，他是几点出门的？」

===Original===
“About eight or a bit after.”

===Chinese===
「八点上下，或者稍晚一点。」

===Original===
“And when did he get back?”

===Chinese===
「什么时候回来的？」

===Original===
“A little before half-past eleven. I brought him some whisky, and when I was coming out of his room the clock struck the half-hour.”

===Chinese===
「十一点半差一点。我给他送了威士忌去，正从他房里出来时，钟敲了半点。」

===Original===
“And he didn’t go out again that night?”

===Chinese===
「当夜他没有再出去吧？」

===Original===
“No, he went to bed about twelve. I heard him go up.”

===Chinese===
「没有，十二点前后他就上楼睡了。我听见他上楼去的。」

===Original===
“I see. And what was the next occasion he wore those shoes?”

===Chinese===
「明白了。那双鞋后来他还什么时候穿过？」

===Original===
“There wasn’t no next occasion. That was the only time he had them on.”

===Chinese===
「后来再没有过。他就上过那一回脚。」

===Original===
Tanner considered. This seemed to be pretty conclusive, but he was anxious to obtain even stronger evidence. After a moment he went on again.

===Chinese===
坦纳思量着。这看来已经相当板上钉钉，但他还想拿到更硬的证据。沉吟片刻，他又接着说了下去。

===Original===
“I’m afraid I’m not quite clear about the thing yet. What I want to get at is whether anyone else could have got hold of those shoes and made the tracks I saw at the Abbey.”

===Chinese===
「这事恐怕我还没弄利落。我想弄明白的是：会不会有旁人拿到过那双鞋，踩出了我在修道院看见的那串脚印。」

===Original===
“If that’s all’s worrying you, you may make your mind easy. Those shoes were in my charge from Monday evening till Mr. Ponson put them on on Wednesday after dinner. Then I brought them down to clean before I went to bed that night, and they were there till he took them on Friday. I remarked them particularly because they were new, and if anyone had touched them I would have known. Besides, there was no one about that could do it.”

===Chinese===
「您操心的若只是这一桩，那尽可以把心放进肚子里。从星期一晚上起，那双鞋就归我照管，直到星期三晚饭后庞森先生穿上它们。当晚我睡前把它们拿下去擦拭，之后它们就一直放在那儿，直到星期五他取走。因为鞋是新的，我格外留意过，谁若碰过，瞒不过我的眼睛。再说，周围也没有能做这件事的人。」

===Original===
“What state were they in when you cleaned them?”

===Chinese===
「您擦鞋的时候，它们是什么模样？」

===Original===
“Muddy﻿—very wet and muddy. I couldn’t think where Mr. Austin had been to get them into such a condition.”

===Chinese===
「满是泥——又湿又泥。我想不出奥斯汀先生去了什么地方，把鞋糟践成那样。」

===Original===
“One more question, Mr. Lewis. You tell me the shoes were not worn except on Wednesday evening. But could someone else not have worn them then? Suppose Mr. Austin went out wearing some other pair, and someone else slipped in and got hold of these and made the tracks, and then put back the shoes without your knowing?”

===Chinese===
「再问一个问题，刘易斯先生。您说那双鞋除星期三晚上外没有穿过。可当晚穿它们的，会不会是别的什么人呢？假定奥斯汀先生换穿了一双别的鞋出门，有人溜进来抓起这双去踩下脚印，再趁您不备把鞋放回原处呢？」

===Original===
The butler looked at the other with an expression of pitying scorn.

===Chinese===
管家望着对方，一脸怜悯加鄙夷的神气。

===Original===
“Why, Mr. Inspector, I’m not altogether a fool. I tell you I saw them on Mr. Austin’s feet when he was going out, and I saw them on his feet when he was coming in, so they weren’t in the house for anyone else to take. And what’s more, if that doesn’t convince you, every other pair of Mr. Austin’s boots and shoes were in the house that evening. I know because I happened to look over them to see if any wanted mending. So if anyone else had his new shoes he must have been going about himself in his socks.”

===Chinese===
「嗐，探长先生，我还不至于是个十足的傻瓜。我告诉您：奥斯汀先生出门时，那鞋就在他脚上；他回来时，鞋还在他脚上。所以那段时间鞋根本不在屋里，旁人无从拿走。再者，倘若这还说服不了您——奥斯汀先生当晚其余所有的靴子鞋子，一双不落全在屋里。这我知道，因为我恰巧翻检过一遍，看看有没有该修的。所以，那晚要是有人穿走了他的新鞋，他自己就得穿着袜子满世界跑了。」

===Original===
It was enough. This placed the affair beyond doubt, or it would if one other point were settled. Tanner rose.

===Chinese===
这就够了。事情至此已无置疑的余地——或者说，只消再把另一点敲定，便再无置疑的余地。坦纳站起身。

===Original===
“I am extremely obliged to you, Mr. Lewis, and now I must beg your pardon for having played a little trick on you. I have the shoes. Mr. Ponson gave them to me on Friday. Come with me to the hotel and have a drink, and tell me if the shoes I have are the ones you were speaking of, and that’ll be all I’ll ask you.”

===Chinese===
「实在感激不尽，刘易斯先生。如今我还得请您原谅——我方才对您使了个小小的花招。鞋在我这儿，庞森先生星期五把它们交给了我。请随我到旅馆喝上一杯，看看我手里的鞋是不是您说的那一双，此后我再无别的相求。」

===Original===
That the butler was suspicious there was more in the questions than met the eye was obvious, but he made no remark, and on seeing the shoes, he identified them unhesitatingly as Austin’s.

===Chinese===
这些问话背后另有文章，管家分明已疑心了，但他一句没说；而一见到那双鞋，便毫不迟疑地认出正是奥斯汀的。

===Original===
Tanner was pleased with the result of his inquiries. As he summed up the situation it stood as follows:

===Chinese===
坦纳对这番查访的结果颇为满意。他把局势归结如下：

===Original===
Austin had left Halford and returned to it at such hours as would have just enabled him to reach the Abbey in the interval. Therefore, if he did reach it he could not have been at Luce Manor, and if he was not at Luce Manor he was innocent. Footprints were made at the Abbey by a certain pair of shoes. Those shoes were at Austin’s house every moment of the time from their purchase till they came into Tanner’s possession, except during the particular period in question. The tracks at the Abbey must therefore have been made during this period. Further, during this period Austin himself must have been wearing the shoes, as not only had he left his house and returned to it wearing them, but he had no others to put on﻿—the remainder were all at his house. If, therefore, Austin did not himself make the tracks at the Abbey, he must have had no shoes during the time this was being done, in which case he could hardly have been at Luce Manor committing the murder. To Tanner the alibi was complete. Short of seeing Austin at the Abbey, he could expect no stronger evidence.

===Chinese===
奥斯汀离开哈尔福德又回到哈尔福德，两个钟点之间恰好够他去一趟修道院。因此，他若真到了修道院，便到不了卢斯庄园；到不了卢斯庄园，他便清白无辜。修道院的脚印是某一双鞋踩下的。那双鞋从买下到落入坦纳之手，时时刻刻都待在奥斯汀家里——唯独所说的那段特定时间除外。修道院的脚印，因此必定是在那段时间里踩下的。进一层说，在那段时间里，穿这双鞋的必定是奥斯汀本人：因为他不但穿着它们离家、又穿着它们进门，而且他无鞋可换——其余的鞋全在家里。所以，倘若修道院的脚印不是奥斯汀本人踩下的，那么脚印留下之时他必定脚上无鞋；果真如此，他也很难再跑到卢斯庄园去行凶了。在坦纳看来，这份不在场证明已告圆满。除非亲眼看见奥斯汀人在修道院，他想不出更硬的证据了。

===Original===
Even if the truth of Austin’s story were unlikely, Tanner would have felt compelled to believe it. But, as he had seen from the start, it was by no means unlikely. On the contrary the whole thing was just the kind of plant the real murderer might probably enough devise to shift suspicion from himself to Austin. That it was such a plant Tanner now felt certain.

===Chinese===
即便奥斯汀的说辞本身不像真的，坦纳也觉得不能不信了。何况正如他从头就看出的，这事绝非不像真的。恰恰相反，整件事正是真凶很可能设计出来的那种栽赃的局，好把嫌疑从自己身上挪到奥斯汀身上。它正是这样一个局——对此，坦纳如今已确信不疑。

===Original===
And if so, had it not one rather suggestive point? The man who made the plant was familiar with Austin and his affairs. Who, of those who knew the affairs of both Austin and Sir William, had an interest in the latter’s death?

===Chinese===
倘若如此，这里面岂不是有一个颇堪玩味之处么？设局之人对奥斯汀及其私事了如指掌。那么，在既知奥斯汀底细、又知威廉爵士底细的人当中，谁盼着后者死呢？

===Original===
The answer was not far to seek. One such at least was Cosgrove Ponson. He had both the knowledge and the motive. Tanner felt his next business must be with the cousin.

===Chinese===
答案不远。这样的人至少有一个：科斯格罗夫·庞森。他既有那份知识，又有那份动机。坦纳感到，下一步的功课，非落在这位堂兄弟身上不可。

===Original===
And then a more sinister idea entered the Inspector’s mind. What if there was more in the plant than a mere attempt to shift the suspicion off the murderer? What if the plan was to encompass Austin’s death as well? If Austin were convicted and executed it would make a great difference to Cosgrove apart from rendering his position safe. Tanner recalled the terms of the will. If Sir William died Cosgrove received £75,000, but if Austin also should lose his life his cousin would net another £30,000. Here was motive enough for anything.

===Chinese===
接着，一个更阴狠的念头钻进探长心里。倘若这个局里，还不止是把嫌疑从凶手身上引开这一层呢？倘若这计划连奥斯汀的性命也要一并算计呢？奥斯汀若被定罪处决，科斯格罗夫的地位固可安然无虞，好处却远不止此。坦纳记起遗嘱的条款：威廉爵士一死，科斯格罗夫得 75,000 英镑；奥斯汀若再丧了命，他这位堂兄弟还能净捞另外 30,000 英镑。这样的动机，干什么都够了。

===Original===
Tanner recollected the woman who, Austin had stated, had handed him the note at the Old Ferry. As the latter’s story must now be taken as true, this must be a real woman, and if Cosgrove were the guilty man she must be his accomplice.

===Chinese===
坦纳想起那个女人——据奥斯汀说，是她在老渡口把字条递给他的。奥斯汀的说辞如今既必须当作实情看待，这女人便必有其人；而倘若科斯格罗夫就是罪人，她必是同谋。

===Original===
Here was a line of inquiry which might lead to something. Tanner decided he would return to town by the next train, and start this new phase of the case.

===Chinese===
这是一条也许通向些什么的线索。坦纳决定搭下一班火车回城，开启案子的这个新阶段。
