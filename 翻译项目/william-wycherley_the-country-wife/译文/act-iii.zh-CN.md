## Act III / 第三幕

### Scene I / 第一场

===Original===
A room in **Pinchwife's** house.

*Enter **Alithea** and **Mrs. Pinchwife**.*

Alithea
Sister, what ails you? you are grown melancholy.

Mrs. Pinchwife
Would it not make anyone melancholy to see you go every day fluttering about abroad, whilst I must stay at home like a poor lonely sullen bird in a cage?

Alithea
Ay, sister; but you came young, and just from the nest to your cage: so that I thought you liked it, and could be as cheerful in't as others that took their flight themselves early, and are hopping abroad in the open air.

Mrs. Pinchwife
Nay, I confess I was quiet enough till my husband told me what pure lives the London ladies live abroad, with their dancing, meetings, and junketings, and dressed every day in their best gowns; and I warrant you, play at ninepins every day of the week, so they do.

===Chinese===
平奇怀夫家中一室。

*（**阿莉西娅**与**平奇怀夫太太**上。）*

**阿莉西娅.** 妹妹，你怎么了？蔫蔫的，闷出病来了。

**平奇怀夫太太.** 天天瞧你在外头扑腾得欢，我倒得像只可怜的、孤零零、闷声不响的鸟儿关在笼子里——换了谁不闷出病来？

**阿莉西娅.** 是这样，妹妹；可你来得早，才出窝就进了笼子：我还当你喜欢呢，也能像那些早早自己飞出来的鸟儿一样自在，在外头露天里蹦跶。

**平奇怀夫太太.** 嗐，我认账，起先我倒是够安分的，直到我男人跟我说，伦敦的太太们在外头过的日子多滋润——又是跳舞，又是聚会，又是野餐出游，天天穿着顶好的衣裳；还保管一礼拜天天打九柱戏呢，真的。

===Original===
*Enter **Pinchwife**.*

Pinchwife
Come, what's here to do? you are putting the town-pleasures in her head, and setting her a-longing.

Alithea
Yes, after ninepins. You suffer none to give her those longings you mean but yourself.

Pinchwife
I tell her of the vanities of the town like a confessor.

Alithea
A confessor! just such a confessor as he that, by forbidding a silly ostler to grease the horse's teeth, taught him to do't.

Pinchwife
Come, Mrs. Flippant, good precepts are lost when bad examples are still before us: the liberty you take abroad makes her hanker after it, and out of humour at home. Poor wretch! she desired not to come to London; I would bring her.

===Chinese===
*（**平奇怀夫**上。）*

**平奇怀夫.** 喂，这是干什么？你们把城里的乐子往她脑袋里灌，勾得她眼馋起来了。

**阿莉西娅.** 是啊，馋九柱戏呢。你说的那种馋，除了你自个儿，你不许任何人勾她。

**平奇怀夫.** 我跟她讲城里的虚荣，就像听罪神父讲道似的。

**阿莉西娅.** 听罪神父！活像那个神父——不许傻马夫往马牙齿上抹油，反倒教会了他这一手。

**平奇怀夫.** 得了，轻嘴薄舌太太，眼前摆着坏榜样，好教训全白搭：你在外头的那份自由，勾得她眼馋心痒，在家里憋气。可怜的人儿！她本不想来伦敦；是我非要带来的。

===Original===
Alithea
Very well.

Pinchwife
She has been this week in town, and never desired till this afternoon to go abroad.

Alithea
Was she not at a play yesterday?

Pinchwife
Yes; but she ne'er asked me; I was myself the cause of her going.

Alithea
Then if she ask you again, you are the cause of her asking, and not my example.

Pinchwife
Well, tomorrow night I shall be rid of you; and the next day, before 'tis light, she and I'll be rid of the town, and my dreadful apprehensions.—Come, be not melancholy; for thou sha't go into the country after tomorrow, dearest.

Alithea
Great comfort!

Mrs. Pinchwife
Pish! what d'ye tell me of the country for?

===Chinese===
**阿莉西娅.** 很好。

**阿莉西娅.** 好大的安慰！

**平奇怀夫太太.** 啐！跟我说乡下做什么？

Let me alone; I am not well.

Pinchwife
O, if that be all—what ails my dearest?

Ha!—

That's by my example too!

Mrs. Pinchwife
Of what sickness?

Pinchwife
O, of that which is worse than the plague, jealousy.

Mrs. Pinchwife
Pish, you jeer! I'm sure there's no such disease in our receipt-book at home.

Pinchwife
No, thou never met'st with it, poor innocent.—Well, if thou cuckold me, 'twill be my own fault—for cuckolds and bastards are generally makers of their own fortune. *Aside.*

Pinchwife

===Chinese===
**平奇怀夫太太.** 害什么病？

**平奇怀夫.** 哦，害那比瘟疫还凶的病——吃醋。

**平奇怀夫太太.** 啐，你逗我！咱们家那本药方书里，保管没有这么一号病。

**平奇怀夫.** 是没有，你从没碰上过它，可怜的天真人。——（旁白）唉，你要真叫我戴了绿帽，那也是我自作自受——绿帽汉和私生子，多半都是自个儿挣来的命。

**平奇怀夫.** 戏刚散，她才从那儿回来。可你怎么这么急着看戏？

===Original===
Mrs. Pinchwife
Faith, dear, not that I care one pin for their talk there; but I like to look upon the playermen, and would see, if I could, the gallant you say loves me: that's all, dear bud.

Pinchwife
Is that all, dear bud?

Alithea
This proceeds from my example!

Mrs. Pinchwife
But if the play be done, let's go abroad, however, dear bud.

Pinchwife
Come have a little patience and thou shalt go into the country on Friday.

Mrs. Pinchwife
Therefore I would see first some sights to tell my neighbours of. Nay, I will go abroad, that's once.

===Chinese===
**平奇怀夫太太.** 说真的，亲爱的，倒不是他们台上说什么我爱听一个子儿；我就是爱瞧戏子爷们，还想瞅一眼——要能瞅着的话——你说的那位爱我的公子爷：就这么点事，亲乖乖。

**阿莉西娅.** 这也是我带的头！

Alithea
I'm the cause of this desire too!

Alithea
No, you, because you would not let him see your handsome wife out of your lodging.

===Chinese===
**阿莉西娅.** 这份馋也是我惹的！

**平奇怀夫.** 不，不。——阿莉西娅小姐，这个该死的问题也是你惹的罢？——（旁白）唉，她说得在理。他爱上我老婆了——是追着她来的——就是这样——可我要把他的情掐死在芽里；免得他一路追到乡下，故意把马车轮子弄断在我家附近，好寻个由头进门。不过我自认懂得城里这一套。

===Original===
Mrs. Pinchwife
Come, pray, bud, let's go abroad before 'tis late; for I will go, that's flat and plain.

Pinchwife
*Aside.* So! the obstinacy already of the town-wife; and I must, whilst she's here, humour her like one.—*Aloud.* Sister, how shall we do, that she may not be seen, or known?

Alithea
Let her put on her mask.

Pinchwife
Pshaw! a mask makes people but the more inquisitive, and is as ridiculous a disguise as a stage-beard: her shape, stature, habit will be known. And if we should meet with Horner, he would be sure to take acquaintance with us, must wish her joy, kiss her, talk to her, leer upon her, and the devil and all. ===Chinese===

**平奇怀夫.**（旁白）瞧！城里娘们的犟劲儿这就上来了；她在这儿一天，我就得拿城里那一套哄她一天。——（高声）妹妹，怎么着才能叫她不叫人瞧见、不叫人认出来？

Alithea

Mrs. Pinchwife
Nay, shall we go? The Exchange will be shut, and I have a mind to see that.

Pinchwife

### Scene II / 第二场

===Original===
The New Exchange.

*Enter **Horner**, **Harcourt**, and **Dorilant**.*

Dorilant
Engaged to women, and not sup with us!

Horner
Ay, a pox on 'em all!

Harcourt
You were much a more reasonable man in the morning, and had as noble resolutions against 'em, as a widower of a week's liberty.

Dorilant
Did I ever think to see you keep company with women in vain!

Horner
In vain: no—'tis since I can't love 'em, to be revenged on 'em.

===Chinese===
新交易所。

*（**霍纳**、**哈考特**与**多里兰特**上。）*

**多里兰特.** 有妇人之约，就不跟我们吃夜宵了！

**霍纳.** 是啊，叫她们一齐遭瘟去！

**哈考特.** 你今天早上还像个明白事理的人，立下那么高贵的决心要抵制她们，跟一个只自由了一个礼拜的鳏夫似的。

**多里兰特.** 我何曾想到会看见你白陪女人的空！

**霍纳.** 白陪：不——如今我爱不成她们，正好报仇。

===Original===
Harcourt
Now your sting is gone, you looked in the box amongst all those women like a drone in the hive; all upon you, shoved and ill-used by 'em all, and thrust from one side to t'other.

Dorilant
Yet he must be buzzing amongst 'em still, like other beetle-headed liquorish drones. Avoid 'em, and hate 'em, as they hate you.

Horner
Because I do hate 'em, and would hate 'em yet more, I'll frequent 'em. You may see by marriage, nothing makes a man hate a woman more than her constant conversation. In short, I converse with 'em, as you do with rich fools, to laugh at 'em and use 'em ill.

Dorilant
But I would no more sup with women, unless I could lie with 'em, than sup with a rich coxcomb, unless I could cheat him.

Horner
Yes, I have known thee sup with a fool for his drinking; if he could set out your hand that way only, you were satisfied, and if he were a wine-swallowing mouth, 'twas enough.

===Chinese===
**哈考特.** 你如今没了螫刺，坐在包厢里混在一群女人当中，活像蜂巢里的雄蜂；谁都骑在你头上，个个作践你，把你从这边推到那边。

**多里兰特.** 可他还非得在她们当中嗡嗡不可，跟别的笨脑袋馋嘴雄蜂一样。躲开她们，恨她们，就像她们恨你一样。

**多里兰特.** 可要我陪女人吃夜宵，就得能睡她们；就像陪有钱的草包吃夜宵，就得能骗他的钱——不然我一口也不去。

**霍纳.** 得了吧，我就见过你为着酒陪傻子吃夜宵；只要他单在这一项上衬你的手，你就满足，只要他是个灌酒的嗓子，就够本了。

===Original===
Harcourt
Yes, a man drinks often with a fool, as he tosses with a marker, only to keep his hand in use. But do the ladies drink?

Horner
Yes, sir; and I shall have the pleasure at least of laying 'em flat with a bottle, and bring as much scandal that way upon 'em as formerly t'other.

Harcourt
Perhaps you may prove as weak a brother among 'em that way as t'other.

Dorilant
Foh! drinking with women is as unnatural as scolding with 'em. But 'tis a pleasure of decayed fornicators, and the basest way of quenching love.

Harcourt
Nay, 'tis drowning love, instead of quenching it. But leave us for civil women too!

Dorilant
Ay, when he can't be the better for 'em. We hardly pardon a man that leaves his friend for a wench, and that's a pretty lawful call.

===Chinese===
**哈考特.** 是啊，人常同傻子对饮，就像同记分员掷骰子，只为活动活动手法。可这几位太太喝酒么？

**霍纳.** 喝，先生；我至少能得一样乐趣——一瓶酒把她们放平，在这一路上给她们招来的丑闻，同先前那一路一样多。

**哈考特.** 只怕你在这条路上，同那条路上一样，也是个不中用的角儿。

**多里兰特.** 呸！同女人喝酒，就跟同女人对骂一样不合天理。那是过气嫖客的乐子，是灭欲最下三滥的法子。

**哈考特.** 何止，那是把爱情淹死，不是浇灭。——竟还为「规矩女人」撇下我们！

**多里兰特.** 是啊，谁叫他占不着便宜呢。为个娘们撇下朋友，我们尚且难饶恕——那还算相当合法的由头。

===Original===
Horner

Dorilant

I have other designs upon women than eating and drinking with them; I am in love with Sparkish's mistress, whom he is to marry tomorrow: now how shall I get her?

Harcourt
He! he, I tell you, is my rival, and will hinder my love.

Horner

===Chinese===
**霍纳.** 说真的，她们要是不灌酒，我也不至于撇下你们。

**霍纳.** 瞧，这就来了个肯帮你到手的。

Harcourt
But I cannot come near his mistress but in his company.

Horner
Still the better for you; for fools are most easily cheated when they themselves are accessories: and he is to be bubbled of his mistress as of his money, the common mistress, by keeping him company.

Sparkish

===Chinese===

**霍纳.** 那对你更有利；傻子在自个儿搭伙当同谋的时候最容易上当：骗他的情人，同骗他的钱——大家共有的情妇——一样，都靠陪着他这一路。

**哈考特.** 我向她求爱！

===Original===
