## Act I / 第一幕

### Scene I / 第一场

===Original===
**Horner's** lodging.

*Enter **Horner**, and **Quack** following him at a distance.*

Horner
*Aside.* A quack is as fit for a pimp, as a midwife for a bawd; they are still but in their way, both helpers of nature.—*Aloud.* Well, my dear doctor, hast thou done what I desired?

Quack
I have undone you forever with the women, and reported you throughout the whole town as bad as an eunuch, with as much trouble as if I had made you one in earnest.
