## XXXII Ta-Ta to Barney’s Gap / 第32章 别了，巴尼山口

===Original===
It chanced at last, as June gave place to July and July to August, that I could bear it no longer. I would go away even if I had to walk, and what I would do I did not know or care, my one idea being to leave Barney’s Gap far and far behind. One evening I got a lot of letters from my little brothers and sisters at home. I fretted over them a good deal, and put them under my pillow; and as I had not slept for nights, and was feeling weak and queer, I laid my head upon them to rest a little before going out to get the tea ready. The next thing I knew was that Mrs. M’Swat was shaking me vigorously with one hand, holding a flaring candle in the other, and saying:

===Chinese===
事情终于临头了。六月让位给七月，七月又让位给八月，我再也无法忍受下去。哪怕靠两条腿走，我也要离开；至于走了去做什么，我既不知道也不在乎，唯一的念头就是把巴尼山口远远地、远远地抛在身后。一天傍晚，我收到家里弟弟妹妹们寄来的一沓信。我为这些信烦闷了好一阵，把它们塞到枕头底下；由于接连好几夜不曾合眼，我只觉得虚弱又异样，便把头搁在信上，想在出去预备茶饭之前稍稍歇一歇。接下来我知道的事，便是麦斯沃特太太一手擎着一支火光直蹿的蜡烛，一手使劲摇我，嘴里说道：

===Original===
“Lizer, shut the winder quick. She’s been lyin’ here in the draught till she’s froze, and must have the nightmare, the way she’s been singin’ out that queer, an’ I can’t git her woke up. What ails ye, child? Are ye sick?”

===Chinese===
“莱泽，快把窗户关上。她躺在这穿堂风里，都快冻透了，准是做了噩梦，瞧她那样儿怪里怪气地喊叫，俺咋弄也弄不醒她。你这孩子是咋啦？是不是病了？”

===Original===
I did not know what ailed me, but learnt subsequently that I laughed and cried very much, and pleaded hard with grannie and some Harold to save me, and kept reiterating, “I cannot bear it, I cannot bear it,” and altogether behaved so strangely that Mr. M’Swat became so alarmed that he sent seventeen miles for the nearest doctor. He came next morning, felt my pulse, asked a few questions, and stated that I was suffering from nervous prostration.

===Chinese===
我不知道自己出了什么毛病，后来才听说，我当时又哭又笑闹得好不厉害，苦苦哀求外婆和某一位哈罗德来救我，嘴里反复念叨着“我受不了了，我受不了了”，总之举止那样古怪，把麦斯沃特先生吓慌了神，赶到十七英里外去请最近的医生。医生次日早晨到了，摸了摸我的脉，问了几句话，断定我害的是神经衰竭。

===Original===
“Why, the child is completely run down, and in a fair way to contract brain fever!” he exclaimed. “What has she been doing? It seems as though she had been under some great mental strain. She must have complete rest and change, plenty of diversion and nourishing food, or her mind will become impaired.”

===Chinese===
“哎呀，这孩子亏虚到了根子上，眼看就要转成脑炎症了！”他叫道。“她一直在做些什么来着？看她这样子，像是经受了什么了不得的精神重压。她必须彻底休养、改换环境，多多消遣散心、吃滋补助养的饭食，不然脑筋就要受损了。”

===Original===
He left me a bottle of tonic and Mr. and Mrs. M’Swat many fears. Poor kindhearted souls, they got in a great state, and understood about as much of the cause of my breakdown as I do of the inside of the moon. They ascribed it to the paltry amount of teaching and work I had done.

===Chinese===
他留给我一瓶补药，留给麦斯沃特先生和太太满怀的惶恐。可怜的热心肠人啊，他们慌得不成样子，而对我垮掉的缘由，他们明白的，同我对月亮内里的明白不相上下。他们把它归到我为数可怜的那点儿教书和活计上头。

===Original===
Mrs. M’Swat killed a fowl and stewed it for my delectation. There was part of the inside with many feathers to flavour the dish, and having no appetite, I did not enjoy it, but made a feint of so doing to please the good-natured cook.

===Chinese===
麦斯沃特太太宰了一只母鸡，炖了一锅供我享用。锅里带着半副下水，还搭着好些羽毛给这道菜提味；我毫无胃口，实在受用不起，但为了不叫那好心的厨娘扫兴，还是装出一副吃得津津有味的样子。

===Original===
They intended writing at once to give my parents notice when I would be put on the train. I was pronounced too ill to act as scribe; Lizer was suggested, and then Jimmy, but M’Swat settled the matter thus:

===Chinese===
他们打算立刻写信给我父母报信，说我哪一天会被送上火车。我被断为病得太重，执不得笔；先提议莱泽，又提议吉米，麦斯沃特却这样把事情一锤定音：

===Original===
“Sure, damn it! I’m the proper one to write on an important business matther like this here.”

===Chinese===
“可不，见鬼！这么要紧的一桩正经事儿，合该俺亲自来写。”

===Original===
So pens, ink, and paper were laid on the dining-room table, and the great proclamation went forth among the youngsters, “Pa is goin’ to write a whole letter all by hisself.”

===Chinese===
于是笔、墨、纸都摆上了饭厅的桌子，一道重大的公告在娃娃们中间传布开来：“爸要自个儿独力写一整封信喽。”

===Original===
My door opened with the dining-room, and from my bed I could see the proceeding. Mr. M’Swat hitched his trousers well through the saddle-strap which he always wore as a belt, took off his coat and folded it on the back of a chair, rolled his shirtsleeves up to his elbows, pulled his hat well over his eyes, and “shaped up” to the writing material, none of which met with his approval. The ink was “warter,” the pens had not enough “pint,” and the paper was “trash;” but on being assured it was the good stuff he had purchased especially for himself, he buckled to the fray, producing in three hours a half-sheet epistle, which in grammar, composition, and spelling quite eclipsed the entries in his diary. However, it served its purpose, and my parents wrote back that, did I reach Goulburn on a certain day, a neighbour who would be in town then would bring me home.

===Chinese===
我的房门开向饭厅，从床上就能看到全过程。麦斯沃特先生把他常年当腰带使的那条马鞍肚带好生勒上一道，把裤腰掖得服服帖帖，脱下外套叠在椅背上，衬衫袖子卷到胳膊肘，帽檐往下一拉，严严实实罩住眼睛，对着那套文具“摆开了架势”——而那几样东西没一样入得了他的法眼。墨水是“清汤寡水”，钢笔的“笔尖儿”不够利，纸又净是“破烂货”；可等到有人向他担保，这些正是他特意给自己置办的上好货色，他便毅然投入战斗，三个钟头拿下一封半张纸的书信——论文法、布局与拼写，把他日记里的各条记录远远盖了过去。不过信总算办成了事，我父母回信说，倘使我某一日抵达古尔本，那天恰巧进城的一位邻居会带我回家。

===Original===
Now that it was settled that I had no more to teach the dirty children, out of dirty books, lessons for which they had great disinclination, and no more to direct Lizer’s greasy fingers over the yellow keys of that demented piano in a vain endeavour to teach her “choones,” of which her mother expected her to learn on an average two daily, it seemed as though I had a mountain lifted off me, and I revived magically, got out of bed and packed my things.

===Chinese===
如今事情说定，我再也用不着教那群脏兮兮的孩子，用不着拿脏兮兮的书本教他们老大不情愿的功课，再也用不着引着莱泽油乎乎的手指在那架疯疯癫癫的钢琴的发黄琴键上摸索，枉费心力地教她“曲子”——她母亲指望她平均每天学会两支——我仿佛有一座大山从身上卸了下来，神奇地还了魂，下了床，收拾起自己的东西。

===Original===
I was delighted at the prospect of throwing off the leaden shackles of Barney’s Gap, but there was a little regret mingled with my relief. The little boys had not been always bold. Did I express a wish for a parrot-wing or water-worn stone, or suchlike, after a time I would be certain, on issuing from my bedroom, to find that it had been surreptitiously laid there, and the little soft-eyed fellows would squabble for the privilege of bringing me my post, simply to give me pleasure. Poor little Lizer, and Rose Jane too, copied me in style of dress and manners in a way that was somewhat ludicrous but more pathetic.

===Chinese===
一想到就要甩脱巴尼山口这副铅打的镣铐，我满心欢喜，可宽慰之中也掺着一丝怅惘。那些小男孩并非一向无礼。但凡我流露过想要一片鹦鹉翎羽或一块水磨的石子之类，过些时日，我一走出卧室，准会发现那东西已被人悄悄摆在了那里；几个眼睛温软的小家伙还会为争着替我送“邮件”的体面吵作一团——只为讨我高兴。可怜的小莱泽，还有罗丝·简，都在衣着做派上一味学我，学得几分滑稽，更多的却是叫人心酸。

===Original===
They clustered round to say goodbye. I would be sure to write. Oh yes, of course, and they would write in return and tell me if the bay mare got well, and where they would find the yellow turkey-hen’s nest. When I got well I must come back, and I wouldn’t have as much work to do, but go for more rides to keep well, and so on. Mrs. M’Swat very anxiously impressed it upon me that I was to explain to my mother that it was not her (Mrs. M’Swat’s) fault that I “ailed” from overwork, as I had never complained and always seemed well.

===Chinese===
他们围拢上来道别。我一定写信来的。噢，那自然；他们也要回信，告诉我那匹枣红母马好了没有，他们又在哪儿找到了那只黄毛火鸡的窝。等我病好了，一定得回来；到时候活计不会再叫我干那么多，还能多骑骑马出去走走，好保住身子，如此等等。麦斯沃特太太十万分焦灼地再三叮嘱，要我向我母亲解释明白：我因劳作过度而“病倒”，不是她（麦斯沃特太太）的过错，因为我从不曾叫过苦，看上去一向好好的。

===Original===
With a kindly light on his homely sunburnt face, M’Swat said, as he put me on the train:

===Chinese===
麦斯沃特送我上火车时，那张朴实、晒得黢黑的脸上泛着和善的光，他说：

===Original===
“Sure, tell yer father he needn’t worry over the money. I’ll never be hard on him, an’ if ever I could help ye, I’d be glad.”

===Chinese===
“可不，告诉你爹，钱的事犯不着愁。俺决计不会难为他，往后但凡有帮得上你的地方，俺都乐意。”

===Original===
“Thank you; you are very good, and have done too much already.”

===Chinese===
“谢谢您；您心眼儿真好，为我做的已经太多了。”

===Original===
“Too much! Sure, damn it, wot’s the good er bein’ alive if we can’t help each other sometimes. I don’t mind how much I help a person if they have a little gratitood, but, damn it, I can’t abear ingratitood.”

===Chinese===
“太多了！可不，见鬼，人活着要是不能时不时彼此帮衬一把，那活着还有啥子意思。只要人家存着一点儿感恩的心，俺帮多少都不嫌多；可是见鬼，忘恩负义这号事，俺断断受不了。”

===Original===
“Goodbye, Mr. M’Swat, and thank you.”

===Chinese===
“再见了，麦斯沃特先生，多谢您。”

===Original===
“Goodbye, me gu-r-r-r-l, and never marry that bloke of yours if he don’t git a bit er prawperty, for the divil’s in a poor match.”

===Chinese===
“再见喽，俺的姑——娘——，你那个后生要是置不下一点儿‘家业’，你可千万别嫁他——穷姻缘里头，住着老魔鬼哩。”
