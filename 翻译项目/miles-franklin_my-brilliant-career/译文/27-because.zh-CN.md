## XXV Because? / 第25章 缘何？

===Original===
The Beechams were vacating Five-Bob almost immediately﻿—before Christmas. Grannie, aunt Helen, and uncle Jay-Jay went down to say goodbye to the ladies, who were very heartbroken about being uprooted from Five-Bob, but they approved of their nephew settling things at once and starting on a clean sheet. They intended taking up their residence﻿—hiding themselves, they termed it﻿—in Melbourne. Harold would be detained in Sydney some time during the settling of his affairs, after which he intended to take anything that turned up. He had been offered the management of Five-Bob by those in authority, but could not bring himself to accept managership where he had been master. His great desire, now that Five-Bob was no longer his, was to get as far away from old associations as possible.

===Chinese===
比彻姆一家几乎立刻就要腾出五博丘了——赶在圣诞节以前。外婆、海伦姨妈和杰杰舅舅下去向两位姑奶奶道别；被连根拔离五博丘，两位老小姐伤心得很，却都赞许侄儿当机立断、把诸事料理停当，在一张干净账页上从头另起。她们打算把住处安到墨尔本——用她们自己的话说，是“把自己藏起来”。了结事务期间，哈罗德要在悉尼耽搁些时日，此后碰到什么出路便接什么出路。当权的那些人曾提出把五博丘交给他管理，可他无法说服自己，在自己当过主人的地方屈就管家之职。五博丘既已不再归他，他如今最大的心愿，便是离旧日的一切人事越远越好。

===Original===
He had seen his aunts off, superintended the muster of all stock on the place, dismissed all the female and most of the male employees, and surrendered the reins of government, and as Harold Augustus Beecham, boss of Five-Bob, on Monday, the 21st of December 1896, was leaving the district forever. On Sunday, the 20th of December, he came to bid us goodbye and to arrive at an understanding with me concerning what I had said to him the Sunday before. Grannie, strange to say, never suspected that there was likely to be anything between us. Harold was so undemonstrative, and had always come and gone as he liked at Caddagat: she overlooked the possibility of his being a lover, and in our intercourse allowed us almost the freedom of sister and brother or cousins.

===Chinese===
他送走了两位姑母，督办了场上全部牲口的大清点，辞退了全部女雇工和大半男雇工，交出了当家的权柄；而身为五博丘之主的哈罗德·奥古斯都·比彻姆，将于 1896 年 12 月 21 日，星期一，永远离开这一带。12 月 20 日，星期天，他来向我们辞行，并要就我上一个星期天对他说的话，同我达成一个明白的谅解。说来奇怪，外婆从不曾疑心我们之间兴许有什么。哈罗德是那样不形于色，在卡达加特又一向如在自己家里般来去随心：她压根儿不曾想到他也可能是位求婚者，待我们往来相处，几乎给了我们如同姐弟或表兄妹一般的自由。

===Original===
On this particular afternoon, after we had talked to grannie for a little while, knowing that he wished to interview me, I suggested that he should come up the orchard with me and get some gooseberries. Without demur from anybody we set off, and were scarcely out of hearing before Harold asked me had I really meant what I said.

===Chinese===
这天下午，我们同外婆谈了一小会儿话，我知道他有话要同我说，便提议他随我上果园里去摘些醋栗。谁也没有二话，我们便动身了；才刚走得出了能叫人听见的范围，哈罗德就问我，我说的话是不是当真。

===Original===
“Certainly,” I replied. “That is, if you really care for me, and think it wise to choose me of all my sex.”

===Chinese===
“当然是当真，”我答道。“那就是说，你得当真疼我，并且当真觉得，在全天下的女人里挑中我是明智的。”

===Original===
Ere he put it in words I read his answer in the clear brown eyes bent upon me.

===Chinese===
他还没把话诉诸言语，我已经从他俯望着我的那双清朗的褐色眼睛里，读出了他的回答。

===Original===
“Syb, you know what I feel and would like, but I think it would be mean of me to allow you to make such a sacrifice.”

===Chinese===
“西比，你知道我的心思，也知道我愿意什么；可是任由你做这样的牺牲，我想自己就太卑鄙了。”

===Original===
I knew I was not dealing with a booby, but with a sensible clear-sighted man, and so studied to express myself in a way which would not for an instant give him the impression that I was promising to marry him because﻿—what I don’t know and it doesn’t matter much, but I said:

===Chinese===
我知道对付的不是个笨蛋，而是个明白而有眼光的人，因此字斟句酌，措辞务求不叫他有一瞬间生出这样的印象，以为我答应嫁他是因为——因为什么呢，我不知道，这也无关紧要——可我还是说道：

===Original===
“Hal, don’t you think it is a little selfish of you to want to throw me over just because you have lost your money? You are young, healthy, have good character and influential connections, and plenty of good practical ability and sense, so, surely, you will know no such thing as failure if you meet the world bravely. Go and be the man you are; and if you fail, when I am twenty-one I will marry you, and we will help each other. I am young and strong, and am used to hard work, so poverty will not alarm me in the least. If you want me, I want you.”

===Chinese===
“哈尔，只因为你丢了钱，就想着把我抛开，你不觉得这有点儿自私吗？你年轻，健康，品行好，亲友情面广，实干的本事和见识都足足有余；只要你勇敢地面对世人，断不会知道什么叫失败。去，做你本来就是的那个男人；倘若你败了，等我满二十一岁，我就嫁给你，我们彼此扶持。我年轻力壮，苦活儿做惯了，贫穷一点儿也吓不着我。你要是要我，我也要你。”

===Original===
“Syb, you are such a perfect little brick that I couldn’t be such a beggarly cur as to let you do that. I knew you were as true as steel under your funny little whims and contrariness; and could you really love me now that I am poor?”

===Chinese===
“西比，你是这么个十全十美的好姑娘，我总不能贱得像条癞皮狗，由着你这样做。我早知道，在你那些滑稽的小性子和别扭底下，你真得像钢一样；可如今我穷了，你当真能爱我吗？”

===Original===
I replied with vigour:

===Chinese===
我理直气壮地答道：

===Original===
“Do you think I am that sort, that cares for a person only because he has a little money? Why! that is the very thing I am always preaching against. If a man was a lord or a millionaire I would not have him if I loved him not, but I would marry a poor cripple if I loved him. It wasn’t because you owned Five-Bob Downs that I liked you, but because you have a big heart in which one would have room to get warm, and because you are true, and because you are kind and big and﻿—” Here I could feel my voice getting shaky, and being afraid I would make a fool of myself by crying, I left off.

===Chinese===
“你把我当成那路人了吗——只因一个人有几个钱，才把他放在心上？嘿！那恰恰是我平日里口口声声反对的东西。一个男人，哪怕他是勋爵、是百万富翁，我不爱他就不要他；可我若爱他，哪怕他是个穷瘸子，我也嫁。我喜欢你，从来不是因为你坐拥五博丘，而是因为你有一颗宽大的心，人靠过去有地方取暖；因为你真，因为你仁慈、宽厚，还因为——”说到这里，我觉得自己的声音发起颤来，生怕哭出来丢人现眼，便住了口。

===Original===
“Syb, I will try and fix matters up a bit, and will claim you in that time if I have a home.”

===Chinese===
“西比，我要尽力把局面稍稍收拾起来；到那时，只要我有了个家，就来娶你。”

===Original===
“Claim me, home or not, if you are so disposed, but I will make this condition. Do not tell anyone we are engaged, and remember you are perfectly free. If you see a woman you like more than me, promise me on your sacred word that you will have none of those idiotic unjust ideas of keeping true to me. Promise.”

===Chinese===
“那就来娶我吧，有没有家都罢，只要你愿意；不过我要定下一个条件。不要告诉任何人我们订了婚，还要记着，你是完全自由的。倘若你遇上一个比我更合意的女人，我要你凭你的神圣信义起誓：绝不存那些愚蠢又不讲理的念头，觉得非对我守着不可。答应我。”

===Original===
“Yes, I will promise,” he said easily, thinking then, no doubt, as many a one before him has thought, that he would never be called upon to fulfil his word.

===Chinese===
“好，我答应。”他轻描淡写地说，心里想的，无疑同他之前的许多人想过的一样：这话是永远不会被要求兑现的。

===Original===
“I will promise in return that I will not look at another man in a matrimonial way until the four years are up, so you need not be jealous and worry yourself; for, Hal, you can trust me, can you not?”

===Chinese===
“我也回敬一个诺言：四年期满之前，我绝不拿挑丈夫的眼光去瞧别的男人，所以你不必吃醋，也不必自寻烦恼；哈尔，你是信得过我的，对不对？”

===Original===
Taking my hand in his and looking at me with a world of love in his eyes, which moved me in spite of myself, he said:

===Chinese===
他把我的手握进他手里，眼里盛着满世界的爱意望着我，我不由得为之动容。他说：

===Original===
“I could trust you in every way to the end of the world.”

===Chinese===
“无论在哪一件事上，我都信得过你，直信到天涯海角。”

===Original===
“Thank you, Harold. What we have said is agreed upon﻿—that is, of course, as things appear now: if anything turns up to disturb this arrangement it is not irrevocable in the least degree, and we can lay out more suitable plans. Four years will not be long, and I will be more sensible at the end of that time﻿—that is, of course, if I ever have any sense. We will not write or have any communication, so you will be perfectly free if you see anyone you like better than me to go in and win. Do you agree?”

===Chinese===
“谢谢你，哈罗德。咱们说定的就算说定了——自然，这是照眼下的光景而论：倘有什么变故搅了这个安排，它丝毫也不是不可更改的，咱们尽可以另订更合适的计划。四年不会长，到那时候我也会更懂事了——自然，这还得看我日后到底有没有懂事的那一天。咱们不写信、不通音讯，这样，你若遇上比我更称心的人，便完全可以放开手去赢得她。你同意吗？”

===Original===
“Certainly; any little thing like that you can settle according to your fancy. I’m set up as long as I get you one way or another, that’s all I want. It was a bit tough being cleared out from all the old ways, but if I have you to stand by me it will be a great start. Say what you said last Sunday, again. Syb, say you will be my wife.”

===Chinese===
“当然；这类小事，随你高兴怎么定就怎么定。只要不管怎样能得着你，我就万事齐备，我要的仅此而已。从旧日的一切里被扫地出门，滋味是有点儿苦；可有你站在我身边，便是个了不起的起点。把上星期天说的话，再说一遍。西比，说你愿意做我的妻子。”

===Original===
I had expected him to put it in that way, and believing in doing all or nothing, had laid out that I would put my hand in his and promise what he asked. But now the word wife finished me up. I was very fond of Harold﻿—fond to such an extent that had I a fortune I would gladly have given it all to him: I felt capable of giving him a life of servitude, but I loved him﻿—big, manly, lovable, wholesome Harold﻿—from the crown of his head to the sole of his foot he was good in my sight, but lacking in that power over me which would make me desirous of being the mother of his children.

===Chinese===
我料到他会这样提出来，而且抱着要么全给、要么全无的宗旨，早已打定主意要把手放进他手里，把所求的应下。可到了这一步，“妻子”这个词却把我击垮了。我深爱哈罗德——爱到这般地步：我若有一份家财，会欢欢喜喜地全部给他；我觉得自己尽可以为他操劳一世；说到爱——魁伟、有男子气、可爱、健全的哈罗德——从头顶到脚底，他在我眼里样样都好，独独缺了那一种慑服我的力量，那种能叫我甘愿去做他孩子们的母亲的力量。

===Original===
As for explaining my feelings to him﻿—ha! He would laughingly call them one of my funny little whims. With his orthodox, practical, plain, commonsense views of these things, he would not understand me. What was there to understand? Only that I was queer and different from other women. ===Chinese===
至于把我的心事解说给他听——哈！他只会笑着把它唤作我又一个小小的怪念头。凭着他对这些事那套正统、实际、直白、凭常识的见解，他是不会懂我的。又有什么可懂的呢？无非是我这个人古怪，跟别的女人不一样罢了。可他正等着我开口。我既已把手扶上了犁，就再不能回头。“妻子”这个词我说不出口，但我把手放进他的手里，定定地望着他，说道——

===Original===

===Chinese===

他满足了。
