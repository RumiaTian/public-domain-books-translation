## XXVIII To Life / 第28章 活下去

===Original===
It is indelibly imprinted on my memory in a manner which royal joy, fame, pleasure, and excitement beyond the dream of poets could never efface, not though I should be cursed with a life of fivescore years. I will paint it truthfully﻿—letter for letter as it was.

===Chinese===
那一幕烙在我记忆里的印迹，任凭王者般的欢乐、名望、声色之娱，任凭超乎诗人梦想的兴奋刺激，都再无法磨灭分毫，哪怕老天咒我活上一百岁也不能。我要把它如实描画出来——一字一母，照它本来的样子。

===Original===
It was twenty-six miles from Yarnung to Barney’s Gap, as M’Swat’s place was named. He had brought a light wagonette and pair to convey me thither.

===Chinese===
从雅农到巴尼山口——麦斯沃特家的地方就叫这名字——有二十六英里路。他赶了一辆双马的轻便四轮马车来，载我前去。

===Original===
As we drove along, I quite liked my master. Of course, we were of calibre too totally unlike ever to be congenial companions, but I appreciated his sound common sense in the little matters within his range, and his bluntly straightforward, fairly good-natured, manner. He was an utterly ignorant man, with small ideas according to the sphere which he fitted, and which fitted him; but he was “a man for a’ that, an’ a’ that.”

===Chinese===
一路行去，我倒相当喜欢我的东家。当然，我们两人的质地差得太远，永远成不了意气相投的伴儿；但凡他见识所及的小事上，他那扎实的常识，连同他那直统统、还算和气的做派，我都看得上。他是个十足无知的人，见识短浅，倒正配他所处的那个圈子，那圈子也正配他这样的人；可他到底是“一个响当当的汉子，管他这一套、那一套”。

===Original===
He and my father had been boys together. Years and years ago M’Swat’s father had been blacksmith on my father’s station, and the little boys had played together, and, in spite of their then difference in station, had formed a friendship which lived and bore fruit at this hour. I wished that their youthful relations had been inimical, not friendly.

===Chinese===
他和我父亲小时候是在一处的。许多许多年以前，麦斯沃特的父亲在我父亲的牧场上当铁匠，两个小男孩一块儿玩耍，尽管那时身份有别，却结下了一段交情，这段交情一直活到如今，恰在这个当口结出了果子。我倒宁愿他们少年时的交往是冤家，不是朋友。

===Original===
We left the pub in Yarnung at nine, and arrived at our destination somewhere about two o’clock in the afternoon.

===Chinese===
我们九点钟离开雅农的旅馆，下午两点来钟到了目的地。

===Original===
I had waxed quite cheerful, and began to look upon the situation in a sensible light. It was necessary that I should stand up to the guns of life at one time or another, and why not now? M’Swat’s might not be so bad after all. Even if they were dirty, they would surely be willing to improve if I exercised tact in introducing a few measures. I was not afraid of work, and would do many things. But all these ideas were knocked on the head, like a dairyman’s surplus calves, when on entering Barney’s Gap we descended a rough road to the house, which was built in a narrow gully between two steep stony hills, which, destitute of grass, rose like grim walls of rock, imparting a desolate and prison-like aspect.

===Chinese===
我原本已经提振起相当的精神，开始用明达的眼光看待自己的处境。人生的炮火，人早晚总得硬起头皮去顶一阵，如今顶便是了，何乐而不为？麦斯沃特家兴许到底没那么糟。就算他们脏，只要我使点手腕、推行几条章程，他们必定也肯改进。我不怕干活，也情愿做许多事。可是这些念头统统被敲掉了脑袋——像奶农打发多余牛犊那样——因为一进巴尼山口，我们便顺着一条崎岖的路下到宅子跟前。宅子盖在两座陡峭多石的山包之间一道窄窄的沟谷里；那两座山寸草不生，像两堵狰狞的石墙般拔地而起，透出一派荒凉、一座牢狱似的气象。

===Original===
Six dogs, two pet lambs, two or three pigs, about twenty fowls, eight children which seemed a dozen, and Mrs. M’Swat bundled out through the back door at our approach. Those children, not through poverty﻿—M’Swat made a boast of his substantial banking account﻿—but on account of ignorance and slatternliness, were the dirtiest urchins I have ever seen, and were so ragged that those parts of them which should have been covered were exposed to view. The majority of them had red hair and wide hanging-open mouths. Mrs. M’Swat was a great, fat, ignorant, pleasant-looking woman, shockingly dirty and untidy. Her tremendous, flabby, stockingless ankles bulged over her unlaced hobnailed boots; her dress was torn and unbuttoned at the throat, displaying one of the dirtiest necks I have seen. It did not seem to worry her that the infant she held under her arm like a roll of cloth howled killingly, while the other little ones clung to her skirts, attempting to hide their heads in its folds like so many emus. She greeted me with a smacking kiss, consigned the baby to the charge of the eldest child, a big girl of fourteen, and seizing upon my trunks as though they were featherweight, with heavy clodhopping step disappeared into the house with them. Returning, she invited me to enter, and following in her wake, I was followed by the children through the dirtiest passage into the dirtiest room, to sit upon the dirtiest chair, to gaze upon the other dirtiest furniture of which I have ever heard. One wild horrified glance at the dirt, squalor, and total benightedness that met me on every side, and I trembled in every limb with suppressed emotion and the frantic longing to get back to Caddagat which possessed me. One instant showed me that I could never, never live here.

===Chinese===
我们的车一到，六条狗、两只养熟的羊羔、两三口猪、二十来只鸡、八个瞧着倒像一打的娃娃，还有麦斯沃特太太，一窝蜂从后门拥了出来。这些孩子——倒不是因为穷，麦斯沃特还夸口过自己在银行里有笔殷实的存款——而是因为无知加邋遢，是我此生见过的最脏的小顽童，衣裳破烂到身上本该遮着的部位都露了出来。他们多半长着红头发，嘴巴老是大张着合不拢。麦斯沃特太太是个高大肥胖、无知无识、模样倒还和气的女人，脏得吓人，也邋遢得吓人。她那双肥厚松垮、光着不穿袜的脚踝，鼓在一双没系鞋带的钉齿靴外头；衣裳破了，领口敞着不扣，露出我此生见过最脏的一条脖子。她胳肢窝下像夹一匹布似的夹着个奶娃娃，娃娃哭得死去活来，她却似乎全不在意；其余的小家伙一个个揪住她的裙子，活像一群小鸸鹋，都想把脑袋藏进裙褶里去。她咂着嘴响亮地亲了我一下，算是见礼，把娃娃移交最大的孩子——一个十四岁的大姑娘——照管，随即拎起我的箱子，倒像拎的是几根鸡毛，迈开沉重笨大的脚步，拎着它们没进屋去。她回出来请我进屋；我跟在她身后，身后又缀着那群孩子，穿过我此生走过的最脏的过道，走进最脏的屋子，坐上最脏的椅子，见识我平生有所耳闻的其余那些最脏的家具。我只狂乱而惊骇地朝四下扫了一眼——满目污秽、穷酸和彻头彻尾的蒙昧——登时便因强抑的情绪和攫住我心的那股回卡达加特去的疯狂渴望，四肢百骸一齐发抖。只这一眼便叫我看明白：我永远、永远不可能在这儿住下去。

===Original===
“Have ye had yer dinner?” my future mistress inquired in a rough uncultivated voice. I replied in the negative.

===Chinese===
“你吃过晌午饭没有？”我这位未来的女主人用一条粗粝未加调教的嗓子问道。我回答没有。

===Original===
“Sure, ye’ll be dyin’ of hunger; but I’ll have it in a twinklin’.”

===Chinese===
“可不，你准要饿坏了；我眨眼工夫就给你弄来。”

===Original===
She threw a crumpled and disgustingly filthy cloth three-cornered ways on to the dusty table and clapped thereon a couple of dirty knives and forks, a pair of cracked plates, two poley cups and chipped saucers. Next came a plate of salt meat, red with saltpetre, and another of dark, dry, sodden bread. She then disappeared to the kitchen to make the tea, and during her absence two of the little boys commenced to fight. One clutched the tablecloth, and over went the whole display with a bang﻿—meat-dish broken, and meat on the dusty floor; while the cats and fowls, ever on the alert for such occurrences, made the most of their opportunities. Mrs. M’Swat returned carrying the tea, which was spilling by the way. She gave those boys each a clout on the head which dispersed them roaring like the proverbial town bull, and alarmed me for the safety of their eardrums. I wondered if their mother was aware of their having eardrums. She grabbed the meat, and wiping it on her greasy apron, carried it around in her hand until she found a plate for it, and by that time the children had collected the other things. A cup was broken, and another, also a poley, was put in its stead.

===Chinese===

===Original===
Mr. M’Swat now appeared, and after taking a nip out of a rum bottle which he produced from a cupboard in the corner, he invited me to sit up to dinner.

===Chinese===
麦斯沃特先生这时露了面，先从墙角一只食柜里摸出一瓶朗姆酒抿了一口，然后请我入席用饭。

===Original===
There was no milk. M’Swat went in entirely for sheep, keeping only a few cows for domestic purposes: these, on account of the drought, had been dry for some months. Mrs. M’Swat apologized for the lack of sugar, stating she was quite out of it and had forgotten to send for a fresh supply.

===Chinese===
没有牛奶。麦斯沃特一心全扑在羊身上，只养几头奶牛供家用：因为天旱，这几头牛已经好几个月是干的了。麦斯沃特太太为缺糖赔不是，说糖全用尽了，忘了打发人去添购。

===Original===
“You damned fool, to miss such a chance wen I was goin’ to town with the wagonette! I mightn’t be groin’ in again for munce [months]. But sugar don’t count much. Them as can’t do without a useless luxury like that for a spell will never make much of a show at gettin’ on in the wu-r-r-r-ld,” concluded Mr. M’Swat, sententiously.

===Chinese===
“你这个该死的蠢货，我赶着车进城那阵儿，你倒把这么个机会放过了！兴许好几个月里我进不着一趟城。不过糖顶不了什么。连这么件没用的奢侈品都缺不得一阵子的人，在这个世——界——上头，永远混不出个模样。”麦斯沃特先生煞有介事地作了结。

===Original===
The children sat in a row and, with mouths open and interest in their big wondering eyes, gazed at me unwinkingly till I felt I must rush away somewhere and shriek to relieve the feeling of overstrained hysteria which was overcoming me. I contained myself sufficiently, however, to ask if this was all the family.

===Chinese===
孩子们坐成一排，嘴巴张着，一双双瞪圆的眼睛里满是好奇，一眨也不眨地盯着我，盯得我觉着自己非得跑到什么地方去尖叫一声不可，好舒散那正把我压倒的、绷得要发狂的情绪。不过我还是把自己按捺住了，只问这一家子人是否全在此地。

===Original===
“All but Peter. Where’s Peter, Mary Ann?”

===Chinese===
“就差彼得。彼得哪儿去了，玛丽·安（Mary Ann）？”

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Chinese===

===Chinese===
“可不；彼得二十一了，蓄着小胡子，还刮脸呢。”大姑娘说，那副口气，只等着我惊得说不出话来。

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Chinese===
麦斯沃特太太惠然见告：彼得和莱泽（Lizer）中间夭折过三个，所以这位不在家的儿子才比弟弟妹妹们大了这么多。

===Chinese===

===Original===

===Chinese===
“是啊。”她答道，肥胖和气地笑着，倒好像这是句玩笑话。

===Chinese===

===Chinese===

===Chinese===
“午饭”过后，我讨了一瓶墨水、几张纸，潦潦草草给外婆和母亲各写了几行，只报告我平安抵达了目的地。我打定主意，先缓一缓、理清思绪，再去恳求把我从巴尼山口开释出去。

===Original===

I looked at pretty, soft-eyed, dirty little Rose Jane, and assured her kindhearted mother I would not be the least lonely, as the sickening despairing loneliness which filled my heart was not of a nature to be cured by having as a bedmate a frowzy wild child.

===Chinese===
