## XXXV The 3rd of December 1898 / 第35章 1898 年 12 月 3 日

===Original===
It was a very hot day. So extreme was the heat that to save the lives of some young swallows my father had to put wet bags over the iron roof above their nest. A galvanized-iron awning connected our kitchen and house: in this some swallows had built, placing their nest so near the iron that the young ones were baking with the heat until rescued by the wet bagging. I had a heavy day’s work before me, and, from my exertions of the day before, was tired at the beginning. Bushfires had been raging in the vicinity during the week, and yesterday had come so close that I had been called out to carry buckets of water all the afternoon in the blazing sun. The fire had been allayed, after making a gap in one of our boundary fences. Father and the boys had been forced to leave the harvesting of the miserable pinched wheat while they went to mend it, as the small allowance of grass the drought gave us was precious, and had to be carefully preserved from neighbours’ stock.

===Chinese===
那天真热得出奇。热到这般地步，为救几只雏燕的性命，父亲只好往燕巢上方的铁皮屋顶上加盖湿麻袋。有一道镀锌铁皮搭的凉棚，把我们的厨房和正屋连在一起：几只燕子就在棚里筑了巢，巢贴着铁皮贴得那么近，雏鸟们眼看要烤个半熟，全靠湿麻袋搭救。这天我面前压着一大堆繁重的活计，加上头一天的劳顿，一开头人就是乏的。这一礼拜，左近的丛林野火烧得正凶，昨天更是逼到近旁，害得我被叫出去，顶着毒日头提了一整个下午的水桶。火总算扑灭了，却在我家一道边界围栏上烧出一个豁口。父亲和男孩们只得撂下那可怜巴巴、遭旱歉瘦的麦子，先去修补围栏——旱年赏给我们的那一点点草料金贵得很，须得仔仔细细护住，别叫邻居家的牲口糟蹋了。

===Original===
I had baked and cooked, scrubbed floors and whitewashed hearths, scoured tinware and cutlery, cleaned windows, swept yards, and discharged numerous miscellaneous jobs, and half-past two in the afternoon found me very dirty and very tired, and with very much more yet to do.

===Chinese===
我烤过饼、做过饭，刷洗过地板、粉刷过炉台，擦过锡器和刀叉餐具，抹过窗户，扫过院子，办完一大堆五花八门的杂务；到下午两点半，我已是又脏又乏，而手头还有许多许多没干。

===Original===
One of my half-starved poddy calves was very ill, and I went out to doctor it previous to bathing and tidying myself for my finishing household duties.

===Chinese===
我那头饿得半死的手喂牛犊病得不轻，我先出去给它瞧病，再洗漱拾掇自己，回头好干家里剩下的活。

===Original===
My mother was busy upon piles and piles of wearying mending, which was one of the most hopeless of the many slaveries of her life. This was hard work, and my father was slaving away in the sun, and mine was arduous labour, and it was a very hot day, and a drought-smitten and a long day, and poddy calves ever have a tendency to make me moralize and snarl. This was life, my life and my parents’ life, and the life of those around us, and if I was a good girl and honoured my parents I would be rewarded with a long stretch of it. Yah!

===Chinese===
母亲正忙着对付一堆又一堆磨人的缝补——那是她一生诸多苦役里最没盼头的一桩。这活计辛苦，父亲在毒日头底下卖命，我的活计也是苦工，天又这么热，又是旱魃肆虐、漫长难熬的一天；而手喂牛犊这类东西，天生就有一股本事，引得我又发感慨又咬牙。这就是生活，我的生活、我父母的生活、我们四周围人的共同生活；只要我做个乖女孩、孝敬父母，往后自会得着长长的一大段这样的生活，算是犒赏。呸！

===Original===
These pagan meditations were interrupted by a footfall slowly approaching. I did not turn to ascertain who it might be, but trusted it was no one of importance, as the poddy and I presented rather a grotesque appearance. It was one of the most miserable and sickly of its miserable kind, and I was in the working uniform of the Australian peasantry. My tattered skirt and my odd and bursted boots, laced with twine, were spattered with whitewash, for coolness my soiled cotton blouse hung loose, an exceedingly dilapidated sunbonnet surmounted my head, and a bottle of castor-oil was in my hand.

===Chinese===
这些异教徒式的冥想，被一阵渐渐走近的脚步声打断了。我没有回头去看是谁，只指望来的不是什么要紧人，因为那牛犊同我凑在一处，模样实在有几分滑稽。它是它那可怜一族里最可怜、最病歪歪的一头，我则穿着澳大利亚庄稼人的工作制服：我那条破裙子和那双不成对儿、胀裂了口的靴子——鞋带拿麻绳充数——溅满了白灰浆；为了凉快，我那件脏了的棉布罩衫敞着怀；头上扣一顶破旧不堪的遮阳帽，手里还握着一瓶蓖麻油。

===Original===
I supposed it was one of the neighbours or a tea-agent, and I would send them to mother.

===Chinese===
我猜是哪位邻居，再不就是个上门兜售茶叶的，叫他们找母亲去就是。

===Original===
The footsteps had come to a halt beside me.

===Chinese===
脚步声在我身旁停了下来。

===Original===
“Could you tell me if﻿—”

===Chinese===
“劳驾，请问您是不是——”

===Original===
I glanced upwards. Horrors! There stood Harold Beecham, as tall and broad as of yore, even more sunburnt than ever, and looking very stylish in a suit of grey and a soft fashionable dinted-in hat; and it was the first time I had ever seen him in a white shirt and high collar.

===Chinese===
我抬眼一望。天哪！站在那儿的正是哈罗德·比彻姆，还是从前那样又高又壮，晒得比以往更黑，一身灰色套服，头戴一顶时兴的软呢帽，帽顶塌出凹痕，瞧着非常入时；而且这是我有生以来头一回见他穿白衬衫、竖硬领。

===Original===
I wished he would explode, or I might sink into the ground, or the calf would disappear, or that something might happen.

===Chinese===
我但愿他当场炸个粉碎，或者我自己一头扎进地里去，或者那牛犊凭空消失，或者随便出点什么岔子才好。

===Original===

===Chinese===
他认出是我，愈发一言不发，可一双眼里明明白白盛满了怜悯，把我刺了个透心彻骨。

===Original===

===Chinese===
自怜的本事我倒是有，可我的自尊心向来一口回绝旁人施舍的哪怕一丝怜悯。

===Original===
I could feel my heart grow as bitterly cold as my demeanour was icily stiff, when I stood up and said curtly:

===Chinese===
我站起身，简慢地说道——只觉得心同我那副冷冰冰的僵硬做派一样，寒得透骨：

===Original===
“This is a great surprise, Mr. Beecham.”

===Chinese===
“这真是天大的意外，比彻姆先生。”

===Original===
“Not an unpleasant one, I hope,” he said pleasantly.

===Chinese===
“但愿不是个不愉快的意外。”他和颜悦色地说。

===Original===
“We will not discuss the matter. Come inside out of the heat.”

===Chinese===
“这件事咱们不谈。快进屋吧，外头热。”

===Original===
“I’m in no hurry, Syb, and couldn’t I help you with that poor little devil?”

===Chinese===
“我着什么急，西比。让我搭把手，帮你照看照看这可怜的小鬼头，不行吗？”

===Original===
“I’m only trying to give it another chance of life.”

===Chinese===
“我不过是想再给它一条活路。”

===Original===
“What will you do with it if it lives?”

===Chinese===
“它要是活下来，你打算拿它怎么办？”

===Original===
“Sell it for half a crown when it’s a yearling.”

===Chinese===
“等它长成周岁牛犊，卖它半克朗（half a crown）。”

===Original===
“It would pay better to shoot the poor little beggar now.”

===Chinese===
“依我看，趁早崩了这可怜的小东西，倒还上算些。”

===Original===
“No doubt it would the owner of Five-Bob, but we have to be more careful,” I said tartly.

===Chinese===
“对五博丘的东家来说自然上算，可我们不得不多加小心。”我尖刻地说。

===Original===
“I didn’t mean to offend you.”

===Chinese===
“我不是存心冒犯你。”

===Original===

===Chinese===
“我才没有恼。”我答道，一边领路往屋里走，心里却针扎似的痛，想着哈罗德·比彻姆这会儿准在纳闷：两年前自己怎么会有那么一时糊涂，竟看上了这么一件货色。

===Original===

She was a lady, and looked it, in spite of the piles of coarse mending, and the pair of trousers, almost bulletproof with patches, out of which she drew her hand, roughened and reddened with hard labour, in spite of her patched and faded cotton gown, and the commonest and most poverty-stricken of peasant surroundings, which failed to hide that she had not been always thus.

===Chinese===

===Original===
Leaving them together, I expeditiously proceeded to relieve the livery-stable horse, on which Harold had come, of the valise, saddle, and bridle with which it was encumbered, and then let it loose in one of the grassless paddocks near at hand.

===Chinese===
把他们俩留在客厅，我手脚麻利地跑去卸哈罗德骑来的那匹租马行的马，把它驮的提包、马鞍和笼头一一卸下，随后撒开放进近旁一块寸草不生的围场里。

===Original===
Then I threw myself on a stool in the kitchen, and felt, to the bone, the sting of having ideas above one’s position.

===Chinese===
然后我一屁股跌坐在厨房的凳子上，彻头彻尾、入骨地尝到了那份“心气儿高过命”的刺痛。

===Original===
In a few minutes mother came hurrying out.

===Chinese===
不多会儿，母亲急匆匆出来了。

===Original===
“Good gracious, what’s the matter? I suppose you didn’t like being caught in such a pickle, but don’t get in the dumps about it. I’ll get him some tea while you clean yourself, and then you’ll be able to help me by and by.”

===Chinese===
“老天爷，这是怎么了？我猜你是嫌让人撞见那副狼狈相，可别为这个蔫头耷脑的。趁你拾掇自己，我去给他备茶，待会儿你也好帮我搭把手。”

===Original===
I found my little sister Aurora, and we climbed through the window into my bedroom to get tidy. I put a pair of white socks and shoes and a clean pinafore on the little girl, and combed her golden curls. She was all mine﻿—slept with me, obeyed me, championed me; while I﻿—well, I worshipped her.

===Chinese===
我寻到小妹妹奥罗拉，娘儿俩从窗口爬进我的卧室去梳洗打扮。我给小姑娘穿上白袜子和鞋，罩上一件干净的围涎衫，又给她梳好一头金色鬈发。她整个儿是我的——同我睡，听我的话，处处护着我；而我呢——嗐，我把她当神明似的拜着。

===Original===
There was a hole in the wall, and through it I could see without being seen.

===Chinese===
墙上有一个洞，隔着洞我看得见人，人却看不见我。

===Original===
Mother was dispensing afternoon tea and talking to Harold. It was pleasant to see that manly figure once again. My spirits rose considerably. After all, if the place was poor, it was very clean, as I had scrubbed it all that morning, and when I came to consider the matter, I remembered that men weren’t such terrible creatures, and never made one feel the sting of one’s poverty half as much as women do.

===Chinese===
母亲正张罗午后茶，陪着哈罗德说话。又见到那副男子汉的身影，真叫人愉快。我的心绪高涨了不少。毕竟，这地方虽说寒素，却非常干净——一早上全是我刷洗出来的；细想起来，我又记起男人原不是那么可怕的生灵，他们叫人尝到的贫穷刺痛，从来抵不上女人给的一半。

===Original===

===Chinese===
“奥罗拉，”我说，我要你出去，告诉比彻姆先生一句话。

===Chinese===
