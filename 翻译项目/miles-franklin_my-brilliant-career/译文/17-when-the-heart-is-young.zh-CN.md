## XV When the Heart Is Young / 第15章 心怀年少时

===Original===
About a week or so after I first met Harold Beecham, aunt Helen allowed me to read a letter she had received from the elder of the two Misses Beecham. It ran as follows:

===Chinese===
我初见哈罗德·比彻姆（Harold Beecham）后约莫一个礼拜，海伦姨妈（aunt Helen）便允许我看一封她收到的信，写信人是比彻姆家两位姑奶奶中年长的那位（the elder of the two Misses Beecham）。信上写道：

===Original===
> “This is a begging letter, and I am writing another to your mother at the same time. I am asking her to allow her granddaughter to spend a few weeks with me, and I want you to use your influence in the matter. Sarah has not been well lately, and is going to Melbourne for a change, and as I will be lonely while she is away Harold insists upon me having someone to keep me company﻿—you know how considerate the dear boy is. I hardly like to ask you to spare your little girl to me. It must be a great comfort to have her. I could have got Miss Benson to stay with me, but Harold will not hear of her. He says she is too slow, and would give us both the mopes. But he says your little niece will keep us all alive. Julius was telling me the other day that he could not part with her, as she makes ‘the old barracks,’ as he always calls Caddagat, echo with fun and noise. I am so looking forward to seeing her, as she is dear Lucy’s child. Give her my love,” etc., etc., and as a postscript the letter had﻿—“Harold will go up for Sybylla on Wednesday afternoon. I do hope you will be able to spare her to me for a while.”

===Chinese===
> “这是一封讨人情的信，同一时候我还给你母亲另写着一张。我求她允许她的外孙女来我这里住上几个礼拜，也想请你在这件事上帮着递句话。莎拉（Sarah）近来身子不大爽利，要去墨尔本换换空气；她不在家的日子我要孤单的，哈罗德便执意要我找个人作伴——你晓得那个好孩子多么体贴人心。要我开口求你把你的小姑娘让给我，实在难以启齿；有她在跟前，想必是极大的安慰。我本可以请本森小姐（Miss Benson）来陪我，可哈罗德连听都不要听。他说她太慢吞吞，会叫我们俩都闷出病来。他又说，你那位小外甥女来了，管保叫我们全都活泛起来。前几天朱利叶斯（Julius）还对我说，他舍不得放她走呢，因为她闹得‘老营房’——他向来这样称呼卡达加特（Caddagat）——回声里全是欢笑与喧闹。我是这么盼着见她，因为她是亲爱的露西（Lucy）的孩子。请代我疼她。”云云；信末又缀着一段附言——“星期三下午哈罗德会去接西比拉。我实在盼望你能把她让给我一阵子。”

===Original===
“Oh, auntie, how lovely!” I exclaimed. “What are you laughing at?”

===Chinese===
“哎呀，姨妈，这多好哇！”我叫道。“你笑什么呢？”

===Original===
“For whom do you think Harry wants the companion? It is nice to have an old auntie, as a blind, is it not? Well, all is fair in love and war. You have permission to use me in any way you like.”

===Chinese===
“你道哈里（Harry）要人作伴，是为了谁？拿一位老姨妈当幌子，岂不妙哉？唉，情场如战场，什么手段都不为过。我准你随意把我派上用场。”

===Original===
I pretended to miss her meaning.

===Chinese===
我装作没领会她的意思。

===Original===
Grannie consented to Miss Beecham’s proposal, and ere the day arrived I had a trunk packed with some lovely new dresses, and was looking forward with great glee to my visit to Five-Bob Downs.

===Chinese===
外婆应允了比彻姆小姐的提议；日子还没到，我已把一只大衣箱装好了几件漂亮的新衣裳，满心欢喜地盼望着去五博丘（Five-Bob Downs）做客这一遭。

===Original===
One o’clock on Wednesday afternoon arrived; two o’clock struck, and I was beginning to fear no one was coming for me, when, turning to look out the window for the eighteenth time, I saw the straight blunt nose of Harold Beecham passing. Grannie was serving afternoon tea on the veranda. I did not want any, so got ready while my escort was having his.

===Chinese===
星期三下午一点钟到了；两点钟又敲响，我开始担心不会有人来接我了，不想第十八回转身朝窗外张望时，正瞧见哈罗德·比彻姆那只挺直的钝鼻子一晃而过。外婆正在游廊上张罗午后茶点。我一点儿茶也不想用，便趁着我那位护送人用茶的当口收拾停当。

===Original===
It was rather late when we bowled away at a tremendous pace in a red sulky, my portmanteau strapped on at the back, and a thoroughbred American trotter, which had taken prizes at Sydney shows, harnessed to the front. We just whizzed! It was splendid! The stones and dust rose in a thick cloud from the whirling wheels and flying hoofs, and the posts of the wire fence on our left passed like magic as we went. Mr. Beecham allowed me to drive after a time while he sat ready to take the reins should an emergency arise.

===Chinese===
等到我们坐上一辆红色双轮轻便马车（sulky），以雷霆般的速度疾驰而去时，时候已经不早；我的旅行皮箱拴在车尾，车前套着一匹在悉尼赛会上得过奖的纯种美国快步马。我们简直是在飞！痛快极了！石子与尘土从飞旋的车轮和翻腾的四蹄下扬起浓浓一团；左手边铁丝围栏的界桩，在我们经过时像变戏法似的直往后掠。过了一阵，比彻姆先生让我执缰驾车，他自己坐在旁边，一有意外便随时准备接回缰绳。

===Original===
It was sunset﻿—most majestic hour of the twenty-four﻿—when we drove up to the great white gates which opened into the avenue leading to the main homestead of Five-Bob Downs station﻿—beautiful far-reaching Five-Bob Downs! Dreamy blue hills rose behind, and wide rich flats stretched before, through which the Yarrangung river, glazed with sunset, could be seen like a silver snake winding between shrubberied banks. The odour from the six-acred flower-garden was overpowering and delightful. A breeze gently swayed the crowd of trees amid the houses, and swept over the great orchard which sloped down from the south side of the houses. In the fading sunlight thirty iron roofs gleamed and glared, and seemed like a little town; and the yelp of many dogs went up at the sound of our wheels. Ah! beautiful, beautiful Five-Bob Downs!

===Chinese===
我们驶到那两扇高大的白大门前，正是日落——一天二十四小时中最庄严的时辰。大门敞向林荫道，直通往五博丘牧场的正屋——美丽而绵延辽阔的五博丘！身后耸起梦一般的蓝色丘陵，眼前铺展着宽绰肥沃的平川，亚兰贡河（Yarrangung）罩着一层落日的釉光，像一条银蛇蜿蜒在灌木丛生的两岸之间。六英亩花圃里涌出的香气浓烈逼人，又叫人心醉。微风轻轻摇着屋舍之间簇聚的树木，又拂过从屋子南面斜铺下去的大果园。三十座铁皮屋顶在渐渐暗下去的斜阳里灼灼闪光，恍如一座小镇；我们的车轮声一响，一片犬吠便迎面而起。啊！美丽的、美丽的五博丘！

===Original===
It seemed as though a hundred dogs leapt forth to greet us when that gate flew open, but I subsequently discovered there were but twenty-three.

===Chinese===
大门倏地敞开时，仿佛有一百条狗蹿出来迎我们；后来我才查明，统共不过二十三条。

===Original===
Two female figures came out to meet us﻿—one nearly six feet high, the other, a tiny creature, seemed about eighteen inches, though, of course, was more than that.

===Chinese===
两个女人的身影迎了出来——一个身高将近六英尺；另一个是个小不点儿，瞧上去约莫十八英寸高，当然喽，实际还不止。

===Original===
“I’ve brought her, aunt Gussie,” said Harold, jumping out of the sulky, though not relinquishing the reins, while he kissed the taller figure, and the small one attached itself to his leg saying, “Dimme wide.”

===Chinese===
“我把她给带来啦，格西姑妈（aunt Gussie）。”哈罗德说着跳下马车，手里的缰绳却不撒开，一边吻了吻那个高个儿的身影；那个小不点儿则一把攀住他的腿，说：“要坐车车。”

===Original===
“Hullo! Possum, why wasn’t old Spanker let go? I see he’s not among the dogs,” and my host picked the tiny individual up in his arms and got into the sulky to give her the desired ride, while after being embraced by Miss Beecham and lifted to the ground by her nephew, I went with the former over an asphalted tennis-court, through the wide garden, then across a broad veranda into the great, spreading, one-storeyed house from which gleamed many lights.

===Chinese===
“喂！小负鼠（Possum），怎么没把老斯潘克（old Spanker）放出来？我瞧它不在狗群里。”我的主人说着，把那个小小的人儿一把抱进怀里，坐进马车，让她称心如意地兜了一遭；这边比彻姆小姐拥抱过我，她外甥又把我抱下车来，我便跟着她走过一片铺了沥青的网球场，穿过宽绰的花园，再越过一道宽敞的游廊，走进那座铺展开去、只有一层高的宅子，宅子里已透出点点灯光。

===Original===
“I am so glad you have come, my dear. I must have a good look at you when we get into the light. I hope you are like your mother.”

===Chinese===
“你来了我真高兴，亲爱的。等到了亮处，我定要好好瞧瞧你。我盼着你生得像你母亲。”

===Original===
This prospect discomfited me. I knew she would find a very ugly girl with not the least resemblance to her pretty mother, and I cursed my appearance under my breath.

===Chinese===
这前景叫我很不自在。我知道她将看到的是一个丑得够呛的姑娘，同她那位漂亮母亲没有一丝一毫的相像，我便在心里暗暗咒骂自己的这副长相。

===Original===
“Your name is Sybylla,” Miss Beecham continued, “Sybylla Penelope. Your mother used to be very dear to me, but I don’t know why she doesn’t write to me now. I have never seen her since her marriage. It seems strange to think of her as the mother of eight﻿—five boys and three girls, is it not?”

===Chinese===
“你的名字叫西比拉，”比彻姆小姐接着说，“西比拉·佩内洛普（Sybylla Penelope）。你母亲从前同我要好得很，可我不明白她如今怎么不给我写信了。打她出嫁那年起，我就没再见过她。想想她竟已是八个孩子的母亲——五个男孩、三个女孩——岂不叫人觉得奇异？”

===Original===
Miss Beecham had piloted me through a wide hall and along an extended passage out of which a row of bedrooms opened, into one of which we went.

===Chinese===
比彻姆小姐领我穿过一道宽敞的门厅，又沿一条长走廊前行，走廊一侧开着一排卧室的房门，我们走进了其中一间。

===Original===
“I hope you will be comfortable here, child. You need not dress for dinner while you are here; we never do, only on very special occasions.”

===Chinese===
“愿你在这里住得舒坦，孩子。你在这儿的日子，晚饭不必更衣盛服；我们从不讲究这个，除非逢着顶特别的场合。”

===Original===
“Neither do we at Caddagat,” I replied.

===Chinese===
“我们在卡达加特也不讲究。”我答道。

===Original===
“Now, child, let me have a good look at you without your hat.”

===Chinese===
“来吧，孩子，摘了帽子，让我好好端详端详你。”

===Original===
“Oh, please don’t!” I exclaimed, covering my face with my hands. “I am so dreadfully ugly that I cannot bear to have anyone look at me.”

===Chinese===
“哎呀，求你别看！”我叫起来，双手捂住脸。“我丑得吓死人，实在受不住叫人瞧我。”

===Original===
“What a silly little girl! You are not like your mother, but you are not at all plain-looking. Harold says you are the best style of girl he has seen yet, and sing beautifully. He got a tuner up from Sydney last week, so we will expect you to entertain us every night.”

===Chinese===
“好一个傻丫头！你是生得不像你母亲，可你一点儿也不难看。哈罗德说，你是他至今见过的姑娘里顶上等的一个，还说你歌唱得美妙极了。上礼拜他专门从悉尼请上来一位调音师，所以我们可指望着你夜夜给我们弹唱取乐呢。”

===Original===
I learnt that what Harold pronounced good no one dared gainsay at Five-Bob Downs.

===Chinese===
我这才明白，在五博丘，凡哈罗德品定为好的，没有人敢道半个不字。

===Original===
We proceeded direct to the dining-room, and had not been there long when Mr. Beecham entered with the little girl on his shoulder. Miss Beecham had told me she was Minnie Benson, daughter of Harold’s married overseer on Wyambeet, his adjoining station. Miss Beecham considered it would have been more seemly for her nephew to have selected a little boy as a plaything, but his sentiments regarding boys were that they were machines invented for the torment of adults.

===Chinese===
我们径直进了饭厅，落座没多久，比彻姆先生便肩上扛着那小姑娘走了进来。比彻姆小姐已经告诉过我，她是明妮·本森（Minnie Benson），哈罗德那座毗邻牧场怀安比特（Wyambeet）上他那位成了家的监工的女儿。比彻姆小姐认为，她外甥若挑个小男孩当玩意儿，倒还更得体些；怎奈他对男孩一族的见解是：那本是专为折磨大人而发明出来的机器。

===Original===
“Well, O’Doolan, what sort of a day has it been?” Harold inquired, setting his human toy upon the floor.

===Chinese===
“喂，奥杜兰（O’Doolan），今儿个这一天怎么样啊？”哈罗德问道，一面把他这件活人玩具安放到地板上。

===Original===
“Fine wezzer for yim duts,” she promptly replied.

===Chinese===
“好天七，鸭鸭玩水水。”她脆生生地应声答道。

===Original===
“Harold, it is shameful to teach a little innocent child such abominable slang; and you might give her a decent nickname,” said Miss Beecham.

===Chinese===
“哈罗德，教这么个天真无邪的小娃娃说这样下流的俚语，真不害臊；你也满可以给她起个体面点儿的绰号。”比彻姆小姐说。

===Original===
“O’Doolan, this is Miss Melvyn, and you have to do the same to her as you do to me.”

===Chinese===
“奥杜兰，这位是梅尔文小姐，你待她要像待我一样。”

===Original===
The little thing held out her arms to me. I took her up, and she hugged and kissed me, saying:

===Chinese===
小家伙朝我伸出两条小胳膊。我把她抱起来，她搂着我又亲了亲，说道：

===Original===
“I luz oo, I luz oo,” and turning to Mr. Beecham, “zat anuff?”

===Chinese===
“我爱油，我爱油。”说着又转向比彻姆先生，“够了吗呀？”

===Original===
“Yes, that will do,” he said; and she struggled to be put down.

===Chinese===
“嗯，够了。”他说；她便挣着要下地来。

===Original===
Three jackeroos, an overseer, and two other young men came in, were introduced to me, and then we began dinner.

===Chinese===
三个牧场学徒（jackeroo）、一名监工，另有两位青年男子走了进来，同我一一引见过，我们便开始用晚饭。

===Original===
O’Doolan sat on a high chair beside Mr. Beecham, and he attended to all her wants. She did everything he did, even taking mustard, and was very brave at quelling the tears that rose to the doll-like blue eyes. When Mr. Beecham wiped his moustache, it was amusing to see her also wipe an imaginary one.

===Chinese===
奥杜兰坐在比彻姆先生手边一张高高的椅子上，他把她种种需求照料得妥妥帖帖。他做什么，她也跟着做什么，连芥末也照尝了一点，一面很勇敢地把涌上那双洋娃娃似的蓝眼睛的泪花强压下去。比彻姆先生抹胡子的时候，看她跟着也抹一抹自己那并不存在的胡子，真叫人忍俊不禁。

===Original===
After dinner the jackeroos and the three other men repaired to a sitting-room in the backyard, which was specially set apart for them, and where they amused themselves as they liked. My host and hostess, myself, and the child, spent the evening in a tiny sitting-room adjoining the dining-room. Miss Beecham entertained me with conversation and the family albums, and Harold amused himself entirely with the child.

===Chinese===
晚饭后，牧场学徒们和那另外三个人转移到后院一间专为他们辟出的起居室里，随意取乐去了。我的男女主人、我，还有那孩子，则在紧邻饭厅的一间小小的起居室里消磨这个夜晚。比彻姆小姐拿谈天和家庭相簿来给我解闷，哈罗德则整个儿的心思都在逗那孩子上头。

===Original===
Once when they were absent for a few minutes, Miss Beecham told me it was ridiculous the way he fussed with the child, and that he had her with him more than half his time. She also asked me what I thought of her nephew. I evaded the question by querying if he was always so quiet and good-tempered.

===Chinese===
有一回他们俩走开了几分钟，比彻姆小姐便告诉我，他这么围着一个孩子团团转，实在可笑，他还有一大半的工夫都把她带在身边。她又问我觉得她这位外甥如何。我避而不答，只反问他是不是一向都这么沉静和气。

===Original===
“Oh dear, no. He is considered a particularly bad-tempered man. Not one of the snarling nasty tempers, but﻿—”

===Chinese===
“哎呀，才不呢。人家都说他是个脾气格外坏的人。倒不是那种龇牙咬人、恶声恶气的坏，只是——”

===Original===
Here the reentry of the owner of the temper put a stop to this conversation.

===Chinese===
恰在此时，这位脾气的主人重新进场，这场谈话便打住了。

===Original===
Harold gave O’Doolan rides on his back, going on all-fours. She shouted in childish glee, and wound up by curling her small proportions on his broad chest, and going to sleep there.

===Chinese===
哈罗德趴下四肢当马，驮着奥杜兰满地爬。她乐得尖声直叫，末了把她那一小团身子蜷伏在他宽阔的胸膛上，就在那儿睡着了。

===Original===
Mrs. Benson had sent for little O’Doolan, and Harold took her home next day. He invited me to accompany him, so we set out in the sulky with O’Doolan on my lap. It was a pleasant drive of twelve miles to and from Wyambeet. O’Doolan was much distressed at parting from Mr. Beecham, but he promised to come for her again shortly.

===Chinese===
本森太太（Mrs. Benson）派人来接小奥杜兰，第二天哈罗德便送她回家。他邀我同行，于是我们坐上那辆双轮轻便马车出发，奥杜兰坐在我的膝头上。去怀安比特来回十二英里，一路驱车甚是惬意。同比彻姆先生分别的时候，奥杜兰难过得不得了，他答应过不久再去接她。

===Original===
“One little girl at a time is enough for me to care for properly,” he said to me in the winning manner with which, and his wealth, unintentionally and unconsciously made slaughter among the hearts of the fair sex.

===Chinese===
“一次照看一个小姑娘，尽够我照看得周周全全了。”他对我说这话时那副招人欢喜的风度——正是这风度，连同他的家财，在既非有意又无所知觉之间，于女儿家们的芳心里大开杀戒。
