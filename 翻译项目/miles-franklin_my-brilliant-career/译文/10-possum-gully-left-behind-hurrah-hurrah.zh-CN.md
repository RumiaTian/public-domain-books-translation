## VIII Possum Gully Left Behind. Hurrah! Hurrah! / 第8章 告别袋熊谷，好哇！好哇！

===Original===
If a Sydney man has friends residing at Goulburn, he says they are up the country. If a Goulburn man has friends at Yass, he says they are up the country. If a Yass man has friends at Young, he says they are up the country, and so on. Caddagat is “up the country.”
===Chinese===
悉尼人若有朋友住在古尔本，他便说人家住在“内地”。古尔本人若有朋友住在雅斯（Yass），他便说人家住在“内地”。雅斯人若有朋友住在扬格（Young），他也还是说人家住在“内地”，如此类推。卡达加特便在“内地”。

===Original===
Bound thither on the second Wednesday in August 1896, I bought a ticket at the Goulburn railway station, and at some time about 1 a.m. took my seat in a second class carriage of the mail-train on its way to Melbourne. I had three or four hours to travel in this train when I would have to change to a branch line for two hours longer. I was the only one from Goulburn in that carriage; all the other passengers had been in some time and were asleep. One or two opened their eyes strugglingly, stared glumly at the intruder, and then went to sleep again. The motion of the train was a joy to me, and sleep never entered my head. I stood up, and pressing my forehead to the cold windowpane, vainly attempted, through the inky blackness of the foggy night, to discern the objects which flew by.
===Chinese===
1896 年 8 月的第二个星期三，我踏上了去那儿的路，在古尔本火车站买了票，凌晨一点来钟，在开往墨尔本的邮政列车一节二等车厢里落了座。我得在这列车上坐三四个钟头，然后换乘一条支线，再走两个钟头。这节车厢里，从古尔本上车的只有我一个；其余乘客都已上来好一阵，正睡着。有一两个勉力睁开眼，闷闷地瞪一眼这不速之客，随即又睡了过去。车轮的颠动于我是桩乐事，睡意压根儿没进过我的脑子。我站起身，把额头抵在冰凉的车窗玻璃上，想透过雾夜墨黑的一片，辨认那一掠而过的景物，只是徒然。

===Original===
I was too full of pleasant anticipation of what was ahead of me to think of those I had left behind. I did not regret leaving Possum Gully. Quite the reverse; I felt inclined to wave my arms and yell for joy at being freed from it. Home! God forbid that my experiences at Possum Gully should form the only food for my reminiscences of home. I had practically grown up there, but my heart refused absolutely to regard it as home. I hated it then, I hate it now, with its narrowing, stagnant monotony. It has and had not provided me with one solitary fond remembrance﻿—only with dreary, wing-clipping, mind-starving recollections. No, no; I was not leaving home behind, I was flying homeward now. Home, home to Caddagat, home to ferny gullies, to the sweet sad rush of many mountain waters, to the majesty of rugged Borgongs; home to dear old grannie, and uncle and aunt, to books, to music; refinement, company, pleasure, and the dear old homestead I love so well.
===Chinese===
我心里满满盛着对前路的甜美期待，哪里还顾得上身后撇下的人。离开袋熊谷，我毫无遗憾。非但如此，我直想挥舞双臂、放声欢呼，庆贺自己从中挣脱出来。家！但愿上帝保佑，别叫我在袋熊谷的经历构成我关于“家”的回忆的唯一食粮。我差不多就是在那儿长大的，可我的心断然不肯把它认作自己的家。当年我恨它，如今我照旧恨它——恨它那叫人日益狭隘、死水一潭的单调。它不曾、也从来不曾给过我一星半点温存的忆念——给的只有凄凉的、剪翅膀的、饿煞心智的回忆。不，不；我抛在身后的不是家，我此刻是正飞奔回家。回家，回卡达加特的家，回那蕨草萋萋的沟谷，回那群山众水甜中带愁的奔泻，回那嶙峋博尔贡（Borgongs）的雄浑气象；回到亲爱的老外婆、舅舅和姨妈身边，回到书籍，回到音乐；回到风雅、伴侣、欢乐，回到我深爱的那座老宅。

===Original===
All in good time I arrived at the end of my train journey, and was taken in charge by a big red-bearded man, who informed me he was the driver of the mail-coach, and had received a letter from Mrs. Bossier instructing him to take care of me. He informed me also that he was glad to do what he termed “that same,” and I would be as safe under his care as I would be in God’s pocket.
===Chinese===
一路顺遂，我到了火车旅程的尽头，一位红胡子大个儿汉子接管了照应我的差事。他自称是邮政马车的车夫，收到博西耶太太的信，托他路上照应我。他还说，他很高兴做他所谓的“那码事”，说在他照应之下，我保管安然无恙，同揣在上帝口袋里一样稳当。

===Original===
My twenty-six miles’ coach drive was neither pleasant nor eventful. I was the only passenger, and so had my choice of seats. The weather being cold and wet, I preferred being inside the box and curled myself up on the seat, to be interrupted every two or three miles by the good-natured driver inquiring if I was “all serene.”
===Chinese===
那二十六英里的马车路，既不舒服，也无波澜。乘客只有我一人，座位尽由我挑。天气又冷又潮，我宁可待在密闭的车厢里，蜷作一团坐在座位上，只是每隔两三英里，好性子的车夫就要来打一回岔，问我“一路安生么”。

===Original===
At the Halfway House, where a change of the team of five horses was affected, I had a meal and a warm, and so tuned myself up for the remainder of the way. It got colder as we went on, and at 2:30 p.m. I was not at all sorry to see the iron roofs of Gool-Gool township disclosing to my view. We first went to the post office, where the mailbags were delivered, and then returned and pulled rein in front of the Woolpack Hotel. A tall young gentleman in a mackintosh and cap, who had been standing on the veranda, stepped out on the street as the coach stopped, and lifting his cap and thrusting his head into the coach, inquired, “Which is Miss Melvyn?”
===Chinese===
在半途客栈（Halfway House）换过一套五匹马的班，我吃了顿饭，暖了暖身子，把精神重新抖擞起来，好走完剩下的路。越往前越冷，到下午2:30，古尔古尔（Gool-Gool）镇的一片铁皮屋顶展现在我眼前，我一点也不觉得遗憾。我们先去了邮局，把邮袋交付了，然后折回来，在羊毛包旅馆（Woolpack Hotel）门前勒住缰绳。一位穿胶布雨衣、戴便帽的高个子年轻绅士本站在游廊上，马车一停便踱到街心，举了举帽子，把头探进车厢，问道：“哪位是梅尔文小姐？”

===Original===
Seeing I was the only occupant, he laughed the pleasantest of laughs, disclosing two wide rows of perfect teeth, and turning to the driver, said, “Is that your only passenger? I suppose it is Miss Melvyn?”
===Chinese===
见我是车里唯一的乘客，他发出了顶愉快的一声笑，露出两排齐整的好牙，转头对车夫说：“这就是您唯一的乘客？我猜这位就是梅尔文小姐吧？”

===Original===
“As I wasn’t present at her birth, I can’t swear, but I believe her to be that same, as sure as eggs is eggs,” he replied.
===Chinese===
“她落草那会儿我又没在场，赌不得咒，不过我信她就是那一位，鸡蛋是鸡蛋——千真万确。”他答道。

===Original===
My identity being thus established, the young gentleman with the greatest of courtesy assisted me to alight, ordered the hotel groom to stow my luggage in the Caddagat buggy, and harness the horses with all expedition. He then conducted me to the private parlour, where a friendly little barmaid had some refreshments on a tray awaiting me, and while warming my feet preparatory to eating I read the letter he had given me, which was addressed in my grandmother’s handwriting. In it she told me that she and my aunt were only just recovering from bad colds, and on account of the inclemency of the weather thought it unwise to come to town to meet me; but Frank Hawden, the jackeroo, would take every care of me, settle the hotel bill, and tip the coach-driver. Caddagat was twenty-four miles distant from Gool-Gool, and the latter part of the road was very hilly. It was already past three o’clock, and, being rainy, the short winter afternoon would close in earlier; so I swallowed my tea and cake with all expedition, so as not to delay Mr. Hawden, who was waiting to assist me into the buggy, where the groom was in charge of the horses in the yard. He struck up a conversation with me immediately.
===Chinese===
我的身份既已这样坐实，这位年轻绅士便以极其周到的礼数扶我下车，吩咐旅馆马夫把我的行李装上卡达加特的小马车，火速套马。随后他把我引进专用客厅，一位和气的小酒吧女侍正端着托盘，备了些茶点候着我。趁进食前烘脚的当口，我读了那封他交给我、出自外婆手笔的信。信上说，她和我姨妈重感冒才刚好，天气又这般酷劣，觉得不宜进城来接我；不过牧场学徒弗兰克·霍登（Frank Hawden）会悉心照应我，付清旅馆账目，再赏车夫几个酒钱。卡达加特离古尔古尔二十四英里，后一段路丘陵起伏得厉害。这时已过三点钟，又落着雨，冬日短短的午后天黑得更早；我便火急把茶和饼吞下肚，免得耽搁霍登先生——他正等着扶我上马车，马夫已在院子里看住马匹。他立刻同我攀谈起来。

===Chinese===

===Chinese===

===Chinese===

===Original===
I hadn’t time to say more than promise to send his remembrances to my father, for Mr. Hawden, saying we would be in the dark, had whipped his horses and was bowling off at a great pace, in less than two minutes covering a rise which put Gool-Gool out of sight. It was raining a little, so I held over us the big umbrella, which grannie had sent, while we discussed the weather, to the effect that rain was badly needed and was a great novelty nowadays, and it was to be hoped it would continue. There had been but little, but the soil here away was of that rich loamy description which little water turns to mud. It clogged the wheels and loaded the break-blocks; and the near side horse had a nasty way of throwing his front feet, so that he deposited soft red lumps of mud in our laps at every step. But, despite these trifling drawbacks, it was delightful to be drawn without effort by a pair of fat horses in splendid harness. It was a great contrast to our poor skinny old horse at home, crawling along in much-broken harness, clumsily and much mended with string and bits of hide.
