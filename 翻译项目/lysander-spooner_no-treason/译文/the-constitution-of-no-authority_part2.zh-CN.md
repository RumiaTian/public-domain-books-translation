===Original===
But this tacit understanding (admitting it to exist) cannot at all justify the conclusion drawn from it. A tacit understanding between A, B, and C, that they will, by ballot, depute D as their agent, to deprive me of my property, liberty, or life, cannot at all authorize D to do so. He is none the less a robber, tyrant, and murderer, because he claims to act as their agent, than he would be if he avowedly acted on his own responsibility alone.
===Chinese===
但这种默契（即便承认它存在）根本不能为由此得出的结论提供正当性。甲、乙、丙之间达成默契，约定以投票方式委托丁充当他们的代理人，来剥夺我的财产、自由或生命，这丝毫不能授权丁这样做。丁并不会因为自称作为他们的代理人行事，就比他公然宣称单凭自身责任行事时，更少一分强盗、暴君和杀人犯的属性。
===Original===
Neither am I bound to recognize him as their agent, nor can he legitimately claim to be their agent, when he brings no *written* authority from them accrediting him as such. I am under no obligation to take his word as to who his principals may be, or whether he has any. Bringing no credentials, I have a right to say he has no such authority even as he claims to have: and that he is therefore intending to rob, enslave, or murder me on his own account.
===Chinese===
我也没有义务承认他是他们的代理人；当他拿不出他们出具的*书面*授权来证明其这一身份时，他也无权正当地自称是他们的代理人。至于他的委托人是谁、他究竟有没有委托人，我没有任何义务听信他的一面之词。他既拿不出任何凭证，我就有权说：他连自己自称拥有的那种授权也并不存在；因此，他图谋抢劫、奴役或谋杀我，完全是为他自己打算。
===Original===
This tacit understanding, therefore, among the voters of the country, amounts to nothing as an authority to their agents. Neither do the ballots by which they select their agents, avail any more than does their tacit understanding; for their ballots are given in secret, and therefore in a way to avoid any personal responsibility for the acts of their agents.
===Chinese===
因此，这个国家选民之间的这种默契，作为对其代理人的授权，一文不值。他们挑选代理人所投的选票，也并不比这种默契更有分量；因为他们的选票是在秘密状态下投出的，也就是说，是以规避对其代理人行为的任何个人责任的方式投出的。
===Original===
No body of men can be said to authorize a man to act as their agent, to the injury of a third person, unless they do it in so open and authentic a manner as to make themselves personally responsible for his acts. None of the voters in this country appoint their political agents in any open authentic manner, or in any manner to make themselves responsible for their acts. Therefore these pretended agents cannot legitimately claim to be really agents. Somebody must be responsible for the acts of these pretended agents; and if they cannot show any open and authentic credentials from their principals, they cannot, in law or reason, be said to have any principals. The maxim applies here, that what does not appear, does not exist. If they can show no principals, they have none.
===Chinese===
任何一群人，除非以足够公开、真实可靠的方式进行授权，使自己为其行为承担个人责任，就谈不上授权某人为其代理人去损害第三人。这个国家的选民，没有一个以任何公开、真实可靠的方式任命其政治代理人，也没有以任何使自己为其行为承担责任的方式任命。因此，这些冒牌代理人无权正当地声称自己是真正的代理人。这些冒牌代理人的行为，总得有人来负责；而如果他们拿不出来自委托人的任何公开、真实可靠的凭证，那么无论在法律上还是理性上，都不能说他们有什么委托人。这里适用这样一条格言：凡未显现者，即不存在。拿不出委托人，他们就没有委托人。
===Original===
But even these pretended agents do not themselves know who their pretended principals are. These latter act in secret; for acting by secret ballot is acting in secret as much as if they were to meet in secret conclave in the darkness of the night. And they are personally as much unknown to the agents they select, as they are to others. No pretended agent therefore can ever know by whose ballots he is selected, or consequently who his real principals are. Not knowing who his principals are, he has no right to say that he has any. He can, at most, say only that he is the agent of a secret band of robbers and murderers, who are bound by that faith which prevails among confederates in crime, to stand by him, if his acts, done in their name, shall be resisted.
===Chinese===
然而，就连这些冒牌代理人自己，也不知道他们那些冒牌委托人是谁。后者行事秘而不宣；因为以秘密投票（secret ballot）方式行事，就如同在夜色笼罩下于密室中聚会行事一样，完全是暗中行事。对于他们所选定的代理人，这些委托人像对其他人一样，个人身份全然不为人知。因此，任何冒牌代理人都不可能知道自己是被谁的选票选出来的，从而也不可能知道他真正的委托人是谁。既然不知道自己的委托人是谁，他就无权宣称自己有委托人。他至多只能说，自己是秘密的强盗和杀人犯团伙的代理人；这个团伙靠着犯罪同伙之间通行的那种信义彼此约束：倘若他以他们的名义所作的行为遭到抵抗，他们必与他站在一起。
===Original===
Men honestly engaged in attempting to establish justice in the world, have no occasion thus to act in secret; or to appoint agents to do acts for which they (the principals) are not willing to be responsible.
===Chinese===
诚心致力于在世界上建立正义的人，没有必要这样暗中行事，也没有必要去任命代理人，去干他们（委托人）自己不愿承担责任的行为。
===Original===
The secret ballot makes a secret government; and a secret government is a secret band of robbers and murderers. Open despotism is better than this. The single despot stands out in the face of all men, and says: “I am the State: My will is law: I am your master: I take the responsibility of my acts: The only arbiter I acknowledge is the sword: If anyone denies my right, let him try conclusions with me.”
===Chinese===
秘密投票造就秘密的政府；而秘密的政府就是秘密的强盗和杀人犯团伙。公开的专制也比这要好。独夫暴君立于众人面前，说：“朕即国家：我的意志就是法律：我是你们的主人：我为自己的行为承担责任：我承认的唯一仲裁者是刀剑：谁若否认我的权力，就让他与我决一雌雄。”
===Original===
But a secret government is little less than a government of assassins. Under it, a man knows not who his tyrants are, until they have struck, and perhaps not then. He may *guess*, beforehand, as to some of his immediate neighbors. But he really *knows* nothing. The man to whom he would most naturally fly for protection, may prove an enemy, when the time of trial comes.
===Chinese===
而秘密的政府几乎就是一个刺客的政府。在这种政府之下，一个人不知道自己的暴君是谁，除非暴君已经出手——甚至到那时也未必知道。事先，对于他身边最切近的某些邻人，他或许可以*猜测*。但他其实一无所*知*。到了考验来临时，他最自然会逃去寻求保护的那个人，也许竟是个敌人。
===Original===
This is the kind of government we have; and it is the only one we are likely to have, until men are ready to say: “We will consent to no Constitution, except such an one as we are neither ashamed nor afraid to sign; and we will authorize no government to do anything in our name which we are not willing to be personally responsible for.”
===Chinese===
我们拥有的就是这种政府；而且，在人们准备说出这样的话之前，我们也只可能拥有这种政府：“我们绝不同意任何宪法，除非它是我们既不耻于、也不惧于签署的宪法；凡是我们不愿为之承担个人责任的事情，我们也绝不授权任何政府以我们的名义去做。”
### IX / 九

===Original===
What is the motive to the secret ballot? This, and only this: Like other confederates in crime, those who use it are not friends, but enemies; and they are afraid to be known, and to have their individual doings known, even to each other. They can contrive to bring about a sufficient understanding to enable them to act in concert against other persons; but beyond this they have no confidence, and no friendship, among themselves. In fact, they are engaged quite as much in schemes for plundering each other, as in plundering those who are not of them. And it is perfectly well understood among them that the strongest party among them will, in certain contingencies, murder each other by the hundreds of thousands (as they lately did do) to accomplish their purposes against each other. Hence they dare not be known, and have their individual doings known, even to each other. And this is avowedly the only reason for the ballot: for a secret government; a government by secret bands of robbers and murderers. And we are insane enough to call this liberty! To be a member of this secret band of robbers and murderers is esteemed a privilege and an honor! Without this privilege, a man is considered a slave; but with it a free man! With it he is considered a free man, because he has the same power to secretly (by secret ballot) procure the robbery, enslavement, and murder of another man, that that other man has to procure his robbery, enslavement, and murder. And this they call equal rights!
===Chinese===
秘密投票的动机是什么？就是这个，而且仅仅是这个：如同其他犯罪同伙一样，使用秘密投票的人彼此不是朋友，而是敌人；他们害怕暴露自己，甚至害怕彼此知道各自的行为。他们能够设法达成足够的谅解，以便协同一致对付其他人；但除此之外，他们彼此之间毫无信任，也毫无情谊。事实上，他们算计掠夺彼此，同算计掠夺他们之外的人，几乎是一样的起劲。而且他们彼此心照不宣：在某种形势下，他们当中最强大的派系会以数十万计地互相残杀（如他们最近所为），以实现其加于彼此的目的。因此他们不敢暴露自己，甚至不敢让彼此知道各自的行为。而这正是公开承认的采用投票的唯一理由：即为了一个秘密政府——一个由秘密的强盗和杀人犯团伙当家作主的政府。而我们竟疯狂到把这称作自由！成为这个秘密的强盗和杀人犯团伙的一员，竟被视为一种特权和荣耀！没有这种特权，一个人被视为奴隶；有了它，便是自由人！有了它他就被视为自由人，因为他拥有同别人一样的权力，可以秘密地（通过秘密投票）促成对另一个人的抢劫、奴役和谋杀，正如那个人也有同样的权力促成对他的抢劫、奴役和谋杀。而他们把这叫做权利平等！
===Original===
If any number of men, many or few, claim the right to govern the people of this country, let them make and sign an open compact with each other to do so. Let them thus make themselves individually known to those whom they propose to govern. And let them thus openly take the legitimate responsibility of their acts. How many of those who now support the Constitution, will ever do this? How many will ever dare openly claim their right to govern? or take the legitimate responsibility of their acts? Not one!
===Chinese===
如果有任何数目的人，无论多寡，宣称有权治理这个国家的人民，那就让他们为此相互缔结并签署一项公开的契约（compact）。让他们以此向他们打算治理的人们公开自己的个人身份。让他们以此公开承担其行为的正当责任。如今支持宪法的人当中，有几个人会这样做？有几个人敢于公开宣称自己的治理权？或者承担其行为的正当责任？一个也没有！

### X / 十

===Original===
It is obvious that, on general principles of law and reason, there exists no such thing as a government created by, or resting upon, any consent, compact, or agreement of “the people of the United States” with each other; that the only visible, tangible, responsible government that exists, is that of a few individuals only, who act in concert, and call themselves by the several names of senators, representatives, presidents, judges, marshals, treasurers, collectors, generals, colonels, captains, etc., etc.
===Chinese===
显而易见，按照法律与理性的一般原则，根本不存在什么由“美利坚合众国人民”彼此之间的同意、契约或协议所创立或所依托的政府；现存的唯一看得见、摸得着、负得起责任的政府，仅仅是一小撮协同行事的人的政府，他们以参议员、众议员、总统、法官、执法官、司库、税务官、将军、上校、上尉等等名号自称。
===Original===
On general principles of law and reason, it is of no importance whatever that these few individuals *profess* to be the agents and representatives of “the people of the United States”; since they can show no credentials from the people themselves; they were never appointed as agents or representatives in any open authentic manner; they do not themselves know, and have no means of knowing, and cannot prove, who their principals (as they call them) are individually; and consequently cannot, in law or reason, be said to have any principals at all.
===Chinese===
按照法律与理性的一般原则，这几个少数人*自诩*为“美利坚合众国人民”的代理人和代表，这一点毫无分量；因为他们拿不出人民自己出具的任何凭证；他们从未以任何公开、真实可靠的方式受任为代理人或代表；他们自己不知道、也无从知道、更无法证明，他们所谓的委托人（他们如此称呼）个人究竟是谁；因此，无论在法律上还是理性上，都根本不能说他们有什么委托人。
===Original===
It is obvious, too, that if these alleged principals ever did appoint these pretended agents, or representatives, they appointed them secretly (by secret ballot), and in a way to avoid all personal responsibility for their acts; that, at most, these alleged principals put these pretended agents forward for the most criminal purposes, viz.: to plunder the people of their property, and restrain them of their liberty; and that the only authority that these alleged principals have for so doing, is simply a *tacit understanding* among themselves that they will imprison, shoot, or hang every man who resists the exactions and restraints which their agents or representatives may impose upon them.
===Chinese===
同样显而易见的是：即使这些所谓的委托人确实任命过这些冒牌代理人或代表，他们也是以秘密方式（通过秘密投票）任命的，并以一种规避其行为的一切个人责任的方式任命的；这些所谓的委托人把冒牌代理人推到前台，至多也是出于最罪恶的目的，即：掠夺人民的财产，剥夺人民的自由；而他们这样做的唯一“授权”，不过是他们彼此之间的一个*默契*：凡抵抗其代理人或代表可能施加于他们的勒索与限制者，一律监禁、枪杀或绞死。
===Original===
Thus it is obvious that the only visible, tangible government we have is made up of these professed agents or representatives of a secret band of robbers and murderers, who, to cover up, or gloss over, their robberies and murders, have taken to themselves the title of “the people of the United States;” and who, on the pretence of being “the people of the United States,” assert their right to subject to their dominion, and to control and dispose of at their pleasure, all property and persons found in the United States.
===Chinese===
由此可见，我们现存的这个唯一看得见、摸得着的政府，就是由秘密的强盗和杀人犯团伙的这些自封代理人或代表组成的；这个团伙为掩饰或粉饰自己的抢劫与谋杀，僭取了“美利坚合众国人民”的名号；并且以“美利坚合众国人民”自居为幌子，宣称自己有权使美利坚合众国境内的一切财产和人屈从其统治，任其随意控制和处置。
### II / 二

===Original===
On general principles of law and reason, the oaths which these pretended agents of the people take “to support the Constitution,” are of no validity or obligation. And why? For this, if for no other reason, viz. *that they are given to nobody*. There is no privity, (as the lawyers say)—that is, no mutual recognition, consent and agreement—between those who take these oaths, and any other persons.
===Chinese===
按照法律与理性的一般原则，人民的这些冒牌代理人所作的“支持宪法”的誓言，不具有任何效力或约束力。为什么？纵使没有别的理由，也至少有这一条：*这些誓言没有对任何人作出*。立誓者与任何其他人之间不存在相对关系（privity，如法学家所言）——也就是说，不存在相互的承认、同意与合意。
===Original===
If I go upon Boston Common, and in the presence of a hundred thousand people, men, women and children, with whom I have no contract on the subject, take an oath that I will enforce upon them the laws of Moses, of Lycurgus, of Solon, of Justinian, or of Alfred, that oath is, on general principles of law and reason, of no obligation. It is of no obligation, not merely because it is intrinsically a criminal one, *but also because it is given to nobody*, and consequently pledges my faith to nobody. It is merely given to the winds.
===Chinese===
倘若我走上波士顿公共草地，在十万男女老幼面前——我与他们之间就此并无任何契约——宣誓说我将把摩西、来库古、梭伦、查士丁尼或阿尔弗雷德的法律强加于他们，那么按照法律与理性的一般原则，这个誓言不具有任何约束力。它之所以没有约束力，不仅因为它本质上是一个犯罪的誓言，*还因为它没有对任何人作出*，因而我的信义未曾向任何人担保。它只不过是对着风作出的。
===Original===
It would not alter the case at all to say that, among these hundred thousand persons, in whose presence the oath was taken, there were two, three, or five thousand male adults, who had *secretly*—by secret ballot, and in a way to avoid making themselves *individually* known to me, or to the remainder of the hundred thousand—designated me as their agent to rule, control, plunder, and, if need be, murder, these hundred thousand people. The fact that they had designated me *secretly*, and in a manner to prevent my knowing them *individually*, prevents all *privity* between them and me; and consequently makes it impossible that there can be any contract, or pledge of faith, on my part towards them; for it is impossible that I can pledge my faith, in any legal sense, to a man whom I neither know, nor have any means of knowing, *individually*.
===Chinese===
即便说，在这立誓时在场的十万人当中，有两千、三千或五千名成年男子，曾以*秘密*方式——通过秘密投票，并采取使他们既不让*我*个别地认识他们、也不让其余九万五千人个别地认识他们的方式——指定我为他们的代理人，去统治、控制、掠夺这十万民众，并在必要时杀害他们，这也丝毫改变不了问题的性质。他们以*秘密*方式指定我，并采取使我无法个别地认识他们的方式，这一事实使他们与我之间不可能有任何*相对关系*；因而也就不可能有我方对他们承担的任何契约或信义担保；因为，对一个我既不认识、也无从认识其*个人*身份的人，我根本不可能在法律意义上向他担保我的信义。
===Original===
So far as I am concerned, then, these two, three, or five thousand persons are a secret band of robbers and murderers, who have secretly, and in a way to save themselves from all responsibility for my acts, designated me as their agent; and have, through some other agent, or pretended agent, made their wishes known to me. But being, nevertheless, individually unknown to me, and having no open, authentic contract with me, my oath is, on general principles of law and reason, of no validity as a pledge of faith *to them*. And being no pledge of faith *to them*, it is no pledge of faith to anybody. It is mere idle wind. At most, it is only a pledge of faith to an unknown band of robbers and murderers, whose instrument for plundering and murdering other people, I thus publicly confess myself to be. And it has no other obligation than a similar oath given to any other unknown body of pirates, robbers, and murderers.
===Chinese===
因此，就我而言，这两千、三千或五千人就是一个秘密的强盗和杀人犯团伙：他们以秘密方式、并以使他们自己免于对我的行为承担一切责任的方式，指定我为他们的代理人；并且经由某个别的代理人或冒牌代理人，把他们的意愿传达给了我。然而，他们始终不为我个别地认识，与我之间也没有公开、真实可靠的契约，所以按照法律与理性的一般原则，我的誓言作为对*他们*的信义担保是无效的。既然对*他们*不构成信义担保，它就对任何人都不构成信义担保。它纯粹是一阵空风。至多，它只是一个不为人知的强盗和杀人犯团伙的信义担保——而我以此公开自认是这个团伙掠夺、杀害他人的工具。它的约束力，同对任何其他不为人知的海盗、强盗和杀人犯团伙立下的类似誓言相比，并无二致。
===Original===
For these reasons the oath taken by members of Congress “to support the Constitution,” are, on general principles of law and reason, of no validity. They are not only criminal in themselves, and therefore void; but they are also void for the further reason *that they are given to nobody*.
===Chinese===
基于这些理由，国会议员所作的“支持宪法”的誓言，按照法律与理性的一般原则，是无效的。这些誓言不仅本身就属犯罪、因而无效，而且还另有无效的缘由：*它们没有对任何人作出*。
===Original===
It cannot be said that, in any legitimate or legal sense, they are given to “the people of the United States;” because neither the whole, nor any large proportion of the whole, people of the United States ever, either openly or secretly, appointed or designated these men as their agents to carry the Constitution into effect. The great body of the people—that is, men, women and children—were never asked, or even permitted, to signify, in any *formal* manner, either openly or secretly, their choice or wish on the subject. The most that these members of Congress can say, in favor of their appointment, is simply this: Each one can say for himself:
===Chinese===
不能说这些誓言在任何正当或法律的意义上是向“美利坚合众国人民”作出的；因为美利坚合众国人民，无论是全体还是其中的大部分，从来不曾以公开或秘密的方式，任命或指定这些人为其实施宪法的代理人。人民中的绝大多数——也就是所有的男人、女人和儿童——从来不曾就此被征询，甚至不被允许以任何*正式*方式，公开地或秘密地表明他们在这个问题上的选择或意愿。这些国会议员能为自己的任命举出的理由，至多不过是下面这一套：每人可以为自己作这样的申明：
===Original===
“I have evidence satisfactory to myself, that there exists, scattered throughout the country, a band of men, having a tacit understanding with each other, and calling themselves ‘the people of the United States,’ whose general purposes are to control and plunder each other, and all other persons in the country, and, so far as they can, even in neighboring countries; and to kill every man who shall attempt to defend his person and property against their schemes of plunder and dominion. Who these men are, *individually*, I have no certain means of knowing, for they sign no papers, and give no open, authentic evidence of their *individual* membership. They are not known individually even to each other. They are apparently as much afraid of being individually known to each other, as of being known to other persons. But nothing of this kind can be proved in regard to the corporation, or body of men, who call themselves “the United States.” Not a man of them, in all the Northern States, can prove by any legitimate evidence, such as is required to prove membership in other legal corporations, that he himself, or any other man whom he can name, is a member of any corporation or association called “the United States,” or “the people of the United States,” or, consequently, that there is any such corporation. And since no such corporation can be proved to exist, it cannot of course be proved that the oaths of Southern men were given to any such corporation. The most that can be claimed is that the oaths were given to a secret band of robbers and murderers, who called themselves “the United States,” and extorted those oaths. But that certainly is not enough to prove that the oaths are of any obligation.
===Chinese===
按照法律与理性的一般原则，凡具有正当法人地位、正当法人权利的法人、社团或有组织的人群，都必须由某些已知的个人组成，*这些人能够以正当而合理的证据证明其成员身份*。然而，对于那些自称“美利坚合众国”的法人或人群，此类事情没有一样能被证明。北方各州之中，没有一个人能以任何正当的证据——如证明其他合法法人成员身份所要求的那种证据——证明他本人、或他能点出姓名的任何其他人，是任何名为“美利坚合众国”或“美利坚合众国人民”的法人或社团的成员，从而也证明不了存在任何这样的法人。既然这样的法人无法被证明存在，当然也就无法证明南方人的誓言是向任何这样的法人作出的。至多只能这样主张：这些誓言是向一个自称“美利坚合众国”、并勒索了这些誓言的秘密的强盗和杀人犯团伙作出的。但这肯定不足以证明这些誓言具有任何约束力。

### XV / 十五

===Original===
On general principles of law and reason, the oaths of soldiers, that they will serve a given number of years, that they will obey the orders of their superior officers, that they will bear true allegiance to the government, and so forth, are of no obligation. Independently of the criminality of an oath, that, for a given number of years, he will kill all whom he may be commanded to kill, without exercising his own judgment or conscience as to the justice or necessity of such killing, there is this further reason why a soldier’s oath is of no obligation, viz. that, like all the other oaths that have now been mentioned, *it is given to nobody*. There being, in no legitimate sense, any such corporation, or nation, as “the United States,” nor, consequently, in any legitimate sense, any such government as “the government of the United States,” a soldier’s oath given to, or contract made with, such nation or government, is necessarily an oath given to, or a contract made with, nobody. Consequently such oath or contract can be of no obligation.
===Chinese===
按照法律与理性的一般原则，士兵们的誓言——什么服役若干年、服从长官命令、真诚效忠政府云云——不具有任何约束力。姑且不论这样一种誓言的罪性——凭它，士兵将在若干年内杀掉一切奉命杀戮之人，而对这种杀戮是否正义、是否必要，不行使他自己的判断和良知——士兵的誓言之所以没有约束力，还有一层理由，即：像上文提到的所有其他誓言一样，*它没有对任何人作出*。既然无论在哪一种正当意义上都不存在“美利坚合众国”这样的法人或国家，从而也不存在任何正当意义上的“美利坚合众国政府”这样的政府，那么士兵向这样的国家或政府立下的誓言、或与它缔结的契约，就必然是没有对任何人立下的誓言、没有与任何人缔结的契约。因此，这样的誓言或契约不可能具有任何约束力。

### XVI / 十六

===Original===
On general principles of law and reason, the treaties, so called, which purport to be entered into with other nations, by certain persons calling themselves ambassadors, secretaries, presidents, and senators of the United States, in the name, and on behalf, of “the people of the United States,” are of no validity. These so-called ambassadors, secretaries, presidents, and senators, who claim to be the agents of “the people of the United States,” for making these treaties, can show no open, written, or other authentic evidence that either the whole “people of the United States,” or any other open, avowed, responsible body of men, calling themselves by that name, ever authorized these pretended ambassadors and others to make treaties in the name of, or binding upon any one of, “the people of the United States.” Neither can they show any open, written, or other authentic evidence that either the whole “people of the United States,” or any other open, avowed, responsible body of men, calling themselves by that name, ever authorized these pretended ambassadors, secretaries, and others, in their name and behalf, to recognize certain other persons, calling themselves emperors, kings, queens, and the like, as the rightful rulers, sovereigns, masters, or representatives of the different peoples whom they assume to govern, to represent, and to bind.
===Chinese===
按照法律与理性的一般原则，那些所谓的条约——由某些自称美利坚合众国大使、国务卿、总统和参议员的人，以“美利坚合众国人民”的名义并代表他们，号称与其他国家缔结——是无效的。这些号称是“美利坚合众国人民”缔约代理人的所谓大使、国务卿、总统和参议员，拿不出任何公开、书面或其他真实可靠的证据，证明全体“美利坚合众国人民”、或任何其他以这个名字自称的公开的、公开申明的、负得起责任的人群，曾经授权这些冒牌大使及其他人，以“美利坚合众国人民”的名义缔结条约，或缔结对“美利坚合众国人民”中任何一人有约束力的条约。他们同样拿不出任何公开、书面或其他真实可靠的证据，证明全体“美利坚合众国人民”、或任何其他以这个名字自称的公开的、公开申明的、负得起责任的人群，曾经授权这些冒牌大使、国务卿及其他人，以他们的名义并代表他们，去承认另外某些自称皇帝、国王、女王等等的人，是这些人所僭称要统治、代表和束缚的各族人民的正当统治者、君主、主人或代表。
===Original===
The “nations,” as they are called, with whom our pretended ambassadors, secretaries, presidents and senators profess to make treaties, are as much myths as our own. On general principles of law and reason, there are no such “nations.” That is to say, neither the whole people of England, for example, nor any open, avowed, responsible body of men, calling themselves by that name, ever, by any open, written, or other authentic contract with each other, formed themselves into any bona fide, legitimate association or organization, or authorized any king, queen, or other representative to make treaties in their name, or to bind them, either individually, or as an association, by such treaties.
===Chinese===
我们那些冒牌大使、国务卿、总统和参议员号称与之缔约的所谓“国家”，同我们自己的“国家”一样是虚构。按照法律与理性的一般原则，不存在这样的“国家”。也就是说，比如说，全体英格兰人民、或任何以这个名字自称的公开的、公开申明的、负得起责任的人群，从来不曾通过彼此之间任何公开、书面或其他真实可靠的契约，把自己组成任何真诚的（bona fide）、正当的社团或组织，也从来不曾授权任何国王、女王或其他代表，以他们的名义缔结条约，或以这类条约束缚他们——无论是束缚其个人，还是束缚其作为社团。
===Original===
Our pretended treaties, then, being made with no legitimate or bona fide nations, or representatives of nations, and being made, on our part, by persons who have no legitimate authority to act for us, have intrinsically no more validity than a pretended treaty made by the Man in the Moon with the king of the Pleiades.
===Chinese===
因此，我们那些冒牌条约，既然既非与正当的、真诚的国家或国家的代表所缔结，就我们这一方而言，又是由根本无正当权限代表我们行事的人所缔结，那么其内在的有效性，同月中人与昴星团国王订立的一纸假条约相比，并不多出一分。
### XVII / 十七

===Original===
On general principles of law and reason, debts contracted in the name of “the United States,” or of “the people of the United States,” are of no validity. It is utterly absurd to pretend that debts to the amount of twenty-five hundred millions of dollars are binding upon thirty-five or forty millions of people, when there is not a particle of legitimate evidence—such as would be required to prove a private debt—that can be produced against any one of them, that either he, or his properly authorized attorney, ever contracted to pay one cent.
===Chinese===
按照法律与理性的一般原则，以“美利坚合众国”或“美利坚合众国人民”的名义订立的债务，是无效的。说什么数额高达二十五亿美元的债务约束着三千五百万或四千万人民，而在能对其中任何一个人出示的证据中，找不到一丝一毫正当的证据——像证明私人债务所要求的那种证据——证明他本人或其正式授权的代理人曾订约偿付一分钱，这种说法荒谬绝伦。
===Original===
Certainly, neither the whole people of the United States, nor any number of them, ever separately or individually contracted to pay a cent of these debts.
===Chinese===
可以肯定，美利坚合众国人民，无论是全体还是其中任何一部分人，从来不曾共同地或个别地订约偿还这些债务的一分钱。
===Original===
Certainly, also, neither the whole people of the United States, nor any number of them, ever, by any open, written, or other authentic and voluntary contract, united themselves as a firm, corporation, or association, by the name of “the United States,” or “the people of the United States,” and authorized their agents to contract debts in their name.
===Chinese===
同样可以肯定，美利坚合众国人民，无论是全体还是其中任何一部分人，从来不曾通过任何公开、书面或其他真实可靠的自愿契约，以“美利坚合众国”或“美利坚合众国人民”之名联合为一个商号、法人或社团，并授权其代理人以他们的名义订立债务。
===Original===
Certainly, too, there is in existence no such firm, corporation, or association as “the United States,” or “the people of the United States,” formed by any open, written, or other authentic and voluntary contract, and having corporate property with which to pay these debts.
===Chinese===
同样可以肯定，世上也并不存在这样一个商号、法人或社团——名为“美利坚合众国”或“美利坚合众国人民”，由任何公开、书面或其他真实可靠的自愿契约组成，并且拥有可用以偿还这些债务的法人财产。
===Original===
How, then, is it possible, on any general principle of law or reason, that debts that are binding upon nobody individually, can be binding upon forty millions of people collectively, when, on general and legitimate principles of law and reason, these forty millions of people neither have, nor ever had, any corporate property? never made any corporate or individual contract? and neither have, nor ever had, any corporate existence?
===Chinese===
那么，按照法律或理性的任何一般原则，怎么可能有一些对任何个人都没有约束力的债务，却对四千万人集体地有约束力——须知，按照正当的法律与理性的一般原则，这四千万人现在没有、也从来不曾有过任何法人财产；从未订立过任何法人契约或个人契约；现在没有、也从来不曾有过任何法人存在？
===Original===
Who, then, created these debts, in the name of “the United States?” Why, at most, only a few persons, calling themselves “members of Congress,” etc. who pretended to represent “the people of the United States,” but who really represented only a secret band of robbers and murderers, who wanted money to carry on the robberies and murders in which they were then engaged; and who intended to extort from the future people of the United States, by robbery and threats of murder (and real murder, if that should prove necessary), the means to pay these debts.
===Chinese===
那么，这些以“美利坚合众国”名义设立的债务，究竟是谁搞出来的呢？咳，至多不过是几个自称“国会议员”等等的人物；他们号称代表“美利坚合众国人民”，实际上只代表一个秘密的强盗和杀人犯团伙；这伙人要钱去进行他们当时正在从事的抢劫和谋杀，并打算靠抢劫和以谋杀相威胁（如果证明有必要，就真刀真枪地杀），从未来的美利坚合众国人民身上勒索偿还这些债务的手段。
===Original===
This band of robbers and murderers, who were the real principals in contracting these debts, is a secret one, because its members have never entered into any open, written, avowed, or authentic contract, by which they may be individually known to the world, or even to each other. Their real or pretended representatives, who contracted these debts in their name, were selected (if selected at all) for that purpose secretly (by secret ballot), and in a way to furnish evidence against none of the principals *individually*; and these principals were really known *individually* neither to their pretended representatives who contracted these debts in their behalf, nor to those who lent the money. The money, therefore, was all borrowed and lent in the dark; that is, by men who did not see each other’s faces, or know each other’s names; who could not then, and cannot now, identify each other as principals in the transactions; and who consequently can prove no contract with each other.
===Chinese===
这个作为订约真正委托人的强盗和杀人犯团伙是个秘密团伙，因为它的成员从未缔结过任何公开的、书面的、公开申明的或真实可靠的契约，使他们得以向世人、甚至向彼此显明个人身份。以他们的名义订立这些债务的、他们真实的或冒牌的代表，是为这一目的（如果毕竟被选过的话）以秘密方式（通过秘密投票）选出的，其选法使人无法据此对任何一位委托人*个人*提出证据；而这些委托人的*个人*身份，无论对替他们订立这些债务的冒牌代表，还是对出借钱款的人，实际上都无从知晓。因此，这笔钱全是在黑暗中借入和贷出的；也就是说，借贷双方是一些彼此没见过面、不知道对方姓名的人；他们当时不能、如今也不能在交易中确认彼此的委托人身份；因此他们无法证明彼此之间存在任何契约。
===Original===
Furthermore, the money was all lent and borrowed for criminal purposes; that is, for purposes of robbery and murder; and for this reason the contracts were all intrinsically void; and would have been so, even though the real parties, borrowers and lenders, had come face to face, and made their contracts openly, in their own proper names.
===Chinese===
况且，这笔钱的贷与借全是为犯罪目的——也就是为抢劫和谋杀的目的；因此这些契约全都在本质上无效；即使真实的当事人——借方和贷方——曾经面对面、以自己的真实名义公开订立契约，这些契约也照样无效。
===Original===
Furthermore, this secret band of robbers and murderers, who were the real borrowers of this money, having no legitimate corporate existence, have no corporate property with which to pay these debts. They do indeed pretend to own large tracts of wild lands, lying between the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans, and between the Gulf of Mexico and the North Pole. But, on general principles of law and reason, they might as well pretend to own the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans themselves; or the atmosphere and the sunlight; and to hold them, and dispose of them, for the payment of these debts.
===Chinese===
再者，这个作为这笔钱真实借入方的秘密的强盗和杀人犯团伙，没有任何正当的法人存在，也就没有任何可用来偿还这些债务的法人财产。他们的确自称拥有横亘于大西洋与太平洋之间、墨西哥湾与北极之间的大片荒地。但是，按照法律与理性的一般原则，他们这样自称，同自称拥有大西洋和太平洋本身、或者拥有大气和阳光，并为了偿还这些债务而持有和处分它们，没有什么两样。
===Original===
Having no corporate property with which to pay what purports to be their corporate debts, this secret band of robbers and murderers are really bankrupt. They have nothing to pay with. In fact, they do not propose to pay their debts otherwise than from the proceeds of their future robberies and murders. These are confessedly their sole reliance; and were known to be such by the lenders of the money, at the time the money was lent. And it was, therefore, virtually a part of the contract, that the money should be repaid only from the proceeds of these future robberies and murders. For this reason, if for no other, the contracts were void from the beginning.
===Chinese===
这个秘密的强盗和杀人犯团伙没有任何可用来偿付其号称的法人债务的法人财产，实际上已经破产。他们一无所有，无从偿付。事实上，他们打算偿债的唯一来源，就是他们未来抢劫和谋杀的所得。他们自己供认，这是他们唯一的指望；而放贷者在放贷之时也知道这一点。因此，这笔钱只从这些未来的抢劫和谋杀所得中偿还，实际上就是契约的一部分。单凭这一条理由——纵使没有别的理由——这些契约从一开始就是无效的。
===Original===
In fact, these apparently two classes, borrowers and lenders, were really one and the same class. They borrowed and lent money from and to themselves. They themselves were not only part and parcel, but the very life and soul, of this secret band of robbers and murderers, who borrowed and spent the money. Individually they furnished money for a common enterprise; taking, in return, what purported to be corporate promises for individual loans. The only excuse they had for taking these so-called corporate promises of, for individual loans by, the same parties, was that they might have some apparent excuse for the future robberies of the band (that is, to pay the debts of the corporation), and that they might also know what shares they were to be respectively entitled to out of the proceeds of their future robberies.
===Chinese===
事实上，这表面上分为两类的借方和贷方，实际上是同一类人。他们是自己向自己借钱、自己把钱贷给自己。对于这个借入和花费这笔钱的秘密的强盗和杀人犯团伙来说，他们不只是其中的一部分，而正是它的生命与灵魂。他们以个人身份为一件共同事业出资，换回来的则是号称法人承诺的凭据，以抵偿个人的放款。他们之所以收下这些同一伙人打的所谓法人借据、以抵个人的放款，唯一的借口是：这样他们就可以为该团伙未来的抢劫预备某种表面上的名目（也就是偿还法人的债务），而且他们也可以借此知道，在未来抢劫的所得中，他们各自有权分得多少份额。
===Original===
Finally, if these debts had been created for the most innocent and honest purposes, and in the most open and honest manner, by the real parties to the contracts, these parties could thereby have bound nobody but themselves, and no property but their own. They could have bound nobody that should have come after them, and no property subsequently created by, or belonging to, other persons.
===Chinese===
最后，即使这些债务是契约的真实当事人为最无辜、最正当的目的，以最公开、最诚实的方式设立的，这些当事人也借此只能约束他们自己，只能支配他们自己的财产。他们约束不了任何后来者，也支配不了其后由他人创设的或属于他人的任何财产。
### XVIII / 十八

===Original===
The Constitution having never been signed by anybody; and there being no other open, written, or authentic contract between any parties whatever, by virtue of which the United States government, so called, is maintained; and it being well known that none but male persons, of twenty-one years of age and upwards, are allowed any voice in the government; and it being also well known that a large number of these adult persons seldom or never vote at all; and that *all* those who do vote, do so secretly (by secret ballot), and in a way to prevent their individual votes being known, either to the world, or even to each other; and consequently in a way to make no one openly responsible for the acts of their agents, or representatives—all these things being known, the questions arise: *Who* compose the real governing power in the country? Who are the men, *the responsible men*, who rob us of our property? Restrain us of our liberty? Subject us to their arbitrary dominion? And devastate our homes, and shoot us down by the hundreds of thousands, if we resist? How shall we find these men? How shall we know them from others? How shall we defend ourselves and our property against them? Who, of our neighbors, are members of this secret band of robbers and murderers? How can we know which are *their* houses, that we may burn or demolish them? Which *their* property, that we may destroy it? Which *their* persons, that we may kill them, and rid the world and ourselves of such tyrants and monsters?
===Chinese===
宪法从未经任何人签署；在任何当事方之间也不存在任何其他公开、书面或真实可靠的契约，凭以维系所谓的合众国政府；众所周知，在政府中享有任何发言权的，只有年满二十一岁的男性；同样众所周知，这些成年男子当中为数众多的人很少投票或从不投票；而*所有*投票的人，都是秘密地投（通过秘密投票），其投法使人无从知道他们的个人选票——既不让世人知道，甚至也不让彼此知道；从而其投法使任何人都无须公开对其代理人或代表的行为负责——凡此种种既已明了，问题便随之而来：*谁*是这个国家真正的统治力量？那些抢劫我们财产、限制我们自由、使我们屈从其专横统治的人，那些一旦我们抵抗就蹂躏我们的家园、以数十万计地枪杀我们的人——这些*负得起责任的人*究竟是谁？我们要到哪里去找这些人？我们如何把他们同其他人区别开来？我们如何保卫自己和自己的财产不受他们侵害？我们的邻人当中，谁是这个秘密的强盗和杀人犯团伙的成员？我们怎样才能知道哪些是*他们的*房子，好去放火焚烧或夷为平地？哪些是*他们的*财产，好去加以毁坏？哪些是*他们的*人身，好去杀掉他们，为世界也为我们自己除掉这样的暴君和怪物？
===Original===
These are questions that must be answered, before men can be free; before they can protect themselves against this secret band of robbers and murderers, who now plunder, enslave, and destroy them.
===Chinese===
在人们能够获得自由之前，在他们能够保护自己不受这个如今正在掠夺、奴役和毁灭他们的秘密的强盗和杀人犯团伙之害之前，这些问题必须得到回答。
===Original===
The answer to these questions is, that only those who have the will and the power to shoot down their fellow men, are the real rulers in this, as in all other (so called) civilized countries; for by no others will civilized men be robbed, or enslaved.
===Chinese===
这些问题的答案是：无论在这个国家，还是在所有其他所谓文明国家，真正的统治者只能是那些既有意志又有权力枪杀自己同类的人；因为文明人受抢劫、受奴役，决不会出自其他任何人之手。
===Original===
Among savages, mere physical strength, on the part of one man, may enable him to rob, enslave, or kill another man.
