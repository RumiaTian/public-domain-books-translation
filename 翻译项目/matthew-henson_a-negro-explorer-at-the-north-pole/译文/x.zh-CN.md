## X / 十

===Original===
Forward! March!
Commander Peary was an officer of the United States Navy, but there never was the slightest military aspect to any of his expeditions. No banners flying, no trumpets blaring, and no sharp, incisive commands. Long ago, crossing the icecap of North Greenland, he carried a wand of bamboo, on one end of which was attached a little silk guidon, with a star embroidered on it, but even that had been discarded and the only thing military about this expedition was his peremptory “Forward! March!” What flags we had were folded and stowed on Commander Peary’s sledge, and broken-out only at the North Pole.
Captain Bartlett and Mr. George Borup were all alert and at attention, the command of preparation and the command of execution were quickly given in rapid succession, and they were off.
From the diary.
February 28, 1909: A bright, clear morning. Captain Bartlett and his crew, Ooqueah, Pooadloonah, and Harrigan; and George Borup and Karko, Seegloo, and Keshungwah, have set sail and are on their way.
Captain Bartlett made the trail and George Borup was the scout, and a rare “Old Scout” he was. He kept up the going for three days and then came back to the land to start again with new loads of supplies.
The party that stayed at Crane City until March 1, consisted of Commander Peary, MacMillan, Goodsell, Marvin, myself, and fourteen Eskimos, whom you don’t know, and ninety-eight dogs, that you may have heard about.
The dogs were double-fed and we put a good meal inside ourselves before turning-in on the night of February 28, 1909. The next morning was to be our launching, and we went to sleep full of the thought of what was before us. From now on it was keep on going, and keep on﻿—and we kept on; sometimes in the face of storms of wind and snow that it is impossible for you to imagine.

===Chinese===
「前进！开步走！」
皮尔里指挥官是美国海军的军官，可他的历次探险，从来不曾有过丝毫的军事气派。没有招展的旗帜，没有齐鸣的号角，也没有短促干脆的口令。很久以前，横穿北格陵兰冰盖的时候，他手里拿过一根竹杖，杖头系着一面小小的丝绸燕尾队旗，上头绣着一颗星；可就连那东西后来也废弃不用了，而这次探险里唯一带点军事意味的，就是他那句不容分说的「前进！开步走！」。我们带着的几面旗子都折叠收拢，安放在皮尔里指挥官的雪橇上，直到北极点才展开亮相。
巴特利特船长和乔治·博鲁普先生早已警觉地立正待命；预令与动令急促地接连下达，他们便出发了。
以下摘自日记。
1909年2月28日：一个明亮晴朗的早晨。巴特利特船长和他的一队人马——乌奎亚（Ooqueah）、普阿德卢纳和哈里根（Harrigan），还有乔治·博鲁普和他的几个人——卡科、西格鲁和克顺瓦（Keshungwah），都已启程上路。
巴特利特船长负责开路，乔治·博鲁普担任斥候，而且是位难得的「老斥候」。他坚持赶了三天的路，然后回到陆地上，装上新的补给再从头出发。
在鹤城一直留到3月1日的那支队伍，成员是皮尔里指挥官、麦克米伦、古德塞尔、马文、我本人，外加十四名你们还不认识的因纽特人，以及九十八条狗——那些狗，你们或许早有耳闻。
1909年2月28日夜里就寝之前，狗都喂了双份的食，我们自己也各自饱餐了一顿。第二天早晨便是我们启程的时刻，我们怀着对前路的种种念想入睡。从今往后，就是不停地走啊走——我们也确实不停地走了下去；有时迎着你们根本无法想象的风雪风暴前进。

Day does not break in the Arctic regions, it just comes on quietly the same as down here, but I must say that at daybreak on March 1, 1909, we were all excitement and attention. A furious wind was blowing, which we took as a good omen; for, on all of Commander Peary’s travelings, a good big, heavy, storm of blinding snow has been his stirrup-cup and here he had his last. Systematically we had completed our preparations on the two days previous, so that, by six a.m. of the 1st of March, we were ready and standing at the upstanders of our sledges, awaiting the command “Forward! March!”
Already, difficulties had commenced. Ooblooyah and Slocum (Eskimo name, Inighito, but, on account of his dilatory habits, known as Slocum) were incapacitated; Ooblooyah with a swelled knee, and Slocum with a frozen heel. The cold gets you in most any place, up there.
I and my three boys were ordered to take the lead. We did so, at about half past six o’clock in the morning. Forward! March! and we were off.

北极地带无所谓破晓，天色同我们这里一样，只是悄悄地亮起来；不过我得说，1909年3月1日拂晓时分，我们个个又兴奋又专注。狂风劲吹，我们把它当作好兆头；因为皮尔里指挥官历次出行，总有一场又大又猛、刮得人睁不开眼的大风雪为他饯行，而这一场，便是他的最后一杯饯行酒。前两天里，我们已井井有条地把各项准备一一办妥，因此到3月1日早晨六点，我们一切就绪，站在各自雪橇的立柱旁，只待那声口令：「前进！开步走！」
麻烦已经开始了。乌布卢亚和斯洛克姆（Slocum，因纽特名字叫伊尼希托［Inighito］，只因做事拖沓，得了这个外号）都上不了阵了：乌布卢亚膝盖肿了，斯洛克姆脚跟冻伤了。在那上面，寒冷差不多哪儿都能找上你。
我和我手下三个小伙子奉命领队打头阵。我们照办了，时间约莫在早晨六点半钟。「前进！开步走！」我们出发了。
