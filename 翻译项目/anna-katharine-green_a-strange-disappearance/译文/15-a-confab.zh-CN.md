## XV A Confab / 十五 一席密谈

===Original===
Next morning Mr. Gryce and I met in serious consultation. How, and in what direction should we extend the inquiries necessary to a discovery of these Schoenmakers?

“I advise a thorough overhauling of the German quarter,” said my superior. “Schmidt, and Rosenthal will help us and the result ought to be satisfactory.”

But I shook my head at this. “I don’t believe,” said I, “that they will hide among their own people. You must remember they are not alone, but have with them a young woman of a somewhat distinguished appearance, whose presence in a crowded district, like that, would be sure to awaken gossip; something which above all else they must want to avoid.”

“That is true; the Germans are a dreadful race for gossip.”

===Chinese===
第二天早晨，格赖斯先生同我碰头，郑重商议。要寻出这舍恩马克父子，我们的查访该怎样进行、朝哪个方向进行？

“我主张把德国人聚居区彻底翻检一遍，”我的上司说。“施密特和罗森塔尔会帮我们，结果应该叫人满意。”

对此我却摇头。“我不信，”我说，“他们会藏在本族人中间。您得记得，他们不是孤身两人，身边还带着一个模样相当出众的年轻女人；她这样的人一出现在那样人烟稠密的地段，必定惹出闲话——这闲话，正是他们比什么都唯恐避之不及的。”

“这话不假；德国人这个族类，闲话起来可了不得。”

===Original===
“If they dared to ill-dress her or ill-treat her, it would be different. But she is a valuable piece of property to them you see, a choice lot of goods which it is for their interest to preserve in first-class condition till the day comes for its disposal. For I presume you have no doubt that it is for the purpose of extorting money from Mr. Blake that they have carried off his young wife.”

“For that reason or one similar. He is a man of resources, they may have hoped he would help them to escape the country.”

“If they don’t hide in the German quarter they certainly won’t in the Italian, French or Irish. What they want is too keep close and rouse no questions. I think they will be found to have gone up the river somewhere, or over to Jersey. Hoboken wouldn’t be a bad place to send Schmidt to.”

===Chinese===
“倘若他们胆敢叫她穿得寒酸，或是虐待她，那就另当别论。可您瞧，她于他们是一件值钱的货色，一批上等的货，值得他们保管得妥妥帖帖，直到脱手的日子。因为我料想，您不会怀疑：他们拐走他这位年轻的妻子，正为的是从布莱克先生身上榨钱。”

“为这个缘故，或差不多的缘故。他是个有财有路数的人，他们也许指望他助他们逃出这个国家。”

“他们若不藏德国人聚居区，便断然不会藏意大利人、法国人或爱尔兰人的地段。他们要的是躲得严实，不惹人发问。我想，到头来会发现他们是在河上游的什么去处，要么就过了河到泽西。派施密特去霍博肯，倒不失为一步好棋。”

===Original===
“You forget what it is they’ve got on their minds; besides no conspicuous party such as they could live in a rural district without attracting more attention than in the most crowded tenement house in the city.”

“Where do you think, then, they would be liable to go?”

“Well my most matured thought on the subject,” returned Mr. Gryce, after a moment’s deliberation, “is this﻿—you say, and I agree, that they have hampered themselves with this woman at this time for the purpose of using her hereafter in a scheme of blackmail upon Mr. Blake. He, then, must be the object about which their thoughts revolve and toward which whatever operations or plans they may be engaged upon must tend. What follows? When a company of men have made up their minds to rob a bank, what is the first thing they do? They hire, if possible, a house next to the especial building they intend to enter, and for months work upon the secret passage through which they hope to reach the safe and its contents; or they make friends with the watchman that guards its treasures, and the janitor who opens and shuts the doors. In short they hang about their prey before they pounce upon it. And so will these Schoenmakers do in the somewhat different robbery which they plan sooner or later to effect. Whatever may keep them close at this moment, Mr. Blake and Mr. Blake’s house is the point toward which their eyes are turned, and if we had time﻿—”

===Chinese===
“您忘了他们心里压着的是什么事；再说，像他们这样惹眼的一伙，住在乡间地带，比住在城里最挤的贫民公寓里还更招眼。”

“那么依你看，他们会往哪里去呢？”

“唔，我在这事上想得最成熟的一个念头，”格赖斯先生沉吟片刻后答道，“是这样——你说，我也同意，他们这会儿拖上这个女人，为的是日后拿她在布莱克先生身上施一桩敲诈。那么，他必是他们心思旋转的轴心，他们的一切动作与计划，无论正在进行什么，都必朝他汇聚。由此推出什么？一伙人打定了主意要抢银行，头一件事做什么？可能的话，他们租下紧挨着目标的那所房子，一连几个月开凿秘密通道，指望由它通到保险柜和柜中之物；要么就同看守财宝的更夫、开门闭户的司阍交朋友。总之，扑上去之前，先在猎物近旁徘徊。这两个舍恩马克，在他们迟早要实施的这场不大一样的抢劫里，也会如法炮制。此刻无论是什么把他们拴在近处，布莱克先生和布莱克先生的宅邸，终是他们眼睛盯住的那一点，倘若我们有工夫——”

===Original===
“But we haven’t,” I broke in impetuously. “It is horrible to think of that grand woman languishing away in the power of such rascals.”

“If we had time,” Mr. Gryce persisted, “all it would be necessary to do would be to wait, they would come into our hands as easily and naturally as a hawk into the snare of the fowler. But as you say we have not, and therefore, I would recommend a little beating of the bush directly about Mr. Blake’s house; for if all my experience is not at fault, those men are already within eyeshot of the prey they intend to run down.”

“But,” said I, “I have been living myself in that very neighborhood and know by this time the ways of every house in the vicinity. There is not a spot up and down the Avenue for ten blocks where they could hide away for two days much less two weeks. And as for the side streets﻿—why I could tell you the names of those who live in each house for a considerable distance. Yet if you say so I will go to work﻿—”

===Chinese===
“可我们没有，”我按捺不住，插嘴道。“一想到那样一位雍容的女人，萎顿在这样一伙恶徒手里，就叫人不寒而栗。”

“倘若我们有工夫，”格赖斯先生执意说下去，“那么只消守候，他们自会像老鹰落进捕鹰人的罗网一样，又轻易又自然地落进我们手里。可是正如您说的，我们没有工夫；因此我建议，就在布莱克先生宅子四近，直接做一番打草惊蛇。因为倘若我的全部经验没有弄错，那两个人早已蹲在他们打算扑倒的猎物的视线之内了。”

“可是，”我说，“我自己就在那一带住着，到如今，左近每一户的底细我都摸熟了。这条大道上下十个街区，没有一个角落容得他们藏上两天，更别说两个星期。至于横街——我简直可以把相当一段路上每户人家的姓名一一说给您听。不过您若这样说，我就去着手——”

===Original===
“Do, and meanwhile Schmidt and Rosenthal shall rummage the German quarter and even go through Williamsburgh and Hoboken. The end justifies any amount of labor that can be spent upon this matter.”

“And you,” I asked.

“Will do my part when you have done yours.”

===Chinese===
“去办吧；同时叫施密特和罗森塔尔去搜德国人聚居区，连威廉斯堡和霍博肯也走一遍。为了这个案子，费多少力气都值。”

“那么您呢？”我问。

“你们做完了你们那份，我自然做我这份。”
