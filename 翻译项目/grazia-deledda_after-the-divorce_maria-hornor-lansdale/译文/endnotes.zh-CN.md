## Endnotes / 尾注

===Original===
1. Porredda, female diminutive for Porru. ↩︎

===Chinese===
1. 波蕾达（Porredda），波鲁（Porru）的阴性指小形式。 ↩︎

===Original===
2. Piedino﻿—little foot. ↩︎

===Chinese===
2. 皮耶迪诺（Piedino）﻿——“小脚”。 ↩︎

===Original===
3. An enclosed pasture, but of vast extent. ↩︎

===Chinese===
3. 一片圈围起来的牧场，只是范围极广。 ↩︎

===Original===
4. *Che ti morsichi il cane*﻿—“May the dog bite you.” ↩︎

===Chinese===
4. *Che ti morsichi il cane*﻿——“愿狗咬你。” ↩︎

===Original===
5. A summer goblin, invoked in Sardinia to frighten children out of the sun. ↩︎

===Chinese===
5. 一种夏季妖怪，撒丁岛人搬出它来吓唬孩子，不让他们待在太阳底下。 ↩︎

===Original===
6. Bread and water. ↩︎

===Chinese===
6. 面包和水。 ↩︎

===Original===
7. *Balla chi trapasset sa busacca, brasciai!*﻿—“I wish a ball would hit him in the pouch, the he-wolf!” ↩︎

===Chinese===
7. *Balla chi trapasset sa busacca, brasciai!*﻿——“但愿一粒子弹打中他的裤裆，那头公狼！” ↩︎

===Original===
8. Stagman. (Translator’s note.) ↩︎

===Chinese===
8. 鹿人（Stagman）。（译者注。） ↩︎

===Original===
9. A grind-stone turned by a small donkey, which grinds a hundred litres of grain in four days. ↩︎

===Chinese===
9. 一副由小毛驴推转的磨盘，四天可磨一百升谷物。 ↩︎

===Original===
10. In Sardinia, farm labourers often own cattle which are either turned out with their master’s herds (whose partners they thus, in a manner, become), or are confided to some other shepherd, who receives half the profits in return for looking after them. ↩︎

===Chinese===
10. 在撒丁岛，雇农往往自养牛只，这些牛或与东家的牛群一同放牧（如此一来，雇农在某种程度上便成了东家的合伙人），或托付给别的牧人照管，那人以照管之劳换得收益的一半。 ↩︎

===Original===
11. *Ispana trista* or *santa*, from which, according to tradition, the crown of thorns was made. The people use the leaves of this tree for medicinal purposes. ↩︎

===Chinese===
11. *Ispana trista* 或 *santa*（圣树），据传说，耶稣的荆棘冠便是用这种树编成的。百姓取这种树的叶子作药用。 ↩︎

===Original===
12. The custom of burying a person bitten by a tarantula in a dunghill, and putting him in an oven, is not so unreasonable as it at first appears, the effect of the poison being neutralised if the sufferer can be made to perspire freely; while the sickening odours of the dunghill induce nausea, also supposed to be very beneficial. Now, however, the people completely ignoring these practical results, the ceremony has come to be an act of pure superstition. The account given above describes such scenes as they have actually been known to occur. ↩︎

===Chinese===
12. 把被狼蛛咬伤的人埋进粪堆、再放进烤炉的风俗，并不像乍看上去那样不合情理：只要能让伤者畅快地出一身大汗，毒性便可化解；而粪堆令人作呕的恶臭会引发恶心呕吐，据说这也大有裨益。然而如今，人们全然无视这些实际功效，这一仪式已沦为纯粹的迷信。上文描述的种种情景，均系此类事情实际发生时的真实情形。 ↩︎

===Original===
13. Head of cattle. ↩︎

===Chinese===
13. 一头牛。 ↩︎

===Original===
14. In Sardinia the fireplaces almost always consist of four stones placed so as to form a square in the centre of the kitchen. They have no chimneys. ↩︎

===Chinese===
14. 在撒丁岛，火塘几乎总是用四块石头在厨房正中围成一个方形。火塘不设烟囱。 ↩︎
