## VIII. The Water War / 八、水之战

===Original===
After the sound of that terrible shouting there came silence﻿—that is, there was silence where the children were, but all above they could hear the rush and rustle of a quick arming.

===Chinese===
那可怕的呐喊声过后，随之而来的是寂静——就是说，孩子们所在的地方一片寂静，可是在他们头顶上，却听得见一阵匆促备战的奔忙骚响。

===Original===
“The war cry of the People of the Depths,” said the Princess.

===Chinese===
"这是深渊族的战吼。"公主说。

===Original===
“I suppose,” said Kathleen forlornly, “that if they’re so near as that all is lost.”

===Chinese===
"我看，"凯思琳绝望地说，"他们既然已经近到这个地步，那就全完了。"

===Original===
“Lost? No, indeed,” cried the Princess. “The People of the Depths are very strong, but they are very heavy. They cannot rise up and come to us from the water above. Before they can get in they must scale the wall.”

===Chinese===
"完了？不，才不呢。"公主喊道。"深渊族力气很大，可他们也非常沉重。他们没法从上方的水里腾起身子扑到我们这儿来。他们要想打进来，就得先攀上城墙。"

===Original===
“But they will get over the wall﻿—won’t they?”

===Chinese===
"可他们总会越过城墙的——不是吗？"

===Original===
“Not while one of the Royal Halibuts still lives. The Halibuts have manned the wall; they will keep back the foe. But they won’t attack yet. They’ll send out their scouts and skirmishers. Till they approach, the Crustacean Brigade can do nothing. It is a hard thing to watch a fight in which you may not share. I must apologize for appointing you to such an unsatisfactory position.”

===Chinese===
"只要王家大比目鱼还有一条活着，他们就过不来。大比目鱼们已经上了城墙，他们会挡住敌人。不过他们暂时还不会出击。他们会先放出侦察兵和散兵。在他们逼近之前，甲壳旅无事可做。眼睁睁看着一场自己不能插手的仗，是件难受的事。我把你们安置在这么一个不尽如人意的位置上，实在要道一声抱歉。"

===Original===
“Thank you, *we* don’t mind,” said Cathay hastily. “What’s that?”

===Chinese===
"谢谢您，*我们*不在乎。"凯西连忙说。"那是什么？"

===Original===
It was a solid, gleaming sheet of silver that rose above them like a great carpet﻿—which split and tore itself into silver threads.

===Chinese===
那是一整片闪闪发亮的银色，像一块巨大的地毯在他们头顶升起来——随即它自己裂开、撕碎，扯成了一根根银线。

===Original===
“It is the Swordfish Brigade,” said the Princess. “We could swim up a little and watch them, if you’re not afraid. You see, the first attack will probably be delivered by one of their Shark regiments. The 7th Sharks have a horrible reputation. But our brave Swordfish are a match for them,” she added proudly.

===Chinese===
"是剑鱼旅。"公主说。"要是你们不害怕，我们可以往上游一点儿，看看他们。你们瞧，他们的第一击多半会由一支鲨鱼团来发动。第七鲨鱼团的名声可怕得很。不过我们勇敢的剑鱼足以同他们匹敌。"她自豪地补了一句。

===Original===
The Swordfish, who were slowly swimming to and fro above, seemed to stiffen as though to meet some danger at present unseen by the others. Then, with a swift, silent, terrible movement, the Sharks rushed on the noble defenders of Merland.

===Chinese===
剑鱼们在头顶缓缓地来回巡游，忽然浑身一绷，仿佛要迎击一个旁人还看不见的危险。紧接着，鲨鱼们以一种迅疾、无声而可怖的动静，向人鱼国高贵的保卫者们猛冲过去。

===Original===
The Swordfish with their deadly weapons were ready﻿—and next moment all the water was a wild whirl of confused conflict. The Sharks fought with a sort of harsh, rough courage, and the children, who had drawn away to a little distance, could not help admiring their desperate onslaught. But the Swordfish were more than their match. With more skill, and an equally desperate gallantry, they met and repulsed the savage onslaught of the Sharks.

===Chinese===
剑鱼们带着致命的武器严阵以待——下一瞬间，整片海水便成了一团翻腾混战的漩流。鲨鱼们打起来有一股凶悍粗野的狠劲，退开一小段距离观战的孩子们，也不由得佩服他们那股拼死的猛劲。可是剑鱼比他们更胜一筹：剑鱼们以更精熟的技艺、同样不要命的英勇气概，迎住鲨鱼凶猛的冲击，把他们击退了。

===Original===
Shoals of large, calm Cod swept up from the depths, and began to shoulder the dead Sharks sideways toward the water above the walls﻿—the dead Sharks and, alas! many a brave, dead Swordfish, too. For the victory had not been a cheap one.

===Chinese===
一群群个头很大、神色沉静的鳕鱼从深处涌了上来，开始侧着身子把死鲨鱼往城墙上方的水里推——推走的不光是死鲨鱼，唉，还有许多条英勇战死的剑鱼。因为这场胜利的代价并不小。

===Original===
The children could not help cheering as the victorious Swordfish reformed.

===Chinese===
得胜的剑鱼重新列队时，孩子们忍不住欢呼起来。

===Original===
“Pursuit is unnecessary,” said the Princess. “The Sharks have lost too heavily to resume the attack.”

===Chinese===
"不必追击了。"公主说，"鲨鱼损失惨重，无力再攻了。"

===Original===
A Shark in terror-stricken retreat passed close by her, and she clipped its tail with her oyster shell.

===Chinese===
一条魂飞魄散、仓皇败退的鲨鱼恰好从她身旁擦过，她挥起牡蛎壳，一下剪掉了它的尾巴。

===Original===
The Shark turned savagely, but the Princess with one tail-swish was out of danger, pushing the children before her outspread arms, and the Shark began to sink, still making vain efforts to pursue them.

===Chinese===
鲨鱼凶狠地回身反扑，可公主尾巴一甩就脱出了险境，张开双臂把孩子们推在身前。鲨鱼开始往下沉，还在徒劳地想追上他们。

===Original===
“The shell will drag him down,” said the Princess; “and now I must go and get a fresh shield. I wish I knew where the next attack would be delivered.”

===Chinese===
"这贝壳会拖着他沉下去。"公主说，"现在我得去弄一面新盾牌了。真希望我知道他们下一击会打向哪里。"

===Original===
They sank slowly through the water.

===Chinese===
他们缓缓沉入水中。

===Original===
“I wonder where Reuben is?” said Bernard.

===Chinese===
"不知鲁本在哪儿？"伯纳德说。

===Original===
“Oh, he’s quite safe,” said the Princess. “The Boy Scouts don’t go outside the walls﻿—they just do a good turn for anybody who wants it, you know﻿—and help the kind Soles to look after the wounded.”

===Chinese===
"哦，他保险得很。"公主说。"童子军是不出城墙的——他们嘛，专门给需要的人搭把手，这你知道——还帮着好心的鳎鱼照料伤员。"

===Original===
They had reached the great flooded garden again and turned toward the Palace, and as they went a Sea Urchin shell suddenly rose from behind one of the clipped hedges﻿—a Sea Urchin shell and behind it a long tail.

===Chinese===
他们又回到了那座水淹的大花园，转身朝王宫走去。正走着，一个海胆壳突然从一道修剪得整整齐齐的树篱后面升起来——一个海胆壳，壳后还拖着一条长尾巴。

===Original===
The shell was raised, and the face under it was Reuben’s.

===Chinese===
壳抬了起来，壳底下那张脸正是鲁本的。

===Original===
“Hi, Princess!” he shouted. “I’ve been looking for you everywhere. We’ve been scouting. I got a lot of seaweed, and they thought I was nothing *but* seaweed; and so I got quite close to the enemy.”

===Chinese===
"喂，公主！"他喊道。"我到处找你们。我们一直在侦察。我披了好多海草，他们竟当我*就*是一堆海草，我就这么着凑到敌人跟前去了。"

===Original===
“It was very rash,” said the Princess severely.

===Chinese===
"这也太莽撞了。"公主严厉地说。

===Original===
“The others don’t think so,” he said, a little hurt. “They began by saying I was only an irregular Sea Urchin, because I’ve got this jolly tail”﻿—he gave it a merry wag﻿—“and they called me Spatangus, and names like that. But they’ve made me their General now﻿—General Echinus. I’m a regular now, and no mistake, and what I was going to say is the enemy is going to attack the North Tower in force in half an hour.”

===Chinese===
"别人可不这么想。"他有点委屈地说。"起初他们说我不算个正经海胆，因为我长了这条好玩儿的尾巴"——他美滋滋地摇了摇尾巴——"管我叫什么斯帕坦古斯，还有一串这样的名儿。可如今他们已经推我当将军了——艾基努斯将军。我这会儿是正规军了，错不了。我要说的是：半个钟头以后，敌人就要大举进攻北塔。"

===Original===
“You good boy,” said the Princess. I do believe if it hadn’t been for his Sea Urchin’s uniform she would have kissed him. “You’re splendid. You’re a hero. If you could do it safely﻿—there’s heaps of seaweed﻿—could you find out if there’s any danger from the Book People? You know﻿—the ones in the cave. It’s always been our fear that they might attack, *too:* and if they did﻿—well, I’d rather be the slave of a Shark than of Mrs. Fairchild.” She gathered an armful of seaweed from the nearest tree, and Reuben wrapped himself in it and drifted off﻿—looking less like a live Boy Scout than you could believe possible.

===Chinese===
"真是好孩子。"公主说。我敢说，要不是他穿着那身海胆军服，她准会亲他一下的。"你真了不起。你是个英雄。要是你能平平安安地办到——海草有的是——能不能去探一探书里人那头有没有危险？你知道的——就是洞里那伙人。我们一直担心他们*也*会来攻：真要那样——唉，我宁可给鲨鱼当奴隶，也不给费尔柴尔德太太当奴隶。"她从最近的一棵树上抱下一捧海草，鲁本把自己裹在里面漂走了——那模样要多不像个活生生的童子军有多不像，简直叫人不敢相信。

===Original===
The defenders of Merland, now acting on Reuben’s information, began to mass themselves near the North Wall.

===Chinese===
人鱼国的守军如今有了鲁本的情报，开始向北墙附近集结。

===Original===
“Now is our time,” said the Princess. “We must go along the tunnel, and when we hear the sound of their heavy feet shaking the flow of ocean we must make sallies, and fix our shell shields in their feet. Major, rally your men.”

===Chinese===
"现在该我们了。"公主说。"我们必须沿隧道进发，一听见他们沉重的脚步震得海水发颤，就冲出去突袭，把贝壳盾牌扎进他们的脚里。少校，集合你的人马。"

===Original===
A tall Merchild in the Crustacean uniform blew a clear note, and the soldiers of the Crustacean Brigade, who having nothing particular to do had been helping anyone and everyone as best they could, which is the way in Merland, though not in Europe, gathered about their officers.

===Chinese===
一个身穿甲壳旅制服的高个小人鱼吹响一声清亮的号音。甲壳旅的士兵们本来就没什么正经差事，一直在尽力帮这个帮那个——这是人鱼国的做派，欧洲可没有——此刻便朝各自的军官聚拢过来。

===Original===
When they were all drawn up before her, the Princess addressed her troops.

===Chinese===
等队伍全在她面前排列整齐，公主开始对部队训话。

===Original===
“My men,” she said, “we have been suddenly plunged into war. But it has not found us unprepared. I am proud to think that my regiments are ready to the last pearl button. And I know that every man among you will be as proud as I am that our post is, as tradition tells us it has always been, the post of danger. We shall go out into the depths of the sea to fight the enemies of our dear country, and to lay down our lives, if need be, for that country’s sake.”

===Chinese===
"弟兄们，"她说，"我们突然之间被卷入了战争。但战争并没有把我们打个措手不及。一想到我的各个团都已准备停当，连最后一颗珍珠纽扣都系得端端正正，我就觉得骄傲。我知道，你们每一个人都会像我一样自豪，因为一如传统所言、历来如此，我们的岗位素来就是最危险的岗位。我们将开赴大海深处，同我们亲爱的祖国的敌人作战，必要时，为这个祖国献出生命。"

===Original===
The soldiers answered by cheers, and the Princess led the way to one of those little buildings, like Temples of Flora in old pictures, which the children had noticed in the gardens. At the order given a sergeant raised a great stone by a golden ring embedded in it and disclosed a dark passage leading underground.

===Chinese===
士兵们以欢呼声回应。公主在前引路，走向一座小建筑——就是孩子们在花园里注意到的那种，活像古画里的花神庙。一声令下，一名中士抓住嵌在石头里的金环，掀起一块大石板，露出一条通向地下的暗道。

===Original===
A splendid captain of Cockles, six feet high if he was an inch, with a sergeant and six men, led the way. Three Oyster officers followed, then a company of Oysters, the advance guard. At the head of the main body following were the Princess and her Staff. As they went the Princess explained why the tunnel was so long and sloped so steeply.

===Chinese===
一位威风凛凛的鸟蛤上尉——足有六英尺高，一英寸也少不了——带着一名中士和六名士兵在前面开路。三名牡蛎军官跟在后面，再后面是一连牡蛎，充当前卫。公主和她的参谋班子走在随后的主力队伍最前头。一路走着，公主顺带解释了这条隧道为什么这么长、坡又为什么这么陡。

===Original===
“You see,” she said, “the inside of our wall is only about ten feet high, but it goes down on the other side for forty feet or more. It is built on a hill. Now, I don’t want you to feel obliged to come out and fight. You can stay inside and get the shields ready for us to take. We shall keep on rushing back for fresh weapons. Of course the tunnel’s much too narrow for the Under Folk to get in, but they have their regiment of highly trained Sea Serpents, who, of course, can make themselves thin and worm through anything.”

===Chinese===
"你们瞧，"她说，"咱们城墙里面不过十来英尺高，可在另一面，墙往下延伸四十多英尺。墙是筑在一座山上的。我说这话，是不想让你们觉得非出去作战不可。你们可以待在里面，替我们把盾牌预备好，供我们取用。我们要不停地冲回来取新兵器。这条隧道当然窄得很，深渊族钻不进来，可他们有一支训练有素的海蛇团，海蛇嘛，自然能把自己变得细细的，什么窟窿都钻得过。"

===Original===
“Cathay doesn’t like serpents,” said Mavis anxiously.

===Chinese===
"凯西不喜欢蛇。"梅维斯不安地说。

===Original===
“You needn’t be afraid,” said the Princess. “They’re dreadful cowards. They know the passage is guarded by our Lobsters. They won’t come within a mile of the entrance. But the main body of the enemy will have to pass quite close. There’s a great sea mountain, and the only way to our North Tower is in the narrow ravine between that mountain and Merland.”

===Chinese===
"用不着怕。"公主说。"他们是天字第一号的胆小鬼。他们知道这条通道由我们的龙虾把守，洞口一英里之内都不敢挨近。不过敌人的主力就不得不从极近的地方经过了。那儿有一座很大的海山，通到我们北塔的路，只有那座大山和人鱼国之间那条窄窄的山峡。"

===Original===
The tunnel ended in a large rocky hall with the armory, hung with ten thousand gleaming shields, on the one side, and the guardroom crowded with enthusiastic Lobsters on the other. The entrance from the sea was a short, narrow passage, in which stood two Lobsters in their beautiful dark coats of mail.

===Chinese===
隧道尽头是一个宽敞的石殿：一边是兵器库，挂着一万面寒光闪闪的盾牌；另一边是卫兵室，挤满了热情高涨的龙虾。通向大海的入口是一条又短又窄的过道，过道里站着两只龙虾，披着漂亮的深色铠甲。

===Original===
Since the moment when the blue sky that looked first so like sky and then so like painted tin had, touched, confessed itself to be a bubble﻿—confessed, too, in the most practical way, by bursting and letting the water into Merland﻿—the children had been carried along by the breathless rush of preparations for the invasion, and the world they were now in had rapidly increased in reality, while their own world, in which till today they had always lived, had been losing reality at exactly the same rate as that by which the new world gained it. So it was that when the Princess said:

===Chinese===
自从那一刻起——那片蓝天起初那么像天空，随后又那么像漆过的马口铁，被他们一碰，终于招认自己是个泡泡，而且是以最切实的法子招认的：当场迸裂，让海水灌进了人鱼国——孩子们就一直被迎击入侵的匆匆准备裹挟着，忙得透不过气来。他们此刻身处的这个世界，真实性在飞快增长；而他们自己的世界，那个他们直到今天还一直生活着的世界，也正以一模一样的速度失去真实。正因如此，当公主说：

===Original===
“You needn’t go out and attack the enemy unless you like,” they all answered, in some astonishment:

===Chinese===
"你们不必出去攻打敌人，除非你们自己乐意。"他们全都有些惊讶地答道：

===Original===
“But we *want* to.”

===Chinese===
"可我们*偏*要去。"

===Original===
“That’s all right,” said the Princess. “I only wanted to see if they were in working order.”

===Chinese===
"这就对了。"公主说。"我不过是想看看它们灵不灵。"

===Original===
“If what were?”

===Chinese===
"什么灵不灵？"

===Original===
“Your coats. They’re coats of valor, of course.”

===Chinese===
"你们的甲衣呀。它们自然是勇气之衣。"

===Original===
“I think I could be brave without a coat,” said Bernard, and began to undo his pearl buttons.

===Chinese===
"我看不用甲衣，我自己也勇敢得了。"伯纳德说着，动手去解他的珍珠纽扣。

===Original===
“Of course you could,” said the Princess. “In fact, you must be brave to begin with, or the coat couldn’t work. It would be no good to a coward. It just keeps your natural valor warm and your wits cool.”

===Chinese===
"你当然能。"公主说。"说实在的，得先有胆量打底，甲衣才使得上劲。胆小鬼穿它一点儿用也没有。它不过是让你天生的勇气保住热气，让你的头脑保持冷静。"

===Original===
“It makes you braver,” said Kathleen suddenly. “At least I hope it’s me﻿—but I expect it’s the coat. Anyhow, I’m glad it does. Because I do want to be brave. Oh, Princess!”

===Chinese===
"它让人更勇敢。"凯思琳忽然说。"至少我希望那是我的功劳——不过我料想是甲衣的。不管怎样，有这效果我挺高兴。因为我实在想做个勇敢的人。哦，公主！"

===Original===
“Well?” said the Princess, gravely, but not unkindly, “what is it?”

===Chinese===
"怎么啦？"公主问，口气郑重，却不严厉。"什么事？"

===Original===
Kathleen stood a moment, her hands twisting in each other and her eyes downcast. Then in an instant she had unbuttoned and pulled off her coat of pearly mail and thrown it at the Princess’s feet.

===Chinese===
凯思琳站了一会儿，两只手彼此绞着，眼睛低垂。忽然间，她一下子解开那件珠光鳞甲的甲衣，把它扯下来，扔到了公主脚边。

===Original===
“I’ll do it without the coat,” she said, and drew a long breath.

===Chinese===
"不用甲衣，我也办得到。"她说，深深吸了一口气。

===Original===
The others looked on in silence, longing to help her, but knowing that no one could help her now but herself.

===Chinese===
其余三个默默望着，一心想帮她，却知道这时节除了她自己，谁也帮不了她。

===Original===
“It was me,” said Kathleen suddenly, and let go a deep breath of relief. “It was me that touched the sky and let in the water; and I am most frightfully sorry, and I know you’ll never forgive me. But﻿—”

===Chinese===
"是我。"凯思琳猛地说了出来，长长地舒了一口气。"是我碰了天，把水放进来的。我难过得要命，我知道你们永远也不会原谅我了。可是——"

===Original===
“Quick,” said the Princess, picking up the coat, “get into your armor; it’ll prevent your crying.” She hustled Kathleen into the coat and kept her arms around her. “Brave girl,” she whispered. “I’m glad you did it without the coat.” The other three thought it polite to turn away. “Of course,” the Princess added, “I knew﻿—but you didn’t know I knew.”

===Chinese===
"快，"公主说着拾起甲衣，"把铠甲穿上，免得你哭出来。"她三下两下帮凯思琳套上甲衣，又张开手臂搂住她。"勇敢的姑娘。"她悄声说。"你能不靠甲衣就做到，我很高兴。"另外三个识趣地转过头去。"其实呢，"公主补了一句，"我早就知道了——只是你不知道我知道罢了。"

===Original===
“How did you know?” said Kathleen.

===Chinese===
"您是怎么知道的？"凯思琳问。

===Original===

===Chinese===
"看你的眼睛。"公主说着，最后搂了她一下。"现在你的眼睛完全两样了。来，我们到城门去，看看咱们的侦察兵有没有在打信号。"

===Original===
