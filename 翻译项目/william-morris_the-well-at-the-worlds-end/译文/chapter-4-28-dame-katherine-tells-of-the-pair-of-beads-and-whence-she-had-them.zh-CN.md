## XXVIII / 凯瑟琳夫人讲述那串珠子及其来历
===Original===

Dame Katherine Tells of the Pair of Beads, and Whence She Had Them

Katherine cast friendly looks on them and said: “Gossip, and thou, Clement, I will make a clean breast of it once for all. In the days when I was first wedded to Master Clement yonder, he found his bed cold without me, for he was a hot lover; therefore would he often have me with him on his journeys, how hard soever or perilous the way might be. Yea, Clement, thou lookest the sooth, though thou sayest it not, I was nought loth thereto, partly because I would not grieve thee, my man; but partly, and belike mostly, because I was wishful to see the ways of the world even at the risk of being thrust out of the world. So it befell us on a time to make a journey together, a journey exceeding long, in the company of certain chapmen, whereof some, and not a few, died on the way. But we lived, and came into the eastern parts of the earth to a city right ancient, and fulfilled of marvels, which hight Sarras the Holy. There saw we wonders whereof were it overlong to tell of here; but one while I will tell thee, my lord. But this I must needs say, that I heard tell of a woman dwelling there, who was not old by seeming, but had in her the wisdom of ten lives, and the longing gat hold of me to see her and learn wisdom of her. So I entreated many who were called wise, some with prayers, and some with gifts also, to help me to speech of her; but I gat nothing either by praying or giving; they that would have helped me could not, and they that could would not. So, what between one thing and another, the longing to see the Wise Woman grew as it were into a madness in me. Amidst of which we fell in with a merchant exceeding wise in ancient lore, who looked at me (though Clement knew it not) with eyes of love. Of this man I asked concerning the Wise Woman, and he seeing my desire, strove to use it merchant-like, and would deal with me and have in payment for his learning a gift which I had nought to do to give. Howbeit madness and my desire for speech with the Wise Woman got the better of me, and I promised to give no less than he would, trusting to beguile him after I had got my desire, and be quit of him. So he led me to the woman and went his ways. She dwelt all by herself in a nook of an ancient ruined palace, erst the house of the ancientest of all the kings of Sarras.

===Chinese===

凯瑟琳朝两人投去友善的目光，说：「教亲，还有你，克莱门特，这回我把心里话一次倒干净。刚嫁给那边克莱门特老爷那阵子，他离了我就觉得被窝冷——他是个火热的情郎；所以出远门常带着我，不管路途多艰难凶险。是啊克莱门特，你心里这么想，嘴上不说：其实我也不怎么不愿意，一半是不愿让你难过，我的夫君；另一半——多半还是那一半——是我渴望见见世面，哪怕被踢出这世界也认了。

「一次机缘，我们结伴做了一趟远得不得了的旅行，同一伙客商同行，路上死的不少——可不是少数。我们活了下来，到了大地的东方，一座极其古老、充满奇观的城市，名叫圣城萨拉斯。

「在那里我们见了许多奇迹，一时讲不完；改日我再讲给你听，主公。可有一桩必须说：我听说那里住着一个女人，看着不老，肚里却有十辈子的智慧；想见她、向她学智慧的渴望攫住了我。我求了许多号称有智的人，有的用恳求，有的还搭上礼物，请他们引我见她；可求也求不来，送也送不通：愿意帮我的没本事，有本事的不愿意。

「就这样，这事那事夹攻，想见智慧妇人的渴望在我心里长成了一种痴狂。正在这时，我们遇上一位精通古学的商人，他看我的眼神（克莱门特并不知道）含着爱意。我向这人打听智慧妇人；他看破我的渴望，便打起商人的算盘，要同我做笔交易：他的学问，要我用一件我根本不该给的礼物来付账。可痴狂和求见智慧妇人的欲望压倒了我，他开什么价我应什么价，只指望达到目的后再哄开他、脱了身。于是他领我到那妇人处，自己走了。她独自住在古旧残破的王宫一角——那宫殿原是萨拉斯历代君王中最古老一王的家。

===Original===

When I came to her, I saw nought dreadful or ugsome about her: she was cheerful of countenance and courteous of demeanour, and greeted me kindly as one neighbour in the street of Wulstead might do to another. I saw her, that she was by seeming a woman of some forty winters, trim and well-fashioned of body, nowise big, but slender, of dark red hair and brown eyes somewhat small.

“Now, she said to me, ‘I have looked for thee a while; now thou art come, thou shalt tell me what thou needest, and thy needs will I fulfil. Yet needs must thou do a thing for me in return, and maybe thou wilt deem it a great thing. Yet whereas thou has struck a bargain before thou camest hither, if I undo that for thee, the bargain with me may be nought so burdensome. How sayest thou?’

“Well, I saw now that I was in the trap, for ill had it been in those days had Clement come to know that I had done amiss; for he was a jealous lover, and a violent man.”

Clement smiled hereat, but said nought, and Katherine went on: “Trap or no trap, if I were eager before, I was overeager now; so when she bade me swear to do her will, I swore it without tarrying.

“Then she said: ‘Sit down before me, and I will teach thee wisdom.’ What did she teach me? say ye. Well, if I told you belike ye would be none the wiser; but so much she told me, that my heart swelled with joy of the wisdom which I garnered. Say thou, Clement, if I have been the worser woman to thee, or thy friends, or mine.”

“Nay, goodwife,” said Clement, “I have nought against thee.”

Katherine laughed and went on:

“At last the Wise Woman said, ‘Now that thou hast of me all that may avail thee, comes the other part of our bargain, wherein I shall take and thou shalt give.’

“Quoth I, ‘That is but fair, and thou shalt find me true to thee.’ She said, ‘If thou be not, I shall know it, and shall amend it in such wise that it shall cost thee much.’

“Then she looked on me long and keenly, and said afterward: ‘Forsooth I should forbear laying this charge upon thee if I did not deem that thou wouldst be no less than true. But now I will try it, whereas I deem that the days of my life henceforward shall not be many; and many days would it take me to find a woman as little foolish as thee and as little false, and thereto as fairly fashioned.’

===Chinese===

「到了她跟前，我看她毫无可怕可憎之处：面色开朗，举止有礼，亲切地招呼我，就像伍尔斯特德街上的一个街坊招呼另一个。看模样，她是个四十来岁的妇人，身段利落匀称，绝不臃肿，而是纤瘦，深红色的头发，棕色的眼睛略小。

「她对我说：『我等你有一阵了；你既来了，就说说你要什么，我必满足你。只是你也须为我做一件事作为回报，你也许会觉得那是件大事。不过你还没来就先做了笔交易；我若替你把那笔勾销，同我这场买卖也许就不算太沉重。你说呢？』

「这下我看清自己落进了圈套：那年月，若是克莱门特知道我行了差踏错，可就糟了——他是个爱吃醋的情郎，又是个性子暴烈的人。」

克莱门特听了微笑，却不说话；凯瑟琳接着讲：「圈套也罢，不是圈套也罢，先前我是热切，如今是热切过了头；她叫我起誓依她行事，我毫不迟疑就起了誓。

「然后她说：『坐到我面前来，我教你智慧。』她教了我什么？你们要问。这么说吧，就算我讲给你们听，你们也未必更明白多少；反正她教了我那么多，我的心因收进来的智慧而欢喜得发胀。你说，克莱门特，我因此变成坏女人了吗——对你们，对我的朋友？」

「没有，老婆，」克莱门特说，「我没有半句怨你。」

凯瑟琳一笑，接着讲：

「最后智慧妇人说：『你已经从我这里得了于你有用的一切；咱们交易的后一半来了——这回是我取，你予。』

「我说：『这很公道，你会看到我守信。』她说：『你若不守，我会知道，也会补正——叫你付出大代价。』

「随后她久久地、锐利地端详我，然后说：『说实话，若不是认定你必守信不渝，我本不该把这担子压给你。可我要试一试，因为我想我余下的寿数不多了；要再找一个像你这样既不蠢又不假、还生得这般齐整的女子，得费我许多时日。』

===Original===

“Therewith she put her hand to her neck, and took thence the selfsame pair of beads which I gave to thee, dear gossip, and which (praise be to All Hallows!) thou hast borne ever since; and she said: ‘Now hearken! Thou shalt take this pair of beads, and do with them as I bid thee. Swear again thereto.’ So I swore by All Angels; and she said again: ‘This pair of beads shall one day lead a man unto the Well at the World’s End, but no woman; forsooth, if a woman have them of a woman, or the like of them, (for there be others,) they may serve her for a token; but will be no talisman or leading-stone to her; and this I tell thee lest thou seek to the Well on the strength of them. For I bid thee give them to a man that thou lovest﻿—that thou lovest well, when he is in most need; only he shall not be of thine own blood. This is all that I lay upon thee; and if thou do it, thou shalt thrive, and if thou do it not, thou shalt come to harm. And I will tell thee now that this meeting betwixt us is not by chance-hap, but of my bringing about; for I have laboured to draw thee to me, knowing that thou alone of women would avail me herein. Now shalt thou go home to thine hostel, and take this for a token of my soothsaying. The wise merchant who led thee unto me is abiding thine homecoming that he may have of thee that which thou promisedst to him. If then thou find him at thine hostel, and he take thee by the hand and lead thee to bed, whereas Clement is away till tomorrow even, then shalt thou call me a vain word-spinner and a liar; but if when thou comest home there, the folk there say to thee merchant Valerius is ridden away hastily, being called afar on a message of life and death, then shalt thou trow in me as a wise woman. Herewith depart, and I bid thee farewell.’

“So I went my ways to my hostel trembling, and at the door I met the chamberlain, who said to me, ‘Lady, the merchant Valerius hath been here seeking thee, and he said that he would abide thy coming; but amidst of his abiding cometh a man who would speak to him privily; whereof it came that he called for his horse and bade me tell thee, Lady, that he was summoned on a matter of life and death, and would return to kiss thine hands in five days’ space.’

===Chinese===

「说着她伸手到颈间，取下正是我后来给你的那串珠子，亲爱的教亲——赞美诸圣！你从那以后一直戴着它——她说：『听着！你要收下这串珠子，按我的吩咐处置它。为此再起一誓。』我便凭众天使起了誓；她又说：『这串珠子终有一日要引一个男人到世界尽头之泉，却引不了女人；说实话，女人若从女人手里得着它，或得着同它一样的串子（那样的还有几串），可以当个信物，却做不成她的护符和引路石；这话告诉你，免得你仗着它去寻泉。因为我吩咐你：把它送给一个你爱的男人——深爱着的男人，在他最需要的时候；只是他不可是你的血亲。我压给你的就这一件事；办到了，你必兴旺；不办，你必遭祸。现在再告诉你：你我今日相逢不是偶然，是我一手引来的；我费了力气把你引到我身边，因为我知道，女人里只有你成得了这件事。现在你回客栈去，拿这个验证我的预言：领你来的那位精明商人正等着你回去，好讨你许给他的东西。你若回到客栈见着他，他拉你的手领你上床——克莱门特要明天傍晚才回来——你就管我叫卖嘴皮子的骗子；你若到家时，那里的人对你说，商人瓦莱里乌斯有生死攸关的信使远召，匆匆骑马走了，你就把我当真正的智者信吧。去吧，别了。』

「我提心吊胆回了客栈；在门口遇见管事的，他对我说：『夫人，商人瓦莱里乌斯来寻过您，说定要等您回来；可等着等着，来了个人同他密谈；随后他便叫马，又吩咐我告诉您，夫人，他因一桩生死攸关的事被召走了，五天后回来吻您的手。』

===Original===

“So I wotted that the woman had spoken sooth, and was wise and foreseeing, and something of a dread of her came upon me. But the next even back cometh Clement, and the day after we rode away from Sarras the Holy, and Valerius I saw never again. And as to the beads, there is nought to tell of them till they came into thine hands; and something tells me that it was the will of the Wise Woman that to no other hands they should come.”

Here Katherine made an end, and both the men sat pondering her tale a little. As for Ralph, he deemed it certain that the Wise Woman of Sarras would be none other than she who had taught lore to the Lady of Abundance; but why she should have meant the beads for him he wotted not. Again he wondered how it was that the Lady of Abundance should have given the beads to Ursula, and whether she knew that they had no might to lead her to the Well at the World’s End. And yet further he wondered how it was that Ursula, unholpen by the talisman, should have done so much to bring him to the Well; yea, and how she was the first to see it while he slept. But his heart told him that whereas he was seeking the Well with her, she must needs come thither with him, unless they were both cast away; withal Katherine looked at him and said: “Yea, dear lord, I wot what thou art thinking of; but couldest thou have left her, when thou hadst once found her again, Well or no Well?” “Sooth is that,” said Ralph, “yet for all that she hath done without help of talisman or witchcraft is she the more worshipful and the dearer.”

===Chinese===

「这下我知道那妇人句句是真，确是智慧而有先见；我心里对她生出几分敬畏。第二天傍晚克莱门特回来了，再过一天我们便骑马离开了圣城萨拉斯；瓦莱里乌斯，我再没见过。至于那串珠子，到你手里之前没什么可讲的；我心里有个声音说，智慧妇人的意愿就是：它不落进任何别人的手里。」

凯瑟琳讲完了；两个男人坐着，把她的故事回味了一会儿。拉尔夫则断定：萨拉斯的那位智慧妇人，必是当年传授丰饶夫人学问的同一个她；只是她为何属意把珠子给他，他想不透。他又纳闷，丰饶夫人怎么把珠子给了厄休拉，她是否知道珠子无力引她去世界尽头之泉。他还进一步纳闷：厄休拉没有护符相助，怎么竟做了那么多、把他带到泉边；是啊，他睡着的时候，还是她第一个望见了泉水。可他的心告诉他：既是同她一起寻泉，她便必然同他一起到达——除非两人一同殒灭。这时凯瑟琳望着他，说：「是啊，亲爱的主公，我知道你在想什么；可你既然又找到了她，还离得开吗——有泉没泉都一样？」「确实，」拉尔夫说，「正因她不靠护符、不靠法术做成了一切，她才更可敬、更可爱。」

Then speech came into Clement’s mouth, and he said: “Wife, it is as I said before, when thy gossip had just departed from us. It was meet enough that thou shouldst have loved him better than me; but now it is even less to be undone than ever, when he has come back bringing with him a woman so valiant and lovely as is my Lady Ursula. So thou must e’en take the life that fate hath sent thee.” Katherine laughed through her tears, and said: “Withal, goodman, I have been no bad wife to thee. And moreover, look thou, gossip dear: when I was wandering about with Clement amongst many perils, when our need seemed sorest, then would I think to give the beads to Clement; but so soon as I began to speak to him of the Well at the World’s End he would belittle the tale of it, and would bid me look to it if it were not so, that where the world endeth the clouds begin.”

As she spoke, Ralph lifted up his hand and pointed to the window, and said: “Friends, as we were speaking of all these marvels we were forgetting the need of Upmeads and the day of battle; and lo now! how the dawn is widening and the candles fading.”

Scarce were the words out of his mouth, when on the quietness of the beginning of day brake out the sound of four trumpets, which were sounding in the four quarters of the town, and blowing men to the gathering. Then rose up both Ralph and Clement and took their weapons, and they kissed Katherine and went soberly out-a-doors into the marketplace, where already weaponed men were streaming in to the muster.

这时克莱门特开了口，说：「老婆，事情同我以前说的一样——那时你的教亲刚离开咱们。你爱他胜过爱我，倒也合情合理；如今他带回来一位这样勇敢可爱的女子，我的厄休拉夫人，这事更没得挽回了。你只好认了命运给你的日子。」凯瑟琳含泪而笑，说：「话说回来，当家的，我做你的老婆也不坏嘛。还有，你听着，亲爱的教亲：当年我跟着克莱门特在重重险境里漂泊，眼看最难的时候，我就想把珠子给克莱门特；可我刚一跟他提起世界尽头之泉，他就把那故事贬得一文不值，还叫我想想是不是这个理——世界到头的地方，云彩才开头。」

她正说着，拉尔夫抬手指向窗户，说：「朋友们，讲着这些奇事，咱们倒忘了阿普米兹的危难和决战的日子；看哪！曙光正在铺开，烛火渐渐暗了。」

话音未落，破晓的静寂中骤然响起四支号角——在城的四方吹响，召集人们集合。拉尔夫和克莱门特双双起身，操起兵器，吻过凯瑟琳，沉稳地出门走到市场上；带兵器的人已络绎不绝地涌向集合地。
