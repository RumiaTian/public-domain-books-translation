## XIX / 十九
===Original===

Ralph Readeth in a Book Concerning the Well at the World’s End

Fairly written was that book, with many pictures therein, the meaning of which Ralph knew not; but amongst them was the image of the fair woman whom he had holpen at the want-ways of the wood, and but four days ago was that, yet it seemed long and long to him. The book told not much about the Well at the World’s End, but much it told of a certain woman whom no man that saw her could forbear to love: of her it told that erewhile she dwelt lonely in the wildwood (though how she came there was not said) and how a king’s son found her there and brought her to his father’s kingdom and wedded her, whether others were lief or loth: and in a little while, when the fame of her had spread, he was put out of his kingdom and his father’s house for the love of her, because other kings and lords hankered after her; whereof befell long and grievous war which she abode not to the end, but sought to her old place in the wildwood; and how she found there another woman a sorceress, who made her her thrall; and tormented her grievously with toil and stripes. And how again there came a knight to that place who was seeking the Well at the World’s End, and bore her away with him; and how the said knight was slain on the way, and she was taken by tyrants and robbers of the folk: but these being entangled in her love fought amongst themselves and she escaped, and went seeking that Well, and found it at the long last, and drank thereof, and throve ever after: and how she liveth yet, and is become the servant of the Well to entangle the seekers in her love and keep them from drinking thereof; because there was no man that beheld her, but anon he was the thrall of her love, and might not pluck his heart away from her to do any of the deeds whereby men thrive and win the praise of the people.

===Chinese===

拉尔夫读一部关于世界尽头之井的书

那部书字迹娟秀，里面有许多图画，画的意思拉尔夫不懂；但画中有一幅，正是他在林中岔路口搭救过的那位美妇人的像——那才不过四天前，在他却仿佛隔了许久许久。书里讲世界尽头之井的不多，讲的却多半是一位女人：凡是见过她的男人，没有能不爱她的。书里说，她从前独自住在荒林里（怎么到那里去的，没有说）；一位王子如何在那里寻见她，带她回父亲的国，娶了她——不问旁人愿不愿；不多时，她的艳名传开，他为爱她，被逐出国门、逐出父亲的家园，因为别的国王王爷都馋诞她；由此起了经年累月的惨烈战事；她没等到战事终局，自回荒林旧地去了。又说，她在那里撞见另一个女人，一个女巫，收她做了奴隶，用苦役同鞭子狠狠折磨她。又说，后来又有一位骑士行到那里，正寻世界尽头之井，把她带走了；那骑士在路上被杀，她落到暴君同强人手里；可那班人缠在她的爱里，自相残杀，她脱身出来，一路去寻那井，终于寻着了，喝了井水，从此长盛不衰。又说，她至今还活着，做了那井的仆役，用爱纠缠寻井的人，不叫他们喝井水：因为没有一个男人见了她不当即做她爱情的奴隶，再不能把心从她身上拔出来，去干任何使人兴旺、赢得世人称誉的事业。

===Original===

Ralph read on and on till the short night waned, and the wax lights failed one after the other, and the windows of the hall grew grey and daylight came, and the throstles burst out a-singing at once in the castle pleasance, and the sun came up over the wood, and the sound of menfolk bestirring themselves afield came to his ears through the open windows; and at last he was done with the tale, and the carline came not near him though the sun had clomb high up the heavens. As for Ralph, what he had read was sweet poison to him; for if before he was somewhat tormented by love, now was his heart sick and sore with it. Though he knew not for certain whether this tale had to do with the Lady of the Forest, and though he knew not if the Lady who should come to the castle were even she, yet he needs must deem that so it was, and his heart was weary with love, and his manhood seemed changed.

===Chinese===

拉尔夫一页页读下去，直到短夜将尽，蜡烛一支接一支燃尽，堂里的窗子泛灰，天亮了，堡园里的画眉蓦地齐声唱起来，太阳升上树林，田野里人们起身做活的声响从敞开的窗子传进他耳朵；末了他把故事读完了；太阳已爬得老高，老妪却没有到他跟前来。至于拉尔夫，他读的东西于他是甜蜜的毒药：先前他已多少被爱情磨着，如今他的心为它病了、痛了。虽说他不确知这故事说的是不是那位林中夫人，虽说他不确知要来堡里的夫人是不是就是她，他却不能不这样认定；他的心被爱情磨得倦了，他的丈夫气概仿佛都变了。
