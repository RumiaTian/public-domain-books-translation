## XVII “Danger!” / XVII 「危险！」

===Original===

Before leaving Plymouth French had wired to Mrs. Berlyn, asking for an interview for the following Monday morning. On reaching the Yard he found a reply. If he called round about half past ten the lady would see him. He rang his bell for Sergeant Carter.

===Chinese===

离开普利茅斯之前，弗伦奇便给伯林太太拍了电报，求下星期一上午一见。他一到苏格兰场就看到了回电：倘若他十点半前后登门，那位太太愿意见他。他摇铃叫来卡特警士。

===Original===

“I shall want you with me today, Carter,” he explained. “Have a taxi ready at ten-fifteen.”

As they were driving toward Chelsea he explained the business.

===Chinese===

「卡特，今天我要你跟我一道去。」他交代道。「十点一刻备好一辆出租汽车。」

车子往切尔西驶去的路上，他把这桩差事交代了一遍。

===Original===

“It’s to help me to shadow a woman, a Mrs. Phyllis Berlyn. Lives at 70b Park Walk. There’s her photograph. When I go in, you keep this taxi and be ready to pick me up when I want you.”

===Chinese===

「是帮我盯一个女人的梢——菲莉斯·伯林太太，住在公园巷70b号。这是她的照片。我进去的时候，你守着这辆车子，我要用车随叫随到。」

===Original===

If he were to tap a possible SOS, he must begin by finding out if his victim had a telephone. He therefore got out at the end of Park Walk, and passing the house, turned into an entry leading to the lane which ran along behind the row. The absence of wires front and rear showed that the house was not connected up. Then he went to the door and knocked. Mrs. Berlyn received him at once.

===Chinese===

要想截获她可能发出的求救信号，第一步先得弄清目标家里有没有电话。于是他在公园巷巷口下了车，走过那宅子，拐进一条过道，通到沿这排屋后身的一条小巷。屋前屋后都不见电线，可见这宅子并没装电话。随后他才上前叫门。伯林太太立刻接待了他。

===Original===

“I am very sorry, madame,” he began, gravely, “to have to come on serious and unpleasant business. In my enquiries into the death of Mr. Pyke certain facts have come out. These facts require an explanation, and they point to you as being perhaps the only person who can give it. I have, therefore, called to ask you some questions, but I have to warn you that you are not bound to answer them, as in certain eventualities anything you say might be used against you.”

===Chinese===

「夫人，非常抱歉，」他神情凝重地开口道，「我这次登门，是为着一桩严重而不愉快的事。在我调查派克先生死亡一案的过程中，查明了若干事实。这些事实需要有人解释，而它们指向您——也许唯有您能作出解释。因此我前来向您提几个问题。但我必须提醒您，您没有义务回答，因为在某些情况下，您所说的任何话都可能被用来对您不利。」

===Original===

Mrs. Berlyn looked startled.

“Whatever do you mean, Inspector?” she demanded. “You don’t mean against me personally, I suppose, but against my husband? I do not forget the terrible suggestion you made.”

===Chinese===

伯林太太显出吃惊的样子。

「探长，您这话究竟是什么意思？」她质问道。「您总不会是指对我本人不利吧——我猜，是针对我丈夫的？您先前那个可怕的猜疑，我可没有忘记。」

===Original===

“I mean against you personally, madam. As I say, I want an explanation of certain facts. If you care to give it I shall hear it with attention, but if you would prefer to consult a solicitor first, you can do so.”

===Chinese===

「正是对您本人，夫人。我说了，我要的是对某些事实的解释。您若愿意作出解释，我会凝神细听；您若想先请教律师，也悉听尊便。」

===Original===

“Good gracious! Inspector, you are terrifying me! You are surely not suggesting that you suspect me of complicity in this awful crime?”

“I make no accusations. All I want is answers to my questions.”

===Chinese===

「天哪！探长，您这是要吓死我啊！您该不会是说，您怀疑我同这桩可怕的罪行有牵连吧？」

「我并不指控谁。我只要我的问题得到回答。」

===Original===

Mrs. Berlyn grew slowly dead white. She moistened her dry lips.

“This is terrible,” she said in low tones. French had some twinges of conscience, for, after all, he was only bluffing. He recognised, however, that the greater the effect he produced, the more likely he was to get what he wanted. He therefore continued his third degree.

===Chinese===

伯林太太的脸色渐渐变得死白。她舔了舔干涩的嘴唇。

「这太可怕了。」她低声说。弗伦奇良心上颇有些不安，毕竟他不过是在唬人。然而他也明白，唬得越狠，越有可能得到自己要的东西。于是他继续他的连番逼问。

===Original===

“If you are innocent, madam, I can assure you that you have nothing to fear,” he encouraged her, thereby naturally increasing her perturbation. “Now would you like to answer my questions or not?”

===Chinese===

「夫人，您若是清白的，我向您保证，您没有什么可怕的。」他这番宽慰的话，自然反倒加重了她的慌乱。「那么现在，您愿不愿意回答我的问题？」

===Original===

She did not hesitate. “I have no option,” she exclaimed in somewhat shaky tones. “If I do not do so your suspicions will be confirmed. Ask what you like. I have nothing to hide and therefore cannot give myself away.”

===Chinese===

她没有迟疑。「我别无选择。」她的声调有些发颤。「我不回答，您的怀疑就算坐实了。您想问什么就问吧。我没什么见不得人的事，也就谈不上露什么马脚。」

===Original===

“I am glad to hear you say so,” French declared, grimly. “First I want you to give me a more detailed account of your relations with Colonel Domlio.”

===Chinese===

「您肯这样讲，我很高兴。」弗伦奇冷冷说道。「首先，我要您更详细地谈谈您同多姆利奥上校的交往。」

===Original===

“Why,” the lady explained, “I told you all about that on your last visit. Colonel Domlio was very friendly, exceedingly friendly, I might say. But we had no relations”﻿—she stressed the word﻿—“in the sense which your question seems to indicate.”

===Chinese===

「咦，」这位太太解释道，「上回您来的时候，我不是原原本本都告诉您了吗？多姆利奥上校同我们很友好——可以说是极其友好。但我们之间并无——」她特意咬重了这个字眼——「『关系』，绝没有您这个问题似乎暗示的那种意思的关系。」

===Original===

“How did your friendship begin?”

“Through my husband. He and Colonel Domlio were old friends and it was natural that we should see something of him. He visited at our house and we at his.”

===Chinese===

「你们的友谊是怎么开始的？」

「通过我丈夫。他同多姆利奥上校是老朋友，我们两家有些来往是自然而然的事。他到我们家做客，我们也到他府上拜访。」

===Original===

“That was when you first went to Ashburton, was it not?”

“Not only then. It was so all the time I lived there.”

===Chinese===

「那是在你们初到阿什伯顿的时候，对不对？」

「不光是那时候。我住在那儿的整个期间，来往一直如此。」

===Original===

“But I don’t mean that. I understand that about four months before the tragedy your friendship became much more intense, if I may use the word?”

===Chinese===

「但我指的不是这个。据我了解，惨案发生前大约四个月，你们的友谊变得亲热得多了——请恕我使用这样的字眼？」

===Original===

“Intense is certainly not the word, but it is true that we met more frequently after the time you mention. I thought I had explained that. It was then that my husband became dissatisfied about my perfectly harmless friendship with Mr. Pyke. As I told you, Mr. Pyke and I decided to see less of each other. I was therefore thrown more on my own resources and frankly I was bored. I filled a little more of the time with Colonel Domlio than formerly. That is all.”

===Chinese===

「『亲热』二字绝对谈不到，不过在您说的那个时候以后，我们见面确实勤了些。这一点我自以为已经解释过了。正是在那个时候，我丈夫对我同派克先生那段全然无害的友谊起了不满。我告诉过您，派克先生和我商量好彼此少来往。这样一来，我的时间便更多要靠自己打发，坦白说，我闷得慌。我便比先前多匀些时间给多姆利奥上校。仅此而已。」

===Original===

“Who began this increased intimacy?”

“Our intimacy was not increased. We saw more of each other﻿—a very different thing. I began it; in this way. In London I heard some lectures on insect life. I was interested in the subject and I asked Colonel Domlio to let me see his collection. We began to talk about it, and it ended in my going out with him occasionally to look for specimens on the moor and also in my helping him to arrange them afterwards. That was the beginning and end of what you are pleased to call our ‘intimacy.’ ”

===Chinese===

「这份升温的亲密，是谁先起的头？」

「我们的亲密并没有升温。我们只是彼此见面多了——这完全是两码事。开头的是我；经过是这样：在伦敦的时候，我听过几场讲昆虫生活的讲座。我对这个题目有了兴趣，便请多姆利奥上校让我看看他的收藏。我们开始谈论这个，到后来，我便偶尔随他到荒原上去采集标本，事后还帮他整理。您乐于称作『亲密』的那段交往，从头到尾就这么多。」

===Original===

The look of fright had left Mrs. Berlyn’s eyes and she was speaking now with more of her usual assurance.

===Chinese===

伯林太太眼里的惧意已经褪去，此刻说话又恢复了平日那几分笃定。

===Original===

“You sprained your ankle one day?”

“I twisted it slightly. It was painful for a few hours, but not really much the worse.”

===Chinese===

「有一天您扭伤了脚踝？」

「只是轻轻崴了一下。疼了几个钟头，其实并不怎么碍事。」

===Original===

“You fell?”

“I did not fall. I should have done so, but Colonel Domlio sprang forward and caught me and helped me down on to the grass. In a few minutes I was better.”

===Chinese===

「您摔倒了？」

「我没有摔倒。本来是要摔的，可多姆利奥上校一个箭步抢上来扶住了我，搀我到草地上坐下。不消几分钟我就好多了。」

===Original===

“Now, Mrs. Berlyn,” and French’s voice was very grave, “what you have to do is to convince me that that fall into Colonel Domlio’s arms really was an accident.”

===Chinese===

「好了，伯林太太，」弗伦奇的嗓音十分凝重，「现在您要做的，是叫我相信，那一跤跌进多姆利奥上校怀里，实在是个意外。」

===Original===

For a moment the lady looked at him uncomprehendingly; then she flushed angrily.

“Oh,” she cried with a gesture of disgust, “how dare you? This is insufferable! I shall not answer you. If you are coming here to insult me I shall apply for protection to your superiors at Scotland Yard.”

===Chinese===

这位太太怔怔地望了他片刻，一脸茫然；随即气红了脸。

「哦！」她厌恶地一挥手，嚷了起来，「您怎么敢这样？这真是叫人忍无可忍！我不回答您了。您若是上这儿来侮辱我，我就向苏格兰场您的上司们要求保护。」

===Original===

“If I were you I should keep away from Scotland Yard as long as you can,” French advised, drily. “In a case like this heroics will not help you any. Tell me, did you know there was a photographer watching the incident?”

===Chinese===

「我要是您，就离苏格兰场越远越好，能避多久避多久。」弗伦奇干巴巴地进言。「这样的案子，耍这套把戏对您没有半点好处。告诉我，有个照相的人在场看着那一幕，您可知道？」

===Original===

“No,” she answered sullenly, while again her face showed fear.

“You knew there wasn’t?”

“I didn’t know anything about it.”

===Chinese===

「不知道。」她闷声答道，脸上又现出惧色。

「您原先就知道没有？」

「这事我什么都不知道。」

===Original===

“But you are not surprised to hear of the photographer?”

“I am. At least I should be if you assured me one was there.”

===Chinese===

「可是听说有那么个照相的人，您并不惊讶？」

「我惊讶呀。至少，您若能肯定当时真有那么一个人，我是该惊讶的。」

===Original===

“Did you know that the handwriting of Colonel Domlio’s letter has been identified?”

Once again the colour ebbed away from her face.

“What letter?” she cried, faintly. “I don’t know what letter you mean or what you are talking about. You have made me quite confused with your questions. I scarcely know what I am saying.”

===Chinese===

「您可知道，多姆利奥上校那封信的笔迹已经鉴定出来了？」

她脸上的血色又一次退了下去。

「什么信？」她声音发虚地叫道。「我不知道您指的是哪封信，也不知道您在说什么。您这些问题把我全弄糊涂了。我简直不知道自己在说些什么。」

===Original===

French felt that he had got the effect he wanted. He therefore reassured her by a few innocuous questions, then with a change of manner he apologised for having given unnecessary annoyance and took his leave.

===Chinese===

弗伦奇觉得要收的效果已经收到了。于是他换上几个无关痛痒的问题安抚她，随后又转了口气，为平白无故让她受惊赔了不是，起身告辞。

===Original===

The taxi was standing far down the street with the bonnet open and the driver bending over the engine. French got in and he and Carter sat watching the house.

For half an hour they waited; then Mrs. Berlyn appeared, and walking to King’s Road, turned in the direction of Sloane Square. Presently she hailed a taxi, causing French to congratulate himself on his prevision.

===Chinese===

那辆出租汽车停在街那头远远的地方，引擎盖敞开着，司机正俯身对着机器。弗伦奇上了车，同卡特一起坐在车里盯住那宅子。

他们等了半个钟头；伯林太太出来了，走到国王路，朝斯隆广场方向拐去。不多时她招手叫了一辆出租汽车，弗伦奇不由得暗自庆幸自己未雨绸缪。

===Original===

Mrs. Berlyn drove to Victoria, and hurriedly paying off their own man, the detectives followed her into the station. With a rapid look round she made her way to the telephone boxes and disappeared into one of them.

“I’ll drop out here, Carter,” French said. “You stick to the woman and as far as possible keep in touch with the Yard.”

===Chinese===

伯林太太的车开到维多利亚车站。两位侦探匆匆打发了自己的车，跟进站去。她飞快地四下一望，径直奔电话亭，闪身进了其中一间。

「我在这儿下车，卡特。」弗伦奇说。「你盯住那个女人，尽量同苏格兰场保持联络。」

===Original===

Approaching the boxes, French slipped into a convenient doorway and watched until Mrs. Berlyn reappeared. As soon as she was out of sight he entered the box she had left.

===Chinese===

弗伦奇蹭到电话亭近旁，闪进一个得劲的门洞里守着，直到伯林太太重新露面。等她走得没了影，他便钻进她方才用过的那间。

===Original===

“Inspector from Scotland Yard speaking,” he told the operator. “Keep the number of that last call. It’s wanted in connection with a murder case. I’ll get you the authority to divulge. Now give me Scotland Yard, please.”

===Chinese===

「我是苏格兰场的探长。」他对接线员说。「把刚才那通电话的号码记下来。这是一桩凶案要用的。准予透露的公文我随后就补来。现在请给我接苏格兰场。」

===Original===

He put through the request for the number, then returned to the Yard to wait for the reply. After a short delay he received both number and name: Thomas Ganope, news agent and tobacconist, 27 Oakley Street, off Russell Street.

In half an hour he reached the place. Ganope’s was a small, untidy shop, and Ganope a ruffianly-looking man with purple cheeks and a cast in his left eye. He was the only occupant of the shop.

===Chinese===

他办妥调取号码的申请，回苏格兰场等候回音。没过多久，号码和户名都到了：托马斯·加诺普，报贩兼烟贩，橡树街 27 号，在罗素街旁。

半个钟头后，他到了那地方。加诺普的铺子又小又乱，加诺普本人是个面目粗横的汉子，两颊紫红，左眼斜视。店里只有他一个人。

===Original===

“Can I use your telephone?” French asked, laying a shilling on the counter.

“Sure.”

===Chinese===

「能借用一下您的电话吗？」弗伦奇问着，把一枚先令搁在柜台上。

「行嘞。」

===Original===

French rang up his wife to say that he had mislaid Mr. Walker’s address and could she let him have it again, a code message designed for such occasions and to which no attention was paid, but which enabled him to use a telephone without arousing suspicion, as well as a writing pad, should such be available. For in this case his quick eye had seen such a pad on the instrument and from many a pad he had read the last message to be written from the impression left on the paper. On chance, therefore, he made a pretense of noting the mythical Mr. Walker’s address, and removing the top sheet, put it in his pocketbook. Then he turned to Mr. Ganope.

===Chinese===

弗伦奇给太太挂了个电话，说他把沃克先生的地址弄丢了，问她能不能再给自己一遍。这是专为这类场合设下的暗语，那边从不去理会，它却能让弗伦奇不惹疑心地用上电话——倘若现场碰巧有拍纸簿，还能顺带用上一本。因为这一回，他那双利眼已经瞧见电话机上正搁着这么一本，而从前不知多少本拍纸簿，他都凭纸页上留下的压痕，读出过最后写下的那条口信。于是他存着侥幸，装模作样记下那位子虚乌有的沃克先生的地址，揭走最上面那一页，揣进随身的记事本。然后他才转向加诺普先生。

===Original===

“Say,” he said, confidentially, “what would you charge for taking in telephone messages and sending them to an address? Private, you know.”

Mr. Ganope looked him over keenly with one of his shrewd little eyes.

===Chinese===

「我说，」他一副交心的口气，「替人收电话口信、再送到一个地址去，你收多少钱？是私事，你懂的。」

加诺普先生用一只精明的小眼睛，锐利地把他上上下下打量了一遍。

===Original===

“A bob a message, if it’s near by.”

“That’s a lot. Do you never do it for less?”

Mr. Ganope seemed disgusted.

===Chinese===

「一条口信一个先令，要是地方近的话。」

「这价钱可不小。就没有便宜些的时候？」

加诺普先生一脸嫌恶。

===Original===

“If you can get anyone to do it for less you’d better go to them,” he advised, sourly.

===Chinese===

「你要是能找到肯少收的，趁早找他去。」他酸溜溜地劝道。

===Original===

“I might manage the money if I was sure the thing would be done right,” French went on. “How do you send out the messages? I mean is your arrangement reliable? Do you do it yourself or have you a messenger?”

“Wot do you tyke me for, mister? Do you think the shop would run itself while I was away? You don’t need to worry. You pay your bob and you’ll get your message all right.”

===Chinese===

「钱倒好说，只要事情办得牢靠。」弗伦奇接着说。「口信都是怎么送出去的？我是说，你这套章程靠得住吗？你自己送，还是雇着送信的孩子？」

「你拿俺当什么人，先生？你以为俺一走，这铺子自己会转起来？你甭操心。先令照付，口信保险送到。」

===Original===

“Not good enough for me. I want to know what kind of messenger you’d send before I trust my business to you.”

“See ’ere,” the man declared, “I’ve been doing this business for long enough to know all about ’ow it’s done. I’ve a good boy, if you must know. You give ’im a penny or two a time if you’re nervous, and you needn’t be afryde but you’ll get all there’s for you.”

===Chinese===

「这我还不能放心。把事情托付给你之前，我总得知道你会派个什么样的送信人。」

「你听着，」那汉子声明道，「这行当我干得够久，个中门道俺全知道。你非要问的话，俺有个不赖的孩子。你要是不放心，一回赏他一两个便士，该你收的口信保险一条不少。」

===Original===

French laid five shillings on the counter.

“Right,” he said. “There’s for the first five messages. Send to Mr. James Hurley, care of Mr. William Wright, tobacconist, corner of Bedford Place and Ivy Street.”

“I know it,” declared Ganope, pocketing the money.

===Chinese===

弗伦奇把五枚先令摆上柜台。

「好，」他说。「这是头五条口信的钱。收件人詹姆斯·赫尔利先生，由烟贩威廉·赖特先生转交，铺子在贝德福德坊与常春藤街的拐角上。」

「那地方俺知道。」加诺普说着，把钱揣进了兜。

===Original===

Mr. William Wright was a distant connection of Mrs. French’s and French knew that he would help him in the matter. He nodded to Ganope and walked across to Ivy Street.

“Hullo, Joe! Some time since we’ve seen you here,” was Mr. Wright’s greeting. “Come in behind and let’s hear the news.”

===Chinese===

威廉·赖特先生是弗伦奇太太的一位远亲，弗伦奇知道，这件事他定会帮忙。他朝加诺普点点头，步行到常春藤街去。

「哟，乔！可有些日子没见你上这儿来了。」赖特先生这样招呼。「到后头来，听听新鲜事儿。」

===Original===

“I want you to do me a good turn, William,” French answered. “There’s a boy I want to get hold of and I’ve fixed it that he’ll come here asking for Mr. Hurley. Will you put him on to me when he turns up? That’s all.”

“Surely, Joe,” and Mr. Wright turned the conversation to more intimate matters.

===Chinese===

「威廉，我要请你帮个忙。」弗伦奇答道。「有个孩子我想找他，我已经安排好了，他会到这儿来讨见赫尔利先生。他来了，你就把他引到我这儿来。就这么多。」
