## XV Blackmail / XV 敲诈

===Original===

“Have you been able to get the pump, Sergeant?” asked French as he reached the police station that afternoon.

===Chinese===

「水泵弄到了吗，警士？」那天下午，弗伦奇一回到警察局便问道。

===Original===

“I’ve got the loan of one, Mr. French, or at least I’ll get it first thing tomorrow. From a quarry close by. It’s a rotary hand-pump, and Mr. Glenn, the manager, tells me that it will throw far faster than anything we’ll want.”

===Chinese===

「借到一台，弗伦奇先生，至少明天一早准能到手。就在附近一家采石场借的。是台手摇回转泵，经理格伦先生告诉我，它出水的快慢，远远够咱们使的。」

===Original===

“We shall have to fix it down in the well?”

===Chinese===

「咱们得把它安装在井里？」

===Original===

“Yes, the well’s forty-two feet deep. It’s thirty to the water and there’s twelve feet of water. But there’ll be no trouble about that. The beams that carry the old pump will take it, too.”

===Chinese===

「是的，这口井深四十二英尺。到水面是三十英尺，井里还存着十二英尺的水。不过这不成问题，架着旧泵的那几根横梁，照样吃得住它。」

===Original===

“You think they’re strong enough?”

===Chinese===

「您看那些横梁够结实？」

===Original===

“We’ll just have to try them.”

===Chinese===

「试过才知道。」

===Original===

“What about ladders?”

===Chinese===

「梯子呢？」

===Original===

“I’ve got a fifty-foot length of rope ladder from the same quarry.”

===Chinese===

「我从那家采石场还借来一条五十英尺长的绳梯。」

===Original===

“Good. What time can we start tomorrow?”

===Chinese===

「好。明天几点能动身？」

===Original===

“I shall have the pump by half past eight.”

===Chinese===

「八点半我就能把泵取到手。」

===Original===

“Then we should be at the colonel’s shortly after nine.”

===Chinese===

「那么九点刚过，我们就该到上校家了。」

===Original===

This time French thought it would be wise to have Domlio present at their experiment. He therefore rang him up and made an appointment for nine-thirty.

===Chinese===

这一次，弗伦奇认为让多姆利奥在场看着他们做试验才是上策。于是他打电话过去，约在了九点半。

===Original===

Early next morning a heavily loaded car left Ashburton. In addition to the driver it contained French, Daw, and two constables in plain clothes, as well as a low, squat pump with detachable handle, an immense coil of armoured hose, another huge coil of rope ladder, and several tools and small parts.

===Chinese===

第二天清早，一辆满载的汽车驶出阿什伯顿。车上除司机外，坐着弗伦奇、道和两名便衣警察，还装着一台矮墩墩、手柄可卸的水泵，一大卷铠装水龙带，另外一大卷绳梯，以及若干工具和零配件。

===Original===

“Another rush to Klondyke,” said French, at which priceless pearl of humour Daw smiled and the plain clothes men guffawed heavily.

===Chinese===

「又一趟克朗代克大淘金。」弗伦奇说道。这颗幽默的无价明珠，换来了道的一笑和两名便衣的放声大笑。

===Original===

“I should have thought that tailor could have fixed up the ownership of the coat,” Daw remarked, presently. “Shouldn’t you, sir?”

===Chinese===

「我原以为那裁缝总能认出上装的主人呢。」过了一会儿，道说道，「您不这么看吗，先生？」

===Original===

“Of course, Sergeant. But we shall get it, all right, even if we have to do all the work ourselves. I thought it wasn’t worth troubling about. It’s pretty certain the coat is not the only thing that was thrown into the well and we shall get our identification from something else.”

===Chinese===

「当然认得出，警士。不过就算事事都得我们自己动手，这一层也终归能弄清。我觉得犯不上为它多费周章。那件上装十有八九不是唯一扔进井里的东西，身份总会从别的东西上认出来。」

===Original===

The car was run into the yard, unloaded, and dismissed, while French went to the hall door and asked for Colonel Domlio.

===Chinese===

汽车开进场院，卸下东西，打发走了；弗伦奇则走到正门，求见多姆利奥上校。

===Original===

“Sorry to trouble you at this hour, Colonel, but I want you to be present at a small experiment I am carrying out.” He watched the other keenly as he spoke. “Will you please come out into the yard, where I have left Sergeant Daw and some men?”

===Chinese===

「抱歉这个时候来打扰您，上校，不过我想请您在场看看我做的一个小小的试验。」他说话时，目光锐利地注视着对方。「请随我到场院里来，道警士和几个人我已留在那儿了。」

===Original===

Surprise showed on the colonel’s face, but not, so far as French could see, apprehension.

===Chinese===

上校脸上露出惊讶之色，但就弗伦奇看来，并无惧意。

===Original===

“This is very interesting, Inspector. I’m glad I’m at least being informed of what is taking place on my own ground. I shall certainly see what you are doing.”

===Chinese===

「这可真有意思，探长。我很高兴，自己地界上发生的事，总算还有人来知会一声。你们要做什么，我自然要看个明白。」

===Original===

As they turned the corner and the purpose of the visit became apparent to Domlio, his surprise seemed to deepen, but still there was no appearance of uneasiness. The police had lifted the cover of the well and were getting the pump rigged. Coombe and Mee had joined the others and stood speechlessly regarding the preparations.

===Chinese===

拐过墙角，此来的目的在多姆利奥眼里一明，他的惊讶似乎更深了一层，却仍不见半点不安的迹象。警察们已掀起井盖，正在架设水泵。库姆和米也加入了人群，一声不响地望着这些准备。

===Original===

“Ah, an invasion? I presume, Inspector, you have adequate authority for these somewhat unusual proceedings?”

===Chinese===

「啊，这是入侵么？我姑且问一句，探长，这般不同寻常的举动，您想必有充分的授权吧？」

===Original===

“I think you’ll find that’s all right, sir. With your permission we’re going to pump out the well.”

===Chinese===

「我想这一层您尽可放心，先生。如今就请您恩准，我们要把这口井的水抽干。」

===Original===

“The removal of the well cover and the pump rather suggested something of the kind, but for the moment I can’t quite recall the permission.”

===Chinese===

「井盖被撬、水泵进场，倒也早叫人猜着了几分。只是一时之间，我怎么也想不起几时给过这份恩准。」

===Original===

“I feel sure that under the circumstance you won’t withhold it. Better lower that lantern with the candle, Sergeant, before you send a man down. We want to be sure the air is good.”

===Chinese===

「我确信在此情形之下，您是不会拒绝的。先把手点蜡烛的风灯放下去，警士，再叫人下去。得先弄准井里的空气没有问题。」

===Original===

“If it’s not an impertinence,” Domlio remarked, with ironic politeness, “I should be interested to know why you are not using the existing pump.”

===Chinese===

「倘若不算冒昧的话，」多姆利奥带着讥讽的客气说道，「我倒很想知道，你们为什么不用现成的那台泵。」

===Original===

“I didn’t think it was in working order. Is the well used?”

===Chinese===

「我看它早已不能用了。这口井还使用吗？」

===Original===

“An explanation, complete, no doubt, but scarcely satisfying. It did not occur to you to try it?”

===Chinese===

「好一个解释，想必滴水不漏，只是难以令人心服。您竟没想到试一试？」

===Original===

“No, sir. Too noisy. But what about the well?”

===Chinese===

「没想到，先生。太吵了。可这井到底用不用？」

===Original===

“Ah yes, the well. The well is used﻿—in summer. We have a gravity supply from the hill behind the house, but it fails in summer; hence pumping from the well.”

===Chinese===

「啊，对了，这口井。这井是用——夏天用。宅子后面山上有自流水源引下来，可一到夏天便断了流，所以才从井里泵水。」

===Original===

This statement was very satisfactory to French. It cleared up a point which had been worrying him. If it were possible to get rid of the clothes by throwing them down the well, why had Pyke’s body not been disposed of in the same way? But now this was explained. The condition of the water in the following summer would have led to investigation.

===Chinese===

这个回答让弗伦奇大为满意。它解开了压在他心头的一个疑团：既然把衣裳扔进井里就能了事，派克的尸体为什么不照此办理？如今有了答案——来年夏天井水一变坏，势必招来追查。

===Original===

“Try the fixed pump, Sergeant. It may save us rigging the other.”

===Chinese===

「试试那台固定泵，警士。兴许能省得再架新的。」

===Original===

But a test showed that the valve leathers were dry and not holding, and they went on with their original program.

===Chinese===

可一试之下，阀门的皮垫早已干透，封不住水，众人便仍照原定计划进行。

===Original===

French had been puzzled by the colonel’s attitude. If beneath his cynical manner he were consumed by the anxiety which, were he guilty, he could scarcely help feeling, he was concealing it in a way that was little short of marvellous. However, the preparations would take time and it was impossible that if the man knew what would be found he could hide all signs of tension.

===Chinese===

上校的态度让弗伦奇捉摸不透。倘若在这副愤世的做派之下，他真怀着那份焦灼——他若当真有罪，这焦灼几乎难免——那他掩饰的功夫简直神了。不过，准备工作总要费些时间，倘若此人知道井底会捞出什么，他绝不可能不露出一丝紧张的痕迹。

===Original===

The candle, lowered to the surface of the water, burned clearly, showing that the air was fresh. The rope ladder was then made fast to the stonework and Sergeant Daw climbed down. Presently he returned to say that the beams on which the old pump rested were sound. The new pump was therefore lowered and one of the constables sent down to begin work.

===Chinese===

蜡烛吊到水面，依然烧得明亮，可见井下空气新鲜。绳梯随即牢牢拴在井壁石架上，道警士爬了下去。片刻他回上来说，架旧泵的横梁结实着呢。新泵便吊了下去，又派一名警察下去动工。

===Original===

Getting rid of the water turned out a bigger job than French had anticipated. Slowly the level dropped. At intervals the men spelled each other, French and Daw taking their turns. By lunchtime the water had gone down seven feet, though during the meal it rose six inches. After that they worked with renewed energy to get the remaining five feet six inches out before dusk.

===Chinese===

抽水这活计，比弗伦奇预想的繁重。水位缓缓下降。众人轮番换班，弗伦奇和道也各领了自己的班。到午饭时分，水下去七英尺——只是吃饭那阵又涨回六英寸。饭后大家鼓足干劲，赶在天黑前把剩下那五英尺六英寸抽干。

===Original===

“You have a second well, have you not, Colonel?” French enquired. “I noticed a pump near the kitchen door.”

===Chinese===

「您还有第二口井，对吧，上校？」弗伦奇问道，「我注意到厨房门口有台泵。」

===Original===

“Yes. We use it for drinking purposes. This is only good enough for washing the car and so on.”

===Chinese===

「是的。那口井的水供饮用。这口井的，洗洗汽车之类的还凑合。」

===Original===

On more than one occasion Domlio had protested against what he called the waste of his time in watching the work. But French insisted on his remaining till the search was complete.

===Chinese===

多姆利奥不止一次提出抗议，说眼睁睁看着做工、白白糟蹋他的光阴。可弗伦奇执意要他留到搜查结束为止。

===Original===

About four o’clock the water was so far lowered as to allow an investigation of the bottom, and the sergeant, squeezing past the man at the pump, went down with his electric torch. French, leaning over the wall, anxiously watched the flickering light. Then came the sergeant’s voice: “There’s a waistcoat and trousers and shoes here, Mr. French.”

===Chinese===

四点来钟，水位低得可以查看井底了。道警士侧身挤过守泵的人，带着手电筒下去。弗伦奇俯在井栏上，焦灼地望着那团晃动的光。随即传来警士的声音：「这儿有一件背心、一条裤子和一双鞋，弗伦奇先生。」

===Original===

“That all?” called French.

===Chinese===

「就这些？」弗伦奇朝下喊道。

===Original===

“That’s all that I see. I’ve got everything of any size, anyhow.”

===Chinese===

「眼下瞧见的就这些。反正像点样的东西都捞上来了。」

===Original===

“Well, tie them to the rope and we’ll pull them up.”

===Chinese===

「好，把它们拴在绳上，我们拉上来。」

===Original===

How Domlio would comport himself when he saw the clothes was now the important matter. French watched him keenly as the dripping bundle appeared and was carried to a bench in the garage.

===Chinese===

此刻要紧的是，多姆利奥见了这些衣裳会如何自处。那滴滴答答的一包东西吊出井口、被抬到车库长凳上的时候，弗伦奇目光锐利地盯住了他。

===Original===

Though the day’s work had prepared the man for some denouement, he certainly appeared to French to be genuinely amazed when the nature of the find was revealed.

===Chinese===

这一天的动静固然让他对某种收场有了思想准备，可捞到的东西一揭开真相，他在弗伦奇眼里分明是货真价实地惊呆了。

===Original===

“Good Heavens! Inspector! What does this mean?” he cried, squaring his shoulders. “Whose are these and how did you know they were there?”

===Chinese===

「天哪！探长！这是怎么回事？」他喊道，端起了肩膀，「这些东西是谁的？你们又怎么知道它们在这儿？」

===Original===

French turned to the plainclothes men. “Just wait outside the door, will you,” he said, then went on gravely to the other: “That is what I have to ask you, Colonel Domlio.”

===Chinese===

弗伦奇转向两名便衣。「到门外稍等一下，好吗。」他说罢，又神情严肃地转向对方：「这正是我要问您的，多姆利奥上校。」

===Original===

“Me?” The man’s sardonic calm was at last broken. “I know nothing about them. The thing is an absolute surprise to me. I swear it.” His face paled and he looked anxious and worried.

===Chinese===

「问我？」此人讥诮的镇定终于绷不住了。「我对这些一无所知。这件事对我来说全然出乎意料。我发誓。」他脸色发白，神色焦灼不安。

===Original===

“There is something I should tell you,” French continued. “On considering this Berlyn-Pyke case I formed a theory. I don’t say it is correct, but I formed it from the facts I had learnt. According to that theory you took out your car on the night of the tragedy, drove into Ashburton, picked up Mr. Pyke’s coat, waistcoat, trousers, shoes, and certain other things, brought them here and threw them into the well. A moment, please.” He raised his hand as Domlio would have spoken. “Rightly or wrongly, that was my theory. But there was a difficulty. You had stated to the sergeant that you had not gone out that night. I came here and found that that statement was not true. You had been out. Then I made further enquiries and learnt that you had taken out your car. You explained that, but I regret to say that I was unable to accept your explanation. I thought, however, that the presence or absence of these objects in the well would settle the matter. I looked at the well and saw that the cover had recently been moved. Two nights ago Sergeant Daw and I came out, and after trying with a line and fishhooks, we drew up a coat﻿—Pyke’s coat. Now, Colonel, if you wish to make a statement I will give it every consideration, but it is my duty again to warn you that anything you say may be used in evidence against you.”

===Chinese===

「有件事我该告诉您。」弗伦奇接着说，「在琢磨伯林—派克这桩案子时，我形成了一套推想。我不敢说它正确，但它是我根据已知的事实推出来的。按照那套推想，惨案当晚您把车开了出去，驶进阿什伯顿，取了派克先生的上装、背心、裤子、鞋子和另外几样东西，带回这里，扔进了井里。请稍等。」多姆利奥正要开口，他抬手止住。「不管对错，那便是我的推想。可其中有个疙瘩。您先前对警士说过，那晚没有出门。我到这里来，发现那句话不实。您出去过。随后我进一步查访，得知您开过车。您作了解释，但恕我直言，我无法接受。不过我想，井里这些东西的有无，终能一锤定音。我察看了水井，发现井盖新近挪动过。两天前的夜里，我和道警士来过，用钓线和钓钩试了一阵，钓上来一件上装——派克的上装。现在，上校，您若想作陈述，我必定逐一斟酌，但我有责任再次提醒您：您说的任何话，都可能成为对您不利的证据。」

===Original===

“What? Are you charging me with a crime?”

===Chinese===

「什么？你要指控我犯罪？」

===Original===

“Unless you can satisfy me of your innocence you will be charged with complicity in the murder of Stanley Pyke.”

===Chinese===

「除非您能使我相信您是清白的，否则您将以同谋谋杀斯坦利·派克的罪名受到指控。」

===Original===

The Colonel drew a deep breath.

===Chinese===

上校深深吸了一口气。

===Original===

“But, good Heavens! How can I satisfy you? I don’t even know what you have against me, except this extraordinary business which I can make neither head nor tail of. You must know more about it than you have said. Tell me the rest.”

===Chinese===

「可是，老天爷！我怎么能使你相信？我甚至不明白你凭什么抓着我的把柄，只除了这桩莫名其妙的怪事——我实在摸不着一点头脑。你一定还知道些没说出口的。把其余的都告诉我吧。」

===Original===

“You tell me this: Was your statement about the loss of the locket on that night true?”

===Chinese===

「那么您先告诉我：那晚关于丢失小画像的说法，是真是假？」

===Original===

Colonel Domlio did not reply. He seemed to be weighing some problem of overwhelming difficulty. French waited patiently, wondering how far his bluff would carry. At last the colonel spoke.

===Chinese===

多姆利奥上校没有作声。他似乎正在掂量一桩天大的难题。弗伦奇耐心等着，心里琢磨着自己这手虚张声势还能撑多远。上校终于开了口。

===Original===

“I have lied to the sergeant and to you, Inspector, with what I now believe was a mistaken motive. I have been turning over the matter in my mind and I see that I have no alternative but to tell you the truth now or to suffer arrest. Possibly things have gone so far that this cannot be avoided. At all events, I will tell you everything.”

===Chinese===

「我对警士和您都撒了谎，探长。如今想来，那动机是打错了算盘。这件事我在心里翻来覆去想了又想，明白摆在自己面前的只有两条路：现在说出真相，或者束手就擒。事情或许已闹到这一步，在所难免了。也罢，我把一切都告诉您。」

===Original===

“You are not forgetting my warning, Colonel Domlio?”

===Chinese===

「您没有忘记我的提醒吧，多姆利奥上校？」

===Original===

“I am not forgetting it. If I am acting foolishly it is my own lookout. I tried to put you off, Inspector, to save bringing Mrs. Berlyn’s name further into the matter, because, though there was nothing against her character, I was sure you would have bothered her with annoying questions. But, though I thought it right to lie with this object, I don’t feel like risking prison for it.”

===Chinese===

「我没有忘记。我若是行事愚蠢，后果也由我自家承担。我先前想把您敷衍过去，探长，为的是别再让伯林太太的名字牵进这桩案子——她的人品无可指摘，可我断定您准会用些缠人的问题去烦扰她。不过，虽说我以为为着这个目的撒谎不算错，我却犯不上为它去蹲监狱。」

===Original===

“I follow you,” said French.

===Chinese===

「我明白你的意思。」弗伦奇说。

===Original===

“You will remember then what I told you about Mrs. Berlyn, that she had been seeing a good deal first of Pyke and then of myself. I’m sorry to have to drag this in again, but otherwise you wouldn’t understand the situation.

===Chinese===

「那么您总还记得我同您说过的伯林太太的情形——她先是同派克过往甚密，后来又同我过从颇多。我很抱歉不得不再把这事扯进来，可不然您就摸不清这其中的局面。

===Original===

“About﻿—let me see﻿—four months before the tragedy Mrs. Berlyn came out here one afternoon. She said that she had been in London to a lecture on entomology and that she had been so much interested that she had read one or two books on the subject. She said that she knew I was doing some research in it and she wondered whether I would let her come and help me and so learn more. I naturally told her I should be delighted, and she began to come out here quite often. On different occasions she has accompanied me on the moor while I was searching for specimens, and she has spent several afternoons with me in my library mounting butterflies and learning to use the microscope. This went on until the day of the tragedy.”

===Chinese===

「大约——让我想想——惨案前四个月，一天下午，伯林太太到了我这儿。她说她去伦敦听了一场昆虫学的讲座，听得入了迷，又找了一两本这方面的书来读。她说她知道我正在从事这项研究，不知我能否让她来帮帮忙，也好多学一点。我自然告诉她乐意之至，此后她便常到这儿来。她不止一次陪我在荒原上采集标本，也曾在我的书房里消磨过好几个下午，跟我一起制作蝴蝶标本，学着使用显微镜。这样一直持续到惨案发生的那一天。」

===Original===

Colonel Domlio paused, squared his shoulders, and continued:

===Chinese===

多姆利奥上校顿了顿，端起肩膀，继续说道：

===Original===
