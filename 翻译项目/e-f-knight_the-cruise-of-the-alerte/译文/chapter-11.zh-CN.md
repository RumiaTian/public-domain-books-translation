## XI / 十一

===Original===
On the Road to Treasure Bay

===Chinese===
通往宝藏湾之路

===Original===
Having smoked our pipes we continued our journey. At first I was a very sanguine guide. I thought I should have no difficulty in recognising the ravine by which, nine years before, I had descended to the windward shore. But in this I was mistaken, for I found it extremely difficult to find my way to it again.

===Chinese===
抽完烟斗，我们继续赶路。起初我是个信心十足的向导。我以为，要认出九年前我循以下到向风岸去的那道裂谷，不会有什么难处。可在这一点上我错了：我发觉，要重新找到它，竟难乎其难。

===Original===
At any rate we were not now about to undergo the great toil, thirst, and danger that I had experienced during my former visit, for I at least knew some of the places to avoid, and this was a matter of importance. As we clambered along the edges of the mountains, looking for the pass, I was able to condemn at once as false passages several promising-looking routes, the vain trial of which had exhausted myself and my companions on my previous expedition.

===Chinese===
无论如何，这一回我们不至于再尝我上次来访时经历的那番辛劳、干渴与危险了，因为至少有哪些地方该避开，我是知道几处的，而这正是件要紧的事。我们沿着山缘手脚并用地攀行，一路寻找隘口；有几条看上去颇有希望的路，我可以当场断定为歧途——上一回探险，我们就是在这些路上白费气力，折腾得自己筋疲力尽。

===Original===
For instance, there was one long slope of volcanic debris of a ruddy colour which appeared from where we stood to join on to the green hills below and so to lead to the sandy beaches. The doctor was anxious to attempt this easy-looking way, but I knew the deceitful place too well of old. It tempts one further and further down, ever getting steeper, until one suddenly finds oneself at the edge of a frightful precipice, invisible from above, which compels one at great risk to retrace one’s painful steps to the heights.

===Chinese===
譬如，有一道长长的红褐色火山碎石坡，从我们站立之处望去，仿佛与下面的绿色冈峦连成一体，由此直通沙滩。医生很想试试这条看着轻省的路，可这骗人的地方我早年就领教透了。它诱着人越走越低，坡却越来越陡，直到人猛然发觉自己已站在一道骇人的绝壁边上——那壁从上面是望不见的——只好冒着大险，拖着酸楚的步子重新攀回高处。

===Original===
In the course of my first exploration we made so many false descents of these ravines and slopes, all terminating in precipices and driving us back again, that at last, finding no water, we were completely worn out and nearly perished of thirst. The heat is intense on Trinidad, especially at this season of the year, when the sun is vertical, and to climb these hot crags through the suffocating air is the most completely exhausting work I have ever undertaken. No other place within the tropics that I have visited has such an oppressive climate. I, therefore, determined to make no foolish experiments on this occasion, and not to attempt the descent until I was certain of my pass.

===Chinese===
头一回勘探时，我们在这些谷谷坡坡之间枉然下了一趟又一趟，趟趟尽头皆是绝壁，逼得我们原路折返；到后来寻不着水，人已困乏至极，险些渴死。特林达迪暑热逼人，尤其在一年的这个时节，太阳正当头顶；在令人窒息的空气里攀爬这些滚烫的危岩，是我平生做过的最耗人心力的活计。在我到过的热带地方里，没有一个的气候这般闷热难当。因此这一回我打定主意，再不做愚蠢的尝试，非到确有把握认准自己的隘口，决不贸然下坡。

===Original===
We crawled along the cliff-side for a long way, looking over at every point; but I could see nothing like my old ravine, and soon got fairly puzzled. At last we had followed the mountain ridges almost to the north end of the island, where the plateau of tree-ferns ceases, and where the mountains fall nearly perpendicularly into the sea, and culminate in needle-like peaks, affording no soil for vegetation of any description. So I knew that we had come too far and had passed the entrance to the ravine. We accordingly retraced our steps. We had now exhausted our bottle of water and were suffering from thirst. My old experience had taught me never, if possible, to be far from a stream while wandering over Trinidad. To toil among these arid rocks produces an insatiable thirst, and one’s strength fails if one is deprived of water even for a short time. Therefore as we saw below us a ravine that looked like a watercourse and which bore some resemblance to the one I was in search of, we decided to explore it. We lowered ourselves down from rock to rock for some way, and soon, to our delight, found a small issue of cool water. But this was not my ravine, for, on descending further, we came to the edge of one of the usual precipices, and we had to clamber up again.

===Chinese===
我们贴着崖边爬行了好长一段路，处处探头张望；可我望来望去，总望不见一点像旧日那道裂谷的影子，不久便当真犯了迷糊。到最后，我们顺着山脊几乎走到了岛的北端——那里树蕨高台已然到头，群山近乎笔直地插入海中，尽处的峰顶尖得像针，寸土不留，任何植物都无从着根。我便明白，我们走过了头，早已把裂谷的谷口抛在了身后。于是我们折返。这时瓶里的水已喝得精光，干渴正折磨着我们。往日的经验教会我：在特林达迪各地游走，只要办得到，千万不可远离溪流。在这些焦干的岩石间苦挣苦熬，会激起一种怎么也解不了的渴；断水哪怕只一小会儿，人的气力便要衰竭。因此，望见下面有一道谷，模样像条水道，又与我寻的那道有几分相像，我们便决意下去探查。我们从一块岩石缒到另一块岩石，下去了一程，不多时便欣喜地发现一小股清凉的泉水。然而这并不是我的那道谷：再往下走，便到了寻常那种绝壁的边上，我们只好又攀了上来。

===Original===
We attempted yet another ravine, which I did not recognise as *the* one, but which might prove to be it nevertheless, for I had to confess that I was quite at sea. This in time led us to a sloping shelf of rock overhanging another precipice. This shelf was extremely slippery, for the stream flowed over it in a thin film and it was covered with a short moss. This, too, exactly corresponds with a description in *Frank Mildmay*, that excellent guide to Trinidad, and what is said about the spot in that work may serve as a warning to any﻿—if such there ever be﻿—who may meditate a tour on this island. Two of Mildmay’s sailors had been lost while goat-hunting, so he sets forth in search of them. “I was some yards in advance of my companions,” he says, “and the dog a little distance from me, near the shelving part of a rock terminating in a precipice. The shelf I had to cross was about six or seven feet wide and ten or twelve long, with a very little inclined plane towards the precipice, so that I thought it perfectly safe. A small rill of water trickled down from the rock above it, and, losing itself among the moss and grass, fell over the precipice below, which, indeed, was of a frightful depth. This causeway was to all appearance safe, compared with many which we had passed, and I was just going to step upon it when my dog ran before me, jumped on the fatal pass﻿—his feet slipped from under him﻿—he fell and disappeared over the precipice! I started back﻿—I heard a heavy squelch and a howl; another fainter succeeded, and all was still. I advanced with the utmost caution to the edge of the precipice, where I discovered that the rill of water had nourished a short moss, close and smooth as velvet, and so slippery as not to admit of the lightest footstep; this accounted for the sudden disappearance and, as I concluded, the inevitable death of my dog.” Later on, far below, he found “the two dead bodies of our companions and that of my dog, all mangled in a shocking manner; both, it would appear, had attempted to cross the shelf in the same careless way which I was about to do when Providence interposed the dog in my behalf.” The adventures of Frank Mildmay and his crew on Trinidad are recorded with such realism and with﻿—as I have before said﻿—such accuracy of local colouring, that I suspect Captain Marryat in this portion of his work is recounting his personal experiences.

===Chinese===
我们又试了一道裂谷。我说不清它究竟是不是那一条，但它说不定偏偏就是，因为我不得不承认，自己已经全然摸不着头脑了。这谷到头来把我们引到一方斜斜的石台上，台外又临着一道绝壁。石台滑得厉害，因为溪水在上面敷着薄薄一层流过，台上又生着短短的青苔。这一节恰与《弗兰克·米尔徳梅》——那部特林达迪绝好的指南——里的一段描写吻合；书中关于此处的记述，对任何当真想上这岛游历的人——倘若世上果真有这样的人——都可作一份鉴戒。米尔徳梅手下有两名水手猎山羊时走失了，他便动身去寻。「我走在同伴们前头几码，」他写道，「狗离我不远，在一方岩石的斜台近旁，那石台尽头便是绝壁。我要横穿的这道石台约六七英尺宽、十一二英尺长，朝绝壁那一面稍稍倾斜，因此我以为它万无一失。一缕细流从上方的岩石渗下来，隐没于苔藓与草间，再从下面的绝壁上跌落下去；那绝壁委实深得骇人。与我们已经走过的许多石台相比，这道路面上看去是安全的；我正要踏上去，我的狗抢在前面跑过，跳上了那条夺命通道——它脚下一滑——跌了下去，翻过绝壁没了踪影！我猛地缩回——听见一声沉闷的扑响和一声哀嚎；接着又是一声，弱了些，随后万籁俱寂。我极其小心地挪到绝壁边缘，才发现那缕细流滋养出一层短苔，密实平滑如天鹅绒，滑得连最轻的脚步也站不住；这一下才解释了它何以骤然消失，也正如我所推断的，解释了我的狗不可避免的死。」随后，在下方深处，他找到了「我们两名同伴的尸体和我那条狗的尸体，全都残破得骇人；看来两人都是想以我方才险些采用的同样漫不经心的方式横穿石台，而那时，是天意把狗遣来替我挡了灾」。弗兰克·米尔徳梅和他一伙人在特林达迪的历险，写得如此逼真，又——如我先前所说——带着如此精确的地方色彩，以致我疑心马里亚特船长在这一部分写的正是他本人的亲身经历。

===Original===
So, foiled once again, we reascended the ravine and walked along the edge of the mountains, till we came to a projecting rock that commanded an extensive view over the cliffs. Here we sat down and discussed the problem before us. I assured the doctor that my ravine was certainly close to us somewhere, but that I altogether failed to identify it among the ravines before us, though I carried in my mind’s eye a very vivid picture of its appearance.

===Chinese===
于是我们又落了空，重新爬上谷来，沿着山缘走，直到一处凸出的岩石跟前——从那里可以纵览崖下一大片景象。我们坐下来，商量眼前的难题。我向医生担保，我那道裂谷必定就在近旁某处，只是眼前这一片谷壑之中，我怎么也认不出它来，虽然它的模样，在我心目中还留着极鲜明的一幅图景。

===Original===
“Perhaps it has disappeared,” suggested the doctor. This seemed scarcely possible, but it might, I acknowledged, have been so changed by landslips as to be unrecognisable.

===Chinese===
「兴许它消失了。」医生提示道。这看上去几乎不可能，但我承认，它或许已被滑坡改得面目全非，以致认不出了。

===Original===
Being people of logical mind, we reasoned that, if the ravine still existed, we ought now to discover it without any difficulty by a simple process of elimination. There was only a limited number of even possible-looking ways down the precipices. Of these we had now tried two in vain. Again, there were several others which I remembered well to have attempted at the time of my previous visit and to have found impracticable. It followed that we had now to confine our attention to any remaining possible routes, and of these there could be very few.

===Chinese===
我们都是有逻辑头脑的人，一番推理下来：那道裂谷倘若还在，如今只须用简单的排除法，便该毫不费力地把它找出来。看上去哪怕仅有一线可能下崖的路，总数本来有限。这其中，我们已经白白试了两条。再者，还有几条我记得清楚，上次来访时试过，明知走不通。由此推来，我们如今只须把注意力集中在余下尚有可能的几条路上，而这为数必然很少。

===Original===
Indeed, after a careful survey along the edge of the cliffs, we found that there was but one such way left to us, and that looked very ugly. Everywhere else were precipices that could obviously only be descended by a means of progression more rapid than we cared to undertake.

===Chinese===
果然，沿着崖缘仔细踏勘一遍之后，我们发现这样的路只剩一条，而且模样凶险。其余各处皆是绝壁，显然，除非采用某种比我们乐意尝试的更为迅捷的行进方式，否则绝无可能下去。

===Original===
This way seemed as if it might afford a passage to the beach, but it was not a ravine at all. The mountain on which we stood had fallen away, leaving a precipitous step some fifty or sixty feet in height, and from this step there sloped down to a depth, I should say, of quite 1,500 feet a great landslip of broken rocks, the debris of the fallen mountain. This landslip appeared to have taken place not long since. It was composed of rocks of all sizes and shapes, almost coal black, piled one on the other at so steep an angle that it was extraordinary how the mass held together and did not topple over. It was indeed in places more like an artificial wall of rough stones on a gigantic scale than a landslip.

===Chinese===
这条路看模样倒也许能通到滩头，但它根本算不上什么裂谷。我们脚下所立的整面山体塌了下去，留下一道五六十英尺高的陡坎；从这道坎往下，斜铺着一大片破岩构成的滑坡——崩塌山体的碎屑——依我看，深达足有 1,500 英尺。这场滑坡看去发生未久。构成它的岩石大大小小、奇形怪状，近乎炭黑，一块摞着一块，堆出的坡角陡得出奇，整堆东西居然拢得住、不曾倾倒，实在叫人称奇。有些地方，它与其说是滑坡，不如说更像一道放大到极致的毛石砌墙。

===Original===
The pass I was searching for was utterly unlike this. I remembered well that I had found a ravine extending from the mountain top to the beach, which I described in my narrative as “a gloomy gorge with sides formed of black rocks piled on each other in chaotic masses, with a small stream trickling into it.” We had experienced little difficulty in ascending or descending it. Before us were now a sufficiency “of black rocks piled on each other in chaotic masses,” but no signs of a ravine or stream.

===Chinese===
我寻的那条通路与此全然不同。我记得清楚，当年我找到的是一道从山顶直通滩头的裂谷，我在自己的记行文字里曾把它描写成「一道阴森的峡谷，两岸是乱纷纷堆叠起来的黑岩，一脉细流涓涓注入其间」。上下这道谷，我们当年没费什么力气。如今眼前倒尽有「乱纷纷堆叠起来的黑岩」，却不见半点裂谷或溪流的踪影。

===Original===
It did not look a tempting route, but we could see nothing else, so decided to try it. The descent was anything but easy and was certainly rather trying to the nerves. To begin with, the descent of the precipitous step I have mentioned was a very creepy business. Having accomplished this without accident, we clambered down the giant staircase of black rocks the best way we could, and also with as much speed as was consistent with safety; for the sun was low, the sudden tropical night would soon be on us, and as it would be, of course, impossible to proceed in the dark, we should be compelled to camp out in this very uncomfortable place if we did not hurry on.

===Chinese===
这条路看着并不诱人，但除它之外再无他途，我们便决意一试。下行的路决谈不上轻巧，而且着实考验神经。首先，下我方才提到的那道陡坎，就是一桩叫人头皮发麻的营生。这一关安然闯过之后，我们便在黑岩砌成的巨大阶梯上，拣着最好的走法往下攀，同时在安全许可之内尽量求快；因为太阳已经低了，热带的黑夜说至即至，而黑地里自然寸步难行，若不赶紧前行，我们就只好在这极不舒服的地方露宿了。

===Original===
We at last reached the foot of the landslip, and were on the green down we had seen from above, and which slopes gently to the beach. All our difficulties were over.

===Chinese===
我们终于下到滑坡脚下，踏上了从上面望见的那片绿色草冈，它缓缓倾斜，直伸向滩头。我们的种种难处就此过去了。

===Original===
These slopes on the windward side of Trinidad are overgrown chiefly with a sturdy species of bean. This plant creeps along the ground, throwing out long tough tendrils, whose mission it evidently is to climb up something for support; but in this they are generally unsuccessful, for nearly all the dead trees have been blown down on this windswept corner of the island. A few trees are still standing, and these are overgrown with clinging creepers more lucky than the rest. The scene reminded me of countries I had visited where there are ten women to one man and where, consequently, the male is properly appreciated and made much of, while thousands of luckless old maids vegetate hopelessly with no one to cling to. When I imparted this simile to the doctor he implored me not to be sentimental.

===Chinese===
特林达迪向风一侧的这些山坡，主要长满了一种粗壮的豆类植物。它贴着地面蔓生，抽出又长又韧的卷须，其天命显然是要攀住些什么作为依傍；可这件事它们多半办不成，因为在这岛上迎风的角落，枯树几乎全被吹倒了。尚有几棵树兀自站着，便缠满了比同类走运的攀缘藤蔓。眼前这景象令我想起我到过的一些国度：那里男女之比是女十男一，于是男子便得到应有的赏识与恩宠，而千百个倒霉的老姑娘无人可依，只能无望地枯守到老。我把这个比方说给医生听，他恳求我不要自作多情。

===Original===
The flowers of this bean are pink, and the pods are as large as broad beans. These the doctor at once pronounced to be edible, for, as he explained to me, none of these Leguminosae are poisonous. This was a good thing to know, for they grow so thickly on these shores that we could have collected any quantity we pleased during our stay on Trinidad; and with these, the fish, the turtle, the birds and their eggs, all of which are procurable here without any difficulty, it would be possible for men left on this island to ward off starvation for any length of time.

===Chinese===
这种豆开粉红色的花，豆荚有蚕豆那么大。医生当即断言这些可以入口，因为——按他给我的解释——这类豆科（Leguminosae）植物没有一种是有毒的。这是件值得知道的事：这些豆在沿岸长得极密，我们居留特林达迪期间想要多少便可采多少；有了它们，再加上鱼、海龟、海鸟和鸟蛋——这一切在此都唾手可得——就算有人被留在岛上，也尽可以无限期地免于饿死。

===Original===
When I speak of the slopes we were now on as downs, the reader must not conjure up a picture of the grassy downs of the English coast, pleasant under foot and easy to travel on. To drag one’s feet over the downs of Trinidad is a very weary business. There are large rocks and deep pits everywhere. One’s progress is impeded by the extreme softness of the soil, into which one’s feet sink deeply, and this is made still worse by the burrows of the land-crabs, while the roots of the tall grasses and the trailing tendrils of the beans try to trip one up at every step.

===Chinese===
我把我们此刻所走的这些山坡叫作草冈，读者切莫由此在脑中描出一幅英国海岸草冈的图景——那里脚下舒坦，行走轻易。在特林达迪的草冈上拖步而行，是件极累人的事。巨石深坑处处皆是。土质松软之极，双脚深深陷进去，行进大受其阻；陆蟹打的洞又使这情形雪上加霜，高草的根和豆蔓拖地的卷须还步步都想绊你一跤。

===Original===
Here, to our relief, we found water again. At the foot of the landslip a deep gully opened out which clove the down to the edge of the shore. At the bottom of this a little stream flowed for a short distance, being absorbed by the thirsty soil long before it could reach the sands below.

===Chinese===
在这里我们又找到了水，甚是宽慰。滑坡脚下岔开一道深深的沟壑，把草冈直劈到岸边。沟底一线小溪流了短短一程，早在够到下面的沙滩之前，就被干渴的泥土吸尽了。

===Original===
In order to avoid the entangling vegetation we walked down this gully, and an exceedingly unpleasant place we found it. For here an incredible number of large fluffy white birds, a sort of gannet, were sitting on their nests with their young. They covered the rocks and the branches of the dead trees. They attacked us savagely whenever we came within reach of them, and the whole of the hot narrow gorge stank most offensively of the rotten fish they had strewed about. The different species of birds occupy different portions of this island, and this ravine is the chief haunt of this particular disagreeable tribe.

===Chinese===
为了避开缠人的草木，我们顺着这道沟往下走，却发现这是个极不痛快的地方。原来这里有数目多得难以置信的白色大鸟，绒毛蓬松，是一种塘鹅，正伏在巢中孵育幼雏。它们密密地盖满岩石和枯树的枝桠。我们一走进它们够得着的地方，它们便凶猛地攻击我们；整条又热又窄的峡谷里，弥漫着它们抛撒四下的烂鱼，臭得叫人掩鼻。不同的鸟种分占此岛的不同地段，而这道谷，正是这个特别讨人嫌的族类聚居的老巢。

===Original===
The whole scene now seemed strangely familiar to me﻿—the ravine, the black rocks, the crowds of brooding white birds﻿—and when at last we came to what appeared to be an old road of piled-up stones crossing the gully I stood still and cried in astonishment: “Why, doctor, this is my ravine after all! I remember this place well!”

===Chinese===
此刻整个景象令我莫名地眼熟——这道谷，这些黑岩，这一群群伏巢的白鸟——待到我们终于走到一处像是垒石铺成的旧路、横跨沟壑的地方，我站定了，惊呼起来：「哎呀，医生，这到底还是我那道谷！这地方我记得清清楚楚！」

===Original===
Then I looked behind me at the mountain we had descended, and I began to understand how it was that I had been unable to find out my old route. As I have explained, the ravine I had travelled down nine years before extended from the plateau of tree-ferns to the shore. But since then a gigantic landslip had evidently taken place. The mountainside had fallen away, and millions and millions of tons of rocks had rolled below, entirely filling up the ravine and destroying all traces of it, until far down, where it appeared again on the downs beyond the limit of the landslip.

===Chinese===
这时我回身仰望我们下来的那座山，才渐渐明白，我何以寻不见自己的旧路。如前所述，九年前我下去的那道裂谷，是从树蕨高台一直通到岸边的。可自那以后，这里显然发生过一场天崩地陷般的巨型滑坡。整面山坡垮了下去，千千万万吨巨石滚落下来，把那道谷整个填平，抹去了它的一切痕迹，直到下方远处，它才在滑坡界线之外的草冈上重新露头。

===Original===
This was one among other instances I can mention showing that enormous changes have taken place on this island even in the course of the last nine years. When this terrific fall of rocks occurred, it would have been a wonderful sight to one gazing at it in safety from the sea, and the noise of it must have made itself heard for many leagues around. It has certainly converted what was once a comparatively easy and perfectly safe road from the mountaintops to the windward shore into an extremely difficult and dangerous one. So much so that the doctor and myself saw at once that it would be useless to establish a depot of stores at the pier, as it would be out of the question to lead the members of the expedition up such a perilous place as this. It was absolutely certain that lives would be lost if this pass were often attempted. No skilful mountaineering would avail against the treacherous rottenness of the precipitous step which surmounts the landslip, and which did not exist of old. There is no certain foothold anywhere upon its face, and we looked forward with no pleasurable anticipation to our enforced return by this way on the morrow.

===Chinese===
这是我可以举出的例证之一，说明单是最近这九年之间，此岛便已发生了天翻地覆的巨变。那场骇人的崩岩倾泻之时，若有人从海上安然遥望，必是一大奇观；其声势之隆，周围许多里格之外想必都听得见。它确凿无疑地把昔日一条自山顶下向风岸的较为平易、十分安全的路，变成了极难走、极凶险的一条。凶险到这个地步，我和医生当下便看出：在天然码头设立给养储存点已属徒劳，因为要率领探险队的队员攀上这样一处险地，根本无从谈起。这条道若常常有人涉足，断然要出人命。那道压在滑坡顶上的陡坎是旧日所无的，其朽坏暗藏杀机，任凭什么高明的登山本领也对付不了。陡坎的坡面上没有任何一处站得牢的落脚点；想到明天还非得原路由此返回，我们实在提不起半点愉快的期待。

===Original===
The birds’ eggs lay on every stone in this valley. We tasted some of them, but the flavour bore too much resemblance to the stench of rotten fish around us to be altogether pleasing.

===Chinese===
这谷里的石头上到处搁着鸟蛋。我们尝了几枚，可那滋味与四下烂鱼的恶臭太相像了，实在谈不上可口。

===Original===
The bank of stones which I had recognised in the ravine was of far too regular formation to be otherwise than the work of men’s hands.

===Chinese===
我在谷中认出的那道石堤，砌得过于规整，除了出自人手，不可能是别的来历。

===Original===
Some hundreds of years ago, the Portuguese had a penal settlement on this side of Trinidad, and this, no doubt, was what remained of one of their roads. Some weeks later, I explored the ruins of this settlement, which is a short distance to the north of this gully. I will describe it when I come to that portion of my narrative.

===Chinese===
数百年前，葡萄牙人曾在特林达迪的这一面设有流放犯的屯居地；这无疑便是他们某条道路的遗存。数周之后，我踏勘了这处屯居地的废墟，它在这道沟壑以北不远的地方。待写到记行的那一段时，我再细细描述。

===Original===
Before we came to the spot where the stream soaks into the earth we filled our bottle with water; then we walked down to the sandy beach, reaching it just before it became too dark to see our way. We were not long in selecting our camp. There was a large rock on the sands above high-water mark, whose hollow side afforded good shelter from wind and rain. In front of this, we lit a fire of the wreckwood, of which there was no lack round us, and after a supper of roasted *ch’arki* and biscuit, we proceeded to make ourselves comfortable over our pipes and rum. We were tired, and would have slept very soundly with the sound of the surf on the reefs as our lullaby, had it not been for the land-crabs, which would not let us alone, but pulled our hair or nipped our necks as soon as we began to doze off.

===Chinese===
走到溪水渗进土里的地方之前，我们先把水瓶灌满；随后下到沙滩，赶在天黑得看不见路之前到了那里。选营地没费多少工夫。高潮线以上的沙地上立着一块大岩石，凹进的一面正好遮风避雨。我们在岩前生起一堆火，烧的是失事船的漂木——四下里这东西有的是——晚饭吃了烤恰尔基风干牛肉和硬面饼，饭后便就着烟斗和朗姆酒，舒舒服服地安顿下来。我们实在乏了，本可伴着礁石上涛声的摇篮曲酣然沉睡，奈何陆蟹不肯放过我们，我们刚一迷糊，它们便来扯头发、掐脖子。

===Original===
At last their conduct became unbearable, and our patience worn out, so we got up, seized two sticks, and slaughtered some fifty of them. Then we had a little rest, for the others left us alone for a while and devoured their dead brethren, making a merry crackling noise all round us, as they pulled the joints asunder and opened the shells. It was, as the doctor remarked, like the sound of many lobster suppers going on together at Scott’s.

===Chinese===
到后来它们的行径实在叫人忍无可忍，我们的耐性也磨光了，便一跃而起，操起两根木棍，结果了大约五十只。此后我们才得了片刻安宁，因为其余的蟹暂且丢下我们不管，专心吞吃起它们的死同类来；它们把蟹腿扯下、把壳掰开，四下里响成一片欢快的咔嚓声。正如医生所说，这就像斯科茨餐馆（Scott’s）里许多顿龙虾晚宴同时在开动。

===Original===
At daybreak (Nov. 22) we started for Southwest Bay. We had drunk all our water, and so were anxious to reach the bay, explore it, and be back to our stream as quickly as possible. While making this same journey nine years before, I had found no signs of fresh water between this and South Point. The streams that flow from the mountaintops are absorbed far up by the slopes of debris and never reach the shore. Mr. A﻿—﻿— did discover a small, but uncertain, supply near his camp at the head of Southwest Bay, but we felt that we could not rely on this, and that the issue in the ravine above us, which we had left on the previous evening, was the only one we could fall back upon with certainty on the whole weather shore of the island.

===Chinese===
破晓时分（11 月 22 日），我们动身前往西南湾。水已喝尽，我们都急着赶到湾边，踏勘一番，尽快回到我们的溪水那里去。九年前走这同一段路时，从这里到南角之间，我没有发现丝毫淡水的迹象。从山顶流下的溪水，早早就被碎石坡吸干，从来不曾流到岸边。A——先生确曾在西南湾湾头他营地附近找到过一小股水源，但时有时无；我们却觉得这一点靠不住——全岛整个向风岸上，我们所能确凿仰仗的，只有前一晚留在身后那道谷里的泉水。

===Original===
We walked along the sandy beach, with the mountains towering to the right of us and the ocean swell breaking heavily on the reefs to our left. The beach was covered with wreckage﻿—planks, barrels, spars, timbers of vessels with the corroded iron bolts still sticking in them﻿—a melancholy spectacle; but I was unable to find one particular wreck which I had seen here nine years before﻿—the complete framework of a vessel partly buried in the sands, into which I had thought it might be worth while for our party now to dig, as some valuables might be lying in her hold. Either the sea had broken up or the sands had completely covered this wreck since my last visit.

===Chinese===
我们沿着沙滩前行，右侧群山巍然耸峙，左侧大洋的涌浪在礁石上重重拍碎。滩上覆满失事船只的残骸——木板、木桶、桅桁、船材，锈蚀的铁螺栓还钉在上面——一派凄凉景象；但九年前我在这里见过的一处特别的残骸，这回却怎么也找不到了——那是一艘船的完整骨架，半埋在沙里；我曾想着，如今让我们这一队人来挖上一挖倒也值得，说不定船舱里还躺着些什么值钱的东西。自上次来访，那残骸不是被大海打散了，便是被流沙整个埋没了。

===Original===
We found traces of turtle on the sands, and we saw that the pools of clear water left by the tide were full of fish, while sea-crabs scampered over the rocks in quantities. The beans, too, grew in profusion on the downs above the beach, so there was plenty of food all round us, and, if there had only been fresh water, we could have made ourselves very comfortable here. ===Chinese===
我们在沙滩上发现了海龟的痕迹，又见潮水退后留下的清水洼里满是游鱼，海蟹则成群地在礁石间窜来窜去。滩上的草冈也盛长着那种豆子，所以我们四周食物俯拾即是；只要再有淡水，我们在此本可以过得十分惬意。陆蟹当然遍地都是，不过真要吃这些丑家伙，人总得被逼到山穷水尽的地步才行。

===Original===

===Chinese===

===Original===
