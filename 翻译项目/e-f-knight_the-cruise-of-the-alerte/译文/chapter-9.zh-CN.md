## IX / 九

===Original===
Treasure Island at Last

===Chinese===
终于到了金银岛

===Original===
We sailed on towards the desert island under all canvas, but did not reach it for eight hours from the time we first sighted it.

===Chinese===
我们张满全部帆朝那座荒岛驶去，但从第一眼望见它算起，过了八个钟头才得以抵达。

===Original===
As we neared it, the features of this extraordinary place could gradually be distinguished. The north side, that which faced us, is the most barren and desolate portion of the island, and appears to be utterly inaccessible. Here the mountains rise sheer from the boiling surf—fantastically shaped of volcanic rock; cloven by frightful ravines; lowering in perpendicular precipices; in places overhanging threateningly, and, where the mountains have been shaken to pieces by the fires and earthquakes of volcanic action, huge landslips slope steeply into the yawning ravines—landslips of black and red volcanic debris, and loose rocks large as houses, ready on the slightest disturbance to roll down, crashing, into the abysses below. On the summit of the island there floats almost constantly, even on the clearest day, a wreath of dense vapour, never still, but rolling and twisting into strange shapes as the wind eddies among the crags. And above this cloud-wreath rise mighty pinnacles of coal-black rock, like the spires of some gigantic Gothic cathedral piercing the blue southern sky.

===Chinese===
随着我们渐渐驶近，这处奇地的形貌一点点可以分辨了。北面——正对着我们的一面——是全岛最荒芜、最凄凉的部分，看来全然无路可通。群山在这里从沸腾的浪涛中拔地直起——火山岩塑成千姿百态的奇形；被一道道骇人的裂谷劈开；垂直的峭壁沉沉压下，有的地方崖岩险恶地悬空外突；而在山体被火山作用的烈火与地震震得崩碎之处，巨大的滑坡陡陡地斜插进豁开的深谷——那是黑红两色火山碎屑堆成的滑坡，还有屋宇般大小的松动巨石，只消一丝扰动，便随时会轰然滚落，坠入下方的深渊。即便在最晴朗的日子，岛顶也几乎终日浮着一环浓密的云气，从不停歇，随着风在峰岩间回旋打转，翻滚扭绞成种种怪状。而在这环云气之上，耸起一座座煤黑色巨岩的尖峰，宛如某座硕大无朋的哥特式大教堂的一丛尖塔，直刺湛蓝的南方天空。

===Original===
The loftiest peak is about three thousand feet above the sea, but on account of the extreme precipitousness of the island it appears much higher.

===Chinese===
最高的山峰海拔约三千英尺，只因此岛地势极其陡峭，望上去要高得多。

===Original===
As a consequence of the recoil of the rollers from the shore we found that, as we got nearer in, the ocean swell under us increased in height, and rose and fell in an uneasy confused fashion. The breakers were dashing up the cliffs with an ominous roar, showing us that, in all probability, landing would be out of the question for the present.

===Chinese===
由于浪涛拍岸后反卷回来，我们发现越往里驶，脚下的洋涌越高，起伏动荡，紊乱不宁。碎浪带着不祥的怒吼冲上峭壁，这情形告诉我们：眼下滑岸多半是想也别想。

===Original===
We passed North Point and opened out Northwest Bay. At the farther end of the bay we saw before us the Monument, or Ninepin, as it is called on the charts—a stupendous pinnacle of basaltic rock 850 feet in height, which rises from the edge of the surf, and is detached from the main cliffs.

===Chinese===
我们驶过北角，西北湾豁然展开。在湾的尽头，我们面前出现了「纪念碑」——海图上称作「九柱石」（Ninepin）——一根 850 英尺高的玄武岩巨柱，从浪涛边缘拔地而起，与主崖分离而立。

===Original===
The scenery was indescribably savage and grand, and its effect was heightened by the roaring of the surf on the beach and the echoes of it in the ravines, as well as by the shrill and melancholy cries of thousands of seabirds so unaccustomed to the presence of man that they came off the crags and flew round us in evident wonder as we sailed by, often approaching so close to us that we could strike them with our hands.

===Chinese===
这景色粗犷壮丽得难以言传；浪涛在滩头轰鸣、回声在裂谷中激荡，千万只海鸟发出凄厉哀切的啼叫，愈发衬得它气象森然。这些鸟从不曾见过人，我们驶过时，它们竟飞下峰岩，满眼惊异地绕着我们盘旋，常常凑得那么近，伸手就能打到它们。

===Original===
My companions had expected, from what I had told them, to find this islet a strange, uncanny place, barren, torn by volcanic action and generally forbidding, and now they gazed at the shore with amazement, and confessed that my description of its scenery was anything but exaggerated. It would be impossible to convey in words a just idea of the mystery of Trinidad. The very colouring seems unearthly—in places dismal black, and in others the fire-consumed crags are of strange metallic hues, vermilion red and copper yellow. When one lands on its shores this uncanny impression is enhanced. It bears all the appearance of being an accursed spot, whereupon no creatures can live, save the hideous land-crabs and foul and cruel seabirds.

===Chinese===
同伴们听我讲过，早料到这小岛是个古怪阴森的去处：荒瘠贫瘠，被火山作用撕扯得支离破碎，总之一派拒人千里的模样。此刻他们望着海岸，个个惊叹不已，承认我对景色的描述丝毫没有夸张。特林达迪的神秘，任凭怎样的文字也难以传达得恰如其分。单是那着色便恍非人间——有的地方一片死黑，有的地方烈火噬过的峰岩泛着奇异的金属色泽，朱红与铜黄。一旦踏上它的海岸，这种阴森之感愈发强烈。这里处处显出一副遭天谴的模样，除了丑陋的陆蟹和污秽凶残的海鸟，什么生灵也活不成。

===Original===
We were now coasting under the lee of the island and our progress was but slow, for the high mountains intercepted the wind from us, and we were often becalmed on the oily swell under the hottest sun we had yet experienced. Occasionally a violent squall, but of short duration, would sweep down on us from some ravine and help us along. What wind there was between the squalls came from every point of the compass in turns, and we were constantly taken aback.

===Chinese===
此刻我们贴着岛的背风面行驶，进展缓慢，因为高山替我们挡住了风，我们常常在油光光的海涌上停滞不前，头顶是我们此行以来最毒的日头。间或有一阵猛烈而短促的阵风从某道裂谷里扑下来，助我们一程。阵风之间那点风，又轮番从罗盘的每一个方位吹来，弄得我们动辄突遭顶头逆风。

===Original===
But at last we passed the rocky islet which I named Bird Island at the time of my former visit, and, doubling the West Point, we entered a bay which I recognised well, for there was the cascade still falling over the cliff, and, near it, the landing-place off which I had anchored in the *Falcon*. As the swell was not high here, I decided to anchor at once; so, bringing the vessel as near in as was prudent—about six cables from the shore—I let go in eighteen fathoms.

===Chinese===
但最后我们驶过了我上次来访时命名的鸟岛（Bird Island），绕过西角，进了一处我熟识的海湾：那道瀑布依旧从崖上泻下，近旁就是当年我驾「猎鹰号」抛锚处的登陆点。这里涌浪不高，我便决定立即下锚；于是把船开到稳妥所容许的最近处——离岸约六链——在十八英寻深的水中抛了锚。

===Original===
The scene before us was a fine one. A very steep and rugged ravine clove the mountain from summit to base. At the bottom of this ravine a stream fell in a cascade over a ledge of black rock on to the beach, about thirty feet below. One could trace the silver line of the falling water in many other parts of the ravine, especially in one place far up, where it fell over a gigantic black precipice.

===Chinese===
眼前的景致甚是可观。一道极陡峭、极崎岖的裂谷把山从顶到脚剖开。谷底一股溪水化作瀑布，翻过一道黑色岩坎，落到约三十英尺下的海滩上。沿着这道裂谷的许多别处，也能辨出落水的一线银光，尤其是在高处一个地方，水流从一面巨大的黑色峭壁上倾泻而下。

===Original===
The mountainsides were barren, save in spots where a coarse grass grew sparsely. At the very head of the ravine were downs beautifully green, with a dense grove of trees the nature of which it was not easy to distinguish from so far below; but, as I had ascended this ravine during my last visit to Trinidad, I knew that these were tree-ferns, which only grow on this portion of the island high up among the damp clouds, and are in charming contrast to the desolation that prevails around them.

===Chinese===
山坡上一片荒芜，只有零星几处长着稀疏的粗硬野草。裂谷的顶端却有一片绿得可爱的草冈，生着茂密的一丛树林——从这么低的远处望上去，不易辨清那是些什么树；但我上次来特林达迪时曾登上过这道裂谷，知道那是树蕨。这种树只见于岛上这一带高处的湿云之间，与四围弥漫的荒凉恰成动人的对照。

===Original===
Between the foot of the mountains and the surf extends a narrow beach of rugged stones of all sizes fallen from above, and the black heads of rocks appear here and there in the middle of the surf, so that any attempt at landing seems a risky venture.

===Chinese===
山脚与浪涛之间伸展着一线狭窄的滩，滩上尽是从山上崩落的、大小不一的粗砺石块；黑色的礁石顶这里那里地从浪涛中间露出，因此任何登岸的尝试，看上去都是一桩冒险。

===Original===
But I knew where the safe landing-place was, and soon recognised it again, though it was not to be easily distinguished from the vessel. I pointed it out to my companions. Some forty yards to the left of the cascade an irregularly shaped rocky ledge extends from the beach some way out into the deep water beyond the beach, and thus forms a natural pier. I had often found it quite an easy matter to land here when to do so anywhere else would be impossible; for, as a rule, the seas do not break until they have rolled some way inside the end of this point; so that, by approaching it carefully, and waiting till the boat is on the summit of a wave and near the level of the top of the rock, one can leap or scramble on to it with the exercise of a little agility. There are occasions, however, when the seas wash right over this ledge.

===Chinese===
但我知道安全的登岸点在哪里，很快又把它认了出来，尽管从船上并不容易辨认。我指给同伴们看：瀑布左侧约四十码处，一道形状不规则的岩台从滩边向外伸展出一段，一直探到滩外的深水里，就此形成一座天然码头。别处全无可能登岸时，在此上岸我常觉得易如反掌；因为照例说来，海浪要滚过这岬尖再往里一段才会破碎；于是只要小心趋近，等小艇乘上浪峰、与岩顶差不多齐平时，稍许施展一点身手，便可跃上或攀上岩去。不过也有时候，海浪会整个漫过这道岩台。

===Original===
Looking from our anchorage we could see the coast as far as West Point on one side of us, with the head of the Ninepin just visible above the cape; and on the other side as far as the promontory of basaltic columns which forms the western extremity of West Bay, and which I named the Ness.

===Chinese===
从锚位望去，一侧的海岸一直看到西角，「九柱石」的顶端刚刚露出海角之上；另一侧则看到那道玄武岩石柱构成的海岬为止——它是西湾的西端，我给它起名叫「内斯」（the Ness）。

===Original===
As soon as the sails were stowed I went below with the doctor to talk over our immediate plans. It was now five in the evening, so it was too late to attempt a landing, even if the conditions were favourable, which they were not; for every now and again a sea would break over the pier, sending showers of spray high into the air.

===Chinese===
帆一收好，我便同医生下到舱里，商量眼下的打算。这时已是下午五点，即便条件合适，想登岸也来不及了，何况条件并不合适：因为时不时就有一道浪打过那座天然码头，把阵阵水花高高抛上半空。

===Original===
While we were discussing things, there suddenly came a violent thumping on the deck above us, and from the shouts and laughter of the men we knew that something exciting was going on; so we went up the companion-ladder to see what the fun might be. We found that a fair-sized shark was tumbling about the deck in very active fashion, while Ted was dodging him, knife in hand, ready to give him his *coup de grâce*. Our sportsman had got his lines out as soon as all had been made snug on deck, but his sport for the first hour consisted of nothing but sharks, of which he caught several. After this he had better luck and was able to supply the cook with fish enough for dinner and breakfast for all hands.

===Chinese===
我们正商量着，头顶甲板上忽然响起一阵猛烈的扑腾声，从船员们的呼喊和大笑里，我们知道准有什么热闹事，便登上舱梯去看个究竟。只见一条个头不小的鲨鱼正在甲板上极活跃地翻腾，特德手里握着刀，与它周旋，准备给它送上致命一击（coup de grâce）。我们的渔猎好手早在甲板上收拾停当后就甩出了钓线，可头一个钟头的收获清一色全是鲨鱼，钓上了好几条。此后他运气转佳，钓到的鱼足供厨子为全船人做出晚饭和早饭。

===Original===
The sea round Trinidad swarms with fish; but, for some reason, though we got as many as we required, they were not to be so readily caught now as at the time of my first visit; for then we hauled them in as fast as we could drop our hooks in the water.

===Chinese===
特林达迪四周的海里鱼多得成群；可不知怎的，这一回虽然要多少钓多少，鱼却远不如我初访时那样容易上钩——那时我们钩子一落水，鱼便一条条被拽上来，简直来不及下钩。

===Original===
There are various species of edible fish here—among others, dolphins, rock-cod, hind-fish, blackfish, and pigfish. None of these hot-water-fish are to be compared in flavour to those of Europe, and we found that the sharks were the least insipid of the lot; stewed shark and onions is not a dish to be despised.

===Chinese===
这里出产各种可食的鱼——其中就有鲯鳅、岩鳕、红斑鱼、黑鱼和猪鱼。这些暖水鱼的滋味没有一种比得上欧洲的鱼；我们还发现，这一众鱼里倒数鲨鱼最不寡淡；炖鲨鱼配洋葱，也算得上一道不可小觑的菜。

===Original===
According to the chart of the South Atlantic which I made use of on this voyage, the island of Trinidad is rather more than five miles long. Another chart which I possess gives its length as only three miles, which I am sure is wrong; but, on the other hand, this latter chart is the more correct in some other respects, and marks outlying shoals which are not indicated on the other. There are, indeed, no absolutely reliable charts of this island; for the different surveys have been somewhat cursory, and each has repeated the faults of its predecessors. The longitude has, I believe, never been accurately determined, and even the latitude of the landing-place is, if I am not much mistaken, more than a mile out on the chart.

===Chinese===
按我此行所用的那张南大西洋海图，特林达迪岛长五英里多。我手头的另一张海图却把岛长标成仅三英里——我敢肯定那是错的；不过反过来说，后一张图在别的几处反倒更准，还标出了外海的几片浅滩，那是前一张图上没有的。说实在的，这座岛根本没有什么绝对可靠的海图；历次测量都失之草率，后一张又都承袭了前一张的错处。据我看，这座岛的经度从未精确测定过，就连登陆点的纬度，如果我没大记错，海图上也偏差了一英里之多。

===Original===
Before going further with the narrative, however, it will be well to enter into some explanation of the task that was before us.

===Chinese===
不过，在继续叙述之前，不妨先把我们面前的任务说明一番。

===Original===
The treasure was supposed to be hidden in Southwest Bay, in a little ravine just to the left of our camp.

===Chinese===
宝藏据信藏在西南湾，就在我们营地左侧的一道小裂谷里。

===Original===
The yacht was anchored out of sight of this spot, and at a distance of two and a half miles from it as the crow flies. My companions were, I imagine, somewhat surprised at this manoeuvre of mine, especially when I told them that it was highly improbable that we should shift our anchorage any nearer to the scene of our operations on shore. Later on, however, they realised that there was a good reason for the course I had taken.

===Chinese===
游艇锚泊的地方望不见这一处，直线距离也有两英里半。我想，同伴们对我这一手颇感诧异，尤其在我告诉他们，我们极不可能把锚地再挪近些、挪到离岸上作业场更近的地方之后。不过后来他们明白了，我这样处置自有充分的道理。

===Original===
My former experiences off Trinidad with the *Falcon* had convinced me that the anchorage off the cascade was far the safest; indeed that here only could one remain at all for any length of time. It must be remembered that a vessel is never really secure when anchored off a small oceanic island like Trinidad. One should be always prepared to slip one's anchor and be off to sea at once should it come on to blow. It is therefore necessary to lie at some distance from the land, so as to have plenty of room to get away on either tack. If one is too near the shore one incurs great risk, as I frequently discovered while coasting later on; for even though it be blowing hard outside, one is becalmed under the cliffs or subjected to shifting flaws and whirlwinds, so that the vessel becomes unmanageable, and is driven straight on to the fatal rocks by the send of the swell. I need scarcely say that to come in contact with this shore, even in the finest weather, would involve the certain destruction of any craft in a very few seconds.

===Chinese===
我当年随「猎鹰号」在特林达迪外海的经历使我确信：瀑布外的锚地远比别处安全；说实在的，也只有在这一处才待得住任何时日。须知，船锚泊在特林达迪这样的洋中小岛之外，从来没有真正安全的时候。一旦起风，就要随时准备弃锚，即刻逃向外海。因此必须离岸有一段距离，好有足够的余地朝任何一舷抢风驶出。若是离岸太近，便要冒大风险——这一点我后来沿海岸航行时屡有体会；因为哪怕外面风刮得再猛，崖下却风平浪静，或者一阵阵乱转的风气和旋风轮番作弄，弄得船无法操控，被海涌的推力径直送上致命的礁石。不必说，同这道海岸一沾边，哪怕天气再好，任何船只都注定在几秒钟内毁灭。

===Original===
The anchorage off the cascade possesses many advantages. The coast here is free from any outlying dangers, and there is a depth of five fathoms close to the beach. One cannot be embayed there, for the coast beyond West Point trends away northward almost at right angles to the southwest shore, so that from the anchorage it is easy to get away on either tack, according to the direction of the wind. Here, too, the sea is smoother than anywhere else, except on rare occasions, for the prevailing winds are northeast to southeast, more generally southeast.

===Chinese===
瀑布外的锚地有许多好处。这一带海岸外围没有任何暗险，紧挨着海滩便有五英寻深的水。在这里也不会被困在湾中脱身不得，因为西角以外的海岸几乎成直角向北拐去，与西南岸分岔，所以从锚地出发，视风向如何，朝哪一舷抢风驶出都容易。而且除个别日子外，这里的海面也比别处平静，因为这一带的盛行风是东北风到东南风，更多是东南风。

===Original===
Now, the only other possible anchorage for us would have been in Southwest Bay, in very convenient proximity to our camp; but this, though it might do for a day or two, was absolutely unfitted for a lengthy stay, more especially as difficulties might occur with the vessel while I was on shore myself and only inexperienced people were in charge of her. In this bay one is surrounded by dangers. South Point is on one side, with the current generally setting directly on to it and across the perilous shoals that extend a mile and a half seaward. On the other side is the cape dividing West and Southwest Bays, off which also lie several dangerous islets and rocks. According to the Admiralty chart Southwest Bay itself is quite clean, with a uniform depth of ten fathoms. As a matter of fact, it is full of sunken rocks, and there is an island right in the middle of it; its existence is ignored by all the charts. Surrounded as the bay is by lofty mountains, the winds are very uncertain within it, so that if one should have to weigh anchor it might be difficult to extricate the vessel from her dangerous position even by the exercise of the smartest seamanship. Lastly, it affords no shelter from the prevailing wind, southeast, which often raises a nasty sea, and, what is more, it is entirely exposed to the storm-wind of these seas, the dreaded *pampero*, which blows right into it. Anyone in charge of a vessel brought up in this trap would be compelled to get underway frequently under most difficult circumstances, and would live an unenviable life of perpetual anxiety. This information will, I trust, be of use to any fresh adventurers who propose to hunt for the treasure of Trinidad.

===Chinese===
