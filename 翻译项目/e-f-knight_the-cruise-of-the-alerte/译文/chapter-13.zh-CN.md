## XIII / 十三

===Original===
A Narrow Escape

===Chinese===
死里逃生

===Original===
We started early on the following morning, November 23, and reached the summit of the landslip before the sun had heated the black rocks, and the layer of close air immediately over them, to that high temperature which we had found so insupportable on the previous day.

===Chinese===
翌日清晨，11 月 23 日，我们早早启程，趁太阳还没有把黑岩烤热、把紧贴岩面的那层滞闷空气晒到头一天那令人难以忍受的高温，便已登上了滑坡的顶端。

===Original===
We managed to ascend the cliff which hangs over the landslip without accident, but it was anxious work, and we experienced a sense of relief when we found ourselves safe once more on the upper plateau.

===Chinese===
我们总算平安无事地登上了悬在滑坡上方的那面峭壁，只是这活计干得人提心吊胆；待重新安然立在高处的高台上，才觉出如释重负。

===Original===
From here we took a shortcut across the groves of tree-ferns towards the head of the cascade ravine, and came unexpectedly upon a green valley in the middle of the plateau which we had not seen before, and which is, without doubt, the most beautiful place on the island. At the bottom of it a cool stream flowed through thickly-growing ferns and grass. The scenery all round us was of a soft and pleasing character, very strange to us after the dreary barrenness of the mountain slopes beneath this elevated and almost inaccessible garden.

===Chinese===
从这里我们抄了近路，穿过一丛丛树蕨林，朝瀑布谷的谷首走去，不料在高台中部与一道翠绿的山谷不期而遇——我们先前竟从未见过它，而它毋庸置疑是全岛最美的地方。谷底一道清凉的溪水，从繁密的蕨类和草丛间流过。四围景致温婉悦目；我们刚从这座高悬于上、几乎人迹难至的花园下方的荒凉枯瘠山坡上来，此刻只觉恍如别一世界。

===Original===
We might have been in some fair vale of Paraguay, instead of on the summit of rugged Trinidad. Here were gently sloping green hills that shut out all view of the jagged peaks. The vegetation was of a more luxuriant nature than in any other portion of the island; tall grasses, bushes, and plants of various kinds, most of them covered with flowers, carpeted the soft red soil, while the tall and beautiful tree-ferns stood in scattered clumps, casting a pleasant shade with their fronds of darker green. Even the dead trees were not so melancholy in appearance as elsewhere on the island; for from their branches—as well as from those of the older bushes and tree-ferns—there hung swaying festoons of a parasitic plant something like the Spanish moss that covers the pines and live-oaks of Florida, but more beautiful, for this was of a silvery white colour.

===Chinese===
我们恍若置身巴拉圭某处明媚的山谷，而不是站在蛮荒嶙峋的特林达迪之巅。这里尽是绿冈缓坡，把参差奇峰的景致遮了个严实。草木之繁茂，胜过岛上任何去处：高草、灌木与形形色色的花草，多半缀着花朵，把松软的红土铺成一片锦绣；高挑秀美的树蕨三五成丛，以色泽较深的蕨叶投下宜人的荫凉。连枯树也不似岛上别处那般面目愁惨：枝桠上——老灌木和老树蕨的枝桠上也一样——垂挂着串串摇曳的花彩，是一种寄生植物，模样有几分像覆满佛罗里达松树与活栎树的西班牙苔藓（Spanish moss），却更为美丽，因为它通体银白。

===Original===
Besides those tyrants of Trinidad, the birds and land-crabs, mice, flies, ants, earwigs, and big spiders dwelt in this happy valley.

===Chinese===
除了特林达迪的两霸——禽鸟与陆蟹——之外，老鼠、苍蝇、蚂蚁、蠼螋和大蜘蛛也栖居在这座快活谷里。

===Original===
From here we walked to the head of our ravine, where the principal grove of tree-ferns crowns the cliffs, and now we looked down upon the *Alerte*, seeming very small from this dizzy height, “and yon tall anchoring bark, diminished to her cock; her cock, a buoy almost too small for sight.” We observed that the wind was blowing rather freshly from an unusual quarter—northwest—making this a lee shore to our vessel, but there were no signs of bad weather in the sky.

===Chinese===
从这里我们走到我们那道谷的谷首，最茂盛的一片树蕨林正冠盖在崖顶。此刻我们俯瞰「阿勒特号」，在这令人目眩的高处她显得那样渺小，「那边高高的泊船，缩成了一叶小艇；那小艇，又成了一枚浮标，几乎小得目不能见。」我们还注意到，风正从不常刮的方向——西北——相当劲急地吹来，使这里成了我们船只的下风岸；好在天空里并无坏天气的迹象。

===Original===
While descending the ravine we were shut in by the walls of rock, so that we were unable to see the yacht; but on reaching a point just above the cascade we again commanded a view over the whole roadstead, and lo! we found, to our dismay, that the *Alerte* was no longer lying at her anchorage, nor was she anywhere in sight.

===Chinese===
下谷途中，岩壁夹峙，我们望不见游艇；但一到瀑布上方不远处，整片锚地又尽收眼底——哎呀！我们愕然发现，「阿勒特号」已不在泊位之上，四下里也全不见她的踪影。

===Original===
We stood and stared round the horizon, scarcely believing the evidence of our eyes. Not an hour before we had looked down upon her from the mountain, riding snugly to her anchor, with sails stowed. What possible mischance could have occurred since then?

===Chinese===
我们伫立着环望水平线，几乎不敢相信自己的眼睛。不到一个钟头之前，我们还从山上俯瞰过她：帆已收妥，安安稳稳地伏在锚上。打那以后，究竟可能出了什么变故？

===Original===
We proceeded to the pier, on to which we perceived that the sea was breaking much more heavily than when we had landed on it, and from here we were enabled to see further round the coast to the northwest. Then we caught a glimpse of our vessel just before she rounded, and was hidden by, the first promontory. She was about two miles away, with all plain sail set, beating against the wind towards the northern end of the island.

===Chinese===
我们走向天然码头，看得出浪头扑上码头的势头，比我们登岸那天凶猛得多了；从这里，我们的视野得以沿海岸朝西北伸展开去。恰在她绕过第一道海岬、被岬角遮没之前，我们瞥见了我们的船。她在约两英里外，满帆尽张，正顶风抢行，朝岛的北端驶去。

===Original===
We surmised that those on board had become anxious about our safety, and were sailing round the island in order, if possible, to discover where we were—a course which they had no right to undertake, seeing that the doctor and myself had not yet been two and a half days away, and were not likely to have lost ourselves. Besides which, I knew that there was no one on board competent to take charge of the vessel on a cruise of this sort. Under these circumstances I was in anything but an amiable temper, more especially as the doctor and myself were now fagged out by our exertions, and had been looking forward to a square meal, and some good red wine with it, on our return on board.

===Chinese===
我们猜想船上的人惦记我们的安危，便驾船环岛巡驶，想探明我们的下落——这一举动他们本无权擅自做主：要知道，我和医生离船还不满两天半，断不至迷了路。何况我清楚，船上并没有一个能胜任指挥这等巡航的人。处此情形，我的心绪实在说不上和善，尤其我和医生已劳累得筋疲力尽，一路只盼着回到船上饱餐一顿，再配上几杯上好的红葡萄酒。

===Original===
As it appeared that they were bent on sailing round the island, and might not be off the pier again until the following day—for the yacht was evidently progressing very slowly, plunging her nose constantly into the steep head seas—I determined to recall them, if possible. So we hurried back to a slope near the cascade where the grass was growing thickly, and applied a match to it. As I expected, there was soon a great blaze, and a dense volume of smoke arose which must have made itself visible for many miles around. The wind fanned the flames, and the fire crept slowly up the mountainside wherever the dry grass afforded a track for it; the dead trees, too, began to burn fiercely, and we discovered that we had started a somewhat larger conflagration than we had intended, and had set the whole of this side of the island on fire.

===Chinese===
看这架势，他们是一意要环岛航行了，说不定要到第二天才会再驶回天然码头外——因为游艇分明行得极慢，船首不住扎进陡峭的迎头浪里——我便打定主意，倘若可能，要把他们召回来。于是我们匆匆赶回瀑布附近一片草木茂密的坡地，划了根火柴点上去。果如所料，顷刻间便腾起大火，一股浓烟冲天而起，方圆许多英里之外必然望得见。风煽着火势，火沿着山坡缓缓爬升，凡干草铺得出路的地方都烧将过去；枯树也开始熊熊燃烧。我们这才发现，自己放的这场火远比本意闹得大，竟把岛的整整这一面都点着了。

===Original===
However, it produced the desired effect: we saw the yacht sail clear of the point again, on the starboard tack, bear away, and run down the coast towards us. And now, at the suggestion, as I afterwards learnt, of Arthur Cotton, who ought to have known better, but who, as having been here before with me, professed to be well acquainted with the pilotage of Trinidad, the anchor was let go, to my horror, quite close to the edge of the breakers. Our vessel was now in very convenient proximity to the end of the pier, it is true, but in a most perilous position: for no sea-room had been allowed her—a very necessary precaution under these cliffs, where the wind is never steady—and I saw that, when the anchor was weighed again, we should run great risk of being carried on to the rocks by the rollers before we could get the yacht under command.

===Chinese===
不过，火到底收到了预期的效果：我们望见游艇重新驶出海岬，抢着右舷风，转舵顺浪，沿海岸朝我们驰来。这时——我后来才得知——是亚瑟·科顿（Arthur Cotton）出的主意把锚抛了下去；此人本该更明白些轻重，只因先前随我来过此地，便自诩深谙特林达迪的引航门道。锚落之处，竟紧贴着碎浪的边缘，吓得我魂飞魄散。不错，我们的船此刻离天然码头尽头确实近便得很，处境却凶险至极：没有给她留出半点回旋的水域——在这些崖壁之下，风从无定准，这一防范本是万不可省的——我看得分明，待到再度起锚，不等游艇受舵控制，我们便大有被滚浪掼上礁石之虞。

===Original===
It may be imagined what was my condition of mind when I realised all this, and the doctor was naturally as savage as myself. We stood on the pier and watched the men as they lowered the sails and then launched the whaleboat in order to fetch us off. Powell, Pursell, and two of the paid hands manned the boat. The sea was now so high that they could not approach very near to the shore. The waves were dashing high up the sides of the pier, and, in recoiling, rushed across the end of it in the form of a cascade.

===Chinese===
可以想见，等我明白了这一切，心里是何等光景；医生自然也同我一样怒不可遏。我们站在天然码头上，看着船员们落下帆，随即放下捕鲸艇来接我们。鲍威尔（Powell）、珀塞尔（Pursell）和两名受雇水手上了艇。此刻浪头极高，他们无法靠岸太近。波浪高高地扑打着码头的侧面，退落时又化作一道瀑布，漫过码头尽头奔泻而去。

===Original===
Seeing that we must swim for it, we took off our coats and placed them in a hole at the top of the rocks. I shouted to those in the boat to keep some distance off, and throw a life-buoy with a line attached to it towards the pier, so that we could jump in and be hauled off by it. This was done. Choosing my time I leapt in, held on to the line, the boat was pulled seaward out of reach of the breakers and I clambered on board. Then we returned for the doctor. He stood on the pier, waiting for his opportunity, but one much higher roller than the rest came up and swept him off into the sea. Luckily, he was not dashed against any of the rocks, but managed to swim out clear of the recoil, while we backed towards him and took him on board.

===Chinese===
眼见非泅水不可，我们脱下上衣，塞进岩顶的一个洞穴里。我朝艇上的人大喊，叫他们保持些距离，把一只系着绳索的救生圈朝码头抛来，我们便可跳进水里，由它拽出去。他们依言照办。我瞅准时机纵身入水，攥牢绳索，小艇被拉向外海、避开了碎浪，我攀上艇去。随后我们折返去接医生。他立在码头上静候时机，不料一个比先前都高出许多的滚浪卷上来，把他扫进了海里。所幸他没有撞上任何礁石，挣扎着游离了回头浪的势力，我们倒划着艇迎上去，把他拉了上来。

===Original===
Once safe on the deck of the *Alerte* I listened to an explanation of the extraordinary manoeuvres which had been taking place.

===Chinese===
一登上「阿勒特号」的甲板，安然无恙，我便听人解释起方才那一连串离奇调度的缘由。

===Original===
It seemed that either the yacht had dragged her anchor, or it was supposed that she had dragged her anchor—for the opinions on the matter were at variance—so the anchor was weighed, and, of course, as the chain got short, the yacht, even if she had not done so before, began to drag at a merry pace. Then sail was hoisted. By this time she had drifted very close to the rocks, but, as far as I understand, she was filling and would soon have been in safety again, when, for some reason or other, down went the anchor, and she lay rolling about close under the rocky Ness and the dangerous islets that lie off it. Up came the anchor once more, and this time the yacht drove so very near to the rocks that everyone on board gave her up as lost, and some were looking out for the safest spot on shore to swim to. A high sea was breaking over the cliffs—one touch and she would have broken up. And now, as by a miracle—for I don’t know how it happened, and no one on board seems to have known—the vessel got way on her and forged ahead, so that she became manageable, and was steered out to sea, clear of danger.

===Chinese===
原来，要么是游艇走了锚，要么是众人以为她走了锚——这桩公案众说纷纭——于是起了锚；自然，锚链一短，游艇便开始撒欢似的拖着锚疾漂，哪怕先前并未如此。接着又升起了帆。这时她已漂得离礁石极近；但据我理解，当时她帆已吃满风，眼看就要脱险归安，却不知出于什么缘故，锚又抛了下去，她便紧挨着嶙峋的尼斯岬（Ness）和岬外那几座凶险的小岛，横冲直撞地翻腾摇摆。锚再一度起了上来，这一回游艇直往礁石上逼，近得船上人人只道她完了，有些人已在物色岸上最稳妥、可以泅水逃命的去处。巨浪正扑过崖顶——只消碰上一下，她便粉身碎骨。就在这时，仿佛有神迹一般——因为我不知道这是怎么成的，船上似乎也没人知道——船身得了进速，硬是向前拱去，于是重新受了控，被驶出外海，脱离了险境。

===Original===
That she had been very nearly wrecked there can be no doubt, and that this had been due to very awkward handling was also certain. I was myself much to blame for the serious risk the poor old vessel had incurred. Had I left the doctor in charge on board, in his capacity of mate, while I was exploring the island, he would, no doubt, have extricated the yacht from her difficulty as soon as she began to drag—an easy task. I did not consider that there was anyone else among the volunteers capable of undertaking the responsibility of command, but I was under the impression—wrongly it seems—that the five paid hands on board would have had the common sense to give her more chain when they perceived that the wind was freshening. Ted, for instance, was bos’n, and might have taken it upon himself to do this, as was indeed his understood duty when no officers were on board.

===Chinese===
她险些失事，这是毋庸置疑的；而这场大险全系操作笨拙所致，也是确定无疑。可怜的老船遭此大祸，我自己难辞其咎。我上岛探察时，若让医生以大副的身份留守船上，那么她一开始走锚，他无疑便能即刻解围——那本是轻而易举之事。我原认为志愿队员里没有第二人担当得起指挥的重任，却又以为——如今看来是错了——船上那五名受雇水手总该有这点常识：一见风势转劲，便给她多放些锚链。譬如特德，他就是水手长，本可当机立断亲自去放，船上没有军官时，这本是他心照不宣的职责。

===Original===
For the first and only time during the cruise these men lost their heads, and, having no recognised leader to direct them, each volunteered his own opinion as to what should be done, or as to whether the vessel was dragging at all; but, as far as I can make out, with one man giving one order at one end of the vessel, and another man giving a contradictory order at the other end, nothing at all was done until it was almost too late.

===Chinese===
整个巡航期间，这些人只此一遭乱了方寸——既没有公认的领头人指挥，人人便争着献策，该做什么也好，船到底走没走锚也好，各执一词；但据我拼凑出的情形：船头一人发一道令，船尾另一人又发一道相反的令，结果什么也没办成，直到几乎来不及。

===Original===
I made up my mind never from this time to leave the vessel, even for a short time, without putting someone definitely in charge, even if he were an incompetent person.

===Chinese===
从此我打定主意：日后哪怕离开船只片刻，也必先指定一人明确掌管全船，纵然此人并不称职。

===Original===
But the danger was not all over yet. The vessel was now tumbling about in the high swell at the edge of the breakers, the wind had dropped, and to have weighed the anchor would have been to have run great risk of being carried on to the rocks by the rollers. So, as she was safe where she was for the time, I saw it was advisable to wait until the conditions should be more favourable, before shifting our anchorage. The doctor and myself enjoyed our square meal to which we had been looking forward, and then I turned in to sleep, giving orders that I should be called at four in the afternoon.

===Chinese===
然而危险尚未全了过去。船此刻在碎浪边缘的高涌里翻腾颠簸，风已停息，此时起锚，便大有被滚浪掼上礁石之险。好在她暂泊原地还算安稳，我看还是等条件更有利些再换锚地为妥。我和医生美美享用了一路盼望的那顿饭菜，随后我便翻身睡下，吩咐下午四点叫醒我。

===Original===
At four the sea had gone down a good deal and there was a moderate breeze, so I decided to move to a safer berth. We hoisted the sails and, while we were getting the anchor up, I took the precaution, seeing what little sea-room we had, of putting the whaleboat in the water, with a long line fastened to the yacht’s bows, ready to pull her head round and tow her seawards should she not cant in the right direction.

===Chinese===
到了四点，海浪已平息了不少，又起了和缓的风，我便决意移往更稳妥的泊位。我们升起帆；起锚的当口，我鉴于回旋水域之狭小，预先把捕鲸艇放到水里，艇上系一根长绳，牢缚于游艇船首，万一她不肯转向正确的方向，便随时可以拨转船头，把她拖向外海。

===Original===
We got away safely, and the anchor was let go in nineteen fathoms close to where we had brought up on our arrival.

===Chinese===
我们安然脱身，锚抛在十九英寻深处，紧挨着我们初到时停泊的地方。

===Original===
The night was fine, but the surf was still roaring on the beach. The mountains now presented a curious appearance, for our fire had spread up the various arms of the ravine almost to the summit, and there were clusters of lights, as of villages, in all directions, while here and there what appeared to be bonfires were blazing, possibly at spots where several dead trees had fallen together. We began to fear lest the illumination, which must have been visible for leagues out to sea, might attract the attention of passing vessels. A captain would naturally conclude that these fires were the signals of a shipwrecked crew, and therefore go out of his course to render assistance. Luckily this did not happen.

===Chinese===
夜里天色晴好，海滩上的碎浪却依旧咆哮。群山此刻呈现出一派奇景：我们放的那场火，沿着山谷的条条支臂一路烧了上去，几乎直抵山顶；点点火光成团成簇，宛如村落灯火，四面八方皆是；又处处有篝火模样的东西熊熊燃烧，多半是几棵枯树倒在一处的地方。我们开始担心，这片数里格外的海上都必然望得见的火光，会不会引来过往船只的注意。船长们自然会断定这些火是海难船员的求救信号，从而偏出航线前来救援。所幸这样的事并没有发生。
