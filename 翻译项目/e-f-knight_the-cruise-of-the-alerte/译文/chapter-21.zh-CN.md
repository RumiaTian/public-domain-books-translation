## XXI / 二十一

===Original===
We Abandon the Search

===Chinese===
我们放弃搜寻

===Original===
The five men I had left on the island had certainly done their work well. The doctor had made an excellent leader, and had organised all the operations capitally. They had toiled hard, and had kept up their spirits all the while, and, what is really wonderful under circumstances so calculated to try the temper and wear out patience, they had got on exceedingly well with each other, and there had been no quarrelling or ill-feeling of any sort.

===Chinese===
留在岛上的那五个人，活计干得确实出色。医生证明是位顶好的领头人，各项工程组织得井井有条。他们操劳吃苦，士气却始终不坠；而最难得的是，处在这种最容易磨人性子、耗人耐心的境况里，他们彼此相处得极好，没有吵过一回嘴，没有生过一丝嫌隙。

===Original===
The ravine had been very thoroughly explored, and we felt that there was but little chance of our finding the treasure. It was highly improbable that the massive golden candlesticks of the Cathedral of Lima would ornament our homes in England. It was decided, however, that, if the weather permitted, we should stay here another three weeks or so, and—as we were satisfied that the treasure could not be at the first bend of the ravine—that we should dig in such other spots as appeared suitable hiding-places, and would be naturally selected for the purpose by a party of men landing in this bay.

===Chinese===
裂谷已经探得十分彻底，我们觉得找到宝藏的希望微乎其微。利马大教堂那些粗大的金烛台，多半不会来装点我们在英格兰的宅子了。然而大家议定：天气若容许，就在此地再留三个星期左右；并且——我们既已认定宝藏绝不会藏在裂谷头一道弯处——便改在别处开挖，专挑那些看着像是藏宝之地、一支在这湾里登岸的人马自然而然会选中的所在。

===Original===
The shore-party were glad of a holiday on the yacht after all their labours and privations, and no attempt was made to take the whaleboat through the surf again that day. All hands stayed on board for the night, and on the following morning, as the sea was still breaking too heavily on the beach of Southwest Bay to permit of a landing, I proposed to my companions that we should take another holiday and go for a picnic on the water. The cook, was, therefore, instructed to prepare an especially good dinner, and, after shaking the reefs out of our mainsail, we proceeded to circumnavigate the island, keeping as close to the shore as we were able, so that we could have a good view of the scenery.

===Chinese===
岸上队伍历尽劳苦困顿，如今到游艇上歇一天假，个个欢喜，这一天便没有再试着让捕鲸艇闯碎浪。全体在船上过夜。次日早晨，西南湾滩上的碎浪仍旧崩得太凶，无法登岸，我便向同伴们提议索性再放一天假，到海上野餐一回。于是吩咐厨子备一餐格外丰盛的饭菜，又抖开主帆上的缩帆结，我们便着手环岛航行，尽量贴着岸边走，好把沿途景致看个痛快。

===Original===
We sailed by the different points which we now knew so well—the Ness, the Pier, the Ninepin—and at last doubled North Point. This extremity of the island is extremely wild and desolate, and is utterly inaccessible. Many of the sharp pinnacles which cap the mountains are out of the perpendicular, and lean threateningly over the sea. I have already explained that the different species of birds occupy different portions of the island; the crags by North Point are inhabited by the frigate-birds and sea-hawks.

===Chinese===
我们驶过一座座如今已熟稔之至的海岬——「内斯」、天然码头、「九柱石」——末了绕过北角。岛的这一端极其荒凉僻远，全然无法靠近。许多覆在山顶的尖峰都已偏离垂直，阴森森地朝大海倾侧过来。我早已说明过，不同种类的海鸟各占岛的一隅；北角一带的峭壁上，栖的是军舰鸟和海鹰。

===Original===
We coasted along the weather side of the island, and when we were nearly opposite to the Portuguese settlement the wind dropped and we had to man the whaleboat and tow the yacht seaward; for we found that she was gradually sagging before the swell towards the reefs, on which the sea was breaking heavily. We could not get round the island, so sailed back, before a very light wind, to Southwest Bay, and hove to as usual for the night.

===Chinese===
我们沿着岛的向风一岸行驶，待到差不多与葡萄牙人的屯居地遥遥相对时，风息了，只得派人上捕鲸艇，把游艇朝外海拖去；因为我们发觉她正被涌浪缓缓压向礁石，而礁上的浪崩得凶猛。环岛一周既然办不成，我们便乘着极微的风驶回西南湾，照例停船过夜。

---

===Original===
Work was resumed the next day, and a boatload of stores was sent on shore. The newly-formed sandbank which I have mentioned appeared to increase and become a more serious obstacle to landing every day. On this occasion the boat again drove her stem into the sand as she crossed this shoal, and the next wave swamped and capsized her, so that boat, men, and stores were tumbling about in the deep water between the sandbank and the shore.

===Chinese===
次日复工，一艇给养送上了岸。我提过的那道新生的沙洲，似乎天天在长大，对登岸的阻碍一天重似一天。这一回，小艇横渡浅洲时，艇首又插进了沙里，下一道浪当即把她灌满掀翻，艇、人、给养，一股脑儿在沙洲与海岸之间的深水里翻腾打滚。

===Original===
They managed to haul the boat safely up, and, by diving in the surf, recovered a good many of the tins of food. Then the boat returned to the yacht, Joe being left alone in the camp. He did not relish this at all, for, like most black men, he was very afraid of ghosts, and had come to the conclusion that Trinidad was a place more than usually haunted by unsettled spirits. He told us that if he were left alone on shore for the night his only course would be to light a ring of fires and sit in the middle, with a tight bandage round his head, keeping awake till dawn. If he failed to take these precautions he would most certainly be torn to pieces, or otherwise seriously damaged, by the spirits. We took compassion on him and did not leave him to face the terrors of the darkness alone. In the afternoon the whaleboat returned to the bay, and Pollock swam on shore to remain with him.

===Chinese===
他们好歹把小艇安全拖了上去，又靠在碎浪里潜进潜出，捞回了为数不少的食物罐头。随后小艇返回游艇，营地里只留下乔一个人。这事他老大不情愿，因为像多数黑人一样，他极怕鬼，而且认定特林达迪这地方，孤魂野鬼闹得比别处更凶。他告诉我们，若让他独自在岸上过夜，唯一的办法便是点起一圈火，自己坐在当中，头上缠一条扎得紧紧的布带，直熬到天亮。这些防备若少了一样，他准会被鬼魂撕成碎片，至少也要吃个大亏。我们可怜他，没有让他独自去面对黑夜的恐怖。下午，捕鲸艇回到湾里，波洛克游上岸去陪伴他。

===Original===
A description of what happened for the next few days would be merely a repetition of what has gone before. The yacht was hove to at night, and sailed about the mouth of the bay all day. The surf was always breaking dangerously on the sands, so that it was impossible to beach the boat, and the men had to swim to and fro from whaleboat to shore, or haul themselves along a line which we had rigged up for the purpose, and which was carried from a rock on shore to a buoy moored with the ship’s kedge outside the breakers. We used also to haul the provisions on shore with a line, having lashed them to the bamboo rafts which we had constructed for this purpose.

===Chinese===
此后几天的事情，若再描述，不过是前文的重复。游艇夜里停船，白天在湾口一带游弋。碎浪总是凶险地崩在沙滩上，小艇无法抢滩，人们只得在捕鲸艇与岸之间来回泅渡，或者攀着一条专为这事架起的绳索，一把一把把自己挪过去——绳索一头系在岸上一块岩石上，另一头系在碎浪之外、用船上小锚泊住的一只浮标上。给养也是用绳子拖上岸的：先把它们捆在我们为此扎制的竹筏上，再拖将过去。

===Original===
The weather became so unsettled and the surf was so invariably high that, after a few days, we came to the conclusion that the sooner we left the island the better, and we decided to take the first favourable opportunity for bringing off our property from the shore. The bad season was approaching—if it had not already commenced—and if we waited much longer we might find it impossible, for months at a time, to carry off stores or men. The yacht only remained hove to for eleven days after the shore-party had first boarded us, and during that time the men with me on the vessel were employed in setting up the rigging, rattling down the shrouds, and effecting all necessary repairs.

===Chinese===
天气变得这般反复无常，碎浪又总是那么高，过了几天我们便断定：离岛越早越好，于是决定一有时机，先把岸上的财物家当撤下来。恶劣的季节正在逼近——兴许已经开始了——再拖延下去，说不定会落到接连几个月既运不走物资、也接不走人的地步。岸上队伍初次回到船上之后，游艇统共只又停泊了十一天；这些日子里，船上随我的人手忙着张紧索具、给桅侧支索扎装横索，并进行一切必要的修理。

===Original===
There was nearly always a high swell running now, which was especially uncomfortable when there was no wind, for then we would often roll scuppers under. For nearly a week it was quite impossible to beach the boat, and all communication with the shore had to be effected in the way I have described above. At last, on February 13, luckily for us, it was exceptionally calm in Southwest Bay, so that it would be very easy to carry off our stores.

===Chinese===
这时海上几乎总涌着高浪，无风时尤其难受，因为船常常摇得连甲板排水口都没进水里。将近一个星期，小艇全然无法抢滩，与岸上的一切往来，只好照我上面说的法子进行。到了 2 月 13 日，我们运气好，西南湾里风平浪静得反常，把存粮撤运下来正是易如反掌。

===Original===
Such a chance was not to be lost. In the morning all hands went off in the boats, with the exception of myself and Wright, who stayed on board to work the vessel. A landing was effected without any difficulty, and the boats returned with heavy loads, bringing off the hydraulic jack, the guns, the bedding, and other articles.

===Chinese===
这样的机会岂容错失。早晨，除我和赖特留在船上操船之外，全体乘艇而去。登岸毫无波折，小艇载着沉重的载荷一趟趟往返，把液压千斤顶、枪械、铺盖和其他物件都运了回来。

===Original===
I, of course, wished to see what work had been done, before giving my final decision as to the continuance or abandonment of our exploration—not that there was any doubt as to what that decision would be, after I had heard the doctor’s report. In the afternoon I went off in the whaleboat, and landed on the island for the first time for forty-eight days, leaving the doctor in charge of the yacht while she lay hove to outside the bay. I had not put foot on shore here for so long that I was astonished at the aspect of the ravine, which had been completely changed in my absence by the labours of my comrades.

===Chinese===
当然，在最终裁决这番勘探是续是止之前，我要亲眼看看干成了什么——虽说听过医生的报告，裁决会是什么，本已毫无悬念。下午，我乘捕鲸艇过去，四十八天来头一回登上这座岛，游艇停泊在湾外，交由医生照管。我这么久不曾在此上岸，一见裂谷的模样不由得吃了一惊——我不在的这些日子，同伴们的辛劳已把它彻底改了容颜。

===Original===
I stood and contemplated the melancholy scene—the great trenches, the piled-up mounds of earth, the uprooted rocks, with broken wheelbarrows and blocks, worn out tools, and other relics of our three months’ work strewed over the ground; and it was sad to think that all the energy of these men had been spent in vain. They well deserved to succeed, and all the more so because they bore their disappointment with such philosophic cheeriness.

===Chinese===
我伫立着，凝望这片凄凉的景象——巨大的壕沟、堆积的土丘、翻起的岩石，摔坏的独轮车和滑车、使废的工具，还有我们三个月劳作留下的其他遗迹，撒了一地；想到这些人的气力尽数付诸流水，实在叫人伤感。他们是完全配得上成功的，尤其难得的是，他们把这份失意承受得那般达观快活。

===Original===
It was, obviously, quite useless to persevere any further in this vain search, especially as the difficulties of landing had so increased of late that our operations could only be conducted at a great risk to life. So the fiat went forth—the expedition was to be abandoned; we were to clear out of Trinidad, bag and baggage, as quickly as we could.

===Chinese===
显而易见，这场徒劳的搜寻再撑下去已毫无意义，何况近来登岸的难处陡增，一切作业都得拿性命去冒大险。于是裁决下达：远征就此放弃；我们要卷起全部家当，尽快撤出特林达迪。

===Original===
We returned to the yacht with a good load of stores, the condensing apparatus, and the faithful Jacko. After dinner we sailed round to the cascade and hove to off it. I remained on board with Wright while all the other hands went off in the boats and obtained six casks of water to replenish the ship’s now nearly empty tanks. This was altogether a most satisfactory day’s work, and we were very well pleased with ourselves when we hove to at sunset and drifted out to the ocean for our well-deserved night’s rest.

===Chinese===
我们载着满满一艇给养、那套蒸馏器，还有忠心的杰科，回到游艇上。正餐之后，我们驶往瀑布外海，在那里停船。我同赖特留在船上，其余人手分乘小艇前去，取回六桶水，灌进船上业已近乎见底的水柜。这一天的工作从头到尾都叫人满意；日落时停船当风，漂向外海，去享受这一晚理所应得的安歇，我们心里都颇为自得。

===Original===
On the following morning—Friday, 14th—we tacked to the north of Southwest Bay, and found that, though there was more surf than on the previous day, landing was feasible. The boat went off under the doctor’s charge, and the tents and all the remaining stores were brought safely on board. Nothing of any value was left; we not only carried off our own tools, but also the picks that had been used by Mr. A——’s expedition. Only broken wheelbarrows and suchlike useless articles remained in the ravine. From the vessel the only sign of our late camp that could be seen was Powell’s disabled armchair, which he had left standing, a melancholy object, on the top of the beach.

===Chinese===
次日早晨——星期五，14 日——我们抢风驶到西南湾以北，发现碎浪虽比前一天更凶，登岸却还办得成。小艇由医生领队前去，帐篷和所有剩余给养都平安运上了船。任何值钱的东西都没有留下；我们不但运走了自家的工具，连 A——先生远征队用过的铁镐也一并带了回来。裂谷里剩下的，只有摔坏的独轮车之类无用的杂物。从船上望去，我们那座营地留下的唯一痕迹，便是鲍威尔那把残破的扶手椅——它孤零零立在滩顶上，一副凄凉模样。

===Original===
We stowed the heavier tools and stores under the saloon floor and then sailed again to the cascade. The whaleboat went off to the pier and a quantity of water was brought on board, so that we had a sufficient supply—but not much to spare—for the voyage we now contemplated.

===Chinese===
我们把较重的工具和给养收进主舱地板之下，随后再度驶往瀑布外海。捕鲸艇开到天然码头，又运回一批淡水；这样，对于眼下筹划的这段航程，我们的水已算充足——只是没有多少富余。

===Original===
When the watering-party returned we had done with Trinidad; so both boats were hoisted on deck, and a melancholy ceremony was performed: our very ancient dinghy, which was too rotten to bear any further patching, and was not worth the room she used to take up on deck, was broken up and handed over to the cook as firewood.

===Chinese===
取水的人们一回来，我们便同特林达迪两讫了；两条艇都被吊上甲板，随后举行了一场凄凉的仪式：我们那条年迈的小划艇，朽得再经不起修补，占着甲板上那块地方又不值得，便被拆散，交给厨子当柴烧了。

===Original===
A tot of rum was served out to each hand, we bade farewell to Trinidad, the foresail was allowed to draw, and we sailed away.

===Chinese===
每人分发了一盅朗姆酒，我们向特林达迪道了别，放开前帆兜风，扬帆而去。

===Original===
It had long since been decided that, whether the treasure was discovered or not, we should sail from our desert island to its wealthy namesake, Trinidad in the West Indies—a very different sort of a place. The distance between the two Trinidads is, roughly, 2,900 miles; but we knew that the voyage before us was not likely to be a lengthy one, for everything is in favour of a vessel bound the way we were going. In the first place, it was very unlikely that we should encounter head winds between our islet and Cape St. Roque, and from that point we should most probably have the wind right aft for the rest of the way, as the trade-winds blow regularly along the coasts of north Brazil and the Guianas. In the next place, by sailing at a certain distance from the land, we could keep our vessel in the full strength of the south equatorial current, which runs at the rate of two or three miles an hour in the direction of our course. We had, it is true, to cross the line once more, with its belt of doldrums; but we knew that we should not be much delayed by these tedious equatorial calms, as they do not prevail on the coast of Brazil to anything like the extent they do in mid-Atlantic; besides which, the favourable current would be carrying us along with it across the belt, and enable us to travel fifty miles or so a day, even in a flat calm.

===Chinese===
早日已经议定：无论宝藏找不找得到，我们都要驶离这座荒岛，前往它那位富庶的同名者——西印度群岛的特立尼达，一个全然不同的地方。这两座同名的岛屿相距约 2,900 英里；但我们知道，前方的航程不见得漫长，因为天时地利样样都眷顾朝这个方向去的船。第一，从我们这座小岛到圣罗克角（Cape St. Roque）之间，遇上逆风的可能微乎其微；过了那里，余下的一路多半是正正的尾风，因为信风沿着巴西北部和圭亚那一带海岸吹得规规矩矩。第二，只要离岸保持一定距离行驶，就能让船吃足南赤道海流的全力——这股海流以每小时两三英里的速度，正朝我们的航向奔流。诚然，我们还得再穿越一次赤道，连同它的那条赤道无风带；但我们知道，那些磨人的赤道平滞不至于怎么耽搁行程，因为在巴西沿海，它们远不像在大西洋中部那般肆虐；况且那股顺流还会载着我们横渡这条地带，哪怕海面纹丝不动，一天也能走上五十英里上下。

===Original===
This kindly current would, indeed, carry us straight to our port, for it sweeps through the Gulf of Paria as well as by the east side of Trinidad, and, as every schoolboy knows in these enlightened days, thence flows round the Caribbean Sea and ultimately emerges from it under another and better-known title—the Gulf Stream.

===Chinese===
这股好心的海流，当真能把我们径直送到目的港：它既掠过特立尼达的东侧，也穿过帕里亚湾（Gulf of Paria）；而且——在这开化的年代，凡学童都知道——它从那里环绕加勒比海流淌，最终以另一个更为人熟知的名号流出这片海域，那便是「墨西哥湾流」（Gulf Stream）。

===Original===
With the old *Falcon* I had sailed over a portion of this route, accomplishing the voyage from Pernambuco to Georgetown, Demerara—a distance of about 2,000 miles—in ten days, thus keeping up an average of 200 miles a day. At this rate the *Alerte* ought to get to Trinidad in fifteen days; but we were not fated to have such luck as that.

===Chinese===
当年乘老「猎鹰号」，我走过这段航线的一部分：从伯南布哥到德梅拉拉的乔治敦（Georgetown, Demerara）——约 2,000 英里的路程——只用了十天，平均一天 200 英里。照这个速度，「阿勒特号」本该十五天就到特立尼达；可惜我们命中没有这等好运。
