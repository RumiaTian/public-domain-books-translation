## V / 五

===Original===
Our First Voyage

===Chinese===
我们的首次航行

===Original===
Our preparations were hurried on at Southampton, and I was never left in peace, but was in a condition of perpetual work and travel, my sole relaxation being the frequent farewell dinners given to myself and my companions by our friends and sympathisers; and very jolly as these dinners were, they were relaxations in the other sense of the term rather than reposeful amusements for a weary man. Some of them were arduous undertakings.

===Chinese===
在南安普敦，各项准备工作加紧推进，我从没有片刻安宁，整天价操劳奔波不歇；唯一的松快，是朋友和支持者们频频为我与同伴们设下的饯行宴。这些宴席固然欢畅热闹，但对一个疲惫的人来说，与其说是安闲的消遣，不如说是「松弛」一词另一种意义上的考验——有几场简直称得上艰巨的工程。

===Original===
Our expedition interested the Southampton people a good deal, and all wished us well; but I do not think many thought that we should be successful in realizing our fortunes on Trinidad.

===Chinese===
我们的远征令南安普敦人大感兴趣，人人祝我们一路平安；但我看，相信我们当真能在特林达迪圆了发财梦的，并没有几个。

===Original===
At last all was ready for our departure, when to my considerable disgust, just as we were about to put to sea, two of the volunteers suddenly found themselves prevented from going with us.

===Chinese===
终于万事俱备、只待启航，不料正当我们即将出海之际，两名志愿者竟突然发现自己无法同行，着实叫我恼火。

===Original===
I forthwith telegraphed to others on my list of applicants, and at the very last moment received telegrams from two gentlemen who were willing to join at this short notice. When their messages arrived, all my crew and other companions were on board, comfortably settled down, having bidden their farewells and done with the shore; so I thought it prudent to send them away from Southampton, where the *Alerte* was perpetually surrounded by boatfuls of visitors, to the seclusion of the little bay under Calshot Castle at the mouth of Southampton Water. Here they would be out of the way of temptation, as there are no buildings save the coastguard station.

===Chinese===
我当即给应征者名单上的其他人拍去电报，并在最后一刻接到两位先生的回电，说他们愿意在如此仓促的通知下加入进来。这几封电报送达时，我的船员和其他同伴都已登船安顿妥当，别已道过、岸上的事已了结；因此我想，还是让他们离开南安普敦为妥——在那儿，「阿勒特号」身边总有一船一船载满访客的小船围个没完——于是把船移往南安普敦水道（Southampton Water）河口、卡尔肖特城堡（Calshot Castle）之下那座幽僻的小湾。在那里，他们远离种种诱惑，因为除了海岸警卫队的站房，四下再无别的屋舍。

===Original===
Therefore, on the evening of August 28, 1889, the *Alerte* sailed slowly down to Calshot, and came to an anchor there, while I waited at Southampton until the following morning, with the object of securing my new volunteers as soon as they should arrive, and carrying them down to the yacht.

===Chinese===
于是，1889 年 8 月 28 日傍晚，「阿勒特号」缓缓驶往卡尔肖特，在那里落了锚；我则留在南安普敦，直等到次日清晨，好在新志愿者一到，便把他们接应下来，带到游艇上去。

===Original===
The said volunteers turned up early on August 29. Then, with a party of some of my old Southampton friends, we steamed down the river on a launch which had been very kindly placed at our disposal for the purpose by the Isle of Wight Steamboat Company. Mr. Picket, of course, would have nothing to do with work in his yard on that day; he took a holiday and came down to see the last of us.

===Chinese===
那两位志愿者于 8 月 29 日一早到了。随后，我同几位南安普敦的老友登上一艘小汽艇，顺河而下——那艘汽艇是怀特岛轮船公司（Isle of Wight Steamboat Company）好意拨给我们专用的。皮克特先生那一天自然全不管他船坞里的活计；他歇了一天工，特地赶来送我们最后一程。

===Original===
We were now all on board; but, finding that some of the fresh stores, such as vegetables and bread, had not yet arrived, we postponed our departure until the following day. In the meanwhile we were not idle; we sent a boat to the Hamble River to fill up those breakers that had been emptied, we got our whaleboat on deck and secured it, and, in short, made all ready for sea.

===Chinese===
此刻我们已全员在船；但发现蔬菜、面包之类的新鲜给养尚未送到，便把启航推迟到翌日。这段时间我们也没闲着：派了一条艇去汉布尔河（Hamble River），把腾空的淡水桶一一灌满；把捕鲸艇吊上甲板缚牢；总而言之，出海的准备样样停当。

===Original===
On the following day the Isle of Wight boat, while passing, left the missing stores with us; then Mr. Picket’s sloop sailed down with some friends who had determined to bid us even yet another last farewell; and, after dinner, we weighed anchor and were off, while the friends on the sloop and the crew of a yacht which was brought up near us gave us a hearty goodbye in British cheers.

===Chinese===
第二天，怀特岛的班船经过时，把那批迟到的给养转到了我们手上；随后，皮克特先生的单桅帆船也载着几位朋友驶了下来——他们打定主意，非要再给我们道一次「最后的」诀别不可；饭后，我们起锚出发，单桅船上的朋友和停泊在我们近旁的一条游艇的船员们，用英伦式的欢呼给我们送上热腾腾的道别。

===Original===
But our anchor had not yet had its last hold of English mud, and we were not to lose sight of the Solent that day; for, in consequence of some clumsiness, or possibly too much zeal on the part of those who were catting the anchor, the bowsprit whisker on the starboard side was doubled up; so we had to proceed to Cowes, and bring up there while we sent the iron on shore to be put in the fire and straightened again. However, this did not delay us much, for it fell a flat calm, which lasted through the night; we were better off sleeping comfortably at anchor than we should have been drifting helplessly up and down with the tides.

===Chinese===
但我们的锚还不曾最后一回咬住英格兰的泥底，那一天我们也注定离不开索伦特海峡（the Solent）的望界；因为收锚的人手不知是笨拙了些，还是热心得过了头，把右舷的船首斜桁撑杆弄折弯了；于是我们只得驶往考斯，在那里停泊，把那根铁件送上岸，进炉烧红再敲直。好在这一点也误不了多少事：入夜后海面平澜无风，我们睡在锚上安安稳稳，总比无可奈何地随潮水漂来荡去强。

===Original===
At 11 a.m. the next morning, it being high-water, we weighed anchor, and were really off at last, the weather glorious and hot, but the wind light and variable.

===Chinese===
第二天上午 11 时，正值满潮，我们起锚出海，这一回是真正动身了；天气晴美炎热，只是风轻而游移不定。

===Original===
For weeks, while we had been lying off Southampton, the weather had been detestable﻿—blusterous northwest winds, accompanied by heavy rains, prevailing. But now, very opportunely for us, a complete change set in just as we started, and it was evident that we were at the commencement of a long spell of settled fine weather. I had anticipated this luck; for I knew by experience that the last weeks of August and the first weeks in September are the most favourable for a voyage south across the bay, for then there generally comes a period of moderate easterly winds and warm weather, which precedes the stormy season of the equinox. Thus, when I sailed in the *Falcon* at this very time of the year, I was fortunate enough to carry a northeast wind all the way from Southampton into the northeast trades, and I was confident that we were destined to do something of the sort now; nor was I disappointed.

===Chinese===
先前我们在南安普敦外停泊的那几个星期，天气可恶至极——狂暴的西北风当令，还夹着滂沱大雨。可如今，正当我们启程，天气竟来了个彻底的转变，时机好得不能再好；显而易见，一段长久的晴稳天气正要开场。这份运气我早有预料；凭经验我知道，8 月的末几个星期和 9 月的头几个星期，最宜扬帆南下横渡比斯开湾（Bay of Biscay），因为那时节往往有一段温和的东风和暖热的天气，正赶在秋分的风暴季节之前。当年我驾「猎鹰号」出海，恰是一年中的这个时节，有幸从南安普敦一路乘着东北风，直驶进东北信风带；我满有把握，这一回我们注定也能有此一番造化；后来果然不曾辜负我。

===Original===
We got outside the Needles, and, the wind being light from west to southwest, we tacked very slowly down Channel, always in sight of the English coast, until nightfall, when the wind dropped altogether, and we lay becalmed in sight of Portland lights. It was our first Saturday night at sea (August 31), so we kept up the good old fashion of drinking to our wives and sweethearts at eight o’clock. We never neglected this sacred duty on any Saturday night during the whole cruise. A light air from the east sprang up at night, but, though we now had racing spinnaker and topsail on the vessel, we made little progress, and it seemed as if we could not lose sight of the lights of Portland.

===Chinese===
我们驶出尼德尔斯（the Needles），风从西到西南，轻轻地吹，我们一路迎风换抢，极慢地沿海峡下行，英格兰海岸始终在望；直到暮色四合，风全然止息，我们呆浮在波特兰（Portland）灯火的望界之内。这是我们出海后的第一个星期六之夜（8 月 31 日），晚上 8 点，我们遵照美好的老传统，举杯敬各自的妻子与情人。整个巡航期间，无论哪个星期六夜晚，这项神圣的义务我们从不曾荒废。夜里东方起了一缕轻风；但尽管船上这时已张起赛艇用的大三角帆和上桅帆，我们依旧寸进有限，仿佛怎么也甩不开波特兰的灯火。

===Original===
Throughout the following day﻿—September 1﻿—the same far too fine weather continued, with light airs from various directions, alternating with calms. But we did at last contrive to get out of sight of land this day; Portland, to our delight, became invisible, and we saw no more of the English coast.

===Chinese===
翌日——9 月 1 日——从早到晚，依旧是那晴得过了头的天气，四面八方来的丝丝软风与无风交替出现。但这一天我们终究设法驶出了陆地的望界；波特兰，令我们大喜，终于从视野里消失，英格兰海岸从此再未入目。

===Original===
This calm weather was trying to the patience; but it was perhaps well for us to have this experience at the commencement of the voyage; for it enabled the raw hands to settle down to their work quickly, and there was but little seasickness on board.

===Chinese===
这样的无风天气最磨耐性；但在航程之初经历这一遭，于我们或许倒是好事：生手们得以迅速定下心来投入各自的活计，船上也没几个人晕船。

===Original===
At midday, September 2, we were off the chops of the Channel, a fresh easterly wind that lasted some hours having carried us so far. Then the wind fell again, and we sailed on in a very leisurely fashion until the morning of September 5, when, being well in the middle of the Bay of Biscay, the wind, which was from the southeast, began gradually to freshen. First we were going five knots through the water, then seven, and by midday we were travelling between eight and nine. In the afternoon the wind increased to the force of a moderate gale and the sea began to rise. During the night some rather high seas rolled up after us occasionally, so that we had to bear away and run before them, and only the old hands could be entrusted with the tiller. We passed Finisterre on this night, but were too far off to see the lights; and now we had done with the Bay of Biscay, which had certainly treated the *Alerte* with great consideration, and not shown us any of its proverbial bad temper. The wind had gone down by midday on the 6th, and the run for the previous twenty-four hours was found to have been 158 miles.

===Chinese===
9 月 2 日正午，我们已行至英吉利海峡的口门，是一阵刮了几个小时的清劲东风把我们送到这里的。随后风又落了，我们从容不迫地一路缓驶，直到 9 月 5 日早晨；其时我们正处比斯开湾的当心，风从东南来，开始渐渐加强。起初船行水中五节，继而七节，到正午已在八至九节之间。午后，风力增至八级大风，海面开始涌起。入夜，偶有相当高的浪从船后滚来，我们只得转舵顺浪奔行，舵柄唯有老水手才托付得出。这一夜我们驶过了菲尼斯特雷角，只是相距太远，望不见灯火；至此比斯开湾算是走完了——它待「阿勒特号」实在优礼有加，那句出了名的坏脾气，半分也不曾让我们领教。6 日正午，风势已平，查得前二十四小时的航程为 158 英里。

===Original===
From this date we kept up a fair average speed; though our voyage could not be termed a smart one, for there was scarcely a day on which we were not retarded by several hours of calm.

===Chinese===
自那日起，我们保持着相当不错的平均航速；只是这一程算不得爽利，几乎无一日不被几个钟头的无风耽搁一遭。

===Original===
While going down Channel we had kept watch and watch in the usual sea fashion, the first mate taking one watch and myself the other. But now that we were out at sea, clear of all danger, it became unnecessary to continue this somewhat wearisome four hours up and four hours down system; so we divided ourselves into three watches, the second mate taking the third watch. This gave the men an eight hours’ rest below at a stretch, instead of only four. As we had three paid hands in addition to the cook, one of these was allotted to each watch. But before reaching the South American coast the second mate resigned his post, and we reverted to the watch-and-watch system again, which was observed until the termination of the cruise.

===Chinese===
沿海峡下行时，我们照海上的老规矩两班轮值，大副带一班，我带另一班。如今到了外海，脱离了一切险地，这套四小时当值、四小时下休的磨人班制便无必要再守；于是我们分作三班，二副带第三班。这样一来，弟兄们在舱下一次便可连歇八个钟头，而不止四个。我们除厨子外另有三名受雇的水手，便各班分派一名。但尚未抵达南美洲海岸，二副便辞了职，我们又改回两班轮值制，一直沿用至巡航终了。

===Original===
A good deal of useless form was kept up at this early stage of the voyage. A log-slate was suspended in the saloon, and each officer as he came below would write up a full account of all that had occurred in his watch. The most uninteresting details were minutely chronicled﻿—only to be rubbed off the slate each midday, and I think there was a little disappointment expressed because I would not copy all these down in my logbook. Had I done so that logbook would have been a dreadful volume to peruse.

===Chinese===
航程初期，还有不少无谓的排场被一本正经维持着。舱室里悬起一块航海石板，每位带班的人下值时，都要把本班发生的种种巨细无遗地记上一番。最索然无味的细枝末节也被一一载录——只为每天正午再擦个干净。我不肯把这些统统抄进我的航海日志，我记得他们还流露了几分失望。真抄了，那本日志可就成了不忍卒读的一部大部头。

===Original===
To us, however, the log-slate was a source of great amusement on account of its utter fallaciousness. The patent log was, of course, put overboard when we were making the land, but when we were out on the ocean and no land was near us we naturally did not take the trouble to do this, neither did we make use of the common log-ship or keep a strict dead reckoning. But, despite this, the officer of a watch would religiously jot down the exact number of knots and furlongs he professed to have sailed during each of his four hours on duty; he did not even try to guess the distance to the best of his ability; he was fired with an ambition to show the best record for his watch; so he would first scan the slate to see how many knots the officer just relieved boasted to have accomplished, and then he would unblushingly write down a slightly greater number of miles as the result of his own watch, quite regardless of any fall in the wind or other retarding cause.

===Chinese===
不过在我们看来，这块石板全凭胡编，倒成了极大的笑料。当然，行近陆地时，我们会把机械计程仪抛出船外；但在大洋之上、四望无陆之时，我们自然犯不着费这道手脚，既不用寻常的计程板，也不严守航迹推算。尽管如此，每班带班的人照旧虔诚地记下他自称在四小时值班里驶过的精确节数与弗隆数；他甚至不肯费心尽力估一估距离；他一心烧着热望，只求自己这一班拿出最漂亮的记录；于是先去细看石板，查一查刚交班的那位夸口驶了几节，然后面不改色地写下比之略多的英里数，算作自己这一班的成绩，全然不管风曾落了多少，或有什么别的耽搁。

===Original===
Thus: if five knots an hour had been made in one watch, five and a quarter would probably be logged for the next, and five and a half for the next. Sometimes there was a flat calm throughout a watch, and then the ingenious officer, though he could not help himself and was compelled to write himself down a zero before three of the hours, would compensate for this by putting down a big number in front of that hour during which he imagined that all the individuals of his rival watches were fast asleep below, and would boldly assert in explanation that just then he had been favoured with a strong squall to help him along.

===Chinese===
比如：这一班驶了每小时五节，下一班多半便记五又四分之一节，再下一班五又二分之一节。有时整整一班海面死寂，那位匠心独运的带班人虽然无可奈何，只得在三个钟头前都写下零，却会在另一个钟头前面填个大数把它补回来——那个钟头，据他盘算，对头各班的诸位正在舱下酣睡——并且振振有词地解释：恰在彼时，天公作美，赏了他一阵强风，助他前行。

===Original===
No one put any confidence in this mendacious slate, which soon became known on board as the “Competition Log,” and inspired our wits with many merry quips. The distance made in each twenty-four hours as recorded by the Competition Log was about fifty percent greater than that calculated from the observations of the sun.

===Chinese===
这块撒谎成性的石板没有一个人当真，它很快在全船得了个「竞赛计程簿」的诨名，还引得我们妙语连珠。照「竞赛计程簿」所载，每二十四小时的航程，比按太阳观测推算出来的足足多出约莫五成。

===Original===
At last, on the morning of September 13, having been fourteen days at sea, and having accomplished a voyage of something under fifteen hundred miles, we knew that we were in the close vicinity of the Salvages, and a sharp lookout for land was accordingly kept. We had seen nothing but water round us since leaving Portland Bill, and all on board were excited at the prospect of so soon discovering what manner of place was this desert treasure-island of which we had been talking so much.

===Chinese===
终于，到 9 月 13 日早晨，我们出海已十四天，航程将近一千五百英里，都知道萨尔瓦任斯群岛已近在咫尺，于是一双双眼加紧瞭望起陆地来。自驶离波特兰角（Portland Bill），我们四围所见唯有海水；我们念叨了这么久的这座荒凉宝岛，究竟是个什么样去处，眼看顷刻便要揭晓，全船上下无不兴奋。

===Original===
The Salvages lie between Madeira and the Canaries, being 160 miles from the former and about 85 from Teneriffe. Vessels avoid their vicinity, especially at night, on account of the dangerous shoals that surround them. The description of the group in the *North Atlantic Memoir* is as follows:﻿

===Chinese===
萨尔瓦任斯群岛位于马德拉与加那利群岛之间，距前者 160 英里，距特内里费（Teneriffe）约 85 英里。船只都避开它附近的海面，尤其夜间，因为群岛四周尽是险恶的浅滩。《北大西洋志略》（North Atlantic Memoir）关于这群岛的描述如下：——

===Original===
“The Salvages consist of an island named the Ilha Grande, or the Great Salvage, a larger island named Great Piton, and a smaller one called the Little Piton, together with several rocks. The Great Salvage lies in lat. 30° 8′, long. 15° 55′. It is of very irregular shape, and has a number of rocks about it within the distance of a mile. It is much intersected, and has several deep inlets, the most accessible of which is on the east side. It is covered with bushes, amongst which the thousands of sea-fowl make their nests. It is surrounded on all sides with dangers, most of which show, but many require all caution in approaching.

===Chinese===
「萨尔瓦任斯群岛由一座名叫伊利亚格兰德（Ilha Grande）——即大萨尔瓦任岛——的岛，一座较大的名叫大皮通的岛，一座较小的名叫小皮通（Little Piton）的岛，以及几处岩礁组成。大萨尔瓦任岛位于北纬 30° 8′、西经 15° 55′。岛的形状极不规整，一英里之内四周尚有多处岩礁。全岛深深割裂，有几道深邃的水湾，最易进出的一道在东侧。岛上灌木丛生，千万只海鸟在其间做窝。四面八方皆有险地环绕，大多数露出水面，但也有不少，接近时须打起全部的小心。

===Original===
“The Great Piton lies at the distance of 8¼ miles W. S. W. ¾ W. from Ilha Grande. This islet is 2⅜ miles long, and has a hill or peak near its centre. The Little Piton lies at a mile from the western side of the former, and is three-quarters of a mile long; both are comparatively narrow. These isles are seated upon and surrounded by one dangerous rocky bank, which extends from the western side of the little isle half a league to the westward.”﻿ ﻿… “The southern part of the Great Piton appears green, its northern part barren. It may be seen 5 or 6 leagues off. The Little Piton is very flat, and is connected to the south point of the greater one by a continued ledge of rocks. The whole of the eastern side of the Great Piton is rocky and dangerous.”

===Chinese===
