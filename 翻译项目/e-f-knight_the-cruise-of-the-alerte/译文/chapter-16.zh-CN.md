## XVI / 十六

===Original===
Discoveries in Southwest Bay

===Chinese===
西南湾的种种发现

===Original===
At last, on December 7, communication between the yacht and the shore was resumed; for the wind and sea had greatly moderated, and the doctor was enabled to come off to us at midday, with four volunteers and paid hands. They had been labouring hard with pick and shovel, and looked like it too. Digging into the volcanic soil of Trinidad soon takes all superfluous flesh off. Indeed, led on by the energetic doctor, they had worked harder, perhaps, than white men should in such a climate, and had a stale overstrained appearance, while they admitted that they felt somewhat slack.

===Chinese===
终于，到 12 月 7 日，游艇与岸上的交通恢复了；因为风浪已大为平息，医生得以在正午带着四名志愿队员和几名受雇水手到船上来。他们一直抡着十字镐和铁锹苦干，模样也着实像是干过苦工的。在特林达迪的火山土里刨掘，用不了多久便能把一身多余的肉都削下来。的确，在那位精力充沛的医生的带动下，他们干的活计，恐怕已超过了白人在这种气候下应有的限度，个个显出一副疲沓透支的神色，自己也承认觉得有些发乏。

===Original===
They brought us off a quantity of turtle-eggs. The female turtle frequent Southwest Bay in large numbers, for the purpose of depositing their eggs in the sand. But up till now, they had failed to catch any of the turtle. The eggs are excellent, and can be used for every purpose for which fowl’s eggs are employed. Here is a receipt for making eggnog which I have tried myself and can recommend:﻿—Two turtle-eggs, a teaspoonful of tinned milk, some water, sugar, and a small glass of rum.

===Chinese===
他们给我们捎来了一批海龟蛋。母海龟成群结队地频频光顾西南湾，为的是把蛋产进沙里。可直到此时，他们连一只海龟也还没能捉到。这蛋滋味极好，鸡蛋派得上什么用场，它便派得上什么用场。这里有一味蛋奶酒的方子，我亲自试过，可以推荐：——海龟蛋两枚，罐装牛奶一茶匙，清水些许，糖适量，再兑一小杯朗姆酒。

===Original===
The shore-party had obtained an abundance of fish; they used to catch them not only with hook and line, but with an extemporized seine net, which they dragged with great success through the pools left by the receding tide. This seine was simply a long piece of the wire-netting which we had brought with us to serve as land-crab-proof fencing round the camp. It seems that this netting did not fulfil its original purpose very satisfactorily, as the crabs could burrow under it.

===Chinese===
岸上的队伍弄到了充裕的鱼；他们平日捕鱼不但用钓钩钓线，还用一张临时凑成的拉网，在退潮留下的大小水潭里拖曳，收获颇丰。这张网其实不过是随身带来的铁丝网的一长条——原是打算围在营地四周防陆蟹的。这铁丝网原先的差事看来干得不大令人满意，因为陆蟹能从网底下打洞钻过去。

===Original===
The land-crabs however, did not molest the shore-party to any extent, and it was only now and then that a man found one of these unpleasant creatures in his bed. It was the custom for the men to sally forth every evening, just before dark, and kill, with sticks, every land-crab they could find in the immediate neighbourhood of the camp, each man slaying his sixty or seventy. This afforded an abundance of food for the others during the night, so that they had no need to stray into the tents. The crabs, I was informed, were excellent scavengers, and consumed all the cook’s refuse.

===Chinese===
不过陆蟹并没有怎么滋扰岸上的队伍，只是偶尔才有人在自家床上发现一只这种讨厌的东西。众人有个惯例：每天傍晚天黑之前倾巢出动，用棍子敲死营地左近所能找到的每一只陆蟹，每人能宰上六七十只。这就为余下的蟹备下了充裕的过夜食粮，它们便无须流窜进帐篷里去了。据他们说，这些蟹是极好的清道夫，厨子的泔水污物全教它们吃了个精光。

===Original===
The doctor and his companions had no lack of news to impart. I was anxious, of course, in the first place, to learn how the work had progressed. I was told that some hundreds of tons of earth had been already removed, and that a broad trench was being dug, along the face of the cliff, through the landslip in the first bend of the ravine, but that, so far, no indications of the treasure had been come across. The chief difficulty consisted in the presence of a great many stones of all sizes that were mixed up with the fallen soil, some of them being of several tons weight. In digging the trench an inclined plane was left at either end, up which the barrows of earth could be wheeled; and when one of the big stones was found, the earth was, in the first place, cleared from round it, and then it was dragged from the bottom of the trench up one of these inclined planes by means of powerful tackle, assisted by the hydraulic jack. When they had got it by these means to the top of the trench, they could easily roll it down the ravine.

===Chinese===
医生和他的同伴们有的是新闻可讲。我自然头一件就急于打听工程进展如何。据说已有几百吨土方被挖走，一道宽阔的壕沟正沿着峭壁的立面、穿过裂谷头一道弯处的滑坡开掘，但至今尚未碰到宝藏的半点迹象。最大的难处，在于坍土里混杂着大大小小数不清的石块，有的重达数吨。挖沟时两头各留一道斜坡，好运送土方的独轮车推上去；碰上大石块，先把四周的土清掉，再用强力的滑轮组、借着液压千斤顶之力，把它从沟底沿斜坡拖上去。用这法子弄到沟顶之后，顺势往裂谷里一滚便了。

===Original===
The doctor explained to me all the routine that he had laid down for observance on shore, and the different details of the work. Sunday was always a holiday, and was occupied, as a rule, in wandering about and exploring; but it was sometimes too terribly hot for this.

===Chinese===
医生向我详述了他为岸上制定的整套章程，以及各项工作的零碎细节。星期日永远是休息日，照例用来四处漫游、探察；可有时热得实在吓人，连这个也做不成。

===Original===
I was informed that a crowbar and several other fresh relics of Mr. A﻿—﻿—’s expedition had been discovered, and that a wooden box had been found, carefully hidden away at the farther end of the bay, which contained a chessboard, a quantity of shot cartridges, and several London and Newcastle newspapers, dated October 1875. Mr. A﻿—﻿—’s expedition took place in 1885, Mr. P﻿—﻿—’s﻿—the first expedition﻿—in 1880; so the papers gave us no clue as to who had brought them here. The shore-party had amused themselves by reading these ancient journals. In them they found accounts of the Wainwright trial and of the collision between the *Mistletoe* and the *Alberta*. It was strange to read, on Trinidad, the old theatrical advertisements in the *Standard*, with Charles Matthews acting at the Gaiety and Miss Marie Wilton at some other house. There was an excellent notice of the latter charming actress in one of these papers.

===Chinese===
我还得知，他们又发现了一根撬棍和A——先生那支远征队留下的另外几件新遗物，还在海湾尽头寻到一只仔细藏好的木箱，里面装着一副棋盘、一批霰弹枪弹，还有几份伦敦和纽卡斯尔的报纸，日期是 1875 年 10 月。A——先生的远征是在 1885 年，P——先生的那次——最早的一次——在 1880 年；所以这些报纸并不能给我们什么线索，说明是谁把它们带到此地来的。岸上的队伍拿这些古旧报章读着消遣，在里面读到了温赖特案的报道，还有「桑寄生号」（Mistletoe）与「艾伯塔号」（Alberta）相撞的消息。在特林达迪读《标准报》上那些旧日的戏剧广告——查尔斯·马修斯在欢乐剧院（Gaiety）登台，玛丽·威尔顿小姐在另一家戏院演出——真是别有一番奇异。其中一份报上，还有一篇对后一位妩媚女演员的精彩剧评。

===Original===
I was told that there had not been so much surf in Southwest Bay as might have been expected with so strong a wind; but, as I have explained, the southeast is the wind that raises the least surf on this sandy beach, though it blows right on to it.

===Chinese===
我听说西南湾里的碎浪，并不像这么大的风本该掀起的那么多；不过正如我先前解释过的，吹在这片沙滩上的诸般风里，数东南风掀起的浪最小，虽然它正对着海岸刮。

===Original===
The doctor told me that they had experienced, on every occasion they had landed, a strong current sweeping along the shore of the bay in an easterly direction, so that, no sooner did the bow of the boat touch the sand, than her stern was driven round by the current to the left, and, unless proper precautions were taken, she would get broadside on to the next sea and be rolled over.

===Chinese===
医生告诉我，他们每回登岸，都遇上一股沿着海湾岸边向东扫去的强劲水流，以致艇头刚一触到沙滩，艇尾就被水流冲得向左转将过去；若不采取适当的防范，她便会横身迎上下一个浪头，被掀得滚翻过去。

===Original===
On being asked whether they had had much rain in the bay, they replied that the showers had been as heavy as those tropical downpours we had experienced in the doldrums. They said that the Sugarloaf presented a magnificent appearance after one of these showers, for then a cascade 700 feet in height would pour down its almost perpendicular sides. They had been enabled to fill their tanks and breakers with rainwater, and had only used the condensing apparatus on one or two occasions, and then more by way of experiment, to see how it worked, than from necessity. It acted perfectly, and with it five gallons of fresh water were distilled from seawater in a very short time.

===Chinese===
被问到湾里雨多不多时，他们回答说，阵雨之大，不亚于我们在赤道无风带经历过的那些热带倾盆。他们说，一阵这样的雨过后，糖面包山的景象蔚为壮观——一道七百英尺高的瀑布，会顺着它几近垂直的山壁倾泻而下。他们借雨水灌满了水箱和淡水桶，蒸馏器只用过一两回，而且多半是当试验、看看它的功效，并非出于必需。那器物运转得尽善尽美，用它从海水里蒸馏淡水，短短工夫便得五加仑。

===Original===
The fortunate discovery had also been made of two small issues of water among the cliffs at the east end of the bay. The supply was sufficient, and though the carrying of the water in breakers from here to the camp over the rough ground entailed heavy labour, it was easier to fetch it in this way than to collect the large quantity of firewood necessary for condensing an equal amount of water.

===Chinese===
还有一桩幸运的发现：湾东尽头的峭壁间，有两股细小的出水。水量是够用的；虽说提着淡水桶、蹚着崎岖地面从这里往营地运水是桩重活，可若要蒸馏出同量的水，得收集一大堆柴火，两下相比，这样取水还是省力些。

===Original===
The doctor reported Arthur Cotton as being ill, and unfit for further digging for the present; so he was left on board with me, while George went on shore to take his place. The doctor promised to come off for me on the following morning, so that I could pay a short visit to the shore and inspect the works﻿—provided, of course, the surf permitted. Then we bade each other farewell, and the working-party returned to the bay.

===Chinese===
医生报告说阿瑟·科顿病了，眼下干不得挖掘的活计；于是他留在船上陪我，乔治则上岸顶了他的缺。医生答应第二天早晨来接我，好让我到岸上作一次短暂探访、察看工程——当然，还得碎浪许可。随后我们彼此道别，工作队返回湾里去了。

===Original===
The boat did not come off for me on the following day, as the surf was dangerous in Southwest Bay; and I held no communication with the shore-party for another week. During this time the wind was from the southeast; but though it rushed down the ravine with the usual violent squalls, it was moderate outside, and we had no more of the heavy sea which had been running throughout the previous week. It would have been possible for me to have landed at the pier on nearly any day, but there was still a sufficient surf to prevent our carrying off any more stones from the shore.

===Chinese===
第二天小艇没有来接我，因为西南湾里碎浪凶险；此后整整一个星期，我同岸上的队伍音讯不通。这段时间里，风一直从东南来；但尽管它照旧夹着惯常的猛烈阵风顺着裂谷冲刮下来，外海的风势却算温和，头一个星期连绵不断的大浪也没有再起。我几乎哪天都能在天然码头登岸，可碎浪仍不算小，我们再没法从岸上运石头回来了。

===Original===
We were anchored on a sandy bottom, but we could feel, by the grumbling of our chain as the yacht swung, that there were many rocks under us as well. These caused us a good deal of annoyance; for on several occasions, when the vessel was lying right over her anchor, the slack of the chain would take a turn round a rock and give us a short nip; so that when a swell passed under us, the vessel could not rise to it, but was held down by the tautened chain, which dragged her bows under, producing a great strain. The rocks must have been of brittle coral formation, for, after giving two or three violent jerks as the sea lifted her, the vessel would suddenly shake herself free with a wrench, evidently by the breaking away of the obstruction. At last all the projecting portions of the coral rock in our immediate neighbourhood must have been torn off, the chain having swept a clear space for itself all round, for after a time we were no longer caught in this way. These great strains loosened our starboard hawsepipe badly, so that we had to slip our chain and pass it through the other hawsepipe.

===Chinese===
我们锚泊在沙质海底上，但游艇来回回转时锚链那一阵阵闷响，让我们觉出脚下还藏着许多礁石。这些礁石教我们大伤脑筋；因为有好几回，船正正压在锚上方，链子的松弛段便绕上一块礁石，把我们紧紧咬住一下；于是一当涌浪从船底滚过，船身浮不起来，反被绷紧的锚链死死拽住，链子把船头拖进水里，吃力极大。这些礁石想必是脆性的珊瑚构造，因为海浪把船抬起、船身猛挫两三下之后，她会猛然一挣脱出身来，显见得是绊阻之物折断了。到后来，近旁珊瑚礁所有凸出的棱角想必全被磨掉，锚链替自己四下里扫出了一圈干净地盘，因为过了一阵，我们便再没这样被咬住过。这几番大力拉扯，把右舷的锚链筒弄得很有些松脱，我们只得把锚链解脱出来，改从另一舷的锚链筒穿过去。

===Original===
On December 9, it being a very fine day, I made an expedition in the dinghy toward the north end of the island. We found no good landing-place in that direction, for a coral ledge extends along the whole coast, causing broken water, and there are dangerous rocks in the midst of the breakers. We pulled into several little bays, each hemmed in by inaccessible barren mountains, so crowded with birds that, from the sea, the black crags looked quite white with them. We pulled inside Bird Island and inspected the Ninepin from close to. This huge cylinder of rock, 900 feet in height, is described by old navigators as having been crowned with large trees. It is now completely bare of vegetation, as it also was when I first saw it in 1881. I observed that, since my last visit, a huge mass had fallen off the top of it, which now lay by its side in shattered fragments. We caught a quantity of fish in these bays, one a fine fellow weighing thirty pounds; and we saw several large turtle floating on the water, but they sank as soon as we got near them.

===Chinese===
12 月 9 日，天色极好，我乘小划艇朝岛的北端作了一次远足。那个方向找不到好的登岸处，因为一道珊瑚礁台沿着整段海岸延展，搅得碎浪翻腾，浪花当中还立着凶险的礁石。我们划进好几座小湾，每座都被人迹难至的荒山环抱，鸟群密密匝匝，从海上望去，黑色的峭岩竟被它们映得一片白。我们划进鸟岛的内侧，近前察看了「九柱石」。这座高达九百英尺的巨石柱，据老一辈航海家记述，顶上昔日曾有大树成冠。如今它寸草不生，我 1881 年初见它时也是这般光秃。我看出自上次造访以来，它顶上又崩落了一大块，如今碎裂成块，卧在它旁边。我们在这些湾里钓到不少鱼，其中一条好汉足足三十磅重；还看见几只大海龟浮在水面上，可我们刚一靠近，它们就沉了下去。

===Original===
The uneventful days passed by, and I grew stout on laziness, salt beef, and duff. At last, on December 14, we pulled off in the dinghy to Southwest Bay, to see how the shore-party was getting on. We took with us a signal code book and the flags, so as to converse with our diggers in case we could not effect a landing﻿—a feat not to be attempted with our rotten little dinghy except under the most exceptional circumstances. The shore-party was, of course, also provided with a code book and set of flags.

===Chinese===
无事的日子一天天过去，我在懒惰、咸牛肉和布丁里养得发起福来。终于，12 月 14 日，我们乘小划艇划往西南湾，去看看岸上队伍的近况。我们随身带了信号码书和旗子，以便万一登不了岸，还能同我们的挖掘手们通话——坐着我们这条朽烂的小划艇登岸这桩壮举，除非情况万分特殊，是断不敢尝试的。岸上的队伍自然也备有一本码书和一套旗。

===Original===
As I required some more specimens of birds, I took with me, not a gun with which to shoot them, but simply a ramrod, the end of which I had loaded with a piece of lead. With this, as I sat in the boat, I found no difficulty in knocking down the inquisitive birds as they flew just over our heads, and I thus procured several good specimens.

===Chinese===
我还需要一些鸟的标本，随身带的不是打鸟的枪，而只是一根通条，一头坠了一块铅。坐在艇里，拿这家伙去敲那些恰巧从头顶掠过的多事的鸟，我发现毫不费力，就这样弄到了几件好标本。

===Original===
When we had pulled round the point and were in Southwest Bay we saw the white tents of the camp in front of us, and we could plainly distinguish, in a ravine behind, the great trench which the men had dug at the side of the cliff. We found little surf in the bay, but I would not risk a landing; for it would not require much bumping to knock our dinghy’s ancient bottom off; so we remained outside the breakers and signalled: “Any news?”

===Chinese===
绕过海岬进了西南湾，营地的白色帐篷便展现在眼前，背后的裂谷里，众人在峭壁旁掘出的那道大壕沟也认得分明。湾里碎浪不大，可我不肯冒险登岸；因为用不了几下磕碰，就足以把我们小划艇那副老船底给磕散了；于是我们留在碎浪线外，打出信号：「有什么新闻吗？」

===Original===
There was no reply with the flags, but some of the men walked down to the rocks under the Sugarloaf, so that we could come near enough to them to hail. A very disreputable lot our friends looked, too: as unkempt and rough as the original pirates might have been. The costume of each consisted merely of shirt, trousers, and belt, some sort of an apology for a hat crowning all. They were all more or less ragged, and were stained from head to foot with the soil in which they had been digging, so that they presented a uniform dirty, brownish yellow appearance, and, from a passing vessel, might easily have been taken for Brazilian convicts.

===Chinese===
对方没有用旗子回答，但几个人走到糖面包山下方的礁石上，好让我们凑得够近，喊得上话。我们这班朋友瞧着也实在不体面：蓬头垢面、粗野不羁，同当年那伙正牌海盗相比，只怕不遑多让。每人的行头不过衬衫、裤子加一条腰带，头顶上再勉强扣着个聊胜于无的帽子。个个身上都有些褴褛，从头到脚沾满他们挖掘的泥土，呈现出一律的污黄颜色，打旁边经过的船望过来，多半会把他们当成巴西的苦役犯。

===Original===
They shouted what news they had to tell. They reported that they were progressing well with the digging, and that they had caught a number of turtle. They promised to come off to the yacht the next morning, surf permitting. I made some sketches of Treasure Bay and West Bay as seen from the sea, and then returned to the vessel, to skin my birds.

===Chinese===
他们把要讲的新闻喊给我们听。据报告，挖掘进展顺利，还捉到了一批海龟。他们答应第二天早晨到游艇上来——只要碎浪许可。我画了几张从海上望见的宝藏湾和西湾的速写，然后回到船上，剥制我的鸟。

===Original===
The whaleboat was alongside on the following morning, December 15, and the doctor, Powell, Pollock, and two paid hands, boarded us. They had brought off some fresh and salted turtle and a quantity of turtle-eggs.

===Chinese===
第二天早晨，12 月 15 日，捕鲸艇靠上了船舷，医生、鲍威尔、波洛克和两名受雇水手上了我们的船。他们带来了一些鲜海龟肉和腌海龟肉，还有一批海龟蛋。

===Original===
The yacht had now been lying off Trinidad for twenty-five days, and the shore-party had been hard at work for seventeen days; so I thought it was quite time for me to join the camp, and do my share of the work. I could see that the energetic doctor was anything but anxious to change the hard labour on shore for the lazy life on board ship, and though, as mate, he would have been the proper person to take charge of the vessel during my absence on land, still we considered it advisable to arrange matters differently.

===Chinese===
游艇在特林达迪海外已停泊了二十五天，岸上的队伍也已苦干了十七天；我想，该是我进驻营地、干上我那份活的时候了。我看得出来，那位精力充沛的医生一点儿也不想拿岸上的苦工，去换船上那份清闲日子；虽说以大副的身份，我上岸期间由他照管船只本是顺理成章，我们还是觉得另作安排更为妥当。

===Original===
The doctor, as I have said, was a most useful man on shore, and, as we were anxious to complete our operations as quickly as possible and leave the island before the stormy season should set in, it seemed a pity to waste so much energy and muscle as his in an idle life on board the yacht. Having remained at anchor for so long, and knowing that our anchor had now got such a firm hold that there was but little chance of its dragging, and having, moreover, discovered by experience that it was possible to ride where we were even in bad weather, I had acquired a considerable confidence in the safety of the vessel, and I believe that she could have remained off the cascade for six months without suffering damage. I, therefore, now came to the conclusion that it would not be very imprudent to leave a somewhat incompetent person in charge, as the chances were that he would have nothing to do. Pollock, who had complained of slackness for some time, was the one from whom the least amount of work could be extracted on shore, and was, therefore, the one who could be the most easily spared. I, consequently, decided to leave him on board the yacht, instead of the doctor.

===Chinese===
我说过，医生在岸上是极得力的一个人；我们既急于尽快完成工程、赶在风暴季节来临之前离岛，把他那样充沛的精力和筋骨，浪费在游艇上的闲散生活里，未免可惜。我们已锚泊了这么久，知道锚如今抓得极牢，走锚的可能微乎其微；而且经验又证明，纵使天气恶劣，泊在原地也撑得住；于是我对这艘船的安全已有了相当的信心，相信她在瀑布外海这么泊上六个月，也不致受损。因此，我这时得出结论：留一个稍稍不那么顶事的人看船，也算不得十分轻率，因为多半根本不会有什么事要他做。波洛克抱怨身子发乏已有些时日，在岸上能从他身上榨出的活计最少，因而也是最容易匀出来的一个。于是我便决定把他留在游艇上，而不用医生。
