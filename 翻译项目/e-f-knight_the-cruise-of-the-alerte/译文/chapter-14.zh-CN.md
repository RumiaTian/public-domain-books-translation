## XIV / 十四

===Original===
We Land the Stores in the Bay

===Chinese===
我们把给养运上湾岸

===Original===
The patience of my men was now to be severely tried. Here before them was the mysterious isle, with all its golden possibilities; but for five days the sea was in far too disturbed a condition to permit of a landing; so they were confined to their floating prison, which rolled and pitched at her anchorage all the while, and gazed with vain desire at the forbidden land.

===Chinese===
我手下人的耐性，如今要经受一场严峻的考验了。那座神秘的岛屿就在眼前，带着一切黄金般的可能；然而一连五天，海面汹涌得太不平静，登岸无从谈起；他们只得困守这座漂浮的牢狱，任她在锚地上不住地横摇纵摆，一面徒然渴慕地遥望那片禁地。

===Original===
It was now that Ted came up to me, as spokesman for the rest of his shipmates in the forecastle, and said that they were all anxious to go on shore in turn, and do their share of digging with the rest of us. It had been part of the original scheme to keep the paid hands﻿—with the exception, perhaps, of the cook﻿—on board the vessel; but as by this time we knew the ways of the *Alerte*, and could handle her with fewer men than when we had started. I decided that an officer and two paid hands would be a sufficient crew while she was lying off the island, and that all the other men could be spared for the work on shore. I therefore acceded to Ted’s request.

===Chinese===
就在这时，特德作为水手舱众伙计的代表来找我，说大家全都盼着能轮番上岸，同我们一起出力挖掘。原定计划里，受雇水手——或许厨子可算例外——本该尽数留在船上；但事到如今，我们已摸熟了「阿勒特号」的脾性，比起航时用更少的人手也能驾驭她。于是我定夺：船泊岛外期间，留一名军官加两名受雇水手充作船员足矣，其余人手尽数可抽去干岸上的活计。我便应允了特德的请求。

===Original===
The men were led to understand that they would be entitled to no share of the proceeds if the treasure were found, though they, of course, knew that, should fortune favour us, a handsome present would be given to them.

===Chinese===
众人也得了明白话：即便寻得宝藏，他们也无权分取一份收益；当然，他们心里有数，一旦时运眷顾我们，必有一份丰厚的馈赠赏到他们头上。

===Original===
The agreement as to the division of the spoil among the gentlemen-adventurers had also to be revised in one respect. It was settled that the shares of those who had abandoned the expedition were to be portioned out among those who remained. By this arrangement each of my companions became nearly twice as rich﻿—in expectations﻿—as when he sailed from England.

===Chinese===
绅士冒险家们之间分赃的协议，也有一处须得修订。议定：中途弃队诸人的份额，摊分给留下来的人。经此一番安排，我的每位同伴——就「期望」而言——比从英格兰启航时几乎阔了一倍。

===Original===
Trinidad is supposed to be outside the limit of the southeast trade-winds, but I think this is doubtful; for, so far as my experience goes, the prevailing winds are from the easterly quarter, and more commonly from the southeast. When the winds are in the west quadrant, and more especially when from the southwest, a heavy sea rises, and landing is rendered altogether impossible. This was our experience for the next few days.

===Chinese===
人们认为特林达迪位于东南信风带的界限之外，我看未必然；据我一己的经验，此地盛行之风出自东面诸向，而以东南为最常见。风入西面诸向时——尤其是西南来风——便涌起大浪，登岸便全然无望。此后数日，我们身受的正是这般。

===Original===
On November 24, there was a high wind from the northwest and a great swell. We were now on a lee shore, and a very dangerous one too; so all was got ready for slipping the anchor and running to the open sea in a moment, should it become necessary to do so. We gave the yacht all her starboard chain﻿—sixty fathoms. We got up the end of the chain, and made it fast to the mainmast in such a way that we could let it go at once. One end of a stout thirty-fathom hawser was attached to the chain, just below the hawsepipe, and to the other end of it we fastened an improvised buoy, made of a breaker and a small bamboo raft. In order to get under way we should now merely have to throw the buoy overboard and cast off the end of the chain from the mast. We could then sail away and leave our moorings behind us.

===Chinese===
11 月 24 日，刮起猛烈的西北风，涌浪滔滔。我们此刻正处下风岸，而且是极危险的下风岸；于是万事备妥，一旦需要，便可立刻弃锚疾驶，逃往外海。我们把游艇右舷的锚链放到了头——整整六十英寻。又把链尾绞上来，设法缚牢在主桅上，好一撒手便能即刻放出。一根三十英寻的结实粗缆，一端系在紧贴锚链筒之下的锚链上，另一端则缚上一只临时扎成的浮标，由一只淡水桶和一张小竹筏拼成。如今要动身，只消把浮标抛下海、再把链尾从桅上解开即可。然后便可扬长驶去，把整套系泊家伙撇在身后。

===Original===
Then we set to work to bend the storm-trysail, a very handy sail, which could be hoisted much more readily than our heavy mainsail. We reefed the foresail, had a storm-jib ready, and housed our topmast. We were now prepared for anything that might turn up.

===Chinese===
接着我们动手装上风暴斜桁帆——一张极为便利的帆，比那面沉重的主帆升起来省事得多。我们把前帆收了缩帆，备妥一张风暴艏帆，又降下了顶桅。至此，无论冒出什么变故，我们都已严阵以待。

===Original===
We were not idle this day, for after making all snug, we got the spades, hydraulic jack, and other tools out of the hold, so as to have them in readiness to put in the boat the moment there was a chance of landing.

===Chinese===
这一天我们也没有闲着：一切收拾妥当之后，我们把铁锹、液压千斤顶和其他工具从货舱里搬了出来，好歹一有登岸的机会，即刻便能装进艇里。

===Original===
Our fire on the mountain blazed away all this night and was not entirely extinguished for six days afterwards.

===Chinese===
我们在山上放的那场火，整整烧了一夜，此后六天都没有完全熄灭。

===Original===
The next day was overcast, and the wind was from the southwest; then it veered to the southward. The sea was higher than on the previous day. The vessel tumbled about a great deal, rolling her scuppers under water, flooding her decks, and running her bowsprit under, all the while. Still, she rode very easily, the great length of heavy chain we had given her acting as a spring. We watched carefully for the first signs of dragging, but the anchor had evidently got a good hold now and she did not budge a foot. In the afternoon the glass fell rapidly and the sky looked very stormy, while the temperature in our saloon fell to 75°, which made us feel quite chilly.

===Chinese===
第二天阴云蔽日，风从西南来，随后又转向南面。海比头一天更高。船颠簸得厉害，一直把排水孔压进水里，甲板时时漫水，艏斜桅也不住往浪里扎。饶是如此，她泊得倒还安稳：我们放给她的那一长串重锚链，恰似一根弹簧，起着缓冲的作用。我们小心提防着走锚的头一丝征兆，但锚这回显然抓得牢靠，她一寸也不曾挪动。午后气压计急落，天色甚是狰狞，舱厅里的温度跌到 75 度（华氏），我们竟觉出相当的寒意。

===Original===
It is probable that this disturbed weather and high sea were the results of a *pampero* raging thousands of miles to the southward of us.

===Chinese===
这番恶劣天气和滔滔巨涌，多半是我们南面数千英里外一场肆虐的帕姆佩罗风所致。

===Original===
On this day we took our dinghy on deck﻿—a dilapidated little boat﻿—and proceeded to stop her leaks, in a novel, but for the time effectual, manner, with plaster of Paris and tar.

===Chinese===
这天我们把小划艇搬上甲板——一条破败的小船——着手给她堵漏，用的是熟石膏和柏油，法子新奇，倒也一时见效。

===Original===
The fish would not be caught while this heavy sea was running, but we secured some sharks and ate their flesh for dinner, to the horror of our black cook, whom I overheard telling his shipmates that he considered it “degrading to eat de meat of de dam shark.”

===Chinese===
这样的大浪天气，鱼是不肯咬钩的，我们却逮住了几条鲨鱼，拿它们的肉当了晚餐，直教我们的黑人厨子骇然；我无意间听见他对同船的伙计说，他觉得「吃那该死的鲨鱼肉，实在有失身份」。

===Original===
November 26.﻿—Same weather, blowing, raining, rolling, and impatient grumbling of men. Even the two amiable blacks, eager to be at work on shore, fretted a bit at the enforced imprisonment on board. They had always been fond of argument, but now the arguments became stormy, and we could hear them laying down the law to each other in the forecastle, while the English sailors sat round them, smoking in silence and listening with amused wonder. One black was a Roman Catholic, the other a Methodist; their discussions were generally theological, and they exchanged vituperations with a fine theological fury. It was grand to hear Theodosius rail at the Pope and call his comrade a heathen idolater, while George would pour the vials of his wrath on the Methodist heretic. These two poor fellows were the greatest friends, but, of course, each was confident that the other was doomed to perdition. When, in the course of one of these controversies, a theologian found himself caught in a dilemma, he would wax impatient and cry, “Oh, chew it!”﻿—an expression I have never heard before﻿—indicating that one has been worsted in argument, but will not allow it, and insists, having had enough of it, on winding up the debate at once.

===Chinese===
11 月 26 日。——天气照旧：刮风，下雨，船摇，人则焦躁地咕哝不休。连那两个和气的黑人——他们一心盼着上岛干活——也因这强加的囚禁生出了几分烦躁。他们俩素来好辩，如今论起来更是火药味十足，我们听得见他们在水手舱里各摆各的教义、互相训诫，英国水手们围坐一圈，闷头抽烟，又惊又乐地听着。一个黑人是天主教徒，另一个是卫理公会教徒；他们的辩题多半属神学，对骂起来义怒勃发，满口神学腔调。听西奥多修斯痛骂教皇、斥他的同伴为拜偶像的异教徒，而乔治把满腔义怒尽数泼向卫理公会异端，实在精彩极了。这两个可怜人是顶顶要好的朋友，可是不消说，两人又都笃信对方注定沉沦。每逢一场论战中哪位神学家自觉落下了风、进退两难，他便按捺不住，嚷一声「噢，嚼它去吧！」——这种说法我从前从未听过——意思是自己辩输了却不肯认账，又实在够了，非要当场收场不可。

===Original===
On the 27th the glass rose, the wind veered to northeast, and the sea moderated; but the surf was still dangerous, and we could see it breaking over a rock sixty feet in height. On this day we sighted two homeward-bound sailing-vessels. During our stay on Trinidad we saw a good many craft, sometimes four or five in a week, all homeward-bounders, for, as I have already explained, it is usual for vessels coming round Cape Horn to make for and sight this island, so as to correct the rate of their chronometers. Few outward-bounders pass it, and it is altogether out of the track of steamers.

===Chinese===
27 日，气压计回升，风转东北，海也平缓了些；但碎浪依然凶险，我们望得见浪头正扑过一块六十英尺高的礁岩。这一天我们望见两条返航的帆船。滞留特林达迪期间，我们见到的船只着实不少，有时一星期多达四五条，全是返航船——缘由我在前面已有交代：绕行合恩角而来的船，惯于驶近并望见此岛，借以校准天文钟的走速。出航的船少有从此经过，这里更完全不在汽船的航线上。

===Original===
On November 28 things looked better, the sea had all gone down. In the morning a few hands pulled off to the pier, where they found the landing perfectly easy, and brought off the coat which the doctor had left on the rock when we had jumped into the sea. My coat could not be found, as it had been washed off by a wave. They also brought off a specimen of a land-crab, which did not seem at all at home on our deck. He was introduced to Master Jacko, our monkey, whose horror at the uncouth apparition was intense. The wise monkey would not get within reach of the crab’s nippers, but, having cleverly driven him into a corner, tried to push his ugly visitor through a scupper into the sea with a bit of firewood.

===Chinese===
11 月 28 日，气象好转，海浪全平了。早晨几名水手划往天然码头，发现登岸易如反掌，还带回了医生那天我们跳海时留在岩上的那件上衣。我的上衣却寻不见了，早被一个浪头卷下了海。他们还带回一只陆蟹标本，这东西在我们甲板上显得浑身不自在。人们把它引见给我们养的猴子——杰科（Jacko）小少爷；这位小少爷见了那副粗野的怪相，骇得魂不附体。这只机灵的猴子死活不肯凑到蟹螯够得着的地方，却伶俐地把客人逼进一个角落，拿起一小块柴火，要把这位其貌不扬的访客从排水孔捅下海去。

===Original===
I must now apologise to Jacko for not having before this introduced him to my readers. He was a delightful little creature that we had purchased on the Praya at Bahia. He was very affectionate, and was free from malice, though, of course, full of mischief. He had a red blanket of his own, which he would carry about with him wherever he went, and, should a few drops of rain fall or spray come on board, he would deftly roll it about him in the fashion of a cloak, with his funny little head just peeping out of the hood. He was very fond of tea, and while we were at sea he took his 4 a.m. cup with the others. As soon as the cook began to lift the boiler of tea from the stove Jacko would give a whistle of delight, clamber up the pantry wall, unhook a pannikin, and walk up with it to be filled, “all de same as a little ole man,” as the cook used to say. It was amusing to see him test the temperature of the tea with his fingers before drinking it. He was a marvellously intelligent and jolly little creature, and is now dwelling happily in a little house on a coconut tree in a plantation near Port-of-Spain. He prefers a West Indian life of warmth and unlimited bananas to an existence in a damp ship on salt junk and biscuit.

===Chinese===
我直到此刻还不曾把杰科引见给诸位读者，得先向它赔个不是。它是我们在巴伊亚的普拉亚买下的一只小家伙，讨人喜欢得很。它极重情义，心里没有半分恶意，当然，淘气也是淘气极了。它有一条自己的红毯子，走到哪儿带到哪儿；天上若飘下几滴雨，或是浪花溅上船来，它便麻利地把毯子往身上一裹，权当斗篷，那滑稽的小脑袋恰从风帽里探出一点。它顶爱喝茶；我们在海上时，凌晨 4 点那一杯，它同大家一样一顿不落。厨子刚把煮茶的大桶从炉子上端起来，杰科便欢哨一声，攀上配膳室的墙壁，摘下一只小铁杯，端端正正走上前去候着斟满——「活脱脱一个小老头儿」，厨子常这么说。看它喝之前先伸指头试试茶的温度，着实好笑。这小东西聪明快活得出奇，如今正在西班牙港（Port-of-Spain）附近一座庄园的椰子树上安居着一间小屋，日子过得快快活活。比起潮乎乎的船上、咸肉硬饼干度日的生涯，它更爱西印度的暖热和吃不尽的香蕉。

===Original===
At noon, as the sea was still smooth, we made our first attempt at landing in Treasure Bay. We put the whaleboat in the water, and loaded her with about a ton of stores, consisting of tinned provisions of various sorts, biscuit, salt beef, the picks, spades, crowbars, wheelbarrows, hydraulic jack, and other tools. We also took in tow a raft constructed of the long bamboos we had brought from Bahia. These we knew would be useful for several purposes.

===Chinese===
正午时分，海面依然平静，我们初次尝试在宝藏湾（Treasure Bay）登岸。我们把捕鲸艇放下水，装上约一吨给养：各色罐头、硬饼干、咸牛肉，外加十字镐、铁锹、撬棍、手推车、液压千斤顶和别的工具。我们又拖上一张筏子，用从巴伊亚带来的长竹扎成，我们知道这东西用处不少。

===Original===
I steered the boat, while the doctor, Powell, Pursell, and two paid hands, took the oars. Having the wind behind us we were not long in crossing the two miles of smoothly heaving sea that lay between us and Southwest Bay. We rounded the point into the bay, and, leaving on our port hand the islet in the middle, we made for the channel which the doctor and myself had surveyed from the mountains. When we came near we found that there were three parallel lines of breakers to be traversed, and, consequently, there was a treble chance of swamping. The surf was much more formidable than we had expected to find it, considering how smooth the sea was outside the bay. The wind was blowing in strong gusts right off shore, over the depression in the mountains at the back of the bay. It drove off the tops of the oncoming waves into great veils of spray, curling over in a contrary direction to the curl of the swell, and bright with shifting rainbows as the sun’s rays fell upon it. The bay presented a most beautiful appearance from the boat, and those who had not seen the pirates’ haunt before uttered exclamations of admiration and wonder. Between the gloomy black mountains on the left and the unearthly-looking dark red walls of Noah’s Ark on the right was a scene in which, flooded with tropical sunlight, earth and ocean vied with each other in vividness of colouring. Directly in front were the great rollers of transparent green, their snowy crests flashing with rainbows; beyond, dazzling golden sands; above, domes of brilliant emerald cleaving the cloudless sky.

===Chinese===
我掌舵，医生、鲍威尔、珀塞尔和两名受雇水手操桨。风从船尾来，我们与西南湾之间那两英里微涌起伏的海面，不多时便划了过去。我们绕过海岬进得湾来，把湾心的小岛让在左舷，直奔医生和我先前从山上勘测过的那道水道。驶近了才发现，须穿过三道平行的碎浪，也就是说，有覆舟的三重风险。湾外海面如此平静，湾里的碎浪却远比我们预想的凶猛。风一阵紧似一阵，正从岸上、从湾后群山的凹口处直吹出海。它把迎面而来的浪头削成大片纱幕似的水沫，卷曲的方向恰与涌浪的卷曲相反，阳光照上去，闪动着变幻不定的虹彩。从艇上望去，这湾景致美得摄人心魄，先前不曾见过海盗巢穴的人们连声惊叹，赞不绝口。左边是黑沉沉的阴郁群山，右边是「诺亚方舟」那暗红色的、恍若非人间的壁墙，其间铺展着一幅图景：热带的阳光漫洒其上，大地与海洋争奇斗艳，各不相让。正前方是一道道碧透的巨浪，雪白的浪峰虹光闪烁；再远处，是晃眼的金色沙滩；头顶上，是鲜绿欲滴的穹丘，劈开万里无云的长天。

===Original===
But this was no time to dwell on the beautiful; we had other matters to consider. The grand rollers with their breaking tops had no charms for us, for we had to get through them﻿—a risky undertaking with a deeply-laden boat.

===Chinese===
但这不是流连美景的时候，我们另有要事待谋。这些顶着碎白浪尖的大滚浪，对我们毫无可爱之处——我们非得从它们中间穿过去不可，对一条满载的小艇来说，这是桩凶险的营生。

===Original===
We discovered afterwards that it is almost impossible to judge from the height of the swell near our anchorage, or from the surf on the pier, whether landing in Southwest Bay is likely to be easy or the reverse. The surf on this sandy beach is governed by a different system of laws to that which prevails on other portions of the coast of Trinidad. Here, curiously enough, there is more surf when the wind is blowing off shore than when it is blowing on. The northeast wind, sweeping in violent gusts down the slopes that back the bay, offers a resistance to the swell rolling in, and piles it into steep walls of water, breaking dangerously. The southeast wind raises a higher swell outside, but, blowing right into this bay, drives the sea down, and the landing becomes comparatively easy. At the anchorage opposite the cascade the contrary is the rule: with a northeast wind blowing off shore the sea is smooth, with a southeast wind the surf increases; but, as I have already stated, it is always smoother there than in Southwest Bay.

===Chinese===
我们后来才发现：想凭锚地附近的涌高、或天然码头上的碎浪，来判断在西南湾登岸的难易，几乎是不可能的。这片沙滩上的碎浪，遵循的是另一套规律，与特林达迪海岸其余地段全然不同。说来奇怪，这里风从岸上往外吹时，碎浪反倒比风从海上往岸吹时更凶。东北风顺着湾后山坡猛扫而下，顶住了滚进湾来的涌浪，把它垒成一道道陡立的水墙，崩裂得惊心动魄。东南风在外海掀起的涌更高，但它径直灌进这湾里，反倒把海面压了下去，登岸便比较容易。瀑布对面的锚地则规律相反：东北风离岸吹时海面平静，东南风一起碎浪便涨；但如我先前所言，那里的海面总是比西南湾平静。

===Original===
The men rested on their oars, and we watched the surf from a safe distance, to discover if there were any chance of picking a favourable opportunity for landing. It would be a disappointing matter if we had to pull our boatload of stores back to the yacht against the wind; so, after a little hesitation, I decided to risk the landing. One must run some risks on such a place as Trinidad, and we might as well commence at once. All in the boat were delighted at the decision.

===Chinese===
