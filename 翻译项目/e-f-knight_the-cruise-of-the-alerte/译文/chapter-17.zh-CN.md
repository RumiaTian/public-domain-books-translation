## XVII / 十七

===Original===
Pick and Shovel

===Chinese===
十字镐与铁锹

===Original===
As it was a Sunday there was no work done on the first day of my stay in camp; all hands had the usual holiday, which they chiefly employed in fishing, and in mending their clothes. I walked up the ravine and was surprised to find that so much of the landslip had been already removed. The trench was about twenty feet broad, and ultimately attained a depth of upwards of twenty feet in places. It extended for some distance along the face of the cliff﻿—if that term can be properly applied to a steep slope of a sort of natural concrete, a compact but somewhat brittle mass of stones and earth. It was at the foot of this cliff that we expected to find the cave described by the pirate, but how far we should have to dig down through the accumulation of earth and rocks that had fallen from above and now filled up the bottom of the ravine it was not easy even to conjecture.

===Chinese===
我进营的头一天恰逢星期日，照例不干活；全体人员放了例假，多半拿来钓鱼、缝补衣裳。我沿着裂谷走上去，惊见滑坡竟已被清除了这么多。壕沟约有二十英尺宽，最终有些地方深达二十英尺以上。它沿着峭壁的立面延展了相当一段——倘若「峭壁」一词当真能恰当地安在一道陡坡头上的话：那坡是一种天然的混凝土，是石块与泥土压成的致密而略嫌松脆的一体。我们指望在这峭壁脚下找到海盗描述过的洞穴，至于还得往下挖多深、穿过多少从上方坍落而今填满谷底的积土乱石，那可连臆测都不容易。

===Original===
Our object, it will be seen, was to clear the face of the cliff until we came to the original bottom of the ravine. Though the cliff was, as I have explained, composed of brittle matter, as if in an intermediate state between earth and rock, and of comparatively modern formation, it was easy to distinguish it from the much looser soil of the landslip that lay along its sides; this last, too, was of a very different colour, being reddish brown, whereas the cliff was slate-blue.

===Chinese===
可以看出，我们的目标是要清理峭壁的立面，一直清到裂谷原本的谷底。这峭壁虽如我解释过的那样，由松脆之物构成，仿佛处在土与石之间的过渡状态，生成的年代也较晚近，但要把它同贴在它两侧、松散得多的滑坡浮土区分开来，却并不费事；这浮土的颜色也截然不同，是红棕色，而峭壁则是板岩青。

===Original===
The men had constructed several little paths leading from the trench, down the ravine, to the edges of the chasms and precipitous steps which are frequent in this gully, and the earth and stones that were dug out of the trench were carried down these paths in the wheelbarrows and tilted over the precipices. As we gradually filled up these chasms the roads had to be extended further down the ravine, and at last we had formed a great dyke which stretched right across it. I was satisfied that all the operations had been conducted with judgment, and, if the treasure were in the ravine at all, there was but little doubt that we should find it.

===Chinese===
众人已经修出了几条小路，从壕沟顺着裂谷往下，通到这道沟谷里比比皆是的裂罅和陡坎边上；从沟里挖出的泥土石块，装进独轮车沿这些小路运下去，倒下悬崖。随着这些裂罅渐渐填满，道路只得向裂谷更下游延展，到最后我们竟筑成了一道横贯全谷的大堤。各项工程都办得有章有法，我很满意；倘若宝藏果真在这道裂谷里，我们找得到它，几乎没有多少可怀疑的余地。

===Original===
The same rules that had been laid down by the doctor for the discipline of the camp were observed during my stay on shore. All hands turned out at dawn, and cocoa and biscuit were served out. Then we worked hard from half-past five till nine, at which hour the temperature in that closed in ravine became so high that it was quite impossible even for a black man to work with pick and shovel. A bath in the sea, to refresh ourselves and wash off the clinging red dust, was our next proceeding. Then we put off our working clothes for others, and partook of a good breakfast, consisting chiefly of oatmeal, which we found by experience was the best food to work on. During the heat of the day we lay in our tents, almost panting for breath at times, so intolerably hot and close it was. At half-past three we returned to the ravine and did another three hours’ work. After this was another bath, then supper. There was a whole holiday on Sunday and a half holiday on Wednesday.

===Chinese===
我住在岸上期间，医生为营地纪律制定的那些规矩依然照行。天一亮全体起身，发放可可和硬面饼。随后从五点半苦干到九点；一到这个钟点，这道闭塞的裂谷里温度便高得叫人抡镐挥锹都干不下去，连黑人也不行。接下来便是下海洗个澡，振作精神，冲掉黏在身上的红尘。然后脱下工装换上别的衣裳，吃一顿丰盛的早餐，主要是燕麦粥——经验证明，干活最顶用的吃食就是它。白天最热的时辰，我们躺在帐篷里，有时几乎喘不上气来，那闷热实在教人受不了。三点半回到裂谷，再干三个钟头。这之后又洗一个澡，然后开晚饭。星期日全天休息，星期三歇半天。

===Original===
Even during the early hours of the morning, when the sides of the ravine shaded us from the sun, digging was hot and trying work for white men. We were, of course, bathed in perspiration all the while, and were, consequently very thirsty, so that the cook was kept busily employed in going backwards and forwards between camp and trench to refill our water-bottles.

===Chinese===
即便在一大早、裂谷两侧替我们遮着太阳的时辰，挖掘对白人也是又热又熬人的活计。我们自始至终汗出如浴，因而口渴得厉害，厨子只得在营地与壕沟之间来来回回地奔忙，替我们把水壶灌满。

===Original===
In the middle of the day the sun, blazing on the sands, made them terribly hot. No one could step on them with bare feet, even for a moment; one could not even lay one’s hand on the ground.

===Chinese===
正午时分，烈日炙烤着沙滩，把沙晒得烫得吓人。谁也不能赤脚在上面落脚，哪怕一下也不行；连把手按到地面上都按不住。

===Original===
The sand here is mixed with a finely granulated black mineral substance, and I think it is the presence of this that causes so great an absorption of heat. I have never found sands elsewhere, even in the Sahara, attain so high a temperature.

===Chinese===
这里的沙里混着一种细粒的黑色矿物，我想正是这东西作祟，沙才吸热吸得这样厉害。我从未见过别处的沙——连撒哈拉的也不例外——升到过这样高的温度。

===Original===
We were not altogether lazy out of digging hours. One’s clothes had to be washed, water had to be brought down in breakers and demijohns from the distant issue in the cliffs, and firewood had to be gathered. We sometimes went out in a body to perform this last duty. We would climb high up the mountainsides, where the dead trees lay thickest, and throw down the timber before us as we descended, until we had accumulated a large quantity at the bottom.

===Chinese===
不挖土的时辰，我们倒也并不全然偷懒。衣裳要洗，水要用淡水桶和细颈大坛从峭壁间那处远远的出水运下来，柴要拾。有时我们便倾队出动，去干最后一桩差事。我们爬到山坡高处枯树躺得最密的地方，一路往下走、一路把木料朝前扔下去，直到山脚下攒起一大堆。

===Original===
I shared one of the tents with Pursell, while the doctor and Powell occupied the other. On my first night on shore we caught three turtle. Our black cook, who was always looking out for them, came to my tent and reported that, while prowling about the beach, he had observed several large females crawling up the sands. It was a very dark night, so, taking a lantern, four of us set out. We soon came across one of the creatures, and followed her quietly until she had reached a spot far above high-water mark, and then we turned her over on her back. This is by no means an easy undertaking when one has to deal with a seven-hundred-pound turtle, and requires at least four men to carry it out. The turtle does not permit this liberty to be taken with her without offering considerable resistance: with her powerful flippers she drives the sand violently into the faces of her aggressors, attempting to blind them, so that caution has to be observed in approaching her. We turned over three turtle, and, on the following day, salted down the meat that we could not eat in a fresh state.

===Chinese===
我同珀塞尔合住一顶帐篷，医生和鲍威尔住另一顶。上岸后的头一夜，我们就捉了三只海龟。我们的黑人厨子一向替它们留神，来到我帐篷里报告说，他在海滩上转悠时，望见几只硕大的母龟正往沙上爬。那夜天黑得很，于是提上一盏灯，我们四个出发了。不久就撞见一只，便悄悄跟着它，直到它爬到远高出满潮线的地方，我们才把它翻了个肚皮朝天。对付一只七百磅重的海龟，这绝非轻而易举的事，至少得四个人才办得成。海龟绝不会任人这般摆布而不奋力抗拒：它用强有力的鳍肢把沙子狠狠扇到冒犯者的脸上，想把他们的眼睛迷住，因此接近它时须得留神。我们一共翻倒了三只，第二天便把鲜着吃不完的肉腌了起来。

===Original===
Turtle are kept alive for weeks on board ship, even in the tropics, and all the care that is taken of them consists in placing pillows under their heads, as they lie on their backs on deck﻿—so as to prevent apoplexy, I suppose﻿—and in throwing an occasional bucket of water over them. These creatures seem to be able to do without food for a very long period. We found that we could not employ this method of keeping alive the turtle we caught, for, though we constantly poured buckets of water over them, and shaded them with matting, they could not exist on these blazing sands; and the practice, cruel enough at sea, would have been much more so here.

===Chinese===
海龟在船上能一连活上几个星期，即便在热带也是如此；人们对它们的全部照料，不过是趁它们仰面躺在甲板上时，往它们头底下塞个枕头——我猜想是为了防止中风——再时不时浇上一桶水。这些生灵似乎能很长时间不吃东西。我们发觉没法用这个法子养活捉到的海龟，因为尽管我们不断往它们身上一桶桶地浇水，又用席子给它们遮阴，它们在这火烧似的沙滩上还是活不成；这法子在海上已够残忍，在这里就更残忍了。

===Original===
The paid hands enjoyed turtle-hunting, and were inclined, thoughtlessly, to turn over more turtle than were required for purposes of food; so that I had to give an order that no turtle should be turned over without leave, and the destruction of the creatures was strictly limited to the requirements of the larder. A similar law was made for the protection of the silly seabirds, and the only animals that could be slaughtered with impunity were the unfortunate land-crabs, for they had no friends among us to take their part and legislate on their behalf. They were now not nearly so plentiful in the vicinity of the camp as they had been. They had begun to give up their ignorant contempt for man, and on only one occasion during my stay on shore was it considered necessary for four of us to sally forth with sticks, before supper, and slay about a hundred each.

===Chinese===
受雇水手们喜欢猎龟，一时无心，动不动就把超出食用所需的龟也翻倒；于是我不得不下一道命令：未得许可，不得翻倒任何海龟，对这些生灵的宰杀，一律严格限制在伙房需要之内。为保护那些憨傻的海鸟，也立了类似的法规；唯一可以随意宰杀而不受罚的动物，只有倒霉的陆蟹，因为在我们中间没有谁肯替它们说话、替它们立法。这时营地左近的陆蟹已远不如先前那般众多。它们开始放弃那套对人的无知轻蔑；我住在岸上期间，只有一回，大家认为有必要在晚饭前提着棍子四人出击，每人击杀约一百只。

===Original===
The turtle were now so plentiful that we could have caught in a fortnight sufficient to last us for six months, had we even lived on nothing else. The Trinidad turtle are of large size﻿—500 to 700 pounds﻿—and their flavour is excellent. We had turtle-soup and turtle-steak every day for breakfast and dinner, so that we became utterly weary of the rich food, and I do not think any of us wish to see calipash or calipee for a long time to come.

===Chinese===
此刻海龟多得很，哪怕我们顿顿只吃它，两个星期里捉的也够撑上六个月。特林达迪的海龟个头硕大——五百到七百磅——滋味又极佳。我们天天早晚饭都是龟汤、龟排，这等肥腴的吃食吃得我们彻底倒了胃口，我想往后很长一段日子里，我们谁也不想再见到龟背肉（calipash）或龟腹肉（calipee）了。

===Original===
We did not neglect the other useful products of the island. We gathered the wild beans, and found them a very welcome addition to our diet. Of fish we always had plenty. Powell was our great fisherman, and was the inventor of the seine constructed of wire-fencing which I have already described. In addition to the edible fish I have mentioned as swarming in these waters there are several other species that we looked upon with some doubt, and refrained from eating. Some of these were of quaint forms and dazzling colours, so that their appearance seemed to warn us of their poisonous nature. There were fish of brilliant blue, others with stripes of white and purple, others with vermilion fins and yellow bands like those of a wasp. Sea-snakes abounded in the pools. These, according to an Italian cook we had on the *Falcon*, are edible; but we did not venture to try them. They attain the length of five feet and are of a grey colour, with yellow stripes. They appear to be of savage disposition, for, when harpooned, they twist about and bite with fury anything within their reach.

===Chinese===
岛上其他有用的出产，我们也没有怠慢。我们采来野豆子，发觉它们很受欢迎，给伙食添了新花样。鱼则从来管够。鲍威尔是我们这里的钓鱼大家，我已描述过的那张铁丝围网做的拉网，发明人便是他。除了我提过在这片水域里成群洄游的可食鱼类之外，还有几种我们看着犯嘀咕、没敢吃的鱼。有的模样古怪、颜色晃眼，那副长相就像在警告我们它们有毒。有通体亮蓝的鱼，有带白紫条纹的，有长着朱红鳍、黄条纹黄得像胡蜂腰带的。水潭里海蛇成群。据我们在猎鹰号上时的一位意大利厨子说，这东西能吃；但我们没敢尝试。它们能长到五英尺长，灰色，带黄条纹。看那性情颇为凶悍，因为一旦被鱼叉叉中，它们便狂扭身子，够得着什么就狂咬什么。

===Original===
I stayed on shore altogether for a fortnight, and kept a journal of our proceedings, which, together with several sketches, specimens of the flora, and other articles, were washed out of the lifeboat and lost when we abandoned the island. The loss of the journal, however, matters little, for our life on shore was almost devoid of incident, and was chiefly made up of monotonous work with pick, shovel, and wheelbarrow.

===Chinese===
我在岸上前后待了两个星期，把我们的大小事务记了一本日志；这本日志，连同几幅速写、几件植物标本和其他物件，后来在我们弃岛离去的当口，被海水从救生艇里冲了出去，就此丢失。不过日志的损失无甚要紧，因为我们岸上的生活几乎没有波澜，主要就是十字镐、铁锹加独轮车的单调活计。

===Original===
We dug away, still through loose soil that had evidently formed part of the landslip, and removed some thousands of tons; but we did not come to the foot of the cliff, or the cave which is described to be there. Some of the stones that we had to remove in the course of our digging were very large. We had a quantity of strong ropes and blocks on shore, and when we came across an exceptionally big rock, we clapped a number of watch-tackles one on the other, and, by putting all hands on the fall of the last tackle, we obtained a very powerful purchase, equivalent, I calculated on one occasion, to the power of five hundred men. We found bones and bits of decayed wood among the earth, but the former always proved to be the remains of a goat and not of a pirate, and the latter were the fragments of dead trees and not of chests of loot.

===Chinese===
我们一路挖下去，穿的仍是明显属于滑坡体的松土，清走了几千吨；却还没有挖到峭壁脚下，也没有挖到据说在那里的洞穴。挖掘途中必须清走的有些石块个头很大。我们在岸上备有不少粗绳和滑轮，碰上特别巨大的岩石，就把几组滑车一组搭一组地串起来，让全体人手齐拉最末一组滑车的力端绳，由此获得一股极其强劲的机械之力——有一回我估算过，抵得上五百个人的力气。我们在土里挖到过骨头和朽木碎片，但前者查来查去总是山羊的遗骸，不是海盗的；后者总是枯树的残片，不是赃物箱子的。

===Original===
But shortly before Christmas there were some encouraging signs. We had now got down to a considerable depth, and we noticed that, when a pick was driven into the bottom of the trench, a hollow sound was given out, as if we were on the roof of a cavern, and, occasionally, little holes would open out and the earth would slip down into some chasm underneath. We dug still deeper, and we came to a collection of very large rocks, which we were unable to move. They were jammed together, and evidently formed the roof of a cavern, for, wherever we could clear away the earth that lay between any two of these rocks, we looked down through the opening into a black, empty space, the bottom of which we could not touch by thrusting through our longest crowbar. This looked promising, for it was just such a cavern as this that we were seeking.

===Chinese===
但圣诞节前不久，出现了一些令人鼓舞的迹象。我们这时已下掘到相当的深度，发觉十字镐凿到沟底时会发出空洞的声响，仿佛我们正踩在一座洞穴的顶上；偶尔还会裂开一些小孔，泥土便溜进底下某道裂罅里去。我们挖得更深，碰到一堆硕大无朋的岩石，怎么也挪不动。它们彼此挤死在一起，显然构成了一座洞穴的顶盖，因为凡是能清走两块岩石之间浮土的地方，我们都能透过豁口俯瞰一片漆黑的空洞，把最长的那根撬棍捅下去也够不着底。这情形大有希望，因为我们苦苦寻找的，正是这样一座洞穴。

===Original===
We found that the rocks were too close together to allow of our effecting an entrance from above, so we dug down along the side of the last and largest of these until we came to its foot; and there indeed was a sort of cavern, partly filled up with loose earth, which we cleared out.

===Chinese===
我们发现这些岩石挤得太紧，没法从顶上打开入口，于是沿着最后也最大的一块的侧边往下挖，一直挖到它的脚下；那里果然有一座洞穴模样的所在，半填着松土，我们把土清了出来。

===Original===
There was no treasure in it, and nothing to show that any human being, before us, had ever visited the spot. I think it was at this stage of our operations that each man began very seriously to doubt whether we were searching in the right place at all, and whether there might not be some further clue that was missing, and, without which, search would almost certainly be futile. But, whatever may have been thought, there was, so far as I can remember, no expression given to these doubts, and each worked on with the same cheery will as at the beginning, even as if he were confident of success. These men were determined, in an almost literal sense, to leave no stone unturned, and not to abandon that ravine until they had satisfied themselves as to whether the treasure was or was not there.

===Chinese===
洞里没有宝藏，也没有半点迹象表明在我们之前曾有任何人到过此地。我想正是在工程的这个阶段，每个人心里都开始很认真地犯嘀咕：我们究竟是不是找对了地方；会不会还缺一条更进一步的线索，少了它，搜寻几乎注定徒劳。但无论心里怎么想，据我记忆所及，这些疑虑谁也没有说出口，人人照旧带着起初那股欢实的劲头干下去，俨然对成功深信不疑。这班人拿定了主意，几乎是照着字面的意思，非要「把每块石头都翻遍」，不弄清宝藏究竟在不在那里，绝不弃了那道裂谷。

===Original===
On the Sunday after my arrival on shore, December 22, we went off in the whaleboat to see how Pollock was getting on. The weather had been exceedingly fine throughout the week in Southwest Bay, and we might have launched the boat on almost any day; but, though there had been no heavy wind in the neighbourhood of the island, there had been a considerable swell at the anchorage for part of the time, and Pollock reported that the yacht had tumbled about a good deal. He had found opportunities for landing at the pier with the dinghy, and had brought off some breakers of water from the cascade and a quantity of firewood. He had been very lucky with his fishing, having caught several germanic, weighing from twenty to forty pounds apiece, and an abundance of other fish. Ted Milner was now taken on shore with us, while Arthur Cotton was left on board.

===Chinese===
我上岸后的第一个星期日，12 月 22 日，我们乘捕鲸艇出海，去看波洛克过得怎样。整整一个星期，西南湾的天气都极好，几乎哪天都可以放艇；但是，虽说岛近旁没有刮过大风，锚地里有一阵子却涌浪不小，波洛克报告说，游艇颠簸得不轻。他寻着机会乘小划艇在天然码头登了岸，从瀑布运回几桶淡水，还弄回不少柴火。他钓鱼的手气极好，钓到几条「热拉尼克」（germanic），每条重二十到四十磅，此外还有大量的其他鱼。这时特德·米尔纳跟我们上了岸，阿瑟·科顿则留在船上。

===Original===
We worked away steadily in the ravine until Christmas Day, when there was, of course, a holiday. We had a most luxurious dinner on shore, as also had the three men on board the vessel. The menu of our shore-dinner was as follows:﻿—Turtle soup, boiled hind-fish, curried turtle-steak, boiled salt junk, tinned plum-pudding. For vegetables we had preserved potatoes and carrots, and Trinidad beans. Good old rum was the only beverage. There were some other luxuries, chief of which was a box of cigars, which had been put away for this occasion. Christmas Day was intensely hot, so that we remained in our tents, having no energy for exploring mountains. With the exception of Jacko’s disgraceful intoxication, no incidents of note occurred.

===Chinese===
我们在裂谷里稳稳当当地干到圣诞节，那天自然放假。我们在岸上吃了一顿极尽奢华的正餐，船上那三个人也同样沾了光。我们这顿岸上大餐的菜单如下：——龟汤，清煮石斑鱼（hind），咖喱龟排，煮咸牛肉，罐装葡萄干布丁。蔬菜有罐头土豆、胡萝卜，还有特林达迪的豆子。上好的陈年朗姆酒是唯一的饮品。此外另有几样奢侈品，头一样是一盒雪茄，专为这个日子收藏至今。圣诞节那天热得厉害，我们便待在帐篷里，没有精力去探山。除了杰科那次不成体统的酗酒之外，没有发生什么值得一提的事。

===Original===
On the Sunday after Christmas Day, Pursell and myself set out to explore the weather side of the island, taking our lunch in our pockets﻿—biscuits, figs, rum, and tobacco. We crossed the Sugarloaf Col and descended upon the coast of Southeast Bay, then we turned to the right and followed the shore to the extreme south end of the island, where Noah’s Ark falls a sheer wall into the surf.

===Chinese===
圣诞节后的那个星期日，我和珀塞尔出发去探察岛的向风一面，午饭揣在口袋里——硬面饼、无花果、朗姆酒和烟草。我们翻过糖面包山隘，下到东南湾的海岸，然后向右转，沿着岸边一直走到岛的最南端，在那里，「诺亚方舟」如一堵笔直的高墙，直插进碎浪之中。

===Original===
There was a quantity of wreckage in this bay, and in one place we found a topmast and some ribs of a vessel which might have been the remains of the hull I had seen here nine years before. The broken bits of planks, timbers, barrels, hen-coops and other relics of ships, were piled quite thickly on the rocks above high-water mark, and we came across a square-faced gin bottle, full of fresh water, which, from its position, could not have been washed ashore, but must have been left here by some human being. We saw the footprints of turtle, showing that every sandy beach on this island is frequented by numbers of these creatures. In view of the threatened turtle-famine we read of, it might be worth someone’s while to come here for a cargo of them; but the difficulty of getting any quantity off alive would be great.

===Chinese===
这座湾里散落着大量船骸，有一处我们还发现一根中桅和若干船肋，说不定就是我九年前在此见过的那具船壳的残余。断裂的船板、木料、桶子、鸡笼和别的船上遗物，厚厚地堆在满潮线以上的礁石上；我们还撞见一只方棱瓶身的金酒瓶，盛着满满一瓶淡水，从它所在的位置看，绝计不是被浪冲上岸的，必定是哪个世人遗落在那里的。我们看见了海龟的足迹，可见这座岛上每一片沙滩，都有成群的这类生灵频频光顾。鉴于我们读到过的那种海龟行将绝迹之忧，专程来此装一船回去倒是值得；只是想把相当数量活着运走，难处可就大了。

===Original===
The scenery of East Bay is very extraordinary, for here the signs of volcanic action are more evident than on any other portion of the island. At the south end of the bay there is no sandy beach; masses of shattered rocks, fallen from above, strew the shore, and between these are solidified streams of black lava, which appear to have followed each other in successive waves, one having cooled before the next has poured down upon it, so that a series of rounded steps is formed. The ledges of lava extend far out to sea, producing a dangerous reef, on which the sea always breaks heavily.

===Chinese===
东湾的景色极为奇特，因为岛上再没有哪一处，火山活动的痕迹比这里更彰明较著。湾的南端没有沙滩；从上方崩落的碎岩大块大块地撒满岸边，碎岩之间是一条条凝固的黑色熔岩流，看去像一波接一波先后袭来，前一波冷却之后，后一波才浇覆其上，于是一道道圆浑的阶台就此层叠而成。熔岩台地远远伸入海中，形成一道凶险的礁石，海浪撞在上面，永远是雷霆万钧。

===Original===
As we advanced over the boulders there towered above us on our right hand the perpendicular side of Noah’s Ark, of a strange red colour, looking like molten iron where the sun’s rays fell upon it. A quantity of red debris from the roof of this mountain was also lying on the shore, and at the north end of it we observed that a gigantic couloir﻿—as it would be called in the Alps﻿—of volcanic ashes and lava sloped down from its summit to the gap which connects it with the Sugarloaf. It was obvious, from the vast amount of these fire-consumed debris and waves of lava surrounding its base, that Noah’s Ark had once been a very active volcano, and I think it highly probable that there is a crater at the top of it. Though it is perpendicular on three sides, it might be possible to ascend it from the fourth side, by the couloir connecting it with the gap under the Sugarloaf; but the attempt would be risky, and a slip on its steep, sloping roof would mean a drop over a wall 800 feet in height.

===Chinese===
我们在巨石间前行，右手边「诺亚方舟」笔直的侧壁在头顶巍然高耸，泛着一种奇异的红色，阳光落到哪里，哪里便像熔化的铁水。这座山「屋顶」上剥落的红色碎屑也大量躺在岸上；在它的北端，我们望见一道巨大的沟槽（couloir）——若在阿尔卑斯山，人们便这样称呼它——由火山灰和熔岩构成，从山顶斜落下来，直通它与糖面包山相连的那处山隘。山脚四周环绕着这么多火焚的碎屑和一道道熔岩的波浪，足见「诺亚方舟」当年是一座非常活跃的火山，我认为它顶上极有可能存在一个火山口。它三面皆是绝壁，从第四面、循着连接它与糖面包山下山隘的那道沟槽，兴许登得上去；但这一尝试颇为冒险，在它那陡斜的「屋顶」上失足一滑，便意味着从一面八百英尺高的墙上跌落下去。

===Original===
We clambered over the rocks until we came to the end of Noah’s Ark, and we stood on a ledge of lava and gazed at one of the strangest sights of this strange island. The base of the great red mountain is pierced by a magnificent tunnel, known as the Archway, which connects Southwest Bay with East Bay. What seem to be gigantic stalactites depend from its roof; and the different gradations of colour and shade on its rugged sides﻿—from glowing red in the blaze of the sun to terra-cotta, delicate pink, and rich purple, and then to deepest black in the inmost recesses﻿—produce a very beautiful effect. The heaving water is black within it, save where the white spray flashes; but, looking through it, one perceives, beyond, the bright green waves of Southwest Bay, and the blue sky above them.

===Chinese===
我们攀过乱石，来到「诺亚方舟」的尽头，站上一道熔岩台地，凝望这座奇岛最奇特的景致之一。大红山的山脚被一条壮丽的隧道洞穿，人称「拱道」（Archway），它把西南湾与东湾连通起来。洞顶垂着俨然巨型石钟乳的东西；它嶙峋的洞壁上，色彩与明暗层层递变——烈日灼照下的炽红，到赤陶色、淡粉、浓紫，再到最深处内窾的漆黑——生出极美的效果。洞里涌动的海水一片黑沉沉，唯有白色浪花闪过的地方是例外；但透过洞子望去，可以望见那一头西南湾亮绿的波涛，和波涛之上的蓝天。

===Original===
The sea does not flow freely through the tunnel, except at high-water; for, on the side we were standing, its mouth is crossed by a ledge of lava, which is left dry by the receding tide. But inside the tunnel there is deep water, and the ocean swell always penetrates it from Southwest Bay, dashing up its sides with a great roar, which is repeated in hoarse echoes by the mountain.

===Chinese===
除非在满潮时分，海水并不能畅快地流过隧道；因为在我们站的这一侧，洞口横着一道熔岩台地，退潮之后便露出干面。但隧道里面水很深，大洋的涌浪总是从西南湾灌进来，轰然撞上两侧洞壁，山又用沙哑的回声一遍遍应和。

===Original===
According to an ancient description of Trinidad quoted in the *South Atlantic Directory*, the Archway is 40 feet in breadth, 50 in height, and 420 in length. I think it far higher and broader than this﻿—at any rate, at its mouth. No doubt the action of the surf is continually enlarging it.

===Chinese===
据《南大西洋航海指南》所引的一段关于特林达迪的古老记述，「拱道」宽 40 英尺，高 50 英尺，长 420 英尺。我却觉得它远比这更高更宽——至少洞口是如此。毫无疑问，碎浪的侵蚀正在不断地把它扩大。

===Original===
Pursell and myself, having admired this beautiful scene for some time, turned back, crossed the rocky promontory of East Point, and proceeded along the sands till we came to the Portuguese settlement, which I wished to examine more carefully than I had been able to do when here with the doctor a month before.

===Chinese===
我和珀塞尔把这幅美景饱览了一阵，便转身折回，越过东角的石质海岬，沿着沙滩前行，一直走到葡萄牙人的屯居地——一个月前我同医生来此，未能细看，这一趟正要补上，把它察看得更仔细些。

===Original===
We had lunch by the side of the river which flows under the Portuguese ruins, and then commenced to explore. The Portuguese had certainly selected the only spot on the island at all suitable for a permanent settlement; for not only is there here the best supply of water, but there is also a considerable area of fairly fertile land, though it is greatly encumbered with rocks. The downs by the river are densely covered with beans, which also grow all over the ruined huts. It is possible that these beans were originally planted here by the settlers, and have since spread over all the downs between this and Southwest Bay; for they are not to be found on the other side of the island.

===Chinese===
我们在流经葡萄牙废墟下方的那条河边用了午饭，随后着手踏勘。葡萄牙人选中的，确是全岛唯一算得上适合长久定居的所在；因为此地不但有全岛最好的水源，还有一片颇为肥沃的可观地面，尽管乱石壅塞得厉害。河边的草冈上密密覆着豆子，坍毁的茅屋上也长得到处都是。这些豆子或许本是屯居者当初种下的，此后便蔓延开来，长满了从这里直到西南湾之间的所有草冈；因为岛的另一面并无它们的踪影。

===Original===
The huts, of which the rough walls of unhewn stone alone remain, are built in terraces one above the other on the hillside. A great deal of labour was evidently expended in the construction of these terraces, and of the roads leading to them, and quantities of stones had been piled-up in order to obtain a level surface. This must have been a picturesque little village in its day﻿—whenever that day was, for, though I have searched diligently, I can find no record to show at what period Trinidad was used as a penal settlement by the Portuguese. Amaso Delano, writing of his visit to the island in 1803, speaks of a “beach above which the Portuguese once had a settlement;” and a still older narrative alludes to a Portuguese penal establishment here as a thing of the long past. Malley, who was here in 1700, took Trinidad in the name of the King of England﻿—as I have already mentioned﻿—and he says nothing of such a settlement.

===Chinese===
那些茅屋如今只剩毛石垒成的粗墙，一层层台地相叠，筑在山坡上。修筑这些台地和通往台地的道路，显然耗费了大量劳力，还堆垒起大量的石头，好造出平整的基面。在它的全盛之日——且不论那是哪年哪月——这里想必曾是一座景致如画的小村；因为虽经我悉心搜求，仍找不到任何记载，能说明葡萄牙人在哪个时期把特林达迪用作流放犯的屯居地。阿马索·德拉诺写他 1803 年游历此岛的经过，提到「一处海滩，葡萄牙人曾在它的上方设过屯居地」；还有一部更古老的纪事，提到葡萄牙人在此的流放机构时，已把它说成遥远的往事了。哈雷于 1700 年至此，以英王之名占取了特林达迪——这一点我已提过——他对这样一处屯居地却只字未提。

===Original===
Near the huts we found places where the soil had been cleared of stones, for purposes of cultivation, and there were several walled-in enclosures.

===Chinese===
茅屋近旁，我们见到几处为耕种而拣净了石头的地面，还有几圈垒墙围起来的园场。

===Original===

For, on the top of a hill overlooking our ravine, we came across a hole that had evidently been dug for the purpose of extracting a sort of clay that is there, and there were signs of fire near it, and many fragments of earthenware, so we conjectured that we were looking at all that remained of the ancient Trinidad pottery-works.

===Chinese===
