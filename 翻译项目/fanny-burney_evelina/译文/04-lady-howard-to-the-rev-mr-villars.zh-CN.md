## Letter / 书信
===Original===

I Lady Howard to the Rev. Mr. Villars
Howard Grove, Kent.
Can anything, my good Sir, be more painful to a friendly mind, than a necessity of communicating disagreeable intelligence? Indeed it is sometimes difficult to determine, whether the relator or the receiver of evil tidings is most to be pitied.
I have just had a letter from Madame Duval; she is totally at a loss in what manner to behave; she seems desirous to repair the wrongs she has done, yet wishes the world to believe her blameless. She would fain cast upon another the odium of those misfortunes for which she alone is answerable. Her letter is violent, sometimes abusive, and that of *you!﻿—you*, to whom she is under obligations which are greater even than her faults, but to whose advice she wickedly imputes all the sufferings of her much injured daughter, the late Lady Belmont. The chief purport of her writing I will acquaint you with; the letter itself is not worthy your notice.
She tells me that she has, for many years past, been in continual expectation of making a journey to England, which prevented her writing for information concerning this melancholy subject, by giving her hopes of making personal enquiries; but family occurrences have still detained her in France, which country she now sees no prospect of quitting. She has, therefore, lately used her utmost endeavours to obtain a faithful account of whatever related to her *ill-advised* daughter; the result of which giving her *some reason* to apprehend, that, upon her deathbed, she bequeathed an infant orphan to the world, she most graciously says, that if *you*, with whom *she understands* the child is placed, will procure authentic proofs of its relationship to her, you may sent it to Paris, where she will properly provide for it.
This woman is, undoubtedly, at length, self-convicted of her most unnatural behaviour; it is evident, from her writing, that she is still as vulgar and illiterate as when her first husband, Mr. Evelyn, had the weakness to marry her; nor does she at all apologize for addressing herself to me, though I was only once in her company.
Her letter has excited in my daughter Mirvan, a strong desire to be informed of the motives which induced Madame Duval to abandon the unfortunate Lady Belmont, at a time when a mother’s protection was peculiarly necessary for her peace and her reputation.

第一封 霍华德夫人致尊敬的维拉尔斯（Villars）牧师先生
肯特郡，霍华德林苑。
亲爱的先生，还有什么比不得不传递坏消息，更叫一颗友爱的心难受的呢？说实在的，坏消息的报信人和收信人到底哪个更可怜，有时真难断定。
我刚收到杜瓦尔夫人（Madame Duval）一封信；她全然不知该拿自己怎么办才好；她像是想弥补自己造下的亏欠，却又盼着世人相信她清白无辜。那些不幸，责任本在她一人，她却巴不得把骂名转嫁旁人。她的信写得暴戾，有时竟出恶言——骂的还是*您！——您*，她欠您的恩情比她的过错还大，可她却恶毒地把她那惨遭伤害的女儿、已故的贝尔蒙特夫人的全部苦难，都归罪于您的劝导。她这封信的要旨，我且说与您；信本身不配劳您的眼。
她告诉我，多年来她一直指望动身来英格兰，指望能当面打听，也就懒得为这桩伤心事写信问讯了；可家事一桩接一桩，把她绊在法国，如今也看不出有离开那国的指望。于是她最近费尽力气，想弄到一份关于她那*失计的*女儿的可靠报告；查出的结果让她*多少有理由*推测：女儿临终时，给这世界留下了一个襁褓中的孤儿。于是她恩典浩荡地说：倘若*您*——*据她所闻*，孩子是放在您那儿的——能办出孩子与她血缘关系的正式凭据，您可以把孩子送到巴黎去，她自会妥当安顿。
这个女人，毫无疑问，到头来在自己笔下招认了自己那最悖逆人情的行为；从她的信看得出，她如今还是那么粗俗、那么目不识丁，同她第一任丈夫伊夫林先生一时软弱娶她时毫无二致；她径直给我写信，也一点不道声不是——我统共只同她见过一回。
她这封信惹得我女儿米尔文太太生了强烈的心思，想弄明白：当年杜瓦尔夫人究竟出于什么动机，抛下了那不幸的贝尔蒙特夫人——在那样一个时节，母亲的维护，于她的安宁、于她的名声，都格外要紧。

Notwithstanding I was personally acquainted with all the parties concerned in that affair, the subject always appeared of too delicate a nature to be spoken of with the principals; I cannot, therefore, satisfy Mrs. Mirvan otherwise than by applying to you.
By saying that you *may* send the child, Madame Duval aims at *conferring*, where she most *owes* obligation. I pretend not to give you advice; you, to whose generous protection this helpless orphan is indebted for everything, are the best and only judge of what she ought to do; but I am much concerned at the trouble and uneasiness which this unworthy woman may occasion you.
My daughter and my grandchild join with me in desiring to be most kindly remembered to the amiable girl; and they bid me remind you, that the annual visit to Howard Grove, which we were formerly promised, has been discontinued for more than four years.
I am, dear Sir, with great regard,

Your most obedient friend and servant,
M. Howard.

那桩事里的各当事人，我虽都亲自认识，这题目却始终显得过于微妙，不便同当事人开口；因此，要满足米尔文太太，我只有来请教您。
杜瓦尔夫人说什么您*可以*把孩子送去，她是想在她最*亏欠*的地方*施恩*。我不敢给您出主意；这个孤苦无依的孤儿，一切都有赖您慷慨的庇护，她该怎么办，您是最好也是唯一的裁判；只是这个不值当的女人恐怕要给您添烦恼、添不安，这叫我挂心。
我女儿和外孙女同我一起，向那位可爱的姑娘致以最亲切的问候；她们还让我提醒您：从前许诺过的每年一趟霍华德林苑之行，到如今已经断四年多了。
亲爱的先生，我怀着十二分的敬意，是您

最恭顺的朋友与仆人，
M. 霍华德。
