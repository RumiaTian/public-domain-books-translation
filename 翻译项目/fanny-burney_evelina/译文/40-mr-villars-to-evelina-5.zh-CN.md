## Letter / 书信
===Original===

XXXVII Mr. Villars to Evelina
Berry Hill, May 21.
Let not my Evelina be depressed by a stroke of fortune for which she is not responsible. No breach of duty on your part has incurred the unkindness which has been shown you; nor have you, by any act of imprudence, provoked either censure or reproach. Let me entreat you, therefore, my dearest child, to support yourself with that courage which your innocency ought to inspire: and let all the affliction you allow yourself be for him only who, not having that support, must one day be but too severely sensible how much he wants it.
The hint thrown out concerning myself is wholly unintelligible to me: my heart, I dare own, fully acquits me of vice; but *without blemish*, I have never ventured to pronounce myself. However, it seems his intention to be hereafter more explicit; and *then*﻿—should anything appear, that has on *my* part contributed to those misfortunes we lament, let me at least say, that the most partial of my friends cannot be so much astonished as I shall myself be at such a discovery.
The mention, also, of any *future applications* I may make, is equally beyond my comprehension. But I will not dwell upon a subject, which almost compels from me reflections that cannot but be wounding to a heart so formed for filial tenderness as my Evelina’s. There is an air of mystery throughout the letter, the explanation of which I will await in silence.
The scheme of Madame Duval is such as might be reasonably expected from a woman so little inured to disappointment, and so totally incapable of considering the delicacy of your situation. Your averseness to her plan gives me pleasure, for it exactly corresponds with my own. Why will she not make the journey she projects by herself? She would not have even the wish of an opposition to encounter. And then, once more, might my child and myself be left to the quiet enjoyment of that peaceful happiness, which she alone has interrupted. As to her coming hither, I could, indeed, dispense with such a visit; but, if she will not be satisfied with my refusal by letter, I must submit to the task of giving it her in person.
My impatience for your return is increased by your account of Sir Clement Willoughby’s visit to Howard Grove.

第三十七封 维拉尔斯先生致伊芙琳娜
5月21日，于贝里山。
我的伊芙琳娜，不要为这一场与她毫不相干的命运的打击而消沉。你未曾在职分上有丝毫亏欠，招来这番苛待；你也没有因任何一步失检，惹来非难或责备。所以，我最亲爱的孩子，我恳求你拿出勇气来支撑自己——你的清白本该给你这份勇气；你容许自己的哀伤，只应落在他一个人身上：他没有这份支撑，将来总有一天，会痛彻地知道自己多么需要它。
信里暗指我本人的那一句，我全然莫名其妙：我的心，我敢说，完全洗清我于邪恶无罪；可*毫无瑕玷*这四个字，我从不曾僭敢自许。不过，看来他存心日后把话说明白；到那时——倘若果真显出什么，在*我*这一方面也促成了我们为之痛惜的那些不幸，那么我至少可以说：这发现之惊人，连最偏袒我的朋友也及不上我自己的一半。
至于说到我日后还有什么*请托*，也同样超出我的理解。但我不愿在这题目上盘桓，它几乎逼着我生出种种感想，而这些感想，不能不伤着一颗像我伊芙琳娜这般天性纯孝的心。这封信从头到尾裹着一层迷雾，它的谜底，我愿静候。
杜瓦尔夫人这计划，出自这样一位不惯受挫、又全然体察不到你处境之微妙的女人，原在情理之中。你对她这打算的厌恶使我欣慰，因为它正合我的心意。她何不独自去做她计划的这趟旅行呢？那样她连一星半点的违逆也碰不着。而那时，我的孩子和我，便又能重新回到那份安恬的享受里——唯有她一个人搅乱了它。至于她说要到这里来，这样的造访，我实在可以省了；可她若不肯凭一封信就满足于我的拒绝，那我只好勉力当面回她一次了。
你叙述克莱门特·威洛比爵士到霍华德庄园来访，我对你的归期越发望眼欲穿了。

===Original===

I am but little surprised at the perseverance of his assiduities to interest you in his favour; but I am very much hurt that you should be exposed to addresses, which, by their privacy, have an air that shocks me. You cannot, my love, be too circumspect; the slightest carelessness on your part will be taken advantage of by a man of his disposition. It is not sufficient for you to be reserved: his conduct even calls for your resentment; and should he again, as will doubtless be his endeavour, contrive to solicit your favour in private, let your disdain and displeasure be so marked, as to constrain a change in his behaviour. Though, indeed, should his visit be repeated while you remain at the Grove, Lady Howard must pardon me if I shorten yours.
Adieu, my child. You will always make my respects to the hospitable family to which we are so much obliged.

他如此锲而不舍地殷勤，一心要你对他生出好感，我不很惊讶；叫我痛心的，是你竟被置于那样一番表白之下：那番表白的偷偷摸摸，透着一股叫我震恐的气味。我的爱，你怎样谨慎都不为过：你这头稍有疏忽，都会被他那等性情的人拿去利用。单是矜持，于你已不够：他的行径简直配得上你的愠怒；倘若他再——无疑他必会设法——私下里乞求你的好感，就让你的轻蔑与不快显豁到那个地步，逼得他改一改行止。不过，倘若你还在庄园期间他再度来访，霍华德夫人须得恕我缩短你的行期了。
再会，我的孩子。请始终代我向那好客的一家人致敬意——我们受他们的恩实在太多。
