## Endnotes / 尾注
===Original===

1. However superior the capacities in which these great writers deserve to be considered, they must pardon me that, for the dignity of my subject, I here rank the authors of *Rasselas* and *Eloise* as Novelists. ↩︎

===Chinese===

1. 无论这些伟大作家理应被置于何等更高的地位，还请他们海涵：为了我主题的体面，我在此处姑且将《拉塞拉斯》与《爱洛漪丝》的作者们列为小说家。
