## Jimmy Goggles the God / 成为神的吉米·护目镜

===Original===
“It isn’t everyone who’s been a god,” said the sunburnt man. “But it’s happened to me—among other things.”
I intimated my sense of his condescension.
“It don’t leave much for ambition, does it?” said the sunburnt man.

===Chinese===
“并不是人人都有当神的经历，”那个晒得黝黑的人说。“可这事就落到了我头上——除了别的种种之外。”
我表示领会了他这番屈尊降贵的意思。
“这事儿把人的野心都消磨光了，不是吗？”晒黑的人说。

===Original===
“I was one of those men who were saved from the *Ocean Pioneer*. Gummy! how time flies! It’s twenty years ago. I doubt if you’ll remember anything of the *Ocean Pioneer*?”
The name was familiar, and I tried to recall when and where I had read it. The *Ocean Pioneer*? “Something about gold dust,” I said vaguely, “but the precise—”
“That’s it,” he said. “In a beastly little channel she hadn’t no business in—dodging pirates. It was before they’d put the kybosh on that business. And there’d been volcanoes or something and all the rocks was wrong. There’s places about by Soona where you fair have to follow the rocks about to see where they’re going next. Down she went in twenty fathoms before you could have dealt for whist, with fifty thousand pounds worth of gold aboard, it was said, in one form or another.”

===Chinese===
“我是从‘海洋先驱号’（*Ocean Pioneer*）上被救下来的人之一。乖乖！时间过得真快！都是二十年前的事了。我拿不准你还记不记得‘海洋先驱号’？”
这名字听着耳熟，我试着回想自己是在何时何地读到过。“海洋先驱号”？“好像跟金粉有点关系，”我含含糊糊地说，“但确切的内容——”
“就是它，”他说。“在一条她本不该去的鬼狭窄的水道里——为了躲海盗。那时候他们还没取缔那行营生。那一带又有火山什么的，连礁石都长得不对劲。索纳（Soona）附近有些地方，你得追着礁石跑才能看清它们下一刻要往哪儿去。她沉下去二十英寻深，你连一局惠斯特牌都还没发完呢——据说船上载着价值五万英镑的黄金，这样那样地折算着。”

===Original===
“Survivors?”
“Three.”
“I remember the case now,” I said. “There was something about salvage—”

===Chinese===
“幸存者？”
“三个。”
“我现在想起这桩案子了，”我说，“好像跟打捞有点——”

===Original===
But at the word salvage the sunburnt man exploded into language so extraordinarily horrible that I stopped aghast. He came down to more ordinary swearing, and pulled himself up abruptly. “Excuse me,” he said, “but—salvage!”
He leant over towards me. “I was in that job,” he said. “Tried to make myself a rich man, and got made a god instead. I’ve got my feelings—”
“It ain’t all jam being a god,” said the sunburnt man, and for some time conversed by means of such pithy but unprogressive axioms. At last he took up his tale again.

===Chinese===
可一听到“打捞”这个词，那晒黑的人便爆出一串不堪入耳的脏话，吓得我瞠目结舌。他渐渐换成平常的咒骂，又猛地刹住。“恕我失礼，”他说，“可——打捞！”
他朝我探过身来。“我参与了那档子事，”他说。“本想让自己变成个富翁，结果倒被造成了神。我心里是有感触的——”
“当神也不全是甜头，”晒黑的人说，接着用诸如此类精辟却没什么进展的格言谈论了好一会儿。末了他才又接上话头。

===Original===
“There was me,” said the sunburnt man, “and a seaman named Jacobs, and Always, the mate of the *Ocean Pioneer*. And him it was that set the whole thing going. I remember him now, when we was in the jolly-boat, suggesting it all to our minds just by one sentence. He was a wonderful hand at suggesting things. ‘There was forty thousand pounds,’ he said, ‘on that ship, and it’s for me to say just where she went down.’ It didn’t need much brains to tumble to that. And he was the leader from the first to the last. He got hold of the Sanderses and their brig; they were brothers, and the brig was the *Pride of Banya*, and he it was bought the diving dress—a secondhand one with a compressed air apparatus instead of pumping. He’d have done the diving too, if it hadn’t made him sick going down. And the salvage people were mucking about with a chart he’d cooked up, as solemn as could be, at Starr Race, a hundred and twenty miles away.

===Chinese===
“当时有我，”晒黑的人说，“还有一个叫雅各布斯（Jacobs）的水手，以及‘海洋先驱号’的大副奥尔韦斯（Always）。正是他张罗起了整件事。我现在还记得，我们坐在小艇上的时候，他就用一句话把整个主意灌进了我们脑子里。他可真是个出主意的好手。‘那条船上有四万英镑，’他说，‘而她沉在哪儿，只有我说了算。’这用不着多聪明的脑瓜也能想明白。他从头到尾都是我们的头儿。他搭上了桑德斯兄弟（the Sanderses）和他们的双桅帆船；他们是两兄弟，那艘帆船叫‘班尼亚的骄傲号’（*Pride of Banya*），正是他去买的潜水服——一具二手货，用的是压缩空气装置，不用人在上面泵气。本来该他下去的，要不是他一下水就犯恶心的话。而那些正经打捞的人呢，正一百二十英里外的斯塔尔雷斯（Starr Race），围着一张他精心伪造的海图煞有介事地瞎忙活。

===Original===
“I can tell you we was a happy lot aboard that brig, jokes and drink and bright hopes all the time. It all seemed so neat and clean and straightforward, and what rough chaps call a ‘cert.’ And we used to speculate how the other blessed lot, the proper salvagers, who’d started two days before us, were getting on, until our sides fairly ached. We all messed together in the Sanderses’ cabin—it was a curious crew, all officers and no men—and there stood the diving-dress waiting its turn. Young Sanders was a humorous sort of chap, and there certainly was something funny in the confounded thing’s great fat head and its stare, and he made us see it too. ‘Jimmy Goggles,’ he used to call it, and talk to it like a Christian. Asked if he was married, and how Mrs. Goggles was, and all the little Goggleses. Fit to make you split. And every blessed day all of us used to drink the health of Jimmy Goggles in rum, and unscrew his eye and pour a glass of rum in him, until, instead of that nasty mackintosheriness, he smelt as nice in his inside as a cask of rum. It was jolly times we had in those days, I can tell you—little suspecting, poor chaps! what was a-coming.

===Chinese===
“我可以告诉你，在那艘双桅帆船上我们可真是一伙快活人，成天说说笑笑、喝酒、怀着美妙的指望。一切看起来那么利落、干净、直截了当，是那些粗人称作的‘稳当买卖’。我们常琢磨那一伙正经打捞的——比我们早出发两天的人——不知进展如何，直想得笑岔了气。我们都在桑德斯兄弟的船舱里一块儿吃饭——船员组成颇为奇特，全是头头脑脑，没有一个兵——那套潜水服就立在那儿等着轮到它上场。小桑德斯（Young Sanders）是个爱逗乐的小伙子，那玩意儿硕大的肥脑袋和直瞪瞪的眼神确实有几分滑稽，他也能让我们都看出这点来。他管它叫‘吉米·护目镜’（Jimmy Goggles），还像对待正经人似的跟它说话。问它结了婚没有，戈格尔太太可好，还有那群小戈格尔们怎么样了。逗得人肚子都要笑破。我们每天都要用朗姆酒为吉米·护目镜的健康干杯，拧开它的眼睛往里头灌一杯朗姆酒，直到它里头不再有那股难闻的胶皮味，而是像一只朗姆酒桶那样闻着香喷喷的。那些日子可真快活，我告诉你——可怜的家伙们哪曾料到，等着我们的是什么。

===Original===
“We weren’t going to throw away our chances by any blessed hurry, you know, and we spent a whole day sounding our way towards where the *Ocean Pioneer* had gone down, right between two chunks of ropy grey rock—lava rocks that rose nearly out of the water. We had to lay off about half a mile to get a safe anchorage, and there was a thundering row who should stay on board. And there she lay just as she had gone down, so that you could see the top of the masts that was still standing perfectly distinctly. The row ended in all coming in the boat. I went down in the diving-dress on Friday morning directly it was light.

===Chinese===
“你知道，我们不会因为急着赶工就白白丢掉机会，我们花了整整一天边测水深边朝‘海洋先驱号’沉没的地方摸过去——正好夹在两大块灰色的绳状熔岩之间，那些熔岩几乎露出水面。我们不得不在半英里外下锚以求稳妥，接着大吵了一架，争谁该留在船上。她就那样躺在那儿，和沉下去时一模一样，你甚至能清清楚楚地看见还立着的那些桅杆的顶端。争吵的结果是谁都进了小艇。星期五一清早天刚亮，我就穿着潜水服下去了。

===Original===
“What a surprise it was! I can see it all now quite distinctly. It was a queer-looking place, and the light was just coming. People over here think every blessed place in the tropics is a flat shore and palm-trees and surf, bless ’em! This place, for instance, wasn’t a bit that way. Not common rocks they were, undermined by waves; but great curved banks like ironwork cinder heaps, with green slime below, and thorny shrubs and things just waving upon them here and there, and the water glassy calm and clear, and showing you a kind of dirty gray-black shine, with huge flaring red-brown weeds spreading motionless, and crawling and darting things going through it. And far away beyond the ditches and pools and the heaps was a forest on the mountain flank, growing again after the fires and cinder showers of the last eruption. And the other way forest, too, and a kind of broken—what is it?—amby-theatre of black and rusty cinders rising out of it all, and the sea in a kind of bay in the middle.

===Chinese===
“那景象真叫人吃惊！我现在仍能看得一清二楚。那是个样子古怪的地方，天光才刚亮起来。咱们这边的人以为热带的每一个地方都是平坦的海岸、椰子树和拍岸的浪花，他们可真是好心肠！就说这地方吧，压根儿不是那么回事。那不是被海浪掏空的普通礁石，而是巨大的弧形堆岸，像铁厂的炉渣堆，底下覆着绿色的黏泥，上面到处有带刺的灌木之类的东西在摇摆，海水平静清澈如玻璃，映出一种脏兮兮的灰黑色光泽，大片大片红褐色的海草纹丝不动地铺展开，还有些爬的、窜的东西在其中穿梭。远远地越过那些沟渠、水洼和石堆，山坡上有一片森林，在上一次喷发的烈火和火山灰雨之后重新长了起来。另一边也是森林，还有一座残破的——怎么说来着？——像露天剧场似的黑色锈迹斑斑的火山灰堆，从这一切之间拔起，大海则嵌在中间的一个海湾里。

===Original===
“The dawn, I say, was just coming, and there wasn’t much colour about things, and not a human being but ourselves anywhere in sight up or down the channel. Except the *Pride of Banya*, lying out beyond a lump of rocks towards the line of the sea.
“Not a human being in sight,” he repeated, and paused.

===Chinese===
“我说，天刚刚破晓，万物都没什么颜色，沿着水道上下游放眼望去，除了我们自己，连一个人影也看不见。只有‘班尼亚的骄傲号’远远地泊在一堆礁石外面，朝着海天一线的方向。
“一个人影也看不见，”他重复了一遍，停顿了一下。

===Original===
“I don’t know where they came from, not a bit. And we were feeling so safe that we were all alone that poor young Sanders was a-singing. I was in Jimmy Goggles, all except the helmet. ‘Easy,’ says Always, ‘there’s her mast.’ And after I’d had just one squint over the gunwale, I caught up the bogey, and almost tipped out as old Sanders brought the boat round. When the windows were screwed and everything was all right, I shut the valve from the air-belt in order to help my sinking, and jumped overboard, feet foremost—for we hadn’t a ladder. I left the boat pitching, and all of them staring down into water after me, as my head sank down into the weeds and blackness that lay about the mast. I suppose nobody, not the most cautious chap in the world, would have bothered about a lookout at such a desolate place. It stunk of solitude.

===Chinese===
“他们是从哪儿冒出来的，我一点都不知道。我们正觉得万无一失，以为周围就只有我们自己，可怜的小桑德斯还唱起了歌。我已经穿好了吉米·护目镜，就差头盔了。‘稳着点，’奥尔韦斯说，‘那就是她的桅杆。’我朝船舷外只瞥了一眼，便一把抄起那个家伙，老桑德斯（old Sanders）把小艇转过来时我差点儿栽出去。等窗口拧紧、一切就绪，我关上空气带上的阀门好帮助自己下沉，然后跳进了海里——脚先下水，因为我们没有梯子。我留下那艘小艇在颠簸，所有人都目不转睛地朝水里盯着我，而我的头则沉入了桅杆周围的海草和黑暗之中。我估摸着，哪怕是天下最谨慎的人，在这么荒凉的地方也不会费心去安排个望风的。那地方荒凉得直透出一股孤独味儿。

===Original===
“Of course you must understand that I was a greenhorn at diving. None of us were divers. We’d had to muck about with the thing to get the way of it, and this was the first time I’d been deep. It feels damnable. Your ears hurt beastly. I don’t know if you’ve ever hurt yourself yawning or sneezing, but it takes you like that, only ten times worse. And a pain over the eyebrows here—splitting—and a feeling like influenza in the head. And it isn’t all heaven in your lungs and things. And going down feels like the beginning of a lift, only it keeps on. And you can’t turn your head to see what’s above you, and you can’t get a fair squint at what’s happening to your feet without bending down something painful. And being deep it was dark, let alone the blackness of the ashes and mud that formed the bottom. It was like going down out of the dawn back into the night, so to speak.

===Chinese===
“当然你得明白，我在潜水这行当上还是个生手。我们一伙人都不是潜水员。我们只能瞎摆弄那玩意儿摸索出门道，而这还是我头一次下深水。那滋味真是糟透了。耳朵疼得要命。我不知道你有没有过打哈欠或打喷嚏时伤到自己，那感觉就类似那样，只不过厉害十倍。眉毛这儿还疼——劈裂似的——脑袋里像得了流感。肺和五脏六腑里也绝不是什么好受的滋味。下沉的感觉就像电梯刚开始下降那一刹那，只不过它一直不停。你没法回头去看头顶上是什么，想正眼瞧瞧脚下发生了什么，就得弯下腰去，疼得厉害。水又深，自然是暗的，更别说海底全是黑色的火山灰和淤泥了。可以说，就像从黎明一路降回到黑夜里去。

===Original===
“The mast came up like a ghost out of the black, and then a lot of fishes, and then a lot of flapping red seaweed, and then whack I came with a kind of dull bang on the deck of the *Ocean Pioneer*, and the fishes that had been feeding on the dead rose about me like a swarm of flies from road stuff in summertime. I turned on the compressed air again—for the suit was a bit thick and mackintoshery after all, in spite of the rum—and stood recovering myself. It struck coolish down there, and that helped take off the stuffiness a bit.”

===Chinese===
“桅杆像幽灵一样从黑暗中升起，接着是一大群鱼，然后是大片飘动的红色海草，再然后，我‘砰’的一声闷响落在了‘海洋先驱号’的甲板上，那些一直在啃食尸体的鱼群在我四周惊起，像夏日里路上的粪堆惊起的一群苍蝇。我又拧开了压缩空气——毕竟这套衣服还是有些厚、有些胶皮味，朗姆酒也压不住——站定喘了口气。下面凉丝丝的，倒也让那股闷气消散了些。”

===Original===
“When I began to feel easier, I started looking about me. It was an extraordinary sight. Even the light was extraordinary, a kind of reddy-coloured twilight, on account of the streamers of seaweed that floated up on either side of the ship. And far overhead just a moony, deep green blue. ===Original===
