## XII Pursuit / 追击

===Original===
For an instant I stood there thinking of her, and then, with a sigh, I tucked the book in the thong that supported my loin cloth, and turned to leave the apartment. At the bottom of the corridor which leads aloft from the lower chambers I whistled in accordance with the prearranged signal which was to announce to Perry and Ghak that I had been successful. A moment later they stood beside me, and to my surprise I saw that Hooja the Sly One accompanied them.
“He joined us,” explained Perry, “and would not be denied. The fellow is a fox. He scents escape, and rather than be thwarted of our chance now I told him that I would bring him to you, and let you decide whether he might accompany us.”
I had no love for Hooja, and no confidence in him. I was sure that if he thought it would profit him he would betray us; but I saw no way out of it now, and the fact that I had killed four Mahars instead of only the three I had expected to, made it possible to include the fellow in our scheme of escape.
“Very well,” I said, “you may come with us, Hooja; but at the first intimation of treachery I shall run my sword through you. Do you understand?”
He said that he did.

===Chinese===
我在那里站了片刻，想着她，然后叹了口气，把那本书塞进系着兜裆布的皮条里，转身要离开房间。到了从底层房间向上通往上面的走廊尽头，我按预先约定的信号吹了声口哨——那是通知佩里（Perry）和格哈克（Ghak）我已得手。片刻后他们站到了我身边，而叫我吃惊的是，我看见「狡猾鬼」胡贾（Hooja the Sly One）也同他们在一起。
「他硬要加入，」佩里解释道，「怎么也甩不脱。这家伙是只狐狸。他嗅出了逃亡的味道，与其让他现在坏了我们的机会，我告诉他要把他带到你面前，由你来决定他能不能同行。」
我不喜欢胡贾，也不信任他。我敢肯定，只要他觉得有利可图，他就会出卖我们；可眼下我看不到脱身之计，何况我杀掉的玛哈尔人（Mahar）是四只而非预想的三只，这就让把这家伙纳入我们的逃跑方案成了可能。
「很好，」我说，「你可以跟我们来，胡贾；但只要有半点背信的苗头，我就一剑捅穿你。明白吗？」
他说他明白。

===Original===
Some time later we had removed the skins from the four Mahars, and so succeeded in crawling inside of them ourselves that there seemed an excellent chance for us to pass unnoticed from Phutra. It was not an easy thing to fasten the hides together where we had split them along the belly to remove them from their carcasses, but by remaining out until the others had all been sewed in with my help, and then leaving an aperture in the breast of Perry’s skin through which he could pass his hands to sew me up, we were enabled to accomplish our design to really much better purpose than I had hoped. We managed to keep the heads erect by passing our swords up through the necks, and by the same means were enabled to move them about in a lifelike manner. We had our greatest difficulty with the webbed feet, but even that problem was finally solved, so that when we moved about we did so quite naturally. Tiny holes punctured in the baggy throats into which our heads were thrust permitted us to see well enough to guide our progress.
Thus we started up toward the main floor of the building. Ghak headed the strange procession, then came Perry, followed by Hooja, while I brought up the rear, after admonishing Hooja that I had so arranged my sword that I could thrust it through the head of my disguise into his vitals were he to show any indication of faltering.

===Chinese===
过了一些时候，我们把四张玛哈尔皮剥了下来，而且成功地钻了进去，从富特拉（Phutra）混出去不被察觉，看来颇有胜算。皮子是顺着肚皮剖开从尸体上剥下的，要把它们重新缀合起来可不容易；但靠着先由我帮着把其余三人一一缝进去，再在佩里那张皮的胸口留一个口子、好让他把手伸出来缝我，我们竟把这桩事办得远比我原先指望的妥帖。我们把手中的剑从脖颈里穿上去，让兽头保持直立，还靠这法子让脑袋活灵活现地转动。带蹼的脚最叫我们为难，可这个问题最后也解决了，于是我们走动起来相当自然。松垮的喉袋上扎出的小孔——我们的头就伸在里面——让我们看得见路，足以引路前行。
我们就这样朝建筑的主层进发。格哈克领头，这一队古怪的行列，接着是佩里，随后胡贾，我殿后；我警告过胡贾，我的剑已安排停当，只消从他那张假头的顶上捅过去，就能扎进他的要害，只要他露出半点动摇的迹象。

===Original===
As the noise of hurrying feet warned me that we were entering the busy corridors of the main level, my heart came up into my mouth. It is with no sense of shame that I admit that I was frightened﻿—never before in my life, nor since, did I experience any such agony of soulsearing fear and suspense as enveloped me. If it be possible to sweat blood, I sweat it then.
Slowly, after the manner of locomotion habitual to the Mahars, when they are not using their wings, we crept through throngs of busy slaves, Sagoths, and Mahars. After what seemed an eternity we reached the outer door which leads into the main avenue of Phutra. Many Sagoths loitered near the opening. They glanced at Ghak as he padded between them. Then Perry passed, and then Hooja. Now it was my turn, and then in a sudden fit of freezing terror I realized that the warm blood from my wounded arm was trickling down through the dead foot of the Mahar skin I wore and leaving its telltale mark upon the pavement, for I saw a Sagoth call a companion’s attention to it.
The guard stepped before me and pointing to my bleeding foot spoke to me in the sign language which these two races employ as a means of communication. Even had I known what he was saying I could not have replied with the dead thing that covered me. I once had seen a great Mahar freeze a presumptuous Sagoth with a look. It seemed my only hope, and so I tried it. Stopping in my tracks I moved my sword so that it made the dead head appear to turn inquiring eyes upon the gorilla-man. For a long moment I stood perfectly still, eyeing the fellow with those dead eyes. Then I lowered the head and started slowly on. For a moment all hung in the balance, but before I touched him the guard stepped to one side, and I passed on out into the avenue.

===Chinese===
急促的脚步声提醒我，我们正进入主层繁忙的走廊，我的心提到了嘴边。我毫不羞愧地承认，我吓坏了——此前此后，我一生中再没有经历过那样煎熬灵魂的恐惧与悬心，像裹住我全身的那一种。倘若人真能汗血，我那时便流了。
我们以玛哈尔人不用翅膀时惯有的步态，慢慢爬行，穿过一群群忙碌的奴隶、萨戈思人（Sagoth）和玛哈尔人。熬过了仿佛永恒的一段时间，我们到了通往富特拉主大道的外门。许多萨戈思人在门口游荡。他们瞥了瞥从他们中间爬过的格哈克。然后佩里过去了，然后是胡贾。现在轮到我了，就在这时，一阵冻彻的恐怖袭来：我受伤手臂的热血，正顺着我所穿玛哈尔皮那只死脚往下滴，在石板路上留下暴露行迹的印记，因为我看见一个萨戈思人唤过同伴来指给他看。
卫兵跨到我面前，指着我流血的脚，用这两个种族用来交流的手语对我说话。就算我懂他在说什么，裹着我的这具死物也没法回话。我曾见过一只大玛哈尔用一个眼色冻住一个放肆的萨戈思人。这似乎是我唯一的指望，我便试了试。我停下脚步，移动剑，让那颗死头仿佛转动起询问的眼睛，望住那猿人。我纹丝不动地站了好一阵，用那双死眼盯住那家伙。随后我垂下头，慢慢继续前行。有一刻，一切都悬于一线，可还没等我碰到他，卫兵便侧身让开，我也走了出去，到了大道上。

===Original===
On we went up the broad street, but now we were safe for the very numbers of our enemies that surrounded us on all sides. Fortunately, there was a great concourse of Mahars repairing to the shallow lake which lies a mile or more from the city. They go there to indulge their amphibian proclivities in diving for small fish, and enjoying the cool depths of the water. It is a freshwater lake, shallow, and free from the larger reptiles which make the use of the great seas of Pellucidar impossible for any but their own kind.
In the thick of the crowd we passed up the steps and out onto the plain. For some distance Ghak remained with the stream that was traveling toward the lake, but finally, at the bottom of a little gully he halted, and there we remained until all had passed and we were alone. Then, still in our disguises, we set off directly away from Phutra.
The heat of the vertical rays of the sun was fast making our horrible prisons unbearable, so that after passing a low divide, and entering a sheltering forest, we finally discarded the Mahar skins that had brought us thus far in safety.

===Chinese===
我们继续沿宽阔的街道行进，不过此刻我们反倒安全，因为四周全是我们敌人的大伙儿。凑巧，有一大群玛哈尔人正赶往离城一英里开外的浅水湖。它们去那儿撒开两栖的习性，潜下水去捉小鱼，享受水的清凉深处。那是一座淡水湖，水浅，又没有大型的爬虫——正是那些大爬虫，使得佩卢西达尔（Pellucidar）的大海除了它们本族，谁也无法利用。
我们混在人群当中上了台阶，出到平原上。有一段路，格哈克随着流向湖去的人流走，但最后，在一道小冲沟的沟底，他停了下来，我们就留在那里，直到人流全部过尽，四下只剩我们。于是我们仍披着伪装，径直朝离开富特拉的方向进发。
垂直照射的阳光的热力，很快让我们那些可怕的牢笼变得无法忍受，于是在翻过一道低矮的分水岭、进入一片蔽日的森林之后，我们终于脱下了这一路护我们平安的玛哈尔皮。

===Original===
I shall not weary you with the details of that bitter and galling flight. How we traveled at a dogged run until we dropped in our tracks. How we were beset by strange and terrible beasts. How we barely escaped the cruel fangs of lions and tigers the size of which would dwarf into pitiful insignificance the greatest felines of the outer world.
On and on we raced, our one thought to put as much distance between ourselves and Phutra as possible. Ghak was leading us to his own land﻿—the land of Sari. No sign of pursuit had developed, and yet we were sure that somewhere behind us relentless Sagoths were dogging our tracks. Ghak said they never failed to hunt down their quarry until they had captured it or themselves been turned back by a superior force.
Our only hope, he said, lay in reaching his tribe which was quite strong enough in their mountain fastness to beat off any number of Sagoths.

===Chinese===
那场艰苦磨人的逃亡，细节我就不拿来烦你了。我们怎样咬着牙一路奔到倒下方休。我们怎样被种种古怪可怕的野兽围袭。我们怎样险些没能逃脱狮虎的利牙——它们的块头，足以让外层世界最巨大的猫科动物相形见绌，小得可怜。
我们不停地飞奔，唯一的念头，就是同富特拉拉开尽可能远的距离。格哈克正把我们引向他的家园——萨里（Sari）之地。追兵一直没有露头，可我们确信，身后的什么地方，穷凶极恶的萨戈思人正循迹紧咬。格哈克说，他们不追到猎物决不罢手，除非自己被更强的武力逼退。
他说，我们唯一的指望，是赶到他的部族那里——他们据守山中险塞，人手足以击退任何数目的萨戈思人。

===Original===
At last, after what seemed months, and may, I now realize, have been years, we came in sight of the dun escarpment which buttressed the foothills of Sari. At almost the same instant, Hooja, who looked ever quite as much behind as before, announced that he could see a body of men far behind us topping a low ridge in our wake. It was the long-expected pursuit.
I asked Ghak if we could make Sari in time to escape them.
“We may,” he replied; “but you will find that the Sagoths can move with incredible swiftness, and as they are almost tireless they are doubtless much fresher than we. Then﻿—” he paused, glancing at Perry.

===Chinese===
终于，在仿佛过了数月之后——如今我明白，说不定已过了数年——我们望见了衬在萨里山麓后面那道土褐色的高崖。几乎同一时刻，那个总往后看得同往前看一样勤的胡贾宣布，他望见远远的身后，有一队人正翻越我们身后的一道低岭。这便是早就在等的追兵。
我问格哈克，我们能不能及时赶到萨里，甩掉他们。
「也许成，」他答道，「可你们会发现，萨戈思人行动快得惊人，而且他们几乎不知疲倦，肯定比我们精神得多。再说——」他顿了顿，瞥了佩里一眼。

===Original===
I knew what he meant. The old man was exhausted. For much of the period of our flight either Ghak or I had half supported him on the march. With such a handicap, less fleet pursuers than the Sagoths might easily overtake us before we could scale the rugged heights which confronted us.
“You and Hooja go on ahead,” I said. “Perry and I will make it if we are able. We cannot travel as rapidly as you two, and there is no reason why all should be lost because of that. It can’t be helped﻿—we have simply to face it.”
“I will not desert a companion,” was Ghak’s simple reply. I hadn’t known that this great, hairy, primeval man had any such nobility of character stowed away inside him. I had always liked him, but now to my liking was added honor and respect. Yes, and love.

===Chinese===
我明白他的意思。老人已经精疲力竭。逃亡的大半时间里，不是格哈克就是我在行军中半搀半架着他。带着这样的累赘，不等我们攀上眼前崎岖的高地，追兵纵然不如萨戈思人迅捷，也能轻易追上我们。
「你和胡贾先走，」我说，「佩里和我能撑就撑到。我们赶路赶不过你们俩，也没有理由让所有人一起赔上。没法子——我们只有面对它。」
「我绝不抛弃同伴。」格哈克的回答就这么简单。我竟不知道，这个高大、多毛的原始人体内，还藏着这样的高贵品格。我一向喜欢他，可现在，喜欢之外又添了敬重。是的，还有爱。

===Original===
But still I urged him to go on ahead, insisting that if he could reach his people he might be able to bring out a sufficient force to drive off the Sagoths and rescue Perry and myself.
No, he wouldn’t leave us, and that was all there was to it, but he suggested that Hooja might hurry on and warn the Sarians of the king’s danger. It didn’t require much urging to start Hooja﻿—the naked idea was enough to send him leaping on ahead of us into the foothills which we now had reached.
Perry realized that he was jeopardizing Ghak’s life and mine and the old fellow fairly begged us to go on without him, although I knew that he was suffering a perfect anguish of terror at the thought of falling into the hands of the Sagoths. Ghak finally solved the problem, in part, by lifting Perry in his powerful arms and carrying him. While the act cut down Ghak’s speed he still could travel faster thus than when half supporting the stumbling old man.

===Chinese===
但我仍劝他先行，坚持说，他若能赶到族人那里，也许能带出一支足够的人马，驱散萨戈思人，救出佩里和我。
不，他不肯离开我们，事情就这么定了；不过他提议，胡贾可以快步先行，去警告萨里人（Sarian），他们的国王身陷险境。打发胡贾上路用不着多劝——光是「国王有难」这个光秃秃的念头，就足以让他蹿到我们前头，跃进我们此刻已到达的山麓丘陵里去了。
佩里明白，他正拿格哈克和我的性命冒险，老人恳求我们丢下他先走；虽然我知道，一想到落入萨戈思人之手，他心里正忍受着十足的恐怖煎熬。最后，格哈克总算解决了问题的一半：他用那双有力的臂膀抱起佩里，扛着走。这一来固然拖慢了格哈克的速度，可比半搀半架着跌跌撞撞的老人，他这样走得还是快些。
