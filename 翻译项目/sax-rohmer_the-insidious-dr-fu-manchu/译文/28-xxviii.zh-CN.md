## XXVIII / XXVIII

===Original===
Of all that we had hoped for in our pursuit of Fu-Manchu how little had we accomplished. Excepting Karamanèh and her brother (who were victims and not creatures of the Chinese doctor’s) not one of the formidable group had fallen alive into our hands. Dreadful crimes had marked Fu-Manchu’s passage through the land. Not one-half of the truth (and nothing of the later developments) had been made public. Nayland Smith’s authority was sufficient to control the press.

In the absence of such a veto a veritable panic must have seized upon the entire country; for a monster﻿—a thing more than humanly evil﻿—existed in our midst.

===Chinese===
追缉傅满洲的行动，我们曾寄予多少期望，而所成者何其寥寥。除了卡拉曼娜和她弟弟（他们是中国医生的受害者，而非其党羽），那个可怖团伙中没有一人活着落入我们手中。骇人的罪行，标刻着傅满洲在这片国土上留下的一道道行迹。公开于众的连真相的一半都不到（后来的种种进展更是一字未提）——奈兰·史密斯的权柄，足以辖制报界。

若没有这道封禁，一场真正的恐慌势必攫住整个国家；因为一个怪物——一种罪恶超乎人间的东西——就存在于我们中间。
---

===Original===
Always Fu-Manchu’s secret activities had centered about the great waterway. There was much of poetic justice in his end; for the Thames had claimed him, who so long had used the stream as a highway for the passage to and fro for his secret forces. Gone now were the yellow men who had been the instruments of his evil will; gone was the giant intellect which had controlled the complex murder machine. Karamanèh, whose beauty he had used as a lure, at last was free, and no more with her smile would tempt men to death﻿—that her brother might live.

===Chinese===
傅满洲的秘密活动，向来以那条大河为轴心。他的下场颇有几分天道好还的意味：泰晤士河终于收走了他——这条被他久已用作秘密党羽往来穿梭通衢的水道。那些充当其邪恶意志之工具的黄种人，如今都已烟消云散；那颗统御着这部复杂杀人机器的巨智，也已殒灭。卡拉曼娜——他曾拿她的美色作饵——终于得到了自由，再不会用一个微笑去引诱男人赴死——好让她弟弟活命。
---

===Original===
Many there are, I doubt not, who will regard the Eastern girl with horror. I ask their forgiveness in that I regarded her quite differently. No man having seen her could have condemned her unheard. Many, having looked into her lovely eyes, had they found there what I found, must have forgiven her almost any crime.

That she valued human life but little was no matter for wonder. Her nationality﻿—her history﻿—furnished adequate excuse for an attitude not condonable in a European equally cultured.

===Chinese===
无疑会有许多人，怀着恐惧看待这个东方少女。我请求他们宽恕——因为我看待她的眼光全然不同。任何见过她的男子，都不可能不问情由便定她的罪。许多人只要在她的妙目中看见我所看见的东西，那么无论她犯下什么罪，几乎都会予以宽宥。

她不甚把人命放在心上，这不足为怪。她的血统——她的身世——足以为这一态度开脱；这态度若换在一位受过同等教养的欧洲女子身上，便是无从宽宥的了。
---

===Original===
But indeed let me confess that hers was a nature incomprehensible to me in some respects. The soul of Karamanèh was a closed book to my shortsighted Western eyes. But the body of Karamanèh was exquisite; her beauty of a kind that was a key to the most extravagant rhapsodies of Eastern poets. Her eyes held a challenge wholly Oriental in its appeal; her lips, even in repose, were a taunt. And, herein, East is West and West is East.

===Chinese===
不过说实在的，请容我承认：她的天性在某些方面于我全然不可解。卡拉曼娜的灵魂，在我这双短视的西方眼睛面前，是一本合着的书。可卡拉曼娜的形体却是精美绝伦；她的美，恰是开启东方诗人最恣肆狂想的一把钥匙。她的眸子里含着一种挑战，那撩人的意味全然是东方的；她的唇，即使在静谧之中，也像一句揶揄。而就在这一点上，东方便是西方，西方便是东方。
---

===Original===
Finally, despite her lurid history, despite the scornful self-possession of which I knew her capable, she was an unprotected girl﻿—in years, I believe, a mere child﻿—whom Fate had cast in my way. At her request, we had booked passages for her brother and herself to Egypt. The boat sailed in three days. But Karamanèh’s beautiful eyes were sad; often I detected tears on the black lashes. Shall I endeavor to describe my own tumultuous, conflicting emotions? It would be useless, since I know it to be impossible. For in those dark eyes burned a fire I might not see; those silken lashes veiled a message I dared not read.

===Chinese===
末了，尽管她有一段骇人的过往，尽管我知道她能摆出何等冷傲自持的神情，她终究是个无依无靠的姑娘——论年纪，我相信还不过是个孩子——被命运抛到了我的路途上。应她的请求，我们为她姐弟俩订了赴埃及的舱位。船三天后启航。可卡拉曼娜那双美丽的眼睛里盛着哀愁；我屡屡看到黑色的睫毛上挂着泪珠。要我试着描摹自己纷乱交战的心绪么？那是徒劳的，因为我知道那不可能。在那双幽暗的眼睛里，燃着一团我不该看的火；那丝缎般的睫毛，掩着一封我不敢读的信。
---

===Original===
Nayland Smith was not blind to the facts of the complicated situation. I can truthfully assert that he was the only man of my acquaintance who, having come in contact with Karamanèh, had kept his head.

We endeavored to divert her mind from the recent tragedies by a round of amusements, though with poor Weymouth’s body still at the mercy of unknown waters Smith and I made but a poor show of gayety; and I took a gloomy pride in the admiration which our lovely companion everywhere excited. I learned, in those days, how rare a thing in nature is a really beautiful woman.

===Chinese===
奈兰·史密斯并未无视这一复杂局面中的种种事实。我可以毫不虚言地断言：凡我相识之人当中，与卡拉曼娜有过接触而仍能保持清醒头脑的，唯他一人。

我们设法带她连日游乐，好把她的心思从近来的惨祸上引开；只是可怜的韦茅斯的遗体还在不知名的河水里听凭摆布，史密斯和我实在强颜欢笑得勉强；而我们那位可爱的女伴所到之处无不引人倾倒，对这份倾倒，我心头怀着一种阴郁的得意。那些日子里我才明白，一个真正美丽的女人，在天地间是何等罕见之物。
---

===Original===
One afternoon we found ourselves at an exhibition of water colors in Bond Street. Karamanèh was intensely interested in the subjects of the drawings﻿—which were entirely Egyptian. As usual, she furnished matter for comment amongst the other visitors, as did the boy, Aziz, her brother, anew upon the world from his living grave in the house of Dr. Fu-Manchu.

===Chinese===
一天下午，我们置身于邦德街的一个水彩画展。卡拉曼娜对画作的题材大感兴趣——那些画清一色是埃及风物。和往常一样，她在其他观者中间惹来纷纷议论；她弟弟阿齐兹也一样——这少年刚从傅满洲博士宅中那座活坟墓里脱身出来，重回人间未久。
---

===Original===
Suddenly Aziz clutched at his sister’s arm, whispering rapidly in Arabic. I saw her peachlike color fade; saw her become pale and wild-eyed﻿—the haunted Karamanèh of the old days.

She turned to me.

===Chinese===
忽然，阿齐兹一把抓住姐姐的手臂，急促地用阿拉伯语低语起来。我眼见她那桃晕般的血色褪去；眼见她变得面色苍白、双目圆睁——又成了往日那个如遭鬼魅缠身的卡拉曼娜。

她转向我。
---

===Original===
“Dr. Petrie﻿—he says that Fu-Manchu is here!”

“Where?”

Nayland Smith rapped out the question violently, turning in a flash from the picture which he was examining.

===Chinese===
「佩特里医生——他说傅满洲就在这儿！」

「在哪儿？」

奈兰·史密斯劈头厉声喝问，霎时间便从他正端详着的画前转过身来。
---

===Original===
“In this room!” she whispered glancing furtively, affrightedly about her. “Something tells Aziz when *he* is near﻿—and I, too, feel strangely afraid. Oh, can it be that he is not dead!”

===Chinese===
「就在这个房间里！」她低声说，一边偷偷摸摸、惶惶不安地朝四下张望。「*他*一靠近，阿齐兹便有所感应——连我也莫名地害怕起来。啊，难道他竟没有死！」
---

===Original===
She held my arm tightly. Her brother was searching the room with big, velvet black eyes. I studied the faces of the several visitors; and Smith was staring about him with the old alert look, and tugging nervously at the lobe of his ear. The name of the giant foe of the white race instantaneously had strung him up to a pitch of supreme intensity.

Our united scrutinies discovered no figure which could have been that of the Chinese doctor. Who could mistake that long, gaunt shape, with the high, mummy-like shoulders, and the indescribable gait, which I can only liken to that of an awkward cat?

===Chinese===
她紧紧攥住我的手臂。她弟弟正睁着一双天鹅绒般的大黑眼睛扫视整个展厅。我端详着在场几位观者的面孔；史密斯正带着往日那副警觉神情环视四周，紧张地扯着耳垂。白种人的那个大敌之名，霎时间便把他绷紧到了极点。

我们几道目光一齐细察，却没有发现任何一个身形可能是那位中国医生的。那瘦长的身影、木乃伊般高耸的双肩，还有那难以名状的步态——我只能拿一只笨拙的猫来比拟——谁能认错呢？
---

===Original===
Then, over the heads of a group of people who stood by the doorway, I saw Smith peering at someone﻿—at someone who passed across the outer room. Stepping aside, I, too, obtained a glimpse of this person.

As I saw him, he was a tall, old man, wearing a black Inverness coat and a rather shabby silk hat. He had long white hair and a patriarchal beard, wore smoked glasses and walked slowly, leaning upon a stick.

===Chinese===
这时，越过门口一小群人的头顶，我看见史密斯正盯视着某个人——一个正穿过外间展厅的人。我侧身一让，也瞥见了此人。

在我眼里，那是个高个子老人，穿一件黑色因弗内斯斗篷式大衣，戴一顶颇显寒酸的大礼帽。他一头长长的白发，蓄着族长般的大胡子，鼻上架着烟色眼镜，拄着手杖，缓步而行。
---

===Original===
Smith’s gaunt face paled. With a rapid glance at Karamanèh, he made off across the room.

Could it be Dr. Fu-Manchu?

===Chinese===
史密斯瘦削的脸刷地白了。他飞快地朝卡拉曼娜瞥了一眼，随即大步穿过展厅而去。

那会否是傅满洲博士？
---

===Original===
Many days had passed since, already half-choked by Inspector Weymouth’s iron grip, Fu-Manchu, before our own eyes, had been swallowed up by the Thames. Even now men were seeking his body, and that of his last victim. Nor had we left any stone unturned. Acting upon information furnished by Karamanèh, the police had searched every known haunt of the murder group. But everything pointed to the fact that the group was disbanded and dispersed; that the lord of strange deaths who had ruled it was no more.

===Chinese===
自那一日至今，已过去许多天了：傅满洲被韦茅斯探长的铁腕扼得半窒息，当着我们自己的眼睛，被泰晤士河吞没。直到此时此刻，仍有人在寻觅他的尸体，连同他最后一个受害者的尸体。我们也未曾放过一块未翻之石。警方根据卡拉曼娜提供的情报，已搜遍那个杀人团伙每一处已知的巢穴。但种种迹象都表明：团伙已然解散四散；那个统御着它的怪异死亡的主宰，已不复存在。
---

===Original===
Yet Smith was not satisfied. Neither, let me confess, was I. Every port was watched; and in suspected districts a kind of house-to-house patrol had been instituted. Unknown to the great public, in those days a secret war waged﻿—a war in which all the available forces of the authorities took the field against one man! But that one man was the evil of the East incarnate.

===Chinese===
然而史密斯并不满足。容我招认，我也不满足。每一个港口都有人盯守；在嫌疑地段，还实行了一种挨家挨户的巡查。广大公众并不知道，那些日子里正在进行着一场秘密战争——一场当局所有可用兵力尽数上阵、对付一个人的战争！可那一个人，乃是东方之恶的化身。
---

===Original===
When we rejoined him, Nayland Smith was talking to the commissionaire at the door. He turned to me.

“That is Professor Jenner Monde,” he said. “The sergeant, here, knows him well.”

===Chinese===
我们回到他身边时，奈兰·史密斯正在门口同门警说话。他转向我。

「那是詹纳·蒙德教授（Professor Jenner Monde）。」他说。「这位中士对他知之甚详。」
---

===Original===
The name of the celebrated Orientalist of course was familiar to me, although I had never before set eyes upon him.

“The Professor was out East the last time I was there, sir,” stated the commissionaire. “I often used to see him. But he’s an eccentric old gentleman. Seems to live in a world of his own. He’s recently back from China, I think.”

===Chinese===
这位著名东方学家的名字，我自然早已耳熟，只是从未亲眼见过其人。

「上回我在东方的时候，教授正在那边，先生。」门警说道。「我从前常常见到他。不过他是位古怪的老先生，仿佛活在自己的天地里。我想，他最近刚从中国回来。」
---

===Original===
Nayland Smith stood clicking his teeth together in irritable hesitation. I heard Karamanèh sigh, and, looking at her, I saw that her cheeks were regaining their natural color.

She smiled in pathetic apology.

“If he was here he is gone,” she said. “I am not afraid now.”

===Chinese===
奈兰·史密斯站在那里，牙齿咬得咯咯作响，焦躁地犹豫不决。我听见卡拉曼娜叹了一声；朝她望去，只见她的双颊正渐渐恢复血色。

她歉然一笑，笑得楚楚可怜。

「他若是在这儿，如今也走了。」她说。「我现在不怕了。」
---

===Original===
Smith thanked the commissionaire for his information and we quitted the gallery.

“Professor Jenner Monde,” muttered my friend, “has lived so long in China as almost to be a Chinaman. I have never met him﻿—never seen him, before; but I wonder﻿—”

“You wonder what, Smith?”

“I wonder if he could possibly be an ally, of the Doctor’s!”

===Chinese===
史密斯谢过门警的指点，我们便离开了画廊。

「詹纳·蒙德教授，」我的朋友喃喃道，「在中国住得太久，几乎都快成中国人了。我从没跟他打过交道——以前从未见过他；可我在想——」

「你在想什么，史密斯？」

「我在想，他会不会是博士的一个同党！」
---

===Original===
I stared at him in amazement.

“If we are to attach any importance to the incident at all,” I said, “we must remember that the boy’s impression﻿—and Karamanèh’s﻿—was that Fu-Manchu was present in person.”

“I *do* attach importance to the incident, Petrie; they are naturally sensitive to such impressions. But I doubt if even the abnormal organization of Aziz could distinguish between the hidden presence of a creature of the Doctor’s and that of the Doctor himself. I shall make a point of calling upon Professor Jenner Monde.”

===Chinese===
我惊愕地瞪着他。

「倘若我们要把这件事当真，」我说，「那就必须记住：那少年的感应——还有卡拉曼娜的——是傅满洲本人就在近旁。」

「我*确实*把这件事当真，佩特里；他们对这类感应天生敏锐。不过，即便是阿齐兹那超常的机体，能否分辨出暗中所藏的是博士的某个爪牙、还是博士本人，我也心存怀疑。我决定专门去拜访一趟詹纳·蒙德教授。」
---

===Original===
But Fate had ordained that much should happen ere Smith made his proposed call upon the Professor.

===Chinese===
然而命运却另有钦定——在史密斯如约登门拜访教授之前，还有许多事要发生。
---

===Original===
Karamanèh and her brother safely lodged in their hotel (which was watched night and day by four men under Smith’s orders), we returned to my quiet suburban rooms.

“First,” said Smith, “let us see what we can find out respecting Professor Monde.”

===Chinese===
把卡拉曼娜和她弟弟安顿进旅馆（那里日夜有四个人奉史密斯之命盯守）之后，我们回到了我那地处郊区、清静的寓所。

「首先，」史密斯说，「咱们来看看，关于蒙德教授能打听到些什么。」
---

===Original===

He lived alone in chambers in New Inn Court, Carey Street. A charwoman did such cleaning as was considered necessary by the Professor, who employed no regular domestic. When he was in London he might be seen fairly frequently at the British Museum, where his shabby figure was familiar to the officials. When he was not in London﻿—that is, during the greater part of each year﻿—no one knew where he went. He never left any address to which letters might be forwarded.

===Chinese===
他走到电话机旁，打给了新苏格兰场。等了不大工夫，所需的消息才弄到手。不过最后我们总算得知：这位教授算得上一位隐士，相识寥寥，朋友更少。

===Chinese===

===Chinese===

“No, Doctor; she was afraid. But I spent last night downstairs in the sitting-room﻿—and *I* looked out!”

===Chinese===
「她朝窗外看了么？」我问。

「没有，医生；她不敢。可昨天夜里，我在楼下起居室里守了一夜——*我*朝外看了！」
