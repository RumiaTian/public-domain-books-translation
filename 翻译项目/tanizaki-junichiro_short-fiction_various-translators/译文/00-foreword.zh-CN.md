## Foreword / 前言

===Original===
This edition of Tanizaki Jun’ichirō’s *Short Fiction* was produced from various translations. “Tattooing” was translated by Ijichi Sumimasa and originally published in 1923. “A Springtime Case” was translated by Iwadō Z. Tamotsu and originally published in 1927.

Robin Whittleton

Billeberga, Sweden, November 2024

===Chinese===
本版谷崎润一郎《短篇集》由各家译本汇编而成。《刺青》（Tattooing）由伊地智巽正（Ijichi Sumimasa）翻译，最初出版于 1923 年；《杀阿艳》（A Springtime Case）由岩堂全智（Iwadō Z. Tamotsu）翻译，最初出版于 1927 年。

罗宾·惠特尔顿（Robin Whittleton）

瑞典，比勒贝加，2024 年 11 月
