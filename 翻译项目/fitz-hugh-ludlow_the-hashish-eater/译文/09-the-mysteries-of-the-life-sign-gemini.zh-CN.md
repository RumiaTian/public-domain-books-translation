## VI The Mysteries of the Life-Sign Gemini / 第六章 双子生命之象的奥秘
===Original===

In this vision the conception of our human duality was presented to me in a manner more striking than ever before. Hitherto it had been more a suggestion than a proof; now it appeared in the light of an intuition. A wonderful field of questions is opened by such an experience, and I am constrained to sketch a few of them as they have occurred to myself.
1st: Are the animal and spiritual conjoint parts of the same life, or two different lives which intensely interact, yet are not altogether dependent upon each other for their continuance?
That the soul is dependent upon aught that we call material for the preservation of its highest functions, very few men will feel disposed to assert. Yet we are all exceedingly loth to concede that the animal has a distinct life of its own, which, for some time after the dissolution of the ties which bind it to the spiritual, might continue to throb on unimpaired. Your critic, who aims altogether at uses comprehended in bread, meat, and broadcloth, may ask, “If it be so, of what practical utility would it be to discover it?” A sufficient answer lies in the fact that men would know one more truth. A truth tested and established may lie for centuries, mildewed and rusted, in the armory of knowledge, until some great soul comes along, draws it out of the rubbish, buckles it on, rushes into the conflict, and with it pries open the portals of one more promised land of blessing for the human race. Gunpowder is a truth; wise men sneer at the monk’s obstreperous plaything. The years float calmly on; that plaything strikes the cliffs of Dover, and as they go toppling down to leave a highway for the nations, contemned truth vindicates her uses with a triumphant voice of thunder.
But there is also a tangible utility in this discovery of an independent animal life (supposing it to be made) which arises out of the fact that we should thus possess much higher notions of the spiritual than we have at present. In the desire to make the body entirely dependent on the soul for all its processes, we have linked the two in so close a marriage that the soul itself has become materialized by contact in our conceptions.

要说灵魂维系其最高机能必须依赖于我们称之为物质的任何事物，恐怕极少有人愿意做出这般断言。然而，我们大家却都极其不情愿承认：肉体本身拥有其独特的独立生命，在断绝了将其与精神相系纽带之后的一段时间里，它仍可能完好无损地持续悸动。那些眼里只有面包、肉食与粗呢等实际功用的批评家或许会问：“即便如此，发现这一点又有何实际用处呢？”一个充分的回答就在于：人类将多认识一桩真理。一桩经过检验并得以确立的真理，可能在知识的军械库中生霉锈蚀数个世纪，直到某位伟大的灵魂降临，将其从瓦砾堆中抽拔而出，披挂在身，冲入阵前，并借此为人类撬开又一片赐福的应许之地的大门。火药便是一桩真理；聪明人曾对修道士那喧闹的玩具报以嗤笑。岁月从容流逝；那玩具轰击着多佛尔（Dover）的悬崖，当悬崖轰然坍塌为列国让出通途时，备受蔑视的真理便用雷霆般胜利的声音为自己的功用辩护。

然而，发现独立的肉体生命（假定该发现成立）亦有其切实的实用价值，因为我们将因此对精神本身获得远比当下高尚得多的观念。出于让肉体在一切机能运作上完全依赖灵魂的愿望，我们把两者捆绑在如此紧密的联姻之中，以至于在我们的观念里，灵魂本身因与之接触而沦为了物质化的存在。

Not to assert the separate of the animal life as proven, let us look at some singular phenomena which, by such an hypothesis, would be explained, and (as it seems to me) by such a one only.
1st: In surgical operations performed while the patient lay under the influence of an anesthetic, as chloroform or ether, I have witnessed contortions of the whole muscular system, and heard outcries so fearful that it was impossible to persuade the lookers-on that the application of the instrument was not causing the severest agony. 除却赋予其活力的那种生命独特质性的特权之外，肉体所应享有的尊崇，绝不比以任何其他形态构筑它的同等重量的碱、水、铁及其他化学物质更多分毫。

若非在生命谱系中特定阶位的赋予活力的元精赋予人类以生机，人类被封入坛罐、注入水壶、吹入气囊、用牛皮纸包裹扎紧时，所享有的器重也理当与活着时完全一样。他的肉体没有哪怕一丝一毫独创性的印记。早先，它曾作为牛肉的肌纤维存在于滋养他父母的食物之中；再往更早追溯，它曾作为牧场上多汁的野草被一头公牛吞下，而那株野草正是通过毛细管引力将它从土壤微管中萃取而出的。冲洗这片泥土，他便会以沉淀物的形式沉积下来；然而，在对他物质谱系进行了这番彻底的追溯之后，我们所得到的结论，也不过与任何一位肯费心在当下状态下将他拆解并施以充分化验测试的熟练化学家所能得出的结论如出一辙。富于幻想心智之人尽可端坐在一颗孤独的卷心菜前，在“你确实可能曾是我的兄弟”的沉思中，为他的思绪——即使不是为了他的味蕾——寻得精神食粮。

如今，在毫无意图证明物质拥有自我意识的情况下，我们难道不能认为存在这样一种可能：进入我们肉体构成的微粒，依然保留着它们曾流经的其他躯体的某些微妙特质（须明白，这并非记忆）；这些特质被我们自身的肉体本性所感知，并由其向属灵生命提示，从而构成了转世观念的根据？

===Original===

The body will then be that part of us which has really transmigrated, while the soul is original.
The idea that the soul has ever transmigrated leads us into painful, disgusting, irrational, and irreligious conclusions. But grant that, in the animal life, a blind perception exists of peculiar qualities in the corporeal particles, arising out of former conditions through which they have passed, and we can then see how it may be possible for the spiritual to sympathize with the animal to such a degree as to etherealize these perceptions into a dreamy echo of its own former being. The problem, therefore, stands thus: Both for the sake of right and reason we must utterly disown the idea of spiritual metempsychosis. How, then, can we explain the fact of its universality among the race? We offer our hypothesis.

===Chinese===

如此一来，肉体便成了我们自身真正经历过轮回流转的那一部分，而灵魂则是独创原始的。

认为灵魂曾经历轮回流转的观念，会将我们引向痛苦、令人作呕、荒谬悖理且背弃宗教的结论。然而，假定在肉体生命中，存在着一种对肉体微粒源自过往所经境况之独特特质的盲目感知，我们便能明了：属灵生命何以能与肉体产生如此程度的共鸣，以至于将这些感知升华为自身前生如梦如幻的回响。因此，问题便摆在眼前：无论为了正道还是理性，我们都必须彻底摒弃灵魂转世的观念。那么，我们又该如何解释该观念在人类中的普遍存在呢？我们谨提出我们的假说。
