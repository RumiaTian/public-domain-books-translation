## XIV Hail! Pythagoras / 第十四章 万岁！毕达哥拉斯
===Original===

The hemisphere of sky which walls us in is something more than a mere product of the laws of sight. It is our shield from unbearable visions. Within our little domain of view, girt by the horizon and arched by the dome of heaven, there is enough of sorrow, enough of danger, yes, enough of beauty and of mirth visible to occupy the soul abundantly in any one single beholding. That lesser and unseen hemisphere which bounds our hearing is also amply large, for within it echo enough of music and lamentation to fill all susceptibility to the utmost. In this world we are but half spirit; we are thus able to hold only the perceptions and emotions of half an orb. Once fully rounded into symmetry ourselves, we shall have strength to bear the pressure of influences from a whole sphere of truth and loveliness.
It is this present half-developed state of ours which makes the infinitude of the hashish awakening so unendurable, even when its sublimity is the sublimity of delight. We have no longer anything to do with horizons, and the boundary which was at once our barrier and our fortress is removed, until we almost perish from the inflow of perceptions.
One most powerful realization of this fact occurred to me when hashish had already become a fascination and a habit. In the broad daylight of a summer afternoon I was walking in the full possession of delirium. For an hour the expansion of all visible things had been growing toward its height; it now reached it, and to the fullest extent I apprehended what is meant by the infinity of space. Vistas no longer converged; sight met no barrier; the world was horizonless, for earth and sky stretched endlessly onward in parallel planes. Above me the heavens were terrible with the glory of a fathomless depth. I looked up, but my eyes, unopposed, every moment penetrated farther and farther into the immensity, and I turned them downward, lest they should presently intrude into the fatal splendors of the Great Presence. Unable to bear visible objects, I shut my eyes. In one moment a colossal music filled the whole hemisphere above me, and I thrilled upward through its environment on visionless wings.

将我们围合在内的苍穹半球，绝非仅仅是视觉法则的产物。它是守护我们免受难以承受之异象侵袭的坚固盾牌。在我们由地平线合围、由天穹拱顶笼罩的狭小视野之内，已然存在着足够多的悲伤、足够多的危险，是的，亦有足够多的美与欢乐在目，足以让灵魂在任何单次凝视中充盈满溢。那限定我们听觉的较小且不可见的半球亦同样辽阔无垠，因为在其中回荡着足够多的仙乐与哀歌，足以将一切感官敏锐度填塞至极致。在此世间，我们不过拥有半副灵性；因此我们仅能承受半个天球的感知与情绪。唯有一旦我们自身的对称性得以完满塑造，我们方有力量承受来自整个由真理与可爱构成的浑圆天球之力量的重压。

正是我们当下这半发育的状态，使得大麻觉醒的无垠浩瀚变得如此令人难以承受，即便其崇高乃是至福的崇高。我们再也与地平线毫无瓜葛，那道曾经既是我们的屏障又是我们要塞的界线已被彻底抹去，直至我们因感知的大量涌入而几乎濒临消亡。

当大麻已然演变成为一种难以抗拒的诱惑与习惯之时，我曾对这一事实产生过一次最为震撼的领悟。在一个夏日午后的灿烂天光之下，我在彻底陷入谵妄的状态中漫步前行。整整一个钟头里，所有可见之物的膨胀一直在朝着巅峰蔓延；此时它终于登峰造极，我最彻底地领悟到了“空间的无垠”究竟所指为何。远景不再收敛于焦点；视线未遇任何阻隔；世界失去了地平线，因为大地与天空在平行的平面上无休止地向前延展。在我上方，诸天因深不可测的幽深荣光而显得令人战栗。我仰望苍穹，但双眼在毫无遮拦之下，每一刻都愈发深邃地洞穿至浩渺苍茫之中，我赶忙将视线转向下方，生怕目光会在顷刻间冒犯侵入“至高临在”那毁灭性的威光之中。因无法承受目之所及的物体，我阖上了双眸。一刹那间，一阵巍峨宏大的仙乐充盈了我上方的整个半球，我在它所构筑的环境中，振动着无形的双翼向着高空战栗飞升。

It was not song, it was not instruments, but the inexpressible spirit of sublime sound﻿—like nothing I had ever heard﻿—impossible to be symbolized; intense, yet not loud; the ideal of harmony, yet distinguishable into a multiplicity of exquisite parts.
I opened my eyes, but it still continued. I sought around me to detect some natural sound which might be exaggerated into such a semblance; but no, it was of unearthly generation, and it thrilled through the universe an inexplicable, a beautiful, yet an awful symphony.
Suddenly my mind grew solemn with the consciousness of a quickened perception. And what a solemnity is that which the hashish-eater feels at such a moment! The very beating of his heart is silenced; he stands with his finger on his lip; his eye is fixed, and he becomes a very statue of awful veneration. The face of such a man, however little glorified in feature or expression during his ordinary states of mind, I have stood and looked upon with the consciousness that I was beholding more of the embodiment of the truly sublime than any created thing could ever offer me.
I looked abroad on fields, and waters, and sky, and read in them a most startling meaning. I wondered how I had ever regarded them in the light of dead matter, at the farthest only *suggesting* lessons. They were now, as in my former vision, grand symbols of the sublimest spiritual truths﻿—truths never before even feebly grasped, and utterly unsuspected.
Like a map, the arcana of the universe lay bare before me. I saw how every created thing not only typifies, but springs forth from some mighty spiritual law as its offspring, its necessary external development﻿—not the mere clothing of the essence, but the essence incarnate.
I am aware that, in this recital, I may seem to be repeating what I have said before of my dreadful night of insight; but between the two visions there was this difference, the view did not stop here. While that music was pouring through the great heavens above me, I became conscious of a numerical order which ran through it, and in marking this order, I beheld it transferred to every movement of the universe. Every sphere wheeled on in its orbit, every emotion of the soul arose and fell, every smallest moss and fungus germinated and grew according to some peculiarity of numbers which severally governed them, and was most admirably typified by them in return.

那并非歌声，亦非乐器之音，而是崇高音律无可名状的纯粹灵性——不同于我所听闻过的任何声响——根本无法用凡尘具象来象征；激昂热烈，却并不喧嚣刺耳；乃是和谐的至高典范，却又能分辨出无数精妙绝伦的细微声部。

我睁开双眼，但那仙乐仍在持续。我环顾周遭，试图探寻是否有某种自然声响可能被夸张渲染成了这般模样；然而绝非如此，它乃是超凡脱俗之造化，化作一首无法索解、瑰丽绝伦却又令人敬畏的交响乐，在整个宇宙间回荡悸动。

突然间，我的心智因感知被陡然唤醒的自觉而变得庄严无比。食大麻者在此类时刻所感受到的，是何等神圣肃穆的庄严啊！连他自己的心跳声都归于沉寂；他伫立着，以指按唇；他的双目凝滞不动，化作了一尊令人肃然起敬的虔敬雕像。这样一个人的面庞，无论在寻常的心智状态下其五官或神态是多么缺乏光彩，当我伫立凝视着他时，心中皆深知自己所目睹的，乃是比世间任何受造物所能展现更为真实的崇高化身。

我纵目远眺田野、水流与苍穹，并在其中读出了一种最令人惊心动魄的深意。我不禁纳罕，自己以往怎会仅将其视作死寂的物质、顶多只能*启发*些许教益呢？如今它们正如我先前的异象中所显现的那样，乃是最崇高的属灵真理之宏伟象征——那些此前甚至从未被微弱领会、且全然未被怀疑过的真理。

宛若一幅地图，宇宙的深奥秘奥毫无保留地展现在我眼前。我看清了每一件受造之物不仅象征着、而且是从某条伟大的属灵法则中作为其子嗣而萌发喷薄而出，成为其必然的外在演进形态——并非仅仅是本质的外衣，而是本质本身的真切化身。

我深知，在这番叙述中，我似乎在重复自己此前关于那个充斥着洞察力的可怖之夜所言之语；但在这两次异象之间却存在着这般差异：眼前的景象并未止步于此。当那阵仙乐在我头顶浩瀚的诸天中奔涌之时，我觉察到有一套数理秩序正贯穿其中；而在留意到这套秩序的瞬间，我目睹它被投射到了宇宙的每一次运转之中。每一个天球在各自的轨道上旋转运转，灵魂的每一次情绪起伏翻腾，每一株最微小的苔藓与真菌皆依据支配它们各自的某种特殊数律而萌发滋长，并反过来将其最为精妙地象征具象。

An exquisite harmony of proportion reigned through space, and I seemed to realize that the music which I heard was but this numerical harmony making itself objective through the development of a grand harmony of tones.
The vividness with which this conception revealed itself to me made it a thing terrible to bear alone. An unutterable ecstasy was carrying me away, but I dared not abandon myself to it. I was no seer who could look on the unveiling of such glories face to face.
An irrepressible yearning came over me to impart what I beheld, to share with another soul the weight of this colossal revelation. With this purpose I scrutinized this vision; I sought in it for some characteristic which might make it translatable to another mind. There was none. In absolute incommunicableness it stood apart. For it, in spoken language, there was no symbol.
For a long time﻿—how long a hashish-eater alone can know﻿—I was in an agony. I searched every pocket for my pencil and notebook, that I might at least set down some representative mark which would afterward recall to me the lineaments of my apocalypse. They were not with me. Jutting into the water of the brook along which I then wandered, and which, before and afterward, was my sole companion through so many ecstasies, lay a broad, flat stone. “Glory in the highest!” I shouted, exultingly; “I will at least grave on this tablet some hieroglyph of what I feel.” Tremblingly I sought for my knife; that, too, was gone! It was then that, in a frenzy, I threw myself prostrate on the stone, and with my nails sought to make some memorial scratch upon it. Hard, hard as flint! In despair I stood up.
Suddenly there came a sense as if some invisible presence walking the dread paths of the vision with me, yet at a distance, as if separated from my side by a long flow of time. Taking courage, I cried, “Who has ever been here before me? who, in years past, has shared with me this unutterable view?” In tones which linger with me to this day, a grand, audible voice responded “Pythagoras!” In an instant I was calm. I heard the footsteps of that sublime sage echoing upward through the ages, and in celestial light I read my vision unterrified, since it had burst upon his sight before me.
For years previous I had been perplexed with his mysterious philosophy.

这一构想向我显现的鲜活性，使得它成为一件令人难以独自承受的可怖之事。一阵难以言状的狂喜将我裹挟而去，但我却不敢任由自己沉沦其中。我并非能同这般荣耀的揭示面对面相觑的先知。

一种难以遏制的渴望袭上心头，促使我想要将目睹的一切传达出去，同另一颗灵魂共同分担这沉重庞大的宏大启示。怀着这一目的，我仔细审视着这场异象；我在其中寻找着某种可能使其转译给另一颗心智的特质。然而一无所获。它在绝对的不可传递性中巍然独立。对于它而言，在口语的世界里根本找不到任何符号象征。

在一段漫长的时间里——究竟有多久唯有食大麻者方能知晓——我饱受折磨。我搜遍了每一个口袋寻找铅笔和笔记本，以便能至少记下一丝代表性的印记，日后能帮我忆起这场启示的轮廓。可我什么也没带在身边。我当时正漫步徜徉在溪流畔——这条小溪在以往和后来的无数次狂喜中都是我唯一的伴侣——一块宽大平整的岩石正斜插在溪水之中。“至高荣耀归于上天！”我狂喜地高呼道，“我至少可以在这块石板上刻下些许代表我所感所悟的象形符号。”我颤抖着去摸我的小刀；可它竟然也不见了！就在那一刻，在一阵狂乱之中，我扑通一声趴倒在石头上，用手指甲拼命试图在上面抠出一些纪念性的划痕。坚硬，坚硬如燧石！在绝望之中，我站起身来。

突然间，我感到仿佛有某种无形的存在正同我一道漫步在这场异象的恐怖幽径之上，然而却保持着一段距离，宛如与我相隔着一段漫长的岁月之河。我鼓起勇气呼喊道：“在我之前究竟有谁曾来过这里？在过去的岁月里，有谁曾同我分享过这无可言说的壮阔奇景？”伴随着至今仍回荡在我心中的音调，一个宏大清晰的话音回应道：“毕达哥拉斯（Pythagoras）！”刹那间，我恢复了平静。我听闻那位崇高圣哲的足音跨越千古岁月激荡回响；在神圣的天光之中，我毫无惧色地审读着我的异象，因为早在我的目光之前，它便已在毕达哥拉斯眼前骤然绽放。

在此前的漫长岁月里，我一直为他那神秘莫测的哲学所困惑不解。

I saw in him an isolation from universal contemporary mind for which I could not account. When the Ionic school was at the height of its dominance, he stood forth alone the originator of a system as distinct from it as the antipodes of mind.
The doctrine of Thales was built up by the uncertain processes of an obscure logic; that of Pythagoras seemed informed by intuition. In his assertions there had always appeared to me a grave conviction of truth, a consciousness of sincerity which gave them a great weight, though I saw them through the dim refracting medium of tradition, and grasped their meaning imperfectly. It was now given to see, in their own light, the truths which he set forth. I also saw, as to this day I firmly believe, the source whence their revelation flowed.
Tell me not that from Phoenicia he received the wand at whose signal the cohorts of the spheres came trooping up before him in review, unveiling the eternal law and itinerary of their revolutions, and pouring on his spiritual ear that tremendous music to which they marched through space. No. During half a lifetime spent in Egypt and India, both motherlands of this nepenthe, doubt not that he quaffed its apocalyptic draught, and awoke, through its terrific quickening, into the consciousness of that ever-present and all-pervading harmony “which we hear not, because the coarseness of the daily life hath dulled our ear.” The dim penetralia of the Theban Memnonium, or the silent spice-groves of the upper Indus, may have been the gymnasium of his wrestling with the mighty revealer; a priest or a gymnosophist may have been the first to anoint him with the palaestric oil, but he conquered alone. On the strange intuitive characteristics of his system; on the spherical music; on the government of all created things, and their development according to the laws of numbers; yes, on the very use of symbols, which could alone have force to the esoteric disciple (and a terrible significancy, indeed, has the simplest form to a mind hashish-quickened to read its meaning)﻿—on all of these is the legible stamp of the hashish inspiration.

我在他身上看到了一种与同时代普世心智格格不入的遗世独立，对此我曾百思不得其解。当爱奥尼亚学派（Ionic school）的统治如日中天之时，他孑然独立，创立了一套与其截然相反、宛若心智两极的完整体系。

切莫告诉我他是从腓尼基（Phoenicia）获得了那根魔杖，在其挥舞之下，群星天球的军旅纷至沓来接受检阅，揭示其永恒的法则与运转轨迹，并将它们穿行宇宙时的浩瀚天籁倾注在他属灵的耳畔。绝非如此。在他耗费半生游历的埃及与印度——这两处皆是这种忘忧药草的故土家园——毋庸置疑，他曾痛饮过其启示之琼浆，并通过其惊心动魄的唤醒，觉察到了那无处不在、弥漫普世的宏大和谐——“我们之所以听不见，皆因日常生活的凡俗粗鄙磨钝了我们的双耳”。底比斯门农神庙（Theban Memnonium）幽暗的至圣所，抑或印度河上游幽静的香料树林，或许正是他同那位伟大的启示者博弈角力的练武场；某位祭司或裸形贤者（gymnosophist）或许是第一位用角力油涂抹他身躯之人，但他却是独自一人赢得了胜利。无论是在他学说体系奇异的直觉特质上、天球的交响神乐上、万物的统御及其依据数理法则的演进演化上；是的，甚至在唯有对秘传门徒方有约束力的象征符号的运用上（对于经大麻唤醒、能洞悉其深意的心智而言，最简单的几何形式亦具有令人胆寒的深远意义）——在所有这一切之上，皆清晰铭刻着源自大麻灵感的醒目烙印。

It would be no hard task to prove, to a strong probability, at least, that the initiation to the Pythagorean mysteries, and the progressive instruction that succeeded it, to a considerable extent consisted in the employment, judiciously, if we may use the word, of hashish, as giving a critical and analytic power to the mind, which enabled the neophyte to roll up the murk and mist from beclouded truths till they stood distinctly seen in the splendor of their own harmonious beauty as an intuition.
One thing related of Pythagoras and his friends has seemed very striking to me. There is a legend that, as he was passing over a river, its waters called up to him, in the presence of his followers, “Hail! Pythagoras.” Frequently, while in the power of the hashish delirium, have I heard inanimate things sonorous with such voices. On every side they have saluted me, from rocks, and trees, and waters, and sky; in my happiness filling me with intense exultation as I heard them welcoming their master; in my agony heaping nameless curses on my head as I went away into an eternal exile from all sympathy. Of this tradition of Iamblichus I feel an appreciation which almost convinces me that the voice of the river was indeed heard, though only by the quickened mind of some hashish-glorified esoterie. Again, it may be that the doctrine of the metempsychosis was first communicated to Pythagoras by Theban priests; but the astonishing illustration which hashish would contribute to this tenet should not be overlooked in our attempt to assign its first suggestion and succeeding spread to their proper causes.
A modern critic, in defending the hypothesis that Pythagoras was an impostor, has triumphantly asked, “Why did he assume the character of Apollo at the Olympic games? Why did he boast that his soul had lived in former bodies, and that he had first been Æthalides, the son of Mercury, then Euphorbus, then Pyrrhus of Delos, and at last Pythagoras, but that he might more easily impose upon the credulity of an ignorant and superstitious people?” To us these facts seem rather an evidence of his sincerity. Had he made these assertions without proof, it is difficult to see how they would not have had a precisely contrary effect from that of paving the way to a more complete imposition upon popular credulity.
