## XXII Grand Divertissement / 第二十二章 华丽消遣
===Original===

As the months went on, the fervor of my longing toward the former hashish life in some measure passed away, and in general the fascination to return did not present itself so much in the form of pining for an affirmative as loathing of a negative state. It was not the ecstasy of the drug which so much attracted me, as its power of disenthrallment from an apathy which no human aid could utterly take away. Yet even now there were seasons of absolute struggle in which I fought as against a giant, or more truly to the nature of things should I say, in which I resisted as against a demon houri, for my tempter was more passing lovely than anything on earth.
As in the earlier period of my warfare, I now and then caught glimpses of ravishing delight, which, through some rift in the thick cloud elsewhere completely enveloping my daily life, broke in upon me for a moment, yet lasted long enough to prove that I could not yet write myself secure, that my integrity was not yet beyond corruption.
Some of my readers will doubtless be amused, others pained, and a few disgusted at the childlike expedients to which I found it necessary to resort for the purpose of appeasing this renewed appetite for visions without a return to hashish. There were three different sets of circumstances which almost infallibly brought on the longing. It was never suggested by dark and stormy weather, since this was too much in consonance with my habitual mood to demand more than a passing notice. The man who has lost an intimate friend does not pay much attention to murk and mist; it is sunshine which seems to mock his melancholy. So in my own case did it happen. The season of most intense longing was a day of clear sky and brilliant light. That beauty which filled the heart of every other living thing with gladness, only spoke of other suns more wondrous rolling through other heavens of a more matchless dye. I looked into the sky, and missed its former unutterable rose and sapphire; no longer did the whole dome of the firmament sound with grand unwritten music.
It was a pain to look into that desert wilderness of blue which of old my sorcery had peopled for me with innumerable celestial riders, with cities of pearl and symphony-haunted streams of silver. I shut my eyes, and in a moment saw all that I had lost.

随着数月的流逝，我对往昔大麻生活的狂热向往在相当程度上逐渐消退，且大体而言，重返旧习的诱惑不再表现为对某种积极体验的渴望，而是化作了对某种消极状态的厌弃。吸引我的与其说是该药物所带来的狂喜，倒不如说是它能将我从任何人力皆无法彻底消除的麻木冷漠中解脱出来的超拔之力。然而即便到了此时，依然时有绝对苦战的关头——在那些时刻，我宛若同泰坦巨人搏斗，或者更契合事物本质地说，我宛若在抵抗一位女恶魔胡里仙女（houri）的魅惑，因为我的诱惑者比尘世间任何凡物都更为美艳绝伦。

正如在我这场战争的早期阶段一样，我时不时仍会瞥见勾魂摄魄的极乐欢愉——透过在其他方面完全笼罩了我日常生活的那团厚重乌云的一道裂隙，它在瞬息间向我奔涌袭来，尽管稍纵即逝，却足以证实我尚不能自诩安全、我的节操操守尚未完全超然于诱惑腐蚀之外。

读者诸君中，凡读到我为满足这种对异象死灰复燃的渴望、同时又避免重返大麻而不得不采取的孩童般的权宜之计，毫无疑问有人会感到忍俊不禁，有人会倍感心酸，亦会有少数人嗤之以鼻。大体有三类不同的环境境遇几乎必然会唤醒这种渴望。它从未由阴沉暴烈的风雨天气所诱发，因为那种天气同我习以为常的心境过于吻合，以至于除了泛泛留意之外引不起更多波澜。痛失挚友之人绝不会对阴霾迷雾投以过多关切；反倒是明媚的阳光似乎在嘲弄他的忧伤。在我自己的经历中亦是如此。诱惑最为强烈的时刻，乃是万里无云、艳阳高照之日。那份让周遭每一个生灵心中充盈欢欣的美景，却只在向我诉说着其他更为奇妙的太阳正穿行于其他拥有更无可匹敌色泽的浩瀚天宇。我仰望苍穹，怀念起它往昔那无可言状的玫瑰色与蓝宝石华彩；整个天宇穹顶已不再回荡着宏大未谱写进乐谱的仙乐神曲。

凝视着那片蔚蓝的荒漠旷野于我而言乃是一种痛苦，昔日我的巫术曾为我在其中布满了无数天界的骑兵、珍珠铸就的城池与回荡着交响乐的银色溪流。我阖上双眼，刹那间便看见了自己所痛失的一切。

The stones beneath my feet gleamed like unhewn crystals. The frosty fretwork on the panels of doors which I passed, at the touch of the divine Moon-Alchemist became exquisite filigrees of silver. The elm-trees and the locust, shedding sparkles of radiance from their ice-encased twigs, might well have been those trees of gleamy ore which Allah buried when man was cast out of Paradise.
Yet mournfully I thought of the old days, when I would have walked down these shining ways as through an ever-lengthening vista of glories, when the moonlight would have fallen on me mysteriously empurpled, when over all the wondrous domain I had felt myself unquestioned sovereign, and out of the beauteous recesses of earth and sky sprite voices had musically hailed me to my kingdom.
As I thought upon these things, now forever irretrievably abandoned to the past, I have wept﻿—yes, though it be unmanly, I have wept to find myself a discrowned king, a sorcerer ravished of his wand, a god shorn of his glories. I am not ashamed to remember that I did this; for if there be any ecstasy possible which we do not now feel imparted to us, if any excellency in things which does not now make itself tangible, it is no more ignominious to lament over it perished than to sigh after it tarrying.
There was another, a bodily condition, which I always found it necessary to avoid if I would not be smitten with repinings after the hashish life. It was the nervous sensitiveness induced by deprivation of tobacco.
In smoking, if in nothing else, could I boast regularity of habit. To be sure, for this regularity neither an unusually developed organ of order nor the possibility of anything like a systematic arrangement in my multiplicity of labors was to be preeminently thanked. To defer for an hour the nicotine indulgence was to bring on a longing for the cannabine which was actual pain. When circumstances have occurred which made it impossible to smoke before entering my daily round of duties, until they closed I have hardly dared to shut my eyes, lest I should be borne incontinently out of the actual life into which necessity called me to a land of colossal visions. If for a moment I yielded to the impulse, I was straightway in the midst of sky and landscape whose splendors were only less vivid than the perfect hallucinations of the fantasia.

我脚下的石块宛如未经雕琢的水晶般熠熠生辉。我走过的一扇扇门扉嵌板上那如霜雪般的镂空花纹，在神圣“月光炼金术士”的轻抚之下，化作了精妙绝伦的纯银掐丝细工。榆树与刺槐在被冰霜包裹的枝杈上散发着点点晶莹璀璨的光华，宛若当年人类被逐出伊甸园时真主安拉（Allah）埋入地底的那些流光溢彩的矿脉神树。

然而我却悲凄地回想起昔日的岁月——在那些日子里，我曾漫步走下这些闪烁着光芒的坦途，宛如穿行在一条不断延伸延展的荣耀远景之中；每当彼时，月色皆神秘莫测地以紫霞倾泻在我身上；在那片奇妙的领地之上，我曾感到自己是无可置疑的至尊主宰；而从大地与苍穹那瑰丽幽深的隐秘之处，精灵的嗓音曾以天籁仙乐恭迎我君临王国。

每当思及这些如今已永远无可挽回地遗落入往昔的事物，我都忍不住怆然泪下——是的，尽管这显得不够男儿气概，但发现自己沦为了一位被剥夺王冠的废王、一位被夺走魔杖的巫师、一位被剪去万道荣光的神明，我依然潸然泪下。忆及当年的这番举止我并不感到羞愧；因为倘若世间确有某种我们如今未曾感受赋予我们的可能狂喜，倘若造化之中确有某种如今未曾显露真容的卓绝完美，那么为其消逝而悲叹，绝不比为其姗姗来迟而长吁短叹更为可耻。

此外，还有另一种肉体层面的状态，倘若我不想被对大麻生活的苦苦怨念所击倒，我便发觉必须时刻竭力避免。那便是因剥夺烟草所诱发的神经敏感脆弱。

在吸烟这件事上，倘若在其他事情上尚且不能的话，我至少能自诩保持着规律的习惯。诚然，对于这份规律性，既不归功于某种异常发达的秩序感官，亦不归功于在我千头万绪的繁重劳作中可能存在的任何系统化安排。将享受尼古丁的时刻推迟哪怕一个钟头，便会引来对大麻素（cannabine）的强烈渴望——那是一种切实的痛苦。每当遇到某些突发境况使我在步入每日例行公事之前无法抽上一斗烟时，在工作结束之前我几乎不敢阖上双眼，生怕自己会身不由己地从现实生活——正是生存必然性召唤我投身其中的生活——被裹挟而去，坠入一片充斥着宏大异象的魔法国度。倘若我哪怕有一刻向这股冲动妥协屈服，我便会立即置身于苍穹与山川之中，其璀璨壮美仅次于奇想全盛时期的彻底幻觉。

But I have not yet spoken of those expedients to which I resorted for relief and to avoid the necessity of resuming the use of hashish. Certainly, in them ingenuity, so far as I possessed any, was tortured to its utmost endurance.
Sometimes I spent the few moments of leisure which during the day could be snatched from business in﻿—mention it not confidently in Gath, breathe it not to the friend of thy bosom in Askelon﻿—blowing soap-bubbles. Not that there is aught deserving of contempt in the enjoyment of that which has been made a philosophic toy by one of the greatest of Anglo-Saxon sages﻿—not that the pleasure of rare beauties from humble elements is of necessity an aesthetic heresy, but because the hashish-eater is well aware of the existence of critics, to whom all that is childlike is also childish, who quarrel with men for being perversely happy on moderate means, and with their Creator because he has not made all the little hills as high as Cotopaxi.
Yes, throwing down the wand of professional majesty, degrading myself to the level of the most callow neophyte of an infant class, did I take up the pipe, and, going into the presence of the nearest sunbeam (a course which, by the way, might well be followed by those who for their light go farther and fare worse), did I create sphere after sphere, not, as the grotesquely but unintentionally blasphemous old poet hath it, snapping them off my fingers into space, but with careful hand taking rest over the back of a chair to counteract the tremulousness of over-anxiety not to tremble, did I inflate them to the maximum, and then sit wrapped up in gazing at their luxuriant sheen until they broke.
There I found some faint actualization of my remembered hashish-sky, and where the actual failed there did the ideal, thus stimulated, come in to complete the vision. Had time allowed me, I could have consumed hours in watching the sliding, the rich intermingling, the changes by origination, and the changes by reaction of those matchless hues, or hues at least so matchless in the real world that to find their parallel we must leave the glories of a waking life, and go floating through the firmament of some iridescent dream. Verily, he who would be meet for the participation in any joys must robe himself in humility and become as a little child.

然而我尚未述及那些为了寻求慰藉、并避免被迫重拾大麻而采取的权宜良策。毋庸置疑，在这些举措之中，我的聪明才智（倘若我尚有一丝的话）已被榨取折磨到了承受力的极限。

有时，我将白天从公务中好不容易偷得的片刻闲暇，用于——切莫在迦特（Gath）公然宣扬，切莫在亚实基伦（Askelon）向你的知心挚友吐露——吹肥皂泡。这绝非因为在一件被盎格鲁-撒克逊最伟大的圣哲之一视作哲思玩具的事物中寻欢作乐有何值得鄙夷之处——亦非因为从卑微的元素中发掘罕见之美必然是一种审美异端，而是因为食大麻者深知世间充斥着这般批评家：在他们眼中，一切具有赤子之心的事物皆是幼稚无知的表现；他们会因人们凭借微薄的物质条件便固执地感到幸福而大动干戈，亦会因造物主未曾将世间所有的矮丘皆造成如科托帕希火山（Cotopaxi）那般雄伟而向上苍怨声载道。

是的，扔下职业威严的神杖，将自己贬低至幼儿园学童中最为不谙世事的黄口孺子的水平，我拿起了吹管；走到距离最近的一束阳光前（顺便提一句，那些为了追寻光明而舍近求远、结果境遇更为糟糕的人，大可效仿这一做法），我吹出了一枚又一枚球体；我并非如那位荒诞怪异却并无亵渎之意的古诗人所写的那样、用手指将它们轻弹入虚空之中，而是用谨慎的手稳稳扶在椅背上以抵御因过度唯恐颤抖而产生的战栗，我将它们吹胀到最大的极限，随后整个人痴痴沉醉于凝视它们华美绚烂的光泽，直至它们破裂消散。

在彼处，我寻觅到了对自己记忆中大麻苍穹的些许微弱具象，而凡在现实有所欠缺之处，由此受到激发的理想之境便随即介入，补全了整场异象。倘若时光允许，我能在那里耗费数个钟头，痴痴注视着那些无双色彩的流转滑动、绚烂交融、因源生而起的变化、以及因互激而生的变幻——抑或至少是在现实世界中如此举世无双的色彩，以至于若要寻觅其对照，我们必须舍弃清醒生活的种种荣耀，飘然而去穿行于某场虹彩斑斓的梦境天穹。诚然，凡欲配得上分享任何欢愉之人，皆必须以谦卑为袍服，让自己蜕变成为一名小小的孩童。

There was one other way in which I measurably reproduced the past for my innocent satisfaction. Had I permitted, at certain seasons, any foreign eye to invade the sanctity of my room, it would have fallen, possibly with some surprise, upon a singular arrangement of the books upon my table into a form somewhat resembling those houses which children build at their play. Yet the stranger would have very little suspected a clue to the mystery in the fact that I had thus been embodying to myself the ideal of the ancient cavern or the resplendent temple in which many a day ago I had exulted through a whole evening, while the rocks echoed with strange music, or oracular voices spoke to me out of the inner shrine. Had he asked me the secret, he had probably not been much the wiser for my answer.
There is still another method, and by far the most efficient of all, by which I gratified the visionary propensity without returning to the old indulgence. I had been advised by the counselor to whose article I originally owed my emancipation, whenever the fascination of the drug came upon me with peculiar power, to evade it by reenacting some former vision upon paper. A truly wise and well-considered counsel did I find this, and one which, whenever the possibility existed from any gap in my daily occupation, I followed scrupulously. As would have resulted from once more superinducing the hashish delirium, my visions, marshaled out of memory, marched past beneath varying banners; some of them banded under hell-black flags, and others carrying the colors of a rainbow of the seventh heaven.
From this reproduction of the past in the order in which it had occurred, I gained a double benefit, the pleasure of appeasing the fascination without increasing it, and the salutary review of abominable horrors without any more than the echo of a pang. In this way some portion of the present narrative was sketched at first, but of necessity a very small one, since the pressure of business made my abode, even in the most innoxious dreamland, that only of a wayfaring man who turneth aside but for a night.

还有另一种方法，能让我在适度范围内重现往昔以获得无伤大雅的慰藉。倘若在某些特定的时刻，我允许任何外人的目光侵入我房间的私密圣殿，那目光或许会带着几分诧异，落在我案头书籍的一种奇异摆放方式上——其形态颇似孩童在嬉戏时搭建的小房屋。然而那位陌生人绝不会猜透其中的玄机线索：我其实正以此向自己具象化那座古老的洞穴、抑或那座金碧辉煌的神庙的理想之境——在多日以前，我曾整整一个傍晚在其中欢欣雀跃，而岩石间回荡着奇异的仙乐，神谕般的话音从内殿深处向我娓娓道来。倘若他向我探问其中的玄机，我的答复恐怕也无法让他变得更明智几分。

还有另一种方法——且是迄今为止所有方法中最为行之有效的一种——能让我在不必重返旧习沉沦的前提下，满足对异象的嗜好偏向。我最初蒙其文章启发而获得解放的那位导师曾告诫我：每当药物的诱惑带着特殊的魔力向我袭来之时，便通过在纸面上重现昔日的某场异象来规避摆脱它。我发觉这是一剂真正睿智且深思熟虑的良方，凡在日常事务的间隙中存在哪怕一丝可能，我都一丝不苟地遵从践行。宛若再度诱发大麻谵妄所必然导致的结果一般，我的异象从记忆的队列中被检阅集结，在各式各样的旗帜下鱼贯走过；其中某些在漆黑如地狱的旌旗下列队进发，另一些则高擎着来自第七重天的七彩虹霓之色。

借由依照昔日发生的顺序对过往进行这番重现，我获得了双重的福祉：既获得了平息诱惑而不使其滋长蔓延的欣悦，亦收获了对可憎恐怖之事的有益回顾检视，且除了剧痛的一丝微弱余响之外别无创伤。通过这种方式，本书叙述的某些片段最初便得以草就成形，但必然只是极其微小的一部分，因为公务的紧迫使得我即便身处最无害的梦境之乡，亦不过是一名仅借宿一晚便匆匆折返的过路行者。
