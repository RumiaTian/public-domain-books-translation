6. *Genn* or *Ginn*, in the Arabic, signifies a Genius or Demon﻿—a being of a higher order, and formed of more subtle matter than man. Consonant to this, we read that, “When the servant of God stood up to invoke him, it wanted little but that the Genii had pressed on him in crowds, to hear him rehearse the Koran.” —﻿Herbelot, p. 357. Al Koran ch. 72 ↩︎
