6. *Genn* or *Ginn*, in the Arabic, signifies a Genius or Demon﻿—a being of a higher order, and formed of more subtle matter than man. According to Oriental mythology, the Genii governed the world long before the creation of Adam. The Muhammadans regarded them as an intermediate race between angels and men, and capable of salvation: whence Muhammad pretended a commission to convert them. Consonant to this, we read that, “When the servant of God stood up to invoke him, it wanted little but that the Genii had pressed on him in crowds, to hear him rehearse the Koran.” —﻿Herbelot, p. 357.
