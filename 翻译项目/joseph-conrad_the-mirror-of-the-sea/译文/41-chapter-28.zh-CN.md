### XXVIII / 第二十八篇

===Original===
The winds of North and South are, as I have said, but small princes amongst the powers of the sea. They have no territory of their own; they are not reigning winds anywhere. Yet it is from their houses that the reigning dynasties which have shared between them the waters of the earth are sprung. All the weather of the world is based upon the contest of the Polar and Equatorial strains of that tyrannous race. The West Wind is the greatest king. The East rules between the Tropics. They have shared each ocean between them. Each has his genius of supreme rule. The King of the West never intrudes upon the recognized dominion of his kingly brother. He is a barbarian, of a northern type. Violent without craftiness, and furious without malice, one may imagine him seated masterfully with a double-edged sword on his knees upon the painted and gilt clouds of the sunset, bowing his shock head of golden locks, a flaming beard over his breast, imposing, colossal, mighty-limbed, with a thundering voice, distended cheeks and fierce blue eyes, urging the speed of his gales. The other, the East king, the king of bloodred sunrises, I represent to myself as a spare Southerner with clear-cut features, black-browed and dark-eyed, grey-robed, upright in sunshine, resting a smooth-shaven cheek in the palm of his hand, impenetrable, secret, full of wiles, fine-drawn, keen—meditating aggressions.

===Chinese===
北方与南方的风，如我所言，不过是海洋诸力中的小公爵罢了。它们没有自己的领地，在任何地方都不是主宰之风。然而，正是从它们的谱系中，诞生了瓜分了地球水域的那些统治王朝。全世界的天气都建立在这暴虐家族中极地血统与赤道血统之间的较量之上。西风（West Wind）是最伟大的王。东风（East Wind）统治着热带之间的海域。它们各自瓜分了每一片海洋。每一位都拥有至上统治的天才。西风之王从不侵入他那王族兄弟的公认领地。他是一个北方类型的蛮族人。暴烈而无狡诈，狂怒而无恶意，你可以想象他威严地坐在落日那色彩斑斓、镀金的云霞之上，膝上横放着一把双刃剑，低垂着一头金发乱蓬蓬的脑袋，胸前火焰般的长须，威仪赫赫，身形巨硕，四肢雄健，声如雷霆，双颊涨鼓，蓝眼凶猛，催促着他的风暴加速前行。另一位，东风之王，血红日出之王，我把他想象成一个清瘦的南方人，五官棱角分明，浓眉黑眼，灰袍加身，在阳光下笔直而立，一只削瘦的手托着光滑的下颏，深不可测，神秘莫测，满腹诡计，精明，犀利——正在筹谋侵略。

===Original===
The West Wind keeps faith with his brother, the King of the Easterly Weather. "What we have divided we have divided," he seems to say in his gruff voice, this ruler without guile, who hurls as if in sport enormous masses of cloud across the sky, and flings the great waves of the Atlantic clear across from the shores of the New World upon the hoary headlands of Old Europe, which harbours more kings and rulers upon its seamed and furrowed body than all the oceans of the world together. "What we have divided we have divided; and if no rest and peace in this world have fallen to my share, leave me alone. Let me play at quoits with cyclonic gales, flinging the discs of spinning cloud and whirling air from one end of my dismal kingdom to the other: over the Great Banks or along the edges of pack-ice—this one with true aim right into the bight of the Bay of Biscay, that other upon the fjords of Norway, across the North Sea where the fishermen of many nations look watchfully into my angry eye. This is the time of kingly sport."

===Chinese===
西风信守着与他的兄弟——东风之王——的盟约。"我们瓜分了的就瓜分了，"他仿佛用粗犷的嗓音这样说，这位没有诡计的统治者，嬉戏般地将大团大团的云抛过天空，将大西洋的巨浪从新大陆的海岸直接甩向古欧洲那苍老的海角——欧洲那沟壑纵横的身躯上容纳的国王和统治者，比全世界所有海洋加在一起还要多。"我们瓜分了的就瓜分了；如果这世间的安逸与和平没有我的份，那就别管我。让我用气旋风暴玩套圈游戏，把旋转的云盘和旋涡气流从我这阴郁王国的一端甩到另一端：越过纽芬兰大浅滩（Great Banks），或者沿着浮冰边缘——这一个准准地射入比斯开湾（Bay of Biscay）的弧形水域，那一个落在挪威的峡湾上空，横跨北海——那里的多国渔民正警觉地注视着我愤怒的眼睛。这才是王者的游戏。"

===Original===
And the royal master of high latitudes sighs mightily, with the sinking sun upon his breast and the double-edged sword upon his knees, as if wearied by the innumerable centuries of a strenuous rule and saddened by the unchangeable aspect of the ocean under his feet—by the endless vista of future ages where the work of sowing the wind and reaping the whirlwind shall go on and on till his realm of living waters becomes a frozen and motionless ocean. But the other, crafty and unmoved, nursing his shaven chin between the thumb and forefinger of his slim and treacherous hand, thinks deep within his heart full of guile: "Aha! our brother of the West has fallen into the mood of kingly melancholy. He is tired of playing with circular gales, and blowing great guns, and unrolling thick streamers of fog in wanton sport at the cost of his own poor, miserable subjects. Their fate is most pitiful. Let us make a foray upon the dominions of that noisy barbarian, a great raid from Finisterre to Hatteras, catching his fishermen unawares, baffling the fleets that trust to his power, and shooting sly arrows into the livers of men who court his good graces. He is, indeed, a worthless fellow." And forthwith, while the West Wind meditates upon the vanity of his irresistible might, the thing is done, and the Easterly weather sets in upon the North Atlantic.

===Chinese===
高纬度的王者之主便长叹一声，落日映在他的胸膛，双刃剑横在膝头，仿佛被无以计数的艰辛统治岁月所累，又为脚下那亘古不变的海洋面容而哀伤——为那无尽的未来远景而哀伤，在那远景中，播撒风暴与收获旋风的劳作将永无止境地持续下去，直到他那生机勃勃的水域王国变成一片冰封死寂的海洋。而另一位——狡诈而无动于衷，用纤细而诡诈的手的拇指与食指托着光洁的下巴，在他那满腹诡计的心灵深处思忖："啊哈！我们的西风兄弟陷入了王者的忧郁。他厌倦了玩气旋风暴的游戏，厌倦了吹大炮，厌倦了以肆意的戏耍为代价，在他那些可怜的臣民头顶上展开厚重的雾帘。他们的命运何其悲惨。让我们去那个喧闹蛮族的领地掠夺一番吧——从菲尼斯特雷角（Finisterre）到哈特拉斯角（Hatteras）的大突袭，打他个措手不及，挫败那些信赖他力量的舰队，将阴险的箭射入那些巴结他恩宠的人的肝胆。他确实是个不值一提的家伙。"说干就干，当西风还在沉思他那不可抗拒之力的虚妄时，事情已经办妥，东风天气登上了北大西洋的舞台。

===Original===
The prevailing weather of the North Atlantic is typical of the way in which the West Wind rules his realm on which the sun never sets. North Atlantic is the heart of a great empire. It is the part of the West Wind's dominions most thickly populated with generations of fine ships and hardy men. Heroic deeds and adventurous exploits have been performed there, within the very stronghold of his sway. The best sailors in the world have been born and bred under the shadow of his sceptre, learning to manage their ships with skill and audacity before the steps of his stormy throne. Reckless adventurers, toiling fishermen, admirals as wise and brave as the world has ever known, have waited upon the signs of his westerly sky. Fleets of victorious ships have hung upon his breath. He has tossed in his hand squadrons of war-scarred three-deckers, and shredded out in mere sport the bunting of flags hallowed in the traditions of honour and glory. He is a good friend and a dangerous enemy, without mercy to unseaworthy ships and fainthearted seamen. In his kingly way he has taken but little account of lives sacrificed to his impulsive policy; he is a king with a double-edged sword bared in his right hand. The East Wind, an interloper in the dominions of Westerly weather, is an impassive-faced tyrant with a sharp poniard held behind his back for a treacherous stab.

===Chinese===
北大西洋的盛行天气，典型地体现了西风统治其日不落王国的方式。北大西洋是一个伟大帝国的心脏。它是西风领地中最密集地聚居着世代精良船只与坚韧水手的区域。英雄事迹与冒险壮举就在他的权力腹地之内上演。世界上最优秀的水手都在他的权杖阴影下出生、成长，在他那风暴王座的阶前学会了以精湛与胆魄驾驭船只。鲁莽的冒险者、劳碌的渔夫、世上最英明勇敢的海军上将，都在仰望他西天的征兆。凯旋舰队的命运系于他的呼吸。他将伤痕累累的战列舰（three-decker）中队在掌中颠弄，以纯粹的嬉戏撕碎了那些在光荣传统中被奉为神圣的旗帜彩带。他是一个好朋友，也是一个危险的敌人，对不适航海的船只和怯懦的水手毫无怜悯。以他王者的方式，他对他那冲动政策所牺牲的生命从不在意；他是一个右手握着出鞘双刃剑的王。东风——西风领地的闯入者——是一个面无表情的暴君，背后藏着一把锋利的匕首，专为背刺而来。

===Original===
In his forays into the North Atlantic the East Wind behaves like a subtle and cruel adventurer without a notion of honour or fair play. Veiling his clear-cut, lean face in a thin layer of a hard, high cloud, I have seen him, like a wizened robber sheik of the sea, hold up large caravans of ships to the number of three hundred or more at the very gates of the English Channel. And the worst of it was that there was no ransom that we could pay to satisfy his avidity; for whatever evil is wrought by the raiding East Wind, it is done only to spite his kingly brother of the West. We gazed helplessly at the systematic, cold, grey-eyed obstinacy of the Easterly Weather, while short rations became the order of the day, and the pinch of hunger under the breastbone grew familiar to every sailor in that held-up fleet. Every day added to our numbers. In knots and groups and straggling parties we flung to and forth before the closed gate. And meantime the outward-bound ships passed, running through our humiliated ranks under all the canvas they could show. It is my idea that the Easterly Wind helps the ships away from home in the wicked hope that they shall all come to an untimely end and be heard of no more. For six weeks did the robber sheik hold the trade route of the earth, while our liege lord, the West Wind, slept profoundly like a tired Titan, or else remained lost in a mood of idle sadness known only to frank natures. All was still to the westward; we looked in vain towards his stronghold: the King slumbered on so deeply that he let his foraging brother steal the very mantle of gold-lined purple clouds from his bowed shoulders. What had become of the dazzling hoard of royal jewels exhibited at every close of day? Gone, disappeared, extinguished, carried off without leaving a single gold band or the flash of a single sunbeam in the evening sky! Day after day through a cold streak of heavens as bare and poor as the inside of a rifled safe a rayless and despoiled sun would slink shamefacedly, without pomp or show, to hide in haste under the waters. And still the King slept on, or mourned the vanity of his might and his power, while the thin-lipped intruder put the impress of his cold and implacable spirit upon the sky and sea. With every daybreak the rising sun had to wade through a crimson stream, luminous and sinister, like the spilt blood of celestial bodies murdered during the night.

===Chinese===
东风在北大西洋的突袭中，活像一个毫无荣誉或公平竞技概念的阴险残暴的冒险家。用一层薄薄的硬质高云遮住他那棱角分明的瘦削面孔，我见过他——如同一个干瘪的海上强盗酋长——在英吉利海峡的大门口拦下了多达三百余艘船的庞大商队。最糟糕的是，我们根本付不出能令他满足的赎金；因为东风掠夺所造成的一切恶行，仅仅是为了与他的西风兄弟作对。我们无助地注视着东风天气那系统性的、冷酷的、灰眼睛般的固执，定量配给成了每日的常态，胸骨下方的饥饿挤压感成了这支被截留舰队中每个水手的家常便饭。每天都增添新的同伴。我们三五成群、零零散散地在这封闭的大门前徘徊。与此同时，出航的船只却从我们这蒙羞的队列中穿过，满帆航行。在我看来，东风帮助船只离家远航，怀着险恶的希望：它们都将遭遇不测，从此杳无音信。整整六周，这个强盗酋长把持着地球的贸易航线，而我们的君主——西风——像一个疲惫的泰坦（Titan）般沉沉大睡，或者陷入了一种只有天性坦率之人才懂的慵懒忧伤。西边一片沉寂；我们徒然地望向他的堡垒：国王沉睡得如此深沉，以至于让他的掠夺兄弟将那镶金紫云的斗篷从他低垂的肩头偷走。那些每日黄昏时分陈列的令人目眩的皇家珠宝般的云霞去了哪里？消散了，消失了，熄灭了，被席卷一空，天空中没有留下一条金边、一道阳光！日复一日，一轮被掠夺的、失去光泽的太阳，灰溜溜地、毫无排场地匆忙钻入水下，穿过一道如被洗劫过的保险柜内壁般空洞寒碜的天空。而国王依然沉睡，或者在哀叹他那力量与权能的虚妄，与此同时，薄唇的入侵者将他冷酷无情的精神烙印压在了天空与海面之上。每一个黎明，初升的太阳都不得不涉过一道猩红的洪流，明亮而不祥，仿佛夜间的天体被谋杀后流淌出的鲜血。

===Original===
In this particular instance the mean interloper held the road for some six weeks on end, establishing his particular administrative methods over the best part of the North Atlantic. It looked as if the Easterly Weather had come to stay forever, or, at least, till we had all starved to death in the held-up fleet—starved within sight, as it were, of plenty, within touch, almost, of the bountiful heart of the Empire. There we were, dotting with our white dry sails the hard blueness of the deep sea. There we were, a growing company of ships, each with her burden of grain, of timber, of wool, of hides, and even of oranges, for we had one or two belated fruit schooners in company. There we were, in that memorable spring of a certain year in the late seventies, dodging to and fro, baffled on every tack, and with our stores running down to sweepings of bread-lockers and scrapings of sugar-casks. It was just like the East Wind's nature to inflict starvation upon the bodies of unoffending sailors, while he corrupted their simple souls by an exasperation leading to outbursts of profanity as lurid as his bloodred sunrises. They were followed by grey days under the cover of high, motionless clouds that looked as if carved in a slab of ash-coloured marble. And each mean starved sunset left us calling with imprecations upon the West Wind even in its most veiled misty mood to wake up and give us our liberty, if only to rush on and dash the heads of our ships against the very walls of our unapproachable home.

===Chinese===
在这个特定的事例中，这个卑鄙的闯入者把持了航路大约六周之久，将其独特的行政手段施加于北大西洋的大半区域。东风天气仿佛要永远留下来了，或者说至少要留到我们这支被截留的舰队全体饿死——饿在丰饶的近旁，几乎触手可及帝国那慷慨心脏的近旁。我们在那里，白色的干帆点缀着深海那坚硬的蔚蓝。我们在那里，一支日益壮大的船队，每条船都满载着粮食、木材、羊毛、皮革，甚至还有橘子——因为我们中间还有一两条迟到的水果纵帆船（schooner）。我们在那里，在那个七十年代末某个值得纪念的春天，来回躲闪，每一个戗风转向都徒劳无功，而我们的存粮已经减少到面包柜底的碎屑和糖桶壁上的刮痕。这正是东风的本性：将饥饿施加于无辜水手的肉体，同时用令人暴怒的挫折腐蚀他们淳朴的灵魂，激发出与他那血红日出一样刺目的咒骂。灰色的日子接踵而至，笼罩在高高悬停、纹丝不动的云层之下——那些云看起来像是用灰白色大理石雕凿而成的。每一个吝啬而饥饿的黄昏都留下我们，用诅咒呼唤西风——即便在他最朦胧多雾的情绪中——醒来，还我们自由，哪怕只是为了让我们冲向前去，将船头撞进我们那遥不可及的家园的城墙。
