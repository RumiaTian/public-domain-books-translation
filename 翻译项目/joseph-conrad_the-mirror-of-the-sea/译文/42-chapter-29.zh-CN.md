### XXIX / 第二十九篇

===Original===
In the atmosphere of the Easterly Weather, as pellucid as a piece of crystal and refracting like a prism, we could see the appalling numbers of our helpless company, even to those who in more normal conditions would have remained invisible, sails down under the horizon. It is the malicious pleasure of the East Wind to augment the power of your eyesight, in order, perhaps, that you should see better the perfect humiliation, the hopeless character of your captivity. Easterly Weather is generally clear, and that is all that can be said for it—almost supernaturally clear when it likes; but whatever its mood, there is something uncanny in its nature. Its duplicity is such that it will deceive a scientific instrument. No barometer will give warning of an Easterly gale, were it ever so wet. It would be an unjust and ungrateful thing to say that a barometer is a stupid contrivance. It is simply that the wiles of the East Wind are too much for its fundamental honesty.

===Chinese===
在东风天气的大气中——澄澈如水晶，折射如棱镜——我们能看见我们那群无助同伴的惊人数量，甚至那些在更正常条件下本该隐没在地平线以下的、帆已降下的船。东风的恶意乐趣在于放大你视力的极限，或许正因如此，你才能更清楚地看到自己那彻底的屈辱、被囚的绝望本质。东风天气通常晴朗，这便是对它唯一的褒词——它乐意时近乎超自然地清澈；但无论何种心情，它的本质中总有些诡异。它的狡诈足以欺骗科学仪器。无论多潮湿，气压表都发不出东风烈风的预警。说气压表是愚蠢的装置，这既不公平也不厚道。只是东风的诡计实在超出了它诚实本性的应对能力。

===Original===
After years and years of experience the most trusty instrument of the sort that ever went to sea screwed on to a ship's cabin bulkhead will, almost invariably, be induced to rise by the diabolic ingenuity of the Easterly Weather, just at the moment when the Easterly Weather, discarding its methods of hard, dry, impassive cruelty, contemplates drowning what is left of your spirit in torrents of a peculiarly cold and horrid rain. The sleet-and-hail squalls following the lightning at the end of a Westerly gale are cold and benumbing and stinging and cruel enough. But the dry, Easterly Weather, when it turns to wet, seems to rain poisoned showers upon your head. It is a sort of steady, persistent, overwhelming, endlessly driving downpour, which makes your heart sick, and opens it to dismal forebodings.

===Chinese===
经过年复一年的经验，那种装在船舱壁板上、出过海的最可靠的仪器，几乎无一例外会被东风天气的恶魔般的心机诱骗而指针上升——偏偏就在东风天气抛弃了它那冷酷、干燥、面无表情的残忍方式，转而盘算着用一场异常冰冷可怖的暴雨淹没你残余的精神之时。西风烈风末尾伴随闪电而来的雨雪飑够冷、够麻、够刺痛、够残忍了。但干燥的东风天气一旦转潮，似乎就在你头顶降下毒雨。那是一种恒定的、执拗的、排山倒海般的、无休无止的倾泻，令人心头发怵，引出种种不祥的预感。

===Original===
And the stormy mood of the Easterly Weather looms black upon the sky with a peculiar and amazing blackness. The West Wind hangs heavy grey curtains of mist and spray before your gaze, but the Eastern interloper of the narrow seas, when he has mustered his courage and cruelty to the point of a gale, puts your eyes out, puts them out completely, makes you feel blind for life upon a lee-shore. It is the wind, also, that brings snow.

===Chinese===
而东风天气暴烈的脾气以一种独特而惊人的漆黑在天际显现。西风在你眼前垂下沉重的灰色水雾与浪花之幕，但这位狭海的东方入侵者——当他鼓足勇气与残忍酝酿成一场烈风时——会弄瞎你的眼睛，彻底弄瞎，让你觉得自己在背风海岸上永远失明。也是这风，带来了雪。

===Original===
Out of his black and merciless heart he flings a white blinding sheet upon the ships of the sea. He has more manners of villainy, and no more conscience than an Italian prince of the seventeenth century. His weapon is a dagger carried under a black cloak when he goes out on his unlawful enterprises. The mere hint of his approach fills with dread every craft that swims the sea, from fishing-smacks to four-masted ships that recognize the sway of the West Wind. Even in his most accommodating mood he inspires a dread of treachery. I have heard upwards of ten score of windlasses spring like one into clanking life in the dead of night, filling the Downs with a panic-struck sound of anchors being torn hurriedly out of the ground at the first breath of his approach.

===Chinese===
他从那颗冷酷无情的心中掷出一片刺目的白色帷幕，罩在海上的船舶上。他的恶行手段比十七世纪的意大利君主还多，良心却一样也没有。他出动从事非法勾当时，武器是藏在黑斗篷下的匕首。他行踪的蛛丝马迹便足以让海上航行的一切船只——从渔船到承认西风霸权的四桅大帆船——心生恐惧。即便在他最随和的时刻，他也会激起一种遭到背叛的恐惧。我曾听见超过两百架起锚机在深夜齐声弹起、铿然作响，整个当斯锚地（the Downs）充斥着锚被仓惶从地面扯出的惊恐之声——那是他接近时吹出的第一口气。

===Original===
Fortunately, his heart often fails him: he does not always blow home upon our exposed coast; he has not the fearless temper of his Westerly brother.

===Chinese===
所幸他经常怯阵：他并不总是在我们毫无遮蔽的海岸上全力施威；他没有他那位西风兄弟的无畏秉性。

===Original===
The natures of those two winds that share the dominions of the great oceans are fundamentally different. It is strange that the winds which men are prone to style capricious remain true to their character in all the various regions of the earth. To us here, for instance, the East Wind comes across a great continent, sweeping over the greatest body of solid land upon this earth. For the Australian east coast the East Wind is the wind of the ocean, coming across the greatest body of water upon the globe; and yet here and there its characteristics remain the same with a strange consistency in everything that is vile and base. The members of the West Wind's dynasty are modified in a way by the regions they rule, as a Hohenzollern, without ceasing to be himself, becomes a Romanian by virtue of his throne, or a Saxe-Coburg learns to put the dress of Bulgarian phrases upon his particular thoughts, whatever they are.

===Chinese===
分治大洋的两种风的本性根本不同。说来奇怪，那些人们动辄称之为反复无常的风，在地球各个区域却始终忠于自身的品性。以我们这里为例，东风跨越一片辽阔的大陆，掠过地球上最大的一块坚实陆地。对澳大利亚东海岸而言，东风却是来自大洋的风，跨越着全球最大的一片水域；然而无论在哪里，它的特性始终如一，在一切卑劣低俗之事上保持着惊人的一致性。西风王朝的成员则在一定程度上被其所统治的地区所改变，正如一位霍亨索伦（Hohenzollern）不丧失自身却因王位而成为罗马尼亚人，或一位萨克森-科堡（Saxe-Coburg）学会用保加利亚式的辞藻包装他那些无论什么内容的独特思想。

===Original===
The autocratic sway of the West Wind, whether forty north or forty south of the Equator, is characterized by an open, generous, frank, barbarous recklessness. For he is a great autocrat, and to be a great autocrat you must be a great barbarian. I have been too much moulded to his sway to nurse now any idea of rebellion in my heart. Moreover, what is a rebellion within the four walls of a room against the tempestuous rule of the West Wind? I remain faithful to the memory of the mighty King with a double-edged sword in one hand, and in the other holding out rewards of great daily runs and famously quick passages to those of his courtiers who knew how to wait watchfully for every sign of his secret mood. As we deep-water men always reckoned, he made one year in three fairly lively for anybody having business upon the Atlantic or down there along the "forties" of the Southern Ocean.

===Chinese===
西风的专制统治——无论在赤道以北四十度还是以南四十度——都以一种坦荡、慷慨、率直、蛮横的不计后果为特征。因为他是伟大的专制君主，而要做伟大的专制君主，就必须是伟大的蛮族人。我被他的统治塑造得太深，如今心中已不敢滋生任何反叛之念。况且，在四壁之内的房间里对西风的暴烈统治发起叛乱又算什么？我仍忠实地铭记着那位强大的君王——他一手持双刃剑，另一手向那些善于敏锐捕捉他隐秘心情之征兆的廷臣们抛出丰厚的日航程与著名快航的奖赏。正如我们这些深海水手一贯的估算，他每三年让大西洋或南方大洋"四十纬度带"上有事可做的人过上一年相当热闹的日子。

===Original===
You had to take the bitter with the sweet; and it cannot be denied he played carelessly with our lives and fortunes. But, then, he was always a great king, fit to rule over the great waters where, strictly speaking, a man would have no business whatever but for his audacity.

===Chinese===
你必须苦乐兼收；不可否认他随意摆弄着我们的生命与财产。但他到底始终是一位伟大的君王，配得上统治那片浩渺的水域——严格说来，若非人的胆大妄为，人类本不该在那里有任何营生。

===Original===
The audacious should not complain. A mere trader ought not to grumble at the tolls levied by a mighty king. His mightiness was sometimes very overwhelming; but even when you had to defy him openly, as on the banks of the Agulhas homeward bound from the East Indies, or on the outward passage round the Horn, he struck at you fairly his stinging blows (full in the face, too), and it was your business not to get too much staggered. And, after all, if you showed anything of a countenance, the good-natured barbarian would let you fight your way past the very steps of his throne. It was only now and then that the sword descended and a head fell; but if you fell you were sure of impressive obsequies and of a roomy, generous grave.

===Chinese===
胆大妄为者不应抱怨。一个普通的商人不应抱怨一位伟大君王征收的过路税。他的威势有时确实令人窒息；但即便你不得不公开违抗他——如同从东印度群岛返航归国时在阿古拉斯（Agulhas）浅滩上，或出海绕行合恩角（Cape Horn）时——他也会公正地向你打出刺痛的一击（而且是正中面部），你的职责就是别被打得太晕。说到底，如果你表现出几分胆气，这位宽厚的蛮族人会让你一路打到他王座的台阶前。只有偶尔才剑落头颅；但即便你倒下了，也必有体面的葬礼和一个宽敞慷慨的坟墓。

===Original===
Such is the king to whom Viking chieftains bowed their heads, and whom the modern and palatial steamship defies with impunity seven times a week. And yet it is but defiance, not victory. The magnificent barbarian sits enthroned in a mantle of gold-lined clouds looking from on high on great ships gliding like mechanical toys upon his sea and on men who, armed with fire and iron, no longer need to watch anxiously for the slightest sign of his royal mood. He is disregarded; but he has kept all his strength, all his splendour, and a great part of his power. Time itself, that shakes all the thrones, is on the side of that king. The sword in his hand remains as sharp as ever upon both its edges; and he may well go on playing his royal game of quoits with hurricanes, tossing them over from the continent of republics to the continent of kingdoms, in the assurance that both the new republics and the old kingdoms, the heat of fire and the strength of iron, with the untold generations of audacious men, shall crumble to dust at the steps of his throne, and pass away, and be forgotten before his own rule comes to an end.

===Chinese===
这便是维京首领们俯首称臣的那位君王，而现代豪华蒸汽轮船每周七次安然无恙地违抗着他。然而这不过是违抗，而非胜利。这位辉煌的蛮王端坐于金线镶边的云毯之上，从高处俯瞰大船如机械玩具般在他的海面上滑行，俯瞰那些手持火与铁、不再需要焦急等待他皇家心情之丝毫征兆的人。他被漠视了；但他保全了全部的力量、全部的辉煌和大半的权势。时间本身——那个动摇一切王座的力量——也站在这位国王一边。他手中的剑两刃依旧锋利如初；他大可继续与飓风对弈他那王室套圈游戏，将它们从共和之国抛向君主之邦，深信无论是新兴的共和国还是古老的王国、火的炽热还是铁的力量，以及世世代代胆大妄为的人，都将在他王座的台阶前化为尘土，在他统治终结之前消逝并被遗忘。
