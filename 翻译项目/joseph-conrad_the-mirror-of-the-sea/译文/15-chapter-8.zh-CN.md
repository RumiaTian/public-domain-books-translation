### VIII / 第八篇

===Original===
The sailing and racing of yachts has developed a class of fore-and-aft sailors, men born and bred to the sea, fishing in winter and yachting in summer; men to whom the handling of that particular rig presents no mystery. It is their striving for victory that has elevated the sailing of pleasure craft to the dignity of a fine art in that special sense. As I have said, I know nothing of racing and but little of fore-and-aft rig; but the advantages of such a rig are obvious, especially for purposes of pleasure, whether in cruising or racing. It requires less effort in handling; the trimming of the sailplanes to the wind can be done with speed and accuracy; the unbroken spread of the sail-area is of infinite advantage; and the greatest possible amount of canvas can be displayed upon the least possible quantity of spars. Lightness and concentrated power are the great qualities of fore-and-aft rig.

===Chinese===
游艇的航行与竞速培育出一类纵帆水手——他们生于海上、长于海上，冬天打鱼，夏天驾艇；对操纵这种特殊帆装驾轻就熟。正是他们对胜利的渴求，将游艇帆船运动提升为一种具有特殊尊严的精妙艺术。如我所说，我对竞速一窍不通，对纵帆装所知亦少；但这种帆装的优势显而易见，无论是巡航还是竞速，用于休闲尤佳。它操驾省力；调帆迎风可迅速而精准地完成；不间断展开的帆面积有无穷之利；在最少的圆材上可张挂最大面积的帆布。轻盈与集中的力量——这正是纵帆装的伟大品质。

===Original===
A fleet of fore-and-afters at anchor has its own slender graciousness. The setting of their sails resembles more than anything else the unfolding of a bird's wings; the facility of their evolutions is a pleasure to the eye. They are birds of the sea, whose swimming is like flying, and resembles more a natural function than the handling of man-invented appliances. The fore-and-aft rig in its simplicity and the beauty of its aspect under every angle of vision is, I believe, unapproachable. A schooner, yawl, or cutter in charge of a capable man seems to handle herself as if endowed with the power of reasoning and the gift of swift execution. One laughs with sheer pleasure at a smart piece of manoeuvring, as at a manifestation of a living creature's quick wit and graceful precision.

===Chinese===
一支纵帆船队泊于锚地，自有一种纤秀的优雅。扬帆时酷似鸟翼舒展；灵动的旋回赏心悦目。它们是海上的飞鸟，游弋如同飞翔，与其说是操纵人造器具，不如说是一种天然本能。纵帆装之简洁、其在任何视角下的美感之隽永，我以为无可匹敌。一艘由能手操持的纵帆船、双桅小艇或单桅快艇，仿佛自行其是、通晓推理、行动迅捷。你看到一次漂亮的操舵会由衷大笑，如同目睹一个活物展露机敏与优雅的精确。

===Original===
Of those three varieties of fore-and-aft rig, the cutter—the racing rig *par excellence*—is of an appearance the most imposing, from the fact that practically all her canvas is in one piece. The enormous mainsail of a cutter, as she draws slowly past a point of land or the end of a jetty under your admiring gaze, invests her with an air of lofty and silent majesty. At anchor a schooner looks better; she has an aspect of greater efficiency and a better balance to the eye, with her two masts distributed over the hull with a swaggering rake aft. The yawl rig one comes in time to love. It is, I should think, the easiest of all to manage.

===Chinese===
在这三种纵帆装中，单桅快艇——竞速帆装中的翘楚——外观最为壮观，原因是她的帆面几乎连成一体。当你以欣赏的目光注视一艘单桅快艇缓缓驶过一个岬角或防波堤的尽头时，那巨大的主帆赋予她一种高傲而静穆的庄严。泊于锚地时，纵帆船更好看；两根桅杆以潇洒的后倾角分布在船体上，给人以更高效、更均衡的视觉印象。双桅小艇则越处越爱。我想，它是所有类型中最容易操驾的。

===Original===
For racing, a cutter; for a long pleasure voyage, a schooner; for cruising in home waters, the yawl; and the handling of them all is indeed a fine art. It requires not only the knowledge of the general principles of sailing, but a particular acquaintance with the character of the craft. All vessels are handled in the same way as far as theory goes, just as you may deal with all men on broad and rigid principles. But if you want that success in life which comes from the affection and confidence of your fellows, then with no two men, however similar they may appear in their nature, will you deal in the same way. There may be a rule of conduct; there is no rule of human fellowship. To deal with men is as fine an art as it is to deal with ships. Both men and ships live in an unstable element, are subject to subtle and powerful influences, and want to have their merits understood rather than their faults found out.

===Chinese===
竞速用单桅快艇，长途休闲远航用纵帆船，近海水域巡航用双桅小艇——而操驾它们的确是一门精妙的艺术。这不仅需要掌握航行的一般原理，更须对每条船的性格有独特的了解。就理论而言，操驾所有船舶的方式都相同，正如你对所有人都可以秉持宽泛而刻板的原则。但若你想要从同僚的爱戴与信任中获得人生的成功，那么面对气质再相似的两个人，你也不能以同样的方式相处。行为可以有准则；人际交往却没有定法。与人打交道和与船打交道同为精妙的艺术。人与船都栖身于不稳定的元素之中，受微妙而强大的力量支配，它们期望自身的优点被理解，而非缺点被揭穿。

===Original===
It is not what your ship will *not* do that you want to know to get on terms of successful partnership with her; it is, rather, that you ought to have a precise knowledge of what she will do for you when called upon to put forth what is in her by a sympathetic touch. At first sight the difference does not seem great in either line of dealing with the difficult problem of limitations. But the difference is great. The difference lies in the spirit in which the problem is approached. After all, the art of handling ships is finer, perhaps, than the art of handling men.

===Chinese===
要与你的船结成成功的伙伴关系，你须知的不是她*不肯*做什么，而是在你以心领神会的触感召唤她倾尽所能时，她会为你做什么。乍看之下，这两种面对局限性难题的方式差别不大。但差别是巨大的。差别在于面对问题时所抱持的精神。说到底，操驾船舶的艺术或许比驾驭人心的艺术更为精微。

===Original===
And, like all fine arts, it must be based upon a broad, solid sincerity, which, like a law of Nature, rules an infinity of different phenomena. Your endeavour must be single-minded. You would talk differently to a coal-heaver and to a professor. But is this duplicity? I deny it. The truth consists in the genuineness of the feeling, in the genuine recognition of the two men, so similar and so different, as your two partners in the hazard of life. Obviously, a humbug, thinking only of winning his little race, would stand a chance of profiting by his artifices. Men, professors or coal-heavers, are easily deceived; they even have an extraordinary knack of lending themselves to deception, a sort of curious and inexplicable propensity to allow themselves to be led by the nose with their eyes open. But a ship is a creature which we have brought into the world, as it were on purpose to keep us up to the mark.

===Chinese===
一如所有精妙的艺术，它必须建立在广博而坚实的真诚之上——这种真诚如同自然法则，统驭着无穷无尽的现象。你的努力必须专一。你会对一个运煤工和一位教授说不同的话。但这是虚伪吗？我不承认。真相在于感情的真挚，在于对这两个人——如此相似又如此不同——作为你人生险途中的伙伴的真诚认知。显然，一个只想着赢自己那场小赛的骗子，或许能靠伎俩获利。人——教授也好运煤工也罢——容易受骗；他们甚至有一种不可思议的本事，心甘情愿地配合欺骗，仿佛一种古怪而难以解释的倾向，睁着眼睛任人牵着鼻子走。但船是我们带到这个世界上来的造物，仿佛专为鞭策我们恪守本分。

===Original===
In her handling a ship will not put up with a mere pretender, as, for instance, the public will do with Mr. X, the popular statesman, Mr. Y, the popular scientist, or Mr. Z, the popular—what shall we say?—anything from a teacher of high morality to a bagman—who have won their little race. But I would like (though not accustomed to betting) to wager a large sum that not one of the few first-rate skippers of racing yachts has ever been a humbug. It would have been too difficult. The difficulty arises from the fact that one does not deal with ships in a mob, but with a ship as an individual. So we may have to do with men. But in each of us there lurks some particle of the mob spirit, of the mob temperament. No matter how earnestly we strive against each other, we remain brothers on the lowest side of our intellect and in the instability of our feelings. With ships it is not so. Much as they are to us, they are nothing to each other. Those sensitive creatures have no ears for our blandishments. It takes something more than words to cajole them to do our will, to cover us with glory.

===Chinese===
操驾一艘船时，她不会容忍一个纯粹的冒牌货——不像公众之于 X 先生（那位受欢迎的政客）、Y 先生（那位受欢迎的科学家）或 Z 先生（那位受欢迎的——怎么说呢？——从道德导师到推销员不等的——他们赢了自己的小赛）。但我虽不惯于打赌，却愿押上一大笔钱：少数顶尖游艇竞速船长中，没有一个是骗子。那太难了。困难在于你不是面对一群船打交道，而是面对一艘作为个体的船。与人打交道亦然。但在我们每个人身上，都潜藏着些许群氓的精神、群氓的气质。无论我们如何真诚地彼此角力，在智识的最底层和情感的不稳定中，我们始终是兄弟。与船则不然。船对我们意义深重，彼此之间却漠不关心。那些敏感的造物对我们的甜言蜜语充耳不闻。要哄骗它们服从我们的意志、为我们赢得荣耀，仅凭言语是不够的。

===Original===
Luckily, too, or else there would have been more shoddy reputations for first-rate seamanship. Ships have no ears, I repeat, though, indeed, I think I have known ships who really seemed to have had eyes, or else I cannot understand on what ground a certain 1,000-ton barque of my acquaintance on one particular occasion refused to answer her helm, thereby saving a frightful smash to two ships and to a very good man's reputation. I knew her intimately for two years, and in no other instance either before or since have I known her to do that thing. The man she had served so well (guessing, perhaps, at the depths of his affection for her) I have known much longer, and in bare justice to him I must say that this confidence-shattering experience (though so fortunate) only augmented his trust in her. Yes, our ships have no ears, and thus they cannot be deceived.

===Chinese===
所幸如此，否则那些所谓一流船艺的虚名会更多。船没有耳朵，我再次声明；不过我确实认识一些船，她们似乎真的长了眼睛——否则我无法解释我所熟识的一艘千吨三桅帆船（barque）在某个特定时刻拒绝服从舵令的原因，而正是这一举动避免了一场可怕的两船碰撞，保全了一位极好之人的声誉。我与她亲密相处了两年，在此之前和之后，我从未见过她再做出那样的事。她所效劳得如此出色的那个人（她或许猜度了他对她的深情），我认识他更久；出于对他公正的评价，我必须说这次震碎信心的经历（尽管如此幸运）只是加深了他对她的信赖。是的，我们的船没有耳朵，因此不可被欺骗。

===Original===
I would illustrate my idea of fidelity as between man and ship, between the master and his art, by a statement which, though it might appear shockingly sophisticated, is really very simple. I would say that a racing-yacht skipper who thought of nothing else but the glory of winning the race would never attain to any eminence of reputation. The genuine masters of their craft—I say this confidently from my experience of ships—have thought of nothing but of doing their very best by the vessel under their charge. To forget one's self, to surrender all personal feeling in the service of that fine art, is the only way for a seaman to the faithful discharge of his trust.

===Chinese===
我想用一个陈述来阐明人与船之间、船长与其艺术之间的忠诚之道——这个陈述乍听或许令人惊异地复杂，实则极为简单。我想说：一个满脑子只想着赢得比赛荣耀的游艇竞速船长，永远不会获得显赫的声誉。这门行当真正的行家——我根据与船舶打交道的经验自信地说——心中所想的唯有一件事：为他们所管辖的船舶倾尽全力。忘却自我，将一切个人情感交付于这门精妙艺术的服务——这是水手忠实履行其职责的唯一途径。

===Original===
Such is the service of a fine art and of ships that sail the sea. And therein I think I can lay my finger upon the difference between the seamen of yesterday, who are still with us, and the seamen of tomorrow, already entered upon the possession of their inheritance. History repeats itself, but the special call of an art which has passed away is never reproduced. It is as utterly gone out of the world as the song of a destroyed wild bird. Nothing will awaken the same response of pleasurable emotion or conscientious endeavour. And the sailing of any vessel afloat is an art whose fine form seems already receding from us on its way to the overshadowed Valley of Oblivion.

===Chinese===
这便是精妙艺术与航海的服役。由此我想我能指出昨日水手——他们尚在我们身边——与明日水手——他们已然踏上继承之路——之间的差别。历史重复自身，但一种已经消逝的艺术所特有的召唤永不会重现。它像一只被毁灭的野鸟的歌声，彻底从世界上消失了。没有什么能再唤起同样的欣悦之情或尽心竭力。而漂浮于水面的任何船舶的帆驶，似乎已是一种正在退向幽暗的遗忘之谷（Valley of Oblivion）的精妙艺术。

===Original===
The taking of a modern steamship about the world (though one would not minimize its responsibilities) has not the same quality of intimacy with nature, which, after all, is an indispensable condition to the building up of an art. It is less personal and a more exact calling; less arduous, but also less gratifying in the lack of close communion between the artist and the medium of his art. It is, in short, less a matter of love. Its effects are measured exactly in time and space as no effect of an art can be. It is an occupation which a man not desperately subject to seasickness can be imagined to follow with content, without enthusiasm, with industry, without affection. Punctuality is its watchword. The incertitude which attends closely every artistic endeavour is absent from its regulated enterprise. It has no great moments of self-confidence, or moments not less great of doubt and heart-searching. It is an industry which, like other industries, has its romance, its honour and its rewards, its bitter anxieties and its hours of ease. But such seagoing has not the artistic quality of a single-handed struggle with something much greater than yourself; it is not the laborious absorbing practice of an art whose ultimate result remains on the knees of the gods. It is not an individual, temperamental achievement, but simply the skilled use of a captured force, merely another step forward upon the way of universal conquest.

===Chinese===
驾驭一艘现代蒸汽轮船环游世界（当然不应低估其责任）却不具备同样与自然亲密的品质——而这种亲密毕竟是造就一门艺术不可或缺的条件。它较少个人色彩，是一种更精确的行当；较不艰辛，但也因艺术家与其艺术媒介之间缺乏亲密交流而较少满足。简言之，它较少关乎爱。它的效果在时间和空间中被精确衡量，而任何艺术的效果都无法如此。这是一种只要不严重晕船便可想象有人能欣然从事的职业——无须热忱，无需深情。准时是它的信条。伴随每一次艺术探索的不确定性，在这种规范化的事业中付之阙如。它没有伟大的自信时刻，也没有同样伟大的犹疑与自省时刻。它是一种产业，如同其他产业，有其浪漫、荣誉与报偿，有其苦涩的焦虑与轻松的时刻。但这样的航海不具备那种与远比自身强大的力量单打独斗的艺术品质；它不是对一门最终结果仍取决于神意的艺术的艰辛而专注的修炼。它不是一项个人的、气质性的成就，而仅仅是对一种被截获之力的精巧运用——不过是普世征服之路上的又一步而已。
