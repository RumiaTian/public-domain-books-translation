### XXXII / 第三十二篇

===Original===
The view of ships lying moored in some of the older docks of London has always suggested to my mind the image of a flock of swans kept in the flooded backyard of grim tenement houses. The flatness of the walls surrounding the dark pool on which they float brings out wonderfully the flowing grace of the lines on which a ship's hull is built. The lightness of these forms, devised to meet the winds and the seas, makes, by contrast with the great piles of bricks, the chains and cables of their moorings appear very necessary, as if nothing less could prevent them from soaring upwards and over the roofs. The least puff of wind stealing round the corners of the dock buildings stirs these captives fettered to rigid shores. It is as if the soul of a ship were impatient of confinement.

===Chinese===
每当看到停泊在伦敦某些老码头里的船只，我脑海里总会浮现出这样一幅画面：一群天鹅被圈养在阴森公寓楼后院的一汪积水里。围合着它们漂浮的暗池的那些平直墙壁，反而奇妙地衬托出船体线条那种流畅的优雅。这些为迎接风与海而生的轻盈形体，与巨大的砖砌建筑群形成了鲜明对比，使得那些系泊的锚链和缆绳显得无比必要，仿佛唯有如此才能阻止它们腾空而起、越过屋顶。哪怕是最微弱的一股风悄悄绕过码头建筑的角落，也会搅动这些被铁链锁在僵硬岸壁上的囚徒。似乎船的灵魂天生不甘于囚禁。

===Original===
Those masted hulls, relieved of their cargo, become restless at the slightest hint of the wind's freedom. However tightly moored, they range a little at their berths, swaying imperceptibly the spire-like assemblages of cordage and spars. You can detect their impatience by watching the sway of the mastheads against the motionless, the soulless gravity of mortar and stones. As you pass alongside each hopeless prisoner chained to the quay, the slight grinding noise of the wooden fenders makes a sound of angry muttering.

===Chinese===
那些卸空了货物的有桅船体，一旦嗅到一丝风的自由气息，便会躁动不安。无论系得多紧，它们总会在泊位上小幅移动，使那尖塔般耸立的索具和帆桁微微摇晃。你只需观察桅顶相对于那静止的、毫无灵魂的砖石之庄严的摆动，便能察觉它们的不耐烦。当你从每一艘被锁在码头边的绝望囚徒身旁经过时，木质护舷轻微的摩擦声，听起来像是一阵愤怒的低语。

===Original===
But, after all, it may be good for ships to go through a period of restraint and repose, as the restraint and self-communion of inactivity may be good for an unruly soul—not, indeed, that I mean to say that ships are unruly; on the contrary, they are faithful creatures, as so many men can testify. And faithfulness is a great restraint, the strongest bond laid upon the self-will of men and ships on this globe of land and sea.

===Chinese===
然而说到底，让船经历一段约束和休憩也许是有益的，正如不作为的约束与自省对于一个桀骜的灵魂也许是好的——当然，我并不是说船是桀骜的；恰恰相反，它们是忠诚的造物，无数人都可以为它们作证。而忠诚本身就是一种巨大的约束，是施加于陆地与海洋这颗星球上人与船之自我意志的最强纽带。

===Original===
This interval of bondage in the docks rounds each period of a ship's life with the sense of accomplished duty, of an effectively played part in the work of the world. The dock is the scene of what the world would think the most serious part in the light, bounding, swaying life of a ship. But there are docks and docks. The ugliness of some docks is appalling.

===Chinese===
在码头里的这段羁绊，为船的每一段生命画上了句号，带来一种尽职尽责之感，一种在世间劳作中扮演了有效角色的满足。码头，在世人看来，是船那轻盈、弹跳、摇曳的生命中最严肃一幕的场景。但码头也分三六九等。有些码头的丑陋触目惊心。

===Original===
Wild horses would not drag from me the name of a certain river in the north whose narrow estuary is inhospitable and dangerous, and whose docks are like a nightmare of dreariness and misery. Their dismal shores are studded thickly with scaffold-like, enormous timber structures, whose lofty heads are veiled periodically by the infernal gritty night of a cloud of coal-dust. The most important ingredient for getting the world's work along is distributed there under the circumstances of the greatest cruelty meted out to helpless ships. Shut up in the desolate circuit of these basins, you would think a free ship would droop and die like a wild bird put into a dirty cage.

===Chinese===
野马也休想从我口中套出北方某条河流的名字——它那狭窄的河口荒凉而危险，它的码头简直是凄凉与苦难的噩梦。它们那阴沉的岸边密集地矗立着脚手架般的巨大木构建筑，高耸的顶端不时被煤尘云团遮蔽——那是地狱般充满沙砾的黑夜。推动世间运转的最重要原料，就在这种对无助船只施以最残酷待遇的环境中被分发。被囚禁在这些水池的荒凉围合中，你会以为一条自由的船会像一只被关进脏笼子的野鸟般枯萎而死。

===Original===
But a ship, perhaps because of her faithfulness to men, will endure an extraordinary lot of ill-usage. Still, I have seen ships issue from certain docks like half-dead prisoners from a dungeon, bedraggled, overcome, wholly disguised in dirt, and with their men rolling white eyeballs in black and worried faces raised to a heaven which, in its smoky and soiled aspect, seemed to reflect the sordidness of the earth below.

===Chinese===
但船——也许是因为对人类的忠诚——能够忍受非同一般的虐待。不过，我确实见过船从某些码头驶出时，活像半死的囚犯从地牢中走出来：邋遢不堪、形容憔悴、全身裹满污垢，船上的水手们抬起焦虑的、被煤灰染黑的脸庞，向上翻着白色的眼球仰望天空——而那天空烟尘弥漫、污浊不堪，似乎映照着下方尘世的肮脏。

===Original===
One thing, however, may be said for the docks of the Port of London on both sides of the river: for all the complaints of their insufficient equipment, of their obsolete rules, of failure (they say) in the matter of quick despatch, no ship need ever issue from their gates in a half-fainting condition. London is a general cargo port, as is only proper for the greatest capital of the world to be. General cargo ports belong to the aristocracy of the earth's trading places, and in that aristocracy London, as it is its way, has a unique physiognomy.

===Chinese===
不过，关于泰晤士河两岸的伦敦港码头，有一点值得一提：尽管人们抱怨设备不足、规则过时、在快速装卸方面有所欠缺（据说如此），但没有一艘船需要从它们的大门驶出时处于半昏迷状态。伦敦是一座杂货港——这对于世界最伟大的首都来说恰如其分。杂货港属于地球贸易场所中的贵族阶层，而在那个贵族阶层中，伦敦一如既往地保持着自己独特的面貌。

===Original===
The absence of picturesqueness cannot be laid to the charge of the docks opening into the Thames. For all my unkind comparisons to swans and backyards, it cannot be denied that each dock or group of docks along the north side of the river has its own individual attractiveness. Beginning with the cosy little St. Katherine's Dock, lying overshadowed and black like a quiet pool amongst rocky crags, through the venerable and sympathetic London Docks, with not a single line of rails in the whole of their area and the aroma of spices lingering between its warehouses, with their far-famed wine-cellars—down through the interesting group of West India Docks, the fine docks at Blackwall, on past the Galleons Reach entrance of the Victoria and Albert Docks, right down to the vast gloom of the great basins in Tilbury, each of those places of restraint for ships has its own peculiar physiognomy, its own expression. And what makes them unique and attractive is their common trait of being romantic in their usefulness.

===Chinese===
缺乏如画之美，这罪名可扣不到泰晤士河沿岸的码头头上。尽管我把它们比作天鹅和后院，但不可否认，河北岸的每一座或每一组码头都有其独特的魅力。从温馨小巧的圣凯瑟琳码头（St. Katherine's Dock）开始——它幽暗如岩石峭壁间一汪静谧的池塘——到古老而富有同情心的伦敦码头（London Docks）——整个区域没有一寸铁轨，香料的芬芳在仓库之间缭绕，还有远近闻名的酒窖——再向下经过西印度码头（West India Docks）那引人入胜的一组、布莱克沃尔（Blackwall）的优良码头，掠过维多利亚与阿尔伯特码头（Victoria and Albert Docks）加利恩水道（Galleons Reach）的入口，直抵蒂尔伯里（Tilbury）巨型水池那深沉的幽暗——这些囚禁船只的地方，每一处都有自己独特的面貌、自己的表情。而使它们独一无二且迷人的，是它们共同的特质：在实用性中蕴含着浪漫。

===Original===
In their way they are as romantic as the river they serve is unlike all the other commercial streams of the world. The cosiness of the St. Katherine's Dock, the old-world air of the London Docks, remain impressed upon the memory. The docks down the river, abreast of Woolwich, are imposing by their proportions and the vast scale of the ugliness that forms their surroundings—ugliness so picturesque as to become a delight to the eye. When one talks of the Thames docks, "beauty" is a vain word, but romance has lived too long upon this river not to have thrown a mantle of glamour upon its banks.

===Chinese===
在各自的方式上，它们与它们所服务的河流一样浪漫——而那条河不同于世界上所有其他商业河流。圣凯瑟琳码头的温馨、伦敦码头的旧日风情，都深深印刻在记忆中。河下游伍利奇（Woolwich）对面的码头，以其宏大的规模和周围丑陋景观的磅礴气势而令人肃然起敬——那种丑陋如此富有画面感，反倒成了赏心悦目之事。当人们谈论泰晤士河的码头时，"美"是一个空洞的词，但浪漫在这条河上住了太久，早已将一层魅力的斗篷披在了它的两岸。

===Original===
The antiquity of the port appeals to the imagination by the long chain of adventurous enterprises that had their inception in the town and floated out into the world on the waters of the river. Even the newest of the docks, the Tilbury Dock, shares in the glamour conferred by historical associations. Queen Elizabeth has made one of her progresses down there, not one of her journeys of pomp and ceremony, but an anxious business progress at a crisis of national history. The menace of that time has passed away, and now Tilbury is known by its docks. These are very modern, but their remoteness and isolation upon the Essex marsh, the days of failure attending their creation, invested them with a romantic air.

===Chinese===
这座港口的古老，通过一长串始于此城、乘着河水漂向世界的冒险事业，激发着人们的想象力。即使是最新的码头——蒂尔伯特码头（Tilbury Dock）——也分享着历史联想所赋予的魅力。伊丽莎白女王曾驾临此地，不是她那些盛装铺张的巡游，而是在国家历史危急关头一次焦灼的公务之行。那个时代的威胁早已消散，如今蒂尔伯里以其码头闻名。这些码头非常现代，但它们在埃塞克斯（Essex）沼泽地上的偏远与孤立，建造过程中那些失败的日子，赋予了它们一种浪漫的气息。

===Original===
Nothing in those days could have been more striking than the vast, empty basins, surrounded by miles of bare quays and the ranges of cargo-sheds, where two or three ships seemed lost like bewitched children in a forest of gaunt, hydraulic cranes. One received a wonderful impression of utter abandonment, of wasted efficiency. From the first the Tilbury Docks were very efficient and ready for their task, but they had come, perhaps, too soon into the field. A great future lies before Tilbury Docks. They shall never fill a long-felt want (in the sacramental phrase that is applied to railways, tunnels, newspapers, and new editions of books). They were too early in the field. The want shall never be felt because, free of the trammels of the tide, easy of access, magnificent and desolate, they are already there, prepared to take and keep the biggest ships that float upon the sea. They are worthy of the oldest river port in the world.

===Chinese===
在那些日子里，没有什么比这些巨大的、空荡荡的水池更令人震撼的了——它们被数英里的空旷码头和一排排货棚环绕着，两三艘船仿佛迷失的孩子，在一片瘦骨嶙峋的液压起重机的森林中不知所措。人会获得一种彻底荒废、效率被虚耗的奇异印象。蒂尔伯特码头从一开始就非常高效、准备就绪，但也许它们进入这个领域太早了。蒂尔伯特码头有着伟大的未来。它们永远不会填上一个"由来已久的需要"（这是人们用来形容铁路、隧道、报纸和书籍新版的那句庄严用语）。它们进场太早了。这个需要永远不会被感受到，因为它们不受潮汐的羁绊、进出便利、宏伟而荒凉，已经在那里了，准备承接并留住漂浮在海上的最大型船只。它们配得上世界上最古老的河港。

===Original===
And truth to say, for all the criticisms flung upon the heads of the dock companies, the other docks of the Thames are no disgrace to the town with a population greater than that of some commonwealths. The growth of London as a well-equipped port has been slow, while not unworthy of a great capital, of a great centre of distribution. It must not be forgotten that London has not the backing of great industrial districts or great fields of natural exploitation. In this it differs from Liverpool, from Cardiff, from Newcastle, from Glasgow; and therein the Thames differs from the Mersey, from the Tyne, from the Clyde. It is an historical river; it is a romantic stream flowing through the centre of great affairs, and for all the criticism of the river's administration, my contention is that its development has been worthy of its dignity.

===Chinese===
说句公道话，尽管人们把批评的矛头对准了码头公司，但泰晤士河的其他码头并没有给这座人口超过某些联邦国家的城市丢脸。伦敦作为一个设备完善的港口，其成长是缓慢的，但对于一个伟大的首都、一个伟大的分销中心来说，并不算辱没身份。不可忘记的是，伦敦背后没有大工业区或大片天然资源的支撑。在这一点上，它不同于利物浦、加的夫、纽卡斯尔和格拉斯哥；泰晤士河也因此有别于默西河（Mersey）、泰恩河（Tyne）和克莱德河（Clyde）。它是一条历史之河；它是一条浪漫的溪流，流经伟大事业的核心。尽管人们对这条河的管理有种种批评，但我坚持认为，它的发展与其尊严相称。

===Original===
For a long time the stream itself could accommodate quite easily the oversea and coasting traffic. That was in the days when, in the part called the Pool, just below London Bridge, the vessels moored stem and stern in the very strength of the tide formed one solid mass like an island covered with a forest of gaunt, leafless trees; and when the trade had grown too big for the river there came the St. Katherine's Docks and the London Docks, magnificent undertakings answering to the need of their time. The same may be said of the other artificial lakes full of ships that go in and out upon this high road to all parts of the world. The labour of the imperial waterway goes on from generation to generation, goes on day and night. Nothing ever arrests its sleepless industry but the coming of a heavy fog, which clothes the teeming stream in a mantle of impenetrable stillness.

===Chinese===
很长一段时间里，河水本身就能轻松容纳远洋和沿岸贸易。那是在所谓的池塘区（the Pool）——伦敦桥正下方——船只首尾相连、在潮汐最强处锚泊的日子，它们聚成一个坚实的整体，像一座覆盖着瘦骨嶙峋、光秃秃的树林的岛屿。当贸易增长到河流无法承载时，圣凯瑟琳码头和伦敦码头便应运而生——那些宏伟的工程回应了它们那个时代的需求。泰晤士河上其他那些满载着船只、在这条通往世界各地的通衢大道上进出往还的人工湖泊，也可以说同样如此。这条帝国水道的劳作代代相传，日日夜夜不曾停歇。唯有大雾降临才能让它的不眠劳作暂停——雾气为这条繁忙的河流披上一袭密不透风的寂静斗篷。

===Original===
After the gradual cessation of all sound and movement on the faithful river, only the ringing of ships' bells is heard, mysterious and muffled in the white vapour from London Bridge right down to the Nore, for miles and miles in a decrescendo tinkling, to where the estuary broadens out into the North Sea, and the anchored ships lie scattered thinly in the shrouded channels between the sandbanks of the Thames' mouth. Through the long and glorious tale of years of the river's strenuous service to its people these are its only breathing times.

===Chinese===
当这条忠诚的河流上所有的声响和活动渐渐止息之后，唯一能听见的，是船钟的鸣响——从伦敦桥一路到诺尔（the Nore），在白茫茫的雾气中神秘而沉闷，一英里又一英里地渐渐减弱，叮叮当当，直到河口开阔处汇入北海（the North Sea），锚泊的船只稀稀落落地散布在泰晤士河口沙洲之间被遮蔽的航道上。在这条河流为它的人民提供漫长而辉煌的服务的岁月里，这些便是它唯一的喘息时光。
