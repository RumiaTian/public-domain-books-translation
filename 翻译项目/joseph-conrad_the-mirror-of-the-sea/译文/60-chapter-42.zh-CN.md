### XLII / 第四十二篇

===Original===
It was not *Tremolino*'s fault that the syndicate depended so much on the wit and wisdom and the information of Doña Rita. She had taken a little furnished house on the Prado for the good of the cause—*Por el Rey*! She was always taking little houses for somebody's good, for the sick or the sorry, for broken-down artists, cleaned-out gamblers, temporarily unlucky speculators—*vieux amis*—old friends, as she used to explain apologetically, with a shrug of her fine shoulders.

===Chinese===
辛迪加如此依赖丽塔夫人（Doña Rita）的智慧、见识与信息，并非特雷莫利诺号（*Tremolino*）的过错。她为了事业的益处——*Por el Rey*（为了国王）！——在普拉多大道租了一幢带家具的小房子。她总是为别人的好处租小房子，为那些病弱的或潦倒的人，为落魄的艺术家、输光的赌徒、暂时不走运的投机者——*vieux amis*（老朋友），她过去总是带着歉意地耸耸她那优美的肩膀这样解释。

===Original===
Whether Don Carlos was one of the "old friends," too, it's hard to say. More unlikely things have been heard of in smoking-rooms. All I know is that one evening, entering incautiously the salon of the little house just after the news of a considerable Carlist success had reached the faithful, I was seized round the neck and waist and whirled recklessly three times round the room, to the crash of upsetting furniture and the humming of a valse tune in a warm contralto voice.

===Chinese===
唐·卡洛斯（Don Carlos）是否也是"老朋友"之一，这很难说。在吸烟室里听到过更不可思议的事。我所知道的只是，一天晚上，就在卡利斯特（Carlist）取得重大胜利的消息传到忠臣们耳中之后，我不小心走进了那幢小房子的客厅，便被搂住脖子和腰，鲁莽地在房间里旋转了三圈，伴随着家具翻倒的碰撞声和一个温暖的女中音哼唱的华尔兹旋律。

===Original===
When released from the dizzy embrace, I sat down on the carpet—suddenly, without affectation. In this unpretentious attitude I became aware that J. M. K. B. had followed me into the room, elegant, fatal, correct and severe in a white tie and large shirtfront. In answer to his politely sinister, prolonged glance of inquiry, I overheard Doña Rita murmuring, with some confusion and annoyance, "*Vous êtes bête mon cher. Voyons! Ça n'a aucune conséquence.*" Well content in this case to be of no particular consequence, I had already about me the elements of some worldly sense.

===Chinese===
从那令人眩晕的拥抱中解脱出来后，我一屁股坐在了地毯上——突然的，毫不做作。在这不事张扬的姿态中，我意识到J. M. K. B.跟着我进了房间，他优雅、致命、得体而严肃，系着白领结，穿着大衬胸。为了回应他那礼貌而不祥的、探究的凝视，我无意中听见丽塔夫人带着几分困惑和烦恼喃喃道："*Vous êtes bête mon cher. Voyons! Ça n'a aucune conséquence.*（你真傻，亲爱的。瞧！这毫无关系。）"我很高兴在这种情况下自己确实无关紧要，我身上已有些许世故的端倪。

===Original===
Rearranging my collar, which, truth to say, ought to have been a round one above a short jacket, but was not, I observed felicitously that I had come to say goodbye, being ready to go off to sea that very night with the *Tremolino*. Our hostess, slightly panting yet, and just a shade dishevelled, turned tartly upon J. M. K. B., desiring to know when *he* would be ready to go off by the *Tremolino*, or in any other way, in order to join the royal headquarters. Did he intend, she asked ironically, to wait for the very eve of the entry into Madrid? Thus by a judicious exercise of tact and asperity we reestablished the atmospheric equilibrium of the room long before I left them a little before midnight, now tenderly reconciled, to walk down to the harbour and hail the *Tremolino* by the usual soft whistle from the edge of the quay. It was our signal, invariably heard by the ever-watchful Dominic, the padrone.

===Chinese===
我整了整领子——说实话，它本该是一件短夹克上的圆领，但并非如此——我机智地说我是来告别的，当晚就准备随特雷莫利诺号出海。我们的女主人，仍微微气喘，衣衫稍稍凌乱，尖刻地转向J. M. K. B.，想知道他什么时候准备好乘特雷莫利诺号，或以任何其他方式出发，以便加入王室大本营。她讽刺地问他，是否打算等到进入马德里的前夜才动身？就这样，通过机智地运用圆滑与尖刻，我们恢复了房间的气氛平衡；午夜前不久，我离开了他们——他们已温柔地和解了——走到港口，从码头边缘以惯常的轻柔口哨呼唤特雷莫利诺号。这是我们的信号，总是被时刻警觉的多米尼克（Dominic），我们的老板（padrone），听到。

===Original===
He would raise a lantern silently to light my steps along the narrow, springy plank of our primitive gangway. "And so we are going off," he would murmur directly my foot touched the deck. I was the harbinger of sudden departures, but there was nothing in the world sudden enough to take Dominic unawares. His thick black moustaches, curled every morning with hot tongs by the barber at the corner of the quay, seemed to hide a perpetual smile. But nobody, I believe, had ever seen the true shape of his lips. From the slow, imperturbable gravity of that broad-chested man you would think he had never smiled in his life. In his eyes lurked a look of perfectly remorseless irony, as though he had been provided with an extremely experienced soul; and the slightest distension of his nostrils would give to his bronzed face a look of extraordinary boldness. This was the only play of feature of which he seemed capable, being a Southerner of a concentrated, deliberate type. His ebony hair curled slightly on the temples. He may have been forty years old, and he was a great voyager on the inland sea.

===Chinese===
他会默默地举起一盏灯笼，照亮我沿着我们原始舷梯那狭窄而有弹性的跳板行走的脚步。"所以我们要出发了，"我的脚一踏上甲板，他便会低声说。我是突然启程的先兆，但世上没有任何事突然得能让多米尼克措手不及。他那浓密的黑色髭须——每天早上由码头拐角的理发师用热钳卷过——似乎隐藏着一种永恒的微笑。但我相信，没有人见过他嘴唇的真实形状。从这个宽胸男人缓慢、镇定的庄重，你会以为他这辈子从未笑过。他的眼中潜伏着一种完全无情的讽刺神情，仿佛他被赋予了一个极其老练的灵魂；而他鼻翼最轻微的翕动，便会让他那古铜色的脸庞显出非凡的勇猛。这是他似乎唯一能表现的面部活动，他是一个沉着、审慎类型的南方人。他乌黑的头发在太阳穴处微微卷曲。他大约四十岁，是内海上的伟大航行者。

===Original===
Astute and ruthless, he could have rivalled in resource the unfortunate son of Laertes and Anticlea. If he did not pit his craft and audacity against the very gods, it is only because the Olympian gods are dead. Certainly no woman could frighten him. A one-eyed giant would not have had the ghost of a chance against Dominic Cervoni, of Corsica, not Ithaca; and no king, son of kings, but of very respectable family—authentic Caporali, he affirmed. But that is as it may be. The Caporali families date back to the twelfth century.

===Chinese===
精明而无情，他的智谋足以与拉厄耳忒斯（Laertes）和安提克勒亚（Anticlea）那不幸的儿子匹敌。如果他没有将自己的狡诈与胆量与众神本身抗衡，那只是因为奥林匹斯诸神已死。当然，没有哪个女人能吓倒他。一个独眼巨人对科西嘉的多米尼克·切尔沃尼（Dominic Cervoni）也毫无胜算——不是伊萨卡（Ithaca）的；而且他并非国王之子，而是出身于非常体面的家族——正宗的卡波拉利（Caporali），他断言。但究竟如何不得而知。卡波拉利家族可追溯到十二世纪。

===Original===
For want of more exalted adversaries Dominic turned his audacity fertile in impious stratagems against the powers of the earth, as represented by the institution of Customhouses and every mortal belonging thereto—scribes, officers, and *guardacostas* afloat and ashore. He was the very man for us, this modern and unlawful wanderer with his own legend of loves, dangers, and bloodshed. He told us bits of it sometimes in measured, ironic tones. He spoke Catalonian, the Italian of Corsica and the French of Provence with the same easy naturalness. Dressed in shore-togs, a white starched shirt, black jacket, and round hat, as I took him once to see Doña Rita, he was extremely presentable. He could make himself interesting by a tactful and rugged reserve set off by a grim, almost imperceptible, playfulness of tone and manner.

===Chinese===
由于缺少更高贵的对手，多米尼克将他的胆量化作不敬的诡计，瞄准世俗的权力——以海关机构及其所有隶属人员为代表：文书、官员，以及水上和岸上的*guardacostas*（缉私巡逻员）。他正是我们需要的人，这个现代的、非法的流浪者，有着自己关于爱情、危险与流血的传奇。他有时以克制的、讽刺的语调向我们讲述其中的片段。他说加泰罗尼亚语、科西嘉的意大利语和普罗旺斯的法语，都同样自如自然。有一次我带他去见丽塔夫人，他穿着岸上的行头——一件浆过的白衬衫、黑色夹克和圆顶帽——极其体面。他能通过一种得体而粗犷的矜持——辅以一种冷峻的、几乎难以察觉的语调与举止中的俏皮——让自己显得有趣。

===Original===
He had the physical assurance of strong-hearted men. After half an hour's interview in the dining-room, during which they got in touch with each other in an amazing way, Rita told us in her best grande dame manner: "*Mais il est parfait, cet homme.*" He was perfect. On board the *Tremolino*, wrapped up in a black *caban*, the picturesque cloak of Mediterranean seamen, with those massive moustaches and his remorseless eyes set off by the shadow of the deep hood, he looked piratical and monkish and darkly initiated into the most awful mysteries of the sea.

===Chinese===
他拥有内心强大之人特有的身体上的自信。在餐厅里会谈了半小时——其间他们以惊人的速度相互了解——丽塔夫人以她最佳的贵妇姿态告诉我们："*Mais il est parfait, cet homme.*（这个人真是完美。）"他是完美的。在特雷莫利诺号上，裹在一件黑色*caban*（地中海水手标志性的斗篷）里，带着那浓密的髭须和无情的眼睛，在深兜帽的阴影映衬下，他看起来既像海盗，又像修士，仿佛深谙大海最可怖的奥秘。
