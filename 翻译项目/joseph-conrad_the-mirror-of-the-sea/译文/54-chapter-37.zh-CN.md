### XXXVII / 第三十七篇

===Original===
The cradle of oversea traffic and of the art of naval combats, the Mediterranean, apart from all the associations of adventure and glory, the common heritage of all mankind, makes a tender appeal to a seaman. It has sheltered the infancy of his craft. He looks upon it as a man may look at a vast nursery in an old, old mansion where innumerable generations of his own people have learned to walk. I say his own people because, in a sense, all sailors belong to one family: all are descended from that adventurous and shaggy ancestor who, bestriding a shapeless log and paddling with a crooked branch, accomplished the first coasting-trip in a sheltered bay ringing with the admiring howls of his tribe. It is a matter of regret that all those brothers in craft and feeling, whose generations have learned to walk a ship's deck in that nursery, have been also more than once fiercely engaged in cutting each other's throats there. But life, apparently, has such exigencies. Without human propensity to murder and other sorts of unrighteousness there would have been no historical heroism. It is a consoling reflection.

===Chinese===
地中海——远洋贸易与海战艺术的摇篮，冒险与荣耀的共同遗产、全人类的共有之物——除了这一切联想之外，它还向水手发出温柔的呼唤。它庇护过他这门行当的幼年。他注视它，如同注视一座古老宅邸中广袤的育儿室，无数代他自己的族人曾在那里学会行走。我说"他自己的族人"，因为在某种意义上，所有水手同属一家：他们都源于那位毛发蓬乱的冒险祖先——他骑跨一段不成形的圆木，以一根弯曲的树枝作桨，在一片受庇护的海湾中完成了第一次沿岸航行，耳边回荡着部落中仰慕的嚎叫。令人遗憾的是，所有那些在行当与情感上同为兄弟的人——他们的世代在那间育儿室里学会在船甲板上行走——在那里也曾不止一次凶猛地互相割喉。但生活显然有这种苛求。若无人类嗜杀及种种不义的倾向，便不会有历史上的英雄壮举。这是一个令人宽慰的反思。

===Original===
And then, if one examines impartially the deeds of violence, they appear of but small consequence. From Salamis to Actium, through Lepanto and the Nile to the naval massacre of Navarino, not to mention other armed encounters of lesser interest, all the blood heroically spilt into the Mediterranean has not stained with a single trail of purple the deep azure of its classic waters.

===Chinese===
而且，若公正地审视那些暴行，它们似乎无足轻重。从萨拉米斯（Salamis）到阿克提乌姆（Actium），经莱潘托（Lepanto）和尼罗河到纳瓦里诺（Navarino）的海军屠杀——更遑论其他次要的武装遭遇——所有英勇倾入地中海的鲜血，都未能在它那经典的深蓝色水面上留下一道紫色的痕迹。

===Original===
Of course, it may be argued that battles have shaped the destiny of mankind. The question whether they have shaped it well would remain open, however. But it would be hardly worth discussing. It is very probable that, had the Battle of Salamis never been fought, the face of the world would have been much as we behold it now, fashioned by the mediocre inspiration and the shortsighted labours of men. From a long and miserable experience of suffering, injustice, disgrace and aggression the nations of the earth are mostly swayed by fear—fear of the sort that a little cheap oratory turns easily to rage, hate, and violence. Innocent, guileless fear has been the cause of many wars. Not, of course, the fear of war itself, which, in the evolution of sentiments and ideas, has come to be regarded at last as a half-mystic and glorious ceremony with certain fashionable rites and preliminary incantations, wherein the conception of its true nature has been lost.

===Chinese===
当然，有人可能会争辩说战役塑造了人类的命运。但它是否塑造得好，这个问题仍然悬而未决。不过这几乎不值得讨论。极有可能的是，倘若萨拉米斯之战从未打响，世界的面貌仍会与我们今天所见大同小异——由人类平庸的灵感和短视的劳作塑造而成。在经历了漫长而悲惨的苦难、不公、耻辱与侵略之后，地球上的民族大多被恐惧所支配——那种只需一点廉价修辞便能轻易转化为愤怒、仇恨与暴力的恐惧。无辜的、毫无心机的恐惧一直是诸多战争的起因。当然不是对战争本身的恐惧——在情感与观念的演进中，战争最终被视为一种半神秘的、光荣的仪式，配有种种时髦的仪轨与预备的咒语，而其真正本质的概念已然失落。

===Original===
To apprehend the true aspect, force, and morality of war as a natural function of mankind one requires a feather in the hair and a ring in the nose, or, better still, teeth filed to a point and a tattooed breast. Unfortunately, a return to such simple ornamentation is impossible. We are bound to the chariot of progress. There is no going back; and, as bad luck would have it, our civilization, which has done so much for the comfort and adornment of our bodies and the elevation of our minds, has made lawful killing frightfully and needlessly expensive.

===Chinese===
要理解战争作为人类自然功能的真实面貌、力量与道德，你须在头发上插一根羽毛、在鼻子上穿一枚环——或更好的是，把牙齿锉成尖的、在胸膛上刺青。不幸的是，回归这种简朴的装饰已不可能。我们被绑缚在进步的战车上，无法回头；而糟糕的是，我们的文明——为身体的舒适与装饰、心智的提升做了如此之多——却让合法的杀戮变得骇人听闻且毫无必要地昂贵。

===Original===
The whole question of improved armaments has been approached by the governments of the earth in a spirit of nervous and unreflecting haste, whereas the right way was lying plainly before them, and had only to be pursued with calm determination. The learned vigils and labours of a certain class of inventors should have been rewarded with honourable liberality as justice demanded; and the bodies of the inventors should have been blown to pieces by means of their own perfected explosives and improved weapons with extreme publicity as the commonest prudence dictated. By this method the ardour of research in that direction would have been restrained without infringing the sacred privileges of science. For the lack of a little cool thinking in our guides and masters this course has not been followed, and a beautiful simplicity has been sacrificed for no real advantage.

===Chinese===
整个改进军备的问题，地球上各政府一直以紧张而未经反思的匆忙态度来应对，而正确的道路明明就在眼前，只需以冷静的决心沿其前行即可。某一类发明家的博学守夜与辛劳理应得到公正要求的体面奖赏；而这些发明家的身体则应以最公开的方式被他们自己完善的炸药和改进的武器炸成碎片——这是最基本的谨慎所要求的。用这种方法，那一方向的研究热情本可被遏制，而不侵犯科学的神圣特权。正因我们的导师和主宰缺乏一点冷静的思考，这条路未被遵循，一种美丽的简洁被牺牲了，却未带来任何实际的好处。

===Original===
A frugal mind cannot defend itself from considerable bitterness when reflecting that at the Battle of Actium (which was fought for no less a stake than the dominion of the world) the fleet of Octavianus Caesar and the fleet of Antonius, including the Egyptian division and Cleopatra's galley with purple sails, probably cost less than two modern battleships, or, as the modern naval book-jargon has it, two capital units. But no amount of lubberly book-jargon can disguise a fact well calculated to afflict the soul of every sound economist. It is not likely that the Mediterranean will ever behold a battle with a greater issue; but when the time comes for another historical fight its bottom will be enriched as never before by a quantity of jagged scrap-iron, paid for at pretty nearly its weight of gold by the deluded populations inhabiting the isles and continents of this planet.

===Chinese===
一个俭省的人在反思时无法不感到相当的苦涩：在阿克提乌姆之战中（那场战役的赌注不亚于世界霸权），屋大维（Octavianus Caesar）的舰队与安东尼（Antonius）的舰队——包括埃及分舰队和克利奥帕特拉（Cleopatra）那紫帆 galley——的总造价大概还不及两艘现代战列舰，或者说用现代海军书本术语来说，两艘主力舰。但无论多么笨拙的书本术语，都无法掩盖一个令每一位健全经济学家灵魂痛苦的事实。地中海不太可能再目睹一场结果更为重大的战役了；但当又一场历史性战斗到来时，它的海底将比以往任何时候都更丰富地堆满锯齿状的废铁——由这颗星球上岛屿和大陆上受骗的民众以近乎等重的黄金代价购得。
