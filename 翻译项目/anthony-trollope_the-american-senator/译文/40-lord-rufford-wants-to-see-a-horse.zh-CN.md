Lord Rufford had been quite right about the Duchess. Arabella had only taken off her hat and was drinking her tea when the Duchess came up to her. “Lord Rufford says that you were too tired to come in,” said the Duchess.

According to her Grace’s view of things it was not and could not be made “all right.” It would not have been all right were the girl to become Lady Rufford tomorrow. The scandal, or loud reproach due to evil doings, may be silenced by subsequent conduct. The merited punishment may not come visibly. But nothing happening after could make it right that a young lady should come home from hunting in a postchaise alone with a young unmarried man. When the Duchess first heard it she thought what would have been her feelings if such a thing had been suggested in reference to one of her own daughters! Lord Rufford had come to her in the drawing-room and had told her the story in a quiet pleasant manner﻿—merely saying that Miss Trefoil was too much fatigued to show herself at the present moment. She had thought from his manner that her niece’s story had been true. There was a cordiality and apparent earnestness as to the girl’s comfort which seemed to be compatible with the story. But still she could hardly understand that Lord Rufford should wish to have it known that he travelled about the country in such a fashion with the girl he intended to marry. But if it were true, then she must look after her niece. And even if it were not true﻿—in which case she would never have the girl at Mistletoe again﻿—yet she could not ignore her presence in the house. It was now the 18th of January. Lord Rufford was to go on the following day, and Arabella on the 20th. The invitation had not been given so as to stretch beyond that.

「我答应了舍妹住到五号；六号瑟比顿带着他那一伙来我处。巴特斯比，你同瑟比顿来拉德福，是六号不是？」
「正是六号，」巴特斯比说。
「可惜去不成。米斯尔托我顶喜欢。地段又居中。」
「打猎顶合适——是不是，拉德福勋爵？」
可那位讨厌的巴特斯比上尉偏偏不挪窝。
「不知奇尔特恩夫人肯否赏个脸，」拉德福勋爵说着，穿屋去寻那位夫人。他也许蠢；可奥米尼姆公爵夫人封他「当代第一蠢」时，我看她用错了字眼。
公爵夫人极有耐性，还打算再试；可那晚她没再得着手。
这一程巴特斯比上尉是拉德福勋爵的至交，随他从瑟比顿先生家一同过来。
「巴特，」当晚两人挨坐在吸烟室里，他说，「明天我要早走。」
「什么；——赶回瑟比顿家？」
「路上有事。我要去斯坦福德看一匹马。」
「我同你一起走。」
「不；——别。我赶自己的小车。我叫我的跟班去寻我的马夫，总有法子。行李我留下，你替我带。明天只说一句：我有事，不得不走。」
「我懂了。」
「你明白的，听到了什么风声，等等。替我向公爵夫人告罪。实打实的：十点我必须到斯坦福德。」
「都交给我，」巴特斯比上尉说——朋友为何甘受这般别扭的折腾，他猜了个八九不离十。
随后拉德福勋爵回房，为明日公爵夫人起身前如何把自个儿挪出是非之地，折腾了几个仆人半宿。
次日清晨，阿拉贝拉听说那人已走——幸而是从自家女仆口中——一时天旋地转。那人自然是从她身边逃了。毫无疑义。
