## V / 下铺十号隔壁车厢的女人

===Original===
With the departure of the conductor and the doctor, the group around lower ten broke up, to re-form in smaller knots through the car. The porter remained on guard. With something of relief I sank into a seat. I wanted to think, to try to remember the details of the previous night. But my inquisitive acquaintance had other intentions. He came up and sat down beside me. Like the conductor, he had taken notes of the dead man’s belongings, his name, address, clothing and the general circumstances of the crime. Now with his little notebook open before him, he prepared to enjoy the minor sensation of the robbery.

“And now for the second victim,” he began cheerfully. “What is your name and address, please?” I eyed him with suspicion.

“I have lost everything but my name and address,” I parried. “What do you want them for? Publication?”

===Chinese===
随着乘务员和医生的离去，围在下铺十号车厢的人群散开了，三三两两重新散布在车厢各处。搬运工仍然守在车厢门口。我松了口气，一屁股坐到座位上。我想好好想想，努力回忆起昨晚那些零散的细节。可我那位好奇心旺盛的熟人却另有打算。他走过来，在我身边坐下。和乘务员一样，他已经把死者的随身物品、姓名地址、衣着打扮以及这起案子的大致情况都记录下来了。此刻，他摊开小笔记本，准备好好品味一番这场盗窃案带来的小小轰动。

"那么，现在来问问第二位受害者，"他兴冲冲地开口道，"请问您的姓名和地址？"我戒备地看着他。

"我除了自己的姓名和地址，什么都没丢，"我搪塞道，"你要这些干嘛？登报吗？"

===Original===
"Oh, no; dear, no!" he said, shocked at my misapprehension. "Merely for my own enlightenment. I like to gather data of this kind and draw my own conclusions. Most interesting and engrossing. Once or twice I have forestalled the results of police investigation﻿—but entirely for my own amusement."

I nodded tolerantly. Most of us have hobbies; I knew a man once who carried his handkerchief up his sleeve and had a mania for old colored prints cut out of *Godey’s Lady’s Book*.

“I use that inductive method originated by Poe and followed since with such success by Conan Doyle. Have you ever read Gaboriau? Ah, you have missed a treat, indeed. And now, to get down to business, what is the name of our escaped thief and probable murderer?”

===Chinese===
"哦，不，当然不是！"他说，对我的误解感到惊讶，"只是我自己想弄明白罢了。我喜欢搜集这类资料，然后推断出自己的结论。有意思极了，叫人着迷。有那么一两次，我甚至抢在警方调查之前就得出结果了——不过纯粹是为了消遣。"

我宽容地点了点头。我们大多数人多少都有些癖好；我认识一个人，他把手帕塞在袖子里，还痴迷于从《戈迪女士杂志》上剪下老式的彩色版画。

"我用的是爱伦·坡开创、后来柯南道尔成功发扬光大的那种归纳推理法。您读过加博里欧吗？啊，您可真是错过了好东西。好了，言归正传，咱们这位逃跑的小偷、八成也是凶手，他叫什么名字？"

===Original===
"How on earth do I know?" I demanded impatiently. "He didn't write it in blood anywhere, did he?"

The little man looked hurt and disappointed.

"Do you mean to say," he asked, "that the pockets of those clothes are entirely empty?" The pockets! In the excitement I had forgotten entirely the sealskin grip which the porter now sat at my feet, and I had not investigated the pockets at all. With the inquisitive man’s pencil taking note of everything that I found, I emptied them on the opposite seat.

Upper left-hand waistcoat, two lead pencils and a fountain pen; lower right waistcoat, matchbox and a small stamp book; right-hand pocket coat, pair of gray suede gloves, new, size seven and a half; left-hand pocket, gunmetal cigarette case studded with pearls, half-full of Egyptian cigarettes. The trousers pockets contained a gold penknife, a small amount of money in bills and change, and a handkerchief with the initial *S* on it.

===Chinese===
"我怎么会知道？"我不耐烦地反问，"他总不会用血写在哪儿吧？"

小个子男人一脸受伤和失望的样子。

"您的意思是说，"他问道，"那些衣服的口袋全都空了？"口袋！兴奋之下，我完全忘了那个海豹皮手提箱——搬运工此刻正坐在我脚边的那只——我根本没检查过那些口袋。好奇的家伙拿着铅笔，把发现的每样东西都记下来，我把口袋里的东西一股脑倒在对面的座位上。

上装左胸口袋：两支铅笔和一支钢笔；右胸口袋：火柴盒和一本小邮票册；上装右边口袋：一副灰麂皮手套，崭新的，七码半；左边口袋：一个枪金属色香烟盒，镶嵌着珍珠，里面装了半盒埃及香烟。裤子口袋里有一把金质小折刀，一些钞票和零钱，还有一块绣着字母"S"的手帕。

===Original===
Further search through the coat discovered a card-case with cards bearing the name Henry Pinckney Sullivan, and a leather flask with gold mountings, filled with what seemed to be very fair whisky, and monogrammed H. P. S.

"His name evidently is Henry Pinckney Sullivan," said the cheerful follower of Poe, as he wrote it down. "Address as yet unknown. Blond, probably. Have you noticed that it is almost always the blond men who affect a very light gray, with a touch of red in the scarf? Fact, I assure you. I kept a record once of the summer attire of men, and ninety percent, followed my rule. Dark men like you affect navy blue, or brown."

In spite of myself I was amused at the man’s shrewdness.

===Chinese===
再仔细搜了搜上装，发现了一个名片盒，里面的名片上印着"亨利·平克尼·沙利文（Henry Pinckney Sullivan）"的名字，还有一个镶金边的皮质酒壶，里面装着看起来品质相当不错的威士忌，壶身上印着"H. P. S."的字母组合。

"他显然是叫亨利·平克尼·沙利文，"爱伦·坡的忠实追随者一边写一边说道，"地址还不知道。大概是金发。您有没有注意到，穿浅灰色衣服的几乎都是金发男人，而且领带里总带点红色？这是事实，我敢担保。我曾经记录过男士的夏季着装，百分之九十都符合我这个规律。像您这样肤色深的人，喜欢海军蓝或者棕色。"

我忍不住被这个男人的敏锐逗乐了。

===Original===
"Yes; the suit he took was dark﻿—a blue," I said. He rubbed his hands and smiled at me delightedly. "Then you wore black shoes, not tan," he said, with a glance at the aggressive yellow ones I wore.

"Right again," I acknowledged. "Black low shoes and black embroidered hose. If you keep on you’ll have a motive for the crime, and the murderer’s present place of hiding. And if you come back to the smoker with me, I’ll give you an opportunity to judge if he knew good whisky from bad."

I put the articles from the pockets back again and got up. "I wonder if there is a diner on?" I said. "I need something sustaining after all this."

===Chinese===
"是的；他拿走的那套衣服是深色的——蓝色，"我说。他搓了搓手，高兴地对我笑了笑。"那您穿的肯定是黑色皮鞋，不是棕色的，"他一边说，一边瞥了一眼我脚上那双扎眼的黄色皮鞋。

"又说对了，"我承认道，"黑色低帮皮鞋，黑色绣花袜子。照你这么推理下去，恐怕连犯罪动机和凶手的藏身之处都能挖出来。要是你跟我去吸烟车厢，我可以给你个机会判断一下，他懂不懂品威士忌。"

我把口袋里的东西放回去，站起身来。"不知道餐车开了没有？"我说，"折腾了这么一遭，我得吃点东西撑一撑。"

===Original===
I was conscious then of someone at my elbow. I turned to see the young woman whose face was so vaguely familiar. In the very act of speaking she drew back suddenly and colored.

"Oh﻿—I beg your pardon," she said hurriedly, "I﻿—thought you were﻿—someone else." She was looking in a puzzled fashion at my coat. I felt all the cringing guilt of a man who has accidentally picked up the wrong umbrella: my borrowed collar sat tight on my neck.

"I’m sorry," I said idiotically. "I’m sorry, but﻿—I’m not." I have learned since that she has bright brown hair, with a loose wave in it that drops over her ears, and dark blue eyes with black lashes and﻿—but what does it matter? One enjoys a picture as a whole: not as the sum of its parts.

===Chinese===
这时我才意识到身旁有人。我转过身，看见那位面容依稀熟悉的女人。她正要开口说话，却突然往后一缩，脸红了。

"哦——抱歉，"她急忙说，"我——以为您是——别人。"她困惑地看着我的上衣。我顿时像个拿错别人雨伞的人一样，浑身不自在：那条借来的领带紧紧勒着我的脖子。

"对不起，"我傻乎乎地说，"我很抱歉，但是——我很高兴。"后来我才知道，她有一头明亮的棕色头发，松松地打着波浪，垂过耳边，还有一双深蓝色的眼睛，长着乌黑的睫毛——不过这有什么关系呢？一幅画要整体地欣赏，不能把每个部件拆开来看。

===Original===
She saw the flask then, and her errand came back to her. "One of the ladies at the end of car has fainted," she explained. "I thought perhaps a stimulant﻿—"

I picked up the flask at once and followed my guide down the aisle. Two or three women were working over the woman who had fainted. They had opened her collar and taken out her hairpins, whatever good that might do. The stout woman was vigorously rubbing her wrists, with the idea, no doubt, of working up her pulse! The unconscious woman was the one for whom I had secured lower eleven at the station.

I poured a little liquor in a bungling masculine fashion between her lips as she leaned back, with closed eyes. She choked, coughed, and rallied somewhat.

===Chinese===
她看见了那只酒壶，这才想起自己来干什么。"车厢那头有位女士晕倒了，"她解释道，"我想也许来点烈酒——"

我立刻拎起酒壶，跟着我的向导沿过道走去。两三个女人正在围着那位晕倒的女人忙活。她们解开她的衣领，拔掉了她的发夹——不管有什么用。那个胖女人正使劲揉着她的手腕，无疑以为这样能唤醒她的脉搏！晕倒的女人正是我在车站为她买到下铺十一号的那位。

她向后靠着，闭着眼睛，我笨手笨脚地往她嘴里灌了点酒。她呛了一下，咳嗽了几声，慢慢缓了过来。

===Original===
"Poor thing," said the stout lady. "As she lies back that way I could almost think it was my mother; she used to faint so much."

"It would make anybody faint," chimed in another. "Murder and robbery in one night and on one car. I’m thankful I always wear my rings in a bag around my neck﻿—even if they do get under me and keep me awake."

The girl in blue was looking at us with wide, startled eyes. I saw her pale a little, saw the quick, apprehensive glance which she threw at her traveling companion, the small woman I had noticed before. There was an exchange﻿—almost a clash﻿—of glances. The small woman frowned. That was all. I turned my attention again to my patient.

===Chinese===
"可怜的人，"胖太太说，"她这样仰面躺着，简直让我想起我母亲；她以前也老是晕倒。"

"换谁都得晕，"另一个女人插嘴道，"一夜之间，同一节车厢里又是凶杀又是抢劫。幸好我一直把戒指装在挂在脖子上的袋子里——虽然它们硌得我睡不好觉。"

穿蓝衣服的姑娘睁大了受惊的眼睛望着我们。我看到她脸色苍白了些，看到她飞快地、忧心忡忡地瞥了一眼她的旅伴——就是我之前注意到的那个小个子女人。两人对视了一眼——几乎是对峙。小个子女人皱了皱眉。仅此而已。我把注意力重新转向我的病人。

===Original===
She had revived somewhat, and now she asked to have the window opened. The train had stopped again and the car was oppressively hot. People around were looking at their watches and grumbling over the delay. The doctor bustled in with a remark about its being his busy day. The amateur detective and the porter together mounted guard over lower ten. Outside the heat rose in shimmering waves from the tracks: the very wood of the car was hot to touch. A Camberwell Beauty darted through the open door and made its way, in erratic plunges, great wings waving, down the sunny aisle. All around lay the peace of harvested fields, the quiet of the country.

===Chinese===
她缓过来一些，要求把窗户打开。火车又停了，车厢里闷热难当。周围的人纷纷看手表，抱怨着晚点。医生匆匆赶进来，说了一句今天忙得很之类的话。业余侦探和搬运工一起守在下铺十号车厢门口。窗外，热浪从铁轨上蒸腾而起，波光粼粼；车厢的木板摸上去都烫手。一只康贝尔美人蝶从敞开的车门冲了进来，挥舞着宽大的翅膀，歪歪扭扭地在洒满阳光的过道里飞舞。四周是一派丰收田野的安宁，乡村的静谧。
