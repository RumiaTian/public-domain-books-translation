## VII / 第七章

===Original===
A Fine Gold Chain

The conductor held it out to me, his face sternly accusing.

“Is this another coincidence?” he asked. “Did the man who left you his clothes and the barred silk handkerchief and the tight shoes leave you the spoil of the murder?”

The men standing around had drawn off a little, and I saw the absolute futility of any remonstrance. Have you ever seen a fly, who, in these hygienic days, finding no cobwebs to entangle him, is caught in a sheet of fly paper, finds himself more and more mired, and is finally quiet with the sticky stillness of despair?

===Chinese===
一根细金链

列车员把那东西递到我面前，脸上带着严厉的指控神情。

"这又是一个巧合吗？"他问道。"把衣服、有条纹的丝绸手帕和紧脚的鞋留给你的那个人，是不是也把谋杀的赃物留给了你？"

围在旁边的人稍微退开了一点，我看出来了，任何辩解都是徒劳。你见过苍蝇吗？在这个讲究卫生的时代，它找不到蛛网来缠住自己，却落在了一张粘蝇纸上，越陷越深，最后带着绝望的黏腻沉寂安静下来。

===Original===
Well, I was the fly. I had seen too much of circumstantial evidence to have any belief that the establishing of my identity would weigh much against the other incriminating details. It meant imprisonment and trial, probably, with all the notoriety and loss of practice they would entail. A man thinks quickly at a time like that. All the probable consequences of the finding of that pocketbook flashed through my mind as I extended my hand to take it. Then I drew my arm back.

“I don’t want it,” I said. “Look inside. Maybe the other man took the money and left the wallet.”

The conductor opened it, and again there was a curious surging forward of the crowd. To my intense disappointment the money was still there.

===Chinese===
好吧，我就是那只苍蝇。我见过太多间接证据的把戏了，绝不相信证明了我的身份就能抵消其他那些对我不利的细节。那意味着坐牢和受审，八成还会惹来满城风雨、丢掉律师饭碗。人在那种时候脑子转得飞快。发现那个钱包可能带来的一切后果在我脑海里一闪而过，我伸出手去接，随即又缩了回来。

"我不要它，"我说。"打开看看。也许另一个人把钱拿走了，只留下了钱包。"

列车员打开钱包，人群又奇怪地向前涌来。让我大失所望的是，钱还在里面。

===Original===
I stood blankly miserable while it was counted out—five one-hundred-dollar bills, six twenties, and some fives and ones that brought the total to six hundred and fifty dollars.

The little man with the notebook insisted on taking the numbers of the notes, to the conductor’s annoyance. It was immaterial to me: small things had lost their power to irritate. I was seeing myself in the prisoner’s box, going through all the nerve-racking routine of a trial for murder—the challenging of the jury, the endless cross-examinances, the alternate hope and fear. I believe I said before that I had no nerves, but for a few minutes that morning I was as near as a man ever comes to hysteria.

===Chinese===
我呆若木鸡地站在那里，看着钱被点出来——五张一百美元钞票、六张二十美元钞票，加上一些五美元和一美元的零钞，总共六百五十美元。

那个拿笔记本的小个子非要抄下钞票号码，列车员被他惹恼了。我倒无所谓了：小事已经失去了激怒我的力量。我看见自己站在被告席上，经受谋杀案审判那套令人神经崩溃的例行程序——陪审团的甄选、没完没了的交叉询问、希望与恐惧交替折磨。我之前大概说过我神经很坚强，但那天早上有那么几分钟，我离一个人所能达到的歇斯底里只有一步之遥了。

===Original===
I folded my arms and gave myself a mental shake. I seemed to be the center of a hundred eyes, expressing every shade of doubt and distrust, but I tried not to flinch. Then someone created a diversion.

The amateur detective was busy again with the sealskin bag, investigating the make of the safety razor and the manufacturer’s name on the bronze-green tie. Now, however, he paused and frowned, as though some pet theory had been upset.

Then from a corner of the bag he drew out and held up for our inspection some three inches of fine gold chain, one end of which was blackened and stained with blood!

The conductor held out his hand for it, but the little man was not ready to give it up. He turned to me.

“You say no watch was left you? Was there a piece of chain like that?”

“No chain at all,” I said sulkily. “No jewelry of any kind, except plain gold buttons in the shirt I am wearing.”

===Chinese===
我抱起双臂，在心里给自己打了一针强心剂。我似乎成了上百双眼睛的靶心，每双眼睛都表达着不同程度的怀疑和不信任，但我尽量不退缩。这时有人打了个岔。

那位业余侦探又忙活起那只海豹皮包来了，检查安全剃须刀的牌子和那条青铜绿色领带上的制造商名字。然而他停了下来，皱起眉头，好像自己珍视的某个理论被推翻了。

接着他从包角里掏出一根大约三英寸长的细金链，举起来给我们看——一端发黑，还沾着血迹！

列车员伸手要接，但小个子还不打算交出来。他转向我。

"你说没有人给你留表？有没有这样一根链子？"

"根本没链子，"我没好气地说。"什么首饰也没有，除了我身上这件衬衫上的素金纽扣。"

===Original===
“Where are your glasses?” he threw at me suddenly: instinctively my hand went to my eyes. My glasses had been gone all morning, and I had not even noticed their absence. The little man smiled cynically and held out the chain.

“I must ask you to examine this,” he insisted. “Isn’t it a part of the fine gold chain you wear over your ear?”

I didn’t want to touch the thing: the stain at the end made me shudder. But with a baker’s dozen of suspicious eyes—well, we’ll say fourteen: there were no one-eyed men—I took the fragment in the tips of my fingers and looked at it helplessly.

“Very fine chains are much alike,” I managed to say. “For all I know, this may be mine, but I don’t know how it got into that sealskin bag. I never saw the bag until this morning after daylight.”

===Chinese===
"你的眼镜呢？"他突然向我发难：我不由自主地伸手摸了摸眼睛。我的眼镜整个上午都不见了，我甚至没注意到它们丢了。小个子冷笑了一下，递过那根链子。

"请你检查一下这个，"他坚持道。"这不就是你戴在耳朵上那根细金链的一部分吗？"

我不想去碰那东西：末端的血迹让我浑身发抖。可是在十几双充满怀疑的眼睛盯着之下——好吧，说是十四双吧：没有独眼龙——我用指尖捏起那截链子，无可奈何地端详着。

"很细的链子长得都很像，"我总算说道。"说不定这根就是我的，可我不知道它是怎么跑到那只海豹皮包里去的。我今天天亮之前从没见过那只包。"

===Original===
“He admits that he had the bag,” somebody said behind me. “How did you guess that he wore glasses, anyhow?” to the amateur sleuth.

That gentleman cleared his throat. “There were two reasons,” he said, “for suspecting it. When you see a man with the lines of his face drooping, a healthy individual with a pensive eye—suspect astigmatism. Besides, this gentleman has a pronounced line across the bridge of his nose and a mark on his ear from the chain.”

After this remarkable exhibition of the theoretical as combined with the practical, he sank into a seat nearby, and still holding the chain, sat with closed eyes and pursed lips. It was evident to all the car that the solution of the mystery was a question of moments. Once he bent forward eagerly and putting the chain on the windowsill, proceeded to go over it with a pocket magnifying glass, only to shake his head in disappointment. All the people around shook their heads too, although they had not the slightest idea what it was about.

===Chinese===
"他承认包是他的了，"我身后有人说。"对了，你怎么猜到他戴眼镜的？"问的是那位业余侦探。

那位先生清了清嗓子。"有两个理由，"他说，"怀疑他戴眼镜。当你看到一个人面部线条下垂、明明是个健康人却眼神忧郁——就该怀疑散光。此外，这位先生鼻梁上有道明显的压痕，耳朵上还有链子留下的印记。"

这番理论与实践相结合的精彩表演结束后，他一屁股坐到旁边的座位上，手里仍攥着那根链子，闭着眼睛，嘴唇紧抿。整节车厢的人都看出来了，谜底马上就要揭晓。有一次他急切地俯身向前，把链子放在窗台上，掏出一个袖珍放大镜仔细检查，结果失望地摇了摇头。周围的人也跟着摇了摇头，尽管他们压根不知道在看什么。

===Original===
The pounding in my ears began again. The group around me seemed to be suddenly motionless in the very act of moving, as if a hypnotist had called “Rigid!” The girl in blue was looking at me, and above the din I thought she said she must speak to me—something vital. The pounding grew louder and merged into a scream. With a grinding and splintering the car rose under my feet. Then it fell away into darkness.

===Chinese===
我耳朵里的轰鸣声又开始了。周围那群人似乎在移动的动作中突然定格了，好像催眠师喊了一声"定！"穿蓝衣服的姑娘正看着我，在一片喧嚣之上，我恍惚听见她说一定要跟我说话——什么要紧的事。轰鸣声越来越大，汇成了一声尖叫。伴随着刺耳的摩擦和碎裂声，车厢在我脚下猛然升起。然后它坠入了黑暗。
