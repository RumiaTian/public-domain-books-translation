## I / 第一章

===Original===
I Go to Pittsburg

McKnight is gradually taking over the criminal end of the business. I never liked it, and since the strange case of the man in lower ten, I have been a bit squeamish. Given a case like that, where you can build up a network of clues that absolutely incriminate three entirely different people, only one of whom can be guilty, and your faith in circumstantial evidence dies of overcrowding. I never see a shivering, white-faced wretch in the prisoners’ dock that I do not hark back with shuddering horror to the strange events on the Pullman car Ontario, between Washington and Pittsburg, on the night of September ninth, last.

McKnight could tell the story a great deal better than I, although he can not spell three consecutive words correctly. But, while he has imagination and humor, he is lazy.

“It didn’t happen to me, anyhow,” he protested, when I put it up to him. “And nobody cares for secondhand thrills. Besides, you want the unvarnished and ungarnished truth, and I’m no hand for that. I’m a lawyer.”

我去匹兹堡

麦克奈特（McKnight）正逐步接手我们事务所的刑事案子。我向来不喜欢这行当，而且自打那桩下铺十号的怪案之后，我变得有点神经兮兮了。试想你手头有这么一桩案子：你能编织出一张铁证如山的线索网，把三个截然不同的人统统牵连进去，可他们中间只有一个是有罪的——你对间接证据的信念就会因证据过多而窒息而死。每当我看见被告席上那个瑟瑟发抖、面色惨白的可怜虫，我就忍不住回想起去年九月九日夜晚，在华盛顿与匹兹堡之间的普尔曼车厢"安大略号"（Pullman car Ontario）上发生的那桩离奇事件，至今仍不寒而栗。

麦克奈特讲这个故事会比我在行得多，尽管他连着三个词都拼不对。不过，他虽然有想象力和幽默感，人却懒得很。

"反正这事没摊到我头上，"我跟他提的时候，他这样抗议道。"没人爱听二手惊悚故事。再说了，你要的是不加修饰、毫无美化的真相，我可干不了那个。我是个律师。"

===Original===
So am I, although there have been times when my assumption in that particular has been disputed. I am unmarried, and just old enough to dance with the grown-up little sisters of the girls I used to know. I am fond of outdoors, prefer horses to the aforesaid grown-up little sisters, am without sentiment1 and completely ruled and frequently routed by my housekeeper, an elderly widow.

In fact, of all the men of my acquaintance, I was probably the most prosaic, the least adventurous, the one man in a hundred who would be likely to go without a deviation from the normal through the orderly procession of the seasons, summer suits to winter flannels, golf to bridge.

So it was a queer freak of the demons of chance to perch on my unsusceptible thirty-year-old chest, tie me up with a crime, ticket me with a love affair, and start me on that sensational and not always respectable journey that ended so surprisingly less than three weeks later in the firm’s private office. It had been the most remarkable period of my life. I would neither give it up nor live it again under any inducement, and yet all that I lost was some twenty yards off my drive!

我也是个律师，尽管我的这个身份时不时会被人质疑。我未婚，年纪刚好能跟以前认识的那些姑娘的成年小妹们跳舞。我喜好户外活动，喜欢马胜过前述那些成年小妹，毫无浪漫情怀，被我的管家——一位上了年纪的寡妇——完全管得死死的，还常常被她杀得片甲不留。

事实上，在我认识的所有男人中，我大概是最乏味、最缺乏冒险精神的那个——一百个人里头唯一可能循规蹈矩、按部就班地从夏天的西装换到冬天的法兰绒、从高尔夫换到桥牌，四季更替中毫无偏离常规的那一个。

所以，命运之魔居然选中了我这副对一切刺激都无动于衷的三十岁胸膛，把我跟一桩犯罪拴在一起，给我贴上一张风流韵事的标签，让我踏上那场惊世骇俗、有失体面的旅程——不到三周后在事务所的私人办公室里画上了一个令人意想不到的句号。那是我一生中最离奇的一段日子。我既不愿放弃它，也绝不愿再重来一次，而我为之付出的代价不过是开球时少了二十码而已！

It was really McKnight’s turn to make the next journey. I had a tournament at Chevy Chase for Saturday, and a short yacht cruise planned for Sunday, and when a man has been grinding at statute law for a week, he needs relaxation. But McKnight begged off. It was not the first time he had shirked that summer in order to run down to Richmond, and I was surly about it. But this time he had a new excuse. “I wouldn’t be able to look after the business if I did go,” he said. He has a sort of wide-eyed frankness that makes one ashamed to doubt him. “I’m always car sick crossing the mountains. It’s a fact, Lollie. Seesawing over the peaks does it. Why, crossing the Alleghany Mountains has the Gulf Stream to Bermuda beaten to a frazzle.”

So I gave him up finally and went home to pack. He came later in the evening with his machine, the Cannonball, to take me to the station, and he brought the forged notes in the Bronson case.

I won three rubbers out of four, finished what cigarettes McKnight had left me, and went to bed at one o’clock. It was growing cooler, and the rain had ceased.
