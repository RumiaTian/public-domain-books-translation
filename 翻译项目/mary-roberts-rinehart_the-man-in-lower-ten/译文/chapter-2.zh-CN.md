## II / 第二章

### A Torn Telegram / 一封撕碎的电报

===Original===
I lunched alone at the Gilmore house, and went back to the city at once. The sun had lifted the mists, and a fresh summer wind had cleared away the smoke pall. The boulevard was full of cars flying countryward for the Saturday half-holiday, toward golf and tennis, green fields and babbling girls. I gritted my teeth and thought of McKnight at Richmond, visiting the lady with the geographical name. And then, for the first time, I associated John Gilmore’s granddaughter with the “West” that McKnight had irritably flung at me.

===Chinese===
我独自在吉尔摩家吃了午饭，然后立刻返回了城里。阳光驱散了晨雾，一阵清新的夏日微风吹散了烟霾。林荫大道上满是朝着乡间疾驰的汽车，人们趁着周六半日假期去打高尔夫球和网球，前往绿油油的田野和叽叽喳喳的姑娘们中间。我咬牙切齿地想着里奇蒙的麦克奈特（McKnight），正去拜访那位名字带有地理特征的女士。接着，我第一次将约翰·吉尔摩（John Gilmore）的孙女与麦克奈特恼火地甩给我的那个“韦斯特（West）”联系了起来。

===Original===
I still carried my traveling-bag, for McKnight’s vision at the window of the empty house had not been without effect. I did not transfer the notes to my pocket, and, if I had, it would not have altered the situation later. Only the other day McKnight put this very thing up to me.

===Chinese===
我依然提着我的旅行包，因为麦克奈特在空房子窗口看到的景象并非毫无影响。我没有把那些笔记转移到口袋里，即使我这么做了，后来的情况也不会改变。就在前几天，麦克奈特还拿这事质问我。

===Original===
“I warned you,” he reminded me. “I told you there were queer things coming, and to be on your guard. You ought to have taken your revolver.”

===Chinese===
“我警告过你的，”他提醒我。“我告诉过你怪事会来的，让你提防着点。你该带上你的左轮手枪。”

===Original===
“It would have been of exactly as much use as a bucket of snow in Africa,” I retorted. “If I had never closed my eyes, or if I had kept my finger on the trigger of a six-shooter (which is novelesque for revolver), the result would have been the same. And the next time you want a little excitement with every variety of thrill thrown in, I can put you by way of you begin by getting the wrong berth in a Pullman car, and end—”

===Chinese===
“那玩意儿就像在非洲的一桶雪一样毫无用处，”我反驳道。“就算我从不闭眼，或者手指一直扣在六响枪（这是小说里对左轮手枪的称呼）的扳机上，结果也是一样。下次你要是想找点刺激，把各种惊险都经历一遍，我可以给你指条路：你先在普尔曼车厢里订错一个铺位，然后结局——”

===Original===
“Oh, I know how it ends,” he finished shortly. “Don’t you suppose the whole thing’s written on my spinal marrow?”

===Chinese===
“哦，我知道结局怎么样，”他简短地打断道。“你以为这事儿没写在我的脊椎骨髓上吗？”

===Original===
But I am wandering again. That is the difficulty with the unprofessional storyteller: he yaws back and forth and can’t keep in the wind; he drops his characters overboard when he hasn’t any further use for them and drowns them; he forgets the coffeepot and the frying-pan and all the other small essentials, and, if he carries a love affair, he mutters a fervent “Allah be praised” when he lands them, drenched with adventures, at the matrimonial dock at the end of the final chapter.

===Chinese===
但我又跑题了。这就是非专业讲故事的人的毛病：他左右摇摆，把握不住方向；当他觉得角色没用了，就把他们扔下船淹死；他忘了咖啡壶、煎锅和所有其他小必需品；如果他写爱情故事，当男女主角历经冒险终于抵达婚姻码头时，他会念叨一声热烈的“感谢真主安拉”。

===Original===
I put in a thoroughly unsatisfactory afternoon. Time dragged eternally. I dropped in at a summer vaudeville, and bought some ties at a haberdasher’s. I was bored but unexpectant; I had no premonition of what was to come. Nothing unusual had ever happened to me; friends of mine had sometimes sailed the high seas of adventure or skirted the coasts of chance, but all of the shipwrecks had occurred after a woman passenger had been taken on. “Ergo,” I had always said “no women!” I repeated it to myself that evening almost savagely, when I found my thoughts straying back to the picture of John Gilmore’s granddaughter. I even argued as I ate my solitary dinner at a downtown restaurant.

===Chinese===
那个下午过得极其不令人满意。时间像永恒一样难熬。我溜进一家夏日杂耍剧院，又在一家服饰用品店买了几条领带。我很无聊，但并不期待什么；我对即将发生的事毫无预感。我从未遇到过什么不寻常的事；我的朋友们有时会航行在冒险的汪洋大海，或是在机遇的海岸边徘徊，但所有的海难都发生在女乘客上船之后。“因此，”我总是说，“不要女人！”那天晚上，当我的思绪又飘回到约翰·吉尔摩孙女的形象时，我几乎是凶狠地对自己重复着这句话。我甚至在市中心餐馆吃着孤独的晚餐时，还在为此争论。

===Original===
“Haven’t you troubles enough,” I reflected, “without looking for more? Hasn’t Bad News gone lame, with a matinée race booked for next week? Otherwise aren’t you comfortable? Isn’t your house in order? Do you want to sell a pony in order to have the library done over in mission or the drawing-room in gold? Do you want somebody to count the empty cigarette boxes lying around every morning?”

===Chinese===
“你的麻烦还不够多吗，”我反思道，“还要自找麻烦？‘坏消息（Bad News）’不是瘸了吗，下周还有场日间赛马。除此之外，你难道不舒服吗？你的房子不是井井有条吗？你想卖掉一匹小马，好把书房改造成传教士风格，或者把客厅贴上金箔吗？你想每天早上让人数数到处乱扔的空烟盒吗？”

===Original===
Lay it to the long idle afternoon, to the new environment, to anything you like, but I began to think that perhaps I did. I was confoundedly lonely. For the first time in my life its even course began to waver: the needle registered warning marks on the matrimonial seismograph, lines vague enough, but lines.

===Chinese===
把这归咎于漫长无聊的下午，归咎于新的环境，随你归咎于什么都行，但我开始想，也许我确实想要。我他妈的太孤独了。我生命中第一次，那平稳的轨迹开始摇摆：婚姻地震仪的指针划出了警告的刻痕，线条虽然模糊，但终究是线条。

===Original===
My alligator bag lay at my feet, still locked. While I waited for my coffee I leaned back and surveyed the people incuriously. There were the usual couples intent on each other: my new state of mind made me regard them with tolerance. But at the next table, where a man and woman dined together, a different atmosphere prevailed. My attention was first caught by the woman’s face. She had been speaking earnestly across the table, her profile turned to me. I had noticed casually her earnest manner, her somber clothes, and the great mass of odd, bronze-colored hair on her neck. But suddenly she glanced toward me and the utter hopelessness—almost tragedy—of her expression struck me with a shock. She half closed her eyes and drew a long breath, then she turned again to the man across the table.

===Chinese===
我的鳄鱼皮包放在脚边，仍然锁着。等咖啡的时候，我向后靠去，漠不关心地打量着周围的人。有常见的几对情侣，彼此专注着：我新近的心境让我宽容地看待他们。但邻桌，一男一女共进晚餐，气氛却截然不同。最初吸引我注意力的是那个女人的脸。她正隔着桌子热切地说着什么，侧脸对着我。我曾不经意地注意到她认真的神态、深色的衣服，以及她颈后那一大绺奇特的、青铜色的头发。但突然她瞥了我一眼，她表情中那彻底的绝望——几乎是悲剧——让我震惊得心猛地一沉。她半闭着眼睛，长长地吸了一口气，然后又转向了对面的男人。

===Original===
Neither one was eating. He sat low in his chair, his chin on his chest, ugly folds of thick flesh protruding over his collar. He was probably fifty, bald, grotesque, sullen, and yet not without a suggestion of power. But he had been drinking; as I looked, he raised an unsteady hand and summoned a waiter with a wine list.

===Chinese===
两人都没在吃东西。他瘫坐在椅子里，下巴抵着胸口，衣领上方堆着难看的肥厚肉褶。他大概五十岁，秃顶，相貌丑陋，阴沉，但隐约透着一股力量。但他喝过酒了；我看着他抬起一只不稳的手，用酒单叫来一个侍者。

===Original===
The young woman bent across the table and spoke again quickly. She had unconsciously raised her voice. Not beautiful, in her earnestness and stress she rather interested me. I had an idle inclination to advise the waiter to remove the bottled temptation from the table. I wonder what would have happened if I had? Suppose Harrington had not been intoxicated when he entered the Pullman car Ontario that night!

===Chinese===
年轻女人俯身越过桌子，又急切地说了起来。她不自觉地提高了声音。她并不漂亮，但她那专注和焦灼的神情倒是引起了我的兴趣。我一时冲动，想建议侍者把那瓶诱惑从桌上拿开。我想知道如果我那样做了，会发生什么？假如那天晚上哈灵顿（Harrington）进入安大略号（Ontario）普尔曼车厢时没有醉酒呢！

===Original===
For they were about to make a journey, I gathered, and the young woman wished to go alone. I drank three cups of coffee, which accounted for my wakefulness later, and shamelessly watched the tableau before me. The woman’s protest evidently went for nothing: across the table the man grunted monosyllabic replies and grew more and more lowering and sullen. Once, during a brief unexpected pianissimo in the music, her voice came to me sharply:

===Chinese===
据我推测，他们正要开始一段旅程，而年轻女人希望独自前往。我喝了三杯咖啡（这解释了我后来为何清醒），然后厚颜无耻地观看着眼前的场景。女人的抗议显然毫无作用：桌子对面的男人用单音节词咕哝着回应，脸色越来越阴沉、越来越阴郁。有一次，音乐意外地短暂轻柔下来，她的声音尖锐地传到我耳中：

===Original===
“If I could only see him in time!” she was saying. “Oh, it’s terrible!”

===Chinese===
“但愿我能及时见到他！”她说。“哦，太可怕了！”

===Original===
In spite of my interest I would have forgotten the whole incident at once, erased it from my mind as one does the inessentials and clutterings of memory, had I not met them again, later that evening, in the Pennsylvania station. The situation between them had not visibly altered: the same dogged determination showed in the man’s face, but the young woman—daughter or wife? I wondered—had drawn down her veil and I could only suspect what white misery lay beneath.

===Chinese===
尽管我对此很感兴趣，但那天晚上稍后在宾夕法尼亚车站再次遇到他们之前，我本会立刻忘记整件事，就像抹去记忆中那些无关紧要的杂物一样。他们之间的状况似乎没有明显变化：男人脸上仍是那副固执的决绝，但年轻女人——女儿还是妻子？我思忖着——放下了面纱，我只能猜测那白色面纱下藏着何等苍白的痛苦。

===Original===
I bought my berth after waiting in a line of some eight or ten people. When, step by step, I had almost reached the window, a tall woman whom I had not noticed before spoke to me from my elbow. She had a ticket and money in her hand.

===Chinese===
我排队等候了八到十个人，才买到我的铺位。当我一步一步几乎快到窗口时，一个之前没注意到的高个子女人在我身旁开口说话了。她手里拿着一张票和钱。

===Original===
“Will you try to get me a lower when you buy yours?” she asked. “I have traveled for three nights in uppers.”

===Chinese===
“你买票时能帮我弄个下铺吗？”她问。“我已经在上铺坐了三个晚上了。”

===Original===
I consented, of course; beyond that I hardly noticed the woman. I had a vague impression of height and a certain amount of stateliness, but the crowd was pushing behind me, and someone was standing on my foot. I got two lowers easily and, turning with the change and berths, held out the tickets.

===Chinese===
我当然同意了；除此之外，我几乎没注意到这个女人。我模模糊糊觉得她很高，有几分威严，但人群在身后推搡，还有人踩着我的脚。我轻易地买到了两个下铺，然后拿着找零和铺位票转过身，递出车票。

===Original===
“Which will you have?” I asked. “Lower eleven or lower ten?”

===Chinese===
“您要哪个？”我问。“十一号下铺还是十号下铺？”

===Original===
“It makes no difference,” she said. “Thank you very indeed.”

===Chinese===
“哪个都行，”她说。“非常感谢。”

===Original===
At random I gave her lower eleven, and called a porter to help her with her luggage. I followed them leisurely to the train shed, and ten minutes more saw us under way.

===Chinese===
我随手把十一号下铺给了她，叫了一个脚夫来帮她搬行李。我慢悠悠地跟着他们走向车棚，十分钟后我们就出发了。

===Original===
I looked into my car, but it presented the peculiarly unattractive appearance common to sleepers. The berths were made up; the center aisle was a path between walls of dingy, breeze-repelling curtains, while the two seats at each end of the car were piled high with suitcases and umbrellas. The perspiring porter was trying to be six places at once: somebody has said that Pullman porters are black so they won’t show the dirt, but they certainly show the heat.

===Chinese===
我朝我的车厢里看了一眼，但它呈现出卧铺车厢那种特有的、毫无吸引力的样子。铺位已经铺好；中央过道是一条夹在灰暗、挡风的帘子墙壁之间的小径，车厢两端的两个座位上则高高堆满了手提箱和雨伞。那个汗流浃背的脚夫恨不得分身六处：有人说普尔曼的脚夫是黑色的，这样就看不出脏，但他们确实显得很热。

===Original===
Nine-fifteen was an outrageous hour to go to bed, especially since I sleep little or not at all on the train, so I made my way to the smoker and passed the time until nearly eleven with cigarettes and a magazine. The car was very close. It was a warm night, and before turning in I stood a short time in the vestibule. The train had been stopping at frequent intervals, and, finding the brakeman there, I asked the trouble.

===Chinese===
九点一刻就寝实在荒唐，尤其我在火车上几乎不睡或根本不睡，所以我去了吸烟车厢，抽着烟、翻着杂志消磨到将近十一点。车厢里很闷热。那是个温暖的夜晚，就寝前我在车厢连接处站了一会儿。火车频繁地停站，我在那儿遇到了制动员，便问发生了什么故障。

===Original===
It seemed that there was a hotbox on the next car, and that not only were we late, but we were delaying the second section, just behind. I was beginning to feel pleasantly drowsy, and the air was growing cooler as we got into the mountains. I said good night to the brakeman and went back to my berth. To my surprise, lower ten was already occupied—a suitcase projected from beneath, a pair of shoes stood on the floor, and from behind the curtains came the heavy, unmistakable breathing of deep sleep. I hunted out the porter and together we investigated.

===Chinese===
似乎是下一节车厢的轴箱过热了，我们不仅晚点了，还耽误了紧随其后的第二趟列车。我开始感到惬意的困倦，随着我们进入山区，空气也变得凉爽起来。我向制动员道了晚安，回到了我的铺位。令我惊讶的是，十号下铺已经被人占了——一只行李箱从下面探出来，一双鞋子摆在地板上，帘子后面传来深沉的、不容置疑的鼾睡声。我找出脚夫，我们一起查看了情况。

===Original===
“Are you asleep, sir?” asked the porter, leaning over deferentially. No answer forthcoming, he opened the curtains and looked in. Yes, the intruder was asleep—very much asleep—and an overwhelming odor of whisky proclaimed that he would probably remain asleep until morning. I was irritated. The car was full, and I was not disposed to take an upper in order to allow this drunken interloper to sleep comfortably in my berth.

===Chinese===
“先生，您睡着了吗？”脚夫毕恭毕敬地俯身问道。没有回答，他拉开帘子往里看。是的，那个不速之客睡着了——睡得很沉——一股浓烈的威士忌气味表明他大概会一直睡到天亮。我很恼火。车厢满员，我可不想为了这个醉醺醺的闯入者舒舒服服地睡在我的铺位，而去睡上铺。

===Original===
“You’ll have to get out of this,” I said, shaking him angrily. But he merely grunted and turned over. As he did so, I saw his features for the first time. It was the quarrelsome man of the restaurant.

===Chinese===
“你得从这儿出去，”我愤怒地摇着他说。但他只是咕哝了一声，翻了个身。就在他翻身时，我第一次看清了他的面貌。正是餐馆里那个好斗的男人。

===Original===
I was less disposed than ever to relinquish my claim, but the porter, after a little quiet investigation, offered a solution of the difficulty. “There’s no one in lower nine,” he suggested, pulling open the curtains just across. “It’s likely nine’s his berth, and he’s made a mistake, owing to his condition. You’d better take nine, sir.”

===Chinese===
我比以往任何时候都不想放弃我的权利，但脚夫经过一番小小的私下查看，提出了一个解决难题的办法。“九号下铺没人，”他建议道，拉开了对面的帘子。“很可能九号才是他的铺位，他弄错了，因为他那状况。您最好睡九号吧，先生。”

===Original===
I did, with a firm resolution that if nine’s rightful owner turned up later I should be just as unwakable as the man opposite. I undressed leisurely, making sure of the safety of the forged notes, and placing my grip as before between myself and the window.

===Chinese===
我照做了，心里打定主意，如果九号铺位的合法主人晚点出现，我就和对面那家伙一样装睡不醒。我不慌不忙地脱了衣服，确认了假钞的安全，然后像之前一样把手提包放在我和窗户之间。

===Original===
Being a man of systematic habits, I arranged my clothes carefully, putting my shoes out for the porter to polish, and stowing my collar and scarf in the little hammock swung for that purpose.

===Chinese===
作为一个有条理的人，我把衣服仔细摆放好，把鞋子拿出来让脚夫擦亮，把衣领和围巾收进专门悬挂的小吊床里。

===Original===
At last, with my pillows so arranged that I could see out comfortably, and with the unhygienic-looking blanket turned back—I have always a distrust of those much-used affairs—I prepared to wait gradually for sleep.

===Chinese===
最后，我把枕头摆得舒舒服服，以便能看清外面，把那条看起来不太卫生的毯子掀开——我向来不信任这种被反复使用的东西——准备慢慢等待睡意降临。

===Original===
But sleep did not visit me. The train came to frequent, grating stops, and I surmised the hot box again. I am not a nervous man, but there was something chilling in the thought of the second section pounding along behind us. Once, as I was dozing, our locomotive whistled a shrill warning—“You keep back where you belong,” it screamed to my drowsy ears, and from somewhere behind came a chastened “All-right-I-will.”

===Chinese===
但睡意没有眷顾我。火车频繁地、刺耳地停下，我猜想又是轴箱过热了。我并非神经质的人，但想到第二趟列车就在我们后面轰隆隆地跟着，就有点不寒而栗。有一次，我正打盹，我们的机车发出一声尖利的警告——“你在后面老实待着，”它对着我昏昏欲睡的耳朵尖叫，后面某处传来一声温顺的“好吧-我会的”。

===Original===
I grew more and more wide-awake. At Cresson I got up on my elbow and blinked out at the station lights. Some passengers boarded the train there and I heard a woman’s low tones, a southern voice, rich and full. Then quiet again. Every nerve was tense: time passed, perhaps ten minutes, possibly half an hour. Then, without the slightest warning, as the train rounded a curve, a heavy body was thrown into my berth. The incident, trivial as it seemed, was startling in its suddenness, for although my ears were painfully strained and awake, I had heard no step outside. The next instant the curtain hung limp again; still without a sound, my disturber had slipped away into the gloom and darkness. In a frenzy of wakefulness, I sat up, drew on a pair of slippers and fumbled for my bathrobe.

===Chinese===
我变得越来越清醒。在克雷森（Cresson），我用手肘撑起身子，眯着眼看站台的灯光。一些乘客在那里上了车，我听到一个女人低沉的声音，是南方口音，浑厚而饱满。然后又安静了。每根神经都紧绷着：时间过去了，也许十分钟，也许半小时。接着，在毫无预警的情况下，当火车转过一个弯时，一个沉重的身体被扔进了我的铺位。这件事看似微不足道，但其突然性却令人吃惊，因为尽管我的耳朵痛苦地警醒着，却没听到外面有脚步声。下一刻，帘子又无力地垂下了；我的打扰者仍悄无声息地溜进了昏暗的黑暗中。在极度的清醒中，我坐起身，套上拖鞋，摸索着我的浴袍。

===Original===
From a berth across, probably lower ten, came that particular aggravating snore which begins lightly, delicately, faintly soprano, goes down the scale a note with every breath, and, after keeping the listener tense with expectation, ends with an explosion that tears the very air. I was more and more irritable: I sat on the edge of the berth and hoped the snorer would choke to death. He had considerable vitality, however; he withstood one shock after another and survived to start again with new vigor. In desperation I found some cigarettes and one match, piled my blankets over my grip, and drawing the curtains together as though the berth were still occupied, I made my way to the vestibule of the car.

===Chinese===
